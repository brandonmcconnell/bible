<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:50; 2 Peter 1:20"> Who is as the wise? and who knoweth the explanation of things? A man's wisdom maketh his face to shine, and the boldness of his face is changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 2:43; Titus 3:1; Proverbs 24:21"> I &#91;say&#93;, Keep the king's commandment, and &#91;that&#93; on account of the oath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4; Acts 5:8–9; Daniel 4:35"> Be not hasty to go out of his sight; persist not in an evil thing: for he doeth whatever pleaseth him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 9:12; Romans 13:1–4; Daniel 4:35"> because the word of a king is power; and who may say unto him, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; 1 Peter 3:13–14; Philippians 1:9–10"> Whoso keepeth the commandment shall know no evil thing; and a wise man's heart knoweth time and manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:17; Ecclesiastes 3:11"> For to every purpose there is time and manner. For the misery of man is great upon him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 9:12; Proverbs 24:22"> for he knoweth not that which shall be; for who can tell him how it shall be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Job 14:5; Hebrews 9:27"> There is no man who hath control over the spirit to retain the spirit; and no one hath control over the day of death; and there is no discharge in that war, neither shall wickedness deliver those that are given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:7–8; Ecclesiastes 5:13"> All this have I seen, and applied my heart unto every work that is done under the sun: there is a time when man ruleth man to his hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Proverbs 10:7"> And I have also seen the wicked buried and going away; and such as had acted rightly went from &#91;the&#93; holy place, and were forgotten in the city. This also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Psalms 50:21–22; Romans 2:4–5"> Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the children of men is fully set in them to do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; 2 Peter 2:9; Luke 1:50"> Though a sinner do evil a hundred times, and prolong his &#91;days&#93;, yet I know that it shall be well with them that fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, because they fear before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:12; Isaiah 3:11; Job 14:2"> but it shall not be well with the wicked, neither shall he prolong &#91;his&#93; days as a shadow, because he feareth not before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 2:14; Malachi 3:15"> There is a vanity which is done upon the earth; that there are righteous &#91;men&#93; unto whom it happeneth according to the work of the wicked; and there are wicked &#91;men&#93; to whom it happeneth according to the work of the righteous. I said that this also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13"> And I commended mirth, because there is nothing better for man under the sun than to eat, and to drink, and to be merry; for that shall abide with him of his labour the days of his life, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God hath given him under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Psalms 127:2; Ecclesiastes 2:23"> When I applied my heart to know wisdom, and to see the business that is done upon the earth (for also there is that neither day nor night seeth sleep with his eyes),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 73:16; Romans 11:33"> then I saw that all &#91;is&#93; the work of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, &#91;and&#93; that man cannot find out the work that is done under the sun: because however man may labour to seek &#91;it&#93; out, yet doth he not find &#91;it&#93;; and even, if a wise &#91;man&#93; think to know &#91;it&#93;, he shall not be able to find &#91;it&#93; out.</span>
