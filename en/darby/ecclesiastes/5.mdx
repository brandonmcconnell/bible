<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:19; Hebrews 12:28–29; Proverbs 15:8"> Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and draw near to hear, rather than to give the sacrifice of fools: for they know not that they do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:7; Proverbs 10:19; Isaiah 55:9"> Be not rash with thy mouth, and let not thy heart be hasty to utter anything before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God: for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God is in the heavens, and thou upon earth; therefore let thy words be few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 10:19; Ecclesiastes 10:12–14"> For a dream cometh through the multitude of business, and a fool's voice through a multitude of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; Deuteronomy 23:21–23; Psalms 76:11"> When thou vowest a vow unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools: pay that which thou hast vowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:4; Deuteronomy 23:22; Proverbs 20:25"> Better is it that thou shouldest not vow, than that thou shouldest vow and not pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:26; 2 John 1:8; James 3:2"> Suffer not thy mouth to cause thy flesh to sin; neither say thou before the angel, that it was an inadvertence. Wherefore should<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God be wroth at thy voice, and destroy the work of thy hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> For in the multitude of dreams are vanities; so with many words: but fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.7 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> If thou seest the oppression of the poor, and violent perverting of judgment and justice in a province, marvel not at the matter; for a higher than the high is watching, and there are higher than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:7–23; Psalms 115:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> Moreover the earth is every way profitable: the king &#91;himself&#93; is dependent upon the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 6:24; Luke 12:15"> He that loveth silver shall not be satisfied with silver, nor he that loveth abundance with increase. This also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Habakkuk 2:13; Psalms 119:36–37"> When goods increase, they are increased that eat them; and what profit is there to the owner thereof, except the beholding &#91;of them&#93; with his eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24; Psalms 4:8; Jeremiah 31:26"> The sleep of the labourer is sweet, whether he have eaten little or much; but the fulness of the rich doth not suffer him to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:1–2; Luke 18:22–23; Genesis 19:14"> There is a grievous evil that I have seen under the sun: riches kept for the owners thereof to their hurt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Haggai 2:16–17; Proverbs 23:5"> or those riches perish by some evil circumstance, and if he have begotten a son, there is nothing in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 49:17; Job 1:21; 1 Timothy 6:7"> As he came forth from his mother's womb, naked shall he go away again as he came, and shall take nothing of his labour, which he may carry away in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:29; Ecclesiastes 1:3; John 6:27"> And this also is a grievous evil, that in all points as he came so doth he go away, and what profit hath he, in having laboured for the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Ezekiel 4:16–17; Psalms 90:7–11"> All his days also he eateth in darkness, and hath much vexation, and sickness, and irritation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> Behold what I have seen good and comely: &#91;it is&#93; to eat and to drink, and to enjoy good in all his labour wherewith &#91;man&#93; laboureth under the sun, all the days of his life which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God hath given him: for that is his portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Ecclesiastes 3:13; Ecclesiastes 6:2"> Every man also to whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="5.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God hath given riches and wealth, and power to eat thereof, and to take his portion and to rejoice in his labour: that is a gift of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="5.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–24; Isaiah 65:13–14; Psalms 37:16"> For he will not much remember the days of his life, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="5.20 Elohim">j</a></sup>God answereth &#91;him&#93; with the joy of his heart.</span>
