<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Proverbs 22:1; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> A &#91;good&#93; name is better than precious ointment, and the day of death than the day of one's birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> It is better to go to the house of mourning than to go to the house of feasting: in that that is the end of all men, and the living taketh it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> Vexation is better than laughter; for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; 1 Samuel 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning, but the heart of fools in the house of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> It is better for a man to hear the rebuke of the wise, than to hear the song of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 58:9; Psalms 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of the fool. This also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> Surely oppression maketh a wise man mad, and a gift destroyeth the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 126:5–6"> Better is the end of a thing than its beginning; better is a patient spirit than a proud spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be vexed; for vexation resteth in the bosom of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; Psalms 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> Say not, How is it that the former days were better than these? for thou dost not inquire wisely concerning this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Wisdom is as good as an inheritance, and profitable to them that see the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> For wisdom is a defence &#91;as&#93; money is a defence; but the excellency of knowledge is, &#91;that&#93; wisdom maketh them that possess it to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; Psalms 107:43"> Consider the work of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God; for who can make straight what he hath made crooked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> In the day of prosperity enjoy good, and in the day of adversity consider:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God hath also set the one beside the other, to the end that man should find out nothing &#91;of what shall be&#93; after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> All &#91;this&#93; have I seen in the days of my vanity: there is a righteous &#91;man&#93; that perisheth by his righteousness, and there is a wicked &#91;man&#93; that prolongeth &#91;his days&#93; by his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Be not righteous overmuch; neither make thyself overwise: why shouldest thou destroy thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> Be not overmuch wicked, neither be thou foolish: why shouldest thou die before thy time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> It is good that thou shouldest take hold of this; yea, also from that withdraw not thy hand: for he that feareth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God cometh forth from them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Wisdom strengtheneth the wise more than ten mighty &#91;men&#93; that are in a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> Surely there is not a righteous man upon earth, that doeth good and sinneth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Also give not heed unto all words that are spoken, lest thou hear thy servant curse thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> For also thine own heart knoweth that oftentimes thou thyself likewise hast cursed others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> All this have I tried by wisdom: I said, I will be wise; but it was far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Whatever hath been, is far off, and exceeding deep: who will find it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> I turned, I and my heart, to know, and to search, and to seek out wisdom and reason, and to know wickedness to be folly, and foolishness to be madness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> and I found more bitter than death the woman whose heart is nets and snares, &#91;and&#93; whose hands are bands: whoso pleaseth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God shall escape from her; but the sinner shall be caught by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> See this which I have found, saith the Preacher, &#91;searching&#93; one by one to find out the reason;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; 1 Kings 11:1–3; Psalms 12:1"> which my soul yet seeketh, and I have not found: one man among a thousand have I found, but a woman among all those have I not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> Only see this which I have found: that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God made man upright, but they have sought out many devices.</span>
