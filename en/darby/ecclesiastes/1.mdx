<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:4; Romans 8:20; Ecclesiastes 5:10"> Vanity of vanities, saith the Preacher, vanity of vanities! all is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:9; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Ecclesiastes 2:22"> What profit hath man of all his labour wherewith he laboureth under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Zechariah 1:5; Ecclesiastes 6:12"> &#91;One&#93; generation passeth away, and &#91;another&#93; generation cometh, but the earth standeth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 19:4–6; Habakkuk 3:11; Psalms 42:1"> The sun also riseth, and the sun goeth down, and hasteth to its place where it ariseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:8; Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 107:25"> The wind goeth towards the south, and turneth about towards the north: it turneth about continually, and the wind returneth again to its circuits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:6–9"> All the rivers run into the sea, yet the sea is not full: unto the place whither the rivers go, thither they go again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Matthew 11:28; Ecclesiastes 4:8"> All things are full of toil; none can express it. The eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:15; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Ecclesiastes 2:12"> That which hath been is that which shall be; and that which hath been done is that which will be done: and there is nothing new under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Luke 17:26–30; Matthew 23:30–32"> Is there a thing whereof it may be said, See, this is new? It hath been already in the ages which were before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 9:6; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> There is no remembrance of former things; neither shall there be remembrance of things that are to come with those who shall live afterwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:1; 1 Kings 4:1–19"> I, the Preacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:10; Genesis 3:19; 1 Timothy 4:15"> And I applied my heart to seek and search out by wisdom concerning all that is done under the heavens: this grievous occupation hath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God given to the children of men to weary themselves therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen all the works that are done under the sun, and behold, all is vanity and pursuit of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:12–13; Isaiah 40:4; Ecclesiastes 3:14"> That which is crooked cannot be made straight; and that which is wanting cannot be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:9; 1 Kings 4:30; 1 Kings 10:7"> I communed with mine own heart, saying, Lo, I have become great and have acquired wisdom more than all they that have been before me over Jerusalem; and my heart hath seen much of wisdom and knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ecclesiastes 1:13–14"> And I applied my heart to the knowledge of wisdom, and to the knowledge of madness and folly: I perceived that this also is a striving after the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 28:28; Ecclesiastes 12:12–13; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20"> For in much wisdom is much vexation, and he that increaseth knowledge increaseth sorrow.</span>
