<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13"> There is an evil that I have seen under the sun, and it is frequent among men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:19; Psalms 17:14; Psalms 73:7"> one to whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God giveth riches, wealth, and honour, and he wanteth nothing for his soul of all that he desireth, yet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God giveth him not power to eat thereof, but a stranger eateth it: this is vanity, and a sore evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 3:16; Jeremiah 22:19; Ecclesiastes 4:3"> If a man beget a hundred &#91;sons&#93;, and live many years, so that the days of his years be many, but his soul be not filled with good, and also he have no burial, I say an untimely birth is better than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:13"> For it cometh in vanity, and departeth in darkness, and its name is covered with darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Job 14:1; Job 3:10–13"> moreover it hath not seen nor known the sun: this hath rest rather than the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 3:20; Job 30:23"> Yea, though he live twice a thousand years, yet hath he seen no good: do not all go to one place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 6:27; Proverbs 16:26; 1 Timothy 6:6–8"> All the labour of man is for his mouth, and yet the appetite is not filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Psalms 101:2"> For what advantage hath the wise above the fool? what hath the poor, that knoweth to walk before the living?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Job 31:7; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> Better is the seeing of the eyes than the wandering of the desire: this also is vanity and pursuit of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:32; Isaiah 45:9–10; Job 40:2"> That which is hath already been named; and what man is, is known, and that he cannot contend with him that is mightier than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:16; Ecclesiastes 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:3–11"> For there are many things that increase vanity: what is man advantaged?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:14; Job 14:2; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> For who knoweth what is good for man in life, all the days of his vain life which he spendeth as a shadow? for who can tell man what shall be after him under the sun?</span>
