<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> To everything there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heavens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> A time to be born, and a time to die; A time to plant, and a time to pluck up that which is planted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> A time to kill, and a time to heal; A time to break down, and a time to build up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> A time to weep, and a time to laugh; A time to mourn, and a time to dance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> A time to cast away stones, and a time to gather stones together; A time to embrace, and a time to refrain from embracing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> A time to seek, and a time to lose; A time to keep, and a time to cast away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> A time to rend, and a time to sew; A time to keep silence, and a time to speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> A time to love, and a time to hate; A time of war, and a time of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What profit hath he that worketh from that wherein he laboureth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen the travail that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath given to the sons of men to toil in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> He hath made everything beautiful in its time; also he hath set the world in their heart, so that man findeth not out from the beginning to the end the work that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God doeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> I know that there is nothing good for them but to rejoice and to do well in their life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> yea also that every man should eat and drink, and enjoy good in all his labour, it is the gift of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 33:11; Romans 11:36"> I know that whatever<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God doeth, it shall be for ever; there is nothing to be added to it, nor anything to be taken from it; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God doeth &#91;it&#93;, that &#91;men&#93; should fear before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> That which is was long ago, and that which is to be hath already been; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God bringeth back again that which is past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalms 94:21–22"> And moreover I saw under the sun, that in the place of judgment, wickedness was there; and in the place of righteousness, wickedness was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> I said in my heart,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God will judge the righteous and the wicked; for there is a time there for every purpose and for every work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I said in my heart, It is thus with the children of men, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God may prove them, and that they should see that they themselves are but beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 89:47–48"> For what befalleth the children of men befalleth beasts; even one thing befalleth them: as the one dieth, so dieth the other, and they have all one breath; and man hath no pre-eminence above the beast: for all is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> All go unto one place: all are of the dust, and all return to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Who knoweth the spirit of the children of men? Doth it go upwards? and the spirit of the beasts, doth it go downwards to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> And I have seen that there is nothing better than that man should rejoice in his own works; for that is his portion; for who shall bring him to see what shall be after him?</span>
