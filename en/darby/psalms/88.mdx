<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let my prayer come before thee; incline thine ear unto my cry.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my soul is full of troubles, and my life draweth nigh to Sheol.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am reckoned with them that go down into the pit; I am as a man that hath no strength:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Prostrate among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave; whom thou rememberest no more, and who are cut off from thy hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast laid me in the lowest pit, in dark places, in the deeps.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thy fury lieth hard upon me, and thou hast afflicted &#91;me&#93; with all thy waves. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast put my familiar friends far from me; thou hast made me an abomination unto them: I am shut up, and I cannot come forth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Mine eye consumeth by reason of affliction. Upon thee, Jehovah, have I called every day; I have stretched out my hands unto thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wilt thou do wonders to the dead? shall the shades arise and praise thee? Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shall thy loving-kindness be declared in the grave? thy faithfulness in Destruction?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shall thy wonders be known in the dark? and thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, Jehovah, I cry unto thee, and in the morning my prayer cometh before thee.</span><br /></span>
