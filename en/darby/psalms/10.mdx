<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Psalms 27:9; Psalms 13:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why, Jehovah, standest thou afar off? &#91;Why&#93; hidest thou thyself in times of distress?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 59:12; Psalms 36:11; Psalms 7:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked, in his pride, doth hotly pursue the afflicted. They shall be taken in the devices that they have imagined.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the wicked boasteth of his soul's desire, and he blesseth the covetous; he contemneth Jehovah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wicked &#91;saith&#93;, in the haughtiness of his countenance, He doth not search out: all his thoughts are, There is no</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 12:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">His ways always succeed; thy judgments are far above out of his sight; &#91;as for&#93; all his adversaries, he puffeth at them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">He saith in his heart, I shall not be moved; from generation to generation I shall be in no adversity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 140:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">His mouth is full of cursing, and deceit, and oppression; under his tongue is mischief and iniquity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He sitteth in the lurking-places of the villages; in the secret places doth he slay the innocent: his eyes watch for the wretched.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 17:12; Psalms 59:3; Micah 7:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He lieth in wait secretly, like a lion in his thicket; he lieth in wait to catch the afflicted: he doth catch the afflicted, drawing him into his net.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He croucheth, he boweth down, that the wretched may fall by his strong ones.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 94:7; Psalms 73:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He saith in his heart,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.11 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God hath forgotten, he hideth his face, he will never see &#91;it&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; Psalms 9:12; Psalms 94:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Arise, Jehovah; O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.12 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, lift up thy hand: forget not the afflicted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; Psalms 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wherefore doth the wicked contemn</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God? He hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require &#91;it&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 146:9; Psalms 55:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast seen &#91;it&#93;, for thou thyself beholdest trouble and vexation, to requite by thy hand. The wretched committeth himself unto thee; thou hast been the helper of the fatherless.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 37:17; Job 38:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Break thou the arm of the wicked, and as for the evil man, seek out his wickedness &#91;till&#93; thou find none.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Jehovah is King for ever and ever: the nations have perished out of his land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; Psalms 34:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Jehovah, thou hast heard the desire of the meek, thou hast established their heart: thou causest thine ear to hear,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">To do justice to the fatherless and the oppressed one, that the man of the earth may terrify no more.</span></span>
