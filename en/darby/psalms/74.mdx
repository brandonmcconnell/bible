<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember thine assembly, which thou hast purchased of old, which thou hast redeemed &#91;to be&#93; the portion of thine inheritance, this mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lift up thy steps unto the perpetual desolations: everything in the sanctuary hath the enemy destroyed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thine adversaries roar in the midst of thy place of assembly; they set up their signs &#91;for&#93; signs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">&#91;A man&#93; was known as he could lift up axes in the thicket of trees;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">And now they break down its carved work altogether, with hatchets and hammers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have set on fire thy sanctuary, they have profaned the habitation of thy name to the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They said in their heart, Let us destroy them together: they have burned up all</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="74.8 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God's places of assembly in the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">We see not our signs; there is no more any prophet, neither is there among us any that knoweth how long.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:50–51; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 89:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="74.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, shall the adversary reproach? Shall the enemy contemn thy name for ever?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; Psalms 78:65–66; Psalms 44:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why withdrawest thou thy hand, and thy right hand? &#91;pluck it&#93; out of thy bosom: consume &#91;them&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="74.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God is my king of old, accomplishing deliverances in the midst of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Psalms 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength; thou didst break the heads of the monsters on the waters:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 104:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou didst break in pieces the heads of leviathan, thou gavest him to be meat to those that people the desert.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; Psalms 105:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou didst cleave fountain and torrent, thou driedst up ever-flowing rivers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 104:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">The day is thine, the night also is thine; thou hast prepared the moon and the sun:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast set all the borders of the earth; summer and winter — thou didst form them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember this, that an enemy hath reproached Jehovah, and a foolish people have contemned thy name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Matthew 10:16; Psalms 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give not up the soul of thy turtle-dove unto the wild beast; forget not the troop of thine afflicted for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have respect unto the covenant; for the dark places of the earth are full of the dwellings of violence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Oh let not the oppressed one return ashamed; let the afflicted and needy praise thy name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 74:18; Psalms 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rise up, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="74.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, plead thine own cause: remember how the foolish man reproacheth thee all the day;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Psalms 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Forget not the voice of thine adversaries: the tumult of those that rise up against thee ascendeth continually.</span></span>
