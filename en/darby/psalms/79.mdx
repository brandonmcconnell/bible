<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="79.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, the nations are come into thine inheritance: thy holy temple have they defiled; they have laid Jerusalem in heaps.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The dead bodies of thy servants have they given to be meat unto the fowl of the heavens, the flesh of thy saints unto the beasts of the earth:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their blood have they shed like water round about Jerusalem, and there was none to bury &#91;them&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We are become a reproach to our neighbours, a mockery and a derision to them that are round about us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, O Jehovah? wilt thou be angry for ever? Shall thy jealousy burn like fire?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out thy fury upon the nations that have not known thee, and upon the kingdoms that call not upon thy name:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have devoured Jacob, and laid waste his habitation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember not against us the iniquities of &#91;our&#93; forefathers; let thy tender mercies speedily come to meet us: for we are brought very low.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="79.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God of our salvation, because of the glory of thy name; and deliver us, and forgive our sins, for thy name's sake.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wherefore should the nations say, Where is their</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="79.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God? Let the avenging of the blood of thy servants that is shed be known among the nations in our sight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the groaning of the prisoner come before thee; according to the greatness of thine arm, preserve those that are appointed to die;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">And render unto our neighbours, sevenfold into their bosom, their reproach, wherewith they have reproached thee, O Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">And we, thy people and the sheep of thy pasture, will give thanks unto thee for ever; we will shew forth thy praise from generation to generation.</span></span>
