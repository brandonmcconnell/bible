<div className="hs">To the chief Musician. On Shushan. Testimony. Michtam of David; to teach: when he strove with the Syrians of Mesopotamia, and the Syrians of Zobah, and Joab returned, and smote the Edomites in the valley of salt, twelve thousand.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:12–13; 2 Samuel 8:3; Psalms 44:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="60.1 Elohim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, thou hast cast us off, thou hast scattered us, thou hast been displeased: restore us again.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast made the earth to tremble, thou hast rent it: heal the breaches thereof; for it shaketh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 71:20; Isaiah 51:22; Isaiah 51:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast shewn thy people hard things; thou hast made us to drink the wine of bewilderment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 20:5; Isaiah 11:12; Exodus 17:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast given a banner to them that fear thee, that it may be displayed because of the truth, (Selah,)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:6–13; Psalms 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">That thy beloved ones may be delivered. Save with thy right hand, and answer me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:27; Psalms 89:35; Genesis 12:6"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="60.6 Elohim">b</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">God hath spoken in his holiness: I will exult, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Gilead is mine, and Manasseh is mine, and Ephraim is the strength of my head; Judah is my law-giver;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 8:1–2; Psalms 108:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Moab is my wash-pot; upon Edom will I cast my sandal; Philistia, shout aloud because of me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me unto Edom?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:11; Joshua 7:12; Psalms 60:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">&#91;Wilt&#93; not thou, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="60.10 Elohim">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, who didst cast us off? and didst not go forth, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="60.10 Elohim">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, with our armies?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 108:12; Psalms 130:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give us help from trouble; for vain is man's deliverance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Through</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="60.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God we shall do valiantly; and he it is that will tread down our adversaries.</span></span>
