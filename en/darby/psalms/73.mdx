<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="73.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God is good to Israel, to such as are of a pure heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, my feet were almost gone, my steps had well nigh slipped;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I was envious at the arrogant, seeing the prosperity of the wicked.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they have no pangs in their death, and their body is well nourished;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have not the hardships of mankind, neither are they plagued like &#91;other&#93; men:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore pride encompasseth them as a neck-chain, violence covereth them &#91;as&#93; a garment;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their eyes stand out from fatness, they exceed the imaginations of their heart:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They mock and speak wickedly of oppression, they speak loftily:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They set their mouth in the heavens, and their tongue walketh through the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore his people turn hither, and waters in fulness are wrung out to them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they say, How can</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="73.11 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God know, and is there knowledge in the Most High?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, these are the wicked, and they prosper in the world: they heap up riches.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly have I purified my heart in vain, and washed my hands in innocency:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For all the day have I been plagued, and chastened every morning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I said, I will speak thus, behold, I should be faithless to the generation of thy children.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I thought to be able to know this, it was a grievous task in mine eyes;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Until I went into the sanctuaries of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="73.17 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God; &#91;then&#93; understood I their end.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly thou settest them in slippery places, thou castest them down in ruins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">How are they suddenly made desolate! they pass away, consumed with terrors.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a dream, when one awaketh, wilt thou, Lord, on arising despise their image.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">When my heart was in a ferment, and I was pricked in my reins,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I was brutish and knew nothing; I was &#91;as&#93; a beast with thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nevertheless I am continually with thee: thou hast holden my right hand;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou wilt guide me by thy counsel, and after the glory, thou wilt receive me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom have I in the heavens? and there is none upon earth I desire beside thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My flesh and my heart faileth:</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="73.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God is the rock of my heart and my portion for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; Psalms 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For behold, they that are far from thee shall perish; thou destroyest every one that goeth a whoring from thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, it is good for me to draw near to</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="73.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God: I have put my trust in the Lord Jehovah, that I may declare all thy works.</span></span>
