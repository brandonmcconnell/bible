<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will judge thy people with righteousness, and thine afflicted with judgment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountains shall bring peace to the people, and the hills, by righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will do justice to the afflicted of the people; he will save the children of the needy, and will break in pieces the oppressor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall fear thee as long as sun and moon endure, from generation to generation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He shall come down like rain on the mown grass, as showers that water the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">In his days shall the righteous flourish, and abundance of peace till the moon be no more.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">The dwellers in the desert shall bow before him, and his enemies shall lick the dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall render presents; the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer tribute:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yea, all kings shall bow down before him; all nations shall serve him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he will deliver the needy who crieth, and the afflicted, who hath no helper;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will have compassion on the poor and needy, and will save the souls of the needy:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will redeem their souls from oppression and violence, and precious shall their blood be in his sight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he shall live; and to him shall be given of the gold of Sheba; and prayer shall be made for him continually: all the day shall he be blessed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">There shall be abundance of corn in the earth, upon the top of the mountains; the fruit thereof shall shake like Lebanon; and they of the city shall bloom like the herb of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">His name shall endure for ever; his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and &#91;men&#93; shall bless themselves in him; all nations shall call him blessed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be Jehovah Elohim, the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="72.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God of Israel, who alone doeth wondrous things!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And blessed be his glorious name for ever! and let the whole earth be filled with his glory! Amen, and Amen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> <span className="pl pl-1">The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.</span></span>
