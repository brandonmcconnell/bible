<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou art fairer than the sons of men; grace is poured into thy lips: therefore</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="45.2 Elohim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God hath blessed thee for ever.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; Psalms 93:2; John 1:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thy throne, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="45.6 Elohim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, is for ever and ever; a sceptre of uprightness is the sceptre of thy kingdom:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; Psalms 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated wickedness; therefore</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="45.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, thy</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="45.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy companions.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Myrrh and aloes, cassia, are all thy garments; out of ivory palaces stringed instruments have made thee glad.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Kings' daughters are among thine honourable women; upon thy right hand doth stand the queen in gold of Ophir.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the daughter of Tyre with a gift, the rich ones among the people, shall court thy favour.</span><br /></span>
