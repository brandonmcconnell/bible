<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 143:1–3; Lamentations 3:55–56"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="64.1 Elohim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, my voice in my plaint; preserve my life from fear of the enemy:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 143:9; Psalms 59:2; Psalms 109:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hide me from the secret counsel of evil-doers, from the tumultuous crowd of the workers of iniquity,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:7; James 3:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who have sharpened their tongue like a sword, &#91;and&#93; have aimed their arrow, a bitter word;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:19; Psalms 11:2; 1 Peter 2:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They encourage themselves in an evil matter, they concert to hide snares; they say, Who will see them?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9–10; Matthew 26:59; Psalms 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They devise iniquities: We have it ready, the plan is diligently sought out. And each one's inward &#91;thought&#93; and heart is deep.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Proverbs 6:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="64.7 Elohim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God will shoot an arrow at them: suddenly are they wounded;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13; Psalms 140:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">By their own tongue they are made to fall over one another: all that see them shall flee away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:10; Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 40:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And all men shall fear, and shall declare</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="64.9 Elohim">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God's doing; and they shall wisely consider his work.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 32:11–1; Psalms 40:3; 1 Corinthians 1:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">The righteous shall rejoice in Jehovah, and trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.</span></span>
