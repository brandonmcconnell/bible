<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:2; Psalms 71:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">In thee, Jehovah, do I trust; let me never be ashamed: deliver me in thy righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 71:2–3; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 86:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Incline thine ear to me, deliver me speedily; be a strong rock to me, a house of defence to save me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; Psalms 79:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For thou art my rock and my fortress; and, for thy name's sake, thou wilt lead me and guide me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:15; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 19:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Draw me out of the net that they have hidden for me; for thou art my strength.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Into thy hand I commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, Jehovah, &#91;thou&#93;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="31.5 El">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God of truth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 2:8; Psalms 24:4; Jeremiah 10:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have hated them that observe lying vanities; and as for me, I have confided in Jehovah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 119:153"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will be glad and rejoice in thy loving-kindness, for thou hast seen mine affliction; thou hast known the troubles of my soul,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; Psalms 18:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">And hast not shut me up into the hand of the enemy: thou hast set my feet in a large place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 73:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be gracious unto me, Jehovah, for I am in trouble: mine eye wasteth away with vexation, my soul and my belly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 88:15; Psalms 32:3–4; Psalms 38:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my life is spent with sorrow, and my years with sighing; my strength faileth through mine iniquity, and my bones are wasted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">More than to all mine oppressors, I am become exceedingly a reproach, even to my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintance: they that see me without flee from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; Psalms 2:9; Romans 9:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am forgotten in &#91;their&#93; heart as a dead man; I am become like a broken vessel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Matthew 27:1; Lamentations 2:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have heard the slander of many — terror on every side — when they take counsel together against me: they plot to take away my life.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 43:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I confided in thee, Jehovah; I said, thou art my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="31.14 Elohim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:1–8; John 17:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">My times are in thy hand: deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from my persecutors.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 6:4; Daniel 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make thy face to shine upon thy servant; save me in thy loving-kindness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 34:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Jehovah, let me not be ashamed; for I have called upon thee: let the wicked be ashamed, let them be silent in Sheol.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Psalms 94:4; 1 Samuel 2:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the lying lips become dumb, which speak insolently against the righteous in pride and contempt.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">&#91;Oh&#93; how great is thy goodness, which thou hast laid up for them that fear thee, &#91;which&#93; thou hast wrought for them that trust in thee, before the sons of men!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:5; Psalms 32:7; Psalms 91:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou keepest them concealed in the secret of thy presence from the conspiracies of man; thou hidest them in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:7–13; Psalms 17:7; 1 Peter 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be Jehovah; for he hath shewn me wondrously his loving-kindness in a strong city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 116:11; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 88:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, I said in my haste, I am cut off from before thine eyes; nevertheless thou heardest the voice of my supplications when I cried unto thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Psalms 94:2; Psalms 145:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Love Jehovah, all ye his saints. Jehovah preserveth the faithful, and plentifully requiteth the proud doer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 146:5; Colossians 1:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be strong, and let your heart take courage, all ye that hope in Jehovah.</span></span>
