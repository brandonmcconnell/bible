<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine indignation; no peace in my bones, because of my sin.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">For mine iniquities are gone over my head: as a heavy burden they are too heavy for me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">My wounds stink, they are corrupt, because of my foolishness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am depressed; I am bowed down beyond measure; I go mourning all the day.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my loins are full of burning, and there is no soundness in my flesh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am faint and broken beyond measure; I roar by reason of the agitation of my heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, all my desire is before thee, and my sighing is not hid from thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart throbbeth, my strength hath left me; and the light of mine eyes, it also is no more with me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> <span className="pl pl-1">My lovers and mine associates stand aloof from my stroke; and my kinsmen stand afar off.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they that seek after my life lay snares &#91;for me&#93;; and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and meditate deceits all the day long.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I, as a deaf &#91;man&#93;, hear not; and am as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yea, I am as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For in thee, Jehovah, do I hope: thou wilt answer, O Lord my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="38.15 Elohim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I said, Let them not rejoice over me! When my foot slipped, they magnified &#91;themselves&#93; against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I am ready to halt, and my pain is continually before me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will declare mine iniquity, I am grieved for my sin.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But mine enemies are lively, they are strong; and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they that render evil for good are adversaries unto me; because I pursue what is good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Forsake me not, Jehovah; O my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="38.21 Elohim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, be not far from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation.</span></span>
