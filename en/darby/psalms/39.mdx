<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was dumb with silence, I held my peace from good; and my sorrow was stirred.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart burned within me; the fire was kindled in my musing: I spoke with my tongue,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make me to know, Jehovah, mine end, and the measure of my days, what it is: I shall know how frail I am.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, thou hast made my days &#91;as&#93; hand-breadths, and my lifetime is as nothing before thee; verily, every man, &#91;even&#93; the high placed, is altogether vanity. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Verily, man walketh in a vain show; verily they are disquieted in vain; he heapeth up &#91;riches&#93;, and knoweth not who shall gather them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> <span className="pl pl-1">And now, what wait I for, Lord? my hope is in thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from all my transgressions; make me not the reproach of the foolish.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was dumb, I opened not my mouth; for thou hast done &#91;it&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remove thy stroke away from me: I am consumed by the blow of thy hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">When thou with rebukes dost correct a man for iniquity, thou makest his beauty to consume away like a moth: surely, every man is vanity. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear my prayer, Jehovah, and give ear unto my cry; be not silent at my tears: for I am a stranger with thee, a sojourner, like all my fathers.</span><br /></span>
