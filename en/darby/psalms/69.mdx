<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> <span className="pl pl-1">Save me, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="69.1 Elohim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God; for the waters are come in unto &#91;my&#93; soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing; I am come into the depths of waters, and the flood overfloweth me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am weary with my crying, my throat is parched; mine eyes fail while I wait for my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="69.3 Elohim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 35:19; Psalms 109:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head; they that would destroy me, being mine enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored that which I took not away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 38:9; Psalms 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="69.5 Elohim">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, knowest my foolishness, and my trespasses are not hidden from thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 25:3; Psalms 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let not them that wait on thee, Lord, Jehovah of hosts, be ashamed through me; let not those that seek thee be confounded through me, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="69.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; confusion hath covered my face.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother's sons;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalms 119:139"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the zeal of thy house hath devoured me, and the reproaches of them that reproach thee have fallen upon me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:13; Psalms 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I wept, my soul was fasting: that also was to my reproach; —</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I made sackcloth my garment: then I became a proverb to them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They that sit in the gate talk of me, and &#91;I am&#93; the song of the drunkards.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalms 55:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, my prayer is unto thee, Jehovah, in an acceptable time: O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="69.13 Elohim">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, in the abundance of thy loving-kindness answer me, according to the truth of thy salvation:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:7; Psalms 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me out of the mire, let me not sink; let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the depths of waters.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let not the flood of waters overflow me, neither let the deep swallow me up; and let not the pit shut its mouth upon me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 109:21; Psalms 25:16; Psalms 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Answer me, O Jehovah; for thy loving-kindness is good: according to the abundance of thy tender mercies, turn toward me;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 27:9; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 70:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">And hide not thy face from thy servant, for I am in trouble: answer me speedily.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:1; Psalms 111:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Draw nigh unto my soul, be its redeemer; ransom me because of mine enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou knowest my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonour: mine adversaries are all before thee.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalms 142:4; Mark 14:50"> <span className="pl pl-1">Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am overwhelmed: and I looked for sympathy, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yea, they gave me gall for my food, and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their table become a snare before them, and their very welfare a trap;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not, and make their loins continually to shake.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out thine indignation upon them, and let the fierceness of thine anger take hold of them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their habitation be desolate; let there be no dweller in their tents.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they persecute him whom thou hast smitten, and they talk for the sorrow of those whom thou hast wounded.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Add iniquity unto their iniquity, and let them not come into thy righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be blotted out of the book of life, and not be written with the righteous.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–16; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 20:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am afflicted and sorrowful: let thy salvation, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="69.29 Elohim">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, set me secure on high.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 34:3; Psalms 118:28–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will praise the name of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="69.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 50:13–14; Psalms 50:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it shall please Jehovah more than an ox, — a bullock with horns and cloven hoofs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:26; Psalms 34:2; John 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The meek shall see it, they shall be glad; ye that seek</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="69.32 Elohim">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, your heart shall live.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Jehovah heareth the needy, and despiseth not his prisoners.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 150:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let heavens and earth praise him; the seas, and everything that moveth therein.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalms 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="69.35 Elohim">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah; and they shall dwell there, and possess it:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 102:28; Psalms 37:29; Acts 2:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the seed of his servants shall inherit it, and they that love his name shall dwell therein.</span></span>
