<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 83:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="109.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God of my praise, be not silent:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 52:4; Proverbs 6:17; Acts 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the mouth of the wicked &#91;man&#93; and the mouth of deceit are opened against me: they have spoken against me with a lying tongue,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 35:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">And with words of hatred have they encompassed me; and they fight against me without a cause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:20; Psalms 69:12–13; Psalms 35:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my love they are mine adversaries; but I &#91;give myself unto&#93; prayer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Genesis 44:4; Psalms 38:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they have rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Matthew 5:25; Matthew 27:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Set a wicked &#91;man&#93; over him, and let &#91;the&#93; adversary stand at his right hand;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">When he shall be judged, let him go out guilty, and let his prayer become sin;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Acts 1:16–26; Matthew 27:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let his days be few, let another take his office;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 18:21; Lamentations 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let his sons be fatherless, and his wife a widow;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; Genesis 4:12–14; Isaiah 16:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let his sons be vagabonds and beg, and let them seek &#91;their bread&#93; far from their desolate places;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:5; Job 20:18; Job 18:9–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the usurer cast the net over all that he hath, and let strangers despoil his labour;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Psalms 137:8–9; James 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let there be none to extend kindness unto him, neither let there be any to favour his fatherless children;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 37:28; Job 18:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let his posterity be cut off; in the generation following let their name be blotted out:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Nehemiah 4:5; Jeremiah 18:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered with Jehovah, and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Psalms 90:8; Job 18:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be before Jehovah continually, that he may cut off the memory of them from the earth:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; James 2:13; Psalms 37:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because he remembered not to shew kindness, but persecuted the afflicted and needy man, and the broken in heart, to slay him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 14:14; Ezekiel 35:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he loved cursing; so let it come unto him. And he delighted not in blessing; and let it be far from him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:6; Numbers 5:22; Matthew 27:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he clothed himself with cursing like his vestment; so let it come into his bowels like water, and like oil into his bones;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let it be unto him as a garment with which he covereth himself, and for a girdle wherewith he is constantly girded.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 94:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let this be the reward of mine adversaries from Jehovah, and of them that speak evil against my soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But do thou for me, Jehovah, Lord, for thy name's sake; because thy loving-kindness is good, deliver me:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:1; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 102:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I am afflicted and needy, and my heart is wounded within me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am gone like a shadow when it lengtheneth; I am tossed about like the locust;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:3–4; Hebrews 12:12; Matthew 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">My knees are failing through fasting, and my flesh hath lost its fatness;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I am become a reproach unto them; &#91;when&#93; they look upon me they shake their heads.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:86; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 69:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help me, Jehovah my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="109.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God; save me according to thy loving-kindness:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 37:7; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Exodus 8:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">That they may know that this is thy hand; that thou, Jehovah, hast done it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:20; John 16:22; Psalms 109:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them curse, but bless thou; when they rise up, let them be ashamed, and let thy servant rejoice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let mine adversaries be clothed with confusion, and let them cover themselves with their shame as with a mantle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 22:22–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will greatly celebrate Jehovah with my mouth; yea, I will praise him among the multitude.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 121:5; Psalms 16:8; Psalms 140:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he standeth at the right hand of the needy, to save him from those that judge his soul.</span></span>
