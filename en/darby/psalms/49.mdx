<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear this, all ye peoples; give ear, all inhabitants of the world:</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will incline mine ear to a parable, I will open my riddle upon the harp.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wherefore should I fear in the days of adversity, &#91;when&#93; the iniquity of my supplanters encompasseth me? —</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They depend upon their wealth, and boast themselves in the abundance of their riches. ...</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">None can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="49.7 Elohim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God a ransom for him,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">(For the redemption of their soul is costly, and must be given up for ever,)</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">That he should still live perpetually, &#91;and&#93; not see corruption.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he seeth that wise men die; all alike, the fool and the brutish perish, and they leave their wealth to others.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their inward thought is, that their houses are for ever, their dwelling-places from generation to generation: they call the lands after their own names.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nevertheless, man being in honour abideth not: he is like the beasts that perish.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">This their way is their folly, yet they that come after them delight in their sayings. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like sheep are they laid in Sheol: Death feedeth on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning; and their comeliness shall be for Sheol to consume, that there be no habitation for them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="49.15 Elohim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God will redeem my soul from the power of Sheol: for he will receive me. Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be not afraid when a man becometh rich, when the glory of his house is increased:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For when he dieth, he shall carry nothing away; his glory shall not descend after him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though he blessed his soul in his lifetime, — and men will praise thee when thou doest well to thyself, —</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">It shall go to the generation of his fathers: they shall never see light.</span><br /></span>
