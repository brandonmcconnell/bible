<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 31:20; Psalms 138:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For in the day of evil he will hide me in his pavilion; in the secret of his tent will he keep me concealed: he will set me high upon a rock.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:4; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Isaiah 55:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart said for thee, Seek ye my face. Thy face, O Jehovah, will I seek.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 102:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hide not thy face from me; put not thy servant away in anger: thou hast been my help; cast me not off, neither forsake me, O</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="27.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God of my salvation.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 5:8; Psalms 86:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Teach me thy way, Jehovah, and lead me in an even path, because of mine enemies.</span><br /></span>
