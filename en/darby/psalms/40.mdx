<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 103:1–5; Psalms 34:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he hath put a new song in my mouth, praise unto our</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="40.3 Elohim">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the man that hath made Jehovah his confidence, and turneth not to the proud, and to such as turn aside to lies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou, O Jehovah my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="40.5 Elohim">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, hast multiplied thy marvellous works, and thy thoughts toward us: they cannot be reckoned up in order unto thee; would I declare and speak &#91;them&#93;, they are more than can be numbered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sacrifice and oblation thou didst not desire: ears hast thou prepared me. Burnt-offering and sin-offering hast thou not demanded;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then said I, Behold, I come, in the volume of the book it is written of me —</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">To do thy good pleasure, my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="40.8 Elohim">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, is my delight, and thy law is within my heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have published righteousness in the great congregation: behold, I have not withheld my lips, Jehovah, thou knowest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have not hidden thy righteousness within my heart; I have declared thy faithfulness and thy salvation: I have not concealed thy loving-kindness and thy truth from the great congregation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Withhold not thou, Jehovah, thy tender mercies from me; let thy loving-kindness and thy truth continually preserve me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For innumerable evils have compassed me about: mine iniquities have taken hold upon me, so that I cannot see; they are more than the hairs of my head: and my heart hath failed me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be pleased, O Jehovah, to deliver me; Jehovah, make haste to my help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be ashamed and brought to confusion together that seek after my soul to destroy it; let them be turned backward and confounded that take pleasure in mine adversity;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be desolate, because of their shame, that say unto me, Aha! Aha!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:27; Psalms 68:3; Acts 19:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let all those that seek thee be glad and rejoice in thee; let such as love thy salvation say continually, Jehovah be magnified!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am afflicted and needy: the Lord thinketh upon me.
