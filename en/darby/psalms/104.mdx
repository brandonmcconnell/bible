<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast set a bound which they may not pass over, that they turn not again to cover the earth.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">They give drink to every beast of the field; the wild asses quench their thirst.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The birds of heaven dwell by them; they give forth their voice from among the branches.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">He watereth the mountains from his upper-chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">He maketh the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man; bringing forth bread out of the earth,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And wine which gladdeneth the heart of man; making &#91;his&#93; face shine with oil; and with bread he strengtheneth man's heart.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou makest darkness, and it is night, wherein all the beasts of the forest creep forth:</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Man goeth forth unto his work, and to his labour until the evening.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">How manifold are thy works, O Jehovah! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yonder is the great and wide sea: therein are moving things innumerable, living creatures small and great.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">There go the ships; &#91;there&#93; that leviathan, which thou hast formed to play therein.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">These all look unto thee, that thou mayest give their food in its season:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">That thou givest unto them, they gather; thou openest thy hand, they are filled with good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled; thou takest away their breath, they expire and return to their dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created, and thou renewest the face of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The glory of Jehovah will endure for ever; Jehovah will rejoice in his works.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth; he toucheth the mountains, and they smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will sing unto Jehovah as long as I live; I will sing psalms to my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="104.33 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God while I have my being.</span><br /></span>
