Selah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 59:14; Psalms 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They return in the evening; they howl like a dog, and go round about the city:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalms 10:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, they belch out with their mouth; swords are in their lips: for who &#91;say they&#93; doth hear?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalms 37:13; Psalms 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But thou, Jehovah, wilt laugh at them; thou wilt have all the nations in derision.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 27:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their strength! ...I will take heed to thee; for</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="59.9 Elohim">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God is my high fortress.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Slay them not, lest my people forget; by thy power make them wander, and bring them down, O Lord, our shield.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalms 10:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">&#91;Because of&#93; the sin of their mouth, the word of their lips, let them even be taken in their pride; and because of cursing and lying which they speak.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make an end in wrath, make an end, that they may be no more; that they may know that</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="59.13 Elohim">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God ruleth in Jacob, unto the ends of the earth.
