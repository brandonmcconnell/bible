<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cause me to hear thy loving-kindness in the morning, for in thee do I confide; make me to know the way wherein I should walk, for unto thee do I lift up my soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me, O Jehovah, from mine enemies: unto thee do I flee for refuge.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="143.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0433&quot;">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God: let thy good Spirit lead me in a plain country.</span><br /></span>
