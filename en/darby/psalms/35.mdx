<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 119:154; Jeremiah 51:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Strive, O Jehovah, with them that strive with me; fight against them that fight against me:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 42:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Take hold of shield and buckler, and stand up for my help;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 27:2; Psalms 51:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And draw out the spear, and stop &#91;the way&#93; against my pursuers: say unto my soul, I am thy salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 70:2–3; Jeremiah 46:5; Psalms 35:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be put to shame and confounded that seek after my life; let them be turned backward and brought to confusion that devise my hurt:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:18; Isaiah 29:5; Isaiah 37:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be as chaff before the wind, and let the angel of Jehovah drive &#91;them&#93; away;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Psalms 73:18; Jeremiah 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their way be dark and slippery, and let the angel of Jehovah pursue them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 9:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For without cause have they hidden for me their net &#91;in&#93; a pit; without cause they have digged &#91;it&#93; for my soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:9–10; Proverbs 5:22; Luke 21:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let destruction come upon him unawares, and let his net which he hath hidden catch himself: for destruction let him fall therein.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Psalms 58:10–11; Luke 1:46–47"> <span className="pl pl-1">And my soul shall be joyful in Jehovah; it shall rejoice in his salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 51:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All my bones shall say, Jehovah, who is like unto thee, who deliverest the afflicted from one stronger than he, yea, the afflicted and the needy from him that spoileth him!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Unrighteous witnesses rise up; they lay to my charge things which I know not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 10:32; Jeremiah 18:20; Psalms 38:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">They reward me evil for good, &#91;to&#93; the bereavement of my soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 69:10–11; Job 30:25; Matthew 5:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth; I chastened my soul with fasting, and my prayer returned into mine own bosom:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; 2 Samuel 1:11–12; Genesis 24:67"> <span className="pl pl-1">I behaved myself as though &#91;he had been&#93; a friend, a brother to me; I bowed down in sadness, as one that mourneth &#91;for&#93; a mother.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Psalms 7:2; Psalms 35:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But at my halting they rejoiced, and gathered together: the slanderers gathered themselves together against me, and I knew &#91;it&#93; not; they did tear &#91;me&#93;, and ceased not:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Job 16:9; Psalms 37:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">With profane jesters for bread, they have gnashed their teeth against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:13; Psalms 22:20–21; Psalms 57:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, how long wilt thou look on? Rescue my soul from their destructions, my only one from the young lions.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:12; Psalms 22:22–25; Psalms 116:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give thee thanks in the great congregation; I will praise thee among much people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; John 15:25; Proverbs 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let not them that are wrongfully mine enemies rejoice over me; let them not wink with the eye that hate me without cause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 120:5–7; Matthew 12:19; Acts 23:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they speak not peace; and they devise deceitful words against the quiet in the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:13; Psalms 40:15; Psalms 70:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they opened their mouth wide against me; they said, Aha! aha! our eye hath seen &#91;it&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Psalms 10:1; Psalms 28:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast seen &#91;it&#93;, Jehovah: keep not silence; O Lord, be not far from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Stir up thyself, and awake for my right, for my cause, my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="35.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God and Lord!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 26:1; Psalms 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Judge me, Jehovah my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="35.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God, according to thy righteousness, and let them not rejoice over me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 124:3; 2 Samuel 20:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them not say in their heart, Aha! so would we have it. Let them not say, We have swallowed him up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 38:16; Job 19:5; Psalms 132:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be ashamed and brought to confusion together that rejoice at mine adversity; let them be clothed with shame and dishonour that magnify themselves against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40–41; Psalms 149:4; Psalms 40:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them exult and rejoice that delight in my righteousness; and let them say continually, Jehovah be magnified, who delighteth in the prosperity of his servant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:21; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And my tongue shall talk of thy righteousness, &#91;and&#93; of thy praise, all the day.</span></span>
