<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:3–5; Jeremiah 49:23–27; Amos 3:12"> The burden of the word of Jehovah, in the land of Hadrach, and &#91;on&#93; Damascus shall it rest; (for Jehovah hath an eye upon men, and upon all the tribes of Israel;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> and also &#91;on&#93; Hamath &#91;which&#93; bordereth thereon; on Tyre and Zidon, though she be very wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> And Tyre hath built herself a stronghold, and hath heaped up silver as the dust, and fine gold as the mire of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> Behold, the Lord will take possession of her, and he will smite her power in the sea; and she shall be devoured with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> Ashkelon shall see &#91;it&#93;, and fear; Gazah also, and she shall be greatly pained; Ekron also, for her expectation shall be put to shame: and the king shall perish from Gazah, and Ashkelon shall not be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> And a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> and I will take away his blood out of his mouth, and his abominations from between his teeth; but he that remaineth, he also shall belong to our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and shall be as a leader in Judah, and Ekron as a Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> And I will encamp about my house because of the army, because of those that pass by and that return; and the exactor shall not pass through them any more: for now have I seen &#91;it&#93; with mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion; shout, daughter of Jerusalem! Behold, thy King cometh to thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly and riding upon an ass, even upon a colt the foal of an ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> And I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim, and the horse from Jerusalem; and the battle-bow shall be cut off. And he shall speak peace unto the nations; and his dominion shall be from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalms 102:19–21"> As for thee also, by the blood of thy covenant, I will send forth thy prisoners out of the pit wherein is no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Turn again to the stronghold, prisoners of hope! even to-day do I declare I will render double unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> For I have bent Judah for me, I have filled the bow with Ephraim; and I will raise up thy sons, O Zion, against thy sons, O Greece, and make thee like the sword of a mighty man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; Psalms 144:5–6"> And Jehovah shall be seen over them, and his arrow shall go forth as the lightning; and the Lord Jehovah will blow the trumpet, and will march with whirlwinds of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> Jehovah of hosts will defend them; and they shall devour, and shall tread down the sling-stones; and they shall drink, &#91;and&#93; make a noise as from wine; and they shall be filled like a bowl, like the corners of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> And Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God shall save them in that day as the flock of his people; for &#91;they shall be as&#93; the stones of a crown, lifted up upon his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> For how great is his goodness, and how great is his beauty! Corn shall make the young men flourish, and new wine the maidens.</span>
