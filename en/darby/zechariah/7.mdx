<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:1; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 1:1"> And it came to pass in the fourth year of king Darius, that the word of Jehovah came unto Zechariah on the fourth &#91;day&#93; of the ninth month, &#91;even&#93; in Chislev,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Jeremiah 26:19; 1 Samuel 13:12"> when Bethel had sent Sherezer and Regem-melech, and his men, to supplicate Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 8:19; James 4:8–10; Malachi 2:7"> &#91;and&#93; to speak unto the priests that were in the house of Jehovah of hosts, and to the prophets, saying, Should I weep in the fifth month, separating myself, as I have done now so many years?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> And the word of Jehovah of hosts came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:12; Isaiah 58:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Speak unto all the people of the land, and to the priests, saying, When ye fasted and mourned in the fifth and in the seventh &#91;month&#93;, even those seventy years, did ye really fast unto me, &#91;even&#93; unto me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:20–21; 1 Corinthians 11:26–29; Hosea 8:13"> And when ye ate, and when ye drank, was it not you that were eating and drinking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–20; Jeremiah 17:26; Jeremiah 7:5"> Are not these the words that Jehovah cried by the former prophets, when Jerusalem was inhabited and at peace, and her cities round about her, when the south and the lowland were inhabited?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of Jehovah came unto Zechariah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Psalms 82:2–4; Proverbs 21:3"> Thus speaketh Jehovah of hosts, saying, Execute true judgment, and shew loving-kindness and mercies one to another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Isaiah 1:23; James 5:4"> and oppress not the widow and the fatherless, the stranger and the afflicted; and let none of you imagine evil against his brother in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Jeremiah 8:5; Hosea 4:16"> But they refused to hearken, and turned a rebellious shoulder, and made their ears heavy, that they should not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Nehemiah 9:29–30; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> And they made their heart &#91;as&#93; an adamant, that they should not hear the law, and the words that Jehovah of hosts sent by his Spirit by the hand of the former prophets: therefore was there great wrath from Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–28; Jeremiah 14:12; Isaiah 1:15"> And it came to pass, like as he called, and they would not hear, so they called, and I would not hear, saith Jehovah of hosts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 23:19"> and I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations whom they knew not, and the land was desolate after them, so that no one passed through nor returned; and they laid the pleasant land desolate.</span>
