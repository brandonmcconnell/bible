<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2–3"> Behold, I send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me; and the Lord whom ye seek will suddenly come to his temple, and the Angel of the covenant, whom ye delight in: behold, he cometh, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Isaiah 4:4; 1 Corinthians 3:13–15"> But who shall endure the day of his coming? and who shall stand when he appeareth? For he will be like a refiner's fire, and like fullers' lye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Proverbs 17:3; Zechariah 13:9"> And he shall sit &#91;as&#93; a refiner and purifier of silver; and he will purify the children of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver; and they shall offer unto Jehovah an oblation in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> Then shall the oblation of Judah and Jerusalem be pleasant unto Jehovah, as in the days of old, and as in former years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:11"> And I will come near to you to judgment; and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against the false swearers, and against those that oppress the hired servant in &#91;his&#93; wages, the widow and the fatherless, and that turn aside the stranger &#91;from his right&#93;, and fear not me, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17"> For I Jehovah change not, and ye, sons of Jacob, are not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:3; Romans 10:21; Romans 10:3"> Since the days of your fathers have ye departed from my statutes, and have not kept them. Return unto me, and I will return unto you, saith Jehovah of hosts. But ye say, Wherein shall we return?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Matthew 22:21; Mark 12:17"> Will a man rob<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God? But ye rob me. And ye say, Wherein do we rob thee? &#91;In&#93; tithes and heave-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–19; Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:14–17"> Ye are cursed with a curse; and me ye rob, &#91;even&#93; this whole nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Bring the whole tithe into the treasure-house, that there may be food in my house, and prove me now herewith, saith Jehovah of hosts, if I open not to you the windows of the heavens, and pour you out a blessing, till there be no place for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Joel 2:22; Zechariah 8:12"> And I will rebuke the devourer for your sakes, and he shall not destroy the fruits of your ground; neither shall your vine cast its fruit before the time in the field, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Isaiah 62:4; Zechariah 8:23"> And all nations shall call you blessed; for ye shall be a delightsome land, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 2:17; Isaiah 37:23; Romans 9:20"> Your words have been stout against me, saith Jehovah; but ye say, What have we been speaking against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 73:8–13"> Ye say, It is vain to serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; and what profit is it that we keep his charge, and that we walk mournfully before Jehovah of hosts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Malachi 2:17"> And now we hold the proud for happy; yea, they that work wickedness are built up; yea, they tempt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and they escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; Psalms 56:8; Malachi 4:2"> Then they that feared Jehovah spoke often one to another; and Jehovah observed &#91;it&#93;, and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared Jehovah, and that thought upon his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 7:6; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> And they shall be unto me a peculiar treasure, saith Jehovah of hosts, in the day that I prepare; and I will spare them as a man spareth his own son that serveth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 25:46; Psalms 58:10–11; Genesis 18:25"> And ye shall return and discern between the righteous and the wicked, between him that serveth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God and him that serveth him not.</span>
