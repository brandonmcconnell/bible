<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Malachi 1:6; Jeremiah 13:13"> And now, ye priests, this commandment is for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:18; Revelation 14:7; Psalms 69:22"> If ye do not hear, and if ye do not lay &#91;it&#93; to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith Jehovah of hosts, I will even send the curse among you, and I will curse your blessings: yea, I have already cursed them, because ye do not lay &#91;it&#93; to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:6; Malachi 2:9; Job 20:7"> Behold, I will rebuke your seed, and spread dung upon your faces, the dung of your feasts; and they shall take you away with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Ezekiel 38:23; Matthew 3:12"> And ye shall know that I have sent this commandment unto you, that my covenant might be with Levi, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 25:12–13; Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 37:26"> My covenant with him was of life and peace, and I gave them to him that he might fear; and he feared me, and trembled before my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 2 Timothy 2:15–16; Revelation 14:5"> The law of truth was in his mouth, and unrighteousness was not found in his lips: he walked with me in peace and uprightness, and he turned many from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Leviticus 10:11; Deuteronomy 21:5"> For the priest's lips should keep knowledge, and at his mouth they seek the law; for he is the messenger of Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; Jeremiah 18:15; Isaiah 30:11"> But ye are departed out of the way; ye have caused many to stumble at the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:30; Deuteronomy 1:17; Matthew 19:17–18"> And I also have made you contemptible and base before all the people, because ye have not kept my ways, but have respect of persons in &#91;administering&#93; the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Malachi 2:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> Have we not all one father? Hath not one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.10 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God created us? Why do we deal unfaithfully every man against his brother, by profaning the covenant of our fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> Judah hath dealt unfaithfully, and an abomination is committed in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Judah hath profaned the sanctuary of Jehovah which he loved, and hath married the daughter of a strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.11 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:29; Leviticus 20:3; Ezekiel 24:21"> Jehovah will cut off from the tents of Jacob the man that doeth this, him that calleth and him that answereth; and him that offereth an oblation unto Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–15; Jeremiah 14:12; Psalms 78:34–37"> And further ye do this: ye cover the altar of Jehovah with tears, with weeping, and with sighing, insomuch that he regardeth not the oblation any more, nor receiveth &#91;it&#93; with satisfaction at your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Isaiah 54:6"> Yet ye say, Wherefore? Because Jehovah hath been a witness between thee and the wife of thy youth, against whom thou hast dealt unfaithfully: yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachi 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> And did not one make &#91;them&#93;? and the remnant of the Spirit was his. And wherefore the one? He sought a seed of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God. Take heed then to your spirit, and let none deal unfaithfully against the wife of his youth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Matthew 5:31–32; Mark 10:2–12"> (for I hate putting away, saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of Israel;) and he covereth with violence his garment, saith Jehovah of hosts: take heed then to your spirit, that ye deal not unfaithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> Ye have wearied Jehovah with your words, and ye say, Wherein have we wearied &#91;him&#93;? In that ye say, Every one that doeth evil is good in the sight of Jehovah, and he delighteth in them; or, Where is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God of judgment?</span>
