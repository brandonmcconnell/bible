<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For he has been counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, by how much he that has built it has more honour than the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For every house is built by some one; but he who has built all things &#91;is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> And Moses indeed &#91;was&#93; faithful in all his house, as a ministering servant, for a testimony of the things to be spoken after;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> but Christ, as Son over his house, whose house are we, if indeed we hold fast the boldness and the boast of hope firm to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Wherefore, even as says the Holy Spirit, To-day if ye will hear his voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> harden not your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> where your fathers tempted &#91;me&#93;, by proving &#91;me&#93;, and saw my works forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> Wherefore I was wroth with this generation, and said, They always err in heart; and they have not known my ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> so I swore in my wrath, If they shall enter into my rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> See, brethren, lest there be in any one of you a wicked heart of unbelief, in turning away from &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> But encourage yourselves each day, as long as it is called To-day, that none of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For we are become companions of the Christ if indeed we hold the beginning of the assurance firm to the end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> in that it is said, To-day if ye will hear his voice, do not harden your hearts, as in the provocation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> (for who was it, who, having heard, provoked? but &#91;was it&#93; not all who came out of Egypt by Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> And with whom was he wroth forty years? &#91;Was it&#93; not with those who had sinned, whose carcases fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom sware he that they should not enter into his rest, but to those who had not hearkened to the word?</span>
