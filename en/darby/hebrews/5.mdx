<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every high priest taken from amongst men is established for men in things relating to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> being able to exercise forbearance towards the ignorant and erring, since he himself also is clothed with infirmity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> and, on account of this &#91;infirmity&#93;, he ought, even as for the people, so also for himself, to offer for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> And no one takes the honour to himself but &#91;as&#93; called by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, even as Aaron also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> Thus the Christ also has not glorified himself to be made a high priest; but he who had said to him, Thou art my Son, I have to-day begotten thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> Even as also in another &#91;place&#93; he says, Thou &#91;art&#93; a priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> Who in the days of his flesh, having offered up both supplications and entreaties to him who was able to save him out of death, with strong crying and tears; (and having been heard because of his piety;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> though he were Son, he learned obedience from the things which he suffered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> and having been perfected, became to all them that obey him, author of eternal salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> addressed by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God &#91;as&#93; high priest according to the order of Melchisedec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> Concerning whom we have much to say, and hard to be interpreted in speaking &#91;of it&#93;, since ye are become dull in hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have again need that &#91;one&#93; should teach you what &#91;are&#93; the elements of the beginning of the oracles of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and are become such as have need of milk, &#91;and&#93; not of solid food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> For every one that partakes of milk &#91;is&#93; unskilled in the word of righteousness, for he is a babe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> but solid food belongs to full-grown men, who, on account of habit, have their senses exercised for distinguishing both good and evil.</span>
