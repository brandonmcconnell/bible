<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> Remember your leaders who have spoken to you the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God; and considering the issue of their conversation, imitate their faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; the same yesterday, and to-day, and to the ages &#91;to come&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> Be not carried away with various and strange doctrines; for &#91;it is&#93; good that the heart be confirmed with grace, not meats; those who have walked in which have not been profited by &#91;them&#93;.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> for of those beasts whose blood is carried &#91;as sacrifices for sin&#93; into the &#91;holy of&#93; holies by the high priest, of these the bodies are burned outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> Wherefore also Jesus, that he might sanctify the people by his own blood, suffered without the gate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> therefore let us go forth to him without the camp, bearing his reproach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> for we have not here an abiding city, but we seek the coming one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21–22; Psalms 50:14; Romans 12:1"> By him therefore let us offer &#91;the&#93; sacrifice of praise continually to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.15 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, that is, &#91;the&#93; fruit of &#91;the&#93; lips confessing his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> But of doing good and communicating &#91;of your substance&#93; be not forgetful, for with such sacrifices<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.16 Elohim">d</a></sup>God is well pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Obey your leaders, and be submissive; for they watch over your souls as those that shall give account; that they may do this with joy, and not groaning, for this &#91;would be&#93; unprofitable for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Pray for us: for we persuade ourselves that we have a good conscience, in all things desirous to walk rightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> But I much more beseech &#91;you&#93; to do this, that I may the more quickly be restored to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; Psalms 23:1"> But the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.20 Elohim">e</a></sup>God of peace, who brought again from among &#91;the&#93; dead our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep, in &#91;the power of the&#93; blood of &#91;the&#93; eternal covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> perfect you in every good work to the doing of his will, doing in you what is pleasing before him through Jesus Christ; to whom &#91;be&#93; glory for the ages of ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> But I beseech you, brethren, bear the word of exhortation, for it is but in few words that I have written to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Know that our brother Timotheus is set at liberty; with whom, if he should come soon, I will see you.</span>
