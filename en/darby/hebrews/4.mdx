<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Hebrews 4:9; Proverbs 28:14"> Let us therefore fear, lest, a promise being left of entering into his rest, any one of you might seem to have failed &#91;of it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> For indeed we have had glad tidings presented to us, even as they also; but the word of the report did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in those who heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> For we enter into the rest who have believed; as he said, As I have sworn in my wrath, If they shall enter into my rest; although the works had been completed from &#91;the&#93; foundation of &#91;the&#93; world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For he has said somewhere of the seventh &#91;day&#93; thus, And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God rested on the seventh day from all his works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> and in this again, If they shall enter into my rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Seeing therefore it remains that some enter into it, and those who first received the glad tidings did not enter in on account of not hearkening to the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> again he determines a certain day, saying, in David, 'To-day,' after so long a time; (according as it has been said before), To-day, if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Jesus had brought them into rest, he would not have spoken afterwards about another day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> There remains then a sabbatism to the people of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For he that has entered into his rest, he also has rested from his works, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God did from his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Let us therefore use diligence to enter into that rest, that no one may fall after the same example of not hearkening to the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; living and operative, and sharper than any two-edged sword, and penetrating to &#91;the&#93; division of soul and spirit, both of joints and marrow, and a discerner of the thoughts and intents of &#91;the&#93; heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalms 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> And there is not a creature unapparent before him; but all things &#91;are&#93; naked and laid bare to his eyes, with whom we have to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Having therefore a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, let us hold fast the confession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we have not a high priest not able to sympathise with our infirmities, but tempted in all things in like manner, sin apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Let us approach therefore with boldness to the throne of grace, that we may receive mercy, and find grace for seasonable help.</span>
