<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Hebrews 9:10–11; Hebrews 8:2"> The first therefore also indeed had ordinances of service, and the sanctuary, a worldly one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 26:35"> For a tabernacle was set up; the first, in which &#91;were&#93; both the candlestick and the table and the exposition of the loaves, which is called Holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 40:3; Matthew 27:51"> but after the second veil a tabernacle which is called Holy of holies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 17:10; 2 Chronicles 5:10; Exodus 40:3"> having a golden censer, and the ark of the covenant, covered round in every part with gold, in which &#91;were&#93; the golden pot that had the manna, and the rod of Aaron that had sprouted, and the tables of the covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:2; Numbers 7:89; Exodus 25:17–22"> and above over it the cherubim of glory shadowing the mercy-seat; concerning which it is not now &#91;the time&#93; to speak in detail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Exodus 27:21; Luke 1:8–11"> Now these things being thus ordered, into the first tabernacle the priests enter at all times, accomplishing the services;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 16:34; Leviticus 5:18; Hebrews 10:19–20"> but into the second, the high priest only, once a year, not without blood, which he offers for himself and for the errors of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 14:6; Hebrews 3:7; Hebrews 10:19–22"> the Holy Spirit shewing this, that the way of the &#91;holy of&#93; holies has not yet been made manifest while as yet the first tabernacle has &#91;its&#93; standing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:21; Hebrews 5:1; Romans 5:14"> the which &#91;is&#93; an image for the present time, according to which both gifts and sacrifices, unable to perfect as to conscience him that worshipped, are offered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:15; Colossians 2:16; Leviticus 11:2–47"> &#91;consisting&#93; only of meats and drinks and divers washings, ordinances of flesh, imposed until &#91;the&#93; time of setting things right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 8:1–2"> But Christ being come high priest of the good things to come, by the better and more perfect tabernacle not made with hand, (that is, not of this creation,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:15"> nor by blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood, has entered in once for all into the &#91;holy of&#93; holies, having found an eternal redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 19:2–21; Leviticus 16:14–16; Acts 15:9"> For if the blood of goats and bulls, and a heifer's ashes sprinkling the defiled, sanctifies for the purity of the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 Peter 1:19; 1 John 1:7"> how much rather shall the blood of the Christ, who by the eternal Spirit offered himself spotless to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.14 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, purify your conscience from dead works to worship &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.14 Elohim">b</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Romans 8:30; Romans 8:28"> And for this reason he is mediator of a new covenant, so that, death having taken place for redemption of the transgressions under the first covenant, the called might receive the promise of the eternal inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12"> (For where &#91;there is&#93; a testament, the death of the testator must needs come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:21; John 14:27; Galatians 3:15"> For a testament &#91;is&#93; of force when men are dead, since it is in no way of force while the testator is alive.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 9:22"> Whence neither the first was inaugurated without blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 24:5–11; Numbers 19:6; Hebrews 10:4"> For every commandment having been spoken according to &#91;the&#93; law by Moses to all the people; having taken the blood of calves and goats, with water and scarlet wool and hyssop, he sprinkled both the book itself and all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 24:8; Matthew 26:28; Hebrews 13:20"> saying, This &#91;is&#93; the blood of the covenant which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.20 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has enjoined to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 8:19; Exodus 29:36"> And the tabernacle too and all the vessels of service he sprinkled in like manner with blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 6:7; Leviticus 4:35"> and almost all things are purified with blood according to the law, and without blood-shedding there is no remission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–21; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:14"> &#91;It was&#93; necessary then that the figurative representations of the things in the heavens should be purified with these; but the heavenly things themselves with sacrifices better than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:11–12; Hebrews 9:23"> For the Christ is not entered into holy places made with hand, figures of the true, but into heaven itself, now to appear before the face of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.24 Elohim">d</a></sup>God for us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 9:14"> nor in order that he should offer himself often, as the high priest enters into the holy places every year with blood not his own;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; 1 Peter 1:20; Hebrews 1:2"> since he had &#91;then&#93; been obliged often to suffer from the foundation of the world. But now once in the consummation of the ages he has been manifested for &#91;the&#93; putting away of sin by his sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> And forasmuch as it is the portion of men once to die, and after this judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Titus 2:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Romans 6:10"> thus the Christ also, having been once offered to bear the sins of many, shall appear to those that look for him the second time without sin for salvation.</span>
