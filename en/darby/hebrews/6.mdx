<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Wherefore, leaving the word of the beginning of the Christ, let us go on &#91;to what belongs&#93; to full growth, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and faith in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> of &#91;the&#93; doctrine of washings, and of imposition of hands, and of resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead, and of eternal judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> and this will we do if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God permit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> For it is impossible to renew again to repentance those once enlightened, and who have tasted of the heavenly gift, and have been made partakers of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> and have tasted the good word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, and &#91;the&#93; works of power of &#91;the&#93; age to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and have fallen away, crucifying for themselves &#91;as they do&#93; the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.6 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and making a show of &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For ground which drinks the rain which comes often upon it, and produces useful herbs for those for whose sakes also it is tilled, partakes of blessing from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.7 Elohim">e</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> but bringing forth thorns and briars, it is found worthless and nigh to a curse, whose end &#91;is&#93; to be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> But we are persuaded concerning you, beloved, better things, and connected with salvation, even if we speak thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.10 Elohim">f</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; not unrighteous to forget your work, and the love which ye have shewn to his name, having ministered to the saints, and &#91;still&#93; ministering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> But we desire earnestly that each one of you shew the same diligence to the full assurance of hope unto the end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> that ye be not sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and patience have been inheritors of the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.13 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, having promised to Abraham, since he had no greater to swear by, swore by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, Surely blessing I will bless thee, and multiplying I will multiply thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> and thus, having had long patience, he got the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> For men indeed swear by a greater, and with them the oath is a term to all dispute, as making matters sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> Wherein<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="6.17 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, willing to shew more abundantly to the heirs of the promise the unchangeableness of his purpose, intervened by an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> that by two unchangeable things, in which &#91;it was&#93; impossible that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="6.18 Elohim">i</a></sup>God should lie, we might have a strong encouragement, who have fled for refuge to lay hold on the hope set before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> which we have as anchor of the soul, both secure and firm, and entering into that within the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> where Jesus is entered as forerunner for us, become for ever a high priest according to the order of Melchisedec.</span>
