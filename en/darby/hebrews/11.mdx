<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> Now faith is &#91;the&#93; substantiating of things hoped for, &#91;the&#93; conviction of things not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Hebrews 11:4"> For in &#91;the power of&#93; this the elders have obtained testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; 2 Peter 3:5; John 1:3"> By faith we apprehend that the worlds were framed by &#91;the&#93; word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, so that that which is seen should not take its origin from things which appear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Genesis 4:3–5; 1 John 3:11–12"> By faith Abel offered to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain, by which he obtained testimony of being righteous,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God bearing testimony to his gifts, and by it, having died, he yet speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:21–24; Jude 1:14; Hebrews 11:6"> By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death; and was not found, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.5 Elohim">d</a></sup>God had translated him; for before &#91;his&#93; translation he has the testimony that he had pleased<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.5 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:17; Jeremiah 29:13–14; Matthew 6:33"> But without faith &#91;it is&#93; impossible to please &#91;him&#93;. For he that draws near to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.6 Elohim">f</a></sup>God must believe that he is, and &#91;that&#93; he is a rewarder of them who seek him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 6:13–1; 1 Peter 3:20; Ezekiel 14:14"> By faith, Noah, oracularly warned concerning things not yet seen, moved with fear, prepared an ark for the saving of his house; by which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which &#91;is&#93; according to faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Acts 7:2–4; Matthew 7:24–25"> By faith Abraham, being called, obeyed to go out into the place which he was to receive for an inheritance, and went out, not knowing where he was going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 13:18; Genesis 13:3"> By faith he sojourned as a stranger in the land of promise as a foreign country, having dwelt in tents with Isaac and Jacob, the heirs with &#91;him&#93; of the same promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Philippians 3:20; Hebrews 12:22"> for he waited for the city which has foundations, of which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="11.10 Elohim">g</a></sup>God is &#91;the&#93; artificer and constructor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Hebrews 10:23; Genesis 18:11–14"> By faith also Sarah herself received strength for &#91;the&#93; conception of seed, and &#91;that&#93; beyond a seasonable age; since she counted him faithful who promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 32:12; Genesis 15:5"> Wherefore also there have been born of one, and that of one become dead, even as the stars of heaven in multitude, and as the countless sand which &#91;is&#93; by the sea shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 23:4; Psalms 39:12; Hebrews 11:27"> All these died in faith, not having received the promises, but having seen them from afar off and embraced &#91;them&#93;, and confessed that they were strangers and sojourners on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18–7; Hebrews 11:16; Hebrews 13:14"> For they who say such things shew clearly that they seek &#91;their&#93; country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 24:6–8; Genesis 12:10; Genesis 32:9–11"> And if they had called to mind that from whence they went out, they had had opportunity to have returned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Hebrews 11:10; Philippians 3:20"> but now they seek a better, that is, a heavenly; wherefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="11.16 Elohim">h</a></sup>God is not ashamed of them, to be called their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="11.16 Elohim">i</a></sup>God; for he has prepared for them a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Genesis 22:1–12; James 2:21–24"> By faith Abraham, &#91;when&#93; tried, offered up Isaac, and he who had received to himself the promises offered up his only begotten &#91;son&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Romans 9:7; Genesis 17:19"> as to whom it had been said, In Isaac shall thy seed be called:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 4:17–21; Matthew 9:28; Ephesians 3:20"> counting that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="11.19 Elohim">j</a></sup>God &#91;was&#93; able to raise &#91;him&#93; even from among &#91;the&#93; dead, whence also he received him in a figure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 27:27–40; Genesis 28:2–3"> By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau concerning things to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:31–1; Genesis 48:5–22"> By faith Jacob &#91;when&#93; dying blessed each of the sons of Joseph, and worshipped on the top of his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32"> By faith Joseph &#91;when&#93; dying called to mind the going forth of the sons of Israel, and gave commandment concerning his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Exodus 1:16; Hebrews 13:6"> By faith Moses, being born, was hid three months by his parents, because they saw the child beautiful; and they did not fear the injunction of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 2:10–11; Acts 7:21–24"> By faith Moses, when he had become great, refused to be called son of Pharaoh's daughter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Timothy 2:3–10; Job 36:21"> choosing rather to suffer affliction along with the people of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="11.25 Elohim">k</a></sup>God than to have &#91;the&#93; temporary pleasure of sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Hebrews 11:6; 1 Peter 4:14"> esteeming the reproach of the Christ greater riches than the treasures of Egypt, for he had respect to the recompense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:1"> By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the wrath of the king; for he persevered, as seeing him who is invisible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 12:21–30; Hebrews 9:19; Hebrews 12:24"> By faith he celebrated the passover and the sprinkling of the blood, that the destroyer of the firstborn might not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–21; Psalms 106:9–11; Psalms 78:13"> By faith they passed through the Red sea as through dry land; of which the Egyptians having made trial were swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 6:3–20; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> By faith the walls of Jericho fell, having been encircled for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 2:25; Joshua 6:22–25; Matthew 1:5"> By faith Rahab the harlot did not perish along with the unbelieving, having received the spies in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:20; 1 Samuel 16:13"> And what more do I say? For the time would fail me telling of Gideon, and Barak, and Samson, and Jephthah, and David and Samuel, and of the prophets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 144:1–2; Hebrews 6:12–15; Hebrews 10:36"> who by faith overcame kingdoms, wrought righteousness, obtained promises, stopped lions' mouths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16–18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Samuel 17:51–52"> quenched &#91;the&#93; power of fire, escaped &#91;the&#93; edge of the sword, became strong out of weakness, became mighty in war, made &#91;the&#93; armies of strangers give way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 4:27–37; 1 Kings 17:22–24; Philippians 3:11"> Women received their dead again by resurrection; and others were tortured, not having accepted deliverance, that they might get a better resurrection;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:2; Genesis 39:20; Jeremiah 37:15–21"> and others underwent trial of mockings and scourgings, yea, and of bonds and imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:21; Jeremiah 26:23; 1 Kings 19:10"> They were stoned, were sawn asunder, were tempted, died by the death of the sword; they went about in sheepskins, in goatskins, destitute, afflicted, evil treated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Samuel 22:1; 1 Kings 19:9"> (of whom the world was not worthy,) wandering in deserts and mountains, and &#91;in&#93; dens and caverns of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:2"> And these all, having obtained witness through faith, did not receive the promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 6:11; Hebrews 8:6; Hebrews 10:11–14"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="11.40 Elohim">l</a></sup>God having foreseen some better thing for us, that they should not be made perfect without us.</span>
