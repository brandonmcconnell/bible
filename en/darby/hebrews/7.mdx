<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:18–20; Psalms 57:2; Hebrews 6:20"> For this Melchisedec, King of Salem, priest of the most high<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, who met Abraham returning from smiting the kings, and blessed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 18:21; Psalms 85:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:17"> to whom Abraham gave also the tenth portion of all; first being interpreted King of righteousness, and then also King of Salem, which is King of peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:23–28; Exodus 6:20–27"> without father, without mother, without genealogy; having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but assimilated to the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, abides a priest continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; Acts 2:29; Acts 7:8–9"> Now consider how great this &#91;personage&#93; was, to whom &#91;even&#93; the patriarch Abraham gave a tenth out of the spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> And they indeed from among the sons of Levi, who receive the priesthood, have commandment to take tithes from the people according to the law, that is from their brethren, though these are come out of the loins of Abraham:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Hebrews 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> but he who has no genealogy from them has tithed Abraham, and blessed him who had the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> But beyond all gainsaying, the inferior is blessed by the better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20; Hebrews 5:6; Revelation 1:18"> And here dying men receive tithes; but there &#91;one&#93; of whom the witness is that he lives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Genesis 14:20; Hebrews 7:4"> and, so to speak, through Abraham, Levi also, who received tithes, has been made to pay tithes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Hebrews 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> For he was yet in the loins of his father when Melchisedec met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 8:7; Galatians 2:21; Galatians 4:9"> If indeed then perfection were by the Levitical priesthood, for the people had their law given to them in connexion with it, what need &#91;was there&#93; still that a different priest should arise according to the order of Melchisedec, and not be named after the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> For, the priesthood being changed, there takes place of necessity a change of law also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> For he, of whom these things are said, belongs to a different tribe, of which no one has &#91;ever&#93; been attached to the service of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; Luke 3:33"> For it is clear that our Lord has sprung out of Juda, as to which tribe Moses spake nothing as to priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17–21; Hebrews 7:3"> And it is yet more abundantly evident, since a different priest arises according to the similitude of Melchisedec,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Colossians 2:20; Hebrews 7:28"> who has been constituted not according to law of fleshly commandment, but according to power of indissoluble life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> For it is borne witness, Thou art a priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:3; Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:9"> For there is a setting aside of the commandment going before for its weakness and unprofitableness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 9:9"> (for the law perfected nothing,) and the introduction of a better hope by which we draw nigh to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.19 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:6; Psalms 110:4"> And by how much &#91;it was&#93; not without the swearing of an oath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 6:16–18"> (for they are become priests without the swearing of an oath, but he with the swearing of an oath, by him who said, as to him, The Lord has sworn, and will not repent &#91;of it&#93;, Thou &#91;art&#93; priest for ever &#91;according to the order of Melchisedec&#93;;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:25; Genesis 44:32; Hebrews 8:6–12"> by so much Jesus became surety of a better covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; Hebrews 7:8"> And they have been many priests, on account of being hindered from continuing by death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 12:34; Hebrews 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> but he, because of his continuing for ever, has the priesthood unchangeable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Whence also he is able to save completely those who approach by him to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.25 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, always living to intercede for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:14–15; 1 Peter 2:22"> For such a high priest became us, holy, harmless, undefiled, separated from sinners, and become higher than the heavens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:14"> who has not day by day need, as the high priests, first to offer up sacrifices for his own sins, then &#91;for&#93; those of the people; for this he did once for all &#91;in&#93; having offered up himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 7:21; Hebrews 1:2"> For the law constitutes men high priests, having infirmity; but the word of the swearing of the oath which &#91;is&#93; after the law, a Son perfected for ever.</span>
