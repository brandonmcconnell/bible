<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> For this reason we should give heed more abundantly to the things &#91;we have&#93; heard, lest in any way we should slip away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the word which was spoken by angels was firm, and every transgression and disobedience received just retribution,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> how shall we escape if we have been negligent of so great salvation, which, having had its commencement in being spoken &#91;of&#93; by the Lord, has been confirmed to us by those who have heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God bearing, besides, witness with &#91;them&#93; to &#91;it&#93;, both by signs and wonders, and various acts of power, and distributions of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, according to his will?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For he has not subjected to angels the habitable world which is to come, of which we speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> but one has testified somewhere, saying, What is man, that thou rememberest him, or son of man that thou visitest him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> Thou hast made him some little inferior to the angels; thou hast crowned him with glory and honour, &#91;and hast set him over the works of thy hands;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> thou hast subjected all things under his feet. For in subjecting all things to him, he has left nothing unsubject to him. But now we see not yet all things subjected to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> but we see Jesus, who &#91;was&#93; made some little inferior to angels on account of the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honour; so that by the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God he should taste death for every thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it became him, for whom &#91;are&#93; all things, and by whom &#91;are&#93; all things, in bringing many sons to glory, to make perfect the leader of their salvation through sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both he that sanctifies and those sanctified &#91;are&#93; all of one; for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> saying, I will declare thy name to my brethren; in &#91;the&#93; midst of &#91;the&#93; assembly will I sing thy praises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again, I will trust in him. And again, Behold, I and the children which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has given me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Since therefore the children partake of blood and flesh, he also, in like manner, took part in the same, that through death he might annul him who has the might of death, that is, the devil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> and might set free all those who through fear of death through the whole of their life were subject to bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For he does not indeed take hold of angels &#91;by the hand&#93;, but he takes hold of the seed of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> Wherefore it behoved him in all things to be made like to &#91;his&#93; brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest in things relating to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, to make propitiation for the sins of the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> for, in that himself has suffered, being tempted, he is able to help those that are being tempted.</span>
