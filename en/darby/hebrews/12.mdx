<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Philippians 2:16"> Let us also therefore, having so great a cloud of witnesses surrounding us, laying aside every weight, and sin which so easily entangles us, run with endurance the race that lies before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:23–24"> looking stedfastly on Jesus the leader and completer of faith: who, in view of the joy lying before him, endured &#91;the&#93; cross, having despised &#91;the&#93; shame, and is set down at the right hand of the throne of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> For consider well him who endured so great contradiction from sinners against himself, that ye be not weary, fainting in your minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> Ye have not yet resisted unto blood, wrestling against sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> And ye have quite forgotten the exhortation which speaks to you as to sons: My son, despise not &#91;the&#93; chastening of &#91;the&#93; Lord, nor faint &#91;when&#93; reproved by him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; Psalms 119:75"> for whom &#91;the&#93; Lord loves he chastens, and scourges every son whom he receives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> Ye endure for chastening,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God conducts himself towards you as towards sons; for who is the son that the father chastens not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; Psalms 73:1"> But if ye are without chastening, of which all have been made partakers, then are ye bastards, and not sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Moreover we have had the fathers of our flesh as chasteners, and we reverenced &#91;them&#93;; shall we not much rather be in subjection to the Father of spirits, and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> For they indeed chastened for a few days, as seemed good to them; but he for profit, in order to the partaking of his holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> But no chastening at the time seems to be &#91;matter&#93; of joy, but of grief; but afterwards yields &#91;the&#93; peaceful fruit of righteousness to those exercised by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Wherefore lift up the hands that hang down, and the failing knees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; Galatians 6:1"> and make straight paths for your feet, that that which is lame be not turned aside; but that rather it may be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> Pursue peace with all, and holiness, without which no one shall see the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> watching lest &#91;there be&#93; any one who lacks the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.15 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; lest any root of bitterness springing up trouble &#91;you&#93;, and many be defiled by it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> lest &#91;there be&#93; any fornicator, or profane person, as Esau, who for one meal sold his birthright;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> for ye know that also afterwards, desiring to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, (for he found no place for repentance) although he sought it earnestly with tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> For ye have not come to &#91;the mount&#93; that might be touched and was all on fire, and to obscurity, and darkness, and tempest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> and trumpet's sound, and voice of words; which they that heard, excusing themselves, declined &#91;the&#93; word being addressed to them any more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> (for they were not able to bear what was enjoined: And if a beast should touch the mountain, it shall be stoned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> and, so fearful was the sight, Moses said, I am exceedingly afraid and full of trembling;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> but ye have come to mount Zion; and to &#91;the&#93; city of &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.22 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, heavenly Jerusalem; and to myriads of angels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> the universal gathering; and to &#91;the&#93; assembly of the firstborn &#91;who are&#93; registered in heaven; and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="12.23 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, judge of all; and to &#91;the&#93; spirits of just &#91;men&#93; made perfect;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> and to Jesus, mediator of a new covenant; and to &#91;the&#93; blood of sprinkling, speaking better than Abel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> See that ye refuse not him that speaks. For if those did not escape who had refused him who uttered the oracles on earth, much more we who turn away from him &#91;who does so&#93; from heaven:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> whose voice then shook the earth; but now he has promised, saying, Yet once will I shake not only the earth, but also the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> But this Yet once, signifies the removing of what is shaken, as being made, that what is not shaken may remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> Wherefore let us, receiving a kingdom not to be shaken, have grace, by which let us serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="12.28 Elohim">f</a></sup>God acceptably with reverence and fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> For also our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="12.29 Elohim">g</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; a consuming fire.</span>
