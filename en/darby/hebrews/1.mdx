<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God having spoken in many parts and in many ways formerly to the fathers in the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> at the end of these days has spoken to us in &#91;the person of the&#93; Son, whom he has established heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> who being &#91;the&#93; effulgence of his glory and &#91;the&#93; expression of his substance, and upholding all things by the word of his power, having made &#91;by himself&#93; the purification of sins, set himself down on the right hand of the greatness on high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> taking a place by so much better than the angels, as he inherits a name more excellent than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For to which of the angels said he ever, Thou art my Son: this day have I begotten thee? and again, I will be to him for father, and he shall be to me for son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> and again, when he brings in the firstborn into the habitable world, he says, And let all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's angels worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> And as to the angels he says, Who makes his angels spirits and his ministers a flame of fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> but as to the Son, Thy throne, O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, &#91;is&#93; to the age of the age, and a sceptre of uprightness &#91;is&#93; the sceptre of thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> Thou hast loved righteousness and hast hated lawlessness; therefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, has anointed thee with oil of gladness above thy companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> And, Thou in the beginning, Lord, hast founded the earth, and works of thy hands are the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> They shall perish, but thou continuest still; and they all shall grow old as a garment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> and as a covering shalt thou roll them up, and they shall be changed; but thou art the Same, and thy years shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Mark 12:36; Matthew 22:44"> But as to which of the angels said he ever, Sit at my right hand until I put thine enemies &#91;as&#93; footstool of thy feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Psalms 103:20–21; Psalms 91:11–12"> Are they not all ministering spirits, sent out for service on account of those who shall inherit salvation?</span>
