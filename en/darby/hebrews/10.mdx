<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the law, having a shadow of the coming good things, not the image itself of the things, can never, by the same sacrifices which they offer continually yearly, perfect those who approach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:12"> Since, would they not indeed have ceased being offered, on account of the worshippers once purged having no longer any conscience of sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But in these &#91;there is&#93; a calling to mind of sins yearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> For blood of bulls and goats &#91;is&#93; incapable of taking away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> Wherefore coming into the world he says, Sacrifice and offering thou willedst not; but thou hast prepared me a body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalms 147:11"> Thou tookest no pleasure in burnt-offerings and sacrifices for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> Then I said, Lo, I come (in &#91;the&#93; roll of the book it is written of me) to do, O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, thy will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> Above, saying Sacrifices and offerings and burnt-offerings and sacrifices for sin thou willedst not, neither tookest pleasure in (which are offered according to the law);</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> then he said, Lo, I come to do thy will. He takes away the first that he may establish the second;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> by which will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> And every priest stands daily ministering, and offering often the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> But he, having offered one sacrifice for sins, sat down in perpetuity at &#91;the&#93; right hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.12 Elohim">b</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalms 110:1; Luke 20:43"> waiting from henceforth until his enemies be set &#91;for the&#93; footstool of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For by one offering he has perfected in perpetuity the sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> And the Holy Spirit also bears us witness &#91;of it&#93;; for after what was said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> This &#91;is&#93; the covenant which I will establish towards them after those days, saith &#91;the&#93; Lord: Giving my laws into their hearts, I will write them also in their understandings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> and their sins and their lawlessnesses I will never remember any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> But where there &#91;is&#93; remission of these, &#91;there is&#93; no longer a sacrifice for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Having therefore, brethren, boldness for entering into the &#91;holy of&#93; holies by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> the new and living way which he has dedicated for us through the veil, that is, his flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> and &#91;having&#93; a great priest over the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.21 Elohim">c</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let us approach with a true heart, in full assurance of faith, sprinkled as to our hearts from a wicked conscience, and washed as to our body with pure water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Let us hold fast the confession of the hope unwavering, (for he &#91;is&#93; faithful who has promised;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> and let us consider one another for provoking to love and good works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the custom &#91;is&#93; with some; but encouraging &#91;one another&#93;, and by so much the more as ye see the day drawing near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For where we sin wilfully after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains any sacrifice for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but a certain fearful expectation of judgment, and heat of fire about to devour the adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> Any one that has disregarded Moses' law dies without mercy on &#91;the testimony of&#93; two or three witnesses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> of how much worse punishment, think ye, shall he be judged worthy who has trodden under foot the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.29 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and esteemed the blood of the covenant, whereby he has been sanctified, common, and has insulted the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know him that said, To me &#91;belongs&#93; vengeance; I will recompense, saith the Lord: and again, The Lord shall judge his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> &#91;It is&#93; a fearful thing falling into &#91;the&#93; hands of &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.31 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> But call to mind the earlier days in which, having been enlightened, ye endured much conflict of sufferings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> on the one hand, when ye were made a spectacle both in reproaches and afflictions; and on the other, when ye became partakers with those who were passing through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> For ye both sympathised with prisoners and accepted with joy the plunder of your goods, knowing that ye have for yourselves a better substance, and an abiding one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> Cast not away therefore your confidence, which has great recompense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> For ye have need of endurance in order that, having done the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.36 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, ye may receive the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> For yet a very little while he that comes will come, and will not delay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> But the just shall live by faith; and, if he draw back, my soul does not take pleasure in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not drawers back to perdition, but of faith to saving &#91;the&#93; soul.</span>
