<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> Now a summary of the things of which we are speaking &#91;is&#93;, We have such a one high priest who has sat down on &#91;the&#93; right hand of the throne of the greatness in the heavens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> minister of the holy places and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord has pitched, &#91;and&#93; not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For every high priest is constituted for the offering both of gifts and sacrifices; whence it is needful that this one also should have something which he may offer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> If then indeed he were upon earth, he would not even be a priest, there being those who offer the gifts according to the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> (who serve the representation and shadow of heavenly things, according as Moses was oracularly told &#91;when&#93; about to make the tabernacle; for See, saith He, that thou make all things according to the pattern which has been shewn to thee in the mountain.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> But now he has got a more excellent ministry, by so much as he is mediator of a better covenant, which is established on the footing of better promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> For if that first was faultless, place had not been sought for a second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> For finding fault, he says to them, Behold, days come, saith the Lord, and I will consummate a new covenant as regards the house of Israel, and as regards the house of Juda;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> not according to the covenant which I made to their fathers in &#91;the&#93; day of my taking their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt; because they did not continue in my covenant, and I did not regard them, saith &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> Because this &#91;is&#93; the covenant that I will covenant to the house of Israel after those days, saith the Lord: Giving my laws into their mind, I will write them also upon their hearts; and I will be to them for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.10 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and they shall be to me for people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> And they shall not teach each his fellow-citizen, and each his brother, saying, Know the Lord; because all shall know me in themselves, from &#91;the&#93; little one &#91;among them&#93; unto &#91;the&#93; great among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> Because I will be merciful to their unrighteousnesses, and their sins and their lawlessnesses I will never remember any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 7:11–12"> In that he says New, he has made the first old; but that which grows old and aged &#91;is&#93; near disappearing.</span>
