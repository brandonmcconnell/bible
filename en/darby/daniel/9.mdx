<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 11:1; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 5:31–1"> In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, who was made king over the realm of the Chaldeans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> in the first year of his reign, I Daniel understood by the books that the number of the years, whereof the word of Jehovah came to Jeremiah the prophet, for the accomplishment of the desolations of Jerusalem, was seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:10–13; James 5:16–18"> And I set my face unto the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, to seek by prayer and supplications, with fasting, and sackcloth, and ashes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Exodus 20:6; Deuteronomy 5:10"> and I prayed unto Jehovah my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and made my confession, and said, Alas Lord! the great and terrible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.4 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God, keeping covenant and loving-kindness with them that love him, and that keep his commandments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:15"> we have sinned, and have committed iniquity, and have done wickedly, and have rebelled, even turning aside from thy commandments and from thine ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Luke 20:10–12"> And we have not hearkened unto thy servants the prophets, who spoke in thy name to our kings, our princes, and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:15; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> Thine, O Lord, is the righteousness, but unto us confusion of face, as at this day, to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and unto all Israel, that are near, and that are far off, in all the countries whither thou hast driven them, because of their unfaithfulness in which they have been unfaithful against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 9:6–7; Lamentations 1:7–8; Lamentations 1:18"> O Lord, unto us is confusion of face, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, because we have sinned against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–8; Daniel 9:7; Daniel 9:5"> With the Lord our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God are mercies and pardons, for we have rebelled against him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:6; 2 Kings 18:12; Nehemiah 9:13–17"> and we have not hearkened unto the voice of Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, to walk in his laws, which he set before us through his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5–10; Isaiah 1:4–6; Deuteronomy 28:15–68"> And all Israel have transgressed thy law, even turning aside so as not to listen unto thy voice. And the curse hath been poured out upon us, and the oath that is written in the law of Moses the servant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God: for we have sinned against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:9; Lamentations 1:12; Job 12:17"> And he hath performed his words, which he spoke against us, and against our judges that judged us, by bringing upon us a great evil; so that there hath not been done under the whole heaven as hath been done upon Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Isaiah 9:13; Daniel 9:11"> As it is written in the law of Moses, all this evil is come upon us; yet we besought not Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, that we might turn from our iniquities, and understand thy truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:7; Nehemiah 9:33; Psalms 51:14"> And Jehovah hath watched over the evil, and brought it upon us; for Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="9.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God is righteous in all his works which he hath done; and we have not hearkened unto his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 32:11; Nehemiah 1:10"> — And now, O Lord our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="9.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, who broughtest thy people forth out of the land of Egypt with a strong hand, and hast made thee a name, as it is this day, — we have sinned, we have done wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Joel 3:17; Psalms 87:1–3"> Lord, according to all thy righteousnesses, I beseech thee, let thine anger and thy fury be turned away from thy city Jerusalem, thy holy mountain; for because of our sins, and because of the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and thy people &#91;are become&#93; a reproach to all round about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Lamentations 5:18; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> And now, our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="9.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, hearken to the prayer of thy servant, and to his supplications, and cause thy face to shine upon thy sanctuary that is desolate, for the Lord's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 37:17; Jeremiah 36:7; 2 Kings 19:16"> Incline thine ear, O my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="9.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, and hear; open thine eyes and behold our desolations, and the city that is called by thy name: for we do not present our supplications before thee because of our righteousnesses, but because of thy manifold mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 9:18; Ephesians 1:12; Amos 7:2"> Lord, hear! Lord, forgive! Lord, hearken and do! defer not, for thine own sake, O my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="9.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God! for thy city and thy people are called by thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Daniel 9:3–4; Romans 3:23"> And whilst I was speaking, and praying, and confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and presenting my supplication before Jehovah my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="9.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God for the holy mountain of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="9.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Daniel 8:16; 1 Kings 18:36"> whilst I was yet speaking in prayer, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning, flying swiftly, touched me about the time of the evening oblation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:24–27; Daniel 10:21; Daniel 8:16"> And he informed &#91;me&#93;, and talked with me, and said, Daniel, I am now come forth to make thee skilful of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 10:11–12"> At the beginning of thy supplications the word went forth, and I am come to declare &#91;it&#93;; for thou art one greatly beloved. Therefore consider the word, and have understanding in the vision:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:11–14; Romans 5:10; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> Seventy weeks are apportioned out upon thy people and upon thy holy city, to close the transgression, and to make an end of sins, and to make expiation for iniquity, and to bring in the righteousness of the ages, and to seal the vision and prophet, and to anoint the holy of holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 4:24; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 55:4"> Know therefore and understand: From the going forth of the word to restore and to build Jerusalem unto Messiah, the Prince, are seven weeks, and sixty-two weeks. The street and the moat shall be built again, even in troublous times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Nahum 1:8; Mark 13:2"> And after the sixty-two weeks shall Messiah be cut off, and shall have nothing; and the people of the prince that shall come shall destroy the city and the sanctuary; and the end thereof shall be with an overflow, and unto the end, war, — the desolations determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:15; Luke 21:20; Daniel 12:11"> And he shall confirm a covenant with the many &#91;for&#93; one week; and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease, and because of the protection of abominations &#91;there shall be&#93; a desolator, even until that the consumption and what is determined shall be poured out upon the desolate.</span>
</div>
