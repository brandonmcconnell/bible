<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:19; Jeremiah 16:20; Isaiah 46:6"> Nebuchadnezzar the king made an image of gold, whose height was sixty cubits, &#91;and&#93; its breadth six cubits; he set it up in the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:27; Proverbs 29:12; 1 Kings 12:32"> And Nebuchadnezzar the king sent to gather together the satraps, the prefects, and the governors, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the justices, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Revelation 17:13; Revelation 13:13–16"> Then the satraps, the prefects, and the governors, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the justices, and all the rulers of the provinces, were gathered together unto the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up; and they stood before the image that Nebuchadnezzar had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Daniel 6:25; Micah 6:16"> And the herald cried aloud, To you it is commanded, &#91;O&#93; peoples, nations, and languages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 3:15; Daniel 3:10; Daniel 3:7"> that at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, pipe, lute, sambuca, psaltery, bagpipe, and all kinds of music, ye fall down and worship the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king hath set up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Matthew 13:42; Jeremiah 29:22"> and whosoever doth not fall down and worship shall that same hour be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 17:8; Revelation 13:3; Revelation 13:8"> Therefore at that time when all the peoples heard the sound of the cornet, pipe, lute, sambuca, psaltery, and all kinds of music, all the peoples, the nations, and the languages fell down &#91;and&#93; worshipped the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:12–16; Daniel 6:12–13; Esther 3:8–9"> Whereupon at that time certain Chaldeans came near, and accused the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:10; Daniel 6:6; Daniel 6:21"> They spoke and said to the king Nebuchadnezzar, O king, live for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 3:4–7; Daniel 6:12; Esther 3:12–14"> Thou, O king, hast made a decree, that every man that shall hear the sound of the cornet, pipe, lute, sambuca, psaltery, and bagpipe, and all kinds of music, shall fall down and worship the golden image;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and &#91;that&#93; whosoever doth not fall down and worship, shall be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:13; Daniel 2:49; Acts 17:7"> There are certain Jews whom thou hast appointed over the administration of the province of Babylon: Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego: these men, O king, regard thee not; they serve not thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">a</a></sup>gods, nor worship the golden image that thou hast set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; Daniel 2:12; Esther 3:5–6"> Then Nebuchadnezzar in rage and fury commanded to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego. Then were these men brought before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Daniel 3:1; Isaiah 46:1"> Nebuchadnezzar spoke and said unto them, Is it of purpose, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, that ye serve not my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.14 Elohim">b</a></sup>god, nor worship the golden image that I have set up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 5:29; 2 Chronicles 32:15–17; Exodus 5:2"> Now if ye be ready at the time that ye hear the sound of the cornet, pipe, lute, sambuca, psaltery, and bagpipe, and all kinds of music, to fall down and worship the image that I have made, &#91;well&#93;: but if ye worship not, ye shall be cast that same hour into the midst of a burning fiery furnace: and who is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.15 Elohim">c</a></sup>God that shall deliver you out of my hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Daniel 3:12"> Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego answered and said to the king, O Nebuchadnezzar, we have no need to answer thee in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Luke 1:37; Hebrews 7:25"> If it be &#91;so&#93;, our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace, and he will deliver &#91;us&#93; out of thy hand, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Matthew 10:39; Revelation 2:10–11"> But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that we will not serve thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.18 Elohim">e</a></sup>gods, nor worship the golden image that thou hast set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 3:13; Leviticus 26:24; Proverbs 21:24"> Then was Nebuchadnezzar full of fury, and the form of his visage was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego. He spoke, and commanded that they should heat the furnace seven times more than it was wont to be heated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:23; Acts 16:25; Daniel 3:15"> And he commanded the most mighty men that were in his army to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, and cast them into the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then these men were bound in their hosen, their tunics, and their cloaks, and their garments, and were cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Proverbs 21:18; Proverbs 11:8"> Forasmuch as the king's commandment was rigorous, and the furnace exceeding hot, the flame of the fire slew those men that had taken up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 6:16–17; Jeremiah 38:6; Psalms 34:19"> And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, fell down bound into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:7; Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:9–10"> Then Nebuchadnezzar the king was astonied, and rose up in haste; he spoke and said unto his counsellors, Did not we cast three men bound into the midst of the fire? They answered and said to the king, True, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Acts 28:5; Mark 16:18"> He answered and said, Lo, I see four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, and they have no hurt; and the appearance of the fourth is like a son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.25 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 3:17; Isaiah 52:12; Revelation 19:5"> Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the opening of the burning fiery furnace; he spoke and said, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, ye servants of the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.26 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, come forth, and come &#91;hither&#93;. Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego came forth from the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Hebrews 11:34; Isaiah 26:11"> And the satraps, the prefects, and the governors, and the king's counsellors, being gathered together, saw these men, upon whose bodies the fire had had no power, nor was the hair of their head singed, neither were their hosen changed, nor had the smell of fire passed on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 14:7–8; Psalms 34:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12"> Nebuchadnezzar spoke and said, Blessed be the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.28 Elohim">h</a></sup>God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, who hath sent his angel, and delivered his servants who trusted in him, and who changed the king's word, and yielded their bodies, that they might not serve nor worship any<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.28 Elohim">i</a></sup>god, except their own<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.28 Elohim">j</a></sup>God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:5; Psalms 76:10; Deuteronomy 32:31"> Therefore I make a decree, that in every people, nation, and language, he who shall speak anything amiss against the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="3.29 Elohim">k</a></sup>God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego, shall be cut in pieces, and his house shall be made a dunghill: because there is no other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="3.29 Elohim">l</a></sup>God that is able to deliver after this sort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 2:49; 1 Samuel 2:30; Daniel 3:12"> Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego in the province of Babylon.</span>
