<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 18:21–22; Daniel 5:31; Esther 1:1"> It pleased Darius to set over the kingdom a hundred and twenty satraps, who should be in all the kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 2:48–49; Daniel 5:29; Esther 7:4"> and over these, three presidents — of whom Daniel was one — to whom these satraps should render account, and that the king should suffer no loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 5:14; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:38–41"> Now this Daniel surpassed the presidents and the satraps, because an excellent spirit was in him; and the king thought to appoint him over the whole realm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Philippians 2:15; 1 Peter 2:12"> Then the presidents and the satraps sought to find a pretext against Daniel with respect to the kingdom; but they could not find any pretext or fault; inasmuch as he was faithful, neither was there any error or fault found in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 24:13–16; Esther 3:8; Acts 24:20–21"> Then said these men, We shall not find any pretext against this Daniel, unless we find &#91;it&#93; against him touching the law of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Nehemiah 2:3; Daniel 6:21"> Then these presidents and satraps came in a body to the king, and said thus unto him: King Darius, live for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; Psalms 62:4; Daniel 3:27"> All the presidents of the kingdom, the prefects, and the satraps, the counsellors, and the governors have consulted together to establish a royal statute, and to make a firm decree, that whosoever shall ask a petition of any<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>god or man for thirty days, except of thee, O king, he shall be cast into the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:15; Esther 1:19; Daniel 6:12"> Now, O king, establish the decree, and sign the writing, that it be not changed, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which may not be revoked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 118:9; Psalms 62:9–10"> Therefore king Darius signed the writing and the decree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Psalms 55:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:17–18"> And when Daniel knew that the writing was signed, he went into his house; and, his windows being open in his upper chamber toward Jerusalem, he kneeled on his knees three times a day, and prayed and gave thanks before his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, as he did aforetime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:32–33; Psalms 10:9; Daniel 6:6"> But those men came in a body, and found Daniel praying and making supplication before his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:8; Daniel 3:8–12; Esther 1:19"> Then they came near, and spoke before the king concerning the king's decree: Hast thou not signed a decree, that every man that shall ask &#91;anything&#93; of any<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.12 Elohim">e</a></sup>god or man within thirty days, except of thee, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions? The king answered and said, The thing is true, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which may not be revoked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 3:12; Acts 5:29; Daniel 2:25"> Then they answered and said before the king, That Daniel, who is of the children of the captivity of Judah, regardeth not thee, O king, nor the decree that thou hast signed, but maketh his petition three times a day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:26; Matthew 27:17–24; 2 Samuel 3:28–29"> Then the king, when he heard these words, was sore distressed thereby, and set his heart on Daniel to save him; and he laboured till the going down of the sun to deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 6:12; Psalms 94:20–21; Daniel 6:8"> Then these men came in a body unto the king, and said unto the king, Know, O king, that the law of the Medes and Persians is, That no decree nor statute which the king establisheth may be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:39–40; Job 5:19; Psalms 91:14–16"> Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel, and cast &#91;him&#93; into the den of lions. The king spoke and said unto Daniel, Thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.16 Elohim">f</a></sup>God whom thou servest continually, he will save thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:53; Acts 12:4; Matthew 27:60–66"> And a stone was brought, and laid upon the mouth of the den; and the king sealed it with his own signet, and with the signet of his nobles, that the purpose might not be changed concerning Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esther 6:1; Daniel 2:1; 2 Samuel 12:16–17"> Then the king went to his palace, and passed the night fasting; neither were concubines brought before him; and his sleep fled from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Mark 16:2; Matthew 28:1"> Then the king arose with the light at break of day, and went in haste unto the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:11; Hebrews 7:25; Daniel 6:27"> And when he came near unto the den, he cried with a mournful voice unto Daniel: the king spoke and said unto Daniel, O Daniel, servant of the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.20 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, hath thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="6.20 Elohim">h</a></sup>God whom thou servest continually been able to save thee from the lions?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 6:6; Nehemiah 2:3"> Then Daniel spoke unto the king, O king, live for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Hebrews 11:33; Acts 12:11"> My<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="6.22 Elohim">i</a></sup>God hath sent his angel, and hath shut the lions' mouths, that they have not hurt me; forasmuch as before him innocence was found in me; and also before thee, O king, have I done no hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; Mark 9:23; Daniel 3:27–28"> Thereupon was the king exceeding glad, and commanded that they should take Daniel up out of the den. So Daniel was taken up out of the den, and no manner of hurt was found upon him, because he believed in his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="6.23 Elohim">j</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Esther 7:10; Deuteronomy 24:16"> And the king commanded, and they brought those men who had accused Daniel, and cast them into the den of lions, them, their children, and their wives; and the lions had the mastery of them, and broke all their bones in pieces ere they came to the bottom of the den.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Ezra 1:1–2; Esther 3:12"> Then king Darius wrote unto all peoples, nations, and languages, that dwell in all the earth: Peace be multiplied unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 4:34; Luke 1:33; Daniel 3:29"> I make a decree, That in every dominion of my kingdom men tremble and fear before the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="6.26 Elohim">k</a></sup>God of Daniel; for he is the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="6.26 Elohim">l</a></sup>God, and steadfast for ever, and his kingdom &#91;that&#93; which shall not be destroyed, and his dominion shall be even unto the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 4:2–3; Psalms 18:50; Acts 4:30"> He saveth and delivereth, and he worketh signs and wonders in the heavens and on the earth: who hath saved Daniel from the power of the lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 1:21; 2 Chronicles 36:22–2; Isaiah 44:28–1"> And this Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius, and in the reign of Cyrus the Persian.</span>
