<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 1:3; Mark 6:21–22; Isaiah 21:4–5"> Belshazzar the king made a great feast to a thousand of his nobles, and drank wine before the thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 1:2; 2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 27:16–22"> Belshazzar, while he tasted the wine, commanded to bring the golden and silver vessels that Nebuchadnezzar his father had taken out of the temple which was in Jerusalem; that the king and his nobles, his wives and his concubines, might drink in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then they brought the golden vessels that were taken out of the temple of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God which was at Jerusalem; and the king and his nobles, his wives and his concubines, drank in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Habakkuk 2:19; Psalms 135:15–18"> They drank wine, and praised the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>gods of gold and of silver, of brass, of iron, of wood, and of stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:31; Daniel 5:24–28; Job 20:5"> In the same hour came forth fingers of a man's hand, and wrote over against the candlestick upon the plaster of the wall of the king's palace: and the king saw the part of the hand that wrote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Daniel 7:28; Nahum 2:10"> Then the king's countenance was changed, and his thoughts troubled him, and the joints of his loins were loosed, and his knees smote one against another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:16; Daniel 2:48"> The king cried aloud to bring in the magicians, the Chaldeans, and the astrologers. The king spoke and said to the wise men of Babylon, Whosoever shall read this writing, and shew me the interpretation thereof, shall be clothed with purple, and have a chain of gold about his neck, and shall be the third ruler in the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:27; Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8"> Then came in all the king's wise men, but they could not read the writing, nor make known to the king the interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Daniel 2:1; Jeremiah 6:24"> Then was king Belshazzar greatly troubled, and his countenance was changed in him, and his nobles were confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 3:9; Daniel 6:6; Daniel 2:4"> — The queen, by reason of the words of the king and his nobles, came into the banquet-house. The queen spoke and said, O king, live for ever! let not thy thoughts trouble thee, neither let thy countenance be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:8–9; Daniel 2:47–48; Daniel 4:18"> There is a man in thy kingdom in whom is the spirit of the holy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>gods; and in the days of thy father, light and understanding and wisdom, like the wisdom of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>gods was found in him; and the king Nebuchadnezzar thy father, &#91;even&#93; the king thy father, made him master of the scribes, magicians, Chaldeans, &#91;and&#93; astrologers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 5:14; Daniel 6:3; Daniel 1:7"> forasmuch as an excellent spirit, and knowledge, and understanding, interpreting of dreams, and shewing of hard sentences, and solving of problems, were found in the same Daniel, whom the king named Belteshazzar. Now let Daniel be called, and he will shew the interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 6:13; Daniel 5:11; Daniel 2:25"> Then was Daniel brought in before the king. The king spoke and said unto Daniel, Art thou that Daniel, of the children of the captivity of Judah, whom the king my father brought out of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12"> And I have heard of thee, that the spirit of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>gods is in thee, and &#91;that&#93; light and understanding and excellent wisdom is found in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:7–8; Isaiah 47:12; Isaiah 29:10–12"> And now the wise men, the magicians, have been brought in before me, that they should read this writing, and make known unto me the interpretation thereof; but they could not shew the interpretation of the thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 5:7; Genesis 40:8; Daniel 5:29"> But I have heard of thee, that thou canst give interpretations, and solve problems. Now if thou canst read the writing, and make known to me the interpretation thereof, thou shalt be clothed with purple, and have a chain of gold about thy neck, and shalt be the third ruler in the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; Genesis 14:23; 2 Kings 5:26"> Then Daniel answered and said before the king, Let thy gifts be to thyself, and give thy rewards to another; yet will I read the writing to the king, and make known to him the interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:37–38; Daniel 4:17; Daniel 4:22–25"> O thou king, the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.18 Elohim">f</a></sup>God gave Nebuchadnezzar thy father the kingdom, and greatness, and glory, and majesty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 3:6; Daniel 2:12–13; Daniel 3:4"> and for the greatness that he gave him, all peoples, nations, and languages, trembled and feared before him: whom he would he slew, and whom he would he kept alive; and whom he would he exalted, and whom he would he humbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 9:17; Jeremiah 13:18; Hebrews 3:13"> But when his heart was lifted up, and his spirit hardened unto presumption, he was deposed from the throne of his kingdom, and they took his glory from him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Ezekiel 17:24; Daniel 4:35"> and he was driven from the sons of men, and his heart was made like the beasts, and his dwelling was with the wild asses; they fed him with grass like oxen, and his body was bathed with the dew of heaven; till he knew that the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.21 Elohim">g</a></sup>God ruleth over the kingdom of men, and that he appointeth over it whomsoever he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Exodus 10:3; James 4:17"> And thou, Belshazzar, his son, hast not humbled thy heart, although thou knewest all this;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:10; Daniel 4:37; Jeremiah 10:23"> but hast lifted up thyself against the Lord of the heavens; and they have brought the vessels of his house before thee, and thou and thy nobles, thy wives and thy concubines, have drunk wine in them; and thou hast praised the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="5.23 Elohim">h</a></sup>gods of silver and gold, of brass, iron, wood, and stone, which see not, nor hear, nor know; and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="5.23 Elohim">i</a></sup>God in whose hand thy breath is, and whose are all thy ways, hast thou not glorified:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:5"> then from before him was sent the part of the hand, and this writing hath been written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And this is the writing that is written: MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:11–12; Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 27:7"> This is the interpretation of the thing: MENE,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="5.26 Elohim">j</a></sup>God hath numbered thy kingdom, and finished it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:6; 1 Corinthians 3:13; Psalms 62:9"> TEKEL, Thou art weighed in the balances, and art found wanting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 13:17; Daniel 6:28; Daniel 5:31"> PERES, Thy kingdom is divided, and given to the Medes and Persians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:7"> Then Belshazzar commanded, and they clothed Daniel with purple, and &#91;put&#93; a chain of gold about his neck, and made proclamation concerning him that he should be the third ruler in the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> In that night was Belshazzar the king of the Chaldeans slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 9:1; Daniel 6:1"> And Darius the Mede received the kingdom, &#91;being&#93; about sixty-two years old.</span>
