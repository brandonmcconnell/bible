<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Proverbs 25:15; Proverbs 15:18"> A soft answer turneth away fury; but a grievous word stirreth up anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:23; Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 13:16"> The tongue of the wise useth knowledge aright; but the mouth of the foolish poureth out folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 16:17; Jeremiah 23:24"> The eyes of Jehovah are in every place, beholding the evil and the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 18:8"> Gentleness of tongue is a tree of life; but crookedness therein is a breaking of the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> A fool despiseth his father's instruction; but he that regardeth reproof becometh prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> In the house of a righteous &#91;man&#93; is much treasure; but in the revenue of a wicked &#91;man&#93; is disturbance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> The lips of the wise disperse knowledge, but not so the heart of the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Proverbs 21:27; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to Jehovah; but the prayer of the upright is his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The way of a wicked &#91;man&#93; is an abomination to Jehovah; but him that pursueth righteousness he loveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> Grievous correction is for him that forsaketh the path; he that hateth reproof shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 139:8"> Sheol and destruction are before Jehovah; how much more then the hearts of the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> A scorner loveth not one that reproveth him; he will not go unto the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> A joyful heart maketh a cheerful countenance; but by sorrow of heart the spirit is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> The heart of an intelligent &#91;man&#93; seeketh knowledge; but the mouth of the foolish feedeth on folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> All the days of the afflicted are evil; but a cheerful heart is a continual feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Psalms 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> Better is little with the fear of Jehovah than great store and disquietude therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> Better is a meal of herbs where love is, than a fatted ox and hatred therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> A furious man stirreth up contention; but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Isaiah 30:21"> The way of the sluggard is as a hedge of thorns; but the path of the upright is made plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise son maketh a glad father; but a foolish man despiseth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> Folly is joy to him that is void of sense; but a man of understanding regulateth his walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> Without counsel purposes are disappointed; but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth; and a word in its season, how good is it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> The path of life is upwards for the wise, that he may depart from Sheol beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> Jehovah plucketh up the house of the proud; but he establisheth the boundary of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> The thoughts of the evil &#91;man&#93; are an abomination to Jehovah; but pure words are pleasant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremiah 17:11"> He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; but he that hateth gifts shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The heart of a righteous &#91;man&#93; studieth to answer; but the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> Jehovah is far from the wicked; but he heareth the prayer of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> That which enlighteneth the eyes rejoiceth the heart; good tidings make the bones fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 25:12; John 15:3–4"> The ear that heareth the reproof of life shall abide among the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own soul; but he that heareth reproof getteth sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:7"> The fear of Jehovah is the discipline of wisdom, and before honour &#91;goeth&#93; humility.</span>
