<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:1; Luke 10:20; 1 Kings 1:47"> A &#91;good&#93; name is rather to be chosen than great riches; loving favour rather than silver and gold.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:12; Proverbs 14:16; Proverbs 29:1"> A prudent &#91;man&#93; seeth the evil, and hideth himself; but the simple pass on, and are punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; James 4:10; Psalms 34:9–10"> The reward of humility &#91;and&#93; the fear of Jehovah is riches, and honour, and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; 1 John 5:18; Proverbs 16:17"> Thorns &#91;and&#93; snares are in the way of the perverse: he that keepeth his soul holdeth himself far from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Train up the child according to the tenor of his way, and when he is old he will not depart from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Proverbs 22:16; Matthew 18:25"> The rich ruleth over the poor; and the borrower is servant to the lender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Job 4:8; Isaiah 30:31"> He that soweth unrighteousness shall reap iniquity, and the rod of his wrath shall have an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed, for he giveth of his bread to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> Cast out the scorner, and contention will depart, and strife and ignominy shall cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> He that loveth pureness of heart, upon whose lips is grace, the king is his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> The eyes of Jehovah preserve knowledge; but he overthroweth the words of the unfaithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> The sluggard saith, There is a lion without, I shall be killed in the streets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The mouth of strange women is a deep ditch: he with whom Jehovah is displeased shall fall therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Folly is bound in the heart of a child; the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> He that oppresseth the poor, it is to enrich him; he that giveth to the rich, &#91;bringeth&#93; only to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Incline thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply thy heart unto my knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> For it is a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee: they shall be together fitted on thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> That thy confidence may be in Jehovah, I have made &#91;them&#93; known to thee this day, even to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Have not I written to thee excellent things, in counsels and knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> that I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth; that thou mightest carry back words of truth to them that send thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Rob not the poor, because he is poor, neither oppress the afflicted in the gate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> for Jehovah will plead their cause, and despoil the soul of those that despoil them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Make no friendship with an angry man, and go not with a furious man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; Psalms 106:35"> lest thou learn his paths, and get a snare to thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Be not of them that strike hands, of them that are sureties for debts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; 2 Kings 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> if thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed from under thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10"> Remove not the ancient landmark which thy fathers have set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 11:28; Matthew 25:21; Proverbs 12:24"> Hast thou seen a man diligent in his work? He shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before the mean.</span>
