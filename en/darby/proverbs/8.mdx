<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding give forth her voice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> On the top of high places by the way, at the cross-paths she taketh her stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> Beside the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors, she crieth aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> Unto you, men, I call, and my voice is to the sons of man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> O ye simple, understand prudence; and ye foolish, understand sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Hear, for I will speak excellent things, and the opening of my lips shall be right things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For my palate shall meditate truth, and wickedness is an abomination to my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing tortuous or perverse in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> for wisdom is better than rubies, and all the things that may be desired are not equal to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> I wisdom dwell &#91;with&#93; prudence, and find the knowledge &#91;which cometh&#93; of reflection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The fear of Jehovah is to hate evil; pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth do I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am intelligence; I have strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> By me kings reign, and rulers make just decrees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> by me princes rule, and nobles, all the judges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I love those that love me; and they that seek me early shall find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Riches and honour are with me; durable wealth and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> My fruit is better than fine gold, yea, than pure gold; and my revenue than choice silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> I walk in the path of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> that I may cause those that love me to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> Jehovah possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> I was set up from eternity, from the beginning, before the earth was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> When there were no depths, I was brought forth, when there were no fountains abounding with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> Before the mountains were settled, before the hills was I brought forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> while as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the beginning of the dust of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When he prepared the heavens I was there; when he ordained the circle upon the face of the deep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> when he established the skies above, when the fountains of the deep became strong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> when he imposed on the sea his decree that the waters should not pass his commandment, when he appointed the foundations of the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> then I was by him &#91;his&#93; nursling, and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth, and my delights &#91;were&#93; with the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> And now, sons, hearken unto me, and blessed are they that keep my ways:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> hear instruction and be wise, and refuse it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For whoso findeth me findeth life, and obtaineth favour of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> but he that sinneth against me doeth violence to his own soul: all they that hate me love death.</span>
