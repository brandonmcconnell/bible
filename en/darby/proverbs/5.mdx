<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:17; Proverbs 4:20; Proverbs 2:1"> My son, attend unto my wisdom, incline thine ear to my understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23; Proverbs 15:7; Malachi 2:6–7"> that thou mayest keep reflection, and that thy lips may preserve knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 7:5"> For the lips of the strange woman drop honey, and her mouth is smoother than oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 55:21"> but her end is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a two-edged sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19"> Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26; Proverbs 5:21; Psalms 16:11"> Lest she should ponder the path of life, her ways wander, she knoweth not &#91;whither&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Proverbs 7:24; Proverbs 22:17–21"> And now, children, hearken unto me, and depart not from the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 6:13"> Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the door of her house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Hosea 4:13–14; Genesis 38:23–26"> lest thou give thine honour unto others, and thy years unto the cruel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Proverbs 6:35; Proverbs 31:3"> lest strangers be filled with thy wealth, and the fruits of thy toil &#91;come&#93; into the house of a stranger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:21; Revelation 22:15; Hebrews 13:4"> and thou mourn in thine end, when thy flesh and thy body are consumed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 15:5"> and thou say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:8; Luke 15:18; Hebrews 13:7"> and I have not hearkened unto the voice of my teachers, nor inclined mine ear to those that instructed me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Peter 2:10–18; 1 Corinthians 10:6–8"> I was well nigh in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2–5; Hebrews 13:4; Proverbs 5:18–19"> Drink waters out of thine own cistern, and running waters out of thine own well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 48:21"> Thy fountains shall be poured forth, as water-brooks in the broadways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Let them be only thine own, and not strangers' with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Malachi 2:14–15; Song of Solomon 4:15"> Let thy fountain be blessed; and have joy of the wife of thy youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:3; Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 4:5"> As a lovely hind and a graceful roe, let her breasts satisfy thee at all times: be thou ravished continually with her love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 7:5; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 23:33"> And why shouldest thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Proverbs 15:3; Job 34:21"> For the ways of man are before the eyes of Jehovah, and he pondereth all his paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:5; Psalms 9:15; Proverbs 1:18"> His own iniquities shall take the wicked, and he shall be holden with the cords of his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 36:12; Job 4:21; Proverbs 10:21"> He shall die for want of discipline; and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.</span>
