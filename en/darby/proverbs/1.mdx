<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> to know wisdom and instruction; to discern the words of understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> to receive the instruction of wisdom, righteousness and judgment, and equity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> to give prudence to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> He that is wise will hear, and will increase learning; and the intelligent will gain wise counsels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> to understand a proverb and an allegory, the words of the wise and their enigmas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The fear of Jehovah is the beginning of knowledge: fools despise wisdom and instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> Hear, my son, the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the teaching of thy mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> for they shall be a garland of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, if sinners entice thee, consent not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, Come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk secretly for the innocent without cause;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> let us swallow them up alive as Sheol, and whole, as those that go down into the pit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> we shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> cast in thy lot among us; we will all have one purse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> — my son, walk not in the way with them, keep back thy foot from their path;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> for their feet run to evil, and they make haste to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> For in vain the net is spread in the sight of anything which hath wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> And these lay wait for their own blood; they lurk secretly for their own lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> So are the paths of every one that is greedy of gain: it taketh away the life of its possessors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom crieth without; she raiseth her voice in the broadways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> she calleth in the chief &#91;place&#93; of concourse, in the entry of the gates; in the city she uttereth her words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> How long, simple ones, will ye love simpleness, and scorners take pleasure in their scorning, and the foolish hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Turn you at my reproof: behold, I will pour forth my spirit unto you, I will make known to you my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> Because I have called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no one regarded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> and ye have rejected all my counsel, and would none of my reproof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> I also will laugh in your calamity, I will mock when your fear cometh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> when your fear cometh as sudden destruction, and your calamity cometh as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish come upon you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> — then will they call upon me, but I will not answer; they will seek me early, and shall not find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> Because they hated knowledge, and did not choose the fear of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> they would none of my counsel, they despised all my reproof:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their way, and be filled with their own devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of the foolish shall cause them to perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely, and shall be at rest from fear of evil.</span>
