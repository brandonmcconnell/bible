<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 3:1; Proverbs 1:3; Proverbs 4:1"> My son, if thou receivest my words, and layest up my commandments with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 18:1; Psalms 119:111–112"> so that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom &#91;and&#93; thou apply thy heart to understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:5; Ephesians 1:17–18; Proverbs 3:6"> yea, if thou criest after discernment &#91;and&#93; liftest up thy voice to understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Proverbs 16:16; Matthew 19:29"> if thou seekest her as silver and searchest for her as for hidden treasures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Jeremiah 24:7; Jeremiah 32:40–41"> then shalt thou understand the fear of Jehovah, and find the knowledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; James 1:5; Job 32:8"> For Jehovah giveth wisdom; out of his mouth &#91;come&#93; knowledge and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 3:15–17; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 84:11"> He layeth up sound wisdom for the upright; &#91;he&#93; is a shield to them that walk in integrity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; John 10:28–29"> guarding the paths of just judgment and keeping the way of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>godly ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Psalms 143:8–10; Matthew 7:13–14"> Then shalt thou understand righteousness and judgment and equity: every good path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Colossians 3:16; Job 23:12"> When wisdom entereth into thy heart and knowledge is pleasant unto thy soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6; Ephesians 5:15; Psalms 25:21"> discretion shall keep thee, understanding shall preserve thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 13:20"> To deliver thee from the way of evil, from the man that speaketh froward things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Proverbs 21:16"> &#91;from those&#93; who leave the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Proverbs 10:23; Jeremiah 11:15"> who rejoice to do evil, &#91;and&#93; delight in the frowardness of evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 125:5; Proverbs 21:8; Philippians 2:15"> whose paths are crooked, and who are perverted in their course:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 23:27"> To deliver thee from the strange woman, from the stranger who flattereth with her words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:59–60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 3:4"> who forsaketh the guide of her youth, and forgetteth the covenant of her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 9:18; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Proverbs 5:4–14"> — for her house inclineth unto death, and her paths unto the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 81:12; Hosea 4:14"> none that go unto her return again, neither do they attain to the paths of life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 119:63"> — that thou mayest walk in the way of the good, and keep the paths of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 37:29; Psalms 37:9"> For the upright shall dwell in the land, and the perfect shall remain in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 52:5; Deuteronomy 7:22; Isaiah 3:10–11"> but the wicked shall be cut off from the land, and the unfaithful shall be plucked up out of it.</span>
