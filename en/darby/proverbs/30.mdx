<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 31:1; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> The words of Agur the son of Jakeh; the prophecy uttered by the man unto Ithiel, &#91;even&#93; unto Ithiel and Ucal:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> Truly I am more stupid than any one; and I have not a man's intelligence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Ephesians 3:18–19; Romans 11:33"> I have neither learned wisdom, nor have I the knowledge of the Holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:13; Ephesians 4:8–10; Job 26:8"> Who hath ascended up into the heavens, and descended? Who hath gathered the wind in his fists? Who hath bound the waters in a mantle? Who hath established all the ends of the earth? What is his name, and what is his son's name, if thou knowest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 18:30; James 3:17; Psalms 12:6"> Every word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.5 Eloah">a</a></sup>God is pure: he is a shield unto them that put their trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19"> Add thou not unto his words, lest he reprove thee, and thou be found a liar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:42; 2 Kings 2:9; Psalms 27:4"> Two things do I ask of thee; deny me &#91;them&#93; not before I die:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Psalms 62:9–10; Psalms 119:37"> Remove far from me vanity and lies; give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with the bread of my daily need:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 31:20; Ezekiel 16:49–50"> lest I be full and deny &#91;thee&#93;, and say, Who is Jehovah? or lest I be poor and steal, and outrage the name of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="30.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Ecclesiastes 7:21; Deuteronomy 23:15"> Speak not too much about a servant to his master, lest he curse thee, and thou be held guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 30:17; Exodus 21:17"> There is a generation that curseth their father, and doth not bless their mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 18:11; Proverbs 16:2; 2 Timothy 3:5"> there is a generation that are pure in their own eyes, yet are not washed from their filthiness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; Psalms 101:5"> there is a generation, — how lofty are their eyes, how their eyelids are lifted up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 3:7; Psalms 14:4"> — a generation whose teeth are swords, and their jaw-teeth knives, to devour the afflicted from off the earth, and the needy from &#91;among&#93; men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21; Proverbs 30:29; Proverbs 6:16"> The leech hath two daughters: Give, give. There are three &#91;things&#93; never satisfied; four which say not, It is enough:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Habakkuk 2:5; Genesis 30:1"> — Sheol, and the barren womb; the earth which is not filled with water, and the fire which saith not, It is enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:22; Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:11"> The eye that mocketh at a father, and despiseth to obey a mother, the ravens of the valley shall pick it out, and the young eagles shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 42:3; Psalms 139:6"> There are three &#91;things&#93; too wonderful for me, and four that I know not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Job 39:27; Exodus 22:16"> The way of an eagle in the heavens, the way of a serpent upon a rock, the way of a ship in the midst of the sea, and the way of a man with a maid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:6; Proverbs 7:13–23; Numbers 5:11–30"> Such is the way of an adulterous woman: she eateth, and wipeth her mouth, and saith, I have done no wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Under three &#91;things&#93; the earth is disquieted, and under four it cannot bear up:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Ecclesiastes 10:7; 1 Samuel 25:10–11"> Under a servant when he reigneth, and a churl when he is filled with meat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 29:21"> under an odious &#91;woman&#93; when she is married, and a handmaid when she is heir to her mistress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 12:7"> There are four &#91;things&#93; little upon the earth, and they are exceeding wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8"> The ants, a people not strong, yet they provide their food in the summer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Leviticus 11:5"> the rock-badgers are but a feeble folk, yet they make their house in the cliff;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Joel 1:6–7; Joel 2:7–11"> the locusts have no king, yet they go forth all of them by bands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> thou takest hold of the lizard with the hands, yet is she in kings' palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> There are three &#91;things&#93; which have a stately step, and four are comely in going:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 23:24; Judges 14:18"> The lion, mighty among beasts, which turneth not away for any;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 3:15–18; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14"> a &#91;horse&#93; girt in the loins; or the he-goat; and a king, against whom none can rise up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 26:12"> If thou hast done foolishly in lifting up thyself, or if thou hast thought evil, &#91;lay&#93; the hand upon thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 29:22; Proverbs 28:25"> For the pressing of milk bringeth forth butter, and the pressing of the nose bringeth forth blood; and the pressing of anger bringeth forth strife.</span>
