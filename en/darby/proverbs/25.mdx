<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 4:32; Micah 1:1"> These are also proverbs of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah king of Judah transcribed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> It is the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God to conceal a thing; but the glory of kings is to search out a thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> The heavens for height, and the earth for depth, and the heart of kings is unsearchable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take away the dross from the silver, and there cometh forth a vessel for the refiner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> take away the wicked from before the king, and his throne shall be established in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Put not thyself forward in the presence of the king, and stand not in the place of the great;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> for better it is that it be said unto thee, Come up hither, than that thou shouldest be put lower in the presence of the prince whom thine eyes see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Go not forth hastily to strive, lest &#91;thou know not&#93; what to do in the end thereof, when thy neighbour hath put thee to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Debate thy cause with thy neighbour, but reveal not the secret of another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> lest he that heareth &#91;it&#93; disgrace thee, and thine evil report turn not away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> &#91;As&#93; apples of gold in pictures of silver, is a word spoken in season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> An ear-ring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold, is a wise reprover upon an attentive ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As the cold of snow in the time of harvest, &#91;so&#93; is a faithful messenger to them that send him: for he refresheth the soul of his masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> Clouds and wind without rain, &#91;so&#93; is a man that boasteth himself of a false gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> By long forbearing is a ruler persuaded, and a soft tongue breaketh the bone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> Hast thou found honey? Eat so much as is sufficient for thee, lest thou be surfeited therewith, and vomit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Let thy foot be seldom in thy neighbour's house; lest he be weary of thee and hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> A maul, and a sword, and a sharp arrow, is a man that beareth false witness against his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> A broken tooth, and a tottering foot, is confidence in an unfaithful &#91;man&#93; in the day of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> &#91;As&#93; he that taketh off a garment in cold weather, &#91;and as&#93; vinegar upon nitre, so is he that singeth songs to a sad heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he be thirsty, give him water to drink:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> for thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head, and Jehovah shall reward thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> The north wind bringeth forth rain, and the angry countenance a backbiting tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a contentious woman, and a house in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> &#91;As&#93; cold waters to a thirsty soul, so is good news from a far country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> A troubled fountain, and a defiled well, is a righteous &#91;man&#93; that giveth way before the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> It is not good to eat much honey; and to search into weighty matters is &#91;itself&#93; a weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> He that hath no rule over his own spirit is &#91;as&#93; a city broken down, without walls.</span>
