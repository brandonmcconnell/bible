<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> A whip for the horse, a bridle for the ass, and a rod for the back of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest thou also be like unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He that sendeth a message by the hand of a fool cutteth off &#91;his own&#93; feet, &#91;and&#93; drinketh damage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> The legs of the lame hang loose; so is a proverb in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> As a bag of gems in a stoneheap, so is he that giveth honour to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> &#91;As&#93; a thorn goeth up into the hand of a drunkard, so is a proverb in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> A master roughly worketh every one: he both hireth the fool and hireth passers-by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As a dog turneth back to its vomit, &#91;so&#93; a fool repeateth his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> Hast thou seen a man wise in his own eyes? There is more hope of a fool than of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The sluggard saith, There is a fierce lion in the way; a lion is in the midst of the streets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> &#91;As&#93; the door turneth upon its hinges, so the sluggard upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> The sluggard burieth his hand in the dish: it wearieth him to bring it again to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> A sluggard is wiser in his own eyes than seven &#91;men&#93; that answer discreetly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> He that passing by vexeth himself with strife belonging not to him, is &#91;like&#93; one that taketh a dog by the ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As a madman who casteth firebrands, arrows, and death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> so is a man that deceiveth his neighbour, and saith, Am I not in sport?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> Where no wood is, the fire goeth out; and where there is no talebearer, the contention ceaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; 2 Samuel 20:1"> &#91;As&#93; coals for hot coals, and wood for fire, so is a contentious man to inflame strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The words of a talebearer are as dainty morsels, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> Ardent lips, and a wicked heart, are &#91;as&#93; an earthen vessel overlaid with silver dross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> He that hateth dissembleth with his lips, but he layeth up deceit within him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 28:3"> when his voice is gracious, believe him not, for there are seven abominations in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 55:21–23; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:21"> Though &#91;his&#93; hatred is covered by dissimulation, his wickedness shall be made manifest in the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> Whoso diggeth a pit shall fall therein; and he that rolleth a stone, it shall return upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:44–49; Luke 20:20–21; John 10:32–33"> A lying tongue hateth those that are injured by it, and a flattering mouth worketh ruin.</span>
