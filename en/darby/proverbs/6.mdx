<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:26; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 20:16"> My son, if thou hast become surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand for a stranger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13"> thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:10; Psalms 31:8; 2 Samuel 24:14"> Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, since thou hast come into the hand of thy friend: go, humble thyself, and be urgent with thy friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 132:4; Proverbs 6:10–11; Mark 13:35–36"> Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; Psalms 124:7; Proverbs 1:17"> deliver thyself as a gazelle from the hand &#91;of the hunter&#93;, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways and be wise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:39–12; Proverbs 30:27; Job 39:26–30"> which having no chief, overseer, or ruler,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; 1 Timothy 6:19; Proverbs 30:25"> provideth her bread in the summer, &#91;and&#93; gathereth her food in the harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Jeremiah 4:14; Ephesians 5:14"> How long, sluggard, wilt thou lie down? When wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> So shall thy poverty come as a roving plunderer, and thy penury as an armed man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> A man of Belial, a wicked person, is he that goeth about with a perverse mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> he winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet, he teacheth with his fingers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> deceits are in his heart; he deviseth mischief at all times, he soweth discords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly: in a moment shall he be broken, and without remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> These six &#91;things&#93; doth Jehovah hate, yea, seven are an abomination unto him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> haughty eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> a heart that deviseth wicked imaginations; feet that are swift in running to mischief;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> a false witness that uttereth lies, and he that soweth discords among brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My son, observe thy father's commandment, and forsake not the teaching of thy mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> bind them continually upon thy heart, tie them about thy neck:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> when thou walkest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and &#91;when&#93; thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> For the commandment is a lamp, and the teaching a light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> to keep thee from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; 2 Kings 9:30"> Lust not after her beauty in thy heart, neither let her take thee with her eyelids;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> for by means of a whorish woman &#91;a man is brought&#93; to a loaf of bread, and another's wife doth hunt for the precious soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his garments not be burned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Can one go upon hot coals, and his feet not be scorched?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> So he that goeth in to his neighbour's wife: whosoever toucheth her shall not be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> They do not despise a thief, if he steal to satisfy his soul when he is hungry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> and if he be found, he shall restore sevenfold; he shall give all the substance of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> Whoso committeth adultery with a woman is void of understanding: he that doeth it destroyeth his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> A wound and contempt shall he get; and his reproach shall not be wiped away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Solomon 8:6"> For jealousy is the rage of a man, and he will not spare in the day of vengeance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:25; Malachi 2:9; Proverbs 7:13"> he will not regard any ransom, neither will he rest content though thou multipliest &#91;thy&#93; gifts.</span>
