<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Be not thou envious of evil men, neither desire to be with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> for their heart studieth destruction, and their lips talk of mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> Through wisdom is a house built, and by understanding it is established;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> and by knowledge are the chambers filled with all precious and pleasant substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> A wise man is strong, and a man of knowledge increaseth strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For with good advice shalt thou make thy war; and in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom is too high for a fool: he will not open his mouth in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> He that deviseth to do evil shall be called a master of intrigues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The purpose of folly is sin, and the scorner is an abomination to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> &#91;If&#93; thou losest courage in the day of trouble, thy strength is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> Deliver them that are taken forth unto death, and withdraw not from them that stagger to slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> If thou sayest, Behold, we knew it not, will not he that weigheth the hearts consider it? And he that preserveth thy soul, he knoweth it; and he rendereth to man according to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> Eat honey, my son, for it is good; and a honeycomb is sweet to thy taste:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> so consider wisdom for thy soul; if thou hast found it, there shall be a result, and thine expectation shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Lay not wait, O wicked &#91;man&#93;, against the dwelling of the righteous; lay not waste his resting-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For the righteous falleth seven times, and riseth up again; but the wicked stumble into disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> Rejoice not when thine enemy falleth, and let not thy heart be glad when he stumbleth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> lest Jehovah see it, and it be evil in his sight, and he turn away his anger from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Fret not thyself because of evil-doers, &#91;and&#93; be not envious of the wicked:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> for there shall be no future to the evil &#91;man&#93;; the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My son, fear Jehovah and the king: meddle not with them that are given to change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For their calamity shall rise suddenly; and who knoweth the ruin of them both?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These things also come from the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> He that saith unto the wicked, Thou art righteous, peoples shall curse him, nations shall abhor him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> but to them that rebuke &#91;him&#93; shall be delight, and a good blessing cometh upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> He kisseth the lips who giveth a right answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Prepare thy work without, and put thy field in order, and afterwards build thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Be not a witness against thy neighbour without cause; and wouldest thou deceive with thy lips?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, I will do so to him as he hath done to me, I will render to the man according to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> I went by the field of a sluggard, and by the vineyard of a man void of understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> and lo, it was all grown over with thistles, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and its stone wall was broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Then I looked, I took it to heart; I saw, I received instruction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> — A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> So shall thy poverty come &#91;as&#93; a roving plunderer, and thy penury as an armed man.</span>
