<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, consider well who is before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> and put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Be not desirous of his dainties; for they are deceitful food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Weary not thyself to become rich; cease from thine own intelligence:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> wilt thou set thine eyes upon it, it is gone; for indeed it maketh itself wings and it flieth away as an eagle towards the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Eat thou not the food of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> For as he thinketh in his soul, so is he. Eat and drink! will he say unto thee; but his heart is not with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Thy morsel which thou hast eaten must thou vomit up, and thou wilt have wasted thy sweet words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Speak not in the ears of a foolish &#91;man&#93;, for he will despise the wisdom of thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Remove not the ancient landmark; and enter not into the fields of the fatherless:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> for their redeemer is mighty; he will plead their cause against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Apply thy heart unto instruction, and thine ears to the words of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Withhold not correction from the child; for &#91;if&#93; thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My son, if thy heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice, even mine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> and my reins shall exult, when thy lips speak right things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Let not thy heart envy sinners, but &#91;be thou&#93; in the fear of Jehovah all the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> for surely there is a result, and thine expectation shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Thou, my son, hear and be wise, and direct thy heart in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Be not among winebibbers, among riotous eaters of flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty; and drowsiness clotheth with rags.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, and despise not thy mother when she is old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; Psalms 119:72"> Buy the truth, and sell it not; wisdom, and instruction, and intelligence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> The father of a righteous &#91;man&#93; shall greatly rejoice, and he that begetteth a wise &#91;son&#93; shall have joy of him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> let thy father and thy mother have joy, and let her that bore thee rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> My son, give me thy heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a whore is a deep ditch; and a strange woman is a narrow pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> She also lieth in wait as a robber, and increaseth the treacherous among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who hath woe? Who hath sorrow? Who contentions? Who complaining? Who wounds without cause? Who redness of eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> — They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to try mixed wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> Look not upon the wine when it is red, when it sparkleth in the cup, and goeth down smoothly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> at the last it biteth like a serpent, and stingeth like an adder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Thine eyes shall behold strange women, and thy heart shall speak froward things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> and thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea, and as he that lieth down upon the top of a mast:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> — “They have smitten me, &#91;and&#93; I am not sore; they have beaten me, &#91;and&#93; I knew it not. When shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.”</span>
