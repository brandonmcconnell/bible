<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 21:6; Proverbs 13:14; Proverbs 2:10–18"> The fear of Jehovah is a fountain of life, to turn away from the snares of death.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; Proverbs 4:23; Proverbs 3:8"> A sound heart is the life of the flesh; but envy the rottenness of the bones.</span>
