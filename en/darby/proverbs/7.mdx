<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:13–15; Romans 13:12–14; Ephesians 5:11"> in the twilight, in the evening of the day, in the blackness of night and the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Isaiah 3:16–24; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> And behold, there met him a woman in the attire of a harlot, and subtle of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 9:13; Titus 2:5; Proverbs 31:10–31"> She is clamorous and unmanageable; her feet abide not in her house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:28; Proverbs 9:14; Revelation 18:3"> now without, now in the broadways, — and she lieth in wait at every corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 39:12; Numbers 25:6–8; Genesis 39:7"> And she caught him and kissed him, and with an impudent face said unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 21:27; Leviticus 7:11; Proverbs 17:1"> I have peace-offerings with me; this day have I paid my vows:</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:9; Ezekiel 27:7; 1 Kings 10:28"> I have decked my bed with tapestry coverlets of variegated linen from Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Isaiah 57:7–9; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> I have perfumed my couch with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Come, let us revel in love until the morning, let us delight ourselves with loves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:11; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:39"> For the husband is not at home, he is gone a long journey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4"> he hath taken the money-bag with him, he will come home on the day of the full moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 5:3; Proverbs 7:5; 2 Kings 4:8"> With her much enticement she beguiled him; with the smoothness of her lips she constrained him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 5:7; Proverbs 4:1; 1 John 2:1"> And now, ye sons, hearken unto me, and attend to the words of my mouth.</span>
