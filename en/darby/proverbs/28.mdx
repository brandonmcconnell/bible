<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> The wicked flee when no man pursueth; but the righteous are bold as a lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> By the transgression of a land many are the princes thereof; but by a man of understanding &#91;and&#93; of knowledge, &#91;its&#93; stability is prolonged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A poor man who oppresseth the helpless is a sweeping rain which leaveth no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> They that forsake the law praise the wicked; but such as keep the law contend with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Evil men understand not judgment; but they that seek Jehovah understand everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse, double in ways, though he be rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> Whoso observeth the law is a son that hath understanding; but he that is a companion of profligates bringeth shame to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance gathereth it for him that is gracious to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer is an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> Whoso causeth the upright to go astray in an evil way, shall himself fall into his own pit; but the perfect shall inherit good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> A rich man is wise in his own eyes; but the poor that hath understanding searcheth him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the righteous triumph, there is great glory; but when the wicked rise, men conceal themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He that covereth his transgressions shall not prosper; but whoso confesseth and forsaketh &#91;them&#93; shall obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> Happy is the man that feareth always; but he that hardeneth his heart shall fall into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> A roaring lion, and a ranging bear, is a wicked ruler over a poor people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> The prince void of intelligence is also a great oppressor: he that hateth covetousness shall prolong &#91;his&#93; days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man laden with the blood of &#91;any&#93; person, fleeth to the pit: let no man stay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> Whoso walketh in integrity shall be saved; but he that is perverted in &#91;his&#93; double ways, shall fall in one &#91;of them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread; but he that followeth the worthless shall have poverty enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A faithful man aboundeth with blessings; but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> To have respect of persons is not good; but for a piece of bread will a man transgress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> He that hath an evil eye hasteth after wealth, and knoweth not that poverty shall come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He that rebuketh a man shall afterwards find more favour than he that flattereth with the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> Whoso robbeth his father and his mother, and saith, It is no transgression, the same is the companion of a destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> He that is puffed up in soul exciteth contention; but he that relieth upon Jehovah shall be made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> He that confideth in his own heart is a fool; but whoso walketh wisely, he shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He that giveth unto the poor shall not lack; but he that withdraweth his eyes shall have many a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> When the wicked rise, men hide themselves; but when they perish, the righteous increase.</span>
