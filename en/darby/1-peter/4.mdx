<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 3:18; Colossians 3:3–5"> Christ, then, having suffered for us in &#91;the&#93; flesh, do ye also arm yourselves with the same mind; for he that has suffered in &#91;the&#93; flesh has done with sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22–24; 1 Peter 1:14; Titus 3:3–8"> no longer to live the rest of &#91;his&#93; time in &#91;the&#93; flesh to men's lusts, but to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:5"> For the time past &#91;is&#93; sufficient &#91;for us&#93; to have wrought the will of the Gentiles, walking in lasciviousness, lusts, wine-drinking, revels, drinkings, and unhallowed idolatries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; 1 Peter 2:12; Jude 1:10"> Wherein they think it strange that ye run not with &#91;them&#93; to the same sink of corruption, speaking injuriously &#91;of you&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:9; Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:1"> who shall render account to him who is ready to judge &#91;the&#93; living and &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:2; 1 Peter 3:19; Romans 8:9–11"> For to this &#91;end&#93; were the glad tidings preached to &#91;the&#93; dead also, that they might be judged, as regards men, after &#91;the&#93; flesh, but live, as regards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, after &#91;the&#93; Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:8–9; 1 Peter 1:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> But the end of all things is drawn nigh: be sober therefore, and be watchful unto prayers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Colossians 3:14; Proverbs 17:9"> but before all things having fervent love among yourselves, because love covers a multitude of sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Romans 12:13; Philippians 2:14"> hospitable one to another, without murmuring;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> each according as he has received a gift, ministering it to one another, as good stewards of &#91;the&#93; various grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Ephesians 6:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> If any one speak — as oracles of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God; if any one minister — as of strength which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.11 Elohim">e</a></sup>God supplies; that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.11 Elohim">f</a></sup>God in all things may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom is the glory and the might for the ages of ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; 1 Peter 1:6–7; 1 Peter 5:9"> Beloved, take not &#91;as&#93; strange the fire &#91;of persecution&#93; which has taken place amongst you for &#91;your&#93; trial, as if a strange thing was happening to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:17; James 1:2–3; Romans 5:3"> but as ye have share in the sufferings of Christ, rejoice, that in the revelation of his glory also ye may rejoice with exultation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; Luke 6:22; Matthew 5:11"> If ye are reproached in &#91;the&#93; name of Christ, blessed &#91;are ye&#93;; for the &#91;Spirit&#93; of glory and the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.14 Elohim">g</a></sup>God rests upon you: &#91;on their part he is blasphemed, but on your part he is glorified.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 1 Timothy 5:13"> Let none of you suffer indeed as murderer, or thief, or evildoer, or as overseer of other people's matters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Philippians 1:29; 1 Peter 4:19"> but if as a christian, let him not be ashamed, but glorify<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.16 Elohim">h</a></sup>God in this name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:29; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 2:9"> For the time of having the judgment begin from the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="4.17 Elohim">i</a></sup>God &#91;is come&#93;; but if first from us, what &#91;shall be&#93; the end of those who obey not the glad tidings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="4.17 Elohim">j</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:31; Ezekiel 18:24; Luke 23:31"> And if the righteous is difficultly saved, where shall the impious and &#91;the&#93; sinner appear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:5; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Peter 3:17"> Wherefore also let them who suffer according to the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="4.19 Elohim">k</a></sup>God commit their souls in well-doing to a faithful Creator.</span>
