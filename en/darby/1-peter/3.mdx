<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:33; Ephesians 5:22–24"> Likewise, wives, &#91;be&#93; subject to your own husbands, that, even if any are disobedient to the word, they may be gained without &#91;the&#93; word by the conversation of the wives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27"> having witnessed your pure conversation &#91;carried out&#93; in fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9–10; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 3:18–24"> whose adorning let it not be that outward one of tressing of hair, and wearing gold, or putting on apparel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 2:29"> but the hidden man of the heart, in the incorruptible &#91;ornament&#93; of a meek and quiet spirit, which in the sight of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God is of great price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> For thus also the holy women who have hoped in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God heretofore adorned themselves, being subject to their own husbands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:12; Romans 9:7–9; Isaiah 57:11"> as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord; whose children ye have become, doing good, and not fearing with any kind of consternation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:19; Genesis 2:23–24; Matthew 19:3–9"> &#91;Ye&#93; husbands likewise, dwell with &#91;them&#93; according to knowledge, as with a weaker, &#91;even&#93; the female, vessel, giving &#91;them&#93; honour, as also fellow-heirs of &#91;the&#93; grace of life, that your prayers be not hindered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Ephesians 4:31–2; 1 Peter 1:22"> Finally, &#91;be&#93; all of one mind, sympathising, full of brotherly love, tender hearted, humble minded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17; Romans 12:14"> not rendering evil for evil, or railing for railing; but on the contrary, blessing &#91;others&#93;, because ye have been called to this, that ye should inherit blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> For he that will love life and see good days, let him cause his tongue to cease from evil and his lips that they speak no guile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalms 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> And let him avoid evil, and do good; let him seek peace and pursue it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> because &#91;the&#93; eyes of &#91;the&#93; Lord &#91;are&#93; on &#91;the&#93; righteous, and his ears towards their supplications; but &#91;the&#93; face of &#91;the&#93; Lord &#91;is&#93; against them that do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Romans 8:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> And who shall injure you if ye have become imitators of that which &#91;is&#93; good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> But if also ye should suffer for righteousness' sake, blessed &#91;are ye&#93;; but be not afraid of their fear, neither be troubled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> but sanctify &#91;the&#93; Lord the Christ in your hearts, and &#91;be&#93; always prepared to &#91;give&#93; an answer &#91;to&#93; every one that asks you to give an account of the hope that &#91;is&#93; in you, but with meekness and fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> having a good conscience, that &#91;as to that&#93; in which they speak against you as evildoers, they may be ashamed who calumniate your good conversation in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> For &#91;it is&#93; better, if the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">c</a></sup>God should will it, to suffer &#91;as&#93; well-doers than &#91;as&#93; evildoers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> for Christ indeed has once suffered for sins, &#91;the&#93; just for &#91;the&#93; unjust, that he might bring us to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.18 Elohim">d</a></sup>God; being put to death in flesh, but made alive in &#91;the&#93; Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> in which also going he preached to the spirits &#91;which are&#93; in prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> heretofore disobedient, when the longsuffering of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.20 Elohim">e</a></sup>God waited in &#91;the&#93; days of Noah while the ark was preparing, into which few, that is, eight souls, were saved through water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Ezekiel 36:25–26; Acts 2:38"> which figure also now saves you, &#91;even&#93; baptism, not a putting away of &#91;the&#93; filth of flesh, but &#91;the&#93; demand as before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.21 Elohim">f</a></sup>God of a good conscience, by &#91;the&#93; resurrection of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 10:12"> who is at &#91;the&#93; right hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.22 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, gone into heaven, angels and authorities and powers being subjected to him.</span>
