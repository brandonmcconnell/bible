<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> Peter, apostle of Jesus Christ, to &#91;the&#93; sojourners of &#91;the&#93; dispersion of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> elect according to &#91;the&#93; foreknowledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father, by sanctification of &#91;the&#93; Spirit, unto &#91;the&#93; obedience and sprinkling of &#91;the&#93; blood of Jesus Christ: Grace to you and peace be multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Blessed &#91;be&#93; the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who, according to his great mercy, has begotten us again to a living hope through &#91;the&#93; resurrection of Jesus Christ from among &#91;the&#93; dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> to an incorruptible and undefiled and unfading inheritance, reserved in &#91;the&#93; heavens for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> who are kept guarded by &#91;the&#93; power of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God through faith for salvation ready to be revealed in &#91;the&#93; last time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> Wherein ye exult, for a little while at present, if needed, put to grief by various trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> that the proving of your faith, much more precious than of gold which perishes, though it be proved by fire, be found to praise and glory and honour in &#91;the&#93; revelation of Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> whom, having not seen, ye love; on whom &#91;though&#93; not now looking, but believing, ye exult with joy unspeakable and filled with &#91;the&#93; glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> receiving the end of your faith, &#91;the&#93; salvation of &#91;your&#93; souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> Concerning which salvation prophets, who have prophesied of the grace towards you, sought out and searched out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> searching what, or what manner of time, the Spirit of Christ which &#91;was&#93; in them pointed out, testifying before of the sufferings which &#91;belonged&#93; to Christ, and the glories after these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> To whom it was revealed, that not to themselves but to you they ministered those things, which have now been announced to you by those who have declared to you the glad tidings by &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, sent from heaven, which angels desire to look into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> Wherefore, having girded up the loins of your mind, &#91;be&#93; sober &#91;and&#93; hope with perfect stedfastness in the grace &#91;which will be&#93; brought to you at &#91;the&#93; revelation of Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> as children of obedience, not conformed to &#91;your&#93; former lusts in your ignorance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> but as he who has called you is holy, be ye also holy in all &#91;your&#93; conversation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> because it is written, Be ye holy, for I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> And if ye invoke as Father him who, without regard of persons, judges according to the work of each, pass your time of sojourn in fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalms 49:7–8"> knowing that ye have been redeemed, not by corruptible &#91;things, as&#93; silver or gold, from your vain conversation handed down from &#91;your&#93; fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> but by precious blood, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot, &#91;the blood&#93; of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> foreknown indeed before &#91;the&#93; foundation of &#91;the&#93; world, but who has been manifested at the end of times for your sakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> who by him do believe on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, who has raised him from among &#91;the&#93; dead and given him glory, that your faith and hope should be in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> Having purified your souls by obedience to the truth to unfeigned brotherly love, love one another out of a pure heart fervently;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by &#91;the&#93; living and abiding word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.23 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalms 103:15; James 4:14"> Because all flesh &#91;is&#93; as grass, and all its glory as &#91;the&#93; flower of grass. The grass has withered and &#91;its&#93; flower has fallen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalms 119:89"> but the word of &#91;the&#93; Lord abides for eternity. But this is the word which in the glad tidings &#91;is&#93; preached to you.</span>
