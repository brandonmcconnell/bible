<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; James 1:21; James 4:11"> Laying aside therefore all malice and all guile and hypocrisies and envyings and all evil speakings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–13; Ephesians 4:15; 2 Peter 3:18"> as newborn babes desire earnestly the pure mental milk of the word, that by it ye may grow up to salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:8; Hebrews 6:5–6; Psalms 63:5"> if indeed ye have tasted that the Lord &#91;is&#93; good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:7"> To whom coming, a living stone, cast away indeed as worthless by men, but with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God chosen, precious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> yourselves also, as living stones, are being built up a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer spiritual sacrifices acceptable to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God by Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:4"> Because it is contained in the scripture: Behold, I lay in Zion a corner stone, elect, precious: and he that believes on him shall not be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12"> To you therefore who believe &#91;is&#93; the preciousness; but to the disobedient, the stone which the builders cast away as worthless, this is become head of &#91;the&#93; corner,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> and a stone of stumbling and rock of offence; &#91;who&#93; stumble at the word, being disobedient to which also they have been appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Revelation 1:6"> But ye &#91;are&#93; a chosen race, a kingly priesthood, a holy nation, a people for a possession, that ye might set forth the excellencies of him who has called you out of darkness to his wonderful light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> who once &#91;were&#93; not a people, but now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God's people; who were not enjoying mercy, but now have found mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> Beloved, I exhort &#91;you&#93;, as strangers and sojourners, to abstain from fleshly lusts, which war against the soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> having your conversation honest among the Gentiles, that &#91;as to that&#93; in which they speak against you as evildoers, they may through &#91;your&#93; good works, &#91;themselves&#93; witnessing &#91;them&#93;, glorify<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God in &#91;the&#93; day of visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Be in subjection &#91;therefore&#93; to every human institution for the Lord's sake; whether to &#91;the&#93; king as supreme,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> or to rulers as sent by him, for vengeance on evildoers, and praise to them that do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> Because so is the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.15 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, that by well-doing ye put to silence the ignorance of senseless men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> as free, and not as having liberty as a cloak of malice, but as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.16 Elohim">f</a></sup>God's bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> Shew honour to all, love the brotherhood, fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, honour the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> Servants, &#91;be&#93; subject with all fear to your masters, not only to the good and gentle, but also to the ill-tempered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this &#91;is&#93; acceptable, if one, for conscience sake towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.19 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, endure griefs, suffering unjustly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> For what glory &#91;is it&#93;, if sinning and being buffeted ye shall bear &#91;it&#93;? but if, doing good and suffering, ye shall bear &#91;it&#93;, this is acceptable with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2.20 Elohim">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> For to this have ye been called; for Christ also has suffered for you, leaving you a model that ye should follow in his steps:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> who did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Peter 4:19; Hebrews 12:3"> who, &#91;when&#93; reviled, reviled not again; &#91;when&#93; suffering, threatened not; but gave &#91;himself&#93; over into the hands of him who judges righteously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–6; Matthew 8:17; Romans 6:11"> who himself bore our sins in his body on the tree, in order that, being dead to sins, we may live to righteousness: by whose stripes ye have been healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Luke 15:4–6"> For ye were going astray as sheep, but have now returned to the shepherd and overseer of your souls.</span>
