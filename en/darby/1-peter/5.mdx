<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> The elders which &#91;are&#93; among you I exhort, who &#91;am their&#93; fellow-elder and witness of the sufferings of the Christ, who also &#91;am&#93; partaker of the glory about to be revealed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> shepherd the flock of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God which &#91;is&#93; among you, exercising oversight, not by necessity, but willingly; not for base gain, but readily;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> not as lording it over your possessions, but being models for the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 John 3:2"> And when the chief shepherd is manifested ye shall receive the unfading crown of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> Likewise &#91;ye&#93; younger, be subject to &#91;the&#93; elder, and all of you bind on humility towards one another; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God sets himself against &#91;the&#93; proud, but to &#91;the&#93; humble gives grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.6 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, that he may exalt you in &#91;the due&#93; time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Philippians 4:6; Psalms 37:5"> having cast all your care upon him, for he cares about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; James 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be vigilant, watch. Your adversary &#91;the&#93; devil as a roaring lion walks about seeking whom he may devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> Whom resist, stedfast in faith, knowing that the selfsame sufferings are accomplished in your brotherhood which &#91;is&#93; in &#91;the&#93; world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> But the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God of all grace who has called you to his eternal glory in Christ Jesus, when ye have suffered for a little while, himself shall make perfect, stablish, strengthen, ground:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> to him &#91;be&#93; the glory and the might for the ages of the ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 11:23; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> By Silvanus, the faithful brother, as I suppose, I have written to you briefly; exhorting and testifying that this is &#91;the&#93; true grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.12 Elohim">e</a></sup>God in which ye stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalms 87:4"> She that is elected with &#91;you&#93; in Babylon salutes you, and Marcus my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 8:1"> Salute one another with a kiss of love. Peace be with you all who &#91;are&#93; in Christ.</span>
