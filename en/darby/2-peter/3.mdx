<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 2 Peter 1:12–15; 1 Peter 1:22"> This, a second letter, beloved, I already write to you, in &#91;both&#93; which I stir up, in the way of putting you in remembrance, your pure mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:17; 2 Peter 2:21; Luke 1:70"> to be mindful of the words spoken before by the holy prophets, and of the commandment of the Lord and Saviour by your apostles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; 2 Peter 2:10"> knowing this first, that there shall come at &#91;the&#93; close of the days mockers with mocking, walking according to their own lusts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:15; Malachi 2:17; Jeremiah 5:12–13"> and saying, Where is the promise of his coming? for from the time the fathers fell asleep all things remain thus from &#91;the&#93; beginning of &#91;the&#93; creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; Genesis 1:9; Psalms 24:2"> For this is hidden from them through their own wilfulness, that heavens were of old, and an earth, having its subsistence out of water and in water, by the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:38–39; 2 Peter 2:5; Luke 17:27"> through which &#91;waters&#93; the then world, deluged with water, perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 51:6; 2 Peter 3:12"> But the present heavens and the earth by his word are laid up in store, kept for fire unto a day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:4; Romans 11:25; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> But let not this one thing be hidden from you, beloved, that one day with &#91;the&#93; Lord &#91;is&#93; as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Romans 2:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> &#91;The&#93; Lord does not delay his promise, as some account of delay, but is longsuffering towards you, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:7"> But the day of &#91;the&#93; Lord will come as a thief, in which the heavens will pass away with a rushing noise, and &#91;the&#93; elements, burning with heat, shall be dissolved, and &#91;the&#93; earth and the works in it shall be burnt up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27; 1 Peter 2:12"> All these things then being to be dissolved, what ought ye to be in holy conversation and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 34:4; Micah 1:4"> waiting for and hastening the coming of the day of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, by reason of which &#91;the&#93; heavens, being on fire, shall be dissolved, and &#91;the&#93; elements, burning with heat, shall melt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Revelation 21:1; Isaiah 66:22"> But, according to his promise, we wait for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwells righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 1:10"> Wherefore, beloved, as ye wait for these things, be diligent to be found of him in peace, without spot and blameless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 1:16"> and account the longsuffering of our Lord &#91;to be&#93; salvation; according as our beloved brother Paul also has written to you according to the wisdom given to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:6; Hebrews 5:11; James 1:8"> as also in all &#91;his&#93; epistles, speaking in them of these things; among which some things are hard to be understood, which the untaught and ill-established wrest, as also the other scriptures, to their own destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Colossians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Ye therefore, beloved, knowing &#91;these&#93; things before, take care lest, being led away along with the error of the wicked, ye should fall from your own stedfastness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Ephesians 4:15; 1 Peter 2:2"> but grow in grace, and in &#91;the&#93; knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. To him &#91;be&#93; glory both now and to &#91;the&#93; day of eternity. Amen.</span>
