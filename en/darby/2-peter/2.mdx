<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; Jude 1:4; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> But there were false prophets also among the people, as there shall be also among you false teachers, who shall bring in by the bye destructive heresies, and deny the master that bought them, bringing upon themselves swift destruction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Jude 1:10; 1 Peter 2:12"> and many shall follow their dissolute ways, through whom the way of the truth shall be blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; Deuteronomy 32:35; Romans 16:18"> And through covetousness, with well-turned words, will they make merchandise of you: for whom judgment of old is not idle, and their destruction slumbers not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:6; Matthew 25:41; Romans 8:32"> For if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God spared not &#91;the&#93; angels who had sinned, but having cast them down to the deepest pit of gloom has delivered them to chains of darkness &#91;to be&#93; kept for judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 3:6; Jude 1:14–15"> and spared not &#91;the&#93; old world, but preserved Noe, &#91;the&#93; eighth, a preacher of righteousness, having brought in &#91;the&#93; flood upon &#91;the&#93; world of &#91;the&#93; ungodly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; Luke 17:28–30; Deuteronomy 29:23"> and having reduced &#91;the&#93; cities of Sodom and Gomorrha to ashes, condemned &#91;them&#93; with an overthrow, setting &#91;them as&#93; an example to those that should &#91;afterwards&#93; live an ungodly life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 19:16; Genesis 19:29; Genesis 19:7–8"> and saved righteous Lot, distressed with the abandoned conversation of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>godless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Ezekiel 9:4; Psalms 119:136"> (for the righteous man through seeing and hearing, dwelling among them, tormented &#91;his&#93; righteous soul day after day with &#91;their&#93; lawless works,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 3:10"> &#91;the&#93; Lord knows &#91;how&#93; to deliver the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>godly out of trial, and to keep &#91;the&#93; unjust to &#91;the&#93; day of judgment &#91;to be&#93; punished;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Jude 1:10; Jude 1:18"> and specially those who walk after the flesh in &#91;the&#93; lust of uncleanness, and despise lordship. Bold &#91;are they&#93;, self-willed; they do not fear speaking injuriously of dignities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Psalms 104:4"> when angels, who are greater in might and power, do not bring against them, before the Lord, an injurious charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Jeremiah 12:3; Galatians 6:8"> But these, as natural animals without reason, made to be caught and destroyed, speaking injuriously in things they are ignorant of, shall also perish in their own corruption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:13; Romans 2:8–9; Hebrews 2:2–3"> receiving &#91;the&#93; reward of unrighteousness; accounting ephemeral indulgence pleasure; spots and blemishes, rioting in their own deceits, feasting with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 2:3; 2 Peter 2:18; Ephesians 2:3"> having eyes full of adultery, and that cease not from sin, alluring unestablished souls; having a heart practised in covetousness, children of curse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 23:4–5"> having left &#91;the&#93; straight way they have gone astray, having followed in the path of Balaam &#91;the son&#93; of Bosor, who loved &#91;the&#93; reward of unrighteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 22:21–33; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Luke 16:11"> but had reproof of his own wickedness — &#91;the&#93; dumb ass speaking with man's voice forbad the folly of the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Ephesians 4:14; Jeremiah 14:3"> These are springs without water, and mists driven by storm, to whom the gloom of darkness is reserved &#91;for ever&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20; Jude 1:15–16; Romans 13:13"> For &#91;while&#93; speaking great highflown words of vanity, they allure with &#91;the&#93; lusts of &#91;the&#93; flesh, by dissoluteness, those who have just fled those who walk in error,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:34; Romans 6:16–22; Galatians 5:13"> promising them liberty, while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for by whom a man is subdued, by him is he also brought into slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Hebrews 6:4–8; Philippians 3:19"> For if after having escaped the pollutions of the world through &#91;the&#93; knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, again entangled, they are subdued by these, their last state is worse than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 6:4–6; James 4:17"> For it were better for them not to have known the way of righteousness, than having known &#91;it&#93; to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 26:11"> But that &#91;word&#93; of the true proverb has happened to them: &#91;The&#93; dog &#91;has&#93; turned back to his own vomit; and, &#91;The&#93; washed sow to &#91;her&#93; rolling in mud.</span>
