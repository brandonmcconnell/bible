<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Romans 3:21–26; Titus 1:4"> Simon Peter, bondman and apostle of Jesus Christ, to them that have received like precious faith with us through &#91;the&#93; righteousness of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and Saviour Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; John 17:3; Philippians 3:8"> Grace and peace be multiplied to you in &#91;the&#93; knowledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and of Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30"> As his divine power has given to us all things which relate to life and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>godliness, through the knowledge of him that has called us by glory and virtue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Ezekiel 36:25–27; 2 Corinthians 3:18"> through which he has given to us the greatest and precious promises, that through these ye may become partakers of &#91;the&#93; divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Proverbs 4:23; 2 Peter 3:18"> But for this very reason also, using therewith all diligence, in your faith have also virtue, in virtue knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Romans 5:3–4; Luke 21:19"> in knowledge temperance, in temperance endurance, in endurance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">d</a></sup>godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:12"> in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.7 Elohim">e</a></sup>godliness brotherly love, in brotherly love love:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; John 15:2; Hebrews 6:12"> for these things existing and abounding in you make &#91;you&#93; to be neither idle nor unfruitful as regards the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18–20; Ephesians 5:26; Titus 2:14"> for he with whom these things are not present is blind, short-sighted, and has forgotten the purging of his former sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Luke 6:47–49; Hebrews 6:11"> Wherefore the rather, brethren, use diligence to make your calling and election sure, for doing these things ye will never fall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Matthew 25:34; Revelation 3:21"> for thus shall the entrance into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ be richly furnished unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:21; Romans 15:14–15; Philippians 3:1"> Wherefore I will be careful to put you always in mind of these things, although knowing &#91;them&#93; and established in the present truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:1; 2 Peter 1:14; Hebrews 13:3"> But I account it right, as long as I am in this tabernacle, to stir you up by putting &#91;you&#93; in remembrance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; John 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> knowing that the putting off of my tabernacle is speedily &#91;to take place&#93;, as also our Lord Jesus Christ has manifested to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; Psalms 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> but I will use diligence, that after my departure ye should have also, at any time, &#91;in your power&#93; to call to mind these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:14; 1 Timothy 1:4"> For we have not made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, following cleverly imagined fables, but having been eyewitnesses of his majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:7; Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17"> For he received from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.17 Elohim">f</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father honour and glory, such a voice being uttered to him by the excellent glory: This is my beloved Son, in whom I have found my delight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:6; Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5"> and this voice we heard uttered from heaven, being with him on the holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 119:105"> And we have the prophetic word &#91;made&#93; surer, to which ye do well taking heed (as to a lamp shining in an obscure place) until &#91;the&#93; day dawn and &#91;the&#93; morning star arise in your hearts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; Romans 12:6; Romans 6:6"> knowing this first, that &#91;the scope of&#93; no prophecy of scripture is had from its own particular interpretation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:15–17; 1 Peter 1:11; 2 Samuel 23:2"> for prophecy was not ever uttered by &#91;the&#93; will of man, but holy men of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">g</a></sup>God spake under the power of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
