<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Luke 11:29–30; 2 Kings 14:25"> And the word of Jehovah came unto Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Genesis 10:11; Isaiah 58:1"> Arise, go to Nineveh, the great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of Jehovah; and he went down to Joppa, and found a ship going to Tarshish; so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it, to go with them unto Tarshish, from the presence of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> But Jehovah sent out a great wind upon the sea, and there was a mighty tempest upon the sea, so that the ship was like to be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; 1 Kings 18:26"> And the mariners were afraid, and cried every one unto his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>god; and they cast forth the wares that were in the ship into the sea, to be lightened of them. But Jonah had gone down into the lower part of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Psalms 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> And the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him, What meanest thou, sleeper? arise, call upon thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; perhaps<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God will think upon us, that we perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And they said each one to his fellow, Come, and let us cast lots, that we may know for whose cause this evil is upon us. And they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Samuel 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> And they said unto him, Tell us, we pray thee, for whose cause this evil is upon us: what is thine occupation? and whence comest thou? what is thy country? and of what people art thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:5–6; Psalms 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> And he said unto them, I am a Hebrew, and I fear Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of the heavens, who hath made the sea and the dry &#91;land&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> Then were the men exceedingly afraid, and said unto him, What is this thou hast done? For the men knew that he fled from the presence of Jehovah: for he had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1–6; Micah 6:6–7; 1 Samuel 6:2–3"> And they said unto him, What shall we do unto thee, that the sea may be calm unto us? for the sea grew more and more tempestuous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:17; 1 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> And he said unto them, Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that because of me this great tempest is upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> But the men rowed hard to regain the land; but they could not; for the sea grew more and more tempestuous against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Psalms 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> And they cried unto Jehovah and said, Ah, Jehovah, we beseech thee, let us not perish for this man's life, and lay not upon us innocent blood: for thou, Jehovah, hast done as it pleased thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 107:29"> And they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from its raging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> And the men feared Jehovah exceedingly, and offered a sacrifice unto Jehovah, and made vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Luke 11:30; Matthew 16:4"> And Jehovah prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.</span>
