<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 15:28; Jonah 4:9; Jonah 4:4"> And it displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Exodus 34:6–7; Numbers 14:18–19"> And he prayed unto Jehovah, and said, Ah, Jehovah, was not this my saying when I was yet in my country? Therefore I was minded to flee at first unto Tarshish; for I knew that thou art a gracious<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.2 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great loving-kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Ecclesiastes 7:1; Numbers 11:15"> And now, Jehovah, take, I beseech thee, my life from me, for it is better for me to die than to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Jonah 4:9; Micah 6:3"> And Jehovah said, Doest thou well to be angry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; 1 Kings 19:13; Jeremiah 17:15–16"> And Jonah went out of the city, and sat on the east side of the city, and there made him a booth, and sat under it in the shade, till he might see what would become of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–14; Isaiah 39:2; Esther 5:9"> And Jehovah Elohim prepared a gourd, and made it to come up over Jonah, that it might be a shade over his head, to deliver him from his trouble. And Jonah was exceeding glad because of the gourd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 1:12; Job 1:21; Psalms 102:10"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day, and it smote the gourd, that it withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Psalms 121:6; Ezekiel 19:12"> And it came to pass, when the sun arose, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God prepared a sultry east wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, so that he fainted; and he requested for himself that he might die, and said, It is better for me to die than to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God said to Jonah, Doest thou well to be angry for the gourd? And he said, I do well to be angry, unto death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; Genesis 17:12"> And Jehovah said, Thou hast pity on the gourd, for which thou hast not laboured, neither madest it grow; which came up in a night, and perished in a night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonah 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> and I, should not I have pity on Nineveh, the great city, wherein are more than a hundred and twenty thousand persons that cannot discern between their right hand and their left hand; and also much cattle?</span>
