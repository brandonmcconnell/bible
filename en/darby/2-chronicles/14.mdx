<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:31; 1 Kings 2:10; 1 Kings 14:31"> And Abijah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David. And Asa his son reigned in his stead. In his days the land was quiet ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 31:20; 1 Kings 15:14"> And Asa did what was good and right in the sight of Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:14"> and he took away the altars of the strange &#91;gods&#93; and the high places, and broke the columns, and cut down the Asherahs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:19; Psalms 119:10; Psalms 101:2–8"> and commanded Judah to seek Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God of their fathers, and to practise the law and the commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Chronicles 34:7"> And he removed out of all the cities of Judah the high places and the sun-images; and the kingdom was quiet before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 22:9; Job 34:29"> And he built fortified cities in Judah, for the land had rest, and he had no war in those years; because Jehovah had given him rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 14:6; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> And he said to Judah, Let us build these cities, and surround them with walls and towers, gates and bars, while the land is yet before us; for we have sought Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; we have sought him, and he has given us rest on every side. And they built and prospered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–19; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And Asa had an army that bore targets and spears: out of Judah three hundred thousand; and out of Benjamin, that bore shields and drew the bow, two hundred and eighty thousand: all these, mighty men of valour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; 2 Chronicles 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:8"> And Zerah the Ethiopian came out against him with a host of a thousand thousand, and three hundred chariots, and he came to Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4"> And Asa went out against him, and they set the battle in array in the valley of Zephathah near Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Psalms 22:5"> And Asa cried unto Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and said, Jehovah, it maketh no difference to thee to help, whether there be much or no power: help us, O Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.11 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, for we rely on thee, and in thy name have we come against this multitude. Jehovah, thou art our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.11 Elohim">f</a></sup>God; let not man prevail against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Psalms 136:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:7"> And Jehovah smote the Ethiopians before Asa and before Judah; and the Ethiopians fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 20:1"> And Asa and the people that were with him pursued them to Gerar; and the Ethiopians were overthrown, that none of them was left alive; for they were crushed before Jehovah and before his army. And they carried away very much spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; Genesis 35:5; 2 Chronicles 20:29"> And they smote all the cities round about Gerar, for the terror of Jehovah came upon them; and they spoiled all the cities, for there was very much spoil in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:30–47; Numbers 31:9; 1 Samuel 30:20"> They smote also the tents of cattle, and carried away sheep and camels in abundance, and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
