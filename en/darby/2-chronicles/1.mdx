<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Kings 2:12; Genesis 39:2"> And Solomon the son of David was strengthened in his kingdom, and Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God was with him and magnified him exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1; 1 Chronicles 27:1; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> And Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and to the judges, and to all the princes of all Israel, the chief fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:29; 1 Chronicles 16:39; Leviticus 1:1"> and Solomon, and all the congregation with him, went to the high place at Gibeon; for there was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God's tent of meeting which Moses the servant of Jehovah had made in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:17; 1 Chronicles 15:25–28; 2 Samuel 6:2"> But the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God had David brought up from Kirjath-jearim to the &#91;place&#93; that David had prepared for it; for he had spread a tent for it at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 31:2; Exodus 38:1–7; 1 Chronicles 2:19–20"> And the brazen altar that Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, had made, was there before the tabernacle of Jehovah; and Solomon and the congregation sought unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Kings 8:63; 1 Chronicles 29:21"> And Solomon offered there upon the brazen altar before Jehovah which was at the tent of meeting; and he offered up a thousand burnt-offerings upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; 1 John 5:14–15; John 16:23"> In that night<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God appeared to Solomon, and said to him, Ask what I shall give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:5; Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 22:51–1"> And Solomon said to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, Thou hast shewn unto David my father great loving-kindness, and hast made me king in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 132:11–12"> Now, Jehovah Elohim, let thy word unto David my father be firm; for thou hast made me king over a people numerous as the dust of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 3:9; Proverbs 4:7; Proverbs 2:2–6"> Give me now wisdom and knowledge, that I may go out and come in before this people; for who can judge this thy great people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 23:7; 1 Samuel 16:7"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God said to Solomon, Because this was in thy heart, and thou hast not asked riches, wealth, or honour, nor the life of them that hate thee, neither yet hast asked long life; but hast asked for thyself wisdom and knowledge, that thou mayest judge my people, over whom I have made thee king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:5; 2 Chronicles 9:22; Matthew 6:33"> wisdom and knowledge are granted unto thee; and I will give thee riches and wealth and honour, such as none of the kings have had that have been before thee, neither shall any after thee have the like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 4:24–25"> Then Solomon came back &#91;from&#93; the high place at Gibeon to Jerusalem, from before the tent of meeting, and reigned over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26–29; 2 Chronicles 9:25"> And Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen; and he had a thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen; and he placed them in the chariot-cities, and with the king at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:27; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> And the king made silver and gold in Jerusalem as stones, and cedars made he as the sycamores that are in the lowland for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:28; 1 Kings 10:28–29"> And the exportation of horses that Solomon had was from Egypt: a caravan of the king's merchants fetched a drove &#91;of horses&#93;, at a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29"> And they fetched up and brought forth out of Egypt a chariot for six hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty; and so they brought &#91;them&#93; by their means, for all the kings of the Hittites and for the kings of Syria.</span>
