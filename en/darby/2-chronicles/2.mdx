<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 5:5; 1 Chronicles 22:10; 1 Kings 7:1"> And Solomon purposed to build a house for the name of Jehovah, and a house for his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:18; 1 Kings 5:15–16"> And Solomon numbered seventy thousand men to bear burdens, and eighty thousand stone-masons in the mountain, and three thousand six hundred to superintend them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:1; 2 Samuel 5:11; 1 Kings 5:1–11"> And Solomon sent to Huram king of Tyre, saying, As thou didst deal with David my father, and didst send him cedars to build him a house to dwell therein &#91;so do for me&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 25:30; 2 Chronicles 2:1"> Behold, I build a house unto the name of Jehovah my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God to dedicate it to him, to burn before him sweet incense, and for the continual arrangement &#91;of the shewbread&#93;, and for the morning and evening burnt-offerings &#91;and&#93; on the sabbaths and on the new moons, and on the set feasts of Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God. This is &#91;an ordinance&#93; for ever to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:25; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 135:5"> And the house that I will build is great; for great is our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God above all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">d</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 66:1; 2 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Kings 8:27"> But who is able to build him a house, seeing the heavens and the heaven of heavens cannot contain him? And who am I that I should build him a house, except to burn sacrifice before him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:15–16; Exodus 31:3–5"> And now send me a man skilful to work in gold, and in silver, and in bronze, and in iron, and in purple and crimson and blue, and experienced in carving, besides the skilful men that are with me in Judah and in Jerusalem, whom David my father provided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 1 Kings 10:11; 2 Chronicles 9:10–11"> Send me also cedar-trees, cypress-trees, and sandal-wood trees, out of Lebanon; for I know that thy servants are experienced in cutting timber in Lebanon; and behold, my servants shall be with thy servants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8; 2 Chronicles 2:5; 2 Chronicles 7:21"> even to prepare me timber in abundance: for the house that I build shall be great and wonderful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 5:11; Ezra 7:22; Romans 13:7–8"> And behold, I will give to thy servants the hewers that fell timber, twenty thousand measures of beaten wheat, and twenty thousand measures of barley, and twenty thousand baths of wine, and twenty thousand baths of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 10:9; 2 Chronicles 9:8; Psalms 72:17"> And Huram king of Tyre answered in writing, which he sent to Solomon, Because Jehovah loved his people, he made thee king over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; 1 Peter 1:3; Revelation 4:11"> And Huram said, Blessed be Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.12 Elohim">e</a></sup>God of Israel, that made the heavens and the earth, who has given to David the king a wise son, endued with prudence and understanding, who will build a house for Jehovah and a house for his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:16"> And now, I send a skilful man, endued with understanding, Huram Abi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:7; Exodus 31:3–4; 1 Kings 7:13–14"> the son of a woman of the daughters of Dan, and whose father was a man of Tyre, experienced in working in gold, and in silver, in bronze, in iron, in stone, and in timber, in purple, in blue, and in byssus, and in crimson, and for doing any manner of engraving, and for inventing every device which shall be put to him, besides thy skilful men, and the skilful men of my lord David thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:10; 1 Kings 5:11"> And now the wheat and the barley, the oil and the wine, which my lord hath spoken of, let him send unto his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:46; 1 Kings 5:8–9; Ezra 3:7"> And we will cut wood out of Lebanon, as much as thou shalt need; and we will bring it to thee &#91;in&#93; floats by sea to Joppa, and thou shalt carry it up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:2; 1 Kings 5:13–16; 2 Chronicles 8:7–8"> And Solomon numbered all the strangers that were in the land of Israel, after the account that David his father had taken of them, and there were found a hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:2"> And he set seventy thousand of them to be bearers of burdens, and eighty thousand to be stone-masons in the mountains, and three thousand six hundred overseers to set the people to work.</span>
