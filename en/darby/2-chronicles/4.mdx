<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Ezekiel 43:13–17; 1 Kings 9:25"> And he made a brazen altar: its length was twenty cubits, and its breadth twenty cubits, and its height ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Zechariah 13:1; Exodus 30:18–21"> And he made the sea, molten, ten cubits from brim to brim, round all about; and its height was five cubits; and a line of thirty cubits encompassed it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> And under it was the similitude of oxen, encompassing it round about, ten in a cubit enclosing the sea round about, two rows of oxen, cast when it was cast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> It stood upon twelve oxen, three looking toward the north, and three looking toward the west, and three looking toward the south, and three looking toward the east; and the sea was above upon them, and all their hinder parts were inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:26"> And its thickness was a hand-breadth, and its brim like the work of the brim of a cup, with lily-blossoms; in capacity it held three thousand baths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; 1 Kings 7:40"> And he made ten lavers, and put five on the right and five on the left, to wash in them: they rinsed in them what they prepared for the burnt-offering; and the sea was for the priests to wash in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; 1 Kings 7:49; John 8:12"> And he made ten candlesticks of gold according to the ordinance respecting them, and set them in the temple, five on the right hand and five on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> And he made ten tables, and placed them in the temple, five on the right hand and five on the left. And he made a hundred golden bowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; 2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> And he made the court of the priests, and the great court, and doors for the court, and overlaid the doors thereof with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 7:39"> And he set the sea on the right side eastward, over against the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:40–51; 1 Kings 7:14"> And Huram made the pots and the shovels and the bowls. So Huram ended doing the work that he made for king Solomon in the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 7:41; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> two pillars, and the globes and the capitals on the top of the pillars, two; and the two networks, to cover the two globes of the capitals which were on the top of the pillars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> and the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, two rows of pomegranates for one network, to cover the two globes of the capitals which were upon the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 7:27–43"> And he made the bases, and he made the lavers on the bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> one sea, and the twelve oxen under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Kings 7:13–14; 2 Chronicles 4:11"> And the pots, and the shovels, and the forks, and all their instruments did Huram Abiv make king Solomon for the house of Jehovah, of bright brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:46"> In the plain of the Jordan did the king cast them, in the clay-ground between Succoth and Zeredathah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> And Solomon made all these vessels in great number; for the weight of the brass was not ascertained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 9:13; Daniel 5:2–3; Exodus 25:23–30"> And Solomon made all the vessels that were &#91;in&#93; the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God: the golden altar; and the tables whereon was the shewbread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:7; Exodus 25:31–37; 1 Kings 6:5"> and the candlesticks with their lamps to burn according to the ordinance before the oracle, of pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 6:29; Exodus 37:20; 1 Kings 6:35"> and the flowers, and the lamps, and the tongs, of gold (it was perfect gold);</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; 2 Kings 25:14; Jeremiah 52:18"> and the knives, and the bowls, and the cups, and the censers, of pure gold; and the entrance of the house, the inner folding-doors thereof for the most holy place, and the doors of the house, of the temple, of gold.</span>
