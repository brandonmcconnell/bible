<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:18; Genesis 22:14; 1 Chronicles 22:1"> And Solomon began to build the house of Jehovah at Jerusalem on mount Moriah, where he appeared to David his father, in the place that David had prepared in the threshing-floor of Ornan the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1"> And he began to build on the second of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Chronicles 28:11–19"> And this was Solomon's foundation for the construction of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God. The length by cubits after the first measure was sixty cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:23; Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> And the porch which was in front was twenty cubits in length, in front of the house broadways, and the height was a hundred and twenty; and he overlaid it within with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:15–17; 1 Kings 6:21–22"> And the greater house he boarded with cypress-wood, which he overlaid with fine gold, and set on it palm-trees and chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:8; Revelation 21:18–21"> And he overlaid the house with precious stones for beauty; and the gold was gold of Parvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20–22; Exodus 26:29; Ezekiel 7:20"> And he covered the house, the beams, the threshold, and its walls, and its doors with gold, and engraved cherubim on the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; 1 Kings 6:19–20; 1 Kings 6:16"> And he made the house of the most holy place, the length of which was according to the breadth of the house, twenty cubits, and its breadth twenty cubits; and he covered it with fine gold, amounting to six hundred talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11"> And the weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. And he covered the upper chambers with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23–28; Exodus 25:18"> And in the house of the most holy place he made two cherubim of image work, and they overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits long: one wing of five cubits touched the wall of the house; and the other wing of five cubits touched the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the wing of the other cherub of five cubits touched the wall of the house; and the other wing was five cubits joining the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:20"> The wings of these cherubim spread forth were twenty cubits; and they stood on their feet, and their faces were toward the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:3; Hebrews 10:20; Matthew 27:51"> And he made the veil of blue, and purple, and crimson, and byssus, and made cherubim upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–24; Jeremiah 52:20–23"> And before the house he made two pillars thirty-five cubits long; and the capital that was on the top of each of them was five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 6:21"> And he made chains &#91;as&#93; in the oracle, and he put them on the top of the pillars; and he made a hundred pomegranates, and put them on the chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:21"> And he set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right hand and the other on the left; and he called the name of that on the right Jachin, and the name of that on the left Boaz.</span>
