<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:11; 2 Chronicles 33:25; 2 Chronicles 26:1"> And the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and made him king in his father's stead, in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign; and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33"> And the king of Egypt put him down at Jerusalem, and imposed a fine upon the land of a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10–12; 2 Kings 23:34–35; Ezekiel 19:3–4"> And the king of Egypt made Eliakim his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem, and changed his name to Jehoiakim. And Necho took Jehoahaz his brother, and carried him to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:36–37; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Jeremiah 26:1"> Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign; and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem; and he did evil in the sight of Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="36.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; 2 Kings 24:5–6; Ezekiel 19:5–9"> Against him came up Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and bound him with chains of brass to carry him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Daniel 5:2–4; Jeremiah 27:16–18"> And Nebuchadnezzar carried &#91;part&#93; of the vessels of the house of Jehovah to Babylon, and put them in his temple at Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 24:5–6; Matthew 1:11–12; 1 Chronicles 3:16–17"> And the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and his abominations which he did, and that which was found in him, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah. And Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:8–17"> Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign; and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem; and he did evil in the sight of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Kings 24:10–17"> And at the turn of the year king Nebuchadnezzar sent and had him brought to Babylon, with the precious vessels of the house of Jehovah; and he made Zedekiah his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:18–20; Jeremiah 52:1–3; Jeremiah 27:1"> Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign; and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Jeremiah 37:2–21; Jeremiah 38:14–28"> And he did evil in the sight of Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="36.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; he humbled not himself before the prophet Jeremiah speaking from the mouth of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:2–3; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Ezekiel 17:11–20"> And he also rebelled against king Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him take oath by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="36.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God; and he stiffened his neck, and hardened his heart from returning to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="36.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Ezekiel 22:6; Micah 3:9–11"> All the chiefs of the priests also, and the people, increased their transgressions, according to all the abominations of the nations; and they defiled the house of Jehovah which he had hallowed in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; Jeremiah 7:13; Jeremiah 25:3–4"> And Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="36.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God of their fathers sent to them by his messengers, rising up early and sending; because he had compassion on his people and on his dwelling-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; 2 Chronicles 30:10; Luke 22:63–64"> But they mocked at the messengers of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="36.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, and despised his words, and scoffed at his prophets, until the fury of Jehovah rose against his people, and there was no remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:20; Ezra 9:7; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> And he brought up &#91;against&#93; them the king of the Chaldees, and slew their young men with the sword in the house of their sanctuary, and spared not young man nor maiden, old man nor him of hoary head: he gave &#91;them&#93; all into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Isaiah 39:6"> And all the vessels of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="36.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, great and small, and the treasures of the house of Jehovah, and the treasures of the king and of his princes, he brought all to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; 1 Kings 9:8; Jeremiah 52:13–15"> And they burned the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="36.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, and burned all the palaces thereof with fire, and all the precious vessels thereof were given up to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7; Ezra 1:1–11; 2 Chronicles 36:22"> And them that had escaped from the sword he carried away to Babylon; and they became servants to him and his sons, until the reign of the kingdom of Persia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Leviticus 26:34–35; Zechariah 1:12"> to fulfil the word of Jehovah by the mouth of Jeremiah, until the land had enjoyed its sabbaths. All the days of its desolation it kept sabbath, to fulfil seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:12; Ezra 1:1–3; Jeremiah 29:10"> And in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, that the word of Jehovah by the mouth of Jeremiah might be accomplished, Jehovah stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and he made a proclamation throughout his kingdom, and also in writing, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26–28; Ezra 7:13; Romans 8:31"> Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: All the kingdoms of the earth has Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="36.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of the heavens given to me, and he has charged me to build him a house at Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Whosoever there is among you of all his people, Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="36.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God be with him, and let him go up.</span>
