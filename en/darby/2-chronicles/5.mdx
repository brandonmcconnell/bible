<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:2; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10; Leviticus 23:34–36"> And all the men of Israel assembled themselves to the king at the feast, that of the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:3; Joshua 3:6; Joshua 6:6"> And all the elders of Israel came; and the Levites took up the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 8:4"> And they brought up the ark, and the tent of meeting, and all the holy vessels that were in the tent: the priests, the Levites, brought them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:21; 2 Samuel 6:13; 1 Chronicles 16:1–2"> And king Solomon, and all the assembly of Israel, that were assembled to him before the ark, sacrificed sheep and oxen which could not be counted nor numbered for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Kings 6:23–28; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> And the priests brought in the ark of the covenant of Jehovah to its place, into the oracle of the house, into the most holy place, under the wings of the cherubim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:12–15; Numbers 4:6; Exodus 37:3–5"> and the cherubim stretched forth &#91;their&#93; wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered the ark and its staves above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8–9"> And the staves were long, so that the ends of the staves were seen outside the ark before the oracle; but they were not seen without. And there they are to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; 2 Chronicles 6:11; Deuteronomy 10:2–5"> There was nothing in the ark save the two tables which Moses put there at Horeb, when Jehovah made &#91;a covenant&#93; with the children of Israel, when they came out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:4; 2 Chronicles 29:5; Job 1:5"> And it came to pass when the priests were come out of the holy place (for all the priests that were present were hallowed without observing the courses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:24; Psalms 68:25; 1 Chronicles 6:39"> and the Levites the singers, all they of Asaph, of Heman, of Jeduthun, with their sons and their brethren, clad in byssus, with cymbals and lutes and harps, stood at the east end of the altar, and with them a hundred and twenty priests sounding with trumpets),</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 3:11; Jeremiah 33:11; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> — it came to pass when the trumpeters and singers were as one, to make one voice to be heard in praising and thanking Jehovah; and when they lifted up their voice with trumpets, and cymbals, and instruments of music, and praised Jehovah: For he is good, for his loving-kindness &#91;endureth&#93; for ever; that then the house, the house of Jehovah, was filled with a cloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 40:35; 1 Kings 8:11; Isaiah 6:1–4"> and the priests could not stand to do their service because of the cloud; for the glory of Jehovah had filled the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
