<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:17; 2 Chronicles 12:13; 2 Chronicles 26:13–16"> And it came to pass when the kingdom of Rehoboam was established, and when he had become strong, &#91;that&#93; he forsook the law of Jehovah, and all Israel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 7:19–20; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> And it came to pass in the fifth year of king Rehoboam, because they had transgressed against Jehovah, that Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:9; 2 Chronicles 16:8; Daniel 11:43"> with twelve hundred chariots and sixty thousand horsemen; and the people were without number that came with him out of Egypt: Libyans, Sukkites, and Ethiopians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:5–12; 2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 8:8"> And he took the fortified cities that belonged to Judah, and came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:2; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 12:22"> And Shemaiah the prophet came to Rehoboam and &#91;to&#93; the princes of Judah that had gathered together to Jerusalem because of Shishak, and said to them, Thus saith Jehovah: Ye have forsaken me, and therefore have I also left you in the hand of Shishak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 9:27; Daniel 9:14; Jeremiah 13:18"> And the princes of Israel and the king humbled themselves; and they said, Jehovah is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:25; Isaiah 42:25; 1 Kings 21:28–29"> And when Jehovah saw that they humbled themselves, the word of Jehovah came to Shemaiah, saying, They have humbled themselves: I will not destroy them, but I will grant them a little deliverance; and my wrath shall not be poured out upon Jerusalem by the hand of Shishak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Isaiah 26:13; Jeremiah 10:24"> Nevertheless they shall be his servants; that they may know my service, and the service of the kingdoms of the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Kings 16:8"> And Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem, and took away the treasures of the house of Jehovah, and the treasures of the king's house; he took away all; and he took away the shields of gold that Solomon had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 23:23; Lamentations 4:1"> And king Rehoboam made in their stead shields of bronze, and committed them to the hands of the chief of the couriers who kept the entrance of the king's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And it was so, that as often as the king entered into the house of Jehovah, the couriers came and fetched them, and brought them again into the chamber of the couriers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> And when he humbled himself, the anger of Jehovah turned away from him, that he would not destroy him altogether; and also in Judah there were good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 14:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6"> And king Rehoboam strengthened himself in Jerusalem, and reigned; for Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that Jehovah had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, to put his name there: and his mother's name was Naamah an Ammonitess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 1 Corinthians 16:13; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> And he did evil, for he applied not his heart to seek Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 12:5; 1 Kings 12:22"> And the acts of Rehoboam, first and last, are they not written in the words of Shemaiah the prophet, and of Iddo the seer, in the genealogical registers? And there were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:10; 2 Chronicles 13:1; Matthew 1:7"> And Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David. And Abijah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
