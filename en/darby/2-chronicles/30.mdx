<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 11:13; Hosea 5:4"> And Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters also to Ephraim and Manasseh, that they should come to the house of Jehovah at Jerusalem, to hold the passover to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 9:10–11; 1 Chronicles 13:1–3; 2 Chronicles 30:13"> And the king took counsel, and his princes, and the whole congregation in Jerusalem, to hold the passover in the second month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18"> For they could not keep it at that time, because the priests had not hallowed themselves in sufficient number, neither had the people been gathered together to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:4"> And the thing pleased the king and the whole congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Leviticus 23:2; Esther 8:8–10"> So they established a decree to make proclamation throughout Israel from Beer-sheba even to Dan, that they should come to hold the passover to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="30.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of Israel, at Jerusalem; because they had not held it for a long time as it was written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Esther 8:14; 2 Kings 15:19"> And the couriers went with the letters from the king and his princes throughout Israel and Judah, and according to the commandment of the king, saying, Ye children of Israel, return to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="30.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, and he will return to the remnant of you that are escaped out of the hand of the kings of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Ezekiel 20:13–18; Zechariah 1:3–4"> And be not like your fathers and like your brethren, who transgressed against Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="30.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of their fathers, so that he gave them up to desolation, as ye see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 32:9; 2 Chronicles 29:10; Deuteronomy 10:16"> Now, harden not your necks, as your fathers; yield yourselves to Jehovah, and come to his sanctuary, which he has sanctified for ever; and serve Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="30.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, that the fierceness of his anger may turn away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18; Psalms 86:5"> For if ye return to Jehovah, your brethren and your children shall find compassion with those that have carried them captive, so that they shall come again unto this land; for Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="30.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God is gracious and merciful, and will not turn away his face from you, if ye return to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 Chronicles 30:6; Nehemiah 2:19"> And the couriers passed from city to city through the country of Ephraim and Manasseh, even to Zebulun; but they laughed them to scorn and mocked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:18; 2 Chronicles 30:21; 2 Chronicles 30:25"> Nevertheless certain of Asher and Manasseh and of Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 24:7; Philippians 2:13"> The hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="30.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God was also upon Judah to give them one heart to do the commandment of the king and of the princes, by the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2; Psalms 84:7"> And there assembled at Jerusalem much people to hold the feast of unleavened bread in the second month, a very great congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; 2 Samuel 15:23; 2 Chronicles 15:16"> And they rose up and took away the altars that were in Jerusalem; and they took away all the incense-altars, and cast them into the torrent Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 5:11; Exodus 19:22"> And they slaughtered the passover on the fourteenth of the second month; and the priests and the Levites were ashamed, and hallowed themselves; and they brought the burnt-offerings into the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; 2 Chronicles 35:15; 2 Kings 11:14"> And they stood in their place after their custom, according to the law of Moses the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="30.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God: the priests sprinkled the blood &#91;receiving it&#93; from the hand of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:3–6; Exodus 12:6"> For there were many in the congregation that were not hallowed; therefore the Levites had the charge of the slaughtering of the passover-lambs for every one not clean, to hallow them unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:28; James 5:15–16; Daniel 9:19"> For a multitude of the people, many of Ephraim and Manasseh, Issachar and Zebulun, had not cleansed themselves, and they ate the passover otherwise than it was written. But Hezekiah prayed for them saying, Jehovah, who is good, forgive every one</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; Proverbs 23:26; Job 11:13"> that has directed his heart to seek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="30.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="30.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God of his fathers, although not according to the purification of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; James 5:15–16; Psalms 103:3"> And Jehovah hearkened to Hezekiah, and healed the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 13:6; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And the children of Israel, that were present at Jerusalem, held the feast of unleavened bread seven days with great gladness; and the Levites and the priests praised Jehovah day by day, with the instruments of praise to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:6; Nehemiah 9:3; Isaiah 40:1–2"> And Hezekiah spoke consolingly to all the Levites that had understanding in the good knowledge of Jehovah; and they ate the feast-offerings the seven days, sacrificing peace-offerings, and extolling Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="30.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 7:9"> And the whole congregation took counsel to observe other seven days; and they observed the seven days with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; Ephesians 4:8"> For Hezekiah king of Judah gave to the congregation as heave-offering: a thousand bullocks and seven thousand sheep; and the princes gave to the congregation a thousand bullocks and ten thousand sheep; and a great number of priests hallowed themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:11; 2 Chronicles 30:18; 1 Chronicles 16:10–11"> And the whole congregation of Judah, with the priests and the Levites, and all the congregation that came out of Israel, and the strangers that came out of the land of Israel, and that dwelt in Judah, rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> And there was great joy in Jerusalem; for since the time of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel, there had not been the like in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 68:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> And the priests the Levites arose and blessed the people; and their voice was heard, and their prayer came up to his holy habitation, to the heavens.</span>
