<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:13; Isaiah 1:1; 2 Kings 16:1–20"> Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign; and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. And he did not what was right in the sight of Jehovah, like David his father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Judges 2:11; 2 Chronicles 22:3–4"> but walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, and even made molten images for the Baals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:2; 2 Chronicles 33:6; Leviticus 18:21"> and he burned incense in the valley of the son of Hinnom, and burned his sons in the fire, according to the abominations of the nations that Jehovah had dispossessed from before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 16:4; Deuteronomy 12:2–3; Leviticus 26:30"> And he sacrificed and burned incense on the high places, and on the hills, and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1; 2 Chronicles 24:24; 2 Kings 16:5–6"> Therefore Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="28.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God gave him into the hand of the king of Syria; and they smote him, and carried away a great multitude of them captives, and brought them to Damascus. And he was also given into the hand of the king of Israel, who smote him with a great slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 15:27; Joshua 24:20; Jeremiah 15:6"> And Pekah the son of Remaliah slew in Judah a hundred and twenty thousand in one day, all valiant men, because they had forsaken Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="28.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 43:15; Genesis 41:43; Esther 10:3"> And Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, slew Maaseiah the king's son, and Azrikam the governor of the house, and Elkanah the second to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:4; Deuteronomy 28:25; Deuteronomy 28:41"> And the children of Israel carried away captive of their brethren two hundred thousand, women, sons, and daughters, and took away also much spoil from them, and brought the spoil to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Ezra 9:6; Revelation 18:5"> But a prophet of Jehovah was there, whose name was Oded; and he went out before the host that came to Samaria, and said unto them, Behold, because Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="28.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of your fathers was wroth with Judah, he gave them into your hand, and ye have slain them in a rage that reaches up to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:39–46; Matthew 7:2–4; Jeremiah 25:29"> And now ye think to subjugate the children of Judah and Jerusalem as your bondmen and bondwomen. Are there not with you, even with you, trespasses against Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="28.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 2:13; Ezra 10:14; Matthew 5:7"> And now hear me, and send back the captives again, whom ye have taken captive of your brethren; for the fierce wrath of Jehovah is upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1; Jeremiah 26:6"> And certain of the heads of the children of Ephraim, Azariah the son of Johanan, Berechiah the son of Meshillemoth, and Hezekiah the son of Shallum, and Amasa the son of Hadlai, stood up against them that came from the war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:14; Matthew 23:32; Joshua 22:17–18"> and said to them, Ye shall not bring in the captives hither; because, for our guilt before Jehovah, ye think to increase our sins and our trespasses: for our trespass is great, and fierce wrath is upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then the armed men left the captives and the spoil before the princes and all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 1:16"> And the men that have been expressed by name rose up, and took the captives, and with the spoil clothed all that were naked among them, and arrayed them, and shod them, and gave them to eat and to drink, and anointed them, and carried all the feeble of them on asses, and brought them to Jericho the city of palm-trees, to their brethren. And they returned to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 16:5–7; Isaiah 7:17; Isaiah 7:1–9"> At that time king Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10; Obadiah 1:13–14; Leviticus 26:18"> And again the Edomites came and smote Judah, and carried away captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:57; Ezekiel 16:27; 1 Samuel 6:9"> And the Philistines invaded the cities of the lowland, and of the south of Judah, and took Beth-shemesh, and Ajalon, and Gederoth, and Socho and its dependent villages, and Timnah and its dependent villages, and Guimzo and its dependent villages; and they dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:2; Job 40:12; Deuteronomy 28:43"> For Jehovah humbled Judah because of Ahaz king of Israel, for he had made Judah lawless, and transgressed much against Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; 1 Chronicles 5:26; Isaiah 30:16"> And Tilgath-Pilneser king of Assyria came to him, and troubled him, and did not support him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 16:8–9; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Chronicles 12:9"> For Ahaz stripped the house of Jehovah, and the house of the king and of the princes, and gave to the king of Assyria; but he was of no help to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; Ezekiel 21:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> And in the time of his trouble he transgressed yet more against Jehovah, this king Ahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; Jeremiah 44:20–28; Habakkuk 1:11"> And he sacrificed to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="28.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods of Damascus, which had smitten him; and he said, Since the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="28.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods of the kings of Syria help them, I will sacrifice to them, that they may help me. But they were the ruin of him, and of all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:7; 2 Kings 16:17–18; 2 Chronicles 30:14"> And Ahaz gathered the vessels of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="28.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, and cut in pieces the vessels of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="28.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, and closed the doors of the house of Jehovah, and he made for himself altars in every corner of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:3"> And in every several city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="28.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>gods, and provoked to anger Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="28.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 16:19–20; 2 Chronicles 20:34; 2 Chronicles 27:7–9"> And the rest of his acts, and all his ways, first and last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:20; 2 Chronicles 24:25; Proverbs 10:7"> And Ahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city, in Jerusalem; but they brought him not into the sepulchres of the kings of Israel. And Hezekiah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
