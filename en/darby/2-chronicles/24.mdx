<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Kings 11:21–15"> Joash was seven years old when he began to reign; and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Zibiah of Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:4–5; 2 Chronicles 25:2; 2 Chronicles 24:17–22"> And Joash did what was right in the sight of Jehovah all the days of Jehoiada the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:15; Matthew 19:4–8; Genesis 4:19"> And Jehoiada took for him two wives; and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And it came to pass after this, that Joash was minded to renew the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 12:4–7; 2 Chronicles 34:8–9; 2 Chronicles 29:3"> And he gathered together the priests and the Levites, and said to them, Go out to the cities of Judah and collect of all Israel money for the repair of the house of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God from year to year, and ye shall hasten the matter. But the Levites hastened it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 30:12–16; Numbers 1:50; Numbers 17:7–8"> And the king called for Jehoiada the chief, and said to him, Why hast thou not required of the Levites to bring in out of Judah and out of Jerusalem the tribute of Moses the servant of Jehovah &#91;laid upon&#93; the congregation of Israel, for the tent of the testimony?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Chronicles 28:22–24; 2 Kings 12:4"> For the wicked Athaliah &#91;and&#93; her sons had devastated the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="24.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; and also all the hallowed things of the house of Jehovah had they employed for the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 12:41; 2 Kings 12:8–9"> And the king commanded, and they made a chest, and set it at the gate of the house of Jehovah without,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:6; Matthew 17:24–27"> and they made a proclamation through Judah and Jerusalem, to bring to Jehovah the tribute of Moses the servant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="24.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God &#91;laid upon&#93; Israel in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:45–47; 2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> And all the princes and all the people rejoiced, and brought in and cast into the chest, until they had finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 12:10–12; 1 Corinthians 16:2"> And it came to pass at the time the chest was brought for the king's control by the hand of the Levites, and when they saw that there was much money, the king's scribe and high priest's officer came, and they emptied the chest, and took it, and carried it to its place again. Thus they did day by day, and gathered money in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 5:15; 2 Chronicles 34:9–11"> And the king and Jehoiada gave it to such as did the work of the service of the house of Jehovah, and they hired masons and carpenters to renew the house of Jehovah, and also such as wrought in iron and bronze, to repair the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:7; Mark 13:1–2; Haggai 2:3"> So the workmen wrought, and the work was perfected by them, and they set the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="24.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God in its state, and strengthened it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 28:2–29; Proverbs 27:22; 2 Kings 12:13–14"> And when they had finished, they brought the rest of the money before the king and Jehoiada; and they made of it vessels for the house of Jehovah, utensils to minister, and with which to offer up, and cups, and utensils of gold and silver. And they offered up burnt-offerings in the house of Jehovah continually all the days of Jehoiada.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 47:9; 1 Chronicles 23:1; Psalms 90:10"> And Jehoiada grew old and was full of days, and he died; he was a hundred and thirty years old when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; 2 Chronicles 31:20; Nehemiah 13:14"> And they buried him in the city of David among the kings, because he had done good in Israel, both toward<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="24.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God and toward his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:27; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 20:29–30"> And after the death of Jehoiada the princes of Judah came and made obeisance to the king; then the king hearkened to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:20; 2 Chronicles 19:2; Ephesians 5:6"> And they forsook the house of Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="24.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of their fathers, and served the Asherahs and idols; and wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem for this their trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 42:23; Luke 20:9–15; Matthew 13:9"> And he sent prophets among them to bring them again to Jehovah, and they testified against them; but they would not give ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 14:41; Jeremiah 5:25; 2 Chronicles 15:1–2"> And the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="24.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God came upon Zechariah the son of Jehoiada the priest; and he stood up above the people and said unto them, Thus saith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="24.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God: Wherefore do ye transgress the commandments of Jehovah? And ye cannot prosper; for ye have forsaken Jehovah, and he hath forsaken you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:58–59; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 38:4–6"> And they conspired against him, and stoned him with stones at the command of the king in the court of the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 9:5; Luke 11:51; Revelation 6:9–11"> And king Joash remembered not the kindness which Jehoiada his father had done to him, but slew his son. And when he died, he said, Jehovah see and require &#91;it&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17–18; Deuteronomy 32:35; 1 Kings 20:22"> And it came to pass at the end of the year &#91;that&#93; the army of Syria came up against him; and they entered into Judah and Jerusalem, and destroyed all the princes of the people from among the people, and sent all the spoil of them to the king at Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:8; Leviticus 26:37; Leviticus 26:8"> Truly with a small company of men came the army of the Syrians, but Jehovah delivered a very great army into their hand, because they had forsaken Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="24.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of their fathers; and they executed judgment upon Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:21–22; 2 Chronicles 28:27; 2 Chronicles 21:18–20"> And when they had departed from him (for they left him in great diseases), his own servants conspired against him for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest, and slew him on his bed, and he died; and they buried him in the city of David, but they did not bury him in the sepulchres of the kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 12:21"> And these are they that conspired against him: Zabad the son of Shimeath an Ammonitess, and Jehozabad the son of Shimrith a Moabitess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:11; 2 Chronicles 13:22; 2 Chronicles 24:12"> And as to his sons, and the greatness of the burdens &#91;laid&#93; upon him, and the building of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="24.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, behold, they are written in the treatise of the book of the kings. And Amaziah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
