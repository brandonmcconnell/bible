<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:21–24; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 33:16"> And Rehoboam came to Jerusalem; and he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand chosen men apt for war, to fight against Israel, that he might bring the kingdom again to Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> But the word of Jehovah came to Shemaiah the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> Speak unto Rehoboam, the son of Solomon, king of Judah, and unto all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Chronicles 10:15–16"> Thus saith Jehovah: Go not up, nor fight with your brethren; return every man to his house; for this thing is from me. And they hearkened to the words of Jehovah, and returned from going against Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> And Rehoboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and built cities for defence in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; 1 Chronicles 4:32"> And he built Bethlehem, and Etam, and Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> and Beth-zur, and Soco, and Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 54:1; Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 23:19"> and Gath, and Mareshah, and Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> and Adoraim, and Lachish, and Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> and Zorah, and Ajalon, and Hebron, which are in Judah and in Benjamin, fortified cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> And he fortified the strongholds, and put captains in them, and stores of victuals, and of oil and wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; 2 Chronicles 26:14–15; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> and in every several city, targets and spears, and made them exceedingly strong. And Judah and Benjamin were his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the priests and the Levites that were in all Israel resorted to him out of all their districts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; 1 Kings 12:28–33"> for the Levites left their suburbs and their possessions, and came to Judah and Jerusalem: for Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off from exercising the priesthood to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:33"> and he ordained for himself priests for the high places, and for the he-goats and for the calves that he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; Acts 11:23; Psalms 84:5–7"> — And after them, those out of all the tribes of Israel that set their heart to seek Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of Israel came to Jerusalem, to sacrifice to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 2 Chronicles 1:1–12; 2 Chronicles 7:17–19"> And they strengthened the kingdom of Judah, and made Rehoboam the son of Solomon strong three years; for during three years they walked in the way of David and Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Chronicles 27:18; 1 Samuel 17:13"> And Rehoboam took Mahalath the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David as wife, &#91;and of&#93; Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And she bore him children: Jeush, and Shemariah, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> And after her he took Maachah the daughter of Absalom; and she bore him Abijah, and Attai, and Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; 1 Kings 11:3"> And Rehoboam loved Maachah the daughter of Absalom above all his wives and his concubines; for he had taken eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he begot twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 1 Chronicles 29:1; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> And Rehoboam established Abijah the son of Maachah at the head, to be ruler among his brethren; for &#91;he thought&#93; to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> And he dealt wisely, and dispersed of all his sons throughout all the lands of Judah and Benjamin, to all the fortified cities; and he gave them food in abundance. And he desired &#91;for them&#93; a multitude of wives.</span>
