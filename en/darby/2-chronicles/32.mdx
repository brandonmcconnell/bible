<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:13–37; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Chronicles 20:1–2"> After these things and this faithfulness, Sennacherib king of Assyria came and entered into Judah, and encamped against the fortified cities, and thought to break into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Luke 9:51; 2 Kings 12:17"> And when Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib was come, and that he was minded to fight against Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> he took counsel with his princes and his mighty men to stop the fountains of waters that were outside the city; and they helped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 2 Kings 18:9; 1 Kings 19:21"> And there was gathered together much people, and they stopped all the fountains, and the torrent that flows through the midst of the land, saying, Why should the kings of Assyria come and find much water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:23; 1 Kings 9:24; 2 Kings 25:4"> And he strengthened himself, and built up all the wall that was broken down, and raised it up to the towers, and &#91;built&#93; another wall outside, and fortified the Millo of the city of David, and made darts and shields in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22; Nehemiah 8:16"> And he set captains of war over the people, and assembled them to him on the open place at the gate of the city, and spoke consolingly to them saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 6:10"> Be strong and courageous, be not afraid nor dismayed for the king of Assyria, nor for all the multitude that is with him; for there are more with us than with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 20:17; Isaiah 41:10"> with him is an arm of flesh, but with us is Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="32.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God to help us and to fight our battles. And the people depended upon the words of Hezekiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Joshua 10:31; Isaiah 36:2"> After this, Sennacherib king of Assyria sent his servants to Jerusalem (but he himself was before Lachish, and all his power with him), unto Hezekiah king of Judah, and unto all Judah that were at Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:19; Isaiah 36:4"> Thus says Sennacherib king of Assyria: On what do ye rely that ye abide in the siege in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:43; Psalms 11:1–3; Isaiah 36:18"> Does not Hezekiah persuade you, to give yourselves over to die by famine and by thirst, saying, Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="32.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God will deliver us out of the hand of the king of Assyria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Deuteronomy 12:26–27"> Has not the same Hezekiah removed his high places and his altars, and commanded Judah and Jerusalem saying, Ye shall worship before one altar, and burn incense upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:33–35; Isaiah 44:8–10; Daniel 4:30"> Do ye not know what I and my fathers have done to all the peoples of the countries? Were the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="32.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods of the nations of the countries in any wise able to deliver their country out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:11–12; Exodus 15:9–11; Isaiah 42:8"> Who is there among all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="32.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods of those nations that my fathers have utterly destroyed, that was able to deliver his people out of my hand, that your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="32.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God should be able to deliver you out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Daniel 3:15; Isaiah 36:18"> And now, let not Hezekiah deceive you, nor persuade you in this manner, neither yet believe him; for no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="32.15 Eloah">f</a></sup>god of any nation or kingdom was able to deliver his people out of my hand, nor out of the hand of my fathers: how much less shall your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="32.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0433&quot;">g</a></sup>God deliver you out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 73:9; John 15:21; Job 15:25–26"> And his servants spoke yet more against Jehovah, the &#91;true&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="32.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, and against his servant Hezekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; 2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:28–29"> And he wrote a letter to rail at Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="32.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of Israel, and to speak against him saying, As the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="32.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>gods of the nations of the countries have not delivered their people out of my hand, so shall not the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="32.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God of Hezekiah deliver his people out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; 2 Kings 18:26–28; 1 Samuel 17:26"> And they cried with a loud voice in the Jewish &#91;language&#93; to the people of Jerusalem that were on the wall, to frighten them and to trouble them; that they might take the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 2:8; Jeremiah 32:30"> And they spoke of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="32.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God of Jerusalem as of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="32.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>gods of the peoples of the earth, the work of man's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–15; Isaiah 37:14–20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And because of this, king Hezekiah and the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz prayed and cried to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Psalms 76:5; Psalms 18:50"> And Jehovah sent an angel, who cut off all the mighty men of valour, and the princes and the captains in the camp of the king of Assyria. And he returned with shame of face to his own land. And when he was come into the house of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="32.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>god, they that came forth of his own bowels made him fall there with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4–5; Psalms 18:48–50; 2 Thessalonians 3:5"> And Jehovah saved Hezekiah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib the king of Assyria, and from the hand of all, and protected them on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; Matthew 2:11; 2 Chronicles 1:1"> And many brought gifts unto Jehovah to Jerusalem, and precious things to Hezekiah king of Judah; and he was thenceforth magnified in the sight of all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Kings 20:1–11; Isaiah 38:21–22"> In those days Hezekiah was sick unto death, and he prayed to Jehovah; and he spoke to him and gave him a sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:18; 2 Chronicles 26:16; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> But Hezekiah rendered not again according to the benefit &#91;done&#93; to him, for his heart was lifted up; and there was wrath upon him, and upon Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18–19; 2 Chronicles 34:27–28; 2 Chronicles 33:12"> And Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of Jehovah came not upon them in the days of Hezekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 1:12; Proverbs 10:22"> And Hezekiah had very much riches and honour; and he made himself treasuries for silver, and for gold, and for precious stones, and for spices, and for shields, and for all manner of pleasant vessels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Kings 4:26"> storehouses also for the increase of corn and new wine and oil, and stalls for all manner of beasts, and &#91;he procured&#93; flocks for the stalls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:12; Proverbs 10:22; 1 Samuel 2:7"> And he provided for himself cities, and possessions of flocks and herds in abundance; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="32.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God gave him very much substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:33; Isaiah 22:9–11; Psalms 1:1–3"> And he, Hezekiah, stopped the upper outlet of the waters of Gihon, and brought it straight down to the west side of the city of David. And Hezekiah prospered in all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Proverbs 17:3; Genesis 22:1"> However in &#91;the matter of&#93; the ambassadors of the princes of Babylon, who sent to him to inquire of the wonder that was done in the land,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="32.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God left him, to try him, that he might know all &#91;that was&#93; in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 36:1–22; 2 Kings 18:1–20; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21"> And the rest of the acts of Hezekiah and his good deeds, behold, they are written in the vision of the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 1:21; 2 Chronicles 16:14"> And Hezekiah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the highest place of the sepulchres of the sons of David; and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem did him honour at his death. And Manasseh his son reigned in his stead.</span>
