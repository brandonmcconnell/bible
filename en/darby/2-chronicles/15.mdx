<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> And the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God came upon Azariah the son of Oded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> And he went out to meet Asa, and said to him, Hear ye me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: Jehovah is with you while ye are with him; and if ye seek him he will be found of you, but if ye forsake him he will forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> Now for a long while Israel &#91;was&#93; without the true<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and without a teaching priest, and without law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> but in their trouble they turned to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel, and sought him, and he was found of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> And in those times there &#91;was&#93; no peace to him that went out nor to him that came in, but great disturbances were amongst all the inhabitants of the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> And nation was broken against nation, and city against city; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God disturbed them with all manner of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> But as for you, be firm and let not your hands be weak; for there is a reward for your deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> And when Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage, and put away the abominations out of all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and out of the cities that he had taken from mount Ephraim, and renewed the altar of Jehovah, that was before the porch of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> And he assembled all Judah and Benjamin, and the strangers with them out of Ephraim and Manasseh, and out of Simeon; for they fell away to him out of Israel in abundance, when they saw that Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> And they assembled themselves at Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> And they sacrificed to Jehovah in that day, of the spoil that they had brought, seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> And they entered into a covenant to seek Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of their fathers, with all their heart, and with all their soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalms 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> and that whoever would not seek Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of Israel should be put to death, whether small or great, whether man or woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalms 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they swore to Jehovah with a loud voice, and with shouting, and with trumpets, and with cornets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> And all Judah rejoiced at the oath; for they took the oath with all their heart, and sought him with their whole desire; and he was found of them. And Jehovah gave them rest round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> And also Maachah, the mother of Asa the king, he removed from being queen, because she had made an idol for the Asherah; and Asa cut down her idol, and stamped it, and burned it in the valley Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> But the high places were not removed from Israel; only, Asa's heart was perfect all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> And he brought into the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God the things which his father had dedicated, and the things which he himself had dedicated, silver, and gold, and vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> And there was no war until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
