<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Kings 15:1–8"> In the eighteenth year of king Jeroboam began Abijah to reign over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Kings 15:6–7; Judges 19:16"> He reigned three years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Michaiah, the daughter of Uriel of Gibeah. And there was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:12–13; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 1 Samuel 17:1–3"> And Abijah began the war with an army of men of war, four hundred thousand chosen men; and Jeroboam set the battle in array against him with eight hundred thousand chosen men, mighty men of valour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:22; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Genesis 10:18"> And Abijah stood up on the top of mount Zemaraim, which is in mount Ephraim, and said, Hear me, Jeroboam, and all Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50"> Ought ye not to know that Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel gave the kingdom over Israel to David for ever, to him and to his sons &#91;by&#93; a covenant of salt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:26; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Chronicles 10:19"> But Jeroboam the son of Nebat, the servant of Solomon the son of David, rose up and rebelled against his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 2 Chronicles 12:13; Acts 17:5"> And vain men, sons of Belial, gathered to him and strengthened themselves against Rehoboam the son of Solomon, and Rehoboam was young and faint-hearted, and did not shew himself strong against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 2 Chronicles 11:15; Luke 19:14"> And now ye think to shew yourselves strong against the kingdom of Jehovah in the hand of the sons of David; and ye are a great multitude, and ye have with you the golden calves that Jeroboam made you for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14–15; Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 29:35"> Have ye not cast out the priests of Jehovah, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and made you priests as the peoples of the lands? whoever comes to consecrate himself with a young bullock and seven rams, he becomes a priest of what is not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Zechariah 13:9"> But as for us, Jehovah is our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and we have not forsaken him; and the priests that serve Jehovah are the sons of Aaron, and the Levites are at their work:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Leviticus 24:2–9; Exodus 29:38"> and they burn to Jehovah every morning and every evening burnt-offerings and sweet incense; the loaves also are set in order upon the pure table; and the candlestick of gold with its lamps to burn every evening: for we keep the charge of Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God; but ye have forsaken him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:39; Romans 8:31; Numbers 10:8–9"> And behold, we have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God with us at our head, and his priests, and the loud-sounding trumpets to sound an alarm against you. Children of Israel, do not fight with Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of your fathers; for ye shall not prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; Joshua 8:9; Proverbs 21:30"> But Jeroboam caused an ambush to come about behind them; and they were before Judah, and the ambush behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:11; Exodus 14:10; Joshua 8:20"> And Judah looked back, and behold, they had the battle in front and behind; and they cried to Jehovah, and the priests sounded with the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:12; Joshua 11:8; Psalms 118:4–7"> And the men of Judah gave a shout; and as the men of Judah shouted, it came to pass that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God smote Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; Judges 11:21; Joshua 10:12"> And the children of Israel fled before Judah; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="13.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God delivered them into their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:6; 2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 13:12"> And Abijah and his people slew them with a great slaughter; and there fell down slain of Israel five hundred thousand chosen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Nahum 1:7"> And the children of Israel were humbled at that time, and the children of Judah were strengthened, because they relied upon Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="13.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; Joshua 15:9; Joshua 10:39"> And Abijah pursued after Jeroboam, and took cities from him: Bethel with its dependent villages, and Jeshanah with its dependent villages, and Ephron with its dependent villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:38; 1 Kings 14:20; Acts 12:23"> And Jeroboam did not recover strength again in the days of Abijah; and Jehovah smote him, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Samuel 5:12–13; Judges 10:4"> But Abijah strengthened himself, and took fourteen wives, and begot twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 24:27"> And the rest of the acts of Abijah, and his ways and his sayings, are written in the treatise of the prophet Iddo.</span>
</div>
