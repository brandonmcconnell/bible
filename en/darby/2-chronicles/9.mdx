<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> And the queen of Sheba heard of the fame of Solomon, and came to Jerusalem to prove Solomon with enigmas; with a very great train, and camels that bore spices and gold in great abundance, and precious stones; and she came to Solomon, and spoke with him of all that was in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 3:12; Mark 4:34"> And Solomon explained to her all she spoke of, and there was not a thing hidden from Solomon that he did not explain to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–4; 1 Kings 10:3; 1 Kings 6:1–7"> And when the queen of Sheba saw the wisdom of Solomon, and the house that he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 12:37; 1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalms 143:7"> and the food of his table, and the deportment of his servants, and the order of service of his attendants and their apparel, and his cupbearers and their apparel, and his ascent by which he went up to the house of Jehovah, there was no more spirit in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 10:6"> And she said to the king, It was a true report that I heard in mine own land of thine affairs and of thy wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 10:7; 2 Chronicles 9:5; 1 Kings 4:34"> but I gave no credit to their words, until I came and mine eyes had seen; and behold, the half of the greatness of thy wisdom was not told me: thou exceedest the report that I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:21"> Happy are thy men, and happy are these thy servants, who stand continually before thee and hear thy wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11; 1 Chronicles 29:23; Isaiah 32:1–2"> Blessed be Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, who delighted in thee, to set thee on his throne, to be king to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God! Because thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God loved Israel, to establish them for ever, therefore did he make thee king over them, to do judgment and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> And she gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, and spices in very great abundance, and precious stones; neither was there any such spice as that which the queen of Sheba gave to king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:18; 1 Kings 10:11; 1 Kings 10:22"> (And the servants also of Huram, and the servants of Solomon, who brought gold from Ophir, brought sandal-wood and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 10:12; 1 Chronicles 23:5; Psalms 92:1–3"> And the king made of the sandal-wood stairs for the house of Jehovah, and for the king's house, and harps and lutes for the singers. And there were none such seen before in the land of Judah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:13; Ephesians 3:20; Psalms 20:4"> And king Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all her desire, whatever she asked, besides what she had brought to the king. And she turned and went to her own land, she and her servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:15; Psalms 68:29; 1 Kings 10:14–28"> And the weight of gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:29; Psalms 72:10"> besides &#91;what&#93; dealers and merchants brought, and &#91;what&#93; all the kings of Arabia and the governors of the country brought of gold and silver to Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> And king Solomon made two hundred targets of beaten gold, — he applied six hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of beaten gold to one target;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2"> and three hundred shields of beaten gold, — he applied three hundred &#91;shekels&#93; of gold to one shield; and the king put them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 10:18–20; Psalms 45:8; Revelation 20:11"> And the king made a great throne of ivory, and overlaid it with pure gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Revelation 5:5; Numbers 23:24"> and the throne had six steps, with a footstool of gold fastened to the throne; and there were arms on each side at the place of the seat, and two lions stood beside the arms;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 21:12"> and twelve lions stood there on the one side and on the other upon the six steps: there was not the like made in any kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 10:21; Esther 1:7; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> And all king Solomon's drinking vessels were of gold, and all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were of precious gold: silver was not of the least account in the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Job 39:13; 1 Kings 10:22"> For the king's ships went to Tarshish with the servants of Huram: once in three years came the ships of Tarshish, bringing gold and silver, ivory, and apes, and peacocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; Psalms 89:27; 1 Kings 4:30–31"> And king Solomon was greater than all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:5; James 3:17; Proverbs 2:6"> And all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God had put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 10:25; Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10"> And they brought every man his present, vessels of silver and vessels of gold, and clothing, armour, and spices, horses and mules, a rate year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> And Solomon had four thousand stalls for horses and chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen; and he placed them in the chariot-cities, and with the king at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 4:21; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 4:24"> And he ruled over all the kings from the river as far as the land of the Philistines, and up to the border of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> And the king made silver in Jerusalem as stones, and cedars made he as the sycamores that are in the lowland for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:16; 1 Kings 10:28; Isaiah 31:1"> And they brought to Solomon horses out of Egypt, and out of all lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 11:41–43; 1 Kings 11:29"> And the rest of the acts of Solomon first and last, are they not written in the words of Nathan the prophet, and in the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Iddo the seer concerning Jeroboam the son of Nebat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 11:42–43"> And Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Solomon slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David his father; and Rehoboam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
