<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now there was a certain &#91;man&#93; sick, Lazarus of Bethany, of the village of Mary and Martha her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> It was &#91;the&#93; Mary who anointed the Lord with ointment and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> The sisters therefore sent to him, saying, Lord, behold, he whom thou lovest is sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> But when Jesus heard &#91;it&#93;, he said, This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, that the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God may be glorified by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> When therefore he heard, He is sick, he remained two days then in the place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after this he says to his disciples, Let us go into Judaea again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples say to him, Rabbi, &#91;even but&#93; now the Jews sought to stone thee, and goest thou thither again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered, Are there not twelve hours in the day? If any one walk in the day, he does not stumble, because he sees the light of this world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> but if any one walk in the night, he stumbles, because the light is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> These things said he; and after this he says to them, Lazarus, our friend, is fallen asleep, but I go that I may awake him out of sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The disciples therefore said to him, Lord, if he be fallen asleep, he will get well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> But Jesus spoke of his death, but they thought that he spoke of the rest of sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Jesus therefore then said to them plainly, Lazarus has died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> And I rejoice on your account that I was not there, in order that ye may believe. But let us go to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Thomas therefore, called Didymus, said to his fellow disciples, Let us also go, that we may die with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> Jesus therefore &#91;on&#93; arriving found him to have been four days already in the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> and many of the Jews came to Martha and Mary, that they might console them concerning their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Martha then, when she heard Jesus is coming, went to meet him; but Mary sat in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha therefore said to Jesus, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> but even now I know, that whatsoever thou shalt ask of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.22 Elohim">c</a></sup>God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.22 Elohim">d</a></sup>God will give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus says to her, Thy brother shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha says to him, I know that he will rise again in the resurrection in the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her, I am the resurrection and the life: he that believes on me, though he have died, shall live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> and every one who lives and believes on me shall never die. Believest thou this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She says to him, Yea, Lord; I believe that thou art the Christ, the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.27 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, who should come into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And having said this, she went away and called her sister Mary secretly, saying, The teacher is come and calls thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> She, when she heard &#91;that&#93;, rises up quickly and comes to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> Now Jesus had not yet come into the village, but was in the place where Martha came to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> The Jews therefore who were with her in the house and consoling her, seeing Mary that she rose up quickly and went out, followed her, saying, She goes to the tomb, that she may weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Mary therefore, when she came where Jesus was, seeing him, fell at his feet, saying to him, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> Jesus therefore, when he saw her weeping, and the Jews who came with her weeping, was deeply moved in spirit, and was troubled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> and said, Where have ye put him? They say to him, Lord, come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> The Jews therefore said, Behold how he loved him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> And some of them said, Could not this &#91;man&#93;, who has opened the eyes of the blind &#91;man&#93;, have caused that this &#91;man&#93; also should not have died?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus therefore, again deeply moved in himself, comes to the tomb. Now it was a cave, and a stone lay upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus says, Take away the stone. Martha, the sister of the dead, says to him, Lord, he stinks already, for he is four days &#91;there&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus says to her, Did I not say to thee, that if thou shouldest believe, thou shouldest see the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.40 Elohim">f</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> They took therefore the stone away. And Jesus lifted up his eyes on high and said, Father, I thank thee that thou hast heard me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> but I knew that thou always hearest me; but on account of the crowd who stand around I have said &#91;it&#93;, that they may believe that thou hast sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> And having said this, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> And the dead came forth, bound feet and hands with graveclothes, and his face was bound round with a handkerchief. Jesus says to them, Loose him and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Many therefore of the Jews who came to Mary and saw what he had done, believed on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> but some of them went to the Pharisees and told them what Jesus had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> The chief priests, therefore, and the Pharisees gathered a council, and said, What do we? for this man does many signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we let him thus alone, all will believe on him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> But a certain one of them, Caiaphas, being high priest that year, said to them, Ye know nothing</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> nor consider that it is profitable for you that one man die for the people, and not that the whole nation perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> But this he did not say of himself; but, being high priest that year, prophesied that Jesus was going to die for the nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not for the nation only, but that he should also gather together into one the children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="11.52 Elohim">g</a></sup>God who were scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> From that day therefore they took counsel that they might kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Jesus therefore walked no longer openly among the Jews, but went away thence into the country near the desert, to a city called Ephraim, and there he sojourned with the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> But the passover of the Jews was near, and many went up to Jerusalem out of the country before the passover, that they might purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> They sought therefore Jesus, and said among themselves, standing in the temple, What do ye think? that he will not come to the feast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had given commandment that if any one knew where he was, he should make it known, that they might take him.</span>
