<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:32; Matthew 26:36; 2 Samuel 15:23"> Jesus, having said these things, went out with his disciples beyond the torrent Cedron, where was a garden, into which he entered, he and his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Luke 22:39; Mark 11:11–12"> And Judas also, who delivered him up, knew the place, because Jesus was often there, in company with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:12; Luke 22:47–53; Psalms 22:12"> Judas therefore, having got the band, and officers of the chief priests and Pharisees, comes there with lanterns and torches and weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 13:1; John 18:7; Psalms 27:3"> Jesus therefore, knowing all things that were coming upon him, went forth and said to them, Whom seek ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 19:19; Matthew 2:23"> They answered him, Jesus the Nazaraean. Jesus says to them, I am &#91;he&#93;. And Judas also, who delivered him up, stood with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 27:2; Psalms 70:2–3; 2 Kings 1:9–15"> When therefore he said to them, I am &#91;he&#93;, they went away backward and fell to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 18:4"> He demanded of them therefore again, Whom seek ye? And they said, Jesus the Nazaraean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:28; Matthew 26:56; 2 Corinthians 12:9"> Jesus answered, I told you that I am &#91;he&#93;: if therefore ye seek me, let these go away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:12"> that the word might be fulfilled which he spoke, &#91;As to&#93; those whom thou hast given me, I have not lost one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 14:47; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> Simon Peter therefore, having a sword, drew it, and smote the bondman of the high priest and cut off his right ear; and the bondman's name was Malchus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 20:22; Matthew 26:39; John 20:17"> Jesus therefore said to Peter, Put the sword into the sheath; the cup which the Father has given me, shall I not drink it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:57; John 18:3; Acts 21:37"> The band therefore, and the chiliarch, and the officers of the Jews, took Jesus and bound him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:24; John 11:51; Matthew 26:3"> and they led him away to Annas first; for he was father-in-law to Caiaphas, who was high priest that year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:49–52"> But it was Caiaphas who counselled the Jews that it was better that one man should perish for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:54; Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:58–68"> Now Simon Peter followed Jesus, and the other disciple. But that disciple was known to the high priest, and went in with Jesus into the palace of the high priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:69; Mark 14:66–68; Luke 22:55–57"> but Peter stood at the door without. The other disciple therefore, who was known to the high priest, went out and spoke to the porteress and brought in Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:69–70; John 21:15; Luke 22:56–57"> The maid therefore, who was porteress, says to Peter, Art thou also of the disciples of this man? He says, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:54; John 18:25; Mark 14:67"> But the bondmen and officers, having made a fire of coals (for it was cold), stood and warmed themselves; and Peter was standing with them and warming himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:55–65; Luke 20:20; Luke 22:63–71"> The high priest therefore demanded of Jesus concerning his disciples and concerning his doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:26; Isaiah 48:16; Matthew 4:23"> Jesus answered him, I spoke openly to the world; I taught always in &#91;the&#93; synagogue and in the temple, where all the Jews come together, and in secret I have spoken nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:12–13; Luke 22:67"> Why demandest thou of me? Demand of those who have heard, what I have spoken to them; behold, they know what I have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:3; Mark 14:65; Luke 22:63–64"> But as he said these things, one of the officers who stood by gave a blow on the face to Jesus, saying, Answerest thou the high priest thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:20–23; 2 Corinthians 10:1; Matthew 5:39"> Jesus answered him, If I have spoken evil, bear witness of the evil; but if well, why smitest thou me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:13; Matthew 26:57"> Annas &#91;then&#93; had sent him bound to Caiaphas the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 14:67–72; John 18:17–18; Genesis 18:15"> But Simon Peter was standing and warming himself. They said therefore to him, Art thou also of his disciples? He denied, and said, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 18:1; John 18:10; Matthew 26:73"> One of the bondmen of the high priest, who was kinsman of him whose ear Peter cut off, says, Did not I see thee in the garden with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:71–72; Luke 22:60–62"> Peter denied therefore again, and immediately &#91;the&#93; cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 18:33; John 19:9; Matthew 27:27"> They lead therefore Jesus from Caiaphas to the praetorium; and it was early morn. And they entered not into the praetorium, that they might not be defiled, but eat the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 27:11–14"> Pilate therefore went out to them and said, What accusation do ye bring against this man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 3:13; Luke 20:19–26; Mark 10:33"> They answered and said to him, If this &#91;man&#93; were not an evildoer, we should not have delivered him up to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:6–7; John 19:15; Acts 25:18–20"> Pilate therefore said to them, Take him, ye, and judge him according to your law. The Jews therefore said to him, It is not permitted to us to put any one to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; Matthew 20:19; Matthew 26:2"> that the word of Jesus might be fulfilled which he spoke, signifying what death he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 23:3–4; John 18:37; John 19:3"> Pilate therefore entered again into the praetorium and called Jesus, and said to him, Thou art the king of the Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Jesus answered &#91;him&#93;, Dost thou say this of thyself, or have others said it to thee concerning me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 18:14–16; Nehemiah 4:2; Romans 3:1–2"> Pilate answered, Am I a Jew? Thy nation and the chief priests have delivered thee up to me: what hast thou done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 17:20–21; Colossians 1:12–14; Romans 14:17"> Jesus answered, My kingdom is not of this world; if my kingdom were of this world, my servants had fought that I might not be delivered up to the Jews; but now my kingdom is not from hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; John 8:47; John 8:14"> Pilate therefore said to him, Thou art then a king? Jesus answered, Thou sayest &#91;it&#93;, that I am a king. I have been born for this, and for this I have come into the world, that I might bear witness to the truth. Every one that is of the truth hears my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:4; Luke 23:4; John 19:6"> Pilate says to him, What is truth? And having said this he went out again to the Jews, and says to them, I find no fault whatever in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:15–18; Luke 23:16–23; Mark 15:6–14"> But ye have a custom that I release &#91;some&#93; one to you at the passover; will ye therefore that I release unto you the king of the Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Mark 15:15; Matthew 27:16"> They cried therefore again all, saying, Not this &#91;man&#93;, but Barabbas. Now Barabbas was a robber.</span>
