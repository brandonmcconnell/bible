<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> And on the third day a marriage took place in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> And Jesus also, and his disciples, were invited to the marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> And wine being deficient, the mother of Jesus says to him, They have no wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> Jesus says to her, What have I to do with thee, woman? mine hour has not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother says to the servants, Whatever he may say to you, do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Now there were standing there six stone water-vessels, according to the purification of the Jews, holding two or three measures each.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus says to them, Fill the water-vessels with water. And they filled them up to the brim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> And he says to them, Draw out now, and carry &#91;it&#93; to the feast-master. And they carried &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> But when the feast-master had tasted the water which had been made wine (and knew not whence it was, but the servants knew who drew the water), the feast-master calls the bridegroom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and says to him, Every man sets on first the good wine, and when &#91;men&#93; have well drunk, then the inferior; thou hast kept the good wine till now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This beginning of signs did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested his glory; and his disciples believed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this he descended to Capernaum, he and his mother and his brethren and his disciples; and there they abode not many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> And the passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> And he found in the temple the sellers of oxen and sheep and doves, and the money-changers sitting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> and, having made a scourge of cords, he cast &#91;them&#93; all out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen; and he poured out the change of the money-changers, and overturned the tables,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> and said to the sellers of doves, Take these things hence; make not my Father's house a house of merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> &#91;And&#93; his disciples remembered that it is written, The zeal of thy house devours me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> The Jews therefore answered and said to him, What sign shewest thou to us, that thou doest these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered and said to them, Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Jews therefore said, Forty and six years was this temple building, and thou wilt raise it up in three days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But he spoke of the temple of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> When therefore he was raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this, and believed the scripture and the word which Jesus had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> And when he was in Jerusalem, at the passover, at the feast, many believed on his name, beholding his signs which he wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus himself did not trust himself to them, because he knew all &#91;men&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and that he had not need that any should testify of man, for himself knew what was in man.</span>
