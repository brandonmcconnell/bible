<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> After these things was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> Now there is in Jerusalem, at the sheepgate, a pool, which is called in Hebrew, Bethesda, having five porches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> In these lay a multitude of sick, blind, lame, withered, &#91;awaiting the moving of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For an angel descended at a certain season in the pool and troubled the water. Whoever therefore first went in after the troubling of the water became well, whatever disease he laboured under.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> But there was a certain man there who had been suffering under his infirmity thirty and eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> Jesus seeing this &#91;man&#93; lying &#91;there&#93;, and knowing that he was &#91;in that state&#93; now a great length of time, says to him, Wouldest thou become well?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The infirm &#91;man&#93; answered him, Sir, I have not a man, in order, when the water has been troubled, to cast me into the pool; but while I am coming another descends before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Jesus says to him, Arise, take up thy couch and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> And immediately the man became well, and took up his couch and walked: and on that day was sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> The Jews therefore said to the healed &#91;man&#93;, It is sabbath, it is not permitted thee to take up thy couch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> He answered them, He that made me well, he said to me, Take up thy couch and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> They asked him &#91;therefore&#93;, Who is the man who said to thee, Take up thy couch and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> But he that had been healed knew not who it was, for Jesus had slidden away, there being a crowd in the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalms 118:18"> After these things Jesus finds him in the temple, and said to him, Behold, thou art become well: sin no more, that something worse do not happen to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> The man went away and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> And for this the Jews persecuted Jesus &#91;and sought to kill him&#93;, because he had done these things on sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> But Jesus answered them, My Father worketh hitherto and I work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> For this therefore the Jews sought the more to kill him, because he had not only violated the sabbath, but also said that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.18 Elohim">a</a></sup>God was his own Father, making himself equal with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.18 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> Jesus therefore answered and said to them, Verily, verily, I say to you, The Son can do nothing of himself save whatever he sees the Father doing: for whatever things he does, these things also the Son does in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> For the Father loves the Son and shews him all things which he himself does; and he will shew him greater works than these, that ye may wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For even as the Father raises the dead and quickens &#91;them&#93;, thus the Son also quickens whom he will:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> for neither does the Father judge any one, but has given all judgment to the Son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> that all may honour the Son, even as they honour the Father. He who honours not the Son, honours not the Father who has sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> Verily, verily, I say unto you, that he that hears my word, and believes him that has sent me, has life eternal, and does not come into judgment, but is passed out of death into life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> Verily, verily, I say unto you, that an hour is coming, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, and they that have heard shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> For even as the Father has life in himself, so he has given to the Son also to have life in himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> and has given him authority to execute judgment &#91;also&#93;, because he is Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Wonder not at this, for an hour is coming in which all who are in the tombs shall hear his voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> and shall go forth; those that have practised good, to resurrection of life, and those that have done evil, to resurrection of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> I cannot do anything of myself; as I hear, I judge, and my judgment is righteous, because I do not seek my will, but the will of him that has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> If I bear witness concerning myself, my witness is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> It is another who bears witness concerning me, and I know that the witness which he bears concerning me is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> Ye have sent unto John, and he has borne witness to the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> But I do not receive witness from man, but I say this that ye might be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> He was the burning and shining lamp, and ye were willing for a season to rejoice in his light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> But I have the witness &#91;that is&#93; greater than &#91;that&#93; of John; for the works which the Father has given me that I should complete them, the works themselves which I do, bear witness concerning me that the Father has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> And the Father who has sent me himself has borne witness concerning me. Ye have neither heard his voice at any time, nor have seen his shape,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> and ye have not his word abiding in you; for whom he hath sent, him ye do not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Ye search the scriptures, for ye think that in them ye have life eternal, and they it is which bear witness concerning me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalms 81:11"> and ye will not come to me that ye might have life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> I do not receive glory from men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> but I know you, that ye have not the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.42 Elohim">d</a></sup>God in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> I am come in my Father's name, and ye receive me not; if another come in his own name, him ye will receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> How can ye believe, who receive glory one of another, and seek not the glory which &#91;comes&#93; from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.44 Elohim">e</a></sup>God alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> Think not that I will accuse you to the Father: there is &#91;one&#93; who accuses you, Moses, on whom ye trust;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> for if ye had believed Moses, ye would have believed me, for he wrote of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> But if ye do not believe his writings, how shall ye believe my words?</span>
