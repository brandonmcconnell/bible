<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> But there was a man from among the Pharisees, his name Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> he came to him by night, and said to him, Rabbi, we know that thou art come a teacher from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, for none can do these signs that thou doest unless<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> Jesus answered and said to him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except any one be born anew he cannot see the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> Nicodemus says to him, How can a man be born being old? can he enter a second time into the womb of his mother and be born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except any one be born of water and of Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.5 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> Do not wonder that I said to thee, It is needful that ye should be born anew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> The wind blows where it will, and thou hearest its voice, but knowest not whence it comes and where it goes: thus is every one that is born of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> Nicodemus answered and said to him, How can these things be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> Jesus answered and said to him, Thou art the teacher of Israel and knowest not these things!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> Verily, verily, I say unto thee, We speak that which we know, and we bear witness of that which we have seen, and ye receive not our witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> If I have said the earthly things to you, and ye believe not, how, if I say the heavenly things to you, will ye believe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> And no one has gone up into heaven, save he who came down out of heaven, the Son of man who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, thus must the Son of man be lifted up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> that every one who believes on him may &#91;not perish, but&#93; have life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.16 Elohim">e</a></sup>God so loved the world, that he gave his only-begotten Son, that whosoever believes on him may not perish, but have life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">f</a></sup>God has not sent his Son into the world that he may judge the world, but that the world may be saved through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> He that believes on him is not judged: but he that believes not has been already judged, because he has not believed on the name of the only-begotten Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.18 Elohim">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> And this is the judgment, that light is come into the world, and men have loved darkness rather than light; for their works were evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> For every one that does evil hates the light, and does not come to the light that his works may not be shewn as they are;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Psalms 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> but he that practises the truth comes to the light, that his works may be manifested that they have been wrought in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.21 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> After these things came Jesus and his disciples into the land of Judaea; and there he abode with them and baptised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> And John also was baptising in Aenon, near Salim, because there was a great deal of water there; and they came to &#91;him&#93; and were baptised:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> for John was not yet cast into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> There was therefore a reasoning of the disciples of John with a Jew about purification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> And they came to John and said to him, Rabbi, he who was with thee beyond the Jordan, to whom thou barest witness, behold, he baptises, and all come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> John answered and said, A man can receive nothing unless it be given him out of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> Ye yourselves bear me witness that I said, I am not the Christ, but, that I am sent before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> He that has the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears him, rejoices in heart because of the voice of the bridegroom: this my joy then is fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Psalms 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> He must increase, but I must decrease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> He who comes from above is above all. He who has his origin in the earth is of the earth, and speaks &#91;as&#93; of the earth. He who comes out of heaven is above all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> &#91;and&#93; what he has seen and has heard, this he testifies; and no one receives his testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> He that has received his testimony has set to his seal that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.33 Elohim">i</a></sup>God is true;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> for he whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.34 Elohim">j</a></sup>God has sent speaks the words of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="3.34 Elohim">k</a></sup>God, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="3.34 Elohim">l</a></sup>God gives not the Spirit by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The Father loves the Son, and has given all things &#91;to be&#93; in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> He that believes on the Son has life eternal, and he that is not subject to the Son shall not see life, but the wrath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="3.36 Elohim">m</a></sup>God abides upon him.</span>
