<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Jesus therefore, six days before the passover, came to Bethany, where was the dead &#91;man&#93; Lazarus, whom Jesus raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> There therefore they made him a supper, and Martha served, but Lazarus was one of those at table with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Mary therefore, having taken a pound of ointment of pure nard of great price, anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair, and the house was filled with the odour of the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> One of his disciples therefore, Judas &#91;son&#93; of Simon, Iscariote, who was about to deliver him up, says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> Why was this ointment not sold for three hundred denarii and given to the poor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> But he said this, not that he cared for the poor, but because he was a thief and had the bag, and carried what was put into &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> Jesus therefore said, Suffer her to have kept this for the day of my preparation for burial;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> for ye have the poor always with you, but me ye have not always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> A great crowd therefore of the Jews knew that he was there; and they came, not because of Jesus only, but also that they might see Lazarus whom he raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> But the chief priests took counsel that they might kill Lazarus also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> because many of the Jews went away on his account and believed on Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the morrow a great crowd who came to the feast, having heard that Jesus is coming into Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> took branches of palms and went out to meet him, and cried, Hosanna, blessed &#91;is&#93; he that comes in the name of &#91;the&#93; Lord, the King of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> And Jesus, having found a young ass, sat upon it; as it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> Fear not, daughter of Zion: behold, thy King cometh, sitting on an ass's colt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> &#91;Now&#93; his disciples knew not these things at the first; but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written of him, and that they had done these things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> The crowd therefore that was with him bore witness because he had called Lazarus out of the tomb, and raised him from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> Therefore also the crowd met him because they had heard that he had done this sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> The Pharisees therefore said to one another, Ye see that ye profit nothing: behold, the world is gone after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> And there were certain Greeks among those who came up that they might worship in the feast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> these therefore came to Philip, who was of Bethsaida of Galilee, and they asked him saying, Sir, we desire to see Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip comes and tells Andrew, &#91;and again&#93; Andrew comes and Philip, and they tell Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> But Jesus answered them saying, The hour is come that the Son of man should be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except the grain of wheat falling into the ground die, it abides alone; but if it die, it bears much fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> He that loves his life shall lose it, and he that hates his life in this world shall keep it to life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> If any one serve me, let him follow me; and where I am, there also shall be my servant. &#91;And&#93; if any one serve me, him shall the Father honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> Now is my soul troubled, and what shall I say? Father, save me from this hour. But on account of this have I come to this hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, glorify thy name. There came therefore a voice out of heaven, I both have glorified and will glorify &#91;it&#93; again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> The crowd therefore, which stood &#91;there&#93; and heard &#91;it&#93;, said that it had thundered. Others said, An angel has spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered and said, Not on my account has this voice come, but on yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> Now is &#91;the&#93; judgment of this world; now shall the prince of this world be cast out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> and I, if I be lifted up out of the earth, will draw all to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> But this he said signifying by what death he was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The crowd answered him, We have heard out of the law that the Christ abides for ever; and how sayest thou that the Son of man must be lifted up? Who is this, the Son of man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Jesus therefore said to them, Yet a little while is the light amongst you. Walk while ye have the light, that darkness may not overtake you. And he who walks in the darkness does not know where he goes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> While ye have the light, believe in the light, that ye may become sons of light. Jesus said these things, and going away hid himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> But though he had done so many signs before them, they believed not on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> that the word of the prophet Esaias which he said might be fulfilled, Lord, who has believed our report? and to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> On this account they could not believe, because Esaias said again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> He has blinded their eyes and hardened their heart, that they may not see with their eyes, and understand with their heart and be converted, and I should heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> These things said Esaias because he saw his glory and spoke of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Although indeed from among the rulers also many believed on him, but on account of the Pharisees did not confess &#91;him&#93;, that they might not be put out of the synagogue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> for they loved glory from men rather than glory from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.43 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> But Jesus cried and said, He that believes on me, believes not on me, but on him that sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> and he that beholds me, beholds him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> I am come into the world &#91;as&#93; light, that every one that believes on me may not abide in darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> and if any one hear my words and do not keep &#91;them&#93;, I judge him not, for I am not come that I might judge the world, but that I might save the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> He that rejects me and does not receive my words, has him who judges him: the word which I have spoken, that shall judge him in the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> For I have not spoken from myself, but the Father who sent me has himself given me commandment what I should say and what I should speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> and I know that his commandment is life eternal. What therefore I speak, as the Father has said to me, so I speak.</span>
