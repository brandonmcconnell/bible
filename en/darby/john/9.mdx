<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And as he passed on, he saw a man blind from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> And his disciples asked him, saying, Rabbi, who sinned, this &#91;man&#93; or his parents, that he should be born blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, Neither has this &#91;man&#93; sinned nor his parents, but that the works of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God should be manifested in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> I must work the works of him that has sent me while it is day. &#91;The&#93; night is coming, when no one can work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> As long as I am in the world, I am &#91;the&#93; light of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> Having said these things, he spat on the ground and made mud of the spittle, and put the mud, as ointment, on his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> And he said to him, Go, wash in the pool of Siloam, which is interpreted, Sent. He went therefore and washed, and came seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> The neighbours therefore, and those who used to see him before, that he was a beggar, said, Is not this he that was sitting and begging?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some said, It is he; others said, No, but he is like him: he said, It is I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They said therefore to him, How have thine eyes been opened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered &#91;and said&#93;, A man called Jesus made mud and anointed mine eyes, and said to me, Go to Siloam and wash: and having gone and washed, I saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> They said therefore to him, Where is he? He says, I do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They bring him who was before blind to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> Now it was sabbath when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> The Pharisees therefore also again asked him how he received his sight. And he said to them, He put mud upon mine eyes, and I washed, and I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Some of the Pharisees therefore said, This man is not of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.16 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, for he does not keep the sabbath. Others said, How can a sinful man perform such signs? And there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> They say therefore again to the blind &#91;man&#93;, What dost thou say of him, that he has opened thine eyes? And he said, He is a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jews therefore did not believe concerning him that he was blind and had received sight, until they had called the parents of him that had received sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> And they asked them saying, This is your son, of whom ye say that he was born blind: how then does he now see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered &#91;them&#93; and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but how he now sees we do not know, or who has opened his eyes we do not know. He is of age: ask him; he will speak concerning himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> His parents said these things because they feared the Jews, for the Jews had already agreed that if any one confessed him &#91;to be the&#93; Christ, he should be excommunicated from the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> On this account his parents said, He is of age: ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> They called therefore a second time the man who had been blind, and said to him, Give glory to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.24 Elohim">c</a></sup>God: we know that this man is sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He answered therefore, If he is sinful I know not. One thing I know, that, being blind &#91;before&#93;, now I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they said to him again, What did he do to thee? how opened he thine eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, I told you already and ye did not hear: why do ye desire to hear again? do ye also wish to become his disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They railed at him, and said, Thou art his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.29 Elohim">d</a></sup>God spoke to Moses; but &#91;as to&#93; this &#91;man&#93;, we know not whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said to them, Now in this is a wonderful thing, that ye do not know whence he is, and he has opened mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> &#91;But&#93; we know that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.31 Elohim">e</a></sup>God does not hear sinners; but if any one be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.31 Elohim">f</a></sup>God-fearing and do his will, him he hears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since time was, it has not been heard that any one opened the eyes of one born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this &#91;man&#93; were not of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.33 Elohim">g</a></sup>God he would be able to do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said to him, Thou hast been wholly born in sins, and thou teachest us? And they cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had cast him out, and having found him, he said to him, Thou, dost thou believe on the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="9.35 Elohim">h</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and said, And who is he, Lord, that I may believe on him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> And Jesus said to him, Thou hast both seen him, and he that speaks with thee is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> And he said, I believe, Lord: and he did him homage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said, For judgment am I come into this world, that they which see not may see, and they which see may become blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> And &#91;some&#93; of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and they said to him, Are we blind also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, If ye were blind ye would not have sin; but now ye say, We see, your sin remains.</span>
