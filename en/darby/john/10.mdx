<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> Verily, verily, I say to you, He that enters not in by the door to the fold of the sheep, but mounts up elsewhere, he is a thief and a robber;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> but he that enters in by the door is &#91;the&#93; shepherd of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> To him the porter opens; and the sheep hear his voice; and he calls his own sheep by name, and leads them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> When he has put forth all his own, he goes before them, and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> But they will not follow a stranger, but will flee from him, because they know not the voice of strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalms 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> This allegory spoke Jesus to them, but they did not know what it was &#91;of&#93; which he spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> Jesus therefore said again to them, Verily, verily, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> All whoever came before me are thieves and robbers; but the sheep did not hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> I am the door: if any one enter in by me, he shall be saved, and shall go in and shall go out and shall find pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> The thief comes not but that he may steal, and kill, and destroy: I am come that they might have life, and might have &#91;it&#93; abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; Psalms 23:1; John 15:13"> I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> but he who serves for wages, and who is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming, and leaves the sheep and flees; and the wolf seizes them and scatters the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> Now he who serves for wages flees because he serves for wages, and is not himself concerned about the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> I am the good shepherd; and I know those that are mine, and am known of those that are mine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> as the Father knows me and I know the Father; and I lay down my life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> And I have other sheep which are not of this fold: those also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one flock, one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> On this account the Father loves me, because I lay down my life that I may take it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> No one takes it from me, but I lay it down of myself. I have authority to lay it down and I have authority to take it again. I have received this commandment of my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> There was a division again among the Jews on account of these words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> but many of them said, He has a demon and raves; why do ye hear him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 94:9"> Others said, These sayings are not &#91;those&#93; of one that is possessed by a demon. Can a demon open blind people's eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now the feast of the dedication was celebrating at Jerusalem, and it was winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> And Jesus walked in the temple in the porch of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> The Jews therefore surrounded him, and said to him, Until when dost thou hold our soul in suspense? If thou art the Christ, say &#91;so&#93; to us openly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus answered them, I told you, and ye do not believe. The works which I do in my Father's name, these bear witness concerning me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> but ye do not believe, for ye are not of my sheep, as I told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> and I give them life eternal; and they shall never perish, and no one shall seize them out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> My Father who has given &#91;them&#93; to me is greater than all, and no one can seize out of the hand of my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> I and the Father are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> The Jews therefore again took stones that they might stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalms 35:12"> Jesus answered them, Many good works have I shewn you of my Father; for which work of them do ye stone me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> The Jews answered him, For a good work we stone thee not, but for blasphemy, and because thou, being a man, makest thyself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.33 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalms 82:1"> Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.34 Elohim">b</a></sup>gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> If he called them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.35 Elohim">c</a></sup>gods to whom the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.35 Elohim">d</a></sup>God came (and the scripture cannot be broken),</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> do ye say of him whom the Father has sanctified and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest, because I said, I am Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.36 Elohim">e</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I do not the works of my Father, believe me not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> but if I do, even if ye believe not me, believe the works, that ye may know &#91;and believe&#93; that the Father is in me and I in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> They sought therefore again to take him; and he went away from out of their hand</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> and departed again beyond the Jordan to the place where John was baptising at the first: and he abode there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> And many came to him, and said, John did no sign; but all things which John said of this &#91;man&#93; were true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> And many believed on him there.</span>
