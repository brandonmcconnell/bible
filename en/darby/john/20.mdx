<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:60; John 19:25"> And on the first &#91;day&#93; of the week Mary of Magdala comes in early morn to the tomb, while it was still dark, and sees the stone taken away from the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> She runs therefore and comes to Simon Peter, and to the other disciple, to whom Jesus was attached, and says to them, They have taken away the Lord out of the tomb, and we know not where they have laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Peter therefore went forth, and the other disciple, and came to the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> And the two ran together, and the other disciple ran forward faster than Peter, and came first to the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> and stooping down he sees the linen cloths lying; he did not however go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Simon Peter therefore comes, following him, and entered into the tomb, and sees the linen cloths lying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> and the handkerchief which was upon his head, not lying with the linen cloths, but folded up in a distinct place by itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then entered in therefore the other disciple also who came first to the tomb, and he saw and believed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> for they had not yet known the scripture, that he must rise from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> The disciples therefore went away again to their own home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary stood at the tomb weeping without. As therefore she wept, she stooped down into the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> and beholds two angels sitting in white &#91;garments&#93;, one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> And they say to her, Woman, why dost thou weep? She says to them, Because they have taken away my Lord, and I know not where they have laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> Having said these things she turned backward and beholds Jesus standing &#91;there&#93;, and knew not that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus says to her, Woman, why dost thou weep? Whom seekest thou? She, supposing that it was the gardener, says to him, Sir, if thou hast borne him hence, tell me where thou hast laid him, and I will take him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus says to her, Mary. She, turning round, says to him in Hebrew, Rabboni, which means Teacher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Jesus says to her, Touch me not, for I have not yet ascended to my Father; but go to my brethren and say to them, I ascend to my Father and your Father, and &#91;to&#93; my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20.17 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20.17 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary of Magdala comes bringing word to the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and &#91;that&#93; he had said these things to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> When therefore it was evening on that day, which was the first &#91;day&#93; of the week, and the doors shut where the disciples were, through fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the midst, and says to them, Peace &#91;be&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> And having said this, he shewed to them his hands and his side. The disciples rejoiced therefore, having seen the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> &#91;Jesus&#93; said therefore again to them, Peace &#91;be&#93; to you: as the Father sent me forth, I also send you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> And having said this, he breathed into &#91;them&#93;, and says to them, Receive &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> whose soever sins ye remit, they are remitted to them; whose soever &#91;sins&#93; ye retain, they are retained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> But Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> The other disciples therefore said to him, We have seen the Lord. But he said to them, Unless I see in his hands the mark of the nails, and put my finger into the mark of the nails, and put my hand into his side, I will not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> And eight days after, his disciples were again within, and Thomas with them. Jesus comes, the doors being shut, and stood in the midst and said, Peace &#91;be&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then he says to Thomas, Bring thy finger here and see my hands; and bring thy hand and put it into my side; and be not unbelieving, but believing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> Thomas answered and said to him, My Lord and my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="20.28 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus says to him, Because thou hast seen me thou hast believed: blessed they who have not seen and have believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Many other signs therefore also Jesus did before his disciples, which are not written in this book;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> but these are written that ye may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="20.31 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and that believing ye might have life in his name.</span>
