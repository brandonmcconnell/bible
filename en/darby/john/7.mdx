<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 5:16–18; John 4:3; Matthew 21:38"> And after these things Jesus walked in Galilee, for he would not walk in Judaea, because the Jews sought to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:16–19; Deuteronomy 16:13–16; Exodus 23:16–17"> Now the tabernacles, the feast of the Jews, was near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; John 7:5; Luke 8:19"> His brethren therefore said to him, Remove hence and go into Judaea, that thy disciples also may see thy works which thou doest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:20; Proverbs 18:1–2; Matthew 23:5"> for no one does anything in secret and himself seeks to be &#91;known&#93; in public. If thou doest these things, manifest thyself to the world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 3:21; John 1:11–13; Micah 7:5–6"> for neither did his brethren believe on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:8; John 2:4; John 7:30"> Jesus therefore says to them, My time is not yet come, but your time is always ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 3:19; Galatians 4:16"> The world cannot hate you, but me it hates, because I bear witness concerning it that its works are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:6; John 8:20; John 7:30"> Ye, go ye up to this feast. I go not up to this feast, for my time is not yet fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Having said these things to them he abode in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Matthew 10:16; Isaiah 42:2–3"> But when his brethren had gone up, then he himself also went up to the feast, not openly, but as in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:56"> The Jews therefore sought him at the feast, and said, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:40–43; John 9:16; John 7:32"> And there was much murmuring concerning him among the crowds. Some said, He is &#91;a&#93; good &#91;man&#93;; others said, No; but he deceives the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 9:22; John 19:38; John 20:19"> However, no one spoke openly concerning him on account of &#91;their&#93; fear of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> But when it was now the middle of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> The Jews therefore wondered, saying, How knows this &#91;man&#93; letters, having never learned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> Jesus therefore answered them and said, My doctrine is not mine, but &#91;that&#93; of him that has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If any one desire to practise his will, he shall know concerning the doctrine, whether it is of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.17 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, or &#91;that&#93; I speak from myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> He that speaks from himself seeks his own glory; but he that seeks the glory of him that has sent him, he is true, and unrighteousness is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Has not Moses given you the law, and no one of you practises the law? Why do ye seek to kill me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The crowd answered &#91;and said&#93;, Thou hast a demon: who seeks to kill thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus answered and said to them, I have done one work, and ye all wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> Therefore Moses gave you circumcision (not that it is of Moses, but of the fathers), and ye circumcise a man on sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> If a man receives circumcision on sabbath, that the law of Moses may not be violated, are ye angry with me because I have made a man entirely sound on sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Judge not according to sight, but judge righteous judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Some therefore of those of Jerusalem said, Is not this he whom they seek to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> and behold, he speaks openly, and they say nothing to him. Have the rulers then indeed recognised that this is the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> But &#91;as to&#93; this &#91;man&#93; we know whence he is. Now &#91;as to&#93; the Christ, when he comes, no one knows whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching and saying, Ye both know me and ye know whence I am; and I am not come of myself, but he that sent me is true, whom ye do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> I know him, because I am from him, and he has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> They sought therefore to take him; and no one laid his hand upon him, because his hour had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> But many of the crowd believed on him, and said, Will the Christ, when he comes, do more signs than those which this &#91;man&#93; has done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things concerning him, and the Pharisees and the chief priests sent officers that they might take him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Jesus therefore said, Yet a little while I am with you, and I go to him that has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> Ye shall seek me and shall not find &#91;me&#93;, and where I am ye cannot come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> The Jews therefore said to one another, Where is he about to go that we shall not find him? Is he about to go to the dispersion among the Greeks, and teach the Greeks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What word is this which he said, Ye shall seek me and shall not find &#91;me&#93;; and where I am ye cannot come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> In the last, the great day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried saying, If any one thirst, let him come to me and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:14; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 58:11"> He that believes on me, as the scripture has said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Joel 2:28; John 14:16–17"> But this he said concerning the Spirit, which they that believed on him were about to receive; for &#91;the&#93; Spirit was not yet, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 1:21; John 6:14"> &#91;Some&#93; out of the crowd therefore, having heard this word, said, This is truly the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:52; John 1:46; John 1:49"> Others said, This is the Christ. Others said, Does then the Christ come out of Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5; Luke 2:4"> Has not the scripture said that the Christ comes of the seed of David, and from the village of Bethlehem, where David was?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> There was a division therefore in the crowd on account of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 7:30; Acts 23:11; John 8:20"> But some of them desired to take him, but no one laid hands upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> The officers therefore came to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, Why have ye not brought him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Luke 4:22; John 7:26"> The officers answered, Never man spoke thus, as this man &#91;speaks&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 7:12; 2 Corinthians 6:8; 2 Kings 18:32"> The Pharisees therefore answered them, Are ye also deceived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 12:42; 1 Corinthians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> Has any one of the rulers believed on him, or of the Pharisees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; John 9:40; John 9:34"> But this crowd, which does not know the law, are accursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:39; John 3:1–2"> Nicodemus says to them (being one of themselves),</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Deuteronomy 1:17; Deuteronomy 19:15–19"> Does our law judge a man before it have first heard from himself, and know what he does?</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 7:41; John 1:46; Isaiah 9:1–2"> They answered and said to him, Art thou also of Galilee? Search and look, that no prophet arises out of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Job 5:12–13; Psalms 76:10"> And every one went to his home.</span>
