<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Then Pilate therefore took Jesus and scourged &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> And the soldiers having plaited a crown of thorns put it on his head, and put a purple robe on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> and came to him and said, Hail, king of the Jews! and gave him blows on the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> And Pilate went out again and says to them, Lo, I bring him out to you, that ye may know that I find in him no fault whatever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> (Jesus therefore went forth without, wearing the crown of thorn, and the purple robe.) And he says to them, Behold the man!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> When therefore the chief priests and the officers saw him they cried out saying, Crucify, crucify &#91;him&#93;. Pilate says to them, Take him ye and crucify &#91;him&#93;, for I find no fault in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jews answered him, We have a law, and according to &#91;our&#93; law he ought to die, because he made himself Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When Pilate therefore heard this word, he was the rather afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and went into the praetorium again and says to Jesus, Whence art thou? But Jesus gave him no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> Pilate therefore says to him, Speakest thou not to me? Dost thou not know that I have authority to release thee and have authority to crucify thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus answered, Thou hadst no authority whatever against me if it were not given to thee from above. On this account he that has delivered me up to thee has &#91;the&#93; greater sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> From this time Pilate sought to release him; but the Jews cried out saying, If thou releasest this &#91;man&#93;, thou art not a friend to Caesar. Every one making himself a king speaks against Caesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> Pilate therefore, having heard these words, led Jesus out and sat down upon &#91;the&#93; judgment-seat, at a place called Pavement, but in Hebrew Gabbatha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> (now it was &#91;the&#93; preparation of the passover; it was about the sixth hour;) and he says to the Jews, Behold your king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> But they cried out, Take &#91;him&#93; away, take &#91;him&#93; away, crucify him. Pilate says to them, Shall I crucify your king? The chief priests answered, We have no king but Caesar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> Then therefore he delivered him up to them, that he might be crucified; and they took Jesus and led him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> And he went out, bearing his cross, to the place called &#91;place&#93; of a skull, which is called in Hebrew, Golgotha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> where they crucified him, and with him two others, &#91;one&#93; on this side, and &#91;one&#93; on that, and Jesus in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> And Pilate wrote a title also and put it on the cross. But there was written: Jesus the Nazaraean, the King of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> This title therefore many of the Jews read, for the place of the city where Jesus was crucified was near; and it was written in Hebrew, Greek, Latin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> The chief priests of the Jews therefore said to Pilate, Do not write, The king of the Jews, but that he said, I am king of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate answered, What I have written, I have written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> The soldiers therefore, when they had crucified Jesus, took his clothes, and made four parts, to each soldier a part, and the body-coat; but the body-coat was seamless, woven through the whole from the top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> They said therefore to one another, Let us not rend it, but let us cast lots for it, whose it shall be; that the scripture might be fulfilled which says, They parted my garments among themselves, and on my vesture they cast lots. The soldiers therefore did these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> And by the cross of Jesus stood his mother, and the sister of his mother, Mary the &#91;wife&#93; of Clopas, and Mary of Magdala.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> Jesus therefore, seeing his mother, and the disciple standing by, whom he loved, says to his mother, Woman, behold thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Then he says unto the disciple, Behold thy mother. And from that hour the disciple took her to his own home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> After this, Jesus, knowing that all things were now finished, that the scripture might be fulfilled, says, I thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> There was a vessel therefore there full of vinegar, and having filled a sponge with vinegar, and putting hyssop round it, they put it up to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> When therefore Jesus had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished; and having bowed his head, he delivered up his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> The Jews therefore, that the bodies might not remain on the cross on the sabbath, for it was &#91;the&#93; preparation, (for the day of that sabbath was a great &#91;day&#93;,) demanded of Pilate that their legs might be broken and they taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> The soldiers therefore came and broke the legs of the first and of the other that had been crucified with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> but coming to Jesus, when they saw that he was already dead they did not break his legs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> but one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and immediately there came out blood and water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> And he who saw it bears witness, and his witness is true, and he knows that he says true that ye also may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> For these things took place that the scripture might be fulfilled, Not a bone of him shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> And again another scripture says, They shall look on him whom they pierced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> And after these things Joseph of Arimathaea, who was a disciple of Jesus, but secretly through fear of the Jews, demanded of Pilate that he might take the body of Jesus: and Pilate allowed it. He came therefore and took away the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> And Nicodemus also, who at first came to Jesus by night, came, bringing a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about a hundred pounds &#91;weight&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> They took therefore the body of Jesus and bound it up in linen with the spices, as it is the custom with the Jews to prepare for burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> But there was in the place where he had been crucified a garden, and in the garden a new tomb in which no one had ever been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> There therefore, on account of the preparation of the Jews, because the tomb was near, they laid Jesus.</span>
