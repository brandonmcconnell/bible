<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> When therefore the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus makes and baptises more disciples than John</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (however, Jesus himself did not baptise, but his disciples),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> he left Judaea and went away again unto Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> And he must needs pass through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> He comes therefore to a city of Samaria called Sychar, near to the land which Jacob gave to his son Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> Now a fountain of Jacob's was there; Jesus therefore, being wearied with the way he had come, sat just as he was at the fountain. It was about the sixth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> A woman comes out of Samaria to draw water. Jesus says to her, Give me to drink</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> (for his disciples had gone away into the city that they might buy provisions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> The Samaritan woman therefore says to him, How dost thou, being a Jew, ask to drink of me who am a Samaritan woman? for Jews have no intercourse with Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered and said to her, If thou knewest the gift of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.10 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and who it is that says to thee, Give me to drink, thou wouldest have asked of him, and he would have given thee living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman says to him, Sir, thou hast nothing to draw with, and the well is deep: whence then hast thou the living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> Art thou greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well, and drank of it himself, and his sons, and his cattle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus answered and said to her, Every one who drinks of this water shall thirst again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> but whosoever drinks of the water which I shall give him shall never thirst for ever, but the water which I shall give him shall become in him a fountain of water, springing up into eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalms 4:6"> The woman says to him, Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst nor come here to draw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> Jesus says to her, Go, call thy husband, and come here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The woman answered and said, I have not a husband. Jesus says to her, Thou hast well said, I have not a husband;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> for thou hast had five husbands, and he whom now thou hast is not thy husband: this thou hast spoken truly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman says to him, Sir, I see that thou art a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers worshipped in this mountain, and ye say that in Jerusalem is the place where one must worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus says to her, Woman, believe me, &#91;the&#93; hour is coming when ye shall neither in this mountain nor in Jerusalem worship the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> Ye worship ye know not what; we worship what we know, for salvation is of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But &#91;the&#93; hour is coming and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and truth; for also the Father seeks such as his worshippers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.24 Elohim">b</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; a spirit; and they who worship him must worship &#91;him&#93; in spirit and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman says to him, I know that Messias is coming, who is called Christ; when he comes he will tell us all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus says to her, I who speak to thee am &#91;he&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> And upon this came his disciples, and wondered that he spoke with a woman; yet no one said, What seekest thou? or, Why speakest thou with her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> The woman then left her waterpot and went away into the city, and says to the men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> Come, see a man who told me all things I had ever done: is not he the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> They went out of the city and came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> But meanwhile the disciples asked him saying, Rabbi, eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalms 119:103; John 4:34"> But he said to them, I have food to eat which ye do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> The disciples therefore said to one another, Has any one brought him &#91;anything&#93; to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus says to them, My food is that I should do the will of him that has sent me, and that I should finish his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Do not ye say, that there are yet four months and the harvest comes? Behold, I say to you, Lift up your eyes and behold the fields, for they are already white to harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> He that reaps receives wages and gathers fruit unto life eternal, that both he that sows and he that reaps may rejoice together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> For in this is &#91;verified&#93; the true saying, It is one who sows and another who reaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I have sent you to reap that on which ye have not laboured; others have laboured, and ye have entered into their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> But many of the Samaritans of that city believed on him because of the word of the woman who bore witness, He told me all things that I had ever done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> When therefore the Samaritans came to him they asked him to abide with them, and he abode there two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And more a great deal believed on account of his word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> and they said to the woman, &#91;It is&#93; no longer on account of thy saying that we believe, for we have heard him ourselves, and we know that this is indeed the Saviour of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> But after the two days he went forth thence and went away into Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> for Jesus himself bore witness that a prophet has no honour in his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> When therefore he came into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all that he had done in Jerusalem during the feast, for they also went to the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> He came therefore again to Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain courtier in Capernaum whose son was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> He, having heard that Jesus had come out of Judaea into Galilee, went to him and asked &#91;him&#93; that he would come down and heal his son, for he was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> Jesus therefore said to him, Unless ye see signs and wonders ye will not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalms 88:10–12; Psalms 40:17"> The courtier says to him, Sir, come down ere my child die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Jesus says to him, Go, thy son lives. And the man believed the word which Jesus said to him, and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> But already, as he was going down, his servants met him and brought &#91;him&#93; word saying, Thy child lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> He inquired therefore from them the hour at which he got better. And they said to him, Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> The father therefore knew that &#91;it was&#93; in that hour in which Jesus said to him, Thy son lives; and he believed, himself and his whole house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> This second sign again did Jesus, being come out of Judaea into Galilee.</span>
