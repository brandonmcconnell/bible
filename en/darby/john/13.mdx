<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Now before the feast of the passover, Jesus, knowing that his hour had come that he should depart out of this world to the Father, having loved his own who were in the world, loved them to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> And during supper, the devil having already put it into the heart of Judas &#91;son&#93; of Simon, Iscariote, that he should deliver him up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> &#91;Jesus,&#93; knowing that the Father had given him all things into his hands, and that he came out from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and was going to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> rises from supper and lays aside his garments, and having taken a linen towel he girded himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalms 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> then he pours water into the washhand basin, and began to wash the feet of the disciples, and to wipe them with the linen towel with which he was girded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> He comes therefore to Simon Peter; and he says to him, Lord, dost thou wash my feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus answered and said to him, What I do thou dost not know now, but thou shalt know hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter says to him, Thou shalt never wash my feet. Jesus answered him, Unless I wash thee, thou hast not part with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Psalms 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter says to him, Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus says to him, He that is washed all over needs not to wash save his feet, but is wholly clean; and ye are clean, but not all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> For he knew him that delivered him up: on account of this he said, Ye are not all clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> When therefore he had washed their feet, and taken his garments, having sat down again, he said to them, Do ye know what I have done to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> Ye call me the Teacher and the Lord, and ye say well, for I am &#91;so&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> If I therefore, the Lord and the Teacher, have washed your feet, ye also ought to wash one another's feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> for I have given you an example that, as I have done to you, ye should do also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> Verily, verily, I say to you, The bondman is not greater than his lord, nor the sent greater than he who has sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> If ye know these things, blessed are ye if ye do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> I speak not of you all. I know those whom I have chosen; but that the scripture might be fulfilled, He that eats bread with me has lifted up his heel against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> I tell you &#91;it&#93; now before it happens, that when it happens, ye may believe that I am &#91;he&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> Verily, verily, I say to you, He who receives whomsoever I shall send receives me; and he that receives me receives him who has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> Having said these things, Jesus was troubled in spirit, and testified and said, Verily, verily, I say to you, that one of you shall deliver me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> The disciples therefore looked one on another, doubting of whom he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> Now there was at table one of his disciples in the bosom of Jesus, whom Jesus loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> Simon Peter makes a sign therefore to him to ask who it might be of whom he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> But he, leaning on the breast of Jesus, says to him, Lord, who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Jesus answers, He it is to whom I, after I have dipped the morsel, give it. And having dipped the morsel, he gives it to Judas &#91;son&#93; of Simon, Iscariote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And, after the morsel, then entered Satan into him. Jesus therefore says to him, What thou doest, do quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But none of those at table knew why he said this to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> for some supposed, because Judas had the bag, that Jesus was saying to him, Buy the things of which we have need for the feast; or that he should give something to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> Having therefore received the morsel, he went out immediately; and it was night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> When therefore he was gone out Jesus says, Now is the Son of man glorified, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.31 Elohim">c</a></sup>God is glorified in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.32 Elohim">d</a></sup>God be glorified in him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.32 Elohim">e</a></sup>God also shall glorify him in himself, and shall glorify him immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> Children, yet a little while I am with you. Ye shall seek me; and, as I said to the Jews, Where I go ye cannot come, I say to you also now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> A new commandment I give to you, that ye love one another; as I have loved you, that ye also love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> By this shall all know that ye are disciples of mine, if ye have love amongst yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter says to him, Lord, where goest thou? Jesus answered him, Where I go thou canst not follow me now, but thou shalt follow me after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter says to him, Lord, why cannot I follow thee now? I will lay down my life for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus answers, Thou wilt lay down thy life for me! Verily, verily, I say to thee, The cock shall not crow till thou hast denied me thrice.</span>
