<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Matthew 13:21; Matthew 26:31–33"> These things I have spoken unto you that ye may not be offended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:22; Acts 26:9–11; Romans 10:2–3"> They shall put you out of the synagogues; but the hour is coming that every one who kills you will think to render service to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:21; John 17:25; John 8:55"> and these things they will do because they have not known the Father nor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 14:29; Mark 13:23; John 17:12–13"> But I have spoken these things to you, that when their hour shall have come, ye may remember them, that I have said &#91;them&#93; unto you. But I did not say these things unto you from &#91;the&#93; beginning, because I was with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:36; John 7:33; John 16:28"> But now I go to him that has sent me, and none of you demands of me, Where goest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:1; John 16:20–22; Luke 24:17"> But because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 15:26"> But I say the truth to you, It is profitable for you that I go away; for if I do not go away, the Comforter will not come to you; but if I go I will send him to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Jude 1:15; Zechariah 12:10"> And having come, he will bring demonstration to the world, of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:22–25; John 8:23–24; John 12:47–48"> of sin, because they do not believe on me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 10:3–4"> of righteousness, because I go away to &#91;my&#93; Father, and ye behold me no longer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 3:8; Hebrews 2:14"> of judgment, because the ruler of this world is judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:33; John 15:15; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2"> I have yet many things to say to you, but ye cannot bear them now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10–13; John 14:17"> But when he is come, the Spirit of truth, he shall guide you into all the truth: for he shall not speak from himself; but whatsoever he shall hear he shall speak; and he will announce to you what is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:13–14; 2 Corinthians 3:14–18; John 15:26"> He shall glorify me, for he shall receive of mine and shall announce &#91;it&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 17:10; Matthew 11:27; John 3:35"> All things that the Father has are mine; on account of this I have said that he receives of mine and shall announce &#91;it&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:33; Acts 1:3; John 14:18–24"> A little while and ye do not behold me; and again a little while and ye shall see me, &#91;because I go away to the Father&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:16; Mark 9:32; John 16:5"> &#91;Some&#93; of his disciples therefore said to one another, What is this he says to us, A little while and ye do not behold me; and again a little while and ye shall see me, and, Because I go away to the Father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:25; Matthew 16:9–11; Hebrews 5:12"> They said therefore, What is this which he says &#91;of&#93; the little while? We do not know &#91;of&#93; what he speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:30; John 16:16; John 14:19"> Jesus knew therefore that they desired to demand of him, and said to them, Do ye inquire of this among yourselves that I said, A little while and ye do not behold me; and again a little while and ye shall see me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:2; Matthew 5:4; Psalms 126:5–6"> Verily, verily, I say to you, that ye shall weep and lament, ye, but the world shall rejoice; and ye will be grieved, but your grief shall be turned to joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> A woman, when she gives birth to a child, has grief because her hour has come; but when the child is born, she no longer remembers the trouble, on account of the joy that a man has been born into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Acts 13:52; John 16:6"> And ye now therefore have grief; but I will see you again, and your heart shall rejoice, and your joy no one takes from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 15:7; John 14:13–14; Matthew 21:22"> And in that day ye shall demand nothing of me: verily, verily, I say to you, Whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in my name, he will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; James 4:2–3; John 15:11"> Hitherto ye have asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:12; Mark 4:13; Matthew 13:34–35"> These things I have spoken to you in allegories; the hour is coming that I will no longer speak to you in allegories, but will declare to you openly concerning the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 8:34; John 17:24; John 17:19"> In that day ye shall ask in my name; and I say not to you that I will demand of the Father for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:23; John 17:23"> for the Father himself has affection for you, because ye have had affection for me, and have believed that I came out from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.27 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:1; John 13:3; John 8:14"> I came out from the Father and have come into the world; again, I leave the world and go to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 16:25"> His disciples say to him, Lo, now thou speakest openly and utterest no allegory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:17; John 17:8; John 16:17"> Now we know that thou knowest all things, and hast not need that any one should demand of thee. By this we believe that thou art come from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.30 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 9:44–45; John 13:38"> Jesus answered them, Do ye now believe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:31; John 8:29"> Behold, &#91;the&#93; hour is coming, and has come, that ye shall be scattered, each to his own, and shall leave me alone; and &#91;yet&#93; I am not alone, for the Father is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 14:27; 1 John 5:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> These things have I spoken to you that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye have tribulation; but be of good courage: I have overcome the world.</span>
