<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> But Jesus went to the mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> And early in the morning he came again into the temple, and all the people came to him; and he sat down and taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the scribes and the Pharisees bring &#91;to him&#93; a woman taken in adultery, and having set her in the midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> they say to him, Teacher, this woman has been taken in the very act, committing adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> Now in the law Moses has commanded us to stone such; thou therefore, what sayest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> But this they said proving him, that they might have &#91;something&#93; to accuse him &#91;of&#93;. But Jesus, having stooped down, wrote with his finger on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> But when they continued asking him, he lifted himself up and said to them, Let him that is without sin among you first cast the stone at her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And again stooping down he wrote on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> But they, having heard &#91;that&#93;, went out one by one beginning from the elder ones until the last; and Jesus was left alone and the woman standing there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> And Jesus, lifting himself up and seeing no one but the woman, said to her, Woman, where are those thine accusers? Has no one condemned thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> And she said, No one, sir. And Jesus said to her, Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Again therefore Jesus spoke to them, saying, I am the light of the world; he that follows me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> The Pharisees therefore said to him, Thou bearest witness concerning thyself; thy witness is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered and said to them, Even if I bear witness concerning myself, my witness is true, because I know whence I came and whither I go: but ye know not whence I come and whither I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> Ye judge according to the flesh, I judge no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> And if also I judge, my judgment is true, because I am not alone, but I and the Father who has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> And in your law too it is written that the testimony of two men is true:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am &#91;one&#93; who bear witness concerning myself, and the Father who has sent me bears witness concerning me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> They said to him therefore, Where is thy Father? Jesus answered, Ye know neither me nor my Father. If ye had known me, ye would have known also my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> These words spoke he in the treasury, teaching in the temple; and no one took him, for his hour was not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> He said therefore again to them, I go away, and ye shall seek me, and shall die in your sin; where I go ye cannot come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> The Jews therefore said, Will he kill himself, that he says, Where I go ye cannot come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> And he said to them, Ye are from beneath; I am from above. Ye are of this world; I am not of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> I said therefore to you, that ye shall die in your sins; for unless ye shall believe that I am &#91;he&#93;, ye shall die in your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> They said therefore to him, Who art thou? &#91;And&#93; Jesus said to them, Altogether that which I also say to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> I have many things to say and to judge concerning you, but he that has sent me is true, and I, what I have heard from him, these things I say to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> They knew not that he spoke to them of the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Jesus therefore said to them, When ye shall have lifted up the Son of man, then ye shall know that I am &#91;he&#93;, and &#91;that&#93; I do nothing of myself, but as the Father has taught me I speak these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> And he that has sent me is with me; he has not left me alone, because I do always the things that are pleasing to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> As he spoke these things many believed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Jesus therefore said to the Jews who believed him, If ye abide in my word, ye are truly my disciples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> and ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall set you free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> They answered him, We are Abraham's seed, and have never been under bondage to any one; how sayest thou, Ye shall become free?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus answered them, Verily, verily, I say to you, Every one that practises sin is the bondman of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> Now the bondman abides not in the house for ever: the son abides for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> If therefore the Son shall set you free, ye shall be really free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> I know that ye are Abraham's seed; but ye seek to kill me, because my word has no entrance in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I speak what I have seen with my Father, and ye then do what ye have seen with your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> They answered and said to him, Abraham is our father. Jesus says to them, If ye were Abraham's children, ye would do the works of Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> but now ye seek to kill me, a man who has spoken the truth to you, which I have heard from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.40 Elohim">a</a></sup>God: this did not Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> Ye do the works of your father. They said &#91;therefore&#93; to him, We are not born of fornication; we have one father,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.41 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus said to them, If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.42 Elohim">c</a></sup>God were your father ye would have loved me, for I came forth from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.42 Elohim">d</a></sup>God and am come &#91;from him&#93;; for neither am I come of myself, but he has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Why do ye not know my speech? Because ye cannot hear my word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> Ye are of the devil, as &#91;your&#93; father, and ye desire to do the lusts of your father. He was a murderer from the beginning, and has not stood in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks falsehood, he speaks of what is his own; for he is a liar and its father:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> and because I speak the truth, ye do not believe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Which of you convinces me of sin? If I speak truth, why do ye not believe me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> He that is of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.47 Elohim">e</a></sup>God hears the words of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.47 Elohim">f</a></sup>God: therefore ye hear &#91;them&#93; not, because ye are not of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="8.47 Elohim">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> The Jews answered and said to him, Say we not well that thou art a Samaritan and hast a demon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jesus answered, I have not a demon; but I honour my Father, and ye dishonour me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> But I do not seek my own glory: there is he that seeks and judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> Verily, verily, I say unto you, If any one shall keep my word, he shall never see death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> The Jews therefore said to him, Now we know that thou hast a demon. Abraham has died, and the prophets, and thou sayest, If any one keep my word, he shall never taste death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> Art thou greater than our father Abraham, who has died? and the prophets have died: whom makest thou thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus answered, If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing: it is my Father who glorifies me, &#91;of&#93; whom ye say, He is our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="8.54 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> And ye know him not; but I know him; and if I said, I know him not, I should be like you, a liar. But I know him, and I keep his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Your father Abraham exulted in that he should see my day, and he saw and rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> The Jews therefore said to him, Thou hast not yet fifty years, and hast thou seen Abraham?</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Before Abraham was, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> They took up therefore stones that they might cast &#91;them&#93; at him; but Jesus hid himself and went out of the temple, &#91;going through the midst of them, and thus passed on.&#93;</span>
