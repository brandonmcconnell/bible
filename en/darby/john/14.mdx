<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; John 13:36; Hebrews 13:14"> In my Father's house there are many abodes; were it not so, I had told you: for I go to prepare you a place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; John 12:26; 2 Timothy 2:12"> and if I go and shall prepare you a place, I am coming again and shall receive you to myself, that where I am ye also may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:9; John 16:28; John 13:3"> And ye know where I go, and ye know the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 11:16; Mark 9:19; John 20:25–28"> Thomas says to him, Lord, we know not where thou goest, and how can we know the way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:12; John 10:9; Ephesians 2:18"> Jesus says to him, I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father unless by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; John 8:19; Luke 10:22"> If ye had known me, ye would have known also my Father, and henceforth ye know him and have seen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:18–23; Revelation 22:3–5; John 1:43–46"> Philip says to him, Lord, shew us the Father and it suffices us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 12:45; John 14:7"> Jesus says to him, Am I so long a time with you, and thou hast not known me, Philip? He that has seen me has seen the Father; and how sayest thou, Shew us the Father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 5:19; John 10:38; John 10:30"> Believest thou not that I &#91;am&#93; in the Father, and that the Father is in me? The words which I speak to you I do not speak from myself; but the Father who abides in me, he does the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:38; John 10:25; John 5:36"> Believe me that I &#91;am&#93; in the Father and the Father in me; but if not, believe me for the works' sake themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:21; Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19"> Verily, verily, I say to you, He that believes on me, the works which I do shall he do also, and he shall do greater than these, because I go to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:7; Mark 11:24; Matthew 7:7"> And whatsoever ye shall ask in my name, this will I do, that the Father may be glorified in the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If ye shall ask anything in my name, I will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:3–5; 1 John 5:2–3; John 14:21–24"> If ye love me, keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:26; John 15:26; John 16:7–15"> And I will beg the Father, and he will give you another Comforter, that he may be with you for ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; John 16:13; 1 John 2:27"> the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it does not see him nor know him; but ye know him, for he abides with you, and shall be in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 14:3; Psalms 23:4; Matthew 18:20"> I will not leave you orphans, I am coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:16; John 7:33; Hebrews 7:25"> Yet a little and the world sees me no longer; but ye see me; because I live ye also shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 15:5–7; Galatians 2:20; John 17:21–23"> In that day ye shall know that I &#91;am&#93; in my Father, and ye in me, and I in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:5"> He that has my commandments and keeps them, he it is that loves me; but he that loves me shall be loved by my Father, and I will love him and will manifest myself to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 6:16; Matthew 10:3"> Judas, not the Iscariote, says to him, Lord, how is it that thou wilt manifest thyself to us and not to the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:15; Revelation 3:20–21"> Jesus answered and said to him, If any one love me, he will keep my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come to him and make our abode with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 7:16; John 14:10; John 8:42"> He that loves me not does not keep my words; and the word which ye hear is not mine, but &#91;that&#93; of the Father who has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:6–8; John 13:19; John 16:12"> These things I have said to you, abiding with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:26; John 14:16; 1 John 2:27"> but the Comforter, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and will bring to your remembrance all the things which I have said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; Isaiah 41:10"> I leave peace with you; I give my peace to you: not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:29–30; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Ye have heard that I have said unto you, I go away and I am coming to you. If ye loved me ye would rejoice that I go to the Father, for &#91;my&#93; Father is greater than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 13:19; John 16:4–31; Matthew 24:24–25"> And now I have told you before it comes to pass, that when it shall have come to pass ye may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:19; 1 John 4:4; Revelation 12:9"> I will no longer speak much with you, for the ruler of the world comes, and in me he has nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 10:18; Philippians 2:8; John 12:49"> but that the world may know that I love the Father, and as the Father has commanded me, thus I do. Rise up, let us go hence.</span>
