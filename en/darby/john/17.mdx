<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:4–5; Philippians 2:9–11"> These things Jesus spoke, and lifted up his eyes to heaven and said, Father, the hour is come; glorify thy Son, that thy Son may glorify thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:28–29; John 6:39; Daniel 7:14"> as thou hast given him authority over all flesh, that &#91;as to&#93; all that thou hast given to him, he should give them life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:20; 1 John 5:11–12; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> And this is the eternal life, that they should know thee, the only true<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="17.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and Jesus Christ whom thou hast sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:34; Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:7"> I have glorified thee on the earth, I have completed the work which thou gavest me that I should do it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:15–17; John 10:30"> and now glorify me, thou Father, along with thyself, with the glory which I had along with thee before the world was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 17:26; John 14:21–24; 1 John 5:20"> I have manifested thy name to the men whom thou gavest me out of the world. They were thine, and thou gavest them me, and they have kept thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> Now they have known that all things that thou hast given me are of thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 17:14; John 15:15; John 16:30"> for the words which thou hast given me I have given them, and they have received &#91;them&#93;, and have known truly that I came out from thee, and have believed that thou sentest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> I demand concerning them; I do not demand concerning the world, but concerning those whom thou hast given me, for they are thine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> (and all that is mine is thine, and &#91;all&#93; that is thine mine,) and I am glorified in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> And I am no longer in the world, and these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep them in thy name which thou hast given me, that they may be one as we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> When I was with them I kept them in thy name; those thou hast given me I have guarded, and not one of them has perished, but the son of perdition, that the scripture might be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> And now I come to thee. And these things I speak in the world, that they may have my joy fulfilled in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> I have given them thy word, and the world has hated them, because they are not of the world, as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> I do not demand that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them out of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 17:14"> They are not of the world, as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalms 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> Sanctify them by the truth: thy word is truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> As thou hast sent me into the world, I also have sent them into the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> and I sanctify myself for them, that they also may be sanctified by truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:6–11; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4"> And I do not demand for these only, but also for those who believe on me through their word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12; Colossians 3:11–14; John 17:11"> that they may be all one, as thou, Father, &#91;art&#93; in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us, that the world may believe that thou hast sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> And the glory which thou hast given me I have given them, that they may be one, as we are one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:21; John 16:27; 1 John 3:1"> I in them and thou in me, that they may be perfected into one &#91;and&#93; that the world may know that thou hast sent me, and &#91;that&#93; thou hast loved them as thou hast loved me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 17:5; John 12:26; John 14:3"> Father, &#91;as to&#93; those whom thou hast given me, I desire that where I am they also may be with me, that they may behold my glory which thou hast given me, for thou lovedst me before &#91;the&#93; foundation of &#91;the&#93; world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:11; John 7:29; John 17:8"> Righteous Father, — and the world has not known thee, but I have known thee, and these have known that thou hast sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 17:23; John 17:6; John 15:9"> And I have made known to them thy name, and will make &#91;it&#93; known; that the love with which thou hast loved me may be in them and I in them.</span>
