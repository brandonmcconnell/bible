<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Colossians 1:10; John 15:16"> I am the vine, ye &#91;are&#93; the branches. He that abides in me and I in him, he bears much fruit; for without me ye can do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Hebrews 6:7–8; Matthew 13:41"> Unless any one abide in me he is cast out as the branch, and is dried up; and they gather them and cast them into the fire, and they are burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; 1 John 5:14; John 14:13"> If ye abide in me, and my words abide in you, ye shall ask what ye will and it shall come to pass to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:31; Matthew 5:16; Philippians 1:11"> In this is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit, and ye shall become disciples of mine.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 3:21–24"> If ye shall keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love, as I have kept my Father's commandments and abide in his love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 17:13; Romans 15:13; John 16:24"> I have spoken these things to you that my joy may be in you, and your joy be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:21; 1 Peter 4:8; John 13:34"> This is my commandment, that ye love one another, as I have loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 10:11; Ephesians 5:2; Romans 5:6–8"> No one has greater love than this, that one should lay down his life for his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:50; 1 John 5:3; James 2:23"> Ye are my friends if ye practise whatever I command you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 2:9–12; Matthew 13:11"> I call you no longer bondmen, for the bondman does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends, for all things which I have heard of my Father I have made known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and have set you that ye should go and &#91;that&#93; ye should bear fruit, and &#91;that&#93; your fruit should abide, that whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in my name he may give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> These things I command you, that ye love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 7:7; Mark 13:13"> If the world hate you, know that it has hated me before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:4–5; John 17:14–16; Ephesians 1:4–11"> If ye were of the world, the world would love its own; but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, on account of this the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> Remember the word which I said unto you, The bondman is not greater than his master. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you; if they have kept my word, they will keep also yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> But they will do all these things to you on account of my name, because they have not known him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> If I had not come and spoken to them, they had not had sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 8:40–42; 2 John 1:9; 1 John 2:23"> He that hates me hates also my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:36; John 14:9; John 7:31"> If I had not done among them the works which no other one has done, they had not had sin; but now they have both seen and hated both me and my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 109:3; Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:19"> But that the word written in their law might be fulfilled, They hated me without a cause.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 21:24; Acts 10:39–42"> and ye too bear witness, because ye are with me from &#91;the&#93; beginning.</span>
