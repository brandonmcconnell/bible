<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 16:35; 2 Kings 1:12"> And it came to pass that when the people murmured, it was evil in the ears of Jehovah; and Jehovah heard it, and his anger was kindled, and the fire of Jehovah burned among them, and consumed &#91;some&#93; in the extremity of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 21:7; James 5:16; Numbers 16:45–48"> And the people cried to Moses; and Moses prayed to Jehovah — and the fire abated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:22"> And they called the name of that place Taberah; because a fire of Jehovah burned among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Exodus 12:38; Psalms 106:14"> And the mixed multitude that was among them lusted; and the children of Israel also wept again and said, Who will give us flesh to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:3; Psalms 17:14; Philippians 3:19"> We remember the fish that we ate in Egypt for nothing; the cucumbers, and the melons, and the leeks, and the onions, and the garlic;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; 2 Samuel 13:4"> and now our soul is dried up: there is nothing at all but the manna before our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 16:31; Genesis 2:12; Exodus 16:14–15"> And the manna was as coriander seed, and its appearance as the appearance of bdellium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 16:31; Exodus 16:16–18; Exodus 16:23"> The people went about, and gathered it, and ground it with hand-mills, or beat it in mortars, and boiled it in pots, and made cakes of it; and the taste of it was as the taste of oil-cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 16:13–14; Psalms 78:23–25; Deuteronomy 32:2"> And when the dew fell upon the camp by night, the manna fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 78:21; Numbers 16:27; Numbers 14:1–2"> And Moses heard the people weep throughout their families, every one at the entrance of his tent; and the anger of Jehovah was kindled greatly; it was also evil in the eyes of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:12; Exodus 5:22; Jeremiah 15:10"> And Moses said to Jehovah, Why hast thou done evil to thy servant, and why have I not found favour in thine eyes, that thou layest the burden of all this people upon me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 40:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> Have I conceived all this people, have I brought them forth, that thou sayest to me, Carry them in thy bosom, as the nursing-father beareth the suckling, unto the land which thou didst swear unto their fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Mark 8:4; John 6:5–9"> Whence should I have flesh to give unto all this people? for they weep unto me, saying, Give us flesh that we may eat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Deuteronomy 1:9–12; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> I am not able to bear all this people alone, for it is too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3"> And if thou deal thus with me, slay me, I pray thee, if I have found favour in thine eyes, that I may not behold my wretchedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:1; Exodus 24:9; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Gather unto me seventy men of the elders of Israel, whom thou knowest to be the elders of the people, and their officers; and take them to the tent of meeting, and they shall stand there with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; 1 Samuel 10:6; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> And I will come down and talk with thee there; and I will take of the Spirit which is upon thee, and will put it upon them; and they shall bear the burden of the people with thee, and thou shalt not bear it alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:10; Acts 7:39; Exodus 16:3–7"> And unto the people shalt thou say, Hallow yourselves for to-morrow, and ye shall eat flesh; for ye have wept in the ears of Jehovah, saying, Who will give us flesh to eat? for it was well with us in Egypt; and Jehovah will give you flesh, and ye shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Not one day shall ye eat, nor two days, nor five days, neither ten days, nor twenty days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 21:5; Psalms 78:27–30"> &#91;but&#93; for a whole month, until it come out at your nostrils, and it become loathsome unto you; because that ye have despised Jehovah who is among you, and have wept before him, saying, Why came we forth out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 1:46; Genesis 12:2"> And Moses said, The people in whose midst I am are six hundred thousand footmen; and thou sayest, I will give them flesh that they may eat a whole month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; 2 Kings 7:2; John 6:9"> Shall flocks and herds be slaughtered for them, to suffice them? or shall all the fish of the sea be gathered for them, to suffice them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:1; Isaiah 50:2; Genesis 18:14"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Hath Jehovah's hand become short? Now shalt thou see whether my word will come to pass unto thee or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 11:16; Numbers 11:26"> And Moses went out and told the people the words of Jehovah; and he gathered the seventy men of the elders of the people, and set them round about the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Joel 2:28–29; 1 Samuel 10:10"> And Jehovah came down in a cloud, and spoke to him, and took of the Spirit that was upon him, and put it upon the seventy men, the elders; and it came to pass, that when the Spirit rested on them, they prophesied, but they did not repeat &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:5; 1 Samuel 20:26; 1 Samuel 10:22"> And two men remained in the camp, the name of the one, Eldad, and the name of the other, Medad; and the Spirit rested upon them (and they were among them that were written, but they had not gone out to the tent); and they prophesied in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And there ran a youth, and told Moses, and said, Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Luke 9:49–50; John 3:26"> And Joshua the son of Nun, the attendant of Moses, one of his young men, answered and said, My lord Moses, forbid them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:5; Matthew 9:37–38; Luke 10:2"> But Moses said to him, Enviest thou for my sake? would that all Jehovah's people were prophets, &#91;and&#93; that Jehovah would put his Spirit upon them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Moses withdrew into the camp, he and the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 16:13; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:26–29"> And there went forth a wind from Jehovah, and drove quails from the sea, and cast them about the camp, about a day's journey on this side, and about a day's journey on the other side, round about the camp, and about two cubits above the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11; Exodus 16:36"> And the people rose up all that day, and the whole night, and all the next day, and they gathered the quails: he that gathered little gathered ten homers; and they spread them abroad for themselves round about the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Psalms 106:14–15; Numbers 25:9"> The flesh was yet between their teeth, before it was chewed, when the wrath of Jehovah was kindled against the people, and Jehovah smote the people with a very great plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:22; 1 Corinthians 10:6; Numbers 33:16"> And they called the name of that place Kibroth-hattaavah; because there they buried the people who lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 33:17; Numbers 12:16; Deuteronomy 1:1"> From Kibroth-hattaavah the people journeyed to Hazeroth; and they were at Hazeroth.</span>
