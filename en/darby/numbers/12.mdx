<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; James 3:13; 2 Corinthians 11:5"> But the man Moses was very meek, above all men that were upon the face of the earth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Exodus 34:5; Psalms 99:7"> And Jehovah came down in the pillar of the cloud, and stood at the entrance of the tent, and called Aaron and Miriam; and they both came forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5; Genesis 31:10–11; Genesis 46:2"> And he said, Hear now my words: If there be a prophet among you, I Jehovah will make myself known to him in a vision, I will speak to him in a dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–6; Joshua 1:1; Psalms 105:26"> Not so my servant Moses: he is faithful in all my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Exodus 33:11"> Mouth to mouth do I speak to him openly, and not in riddles; and the form of Jehovah doth he behold. Why then were ye not afraid to speak against my servant, against Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Hosea 5:15; Numbers 11:1"> And the anger of Jehovah was kindled against them, and he went away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:9; 2 Kings 5:27; Exodus 4:6"> and the cloud departed from off the tent. And behold, Miriam was leprous as snow; and Aaron turned toward Miriam, and behold, she was leprous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> Then Aaron said to Moses, Alas, my lord, I beseech thee, lay not this sin upon us, wherein we have been foolish, and have sinned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Timothy 5:6"> Let her not be as one stillborn, half of whose flesh is consumed when he comes out of his mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:21; Luke 6:28; James 5:15"> And Moses cried to Jehovah, saying, O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.13 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God, heal her, I beseech thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:9; Job 30:10; Isaiah 50:6"> And Jehovah said to Moses, But had her father anyways spat in her face, should she not be shamed seven days? She shall be shut outside the camp seven days, and afterwards she shall be received in &#91;again&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8–9; Micah 7:8–9; Micah 6:4"> And Miriam was shut outside the camp seven days; and the people did not journey till Miriam was received in &#91;again&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:35; Numbers 33:18; Numbers 10:12"> And afterwards the people journeyed from Hazeroth, and encamped in the wilderness of Paran.</span>
