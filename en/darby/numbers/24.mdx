<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 23:23; Numbers 23:28"> And Balaam saw that it was good in the sight of Jehovah to bless Israel, and he went not, as at other times, to seek for enchantments, but he set his face toward the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:20; 2 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Samuel 19:23"> And Balaam lifted up his eyes and saw Israel dwelling &#91;in tents&#93; according to his tribes; and the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; Numbers 24:16; Numbers 23:7"> And he took up his parable, and said, Balaam the son of Beor saith, and the man of opened eye saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Revelation 1:10; Numbers 12:6"> He saith, who heareth the words of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="24.4 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God, who seeth the vision of the Almighty, who falleth down, and who hath his eyes open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> How goodly are thy tents, Jacob, and thy tabernacles, Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 104:16"> Like valleys are they spread forth, like gardens by the river side, Like aloe-trees which Jehovah hath planted, like cedars beside the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:12; 1 Chronicles 14:2; Revelation 17:1"> Water shall flow out of his buckets, and his seed shall be in great waters, And his king shall be higher than Agag, and his kingdom shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Psalms 2:9; Numbers 14:9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="24.8 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God brought him out of Egypt; he hath as it were the strength of a buffalo. He shall consume the nations his enemies, and break their bones, and with his arrows shall smite &#91;them&#93; in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 49:9"> He stooped, he lay down like a lion, and like a lioness: who will stir him up? Blessed is he that blesseth thee, and cursed is he that curseth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 23:11; Ezekiel 21:14; Ezekiel 21:17"> Then Balak's anger was kindled against Balaam, and he smote his hands together; and Balak said to Balaam, I called thee to curse mine enemies, and behold, thou hast altogether blessed &#91;them&#93; these three times!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 22:17; Numbers 22:37; Hebrews 11:24–26"> And now flee thou to thy place; I said I would very highly honour thee, and behold, Jehovah has kept thee back from honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:38"> And Balaam said to Balak, Did I not also speak to thy messengers whom thou sentest to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 16:28; Numbers 22:18; Numbers 22:20"> If Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the commandment of Jehovah to do good or bad out of my heart: what Jehovah shall say, that will I speak?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Revelation 2:14; Genesis 49:1"> And now behold, I go to my people: come, I will admonish thee what this people will do to thy people at the end of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 24:3–4; Numbers 23:7; Matthew 13:35"> And he took up his parable, and said, Balaam the son of Beor saith, and the man of opened eye saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:4; 1 Corinthians 8:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> He saith, who heareth the words of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="24.16 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God, who knoweth the knowledge of the Most High, Who seeth the vision of the Almighty, who falleth down, and who hath his eyes open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32–33; Genesis 49:10"> I shall see him, but not now; I shall behold him, but not nigh: There cometh a Star out of Jacob, and a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel, and he shall cut in pieces the corners of Moab, and destroy all the sons of tumult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 9:12; Genesis 27:29; 2 Samuel 8:14"> And Edom shall be a possession, and Seir a possession, — they, his enemies; but Israel will do valiantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Micah 5:2; Psalms 2:1–12"> And one out of Jacob shall have dominion, and will destroy out of the city what remaineth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Judges 6:3; 1 Chronicles 4:43"> And he saw Amalek, and took up his parable, and said, Amalek is the first of the nations, but his latter end shall be for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:19; Judges 1:16; Job 29:18"> And he saw the Kenites, and took up his parable, and said, Firm is thy dwelling-place, and thy nest fixed in the rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:8; Genesis 10:22; Ezra 4:2"> But the Kenite shall be consumed, until Asshur shall carry thee away captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Malachi 3:2; 2 Kings 5:1"> And he took up his parable, and said, Alas! Who shall live when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="24.23 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God doeth this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:20; Daniel 10:20"> And ships shall come from the coast of Chittim, and afflict Asshur, and afflict Eber, and he also shall be for destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:22; Numbers 24:11"> And Balaam rose up, and went and returned to his place; and Balak also went his way.</span>
