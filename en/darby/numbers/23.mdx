<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> And Balaam said to Balak, Build me here seven altars, and prepare me here seven bullocks and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> And Balak did as Balaam had said; and Balak and Balaam offered up a bullock and a ram on &#91;each&#93; altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> And Balaam said to Balak, Stand by thy burnt-offering, and I will go; perhaps Jehovah will come to meet me; and whatever he shews me I will tell thee. And he went to a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God met Balaam; and &#91;Balaam&#93; said to him, I have disposed seven altars, and have offered up a bullock and a ram upon &#91;each&#93; altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> And Jehovah put a word in Balaam's mouth, and said, Return to Balak, and thus shalt thou speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he returned to him, and behold, he was standing by his burnt-offering, he, and all the princes of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And he took up his parable, and said, Balak the king of Moab hath brought me from Aram, from the mountains of the east: Come, curse me Jacob, and come, denounce Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> How shall I curse whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.8 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God hath not cursed? or how shall I denounce whom Jehovah doth not denounce?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> For from the top of the rocks I see him, and from the hills I behold him: Lo, &#91;it is&#93; a people that shall dwell alone and shall not be reckoned among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalms 116:15"> Who can count the dust of Jacob, and the number of the fourth part of Israel? Let my soul die the death of the righteous, and let my end be like his!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> And Balak said to Balaam, What hast thou done to me? I took thee to curse mine enemies, and behold, thou hast blessed them altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> And he answered and said, Must I not take heed to speak that which Jehovah puts in my mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> And Balak said to him, Come, I pray thee, with me to another place, from whence thou wilt see them; thou shalt see only the extremity of them and shalt not see them all, and curse me them from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> And he took him to the watchmen's field, to the top of Pisgah, and built seven altars, and offered up a bullock and a ram on &#91;each&#93; altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> And &#91;Balaam&#93; said to Balak, Stand here by thy burnt-offering, and I will go to meet yonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> And Jehovah met Balaam, and put a word in his mouth, and said, Return to Balak, and thus shalt thou speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> And he came to him, and behold, he was standing by his burnt-offering, and the princes of Moab with him; and Balak said to him, What has Jehovah spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> Then he took up his parable and said, Rise up, Balak, and hear! hearken unto me, son of Zippor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:29; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.19 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God is not a man, that he should lie; neither a son of man, that he should repent. Shall he say and not do? and shall he speak and not make it good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> Behold, I have received &#91;mission&#93; to bless; and he hath blessed, and I cannot reverse it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> He hath not beheld iniquity in Jacob, neither hath he seen wrong in Israel; Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God is with him, and the shout of a king is in his midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="23.22 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God brought him out of Egypt; he hath as it were the strength of a buffalo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> For there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel. At this time it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel, What hath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="23.23 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">f</a></sup>God wrought!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> Lo, the people will rise up as a lioness, and lift himself up as a lion. He shall not lie down until he have eaten the prey and drunk the blood of the slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–3"> And Balak said to Balaam, Neither curse them at all, nor bless them at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> And Balaam answered and said to Balak, Did I not tell thee, saying, All that Jehovah shall speak, that will I do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> And Balak said to Balaam, Come, I pray thee, I will bring thee to another place; perhaps it will be right in the sight of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="23.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God that thou curse me them from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalms 106:28"> And Balak brought Balaam to the top of Peor, which looks over the surface of the waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> And Balaam said to Balak, Build me here seven altars, and prepare me here seven bullocks and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Balak did as Balaam had said, and offered up a bullock and a ram on each altar.</span>
