<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> And the Canaanite king of Arad, who dwelt in the south, heard that Israel came by the way of Atharim, and he fought against Israel, and took some of them prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Then Israel vowed a vow to Jehovah, and said, If thou give this people wholly into my hand, then I will utterly destroy their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; Psalms 10:17"> And Jehovah listened to the voice of Israel, and delivered up the Canaanites; and they utterly destroyed them, and their cities. And they called the name of the place Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> And they journeyed from mount Hor by the way of the Red sea, to go round the land of Edom; and the soul of the people became impatient on the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> and the people spoke against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="21.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and against Moses, Why have ye brought us up out of Egypt that we should die in the wilderness? for there is no bread, and no water, and our soul loathes this light bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> Then Jehovah sent fiery serpents among the people, which bit the people; and much people of Israel died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; Psalms 78:34"> And the people came to Moses and said, We have sinned, in that we have spoken against Jehovah, and against thee: pray to Jehovah that he take away the serpents from us. And Moses prayed for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; Psalms 106:43–45; Psalms 145:8"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Make thee a fiery &#91;serpent&#93;, and set it upon a pole; and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, and looketh upon it, shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> And Moses made a serpent of brass, and put it upon a pole; and it came to pass, if a serpent had bitten any man, and he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> And the children of Israel journeyed, and encamped in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they removed from Oboth, and encamped at Ijim-Abarim, in the wilderness that is before Moab, toward the sun-rising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> From thence they removed, and encamped at the torrent Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> From thence they removed, and encamped on the other side of the Arnon, which is in the wilderness that comes out of the border of the Amorites. For the Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> Therefore it is said in the book of the wars of Jehovah, Vaheb in Suphah, and the brooks of Arnon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> And the stream of the brooks which turneth to the dwelling of Ar, And inclineth toward the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> And from thence to Beer: that is the well of which Jehovah spoke to Moses, Assemble the people, and I will give them water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; Psalms 106:12"> Then Israel sang this song, Rise up, well! sing unto it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> Well which princes digged, which the nobles of the people hollowed out at &#91;the word of&#93; the lawgiver, with their staves. And from the wilderness &#91;they went&#93; to Mattanah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and from Mattanah to Nahaliel; and from Nahaliel to Bamoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> and from Bamoth to the valley that is in the fields of Moab, to the top of Pisgah, which looks over the surface of the waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> And Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> Let us pass through thy land; we will not turn into the fields, or into the vineyards; we will not drink water out of the wells; on the king's road will we go until we have passed thy border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> But Sihon would not suffer Israel to go through his border; and Sihon gathered all his people, and went out against Israel into the wilderness, and came to Jahaz, and fought against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> And Israel smote him with the edge of the sword, and took possession of his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, even unto the children of Ammon; for the border of the children of Ammon was strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> And Israel took all these cities, and Israel dwelt in all the cities of the Amorites, at Heshbon, and in all its dependent villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For Heshbon was the city of Sihon the king of the Amorites; and he had fought against the former king of Moab, and had taken all his land out of his hand, even unto the Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> Therefore the poets say, Come to Heshbon; let the city of Sihon be built and established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> For there went forth fire from Heshbon, a flame from the city of Sihon; It consumed Ar of Moab, the lords of the high places of the Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> Woe to thee, Moab! thou art undone, people of Chemosh: He gave his sons that had escaped, and his daughters into captivity to Sihon the king of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> And we have shot at them; Heshbon is perished even unto Dibon; and we have laid &#91;them&#93; waste even unto Nophah, which reacheth unto Medeba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> And Israel dwelt in the land of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> And Moses sent to spy out Jaazer, and they took its dependent villages, and he dispossessed the Amorites that were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> And they turned and went up by the way to Bashan; and Og the king of Bashan went out against them, he and all his people, for battle to Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Fear him not! for into thy hand have I given him, and all his people, and his land; and thou shalt do to him as thou didst unto Sihon the king of the Amorites, who dwelt at Heshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> And they smote him, and his sons, and all his people, so that they left him none remaining, and took possession of his land.</span>
