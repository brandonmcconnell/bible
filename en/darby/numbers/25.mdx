<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:15–16"> And they invited the people to the sacrifices of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods; and the people ate, and bowed down to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; Psalms 106:28–29; Hosea 9:10"> And Israel joined himself to Baal-Peor; and the anger of Jehovah was kindled against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Take all the heads of the people, and hang them up to Jehovah before the sun, that the fierce anger of Jehovah may be turned away from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> And Moses said to the judges of Israel, Slay every one his men that have joined themselves to Baal-Peor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> And behold, a man of the children of Israel came and brought a Midianitish woman to his brethren, in the sight of Moses, and in the sight of the whole assembly of the children of Israel, who were weeping before the entrance of the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalms 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> And Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, and rose up from among the assembly, and took a javelin in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalms 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> and he went after the man of Israel into the tent-chamber, and thrust both of them through, the man of Israel and the woman through her belly. And the plague was stayed from the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> And those that died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalms 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, hath turned my wrath away from the children of Israel, in that he was jealous with my jealousy among them, so that I consumed not the children of Israel in my jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> Therefore say, Behold, I give unto him my covenant of peace!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> And he shall have it, and his seed after him, the covenant of an everlasting priesthood; because he was jealous for his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="25.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and made atonement for the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> And the name of the man of Israel that was slain, who was slain with the Midianitish woman, was Zimri, the son of Salu, the prince of a father's house of the Simeonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> And the name of the Midianitish woman that was slain was Cozbi, the daughter of Zur; he was tribal head of a father's house in Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> Harass the Midianites, and smite them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> for they have harassed you with their wiles, wherewith they have beguiled you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a prince of Midian, their sister, who was slain on the day of the plague because of the matter of Peor.</span>
