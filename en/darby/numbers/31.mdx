<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> Avenge the children of Israel upon the Midianites; afterwards shalt thou be gathered unto thy peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> And Moses spoke to the people, saying, Arm from amongst you men for military service, that they go against Midian to execute Jehovah's vengeance upon Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> Of every tribe a thousand, of all the tribes of Israel, shall ye send to the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And there were levied out of the thousands of Israel, a thousand by tribe, twelve thousand armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> And Moses sent them to the war, a thousand by the tribe, them and Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, to the war, and the holy instruments, even the alarm-trumpets in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> And they warred against Midian, as Jehovah had commanded Moses, and slew all the males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> And they slew the kings of Midian, besides the others slain, Evi, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, five kings of Midian; and Balaam the son of Beor they slew with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> And the children of Israel took the women of Midian captives, and their little ones, and took for a spoil all their cattle and all their flocks and all their goods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> and all their cities in their settlements and all their encampments they burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> And they took all the booty, and all the prey, of man and of cattle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> and they brought to Moses and Eleazar the priest, and to the assembly of the children of Israel, the captives and the prey and the booty, to the camp in the plains of Moab, which are by the Jordan of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the princes of the assembly went forth to meet them outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> And Moses was wroth with the officers of the army, with the captains of thousands, and captains of hundreds, who came from the service of the war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> and Moses said to them, Have ye saved all the women alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Lo, these, through the counsel of Balaam, caused the children of Israel to commit sin against Jehovah in the matter of Peor, and there was a plague on the assembly of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> And now slay every male among the little ones, and slay every woman that hath known man by lying with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> but all the children among the women that have not known lying with a man, keep alive for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> And encamp outside the camp seven days; whoever hath killed a person, and whoever hath touched any slain; ye shall purify yourselves on the third day, and on the seventh day, you and your captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> And every garment, and every vessel of skin, and all work of goat's hair, and every utensil of wood shall ye purify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> And Eleazar the priest said to the men of war that had gone to the battle, This is the statute of the law which Jehovah hath commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only the gold, and the silver, the copper, the iron, the tin, and the lead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything that passeth through the fire, ye shall make it go through the fire, and it shall be clean; only it shall be purified with the water of separation; and everything that cannot pass through the fire ye shall make go through the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> And ye shall wash your garments on the seventh day, and ye shall be clean; and afterwards ye may come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Take the sum of the prey that was taken, of man and of cattle, thou, and Eleazar the priest, and the chief fathers of the assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> and divide the prey into halves, between them that conducted the war, who went out to the battle, and the whole assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> And thou shalt levy a tribute for Jehovah of the men of war who went out to the army, one soul of five hundred of the persons, and of the oxen, and of the asses, and of the small cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Of their half shall ye take it, and give it unto Eleazar the priest, for a heave-offering of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> And of the children of Israel's half, thou shalt take one portion of fifty, of the persons, of the oxen, of the asses, and of the small cattle, of every &#91;kind of&#93; beasts, and thou shalt give them unto the Levites, who keep the charge of the tabernacle of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest did as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the prey, the rest of the spoil, which the men of war had taken, was six hundred and seventy-five thousand sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and seventy-two thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and sixty-one thousand asses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and of human persons, of the women that had not known lying with a man, all the persons were thirty-two thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the half, the portion of them that had gone out to the war, was in number three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and the tribute for Jehovah of the sheep was six hundred and seventy-five;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and the oxen were thirty-six thousand, and the tribute thereof for Jehovah, seventy-two;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and the asses were thirty thousand five hundred, and the tribute thereof for Jehovah, sixty-one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and the human persons were sixteen thousand, of whom the tribute for Jehovah was thirty-two persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moses gave the tribute of Jehovah's heave-offering to Eleazar the priest, as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And of the children of Israel's half, which Moses had divided, &#91;taking it&#93; from the men that served in the war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> (now the half belonging to the assembly was of the sheep, three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and thirty-six thousand oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and thirty thousand five hundred asses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and sixteen thousand human persons,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> ...of the children of Israel's half, Moses took one portion of fifty, of man and of cattle, and gave them to the Levites who kept the charge of the tabernacle of Jehovah; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And the officers who were over the thousands of the host, the captains of thousands, and captains of hundreds, came near to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> and they said to Moses, Thy servants have taken the sum of the men of war who were under our hand, and there is not one man of us lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> So we present the offering of Jehovah, that which each one hath found, jewels of gold, chains, and bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces, to make atonement for our souls before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold of them, all manner of wrought jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And all the gold of the heave-offering that they offered to Jehovah was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels, from the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> (The men of war had taken spoil each one for himself.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold of the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and brought it into the tent of meeting, as a memorial for the children of Israel before Jehovah.</span>
