<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land of your dwellings, which I give unto you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> and will make an offering by fire to Jehovah, a burnt-offering or a sacrifice for the performance of a vow, or as a voluntary offering, or in your set feasts, to make a sweet odour to Jehovah, of the herd or of the flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> then shall he that presenteth his offering to Jehovah bring as oblation a tenth part of fine flour mingled with a fourth part of a hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> and of wine for a drink-offering shalt thou offer the fourth part of a hin with the burnt-offering, or with the sacrifice, for one lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> And for a ram thou shalt offer as oblation two tenth parts of fine flour mingled with oil, a third part of a hin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and of wine for a drink-offering shalt thou offer the third part of a hin; for a sweet odour to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> And when thou offerest a bullock for a burnt-offering, or a sacrifice for the performance of a vow, or for a peace-offering to Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> then shall they present with the bullock as oblation three tenth parts of fine flour mingled with half a hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> and of wine shalt thou present half a hin, for a drink-offering, as an offering by fire, of a sweet odour to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thus shall it be done for one ox, or for one ram, or for a lamb, or for a kid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> according to the number that ye offer, so shall ye do to every one according to their number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And all that are born in the land shall do these things thus, in presenting an offering by fire of a sweet odour to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And if a stranger sojourn with you, or whoever be among you throughout your generations, and will offer an offering by fire of a sweet odour to Jehovah, — as ye do, so shall he do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> As to the congregation, there shall be one statute for you, and for the stranger that sojourneth with you, an everlasting statute throughout your generations: as ye are, so shall the stranger be, before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> One law and one ordinance shall be for you, and for the stranger that sojourneth with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land whither I bring you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> then it shall be, when ye eat of the bread of the land, that ye shall offer a heave-offering to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> the first of your dough shall ye offer, a cake, for a heave-offering; as the heave-offering of the threshing-floor, so shall ye offer this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> Of the first of your dough ye shall give to Jehovah a heave-offering throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> And if ye sin inadvertently, and do not all these commandments, which Jehovah hath spoken unto Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> all that Jehovah hath commanded you through Moses, from the day that Jehovah gave commandment, and henceforward throughout your generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> then it shall be, if ought be committed by inadvertence &#91;hid&#93; from the eyes of the assembly, that the whole assembly shall offer one young bullock for a burnt-offering, for a sweet odour to Jehovah, and its oblation and its drink-offering according to the ordinance, and one buck of the goats for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> And the priest shall make atonement for the whole assembly of the children of Israel, and it shall be forgiven them; for it was a sin of inadvertence, and they have brought before Jehovah their offering, as an offering by fire to Jehovah, and their sin-offering for their &#91;sin of&#93; inadvertence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> and it shall be forgiven the whole assembly of the children of Israel, and the stranger that sojourneth among them; for with all the people there was &#91;a sin of&#93; inadvertence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> And if one soul sin through inadvertence, then he shall present a yearling she-goat for a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> And the priest shall make atonement for the soul that hath done inadvertently, when he sinneth by inadvertence before Jehovah, to make atonement for him; and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> For him that is born in the land among the children of Israel, and for the stranger that sojourneth among them — there shall be one law for you, for him who doeth anything through inadvertence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> But the soul that doeth ought with a high hand, whether born in the land, or a stranger, he reproacheth Jehovah; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> For he hath despised the word of Jehovah, and hath broken his commandment: that soul shall surely be cut off; his iniquity is upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> And while the children of Israel were in the wilderness they found a man gathering sticks on the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> And they that found him gathering sticks brought him to Moses and Aaron, and to the whole assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> And they put him in custody, for it was not declared what should be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> And Jehovah said to Moses, The man shall certainly be put to death: the whole assembly shall stone him with stones outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> And the whole assembly led him outside the camp, and stoned him with stones, and he died, as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and bid them that they make them tassels on the corners of their garments, throughout their generations, and that they attach to the tassel of the corners a lace of blue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> and it shall be unto you for a tassel, that ye may look upon it, and remember all the commandments of Jehovah, and do them; and that ye seek not after &#91;the lusts of&#93; your own heart and your own eyes, after which ye go a whoring;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> that ye may remember and do all my commandments, and be holy unto your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.40 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.41 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.41 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.41 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
</div>
