<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:1–2; 1 Chronicles 29:27–28; 1 Chronicles 23:1"> And king David was old &#91;and&#93; advanced in age; and they covered him with clothes, but he obtained no warmth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:11; Genesis 16:5; Micah 7:5"> And his servants said to him, Let there be found for my lord the king a young virgin; and let her stand before the king, and let her cherish him, and let her lie in thy bosom, that my lord the king may get warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Esther 2:4; Esther 2:2"> And they sought for a fair damsel throughout the territory of Israel, and found Abishag a Shunammite, and brought her to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:25"> And the damsel was very fair; and cherished the king, and ministered to him; but the king knew her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:1; 2 Samuel 3:4; Deuteronomy 17:15–16"> And Adonijah the son of Haggith exalted himself, saying, I will be king; and he provided himself chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to run before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 3:13; Hebrews 12:5–6"> And his father had not grieved him at any time in saying, Why doest thou so? and he was also a very comely man; and &#91;his mother&#93; bore him after Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> And he conferred with Joab the son of Zeruiah, and with Abiathar the priest; and they helped Adonijah and followed &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 4:18; 2 Samuel 23:8–39; 2 Samuel 20:25"> But Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and Nathan the prophet, and Shimei, and Rei, and the mighty men whom David had, were not with Adonijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 13:23–27; 2 Samuel 15:11–12"> And Adonijah sacrificed sheep and oxen and fatted cattle by the stone of Zoheleth, which is by En-rogel, and invited all his brethren, the king's sons, and all the men of Judah, the king's servants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 2 Samuel 12:1–15"> but Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah, and the mighty men, and Solomon his brother, he did not invite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:4; 2 Samuel 7:12–17; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> And Nathan spoke to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon, saying, Hast thou not heard that Adonijah the son of Haggith is king, and David our lord does not know &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Acts 27:31; Genesis 19:17"> And now, come, let me, I pray thee, give thee counsel, that thou mayest save thine own life, and the life of thy son Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 1:30; 1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 132:11–12"> Go and get thee in to king David, and say to him, Didst not thou, my lord, O king, swear to thy handmaid saying, Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne? why then does Adonijah reign?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Kings 1:17–27"> Behold, while thou yet talkest there with the king, I also will come in after thee and confirm thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1"> And Bathsheba went in to the king into the chamber; and the king was very old; and Abishag the Shunammite ministered to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; 1 Samuel 24:8; 1 Samuel 20:41"> And Bathsheba bowed and did obeisance to the king. And the king said, What wouldest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:30; 1 Peter 3:6"> And she said to him, My lord, thou hast sworn by Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God to thy handmaid, &#91;saying,&#93; Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:24; 1 Kings 1:11; Acts 3:17"> And now behold, Adonijah is king; and now, my lord the king, thou knowest it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:7–10"> And he has sacrificed oxen and fatted cattle and sheep in abundance, and has invited all the sons of the king and Abiathar the priest and Joab the captain of the host; but Solomon thy servant has he not invited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:1; Psalms 123:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8–10"> And thou, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are upon thee, that thou shouldest tell them who shall sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Deuteronomy 31:16; Genesis 15:15"> Otherwise it shall come to pass when my lord the king shall sleep with his fathers, that I and my son Solomon will be counted offenders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 1:16–18; Genesis 24:15; Daniel 9:20"> And behold, while she yet talked with the king, Nathan the prophet also came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 1 Peter 2:17; Romans 13:7"> And they told the king saying, Behold, Nathan the prophet. And when he was come in before the king, he bowed himself before the king with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17–18; 1 Kings 1:5; 1 Kings 1:13–14"> And Nathan said, My lord, O king, hast thou said, Adonijah shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:19; 1 Kings 1:9"> For he is gone down this day, and has sacrificed oxen and fatted cattle and sheep in abundance, and has invited all the king's sons, and the captains of the host, and Abiathar the priest; and behold, they eat and drink before him, and say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.25 Elohim">b</a></sup>God save king Adonijah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:10; 2 Samuel 7:2"> But me, &#91;even&#93; me thy servant, and Zadok the priest, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and thy servant Solomon, he has not invited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 1:24; 2 Kings 4:27"> Is this thing done by my lord the king, and thou hast not shewn to thy servants who should sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then king David answered and said, Call me Bathsheba. And she came into the king's presence, and stood before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:9; Psalms 136:24; 1 Samuel 14:45"> And the king swore, and said, &#91;As&#93; Jehovah liveth, who has redeemed my soul out of all distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:13; 1 Kings 1:17"> even as I swore to thee by Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel, saying, Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne in my stead; even so will I certainly do this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 5:10; Daniel 3:9"> And Bathsheba bowed with her face to the earth, and did reverence to the king, and said, Let my lord king David live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:38; 1 Kings 1:26"> And king David said, Call me Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada. And they came before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Chronicles 33:14"> And the king said to them, Take with you the servants of your lord, and cause Solomon my son to ride upon mine own mule, and bring him down to Gihon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 2 Samuel 15:10; 1 Samuel 10:1"> and let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him there king over Israel; and blow ye with the trumpet, and say, Long live king Solomon!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17; Psalms 72:1–2; 1 Kings 1:13"> And ye shall come up after him, that he may come and sit on my throne; for he shall reign in my stead, and I have appointed him to be ruler over Israel and over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:6; 1 Corinthians 14:16; Psalms 72:19"> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king and said, Amen: Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of my lord the king, say so too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Kings 1:47; Joshua 1:17"> As Jehovah has been with my lord the king, even so be he with Solomon, and make his throne greater than the throne of my lord king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:33"> And Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites went down, and caused Solomon to ride upon king David's mule, and brought him to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 89:20; 1 Kings 1:34; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> And Zadok the priest took the horn of oil out of the tabernacle, and anointed Solomon. And they blew the trumpet; and all the people said, Long live king Solomon!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; Zechariah 9:9; 2 Kings 11:20"> And all the people came up after him, and the people piped with pipes, and rejoiced with great joy, so that the earth rent with the sound of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Job 15:21–22; Luke 17:26–29; Acts 21:31"> And Adonijah and all the guests that were with him heard &#91;it&#93; as they were finishing their repast; and Joab heard the sound of the trumpet, and he said, Wherefore this noise of the city in an uproar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 18:27"> While he yet spoke, behold, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came; and Adonijah said &#91;to him&#93;, Come in; for thou art a valiant man, and bringest good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Jonathan answered and said to Adonijah, Indeed, our lord king David has made Solomon king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the king has sent with him Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and they have caused him to ride upon the king's mule;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 1:40; Daniel 5:26–28; 1 Samuel 4:5"> and Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him king at Gihon; and they are come up from thence rejoicing, so that the city is in an uproar. This is the noise which ye have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:23; Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:13"> And also Solomon is sitting on the throne of the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 47:31; 1 Kings 1:37; Ezra 6:10"> And moreover the king's servants came to bless our lord king David, saying, Thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.47 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God make the name of Solomon more excellent than thy name, and make his throne greater than thy throne! And the king bowed himself on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 3:6; Psalms 132:11–12; Psalms 145:2"> And also thus said the king: Blessed be Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.48 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of Israel, who has given one to sit on my throne this day, mine eyes even seeing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 21:4–5; Proverbs 28:1; Daniel 5:4–6"> And all Adonijah's guests were afraid, and rose up and went every man his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28; Exodus 21:14; Exodus 27:2"> And Adonijah feared because of Solomon, and arose and went and caught hold of the horns of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And it was told Solomon saying, Behold, Adonijah fears king Solomon; for behold, he has caught hold of the horns of the altar, saying, Let king Solomon swear to me this day that he will not put his servant to death with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Samuel 14:11; Proverbs 13:6"> And Solomon said, If he be a worthy man, there shall not one of his hairs fall to the earth; but if wickedness be found in him, he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 2 Samuel 14:24; 2 Samuel 1:2"> And king Solomon sent, and they brought him down from the altar. And he came and bowed himself to king Solomon; and Solomon said to him, Go to thy house.</span>
