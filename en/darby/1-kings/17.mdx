<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25–26; James 5:17; Luke 1:17"> And Elijah the Tishbite, of the inhabitants of Gilead, said to Ahab, As Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="17.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel liveth, before whom I stand, there shall not be dew nor rain these years, except by my word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:1; Jeremiah 7:1; Hosea 1:1–2"> And the word of Jehovah came to him saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Jeremiah 36:26; Psalms 83:3"> Get thee hence, and turn thee eastward, and hide thyself by the torrent Cherith, which is before the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> And it shall be, that thou shalt drink of the torrent; and I have commanded the ravens to feed thee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; Matthew 16:24; John 15:14"> And he went and did according to the word of Jehovah; he went and abode by the torrent Cherith, which is before the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:23–24; Matthew 6:31–33; Jeremiah 37:21"> And the ravens brought him bread and flesh in the morning, and bread and flesh in the evening; and he drank of the torrent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 54:10; Isaiah 40:30–31"> And it came to pass after a while that the torrent dried up, for there had been no rain in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; 1 Kings 17:2; Hebrews 13:6"> And the word of Jehovah came to him saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:26; Obadiah 1:20; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> Arise, go to Zarephath, which is by Zidon, and abide there: behold, I have commanded a widow woman there to maintain thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:7; Genesis 24:17; Genesis 21:15"> And he arose and went to Zarephath; and when he came to the entrance of the city, behold, a widow woman was there gathering sticks. And he called to her and said, Fetch me, I pray thee, a little water in a vessel, that I may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–40; 1 Kings 18:4; Hebrews 13:2"> And she went to fetch &#91;it&#93;, and he called to her and said, Bring me, I pray thee, a morsel of bread in thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; 1 Kings 17:1; Genesis 21:16"> And she said, As Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="17.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God liveth, I have not a cake, but a handful of meal in a barrel, and a little oil in a cruse; and behold, I am gathering two sticks, that I may go in and dress it for me and my son, that we may eat it, and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Malachi 3:10; Genesis 22:1–2"> And Elijah said to her, Fear not; go, do as thou hast said; but make me thereof a little cake first; and bring it to me; and afterwards make for thee and for thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:36–38; Matthew 14:17–20; 2 Kings 4:42–44"> For thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="17.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel: The meal in the barrel shall not waste, neither shall the oil in the cruse fail, until the day that Jehovah sendeth rain upon the face of the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; Mark 12:43; Matthew 15:28"> And she went and did according to the word of Elijah; and she, and he, and her house, ate a whole year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:28–30; John 4:50–51; Luke 1:37"> The meal in the barrel did not waste, neither did the oil in the cruse fail, according to the word of Jehovah which he had spoken through Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; Daniel 5:23; John 11:14"> And it came to pass after these things, that the son of the woman, the mistress of the house, fell sick; and his sickness was so severe that there was no breath left in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 5:8; John 2:4; 2 Kings 3:13"> And she said to Elijah, What have I to do with thee, O thou man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="17.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God? art thou come to me to call mine iniquity to remembrance, and to slay my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; Acts 9:37"> And he said to her, Give me thy son. And he took him out of her bosom, and carried him up into the upper chamber where he abode, and laid him upon his own bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 5:15–18; Matthew 21:22; Joshua 7:8–9"> And he cried to Jehovah and said, Jehovah, my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="17.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, hast thou also brought evil upon the widow with whom I sojourn, by slaying her son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:10; Acts 9:40; 2 Kings 4:33–35"> And he stretched himself upon the child three times, and cried to Jehovah and said, Jehovah, my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="17.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, I pray thee, let this child's soul come into him again!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 11:11; Hebrews 11:35"> And Jehovah heard the voice of Elijah, and the soul of the child came into him again, and he lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 4:36–37; Acts 9:41; Luke 7:15"> And Elijah took the child, and brought him down from the upper chamber into the house, and delivered him to his mother; and Elijah said, See, thy son lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:30; John 2:11; John 3:2"> And the woman said to Elijah, Now by this I know that thou art a man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="17.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, and that the word of Jehovah in thy mouth is truth.</span>
