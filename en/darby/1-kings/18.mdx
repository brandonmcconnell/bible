<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25; James 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:12"> And it came to pass after many days, that the word of Jehovah came to Elijah in the third year, saying, Go, shew thyself to Ahab; and I will send rain upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25; Proverbs 28:1; Leviticus 26:26"> And Elijah went to shew himself to Ahab. And the famine was severe in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Kings 16:9; Matthew 10:28"> And Ahab called Obadiah, who was the steward of his house (now Obadiah feared Jehovah greatly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; Matthew 10:40–42; Revelation 17:4–6"> and it was so, when Jezebel cut off the prophets of Jehovah, that Obadiah took a hundred prophets, and hid them by fifty in a cave, and maintained them with bread and water);</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:5–6; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:22"> and Ahab said to Obadiah, Go through the land, to all the fountains of water and to all the torrents, perhaps we may find grass to save the horses and the mules alive, so that we may not have to destroy some of &#91;our&#93; beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3"> And they divided the land between them to pass through it: Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went another way by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6–8; Genesis 44:20; Isaiah 60:14"> And as Obadiah was on the way, behold, Elijah met him; and he knew him, and fell on his face, and said, Is it indeed thou, my lord Elijah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Kings 18:3; 1 Peter 2:17–18"> And he said to him, I &#91;am he&#93;: go, say to thy lord, Behold Elijah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; 1 Kings 18:12; 1 Kings 17:18"> And he said, What have I sinned, that thou givest thy servant into the hand of Ahab, to put me to death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 1:29; 1 Kings 18:15"> As Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God liveth, there is no nation or kingdom whither my lord has not sent to seek thee; and when they said, He is not &#91;here&#93;, he took an oath of the kingdom or nation that they found thee not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 18:8; 1 Kings 18:14"> And now thou sayest, Go, say to thy lord, Behold Elijah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 2:16; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 8:3"> And it shall come to pass when I am gone from thee, that the Spirit of Jehovah shall carry thee whither I know not; and when I come and tell Ahab, and he cannot find thee, he will kill me; and I thy servant fear Jehovah from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; Matthew 25:35; Psalms 18:21–24"> Was it not told my lord what I did when Jezebel slew the prophets of Jehovah, how I hid a hundred men of Jehovah's prophets by fifty in a cave, and maintained them with bread and water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28"> And now thou sayest, Go, say to thy lord, Behold Elijah! and he will kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 18:10; Luke 2:13–14"> And Elijah said, As Jehovah of hosts liveth, before whom I stand, I will certainly shew myself to him to-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Obadiah went to meet Ahab, and told him. And Ahab went to meet Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; 1 Kings 21:20; Acts 16:20"> And it came to pass when Ahab saw Elijah, that Ahab said to him, Is it thou, the troubler of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 9:9; Jeremiah 2:19"> And he said, I have not troubled Israel, but thou and thy father's house, in that ye have forsaken the commandments of Jehovah, and thou hast followed the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 19:26; 1 Kings 16:33; 2 Peter 2:1"> And now send, gather to me all Israel to mount Carmel, and the prophets of Baal four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of the Asherah four hundred, who eat at Jezebel's table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9"> So Ahab sent to all the children of Israel, and gathered the prophets together unto mount Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15; 1 Corinthians 10:21–22"> Then Elijah drew near to all the people, and said, How long do ye halt between two opinions? if Jehovah be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, follow him; and if Baal, follow him. And the people answered him not a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; 1 Kings 19:14; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> And Elijah said to the people, I, only I, remain a prophet of Jehovah; and Baal's prophets are four hundred and fifty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Let them therefore give us two bullocks: and let them choose one bullock for themselves, and cut it in pieces, and put it on the wood, and put no fire; and I will sacrifice the other bullock, and put it on the wood, and put no fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 18:38; 1 Chronicles 21:26; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> And call ye on the name of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods, and I will call on the name of Jehovah; and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>god that answers by fire, let him be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="18.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God. And all the people answered and said, The word is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, Choose one bullock for yourselves, and sacrifice it first; for ye are the many; and call on the name of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="18.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>god, but put no fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Matthew 6:7"> And they took the bullock which had been given them, and sacrificed it, and called on the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, O Baal, answer us! But there was no voice, and none answered. And they leaped about the altar that had been made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:65–66; Isaiah 41:23; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> And it came to pass at noon that Elijah mocked them and said, Cry aloud; for he is a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="18.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>god; for he is meditating, or gone aside, or he is on a journey; perhaps he sleeps, and will awake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:28; Deuteronomy 14:1; Mark 5:5"> And they cried aloud, and cut themselves after their manner with swords and spears, till the blood gushed out upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:26; 1 Samuel 18:10; Exodus 29:39"> And it came to pass when midday was past, that they prophesied until the &#91;time&#93; of the offering up of the oblation; but there was neither voice, nor any that answered, nor any attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:16; 1 Kings 19:14; 1 Kings 19:10"> Then Elijah said to all the people, Draw near to me. And all the people drew near to him. And he repaired the altar of Jehovah which was broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 35:10; Genesis 32:28"> And Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, to whom the word of Jehovah came saying, Israel shall be thy name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Exodus 20:24–25; Judges 21:4"> and with the stones he built an altar in the name of Jehovah, and made a trench round about the altar, of the capacity of two measures of seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 22:9; Judges 6:20; Leviticus 1:6–8"> and he put the wood in order, and cut the bullock in pieces, and laid it on the wood. And he said, Fill four pitchers with water, and pour it on the burnt-offering, and on the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> And he said, Do it the second time. And they did it the second time. And he said, Do it the third time. And they did it the third time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 18:32; 1 Kings 18:38"> And the water ran round about the altar; and he filled the trench also with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 18:29; 2 Kings 19:19; Exodus 3:6"> And it came to pass at &#91;the time of&#93; the offering up of the oblation, that Elijah the prophet drew near, and said, Jehovah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="18.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, let it be known this day that thou art<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="18.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God in Israel, and that I am thy servant, and that I have done all these things by thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Luke 1:16–17; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> Answer me, Jehovah, answer me, that this people may know that thou Jehovah art<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="18.37 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, and &#91;that&#93; thou hast turned their heart back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26; Leviticus 9:24; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> And the fire of Jehovah fell, and consumed the burnt-offering, and the wood, and the stones, and the dust, and licked up the water that was in the trench.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:24; 2 Chronicles 7:3; 1 Kings 18:21"> And all the people saw &#91;it&#93;, and they fell on their faces and said, Jehovah, he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="18.39 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God! Jehovah, he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="18.39 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Judges 4:7; Deuteronomy 13:5"> And Elijah said to them, Seize the prophets of Baal; let not one of them escape! And they seized them; and Elijah brought them down to the torrent of Kishon, and slaughtered them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 18:1; Ecclesiastes 9:7; 1 Kings 17:1"> And Elijah said to Ahab, Go up, eat and drink; for there is a sound of abundance of rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Daniel 9:7; Joshua 7:6"> And Ahab went up to eat and to drink. And Elijah went up to the top of Carmel; and he bowed down on the earth, and put his face between his knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 18:1"> And he said to his servant, Go up now, look toward the sea. And he went up and looked, and said, &#91;There is&#93; nothing. And he said, Go again seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 12:54; Job 8:7; Zechariah 4:10"> And it came to pass at the seventh time that he said, Behold there is a cloud, small as a man's hand, arising out of the sea. And he said, Go up, say to Ahab, Harness and go down, that the pour of rain stop thee not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 18:39–40; Joshua 17:16; 1 Kings 21:1"> And it came to pass in the mean while, that the heavens became black &#91;with&#93; clouds and wind, and there was a great pour of rain. And Ahab got on the chariot, and went to Jizreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 1:3; 2 Kings 9:1"> And the hand of Jehovah was upon Elijah; and he girded up his loins, and ran before Ahab to the entrance of Jizreel.</span>
