<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32–33; 2 Kings 23:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:15"> And behold, there came a man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God from Judah, by the word of Jehovah, to Bethel; and Jeroboam stood by the altar to burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:1; 2 Kings 23:15–18; 2 Kings 22:1–2"> And he cried against the altar by the word of Jehovah, and said, O altar, altar! thus saith Jehovah: Behold, a child shall be born unto the house of David, Josiah by name; and upon thee shall he sacrifice the priests of the high places that burn incense upon thee, and men's bones shall be burned upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Exodus 4:3–5; Judges 6:17"> And he gave a sign the same day, saying, This is the sign that Jehovah hath spoken: Behold, the altar shall be rent, and the ashes that are upon it shall be poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 11:5; 2 Chronicles 18:25–27; Luke 6:10"> And it came to pass when the king heard the word of the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, which he cried against the altar in Bethel, that Jeroboam stretched forth his hand from the altar, saying, Lay hold on him. And his hand which he stretched out against him dried up, so that he could not bring it back again to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 13:3; Jeremiah 28:16–17; Deuteronomy 18:22"> And the altar was rent, and the ashes poured out from the altar, according to the sign that the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God had given by the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; Acts 8:24; Exodus 10:17"> And the king answered and said to the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, Intreat now Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, and pray for me, that my hand may be restored to me again. And the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God intreated Jehovah, and the king's hand was restored to him again, and became as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:15; 1 Samuel 9:7–8; Judges 13:15"> And the king said to the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, Come home with me, and refresh thyself, and I will give thee a present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; Mark 6:23"> And the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God said to the king, If thou wilt give me half thy house, I will not go in with thee, neither will I eat bread nor drink water in this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 13:17; Deuteronomy 13:13–18; Romans 16:17"> for so was it charged me by the word of Jehovah, saying, Thou shalt eat no bread, nor drink water, nor return by the way that thou shalt go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So he went another way, and returned not by the way that he had come to Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:18; 1 Kings 13:20–21; Numbers 24:2"> Now there dwelt a certain old prophet in Bethel; and his sons came and told him all the works that the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="13.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God had done that day in Bethel; the words that he had spoken to the king, them they told also to their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And their father said to them, Which way went he? for his sons had seen which way the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="13.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God went, who came from Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:4; 2 Samuel 19:26; Judges 5:10"> And he said to his sons, Saddle me the ass. So they saddled him the ass; and he rode thereon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 4:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 4:11–12"> and went after the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="13.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, and found him sitting under a terebinth; and he said to him, Art thou the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="13.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God that camest from Judah? And he said, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said to him, Come home with me, and eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 13:8–9; Matthew 16:23; Numbers 22:19"> And he said, I cannot return with thee, nor go in with thee; neither will I eat bread nor drink water with thee in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 20:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Kings 13:1"> For it was said to me by the word of Jehovah, Thou shalt eat no bread nor drink water there, nor turn again to go by the way that thou wentest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Matthew 7:15; Numbers 22:35"> And he said to him, I am a prophet also as thou art; and an angel spoke to me by the word of Jehovah saying, Bring him back with thee into thy house, that he may eat bread and drink water. He lied unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 3:6; Acts 4:19"> Then he went back with him, and ate bread in his house, and drank water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Numbers 24:4; Numbers 24:16–24"> And it came to pass as they sat at the table, that the word of Jehovah came to the prophet that brought him back;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 13:17; 1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Samuel 4:18"> and he cried to the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="13.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God that came from Judah, saying, Thus saith Jehovah: Forasmuch as thou hast disobeyed the word of Jehovah, and hast not kept the commandment that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="13.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God commanded thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–20; 1 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 22:18–19"> but camest back, and hast eaten bread and drunk water in the place of which he said to thee, Eat no bread and drink no water; thy carcase shall not come to the sepulchre of thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And it came to pass, after he had eaten bread, and after he had drunk, that he saddled the ass for him, for the prophet that he had brought back;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:36; 2 Kings 2:24; Proverbs 26:13"> and he departed. And a lion met him by the way and slew him; and his corpse was cast in the way, and the ass stood by it; the lion also stood by the corpse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 13:11"> And behold, men passed by, and saw the corpse cast in the way and the lion standing by the corpse; and they came and told it in the city where the old prophet dwelt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:6; Proverbs 11:31; Psalms 119:120"> And the prophet that brought him back from the way heard &#91;of it&#93; and said, It is the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="13.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God who was disobedient to the word of Jehovah; therefore Jehovah has delivered him to the lion, which has torn him and slain him, according to the word of Jehovah which he spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he spoke to his sons saying, Saddle me the ass; and they saddled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Psalms 148:7–8; Daniel 6:22–24"> And he went and found his corpse cast in the way, and the ass and the lion standing by the corpse: the lion had not eaten the corpse, nor torn the ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the prophet took up the corpse of the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="13.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God, and laid it upon the ass, and brought it back; and the old prophet came into the city, to mourn and to bury him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; Acts 8:2; 1 Kings 14:13"> And he laid his corpse in his own sepulchre; and they mourned over him &#91;saying&#93;, Alas, my brother!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 23:17–18; Numbers 23:10; Luke 16:22–23"> And it came to pass after he had buried him, that he spoke to his sons saying, When I am dead, bury me in the sepulchre in which the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="13.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God is buried; lay my bones beside his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 16:24; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Kings 12:31"> For the word that he cried by the word of Jehovah against the altar in Bethel, and against all the houses of the high places that are in the cities of Samaria, shall certainly come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:15; 2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 17:12–13"> After this thing Jeroboam turned not from his evil way, but made again from all classes of the people priests of the high places: whosoever would, he consecrated him, and he became &#91;one&#93; of the priests of the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Kings 15:29–30; 1 Kings 12:30"> And by this thing there was sin on the house of Jeroboam, even to cut it off and to destroy it from off the face of the earth.</span>
