<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:40; Proverbs 21:30; John 12:19"> For they said, Not in the feast, lest perhaps there be a tumult of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8; John 11:2"> And when he was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, as he lay at table, there came a woman having an alabaster flask of ointment of pure nard, very costly; and having broken the alabaster flask, she poured it out upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:4–5; Matthew 26:8–9; Malachi 1:12–13"> And there were some indignant in themselves, and saying, Why has this waste been made of the ointment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:29; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Corinthians 10:10"> for this ointment could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii and given to the poor. And they spoke very angrily at her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 10:24; Hebrews 13:21"> But Jesus said, Let her alone; why do ye trouble her? she has wrought a good work as to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Matthew 26:11; James 2:14–16"> for ye have the poor always with you, and whenever ye would ye can do them good; but me ye have not always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; John 12:7; Mark 15:42–1"> What she could she has done. She has beforehand anointed my body for the burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 26:12–13; Mark 16:15; Matthew 24:14"> And verily I say unto you, Wheresoever these glad tidings may be preached in the whole world, what this &#91;woman&#93; has done shall be also spoken of for a memorial of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 26:14–16; John 13:2"> And Judas Iscariote, one of the twelve, went away to the chief priests that he might deliver him up to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Hosea 7:3; Jude 1:11"> and they, when they heard it, rejoiced, and promised him to give money. And he sought how he could opportunely deliver him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18; Matthew 26:17–19"> And the first day of unleavened bread, when they slew the passover, his disciples say to him, Where wilt thou that we go and prepare, that thou mayest eat the passover?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:10–13; Matthew 26:18–19; Luke 19:30–33"> And he sends two of his disciples, and says to them, Go into the city, and a man shall meet you carrying a pitcher of water; follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Mark 11:3; John 13:13"> And wheresoever he enters, say to the master of the house, The Teacher says, Where is my guest-chamber where I may eat the passover with my disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Psalms 110:3; Proverbs 21:1–2"> and he will shew you a large upper room furnished ready. There make ready for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 16:4; Luke 22:13; Luke 22:35"> And his disciples went away and came into the city, and found as he had said to them; and they made ready the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:14; John 13:21–26; Matthew 26:20–24"> And when evening was come, he comes with the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 6:47; John 6:70"> And as they lay at table and were eating, Jesus said, Verily I say to you, One of you shall deliver me up; he who is eating with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:21–23; Matthew 26:22; John 13:22"> And they began to be grieved, and to say to him, one by one, Is it I? &#91;and another, Is it I?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 13:26; Matthew 26:23; John 6:71"> But he answered and said to them, One of the twelve, he who dips with me in the dish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:24–25; Isaiah 53:1–12; John 19:28"> The Son of man goes indeed as it is written concerning him, but woe to that man by whom the Son of man is delivered up; &#91;it were&#93; good for that man if he had not been born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–29; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And as they were eating, Jesus, having taken bread, when he had blessed, broke &#91;it&#93;, and gave &#91;it&#93; to them, and said, Take &#91;this&#93;: this is my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:22; Matthew 26:27; 1 Corinthians 10:16"> And having taken &#91;the&#93; cup, when he had given thanks, he gave &#91;it&#93; to them, and they all drank out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Exodus 24:8; Revelation 7:9–17"> And he said to them, This is my blood, that of the &#91;new&#93; covenant, that shed for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Psalms 104:15; Luke 22:16–18"> Verily I say to you, I will no more drink at all of the fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.25 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:30; Luke 22:39; Acts 16:25"> And having sung a hymn, they went out to the mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Matthew 26:31"> And Jesus says to them, All ye shall be offended, for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 28:16; Mark 16:7; Matthew 28:7"> But after I am risen, I will go before you into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 26:33–35; Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38"> But Peter said to him, Even if all should be offended, yet not I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 13:38; Mark 14:66–72; Genesis 1:5"> And Jesus says to him, Verily I say to thee, that thou to-day, in this night, before &#91;the&#93; cock shall crow twice, thou shalt thrice deny me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 19:8; John 13:37; Proverbs 16:18"> But he said &#91;so much&#93; exceedingly the more, If I should have to die with thee, I will in no wise deny thee. And likewise said they all too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 14:36; Mark 14:39; Psalms 18:5–6"> And they come to a place of which the name &#91;is&#93; Gethsemane, and he says to his disciples, Sit here while I shall pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:44; Hebrews 5:7; Mark 5:37"> And he takes with him Peter and James and John, and he began to be amazed and oppressed in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 12:27; Mark 13:35–37; 1 Peter 5:8"> And he says to them, My soul is full of grief even unto death; abide here and watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 14:41; Deuteronomy 9:18; Hebrews 5:7"> And, going forward a little, he fell upon the earth; and he prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Romans 8:15–16; John 5:30"> And he said, Abba, Father, all things are possible to thee: take away this cup from me; but not what I will, but what thou &#91;wilt&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 26:40; Mark 14:29–31; Hebrews 12:3"> And he comes and finds them sleeping. And he says to Peter, Simon, dost thou sleep? Hast thou not been able to watch one hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 26:41; Matthew 24:42; Luke 21:36"> Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation. The spirit indeed &#91;is&#93; willing, but the flesh weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 26:42–44; 2 Corinthians 12:8; Matthew 6:7"> And going away, he prayed again, saying the same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 9:33–34; Romans 3:19; Genesis 44:16"> And returning, he found them again sleeping, for their eyes were heavy; and they knew not what they should answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:35; Mark 9:31; John 17:1"> And he comes the third time and says to them, Sleep on now, and take your rest. It is enough; the hour is come; behold, the Son of man is delivered up into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:46; John 18:1–2"> Arise, let us go; behold, he that delivers me up has drawn nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 18:3–11; Luke 22:47–53; Matthew 26:47–56"> And immediately, while he was yet speaking, Judas comes up, &#91;being&#93; one of the twelve, and with him a great crowd, with swords and sticks, from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 26:48–50; Proverbs 27:6; Psalms 55:20–21"> Now he that delivered him up had given them a sign between them, saying, Whomsoever I shall kiss, that is he; seize him, and lead &#91;him&#93; away safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 23:7–10; Malachi 1:6; Mark 12:14"> And being come, straightway coming up to him, he says, Rabbi, Rabbi; and he covered him with kisses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 18:12; Lamentations 4:20; Acts 2:23"> And they laid their hands upon him and seized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> But a certain one of those who stood by, having drawn his sword, struck the bondman of the high priest, and took off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 22:52–53; 1 Samuel 26:18; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> And Jesus answering said to them, Are ye come out as against a robber, with swords and sticks to take me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:20; John 8:2; Luke 22:37"> I was daily with you teaching in the temple, and ye did not seize me; but &#91;it is&#93; that the scriptures may be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 14:27; John 16:32; Psalms 88:18"> And all left him and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And a certain young man followed him with a linen cloth cast about his naked &#91;body&#93;; and &#91;the young men&#93; seize him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 13:14–16; Genesis 39:12; Job 2:4"> but he, leaving the linen cloth behind &#91;him&#93;, fled from them naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 26:57–68; John 18:12–14; Luke 22:54–62"> And they led away Jesus to the high priest. And there come together to him all the chief priests and the elders and the scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 18:18; Matthew 26:3; John 18:25"> And Peter followed him at a distance, till &#91;he was&#93; within the court of the high priest's palace; and he was sitting with the officers and warming himself in the light &#91;of the fire&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16–18; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 5:22"> And the chief priests and the whole sanhedrim sought testimony against Jesus to cause him to be put to death, and did not find &#91;any&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> For many bore false witness against him, and their testimony did not agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Acts 6:13–14; Matthew 26:60–61; John 2:18–21"> And certain persons rose up and bore false witness against him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 2:19; Hebrews 9:11; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> We heard him saying, I will destroy this temple which is made with hands, and in the course of three days I will build another not made with hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And neither thus did their testimony agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 19:9–10; Matthew 26:62–63; Mark 15:3–5"> And the high priest, rising up before them all, asked Jesus, saying, Answerest thou nothing? What do these testify against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 26:63–64; Mark 15:2; Isaiah 53:7"> But he was silent, and answered nothing. Again the high priest asked him, and says to him, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:3"> And Jesus said, I am, and ye shall see the Son of man sitting at the right hand of power, and coming with the clouds of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Isaiah 36:22–1; Numbers 14:6"> And the high priest, having rent his clothes, says, What need have we any more of witnesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 10:31–33; John 19:7"> Ye have heard the blasphemy; what think ye? And they all condemned him to be guilty of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 26:67–68; Acts 23:2; Isaiah 50:6"> And some began to spit upon him, and cover up his face, and buffet him, and say to him, Prophesy; and the officers struck him with the palms of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:69–75; John 18:25–27"> And Peter being below in the palace-court, there comes one of the maids of the high priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Mark 14:54; Mark 10:47; Matthew 2:23"> and seeing Peter warming himself, having looked at him, says, And thou wast with the Nazarene, Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Mark 14:29–31; Matthew 26:71–72; John 13:36–38"> But he denied, saying, I know not nor understand what thou sayest. And he went out into the vestibule; and a cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Luke 22:58; Mark 14:38; Galatians 6:1"> And the maid, seeing him, again began to say to those that stood by, This is &#91;one&#93; of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 22:59–60; Matthew 26:73–74; Acts 2:7"> And he again denied. And again, after a little, those that stood by said to Peter, Truly thou art &#91;one&#93; of them, for also thou art a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; 1 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Kings 10:32"> But he began to curse and to swear, I know not this man of whom ye speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Mark 14:30; Mark 14:68; Ezekiel 7:16"> And the second time a cock crew. And Peter remembered the word that Jesus said to him, Before &#91;the&#93; cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice; and when he thought thereon he wept.</span>
