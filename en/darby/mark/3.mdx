<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And he entered again into the synagogue; and there was there a man having his hand dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> And they watched him if he would heal him on the sabbath, that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And he says to the man who had his hand dried up, Rise up &#91;and come&#93; into the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he says to them, Is it lawful on the sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill? But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And looking round upon them with anger, distressed at the hardening of their heart, he says to the man, Stretch out thy hand. And he stretched &#91;it&#93; out, and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> And the Pharisees going out straightway with the Herodians took counsel against him, how they might destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> And Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the sea; and a great multitude from Galilee followed him, and from Judaea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem, and from Idumaea and beyond the Jordan; and they of around Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, having heard what things he did, came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And he spoke to his disciples, in order that a little ship should wait upon him on account of the crowd, that they might not press upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For he healed many, so that they beset him that they might touch him, as many as had plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And the unclean spirits, when they beheld him, fell down before him, and cried saying, Thou art the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.11 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And he rebuked them much, that they might not make him manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And he goes up into the mountain, and calls whom he himself would, and they went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And he appointed twelve that they might be with him, and that he might send them to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have power &#91;to heal diseases, and&#93; to cast out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> And he gave to Simon the surname of Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and James the &#91;son&#93; of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, and he gave them the surname of Boanerges, that is, Sons of thunder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the &#91;son&#93; of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Cananaean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariote, who also delivered him up. And they come to &#91;the&#93; house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And again a crowd comes together, so that they cannot even eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> And his relatives having heard &#91;of it&#93; went out to lay hold on him, for they said, He is out of his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribes who had come down from Jerusalem said, He has Beelzebub, and, By the prince of the demons he casts out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And having called them to &#91;him&#93;, he said to them in parables, How can Satan cast out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And if a kingdom has become divided against itself, that kingdom cannot subsist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> And if a house has become divided against itself, that house cannot subsist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Satan rise up against himself, and is divided, he cannot subsist, but has an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> But no one can, having entered into his house, plunder the goods of the strong &#91;man&#93; unless he first bind the strong &#91;man&#93;, and then he will plunder his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Verily I say unto you, that all sins shall be forgiven to the sons of men, and all the injurious speeches &#91;with&#93; which they may speak injuriously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but whosoever shall speak injuriously against the Holy Spirit, to eternity has no forgiveness; but lies under the guilt of an everlasting sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> — because they said, He has an unclean spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> And his brethren and his mother come, and standing without sent to him calling him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And a crowd sat around him. And they said to him, Behold, thy mother and thy brethren seek thee without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And he answered them, saying, Who is my mother or my brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And looking around in a circuit at those that were sitting around him, he says, Behold my mother and my brethren:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> for whosoever shall do the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.35 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.</span>
