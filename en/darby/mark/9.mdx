<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Isaiah 33:17; Matthew 17:1–8"> And after six days Jesus takes with &#91;him&#93; Peter and James and John, and takes them up on a high mountain by themselves apart. And he was transfigured before them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 28:3; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 7:14"> and his garments became shining, exceeding white &#91;as snow&#93;, such as fuller on earth could not whiten &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 9:30–31; Matthew 17:3–4; Acts 3:21–24"> And there appeared to them Elias with Moses, and they were talking with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; John 14:8–9; Revelation 22:3–4"> And Peter answering says to Jesus, Rabbi, it is good that we should be here; and let us make three tabernacles, for thee one, and for Moses one, and for Elias one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:15–19; Mark 16:5–8"> For he knew not what he should say, for they were filled with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Matthew 3:17; Mark 1:11"> And there came a cloud overshadowing them, and there came a voice out of the cloud, This is my beloved Son: hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 10:16; Acts 8:39–40; Luke 24:31"> And suddenly having looked around, they no longer saw any one, but Jesus alone with themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 12:40; Matthew 17:9–13; Mark 5:43"> And as they descended from the mountain, he charged them that they should relate to no one what they had seen, unless when the Son of man should be risen from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Luke 24:7–8; Luke 18:33–34"> And they kept that saying, questioning among themselves, what rising from among &#91;the&#93; dead was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:14"> And they asked him saying, Why do the scribes say that Elias must first have come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 52:14"> And he answering said to them, Elias indeed, having first come, restores all things; and how is it written of the Son of man that he must suffer much, and be set at nought:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 14:3–11; Matthew 17:12–13; Matthew 11:14"> but I say unto you that Elias also is come, and they have done to him whatever they would, as it is written of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:37–42; Matthew 17:14–20; Luke 11:53–54"> And when he came to the disciples he saw a great crowd around them, and scribes disputing against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:30; Mark 9:2–3"> And immediately all the crowd seeing him were amazed, and running to &#91;him&#93;, saluted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 5:30–32; Mark 8:11"> And he asked them, What do ye question with them about?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Luke 11:14; John 4:47"> And one out of the crowd answered him, Teacher, I brought to thee my son, who has a dumb spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 11:23; Matthew 17:16"> and wheresoever it seizes him it tears him, and he foams and gnashes his teeth, and he is withering away. And I spoke to thy disciples, that they might cast him out, and they could not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; John 20:27; Numbers 14:27"> But he answering them says, O unbelieving generation! how long shall I be with you? how long shall I bear with you? bring him to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:26; Luke 4:35; John 8:44"> And they brought him to him. And seeing him the spirit immediately tore him; and falling upon the earth he rolled foaming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:33; Acts 4:22; Mark 5:25"> And he asked his father, How long a time is it that it has been like this with him? And he said, From childhood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:22–28; Mark 5:19; Matthew 20:34"> and often it has cast him both into fire and into waters that it might destroy him: but if thou couldst &#91;do&#93; anything, be moved with pity on us, and help us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:40; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:23"> And Jesus said to him, The 'if thou couldst' is &#91;if thou couldst&#93; believe: all things are possible to him that believes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:5; Hebrews 12:2; Ephesians 2:8"> And immediately the father of the young child crying out said &#91;with tears&#93;, I believe, help mine unbelief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:18; Luke 9:42; Mark 9:15"> But Jesus, seeing that &#91;the&#93; crowd was running up together, rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to him, Thou dumb and deaf spirit, I command thee, come out of him, and enter no more into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Mark 1:26; Mark 9:18"> And having cried out and torn &#91;him&#93; much, he came out; and he became as if dead, so that the most said, He is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 3:7; Mark 1:41"> But Jesus, having taken hold of him by the hand, lifted him up, and he arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 7:17; Matthew 15:15"> And when he was entered into the house, his disciples asked him privately, Wherefore could not we cast him out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 5:15; Daniel 9:3; Matthew 17:20"> And he said to them, This kind can go out by nothing but by prayer and fasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:43–45; Mark 6:31–32"> And going forth from thence they went through Galilee; and he would not that any one knew it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 9:12; Luke 24:44–46"> for he taught his disciples and said to them, The Son of man is delivered into men's hands, and they shall kill him; and having been killed, after three days he shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 2:50; Luke 9:45; Luke 18:34"> But they understood not the saying, and feared to ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 18:1–5; Hebrews 4:13; Luke 9:46–48"> And he came to Capernaum, and being in the house, he asked them, Of what were ye reasoning by the way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Matthew 20:21–24; Matthew 18:1–5"> And they remained silent, for by the way they had been reasoning with one another who &#91;was&#93; greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:42–45; Luke 22:26"> And sitting down he called the twelve; and he says to them, If any one would be first, he shall be last of all, and minister of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 10:16; Matthew 19:14–15; Matthew 18:2"> And taking a little child he set it in their midst, and having taken it in his arms he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–5; Luke 10:16; Matthew 10:40–42"> Whosoever shall receive one of such little children in my name, receives me; and whosoever shall receive me, does not receive me, but him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 9:49–50; Numbers 11:26–29; Luke 11:19"> And John answered him saying, Teacher, we saw some one casting out demons in thy name, who does not follow us, and we forbad him, because he does not follow us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Matthew 13:28–29"> But Jesus said, Forbid him not; for there is no one who shall do a miracle in my name, and be able soon &#91;after&#93; to speak ill of me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23"> for he who is not against us is for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Matthew 25:40; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> For whosoever shall give you a cup of water to drink in &#91;my&#93; name, because ye are Christ's, verily I say unto you, he shall in no wise lose his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> And whosoever shall be a snare to one of the little ones who believe &#91;in me&#93;, it were better for him if a millstone were hung about his neck, and he cast into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Matthew 18:8–9; Matthew 25:41"> And if thy hand serve as a snare to thee, cut it off: it is better for thee to enter into life maimed, than having thy two hands to go away into hell, into the fire unquenchable;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> &#91;where their worm dies not, and the fire is not quenched&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:22; Mark 9:43"> And if thy foot serve as a snare to thee, cut it off: it is better for thee to enter into life lame, than having thy two feet to be cast into hell, into the fire unquenchable;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> &#91;where their worm dies not, and the fire is not quenched&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 18:9; Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:22"> And if thine eye serve as a snare to thee, cast it out: it is better for thee to enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.47 Elohim">b</a></sup>God with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into the hell of fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Mark 9:43–45; Matthew 25:41"> where their worm dies not, and the fire is not quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 43:24"> For every one shall be salted with fire, and every sacrifice shall be salted with salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Romans 12:18; Matthew 5:13"> Salt &#91;is&#93; good, but if the salt is become saltless, wherewith will ye season it? Have salt in yourselves, and be at peace with one another.</span>
