<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:16–30; Matthew 13:54–58; Matthew 2:23"> And he went out thence and came to his own country, and his disciples follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:15; John 6:42; Acts 4:13–14"> And when sabbath was come he began to teach in the synagogue, and many hearing were amazed, saying, Whence &#91;has&#93; this &#91;man&#93; these things? and what &#91;is&#93; the wisdom that is given to him, and such works of power are done by his hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–57; John 6:42"> Is not this the carpenter, the son of Mary, and brother of James, and Joses, and Judas, and Simon? and are not his sisters here with us? And they were offended in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:44; Luke 4:24; Matthew 13:57"> But Jesus said to them, A prophet is not despised save in his own country, and among &#91;his&#93; kinsmen, and in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:58; Mark 9:23; Mark 5:23"> And he could not do any work of power there, save that laying his hands on a few infirm persons he healed &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Matthew 8:10; Luke 13:22"> And he wondered because of their unbelief. And he went round the villages in a circuit, teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–10; Luke 10:17–20; Matthew 10:1–5"> And he calls the twelve to &#91;him&#93;; and he began to send them out two &#91;and&#93; two, and gave to them power over the unclean spirits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:3; Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4"> and he commanded them that they should take nothing for the way, save a staff only; no scrip, no bread, no money in their belt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 12:8; Ephesians 6:15"> but be shod with sandals, and put not on two body-coats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Luke 9:4; Matthew 10:11–13"> And he said to them, Wheresoever ye shall enter into a house, there remain till ye shall go thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:14–15; Acts 18:6; Luke 10:10–15"> And whatsoever place shall not receive you nor hear you, departing thence, shake off the dust which is under your feet for a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Luke 13:3; Acts 20:21"> And they went forth and preached that they should repent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:14–15; Mark 6:7; Luke 10:17"> and they cast out many demons, and anointed with oil many infirm, and healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Mark 6:14–29; Luke 23:7–12"> And Herod the king heard &#91;of him&#93; (for his name had become public), and said, John the baptist is risen from among &#91;the&#93; dead, and on this account works of power are wrought by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; Malachi 4:5; Matthew 21:11"> And others said, It is Elias; and others said, It is a prophet, as one of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; Matthew 27:4; Luke 9:9"> But Herod when he heard &#91;it&#93; said, John whom I beheaded, he it is; he is risen &#91;from among the dead&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 11:2; Luke 3:1"> For the same Herod had sent and seized John, and had bound him in prison on account of Herodias, the wife of Philip his brother, because he had married her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; 1 Kings 22:14"> For John said to Herod, It is not lawful for thee to have the wife of thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Ephesians 4:26–27; Genesis 39:17–20"> But Herodias kept it &#91;in her mind&#93; against him, and wished to kill him, and could not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5; 2 Chronicles 24:15–22"> for Herod feared John knowing that he was a just and holy man, and kept him safe; and having heard him, did many things, and heard him gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 2:18; Genesis 40:20; 1 Peter 4:3"> And a holiday being come, when Herod, on his birthday, made a supper to his grandees, and to the chiliarchs, and the chief &#91;men&#93; of Galilee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 1:10–12; Matthew 14:6; Daniel 5:2"> and the daughter of the same Herodias having come in, and danced, pleased Herod and those that were with &#91;him&#93; at table; and the king said to the damsel, Ask of me whatsoever thou wilt and I will give it thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6"> And he swore to her, Whatsoever thou shalt ask me I will give thee, to half of my kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 31:31; Ezekiel 19:2–3; Genesis 27:8–11"> And she went out, and said to her mother, What should I ask? And she said, The head of John the baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Numbers 7:19–89; Numbers 7:13"> And immediately going in with haste to the king, she asked saying, I desire that thou give me directly upon a dish the head of John the baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:24–25; Matthew 14:9; Matthew 27:3–5"> And the king, &#91;while&#93; made very sorry, on account of the oaths and those lying at table with &#91;him&#93; would not break his word with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 14:10–11"> And immediately the king, having sent one of the guard, ordered his head to be brought. And he went out and beheaded him in the prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and brought his head upon a dish, and gave it to the damsel, and the damsel gave it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 14:12; 2 Chronicles 24:16; Matthew 27:57–60"> And his disciples having heard &#91;it&#93;, came and took up his body, and laid it in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:10; Luke 10:17; Matthew 10:2"> And the apostles are gathered together to Jesus. And they related to him all things, &#91;both&#93; what they had done and what they had taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 3:20; John 6:1; Mark 1:45"> And he said to them, Come ye yourselves apart into a desert place and rest a little. For those coming and those going were many, and they had not leisure even to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 8:2–9; Mark 6:45; Mark 4:36"> And they went away apart into a desert place by ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 6:2; Matthew 15:29–31; Mark 6:54–55"> And many saw them going, and recognised them, and ran together there on foot, out of all the cities, and got &#91;there&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Luke 9:11; Numbers 27:17"> And on leaving &#91;the ship&#93; &#91;Jesus&#93; saw a great crowd, and he was moved with compassion for them, because they were as sheep not having a shepherd. And he began to teach them many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:5–15; Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:15–21"> And when it was already late in the day, his disciples coming to him say, The place is desert, and it is already late in the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 3:21; Mark 5:31"> send them away that they may go into the country and villages around, and buy themselves bread, for they have not anything they can eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 15:32–33; Mark 8:2–3"> And he answering said to them, Give ye them to eat. And they say to him, Shall we go and buy two hundred denarii worth of bread and give them to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:34; Mark 8:5; John 6:9"> And he says to them, How many loaves have ye? Go &#91;and&#93; see. And when they knew they say, Five, and two fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Corinthians 14:33; Matthew 15:35"> And he ordered them to make them all sit down by companies on the green grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:14–15"> And they sat down in ranks by hundreds and by fifties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Mark 7:34; Luke 9:16"> And having taken the five loaves and the two fishes, looking up to heaven, he blessed, and broke the loaves, and gave &#91;them&#93; to his disciples that they might set &#91;them&#93; before them. And the two fishes he divided among all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 9:17; Mark 8:8–9; Matthew 15:37–38"> And they all ate and were satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 8:19–20"> And they took up of fragments the fillings of twelve hand-baskets, and of the fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And those that ate of the loaves were five thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 14:22–33; John 6:15–21; Mark 6:32"> And immediately he compelled his disciples to go on board ship, and to go on before to the other side to Bethsaida, while he sends the crowd away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 1:35; Matthew 6:6; Luke 6:12"> And, having dismissed them, he departed into the mountain to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 14:23; John 6:16–17"> And when evening was come, the ship was in the midst of the sea, and he alone upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 24:28; Luke 12:38; Job 9:8"> And seeing them labouring in rowing, for the wind was contrary to them, about the fourth watch of the night he comes to them walking on the sea, and would have passed them by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 14:25–26; Luke 24:37; Job 9:8"> But they, seeing him walking on the sea, thought that it was an apparition, and cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 14:27; John 6:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> For all saw him and were troubled. And immediately he spoke with them, and says to them, Be of good courage: it is I; be not afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Mark 4:39; John 6:21"> And he went up to them into the ship, and the wind fell. And they were exceedingly beyond measure astonished in themselves and wondered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 8:17–21; Luke 24:25; Isaiah 63:17"> for they understood not through the loaves: for their heart was hardened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 14:34–36; John 6:24–25; Luke 5:1"> And having passed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret and made the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 9:10; Philippians 3:10"> And on their coming out of the ship, immediately recognising him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 2:1–3; Mark 3:7–11"> they ran through that whole country around, and began to carry about those that were ill on couches, where they heard that he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 9:20; Luke 6:19; Numbers 15:38–39"> And wherever he entered into villages, or cities, or the country, they laid the sick in the market-places, and besought him that they might touch if it were only the hem of his garment; and as many as touched him were healed.</span>
