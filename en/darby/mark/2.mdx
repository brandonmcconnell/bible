<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:47; Luke 5:18; Luke 18:35–38"> And he entered again into Capernaum after &#91;several&#93; days, and it was reported that he was at &#91;the&#93; house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Mark 1:45; Psalms 40:9"> and straightway many were gathered together, so that there was no longer any room, not even at the door; and he spoke the word to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:18–26; Matthew 9:1–8; Matthew 4:24"> And there come to him &#91;men&#93; bringing a paralytic, borne by four;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 5:19; Deuteronomy 22:8"> and, not being able to get near to him on account of the crowd, they uncovered the roof where he was, and having dug &#91;it&#93; up they let down the couch on which the paralytic lay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; James 5:15; Mark 2:9–10"> But Jesus, seeing their faith, says to the paralytic, Child, thy sins are forgiven &#91;thee&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:21–22; Mark 8:17; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> But certain of the scribes were there sitting, and reasoning in their hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Luke 5:21; Micah 7:18"> Why does this &#91;man&#93; thus speak? he blasphemes. Who is able to forgive sins except<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Acts 8:22; Luke 5:22"> And straightway Jesus, knowing in his spirit that they are reasoning thus within themselves, said to them, Why reason ye these things in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:22–25; Matthew 9:5"> Which is easier, to say to the paralytic, &#91;Thy&#93; sins are forgiven &#91;thee&#93;; or to say, Arise, and take up thy couch and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Matthew 9:6–8; John 5:20–27"> But that ye may know that the Son of man has power on earth to forgive sins, he says to the paralytic,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 1:41; John 5:8–10; John 6:63"> To thee I say, Arise, take up thy couch and go to thine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 9:33; Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:16"> And he rose up straightway, and, having taken up his couch, went out before &#91;them&#93; all, so that all were amazed, and glorified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.12 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, saying, We never saw it thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 4:1; Proverbs 1:20–22; Luke 19:48"> And he went out again by the sea, and all the crowd came to him, and he taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:27–32; Luke 6:15; Matthew 9:9–13"> And passing by, he saw Levi the &#91;son&#93; of Alphaeus sitting at the tax-office, and says to him, Follow me. And he rose up and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:1; Luke 5:29–30; Matthew 9:10–11"> And it came to pass as he lay at table in his house, that many tax-gatherers and sinners lay at table with Jesus and his disciples; for they were many, and they followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 19:7; Luke 18:11"> And the scribes and the Pharisees, seeing him eating with sinners and tax-gatherers, said to his disciples, Why &#91;is it&#93; that he eats and drinks with tax-gatherers and sinners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:12–13; Luke 15:7; Luke 5:31–32"> And Jesus having heard &#91;it&#93; says to them, They that are strong have not need of a physician, but those who are ill. I have not come to call righteous &#91;men&#93;, but sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:12; Matthew 6:18; Matthew 9:14–17"> And the disciples of John and the Pharisees were fasting; and they come and say to him, Why do the disciples of John and &#91;the disciples&#93; of the Pharisees fast, but thy disciples fast not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 25:1–10; Genesis 29:22; Judges 14:10–11"> And Jesus said to them, Can the sons of the bride-chamber fast while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them they cannot fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Revelation 21:9; Isaiah 54:5"> But days will come when the bridegroom shall have been taken away from them, and then shall they fast in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:16; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Isaiah 57:16"> No one sews a patch of new cloth on an old garment: otherwise its new filling-up takes from the old &#91;stuff&#93;, and there is a worse rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:17; Psalms 119:83; Luke 5:37–38"> And no one puts new wine into old skins; otherwise the wine bursts the skins, and the wine is poured out, and the skins will be destroyed; but new wine is to be put into new skins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–8; Deuteronomy 23:24–25; Luke 6:1–5"> And it came to pass that he went on the sabbath through the cornfields; and his disciples began to walk on, plucking the ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:10; Hebrews 12:3; Matthew 12:2"> And the Pharisees said to him, Behold, why do they on the sabbath what is not lawful?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 10:26; Matthew 22:31"> And he said to them, Have ye never read what David did when he had need and hungered, he and those with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; Leviticus 24:5–9; 1 Samuel 21:1"> how he entered into the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.26 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, in &#91;the section of&#93; Abiathar &#91;the&#93; high priest, and ate the shew-bread, which it is not lawful unless for the priests to eat, and gave even to those that were with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 2:16; Luke 6:9; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And he said to them, The sabbath was made on account of man, not man on account of the sabbath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 12:8; Revelation 1:10; John 5:17"> so that the Son of man is lord of the sabbath also.</span>
