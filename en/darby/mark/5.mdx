<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> And they came to the other side of the sea, to the country of the Gadarenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> And immediately on his going out of the ship there met him out of the tombs a man possessed by an unclean spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> who had his dwelling in the tombs; and no one was able to bind him, not even with chains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> because he had been often bound with fetters and chains, and the chains had been torn asunder by him, and the fetters were shattered; and no one was able to subdue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> And continually night and day, in the tombs and in the mountains, he was crying and cutting himself with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalms 66:3; Luke 4:41"> But seeing Jesus from afar off, he ran and did him homage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> and crying with a loud voice he says, What have I to do with thee, Jesus, Son of the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God? I adjure thee by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, torment me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> For he said to him, Come forth, unclean spirit, out of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> And he asked him, What is thy name? And he says to him, Legion is my name, because we are many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> And he besought him much that he would not send them away out of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> Now there was there just at the mountain a great herd of swine feeding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> and they besought him, saying, Send us into the swine that we may enter into them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> And Jesus &#91;immediately&#93; allowed them. And the unclean spirits going out entered into the swine, and the herd rushed down the steep slope, into the sea (about two thousand), and were choked in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> And those that were feeding them fled and reported it in the city and in the country. And they went out to see what it was that had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> And they come to Jesus, and they see the possessed of demons sitting &#91;and&#93; clothed and sensible, &#91;him&#93; that had had the legion: and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they that had seen &#91;it&#93; related to them how it had happened to the &#91;man&#93; possessed by demons, and concerning the swine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> And they began to beg him to depart from their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> And as he went on board ship, the man that had been possessed by demons besought him that he might be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> And he suffered him not, but says to him, Go to thine home to thine own people, and tell them how great things the Lord has done for thee, and has had mercy on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> And he went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him; and all wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> And Jesus having passed over in the ship again to the other side, a great crowd gathered to him; and he was by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> And &#91;behold&#93; there comes one of the rulers of the synagogue, by name Jairus, and seeing him, falls down at his feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> and he besought him much, saying, My little daughter is at extremity; &#91;I pray&#93; that thou shouldest come and lay thy hands upon her so that she may be healed, and may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> And he went with him, and a large crowd followed him and pressed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> And a certain woman who had had a flux of blood twelve years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalms 108:12"> and had suffered much under many physicians, and had spent everything she had and had found no advantage from it, but had rather got worse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> having heard concerning Jesus, came in the crowd behind and touched his clothes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> for she said, If I shall touch but his clothes I shall be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalms 30:2; Mark 3:10"> And immediately her fountain of blood was dried up, and she knew in her body that she was cured from the scourge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> And immediately Jesus, knowing in himself the power that had gone out of him, turning round in the crowd said, Who has touched my clothes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> And his disciples said to him, Thou seest the crowd pressing on thee, and sayest thou, Who touched me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And he looked round about to see her who had done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> But the woman, frightened and trembling, knowing what had taken place in her, came and fell down before him, and told him all the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> And he said to her, Daughter, thy faith has healed thee; go in peace, and be well of thy scourge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While he was yet speaking, they come from the ruler of the synagogue's &#91;house&#93;, saying, Thy daughter has died, why troublest thou the teacher any further?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> But Jesus &#91;immediately&#93;, having heard the word spoken, says to the ruler of the synagogue, Fear not; only believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And he suffered no one to accompany him save Peter and James, and John the brother of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> And he comes to the house of the ruler of the synagogue, and sees the tumult, and people weeping and wailing greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> And entering in he says to them, Why do ye make a tumult and weep? the child has not died, but sleeps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> And they derided him. But he, having put &#91;them&#93; all out, takes with &#91;him&#93; the father of the child, and the mother, and those that were with him, and enters in where the child was lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> And having laid hold of the hand of the child, he says to her, Talitha koumi, which is, interpreted, Damsel, I say to thee, Arise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> And immediately the damsel arose and walked, for she was twelve years old. And they were astonished with great astonishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> And he charged them much that no one should know this; and he desired that &#91;something&#93; should be given her to eat.</span>
