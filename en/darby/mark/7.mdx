<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> And the Pharisees and some of the scribes, coming from Jerusalem, are gathered together to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> and seeing some of his disciples eat bread with defiled, that is, unwashed, hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> (for the Pharisees and all the Jews, unless they wash their hands diligently, do not eat, holding what has been delivered by the ancients;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> and &#91;on coming&#93; from the market-place, unless they are washed, they do not eat; and there are many other things which they have received to hold, the washing of cups and vessels, and brazen utensils, and couches),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> then the Pharisees and the scribes ask him, Why do thy disciples not walk according to what has been delivered by the ancients, but eat the bread with defiled hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> But he answering said to them, Well did Esaias prophesy concerning you hypocrites, as it is written, This people honour me with their lips, but their heart is far away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> But in vain do they worship me, teaching &#91;as their&#93; teachings commandments of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> &#91;For&#93;, leaving the commandment of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.8 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, ye hold what is delivered by men &#91;to keep&#93; — washings of vessels and cups, and many other such like things ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> And he said to them, Well do ye set aside the commandment of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, that ye may observe what is delivered by yourselves &#91;to keep&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> For Moses said, Honour thy father and thy mother; and, he who speaks ill of father or mother, let him surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> But ye say, If a man say to his father or his mother, &#91;It is&#93; corban (that is, gift), whatsoever thou mightest have profit from me by...</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And ye no longer suffer him to do anything for his father or his mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> making void the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God by your traditional teaching which ye have delivered; and many such like things ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> And having called again the crowd, he said to them, Hear me, all &#91;of you&#93;, and understand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> There is nothing from outside a man entering into him which can defile him; but the things which go out from him, those it is which defile the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> If any one have ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> And when he went indoors from the crowd, his disciples asked him concerning the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> And he says to them, Are ye also thus unintelligent? Do ye not perceive that all that is outside entering into the man cannot defile him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> because it does not enter into his heart but into his belly, and goes out into the draught, purging all meats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> And he said, That which goes forth out of the man, that defiles the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> For from within, out of the heart of men, go forth evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> thefts, covetousness, wickednesses, deceit, licentiousness, a wicked eye, injurious language, haughtiness, folly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> all these wicked things go forth from within and defile the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> And he rose up and went away thence into the borders of Tyre and Sidon; and having entered into a house he would not have any one know &#91;it&#93;, and he could not be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> But immediately a woman, whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, having heard of him, came and fell at his feet</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> (and the woman was a Greek, Syrophenician by race), and asked him that he would cast the demon out of her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> But &#91;Jesus&#93; said to her, Suffer the children to be first filled; for it is not right to take the children's bread and cast it to the dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> But she answered and says to him, Yea, Lord; for even the dogs under the table eat of the children's crumbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> And he said to her, Because of this word, go thy way, the demon is gone out of thy daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> And having gone away to her house she found the demon gone out, and her daughter lying on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> And again having left the borders of Tyre and Sidon, he came to the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> And they bring to him a deaf &#91;man&#93; who could not speak right, and they beseech him that he might lay his hand on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> And having taken him away from the crowd apart, he put his fingers to his ears; and having spit, he touched his tongue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> and looking up to heaven he groaned, and says to him, Ephphatha, that is, Be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> And immediately his ears were opened, and the band of his tongue was loosed and he spoke right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> And he charged them that they should speak to no one &#91;of it&#93;. But so much the more he charged them, so much the more abundantly they proclaimed it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> and they were astonished above measure, saying, He does all things well; he makes both the deaf to hear, and the speechless to speak.</span>
