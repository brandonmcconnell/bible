<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> And he began to say to them in parables, A man planted a vineyard, and made a fence round &#91;it&#93; and dug a wine-vat, and built a tower, and let it out to husbandmen, and left the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> And he sent a bondman to the husbandmen at the season, that he might receive from the husbandmen of the fruit of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> But they took him, and beat &#91;him&#93;, and sent &#91;him&#93; away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And again he sent to them another bondman; and &#91;at&#93; him they &#91;threw stones, and&#93; struck &#91;him&#93; on the head, and sent &#91;him&#93; away with insult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> And &#91;again&#93; he sent another, and him they killed; and many others, beating some and killing some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> Having yet therefore one beloved son, he sent also him to them the last, saying, They will have respect for my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But those husbandmen said to one another, This is the heir: come, let us kill him and the inheritance will be ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> And they took him and killed him, and cast him forth out of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> What therefore shall the lord of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the husbandmen, and will give the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Have ye not even read this scripture, The stone which they that builded rejected, this has become the corner-stone:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> this is of &#91;the&#93; Lord, and it is wonderful in our eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> And they sought to lay hold of him, and they feared the crowd; for they knew that he had spoken the parable of them. And they left him and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> And they send to him certain of the Pharisees and of the Herodians, that they might catch him in speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> And they come and say to him, Teacher, we know that thou art true, and carest not for any one; for thou regardest not men's person, but teachest the way of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.14 Elohim">a</a></sup>God with truth: Is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> Should we give, or should we not give? But he knowing their hypocrisy said unto them, Why tempt ye me? Bring me a denarius that I may see &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> And they brought &#91;it&#93;. And he says to them, Whose &#91;is&#93; this image and superscription? And they said to him, Caesar's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> And Jesus answering said to them, Pay what is Caesar's to Caesar, and what is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.17 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.17 Elohim">c</a></sup>God. And they wondered at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> And Sadducees come to him, that say there is no resurrection; and they demanded of him saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if any one's brother die, and leave a wife behind, and leave no children, that his brother shall take his wife, and raise up seed to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> There were seven brethren; and the first took a wife, and dying did not leave seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the second took her and died, and neither did he leave seed; and the third likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the seven &#91;took her and&#93; did not leave seed. Last of all the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, when they shall rise again, of which of them shall she be wife, for the seven had her as wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> And Jesus answering said to them, Do not ye therefore err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.24 Elohim">d</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For when they rise from among &#91;the&#93; dead they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as angels &#91;who are&#93; in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> But concerning the dead that they rise, have ye not read in the book of Moses, in &#91;the section of&#93; the bush, how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="12.26 Elohim">e</a></sup>God spoke to him, saying, I &#91;am&#93; the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="12.26 Elohim">f</a></sup>God of Abraham, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="12.26 Elohim">g</a></sup>God of Isaac, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="12.26 Elohim">h</a></sup>God of Jacob?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="12.27 Elohim">i</a></sup>God of &#91;the&#93; dead, but of &#91;the&#93; living. Ye therefore greatly err.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> And one of the scribes who had come up, and had heard them reasoning together, perceiving that he had answered them well, demanded of him, Which is &#91;the&#93; first commandment of all?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> And Jesus answered him, &#91;The&#93; first commandment of all &#91;is&#93;, Hear, Israel: the Lord our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="12.29 Elohim">j</a></sup>God is one Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> and thou shalt love the Lord thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="12.30 Elohim">k</a></sup>God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thine understanding, and with all thy strength. This is &#91;the&#93; first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> And a second like it &#91;is&#93; this: Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. There is not another commandment greater than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> And the scribe said to him, Right, teacher; thou hast spoken according to &#91;the&#93; truth. For he is one, and there is none other besides him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> and to love him with all the heart, and with all the intelligence, and with all the soul, and with all the strength, and to love one's neighbour as one's self, is more than all the burnt-offerings and sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> And Jesus, seeing that he had answered intelligently, said to him, Thou art not far from the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="12.34 Elohim">l</a></sup>God. And no one dared question him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> And Jesus answering said &#91;as he was&#93; teaching in the temple, How do the scribes say that the Christ is son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> &#91;for&#93; David himself said &#91;speaking&#93; in the Holy Spirit, The Lord said to my Lord, Sit on my right hand until I put thine enemies &#91;as&#93; footstool of thy feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> David himself &#91;therefore&#93; calls him Lord, and whence is he his son? And the mass of the people heard him gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> And he said to them in his doctrine, Beware of the scribes, who like to walk about in long robes, and salutations in the marketplaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and first seats in the synagogues, and first places at suppers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> who devour the houses of widows, and as a pretext make long prayers. These shall receive a severer judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> And Jesus, having sat down opposite the treasury, saw how the crowd was casting money into the treasury; and many rich cast in much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And a poor widow came and cast in two mites, which is a farthing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> And having called his disciples to &#91;him&#93; he said to them, Verily I say unto you, This poor widow has cast in more than all who have cast into the treasury:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> for all have cast in of that which they had in abundance, but she of her destitution has cast in all that she had, the whole of her living.</span>
