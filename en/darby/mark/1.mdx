<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> Beginning of the glad tidings of Jesus Christ, Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> as it is written in &#91;Isaiah&#93; the prophet, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, who shall prepare thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> Voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare the way of &#91;the&#93; Lord, make his paths straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> There came John baptising in the wilderness, and preaching &#91;the&#93; baptism of repentance for remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> And there went out to him all the district of Judaea, and all they of Jerusalem, and were baptised by him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> And John was clothed in camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins, and ate locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 13:25; Luke 3:16; John 1:27"> And he preached, saying, There comes he that is mightier than I after me, the thong of whose sandals I am not fit to stoop down and unloose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> I indeed have baptised you with water, but he shall baptise you with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:13–17; Matthew 2:23; Luke 3:21–22"> And it came to pass in those days &#91;that&#93; Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptised by John at the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:31–34; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 42:1"> And straightway going up from the water, he saw the heavens parting asunder, and the Spirit, as a dove, descending upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Mark 9:7; Matthew 3:17"> And there came a voice out of the heavens: Thou art my beloved Son, in thee I have found my delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4:1–13"> And immediately the Spirit drives him out into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; Hebrews 4:15; Exodus 34:28"> And he was in the wilderness forty days tempted by Satan, and was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 4:12; Luke 8:1"> But after John was delivered up, Jesus came into Galilee preaching the glad tidings of the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.14 Elohim">b</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:36–38; Matthew 4:17"> and saying, The time is fulfilled and the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.15 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has drawn nigh; repent and believe in the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> And walking by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon, and Andrew, &#91;Simon's&#93; brother, casting out a net in the sea, for they were fishers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> And Jesus said to them, Come after me, and I will make you become fishers of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> and straightway leaving their trawl-nets they followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> And going on thence a little, he saw James the &#91;son&#93; of Zebedee, and John his brother, and these &#91;were&#93; in the ship repairing the trawl-nets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> and straightway he called them; and leaving their father Zebedee in the ship with the hired servants, they went away after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> And they go into Capernaum. And straightway on the sabbath he entered into the synagogue and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> And they were astonished at his doctrine, for he taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> And there was in their synagogue a man &#91;possessed&#93; by an unclean spirit, and he cried out</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> saying, Eh! what have we to do with thee, Jesus, Nazarene? Art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the holy one of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.24 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Hold thy peace and come out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> And the unclean spirit, having torn him, and uttered a cry with a loud voice, came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> And all were amazed, so that they questioned together among themselves, saying, What is this? what new doctrine is this? for with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> And his fame went out straightway into the whole region of Galilee around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> And straightway going out of the synagogue, they came with James and John into the house of Simon and Andrew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> And the mother-in-law of Simon lay in a fever. And straightway they speak to him about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalms 116:12; Mark 5:41"> And he went up to &#91;her&#93; and raised her up, having taken her by the hand, and straightway the fever left her, and she served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> But evening being come, when the sun had gone down, they brought to him all that were suffering, and those possessed by demons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> and the whole city was gathered together at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> And he healed many suffering from various diseases; and he cast out many demons, and did not suffer the demons to speak because they knew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> And rising in the morning long before day, he went out and went away into a desert place, and there prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Simon and those with him went after him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> and having found him, they say to him, All seek thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And he says to them, Let us go elsewhere into the neighbouring country towns, that I may preach there also, for for this purpose am I come forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> And he was preaching in their synagogues in the whole of Galilee, and casting out demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> And there comes to him a leper, beseeching him, and falling on his knees to him, and saying to him, If thou wilt thou canst cleanse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> But Jesus, moved with compassion, having stretched out his hand, touched him, and says to him, I will, be thou cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> And as he spoke straightway the leprosy left him, and he was cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> And having sharply charged him, he straightway sent him away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> and says to him, See thou say nothing to any one, but go, shew thyself to the priest, and offer for thy cleansing what Moses ordained, for a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But he, having gone forth, began to proclaim &#91;it&#93; much, and to spread the matter abroad, so that he could no longer enter openly into the city, but was without in desert places, and they came to him from every side.</span>
