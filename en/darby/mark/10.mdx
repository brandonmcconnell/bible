<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Mark 4:2; John 11:7"> And rising up thence he comes into the coasts of Judaea, and the other side of the Jordan. And again crowds come together to him, and, as he was accustomed, again he taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 11:53–54; John 8:6"> And Pharisees coming to &#91;him&#93; asked him, Is it lawful for a man to put away &#91;his&#93; wife? (tempting him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 4:21; Luke 10:25"> But he answering said to them, What did Moses command you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31–32; Isaiah 50:1"> And they said, Moses allowed to write a bill of divorce, and to put away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 19:8; Nehemiah 9:26; Acts 7:51"> And Jesus answering said to them, In view of your hard-heartedness he wrote this commandment for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2; Genesis 1:1"> but from &#91;the&#93; beginning of &#91;the&#93; creation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God made them male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5–6; Ephesians 5:31"> For this cause a man shall leave his father and mother and shall be united to his wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Genesis 2:24; 1 Corinthians 6:16"> and the two shall be one flesh: so that they are no longer two but one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 7:1–3; 1 Chronicles 7:10–13"> What therefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God has joined together, let not man separate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 9:33; Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> And again in the house the disciples asked him concerning this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:31–32; Matthew 19:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11"> And he says to them, Whosoever shall put away his wife and shall marry another, commits adultery against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:11; 1 Corinthians 7:13"> And if a woman put away her husband and shall marry another, she commits adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–15; Luke 18:15–17; Exodus 10:9–11"> And they brought little children to him that he might touch them. But the disciples rebuked those that brought &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 19:14; Matthew 18:10; Matthew 18:4"> But Jesus seeing &#91;it&#93;, was indignant, and said to them, Suffer the little children to come to me; forbid them not; for of such is the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.14 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:17; John 3:3–6"> Verily I say to you, Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.15 Elohim">d</a></sup>God as a little child, shall in no wise enter into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 9:36; Isaiah 40:11; John 21:15–17"> And having taken them in his arms, having laid his hands on them, he blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 19:16–30; Luke 18:18–30; Romans 2:7"> And as he went forth into the way, a person ran up to &#91;him&#93;, and kneeling to him asked him, Good Teacher, what shall I do that I may inherit eternal life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:19; Matthew 19:17; Romans 3:12"> But Jesus said to him, Why callest thou me good? no one is good but one, &#91;that is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.18 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Romans 13:9; James 2:11"> Thou knowest the commandments: Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Do not defraud, Honour thy father and mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; 2 Timothy 3:5; Philippians 3:6"> And he answering said to him, Teacher, all these things have I kept from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; Revelation 2:4; Luke 18:22"> And Jesus looking upon him loved him, and said to him, One thing lackest thou: go, sell whatever thou hast and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven; and come, follow me, &#91;taking up the cross&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15"> But he, sad at the word, went away grieved, for he had large possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26; Luke 18:24; Mark 3:5"> And Jesus looking around says to his disciples, How difficultly shall they that have riches enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.23 Elohim">f</a></sup>God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:1–3; Proverbs 11:28; Psalms 52:7"> And the disciples were amazed at his words. And Jesus again answering says to them, Children, how difficult it is that those who trust in riches should enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10.24 Elohim">g</a></sup>God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 19:24–25; Luke 18:25; Matthew 23:24"> It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="10.25 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 16:31; 2 Corinthians 11:23; Romans 10:9–13"> And they were exceedingly astonished, saying to one another, And who can be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Job 42:2"> But Jesus looking on them says, With men it is impossible, but not with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="10.27 Elohim">i</a></sup>God; for all things are possible with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="10.27 Elohim">j</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 19:27–30; Luke 18:28–30; Luke 14:33"> Peter began to say to him, Behold, we have left all things and have followed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:23; Matthew 5:10–11; Mark 8:35"> Jesus answering said, Verily I say to you, There is no one who has left house, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, &#91;or wife&#93;, or children, or lands, for my sake and for the sake of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 18:30; Philippians 3:8; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> that shall not receive a hundredfold now in this time: houses, and brethren, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and lands, with persecutions, and in the coming age life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Luke 13:30; Matthew 20:16"> But many first shall be last, and the last first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Luke 18:31–34; Luke 9:51"> And they were in the way going up to Jerusalem, and Jesus was going on before them; and they were amazed, and were afraid as they followed. And taking the twelve again to &#91;him&#93;, he began to tell them what was going to happen to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Matthew 20:17–19; Mark 8:31"> Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of man shall be delivered up to the chief priests and to the scribes, and they shall condemn him to death, and shall deliver him up to the nations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Matthew 16:21; Mark 14:65"> and they shall mock him, and shall scourge him, and shall spit upon him, and shall kill him; and after three days he shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:20–28; 2 Samuel 14:4–11; 1 Kings 2:20"> And there come to him James and John, the sons of Zebedee, saying &#91;to him&#93;, Teacher, we would that whatsoever we may ask thee, thou wouldst do it for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 15:7; 1 Kings 3:5–15; Mark 10:51"> And he said to them, What would ye that I should do for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 16:19; Luke 24:26"> And they said to him, Give to us that we may sit, one on thy right hand, and one on thy left hand, in thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:50; Matthew 20:21–22; Psalms 75:8"> And Jesus said to them, Ye do not know what ye ask. Are ye able to drink the cup which I drink, or be baptised with the baptism that I am baptised with?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 12:2; Revelation 1:9; John 17:14"> And they said to him, We are able. And Jesus said to them, The cup that I drink ye will drink and with the baptism that I am baptised with ye will be baptised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:23; Matthew 25:34; John 17:24"> but to sit on my right hand or on my left is not mine to give, but for those for whom it is prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 20:24; Proverbs 13:10; Romans 12:10"> And the ten having heard &#91;of it&#93;, began to be indignant about James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; Luke 22:25; Matthew 20:25"> But Jesus having called them to &#91;him&#93;, says to them, Ye know that those who are esteemed to rule over the nations exercise lordship over them; and their great men exercise authority over them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 23:8–12; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:35"> but it is not thus among you; but whosoever would be great among you, shall be your minister;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and whosoever would be first of you shall be bondman of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Philippians 2:5–8; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> For also the Son of man did not come to be ministered to, but to minister, and give his life a ransom for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 18:35–1; Matthew 20:29–34; John 9:8"> And they come to Jericho, and as he was going out from Jericho, and his disciples and a large crowd, the son of Timaeus, Bartimaeus, the blind &#91;man&#93;, sat by the wayside begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Matthew 9:27; Revelation 22:16"> And having heard that it was Jesus the Nazaraean, he began to cry out and to say, O Son of David, Jesus, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; Mark 7:26–29; Hebrews 5:7"> And many rebuked him, that he might be silent; but he cried so much the more, Son of David, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 18:40; Matthew 20:32–34; John 11:28"> And Jesus, standing still, desired him to be called. And they call the blind &#91;man&#93;, saying to him, Be of good courage, rise up, he calls thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Hebrews 12:1"> And, throwing away his garment, he started up and came to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 18:41–43; Mark 10:36; Matthew 7:7"> And Jesus answering says to him, What wilt thou that I shall do to thee? And the blind &#91;man&#93; said to him, Rabboni, that I may see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Acts 26:18; Mark 8:25"> And Jesus said to him, Go, thy faith has healed thee. And he saw immediately, and followed him in the way.</span>
