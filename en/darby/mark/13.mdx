<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:1–51; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 10:19"> And as he was going out of the temple, one of his disciples says to him, Teacher, see what stones and what buildings!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:7–8"> And Jesus answering said to him, Seest thou these great buildings? not a stone shall be left upon a stone, which shall not be thrown down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> And as he sat on the mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> Tell us, when shall these things be, and what is the sign when all these things are going to be fulfilled?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> And Jesus answering them began to say, Take heed lest any one mislead you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> For many shall come in my name, saying, It is I, and shall mislead many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> But when ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars, be not disturbed, for &#91;this&#93; must happen, but the end is not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> For nation shall rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there shall be earthquakes in &#91;different&#93; places, and there shall be famines and troubles: these things &#91;are the&#93; beginnings of throes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> But ye, take heed to yourselves, for they shall deliver you up to sanhedrims and to synagogues: ye shall be beaten and brought before rulers and kings for my sake, for a testimony to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> and the gospel must first be preached to all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> But when they shall lead you away to deliver you up, be not careful beforehand as to what ye shall say, &#91;nor prepare your discourse&#93;: but whatsoever shall be given you in that hour, that speak; for ye are not the speakers, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> But brother shall deliver up brother to death, and father child; and children shall rise up against parents, and cause them to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> And ye will be hated of all on account of my name; but he that has endured to the end, he shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> But when ye shall see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not, (he that reads let him consider &#91;it&#93;,) then let those in Judaea flee to the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> and him that is upon the housetop not come down into the house, nor enter &#91;into it&#93; to take away anything out of his house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and him that is in the field not return back to take his garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> But woe to those that are with child and to those that give suck in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And pray that it may not be in winter time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> for those days shall be distress such as there has not been the like since &#91;the&#93; beginning of creation which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.19 Elohim">a</a></sup>God created, until now, and never shall be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> and if &#91;the&#93; Lord had not cut short those days, no flesh should have been saved; but on account of the elect whom he has chosen, he has cut short those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> And then if any one say to you, Lo, here &#91;is&#93; the Christ, or Lo, there, believe &#91;it&#93; not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> For false Christs and false prophets will arise, and give signs and wonders to deceive, if possible, even the elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> But do ye take heed: behold, I have told you all things beforehand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> But in those days, after that distress, the sun shall be darkened and the moon shall not give its light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> and the stars of heaven shall be falling down, and the powers which are in the heavens shall be shaken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> and then shall they see the Son of man coming in clouds with great power and glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> and then shall he send his angels and shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from end of earth to end of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> But learn the parable from the fig-tree: when its branch already becomes tender and puts forth the leaves, ye know that the summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> Thus also ye, when ye see these things happening, know that it is near, at the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> Verily I say unto you, This generation shall in no wise pass away, till all these things take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> The heaven and the earth shall pass away, but my words shall in no wise pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> But of that day or of that hour no one knows, neither the angels who are in heaven, nor the Son, but the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Take heed, watch and pray, for ye do not know when the time is:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> &#91;it is&#93; as a man gone out of the country, having left his house and given to his bondmen the authority, and to each one his work, and commanded the doorkeeper that he should watch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> Watch therefore, for ye do not know when the master of the house comes: evening, or midnight, or cock-crow, or morning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> lest coming suddenly he find you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> But what I say to you, I say to all, Watch.</span>
