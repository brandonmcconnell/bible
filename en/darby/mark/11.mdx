<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1–11; John 12:14–19; Luke 19:29–40"> And when they draw near to Jerusalem, to Bethphage and Bethany, at the mount of Olives, he sends two of his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 21:2–3; Luke 19:30–31"> and says to them, Go into the village which is over against you, and immediately on entering into it ye will find a colt tied, upon which no &#91;child&#93; of man has ever sat: loose it and lead it &#91;here&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:36; Mark 14:15; Psalms 110:3"> And if any one say to you, Why do ye this? say, The Lord has need of it; and straightway he sends it hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> And they departed, and found a colt bound to the door without at the crossway, and they loose him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And some of those who stood there said to them, What are ye doing, loosing the colt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they said to them as Jesus had commanded. And they let them &#91;do it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> And they led the colt to Jesus, and cast their clothes upon it, and he sat on it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> and many strewed their clothes on the way, and others cut down branches from the trees &#91;and went on strewing them on the way&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> And those going on before and those following cried out, Hosanna! blessed &#91;be&#93; he that comes in &#91;the&#93; Lord's name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> Blessed &#91;be&#93; the coming kingdom of our father David. Hosanna in the highest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> And he entered into Jerusalem and into the temple; and having looked round on all things, the hour being already late, he went out to Bethany with the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> And on the morrow, when they were gone out of Bethany, he hungered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> And seeing from afar off a fig-tree which had leaves, he came, if perhaps he might find something on it. And having come up to it he found nothing but leaves, for it was not the time of figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> And answering he said to it, Let no one eat fruit of thee any more for ever. And his disciples heard &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> And they come to Jerusalem, and entering into the temple, he began to cast out those who sold and who bought in the temple, and he overthrew the tables of the moneychangers and the seats of the dove-sellers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and suffered not that any one should carry any package through the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> And he taught saying to them, Is it not written, My house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations? but ye have made it a den of robbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> And the chief priests and the scribes heard &#91;it&#93;, and they sought how they might destroy him; for they feared him, because all the crowd were astonished at his doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> And when it was evening he went forth without the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> And passing by early in the morning they saw the fig-tree dried up from the roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> And Peter, remembering &#91;what Jesus had said&#93;, says to him, Rabbi, see, the fig-tree which thou cursedst is dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; John 14:1; Mark 9:23"> And Jesus answering says to them, Have faith in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.22 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> Verily I say to you, that whosoever shall say to this mountain, Be thou taken away and cast into the sea, and shall not doubt in his heart, but believe that what he says takes place, whatever he shall say shall come to pass for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> For this reason I say to you, All things whatsoever ye pray for and ask, believe that ye receive it, and it shall come to pass for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Colossians 3:13; Ephesians 4:32"> And when ye stand praying, forgive if ye have anything against any one, that your Father also who is in the heavens may forgive you your offences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if ye do not forgive, neither will your Father who is in the heavens forgive your offences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; Psalms 2:1–5"> And they come again to Jerusalem. And as he walked about in the temple, the chief priests and the scribes and the elders come to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> and they say to him, By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority, that thou shouldest do these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> And Jesus answering said to them, I also will ask you one thing, and answer me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> The baptism of John, was it of heaven, or of men? answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> And they reasoned with themselves, saying, If we should say, Of heaven, he will say, Why &#91;then&#93; have ye not believed him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> but should we say, Of men — they feared the people; for all held of John that he was truly a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> And they answering say to Jesus, We do not know. And Jesus &#91;answering&#93; says to them, Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.</span>
