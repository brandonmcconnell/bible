<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Isaiah 30:12; Isaiah 59:13"> Woe to her that is rebellious and corrupted, to the oppressing city!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 22:21; Jeremiah 7:23–28"> She hearkened not to the voice; she received not correction; she confided not in Jehovah; she drew not near her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Ezekiel 22:25–27; Jeremiah 5:6"> Her princes in the midst of her are roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves, that leave nothing for the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Hosea 9:7; Malachi 2:8"> Her prophets are vain-glorious, treacherous persons; her priests profane the sanctuary, they do violence to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 145:17; Lamentations 3:23"> The righteous Jehovah is in the midst of her: he doeth no wrong. Every morning doth he bring his judgment to light; it faileth not: but the unrighteous knoweth no shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Zephaniah 2:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11"> I have cut off nations: their battlements are desolate; I made their streets waste, that none passeth by; their cities are destroyed, so that there is no man, so that there is no inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Jeremiah 36:3; Micah 2:1–2"> I said, Only fear me, receive correction; so her dwelling shall not be cut off, howsoever I may punish her. But they rose early, they corrupted all their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Zephaniah 1:18; Micah 7:7"> Therefore wait ye for me, saith Jehovah, until the day that I rise up to the prey; for my determination is to assemble the nations, that I may gather the kingdoms together, to pour upon them mine indignation, — all my fierce anger: for all the earth shall be devoured with the fire of my jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 11:1; Psalms 22:27; Isaiah 19:18"> For then will I turn to the peoples a pure language, that they may all call upon the name of Jehovah, to serve him with one consent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 18:1"> From beyond the rivers of Cush my suppliants, the daughter of my dispersed, shall bring mine oblation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Isaiah 11:9"> In that day thou shalt not be ashamed for all thy doings wherein thou hast transgressed against me; for then I will take away out of the midst of thee them that exult in thy pride, and thou shalt no more be haughty because of my holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 14:32; Nahum 1:7; Zechariah 13:8–9"> And I will leave in the midst of thee an afflicted and poor people, and they shall trust in the name of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Zephaniah 2:7; Micah 4:7"> The remnant of Israel shall not work unrighteousness, nor speak lies; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth: but they shall feed and lie down, and none shall make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Zechariah 2:10–11; Micah 4:8"> Exult, O daughter of Zion; shout, O Israel; rejoice and be glad with all the heart, O daughter of Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:22; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 33:22"> Jehovah hath taken away thy judgments, he hath cast out thine enemy; the King of Israel, Jehovah, is in the midst of thee; thou shalt not see evil any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Isaiah 35:3–4; Isaiah 41:10"> In that day it shall be said to Jerusalem, Fear not; Zion, let not thy hands be slack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 149:4; Psalms 147:11; Jeremiah 32:41"> Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God is in thy midst, a mighty one that will save: he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love; he will exult over thee with singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 42:2–4; Ezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> I will gather them that sorrow for the solemn assemblies, who were of thee: the reproach of it was a burden &#91;unto them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25–26; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> Behold, at that time I will deal with all them that afflict thee; and I will save her that halted, and gather her that was driven out; and I will make them a praise and a name in all the lands where they have been put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Malachi 3:12; Isaiah 56:5"> At that time will I bring you, yea, at the time that I gather you; for I will make you a name and a praise, among all the peoples of the earth, when I shall turn again your captivity before your eyes, saith Jehovah.</span>
