<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Speak unto the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say unto them, There shall none make himself unclean for a dead person among his peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> except for his immediate relation, who is near unto him — for his mother, and for his father, and for his son, and for his daughter, and for his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and for his sister, a virgin, that is near unto him, who hath had no husband, for her may he make himself unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> He shall not make himself unclean &#91;who is&#93; a chief among his peoples, to profane himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> They shall not make any baldness upon their head, neither shall they shave off the corners of their beard, nor make any cuttings in their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They shall be holy unto their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="21.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and not profane the name of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="21.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; for they present Jehovah's offerings by fire, the bread of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="21.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God; therefore shall they be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> They shall not take as wife a whore, or a dishonoured woman; neither shall they take a woman put away from her husband; for he is holy unto his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="21.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And thou shalt hallow him; for the bread of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="21.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God doth he present: he shall be holy unto thee; for I, Jehovah, who hallow you am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> And the daughter of any priest, if she profane herself by playing the whore, she profaneth her father: she shall be burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> And the high priest among his brethren, on whose head the anointing oil was poured, and who is consecrated to put on the garments, shall not uncover his head, nor rend his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> Neither shall he come near any person dead, nor make himself unclean for his father and for his mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> neither shall he go out of the sanctuary, nor profane the sanctuary of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="21.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God; for the consecration of the anointing oil of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="21.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God is upon him: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> And he shall take a wife in her virginity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> A widow, or a divorced woman, or a dishonoured one, a harlot, these shall he not take; but he shall take as wife a virgin from among his peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> And he shall not profane his seed among his peoples; for I am Jehovah who do hallow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> Speak unto Aaron, saying, Any of thy seed throughout their generations that hath any defect, shall not approach to present the bread of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="21.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> for whatever man hath a defect, he shall not approach: a blind man, or a lame, or he that hath a flat nose, or one limb longer than the other,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> or a man that is broken-footed, or broken-handed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> or hump-backed, or withered, or that hath a spot in his eye, or hath the itch, or scabs, or his testicles broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> No man of the seed of Aaron the priest that hath defect shall come near to present Jehovah's offerings by fire: he hath a defect; he shall not come near to present the bread of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="21.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> The bread of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="21.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, of the most holy and of the holy, shall he eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> only he shall not come in unto the veil, nor shall he draw near unto the altar; for he hath a defect: that he profane not my sanctuaries; for I am Jehovah who do hallow them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> And Moses told it to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the children of Israel.</span>
