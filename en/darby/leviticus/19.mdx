<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Speak unto all the assembly of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Holy shall ye be, for I Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> Ye shall reverence every man his mother, and his father, and my sabbaths shall ye keep: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; Psalms 115:4–7"> Ye shall not turn unto idols, and ye shall not make to yourselves molten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="19.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="19.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> And if ye sacrifice a sacrifice of peace-offering to Jehovah, ye shall sacrifice it for your acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> On the day when ye sacrifice it shall it be eaten, and on the morrow; and that which remaineth until the third day shall be burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 16:18"> And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it is an unclean thing: it shall not be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> And he that eateth it shall bear his iniquity; for he hath profaned the hallowed thing of Jehovah; and that soul shall be cut off from among his peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, and the gleaning of thy harvest thou shalt not gather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> And thy vineyard shalt thou not glean, neither shalt thou gather what hath been left of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and the stranger: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="19.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> Ye shall not steal, and ye shall not deal falsely, and ye shall not lie one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, and profane the name of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="19.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Thou shalt not oppress thy neighbour, neither rob him. The wages of the hired servant shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> Thou shalt not revile a deaf person, and thou shalt not put a stumbling-block before a blind one; but thou shalt fear thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="19.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment; thou shalt not respect the person of the lowly, nor honour the person of the great; in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> Thou shalt not go about as a talebearer among thy people; thou shalt not stand up against the life of thy neighbour: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 2:9; 1 John 2:11; Luke 17:3"> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thy heart; thou shalt earnestly rebuke thy neighbour, lest thou bear sin on account of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> Thou shalt not avenge thyself, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> My statutes shall ye observe. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with another sort; thou shalt not sow thy field with seed of two sorts; and a garment woven of two materials shall not come upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> And if a man lie with a woman for copulation, and she is a bondwoman betrothed to a husband, but not at all ransomed, nor hath freedom been given to her, there shall be a chastisement: they shall not be put to death, for she was not free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> And he shall bring his trespass-offering to Jehovah, unto the entrance of the tent of meeting, a ram for a trespass-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the trespass-offering before Jehovah for his sin which he hath done; and the sin which he hath done shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> And when ye come into the land and plant all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count its fruit as uncircumcised, three years shall it be uncircumcised unto you: it shall not be eaten of;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> and in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy for praise to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; Malachi 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> and in the fifth year shall ye eat the fruit thereof, that it may increase unto you the produce thereof: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="19.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> Ye shall eat nothing with the blood. — Ye shall not practise enchantment, nor use auguries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20"> — Ye shall not shave the corners of your head round, neither shalt thou mutilate the corners of thy beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; 1 Kings 18:28"> And cuttings for a dead person shall ye not make in your flesh, nor put any tattoo writing upon you: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Hosea 4:12–14"> — Do not profane thy daughter, to give her up to whoredom; lest the land practise whoredom, and the land become full of infamy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> — My sabbaths shall ye keep, and my sanctuary shall ye reverence: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> — Turn not unto necromancers and unto soothsayers; seek not after them to make yourselves unclean: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="19.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> Before the hoary head thou shalt rise up, and shalt honour the face of an old man; and thou shalt fear thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="19.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachi 3:5"> And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not molest him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> As one born among you shall the stranger who sojourneth with you be unto you; and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="19.34 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in measure of length, in weight, and in measure of capacity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin shall ye have: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="19.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> And ye shall observe all my statutes, and all mine ordinances, and do them: I am Jehovah.</span>
</div>
