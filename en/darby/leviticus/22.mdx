<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 15:19; Leviticus 22:32"> Speak unto Aaron, and to his sons, that they separate themselves from the holy things of the children of Israel, and that they profane not my holy name in the things that they hallow unto me: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20–21; Numbers 19:13; Psalms 16:11"> Say unto them, Every one of all your seed, throughout your generations, that approacheth the holy things, which the children of Israel hallow unto Jehovah, having his uncleanness upon him, that soul shall be cut off from before me: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:39; Leviticus 15:2–3; Leviticus 15:13–16"> Whatsoever man of the seed of Aaron is a leper, or hath a flux, he shall not eat of the holy things, until he is clean. And he that toucheth any one that is unclean by a dead person, or a man whose seed of copulation hath passed from him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:43–44; Leviticus 15:7; Leviticus 15:19"> or a man that toucheth any crawling thing whereby he becometh unclean, or a man by whom he may become unclean, whatever may be his uncleanness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Hebrews 10:22; Haggai 2:13"> — a person that toucheth any such shall be unclean until even, and shall not eat of the holy things; but he shall bathe his flesh with water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Deuteronomy 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> and when the sun goeth down, he shall be clean, and may afterwards eat of the holy things; for it is his food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 17:15; Exodus 22:31; Ezekiel 44:31"> Of a dead carcase and what is torn shall he not eat, to make himself unclean therewith: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Numbers 18:22; Numbers 18:32"> And they shall keep my charge, lest they bear sin for it, and die by it, if they profane it: I am Jehovah who do hallow them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; 1 Samuel 21:6; Exodus 29:33"> And no stranger shall eat the holy thing; the sojourner with the priest, and the hired servant, shall not eat of the holy thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:13; Exodus 12:44; Numbers 18:11–13"> But if a priest buy any one for money, he may eat of it, and he that is born in his house: they may eat of his food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; Leviticus 21:3"> And a priest's daughter who is &#91;married&#93; to a stranger may not eat of the heave-offering of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:14; Genesis 38:11; Numbers 18:11–19"> But a priest's daughter that becometh a widow, or is divorced, and hath no seed, and returneth unto her father's house, as in her youth, she may eat of her father's food; but no stranger shall eat thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 27:13; Leviticus 5:15–19"> And if a man eat of a holy thing through inadvertence, then he shall put the fifth part thereof unto it, and shall give it unto the priest with the holy thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 18:32; Leviticus 19:8; Ezekiel 22:26"> And they shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel which they offer unto Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:11–12"> and cause them to bear the iniquity of trespass when they eat their holy things; for I am Jehovah who do hallow them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; Deuteronomy 12:6; Numbers 15:3"> Speak unto Aaron, and to his sons, and unto all the children of Israel, and say unto them, Whatever man of the house of Israel, or of the sojourners in Israel, that presenteth his offering for any of his vows, and for any of his voluntary offerings, which they present to Jehovah as a burnt-offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Leviticus 1:3; Ephesians 5:27"> it shall be for your acceptance, without blemish, a male of the oxen, of the sheep, and of the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 15:21"> Nothing that hath a defect shall ye present; for it shall not be acceptable for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 15:8; Numbers 15:3; Leviticus 3:6"> And if any present a sacrifice of peace-offering to Jehovah to accomplish a vow, or a voluntary offering of oxen or small cattle, it shall be without blemish to be accepted: there shall be no defect therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Malachi 1:8; Leviticus 1:9"> Blind, or broken, or maimed, or ulcerous, or with itch, or scabbed — ye shall not present these to Jehovah, nor make an offering by fire of them on the altar to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:18"> A bullock and a sheep that hath a member too long or too short, that mayest thou offer as a voluntary offering; but as a vow it shall not be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Deuteronomy 23:1; Leviticus 21:20"> That which is bruised, or crushed, or broken, or cut shall ye not present to Jehovah; neither in your land shall ye do &#91;the like&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 21:8; Ezra 6:8–10; Numbers 15:14–16"> Nor from the hand of the stranger shall ye present the bread of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, of any of these; for their corruption is in them: a defect is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:30; Leviticus 19:23–24; Leviticus 22:25"> An ox, or a sheep, or a goat, when it is brought forth, shall be seven days under its dam; and from the eighth day and thenceforth it shall be accepted for an offering by fire to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:6–7; Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 34:26"> A cow, or sheep — it and its young shall ye not slaughter in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; Psalms 107:22; Amos 4:5"> And when ye sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to Jehovah, ye shall sacrifice it for your acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 7:15–18; Exodus 16:19–20; Leviticus 19:7"> On that day shall it be eaten: ye shall leave none of it until morning: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Leviticus 19:37; Numbers 15:40"> And ye shall observe my commandments and do them: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Luke 11:2; Matthew 6:9"> And ye shall not profane my holy name; but I will be hallowed among the children of Israel: I am Jehovah who do hallow you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 25:38"> who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.33 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God: I am Jehovah.</span>
