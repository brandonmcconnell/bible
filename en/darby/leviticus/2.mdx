<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:14–18; Leviticus 9:17; Isaiah 66:20"> And when any one will present an oblation to Jehovah, his offering shall be of fine flour; and he shall pour oil on it, and put frankincense thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:12; Leviticus 6:15; Acts 10:4"> And he shall bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests; and he shall take thereout his handful of the flour thereof, and of the oil thereof, with all the frankincense thereof; and the priest shall burn the memorial thereof on the altar, an offering by fire to Jehovah of a sweet odour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 10:12–13; Numbers 18:9; Leviticus 21:22"> And the remainder of the oblation shall be Aaron's and his sons': &#91;it is&#93; most holy of Jehovah's offerings by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Psalms 22:14"> And if thou present an offering of an oblation baken in the oven, it shall be unleavened cakes of fine flour mingled with oil, or unleavened wafers anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:21; Leviticus 7:9"> And if thine offering be an oblation &#91;baken&#93; on the pan, it shall be fine flour unleavened, mingled with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:1–19; Psalms 22:1–21; Mark 14:1–15"> Thou shalt part it in pieces, and pour oil thereon: it is an oblation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; Leviticus 7:9"> And if thine offering be an oblation &#91;prepared&#93; in the cauldron, it shall be made of fine flour with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And thou shalt bring the oblation that is made of these things to Jehovah; and it shall be presented to the priest, and he shall bring it to the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Philippians 4:18; Leviticus 6:15"> And the priest shall take from the oblation a memorial thereof, and shall burn it on the altar, an offering by fire to Jehovah of a sweet odour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3"> And the remainder of the oblation &#91;shall be&#93; Aaron's and his sons': &#91;it is&#93; most holy of Jehovah's offerings by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; Luke 12:1; Exodus 23:18"> No oblation which ye shall present to Jehovah shall be made with leaven; for no leaven and no honey shall ye burn &#91;in&#93; any fire-offering to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Genesis 23:10–11; Exodus 22:29"> As to the offering of the first-fruits, ye shall present them to Jehovah; but they shall not be offered upon the altar for a sweet odour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:24; Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6"> And every offering of thine oblation shalt thou season with salt; neither shalt thou suffer the salt of the covenant of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God to be lacking from thine oblation: with all thine offerings thou shalt offer salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Malachi 1:11; Numbers 28:2"> And if thou present an oblation of thy first-fruits to Jehovah, thou shalt present as the oblation of thy first-fruits green ears of corn roasted in fire, corn beaten out of full ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And thou shalt put oil on it, and lay frankincense thereon: it is an oblation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Romans 8:26–27; Isaiah 61:1"> And the priest shall burn the memorial thereof, &#91;part&#93; of the beaten corn thereof, and &#91;part&#93; of the oil thereof, with all the frankincense thereof: &#91;it is&#93; an offering by fire to Jehovah.</span>
