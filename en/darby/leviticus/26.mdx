<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Deuteronomy 27:15; Numbers 33:52"> Ye shall make yourselves no idols, neither rear you up for yourselves carved image, or statue, nor shall ye set up a figured stone in your land, to bow down unto it; for I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="26.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:30"> Ye shall observe my sabbaths, and my sanctuary shall ye reverence: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 11:13–15; Deuteronomy 28:1–14"> If ye walk in my statutes, and observe my commandments and do them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalms 67:6; Leviticus 25:21"> then I will give your rain in the season thereof, and the land shall yield its produce, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:13; Joel 2:26; Joel 2:19"> and your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage shall reach unto the sowing-time; and ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in your land securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9; Zephaniah 3:13; Psalms 147:14"> And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid; and I will put away the evil beasts out of the land; and the sword shall not go through your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:30"> and five of you shall chase a hundred, and a hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight; and your enemies shall fall beside you by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:6–7; Nehemiah 9:23; Ezekiel 16:62"> And I will turn my face towards you and make you fruitful, and multiply you, and establish my covenant with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:22; 2 Kings 19:29; Luke 12:17"> And ye shall eat old store, and clear away the old because of the new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45"> And I will set my habitation among you; and my soul shall not abhor you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 7:23"> and I will walk among you, and will be your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="26.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and ye shall be to me a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Leviticus 25:38; Psalms 116:16"> I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="26.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, who brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, that ye should not be their bondmen; and I have broken the bands of your yoke, and made you walk upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:18; Jeremiah 17:27"> But if ye hearken not unto me, and do not all these commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Deuteronomy 31:16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> and if ye shall despise my statutes, and if your soul shall abhor mine ordinances, so that ye do not all my commandments, that ye break my covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:8; Jeremiah 5:17; Micah 6:15"> I also will do this unto you — I will even appoint over you terror, consumption, and fever, which shall cause the eyes to fail, and the soul to waste away; and ye shall sow your seed in vain, for your enemies shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 53:5"> And I will set my face against you, that ye may be routed before your enemies; they that hate you shall have dominion over you; and ye shall flee when none pursueth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> And if for this ye hearken not unto me, I will punish you sevenfold more for your sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; 1 Kings 17:1; Daniel 4:37"> and I will break the arrogance of your power; and I will make your heaven as iron, and your earth as bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 127:1; Deuteronomy 11:17; Isaiah 49:4"> and your strength shall be spent in vain, and your land shall not yield its produce; and the trees of the land shall not yield their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:18; Leviticus 26:27"> And if ye walk contrary unto me, and will not hearken unto me, I will bring sevenfold more plagues upon you according to your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:15; Deuteronomy 32:24; Judges 5:6"> And I will send the beasts of the field among you, that they may rob you of your children, and cut off your cattle, and make you few in number; and your streets shall be desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 2:30; Amos 4:6–12"> And if ye will not be disciplined by me through these, but walk contrary unto me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 18:26; 2 Samuel 22:27; Job 9:4"> then will I also walk contrary unto you, and will smite you, even I, sevenfold for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:12; Ezekiel 5:17; Jeremiah 29:17–18"> And I will bring a sword upon you that avengeth with the vengeance of the covenant, and ye shall be gathered together into your cities, and I will send the pestilence among you; and ye shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Isaiah 3:1; Psalms 105:16"> When I break the staff of your bread, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven, and shall deliver you the bread again by weight; and ye shall eat, and not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Leviticus 26:21"> And if for this ye hearken not to me, but walk contrary unto me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Jeremiah 21:5; Isaiah 66:15"> then I will walk contrary unto you also in fury; and I, even I, will chastise you seven-fold for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Lamentations 4:10; Matthew 24:19"> And ye shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 23:20; Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 6:13"> And I will lay waste your high places, and cut down your sun-pillars, and cast your carcases upon the carcases of your idols; and my soul shall abhor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:3; Lamentations 1:10; Hebrews 10:26"> And I will lay waste your cities and desolate your sanctuaries; and I will not smell your sweet odours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 19:8; Jeremiah 18:16"> And I will bring the land into desolation; that your enemies who dwell there in may be astonished at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 20:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–66"> And I will scatter you among the nations, and will draw out the sword after you; and your land shall be desolation, and your cities waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:43; Leviticus 25:2–4"> Then shall the land enjoy its sabbaths all the days of the desolation, when ye are in your enemies' land; then shall the land rest, and enjoy its sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5–6; Romans 8:22"> All the days of the desolation it shall rest, &#91;the days in&#93; which it did not rest on your sabbaths, when ye dwelt therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Isaiah 30:17; Leviticus 26:17"> And as to those that remain of you — I will send faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies, that the sound of a driven leaf shall chase them, and they shall flee, as fleeing from a sword; and they shall fall when none pursueth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 7:12–13; Judges 2:14; Jeremiah 37:10"> and they shall stumble one over another, as it were before a sword, when none pursueth; and ye shall have no power to stand before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Isaiah 27:13; Jeremiah 42:17–18"> And ye shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall eat you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:17; Ezekiel 24:23; Ezekiel 33:10"> And they that remain of you shall waste away through their iniquity in your enemies' lands; and also through the iniquities of their fathers shall they waste away with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Nehemiah 9:2–5; 1 John 1:8–10"> And they shall confess their iniquity, and the iniquity of their fathers, through their unfaithfulness wherein they were unfaithful to me, and also that they have walked contrary unto me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:7; 2 Chronicles 32:26; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7"> so that I also walked contrary unto them, and brought them into the land of their enemies. If then their uncircumcised heart be humbled, and they then accept the punishment of their iniquity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Exodus 6:5; Ezekiel 16:60"> I will remember my covenant with Jacob, and also my covenant with Isaac, and also my covenant with Abraham will I remember; and I will remember the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Leviticus 26:15; John 7:7"> For the land shall be left by them, and shall enjoy its sabbaths, when it is in desolation without them; and they shall accept the punishment of their iniquity; because, even because they despised my judgments, and their soul abhorred my statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 11:2; Nehemiah 9:31; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> And yet for all that, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not despise them, and will not abhor them, to make an end of them utterly, to break my covenant with them, for I am Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="26.44 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 25:38; Ezekiel 20:22; Ezekiel 20:9"> But I will remember toward them the covenant with their ancestors whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt before the eyes of the nations, that I might be their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="26.45 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 6:1"> These are the statutes and ordinances and laws which Jehovah made between him and the children of Israel in mount Sinai, by the hand of Moses.</span>
