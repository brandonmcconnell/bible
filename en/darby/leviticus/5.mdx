<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Proverbs 29:24; Leviticus 19:8"> And if any one sin, and hear the voice of adjuration, and he is a witness whether he hath seen or known &#91;it&#93;, if he do not give information, then he shall bear his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Deuteronomy 14:8; Haggai 2:13"> Or if any one touch any unclean thing, whether it be the carcase of an unclean beast, or the carcase of unclean cattle, or the carcase of an unclean crawling thing, and it be hid from him, — he also is unclean and guilty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–16; Leviticus 15:1–33; Leviticus 12:1–8"> or if he touch the uncleanness of man, any uncleanness of him by which he is defiled, and it be hid from him, when he knoweth &#91;it&#93;, then he is guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–6; Acts 23:12; Judges 11:31"> Or if any one swear, talking rashly with the lips, to do evil or to do good, in everything that a man shall say rashly with an oath, and it be hid from him, when he knoweth &#91;it&#93;, then is he guilty in one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 26:40"> And it shall be, when he shall be guilty in one of these, that he shall confess wherein he hath sinned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:20"> and he shall bring his trespass-offering to Jehovah for his sin which he hath sinned, a female from the small cattle, a sheep or doe goat, for a sin-offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him &#91;to cleanse him&#93; from his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 14:21–22; Matthew 3:16"> And if his hand be not able to bring what is so much as a sheep, then he shall bring for his trespass which he hath sinned two turtle-doves or two young pigeons, to Jehovah; one for a sin-offering, and the other for a burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:15; Leviticus 1:17; Romans 4:25"> And he shall bring them unto the priest, who shall present that which is for the sin-offering first, and pinch off his head at the neck, but shall not divide it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:30; Leviticus 4:34; Leviticus 4:18"> and he shall sprinkle of the blood of the sin-offering on the wall of the altar; and the rest of the blood shall be wrung out at the bottom of the altar: it is a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:14–17; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> And he shall offer the other for a burnt-offering, according to the ordinance. And the priest shall make atonement for him &#91;to cleanse him&#93; from his sin which he hath sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Leviticus 2:1–2; Exodus 16:36"> But if his hand cannot attain to two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons, then he that sinned shall bring for his offering the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a sin-offering: he shall put no oil on it, neither shall he put frankincense thereon; for it is a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 2:2"> And he shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take his handful of it, the memorial thereof, and burn it on the altar, with Jehovah's offerings by fire: it is a sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 4:26; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> And the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his sin which he hath sinned in one of these, and it shall be forgiven him; and it shall be the priest's, as the oblation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 6:6"> If any one act unfaithfully and sin through inadvertence in the holy things of Jehovah, then he shall bring his trespass-offering to Jehovah, a ram without blemish out of the small cattle, according to thy valuation by shekels of silver, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:14; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 27:27"> And he shall make restitution for the wrong that he hath done in the holy things, and shall add the fifth part thereto, and shall give it unto the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the trespass-offering, and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 4:13; Leviticus 4:2–4"> And if any one sin and do against any of all the commandments of Jehovah what should not be done, and hath not known &#91;it&#93;, yet is he guilty, and shall bear his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15–16; 1 Timothy 2:5–6; Leviticus 1:4"> And he shall bring a ram without blemish out of the small cattle, according to thy valuation, as trespass-offering, unto the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him concerning his inadvertence wherein he sinned inadvertently, and knew &#91;it&#93; not, and it shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Malachi 3:8; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21"> It is a trespass-offering: he hath certainly trespassed against Jehovah.</span>
