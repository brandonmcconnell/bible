<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the animals which ye shall eat of all the beasts which are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Whatever hath cloven hoofs, and feet quite split open, and cheweth the cud, among the beasts — that shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Only these shall ye not eat of those that chew the cud, or of those with cloven hoofs: the camel, for it cheweth the cud, but hath not cloven hoofs — it shall be unclean unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> and the rock-badger, for it cheweth the cud, but hath not cloven hoofs — it shall be unclean unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> and the hare, for it cheweth the cud, but hath not cloven hoofs — it shall be unclean unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> and the swine, for it hath cloven hoofs, and feet quite split open, but it cheweth not the cud — it shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch: they shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatever hath fins and scales in waters, in seas and in rivers, these shall ye eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> but all that have not fins and scales in seas and in rivers, of all that swarm in the waters, and of every living soul which is in the waters — they shall be an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They shall be even an abomination unto you: of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase ye shall have in abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Whatever in the waters hath no fins and scales, that shall be an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> And these shall ye have in abomination of the fowls; they shall not be eaten; an abomination shall they be: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the sea-eagle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the falcon, and the kite, after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> every raven after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> and the female ostrich and the male ostrich, and the sea-gull, and the hawk, after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the owl, and the gannet, and the ibis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the swan, and the pelican, and the carrion vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the stork; the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> Every winged crawling thing that goeth upon all four shall be an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yet these shall ye eat of every winged crawling thing that goeth upon all four: those which have legs above their feet with which to leap upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> These shall ye eat of them: the arbeh after its kind, and the solam after its kind, and the hargol after its kind, and the hargab after its kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But every winged crawling thing that hath four feet shall be an abomination unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And by these ye shall make yourselves unclean; whoever toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> And whoever carrieth &#91;ought&#93; of their carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Every beast that hath cloven hoofs, but not feet quite split open, nor cheweth the cud, shall be unclean unto you: every one that toucheth them shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> And whatever goeth on its paws, among all manner of beasts that go upon all four, those are unclean unto you: whoever toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> And he that carrieth their carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even: they shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> And these shall be unclean unto you among the crawling things which crawl on the earth: the mole, and the field-mouse, and the lizard, after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the groaning lizard, and the great red lizard, and the climbing lizard, and the chomet, and the chameleon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These shall be unclean unto you among all that crawl: whoever toucheth them when they are dead, shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> And on whatever any of them when they are dead doth fall, it shall be unclean; all vessels of wood, or garment, or skin, or sack, every vessel wherewith work is done — it shall be put into water, and be unclean until the even; then shall it be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> And every earthen vessel into which &#91;any&#93; of them falleth — whatever is in it shall be unclean; and ye shall break it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> All food that is eaten on which &#91;such&#93; water hath come shall be unclean; and all drink that is drunk shall be unclean, in every &#91;such&#93; vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> And everything where upon &#91;any part&#93; of their carcase falleth shall be unclean; oven and hearth shall be broken down: they are unclean, and shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Nevertheless, a spring or a well, a quantity of water, shall be clean. But he that toucheth their carcase shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> And if any part of their carcase fall upon any sowing-seed which is to be sown, it shall be clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> but if water have been put on the seed, and any part of their carcase fall thereon, it shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> And if any beast which is to you for food die, he that toucheth the carcase thereof shall be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And he that eateth of its carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even: he also that carrieth its carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> And every crawling thing which crawleth on the earth shall be an abomination; it shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Whatever goeth on the belly, and whatever goeth on all four, and all that have a great many feet, of every manner of crawling thing which crawleth on the earth — these ye shall not eat; for they are an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> Ye shall not make yourselves abominable through any crawling thing which crawleth, neither shall ye make yourselves unclean with them, that ye should be defiled thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.44 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God; and ye shall hallow yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy; and ye shall not make yourselves unclean through any manner of crawling thing which creepeth on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am Jehovah who brought you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.45 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> This is the law of cattle, and of fowl, and of every living soul that moveth in the waters, and of every soul that crawleth on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> to make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that is to be eaten and the beast that is not to be eaten.</span>
