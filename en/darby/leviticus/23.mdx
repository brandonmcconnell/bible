<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:4; Leviticus 23:37; Numbers 29:39"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, &#91;Concerning&#93; the set feasts of Jehovah, which ye shall proclaim as holy convocations — these are my set feasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 23:12; Exodus 35:2–3"> Six days shall work be done; but on the seventh day is the sabbath of rest, a holy convocation; no manner of work shall ye do: it is the sabbath to Jehovah in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Exodus 23:14; Leviticus 23:37"> These are the set feasts of Jehovah, holy convocations, which ye shall proclaim in their seasons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Numbers 28:16–17"> In the first month, on the fourteenth of the month, between the two evenings, is the passover to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Deuteronomy 16:8; Exodus 12:15–16"> And on the fifteenth day of this month is the feast of unleavened bread to Jehovah; seven days shall ye eat unleavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 28:18–25; Leviticus 23:8; Leviticus 23:21"> On the first day ye shall have a holy convocation: no manner of servile work shall ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And ye shall present to Jehovah an offering by fire seven days; on the seventh day is a holy convocation: no manner of servile work shall ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 16:9"> Speak unto the children of Israel and say unto them, When ye come into the land that I give unto you, and ye reap the harvest thereof, then ye shall bring a sheaf of the first-fruits of your harvest unto the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 29:24; Leviticus 10:14; Leviticus 9:21"> And he shall wave the sheaf before Jehovah, to be accepted for you; on the next day after the sabbath the priest shall wave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10–12; 1 Peter 1:19; Leviticus 1:10"> And ye shall offer that day when ye wave the sheaf, a he-lamb without blemish, a yearling, for a burnt-offering to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14–16; Joel 1:9; Numbers 28:10"> and the oblation thereof: two tenths of fine flour mingled with oil, an offering by fire to Jehovah for a sweet odour; and the drink-offering thereof, of wine, a fourth part of a hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Genesis 4:4–5; Leviticus 3:17"> And ye shall not eat bread, or roast corn, or green ears, until the same day that ye have brought the offering of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God: &#91;it is&#93; an everlasting statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:8; Deuteronomy 16:9–10"> And ye shall count from the morning after the sabbath, from the day that ye brought the sheaf of the wave-offering, seven weeks; they shall be complete;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Numbers 28:26"> even unto the morning after the seventh sabbath shall ye count fifty days; and ye shall present a new oblation to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Exodus 34:26; Exodus 34:22"> Out of your dwellings shall ye bring two wave-loaves, of two tenths of fine flour; with leaven shall they be baken; &#91;as&#93; first-fruits to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 28:27–31; Leviticus 23:12–13; Malachi 1:13–14"> And ye shall present with the bread seven he-lambs without blemish, yearlings, and one young bullock, and two rams: they shall be a burnt-offering to Jehovah with their oblation, and their drink-offerings, an offering by fire of a sweet odour to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 28:30; Leviticus 4:23–28; Leviticus 7:11–18"> And ye shall sacrifice one buck of the goats for a sin-offering, and two he-lambs, yearlings, for a sacrifice of peace-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Ephesians 2:14; Numbers 18:8–12"> And the priest shall wave them with the bread of the first-fruits as a wave-offering before Jehovah, with the two he-lambs; they shall be holy to Jehovah, for the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Numbers 18:23"> And ye shall make proclamation on that same day — a holy convocation shall it be unto you: no manner of servile work shall ye do: &#91;it is&#93; an everlasting statute in all your dwellings throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:19–21; Leviticus 19:9–10; Isaiah 58:10"> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not in thy harvest entirely reap the corners of thy field, and the gleaning of thy harvest shalt thou not gather: thou shalt leave them unto the poor and to the stranger: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Leviticus 25:9"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, In the seventh month, on the first of the month, shall ye have a rest, a memorial of blowing of trumpets, a holy convocation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:21"> No manner of servile work shall ye do; and ye shall present an offering by fire to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 16:29–31; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 16:24"> Also on the tenth of this seventh month is the day of the atonement: a holy convocation shall it be unto you; and ye shall afflict your souls, and present an offering by fire to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 3:9; Leviticus 16:34; Hebrews 10:10"> And ye shall do no manner of work on that same day; for it is a day of atonement, to make atonement for you before Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 5:2; Leviticus 23:27"> For every soul that is not afflicted on that same day, shall be cut off from among his peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:3; Zephaniah 2:5; Jeremiah 15:7"> And every soul that doeth any manner of work on that same day, the same soul will I destroy from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> No manner of work shall ye do: &#91;it is&#93; an everlasting statute throughout your generations in all your dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 16:31; Matthew 11:28–30; Psalms 69:10–11"> A sabbath of rest shall it be unto you; and ye shall afflict your souls. On the ninth of the month at even, from even unto even, shall ye celebrate your sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 7:2; Nehemiah 8:14; Ezra 3:4"> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the feast of booths seven days to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24–25; Leviticus 23:7–8"> On the first day there shall be a holy convocation: no manner of servile work shall ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:18; John 7:37; 2 Chronicles 7:8–11"> Seven days ye shall present an offering by fire to Jehovah; on the eighth day shall be a holy convocation unto you; and ye shall present an offering by fire to Jehovah: it is a solemn assembly; no manner of servile work shall ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:4; Ecclesiastes 3:1"> These are the set feasts of Jehovah, which ye shall proclaim as holy convocations, to present an offering by fire to Jehovah, a burnt-offering, and an oblation, a sacrifice, and drink-offerings, everything upon its day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 29:39; Leviticus 19:3; Ezra 2:68–69"> besides the sabbaths of Jehovah, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides all your voluntary offerings, which ye give to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:13; Leviticus 23:34"> But on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when ye have gathered in the produce of the land, ye shall celebrate the feast of Jehovah seven days: on the first day there shall be rest, and on the eighth day there shall be rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Revelation 7:9; Deuteronomy 16:14–15"> And ye shall take on the first day the fruit of beautiful trees, palm branches and the boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook; and ye shall rejoice before Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.40 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 29:12; Nehemiah 8:18"> And ye shall celebrate it as a feast to Jehovah seven days in the year: &#91;it is&#93; an everlasting statute throughout your generations; in the seventh month shall ye celebrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; Genesis 33:17; Numbers 24:5"> In booths shall ye dwell seven days; all born in Israel shall dwell in booths;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10–13; Psalms 78:5–6; Exodus 13:14"> that your generations may know that I caused the children of Israel to dwell in booths, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="23.43 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 23:1–2; Matthew 18:20; Leviticus 21:24"> And Moses declared the set feasts of Jehovah to the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
