<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> After the doings of the land of Egypt, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do; and after the doings of the land of Canaan, whither I bring you, shall ye not do; neither shall ye walk in their customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> Mine ordinances shall ye do and my statutes shall ye observe to walk therein: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> And ye shall observe my statutes and my judgments, by which the man that doeth them shall live: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> No one shall approach to any that is his near relation, to uncover his nakedness: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> The nakedness of thy father, and the nakedness of thy mother, shalt thou not uncover: she is thy mother: thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> The nakedness of thy father's wife shalt thou not uncover: it is thy father's nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> The nakedness of thy sister, the daughter of thy father, or daughter of thy mother, born at home, or born abroad — their nakedness shalt thou not uncover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The nakedness of thy son's daughter, or of thy daughter's daughter — their nakedness shalt thou not uncover; for theirs is thy nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The nakedness of thy father's wife's daughter, begotten of thy father: she is thy sister: thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> The nakedness of thy father's sister shalt thou not uncover: she is thy father's near relation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The nakedness of thy mother's sister shalt thou not uncover; for she is thy mother's near relation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> The nakedness of thy father's brother shalt thou not uncover; thou shalt not approach his wife: she is thine aunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> The nakedness of thy daughter-in-law shalt thou not uncover: she is thy son's wife; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> The nakedness of thy brother's wife shalt thou not uncover: it is thy brother's nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> The nakedness of a woman and her daughter shalt thou not uncover; thou shalt not take her son's daughter, nor her daughter's daughter, to uncover her nakedness: they are her near relations: it is wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> And thou shalt not take a wife to her sister, to vex her, to uncover her nakedness beside her, during her life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> And thou shalt not approach a woman in the separation of her uncleanness to uncover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> And thou shalt not lie carnally with thy neighbour's wife, to become unclean with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> And thou shalt not give of thy seed to let them pass through &#91;the fire&#93; to Molech, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> And thou shalt not lie with mankind as one lieth with a woman: it is an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> And thou shalt lie with no beast to become unclean therewith; and a woman shall not stand before a beast to lie down with it: it is confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> Make not yourselves unclean in any of these things; for in all these have the nations which I am casting out before you made themselves unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> And the land hath become unclean; and I visit the iniquity thereof upon it, and the land vomiteth out its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> But ye shall observe my statutes and my judgments, and shall not commit any of all these abominations: the home-born, and the stranger that sojourneth among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> (for all these abominations have the men of the land done, who were before you, and the land hath been made unclean);</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> that the land vomit you not out, when ye make it unclean, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> For whoever committeth any of these abominations, ...the souls that commit them shall be cut off from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> And ye shall observe my charge, that ye commit not &#91;any&#93; of the abominable customs which were committed before you; and ye shall not make yourselves unclean therein: I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
