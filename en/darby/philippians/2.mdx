<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 John 4:12; 1 John 4:7–8"> If then &#91;there be&#93; any comfort in Christ, if any consolation of love, if any fellowship of &#91;the&#93; Spirit, if any bowels and compassions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 1 Peter 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> fulfil my joy, that ye may think the same thing, having the same love, joined in soul, thinking one thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> &#91;let&#93; nothing &#91;be&#93; in the spirit of strife or vain glory, but, in lowliness of mind, each esteeming the other as more excellent than themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Romans 15:1; James 2:8"> regarding not each his own &#91;qualities&#93;, but each those of others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 2:21"> For let this mind be in you which &#91;was&#93; also in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> who, subsisting in the form of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, did not esteem it an object of rapine to be on an equality with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> but emptied himself, taking a bondman's form, taking his place in &#91;the&#93; likeness of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> and having been found in figure as a man, humbled himself, becoming obedient even unto death, and &#91;that the&#93; death of &#91;the&#93; cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> Wherefore also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God highly exalted him, and granted him a name, that which is above every name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of heavenly and earthly and infernal &#91;beings&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ &#91;is&#93; Lord to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father's glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So that, my beloved, even as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much rather in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> for it is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">e</a></sup>God who works in you both the willing and the working according to &#91;his&#93; good pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> Do all things without murmurings and reasonings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> that ye may be harmless and simple, irreproachable children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.15 Elohim">f</a></sup>God in the midst of a crooked and perverted generation; among whom ye appear as lights in &#91;the&#93; world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> holding forth &#91;the&#93; word of life, so as to be a boast for me in Christ's day, that I have not run in vain nor laboured in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> But if also I am poured out as a libation on the sacrifice and ministration of your faith, I rejoice, and rejoice in common with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> In like manner do ye also rejoice, and rejoice with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Thessalonians 3:6–8; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> But I hope in &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus to send Timotheus to you shortly, that I also may be refreshed, knowing how ye get on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; Philippians 2:22"> For I have no one like-minded who will care with genuine feeling how ye get on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Matthew 16:24; Philippians 2:4"> For all seek their own things, not the things of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:2"> But ye know the proof of him, that, as a child a father, he has served with me in the work of the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; Philippians 2:19"> Him therefore I hope to send immediately, as soon as I shall see how it goes with me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="3 John 1:14; Philemon 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> but I trust in &#91;the&#93; Lord that I myself also shall soon come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; Philemon 1:1–2; Philippians 4:3"> but I have thought it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow-workman and fellow-soldier, but your messenger and minister to my need,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> since he had a longing desire after you all, and was distressed because ye had heard that he was sick;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Philippians 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> for he was also sick close to death, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.27 Elohim">g</a></sup>God had mercy on him, and not indeed on him alone, but also on me, that I might not have sorrow upon sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Timothy 1:4; Philippians 2:26–27"> I have sent him therefore the more diligently, that seeing him ye might again rejoice, and that I might be the less sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12; 1 Corinthians 16:18; 1 Timothy 5:17"> Receive him therefore in &#91;the&#93; Lord with all joy, and hold such in honour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 20:24; Philippians 4:10"> because for the sake of the work he drew near even to death, venturing his life that he might fill up what lacked in your ministration toward me.</span>
