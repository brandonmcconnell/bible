<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 5:11; Philippians 4:4; James 1:2"> For the rest, my brethren, rejoice in &#91;the&#93; Lord: to write the same things to you, to me &#91;is&#93; not irksome, and for you safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13; Titus 1:16; Revelation 22:15"> See to dogs, see to evil workmen, see to the concision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 7:6"> For we are the circumcision, who worship by &#91;the&#93; Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and boast in Christ Jesus, and do not trust in flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:18–22"> Though I have &#91;my&#93; trust even in flesh; if any other think to trust in flesh, I rather:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; Romans 11:1; Acts 23:6"> as to circumcision, &#91;I received it&#93; the eighth day; of &#91;the&#93; race of Israel, of &#91;the&#93; tribe of Benjamin, Hebrew of Hebrews; as to &#91;the&#93; law, a Pharisee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:13–14; Acts 8:3; Acts 26:9–10"> as to zeal, persecuting the assembly; as to righteousness which &#91;is&#93; in &#91;the&#93; law, found blameless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:44–46"> but what things were gain to me these I counted, on account of Christ, loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; Romans 8:18; Philippians 3:7"> But surely I count also all things to be loss on account of the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, on account of whom I have suffered the loss of all, and count them to be filth, that I may gain Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Galatians 2:16"> and that I may be found in him, not having my righteousness, which &#91;would be&#93; on the principle of law, but that which is by faith of Christ, the righteousness which &#91;is&#93; of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God through faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 8:17; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> to know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being conformed to his death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Revelation 20:5; Acts 23:6"> if any way I arrive at the resurrection from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11–12; 2 Peter 1:5–8; Psalms 138:8"> Not that I have already obtained &#91;the prize&#93;, or am already perfected; but I pursue, if also I may get possession &#91;of it&#93;, seeing that also I have been taken possession of by Christ &#91;Jesus&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:62; Hebrews 12:1–2; Philippians 3:12"> Brethren, I do not count to have got possession myself; but one thing — forgetting the things behind, and stretching out to the things before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; 1 Peter 5:10; Revelation 3:21"> I pursue, &#91;looking&#93; towards &#91;the&#93; goal, for the prize of the calling on high of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.14 Elohim">c</a></sup>God in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 7:17; James 1:4–5; Proverbs 2:3–6"> As many therefore as &#91;are&#93; perfect, let us be thus minded; and if ye are any otherwise minded, this also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.15 Elohim">d</a></sup>God shall reveal to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:6; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 2:4–5"> But whereto we have attained, &#91;let us&#93; walk in the same steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philippians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Peter 5:3"> Be imitators &#91;all&#93; together of me, brethren, and fix your eyes on those walking thus as you have us for a model;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17; Isaiah 8:11; Galatians 6:12"> (for many walk of whom I have told you often, and now tell you even weeping, that they &#91;are&#93; the enemies of the cross of Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 16:18; Romans 8:5–7; 2 Peter 2:3"> whose end &#91;is&#93; destruction, whose<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.19 Elohim">e</a></sup>god &#91;is&#93; the belly, and &#91;their&#93; glory in their shame, who mind earthly things:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19; Colossians 3:1–3; 2 Corinthians 4:18–1"> for our commonwealth has its existence in &#91;the&#93; heavens, from which also we await the Lord Jesus Christ &#91;as&#93; Saviour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 3:2; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 15:42–56"> who shall transform our body of humiliation into conformity to his body of glory, according to the working of &#91;the&#93; power which he has even to subdue all things to himself.</span>
