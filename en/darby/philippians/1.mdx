<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:1; Acts 16:12–15; Romans 1:1"> Paul and Timotheus, bondmen of Jesus Christ, to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are in Philippi, with &#91;the&#93; overseers and ministers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> grace to you, and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God our Father and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:2–3; Philemon 1:4–5"> I thank my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God for my whole remembrance of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Romans 1:9; 2 John 1:4"> constantly in my every supplication, making the supplication for you all with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:14–15; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 3:14"> because of your fellowship with the gospel, from the first day until now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 4:12"> having confidence of this very thing, that he who has begun in you a good work will complete it unto Jesus Christ's day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3; Philippians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> as it is righteous for me to think this as to you all, because ye have me in your hearts, and that both in my bonds and in the defence and confirmation of the glad tidings ye are all participators in my grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:4"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">c</a></sup>God is my witness how I long after you all in &#91;the&#93; bowels of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:12; Colossians 1:9; 2 Peter 1:5–6"> And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in full knowledge and all intelligence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> that ye may judge of and approve the things that are more excellent, in order that ye may be pure and without offence for Christ's day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; John 15:8; John 15:4–5"> being complete as regards the fruit of righteousness, which &#91;is&#93; by Jesus Christ, to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's glory and praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Romans 8:28; Acts 11:19–21"> But I would have you know, brethren, that the circumstances in which I am have turned out rather to the furtherance of the glad tidings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Ephesians 3:1; Philippians 1:7"> so that my bonds have become manifest &#91;as being&#93; in Christ in all the praetorium and to all others;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Luke 1:74"> and that the most of the brethren, trusting in &#91;the&#93; Lord through my bonds, dare more abundantly to speak the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>God fearlessly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:17–18; Philippians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> Some indeed also for envy and strife, but some also for good will, preach the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:1–2; Psalms 69:26"> These indeed out of love, knowing that I am set for the defence of the glad tidings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:11–12; Philippians 2:3; Philippians 1:7"> but those out of contention, announce the Christ, not purely, supposing to arouse tribulation for my bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 9:50; Mark 9:38–40; 1 Corinthians 15:11"> What is it then? at any rate, in every way, whether in pretext or in truth, Christ is announced; and in this I rejoice, yea, also I will rejoice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Acts 16:7; 2 Corinthians 1:11"> for I know that this shall turn out for me to salvation, through your supplication and &#91;the&#93; supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Isaiah 54:4"> according to my earnest expectation and hope, that in nothing I shall be ashamed, but in all boldness, as always, now also Christ shall be magnified in my body whether by life or by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:8; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> For for me to live &#91;is&#93; Christ, and to die gain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Galatians 2:20; Philippians 1:24"> but if to live in flesh &#91;is my lot&#93;, this is for me worth the while: and what I shall choose I cannot tell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalms 17:15; Revelation 14:13"> But I am pressed by both, having the desire for departure and being with Christ, &#91;for&#93; &#91;it is&#93; very much better,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:7; Philippians 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> but remaining in the flesh &#91;is&#93; more necessary for your sakes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 2:24; 2 Corinthians 1:24; Ephesians 4:11–13"> and having confidence of this, I know that I shall remain and abide along with you all, for your progress and joy in faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 2 Corinthians 5:12; Philippians 3:3"> that your boasting may abound in Christ Jesus through me by my presence again with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 Corinthians 16:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Only conduct yourselves worthily of the glad tidings of the Christ, in order that whether coming and seeing you, or absent, I may hear of what concerns you, that ye stand firm in one spirit, with one soul, labouring together in the same conflict with the faith of the glad tidings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Matthew 10:28"> and not frightened in anything by the opposers, which is to them a demonstration of destruction, but of your salvation, and that from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.28 Elohim">f</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13; Acts 5:41"> because to you has been given, as regards Christ, not only the believing on him but the suffering for him also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 16:19–40; 2 Timothy 4:7"> having the same conflict which ye have seen in me, and now hear of in me.</span>
