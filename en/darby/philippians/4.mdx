<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 7:6–7"> But I rejoiced in &#91;the&#93; Lord greatly, that now however at length ye have revived your thinking of me, though surely ye did also think &#91;of me&#93;, but lacked opportunity.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> I know both how to be abased and I know how to abound. In everything and in all things I am initiated both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer privation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Colossians 1:11"> I have strength for all things in him that gives me power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 10:34"> But ye have done well in taking part in my affliction.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; Acts 17:1; 1 Thessalonians 2:18"> for also in Thessalonica once and even twice ye sent to me for my need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Corinthians 9:9–13"> Not that I seek gift, but I seek fruit abounding to your account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have all things in full supply and abound; I am full, having received of Epaphroditus the things &#91;sent&#93; from you, an odour of sweet savour, an acceptable sacrifice, agreeable to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.18 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 5:10"> But my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.19 Elohim">e</a></sup>God shall abundantly supply all your need according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Psalms 115:1; Revelation 14:7"> But to our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.20 Elohim">f</a></sup>God and Father &#91;be&#93; glory to the ages of ages. Amen.</span>
