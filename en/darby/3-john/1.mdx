<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> The elder to the beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> Beloved, I desire that in all things thou shouldest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalms 119:11"> For I rejoiced exceedingly when &#91;the&#93; brethren came and bore testimony to thy &#91;holding fast the&#93; truth, even as thou walkest in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater joy than these things that I hear of my children walking in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Beloved, thou doest faithfully &#91;in&#93; whatever thou mayest have wrought towards the brethren and that strangers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> (who have witnessed of thy love before &#91;the&#93; assembly,) in setting forward whom on their journey worthily of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, thou wilt do well;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> for for the name have they gone forth, taking nothing of those of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> We therefore ought to receive such, that we may be fellow-workers with the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote something to the assembly; but Diotrephes, who loves to have the first place among them, receives us not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> For this reason, if I come, I will bring to remembrance his works which he does, babbling against us with wicked words; and not content with these, neither does he himself receive the brethren; and those who would he prevents, and casts &#91;them&#93; out of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalms 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Beloved, do not imitate what is evil, but what is good. He that does good is of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God. He that does evil has not seen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius has witness borne to him by all, and by the truth itself; and we also bear witness, and thou knowest that our witness is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I had many things to write to thee, but I will not with ink and pen write to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> but I hope soon to see thee, and we will speak mouth to mouth. Peace &#91;be&#93; to thee. The friends greet thee. Greet the friends by name.</span>
