<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2–3; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 41:23"> And he brought me to the temple; and he measured the posts, six cubits broad on the one side, and six cubits broad on the other side, the breadth of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:17; 2 Chronicles 3:3; 1 Kings 6:2"> And the breadth of the entry was ten cubits, and the sides of the entry were five cubits on this side, and five cubits on that side; and he measured its length, forty cubits, and the breadth, twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16"> And he went inwards, and measured the post of the entry, two cubits; and the entry, six cubits; and the breadth of the entry, seven cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20; Hebrews 9:3–8; Exodus 26:33–34"> And he measured its length, twenty cubits; and the breadth, twenty cubits, before the temple: and he said unto me, This is the most holy &#91;place&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 42:3–14; Ezekiel 41:6–9"> And he measured the wall of the house, six cubits; and the breadth of the side-chambers, four cubits, round about the house on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:10; 1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 40:17"> And the side-chambers were three, chamber over chamber, and thirty in order; and they entered into the wall which the house had for the side-chambers round about, that they might have hold; but they had not hold in the wall of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:8; Hebrews 6:1; Matthew 13:32"> And for the side-chambers there was an enlarging, and it went round about &#91;the house&#93; increasing upward; for the surrounding of the house increased upward round about the house; therefore the house had width upward, and so ascended &#91;from&#93; the lower &#91;story&#93; to the upper, by the middle one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Revelation 21:16"> And I saw that the house had an elevation round about: the foundations of the side-chambers, a full reed, six cubits to the joint.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:11; Ezekiel 42:4; Ezekiel 42:1"> The thickness of the wall, which was for the side-chambers without, was five cubits, as also what was left free along the building of the side-chambers that pertained to the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:17"> And between the cells &#91;and the house&#93; was a width of twenty cubits round about the house on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:9; Ezekiel 42:4"> And the entry of the side-chambers was toward what was left free, one entry toward the north, and one entry toward the south; and the width of the space left free was five cubits round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> And the building that was before the separate place at the end toward the west was seventy cubits broad; and the wall of the building was five cubits thick round about; and its length ninety cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:47"> And he measured the house: the length a hundred cubits; and the separate place, and the building, and its walls, the length a hundred cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the breadth of the front of the house, and of the separate places toward the east, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:3; Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 41:12"> And he measured the length of the building before the separate place, which was at the back of it, with its galleries on the one side and on the other side, a hundred cubits; and the inner temple, and the porches of the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16"> The thresholds, and the closed windows, and the galleries round about the three of them (opposite the thresholds it was wainscoted with wood round about, and from the ground up to the windows, and the windows were covered),</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:15"> &#91;and&#93; above, over the entry, even unto the inner house, and without, and by all the wall round about, within and without, &#91;all was&#93; by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:36; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:22"> And it was made with cherubim and palm-trees, and a palm-tree was between cherub and cherub; and the cherub had two faces:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10"> the face of a man was toward the palm-tree on the one side, and the face of a young lion toward the palm-tree on the other side: &#91;so&#93; was it made upon all the house round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> From the ground unto above the entry were the cherubim and the palm-trees made, and &#91;on&#93; the wall of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:1; 1 Kings 6:33; Ezekiel 40:14"> As for the temple, the door-posts were squared; and the front of the sanctuary had the same appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; Ezekiel 23:41; Malachi 1:7"> The altar was of wood, three cubits high, and its length two cubits; and its corners, and its length, and its walls were of wood. And he said unto me, This is the table which is before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 6:31–35; 2 Chronicles 4:22; Ezekiel 41:1"> And the temple and the sanctuary had two doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; Ezekiel 40:48"> And the doors had two leaves, two turning-leaves: two for the one door, and two leaves for the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> And there were made on them, on the doors of the temple, cherubim and palm-trees, as there were made upon the walls; and there was a wooden portal in front of the porch without,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:16; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:5"> and closed windows and palm-trees on the one side and on the other side, on the sides of the porch and the side chambers of the house and the portals.</span>
