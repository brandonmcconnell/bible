<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Jeremiah 3:15; Isaiah 40:11"> Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel, prophesy; and say unto them, unto the shepherds, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Woe to the shepherds of Israel that feed themselves! Should not the shepherds feed the flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 11:16; Jeremiah 2:30; Ezekiel 33:25–26"> Ye eat the fat, and ye clothe you with the wool; ye kill them that are fattened: &#91;but&#93; ye feed not the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 18:12–13; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:16"> The weak have ye not strengthened, nor have ye healed the sick, and ye have not bound up &#91;what was&#93; broken, neither have ye brought again that which was driven away, neither have ye sought for that which was lost; but with harshness and with rigour have ye ruled over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; 2 Chronicles 18:16; Jeremiah 23:2"> And they were scattered because there was no shepherd; and they became meat to all the beasts of the field, and were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 142:4; 1 Peter 2:25; John 10:16"> My sheep wandered through all the mountains, and upon every high hill, and my sheep have been scattered upon all the face of the earth, and there was none that searched, or that sought for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:2–3; Micah 3:8–9; Psalms 82:1–7"> Therefore, ye shepherds, hear the word of Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Acts 20:33; Ezekiel 34:5–6"> &#91;As&#93; I live, saith the Lord Jehovah, verily because my sheep have been a prey, and my sheep have been meat to every beast of the field, because there was no shepherd, and my shepherds searched not for my flock, but the shepherds fed themselves, and fed not my flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> — therefore, ye shepherds, hear the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Zechariah 10:3; Ezekiel 13:8"> Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I am against the shepherds; and I will require my sheep at their hand, and cause them to cease from feeding the flock: that the shepherds may feed themselves no more; and I will deliver my sheep from their mouth, that they may not be food for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:16; Luke 19:10; Jeremiah 23:3"> For thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold I, &#91;even&#93; I, will both search for my sheep, and tend them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; John 10:11–12; Luke 19:10"> As a shepherd tendeth his flock in the day that he is among his scattered sheep, so will I tend my sheep, and will deliver them out of all places whither they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25–26; Ezekiel 11:17; Isaiah 11:11–16"> And I will bring them out from the peoples, and gather them from the countries, and will bring them to their own land; and I will feed them upon the mountains of Israel by the water-courses, and in all the habitable places of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:9; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 31:8–10"> I will feed them in a good pasture, and upon the high mountains of Israel shall their fold be: there shall they lie down in a good fold, and in a fat pasture they shall feed upon the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Psalms 23:1–2; John 21:15"> I will myself feed my flock, and I will cause them to lie down, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> I will seek the lost, and bring again that which was driven away, and will bind up the broken, and will strengthen that which was sick; but I will destroy the fat and the strong: I will feed them with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20–22; Matthew 25:32–33; Zechariah 10:3"> And as for you, my flock, thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I judge between sheep and sheep, between the rams and the he-goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 11:52; Numbers 16:13"> Is it too small a thing unto you to have eaten up the good pastures, but ye must tread down with your feet the rest of your pastures; and to have drunk of the settled waters, but ye must foul the rest with your feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And my sheep have to eat that which ye have trodden with your feet, and to drink that which ye have fouled with your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; Ezekiel 34:10; Psalms 22:12–16"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah unto them: Behold, &#91;it is&#93; I, and I will judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 11:5; Deuteronomy 33:17; Luke 13:14–16"> Because ye thrust with side and with shoulder, and push all the weak ones with your horns, till ye have scattered them abroad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:2–3; Psalms 72:12–14; Ezekiel 34:10"> — I will save my flock, that they may no more be a prey; and I will judge between sheep and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Jeremiah 30:9; Isaiah 40:11"> And I will set up one shepherd over them, and he shall feed them, even my servant David: he shall feed them, and he shall be their shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> And I Jehovah will be their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="34.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and my servant David a prince in their midst: I Jehovah have spoken &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Ezekiel 37:26; Leviticus 26:6"> And I will make with them a covenant of peace, and will cause evil beasts to cease out of the land; and they shall dwell in safety in the wilderness, and sleep in the woods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Isaiah 44:3; Leviticus 26:4"> And I will make them and the places round about my hill a blessing; and I will cause the shower to come down in its season: there shall be showers of blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Leviticus 26:4; Leviticus 26:13"> And the tree of the field shall yield its fruit, and the earth shall yield its increase; and they shall be in safety in their land, and shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, when I have broken the bands of their yoke and delivered them out of the hand of those that kept them in servitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27; Jeremiah 30:10; Ezekiel 39:26"> And they shall no more be a prey to the nations, neither shall the beast of the earth devour them; but they shall dwell in safety, and none shall make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:29; Isaiah 4:2; Ezekiel 34:26–27"> And I will raise up for them a plant of renown, and they shall be no more consumed with hunger in the land, neither bear the ignominy of the nations any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; Ezekiel 34:24; Psalms 46:11"> And they shall know that I Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="34.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God &#91;am&#93; with them, and that they, the house of Israel, are my people, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:16; Psalms 78:52"> And ye, my flock, the flock of my pasture, are men: I &#91;am&#93; your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="34.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
