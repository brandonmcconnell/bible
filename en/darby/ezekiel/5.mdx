<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 7:20; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 21:5"> And thou, son of man, take thee a sharp knife; a barber's razor shalt thou take; and cause it to pass upon thy head and upon thy beard: and thou shalt take balances to weigh, and divide the &#91;hair&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 4:1–8"> A third part shalt thou burn in the fire in the midst of the city, when the days of the siege are fulfilled; and thou shalt take a third part, &#91;and&#93; smite about it with a knife; and a third part thou shalt scatter to the wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:16; Jeremiah 40:6; Jeremiah 39:10"> And thou shalt take thereof a few in number, and bind them in thy skirts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Jeremiah 41:1–18; 2 Kings 25:25"> and thou shalt take of these again, and cast them into the midst of the fire, and burn them in the fire: from it shall a fire come forth against all the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1; Deuteronomy 4:6; Jeremiah 6:6"> Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: This is Jerusalem: I have set her in the midst of the nations, and the countries are round about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:10; Ezekiel 16:47–48; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> And she hath rebelled against my judgments in wickedness more than the nations, and against my statutes more than the countries that are round about her: for mine ordinances have they refused; and my statutes, they have not walked in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:10–11; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:9–11"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Because ye have been turbulent more than the nations that are round about you, &#91;and&#93; have not walked in my statutes, neither have kept mine ordinances, nor &#91;even&#93; have done according to the ordinances of the nations that are round about you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Ezekiel 21:3"> therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I, even I, am against thee, and will execute judgments in the midst of thee in the sight of the nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:21; Daniel 9:12; Amos 3:2"> and I will do in thee that which I have not done, and whereunto I will not do any more the like, because of all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 12:14"> Therefore the fathers shall eat the sons in the midst of thee, and the sons shall eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments in thee, and the whole remnant of thee will I scatter to all the winds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:20; Ezekiel 7:9; Ezekiel 11:18"> Wherefore, &#91;as&#93; I live, saith the Lord Jehovah, verily because thou hast defiled my sanctuary with all thy detestable things, and with all thine abominations, therefore will I also withdraw mine eye, and it shall not spare, nor will I have any pity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 43:10–11"> A third part of thee shall die by the pestilence, and shall be consumed by the famine in the midst of thee; and a third part shall fall by the sword round about thee; and I will scatter a third part to all the winds, and I will draw out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:24; Ezekiel 36:5–6; Ezekiel 21:17"> And mine anger shall be accomplished, and I will satisfy my fury upon them, and I will comfort myself; and they shall know that I Jehovah have spoken in my jealousy, when I have accomplished my fury upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:4; Nehemiah 2:17; Psalms 79:1–4"> And I will make thee a waste and a reproach among the nations that are round about thee, in the sight of all that pass by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:17; Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:7"> And it shall be a reproach, and a taunt, an instruction, and an astonishment unto the nations that are round about thee, when I shall execute judgments upon thee in anger, and in fury, and in furious rebukes: I, Jehovah, have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Deuteronomy 32:23–24; Ezekiel 14:13"> When I send upon them the evil arrows of famine, that are for &#91;their&#93; destruction, which I send to destroy you, then will I increase the famine upon you, and will break your staff of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 14:21; Leviticus 26:22"> And I will send upon you famine and evil beasts, which shall bereave thee of children; and pestilence and blood shall pass through thee; and I will bring the sword upon thee: I, Jehovah, have spoken.</span>
