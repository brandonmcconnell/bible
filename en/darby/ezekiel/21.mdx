<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Ezekiel 4:7; Ezekiel 25:2"> Son of man, set thy face against Jerusalem, and drop &#91;words&#93; against the holy places, and prophesy against the land of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremiah 21:13; Ezekiel 5:8"> and say to the land of Israel, Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I am against thee, and I will draw forth my sword out of its sheath, and will cut off from thee the righteous and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> Seeing that I will cut off from thee the righteous and the wicked, therefore shall my sword go forth out of its sheath against all flesh, from the south to the north;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> and all flesh shall know that I Jehovah have drawn forth my sword out of its sheath: it shall not return any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> Sigh then, thou son of man; with breaking of the loins, and with bitterness sigh before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Ezekiel 7:26; Isaiah 13:7"> And it shall be, when they say unto thee, Wherefore dost thou sigh? that thou shalt say, Because of the tidings, for it cometh; and every heart shall melt, and all hands shall be feeble, and every spirit shall languish, and all knees shall melt into water: behold, it cometh; it is here, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> Son of man, prophesy, and say, Thus saith Jehovah: Say, A sword, a sword is sharpened, and also furbished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> It is sharpened for sore slaughter, it is furbished that it may glitter. Shall we then make mirth, &#91;saying,&#93; The sceptre of my son contemneth all wood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> And he hath given it to be furbished that it may be handled. The sword, — it is sharpened, and it is furbished to give it into the hand of the slayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> Cry and howl, son of man; for it shall be against my people, it shall be against all the princes of Israel: they are given up to the sword along with my people: smite therefore upon the thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> For the trial &#91;is made&#93;; and what if even the contemning sceptre shall be no &#91;more&#93;? saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> And thou, son of man, prophesy, and smite thy hands together; for &#91;the strokes of&#93; the sword shall be doubled the third time: it is the sword of the slain, the sword that hath slain the great one, which encompasseth them privily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> In order that the heart may melt, and the stumbling-blocks be multiplied, I have set the threatening sword against all their gates: ah! it is made glittering, it is whetted for the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> Gather up &#91;strength&#93;, go to the right hand, turn thee, go to the left, whithersoever thy face is appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:14; Zechariah 6:8"> And I myself will smite my hands together, and I will satisfy my fury: I Jehovah have spoken &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremiah 1:10; Ezekiel 5:1–17"> And thou, son of man, set thee two ways, by which the sword of the king of Babylon may come — out of one land shall they both come — and make thee a signpost, make it at the head of the way to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremiah 49:2; Ezekiel 25:5"> Appoint a way for the coming of the sword to Rabbah of the children of Ammon, and to Judah at the fenced &#91;city&#93; of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> For the king of Babylon standeth at the parting of the way, at the head of the two ways, to use divination: he shaketh &#91;his&#93; arrows, he inquireth of the teraphim, he looketh in the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 26:9; Jeremiah 51:14"> In his right hand is the lot of Jerusalem to appoint battering-rams, to open the mouth for bloodshed, to lift up the voice with shouting, to appoint battering-rams against the gates, to cast mounds, to build siege-towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Ezekiel 21:24; Ezekiel 29:16"> And this shall be a false divination in their sight, for them that have sworn oaths; but he will call to remembrance the iniquity, that they may be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 4:2; Micah 3:10–12; Jeremiah 3:2"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Because ye make your iniquity to be remembered in that your transgressions are discovered, so that in all your doings your sins appear; because ye are come to remembrance, ye shall be taken with the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 21:29; Ezekiel 17:19"> And thou, profane, wicked prince of Israel, whose day is come, at the time of the iniquity of the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> — thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Remove the mitre and take off the crown; what is shall be no &#91;more&#93;. Exalt that which is low, and abase that which is high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Zechariah 9:9"> I will overturn, overturn, overturn it! This also shall be no &#91;more&#93;, until he come whose right it is; and I will give it &#91;to him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:9–10; Zephaniah 2:8–10"> And thou, son of man, prophesy and say, Thus speaketh the Lord Jehovah concerning the children of Ammon, and concerning their reproach; and thou shalt say, A sword, a sword is drawn; for the slaughter is it furbished, that it may consume, that it may glitter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; Psalms 37:13"> whilst they see vanity for thee, whilst they divine a lie unto thee, to lay thee upon the necks of the wicked that are slain, whose day is come at the time of the iniquity of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremiah 47:6–7"> Restore &#91;it&#93; to its sheath. I will judge thee in the place where thou wast created, in the land of thy birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Ezekiel 14:19"> And I will pour out mine indignation upon thee, I will blow upon thee the fire of my wrath, and give thee into the hand of brutish men, skilful to destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:10; Malachi 4:1; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> Thou shalt be for fuel to the fire; thy blood shall be in the midst of the land; thou shalt not be remembered: for I Jehovah have spoken.</span>
