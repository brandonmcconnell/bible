<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:17; Jeremiah 37:19; Ezekiel 22:28"> Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel that prophesy, and say unto them that prophesy out of their own heart, Hear ye the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 23:28–32; Hosea 9:7"> Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Woe unto the foolish prophets, that follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Micah 2:11; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> O Israel, thy prophets have been like foxes in desert places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 58:12"> Ye have not gone up into the breaches, nor made up the fence for the house of Israel, to stand in the battle in the day of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8; Ezekiel 22:28; Jeremiah 28:15"> They have seen vanity and lying divination, that say, Jehovah saith! and Jehovah hath not sent them; and they make &#91;them&#93; to hope that the word will be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Matthew 24:23–24; Ezekiel 13:2–3"> Have ye not seen a vain vision, and spoken a lying divination, when ye say, Jehovah saith; and I have not spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 21:3; Ezekiel 28:22"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Because ye speak vanity, and have seen lies, therefore behold, I am against you, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 87:6; Psalms 69:28; Ezekiel 20:38"> And my hand shall be against the prophets that see vanity and that divine lies: they shall not be in the council of my people, neither shall they be written in the register of the house of Israel, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel: and ye shall know that I &#91;am&#93; the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Ezekiel 13:16; Jeremiah 6:14"> Because, yea because they have seduced my people, saying, Peace! and there is no peace; and one buildeth up a wall, and lo, they daub it with untempered &#91;mortar&#93; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Isaiah 28:2; Nahum 1:7–8"> say unto them which daub it with untempered &#91;mortar&#93; that it shall fall: there shall be an overflowing rain, and ye, O great hailstones, shall fall, and a stormy wind shall burst forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 3:13; Lamentations 2:14–15; Judges 9:38"> And lo, when the wall is fallen, shall it not be said unto you, Where is the daubing with which ye have daubed &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Revelation 16:21; Psalms 148:8"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: I will cause to burst forth a stormy wind in my fury; and there shall be an overflowing rain in mine anger, and hail-stones in fury for utter destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 1:6; Jeremiah 14:15; Jeremiah 6:15"> And I will break down the wall that ye have daubed with untempered &#91;mortar&#93;, and bring it down to the ground, so that the foundation thereof shall be discovered; and it shall fall, and ye shall be destroyed in the midst thereof; and ye shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:13; Psalms 62:3; Nehemiah 4:3"> And I will accomplish my fury upon the wall, and upon them that daub it with untempered &#91;mortar&#93;, and will say unto you, The wall is no &#91;more&#93;, neither they that daubed it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 8:11"> the prophets of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem, and who see a vision of peace for her, and there is no peace, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:2; 2 Kings 22:14; Judges 4:4"> And thou, son of man, set thy face against the daughters of thy people, who prophesy out of their own heart; and prophesy against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Ezekiel 13:20; 2 Timothy 4:3"> and say, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Woe unto the women that sew pillows for all wrists, and that make veils for the head &#91;of persons&#93; of every stature to catch souls! Will ye catch the souls of my people, and will ye save your own souls alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 3:5; Proverbs 28:21; Jeremiah 23:17"> And will ye profane me among my people for handfuls of barley and for morsels of bread, to slay the souls that should not die, and to save the souls alive that should not live, by your lying to my people that listen to lying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:18; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; Ezekiel 13:8–9"> Wherefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I am against your pillows, that the souls which ye catch by their means may fly away; and I will tear them from your arms, and will let the souls go, the souls that ye catch, that they may fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 91:3"> And I will tear your veils and deliver my people out of your hand, and they shall be no more in your hand to be hunted; and ye shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 14:13–17; 2 Peter 2:18–19"> Because with falsehood ye have grieved the heart of the righteous, whom I have not made sad; and have strengthened the hands of the wicked, that he should not return from his wicked way, to save his life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:21; Ezekiel 12:24; Micah 3:6"> therefore ye shall no more see vanity, nor divine divinations; and I will deliver my people out of your hand: and ye shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
