<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 29:17"> And in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth of the month, the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1; Jeremiah 52:4; 2 Kings 25:1"> Son of man, write thee the name of the day, of this selfsame day: on this selfsame day the king of Babylon draws near to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 17:2; Ezekiel 20:49"> And propose a parable unto the rebellious house, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Set on the pot, set &#91;it&#93; on, and also pour water into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Ezekiel 22:18–22; Matthew 7:2"> Gather the pieces thereof into it, every good piece, the thigh and the shoulder; fill &#91;it&#93; with the choice bones:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 39:6"> take the choice of the flock; and also &#91;put&#93; a pile of wood under it, for the bones; make it boil well, and let the bones of it seethe therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Nahum 3:1; Obadiah 1:11"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Woe to the bloody city, to the pot whose rust is therein, and whose rust is not gone out of it! Bring it out piece by piece; let no lot fall upon it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 17:13; Deuteronomy 12:16; Job 16:18"> for her blood is in the midst of her; she set it upon the bare rock; she poured it not upon the ground, to cover it with dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21–22; Revelation 18:5–10; Ezekiel 22:30–31"> That it might cause fury to come up to execute vengeance, I have set her blood upon the bare rock, that it should not be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6; Habakkuk 2:12; Isaiah 31:9"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Woe to the bloody city! I also will make the pile great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Jeremiah 17:3; Lamentations 1:10"> Heap on the wood, kindle the fire, boil thoroughly the flesh, and spice it well, and let the bones be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 1:25"> Then set it empty upon its coals, that it may be hot, and the brass of it may burn, and that its filthiness may be molten in it, &#91;and&#93; that its rust may be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:5; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> She hath exhausted &#91;her&#93; labours, yet her great rust goeth not forth out of her: let her rust be in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:24; Ezekiel 5:13; Zephaniah 3:2"> In thy filthiness is lewdness, for I have purged thee, and thou art not pure. Thou shalt no more be purged from thy filthiness, till I have satisfied my fury upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 3:11"> I Jehovah have spoken &#91;it&#93;: it shall come to pass, and I will do it; I will not go back, neither will I spare, nor will I repent. According to thy ways, and according to thy doings, shall they judge thee, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10; Jeremiah 13:17; Song of Solomon 7:10"> Son of man, behold, I take away from thee the desire of thine eyes with a stroke; yet thou shalt not mourn nor weep, neither shall thy tears run down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:4; 2 Samuel 15:30; Leviticus 10:6"> Sigh in silence, make no mourning for the dead; bind thy turban upon thee, and put thy sandals upon thy feet, and cover not the beard, and eat not the bread of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:29–30"> — And I spoke unto the people in the morning; and at even my wife died. And I did in the morning as I was commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:9; Ezekiel 37:18; Malachi 3:7–8"> And the people said unto me, Wilt thou not tell us what these things are to us, which thou doest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I said to them, The word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 23:25; Psalms 27:4"> Say unto the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I will profane my sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the desire of your eyes, and your soul's longing; and your sons and your daughters whom ye have left behind shall fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 16:4–7; Ezekiel 24:16–17"> And ye shall do as I have done: ye shall not cover the beard, neither eat the bread of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:10; Ezekiel 4:17; Leviticus 26:39"> and your turbans shall be upon your heads, and your sandals upon your feet: ye shall not mourn nor weep; but ye shall waste away in your iniquities and moan one toward another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:3; Isaiah 20:3; Luke 11:29–30"> Thus Ezekiel shall be unto you a sign; according to all that he hath done shall ye do: when it cometh, then ye shall know that I &#91;am&#93; the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Psalms 48:2; Jeremiah 11:22"> And thou, son of man, shall it not be in the day when I take from them their strength, the joy of their glory, the desire of their eyes, and that whereunto they lift up their soul, their sons and their daughters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; Ezekiel 33:21–22; 1 Samuel 4:12–18"> that in that day he that escapeth shall come unto thee, to cause &#91;thine&#93; ears to hear &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:22; Ezekiel 29:21; Luke 21:15"> In that day shall thy mouth be opened to him that is escaped, and thou shalt speak, and be no more dumb. So shalt thou be a sign unto them; and they shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
