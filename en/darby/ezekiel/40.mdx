<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 3:14"> In the twenty-fifth year of our captivity, in the beginning of the year, on the tenth of the month, in the fourteenth year after that the city was smitten, on that same day the hand of Jehovah was upon me, and he brought me thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Daniel 7:1; Daniel 7:7"> In the visions of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="40.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God brought he me into the land of Israel, and set me upon a very high mountain; and upon it was as the building of a city, on the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:3; Revelation 11:1; Daniel 10:5–6"> And he brought me thither, and behold, there was a man whose appearance was like the appearance of brass, with a flax-cord in his hand, and a measuring-reed; and he stood in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Matthew 10:27; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> And the man said unto me, Son of man, behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears, and set thy heart upon all that I shall shew thee; for in order that it might be shewn unto thee art thou brought hither. Declare to the house of Israel all that thou seest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:20; Isaiah 26:1; Revelation 21:12"> And behold, there was a wall on the outside of the house round about, and in the man's hand a measuring-reed of six cubits, &#91;each&#93; of one cubit and a hand breadth. And he measured the breadth of the building, one reed; and the height, one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 11:1"> And he came to the gate which looked toward the east, and went up its steps; and he measured the threshold of the gate, one reed broad; and the other threshold one reed broad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:33; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> And &#91;each&#93; chamber was one reed long and one reed broad; and between the chambers were five cubits; and the threshold of the gate, beside the porch of the gate within, was one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he measured the porch of the gate within, one reed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:19"> And he measured the porch of the gate, eight cubits; and the posts thereof, two cubits; and the porch of the gate was inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the chambers of the gate which was toward the east were three on this side and three on that side: they three were of one measure; and the posts on this side and on that side had one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he measured the breadth of the entry of the gate, ten cubits; &#91;and&#93; the length of the gate, thirteen cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And there was a border before the chambers of one cubit, and a border of one cubit on the other side; and the chambers were six cubits on this side, and six cubits on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he measured the gate from the roof of &#91;one&#93; chamber to the roof &#91;of the other&#93;, a breadth of five and twenty cubits, entry opposite entry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Isaiah 62:9; Ezekiel 42:1"> And he made posts, sixty cubits, and by the post was the court of the gate round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And from the front of the gate of the entrance unto the front of the porch of the inner gate were fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:5; 1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 41:26"> And there were closed windows to the chambers, and to their posts within the gate round about, and likewise to the projections; and the windows round about were inward; and upon &#91;each&#93; post were palm-trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Ezekiel 42:1; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> And he brought me into the outer court, and behold, there were cells, and a pavement made for the court round about: thirty cells were upon the pavement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the pavement was by the side of the gates, answering to the length of the gates, &#91;namely&#93; the lower pavement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27; Ezekiel 46:1–2"> And he measured the breadth from the front of the lower gate unto the front of the inner court outside, a hundred cubits eastward and northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:6"> And the gate of the outer court, that looked toward the north, he measured its length and its breadth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29–30; Ezekiel 40:25–26; Ezekiel 40:10–16"> And its chambers were three on this side and three on that side; and its posts and its projections were according to the measure of the first gate: its length was fifty cubits, and breadth five and twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37; Ezekiel 40:6"> And its windows, and its projections, and its palm-trees were according to the measure of the gate that looked toward the east; and they went up to it by seven steps; and the projections thereof were before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:27–28; Exodus 38:9–12"> And the gate of the inner court was opposite to the gate toward the north, and toward the east; and he measured from gate to gate a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 40:6; Ezekiel 40:20–21"> And he brought me toward the south: and behold, there was a gate toward the south; and he measured its posts and its projections according to these measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:33; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:21–22"> And there were windows to it and to its projections round about, like those windows: the length was fifty cubits, and the breadth five and twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:6"> And there were seven steps to go up to it; and its projections were before them; and it had palm-trees, one on this side and one on that side, upon its posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 40:19"> And there was a gate to the inner court toward the south; and he measured from gate to gate toward the south, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:32"> And he brought me into the inner court by the south gate; and he measured the south gate according to these measures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:7"> and its chambers, and its posts, and its projections, according to these measures; and there were windows to it and to its projections round about: the length was fifty cubits, and the breadth twenty-five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> And there were projections round about, twenty-five cubits long, and five cubits broad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:26; Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:22"> And its projections were toward the outer court; and there were palm-trees upon its posts: and its ascent was &#91;by&#93; eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:28–31"> And he brought me into the inner court toward the east; and he measured the gate according to these measures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:36; Ezekiel 40:25"> and its chambers, and its posts, and its projections, according to these measures; and there were windows to it and to its projections round about: the length was fifty cubits, and the breadth twenty-five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:22; Ezekiel 40:31; Ezekiel 40:37"> And its projections were toward the outer court; and there were palm-trees upon its posts on this side and on that side: and its ascent was &#91;by&#93; eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:2; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 44:4"> And he brought me to the north gate, and he measured &#91;it&#93; according to these measures:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:29; Ezekiel 40:21; Ezekiel 40:16"> its chambers, its posts, and its projections; and there were windows to it round about: the length was fifty cubits, and the breadth twenty-five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:34; Ezekiel 40:31"> And its posts were toward the outer court; and there were palm-trees upon its posts, on this side and on that side: and its ascent was &#91;by&#93; eight steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:6; Ezekiel 40:17; Leviticus 8:21"> And there was a cell and its entry by the posts of the gates; there they rinsed the burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Leviticus 1:3–17; Leviticus 4:2–3"> And in the porch of the gate were two tables on this side, and two tables on that side, to slay thereon the burnt-offering and the sin-offering and the trespass-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And at the side without, at the ascent to the entry of the north gate, were two tables; and on the other side, which was at the porch of the gate, were two tables:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39–40"> four tables on this side, and four tables on that side, by the side of the gate, — eight tables, whereon they slew &#91;the sacrifice&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:39; Exodus 20:25"> — and at the ascent, four tables of hewn stone, of a cubit and a half long, and a cubit and a half broad, and one cubit high; whereon also they laid the instruments with which they slew the burnt-offering and the sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 1:8; Leviticus 1:6; Leviticus 8:20"> And the double hooks of a hand breadth were fastened round about within; and upon the tables &#91;they put&#93; the flesh of the offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:31–32; 1 Chronicles 16:41–43; Ezekiel 40:27"> And outside the inner gate were two cells in the inner court, one at the side of the north gate, and its front towards the south; the other was at the side of the south gate, the front towards the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:23; Psalms 134:1; Leviticus 8:35"> And he said unto me, This cell whose front is towards the south is for the priests, the keepers of the charge of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:15; Ezekiel 43:19; 1 Kings 2:35"> And the cell whose front is toward the north is for the priests, the keepers of the charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, those who, from among the sons of Levi, approach unto Jehovah to minister unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:19; Ezekiel 40:23; Ezekiel 40:27"> And he measured the court, the length a hundred cubits, and the breadth a hundred cubits, four square: and the altar was before the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 6:3"> And he brought me to the porch of the house; and he measured the post of the porch, five cubits on this side, and five cubits on that side; and the breadth of the gate, three cubits on this side, and three cubits on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–21; Ezekiel 40:34; 2 Chronicles 3:17"> The length of the porch was twenty cubits, and the breadth eleven cubits, even by the steps whereby they went up to it; and there were pillars by the posts, one on this side, and one on that side.</span>
