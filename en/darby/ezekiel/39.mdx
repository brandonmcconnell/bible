<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:2–3; Ezekiel 35:3; Nahum 3:5"> And thou, son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I am against thee, O Gog, prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:14; Ezekiel 38:15; Isaiah 37:29"> and I will turn thee back, and lead thee, and will cause thee to come up from the uttermost north, and will bring thee upon the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 76:3; Hosea 1:5; Jeremiah 21:4–5"> And I will smite thy bow out of thy left hand, and will cause thine arrows to fall out of thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 38:21"> Thou shalt fall upon the mountains of Israel, thou, and all thy bands, and the peoples that are with thee: I have given thee to be meat for the birds of prey of every wing, and to the beasts of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:19; Ezekiel 32:4; Jeremiah 8:2"> Thou shalt fall on the open field; for I have spoken &#91;it&#93;, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:16; Amos 1:4; Ezekiel 30:8"> And I will send a fire on Magog, and among them that dwell at ease in the isles: and they shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Exodus 20:7"> And my holy name will I make known in the midst of my people Israel; and I will not suffer my holy name to be profaned any more: and the nations shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah, the Holy One in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:17; Revelation 16:17; Ezekiel 7:2–10"> Behold, it cometh, and shall be done, saith the Lord Jehovah. This is the day whereof I have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Isaiah 66:24; Malachi 1:5"> And they that dwell in the cities of Israel shall go forth, and shall kindle fire, and burn weapons, and shields, and targets, bows, and arrows, and hand-staves, and spears: and they shall make fires with them seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 14:2; Micah 5:8"> And no wood shall be taken out of the field, neither cut down out of the forests; for they shall make fire with the weapons; and they shall spoil those that spoiled them, and plunder those that plundered them, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:18; Numbers 34:11; Ezekiel 38:2"> And it shall come to pass in that day, &#91;that&#93; I will give unto Gog a place there for burial in Israel, the valley of the passers-by to the east of the sea; and it shall stop &#91;the way&#93; of the passers-by; and there shall they bury Gog and all the multitude; and they shall call it, Valley of Hamon-Gog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; Ezekiel 39:14; Ezekiel 39:16"> And seven months shall the house of Israel be burying them, that they may cleanse the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:22; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 33:9"> and all the people of the land shall bury &#91;them&#93;; and it shall be to them for renown in the day that I shall be glorified, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:12; Numbers 19:11–19"> And they shall sever out men of continual employment to go through the land, who, with the passers-by, shall bury those that remain upon the face of the land, to cleanse it: at the end of seven months shall they make a search.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:44"> And the passers-by shall pass through the land, and when &#91;any&#93; seeth a man's bone, he shall set up a sign by it, till the buriers have buried it in the Valley of Hamon-Gog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And also the name of the city shall be Hamonah. Thus shall they cleanse the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:7; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 34:6"> And thou, son of man, thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Speak unto the birds of every wing, and to every beast of the field, Gather yourselves together and come, assemble yourselves on every side to my sacrifice which I sacrifice for you, a great sacrifice upon the mountains of Israel, that ye may eat flesh, and drink blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:12; Jeremiah 51:40; Revelation 19:17–18"> Ye shall eat the flesh of the mighty, and drink the blood of the princes of the earth, of rams, of lambs, and of goats, &#91;and&#93; of bullocks, all of them fatted beasts of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And ye shall eat fat till ye are full, and drink blood till ye are drunken, of my sacrifice which I sacrifice for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 19:18; Haggai 2:22; Psalms 76:5–6"> And ye shall be filled at my table with horses and charioteers, with mighty men, and with all men of war, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Isaiah 37:20"> And I will set my glory among the nations, and all the nations shall see my judgment which I have executed, and my hand which I have laid upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 28:26; Ezekiel 39:28"> And the house of Israel shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="39.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God from that day and forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Isaiah 1:15; Isaiah 59:2"> And the nations shall know that the house of Israel went into captivity for their iniquity, because they were unfaithful against me; and I hid my face from them, and gave them into the hand of their enemies, so that they fell all of them by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:19; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 4:18"> According to their uncleanness and according to their transgressions I did unto them, and I hid my face from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Isaiah 27:12–13; Hosea 1:11"> Therefore, thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Now will I bring again the captivity of Jacob, and have mercy upon the whole house of Israel, and will be jealous for my holy name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 4:4; 1 Kings 4:25; Ezekiel 16:63"> and they shall bear their confusion, and all their unfaithfulness in which they have acted unfaithfully against me, when they shall dwell safely in their land, and none shall make them afraid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> when I have brought them again from the peoples, and gathered them out of their enemies' lands, and am hallowed in them in the sight of many nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:22; Amos 9:9; Isaiah 27:12"> And they shall know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="39.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, in that I caused them to be led into captivity among the nations, and have gathered them unto their own land, and have left none of them any more there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 2:28; 1 John 3:24; Isaiah 32:15"> And I will not hide my face any more from them, for I shall have poured out my Spirit upon the house of Israel, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
