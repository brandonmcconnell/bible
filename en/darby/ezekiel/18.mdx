<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29–30; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 37:11"> What mean ye, ye who use this proverb of the land of Israel, saying, &#91;The&#93; fathers eat sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 33:11–20; Ezekiel 18:30"> &#91;As&#93; I live, saith the Lord Jehovah, ye shall not have any more to use this proverb in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Romans 6:23; Numbers 16:22"> Behold, all the souls are mine; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son is mine: the soul that sinneth, it shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:14; 1 John 3:7; Proverbs 21:3"> And if a man be righteous, and do judgment and justice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:15; Deuteronomy 4:19; Ezekiel 6:13"> — he hath not eaten upon the mountains, nor lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, neither hath defiled his neighbour's wife, nor come near to a woman in her separation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:16; Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12"> and hath not oppressed any; he hath restored to the debtor his pledge, hath not exercised robbery, hath given his bread to the hungry, and covered the naked with a garment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Zechariah 8:16; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> he hath not given forth upon usury, nor taken increase; he hath withdrawn his hand from unrighteousness, hath executed true judgment between man and man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Amos 5:4"> hath walked in my statutes, and kept mine ordinances, to deal faithfully: he is righteous, he shall certainly live, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 John 3:12; John 18:40"> And if he have begotten a son that is violent, a shedder of blood, and that doeth only one of any of these &#91;things&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:14; Matthew 7:21–27; 1 Kings 13:8"> and that doeth not any of those &#91;duties&#93;, but also hath eaten upon the mountains, and defiled his neighbour's wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Ezekiel 8:6; 2 Kings 21:11"> hath oppressed the poor and needy, exercised robbery, hath not restored the pledge, and hath lifted up his eyes to the idols, committed abomination,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 20:9; Ezekiel 3:18"> given forth upon usury, and taken increase; shall he then live? He shall not live: he hath done all these abominations; he shall certainly die; his blood shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Proverbs 23:24; Jeremiah 8:6"> But lo, if he have begotten a son that seeth all his father's sins which he hath done, and considereth, and doeth not such like:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:6–7; Ezekiel 18:11–13"> — he hath not eaten upon the mountains, nor lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel; he hath not defiled his neighbour's wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:7; Isaiah 58:7–10; Job 22:7"> and hath not oppressed any, nor withholden the pledge, neither hath exercised robbery; he hath given his bread to the hungry, and covered the naked with a garment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:8–9; Matthew 18:27–35; Ezekiel 18:13"> he hath withdrawn his hand from the poor, hath not received usury nor increase, hath executed my judgments, &#91;and&#93; walked in my statutes: he shall not die for the iniquity of his father, he shall certainly live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 18:24"> As for his father, because he practised oppression, exercised robbery upon his brother, and did what was not good among his people, behold, he shall die in his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Zechariah 1:3–6; Deuteronomy 5:9"> And ye say, Why doth not the son bear the iniquity of the father? But the son hath done judgment and justice, hath kept all my statutes, and hath done them; he shall certainly live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; 2 Kings 14:6; Ezekiel 18:4"> The soul that sinneth, it shall die. The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son; the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:27–28; Titus 2:11–14; Ezekiel 33:19"> And the wicked, if he turn from all his sins which he hath committed, and keep all my statutes, and do judgment and justice, he shall certainly live, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 7:19; Ezekiel 33:16; Isaiah 43:25"> None of his transgressions which he hath committed shall be remembered against him; in his righteousness which he hath done shall he live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; 1 Timothy 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9"> Have I any pleasure at all in the death of the wicked? saith the Lord Jehovah; is it not in his turning from his way, that he may live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; Proverbs 21:16; 2 John 1:8"> And when the righteous turneth from his righteousness and practiseth what is wrong, &#91;and&#93; doeth according to all the abominations that the wicked doeth, shall he live? None of his righteous acts which he hath done shall be remembered: in his unfaithfulness which he hath wrought, and in his sin which he hath sinned, in them shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:17; Malachi 3:13–15; Malachi 2:17"> And ye say, The way of the Lord is not equal. Hear then, house of Israel. Is not my way equal? are not your ways unequal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When the righteous turneth away from his righteousness, and practiseth what is wrong, and dieth for it; in his wrong that he hath done shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 55:7"> And when the wicked turneth from his wickedness which he hath committed, and doeth judgment and justice, he shall keep his soul alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Luke 15:17–18; James 2:10–12"> Because he considereth, and turneth from all his transgressions which he hath committed, he shall certainly live, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Proverbs 19:3; Ezekiel 18:2"> But the house of Israel say, The way of the Lord is not equal. O house of Israel, are not my ways equal? Is it not your ways that are unequal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 3:2"> Therefore I will judge you, house of Israel, every one according to his ways, saith the Lord Jehovah. Return ye, and turn from all your transgressions; so iniquity shall not be your snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> Cast away from you all your transgressions wherewith ye have transgressed, and make you a new heart and a new spirit: why then will ye die, house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23; Ezekiel 33:11"> For I have no pleasure in the death of him that dieth, saith the Lord Jehovah; therefore turn ye and live.</span>
