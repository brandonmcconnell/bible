<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:2; Ezekiel 26:17; 2 Kings 24:6"> And thou, take thou up a lamentation for the princes of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; Job 4:11"> and say, What was thy mother? A lioness: she lay down among lions, she nourished her whelps in the midst of the young lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:25; 2 Kings 23:31–32; 2 Chronicles 36:1–2"> And she brought up one of her whelps; it became a young lion, and he learned to catch the prey; he devoured men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:33–34; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> And the nations heard of him; he was taken in their pit, and they brought him with nose-rings into the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3; 2 Kings 23:34–37"> And when she saw that she had waited &#91;and&#93; her hope was lost, she took another of her whelps, &#91;and&#93; made him a young lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; 2 Chronicles 36:9; Jeremiah 26:1–24"> And he went up and down among the lions; he became a young lion, and learned to catch the prey; he devoured men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 12:19; Micah 1:2"> And he knew their &#91;desolate&#93; palaces, and he laid waste their cities, so that the land was desolate, and all it contained, by the noise of his roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 19:4; Ezekiel 12:13"> Then the nations set against him on every side from the provinces, and spread their net over him; he was taken in their pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 36:30–31"> And they put him in a cage with nose-rings, and brought him to the king of Babylon; they brought him into strongholds, that his voice should no more be heard upon the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Deuteronomy 8:7; Numbers 24:6–7"> Thy mother was as a vine, in thy rest, planted by the waters: it was fruitful and full of branches by reason of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:3; Psalms 80:15; Daniel 4:11"> And it had strong rods for sceptres of them that bear rule, and its stature was exalted between the thick boughs; and it was conspicuous by its height with the multitude of its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> But it was plucked up in fury, it was cast down to the ground, and the east wind dried up its fruit; its strong rods were broken and withered; the fire consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 19:10; Psalms 63:1"> And now it is planted in the wilderness, in a dry and thirsty ground:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 80:15–16; Ezekiel 19:11; 2 Kings 24:20"> and a fire is gone out of a rod of its branches, &#91;which&#93; hath devoured its fruit; so that it hath no strong rod to be a sceptre for ruling. This is a lamentation, and shall be for a lamentation.</span>
