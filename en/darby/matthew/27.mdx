<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:1; John 18:28; Micah 2:1"> And when it was morning all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus so that they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 20:19; Acts 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:13"> And having bound him they led him away, and delivered him up to Pontius Pilate, the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Luke 22:47–48; Job 20:15–29"> Then Judas, who delivered him up, seeing that he had been condemned, filled with remorse, returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and the elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:30; Titus 1:16; Acts 18:15–17"> saying, I have sinned &#91;in&#93; having delivered up guiltless blood. But they said, What is that to us? see thou &#91;to that&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 1:18–19; 2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Kings 16:18"> And having cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, he left the place, and went away and hanged himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:7–9; Deuteronomy 23:18; Isaiah 61:8"> And the chief priests took the pieces of silver and said, It is not lawful to cast them into the Corban, since it is &#91;the&#93; price of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And having taken counsel, they bought with them the field of the potter for a burying-ground for strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:19; Matthew 28:15; Joshua 4:9"> Wherefore that field has been called Blood-field unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 1:22; Leviticus 27:2–7"> Then was fulfilled that which was spoken through Jeremias the prophet, saying, And I took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was set a price on, whom &#91;they who were&#93; of the sons of Israel had set a price on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 11:13"> and they gave them for the field of the potter, according as &#91;the&#93; Lord commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 23:2–3; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 26:25"> But Jesus stood before the governor. And the governor questioned him, saying, Art thou the King of the Jews? And Jesus said to him, Thou sayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Isaiah 53:7; Matthew 27:14"> And when he was accused of the chief priests and the elders, he answered nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:62; John 18:35; Acts 22:24"> Then says Pilate to him, Hearest thou not how many things they witness against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Psalms 71:7; 1 Corinthians 4:9"> And he answered him not so much as one word, so that the governor wondered exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now at &#91;the&#93; feast the governor was accustomed to release one prisoner to the crowd, whom they would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> And they had then a notable prisoner, named Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> They therefore being gathered together, Pilate said to them, Whom will ye that I release to you, Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> For he knew that they had delivered him up through envy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> But, as he was sitting on the judgment-seat, his wife sent to him, saying, Have thou nothing to do with that righteous &#91;man&#93;; for I have suffered to-day many things in a dream because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should beg for Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the governor answering said to them, Which of the two will ye that I release unto you? And they said, Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate says to them, What then shall I do with Jesus, who is called Christ? They all say, Let him be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–19; Acts 7:57; Acts 23:12–15"> And the governor said, What evil then has he done? But they cried more than ever, saying, Let him be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:4; Psalms 26:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> And Pilate, seeing that it availed nothing, but that rather a tumult was arising, having taken water, washed his hands before the crowd, saying, I am guiltless of the blood of this righteous one: see ye &#91;to it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:19; Acts 5:28; Acts 7:52"> And all the people answering said, His blood &#91;be&#93; on us and on our children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; John 19:1; Mark 15:15"> Then he released to them Barabbas; but Jesus, having scourged &#91;him&#93;, he delivered up that he might be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:28; John 18:33; Acts 10:1"> Then the soldiers of the governor, having taken Jesus with &#91;them&#93; to the praetorium, gathered against him the whole band,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 15:17; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> and having taken off his garment, put on him a scarlet cloak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 20:19; Psalms 69:19–20"> and having woven a crown out of thorns, they put it on his head, and a reed in his right hand; and, bowing the knee before him, they mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 53:7"> And having spit upon him, they took the reed and beat &#91;him&#93; on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Kings 21:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> And when they had mocked him, they took the cloak off him, and put his own clothes on him, and led him away to crucify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:21; Matthew 16:24; Luke 23:26"> And as they went forth they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name; him they compelled to go &#91;with them&#93; that he might bear his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 19:17–19; Mark 15:22–32; Mark 15:34"> And having come to a place called Golgotha, which means Place of a skull,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; Matthew 27:48"> they gave to him to drink vinegar mingled with gall; and having tasted &#91;it&#93;, he would not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; Mark 15:24–32; Luke 23:34"> And having crucified him, they parted his clothes amongst &#91;themselves&#93;, casting lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:44; Mark 15:39"> And sitting down, they kept guard over him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 23:38; John 19:19–22; Mark 15:26"> And they set up over his head his accusation written: This is Jesus, the King of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43; Isaiah 53:12"> Then are crucified with him two robbers, one on the right hand and one on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12; Psalms 69:7–12"> But the passers-by reviled him, shaking their heads</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 26:61; Matthew 4:6; Matthew 4:3"> and saying, Thou that destroyest the temple and buildest it in three days, save thyself. If thou art Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="27.40 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, descend from the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Isaiah 28:22; Psalms 22:12–13"> &#91;And&#93; in like manner the chief priests also, mocking, with the scribes and elders, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 1:49; Matthew 27:37; Acts 4:14"> He saved others, himself he cannot save. He is King of Israel: let him descend now from the cross, and we will believe on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 22:8; John 10:36; Isaiah 37:10"> He trusted upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="27.43 Elohim">b</a></sup>God; let him save him now if he will &#91;have&#93; him. For he said, I am Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="27.43 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:38; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:39–43"> And the robbers also who had been crucified with him cast the same reproaches on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Mark 15:33–34; Luke 23:44–45"> Now from &#91;the&#93; sixth hour there was darkness over the whole land until &#91;the&#93; ninth hour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 53:10"> but about the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani? that is, My<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="27.46 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="27.46 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, why hast thou forsaken me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Mark 15:35–36; Matthew 11:14"> And some of those who stood there, when they heard &#91;it&#93;, said, This &#91;man&#93; calls for Elias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36"> And immediately one of them running and getting a sponge, having filled &#91;it&#93; with vinegar and fixed &#91;it&#93; on a reed, gave him to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But the rest said, Let be; let us see if Elias comes to save him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:30; Isaiah 53:9–12; Psalms 22:14–15"> And Jesus, having again cried with a loud voice, gave up the ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Mark 15:38; Hebrews 6:19"> And lo, the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top to the bottom, and the earth was shaken, and the rocks were rent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> and the tombs were opened; and many bodies of the saints fallen asleep arose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 48:2; Revelation 21:2"> and going out of the tombs after his arising, entered into the holy city and appeared unto many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:36; Mark 15:39; Matthew 27:43"> But the centurion, and they who were with him on guard over Jesus, seeing the earthquake and the things that took place, feared greatly, saying, Truly this &#91;man&#93; was Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="27.54 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Luke 23:48–49; John 19:25–27"> And there were there many women beholding from afar off, who had followed Jesus from Galilee ministering to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 19:25; Mark 15:47–1; Matthew 28:1"> among whom was Mary of Magdala, and Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56"> Now when even was come there came a rich man of Arimathaea, his name Joseph, who also himself was a disciple to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 23:52–53; Mark 15:44–46"> He, going to Pilate, begged the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And Joseph having got the body, wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 28:2; Matthew 27:66"> and laid it in his new tomb which he had hewn in the rock; and having rolled a great stone to the door of the tomb, went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Matthew 27:56"> But Mary of Magdala was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 15:42; John 19:14; John 19:42"> Now on the morrow, which is after the preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees came together to Pilate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 9:22; Matthew 16:21; John 2:19"> saying, Sir, we have called to mind that that deceiver said when he was still alive, After three days I arise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Matthew 28:13; Matthew 12:45"> Command therefore that the sepulchre be secured until the third day, lest his disciples should come and steal him away, and say to the people, He is risen from the dead; and the last error shall be worse than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Matthew 28:11–15; Proverbs 21:30"> And Pilate said to them, Ye have a watch: go, secure it as well as ye know how.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Matthew 27:60; 2 Timothy 2:19"> And they went and secured the sepulchre, having sealed the stone, with the watch &#91;besides&#93;.</span>
