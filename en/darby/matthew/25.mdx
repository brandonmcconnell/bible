<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:35–36; Philippians 2:15–16; Matthew 24:42–51"> Then shall the kingdom of the heavens be made like to ten virgins that having taken their torches, went forth to meet the bridegroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5; Matthew 13:19–23"> And five of them were prudent and five foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:15–16; 2 Timothy 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> They that were foolish took their torches and did not take oil with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:9; 1 John 2:27; 1 John 2:20"> but the prudent took oil in their vessels with their torches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; 2 Peter 3:4–9"> Now the bridegroom tarrying, they all grew heavy and slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 24:44"> But in &#91;the&#93; middle of &#91;the&#93; night there was a cry, Behold, the bridegroom; go forth to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 12:35; Revelation 3:19–20; 2 Peter 3:14"> Then all those virgins arose and trimmed their torches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Luke 12:35; Hebrews 4:1"> And the foolish said to the prudent, Give us of your oil, for our torches are going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–9; Revelation 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> But the prudent answered saying, &#91;We cannot,&#93; lest it might not suffice for us and for you. Go rather to those that sell, and buy for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Matthew 24:44; Luke 12:36–37"> But as they went away to buy, the bridegroom came, and the &#91;ones that were&#93; ready went in with him to the wedding feast, and the door was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:21–23; Hebrews 12:16–17"> Afterwards come also the rest of the virgins, saying, Lord, Lord, open to us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:27"> but he answering said, Verily I say unto you, I do not know you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:42–44; Revelation 16:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:6"> Watch therefore, for ye know not the day nor the hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> For &#91;it is&#93; as &#91;if&#93; a man going away out of a country called his own bondmen and delivered to them his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> And to one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one; to each according to his particular ability, and immediately went away out of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> And he that had received the five talents went and trafficked with them, and made five other talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> In like manner also he that &#91;had received&#93; the two, &#91;he also&#93; gained two others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But he that had received the one went and dug in the earth, and hid the money of his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:12–15; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:7–12"> And after a long time the lord of those bondmen comes and reckons with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> And he that had received the five talents came to &#91;him&#93; and brought five other talents, saying, &#91;My&#93; lord, thou deliveredst me five talents; behold, I have gained five other talents besides them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His lord said to him, Well, good and faithful bondman, thou wast faithful over a few things, I will set thee over many things: enter into the joy of thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> And he also that had received the two talents came to &#91;him&#93; and said, &#91;My&#93; lord, thou deliveredst me two talents; behold, I have gained two other talents besides them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His lord said to him, Well, good and faithful bondman, thou wast faithful over a few things, I will set thee over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> And he also that had received the one talent coming to &#91;him&#93; said, &#91;My&#93; lord, I knew thee that thou art a hard man, reaping where thou hadst not sowed, and gathering from where thou hadst not scattered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> and being afraid I went away and hid thy talent in the earth; behold, thou hast that which is thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Job 15:5–6"> And his lord answering said to him, Wicked and slothful bondman, thou knewest that I reap where I had not sowed, and gather from where I had not scattered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Romans 3:19; Jude 1:15"> thou oughtest then to have put my money to the money-changers, and when I came I should have got what is mine with interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Luke 10:42"> Take therefore the talent from him, and give it to him that has the ten talents:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 8:18; Mark 4:25; Matthew 13:12"> for to every one that has shall be given, and he shall be in abundance; but from him that has not, that even which he has shall be taken from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Revelation 3:15–16; Matthew 13:50"> And cast out the useless bondman into the outer darkness; there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> But when the Son of man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then shall he sit down upon his throne of glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> and all the nations shall be gathered before him; and he shall separate them from one another, as the shepherd separates the sheep from the goats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> and he will set the sheep on his right hand, and the goats on &#91;his&#93; left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; James 2:5; Revelation 21:7"> Then shall the King say to those on his right hand, Come, blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from &#91;the&#93; world's foundation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> for I hungered, and ye gave me to eat; I thirsted, and ye gave me to drink; I was a stranger, and ye took me in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 1:27; 2 Timothy 1:16–18; James 5:14–15"> naked, and ye clothed me; I was ill, and ye visited me; I was in prison, and ye came to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 6:3; 1 Chronicles 29:14; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Then shall the righteous answer him saying, Lord, when saw we thee hungering, and nourished thee; or thirsting, and gave thee to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and when saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in; or naked, and clothed thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and when saw we thee ill, or in prison, and came to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Proverbs 14:31; Matthew 10:42"> And the King answering shall say to them, Verily, I say to you, Inasmuch as ye have done it to one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6–7; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Then shall he say also to those on the left, Go from me, cursed, into eternal fire, prepared for the devil and his angels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="James 2:15–24; John 5:23; Matthew 12:30"> for I hungered, and ye gave me not to eat; I thirsted, and ye gave me not to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was a stranger, and ye took me not in; naked, and ye did not clothe me; ill, and in prison, and ye did not visit me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 7:22; Luke 10:29; Matthew 25:24–27"> Then shall they also answer saying, Lord, when saw we thee hungering, or thirsting, or a stranger, or naked, or ill, or in prison, and have not ministered to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 21:13; Proverbs 17:5; Proverbs 14:31"> Then shall he answer them saying, Verily I say to you, Inasmuch as ye have not done it to one of these least, neither have ye done it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 3:36; John 5:29"> And these shall go away into eternal punishment, and the righteous into life eternal.</span>
