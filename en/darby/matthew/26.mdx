<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Matthew 19:1"> And it came to pass when Jesus had finished all these sayings, he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:1–2; Mark 14:1–2; John 13:1–2"> Ye know that after two days the passover takes place, and the Son of man is delivered up to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:47–53; John 18:24; Mark 14:54"> Then the chief priests and the elders of the people were gathered together to the palace of the high priest who was called Caiaphas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:14; Psalms 2:2; Acts 13:10"> and took counsel together in order that they might seize Jesus by subtlety and kill him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:24; Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5"> but they said, Not in the feast, that there be not a tumult among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8"> But Jesus being in Bethany, in Simon the leper's house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 12:2–3; Luke 7:46; Luke 7:37–38"> a woman, having an alabaster flask of very precious ointment, came to him and poured it out upon his head as he lay at table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:4–6; Mark 14:4; Exodus 5:17"> But the disciples seeing it became indignant, saying, To what end &#91;was&#93; this waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:5–6; Mark 14:5; Joshua 7:20–21"> for this might have been sold for much and been given to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Timothy 5:10"> But Jesus knowing &#91;it&#93; said to them, Why do ye trouble the woman? for she has wrought a good work toward me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8"> For ye have the poor always with you, but me ye have not always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 14:8; John 12:7; Mark 16:1"> For in pouring out this ointment on my body, she has done it for my burying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 14:9; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 112:6"> Verily I say to you, Wheresoever these glad tidings may be preached in the whole world, that also which this &#91;woman&#93; has done shall be spoken of for a memorial of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:3–6; Matthew 10:4; Mark 14:10–11"> Then one of the twelve, he who was called Judas Iscariote, went to the chief priests</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12–13; Genesis 37:26–28; Exodus 21:32"> and said, What are ye willing to give me, and I will deliver him up to you? And they appointed to him thirty pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:11; Luke 22:6"> And from that time he sought a good opportunity that he might deliver him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:7–13; Mark 14:12–16; Exodus 12:18–20"> Now on the first &#91;day&#93; of &#91;the feast of&#93; unleavened bread, the disciples came to Jesus, saying, Where wilt thou that we prepare for thee to eat the passover?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; John 7:6; John 17:1"> And he said, Go into the city unto such a one, and say to him, The Teacher says, My time is near, I will keep the passover in thy house with my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Matthew 21:6"> And the disciples did as Jesus had directed them, and they prepared the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 14:17–21; John 13:21; Luke 22:14–16"> And when the evening was come he lay down at table with the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:21; Hebrews 4:13; John 6:70–71"> And as they were eating he said, Verily I say to you, that one of you shall deliver me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Luke 22:23; Mark 14:19–20"> And being exceedingly grieved they began to say to him, each of them, Is it I, Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:18; Psalms 41:9; John 13:26–28"> But he answering said, He that dips his hand with me in the dish, he it is who shall deliver me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23; Luke 24:46; Matthew 18:7"> The Son of man goes indeed, according as it is written concerning him, but woe to that man by whom the Son of man is delivered up; it were good for that man if he had not been born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Matthew 27:11; Luke 22:70"> And Judas, who delivered him up, answering said, Is it I, Rabbi? He says to him, Thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Luke 22:18–20; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> And as they were eating, Jesus, having taken &#91;the&#93; bread and blessed, broke &#91;it&#93; and gave &#91;it&#93; to the disciples, and said, Take, eat: this is my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Psalms 116:13"> And having taken &#91;the&#93; cup and given thanks, he gave &#91;it&#93; to them, saying, Drink ye all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–14; Hebrews 9:14–22; Leviticus 17:11"> For this is my blood, that of the &#91;new&#93; covenant, that shed for many for remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Revelation 7:17; Mark 14:25"> But I say to you, that I will not at all drink henceforth of this fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it new with you in the kingdom of my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Ephesians 5:19–20; Luke 22:39"> And having sung a hymn, they went out to the mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Mark 14:27–28; John 16:32"> Then saith Jesus to them, All ye shall be offended in me during this night. For it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Luke 18:33–34; Matthew 28:16"> But after that I shall be risen, I will go before you to Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 22:33; John 13:36–38; Proverbs 28:25–26"> And Peter answering said to him, If all shall be offended in thee, I will never be offended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:75"> Jesus said to him, Verily I say to thee, that during this night, before &#91;the&#93; cock shall crow, thou shalt deny me thrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 11:20; John 13:37; Proverbs 29:23"> Peter says to him, If I should needs die with thee, I will in no wise deny thee. Likewise said all the disciples also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:39–46"> Then Jesus comes with them to a place called Gethsemane, and says to the disciples, Sit here until I go away and pray yonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Matthew 17:1; Mark 5:37"> And taking with &#91;him&#93; Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, he began to be sorrowful and deeply depressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 12:27; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:24"> Then he says to them, My soul is very sorrowful even unto death; remain here and watch with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 6:38; Philippians 2:8; Luke 22:41–42"> And going forward a little he fell upon his face, praying and saying, My Father, if it be possible let this cup pass from me; but not as I will, but as thou &#91;wilt&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 14:37; Luke 22:45; Matthew 26:43"> And he comes to the disciples and finds them sleeping, and says to Peter, Thus ye have not been able to watch one hour with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 14:38; Luke 22:46; Ephesians 6:18"> Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed &#91;is&#93; ready, but the flesh weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:39–40; Hebrews 4:15"> Again going away a second time he prayed saying, My Father, if this cannot pass &#91;from me&#93; unless I drink it, thy will be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:32; Romans 13:1; Proverbs 23:34"> And coming he found them again sleeping, for their eyes were heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8; Luke 18:1; Matthew 6:7"> And leaving them, he went away again and prayed the third time, saying the same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 13:1; John 12:27; Mark 14:41–42"> Then he comes to the disciples and says to them, Sleep on now and take your rest; behold, the hour has drawn nigh, and the Son of man is delivered up into the hands of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 9:51; John 14:31; 1 Samuel 17:48"> Arise, let us go; behold, he that delivers me up has drawn nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 1:16; Matthew 26:55; Mark 14:43–50"> And while he was yet speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great crowd with swords and sticks from the chief priests and elders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 55:20–21; Psalms 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Now he that delivered him up had given them a sign, saying, Whomsoever I shall kiss, he it is: seize him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:25; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; Proverbs 27:6"> And immediately coming up to Jesus he said, Hail, Rabbi, and covered him with kisses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 20:13; Matthew 22:12; Psalms 41:9"> But Jesus said to him, &#91;My&#93; friend, for what purpose art thou come? Then coming up they laid hands upon Jesus and seized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 18:36; Mark 14:47; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And behold, one of those with Jesus stretched out his hand and drew his sword, and smiting the bondman of the high priest took off his ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Genesis 9:6; Romans 12:19"> Then saith Jesus to him, Return thy sword to its place; for all who take the sword shall perish by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Matthew 4:11; Daniel 7:10"> Or thinkest thou that I cannot now call upon my Father, and he will furnish me more than twelve legions of angels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:24; Acts 1:16"> How then should the scriptures be fulfilled that thus it must be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 12:35; John 8:2; Luke 22:52–53"> In that hour Jesus said to the crowds, Are ye come out as against a robber with swords and sticks to take me? I sat daily &#91;with you&#93; teaching in the temple, and ye did not seize me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; John 16:32; Mark 14:50–52"> But all this is come to pass that the scriptures of the prophets may be fulfilled. Then all the disciples left him and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; Mark 14:53–65; Psalms 56:5–6"> Now they that had seized Jesus led &#91;him&#93; away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 7:32; John 18:15–16; John 18:25"> And Peter followed him at a distance, even to the palace of the high priest, and entering in sat with the officers to see the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Acts 6:11–13; Deuteronomy 19:16–21; Mark 14:55–56"> And the chief priests and the elders and the whole sanhedrim sought false witness against Jesus, so that they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Psalms 35:11; Psalms 27:12"> And they found none, though many false witnesses came forward. But at the last two false witnesses came forward</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:19–21; Matthew 27:40; Mark 15:29"> and said, He said, I am able to destroy the temple of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="26.61 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and in three days build it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="John 19:9–11; John 18:19–24; Mark 14:60"> And the high priest standing up said to him, Answerest thou nothing? What do these witness against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Matthew 16:16; Isaiah 53:7"> But Jesus was silent. And the high priest answering said to him, I adjure thee by the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="26.63 Elohim">b</a></sup>God that thou tell us if thou art the Christ the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="26.63 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Mark 14:62; Psalms 110:1"> Jesus says to him, Thou hast said. Moreover, I say to you, From henceforth ye shall see the Son of man sitting at the right hand of power, and coming on the clouds of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; John 10:33; Matthew 9:3"> Then the high priest rent his clothes, saying, He has blasphemed: what need have we any more of witnesses? behold, now ye have heard the blasphemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="John 19:7; Acts 7:52; Leviticus 24:11–16"> What think ye? And they answering said, He is liable to the penalty of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3; Matthew 27:30"> Then they spit in his face, and buffeted him, and some struck him with the palms of their hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 22:63–65; Mark 14:65; 1 Peter 2:4–8"> saying, Prophesy to us, Christ, Who is it who struck thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Matthew 26:71; Acts 5:37"> But Peter sat without in the palace-court; and a maid came to him, saying, And thou wast with Jesus the Galilaean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Revelation 21:8; Matthew 26:40–43"> But he denied before all, saying, I do not know what thou sayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Mark 14:68–69; John 18:25–27; Luke 22:58"> And when he had gone out into the entrance, another &#91;maid&#93; saw him, and says to those there, This &#91;man&#93; also was with Jesus the Nazaraean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Matthew 26:74; Isaiah 48:1; Zechariah 8:17"> And again he denied with an oath: I do not know the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Judges 12:6; John 18:26–27; Nehemiah 13:24"> And after a little, those who stood &#91;there&#93;, coming to &#91;him&#93;, said to Peter, Truly thou too art of them, for also thy speech makes thee manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Mark 14:71–72"> Then he began to curse and to swear, I know not the man. And immediately &#91;the&#93; cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Matthew 26:34; Romans 7:18–20; Luke 22:31–34"> And Peter remembered the word of Jesus, who had said &#91;to him&#93;, Before &#91;the&#93; cock crow thou shalt deny me thrice. And he went forth without, and wept bitterly.</span>
