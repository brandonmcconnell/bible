<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And Jesus answering spoke to them again in parables, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> The kingdom of the heavens has become like a king who made a wedding feast for his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> and sent his bondmen to call the persons invited to the wedding feast, and they would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again he sent other bondmen, saying, Say to the persons invited, Behold, I have prepared my dinner; my oxen and my fatted beasts are killed, and all things ready; come to the wedding feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they made light of it, and went, one to his own land, and another to his commerce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> And the rest, laying hold of his bondmen, ill-treated and slew &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> And &#91;when&#93; the king &#91;heard of it he&#93; was wroth, and having sent his forces, destroyed those murderers and burned their city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then he says to his bondmen, The wedding feast is ready, but those invited were not worthy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> go therefore into the thoroughfares of the highways, and as many as ye shall find invite to the wedding feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> And those bondmen went out into the highways, and brought together all as many as they found, both evil and good; and the wedding feast was furnished with guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And the king, having gone in to see the guests, beheld there a man not clothed with a wedding garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> And he says to him, &#91;My&#93; friend, how camest thou in here not having on a wedding garment? But he was speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then said the king to the servants, Bind him feet and hands, and take him away, and cast him out into the outer darkness: there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are called ones, but few chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then went the Pharisees and held a council how they might ensnare him in speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they send out to him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, Teacher, we know that thou art true and teachest the way of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.16 Elohim">a</a></sup>God in truth, and carest not for any one, for thou regardest not men's person;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> tell us therefore what thou thinkest: Is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus, knowing their wickedness, said, Why tempt ye me, hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Shew me the money of the tribute. And they presented to him a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And he says to them, Whose &#91;is&#93; this image and superscription?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They say to him, Caesar's. Then he says to them, Pay then what is Caesar's to Caesar, and what is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.21 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.21 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> And when they heard &#91;him&#93;, they wondered, and left him, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> On that day came to him Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection; and they demanded of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, Teacher, Moses said, If any one die, not having children, his brother shall marry his wife and shall raise up seed to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were with us seven brethren; and the first having married died, and not having seed, left his wife to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In like manner also the second and the third, unto the seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And last of all the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the resurrection therefore of which of the seven shall she be wife, for all had her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> And Jesus answering said to them, Ye err, not knowing the scriptures nor the power of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="22.29 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are as angels of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="22.30 Elohim">e</a></sup>God in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read what was spoken to you by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="22.31 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> I am the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="22.32 Elohim">g</a></sup>God of Abraham, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="22.32 Elohim">h</a></sup>God of Isaac, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="22.32 Elohim">i</a></sup>God of Jacob?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="22.32 Elohim">j</a></sup>God is not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="22.32 Elohim">k</a></sup>God of &#91;the&#93; dead, but of &#91;the&#93; living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And when the crowds heard &#91;it&#93; they were astonished at his doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> But the Pharisees, having heard that he had put the Sadducees to silence, were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> And one of them, a lawyer, demanded, tempting him, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> Teacher, which is the great commandment in the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he said to him, Thou shalt love &#91;the&#93; Lord thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="22.37 Elohim">l</a></sup>God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is &#91;the&#93; great and first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And &#91;the&#93; second is like it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> On these two commandments the whole law and the prophets hang.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> And the Pharisees being gathered together, Jesus demanded of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> saying, What think ye concerning the Christ? whose son is he? They say to him, David's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He says to them, How then does David in Spirit call him Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> The Lord said to my Lord, Sit on my right hand until I put thine enemies under thy feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If therefore David call him Lord, how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And no one was able to answer him a word, nor did any one dare from that day to question him any more.</span>
