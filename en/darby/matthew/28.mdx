<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> Now late on sabbath, as it was the dusk of the next day after sabbath, came Mary of Magdala and the other Mary to look at the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> And behold, there was a great earthquake; for an angel of &#91;the&#93; Lord, descending out of heaven, came and rolled away the stone and sat upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> And his look was as lightning, and his clothing white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> And for fear of him the guards trembled and became as dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> And the angel answering said to the women, Fear not ye, for I know that ye seek Jesus the crucified one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He is not here, for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> And go quickly and say to his disciples that he is risen from the dead; and behold, he goes before you into Galilee, there shall ye see him. Behold, I have told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> And going out quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, they ran to bring his disciples word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> And as they went to bring his disciples word, behold also, Jesus met them, saying, Hail! And they coming up took him by the feet, and did him homage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then Jesus says to them, Fear not; go, bring word to my brethren that they go into Galilee, and there they shall see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> And as they went, behold, some of the watch went into the city, and brought word to the chief priests of all that had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> And having assembled with the elders, and having taken counsel, they gave a large sum of money to the soldiers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> saying, Say that his disciples coming by night stole him &#91;while&#93; we &#91;were&#93; sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> And if this should come to the hearing of the governor, we will persuade him, and save you from all anxiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> And they took the money and did as they had been taught. And this report is current among the Jews until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> But the eleven disciples went into Galilee to the mountain which Jesus had appointed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Psalms 2:12; John 5:23"> And when they saw him, they did homage to him: but some doubted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> And Jesus coming up spoke to them, saying, All power has been given me in heaven and upon earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> Go &#91;therefore&#93; and make disciples of all the nations, baptising them to the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:5; Acts 18:9–10"> teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have enjoined you. And behold, I am with you all the days, until the completion of the age.</span>
