<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time Herod the tetrarch heard of the fame of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> and said to his servants, This is John the baptist: he is risen from the dead, and because of this these works of power display their force in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod had seized John, and had bound him and put him in prison on account of Herodias the wife of Philip his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> For John said to him, It is not lawful for thee to have her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> And &#91;while&#93; desiring to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they held him for a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herod's birthday was celebrated, the daughter of Herodias danced before them, and pleased Herod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> whereupon he promised with oath to give her whatsoever she should ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> But she, being set on by her mother, says, Give me here upon a dish the head of John the baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> And the king was grieved; but on account of the oaths, and those lying at table with &#91;him&#93;, he commanded &#91;it&#93; to be given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> And he sent and beheaded John in the prison;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> and his head was brought upon a dish, and was given to the damsel, and she carried &#91;it&#93; to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> And his disciples came and took the body and buried it, and came and brought word to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> And Jesus, having heard it, went away thence by ship to a desert place apart. And the crowds having heard &#91;of it&#93; followed him on foot from the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> And going out he saw a great crowd, and was moved with compassion about them, and healed their infirm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> But when even was come, his disciples came to him saying, The place is desert, and &#91;much of&#93; the &#91;day&#93; time already gone by; dismiss the crowds, that they may go into the villages and buy food for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them, They have no need to go: give ye them to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> But they say to him, We have not here save five loaves and two fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he said, Bring them here to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> And having commanded the crowds to recline upon the grass, having taken the five loaves and the two fishes, he looked up to heaven, and blessed: and having broken the loaves, he gave &#91;them&#93; to the disciples, and the disciples &#91;gave them&#93; to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> And all ate and were filled, and they took up what was over and above of fragments twelve hand-baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> But those that had eaten were about five thousand men, besides women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> And immediately he compelled the disciples to go on board ship, and to go on before him to the other side, until he should have dismissed the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> And having dismissed the crowds, he went up into the mountain apart to pray. And when even was come, he was alone there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> but the ship was already in the middle of the sea tossed by the waves, for the wind was contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> But in the fourth watch of the night he went off to them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> And the disciples, seeing him walking on the sea, were troubled, saying, It is an apparition. And they cried out through fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But Jesus immediately spoke to them, saying, Take courage; it is I: be not afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> And Peter answering him said, Lord, if it be thou, command me to come to thee upon the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> And he said, Come. And Peter, having descended from the ship, walked upon the waters to go to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But seeing the wind strong he was afraid; and beginning to sink he cried out, saying, Lord, save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> And immediately Jesus stretched out his hand and caught hold of him, and says to him, O thou of little faith, why didst thou doubt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And when they had gone up into the ship, the wind fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> But those in the ship came and did homage to him, saying, Truly thou art<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.33 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> And having crossed over they came to the land of Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> And when the men of that place recognised him, they sent to that whole country around, and they brought to him all that were ill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> and besought him that they might only touch the hem of his garment; and as many as touched were made thoroughly well.</span>
