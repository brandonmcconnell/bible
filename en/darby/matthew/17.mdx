<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Matthew 26:37; Mark 5:37"> And after six days Jesus takes with &#91;him&#93; Peter, and James, and John his brother, and brings them up into a high mountain apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 104:2; Matthew 28:3; Luke 9:29"> And he was transfigured before them. And his face shone as the sun, and his garments became white as the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Mark 9:4; Malachi 4:5"> and lo, Moses and Elias appeared to them talking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Luke 9:33; John 14:8–9"> And Peter answering said to Jesus, Lord, it is good we should be here. If thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles: for thee one, and for Moses one, and one for Elias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; Matthew 12:18; Deuteronomy 18:15"> While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them, and lo, a voice out of the cloud, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I have found my delight: hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 26:14; 2 Peter 1:18; Daniel 8:17"> And the disciples hearing &#91;it&#93; fell upon their faces, and were greatly terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Matthew 14:27; Daniel 8:18"> And Jesus coming to &#91;them&#93; touched them, and said, Rise up, and be not terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:36; Mark 9:8; Acts 12:10–11"> And lifting up their eyes, they saw no one but Jesus alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Mark 9:9–13; Matthew 17:23"> And as they descended from the mountain, Jesus charged them, saying, Tell the vision to no one, until the Son of man be risen up from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; John 1:21"> And &#91;his&#93; disciples demanded of him saying, Why then say the scribes that Elias must first have come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 1:16–17; Malachi 4:6"> And he answering said to them, Elias indeed comes first and will restore all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; Matthew 16:21; Acts 13:24–28"> But I say unto you that Elias has already come, and they have not known him, but have done unto him whatever they would. Thus also the Son of man is about to suffer from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 11:14"> Then the disciples understood that he spoke to them of John the baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> And when they came to the crowd, a man came to him, falling on his knees before him, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is lunatic, and suffers sorely; for often he falls into the fire and often into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> And I brought him to thy disciples, and they were not able to heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Matthew 6:30; Mark 16:14"> And Jesus answering said, O unbelieving and perverted generation, how long shall I be with you? how long shall I bear with you? Bring him here to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> And Jesus rebuked him, and the demon went out from him, and the boy was healed from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> Then the disciples, coming to Jesus apart, said &#91;to him&#93;, Why were not we able to cast him out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:6; Mark 11:23; Luke 1:37"> And he says to them, Because of your unbelief; for verily I say unto you, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard &#91;seed&#93;, ye shall say to this mountain, Be transported hence there, and it shall transport itself; and nothing shall be impossible to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But this kind does not go out but by prayer and fasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 17:23"> And while they abode in Galilee, Jesus said to them, The Son of man is about to be delivered up into &#91;the&#93; hands of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; Mark 8:31"> and they shall kill him; and the third day he shall be raised up. And they were greatly grieved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:13; Mark 9:33"> And when they came to Capernaum, those who received the didrachmas came to Peter and said, Does your teacher not pay the didrachmas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:21; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 22:17"> He says, Yes. And when he came into the house, Jesus anticipated him, saying, What dost thou think, Simon? the kings of the earth, from whom do they receive custom or tribute? from their own sons or from strangers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Peter says to him, From strangers. Jesus said to him, Then are the sons free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> But that we may not be an offence to them, go to the sea and cast a hook, and take the first fish that comes up, and when thou hast opened its mouth thou wilt find a stater; take that and give it to them for me and thee.</span>
