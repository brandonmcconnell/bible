<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> For the kingdom of the heavens is like a householder who went out with the early morn to hire workmen for his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> And having agreed with the workmen for a denarius the day, he sent them into his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> And having gone out about &#91;the&#93; third hour, he saw others standing in the market-place idle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> and to them he said, Go also ye into the vineyard, and whatsoever may be just I will give you. And they went their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> Again, having gone out about the sixth and ninth hour, he did likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> But about the eleventh &#91;hour&#93;, having gone out, he found others standing, and says to them, Why stand ye here all the day idle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> They say to him, Because no man has hired us. He says to them, Go also ye into the vineyard &#91;and whatsoever may be just ye shall receive&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> But when the evening was come, the lord of the vineyard says to his steward, Call the workmen and pay &#91;them&#93; their wages, beginning from the last even to the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> And when they &#91;who came to work&#93; about the eleventh hour came, they received each a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And when the first came, they supposed that they would receive more, and they received also themselves each a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> And on receiving it they murmured against the master of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> saying, These last have worked one hour, and thou hast made them equal to us, who have borne the burden of the day and the heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> But he answering said to one of them, &#91;My&#93; friend, I do not wrong thee. Didst thou not agree with me for a denarius?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take what is thine and go. But it is my will to give to this last even as to thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> is it not lawful for me to do what I will in my own affairs? Is thine eye evil because I am good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> Thus shall the last be first, and the first last; for many are called ones, but few chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> And Jesus, going up to Jerusalem, took the twelve disciples with &#91;him&#93; apart in the way, and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> Behold we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of man will be delivered up to the chief priests and scribes, and they will condemn him to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> and they will deliver him up to the nations to mock and to scourge and to crucify, and the third day he shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then came to him the mother of the sons of Zebedee, with her sons, doing homage, and asking something of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> And he said to her, What wilt thou? She says to him, Speak &#91;the word&#93; that these my two sons may sit, one on thy right hand and one on thy left in thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> And Jesus answering said, Ye know not what ye ask. Can ye drink the cup which I am about to drink? They say to him, We are able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> &#91;And&#93; he says to them, Ye shall drink indeed my cup, but to sit on my right hand and on &#91;my&#93; left, is not mine to give, but to those for whom it is prepared of my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> And the ten, having heard &#91;of it&#93;, were indignant about the two brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> But Jesus having called them to &#91;him&#93;, said, Ye know that the rulers of the nations exercise lordship over them, and the great exercise authority over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> It shall not be thus amongst you, but whosoever will be great among you, shall be your servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> and whosoever will be first among you, let him be your bondman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> as indeed the Son of man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life a ransom for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:46–52; Luke 18:35–43; Matthew 9:27–31"> And as they went out from Jericho a great crowd followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; Matthew 21:9"> And lo, two blind men, sitting by the wayside, having heard that Jesus was passing by, cried out saying, Have mercy on us, Lord, Son of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:13; Colossians 4:2; Matthew 7:7–8"> But the crowd rebuked them, that they might be silent. But they cried out the more, saying, Have mercy on us, Lord, Son of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:37; Philippians 4:6; Matthew 20:21"> And Jesus, having stopped, called them and said, What will ye that I shall do to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 119:18; Ephesians 1:17–19"> They say to him, Lord, that our eyes may be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15–16; Matthew 9:29; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus, moved with compassion, touched their eyes; and immediately their eyes had sight restored to them, and they followed him.</span>
