<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> And that &#91;same&#93; day Jesus went out from the house and sat down by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> And great crowds were gathered together to him, so that going on board ship himself he sat down, and the whole crowd stood on the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalms 78:2"> And he spoke to them many things in parables, saying, Behold, the sower went out to sow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> and as he sowed, some &#91;grains&#93; fell along the way, and the birds came and devoured them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> and others fell upon the rocky places where they had not much earth, and immediately they sprang up out of &#91;the ground&#93; because of not having &#91;any&#93; depth of earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> but when the sun rose they were burned up, and because of not having &#91;any&#93; root were dried up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> and others fell upon the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> and others fell upon the good ground, and produced fruit, one a hundred, one sixty, and one thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> He that has ears, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> And the disciples came up and said to him, Why speakest thou to them in parables?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> And he answering said to them, Because to you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of the heavens, but to them it is not given;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> for whoever has, to him shall be given, and he shall be caused to be in abundance; but he who has not, even what he has shall be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> For this cause I speak to them in parables, because seeing they do not see, and hearing they do not hear nor understand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> and in them is filled up the prophecy of Esaias, which says, Hearing ye shall hear and shall not understand, and beholding ye shall behold and not see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> for the heart of this people has grown fat, and they have heard heavily with their ears, and they have closed their eyes as asleep, lest they should see with the eyes, and hear with the ears, and understand with the heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> But blessed are your eyes because they see, and your ears because they hear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> for verily I say unto you, that many prophets and righteous &#91;men&#93; have desired to see the things which ye behold and did not see &#91;them&#93;, and to hear the things which ye hear and did not hear &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> Ye, therefore, hear the parable of the sower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> From every one who hears the word of the kingdom and does not understand &#91;it&#93;, the wicked one comes and catches away what was sown in his heart: this is he that is sown by the wayside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> But he that is sown on the rocky places — this is he who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> but has no root in himself, but is for a time only; and when tribulation or persecution happens on account of the word, he is immediately offended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And he that is sown among the thorns — this is he who hears the word, and the anxious care of this life, and the deceit of riches choke the word, and he becomes unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> But he that is sown upon the good ground — this is he who hears and understands the word, who bears fruit also, and produces, one a hundred, one sixty, and one thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> Another parable set he before them, saying, The kingdom of the heavens has become like a man sowing good seed in his field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> but while men slept, his enemy came and sowed darnel amongst the wheat, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> But when the blade shot up and produced fruit, then appeared the darnel also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> And the bondmen of the householder came up and said to him, Sir, hast thou not sown good seed in thy field? whence then has it darnel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> And he said to them, A man &#91;that is&#93; an enemy has done this. And the bondmen said to him, Wilt thou then that we should go and gather it &#91;up&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But he said, No; lest &#91;in&#93; gathering the darnel ye should root up the wheat with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Suffer both to grow together unto the harvest, and in time of the harvest I will say to the harvestmen, Gather first the darnel, and bind it into bundles to burn it; but the wheat bring together into my granary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> Another parable set he before them, saying, The kingdom of the heavens is like a grain of mustard &#91;seed&#93; which a man took and sowed in his field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> which is less indeed than all seeds, but when it is grown is greater than herbs, and becomes a tree, so that the birds of heaven come and roost in its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Luke 13:21"> He spoke another parable to them: The kingdom of the heavens is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal until it had been all leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; John 16:25; Matthew 13:13"> All these things Jesus spoke to the crowds in parables, and without a parable he did not speak to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Ephesians 3:9; 1 Peter 1:20–21"> so that that should be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophet, saying, I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things hidden from &#91;the&#93; world's foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then, having dismissed the crowds, he went into the house; and his disciples came to him, saying, Expound to us the parable of the darnel of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> But he answering said, He that sows the good seed is the Son of man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:44; Philippians 3:18–19; Acts 13:10"> and the field is the world; and the good seed, these are the sons of the kingdom, but the darnel are the sons of the evil &#91;one&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 13:49; Revelation 14:15–19; Matthew 25:31"> and the enemy who has sowed it is the devil; and the harvest is &#91;the&#93; completion of &#91;the&#93; age, and the harvestmen are angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> As then the darnel is gathered and is burned in the fire, thus it shall be in the completion of the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> The Son of man shall send his angels, and they shall gather out of his kingdom all offences, and those that practise lawlessness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Luke 16:23–24; Matthew 8:12"> and they shall cast them into the furnace of fire; there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> Then the righteous shall shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father. He that has ears, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Proverbs 2:2–5; Colossians 2:3; Philippians 3:7–9"> The kingdom of the heavens is like a treasure hid in the field, which a man having found has hid, and for the joy of it goes and sells all whatever he has, and buys that field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 3:13–18; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:24"> Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a merchant seeking beautiful pearls;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 18:28–30; Proverbs 2:4"> and having found one pearl of great value, he went and sold all whatever he had and bought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 15:6; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Matthew 22:9–10"> Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a seine which has been cast into the sea, and which has gathered together of every kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43; Matthew 3:12"> which, when it has been filled, having drawn up on the shore and sat down, they gathered the good into vessels and cast the worthless out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 20:12–15; Matthew 24:31; Matthew 13:39"> Thus shall it be in the completion of the age: the angels shall go forth and sever the wicked from the midst of the just,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 13:42; Matthew 8:12; Revelation 14:10–11"> and shall cast them into the furnace of fire; there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:11; Mark 4:34"> Jesus says to them, Have ye understood all these things? They say to him, Yea, &#91;Lord&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 John 2:7–8; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Titus 1:9"> And he said to them, For this reason every scribe discipled to the kingdom of the heavens is like a man &#91;that is&#93; a householder who brings out of his treasure things new and old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 4:33–35"> And it came to pass when Jesus had finished these parables he withdrew thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; John 7:15–16; Mark 6:1–6"> And having come into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished, and said, Whence has this &#91;man&#93; this wisdom and these works of power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 6:42; Mark 6:3; Luke 3:23"> Is not this the son of the carpenter? Is not his mother called Mary, and his brethren James, and Joseph, and Simon, and Judas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And his sisters, are they not all with us? Whence then has this &#91;man&#93; all these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 4:44; Matthew 11:6; Luke 4:24"> And they were offended in him. And Jesus said to them, A prophet is not without honour, unless in his country and in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Mark 6:5–6; Romans 11:20; Hebrews 3:12–19"> And he did not there many works of power, because of their unbelief.</span>
