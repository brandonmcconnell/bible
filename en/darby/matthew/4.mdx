<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Luke 4:1–13; Mark 1:12–15"> Then Jesus was carried up into the wilderness by the Spirit to be tempted of the devil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Exodus 34:28; 1 Kings 19:8"> and having fasted forty days and forty nights, afterwards he hungered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> And the tempter coming up to him said, If thou be Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, speak, that these stones may become loaves of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> But he answering said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word which goes out through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Then the devil takes him to the holy city, and sets him upon the edge of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 34:7"> and says to him, If thou be Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">c</a></sup>God cast thyself down; for it is written, He shall give charge to his angels concerning thee, and on &#91;their&#93; hands shall they bear thee, lest in anywise thou strike thy foot against a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalms 78:18"> Jesus said to him, It is again written, Thou shalt not tempt &#91;the&#93; Lord thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; Psalms 49:16–17"> Again the devil takes him to a very high mountain, and shews him all the kingdoms of the world, and their glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> and says to him, All these things will I give thee if, falling down, thou wilt do me homage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> Then says Jesus to him, Get thee away, Satan, for it is written, Thou shalt do homage to &#91;the&#93; Lord thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.10 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, and him alone shalt thou serve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Then the devil leaves him, and behold, angels came and ministered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> But having heard that John was delivered up, he departed into Galilee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> and having left Nazareth, he went and dwelt at Capernaum, which is on the sea-side in the borders of Zabulon and Nepthalim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> that that might be fulfilled which was spoken through Esaias the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> Land of Zabulon and land of Nepthalim, way of &#91;the&#93; sea beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> — the people sitting in darkness has seen a great light, and to those sitting in &#91;the&#93; country and shadow of death, to them has light sprung up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From that time began Jesus to preach and to say, Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> And walking by the sea of Galilee, he saw two brothers, Simon called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> and he says to them, Come after me, and I will make you fishers of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> And they, having left their trawl-nets, immediately followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> And going on thence he saw other two brothers, James the &#91;son&#93; of Zebedee and John his brother, in the ship with Zebedee their father, mending their trawl-nets, and he called them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> and they, having left the ship and their father, immediately followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> And &#91;Jesus&#93; went round the whole &#91;of&#93; Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the glad tidings of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every bodily weakness among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> And his fame went out into the whole &#91;of&#93; Syria, and they brought to him all that were ill, suffering under various diseases and pains, and those possessed by demons, and lunatics, and paralytics; and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> And great crowds followed him from Galilee, and Decapolis, and Jerusalem, and Judaea, and beyond the Jordan.</span>
