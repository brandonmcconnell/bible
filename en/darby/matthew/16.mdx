<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Matthew 16:6; Acts 4:1"> And the Pharisees and Sadducees, coming to &#91;him&#93;, asked him, tempting &#91;him&#93;, to shew them a sign out of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:54–56"> But he answering said to them, When evening is come, ye say, Fine weather, for the sky is red;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 12:56; 1 Chronicles 12:32; Matthew 11:5"> and in the morning, A storm to-day, for the sky is red &#91;and&#93; lowering; ye know &#91;how&#93; to discern the face of the sky, but ye cannot the signs of the times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:29–30; Matthew 12:39–40; Jonah 1:17"> A wicked and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and a sign shall not be given to it save the sign of Jonas. And he left them and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:13–14; Matthew 15:39"> And when his disciples were come to the other side, they had forgotten to take bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Galatians 5:9; Matthew 16:12"> And Jesus said to them, See and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 8:16–18; Matthew 15:16–18; Acts 10:14"> And they reasoned among themselves, saying, Because we have taken no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:30; Matthew 14:31; Matthew 8:26"> And Jesus knowing &#91;it&#93;, said, Why reason ye among yourselves, O ye of little faith, because ye have taken no bread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:9–13; Luke 9:13–17"> Do ye not yet understand nor remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many hand-baskets ye took &#91;up&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:17–21; Mark 8:5–9"> nor the seven loaves of the four thousand, and how many baskets ye took &#91;up&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 16:6; Luke 12:56; Mark 8:21"> How do ye not understand that &#91;it was&#93; not concerning bread I said to you, Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Acts 23:8; Matthew 15:4–9"> Then they comprehended that he did not speak of being beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:18–20; Mark 8:27–29; Daniel 7:13"> But when Jesus was come into the parts of Caesarea-Philippi, he demanded of his disciples, saying, Who do men say that I the Son of man am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:15; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> And they said, Some, John the baptist; and others, Elias; and others again, Jeremias or one of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 9:20; Matthew 13:11; Mark 8:29"> He says to them, But ye, who do ye say that I am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; John 11:27; 1 John 4:15"> And Simon Peter answering said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.16 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 6:45; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 11:25–27"> And Jesus answering said to him, Blessed art thou, Simon Bar-jona, for flesh and blood has not revealed &#91;it&#93; to thee, but my Father who is in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Corinthians 3:9–11; Isaiah 28:16"> And I also, I say unto thee that thou art Peter, and on this rock I will build my assembly, and hades' gates shall not prevail against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Isaiah 22:22; Revelation 1:18"> And I will give to thee the keys of the kingdom of the heavens; and whatsoever thou mayest bind upon the earth shall be bound in the heavens; and whatsoever thou mayest loose on the earth shall be loosed in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Luke 9:21; John 1:41"> Then he enjoined on his disciples that they should say to no man that he was the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 24:6–7; Matthew 20:17–19; Matthew 17:22–23"> From that time Jesus began to shew to his disciples that he must go away to Jerusalem, and suffer many things from the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and the third day be raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 8:32; John 13:6–8; Matthew 16:16–17"> And Peter taking him to &#91;him&#93; began to rebuke him, saying, &#91;God&#93; be favourable to thee, Lord; this shall in no wise be unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 8:5–8; Colossians 3:2; Philippians 3:19"> But turning round, he said to Peter, Get away behind me, Satan; thou art an offence to me, for thy mind is not on the things that are of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.23 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, but on the things that are of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:27; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> Then Jesus said to his disciples, If any one desires to come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Mark 8:35; Revelation 12:11"> For whosoever shall desire to save his life shall lose it; but whosoever shall lose his life for my sake shall find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 8:36–37; Job 27:8; Luke 9:25"> For what does a man profit, if he should gain the whole world and suffer the loss of his soul? or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Psalms 62:12; Romans 2:6"> For the Son of man is about to come in the glory of his Father with his angels, and then he will render to each according to his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Mark 9:1; John 8:52"> Verily I say unto you, There are some of those standing here that shall not taste of death at all until they shall have seen the Son of man coming in his kingdom.</span>
