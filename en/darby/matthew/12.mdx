<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Luke 6:1–5"> At that time Jesus went on the sabbath through the cornfields; and his disciples were hungry, and began to pluck the ears and to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; John 7:21–24; Luke 14:3"> But the Pharisees, seeing &#91;it&#93;, said to him, Behold, thy disciples are doing what is not lawful to do on sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 6:3; Mark 2:25–26"> But he said to them, Have ye not read what David did when he was hungry, and they that were with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 8:31"> How he entered into the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and ate the shewbread, which it was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those with him, but for the priests only?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:9–10; John 7:22–23; Nehemiah 13:17"> Or have ye not read in the law that on the sabbaths the priests in the temple profane the sabbath, and are blameless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; 2 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Peter 2:4–5"> But I say unto you, that there is here what is greater than the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Matthew 9:13; Micah 6:6–8"> But if ye had known what is: I will have mercy and not sacrifice, ye would not have condemned the guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 2:28; Luke 6:5; John 5:17–23"> For the Son of man is Lord of the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Luke 6:6–11"> And, going away from thence, he came into their synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:14; John 9:16; John 8:6"> And behold, there was a man having his hand withered. And they asked him, saying, Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath? that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4; Luke 14:5; Luke 13:15–17"> But he said to them, What man shall there be of you who has one sheep, and if this fall into a pit on the sabbath, will not lay hold of it and raise &#91;it&#93; up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:26; Luke 6:9; Mark 3:4"> How much better then is a man than a sheep! So that it is lawful to do well on the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Acts 3:7–8"> Then he says to the man, Stretch out thy hand. And he stretched &#91;it&#93; out, and it was restored sound as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 11:53; John 10:39; Mark 3:6"> But the Pharisees, having gone out, took counsel against him, how they might destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 3:7–12; Matthew 10:23; Matthew 19:2"> But Jesus knowing &#91;it&#93;, withdrew thence, and great crowds followed him; and he healed them all:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Matthew 17:9"> and charged them strictly that they should not make him publicly known:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1–4; Isaiah 42:9; Matthew 13:35"> that that might be fulfilled which was spoken through Esaias the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Luke 4:18; Acts 10:38"> Behold my servant, whom I have chosen, my beloved, in whom my soul has found its delight. I will put my Spirit upon him, and he shall shew forth judgment to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 18:36–38; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> He shall not strive or cry out, nor shall any one hear his voice in the streets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 42:3–4; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 61:1–3"> a bruised reed shall he not break, and smoking flax shall he not quench, until he bring forth judgment unto victory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Romans 15:12–13; Isaiah 42:4"> and on his name shall &#91;the&#93; nations hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:14–15; Mark 7:35–37; Isaiah 29:18"> Then was brought to him one possessed by a demon, blind and dumb, and he healed him, so that the dumb &#91;man&#93; spake and saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; John 4:29; Matthew 22:42–43"> And all the crowds were amazed and said, Is this &#91;man&#93; the Son of David?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 9:34; Luke 11:15"> But the Pharisees, having heard &#91;it&#93;, said, This &#91;man&#93; does not cast out demons, but by Beelzebub, prince of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 9:4"> But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation, and every city or house divided against itself will not subsist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; Revelation 20:2–3; 1 John 5:19"> And if Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself; how then shall his kingdom subsist?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:13–16; Mark 9:38–39; Luke 9:49–50"> And if I cast out demons by Beelzebub, your sons, by whom do they cast &#91;them&#93; out? For this reason they shall be your judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 11:20; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 6:33"> But if I by &#91;the&#93; Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.28 Elohim">b</a></sup>God cast out demons, then indeed the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.28 Elohim">c</a></sup>God is come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24; Mark 3:27; Luke 11:21–22"> Or how can any one enter into the house of the strong &#91;man&#93; and plunder his goods, unless first he bind the strong &#91;man&#93;? and then he will plunder his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:40; Luke 11:23; Joshua 24:15"> He that is not with me is against me, and he that gathers not with me scatters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Mark 3:28–30; Hebrews 10:29"> For this reason I say unto you, Every sin and injurious speaking shall be forgiven to men, but speaking injuriously of the Spirit shall not be forgiven to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 3:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Hebrews 6:4–6"> And whosoever shall have spoken a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him; but whosoever shall speak against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven him, neither in this age nor in the coming &#91;one&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 7:16–20; James 4:8"> Either make the tree good, and its fruit good; or make the tree corrupt, and its fruit corrupt. For from the fruit the tree is known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 15:18; Ephesians 4:29"> Offspring of vipers! how can ye speak good things, being wicked? For of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 15:4; Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 15:28"> The good man out of the good treasure brings forth good things; and the wicked man out of the wicked treasure brings forth wicked things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 2:16; Revelation 20:12"> But I say unto you, that every idle word which men shall say, they shall render an account of it in judgment-day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; James 2:21–25"> for by thy words thou shalt be justified, and by thy words thou shalt be condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12; Luke 11:16"> Then answered him some of the scribes and Pharisees, saying, Teacher, we desire to see a sign from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 16:4; Luke 11:29–32; Mark 8:38"> But he, answering, said to them, A wicked and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and a sign shall not be given to it save the sign of Jonas the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jonah 1:17; Matthew 27:63–64; Matthew 16:21"> For even as Jonas was in the belly of the great fish three days and three nights, thus shall the Son of man be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 12:6"> Ninevites shall stand up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it: for they repented at the preaching of Jonas; and behold, more than Jonas &#91;is&#93; here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1–13; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12; Hebrews 1:2–4"> A queen of &#91;the&#93; south shall rise up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it; for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, more than Solomon &#91;is&#93; here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 11:24–26; Luke 8:28–32; Matthew 8:29"> But when the unclean spirit has gone out of the man, he goes through dry places, seeking rest, and does not find &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Revelation 13:8–9; Jude 1:4–5"> Then he says, I will return to my house whence I came out; and having come, he finds &#91;it&#93; unoccupied, swept, and adorned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 11:26; Mark 5:9; Hebrews 6:4–8"> Then he goes and takes with himself seven other spirits worse than himself, and entering in, they dwell there; and the last condition of that man becomes worse than the first. Thus shall it be to this wicked generation also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; John 2:12; John 7:3"> But while he was yet speaking to the crowds, behold, his mother and his brethren stood without, seeking to speak to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Then one said unto him, Behold, thy mother and thy brethren are standing without, seeking to speak to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 3:32–33; Matthew 10:37; Luke 2:52"> But he answering said to him that spoke to him, Who is my mother, and who are my brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 3:34; John 17:8–9; John 20:17–20"> And, stretching out his hand to his disciples, he said, Behold my mother and my brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 8:21; Mark 3:35; John 15:14"> for whosoever shall do the will of my Father who is in &#91;the&#93; heavens, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.</span>
