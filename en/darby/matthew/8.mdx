<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Matthew 4:25–1; Luke 5:15"> And when he had come down from the mountain, great crowds followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:25; Luke 17:12–19; Matthew 18:26"> And behold, a leper came up to &#91;him&#93; and did him homage, saying, Lord, if thou wilt, thou art able to cleanse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:13; Psalms 33:9; 2 Kings 5:14"> And he stretched out his hand and touched him, saying, I will; be cleansed. And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 5:14; Luke 17:14"> And Jesus says to him, See thou tell no man, but go, shew thyself to the priest, and offer the gift which Moses ordained, for a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:1–10; Acts 27:43; Acts 10:1–33"> And when he had entered into Capernaum, a centurion came to him, beseeching him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 9:33"> and saying, Lord, my servant lies paralytic in the house, suffering grievously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 9:18–19; Luke 7:6; Mark 5:23–24"> And Jesus says to him, I will come and heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Luke 7:6–7; Psalms 33:9"> And the centurion answered and said, Lord, I am not fit that thou shouldest enter under my roof; but only speak a word, and my servant shall be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:35–36; Titus 2:9; Colossians 3:22"> For I also am a man under authority, having under me soldiers, and I say to this &#91;one&#93;, Go, and he goes; and to another, Come, and he comes; and to my bondman, Do this, and he does it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Luke 7:9; Mark 6:6"> And when Jesus heard it, he wondered, and said to those who followed, Verily I say unto you, Not even in Israel have I found so great faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:28–29; Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 3:28–29"> But I say unto you, that many shall come from &#91;the&#93; rising and setting &#91;sun&#93;, and shall lie down at table with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of the heavens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:30; Luke 13:28; Matthew 21:43"> but the sons of the kingdom shall be cast out into the outer darkness: there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; Mark 9:23; John 4:50"> And Jesus said to the centurion, Go, and as thou hast believed, be it to thee. And his servant was healed in that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 4:38–41; Mark 1:29–34; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> And when Jesus had come to Peter's house, he saw his mother-in-law laid down and in a fever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; Luke 4:38–39; Luke 8:54"> and he touched her hand, and the fever left her, and she arose and served him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Matthew 14:14; Mark 1:32–34"> And when the evening was come, they brought to him many possessed by demons, and he cast out the spirits with a word, and healed all that were ill;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; 1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 1:22"> so that that should be fulfilled which was spoken through Esaias the prophet, saying, Himself took our infirmities and bore our diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 4:42–43; Luke 8:22; Mark 4:35"> And Jesus, seeing great crowds around him, commanded to depart to the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 22:33–34; John 13:36–38; Luke 14:25–27"> And a scribe came up and said to him, Teacher, I will follow thee whithersoever thou mayest go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 84:3; Luke 2:7; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And Jesus says to him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of the heaven roosting-places; but the Son of man has not where he may lay his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 9:59–62; Leviticus 21:11–12; Numbers 6:6–7"> But another of his disciples said to him, Lord, suffer me first to go away and bury my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Luke 15:32; Ephesians 5:14"> But Jesus said to him, Follow me, and leave the dead to bury their own dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 8:22–25; Mark 4:36–41; Matthew 9:1"> And he went on board ship and his disciples followed him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 8:23; Psalms 107:23–27; Mark 4:37–38"> and behold, &#91;the water&#93; became very agitated on the sea, so that the ship was covered by the waves; but he slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Mark 4:38–39"> And the disciples came and awoke him, saying, Lord save: we perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:28–30"> And he says to them, Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith? Then, having arisen, he rebuked the winds and the sea, and there was a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:33"> But the men were astonished, saying, What sort &#91;of man&#93; is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Mark 5:1–20; Acts 10:38"> And there met him, when he came to the other side, to the country of the Gergesenes, two possessed by demons, coming out of the tombs, exceeding dangerous, so that no one was able to pass by that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34; James 2:19"> And behold, they cried out, saying, What have we to do with thee, Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.29 Elohim">a</a></sup>God? hast thou come here before the time to torment us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:8; Leviticus 11:7; Mark 5:11"> Now there was, a great way off from them, a herd of many swine feeding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 20:1–2; Luke 8:30–33; Mark 5:12"> and the demons besought him, saying, If thou cast us out, send us away into the herd of swine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 5:13; Acts 2:23; Revelation 20:7"> And he said to them, Go. And they, going out, departed into the herd of swine; and lo, the whole herd &#91;of swine&#93; rushed down the steep slope into the sea, and died in the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:34–36; Mark 5:14–16; Acts 19:15–17"> But they that fed them fled, and went away into the city and related everything, and what had happened as to those possessed by demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:37–39; Luke 5:8"> And behold, the whole city went out to meet Jesus; and when they saw him, they begged &#91;him&#93; to go away out of their coasts.</span>
