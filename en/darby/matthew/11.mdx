<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 10:38; Luke 8:1"> And it came to pass when Jesus had finished commanding his twelve disciples, he departed thence to teach and preach in their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Matthew 14:3; Matthew 9:14"> But John, having heard in the prison the works of the Christ, sent by his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:27; Malachi 3:1; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> and said to him, Art thou the coming &#91;one&#93;? or are we to wait for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Jesus answering said to them, Go, report to John what ye hear and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> Blind &#91;men&#93; see and lame walk; lepers are cleansed, and deaf hear; and dead are raised, and poor have glad tidings preached to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 26:31"> and blessed is whosoever shall not be offended in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:6; John 5:35; Mark 1:3–5"> But as they went &#91;away&#93;, Jesus began to say to the crowds concerning John, What went ye out into the wilderness to see? a reed moved about by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; 2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4"> But what went ye out to see? a man clothed in delicate raiment? behold, those who wear delicate things are in the houses of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:76; Matthew 14:5; Matthew 21:24–26"> But what went ye out to see? a prophet? Yea, I say to you, and more than a prophet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 4:5"> this is he of whom it is written, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, who shall prepare thy way before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 7:28; Ephesians 3:8; Matthew 3:11"> Verily I say to you, that there is not arisen among &#91;the&#93; born of women a greater than John the baptist. But he who is a little one in the kingdom of the heavens is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> But from the days of John the baptist until now, the kingdom of the heavens is taken by violence, and &#91;the&#93; violent seize on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:17–18; Romans 3:21; Acts 13:27"> For all the prophets and the law have prophesied unto John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> And if ye will receive it, this is Elias, who is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:8; Matthew 13:9; Mark 4:23"> He that has ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 7:31–35; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 23:36"> But to whom shall I liken this generation? It is like children sitting in the markets, which, calling to their companions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 28:9–13; Isaiah 30:29; Jeremiah 9:17–20"> say, We have piped to you, and ye have not danced: we have mourned to you, and ye have not wailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Matthew 3:4; John 10:20"> For John has come neither eating nor drinking, and they say, He has a demon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:10–11; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 19:7"> The Son of man has come eating and drinking, and they say, Behold, a man &#91;that is&#93; eating and wine-drinking, a friend of tax-gatherers, and of sinners: — and wisdom has been justified by her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:13–15; Jeremiah 8:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Then began he to reproach the cities in which most of his works of power had taken place, because they had not repented.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:6–7; Acts 12:20; Luke 9:10"> Woe to thee, Chorazin! woe to thee Bethsaida! for if the works of power which have taken place in you, had taken place in Tyre and Sidon, they had long ago repented in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:24; Matthew 10:15; Hebrews 10:26–31"> But I say to you, that it shall be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in judgment-day than for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; John 4:46–54; Genesis 13:13"> And thou, Capernaum, who hast been raised up to heaven, shalt be brought down even to hades. For if the works of power which have taken place in thee, had taken place in Sodom, it had remained until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:22; Mark 6:11"> But I say to you, that it shall be more tolerable for &#91;the&#93; land of Sodom in judgment-day than for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; Psalms 8:2; Matthew 16:17"> At that time, Jesus answering said, I praise thee, Father, Lord of the heaven and of the earth, that thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent, and hast revealed them to babes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Job 33:13"> Yea, Father, for thus has it been well-pleasing in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:6–9; Matthew 28:18; Luke 10:22"> All things have been delivered to me by my Father, and no one knows the Son but the Father, nor does any one know the Father, but the Son, and he to whom the Son may be pleased to reveal &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 7:37; John 6:37"> Come to me, all ye who labour and are burdened, and I will give you rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; 1 John 2:6; Philippians 2:7–8"> Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me; for I am meek and lowly in heart; and ye shall find rest to your souls;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:3; John 16:33; Philippians 4:13"> for my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.</span>
</div>
