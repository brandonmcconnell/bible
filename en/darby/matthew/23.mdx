<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> saying, The scribes and the Pharisees have set themselves down in Moses' seat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> all things therefore, whatever they may tell you, do and keep. But do not after their works, for they say and do not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> but bind burdens heavy and hard to bear, and lay them on the shoulders of men, but will not move them with their finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> And all their works they do to be seen of men: for they make broad their phylacteries and enlarge the borders &#91;of their garments&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> and love the chief place in feasts and the first seats in the synagogues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> and salutations in the market-places, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> But ye, be not ye called Rabbi; for one is your instructor, and all ye are brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> And call not &#91;any one&#93; your father upon the earth; for one is your Father, he who is in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Neither be called instructors, for one is your instructor, the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> But the greatest of you shall be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> And whoever shall exalt himself shall be humbled, and whoever shall humble himself shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye shut up the kingdom of the heavens before men; for ye do not enter, nor do ye suffer those that are entering to go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye compass the sea and the dry &#91;land&#93; to make one proselyte, and when he is become &#91;such&#93;, ye make him twofold more &#91;the&#93; son of hell than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> Woe to you, blind guides, who say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalms 94:8"> Fools and blind, for which is greater, the gold, or the temple which sanctifies the gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And, Whosoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gift that is upon it is a debtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> &#91;Fools and&#93; blind ones, for which is greater, the gift, or the altar which sanctifies the gift?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He therefore that swears by the altar swears by it and by all things that are upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> And he that swears by the temple swears by it and by him that dwells in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalms 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> And he that swears by heaven swears by the throne of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.22 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and by him that sits upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye pay tithes of mint and anise and cummin, and ye have left aside the weightier matters of the law, judgment and mercy and faith: these ye ought to have done and not have left those aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> Blind guides, who strain out the gnat, but drink down the camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the dish, but within they are full of rapine and intemperance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> Blind Pharisee, make clean first the inside of the cup and of the dish, that their outside also may become clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye are like whited sepulchres, which appear beautiful outwardly, but within are full of dead men's bones and all uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalms 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> Thus also ye, outwardly ye appear righteous to men, but within are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye build the sepulchres of the prophets and adorn the tombs of the just,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> and ye say, If we had been in the days of our fathers we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> So that ye bear witness of yourselves that ye are sons of those who slew the prophets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> and ye, fill ye up the measure of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> Serpents, offspring of vipers, how should ye escape the judgment of hell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> Therefore, behold, I send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes; and &#91;some&#93; of them ye will kill and crucify, and &#91;some&#93; of them ye will scourge in your synagogues, and will persecute from city to city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> so that all righteous blood shed upon the earth should come upon you, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the temple and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalms 91:4"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, &#91;the city&#93; that kills the prophets and stones those that are sent unto her, how often would I have gathered thy children as a hen gathers her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Behold, your house is left unto you desolate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> for I say unto you, Ye shall in no wise see me henceforth until ye say, Blessed &#91;be&#93; he that comes in the name of &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
