<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; John 12:43; Hebrews 6:10"> Take heed not to do your alms before men to be seen of them, otherwise ye have no reward with your Father who is in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Corinthians 9:6–15"> When therefore thou doest alms, sound not a trumpet before thee, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, so that they may have glory from men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:44"> But thou, when thou doest alms, let not thy left hand know what thy right hand does;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:18; Matthew 6:6; Jeremiah 17:10"> so that thine alms may be in secret, and thy Father who sees in secret will render &#91;it&#93; to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:10–11; Matthew 6:2; Luke 18:13"> And when thou prayest, thou shalt not be as the hypocrites; for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets so that they should appear to men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 14:23; Isaiah 65:24"> But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy chamber, and having shut thy door, pray to thy Father who is in secret, and thy Father who sees in secret will render &#91;it&#93; to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–3; Ecclesiastes 5:7; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> But when ye pray, use not vain repetitions, as those who are of the nations: for they think they shall be heard through their much speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Luke 12:30; Philippians 4:6"> Be not ye therefore like them, for your Father knows of what things ye have need before ye beg &#91;anything&#93; of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:1–4; Revelation 4:11; Romans 8:15"> Thus therefore pray ye: Our Father who art in the heavens, let thy name be sanctified,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 12:50"> let thy kingdom come, let thy will be done as in heaven so upon the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 30:8; Luke 11:3; Matthew 4:4"> give us to-day our needed bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Ephesians 1:7"> and forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 7:14–17; Matthew 26:41"> and lead us not into temptation, but save us from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Colossians 3:13"> For if ye forgive men their offences, your heavenly Father also will forgive you &#91;yours&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:35"> but if ye do not forgive men their offences, neither will your Father forgive your offences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3–5; Matthew 9:14–15; Daniel 9:3"> And when ye fast, be not as the hypocrites, downcast in countenance; for they disfigure their faces, so that they may appear fasting to men: verily I say unto you, They have their reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 3:3; Ecclesiastes 9:8; 2 Samuel 12:20"> But thou, &#91;when&#93; fasting, anoint thy head and wash thy face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Matthew 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:18"> so that thou mayest not appear fasting unto men, but to thy Father who is in secret; and thy Father who sees in secret shall render &#91;it&#93; to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 12:21"> Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon the earth, where moth and rust spoils, and where thieves dig through and steal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:33; Matthew 19:21; James 2:5"> but lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust spoils, and where thieves do not dig through nor steal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Luke 12:34; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> for where thy treasure is, there will be also thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 11:34–36; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Colossians 3:22"> The lamp of the body is the eye; if therefore thine eye be single, thy whole body will be light:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:11; Matthew 6:22; Isaiah 5:20–21"> but if thine eye be wicked, thy whole body will be dark. If therefore the light that is in thee be darkness, how great the darkness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Luke 16:13; James 4:4"> No one can serve two masters; for either he will hate the one and will love the other, or he will hold to the one and despise the other. Ye cannot serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.24 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and mammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; 1 Peter 5:7; Romans 8:32"> For this cause I say unto you, Do not be careful about your life, what ye should eat and what ye should drink; nor for your body what ye should put on. Is not the life more than food, and the body than raiment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:29–31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:24–32"> Look at the birds of the heaven, that they sow not, nor reap, nor gather into granaries, and your heavenly Father nourishes them. Are ye not much more excellent than they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:25–26; Ecclesiastes 3:14; Psalms 39:5–6"> But which of you by carefulness can add to his growth one cubit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; Philippians 4:6; Luke 12:27"> And why are ye careful about clothing? Observe with attention the lilies of the field, how they grow: they toil not, neither do they spin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 9:4–6; 1 Peter 3:2–5"> but I say unto you, that not even Solomon in all his glory was clothed as one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Luke 12:28; 1 Peter 1:24"> But if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.30 Elohim">b</a></sup>God so clothe the herbage of the field, which is to-day, and to-morrow is cast into &#91;the&#93; oven, will he not much rather you, O &#91;ye&#93; of little faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Luke 12:29; Matthew 4:4"> Be not therefore careful, saying, What shall we eat? or What shall we drink? or What shall we put on?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:8; Luke 12:30; Ephesians 4:17"> for all these things the nations seek after; for your heavenly Father knows that ye have need of all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 12:31; Matthew 5:6; John 6:27"> But seek ye first the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.33 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and his righteousness, and all these things shall be added unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; John 14:27; Hebrews 13:5–6"> Be not careful therefore for the morrow, for the morrow shall be careful about itself. Sufficient to the day &#91;is&#93; its own evil.</span>
