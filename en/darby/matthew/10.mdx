<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> And having called to &#91;him&#93; his twelve disciples, he gave them power over unclean spirits, so that they should cast them out, and heal every disease and every bodily weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> Now the names of the twelve apostles are these: first, Simon, who was called Peter, and Andrew his brother; James the &#91;son&#93; of Zebedee, and John his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas, and Matthew the tax-gatherer; James the &#91;son&#93; of Alphaeus, and Lebbaeus, who was surnamed Thaddaeus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Simon the Cananaean, and Judas the Iscariote, who also delivered him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> These twelve Jesus sent out when he had charged them, saying, Go not off into &#91;the&#93; way of &#91;the&#93; nations, and into a city of Samaritans enter ye not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> And as ye go, preach, saying, The kingdom of the heavens has drawn nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> Heal &#91;the&#93; infirm, &#91;raise the dead&#93;, cleanse lepers, cast out demons: ye have received gratuitously, give gratuitously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Do not provide yourselves with gold, or silver, or brass, for your belts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> nor scrip for the way, nor two body coats, nor sandals, nor a staff: for the workman is worthy of his nourishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> But into whatsoever city or village ye enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and there remain till ye go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> And as ye enter into a house salute it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalms 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> And if the house indeed be worthy, let your peace come upon it; but if it be not worthy, let your peace return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words, as ye go forth out of that house or city, shake off the dust of your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> Verily I say unto you, It shall be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrha in judgment-day than for that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> Behold, I send you as sheep in the midst of wolves; be therefore prudent as the serpents, and guileless as the doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> But beware of men; for they will deliver you up to sanhedrims, and scourge you in their synagogues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalms 2:1–6"> and ye shall be brought before rulers and kings for my sake, for a testimony to them and to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But when they deliver you up, be not careful how or what ye shall speak; for it shall be given to you in that hour what ye shall speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> For ye are not the speakers, but the Spirit of your Father which speaks in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> But brother shall deliver up brother to death, and father child; and children shall rise up against parents and shall put them to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> and ye shall be hated of all on account of my name. But he that has endured to &#91;the&#93; end, he shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> But when they persecute you in this city, flee to the other; for verily I say to you, Ye shall not have completed the cities of Israel until the Son of man be come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> The disciple is not above his teacher, nor the bondman above his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> &#91;It is&#93; sufficient for the disciple that he should become as his teacher, and the bondman as his lord. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, how much more those of his household?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> Fear them not therefore; for there is nothing covered which shall not be revealed, and secret which shall not be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> What I say to you in darkness speak in the light, and what ye hear in the ear preach upon the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> And be not afraid of those who kill the body, but cannot kill the soul; but fear rather him who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalms 104:27–30"> Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing? and one of them shall not fall to the ground without your Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> but of you even the hairs of the head are all numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> Fear not therefore; ye are better than many sparrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> Every one therefore who shall confess me before men, I also will confess him before my Father who is in &#91;the&#93; heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But whosoever shall deny me before men, him will I also deny before my Father who is in &#91;the&#93; heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> Do not think that I have come to send peace upon the earth: I have not come to send peace, but a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> For I have come to set a man at variance with his father, and the daughter with her mother, and the daughter-in-law with her mother-in-law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 41:9"> and they of his household &#91;shall be&#93; a man's enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> He who loves father or mother above me is not worthy of me; and he who loves son or daughter above me is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> And he who does not take up his cross and follow after me is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> He that finds his life shall lose it, and he who has lost his life for my sake shall find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> He that receives you receives me, and he that receives me receives him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> He that receives a prophet in the name of a prophet, shall receive a prophet's reward; and he that receives a righteous man in the name of a righteous man, shall receive a righteous man's reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> And whosoever shall give to drink to one of these little ones a cup of cold &#91;water&#93; only, in the name of a disciple, verily I say unto you, he shall in no wise lose his reward.</span>
