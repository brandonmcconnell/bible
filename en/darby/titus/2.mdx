<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3; Titus 2:11–14"> But do thou speak the things that become sound teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> that the elder men be sober, grave, discreet, sound in faith, in love, in patience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> that the elder women in like manner be in deportment as becoming those who have to say to sacred things, not slanderers, not enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is right;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> that they may admonish the young women to be attached to &#91;their&#93; husbands, to be attached to &#91;their&#93; children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> discreet, chaste, diligent in home work, good, subject to their own husbands, that the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God may not be evil spoken of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; 1 Peter 5:5; Joel 2:28"> The younger men in like manner exhort to be discreet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> in all things affording thyself as a pattern of good works; in teaching uncorruptedness, gravity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> a sound word, not to be condemned; that he who is opposed may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say about us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–25; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Colossians 3:22–25"> bondmen to be subject to their own masters, to make themselves acceptable in everything; not gainsaying;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 John 1:9"> not robbing &#91;their masters&#93;, but shewing all good fidelity, that they may adorn the teaching which &#91;is&#93; of our Saviour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.10 Elohim">b</a></sup>God in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God which carries with it salvation for all men has appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> teaching us that, having denied impiety and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, and justly, and piously in the present course of things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> awaiting the blessed hope and appearing of the glory of our great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">d</a></sup>God and Saviour Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all lawlessness, and purify to himself a peculiar people, zealous for good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; 1 Timothy 4:12–13; Titus 1:13"> These things speak, and exhort, and rebuke with all authority. Let no one despise thee.</span>
