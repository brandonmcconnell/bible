<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> Put them in mind to be subject to rulers, to authorities, to be obedient to rule, to be ready to do every good work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:2"> to speak evil of no one, not to be contentious, &#91;to be&#93; mild, shewing all meekness towards all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–3; Romans 6:17; Colossians 1:21"> For we were once ourselves also without intelligence, disobedient, wandering in error, serving various lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, &#91;and&#93; hating one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–10; Titus 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:10"> But when the kindness and love to man of our Saviour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> not on the principle of works which &#91;have been done&#93; in righteousness which we had done, but according to his own mercy he saved us through &#91;the&#93; washing of regeneration and renewal of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Romans 5:5; Acts 10:45"> which he poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Saviour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Titus 2:11; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> that, having been justified by his grace, we should become heirs according to &#91;the&#93; hope of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Titus 1:9; Psalms 16:2–3"> The word &#91;is&#93; faithful, and I desire that thou insist strenuously on these things, that they who have believed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>God may take care to pay diligent attention to good works. These things are good and profitable to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; 2 Timothy 2:23; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But foolish questions, and genealogies, and strifes, and contentions about the law, shun; for they are unprofitable and vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 John 1:10; Matthew 18:15–17"> An heretical man after a first and second admonition have done with,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26–28"> knowing that such a one is perverted, and sins, being self-condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; 2 Timothy 4:12; 1 Corinthians 16:8–9"> When I shall send Artemas to thee, or Tychicus, use diligence to come to me to Nicopolis; for I have decided to winter there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:24; Matthew 22:35; 3 John 1:6–8"> Zenas the lawyer and Apollos set forward diligently on their way, that nothing may be lacking to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Titus 3:8; Matthew 7:19"> and let ours also learn to apply themselves to good works for necessary wants, that they may not be unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Ephesians 6:23–24; 2 John 1:1–2"> All with me salute thee. Salute those who love us in &#91;the&#93; faith. Grace &#91;be&#93; with you all.</span>
