<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; 1 Timothy 6:3; 2 Timothy 2:25"> Paul, bondman of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and apostle of Jesus Christ according to &#91;the&#93; faith of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's elect, and knowledge of &#91;the&#93; truth which &#91;is&#93; according to piety;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 John 2:25; 2 Timothy 1:9"> in &#91;the&#93; hope of eternal life, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, who cannot lie, promised before the ages of time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:11; Titus 3:4–6; 1 Timothy 1:1"> but has manifested in its own due season his word, in &#91;the&#93; proclamation with which I have been entrusted, according to &#91;the&#93; commandment of our Saviour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">d</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Peter 1:1"> to Titus, my own child according to &#91;the&#93; faith common &#91;to us&#93;: Grace and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">e</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father, and Christ Jesus our Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 14:23; 1 Timothy 1:3; Ecclesiastes 12:9"> For this cause I left thee in Crete, that thou mightest go on to set right what remained &#91;unordered&#93;, and establish elders in each city, as I had ordered thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–7; 1 Timothy 3:12; Titus 1:6–8"> if any one be free from all charge &#91;against him&#93;, husband of one wife, having believing children not accused of excess or unruly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; 2 Peter 2:10; 1 Peter 4:10"> For the overseer must be free from all charge &#91;against him&#93; as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.7 Elohim">f</a></sup>God's steward; not headstrong, not passionate, not disorderly through wine, not a striker, not seeking gain by base means;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 2:7; 1 Kings 5:7"> but hospitable, a lover of goodness, discreet, just, pious, temperate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3"> clinging to the faithful word according to the doctrine taught, that he may be able both to encourage with sound teaching and refute gainsayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:6; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 2:4"> For there are many and disorderly vain speakers and deceivers of people's minds, specially those of &#91;the&#93; circumcision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:5; Romans 3:19"> who must have their mouths stopped, who subvert whole houses, teaching things which ought not &#91;to be taught&#93; for the sake of base gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 1 Timothy 4:2; Acts 17:28"> One of themselves, a prophet of their own, has said, Cretans are always liars, evil wild beasts, lazy gluttons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:20; Titus 2:15"> This testimony is true; for which cause rebuke them severely, that they may be sound in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Colossians 2:22; Galatians 4:9"> not turning &#91;their&#93; minds to Jewish fables and commandments of men turning away from the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:23"> All things &#91;are&#93; pure to the pure; but to the defiled and unbelieving nothing &#91;is&#93; pure; but both their mind and their conscience are defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; Ephesians 5:6"> They profess to know<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.16 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, but in works deny &#91;him&#93;, being abominable, and disobedient, and found worthless as to every good work.</span>
