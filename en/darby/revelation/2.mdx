<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:20; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 1:11–13"> To the angel of the assembly in Ephesus write: These things says he that holds the seven stars in his right hand, who walks in the midst of the seven golden lamps:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21"> I know thy works and &#91;thy&#93; labour, and thine endurance, and that thou canst not bear evil &#91;men&#93;; and thou hast tried them who say that themselves &#91;are&#93; apostles and are not, and hast found them liars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 6:9; James 5:7–11"> and endurest, and hast borne for my name's sake, and hast not wearied:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 24:12–13; Jeremiah 2:2–5; Hebrews 6:10–11"> but I have against thee, that thou hast left thy first love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:2–3; Revelation 3:19; Revelation 2:16"> Remember therefore whence thou art fallen, and repent, and do the first works: but if not, I am coming to thee, and I will remove thy lamp out of its place, except thou shalt repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 101:3; 2 John 1:9–10; Psalms 26:5"> But this thou hast, that thou hatest the works of the Nicolaitanes, which I also hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 2:17; Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15"> He that has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. To him that overcomes, I will give to him to eat of the tree of life which is in the paradise of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Revelation 1:8; Revelation 1:11"> And to the angel of the assembly in Smyrna write: These things says the first and the last, who became dead, and lived:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; 2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:5–6"> I know thy tribulation and thy poverty; but thou art rich; and the railing of those who say that they themselves are Jews, and are not, but a synagogue of Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; Matthew 10:22; Revelation 3:10–11"> Fear nothing &#91;of&#93; what thou art about to suffer. Behold, the devil is about to cast of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days. Be thou faithful unto death, and I will give to thee the crown of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; Revelation 20:14; Revelation 21:8"> He that has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. He that overcomes shall in no wise be injured of the second death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:16; Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16"> And to the angel of the assembly in Pergamos write: These things says he that has the sharp two-edged sword:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:9–10; 1 Timothy 5:8; Revelation 3:8–9"> I know where thou dwellest, where the throne of Satan &#91;is&#93;; and thou holdest fast my name, and hast not denied my faith, even in the days in which Antipas my faithful witness &#91;was&#93;, who was slain among you, where Satan dwells.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 31:16; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:20"> But I have a few things against thee: that thou hast there those who hold the doctrine of Balaam, who taught Balak to cast a snare before the sons of Israel, to eat &#91;of&#93; idol sacrifices and commit fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:6"> So thou also hast those who hold the doctrine of Nicolaitanes in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:5"> Repent therefore: but if not, I come to thee quickly, and I will make war with them with the sword of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:2; Isaiah 65:15; Revelation 19:12–13"> He that has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. To him that overcomes, to him will I give of the hidden manna; and I will give to him a white stone, and on the stone a new name written, which no one knows but he that receives &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 1:14–15; Revelation 1:11; John 3:35–36"> And to the angel of the assembly in Thyatira write: These things says the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.18 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, he that has his eyes as a flame of fire, and his feet &#91;are&#93; like fine brass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Peter 1:7; John 15:2"> I know thy works, and love, and faith, and service, and thine endurance, and thy last works &#91;to be&#93; more than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Corinthians 10:18–21"> But I have against thee that thou permittest the woman Jezebel, she who calls herself prophetess, and she teaches and leads astray my servants to commit fornication and eat of idol sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Romans 2:4–5; Revelation 9:20–21"> And I gave her time that she should repent, and she will not repent of her fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 17:2; Luke 13:5; Ezekiel 16:37–41"> Behold, I cast her into a bed, and those that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, unless they repent of her works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Romans 8:27; Psalms 7:9"> and her children will I kill with death; and all the assemblies shall know that I am he that searches &#91;the&#93; reins and &#91;the&#93; hearts; and I will give to you each according to your works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Revelation 12:9; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> But to you I say, the rest who &#91;are&#93; in Thyatira, as many as have not this doctrine, who have not known the depths of Satan, as they say, I do not cast upon you any other burden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 3:11; Revelation 3:3; Hebrews 10:23"> but what ye have hold fast till I shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 3:21; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 2:7"> And he that overcomes, and he that keeps unto the end my works, to him will I give authority over the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 2:8–9; Revelation 12:5; Revelation 19:15"> and he shall shepherd them with an iron rod; as vessels of pottery are they broken in pieces, as I also have received from my Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; 2 Peter 1:19; Luke 1:78–79"> and I will give to him the morning star.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He that has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.</span>
</div>
