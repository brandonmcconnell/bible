<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Isaiah 29:11; Revelation 5:7"> And I saw on the right hand of him that sat upon the throne a book, written within and on the back, sealed with seven seals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:11–12; Revelation 5:5; Revelation 10:1"> And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, Who &#91;is&#93; worthy to open the book, and to break its seals?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34"> And no one was able in the heaven, or upon the earth, or underneath the earth, to open the book, or to regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:8–9; Revelation 4:1"> And I wept much because no one had been found worthy to open the book nor to regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Revelation 22:16"> And one of the elders says to me, Do not weep. Behold, the lion which &#91;is&#93; of the tribe of Juda, the root of David, has overcome &#91;so as&#93; to open the book, and its seven seals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> And I saw in the midst of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb standing, as slain, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God &#91;which are&#93; sent into all the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:1; Revelation 4:2–3"> and it came and took &#91;it&#93; out of the right hand of him that sat upon the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Philippians 2:9–11"> And when it took the book, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell before the Lamb, having each a harp and golden bowls full of incenses, which are the prayers of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; 1 John 1:7; Matthew 26:28"> And they sing a new song, saying, Thou art worthy to take the book, and to open its seals; because thou hast been slain, and hast redeemed to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, by thy blood, out of every tribe, and tongue, and people, and nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 20:6"> and made them to our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God kings and priests; and they shall reign over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Hebrews 12:22; Revelation 4:4"> And I saw, and I heard &#91;the&#93; voice of many angels around the throne and the living creatures and the elders; and their number was ten thousands of ten thousands and thousands of thousands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 19:1; Revelation 4:11"> saying with a loud voice, Worthy is the Lamb that has been slain, to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Psalms 148:2–13"> And every creature which is in the heaven and upon the earth and under the earth, and &#91;those that are&#93; upon the sea, and all things in them, heard I saying, To him that sits upon the throne, and to the Lamb, blessing, and honour, and glory, and might, to the ages of ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 19:4; Revelation 4:9–11; Revelation 5:8"> And the four living creatures said, Amen; and the elders fell down and did homage.</span>
