<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:15; Ezekiel 42:15–20; Zechariah 2:1–2"> And there was given to me a reed like a staff, saying, Rise, and measure the temple of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and the altar, and them that worship in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Daniel 7:25; Luke 21:24"> And the court which &#91;is&#93; without the temple cast out, and measure it not; because it has been given &#91;up&#93; to the nations, and the holy city shall they tread under foot forty-two months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> And I will give &#91;power&#93; to my two witnesses, and they shall prophesy a thousand two hundred &#91;and&#93; sixty days, clothed in sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> These are the two olive trees and the two lamps which stand before the Lord of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> and if any one wills to injure them, fire goes out of their mouth, and devours their enemies. And if any one wills to injure them, thus must he be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Psalms 105:26–36"> These have power to shut the heaven that no rain may fall during the days of their prophecy; and they have power over the waters to turn them into blood, and to smite the earth as often as they will with every plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> And when they shall have completed their testimony, the beast who comes up out of the abyss shall make war with them, and shall conquer them, and shall kill them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> and their body &#91;shall be&#93; on the street of the great city, which is called spiritually Sodom and Egypt, where also their Lord was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> And &#91;men&#93; of the peoples and tribes and tongues and nations see their body three days and a half, and they do not suffer their bodies to be put into a sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> And they that dwell upon the earth rejoice over them, and are full of delight, and shall send gifts one to another, because these, the two prophets, tormented them that dwell upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> And after the three days and a half &#91;the&#93; spirit of life from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God came into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon those beholding them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> And I heard a great voice out of the heaven saying to them, Come up here; and they went up to the heaven in the cloud, and their enemies beheld them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> And in that hour there was a great earthquake, and the tenth of the city fell, and seven thousand names of men were slain in the earthquake. And the remnant were filled with fear, and gave glory to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God of the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second woe has passed; behold, the third woe comes quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Revelation 12:10; Daniel 7:27"> And the seventh angel sounded &#91;his&#93; trumpet: and there were great voices in the heaven, saying, The kingdom of the world of our Lord and of his Christ is come, and he shall reign to the ages of ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> And the twenty-four elders, who sit on their thrones before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.16 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, fell upon their faces, and worshipped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.16 Elohim">e</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> saying, We give thee thanks, Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.17 Elohim">f</a></sup>God Almighty, &#91;He&#93; who is, and who was, that thou hast taken thy great power and hast reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 19:5; Revelation 20:12; Revelation 6:10–11"> And the nations have been full of wrath, and thy wrath is come, and the time of the dead to be judged, and to give the recompense to thy servants the prophets, and to the saints, and to those who fear thy name, small and great; and to destroy those that destroy the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Revelation 15:5–8; Ezekiel 38:22"> And the temple of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="11.19 Elohim">g</a></sup>God in the heaven was opened, and the ark of his covenant was seen in his temple: and there were lightnings, and voices, and thunders, and an earthquake, and great hail.</span>
