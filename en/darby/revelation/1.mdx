<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Revelation 22:6; Galatians 1:12"> Revelation of Jesus Christ, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God gave to him, to shew to his bondmen what must shortly take place; and he signified &#91;it&#93;, sending by his angel, to his bondman John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:6; John 19:35; 1 John 1:1"> who testified the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, and the testimony of Jesus Christ, all things that he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Revelation 22:10; Romans 13:11"> Blessed &#91;is&#93; he that reads, and they that hear the words of the prophecy, and keep the things written in it; for the time &#91;is&#93; near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; John 1:1; Hebrews 13:8"> John to the seven assemblies which &#91;are&#93; in Asia: Grace to you and peace from &#91;him&#93; who is, and who was, and who is to come; and from the seven Spirits which &#91;are&#93; before his throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:27; Hebrews 9:14; 1 John 1:7"> and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn from the dead, and the prince of the kings of the earth. To him who loves us, and has washed us from our sins in his blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Revelation 20:6; Exodus 19:6"> and made us a kingdom, priests to his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and Father: to him &#91;be&#93; the glory and the might to the ages of ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Zechariah 12:10; Daniel 7:13"> Behold, he comes with the clouds, and every eye shall see him, and they which have pierced him, and all the tribes of the land shall wail because of him. Yea. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Isaiah 43:10"> I am the Alpha and the Omega, saith &#91;the&#93; Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, he who is, and who was, and who is to come, the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Revelation 3:10; John 16:33"> I John, your brother and fellow-partaker in the tribulation and kingdom and patience, in Jesus, was in the island called Patmos, for the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, and for the testimony of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:10; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4; Revelation 4:1–2"> I became in &#91;the&#93; Spirit on the Lord's day, and I heard behind me a great voice as of a trumpet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:1; Jeremiah 30:2; Habakkuk 2:2"> saying, What thou seest write in a book, and send to the seven assemblies: to Ephesus, and to Smyrna, and to Pergamos, and to Thyatira, and to Sardis, and to Philadelphia, and to Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And I turned back to see the voice which spoke with me; and having turned, I saw seven golden lamps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Daniel 10:5–6; Daniel 10:16"> and in the midst of the &#91;seven&#93; lamps &#91;one&#93; like &#91;the&#93; Son of man, clothed with a garment reaching to the feet, and girt about at the breasts with a golden girdle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 19:12"> his head and hair white like white wool, as snow; and his eyes as a flame of fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 14:2"> and his feet like fine brass, as burning in a furnace; and his voice as the voice of many waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:12"> and having in his right hand seven stars; and out of his mouth a sharp two-edged sword going forth; and his countenance as the sun shines in its power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Daniel 10:8–10"> And when I saw him I fell at his feet as dead; and he laid his right hand upon me, saying, Fear not; I am the first and the last,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:9; Hebrews 7:25; Romans 14:8–9"> and the living one: and I became dead, and behold, I am living to the ages of ages, and have the keys of death and of hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 4:1–11; Revelation 1:11–3"> Write therefore what thou hast seen, and the things that are, and the things that are about to be after these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:1; Revelation 3:1; Revelation 1:16"> The mystery of the seven stars which thou hast seen on my right hand, and the seven golden lamps. — The seven stars are angels of the seven assemblies; and the seven lamps are seven assemblies.</span>
