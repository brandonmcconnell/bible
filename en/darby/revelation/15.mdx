<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 10:6; Revelation 5:8; Revelation 16:2–1"> And one of the four living creatures gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls, full of the fury of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.7 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, who lives to the ages of ages.</span>
