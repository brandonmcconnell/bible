<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; Revelation 1:10; Isaiah 6:1"> Immediately I became in &#91;the&#93; Spirit; and behold, a throne stood in the heaven, and upon the throne one sitting,</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Revelation 8:5; Revelation 1:4"> And out of the throne go forth lightnings, and voices, and thunders; and seven lamps of fire, burning before the throne, which are the seven Spirits of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God;</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:21"> and the first living creature like a lion, and the second living creature like a calf, and the third living creature having the face as of a man, and the fourth living creature like a flying eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2–13; Revelation 1:8; Hebrews 13:8"> And the four living creatures, each one of them having respectively six wings; round and within they are full of eyes; and they cease not day and night saying, Holy, holy, holy, Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>God Almighty, who was, and who is, and who is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:13–14; Revelation 7:11–12; Revelation 10:6"> And when the living creatures shall give glory and honour and thanksgiving to him that sits upon the throne, who lives to the ages of ages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 19:4; Psalms 95:6"> the twenty-four elders shall fall before him that sits upon the throne, and do homage to him that lives to the ages of ages; and shall cast their crowns before the throne, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Revelation 5:12; Isaiah 40:28"> Thou art worthy, O our Lord and &#91;our&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, to receive glory and honour and power; for thou hast created all things, and for thy will they were, and they have been created.</span>
