<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> And he says to me, Seal not the words of the prophecy of this book. The time is near.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> Behold, I come quickly, and my reward with me, to render to every one as his work shall be.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed &#91;are&#93; they that wash their robes, that they may have right to the tree of life, and that they should go in by the gates into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> Without &#91;are&#93; the dogs, and the sorcerers, and the fornicators, and the murderers, and the idolaters, and every one that loves and makes a lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> I Jesus have sent mine angel to testify these things to you in the assemblies. I am the root and offspring of David, the bright &#91;and&#93; morning star.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 55:1–3"> And the Spirit and the bride say, Come. And let him that hears say, Come. And let him that is athirst come; he that will, let him take &#91;the&#93; water of life freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:6"> I testify to every one who hears the words of the prophecy of this book, If any one shall add to these things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="22.18 Elohim">f</a></sup>God shall add to him the plagues which are written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> And if any one take from the words of the book of this prophecy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="22.19 Elohim">g</a></sup>God shall take away his part from the tree of life, and out of the holy city, which are written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 22:7; Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 22:12"> He that testifies these things says, Yea, I come quickly. Amen; come, Lord Jesus.</span>
