<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> out of &#91;the&#93; tribe of Zabulun, twelve thousand; out of &#91;the&#93; tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand; out of &#91;the&#93; tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand sealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; John 12:13; Psalms 117:1–2"> After these things I saw, and lo, a great crowd, which no one could number, out of every nation and tribes and peoples and tongues, standing before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palm branches in their hands.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:11–13; Revelation 19:4–6; Revelation 4:10"> And all the angels stood around the throne, and the elders, and the four living creatures, and fell before the throne upon their faces, and worshipped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:12–14; Jude 1:25; Psalms 100:4"> saying, Amen: Blessing, and glory, and wisdom, and thanksgiving, and honour, and power, and strength, to our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.12 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, to the ages of ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 5:5"> And one of the elders answered, saying to me, These who are clothed with white robes, who are they, and whence came they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Revelation 22:14; Hebrews 13:12"> And I said to him, My lord, thou knowest. And he said to me, These are they who come out of the great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and have made them white in the blood of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:3–4; Revelation 22:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Therefore are they before the throne of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="7.15 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, and serve him day and night in his temple, and he that sits upon the throne shall spread his tabernacle over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 121:6; Matthew 5:6"> They shall not hunger any more, neither shall they thirst any more, nor shall the sun at all fall on them, nor any burning heat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:37–38; Psalms 36:8–9; John 4:14"> because the Lamb which is in the midst of the throne shall shepherd them, and shall lead them to fountains of waters of life, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="7.17 Elohim">g</a></sup>God shall wipe away every tear from their eyes.</span>
