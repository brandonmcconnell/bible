<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:10–12; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Revelation 11:15"> After these things I heard as a loud voice of a great multitude in the heaven, saying, Hallelujah: the salvation and the glory and the power of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:43; Revelation 6:10; Revelation 18:20"> for true and righteous &#91;are&#93; his judgments; for he has judged the great harlot which corrupted the earth with her fornication, and has avenged the blood of his bondmen at her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Revelation 18:18; Revelation 14:11"> And a second time they said, Hallelujah. And her smoke goes up to the ages of ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 5:14; Nehemiah 8:6; Psalms 106:48"> And the twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell down and did homage to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God who sits upon the throne, saying, Amen, Hallelujah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 134:1; Psalms 148:11–13; Psalms 150:6"> And a voice came out of the throne, saying, Praise our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="19.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, all ye his bondmen, &#91;and&#93; ye that fear him, small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:1; Revelation 14:2; Psalms 93:1"> And I heard as a voice of a great crowd, and as a voice of many waters, and as a voice of strong thunders, saying, Hallelujah, for &#91;the&#93; Lord our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="19.6 Elohim">d</a></sup>God the Almighty has taken to himself kingly power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 21:9; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:9"> Let us rejoice and exult, and give him glory; for the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife has made herself ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Revelation 7:13–14; Psalms 132:9"> And it was given to her that she should be clothed in fine linen, bright &#91;and&#93; pure; for the fine linen is the righteousnesses of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:6; Matthew 22:2–4; Luke 14:15–16"> And he says to me, Write, Blessed &#91;are&#93; they who are called to the supper of the marriage of the Lamb. And he says to me, These are the true words of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="19.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 10:25–26; John 5:39"> And I fell before his feet to do him homage. And he says to me, See &#91;thou do it&#93; not. I am thy fellow-bondman, and &#91;the fellow-bondman&#93; of thy brethren who have the testimony of Jesus. Do homage to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="19.10 Elohim">f</a></sup>God. For the spirit of prophecy is the testimony of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Isaiah 11:3–5; Psalms 98:9"> And I saw the heaven opened, and behold, a white horse, and one sitting on it, &#91;called&#93; Faithful and True, and he judges and makes war in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Luke 10:22; Revelation 2:17–18"> And his eyes are a flame of fire, and upon his head many diadems, having a name written which no one knows but himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 63:1–6; John 1:14"> and &#91;he is&#93; clothed with a garment dipped in blood; and his name is called The Word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="19.13 Elohim">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 19:8"> And the armies which &#91;are&#93; in the heaven followed him upon white horses, clad in white, pure, fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:27"> And out of his mouth goes a sharp &#91;two-edged&#93; sword, that with it he might smite the nations; and he shall shepherd them with an iron rod; and he treads the wine-press of the fury of the wrath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="19.15 Elohim">h</a></sup>God the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 6:15; Daniel 2:47"> And he has upon his garment, and upon his thigh, a name written, King of kings, and Lord of lords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:21; Revelation 8:13; Ezekiel 39:17–20"> And I saw an angel standing in the sun; and he cried with a loud voice, saying to all the birds that fly in mid-heaven, Come, gather yourselves to the great supper of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="19.17 Elohim">i</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:18–20; Revelation 6:15; 1 Samuel 17:44"> that ye may eat flesh of kings, and flesh of chiliarchs, and flesh of strong men, and flesh of horses and of those that sit upon them, and flesh of all, both free and bond, and small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:16; Revelation 16:14; Revelation 17:12–14"> And I saw the beast and the kings of the earth and their armies gathered together to make war against him that sat upon the horse, and against his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:10; Revelation 21:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–11"> And the beast was taken, and the false prophet that &#91;was&#93; with him, who wrought the signs before him by which he deceived them that received the mark of the beast, and those that worship his image. Alive were both cast into the lake of fire which burns with brimstone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 19:11–15; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 19:17–19"> and the rest were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse, which goes out of his mouth; and all the birds were filled with their flesh.</span>
