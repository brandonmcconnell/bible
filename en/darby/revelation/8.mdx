<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Habakkuk 2:20; Revelation 6:1"> And when it opened the seventh seal, there was silence in the heaven about half an hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Revelation 11:15; Revelation 8:6–1"> And I saw the seven angels who stand before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and seven trumpets were given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 8:4; Psalms 141:2"> And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and much incense was given to him, that he might give &#91;efficacy&#93; to the prayers of all saints at the golden altar which &#91;was&#93; before the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Luke 1:10; Revelation 8:3"> And the smoke of the incense went up with the prayers of the saints, out of the hand of the angel before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19; Ezekiel 10:2–7"> And the angel took the censer, and filled it from the fire of the altar, and cast &#91;it&#93; on the earth: and there were voices, and thunders and lightnings, and an earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:2"> And the seven angels who had the seven trumpets prepared themselves that they might sound with &#91;their&#93; trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Revelation 9:4; Revelation 9:18"> And the first sounded &#91;his&#93; trumpet: and there was hail and fire, mingled with blood, and they were cast upon the earth; and the third part of the earth was burnt up, and the third part of the trees was burnt up, and all green grass was burnt up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Exodus 7:17–21; Mark 11:23"> And the second angel sounded &#91;his&#93; trumpet: and as a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea, and the third part of the sea became blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:3; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:12"> and the third part of the creatures which were in the sea which had life died; and the third part of the ships were destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 16:4; Revelation 9:1; Isaiah 14:12"> And the third angel sounded &#91;his&#93; trumpet: and there fell out of the heaven a great star, burning as a torch, and it fell upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Deuteronomy 29:18"> And the name of the star is called Wormwood; and the third part of the waters became wormwood, and many of the men died of the waters because they were made bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:10"> And the fourth angel sounded &#91;his&#93; trumpet: and the third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars; so that the third part of them should be darkened, and that the day should not appear &#91;for&#93; the third part of it, and the night the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 9:12; Revelation 11:14; Revelation 14:6"> And I saw, and I heard an eagle flying in mid-heaven, saying with a loud voice, Woe, woe, woe, to them that dwell upon the earth, for the remaining voices of the trumpet of the three angels who are about to sound.</span>
