<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Deuteronomy 32:40; Revelation 16:17"> And the angel whom I saw stand on the sea and on the earth lifted up his right hand to the heaven,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:3–9; Romans 16:25; Luke 24:44–47"> but in the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he is about to sound the trumpet, the mystery of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God also shall be completed, as he has made known the glad tidings to his own bondmen the prophets.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:1–3; Jeremiah 15:16; Ezekiel 2:8"> And I went to the angel, saying to him to give me the little book. And he says to me, Take and eat it up: and it shall make thy belly bitter, but in thy mouth it shall be sweet as honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Proverbs 16:24; Psalms 119:103"> And I took the little book out of the hand of the angel, and ate it up; and it was in my mouth as honey, sweet; and when I had eaten it my belly was made bitter.</span>
