<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> And I saw an angel descending from the heaven, having the key of the abyss, and a great chain in his hand.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 20:1; Revelation 17:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> and cast him into the abyss, and shut &#91;it&#93; and sealed &#91;it&#93; over him, that he should not any more deceive the nations until the thousand years were completed; after these things he must be loosed for a little time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:18; Matthew 19:28"> And I saw thrones; and they sat upon them, and judgment was given to them; and the souls of those beheaded on account of the testimony of Jesus, and on account of the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God; and those who had not done homage to the beast nor to his image, and had not received the mark on their forehead and hand; and they lived and reigned with the Christ a thousand years:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:11; Revelation 11:15; Luke 14:14"> the rest of the dead did not live till the thousand years had been completed. This &#91;is&#93; the first resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Revelation 1:6; 2 Timothy 2:12"> Blessed and holy he who has part in the first resurrection: over these the second death has no power; but they shall be priests of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and of the Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 20:2"> And when the thousand years have been completed, Satan shall be loosed from his prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Ezekiel 38:1; Revelation 20:10"> and shall go out to deceive the nations which &#91;are&#93; in the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to the war, whose number &#91;is&#93; as the sand of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 39:6; Ezekiel 38:9"> And they went up on the breadth of the earth, and surrounded the camp of the saints and the beloved city: and fire came down &#91;from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="20.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God&#93; out of the heaven and devoured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 19:20; Matthew 25:46"> And the devil who deceived them was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone, where &#91;are&#93; both the beast and the false prophet; and they shall be tormented day and night for the ages of ages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; 2 Peter 3:7; Matthew 25:31"> And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled, and place was not found for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Matthew 16:27; Daniel 7:10"> And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened; and another book was opened, which is &#91;that&#93; of life. And the dead were judged out of the things written in the books according to their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Revelation 6:8; Hosea 13:14"> And the sea gave up the dead which &#91;were&#93; in it, and death and hades gave up the dead which &#91;were&#93; in them; and they were judged each according to their works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:26; Revelation 20:6; Revelation 21:8"> and death and hades were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death, &#91;even&#93; the lake of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:36; Matthew 13:50; Matthew 25:41"> And if any one was not found written in the book of life, he was cast into the lake of fire.</span>
