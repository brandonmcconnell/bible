<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:6; James 2:26; Revelation 1:4"> And to the angel of the assembly in Sardis write: These things saith he that has the seven Spirits of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and the seven stars: I know thy works, that thou hast a name that thou livest, and art dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Timothy 4:1–4; Revelation 16:15"> Be watchful, and strengthen the things that remain, which are about to die, for I have not found thy works complete before my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; 2 Peter 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> Remember therefore how thou hast received and heard, and keep &#91;it&#93; and repent. If therefore thou shalt not watch, I will come &#91;upon thee&#93; as a thief, and thou shalt not know at what hour I shall come upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Revelation 7:9; Ecclesiastes 9:8"> But thou hast a few names in Sardis which have not defiled their garments, and they shall walk with me in white, because they are worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 10:32; Luke 12:8; Revelation 21:27"> He that overcomes, he shall be clothed in white garments, and I will not blot his name out of the book of life, and will confess his name before my Father and before his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He that has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Matthew 16:19; Revelation 1:18"> And to the angel of the assembly in Philadelphia write: These things saith the holy, the true; he that has the key of David, he who opens and no one shall shut, and shuts and no one shall open:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:9; Colossians 4:3; Revelation 3:7"> I know thy works: behold, I have set before thee an opened door, which no one can shut, because thou hast a little power, and hast kept my word, and hast not denied my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Revelation 2:9; Isaiah 49:23"> Behold, I make them of the synagogue of Satan who say that they are Jews, and are not, but lie; behold, I will cause that they shall come and shall do homage before thy feet, and shall know that I have loved thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; Revelation 14:12; Zechariah 13:9"> Because thou hast kept the word of my patience, I also will keep thee out of the hour of trial, which is about to come upon the whole habitable world, to try them that dwell upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 22:12; Revelation 2:10"> I come quickly: hold fast what thou hast, that no one take thy crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 14:1"> He that overcomes, him will I make a pillar in the temple of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, and he shall go no more at all out; and I will write upon him the name of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and the name of the city of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down out of heaven, from my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, and my new name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:7"> He that has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Revelation 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> And to the angel of the assembly in Laodicea write: These things says the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of the creation of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.14 Elohim">g</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21"> I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot; I would thou wert cold or hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:5; Zechariah 11:8–9; Jeremiah 14:19"> Thus because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I am about to spue thee out of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:7; Hosea 12:8; Romans 7:24"> Because thou sayest, I am rich, and am grown rich, and have need of nothing, and knowest not that thou art the wretched and the miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Isaiah 55:1"> I counsel thee to buy of me gold purified by fire, that thou mayest be rich; and white garments, that thou mayest be clothed, and that the shame of thy nakedness may not be made manifest; and eye-salve to anoint thine eyes, that thou mayest see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Hebrews 12:5–11"> I rebuke and discipline as many as I love; be zealous therefore and repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:36–37; Revelation 19:9; John 14:21–23"> Behold, I stand at the door and am knocking; if any one hear my voice and open the door, I will come in unto him and sup with him, and he with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Matthew 19:28; Revelation 2:26–27"> He that overcomes, to him will I give to sit with me in my throne; as I also have overcome, and have sat down with my Father in his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:17"> He that has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies.</span>
