<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17–19; 2 Peter 3:13; Isaiah 66:22"> And I saw a new heaven and a new earth; for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away, and the sea exists no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 21:10"> And I saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down out of the heaven from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="21.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16; John 14:23"> And I heard a loud voice out of the heaven, saying, Behold, the tabernacle of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="21.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; with men, and he shall tabernacle with them, and they shall be his people, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="21.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God himself shall be with them, their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="21.3 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:26"> And he shall wipe away every tear from their eyes; and death shall not exist any more, nor grief, nor cry, nor distress shall exist any more, for the former things have passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 43:19"> And he that sat on the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he says &#91;to me&#93;, Write, for these words are true and faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 4:10; Revelation 7:17"> And he said to me, It is done. I am the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give to him that thirsts of the fountain of the water of life freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Hebrews 8:10; Revelation 2:11"> He that overcomes shall inherit these things, and I will be to him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="21.7 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, and he shall be to me son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Revelation 2:11; Ephesians 5:5–6"> But to the fearful and unbelieving, &#91;and sinners&#93;, and those who make themselves abominable, and murderers, and fornicators, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, their part &#91;is&#93; in the lake which burns with fire and brimstone; which is the second death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:7; Revelation 16:1–17"> And there came one of the seven angels which had had the seven bowls full of the seven last plagues, and spoke with me, saying, Come here, I will shew thee the bride, the Lamb's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 40:1–49"> And he carried me away in &#91;the&#93; Spirit, &#91;and set me&#93; on a great and high mountain, and shewed me the holy city, Jerusalem, coming down out of the heaven from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="21.10 Elohim">f</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 22:1"> having the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="21.11 Elohim">g</a></sup>God. Her shining &#91;was&#93; like a most precious stone, as a crystal-like jasper stone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34; Revelation 7:4–8; Revelation 21:17–21"> having a great and high wall; having twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names inscribed, which are those of the twelve tribes of &#91;the&#93; sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34"> On &#91;the&#93; east three gates; and on &#91;the&#93; north three gates; and on &#91;the&#93; south three gates; and on &#91;the&#93; west three gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 54:11"> And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and on them twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1; Revelation 11:1–2; Ezekiel 41:1–5"> And he that spoke with me had a golden reed &#91;as&#93; a measure, that he might measure the city, and its gates, and its wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:35; Ezekiel 48:8–20"> And the city lies four-square, and its length &#91;is&#93; as much as the breadth. And he measured the city with the reed — twelve thousand stadia: the length and the breadth and height of it are equal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Deuteronomy 3:11; Revelation 13:18"> And he measured its wall, a hundred &#91;and&#93; forty-four cubits, &#91;a&#93; man's measure, that is, &#91;the&#93; angel's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:11; Revelation 21:21; Revelation 21:19"> And the building of its wall &#91;was&#93; jasper; and the city pure gold, like pure glass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; Exodus 28:17–21; Job 28:16–19"> the foundations of the wall of the city &#91;were&#93; adorned with every precious stone: the first foundation, jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 4:3"> the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprasus; the eleventh, jacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:18; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 22:2"> And the twelve gates, twelve pearls; each one of the gates, respectively, was of one pearl; and the street of the city pure gold, as transparent glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:23; 1 Kings 8:27; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> And I saw no temple in it; for the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="21.22 Elohim">h</a></sup>God Almighty is its temple, and the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:11"> And the city has no need of the sun nor of the moon, that they should shine for it; for the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="21.23 Elohim">i</a></sup>God has enlightened it, and the lamp thereof &#91;is&#93; the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 21:26; Revelation 22:2; Zechariah 8:22–23"> And the nations shall walk by its light; and the kings of the earth bring their glory to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 60:11; Zechariah 14:7; Revelation 22:5"> And its gates shall not be shut at all by day, for night shall not be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 21:24"> And they shall bring the glory and the honour of the nations to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 20:15"> And nothing common, nor that maketh an abomination and a lie, shall at all enter into it; but those only who &#91;are&#93; written in the book of life of the Lamb.</span>
