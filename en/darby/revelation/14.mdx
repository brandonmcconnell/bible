<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:32; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 7:3–17"> And I saw, and behold, the Lamb standing upon mount Zion, and with him a hundred &#91;and&#93; forty-four thousand, having his name and the name of his Father written upon their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Ezekiel 43:2; Revelation 1:15"> And I heard a voice out of the heaven as a voice of many waters, and as a voice of great thunder. And the voice which I heard &#91;was&#93; as of harp-singers harping with their harps;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 15:3"> and they sing a new song before the throne, and before the four living creatures and the elders. And no one could learn that song save the hundred &#91;and&#93; forty-four thousand who were bought from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; 2 Corinthians 11:2; James 1:18"> These are they who have not been defiled with women, for they are virgins: these are they who follow the Lamb wheresoever it goes. These have been bought from men &#91;as&#93; first-fruits to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and to the Lamb:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Psalms 32:2; Zephaniah 3:13"> and in their mouths was no lie found; &#91;for&#93; they are blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:15; Revelation 8:13; Ephesians 3:9–11"> And I saw another angel flying in mid-heaven, having &#91;the&#93; everlasting glad tidings to announce to those settled on the earth, and to every nation and tribe and tongue and people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Revelation 15:4; Nehemiah 9:6"> saying with a loud voice, Fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and give him glory, for the hour of his judgment has come; and do homage to him who has made the heaven and the earth and the sea and fountains of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:7–8; Revelation 18:2–3"> And another, a second, angel followed, saying, Great Babylon has fallen, has fallen, which of the wine of the fury of her fornication has made all nations drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 14:11; Revelation 14:6–8; Revelation 13:3–6"> And another, a third, angel followed them, saying with a loud voice, If any one do homage to the beast and its image, and receive a mark upon his forehead or upon his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 16:19; Jeremiah 25:15–17"> he also shall drink of the wine of the fury of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God prepared unmixed in the cup of his wrath, and he shall be tormented in fire and brimstone before the holy angels and before the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 34:10; Matthew 25:41"> And the smoke of their torment goes up to ages of ages, and they have no respite day and night who do homage to the beast and to its image, and if any one receive the mark of its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Revelation 12:17; Revelation 3:10"> Here is the endurance of the saints, who keep the commandments of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God and the faith of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> And I heard a voice out of the heaven saying, Write, Blessed the dead who die in &#91;the&#93; Lord from henceforth. Yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; for their works follow with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Isaiah 19:1; Matthew 17:5"> And I saw, and behold, a white cloud, and on the cloud one sitting like &#91;the&#93; Son of man, having upon his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:39; Mark 4:29"> And another angel came out of the temple, crying with a loud voice to him that sat on the cloud, Send thy sickle and reap; for the hour of reaping is come, for the harvest of the earth is dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 14:19; Revelation 14:14"> And he that sat on the cloud put his sickle on the earth, and the earth was reaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:18; Revelation 14:14–15; Revelation 15:5–6"> And another angel came out of the temple which &#91;is&#93; in the heaven, he also having a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Revelation 16:8; Revelation 14:15–16"> And another angel came out of the altar, having power over fire, and called with a loud cry to him that had the sharp sickle, saying, Send thy sharp sickle, and gather the bunches of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are fully ripened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Revelation 19:15–21"> And the angel put his sickle to the earth, and gathered the vine of the earth, and cast &#91;the bunches&#93; into the great wine-press of the fury of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.19 Elohim">e</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Revelation 19:14–21; Isaiah 63:1–3"> and the wine-press was trodden without the city, and blood went out of the wine-press to the bits of the horses for a thousand six hundred stadia.</span>
