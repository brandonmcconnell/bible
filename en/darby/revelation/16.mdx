<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Jeremiah 10:25; Revelation 15:1"> And I heard a great voice out of the temple, saying to the seven angels, Go and pour out the seven bowls of the fury of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:7; Revelation 16:11; Revelation 13:15–18"> And the first went and poured out his bowl on the earth; and there came an evil and grievous sore upon the men that had the mark of the beast, and those who worshipped its image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–21; Revelation 8:8–9; Revelation 11:6"> And the second poured out his bowl on the sea; and it became blood, as of a dead man; and every living soul died in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–20; Revelation 8:10–11; Hosea 13:15"> And the third poured out his bowl on the rivers, and &#91;on&#93; the fountains of waters; and they became blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 11:17; Revelation 1:4"> And I heard the angel of the waters saying, Thou art righteous, who art and wast, the holy one, that thou hast judged so;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Revelation 11:18; Revelation 6:10–11"> for they have poured out the blood of saints and prophets, and thou hast given them blood to drink; they are worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 6:9; Revelation 15:3–4"> And I heard the altar saying, Yea, Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God Almighty, true and righteous &#91;are&#93; thy judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:20; Revelation 14:18; Jonah 4:8"> And the fourth poured out his bowl on the sun; and it was given to it to burn men with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Revelation 16:21; Revelation 11:13"> And the men were burnt with great heat, and blasphemed the name of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, who had authority over these plagues, and did not repent to give him glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:2; Matthew 8:12; Isaiah 8:21–22"> And the fifth poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast; and its kingdom became darkened; and they gnawed their tongues with distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:2; Revelation 16:21"> and blasphemed the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="16.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God of the heaven for their distresses and their sores, and did not repent of their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:14; Jeremiah 51:36; Isaiah 44:27"> And the sixth poured out his bowl on the great river Euphrates; and its water was dried up, that the way of the kings from the rising of the sun might be prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 20:10"> And I saw out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet, three unclean spirits, as frogs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 4:1; Revelation 20:8"> for they are &#91;the&#93; spirits of demons, doing signs; which go out to the kings of the whole habitable world to gather them together to the war of &#91;that&#93; great day of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="16.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>God the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Matthew 24:42–43; 2 Peter 3:10"> (Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed &#91;is&#93; he that watches and keeps his garments, that he may not walk naked, and that they &#91;may not&#93; see his shame.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; Judges 5:19; Joel 3:9–14"> And he gathered them together to the place called in Hebrew, Armagedon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:30; Revelation 21:6; Ephesians 6:12"> And the seventh poured out his bowl on the air; and there came out a great voice from the temple of the heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> And there were lightnings, and voices, and thunders; and there was a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth, such an earthquake, so great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:5; Revelation 17:18; Revelation 18:21"> And the great city was &#91;divided&#93; into three parts; and the cities of the nations fell: and great Babylon was remembered before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="16.19 Elohim">f</a></sup>God to give her the cup of the wine of the fury of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Revelation 20:11; Jeremiah 4:23–25"> And every island fled, and mountains were not found;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:9"> and a great hail, as of a talent weight, comes down out of the heaven upon men; and men blasphemed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="16.21 Elohim">g</a></sup>God because of the plague of hail, for the plague of it is exceeding great.</span>
