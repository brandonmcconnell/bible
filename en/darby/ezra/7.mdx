<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:1; 1 Chronicles 6:4–14; Ezra 7:21"> And after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes, king of Persia, Ezra the son of Seraiah, the son of Azariah, the son of Hilkijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 2 Samuel 8:17"> the son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> the son of Amariah, the son of Azariah, the son of Meraioth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> the son of Zerahiah, the son of Uzzi, the son of Bukki,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; 2 Chronicles 26:20; 1 Chronicles 6:4"> the son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the chief priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 8:22; Ezra 7:28; Ezra 8:31"> — this Ezra went up from Babylon; and he was a ready scribe in the law of Moses, which Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel had given. And the king granted him all his request, according to the hand of Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 8:1–20; 1 Chronicles 6:31–48; 1 Chronicles 9:17–27"> (And there went up &#91;some&#93; of the children of Israel, and of the priests, and the Levites, and the singers, and the doorkeepers, and the Nethinim, to Jerusalem, in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, which was in the seventh year of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 7:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Nehemiah 2:18"> For upon the first of the first month the project of going up from Babylon was determined on, and on the first of the fifth month he came to Jerusalem, according to the good hand of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 2:7; 2 Timothy 4:2; Matthew 7:24"> For Ezra had directed his heart to seek the law of Jehovah and to do it, and to teach in Israel the statutes and the ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 5:6; Ezra 7:6; Matthew 23:2"> And this is the copy of the letter that king Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, a scribe of the words of the commandments of Jehovah, and of his statutes to Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:37; Ezekiel 26:7; Ezra 4:17"> Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, an accomplished scribe of the law of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God of the heavens, and so forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 6:1; Psalms 148:6; 2 Chronicles 30:5"> I have given orders that all they of the people of Israel, and of their priests and the Levites, in my realm, who are disposed to go to Jerusalem, go with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 1:14; Ezra 7:25–26; Ezra 7:28"> Because thou art sent by the king, and by his seven counsellors, to inquire concerning Judah and Jerusalem, according to the law of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>God which is in thy hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 135:21; 2 Chronicles 6:2; Ezra 6:12"> and to carry the silver and gold which the king and his counsellors have freely offered to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="7.15 Elohim">f</a></sup>God of Israel, whose habitation is at Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:6; Ezra 1:4; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> and all the silver and gold that thou canst find in all the province of Babylon, besides the voluntary offering of the people, and of the priests, who offer willingly for the house of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="7.16 Elohim">g</a></sup>God which is at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–11; Numbers 15:4–13; Ezra 6:9–10"> Therefore thou shalt buy diligently with this money bullocks, rams, lambs, with their oblations and their drink-offerings, and offer them upon the altar of the house of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="7.17 Elohim">h</a></sup>God which is at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 22:7; Ephesians 5:17; 2 Kings 12:15"> And whatever shall seem good to thee and to thy brethren to do with the rest of the silver and the gold, that do according to the will of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="7.18 Elohim">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:17; Ezra 8:33–34; 2 Chronicles 32:19"> And the vessels that have been given thee for the service of the house of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="7.19 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, deliver before the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="7.19 Elohim">k</a></sup>God of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 6:4; Ezra 6:8–18"> And whatever more shall be needful for the house of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="7.20 Elohim">l</a></sup>God which thou shalt have occasion to bestow, bestow it out of the king's treasure house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:16; Ezra 7:6; Ezra 7:10–13"> And I, I Artaxerxes the king, do give orders to all the treasurers that are beyond the river, that whatever Ezra the priest and scribe of the law of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="7.21 Elohim">m</a></sup>God of the heavens shall require of you, it be done diligently,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 16:6–7; Leviticus 2:13; Ezekiel 45:14"> unto a hundred talents of silver, and to a hundred measures of wheat, and to a hundred baths of wine, and to a hundred baths of oil, and salt without prescribing &#91;how much&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:4; Ezra 7:18; Ezra 6:10–12"> Whatever is commanded by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="7.23 Elohim">n</a></sup>God of the heavens, let it be carefully done for the house of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="7.23 Elohim">o</a></sup>God of the heavens; for why should there be wrath against the realm of the king and his sons?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 7:7; Ezra 4:13; Ezra 2:36–55"> Also we inform you, as regards all the priests and Levites, singers, doorkeepers, Nethinim, and ministers of this house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="7.24 Elohim">p</a></sup>God, it shall not be lawful to impose tribute, tax, and toll upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 7:10; Deuteronomy 16:18; James 1:5"> And thou, Ezra, after the wisdom of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="7.25 Elohim">q</a></sup>God, which is in thy hand, set magistrates and judges who may judge all the people that are beyond the river, all such as know the laws of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="7.25 Elohim">r</a></sup>God; and teach ye him that knows &#91;them&#93; not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 6:11; Deuteronomy 13:1–18; Leviticus 20:1–27"> And whosoever will not do the law of thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="7.26 Elohim">s</a></sup>God and the law of the king, let judgment be executed diligently upon him, whether unto death, or to banishment, or to confiscation of goods, or to imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 6:22; Revelation 17:17; Isaiah 60:13"> Blessed be Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="7.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">t</a></sup>God of our fathers, who has put &#91;such a thing&#93; as this in the king's heart, to beautify the house of Jehovah which is at Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Ezra 5:5; Genesis 32:28"> and has extended mercy to me before the king and his counsellors, and before all the king's mighty princes. And I was strengthened, as the hand of Jehovah my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="7.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">u</a></sup>God was upon me; and I gathered together out of Israel chief men to go up with me.</span>
</div>
