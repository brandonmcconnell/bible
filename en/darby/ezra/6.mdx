<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 5:17; Proverbs 25:2; Ezra 4:15"> Then king Darius gave orders, and search was made in the house of the rolls, where the treasures were laid up in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6"> And there was found at Achmetha in the fortress that is in the province of Media a roll, and therein was a record thus written:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22–4; Revelation 21:16; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> In the first year of king Cyrus, king Cyrus made a decree &#91;concerning&#93; the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God at Jerusalem: Let the house be built for a place where they offer sacrifices, and let its foundations be solidly laid; its height sixty cubits, its breadth sixty cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:23"> &#91;with&#93; three rows of great stones, and a row of new timber; and let the expenses be given out of the king's house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 5:2; Ezra 5:14; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7"> and also let the golden and silver vessels of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, which Nebuchadnezzar took out of the temple that is at Jerusalem and brought to Babylon, be restored and brought again to the temple that is at Jerusalem, in their place; and thou shalt put &#91;them&#93; in the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 5:3; Romans 8:31; Proverbs 21:30"> Therefore Tatnai, governor beyond the river, Shethar-boznai, and your companions the Apharsachites, who are beyond the river, be ye far from thence:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:38–39"> let the work of this house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.7 Elohim">d</a></sup>God alone; let the governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews build this house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.7 Elohim">e</a></sup>God in its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 6:4; Haggai 2:8; Ezra 7:15–22"> Moreover, I give orders what ye shall do to these elders of the Jews, for the building of this house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.8 Elohim">f</a></sup>God: that of the king's goods, of the tribute beyond the river, expenses be diligently given to these men, that they be not hindered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Psalms 50:9–13; Mark 9:49"> And that which they have need of, both young bullocks and rams and lambs, for the burnt-offerings to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.9 Elohim">g</a></sup>God of the heavens, wheat, salt, wine, and oil, according to the appointment of the priests that are at Jerusalem, let it be given them day by day without fail;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 7:23; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> that they may present sweet odours to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="6.10 Elohim">h</a></sup>God of the heavens, and pray for the life of the king and of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra 7:26; Daniel 2:5"> Also I have given order that whosoever shall alter this rescript, let timber be pulled down from his house, and being set up, let him be hanged thereon, and let his house be made a dunghill for this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 9:3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 12:11"> And the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="6.12 Elohim">i</a></sup>God that has caused his name to dwell there overthrow every king and people that shall put forth their hand to alter &#91;or&#93; to destroy this house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="6.12 Elohim">j</a></sup>God which is at Jerusalem. I Darius have given &#91;this&#93; order; let it be done diligently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 4:9; Ezra 6:6; Job 5:12–13"> Then Tatnai, governor on this side the river, Shethar-boznai, and their companions, because of that which king Darius had sent, did so diligently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 7:1; Ezra 5:13; Ezra 4:24–2"> And the elders of the Jews built; and they prospered through the prophesying of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo. And they built and completed &#91;it&#93; according to the commandment of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="6.14 Elohim">k</a></sup>God of Israel, and according to the commandment of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes king of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esther 3:7; Esther 9:21; Esther 9:1"> And this house was finished on the third day of the month Adar, which was in the sixth year of the reign of king Darius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:5; 1 Kings 8:63; John 10:22"> And the children of Israel, the priests and the Levites, and the rest of the children of the captivity, kept the dedication of this house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="6.16 Elohim">l</a></sup>God with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 8:35; Revelation 7:4–8; 2 Chronicles 29:21–23"> and they presented at the dedication of this house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="6.17 Elohim">m</a></sup>God a hundred bullocks, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, and for a sin-offering for all Israel, twelve he-goats, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 3:6; 1 Chronicles 24:1; 2 Chronicles 35:4–5"> And they set the priests in their classes, and the Levites in their divisions, for the service of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="6.18 Elohim">n</a></sup>God, which is at Jerusalem: as it is written in the book of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–36; 2 Chronicles 30:1–27; Joshua 5:10"> And the children of the captivity held the passover upon the fourteenth of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:11; Hebrews 7:27; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> For the priests and the Levites had purified themselves as one &#91;man&#93;: they were all pure; and they killed the passover for all the children of the captivity, and for their brethren the priests, and for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 9:11; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 52:11"> And the children of Israel that were come back out of captivity, and all such as had separated themselves to them from the filthiness of the nations of the land, to seek Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="6.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God of Israel, did eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 30:21; Proverbs 21:1"> and they kept the feast of unleavened bread seven days with joy; for Jehovah had made them joyful, and turned the heart of the king of Assyria to them, to strengthen their hands in the work of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="6.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="6.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God of Israel.</span>
</div>
