<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; Ezra 1:11; Ezra 10:16"> And the adversaries of Judah and Benjamin heard that the children of the captivity were building the temple to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; 2 Kings 17:24; Galatians 2:4"> and they came to Zerubbabel and to the chief fathers, and said to them, We would build with you; for we seek your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, as ye; and we have sacrificed to him since the days of Esar-haddon king of Assyria, who brought us up hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:20; Matthew 10:16; 2 Chronicles 36:22–3"> But Zerubbabel and Jeshua and the rest of the chief fathers of Israel said to them, Ye have nothing to do with us to build a house to our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, but we alone will build to Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of Israel, as king Cyrus, the king of Persia, has commanded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 3:3; Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 38:4"> And the people of the land weakened the hands of the people of Judah, and troubled them in building;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:11; Ezra 5:5–18; Psalms 2:1–2"> and they hired counsellors against them, to frustrate their purpose, all the days of Cyrus king of Persia, even until the reign of Darius king of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 1:1; Daniel 9:1; Acts 24:5–9"> And in the reign of Ahasuerus, in the beginning of his reign, they wrote an accusation against the inhabitants of Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:26; Daniel 2:4; Isaiah 36:11"> And in the days of Artaxerxes, Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and the rest of his companions, wrote to Artaxerxes king of Persia; and the writing of the letter was written in Aramaic, and interpreted in Aramaic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:9; 2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25"> Rehum the chancellor and Shimshai the scribe wrote a letter against Jerusalem to Artaxerxes the king after this sort:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 5:6; Ezra 6:6; 2 Kings 17:24"> Rehum the chancellor, and Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their companions, the Dinaites, and the Apharsathchites, the Tarpelites, the Apharsites, the Archevites, the Babylonians, the Shushanchites, the Dehaites, the Elamites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 4:17; Ezra 4:11; Ezra 7:12"> and the rest of the peoples whom the great and noble Osnappar brought over and settled in the cities of Samaria, and the rest &#91;of the country&#93; on this side the river, and so forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> This is the copy of the letter that they sent to him: To Artaxerxes the king: Thy servants the men on this side the river, and so forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 5:9; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Ezra 5:3"> Be it known to the king that the Jews who came up from thee unto us have come to Jerusalem; they are building the rebellious and the bad city, and they complete the walls and join up the foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Romans 13:6–7; Nehemiah 5:4"> Be it known therefore unto the king, that, if this city be built and the walls be completed, they will not pay tribute, tax, and toll, and in the end it will bring damage to the kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 19:12–15; Ezekiel 33:31; John 12:5–6"> Now, since we eat the salt of the palace, and it is not right for us to see the king's injury, therefore have we sent and informed the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezra 4:12; Esther 3:5–8; 2 Kings 24:20–1"> that search may be made in the book of the annals of thy fathers: so shalt thou find in the book of the annals and know that this city is a rebellious city, which has done damage to kings and provinces, and that they have raised sedition within the same of old time, for which cause this city was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 4:20; 1 Kings 4:24; 2 Samuel 8:3"> We inform the king that if this city be built and its walls be completed, by this means thou shalt have no portion on this side the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ezra 5:7; Ezra 4:9–11"> The king sent an answer to Rehum the chancellor, and Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their companions that dwell in Samaria, and the other places beyond the river: Peace, and so forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:8"> The letter that ye sent to us has been read before me distinctly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 18:7; Proverbs 25:2; Deuteronomy 13:14"> And I gave orders, and search has been made, and it has been found that this city of old time has made insurrection against the kings, and that rebellion and sedition have been raised therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 4:21; 1 Chronicles 18:3"> And there have been mighty kings over Jerusalem, who have ruled over all beyond the river; and tribute, tax, and toll were paid to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Now give order to make these men to cease, and that this city be not built, until the order shall be given from me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 7:3–4; Esther 3:8–9; Ezra 4:13"> and take heed that ye fail not to do this: why should harm grow to the damage of the kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Micah 2:1; Ezra 4:17"> As soon as the copy of king Artaxerxes' letter was read before Rehum, and Shimshai the scribe, and their companions, they went up in haste to Jerusalem to the Jews, and made them cease by force and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Haggai 1:15; Nehemiah 6:9; Ezra 5:5"> Then ceased the work of the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.24 Elohim">e</a></sup>God which is at Jerusalem; and it ceased until the second year of the reign of Darius king of Persia.</span>
