<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days there was no king in Israel, and in those days the tribe of the Danites sought for themselves an inheritance to dwell in; for to that day &#91;their lot&#93; had not fallen to them for inheritance among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> And the children of Dan sent of their family five men of their whole number, men of valour, from Zoreah and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land, and to examine it; and they said to them, Go, examine the land. And they came to the hill-country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> When they were by the house of Micah, they knew the voice of the young man, the Levite; and they turned in thither, and said to him, Who brought thee hither? and what doest thou in this &#91;place&#93;? and what hast thou here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> And he said to them, Thus and thus has Micah done to me; and he has hired me, and I am his priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; 1 Kings 22:5"> And they said to him, Inquire, we pray thee, of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, that we may know whether our way on which we go shall be prosperous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 22:15; Psalms 33:18"> And the priest said to them, Go in peace: before Jehovah is your way on which ye go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; 1 Samuel 3:13"> And the five men departed, and came to Laish; and they saw the people that were therein, dwelling securely, after the manner of the Zidonians, quiet and secure; and no one was in the land who possessed authority, that might put &#91;them&#93; to shame in anything; and they were far from the Zidonians, and had nothing to do with &#91;any&#93; man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> — And they came to their brethren at Zoreah and Eshtaol. And their brethren said to them, What &#91;say&#93; ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> And they said, Arise, and let us go up against them; for we have seen the land, and behold, it is very good; and ye are still! Be not slothful to go, to enter to take possession of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> When ye go in, ye shall come to a people secure, and the land is spacious in every direction; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God has given it into your hands; &#91;it is&#93; a place where there is no want of anything that is on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> And there went from thence of the family of the Danites, out of Zoreah and out of Eshtaol, six hundred men girded with weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; 2 Chronicles 1:4"> And they went up and encamped in Kirjath-jearim, in Judah; therefore they call that place Mahaneh-Dan to this day: behold, it is behind Kirjath-jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> And they passed thence to mount Ephraim, and came to the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; 1 Samuel 14:28"> Then the five men that had gone to spy out the country of Laish spoke and said to their brethren, Do ye know that there is in these houses an ephod, and teraphim, and a graven image, and a molten image? And now ye know what to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; 1 Samuel 17:22; John 14:27"> And they turned thither, and came to the house of the young man the Levite, the house of Micah, and inquired after his welfare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> And the six hundred men of the children of Dan, girded with their weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> And the five men that had gone to spy out the land went up, entered in thither, &#91;and&#93; took the graven image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image; and the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men that were girded with weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And these came into Micah's house, and took the carved image, the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image. And the priest said to them, What do ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> And they said to him, Hold thy peace, lay thy hand upon thy mouth, and go with us, and be to us a father and a priest. Is it better for thee to be a priest for the house of one man, or to be priest for a tribe and a family in Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> Then the priest's heart was glad, and he took the ephod, and the teraphim, and the graven image, and went in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And they turned and departed, and put the little ones and the cattle and the baggage before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They were already far from the house of Micah, when the men that were in the houses near to Micah's house were gathered together, and overtook the children of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 114:5; 2 Samuel 14:5; 1 Samuel 11:5"> And they cried to the children of Dan. And they turned their faces, and said to Micah, What aileth thee, that thou comest with such a company?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; Psalms 115:8"> And he said, Ye have taken away my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods which I made, and the priest, and ye are gone away; and what have I more? and what is this that ye say to me, What aileth thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; 1 Samuel 30:6"> And the children of Dan said to him, Let not thy voice be heard among us, lest men of exasperated spirit run upon you, and thou lose thy life and the lives of thy household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the children of Dan went their way; and Micah saw that they were too strong for him, and he turned and went back to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> And they took that which Micah had made, and the priest that he had had, and came upon Laish, upon a people quiet and secure; and they smote them with the edge of the sword, and burned the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> And there was no deliverer, for it was far from Zidon, and they had nothing to do with &#91;any&#93; man; and it &#91;lay&#93; in the valley that is by Beth-rehob. And they built the city and dwelt therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; 1 Kings 15:20"> And they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; howbeit Laish was the name of the city at the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> And the children of Dan set up the graven image; and Jehonathan, the son of Gershom, the son of Moses; he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 1:3; Judges 19:18"> And they set up for themselves Micah's graven image, which he had made, all the time that the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God was in Shiloh.</span>
