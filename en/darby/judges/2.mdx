<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Genesis 17:7–8; Leviticus 26:42"> And the Angel of Jehovah came up from Gilgal to Bochim, and said, I made you to go up out of Egypt, and have brought you to the land which I swore unto your fathers; and I said, I will never break my covenant with you; and as for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–4; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezra 9:1–3"> ye shall make no covenant with the inhabitants of this land; ye shall throw down their altars: but ye have not hearkened unto my voice. Why have ye done this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 23:13; Numbers 33:55; Deuteronomy 7:16"> Wherefore I also said, I will not drive them out from before you; but they shall be &#91;scourges&#93; in your sides, and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods shall be a snare unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Proverbs 17:10; Zechariah 12:10"> And it came to pass, when the Angel of Jehovah spoke these words to all the children of Israel, that the people lifted up their voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 35:8; Joshua 7:26"> And they called the name of that place Bochim; and they sacrificed there to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 24:28–31; Joshua 22:6"> And Joshua dismissed the people, and the children of Israel went every man to his inheritance to possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Joshua 24:31; 2 Chronicles 24:2"> And the people served Jehovah all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders whose days were prolonged after Joshua, who had seen all the great works of Jehovah, which he had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:29–30"> And Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of Jehovah, died, a hundred and ten years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Joshua 24:30"> And they buried him in the border of his inheritance in Timnath-Heres, in mount Ephraim, on the north side of the mountain of Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Titus 1:16; Exodus 5:2"> And also all that generation were gathered to their fathers; and there arose another generation after them, which knew not Jehovah, nor yet the works which he had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 4:1; Judges 6:1; Judges 3:7"> And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of Jehovah, and served the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 6:14–15"> And they forsook Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of their fathers, who had brought them up out of the land of Egypt, and followed other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods of the peoples that were round about them, and bowed themselves to them, and provoked Jehovah to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Psalms 106:36; Judges 3:7"> And they forsook Jehovah, and served Baal and the Ashtoreths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 17:20; Psalms 106:40–42; Deuteronomy 32:30"> And the anger of Jehovah was hot against Israel, and he delivered them into the hands of spoilers that spoiled them, and he sold them into the hands of their enemies round about; and they could not any longer stand before their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Joshua 23:15–16; Leviticus 26:14–46"> Whithersoever they went out the hand of Jehovah was against them for evil, as Jehovah had said, and as Jehovah had sworn unto them; and they were greatly distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:20; Psalms 106:43–45; 1 Samuel 12:11"> And Jehovah raised up judges, and they saved them out of the hand of those that spoiled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:12; Judges 2:7"> But they did not even hearken to their judges, for they went a whoring after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods, and bowed themselves to them; they turned quickly out of the way that their fathers had walked in, obeying the commandments of Jehovah; they did not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Joshua 1:5; Exodus 2:24"> And when Jehovah raised them up judges, then Jehovah was with the judge, and saved them out of the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge; for it repented Jehovah because of their groanings by reason of them that oppressed them and crushed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 4:1; Matthew 23:32"> And it came to pass when the judge died, that they turned back and corrupted themselves more than their fathers, in following other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods to serve them, and to bow down to them: they ceased not from their own doings, nor from their stubborn way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Joshua 23:16; Exodus 32:10–11"> And the anger of Jehovah was hot against Israel; and he said, Because this nation hath transgressed my covenant which I commanded their fathers, and hath not hearkened unto my voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:3; Judges 3:3"> I also will not henceforth dispossess from before them any of the nations that Joshua left when he died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Deuteronomy 8:16; Deuteronomy 13:3"> that through them I may prove Israel, whether they will keep the way of Jehovah to walk therein, as their fathers did keep &#91;it&#93;, or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Therefore Jehovah left those nations, without dispossessing them hastily, neither delivered he them into the hand of Joshua.</span>
