<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 6:1; Romans 2:6"> And the children of Israel again did evil in the sight of Jehovah; and Jehovah gave them into the hand of the Philistines forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 19:41; Luke 1:7; Joshua 15:33"> And there was a certain man of Zoreah, of the family of the Danites, and his name was Manoah. And his wife was barren and did not bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Luke 1:11; Judges 6:11–12"> And the Angel of Jehovah appeared to the woman, and said to her, Behold now, thou art barren and bearest not; but thou shalt conceive and bear a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Judges 13:14; Numbers 6:2–3"> And now beware, I pray thee, and drink not wine nor strong drink, and eat nothing unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Chronicles 18:1"> For lo, thou shalt conceive, and bear a son, and no razor shall come on his head; for the boy shall be a Nazarite of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God from the womb; and he shall begin to save Israel out of the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 13:17–18; 1 Samuel 9:6; Matthew 28:3–4"> And the woman came and told her husband, saying, A man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of an angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, very terrible; but I did not ask him whence he was, neither did he tell me his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 13:4"> And he said to me, Behold, thou shalt conceive and bear a son; and now drink not wine nor strong drink, and eat not anything unclean; for the boy shall be a Nazarite of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God from the womb to the day of his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Job 34:32; Proverbs 3:5–6"> Then Manoah prayed to Jehovah, and said, Ah Lord! let the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God which thou didst send come again unto us, I pray thee, and teach us what we shall do unto the child that shall be born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:2; Matthew 7:7–11"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God hearkened to the voice of Manoah; and the Angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God came again to the woman whilst she sat in the field; but Manoah her husband was not with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:28–29; John 1:41–42"> Then the woman hasted and ran, and informed her husband, and said to him, Behold, the man has appeared to me, that came to me that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Manoah rose up and went after his wife, and came to the man, and said to him, Art thou the man that didst speak to the woman? And he said, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 22:6; Ephesians 6:4; Genesis 18:19"> And Manoah said, When thy words then come to pass, what shall be the child's manner and his doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Angel of Jehovah said to Manoah, Of all that I said unto the woman let her beware:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 2:5; Judges 13:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:4"> she shall not eat of anything that cometh of the vine, neither shall she drink wine or strong drink, nor eat anything unclean: all that I commanded her shall she observe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:18–19; Genesis 18:3–5; Judges 13:3"> And Manoah said to the Angel of Jehovah, I pray thee, let us detain thee, and we will make ready a kid of the goats for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 13:23; Judges 6:20; Judges 6:26"> And the Angel of Jehovah said to Manoah, Though thou shouldest detain me, I will not eat of thy bread; and if thou wilt offer a burnt-offering, thou shalt offer it up to Jehovah. For Manoah knew not that he was the Angel of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 32:29"> And Manoah said to the Angel of Jehovah, What is thy name, that when thy word cometh to pass we may do thee honour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:29; Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:6"> And the Angel of Jehovah said to him, How is it that thou askest after my name, seeing it is wonderful?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 6:19–21; 1 Kings 18:30–38"> Then Manoah took the kid and the oblation, and offered it up to Jehovah upon the rock. And he did wondrously, and Manoah and his wife looked on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Chronicles 21:16; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it came to pass, as the flame went up from off the altar towards the heavens, that the Angel of Jehovah ascended in the flame of the altar; and Manoah and his wife looked on, and fell on their faces to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Hosea 12:4–5; Judges 13:16"> And the Angel of Jehovah appeared no more to Manoah and to his wife. Then Manoah knew that it was the Angel of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Deuteronomy 5:26; John 1:18"> And Manoah said to his wife, We shall surely die, because we have seen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; 1 Corinthians 12:21; John 15:15"> But his wife said to him, If Jehovah were pleased to kill us, he would not have received a burnt-offering and an oblation at our hands, neither would he have shewed us all these things, nor would he at this time have told us &#91;such things&#93; as these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:80; 1 Samuel 3:19; Hebrews 11:32"> And the woman bore a son, and called his name Samson. And the child grew, and Jehovah blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Joshua 15:33; John 3:34"> And the Spirit of Jehovah began to move him at Mahaneh-Dan, between Zoreah and Eshtaol.</span>
