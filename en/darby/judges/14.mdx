<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> And Samson went down to Timnathah, and saw a woman in Timnathah of the daughters of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> And he went up, and told his father and his mother, and said, I have seen a woman in Timnathah of the daughters of the Philistines; and now take her for me as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 14:6"> And his father and his mother said to him, Is there no woman among the daughters of thy brethren, and among all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the Philistines, the uncircumcised? And Samson said to his father, Take her for me, for she pleases me well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> And his father and his mother did not know that it was of Jehovah, that he was seeking an occasion against the Philistines. Now at that time the Philistines were ruling over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Samson went down, and his father and his mother, to Timnathah; and they came to the vineyards of Timnathah. And behold, a young lion roared against him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6"> and the Spirit of Jehovah came upon him, and he rent it as one rends a kid, and nothing was in his hand. And he did not tell his father or his mother what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he went down and talked with the woman; and she pleased Samson well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> And he returned after a time to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcase of the lion; and behold, &#91;there was&#93; a swarm of bees in the carcase of the lion, and honey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> and he took it out in his hands, and went on, and ate as he went. And he came to his father and to his mother, and gave them, and they ate; but he did not tell them that he had taken the honey out of the carcase of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> And his father went down to the woman, and Samson made there a feast; for so used the young men to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; 1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 10:23"> And it came to pass when they saw him, that they brought thirty companions, and they were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> And Samson said to them, Let me now propound a riddle to you; if ye clearly explain it to me within the seven days of the feast, and find &#91;it&#93; out, then I will give you thirty shirts, and thirty changes of garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But if ye cannot explain &#91;it&#93; to me, then shall ye give me thirty shirts, and thirty changes of garments. And they said to him, Propound thy riddle, that we may hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; 1 Kings 17:6; Romans 8:37"> And he said to them, Out of the eater came forth food, And out of the strong came forth sweetness. And they could not in three days explain the riddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> And it came to pass on the seventh day, that they said to Samson's wife, Persuade thy husband, that he may explain to us the riddle, lest we burn thee and thy father's house with fire. Have ye invited us to impoverish us, — is it not &#91;so&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> And Samson's wife wept before him, and said, Thou dost but hate me, and lovest me not. Thou hast propounded the riddle to the children of my people, and hast not explained it to me. And he said to her, Behold, I have not explained it to my father nor my mother, and shall I explain it to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> And she wept before him the seven days, while they had the feast. And it came to pass on the seventh day, that he explained it to her, for she pressed him. And she explained the riddle to the children of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the men of the city said to him on the seventh day before the sun went down, What is sweeter than honey, And what stronger than a lion? And he said to them, If ye had not ploughed with my heifer, Ye had not found out my riddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> And the Spirit of Jehovah came upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon, and slew of them thirty men, and took their spoil, and gave the changes of garments unto them that explained the riddle. And his anger was kindled, and he went up to his father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> And Samson's wife was &#91;given&#93; to his companion, whom he had made his friend.</span>
