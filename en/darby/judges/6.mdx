<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26–29; Judges 2:19–20; Judges 2:13–14"> And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of Jehovah; and Jehovah delivered them into the hand of Midian seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:38; 1 Samuel 13:6; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> And the hand of Midian prevailed against Israel. Because of the Midianites the children of Israel made for themselves the dens that are in the mountains, and the caves, and the strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Judges 3:13; Judges 7:12"> And it came to pass when Israel sowed, that Midian came up, and Amalek, and the children of the east, and came up against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:51; Micah 6:15"> And they encamped against them, and destroyed the produce of the land, until thou come to Gazah, and they left no sustenance in Israel, neither sheep, nor ox, nor ass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 8:10; Jeremiah 46:23"> For they came up with their cattle and their tents, and they came as locusts for multitude; both they and their camels were without number; and they entered into the land to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Jeremiah 5:17; Psalms 50:15"> And Israel was greatly impoverished because of Midian. And the children of Israel cried to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it came to pass when the children of Israel cried to Jehovah because of Midian,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–32; Isaiah 63:9–14; Psalms 136:10–16"> that Jehovah sent a prophet to the children of Israel, who said to them, Thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel: I brought you up from Egypt, and brought you forth out of the house of bondage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 44:2–3"> and I delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all that oppressed you, and drove them out from before you, and gave you their land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:2; 2 Kings 17:35–39; Jeremiah 9:13"> and I said to you, I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; fear not the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell. But ye have not hearkened to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 17:2; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 13:3"> And an angel of Jehovah came and sat under the terebinth that was in Ophrah, that &#91;belonged&#93; to Joash the Abi-ezrite. And his son Gideon threshed wheat in the winepress, to secure &#91;it&#93; from the Midianites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Luke 1:28; Judges 2:18"> And the Angel of Jehovah appeared to him, and said to him, Jehovah is with thee, thou mighty man of valour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 77:7–9; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 59:1–2"> And Gideon said to him, Ah my Lord, if Jehovah be with us, why then is all this befallen us? and where are all his miracles that our fathers told us of, saying, Did not Jehovah bring us up from Egypt? And now Jehovah hath cast us off, and given us into the hand of Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:5–9; Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 12:11"> And Jehovah looked upon him, and said, Go in this thy might, and thou shalt save Israel from the hand of Midian. Have not I sent thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Exodus 3:11; Exodus 4:10"> And he said to him, Ah Lord, wherewith shall I save Israel? behold, my thousand is the poorest in Manasseh, and I am the least in my father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Exodus 3:12; Acts 11:21"> And Jehovah said to him, I will certainly be with thee; and thou shalt smite Midian as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:17; Exodus 33:13; Isaiah 7:11"> And he said to him, If now I have found favour in thine eyes, shew me a sign that it is thou who talkest with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 13:15; Genesis 18:3; Genesis 18:5"> Depart not hence, I pray thee, until I come unto thee, and bring forth my present, and set it before thee. And he said, I will tarry until thou come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 13:15–19; Leviticus 2:4"> And Gideon went in, and made ready a kid of the goats, and an ephah of flour in unleavened cakes: the flesh he put in a basket, and he put the broth in a pot, and brought it out to him under the terebinth, and presented it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:19; 1 Kings 18:33–34"> And the Angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God said to him, Take the flesh and the unleavened cakes, and lay them upon this rock, and pour out the broth. And he did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Kings 18:38; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> And the Angel of Jehovah put forth the end of the staff that was in his hand, and touched the flesh and the unleavened cakes; and there rose up fire out of the rock, and consumed the flesh and the unleavened cakes. And the Angel of Jehovah departed out of his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Exodus 33:20; Deuteronomy 5:26"> And Gideon perceived that he was an angel of Jehovah; and Gideon said, Alas, Lord Jehovah! for because I have seen an angel of Jehovah face to face...</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; John 20:26; Genesis 43:23"> And Jehovah said to him, Peace be unto thee: fear not; thou shalt not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Exodus 17:15; Judges 8:32"> And Gideon built there an altar to Jehovah, and called it Jehovah-shalom. To this day it is yet in Ophrah of the Abi-ezrites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; Judges 3:7; Acts 5:29"> And it came to pass the same night, that Jehovah said to him, Take the young bullock, which thy father hath, even the second bullock of seven years old, and throw down the altar of Baal that thy father hath, and cut down the Asherah that is by it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Samuel 24:18; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> and build an altar to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God upon the top of this strong place in the ordered manner, and take the second bullock, and offer up a burnt-offering with the wood of the Asherah that thou shalt cut down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; John 2:5; Deuteronomy 4:1"> And Gideon took ten men of his servants, and did as Jehovah had said to him. And it came to pass, because he feared his father's house, and the men of the city, if he did it by day, that he did it by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And when the men of the city arose early in the morning, behold, the altar of Baal was broken down, and the Asherah was cut down that was by it, and the second bullock was offered up upon the altar that was built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they said one to another, Who has done this thing? And when they inquired and asked, they said, Gideon the son of Joash has done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:9; Philippians 3:6; Jeremiah 50:38"> And the men of the city said to Joash, Bring out thy son, that he may die, because he has broken down the altar of Baal, and because he has cut down the Asherah that was by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 5:11"> And Joash said to all that stood near him, Will ye contend for Baal? or will ye save him? he that contends for him, let him be put to death whilst it is yet morning. If he be a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>god, let him plead for himself, because they have broken down his altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:11; Judges 7:1; 2 Samuel 11:21"> And on that day they called him Jerubbaal, saying, Let Baal plead with him, because he has broken down his altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 17:16; Judges 6:3; Romans 8:35–39"> And all Midian and Amalek and the children of the east were gathered together, and went over, and encamped in the valley of Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 3:27; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Judges 3:10"> And the Spirit of Jehovah came upon Gideon, and he blew the trumpet, and the Abi-ezrites were gathered after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6–12; Judges 4:6"> And he sent messengers throughout Manasseh, and they also were gathered after him; and he sent messengers to Asher, and to Zebulun, and to Naphtali; and they came up to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 6:14; Matthew 16:1; Judges 6:17–20"> And Gideon said to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, If thou wilt save Israel by my hand, as thou hast said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hosea 14:5; Matthew 10:5–6; Matthew 15:24"> behold, I put a fleece of wool on the threshing-floor; if dew shall be on the fleece only, and it be dry upon all the ground, then shall I know that thou wilt save Israel by my hand, as thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7"> And it was so. And when he rose up early on the morrow, he pressed the fleece together, and wrung dew out of the fleece, a bowl-full of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 18:32; Isaiah 50:2; Psalms 107:33–35"> And Gideon said to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="6.39 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, Let not thine anger be hot against me, and I will speak but this once! Let me prove, I pray thee, but this once with the fleece; let it, I pray thee, be dry upon the fleece only, and upon all the ground let there be dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="6.40 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God did so that night, and it was dry upon the fleece only, but on all the ground there was dew.</span>
