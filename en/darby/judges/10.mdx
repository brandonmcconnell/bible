<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:16; Judges 3:9; Joshua 15:48"> And after Abimelech, there rose up to save Israel Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of Issachar; and he dwelt in Shamir on mount Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he judged Israel twenty-three years; and he died, and was buried in Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Genesis 31:48"> And after him rose up Jair, a Gileadite; and he judged Israel twenty-two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Judges 5:10; Deuteronomy 3:14"> And he had thirty sons who rode on thirty ass colts; and they had thirty cities, which are called the villages of Jair to this day, which are in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jair died, and was buried in Kamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> And the children of Israel again did evil in the sight of Jehovah, and served the Baals, and the Ashtoreths, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods of Syria, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>gods of Zidon, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods of Moab, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods of the children of Ammon, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods of the Philistines; and they forsook Jehovah, and served him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> And the anger of Jehovah was hot against Israel, and he sold him into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> And they oppressed and crushed the children of Israel in that year; eighteen years &#91;they oppressed&#93; all the children of Israel that were beyond the Jordan in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> And the children of Ammon passed over the Jordan to fight also against Judah, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim; and Israel was greatly distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:13"> And the children of Israel cried to Jehovah, saying, We have sinned against thee, both because we have forsaken our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, and also served the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> And Jehovah said to the children of Israel, Did I not &#91;save you&#93; from the Egyptians, and from the Amorites, from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalms 106:42–43"> The Zidonians also, and Amalek and Maon oppressed you, and ye cried to me, and I saved you out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> But ye have forsaken me, and served other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>gods; therefore I will save you no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go and cry to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="10.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>gods that ye have chosen: let them save you in the time of your trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> And the children of Israel said to Jehovah, We have sinned. Do thou unto us according to all that is good in thy sight; only deliver us, we pray thee, this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> And they put away the strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="10.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>gods from among them, and served Jehovah; and his soul was grieved for the misery of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> And the children of Ammon were called together and encamped in Gilead; and the children of Israel gathered together and encamped in Mizpeh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> And the people, the chief men of Gilead, said one to another, Who is the man that will begin to fight against the children of Ammon? he shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.</span>
