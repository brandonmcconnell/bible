<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:31; Psalms 83:2–4; 2 Samuel 15:6"> And Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem to his mother's brethren, and spoke to them, and to all the family of the house of his mother's father, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:14; Judges 8:30; Hebrews 2:14"> Speak, I pray you, in the ears of all the citizens of Shechem, Which is better for you, that seventy persons, all sons of Jerubbaal, rule over you, or that one man rule over you? And remember that I am your bone and your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Psalms 10:3; Proverbs 1:11–14"> And his mother's brethren spoke of him in the ears of all the citizens of Shechem all these words. And their heart inclined after Abimelech; for they said, He is our brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Acts 17:5; 2 Chronicles 13:7"> And they gave him seventy pieces of silver out of the house of Baal-Berith, and Abimelech hired with them vain and wanton men, and they followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 11:1–2; 2 Chronicles 21:4; Judges 6:24"> And he came to his father's house, to Ophrah, and slew his brethren the sons of Jerubbaal, seventy persons upon one stone; but there remained Jotham the youngest son of Jerubbaal; for he had hid himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:9; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 12:20"> And all the citizens of Shechem gathered together, and all the house of Millo, and went and made Abimelech king, by the memorial-oak that is in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; John 4:20; Deuteronomy 27:12"> And they told it to Jotham, and he went and stood on the top of mount Gerizim, and lifted up his voice, and cried, and said to them, Hearken to me, ye citizens of Shechem, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God may hearken to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 8:22–23; Daniel 4:10–18; 2 Kings 14:9"> The trees once went forth to anoint a king over them; and they said to the olive-tree, Reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:7; Acts 4:27"> And the olive-tree said to them, Should I leave my fatness, wherewith by me they honour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God and man, and go to wave over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the trees said to the fig-tree, Come thou, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:6–7"> But the fig-tree said to them, Should I leave my sweetness, and my good fruit, and go to wave over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then said the trees to the vine, Come thou, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Numbers 15:5"> And the vine said to them, Should I leave my new wine, which cheers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God and man, and go to wave over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 14:9"> Then said all the trees to the thorn-bush, Come thou, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:2; Judges 9:20; Hosea 14:7"> And the thorn-bush said to the trees, If in truth ye anoint me king over you, come, put confidence in my shadow; but if not, fire shall come out of the thorn-bush and devour the cedars of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:35"> Now therefore, if ye have dealt truly and sincerely in that ye have made Abimelech king, and if ye have dealt well with Jerubbaal and his house, and if ye have done to him according to the deserving of his hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:4; Romans 5:8; Revelation 12:11"> — for my father fought for you, and endangered his life, and delivered you out of the hand of Midian;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:5–6; Judges 8:30–31; Judges 9:14"> but ye are risen up against my father's house this day, and have slain his sons, seventy men, upon one stone, and have made Abimelech, the son of his handmaid, king over the citizens of Shechem, because he is your brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:6; James 4:16; Philippians 3:3"> — if ye then have dealt truly and sincerely with Jerubbaal and with his house this day, rejoice ye in Abimelech, and let him also rejoice in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:15; Judges 9:56–57; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23"> but if not, let fire come out from Abimelech, and devour the citizens of Shechem and the house of Millo; and let fire come out from the citizens of Shechem and from the house of Millo, and devour Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 21:16; 2 Samuel 20:14; Joshua 19:8"> And Jotham ran away, and fled, and went to Beer, and dwelt there, because of Abimelech his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Abimelech ruled over Israel three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 19:14; Isaiah 19:2"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the citizens of Shechem; and the citizens of Shechem dealt treacherously with Abimelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 2:32; Psalms 7:16; Esther 9:25"> that the violence &#91;done&#93; to the seventy sons of Jerubbaal might come, and their blood be laid upon Abimelech their brother, who slew them, and upon the citizens of Shechem, who had strengthened his hands to slay his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:12–13; Proverbs 1:11–12; Joshua 8:4"> And the citizens of Shechem set liers in wait for him on the tops of the mountains, and they robbed all that came along that way by them. And it was told Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 19:7"> And Gaal the son of Ebed came with his brethren, and went over to Shechem; and the citizens of Shechem put confidence in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 9:4; Judges 9:46; Psalms 109:17"> And they went out into the fields, and gathered their vineyards, and trode &#91;the grapes&#93;, and made rejoicings, and went into the house of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>god, and ate and drank, and cursed Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 34:2; Genesis 34:6; 1 Samuel 25:10"> And Gaal the son of Ebed said, Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? is he not the son of Jerubbaal? and Zebul his overseer? Serve the men of Hamor the father of Shechem! and why should we serve him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:4; 2 Samuel 2:14–17; Isaiah 36:8–9"> Oh! would that this people were under my hand! then would I remove Abimelech. And he said to Abimelech, Increase thine army, and come out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Zebul the ruler of the city heard the words of Gaal the son of Ebed, and his anger was kindled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and he sent messengers to Abimelech craftily, saying, Behold, Gaal the son of Ebed and his brethren are come to Shechem, and behold, they shut up the city against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Psalms 36:4; Proverbs 1:11–16"> and now, rise up by night, thou and the people that are with thee, and lie in ambush in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:7; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Leviticus 25:26"> And it shall be in the morning when the sun is up, thou shalt rise early, and fall upon the city; and behold, he and the people that is with him shall come out against thee, and thou shalt do with him as thou shalt find occasion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Abimelech rose up, and all the people that were with him, by night, and they lay in ambush against Shechem in four companies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Gaal the son of Ebed went out, and stood in the entrance of the gate of the city. Then Abimelech rose up, and the people that were with him, out of the ambush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 8:24; Ezekiel 7:7"> And Gaal saw the people, and said to Zebul, Behold, people are coming down from the tops of the mountains. And Zebul said to him, Thou seest the shadow of the mountains as men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:14"> And Gaal spoke again, and said, Behold, people are coming down from the high part of the land, and one company is coming along by the way of the Magician's oak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 9:28–29; 2 Kings 14:8–14; 2 Samuel 2:26–27"> Then said Zebul to him, Where is now thy mouth, thou that saidst, Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him? is not this the people that thou hast despised? go out now, I pray, and fight against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Gaal went out before the citizens of Shechem, and fought against Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 20:18–21; 1 Kings 20:30"> And Abimelech pursued him, and he fled before him, and there fell many wounded, as far as the entrance of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Judges 9:30"> And Abimelech dwelt at Arumah. And Zebul drove out Gaal and his brethren, that they might not dwell in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that the people went out into the field; and they told Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And he took the people, and divided them into three companies, and lay in ambush in the field. And he looked, and behold, the people came forth out of the city; and he rose up against them and smote them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Judges 9:15; Judges 9:20"> And Abimelech, and the companies that were with him, rushed forward, and stood in the entrance of the gate of the city; and two of the companies ran upon all that were in the fields, and slew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Deuteronomy 29:23; James 2:13"> And Abimelech fought against the city all that day; and he took the city, and slew the people that were in it, and broke down the city, and sowed it with salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 9:4; 1 Kings 8:26"> And all the men of the tower of Shechem heard &#91;that&#93;, and they entered into the stronghold of the house of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.46 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">f</a></sup>god Berith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And it was told Abimelech that all the men of the tower of Shechem had gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 68:14; Proverbs 1:11–12; Judges 7:17–18"> Then Abimelech went up to mount Zalmon, he and all the people that were with him; and Abimelech took an axe in his hand, and cut down a bough from the trees, and took it up and laid it on his shoulder, and said to the people that was with him, What ye have seen me do, make haste, do as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; James 3:16; Judges 9:20"> And all the people likewise cut down every man his bough, and they followed Abimelech, and put &#91;them&#93; to the hold, and burned the hold with fire upon them. And all the men of the tower of Shechem died also, about a thousand men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And Abimelech went to Thebez, and encamped against Thebez, and took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> But there was a strong tower in the midst of the city, and thither fled all the men and women, all the citizens of the city; and they shut it behind them, and went up to the roof of the tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Judges 9:48–49; 2 Kings 14:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> And Abimelech came to the tower, and fought against it, and he drew near to the entrance of the tower to burn it with fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:21; Job 31:3; Jeremiah 49:20"> and a woman cast the upper stone of a handmill on Abimelech's head, and crushed his skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4–5"> Then he called hastily to the young man that carried his armour, and said to him, Draw thy sword, and slay me, that they say not of me, A woman killed him. And his young man thrust him through, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:16; 2 Samuel 20:21–22; 1 Kings 22:35–36"> And when the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, they went every man to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Psalms 94:23; Judges 9:24; Proverbs 5:22"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.56 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God rendered back the wickedness of Abimelech, which he did to his father in slaying his seventy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Judges 9:20; Joshua 6:26; 1 Kings 16:34"> And all the evil of the men of Shechem did<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="9.57 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God render back upon their heads; and upon them came the curse of Jotham the son of Jerubbaal.</span>
