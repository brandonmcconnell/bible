<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 4:12; Jeremiah 44:17"> Rejoice not, Israel, exultingly, as the peoples; for thou hast gone a whoring from thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, thou hast loved harlot's hire upon every corn-floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:9; Micah 6:13–16; Joel 1:3–7"> The floor and the winepress shall not feed them, and the new wine shall fail her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Hosea 8:13; Leviticus 25:23"> They shall not dwell in Jehovah's land; but Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and in Assyria shall they eat that which is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Deuteronomy 26:14; Jeremiah 6:20"> They shall pour out no &#91;offerings of&#93; wine to Jehovah, neither shall their sacrifices be pleasing unto him: they shall be unto them as the bread of mourners; all that eat thereof shall be defiled: for their bread shall be for themselves; it shall not come into the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:3; Jeremiah 5:31; Hosea 2:11"> What will ye do in the day of assembly, and in the day of the feast of Jehovah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 5:6; Isaiah 7:23"> For behold, they are gone away because of destruction: Egypt shall gather them up, Moph shall bury them: their pleasant things of silver, nettles shall possess them; thorns shall be in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:24–25; Micah 7:4; 2 Kings 9:11"> The days of visitation are come; the days of recompence are come: Israel shall know &#91;it&#93;: the prophet is a fool, the inspired man is mad, because of the greatness of thine iniquity, and the great enmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Ezekiel 3:17; Isaiah 62:6"> Is Ephraim a watchman with my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God? &#91;nay&#93; the prophet is a fowler's snare on all his ways, enmity in the house of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:9; Judges 19:16–21; Hosea 8:13"> They have deeply corrupted themselves as in the days of Gibeah. He will remember their iniquity, he will visit their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Hosea 4:14; Numbers 25:1–18"> I found Israel as grapes in the wilderness; as first-ripe fruit on the fig-tree, I saw your fathers at the beginning: they went to Baal-Peor, and separated themselves unto that shame, and became abominations like their lover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:18; Hosea 9:14; Hosea 4:7"> As for Ephraim, their glory shall fly away as a bird, — no birth, no pregnancy, no conception!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Deuteronomy 31:17"> For even should they bring up their children, yet will I bereave them, &#91;that&#93; not a man &#91;remain&#93;: for woe also to them when I shall have departed from them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:1–21; Ezekiel 27:3; Hosea 13:8"> Ephraim, as I saw &#91;him&#93;, was a Tyre planted in a beautiful place; but Ephraim shall bring forth his children to the slayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:29; 1 Corinthians 7:26; Mark 13:17"> Give them, Jehovah — what wilt thou give? — give them a miscarrying womb and dry breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Isaiah 1:23; Amos 5:5"> All their wickedness is in Gilgal; for there I hated them: because of the wickedness of their doings, I will drive them out of my house, I will love them no more: all their princes are rebellious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:11–13; Ezekiel 24:21; Isaiah 5:24"> Ephraim is smitten: their root is dried up, they shall bear no fruit; yea, though they should bring forth, yet will I slay the beloved &#91;fruit&#93; of their womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Deuteronomy 28:64–65; Acts 3:23"> My<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God hath rejected them, because they hearkened not unto him; and they shall be wanderers among the nations.</span>
