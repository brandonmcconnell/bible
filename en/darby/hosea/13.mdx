<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:2; Joshua 3:7; Numbers 13:16"> When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling; he exalted himself in Israel: but he trespassed through Baal, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 44:17–20; 1 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 46:6"> And now they sin more and more, and have made them molten images of their silver, idols according to their own understanding, all of it the work of the craftsmen: they say of them, Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; Psalms 1:4; Psalms 68:2"> Therefore they shall be as the morning cloud, and as the early dew that passeth away, as the chaff driven with the whirlwind out of the threshing-floor, and as the smoke out of the lattice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21–22; Hosea 12:9; Isaiah 43:10–13"> Yet I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God from the land of Egypt, and thou hast known no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God but me; and there is no saviour besides me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:10"> I knew thee in the wilderness, in the land of drought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–15; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Nehemiah 9:35"> According to their pasture, they became full; they became full, and their heart was exalted: therefore have they forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Jeremiah 5:6; Lamentations 3:10"> And I will be unto them as a lion; as a leopard I will lurk for them by the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:8; Isaiah 5:29; Psalms 50:22"> I will meet them as a bear bereaved of her &#91;whelps&#93;, and will rend the covering of their heart, and there will I devour them like a lioness: the beast of the field shall tear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:19; Deuteronomy 33:26"> It is thy destruction, O Israel, that &#91;thou art&#93; against me, against thy help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 8:4; 1 Samuel 8:5–6; Hosea 10:3"> Where then is thy king, that he may save thee in all thy cities? and thy judges of whom thou saidst, Give me a king and princes? —</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:19; 1 Samuel 15:22–23; 1 Kings 14:7–16"> I gave thee a king in mine anger, and took him away in my wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34–35; Job 14:17; Romans 2:5"> The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up; his sin is laid by in store.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6"> The pangs of a woman in travail shall come upon him: he is a son not wise; for at the time of the breaking forth of children, he was not there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 26:19"> I will ransom them from the power of Sheol. I will redeem them from death: where, O death, are thy plagues? where, O Sheol, is thy destruction? Repentance shall be hid from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:12; Ezekiel 17:10; Genesis 41:52"> Though he be fruitful among &#91;his&#93; brethren, an east wind shall come, a wind of Jehovah &#91;that&#93; cometh up from the wilderness, and his spring shall become dry, and his fountain shall be dried up: he shall spoil the treasure of all pleasant vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 15:16; Isaiah 13:16"> Samaria shall bear her guilt; for she hath rebelled against her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.16 Elohim">c</a></sup>God: they shall fall by the sword; their infants shall be dashed in pieces, and their women with child shall be ripped up.</span>
