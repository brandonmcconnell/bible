<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Isaiah 42:8; Psalms 135:13"> — even Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God of hosts, — Jehovah is his memorial.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7"> And Ephraim saith, Nevertheless I am become rich, I have found me out substance; in all my labours they shall find none iniquity in me that were sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40–43; Nehemiah 8:15–17; Hosea 13:4"> But I &#91;that am&#93; Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="12.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God from the land of Egypt will again make thee to dwell in tents, as in the days of the solemn feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Ezekiel 20:49; Jeremiah 19:1"> And I have spoken to the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and by means of the prophets have I used similitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 6:8; Hosea 8:11"> If Gilead is iniquity, surely they are but vanity: they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal; yea, their altars are as heaps in the furrows of the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 27:43; Genesis 28:1–22"> And Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept &#91;sheep&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:50–51; Acts 3:22–23; Psalms 77:20"> And by a prophet Jehovah brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved.</span>
