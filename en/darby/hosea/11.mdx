<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Hosea 8:13; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> He shall not return into the land of Egypt, but the Assyrian shall be his king; for they refused to return &#91;to me&#93;;</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Matthew 23:37; Hosea 6:4"> How shall I give thee over, Ephraim? &#91;how&#93; shall I deliver thee up, Israel? how shall I make thee as Admah? &#91;how&#93; shall I set thee as Zeboim? My heart is turned within me, my repentings are kindled together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:8–9; Micah 7:18–20"> I will not execute the fierceness of mine anger, I will not again destroy Ephraim; for I am<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.9 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and not man, — the Holy One in the midst of thee: and I will not come in anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Joel 3:16; Amos 1:2"> They shall walk after Jehovah; he shall roar like a lion; when he shall roar, then the children shall hasten from the west:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Isaiah 60:8; Zechariah 10:10"> they shall hasten as a bird out of Egypt, and as a dove out of the land of Assyria; and I will cause them to dwell in their houses, saith Jehovah.</span>
