<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:2; 1 Samuel 10:19; Genesis 33:18–19"> And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, and called for the elders of Israel, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers; and they presented themselves before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:15; Ezekiel 16:3; Genesis 31:30"> And Joshua said unto all the people, Thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="24.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of Israel: Your fathers dwelt of old on the other side of the river, Terah, the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor, and they served other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="24.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Genesis 21:2–3; Acts 7:2–3"> And I took your father Abraham from the other side of the river, and led him throughout the land of Canaan, and multiplied his seed and gave him Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Deuteronomy 2:5; Genesis 25:24–26"> And I gave unto Isaac Jacob and Esau; and I gave unto Esau mount Seir, to possess it; and Jacob and his sons went down into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 7:1–12; Psalms 105:26–36"> And I sent Moses and Aaron, and I plagued Egypt, according to that which I did among them; and afterwards I brought you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Hebrews 11:29; Isaiah 63:12–13"> And I brought your fathers out of Egypt, and ye came unto the sea; and the Egyptians pursued after your fathers with chariots and horsemen unto the Red sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Exodus 14:10; Exodus 14:20"> Then they cried to Jehovah, and he put darkness between you and the Egyptians, and brought the sea upon them, and covered them; and your eyes saw what I did in Egypt; and ye dwelt in the wilderness a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Psalms 135:10–11; Nehemiah 9:22"> And I brought you into the land of the Amorites, who dwelt beyond the Jordan, and they fought with you, and I gave them into your hand, and ye took possession of their land, and I destroyed them from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Micah 6:5; Numbers 22:2"> And Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and warred against Israel, and sent and called Balaam the son of Beor to curse you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 24:5–10; Isaiah 54:17"> but I would not hearken unto Balaam, and he blessed you expressly, and I delivered you out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 4:10–12; Joshua 3:14–17; Psalms 114:5"> And ye went over the Jordan, and came unto Jericho, and the men of Jericho fought against you, the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Girgashites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; and I delivered them into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Exodus 23:28; Psalms 44:3–6"> And I sent the hornet before you, which drove them out from before you, &#91;as&#93; the two kings of the Amorites; not with thy sword, nor with thy bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Deuteronomy 8:7; Joshua 21:45"> And I have given you a land for which ye did not labour, and cities which ye built not, and ye dwell in them; of vineyards and oliveyards which ye planted not do ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Deuteronomy 10:12; Genesis 35:2"> And now fear Jehovah and serve him in perfectness and in truth; and put away the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="24.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods which your fathers served on the other side of the river, and in Egypt; and serve Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; John 6:67–68; Ruth 1:15–16"> And if it seem evil unto you to serve Jehovah, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="24.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods whom your fathers that were on the other side of the river served, or the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="24.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods of the Amorite, in whose land ye dwell; but as for me and my house, we will serve Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38–39; 1 Samuel 12:23; Romans 6:2"> And the people answered and said, Far be it from us that we should forsake Jehovah, to serve other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="24.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 63:7–14; Isaiah 46:4"> for Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="24.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, he it is that brought us up and our fathers out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage, and who did those great signs before our eyes, and preserved us in all the way wherein we went, and among all the peoples through whom we passed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 4:2; Exodus 10:2; Psalms 116:16"> And Jehovah drove out from before us all the peoples, and the Amorites the inhabitants of the land: so therefore we will serve Jehovah, for he is our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="24.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 5:16; Exodus 23:21; Psalms 99:9"> And Joshua said to the people, Ye cannot serve Jehovah, for he is a holy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="24.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God; he is a jealous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="24.19 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">k</a></sup>God; he will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Ezekiel 18:24; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> If ye forsake Jehovah, and serve strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="24.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>gods, then he will turn and do you hurt, and consume you, after that he hath done you good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 44:5; Deuteronomy 5:27–28"> And the people said to Joshua, No; but we will serve Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:173; Psalms 119:11; Job 15:6"> And Joshua said unto the people, Ye are witnesses against yourselves that ye have chosen you Jehovah, to serve him. And they said, &#91;We are&#93; witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16–18; Joshua 24:14; Hebrews 12:28–29"> Now therefore put away the strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="24.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>gods that are among you, and incline your heart unto Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="24.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Deuteronomy 5:27–29"> And the people said unto Joshua, Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="24.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God will we serve, and to his voice will we hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:38; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Exodus 15:25"> So Joshua made a covenant with the people that day, and set them a statute and an ordinance in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Genesis 35:4; Joshua 4:20–24"> And Joshua wrote these words in the book of the law of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="24.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God, and took a great stone, and set it up there under the oak that was by the sanctuary of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 22:34; Joshua 22:27–28"> And Joshua said unto all the people, Behold, this stone shall be a witness unto us, for it hath heard all the words of Jehovah which he spoke unto us; and it shall be a witness against you, lest ye deny your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="24.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 2:6"> And Joshua dismissed the people, every man to his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:8; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 34:5"> And it came to pass after these things, that Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of Jehovah, died, a hundred and ten years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 23:30"> And they buried him in the border of his inheritance in Timnath-serah, which is in mount Ephraim, on the north side of the mountain of Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 2:7; Deuteronomy 31:13; Deuteronomy 11:2"> And Israel served Jehovah all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders whose days were prolonged after Joshua, and who had known all the works of Jehovah, which he had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 50:25; Genesis 33:19; Exodus 13:19"> And the bones of Joseph, which the children of Israel had brought up out of Egypt, buried they in Shechem in the portion of the field which Jacob had bought of the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for a hundred kesitahs; and it became the inheritance of the children of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 22:13; Exodus 6:25; Isaiah 57:1–2"> And Eleazar the son of Aaron died; and they buried him in the hill of Phinehas his son, which had been given him in mount Ephraim.</span>
