<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 23:23"> And it came to pass when all the kings who were on this side the Jordan, in the hill-country, and in the lowland, and along all the coast of the great sea as far as opposite to Lebanon, the Hittite, and the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite, heard &#91;of it&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Joel 3:9–13; Psalms 2:1–2"> that they assembled together, to fight with Joshua and with Israel, with one accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 21:1–2"> And when the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and to Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Matthew 10:16; Mark 2:22"> then they also acted with craft, and they went prepared as on a journey, and took old sacks upon their asses, and wine-flasks, old and rent and tied up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 29:5"> and old and patched sandals upon their feet, and old garments upon them; and all the bread of their provision was dry &#91;and&#93; mouldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; 2 Kings 20:14; Joshua 9:9"> And they went to Joshua unto the camp at Gilgal, and said to him, and to the men of Israel, From a far country are we come; and now make a covenant with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 11:19; Judges 2:2; Exodus 34:12"> And the men of Israel said to the Hivite, Perhaps thou dwellest in the midst of us, and how should I make a covenant with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:11; 2 Kings 10:5; Joshua 9:11"> And they said unto Joshua, We are thy servants. And Joshua said to them, Who are ye? and from whence come ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15; Joshua 9:24; Isaiah 55:5"> And they said to him, From a very far country are thy servants come, because of the name of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God; for we have heard the fame of him, and all that he did in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 12:4"> and all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites that were beyond Jordan, to Sihon the king of Heshbon, and to Og the king of Bashan, who was at Ashtaroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 9:8; Esther 8:17; Joshua 1:11"> And our elders and all the inhabitants of our country spoke to us, saying, Take victuals in your hand for the way, and go to meet them, and say to them, We are your servants, and now make a covenant with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 9:4–5"> This our bread we took warm for our provision out of our houses on the day we came forth to go unto you; and now, behold, it is dry, and is become mouldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And these flasks of wine which we filled new, behold, they are rent; and these our garments and our sandals are become old by reason of the very long journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Numbers 27:21; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14"> And the men took of their victuals, but they did not inquire at the mouth of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Deuteronomy 20:10–11; Joshua 11:19"> And Joshua made peace with them, and made a covenant with them, to let them live; and the princes of the assembly swore unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 12:19"> And it came to pass at the end of three days after they had made a covenant with them, that they heard that they were their neighbours, and that they dwelt in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:25; Joshua 18:25–28; Joshua 18:14"> And the children of Israel journeyed, and came to their cities on the third day; and their cities were Gibeon, and Chephirah, and Beeroth, and Kirjath-jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Samuel 21:7; Ecclesiastes 5:6"> And the children of Israel did not smite them, because the princes of the assembly had sworn unto them by Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of Israel. Then all the assembly murmured against the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Joshua 9:20; Ecclesiastes 9:2"> And all the princes said to all the assembly, We have sworn unto them by Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel, and now we may not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 20:25; 1 Timothy 1:10"> This we will do to them, and let them live, lest wrath come upon us, because of the oath which we swore unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:11; Joshua 9:27; Joshua 9:23"> And the princes said to them, Let them live. And they were hewers of wood and drawers of water for all the assembly; as the princes had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 9:16; Genesis 3:13–14"> And Joshua called for them, and he spoke to them, saying, Why have ye deceived us, saying, We are very far from you; whereas ye dwell in our midst?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 9:21; Genesis 9:25–26; Joshua 9:27"> And now ye are cursed, and ye shall never cease to be bondmen, and hewers of wood, and drawers of water for the house of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15–17; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; Exodus 23:31–33"> And they answered Joshua and said, Because it was certainly told thy servants how that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God commanded his servant Moses to give you all the land, and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land from before you; and we feared greatly for our lives because of you, and did this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 16:6; Judges 10:15; Jeremiah 26:14"> And now behold, we are in thy hand: as it is good and right in thine eyes to do to us, do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he did so to them, and delivered them out of the hand of the children of Israel, and they did not slay them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5"> And Joshua made them that day hewers of wood and drawers of water for the assembly, and for the altar of Jehovah, to this day, in the place which he should choose.</span>
