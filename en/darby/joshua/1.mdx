<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Deuteronomy 1:38; Deuteronomy 31:23"> And it came to pass after the death of Moses the servant of Jehovah, that Jehovah spoke to Joshua the son of Nun, Moses' attendant, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7; Numbers 27:16–21"> Moses my servant is dead; and now, rise up, go over this Jordan, thou and all this people, into the land which I give unto them, to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 14:9; Titus 1:2"> Every place whereon the sole of your foot shall tread have I given to you, as I said unto Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 3:25"> From the wilderness and this Lebanon to the great river, the river Euphrates, the whole land of the Hittites, to the great sea, toward the going down of the sun, shall be your border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Matthew 28:20"> None shall be able to stand before thee all the days of thy life: as I was with Moses, so will I be with thee; I will not leave thee, neither will I forsake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9"> Be strong and courageous, for thou shalt cause this people to inherit the land which I have sworn unto their fathers to give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:8"> Only be strong and very courageous, that thou mayest take heed to do according to all the law that Moses my servant commanded thee. Turn not from it to the right or to the left, that thou mayest prosper whithersoever thou goest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:15; Psalms 19:14; Colossians 3:16"> This book of the law shall not depart from thy mouth; and thou shalt meditate upon it day and night, that thou mayest take heed to do according to all that is written therein; for then shalt thou have good success in thy ways, and then shalt thou prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Jeremiah 1:7–8; Joshua 1:6–7"> Have I not commanded thee: Be strong and courageous? Be not afraid, neither be dismayed; for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Joshua commanded the officers of the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1; Hosea 6:2; 2 Kings 20:5"> Go through the midst of the camp, and command the people, saying, Prepare yourselves victuals, for in three days ye shall pass over this Jordan, that ye may enter in to take possession of the land which Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God giveth you to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–22"> And to the Reubenites and to the Gadites and to half the tribe of Manasseh spoke Joshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–28; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 22:1–4"> Remember the word that Moses the servant of Jehovah commanded you, saying, Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God has given you rest and has given you this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Exodus 13:18; Revelation 17:4"> Your wives, your little ones, and your cattle shall abide in the land that Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan, but ye, all the valiant men, shall go over in array before your brethren and help them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:4; Numbers 32:17–22; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> until Jehovah give your brethren rest as to you, and they also take possession of the land which Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God giveth them; then shall ye return into the land of your possession and possess it, which Moses the servant of Jehovah gave you on this side of the Jordan toward the sun-rising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Deuteronomy 5:27; 1 Peter 2:13–15"> And they answered Joshua, saying, All that thou hast commanded us will we do, and whither thou shalt send us will we go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; 1 Kings 1:37; Joshua 1:9"> According as we hearkened to Moses in all things, so will we hearken to thee; only may Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God be with thee as he was with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9; Ezra 10:4"> Every one that is rebellious against thy commandment and hearkeneth not to thy words in everything that thou commandest us, shall be put to death. Only be strong and courageous.</span>
