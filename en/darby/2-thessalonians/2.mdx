<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Mark 13:27; Matthew 24:31"> Now we beg you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1–2; Mark 13:7; Matthew 24:4–6"> that ye be not soon shaken in mind, nor troubled, neither by spirit, nor by word, nor by letter, as &#91;if it were&#93; by us, as that the day of the Lord is present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:4–6; 1 John 2:18; Ephesians 5:6"> Let not any one deceive you in any manner, because &#91;it will not be&#93; unless the apostasy have first come, and the man of sin have been revealed, the son of perdition;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 13:6–7"> who opposes and exalts himself on high against all called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, or object of veneration; so that he himself sits down in the temple of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, shewing himself that he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 1:15; Galatians 5:21; Matthew 16:9"> Do ye not remember that, being yet with you, I said these things to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And now ye know that which restrains, that he should be revealed in his own time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:3; Revelation 17:7; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> For the mystery of lawlessness already works; only &#91;there is&#93; he who restrains now until he be gone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; Revelation 19:20–21; Isaiah 11:4"> and then the lawless one shall be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus shall consume with the breath of his mouth, and shall annul by the appearing of his coming;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; Ephesians 2:2"> whose coming is according to the working of Satan in all power and signs and wonders of falsehood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Proverbs 1:7; 2 Corinthians 4:2–3"> and in all deceit of unrighteousness to them that perish, because they have not received the love of the truth that they might be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:28; John 12:39–43; 1 Timothy 4:1"> And for this reason<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God sends to them a working of error, that they should believe what is false,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 8:7–8; Mark 16:16"> that all might be judged who have not believed the truth, but have found pleasure in unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 1 Thessalonians 1:4"> But we ought to give thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">e</a></sup>God always for you, brethren beloved of &#91;the&#93; Lord, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">f</a></sup>God has chosen you from &#91;the&#93; beginning to salvation in sanctification of &#91;the&#93; Spirit and belief of &#91;the&#93; truth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 1:5"> whereto he has called you by our glad tidings, to &#91;the&#93; obtaining of &#91;the&#93; glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> So then, brethren, stand firm, and hold fast the instructions which ye have been taught, whether by word or by our letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:2–5; John 3:16; 1 Peter 1:3–8"> But our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.16 Elohim">g</a></sup>God and Father, who has loved us, and given &#91;us&#93; eternal consolation and good hope by grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> encourage your hearts, and establish you in every good work and word.</span>
