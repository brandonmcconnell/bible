<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:19; Acts 16:1; 1 Thessalonians 1:1–10"> Paul and Silvanus and Timotheus to the assembly of Thessalonians in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God our Father and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 1:8"> Grace to you, and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God our Father, and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; 2 Peter 1:5–10; Proverbs 4:18"> We ought to thank<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God always for you, brethren, even as it is meet, because your faith increases exceedingly, and the love of each one of you all towards one another abounds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; James 5:7–8; Hebrews 12:1–3"> so that we ourselves make our boast in you in the assemblies of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God for your endurance and faith in all your persecutions and tribulations, which ye are sustaining;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:28; Acts 14:22; Luke 20:35"> a manifest token of the righteous judgment of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.5 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, to the end that ye should be counted worthy of the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.5 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, for the sake of which ye also suffer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Colossians 3:25; Psalms 94:20–23"> if at least &#91;it is a&#93; righteous thing with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">g</a></sup>God to render tribulation to those that trouble you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Matthew 16:27; Jude 1:14–15"> and to you that are troubled repose with us, at the revelation of the Lord Jesus from heaven, with &#91;the&#93; angels of his power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 2 Peter 3:7; Hebrews 10:27"> in flaming fire taking vengeance on those who know not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, and those who do not obey the glad tidings of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 14:10–11; Matthew 25:46"> who shall pay the penalty &#91;of&#93; everlasting destruction from &#91;the&#93; presence of the Lord, and from the glory of his might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:18; 1 Corinthians 3:13; John 17:10"> when he shall have come to be glorified in his saints, and wondered at in all that have believed, (for our testimony to you has been believed,) in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:9–13; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> To which end we also pray always for you, that our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.11 Elohim">i</a></sup>God may count you worthy of the calling, and fulfil all &#91;the&#93; good pleasure of &#91;his&#93; goodness and &#91;the&#93; work of faith with power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 4:14; John 17:10"> so that the name of our Lord Jesus &#91;Christ&#93; may be glorified in you and ye in him, according to the grace of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.12 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, and of &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
