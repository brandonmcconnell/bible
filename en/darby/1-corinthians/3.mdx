<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:13; 1 Corinthians 2:14–15; Ephesians 4:13–14"> And I, brethren, have not been able to speak to you as to spiritual, but as to fleshly; as to babes in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:11–14; John 16:12; 1 Peter 2:2"> I have given you milk to drink, not meat, for ye have not yet been able, nor indeed are ye yet able;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:16; Galatians 5:15; Titus 3:3"> for ye are yet carnal. For whereas &#91;there are&#93; among you emulation and strife, are ye not carnal, and walk according to man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 4:6"> For when one says, I am of Paul, and another, I of Apollos, are ye not men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 1 Peter 4:10"> Who then is Apollos, and who Paul? Ministering servants, through whom ye have believed, and as the Lord has given to each.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; 2 Corinthians 10:14–15"> I have planted; Apollos watered; but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God has given the increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:5; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> So that neither the planter is anything, nor the waterer; but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God the giver of the increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Matthew 16:27; 2 John 1:8"> But the planter and the waterer are one; but each shall receive his own reward according to his own labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> For we are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God's fellow-workmen; ye are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's husbandry,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God's building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:11; Ephesians 2:20; Romans 15:20"> According to the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.10 Elohim">f</a></sup>God which has been given to me, as a wise architect, I have laid the foundation, but another builds upon it. But let each see how he builds upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; 2 Timothy 2:19"> For other foundation can no man lay besides that which &#91;is&#93; laid, which is Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:7; 2 Timothy 2:20"> Now if any one build upon &#91;this&#93; foundation, gold, silver, precious stones, wood, grass, straw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; 1 Corinthians 3:14–15; Revelation 20:12"> the work of each shall be made manifest; for the day shall declare &#91;it&#93;, because it is revealed in fire; and the fire shall try the work of each what it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:8; Matthew 25:21–23; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> If the work of any one which he has built upon &#91;the foundation&#93; shall abide, he shall receive a reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13; Jude 1:23"> If the work of any one shall be consumed, he shall suffer loss, but he shall be saved, but so as through &#91;the&#93; fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Do ye not know that ye are &#91;the&#93; temple of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.16 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, and &#91;that&#93; the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.16 Elohim">h</a></sup>God dwells in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> If any one corrupt the temple of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">i</a></sup>God, him shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">j</a></sup>God destroy; for the temple of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">k</a></sup>God is holy, and such are ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; Galatians 6:3"> Let no one deceive himself: if any one thinks himself to be wise among you in this world, let him become foolish, that he may be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="3.19 Elohim">l</a></sup>God; for it is written, He who takes the wise in their craftiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; Colossians 2:8; Romans 1:21"> And again, &#91;The&#93; Lord knows the reasonings of the wise that they are vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Romans 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> So that let no one boast in men; for all things are yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 1:21; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> Whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or &#91;the&#93; world, or life, or death, or things present, or things coming, all are yours;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 11:3; John 17:9–10"> and ye &#91;are&#93; Christ's, and Christ &#91;is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="3.23 Elohim">m</a></sup>God's.</span>
