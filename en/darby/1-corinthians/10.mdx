<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 14:29; Nehemiah 9:11–12; Psalms 78:13–14"> For I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–3; 1 Corinthians 1:13–16; Exodus 14:31"> and all were baptised unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Nehemiah 9:15; Exodus 16:35"> and all ate the same spiritual food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; John 7:37; Isaiah 48:21"> and all drank the same spiritual drink, for they drank of a spiritual rock which followed &#91;them&#93;: (now the rock was the Christ;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 3:17; Jude 1:5; Numbers 14:37"> yet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God was not pleased with the most of them, for they were strewed in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> But these things happened &#91;as&#93; types of us, that we should not be lusters after evil things, as they also lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalms 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> Neither be ye idolaters, as some of them; as it is written, The people sat down to eat and to drink, and rose up to play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalms 106:29"> Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them committed fornication, and fell in one day three and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; Psalms 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> Neither let us tempt the Christ, as some of them tempted, and perished by serpents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> Neither murmur ye, as some of them murmured, and perished by the destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> Now all these things happened to them &#91;as&#93; types, and have been written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the ages are come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> So that let him that thinks that he stands take heed lest he fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> No temptation has taken you but such as is according to man's nature; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.13 Elohim">b</a></sup>God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above what ye are able &#91;to bear&#93;, but will with the temptation make the issue also, so that &#91;ye&#93; should be able to bear &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> Wherefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I speak as to intelligent &#91;persons&#93;: do ye judge what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not &#91;the&#93; communion of the blood of the Christ? The bread which we break, is it not &#91;the&#93; communion of the body of the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Because we, &#91;being&#93; many, are one loaf, one body; for we all partake of that one loaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> See Israel according to flesh: are not they who eat the sacrifices in communion with the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> What then do I say? that what is sacrificed to an idol is anything, or that an idol is anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalms 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> But that what &#91;the nations&#93; sacrifice they sacrifice to demons, and not to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.20 Elohim">c</a></sup>God. Now I do not wish you to be in communion with demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> Ye cannot drink &#91;the&#93; Lord's cup, and &#91;the&#93; cup of demons: ye cannot partake of &#91;the&#93; Lord's table, and of &#91;the&#93; table of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? are we stronger than he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> All things are lawful, but all are not profitable; all things are lawful, but all do not edify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let no one seek his own &#91;advantage&#93;, but that of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Everything sold in the shambles eat, making no inquiry for conscience sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalms 50:12"> For the earth &#91;is&#93; the Lord's and its fulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> But if any one of the unbelievers invite you, and ye are minded to go, all that is set before you eat, making no inquiry for conscience sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if any one say to you, This is offered to holy purposes, do not eat, for his sake that pointed it out, and conscience sake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> but conscience, I mean, not thine own, but that of the other: for why is my liberty judged by another conscience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> If I partake with thanksgiving, why am I spoken evil of for what I give thanks for?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatever ye do, do all things to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.31 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> Give no occasion to stumbling, whether to Jews, or Greeks, or the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.32 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> Even as I also please all in all things; not seeking my own profit, but that of the many, that they may be saved.</span>
