<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> But I make known to you, brethren, the glad tidings which I announced to you, which also ye received, in which also ye stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> by which also ye are saved, (if ye hold fast the word which I announced to you as the glad tidings,) unless indeed ye have believed in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> For I delivered to you, in the first place, what also I had received, that Christ died for our sins, according to the scriptures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; Psalms 16:10–11"> and that he was buried; and that he was raised the third day, according to the scriptures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> Then he appeared to above five hundred brethren at once, of whom the most remain until now, but some also have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> Then he appeared to James; then to all the apostles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> and last of all, as to an abortion, he appeared to me also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> For I am the least of the apostles, who am not fit to be called apostle, because I have persecuted the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.9 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> But by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.10 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's grace I am what I am; and his grace, which &#91;was&#93; towards me, has not been vain; but I have laboured more abundantly than they all, but not I, but the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God which &#91;was&#93; with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> Whether, therefore, I or they, thus we preach, and thus ye have believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> Now if Christ is preached that he is raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead, how say some among you that there is not a resurrection of &#91;those that are&#93; dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> But if there is not a resurrection of &#91;those that are&#93; dead, neither is Christ raised:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> but if Christ is not raised, then, indeed, vain also &#91;is&#93; our preaching, and vain also your faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> And we are found also false witnesses of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.15 Elohim">d</a></sup>God; for we have witnessed concerning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.15 Elohim">e</a></sup>God that he raised the Christ, whom he has not raised if indeed &#91;those that are&#93; dead are not raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if &#91;those that are&#93; dead are not raised, neither is Christ raised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> but if Christ be not raised, your faith &#91;is&#93; vain; ye are yet in your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> Then indeed also those who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are &#91;the&#93; most miserable of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> (But now Christ is raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead, first-fruits of those fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> For since by man &#91;came&#93; death, by man also resurrection of &#91;those that are&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> For as in the Adam all die, thus also in the Christ all shall be made alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> But each in his own rank: &#91;the&#93; first-fruits, Christ; then those that are the Christ's at his coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> Then the end, when he gives up the kingdom to him &#91;who is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.24 Elohim">f</a></sup>God and Father; when he shall have annulled all rule and all authority and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> For he must reign until he put all enemies under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> &#91;The&#93; last enemy &#91;that&#93; is annulled &#91;is&#93; death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> For he has put all things in subjection under his feet. But when he says that all things are put in subjection, &#91;it is&#93; evident that &#91;it is&#93; except him who put all things in subjection to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> But when all things shall have been brought into subjection to him, then the Son also himself shall be placed in subjection to him who put all things in subjection to him, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.28 Elohim">g</a></sup>God may be all in all.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> Since what shall the baptised for the dead do if &#91;those that are&#93; dead rise not at all? why also are they baptised for them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> Why do we also endanger ourselves every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> Daily I die, by your boasting which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> If, &#91;to speak&#93; after the manner of man, I have fought with beasts in Ephesus, what is the profit to me if &#91;those that are&#93; dead do not rise? let us eat and drink; for to-morrow we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Awake up righteously, and sin not; for some are ignorant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15.34 Elohim">h</a></sup>God: I speak to you as a matter of shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> But some one will say, How are the dead raised? and with what body do they come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> Fool; what thou sowest is not quickened unless it die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And what thou sowest, thou sowest not the body that shall be, but a bare grain: it may be of wheat, or some one of the rest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="15.38 Elohim">i</a></sup>God gives to it a body as he has pleased, and to each of the seeds its own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> Every flesh &#91;is&#93; not the same flesh, but one &#91;is&#93; of men, and another flesh of beasts, and another &#91;flesh&#93; of birds, and another of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And &#91;there are&#93; heavenly bodies, and earthly bodies: but different is the glory of the heavenly, different that of the earthly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; Psalms 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> one &#91;the&#93; sun's glory, and another &#91;the&#93; moon's glory, and another &#91;the&#93; stars' glory; for star differs from star in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> Thus also &#91;is&#93; the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruptibility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> It is sown in dishonour, it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body: if there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual &#91;one&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> Thus also it is written, The first man Adam became a living soul; the last Adam a quickening spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> But that which is spiritual &#91;was&#93; not first, but that which is natural, then that which is spiritual:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> the first man out of &#91;the&#93; earth, made of dust; the second man, out of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> Such as he made of dust, such also those made of dust; and such as the heavenly &#91;one&#93;, such also the heavenly &#91;ones&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> And as we have borne the image of the &#91;one&#93; made of dust, we shall bear also the image of the heavenly &#91;one&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> But this I say, brethren, that flesh and blood cannot inherit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="15.50 Elohim">j</a></sup>God's kingdom, nor does corruption inherit incorruptibility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Behold, I tell you a mystery: We shall not all fall asleep, but we shall all be changed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in an instant, in &#91;the&#93; twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet; for the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> For this corruptible must needs put on incorruptibility, and this mortal put on immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> But when this corruptible shall have put on incorruptibility, and this mortal shall have put on immortality, then shall come to pass the word written: Death has been swallowed up in victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Psalms 89:48; Acts 2:27"> Where, O death, &#91;is&#93; thy sting? where, O death, thy victory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> Now the sting of death &#91;is&#93; sin, and the power of sin the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> but thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="15.57 Elohim">k</a></sup>God, who gives us the victory by our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> So then, my beloved brethren, be firm, immovable, abounding always in the work of the Lord, knowing that your toil is not in vain in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
