<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Follow after love, and be emulous of spiritual &#91;manifestations&#93;, but rather that ye may prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 19:6; Acts 10:46"> For he that speaks with a tongue does not speak to men but to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God: for no one hears; but in spirit he speaks mysteries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:12; 1 Timothy 4:13; Jude 1:20"> But he that prophesies speaks to men &#91;in&#93; edification, and encouragement, and consolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:22; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Mark 16:17"> He that speaks with a tongue edifies himself; but he that prophesies edifies &#91;the&#93; assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28–30; 1 Corinthians 12:10; Mark 16:17"> Now I desire that ye should all speak with tongues, but rather that ye should prophesy. But greater is he that prophesies than he that speaks with tongues, unless he interpret, that the assembly may receive edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; Romans 6:17; 2 Timothy 3:16"> And now, brethren, if I come to you speaking with tongues, what shall I profit you, unless I shall speak to you either in revelation, or in knowledge, or in prophecy, or in teaching?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:8; Luke 7:32; 1 Corinthians 13:1"> Even lifeless things giving a sound, whether pipe or harp, if they give not distinction to the sounds, how shall it be known what is piped or harped?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> For also, if the trumpet give an uncertain sound, who shall prepare himself for war?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26"> Thus also ye with the tongue, unless ye give a distinct speech, how shall it be known what is spoken? for ye will be speaking to the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are, it may be, so many kinds of voices in the world, and none of undistinguishable sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:2; Romans 1:14; Colossians 3:11"> If therefore I do not know the power of the sound, I shall be to him that speaks a barbarian, and he that speaks a barbarian for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> Thus ye also, since ye are desirous of spirits, seek that ye may abound for the edification of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:24; 1 Corinthians 12:30; John 14:13–14"> Wherefore let him that speaks with a tongue pray that he may interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:2; 1 Corinthians 14:15–16; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> For if I pray with a tongue, my spirit prays, but my understanding is unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; John 4:23–24; Ephesians 6:18"> What is it then? I will pray with the spirit, but I will pray also with the understanding; I will sing with the spirit, but I will sing also with the understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalms 106:48; Revelation 5:14"> Since otherwise, if thou blessest with &#91;the&#93; spirit, how shall he who fills the place of the simple &#91;Christian&#93; say Amen, at thy giving of thanks, since he does not know what thou sayest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:4; 1 Corinthians 14:6"> For thou indeed givest thanks well, but the other is not edified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4–6; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> I thank<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.18 Elohim">b</a></sup>God I speak in a tongue more than all of you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21–22; 1 Corinthians 14:4"> but in &#91;the&#93; assembly I desire to speak five words with my understanding, that I may instruct others also, &#91;rather&#93; than ten thousand words in a tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:2; 1 Corinthians 13:11; Romans 16:19"> Brethren, be not children in &#91;your&#93; minds, but in malice be babes; but in &#91;your&#93; minds be grown &#91;men&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> It is written in the law, By people of other tongues, and by strange lips, will I speak to this people; and neither thus will they hear me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:6–12; Mark 16:17; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> So that tongues are for a sign, not to those who believe, but to unbelievers; but prophecy, not to unbelievers, but to those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 2:13; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 11:18"> If therefore the whole assembly come together in one place, and all speak with tongues, and simple &#91;persons&#93; enter in, or unbelievers, will not they say ye are mad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:37; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But if all prophesy, and some unbeliever or simple &#91;person&#93; come in, he is convicted of all, he is judged of all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 72:11"> the secrets of his heart are manifested; and thus, falling upon &#91;his&#93; face, he will do homage to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, reporting that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.25 Elohim">d</a></sup>God is indeed amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 12:7–10"> What is it then, brethren? whenever ye come together, each &#91;of you&#93; has a psalm, has a teaching, has a tongue, has a revelation, has an interpretation. Let all things be done to edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26"> If any one speak with a tongue, &#91;let it be&#93; two, or at the most three, and separately, and let one interpret;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> but if there be no interpreter, let him be silent in &#91;the&#93; assembly, and let him speak to himself and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.28 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21; 1 John 4:1–3"> And let two or three prophets speak, and let the others judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> But if there be a revelation to another sitting &#91;there&#93;, let the first be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> For ye can all prophesy one by one, that all may learn and all be encouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:19–20; 1 John 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> And spirits of prophets are subject to prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 3:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.33 Elohim">f</a></sup>God is not &#91;a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="14.33 Elohim">g</a></sup>God&#93; of disorder but of peace, as in all the assemblies of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> Let &#91;your&#93; women be silent in the assemblies, for it is not permitted to them to speak; but to be in subjection, as the law also says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; 1 Peter 3:7; Ephesians 5:25–27"> But if they wish to learn anything, let them ask their own husbands at home; for it is a shame for a woman to speak in assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; Acts 17:15"> Did the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="14.36 Elohim">h</a></sup>God go out from you, or did it come to you only?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:7; 2 Peter 3:2"> If any one thinks himself to be a prophet or spiritual, let him recognise the things that I write to you, that it is &#91;the&#93; Lord's commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; Revelation 22:11–12"> But if any be ignorant, let him be ignorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:31; 1 Corinthians 14:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:20"> So that, brethren, desire to prophesy, and do not forbid the speaking with tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; Titus 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33"> But let all things be done comelily and with order.</span>
