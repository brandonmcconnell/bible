<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 14:37; 1 Corinthians 14:1–18"> But concerning spiritual &#91;manifestations&#93;, brethren, I do not wish you to be ignorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:9; Psalms 115:5"> Ye know that when ye were &#91;of the&#93; nations &#91;ye were&#93; led away to dumb idols, in whatever way ye might be led.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:2–3; Matthew 16:16–17; Romans 10:9"> I give you therefore to know, that no one, speaking in &#91;the power of the&#93; Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, says, Curse &#91;on&#93; Jesus; and no one can say, Lord Jesus, unless in &#91;the power of the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:4–6"> But there are distinctions of gifts, but the same Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Romans 12:6–8; Romans 14:8–9"> and there are distinctions of services, and the same Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 12:11; John 5:17"> and there are distinctions of operations, but the same<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God who operates all things in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> But to each the manifestation of the Spirit is given for profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:6–10; Psalms 143:10"> For to one, by the Spirit, is given &#91;the&#93; word of wisdom; and to another &#91;the&#93; word of knowledge, according to the same Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 5:14–15; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Ephesians 2:8"> and to a different one faith, in &#91;the power of&#93; the same Spirit; and to another gifts of healing in &#91;the power of&#93; the same Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 John 4:1; Mark 16:17"> and to another operations of miracles; and to another prophecy; and to another discerning of spirits; and to a different one kinds of tongues; and to another interpretation of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Hebrews 2:4; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> But all these things operates the one and the same Spirit, dividing to each in particular according as he pleases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For even as the body is one and has many members, but all the members of the body, being many, are one body, so also &#91;is&#93; the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> For also in &#91;the power of&#93; one Spirit we have all been baptised into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether bondmen or free, and have all been given to drink of one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> For also the body is not one member but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> If the foot say, Because I am not a hand I am not of the body, is it on account of this not indeed of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> And if the ear say, Because I am not an eye I am not of the body, is it on account of this not indeed of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; Psalms 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body &#91;were&#93; an eye, where the hearing? if all hearing, where the smelling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> But now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.18 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has set the members, each one of them in the body, according as it has pleased &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But if all were one member, where the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> But now the members &#91;are&#93; many, and the body one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> The eye cannot say to the hand, I have not need of thee; or again, the head to the feet, I have not need of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> But much rather, the members of the body which seem to be weaker are necessary;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> and those &#91;parts&#93; of the body which we esteem to be the more void of honour, these we clothe with more abundant honour; and our uncomely &#91;parts&#93; have more abundant comeliness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> but our comely &#91;parts&#93; have not need. But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.24 Elohim">d</a></sup>God has tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to &#91;the part&#93; that lacked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> that there might be no division in the body, but that the members might have the same concern one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> And if one member suffer, all the members suffer with &#91;it&#93;; and if one member be glorified, all the members rejoice with &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> Now ye are Christ's body, and members in particular.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="12.28 Elohim">e</a></sup>God has set certain in the assembly: first, apostles; secondly, prophets; thirdly, teachers; then miraculous powers; then gifts of healings; helps; governments; kinds of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> &#91;Are&#93; all apostles? &#91;are&#93; all prophets? &#91;are&#93; all teachers? &#91;are&#93; all &#91;in possession of&#93; miraculous powers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> have all gifts of healings? do all speak with tongues? do all interpret?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> But desire earnestly the greater gifts, and yet shew I unto you a way of more surpassing excellence.</span>
