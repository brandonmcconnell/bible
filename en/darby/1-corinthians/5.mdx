<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Revelation 2:21; Colossians 3:5"> It is universally reported &#91;that there is&#93; fornication among you, and such fornication as &#91;is&#93; not even among the nations, so that one should have his father's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 7:7–11; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> And ye are puffed up, and ye have not rather mourned, in order that he that has done this deed might be taken away out of the midst of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 13:2"> For I, &#91;as&#93; absent in body but present in spirit, have already judged as present,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:23; Colossians 3:17; Matthew 16:19"> &#91;to deliver,&#93; in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ (ye and my spirit being gathered together, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ), him that has so wrought this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15; Galatians 6:1–2"> to deliver him, &#91;I say,&#93; &#91;being&#93; such, to Satan for destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 15:33; James 4:16"> Your boasting &#91;is&#93; not good. Do ye not know that a little leaven leavens the whole lump?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; Ephesians 4:22; Colossians 3:5–9"> Purge out the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, according as ye are unleavened. For also our passover, Christ, has been sacrificed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 12:15"> so that let us celebrate the feast, not with old leaven, nor with leaven of malice and wickedness, but with unleavened &#91;bread&#93; of sincerity and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Corinthians 6:14"> I have written to you in the epistle not to mix with fornicators;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; John 17:15–16; Matthew 5:14–16"> not altogether with the fornicators of this world, or with the avaricious and rapacious, or idolaters, since &#91;then&#93; ye should go out of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But now I have written to you, if any one called brother be fornicator, or avaricious, or idolater, or abusive, or a drunkard, or rapacious, not to mix with &#91;him&#93;; with such a one not even to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:11; 1 Timothy 3:7; Luke 12:14"> For what have I &#91;to do&#93; with judging those outside also? ye, do not ye judge them that are within?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 21:21"> But those without<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.13 Elohim">a</a></sup>God judges. Remove the wicked person from amongst yourselves.</span>
