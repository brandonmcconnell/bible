<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; Romans 14:3"> But concerning things sacrificed to idols, we know, (for we all have knowledge: knowledge puffs up, but love edifies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> But if any one love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, he is known of him):</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Isaiah 41:24; 1 Corinthians 10:19–20"> — concerning then the eating of things sacrificed to idols, we know that an idol &#91;is&#93; nothing in &#91;the&#93; world, and that there &#91;is&#93; no other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God save one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; Galatians 4:8"> For and if indeed there are &#91;those&#93; called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>gods, whether in heaven or on earth, (as there are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.5 Elohim">d</a></sup>gods many, and lords many,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> yet to us &#91;there is&#93; one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.6 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, the Father, of whom all things, and we for him; and one Lord, Jesus Christ, by whom &#91;are&#93; all things, and we by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 14:14; 1 Corinthians 10:28–29; Romans 14:22–23"> But knowledge &#91;is&#93; not in all: but some, with conscience of the idol, until now eat as of a thing sacrificed to idols; and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:17; Hebrews 13:9; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> But meat does not commend us to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.8 Elohim">f</a></sup>God; neither if we should not eat do we come short; nor if we should eat have we an advantage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 14:20–21; 1 Peter 2:16"> But see lest anywise this your right &#91;to eat&#93; itself be a stumbling-block to the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:28–29; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; Romans 14:23"> For if any one see thee, who hast knowledge, sitting at table in an idol-house, shall not his conscience, he being weak, be emboldened to eat the things sacrificed to the idol?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:15; Romans 15:1–3; 1 Corinthians 8:13"> and the weak &#91;one&#93;, the brother for whose sake Christ died, will perish through thy knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Matthew 25:45; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> Now, thus sinning against the brethren, and wounding their weak conscience, ye sin against Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 14:21; 2 Corinthians 6:3; 2 Corinthians 11:29"> Wherefore if meat be a fall-trap to my brother, I will eat no flesh for ever, that I may not be a fall-trap to my brother.</span>
