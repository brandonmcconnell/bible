<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27; 1 Corinthians 7:37–38"> But concerning the things of which ye have written &#91;to me&#93;: &#91;It is&#93; good for a man not to touch a woman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Ephesians 5:33; 1 Corinthians 7:9"> but on account of fornications, let each have his own wife, and each &#91;woman&#93; have her own husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Exodus 21:10"> Let the husband render her due to the wife, and in like manner the wife to the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 3:3; Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12"> The wife has not authority over her own body, but the husband: in like manner also the husband has not authority over his own body, but the wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Joel 2:16; Zechariah 12:12–14"> Defraud not one another, unless, it may be, by consent for a time, that ye may devote yourselves to prayer, and again be together, that Satan tempt you not because of your incontinency.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; 1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:12"> But this I say, as consenting &#91;to&#93;, not as commanding &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:11–12; Romans 12:6; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> Now I wish all men to be even as myself: but every one has his own gift of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.7 Elohim">a</a></sup>God: one man thus, and another thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32; 1 Corinthians 7:34–35; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27"> But I say to the unmarried and to the widows, It is good for them that they remain even as I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:11"> But if they have not control over themselves, let them marry; for it is better to marry than to burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; Matthew 19:3–9"> But to the married I enjoin, not I, but the Lord, Let not wife be separated from husband;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; 1 Corinthians 7:10; Isaiah 50:1"> (but if also she shall have been separated, let her remain unmarried, or be reconciled to her husband;) and let not husband leave wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:17; 1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> But as to the rest, I say, not the Lord, If any brother have an unbelieving wife, and she consent to dwell with him, let him not leave her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And a woman who has an unbelieving husband, and he consents to dwell with her, let her not leave &#91;her&#93; husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 4:5; Titus 1:15"> For the unbelieving husband is sanctified in the wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified in the brother; since &#91;otherwise&#93; indeed your children are unclean, but now they are holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 2 Corinthians 13:11; James 3:17–18"> But if the unbeliever go away, let them go away; a brother or a sister is not bound in such &#91;cases&#93;, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.15 Elohim">b</a></sup>God has called us in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Romans 11:14"> For what knowest thou, O wife, if thou shalt save thy husband? or what knowest thou, O husband, if thou shalt save thy wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; Romans 12:3–8; 1 Corinthians 7:24"> However, as the Lord has divided to each, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.17 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has called each, so let him walk; and thus I ordain in all the assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:5; Acts 15:28; Acts 15:24"> Has any one been called circumcised? let him not become uncircumcised: has any one been called in uncircumcision? let him not be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; 1 John 2:3–4"> Circumcision is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing; but keeping<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.19 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:12"> Let each abide in that calling in which he has been called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–24; Colossians 3:11; Luke 10:40–41"> Hast thou been called &#91;being&#93; a bondman, let it not concern thee; but and if thou canst become free, use &#91;it&#93; rather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Philemon 1:16; Colossians 3:22–24"> For the bondman that is called in &#91;the&#93; Lord is the Lord's freedman; in like manner &#91;also&#93; the freeman being called is Christ's bondman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 2:14"> Ye have been bought with a price; do not be the bondmen of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; Colossians 3:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> Let each, wherein he is called, brethren, therein abide with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.24 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> But concerning virgins, I have no commandment of &#91;the&#93; Lord; but I give my opinion, as having received mercy of &#91;the&#93; Lord to be faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Peter 4:17"> I think then that this is good, on account of the present necessity, that &#91;it is&#93; good for a man to remain so as he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; 1 Corinthians 7:12–14"> Art thou bound to a wife? seek not to be loosed; art thou free from a wife? do not seek a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:32–36; 1 Corinthians 7:26"> But if thou shouldest also marry, thou hast not sinned; and if the virgin marry, they have not sinned: but such shall have tribulation in the flesh; but I spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Corinthians 7:31; Romans 13:11–12"> But this I say, brethren, the time is straitened. For the rest, that they who have wives, be as not having &#91;any&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Luke 6:25; Luke 6:21"> and they that weep, as not weeping; and they that rejoice, as not rejoicing; and they that buy, as not possessing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Peter 4:7; James 4:14"> and they that use the world, as not disposing of it as their own; for the fashion of this world passes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; 1 Timothy 5:5; Philippians 4:6"> But I wish you to be without care. The unmarried cares for the things of the Lord, how he shall please the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:25–33"> but he that has married cares for the things of the world, how he shall please his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Luke 2:36–37"> There is a difference between the wife and the virgin. The unmarried cares for the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and spirit; but she that has married cares for the things of the world, how she shall please her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:33–34; 1 Corinthians 7:36; Luke 8:14"> But I say this for your own profit; not that I may set a snare before you, but for what &#91;is&#93; seemly, and waiting on the Lord without distraction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:37; 1 Corinthians 7:9; 1 Samuel 2:33"> But if any one think that he behaves unseemly to his virginity, if he be beyond the flower of his age, and so it must be, let him do what he will, he does not sin: let them marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But he who stands firm in his heart, having no need, but has authority over his own will, and has judged this in his heart to keep his own virginity, he does well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> So that he that marries himself does well; and he that does not marry does better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 7:2–3; 2 Corinthians 6:14–16; 1 Corinthians 7:10"> A wife is bound for whatever time her husband lives; but if the husband be fallen asleep, she is free to be married to whom she will, only in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:35; 2 Peter 3:15–16; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> But she is happier if she so remain, according to my judgment; but I think that I also have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="7.40 Elohim">f</a></sup>God's Spirit.</span>
</div>
