<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:9"> Let a man so account of us as servants of Christ, and stewards of &#91;the&#93; mysteries of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Here, further, it is sought in stewards, that a man be found faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> But for me it is the very smallest matter that I be examined of you or of man's day. Nor do I even examine myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:2; 1 John 3:20–21; Psalms 130:3"> For I am conscious of nothing in myself; but I am not justified by this: but he that examines me is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> So that do not judge anything before &#91;the&#93; time, until the Lord shall come, who shall also both bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and shall make manifest the counsels of hearts; and then shall each have &#91;his&#93; praise from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 12:19"> Now these things, brethren, I have transferred, in their application, to myself and Apollos, for your sakes, that ye may learn in us the &#91;lesson of&#93; not &#91;letting your thoughts go&#93; above what is written, that ye may not be puffed up one for &#91;such a&#93; one against another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:27; 1 Peter 4:10; James 1:17"> For who makes thee to differ? and what hast thou which thou hast not received? but if also thou hast received, why boastest thou as not receiving?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Already ye are filled; already ye have been enriched; ye have reigned without us; and I would that ye reigned, that we also might reign with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:33; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> For I think that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has set us the apostles for the last, as appointed to death. For we have become a spectacle to the world, both to angels and men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> We &#91;are&#93; fools for Christ's sake, but ye prudent in Christ: we weak, but ye strong: ye glorious, but we in dishonour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:35; 2 Corinthians 11:23–27; Philippians 4:12"> To the present hour we both hunger and thirst, and are in nakedness, and buffeted, and wander without a home,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:11; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> and labour, working with our own hands. Railed at, we bless; persecuted, we suffer &#91;it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; Acts 22:22"> insulted, we entreat: we are become as &#91;the&#93; offscouring of the world, &#91;the&#93; refuse of all, until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 3 John 1:4"> Not &#91;as&#93; chiding do I write these things to you, but as my beloved children I admonish &#91;you&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 1 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Corinthians 9:14"> For if ye should have ten thousand instructors in Christ, yet not many fathers; for in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17"> I entreat you therefore, be my imitators.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> For this reason I have sent to you Timotheus, who is my beloved and faithful child in &#91;the&#93; Lord, who shall put you in mind of my ways &#91;as&#93; they &#91;are&#93; in Christ, according as I teach everywhere in every assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6–8; 1 Corinthians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> But some have been puffed up, as if I were not coming to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1–4; Acts 18:21; Acts 19:21"> but I will come quickly to you, if the Lord will; and I will know, not the word of those that are puffed up, but the power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> For the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.20 Elohim">d</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; not in word, but in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> What will ye? that I come to you with a rod; or in love, and &#91;in&#93; a spirit of meekness?</span>
