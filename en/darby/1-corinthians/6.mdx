<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15–17; Acts 19:38; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> Dare any one of you, having a matter against another, prosecute his suit before the unjust, and not before the saints?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Daniel 7:22; Revelation 20:4"> Do ye not then know that the saints shall judge the world? and if the world is judged by you, are ye unworthy of &#91;the&#93; smallest judgments?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:6; Matthew 25:41; 1 Corinthians 6:4"> Do ye not know that we shall judge angels? and not then matters of this life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12; Acts 6:2–4"> If then ye have judgments as to things of this life, set those &#91;to judge&#93; who are little esteemed in the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:34; 1 Corinthians 4:14; James 3:13–18"> I speak to you &#91;to put you&#93; to shame. Thus there is not a wise person among you, not even one, who shall be able to decide between his brethren!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 2:14–15; 1 John 2:9–11; 1 John 3:11–15"> But brother prosecutes his suit with brother, and that before unbelievers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 12:17–19; Proverbs 20:22; 1 Peter 2:19–23"> Already indeed then it is altogether a fault in you that ye have suits between yourselves. Why do ye not rather suffer wrong? why are ye not rather defrauded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Colossians 3:25; Mark 10:19"> But ye do wrong, and defraud, and this &#91;your&#93; brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9–10; Galatians 5:19–21; Ephesians 5:4–5"> Do ye not know that unrighteous &#91;persons&#93; shall not inherit &#91;the&#93; kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.9 Elohim">a</a></sup>God? Do not err: neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor those who make women of themselves, nor who abuse themselves with men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:11; Galatians 5:21; Ezekiel 22:29"> nor thieves, nor covetous, nor drunkards, nor abusive persons, nor &#91;the&#93; rapacious, shall inherit &#91;the&#93; kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.10 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 3:5–7; 1 Corinthians 1:2"> And these things were some of you; but ye have been washed, but ye have been sanctified, but ye have been justified in the name of the Lord Jesus, and by the Spirit of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:23–33; 1 Corinthians 9:27; Hebrews 12:15–16"> All things are lawful to me, but all things do not profit; all things are lawful to me, but I will not be brought under the power of any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Romans 6:12"> Meats for the belly, and the belly for meats; but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.13 Elohim">d</a></sup>God will bring to nothing both it and them: but the body &#91;is&#93; not for fornication, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 1:19–20"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>God has both raised up the Lord, and will raise us up from among &#91;the dead&#93; by his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:30; Romans 12:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> Do ye not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Shall I then, taking the members of the Christ, make &#91;them&#93; members of a harlot? Far be the thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Ephesians 5:31; Mark 10:8"> Do ye not know that he &#91;that is&#93; joined to the harlot is one body? for the two, he says, shall be one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 17:21–23; Galatians 2:20; 1 Corinthians 12:13"> But he that &#91;is&#93; joined to the Lord is one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:3; Colossians 3:5"> Flee fornication. Every sin which a man may practise is without the body, but he that commits fornication sins against his own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Peter 2:5"> Do ye not know that your body is &#91;the&#93; temple of the Holy Spirit which &#91;is&#93; in you, which ye have of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.19 Elohim">f</a></sup>God; and ye are not your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:23; Romans 12:1; 1 Peter 2:9"> for ye have been bought with a price: glorify now then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.20 Elohim">g</a></sup>God in your body.</span>
