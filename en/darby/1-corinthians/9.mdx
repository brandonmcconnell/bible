<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am I not free? am I not an apostle? have I not seen Jesus our Lord? are not ye my work in &#91;the&#93; Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> If I am not an apostle to others, yet at any rate I am to you: for the seal of mine apostleship are ye in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> My defence to those who examine me is this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Have we not a right to eat and to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> have we not a right to take round a sister &#91;as&#93; wife, as also the other apostles, and the brethren of the Lord, and Cephas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Or I alone and Barnabas, have we not a right not to work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Who ever carries on war at his own charges? who plants a vineyard and does not eat of its fruit? or who herds a flock and does not eat of the milk of the flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> Do I speak these things as a man, or does not the law also say these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; Psalms 104:27"> For in the law of Moses it is written, Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that is treading out corn. Is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.9 Elohim">a</a></sup>God occupied about the oxen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> or does he say &#91;it&#93; altogether for our sakes? For for our sakes it has been written, that the plougher should plough in hope, and he that treads out corn, in hope of partaking of &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> If we have sown to you spiritual things, &#91;is it a&#93; great &#91;thing&#93; if we shall reap your carnal things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others partake of this right over you, should not rather we? But we have not used this right, but we bear all things, that we may put no hindrance in the way of the glad tidings of the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Do ye not know that they who labour &#91;at&#93; sacred things eat of the &#91;offerings offered in the&#93; temple; they that attend at the altar partake with the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> So also the Lord has ordained to those that announce the glad tidings to live of the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have used none of these things. Now I have not written these things that it should be thus in my case; for &#91;it were&#93; good for me rather to die than that any one should make vain my boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> For if I announce the glad tidings, I have nothing to boast of; for a necessity is laid upon me; for it is woe to me if I should not announce the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> For if I do this voluntarily, I have a reward; but if not of my own will, I am entrusted with an administration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> What is the reward then that I have? That in announcing the glad tidings I make the glad tidings costless &#91;to others&#93;, so as not to have made use, as belonging to me, of my right in &#91;announcing&#93; the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> For being free from all, I have made myself bondman to all, that I might gain the most &#91;possible&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> And I became to the Jews as a Jew, in order that I might gain the Jews: to those under law, as under law, not being myself under law, in order that I might gain those under law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> to those without law, as without law, (not as without law to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.21 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, but as legitimately subject to Christ,) in order that I might gain &#91;those&#93; without law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> I became to the weak, &#91;as&#93; weak, in order that I might gain the weak. To all I have become all things, in order that at all events I might save some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> And I do all things for the sake of the glad tidings, that I may be fellow-partaker with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Know ye not that they who run in &#91;the&#93; race-course run all, but one receives the prize? Thus run in order that ye may obtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> But every one that contends &#91;for a prize&#93; is temperate in all things: they then indeed that they may receive a corruptible crown, but we an incorruptible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> I therefore thus run, as not uncertainly; so I combat, as not beating the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> But I buffet my body, and lead it captive, lest &#91;after&#93; having preached to others I should be myself rejected.</span>
