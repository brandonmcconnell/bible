<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; Ephesians 1:1; 2 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, &#91;a&#93; called apostle of Jesus Christ, by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's will, and Sosthenes the brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> to the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God which is in Corinth, to &#91;those&#93; sanctified in Christ Jesus, called saints, with all that in every place call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, both theirs and ours:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace to you and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God our Father, and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; John 14:26; 1 Timothy 1:14"> I thank my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God always about you, in respect of the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">e</a></sup>God given to you in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:11; 2 Corinthians 8:7; Colossians 2:3"> that in everything ye have been enriched in him, in all word &#91;of doctrine&#93;, and all knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Timothy 2:6"> (according as the testimony of the Christ has been confirmed in you,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 2 Peter 3:12; Hebrews 9:28"> so that ye come short in no gift, awaiting the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Colossians 1:22"> who shall also confirm you to &#91;the&#93; end, unimpeachable in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Hebrews 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">f</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; faithful, by whom ye have been called into &#91;the&#93; fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:1–7; 1 Corinthians 12:25"> Now I exhort you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all say the same thing, and that there be not among you divisions; but that ye be perfectly united in the same mind and in the same opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Timothy 2:23–25"> For it has been shewn to me concerning you, my brethren, by those of &#91;the house of&#93; Chloe, that there are strifes among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:21–23; John 1:42; Matthew 23:9–10"> But I speak of this, that each of you says, I am of Paul, and I of Apollos, and I of Cephas, and I of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; 2 Corinthians 11:4; Acts 2:38"> Is the Christ divided? has Paul been crucified for you? or have ye been baptised unto the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 18:8; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:4"> I thank<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.14 Elohim">g</a></sup>God that I have baptised none of you, unless Crispus and Gaius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; John 3:28–29; John 7:18"> that no one may say that I have baptised unto my own name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 16:33"> Yes, I baptised also the house of Stephanas; for the rest I know not if I have baptised any other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:1; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:16"> For Christ has not sent me to baptise, but to preach glad tidings; not in wisdom of word, that the cross of the Christ may not be made vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:10"> For the word of the cross is to them that perish foolishness, but to us that are saved it is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.18 Elohim">h</a></sup>God's power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> For it is written, I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and set aside the understanding of the understanding ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Job 12:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Where &#91;is the&#93; wise? where scribe? where disputer of this world? has not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.20 Elohim">i</a></sup>God made foolish the wisdom of the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 10:21; Romans 1:20–22; Matthew 11:25"> For since, in the wisdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, the world by wisdom has not known<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">k</a></sup>God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">l</a></sup>God has been pleased by the foolishness of the preaching to save those that believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:1–4; Matthew 12:38–39; Mark 8:11"> Since Jews indeed ask for signs, and Greeks seek wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Peter 2:8"> but we preach Christ crucified, to Jews an offence, and to nations foolishness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> but to those that &#91;are&#93; called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1.24 Elohim">m</a></sup>God's power and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1.24 Elohim">n</a></sup>God's wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 11:33–36; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> Because the foolishness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="1.25 Elohim">o</a></sup>God is wiser than men, and the weakness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="1.25 Elohim">p</a></sup>God is stronger than men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:5; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20; James 1:9–11"> For consider your calling, brethren, that &#91;there are&#93; not many wise according to flesh, not many powerful, not many high-born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 11:25; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="1.27 Elohim">q</a></sup>God has chosen the foolish things of the world, that he may put to shame the wise; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="1.27 Elohim">r</a></sup>God has chosen the weak things of the world, that he may put to shame the strong things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; Romans 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> and the ignoble things of the world, and the despised, has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="1.28 Elohim">s</a></sup>God chosen, &#91;and&#93; things that are not, that he may annul the things that are;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; Ephesians 2:9"> so that no flesh should boast before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="1.29 Elohim">t</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1"> But of him are ye in Christ Jesus, who has been made to us wisdom from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="1.30 Elohim">u</a></sup>God, and righteousness, and holiness, and redemption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 10:17; Philippians 3:3"> that according as it is written, He that boasts, let him boast in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
