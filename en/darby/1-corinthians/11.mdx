<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> Be my imitators, even as I also &#91;am&#93; of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> Now I praise you, that in all things ye are mindful of me; and that as I have directed you, ye keep the directions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> But I wish you to know that the Christ is the head of every man, but woman's head &#91;is&#93; the man, and the Christ's head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Every man praying or prophesying, having &#91;anything&#93; on his head, puts his head to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> But every woman praying or prophesying with her head uncovered puts her own head to shame; for it is one and the same as a shaved &#91;woman&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> For if a woman be not covered, let her hair also be cut off. But if &#91;it be&#93; shameful to a woman to have her hair cut off or to be shaved, let her be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalms 8:6"> For man indeed ought not to have his head covered, being<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's image and glory; but woman is man's glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> For man is not of woman, but woman of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> For also man was not created for the sake of the woman, but woman for the sake of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> Therefore ought the woman to have authority on her head, on account of the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> However, neither &#91;is&#93; woman without man, nor man without woman, in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> For as the woman &#91;is&#93; of the man, so also &#91;is&#93; the man by the woman, but all things of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.12 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Judge in yourselves: is it comely that a woman should pray to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.13 Elohim">d</a></sup>God uncovered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> Does not even nature itself teach you, that man, if he have long hair, it is a dishonour to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But woman, if she have long hair, &#91;it is&#93; glory to her; for the long hair is given &#91;to her&#93; in lieu of a veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> But if any one think to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor the assemblies of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.16 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> But &#91;in&#93; prescribing &#91;to you on&#93; this &#91;which I now enter on&#93;, I do not praise, &#91;namely,&#93; that ye come together, not for the better, but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For first, when ye come together in assembly, I hear there exist divisions among you, and I partly give credit &#91;to it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> For there must also be sects among you, that the approved may become manifest among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> When ye come therefore together into one place, it is not to eat &#91;the&#93; Lord's supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For each one in eating takes his own supper before &#91;others&#93;, and one is hungry and another drinks to excess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> Have ye not then houses for eating and drinking? or do ye despise the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.22 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, and put to shame them who have not? What shall I say to you? shall I praise you? In this &#91;point&#93; I do not praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received from the Lord, that which I also delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus, in the night in which he was delivered up, took bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> and having given thanks broke &#91;it&#93;, and said, This is my body, which &#91;is&#93; for you: this do in remembrance of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> In like manner also the cup, after having supped, saying, This cup is the new covenant in my blood: this do, as often as ye shall drink &#91;it&#93;, in remembrance of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> For as often as ye shall eat this bread, and drink the cup, ye announce the death of the Lord, until he come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> So that whosoever shall eat the bread, or drink the cup of the Lord, unworthily, shall be guilty in respect of the body and of the blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> But let a man prove himself, and thus eat of the bread, and drink of the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> For &#91;the&#93; eater and drinker eats and drinks judgment to himself, not distinguishing the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> On this account many among you &#91;are&#93; weak and infirm, and a good many are fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalms 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> But if we judged ourselves, so were we not judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalms 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But being judged, we are disciplined of &#91;the&#93; Lord, that we may not be condemned with the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So that, my brethren, when ye come together to eat, wait for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> If any one be hungry, let him eat at home, that ye may not come together for judgment. But the other things, whenever I come, I will set in order.</span>
