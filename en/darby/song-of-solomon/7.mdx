<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:4; Song of Solomon 4:8–9; Psalms 144:12"> Thy neck is as a tower of ivory; Thine eyes, &#91;like&#93; the pools in Heshbon, By the gate of Bath-rabbim; Thy nose like the tower of Lebanon, Which looketh toward Damascus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Song of Solomon 4:1; Micah 7:14"> Thy head upon thee is like Carmel, And the locks of thy head like purple; The king is fettered by &#91;thy&#93; ringlets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:15–16; Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 4:10"> How fair and how pleasant art thou, &#91;my&#93; love, in delights!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:5; Song of Solomon 1:13; Ephesians 4:13"> This thy stature is like to a palm-tree, And thy breasts to grape clusters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Solomon 2:5"> I said, I will go up to the palm-tree, I will take hold of the boughs thereof; And thy breasts shall indeed be like clusters of the vine, And the fragrance of thy nose like apples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:16; Isaiah 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> And the roof of thy mouth like the best wine, ...That goeth down smoothly for my beloved, And stealeth over the lips of them that are asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Song of Solomon 2:16; Song of Solomon 6:3"> I am my beloved's, And his desire is toward me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:8; Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Song of Solomon 1:4"> — Come, my beloved, let us go forth into the fields; Let us lodge in the villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:11; Ephesians 6:24; Song of Solomon 7:6"> We will go up early to the vineyards, We will see if the vine hath budded, &#91;If&#93; the blossom is opening, And the pomegranates are in bloom: There will I give thee my loves.</span>
