<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; John 15:9–15; Romans 5:8–10"> He hath brought me to the house of wine, And his banner over me is love.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:27; Revelation 3:20; John 3:29"> The voice of my beloved! Behold, he cometh Leaping upon the mountains, Skipping upon the hills.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:13; Song of Solomon 4:7–8; Revelation 22:17"> My beloved spake and said unto me, Rise up, my love, my fair one, and come away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Isaiah 54:6–8"> For behold, the winter is past, The rain is over, it is gone:</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10; Song of Solomon 7:11–13; Isaiah 55:10–11"> The fig-tree melloweth her winter figs, And the vines in bloom give forth &#91;their&#93; fragrance. Arise, my love, my fair one, and come away!</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 13:32; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Song of Solomon 7:12"> Take us the foxes, The little foxes, that spoil the vineyards; For our vineyards are in bloom.</span>
