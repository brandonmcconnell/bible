<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> The word &#91;is&#93; faithful: if any one aspires to exercise oversight, he desires a good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> The overseer then must be irreproachable, husband of one wife, sober, discreet, decorous, hospitable, apt to teach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not given to excesses from wine, not a striker, but mild, not addicted to contention, not fond of money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> conducting his own house well, having &#91;his&#93; children in subjection with all gravity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> (but if one does not know how to conduct his own house, how shall he take care of the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> not a novice, that he may not, being inflated, fall into &#91;the&#93; fault of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> But it is necessary that he should have also a good testimony from those without, that he may fall not into reproach and &#91;the&#93; snare of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Ministers, in like manner, grave, not double-tongued, not given to much wine, not seeking gain by base means,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> And let these be first proved, then let them minister, being without charge &#91;against them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> &#91;The&#93; women in like manner grave, not slanderers, sober, faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Let &#91;the&#93; ministers be husbands of one wife, conducting &#91;their&#93; children and their own houses well:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> for those who shall have ministered well obtain for themselves a good degree, and much boldness in faith which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> These things I write to thee, hoping to come to thee more quickly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> but if I delay, in order that thou mayest know how one ought to conduct oneself in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.15 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's house, which is &#91;the&#93; assembly of &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.15 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, &#91;the&#93; pillar and base of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> And confessedly the mystery of piety is great.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.16 Elohim">d</a></sup>God has been manifested in flesh, has been justified in &#91;the&#93; Spirit, has appeared to angels, has been preached among &#91;the&#93; nations, has been believed on in &#91;the&#93; world, has been received up in glory.</span>
