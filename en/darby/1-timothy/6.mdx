<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Peter 2:17–20; Titus 2:8–10"> Let as many bondmen as are under yoke count their own masters worthy of all honour, that the name of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and the teaching be not blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; 1 Timothy 4:11; Genesis 16:4–5"> And they that have believing masters, let them not despise &#91;them&#93; because they are brethren; but let them the rather serve them with subjection, because they are faithful and beloved, who profit by the good and ready service &#91;rendered&#93;. These things teach and exhort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:3; Romans 16:17; 1 Timothy 1:10"> If any one teach differently, and do not accede to sound words, those of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the teaching which &#91;is&#93; according to piety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 3:4; 1 Timothy 3:6"> he is puffed up, knowing nothing, but sick about questions and disputes of words, out of which arise envy, strife, injurious words, evil suspicions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3"> constant quarrellings of men corrupted in mind and destitute of the truth, holding gain to be &#91;the end of&#93; piety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:8; Proverbs 16:8"> But piety with contentment is great gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 1:21; Psalms 49:17; Luke 12:20–21"> For we have brought nothing into the world: &#91;it is&#93; &#91;manifest&#93; that neither can we carry anything out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:25–33"> But having sustenance and covering, we will be content with these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 3:7"> But those who desire to be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and many unwise and hurtful lusts, which plunge men into destruction and ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 1:19; 1 Timothy 6:9; Psalms 32:10"> For the love of money is &#91;the&#93; root of every evil; which some having aspired after, have wandered from the faith, and pierced themselves with many sorrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 3:17; 1 Timothy 4:12"> But thou, O man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, flee these things, and pursue righteousness, piety, faith, love, endurance, meekness of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25–26"> Strive earnestly &#91;in&#93; the good conflict of faith. Lay hold of eternal life, to which thou hast been called, and hast confessed the good confession before many witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:36–37; Matthew 27:11; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I enjoin thee before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God who preserves all things in life, and Christ Jesus who witnessed before Pontius Pilate the good confession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> that thou keep the commandment spotless, irreproachable, until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 1:17"> which in its own time the blessed and only Ruler shall shew, the King of those that reign, and Lord of those that exercise lordship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; John 1:18; 1 John 1:5"> who only has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light; whom no man has seen, nor is able to see; to whom &#91;be&#93; honour and eternal might. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; Acts 14:17; Proverbs 27:24"> Enjoin on those rich in the present age not to be high-minded, nor to trust on the uncertainty of riches; but in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God who affords us all things richly for &#91;our&#93; enjoyment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 John 3:17; Psalms 37:3"> to do good, to be rich in good works, to be liberal in distributing, disposed to communicate &#91;of their substance&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; 1 Timothy 6:12; 2 Timothy 2:19"> laying by for themselves a good foundation for the future, that they may lay hold of &#91;what is&#93; really life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 2 Timothy 2:16; 2 Timothy 1:12–14"> O Timotheus, keep the entrusted deposit, avoiding profane, vain babblings, and oppositions of false-named knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:18; 1 Timothy 1:6; 2 Timothy 4:22"> of which some having made profession, have missed the faith. Grace &#91;be&#93; with thee.</span>
