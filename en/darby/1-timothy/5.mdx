<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> Rebuke not an elder sharply, but exhort &#91;him&#93; as a father, younger &#91;men&#93; as brethren,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Matthew 12:50; Philippians 4:8"> elder women as mothers, younger women as sisters, with all purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honour widows who are really widows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> but if any widow have children or descendants, let them learn first to be pious as regards their own house, and to render a return on their side to &#91;their&#93; parents; for this is acceptable in the sight of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> Now she who &#91;is&#93; a widow indeed, and is left alone, has put &#91;her&#93; hope in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, and continues in supplications and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> But she that lives in habits of self-indulgence is dead &#91;while&#93; living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> And these things enjoin, that they may be irreproachable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> But if any one does not provide for his own, and specially for those of &#91;his&#93; house, he has denied the faith, and is worse than the unbeliever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> Let a widow be put upon the list, being of not less than sixty years, &#91;having been&#93; wife of one man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> borne witness to in good works, if she have brought up children, if she have exercised hospitality, if she have washed saints' feet, if she have imparted relief to the distressed, if she have diligently followed every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> But younger widows decline; for when they grow wanton against Christ, they desire to marry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> being guilty, because they have cast off their first faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> And, at the same time, they learn also &#91;to be&#93; idle, going about to people's houses; and not only idle, but also gossipers and meddlers, speaking things not becoming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> I will therefore that the younger marry, bear children, rule the house, give no occasion to the adversary in respect of reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> For already some have turned aside after Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> If any believing man or woman have widows, let them impart relief to them, and let not the assembly be charged, that it may impart relief to those &#91;that are&#93; widows indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> Let the elders who take the lead &#91;among the saints&#93; well be esteemed worthy of double honour, specially those labouring in word and teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> for the scripture says, Thou shalt not muzzle an ox that treadeth out corn, and, The workman &#91;is&#93; worthy of his hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Against an elder receive not an accusation unless where there are two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Those that sin convict before all, that the rest also may have fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> I testify before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.21 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels, that thou keep these things without prejudice, doing nothing by favour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Lay hands quickly on no man, nor partake in others' sins. Keep thyself pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> Drink no longer only water, but use a little wine on account of thy stomach and thy frequent illnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> Of some men the sins are manifest beforehand, going before to judgment, and some also they follow after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; Psalms 37:5–6"> In like manner good works also are manifest beforehand, and those that are otherwise cannot be hid.</span>
