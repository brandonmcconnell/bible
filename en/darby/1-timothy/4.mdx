<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:1–9; 2 Peter 2:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> But the Spirit speaks expressly, that in latter times some shall apostatise from the faith, giving their mind to deceiving spirits and teachings of demons</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> speaking lies in hypocrisy, cauterised as to their own conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> forbidding to marry, &#91;bidding&#93; to abstain from meats, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God has created for receiving with thanksgiving for them who are faithful and know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> For every creature of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; good, and nothing &#91;is&#93; to be rejected, being received with thanksgiving;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Luke 4:4"> for it is sanctified by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God's word and freely addressing &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> Laying these things before the brethren, thou wilt be a good minister of Christ Jesus, nourished with the words of the faith and of the good teaching which thou hast fully followed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But profane and old wives' fables avoid, but exercise thyself unto piety;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; Psalms 37:3–4"> for bodily exercise is profitable for a little, but piety is profitable for everything, having promise of life, of the present one, and of that to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> The word &#91;is&#93; faithful and worthy of all acceptation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> for, for this we labour and suffer reproach, because we hope in a living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, who is preserver of all men, specially of those that believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> Enjoin and teach these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Let no one despise thy youth, but be a model of the believers, in word, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Till I come, give thyself to reading, to exhortation, to teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Be not negligent of the gift &#91;that is&#93; in thee, which has been given to thee through prophecy, with imposition of the hands of the elderhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; Psalms 143:5"> Occupy thyself with these things; be wholly in them, that thy progress may be manifest to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 John 1:8–9"> Give heed to thyself and to the teaching; continue in them; for, doing this, thou shalt save both thyself and those that hear thee.</span>
