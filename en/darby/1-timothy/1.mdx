<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:3; Colossians 1:27; Acts 9:15"> Paul, apostle of Jesus Christ, according to &#91;the&#93; command of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God our Saviour, and of Christ Jesus our hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:14–17"> to Timotheus, &#91;my&#93; true child in faith: grace, mercy, peace, from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God our Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 John 1:9–10"> Even as I begged thee to remain in Ephesus, &#91;when I was&#93; going to Macedonia, that thou mightest enjoin some not to teach other doctrines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> nor to turn their minds to fables and interminable genealogies, which bring questionings rather than &#91;further&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God's dispensation, which &#91;is&#93; in faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> But the end of what is enjoined is love out of a pure heart and a good conscience and unfeigned faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> which &#91;things&#93; some having missed, have turned aside to vain discourse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> desiring to be law-teachers, not understanding either what they say or concerning what they &#91;so&#93; strenuously affirm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; Psalms 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> Now we know that the law &#91;is&#93; good if any one uses it lawfully,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> knowing this, that law has not its application to a righteous person, but to &#91;the&#93; lawless and insubordinate, to &#91;the&#93; impious and sinful, to &#91;the&#93; unholy and profane, to smiters of fathers and smiters of mothers; to murderers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> fornicators, sodomites, kidnappers, liars, perjurers; and if any other thing is opposed to sound teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> according to the glad tidings of the glory of the blessed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, with which I have been entrusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> &#91;And&#93; I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who has given me power, that he has counted me faithful, appointing to ministry him</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> who before was a blasphemer and persecutor, and an insolent overbearing &#91;man&#93;: but mercy was shewn me because I did it ignorantly, in unbelief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> But the grace of our Lord surpassingly over-abounded with faith and love, which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; John 3:16–17"> Faithful &#91;is&#93; the word, and worthy of all acceptation, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am &#91;the&#93; first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> But for this reason mercy was shewn me, that in me, &#91;the&#93; first, Jesus Christ might display the whole long-suffering, for a delineation of those about to believe on him to life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Jude 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> Now to the King of the ages, &#91;the&#93; incorruptible, invisible, only<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.17 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, honour and glory to the ages of ages. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> This charge, &#91;my&#93; child Timotheus, I commit to thee, according to the prophecies as to thee preceding, in order that thou mightest war by them the good warfare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> maintaining faith and a good conscience; which &#91;last&#93; some, having put away, have made shipwreck as to faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> of whom is Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have delivered to Satan, that they may be taught by discipline not to blaspheme.</span>
</div>
