<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:3; Matthew 6:9–10; James 5:16"> I exhort therefore, first of all, that supplications, prayers, intercessions, thanksgivings be made for all men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> for kings and all that are in dignity, that we may lead a quiet and tranquil life in all piety and gravity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> for this is good and acceptable before our Saviour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> who desires that all men should be saved and come to &#91;the&#93; knowledge of &#91;the&#93; truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God is one, and &#91;the&#93; mediator of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and men one, &#91;the&#93; man Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> who gave himself a ransom for all, the testimony &#91;to be rendered&#93; in its own times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> to which I have been appointed a herald and apostle, (I speak &#91;the&#93; truth, I do not lie,) a teacher of &#91;the&#93; nations in faith and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 134:2; Psalms 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> I will therefore that the men pray in every place, lifting up pious hands, without wrath or reasoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalms 149:4"> In like manner also that the women in decent deportment and dress adorn themselves with modesty and discretion, not with plaited &#91;hair&#93; and gold, or pearls, or costly clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but, what becomes women making profession of the fear of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, by good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Let a woman learn in quietness in all subjection;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> but I do not suffer a woman to teach nor to exercise authority over man, but to be in quietness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> for Adam was formed first, then Eve:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> and Adam was not deceived; but the woman, having been deceived, was in transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> But she shall be preserved in childbearing, if they continue in faith and love and holiness with discretion.</span>
