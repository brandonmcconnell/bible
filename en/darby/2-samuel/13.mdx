<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:9; 2 Samuel 3:2–3; 2 Samuel 13:15"> And it came to pass after this, that Absalom the son of David having a beautiful sister, whose name was Tamar, Amnon the son of David loved her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; 1 Kings 21:4; Song of Solomon 5:8"> And Amnon was so vexed, that he fell sick for his sister Tamar; for she was a virgin; and it seemed difficult for Amnon to do the least thing to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19; James 3:15"> And Amnon had a friend whose name was Jonadab, the son of Shimeah David's brother; and Jonadab was a very shrewd man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Esther 5:13–14"> And he said to him, Why dost thou, the king's son, get thinner from morning to morning? Wilt thou not tell me? And Amnon said to him, I love Tamar, my brother Absalom's sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 6:24–25; Proverbs 19:27; Acts 23:15"> And Jonadab said to him, Lay thee down on thy bed and feign thyself sick; and when thy father comes to see thee, say to him, I pray thee, let my sister Tamar come, and give me food, and dress the food in my sight, that I may see it, and eat it from her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:6; Matthew 13:33"> So Amnon lay down and feigned himself sick; and the king came to see him, and Amnon said to the king, I pray thee, let Tamar my sister come, and make a couple of cakes in my sight, that I may eat from her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And David sent home to Tamar, saying, Go, I pray thee, to thy brother Amnon's house, and dress him food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Tamar went to her brother Amnon's house; and he had lain down. And she took flour and kneaded it, and made cakes in his sight, and baked the cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; John 3:20; Judges 3:19"> And she took the pan, and poured them out before him; but he refused to eat. And Amnon said, Put every man out from me. And they went out every man from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Amnon said to Tamar, Bring the dish into the chamber, that I may eat of thy hand. And Tamar took the cakes that she had made, and brought them into the chamber to Amnon her brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:11–12"> And she presented them to him to eat; and he took hold of her, and said to her, Come, lie with me, my sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:17; Judges 20:6"> And she said to him, No, my brother, do not humble me; for no such thing is done in Israel: do not this infamy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 20:12; Genesis 19:8"> And I, whither shall I carry my reproach? and thou wouldest be as one of the infamous in Israel. And now, I pray thee, speak to the king; for he will not withhold me from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11; Esther 7:8; Judges 20:5"> But he would not hearken to her voice, and was stronger than she, and humbled her and lay with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:17"> And Amnon hated her with an exceeding great hatred, for the hatred with which he hated her was greater than the love with which he had loved her. And Amnon said to her, Arise, be gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she said to him, There is no cause for this evil in sending me away, &#91;which&#93; is greater than the other that thou didst to me. But he would not hearken to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then he called his young man that attended upon him, and said, Put now this &#91;woman&#93; out from me, and bolt the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Genesis 37:3; Psalms 45:13–14"> Now she had a vest of many colours upon her; for so were the king's daughters that were virgins apparelled. And his attendant brought her out, and bolted the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Jeremiah 2:37; 2 Samuel 1:11"> And Tamar put ashes on her head, and rent her vest of many colours which was on her, and laid her hand on her head, and went away, crying out as she went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 46:15; Genesis 34:2"> And Absalom her brother said to her, Has Amnon thy brother been with thee? and now, my sister, be still: he is thy brother; take not this thing to heart. And Tamar remained, and &#91;that&#93; desolate, in her brother Absalom's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Samuel 2:29"> And king David heard of all these things, and he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17–18; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 24:50"> And Absalom spoke to Amnon neither good nor bad; for Absalom hated Amnon, because he had humbled his sister Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 38:12–13; 1 Samuel 25:4; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And it came to pass after two full years, that Absalom had sheepshearers in Baal-Hazor, which is beside Ephraim; and Absalom invited all the king's sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:8–15; Jeremiah 41:6–7; Psalms 55:21"> And Absalom came to the king, and said, Behold, now, thy servant has sheepshearers; let the king, I pray thee, and his servants go with thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 14:23; Judges 19:7–10; 2 Samuel 14:22"> And the king said to Absalom, No, my son, let us not all go, lest we be burdensome to thee. And he urged him, but he would not go; and he blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 11:13–15"> And Absalom said, If not, I pray thee, let my brother Amnon go with us. And the king said to him, Why should he go with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26"> But Absalom pressed him; and he let Amnon and all the king's sons go with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Judges 19:22; Judges 19:9"> And Absalom commanded his servants, saying, Mark ye now when Amnon's heart is merry with wine, and when I say to you, Smite Amnon; then slay him, fear not: have not I commanded you? be courageous, and be valiant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 1:33; 2 Kings 1:9–12"> And the servants of Absalom did to Amnon as Absalom had commanded. Then all the king's sons arose, and they rode each upon his mule and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass, while they were in the way, that tidings came to David, saying, Absalom has smitten all the king's sons, and there is not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 12:16; Job 1:20"> Then the king arose, and rent his garments, and lay on the earth; and all his servants stood by with their garments rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 2 Samuel 13:3; Genesis 27:41"> And Jonadab, the son of Shimeah David's brother, answered and said, Let not my lord suppose that they have slain all the young men the king's sons; for Amnon only is dead; for by the appointment of Absalom this has been determined from the day that he humbled his sister Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19"> And now let not my lord the king take the thing to heart, to say, All the king's sons are dead; for Amnon only is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 4:8–14; Proverbs 28:17; Amos 5:19"> And Absalom fled. And the young man that watched lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, there came much people from the way behind him, from the hill-side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Jonadab said to the king, Behold, the king's sons come: as thy servant said, so it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 12:21; 2 Samuel 13:15"> And as soon as he had ended speaking, behold, the king's sons came, and lifted up their voice and wept; and the king also and all his servants wept very bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 14:32; 2 Samuel 13:34"> And Absalom fled, and went to Talmai, the son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. And &#91;David&#93; mourned for his son every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:8; 2 Samuel 14:23; 2 Samuel 14:32"> Now Absalom fled, and went to Geshur, and was there three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 38:12; Genesis 37:35"> And king David longed to go forth to Absalom; for he was comforted concerning Amnon, seeing he was dead.</span>
