<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 31:6; 1 Samuel 30:17–26"> And it came to pass after the death of Saul, when David had returned from the slaughter of the Amalekites, that David abode two days in Ziklag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> And it came to pass on the third day, that behold, a man came out of the camp from Saul with his garments rent, and earth upon his head; and as soon as he came to David, he fell to the earth and did obeisance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> And David said to him, Whence comest thou? And he said to him, Out of the camp of Israel am I escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> And David said to him, What has taken place? I pray thee, tell me. And he said that the people had fled from the battle, and many of the people also had fallen and died, and that Saul and Jonathan his son were dead also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> And David said to the young man that told him, How knowest thou that Saul and Jonathan his son are dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> And the young man that told him said, I happened by chance to be upon mount Gilboa, and behold, Saul leaned on his spear; and behold, the chariots and horsemen followed hard after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> And he looked behind him, and saw me, and called to me. And I said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> And he said to me, Who art thou? And I said to him, I am an Amalekite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He said to me again, Stand, I pray thee, over me, and slay me; for anguish has seized me; for my life is yet whole in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I stood over him, and put him to death, for I knew that he would not live after his fall; and I took the crown that was upon his head, and the bracelet that was on his arm, and have brought them hither to my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> Then David took hold of his garments and rent them; and all the men that were with him &#91;did&#93; likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> And they mourned, and wept, and fasted until even for Saul, and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of Jehovah, and for the house of Israel; because they were fallen by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> And David said to the young man that told him, Whence art thou? And he said, I am the son of an Amalekite stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> And David said to him, How wast thou not afraid to stretch forth thy hand to destroy Jehovah's anointed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> Then David called one of the young men and said, Draw near, &#91;and&#93; fall on him. And he smote him that he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> And David said to him, Thy blood be upon thy head; for thy mouth has testified against thee, saying, I have slain Jehovah's anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> And David lamented with this lamentation over Saul and over Jonathan his son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> and he bade them teach the children of Judah &#91;the song of&#93; the bow. Behold, it is written in the book of Jasher: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> The beauty of Israel is slain upon thy high places: how are the mighty fallen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> Tell &#91;it&#93; not in Gath, carry not the tidings in the streets of Ashkelon; Lest the daughters of the Philistines rejoice, Lest the daughters of the uncircumcised triumph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Ye mountains of Gilboa, let there be no dew, no rain upon you, nor fields of heave-offerings! For there the shield of the mighty was vilely cast away, The shield of Saul, &#91;as&#93; not anointed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> From the blood of the slain, from the fat of the mighty, The bow of Jonathan turned not back, And the sword of Saul returned not empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> Saul and Jonathan, beloved and pleasant in their lives, Even in their death were not divided; They were swifter than eagles, they were stronger than lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> Ye daughters of Israel, weep over Saul, who clothed you in scarlet with splendour, Who put ornaments of gold upon your apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> How are the mighty fallen in the midst of the battle! Jonathan is slain upon thy high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> I am distressed for thee, my brother Jonathan: very pleasant wast thou unto me; Thy love to me was wonderful, passing women's love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> How are the mighty fallen, and the instruments of war perished!</span>
