<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:39; 2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 18:33"> And Joab the son of Zeruiah perceived that the king's heart was toward Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:6; Amos 1:1; Ruth 3:3"> And Joab sent to Tekoah, and fetched thence a wise woman, and said to her, I pray thee, feign thyself to be a mourner, and put on mourning garments, I pray, and anoint not thyself with oil, but be as a woman that hath a long time mourned for the dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:19; Exodus 4:15; Isaiah 51:16"> and come to the king, and speak after this manner to him. And Joab put the words into her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:2; 2 Kings 6:26–28; 1 Samuel 25:23"> And the woman of Tekoah spoke to the king, and she fell on her face to the ground and did obeisance, and said, Save, O king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Samuel 12:1–3"> And the king said to her, What aileth thee? And she said, I am indeed a widow woman, and my husband is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Deuteronomy 22:26–27; Exodus 2:13"> And thy bondmaid had two sons, and they two strove together in the field, and there was none to part them, but the one smote the other and slew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Matthew 21:38; 2 Samuel 12:17"> And behold, the whole family is risen against thy bondmaid, and they say, Deliver him that smote his brother, that we may put him to death, for the life of his brother whom he killed; and we will destroy the heir also: so they will quench my coal which is left, and will not leave to my husband a name or remnant on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Isaiah 11:3–4; Job 29:16"> And the king said to the woman, Go to thy house, and I will give charge concerning thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:24; 1 Kings 2:33; Matthew 27:25"> And the woman of Tekoah said to the king, Upon me, my lord, O king, be the iniquity, and upon my father's house; and the king and his throne be guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the king said, Whoever speaks to thee, bring him to me, and he shall not touch thee any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; Matthew 10:30; Numbers 35:19"> Then she said, I pray thee, let the king remember Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, that thou wouldest not suffer the revengers of blood to destroy any more, lest they cut off my son. And he said, &#91;As&#93; Jehovah liveth, there shall not one hair of thy son fall to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 18:27; 1 Samuel 25:24; Acts 26:1"> And the woman said, Let thy bondmaid, I pray thee, speak a word to my lord the king. And he said, Speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:37–38; 2 Samuel 12:7; Judges 20:2"> And the woman said, Why then hast thou thought such a thing against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God's people? and the king in saying this thing, is as one guilty, in that the king does not bring back his banished one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 30:23; Numbers 35:28; Hebrews 9:27"> For we must needs die, and are as water spilt on the ground, which cannot be gathered up again; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God has not taken away his life, but devises means that the banished one be not expelled from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And now that I am come to speak of this thing to my lord the king, it is because the people have made me afraid; and thy bondmaid said, I will now speak to the king; perhaps the king will perform the request of his handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:19"> For the king will hear, to deliver his handmaid out of the hand of the man that would destroy me and my son together out of the inheritance of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:9; 2 Samuel 19:27; 2 Samuel 14:20"> And thy bondmaid said, Let the word of my lord the king now be comfortable; for as an angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, so is my lord the king to discern good and bad; and Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God will be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:25; 1 Samuel 3:17–18; Jeremiah 38:14"> And the king answered and said to the woman, Hide not from me, I pray thee, the thing that I shall ask thee. And the woman said, Let my lord the king now speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 1:26; 1 Samuel 20:3"> And the king said, Is the hand of Joab with thee in all this? And the woman answered and said, &#91;As&#93; thy soul liveth, my lord, O king, there is no turning to the right hand or to the left from aught that my lord the king has spoken; for thy servant Joab, he bade me, and he put all these words into the mouth of thy bondmaid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 19:27; Job 32:21–22"> in order to turn the appearance of the thing has thy servant Joab done this thing; but my lord is wise, according to the wisdom of an angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="14.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, to know all that is in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:11; 1 Samuel 14:39; Mark 6:26"> And the king said to Joab, Behold now, I have done this thing: so go, bring back the young man Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:2; Job 31:20; Job 29:11"> And Joab fell to the ground on his face and bowed himself, and blessed the king; and Joab said, To-day thy servant knows that I have found favour in thy sight, my lord, O king, in that the king has fulfilled the request of his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 13:37–38"> And Joab arose and went to Geshur, and brought Absalom to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Genesis 43:3; 2 Samuel 3:13"> And the king said, Let him turn to his own house, and let him not see my face. And Absalom withdrew to his own house, and saw not the king's face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:35; Job 2:7"> But in all Israel there was none to be so much praised as Absalom for his beauty: from the sole of his foot even to the crown of his head there was no blemish in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:20; 1 Corinthians 11:14; 2 Samuel 18:9"> And when he shaved his head (for it was at every year's end that he shaved it, because it was heavy on him, therefore he shaved it), he weighed the hair of his head at two hundred shekels after the king's weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; 2 Samuel 13:1; Isaiah 14:22"> And to Absalom there were born three sons, and one daughter, whose name was Tamar: she was a woman of a beautiful countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Absalom dwelt two full years in Jerusalem, and did not see the king's face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30–31; Esther 1:12; Matthew 22:3"> Then Absalom sent for Joab, to send him to the king; but he would not come to him; and he sent again the second time, but he would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 15:4–5; 2 Kings 10:6–7; 2 Kings 9:33"> Then he said to his servants, See, Joab's allotment is near mine and he has barley there: go and set it on fire. And Absalom's servants set the allotment on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then Joab arose, and came to Absalom to &#91;his&#93; house, and said to him, Why have thy servants set my allotment on fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8; Exodus 14:12; Proverbs 28:13"> And Absalom said to Joab, Behold, I sent to thee, saying, Come hither, that I may send thee to the king, to say, Why am I come from Geshur? it would have been better for me to be there still. And now let me see the king's face; and if there be iniquity in me, let him slay me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Luke 15:20; Genesis 27:26"> And Joab came to the king, and told him. And he called Absalom, and he came to the king, and bowed himself on his face to the ground before the king; and the king kissed Absalom.</span>
