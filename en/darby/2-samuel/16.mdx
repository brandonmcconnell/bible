<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 15:32; 2 Samuel 9:2–13"> And when David was a little past the summit, behold, Ziba, Mephibosheth's servant, met him, with a couple of asses saddled, and upon them two hundred &#91;loaves&#93; of bread, and a hundred raisin-cakes, and a hundred cakes of summer fruits, and a flask of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:29; Judges 10:4; Judges 5:10"> And the king said to Ziba, What meanest thou by these? And Ziba said, The asses are for the king's household to ride on; and the bread and summer fruits for the young men to eat; and the wine, that such as are faint in the wilderness may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:9–10; Psalms 15:3; 2 Samuel 19:24–30"> And the king said, And where is thy master's son? And Ziba said to the king, Behold, he abides at Jerusalem; for he said, To-day shall the house of Israel restore me the kingdom of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 19:2"> And the king said to Ziba, Behold, thine are all that pertained to Mephibosheth. And Ziba said, I humbly bow myself: may I find favour in thy sight, my lord, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 1 Samuel 17:43; Exodus 22:28"> And when king David came to Bahurim, behold, there came out from thence a man of the family of the house of Saul, whose name was Shimei, the son of Gera: he came forth, and cursed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and cast stones at David, and at all the servants of king David; and all the people and all the mighty men were on his right hand and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:17; 1 Kings 21:10"> And thus said Shimei as he cursed: Away, away, thou man of blood and man of Belial!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Kings 2:32–33"> Jehovah has returned upon thee all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose stead thou hast reigned; and Jehovah has given the kingdom into the hand of Absalom thy son; and behold, thou art &#91;taken&#93; in thine own evil, for thou art a man of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; 2 Samuel 9:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> And Abishai the son of Zeruiah said to the king, Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? let me go over, I pray thee, and take off his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; Romans 9:20; John 18:11"> And the king said, What have I to do with you, ye sons of Zeruiah? so let him curse, for Jehovah has said to him, Curse David! Who shall then say, Why dost thou so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:1–4; Genesis 45:5; 2 Samuel 12:11–12"> And David said to Abishai, and to all his servants, Behold, my son, who came forth of my bowels, seeks my life: how much more now a Benjaminite? let him alone and let him curse; for Jehovah has bidden him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Hebrews 12:10; Psalms 25:18"> It may be that Jehovah will look on mine affliction, and that Jehovah will requite me good for my being cursed this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 23:23; 2 Samuel 16:5–6"> And David and his men went by the way; and Shimei went along on the hill's side over against him, and cursed as he went, and threw stones at him, and cast dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the king, and all the people that were with him, came weary, and refreshed themselves there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:12"> Now Absalom, and all the people, the men of Israel, came to Jerusalem, and Ahithophel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 11:12; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:25"> And it came to pass, when Hushai the Archite, David's friend, came to Absalom, that Hushai said to Absalom, Long live the king! Long live the king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:25; 2 Samuel 15:32–37; Deuteronomy 32:6"> And Absalom said to Hushai, Is this thy kindness to thy friend? why didst thou not go with thy friend?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13; 2 Samuel 5:1–3"> And Hushai said to Absalom, No; but whom Jehovah, and this people, and all the men of Israel choose, his will I be, and with him will I abide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:34; Psalms 55:21; 1 Samuel 28:2"> And again, whom should I serve? should it not be in the presence of his son? as I have served in thy father's presence, so will I be in thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Psalms 37:12–13; Isaiah 29:15"> And Absalom said to Ahithophel, Give counsel among you what we shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:3; 2 Samuel 15:16; Zechariah 8:13"> And Ahithophel said to Absalom, Go in to thy father's concubines, whom he has left to keep the house; and all Israel shall hear that thou art become odious with thy father; and the hands of all that are with thee shall be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11–12; 2 Samuel 15:16; 2 Samuel 20:3"> So they spread a tent for Absalom upon the roof; and Absalom went in to his father's concubines in the sight of all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 17:14; 2 Samuel 17:23"> And the counsel of Ahithophel, which he counselled in those days, was as if a man had inquired of the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God: so was all the counsel of Ahithophel both with David and with Absalom.</span>
