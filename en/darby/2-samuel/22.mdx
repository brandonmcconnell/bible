<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Judges 5:1; Isaiah 12:1–6"> And David spoke to Jehovah the words of this song in the day that Jehovah had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:2–50; Psalms 31:3; Psalms 71:3"> And he said, Jehovah is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Isaiah 12:2; Proverbs 18:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God is my rock, in him will I trust — My shield, and the horn of my salvation, My high tower, and my refuge, My saviour: thou wilt save me from violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:3; Psalms 48:1; Psalms 96:4"> I will call upon Jehovah, who is to be praised; So shall I be saved from mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Revelation 17:15"> For the waves of death encompassed me, Torrents of Belial made me afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Proverbs 13:14; Psalms 140:5"> The bands of Sheol surrounded me; The cords of death encountered me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 116:4; Exodus 3:7"> In my distress I called upon Jehovah, And I cried to my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; And he heard my voice out of his temple, And my cry &#91;came&#93; into his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 26:11; Psalms 97:4; Judges 5:4"> Then the earth shook, and quaked; The foundations of the heavens trembled And shook because he was wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 18:15; Psalms 18:8"> There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, And fire out of his mouth devoured: Coals burned forth from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 144:5"> And he bowed the heavens, and came down; And darkness was under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly; And he was seen upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; 2 Samuel 22:10; Job 36:29"> And he made darkness round about him a tent, Gatherings of waters, thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:9"> From the brightness before him Burned forth coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; 1 Samuel 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> Jehovah thundered from the heavens, And the Most High uttered his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Habakkuk 3:11"> And he sent arrows, and scattered &#91;mine enemies&#93;; Lightning, and discomfited them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 1:4; Habakkuk 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And the beds of the sea were seen, The foundations of the world were uncovered At the rebuke of Jehovah, At the blast of the breath of his nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He reached forth from above, he took me, He drew me out of great waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivered me from my strong enemy, From them that hated me; For they were mightier than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> They encountered me in the day of my calamity; But Jehovah was my stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> And he brought me forth into a large place; He delivered me, because he delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> Jehovah hath rewarded me according to my righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not wickedly departed from my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all his ordinances were before me, And his statutes, I did not depart from them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> And I was upright before him, And kept myself from mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> And Jehovah hath recompensed me according to my righteousness, According to my cleanness in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the gracious thou dost shew thyself gracious; With the upright man thou dost shew thyself upright;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 18:26; Leviticus 26:23–28"> With the pure thou dost shew thyself pure; And with the perverse thou dost shew thyself contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And the afflicted people thou dost save; And thine eyes are upon the haughty, &#91;whom&#93; thou bringest down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For thou art my lamp, Jehovah; And Jehovah enlighteneth my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For by thee I have run through a troop; By my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="22.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God have I leaped over a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="22.31 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God, his way is perfect; The word of Jehovah is tried: He is a shield to all that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremiah 10:6–7"> For who is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="22.32 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">f</a></sup>God, save Jehovah? And who is a rock, save our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="22.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="22.33 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">h</a></sup>God is my strong fortress, And he maketh my way perfectly smooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Isaiah 58:14; Habakkuk 3:19"> He maketh my feet like hinds' &#91;feet&#93;, And setteth me upon my high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> He teacheth my hands to war, And mine arms bend a bow of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> And thou didst give me the shield of thy salvation, And thy condescending gentleness hath made me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Thou enlargedst my steps under me; And mine ankles did not slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them, And I turned not again till they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> And I have consumed them and have crushed them, and they rose not again; Yea, they fell under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> And thou girdedst me with strength to battle: Thou didst subdue under me those that rose up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> And mine enemies didst thou make to turn their backs unto me, And those that hated me I destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They looked, and there was none to save — Unto Jehovah, and he answered them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> And I did beat them small as the dust of the earth, I trod them as the mire of the streets; I stamped upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> And thou hast delivered me from the strivings of my people, Thou hast kept me to be head of the nations: A people I knew not doth serve me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> Strangers come cringing unto me: At the hearing of the ear, they obey me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> Strangers have faded away, And they come trembling forth from their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> Jehovah liveth; and blessed be my rock; And exalted be the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="22.47 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, the rock of my salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="22.48 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">j</a></sup>God who hath avenged me, And hath brought the peoples under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> He brought me forth from mine enemies: Yea, thou hast lifted me up above them that rose up against me; From the man of violence hast thou delivered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Romans 15:9; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 145:1–2"> Therefore will I give thanks to thee, Jehovah, among the nations, And will sing psalms to thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 89:29; Psalms 18:50; Psalms 89:20"> &#91;It is he&#93; who giveth great deliverances to his king, And sheweth loving-kindness to his anointed, To David, and to his seed for evermore.</span>
