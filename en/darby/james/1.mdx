<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:15; 1 Peter 1:1; John 7:35"> James, bondman of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and of &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which &#91;are&#93; in the dispersion, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Count it all joy, my brethren, when ye fall into various temptations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> knowing that the proving of your faith works endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> But let endurance have &#91;its&#93; perfect work, that ye may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; John 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> But if any one of you lack wisdom, let him ask of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, who gives to all freely and reproaches not, and it shall be given to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> but let him ask in faith, nothing doubting. For he that doubts is like a wave of the sea driven by the wind and tossed about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> for let not that man think that he shall receive anything from the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; James 4:8"> &#91;he is&#93; a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; James 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> But let the brother of low degree glory in his elevation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 John 2:17; Job 14:2"> and the rich in his humiliation, because as &#91;the&#93; grass's flower he will pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 49:6–14"> For the sun has risen with its burning heat, and has withered the grass, and its flower has fallen, and the comeliness of its look has perished: thus the rich also shall wither in his goings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Blessed &#91;is the&#93; man who endures temptation; for, having been proved, he shall receive the crown of life, which He has promised to them that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; James 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> Let no man, being tempted, say, I am tempted of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God. For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.13 Elohim">d</a></sup>God cannot be tempted by evil things, and himself tempts no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> But every one is tempted, drawn away, and enticed by his own lust;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> then lust, having conceived, gives birth to sin; but sin fully completed brings forth death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; James 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> Do not err, my beloved brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> Every good gift and every perfect gift comes down from above, from the Father of lights, with whom is no variation nor shadow of turning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:3–5"> According to his own will begat he us by the word of truth, that we should be a certain first-fruits of his creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> So that, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; James 3:17–18"> for man's wrath does not work<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.20 Elohim">e</a></sup>God's righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> Wherefore, laying aside all filthiness and abounding of wickedness, accept with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; John 13:17"> But be ye doers of &#91;the&#93; word and not hearers only, beguiling yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; James 2:14–26"> For if any man be a hearer of &#91;the&#93; word and not a doer, he is like to a man considering his natural face in a mirror:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> for he has considered himself and is gone away, and straightway he has forgotten what he was like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; John 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But he that fixes his view on &#91;the&#93; perfect law, that of liberty, and abides in &#91;it&#93;, being not a forgetful hearer but a doer of &#91;the&#93; work, he shall be blessed in his doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Psalms 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If any one think himself to be religious, not bridling his tongue, but deceiving his heart, this man's religion is vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 John 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> Pure and undefiled religion before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.27 Elohim">f</a></sup>God and the Father is this: to visit orphans and widows in their affliction, to keep oneself unspotted from the world.</span>
</div>
