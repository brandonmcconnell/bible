<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> Be not many teachers, my brethren, knowing that we shall receive greater judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For we all often offend. If any one offend not in word, he &#91;is&#93; a perfect man, able to bridle the whole body too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> Behold, we put the bits in the mouths of the horses, that they may obey us, and we turn round their whole bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Behold also the ships, which are so great, and driven by violent winds, are turned about by a very small rudder, wherever the pleasure of the helmsman will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> Thus also the tongue is a little member, and boasts great things. See how little a fire, how large a wood it kindles!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> and the tongue &#91;is&#93; fire, the world of unrighteousness; the tongue is set in our members, the defiler of the whole body, and which sets fire to the course of nature, and is set on fire of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every species both of beasts and of birds, both of creeping things and of sea animals, is tamed and has been tamed by the human species;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> but the tongue can no one among men tame; &#91;it is&#93; an unsettled evil, full of death-bringing poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> Therewith bless we the Lord and Father, and therewith curse we men made after &#91;the&#93; likeness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> Out of the same mouth goes forth blessing and cursing. It is not right, my brethren, that these things should be thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Does the fountain, out of the same opening, pour forth sweet and bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> Can, my brethren, a fig produce olives, or a vine figs? Neither &#91;can&#93; salt &#91;water&#93; make sweet water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who &#91;is&#93; wise and understanding among you; let him shew out of a good conversation his works in meekness of wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> but if ye have bitter emulation and strife in your hearts, do not boast and lie against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This is not the wisdom which comes down from above, but earthly, natural, devilish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where emulation and strife &#91;are&#93;, there &#91;is&#93; disorder and every evil thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom from above first is pure, then peaceful, gentle, yielding, full of mercy and good fruits, unquestioning, unfeigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> But &#91;the&#93; fruit of righteousness in peace is sown for them that make peace.</span>
