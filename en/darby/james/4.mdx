<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> Ye lust and have not: ye kill and are full of envy, and cannot obtain; ye fight and war; ye have not because ye ask not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> Ye ask and receive not, because ye ask evilly, that ye may consume &#91;it&#93; in your pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> Adulteresses, know ye not that friendship with the world is enmity with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God? Whoever therefore is minded to be &#91;the&#93; friend of the world is constituted enemy of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Think ye that the scripture speaks in vain? Does the Spirit which has taken his abode in us desire enviously?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But he gives more grace. Wherefore he says,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">c</a></sup>God sets himself against &#91;the&#93; proud, but gives grace to &#91;the&#93; lowly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> Subject yourselves therefore to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">d</a></sup>God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Draw near to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse &#91;your&#93; hands, sinners, and purify &#91;your&#93; hearts, ye double-minded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Be wretched, and mourn, and weep: let your laughter be turned to mourning, and &#91;your&#93; joy to heaviness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Humble yourselves before &#91;the&#93; Lord, and he shall exalt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Speak not against one another, brethren. He that speaks against &#91;his&#93; brother, or judges his brother, speaks against &#91;the&#93; law and judges &#91;the&#93; law. But if thou judgest &#91;the&#93; law, thou art not doer of &#91;the&#93; law, but judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> One is the lawgiver and judge, who is able to save and to destroy: but who art thou who judgest thy neighbour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Go to now, ye who say, To-day or to-morrow will we go into such a city and spend a year there, and traffic and make gain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalms 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> ye who do not know what will be on the morrow, (&#91;for&#93; what &#91;is&#93; your life? It is even a vapour, appearing for a little while, and then disappearing,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> instead of your saying, If the Lord should &#91;so&#93; will and we should live, we will also do this or that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalms 52:7"> But now ye glory in your vauntings: all such glorying is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> To him therefore who knows how to do good, and does it not, to him it is sin.</span>
