<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; John 17:3; John 10:28"> Paul, apostle of Jesus Christ by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's will, according to promise of life, the &#91;life&#93; which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:1; 1 Timothy 1:2; Acts 16:1"> to Timotheus, &#91;my&#93; beloved child: grace, mercy, peace, from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father, and Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Hebrews 13:8; Acts 23:1"> I am thankful to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, whom I serve from &#91;my&#93; forefathers with pure conscience, how unceasingly I have the remembrance of thee in my supplications night and day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–1"> earnestly desiring to see thee, remembering thy tears, that I may be filled with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Acts 16:1; 2 Timothy 3:15"> calling to mind the unfeigned faith which &#91;has been&#93; in thee, which dwelt first in thy grandmother Lois, and in thy mother Eunice, and I am persuaded that in thee also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8"> For which cause I put thee in mind to rekindle the gift of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">d</a></sup>God which is in thee by the putting on of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:18; Galatians 5:22"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.7 Elohim">e</a></sup>God has not given us a spirit of cowardice, but of power, and of love, and of wise discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Mark 8:38; 1 Peter 4:13–15"> Be not therefore ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner; but suffer evil along with the glad tidings, according to the power of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">f</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; Titus 3:4–5; Ephesians 1:3–4"> who has saved us, and has called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to &#91;his&#93; own purpose and grace, which &#91;was&#93; given to us in Christ Jesus before &#91;the&#93; ages of time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:2; Romans 16:26; 2 Peter 3:18"> but has been made manifest now by the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who has annulled death, and brought to light life and incorruptibility by the glad tidings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:7–8; Acts 9:15"> to which I have been appointed a herald and apostle and teacher of &#91;the&#93; nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; Jude 1:24; 1 Peter 4:19"> For which cause also I suffer these things; but I am not ashamed; for I know whom I have believed, and am persuaded that he is able to keep for that day the deposit I have entrusted to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 1:9; 2 Timothy 3:14; Philippians 4:9"> Have an outline of sound words, which &#91;words&#93; thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which &#91;are&#93; in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:17; Romans 8:13; 1 Timothy 6:20"> Keep, by the Holy Spirit which dwells in us, the good deposit entrusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:16; Philippians 2:21; Acts 19:10"> Thou knowest this, that all who &#91;are&#93; in Asia, of whom is Phygellus and Hermogenes, have turned away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:19; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> The Lord grant mercy to the house of Onesiphorus, for he has often refreshed me, and has not been ashamed of my chain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 28:30–31"> but being in Rome sought me out very diligently, and found &#91;me&#93; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Timothy 1:3"> the Lord grant to him to find mercy from &#91;the&#93; Lord in that day — and how much service he rendered in Ephesus thou knowest best.</span>
