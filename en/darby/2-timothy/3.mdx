<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> But this know, that in &#91;the&#93; last days difficult times shall be there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:21; Romans 1:29–31; Jude 1:16"> for men shall be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, evil speakers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, profane,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; Romans 1:31; Matthew 10:21"> without natural affection, implacable, slanderers, of unsubdued passions, savage, having no love for what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; 1 Timothy 3:6; Jude 1:4"> traitors, headlong, of vain pretensions, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 23:27–28"> having a form of piety but denying the power of it: and from these turn away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 3:17; Titus 3:3"> For of these are they who are getting into houses, and leading captive silly women, laden with sins, led by various lusts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 2:4"> always learning, and never able to come to &#91;the&#93; knowledge of &#91;the&#93; truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:8–11; Revelation 2:20; 1 Timothy 6:5"> Now in the same manner in which Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, thus these also withstand the truth; men corrupted in mind, found worthless as regards the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 7:12; Jeremiah 28:15–17"> But they shall not advance farther; for their folly shall be completely manifest to all, as that of those also became.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Titus 2:7; 2 Peter 1:5–7"> But thou hast been thoroughly acquainted with my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, longsuffering, love, endurance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28"> persecutions, sufferings: what &#91;sufferings&#93; happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium, in Lystra; what persecutions I endured; and the Lord delivered me out of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:12–16; 1 Peter 2:20–21"> And all indeed who desire to live piously in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1; 2 Peter 2:20; Titus 3:3"> But wicked men and juggling impostors shall advance in evil, leading and being led astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 2 Timothy 2:2; 2 Timothy 1:13"> But thou, abide in those things which thou hast learned, and &#91;of which&#93; thou hast been fully persuaded, knowing of whom thou hast learned &#91;them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:98–99; Luke 24:45; Proverbs 22:6"> and that from a child thou hast known the sacred letters, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation, through faith which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Hebrews 4:12; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Every scripture &#91;is&#93; divinely inspired, and profitable for teaching, for conviction, for correction, for instruction in righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:21; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> that the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">b</a></sup>God may be complete, fully fitted to every good work.</span>
