<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> Thou therefore, my child, be strong in the grace which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> And the things thou hast heard of me in the presence of many witnesses, these entrust to faithful men, such as shall be competent to instruct others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Take thy share in suffering as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No one going as a soldier entangles himself with the affairs of life, that he may please him who has enlisted him as a soldier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And if also any one contend &#91;in the games&#93;, he is not crowned unless he contend lawfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The husbandman must labour before partaking of the fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Think of what I say, for the Lord will give thee understanding in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember Jesus Christ raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead, of &#91;the&#93; seed of David, according to my glad tidings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> in which I suffer even unto bonds as an evil-doer: but the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.9 Elohim">a</a></sup>God is not bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> For this cause I endure all things for the sake of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> The word &#91;is&#93; faithful; for if we have died together with &#91;him&#93;, we shall also live together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> if we endure, we shall also reign together; if we deny, he also will deny us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> if we are unfaithful, he abides faithful, for he cannot deny himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Of these things put in remembrance, testifying earnestly before the Lord not to have disputes of words, profitable for nothing, to the subversion of the hearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Strive diligently to present thyself approved to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.15 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, a workman that has not to be ashamed, cutting in a straight line the word of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But profane, vain babblings shun, for they will advance to greater impiety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and their word will spread as a gangrene; of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> &#91;men&#93; who as to the truth have gone astray, saying that the resurrection has taken place already; and overthrow the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> Yet the firm foundation of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.19 Elohim">c</a></sup>God stands, having this seal, &#91;The&#93; Lord knows those that are his; and, Let every one who names the name of &#91;the&#93; Lord withdraw from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> But in a great house there are not only gold and silver vessels, but also wooden and earthen; and some to honour, and some to dishonour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> If therefore one shall have purified himself from these, &#91;in separating himself from them&#93;, he shall be a vessel to honour, sanctified, serviceable to the Master, prepared for every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> But youthful lusts flee, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace, with those that call upon the Lord out of a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But foolish and senseless questionings avoid, knowing that they beget contentions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> And a bondman of &#91;the&#93; Lord ought not to contend, but be gentle towards all; apt to teach; forbearing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> in meekness setting right those who oppose, if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.25 Elohim">d</a></sup>God perhaps may sometime give them repentance to acknowledgment of &#91;the&#93; truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> and that they may awake up out of the snare of the devil, &#91;who are&#93; taken by him, for his will.</span>
