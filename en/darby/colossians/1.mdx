<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Philemon 1:1"> Paul, apostle of Christ Jesus, by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's will, and Timotheus the brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:2; Ephesians 1:1; Romans 1:7"> to the holy and faithful brethren in Christ which &#91;are&#93; in Colosse. Grace to you and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God our Father &#91;and Lord Jesus Christ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Colossians 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> We give thanks to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ continually &#91;when&#93; praying for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:15; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> having heard of your faith in Christ Jesus, and the love which ye have towards all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3–4; Ephesians 1:13; 2 Timothy 4:8"> on account of the hope which &#91;is&#93; laid up for you in the heavens; of which ye heard before in the word of the truth of the glad tidings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Philippians 1:11; Colossians 1:23"> which are come to you, as &#91;they are&#93; in all the world, &#91;and&#93; are bearing fruit and growing, even as also among you, from the day ye heard &#91;them&#93; and knew indeed the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, in truth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; Philemon 1:23; Colossians 4:7"> even as ye learned from Epaphras our beloved fellow-bondman, who is a faithful minister of Christ for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 15:30; 2 Timothy 1:7; Galatians 5:22"> who has also manifested to us your love in &#91;the&#93; Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 3:17; Philippians 1:9–11; James 1:5"> For this reason we also, from the day we heard &#91;of your faith and love&#93;, do not cease praying and asking for you, to the end that ye may be filled with the full knowledge of his will, in all wisdom and spiritual understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; Hebrews 13:21; 2 Peter 1:2–3"> &#91;so as&#93; to walk worthily of the Lord unto all well-pleasing, bearing fruit in every good work, and growing by the true knowledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.10 Elohim">e</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 4:2"> strengthened with all power according to the might of his glory unto all endurance and longsuffering with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:32; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:18"> giving thanks to the Father, who has made us fit for sharing the portion of the saints in light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; 1 Peter 2:9"> who has delivered us from the authority of darkness, and translated &#91;us&#93; into the kingdom of the Son of his love:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Romans 3:24–25; 1 Peter 1:19–20"> in whom we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; John 14:9; John 1:1"> who is image of the invisible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.15 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, firstborn of all creation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> because by him were created all things, the things in the heavens and the things upon the earth, the visible and the invisible, whether thrones, or lordships, or principalities, or authorities: all things have been created by him and for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Acts 17:28"> And he is before all, and all things subsist together by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:22–23; Revelation 1:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> And he is the head of the body, the assembly; who is &#91;the&#93; beginning, firstborn from among the dead, that he might have the first place in all things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 2:9; John 1:16; Ephesians 1:23"> for in him all the fulness &#91;of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.19 Elohim">g</a></sup>Godhead&#93; was pleased to dwell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–21; Ephesians 1:10; 1 John 4:9–10"> and by him to reconcile all things to itself, having made peace by the blood of his cross — by him, whether the things on the earth or the things in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Titus 3:3–7; Ephesians 2:1–3"> And you, who once were alienated and enemies in mind by wicked works, yet now has it reconciled</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jude 1:24; Ephesians 1:4"> in the body of his flesh through death; to present you holy and unblamable and irreproachable before it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; Matthew 24:13–14"> if indeed ye abide in the faith founded and firm, and not moved away from the hope of the glad tidings, which ye have heard, which have been proclaimed in the whole creation which &#91;is&#93; under heaven, of which I Paul became minister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 5:3; 2 Timothy 1:8; 2 Corinthians 1:5–8"> Now, I rejoice in sufferings for you, and I fill up that which is behind of the tribulations of Christ in my flesh, for his body, which is the assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 3:2; Colossians 1:23; Galatians 2:7–8"> of which I became minister, according to the dispensation of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.25 Elohim">h</a></sup>God which &#91;is&#93; given me towards you to complete the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.25 Elohim">i</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:10; Romans 16:25–26; Ephesians 3:3–10"> the mystery which &#91;has been&#93; hidden from ages and from generations, but has now been made manifest to his saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:20; Romans 8:10; Ephesians 3:16–17"> to whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.27 Elohim">j</a></sup>God would make known what are the riches of the glory of this mystery among the nations, which is Christ in you the hope of glory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jeremiah 3:15; Ephesians 4:11–13"> whom we announce, admonishing every man, and teaching every man, in all wisdom, to the end that we may present every man perfect in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Ephesians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> Whereunto also I toil, combating according to his working, which works in me in power.</span>
