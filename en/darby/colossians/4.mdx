<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21–23; Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12"> Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister and fellow-bondman in &#91;the&#93; Lord, will make known to you all that concerns me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:22; Colossians 2:2; Philippians 2:28"> whom I have sent to you for this very purpose, that he might know your state, and that he might encourage your hearts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philemon 1:10–19; Colossians 4:7"> with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is &#91;one&#93; of you. They shall make known to you everything here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 19:29"> Aristarchus my fellow-captive salutes you, and Mark, Barnabas's cousin, concerning whom ye have received orders, (if he come to you, receive him,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 11:2; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 10:45"> and Jesus called Justus, who are of the circumcision. These &#91;are the&#93; only fellow-workers for the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God who have been a consolation to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:30; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:7"> Epaphras, who is &#91;one&#93; of you, &#91;the&#93; bondman of Christ Jesus, salutes you, always combating earnestly for you in prayers, to the end that ye may stand perfect and complete in all &#91;the&#93; will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.12 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 2:1; Romans 10:2; Revelation 1:11"> For I bear him witness that he labours much for you, and them in Laodicea, and them in Hierapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:10–11"> Luke, the beloved physician, salutes you, and Demas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:5; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Salute the brethren in Laodicea, and Nymphas, and the assembly which &#91;is&#93; in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:14"> And when the letter has been read among you, cause that it be read also in the assembly of Laodiceans, and that ye also read that from Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philemon 1:2; Acts 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> And say to Archippus, Take heed to the ministry which thou hast received in &#91;the&#93; Lord, to the end that thou fulfil it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; 2 Timothy 4:22; 1 Timothy 6:21"> The salutation by the hand of me Paul. Remember my bonds. Grace &#91;be&#93; with you.</span>
