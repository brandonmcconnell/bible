<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 8:6; Matthew 6:33; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> If therefore ye have been raised with the Christ, seek the things &#91;which are&#93; above, where the Christ is, sitting at &#91;the&#93; right hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Chronicles 22:19; Colossians 3:1"> have your mind on the things &#91;that are&#93; above, not on the things &#91;that are&#93; on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:2; Colossians 2:20"> for ye have died, and your life is hid with the Christ in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:25; 1 John 3:2; Galatians 2:20"> When the Christ is manifested who &#91;is&#93; our life, then shall ye also be manifested with him in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:19–21; Galatians 5:24"> Put to death therefore your members which &#91;are&#93; upon the earth, fornication, uncleanness, vile passions, evil lust, and unbridled desire, which is idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18; 1 Peter 1:14"> On account of which things the wrath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.6 Elohim">c</a></sup>God comes upon the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:2; 1 Peter 4:3–4; Romans 6:19–20"> In which ye also once walked when ye lived in these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Ephesians 4:22; James 1:20–21"> But now, put off, ye also, all &#91;these&#93; things, wrath, anger, malice, blasphemy, vile language out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 4:25; Leviticus 19:11"> Do not lie to one another, having put off the old man with his deeds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:23–24; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 2:10"> and having put on the new, renewed into full knowledge according to &#91;the&#93; image of him that has created him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 10:12"> wherein there is not Greek and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, Scythian, bondman, freeman; but Christ &#91;is&#93; everything, and in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Ephesians 4:2; 1 John 4:19"> Put on therefore, as &#91;the&#93; elect of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, holy and beloved, bowels of compassion, kindness, lowliness, meekness, longsuffering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; James 2:13"> forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any should have a complaint against any; even as the Christ has forgiven you, so also &#91;do&#93; ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 13:1–13"> And to all these &#91;add&#93; love, which is the bond of perfectness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; John 14:27; Isaiah 26:3"> And let the peace of Christ preside in your hearts, to which also ye have been called in one body, and be thankful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Ephesians 5:19; John 15:7"> Let the word of the Christ dwell in you richly, in all wisdom teaching and admonishing one another, in psalms, hymns, spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.16 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossians 3:23; Proverbs 3:6"> And everything, whatever ye may do in word or in deed, &#91;do&#93; all things in &#91;the&#93; name of &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus, giving thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">f</a></sup>God the Father by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:22–9; Titus 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Wives, be subject to &#91;your&#93; husbands, as is fitting in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Genesis 2:23–24; Colossians 3:21"> Husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well-pleasing in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 103:13"> Fathers, do not vex your children, to the end that they be not disheartened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:5–7; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Galatians 1:10"> Bondmen, obey in all things your masters according to flesh; not with eye-services, as men-pleasers, but in simplicity of heart, fearing the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Ephesians 6:6–7; Romans 14:8"> Whatsoever ye do, labour at it heartily, as &#91;doing it&#93; to the Lord, and not to men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 6:8; Galatians 1:10; Acts 20:32"> knowing that of &#91;the&#93; Lord ye shall receive the recompense of the inheritance; ye serve the Lord Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For he that does a wrong shall receive the wrong he has done, and there is no respect of persons.</span>
