<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12; Galatians 3:26; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> See what love the Father has given to us, that we should be called &#91;the&#93; children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God. For this reason the world knows us not, because it knew him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Beloved, now are we children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, and what we shall be has not yet been manifested; we know that if it is manifested we shall be like him, for we shall see him as he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 John 2:6; 2 Peter 3:14"> And every one that has this hope in him purifies himself, even as he is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 5:17; Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15"> Every one that practises sin practises also lawlessness; and sin is lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> And ye know that he has been manifested that he might take away our sins; and in him sin is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> Whoever abides in him, does not sin: whoever sins, has not seen him or known him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> Children, let no man lead you astray; he that practises righteousness is righteous, even as he is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> He that practises sin is of the devil; for from &#91;the&#93; beginning the devil sins. To this end the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.8 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has been manifested, that he might undo the works of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Whoever has been begotten of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">d</a></sup>God does not practise sin, because his seed abides in him, and he cannot sin, because he has been begotten of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> In this are manifest the children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.10 Elohim">f</a></sup>God and the children of the devil. Whoever does not practise righteousness is not of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.10 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, and he who does not love his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> For this is the message which ye have heard from the beginning, that we should love one another:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> not as Cain was of the wicked one, and slew his brother; and on account of what slew he him? because his works were wicked, and those of his brother righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> Do not wonder, brethren, if the world hate you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have passed from death to life, because we love the brethren. He who does not love &#91;his&#93; brother abides in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Every one that hates his brother is a murderer, and ye know that no murderer has eternal life abiding in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> Hereby we have known love, because he has laid down his life for us; and we ought for the brethren to lay down &#91;our&#93; lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> But whoso may have the world's substance, and see his brother having need, and shut up his bowels from him, how abides the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">h</a></sup>God in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> Children, let us not love with word, nor with tongue, but in deed and in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> And hereby we shall know that we are of the truth, and shall persuade our hearts before him —</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> that if our heart condemn us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.20 Elohim">i</a></sup>God is greater than our heart and knows all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Beloved, if our heart condemn us not, we have boldness towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.21 Elohim">j</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> and whatsoever we ask we receive from him, because we keep his commandments, and practise the things which are pleasing in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> And this is his commandment, that we believe on the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and that we love one another, even as he has given us commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> And he that keeps his commandments abides in him, and he in him. And hereby we know that he abides in us, by the Spirit which he has given to us.</span>
