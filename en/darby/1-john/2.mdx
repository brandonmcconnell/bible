<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Romans 8:34; John 14:6"> My children, these things I write to you in order that ye may not sin; and if any one sin, we have a patron with the Father, Jesus Christ &#91;the&#93; righteous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:14; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 4:10"> and he is the propitiation for our sins; but not for ours alone, but also for the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; Luke 6:46"> And hereby we know that we know him, if we keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Titus 1:16; James 2:14–16"> He that says, I know him, and does not keep his commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; 1 John 4:12–13"> but whoever keeps his word, in him verily the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God is perfected. Hereby we know that we are in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21; John 13:15; 1 Corinthians 11:1"> He that says he abides in him ought, even as he walked, himself also &#91;so&#93; to walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Leviticus 19:18; Mark 12:29–34"> Beloved, I write no new commandment to you, but an old commandment, which ye have had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word which ye heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; John 13:34"> Again, I write a new commandment to you, which thing is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing and the true light already shines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:11; 1 John 3:13–17"> He who says he is in the light, and hates his brother, is in the darkness until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 3:14; Romans 9:32–33; Romans 14:13"> He that loves his brother abides in light, and there is no occasion of stumbling in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:35; Titus 3:3; 1 John 1:6"> But he that hates his brother is in the darkness, and walks in the darkness, and knows not where he goes, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Acts 13:38; Ephesians 1:7"> I write to you, children, because &#91;your&#93; sins are forgiven you for his name's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:7; 1 John 2:14; Luke 10:22"> I write to you, fathers, because ye have known him &#91;that is&#93; from the beginning. I write to you, young men, because ye have overcome the wicked &#91;one&#93;. I write to you, little children, because ye have known the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 1 John 2:13; Colossians 3:16"> I have written to you, fathers, because ye have known him &#91;that is&#93; from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because ye are strong, and the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.14 Elohim">b</a></sup>God abides in you, and ye have overcome the wicked &#91;one&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 4:4; Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:24"> Love not the world, nor the things in the world. If any one love the world, the love of the Father is not in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 13:14; Galatians 5:17; Psalms 119:36–37"> because all that &#91;is&#93; in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 7:31"> And the world is passing, and its lust, but he that does the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">c</a></sup>God abides for eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7; Matthew 24:24"> Little children, it is &#91;the&#93; last hour, and, according as ye have heard that antichrist comes, even now there have come many antichrists, whence we know that it is &#91;the&#93; last hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 20:30; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Luke 8:13"> They went out from among us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would have surely remained with us, but that they might be made manifest that none are of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:27; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22"> And ye have &#91;the&#93; unction from the holy &#91;one&#93;, and ye know all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Jude 1:5; Proverbs 1:5"> I have not written to you because ye do not know the truth, but because ye know it, and that no lie is of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 John 1:7; 1 John 4:3; 1 John 2:18"> Who is the liar but he who denies that Jesus is the Christ? He is the antichrist who denies the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 4:15; John 8:19; John 5:23"> Whoever denies the Son has not the Father either; he who confesses the Son has the Father also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 14:23; John 15:9–10; 1 John 4:13"> As for you let that which ye have heard from the beginning abide in you: if what ye have heard from the beginning abides in you, ye also shall abide in the Son and in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:28; Titus 3:7; Jude 1:21"> And this is the promise which he has promised us, life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 John 1:7; Acts 20:29–30"> These things have I written to you concerning those who lead you astray:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:20–21; 1 Corinthians 2:13"> and yourselves, the unction which ye have received from him abides in you, and ye have not need that any one should teach you; but as the same unction teaches you as to all things, and is true and is not a lie, and even as it has taught you, ye shall abide in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 4:17; Mark 8:38; 1 John 3:2"> And now, children, abide in him, that if he be manifested we may have boldness, and not be put to shame from before him at his coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 3:3–5; 1 John 3:7; 1 John 5:1"> If ye know that he is righteous, know that every one who practises righteousness is begotten of him.</span>
