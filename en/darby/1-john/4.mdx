<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; 2 Peter 2:1; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Beloved, believe not every spirit, but prove the spirits, if they are of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God; because many false prophets are gone out into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> Hereby ye know the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God: every spirit which confesses Jesus Christ come in flesh is of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 2:22; 2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18"> and every spirit which does not confess Jesus Christ come in flesh is not of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.3 Elohim">d</a></sup>God: and this is that &#91;power&#93; of the antichrist, &#91;of&#93; which ye have heard that it comes, and now it is already in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> Ye are of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, children, and have overcome them, because greater is he that &#91;is&#93; in you than he that &#91;is&#93; in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They are of the world; for this reason they speak &#91;as&#93; of the world, and the world hears them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> We are of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">f</a></sup>God; he that knows<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">g</a></sup>God hears us; he who is not of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">h</a></sup>God does not hear us. From this we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Beloved, let us love one another; because love is of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">i</a></sup>God, and every one that loves has been begotten of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, and knows<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">k</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Psalms 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> He that loves not has not known<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">l</a></sup>God; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">m</a></sup>God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> Herein as to us has been manifested the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">n</a></sup>God, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">o</a></sup>God has sent his only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> Herein is love, not that we loved<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="4.10 Elohim">p</a></sup>God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son a propitiation for our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Beloved, if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="4.11 Elohim">q</a></sup>God has so loved us, we also ought to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> No one has seen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="4.12 Elohim">r</a></sup>God at any time: if we love one another,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="4.12 Elohim">s</a></sup>God abides in us, and his love is perfected in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> Hereby we know that we abide in him and he in us, that he has given to us of his Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> And we have seen, and testify, that the Father has sent the Son &#91;as&#93; Saviour of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> Whosoever shall confess that Jesus is the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="4.15 Elohim">t</a></sup>God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="4.15 Elohim">u</a></sup>God abides in him, and he in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="4.15 Elohim">v</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Psalms 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> And we have known and have believed the love which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="4.16 Elohim">w</a></sup>God has to us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="4.16 Elohim">x</a></sup>God is love, and he that abides in love abides in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="4.16 Elohim">y</a></sup>God, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="4.16 Elohim">z</a></sup>God in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> Herein has love been perfected with us that we may have boldness in the day of judgment, that even as he is, we also are in this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear; for fear has torment, and he that fears has not been made perfect in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> We love because he has first loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> If any one say, I love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="4.20 Elohim">aa</a></sup>God, and hate his brother, he is a liar: for he that loves not his brother whom he has seen, how can he love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="4.20 Elohim">ab</a></sup>God whom he has not seen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> And this commandment have we from him, That he that loves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="4.21 Elohim">ac</a></sup>God love also his brother.</span>
