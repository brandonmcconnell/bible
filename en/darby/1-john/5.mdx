<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12–13; John 8:42; 1 John 2:29"> Every one that believes that Jesus is the Christ is begotten of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God; and every one that loves him that has begotten loves also him that is begotten of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 3:22–24"> Hereby know we that we love the children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, when we love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; John 14:21–24"> For this is the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.3 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, that we keep his commandments; and his commandments are not grievous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; John 16:33"> For all that has been begotten of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.4 Elohim">e</a></sup>God gets the victory over the world; and this is the victory which has gotten the victory over the world, our faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:15; 1 John 5:1"> Who is he that gets the victory over the world, but he that believes that Jesus is the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.5 Elohim">f</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:26; Hebrews 9:14; John 16:13"> This is he that came by water and blood, Jesus &#91;the&#93; Christ; not by water only, but by water and blood. And it is the Spirit that bears witness, for the Spirit is the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Acts 5:32"> For they that bear witness are three:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 5:6–7; John 15:26; 1 Peter 3:21"> the Spirit, and the water, and the blood; and the three agree in one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:16–17; Matthew 17:5; Acts 17:31"> If we receive the witness of men, the witness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.9 Elohim">g</a></sup>God is greater. For this is the witness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="5.9 Elohim">h</a></sup>God &#91;which&#93; he has witnessed concerning his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:16; Galatians 4:6; John 3:33"> He that believes on the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="5.10 Elohim">i</a></sup>God has the witness in himself; he that does not believe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="5.10 Elohim">j</a></sup>God has made him a liar, because he has not believed in the witness which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="5.10 Elohim">k</a></sup>God has witnessed concerning his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:23; 1 John 2:25; John 3:36"> And this is the witness, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="5.11 Elohim">l</a></sup>God has given to us eternal life; and this life is in his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 3:36; John 5:24; John 1:12"> He that has the Son has life: he that has not the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="5.12 Elohim">m</a></sup>God has not life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:31; John 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> These things have I written to you that ye may know that ye have eternal life who believe on the name of the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="5.13 Elohim">n</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> And this is the boldness which we have towards him, that if we ask him anything according to his will he hears us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> And if we know that he hears us, whatsoever we ask, we know that we have the petitions which we have asked of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> If any one see his brother sinning a sin not unto death, he shall ask, and he shall give him life, for those that do not sin unto death. There is a sin to death: I do not say of that that he should make a request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 3:4; 1 John 5:16; James 4:7–10"> Every unrighteousness is sin; and there is a sin not to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:9; 1 John 5:4; 1 John 5:1"> We know that every one begotten of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="5.18 Elohim">o</a></sup>God does not sin, but he that has been begotten of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="5.18 Elohim">p</a></sup>God keeps himself, and the wicked &#91;one&#93; does not touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we are of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="5.19 Elohim">q</a></sup>God, and the whole world lies in the wicked &#91;one&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:3; Luke 24:45; John 14:9"> And we know that the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="5.20 Elohim">r</a></sup>God has come, and has given us an understanding that we should know him that &#91;is&#93; true; and we are in him that &#91;is&#93; true, in his Son Jesus Christ. He is the true<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="5.20 Elohim">s</a></sup>God and eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:14; 1 Corinthians 10:7; Exodus 20:3–4"> Children, keep yourselves from idols.</span>
