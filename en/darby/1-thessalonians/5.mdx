<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Matthew 24:36"> But concerning the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that ye should be written to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:42–44"> for ye know perfectly well yourselves, that the day of &#91;the&#93; Lord so comes as a thief by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> When they may say, Peace and safety, then sudden destruction comes upon them, as travail upon her that is with child; and they shall in no wise escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that the day should overtake you as a thief:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> for all ye are sons of light and sons of day; we are not of night nor of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> So then do not let us sleep as the rest do, but let us watch and be sober;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> for they that sleep sleep by night, and they that drink drink by night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> but we being of &#91;the&#93; day, let us be sober, putting on &#91;the&#93; breastplate of faith and love, and as helmet &#91;the&#93; hope of salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.9 Elohim">a</a></sup>God has not set us for wrath, but for obtaining salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> who has died for us, that whether we may be watching or sleep, we may live together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> Wherefore encourage one another, and build up each one the other, even as also ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> But we beg you, brethren, to know those who labour among you, and take the lead among you in &#91;the&#93; Lord, and admonish you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> and to regard them exceedingly in love on account of their work. Be in peace among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> But we exhort you, brethren, admonish the disorderly, comfort the faint-hearted, sustain the weak, be patient towards all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> See that no one render to any evil for evil, but pursue always what is good towards one another and towards all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> rejoice always;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> pray unceasingly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> in everything give thanks, for this is &#91;the&#93; will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.18 Elohim">b</a></sup>God in Christ Jesus towards you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> quench not the Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> do not lightly esteem prophecies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> but prove all things, hold fast the right;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> hold aloof from every form of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:24; Hebrews 4:12; 1 Corinthians 1:8–9"> Now the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.23 Elohim">c</a></sup>God of peace himself sanctify you wholly: and your whole spirit, and soul, and body be preserved blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 1:9"> He &#91;is&#93; faithful who calls you, who will also perform &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:18–19; Philippians 1:19; Philemon 1:22"> Brethren, pray for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Corinthians 16:20"> Greet all the brethren with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 4:16; Acts 19:13; 1 Timothy 1:3"> I adjure you by the Lord that the letter be read to all the &#91;holy&#93; brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;be&#93; with you.</span>
