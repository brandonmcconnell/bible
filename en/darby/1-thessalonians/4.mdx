<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Peter 3:18"> For the rest, then, brethren, we beg you and exhort you in &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus, even as ye have received from us how ye ought to walk and please<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, even as ye also do walk, that ye would abound still more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> For ye know what charges we gave you through the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> For this is &#91;the&#93; will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, &#91;even&#93; your sanctification, that ye should abstain from fornication;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:9; Romans 12:1; Hebrews 13:4"> that each of you know how to possess his own vessel in sanctification and honour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Acts 17:30–31; Ephesians 4:17–19"> (not in passionate desire, even as the nations who know not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Exodus 20:17; Romans 12:19"> not overstepping the rights of and wronging his brother in the matter, because the Lord &#91;is&#93; the avenger of all these things, even as we also told you before, and have fully testified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 2:9–12"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.7 Elohim">d</a></sup>God has not called us to uncleanness, but in sanctification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Luke 10:16; John 12:48"> He therefore that &#91;in this&#93; disregards &#91;his brother&#93;, disregards, not man, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, who has given also his Holy Spirit to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:23"> Now concerning brotherly love ye have no need that we should write to you, for ye yourselves are taught of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">f</a></sup>God to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:12; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> For also ye do this towards all the brethren in the whole of Macedonia; but we exhort you, brethren, to abound still more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Peter 3:4"> and to seek earnestly to be quiet and mind your own affairs, and work with your &#91;own&#93; hands, even as we charged you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; 1 Peter 2:12; Romans 12:17"> that ye may walk reputably towards those without, and may have need of no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> But we do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, concerning them that are fallen asleep, to the end that ye be not grieved even as also the rest who have no hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Philippians 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> For if we believe that Jesus has died and has risen again, so also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.14 Elohim">g</a></sup>God will bring with him those who have fallen asleep through Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–53; 2 Corinthians 4:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:19"> (For this we say to you in &#91;the&#93; word of &#91;the&#93; Lord, that we, the living, who remain to the coming of the Lord, are in no way to anticipate those who have fallen asleep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> for the Lord himself, with an assembling shout, with archangel's voice and with trump of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.16 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, shall descend from heaven; and the dead in Christ shall rise first;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:12; John 14:3"> then we, the living who remain, shall be caught up together with them in &#91;the&#93; clouds, to meet the Lord in &#91;the&#93; air; and thus we shall be always with &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Luke 21:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> So encourage one another with these words.)</span>
