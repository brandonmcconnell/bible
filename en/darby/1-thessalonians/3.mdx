<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> But Timotheus having just come to us from you, and brought to us the glad tidings of your faith and love, and that ye have always good remembrance of us, desiring much to see us, even as we also you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> for this reason we have been comforted in you, brethren, in all our distress and tribulation, through your faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Revelation 3:11"> because now we live if ye stand firm in &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:14–15; Psalms 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> For what thanksgiving can we render to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God for you, for all the joy wherewith we rejoice on account of you before our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> night and day beseeching exceedingly to the end that we may see your face, and perfect what is lacking in your faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> But our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God and Father himself, and our Lord Jesus, direct our way to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> But you, may the Lord make to exceed and abound in love toward one another, and toward all, even as we also towards you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; Ephesians 5:27"> in order to the confirming of your hearts unblamable in holiness before our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.13 Elohim">e</a></sup>God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his saints.</span>
