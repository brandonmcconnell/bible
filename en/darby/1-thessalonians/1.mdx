<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1; Acts 18:5"> Paul and Silvanus and Timotheus to the assembly of Thessalonians in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ. Grace to you and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Ephesians 1:15–16; Philemon 1:4"> We give thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God always for you all, making mention of you at our prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; Revelation 2:19; Galatians 5:6"> remembering unceasingly your work of faith, and labour of love, and enduring constancy of hope, of our Lord Jesus Christ, before our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and Father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Titus 3:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> knowing, brethren beloved by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, your election.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Mark 16:20; 1 Corinthians 2:2–5"> For our glad tidings were not with you in word only, but also in power, and in &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, and in much assurance; even as ye know what we were among you for your sakes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 4:16; Acts 13:52"> and ye became our imitators, and of the Lord, having accepted the word in much tribulation with joy of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12"> so that ye became models to all that believe in Macedonia and in Achaia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Romans 10:14–18; Isaiah 52:7"> for the word of the Lord sounded out from you, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but in every place your faith which &#91;is&#93; towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">e</a></sup>God has gone abroad, so that we have no need to say anything;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Galatians 4:8–9"> for they themselves relate concerning us what entering in we had to you, and how ye turned to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">f</a></sup>God from idols to serve a living and true<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">g</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:9; Isaiah 25:8–9; Romans 5:9–10"> and to await his Son from the heavens, whom he raised from among the dead, Jesus, our deliverer from the coming wrath.</span>
