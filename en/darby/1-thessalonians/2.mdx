<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 15:2; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> For ye know yourselves, brethren, our entering in which &#91;we had&#93; to you, that it has not been in vain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:22–24; Acts 4:13; Acts 17:2–9"> but, having suffered before and been insulted, even as ye know, in Philippi, we were bold in our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God to speak unto you the glad tidings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God with much earnest striving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> For our exhortation &#91;was&#93; not of deceit, nor of uncleanness, nor in guile;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:10; Romans 8:27; Galatians 2:7"> but even as we have been approved of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God to have the glad tidings entrusted to us, so we speak; not as pleasing men, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, who proves our hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 2 Corinthians 12:17; Acts 20:33"> For we have not at any time been &#91;among you&#93; with flattering discourse, even as ye know, nor with a pretext for covetousness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">e</a></sup>God &#91;is&#93; witness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; John 5:41"> nor seeking glory from men, neither from you nor from others, when we might have been a charge as Christ's apostles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> but have been gentle in the midst of you, as a nurse would cherish her own children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:16; 2 Corinthians 12:15; Philippians 2:17"> Thus, yearning over you, we had found our delight in having imparted to you not only the glad tidings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.8 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, but our own lives also, because ye had become beloved of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Acts 18:3"> For ye remember, brethren, our labour and toil: working night and day, not to be chargeable to any one of you, we have preached to you the glad tidings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.9 Elohim">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Ye &#91;are&#93; witnesses, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.10 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, how piously and righteously and blamelessly we have conducted ourselves with you that believe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:14–15; Hebrews 13:22; 1 Timothy 5:21"> as ye know how, as a father his own children, we used to exhort each one of you, and comfort and testify,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 John 2:6; 1 John 1:6–7"> that ye should walk worthy of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2.12 Elohim">i</a></sup>God, who calls you to his own kingdom and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Romans 10:17; 1 Peter 1:23"> And for this cause we also give thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">j</a></sup>God unceasingly that, having received &#91;the&#93; word of &#91;the&#93; report of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">k</a></sup>God by us, ye accepted, not men's word, but, even as it is truly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">l</a></sup>God's word, which also works in you who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:4; Hebrews 10:33–34; Hebrews 5:7–8"> For ye, brethren, have become imitators of the assemblies of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="2.14 Elohim">m</a></sup>God which are in Judaea in Christ Jesus; for ye also have suffered the same things of your own countrymen as also they of the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; Luke 11:48–53; Acts 7:52"> who have both slain the Lord Jesus and the prophets, and have driven us out by persecution, and do not please<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="2.15 Elohim">n</a></sup>God, and &#91;are&#93; against all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:13; Acts 14:19; Mark 16:16"> forbidding us to speak to the nations that they may be saved, that they may fill up their sins always: but wrath has come upon them to the uttermost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Corinthians 5:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:6"> But we, brethren, having been bereaved of you and separated for a little moment in person, not in heart, have used more abundant diligence to see your face with much desire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 15:22; Romans 1:13; Revelation 2:10"> wherefore we have desired to come to you, even I Paul, both once and twice, and Satan has hindered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 2:16"> For what &#91;is&#93; our hope, or joy, or crown of boasting? &#91;are&#93; not ye also before our Lord Jesus at his coming?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 11:7; Proverbs 17:6"> for ye are our glory and joy.</span>
