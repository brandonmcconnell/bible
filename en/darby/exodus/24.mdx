<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Deuteronomy 31:9; Joshua 4:8–9"> And Moses wrote all the words of Jehovah, and rose up early in the morning, and built an altar under the mountain, and twelve pillars, according to the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Leviticus 3:1–17; Exodus 19:22"> And he sent the youths of the children of Israel, and they offered up burnt-offerings, and sacrificed sacrifices of peace-offering of bullocks to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19; Exodus 24:8; Hebrews 9:18"> And Moses took half the blood, and put &#91;it&#93; in basons; and half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–5; Exodus 24:6; Ezekiel 36:25"> And Moses took the blood, and sprinkled &#91;it&#93; on the people, and said, Behold the blood of the covenant that Jehovah has made with you concerning all these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 24:1"> And Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:18; Ezekiel 10:1; Genesis 32:30"> and they saw the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel; and there was under his feet as it were work of transparent sapphire, and as it were the form of heaven for clearness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 15:23–24; Exodus 18:12; Genesis 31:54"> And on the nobles of the children of Israel he laid not his hand: they saw<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="24.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:22; Exodus 31:18; Exodus 32:15–16"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Come up to me into the mountain, and be there; and I will give thee the tables of stone, and the law, and the commandment that I have written, for their instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 33:11; Numbers 11:28; Exodus 3:1"> And Moses rose up, and Joshua his attendant; and Moses went up to the mountain of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="24.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:10; Exodus 17:12; Genesis 22:5"> And he said to the elders, Wait here for us, until we return to you; and behold, Aaron and Hur are with you: if any man have any matter, let him come before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Matthew 17:5; Exodus 19:16"> And Moses went up to the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:10; Numbers 16:42; Ezekiel 1:28"> And the glory of Jehovah abode on mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days; and on the seventh day he called to Moses out of the midst of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 3:2"> And the appearance of the glory of Jehovah was like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain, before the eyes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 10:10"> And Moses went into the midst of the cloud, and ascended the mountain. And Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights.</span>
