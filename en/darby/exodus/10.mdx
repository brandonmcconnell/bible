<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 7:13–14; Exodus 3:20"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Go in unto Pharaoh; for I have hardened his heart, and the heart of his bondmen, that I might do these my signs in their midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:3; Deuteronomy 4:9; Deuteronomy 6:20–22"> and that thou mightest tell in the ears of thy son and thy son's son what I have wrought in Egypt, and my signs which I have done among them; and ye shall know that I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 1 Kings 21:29; James 4:10"> And Moses and Aaron came to Pharaoh, and said to him, Thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of the Hebrews: How long dost thou refuse to humble thyself before me? let my people go, that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:3; Joel 2:25; Exodus 11:4–5"> For, if thou refuse to let my people go, behold, I will to-morrow bring locusts into thy borders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Exodus 9:32; Joel 2:25"> and they shall cover the face of the land, so that ye will not be able to see the land; and they shall eat the residue of that which is escaped, which ye have remaining from the hail, and shall eat every tree which ye have growing in the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 8:21; Exodus 8:3; Exodus 11:6"> and they shall fill thy houses, and the houses of all thy bondmen, and the houses of all the Egyptians; which neither thy fathers nor thy fathers' fathers have seen, since the day that they were upon the earth unto this day. And he turned and went out from Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21; Exodus 23:33; Joshua 23:13"> And Pharaoh's bondmen said to him, How long shall this man be a snare to us? let the men go, that they may serve Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God: dost thou not yet know that Egypt is ruined?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 10:24; Exodus 12:31; Exodus 8:8"> And Moses and Aaron were brought again to Pharaoh. And he said to them, Go, serve Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God. Who are they that shall go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:1; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> And Moses said, We will go with our young and with our old, with our sons and with our daughters; with our flocks and with our herds will we go; for we have a feast of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; 2 Chronicles 32:15; Exodus 12:30–31"> And he said to them, Let Jehovah be so with you, as I let you go, and your little ones: see that evil is before you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 10:28; Psalms 119:69; Exodus 5:4"> Not so: go now, ye &#91;that are&#93; men, and serve Jehovah! for it is that ye have desired. And they were driven out from Pharaoh's presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 7:19; Exodus 10:4–5"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Stretch out thy hand over the land of Egypt for the locusts, that they may come up over the land of Egypt, and eat every herb of the land — all that the hail hath left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 1:4; Psalms 78:26; Psalms 148:8"> And Moses stretched out his staff over the land of Egypt, and Jehovah brought an east wind on the land all that day and all that night. When it was morning, the east wind brought the locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:2–4; Psalms 105:34–35"> And the locusts went up over all the land of Egypt, and rested in all the borders of Egypt, very grievous; before them there were no such locusts as they, neither after them will be such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 10:5; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:6–7"> And they covered the face of the whole land, so that the land was darkened; and they ate every herb of the land, and all the fruit of the trees that the hail had left; and there remained not any green thing on the trees, and in the herbs of the field, throughout the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:27; 2 Samuel 19:20; 1 Samuel 15:30"> And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron in haste; and he said, I have sinned against Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:28; 1 Kings 13:6"> And now, forgive, I pray you, my sin only this time, and intreat Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God that he may take away from me this death only!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 6:28; Exodus 8:28–30; Exodus 8:9"> And he went out from Pharaoh, and intreated Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:20; Exodus 13:18; Exodus 15:4"> And Jehovah turned a very powerful west wind, which took away the locusts, and drove them into the Red Sea: there remained not one locust in all the borders of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 11:10; Deuteronomy 2:30"> And Jehovah made Pharaoh's heart stubborn, and he did not let the children of Israel go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 16:10–11; Jude 1:6; Luke 23:44"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Stretch out thy hand toward the heavens, that there may be darkness in the land of Egypt — so that one may feel darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:28; Revelation 16:10; Exodus 20:21"> And Moses stretched out his hand toward the heavens; and there was a thick darkness throughout the land of Egypt three days:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 8:22; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> they saw not one another, neither rose any from his place, for three days. But all the children of Israel had light in their dwellings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 10:8–10; Exodus 8:28; Genesis 34:23"> And Pharaoh called Moses and said, Go, serve Jehovah; only, let your flocks and your herds remain; let your little ones also go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 9:22; Leviticus 16:9; Exodus 29:1–46"> And Moses said, Thou must give also sacrifices and burnt-offerings into our hands, that we may sacrifice to Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Acts 2:44–45; Proverbs 3:9"> Our cattle also must go with us: there shall not a hoof be left behind; for we must take thereof to serve Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God; and we do not know with what we must serve Jehovah, until we come there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 10:20; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 14:8"> But Jehovah made Pharaoh's heart stubborn, and he would not let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:10; Amos 7:13"> And Pharaoh said to him, Get thee from me, take heed to thyself, see my face no more; for in the day thou seest my face thou shalt die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 11:27; Exodus 12:30–31; Exodus 11:4–8"> And Moses said, Thou hast spoken rightly: I will see thy face again no more!</span>
