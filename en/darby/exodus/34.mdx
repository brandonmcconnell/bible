<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 32:19; Exodus 31:18; Psalms 119:89"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Hew for thyself two tables of stone like the first; and I will write upon the tables the words that were upon the first tables, which thou hast broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:20; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 19:11"> And be ready for the morning, and go up in the morning to mount Sinai, and stand there before me on the top of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:21; 1 Timothy 2:5"> And let no man go up with thee, neither shall any man be seen on all the mountain; neither shall sheep and oxen feed in front of that mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he hewed two tables of stone like the first; and Moses rose up early in the morning and went up to mount Sinai, as Jehovah had commanded him, and took in his hand the two tables of stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Numbers 11:25; Luke 9:34–35"> And Jehovah came down in the cloud, and stood beside him there, and proclaimed the name of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Psalms 103:8–13"> And Jehovah passed by before his face, and proclaimed, Jehovah, Jehovah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="34.6 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in goodness and truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Psalms 103:3; Deuteronomy 5:9–10"> keeping mercy unto thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin, but by no means clearing &#91;the guilty&#93;; visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, and upon the children's children, upon the third and upon the fourth &#91;generation&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 20:18; Genesis 17:3"> And Moses made haste, and bowed his head to the earth and worshipped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Deuteronomy 32:9; Psalms 94:14"> and said, If indeed I have found grace in thine eyes, Lord, let the Lord, I pray thee, go in our midst; for it is a stiff-necked people; and pardon our iniquity and our sin, and take us for an inheritance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 77:14; Isaiah 64:3"> And he said, Behold, I make a covenant: before all thy people I will do marvels that have not been done in all the earth, nor in any nation; and all the people in the midst of which thou &#91;art&#93; shall see the work of Jehovah; for a terrible thing it shall be that I will do with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8"> Observe what I command thee this day: behold, I will drive out before thee the Amorite, and the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:32–33; Joshua 23:12–13; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Take heed to thyself, that thou make no covenant with the inhabitants of the land to which thou shalt come, lest it be a snare in the midst of thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> but ye shall demolish their altars, shatter their statues, and hew down their Asherahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 32:21; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> For thou shalt worship no other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="34.14 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God; for Jehovah — Jealous is his name — is a jealous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="34.14 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 2:17; Psalms 106:28; Numbers 25:2"> lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, and then, when they go a whoring after their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="34.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods, and sacrifice unto their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="34.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods, thou be invited, and eat of their sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; 1 Kings 11:2–4; Ezra 9:2"> and thou take of their daughters unto thy sons, and their daughters go a whoring after their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="34.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods, and make thy sons go a whoring after their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="34.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Exodus 32:8; Isaiah 46:6–7"> — Thou shalt make thyself no molten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="34.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 13:6–7"> — The feast of the unleavened bread shalt thou keep: seven days shalt thou eat unleavened bread, as I have commanded thee, at the appointed time of the month Abib; for in the month Abib thou camest out from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Exodus 13:12"> — All that openeth the womb &#91;is&#93; mine; and all the cattle that is born a male, the firstling of ox and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:16"> But the firstling of an ass thou shalt ransom with a lamb; and if thou ransom &#91;it&#93; not, then shalt thou break its neck. All the first-born of thy sons thou shalt ransom; and none shall appear before me empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 21:4; Luke 13:14"> — Six days shalt thou work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest; in ploughing time and in harvest thou shalt rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Deuteronomy 16:10–15; John 7:2"> — And thou shalt observe the feast of weeks, of the first-fruits of wheat-harvest, and the feast of ingathering at the turn of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:14; Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:17"> Thrice in the year shall all thy males appear before the Lord Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="34.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 33:2; Proverbs 16:7"> For I will dispossess the nations before thee, and enlarge thy border, and no man shall desire thy land, when thou goest up to appear before the face of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="34.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God thrice in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 12:10; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8"> — Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leaven; neither shall the sacrifice of the feast of the passover be left over night until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:2"> — The first of the first-fruits of thy land shalt thou bring into the house of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="34.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God. Thou shalt not boil a kid in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 24:4; Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 4:13"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Write thee these words; for after the tenor of these words have I made a covenant with thee and with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 34:1; Exodus 24:18; Deuteronomy 9:18"> — And he was there with Jehovah forty days and forty nights; he ate no bread, and drank no water. — And he wrote on the tables the words of the covenant, the ten words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Luke 9:29; Exodus 32:15"> And it came to pass, when Moses came down from mount Sinai — and the two tables of testimony were in Moses' hand, when he came down from the mountain — that Moses knew not that the skin of his face shone through his talking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Luke 5:8; Numbers 12:8"> And Aaron and all the children of Israel saw Moses, and behold, the skin of his face shone; and they were afraid to come near him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:15; Exodus 24:1–3; Genesis 45:3"> And Moses called to them; and they turned to him, — Aaron and all the principal men of the assembly; and Moses talked with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:14; Exodus 24:3; Matthew 28:20"> And afterwards, all the children of Israel came near; and he gave them in commandment all that Jehovah had spoken with him on mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4–6; 2 Corinthians 3:13–18; Romans 10:4"> And Moses ended speaking with them; and he had put on his face a veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 4:16"> And when Moses went in before Jehovah to speak with him, he took the veil off, until he came out; and he came out, and spoke to the children of Israel what he was commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 34:29–30; John 5:35; Daniel 12:3"> And the children of Israel saw the face of Moses, that the skin of Moses' face shone; and Moses put the veil on his face again, until he went in to speak with him.</span>
