<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Ezekiel 43:13–17; Exodus 40:6"> And he made the altar of burnt-offering of acacia-wood; five cubits the length thereof, and five cubits the breadth thereof, square, and three cubits the height thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2; Job 6:12"> And he made its horns on the four corners thereof; its horns were of itself; and he overlaid it with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; 1 Samuel 2:13"> And he made all the utensils of the altar: the pots, and the shovels, and the bowls, the forks, and the firepans; all its utensils made he of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he made for the altar a grating of network of copper under its ledge from beneath, to the very middle of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:4"> And he cast four rings for the four corners of the grating of copper, as receptacles for the staves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:3; Exodus 25:6"> And he made the staves of acacia-wood, and overlaid them with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Corinthians 1:24"> And he put the staves into the rings on the sides of the altar, with which to carry it. Hollow with boards did he make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:22; Luke 2:37; Exodus 40:7"> And he made the laver of copper, and its stand of copper, of the mirrors of the crowds of women who crowded before the entrance of the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–19; Psalms 89:7; 1 Kings 6:36"> And he made the court. On the south side southward, the hangings of the court were of twined byssus, a hundred cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> their pillars twenty, and their bases twenty, of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their connecting-rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And on the north side, a hundred cubits; their pillars twenty, and their bases twenty, of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their connecting-rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And on the west side, hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars ten, and their bases ten; the hooks of the pillars and their connecting-rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for the east side, eastward, fifty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:14"> the hangings on the one wing of fifteen cubits, their pillars three, and their bases three;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and on the other wing, on this side as on that side of the gate of the court, hangings of fifteen cubits, their pillars three, and their bases three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> All the hangings of the court round about were of twined byssus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the bases of the pillars of copper, the hooks of the pillars and their connecting-rods of silver, and the overlaying of their capitals of silver; and all the pillars of the court were fastened together with &#91;rods of&#93; silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:14"> — And the curtain of the gate of the court was of embroidery of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus; and the length was twenty cubits, and the height like the breadth, five cubits, just as the hangings of the court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and their pillars four, and their bases four, of copper; their hooks of silver, and the overlaying of their capitals and their connecting-rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:9; Exodus 27:19; Ephesians 2:21–22"> And all the pegs for the tabernacle and for the court round about were of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Numbers 9:15; 2 Chronicles 24:6"> These are the things numbered of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the testimony, which were counted, according to the commandment of Moses, by the service of the Levites, under the hand of Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 35:30–3; Exodus 31:1–5; Psalms 119:6"> And Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made all that Jehovah had commanded Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 35:34; Exodus 31:6"> and with him Aholiab, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, an engraver and artificer, and an embroiderer in blue, and in purple, and in scarlet, and in byssus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> All the gold that it took for the work in all the work of the sanctuary — the gold of the wave-offering, was twenty-nine talents, and seven hundred and thirty shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:12"> And the silver of them that were numbered of the assembly was a hundred talents, and a thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 12:37"> a bekah the head — half a shekel, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, for every one that passed the numbering from twenty years old and upward, &#91;of&#93; the six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:19; Exodus 26:25; Exodus 26:21"> And there were a hundred talents of silver for casting the bases of the sanctuary, and the bases of the veil; a hundred bases of a hundred talents, a talent for a base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 27:17"> And of the thousand seven hundred and seventy-five &#91;shekels&#93; he made the hooks for the pillars, and overlaid their capitals, and fastened them &#91;with rods&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And the copper of the wave-offering was seventy talents, and two thousand four hundred shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 27:17; Exodus 27:10"> And he made with it the bases for the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the copper altar, and the copper grating for it, and all the utensils of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the bases of the court round about, and the bases of the gate of the court, and all the pegs of the tabernacle, and all the pegs of the court round about.</span>
