<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 12:6; Leviticus 23:16–18"> In the third month after the departure of the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt, the same day came they &#91;into&#93; the wilderness of Sinai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 17:1; Exodus 3:12"> they departed from Rephidim, and came &#91;into&#93; the wilderness of Sinai, and encamped in the wilderness; and Israel encamped there before the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:4; Acts 7:38; Exodus 20:21"> And Moses went up to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and Jehovah called to him out of the mountain, saying, Thus shalt thou say to the house of Jacob, and tell the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 40:31"> Ye have seen what I have done to the Egyptians, and &#91;how&#93; I have borne you on eagles' wings and brought you to myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18"> And now, if ye will hearken to my voice indeed and keep my covenant, then shall ye be my own possession out of all the peoples — for all the earth is mine —</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Peter 2:5"> and ye shall be to me a kingdom of priests, and a holy nation. These are the words which thou shalt speak to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:29–30; Exodus 3:16; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> And Moses came and called the elders of the people, and laid before them all these words which Jehovah had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Joshua 24:24"> And all the people answered together, and said, All that Jehovah has spoken will we do! And Moses brought the words of the people back to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:36; Exodus 19:16; Exodus 24:15–16"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Lo, I will come to thee in the cloud's thick darkness, that the people may hear when I speak with thee, and believe thee also for ever. And Moses told the words of the people to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Genesis 35:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Go to the people, and hallow them to-day and to-morrow, and let them wash their clothes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Exodus 34:5; Deuteronomy 33:2"> and let them be ready for the third day; for on the third day Jehovah will come down before the eyes of all the people on mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:20–21; Joshua 3:4; Exodus 19:21"> And set bounds round about the people, saying, Take heed to yourselves, &#91;not&#93; to go up unto the mountain nor touch the border of it: whatever toucheth the mountain shall certainly be put to death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Hebrews 12:20; Exodus 19:16"> not a hand shall touch it, but it shall certainly be stoned, or shot through; whether it be a beast or a man, it shall not live. When the long drawn note of the trumpet soundeth, they shall come up to the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:10"> And Moses came down from the mountain to the people, and hallowed the people; and they washed their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:5; 1 Samuel 21:4–5; Exodus 19:11"> And he said to the people, Be ready for the third day; do not come near &#91;your&#93; wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:18–19; Psalms 18:11–14; Hebrews 12:21"> And it came to pass on the third day, when it was morning, that there were thunders and lightnings and a heavy cloud on the mountain, and the sound of the trumpet exceeding loud; and the whole people that was in the camp trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 5:5"> And Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; and they stood at the foot of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:5; Exodus 3:2; Psalms 144:5"> And the whole of mount Sinai smoked, because Jehovah descended on it in fire; and its smoke ascended as the smoke of a furnace; and the whole mountain shook greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 81:7; Exodus 19:16; Nehemiah 9:13"> And the sound of the trumpet increased and became exceeding loud; Moses spoke, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="19.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God answered him by a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Exodus 34:2; Exodus 19:11"> And Jehovah came down on mount Sinai, on the top of the mountain; and Jehovah called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 3:5; Hebrews 12:28–29"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Go down, testify to the people that they break not through to Jehovah to gaze, and many of them perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:5; Acts 5:10; 1 Chronicles 13:9–11"> And the priests also, who come near to Jehovah, shall hallow themselves, lest Jehovah break forth on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 19:12; Joshua 3:4–5"> And Moses said to Jehovah, The people cannot come up to mount Sinai; for thou hast testified to us, saying, Set bounds about the mountain, and hallow it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 12:18–25; Hebrews 12:29"> And Jehovah said to him, Go, descend, and thou shalt come up, thou, and Aaron with thee; but the priests and the people shall not break through to go up to Jehovah, lest he break forth on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So Moses went down to the people, and told them.</span>
