<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 4:15–16; Psalms 82:6; John 10:35–36"> And Jehovah said to Moses, See, I have made thee<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God to Pharaoh; and Aaron thy brother shall be thy prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:15; Exodus 6:29; Deuteronomy 4:2"> Thou shalt speak all that I command thee; and Aaron thy brother shall speak unto Pharaoh, that he let the children of Israel go out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 11:9; Psalms 105:27–36"> And I will render Pharaoh's heart obdurate, and multiply my signs and my wonders in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 11:9; Exodus 6:6; Exodus 12:51"> And Pharaoh will not hearken unto you; and I will lay my hand upon Egypt, and bring forth my hosts, my people, the children of Israel, out of the land of Egypt by great judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:18; Exodus 7:17; Exodus 8:22"> And the Egyptians shall know that I am Jehovah, when I stretch forth my hand on Egypt, and bring out the children of Israel from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 7:2; Genesis 6:22; Exodus 7:10"> And Moses and Aaron did as Jehovah had commanded them: so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Acts 7:30; Deuteronomy 31:2"> And Moses was eighty years old, and Aaron was eighty-three years old, when they spoke to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; John 2:18; Isaiah 7:11"> When Pharaoh shall speak to you, saying, Do a miracle for yourselves, — then thou shalt say unto Aaron, Take thy staff and cast &#91;it&#93; before Pharaoh — it will become a serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:3; Luke 10:19; Amos 9:3"> And Moses and Aaron went in to Pharaoh, and did so, as Jehovah had commanded; and Aaron cast down his staff before Pharaoh, and before his bondmen, and it became a serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 7:22; 2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 8:18"> And Pharaoh also called the sages and the sorcerers; and they too, the scribes of Egypt, did so with their enchantments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Exodus 9:11; Exodus 8:18–19"> they cast down every man his staff, and they became serpents; but Aaron's staff swallowed up their staves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 8:15; Zechariah 7:11–12"> And Pharaoh's heart was stubborn, and he hearkened not to them, as Jehovah had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 10:20; Exodus 8:15; Exodus 10:1"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Pharaoh's heart is hardened: he refuseth to let the people go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 8:20; Exodus 2:5; Exodus 7:10"> Go unto Pharaoh in the morning — behold, he will go out unto the water — and take thy stand by the bank of the river in front of him; and take in thy hand the staff that was turned into a serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 9:1; Exodus 9:13"> And say unto him, Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of the Hebrews has sent me to thee, saying, Let my people go, that they may serve me in the wilderness; but behold, hitherto thou hast not hearkened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:9; Exodus 5:2; Exodus 7:5"> Thus saith Jehovah: In this shalt thou know that I am Jehovah — behold, I will smite with the staff that is in my hand upon the water which is in the river, and it shall be turned into blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:24; Exodus 7:21; Numbers 11:20"> And the fish that is in the river shall die; and the river shall stink; and the Egyptians shall loathe to drink the water out of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 10:12; Exodus 8:5–6"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Say unto Aaron, Take thy staff, and stretch out thy hand upon the waters of the Egyptians — upon their streams, upon their rivers, and upon their ponds, and upon all their reservoirs of water, that they may become blood; and there shall be blood throughout the land of Egypt, both in &#91;vessels of&#93; wood and in &#91;vessels of&#93; stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 78:44; Psalms 105:29; Exodus 7:17–18"> And Moses and Aaron did so, as Jehovah had commanded; and he lifted up the staff, and smote the waters that were in the river before the eyes of Pharaoh, and before the eyes of his bondmen; and all the waters that were in the river were turned into blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 7:18; Revelation 8:9"> And the fish that was in the river died; and the river stank, and the Egyptians could not drink the water of the river; and the blood was throughout the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; 2 Timothy 3:8; Jeremiah 27:18"> And the scribes of Egypt did so with their sorceries; and Pharaoh's heart was stubborn, neither did he hearken to them, as Jehovah had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:24; Ezekiel 40:4; Malachi 2:2"> And Pharaoh turned and went into his house, and took not this to heart either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And all the Egyptians dug round about the river for water to drink; for they could not drink of the water of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:23; Exodus 8:9–10; 2 Samuel 24:13"> And seven days were fulfilled, after Jehovah had smitten the river.</span>
