<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> And these are the judgments which thou shalt set before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> If thou buy a Hebrew bondman, six years shall he serve; and in the seventh he shall go out free for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> If he came in alone, he shall go out alone: if he had a wife, then his wife shall go out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his master have given him a wife, and she have borne him sons or daughters, the wife and her children shall be her master's, and he shall go out alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> But if the bondman shall say distinctly, I love my master, my wife, and my children, I will not go free;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> then his master shall bring him before the judges, and shall bring him to the door, or to the door-post; and his master shall bore his ear through with an awl; and he shall be his bondman for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> And if a man shall sell his daughter as a handmaid, she shall not go out as the bondmen go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> If she is unacceptable in the eyes of her master, who had taken her for himself, then shall he let her be ransomed: to sell her unto a foreign people he hath no power, after having dealt unfaithfully with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he have appointed her unto his son, he shall deal with her after the law of daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he take himself another, her food, her clothing, and her conjugal rights he shall not diminish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And if he do not these three things unto her, then shall she go out free without money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> He that striketh a man, so that he die, shall certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> But if he have not lain in wait, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="21.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God have delivered &#91;him&#93; into his hand, I will appoint thee a place to which he shall flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> But if a man act wantonly toward his neighbour, and slay him with guile, thou shalt take him from mine altar, that he may die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> And he that striketh his father, or his mother, shall certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> And he that stealeth a man, and selleth him, or if he be found in his hand, he shall certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> And he that curseth his father, or his mother, shall certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> And if men dispute, and one strike the other with a stone, or with the fist, and he die not, but take to &#91;his&#93; bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> — if he rise, and walk abroad upon his staff, then shall he that struck &#91;him&#93; be guiltless; only he shall pay &#91;for&#93; the loss of his time, and shall cause &#91;him&#93; to be thoroughly healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> And if a man strike his bondman or his handmaid with a staff, and he die under his hand, he shall certainly be avenged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> Only, if he continue &#91;to live&#93; a day or two days, he shall not be avenged; for he is his money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And if men strive together, and strike a woman with child, so that she be delivered, and no mischief happen, he shall in any case be fined, according as the woman's husband shall impose on him, and shall give it as the judges estimate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> But if mischief happen, then thou shalt give life for life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> branding for branding, wound for wound, stripe for stripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> And if a man strike the eye of his bondman or the eye of his handmaid, and it be marred, he shall let him go for his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And if he knock out his bondman's tooth or his handmaid's tooth, he shall let him go free for his tooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> And if an ox gore a man or a woman, so that they die, then the ox shall certainly be stoned, and its flesh shall not be eaten; but the owner of the ox shall be guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if the ox have gored heretofore, and it have been testified to its owner, and he have not kept it in, and it kill a man or a woman, — the ox shall be stoned, and its owner also shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> If there be imposed on him a satisfaction, then he shall give the ransom of his life, according to what is imposed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Whether it gore a son or gore a daughter, according to this judgment shall it be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the ox gore a bondman or a handmaid, he shall give to their master thirty shekels of silver, and the ox shall be stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> — And if a man open a pit, or if a man dig a pit, and do not cover it, and an ox or an ass fall into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit shall make it good, shall give money to the owner of them; and the dead &#91;ox&#93; shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> — And if one man's ox gore his neighbour's ox, and it die, then they shall sell the live ox, and divide the money thereof, and divide the dead also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Or if it be known that the ox have gored heretofore, and its owner have not kept him in, he shall in any case restore ox for ox; and the dead shall be his.</span>
