<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:40; Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 24:18"> And when the people saw that Moses delayed to come down from the mountain, the people collected together to Aaron, and said to him, Up, make us a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="32.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>god, who will go before us; for this Moses, the man that has brought us up out of the land of Egypt, — we do not know what is become of him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:24–27; Exodus 12:35–36; Ezekiel 16:17"> And Aaron said to them, Break off the golden rings that are in the ears of your wives, of your sons, and of your daughters, and bring &#91;them&#93; to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Isaiah 40:19–20; Jeremiah 10:9"> Then all the people broke off the golden rings that were in their ears, and brought &#91;them&#93; to Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16; 1 Kings 12:28"> And he took &#91;them&#93; out of their hand, and fashioned it with a chisel and made of it a molten calf: and they said, This is thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="32.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>god, Israel, who has brought thee up out of the land of Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 10:20; Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:37"> And Aaron saw &#91;it&#93;, and built an altar before it; and Aaron made a proclamation, and said, To-morrow is a feast to Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:7; Numbers 25:2; Acts 7:41–42"> And they rose up early on the morrow, and offered up burnt-offerings, and brought peace-offerings; and the people sat down to eat and to drink, and rose up to sport.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Hosea 9:9; Judges 2:19"> Then Jehovah said to Moses, Away, go down! for thy people, which thou hast brought out of the land of Egypt, is acting corruptly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Exodus 20:23; Exodus 20:3–4"> They have turned aside quickly out of the way that I commanded them: they have made themselves a molten calf, and have bowed down to it, and have sacrificed thereunto, and said, This is thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="32.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>god, Israel, who has brought thee up out of the land of Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:13; Isaiah 48:4"> And Jehovah said to Moses, I see this people, and behold, it is a stiff-necked people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:14; Numbers 14:12; Deuteronomy 9:19"> And now let me alone, that my anger may burn against them, and I may consume them; and I will make of thee a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–20; Deuteronomy 9:26–29; Psalms 106:23"> And Moses besought Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="32.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and said, Why, Jehovah, doth thy wrath burn against thy people, which thou hast brought forth out of the land of Egypt with great power and with a strong hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 14:13–16; Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9"> Why should the Egyptians speak, and say, For misfortune he has brought them out, to slay them on the mountains, and to annihilate them from the face of the earth? Turn from the heat of thine anger, and repent of this evil against thy people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 22:16"> Remember Abraham, Isaac and Israel, thy servants, to whom thou sworest by thyself, and saidst to them, I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and all this land that I have spoken of will I give to your seed, and they shall possess &#91;it&#93; for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:10; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 26:13"> And Jehovah repented of the evil that he had said he would do to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:15; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And Moses turned and went down from the mountain, &#91;with&#93; the two tables of the testimony in his hand — tables written on both their sides: on this side and on that were they written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:1"> And the tables &#91;were&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="32.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God's work, and the writing was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="32.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God's writing, engraven on the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Exodus 17:9; Joshua 6:20"> And Joshua heard the noise of the people as they shouted, and said to Moses, There is a shout of war in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–18; Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23"> And he said, It is not the sound of a shout of victory, neither is it the sound of a shout of defeat: it is the noise of alternate singing I hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:16–17; Jeremiah 31:32; Lamentations 5:15"> And it came to pass, when he came near the camp, and saw the calf and the dancing, that Moses' anger burned, and he cast the tables out of his hands, and shattered them beneath the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:21; 2 Kings 23:6; 2 Kings 23:15"> And he took the calf that they had made, and burned &#91;it&#93; with fire, and ground it to powder, and strewed &#91;it&#93; on the water, and made the children of Israel drink &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Joshua 7:19–26; 1 Kings 21:22"> And Moses said to Aaron, What has this people done to thee, that thou hast brought so great a sin on them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:24; Exodus 15:24; Exodus 16:20"> And Aaron said, Let not the anger of my lord burn! thou knowest the people, that they are &#91;set&#93; on mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 32:1–4; Exodus 32:8"> And they said to me, Make us a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="32.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>god, who will go before us; for this Moses, the man that has brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what is become of him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 32:4; Luke 10:29; Romans 3:10"> And I said to them, Who has gold? They broke &#91;it&#93; off, and gave &#91;it&#93; me, and I cast it into the fire, and there came out this calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:15"> And Moses saw the people how they were stripped; for Aaron had stripped them to &#91;their&#93; shame before their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Matthew 12:30; 2 Samuel 20:11"> And Moses stood in the gate of the camp, and said, He that is for Jehovah, &#91;let him come&#93; to me. And all the sons of Levi gathered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 25:5; Luke 14:26; Deuteronomy 33:8–9"> And he said to them, Thus saith Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="32.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God of Israel: Put every man his sword upon his hip; go and return from gate to gate through the camp, and slay every man his brother, and every man his friend, and every man his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:9; Malachi 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:8"> And the sons of Levi did according to the word of Moses; and there fell of the people that day about three thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Matthew 10:37; Zechariah 13:3"> And Moses said, Consecrate yourselves to-day to Jehovah, yea, every man with his son, and with his brother, and bring on yourselves a blessing to-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:20; Numbers 25:13; 2 Kings 17:21"> And it came to pass the next day, that Moses said to the people, Ye have sinned a great sin. And now I will go up to Jehovah: perhaps I shall make atonement for your sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 20:23; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:8"> And Moses returned to Jehovah, and said, Alas, this people has sinned a great sin, and they have made themselves a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="32.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>god of gold!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 69:28; Romans 9:3; Revelation 21:27"> And now, if thou wilt forgive their sin...but if not, blot me, I pray thee, out of thy book that thou hast written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Ezekiel 18:4; Revelation 20:12"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Whoever hath sinned against me, him will I blot out of my book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Deuteronomy 32:35; Matthew 23:35"> And now go, lead the people whither I have told thee: behold, my Angel shall go before thee; but in the day of my visiting I will visit their sin upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:41; Exodus 32:25; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> And Jehovah smote the people, because they made the calf, which Aaron had made.</span>
