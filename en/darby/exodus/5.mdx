<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> And afterwards Moses and Aaron went in, and said to Pharaoh, Thus saith Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel, Let my people go that they may celebrate a feast to me in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> And Pharaoh said, Who is Jehovah, to whose voice I am to hearken to let Israel go? I do not know Jehovah, neither will I let Israel go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> And they said, The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of the Hebrews has met with us: let us go, we pray thee, three days' journey into the wilderness, and sacrifice to Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God; lest he fall upon us with pestilence or with sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> And the king of Egypt said to them, Why do ye, Moses and Aaron, wish to have the people go off from their works? Away, to your burdens!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> And Pharaoh said, Behold the people of the land are now many, and ye wish to make them rest from their burdens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Pharaoh commanded the same day the taskmasters of the people, and their officers, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> Ye shall no more give the people straw to make brick, as heretofore: let them go and gather straw for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> And the number of the bricks they have made heretofore shall ye lay upon them: ye shall not diminish any of it, for they are idle; therefore they cry, saying, Let us go &#91;and&#93; sacrifice to our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Let them put heavier labour on the men, that they may be taken up with it, and not regard vain words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> And the taskmasters of the people and their officers went out and spoke to the people, saying, Thus says Pharaoh: I will not give you straw:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> go ye, get yourselves straw where ye may find it; but none of your work shall be diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> And the people were scattered abroad throughout the land of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the taskmasters urged &#91;them&#93;, saying, Fulfil your labours, the daily work, as when there was straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> And the officers of the children of Israel, whom Pharaoh's taskmasters had set over them, were beaten, &#91;and&#93; it was said, Why have ye not fulfilled your task in making brick, both yesterday and to-day, as heretofore?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the officers of the children of Israel came and cried to Pharaoh, saying, Why dost thou deal thus with thy bondmen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There is no straw given to thy bondmen, and they say to us, Make brick; and behold, thy bondmen are beaten, but it is the fault of thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> And he said, Ye are idle, idle! therefore ye say, Let us go and sacrifice to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> And now go — work! and straw shall not be given you, and ye shall deliver the measure of bricks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> And the officers of the children of Israel saw &#91;that&#93; it stood ill with them, because it was said, Ye shall not diminish anything from your bricks, the daily work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they met Moses and Aaron, who stood there to meet them, as they came out from Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> And they said to them, Jehovah look upon you and judge, that ye have made our odour to stink in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his bondmen, putting a sword into their hand to kill us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> And Moses returned to Jehovah, and said, Lord, why hast thou done evil to this people? why now hast thou sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> For ever since I came to Pharaoh to speak in thy name, he hath done evil to this people; neither hast thou delivered thy people at all!</span>
