<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:32; Exodus 31:1–11; Romans 2:13"> And Moses collected all the assembly of the children of Israel, and said to them, These are the things which Jehovah has commanded, to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Luke 13:14–15; Exodus 20:9–10"> Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day there shall be to you a holy day, a sabbath of rest to Jehovah: whoever does work on it shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Numbers 15:32–36; Exodus 12:16"> Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your dwellings upon the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:1–2"> And Moses spoke to all the assembly of the children of Israel, saying, This is the word which Jehovah has commanded, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Psalms 110:3; Exodus 25:2–7"> Take from among you a heave-offering to Jehovah: every one whose heart &#91;is&#93; willing, let him bring it, Jehovah's heave-offering — gold, and silver, and copper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–14; Exodus 26:31; Exodus 28:15"> and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and byssus, and goats' &#91;hair&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and rams' skins dyed red, and badgers' skins, and acacia-wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:1–40; Exodus 30:23"> and oil for the light, and spices for the anointing oil, and for the incense of fragrant drugs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 39:6–14; Exodus 28:9; Exodus 25:5"> and onyx stones, and stones to be set for the ephod, and for the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; Exodus 36:1–4"> And all who are wise-hearted among you shall come and make all that Jehovah has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–37; Exodus 36:8–34; Exodus 31:7–9"> the tabernacle, its tent, and its covering, its clasps, and its boards, its bars, its pillars, and its bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 37:1–9"> the ark, and its staves; the mercy-seat, and the veil of separation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:23–30; Leviticus 24:5–6; Exodus 37:10–16"> the table and its staves, and all its utensils, and the shewbread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Matthew 5:14–15; Exodus 37:17–24"> and the lamp-stand for the light, and its utensils, and its lamps, and the oil for the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 30:22–38; Exodus 37:25–28"> and the altar of incense, and its staves; and the anointing-oil, and the incense of fragrant drugs; and the entrance-curtain at the entrance of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 30:18–21; Exodus 38:1–8"> the altar of burnt-offering, and the copper grating for it, its staves, and all its utensils; the laver and its stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Exodus 38:9–20; Exodus 27:9–19"> the hangings of the court, its pillars, and its bases, and the curtains of the gate of the court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:19"> the pegs of the tabernacle, and the pegs of the court, and their cords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Numbers 4:5–15; Exodus 28:1–43"> the garments of service, to do service in the sanctuary, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to serve as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the assembly of the children of Israel departed from before Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:2; Exodus 35:26; 1 Chronicles 29:14"> And they came, every one whose heart moved him, and every one whose spirit prompted him; they brought Jehovah's heave-offering for the work of the tent of meeting, and for all its service, and for the holy garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; Matthew 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> And they came, both men and women; every one who was of willing heart brought nose-rings, and earrings, and rings, and bracelets, all kinds of utensils of gold: every man that waved a wave-offering of gold to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:8; Exodus 25:2–7; Exodus 35:6–10"> And every man with whom was found blue, and purple, and scarlet, and byssus, and goats' &#91;hair&#93;, and rams' skins dyed red, and badgers' skins, brought &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12"> All they that offered a heave-offering of silver and copper brought Jehovah's heave-offering. And every one with whom was found acacia-wood for all manner of work of the service, brought &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Luke 8:2–3; Proverbs 14:1"> And every woman that was wise-hearted spun with her hands, and brought what she had spun: the blue, and the purple, and the scarlet, and the byssus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:21; Exodus 36:8; Exodus 35:29"> And all the women whose heart moved them in wisdom spun goats' &#91;hair&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 Chronicles 29:6; Exodus 35:9"> And the principal men brought the onyx stones, and the stones to be set, for the ephod, and for the breastplate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 35:8"> and the spice, and the oil for the light, and for the anointing oil, and for the incense of fragrant drugs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Exodus 35:21–22; Exodus 35:4"> The children of Israel brought a voluntary offering to Jehovah, every man and woman whose heart prompted them to bring for all manner of work, which Jehovah, by the hand of Moses, had commanded to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 31:1–6; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And Moses said to the children of Israel, See, Jehovah has called by name Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> and he has filled him with the spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="35.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, in wisdom, in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and to devise artistic things: to work in gold, and in silver, and in copper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and in cutting of stones, for setting, and in carving of wood, to execute all artistic work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 31:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> and he has put in his heart to teach, he and Aholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> he has filled them with wisdom of heart, to work all manner of work of the engraver, and of the artificer, and of the embroiderer, in blue, and in purple, in scarlet, and in byssus, and of the weaver, &#91;even&#93; of them that do every kind of work, and of those that devise artistic work.</span>
