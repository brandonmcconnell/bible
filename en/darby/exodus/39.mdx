<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 31:10; Exodus 35:23; Exodus 35:19"> And of the blue and purple and scarlet they made garments of service, for service in the sanctuary, and made the holy garments for Aaron; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 28:6–12; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 25:7"> And he made the ephod of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:8; Exodus 26:1"> And they beat the gold into thin plates, and cut it &#91;into&#93; wires, to work it artistically into the blue, and into the purple, and into the scarlet, and into the byssus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They made shoulder-pieces for it, joining it: at its two ends was it joined together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Matthew 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> And the girdle of his ephod, which was on it, was of the same, according to its work, of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 28:9; Ezekiel 28:13; Job 28:16"> And they wrought the onyx stones mounted in enclosures of gold, engraved with the engravings of a seal, according to the names of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:7; Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:29"> And he put them on the shoulder-pieces of the ephod, &#91;as&#93; stones of memorial for the children of Israel; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Leviticus 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> And he made the breastplate of artistic work, like the work of the ephod, of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was square; double did they make the breastplate, a span the length thereof, and a span the breadth thereof, doubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 28:21; Exodus 28:16–17; Revelation 21:19–21"> And they set in it four rows of stones: &#91;one&#93; row, a sardoin, a topaz, and an emerald — the first row;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 28:18; Ezekiel 28:13"> and the second row, a carbuncle, a sapphire, and a diamond;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the third row, an opal, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the fourth row, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a jasper; mounted in enclosures of gold in their settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:12"> And the stones were according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve, according to their names, engraved as a seal, every one according to his name, for the twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:28; John 17:12; 1 Peter 1:5"> And they made on the breastplate chains of laced work of wreathen work, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:12"> And they made two settings of gold, and two gold rings, and put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And they put the two wreathen &#91;cords&#93; of gold in the two rings on the ends of the breastplate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Song of Solomon 1:10"> and the two ends of the two wreathen &#91;cords&#93; they fastened to the two settings, and put them on the shoulder-pieces of the ephod, on the front thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they made two rings of gold, and put &#91;them&#93; on the two ends of the breastplate, on the border thereof, which faceth the ephod inwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 26:3"> And they made two rings of gold, and put them upon the two shoulder-pieces of the ephod underneath, to the front thereof, just by the coupling thereof, above the girdle of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; 1 Corinthians 1:25; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> And they bound the breastplate with its rings to the rings of the ephod with lace of blue, that it might be above the girdle of the ephod, and that the breastplate might not be loosed from the ephod; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:31–35"> And he made the cloak of the ephod of woven work, all of blue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and the opening of the cloak in its middle, as the opening of a coat of mail; a binding was round about the opening, that it should not rend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 28:33; Galatians 5:22"> And they made on the skirts of the cloak pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet, twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 28:33–34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Psalms 89:15"> And they made bells of pure gold, and put the bells between the pomegranates, in the skirts of the cloak, round about, between the pomegranates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 28:34; Song of Solomon 4:13; Song of Solomon 6:7"> a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, in the skirts of the cloak round about, for service; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; 1 Peter 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> And they made the vests of byssus of woven work, for Aaron, and for his sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Ezekiel 44:18; Exodus 28:39"> and the turban of byssus; and the ornamental caps, of byssus; and the linen trousers, of twined byssus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:39"> and the girdle, of twined byssus, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, of embroidery; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 1:3"> And they made the thin plate, the holy diadem, of pure gold, and wrote on it with the writing of the engravings of a seal, Holiness to Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And they fastened to it a lace of blue, to fasten &#91;it&#93; on the turban above; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 39:42–43; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 25:40"> And all the labour of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was ended; and the children of Israel had done &#91;it&#93; according to all that Jehovah had commanded Moses — so had they done &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 35:11–19; Exodus 31:7–11; Exodus 36:1–38"> And they brought the tabernacle to Moses — the tent, and all its utensils, its clasps, its boards, its bars, and its pillars, and its bases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and the covering of rams' skins dyed red, and the covering of badgers' skins, and the veil of separation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 25:17; Hebrews 9:8"> the ark of the testimony, and its staves, and the mercy-seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; 1 Kings 7:48"> the table, all its utensils, and the shewbread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Exodus 27:21; Matthew 5:14–16"> the pure candlestick, its lamps, the lamps set in order, and all its utensils, and the oil for the light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:7; Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:3"> and the golden altar, and the anointing oil, and the incense of fragrant drugs; and the curtain for the entrance of the tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Exodus 38:30"> the copper altar, and the copper grating that was for it, its staves and all its utensils; the laver and its stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 27:9–16"> the hangings of the court, its pillars, and its bases; and the curtain for the gate of the court, its cords, and its pegs; and all the vessel's of service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 28:2; Exodus 31:10; Exodus 39:1"> the garments of service, for service in the sanctuary; the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and his sons' garments, to serve as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 23:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> According to all that Jehovah had commanded Moses, so had the children of Israel done all the labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27; Leviticus 9:22–23; 2 Samuel 6:18"> And Moses saw all the work, and behold, they had done it as Jehovah had commanded — so had they done &#91;it&#93;; and Moses blessed them.</span>
