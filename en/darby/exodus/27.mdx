<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> And thou shalt make the altar of acacia-wood, five cubits the length, and five cubits the breadth; the altar shall be square; and the height thereof three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> And thou shalt make its horns at the four corners thereof; its horns shall be of itself; and thou shalt overlay it with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> And thou shalt make its pots to cleanse it of the fat, its shovels, and its bowls, and its forks, and its firepans; for all the utensils thereof thou shalt employ copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> And thou shalt make for it a grating of network of copper; and on the net shalt thou make four copper rings at its four corners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> and thou shalt put it under the ledge of the altar beneath, and the net shall be to the very middle of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> And thou shalt make staves for the altar, staves of acacia-wood, and overlay them with copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> And its staves shall be put into the rings, that the staves may be on both sides of the altar, when it is carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> Hollow with boards shalt thou make it: as it hath been shewn thee on the mountain, so shall they make &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> And thou shalt make the court of the tabernacle. On the south side, southward, hangings for the court of twined byssus; a hundred cubits the length for the one side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> and the twenty pillars thereof, and their twenty bases of copper, the hooks of the pillars and their connecting-rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And likewise on the north side in length, hangings a hundred &#91;cubits&#93; long, and its twenty pillars, and their twenty bases of copper; the hooks of the pillars and their connecting-rods of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> — And the breadth of the court on the west side, hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars ten, and their bases ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> — And the breadth of the court on the east side, eastward, fifty cubits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> the hangings on the one wing, of fifteen cubits; their pillars three, and their bases three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And on the other wing hangings of fifteen &#91;cubits&#93;; their pillars three, and their bases three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> — And for the gate of the court a curtain of twenty cubits, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus, embroidered with needlework; their pillars four, and their bases four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars of the court round about shall be fastened together with &#91;rods of&#93; silver; their hooks of silver, and their bases of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the court shall be a hundred cubits, and the breadth fifty everywhere, and the height five cubits of twined byssus; and their bases of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the utensils of the tabernacle for the service thereof and all the pegs thereof, and all the pegs of the court shall be of copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> And thou shalt command the children of Israel, that they bring thee olive oil, pure, beaten, for the light, to light the lamp continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the tent of meeting outside the veil, which is before the testimony, Aaron and his sons shall dress them from evening to morning before Jehovah: &#91;it is&#93; an everlasting statute, for their generations, on the part of the children of Israel.</span>
