<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> And Bezaleel made the ark of acacia-wood; two cubits and a half the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> And he overlaid it with pure gold inside and outside, and made a border of gold upon it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he cast for it four rings of gold, for its four corners: two rings on the one side of it, and two rings on the other side of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> And he made staves of acacia-wood and overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> And he put the staves into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> — And he made a mercy-seat of pure gold; two cubits and a half the length thereof, and one cubit and a half the breadth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> And he made two cherubim of gold; of beaten work did he make them, at the two ends of the mercy-seat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> one cherub at the end of one side, and one cherub at the end of the other side; out of the mercy-seat he made the two cherubim at the two ends thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> And the cherubim spread out &#91;their&#93; wings over it, covering over with their wings the mercy-seat; and their faces were opposite to one another: the faces of the cherubim were &#91;turned&#93; toward the mercy-seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> And he made the table of acacia-wood; two cubits the length thereof, and a cubit the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he overlaid it with pure gold, and made upon it a border of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he made for it a margin of a hand-breadth round about; and made a border of gold for the margin thereof round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he cast for it four rings of gold, and put the rings on the four corners that were on the four feet thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Close to the margin were the rings, as receptacles of the staves to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he made the staves of acacia-wood, and overlaid them with gold, to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> And he made the utensils that were on the table, the dishes thereof, and the cups thereof, and the bowls thereof, and the goblets with which to pour out, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> And he made the candlestick of pure gold; &#91;of&#93; beaten work he made the candlestick: its base, and its shaft, its cups, its knobs, and its flowers were of itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And six branches went out of the sides thereof — three branches of the candlestick out of the one side thereof, and three branches of the candlestick out of the other side thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> &#91;there were&#93; three cups shaped like almonds in the one branch, a knob and a flower; and three cups shaped like almonds in the other branch, a knob and a flower: so in the six branches which went out of the candlestick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And in the candlestick were four cups, shaped like almonds, its knobs, and its flowers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> and a knob under two branches thereof, and &#91;again&#93; a knob under two branches thereof, and &#91;again&#93; a knob under two branches thereof, for the six branches which went out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> Their knobs and their branches were of itself — all of one beaten work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And he made the seven lamps thereof, and the snuffers thereof, and the snuff-trays thereof, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of a talent of pure gold he made it, and all its utensils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> And he made the altar of incense of acacia-wood; a cubit the length thereof, and a cubit the breadth thereof, square, and two cubits the height thereof: its horns were of itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he overlaid it with pure gold, the top thereof, and the sides thereof round about, and the horns thereof; and made upon it a border of gold round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And he made two rings of gold for it under its border, by its two corners, on the two sides thereof, as receptacles for the staves with which to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he made the staves of acacia-wood, and overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> And he made the holy anointing oil, and the pure incense of fragrant drugs, according to the work of the perfumer.</span>
