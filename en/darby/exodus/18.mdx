<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:16; Psalms 77:14–15; Exodus 3:1"> And Jethro the priest of Midian, Moses' father-in-law, heard of all that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God had done to Moses, and to Israel his people; that Jehovah had brought Israel out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Exodus 4:25–26"> And Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, took Zipporah, Moses' wife, after he had sent her back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 2:22; Acts 7:29; Psalms 39:12"> and her two sons, of whom the name of the one was Gershom — for he said, I have been a sojourner in a foreign land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> — and the name of the other, Eliezer — For the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of my father has been my help, and has delivered me from the sword of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 24:16–17"> And Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, came to Moses with his sons and his wife into the wilderness, where he encamped at the mountain of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he sent word to Moses: I, thy father-in-law Jethro, am come to thee, and thy wife, and her two sons with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 29:13; Genesis 14:17; 2 Samuel 11:7"> And Moses went out to meet his father-in-law, and did obeisance, and kissed him; and they asked each other &#91;after&#93; their welfare, and went into the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; Psalms 107:2"> And Moses told his father-in-law all that Jehovah had done to Pharaoh and to the Egyptians for Israel's sake; all the trouble that had befallen them on the way, and &#91;how&#93; Jehovah had delivered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> And Jethro rejoiced for all the goodness that Jehovah had done to Israel; that he had delivered them out of the hand of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; 2 Samuel 18:28; Luke 1:68"> And Jethro said, Blessed be Jehovah, who has delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of Pharaoh; who has delivered the people from under the hand of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:3; Exodus 15:11; 2 Chronicles 2:5"> Now I know that Jehovah is greater than all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>gods; for in the thing in which they acted haughtily &#91;he was&#93; above them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Deuteronomy 12:7; Exodus 24:11"> And Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, took a burnt-offering and sacrifices for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="18.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God; and Aaron came, and all the elders of Israel, to eat bread with Moses' father-in-law in the presence of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="18.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; Job 29:7; Judges 5:10"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that Moses sat to judge the people; and the people stood by Moses from the morning to the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Moses' father-in-law saw all that he did with the people, and said, What is this thing which thou art doing with the people? why dost thou sit alone, and all the people are standing by thee from morning to evening?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5; Numbers 9:8"> And Moses said to his father-in-law, Because the people come to me to enquire of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="18.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:14; 1 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Samuel 15:3"> When they have a matter, they come to me, and I judge between one and another; and I make known &#91;to them&#93; the statutes of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="18.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, and his laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Moses' father-in-law said to him, The thing that thou art doing is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 11:14–17; Acts 6:1–4; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> Thou wilt be quite exhausted, both thou and this people that is with thee; for the thing is too heavy for thee: thou canst not perform it alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 27:5; Deuteronomy 5:5; Exodus 20:19"> Hearken now to my voice: I will give thee counsel, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="18.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God shall be with thee. Be thou for the people with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="18.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, and bring the matters before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="18.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:1; Psalms 143:8; Deuteronomy 1:18"> and teach them the statutes and the laws, and make known to them the way in which they must walk, and the work that they must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; 2 Chronicles 19:5–10"> But do thou provide among all the people able men, such as fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="18.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God, men of truth, hating covetousness; and place &#91;them&#93; over them, chiefs of thousands, chiefs of hundreds, chiefs of fifties, and chiefs of tens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Numbers 27:2; Numbers 15:33"> that they may judge the people at all times; and it shall be &#91;that&#93; they shall bring to thee every great matter, and that they shall judge every small matter, and they shall lighten &#91;the task&#93; on thee, and they shall bear &#91;it&#93; with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:6–7; Exodus 18:18; 1 Samuel 8:22"> If thou do this thing, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="18.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God command thee &#91;so&#93;, thou wilt be able to endure, and all this people shall also go to their place in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; Exodus 18:19; Exodus 18:2–5"> And Moses hearkened to the voice of his father-in-law, and did all that he had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21; Acts 6:5"> And Moses chose able men out of all Israel, and made them heads over the people, chiefs of thousands, chiefs of hundreds, chiefs of fifties, and chiefs of tens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 18:22; Exodus 18:14–15; Deuteronomy 17:8"> And they judged the people at all times: the hard matters they brought to Moses, but every small matter they judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–30; Judges 19:9; Genesis 31:55"> And Moses sent away his father-in-law, and he departed into his land.</span>
