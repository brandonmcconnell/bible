<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> Then wrought Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every man that was wise-hearted, in whom Jehovah had put wisdom and understanding to know how to work all manner of work of the service of the sanctuary — according to all that Jehovah had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And Moses called Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every man that was wise-hearted, in whose heart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="36.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God had put wisdom, every one whose heart moved him to come to the work to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> And they took from Moses every heave-offering that the children of Israel had brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary, to make it. And they still brought him voluntary offerings morning by morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> And all the wise men that wrought all the work of the sanctuary came, every man from his work which they wrought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> and spoke to Moses, saying, The people bring much more than enough for the service of the work that Jehovah commanded to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Moses gave commandment, and they caused it to be proclaimed through the camp, saying, Let neither man nor woman make any more work for the heave-offering of the sanctuary! So the people were restrained from bringing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> for the work they had was sufficient for all the work to do it, and it was too much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And every wise-hearted man among those that wrought the work of the tabernacle made ten curtains of twined byssus, and blue, and purple, and scarlet: &#91;with&#93; cherubim of artistic work did he make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of one curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the breadth of one curtain four cubits — one measure for all the curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he coupled five of the curtains one to another, and &#91;the other&#93; five curtains coupled he one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> And he made loops of blue on the edge of one curtain at the edge of the coupling; he did likewise in the edge of the outermost curtain in the other coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> He made fifty loops in one curtain, and he made fifty loops at the end of the curtain that was in the other coupling: the loops were opposite to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> And he made fifty clasps of gold, and coupled the curtains together with the clasps, so that the tabernacle became one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> And he made curtains of goats' &#91;hair&#93; for the tent over the tabernacle: eleven curtains did he make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of one curtain was thirty cubits, and four cubits the breadth of one curtain — one measure for the eleven curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he coupled five of the curtains by themselves, and six of the curtains by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in the coupling, and fifty loops made he on the edge of the curtain in the other coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he made fifty clasps of copper to couple the tent, that it might be one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> And he made a covering for the tent &#91;of&#93; rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of badgers' skins above &#91;that&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> And he made the boards for the tabernacle of acacia-wood, standing up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> ten cubits the length of the boards, and one cubit and a half the breadth of one board;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> two tenons in one board, connected one with the other: thus did he make for all the boards of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he made the boards for the tabernacle: twenty boards for the south side southward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and he made forty bases of silver under the twenty boards, two bases under one board, for its two tenons, and two bases under another board for its two tenons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And for the other side of the tabernacle, on the side toward the north, he made twenty boards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and their forty bases of silver, two bases under one board, and two bases under another board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> And at the rear of the tabernacle, westward, he made six boards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and he made two boards for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> and they were joined beneath, and were coupled together at the top thereof into one ring: thus he did to both of them in both the corners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> and there were eight boards, and their silver bases: sixteen bases, under every board two bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> — And he made bars of acacia-wood: five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> and five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the tabernacle at the rear, westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he made the middle bar in the midst of the boards reach from one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he overlaid the boards with gold; and made their rings of gold &#91;as&#93; receptacles for the bars; and overlaid the bars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> And he made the veil of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus: of artistic work he made it &#91;with&#93; cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> And he made four pillars of acacia&#91;-wood&#93; for it, and overlaid them with gold; their hooks were of gold; and he cast for them four bases of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> And he made a curtain for the entrance of the tent of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus, of embroidery;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> and its five pillars with their hooks; and he overlaid their capitals and their connecting-rods with gold; and their five bases were of copper.</span>
