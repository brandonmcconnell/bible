<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> And a man of the house of Levi went and took a daughter of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; Psalms 112:5"> And the woman conceived, and bore a son. And she saw him that he was fair, and hid him three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> And when she could no longer hide him, she took for him an ark of reeds, and plastered it with resin and with pitch, and put the child in it, and laid &#91;it&#93; in the sedge on the bank of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> And his sister stood afar off to see what would happen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 9:9"> And the daughter of Pharaoh went down to bathe in the river; and her maids went along by the river's side. And she saw the ark in the midst of the sedge, and sent her handmaid and fetched it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> And she opened &#91;it&#93;, and saw the child, and behold, the boy wept. And she had compassion on him, and said, This is &#91;one&#93; of the Hebrews' children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> And his sister said to Pharaoh's daughter, Shall I go and call thee a wet-nurse of the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalms 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> And Pharaoh's daughter said to her, Go. And the damsel went and called the child's mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Pharaoh's daughter said to her, Take this child away and nurse it for me, and I will give &#91;thee&#93; thy wages. And the woman took the child and nursed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> And when the child was grown, she brought him to Pharaoh's daughter, and he became her son. And she called his name Moses, and said, Because I drew him out of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> And it came to pass in those days, when Moses was grown, that he went out to his brethren and looked on their burdens; and he saw an Egyptian smiting a Hebrew, one of his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> And he turned this way and that way, and when he saw that there was no man, he smote the Egyptian, and hid him in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> And he went out on the second day, and behold, two Hebrew men were quarrelling; and he said to him that was in the wrong, Why art thou smiting thy neighbour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> And he said, Who made thee ruler and judge over us? dost thou intend to kill me, as thou killedst the Egyptian? Then Moses feared, and said, Surely the matter is known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> And Pharaoh heard of this matter, and sought to slay Moses. But Moses fled from before Pharaoh, and dwelt in the land of Midian. And he sat by the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> And the priest of Midian had seven daughters; and they came and drew &#91;water&#93;, and filled the troughs, to water their father's flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> And the shepherds came and drove them away; but Moses rose and helped them, and watered their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> And when they came to Reuel their father, he said, Why are ye come so soon to-day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> And they said, An Egyptian delivered us out of the hand of the shepherds, and also drew &#91;water&#93; abundantly for us, and watered the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> And he said to his daughters, And where is he? why then have ye left the man behind? Call him, that he may eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> And Moses consented to remain with the man; and he gave Moses Zipporah his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> And she bore a son, and he called his name Gershom; for he said, I have been a sojourner in a foreign land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> And it came to pass during those many days, that the king of Egypt died. And the children of Israel sighed because of the bondage, and cried; and their cry came up to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God because of the bondage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Psalms 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God heard their groaning, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Psalms 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God looked upon the children of Israel, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God acknowledged &#91;them&#93;.</span>
