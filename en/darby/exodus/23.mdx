<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:5; Proverbs 19:5; Deuteronomy 19:16–21"> Thou shalt not accept a false report; extend not thy hand to the wicked, to be an unrighteous witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:10–11; Proverbs 1:15; Luke 23:23–24"> Thou shalt not follow the multitude for evil; neither shalt thou answer in a cause, to go after the multitude to pervert &#91;judgment&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; Leviticus 19:15; Psalms 82:2–3"> Neither shalt thou favour a poor man in his cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:1–4; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:44"> — If thou meet thine enemy's ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt certainly bring it back to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4"> If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lying under its burden, beware of leaving &#91;it&#93; to him: thou shalt certainly loosen &#91;it&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 5:28; Leviticus 19:15"> Thou shalt not pervert the judgment of thy poor in his cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Exodus 34:7; Exodus 23:1"> Thou shalt keep far from the cause of falsehood; and the innocent and righteous slay not; for I will not justify the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> And thou shalt take no bribe; for the bribe blindeth those whose eyes are open, and perverteth the words of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 22:21; Deuteronomy 24:14–18"> And the stranger thou shalt not oppress; for ye know the spirit of the stranger, for ye have been strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:3–4"> And six years thou shalt sow thy land, and gather in its produce;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:34–35; Leviticus 25:2–7; Leviticus 25:11–12"> but in the seventh thou shalt let it rest and lie &#91;fallow&#93;, that the poor of thy people may eat &#91;of it&#93;; and what they leave, the beasts of the field shall eat. In like manner thou shalt deal with thy vineyard, and with thine olive-tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 35:3"> — Six days thou shalt do thy work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest; that thine ox and thine ass may rest, and the son of thy handmaid, and the stranger may be refreshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:7; Zechariah 13:2; 1 Timothy 4:16"> And ye shall be on your guard as to everything that I have said unto you; and shall make no mention of the name of other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods — it shall not be heard in thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:22–24; Leviticus 23:34"> Thrice in the year thou shalt celebrate a feast to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:7; Exodus 34:20; Numbers 28:16–25"> Thou shalt keep the feast of unleavened bread, (thou shalt eat unleavened bread seven days, as I have commanded thee, in the time appointed of the month Abib; for in it thou camest out from Egypt; and none shall appear in my presence empty;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 22:29; Leviticus 23:9–21"> and the feast of harvest, the first-fruits of thy labours which thou hast sown in the field, and the feast of in-gathering, at the end of the year, when thou gatherest in thy labours out of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:14"> Three times in the year all thy males shall appear in the presence of the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Leviticus 2:11; Deuteronomy 16:4"> Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leavened bread; neither shall the fat of my feast remain all night until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 14:21; Deuteronomy 26:10"> The first of the first-fruits of thy land thou shalt bring into the house of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God. Thou shalt not boil a kid in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Psalms 91:11; Exodus 32:34"> Behold, I send an Angel before thee, to keep thee in the way, and to bring thee to the place that I have prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:56; Psalms 78:40; Hebrews 12:25"> Be careful in his presence, and hearken unto his voice: do not provoke him, for he will not forgive your transgressions; for my name is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Deuteronomy 30:7; Zechariah 2:8"> But if thou shalt diligently hearken unto his voice, and do all that I shall say, then I will be an enemy to thine enemies, and an adversary to thine adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; Genesis 15:19–21"> For mine Angel shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Amorites and the Hittites and the Perizzites and the Canaanites, the Hivites and the Jebusites; and I will cut them off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Numbers 33:52"> Thou shalt not bow down to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods, nor serve them, nor do after their deeds; but thou shalt utterly destroy them, and utterly shatter their statues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Joshua 22:5"> And ye shall serve Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God; and he shall bless thy bread and thy water; and I will take sickness away from thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:14; Job 5:26; Deuteronomy 28:4"> There shall nothing cast their young, nor be barren, in thy land; the number of thy days will I fulfil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Genesis 35:5; Deuteronomy 11:25"> I will send my fear before thee, and confound every people to which thou comest, and will make all thine enemies turn their back to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Joshua 24:11–12"> And I will send hornets before thee, which shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite, from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:22; Joshua 17:12–13; Judges 3:1–4"> I will not drive them out from before thee in one year: lest the land become desolate, and the beast of the field multiply against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> By little and little I will drive them out from before thee, until thou art fruitful, and possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 21:44"> And I will set thy bounds from the Red Sea even unto the sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness unto the river; for I will give the inhabitants of the land into your hand, that thou mayest dispossess them from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Exodus 34:12; Exodus 34:15"> Thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor with their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="23.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 106:36; Deuteronomy 7:16; Exodus 34:12"> They shall not dwell in thy land, lest they make thee sin against me; for if thou serve their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="23.33 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods, it is sure to be a snare unto thee.</span>
