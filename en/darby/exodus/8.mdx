<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 5:1"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Go unto Pharaoh, and say unto him, Thus saith Jehovah: Let my people go, that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:45; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 7:14"> And if thou refuse to let &#91;them&#93; go, behold, I will smite all thy borders with frogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 105:30; Exodus 12:34"> And the river shall swarm with frogs, and they shall go up and come into thy house, and into thy bedroom, and upon thy bed, and into the house of thy bondmen, and upon thy people, and into thine ovens, and into thy kneading-troughs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 4:37; Acts 12:22–23"> And the frogs shall come up both upon thee and upon thy people, and upon all thy bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 7:19"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Say unto Aaron, Stretch out thy hand with thy staff over the streams, over the rivers, and over the ponds, and cause frogs to come up on the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 105:30; Psalms 78:45; Revelation 16:13"> And Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt; and the frogs came up, and covered the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; Exodus 7:22; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> And the scribes did so with their sorceries, and brought up frogs on the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 10:17; Exodus 9:28; Exodus 8:25–28"> And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron, and said, Intreat Jehovah, that he may take away the frogs from me and from my people; and I will let the people go, that they may sacrifice to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 10:15; Judges 7:2"> And Moses said to Pharaoh, Glory over me, for what time shall I intreat for thee, and for thy bondmen, and for thy people, to cut off the frogs from thee and from thy houses; &#91;so that&#93; they shall remain in the river only?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 46:9; Psalms 86:8; 1 Chronicles 17:20"> And he said, For to-morrow. And he said, Be it according to thy word; that thou mayest know that there is none like Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.10 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 8:3; Exodus 8:9"> And the frogs shall depart from thee, and from thy houses, and from thy bondmen, and from thy people: they shall remain in the river only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 5:16–18; Exodus 8:30; Exodus 10:18"> And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh; and Moses cried to Jehovah because of the frogs that he had brought against Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10–12"> And Jehovah did according to the word of Moses; and the frogs died out of the houses, out of the courts, and out of the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 8:24; Exodus 7:21; Joel 2:20"> And they gathered them in heaps; and the land stank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Hebrews 3:8"> And Pharaoh saw that there was respite; and he hardened his heart, and hearkened not to them, as Jehovah had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 8:5; Exodus 8:17"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Say unto Aaron, Stretch out thy staff, and smite the dust of the earth, and it shall become gnats throughout the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 105:31; Isaiah 23:9; Acts 12:23"> And they did so; and Aaron stretched out his hand with his staff, and smote the dust of the earth, and there arose gnats on man and on beast: all the dust of the land became gnats throughout the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 7:11; Exodus 9:11; Daniel 5:8"> And the scribes did so with their sorceries, to bring forth gnats; but they could not. And the gnats were on man and on beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 11:20; Psalms 8:3; Exodus 7:5"> Then the scribes said to Pharaoh, This is the finger of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.19 Elohim">b</a></sup>God! But Pharaoh's heart was stubborn, and he hearkened not to them, as Jehovah had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 7:15; Exodus 9:13; Exodus 8:1"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Rise up early in the morning, and stand before Pharaoh — behold, he will go out to the water — and say to him, Thus saith Jehovah, Let my people go, that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 78:45; Isaiah 7:18; Psalms 105:31"> For, if thou do not let my people go, behold, I will send dog-flies upon thee, and upon thy bondmen, and upon thy people, and into thy houses; and the houses of the Egyptians shall be full of dog-flies, and also the ground on which they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 9:4; Malachi 3:18; Exodus 10:23"> And I will distinguish in that day the land of Goshen, in which my people dwell, that no dog-flies shall be there; that thou mayest know that I Jehovah am in the midst of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And I will put a separation between my people and thy people; to-morrow shall this sign be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 105:31; Psalms 78:45; Exodus 8:21"> And Jehovah did so; and there came dog-flies in a multitude into the house of Pharaoh, and &#91;into&#93; the houses of his bondmen; and throughout the land of Egypt, the land was corrupted by the dog-flies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:27; Exodus 10:16"> And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron, and said, Go, sacrifice to your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Genesis 46:34; Deuteronomy 7:25–26"> And Moses said, It is not proper to do so; for we should sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians to Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.26 Elohim">d</a></sup>God: lo, if we sacrificed the abomination of the Egyptians before their eyes, would they not stone us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 3:12; Exodus 34:11"> We will go three days' journey into the wilderness, and sacrifice to Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.27 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, as he shall command us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 9:28; 1 Kings 13:6"> And Pharaoh said, I will let you go, that you may sacrifice to Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.28 Elohim">f</a></sup>God in the wilderness; only, go not very far away: intreat for me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Jeremiah 42:20–21; Exodus 8:8"> And Moses said, Behold, I go out from thee, and will intreat Jehovah; and the dog-flies will depart from Pharaoh, from his bondmen, and from his people, to-morrow; only let not Pharaoh deal deceitfully any more in not letting the people go to sacrifice to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 8:12; Exodus 9:33; James 5:16"> And Moses went out from Pharaoh, and intreated Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Jehovah did according to the word of Moses; and he removed the dog-flies from Pharaoh, from his bondmen, and from his people; there remained not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 8:15; Exodus 4:21; Romans 2:5"> And Pharaoh hardened his heart this time also, and would not let the people go.</span>
