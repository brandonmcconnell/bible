<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Exodus 15:21; Psalms 106:12"> Then sang Moses and the children of Israel this song to Jehovah, and spoke, saying, I will sing unto Jehovah, for he is highly exalted: The horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 118:14; Psalms 18:46"> My strength and song is Jah, and he is become my salvation: This is my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.2 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and I will glorify him; My father's<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and I will extol him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 24:8; Exodus 14:14; Revelation 19:11–21"> Jehovah is a man of war; Jehovah, his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–28; Exodus 14:6–7"> Pharaoh's chariots and his army hath he cast into the sea; His chosen captains also are drowned in the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:11; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:28"> The depths covered them; they sank to the bottom as a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 118:15–16; Isaiah 51:9; Revelation 2:27"> Thy right hand, Jehovah, is become glorious in power: Thy right hand, Jehovah, hath dashed in pieces the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14; Malachi 4:1"> And by the greatness of thine excellency thou hast overthrown thine adversaries: Thou sentest forth thy burning wrath, it consumed them as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 4:9; Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22"> And by the breath of thy nostrils the waters were heaped up; The streams stood as a mound; The depths were congealed in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 14:5; Isaiah 53:12"> The enemy said, I will pursue, I will overtake, I will divide the spoil; my soul shall be sated upon them; I will unsheath my sword, my hand shall dispossess them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Deuteronomy 11:4; Exodus 15:5"> Thou didst blow with thy breath, the sea covered them; They sank as lead in the mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 8:23; Psalms 89:5–8; 1 Samuel 2:2"> Who is like unto thee, Jehovah, among the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>gods? Who is like unto thee, glorifying thyself in holiness, Fearful &#91;in&#93; praises, doing wonders?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:6"> Thou stretchedst out thy right hand, the earth swallowed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; 1 Peter 1:5; Psalms 77:14–15"> Thou by thy mercy hast led forth the people that thou hast redeemed; Thou hast guided them by thy strength unto the abode of thy holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:9–10; Psalms 48:6"> The peoples heard it, they were afraid: A thrill seized the inhabitants of Philistia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 5:1; Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9"> Then the princes of Edom were amazed; The mighty men of Moab, trembling hath seized them; All the inhabitants of Canaan melted away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Psalms 74:2; Joshua 2:9"> Fear and dread fall upon them; By the greatness of thine arm they are still as a stone; Till thy people pass over, Jehovah, Till the people pass over that thou hast purchased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Psalms 80:8; Psalms 78:54–55"> Thou shalt bring them in, and plant them in the mountain of thine inheritance, The place that thou, Jehovah, hast made thy dwelling, The Sanctuary, Lord, that thy hands have prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 10:16; Psalms 29:10; Psalms 146:10"> Jehovah shall reign for ever and ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28–29; Exodus 14:22–23; Hebrews 11:29"> For the horse of Pharaoh, with his chariots and with his horsemen, came into the sea, and Jehovah brought again the waters of the sea upon them; and the children of Israel went on dry &#91;ground&#93; through the midst of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; 1 Samuel 18:6; Psalms 68:25"> And Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took the tambour in her hand, and all the women went out after her with tambours and with dances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Revelation 15:3; Revelation 7:10–12"> And Miriam answered them, Sing to Jehovah, for he is highly exalted: The horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; 1 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 25:18"> And Moses brought Israel from the Red Sea, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur; and they went three days in the wilderness, and found no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:20; Numbers 33:8"> And they came to Marah, and could not drink the waters of Marah, for they were bitter; therefore the name of it was called Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 20:2–5"> And the people murmured against Moses, saying, What shall we drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Psalms 66:10; Judges 3:1"> And he cried to Jehovah; and Jehovah shewed him wood, and he cast it into the waters, and the waters became sweet. There he made for them a statute and an ordinance; and there he tested them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 103:3; Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 23:25"> And he said, If thou wilt diligently hearken to the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and do what is right in his eyes, and incline thine ears to his commandments, and keep all his statutes, I will put none of the complaints upon thee that I have put upon the Egyptians; for I am Jehovah who healeth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Revelation 7:17; Numbers 33:9"> And they came to Elim; and twelve springs of water were there, and seventy palm trees; and they encamped there by the waters.</span>
