<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Hosea 13:4; Leviticus 26:13"> I am Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, who have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:7; Matthew 4:10; Psalms 81:9"> Thou shalt have no other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="20.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:8; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> Thou shalt not make thyself any graven image, or any form of what is in the heavens above, or what is in the earth beneath, or what is in the waters under the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:9; Numbers 14:18; Deuteronomy 4:24"> thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them; for I, Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="20.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, am a jealous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="20.5 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the sons to the third and to the fourth &#91;generation&#93; of them that hate me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; John 14:21; 1 John 5:3"> and shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me and keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 19:12; James 5:12"> Thou shalt not idly utter the name of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="20.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God; for Jehovah will not hold him guiltless that idly uttereth his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 31:13–16"> Remember the sabbath day to hallow it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Exodus 34:21; Exodus 23:12"> Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:14–15; Exodus 34:21; Numbers 15:32–36"> but the seventh day is the sabbath of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="20.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God: thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy bondman, nor thy handmaid, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–2; Genesis 2:2–3; Exodus 31:17"> For in six days Jehovah made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested on the seventh day; therefore Jehovah blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20; Deuteronomy 5:16"> Honour thy father and thy mother, that thy days may be prolonged in the land that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="20.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Deuteronomy 5:17; Genesis 9:5–6"> Thou shalt not kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:18; Romans 7:2–3"> Thou shalt not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28; Leviticus 19:11"> Thou shalt not steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Matthew 19:18; Psalms 15:3"> Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15; Hebrews 13:5"> Thou shalt not desire thy neighbour's house, thou shalt not desire thy neighbour's wife, nor his bondman, nor his handmaid, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor anything that is thy neighbour's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:16–18; Jeremiah 23:23; Psalms 139:7–8"> And all the people saw the thunderings, and the flames, and the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; and when the people saw &#91;it&#93;, they trembled, and stood afar off,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Acts 7:38; Galatians 3:19"> and said to Moses, Speak thou with us, and we will hear; but let not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="20.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God speak with us, lest we die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:3; Proverbs 3:7; 1 Samuel 12:20"> And Moses said to the people, Fear not; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="20.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God is come to prove you, and that his fear may be before you, that ye sin not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 18:9"> And the people stood afar off, and Moses drew near to the obscurity where<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="20.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; Deuteronomy 4:36; Hebrews 12:25–26"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Thus shalt thou say to the children of Israel: Ye have seen that I have spoken with you from the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:39; Zephaniah 1:5; 2 Kings 17:33"> Ye shall not make beside me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="20.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>gods of silver, and ye shall not make to you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="20.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>gods of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 2 Chronicles 6:6; Psalms 132:13–14"> An altar of earth shalt thou make unto me, and shalt sacrifice on it thy burnt-offerings, and thy peace-offerings, thy sheep and thine oxen: in all places where I shall make my name to be remembered, I will come unto thee, and bless thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Deuteronomy 27:5–6"> And if thou make me an altar of stone, thou shalt not build it of hewn stone; for if thou lift up thy sharp tool upon it, thou hast profaned it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:16; Ecclesiastes 5:1; Leviticus 10:3"> Neither shalt thou go up by steps unto mine altar, that thy nakedness be not discovered thereon.</span>
