<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 19:8; Exodus 17:6; Psalms 78:70–72"> And Moses tended the flock of Jethro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian. And he led the flock behind the wilderness, and came to the mountain of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God — to Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:30–35; Isaiah 43:2; Mark 12:26"> And the Angel of Jehovah appeared to him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a thorn-bush: and he looked, and behold, the thorn-bush burned with fire, and the thorn-bush was not being consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:31; Psalms 107:8; Job 37:14"> And Moses said, Let me now turn aside and see this great sight, why the thorn-bush is not burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:16; 1 Samuel 3:6; Acts 9:4"> And Jehovah saw that he turned aside to see, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God called to him out of the midst of the thorn-bush and said, Moses, Moses! And he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:15; Acts 7:33; Exodus 19:12"> And he said, Draw not nigh hither: loose thy sandals from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:37; Matthew 22:32; Exodus 4:5"> And he said, I am the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of thy father, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of Abraham, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God of Isaac, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of Jacob. And Moses hid his face; for he was afraid to look at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 106:44; Exodus 2:23–25"> And Jehovah said, I have seen assuredly the affliction of my people who are in Egypt, and their cry have I heard on account of their taskmasters; for I know their sorrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 15:18–21; Exodus 3:17"> And I am come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to bring them up out of that land unto a good and spacious land, unto a land flowing with milk and honey, unto the place of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 1:13–14"> And now behold, the cry of the children of Israel is come unto me; and I have also seen the oppression with which the Egyptians oppress them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:4; 1 Samuel 12:6; Psalms 105:26"> And now come, and I will send thee unto Pharaoh, that thou mayest bring forth my people the children of Israel out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:18; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Exodus 6:12"> And Moses said to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, Who am I, that I should go unto Pharaoh, and that I should bring forth the children of Israel out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 41:10"> And he said, For I will be with thee; and this shall be the sign to thee that I have sent thee: when thou hast brought forth the people out of Egypt, ye shall serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God upon this mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Isaiah 9:6; Jeremiah 23:6"> And Moses said to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, Behold, &#91;when&#93; I come unto the children of Israel, and shall say unto them, The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="3.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God of your fathers hath sent me unto you; and they shall say, What is his name? what shall I say unto them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:58; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:8"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="3.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God said to Moses, I AM THAT I AM. And he said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel: I AM hath sent me unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 13:8; Malachi 3:6"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="3.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God said moreover to Moses, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel: Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="3.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God of your fathers, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="3.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God of Abraham, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="3.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God of Isaac, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="3.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God of Jacob, hath sent me unto you. This is my name for ever, and this is my memorial unto all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 4:29"> Go and gather the elders of Israel together, and say unto them, Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="3.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">r</a></sup>God of your fathers, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="3.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">s</a></sup>God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, hath appeared to me, saying, I have indeed visited you, and &#91;seen&#93; that which is done unto you in Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:13–21; Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4"> and I have said, I will bring you up out of the affliction of Egypt, unto the land of the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, unto a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 9:1; Exodus 7:16; Exodus 10:24–26"> And they shall hearken to thy voice. And thou shalt come, thou and the elders of Israel, unto the king of Egypt, and ye shall say unto him, Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="3.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">t</a></sup>God of the Hebrews, hath met with us; and now, let us go, we pray thee, three days' journey into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="3.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">u</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 7:1–14"> But I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go, no, not by a powerful hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 9:15"> And I will stretch out my hand and smite Egypt with all my wonders which I will do in the midst thereof; and after that he will let you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:36; Psalms 105:37; Exodus 11:3"> And I will give this people favour in the eyes of the Egyptians, and it shall come to pass, when ye go out, that ye shall not go out empty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:10; Exodus 11:2; Exodus 12:35–36"> but every woman shall ask of her neighbour, and of her that is the inmate of her house, utensils of silver, and utensils of gold, and clothing; and ye shall put &#91;them&#93; on your sons and on your daughters, and shall spoil the Egyptians.</span>
