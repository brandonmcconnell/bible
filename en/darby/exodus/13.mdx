<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Numbers 3:13; Deuteronomy 15:19"> Hallow unto me every firstborn, whatever breaketh open the womb among the children of Israel, of man and of cattle: it is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 20:2"> And Moses said to the people, Remember this day, in which ye came out from Egypt, out of the house of bondage; for with a powerful hand hath Jehovah brought you out from this; and nothing leavened shall be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 12:2"> Ye come out to-day, in the month Abib.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Joshua 24:11; Exodus 12:25–26"> And it shall be when Jehovah hath brought thee into the land of the Canaanite and the Hittite and the Amorite and the Hivite and the Jebusite, which he swore to thy fathers to give thee, a land flowing with milk and honey, that thou shalt keep this service in this month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; Exodus 34:18; Leviticus 23:8"> Seven days shalt thou eat unleavened bread; and in the seventh day is a feast to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:19; Matthew 16:6"> Unleavened bread shall be eaten the seven days; and leavened bread shall not be seen with thee, neither shall there be leaven seen with thee in all thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26–27; Psalms 44:1"> And thou shalt inform thy son in that day, saying, It is because of what Jehovah did to me when I came out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:8; Deuteronomy 11:18–19; Numbers 15:39"> And it shall be for a sign to thee on thy hand, and for a memorial between thine eyes, that the law of Jehovah may be in thy mouth; for with a powerful hand hath Jehovah brought thee out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Exodus 12:24–25; Exodus 23:15"> And thou shalt keep this ordinance at its set time from year to year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 13:5"> And it shall be when Jehovah hath brought thee into the land of the Canaanites, as he hath sworn to thee and to thy fathers, and hath given it thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 18:15; Leviticus 27:26; Exodus 34:19"> that thou shalt offer unto Jehovah all that breaketh open the womb, and every firstling that cometh of cattle which is thine: the males &#91;shall be&#93; Jehovah's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Numbers 18:15–17; Revelation 14:4"> And every firstling of an ass shalt thou ransom with a lamb; and if thou do not ransom it, thou shalt break its neck; and every firstborn of a man among thy sons shalt thou ransom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 13:3; Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4"> And it shall be when thy son asketh thee in time to come, saying, What is this? that thou shalt say to him, With a powerful hand Jehovah brought us out from Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:29"> And it came to pass, when Pharaoh stubbornly refused to let us go, that Jehovah slew all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, both the firstborn of men and the firstborn of cattle: therefore I sacrifice to Jehovah all that breaketh open the womb — being males; and every firstborn of my children I ransom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 13:9; Deuteronomy 11:18; Deuteronomy 6:7–9"> And it shall be for a sign on thy hand, and for frontlets between thine eyes, for with a powerful hand Jehovah brought us forth out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Nehemiah 9:17; Numbers 14:1–4"> And it came to pass, when Pharaoh let the people go, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God did not lead them the way of the land of the Philistines, although that was near; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God said, That the people may not repent when they see conflict, and return to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:2; Deuteronomy 32:10; Numbers 33:6–8"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God led the people about, the way of the wilderness of the Red Sea; and the children of Israel went arrayed out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32; Acts 7:16"> And Moses took the bones of Joseph with him; for he had made the children of Israel swear an oath, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God will be sure to visit you; then ye shall carry my bones with you hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 33:5–6; Exodus 12:37"> And they took their journey from Succoth, and encamped in Etham, at the end of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1–2; Nehemiah 9:12; Deuteronomy 1:33"> And Jehovah went before their face by day in a pillar of a cloud, to lead them &#91;in&#93; the way; and by night in a pillar of fire, to give them light; so that they could go day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 121:5–8; Revelation 10:1"> The pillar of the cloud did not remove &#91;from&#93; before the people by day, nor the pillar of fire by night.</span>
