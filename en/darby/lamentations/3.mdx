<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 88:7; Job 19:21; Psalms 88:15–16"> I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 30:26; Isaiah 59:9; Amos 5:18–20"> Me hath he led, and brought into darkness, and not into light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Job 31:21; Isaiah 1:25"> Surely against me hath he turned again and again his hand all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> My flesh and my skin hath he wasted away, he hath broken my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:19; Jeremiah 23:15; Job 19:8"> He hath built against me, and encompassed &#91;me&#93; with gall and toil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 88:5–6; Psalms 143:3; Psalms 143:7"> He hath made me to dwell in dark places as those that have been long dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Jeremiah 40:4"> He hath hedged me about that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 30:20; Psalms 22:2; Job 19:7"> Even when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:17; Lamentations 3:11; Isaiah 30:28"> He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 10:9; Amos 5:18–20; Hosea 13:7–8"> He is unto me &#91;as&#93; a bear lying in wait, a lion in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Job 16:12–13; Lamentations 1:13"> He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces; he hath made me desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 7:20; Job 6:4"> He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 41:28; Deuteronomy 32:23"> He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; Lamentations 3:63; Psalms 22:6–7"> I am become a derision to all my people; their song all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:15; Jeremiah 23:15; Ruth 1:20"> He hath sated me with bitterness, he hath made me drunk with wormwood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 3:7"> He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Zechariah 8:10; Jeremiah 8:15"> And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I have forgotten prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 17:15; 1 Samuel 27:1; Psalms 116:11"> And I said, My strength is perished, and my hope in Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 3:15; Lamentations 3:5; Psalms 89:50"> Remember thou mine affliction and my wandering, the wormwood and the gall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 42:11; Psalms 42:5–6; Job 21:6"> My soul hath &#91;them&#93; constantly in remembrance, and is humbled in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Psalms 130:7; Psalms 77:7–11"> — This I recall to heart, therefore have I hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Luke 1:50; Nehemiah 9:31"> It is of Jehovah's loving-kindness we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; Psalms 30:5; Isaiah 33:2"> they are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 16:5; Lamentations 3:21"> Jehovah is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 7:7–8; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 130:5–6"> Jehovah is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul &#91;that&#93; seeketh him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:34; Psalms 130:5"> It is good that one should both wait, and that in silence, for the salvation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 11:29–30; Psalms 119:71"> It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:17; Lamentations 2:10; Psalms 102:7"> He sitteth solitary and keepeth silence, because he hath laid it upon him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 40:4; Luke 15:18–19; Romans 3:19"> he putteth his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be hope;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 16:10; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 26:67"> he giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him; he is filled full with reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Psalms 103:8–10; Psalms 77:7"> For the Lord will not cast off for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:38; Psalms 30:5; Psalms 103:11"> but if he have caused grief, he will have compassion according to the multitude of his loving-kindnesses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:32; Hebrews 12:9–10"> for he doth not willingly afflict or grieve the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11–12; Isaiah 51:22–23; Psalms 102:20"> To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> to turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most High,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 59:15; Habakkuk 1:13; Jeremiah 22:3"> to wrong a man in his cause, — will not the Lord see it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Psalms 33:9–11; Isaiah 46:10; Proverbs 16:9"> Who is he that saith, and there cometh to pass, what the Lord hath not commanded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Amos 3:6; Jeremiah 32:42"> Out of the mouth of the Most High doth not there proceed evil and good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 19:3; Micah 7:9; Genesis 4:5–7"> Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 139:23–24; Joel 2:12–13"> Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 25:1; Psalms 28:2; Psalms 86:4"> Let us lift up our heart with &#91;our&#93; hands unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.41 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 15:18–19; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Kings 24:4"> We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Lamentations 2:21; Lamentations 3:66"> Thou hast covered thyself with anger, and pursued us; thou hast slain, thou hast not spared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 3:8; Zechariah 7:13; Psalms 97:2"> Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that prayer should not pass through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:13; Lamentations 2:15; Lamentations 3:14"> Thou hast made us the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–8; Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 44:13–14"> All our enemies have opened their mouth against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Isaiah 51:19; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> Fear and the pit are come upon us, devastation and ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 9:18; Jeremiah 9:1"> Mine eye runneth down with streams of water for the ruin of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Psalms 77:2; Lamentations 1:16"> Mine eye poureth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Lamentations 5:1; Daniel 9:16–19"> till Jehovah look down and behold from the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:9; Luke 19:41–44; Lamentations 1:18"> Mine eye affecteth my soul, because of all the daughters of my city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Psalms 35:7; Jeremiah 37:15–16"> They that are mine enemies without cause have chased me sore like a bird.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Daniel 6:17; Jeremiah 38:9; Jeremiah 38:6"> They have cut off my life in a pit, and cast a stone upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jonah 2:3–5; Ezekiel 37:11; Lamentations 3:18"> Waters streamed over my head; I said, I am cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Acts 16:24–28; Psalms 142:3–7; Jonah 2:2–4"> I called upon thy name, Jehovah, out of the lowest pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 38:5; Job 34:28; Psalms 6:8–9"> Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my sighing, at my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 58:9"> Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee; thou saidst, Fear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Psalms 34:22–1; Jeremiah 51:36; Psalms 103:4"> Lord, thou hast pleaded the cause of my soul, thou hast redeemed my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 9:4; Genesis 31:42"> Jehovah, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:19–20; Psalms 10:14; Lamentations 3:59"> Thou hast seen all their vengeance, all their imaginations against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:50; Zephaniah 2:8"> Thou hast heard their reproach, O Jehovah, all their imaginations against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 59:7; Psalms 140:3"> the lips of those that rise up against me and their meditation against me all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Lamentations 3:14; Job 30:9"> Behold thou their sitting down and their rising up: I am their song.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Psalms 28:4; Jeremiah 11:20; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Render unto them a recompence, O Jehovah, according to the work of their hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Deuteronomy 2:30"> give them obduracy of heart, thy curse unto them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Lamentations 3:43; Psalms 115:16"> pursue them in anger, and destroy them from under the heavens of Jehovah.</span>
