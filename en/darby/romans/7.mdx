<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:6; Romans 6:3"> Are ye ignorant, brethren, (for I speak to those knowing law,) that law rules over a man as long as he lives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 2:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:39; 1 Corinthians 7:4"> For the married woman is bound by law to her husband so long as he is alive; but if the husband should die, she is clear from the law of the husband:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Matthew 5:32; Mark 10:6–12"> so then, the husband being alive, she shall be called an adulteress if she be to another man; but if the husband should die, she is free from the law, so as not to be an adulteress, though she be to another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 54:5"> So that, my brethren, ye also have been made dead to the law by the body of the Christ, to be to another, who has been raised up from among &#91;the&#93; dead, in order that we might bear fruit to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 5:24"> For when we were in the flesh the passions of sins, which &#91;were&#93; by the law, wrought in our members to bring forth fruit to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:23–25; 2 Corinthians 3:6; Romans 7:4"> but now we are clear from the law, having died in that in which we were held, so that we should serve in newness of spirit, and not in oldness of letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56"> What shall we say then? &#91;is&#93; the law sin? Far be the thought. But I had not known sin, unless by law: for I had not had conscience also of lust unless the law had said, Thou shalt not lust;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:11; 1 Corinthians 15:56; Romans 4:15"> but sin, getting a point of attack by the commandment, wrought in me every lust; for without law sin &#91;was&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 8:7; James 2:10–11"> But I was alive without law once; but the commandment having come, sin revived, but I died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:7; Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5"> And the commandment, which &#91;was&#93; for life, was found, &#91;as&#93; to me, itself &#91;to be&#93; unto death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 3:13; Romans 7:8; Hebrews 3:13"> for sin, getting a point of attack by the commandment, deceived me, and by it slew &#91;me&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:8; Psalms 119:137; Psalms 19:7–12"> So that the law indeed &#91;is&#93; holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:13–15; Galatians 3:21; Romans 8:3"> Did then that which is good become death to me? Far be the thought. But sin, that it might appear sin, working death to me by that which is good; in order that sin by the commandment might become exceeding sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:18"> For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am fleshly, sold under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:19–20; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> For that which I do, I do not own: for not what I will, this I do; but what I hate, this I practise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 7:12; Romans 7:14; 1 Timothy 1:8"> But if what I do not will, this I practise, I consent to the law that &#91;it is&#93; right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 7:20; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 7:18"> Now then &#91;it is&#93; no longer I &#91;that&#93; do it, but the sin that dwells in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:6; Psalms 51:5; Galatians 5:17"> For I know that in me, that is, in my flesh, good does not dwell: for to will is there with me, but to do right &#91;I find&#93; not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:15"> For I do not practise the good that I will; but the evil I do not will, that I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 7:17"> But if what I do not will, this I practise, &#91;it is&#93; no longer I &#91;that&#93; do it, but the sin that dwells in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:23; Psalms 119:133"> I find then the law upon me who will to practise what is right, that with me evil is there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 1:2; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 119:35"> For I delight in the law of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.22 Elohim">b</a></sup>God according to the inward man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; James 4:1; Romans 8:2"> but I see another law in my members, warring in opposition to the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which exists in my members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; 2 Timothy 4:18; Titus 2:14"> O wretched man that I &#91;am&#93;! who shall deliver me out of this body of death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:17–24; Colossians 3:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I thank<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then I myself with the mind serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.25 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's law; but with the flesh sin's law.</span>
</div>
