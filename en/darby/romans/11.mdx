<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> I say then, Has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God cast away his people? Far be the thought. For I also am an Israelite, of &#91;the&#93; seed of Abraham, of &#91;the&#93; tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God has not cast away his people whom he foreknew. Know ye not what the scripture says in &#91;the history of&#93; Elias, how he pleads with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God against Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> Lord, they have killed thy prophets, they have dug down thine altars; and I have been left alone, and they seek my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> But what says the divine answer to him? I have left to myself seven thousand men, who have not bowed knee to Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> Thus, then, in the present time also there has been a remnant according to election of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> But if by grace, no longer of works: since &#91;otherwise&#93; grace is no more grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What &#91;is it&#93; then? What Israel seeks for, that he has not obtained; but the election has obtained, and the rest have been blinded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> according as it is written,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.8 Elohim">d</a></sup>God has given to them a spirit of slumber, eyes not to see, and ears not to hear, unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> And David says, Let their table be for a snare, and for a gin, and for a fall-trap, and for a recompense to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> let their eyes be darkened not to see, and bow down their back alway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I say then, Have they stumbled in order that they might fall? Far be the thought: but by their fall &#91;there is&#93; salvation to the nations to provoke them to jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> But if their fall &#91;be the&#93; world's wealth, and their loss &#91;the&#93; wealth of &#91;the&#93; nations, how much rather their fulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> For I speak to you, the nations, inasmuch as I am apostle of nations, I glorify my ministry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> if by any means I shall provoke to jealousy &#91;them which are&#93; my flesh, and shall save some from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> For if their casting away &#91;be the&#93; world's reconciliation, what &#91;their&#93; reception but life from among &#91;the&#93; dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> Now if the first-fruit &#91;be&#93; holy, the lump also; and if the root &#91;be&#93; holy, the branches also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> Now if some of the branches have been broken out, and thou, being a wild olive tree, hast been grafted in amongst them, and hast become a fellow-partaker of the root and of the fatness of the olive tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> boast not against the branches; but if thou boast, &#91;it is&#93; not thou bearest the root, but the root thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> Thou wilt say then, The branches have been broken out in order that I might be grafted in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> Right: they have been broken out through unbelief, and thou standest through faith. Be not high-minded, but fear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.21 Elohim">e</a></sup>God indeed has not spared the natural branches; lest it might be he spare not thee either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> Behold then &#91;the&#93; goodness and severity of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.22 Elohim">f</a></sup>God: upon them who have fallen, severity; upon thee goodness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="11.22 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, if thou shalt abide in goodness, since &#91;otherwise&#93; thou also wilt be cut away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And they too, if they abide not in unbelief, shall be grafted in; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="11.23 Elohim">h</a></sup>God is able again to graft them in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> For if thou hast been cut out of the olive tree wild by nature, and, contrary to nature, hast been grafted into the good olive tree, how much rather shall they, who are according to nature be grafted into their own olive tree?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> For I do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, of this mystery, that ye may not be wise in your own conceits, that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the nations be come in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> and so all Israel shall be saved. According as it is written, The deliverer shall come out of Zion; he shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> And this is the covenant from me to them, when I shall have taken away their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> As regards the glad tidings, &#91;they are&#93; enemies on your account; but as regards election, beloved on account of the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> For the gifts and the calling of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="11.29 Elohim">i</a></sup>God &#91;are&#93; not subject to repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> For as indeed ye &#91;also&#93; once have not believed in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="11.30 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, but now have been objects of mercy through the unbelief of these;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so these also have now not believed in your mercy, in order that they also may be objects of mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="11.32 Elohim">k</a></sup>God hath shut up together all in unbelief, in order that he might shew mercy to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalms 92:5"> O depth of riches both of &#91;the&#93; wisdom and knowledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="11.33 Elohim">l</a></sup>God! how unsearchable his judgments, and untraceable his ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> For who has known &#91;the&#93; mind of &#91;the&#93; Lord, or who has been his counsellor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> or who has first given to him, and it shall be rendered to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> For of him, and through him, and for him &#91;are&#93; all things: to him be glory for ever. Amen.</span>
