<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 15:16; 1 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, bondman of Jesus Christ, &#91;a&#93; called apostle, separated to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's glad tidings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:2; Luke 1:70; Romans 3:21"> (which he had before promised by his prophets in holy writings,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Galatians 4:4; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> concerning his Son (come of David's seed according to flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 5:5–6; Ephesians 1:19–23"> marked out Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God in power, according to &#91;the&#93; Spirit of holiness, by resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead) Jesus Christ our Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 16:26; Acts 6:7"> by whom we have received grace and apostleship in behalf of his name, for obedience of faith among all the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 17:14; 2 Peter 1:10"> among whom are ye also &#91;the&#93; called of Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Jude 1:1–2; Ephesians 1:2"> to all that are in Rome, beloved of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, called saints: Grace to you and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.7 Elohim">d</a></sup>God our Father and &#91;our&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 16:19; 1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> First, I thank my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">e</a></sup>God through Jesus Christ for you all, that your faith is proclaimed in the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 2 Timothy 1:3; Ephesians 1:16–19"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">f</a></sup>God is my witness, whom I serve in my spirit in the glad tidings of his Son, how unceasingly I make mention of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:30–32; Acts 18:21; Philemon 1:22"> always beseeching at my prayers, if any way now at least I may be prospered by the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.10 Elohim">g</a></sup>God to come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Romans 16:25; Romans 15:29"> For I greatly desire to see you, that I may impart to you some spiritual gift to establish you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:7–10; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> that is, to have mutual comfort among you, each by the faith &#91;which is&#93; in the other, both yours and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 19:21; Philippians 4:17; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But I do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, that I often proposed to come to you, (and have been hindered until the present time,) that I might have some fruit among you too, even as among the other nations also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–23; Acts 9:15; Colossians 3:11"> I am a debtor both to Greeks and barbarians, both to wise and unintelligent:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> so, as far as depends on me, am I ready to announce the glad tidings to you also who &#91;are&#93; in Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 8:38; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 4:16"> For I am not ashamed of the glad tidings; for it is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.16 Elohim">h</a></sup>God's power to salvation, to every one that believes, both to Jew first and to Greek:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Galatians 3:11; Hebrews 10:38"> for righteousness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.17 Elohim">i</a></sup>God is revealed therein, on the principle of faith, to faith: according as it is written, But the just shall live by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; Romans 1:32"> For there is revealed wrath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.18 Elohim">j</a></sup>God from heaven upon all impiety, and unrighteousness of men holding the truth in unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:16–17; Psalms 19:1–6; Romans 1:20"> Because what is known of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.19 Elohim">k</a></sup>God is manifest among them, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1.19 Elohim">l</a></sup>God has manifested &#91;it&#93; to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:19; Isaiah 40:26; Psalms 19:1–6"> — for from &#91;the&#93; world's creation the invisible things of him are perceived, being apprehended by the mind through the things that are made, both his eternal power and divinity, — so as to render them inexcusable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17–18; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:5"> Because, knowing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">m</a></sup>God, they glorified &#91;him&#93; not as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">n</a></sup>God, neither were thankful; but fell into folly in their thoughts, and their heart without understanding was darkened:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:19–21; Romans 11:25; Proverbs 26:12"> professing themselves to be wise, they became fools,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11; Acts 17:29"> and changed the glory of the incorruptible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="1.23 Elohim">o</a></sup>God into &#91;the&#93; likeness of an image of corruptible man and of birds and quadrupeds and reptiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Acts 7:42; Acts 14:16"> Wherefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="1.24 Elohim">p</a></sup>God gave them up &#91;also&#93; in the lusts of their hearts to uncleanness, to dishonour their bodies between themselves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 13:25; 1 John 2:15–16"> who changed the truth of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="1.25 Elohim">q</a></sup>God into falsehood, and honoured and served the creature more than him who had created &#91;it&#93;, who is blessed for ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Romans 1:24"> For this reason<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="1.26 Elohim">r</a></sup>God gave them up to vile lusts; for both their females changed the natural use into that contrary to nature;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Leviticus 18:22; Romans 1:23–24"> and in like manner the males also, leaving the natural use of the female, were inflamed in their lust towards one another; males with males working shame, and receiving in themselves the recompense of their error which was fit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Romans 1:21; Titus 1:16"> And according as they did not think good to have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="1.28 Elohim">s</a></sup>God in &#91;their&#93; knowledge,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="1.28 Elohim">t</a></sup>God gave them up to a reprobate mind to practise unseemly things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 41:7"> being filled with all unrighteousness, wickedness, covetousness, malice; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, evil dispositions; whisperers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2; Jude 1:16; 2 Peter 2:18"> back-biters, hateful to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="1.30 Elohim">u</a></sup>God, insolent, proud, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:3; Proverbs 18:2; Romans 3:11"> void of understanding, faithless, without natural affection, unmerciful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 2:1–5; Romans 1:21; Romans 6:21"> who knowing the righteous judgment of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="1.32 Elohim">v</a></sup>God, that they who do such things are worthy of death, not only practise them, but have fellow delight in those who do &#91;them&#93;.</span>
</div>
