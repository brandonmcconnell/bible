<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Romans 6:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the compassions of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, to present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.1 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, &#91;which is&#93; your intelligent service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:14; Ephesians 4:22–24; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> And be not conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of &#91;your&#93; mind, that ye may prove what &#91;is&#93; the good and acceptable and perfect will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For I say, through the grace which has been given to me, to every one that is among you, not to have high thoughts above what he should think; but to think so as to be wise, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.3 Elohim">d</a></sup>God has dealt to each a measure of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> For, as in one body we have many members, but all the members have not the same office;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> thus we, &#91;being&#93; many, are one body in Christ, and each one members one of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> But having different gifts, according to the grace which has been given to us, whether &#91;it be&#93; prophecy, &#91;let us prophesy&#93; according to the proportion of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> or service, &#91;let us occupy ourselves&#93; in service; or he that teaches, in teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> or he that exhorts, in exhortation; he that gives, in simplicity; he that leads, with diligence; he that shews mercy, with cheerfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let love be unfeigned; abhorring evil; cleaving to good:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; John 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> as to brotherly love, kindly affectioned towards one another: as to honour, each taking the lead in paying it to the other:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> as to diligent zealousness, not slothful; in spirit fervent; serving the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 62:8"> As regards hope, rejoicing: as regards tribulation, enduring: as regards prayer, persevering:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 John 3:17"> distributing to the necessities of the saints; given to hospitality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless them that persecute you; bless, and curse not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> Rejoice with those that rejoice, weep with those that weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; James 3:13–17"> Have the same respect one for another, not minding high things, but going along with the lowly: be not wise in your own eyes:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> recompensing to no one evil for evil: providing things honest before all men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Matthew 5:9; Romans 14:19"> if possible, as far as depends on you, living in peace with all men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35; Hebrews 10:30; Proverbs 24:29"> not avenging yourselves, beloved, but give place to wrath; for it is written, Vengeance &#91;belongs&#93; to me, I will recompense, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; Matthew 5:44; Luke 6:27"> If therefore thine enemy should hunger, feed him; if he should thirst, give him drink; for, so doing, thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Be not overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.</span>
