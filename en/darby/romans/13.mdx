<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Let every soul be subject to the authorities that are above &#91;him&#93;. For there is no authority except from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God; and those that exist are set up by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.1 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> So that he that sets himself in opposition to the authority resists the ordinance of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.2 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; and they who &#91;thus&#93; resist shall bring sentence of guilt on themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For rulers are not a terror to a good work, but to an evil &#91;one&#93;. Dost thou desire then not to be afraid of the authority? practise &#91;what is&#93; good, and thou shalt have praise from it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> for it is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's minister to thee for good. But if thou practisest evil, fear; for it bears not the sword in vain; for it is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.4 Elohim">e</a></sup>God's minister, an avenger for wrath to him that does evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> Wherefore it is necessary to be subject, not only on account of wrath, but also on account of conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> For on this account ye pay tribute also; for they are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.6 Elohim">f</a></sup>God's officers, attending continually on this very thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> Render to all their dues: to whom tribute &#91;is due&#93;, tribute; to whom custom, custom; to whom fear, fear; to whom honour, honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Owe no one anything, unless to love one another: for he that loves another has fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> For, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not lust; and if there be any other commandment, it is summed up in this word, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love works no ill to its neighbour; love therefore &#91;is the&#93; whole law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> This also, knowing the time, that it is already time that we should be aroused out of sleep; for now &#91;is&#93; our salvation nearer than when we believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night is far spent, and the day is near; let us cast away therefore the works of darkness, and let us put on the armour of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> As in the day, let us walk becomingly; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and lasciviousness, not in strife and emulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and do not take forethought for the flesh to &#91;fulfil its&#93; lusts.</span>
