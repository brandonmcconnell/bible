<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:15"> What then shall we say? Should we continue in sin that grace may abound?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:9; Colossians 3:3; Romans 7:4"> Far be the thought. We who have died to sin, how shall we still live in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:27; Galatians 2:20–21; Romans 6:8"> Are you ignorant that we, as many as have been baptised unto Christ Jesus, have been baptised unto his death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:22–24"> We have been buried therefore with him by baptism unto death, in order that, even as Christ has been raised up from among &#91;the&#93; dead by the glory of the Father, so we also should walk in newness of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:10–11; 2 Corinthians 4:10; Colossians 2:12"> For if we are become identified with &#91;him&#93; in the likeness of his death, so also we shall be of &#91;his&#93; resurrection;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:24"> knowing this, that our old man has been crucified with &#91;him&#93;, that the body of sin might be annulled, that we should no longer serve sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:1; Colossians 3:1–3; Romans 7:4"> For he that has died is justified from sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:11–12; Colossians 3:3–4; 2 Corinthians 4:10–14"> Now if we have died with Christ, we believe that we shall also live with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Revelation 1:18; Acts 2:24–28"> knowing that Christ having been raised up from among &#91;the&#93; dead dies no more: death has dominion over him no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 4:6; 1 Peter 3:18"> For in that he has died, he has died to sin once for all; but in that he lives, he lives to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.10 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Romans 6:2"> So also ye, reckon yourselves dead to sin and alive to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 6:16; Romans 8:13"> Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body to obey its lusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Romans 6:16; Colossians 3:5"> Neither yield your members instruments of unrighteousness to sin, but yield yourselves to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God as alive from among &#91;the&#93; dead, and your members instruments of righteousness to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.13 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Romans 8:2; Romans 7:4–11"> For sin shall not have dominion over you, for ye are not under law but under grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; Ephesians 2:8–10; Romans 6:1–2"> What then? should we sin because we are not under law but under grace? Far be the thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 8:34; Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15"> Know ye not that to whom ye yield yourselves bondmen for obedience, ye are bondmen to him whom ye obey, whether of sin unto death, or of obedience unto righteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 1:13; Romans 1:8"> But thanks &#91;be&#93; to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.17 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, that ye were bondmen of sin, but have obeyed from the heart the form of teaching into which ye were instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:22; Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:1"> Now, having got your freedom from sin, ye have become bondmen to righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 6:13; 1 Peter 4:2–4; Colossians 3:5–7"> I speak humanly on account of the weakness of your flesh. For even as ye have yielded your members in bondage to uncleanness and to lawlessness unto lawlessness, so now yield your members in bondage to righteousness unto holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:16–17; John 8:34"> For when ye were bondmen of sin ye were free from righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 7:5; Romans 6:23"> What fruit therefore had ye then in the things of which ye are now ashamed? for the end of them &#91;is&#93; death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Romans 6:18; Romans 8:2"> But now, having got your freedom from sin, and having become bondmen to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.22 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, ye have your fruit unto holiness, and the end eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:36; Romans 5:12; James 1:15"> For the wages of sin &#91;is&#93; death; but the act of favour of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.23 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
