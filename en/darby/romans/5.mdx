<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Romans 15:13; John 16:33"> Therefore having been justified on the principle of faith, we have peace towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God through our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 12:12"> by whom we have also access by faith into this favour in which we stand, and we boast in hope of the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:2–3; James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> And not only &#91;that&#93;, but we also boast in tribulations, knowing that tribulation works endurance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> and endurance, experience; and experience, hope;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Ephesians 3:16–19; Philippians 1:20"> and hope does not make ashamed, because the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God is shed abroad in our hearts by &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit which has been given to us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–5; Romans 5:8; Colossians 2:13"> for we being still without strength, in &#91;the&#93; due time Christ has died for &#91;the&#93; ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:13; 1 John 3:16; Romans 16:4"> For scarcely for &#91;the&#93; just &#91;man&#93; will one die, for perhaps for &#91;the&#93; good &#91;man&#93; some one might also dare to die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:13; John 3:16; Romans 5:6"> but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.8 Elohim">d</a></sup>God commends his love to us, in that, we being still sinners, Christ has died for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 8:1; Ephesians 2:13"> Much rather therefore, having been now justified in &#91;the power of&#93; his blood, we shall be saved by him from wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:32"> For if, being enemies, we have been reconciled to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.10 Elohim">e</a></sup>God through the death of his Son, much rather, having been reconciled, we shall be saved in &#91;the power of&#93; his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; Romans 5:10; Isaiah 61:10"> And not only &#91;that&#93;, but &#91;we are&#93; making our boast in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.11 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom now we have received the reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; Romans 5:19; Romans 6:23"> For this &#91;cause&#93;, even as by one man sin entered into the world, and by sin death; and thus death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56; 1 John 3:4"> (for until law sin was in &#91;the&#93; world; but sin is not put to account when there is no law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:45; Romans 5:17; Hebrews 9:27"> but death reigned from Adam until Moses, even upon those who had not sinned in the likeness of Adam's transgression, who is &#91;the&#93; figure of him to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:12"> But &#91;shall&#93; not the act of favour &#91;be&#93; as the offence? For if by the offence of one the many have died, much rather has the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.15 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, and the free gift in grace, which &#91;is&#93; by the one man Jesus Christ, abounded unto the many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:38–39; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 44:22"> And &#91;shall&#93; not as by one that has sinned &#91;be&#93; the gift? For the judgment &#91;was&#93; of one to condemnation, but the act of favour, of many offences unto justification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; 1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 5:12"> For if by the offence of the one death reigned by the one, much rather shall those who receive the abundance of grace, and of the free gift of righteousness, reign in life by the one Jesus Christ:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12; Romans 5:15"> so then as &#91;it was&#93; by one offence towards all men to condemnation, so by one righteousness towards all men for justification of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 5:18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Philippians 2:8"> For as indeed by the disobedience of the one man the many have been constituted sinners, so also by the obedience of the one the many will be constituted righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Titus 3:3–7; Romans 6:14"> But law came in, in order that the offence might abound; but where sin abounded grace has overabounded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 2:11; Romans 5:14; Romans 8:10"> in order that, even as sin has reigned in &#91;the power of&#93; death, so also grace might reign through righteousness to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
