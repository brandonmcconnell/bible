<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:3; John 8:7–9"> Therefore thou art inexcusable, O man, every one who judgest, for in that in which thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest doest the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 9:7–8; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 145:17"> But we know that the judgment of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God is according to truth upon those who do such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:5; Luke 12:14; Romans 1:32–1"> And thinkest thou this, O man, who judgest those that do such things, and practisest them &#91;thyself&#93;, that thou shalt escape the judgment of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Isaiah 30:18; Exodus 34:6"> or despisest thou the riches of his goodness, and forbearance, and long-suffering, not knowing that the goodness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God leads thee to repentance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 1:18; Romans 2:2–3"> but, according to thy hardness and impenitent heart, treasurest up to thyself wrath, in &#91;the&#93; day of wrath and revelation of &#91;the&#93; righteous judgment of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.5 Elohim">d</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; Jeremiah 32:19; Matthew 16:27"> who shall render to each according to his works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7–8; Luke 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:10"> to them who, in patient continuance of good works, seek for glory and honour and incorruptibility, life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:18–21; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; 1 Timothy 6:3–4"> But to those that are contentious, and are disobedient to the truth, but obey unrighteousness, &#91;there shall be&#93; wrath and indignation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; Ezekiel 18:4; Romans 1:16"> tribulation and distress, on every soul of man that works evil, both of Jew first, and of Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 26:12; Numbers 6:26"> but glory and honour and peace to every one that works good, both to Jew first and to Greek:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> for there is no acceptance of persons with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.11 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:21; Galatians 3:10; Galatians 2:16–19"> For as many as have sinned without law shall perish also without law; and as many as have sinned under law shall be judged by law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:22–25; 1 John 3:7; James 2:21–25"> (for not the hearers of the law &#91;are&#93; just before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, but the doers of the law shall be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 1:19–20; Romans 2:27"> For when &#91;those of the&#93; nations, which have no law, practise by nature the things of the law, these, having no law, are a law to themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Romans 1:18–19"> who shew the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience also bearing witness, and their thoughts accusing or else excusing themselves between themselves;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Acts 17:31"> in &#91;the&#93; day when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.16 Elohim">g</a></sup>God shall judge the secrets of men, according to my glad tidings, by Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 2:23; Micah 3:11; Isaiah 48:1–2"> But if thou art named a Jew, and restest in the law, and makest thy boast in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">h</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Deuteronomy 4:8; James 4:17"> and knowest the will, and discerningly approvest the things that are more excellent, being instructed out of the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 3:17–18; Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 49:6"> and hast confidence that thou thyself art a leader of the blind, a light of those who &#91;are&#93; in darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 1:13"> an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes, having the form of knowledge and of truth in the law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:27; Matthew 23:3–28; Luke 12:47"> thou then that teachest another, dost thou not teach thyself? thou that preachest not to steal, dost thou steal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 3:8; Matthew 12:39; Mark 11:17"> thou that sayest &#91;man should&#93; not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 4:16–17; Romans 2:17; Romans 3:2"> thou who boastest in law, dost thou by transgression of the law dishonour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2.23 Elohim">i</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:20–23; Isaiah 52:5; 2 Peter 2:2"> For the name of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="2.24 Elohim">j</a></sup>God is blasphemed on your account among the nations, according as it is written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Galatians 5:3–6; Acts 7:51"> For circumcision indeed profits if thou keep &#91;the&#93; law; but if thou be a law-transgressor, thy circumcision is become uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 7:18–19; Romans 8:4"> If therefore the uncircumcision keep the requirements of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be reckoned for circumcision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; Romans 2:29; 2 Corinthians 3:6"> and uncircumcision by nature, fulfilling the law, judge thee, who, with letter and circumcision, &#91;art&#93; a law-transgressor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Matthew 3:9; Romans 9:6–8"> For he is not a Jew who &#91;is&#93; one outwardly, neither that circumcision which is outward in flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Philippians 3:3; Deuteronomy 10:16"> but he &#91;is&#93; a Jew &#91;who is so&#93; inwardly; and circumcision, of the heart, in spirit, not in letter; whose praise &#91;is&#93; not of men, but of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="2.29 Elohim">k</a></sup>God.</span>
