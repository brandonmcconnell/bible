<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:2; Romans 4:16; 2 Corinthians 11:22"> What shall we say then that Abraham our father according to flesh has found?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:31; 2 Corinthians 11:30; Philippians 3:9"> For if Abraham has been justified on the principle of works, he has whereof to boast: but not before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–8"> for what does the scripture say? And Abraham believed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Romans 9:32; Romans 11:35"> Now to him that works the reward is not reckoned as of grace, but of debt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Romans 3:22; Titus 3:3–7"> but to him who does not work, but believes on him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is reckoned as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> Even as David also declares the blessedness of the man to whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">c</a></sup>God reckons righteousness without works:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 32:1–2; Psalms 130:3–4; Luke 7:47–50"> Blessed &#91;they&#93; whose lawlessnesses have been forgiven, and whose sins have been covered:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 32:2; 1 Peter 2:24; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20"> blessed &#91;the&#93; man to whom &#91;the&#93; Lord shall not at all reckon sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:26–28"> &#91;Does&#93; this blessedness then &#91;rest&#93; on the circumcision, or also on the uncircumcision? For we say that faith has been reckoned to Abraham as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; Genesis 17:10"> How then has it been reckoned? when he was in circumcision, or in uncircumcision? Not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:22; Luke 19:9; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> And he received &#91;the&#93; sign of circumcision &#91;as&#93; seal of the righteousness of faith which &#91;he had&#93; being in uncircumcision, that he might be &#91;the&#93; father of all them that believe being in uncircumcision, that righteousness might be reckoned to them also;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; John 8:39–40; Proverbs 2:20"> and father of circumcision, not only to those who are of &#91;the&#93; circumcision, but to those also who walk in the steps of the faith, during uncircumcision, of our father Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Genesis 22:17–18; Genesis 28:14"> For &#91;it was&#93; not by law that the promise was to Abraham, or to his seed, that he should be heir of &#91;the&#93; world, but by righteousness of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:18–24; Philippians 3:9; Romans 4:16"> For if they which &#91;are&#93; of law be heirs, faith is made vain, and the promise made of no effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:13; Galatians 3:10; Romans 3:19–20"> For law works wrath; but where no law is neither &#91;is there&#93; transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Ephesians 2:8; Galatians 3:22"> Therefore &#91;it is&#93; on the principle of faith, that &#91;it might be&#93; according to grace, in order to the promise being sure to all the seed, not to that only which &#91;is&#93; of the law, but to that also which &#91;is&#93; of Abraham's faith, who is father of us all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:28; Genesis 17:4–5; John 5:21"> (according as it is written, I have made thee father of many nations,) before the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God whom he believed, who quickens the dead, and calls the things which be not as being;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:24; Genesis 15:5–6; Acts 27:25"> who against hope believed in hope to his becoming father of many nations, according to that which was spoken, So shall thy seed be:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11–19; Romans 4:20–21; Genesis 17:17"> and not being weak in faith, he considered not his own body already become dead, being about a hundred years old, and the deadening of Sarah's womb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 1:45; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Daniel 10:19"> and hesitated not at the promise of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.20 Elohim">e</a></sup>God through unbelief; but found strength in faith, giving glory to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.20 Elohim">f</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> and being fully persuaded that what he has promised he is able also to do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 4:6; Romans 4:3"> wherefore also it was reckoned to him as righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Romans 15:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Now it was not written on his account alone that it was reckoned to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:24; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30"> but on ours also, to whom, believing on him who has raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead Jesus our Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 4:9–10; Isaiah 53:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> who has been delivered for our offences and has been raised for our justification, it will be reckoned.</span>
