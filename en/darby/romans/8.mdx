<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:18–19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 3:13"> &#91;There is&#93; then now no condemnation to those in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 6:14; 2 Corinthians 3:17; Romans 6:18"> For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has set me free from the law of sin and of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:4–5; Hebrews 7:18–19"> For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, having sent his own Son, in likeness of flesh of sin, and for sin, has condemned sin in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Galatians 5:22–25; Colossians 1:22"> in order that the righteous requirement of the law should be fulfilled in us, who do not walk according to flesh but according to Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Galatians 5:19–25; John 3:6"> For they that are according to flesh mind the things of the flesh; and they that are according to Spirit, the things of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:7; Romans 8:13"> For the mind of the flesh &#91;is&#93; death; but the mind of the Spirit life and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 John 2:15–16; James 4:4"> Because the mind of the flesh is enmity against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God: for it is not subject to the law of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; for neither indeed can it be:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:5–6; Hebrews 11:5–6; Romans 8:9"> and they that are in flesh cannot please<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.8 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Timothy 1:14"> But ye are not in flesh but in Spirit, if indeed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God's Spirit dwell in you; but if any one has not &#91;the&#93; Spirit of Christ he is not of him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> but if Christ be in you, the body is dead on account of sin, but the Spirit life on account of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> But if the Spirit of him that has raised up Jesus from among &#91;the&#93; dead dwell in you, he that has raised up Christ from among &#91;the&#93; dead shall quicken your mortal bodies also on account of his Spirit which dwells in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:2–15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> So then, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live according to flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; 1 Peter 2:11; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> for if ye live according to flesh, ye are about to die; but if, by the Spirit, ye put to death the deeds of the body, ye shall live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; John 1:12; Galatians 3:26"> for as many as are led by &#91;the&#93; Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.14 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, these are sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="8.14 Elohim">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:5–7; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Corinthians 2:12"> For ye have not received a spirit of bondage again for fear, but ye have received a spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:13; 2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5"> The Spirit itself bears witness with our spirit, that we are children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="8.16 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 4:7; Galatians 3:29; Ephesians 3:6"> And if children, heirs also: heirs of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="8.17 Elohim">i</a></sup>God, and Christ's joint heirs; if indeed we suffer with &#91;him&#93;, that we may also be glorified with &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17–18; 1 Peter 4:13; 1 Peter 1:5–7"> For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy &#91;to be compared&#93; with the coming glory to be revealed to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; Romans 8:23; 1 John 3:2"> For the anxious looking out of the creature expects the revelation of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="8.19 Elohim">j</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; Romans 8:22"> for the creature has been made subject to vanity, not of its will, but by reason of him who has subjected &#91;the same&#93;, in hope</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 22:3–5; Acts 3:21; 2 Peter 3:13"> that the creature itself also shall be set free from the bondage of corruption into the liberty of the glory of the children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="8.21 Elohim">k</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; John 16:21"> For we know that the whole creation groans together and travails in pain together until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–5; Hebrews 9:28"> And not only &#91;that&#93;, but even we ourselves, who have the first-fruits of the Spirit, we also ourselves groan in ourselves, awaiting adoption, &#91;that is&#93; the redemption of our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> For we have been saved in hope; but hope seen is not hope; for what any one sees, why does he also hope?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 27:14"> But if what we see not we hope, we expect in patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; Ephesians 6:18; Galatians 4:6"> And in like manner the Spirit joins also its help to our weakness; for we do not know what we should pray for as is fitting, but the Spirit itself makes intercession with groanings which cannot be uttered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; 1 John 5:14–15; Ephesians 2:18"> But he who searches the hearts knows what &#91;is&#93; the mind of the Spirit, because he intercedes for saints according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="8.27 Elohim">l</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; Genesis 50:20"> But we do know that all things work together for good to those who love<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="8.28 Elohim">m</a></sup>God, to those who are called according to purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4–5; Jeremiah 1:5; Ephesians 1:11"> Because whom he has foreknown, he has also predestinated &#91;to be&#93; conformed to the image of his Son, so that he should be &#91;the&#93; firstborn among many brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Romans 8:28; Ephesians 1:11"> But whom he has predestinated, these also he has called; and whom he has called, these also he has justified; but whom he has justified, these also he has glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; Jeremiah 1:19; Psalms 56:11"> What shall we then say to these things? If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="8.31 Elohim">n</a></sup>God &#91;be&#93; for us, who against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:28; Psalms 84:11"> He who, yea, has not spared his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not also with him grant us all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 12:10–11; Isaiah 50:8–9; Isaiah 54:17"> Who shall bring an accusation against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="8.33 Elohim">o</a></sup>God's elect? &#91;It is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="8.33 Elohim">p</a></sup>God who justifies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Romans 8:27; Hebrews 9:24"> who is he that condemns? &#91;It is&#93; Christ who has died, but rather has been &#91;also&#93; raised up; who is also at the right hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="8.34 Elohim">q</a></sup>God; who also intercedes for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 10:28; Psalms 103:17; John 16:33"> Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? tribulation or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Isaiah 53:7; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> According as it is written, For thy sake we are put to death all the day long; we have been reckoned as sheep for slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:57; 1 John 4:4; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> But in all these things we more than conquer through him that has loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 10:28; Romans 14:8; 2 Timothy 1:12"> For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:28–30; Romans 8:35; Romans 5:8"> nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="8.39 Elohim">r</a></sup>God, which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
