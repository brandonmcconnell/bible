<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I say &#91;the&#93; truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience bearing witness with me in &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> that I have great grief and uninterrupted pain in my heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> for I have wished, I myself, to be a curse from the Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen, according to flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> who are Israelites; whose &#91;is&#93; the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the law-giving, and the service, and the promises;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> whose &#91;are&#93; the fathers; and of whom, as according to flesh, &#91;is&#93; the Christ, who is over all,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God blessed for ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> Not however as though the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God had failed; for not all &#91;are&#93; Israel which &#91;are&#93; of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> nor because they are seed of Abraham &#91;are&#93; all children: but, In Isaac shall a seed be called to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> That is, &#91;they that are&#93; the children of the flesh, these &#91;are&#93; not the children of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.8 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; but the children of the promise are reckoned as seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this word &#91;is&#93; of promise, According to this time I will come, and there shall be a son to Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> And not only &#91;that&#93;, but Rebecca having conceived by one, Isaac our father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> &#91;the children&#93; indeed being not yet born, or having done anything good or worthless (that the purpose of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God according to election might abide, not of works, but of him that calls),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> it was said to her, The greater shall serve the less:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> according as it is written, I have loved Jacob, and I have hated Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What shall we say then? &#91;Is there&#93; unrighteousness with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.14 Elohim">e</a></sup>God? Far be the thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For he says to Moses, I will shew mercy to whom I will shew mercy, and I will feel compassion for whom I will feel compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> So then &#91;it is&#93; not of him that wills, nor of him that runs, but of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.16 Elohim">f</a></sup>God that shews mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the scripture says to Pharaoh, For this very thing I have raised thee up from amongst &#91;men&#93;, that I might thus shew in thee my power, and so that my name should be declared in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> So then, to whom he will he shews mercy, and whom he will he hardens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> Thou wilt say to me then, Why does he yet find fault? for who resists his purpose?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> Aye, but thou, O man, who art thou that answerest again to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.20 Elohim">g</a></sup>God? Shall the thing formed say to him that has formed it, Why hast thou made me thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Or has not the potter authority over the clay, out of the same lump to make one vessel to honour, and another to dishonour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> And if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="9.22 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, minded to shew his wrath and to make his power known, endured with much long-suffering vessels of wrath fitted for destruction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> and that he might make known the riches of his glory upon vessels of mercy, which he had before prepared for glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> us, whom he has also called, not only from amongst &#91;the&#93; Jews, but also from amongst &#91;the&#93; nations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As he says also in Hosea, I will call not-my-people My people; and the-not-beloved Beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> And it shall be, in the place where it was said to them, Ye &#91;are&#93; not my people, there shall they be called Sons of &#91;the&#93; living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="9.26 Elohim">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> But Esaias cries concerning Israel, Should the number of the children of Israel be as the sand of the sea, the remnant shall be saved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> for &#91;he&#93; is bringing the matter to an end, and &#91;cutting &#91;it&#93; short in righteousness; because&#93; a cutting short of the matter will &#91;the&#93; Lord accomplish upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> And according as Esaias said before, Unless &#91;the&#93; Lord of hosts had left us a seed, we had been as Sodom, and made like even as Gomorrha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What then shall we say? That &#91;they of the&#93; nations, who did not follow after righteousness, have attained righteousness, but &#91;the&#93; righteousness that is on the principle of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> But Israel, pursuing after a law of righteousness, has not attained to &#91;that&#93; law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Wherefore? Because &#91;it was&#93; not on the principle of faith, but as of works. They have stumbled at the stumblingstone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> according as it is written, Behold, I place in Zion a stone of stumbling and rock of offence: and he that believes on him shall not be ashamed.</span>
