<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; Romans 14:1; Galatians 6:1–2"> But we ought, we that are strong, to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let each one of us please his neighbour with a view to what is good, to edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> For the Christ also did not please himself; but according as it is written, The reproaches of them that reproach thee have fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> For as many things as have been written before have been written for our instruction, that through endurance and through encouragement of the scriptures we might have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> Now the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God of endurance and of encouragement give to you to be like-minded one toward another, according to Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> that ye may with one accord, with one mouth, glorify the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> Wherefore receive ye one another, according as the Christ also has received you to &#91;the&#93; glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> For I say that Jesus Christ became a minister of &#91;the&#93; circumcision for &#91;the&#93; truth of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.8 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, to confirm the promises of the fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalms 18:49; Romans 3:29"> and that the nations should glorify<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God for mercy; according as it is written, For this cause I will confess to thee among &#91;the&#93; nations, and will sing to thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> And again he says, Rejoice, nations, with his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 117:1"> And again, Praise the Lord, all &#91;ye&#93; nations, and let all the peoples laud him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And again, Esaias says, There shall be the root of Jesse, and one that arises, to rule over &#91;the&#93; nations: in him shall &#91;the&#93; nations hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> Now the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.13 Elohim">f</a></sup>God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that ye should abound in hope by &#91;the&#93; power of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> But I am persuaded, my brethren, I myself also, concerning you, that yourselves also are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> But I have written to you the more boldly, &#91;brethren,&#93; in part, as putting you in mind, because of the grace given to me by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.15 Elohim">g</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> for me to be minister of Christ Jesus to the nations, carrying on as a sacrificial service the &#91;message of&#93; glad tidings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15.16 Elohim">h</a></sup>God, in order that the offering up of the nations might be acceptable, sanctified by &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> I have therefore &#91;whereof to&#93; boast in Christ Jesus in the things which pertain to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="15.17 Elohim">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> For I will not dare to speak anything of the things which Christ has not wrought by me, for &#91;the&#93; obedience of &#91;the&#93; nations, by word and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> in &#91;the&#93; power of signs and wonders, in &#91;the&#93; power of &#91;the&#93; Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="15.19 Elohim">j</a></sup>God; so that I, from Jerusalem, and in a circuit round to Illyricum, have fully preached the glad tidings of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> and so aiming to announce the glad tidings, not where Christ has been named, that I might not build upon another's foundation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> but according as it is written, To whom there was nothing told concerning him, they shall see; and they that have not heard shall understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> Wherefore also I have been often hindered from coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> But now, having no longer place in these regions, and having great desire to come to you these many years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> whenever I should go to Spain; (for I hope to see you as I go through, and by you to be set forward thither, if first I shall have been in part filled with your company;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> but now I go to Jerusalem, ministering to the saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> for Macedonia and Achaia have been well pleased to make a certain contribution for the poor of the saints who &#91;are&#93; in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> They have been well pleased indeed, and they are their debtors; for if the nations have participated in their spiritual things, they ought also in fleshly to minister to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> Having finished this therefore, and having sealed to them this fruit, I will set off by you into Spain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> But I know that, coming to you, I shall come in &#91;the&#93; fulness of &#91;the&#93; blessing of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> But I beseech you, brethren, by our Lord Jesus Christ, and by the love of the Spirit, that ye strive together with me in prayers for me to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="15.30 Elohim">k</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> that I may be saved from those that do not believe in Judaea; and that my ministry which &#91;I have&#93; for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> in order that I may come to you in joy by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="15.32 Elohim">l</a></sup>God's will, and that I may be refreshed with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> And the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="15.33 Elohim">m</a></sup>God of peace be with you all. Amen.</span>
