<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brethren, the delight of my own heart and my supplication which &#91;I address&#93; to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God for them is for salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I bear them witness that they have zeal for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, but not according to knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For they, being ignorant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God's righteousness, and seeking to establish their own &#91;righteousness&#93;, have not submitted to the righteousness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.3 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is &#91;the&#93; end of law for righteousness to every one that believes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For Moses lays down in writing the righteousness which is of the law, The man who has practised those things shall live by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness of faith speaks thus: Do not say in thine heart, Who shall ascend to the heavens? that is, to bring Christ down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or, Who shall descend into the abyss? that is, to bring up Christ from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what says it? The word is near thee, in thy mouth and in thy heart: that is, the word of faith, which we preach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> that if thou shalt confess with thy mouth Jesus as Lord, and shalt believe in thine heart that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.9 Elohim">e</a></sup>God has raised him from among &#91;the&#93; dead, thou shalt be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with &#91;the&#93; heart is believed to righteousness; and with &#91;the&#93; mouth confession made to salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> For the scripture says, No one believing on him shall be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> For there is no difference of Jew and Greek; for the same Lord of all &#91;is&#93; rich towards all that call upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For every one whosoever, who shall call on the name of the Lord, shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then shall they call upon him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe on him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without one who preaches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> and how shall they preach unless they have been sent? according as it is written, How beautiful the feet of them that announce glad tidings of peace, of them that announce glad tidings of good things!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But they have not all obeyed the glad tidings. For Esaias says, Lord, who has believed our report?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> So faith then &#91;is&#93; by a report, but the report by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.17 Elohim">f</a></sup>God's word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I say, Have they not heard? Yea, surely, Their voice has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the extremities of the habitable world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> But I say, Has not Israel known? First, Moses says, I will provoke you to jealousy through &#91;them that are&#93; not a nation: through a nation without understanding I will anger you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> But Esaias is very bold, and says, I have been found by those not seeking me; I have become manifest to those not inquiring after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But unto Israel he says, All the day long I have stretched out my hands unto a people disobeying and opposing.</span>
