<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> Now him that is weak in the faith receive, not to &#91;the&#93; determining of questions of reasoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> One man is assured that he may eat all things; but the weak eats herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> Let not him that eats make little of him that eats not; and let not him that eats not judge him that eats: for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God has received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Who art thou that judgest the servant of another? to his own master he stands or falls. And he shall be made to stand; for the Lord is able to make him stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One man esteems day more than day; another esteems every day &#91;alike&#93;. Let each be fully persuaded in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He that regards the day, regards it to &#91;the&#93; Lord. And he that eats, eats to &#91;the&#93; Lord, for he gives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God thanks; and he that does not eat, &#91;it is&#93; to &#91;the&#93; Lord he does not eat, and gives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.6 Elohim">c</a></sup>God thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of us lives to himself, and none dies to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> For both if we should live, &#91;it is&#93; to the Lord we live; and if we should die, &#91;it is&#93; to the Lord we die: both if we should live then, and if we should die, we are the Lord's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> For to this &#91;end&#93; Christ has died and lived &#91;again&#93;, that he might rule over both dead and living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> But thou, why judgest thou thy brother? or again, thou, why dost thou make little of thy brother? for we shall all be placed before the judgment-seat of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> For it is written, I live, saith &#91;the&#93; Lord, that to me shall bow every knee, and every tongue shall confess to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.11 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So then each of us shall give an account concerning himself to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.12 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Let us no longer therefore judge one another; but judge ye this rather, not to put a stumbling-block or a fall-trap before his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I know, and am persuaded in the Lord Jesus, that nothing is unclean of itself; except to him who reckons anything to be unclean, to that man &#91;it is&#93; unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> For if on account of meat thy brother is grieved, thou walkest no longer according to love. Destroy not him with thy meat for whom Christ has died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Let not then your good be evil spoken of;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> for the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="14.17 Elohim">g</a></sup>God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness, and peace, and joy in &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> For he that in this serves the Christ &#91;is&#93; acceptable to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="14.18 Elohim">h</a></sup>God and approved of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> So then let us pursue the things which tend to peace, and things whereby one shall build up another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> For the sake of meat do not destroy the work of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="14.20 Elohim">i</a></sup>God. All things indeed &#91;are&#93; pure; but &#91;it is&#93; evil to that man who eats while stumbling &#91;in doing so&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> &#91;It is&#93; right not to eat meat, nor drink wine, nor &#91;do anything&#93; in which thy brother stumbles, or is offended, or is weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> Hast thou faith? have &#91;it&#93; to thyself before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="14.22 Elohim">j</a></sup>God. Blessed &#91;is&#93; he who does not judge himself in what he allows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> But he that doubts, if he eat, is condemned; because &#91;it is&#93; not of faith; but whatever &#91;is&#93; not of faith is sin.</span>
