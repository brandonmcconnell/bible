<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> What then &#91;is&#93; the superiority of the Jew? or what the profit of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much every way: and first, indeed, that to them were entrusted the oracles of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> For what? if some have not believed, shall their unbelief make the faith of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God of none effect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> Far be the thought: but let<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God be true, and every man false; according as it is written, So that thou shouldest be justified in thy words, and shouldest overcome when thou art in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness commend<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.5 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's righteousness, what shall we say? Is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.5 Elohim">e</a></sup>God unrighteous who inflicts wrath? I speak according to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> Far be the thought: since how shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.6 Elohim">f</a></sup>God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> For if the truth of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.7 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, in my lie, has more abounded to his glory, why yet am I also judged as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> and not, according as we are injuriously charged, and according as some affirm that we say, Let us practise evil things, that good ones may come? whose judgment is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? are we better? No, in no wise: for we have before charged both Jews and Greeks with being all under sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> according as it is written, There is not a righteous &#91;man&#93;, not even one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> there is not the &#91;man&#93; that understands, there is not one that seeks after<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.11 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> All have gone out of the way, they have together become unprofitable; there is not one that practises goodness, there is not so much as one:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit; asps' poison &#91;is&#93; under their lips:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> swift their feet to shed blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> ruin and misery &#91;are&#93; in their ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> and way of peace they have not known:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> there is no fear of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.18 Elohim">i</a></sup>God before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever the things the law says, it speaks to those under the law, that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world be under judgment to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.19 Elohim">j</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> Wherefore by works of law no flesh shall be justified before him; for by law &#91;is&#93; knowledge of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now without law righteousness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="3.21 Elohim">k</a></sup>God is manifested, borne witness to by the law and the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> righteousness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="3.22 Elohim">l</a></sup>God by faith of Jesus Christ towards all, and upon all those who believe: for there is no difference;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all have sinned, and come short of the glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="3.23 Elohim">m</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> being justified freely by his grace through the redemption which &#91;is&#93; in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="3.25 Elohim">n</a></sup>God has set forth a mercy-seat, through faith in his blood, for &#91;the&#93; shewing forth of his righteousness, in respect of the passing by the sins that had taken place before, through the forbearance of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="3.25 Elohim">o</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> for &#91;the&#93; shewing forth of his righteousness in the present time, so that he should be just, and justify him that is of &#91;the&#93; faith of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where then &#91;is&#93; boasting? It has been excluded. By what law? of works? Nay, but by law of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> for we reckon that a man is justified by faith, without works of law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Is &#91;God&#93; the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="3.29 Elohim">p</a></sup>God of Jews only? is he not of &#91;the&#93; nations also? Yea, of nations also:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> since indeed &#91;it is&#93; one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="3.30 Elohim">q</a></sup>God who shall justify &#91;the&#93; circumcision on the principle of faith, and uncircumcision by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Do we then make void law by faith? Far be the thought: &#91;no,&#93; but we establish law.</span>
