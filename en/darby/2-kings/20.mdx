<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–22; 2 Chronicles 32:24–26; Philippians 2:27"> In those days Hezekiah was sick unto death. And the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz came to him and said to him, Thus saith Jehovah: Set thy house in order; for thou shalt die, and not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Matthew 6:6; Isaiah 38:2–3"> And he turned his face to the wall, and prayed to Jehovah saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:14; Nehemiah 13:22; Nehemiah 5:19"> Ah! Jehovah, remember, I beseech thee, how I have walked before thee in truth and with a perfect heart, and have done what is good in thy sight. And Hezekiah wept much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 22:14; 1 Kings 7:8"> And it came to pass before Isaiah had gone out into the middle city that the word of Jehovah came to him saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 66:19–20; Isaiah 38:5; 1 Samuel 9:16"> Return, and tell Hezekiah the captain of my people, Thus saith Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of David thy father: I have heard thy prayer, I have seen thy tears; behold, I will heal thee: on the third day thou shalt go up to the house of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 19:34; Acts 27:24; Isaiah 10:24"> and I will add to thy days fifteen years; and I will deliver thee and this city out of the hand of the king of Assyria, and I will defend this city for mine own sake, and for my servant David's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 38:21; 2 Kings 2:20–22; 2 Kings 4:41"> And Isaiah said, Take a cake of figs. And they took and laid it on the boil, and he recovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 6:17; Isaiah 7:11; 2 Kings 19:29"> And Hezekiah said to Isaiah, What &#91;shall be&#93; the sign that Jehovah will heal me, and that I shall go up into the house of Jehovah the third day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 38:7–8; Mark 8:11–12; Matthew 16:1–4"> And Isaiah said, This &#91;shall be&#93; the sign to thee from Jehovah, that Jehovah will do the thing that he hath spoken: shall the shadow go forward ten degrees, or go back ten degrees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 3:18; 2 Kings 2:10; John 14:12"> And Hezekiah said, It is a light thing for the shadow to go down ten degrees: no, but let the shadow return backward ten degrees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 38:8; Joshua 10:12–14; 2 Chronicles 32:24"> And Isaiah the prophet cried to Jehovah, and he brought the shadow back on the degrees by which it had gone down on the dial of Ahaz, ten degrees backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 39:1–8; 2 Chronicles 32:31; Isaiah 13:1"> At that time Berodach-Baladan, the son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent a letter and a present to Hezekiah, for he had heard that Hezekiah had been sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 32:25–27; 1 Kings 10:15"> And Hezekiah hearkened to them, and shewed them all his treasure-house, the silver and the gold, and the spices and the fine oil, and all the house of his armour, and all that was found among his treasures: there was nothing in his house, nor in all his dominion, that Hezekiah did not shew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:5; 2 Samuel 12:7–15; 2 Kings 5:25–26"> Then came the prophet Isaiah to king Hezekiah and said to him, What said these men? and from whence came they to thee? And Hezekiah said, They came from a far country, from Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:33; Joshua 7:19; 2 Kings 20:13"> And he said, What have they seen in thy house? And Hezekiah said, All that is in my house have they seen: there is nothing among my treasures that I have not shewn them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 22:19; 2 Kings 7:1; Isaiah 1:10"> And Isaiah said to Hezekiah, Hear the word of Jehovah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Jeremiah 52:17–19"> Behold, days come that all that is in thy house, and what thy fathers have laid up until this day, shall be carried to Babylon; nothing shall be left, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; 2 Kings 24:12; Daniel 1:3"> And of thy sons that shall issue from thee, whom thou shalt beget, shall they take away; and they shall be chamberlains in the palace of the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; Zechariah 8:19; Luke 2:10"> And Hezekiah said to Isaiah, Good is the word of Jehovah which thou hast spoken. And he said, Is it not so? if only there shall be peace and truth in my days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:16; 2 Chronicles 32:32; 2 Chronicles 32:4"> And the rest of the acts of Hezekiah, and all his might, and how he made the pool and the aqueduct, and brought the water into the city, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33; 1 Kings 14:31; 2 Chronicles 26:23"> And Hezekiah slept with his fathers; and Manasseh his son reigned in his stead.</span>
