<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Kings 11:21; 2 Kings 11:1–4"> In the seventh year of Jehu, Jehoash began to reign; and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem; and his mother's name was Zibiah of Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:17–22; 2 Chronicles 26:4; 2 Chronicles 25:2"> And Jehoash did what was right in the sight of Jehovah, all the days wherein Jehoiada the priest instructed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 1 Kings 22:43; 1 Kings 15:14"> Only, the high places were not removed: the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 22:4; Exodus 35:5; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9"> And Jehoash said to the priests, All the money of the hallowed things that is brought into the house of Jehovah, the money of every one that passes &#91;the account&#93;, the money at which every man is valued, &#91;and&#93; all the money that comes into any man's heart to bring into the house of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; 1 Kings 11:27; 2 Kings 22:5–6"> let the priests take it, every man of his acquaintance; and let them repair the breaches of the house, wherever any breach is found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 1 Peter 5:2; Isaiah 56:10–12"> And it was &#91;so that&#93; in the twenty-third year of king Jehoash, the priests had not repaired the breaches of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:16; 2 Chronicles 24:5–14; 2 Kings 11:4"> Then king Jehoash called for Jehoiada the priest, and the priests, and said to them, Why have ye not repaired the breaches of the house? And now receive no money of your acquaintances, but give it for the breaches of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the priests consented to receive no money of the people, and that they should only repair the breaches of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 12:41; Psalms 84:10; Jeremiah 35:4"> And Jehoiada the priest took a chest, and bored a hole in the lid, and set it beside the altar, on the right side as one comes into the house of Jehovah; and the priests that kept the door put into it all the money brought into the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Kings 22:12; 2 Kings 19:2"> And it came to pass when they saw that there was much money in the chest, that the king's scribe and the high priest came up, and they tied up and counted the money that was found in the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 22:5–6; 2 Chronicles 24:11–12; 2 Chronicles 34:9–11"> And they gave the money, weighed out into the hands of them that did the work, who were appointed over the house of Jehovah; and they laid it out to the carpenters and builders that wrought upon the house of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 22:5–6; 1 Kings 5:17–18; Ezra 3:7"> and to masons, and hewers of stone, and to buy timber and hewn stone to repair the breaches of the house of Jehovah, and for all that had to be laid out on the house for repairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:14; 1 Kings 7:48–50; Ezra 1:9–11"> However there were not made for the house of Jehovah basons of silver, knives, bowls, trumpets, nor any utensil of gold or utensil of silver, of the money that was brought &#91;into&#93; the house of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> but they gave that to the workmen, and repaired the house of Jehovah with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 22:7; 3 John 1:5; 1 Corinthians 4:2–3"> And they did not reckon with the men into whose hand they gave the money to be bestowed on workmen; for they dealt faithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 5:15–18; Leviticus 4:24"> The money of trespass-offerings, and the money of sin-offerings, was not brought into the house of Jehovah: it was for the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:23–24; Luke 9:53; Jeremiah 42:15"> Then Hazael king of Syria went up, and fought against Gath, and took it. And Hazael set his face to go up against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:4"> And Jehoash king of Judah took all the hallowed things that Jehoshaphat and Jehoram and Ahaziah, his fathers, kings of Judah, had dedicated, and his own hallowed things, and all the gold found in the treasures of the house of Jehovah and in the king's house, and sent it to Hazael king of Syria; and he went away from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:19; 1 Kings 11:41"> And the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:24–27; 2 Kings 14:5; 2 Samuel 5:9"> And his servants rose up and made a conspiracy, and smote Joash in the house of Millo, at the descent of Silla.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 14:1; 2 Chronicles 24:26–27"> And Jozachar the son of Shimeath and Jehozabad the son of Shomer, his servants, smote him and he died; and they buried him with his fathers in the city of David. And Amaziah his son reigned in his stead.</span>
