<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; Numbers 24:7; 2 Kings 3:4–5"> And Moab rebelled against Israel after the death of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 1:16; Matthew 10:25; Mark 3:22"> And Ahaziah fell down through the lattice in his upper chamber which was in Samaria, and was sick; and he sent messengers and said to them, Go, inquire of Baal-zebub the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>god of Ekron, whether I shall recover from this disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Jonah 2:8; Acts 12:7–11"> And the angel of Jehovah said to Elijah the Tishbite, Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and say to them, Is it because there is not a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God in Israel, that ye go to inquire of Baal-zebub the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>god of Ekron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 3:4; Genesis 2:17; Ezekiel 18:4"> Now therefore thus saith Jehovah: Thou shalt not come down from the bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt certainly die. And Elijah departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the messengers returned to him; and he said to them, Why have ye returned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:4; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14; Isaiah 41:22–23"> And they said unto him, A man came up to meet us, and said to us, Go, return to the king that sent you and say to him, Thus saith Jehovah: Is it because there is not a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God in Israel, that thou sendest to inquire of Baal-zebub the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>god of Ekron? therefore thou shalt not come down from the bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt certainly die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:14; Judges 8:18"> And he said to them, What manner of man was he that came up to meet you, and told you these words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> And they said to him, He was a man in a hairy &#91;garment&#93;, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins. And he said, It is Elijah the Tishbite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13–14; Luke 6:11–12; Hebrews 11:36"> And he sent to him a captain of fifty with his fifty. And he went up to him, and behold, he sat on the top of the mount. And he spoke to him: Man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, the king says, Come down!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:54; Daniel 3:25; 1 Kings 18:36–38"> And Elijah answered and said to the captain of fifty, And if I be a man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, let fire come down from the heavens and consume thee and thy fifty. And there came down fire from the heavens, and consumed him and his fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 2:16; Luke 22:63–64; Jeremiah 5:3"> And again he sent to him another captain of fifty with his fifty. And he spoke and said to him, Man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, thus says the king: Come down quickly!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 1:9–10"> And Elijah answered and said to them, If I be a man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, let fire come down from the heavens and consume thee and thy fifty. And the fire of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God came down from the heavens, and consumed him and his fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; 1 Samuel 26:21; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And again he sent the captain of a third fifty with his fifty. And the third captain of fifty went up, and came and fell on his knees before Elijah, and besought him and said to him, Man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, I pray thee, let my life, and the life of these fifty thy servants, be precious in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Psalms 49:8; Proverbs 6:26"> Behold, there came down fire from the heavens, and consumed the two captains of the former fifties with their fifties; but now, let my life be precious in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:12; Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 1:17"> And the angel of Jehovah said to Elijah, Go down with him: be not afraid of him. And he arose, and went down with him to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:2–4; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 4:22–23"> And he said to him, Thus saith Jehovah: Forasmuch as thou hast sent messengers to inquire of Baal-zebub the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>god of Ekron, is it because there is no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God in Israel to inquire of his word? therefore thou shalt not come down from the bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt certainly die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 22:51; 2 Kings 8:16–17"> And he died according to the word of Jehovah that Elijah had spoken. And Jehoram began to reign in his stead, in the second year of Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah; for he had no son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; 1 Kings 14:19"> And the rest of the acts of Ahaziah, what he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
</div>
