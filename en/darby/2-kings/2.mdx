<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 5:24; Hebrews 11:5; 1 Kings 19:16–21"> And it came to pass when Jehovah would take up Elijah into the heavens by a whirlwind, that Elijah went with Elisha from Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 2:6; Ruth 1:15–18; 2 Kings 4:30"> And Elijah said to Elisha, Abide here, I pray thee; for Jehovah has sent me to Bethel. And Elisha said, As Jehovah liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee! So they went down to Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 9:1"> And the sons of the prophets that were at Bethel came forth to Elisha, and said to him, Dost thou know that Jehovah will take away thy master from over thy head to-day? And he said, I also know it: be silent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 2:2; Joshua 6:26; 1 Kings 16:34"> And Elijah said to him, Elisha, abide here, I pray thee; for Jehovah has sent me to Jericho. And he said, As Jehovah liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee! And they came to Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 2:3; Joshua 1:1–2; Acts 1:11"> And the sons of the prophets that were at Jericho drew near to Elisha and said to him, Dost thou know that Jehovah will take away thy master from over thy head to-day? And he said, I also know it: be silent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 2:2; Joshua 3:15"> And Elijah said to him, Abide here, I pray thee; for Jehovah has sent me to the Jordan. And he said, As Jehovah liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee! And they two went on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; 2 Kings 2:17; 1 Kings 18:4"> And fifty men of the sons of the prophets went and stood opposite afar off; and they two stood by the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Exodus 14:21–22; Joshua 3:14–17"> And Elijah took his mantle, and wrapped it together, and smote the waters, and they were divided hither and thither; and they two went over on dry ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Deuteronomy 21:17; John 16:7"> And it came to pass when they had gone over, that Elijah said to Elisha, Ask what I shall do for thee, before I am taken away from thee. And Elisha said, I pray thee, let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 11:22–24; 2 Kings 2:12; John 16:24"> And he said, Thou hast asked a hard thing: if thou see me when I am taken from thee, it shall be so to thee; but if not, it shall not be &#91;so&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Psalms 104:3–4; Hebrews 1:14"> And it came to pass as they went on, and talked, that behold, a chariot of fire and horses of fire; and they parted them both asunder; and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; Isaiah 37:21; Mark 16:19"> And Elisha saw &#91;it&#93;, and he cried, My father, my father! the chariot of Israel and the horsemen thereof! And he saw him no more. Then he took hold of his own garments and rent them in two pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26"> And he took up the mantle of Elijah which fell from him, and went back and stood by the bank of the Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:12; Psalms 42:10; Psalms 115:2"> and he took the mantle of Elijah which had fallen from him, and smote the waters, and said, Where is Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Elijah? He also smote the waters, and they parted hither and thither, and Elisha went over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Acts 1:8; Joshua 3:7"> And the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho on the opposite side saw him, and they said, The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha. And they came to meet him, and bowed themselves to the ground before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; Acts 8:39; 2 Corinthians 12:2–3"> and said to him, Behold now, there are with thy servants fifty valiant men; let them go, we pray thee, and seek thy master, lest perhaps the Spirit of Jehovah have taken him up, and cast him upon some mountain, or into some ravine. And he said, Ye shall not send.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 8:11; Hebrews 11:5; Romans 10:2"> And they pressed him till he was ashamed, and he said, Send. They sent therefore fifty men, and they sought three days, but did not find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they came again to him (now he was staying at Jericho); and he said to them, Did I not say to you, Go not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Deuteronomy 28:2–4; Deuteronomy 28:11"> And the men of the city said to Elisha, Behold now, the situation of the city is good, as my lord sees; but the water is bad, and the land is barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:45; Zephaniah 2:9; Ezekiel 47:11"> And he said, Bring me a new cruse, and put salt in it. And they brought it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:25–26; 2 Kings 4:41; Leviticus 2:13"> And he went forth to the source of the waters, and cast the salt in there, and said, Thus saith Jehovah: I have healed these waters: there shall not be from thence any more death or barrenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the waters were healed to this day, according to the saying of Elisha which he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Job 19:18; Job 30:1"> And he went up from thence to Bethel, and as he went up by the way, there came forth little boys out of the city, and mocked him, and said to him, Go up, bald head; go up, bald head!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 13:9–11; Acts 5:5; Hosea 13:8"> And he turned back, and looked on them, and cursed them in the name of Jehovah. And there came forth two she-bears out of the wood, and tore forty-two children of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 18:19–20; 1 Kings 18:42"> And he went from thence to mount Carmel, and from thence he returned to Samaria.</span>
