<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:25; 2 Kings 2:3; Nehemiah 5:2–5"> And a woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried to Elisha saying, Thy servant my husband is dead, and thou knowest that thy servant feared Jehovah; and the creditor is come to take my two children to be bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 17:12; James 2:5; 2 Kings 6:26–27"> And Elisha said to her, What shall I do for thee? Tell me, what hast thou in the house? And she said, Thy handmaid has not anything at all in the house but a pot of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 2:7; Psalms 81:10; John 16:24"> And he said, Go, borrow for thyself vessels abroad from all thy neighbours, empty vessels; let it not be few;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 8:5–9; Matthew 6:6; Ephesians 3:20"> and go in, and shut the door upon thee and upon thy sons, and pour out into all those vessels, and set aside what is full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 5:11; Hebrews 11:7–8; 1 Kings 17:15–16"> And she went from him, and shut the door upon her and upon her sons: they brought &#91;the vessels&#93; to her, and she poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; 2 Kings 13:19; Luke 6:19"> And it came to pass when the vessels were full, that she said to her son, Bring me yet a vessel. And he said to her, There is not a vessel more. And the oil stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–12; 1 Kings 12:22; Romans 12:17"> And she came and told the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God; and he said, Go, sell the oil, and pay thy debt, and live thou &#91;and&#93; thy sons on the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Genesis 19:3; 2 Kings 4:11–12"> And it came to pass on a day, that Elisha passed to Shunem, where was a wealthy woman; and she constrained him to eat bread. And so it was, &#91;that&#93; as oft as he passed by, he turned in thither to eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 17:24; Proverbs 31:10–11; 2 Kings 4:7"> And she said to her husband, Behold now, I perceive that this is a holy man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, who passes by us continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:13; Matthew 10:41–42; Matthew 25:40"> Let us make, I pray thee, a small upper chamber with walls, and let us set for him there a bed, and a table, and a seat, and a lampstand; and it shall be when he cometh to us, he shall turn in thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And it came to pass on a day, that he came thither, and he turned into the upper chamber, and lay there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 8:4–5; 2 Kings 3:11; 2 Kings 4:29–31"> And he said to Gehazi his servant, Call this Shunammite. And he called her, and she stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:16–18; Philippians 4:18–19; Hebrews 6:10"> And he said to him, Say now to her, Behold, thou hast been careful for us with all this care; what is to be done for thee? wouldest thou be spoken for to the king, or to the captain of the host? And she said, I dwell among mine own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:7; Genesis 15:2–3; 1 Samuel 1:8"> And he said, What then is to be done for her? And Gehazi said, Verily, she has no son, and her husband is old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said, Call her; and he called her; and she stood in the doorway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Luke 1:13; 2 Kings 4:28"> And he said, At this appointed time, when thy term is come, thou shalt embrace a son. And she said, No, my lord, man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, do not lie to thy handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Luke 1:36; Genesis 21:1"> And the woman conceived, and bore a son at that appointed time in the next year as Elisha had said to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:4"> And the child grew, and it came to pass one day, that he went out to his father to the reapers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 14:1–2; Jeremiah 4:19"> And he said to his father, My head, my head! And he said to the servant, Carry him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:12; John 11:3; John 11:14"> And he carried him, and brought him to his mother; and he sat on her knees till noon, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; 2 Kings 4:7"> And she went up, and laid him on the bed of the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and shut &#91;the door&#93; upon him, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:38; 2 Kings 4:26; John 11:3"> And she called to her husband, and said, Send me, I pray thee, one of the young men, and one of the asses, and I will run to the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, and come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Numbers 28:11; 1 Chronicles 23:31"> And he said, Why wilt thou go to him to-day? It is neither new moon nor sabbath. And she said, &#91;It is&#93; well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 4:20; 1 Kings 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:19–20"> Then she saddled the ass, and said to her servant, Drive and go forward; slack not the riding for me, except I bid thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 2:25; Isaiah 35:2; 1 Kings 18:19"> And she went and came to the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, to mount Carmel. And it came to pass when the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God saw her afar off, that he said to Gehazi his servant, Behold, there is the Shunammite:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:36; 2 Kings 4:23; 1 Samuel 17:18"> run now, I pray thee, to meet her, and say unto her, Is it well with thee? is it well with thy husband? is it well with the child? And she said, It is well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Genesis 18:17; Amos 3:7"> And she came to the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God to the mountain, and caught him by the feet; and Gehazi drew near to thrust her away; but the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="4.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God said, Let her alone, for her soul is troubled within her, and Jehovah has hidden it from me, and has not told me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 30:1; 2 Kings 4:16"> And she said, Did I desire a son of my lord? did I not say, Do not deceive me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 2:14; 1 Kings 18:46; Exodus 14:16"> And he said to Gehazi, Gird up thy loins, and take my staff in thy hand, and go thy way. If thou meet any man, salute him not, and if any salute thee, answer him not again; and lay my staff upon the face of the lad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–18; 2 Kings 2:2; Exodus 33:12–16"> And the mother of the lad said, As Jehovah liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee! And he rose up and followed her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:11; Mark 5:39; Mark 9:19–29"> And Gehazi passed on before them, and laid the staff upon the face of the lad; but there was neither voice, nor sign of attention. And he returned to meet him, and told him saying, The lad is not awaked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 8:52–53; John 11:17; 1 Kings 17:17"> And when Elisha came into the house, behold, the child was dead, &#91;and&#93; laid upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; 2 Kings 4:4; Acts 9:40"> And he went in and shut the door upon them both, and prayed to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 17:21; Acts 20:10"> And he went up, and lay upon the child, and put his mouth upon his mouth, and his eyes upon his eyes, and his hands upon his hands, and bent over him; and the flesh of the child grew warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 8:5; 2 Kings 13:21; 1 Kings 17:22"> And he returned, and walked in the house to and fro; and went up, and bent over him. And the lad sneezed seven times, and the lad opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 1 Kings 17:23; Luke 7:15"> And he called Gehazi, and said, Call this Shunammite. And he called her; and she came to him. And he said, Take up thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 2 Kings 2:15; 1 Kings 17:24"> And she came and fell at his feet, and bowed herself to the ground; and she took up her son, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 2:1"> And Elisha came again to Gilgal. And there was a famine in the land; and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him. And he said to his servant, Set on the great pot, and boil pottage for the sons of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 5:4; Matthew 15:13"> Then one went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a wild vine, and gathered from it his lap full of wild colocynths, and came and shred them into the pot of pottage; for they did not know them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 16:18; Exodus 15:23; Exodus 10:17"> And they poured out for the men to eat. And it came to pass, as they were eating of the pottage, that they cried out and said, Man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="4.40 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, there is death in the pot! And they could not eat &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Exodus 15:25; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> And he said, Then bring meal. And he cast &#91;it&#93; into the pot, and said, Pour out for the people, that they may eat. And there was no harm in the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7; John 6:9; Matthew 14:16–21"> And there came a man from Baal-shalishah, and brought the man of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="4.42 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God bread of the first-fruits, twenty loaves of barley, and fresh ears of corn in his sack. And he said, Give to the people that they may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:13; Matthew 14:20; John 6:9"> And his attendant said, How shall I set this before a hundred men? And he said, Give the people that they may eat; for thus saith Jehovah: They shall eat, and shall have to spare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And he set &#91;it&#93; before them, and they ate and left &#91;thereof&#93;, according to the word of Jehovah.</span>
