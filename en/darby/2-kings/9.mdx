<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 2 Kings 4:29; 1 Kings 1:39"> And Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets, and said to him, Gird up thy loins, and take this vial of oil in thy hand, and go to Ramoth-Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:5; 1 Kings 19:16–17; 2 Kings 9:11"> And when thou art come thither, look out there Jehu the son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, and go in, and make him rise up from among his brethren, and bring him to an inner chamber;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:16; Exodus 29:7; 1 Samuel 16:2"> then take the vial of oil, and pour it on his head and say, Thus saith Jehovah: I have anointed thee king over Israel; and open the door, and flee, and tarry not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the young man, the young prophet, went to Ramoth-Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:19"> And when he came, behold, the captains of the host were sitting. And he said, I have an errand to thee, captain. And Jehu said, To which of all of us? And he said, To thee, captain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 19:16; 2 Kings 9:3"> And he rose up and went into the house; and he poured the oil on his head, and said to him, Thus saith Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel: I have anointed thee king over the people of Jehovah, over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:15; 1 Kings 18:4; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And thou shalt smite the house of Ahab thy master; and I will avenge the blood of my servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of Jehovah at the hand of Jezebel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; Deuteronomy 32:36; 2 Kings 14:26"> And the whole house of Ahab shall perish, and I will cut off from Ahab every male, and him that is shut up and left in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 15:29; 1 Kings 21:22; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> And I will make the house of Ahab as the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and as the house of Baasha the son of Ahijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; 2 Kings 9:35–36; 2 Kings 9:3"> And the dogs shall eat Jezebel in the plot of Jizreel, and none shall bury her. And he opened the door and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:26; Acts 26:24; Hosea 9:7"> And Jehu came forth to the servants of his lord. And one said to him, Is all well? why came this madman to thee? And he said to them, Ye know the man, and his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And they said, It is false! tell us now. And he said, Thus and thus spoke he to me saying, Thus saith Jehovah: I have anointed thee king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; 1 Kings 1:39; 1 Kings 1:34"> Then they hasted and took every man his garment, and put it under him on the very stairs, and blew with trumpets, and said, Jehu is king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:28; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Kings 16:16"> And Jehu the son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. (Now Joram kept Ramoth-Gilead, he and all Israel, because of Hazael king of Syria;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 1 Samuel 27:9–11; 2 Chronicles 22:6"> and king Joram had returned to be healed in Jizreel of the wounds that the Syrians had given him, when he fought against Hazael king of Syria.) And Jehu said, If it be your will, let not a fugitive escape out of the city to go to tell &#91;it&#93; in Jizreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6–7; 2 Kings 8:28–29"> And Jehu rode in a chariot and went to Jizreel; for Joram lay there. And Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to see Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:4; Ezekiel 33:2–9; 2 Kings 7:14"> And the watchman stood on the tower in Jizreel, and saw Jehu's company as he came, and said, I see a company. And Joram said, Take a horseman, and send to meet them, and let him say, Is it peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 9:19; 2 Kings 9:22; Jeremiah 16:5"> So there went one on horseback to meet him; and he said, Thus saith the king: Is it peace? And Jehu said, What hast thou to do with peace? turn thee behind me. And the watchman told saying, The messenger came to them, and he does not return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he sent out a second on horseback; and he came to them and said, Thus saith the king: Is it peace? And Jehu said, What hast thou to do with peace? turn thee behind me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:27; 1 Kings 19:17; Ecclesiastes 9:10"> And the watchman told saying, He came to them, and does not return. And the driving is like the driving of Jehu the son of Nimshi; for he drives furiously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 21:1–7; 2 Chronicles 22:7; Micah 1:13"> Then Joram said, Make ready! And they made ready his chariot. And Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each in his chariot; and they went out to meet Jehu, and met him in the plot of Naboth the Jizreelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:13; Nahum 3:4; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> And it came to pass when Joram saw Jehu, that he said, Is it peace, Jehu? And he said, What peace, so long as the fornications of thy mother Jezebel and her sorceries are so many?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; 2 Chronicles 23:13"> Then Joram turned his hand, and fled, and said to Ahaziah, Treachery, Ahaziah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> And Jehu took his bow in his hand, and smote Jehoram between his arms, and the arrow went out through his heart; and he sank down in his chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; 1 Kings 21:24–29; Isaiah 13:1"> And he said to Bidkar his captain, Take him up &#91;and&#93; cast him in the plot of the field of Naboth the Jizreelite. For remember how, when I and thou rode together after Ahab his father, that Jehovah laid this burden upon him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Deuteronomy 5:9; 2 Chronicles 25:4"> Certainly I have seen yesterday the blood of Naboth, and the blood of his sons, saith Jehovah; and I will requite thee in this plot, saith Jehovah. And now, take &#91;and&#93; cast him into the plot, according to the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11; 2 Kings 8:29"> When Ahaziah king of Judah saw &#91;that&#93;, he fled by the way of the garden-house. And Jehu followed after him, and said, Smite him also in his chariot. It was on the ascent of Gur, which is by Jibleam. And he fled to Megiddo, and died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:30; 2 Kings 12:21"> And his servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem, and buried him in his sepulchre with his fathers in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; 2 Chronicles 22:1–2; 2 Kings 8:24–25"> (And in the eleventh year of Joram the son of Ahab had Ahaziah begun to reign over Judah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 23:40; Ezekiel 24:17"> And Jehu came to Jizreel; and Jezebel heard of it, and she put paint to her eyes, and decked her head, and looked out at the window.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 16:9–20"> And when Jehu came in at the gate, she said, Is it peace, Zimri, murderer of his master?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esther 2:15; Exodus 32:26; 2 Chronicles 11:12"> And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, Who is on my side? who? And two or three chamberlains looked out to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Lamentations 1:15; Isaiah 25:10"> And he said, Throw her down! And they threw her down; and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses; and he trampled on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 21:25; Isaiah 65:15"> And he came in, and ate and drank; and he said, Go, look, I pray you, after this cursed &#91;woman&#93;, and bury her; for she is a king's daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Isaiah 14:18–20; Job 31:3"> And they went to bury her; but they found no more of her than the skull, and the feet, and the palms of the hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; Leviticus 8:36; 2 Kings 14:25"> And they came back and told him. And he said, This is the word of Jehovah, which he spoke through his servant Elijah the Tishbite saying, In the plot of Jizreel shall dogs eat the flesh of Jezebel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 16:4"> and the carcase of Jezebel shall be as dung upon the open field in the plot of Jizreel, so that they shall not say, This is Jezebel.</span>
