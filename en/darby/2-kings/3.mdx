<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 22:51; 2 Kings 8:16"> And Jehoram the son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah; and he reigned twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 10:18; 2 Kings 10:26–28; 1 Kings 21:25"> And he wrought evil in the sight of Jehovah, but not like his father, and like his mother; and he took away the column of Baal that his father had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 15:34; 2 Kings 15:18"> Only, he clave to the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat who made Israel to sin: he did not depart therefrom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; Genesis 26:13–14; Job 1:3"> And Mesha king of Moab was a sheepmaster, and rendered to the king of Israel a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams, with the wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 1:1; 2 Kings 8:20; 2 Chronicles 21:8–10"> And it came to pass when Ahab was dead, that the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:1–25; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 15:4"> And at that time king Jehoram went out of Samaria and inspected all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:3"> And he went and sent to Jehoshaphat the king of Judah saying, The king of Moab has rebelled against me: wilt thou go with me against Moab to battle? And he said, I will go up: I am as thou, my people as thy people, my horses as thy horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Numbers 21:4"> And he said, Which way shall we go up? And he said, By way of the wilderness of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; Numbers 20:2; Exodus 17:1"> And the king of Israel went, and the king of Judah, and the king of Edom, and they made a circuit of seven days' journey. And there was no water for the army, and for the cattle that followed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–36; Isaiah 51:20; Isaiah 8:21"> And the king of Israel said, Alas! that Jehovah has called these three kings together, to give them into the hand of Moab!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7; 1 Kings 19:21; 2 Kings 3:3"> And Jehoshaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of Jehovah that we may inquire of Jehovah by him? And one of the king of Israel's servants answered and said, Here is Elisha the son of Shaphat, who poured water on the hands of Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 5:15; 2 Kings 2:21; Isaiah 60:14"> And Jehoshaphat said, The word of Jehovah is with him. And the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat and the king of Edom went down to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 18:19; 1 Kings 22:10–11"> And Elisha said to the king of Israel, What have I to do with thee? go to the prophets of thy father and to the prophets of thy mother. And the king of Israel said to him, Not so, for Jehovah has called these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 1 Kings 17:1; 2 Chronicles 19:3–4"> And Elisha said, As Jehovah of hosts liveth, before whom I stand, surely, were it not that I regard the presence of Jehoshaphat the king of Judah, I would not look toward thee nor see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:23; Ephesians 5:18–19; Ezekiel 1:3"> And now fetch me a minstrel. And it came to pass when the minstrel played, that the hand of Jehovah was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 2:16–18; 2 Kings 4:3"> And he said, Thus saith Jehovah: Make this valley full of ditches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 41:17–18; Isaiah 48:21"> For thus saith Jehovah: Ye shall not see wind, neither shall ye see rain; yet this valley shall be filled with water, and ye shall drink, both ye, and your cattle, and your beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:17"> And this is a light thing in the sight of Jehovah: he will give the Moabites also into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Judges 6:16"> And ye shall smite every fortified city, and every choice city, and shall fell every good tree, and stop all wells of water, and mar every good piece of land with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:39–40; Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36"> And it came to pass in the morning, when the oblation was offered up, that behold, there came water by the way of Edom, and the country was filled with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; 1 Kings 20:11"> And all the Moabites heard that the kings were come up to fight against them, and they were called together, all that were able to put on armour, and upward, and stood by the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they rose up early in the morning, and the sun rose upon the water, and the Moabites saw the water on the other side red as blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 15:9; Isaiah 10:14"> And they said, This is blood: the kings are entirely destroyed, and have smitten one another; and now, Moab, to the spoil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3–4; Joshua 8:20–22; Judges 20:40–46"> And when they came to the camp of Israel, the Israelites rose up and smote the Moabites, and they fled before them; and they entered in and smote Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 16:7; Jeremiah 48:36; Jeremiah 48:31"> And they beat down the cities, and on every good piece of land cast every man his stone and filled it, and they stopped every well of water, and felled every good tree, until they left &#91;only&#93; the stones at Kirhareseth; and the slingers went about it, and smote it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Amos 2:1"> And the king of Moab saw that the battle was too severe for him, and he took with him seven hundred men that drew swords, to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 6:7; Deuteronomy 12:31; Judges 11:31"> And he took his eldest son, that should have reigned in his stead, and offered him up for a burnt-offering upon the wall. And there was great wrath against Israel; and they departed from him, and returned to &#91;their own&#93; land.</span>
