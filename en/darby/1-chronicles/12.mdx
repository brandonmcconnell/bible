<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 17:10; 2 Samuel 23:20"> And of the Gadites, there separated themselves to David in the stronghold in the wilderness mighty men of valour, men fit for the service of war, armed with shield and spear; whose faces were &#91;like&#93; the faces of lions, and who were swift as the gazelles upon the mountains:</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; Joshua 4:18; Jeremiah 49:19"> These are they that went over Jordan in the first month, when it overflows all its banks, and they put to flight all &#91;them&#93; of the valleys, toward the east and toward the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And there came of the children of Benjamin and Judah to the stronghold to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Jude 1:9; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> And David went out to meet them, and answered and said to them, If ye come peaceably to me to help me, my heart shall be knit unto you; but if to betray me to mine enemies, seeing there is no wrong in my hands, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.17 Elohim">a</a></sup>God of our fathers see &#91;it&#93; and rebuke &#91;it&#93;.</span>

And David received them, and made them chiefs of bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:2–9"> And there fell some of Manasseh to David, when he came with the Philistines against Saul to battle: but they helped them not; for the lords of the Philistines upon deliberation sent him away, saying, He will fall to his master Saul at the peril of our heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Deuteronomy 1:15; 1 Samuel 29:11"> As he went away to Ziklag, there fell to him of Manasseh: Adnah, and Jozabad, and Jediael, and Michael, and Jozabad, and Elihu, and Zilthai, captains of the thousands that were of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1–17; 1 Chronicles 12:20; 1 Chronicles 5:24"> And they helped David in his expeditions; for they were all mighty men of valour, and were captains in the host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Job 17:9; Genesis 32:2"> For day by day there came &#91;men&#93; to David to help him, until it was a great camp, like the camp of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.22 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:3–4; 1 Chronicles 11:10; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And this is the number of the men equipped for military service, who came to David to Hebron, to transfer the kingdom of Saul to him, according to the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The children of Judah that bore shield and spear were six thousand eight hundred, equipped for military service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the children of Simeon, mighty men of valour for war, seven thousand one hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the children of Levi four thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:49–57"> And Jehoiada the prince of Aaron, and with him were three thousand seven hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 6:53"> and Zadok, a valiant young man, and his father's house two and twenty chief men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Chronicles 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> And of the children of Benjamin, the brethren of Saul, three thousand; but hitherto the greater part of them had adhered to the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> And of the children of Ephraim twenty thousand eight hundred, mighty men of valour, men of name in their fathers' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> And of the half tribe of Manasseh eighteen thousand, who were expressed by name, to come and make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> And of the children of Issachar, who had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do, the heads of them were two hundred; and all their brethren were at their bidding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; John 1:47"> Of Zebulun, such as went forth in the host, armed for war with all weapons of war, fifty thousand, keeping rank without double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And of Naphtali a thousand captains, and with them thirty-seven thousand with shield and spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And of the Danites armed for war twenty-eight thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:33; Joel 2:7"> And of Asher such as went forth in the host, to set themselves in battle array, forty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> And from the other side of the Jordan, of the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and of the half tribe of Manasseh, with all manner of weapons of war for battle, a hundred and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; 1 Kings 8:61; Psalms 101:2"> All of them men of war, keeping rank in battle array, came with a perfect heart to Hebron, to make David king over all Israel; and all the rest also of Israel were of one heart to make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 2 Samuel 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> And there they were with David three days, eating and drinking; for their brethren had prepared for them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 16:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> and those too that were near them, as far as Issachar and Zebulun and Naphtali, brought food on asses, and on camels, and on mules, and on oxen; provisions of meal, fig-cakes and raisin-cakes, and wine and oil, and oxen and sheep, abundantly; for there was joy in Israel.</span>
