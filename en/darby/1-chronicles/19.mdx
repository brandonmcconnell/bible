<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–2; 2 Samuel 10:1–19; 1 Samuel 12:12"> And it came to pass after this that Nahash king of the children of Ammon died, and his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3–6; 1 Samuel 30:26; Genesis 19:37–38"> And David said, I will shew kindness to Hanun the son of Nahash, because his father shewed kindness to me. And David sent messengers to comfort him concerning his father. And David's servants came into the land of the children of Ammon to Hanun, to comfort him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:23–24; 1 Corinthians 13:5–7; 1 Kings 12:8–11"> And the princes of the children of Ammon said to Hanun, Is it, in thine eyes, to honour thy father that David has sent comforters to thee? Is it not to search and overthrow, and to spy out the land that his servants are come to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:4–5; Isaiah 15:2; Mark 12:4"> And Hanun took David's servants, and had them shaved, and their raiment cut off in the midst, as far as the hip, and sent them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:24–26; Matthew 18:31; 1 Kings 16:34"> And &#91;certain&#93; went and told David concerning the men; and he sent to meet them, for the men were greatly ashamed. And the king said, Abide at Jericho until your beards be grown, and then return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:5; Genesis 34:30; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> And the children of Ammon saw that they had made themselves odious to David; and Hanun and the children of Ammon sent a thousand talents of silver to hire them chariots and horsemen out of Mesopotamia, and from the Syrians of Maacah, and from Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Joshua 13:9; Joshua 13:16"> And they hired thirty-two thousand chariots, and the king of Maacah and his people; and they came and encamped before Medeba. And the children of Ammon gathered together from their cities, and came to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Chronicles 11:10–47; 2 Samuel 23:8–39"> And David heard &#91;of it&#93;, and he sent Joab, and all the host, the mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 20:24; Isaiah 28:6"> And the children of Ammon came out, and put the battle in array at the entrance of the city; and the kings that had come were by themselves on the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:9–14; Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:22"> And Joab saw that the front of the battle was against him before and behind; and he chose out of all the choice men of Israel, and put them in array against the Syrians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:12; 1 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> and the rest of the people he gave into the hand of Abishai his brother, and they set themselves in array against the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:27–28; Nehemiah 4:20; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> And he said, If the Syrians be too strong for me, then thou shalt help me; and if the children of Ammon be too strong for thee, then I will help thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:14; Psalms 27:14; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Be strong, and let us shew ourselves valiant for our people, and for the cities of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God; and Jehovah will do what is good in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 20:19–21; 2 Chronicles 13:5–16; Jeremiah 46:15–16"> And Joab and the people that were with him drew near in front of the Syrians to the battle; and they fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Leviticus 26:7"> And when the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians fled, they likewise fled before Abishai his brother, and entered into the city. And Joab came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:16; Zechariah 14:1–3; Micah 4:11–12"> And when the Syrians saw that they were routed before Israel, they sent messengers, and drew forth the Syrians that were beyond the river; and Shophach the captain of the host of Hadarezer &#91;went&#93; before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6–7; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> And it was told David; and he gathered all Israel, and passed over the Jordan, and came upon them, and set &#91;the battle&#93; in array against them. And David put the battle in array against the Syrians, and they fought with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:18; Psalms 33:16; Psalms 18:32"> And the Syrians fled from before Israel; and David slew of the Syrians seven thousand &#91;in&#93; chariots, and forty thousand footmen, and slew Shophach the captain of the host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:19; Joshua 9:9–11; 1 Kings 20:1"> And the servants of Hadarezer saw that they were routed before Israel, and they made peace with David, and became his servants. And the Syrians would no more help the children of Ammon.</span>
