<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; Ezra 6:16–18; 2 Chronicles 5:6–7"> And they brought in the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and set it in the midst of the tent that David had spread for it; and they presented burnt-offerings and peace-offerings before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:10; Genesis 14:19; Leviticus 1:3"> And when David had ended offering up the burnt-offerings and the peace-offerings, he blessed the people in the name of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; Ezekiel 45:17; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8"> And he dealt to every one of Israel, both man and woman, to every one a loaf of bread and a measure &#91;of wine&#93; and a raisin-cake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:27–32; Psalms 72:18; Psalms 103:2"> And he appointed certain of the Levites to do the service before the ark of Jehovah, and to celebrate, and to thank and praise Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> Asaph the chief, and second to him Zechariah, Jeiel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Mattithiah, and Eliab, and Benaiah and Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, with instruments of lutes and with harps; and Asaph sounding with cymbals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:12; 2 Chronicles 29:26–28; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests with trumpets continually, before the ark of the covenant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="16.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1; Psalms 18:1; 2 Samuel 23:1–2"> Then on that day David delivered first &#91;this psalm&#93; to give thanks to Jehovah through Asaph and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 105:1–15; Isaiah 12:4; 2 Kings 19:19"> Give thanks unto Jehovah, call upon his name; Make known his acts among the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Psalms 96:1–3; Psalms 98:1–4"> Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him; Meditate upon all his wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremiah 29:13; Psalms 34:2"> Glory in his holy name: Let the heart of them rejoice that seek Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:8–9; Zephaniah 2:2–3; Psalms 68:35"> Seek Jehovah and his strength, Seek his face continually;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 103:2"> Remember his wondrous works which he hath done; His miracles, and the judgments of his mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:5–6; Psalms 135:4"> Ye seed of Israel his servant, Ye sons of Jacob, his chosen ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 48:10–11; Isaiah 26:9; Exodus 15:2"> He, Jehovah, is our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="16.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God; His judgments are in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Malachi 4:4; Deuteronomy 7:9"> Be ye ever mindful of his covenant, The word which he commanded to a thousand generations, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 17:2; Genesis 35:11"> Which he made with Abraham, And of his oath unto Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Genesis 35:11–12; Joshua 24:11–13"> And he confirmed it unto Jacob for a statute, Unto Israel for an everlasting covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Micah 2:5; Genesis 17:8"> Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, The lot of your inheritance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Acts 7:5"> When ye were a few men in number, Of small account, and strangers in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Genesis 20:1; Genesis 46:3"> And they went from nation to nation, And from one kingdom to another people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 20:3; Exodus 7:15–18"> He suffered no man to oppress them, And reproved kings for their sakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Psalms 105:15; Genesis 49:8–10"> &#91;Saying,&#93; Touch not mine anointed ones, And do my prophets no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 96:1–13; Psalms 71:15; Isaiah 51:6–8"> Sing unto Jehovah, all the earth; Publish his salvation from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–6; Daniel 4:1–3; Psalms 22:27"> Declare his glory among the nations, His wondrous works among all peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 76:7; Psalms 66:3–5"> For Jehovah is great, and exceedingly to be praised; And he is terrible above all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="16.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 102:25; Leviticus 19:4; Isaiah 42:5"> For all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="16.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>gods of the peoples are idols; But Jehovah made the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 96:6; Psalms 28:7–8"> Majesty and splendour are before him; Strength and gladness in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 100:1–2"> Give unto Jehovah, ye families of peoples, Give unto Jehovah glory and strength!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 96:9; Isaiah 6:3"> Give unto Jehovah the glory of his name! Bring an oblation, and come before him: Worship Jehovah in holy splendour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Psalms 148:5–6; Hebrews 1:3"> Tremble before him, all the earth: The world also is established, it shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 96:10; Revelation 19:6; Psalms 97:1"> Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; And let them say among the nations, Jehovah reigneth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 148:9–10; Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 98:7–8"> Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; Let the field exult, and all that is therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Psalms 96:12–13; Revelation 11:17–18"> Then shall the trees of the forest sing out at the presence of Jehovah, For he cometh to judge the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Psalms 107:1"> Give thanks unto Jehovah, for he is good; For his loving-kindness &#91;endureth&#93; for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 106:47–48; 1 Peter 2:9; Psalms 14:7"> And say, Save us, O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="16.35 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God of our salvation, And gather us, and deliver us from the nations, To give thanks unto thy holy name, To triumph in thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 8:15; Psalms 72:18–19; 1 Kings 8:56"> Blessed be Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="16.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of Israel, from eternity and to eternity! And all the people said, Amen! and praised Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; Ezra 3:4; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> And he left there, before the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, Asaph and his brethren, to do the service before the ark continually, as every day's duty required;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 13:14; 1 Chronicles 25:3"> and Obed-Edom, and their brethren, sixty-eight; Obed-Edom also, the son of Jeduthun, and Hosah as doorkeepers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; 1 Chronicles 15:11; 2 Chronicles 1:3–4"> And Zadok the priest, and his brethren the priests, before the tabernacle of Jehovah in the high place that was at Gibeon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Exodus 29:38–42; 2 Chronicles 31:3"> to offer up burnt-offerings to Jehovah on the altar of burnt-offering continually, morning and evening, and according to all that is written in the law of Jehovah, which he commanded Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 25:1–6; 2 Chronicles 20:21"> and with them Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest that were chosen, who were expressed by name, to give thanks to Jehovah, because his loving-kindness &#91;endureth&#93; for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–6; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; 2 Chronicles 29:25–28"> and with them, &#91;with&#93; Heman and Jeduthun, trumpets and cymbals for those that should sound aloud; and the musical instruments of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="16.42 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God. And the sons of Jeduthun were at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:19–20; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 101:2"> And all the people departed every one to his house; and David returned to bless his household.</span>
