<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:20; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 2 Chronicles 29:20"> And David consulted with the captains of thousands and hundreds, with every prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 4:4–20; 1 Samuel 31:1; 1 Chronicles 10:7"> And David said to all the congregation of Israel, If it seem good to you, and it be of Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, let us send abroad to our brethren everywhere, that are left in all the lands of Israel, and with them to the priests and Levites in their cities and suburbs, that they may gather themselves to us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1–2; 1 Samuel 23:2; 1 Samuel 14:18"> and let us bring again the ark of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God to us; for we inquired not of it in the days of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 8:5; 2 Samuel 3:36; 1 Samuel 18:20"> And all the congregation said that they should do so; for the thing was right in the eyes of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 1 Kings 8:65; 2 Samuel 6:1"> And David assembled all Israel from the Shihor of Egypt unto the entrance of Hamath, to bring the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God from Kirjath-jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Joshua 15:9; 2 Kings 19:15"> And David went up, and all Israel, to Baalah, to Kirjath-jearim, which belonged to Judah, to bring up from thence the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, of Jehovah, who sitteth between the cherubim, whose name is placed &#91;there&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 1 Samuel 6:7; 1 Chronicles 15:2"> And they carried the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God on a new cart out of the house of Abinadab; and Uzza and Ahio drove the cart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–5; Psalms 68:25–27; Amos 6:5"> And David and all Israel played before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God with all their might, and with singing, and with harps, and with lutes, and with tambours, and with cymbals, and with trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6"> And when they came to the threshing-floor of Chidon, Uzza put forth his hand to hold the ark; for the oxen had stumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Numbers 4:15"> And the anger of Jehovah was kindled against Uzza, and he smote him, because he had put forth his hand to the ark; and there he died before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:7; 2 Samuel 6:9; Genesis 32:32"> And David was indignant; for Jehovah had made a breach upon Uzza; and he called that place Perez-Uzza to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Kings 8:27; Job 25:5–6"> And David was afraid of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God that day, saying, How shall I bring the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="13.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 2 Samuel 4:3"> And David brought not the ark home unto himself into the city of David, but he carried it aside into the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–5; Proverbs 10:22; Proverbs 3:9–10"> And the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="13.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God remained with the family of Obed-Edom in his house three months. And Jehovah blessed the house of Obed-Edom, and all that he had.</span>
