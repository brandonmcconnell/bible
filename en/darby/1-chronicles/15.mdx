<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–5; Psalms 132:5"> And he made him houses in the city of David, and prepared a place for the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and spread a tent for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then David said, None ought to carry the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God but the Levites, for them has Jehovah chosen to carry the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and to serve him for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:5; 1 Chronicles 15:1"> And David assembled all Israel to Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of Jehovah to its place that he had prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; 1 Chronicles 12:26–28; 1 Chronicles 6:16–30"> And David gathered the sons of Aaron and the Levites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–24"> of the sons of Kohath, Uriel the chief, and his brethren a hundred and twenty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:29–30"> of the sons of Merari, Asaiah the chief, and his brethren two hundred and twenty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:11; 1 Chronicles 23:8"> of the sons of Gershom, Joel the chief, and his brethren a hundred and thirty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> of the sons of Elizaphan, Shemaiah the chief, and his brethren two hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:30–31"> of the sons of Hebron, Eliel the chief, and his brethren eighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> of the sons of Uzziel, Amminadab the chief, and his brethren a hundred and twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> And David called for Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and for the Levites, for Uriel, Asaiah, and Joel, Shemaiah, and Eliel, and Amminadab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> and he said to them, Ye are the chief fathers of the Levites; hallow yourselves, ye and your brethren, that ye may bring up the ark of Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God of Israel to &#91;the place that&#93; I have prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> For because ye did &#91;it&#93; not at the first, Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God made a breach upon us, for that we sought him not after the due order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> So the priests and the Levites hallowed themselves to bring up the ark of Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And the children of the Levites bore the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God upon their shoulders with the staves upon them, as Moses had commanded according to the word of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> And David spoke to the chief of the Levites to appoint their brethren, the singers, with instruments of music, lutes, and harps, and cymbals, that they should sound aloud, lifting up the voice with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> And the Levites appointed Heman the son of Joel; and of his brethren, Asaph the son of Berechiah; and of the sons of Merari their brethren, Ethan the son of Kushaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> and with them their brethren of the second &#91;rank&#93;, Zechariah, Ben, and Jaaziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Unni, Eliab, and Benaiah, and Maaseiah, and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah, and Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, the doorkeepers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> and the singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, to sound aloud with cymbals of brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:1–11"> and Zechariah, and Aziel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Unni, and Eliab, and Maaseiah, and Benaiah, with lutes on Alamoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; Psalms 81:1–2"> and Mattithiah, and Elipheleh, and Mikneiah, and Obed-Edom and Jeiel, and Azaziah, with harps on the Sheminith to lead &#91;the singing&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> And Chenaniah, chief of the Levites for the music, gave instruction in music, for he was skilful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> And Berechiah and Elkanah were doorkeepers for the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> And Shebaniah, and Jehoshaphat, and Nethaneel, and Amasai, and Zechariah, and Benaiah, and Eliezer, the priests, blew with the trumpets before the ark of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God; and Obed-Edom and Jehijah were doorkeepers for the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> And David, and the elders of Israel, and the captains over thousands, went to bring up the ark of the covenant of Jehovah out of the house of Obed-Edom with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalms 66:13–15"> And it came to pass, when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="15.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God helped the Levites that bore the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, that they sacrificed seven bullocks and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> And David was clothed with a robe of byssus, and all the Levites that bore the ark, and the singers, and Chenaniah chief of the music of the singers; and David had upon him a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> And all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of Jehovah with shouting, and with sound of the trumpet, and with clarions, and with cymbals, playing aloud with lutes and harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> And it came to pass as the ark of the covenant of Jehovah came to the city of David, that Michal the daughter of Saul looked through a window, and saw king David dancing and playing; and she despised him in her heart.</span>
