<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:7–9; 1 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Chronicles 17:5"> And it came to pass as David dwelt in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, Behold, I dwell in a house of cedars, and the ark of the covenant of Jehovah under curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; 1 Corinthians 13:9; Zechariah 8:23"> And Nathan said to David, Do all that is in thy heart; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="17.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Numbers 12:6; Isaiah 30:21"> And it came to pass that night that the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="17.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God came to Nathan saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2–3; Isaiah 55:8–9; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> Go and say to David my servant, Thus saith Jehovah: Thou shalt not build me a house to dwell in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:6; Exodus 40:2–3; 1 Kings 8:16"> for I have not dwelt in a house since the day that I brought up Israel to this day; but I have been from tent to tent, and from &#91;one&#93; tabernacle &#91;to another&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:7; Numbers 10:33–36; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> In all my going about with all Israel, did I speak a word to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people, saying, Why build ye me not a house of cedars?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:21; Exodus 3:1–10; 1 Samuel 17:15"> And now, thus shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith Jehovah of hosts: I took thee from the pasture-grounds, from following the sheep, to be prince over my people Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; 1 Chronicles 17:2; Psalms 113:7–8"> and I have been with thee whithersoever thou wentest, and have cut off all thine enemies from before thee, and have made thee a name, like unto the name of the great men that are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Ezekiel 28:4; Ephesians 5:6"> And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in a place of their own, and be disturbed no more; neither shall the sons of wickedness waste them any more, as formerly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Judges 2:14–18; Exodus 1:21"> and since the days that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. And I will subdue all thine enemies; and I tell thee that Jehovah will build thee a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 1 Kings 1:21; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> And it shall come to pass, when thy days are fulfilled that thou must go &#91;to be&#93; with thy fathers, that I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall be of thy sons; and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 5:5; Daniel 2:44; Isaiah 9:7"> It is he who shall build me a house, and I will establish his throne for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Isaiah 55:3; 1 Chronicles 17:12"> I will be his father, and he shall be my son; and I will not take away my mercy from him, as I took it from him that was before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 89:36; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 72:17"> and I will settle him in my house and in my kingdom for ever; and his throne shall be established for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 7:17"> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; Genesis 32:10; Ephesians 3:8"> And king David went in and sat before Jehovah, and said, Who am I, Jehovah Elohim, and what is my house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:11–15; Philippians 2:8–11; Isaiah 49:6"> And this hath been a small thing in thy sight, O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="17.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God; and thou hast spoken of thy servant's house for a great while to come, and hast regarded me according to the rank of a man of high degree, Jehovah Elohim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 2:30; 2 Samuel 7:20–24"> What can David &#91;say&#93; more to thee for the glory of thy servant? thou indeed knowest thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; 2 Samuel 7:21; Daniel 9:17"> Jehovah, for thy servant's sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou done all this greatness, to make known all these great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Jeremiah 10:6–7; Ephesians 3:20"> Jehovah, there is none like thee, neither is there any<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="17.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God beside thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9–10; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 48:9"> And who is like thy people Israel, the one nation in the earth that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="17.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God went to redeem to be a people to himself, to make thee a name of greatness and terribleness, by driving out nations from before thy people, which thou hast redeemed out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9; Romans 11:1–12"> And thy people Israel hast thou made thine own people for ever; and thou, Jehovah, art become their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="17.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Jeremiah 11:5; Luke 1:38"> And now, Jehovah, let the word that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant and concerning his house be established for ever, and do as thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Jeremiah 31:1; Revelation 21:3"> Let it even be established, and let thy name be magnified for ever, saying, Jehovah of hosts, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="17.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of Israel, is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="17.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God to Israel; and let the house of David thy servant be established before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; Ezekiel 36:37; Psalms 10:17"> For thou, my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="17.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, hast revealed to thy servant that thou wilt build him a house; therefore hath thy servant found &#91;in his heart&#93; to pray before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Hebrews 6:18; Titus 1:2"> And now, Jehovah, thou art that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="17.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, and hast promised this goodness unto thy servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Romans 11:29; Psalms 72:17"> and now, let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may be before thee for ever; for thou, Jehovah, hast blessed &#91;it&#93;, and it shall be blessed for ever.</span>
