But David abode at Jerusalem. And Joab smote Rabbah, and overthrew it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30–31; 2 Samuel 8:11–12; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> And David took the crown of their king from off his head, and found it to weigh a talent of gold, and there were precious stones in it; and it was &#91;set&#93; upon David's head: and he brought forth the spoil of the city in great abundance.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> And it came to pass after this, that there arose war at Gezer with the Philistines; then Sibbechai the Hushathite smote Sippai, one of the children of Rapha; and they were subdued.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> And there was again a battle, at Gath; and there was a man &#91;there&#93; of great stature, whose fingers &#91;and toes&#93; were four and twenty, six &#91;on each hand&#93;, and six &#91;on each foot&#93;; and he also was born to Rapha.</span>
