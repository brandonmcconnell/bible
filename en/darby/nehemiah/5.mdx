<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Exodus 22:25–27; 1 Corinthians 6:6–8"> And there was a great cry of the people and of their wives against their brethren the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Psalms 128:2–4; Psalms 127:3–5"> And there were that said, We, our sons and our daughters, are many, and we must procure corn that we may eat and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 25:35–39; Malachi 3:8–11; Genesis 47:15–25"> And there were that said, We have had to pledge our fields, and our vineyards, and our houses, that we might procure corn in the dearth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 4:13; Ezra 4:20; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> And there were that said, We have borrowed money for the king's tribute upon our fields and vineyards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Leviticus 25:39–43; Isaiah 58:7"> yet now our flesh is as the flesh of our brethren, our children as their children; and behold, we must bring into bondage our sons and our daughters to be servants, and some of our daughters are brought into bondage &#91;already&#93;; neither is it in the power of our hand &#91;to redeem them&#93;, for other men have our fields and our vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 11:8; Nehemiah 13:25; Ephesians 4:26"> And I was very angry when I heard their cry and these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Leviticus 25:36; Exodus 22:25"> And I consulted with myself; and I remonstrated with the nobles and the rulers, and said to them, Ye exact usury, every one of his brother! And I set a great assembly against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:47–49; 1 Corinthians 8:11; Matthew 25:29"> And I said to them, We, according to our ability, have redeemed our brethren the Jews, who were sold to the nations; and will ye even sell your brethren? or shall they be sold unto us? And they were silent and found no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 25:36; 2 Samuel 12:14; Romans 2:24"> And I said, The thing that ye do is not good. Ought ye not to walk in the fear of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, so as not to be the reproach of the nations our enemies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:1; Exodus 22:25–27; Ezekiel 18:13"> I also, my brethren and my servants, we might exact usury of them, money and corn. I pray you, let us leave off this usury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Leviticus 6:4–5; Luke 3:8"> Restore, I pray you, to them this very day their fields, their vineyards, their olive-gardens, and their houses, also the hundredth &#91;part&#93; of the money, and of the corn, the wine and the oil, that ye have exacted of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 10:5; Jeremiah 34:8–10; Matthew 19:21–22"> And they said, We will restore &#91;them&#93;, and will require nothing of them; so will we do, as thou hast said. And I called the priests, and took an oath of them, that they should do according to this promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:6; 1 Chronicles 16:36; Matthew 10:14"> Also I shook my lap, and said, So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God shake out every man from his house and from his earnings, that performeth not this promise: even thus be he shaken out and emptied! And all the congregation said, Amen! And they praised Jehovah. And the people did according to this promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:4–15"> Moreover from the time that I was appointed to be their governor in the land of Judah, from the twentieth year even to the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes the king, twelve years, I and my brethren have not eaten the bread of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:9; Isaiah 50:10; Proverbs 16:6"> But the former governors that were before me had been chargeable to the people, and had taken of them bread and wine, besides forty shekels of silver: even their servants bore rule over the people. But I did not so, because of the fear of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Yea, also I applied myself to this work of the wall, and we bought no fields; and all my servants were gathered thither for the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 18:19; 1 Peter 4:9–10; 2 Samuel 9:7"> And there were at my table a hundred and fifty of the Jews and the rulers, besides those that came to us from among the nations that were about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalms 37:21; Psalms 37:26"> And that which was prepared daily was one ox &#91;and&#93; six choice sheep; also fowls were prepared for me, and once in ten days all sorts of wine in abundance. Yet for all this I demanded not the bread of the governor; for the service was heavy upon this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:31; Psalms 106:4; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember for me, my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, for good, all that I have done for this people.</span>
