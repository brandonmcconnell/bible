<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; Zechariah 7:1; Esther 1:2"> The words of Nehemiah the son of Hachaliah. And it came to pass in the month Chislev, in the twentieth year, as I was in Shushan the fortress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; Ezekiel 6:9; Jeremiah 44:14"> that Hanani, one of my brethren, came, he and &#91;certain&#93; men of Judah. And I asked them concerning the Jews that had escaped, who were left of the captivity, and concerning Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:17; 2 Kings 25:10; Nehemiah 7:6"> And they said to me, Those who remain, that are left of the captivity there in the province, are in great affliction and reproach; and the wall of Jerusalem is in ruins, and its gates are burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Psalms 137:1; Ezra 10:1"> And it came to pass, when I heard these words, that I sat and wept, and mourned for days, and fasted, and prayed before the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; Daniel 9:4–19; Deuteronomy 7:21"> and said, I beseech thee, Jehovah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God of the heavens, the great and terrible<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.5 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God, that keepeth covenant and mercy for them that love him and keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:20; Daniel 9:4; Psalms 34:15"> Let thine ear now be attentive, and thine eyes open, to hear the prayer of thy servant, which I pray before thee at this time, day and night, for the children of Israel thy servants, confessing the sins of the children of Israel, which we have sinned against thee: both I and my father's house have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 9:5–6; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:13"> We have acted very perversely against thee, and have not kept the commandments, nor the statutes, nor the ordinances that thou commandedst thy servant Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Deuteronomy 4:25–27; Leviticus 26:33–46"> Remember, I beseech thee, the word that thou commandedst thy servant Moses, saying, If ye act unfaithfully, I will scatter you among the peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11–14; Deuteronomy 30:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> but if ye return to me, and keep my commandments and do them, though there were of you cast out unto the uttermost part of the heavens, yet will I gather them from thence, and will bring them unto the place that I have chosen to set my name there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:29; Daniel 9:15–27; Exodus 32:11"> And they are thy servants and thy people, whom thou hast redeemed by thy great power and by thy strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Proverbs 21:1"> O Lord, I beseech thee, let now thine ear be attentive to the prayer of thy servant, and to the prayer of thy servants who delight to fear thy name; and prosper, I pray thee, thy servant this day, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man. Now I was the king's cupbearer.</span>
