<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; Ruth 3:12; Ruth 3:2"> And Naomi had a relation of her husband's, a mighty man of wealth, of the family of Elimelech, and his name was Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And Ruth the Moabitess said to Naomi, Let me, I pray, go to the field and glean among the ears of corn after &#91;him&#93; in whose sight I shall find favour. And she said to her, Go, my daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:12; Matthew 10:29; Luke 10:31"> And she went; and she came and gleaned in the fields after the reapers; and she chanced to light on an allotment of Boaz, who was of the family of Elimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 129:7–8; Judges 6:12; Luke 1:28"> And behold, Boaz came from Bethlehem; and he said to the reapers, Jehovah be with you! And they said to him, Jehovah bless thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> And Boaz said to his servant that was set over the reapers, Whose maiden is this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Matthew 24:45; Genesis 24:2"> And the servant that was set over the reapers answered and said, It is the Moabitish maiden who came back with Naomi out of the fields of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 18:23; Proverbs 15:33"> and she said, I pray you, let me glean and gather among the sheaves after the reapers. And she came, and has continued from the morning until now: her sitting in the house has been little as yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; 1 Samuel 3:6"> And Boaz said to Ruth, Hearest thou not, my daughter? Go not to glean in another field, neither go from here, but keep here with my maidens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Let thine eyes be on the field which is being reaped, and go thou after them; have I not charged the young men not to touch thee? And when thou art athirst, go to the vessels and drink of what the young men draw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; Ruth 2:13; Luke 1:43"> Then she fell on her face, and bowed herself to the ground, and said to him, Why have I found favour in thine eyes, that thou shouldest regard me, seeing I am a foreigner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Luke 18:29–30; Luke 5:11"> And Boaz answered and said to her, It has fully been shewn me, all that thou hast done to thy mother-in-law since the death of thy husband; and how thou hast left thy father and thy mother, and the land of thy nativity, and art come to a people that thou hast not known heretofore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 36:7; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 63:7"> Jehovah recompense thy work, and let thy reward be full from Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel, under whose wings thou art come to take refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:18; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 33:8"> And she said, Let me find favour in thine eyes, my lord; for that thou hast comforted me, and for that thou hast spoken kindly to thy handmaid, though I am not like one of thy handmaidens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 2:18; 2 Kings 4:43–44; Matthew 14:20"> And Boaz said to her at mealtime, Come hither and eat of the bread, and dip thy morsel in the vinegar. And she sat beside the reapers; and he reached her parched corn, and she ate and was sufficed, and reserved &#91;some&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 1:5"> And when she rose up to glean, Boaz commanded his young men, saying, Let her glean even among the sheaves, and ye shall not reproach her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 1 John 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And ye shall also sometimes draw out for her &#91;some ears&#93; out of the handfuls, and leave them that she may glean, and rebuke her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–12; Proverbs 31:27; Exodus 16:36"> And she gleaned in the field until even, and beat out what she had gleaned; and it was about an ephah of barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:14; John 6:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:4"> And she took &#91;it&#93; up, and came into the city, and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned; and she brought forth and gave to her that which she had reserved after she was sufficed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Psalms 41:1; 1 Kings 7:21"> And her mother-in-law said to her, Where hast thou gleaned to-day? and where hast thou wrought? Blessed be he that did regard thee! And she told her mother-in-law with whom she had wrought, and said, The man's name with whom I wrought to-day is Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:5; Ruth 3:9–10; Leviticus 25:25"> And Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, Blessed be he of Jehovah, who has not left off his kindness to the living and to the dead! And Naomi said to her, The man is near of kin to us, one of those who have the right of our redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Ruth 2:7–8; Ruth 2:22"> And Ruth the Moabitess said, He said to me also, Thou shalt keep with my young men until they have ended all my harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; Proverbs 27:10"> And Naomi said to Ruth her daughter-in-law, It is good, my daughter, that thou go out with his maidens, that they meet thee not in any other field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8; Deuteronomy 16:9; Ephesians 6:1–3"> So she kept with the maidens of Boaz to glean, until the end of the barley-harvest and of the wheat-harvest. And she dwelt with her mother-in-law.</span>
