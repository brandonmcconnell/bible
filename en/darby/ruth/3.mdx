<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 2:20–23; Ruth 2:8"> And now, is not Boaz of our kindred, with whose maidens thou wast? Behold, he is winnowing barley in the threshing-floor to-night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Timothy 2:9–10; Esther 5:1"> Wash thyself therefore, and anoint thyself, and put thy raiment upon thee, and go down to the floor; make not thyself known to the man, until he shall have done eating and drinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And it shall be, when he lies down, that thou shalt mark the place where he shall have lain down, and thou shalt go in, and uncover his feet, and lay thyself down; and he will shew thee what thou shalt do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:20; Ephesians 6:1"> And she said to her, All that thou sayest will I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 1:8; John 2:5; Exodus 20:12"> And she went down to the floor, and did according to all that her mother-in-law had bidden her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Esther 1:10; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And Boaz ate and drank, and his heart was merry, and he went to lie down at the end of the heap of corn. Then she went softly, and uncovered his feet, and laid herself down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And it came to pass at midnight, that the man was startled, and turned himself; and behold, a woman lay at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Ruth 2:20; Ruth 3:12"> And he said, Who art thou? And she answered, I am Ruth, thy handmaid: spread thy skirt over thy handmaid; for thou hast the right of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5; Ruth 1:8"> And he said, Blessed be thou of Jehovah, my daughter! Thou hast shewn more kindness at the end than at the first, inasmuch as thou followedst not young men, whether poor or rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:29–31; Proverbs 31:10; Proverbs 12:4"> And now, my daughter, fear not: all that thou sayest will I do to thee; for all the gate of my people knows that thou art a woman of worth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Matthew 7:12"> And now, truly I am one that has the right of redemption, yet there is one that has the right of redemption who is nearer than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 4:5; Jeremiah 4:2; Ruth 2:20"> Stay over to-night, and it shall be in the morning, if he will redeem thee, well — let him redeem; but if he like not to redeem thee, then will I redeem thee, &#91;as&#93; Jehovah liveth. Lie down until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; Romans 14:16; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> And she lay at his feet until the morning; and she rose up before one could know another. And he said, Let it not be known that a woman came into the threshing-floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; Galatians 6:10"> And he said, Bring the cloak that thou hast upon thee, and hold it. And she held it, and he measured six &#91;measures&#93; of barley, and laid &#91;it&#93; on her; and he went into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she came to her mother-in-law; and she said, Who art thou, my daughter? And she told her all that the man had done to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she said, These six &#91;measures&#93; of barley gave he me; for he said to me, Go not empty to thy mother-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 37:3–5; Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 30:7"> Then she said, Be still, my daughter, until thou know how the matter will fall; for the man will not rest until he have completed the matter this day.</span>
