<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 26:1; Ezekiel 14:13"> And it came to pass in the days when the judges ruled, that there was a famine in the land. And a certain man went from Bethlehem-Judah, to sojourn in the country of Moab, he, and his wife, and his two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:19; Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 17:21"> And the name of the man was Elimelech, and the name of his wife Naomi, and the name of his two sons Mahlon and Chilion, Ephrathites of Bethlehem-Judah. And they came into the country of Moab, and continued there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; Psalms 34:19; Hebrews 12:6"> And Elimelech Naomi's husband died; and she was left, and her two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:5; 1 Kings 11:1–2; Deuteronomy 23:3"> And they took them Moabitish wives; the name of the one was Orpah, and the name of the second Ruth: and they abode there about ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Psalms 89:30–32; Isaiah 49:21"> And Mahlon and Chilion died also, both of them; and the woman was left of her two children and of her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 132:15; Exodus 3:16; Psalms 111:5"> And she arose, she and her daughters-in-law, and returned from the fields of Moab; for she had heard in the fields of Moab how that Jehovah had visited his people to give them bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 8:3; Ruth 1:14; Exodus 18:27"> Wherefore she went forth out of the place where she had been, and her two daughters-in-law with her; and they went on the way to return to the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Ruth 1:5; Colossians 3:18"> And Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, Go, return each to her mother's house. Jehovah deal kindly with you, as ye have dealt with the dead and with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 3:1; Acts 20:37; Genesis 29:11"> Jehovah grant you that ye may find rest, each in the house of her husband. And she kissed them; and they lifted up their voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 16:3; Psalms 119:63"> And they said to her, We will certainly return with thee to thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Genesis 38:11"> And Naomi said, Return, my daughters: why will ye go with me? Are there yet sons in my womb, that they could be your husbands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; 1 Timothy 5:9"> Return, my daughters, go; for I am too old to have a husband. If I should say, I have hope, should I even have a husband to-night, and should I also bear sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 19:21; Judges 2:15; Psalms 32:4"> would ye wait on that account till they were grown? Would ye stay on that account from having husbands? No, my daughters, for I am in much more bitterness than you; for the hand of Jehovah is gone out against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Matthew 16:24; Matthew 10:37"> And they lifted up their voice and wept again. And Orpah kissed her mother-in-law, but Ruth clave to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 11:24; Joshua 24:15; Zephaniah 1:6"> And she said, Behold, thy sister-in-law is gone back to her people and to her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>gods: return after thy sister-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:21; Ruth 2:11–12; 2 Kings 2:2–6"> And Ruth said, Do not intreat me to leave thee, to return from &#91;following&#93; after thee; for whither thou goest I will go, and where thou lodgest I will lodge: thy people shall be my people, and thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:17; 1 Kings 2:23; 2 Kings 6:31"> where thou diest will I die, and there will I be buried. Jehovah do so to me, and more also, if aught but death part me and thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:14; Ephesians 6:10; Acts 2:42"> And when she saw that she was stedfastly minded to go with her, she left off speaking to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:10; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:15"> And they two went until they came to Bethlehem. And it came to pass, when they came to Bethlehem, that all the city was moved about them, and the &#91;women&#93; said, Is this Naomi?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 6:4; Hebrews 12:11; Psalms 73:14"> And she said to them, Call me not Naomi — call me Mara; for the Almighty has dealt very bitterly with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Job 1:21; Job 16:8"> I went out full, and Jehovah has brought me home again empty. Why do ye call me Naomi, seeing Jehovah has brought me low, and the Almighty has afflicted me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:23; 2 Samuel 21:9; Exodus 9:31–32"> So Naomi returned, and Ruth the Moabitess, her daughter-in-law, with her, who returned out of the fields of Moab; and they came to Bethlehem in the beginning of the barley-harvest.</span>
