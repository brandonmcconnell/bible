<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> And it came to pass on one of the days, as he was teaching the people in the temple, and announcing the glad tidings, the chief priests and the scribes with the elders came up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> and spoke to him saying, Tell us by what authority thou doest these things, or who is it who has given thee this authority?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> And he answering said to them, I also will ask you &#91;one&#93; thing, and tell me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> The baptism of John, was it of heaven or of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> And they reasoned among themselves, saying, If we should say, Of heaven, he will say, Why have ye not believed him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> but if we should say, Of men, the whole people will stone us, for they are persuaded that John was a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> And they answered, they did not know whence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> And Jesus said to them, Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> And he began to speak to the people this parable: A man planted a vineyard and let it out to husbandmen, and left the country for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> And in the season he sent to the husbandmen a bondman, that they might give to him of the fruit of the vineyard; but the husbandmen, having beaten him, sent &#91;him&#93; away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> And again he sent another bondman; but they, having beaten him also, and cast insult upon him, sent &#91;him&#93; away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And again he sent a third; and they, having wounded him also, cast &#91;him&#93; out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> And the lord of the vineyard said, What shall I do? I will send my beloved son: perhaps when they see him they will respect &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> But when the husbandmen saw him, they reasoned among themselves, saying, This is the heir; &#91;come,&#93; let us kill him, that the inheritance may become ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> And having cast him forth out of the vineyard, they killed &#91;him&#93;. What therefore shall the lord of the vineyard do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come and destroy those husbandmen, and will give the vineyard to others. And when they heard it they said, May it never be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> But he looking at them said, What then is this that is written, The stone which they that builded rejected, this has become the corner-stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> Every one falling on this stone shall be broken, but on whomsoever it shall fall, it shall grind him to powder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> And the chief priests and the scribes sought the same hour to lay hands on him, and they feared the people; for they knew that he had spoken this parable of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> And having watched &#91;him&#93;, they sent out suborned persons, pretending to be just men, that they might take hold of him in &#91;his&#93; language, so that they might deliver him up to the power and authority of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> And they asked him saying, Teacher, we know that thou sayest and teachest rightly, and acceptest no &#91;man's&#93; person, but teachest with truth the way of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20.21 Elohim">a</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it lawful for us to give tribute to Caesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But perceiving their deceit he said to them, Why do ye tempt me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> Shew me a denarius. Whose image and superscription has it? And answering they said, Caesar's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> And he said to them, Pay therefore what is Caesar's to Caesar, and what is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20.25 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="20.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> And they were not able to take hold of him in &#91;his&#93; expressions before the people, and, wondering at his answer, they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> And some of the Sadducees, who deny that there is any resurrection, coming up &#91;to him&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> demanded of him saying, Teacher, Moses wrote to us, If any one's brother, who has a wife, die, and he die childless, his brother shall take the wife and raise up seed to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> There were then seven brethren: and the first, having taken a wife, died childless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the second &#91;took the woman, and he died childless&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and the third took her: and in like manner also the seven left no children and died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> and last of all the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> In the resurrection therefore of which of them does she become wife, for the seven had her as wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> And Jesus said to them, The sons of this world marry and are given in marriage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> but they who are counted worthy to have part in that world, and the resurrection from among &#91;the&#93; dead, neither marry nor are given in marriage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> for neither can they die any more, for they are equal to angels, and are sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="20.36 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, being sons of the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> But that the dead rise, even Moses shewed in &#91;the section of&#93; the bush, when he called &#91;the&#93; Lord the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="20.37 Elohim">e</a></sup>God of Abraham and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="20.37 Elohim">f</a></sup>God of Isaac and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="20.37 Elohim">g</a></sup>God of Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> but he is not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="20.38 Elohim">h</a></sup>God of &#91;the&#93; dead but of &#91;the&#93; living; for all live for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> And some of the scribes answering said, Teacher, thou hast well spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> For they did not dare any more to ask him anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> And he said to them, How do they say that the Christ is David's son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> and David himself says in the book of Psalms, The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> until I put thine enemies &#91;as&#93; footstool of thy feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> David therefore calls him Lord, and how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> And, as all the people were listening, he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> Beware of the scribes, who like to walk about in long robes, and who love salutations in the market-places, and first seats in the synagogues, and first places at suppers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> who devour the houses of widows, and as a pretext make long prayers. These shall receive a severer judgment.</span>
