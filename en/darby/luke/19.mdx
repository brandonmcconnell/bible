<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> And he entered and passed through Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> And behold, &#91;there was&#93; a man by name called Zacchaeus, and he was chief tax-gatherer, and he was rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> And he sought to see Jesus who he was: and he could not for the crowd, because he was little in stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> And running on before, he got up into a sycamore that he might see him, for he was going to pass that &#91;way&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> And when he came up to the place, Jesus looked up and saw him, and said to him, Zacchaeus, make haste and come down, for to-day I must remain in thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> And he made haste and came down, and received him with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> And all murmured when they saw &#91;it&#93;, saying, He has turned in to lodge with a sinful man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> But Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor, and if I have taken anything from any man by false accusation, I return &#91;him&#93; fourfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> And Jesus said to him, To-day salvation is come to this house, inasmuch as he also is a son of Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> for the Son of man has come to seek and to save that which is lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> But as they were listening to these things, he added and spake a parable, because he was near to Jerusalem and they thought that the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.11 Elohim">a</a></sup>God was about to be immediately manifested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> He said therefore, A certain high-born man went to a distant country to receive for himself a kingdom and return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> And having called his own ten bondmen, he gave to them ten minas, and said to them, Trade while I am coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his citizens hated him, and sent an embassy after him, saying, We will not that this &#91;man&#93; should reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> And it came to pass on his arrival back again, having received the kingdom, that he desired these bondmen to whom he gave the money to be called to him, in order that he might know what every one had gained by trading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> And the first came up, saying, &#91;My&#93; Lord, thy mina has produced ten minas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> And he said to him, Well &#91;done&#93;, thou good bondman; because thou hast been faithful in that which is least, be thou in authority over ten cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> And the second came, saying, &#91;My&#93; Lord, thy mina has made five minas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> And he said also to this one, And thou, be over five cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> And another came, saying, &#91;My&#93; Lord, lo, &#91;there is&#93; thy mina, which I have kept laid up in a towel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> For I feared thee because thou art a harsh man: thou takest up what thou hast not laid down, and thou reapest what thou hast not sowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> He says to him, Out of thy mouth will I judge thee, wicked bondman: thou knewest that I am a harsh man, taking up what I have not laid down and reaping what I have not sowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> And why didst thou not give my money to &#91;the&#93; bank; and I should have received it, at my coming, with interest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> And he said to those that stood by, Take from him the mina and give &#91;it&#93; to him who has the ten minas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> And they said to him, Lord, he has ten minas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> For I say unto you, that to every one that has shall be given; but from him that has not, that even which he has shall be taken from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> Moreover those mine enemies, who would not &#91;have&#93; me to reign over them, bring them here and slay &#91;them&#93; before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> And having said these things, he went on before, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> And it came to pass as he drew near to Bethphage and Bethany at the mountain called &#91;the mount&#93; of Olives, he sent two of his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> saying, Go into the village over against &#91;you&#93;, in which ye will find, on entering it, a colt tied up, on which no &#91;child&#93; of man ever sat at any time: loose it and lead it &#91;here&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> And if any one ask you, Why do ye loose &#91;it&#93;? thus shall ye say to him, Because the Lord has need of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> And they that were sent, having gone their way, found as he had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And as they were loosing the colt, its masters said to them, Why loose ye the colt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And they said, Because the Lord has need of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> And they led it to Jesus; and having cast their own garments on the colt, they put Jesus on &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> And as he went, they strewed their clothes in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> And as he drew near, already at the descent of the mount of Olives, all the multitude of the disciples began, rejoicing, to praise<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.37 Elohim">b</a></sup>God with a loud voice for all the works of power which they had seen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> saying, Blessed the King that comes in the name of &#91;the&#93; Lord: peace in heaven, and glory in the highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> And some of the Pharisees from the crowd said to him, Teacher, rebuke thy disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> And he answering said to them, I say unto you, If these shall be silent, the stones will cry out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> And as he drew near, seeing the city, he wept over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> saying, If thou hadst known, even thou, even at least in this thy day, the things that are for thy peace: but now they are hid from thine eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> for days shall come upon thee, that thine enemies shall make a palisaded mound about thee, and shall close thee around, and keep thee in on every side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> and shall lay thee even with the ground, and thy children in thee; and shall not leave in thee a stone upon a stone: because thou knewest not the season of thy visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> And entering into the temple, he began to cast out those that sold and bought in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> saying to them, It is written, My house is a house of prayer, but ye have made it a den of robbers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> And he was teaching day by day in the temple: and the chief priests and the scribes and the chief of the people sought to destroy him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> and did not find what they could do, for all the people hung on him to hear.</span>
