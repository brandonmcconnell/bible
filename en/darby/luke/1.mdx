<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:1–3; 2 Peter 1:16–19; John 20:31"> Forasmuch as many have undertaken to draw up a relation concerning the matters fully believed among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:27; Acts 26:16; Hebrews 2:3"> as those who from the beginning were eye-witnesses of and attendants on the Word have delivered them to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 1:1; Acts 26:25; Acts 11:4"> it has seemed good to me also, accurately acquainted from the origin with all things, to write to thee with method, most excellent Theophilus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> that thou mightest know the certainty of those things in which thou hast been instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judaea, a certain priest, by name Zacharias, of the course of Abia, and his wife of the daughters of Aaron, and her name Elizabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> And they were both just before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> And they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they were both advanced in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> And it came to pass, as he fulfilled his priestly service before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>God in the order of his course,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> it fell to him by lot, according to the custom of the priesthood, to enter into the temple of the Lord to burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> And all the multitude of the people were praying without at the hour of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> And an angel of &#91;the&#93; Lord appeared to him, standing on the right of the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> And Zacharias was troubled, seeing &#91;him&#93;, and fear fell upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said to him, Fear not, Zacharias, because thy supplication has been heard, and thy wife Elizabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> And he shall be to thee joy and rejoicing, and many shall rejoice at his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> For he shall be great before &#91;the&#93; Lord, and he shall drink no wine nor strong drink; and he shall be filled with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, even from his mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> And many of the sons of Israel shall he turn to &#91;the&#93; Lord their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.16 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> And he shall go before him in &#91;the&#93; spirit and power of Elias, to turn hearts of fathers to children, and disobedient ones to &#91;the&#93; thoughts of just &#91;men&#93;, to make ready for &#91;the&#93; Lord a prepared people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> And Zacharias said to the angel, How shall I know this, for I am an old man, and my wife advanced in years?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> And the angel answering, said to him, I am Gabriel, who stand before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.19 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and I have been sent to speak to thee, and to bring these glad tidings to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> and behold, thou shalt be silent and not able to speak, till the day in which these things shall take place, because thou hast not believed my words, the which shall be fulfilled in their time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> And the people were awaiting Zacharias, and they wondered at his delaying in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> But when he came out he could not speak to them, and they recognised that he had seen a vision in the temple. And he was making signs to them, and continued dumb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> And it came to pass, when the days of his service were completed, he departed to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now after these days, Elizabeth his wife conceived, and hid herself five months, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> Thus has &#91;the&#93; Lord done to me in &#91;these&#93; days in which he looked upon &#91;me&#93; to take away my reproach among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> But in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.26 Elohim">e</a></sup>God to a city of Galilee, of which &#91;the&#93; name &#91;was&#93; Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name &#91;was&#93; Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name &#91;was&#93; Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> And the angel came in to her, and said, Hail, &#91;thou&#93; favoured one! the Lord &#91;is&#93; with thee: &#91;blessed art thou amongst women&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> But she, &#91;seeing&#93; &#91;the angel&#93;, was troubled at his word, and reasoned in her mind what this salutation might be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> And the angel said to her, Fear not, Mary, for thou hast found favour with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.30 Elohim">f</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> and behold, thou shalt conceive in the womb and bear a son, and thou shalt call his name Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> He shall be great, and shall be called Son of &#91;the&#93; Highest; and &#91;the&#93; Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.32 Elohim">g</a></sup>God shall give him the throne of David his father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> and he shall reign over the house of Jacob for the ages, and of his kingdom there shall not be an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> But Mary said to the angel, How shall this be, since I know not a man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> And the angel answering said to her, &#91;The&#93; Holy Spirit shall come upon thee, and power of &#91;the&#93; Highest overshadow thee, wherefore the holy thing also which shall be born shall be called Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.35 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And behold, Elizabeth, thy kinswoman, she also has conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month to her that was called barren:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> for nothing shall be impossible with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.37 Elohim">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> And Mary said, Behold the bondmaid of &#91;the&#93; Lord; be it to me according to thy word. And the angel departed from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> And Mary, rising up in those days, went into the hill country with haste, to a city of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and entered into the house of Zacharias, and saluted Elizabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> And it came to pass, as Elizabeth heard the salutation of Mary, the babe leaped in her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 1:28; Judges 5:24; Genesis 22:18"> and cried out with a loud voice and said, Blessed &#91;art&#93; thou amongst women, and blessed the fruit of thy womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> And whence &#91;is&#93; this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For behold, as the voice of thy salutation sounded in my ears, the babe leaped with joy in my womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> And blessed &#91;is&#93; she that has believed, for there shall be a fulfilment of the things spoken to her from &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:1–10; Psalms 34:2–3; Psalms 35:9"> And Mary said, My soul magnifies the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 35:9; 1 Timothy 2:3; Habakkuk 3:18"> and my spirit has rejoiced in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.47 Elohim">j</a></sup>God my Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Malachi 3:12; 1 Corinthians 1:26–28"> For he has looked upon the low estate of his bondmaid; for behold, from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Psalms 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> For the Mighty One has done to me great things, and holy &#91;is&#93; his name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> and his mercy &#91;is&#93; to generations and generations to them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> He has wrought strength with his arm; he has scattered haughty &#91;ones&#93; in the thought of their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; James 4:10; Psalms 107:40–41"> He has put down rulers from thrones, and exalted the lowly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; Psalms 34:10"> He has filled the hungry with good things, and sent away the rich empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> He has helped Israel his servant, in order to remember mercy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; Psalms 132:11–17"> (as he spoke to our fathers,) to Abraham and to his seed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And Mary abode with her about three months, and returned to her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> But the time was fulfilled for Elizabeth that she should bring forth, and she gave birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> And her neighbours and kinsfolk heard that &#91;the&#93; Lord had magnified his mercy with her, and they rejoiced with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> And it came to pass on the eighth day they came to circumcise the child, and they called it after the name of his father, Zacharias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> And his mother answering said, No; but he shall be called John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they said to her, There is no one among thy kinsfolk who is called by this name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> And they made signs to his father as to what he might wish it to be called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> And having asked for a writing-table, he wrote saying, John is his name. And they all wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> And his mouth was opened immediately, and his tongue, and he spake, blessing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.64 Elohim">k</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> And fear came upon all who dwelt round about them; and in the whole hill-country of Judaea all these things were the subject of conversation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And all who heard them laid them up in their heart, saying, What then will this child be? And &#91;the&#93; Lord's hand was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Luke 1:41; Luke 1:15"> And Zacharias his father was filled with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, and prophesied, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Luke 7:16; Psalms 41:13"> Blessed be &#91;the&#93; Lord the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1.68 Elohim">l</a></sup>God of Israel, because he has visited and wrought redemption for his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Samuel 22:3"> and raised up a horn of deliverance for us in the house of David his servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> as he spoke by &#91;the&#93; mouth of his holy prophets, who have been since the world began;</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Psalms 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> deliverance from our enemies and out of the hand of all who hate us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–18; Micah 7:20; Psalms 106:45"> to fulfil mercy with our fathers and remember his holy covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Psalms 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> &#91;the&#93; oath which he swore to Abraham our father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> to give us, that, saved out of the hand of our enemies, we should serve him without fear</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> in piety and righteousness before him all our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> And thou, child, shalt be called &#91;the&#93; prophet of &#91;the&#93; Highest; for thou shalt go before the face of &#91;the&#93; Lord to make ready his ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Acts 2:38; Acts 10:43"> to give knowledge of deliverance to his people by &#91;the&#93; remission of their sins</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="Malachi 4:2; 2 Peter 1:19; Revelation 22:16"> on account of &#91;the&#93; bowels of mercy of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1.78 Elohim">m</a></sup>God; wherein &#91;the&#93; dayspring from on high has visited us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Matthew 4:16; Acts 26:18"> to shine upon them who were sitting in darkness and in &#91;the&#93; shadow of death, to guide our feet into &#91;the&#93; way of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Mark 1:3–4; Luke 2:52"> — And the child grew and was strengthened in spirit; and he was in the deserts until the day of his shewing to Israel.</span>
