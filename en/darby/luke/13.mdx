<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Lamentations 2:20; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> Now at the same time there were present some who told him of the Galileans whose blood Pilate mingled with &#91;that of&#93; their sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 9:2; Acts 28:4; Job 22:5–16"> And he answering said to them, Think ye that these Galileans were sinners beyond all the Galileans because they suffered such things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38–40"> No, I say to you, but if ye repent not, ye shall all perish in the same manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:15; Job 1:19; John 9:11"> Or those eighteen on whom the tower in Siloam fell and killed them, think ye that they were debtors beyond all the men who dwell in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Luke 13:3; Isaiah 28:10–13"> No, I say to you, but if ye repent not, ye shall all perish in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Isaiah 5:1–4; Galatians 5:22"> And he spoke this parable: A certain &#91;man&#93; had a fig-tree planted in his vineyard, and he came seeking fruit upon it and did not find &#91;any&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Luke 3:9; John 15:6"> And he said to the vinedresser, Behold, &#91;these&#93; three years I come seeking fruit on this fig-tree and find none: cut it down; why does it also render the ground useless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Joel 2:17; Romans 10:1"> But he answering says to him, Sir, let it alone for this year also, until I shall dig about it and put dung,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Hebrews 6:8"> and if it shall bear fruit — but if not, after that thou shalt cut it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Luke 4:44; Luke 4:15–16"> And he was teaching in one of the synagogues on the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Luke 8:27; Luke 13:16"> And lo, &#91;there was&#93; a woman having a spirit of infirmity eighteen years, and she was bent together and wholly unable to lift her head up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Matthew 8:16; Luke 6:8–10"> And Jesus, seeing her, called to &#91;her&#93;, and said to her, Woman, thou art loosed from thine infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:20–22; Mark 16:18; Mark 5:23"> And he laid his hands upon her; and immediately she was made straight, and glorified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.13 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:9; Matthew 12:2; John 9:14–16"> But the ruler of the synagogue, indignant because Jesus healed on the sabbath, answering said to the crowd, There are six days in which &#91;people&#93; ought to work; in these therefore come and be healed, and not on the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 14:5; John 7:21–24; Acts 13:9–10"> The Lord therefore answered him and said, Hypocrites! does not each one of you on the sabbath loose his ox or his ass from the manger and leading &#91;it&#93; away, water &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 2:27; 2 Timothy 2:26; Luke 19:9"> And this &#91;woman&#93;, who is a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan has bound, lo, &#91;these&#93; eighteen years, ought she not to be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Psalms 132:18; Isaiah 45:24"> And as he said these things, all who were opposed to him were ashamed; and all the crowd rejoiced at all the glorious things which were being done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:30–34; Luke 17:21; Matthew 13:31–32"> And he said, To what is the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.18 Elohim">b</a></sup>God like? and to what shall I liken it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:12; Daniel 4:21; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is like a grain of mustard &#91;seed&#93; which a man took and cast into his garden; and it grew and became a great tree, and the birds of heaven lodged in its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And again he said, To what shall I liken the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.20 Elohim">c</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:23–24"> It is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal until the whole was leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Matthew 9:35; Mark 10:32–34"> And he went through one city and village after another, teaching, and journeying to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:14; Matthew 22:14; John 21:21–22"> And one said to him, Sir, &#91;are&#93; such as are to be saved few in number? But he said unto them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27"> Strive with earnestness to enter in through the narrow door, for many, I say to you, will seek to enter in and will not be able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 25:10–12; Luke 13:27; Matthew 7:21–23"> From the time that the master of the house shall have risen up and shall have shut the door, and ye shall begin to stand without and to knock at the door, saying, Lord, open to us; and he answering shall say to you, I know you not whence ye are:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 58:2; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> then shall ye begin to say, We have eaten in thy presence and drunk, and thou hast taught in our streets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 6:8; Matthew 25:41; Matthew 7:22–23"> and he shall say, I tell you, I do not know you whence ye are; depart from me, all &#91;ye&#93; workers of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:11–12; Matthew 22:13; Matthew 25:30"> There shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth, when ye shall see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets in the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.28 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, but yourselves cast out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 43:6; Isaiah 66:18–20; Isaiah 49:6"> And they shall come from east and west, and from north and south, and shall lie down at table in the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.29 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 20:16"> And behold, there are last who shall be first, and there are first who shall be last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9–11; Psalms 11:1–2; Matthew 14:1"> The same hour certain Pharisees came up, saying to him, Get out, and go hence, for Herod is desirous to kill thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 5:9; John 17:4–5"> And he said to them, Go, tell that fox, Behold, I cast out demons and accomplish cures to-day and to-morrow, and the third &#91;day&#93; I am perfected;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Matthew 21:11; Acts 13:27"> but I must needs walk to-day and to-morrow and the &#91;day&#93; following, for it must not be that a prophet perish out of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 23:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Acts 7:52"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, the &#91;city&#93; that kills the prophets and stones those that are sent unto her, how often would I have gathered thy children together, as a hen her brood under her wings, and ye would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Mark 11:9–10; Matthew 21:9"> Behold, your house is left unto you; and I say unto you, that ye shall not see me until it come that ye say, Blessed &#91;is&#93; he that comes in the name of &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
