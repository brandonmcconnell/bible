<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> And when he had completed all his words in the hearing of the people, he entered into Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> And a certain centurion's bondman who was dear to him was ill and about to die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> and having heard of Jesus, he sent to him elders of the Jews, begging him that he might come and save his bondman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> But they, being come to Jesus, besought him diligently, saying, He is worthy to whom thou shouldest grant this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> for he loves our nation, and himself has built the synagogue for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> And Jesus went with them. But already, when he was not far from the house, the centurion sent to him friends, saying to him, Lord, do not trouble thyself, for I am not worthy that thou shouldest enter under my roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Psalms 33:9; Luke 4:36"> Wherefore neither did I count myself worthy to come to thee. But say by a word and my servant shall be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> For I also am a man placed under authority, having under myself soldiers, and I say to this &#91;one&#93;, Go, and he goes; and to another, Come, and he comes; and to my bondman, Do this, and he does &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> And Jesus hearing this wondered at him, and turning to the crowd following him said, I say to you, Not even in Israel have I found so great faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> And they who had been sent returning to the house found the bondman, who was ill, in good health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> And it came to pass afterwards he went into a city called Nain, and many of his disciples and a great crowd went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> And as he drew near to the gate of the city, behold, a dead man was carried out, the only son of his mother, and she a widow, and a very considerable crowd of the city &#91;was&#93; with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 86:15"> And the Lord, seeing her, was moved with compassion for her, and said to her, Weep not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> and coming up he touched the bier, and the bearers stopped. And he said, Youth, I say to thee, Wake up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> And the dead sat up and began to speak; and he gave him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> And fear seized on all, and they glorified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.16 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, saying, A great prophet has been raised up amongst us; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.16 Elohim">b</a></sup>God has visited his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> And this report went out in all Judaea concerning him, and in all the surrounding country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> And the disciples of John brought him word concerning all these things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> and John, having called two of his disciples, sent to Jesus, saying, Art thou he that is coming, or are we to wait for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> But the men having come to him said, John the baptist has sent us to thee, saying, Art thou he that is coming, or are we to wait for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; Psalms 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> In that hour he healed many of diseases and plagues and evil spirits, and to many blind he granted sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> And Jesus answering said to them, Go, bring back word to John of what ye have seen and heard: that blind see, lame walk, lepers are cleansed, deaf hear, dead are raised, poor are evangelized;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> and blessed is whosoever shall not be offended in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> And the messengers of John having departed, he began to speak to the crowds concerning John: What went ye out into the wilderness to behold? a reed shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> But what went ye out to see? a man clothed in delicate garments? Behold, those who are in splendid clothing and live luxuriously are in the courts of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> But what went ye out to see? a prophet? Yea, I say to you, and &#91;what is&#93; more excellent than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> This is he concerning whom it is written, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, who shall prepare thy way before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> for I say unto you, Among them that are born of women a greater &#91;prophet&#93; is no one than John &#91;the baptist&#93;; but he who is a little one in the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.28 Elohim">c</a></sup>God is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> (And all the people who heard &#91;it&#93;, and the tax-gatherers, justified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.29 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, having been baptised with the baptism of John;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> but the Pharisees and the lawyers rendered null as to themselves the counsel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.30 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, not having been baptised by him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> To whom therefore shall I liken the men of this generation, and to whom are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They are like children sitting in the market-place, and calling one to another and saying, We have piped to you, and ye have not danced; we have mourned to you, and ye have not wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> For John the baptist has come neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and ye say, He has a demon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> The Son of man has come eating and drinking, and ye say, Behold an eater and wine-drinker, a friend of tax-gatherers and sinners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> and wisdom has been justified of all her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> But one of the Pharisees begged him that he would eat with him. And entering into the house of the Pharisee he took his place at table;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> and behold, a woman in the city, who was a sinner, and knew that he was sitting at meat in the house of the Pharisee, having taken an alabaster box of myrrh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> and standing at his feet behind &#91;him&#93; weeping, began to wash his feet with tears; and she wiped them with the hairs of her head, and kissed his feet, and anointed &#91;them&#93; with the myrrh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> And the Pharisee who had invited him, seeing it, spoke with himself saying, This &#91;person&#93; if he were a prophet would have known who and what the woman is who touches him, for she is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> And Jesus answering said to him, Simon, I have somewhat to say to thee. And he says, Teacher, say &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> There were two debtors of a certain creditor: one owed five hundred denarii and the other fifty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> but as they had nothing to pay, he forgave both of them &#91;their debt&#93;: &#91;say,&#93; which of them therefore will love him most?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> And Simon answering said, I suppose he to whom he forgave the most. And he said to him, Thou hast rightly judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> And turning to the woman he said to Simon, Seest thou this woman? I entered into thy house; thou gavest me not water on my feet, but she has washed my feet with tears, and wiped them with her hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> Thou gavest me not a kiss, but she from the time I came in has not ceased kissing my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> My head with oil thou didst not anoint, but she has anointed my feet with myrrh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> For which cause I say to thee, Her many sins are forgiven; for she loved much; but he to whom little is forgiven loves little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> And he said to her, Thy sins are forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> And they that were with &#91;them&#93; at table began to say within themselves, Who is this who forgives also sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> And he said to the woman, Thy faith has saved thee; go in peace.</span>
