<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:7; Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 11:19"> And he said to his disciples, It cannot be but that offences come, but woe &#91;to him&#93; by whom they come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Matthew 18:14; Matthew 18:3–6"> It would be &#91;more&#93; profitable for him if a millstone were hanged about his neck and he cast into the sea, than that he should be a snare to one of these little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Matthew 18:21; Matthew 18:15–17"> Take heed to yourselves: if thy brother should sin, rebuke him; and if he should repent, forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 18:21–22; Romans 12:20"> And if he should sin against thee seven times in the day, and seven times should return to thee, saying, I repent, thou shalt forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> And the apostles said to the Lord, Give more faith to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> But the Lord said, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard &#91;seed&#93;, ye had said to this sycamine tree, Be thou rooted up, and be thou planted in the sea, and it would have obeyed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> But which of you &#91;is there&#93; who, having a bondman ploughing or shepherding, when he comes in out of the field, will say, Come and lie down immediately to table?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> But will he not say to him, Prepare what I shall sup on, and gird thyself and serve me that I may eat and drink; and after that thou shalt eat and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Is he thankful to the bondman because he has done what was ordered? I judge not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Thus ye also, when ye shall have done all things that have been ordered you, say, We are unprofitable bondmen; we have done what it was our duty to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> And it came to pass as he was going up to Jerusalem, that he passed through the midst of Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> And as he entered into a certain village ten leprous men met him, who stood afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> And they lifted up &#91;their&#93; voice saying, Jesus, Master, have compassion on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> And seeing &#91;them&#93; he said to them, Go, shew yourselves to the priests. And it came to pass as they were going they were cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 103:1–4; Psalms 118:18–19"> And one of them, seeing that he was cured, turned back, glorifying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="17.15 Elohim">a</a></sup>God with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> and fell on &#91;his&#93; face at his feet giving him thanks: and he was a Samaritan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalms 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> And Jesus answering said, Were not the ten cleansed? but the nine, where &#91;are they&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; Psalms 106:13"> There have not been found to return and give glory to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="17.18 Elohim">b</a></sup>God save this stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> And he said to him, Rise up and go thy way: thy faith has made thee well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> And having been asked by the Pharisees, When is the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="17.20 Elohim">c</a></sup>God coming? he answered them and said, The kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="17.20 Elohim">d</a></sup>God does not come with observation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> nor shall they say, Lo here, or, Lo there; for behold, the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="17.21 Elohim">e</a></sup>God is in the midst of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> And he said to the disciples, Days are coming, when ye shall desire to see one of the days of the Son of man, and shall not see &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> And they will say to you, Lo here, or Lo there; go not, nor follow &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> For as the lightning shines which lightens from &#91;one end&#93; under heaven to &#91;the other end&#93; under heaven, thus shall the Son of man be in his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> But first he must suffer many things and be rejected of this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> And as it took place in the days of Noe, thus also shall it be in the days of the Son of man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> they ate, they drank, they married, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark, and the flood came and destroyed all &#91;of them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> and in like manner as took place in the days of Lot: they ate, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they builded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> but on the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulphur from heaven, and destroyed all &#91;of them&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> after this &#91;manner&#93; shall it be in the day that the Son of man is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> In that day, he who shall be on the housetop, and his stuff in the house, let him not go down to take it away; and he that is in the field, let him likewise not return back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> Remember the wife of Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> Whosoever shall seek to save his life shall lose it, and whosoever shall lose it shall preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I say to you, In that night there shall be two &#91;men&#93; upon one bed; one shall be seized and the other shall be let go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> Two &#91;women&#93; shall be grinding together; the one shall be seized and the other shall be let go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> &#91;Two men shall be in the field; the one shall be seized and the other let go.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> And answering they say to him, Where, Lord? And he said to them, Where the body &#91;is&#93;, there the eagles will be gathered together.</span>
