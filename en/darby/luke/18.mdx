<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> And he spoke also a parable to them to the purport that they should always pray and not faint,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> saying, There was a judge in a city, not fearing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and not respecting man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> and there was a widow in that city, and she came to him, saying, Avenge me of mine adverse party.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> And he would not for a time; but afterwards he said within himself, If even I fear not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and respect not man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> at any rate because this widow annoys me I will avenge her, that she may not by perpetually coming completely harass me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> And shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God at all avenge his elect, who cry to him day and night, and he bears long as to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I say unto you that he will avenge them speedily. But when the Son of man comes, shall he indeed find faith on the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> And he spoke also to some, who trusted in themselves that they were righteous and made nothing of all the rest &#91;of men&#93;, this parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> Two men went up into the temple to pray; the one a Pharisee, and the other a tax-gatherer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee, standing, prayed thus to himself:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.11 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, I thank thee that I am not as the rest of men, rapacious, unjust, adulterers, or even as this tax-gatherer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice in the week, I tithe everything I gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> And the tax-gatherer, standing afar off, would not lift up even his eyes to heaven, but smote upon his breast, saying, O<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="18.13 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, have compassion on me, the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I say unto you, This &#91;man&#93; went down to his house justified rather than that &#91;other&#93;. For every one who exalts himself shall be humbled, and he that humbles himself shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> And they brought to him also infants that he might touch them, but the disciples when they saw &#91;it&#93; rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But Jesus calling them to &#91;him&#93; said, Suffer little children to come to me, and do not forbid them, for of such is the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="18.16 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; Psalms 131:1–2"> Verily I say to you, Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="18.17 Elohim">g</a></sup>God as a little child shall in no wise enter therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> And a certain ruler asked him saying, Good teacher, having done what, shall I inherit eternal life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> But Jesus said to him, Why callest thou me good? There is none good but one,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="18.19 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> Thou knowest the commandments: Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Honour thy father and thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> And he said, All these things have I kept from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> And when Jesus had heard this, he said to him, One thing is lacking to thee yet: Sell all that thou hast and distribute to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in the heavens, and come, follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when he heard this he became very sorrowful, for he was very rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> But when Jesus saw that he became very sorrowful, he said, How difficultly shall those who have riches enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="18.24 Elohim">i</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> for it is easier for a camel to enter through a needle's eye than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="18.25 Elohim">j</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> And those who heard it said, And who can be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> But he said, The things that are impossible with men are possible with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="18.27 Elohim">k</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> And Peter said, Behold, we have left all things and have followed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> And he said to them, Verily I say to you, There is no one who has left home, or parents, or brethren, or wife, or children, for the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="18.29 Elohim">l</a></sup>God's sake,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who shall not receive manifold more at this time, and in the coming age life eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> And he took the twelve to &#91;him&#93; and said to them, Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and all things that are written of the Son of man by the prophets shall be accomplished;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> for he shall be delivered up to the nations, and shall be mocked, and insulted, and spit upon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> And when they have scourged &#91;him&#93; they will kill him; and on the third day he will rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> And they understood nothing of these things. And this word was hidden from them, and they did not know what was said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> And it came to pass when he came into the neighbourhood of Jericho, a certain blind man sat by the wayside begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> And when he heard the crowd passing, he inquired what this might be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> And they told him that Jesus the Nazaraean was passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> And he called out saying, Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> And those &#91;who were&#93; going before rebuked him that he might be silent; but he cried out so much the more, Son of David, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> And Jesus stood still, and commanded him to be led to him. And when he drew nigh he asked him &#91;saying&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> What wilt thou that I shall do to thee? And he said, Lord, that I may see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> And Jesus said to him, See: thy faith has healed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> And immediately he saw, and followed him, glorifying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="18.43 Elohim">m</a></sup>God. And all the people when they saw &#91;it&#93; gave praise to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="18.43 Elohim">n</a></sup>God.</span>
