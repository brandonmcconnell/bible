<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Matthew 12:1–8"> And it came to pass on &#91;the&#93; second-first sabbath, that he went through cornfields, and his disciples were plucking the ears and eating &#91;them&#93;, rubbing &#91;them&#93; in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:2; John 5:16; John 5:9–11"> But some of the Pharisees said to them, Why do ye what is not lawful to do on the sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Mark 2:25–26; Matthew 12:3–5"> And Jesus answering said to them, Have ye not read so much as this, what David did when he hungered, he and those who were with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9"> how he entered into the house of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and took the shewbread and ate, and gave to those also who were with him, which it is not lawful that &#91;any&#93; eat, unless the priests alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 2:27; Revelation 1:10; Mark 9:7"> And he said to them, The Son of man is Lord of the sabbath also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 4:31"> And it came to pass on another sabbath also that he entered into the synagogue and taught; and there was a man there, and his right hand was withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Psalms 38:12; Luke 13:14"> And the scribes and the Pharisees were watching if he would heal on the sabbath, that they might find something of which to accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 42:4; Matthew 9:4; Luke 5:22"> But he knew their thoughts, and said to the man who had the withered hand, Get up, and stand in the midst. And having risen up he stood &#91;there&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:4; Matthew 12:12–13; Luke 14:3"> Jesus therefore said to them, I will ask you if it is lawful on the sabbath to do good, or to do evil? to save life, or to destroy &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:6–7; Mark 3:5; John 5:8"> And having looked around on them all, he said to him, Stretch out thy hand. And he did &#91;so&#93; and his hand was restored as the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–2; Acts 5:33; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> But they were filled with madness, and they spoke together among themselves what they should do to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35"> And it came to pass in those days that he went out into the mountain to pray, and he spent the night in prayer to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.12 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 3:13–19; Matthew 9:36–4; Revelation 21:14"> And when it was day he called his disciples, and having chosen out twelve from them, whom also he named apostles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Matthew 4:21; Matthew 4:18"> Simon, to whom also he gave the name of Peter, and Andrew his brother, &#91;and&#93; James and John, &#91;and&#93; Philip and Bartholomew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13"> &#91;and&#93; Matthew and Thomas, James the &#91;son&#93; of Alphaeus and Simon who was called Zealot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:22; John 6:70–71; Mark 3:18"> &#91;and&#93; Judas &#91;brother&#93; of James, and Judas Iscariote, who was also &#91;his&#93; betrayer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Matthew 4:23–25; Psalms 107:17–20"> and having descended with them, he stood on a level place, and a crowd of his disciples, and a great multitude of the people from all Judaea and Jerusalem, and the sea coast of Tyre and Sidon, who came to hear him, and to be healed of their diseases;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:15; Matthew 15:22; Acts 5:16"> and those that were beset by unclean spirits were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 3:10; Acts 5:15–16"> And all the crowd sought to touch him, for power went out from him and healed all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:5; Luke 6:20–24; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And he, lifting up his eyes upon his disciples, said, Blessed &#91;are&#93; ye poor, for yours is the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.20 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 6:35; Matthew 5:6; James 1:12"> Blessed ye that hunger now, for ye shall be filled. Blessed ye that weep now, for ye shall laugh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; Matthew 10:22; 1 Peter 4:12–16"> Blessed are ye when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you &#91;from them&#93;, and shall reproach &#91;you&#93;, and cast out your name as wicked, for the Son of man's sake:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:41; Revelation 21:7; Revelation 3:12"> rejoice in that day and leap for joy, for behold, your reward is great in the heaven, for after this manner did their fathers act toward the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:2; James 5:1–6; Psalms 49:6–7"> But woe to you rich, for ye have received your consolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 65:13; James 4:9; Proverbs 14:13"> Woe to you that are filled, for ye shall hunger. Woe to you who laugh now, for ye shall mourn and weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:19; James 4:4; Matthew 7:15"> Woe, when all men speak well of you, for after this manner did their fathers to the false prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; Luke 6:35; Romans 12:17–21"> But to you that hear I say, Love your enemies; do good to those that hate you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:12; Romans 12:14"> bless those that curse you; pray for those who use you despitefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 10:34; Matthew 5:39–42; 2 Corinthians 11:20"> To him that smites thee on the cheek, offer also the other; and from him that would take away thy garment, forbid not thy body-coat also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–10; 2 Corinthians 9:6–14; Matthew 6:12"> To every one that asks of thee, give; and from him that takes away what is thine, ask it not back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> And as ye wish that men should do to you, do ye also to them in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 5:46–47; 1 Peter 2:19–20"> And if ye love those that love you, what thank is it to you? for even sinners love those that love them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And if ye do good to those that do good to you, what thank is it to you? for even sinners do the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Luke 14:12–14; Luke 6:35"> And if ye lend to those from whom ye hope to receive, what thank is it to you? &#91;for&#93; even sinners lend to sinners that they may receive the like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 5:44–45; John 13:35; Proverbs 19:17"> But love your enemies, and do good, and lend, hoping for nothing in return, and your reward shall be great, and ye shall be sons of &#91;the&#93; Highest; for he is good to the unthankful and wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:1–2; Matthew 5:48; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Be ye therefore merciful, even as your Father also is merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; James 5:9; Matthew 7:1–5"> And judge not, and ye shall not be judged; condemn not, and ye shall not be condemned. Remit, and it shall be remitted to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Deuteronomy 15:10; Mark 4:24"> Give, and it shall be given to you; good measure, pressed down, and shaken together, and running over, shall be given into your bosom: for with the same measure with which ye mete it shall be measured to you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; 2 Timothy 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> And he spoke also a parable to them: Can a blind &#91;man&#93; lead a blind &#91;man&#93;? shall not both fall into &#91;the&#93; ditch?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:16; Matthew 10:24–25; John 15:20"> The disciple is not above his teacher, but every one that is perfected shall be as his teacher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 8:7; James 1:24; Romans 2:1"> But why lookest thou on the mote which is in the eye of thy brother, but perceivest not the beam which is in thine own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 2:21–29; 1 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 51:9–13"> or how canst thou say to thy brother, Brother, allow &#91;me&#93;, I will cast out the mote that is in thine eye, thyself not seeing the beam that is in thine eye? Hypocrite, cast out first the beam out of thine eye, and then thou shalt see clear to cast out the mote which is in the eye of thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> For there is no good tree which produces corrupt fruit, nor a corrupt tree which produces good fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 12:33; James 3:12; Galatians 5:19–23"> for every tree is known by its own fruit, for figs are not gathered from thorns, nor grapes vintaged from a bramble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Matthew 12:34–37; Ephesians 4:29"> The good man, out of the good treasure of his heart, brings forth good; and the wicked &#91;man&#93; out of the wicked, brings forth what is wicked: for out of the abundance of the heart his mouth speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; Malachi 1:6; Matthew 7:21–23"> And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things that I say?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 1:22–25; Matthew 7:24–27; Luke 11:28"> Every one that comes to me, and hears my words and does them, I will shew you to whom he is like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10–15; Psalms 62:2; Matthew 7:25–26"> He is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep, and laid a foundation on the rock; but a great rain coming, the stream broke upon that house, and could not shake it, for it had been founded on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; James 1:22–26; Matthew 23:3"> And he that has heard and not done, is like a man who has built a house on the ground without &#91;a&#93; foundation, on which the stream broke, and immediately it fell, and the breach of that house was great.</span>
