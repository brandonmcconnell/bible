<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> And it came to pass, as he went into the house of one of the rulers, &#91;who was&#93; of the Pharisees, to eat bread on &#91;the&#93; sabbath, that they were watching him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And behold, there was a certain dropsical &#91;man&#93; before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> And Jesus answering spoke unto the doctors of the law and Pharisees, saying, Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they were silent. And taking him he healed him and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> And answering he said to them, Of which of you shall an ass or ox fall into a well, that he does not straightway pull him up on the sabbath day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they were not able to answer him to these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> And he spoke a parable to those that were invited, remarking how they chose out the first places, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> When thou art invited by any one to a wedding, do not lay thyself down in the first place at table, lest perhaps a more honourable than thou be invited by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> and he who invited thee and him come and say to thee, Give place to this &#91;man&#93;, and then thou begin with shame to take the last place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when thou hast been invited, go and put thyself down in the last place, that when he who has invited thee comes, he may say to thee, Friend, go up higher: then shalt thou have honour before all that are lying at table with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> for every one that exalts himself shall be abased, and he that abases himself shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> And he said also to him that had invited him, When thou makest a dinner or a supper, call not thy friends, nor thy brethren, nor thy kinsfolk, nor rich neighbours, lest it may be they also should invite thee in return, and a recompense be made thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But when thou makest a feast, call poor, crippled, lame, blind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> and thou shalt be blessed; for they have not &#91;the means&#93; to recompense thee; for it shall be recompensed thee in the resurrection of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> And one of those that were lying at table with &#91;them&#93;, hearing these things, said to him, Blessed &#91;is&#93; he who shall eat bread in the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.15 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> And he said to him, A certain man made a great supper and invited many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> And he sent his bondman at the hour of supper to say to those who were invited, Come, for already all things are ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> And all began, without exception, to excuse themselves. The first said to him, I have bought land, and I must go out and see it; I pray thee hold me for excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I go to prove them; I pray thee hold me for excused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> And another said, I have married a wife, and on this account I cannot come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> And the bondman came up and brought back word of these things to his lord. Then the master of the house, in anger, said to his bondman, Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring here the poor and crippled and lame and blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the bondman said, Sir, it is done as thou hast commanded, and there is still room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> And the lord said to the bondman, Go out into the ways and fences and compel to come in, that my house may be filled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> for I say to you, that not one of those men who were invited shall taste of my supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> And great crowds went with him; and, turning round, he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> If any man come to me, and shall not hate his own father and mother, and wife, and children, and brothers, and sisters, yea, and his own life too, he cannot be my disciple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> and whoever does not carry his cross and come after me cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For which of you, desirous of building a tower, does not first sit down and count the cost, if he have what &#91;is needed&#93; to complete it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> in order that, having laid the foundation of it, and not being able to finish it, all who see it do not begin to mock at him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> saying, This man began to build and was not able to finish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king, going on his way to engage in war with another king, does not, sitting down first, take counsel whether he is able with ten thousand to meet him coming against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> and if not, while he is yet far off, having sent an embassy, he asks for terms of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> Thus then every one of you who forsakes not all that is his own cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> Salt &#91;then&#93; &#91;is&#93; good, but if the salt also has become savourless, wherewith shall it be seasoned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is proper neither for land nor for dung; it is cast out. He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span>
