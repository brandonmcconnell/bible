<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> But on the morrow of the sabbath, very early indeed in the morning, they came to the tomb, bringing the aromatic spices which they had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> And they found the stone rolled away from the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> And when they had entered they found not the body of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> And it came to pass as they were in perplexity about it, that behold, two men suddenly stood by them in shining raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> And as they were filled with fear and bowed their faces to the ground, they said to them, Why seek ye the living one among the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spoke to you, being yet in Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> saying, The Son of man must be delivered up into the hands of sinners, and be crucified, and rise the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembered his words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> and, returning from the sepulchre, related all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> Now it was Mary of Magdala, and Johanna, and Mary the &#91;mother&#93; of James, and the others with them, who told these things to the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> And their words appeared in their eyes as an idle tale, and they disbelieved them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> But Peter, rising up, ran to the sepulchre, and stooping down he sees the linen clothes lying there alone, and went away home, wondering at what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> And behold, two of them were going on the same day to a village distant sixty stadia from Jerusalem, called Emmaus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> and they conversed with one another about all these things which had taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> And it came to pass as they conversed and reasoned, that Jesus himself drawing nigh, went with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> but their eyes were holden so as not to know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> And he said to them, What discourses are these which pass between you as ye walk, and are downcast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> And one &#91;of them&#93;, named Cleopas, answering said to him, Thou sojournest alone in Jerusalem, and dost not know what has taken place in it in these days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> And he said to them, What things? And they said to him, The things concerning Jesus the Nazaraean, who was a prophet mighty in deed and word before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.19 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and all the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered him up to &#91;the&#93; judgment of death and crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we had hoped that he was &#91;the one&#93; who is about to redeem Israel. But then, besides all these things, it is now, to-day, the third day since these things took place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> And withal, certain women from amongst us astonished us, having been very early at the sepulchre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and, not having found his body, came, saying that they also had seen a vision of angels, who say that he is living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> And some of those with us went to the sepulchre, and found it so, as the women also had said, but him they saw not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> And he said to them, O senseless and slow of heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> Ought not the Christ to have suffered these things and to enter into his glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And having begun from Moses and from all the prophets, he interpreted to them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they drew near to the village where they were going, and he made as though he would go farther.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> And they constrained him, saying, Stay with us, for it is toward evening and the day is declining. And he entered in to stay with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it came to pass as he was at table with them, having taken the bread, he blessed, and having broken it, gave it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> And their eyes were opened, and they recognised him. And he disappeared from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they said to one another, Was not our heart burning in us as he spoke to us on the way, &#91;and&#93; as he opened the scriptures to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And rising up the same hour, they returned to Jerusalem. And they found the eleven, and those with them, gathered together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> saying, The Lord is indeed risen and has appeared to Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> And they related what &#91;had happened&#93; on the way, and how he was made known to them in the breaking of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> And as they were saying these things, he himself stood in their midst, and says to them, Peace &#91;be&#93; unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But they, being confounded and being frightened, supposed they beheld a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> And he said to them, Why are ye troubled? and why are thoughts rising in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> behold my hands and my feet, that it is I myself. Handle me and see, for a spirit has not flesh and bones as ye see me having.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And having said this he shewed them his hands and his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> But while they yet did not believe for joy, and were wondering, he said to them, Have ye anything here to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they gave him part of a broiled fish and of a honeycomb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> and he took it and ate before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> And he said to them, These &#91;are&#93; the words which I spoke to you while I was yet with you, that all that is written concerning me in the law of Moses and prophets and psalms must be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then he opened their understanding to understand the scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> and said to them, Thus it is written, and thus it behoved the Christ to suffer, and to rise from among the dead the third day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name to all the nations beginning at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> And ye are witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And behold, I send the promise of my Father upon you; but do ye remain in the city till ye be clothed with power from on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> And he led them out as far as Bethany, and having lifted up his hands, he blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it came to pass as he was blessing them, he was separated from them and was carried up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> And they, having done him homage, returned to Jerusalem with great joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> and were continually in the temple praising and blessing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="24.53 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
