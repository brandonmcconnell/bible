<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> And he looked up and saw the rich casting their gifts into the treasury;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> but he saw also a certain poor widow casting therein two mites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> And he said, Verily I say unto you, that this poor widow has cast in more than all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> for all these out of their abundance have cast into the gifts &#91;of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="21.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God&#93;; but she out of her need has cast in all the living which she had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> And as some spoke of the temple, that it was adorned with goodly stones and consecrated offerings, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> &#91;As to&#93; these things which ye are beholding, days are coming in which there shall not be left stone upon stone which shall not be thrown down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> And they asked him saying, Teacher, when then shall these things be; and what &#91;is&#93; the sign when these things are going to take place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> And he said, See that ye be not led astray, for many shall come in my name, saying, I am &#91;he&#93;, and the time is drawn nigh: go ye not &#91;therefore&#93; after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> And when ye shall hear of wars and tumults, be not terrified, for these things must first take place, but the end is not immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then he said to them, Nation shall rise up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> there shall be both great earthquakes in different places, and famines and pestilences; and there shall be fearful sights and great signs from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But before all these things they shall lay their hands upon you and persecute you, delivering &#91;you&#93; up to synagogues and prisons, bringing &#91;you&#93; before kings and governors on account of my name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> but it shall turn out to you for a testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> Settle therefore in your hearts not to meditate beforehand &#91;your&#93; defence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> for I will give you a mouth and wisdom which all your opposers shall not be able to reply to or resist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> But ye will be delivered up even by parents and brethren and relations and friends, and they shall put to death &#91;some&#93; from among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> and ye will be hated of all for my name's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> And a hair of your head shall in no wise perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> By your patient endurance gain your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> But when ye see Jerusalem encompassed with armies, then know that its desolation is drawn nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> Then let those who are in Judaea flee to the mountains, and those who are in the midst of it depart out, and those who are in the country not enter into it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> for these are days of avenging, that all the things that are written may be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> But woe to them that are with child and to them who give suck in those days, for there shall be great distress upon the land and wrath upon this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and be led captive into all the nations; and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of &#91;the&#93; nations until &#91;the&#93; times of &#91;the&#93; nations be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> And there shall be signs in sun and moon and stars, and upon the earth distress of nations in perplexity &#91;at&#93; the roar of the sea and rolling waves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> men ready to die through fear and expectation of what is coming on the habitable earth, for the powers of the heavens shall be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> And then shall they see the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> But when these things begin to come to pass, look up and lift up your heads, because your redemption draws nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> And he spoke a parable to them: Behold the fig-tree and all the trees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> when they already sprout, ye know of your own selves, &#91;on&#93; looking &#91;at them&#93;, that already the summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> So also ye, when ye see these things take place, know that the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="21.31 Elohim">b</a></sup>God is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> Verily I say unto you, that this generation shall in no wise pass away until all come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> The heaven and the earth shall pass away, but my words shall in no wise pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> But take heed to yourselves lest possibly your hearts be laden with surfeiting and drinking and cares of life, and that day come upon you suddenly unawares;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Psalms 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> for as a snare shall it come upon all them that dwell upon the face of the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> Watch therefore, praying at every season, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things which are about to come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> And by day he was teaching in the temple, and by night, going out, he remained abroad on the mountain called &#91;the mount&#93; of Olives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> and all the people came early in the morning to him in the temple to hear him.</span>
