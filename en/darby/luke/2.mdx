<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> But it came to pass in those days that a decree went out from Caesar Augustus, that a census should be made of all the habitable world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> The census itself first took place when Cyrenius had the government of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And all went to be inscribed in the census roll, each to his own city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> and Joseph also went up from Galilee out of the city Nazareth to Judaea, to David's city, the which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> to be inscribed in the census roll with Mary who was betrothed to him &#91;as his&#93; wife, she being great with child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> And it came to pass, while they were there, the days of her giving birth &#91;to her child&#93; were fulfilled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> and she brought forth her first-born son, and wrapped him up in swaddling-clothes and laid him in the manger, because there was no room for them in the inn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> And there were shepherds in that country abiding without, and keeping watch by night over their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And lo, an angel of &#91;the&#93; Lord was there by them, and &#91;the&#93; glory of &#91;the&#93; Lord shone around them, and they feared &#91;with&#93; great fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> And the angel said to them, Fear not, for behold, I announce to you glad tidings of great joy, which shall be to all the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> for to-day a Saviour has been born to you in David's city, who is Christ &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> And this is the sign to you: ye shall find a babe wrapped in swaddling-clothes, and lying in a manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host, praising<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.13 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> Glory to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.14 Elohim">b</a></sup>God in the highest, and on earth peace, good pleasure in men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> And it came to pass, as the angels departed from them into heaven, that the shepherds said to one another, Let us make our way then now as far as Bethlehem, and let us see this thing that is come to pass, which the Lord has made known to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> And they came with haste, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the babe lying in the manger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> and having seen &#91;it&#93; they made known about the country the thing which had been said to them concerning this child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> And all who heard &#91;it&#93; wondered at the things said to them by the shepherds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary kept all these things &#91;in her mind&#93;, pondering &#91;them&#93; in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.20 Elohim">c</a></sup>God for all things which they had heard and seen, as it had been said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> And when eight days were fulfilled for circumcising him, his name was called Jesus, which was the name given by the angel before he had been conceived in the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> And when the days were fulfilled for their purifying according to the law of Moses, they brought him to Jerusalem to present &#91;him&#93; to the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> (as it is written in the law of &#91;the&#93; Lord: Every male that opens the womb shall be called holy to the Lord),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> and to offer a sacrifice according to what is said in the law of &#91;the&#93; Lord: A pair of turtle doves, or two young pigeons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon; and this man was just and pious, awaiting the consolation of Israel, and &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> And it was divinely communicated to him by the Holy Spirit, that he should not see death before he should see &#91;the&#93; Lord's Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> And he came in the Spirit into the temple; and as the parents brought in the child Jesus that they might do for him according to the custom of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> he received him into his arms, and blessed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.28 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> Lord, now thou lettest thy bondman go, according to thy word, in peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> for mine eyes have seen thy salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> which thou hast prepared before the face of all peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> a light for revelation of &#91;the&#93; Gentiles and &#91;the&#93; glory of thy people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> And his father and mother wondered at the things which were said concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> And Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary his mother, Lo, this &#91;child&#93; is set for the fall and rising up of many in Israel, and for a sign spoken against;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> (and even a sword shall go through thine own soul;) so that &#91;the&#93; thoughts may be revealed from many hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> And there was a prophetess, Anna, daughter of Phanuel, of &#91;the&#93; tribe of Asher, who was far advanced in years, having lived with &#91;her&#93; husband seven years from her virginity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalms 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and herself a widow up to eighty-four years; who did not depart from the temple, serving night and day with fastings and prayers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> and she coming up the same hour gave praise to the Lord, and spoke of him to all those who waited for redemption in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And when they had completed all things according to the law of &#91;the&#93; Lord, they returned to Galilee to their own city Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> And the child grew and waxed strong &#91;in spirit&#93;, filled with wisdom, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.40 Elohim">e</a></sup>God's grace was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> And his parents went yearly to Jerusalem at the feast of the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when he was twelve years old, and they went up &#91;to Jerusalem&#93; according to the custom of the feast</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> and had completed the days, as they returned, the boy Jesus remained behind in Jerusalem, and his parents knew not &#91;of it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> but, supposing him to be in the company that journeyed together, they went a day's journey, and sought him among their relations and acquaintances:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and not having found him they returned to Jerusalem seeking him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> And it came to pass, after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers and hearing them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> And all who heard him were astonished at his understanding and answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> And when they saw him they were amazed: and his mother said to him, Child, why hast thou dealt thus with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> And he said to them, Why &#91;is it&#93; that ye have sought me? did ye not know that I ought to be &#91;occupied&#93; in my Father's business?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> And they understood not the thing that he said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> And he went down with them and came to Nazareth, and he was in subjection to them. And his mother kept all these things in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus advanced in wisdom and stature, and in favour with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.52 Elohim">f</a></sup>God and men.</span>
