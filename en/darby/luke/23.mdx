<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> And the whole multitude of them, rising up, led him to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> And they began to accuse him, saying, We have found this &#91;man&#93; perverting our nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, saying that he himself is Christ, a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> And Pilate demanded of him saying, Art thou the king of the Jews? And he answering him said, Thou sayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> And Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, I find no guilt in this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they insisted, saying, He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judaea, beginning from Galilee even on to here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> But Pilate, having heard Galilee &#91;named&#93;, demanded if the man were a Galilaean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> and having learned that he was of Herod's jurisdiction, remitted him to Herod, who himself also was at Jerusalem in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> And when Herod saw Jesus he greatly rejoiced, for he had been a long while desirous of seeing him, because of hearing many things concerning him, and he hoped to see some sign done by him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> and he questioned him in many words, but he answered him nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> And the chief priests and the scribes stood and accused him violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> And Herod with his troops having set him at nought and mocked him, having put a splendid robe upon him, sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> And Pilate and Herod became friends with one another the same day, for they had been at enmity before between themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> And Pilate, having called together the chief priests and the rulers and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> said to them, Ye have brought to me this man as turning away the people &#91;to rebellion&#93;, and behold, I, having examined him before you, have found nothing criminal in this man as to the things of which ye accuse him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> nor Herod either, for I remitted you to him, and behold, nothing worthy of death is done by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> Having chastised him therefore, I will release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> (Now he was obliged to release one for them at the feast.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But they cried out in a mass saying, Away with this &#91;man&#93; and release Barabbas to us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> who was one who, for a certain tumult which had taken place in the city, and &#91;for&#93; murder, had been cast into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Pilate therefore, desirous to release Jesus, again addressed &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> But they cried out in reply saying, Crucify, crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> And he said the third time to them, What evil then has this &#91;man&#93; done? I have found no cause of death in him: I will chastise him therefore and release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> But they were urgent with loud voices, begging that he might be crucified. And their voices &#91;and those of the chief priests&#93; prevailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> And Pilate adjudged that what they begged should take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> And he released him who, for tumult and murder, had been cast into prison, whom they begged for, and Jesus he delivered up to their will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> And as they led him away, they laid hold on a certain Simon, a Cyrenian, coming from the field, and put the cross upon him to bear it behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> And a great multitude of the people, and of women who wailed and lamented him, followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> And Jesus turning round to them said, Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep over me, but weep over yourselves and over your children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> for behold, days are coming in which they will say, Blessed &#91;are&#93; the barren, and wombs that have not borne, and breasts that have not given suck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> Then shall they begin to say to the mountains, Fall upon us; and to the hills, Cover us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> for if these things are done in the green tree, what shall take place in the dry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> Now two others also, malefactors, were led with him to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> And when they came to the place which is called Skull, there they crucified him, and the malefactors, one on the right hand, the other on the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> And Jesus said, Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do. And, parting out his garments, they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the people stood beholding, and the rulers also &#91;with them&#93; sneered, saying, He has saved others; let him save himself if this is the Christ, the chosen one of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.35 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> And the soldiers also made game of him, coming up offering him vinegar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, If thou be the king of the Jews, save thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> And there was also an inscription &#91;written&#93; over him in Greek, and Roman, and Hebrew letters: This is the King of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> Now one of the malefactors who had been hanged spoke insultingly to him, saying, Art not thou the Christ? save thyself and us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other answering rebuked him, saying, Dost thou too not fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.40 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, thou that art under the same judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> and we indeed justly, for we receive the just recompense of what we have done; but this &#91;man&#93; has done nothing amiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> And he said to Jesus, Remember me, &#91;Lord,&#93; when thou comest in thy kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> And Jesus said to him, Verily I say to thee, To-day shalt thou be with me in paradise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> And it was about &#91;the&#93; sixth hour, and there came darkness over the whole land until &#91;the&#93; ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> And the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple rent in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> And Jesus, having cried with a loud voice, said, Father, into thy hands I commit my spirit. And having said this, he expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> Now the centurion, seeing what took place, glorified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.47 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, saying, In very deed this man was just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> And all the crowds who had come together to that sight, having seen the things that took place, returned, beating &#91;their&#93; breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> And all those who knew him stood afar off, the women also who had followed him from Galilee, beholding these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> And behold, a man named Joseph, who was a councillor, a good man and a just</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> (this &#91;man&#93; had not assented to their counsel and deed), of Arimathaea, a city of the Jews, who also waited, &#91;himself also,&#93; for the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.51 Elohim">d</a></sup>God</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> — he having gone to Pilate begged the body of Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> and having taken it down, wrapped it in fine linen and placed him in a tomb hewn in the rock, where no one had ever been laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> And it was preparation day, and &#91;the&#93; sabbath twilight was coming on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> And women, who had come along with him out of Galilee, having followed, saw the sepulchre and how his body was placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And having returned they prepared aromatic spices and ointments, and remained quiet on the sabbath, according to the commandment.</span>
