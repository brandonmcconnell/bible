<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 11:47–53; Matthew 12:14; Luke 20:19"> and the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might kill him; for they feared the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 13:2; Mark 14:10–11; John 13:26–27"> And Satan entered into Judas, who was surnamed Iscariote, being of the number of the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:24; Luke 22:52"> And he went away and spoke with the chief priests and captains as to how he should deliver him up to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:15–16; Acts 1:18; Matthew 27:3–5"> And they were rejoiced, and agreed to give him money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 14:2; Matthew 26:5"> And he came to an agreement to do it, and sought an opportunity to deliver him up to them away from the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:18; Mark 14:12–16; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> And the day of unleavened bread came, in which the passover was to be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; Acts 4:19; Acts 4:13"> And he sent Peter and John, saying, Go and prepare the passover for us, that we may eat &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But they said to him, Where wilt thou that we prepare &#91;it&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:4; Matthew 26:18–19; Acts 8:26–29"> And he said to them, Behold, as ye enter into the city a man will meet you, carrying an earthen pitcher of water; follow him into the house where he goes in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:28; Revelation 3:20; Luke 19:34"> and ye shall say to the master of the house, The Teacher says to thee, Where is the guest-chamber where I may eat the passover with my disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:14–15; Acts 1:13; John 2:25"> And he will shew you a large upper room furnished: there make ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:33; Luke 19:32; John 2:5"> And having gone they found it as he had said to them; and they prepared the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:20; Mark 14:17; Deuteronomy 16:6–7"> And when the hour was come, he placed himself at table, and the &#91;twelve&#93; apostles with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:50; John 4:34; John 17:1"> And he said to them, With desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 14:15; Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30"> For I say unto you, that I will not eat any more at all of it until it be fulfilled in the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.16 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Psalms 116:13; Deuteronomy 8:10"> And having received a cup, when he had given thanks he said, Take this and divide it among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:29; Luke 22:16; Mark 14:25"> For I say unto you, that I will not drink at all of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.18 Elohim">b</a></sup>God come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:23–29; 1 Corinthians 10:16; Mark 14:22–24"> And having taken a loaf, when he had given thanks, he broke &#91;it&#93;, and gave &#91;it&#93; to them, saying, This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; 1 Corinthians 11:25; Zechariah 9:11"> In like manner also the cup, after having supped, saying, This cup &#91;is&#93; the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:26; John 13:21–22"> Moreover, behold, the hand of him that delivers me up &#91;is&#93; with me on the table;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Psalms 109:6–15; Psalms 55:12–15"> and the Son of man indeed goes as it is determined, but woe unto that man by whom he is delivered up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Mark 14:19; Matthew 26:22"> And they began to question together among themselves who then it could be of them who was about to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> And there was also a strife among them which of them should be held to be &#91;the&#93; greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> And he said to them, The kings of the nations rule over them, and they that exercise authority over them are called benefactors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> But ye &#91;shall&#93; not &#91;be&#93; thus; but let the greater among you be as the younger, and the leader as he that serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> For which &#91;is&#93; greater, he that is at table or he that serves? &#91;Is&#93; not he that is at table? But I am in the midst of you as the one that serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> But ye are they who have persevered with me in my temptations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; 2 Timothy 2:12; James 2:5"> And I appoint unto you, as my Father has appointed unto me, a kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> that ye may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Zechariah 3:1; Job 1:6–12"> And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan has demanded to have you, to sift &#91;you&#93; as wheat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> but I have besought for thee that thy faith fail not; and thou, when once thou hast been restored, confirm thy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> And he said to him, Lord, with thee I am ready to go both to prison and to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> And he said, I tell thee, Peter, &#91;the&#93; cock shall not crow to-day before that thou shalt thrice deny that thou knowest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalms 23:1"> And he said to them, When I sent you without purse and scrip and sandals, did ye lack anything? And they said, Nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> He said therefore to them, But now he that has a purse let him take &#91;it&#93;, in like manner also a scrip, and he that has none let him sell his garment and buy a sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> for I say unto you, that this that is written must yet be accomplished in me, And he was reckoned with &#91;the&#93; lawless: for also the things concerning me have an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> And they said, Lord, behold here are two swords. And he said to them, It is enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> And going forth he went according to his custom to the mount of Olives, and the disciples also followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> And when he was at the place he said to them, Pray that ye enter not into temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> And he was withdrawn from them about a stone's throw, and having knelt down he prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> saying, Father, if thou wilt remove this cup from me: — but then, not my will, but thine be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; Psalms 91:11–12"> And an angel appeared to him from heaven strengthening him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 22:12–21"> And being in conflict he prayed more intently. And his sweat became as great drops of blood, falling down upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> And rising up from his prayer, coming to the disciples, he found them sleeping from grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 22:40; Jonah 1:6; Luke 21:34–36"> And he said to them, Why sleep ye? rise up and pray that ye enter not into temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> As he was yet speaking, behold, a crowd, and he that was called Judas, one of the twelve, went on before them, and drew near to Jesus to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> And Jesus said to him, Judas, deliverest thou up the Son of man with a kiss?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> And they who were around him, seeing what was going to follow, said &#91;to him&#93;, Lord, shall we smite with &#91;the&#93; sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> And a certain one from among them smote the bondman of the high priest and took off his right ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> And Jesus answering said, Suffer thus far; and having touched his ear, he healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> And Jesus said to the chief priests and captains of the temple and elders, who were come against him, Have ye come out as against a robber with swords and sticks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> When I was day by day with you in the temple ye did not stretch out your hands against me; but this is your hour and the power of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> And having laid hold on him, they led him &#91;away&#93;, and they led &#91;him&#93; into the house of the high priest. And Peter followed afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> And they having lit a fire in the midst of the court and sat down together, Peter sat among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> And a certain maid, having seen him sitting by the light, and having fixed her eyes upon him, said, And this &#91;man&#93; was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> But he denied &#91;him&#93;, saying, Woman, I do not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> And after a short time another seeing him said, And thou art of them. But Peter said, Man, I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> And after the lapse of about one hour another stoutly maintained it, saying, In truth this &#91;man&#93; also was with him, for also he is a Galilaean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> And Peter said, Man, I know not what thou sayest. And immediately, while he was yet speaking, &#91;the&#93; cock crew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> And the Lord, turning round, looked at Peter; and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he said to him, Before &#91;the&#93; cock crow thou shalt deny me thrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> And Peter, going forth without, wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> And the men who held him mocked him, beating &#91;him&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> and covering him up, asked him saying, Prophesy, who is it that struck thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> And they said many other injurious things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> And when it was day, the elderhood of the people, both &#91;the&#93; chief priests and scribes, were gathered together, and led him into their council, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> If thou art the Christ, tell us. And he said to them, If I tell you, ye will not at all believe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> and if I should ask &#91;you&#93;, ye would not answer me at all, nor let me go;</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Mark 16:19"> but henceforth shall the Son of man be sitting on the right hand of the power of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.69 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> And they all said, Thou then art the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="22.70 Elohim">d</a></sup>God? And he said to them, Ye say that I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> And they said, What need have we any more of witness, for we have heard ourselves out of his mouth?</span>
