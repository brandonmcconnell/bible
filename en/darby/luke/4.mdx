<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Acts 10:38; Mark 1:12–13"> But Jesus, full of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan, and was led by the Spirit in the wilderness</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 4:15; Deuteronomy 9:9"> forty days, tempted of the devil; and in those days he did not eat anything, and when they were finished he hungered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:3; Luke 3:22"> And the devil said to him, If thou be Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, speak to this stone, that it become bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4; Matthew 6:31"> And Jesus answered unto him saying, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Matthew 4:8"> And &#91;the devil&#93;, leading him up into a high mountain, shewed him all the kingdoms of the habitable world in a moment of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 5:19; John 14:30"> And the devil said to him, I will give thee all this power, and their glory; for it is given up to me, and to whomsoever I will I give it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 8:28; Psalms 72:11; Isaiah 45:14"> If therefore thou wilt do homage before me, all &#91;of it&#93; shall be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> And Jesus answering him said, It is written, Thou shalt do homage to &#91;the&#93; Lord thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.8 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, and him alone shalt thou serve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 4:5–6; Luke 4:3; Matthew 8:29"> And he led him to Jerusalem, and set him on the edge of the temple, and said to him, If thou be Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.9 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, cast thyself down hence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Corinthians 11:14"> for it is written, He shall give charge to his angels concerning thee to keep thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:12"> and on &#91;their&#93; hands shall they bear thee, lest in any wise thou strike thy foot against a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Hebrews 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And Jesus answering said to him, It is said, Thou shalt not tempt &#91;the&#93; Lord thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.12 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; James 4:7; Matthew 4:11"> And the devil, having completed every temptation, departed from him for a time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–25; Acts 10:37; Matthew 4:12"> And Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit to Galilee; and a rumour went out into the whole surrounding country about him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 9:8; Matthew 9:35"> and he taught in their synagogues, being glorified of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:2; Acts 13:14–16; Matthew 2:23"> And he came to Nazareth, where he was brought up; and he entered, according to his custom, into the synagogue on the sabbath day, and stood up to read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> And &#91;the&#93; book of the prophet Esaias was given to him; and having unrolled the book he found the place where it was written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; Psalms 146:7–8"> &#91;The&#93; Spirit of &#91;the&#93; Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me to preach glad tidings to &#91;the&#93; poor; he has sent me to preach to captives deliverance, and to &#91;the&#93; blind sight, to send forth &#91;the&#93; crushed delivered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 61:2; Isaiah 63:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> to preach &#91;the&#93; acceptable year of &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 4:17; John 8:2; Luke 19:48"> And having rolled up the book, when he had delivered it up to the attendant, he sat down; and the eyes of all in the synagogue were fixed upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> And he began to say to them, To-day this scripture is fulfilled in your ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; Psalms 45:2; Acts 6:10"> And all bore witness to him, and wondered at the words of grace which were coming out of his mouth. And they said, Is not this the son of Joseph?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> And he said to them, Ye will surely say to me this parable, Physician, heal thyself; whatsoever we have heard has taken place in Capernaum do here also in thine own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; John 4:44; Mark 6:4–5"> And he said, Verily I say to you, that no prophet is acceptable in his &#91;own&#93; country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 5:17; 1 Kings 17:1; Matthew 20:15"> But of a truth I say to you, There were many widows in Israel in the days of Elias, when the heaven was shut up for three years and six months, so that a great famine came upon all the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> and to none of them was Elias sent but to Sarepta of Sidonia, to a woman &#91;that was&#93; a widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 5:1–27; John 17:12; Matthew 12:4"> And there were many lepers in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet, and none of them was cleansed but Naaman the Syrian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> And they were all filled with rage in the synagogue, hearing these things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:59; Acts 21:28–32; Numbers 15:35"> and rising up they cast him forth out of the city, and led him up to the brow of the mountain upon which their city was built, so that they might throw him down the precipice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> but he, passing through the midst of them, went his way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> and descended to Capernaum, a city of Galilee, and taught them on the sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> And they were astonished at his doctrine, for his word was with authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> And there was in the synagogue a man having a spirit of an unclean demon, and he cried with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> saying, Eh! what have we to do with thee, Jesus, Nazarene? hast thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the Holy &#91;One&#93; of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.34 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Hold thy peace, and come out from him. And the demon, having thrown him down into the midst, came out from him without doing him any injury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> And astonishment came upon all, and they spoke to one another, saying, What word &#91;is&#93; this? for with authority and power he commands the unclean spirits, and they come out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> And a rumour went out into every place of the country round concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> And rising up out of the synagogue, he entered into the house of Simon. But Simon's mother-in-law was suffering under a bad fever; and they asked him for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> And standing over her, he rebuked the fever, and it left her; and forthwith standing up she served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> And when the sun went down, all, as many as had persons sick with divers diseases, brought them to him, and having laid his hands on every one of them, he healed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> and demons also went out from many, crying out and saying, Thou art the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.41 Elohim">g</a></sup>God. And rebuking them, he suffered them not to speak, because they knew him to be the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> And when it was day he went out, and went into a desert place, and the crowds sought after him, and came up to him, and &#91;would have&#93; kept him back that he should not go from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> But he said to them, I must needs announce the glad tidings of the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.43 Elohim">h</a></sup>God to the other cities also, for for this I have been sent forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> And he was preaching in the synagogues of Galilee.</span>
