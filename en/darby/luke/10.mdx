<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:7–13; Luke 9:52; Numbers 11:24–26"> Now after these things the Lord appointed seventy others also, and sent them two and two before his face into every city and place where he himself was about to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36–38; John 4:35–38; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> And he said to them, The harvest indeed &#91;is&#93; great, but the workmen few; supplicate therefore the Lord of the harvest that he may send out workmen into his harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Acts 20:29; Ezekiel 2:3–6"> Go: behold I send you forth as lambs in the midst of wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:35; 2 Kings 4:29; Luke 10:4–12"> Carry neither purse nor scrip nor sandals, and salute no one on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Matthew 10:12–13; Isaiah 57:19"> And into whatsoever house ye enter, first say, Peace to this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Ephesians 5:6"> And if a son of peace be there, your peace shall rest upon it; but if not it shall turn to you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Matthew 10:10–11; Galatians 6:6"> And in the same house abide, eating and drinking such things as they have; for the workman is worthy of his hire. Remove not from house to house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; Matthew 10:40; Luke 10:10"> And into whatsoever city ye may enter and they receive you, eat what is set before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; John 3:5; Luke 10:11"> and heal the sick in it, and say to them, The kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.9 Elohim">a</a></sup>God is come nigh to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 10:14; Acts 13:51"> But into whatsoever city ye may have entered and they do not receive you, go out into its streets and say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:9; Acts 13:51; Acts 13:26"> Even the dust of your city, which cleaves to us on the feet, do we shake off against you; but know this, that the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God is come nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:24; Lamentations 4:6"> I say to you that it shall be more tolerable for Sodom in that day than for that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8; Ezekiel 26:1–26"> Woe to thee, Chorazin! woe to thee, Bethsaida! for if the works of power which have taken place in you had taken place in Tyre and Sidon, they had long ago repented, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:22–25; Romans 2:1; Luke 12:47–48"> But it shall be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the judgment than for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Matthew 4:13; Luke 13:28"> And thou, Capernaum, who hast been raised up to heaven, shalt be brought down even to hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:20; Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> He that hears you hears me; and he that rejects you rejects me; and he that rejects me rejects him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Mark 16:17; Luke 9:1"> And the seventy returned with joy, saying, Lord, even the demons are subject to us through thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 12:7–9; Isaiah 14:12; Revelation 9:1"> And he said to them, I beheld Satan as lightning falling out of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:13; Mark 16:18; Romans 16:20"> Behold, I give you the power of treading upon serpents and scorpions and over all the power of the enemy, and nothing shall in anywise injure you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:15; Revelation 20:12; Hebrews 12:23"> Yet in this rejoice not, that the spirits are subjected to you, but rejoice that your names are written in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Matthew 13:11–16; Matthew 11:25–27"> In the same hour Jesus rejoiced in spirit and said, I praise thee, Father, Lord of the heaven and of the earth, that thou hast hid these things from wise and prudent, and hast revealed them to babes: yea, Father, for thus has it been well-pleasing in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:27; John 1:18; John 6:44–46"> All things have been delivered to me by my Father, and no one knows who the Son is but the Father, and who the Father is but the Son, and he to whomsoever the Son is pleased to reveal &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17"> And having turned to the disciples privately he said, Blessed are the eyes which see the things that ye see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For I say to you that many prophets and kings have desired to see the things which ye behold, and did not see &#91;them&#93;; and to hear the things which ye hear, and did not hear &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–39; Luke 18:18; Matthew 19:16–19"> And behold, a certain lawyer stood up tempting him, and saying, Teacher, having done what, shall I inherit life eternal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 3:21–22"> And he said to him, What is written in the law? how readest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 12:30–31; Matthew 22:37–40; Deuteronomy 6:5"> But he answering said, Thou shalt love the Lord thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.27 Elohim">c</a></sup>God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy strength, and with all thine understanding; and thy neighbour as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5; Matthew 19:17"> And he said to him, Thou hast answered right: this do and thou shalt live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Romans 4:2; Leviticus 19:34"> But he, desirous of justifying himself, said to Jesus, And who is my neighbour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 88:4; Luke 18:31; Lamentations 2:12"> And Jesus replying said, A certain man descended from Jerusalem to Jericho and fell into &#91;the hands of&#93; robbers, who also, having stripped him and inflicted wounds, went away leaving him in a half-dead state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Psalms 69:20; Jeremiah 5:31"> And a certain priest happened to go down that way, and seeing him, passed on on the opposite side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 27:10; Acts 18:17; Psalms 109:25"> and in like manner also a Levite, being at the spot, came and looked &#91;at him&#93; and passed on on the opposite side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; Jeremiah 38:7–13; Luke 7:13"> But a certain Samaritan journeying came to him, and seeing &#91;him&#93;, was moved with compassion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 12:20; Psalms 147:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> and came up &#91;to him&#93; and bound up his wounds, pouring in oil and wine; and having put him on his own beast, took him to &#91;the&#93; inn and took care of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 14:13; Romans 16:23"> And on the morrow &#91;as he left&#93;, taking out two denarii he gave them to the innkeeper, and said to him, Take care of him, and whatsoever thou shalt expend more, I will render to thee on my coming back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 7:42; Matthew 21:28–31; Matthew 17:25"> Which &#91;now&#93; of these three seems to thee to have been neighbour of him who fell into &#91;the hands of&#93; the robbers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Micah 6:8; 1 Peter 2:21"> And he said, He that shewed him mercy. And Jesus said to him, Go, and do thou likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:19–20; John 11:1–5; John 12:1–3"> And it came to pass as they went that he entered into a certain village; and a certain woman, Martha by name, received him into her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Luke 8:35; John 12:3"> And she had a sister called Mary, who also, having sat down at the feet of Jesus was listening to his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:27; Luke 12:29; Matthew 14:15"> Now Martha was distracted with much serving, and coming up she said, Lord, dost thou not care that my sister has left me to serve alone? Speak to her therefore that she may help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Luke 21:34; Matthew 6:25–34"> But Jesus answering said to her, Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 16:5–6; John 10:27–28"> but there is need of one, and Mary has chosen the good part, the which shall not be taken from her.</span>
