<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:2; Ezra 3:8; Ezra 3:2"> In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, came the word of Jehovah by the prophet Haggai unto Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:23–2; Nehemiah 4:10; Ecclesiastes 11:4"> Thus speaketh Jehovah of hosts, saying, This people say, The time is not come, the time that Jehovah's house should be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 5:1; Zechariah 1:1"> And the word of Jehovah came by Haggai the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Psalms 132:3–5; Philippians 2:21"> Is it time for you that ye should dwell in your wainscoted houses, while this house lieth waste?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Haggai 1:7"> And now thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Consider your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 1:9; Deuteronomy 28:38–40; Hosea 4:10"> Ye have sown much, and bring in little; ye eat, but are not satisfied; ye drink, but are not filled with drink; ye clothe yourselves, but there is none warm; and he that earneth wages earneth wages for a bag with holes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:59–60; Haggai 1:5; Philippians 3:1"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Consider your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 132:13–14; 2 Chronicles 7:16; Haggai 2:7"> Go up to the mountain and bring wood, and build the house, and I will take pleasure in it, and I will be glorified, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Haggai 2:16–17; Haggai 1:6"> Ye looked for much, and behold it was little; and when ye brought it home, I blew upon it. Wherefore? saith Jehovah of hosts. Because of my house that lieth waste, whilst ye run every man to his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Leviticus 26:19; Deuteronomy 28:23–24"> Therefore over you the heavens withhold their dew, and the earth withholdeth its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Haggai 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:22; 2 Kings 8:1"> And I called for a drought upon the land, and upon the mountains, and upon the corn, and upon the new wine, and upon the oil, and upon that which the ground bringeth forth, and upon man, and upon cattle, and upon all the labour of the hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 50:10; Ezra 5:2; Haggai 1:14"> And Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people, hearkened to the voice of Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and the words of Haggai the prophet, according as Jehovah their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God had sent him, and the people feared before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Matthew 28:20; Malachi 3:1"> Then spoke Haggai, Jehovah's messenger, in Jehovah's message unto the people, saying, I am with you, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:22; Hebrews 13:21; Haggai 2:21"> And Jehovah stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people; and they came and worked at the house of Jehovah of hosts, their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:1"> in the four and twentieth day of the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.</span>
