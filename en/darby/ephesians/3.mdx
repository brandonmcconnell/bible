<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 1:8; Philippians 1:13–16"> For this reason I Paul, prisoner of the Christ Jesus for you nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:25–27; 1 Timothy 1:11; 2 Timothy 1:11"> (if indeed ye have heard of the administration of the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God which has been given to me towards you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; Romans 16:25; Ephesians 1:9–11"> that by revelation the mystery has been made known to me, (according as I have written before briefly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:6"> by which, in reading it, ye can understand my intelligence in the mystery of the Christ,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 3:2; Titus 1:1–3"> which in other generations has not been made known to the sons of men, as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets in &#91;the power of the&#93; Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–29; 1 John 1:3; 1 John 2:25"> that &#91;they who are of&#93; the nations should be joint heirs, and a joint body, and joint partakers of &#91;his&#93; promise in Christ Jesus by the glad tidings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 1:19; Ephesians 3:20; Ephesians 3:2"> of which I am become minister according to the gift of the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God given to me, according to the working of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Ephesians 2:7"> To me, less than the least of all saints, has this grace been given, to announce among the nations the glad tidings of the unsearchable riches of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 13:35; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and to enlighten all &#91;with the knowledge of&#93; what is the administration of the mystery hidden throughout the ages in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, who has created all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> in order that now to the principalities and authorities in the heavenlies might be made known through the assembly the all-various wisdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:4"> according to &#91;the&#93; purpose of the ages, which he purposed in Christ Jesus our Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 5:2; Hebrews 4:14–16"> in whom we have boldness and access in confidence by the faith of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; 2 Corinthians 1:6"> Wherefore I beseech &#91;you&#93; not to faint through my tribulations for you, which is your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> For this reason I bow my knees to the Father &#91;of our Lord Jesus Christ&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> of whom every family in &#91;the&#93; heavens and on earth is named,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> in order that he may give you according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with power by his Spirit in the inner man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 John 4:16; John 14:23"> that the Christ may dwell, through faith, in your hearts, being rooted and founded in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> in order that ye may be fully able to apprehend with all the saints what &#91;is&#93; the breadth and length and depth and height;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> and to know the love of the Christ which surpasses knowledge; that ye may be filled &#91;even&#93; to all the fulness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.19 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> But to him that is able to do far exceedingly above all which we ask or think, according to the power which works in us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> to him be glory in the assembly in Christ Jesus unto all generations of the age of ages. Amen).</span>
