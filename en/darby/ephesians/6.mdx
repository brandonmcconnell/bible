<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:20–25; Proverbs 23:22; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in &#91;the&#93; Lord, for this is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 27:16; Matthew 15:4–6"> Honour thy father and thy mother, which is the first commandment with a promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:28"> that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest be long-lived on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:21; Proverbs 22:6; Proverbs 29:15"> And &#91;ye&#93; fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in &#91;the&#93; discipline and admonition of &#91;the&#93; Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–21; Titus 2:9–10; Colossians 3:17–24"> Bondmen, obey masters according to flesh, with fear and trembling, in simplicity of your heart as to the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:22–23; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:10"> not with eye-service as men-pleasers; but as bondmen of Christ, doing the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God from &#91;the&#93; soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 6:5–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> serving with good will as to the Lord, and not to men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Luke 14:14; Proverbs 11:18"> knowing that whatever good each shall do, this he shall receive of &#91;the&#93; Lord, whether bond or free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:25–1; Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11"> And, masters, do the same things towards them, giving up threatening, knowing that both their and your Master is in heaven, and there is no acceptance of persons with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> For the rest, brethren, be strong in &#91;the&#93; Lord, and in the might of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Romans 13:12; Ephesians 6:13"> Put on the panoply of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, that ye may be able to stand against the artifices of the devil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Ephesians 2:2"> because our struggle is not against blood and flesh, but against principalities, against authorities, against the universal lords of this darkness, against spiritual &#91;power&#93; of wickedness in the heavenlies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Luke 21:36; Ephesians 6:11–17"> For this reason take &#91;to you&#93; the panoply of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and, having accomplished all things, to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> Stand therefore, having girt about your loins with truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and shod your feet with &#91;the&#93; preparation of the glad tidings of peace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; 1 John 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> besides all &#91;these&#93;, having taken the shield of faith with which ye will be able to quench all the inflamed darts of the wicked one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; Revelation 19:15"> Have also the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God's word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; Colossians 4:2; Philippians 4:6"> praying at all seasons, with all prayer and supplication in &#91;the&#93; Spirit, and watching unto this very thing with all perseverance and supplication for all the saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; Acts 4:29; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> and for me in order that utterance may be given to me in &#91;the&#93; opening of my mouth to make known with boldness the mystery of the glad tidings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Colossians 4:4; 2 Timothy 2:9"> for which I am an ambassador &#91;bound&#93; with a chain, that I may be bold in it as I ought to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12; Titus 3:12"> But in order that ye also may know what concerns me, how I am getting on, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister in &#91;the&#93; Lord, shall make all things known to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 2:17"> whom I have sent to you for this very thing, that ye may know of our affairs and that he may encourage your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:16; Galatians 5:6; 1 Peter 5:14"> Peace to the brethren, and love with faith, from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.23 Elohim">e</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Matthew 6:13"> Grace with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruption.</span>
