<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> Be ye therefore imitators of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, as beloved children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> and walk in love, even as the Christ loved us, and delivered himself up for us, an offering and sacrifice to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God for a sweet-smelling savour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> But fornication and all uncleanness or unbridled lust, let it not be even named among you, as it becomes saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> and filthiness and foolish talking, or jesting, which are not convenient; but rather thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> For this ye are &#91;well&#93; informed of, knowing that no fornicator, or unclean person, or person of unbridled lust, who is an idolater, has inheritance in the kingdom of the Christ and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Let no one deceive you with vain words, for on account of these things the wrath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.6 Elohim">d</a></sup>God comes upon the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Be not ye therefore fellow-partakers with them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> for ye were once darkness, but now light in &#91;the&#93; Lord; walk as children of light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> (for the fruit of the light &#91;is&#93; in all goodness and righteousness and truth,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 19:14"> proving what is agreeable to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> and do not have fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather also reprove &#91;them&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> for the things that are done by them in secret it is shameful even to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> But all things having their true character exposed by the light are made manifest; for that which makes everything manifest is light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> Wherefore he says, Wake up, &#91;thou&#93; that sleepest, and arise up from among the dead, and the Christ shall shine upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> See therefore how ye walk carefully, not as unwise but as wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> redeeming the time, because the days are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> For this reason be not foolish, but understanding what &#91;is&#93; the will of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> And be not drunk with wine, in which is debauchery; but be filled with the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalms 95:2"> speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and chanting with your heart to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> giving thanks at all times for all things to him &#91;who is&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.20 Elohim">e</a></sup>God and &#91;the&#93; Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> submitting yourselves to one another in &#91;the&#93; fear of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wives, &#91;submit yourselves&#93; to your own husbands, as to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> for a husband is head of the wife, as also the Christ &#91;is&#93; head of the assembly. He &#91;is&#93; Saviour of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> But even as the assembly is subjected to the Christ, so also wives to their own husbands in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, love your own wives, even as the Christ also loved the assembly, and has delivered himself up for it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> in order that he might sanctify it, purifying &#91;it&#93; by the washing of water by &#91;the&#93; word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> that he might present the assembly to himself glorious, having no spot, or wrinkle, or any of such things; but that it might be holy and blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> So ought men also to love their own wives as their own bodies: he that loves his own wife loves himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> For no one has ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, even as also the Christ the assembly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> for we are members of his body; &#91;we are of his flesh, and of his bones.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> Because of this a man shall leave his father and mother, and shall be united to his wife, and the two shall be one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This mystery is great, but I speak as to Christ, and as to the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> But ye also, every one of you, let each so love his own wife as himself; but as to the wife &#91;I speak&#93; that she may fear the husband.</span>
