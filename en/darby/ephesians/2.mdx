<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Ephesians 2:5–6; Colossians 3:1–4"> and you, being dead in your offences and sins —</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ephesians 2:3"> in which ye once walked according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, the spirit who now works in the sons of disobedience:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:16–24"> among whom we also all once had our conversation in the lusts of our flesh, doing what the flesh and the thoughts willed to do, and were children, by nature, of wrath, even as the rest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Ephesians 1:7; Psalms 86:15"> but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, being rich in mercy, because of his great love wherewith he loved us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:10"> (we too being dead in offences,) has quickened us with the Christ, (ye are saved by grace,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Ephesians 1:3; Romans 6:4–5"> and has raised &#91;us&#93; up together, and has made &#91;us&#93; sit down together in the heavenlies in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:16–17; Titus 3:4; Ephesians 2:4"> that he might display in the coming ages the surpassing riches of his grace in kindness towards us in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 2:5; Romans 4:16"> For ye are saved by grace, through faith; and this not of yourselves; it is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>God's gift:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 11:6; Titus 3:3–5"> not on the principle of works, that no one might boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:10; Hebrews 13:21"> For we are his workmanship, having been created in Christ Jesus for good works, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.10 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has before prepared that we should walk in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Colossians 2:13; Colossians 3:11"> Wherefore remember that ye, once nations in &#91;the&#93; flesh, who &#91;are&#93; called uncircumcision by that called circumcision in &#91;the&#93; flesh done with the hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Colossians 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> that ye were at that time without Christ, aliens from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers to the covenants of promise, having no hope, and without<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.12 Elohim">d</a></sup>God in the world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Colossians 1:20–22; Colossians 1:13–14"> but now in Christ Jesus ye who once were afar off are become nigh by the blood of the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; Colossians 3:15"> For he is our peace, who has made both one, and has broken down the middle wall of enclosure,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 2:14; Galatians 3:10; Colossians 1:21–22"> having annulled the enmity in his flesh, the law of commandments in ordinances, that he might form the two in himself into one new man, making peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–22; Romans 5:10; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and might reconcile both in one body to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.16 Elohim">e</a></sup>God by the cross, having by it slain the enmity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; Acts 10:36; Romans 5:1"> and, coming, he has preached the glad tidings of peace to you who &#91;were&#93; afar off, and &#91;the glad tidings of&#93; peace to those &#91;who were&#93; nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 5:2"> For through him we have both access by one Spirit to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 1 John 3:1; Galatians 6:10"> So then ye are no longer strangers and foreigners, but ye are fellow-citizens of the saints, and of the household of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.19 Elohim">f</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> being built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the corner-stone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 1 Corinthians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> in whom all &#91;the&#93; building fitted together increases to a holy temple in the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:16"> in whom ye also are built together for a habitation of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.22 Elohim">g</a></sup>God in &#91;the&#93; Spirit.</span>
