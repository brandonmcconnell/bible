<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> I, the prisoner in &#91;the&#93; Lord, exhort you therefore to walk worthy of the calling wherewith ye have been called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> with all lowliness and meekness, with long-suffering, bearing with one another in love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> using diligence to keep the unity of the Spirit in the uniting bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> &#91;There is&#93; one body and one Spirit, as ye have been also called in one hope of your calling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God and Father of all, who is over all, and through all, and in us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> But to each one of us has been given grace according to the measure of the gift of the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> Wherefore he says, Having ascended up on high, he has led captivity captive, and has given gifts to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> But that he ascended, what is it but that he also descended into the lower parts of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> He that descended is the same who has also ascended up above all the heavens, that he might fill all things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> and he has given some apostles, and some prophets, and some evangelists, and some shepherds and teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> for the perfecting of the saints; with a view to &#91;the&#93; work of &#91;the&#93; ministry, with a view to the edifying of the body of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> until we all arrive at the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.13 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, at &#91;the&#93; full-grown man, at &#91;the&#93; measure of the stature of the fulness of the Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> in order that we may be no longer babes, tossed and carried about by every wind of that teaching &#91;which is&#93; in the sleight of men, in unprincipled cunning with a view to systematized error;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> but, holding the truth in love, we may grow up to him in all things, who is the head, the Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> from whom the whole body, fitted together, and connected by every joint of supply, according to &#91;the&#93; working in &#91;its&#93; measure of each one part, works for itself the increase of the body to its self-building up in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> This I say therefore, and testify in &#91;the&#93; Lord, that ye should no longer walk as &#91;the rest of&#93; the nations walk in &#91;the&#93; vanity of their mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> being darkened in understanding, estranged from the life of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.18 Elohim">c</a></sup>God by reason of the ignorance which is in them, by reason of the hardness of their hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> who having cast off all feeling, have given themselves up to lasciviousness, to work all uncleanness with greedy unsatisfied lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But ye have not thus learnt the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> if ye have heard him and been instructed in him according as &#91;the&#93; truth is in Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> &#91;namely&#93; your having put off according to the former conversation the old man which corrupts itself according to the deceitful lusts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> and being renewed in the spirit of your mind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and &#91;your&#93; having put on the new man, which according to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.24 Elohim">d</a></sup>God is created in truthful righteousness and holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Wherefore, having put off falsehood, speak truth every one with his neighbour, because we are members one of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> Be angry, and do not sin; let not the sun set upon your wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> neither give room for the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> Let the stealer steal no more, but rather let him toil, working what is honest with &#91;his&#93; hands, that he may have to distribute to him that has need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no corrupt word go out of your mouth, but if &#91;there be&#93; any good one for needful edification, that it may give grace to those that hear &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.30 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, with which ye have been sealed for &#91;the&#93; day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Let all bitterness, and heat of passion, and wrath, and clamour, and injurious language, be removed from you, with all malice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> and be to one another kind, compassionate, forgiving one another, so as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.32 Elohim">f</a></sup>God also in Christ has forgiven you.</span>
