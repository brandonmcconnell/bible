<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1–2"> Paul, apostle of Jesus Christ by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God's will, to the saints and faithful in Christ Jesus who are at Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Galatians 1:3; Titus 1:4"> Grace to you and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God our Father, and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:3; Ephesians 2:6"> Blessed &#91;be&#93; the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenlies in Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30; Ephesians 2:10"> according as he has chosen us in him before &#91;the&#93; world's foundation, that we should be holy and blameless before him in love;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:11; Galatians 4:5–6"> having marked us out beforehand for adoption through Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 1:12; Colossians 1:13"> to &#91;the&#93; praise of &#91;the&#93; glory of his grace, wherein he has taken us into favour in the Beloved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Romans 3:24; 1 John 1:7–9"> in whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of offences, according to the riches of his grace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 5:12; Ephesians 3:10; Romans 5:15"> which he has caused to abound towards us in all wisdom and intelligence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–28; Romans 16:25–27; Ephesians 3:3–9"> having made known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he purposed in himself</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Philippians 2:9–10; Galatians 4:4"> for &#91;the&#93; administration of the fulness of times; to head up all things in the Christ, the things in the heavens and the things upon the earth; in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 Peter 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> in whom we have also obtained an inheritance, being marked out beforehand according to the purpose of him who works all things according to the counsel of his own will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; Ephesians 1:13–14"> that we should be to &#91;the&#93; praise of his glory who have pre-trusted in the Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30; 2 Timothy 2:19"> in whom ye also &#91;have trusted&#93;, having heard the word of the truth, the glad tidings of your salvation; in whom also, having believed, ye have been sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 4:30"> who is &#91;the&#93; earnest of our inheritance to the redemption of the acquired possession to &#91;the&#93; praise of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philemon 1:5; Colossians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> Wherefore I also, having heard of the faith in the Lord Jesus which &#91;is&#93; in you, and the love which &#91;ye have&#93; towards all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:3"> do not cease giving thanks for you, making mention &#91;of you&#93; at my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; James 3:17–18; Colossians 1:9–10"> that the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, would give you &#91;the&#93; spirit of wisdom and revelation in the full knowledge of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Luke 24:45"> being enlightened in the eyes of your heart, so that ye should know what is the hope of his calling, &#91;and&#93; what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 2:12"> and what the surpassing greatness of his power towards us who believe, according to the working of the might of his strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12; Mark 16:19; Psalms 110:1"> &#91;in&#93; which he wrought in the Christ &#91;in&#93; raising him from among &#91;the&#93; dead, and he set him down at his right hand in the heavenlies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Colossians 2:10; Colossians 1:15–16"> above every principality, and authority, and power, and dominion, and every name named, not only in this age, but also in that to come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Colossians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:25–27"> and has put all things under his feet, and gave him &#91;to be&#93; head over all things to the assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:18–19; John 1:16; Colossians 2:9–10"> which is his body, the fulness of him who fills all in all:</span>
