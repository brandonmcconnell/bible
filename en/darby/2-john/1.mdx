<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 3:18; John 8:32"> The elder to &#91;the&#93; elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not I only but also all who have known the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:14; 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:17"> for the truth's sake which abides in us and shall be with us to eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:10; Romans 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:14"> Grace shall be with you, mercy, peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father, and from &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="3 John 1:3–4; Ephesians 5:8; Ephesians 5:2"> I rejoiced greatly that I have found of thy children walking in truth, as we have received commandment from the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 3:11; John 13:34–35; John 15:12"> And now I beseech thee, lady, not as writing to thee a new commandment, but that which we have had from &#91;the&#93; beginning, that we should love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:24; John 15:10"> And this is love, that we should walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, according as ye have heard from the beginning, that ye might walk in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:26; 1 John 4:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22"> For many deceivers have gone out into the world, they who do not confess Jesus Christ coming in flesh — this is the deceiver and the antichrist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Revelation 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:14"> See to yourselves, that we may not lose what we have wrought, but may receive full wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:16–17; Colossians 3:16; 1 John 2:22–24"> Whosoever goes forward and abides not in the doctrine of the Christ has not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God. He that abides in the doctrine, he has both the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:10; Romans 16:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> If any one come to you and bring not this doctrine, do not receive him into &#91;the&#93; house, and greet him not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; Ephesians 5:11; Revelation 18:4"> for he who greets him partakes in his wicked works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="3 John 1:13–14; John 16:12; 1 Corinthians 16:5–7"> Having many things to write to you, I would not with paper and ink; but hope to come to you, and to speak mouth to mouth, that our joy may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:13; 2 John 1:1–13"> The children of thine elect sister greet thee.</span>
