<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Also of the fowl of the heavens by sevens, male and female; to keep seed alive on the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:12; Genesis 7:17"> For in yet seven days I will cause it to rain on the earth forty days and forty nights; and every living being which I have made will I destroy from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 6:22; Luke 8:21; Exodus 39:32"> And Noah did according to all that Jehovah had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 5:32; Genesis 8:13"> And Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters was on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:27; Genesis 6:18; Matthew 24:38"> And Noah went in, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him, into the ark, because of the waters of the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Of clean beasts, and of beasts that are not clean, and of fowl, and of everything that creeps on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 7:16; Genesis 2:19; Jeremiah 8:7"> there came two and two unto Noah into the ark, male and female, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="7.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God had commanded Noah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 6:17; Genesis 7:4; Luke 17:27"> And it came to pass after seven days that the waters of the flood were on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 8:2; Malachi 3:10; Proverbs 8:28–29"> In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that same day all the fountains of the great deep were broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Matthew 4:2; Genesis 7:17"> And the pour of rain was on the earth forty days and forty nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Genesis 7:1; Genesis 6:10"> On the same day went Noah, and Shem and Ham and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife, and the three wives of his sons with them, into the ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 7:2–3"> they, and every beast after its kind, and all the cattle after their kind, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth after its kind, and all fowl after its kind — every bird of every wing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> And they went to Noah, into the ark, two and two of all flesh, in which was the breath of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Luke 13:25; Psalms 91:1–10"> And they that came, came male and female of all flesh, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God had commanded him. And Jehovah shut him in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:12"> And the flood was forty days on the earth. And the waters increased, and bore up the ark; and it was lifted up above the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 69:15; Job 22:16"> And the waters prevailed and increased greatly on the earth; and the ark went on the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:6–9; Psalms 46:2–3; 2 Peter 3:6"> And the waters prevailed exceedingly on the earth; and all the high mountains that are under all the heavens were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:23; Psalms 104:6"> Fifteen cubits upward the waters prevailed; and the mountains were covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 6:13; Genesis 7:4; Genesis 6:17"> And all flesh that moved on the earth expired, fowl as well as cattle, and beasts, and all crawling things which crawl on the earth, and all mankind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Genesis 6:17"> everything which had in its nostrils the breath of life, of all that was on the dry &#91;land&#93;, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 3:20; Hebrews 11:7"> And every living being was destroyed that was on the ground, both man, and cattle, and creeping things, and fowl of the heavens; and they were destroyed from the earth. And Noah alone remained, and what was with him in the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 8:3–4"> And the waters prevailed on the earth a hundred and fifty days.</span>
