<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Genesis 29:14; Exodus 4:9"> And it came to pass at the end of two full years, that Pharaoh dreamed, and behold, he stood by the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 19:6"> And behold, there came up out of the river seven kine, fine-looking and fat-fleshed, and they fed in the reed-grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 41:20–21; Genesis 41:4"> And behold, seven other kine came up after them out of the river, bad-looking and lean-fleshed, and stood by the kine on the bank of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 3:15"> And the kine that were bad-looking and lean-fleshed ate up the seven kine that were fine-looking and fat. And Pharaoh awoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:14"> And he slept and dreamed the second time; and behold, seven ears of corn grew up on one stalk, fat and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Hosea 13:15; Ezekiel 19:12"> And behold, seven ears, thin and parched with the east wind, sprung up after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:5; Genesis 20:3"> And the thin ears devoured the seven fat and full ears. And Pharaoh awoke; and behold, it was a dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Exodus 7:22; Matthew 2:1"> And it came to pass in the morning, that his spirit was troubled; and he sent and called for all the scribes of Egypt, and all the sages who were therein, and Pharaoh told them his dream; but &#91;there was&#93; none to interpret them to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Genesis 40:23; Genesis 40:1–3"> Then spoke the chief of the cup-bearers to Pharaoh, saying, I remember mine offences this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:2–3; Genesis 37:36"> Pharaoh was wroth with his bondmen, and put me in custody into the captain of the life-guard's house, me and the chief of the bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 40:5–8"> And we dreamed a dream in one night, I and he; we dreamed each according to the interpretation of his dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 40:12–19; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 39:1"> And there was there with us a Hebrew youth, a bondman of the captain of the life-guard, to whom we told &#91;them&#93;, and he interpreted to us our dreams; to each he interpreted according to his dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:3; Genesis 40:20–22; Genesis 40:12"> And it came to pass, just as he interpreted to us, so it came about: me has he restored to my office, and him he hanged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:25; Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 105:19–22"> Then Pharaoh sent and called Joseph; and they brought him hastily out of the dungeon. And he shaved &#91;himself&#93;, and changed his clothes, and came in to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:12; Psalms 25:14"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, I have dreamt a dream, and there is none to interpret it. And I have heard say of thee, thou understandest a dream to interpret it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Genesis 40:8; Daniel 2:47"> And Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, It is not in me:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="41.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God will give Pharaoh an answer of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, In my dream, behold, I stood on the bank of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:8; Jeremiah 24:1–3; Jeremiah 24:5"> And behold, there came up out of the river seven kine, fat-fleshed and of fine form, and they fed in the reed-grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And behold, seven other kine came up after them, poor, and very ill-formed, and lean-fleshed — such as I never saw in all the land of Egypt for badness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the lean and bad kine ate up the seven first fat kine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Psalms 37:19; Revelation 10:9–10"> and they came into their belly, and it could not be known that they had come into their belly; and their look was bad, as at the beginning. And I awoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And I saw in my dream, and behold, seven ears came up on one stalk, full and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 129:6–7; Hosea 13:15; 2 Kings 19:26"> And behold, seven ears, withered, thin, parched with the east wind, sprung up after them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8; Exodus 8:19"> and the thin ears devoured the seven good ears. And I told it to the scribes; but there was none to make it known to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 2:45; Revelation 4:1; Ephesians 1:17"> And Joseph said to Pharaoh, The dream of Pharaoh is one. What<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="41.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God will do he has made known to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 41:47; Genesis 41:2; Genesis 41:53"> The seven fine kine are seven years; and the seven good ears are seven years: the dream is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Samuel 24:19"> And the seven lean and bad kine that came up after them are seven years; and the seven empty ears, parched with the east wind, will be seven years of famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 41:25; Genesis 41:16"> This is the word which I have spoken to Pharaoh: what<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="41.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God is about to do he has let Pharaoh see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 41:26; Genesis 41:46–47; Genesis 41:49"> Behold, there come seven years of great plenty throughout the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:13; Genesis 41:54; James 5:17"> And there will arise after them seven years of famine; and all the plenty will be forgotten in the land of Egypt, and the famine will waste away the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 24:20; 1 Samuel 5:6"> And the plenty will not be known afterwards in the land by reason of that famine; for it will be very grievous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 46:10–11; Isaiah 14:24–27"> And as regards the double repetition of the dream to Pharaoh, it is that the thing is established by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="41.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="41.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God will hasten to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 41:39; Acts 6:3; Deuteronomy 1:13"> And now let Pharaoh look himself out a man discreet and wise, and set him over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Luke 16:5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Let Pharaoh do &#91;this&#93;: let him appoint overseers over the land, and take the fifth part of the land of Egypt during the seven years of plenty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 45:6–7; Genesis 41:56; Exodus 4:13"> and let them gather all the food of these coming good years, and lay up corn under the hand of Pharaoh, for food in the cities, and keep &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 47:13–25"> And let the food be as store for the land for the seven years of famine, which will be in the land of Egypt, that the land perish not through the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 7:10; 1 Kings 21:2; 2 Samuel 3:36"> And the word was good in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of all his bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:8; Daniel 5:14; Numbers 27:18"> And Pharaoh said to his bondmen, Shall we find &#91;one&#93; as this, a man in whom the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="41.38 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God is?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:28; Genesis 41:25"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, Since<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="41.39 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God has made all this known to thee, there is none &#91;so&#93; discreet and wise as thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Acts 7:10; Psalms 105:21–22"> Thou shalt be over my house, and according to thy commandment shall all my people regulate themselves; only concerning the throne will I be greater than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Philippians 2:9–11; Genesis 42:6"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, See, I have set thee over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:7; Esther 3:10"> And Pharaoh took off his ring from his hand, and put it on Joseph's hand, and arrayed him in clothes of byssus, and put a gold chain on his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 45:26"> And he caused him to ride in the second chariot that he had; and they cried before him, Bow the knee! and he set him over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:22; Exodus 11:7"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, I am Pharaoh; and without thee shall no man lift up his hand or his foot in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Ezekiel 30:17; Exodus 2:16"> And Pharaoh called Joseph's name Zaphnath-paaneah, and gave him as wife Asnath the daughter of Potipherah the priest in On. And Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 12:6; 1 Samuel 16:21"> And Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from Pharaoh, and passed through the whole land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 26:12; Psalms 72:16"> And in the seven years of plenty the land brought forth by handfuls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 41:34–36; Genesis 47:21"> And he gathered up all the food of the seven years that was in the land of Egypt, and put the food in the cities; the food of the fields of the city, which were round about it, he laid up in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 78:27; Judges 6:5; Genesis 22:17"> And Joseph laid up corn as sand of the sea exceeding much, until they left off numbering; for it was without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48:5; 2 Samuel 8:18"> And to Joseph were born two sons before the year of famine came, whom Asnath the daughter of Potipherah the priest in On bore to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; Proverbs 31:7; Deuteronomy 33:17"> And Joseph called the name of the firstborn Manasseh — For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="41.51 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God has made me forget all my toil, and all my father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 49:22; Genesis 50:23; Genesis 17:6"> And the name of the second he called Ephraim — For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="41.52 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God has caused me to be fruitful in the land of my affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 41:29–31; Psalms 73:20; Luke 16:25"> And the seven years of plenty that were in the land of Egypt were ended;</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 45:11; Acts 7:11; Psalms 105:16"> and the seven years of the dearth began to come, according as Joseph had said. And there was dearth in all lands; but in all the land of Egypt there was bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 17:5; Matthew 3:17; Jeremiah 14:1–6"> And all the land of Egypt suffered from the dearth. And the people cried to Pharaoh for bread; and Pharaoh said to all the Egyptians, Go to Joseph: what he says to you, that do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Zechariah 5:3; Luke 21:35"> And the famine was on all the earth. And Joseph opened every place in which there was &#91;provision&#93;, and sold grain to the Egyptians; and the famine was grievous in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Genesis 41:54; Genesis 42:5; Genesis 41:56"> And all countries came into Egypt to Joseph, to buy &#91;grain&#93;, because the famine was grievous on the whole earth.</span>
