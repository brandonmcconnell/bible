<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:12; Joshua 7:10; 2 Kings 8:3–4"> And Jacob saw that there was grain in Egypt, and Jacob said to his sons, Why do ye look one upon another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 43:8; Genesis 45:9; Genesis 43:2"> And he said, Behold, I have heard that there is grain in Egypt; go down thither and buy &#91;grain&#93; for us from thence, in order that we may live, and not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:5; Genesis 42:13"> And Joseph's ten brethren went down to buy &#91;grain&#93; out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 42:38; Genesis 43:29; Genesis 11:4"> But Benjamin, Joseph's brother, Jacob sent not with his brethren; for he said, Lest mischief may befall him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:57; Acts 7:11; Genesis 12:10"> So the sons of Israel came to buy &#91;grain&#93; among those that came; for the famine was in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 37:7–10; Genesis 41:40–41"> And Joseph, he was the governor over the land — he it was that sold &#91;the corn&#93; to all the people of the land. And Joseph's brethren came and bowed down to him, the face to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 42:30; Genesis 42:14–17; Matthew 15:23–26"> And Joseph saw his brethren, and knew them; but he made himself strange to them, and spoke roughly to them, and said to them, Whence come ye? And they said, From the land of Canaan, to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 37:2; John 20:14; Luke 24:16"> And Joseph knew his brethren, but they did not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 37:5–9; Exodus 32:35; Joshua 2:1"> And Joseph remembered the dreams that he had dreamt of them; and he said to them, Ye are spies: to see the exposed places of the land ye are come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 37:8; 1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 27:37"> And they said to him, No, my lord; but to buy food are thy servants come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 42:19; 2 Corinthians 6:4; John 7:18"> We are all one man's sons; we are honest: thy servants are not spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he said to them, No; but to see the exposed places of the land are ye come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:30; Genesis 42:32; Genesis 44:20"> And they said, Thy servants were twelve brethren, sons of one man, in the land of Canaan; and behold, the youngest is this day with our father, and one is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Job 13:24; Genesis 42:9–11"> And Joseph said to them, That is it that I have spoken to you, saying, Ye are spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; Jeremiah 5:7; Genesis 42:16"> By this ye shall be put to the proof: as Pharaoh lives, ye shall not go forth hence, unless your youngest brother come hither!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> Send one of you, that he may fetch your brother, but ye shall be imprisoned, and your words shall be put to the proof, whether the truth is in you; and if not, as Pharaoh lives, ye are spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 40:4; Hebrews 12:10; Genesis 40:7"> And he put them in custody three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:43; Nehemiah 5:15; Luke 18:2"> And Joseph said to them the third day, This do, that ye may live: I fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="42.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 45:23; Isaiah 42:7; Genesis 40:3"> If ye are honest, let one of your brethren remain bound in the house of your prison, but go ye, carry grain for the hunger of your households;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; Genesis 42:34"> and bring your youngest brother to me, in order that your words be verified, and that ye may not die. And they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Mark 9:43–45; Genesis 37:23–28"> Then they said one to another, We are indeed guilty concerning our brother, whose anguish of soul we saw when he besought us, and we did not hearken; therefore this distress is come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:22; Psalms 9:12; 1 Kings 2:32"> And Reuben answered them, saying, Did I not speak to you, saying, Do not sin against the lad? But ye did not hearken; and now behold, his blood also is required.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 16:13–14"> And they did not know that Joseph understood, for the interpreter was between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 43:30; Jude 1:22–23; Hebrews 4:15"> And he turned away from them, and wept. And he returned to them, and spoke to them, and took Simeon from among them, and bound him before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–21; Genesis 45:21"> And Joseph gave orders to fill their vessels with corn, and to restore every man's money into his sack, and to give them provision for the way. And thus did they to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they loaded their asses with their grain, and departed thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Luke 2:7; Luke 10:34"> And one of them opened his sack to give his ass food in the inn, and saw his money, and behold, it was in the mouth of his sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:37; Genesis 42:36"> And he said to his brethren, My money is returned &#91;to me&#93;, and behold, it is even in my sack. And their heart failed &#91;them&#93;, and they were afraid, saying one to another, What is this &#91;that&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="42.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God has done to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they came into the land of Canaan, to Jacob their father, and told him all that had befallen them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:7"> The man, the lord of the land, spoke roughly to us, and treated us as spies of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> And we said to him, We are honest; we are not spies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> we are twelve brethren, sons of our father; one is not, and the youngest is this day with our father in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 42:19–20; Genesis 42:15"> And the man, the lord of the land, said to us, Hereby shall I know that ye are honest: leave one of your brethren with me, and take &#91;for&#93; the hunger of your households, and go,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 34:21; 1 Kings 10:15"> and bring your youngest brother to me, and I shall know that ye are not spies, but are honest. Your brother will I give up to you; and ye may trade in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 43:21; Genesis 43:12; Genesis 43:15"> And it came to pass as they emptied their sacks, that behold, every man had his bundle of money in his sack; and they saw their bundles of money, they and their father, and were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 43:14; Romans 8:31; Isaiah 27:9"> And Jacob their father said to them, Ye have bereaved me of children: Joseph is not, and Simeon is not, and ye will take Benjamin! All these things are against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; Micah 6:7; Genesis 43:9"> And Reuben spoke to his father, saying, Slay my two sons if I bring him not back to thee: give him into my hand, and I will bring him to thee again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Genesis 42:4; Genesis 37:33"> But he said, My son shall not go down with you, for his brother is dead, and he alone is left; and if mischief should befall him by the way in which ye go, then would ye bring down my grey hairs with sorrow to Sheol.</span>
