<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Kings 4:8; Proverbs 13:20"> And it came to pass at that time, that Judah went down from his brethren, and turned in to a man of Adullam whose name was Hirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 24:3; Genesis 6:2"> And Judah saw there the daughter of a Canaanitish man whose name was Shua; and he took her, and went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And she conceived and bore a son; and he called his name Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And she again conceived and bore a son, and she called his name Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Genesis 38:26; Genesis 46:12"> And again she bore a son, and she called his name Shelah; and he was at Chezib when she bore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 21:21; Matthew 1:3"> And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:19"> And Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in the sight of Jehovah, and Jehovah slew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Matthew 22:23–27; Ruth 4:5–11"> Then Judah said to Onan, Go in to thy brother's wife, and fulfil to her the brother-in-law's duty, and raise up seed to thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Deuteronomy 25:6; James 3:16"> But when Onan knew that the seed should not be his own, it came to pass when he went in to his brother's wife, that he spilled &#91;it&#93; on the ground, in order to give no seed to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Numbers 11:1; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> And the thing which he did was evil in the sight of Jehovah, and he slew him also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:13; Ruth 1:11–13"> And Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, Remain a widow in thy father's house, until Shelah my son is grown; for he said, Lest he die also, as his brethren. And Tamar went and remained in her father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; 2 Samuel 13:39; Judges 14:1"> And as the days were multiplied, Judah's wife, the daughter of Shua, died. And Judah was comforted, and he went up to his sheep-shearers, to Timnah, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it was told Tamar, saying, Behold thy father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 38:26; Genesis 38:11–13; Jeremiah 3:2"> And she put the garments of her widowhood off from her, and covered herself with a veil, and wrapped herself, and sat in the entry of Enaim, which is on the way to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah was grown, and she was not given to him as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Judah saw her, and took her for a harlot; because she had covered her face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 26:15; 2 Samuel 13:11"> And he turned aside to her by the way, and said, Come, I pray thee, let me go in to thee; for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, What wilt thou give me, that thou mayest come in to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33; Genesis 38:20; Proverbs 20:16"> And he said, I will send &#91;thee&#93; a kid of the goats from the flock. And she said, Wilt thou give &#91;me&#93; a pledge, until thou send it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 38:25–26; Hosea 4:11; Jeremiah 22:24"> And he said, What pledge shall I give thee? And she said, Thy signet, and thy lace, and thy staff which is in thy hand. And he gave &#91;it&#93; her, and went in to her; and she conceived by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 38:14; 2 Samuel 14:5; 2 Samuel 14:2"> And she arose and went away; and she laid by her veil from her, and put on the garments of her widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Leviticus 19:17; 2 Samuel 13:3"> And Judah sent the kid of the goats by the hand of his friend the Adullamite, to receive the pledge from the woman's hand; but he found her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he asked the men of her place, saying, Where is the prostitute that was at Enaim, by the way-side? And they said, There was no prostitute here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he returned to Judah, and said, I have not found her; and also the men of the place said, No prostitute has been here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Proverbs 6:33; Revelation 16:15"> Then Judah said, Let her take &#91;it&#93; for herself, lest we be put to shame. Behold, I sent this kid, and thou hast not found her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 21:9; Ezekiel 23:19; Judges 19:2"> And it came to pass about three months after, that it was told Judah, saying, Tamar thy daughter-in-law has committed fornication, and behold, she is also with child by fornication. And Judah said, Bring her forth, that she may be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 37:32; Genesis 38:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> When she was brought forth, she sent to her father-in-law, saying, By the man to whom these &#91;belong&#93; am I with child; and she said, Acknowledge, I pray thee, whose are this signet, and this lace, and this staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:17; John 8:9; Titus 2:11–12"> And Judah acknowledged &#91;them&#93;, and said, She is more righteous than I, because I have not given her to Shelah my son. And he knew her again no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 25:24"> And it came to pass at the time of her delivery, that behold, twins were in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it came to pass when she brought forth, that one stretched out &#91;his&#93; hand, and the midwife took it and bound round his hand a scarlet thread, saying, This came out first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3; Genesis 46:12"> And it came to pass as he drew back his hand, that behold, his brother came out; and she said, How hast thou broken forth! on thee be the breach! And they called his name Pherez.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:6; 1 Chronicles 2:4; Matthew 1:3"> And afterwards came out his brother, round whose hand was the scarlet thread; and they called his name Zerah.</span>
