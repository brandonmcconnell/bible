<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Colossians 3:10; Genesis 1:26–27"> This is the book of Adam's generations. In the day that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God created man, in the likeness of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God made he him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Mark 10:6; Genesis 2:23"> Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalms 51:5"> And Adam lived a hundred and thirty years, and begot &#91;a son&#93; in his likeness, after his image, and called his name Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> And the days of Adam after he had begotten Seth were eight hundred years; and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:8; Genesis 5:11; Genesis 3:19"> And all the days of Adam that he lived were nine hundred and thirty years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> And Seth lived a hundred and five years, and begot Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Seth lived after he had begotten Enosh eight hundred and seven years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; 1 Chronicles 1:2"> And Enosh lived ninety years, and begot Cainan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Enosh lived after he had begotten Cainan eight hundred and fifteen years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the days of Enosh were nine hundred and five years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Cainan lived seventy years, and begot Mahalaleel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Cainan lived after he had begotten Mahalaleel eight hundred and forty years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:2"> And Mahalaleel lived sixty-five years, and begot Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Mahalaleel lived after he had begotten Jared eight hundred and thirty years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And all the days of Mahalaleel were eight hundred and ninety-five years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; 1 Chronicles 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> And Jared lived a hundred and sixty-two years, and begot Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Jared lived after he had begotten Enoch eight hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Enoch lived sixty-five years, and begot Methushelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> And Enoch walked with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God after he had begotten Methushelah three hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 11:5–6; 1 John 1:7; Genesis 5:22"> And Enoch walked with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God; and he was not, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God took him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> And Methushelah lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, and begot Lemech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Methushelah lived after he had begotten Lemech seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And all the days of Methushelah were nine hundred and sixty-nine years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Lemech lived a hundred and eighty-two years, and begot a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> And he called his name Noah, saying, This &#91;one&#93; shall comfort us concerning our work and concerning the toil of our hands, because of the ground which Jehovah has cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Lemech lived after he had begotten Noah five hundred and ninety-five years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And all the days of Lemech were seven hundred and seventy-seven years; and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 6:10; Genesis 10:21; Genesis 9:18–19"> And Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah begot Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
