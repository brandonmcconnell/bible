<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 31:13; Hosea 12:4; Psalms 91:15"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="35.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God said to Jacob, Arise, go up to Bethel, and dwell there, and make there an altar unto the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="35.1 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God that appeared unto thee when thou fleddest from the face of Esau thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:14; Exodus 19:10; Ezekiel 18:31"> And Jacob said to his household, and to all that were with him, Put away the strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="35.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods that are among you, and cleanse yourselves, and change your garments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 32:7; Genesis 31:3"> and we will arise, and go up to Bethel; and I will make there an altar to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="35.3 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God that answered me in the day of my distress, and was with me in the way that I went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Deuteronomy 7:25; Exodus 32:20"> And they gave to Jacob all the strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="35.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>gods that were in their hand, and the rings that were in their ears, and Jacob hid them under the terebinth that &#91;is&#93; by Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; 2 Chronicles 17:10; Joshua 5:1"> And they journeyed; and the terror of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="35.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God was upon the cities that were round about them, and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 28:22; Genesis 12:8"> And Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan, that is, Bethel, he and all the people that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:13"> And he built there an altar, and called the place El-beth-el; because there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="35.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God had appeared to him when he fled from the face of his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:59; 1 Samuel 31:13; Judges 2:5"> And Deborah, Rebecca's nurse, died; and she was buried beneath Bethel, under the oak; and the name of it was called Allon-bachuth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 35:1; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 26:2"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="35.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God appeared to Jacob again after he had come from Padan-Aram, and blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:5; Genesis 32:27–28; Genesis 17:15"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="35.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God said to him, Thy name is Jacob: thy name shall not henceforth be called Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name. And he called his name Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 22:17"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="35.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God said to him, I am the Almighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="35.11 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">k</a></sup>God: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee; and kings shall come out of thy loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:3–4"> And the land that I gave Abraham and Isaac, to thee will I give it, and to thy seed after thee will I give the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Genesis 18:33; Judges 6:21"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="35.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God went up from him in the place where he had talked with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–19; 1 Samuel 7:12; Exodus 17:15"> And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where he had talked with him, a pillar of stone, and poured on it a drink-offering, and poured oil on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 28:19"> And Jacob called the name of the place where<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="35.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God had talked with him, Beth-el.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; 2 Kings 5:19"> And they journeyed from Bethel. And there was yet a certain distance to come to Ephrath, when Rachel travailed in childbirth; and it went hard with her in her childbearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:24; 1 Samuel 4:19–21"> And it came to pass when it went hard with her in her childbearing, that the midwife said to her, Fear not; for this also is a son for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Acts 7:59; Luke 23:46"> And it came to pass as her soul was departing — for she died — that she called his name Benoni; but his father called him Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; Ruth 4:11"> And Rachel died, and was buried on the way to Ephrath, which &#91;is&#93; Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; Genesis 35:14; 2 Samuel 18:17–18"> And Jacob erected a pillar upon her grave: that is the pillar of Rachel's grave to &#91;this&#93; day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 4:8; Luke 2:8"> And Israel journeyed, and spread his tent on the other side of Migdal-Eder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:1; Leviticus 18:8; 2 Samuel 20:3"> And it came to pass when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine; and Israel heard of it. And the sons of Jacob were twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 30:18–20; Genesis 46:8–15; Genesis 29:32–35"> The sons of Leah: Reuben — Jacob's firstborn — and Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 35:16–18"> The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 46:23–25; Genesis 37:2; Genesis 30:4–8"> And the sons of Bilhah, Rachel's maidservant: Dan and Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 30:9–13; Genesis 46:16–18; Genesis 25:20"> And the sons of Zilpah, Leah's maidservant: Gad and Asher. These are the sons of Jacob that were born to him in Padan-Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 18:1"> And Jacob came to Isaac his father to Mamre — to Kirjath-Arba, which is Hebron; where Abraham had sojourned, and Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 25:7; Genesis 50:26; Genesis 47:28"> And the days of Isaac were a hundred and eighty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 49:33; Genesis 49:31"> And Isaac expired and died, and was gathered to his peoples, old and full of days. And his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.</span>
