<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; Psalms 49:16–17; Job 31:31"> And he heard the words of Laban's sons, saying, Jacob has taken away all that was our father's, and of what was our father's he has acquired all this glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; 1 Samuel 18:9–11; Genesis 4:5"> And Jacob saw the countenance of Laban, and behold, it was not toward him as previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 32:9; Hebrews 13:5"> And Jehovah said to Jacob, Return into the land of thy fathers, and to thy kindred; and I will be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Jacob sent and called Rachel and Leah to the fields to his flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:2–3; Genesis 31:53; Genesis 48:15"> and said to them, I see your father's countenance, that it is not toward me as previously; but the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="31.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of my father has been with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 30:29; Ephesians 6:5–8; Titus 2:9–10"> And you know that with all my power I have served your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; Genesis 31:29; Numbers 14:22"> And your father has mocked me, and has changed my wages ten times; but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="31.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God suffered him not to hurt me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 30:32"> If he said thus; The speckled shall be thy hire, then all the flocks bore speckled; and if he said thus: The ringstraked shall be thy hire, then all the flocks bore ringstraked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:1; Genesis 31:16; Proverbs 13:22"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="31.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God has taken away the cattle of your father, and given &#91;them&#93; to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 30:39; Genesis 20:6"> And it came to pass at the time of the ardour of the flocks, that I lifted up mine eyes, and saw in a dream, and behold, the rams that leaped upon the flocks were ringstraked, speckled, and spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Genesis 31:13; 1 Samuel 3:6"> And the Angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="31.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God said to me in a dream, Jacob! And I said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Deuteronomy 24:15; Genesis 31:42"> And he said, Lift up now thine eyes, and see: all the rams that leap upon the flock are ringstraked, speckled, and spotted; for I have seen all that Laban does to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:3; Genesis 32:9; Genesis 28:12–22"> I am the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="31.13 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God of Bethel, where thou anointedst the pillar, where thou vowedst a vow to me. Now arise, depart out of this land, and return to the land of thy kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 2:24; Genesis 29:24"> And Rachel and Leah answered and said to him, Is there yet any portion or inheritance for us in our father's house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:15–20; Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:27–30"> Are we not reckoned of him strangers? for he has sold us, and has even constantly devoured our money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:35–1; Genesis 31:9; Psalms 45:10"> For all the wealth that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="31.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God has taken from our father is ours and our children's; and now whatever<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="31.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God has said to thee do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; Genesis 24:61; Genesis 24:10"> And Jacob rose up, and set his sons and his wives upon camels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:1–2; Genesis 27:41; Genesis 35:27–29"> and carried away all his cattle, and all his property that he had acquired — the cattle of his possessions that he had acquired in Padan-Aram, to go to Isaac his father, into the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:13; Genesis 31:30; Hosea 3:4"> And Laban had gone to shear his sheep. And Rachel stole the teraphim that &#91;belonged&#93; to her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Jacob deceived Laban the Syrian, in that he did not tell him that he fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 46:28"> And he fled with all that he had; and he rose up and passed over the river, and set his face &#91;toward&#93; mount Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:12–13; Genesis 30:36; Exodus 14:5–31"> And it was told Laban on the third day that Jacob had fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 24:27; Exodus 2:11"> And he took his brethren with him, and pursued after him seven days' journey, and overtook him on mount Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:50; Genesis 31:29; Genesis 20:3"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="31.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God came to Laban the Syrian in a dream by night, and said to him, Take care thou speak not to Jacob either good or bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 33:18; Hebrews 11:9"> And Laban came up with Jacob; and Jacob had pitched his tent on the mountain; Laban also with his brethren pitched on mount Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:2; Genesis 12:18; Genesis 20:9–10"> And Laban said to Jacob, What hast thou done, that thou hast deceived me, and hast carried away my daughters as captives of war?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Proverbs 26:23–26; Genesis 31:3–5"> Why didst thou flee away covertly, and steal away from me, and didst not tell me, that I might have conducted thee with mirth and with songs, with tambour and with harp;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 31:55; Ruth 1:14; Acts 20:37"> and hast not suffered me to kiss my sons and my daughters? Now thou hast acted foolishly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Genesis 31:42; Genesis 31:24"> It would be in the power of my hand to do you hurt; but the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="31.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of your father spoke to me last night, saying, Take care that thou speak not to Jacob either good or bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:24"> And now that thou must needs be gone, because thou greatly longedst after thy father's house, why hast thou stolen my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="31.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 31:26–27; Genesis 20:11"> And Jacob answered and said to Laban, I was afraid; for I said, Lest thou shouldest take by force thy daughters from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3–5; Genesis 31:30; Genesis 31:19"> With whomsoever thou findest thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="31.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>gods, he shall not live. Before our brethren discern what is thine with me, and take &#91;it&#93; to thee. But Jacob did not know that Rachel had stolen them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:28"> And Laban went into Jacob's tent, and into Leah's tent, and into the two handmaids' tents, and found nothing; and he went out of Leah's tent, and entered into Rachel's tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Genesis 31:17"> Now Rachel had taken the teraphim and put them under the camel's saddle; and she sat upon them. And Laban explored all the tent, but found nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Exodus 20:12; 1 Peter 2:18"> And she said to her father, Let it not be an occasion of anger in the eyes of my lord that I cannot rise up before thee, for it is with me after the manner of women. And he searched carefully, but did not find the teraphim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Genesis 49:7; Numbers 16:15"> And Jacob was angry, and he disputed with Laban. And Jacob answered and said to Laban, What is my fault, what my sin, that thou hast so hotly pursued after me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Corinthians 6:4–5; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> Whereas thou hast explored all my baggage, what hast thou found of all thy household stuff? Set &#91;it&#93; here before my brethren and thy brethren, and let them decide between us both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Deuteronomy 28:4; Ezekiel 34:2–4"> These twenty years have I been with thee: thy ewes and thy she-goats have not cast their young, and the rams of thy flock I have not eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Exodus 22:10–13; Leviticus 22:8"> What was torn I have not brought to thee; I had to bear the loss of it: of my hand hast thou required it, &#91;whether&#93; stolen by day or stolen by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:8; Exodus 2:19–22; 1 Peter 5:2–4"> Thus it was with me: in the day the heat consumed me, and the frost by night; and my sleep fled from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> I have been these twenty years in thy house: I have served thee fourteen years for thy two daughters, and six years for thy flock; and thou hast changed my wages ten times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 31:7; Genesis 31:53; Isaiah 8:13"> Had not the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="31.42 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God of my father, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="31.42 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God of Abraham, and the fear of Isaac, been with me, it is certain thou wouldest have sent me away now empty.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="31.42 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God has looked upon my affliction and the labour of my hands, and has judged last night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Laban answered and said to Jacob, The daughters are my daughters, and the sons are my sons, and the flock is my flock, and all that thou seest is mine; but as for my daughters, what can I do this day to them, or to their sons whom they have brought forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:25–27; Joshua 22:27"> And now, come, let us make a covenant, I and thou; and let it be a witness between me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–22"> And Jacob took a stone, and set it up &#91;for&#93; a pillar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:5; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:54"> And Jacob said to his brethren, Gather stones. And they took stones, and made a heap, and ate there upon the heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1"> And Laban called it Jegar-sahadutha, and Jacob called it Galeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Joshua 13:8–9; Genesis 31:23"> And Laban said, This heap is a witness between me and thee this day. Therefore was the name of it called Galeed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> — and Mizpah; for he said, Let Jehovah watch between me and thee, when we shall be hidden one from another:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Jeremiah 29:23; Micah 1:2"> if thou shouldest afflict my daughters, or if thou shouldest take wives besides my daughters, — no man is with us; see,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="31.50 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God is witness between me and thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And Laban said to Jacob, Behold this heap, and behold the pillar which I have set up between me and thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 31:48; Genesis 31:44–45"> &#91;let&#93; this heap be witness, and the pillar a witness, that neither I pass this heap &#91;to go&#93; to thee, nor thou pass this heap and this pillar &#91;to come&#93; to me, for harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Genesis 16:5; Joshua 24:2"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="31.53 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God of Abraham, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="31.53 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God of Nahor, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="31.53 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">r</a></sup>God of their father, judge between us! And Jacob swore by the fear of his father Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 26:30"> And Jacob offered a sacrifice upon the mountain, and invited his brethren to eat bread: and they ate bread, and lodged on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 31:28; Deuteronomy 23:5; Genesis 18:33"> And Laban rose early in the morning, and kissed his sons and his daughters, and blessed them; and Laban went and returned to his place.</span>
