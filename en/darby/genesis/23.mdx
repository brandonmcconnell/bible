<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 17:17"> And the life of Sarah was a hundred and twenty-seven years: &#91;these were&#93; the years of the life of Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 23:19; Genesis 13:18; 1 Chronicles 6:57"> And Sarah died in Kirjath-Arba: that is Hebron, in the land of Canaan. And Abraham came to mourn for Sarah, and to weep for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 10:15; Genesis 23:7; 1 Samuel 26:6"> And Abraham rose up from before his dead, and spoke to the sons of Heth, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 17:8"> I am a stranger and a sojourner with you; give me a possession of a sepulchre with you, that I may bury my dead from before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the sons of Heth answered Abraham, saying to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 14:14; Exodus 32:22"> Hear us, my lord: thou art a prince of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God among us: in the choicest of our sepulchres bury thy dead: none of us shall withhold from thee his sepulchre for burying thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Genesis 19:1; Proverbs 18:24"> And Abraham rose up, and bowed down to the people of the land, to the sons of Heth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Kings 2:17"> and spoke to them, saying, If it be your will that I should bury my dead from before me, hear me, and entreat for me Ephron the son of Zohar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 13:8; Romans 12:17"> that he may give me the cave of Machpelah, which is his, which is at the end of his field; for the full money let him give it to me amongst you for a possession of a sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 34:20; Genesis 34:24; Genesis 23:18"> And Ephron was dwelling among the sons of Heth. And Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham, in the ears of the sons of Heth, &#91;even&#93; of all that went in at the gate of his city, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:24; Isaiah 32:8; Deuteronomy 19:15"> No, my lord: hear me. The field give I thee; and the cave that is in it, to thee I give it; before the eyes of the sons of my people give I it thee: bury thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 23:7; Genesis 18:2; Genesis 19:1"> And Abraham bowed down before the people of the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:8; 2 Samuel 24:24; Genesis 14:22–23"> and he spoke to Ephron, in the ears of the people of the land, saying, But if only thou wouldst listen to me, I give the money for the field: take &#91;it&#93; of me, and I will bury my dead there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Ephron answered Abraham, saying to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:12; Exodus 30:13; Exodus 30:15"> My lord, hearken to me. A field of four hundred shekels of silver, what is that between me and thee? bury therefore thy dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 11:12; Jeremiah 32:9; Exodus 30:13"> And Abraham hearkened to Ephron; and Abraham weighed to Ephron the money that he had named in the ears of the sons of Heth — four hundred shekels of silver, current with the merchant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 23:20"> So the field of Ephron, which was at Machpelah, which was before Mamre, the field, and the cave that was in it, and all the trees that were in the field, that were in all its borders round about,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Jeremiah 32:12; Genesis 34:20"> were assured to Abraham for a possession before the eyes of the sons of Heth, before all who went in at the gate of his city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:5"> And after this, Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the cave of the field at Machpelah, opposite to Mamre: that is Hebron, in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–14; Ruth 4:7–10; Genesis 49:31–32"> And the field and the cave that was in it were assured to Abraham for a possession of a sepulchre by the sons of Heth.</span>
