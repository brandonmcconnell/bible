<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> And it came to pass when mankind began to multiply on the earth, and daughters were born to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> that the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God saw the daughters of men that they were fair, and took themselves wives of all that they chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> And Jehovah said, My Spirit shall not always plead with Man; for he indeed is flesh; but his days shall be a hundred and twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> In those days were the giants on the earth, and also afterwards, when the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God had come in to the daughters of men, and they had borne &#91;children&#93; to them; these were the heroes, who of old were men of renown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> And Jehovah saw that the wickedness of Man was great on the earth, and every imagination of the thoughts of his heart only evil continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> And Jehovah repented that he had made Man on the earth, and it grieved him in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> And Jehovah said, I will destroy Man, whom I have created, from the earth — from man to cattle, to creeping things, and to fowl of the heavens; for I repent that I have made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> But Noah found favour in the eyes of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> This is the history of Noah. Noah was a just man, perfect amongst his generations: Noah walked with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> And Noah begot three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> And the earth was corrupt before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="6.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and the earth was full of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="6.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God looked upon the earth, and behold, it was corrupt; for all flesh had corrupted its way on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="6.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God said to Noah, The end of all flesh is come before me, for the earth is full of violence through them; and behold, I will destroy them with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make thyself an ark of gopher wood: &#91;with&#93; cells shalt thou make the ark; and pitch it inside and outside with pitch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> And thus shalt thou make it: let the length of the ark be three hundred cubits, the breadth of it fifty cubits, and the height of it thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> A light shalt thou make to the ark; and to a cubit high shalt thou finish it above. And the door of the ark shalt thou set in its side: &#91;with&#93; a lower, second, and third &#91;story&#93; shalt thou make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> For I, behold, I bring a flood of waters on the earth, to destroy all flesh under the heavens in which is the breath of life: everything that is on the earth shall expire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> But with thee will I establish my covenant; and thou shalt go into the ark, thou, and thy sons, and thy wife, and thy sons' wives with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> And of every living thing of all flesh, two of every &#91;sort&#93; shalt thou bring into the ark, to keep &#91;them&#93; alive with thee: they shall be male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> Of fowl after their kind, and of the cattle after their kind, of every creeping thing of the ground after its kind, two of each shall go in to thee, to keep &#91;them&#93; alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> And take thou of all food that is eaten, and gather &#91;it&#93; to thee, that it may be for food for thee and for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> And Noah did it; according to all that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="6.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God had commanded him, so did he.</span>
