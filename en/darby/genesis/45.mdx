<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15; Acts 10:41; Genesis 43:30–31"> And Joseph could not control himself before all them that stood by him, and he cried, Put every man out from me! And no man stood with him when Joseph made himself known to his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 46:29; Acts 20:37; Numbers 14:1"> And he raised his voice in weeping; and the Egyptians heard, and the house of Pharaoh heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 7:13; Acts 9:5; Zechariah 12:10"> And Joseph said to his brethren, I am Joseph. Does my father yet live? And his brethren could not answer him, for they were troubled at his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Matthew 14:27; Genesis 50:18"> And Joseph said to his brethren, Come near to me, I pray you. And they came near. And he said, I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9–15; Genesis 45:7–8"> And now, be not grieved, and be not angry with yourselves, that ye sold me hither, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="45.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God sent me before you to preserve life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 47:23; Genesis 41:54; Genesis 41:56"> For the famine has been these two years in the land; and yet there are five years in which there will be neither ploughing nor harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Acts 7:35; Psalms 18:50"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="45.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God sent me before you to preserve you a remnant in the earth, and to save you alive by a great deliverance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:16; Romans 9:16; John 19:11"> And now it was not you &#91;that&#93; sent me here, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="45.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God; and he has made me a father to Pharaoh, and lord of all his house, and governor over all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:19–20; Genesis 45:13"> Haste and go up to my father, and say to him, Thus says thy son Joseph:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="45.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God has made me lord of all Egypt; come down to me, tarry not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 9:26; Exodus 8:22; Genesis 46:34–6"> And thou shalt dwell in the land of Goshen, and thou shalt be near to me, thou, and thy sons, and thy sons' sons, and thy sheep, and thy cattle, and all that thou hast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; Genesis 47:6; Matthew 15:5–6"> And there will I maintain thee; for yet there are five years of famine; in order that thou be not impoverished, thou, and thy household, and all that thou hast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:23; Luke 24:39; John 20:27"> And behold, your eyes see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin, that it is my mouth which speaks to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:14; Revelation 21:23; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> And tell my father of all my glory in Egypt, and of all that ye have seen, and haste and bring down my father hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Genesis 29:11; Genesis 46:29"> And he fell on his brother Benjamin's neck, and wept; and Benjamin wept on his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:20; 1 Samuel 10:1; Psalms 77:4"> And he kissed all his brethren, and wept upon them; and after that his brethren talked with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:33; Esther 1:21; Genesis 20:15"> And the report was heard in Pharaoh's house, saying, Joseph's brethren are come. And it was good in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 42:25–26; Genesis 44:1–2"> And Pharaoh said to Joseph, Say to thy brethren, Do this: load your beasts and depart, go into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Numbers 18:12; Genesis 47:6"> and take your father and your households, and come to me; and I will give you the good of the land of Egypt, and ye shall eat the fat of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 46:5; Genesis 45:27; Genesis 31:17–18"> And thou art commanded — this do: take waggons out of the land of Egypt for your little ones and for your wives, and take up your father, and come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Luke 17:31; Deuteronomy 7:16"> And let not your eye regret your stuff; for the good of all the land of Egypt shall be yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 45:19; Ezekiel 23:24; Lamentations 1:18"> And the sons of Israel did so; and Joseph gave them waggons, according to the commandment of Pharaoh, and gave them provision for the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:34; 2 Kings 5:5; Judges 14:12"> To each one of them all he gave changes of clothing; but to Benjamin he gave three hundred &#91;pieces&#93; of silver and five changes of clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:10; Exodus 16:3; Genesis 43:11"> And to his father he sent this: ten asses laden with the good things of Egypt, and ten she-asses laden with corn and bread, and food for his father by the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:13; Psalms 133:1–3; Genesis 42:21–22"> And he sent his brethren away, and they departed. And he said to them, Do not quarrel on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they went up out of Egypt, and came into the land of Canaan to Jacob their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:28; Genesis 37:35; Luke 24:34"> And they told him, saying, Joseph is still alive, and he is governor over all the land of Egypt. And his heart fainted, for he did not believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Isaiah 57:15; Genesis 45:19"> And they spoke to him all the words of Joseph, which he had spoken to them. And he saw the waggons that Joseph had sent to carry him. And the spirit of Jacob their father revived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:28–30; John 16:21–22; Genesis 46:30"> And Israel said, It is enough: Joseph my son is yet alive; I will go and see him before I die.</span>
