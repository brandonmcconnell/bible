<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> And when Rachel saw that she bore Jacob no children, Rachel envied her sister, and said to Jacob, Give me children, or else I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> And Jacob's anger was kindled against Rachel, and he said, Am I in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God's stead, who has withheld from thee the fruit of the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> And she said, Behold, there is my maid, Bilhah: go in to her, in order that she may bear on my knees, and I may also be built up by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> And she gave him Bilhah her maidservant as wife, and Jacob went in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Bilhah conceived, and bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> And Rachel said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="30.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God has done me justice, and has also heard my voice, and given me a son; therefore she called his name Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Bilhah Rachel's maidservant again conceived, and bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> And Rachel said, Wrestlings of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="30.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God have I wrestled with my sister, and have prevailed; and she called his name Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> And when Leah saw that she had ceased to bear, she took Zilpah her maidservant and gave her to Jacob as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Zilpah Leah's maidservant bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> And Leah said, Fortunately! and she called his name Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Zilpah Leah's maidservant bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> And Leah said, Happy am I; for the daughters will call me blessed! and she called his name Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> And Reuben went out in the days of wheat-harvest, and found mandrakes in the fields; and he brought them to his mother Leah. And Rachel said to Leah, Give me, I pray thee, of thy son's mandrakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> And she said to her, Is it &#91;too&#93; little that thou hast taken my husband, that thou wilt take my son's mandrakes also? And Rachel said, Therefore he shall lie with thee to-night for thy son's mandrakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when Jacob came from the fields in the evening, Leah went out to meet him, and said, Thou must come in to me, for indeed I have hired thee with my son's mandrakes. And he lay with her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="30.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God hearkened to Leah, and she conceived, and bore Jacob a fifth son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> And Leah said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="30.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God has given me my hire, because I have given my maidservant to my husband; and she called his name Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Leah again conceived, and bore Jacob a sixth son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> and Leah said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="30.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God has endowed me with a good dowry; this time will my husband dwell with me, because I have borne him six sons. And she called his name Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> And afterwards she bore a daughter, and called her name Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="30.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God remembered Rachel, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="30.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God listened to her, and opened her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> And she conceived, and bore a son, and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="30.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God has taken away my reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> And she called his name Joseph; and said, Jehovah will add to me another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> And it came to pass when Rachel had borne Joseph, that Jacob said to Laban, Send me away, that I may go to my place and to my country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Give &#91;me&#93; my wives for whom I have served thee, and my children; that I may go away, for thou knowest my service which I have served thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> And Laban said to him, I pray thee, if I have found favour in thine eyes — I have discovered that Jehovah has blessed me for thy sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> And he said, Appoint to me thy wages, and I will give it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> And he said to him, Thou knowest how I have served thee, and what thy cattle has become with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> For it was little that thou hadst before me, and it is increased to a multitude, and Jehovah has blessed thee from the time I came; and now, when shall I also provide for my house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> And he said, What shall I give thee? And Jacob said, Thou shalt not give me anything. If thou doest this for me, I will again feed &#91;and&#93; keep thy flock:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> I will pass through all thy flock to-day, to remove thence all the speckled and spotted sheep, and all the brown lambs, and the spotted and speckled among the goats; and &#91;that&#93; shall be my hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And my righteousness shall answer for me hereafter, when thou comest about my hire, before thy face: all that is not speckled and spotted among the goats, and brown among the lambs, let that be stolen with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> And Laban said, Well, let it be according to thy word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> And he removed that day the he-goats that were ringstraked and spotted, and all the she-goats that were speckled and spotted, every one that had white in it, and all the brown among the lambs, and gave &#91;them&#93; into the hand of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And he put three days' journey between himself and Jacob. And Jacob fed the rest of Laban's flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> And Jacob took fresh rods of white poplar, almond-tree, and maple; and peeled off white stripes in them, uncovering the white which was on the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he set the rods which he had peeled before the flock, in the troughs at the watering-places where the flock came to drink, and they were ardent when they came to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> And the flock was ardent before the rods; and the flock brought forth ringstraked, speckled, and spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jacob separated the lambs, and set the faces of the flock toward the ringstraked, and all the brown in the flock of Laban; and he made himself separate flocks, and did not put them with Laban's flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And it came to pass whensoever the strong cattle were ardent, that Jacob laid the rods before the eyes of the flock in the gutters, that they might become ardent among the rods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> but when the sheep were feeble, he put &#91;them&#93; not in; so the feeble were Laban's, and the strong Jacob's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> And the man increased very, very much, and had much cattle, and bondwomen, and bondmen, and camels, and asses.</span>
