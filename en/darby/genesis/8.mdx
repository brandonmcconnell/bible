<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:22; Genesis 19:29; Psalms 136:23"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God remembered Noah, and all the animals, and all the cattle that were with him in the ark; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God made a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters subsided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> And the fountains of the deep and the windows of heaven were closed, and the pour of rain from heaven was stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And the waters retired from the earth, continually retiring; and in the course of a hundred and fifty days the waters abated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on the mountains of Ararat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And the waters abated continually until the tenth month: in the tenth &#91;month&#93;, on the first of the month, the tops of the mountains were seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And it came to pass at the end of forty days that Noah opened the window of the ark which he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> And he sent out the raven, which went forth going to and fro, until the waters were dried from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> And he sent out the dove from him, to see if the waters had become low on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> But the dove found no resting-place for the sole of her foot, and returned to him into the ark; for the waters were on the whole earth; and he put forth his hand, and took her, and brought her to him into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And he waited yet other seven days, and again he sent forth the dove out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And the dove came to him at eventide; and behold, in her beak was an olive-leaf plucked off; and Noah knew that the waters had become low on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he waited yet other seven days, and sent forth the dove; but she returned no more to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And it came to pass in the six hundred and first year, in the first &#91;month&#93;, on the first of the month, that the waters were dried up from the earth. And Noah removed the covering of the ark, and looked, and behold, the surface of the ground was dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God spoke to Noah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> Go out of the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and thy sons' wives with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Bring forth with thee every animal which is with thee, of all flesh, fowl as well as cattle, and all the creeping things which creep on the earth, that they may swarm on the earth, and may be fruitful and multiply on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> And Noah went out, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> All the animals, all the creeping things, and all the fowl — everything that moves on the earth, after their kinds, went out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noah built an altar to Jehovah; and took of every clean animal, and of all clean fowl, and offered up burnt-offerings on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And Jehovah smelled the sweet odour. And Jehovah said in his heart, I will no more henceforth curse the ground on account of Man, for the thought of Man's heart is evil from his youth; and I will no more smite every living thing, as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> Henceforth, all the days of the earth, seed &#91;time&#93; and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night, shall not cease.</span>
