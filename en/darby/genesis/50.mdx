<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 46:4; Deuteronomy 6:7–8; Acts 8:2"> And Joseph fell upon his father's face, and wept upon him, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:26; 2 Chronicles 16:14; John 19:39–40"> And Joseph commanded his servants the physicians to embalm his father. And the physicians embalmed Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 20:29; Deuteronomy 34:8; Genesis 50:10"> And forty days were fulfilled for him; for so are fulfilled the days of those who are embalmed. And the Egyptians mourned for him seventy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:3; Esther 4:2"> And when the days of his mourning were past, Joseph spoke to the house of Pharaoh, saying, If now I have found favour in your eyes, speak, I pray you, in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Isaiah 22:16; Matthew 27:60"> My father made me swear, saying, Behold, I die; in my grave which I have dug myself in the land of Canaan, there shalt thou bury me. And now, let me go up, I pray thee, that I may bury my father; and I will come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 48:21"> And Pharaoh said, Go up and bury thy father, according as he made thee swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> And Joseph went up to bury his father; and with him went up all the bondmen of Pharaoh, the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:24–27; Exodus 10:26; Exodus 10:8–9"> and all the house of Joseph, and his brethren, and his father's house; only their little ones, and their flocks, and their herds, they left in the land of Goshen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; 2 Kings 18:24; Song of Solomon 1:9"> And there went up with him both chariots and horsemen; and the camp was very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 2:13; Acts 8:2; 2 Samuel 1:17"> And they came to the threshing-floor of Atad, which is beyond the Jordan; and there they lamented with a great and very grievous lamentation; and he made a mourning for his father of seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 10:15–19; Genesis 24:6; Deuteronomy 11:30"> And the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, saw the mourning at the threshing-floor of Atad, and they said, This is a grievous mourning of the Egyptians. Therefore the name of it was called Abel-Mizraim, which is beyond the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:16; Genesis 49:29–32; Ephesians 6:1"> And his sons did to him according as he had commanded them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:29–31; Genesis 23:16–18; Acts 7:16"> and his sons carried him into the land of Canaan, and buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah which Abraham had bought along with the field, for a possession of a sepulchre, of Ephron the Hittite, opposite to Mamre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And, after he had buried his father, Joseph returned to Egypt, he and his brethren, and all that had gone up with him to bury his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Genesis 42:21–22; Psalms 53:5"> And when Joseph's brethren saw that their father was dead, they said, If now Joseph should be hostile to us, and should indeed requite us all the evil that we did to him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25"> And they sent a messenger to Joseph, saying, Thy father commanded before he died, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Matthew 6:14–15; Genesis 45:8"> Thus shall ye speak to Joseph: Oh forgive, I pray thee, the transgression of thy brethren, and their sin! for they did evil to thee. And now, we pray thee, forgive the transgression of the servants of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="50.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of thy father. And Joseph wept when they spoke to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 37:7–11; Genesis 42:6; Genesis 45:3"> And his brethren also went and fell down before his face, and said, Behold, we are thy bondmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 45:5; Genesis 30:2"> And Joseph said to them, Fear not: am I then in the place of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="50.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Psalms 119:71; Genesis 45:5–8"> Ye indeed meant evil against me:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="50.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God meant it for good, in order that he might do as &#91;it is&#93; this day, to save a great people alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:12; 1 Peter 3:9; Genesis 45:10–11"> And now, fear not: I will maintain you and your little ones. And he comforted them, and spoke consolingly to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Joseph dwelt in Egypt, he and his father's house; and Joseph lived a hundred and ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:39; Genesis 30:3; Job 42:16"> And Joseph saw Ephraim's children of the third &#91;generation&#93;; the sons also of Machir the son of Manasseh were born on Joseph's knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 3:16–17; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 26:3"> And Joseph said to his brethren, I die; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="50.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God will certainly visit you, and bring you up out of this land, into the land that he swore unto Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Joshua 24:32; Genesis 47:29–31"> And Joseph took an oath of the children of Israel, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="50.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God will certainly visit you; and ye shall carry up my bones hence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 50:2–3; Genesis 47:9; Joshua 24:29"> And Joseph died, a hundred and ten years old; and they embalmed him; and he was put in a coffin in Egypt.</span>
