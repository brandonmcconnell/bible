<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; James 2:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And it came to pass after these things, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God tried Abraham, and said to him, Abraham! and he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:32; Hebrews 11:17"> And he said, Take now thy son, thine only &#91;son&#93;, whom thou lovest, Isaac, and get thee into the land of Moriah, and there offer him up for a burnt-offering on one of the mountains which I will tell thee of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26"> And Abraham rose early in the morning, and saddled his ass, and took two of his young men with him, and Isaac his son; and he clave the wood for the burnt-offering, and rose up and went to the place that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God had told him of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:32; Matthew 17:23; Hosea 6:2"> On the third day Abraham lifted up his eyes and saw the place from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:19; Hebrews 12:1"> And Abraham said to his young men, Abide ye here with the ass; and I and the lad will go yonder and worship, and come again to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:17; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:6"> And Abraham took the wood of the burnt-offering, and laid it on Isaac his son; and he took the fire in his hand, and the knife, and they went both of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; Matthew 26:39; Genesis 8:20"> And Isaac spoke to Abraham his father, and said, My father! And he said, Here am I, my son. And he said, Behold the fire and the wood; but where is the sheep for a burnt-offering?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:36; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> And Abraham said, My son,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God will provide himself with the sheep for a burnt-offering. And they went both of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 2:21; Hebrews 11:17–19; Philippians 2:7–8"> And they came to the place of which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="22.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God had told him. And Abraham built the altar there, and piled the wood; and he bound Isaac his son, and laid him on the altar upon the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:21–23; Hebrews 11:17–19; Isaiah 53:6–12"> And Abraham stretched out his hand, and took the knife to slaughter his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 21:17; Exodus 3:4; Genesis 16:9–10"> And the Angel of Jehovah called to him from the heavens, and said, Abraham, Abraham! And he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:18; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And he said, Stretch not out thy hand against the lad, neither do anything to him; for now I know that thou fearest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="22.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, and hast not withheld thy son, thine only &#91;son&#93;, from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Psalms 40:6–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And Abraham lifted up his eyes, and looked, and behold, behind was a ram caught in the thicket by its horns; and Abraham went and took the ram, and offered it up for a burnt-offering instead of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:13; Exodus 17:15; Genesis 22:8"> And Abraham called the name of that place Jehovah-jireh; as it is said at the present day, On the mount of Jehovah will be provided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the Angel of Jehovah called to Abraham from the heavens a second time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–14; Luke 1:73; Psalms 105:9"> and said, By myself I swear, saith Jehovah, that, because thou hast done this, and hast not withheld thy son, thine only &#91;son&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 24:60; Jeremiah 33:22"> I will richly bless thee, and greatly multiply thy seed, as the stars of heaven, and as the sand that is on the sea-shore; and thy seed shall possess the gate of his enemies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16"> and in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth bless themselves, because thou hast hearkened to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Judges 20:1; Joshua 15:28"> And Abraham returned to his young men, and they rose up and went together to Beer-sheba. And Abraham dwelt at Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:24; Proverbs 25:25"> And it came to pass after these things, that it was told Abraham, saying, Behold, Milcah, she also has borne sons to thy brother Nahor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Job 32:2; Psalms 60:1–12"> Uz his first-born, and Buz his brother, and Kemuel the father of Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and Chesed, and Hazo, and Pildash, and Jidlaph, and Bethuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 28:5; Genesis 24:51"> (And Bethuel begot Rebecca.) These eight Milcah bore to Nahor, Abraham's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:3; Genesis 25:6; Proverbs 15:25"> And his concubine, named Reumah, she also bore Tebah, and Gaham, and Thahash, and Maacah.</span>
