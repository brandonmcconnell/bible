<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Genesis 46:20; Psalms 128:6"> And it came to pass after these things, that one told Joseph, Behold, thy father is sick. And he took with him his two sons Manasseh and Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; 1 Samuel 23:16; Deuteronomy 3:28"> And one told Jacob and said, Behold, thy son Joseph is coming to thee. And Israel strengthened himself, and sat upon the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:9–12; Exodus 6:3; Genesis 28:3"> And Jacob said to Joseph, The Almighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="48.3 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God appeared to me at Luz in the land of Canaan, and blessed me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 35:11; Genesis 26:4"> and he said to me, Behold, I will make thee fruitful and multiply thee, and I will make of thee a company of peoples; and will give this land to thy seed after thee &#91;for&#93; an everlasting possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:50–52; Joshua 14:4; Genesis 46:20"> And now thy two sons, who were born to thee in the land of Egypt before I came to thee into Egypt, shall be mine: Ephraim and Manasseh shall be mine, as Reuben and Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:4"> And thy family which thou hast begotten after them shall be thine: they shall be called after the name of their brethren in their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:16–19; Matthew 2:18; Genesis 35:9"> And as for me, when I came from Padan, Rachel died by me in the land of Canaan on the way, when there was yet a certain distance to come to Ephrath; and I buried her there on the way to Ephrath, that is, Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Israel beheld Joseph's sons, and said, Who are these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:4; Genesis 33:5; Hebrews 11:21"> And Joseph said to his father, They are my sons, whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="48.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God has given me here. And he said, Bring them, I pray thee, to me, that I may bless them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 27:1; Genesis 27:27; 1 Samuel 3:2"> But the eyes of Israel were heavy from age: he could not see. And he brought them nearer to him; and he kissed them, and embraced them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Genesis 45:26; Genesis 42:36"> And Israel said to Joseph, I had not thought to see thy face; and behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="48.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God has let me see also thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Leviticus 19:32; Genesis 19:1"> And Joseph brought them out from his knees, and bowed down with his face to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Joseph took them both, Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel's left hand, and Manasseh in his left hand toward Israel's right hand, and brought &#91;them&#93; near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 41:51; Psalms 118:16; Matthew 19:15"> But Israel stretched out his right hand, and laid &#91;it&#93; on Ephraim's head — now he was the younger — and his left hand on Manasseh's head; guiding his hands intelligently, for Manasseh was the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Colossians 2:6; Isaiah 33:16"> And he blessed Joseph, and said, The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="48.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac walked, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="48.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God that shepherded me all my life long to this day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:22; Genesis 49:22; Genesis 48:5"> the Angel that redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads; and let my name be named upon them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and let them grow into a multitude in the midst of the land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:14; Romans 9:7–8; 1 Chronicles 21:7"> When Joseph saw that his father laid his right hand on the head of Ephraim, it was evil in his eyes; and he took hold of his father's hand to remove it from Ephraim's head to Manasseh's head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 19:18; Genesis 29:26; Genesis 43:33"> And Joseph said to his father, Not so, my father, for this is the firstborn: put thy right hand on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 1:33–35; Genesis 25:28"> But his father refused and said, I know, my son, I know: he also will become a people, and he also will be great; but truly his younger brother will be greater than he; and his seed will become the fulness of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:60; Ruth 4:11–12; Genesis 28:3"> And he blessed them that day, saying, In thee will Israel bless, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="48.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God make thee as Ephraim and Manasseh! And he set Ephraim before Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 50:24; Genesis 46:4; Genesis 28:15"> And Israel said to Joseph, Behold, I die; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="48.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God will be with you, and bring you again to the land of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:5; Joshua 24:32; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> And I have given to thee one tract &#91;of land&#93; above thy brethren, which I took out of the hand of the Amorite with my sword and with my bow.</span>
