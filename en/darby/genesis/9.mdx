<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 8:17"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; James 3:7"> And let the fear of you and the dread of you be upon every animal of the earth, and upon all fowl of the heavens: upon all that moveth &#91;on&#93; the ground; and upon all the fishes of the sea: into your hand are they delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Colossians 2:21–22; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Every moving thing that liveth shall be food for you: as the green herb I give you everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:23; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:16"> Only, the flesh with its life, its blood, ye shall not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 21:28–29; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 4:9–10"> And indeed your blood, &#91;the blood&#93; of your lives, will I require: at the hand of every animal will I require it, and at the hand of Man, at the hand of each &#91;the blood&#93; of his brother, will I require the life of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Matthew 26:52; Revelation 13:10"> Whoso sheddeth Man's blood, by Man shall his blood be shed; for in the image of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God he hath made Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:19"> And ye, be fruitful and multiply: swarm on the earth, and multiply on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="9.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God spoke to Noah, and to his sons with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; Romans 1:3"> And I, behold, I establish my covenant with you, and with your seed after you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Genesis 9:15–16; Psalms 36:5–6"> and with every living soul which is with you, fowl as well as cattle, and all the animals of the earth with you, of all that has gone out of the ark — every animal of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; Genesis 8:21–22; Genesis 7:21–23"> And I establish my covenant with you, neither shall all flesh be cut off any more by the waters of a flood, and henceforth there shall be no flood to destroy the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:11; Matthew 26:26–28; Exodus 12:13"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="9.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God said, This is the sign of the covenant that I set between me and you and every living soul that is with you, for everlasting generations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 4:3; Revelation 10:1"> I set my bow in the clouds, and it shall be for a sign of the covenant between me and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And it shall come to pass when I bring clouds over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Ezekiel 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> and I will remember my covenant which is between me and you and every living soul of all flesh; and the waters shall not henceforth become a flood to destroy all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; Jeremiah 32:40; 2 Samuel 23:5"> And the bow shall be in the cloud; and I will look upon it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant between<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="9.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God and every living soul of all flesh that is upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="9.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God said to Noah, This is the sign of the covenant which I have established between me and all flesh that is upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 10:1; Genesis 10:6; Genesis 9:25–27"> And the sons of Noah who went out of the ark were Shem, and Ham, and Japheth. And Ham is the father of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These three are the sons of Noah; and from these was &#91;the population of&#93; the whole earth spread abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> And Noah began &#91;to be&#93; a husbandman, and planted a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> And he drank of the wine, and was drunken, and he uncovered himself in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15"> And Ham the father of Canaan saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brethren outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Exodus 20:12; 1 Timothy 5:17"> And Shem and Japheth took the upper garment and both laid &#91;it&#93; upon their shoulders, and went backwards, and covered the nakedness of their father. And their faces were turned away, that they saw not their father's nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And Noah awoke from his wine, and learned what his youngest son had done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:23; Deuteronomy 27:16; Judges 1:28–30"> And he said, Cursed be Canaan; Let him be a bondman of bondmen to his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; Genesis 12:1–3; Luke 3:23–36"> And he said, Blessed be Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="9.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of Shem, And let Canaan be his bondman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:12; Romans 15:12; Hebrews 11:9–10"> Let<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="9.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God enlarge Japheth, and let him dwell in the tents of Shem, And let Canaan be his bondman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 5:27; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 5:32"> And all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years; and he died.</span>
