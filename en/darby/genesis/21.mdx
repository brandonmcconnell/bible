<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14"> And Jehovah visited Sarah as he had said, and Jehovah did to Sarah as he had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 4:22; Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:10"> And Sarah conceived, and bore Abraham a son in his old age, at the appointed time of which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="21.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God had spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 17:19; Romans 9:7; Genesis 21:6"> And Abraham called the name of his son who was born to him, whom Sarah bore to him, Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 12:32; John 7:22–23"> And Abraham circumcised his son Isaac, being eight days old, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="21.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19; Genesis 17:1"> And Abraham was a hundred years old when his son Isaac was born to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; Genesis 17:17"> And Sarah said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="21.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God has made me laugh: all that hear will laugh with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> And she said, Who would have said to Abraham, Sarah will suckle children? For I have borne &#91;him&#93; a son in his old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Hosea 1:8; Genesis 40:20"> And the child grew, and was weaned. And Abraham made a great feast on the day that Isaac was weaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> And Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, whom she had borne to Abraham, mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> And she said to Abraham, Cast out this handmaid and her son; for the son of this handmaid shall not inherit with my son — with Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> And the thing was very grievous in Abraham's sight because of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="21.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God said to Abraham, Let it not be grievous in thy sight because of the lad and because of thy handmaid: &#91;in&#93; all that Sarah hath said to thee hearken to her voice, for in Isaac shall a seed be called to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> But also the son of the handmaid will I make a nation, because he is thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> And Abraham rose up early in the morning, and took bread, and a flask of water, and gave &#91;it&#93; to Hagar, putting &#91;it&#93; on her shoulder — and the child, and sent her away. And she departed, and wandered about in the wilderness of Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Psalms 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> And the water was exhausted from the flask; and she cast the child under one of the shrubs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> and she went and sat down over against &#91;him&#93;, a bow-shot off; for she said, Let me not behold the death of the child. And she sat over against &#91;him&#93;, and lifted up her voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalms 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="21.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God heard the voice of the lad. And the Angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="21.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God called to Hagar from the heavens, and said to her, What &#91;aileth&#93; thee, Hagar? Fear not; for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="21.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God hath heard the voice of the lad there, where he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> Arise, take the lad, and hold him in thy hand; for I will make of him a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="21.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water; and she went and filled the flask with water, and gave the lad drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 39:21; Genesis 16:12"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="21.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God was with the lad, and he grew; and he dwelt in the wilderness, and became an archer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> And he dwelt in the wilderness of Paran. And his mother took him a wife out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> And it came to pass at that time that Abimelech, and Phichol the captain of his host, spoke to Abraham, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="21.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God is with thee in all that thou doest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> And now swear to me here by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="21.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God that thou wilt not deal deceitfully with me, nor with my son, nor with my grandson. According to the kindness that I have done to thee, thou shalt do to me, and to the land in which thou sojournest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> And Abraham said, I will swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> And Abraham reproved Abimelech because of a well of water that Abimelech's servants had violently taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; 2 Kings 5:20–24"> And Abimelech said, I do not know who has done this, neither hast thou told me &#91;of it&#93;, neither have I heard &#91;of it&#93; but to-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> And Abraham took sheep and oxen, and gave them to Abimelech; and both of them made a covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Abraham set seven ewe-lambs of the flock by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> And Abimelech said to Abraham, What &#91;mean&#93; these seven ewe-lambs, these which thou hast set by themselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> And he said, That thou take the seven ewe-lambs of my hand, that they may be a witness to me that I have dug this well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; 1 Kings 4:25"> Therefore he called that place Beer-sheba, because there they had sworn, both of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> And they made a covenant at Beer-sheba. And Abimelech rose up, and Phichol the captain of his host, and returned into the land of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 90:2"> And &#91;Abraham&#93; planted a tamarisk in Beer-sheba, and called there on the name of Jehovah, the Eternal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="21.33 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">l</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> And Abraham sojourned in the Philistines' land many days.</span>
