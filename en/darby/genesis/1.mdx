<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Hebrews 11:3; Isaiah 45:18"> In the beginning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God created the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:23; Psalms 104:30; Isaiah 45:18"> And the earth was waste and empty, and darkness was on the face of the deep, and the Spirit of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God was hovering over the face of the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; John 1:5; Isaiah 45:7"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God said, Let there be light. And there was light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:13; Genesis 1:18; Ecclesiastes 11:7"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God saw the light that it was good; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God divided between the light and the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Isaiah 45:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:5"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And there was evening, and there was morning — the first day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:15; Psalms 33:6; Job 37:18"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God said, Let there be an expanse in the midst of the waters, and let it be a division between waters and waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 8:28–29; Psalms 148:4; Matthew 8:27"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="1.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God made the expanse, and divided between the waters that are under the expanse and the waters that are above the expanse; and it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:5; Genesis 1:19; Genesis 1:10"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="1.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God called the expanse Heavens. And there was evening, and there was morning — a second day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 3:5; Jonah 1:9; Jeremiah 5:22"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="1.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God said, Let the waters under the heavens be gathered together to one place, and let the dry &#91;land&#93; appear. And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 104:31; Genesis 1:4"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="1.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God called the dry &#91;land&#93; Earth, and the gathering together of the waters he called Seas. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="1.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:14–17; Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 147:8"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God said, Let the earth cause grass to spring up, herb producing seed, fruit-trees yielding fruit after their kind, the seed of which is in them, on the earth. And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:28; Isaiah 55:10–11; Matthew 13:24–26"> And the earth brought forth grass, herb producing seed after its kind, and trees yielding fruit, the seed of which is in them, after their kind. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And there was evening, and there was morning — a third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19–20; Deuteronomy 4:19; Jeremiah 31:35"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="1.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God said, Let there be lights in the expanse of the heavens, to divide between the day and the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days and years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and let them be for lights in the expanse of the heavens, to give light on the earth. And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Deuteronomy 4:19; Psalms 8:3"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="1.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God made the two great lights, the great light to rule the day, and the small light to rule the night, — and the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Psalms 8:1; Job 38:12"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="1.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God set them in the expanse of the heavens, to give light on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 19:6"> and to rule during the day and during the night, and to divide between the light and the darkness. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="1.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">r</a></sup>God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And there was evening, and there was morning — a fourth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:19; Psalms 104:24–25; 1 Kings 4:33"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="1.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">s</a></sup>God said, Let the waters swarm with swarms of living souls, and let fowl fly above the earth in the expanse of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–26; Genesis 1:25; Genesis 8:19"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="1.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">t</a></sup>God created the great sea monsters, and every living soul that moves with which the waters swarm, after their kind, and every winged fowl after its kind. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="1.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">u</a></sup>God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 40:15; Genesis 8:17; Psalms 107:38"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="1.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">v</a></sup>God blessed them, saying, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let fowl multiply on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And there was evening, and there was morning — a fifth day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 50:9–10; Genesis 6:20; Genesis 8:19"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="1.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">w</a></sup>God said, Let the earth bring forth living souls after their kind, cattle, and creeping thing, and beast of the earth, after their kind. And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Job 12:8–10; Genesis 2:19–20"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="1.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">x</a></sup>God made the beast of the earth after its kind, and the cattle after their kind, and every creeping thing of the ground after its kind. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="1.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">y</a></sup>God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:24; Psalms 8:4–8"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="1.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">z</a></sup>God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness; and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the heavens, and over the cattle, and over the whole earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Ephesians 2:10; Genesis 5:1–2"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="1.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">aa</a></sup>God created Man in his image, in the image of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="1.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">ab</a></sup>God created he him; male and female created he them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:7; Leviticus 26:9"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="1.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">ac</a></sup>God blessed them; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="1.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">ad</a></sup>God said to them, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it; and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the heavens, and over every animal that moveth on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 9:3; Psalms 104:14–15; Psalms 145:15–16"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="1.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">ae</a></sup>God said, Behold, I have given you every herb producing seed that is on the whole earth, and every tree in which is the fruit of a tree producing seed: it shall be food for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Psalms 145:15–16; Psalms 147:9"> and to every animal of the earth, and to every fowl of the heavens, and to everything that creepeth on the earth, in which is a living soul, every green herb for food. And it was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Psalms 104:24; Psalms 19:1–2"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="1.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">af</a></sup>God saw everything that he had made, and behold it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning — the sixth day.</span>
