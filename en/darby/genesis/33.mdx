<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 37:3"> and he put the maidservants and their children foremost, and Leah and her children after, and Rachel and Joseph hindmost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 18:2"> And he passed on before them, and bowed to the earth seven times, until he came near to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 45:14–15; Psalms 34:4; Luke 15:20"> And Esau ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell on his neck, and kissed him; and they wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; Genesis 48:9"> And he lifted up his eyes and saw the women and the children, and said, Who are these with thee? And he said, The children that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="33.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God has graciously given thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the maidservants drew near, they and their children, and they bowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Leah also, with her children, drew near, and they bowed. And lastly Joseph drew near, and Rachel, and they bowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:5; Genesis 39:5; Genesis 32:13–20"> And he said, What &#91;meanest&#93; thou by all the drove which I met? And he said, To find favour in the eyes of my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:39; Philemon 1:7; Genesis 27:41"> And Esau said, I have enough, my brother; let what thou hast be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 33:26; Revelation 22:4; Genesis 32:30"> And Jacob said, No, I pray thee; if now I have found favour in thine eyes, then receive my gift from my hand; for therefore have I seen thy face, as though I had seen the face of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="33.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and thou hast received me with pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 30:43"> Take, I pray thee, my blessing which has been brought to thee; because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="33.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God has been gracious to me, and because I have everything. And he urged him, and he took &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he said, Let us take our journey, and go on, and I will go before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Ezekiel 34:23–25"> And he said to him, My lord knows that the children are tender, and the suckling sheep and kine are with me; and if they should overdrive them only one day, all the flock would die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Let my lord, I pray thee, pass on before his servant, and I will drive on at my ease according to the pace of the cattle that is before me, and according to the pace of the children, until I come to my lord, to Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> And Esau said, Let me now leave with thee &#91;some&#93; of the people that are with me. And he said, What need? Let me find favour in the eyes of my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Esau returned that day on his way to Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 60:6; Joshua 13:27; Judges 8:5"> And Jacob journeyed to Succoth, and built himself a house, and for his cattle he made booths. Therefore the name of the place was called Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:1; Acts 7:16; Joshua 24:1"> And Jacob came safely &#91;to the&#93; city Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, when he came from Padan-Aram; and he encamped before the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:16; John 4:5; Joshua 24:32"> And he bought the portion of the field where he had spread his tent, of the hand of the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for a hundred kesitahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 21:33; Genesis 32:28"> And there he set up an altar, and called it El-Elohe-Israel.</span>
