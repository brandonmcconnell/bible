<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 37:12"> And it came to pass in the days of Amraphel the king of Shinar, Arioch the king of El-lasar, Chedorlaomer the king of Elam, and Tidal the king of nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Deuteronomy 29:23; Genesis 10:19"> &#91;that&#93; they made war with Bera the king of Sodom, and with Birsha the king of Gomorrah, Shinab the king of Admah, and Shemeber the king of Zeboim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; Numbers 34:12"> All these were joined in the vale of Siddim, which is the salt sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:25–26; Ezekiel 17:15"> Twelve years had they served Chedorlaomer; and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 15:20; Deuteronomy 1:4"> And in the fourteenth year came Chedorlaomer and the kings that were with him, and smote the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim, and the Zuzim in Ham, and the Emim in Shaveh-Kirjathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Deuteronomy 2:12; Genesis 21:21"> and the Horites on their mount Seir, to El-Paran, which is by the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 20:1"> And they returned, and came to En-mishpat, which is Kadesh, and smote all the country of the Amalekites, and also the Amorites that dwelt at Hazazon-Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:22; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:20"> And the king of Sodom and the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Zeboim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar, went out, and they joined battle with them in the vale of Siddim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> with Chedorlaomer the king of Elam, and Tidal the king of nations, and Amraphel the king of Shinar, and Arioch the king of Ellasar — four kings with the five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:17; Genesis 19:30; Isaiah 24:18"> And the vale of Siddim was full of pits of asphalt. And the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled, and fell there: and they that remained fled to the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:21; Genesis 14:16; Deuteronomy 28:31"> And they took all the property of Sodom and Gomorrah, and all their victuals, and departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:5; Genesis 11:27; Revelation 3:19"> And they took Lot and his property, Abram's brother's son, and departed. For he dwelt in Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 14:24; Genesis 40:15"> And one who had escaped came and told Abram the Hebrew. And he dwelt by the oaks of Mamre the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol, and the brother of Aner. And these were Abram's allies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Genesis 15:3; Genesis 17:27"> And Abram heard that his brother was taken captive; and he led out his trained &#91;servants&#93;, born in his house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued &#91;them&#93; as far as Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Psalms 112:5; 1 Kings 15:18"> And he divided himself against them by night, he and his servants, and smote them, and pursued them as far as Hobah, which is to the left of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18–19; 1 Samuel 30:8; Genesis 12:2"> And he brought back all the property, and brought again his brother Lot and his property, and the women also, and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; Proverbs 19:4; Hebrews 7:1"> And the king of Sodom went out to meet him after he had returned from smiting Chedorlaomer, and the kings that were with him, into the valley of Shaveh, which is the king's valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–3; Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6"> And Melchisedec king of Salem brought out bread and wine. And he was priest of the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.18 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 7:6–7; Genesis 14:22; Psalms 24:1"> And he blessed him, and said, Blessed be Abram of the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.19 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God, possessor of heavens and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 28:22; Hebrews 7:4–9; Malachi 3:10"> And blessed be the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.20 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God, who has delivered thine enemies into thy hand. And he gave him the tenth of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the king of Sodom said to Abram, Give me the souls, and take the property for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 4:34; Deuteronomy 32:40"> And Abram said to the king of Sodom, I have lifted up my hand to Jehovah, the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.22 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God, possessor of heavens and earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 2 Corinthians 11:9–12; Esther 9:15–16"> if from a thread even to a sandal-thong, yes, if of all that is thine, I take &#91;anything&#93;...; that thou mayest not say, I have made Abram rich;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Proverbs 3:27; Matthew 7:12"> save only that which the young men have eaten, and the portion of the men that went with me, Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre, let them take their portion.</span>
