<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Acts 7:11–13; Lamentations 5:10"> And the famine was grievous in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:6–8; Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 15:16"> And it came to pass, when they had finished eating the grain which they had brought from Egypt, that their father said to them, Go again, buy us a little food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 44:23; Genesis 43:5; 2 Samuel 3:13"> And Judah spoke to him, saying, The man did positively testify to us, saying, Ye shall not see my face, unless your brother be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If thou wilt send our brother with us, we will go down and buy thee food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Genesis 44:26; Genesis 42:38"> but if thou do not send &#91;him&#93;, we will not go down, for the man said to us, Ye shall not see my face, unless your brother be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Israel said, Why did ye deal &#91;so&#93; ill with me &#91;as&#93; to tell the man whether ye had yet a brother?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; Genesis 42:13; Genesis 43:27"> And they said, The man asked very closely after us, and after our kindred, saying, Is your father yet alive? have ye a brother? And we told him according to the tenor of these words. Could we at all know that he would say, Bring your brother down?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 42:2; Deuteronomy 33:6; Genesis 45:19"> And Judah said to Israel his father, Send the lad with me, and we will arise and go, that we may live, and not die, both we and thou and our little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 42:37; Philemon 1:18–19; Hebrews 7:22"> I will be surety for him: of my hand shalt thou require him; if I bring him not to thee, and set him before thy face, then shall I be guilty toward thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 19:16"> For had we not lingered, we should now certainly have returned already twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 8:22; Ezekiel 27:17"> And their father Israel said to them, If it is then so, do this: take of the best fruits in the land in your vessels, and carry down the man a gift: a little balsam and a little honey, tragacanth and ladanum, pistacia-nuts and almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:25; Genesis 42:35; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> And take other money in your hand, and the money that was returned to you in the mouth of your sacks, carry back in your hand: perhaps it is an oversight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 42:38"> And take your brother, and arise, go again to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:16; Genesis 17:1; Acts 21:14"> And the Almighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="43.14 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God give you mercy before the man, that he may send away your other brother and Benjamin! And I, if I be bereaved of children, am bereaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the men took that gift, and took double money in their hand, and Benjamin, and rose up, and went down to Egypt, and came before Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 44:1; Genesis 43:19; Genesis 24:2–10"> And Joseph saw Benjamin with them, and said to the &#91;man&#93; who was over his house, Bring the men into the house, and slaughter cattle, and make ready; for the men shall eat with me at noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the man did as Joseph had said; and the man brought the men into Joseph's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 42:35; Romans 7:8; Genesis 42:21"> And the men were afraid because they were brought into Joseph's house, and said, Because of the money that was returned to us in our sacks at the beginning are we brought in, that he may turn against us, and fall upon us and take us for bondmen, and our asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they came up to the man that was over Joseph's house, and they spoke to him at the door of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 42:3; Genesis 42:10; Genesis 42:27"> and said, Ah! my lord, we came indeed down at the first to buy food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 43:12; Genesis 42:27–35; Romans 13:8"> And it came to pass when we came to the inn, that we opened our sacks, and behold, &#91;every&#93; man's money was in the mouth of his sack, our money according to its weight; and we have brought it again in our hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And other money have we brought down in our hand to buy food. We do not know who put our money in our sacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 20:26; Genesis 42:24; John 14:27"> And he said, Peace be to you, fear not: your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="43.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="43.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of your father, has given you treasure in your sacks; your money came to me. And he brought Simeon out to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 18:4; Genesis 24:32; Luke 7:44"> And the man brought the men into Joseph's house, and gave water, and they washed their feet; and he gave their asses food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 43:11; Genesis 43:16"> And they made ready the gift for Joseph's coming at noon; for they had heard that they should eat bread there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Philippians 2:10–11; Genesis 27:29; Genesis 42:6"> When Joseph came home, they brought him the gift that was in their hand, into the house, and bowed themselves to him to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; 1 Samuel 25:5; 1 Samuel 17:22"> And he asked them of their welfare, and said, Is your father well — the old man of whom ye spoke? Is he yet alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 37:7; Genesis 43:26; 2 Samuel 14:4"> And they said, Thy servant our father is well; he is yet alive. And they bowed, and made obeisance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 42:13; Numbers 6:25; Genesis 30:22–24"> And he lifted up his eyes and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother's son, and said, Is this your younger brother of whom ye spoke to me? And he said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="43.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God be gracious to thee, my son!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:24; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 Kings 3:26"> And Joseph made haste, for his bowels burned for his brother; and he sought &#91;a place&#93; to weep, and he went into the chamber, and wept there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; 1 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 42:14"> And he washed his face, and came out, and controlled himself, and said, Set on bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; Exodus 8:26; Genesis 43:16"> And they set on for him by himself, and for them by themselves, and for the Egyptians who ate with him by themselves; because the Egyptians might not eat bread with the Hebrews, for that is an abomination unto the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright, and the youngest according to his youth; and the men marvelled one at another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 45:22; 2 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 1:5"> And he had portions carried to them from before him. And Benjamin's portion was five times greater than the portions of them all. And they drank, and made merry with him.</span>
