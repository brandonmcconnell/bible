<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 24:14; Jeremiah 23:20; Daniel 10:14"> And Jacob called his sons, and said, Gather yourselves together, and I will tell you what will befall you at the end of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 7:1; Proverbs 23:22"> Assemble yourselves, and hear, ye sons of Jacob, And listen to Israel your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:17; Psalms 78:51; Psalms 105:36"> Reuben, thou art my firstborn, My might, and the firstfruits of my vigour: Excellency of dignity, and excellency of strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; 1 Chronicles 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> Impetuous as the waters, thou shalt have no pre-eminence; Because thou wentest up to thy father's couch: Then defiledst thou &#91;it&#93;: he went up to my bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 34:25–31; Genesis 46:10–11; Genesis 29:33–34"> Simeon and Levi are brethren: Instruments of violence their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> My soul, come not into their council; Mine honour, be not united with their assembly; For in their anger they slew men, And in their wantonness houghed oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; 1 Chronicles 4:24–31; 1 Chronicles 6:65"> Cursed be their anger, for it &#91;was&#93; violent; And their rage, for it &#91;was&#93; cruel! I will divide them in Jacob, And scatter them in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 7:14; Genesis 27:29; 1 Chronicles 5:2"> Judah — &#91;as to&#93; thee, thy brethren will praise thee; Thy hand will be upon the neck of thine enemies; Thy father's children will bow down to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:24; Revelation 5:5"> Judah is a young lion; From the prey, my son, thou art gone up. He stoopeth, he layeth himself down as a lion, And as a lioness: who will rouse him up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Numbers 24:17; Isaiah 9:6"> The sceptre will not depart from Judah, Nor the lawgiver from between his feet, Until Shiloh come, And to him will be the obedience of peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> He bindeth his foal to the vine, And his ass's colt to the choice vine; He washeth his dress in wine, And his garment in the blood of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29"> The eyes are red with wine, And the teeth &#91;are&#93; white with milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Genesis 30:20; Joshua 19:10–16"> Zebulun will dwell at the shore of the seas; Yea, he will be at the shore of the ships, And his side &#91;toucheth&#93; upon Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Genesis 30:18; Deuteronomy 33:18"> Issachar is a bony ass, Crouching down between two hurdles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> And he saw the rest that it was good, And the land that it was pleasant; And he bowed his shoulder to bear, And was a tributary servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Deuteronomy 33:22; Judges 18:26–27"> Dan will judge his people, As another of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:35; Judges 18:22–31; Judges 14:1–15"> Dan will be a serpent on the way, A horned snake on the path, Which biteth the horse's heels, So that the rider falleth backwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Micah 7:7"> I wait for thy salvation, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; 1 Chronicles 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> Gad — troops will rush upon him; But he will rush upon the heel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> Out of Asher, his bread shall be fat, And he will give royal dainties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> Naphtali is a hind let loose; He giveth goodly words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Joseph is a fruitful bough; A fruitful bough by a well; &#91;His&#93; branches shoot over the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> The archers have provoked him, And shot at, and hated him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Isaiah 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> But his bow abideth firm, And the arms of his hands are supple By the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob. From thence is the shepherd, the stone of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> From the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="49.25 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God of thy father, and he will help thee; And from the Almighty, and he will bless thee — With blessings of heaven from above, With blessings of the deep that lieth under, With blessings of the breast and of the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Habakkuk 3:6; Isaiah 66:5"> The blessings of thy father surpass the blessings of my ancestors, Unto the bounds of the everlasting hills: They shall be on the head of Joseph, And on the crown of the head of him that was separated from his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> Benjamin — &#91;as&#93; a wolf will he tear to pieces; In the morning he will devour the prey, And in the evening he will divide the booty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; 1 Kings 18:31"> All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and this is what their father spoke to them; and he blessed them: every one according to his blessing he blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> And he charged them, and said to them, I am gathered to my people: bury me with my fathers in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> in the cave that is in the field of Machpelah, which is opposite to Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought of Ephron the Hittite along with the field for a possession of a sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebecca his wife; and there I buried Leah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> The purchase of the field, and of the cave that is in it, was from the children of Heth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> And when Jacob had made an end of commanding his sons, he gathered his feet into the bed, and expired, and was gathered to his peoples.</span>
