<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 3:20; Luke 1:6"> And Abram was ninety-nine years old, when Jehovah appeared to Abram, and said to him, I &#91;am&#93; the Almighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="17.1 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God: walk before my face, and be perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:4–6; Genesis 12:2; Psalms 105:8–11"> And I will set my covenant between me and thee, and will very greatly multiply thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 13:20; Genesis 17:17; Numbers 16:45"> And Abram fell on his face; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="17.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God talked with him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Galatians 3:28–29; Genesis 12:2"> It is I: behold, my covenant is with thee, and thou shalt be a father of a multitude of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; Genesis 17:15"> And thy name shall no more be called Abram, but thy name shall be Abraham; for a father of a multitude of nations have I made thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 17:4; Matthew 1:6–17; Genesis 35:11"> And I will make thee exceedingly fruitful, and I will make nations of thee, and kings shall come out of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–9; Acts 2:39; Genesis 26:24"> And I will establish my covenant between me and thee, and thy seed after thee in their generations, for an everlasting covenant, to be a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="17.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God to thee, and to thy seed after thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Psalms 105:11; Exodus 6:7"> And I give to thee, and to thy seed after thee, the land of thy sojourning, all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession; and I will be a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="17.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; Psalms 25:10; Psalms 103:18"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="17.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God said to Abraham, And &#91;as for&#93; thee, thou shalt keep my covenant, thou and thy seed after thee in their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:11–12; Romans 4:9–11; Acts 7:8"> This is my covenant which ye shall keep, between me and you and thy seed after thee — that every male among you be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> And ye shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin; and &#91;that&#93; shall be a sign of the covenant between me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Luke 2:21; Exodus 12:48–49"> And at eight days old shall every male in your generations be circumcised among you — he who is born in the house, and he who is bought with money, any stranger who is not of thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> He who is born in thy house, and he who is bought with thy money, must be circumcised; and my covenant shall be in your flesh for an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> And the uncircumcised male who hath not been circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin, that soul shall be cut off from his peoples: he hath broken my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="17.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God said to Abraham, &#91;As to&#93; Sarai thy wife, thou shalt not call her name Sarai, but Sarah shall be her name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> And I will bless her, and I will give thee a son also of her; and I will bless her, and she shall become nations: kings of peoples shall be of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> And Abraham fell on his face and laughed, and said in his heart, Shall &#91;a child&#93; be born to him that is a hundred years old? and shall Sarah, who is ninety years old, bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Psalms 41:12; Psalms 4:6"> And Abraham said to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="17.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, Oh that Ishmael might live before thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:13–20; Genesis 21:2–3; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="17.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God said, Sarah thy wife shall indeed bear thee a son; and thou shalt call his name Isaac; and I will establish my covenant with him, for an everlasting covenant for his seed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> And for Ishmael I have heard thee: behold, I will bless him, and will make him fruitful, and will very greatly multiply him; twelve princes shall he beget, and I will make him a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> But my covenant will I establish with Isaac, whom Sarah shall bear to thee at this appointed time in the next year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> And he left off talking with him; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="17.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God went up from Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 18:19; Genesis 17:10–14"> And Abraham took Ishmael his son, and all who were born in his house, and all who were bought with his money — every male among the people of Abraham's house — and circumcised the flesh of their foreskin on that same day, as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="17.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God had said to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> And Abraham was ninety-nine years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Ishmael his son was thirteen years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3–4; Genesis 12:4"> In the selfsame day was Abraham circumcised, and Ishmael his son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 18:19"> and all the men of his house, born in his house, or bought with money of the stranger, were circumcised with him.</span>
