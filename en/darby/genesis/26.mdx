<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> And there was a famine in the land, besides the former famine which had been in the days of Abraham. And Isaac went to Abimelech the king of the Philistines, to Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> And Jehovah appeared to him and said, Go not down to Egypt: dwell in the land that I shall tell thee of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> Sojourn in this land; and I will be with thee and bless thee; for unto thee, and unto thy seed, I will give all these countries; and I will perform the oath which I swore unto Abraham thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> And I will multiply thy seed as the stars of heaven, and unto thy seed will I give all these countries; and in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth bless themselves —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> because that Abraham hearkened to my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> And Isaac dwelt at Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> And the men of the place asked about his wife. And he said, She is my sister; for he feared to say, my wife, &#91;saying to himself,&#93; Lest the men of the place slay me on account of Rebecca — because she was fair in countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> And it came to pass when he had been there some time, that Abimelech the king of the Philistines looked out of the window, and saw, and behold, Isaac was dallying with Rebecca his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Abimelech called Isaac, and said, Behold, she is certainly thy wife; and how saidst thou, She is my sister? and Isaac said to him, Because I said, Lest I die on account of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> And Abimelech said, What is this thou hast done to us? But a little and one of the people might have lain with thy wife, and thou wouldest have brought a trespass on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> And Abimelech charged all the people, saying, He that touches this man or his wife shall certainly be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> And Isaac sowed in that land, and received in the same year a hundredfold; and Jehovah blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> And the man became great, and he became continually greater, until he was very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> And he had possessions of flocks, and possessions of herds, and a great number of servants; and the Philistines envied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> And all the wells that his father's servants had dug in the days of Abraham his father, the Philistines stopped them and filled them with earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> And Abimelech said to Isaac, Go from us; for thou art become much mightier than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Isaac departed thence, and pitched his camp in the valley of Gerar, and dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> And Isaac dug again the wells of water that they had dug in the days of Abraham his father, and that the Philistines had stopped after the death of Abraham; and he called their names after the names by which his father had called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Solomon 4:15"> And Isaac's servants dug in the valley, and found there a well of springing water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> But the shepherds of Gerar strove with Isaac's shepherds, saying, The water is ours. And he called the name of the well Esek, because they had quarrelled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> And they dug another well, and they strove for that also; and he called the name of it Sitnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> And he removed thence and dug another well; and they did not strive for that. And he called the name of it Rehoboth, and said, For now Jehovah has made room for us, and we shall be fruitful in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> And he went up thence to Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> And Jehovah appeared to him the same night, and said, I am the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="26.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Abraham thy father: fear not, for I am with thee, and will bless thee, and multiply thy seed for my servant Abraham's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> And he built an altar there, and called upon the name of Jehovah. And he pitched his tent there; and there Isaac's servants dug a well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> And Abimelech, and Ahuzzath his friend, and Phichol the captain of his host, went to him from Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> And Isaac said to them, Why are ye come to me, seeing ye hate me, and have driven me away from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> And they said, We saw certainly that Jehovah is with thee; and we said, Let there be then an oath between us — between us and thee, and let us make a covenant with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> that thou wilt do us no wrong, as we have not touched thee, and as we have done to thee nothing but good, and have let thee go in peace; thou art now blessed of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> And he made them a feast, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> And they rose early in the morning, and swore one to another; and Isaac sent them away, and they departed from him in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> And it came to pass the same day, that Isaac's servants came, and told him concerning the well that they had dug, and said to him, We have found water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> And he called it Shebah; therefore the name of the city is Beer-sheba to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> And Esau was forty years old, when he took as wives Judith the daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basmath the daughter of Elon the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> And they were a grief of mind to Isaac and to Rebecca.</span>
