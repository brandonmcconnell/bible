<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Mark 1:10; Revelation 20:11"> Oh, that thou wouldest rend the heavens, that thou wouldest come down, — that the mountains might flow down at thy presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 99:1; Jeremiah 33:9; Ezekiel 38:22–23"> — as fire kindleth brushwood, as the fire causeth water to boil, to make thy name known to thine adversaries, that the nations might tremble at thy presence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Psalms 65:5–6; Psalms 66:3"> When thou didst terrible things &#91;which&#93; we looked not for, thou camest down, and the mountains flowed down at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; Psalms 31:19; James 5:7"> Never have &#91;men&#93; heard, nor perceived by the ear, nor hath eye seen a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="64.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God beside thee, who acteth for him that waiteth for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> Thou meetest him that rejoiceth to do righteousness, those that remember thee in thy ways: (behold, thou wast wroth, and we have sinned:) in those is perpetuity, and we shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> And we are all become as an unclean &#91;thing&#93;, and all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags; and we all fade as a leaf, and our iniquities, like the wind, have carried us away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> and there is none that calleth upon thy name, that stirreth up himself to take hold of thee; for thou hast hidden thy face from us, and hast caused us to melt away through our iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> And now, Jehovah, thou art our Father; we are the clay, and thou our potter; and we all are the work of thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; Psalms 74:1–2; Psalms 79:13"> Be not wroth very sore, O Jehovah, neither remember iniquity for ever. Behold, see, we beseech thee, we are all thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> Thy holy cities are become a wilderness, Zion is a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> Our holy and our beautiful house, where our fathers praised thee, is burnt up with fire, and all our precious things are laid waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; Psalms 74:10–11; Psalms 83:1"> Wilt thou restrain thyself in presence of these things, Jehovah? Wilt thou hold thy peace, and afflict us very sore?</span>
