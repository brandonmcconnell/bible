<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Ezekiel 29:3; Isaiah 66:16"> In that day Jehovah, with his sore and great and strong sword, will visit leviathan the fleeing serpent, and leviathan the crooked serpent; and he will slay the monster that is in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Luke 20:9–18; Isaiah 5:1–7"> In that day &#91;there shall be&#93; a vineyard of pure wine; sing concerning it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; John 15:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:9"> I Jehovah keep it, I will water it every moment; lest any harm it, I will keep it night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:17; Hebrews 6:8; Matthew 3:12"> Fury is not in me. Oh that I had briars &#91;and&#93; thorns in battle against me! I would march against them, I would burn them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Job 22:21; Ephesians 2:16–17"> Or let him take hold of my strength; let him make peace with me: &#91;yea,&#93; let him make peace with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31; Hosea 14:5–6; Romans 11:16–26"> In the future Jacob shall take root; Israel shall blossom and bud, and they shall fill the face of the world with fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 10:20–25"> Hath he smitten him according to the smiting of those that smote him? Is he slain according to the slaughter of those slain by him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 4:11; Ezekiel 19:12"> In measure, when sending her away, didst thou contend with her: he hath taken &#91;her&#93; away with his rough wind in the day of the east wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:27; Isaiah 48:10; Isaiah 17:8"> By this, therefore, shall the iniquity of Jacob be purged; and this is all the fruit of the taking away of his sin: when he shall make all the stones of the altar as chalkstones that are crumbled in pieces, — the Asherahs and the sun-images shall not stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 32:13–14"> For the fortified city is solitary, a habitation abandoned and forsaken like a wilderness; there shall the calf feed, and there shall he lie down, and consume its boughs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 43:1"> When its branches are withered they shall be broken off; women shall come &#91;and&#93; set them on fire. For it is a people of no intelligence; therefore he that made them will not have mercy on them, and he who formed them will shew them no favour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Isaiah 56:8"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that Jehovah shall beat out from the flood of the river unto the torrent of Egypt, and ye shall be gathered one by one, &#91;ye&#93; children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Leviticus 25:9; Hebrews 12:22"> And it shall come to pass in that day, &#91;that&#93; the great trumpet shall be blown; and they shall come that were perishing in the land of Assyria, and the outcasts in the land of Egypt, and they shall worship Jehovah in the holy mountain at Jerusalem.</span>
