<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:15; Hosea 8:1; Acts 20:26–27"> Cry aloud, spare not, lift up thy voice like a trumpet, and declare unto my people their transgression, and to the house of Jacob their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:7–9"> Yet they seek me daily, and delight to know my ways, as a nation that doeth righteousness, and hath not forsaken the ordinance of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="58.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God; they ask of me the ordinances of righteousness, they take delight in approaching to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="58.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Malachi 3:14; Leviticus 16:29"> — Wherefore have we fasted, and thou seest not; have afflicted our soul, and thou takest no knowledge? Behold, in the day of your fast ye find what pleaseth &#91;you&#93;, and exact all your labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 21:9–13; Isaiah 59:2; Proverbs 21:27"> Behold, ye have fasted for strife and debate, and to smite with the fist of wickedness; ye do not at present fast, to cause your voice to be heard on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–19; Zechariah 7:5; Isaiah 49:8"> Is such the fast that I have chosen, a day for a man to afflict his soul, — that he should bow down his head as a bulrush, and spread sackcloth and ashes &#91;under him&#93;? Wilt thou call this a fast, and a day acceptable to Jehovah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:10–12; Isaiah 58:9; Jeremiah 34:8–11"> Is not this the fast which I have chosen: to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the thongs of the yoke, and to send forth free the crushed, and that ye break every yoke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–45; Luke 3:11; 1 John 3:17–18"> Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring to thy house the needy wanderers; when thou seest the naked, that thou cover him; and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 37:6"> Then shall thy light break forth as the dawn, and thy health shall spring forth speedily; and thy righteousness shall go before thee, the glory of Jehovah shall be thy rearguard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> Then shalt thou call, and Jehovah will answer; thou shalt cry, and he will say, Here I am. If thou take away from the midst of thee the yoke, the putting forth of the finger and the unjust speech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Isaiah 58:7–8; Isaiah 42:16"> and thou proffer thy soul to the hungry, and satisfy the afflicted soul: then shall thy light rise in the darkness, and thine obscurity be as midday;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 4:14; Jeremiah 31:12; Psalms 32:8"> and Jehovah will guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought, and strengthen thy bones; and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a water-spring, whose waters deceive not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Ezekiel 36:33; Amos 9:14"> And they &#91;that come&#93; of thee shall build the old waste places: thou shalt raise up the foundations &#91;that have remained&#93; from generation to generation; and thou shalt be called, Repairer of the breaches, restorer of frequented paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–27; Psalms 92:1–2; Nehemiah 13:15–22"> If thou turn back thy foot from the sabbath, &#91;from&#93; doing thy pleasure on my holy day, and call the sabbath a delight, the holy &#91;day&#93; of Jehovah, honourable; and thou honour him, not doing thine own ways, nor finding thine own pleasure, nor speaking &#91;idle&#93; words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Job 22:26; Isaiah 33:16"> then shalt thou delight thyself in Jehovah, and I will cause thee to ride on the high places of the earth, and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father: for the mouth of Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
