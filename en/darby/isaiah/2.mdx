<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 13:1; Habakkuk 1:1"> The word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> And it shall come to pass in the end of days, &#91;that&#93; the mountain of Jehovah's house shall be established on the top of the mountains, and shall be lifted up above the hills; and all the nations shall flow unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Luke 24:47; Zechariah 8:20–23"> And many peoples shall go and say, Come, and let us go up to the mountain of Jehovah, to the house of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Jacob; and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths. For out of Zion shall go forth the law, and Jehovah's word from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:18; Micah 4:3; Isaiah 11:6–9"> And he shall judge among the nations, and shall reprove many peoples; and they shall forge their swords into ploughshares, and their spears into pruning-knives: nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Luke 1:79; Isaiah 60:19–20"> House of Jacob, come ye, and let us walk in the light of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Kings 1:2; Psalms 106:35"> For thou hast cast off thy people, the house of Jacob, because they are filled &#91;with what comes&#93; from the east, and use auguries like the Philistines, and ally themselves with the children of foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Isaiah 30:16; Isaiah 31:1"> And their land is full of silver and gold, and there is no end of their treasures: their land also is full of horses, and there is no end of their chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 115:4–8; Revelation 9:20; Jeremiah 2:28"> And their land is full of idols; they bow themselves down to the work of their own hands, to that which their fingers have made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15; Joshua 24:19; Romans 3:23"> And the mean man shall be bowed down, and the great man shall be brought low: and do not thou forgive them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 6:15–16; Isaiah 2:19–21; Luke 23:30"> Enter into the rock, and hide thee in the dust, from before the terror of Jehovah, and from the glory of his majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:15–16; Psalms 18:27; Isaiah 2:17"> The lofty eyes of man shall be brought low, and the haughtiness of men shall be bowed down, and Jehovah alone shall be exalted in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 23:9; Proverbs 16:5"> For there shall be a day of Jehovah of hosts upon everything proud and lofty, and upon everything lifted up, and it shall be brought low;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Zechariah 11:1–2; Ezekiel 31:3–12"> and upon all the cedars of Lebanon, high and lifted up, and upon all the oaks of Bashan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:25; Isaiah 40:4; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> and upon all the lofty mountains, and upon all the hills that are lifted up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 25:12"> and upon every high tower, and upon every fenced wall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; Isaiah 23:1; Numbers 33:52"> and upon all the ships of Tarshish, and upon all pleasant works of art.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:29–30; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 13:11"> And the loftiness of man shall be bowed down, and the haughtiness of men shall be brought low; and Jehovah alone shall be exalted in that day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 36:25; Isaiah 21:9"> and the idols shall utterly pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Luke 23:30; Hosea 10:8"> And they shall go into the caves of the rocks, and into the holes of the earth, from before the terror of Jehovah, and from the glory of his majesty, when he shall arise to terrify the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 46:6"> In that day men shall cast away their idols of silver and their idols of gold, which they made &#91;each&#93; for himself to worship, to the moles and to the bats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Song of Solomon 2:14; Isaiah 2:10"> to go into the clefts of the rocks, and into the fissures of the cliffs, from before the terror of Jehovah, and from the glory of his majesty, when he shall arise to terrify the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Psalms 146:3; James 4:14"> Cease ye from man, whose breath is in his nostrils; for what account is to be made of him?</span>
