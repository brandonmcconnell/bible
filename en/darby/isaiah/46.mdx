<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:7; Judges 18:17–18; 2 Samuel 5:21"> They bend, they are bowed down together; they could not deliver the burden, and themselves are gone into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:31; Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1"> Hearken unto me, house of Jacob, and all the remnant of the house of Israel, ye who have been borne from the belly, who have been carried from the womb:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 48:14; Psalms 92:14"> Even to old age, I &#91;am&#93; HE, and unto hoary hairs I will carry &#91;you&#93;: It is I that have made, and I will bear, and I will carry, and will deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Isaiah 40:25; Psalms 89:6"> To whom will ye liken me and make me equal, or compare me, that we may be like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 40:19–20; Habakkuk 2:18–20"> — They lavish gold out of the bag, and weigh silver in the balance; they hire a goldsmith, and he maketh it a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="46.6 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>god: they fall down, yea, they worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 45:20; Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 46:1"> They bear him on the shoulder, they carry him, and set him in his place; there he standeth, he doth not remove from his place: yea, one crieth unto him, and he answereth not; he saveth him not out of his trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:18–21; Psalms 115:8; Ezekiel 18:28"> Remember this, and shew yourselves men; call it to mind, ye transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 111:4; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 45:21–22"> Remember the former things of old; for I &#91;am&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="46.9 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and there is none else; &#91;I am&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="46.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and there is none like me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 33:11; Ephesians 1:9–11"> declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 14:24–27; Job 23:13"> calling a bird of prey from the east, the man of my counsel from a far country. Yea, I have spoken, I will also bring it to pass; I have purposed it, I will also do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:5; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 76:5"> Hearken unto me, ye stout-hearted, that are far from righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 61:3"> I bring near my righteousness; it shall not be far off, and my salvation shall not delay; and I will give salvation in Zion, &#91;and&#93; unto Israel my glory.</span>
