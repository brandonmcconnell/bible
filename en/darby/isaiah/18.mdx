<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; 2 Kings 19:9; Zephaniah 2:12"> Ha! land shadowing with wings, which art beyond the rivers of Cush,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 18:7; Genesis 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 16:8"> that sendest ambassadors over the sea, and in vessels of papyrus upon the waters, &#91;saying,&#93; Go, swift messengers, to a nation scattered and ravaged, to a people terrible from their existence and thenceforth; to a nation of continued waiting and of treading down, whose land the rivers have spoiled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 26:11; Matthew 13:9"> All ye inhabitants of the world, and dwellers on the earth, when a banner is lifted up on the mountains, see ye, and when a trumpet is blown, hear ye!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Isaiah 26:21; Isaiah 18:7"> For thus hath Jehovah said unto me: I will take my rest, and I will observe from my dwelling-place like clear heat upon herbs, like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Song of Solomon 2:13; Ezekiel 17:6–10"> For before the harvest, when the blossoming is over, and the flower becometh a ripening grape, he shall both cut off the sprigs with pruning-knives, and take away &#91;and&#93; cut down the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Ezekiel 39:17–20; Ezekiel 32:4–6"> They shall be left together unto the mountain birds of prey, and to the beasts of the earth; and the birds of prey shall summer upon them, and all the beasts of the earth shall winter upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Acts 8:27–28; Zechariah 14:16–17"> In that time shall a present be brought unto Jehovah of hosts of a people scattered and ravaged, — and from a people terrible from their existence and thenceforth, a nation of continued waiting and of treading down, whose land the rivers have spoiled, ...to the place of the name of Jehovah of hosts, the mount Zion.</span>
