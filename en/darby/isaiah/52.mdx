<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Isaiah 51:17; Zechariah 3:4"> Awake, awake; put on thy strength, O Zion; put on thy beautiful garments, Jerusalem, the holy city: for henceforth there shall no more come into thee the uncircumcised and the unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Jeremiah 51:50; Luke 4:18"> Shake thyself from the dust; arise, sit down, Jerusalem: loose thyself from the bands of thy neck, captive daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; Isaiah 45:13; Psalms 44:12"> For thus saith Jehovah: Ye have sold yourselves for nought, and ye shall be redeemed without money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Psalms 69:4; Acts 7:14–15"> For thus saith the Lord Jehovah: My people went down at the first into Egypt to sojourn there, and Assyria oppressed them without cause;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 74:10"> and now, what have I here, saith Jehovah, that my people hath been taken away for nought? They that rule over them make them to howl, saith Jehovah; and continually all the day is my name scorned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10–11; Ezekiel 37:13–14; Ezekiel 20:44"> Therefore my people shall know my name; therefore &#91;they shall know&#93; in that day that I &#91;am&#93; HE, that saith, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:12–15; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 61:1–3"> How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that announceth glad tidings, that publisheth peace; that announceth glad tidings of good, that publisheth salvation, that saith unto Zion, Thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="52.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God reigneth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6; Ezekiel 33:7; Jeremiah 32:39"> — The voice of thy watchmen, they lift up the voice, they sing aloud together; for they shall see eye to eye, when Jehovah shall bring again Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 98:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Break forth, sing aloud together, waste places of Jerusalem; for Jehovah comforteth his people, he hath redeemed Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 3:6; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 98:1–3"> Jehovah hath made bare his holy arm in the sight of all the nations; and all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="52.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17; Zechariah 2:6–7; Isaiah 48:20"> — Depart, depart, go out from thence, touch not what is unclean; go out of the midst of her, be ye clean, that bear the vessels of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8; Deuteronomy 20:4; Exodus 14:19–20"> For ye shall not go out with haste, nor go by flight; for Jehovah will go before you, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="52.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of Israel will be your rear-guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–11; Matthew 28:18; John 3:31"> Behold, my servant shall deal prudently; he shall be exalted and be lifted up, and be very high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:2–5; Matthew 26:67"> As many were astonished at thee — his visage was so marred more than any man, and his form more than the children of men</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20–21; Isaiah 49:7; Isaiah 55:5"> — so shall he astonish many nations; kings shall shut their mouths at him: for what had not been told them shall they see, and what they had not heard shall they consider.</span>
