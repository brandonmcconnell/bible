<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 14:13; Isaiah 1:24"> For behold, the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, will take away from Jerusalem and from Judah stay and staff, the whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 2 Kings 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> the mighty man and the man of war, the judge and the prophet, and the diviner and the elder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> the captain of fifty, and the honourable man, and the counsellor, and the clever among artificers, and the one versed in enchantments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I will appoint youths as their princes, and children shall rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–8; Malachi 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> And the people shall be oppressed one by the other, and each by his neighbour; the child will be insolent against the elder, and the base against the honourable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> When a man shall take hold of his brother, in his father's house, &#91;and shall say:&#93; Thou hast clothing; be our chief, and let this ruin be under thy hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Ezekiel 34:4"> he will lift up &#91;his hand&#93; in that day, saying, I cannot be a healer, and in my house there is neither bread nor clothing; ye shall not make me a chief of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Ezekiel 9:9; Malachi 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem stumbleth and Judah falleth, because their tongue and their doings are against Jehovah, to provoke the eyes of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; 2 Kings 9:30"> The look of their face doth witness against them, and they declare their sin as Sodom: they hide it not. Woe unto their soul! for they have brought evil upon themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Say ye of the righteous that it shall be well &#91;with him&#93;, for they shall eat the fruit of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; Psalms 62:12"> Woe unto the wicked! it shall be ill &#91;with him&#93;, because the desert of his hands shall be rendered unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4; Nahum 3:13; 2 Kings 11:1"> &#91;As for&#93; my people, children are their oppressors, and women rule over them. My people! they that guide thee mislead &#91;thee&#93;, and destroy the way of thy paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Hosea 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> Jehovah setteth himself to plead, and standeth to judge the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; Psalms 143:2; Isaiah 3:2–3"> Jehovah will enter into judgment with the elders of his people and their princes, &#91;saying:&#93; It is ye that have eaten up the vineyard: the spoil of the poor is in your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Psalms 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> What mean ye that ye crush my people, and grind the faces of the afflicted? saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 4:4"> And Jehovah said, Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, and walk with stretched-out neck and wanton eyes, and go along mincing, and making a tinkling with their feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:22; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> therefore the Lord will make bald the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion, and Jehovah will lay bare their secret parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> In that day the Lord will take away the ornament of anklets, and the little suns and crescents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> the pearl-drops, and the bracelets, and the veils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Ezekiel 16:12"> the head-dresses, and the stepping chains, and the girdles, and the scent-boxes, and the amulets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> the finger-rings, and the nose-rings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> the festival-robes, and the tunics, and the mantles, and the wallets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> the mirrors, and the fine linen bodices, and the turbans, and the flowing veils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:31; Isaiah 22:12"> And it shall come to pass, instead of perfume there shall be rottenness; and instead of a girdle, a rope; and instead of well-set hair, baldness; and instead of a robe of display, a girding of sackcloth; brand instead of beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:9; Isaiah 1:20; Amos 9:10"> Thy men shall fall by the sword, and thy mighty in the fight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jeremiah 14:2; Lamentations 1:4"> and her gates shall lament and mourn; and, stripped, she shall sit upon the ground.</span>
