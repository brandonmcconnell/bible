<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Isaiah 51:12; Isaiah 51:3"> Comfort ye, comfort ye my people, saith your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="40.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:12; Isaiah 61:7; Hosea 2:14"> Speak to the heart of Jerusalem, and cry unto her, that her time of suffering is accomplished, that her iniquity is pardoned; for she hath received of Jehovah's hand double for all her sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> The voice of one crying in the wilderness: Prepare ye the way of Jehovah, make straight in the desert a highway for our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="40.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Ezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Every valley shall be raised up, and every mountain and hill shall be brought low; and the crooked shall be made straight, and the rough places a plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Joel 2:28; Isaiah 52:10"> And the glory of Jehovah shall be revealed, and all flesh shall see &#91;it&#93; together: for the mouth of Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> A voice saith, Cry. And he saith, What shall I cry? — All flesh is grass, and all the comeliness thereof as the flower of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 41:21"> The grass withereth, the flower fadeth, for the breath of Jehovah bloweth upon it: surely the people is grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> The grass withereth, the flower fadeth; but the word of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="40.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God abideth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7–8; Isaiah 12:2; Isaiah 25:9"> O Zion, that bringest glad tidings, get thee up into a high mountain; O Jerusalem, that bringest glad tidings, lift up thy voice with strength: lift it up, be not afraid; say unto the cities of Judah, Behold your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="40.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Isaiah 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> Behold, the Lord Jehovah will come with might, and his arm shall rule for him; behold, his reward is with him, and his recompence before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Micah 5:4; Psalms 23:1–6"> He will feed his flock like a shepherd: he will gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom; he will gently lead those that give suck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25–26"> Who hath measured the waters in the hollow of his hand, and meted out the heavens with &#91;his&#93; span, and grasped the dust of the earth in a measure, and weighed the mountains in a balance, and the hills in scales?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> Who hath directed the Spirit of Jehovah, and, &#91;as&#93; his counsellor, hath taught him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> With whom took he counsel, and &#91;who&#93; gave him intelligence, and instructed him in the path of judgment, and taught him knowledge, and shewed him the way of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremiah 10:10"> Behold, the nations are esteemed as a drop of the bucket, and as the fine dust on the scales; behold, he taketh up the isles as an atom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Psalms 40:6; Psalms 50:9–12"> And Lebanon is not sufficient to burn, nor the beasts thereof sufficient for a burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Isaiah 29:7"> All the nations are as nothing before him; they are esteemed by him less than a cipher, and vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:5; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 40:25"> To whom then will ye liken<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="40.18 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God? and what likeness will ye compare unto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 46:6–7"> The workman casteth a graven image, and the goldsmith spreadeth it over with gold, and casteth silver chains &#91;for it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:3–4; Isaiah 41:7; Isaiah 46:7"> He that is impoverished, so that he hath no offering, chooseth a tree that doth not rot; he seeketh unto him a skilled workman to prepare a graven image that shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:13; Psalms 50:6; Acts 14:17"> — Do ye not know? Have ye not heard? Hath it not been told you from the beginning? Have ye not understood the foundation of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 42:5"> &#91;It is&#93; he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as grasshoppers; that stretcheth out the heavens as a gauze curtain, and spreadeth them out as a tent to dwell in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 34:12"> that bringeth the princes to nothing, that maketh the judges of the earth as vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Isaiah 17:13; Haggai 1:9"> Scarcely are they planted, scarcely are they sown, scarcely hath their stock taken root in the earth, but he also bloweth upon them and they wither, and the whirlwind taketh them away as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> To whom then will ye liken me, or shall I be equal? saith the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremiah 32:17–19; Isaiah 48:13"> Lift up your eyes on high, and see! Who hath created these things, bringing out their host by number? He calleth them all by name; through the greatness of his might and strength of power, not one faileth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Isaiah 49:4; Isaiah 49:14"> Why sayest thou, Jacob, and speakest, O Israel, My way is hid from Jehovah, and my right is passed away from my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="40.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Psalms 147:5; Isaiah 55:8–9"> Dost thou not know, hast thou not heard, that the everlasting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="40.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, Jehovah, the Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not nor tireth? There is no searching of his understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He giveth power to the faint; and to him that hath no might he increaseth strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Psalms 34:10; Psalms 33:16"> Even the youths shall faint and shall tire, and the young men shall stumble and fall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Psalms 27:13–14"> but they that wait upon Jehovah shall renew &#91;their&#93; strength: they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not tire; they shall walk, and not faint.</span>
