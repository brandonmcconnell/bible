<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Isaiah 24:16; Isaiah 10:12"> Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and that dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! When thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; when thou shalt make an end of dealing treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Isaiah 59:16; Isaiah 30:18–19"> Jehovah, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be their arm every morning, yea, our salvation in the time of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–14; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 37:11–18"> At the noise of the tumult the peoples fled; at the lifting up of thyself the nations were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Joel 2:25; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And your spoil shall be gathered &#91;like&#93; the gathering of the caterpillar: as the running of locusts shall they run upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Isaiah 32:15–18"> Jehovah is exalted; for he dwelleth on high: he hath filled Zion with justice and righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> and he shall be the stability of thy times, the riches of salvation, wisdom and knowledge: the fear of Jehovah shall be your treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 18:37–3; Isaiah 36:22"> Behold, their valiant ones cry without; the messengers of peace weep bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Isaiah 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> The highways are desolate, the wayfaring man ceaseth. He hath broken the covenant, he hath despised the cities, he regardeth no man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Nahum 1:4; Isaiah 65:10"> The land mourneth, it languisheth; Lebanon is ashamed, is withered; the Sharon is become as a desert, and Bashan and Carmel are stripped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> Now will I arise, saith Jehovah; now will I be exalted, now will I lift up myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Isaiah 59:4; James 1:15"> Ye shall conceive dry grass, ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath shall devour you &#91;as&#93; fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; 2 Samuel 23:6–7; Isaiah 10:17"> And the peoples shall be &#91;as&#93; burnings of lime, &#91;as&#93; thorns cut up shall they be burned in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> Hear, ye that are far off, what I have done; and ye that are near, acknowledge my might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Isaiah 66:24"> The sinners in Zion are afraid; trembling hath surprised the hypocrites: Who among us shall dwell with the consuming fire? who among us shall dwell with everlasting flames?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> — He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from taking hold of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Isaiah 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> he shall dwell on high, the fortresses of the rocks shall be his high retreat; bread shall be given him, his water shall be sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> Thine eyes shall see the King in his beauty; they shall behold the land that is far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 31:7–8; Isaiah 38:9–22"> Thy heart shall meditate on terror: Where is the scribe? where is the receiver? where is he that counted the towers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremiah 5:15"> Thou shalt no more see the fierce people, a people of a deeper speech than thou canst comprehend, of a stammering tongue that cannot be understood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:5; Isaiah 32:18; Isaiah 54:2"> Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tent that shall not be removed, the stakes whereof shall never be pulled up, neither shall any of its cords be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 46:4–5; Isaiah 48:18; Isaiah 66:12"> but there Jehovah is unto us glorious, — a place of rivers, of broad streams: no galley with oars shall go there, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> For Jehovah is our judge, Jehovah, our lawgiver, Jehovah, our king: he will save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 7:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> Thy tacklings are loosed; they strengthen not the socket of their mast, they cannot spread the sail: then is the prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> And the inhabitant shall not say, I am sick: the people that dwell therein shall be forgiven &#91;their&#93; iniquity.</span>
