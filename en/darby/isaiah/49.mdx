<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Galatians 1:15; Isaiah 44:2"> Listen, O isles, unto me; and hearken, ye peoples from afar. Jehovah hath called me from the womb; from the bowels of my mother hath he made mention of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 50:4"> And he hath made my mouth like a sharp sword, he hath concealed me under the shadow of his hand, and he hath made me a polished shaft: in his quiver hath he hidden me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:9; Isaiah 44:23"> And he said unto me, Thou art my servant, Israel, in whom I will glorify myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Galatians 4:11; Isaiah 53:10–12"> — And I said, I have laboured in vain, I have spent my strength for nought and in vain; nevertheless my judgment is with Jehovah, and my work with my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="49.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Matthew 17:5; John 3:35"> And now, saith Jehovah, that formed me from the womb to be his servant, that I should bring Jacob again to him; (though Israel be not gathered, yet shall I be glorified in the eyes of Jehovah, and my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="49.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God shall be my strength;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> — and he saith, It is a small thing that thou shouldest be my servant to raise up the tribes of Jacob, and to restore the preserved of Israel; I have even given thee for a light of the nations, that thou mayest be my salvation unto the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; 1 Peter 2:4; Isaiah 53:3"> Thus saith Jehovah, the Redeemer of Israel, his Holy One, to him whom man despiseth, to him whom the nation abhorreth, to the servant of rulers: Kings shall see and arise, princes, and they shall worship, because of Jehovah who is faithful, the Holy One of Israel, who hath chosen thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 42:6; Psalms 69:13"> Thus saith Jehovah: In a time of acceptance have I answered thee, and in the day of salvation have I helped thee; and I will preserve thee, and give thee for a covenant of the people, to establish the land, to cause to inherit the desolate heritages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 42:7; Isaiah 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> saying to the prisoners, Go forth; to them that are in darkness, Shew yourselves. They shall feed in the ways, and their pasture shall be on all bare hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Matthew 5:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> They shall not hunger nor thirst, neither shall the heat nor sun smite them; for he that hath mercy on them will lead them, and by the springs of water will he guide them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8–10"> And I will make all my mountains a way, and my highways shall be raised up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 11:15"> Behold, these shall come from afar; and behold, these from the north and from the west; and these from the land of Sinim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 44:23; Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 96:11–13"> Shout, ye heavens; and be joyful, thou earth; and break forth into singing, ye mountains: for Jehovah hath comforted his people, and will have mercy upon his afflicted ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27; Romans 11:1–5; Psalms 22:1"> But Zion said, Jehovah hath forsaken me, and the Lord hath forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:21; Psalms 103:13; Matthew 7:11"> Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? Even these forget, but I will not forget thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 2:23; Isaiah 62:6; Isaiah 60:18"> Lo, I have graven thee upon the palms of &#91;my&#93; hands; thy walls are continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:13; Isaiah 62:5; Ezekiel 28:24"> Thy sons shall make haste; thy destroyers and they that laid thee waste shall go forth from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–3; Isaiah 66:12–13; Isaiah 61:10"> Lift up thine eyes round about and behold: they all gather themselves together, they come to thee. As I live, saith Jehovah, thou shalt indeed clothe thee with them all as with an ornament, and bind them on as a bride doth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10; Isaiah 54:1–2; Jeremiah 30:18–19"> For &#91;in&#93; thy waste and thy desolate places, and thy destroyed land, thou shalt even now be too straitened by reason of the inhabitants, and they that swallowed thee up shall be far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 1:10; Joshua 17:14–16; Galatians 4:26–28"> The children of thy bereavement shall yet say in thine ears, The place is too narrow for me: make room for me, that I may dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Isaiah 3:26; Lamentations 1:1–3"> And thou shalt say in thy heart, Who hath borne me these, seeing I had lost my children and was desolate, an exile, and driven about? and who hath brought up these? behold, I was left alone; these, where were they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 14:2"> Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I will lift up my hand to the nations, and set up my banner to the peoples; and they shall bring thy sons in &#91;their&#93; bosom, and thy daughters shall be carried upon the shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 60:3"> And kings shall be thy nursing-fathers, and their princesses thy nursing-mothers: they shall bow down to thee with the face toward the earth, and lick up the dust of thy feet. And thou shalt know that I &#91;am&#93; Jehovah; for they shall not be ashamed who wait on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:21–22; Psalms 124:6–7; Matthew 12:29"> Shall the prey be taken from the mighty? and shall he that is rightfully captive be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 54:15–17; Isaiah 35:4; Isaiah 41:11–12"> For thus saith Jehovah: Even the captive of the mighty shall be taken away, and the prey of the terrible shall be delivered; and I will strive with him that striveth with thee, and I will save thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Isaiah 41:14–20; Revelation 16:6"> And I will feed them that oppress thee with their own flesh; and they shall be drunken with their own blood, as with new wine. And all flesh shall know that I, Jehovah, &#91;am&#93; thy Saviour and thy Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.</span>
