<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:35; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 55:12–13"> The wilderness and the dry land shall be gladdened; and the desert shall rejoice, and blossom as the rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 60:13; Amos 9:13–15"> It shall blossom abundantly and rejoice even with joy and shouting: the glory of Lebanon shall be given unto it, the excellency of Carmel and Sharon. They shall see the glory of Jehovah, the excellency of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="35.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Job 4:3–4; Luke 22:32"> Strengthen the weak hands and confirm the tottering knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Isaiah 41:10–14; Haggai 2:4"> Say to them that are of a timid heart, Be strong, fear not; behold your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="35.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God: vengeance cometh, the recompense of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="35.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God! He will come himself, and save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6–7; Luke 7:20–23; Isaiah 29:18"> Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf be unstopped;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:8–9; John 7:37–39; Luke 11:14"> then shall the lame &#91;man&#93; leap as a hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and torrents in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Isaiah 49:10; John 4:14"> And the mirage shall become a pool, and the thirsty land springs of water: in the habitation of wild dogs, where they lay down, shall be grass with reeds and rushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; Isaiah 42:16; John 14:6"> And a highway shall be there and a way, and it shall be called, The way of holiness: the unclean shall not pass through it; but it shall be for these. Those that go &#91;this&#93; way — even fools, — shall not err &#91;therein&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Hosea 2:18; Leviticus 26:6"> No lion shall be there, nor shall ravenous beast go up thereon, nor be found there; but the redeemed shall walk &#91;there&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:19; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 25:8"> And the ransomed of Jehovah shall return, and come to Zion with singing; and everlasting joy shall be upon their heads: they shall obtain gladness and joy, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.</span>
