<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2; Isaiah 1:2; Hosea 7:13"> Woe to the rebellious children, saith Jehovah, who take counsel, but not of me, and who make leagues, but not by my Spirit, that they may heap sin upon sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Numbers 27:21; Deuteronomy 28:68"> who walk to go down into Egypt, and have not asked of my mouth, — to take refuge under the protection of Pharaoh, and trust in the shadow of Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 20:5; Romans 10:11; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> For to you the protection of Pharaoh shall be a shame, and the trust in the shadow of Egypt a confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Isaiah 57:9; Jeremiah 43:7"> For his princes were at Zoan, and his ambassadors came to Hanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:36; Isaiah 30:7; Isaiah 30:16"> They were all ashamed of a people &#91;that&#93; did not profit them, nor were a help or profit, but a shame, and also a reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 11:4"> — The burden of the beasts of the south: Through a land of trouble and anguish, whence come the lioness and lion, the viper and fiery flying serpent, they carry their riches upon the shoulders of asses, and their treasures upon the bunches of camels, to the people that shall not profit &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:22; Isaiah 7:4; Psalms 118:8–9"> For Egypt shall help in vain, and to no purpose; therefore have I named her, Arrogance, that doeth nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:22; Habakkuk 2:2; Isaiah 8:1"> Now go, write it before them on a tablet, and record it in a book, that it may be for the time to come, as a witness for ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Acts 7:51; Zechariah 1:4–6"> that this is a rebellious people, lying children, children that will not hear the law of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–10; Romans 16:18; Amos 2:12"> who say to the seers, See not; and to the prophets, Prophesy not unto us right things; speak unto us smooth things, prophesy deceits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Romans 1:30; John 15:23–24"> get you out of the way, turn aside out of the path, cause the Holy One of Israel to cease from before us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Isaiah 5:24; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Therefore thus saith the Holy One of Israel: Because ye reject this word, and confide in oppression and wilfulness, and depend thereon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 29:5; Ezekiel 13:10–15; 1 Kings 20:30"> therefore this iniquity shall be to you as a breach ready to fall, swelling out in a towering wall, whose breaking shall come suddenly in an instant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:10–11; 2 Peter 2:4–5"> And he shall break it as the breaking of a potter's vessel, that is broken in pieces unsparingly; and in the pieces of it there shall not be found a sherd to take fire from the hearth, or to scoop water out of the cistern.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Isaiah 32:17; Jeremiah 3:22–23"> For thus saith the Lord Jehovah, the Holy One of Israel: In returning and rest shall ye be saved, in quietness and confidence shall be your strength; but ye would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1; Isaiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 28:25"> And ye said, No, but we will flee upon horses, — therefore shall ye flee; and, We will ride upon the swift, — therefore shall they that pursue you be swift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 32:30"> One thousand &#91;shall flee&#93; at the rebuke of one; at the rebuke of five shall ye flee: till ye be left as a beacon upon the top of a mountain, and as a banner on a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Peter 3:9; Proverbs 16:20"> And therefore will Jehovah wait, that he may be gracious unto you, and therefore will he lift himself up, that he may have mercy upon you; for Jehovah is a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of judgment: blessed are all they that wait for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:20; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> For the people shall dwell in Zion, at Jerusalem. Thou shalt weep no more; he will be very gracious unto thee at the voice of thy cry; as he heareth it, he will answer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; Psalms 80:5; Amos 8:11–12"> And the Lord will give you the bread of adversity, and the water of oppression; yet thy teachers shall not be hidden any more, but thine eyes shall see thy teachers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 58:11"> And when ye turn to the right hand or when ye turn to the left, thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 17:3–4; Isaiah 31:7; Zechariah 13:2"> And ye shall defile the silver covering of your graven images, and the gold overlaying of your molten images; thou shalt cast them away as a menstruous cloth: Out! shalt thou say unto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:11–12; Isaiah 32:20; Psalms 65:9–13"> And he will give the rain of thy seed with which thou shalt sow the ground; and bread, the produce of the ground, and it shall be fat and rich. In that day shall thy cattle feed in large pastures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 45:6; Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Samuel 8:12"> and the oxen and the asses that till the ground shall eat salted provender, which hath been winnowed with the shovel and with the fan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19–20; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 2:14–15"> And there shall be upon every high mountain and upon every hill that is lifted up, brooks &#91;and&#93; water-courses, in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 22:5"> And the light of the moon shall be as the light of the sun, and the light of the sun shall be sevenfold, as the light of seven days, in the day that Jehovah bindeth up the breach of his people, and healeth the wound of their stroke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Nahum 1:5–6; Psalms 18:7–9"> Behold, the name of Jehovah cometh from far, burning &#91;with&#93; his anger — a grievous conflagration; his lips are full of indignation, and his tongue as a consuming fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 11:4; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> and his breath as an overflowing torrent, which reacheth even to the neck, to sift the nations with the sieve of destruction, and &#91;to put&#93; a bridle into the jaws of the peoples, that causeth them to go astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 95:1–2; Exodus 15:1–21"> Ye shall have a song, as in the night when a feast is sanctified; and joy of heart, as of one who goeth with a pipe to come unto the mountain of Jehovah, to the Rock of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:13–14; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 29:6"> And Jehovah will cause the majesty of his voice to be heard, and will shew the lighting down of his arm with indignation of anger, and a flame of consuming fire, with waterflood and storm and hailstones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 9:4; Micah 5:5–6"> For through the voice of Jehovah shall the Assyrian be broken down: he will smite &#91;him&#93; with the rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 11:15; Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And wherever shall pass the appointed staff, which Jehovah shall lay upon him, it shall be with tambours and harps; and with tumultuous battles will he fight with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Genesis 19:24; Jeremiah 19:6"> For Topheth is prepared of old; for the king also it is prepared: he hath made it deep and large; its pile is fire and much wood; the breath of Jehovah, like a stream of brimstone, doth kindle it.</span>
