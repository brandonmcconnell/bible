<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:15–16; 2 Kings 15:29; Isaiah 8:22"> Nevertheless the darkness shall not be as when the distress was in the &#91;land&#93;, at the time he at first lightly, and afterwards heavily, visited the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, — the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:16; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> the people that walked in darkness have seen a great light; they that dwelt in the land of the shadow of death, upon them light hath shone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Psalms 119:162; Isaiah 26:15"> Thou hast multiplied the nation, hast increased its joy: they joy before thee like to the joy in harvest; as &#91;men&#93; rejoice when they divide the spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 10:26–27"> For thou hast broken the yoke of his burden and the staff of his shoulder, the rod of his oppressor, as in the day of Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 46:9; Isaiah 30:33"> For every boot of him that is shod for the tumult, and the garment rolled in blood, shall be for burning, fuel for fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:11; Matthew 28:18; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given; and the government shall be upon his shoulder; and his name is called Wonderful, Counsellor, Mighty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.6 El">a</a></sup>God, Father of Eternity, Prince of Peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Daniel 2:44; Daniel 7:14"> Of the increase of his government and of peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David and over his kingdom, to establish it, and to uphold it with judgment and with righteousness, from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of Jehovah of hosts will perform this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–8; Zechariah 1:6; Zechariah 5:1–4"> The Lord sent a word unto Jacob, and it lighteth upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:12; 1 Peter 5:5; Ezekiel 7:27"> And all the people shall know &#91;it&#93;, Ephraim and the inhabitant of Samaria, that say in pride and stoutness of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; 1 Kings 10:27; 1 Kings 7:9–12"> The bricks are fallen down, but we will build with hewn stones; the sycamore trees are cut down, but we will replace them with cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4–7; Isaiah 17:1–5; Isaiah 7:8"> And Jehovah will set up the adversaries of Rezin against him, and arm his enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; 2 Chronicles 28:18; Psalms 79:7"> the Syrians on the east, and the Philistines on the west; and they shall devour Israel with open mouth. For all this his anger is not turned away, and his hand is stretched out still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 5:3"> But the people turneth not unto him that smiteth them, and they do not seek Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 19:15; Revelation 18:8; Hosea 1:6"> And Jehovah will cut off from Israel head and tail, palm-branch and rush, in one day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Matthew 24:24; 1 Samuel 9:6"> the ancient and honourable, he is the head; and the prophet that teacheth lies, he is the tail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 3:12; Matthew 23:16–36"> For the guides of this people mislead &#91;them&#93;; and they that are guided by them are swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Isaiah 10:6; Isaiah 5:25"> Therefore the Lord will not rejoice in their young men, neither will he have mercy on their fatherless and on their widows; for every one is a hypocrite and an evildoer, and every mouth speaketh folly. For all this his anger is not turned away, and his hand is stretched out still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 83:14; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 5:24"> For wickedness burneth as a fire: it devoureth briars and thorns, and kindleth in the thickets of the forest, and they go rolling up like a pillar of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Micah 7:6; Joel 2:2"> Through the wrath of Jehovah of hosts is the land burned up, and the people is as fuel for fire: a man spareth not his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Jeremiah 19:9; Lamentations 4:10"> and he snatcheth on the right hand, and is hungry, and eateth on the left hand; and they are not satisfied. They eat every man the flesh of his own arm:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Galatians 5:15; Isaiah 9:12"> Manasseh, Ephraim, and Ephraim, Manasseh; &#91;and&#93; they together are against Judah. For all this his anger is not turned away, and his hand is stretched out still.</span>
