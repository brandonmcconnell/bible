<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; Psalms 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> Jehovah, thou art my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God: I will exalt thee; I will celebrate thy name, for thou hast done wonderful things; counsels of old &#91;which are&#93; faithfulness &#91;and&#93; truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1; Isaiah 13:22; Isaiah 25:12"> For thou hast made of the city a heap, of the fortified town a ruin, the palace of strangers to be no city; it shall never be built up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Isaiah 49:23–26; Psalms 46:10–11"> Therefore shall the mighty people glorify thee, the city of terrible nations shall fear thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Isaiah 32:2; James 2:5"> For thou hast been a fortress to the poor, a fortress for the needy in his distress, a refuge from the storm, a shadow from the heat: for the blast of the terrible ones &#91;has been&#93; as the storm &#91;against&#93; a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 105:39"> Thou hast subdued the tumult of strangers, as the heat in a dry place; &#91;as&#93; the heat, by the shadow of a cloud, &#91;so&#93; the song of the terrible ones is brought low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; Psalms 63:5; Zechariah 9:16–17"> And in this mountain will Jehovah of hosts make unto all peoples a feast of fat things, a feast of wines on the lees, of fat things full of marrow, of wines on the lees well refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> And he will destroy in this mountain the face of the veil which veileth all the peoples, and the covering that is spread over all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> He will swallow up death in victory. And the Lord Jehovah will wipe away tears from off all faces; and the reproach of his people will he take away from off all the earth: for Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Micah 7:7"> And it shall be said in that day, Behold, this is our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God; we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is Jehovah, we have waited for him; we will be glad and rejoice in his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Isaiah 18:4; Zephaniah 2:9"> For in this mountain shall the hand of Jehovah rest, and Moab shall be trodden down under him, as straw is trodden down in the dunghill;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 16:6"> and he shall spread forth his hands in the midst of them, as he that swimmeth spreadeth them forth to swim; and he shall bring down their pride together with the plots of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Isaiah 14:23"> And the fortress of the high defences of thy walls will he bring down, lay low, bring to the ground, into the dust.</span>
