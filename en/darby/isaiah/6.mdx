<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:41; Revelation 15:8; Revelation 7:15–17"> In the year of the death of king Uzziah, I saw the Lord sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up; and his train filled the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:8; Ezekiel 1:11; Psalms 104:4"> Seraphim were standing above him: each had six wings; with twain he covered his face, and with twain he covered his feet, and with twain he flew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; Psalms 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> And one called to the other and said, Holy, holy, holy is Jehovah of hosts; the whole earth is full of his glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Exodus 40:34; 2 Chronicles 5:13–1"> And the foundations of the thresholds shook at the voice of him that cried, and the house was filled with smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 5:8–9; James 3:6–10; Jeremiah 9:3–8"> And I said, Woe unto me! for I am undone; for I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips: for mine eyes have seen the King, Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Ezekiel 10:2"> And one of the seraphim flew unto me, and he had in his hand a glowing coal, which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Isaiah 43:25"> and he made it touch my mouth, and said, Behold, this hath touched thy lips; and thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin expiated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> And I heard the voice of the Lord saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? And I said, Here am I; send me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> And he said, Go; and thou shalt say unto this people, Hearing ye shall hear and shall not understand, and seeing ye shall see and shall not perceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremiah 5:21"> Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and blind their eyes; lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and be converted, and healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1–12; Psalms 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> And I said, Lord, how long? And he said, Until the cities be wasted, without inhabitant, and the houses without man, and the land become an utter desolation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 25:11"> and Jehovah have removed men far away, and the solitude be great in the midst of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Romans 11:5–6; Genesis 22:18"> But a tenth part shall still be therein, and it shall return and be eaten; as the terebinth and as the oak whose trunk &#91;remaineth&#93; after the felling: the holy seed shall be the trunk thereof.</span>
