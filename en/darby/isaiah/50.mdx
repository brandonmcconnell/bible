<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Jeremiah 3:8; Matthew 18:25"> Thus saith Jehovah: Where is the bill of your mother's divorce, whom I have put away? or which of my creditors is it to whom I have sold you? Behold, through your iniquities have ye sold yourselves, and for your transgressions is your mother put away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 59:1; Isaiah 66:4; Numbers 11:23"> Wherefore did I come, and there was no man? I called, and there was none to answer? Is my hand at all shortened that I cannot redeem, or have I no power to deliver? behold, at my rebuke I dry up the sea, I make rivers a wilderness; their fish stink because there is no water, and die for thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:12; Psalms 18:11–12; Exodus 10:21"> I clothe the heavens with blackness, and I make sackcloth their covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Jeremiah 1:9; John 7:15–17"> The Lord Jehovah hath given me the tongue of the instructed, that I should know how to succour by a word him that is weary. He wakeneth morning by morning, he wakeneth mine ear to hear as the instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:31; Hebrews 5:8; Isaiah 35:5"> The Lord Jehovah hath opened mine ear, and I was not rebellious; I turned not away back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Mark 14:65; Mark 15:19"> I gave my back to smiters, and my cheeks to them that plucked off the hair; I hid not my face from shame and spitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Ezekiel 3:8–9; John 16:33"> But the Lord Jehovah will help me: therefore shall I not be confounded; therefore have I set my face like a flint, and I know that I shall not be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:32–34; Revelation 12:10; Isaiah 41:1"> He is near that justifieth me: who will contend with me? let us stand together; who is mine adverse party? let him draw near unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:28; Isaiah 41:10; Hebrews 1:11–12"> Behold, the Lord Jehovah will help me; who is he that shall condemn me? Behold, they all shall grow old as a garment; the moth shall eat them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 12:46; Micah 7:7–9; Isaiah 26:3–4"> Who is among you that feareth Jehovah, that hearkeneth to the voice of his servant? he that walketh in darkness, and hath no light, — let him confide in the name of Jehovah, and stay himself upon his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="50.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–7; Romans 1:21–22; Romans 10:3"> Behold, all ye that kindle a fire, that compass yourselves about with sparks: walk in the light of your fire, and among the sparks &#91;that&#93; ye have kindled. This shall ye have of my hand: ye shall lie down in sorrow.</span>
