<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 19:23–24; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> And Jehovah said to me, Take thee a great tablet, and write thereon with a man's style, concerning Maher-shalal-hash-baz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–11; 2 Kings 18:2; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And I took unto me to witness, sure witnesses, Urijah the priest, and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 1:3–9; Judges 4:4; Isaiah 7:13–14"> And I came near to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son; and Jehovah said unto me, Call his name, Maher-shalal-hash-baz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:11; 2 Kings 16:9; Deuteronomy 1:39"> For before the lad knoweth to cry, My father! and, My mother! the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 7:10"> And Jehovah spoke again to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 9:7; Nehemiah 3:15; Isaiah 7:6"> Forasmuch as this people refuseth the waters of Shiloah which flow softly, and rejoiceth in Rezin and in the son of Remaliah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12–13; Genesis 6:17; Isaiah 7:17"> therefore behold, the Lord will bring up upon them the waters of the river, strong and many, the king of Assyria and all his glory; and he shall mount up over all his channels, and go over all his banks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 30:28; Matthew 1:23"> and he shall pass through Judah; he shall overflow it and go further, he shall reach even to the neck; and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:5–6; Proverbs 11:21; Psalms 37:14–15"> Rage, ye peoples, and be broken in pieces! And give ear, all ye distant parts of the earth: Gird yourselves, and be broken in pieces; gird yourselves, and be broken in pieces!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:37; Romans 8:31; Job 5:12"> Settle a plan, and it shall come to nought; speak a word, and it shall not stand: for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.10 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God is with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Proverbs 1:15; Jeremiah 15:19"> For Jehovah spoke thus to me with a strong hand, and he instructed me not to walk in the way of this people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14–15; Luke 21:9; Psalms 53:5"> Ye shall not say, Conspiracy, of everything of which this people saith, Conspiracy; and fear ye not their fear, and be not in dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:5; Isaiah 26:3–4; Revelation 15:4"> Jehovah of hosts, him shall ye sanctify; and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 2:8; Romans 9:32–33; Isaiah 28:16"> And he will be for a sanctuary; and for a stone of stumbling, and for a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, for a gin and for a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:44; Luke 20:17–18; Isaiah 28:13"> And many among them shall stumble, and fall, and be broken, and snared, and taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Hebrews 3:5; Mark 4:10–11"> Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36–39; Habakkuk 2:3; Lamentations 3:25–26"> And I will wait for Jehovah, who hideth his face from the house of Jacob; and I will look for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13–14; Luke 2:34; Zechariah 8:3"> Behold, I and the children that Jehovah hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel, from Jehovah of hosts, who dwelleth in mount Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:13; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> And when they shall say unto you, Seek unto the necromancers and unto the soothsayers, who chirp and who mutter, &#91;say,&#93; Shall not a people seek unto their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God? &#91;Will they go&#93; for the living unto the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Isaiah 30:8–11"> To the law and the testimony! If they speak not according to this word, for them there is no daybreak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 19:3; Lamentations 4:4–5"> And they shall pass through it, hard pressed and hungry; and it shall come to pass when they are hungry, they will fret themselves, and curse their king and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and will gaze upward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Matthew 22:13; Jeremiah 13:16"> and they will look to the earth; and behold, trouble and darkness, gloom of anguish; and they shall be driven into thick darkness.</span>
