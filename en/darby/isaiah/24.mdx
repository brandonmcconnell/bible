<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Behold, Jehovah maketh the land empty, and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest; as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the taker of usury, so with him from whom usury is taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The land shall be utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled; for Jehovah hath spoken this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> The land mourneth, it fadeth away; the world languisheth, it fadeth away: the haughty people of the land do languish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> And the land is polluted under the inhabitants thereof; for they have violated the laws, changed the statute, broken the everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> Therefore doth the curse devour the earth, and they that dwell therein are held guilty; therefore the inhabitants of the earth are consumed, and few men are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine mourneth, the vine languisheth, all that were merry-hearted do sigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> the mirth of tambours ceaseth, the noise of them that rejoice endeth, the joy of the harp ceaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> They do not drink wine with a song; strong drink is bitter to them that drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> The city of solitude is broken down; every house is shut up, so that none entereth in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> There is a crying for wine in the streets; all joy is darkened, the mirth of the land is gone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> desolation remaineth in the city, and the gate is smitten, — a ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> For so will it be in the midst of the land among the peoples, as the shaking of an olive-tree, as the grape-gleanings when the vintage is done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> These shall lift up their voice, they shall shout for the majesty of Jehovah, they shall cry aloud from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> Therefore glorify Jehovah in the east, the name of Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of Israel, in the isles of the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> From the end of the earth have we heard songs: Glory to the righteous! And I said, My leanness, my leanness, woe unto me! The treacherous have dealt treacherously; yea, the treacherous have dealt very treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Fear, and the pit, and the snare are upon thee, inhabitant of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> And it shall come to pass, &#91;that&#93; he who fleeth from the sound of the fear shall fall into the pit; and he that cometh up out of the midst of the pit shall be taken in the snare; for the windows on high are open, and the foundations of the earth shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth is utterly broken down, the earth is completely dissolved, the earth is violently moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth reeleth to and fro like a drunkard, and is shaken like a night hut; and its transgression is heavy upon it; and it falleth and shall not rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> And it shall come to pass in that day, &#91;that&#93; Jehovah will punish the host of the high ones on high, and the kings of the earth upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> And they shall be brought together, &#91;as&#93; an assemblage of prisoners for the pit, and shall be shut up in prison, and after many days shall they be visited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> And the moon shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed; for Jehovah of hosts shall reign on mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, and before his ancients in glory.</span>
