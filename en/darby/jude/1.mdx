<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 1:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Peter 2:9"> Jude, bondman of Jesus Christ, and brother of James, to the called ones beloved in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God &#91;the&#93; Father and preserved in Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2; Revelation 1:4–6"> Mercy to you, and peace, and love be multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; Philippians 1:27; Jude 1:20"> Beloved, using all diligence to write to you of our common salvation, I have been obliged to write to you exhorting &#91;you&#93; to contend earnestly for the faith once delivered to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; Galatians 2:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> For certain men have got in unnoticed, they who of old were marked out beforehand to this sentence, ungodly &#91;persons&#93;, turning the grace of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God into dissoluteness, and denying our only Master and Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Hebrews 3:16–2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–12"> But I would put you in remembrance, you who once knew all things, that the Lord, having saved a people out of &#91;the&#93; land of Egypt, in the second place destroyed those who had not believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 25:41; Ephesians 6:12"> And angels who had not kept their own original state, but had abandoned their own dwelling, he keeps in eternal chains under gloomy darkness, to &#91;the&#93; judgment of &#91;the&#93; great day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; 2 Peter 2:6; Romans 1:26–27"> as Sodom and Gomorrha, and the cities around them, committing greedily fornication, in like manner with them, and going after other flesh, lie there as an example, undergoing the judgment of eternal fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Peter 2:10–12"> Yet in like manner these dreamers also defile &#91;the&#93; flesh, and despise lordship, and speak railingly against dignities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 3:2; 2 Peter 2:11; Deuteronomy 34:6"> But Michael the archangel, when disputing with the devil he reasoned about the body of Moses, did not dare to bring a railing judgment against &#91;him&#93;, but said, &#91;The&#93; Lord rebuke thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Romans 1:21–22"> But these, whatever things they know not, they speak railingly against; but what even, as the irrational animals, they understand by mere nature, in these things they corrupt themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:15; 1 John 3:12; Revelation 2:14"> Woe to them! because they have gone in the way of Cain, and given themselves up to the error of Balaam for reward, and perished in the gainsaying of Core.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17–20; Matthew 15:13; Ezekiel 34:8"> These are spots in your love-feasts, feasting together &#91;with you&#93; without fear, pasturing themselves; clouds without water, carried along by &#91;the&#93; winds; autumnal trees, without fruit, twice dead, rooted up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17; Philippians 3:19; Isaiah 57:20"> raging waves of the sea, foaming out their own shames; wandering stars, to whom has been reserved the gloom of darkness for eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 50:3–5; Hebrews 11:5–6"> And Enoch, &#91;the&#93; seventh from Adam, prophesied also as to these, saying, Behold, &#91;the&#93; Lord has come amidst his holy myriads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:16; Ecclesiastes 11:9; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> to execute judgment against all; and to convict all the ungodly of them of all their works of ungodliness, which they have wrought ungodlily, and of all the hard &#91;things&#93; which ungodly sinners have spoken against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18; 2 Peter 2:10; Philippians 2:14"> These are murmurers, complainers, walking after their lusts; and their mouth speaks swelling words, admiring persons for the sake of profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:2; 1 John 4:6; Ephesians 4:11"> But ye, beloved, remember the words spoken before by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:1–5"> that they said to you, that at &#91;the&#93; end of the time there should be mockers, walking after their own lusts of ungodlinesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Proverbs 18:1; Romans 8:9"> These are they who set &#91;themselves&#93; apart, natural &#91;men&#93;, not having &#91;the&#93; Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> But ye, beloved, building yourselves up on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Lamentations 3:25–26; Hebrews 9:28"> keep yourselves in the love of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.21 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, awaiting the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Galatians 6:1; 1 John 5:16–18"> And of some have compassion, making a difference,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Revelation 3:4"> but others save with fear, snatching &#91;them&#93; out of the fire; hating even the garment spotted by the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 1:22"> But to him that is able to keep you without stumbling, and to set &#91;you&#93; with exultation blameless before his glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Revelation 5:13–14; 1 Peter 5:10–11"> to the only<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.25 Elohim">d</a></sup>God our Saviour, through Jesus Christ our Lord, &#91;be&#93; glory, majesty, might, and authority, from before the whole age, and now, and to all the ages. Amen.</span>
</div>
