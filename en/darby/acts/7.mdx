<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Hebrews 11:8; Joshua 24:3"> and said to him, Go out of thy land and out of thy kindred, and come into the land which I will shew thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 12:4–5; Genesis 11:31–32; Isaiah 41:9"> Then going out of the land of the Chaldeans he dwelt in Charran, and thence, after his father died, he removed him into this land in which ye now dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 13:15"> And he did not give him an inheritance in it, not even what his foot could stand on; and promised to give it to him for a possession, and to his seed after him, when he had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Exodus 12:40–41; Galatians 3:17"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="7.6 Elohim">b</a></sup>God spoke thus: His seed shall be a sojourner in a strange land, and they shall enslave them and evil entreat &#91;them&#93; four hundred years;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 3:12; Psalms 105:27–36; Nehemiah 9:9–11"> and the nation to which they shall be in bondage will I judge, said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="7.7 Elohim">c</a></sup>God; and after these things they shall come forth and serve me in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:23–26; Genesis 17:9–14; Genesis 21:1–4"> And he gave to him &#91;the&#93; covenant of circumcision; and thus he begat Isaac and circumcised him the eighth day; and Isaac Jacob, and Jacob the twelve patriarchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; Psalms 105:17; Isaiah 43:2"> And the patriarchs, envying Joseph, sold him away into Egypt. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="7.9 Elohim">d</a></sup>God was with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:4; 2 Timothy 4:18; Proverbs 2:6"> and delivered him out of all his tribulations, and gave him favour and wisdom in the sight of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he appointed him chief over Egypt and all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 41:54–57; Genesis 45:11; Genesis 42:5"> But a famine came upon all the land of Egypt and Canaan, and great distress, and our fathers found no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 42:1–24; Genesis 43:2"> But Jacob, having heard of there being corn in Egypt, sent out our fathers first;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 45:1–18; Genesis 46:31–10"> and the second time Joseph was made known to his brethren, and the family of Joseph became known to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:26–27; Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 1:5"> And Joseph sent and called down to him his father Jacob and all &#91;his&#93; kindred, seventy-five souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 49:33; Exodus 1:6; Genesis 46:3–7"> And Jacob went down into Egypt and died, he and our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 23:16; Exodus 13:19"> and were carried over to Sychem and placed in the sepulchre which Abraham bought for a sum of money of the sons of Emmor the &#91;father&#93; of Sychem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–12; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> But as the time of promise drew near which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="7.17 Elohim">e</a></sup>God had promised to Abraham, the people increased and multiplied in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 1:8"> until another king over Egypt arose who did not know Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 105:25; Exodus 1:9–22; Revelation 12:4–5"> He dealt subtilly with our race, and evil entreated the fathers, casting out their infants that they might not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Exodus 2:2–10; 1 Samuel 16:12"> In which time Moses was born, and was exceedingly lovely, who was nourished three months in the house of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Exodus 2:2–10; Deuteronomy 32:26"> And when he was cast out, the daughter of Pharaoh took him up, and brought him up for herself &#91;to be&#93; for a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 1:17–20; 2 Chronicles 9:22"> And Moses was instructed in all &#91;the&#93; wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was mighty in his words and deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 2:11–12; Proverbs 21:1; Hebrews 11:24–26"> And when a period of forty years was fulfilled to him, it came into his heart to look upon his brethren, the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Acts 7:28; John 18:25–27"> and seeing a certain one wronged, he defended &#91;him&#93;, and avenged him that was being oppressed, smiting the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:7; Mark 9:32; 2 Kings 5:1"> For he thought that his brethren would understand that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="7.25 Elohim">f</a></sup>God by his hand was giving them deliverance. But they understood not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 2:13–15; Proverbs 18:19; 1 John 3:11–15"> And on the morrow he shewed himself to them as they were contending, and compelled them to peace, saying, Ye are brethren, why do ye wrong one another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Luke 12:14; Acts 7:54"> But he that was wronging his neighbour thrust him away, saying, Who established thee ruler and judge over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Dost thou wish to kill me as thou killedst the Egyptian yesterday?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 18:2–4; Exodus 2:14–22; Exodus 4:19–20"> And Moses fled at this saying, and became a sojourner in the land of Madiam, where he begat two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 3:1–2; Acts 7:35; Isaiah 43:2"> And when forty years were fulfilled, an angel appeared to him in the wilderness of mount Sinai, in a flame of fire of a bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:3–4"> And Moses seeing it wondered at the vision; and as he went up to consider it, there was a voice of &#91;the&#93; Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Matthew 22:32; Job 4:14"> I am the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="7.32 Elohim">g</a></sup>God of thy fathers, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="7.32 Elohim">h</a></sup>God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob. And Moses trembled, and durst not consider &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> And the Lord said to him, Loose the sandal of thy feet, for the place on which thou standest is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–10; Exodus 4:31; Psalms 106:44"> I have surely seen the ill treatment of my people which is in Egypt, and I have heard their groan, and have come down to take them out of it; and now, come, I will send thee to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Luke 19:14; Psalms 113:7–8"> This Moses, whom they refused, saying, Who made thee ruler and judge? him did<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="7.35 Elohim">i</a></sup>God send &#91;to be&#93; a ruler and deliverer with the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 14:21; Exodus 33:1"> He led them out, having wrought wonders and signs in the land of Egypt, and in the Red sea, and in the wilderness forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 3:22–23; John 18:37"> This is the Moses who said to the sons of Israel, A prophet shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="7.37 Elohim">j</a></sup>God raise up to you out of your brethren like me &#91;him shall ye hear&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 3:2; Hebrews 5:12; 1 Peter 4:11"> This is he who was in the assembly in the wilderness, with the angel who spoke to him in the mount Sinai, and with our fathers; who received living oracles to give to us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 14:3–4; Numbers 11:5; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> to whom our fathers would not be subject, but thrust &#91;him&#93; from them, and in their hearts turned back to Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 32:1; Exodus 32:23"> saying to Aaron, Make us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="7.40 Elohim">k</a></sup>gods who shall go before us; for this Moses, who brought us out of the land of Egypt, we know not what has happened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–21; Nehemiah 9:18; Revelation 9:20"> And they made a calf in those days, and offered sacrifice to the idol, and rejoiced in the works of their own hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 5:25–26; Ezekiel 20:39; Jeremiah 19:13"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="7.42 Elohim">l</a></sup>God turned and delivered them up to serve the host of heaven; as it is written in &#91;the&#93; book of the prophets, Have ye offered me victims and sacrifices forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Amos 5:26–27; Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9"> Yea, ye took up the tent of Moloch, and the star of &#91;your&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="7.43 Elohim">m</a></sup>god Remphan, the forms which ye made to do homage to them; and I will transport you beyond Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 38:21; Hebrews 8:5"> Our fathers had the tent of the testimony in the wilderness, as he that spoke to Moses commanded to make it according to the model which he had seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Joshua 18:1; Joshua 23:9"> which also our fathers, receiving from their predecessors, brought in with Joshua when they entered into possession of &#91;the lands of&#93; the nations, whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="7.45 Elohim">n</a></sup>God drove out from &#91;the&#93; face of our fathers, until the days of David;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 132:1–5; Acts 13:22; 2 Samuel 7:1–5"> who found favour before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="7.46 Elohim">o</a></sup>God, and asked to find a tabernacle for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="7.46 Elohim">p</a></sup>God of Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; 1 Kings 6:37–38; 2 Samuel 7:13"> but Solomon built him a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 17:24–25; Isaiah 66:1–2; 1 Kings 8:27"> But the Most High dwells not in &#91;places&#93; made with hands; as says the prophet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 11:4; John 4:21"> The heaven &#91;is&#93; my throne and the earth the footstool of my feet: what house will ye build me? saith &#91;the&#93; Lord, or where &#91;is the&#93; place of my rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Psalms 33:6–9; Acts 14:15"> has not my hand made all these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> O stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Spirit; as your fathers, ye also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 1 Kings 19:10"> Which of the prophets have not your fathers persecuted? and they have slain those who announced beforehand concerning the coming of the Just One, of whom ye have now become deliverers up and murderers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Galatians 3:19; Acts 7:38"> who have received the law as ordained by &#91;the&#93; ministry of angels, and have not kept &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Psalms 35:16; Matthew 25:30"> And hearing these things they were cut to the heart, and gnashed their teeth against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Mark 16:19; Hebrews 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> But being full of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, having fixed his eyes on heaven, he saw &#91;the&#93; glory of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="7.55 Elohim">q</a></sup>God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="7.55 Elohim">r</a></sup>God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 26:64–65; Acts 10:11; Revelation 11:19"> and said, Lo, I behold the heavens opened, and the Son of man standing at the right hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="7.56 Elohim">s</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; Psalms 58:4; Acts 23:27"> And they cried out with a loud voice, and held their ears, and rushed upon him with one accord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Acts 8:1; Acts 6:13"> and having cast &#91;him&#93; out of the city, they stoned &#91;him&#93;. And the witnesses laid aside their clothes at the feet of a young man called Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Psalms 31:5; Acts 22:16"> And they stoned Stephen, praying, and saying, Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Luke 23:34; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> And kneeling down, he cried with a loud voice, Lord, lay not this sin to their charge. And having said this, he fell asleep.</span>
