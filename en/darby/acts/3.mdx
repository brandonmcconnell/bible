<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 55:17; Acts 10:3; Acts 4:13"> And Peter and John went up together into the temple at the hour of prayer, &#91;which is&#93; the ninth &#91;hour&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:8; John 9:8; Luke 16:20"> and a certain man who was lame from his mother's womb was being carried, whom they placed every day at the gate of the temple called Beautiful, to ask alms of those who were going into the temple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> who, seeing Peter and John about to enter into the temple, asked to receive alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:9–10; John 11:40; Luke 4:20"> And Peter, looking stedfastly upon him with John, said, Look on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he gave heed to them, expecting to receive something from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 3:16; Acts 10:38; Acts 4:10"> But Peter said, Silver and gold I have not; but what I have, this give I to thee: In the name of Jesus Christ the Nazaraean rise up and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Mark 9:27; Acts 9:41"> And having taken hold of him &#91;by&#93; the right hand he raised him up, and immediately his feet and ankle bones were made strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 14:10; Isaiah 35:6; Psalms 107:20–22"> And leaping up he stood and walked, and entered with them into the temple, walking, and leaping, and praising<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.8 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:21; Acts 4:16; Luke 13:17"> And all the people saw him walking and praising<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:2; John 5:20; John 9:3"> and they recognised him, that it was he who sat for alms at the Beautiful gate of the temple; and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:12; John 10:23; Luke 8:38"> And as he held Peter and John, all the people ran together to them in the portico which is called Solomon's, greatly wondering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; John 3:27–28; Daniel 2:28–30"> And Peter, seeing it, answered the people, Men of Israel, why are ye astonished at this? or why do ye gaze on us as if we had by our own power or piety made him to walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 22:32; Acts 7:32; Matthew 27:17–25"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>God of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.13 Elohim">d</a></sup>God of our fathers, has glorified his servant Jesus, whom ye delivered up, and denied him in the presence of Pilate, when he had judged that he should be let go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Acts 7:52; 1 Peter 3:18"> But ye denied the holy and righteous one, and asked that a man &#91;that was&#93; a murderer should be granted to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Revelation 21:6; Acts 2:32"> but the originator of life ye slew, whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.15 Elohim">e</a></sup>God raised from among &#91;the&#93; dead, whereof we are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Acts 4:10; Acts 14:9"> And, by faith in his name, his name has made this &#91;man&#93; strong whom ye behold and know; and the faith which is by him has given him this complete soundness in the presence of you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Acts 13:27; John 16:3"> And now, brethren, I know that ye did it in ignorance, as also your rulers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 2:23"> but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.18 Elohim">f</a></sup>God has thus fulfilled what he had announced beforehand by the mouth of all the prophets, that his Christ should suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> Repent therefore and be converted, for the blotting out of your sins, so that times of refreshing may come from &#91;the&#93; presence of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:30–36; Revelation 19:11–16; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> and he may send Jesus Christ, who was foreordained for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:21; Isaiah 1:26; Acts 1:11"> whom heaven indeed must receive till &#91;the&#93; times of &#91;the&#93; restoring of all things, of which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="3.21 Elohim">g</a></sup>God has spoken by the mouth of his holy prophets since time began.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 7:37; Galatians 4:4"> Moses indeed said, A prophet shall &#91;the&#93; Lord your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="3.22 Elohim">h</a></sup>God raise up to you out of your brethren like me: him shall ye hear in everything whatsoever he shall say to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:19; John 12:48; Hebrews 10:28–30"> And it shall be that whatsoever soul shall not hear that prophet shall be destroyed from among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 24:27; 1 Samuel 3:20"> And indeed all the prophets from Samuel and those in succession after &#91;him&#93;, as many as have spoken, have announced also these days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:8; Acts 2:39"> Ye are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="3.25 Elohim">i</a></sup>God appointed to our fathers, saying to Abraham, And in thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 1:21; Acts 3:22; Jude 1:24"> To you first<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="3.26 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, having raised up his servant, has sent him, blessing you in turning each one &#91;of you&#93; from your wickedness.</span>
