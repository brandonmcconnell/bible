<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:5; Leviticus 12:3"> And certain persons, having come down from Judaea, taught the brethren, If ye shall not have been circumcised according to the custom of Moses, ye cannot be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 15:22–23; Acts 15:6–7; Galatians 2:1–2"> A commotion therefore having taken place, and no small discussion on the part of Paul and Barnabas against them, they arranged that Paul and Barnabas, and certain others from amongst them, should go up to Jerusalem to the apostles and elders about this question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 21:5; 1 Corinthians 16:11"> They therefore, having been set on their way by the assembly, passed through Phoenicia and Samaria, relating the conversion of &#91;those of&#93; the nations. And they caused great joy to all the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:12; Acts 15:3"> And being arrived at Jerusalem, they were received by the assembly, and the apostles, and the elders, and related all that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God had wrought with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:1–3; Philippians 3:5–8; Acts 15:1"> And some of those who were of the sect of the Pharisees, who believed, rose up from among &#91;them&#93;, saying that they ought to circumcise them and enjoin them to keep the law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 15:22; Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17"> And the apostles and the elders were gathered together to see about this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Galatians 2:7–9; 1 Chronicles 28:4–5"> And much discussion having taken place, Peter, standing up, said to them, Brethren, ye know that from the earliest days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God amongst you chose that the nations by my mouth should hear the word of the glad tidings and believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Acts 10:47; Acts 14:3"> And the heart-knowing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.8 Elohim">c</a></sup>God bore them witness, giving &#91;them&#93; the Holy Spirit as to us also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 5:6; Acts 10:28"> and put no difference between us and them, having purified their hearts by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; Matthew 23:4; Galatians 4:9"> Now therefore why tempt ye<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.10 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, by putting a yoke upon the neck of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we have been able to bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Titus 3:4–7; Titus 2:11"> But we believe that we shall be saved by the grace of the Lord Jesus, in the same manner as they also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:4; Acts 21:19"> And all the multitude kept silence and listened to Barnabas and Paul relating all the signs and wonders which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.12 Elohim">e</a></sup>God had wrought among the nations by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Galatians 2:12; James 1:1"> And after they had held their peace, James answered, saying, Brethren, listen to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:1; Acts 15:7–9; Luke 2:31–32"> Simon has related how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.14 Elohim">f</a></sup>God first visited to take out of &#91;the&#93; nations a people for his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:8–12; Acts 13:47"> And with this agree the words of the prophets; as it is written:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 9:11–12; Psalms 89:35–49; Luke 1:31–33"> After these things I will return, and will rebuild the tabernacle of David which is fallen, and will rebuild its ruins, and will set it up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 2:11; Zechariah 8:20–23; Isaiah 65:1"> so that the residue of men may seek out the Lord, and all the nations on whom my name is invoked, saith &#91;the&#93; Lord, who does these things</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Ephesians 3:9; Isaiah 46:9–10"> known from eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 15:28; Galatians 1:7–10; Acts 15:24"> Wherefore I judge, not to trouble those who from the nations turn to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.19 Elohim">g</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Revelation 2:20; Leviticus 3:17"> but to write to them to abstain from pollutions of idols, and from fornication, and from what is strangled, and from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:15; Acts 13:27; 2 Corinthians 3:14–15"> For Moses, from generations of old, has in every city those who preach him, being read in the synagogues every sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:27; Acts 15:40; 1 Thessalonians 1:1"> Then it seemed good to the apostles and to the elders, with the whole assembly, to send chosen men from among them with Paul and Barnabas to Antioch, Judas called Barsabas and Silas, leading men among the brethren,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:26; James 1:1; Galatians 1:21"> having by their hand written &#91;thus&#93;: The apostles, and the elders, and the brethren, to the brethren who are from among &#91;the&#93; nations at Antioch, and &#91;in&#93; Syria and Cilicia, greeting:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; Galatians 5:10; Acts 15:1"> Inasmuch as we have heard that some who went out from amongst us have troubled you by words, upsetting your souls, &#91;saying that ye must be circumcised and keep the law&#93;; to whom we gave no commandment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Peter 3:15; Acts 1:14; Acts 15:2"> it seemed good to us, having arrived at a common judgment, to send chosen men to you with our beloved Barnabas and Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 9:23–25; Philippians 2:29–30"> men who have given up their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 15:32; 3 John 1:13"> We have therefore sent Judas and Silas, who themselves also will tell you by word &#91;of mouth&#93; the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:13; Revelation 2:24; Matthew 11:30"> For it has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us to lay upon you no greater burden than these necessary things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:20; Acts 21:25; Revelation 2:14"> to abstain from things sacrificed to idols, and from blood, and from what is strangled, and from fornication; keeping yourselves from which ye will do well. Farewell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 16:4; Acts 6:2; Acts 23:33"> They therefore, being let go, came to Antioch, and having gathered the multitude delivered to &#91;them&#93; the epistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:4–5; Philippians 3:3; Galatians 5:1"> And having read it, they rejoiced at the consolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:1"> And Judas and Silas, being themselves also prophets, exhorted the brethren with much discourse, and strengthened them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 16:36; Hebrews 11:31"> And having passed some time &#91;there&#93;, they were let go in peace from the brethren to those who sent them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:1"> And Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch, teaching and announcing the glad tidings, with many others also, of the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 14:6; Acts 13:4; Acts 13:51"> But after certain days Paul said to Barnabas, Let us return now and visit the brethren in every city where we have announced the word of the Lord, &#91;and see&#93; how they are getting on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 13:5"> And Barnabas proposed to take with &#91;them&#93; John also, called Mark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:13; Proverbs 25:19; Luke 14:27–34"> but Paul thought it not well to take with them him who had abandoned them, &#91;going back&#93; from Pamphylia, and had not gone with them to the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Colossians 4:10; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Acts 4:36"> There arose therefore very warm feeling, so that they separated from one another; and Barnabas taking Mark sailed away to Cyprus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 14:26; Acts 11:23"> but Paul having chosen Silas went forth, committed by the brethren to the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15.40 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Galatians 1:21; Acts 15:32"> And he passed through Syria and Cilicia, confirming the assemblies.</span>
