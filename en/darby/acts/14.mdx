<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:4; Acts 13:51; Acts 13:46"> And it came to pass in Iconium that they entered together into the synagogue of the Jews, and so spake that a great multitude of both Jews and Greeks believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 13:50; Acts 21:27–30; Acts 14:19"> But the Jews who did not believe stirred up the minds of &#91;those of&#93; the nations and made &#91;them&#93; evil-affected against the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 2:4; Ephesians 6:18–20; Acts 20:32"> They stayed therefore a good while, speaking boldly, &#91;confiding&#93; in the Lord, who gave witness to the word of his grace, giving signs and wonders to be done by their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 28:24; Acts 14:14; Luke 11:21–23"> And the multitude of the city was divided, and some were with the Jews and some with the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 14:19; 2 Timothy 3:11; Acts 17:5"> And when an assault was making, both of &#91;those of&#93; the nations and &#91;the&#93; Jews with their rulers, to use &#91;them&#93; ill and stone them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 14:20–21; Matthew 10:23; 2 Timothy 3:11"> they, being aware of it, fled to the cities of Lycaonia, Lystra and Derbe, and the surrounding country,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 14:21; Acts 16:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> and there they were announcing the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:2; John 9:1–2; John 5:3–5"> And a certain man in Lystra, impotent in his feet, sat, &#91;being&#93; lame from his mother's womb, who had never walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:4; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:28–29"> This &#91;man&#93; heard Paul speaking, who, fixing his eyes on him, and seeing that he had faith to be healed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; John 5:8–9; John 14:12"> said with a loud voice, Rise up straight upon thy feet: and he sprang up and walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:6; Acts 8:10; Acts 12:22"> But the crowds, who saw what Paul had done, lifted up their voices in Lycaonian, saying, The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.11 Elohim">a</a></sup>gods, having made themselves like men, are come down to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:35"> And they called Barnabas Jupiter, and Paul Mercury, because he took the lead in speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 2:46; Acts 10:25"> And the priest of Jupiter who was before the city, having brought bulls and garlands to the gates, would have done sacrifice along with the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; 1 Corinthians 9:5–6; 2 Kings 5:7"> But the apostles Barnabas and Paul, having heard &#91;it&#93;, rent their garments, and rushed out to the crowd, crying</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:26; 1 Corinthians 8:4; James 5:17"> and saying, Men, why do ye these things? We also are men of like passions with you, preaching to you to turn from these vanities to the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.15 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, who made the heaven, and the earth, and the sea, and all things in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:30; Psalms 81:12; Romans 1:28"> who in the past generations suffered all the nations to go in their own ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:19–20; Joel 2:23; Job 5:10"> though indeed he did not leave himself without witness, doing good, and giving to you from heaven rain and fruitful seasons, filling your hearts with food and gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Genesis 19:9; Genesis 11:6"> And saying these things, they with difficulty kept the crowds from sacrificing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:25; 2 Timothy 3:11; Acts 13:50–51"> But there came Jews from Antioch and Iconium, and having persuaded the crowds and stoned Paul, drew him out of the city, supposing him to have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9; Acts 14:6"> But while the disciples encircled him, he rose up and entered into the city. And on the morrow he went away with Barnabas to Derbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 14:1; Acts 13:51"> And having announced the glad tidings to that city, and having made many disciples, they returned to Lystra, and Iconium, and Antioch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 2 Timothy 3:12; John 16:33"> establishing the souls of the disciples, exhorting them to abide in the faith, and that through many tribulations we must enter into the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.22 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; James 5:14"> And having chosen them elders in each assembly, having prayed with fastings, they committed them to the Lord, on whom they had believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 13:13–14; Acts 15:38"> And having passed through Pisidia they came to Pamphylia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and having spoken the word in Perga, they came down to Attalia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:40; Acts 11:19; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> and thence they sailed away to Antioch, whence they had been committed to the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.26 Elohim">d</a></sup>God for the work which they had fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 15:12; Colossians 4:3"> And having arrived, and having brought together the assembly, they related to them all that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.27 Elohim">e</a></sup>God had done with them, and that he had opened a door of faith to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 11:26; Acts 15:35"> And they stayed no little time with the disciples.</span>
