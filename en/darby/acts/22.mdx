<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Acts 28:17; Acts 13:26"> Brethren and fathers, hear my defence which I now make to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:40"> And hearing that he addressed them in the Hebrew tongue, they kept the more quiet; and he says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:5–6; Acts 9:11"> I am a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia, but brought up in this city, at the feet of Gamaliel, educated according to &#91;the&#93; exactness of the law of &#91;our&#93; fathers, being zealous for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, as ye are all this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 22:19–20; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 26:9–11"> who have persecuted this way unto death, binding and delivering up to prisons both men and women;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:1–2; Luke 22:66; Acts 5:21"> as also the high priest bears me witness, and all the elderhood: from whom also, having received letters to the brethren, I went to Damascus to bring those also who were there, bound, to Jerusalem, to be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:3–8; Acts 26:12–18; Revelation 1:16"> And it came to pass, as I was journeying and drawing near to Damascus, that, about mid-day, there suddenly shone out of heaven a great light round about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:9; Matthew 25:45; Genesis 22:11"> And I fell to the ground, and heard a voice saying to me, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:9; 1 Samuel 8:7; Exodus 16:7–8"> And I answered, Who art thou, Lord? And he said to me, I am Jesus the Nazaraean, whom thou persecutest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13"> But they that were with me beheld the light, &#91;and were filled with fear&#93;, but heard not the voice of him that was speaking to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 22:12–16; Acts 2:37"> And I said, What shall I do, Lord? And the Lord said to me, Rise up, and go to Damascus, and there it shall be told thee of all things which it is appointed thee to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 9:8–9; Acts 13:11; Isaiah 42:16"> And as I could not see, through the glory of that light, being led by the hand of those who were with me, I came to Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:22; Acts 6:3; 3 John 1:12"> And a certain Ananias, a pious man according to the law, borne witness to by all the Jews who dwelt &#91;there&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:17–18; Philemon 1:16"> coming to me and standing by me, said to me, Brother Saul, receive thy sight. And I, in the same hour, received my sight and saw him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Corinthians 9:1"> And he said, The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.14 Elohim">b</a></sup>God of our fathers has chosen thee beforehand to know his will, and to see the just one, and to hear a voice out of his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Acts 4:20; John 15:27"> for thou shalt be a witness for him to all men of what thou hast seen and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 2:21; Titus 3:5"> And now why lingerest thou? Arise and get baptised, and have thy sins washed away, calling on his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:1–4; Acts 10:9–10; Acts 9:26–28"> And it came to pass when I had returned to Jerusalem, and as I was praying in the temple, that I became in ecstasy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:29; Luke 21:21; Ezekiel 3:6–7"> and saw him saying to me, Make haste and go quickly out of Jerusalem, for they will not receive thy testimony concerning me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 22:4; Matthew 10:17; Acts 8:3"> And I said, Lord, they themselves know that I was imprisoning and beating in every synagogue those that believe on thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Acts 8:1; Romans 1:32"> and when the blood of thy witness Stephen was shed, I also myself was standing by and consenting, and kept the clothes of them who killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:6–8"> And he said to me, Go, for I will send thee to the nations afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:24; Acts 21:36; John 19:15"> And they heard him until this word, and lifted up their voice, saying, Away with such a one as that from the earth, for it was not fit he should live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:13; Acts 26:11; Ecclesiastes 10:3"> And as they were crying, and throwing away their clothes, and casting dust into the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:31–32; Acts 21:34; John 19:1"> the chiliarch commanded him to be brought into the fortress, saying that he should be examined by scourging, that he might ascertain for what cause they cried thus against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:37; Acts 25:16; Acts 27:43"> But as they stretched him forward with the thongs, Paul said to the centurion who stood &#91;by&#93;, Is it lawful for you to scourge a man &#91;who is&#93; a Roman and uncondemned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Acts 22:29"> And the centurion, having heard it, went and reported it to the chiliarch, saying, What art thou going to do? for this man is a Roman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And the chiliarch coming up said to him, Tell me, Art thou a Roman? And he said, Yes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the chiliarch answered, I, for a great sum, bought this citizenship. And Paul said, But I was also &#91;free&#93; born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Acts 22:24–26; Acts 16:38–39"> Immediately therefore those who were going to examine him left him, and the chiliarch also was afraid when he ascertained that he was a Roman, and because he had bound him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:28; Acts 21:33; Acts 21:11"> And on the morrow, desirous to know the certainty &#91;of the matter&#93; why he was accused of the Jews, he loosed him, and commanded the chief priests and all the council to meet, and having brought Paul down set him before them.</span>
