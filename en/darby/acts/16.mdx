<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:5; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:17"> And he came to Derbe and Lystra: and behold, a certain disciple was there, by name Timotheus, son of a Jewish believing woman, but &#91;the&#93; father a Greek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; 2 Timothy 3:15; Acts 14:21"> who had a &#91;good&#93; testimony of the brethren in Lystra and Iconium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20; Galatians 5:6"> Him would Paul have go forth with him, and took &#91;him and&#93; circumcised him on account of the Jews who were in those places, for they all knew his father that he was a Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 15:28–29; Acts 15:2; Acts 11:30"> And as they passed through the cities they instructed them to observe the decrees determined on by the apostles and elders who were in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Acts 2:47; Acts 6:7"> The assemblies therefore were confirmed in the faith, and increased in number every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 18:23; Acts 16:7; Acts 13:2–4"> And having passed through Phrygia and the Galatian country, having been forbidden by the Holy Spirit to speak the word in Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:9; Philippians 1:19; Galatians 4:6"> having come down to Mysia, they attempted to go to Bithynia, and the Spirit of Jesus did not allow them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; 2 Timothy 4:13; Acts 16:11"> and having passed by Mysia they descended to Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:10–12; Acts 19:21; Acts 18:5"> And a vision appeared to Paul in the night: There was a certain Macedonian man, standing and beseeching him, and saying, Pass over into Macedonia and help us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12–13; Acts 14:7; Acts 16:10–17"> And when he had seen the vision, immediately we sought to go forth to Macedonia, concluding that the Lord had called us to announce to them the glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:1; Acts 16:8"> Having sailed therefore away from Troas, we went in a straight course to Samothracia, and on the morrow to Neapolis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Philippians 1:1; Acts 20:6"> and thence to Philippi, which is &#91;the&#93; first city of that part of Macedonia, a colony. And we were staying in that city certain days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Colossians 1:23; Acts 17:2"> And on the sabbath day we went outside the gate by the river, where it was the custom for prayer to be, and we sat down and spoke to the women who had assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Luke 24:45; Isaiah 50:5"> And a certain woman, by name Lydia, a seller of purple, of the city of Thyatira, who worshipped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.14 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, heard; whose heart the Lord opened to attend to the things spoken by Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:33"> And when she had been baptised and her house, she besought &#91;us&#93;, saying, If ye have judged me to be faithful to the Lord, come into my house and abide &#91;there&#93;. And she constrained us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> And it came to pass as we were going to prayer that a certain female slave, having a spirit of Python, met us, who brought much profit to her masters by prophesying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Peter 2:16; Mark 5:7"> She, having followed Paul and us, cried saying, These men are bondmen of the Most High<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.17 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, who announce to you &#91;the&#93; way of salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19; Acts 3:6"> And this she did many days. And Paul, being distressed, turned, and said to the spirit, I enjoin thee in the name of Jesus Christ to come out of her. And it came out the same hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Acts 21:30; Acts 8:3"> And her masters, seeing that the hope of their gains was gone, having seized Paul and Silas, dragged &#91;them&#93; into the market before the magistrates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 4:4; John 15:18–20; Acts 28:22"> and having brought them up to the praetors, said, These men utterly trouble our city, being Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 3:8; Acts 26:3; Jeremiah 10:3"> and announce customs which it is not lawful for us to receive nor practise, being Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> And the crowd rose up too against them; and the praetors, having torn off their clothes, commanded to scourge &#91;them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:9; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> And having laid many stripes upon them they cast &#91;them&#93; into prison, charging the jailor to keep them safely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 33:11; Jeremiah 29:26"> who, having received such a charge, cast them into the inner prison, and secured their feet to the stocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Psalms 119:62; James 1:2"> And at midnight Paul and Silas, in praying, were praising<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God with singing, and the prisoners listened to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Acts 5:19; Acts 12:10"> And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison shook, and all the doors were immediately opened, and the bonds of all loosed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 12:19; Matthew 27:5; Judges 9:54"> And the jailor being awakened out of his sleep, and seeing the doors of the prison opened, having drawn a sword was going to kill himself, thinking the prisoners had fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; Luke 23:34; Luke 6:27–28"> But Paul called out with a loud voice, saying, Do thyself no harm, for we are all here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Acts 24:25; Isaiah 60:14"> And having asked for lights, he rushed in, and, trembling, fell down before Paul and Silas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:37; John 6:27–29; Isaiah 58:9"> And leading them out said, Sirs, what must I do that I may be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:12; Acts 2:38–39; John 3:36"> And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus and thou shalt be saved, thou and thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Colossians 1:27–28; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> And they spoke to him the word of the Lord, with all that were in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Acts 16:15; Acts 16:25"> And he took them the same hour of the night and washed &#91;them&#93; from their stripes; and was baptised, he and all his straightway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Acts 2:46; Romans 5:11"> And having brought them into his house he laid the table &#91;for them&#93;, and rejoiced with all his house, having believed in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="16.34 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 76:10; Acts 5:40"> And when it was day, the praetors sent the lictors, saying, Let those men go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 15:33; John 14:27; 1 Samuel 1:17"> And the jailor reported these words to Paul: The praetors have sent that ye may be let go. Now therefore go out and depart in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Psalms 58:1–2; Matthew 10:16"> But Paul said to them, Having beaten us publicly uncondemned, us who are Romans, they have cast us into prison, and now they thrust us out secretly? no, indeed, but let them come themselves and bring us out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:29; Matthew 21:46; Matthew 14:5"> And the lictors reported these words to the praetors. And they were afraid when they heard they were Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 5:17; Daniel 6:23"> And they came and besought them, and having brought them out, asked them to go out of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Acts 16:14; Acts 14:22"> And having gone out of the prison, they came to Lydia; and having seen the brethren, they exhorted them and went away.</span>
