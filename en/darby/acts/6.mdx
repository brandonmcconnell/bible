<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7–15; James 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Look out therefore, brethren, from among yourselves seven men, well reported of, full of &#91;the&#93; &#91;Holy&#93; Spirit and wisdom, whom we will establish over this business:</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 11:24; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:5–40"> And the saying pleased the whole multitude: and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolas, a proselyte of Antioch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; Numbers 8:10; Acts 1:24"> whom they set before the apostles; and, having prayed, they laid their hands on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Acts 12:24; Colossians 1:6"> And the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God increased; and the number of the disciples in Jerusalem was very greatly multiplied, and a great crowd of the priests obeyed the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> And Stephen, full of grace and power, wrought wonders and great signs among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> And there arose up certain of those of the synagogue called of freedmen, and of Cyrenians, and of Alexandrians, and of those of Cilicia and Asia, disputing with Stephen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And they were not able to resist the wisdom and the Spirit with which he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Then they suborned men, saying, We have heard him speaking blasphemous words against Moses and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.11 Elohim">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> And they roused the people, and the elders, and the scribes. And coming upon &#91;him&#93; they seized him and brought &#91;him&#93; to the council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> And they set false witnesses, saying, This man does not cease speaking words against the holy place and the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; Hebrews 12:26–28; Acts 21:21"> for we have heard him saying, This Jesus the Nazaraean shall destroy this place, and change the customs which Moses taught us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Matthew 17:2"> And all who sat in the council, looking fixedly on him, saw his face as &#91;the&#93; face of an angel.</span>
