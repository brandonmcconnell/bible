<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; 2 Timothy 2:20; Matthew 13:47–48"> But a certain man, Ananias by name, with Sapphira his wife, sold a possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:37; 2 Peter 2:14–15; Matthew 23:5"> and put aside for himself part of the price, &#91;his&#93; wife also being privy to it; and having brought a certain part, laid it at the feet of the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Isaiah 29:15; John 13:2"> But Peter said, Ananias, why has Satan filled thy heart that thou shouldest lie to the Holy Spirit, and put aside for thyself a part of the price of the estate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Joshua 7:25–26; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> While it remained did it not remain to thee? and sold, was &#91;it not&#93; in thine own power? Why is it that thou hast purposed this thing in thine heart? Thou hast not lied to men, but to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.4 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:10–11; Acts 2:43; Acts 5:13"> And Ananias, hearing these words, fell down and expired. And great fear came upon all who heard &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:40; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:23"> And the young men, rising up, swathed him up for burial, and having carried him out, buried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it came to pass about three hours afterwards, that his wife, not knowing what had happened, came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 5:2"> And Peter answered her, Tell me if ye gave the estate for so much? And she said, Yes, for so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Acts 5:3–4; Psalms 95:8–11"> And Peter said to her, Why &#91;is it&#93; that ye have agreed together to tempt the Spirit of &#91;the&#93; Lord? Lo, the feet of those that have buried thy husband &#91;are&#93; at the door, and they shall carry thee out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:5"> And she fell down immediately at his feet and expired. And when the young men came in they found her dead; and, having carried her out, they buried her by her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11–12; Acts 19:17"> And great fear came upon all the assembly, and upon all who heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:11; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4"> And by the hands of the apostles were many signs and wonders done among the people; (and they were all with one accord in Solomon's porch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:21; John 12:42"> but of the rest durst no man join them, but the people magnified them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:4; Isaiah 55:11–13"> and believers were more than ever added to the Lord, multitudes both of men and women;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Acts 19:11–12; John 14:12"> so that they brought out the sick into the streets and put &#91;them&#93; on beds and couches, that at least the shadow of Peter, when he came, might overshadow some one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 16:17–18; Mark 2:3–4; Matthew 15:30–31"> And the multitude also of the cities round about came together to Jerusalem, bringing sick persons and persons beset by unclean spirits, who were all healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:45; Acts 7:9; Acts 17:5"> And the high priest rising up, and all they that were with him, which is the sect of the Sadducees, were filled with wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 4:3; Luke 21:12; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> and laid hands on the apostles and put them in the public prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:26; Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7–11"> But an angel of &#91;the&#93; Lord during the night opened the doors of the prison, and leading them out, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 6:63; John 6:68; 1 John 5:11–12"> Go ye and stand and speak in the temple to the people all the words of this life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:2; Acts 22:15; Acts 12:18–19"> And when they heard it, they entered very early into the temple and taught. And when the high priest was come, and they that were with him, they called together the council and all the elderhood of the sons of Israel, and sent to the prison to have them brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when the officers were come, they did not find them in the prison; and returned and reported</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Psalms 33:10; Daniel 6:22–24"> saying, We found the prison shut with all security, and the keepers standing at the doors; but when we had opened &#91;them&#93;, within we found no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:26; John 12:19"> And when they heard these words, both the priest and the captain of the temple and the chief priests were in perplexity as to them, what this would come to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And some one coming reported to them, Lo, the men whom ye put in the prison are in the temple, standing and teaching the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:13; Acts 4:21; Matthew 26:5"> Then the captain, having gone with the officers, brought them, not with violence, for they feared the people, lest they should be stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:22; Acts 4:7; Luke 22:66"> And they bring them and set them in the council. And the high priest asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Acts 7:52; Acts 4:10–11"> saying, We strictly enjoined you not to teach in this name: and lo, ye have filled Jerusalem with your doctrine, and purpose to bring upon us the blood of this man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:19; 1 Samuel 15:24; Mark 7:7–9"> But Peter answering, and the apostles, said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.29 Elohim">b</a></sup>God must be obeyed rather than men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Acts 10:39"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.30 Elohim">c</a></sup>God of our fathers has raised up Jesus, whom ye have slain, having hanged on a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:47; Acts 2:33; Hebrews 2:10"> Him has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.31 Elohim">d</a></sup>God exalted by his right hand as leader and saviour, to give repentance to Israel and remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:26–27; Hebrews 2:3–4; Acts 1:8"> And we are &#91;his&#93; witnesses of these things, and the Holy Spirit also, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.32 Elohim">e</a></sup>God has given to those that obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 7:54; Acts 2:37; Luke 4:28–29"> But they, when they heard &#91;these things&#93;, were cut to the heart, and took counsel to kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Luke 2:46; Luke 5:17"> But a certain &#91;man&#93;, a Pharisee, named Gamaliel, a teacher of the law, held in honour of all the people, rose up in the council, and commanded to put the men out for a short while,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 22:26; Jeremiah 26:19; Matthew 27:19"> and said to them, Men of Israel, take heed to yourselves as regards these men what ye are going to do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 8:9; 2 Peter 2:2; Galatians 6:3"> for before these days Theudas rose up, alleging himself to be somebody, to whom a number of men, about four hundred, were joined; who was slain, and all, as many as obeyed him, were dispersed and came to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 2:1–2; Psalms 7:14–15; Matthew 26:52"> After him rose Judas the Galilean in the days of the census, and drew away &#91;a number of&#93; people after him; and he perished, and all, as many as obeyed him, were scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And now I say to you, Withdraw from these men and let them alone, for if this counsel or this work have its origin from men, it will be destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Isaiah 46:10; Matthew 16:18"> but if it be from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.39 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, ye will not be able to put them down, lest ye be found also fighters against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.39 Elohim">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:28; Acts 4:17–21"> And they listened to his advice; and having called the apostles, they beat them, and enjoined them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and dismissed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13–16; Luke 6:22"> They therefore went their way from &#91;the&#93; presence of the council, rejoicing that they were counted worthy to be dishonoured for the name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 2:46; Acts 20:20; Luke 21:37"> And every day, in the temple and in the houses, they ceased not teaching and announcing the glad tidings that Jesus &#91;was&#93; the Christ.</span>
