<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5; Acts 19:21; 1 Timothy 1:3"> But after the tumult had ceased, Paul having called the disciples to &#91;him&#93; and embraced &#91;them&#93;, went away to go to Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1; Colossians 1:28"> And having passed through those parts, and having exhorted them with much discourse, he came to Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:19; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 9:23–24"> And having spent three months &#91;there&#93;, a treacherous plot against him having been set on foot by the Jews, as he was going to sail to Syria, &#91;the&#93; resolution was adopted of returning through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Ephesians 6:21; 2 Timothy 4:20"> And there accompanied him as far as Asia, Sopater &#91;son&#93; of Pyrrhus, a Berean; and of Thessalonians, Aristarchus and Secundus, and Gaius and Timotheus of Derbe, and of Asia, Tychicus and Trophimus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 16:10–11; 2 Timothy 4:13"> These going before waited for us in Troas;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:3; Acts 16:12; Exodus 23:15"> but we sailed away from Philippi after the days of unleavened bread, and we came to them to Troas in five days, where we spent seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 2:42; John 20:19"> And the first day of the week, we being assembled to break bread, Paul discoursed to them, about to depart on the morrow. And he prolonged the discourse till midnight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 22:12"> And there were many lights in the upper room where we were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 13:36; Matthew 26:40–41; Jonah 1:5–6"> And a certain youth, by name Eutychus, sitting at the window-opening, overpowered by deep sleep, while Paul discoursed very much at length, having been overpowered by the sleep, fell from the third story down to the bottom, and was taken up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 5:39; Matthew 9:23–24; 1 Kings 17:21–22"> But Paul descending fell upon him, and enfolding &#91;him&#93; &#91;in his arms&#93;, said, Be not troubled, for his life is in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 20:7; Acts 20:9"> And having gone up, and having broken the bread, and eaten, and having long spoken until daybreak, so he went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 2 Corinthians 1:4"> And they brought &#91;away&#93; the boy alive, and were no little comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35; Mark 6:31–33"> And we, having gone before on board ship, sailed off to Assos, going to take in Paul there; for so he had directed, he himself being about to go on foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when he met with us at Assos, having taken him on board, we came to Mitylene;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 20:17"> and having sailed thence, on the morrow arrived opposite Chios, and the next day put in at Samos; and having stayed at Trogyllium, the next day we came to Miletus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:8; Acts 2:1; Acts 19:21"> for Paul thought it desirable to sail by Ephesus, so that he might not be made to spend time in Asia; for he hastened, if it was possible for him, to be the day of Pentecost at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; 1 Timothy 5:17"> But from Miletus having sent to Ephesus, he called over &#91;to him&#93; the elders of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Acts 18:19; Acts 19:1"> And when they were come to him, he said to them, Ye know how I was with you all the time from the first day that I arrived in Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; 2 Corinthians 2:4; Romans 12:11"> serving the Lord with all lowliness, and tears, and temptations, which happened to me through the plots of the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 5:42"> how I held back nothing of what is profitable, so as not to announce &#91;it&#93; to you, and to teach you publicly and in every house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 11:18; 1 John 5:11–13"> testifying to both Jews and Greeks repentance towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20.21 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, and faith towards our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; 2 Peter 1:14"> And now, behold, bound in my spirit I go to Jerusalem, not knowing what things shall happen to me in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 9:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:4"> only that the Holy Spirit testifies to me in every city, saying that bonds and tribulations await me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6–8; 2 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 12:10"> But I make no account of &#91;my&#93; life &#91;as&#93; dear to myself, so that I finish my course, and the ministry which I have received of the Lord Jesus, to testify the glad tidings of the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20.24 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Acts 20:38; Matthew 4:23"> And now, behold, I know that ye all, among whom I have gone about preaching the kingdom &#91;of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="20.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God&#93;, shall see my face no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21; Ezekiel 33:2–9"> Wherefore I witness to you this day, that I am clean from the blood of all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 20:20; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:7–10"> for I have not shrunk from announcing to you all the counsel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="20.27 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> Take heed therefore to yourselves, and to all the flock, wherein the Holy Spirit has set you as overseers, to shepherd the assembly of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="20.28 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, which he has purchased with the blood of his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 2 Peter 2:1; John 10:12"> &#91;For&#93; I know &#91;this,&#93; that there will come in amongst you after my departure grievous wolves, not sparing the flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19–20; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Proverbs 23:33"> and from among your own selves shall rise up men speaking perverted things to draw away the disciples after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:8; Acts 20:19; Acts 19:10"> Wherefore watch, remembering that for three years, night and day, I ceased not admonishing each one &#91;of you&#93; with tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 3:24"> And now I commit you to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="20.32 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, and to the word of his grace, which is able to build &#91;you&#93; up and give &#91;to you&#93; an inheritance among all the sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:18"> I have coveted &#91;the&#93; silver or gold or clothing of no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 18:3; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> Yourselves know that these hands have ministered to my wants, and to those who were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 19:17; 2 Corinthians 9:6–12"> I have shewed you all things, that thus labouring &#91;we&#93; ought to come in aid of the weak, and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, that he himself said, It is more blessed to give than to receive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 21:5; Luke 22:41; Philippians 4:6"> And having said these things, he knelt down and prayed with them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> And they all wept sore; and falling upon the neck of Paul they ardently kissed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 15:3; 1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 20:25"> specially pained by the word which he had said, that they would no more see his face. And they went down with him to the ship.</span>
