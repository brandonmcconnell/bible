<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:23–28; 1 Corinthians 16:12; 1 Corinthians 1:12"> And it came to pass, while Apollos was at Corinth, Paul, having passed through the upper districts, came to Ephesus, and finding certain disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:39; Acts 11:15–17; Galatians 3:5"> he said to them, Did ye receive &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit when ye had believed? And they &#91;said&#93; to him, We did not even hear if &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit was &#91;come&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 8:16"> And he said, To what then were ye baptised? And they said, To the baptism of John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:29–34; Acts 11:16; Acts 1:5"> And Paul said, John indeed baptised &#91;with&#93; the baptism of repentance, saying to the people that they should believe on him that was coming after him, that is, on Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:12; Acts 8:16; Romans 6:3–4"> And when they heard that, they were baptised to the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> And Paul having laid &#91;his&#93; hands on them, the Holy Spirit came upon them, and they spoke with tongues and prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And all the men were about twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 28:23; Acts 1:3; Acts 9:20–22"> And entering into the synagogue, he spoke boldly during three months, reasoning and persuading &#91;the things&#93; concerning the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.8 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 19:23; Acts 9:2; Acts 18:6–8"> But when some were hardened and disbelieved, speaking evil of the way before the multitude, he left them and separated the disciples, reasoning daily in the school of Tyrannus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 20:31; 2 Timothy 1:15; Acts 16:6"> And this took place for two years, so that all that inhabited Asia heard the word of the Lord, both Jews and Greeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 16:17–20; Acts 5:12; Romans 15:18–19"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.11 Elohim">b</a></sup>God wrought no ordinary miracles by the hands of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; Mark 16:17"> so that even napkins or aprons were brought from his body &#91;and put&#93; upon the sick, and the diseases left them, and the wicked spirits went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 12:27; Mark 9:38; Luke 11:19"> And certain of the Jewish exorcists also, who went about, took in hand to call upon those who had wicked spirits the name of the Lord Jesus, saying, I adjure you by Jesus, whom Paul preaches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And there were certain &#91;men&#93;, seven sons of Sceva, Jewish high priest, who were doing this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 16:17–18; Luke 4:33–35; Mark 1:34"> But the wicked spirit answering said to them, Jesus I know, and Paul I am acquainted with; but ye, who are ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 8:29; Mark 5:3–4; Mark 5:15"> And the man in whom the wicked spirit was leaped upon them, and having mastered both, prevailed against them, so that they fled out of that house naked and wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:12; Acts 5:5; Philippians 2:9–11"> And this became known to all, both Jews and Greeks, who inhabited Ephesus, and fear fell upon all of them, and the name of the Lord Jesus was magnified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:10; Psalms 32:5; Matthew 3:6"> And many of those that believed came confessing and declaring their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> And many of those that practised curious arts brought their books &#91;of charms&#93; and burnt them before all. And they reckoned up the prices of them, and found it fifty thousand pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> Thus with might the word of the Lord increased and prevailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:22; Acts 20:16; Acts 23:11"> And when these things were fulfilled, Paul purposed in his spirit to go to Jerusalem, passing through Macedonia and Achaia, saying, After I have been there I must see Rome also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 16:3"> And having sent into Macedonia two of those ministering to him, Timotheus and Erastus, he remained himself awhile in Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 24:22; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And there took place at that time no small disturbance about the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Acts 16:16; Acts 16:19"> For a certain &#91;man&#93; by name Demetrius, a silver-beater, making silver temples of Artemis, brought no small gain to the artisans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; Revelation 18:11–19"> whom having brought together, and those who wrought in such things, he said, Men, ye know that our well-living arises from this work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 17:29; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> and ye see and hear that this Paul has persuaded and turned away a great crowd, not only of Ephesus, but almost of all Asia, saying that they are no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="19.26 Elohim">c</a></sup>gods which are made with hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; 1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 23:13"> Now not only there is danger for us that our business come into discredit, but also that the temple of the great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="19.27 Elohim">d</a></sup>goddess Artemis be counted for nothing, and that her greatness should be destroyed whom the whole of Asia and the world reveres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Revelation 17:13; Acts 16:19–24"> And having heard &#91;this&#93;, and being filled with rage, they cried out, saying, Great &#91;is&#93; Artemis of the Ephesians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Colossians 4:10; Acts 27:2"> And the &#91;whole&#93; city was filled with confusion, and they rushed with one accord to the theatre, having seized and carried off with &#91;them&#93; Gaius and Aristarchus, Macedonians, fellow-travellers of Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; Acts 17:22–31"> But Paul intending to go in to the people, the disciples suffered him not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:10; Acts 21:12; Acts 16:6"> and some of the Asiarchs also, who were his friends, sent to him and urged him not to throw himself into the theatre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Acts 19:40; Matthew 11:7–9"> Different persons therefore cried out some different thing; for the assembly was tumultuous, and the most did not know for what cause they had come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 12:17; 2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 1:20"> But from among the crowd they put forward Alexander, the Jews pushing him forward. And Alexander, beckoning with his hand, would have made a defence to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:28; Matthew 6:7; Revelation 13:4"> But, recognising that he was a Jew, there was one cry from all, shouting for about two hours, Great &#91;is&#93; Artemis of the Ephesians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:2; Ephesians 2:12"> And the townclerk, having quieted the crowd, said, Ephesians, what man is there then who does not know that the city of the Ephesians is temple-keeper of Artemis the great, and of the &#91;image&#93; which fell down from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Acts 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> These things therefore being undeniable, it is necessary that ye should be calm and do nothing headlong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Romans 2:22; Acts 25:8"> For ye have brought these men, &#91;who are&#93; neither temple-plunderers, nor speak injuriously of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="19.37 Elohim">e</a></sup>goddess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:7; 1 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 18:14"> If therefore Demetrius and the artisans who &#91;are&#93; with him have a matter against any one, the courts are being held, and there are proconsuls: let them accuse one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But if ye inquire anything concerning other matters, it will be settled in the regular assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 17:5–8; Matthew 26:5; Acts 20:1"> For also we are in danger to be put in accusation for sedition for this &#91;affair&#93; of to-day, no cause existing in reference to which we shall be able to give a reason for this concourse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:17; Psalms 65:7; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And having said these things, he dismissed the assembly.</span>
