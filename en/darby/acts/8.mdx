<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 22:20; Daniel 3:16–18; Acts 5:40"> And Saul was consenting to his being killed. And on that day there arose a great persecution against the assembly which was in Jerusalem, and all were scattered into the countries of Judaea and Samaria except the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 50:10–11; Genesis 23:2; Jeremiah 22:18"> And pious men buried Stephen and made great lamentation over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 15:9"> But Saul ravaged the assembly, entering into the houses one after another, and dragging off both men and women delivered them up to prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> Those then that had been scattered went through &#91;the countries&#93; announcing the glad tidings of the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> And Philip, going down to a city of Samaria, preached the Christ to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> and the crowds with one accord gave heed to the things spoken by Philip, when they heard &#91;him&#93; and saw the signs which he wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> For from many who had unclean spirits they went out, crying with a loud voice; and many that were paralysed and lame were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> And there was great joy in that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> But a certain man, by name Simon, had been before in the city, using magic arts, and astonishing the nation of Samaria, saying that himself was some great one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> To whom they had all given heed, from small to great, saying, This is the power of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.10 Elohim">a</a></sup>God which is called great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> And they gave heed to him, because that for a long time he had astonished them by his magic arts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they believed Philip announcing the glad tidings concerning the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.12 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptised, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> And Simon also himself believed; and, having been baptised, continued constantly with Philip; and, beholding the signs and great works of power which took place, was astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> And the apostles who were in Jerusalem, having heard that Samaria had received the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.14 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, sent to them Peter and John;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> who, having come down, prayed for them that they might receive &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> for he was not yet fallen upon any of them, only they were baptised to the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then they laid their hands upon them, and they received &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> But Simon, having seen that by the laying on of the hands of the apostles the &#91;Holy&#93; Spirit was given, offered them money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> saying, Give to me also this power, in order that on whomsoever I may lay hands he may receive &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> And Peter said to him, Thy money go with thee to destruction, because thou hast thought that the gift of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.20 Elohim">d</a></sup>God can be obtained by money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> Thou hast neither part nor lot in this matter, for thy heart is not upright before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.21 Elohim">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Repent therefore of this thy wickedness, and supplicate the Lord, if indeed the thought of thy heart may be forgiven thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> for I see thee to be in the gall of bitterness, and bond of unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> And Simon answering said, Supplicate ye for me to the Lord, so that nothing may come upon me of the things of which ye have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> They therefore, having testified and spoken the word of the Lord, returned to Jerusalem, and announced the glad tidings to many villages of the Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> But &#91;the&#93; angel of &#91;the&#93; Lord spoke to Philip, saying, Rise up and go southward on the way which goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza: the same is desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> And he rose up and went. And lo, an Ethiopian, a eunuch, a man in power under Candace queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasure, who had come to worship at Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> was returning and sitting in his chariot: and he was reading the prophet Esaias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> And the Spirit said to Philip, Approach and join this chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> And Philip, running up, heard him reading the prophet Esaias, and said, Dost thou then know what thou art reading of?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> And he said, How should I then be able unless some one guide me? And he begged Philip to come up and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> And the passage of the scripture which he read was this: He was led as a sheep to slaughter, and as a lamb is dumb in presence of him that shears him, thus he opens not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> In his humiliation his judgment has been taken away, and who shall declare his generation? for his life is taken from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> And the eunuch answering Philip said, I pray thee, concerning whom does the prophet say this? of himself or of some other?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> And Philip, opening his mouth and beginning from that scripture, announced the glad tidings of Jesus to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> And as they went along the way, they came upon a certain water, and the eunuch says, Behold water; what hinders my being baptised?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> And he commanded the chariot to stop. And they went down both to the water, both Philip and the eunuch, and he baptised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> But when they came up out of the water &#91;the&#93; Spirit of &#91;the&#93; Lord caught away Philip, and the eunuch saw him no longer, for he went on his way rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> And Philip was found at Azotus, and passing through he announced the glad tidings to all the cities till he came to Caesarea.</span>
