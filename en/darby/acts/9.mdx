<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13"> But Saul, still breathing out threatenings and slaughter against the disciples of the Lord, came to the high priest</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 19:23; Acts 24:14"> and asked of him letters to Damascus, to the synagogues, so that if he found any who were of the way, both men and women, he might bring &#91;them&#93; bound to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:12–13; 1 Corinthians 15:8; Acts 22:6"> But as he was journeying, it came to pass that he drew near to Damascus; and suddenly there shone round about him a light out of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 22:11; Romans 11:22"> and falling on the earth he heard a voice saying to him, Saul, Saul, why dost thou persecute me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; 1 Samuel 3:4–10; Job 9:4"> And he said, Who art thou, Lord? And he &#91;said&#93;, I am Jesus, whom thou persecutest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 22:10; Acts 26:16; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> But rise up and enter into the city, and it shall be told thee what thou must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 22:9; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13–14"> But the men who were travelling with him stood speechless, hearing the voice but beholding no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 22:11; Acts 9:18; 2 Kings 6:17–20"> And Saul rose up from the earth, and his eyes being opened he saw no one. But leading &#91;him&#93; by the hand they brought him into Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Esther 4:16; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> And he was three days without seeing, and neither ate nor drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Acts 10:3; Acts 22:12"> And there was a certain disciple in Damascus by name Ananias. And the Lord said to him in a vision, Ananias. And he said, Behold, &#91;here am&#93; I, Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 22:3; Acts 9:30"> And the Lord &#91;said&#93; to him, Rise up and go into the street which is called Straight, and seek in the house of Judas one by name Saul, &#91;he is&#93; of Tarsus: for, behold, he is praying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Acts 9:10; Acts 9:17–18"> and has seen &#91;in a vision&#93; a man by name Ananias coming in and putting his hand on him, so that he should see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3; Acts 22:19–20"> And Ananias answered, Lord, I have heard from many concerning this man how much evil he has done to thy saints at Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:21; Acts 7:59; 2 Timothy 2:22"> and here he has authority from the chief priests to bind all who call upon thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:15–16; Ephesians 3:7–8; 2 Timothy 4:16–17"> And the Lord said to him, Go, for this &#91;man&#93; is an elect vessel to me, to bear my name before both nations and kings and &#91;the&#93; sons of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:11; 2 Corinthians 6:4"> for I will shew to him how much he must suffer for my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 8:17; Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4"> And Ananias went and entered into the house; and laying his hands upon him he said, Saul, brother, the Lord has sent me, Jesus that appeared to thee in the way in which thou camest, that thou mightest see, and be filled with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:14; Acts 22:16; Acts 2:38"> And straightway there fell from his eyes as it were scales, and he saw, and rising up was baptised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:20; 1 Samuel 30:12; Galatians 1:17"> and, having received food, got strength. And he was with the disciples who &#91;were&#93; in Damascus certain days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 20:28; Galatians 2:20; 1 John 4:14–15"> And straightway in the synagogues he preached Jesus that he is the Son of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.20 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:13–14; Acts 8:3; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> And all who heard were astonished and said, Is not this he who destroyed in Jerusalem those who called on this name, and here was come for this purpose, that he might bring them bound to the chief priests?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 18:5; Isaiah 40:29; Luke 21:15"> But Saul increased the more in power, and confounded the Jews who dwelt in Damascus, proving that this is the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:17–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Acts 14:19"> Now when many days were fulfilled, the Jews consulted together to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:32; Acts 25:3; Acts 20:3"> But their plot became known to Saul. And they watched also the gates both day and night, that they might kill him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; 2 Corinthians 11:33; 1 Samuel 19:11–12"> but the disciples took him by night and let him down through the wall, lowering him in a basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:20; Galatians 1:17–19; Acts 9:19"> And having arrived at Jerusalem he essayed to join himself to the disciples, and all were afraid of him, not believing that he was a disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 4:29; Acts 4:13"> But Barnabas took him and brought him to the apostles, and related to them how he had seen the Lord in the way, and that he had spoken to him, and how in Damascus he had spoken boldly in the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 1:21; Numbers 27:16–17; Psalms 121:8"> And he was with them coming in and going out at Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 6:1; Jude 1:9; Acts 11:20"> and speaking boldly in the name of the Lord. And he spoke and discussed with the Hellenists; but they sought to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 11:25; Acts 8:40; Acts 9:11"> And the brethren knowing it, brought him down to Caesarea and sent him away to Tarsus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; John 14:16–18; Isaiah 33:6"> The assemblies then throughout the whole of Judaea and Galilee and Samaria had peace, being edified and walking in the fear of the Lord, and were increased through the comfort of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:13; Acts 8:25; Proverbs 2:8"> Now it came to pass that Peter, passing through all &#91;quarters&#93;, descended also to the saints who inhabited Lydda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 13:16; John 5:5; Mark 2:3–11"> And he found there a certain man, Aeneas by name, who had been lying for eight years upon a couch, who was paralysed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Acts 3:12; Acts 3:6"> And Peter said to him, Aeneas, Jesus, the Christ, heals thee: rise up, and make thy couch for thyself. And straightway he rose up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 9:42; 1 Chronicles 5:16"> And all who inhabited Lydda and the Saron saw him, who turned to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Titus 2:7; 2 Chronicles 2:16"> And in Joppa there was a certain female disciple, by name Tabitha, which being interpreted means Dorcas. She was full of good works and alms-deeds which she did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Acts 20:8; John 11:36–37"> And it came to pass in those days that she grew sick and died; and, having washed her, they put her in &#91;the&#93; upper room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Acts 11:26; Acts 9:32"> But Lydda being near to Joppa, the disciples having heard that Peter was there, sent two men to him, beseeching him, Thou must not delay coming to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Proverbs 31:30–31; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> And Peter rising up went with them, whom, when arrived, they brought up into the upper chamber; and all the widows stood by him weeping and shewing him the body-coats and garments which Dorcas had made while she was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:25; Mark 5:40–42; Luke 8:54"> But Peter, putting them all out, and kneeling down, prayed. And, turning to the body, he said, Tabitha, arise. And she opened her eyes, and, seeing Peter, sat up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 6:1; Genesis 45:26"> And having given her &#91;his&#93; hand, he raised her up, and having called the saints and the widows, presented her living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:44; John 12:11; John 11:45"> And it became known throughout the whole of Joppa, and many believed on the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:6; Acts 10:32"> And it came to pass that he remained many days in Joppa with a certain Simon, a tanner.</span>
