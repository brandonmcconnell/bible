<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:16; 2 Timothy 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> And Paul, fixing his eyes on the council, said, Brethren, I have walked in all good conscience with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 18:22; Acts 24:1; Micah 5:1"> But the high priest Ananias ordered those standing by him to smite his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:1–2; John 7:51; Matthew 23:27–28"> Then Paul said to him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.3 Elohim">b</a></sup>God will smite thee, whited wall. And thou, dost thou sit judging me according to the law, and breaking the law commandest me to be smitten?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And those that stood by said, Dost thou rail against the high priest of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.4 Elohim">c</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Ecclesiastes 10:20; Jude 1:8–9"> And Paul said, I was not conscious, brethren, that he was high priest; for it is written, Thou shalt not speak evilly of the ruler of thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:15; Acts 24:21; Acts 26:5–8"> But Paul, knowing that the one part &#91;of them&#93; were of the Sadducees and the other of the Pharisees, cried out in the council, Brethren, I am a Pharisee, son of Pharisees: I am judged concerning the hope and resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:9; John 7:40–43; Matthew 10:34"> And when he had spoken this, there was a tumult of the Pharisees and the Sadducees, and the multitude was divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 20:27; Matthew 22:23; Mark 12:18"> For Sadducees say there is no resurrection, nor angel, nor spirit; but Pharisees confess both of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 22:7; Acts 22:17–18; John 12:29"> And there was a great clamour, and the scribes of the Pharisees' part rising up contended, saying, We find nothing evil in this man; and if a spirit has spoken to him, or an angel...</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:14–2; Acts 22:24"> And a great tumult having arisen, the chiliarch, fearing lest Paul should have been torn in pieces by them, commanded the troop to come down and take him by force from the midst of them, and to bring &#91;him&#93; into the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 18:9; Acts 19:21; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But the following night the Lord stood by him, and said, Be of good courage; for as thou hast testified the things concerning me at Jerusalem, so thou must bear witness at Rome also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Isaiah 8:9–10; Acts 23:21"> And when it was day, the Jews, having banded together, put themselves under a curse, saying that they would neither eat nor drink till they should kill Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 16:2; 2 Samuel 15:31; 2 Samuel 15:12"> And they were more than forty who had joined together in this oath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 23:12; Jeremiah 8:12; Isaiah 3:9"> and they went to the chief priests and elders, and said, We have cursed ourselves with a curse to taste nothing until we kill Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalms 37:32–33; Proverbs 1:16"> Now therefore do ye with the council make a representation to the chiliarch so that he may bring him down to you, as about to determine more precisely what concerns him, and we, before he draws near, are ready to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:10; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Acts 23:32"> But Paul's sister's son, having heard of the lying in wait, came and entered into the fortress and reported &#91;it&#93; to Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Matthew 8:8–9; Matthew 10:16"> And Paul, having called one of the centurions, said, Take this youth to the chiliarch, for he has something to report to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 3:1; Ephesians 4:1; Acts 28:17"> He therefore, having taken him with &#91;him&#93;, led him to the chiliarch, and says, The prisoner Paul called me to &#91;him&#93; and asked me to lead this youth to thee, who has something to say to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Esther 9:12; Esther 7:2"> And the chiliarch having taken him by the hand, and having gone apart in private, inquired, What is it that thou hast to report to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 23:14–15; Acts 23:1; Psalms 12:2"> And he said, The Jews have agreed together to make a request to thee, that thou mayest bring Paul down to-morrow into the council, as about to inquire something more precise concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:12–14; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> Do not thou then be persuaded by them, for there lie in wait for him of them more than forty men, who have put themselves under a curse neither to eat nor drink till they kill him; and now they are ready waiting the promise from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 1:44; Joshua 2:14"> The chiliarch then dismissed the youth, commanding &#91;him&#93;, Utter to no one that thou hast represented these things to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:25; Acts 23:33; Luke 12:38"> And having called to &#91;him&#93; certain two of the centurions, he said, Prepare two hundred soldiers that they may go as far as Caesarea, and seventy horsemen, and two hundred light-armed footmen, for the third hour of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 25:14; Acts 23:26; Acts 24:10"> And &#91;he ordered them&#93; to provide beasts, that they might set Paul on them and carry &#91;him&#93; safe through to Felix the governor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> having written a letter, couched in this form:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> Claudius Lysias to the most excellent governor Felix, greeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Acts 21:31–33; Acts 24:6"> This man, having been taken by the Jews, and being about to be killed by them, I came up with the military and took out &#91;of their hands&#93;, having learned that he was a Roman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 22:30"> And desiring to know the charge on which they accused him, I brought him down to their council;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 26:31; Acts 25:25; Acts 18:15"> whom I found to be accused of questions of their law, but to have no charge laid against him &#91;making him&#93; worthy of death or of bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:16; Acts 25:5–6"> But having received information of a plot about to be put in execution against the man &#91;by the Jews&#93;, I have immediately sent him to thee, commanding also his accusers to say before thee the things that are against him. &#91;Farewell.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:3–4; Luke 7:8; Acts 23:23–24"> The soldiers therefore, according to what was ordered them, took Paul and brought him by night to Antipatris,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:23; Acts 23:10"> and on the morrow, having left the horsemen to go with him, returned to the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 23:23–24; Acts 28:16"> And these, having entered into Caesarea, and given up the letter to the governor, presented Paul also to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 25:1; Daniel 2:49"> And having read &#91;it&#93;, and asked of what eparchy he was, and learned that &#91;he was&#93; of Cilicia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16; Matthew 27:27"> he said, I will hear thee fully when thine accusers also are arrived. And he commanded him to be kept in Herod's praetorium.</span>
