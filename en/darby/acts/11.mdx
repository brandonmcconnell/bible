<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 32:15"> And the apostles and the brethren who were in Judaea heard that the nations also had received the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.1 Elohim">a</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 10:9; Acts 15:1"> and when Peter went up to Jerusalem, they of the circumcision contended with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Galatians 2:12; Luke 15:2"> saying, Thou wentest in to men uncircumcised and hast eaten with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Acts 14:27; Joshua 22:21–31"> But Peter began and set forth &#91;the matter&#93; to them in order, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 10:9–32; Acts 9:10; Acts 22:17"> I was in the city of Joppa praying, and in an ecstasy I saw a vision, a certain vessel descending like a great sheet, let down by four corners out of heaven, and it came even to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 4:20; Acts 3:4"> on which having fixed mine eyes, I considered, and saw the quadrupeds of the earth, and the wild beasts, and the creeping things, and the fowls of the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And I heard also a voice saying to me, Rise up, Peter, slay and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Hosea 9:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> And I said, In no wise, Lord, for common or unclean has never entered into my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:9; Hebrews 9:13–14; Acts 10:34–35"> And a voice answered the second time out of heaven, What<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.9 Elohim">b</a></sup>God has cleansed, do not thou make common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 21:17; Numbers 24:10; 2 Corinthians 12:8"> And this took place thrice, and again all was drawn up into heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 4:27; Acts 9:10–12; Exodus 4:14"> and lo, immediately three men were at the house in which I was, sent to me from Caesarea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Romans 3:22; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> And the Spirit said to me to go with them, nothing doubting. And there went with me these six brethren also, and we entered into the house of the man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:30–32; Acts 10:3–6; Acts 9:43"> and he related to us how he had seen the angel in his house, standing and saying &#91;to him&#93;, Send &#91;men&#93; to Joppa and fetch Simon, who is surnamed Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:15; Acts 10:22; Acts 16:31–34"> who shall speak words to thee whereby thou shalt be saved, thou and all thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 10:34–46; Acts 2:2–12"> And as I began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell upon them even as upon us also at the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Mark 1:8; Proverbs 1:23"> And I remembered the word of the Lord, how he said, John baptised with water, but ye shall be baptised with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:47; Romans 9:20–26; Daniel 4:35"> If then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.17 Elohim">c</a></sup>God has given them the same gift as also to us when we had believed on the Lord Jesus Christ, who indeed was I to be able to forbid<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 36:26"> And when they heard these things they held their peace, and glorified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.18 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, saying, Then indeed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.18 Elohim">f</a></sup>God has to the nations also granted repentance to life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 21:2; Acts 15:3"> They then who had been scattered abroad through the tribulation that took place on the occasion of Stephen, passed through &#91;the country&#93; to Phoenicia and Cyprus and Antioch, speaking the word to no one but to Jews alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 1:23–24"> But there were certain of them, Cyprians and Cyrenians, who entering into Antioch spoke to the Greeks also, announcing the glad tidings of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:66; Acts 2:47; Acts 5:14"> And &#91;the&#93; Lord's hand was with them, and a great number believed and turned to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:27; Acts 8:14; Acts 13:1–3"> And the report concerning them reached the ears of the assembly which was in Jerusalem, and they sent out Barnabas to go through as far as Antioch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:43"> who, having arrived and seeing the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="11.23 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, rejoiced, and exhorted all with purpose of heart to abide with the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 5:14; Acts 9:31"> for he was a good man and full of &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit and of faith; and a large crowd &#91;of people&#93; were added to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 9:11; Acts 9:27; Acts 9:30"> And he went away to Tarsus to seek out Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; Acts 26:28; 1 Peter 4:14"> And having found &#91;him&#93;, he brought him to Antioch. And so it was with them that for a whole year they were gathered together in the assembly and taught a large crowd: and the disciples were first called Christians in Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Ephesians 4:11"> Now in these days prophets went down from Jerusalem to Antioch;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 21:10; Acts 18:2; Luke 3:1"> and one from among them, by name Agabus, rose up and signified by the Spirit that there was going to be a great famine over all the inhabited earth, which also came to pass under Claudius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12–14; Galatians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–4"> And they determined, according as any one of the disciples was well off, each of them to send to the brethren who dwelt in Judaea, to minister &#91;to them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:23; Acts 12:25; Acts 15:4"> which also they did, sending it to the elders by the hand of Barnabas and Saul.</span>
