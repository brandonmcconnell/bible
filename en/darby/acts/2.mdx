<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> And when the day of Pentecost was now accomplishing, they were all together in one place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalms 18:10"> And there came suddenly a sound out of heaven as of a violent impetuous blowing, and filled all the house where they were sitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> And there appeared to them parted tongues, as of fire, and it sat upon each one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> And they were all filled with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, and began to speak with other tongues as the Spirit gave to them to speak forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> Now there were dwelling at Jerusalem Jews, pious men, from every nation of those under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> But the rumour of this having spread, the multitude came together and were confounded, because each one heard them speaking in his own dialect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> And all were amazed and wondered, saying, Behold, are not all these who are speaking Galilaeans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and how do we hear &#91;them&#93; each in our own dialect in which we have been born,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians, and Medes, and Elamites, and those who inhabit Mesopotamia, and Judaea, and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> both Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt, and the parts of Libya which adjoin Cyrene, and the Romans sojourning &#91;here&#93;, both Jews and proselytes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 78:4"> Cretans and Arabians, we hear them speaking in our own tongues the great things of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.11 Elohim">a</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> And they were all amazed and in perplexity, saying one to another, What would this mean?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> But others mocking said, They are full of new wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> But Peter, standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice and spoke forth to them, Men of Judaea, and all ye inhabitants of Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and give heed to my words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> for these are not full of wine, as ye suppose, for it is the third hour of the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> but this is that which was spoken through the prophet Joel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> And it shall be in the last days, saith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.17 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, &#91;that&#93; I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your young men shall see visions, and your elders shall dream with dreams;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> yea, even upon my bondmen and upon my bondwomen in those days will I pour out of my Spirit, and they shall prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> And I will give wonders in the heaven above and signs on the earth below, blood, and fire, and vapour of smoke:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> the sun shall be changed to darkness and the moon to blood, before the great and gloriously appearing day of &#91;the&#93; Lord come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> And it shall be that whosoever shall call upon the name of &#91;the&#93; Lord shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> Men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus the Nazaraean, a man borne witness to by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.22 Elohim">c</a></sup>God to you by works of power and wonders and signs, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.22 Elohim">d</a></sup>God wrought by him in your midst, as yourselves know</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> — him, given up by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.23 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, ye, by &#91;the&#93; hand of lawless &#91;men&#93;, have crucified and slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> Whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.24 Elohim">f</a></sup>God has raised up, having loosed the pains of death, inasmuch as it was not possible that he should be held by its power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> for David says as to him, I foresaw the Lord continually before me, because he is at my right hand that I may not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> Therefore has my heart rejoiced and my tongue exulted; yea more, my flesh also shall dwell in hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> for thou wilt not leave my soul in hades, nor wilt thou give thy gracious one to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> Thou hast made known to me &#91;the&#93; paths of life, thou wilt fill me with joy with thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> Brethren, let it be allowed to speak with freedom to you concerning the patriarch David, that he has both died and been buried, and his monument is amongst us unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> Being therefore a prophet, and knowing that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.30 Elohim">g</a></sup>God had sworn to him with an oath, of the fruit of his loins to set upon his throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> he, seeing &#91;it&#93; before, spoke concerning the resurrection of the Christ, that neither has he been left in hades nor his flesh seen corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="2.32 Elohim">h</a></sup>God raised up, whereof all we are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Having therefore been exalted by the right hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2.33 Elohim">i</a></sup>God, and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, he has poured out this which ye behold and hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David has not ascended into the heavens, but he says himself, The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit at my right hand</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> until I have put thine enemies &#91;to be&#93; the footstool of thy feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Let the whole house of Israel therefore know assuredly that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="2.36 Elohim">j</a></sup>God has made him, this Jesus whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And having heard &#91;it&#93; they were pricked in heart, and said to Peter and the other apostles, What shall we do, brethren?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> And Peter said to them, Repent, and be baptised, each one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ, for remission of sins, and ye will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For to you is the promise and to your children, and to all who &#91;are&#93; afar off, as many as &#91;the&#93; Lord our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="2.39 Elohim">k</a></sup>God may call.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> And with many other words he testified and exhorted them, saying, Be saved from this perverse generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> Those then who had accepted his word were baptised; and there were added in that day about three thousand souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> And they persevered in the teaching and fellowship of the apostles, in breaking of bread and prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> And fear was upon every soul, and many wonders and signs took place through the apostles' means.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And all that believed were together, and had all things common,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> and sold their possessions and substance, and distributed them to all, according as any one might have need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> And every day, being constantly in the temple with one accord, and breaking bread in &#91;the&#93; house, they received their food with gladness and simplicity of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="2.47 Elohim">l</a></sup>God, and having favour with all the people; and the Lord added &#91;to the assembly&#93; daily those that were to be saved.</span>
