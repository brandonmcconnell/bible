<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:35; Acts 11:22–27"> Now there were in Antioch, in the assembly which was &#91;there&#93;, prophets and teachers: Barnabas, and Simeon who was called Niger, and Lucius the Cyrenian, and Manaen, foster-brother of Herod the tetrarch, and Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And as they were ministering to the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, Separate me now Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 2:2; Acts 13:2"> Then, having fasted and prayed, and having laid &#91;their&#93; hands on them, they let &#91;them&#93; go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 13:2; Acts 11:19"> They therefore, having been sent forth by the Holy Spirit, went down to Seleucia, and thence sailed away to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Acts 13:46; Acts 14:1"> And being in Salamis, they announced the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God in the synagogues of the Jews. And they had John also as &#91;their&#93; attendant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 1 John 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> And having passed through the whole island as far as Paphos, they found a certain man a magician, a false prophet, a Jew, whose name was Bar-jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:38; Acts 13:12; Acts 18:12"> who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man. He, having called Barnabas and Saul to &#91;him&#93;, desired to hear the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.7 Elohim">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Acts 13:6–7; 2 Timothy 4:14–15"> But Elymas the magician (for so his name is by interpretation) opposed them, seeking to turn away the proconsul from the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 3:8; Acts 4:8; Acts 2:4"> But Saul, who also &#91;is&#93; Paul, filled with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit, fixing his eyes upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 13:38"> said, O full of all deceit and all craft: son of &#91;the&#93; devil, enemy of all righteousness; wilt thou not cease perverting the right paths of &#91;the&#93; Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 9:3; Psalms 32:4; Hebrews 10:31"> And now behold, &#91;the&#93; Lord's hand &#91;is&#93; upon thee, and thou shalt be blind, not seeing the sun for a season. And immediately there fell upon him a mist and darkness; and going about he sought persons who should lead him by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Acts 13:7; Luke 4:22"> Then the proconsul, seeing what had happened, believed, being amazed at the teaching of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 15:38; Acts 2:10; Acts 14:24–25"> And having sailed from Paphos, Paul and his company came to Perga of Pamphylia; and John separated from them and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:13; Acts 14:19; Acts 13:5"> But they, passing through from Perga, came to Antioch of Pisidia; and entering into the synagogue on the sabbath day they sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 15:21; Mark 5:22"> And after the reading of the law and the prophets, the rulers of the synagogue sent to them, saying, Brethren, if ye have any word of exhortation to the people, speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 13:26; Acts 10:2"> And Paul, rising up and making a sign with the hand, said, Israelites, and ye that fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.16 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; Isaiah 63:9–14; Psalms 105:23–24"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.17 Elohim">d</a></sup>God of this people Israel chose our fathers, and exalted the people in their sojourn in &#91;the&#93; land of Egypt, and with a high arm brought them out of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Psalms 95:8–11; Psalms 106:13–29"> and for a time of about forty years he nursed them in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Psalms 78:55; Acts 7:45"> And having destroyed seven nations in the land of Canaan, he gave them their land as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:16; 1 Samuel 3:20; 2 Kings 23:22"> And after these things he gave &#91;them&#93; judges till Samuel the prophet, &#91;to the end of&#93; about four hundred and fifty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 1 Samuel 15:1; 1 Samuel 12:12–19"> And then they asked for a king, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.21 Elohim">e</a></sup>God gave to them Saul, son of Kis, a man of the tribe of Benjamin, during forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalms 78:70–72"> And having removed him he raised up to them David for king, of whom also bearing witness he said, I have found David, the son of Jesse, a man after my heart, who shall do all my will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Psalms 132:11; Revelation 22:16"> Of this man's seed according to promise has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.23 Elohim">f</a></sup>God brought to Israel a Saviour, Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 1:22; John 1:6–8; Luke 3:2–20"> John having proclaimed before the face of his entry &#91;among the people&#93; &#91;the&#93; baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Mark 1:7; John 1:26–27"> And as John was fulfilling his course he said, Whom do ye suppose that I am? I am not &#91;he&#93;. But behold, there comes one after me, the sandal of whose feet I am not worthy to loose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Romans 1:16; Luke 1:69"> Brethren, sons of Abraham's race, and those who among you fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.26 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, to you has the word of this salvation been sent:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 3:17; Luke 24:24–27; Luke 24:20"> for those who dwell in Jerusalem, and their rulers, not having known him, have fulfilled also the voices of the prophets which are read on every sabbath, &#91;by&#93; judging &#91;him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 23:21–25; Luke 23:14–16; John 19:4"> And having found no cause of death &#91;in him&#93;, they begged of Pilate that he might be slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 23:53; John 19:28; John 19:30"> And when they had fulfilled all things written concerning him, they took him down from the cross and put him in a sepulchre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Acts 17:31; Matthew 28:6"> but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13.30 Elohim">h</a></sup>God raised him from among &#91;the&#93; dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:3; Acts 1:11"> who appeared for many days to those who had come up with him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are now his witnesses to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Acts 26:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> And we declare unto you the glad tidings of the promise made to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Hebrews 5:5; Hebrews 1:5–6"> that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="13.33 Elohim">i</a></sup>God has fulfilled this to us their children, having raised up Jesus; as it is also written in the second psalm, Thou art my Son: this day have I begotten thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 89:2–4; Romans 6:9"> But that he raised him from among &#91;the&#93; dead, no more to return to corruption, he spoke thus: I will give to you the faithful mercies of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:27–31; Psalms 49:9"> Wherefore also he says in another, Thou wilt not suffer thy gracious one to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:29; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 2:10"> For David indeed, having in his own generation ministered to the will of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="13.36 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, fell asleep, and was added to his fathers and saw corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 13:30; Acts 2:24"> But he whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="13.37 Elohim">k</a></sup>God raised up did not see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 24:47; 1 John 2:12; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> Be it known unto you, therefore, brethren, that through this man remission of sins is preached to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Galatians 2:16; Romans 8:3"> and from all things from which ye could not be justified in the law of Moses, in him every one that believes is justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Hebrews 3:12; Malachi 3:2"> See therefore that that which is spoken in the prophets do not come upon &#91;you&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Peter 4:17; Romans 11:7–14"> Behold, ye despisers, and wonder and perish; for I work a work in your days, a work which ye will in no wise believe if one declare it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Acts 10:33; Matthew 19:30"> And as they went out they begged that these words might be spoken to them the ensuing sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 2:8"> And the congregation of the synagogue having broken up, many of the Jews and of the worshipping proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who speaking to them, persuaded them to continue in the grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="13.43 Elohim">l</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:3; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 11:10"> And on the coming sabbath almost all the city was gathered together to hear the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="13.44 Elohim">m</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Acts 18:6; Acts 5:17"> But the Jews, seeing the crowds, were filled with envy, and contradicted the things said by Paul, &#91;contradicting and&#93; speaking injuriously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Matthew 21:43; Acts 18:5–6"> And Paul and Barnabas spoke boldly and said, It was necessary that the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="13.46 Elohim">n</a></sup>God should be first spoken to you; but, since ye thrust it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of eternal life, lo, we turn to the nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> for thus has the Lord enjoined us: I have set thee for a light of the nations, that thou shouldest be for salvation to the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:4; Romans 11:7"> And &#91;those of&#93; the nations, hearing it, rejoiced, and glorified the word of the Lord, and believed, as many as were ordained to eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 19:10; Acts 19:26"> And the word of the Lord was carried through the whole country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 13:45; Acts 14:2"> But the Jews excited the women of the upper classes who were worshippers, and the first people of the city, and raised a persecution against Paul and Barnabas, and cast them out of their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:14; Acts 18:6; Luke 9:5"> But they, having shaken off the dust of their feet against them, came to Iconium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Romans 15:13; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; Acts 4:31"> And the disciples were filled with joy and &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit.</span>
