<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:11; Acts 23:2; Acts 25:2"> And after five days came down the high priest Ananias, with the elders, and a certain orator called Tertullus, and laid their informations against Paul before the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:5; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 24:26–27"> And he having been called, Tertullus began to accuse, saying, Seeing we enjoy great peace through thee, and that excellent measures are executed for this nation by thy forethought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> we receive &#91;it&#93; always and everywhere, most excellent Felix, with all thankfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32"> But that I may not too much intrude on thy time, I beseech thee to hear us briefly in thy kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 28:22; Acts 24:14; Acts 26:5"> For finding this man a pest, and moving sedition among all the Jews throughout the world, and a leader of the sect of the Nazaraeans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 21:27–32; John 18:31"> who also attempted to profane the temple; whom we also had seized, &#91;and would have judged according to our law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> but Lysias, the chiliarch, coming up, took &#91;him&#93; away with great force out of our hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:15–16; Acts 25:5"> having commanded his accusers to come to thee;&#93; of whom thou canst thyself, in examining &#91;him&#93;, know the certainty of all these things of which we accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Micah 7:2–3; Psalms 4:2"> And the Jews also joined in pressing the matter against &#91;Paul&#93;, saying that these things were so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> But Paul, the governor having beckoned to him to speak, answered, Knowing that for many years thou hast been judge to this nation, I answer readily as to the things which concern myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> As thou mayest know that there are not more than twelve days since I went up to worship at Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> and neither in the temple did they find me discoursing to any one, or making any tumultuous gathering together of the crowd, nor in the synagogues, nor in the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> neither can they make good the things of which they now accuse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> But this I avow to thee, that in the way which they call sect, so I serve my fathers'<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.14 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, believing all things which are written throughout the law, and in the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> having hope towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="24.15 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, which they themselves also receive, that there is to be a resurrection both of just and unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> For this cause I also exercise &#91;myself&#93; to have in everything a conscience without offence towards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="24.16 Elohim">c</a></sup>God and men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> And after a lapse of many years I arrived, bringing alms to my nation, and offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> Whereupon they found me purified in the temple, with neither crowd nor tumult. But it was certain Jews from Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> who ought to appear before thee and accuse, if they have anything against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> or let these themselves say what wrong they found in me when I stood before the council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> &#91;other&#93; than concerning this one voice which I cried standing amongst them: I am judged this day by you touching &#91;the&#93; resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> And Felix, knowing accurately the things concerning the way, adjourned them, saying, When Lysias the chiliarch is come down I will determine your affair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> ordering the centurion to keep him, and that he should have freedom, and to hinder none of his friends to minister to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> And after certain days, Felix having arrived with Drusilla his wife, who was a Jewess, he sent for Paul and heard him concerning the faith in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> And as he reasoned concerning righteousness, and temperance, and the judgment about to come, Felix, being filled with fear, answered, Go for the present, and when I get an opportunity I will send for thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> hoping at the same time that money would be given him by Paul: wherefore also he sent for him the oftener and communed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> But when two years were completed, Felix was relieved by Porcius Festus as his successor; and Felix, desirous to oblige the Jews, to acquire their favour, left Paul bound.</span>
</div>
