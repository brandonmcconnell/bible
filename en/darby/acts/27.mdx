<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 25:25; Acts 25:12; Acts 10:1"> But when it had been determined that we should sail to Italy, they delivered up Paul and certain other prisoners to a centurion, by name Julius, of Augustus' company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Philemon 1:24; Colossians 4:10"> And going on board a ship of Adramyttium about to navigate by the places along Asia, we set sail, Aristarchus, a Macedonian of Thessalonica, being with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 24:23; Acts 28:16; Acts 27:43"> And the next day we arrived at Sidon. And Julius treated Paul kindly and suffered him to go to his friends and refresh himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:39; Acts 13:4"> And setting sail thence we sailed under the lee of Cyprus, because the winds were contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:9; Acts 13:13; Acts 15:41"> And having sailed over the waters of Cilicia and Pamphylia we came to Myra in Lycia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 28:11; Acts 27:1; Acts 6:9"> and there the centurion having found a ship of Alexandria sailing to Italy, he made us go on board her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 27:21; Titus 1:5; Titus 1:12"> And sailing slowly for many days, and having with difficulty got abreast of Cnidus, the wind not suffering us, we sailed under the lee of Crete abreast of Salmone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and coasting it with difficulty we came to a certain place called Fair Havens, near to which was &#91;the&#93; city of Lasaea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 29:7; Leviticus 23:27–29; Leviticus 16:29–31"> And much time having now been spent, and navigation being already dangerous, because the fast also was already past, Paul counselled them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 2:30; Acts 27:20–26; Acts 27:41–44"> saying, Men, I perceive that the navigation will be with disaster and much loss, not only of the cargo and the ship, but also of our lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:4; Hebrews 11:7; Ezekiel 3:17–18"> But the centurion believed rather the helmsman and the shipowner than what was said by Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:30; Acts 27:8"> And the harbour being ill adapted to winter in, the most counselled to set sail thence, if perhaps they might reach Phoenice to winter in, a port of Crete looking north-east and south-east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:55; Song of Solomon 4:16; Job 37:17"> And &#91;the&#93; south wind blowing gently, supposing that they had gained their object, having weighed anchor they sailed close in shore along Crete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 4:37; Psalms 107:25–27; Exodus 14:21–27"> But not long after there came down it a hurricane called Euroclydon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:4; Acts 27:27"> And the ship being caught and driven, and not able to bring her head to the wind, letting her go we were driven &#91;before it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But running under the lee of a certain island called Clauda, we were with difficulty able to make ourselves masters of the boat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 27:29; Acts 27:26; Acts 27:41"> which having hoisted up, they used helps, frapping the ship; and fearing lest they should run into Syrtis and run aground, and having lowered the gear they were so driven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jonah 1:5; Acts 27:38; Matthew 16:26"> But the storm being extremely violent on us, on the next day they threw cargo overboard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:4; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> and on the third day with their own hands they cast away the ship furniture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Matthew 8:24–25; Jeremiah 2:25"> And neither sun nor stars appearing for many days, and no small storm lying on us, in the end all hope of our being saved was taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 42:22; Psalms 107:5–6; Acts 27:7"> And when they had been a long while without taking food, Paul then standing up in the midst of them said, Ye ought, O men, to have hearkened to me, and not have made sail from Crete and have gained this disaster and loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 27:25; Acts 27:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> And now I exhort you to be of good courage, for there shall be no loss at all of life of &#91;any&#93; of you, only of the ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 4:17"> For an angel of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="27.23 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, whose I am and whom I serve, stood by me this night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 23:11; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Matthew 10:28"> saying, Fear not, Paul; thou must stand before Caesar; and behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="27.24 Elohim">b</a></sup>God has granted to thee all those that sail with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Luke 1:45; Numbers 23:19"> Wherefore be of good courage, men, for I believe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="27.25 Elohim">c</a></sup>God that thus it shall be, as it has been said to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 28:1; Acts 27:17; Acts 27:29"> But we must be cast ashore on a certain island.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Acts 27:30; Revelation 18:17"> And when the fourteenth night was come, we being driven about in Adria, towards the middle of the night the sailors supposed that some land neared them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and having sounded found twenty fathoms, and having gone a little farther and having again sounded they found fifteen fathoms;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 27:17; Hebrews 6:19; Deuteronomy 28:67"> and fearing lest we should be cast on rocky places, casting four anchors out of the stern, they wished that day were come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 27:16; Acts 27:32"> But the sailors wishing to flee out of the ship, and having let down the boat into the sea under pretext of being about to carry out anchors from the prow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13–14; Psalms 91:11–12; Jeremiah 29:11–13"> Paul said to the centurion and the soldiers, Unless these abide in the ship ye cannot be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Luke 16:8"> Then the soldiers cut away the ropes of the boat and let her fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And while it was drawing on to daylight, Paul exhorted them all to partake of food, saying, Ye have passed the fourteenth day watching in expectation without taking food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Kings 1:52; Luke 21:18"> Wherefore I exhort you to partake of food, for this has to do with your safety; for not a hair from the head of any one of you shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 15:36; 1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> And, having said these things and taken a loaf, he gave thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="27.35 Elohim">d</a></sup>God before all, and having broken it began to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4–6; Acts 27:22; Psalms 27:14"> And all taking courage, themselves also took food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Romans 13:1; 1 Peter 3:20"> And we were in the ship, all the souls, two hundred and seventy-six.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jonah 1:5; Matthew 6:25; Acts 27:18–19"> And having satisfied themselves with food, they lightened the ship, casting out the wheat into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 28:1"> And when it was day they did not recognise the land; but they perceived a certain bay having a strand, on which they were minded, if they should be able, to run the ship ashore;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; Acts 27:29–30"> and, having cast off the anchors, they left &#91;them&#93; in the sea, at the same time loosening the lashings of the rudders, and hoisting the foresail to the wind, they made for the strand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:25–26; Ezekiel 27:26; Acts 27:26–29"> And falling into a place where two seas met they ran the ship aground, and the prow having stuck itself fast remained unmoved, but the stern was broken by the force of the waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 15:15–20; Ecclesiastes 9:3; Psalms 74:20"> And &#91;the&#93; counsel of the soldiers was that they should kill the prisoners, lest any one should swim off and escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7; Acts 27:11"> But the centurion, desirous of saving Paul, hindered them of their purpose, and commanded those who were able to swim, casting themselves first &#91;into the sea&#93;, to get out on land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 27:22; Psalms 107:28–30; John 6:39–40"> and the rest, some on boards, some on some of the things &#91;that came&#93; from the ship; and thus it came to pass that all got safe to land.</span>
