<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 15:20; Matthew 10:17–18"> At that time Herod the king laid his hands on some of those of the assembly to do them hurt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:37; Matthew 20:23; Matthew 4:21–22"> and slew James, the brother of John, with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:15; John 12:43; Acts 24:27"> And seeing that it was pleasing to the Jews, he went on to take Peter also: (and they were the days of unleavened bread:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Luke 22:33; Luke 21:12"> whom having seized he put in prison, having delivered him to four quaternions of soldiers to keep, purposing after the passover to bring him out to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16"> Peter therefore was kept in the prison; but unceasing prayer was made by the assembly to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Hebrews 13:6; Philippians 4:6–7"> And when Herod was going to bring him forth, that night Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains, and guards before the door kept the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Psalms 146:7"> And lo, an angel of &#91;the&#93; Lord came there, and a light shone in the prison: and having smitten the side of Peter, he roused him up, saying, Rise up quickly. And his chains fell off his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the angel said to him, Gird thyself, and bind on thy sandals. And he did so. And he says to him, Cast thine upper garment about thee and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:1; Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 45:26"> And going forth he followed &#91;him&#93; and did not know that what was happening by means of the angel was real, but supposed he saw a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Isaiah 45:1–2"> And having passed through a first and second guard, they came to the iron gate which leads into the city, which opened to them of itself; and going forth they went down one street, and immediately the angel left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:28; 2 Peter 2:9"> And Peter, being come to himself, said, Now I know certainly that &#91;the&#93; Lord has sent forth his angel and has taken me out of the hand of Herod and all the expectation of the people of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Acts 12:5; Matthew 18:19–20"> And having become clearly conscious &#91;in himself&#93;, he came to the house of Mary, the mother of John who was surnamed Mark, where were many gathered together and praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:16; Luke 13:25; John 18:16–17"> And when he had knocked at the door of the entry, a maid came to listen, by name Rhoda;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:41; Matthew 28:8"> and having recognised the voice of Peter, through joy did not open the entry, but running in, reported that Peter was standing before the entry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:10; Luke 24:11; Mark 16:14"> And they said to her, Thou art mad. But she maintained that it was so. And they said, It is his angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But Peter continued knocking: and having opened, they saw him and were astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 13:16; Acts 19:33"> And having made a sign to them with his hand to be silent, he related &#91;to them&#93; how the Lord had brought him out of prison; and he said, Report these things to James and to the brethren. And he went out and went to another place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:22–25; Acts 16:27; Acts 19:23"> And when it was day there was no small disturbance among the soldiers, what then was become of Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:27; Psalms 37:32–33; John 12:10–11"> And Herod having sought him and not found him, having examined the guards, commanded &#91;them&#93; to be executed. And he went down from Judaea to Caesarea and stayed &#91;there&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Matthew 11:21–22; Ezekiel 27:17"> And he was in bitter hostility with &#91;the&#93; Tyrians and Sidonians; but they came to him with one accord, and, having gained Blastus the king's chamberlain, sought peace, because their country was nourished by the king's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And on a set day, clothed in royal apparel and sitting on the elevated seat &#91;of honour&#93;, Herod made a public oration to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 14:10–13; Revelation 13:4; Jude 1:16"> And the people cried out, A<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.22 Elohim">b</a></sup>god's voice and not a man's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Acts 10:25–26; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And immediately an angel of &#91;the&#93; Lord smote him, because he did not give the glory to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.23 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, and he expired, eaten of worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 6:7; Acts 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> But the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.24 Elohim">d</a></sup>God grew and spread itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 12:12; 1 Peter 5:13; Acts 15:37"> And Barnabas and Saul returned from Jerusalem, having fulfilled the service &#91;entrusted to them&#93;, taking also with them John, surnamed Mark.</span>
