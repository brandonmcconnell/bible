<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:26; Acts 27:44; Acts 27:39"> And when we got safe &#91;to land&#93; we then knew that the island was called Melita.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Hebrews 13:2; Colossians 3:11"> But the barbarians shewed us no common kindness; for, having kindled a fire, they took us all in because of the rain that was falling and because of the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> And Paul having gathered a &#91;certain&#93; quantity of sticks together in a bundle and laid &#91;it&#93; on the fire, a viper coming out from the heat seized his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; John 7:24"> And when the barbarians saw the beast hanging from his hand, they said to one another, This man is certainly a murderer, whom, &#91;though&#93; saved out of the sea, Nemesis has not allowed to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; Psalms 91:13"> He however, having shaken off the beast into the fire, felt no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> But they expected that he would have swollen or fallen down suddenly dead. But when they had expected a long time and saw nothing unusual happen to him, changing their opinion, they said he was a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="28.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> Now in the country surrounding that place were the lands belonging to the chief man of the island, by name Publius, who received us and gave &#91;us&#93; hospitality three days in a very friendly way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; James 5:14–16"> And it happened that the father of Publius lay ill of fever and dysentery; to whom Paul entered in, and having prayed and laid his hands on him cured him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> But this having taken place, the rest also who had sicknesses in the island came and were healed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> who also honoured us with many honours, and on our leaving they made presents to us of what should minister to our wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> And after three months we sailed in a ship which had wintered in the island, an Alexandrian, with &#91;the&#93; Dioscuri for its ensign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And having come to Syracuse we remained three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> Whence, going in a circuitous course, we arrived at Rhegium; and after one day, the wind having changed to south, on the second day we came to Puteoli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> where, having found brethren, we were begged to stay with them seven days. And thus we went to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> And thence the brethren, having heard about us, came to meet us as far as Appii Forum and Tres Tabernae, whom when Paul saw, he thanked<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="28.15 Elohim">b</a></sup>God and took courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> And when we came to Rome, &#91;the centurion delivered up the prisoners to the praetorian prefect, but&#93; Paul was allowed to remain by himself with the soldier who kept him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> And it came to pass after three days, that he called together those who were the chief of the Jews; and when they had come together he said to them, Brethren, I having done nothing against the people or the customs of our forefathers, have been delivered a prisoner from Jerusalem into the hands of the Romans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> who having examined me were minded to let me go, because there was nothing worthy of death in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> But the Jews speaking against it, I was compelled to appeal to Caesar, not as having anything to accuse my nation of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> For this cause therefore I have called you to &#91;me&#93; to see and to speak to you; for on account of the hope of Israel I have this chain about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> And they said to him, For our part, we have neither received letters from Judaea concerning thee, nor has any one of the brethren who has arrived reported or said anything evil concerning thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> But we beg to hear of thee what thou thinkest, for as concerning this sect it is known to us that it is everywhere spoken against.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> And having appointed him a day many came to him to the lodging, to whom he expounded, testifying of the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="28.23 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, and persuading them concerning Jesus, both from the law of Moses and the prophets, from early morning to evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> And some were persuaded of the things which were said, but some disbelieved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> And being disagreed among themselves they left; Paul having spoken one word, Well spoke the Holy Spirit through Esaias the prophet to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> saying, Go to this people, and say, Hearing ye shall hear and not understand, and seeing ye shall see and not perceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> For the heart of this people has become fat, and they hear heavily with their ears, and they have closed their eyes; lest they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> Be it known to you therefore, that this salvation of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="28.28 Elohim">d</a></sup>God has been sent to the nations; they also will hear &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> &#91;And he having said this, the Jews went away, having great reasoning among themselves.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And he remained two whole years in his own hired lodging, and received all who came to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> preaching the kingdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="28.31 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, and teaching the things concerning the Lord Jesus Christ, with all freedom unhinderedly.</span>
