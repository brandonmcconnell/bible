<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:1; Matthew 8:5–13; Matthew 27:27"> But a certain man in Caesarea, — by name Cornelius, a centurion of the band called Italic,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Acts 10:4; Acts 10:22"> pious, and fearing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>God with all his house, &#91;both&#93; giving much alms to the people, and supplicating<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.2 Elohim">b</a></sup>God continually,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:20–21; Acts 3:1; Acts 5:19"> — saw plainly in a vision, about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.3 Elohim">c</a></sup>God coming unto him, and saying to him, Cornelius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 10:31"> But he, having fixed his eyes upon him, and become full of fear, said, What is it, Lord? And he said to him, Thy prayers and thine alms have gone up for a memorial before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.4 Elohim">d</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:9; Acts 9:38; Acts 15:7"> And now send men to Joppa and fetch Simon, who is surnamed Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:43; Acts 11:13–14; Romans 10:14–17"> He lodges with a certain Simon, a tanner, whose house is by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philemon 1:16; 1 Samuel 14:6–7; Matthew 8:9–10"> And when the angel who was speaking to him had departed, having called two of his household and a pious soldier of those who were constantly with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Psalms 119:59–60; Acts 10:33"> and related all things to them, he sent them to Joppa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:5; Psalms 55:17; 1 Samuel 9:25"> And on the morrow, as these were journeying and drawing near to the city, Peter went up on the house to pray, about the sixth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Revelation 4:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4"> And he became hungry and desired to eat. But as they were making ready an ecstasy came upon him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; John 1:51; Romans 16:25–26"> and he beholds the heaven opened, and a certain vessel descending, as a great sheet, &#91;bound&#93; by &#91;the&#93; four corners &#91;and&#93; let down to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; John 7:37; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> in which were all the quadrupeds and creeping things of the earth, and the fowls of the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:31–34; Jeremiah 35:2–5; Acts 10:10"> And there was a voice to him, Rise, Peter, slay and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Ezekiel 4:14; Daniel 1:8"> And Peter said, In no wise, Lord; for I have never eaten anything common or unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Matthew 15:11; Titus 1:15"> And &#91;there was&#93; a voice again the second time to him, What<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.15 Elohim">e</a></sup>God has cleansed, do not thou make common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:32; John 21:17; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And this took place thrice, and the vessel was straightway taken up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:12; Acts 10:7–19; Acts 5:24"> And as Peter doubted in himself what the vision which he had seen might mean, behold also the men who were sent by Cornelius, having sought out the house of Simon, stood at the gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 10:5–6; Acts 11:11"> and having called &#91;some one&#93;, they inquired if Simon who was surnamed Peter was lodged there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Acts 11:12; Acts 21:4"> But as Peter continued pondering over the vision, the Spirit said to him, Behold, three men seek thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:7–9; Zechariah 2:9–11; Isaiah 48:16"> but rise up, go down, and go with them, nothing doubting, because I have sent them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Mark 10:51; Acts 10:29"> And Peter going down to the men said, Behold, I am he whom ye seek: what is the cause for which ye come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Acts 10:2; Acts 22:12"> And they said, Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man, and fearing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.22 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, and borne witness to by the whole nation of the Jews, has been divinely instructed by a holy angel to send for thee to his house, and hear words from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 11:12; 1 Peter 4:9"> Having therefore invited them in, he lodged them. And on the morrow, rising up he went away with them, and certain of the brethren from Joppa went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Zechariah 8:20–23; Zechariah 3:10"> And on the morrow they came to Caesarea. But Cornelius was looking for them, having called together his kinsmen and &#91;his&#93; intimate friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Daniel 2:46"> And when Peter was now coming in, Cornelius met him, and falling down did &#91;him&#93; homage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 14:14–15"> But Peter made him rise, saying, Rise up: I myself also am a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; John 4:35; Acts 10:24"> And he went in, talking with him, and found many gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:9; Ephesians 3:6–7; Galatians 2:12–14"> And he said to them, Ye know how it is unlawful for a Jew to be joined or come to one of a strange race, and to me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10.28 Elohim">g</a></sup>God has shewn to call no man common or unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:19–20"> Wherefore also, having been sent for, I came without saying anything against it. I inquire therefore for what reason ye have sent for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 10:3; Daniel 9:20–21; Nehemiah 9:1–3"> And Cornelius said, Four days ago I had been &#91;fasting&#93; unto this hour, and the ninth &#91;I was&#93; praying in my house, and lo, a man stood before me in bright clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:4; Daniel 9:23; Luke 1:13"> and said, Cornelius, thy prayer has been heard, and thy alms have come in remembrance before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="10.31 Elohim">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Send therefore to Joppa and fetch Simon, who is surnamed Peter; he lodges in the house of Simon, a tanner, by the sea &#91;who when he is come will speak to thee&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 1:19; Galatians 4:14; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> Immediately therefore I sent to thee, and thou hast well done in coming. Now therefore we are all present before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="10.33 Elohim">i</a></sup>God to hear all things that are commanded thee of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="10.33 Elohim">j</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 2:11; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Deuteronomy 10:17"> And Peter opening his mouth said, Of a truth I perceive that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="10.34 Elohim">k</a></sup>God is no respecter of persons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:29; Acts 10:2; Romans 10:12–13"> but in every nation he that fears him and works righteousness is acceptable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Acts 2:36; Revelation 17:14"> The word which he sent to the sons of Israel, preaching peace by Jesus Christ, (he is Lord of all things,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 4:14; Acts 2:22; Acts 28:22"> ye know; the testimony which has spread through the whole of Judaea, beginning from Galilee after the baptism which John preached —</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:18; Acts 2:22; Isaiah 61:1–3"> Jesus who &#91;was&#93; of Nazareth: how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="10.38 Elohim">l</a></sup>God anointed him with &#91;the&#93; Holy Spirit and with power; who went through &#91;all quarters&#93; doing good, and healing all that were under the power of the devil, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="10.38 Elohim">m</a></sup>God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:8; Acts 10:41"> We also &#91;are&#93; witnesses of all things which he did both in the country of the Jews and in Jerusalem; whom they also slew, having hanged him on a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30–31; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4"> This &#91;man&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="10.40 Elohim">n</a></sup>God raised up the third day and gave him to be openly seen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 14:22; Acts 10:39; John 21:13"> not of all the people, but of witnesses who were chosen before of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="10.41 Elohim">o</a></sup>God, us who have eaten and drunk with him after he arose from among &#91;the&#93; dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Peter 4:5"> And he commanded us to preach to the people, and to testify that he it is who was determinately appointed of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="10.42 Elohim">p</a></sup>God &#91;to be&#93; judge of living and dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Acts 2:38; Isaiah 53:11"> To him all the prophets bear witness that every one that believes on him will receive through his name remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 11:15; Acts 19:6; Acts 15:8"> While Peter was yet speaking these words the Holy Spirit fell upon all those who were hearing the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 10:23; Acts 2:38; Galatians 3:13–14"> And the faithful of the circumcision were astonished, as many as came with Peter, that upon the nations also the gift of the Holy Spirit was poured out:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> for they heard them speaking with tongues and magnifying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="10.46 Elohim">q</a></sup>God. Then Peter answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 8:36; Romans 10:12; Acts 11:15–17"> Can any one forbid water that these should not be baptised, who have received the Holy Spirit as we also &#91;did&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 8:16; Galatians 3:27"> And he commanded them to be baptised in the name of the Lord. Then they begged him to stay some days.</span>
