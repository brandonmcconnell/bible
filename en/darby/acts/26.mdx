<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Proverbs 1:24; Acts 26:2"> And Agrippa said to Paul, It is permitted thee to speak for thyself. Then Paul stretching out his hand answered in his defence:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I count myself happy, king Agrippa, in having to answer to-day before thee concerning all of which I am accused by the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:14; Acts 26:26; Acts 26:7"> especially because thou art acquainted with all the customs and questions which are among the Jews; wherefore I beseech thee to hear me patiently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:13; 2 Timothy 3:10; Philippians 3:5"> My manner of life then from my youth, which from its commencement was passed among my nation in Jerusalem, know all the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 22:3; Acts 24:5"> who knew me before from the outset &#91;of my life&#93;, if they would bear witness, that according to the strictest sect of our religion I lived a Pharisee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Acts 24:15; Acts 28:20"> And now I stand to be judged because of the hope of the promise made by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="26.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:1; Philippians 3:11; 1 Timothy 5:5"> to which our whole twelve tribes serving incessantly day and night hope to arrive; about which hope, O king, I am accused of &#91;the&#93; Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12–20; Acts 4:2; Acts 17:31–32"> Why should it be judged a thing incredible in your sight if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="26.8 Elohim">b</a></sup>God raises the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Acts 22:8; Philippians 3:6"> I indeed myself thought that I ought to do much against the name of Jesus the Nazaraean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 8:3; Acts 9:13–14; Acts 9:21"> Which also I did in Jerusalem, and myself shut up in prisons many of the saints, having received the authority from the chief priests; and when they were put to death I gave my vote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 22:19; Matthew 10:17; Acts 13:45"> And often punishing them in all the synagogues, I compelled them to blaspheme. And, being exceedingly furious against them, I persecuted them even to cities out &#91;of our own land&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:10; Acts 22:5–11; Acts 9:1–8"> And when, &#91;engaged&#93; in this, I was journeying to Damascus, with authority and power from the chief priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Revelation 21:23; Revelation 1:16"> at mid-day, on the way, I saw, O king, a light above the brightness of the sun, shining from heaven round about me and those who were journeying with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 22:2; Acts 9:7"> And, when we were all fallen to the ground, I heard a voice saying to me in the Hebrew tongue, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me? &#91;it is&#93; hard for thee to kick against goads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:20–21; Matthew 25:45; Exodus 16:8"> And I said, Who art thou, Lord? And the Lord said, I am Jesus whom thou persecutest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 22:14–15; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 10:11"> but rise up and stand on thy feet; for, for this purpose have I appeared to thee, to appoint thee to be a servant and a witness both of what thou hast seen, and of what I shall appear to thee in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Jeremiah 1:19"> taking thee out from among the people, and the nations, to whom I send thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Ephesians 1:18; Ephesians 5:8"> to open their eyes, that they may turn from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="26.18 Elohim">c</a></sup>God, that they may receive remission of sins and inheritance among them that are sanctified by faith in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Galatians 1:16; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> Whereupon, king Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Acts 3:19; Acts 2:38"> but have, first to those both in Damascus and Jerusalem, and to all the region of Judaea, and to the nations, announced that they should repent and turn to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="26.20 Elohim">d</a></sup>God, doing works worthy of repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 21:30–31; Acts 21:27; Acts 25:3"> On account of these things the Jews, having seized me in the temple, attempted to lay hands on and destroy me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:14; John 5:46; Romans 3:21"> Having therefore met with &#91;the&#93; help which is from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="26.22 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, I have stood firm unto this day, witnessing both to small and great, saying nothing else than those things which both the prophets and Moses have said should happen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:26; Colossians 1:18"> &#91;namely,&#93; whether Christ should suffer; whether he first, through resurrection of &#91;the&#93; dead, should announce light both to the people and to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 4:10"> And as he answered for his defence with these things, Festus says with a loud voice, Thou art mad, Paul; much learning turns thee to madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7–8"> But Paul said, I am not mad, most excellent Festus, but utter words of truth and soberness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:2–3; Acts 2:1–12; Isaiah 30:20"> for the king is informed about these things, to whom also I speak with all freedom. For I am persuaded that of these things nothing is hidden from him; for this was not done in a corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23"> King Agrippa, believest thou the prophets? I know that thou believest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 11:26; Matthew 10:18; Acts 26:29"> And Agrippa &#91;said&#93; to Paul, In a little thou persuadest me to become a Christian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 1:28; Romans 10:1"> And Paul &#91;said&#93;, I would to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="26.29 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, both in little and in much, that not only thou, but all who have heard me this day, should become such as I also am, except these bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Acts 28:22; Acts 25:23"> And the king stood up, and the governor and Bernice, and those who sat with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 23:9; Acts 28:18"> and having gone apart, they spoke to one another saying, This man does nothing worthy of death or of bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 28:18; Acts 25:11–12; Acts 25:25"> And Agrippa said to Festus, This man might have been let go if he had not appealed to Caesar.</span>
