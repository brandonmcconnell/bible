<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:17; 2 Peter 3:7–8"> Why are not times treasured up with the Almighty? why do not they that know him see his days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 23:10"> They remove the landmarks; they violently take away the flocks and pasture them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:6; Deuteronomy 24:10–13; 1 Samuel 12:3"> They drive away the ass of the fatherless, they take the widow's ox for a pledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 28:28; Job 24:14; Ezekiel 22:29"> They turn the needy out of the way: the afflicted of the land all hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Job 39:5–7; Psalms 104:23"> Lo, &#91;as&#93; wild asses in the desert, they go forth to their work, seeking early for the prey: the wilderness &#91;yieldeth&#93; them food for &#91;their&#93; children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:51; Deuteronomy 28:33"> They reap in the field the fodder thereof, and they gather the vintage of the wicked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:26–27; Job 22:6; Deuteronomy 24:11–13"> They pass the night naked without clothing, and have no covering in the cold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Hebrews 11:38"> They are wet with the showers of the mountains, and for want of a shelter embrace the rock...</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; 2 Kings 4:1"> They pluck the fatherless from the breast, and take a pledge of the poor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 2:7–8; Deuteronomy 24:19; Amos 5:11–12"> These go naked without clothing, and, hungry, they bear the sheaf;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:4; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 25:4"> They press out oil within their walls, they tread their winepresses, and suffer thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Malachi 2:17; Romans 2:4–5"> Men groan from out of the city, and the soul of the wounded crieth out; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="24.12 Eloah">a</a></sup>God imputeth not the impiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Jude 1:6"> There are those that rebel against the light; they know not the ways thereof, nor abide in the paths thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 2:1–2; Psalms 10:8–10; Revelation 3:3"> The murderer riseth with the light, killeth the afflicted and needy, and in the night is as a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:11; Proverbs 7:9–10; Psalms 94:7"> And the eye of the adulterer waiteth for the twilight, saying, No eye shall see me; and he putteth a covering on &#91;his&#93; face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 3:20; Ephesians 5:11–13; Matthew 24:43"> In the dark they dig through houses; by day they shut themselves in; they know not the light:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 3:5; Jeremiah 2:26; Psalms 73:18–19"> For the morning is to them all &#91;as&#93; the shadow of death; for they are familiar with the terrors of the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Psalms 58:7; Job 9:26"> He is swift on the face of the waters; their portion is cursed on the earth: he turneth not unto the way of the vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:14; Luke 16:22; Proverbs 14:32"> Drought and heat consume snow waters; so doth Sheol those that have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Daniel 4:14; Job 18:16–17"> The womb forgetteth him; the worm feedeth sweetly on him: he shall be no more remembered; and unrighteousness is broken as a tree, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:6–7; Job 22:9; Job 29:13"> He that despoileth the barren that beareth not, and doeth not good to the widow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> He draweth also the mighty with his power; he riseth up, and no &#91;man&#93; is sure of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 2:23"> &#91;God&#93; setteth him in safety, and he resteth thereon; but his eyes are upon their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Psalms 37:35–36; James 5:1–3"> They are exalted for a little, and are no more; they are laid low; like all &#91;other&#93; are they gathered, and are cut off as the tops of the ears of corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:24; Job 11:2–3; Job 6:28"> If it be not so now, who will make me a liar, and make my speech nothing worth?</span>
