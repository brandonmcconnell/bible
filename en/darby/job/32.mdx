<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> I said, Let days speak, and multitude of years teach wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But there is a spirit which is in man; and the breath of the Almighty giveth them understanding.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Therefore I say, Hearken to me; I also will shew what I know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> Lo, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasonings, until ye searched out what to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> Yea, I gave you mine attention, and behold, there was none of you that confuted Job, that answered his words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> That ye may not say, We have found out wisdom;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="32.13 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God will make him yield, not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Now he hath not directed &#91;his&#93; words against me; and I will not answer him with your speeches. ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> They were amazed, they answered no more; words failed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> And I waited, for they spoke not, but stood still, and answered no more; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> I will answer, I also in my turn, I also will shew what I know:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> For I am full of matter, the spirit within me constraineth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> Behold, my belly is as wine which hath no vent; like new flasks, it is ready to burst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> I will speak, that I may find relief; I will open my lips and answer.</span>
