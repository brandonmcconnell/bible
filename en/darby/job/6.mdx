<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> Oh that my grief were thoroughly weighed, and all my calamity laid in the balances!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas; therefore my words are vehement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Almighty are within me, their poison drinketh up my spirit: the terrors of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="6.4 Eloah">a</a></sup>God are arrayed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Doth the wild ass bray by the grass? loweth an ox over his fodder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Shall that which is insipid be eaten without salt? Is there any taste in the white of an egg?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> What my soul refuseth to touch, that is as my loathsome food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> Oh that I might have my request, and that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="6.8 Eloah">b</a></sup>God would grant my desire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> And that it would please<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="6.9 Eloah">c</a></sup>God to crush me, that he would let loose his hand and cut me off!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> Then should I yet have comfort; and in the pain which spareth not I would rejoice that I have not denied the words of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> What is my strength, that I should hope? and what is mine end, that I should have patience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones? is my flesh of brass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Is it not that there is no help in me, and soundness is driven away from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> For him that is fainting kindness &#91;is meet&#93; from his friend; or he forsaketh the fear of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My brethren have dealt deceitfully as a stream, as the channel of streams which pass away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Which are turbid by reason of the ice, in which the snow hideth itself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> At the time they diminish, they are dried up; when heat affecteth them, they vanish from their place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They wind about in the paths of their course, they go off into the waste and perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> The caravans of Tema looked, the companies of Sheba counted on them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They are ashamed at their hope; they come thither, and are confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> So now ye are nothing; ye see a terrible object and are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Did I say, Bring unto me, and make me a present from your substance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> Or, rescue me from the hand of the oppressor, and redeem me from the hand of the violent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Teach me, and I will hold my tongue; and cause me to understand wherein I have erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> How forcible are right words! but what doth your upbraiding reprove?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Do ye imagine to reprove words? The speeches of one that is desperate are indeed for the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Yea, ye overwhelm the fatherless, and dig &#91;a pit&#93; for your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> Now therefore if ye will, look upon me; and it shall be to your face if I lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Return, I pray you, let there be no wrong; yea, return again, my righteousness shall be in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> Is there wrong in my tongue? cannot my taste discern mischievous things?</span>
