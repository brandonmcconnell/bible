<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> He sendeth it forth under the whole heaven, and his lightning unto the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> After it a voice roareth: he thundereth with the voice of his excellency, and holdeth not back the flashes when his voice is heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="37.5 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God thundereth marvellously with his voice, doing great things which we do not comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For he saith to the snow, Fall on the earth! and to the pouring rain, even the pouring rains of his might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> He sealeth up the hand of every man; that all men may know his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> And the wild beast goeth into its lair, and they remain in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> From the chamber &#91;of the south&#93; cometh the whirlwind; and cold from the winds of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> By the breath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="37.10 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God ice is given; and the breadth of the waters is straitened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> Also with plentiful moisture he loadeth the thick clouds, his light dispels the cloud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And they are turned every way by his guidance, that they may do whatsoever he commandeth them upon the face of the circuit of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> Whether he cause it to come as a rod, or for his land, or in mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Hearken unto this, Job; stand still and discern the wondrous works of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="37.14 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Dost thou know how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="37.15 Eloah">d</a></sup>God hath disposed them, and how he causeth the lightning of his cloud to flash?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Dost thou know about the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him that is perfect in knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> How thy garments become warm when he quieteth the earth by the south wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Hast thou with him spread out the sky, firm, like a molten mirror?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Teach us what we shall say unto him! We cannot order &#91;our words&#93; by reason of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> Shall it be told him if I would speak? if a man &#91;so&#93; say, surely he shall be swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> And now &#91;men&#93; see not the light as it gleameth, it is &#91;hidden&#93; in the skies. But the wind passeth by and cleareth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> From the north cometh gold; with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="37.22 Eloah">e</a></sup>God is terrible majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> The Almighty, we cannot find him out: excellent in power, and in judgment, and in abundance of justice, he doth not afflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> Men do therefore fear him: he respecteth not any that are wise of heart.</span>
