<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Let the day perish in which I was born, and the night that said, There is a man child conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> That day — let it be darkness, let not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.4 Eloah">a</a></sup>God care for it from above, neither let light shine upon it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> Let darkness and the shadow of death claim it; let clouds dwell upon it; let darkeners of the day terrify it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> That night — let gloom seize upon it; let it not rejoice among the days of the year; let it not come into the number of the months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> Behold, let that night be barren; let no joyful sound come therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> Let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to rouse Leviathan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let the stars of its twilight be dark; let it wait for light, and have none, neither let it see the eyelids of the dawn:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> Because it shut not up the doors of the womb that bore me, and hid not trouble from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> Wherefore did I not die from the womb, — come forth from the belly and expire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> Why did the knees meet me? and wherefore the breasts, that I should suck?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> For now should I have lain down and been quiet; I should have slept: then had I been at rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> With kings and counsellors of the earth, who build desolate places for themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Or with princes who had gold, who filled their houses with silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> Or as a hidden untimely birth I had not been; as infants that have not seen the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> There the wicked cease from troubling; and there the wearied are at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> The prisoners together are at ease; they hear not the voice of the taskmaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> The small and great are there, and the bondman freed from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Wherefore is light given to him that is in trouble, and life to those bitter of soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> Who long for death, and it &#91;cometh&#93; not, and dig for it more than for hidden treasures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Who rejoice even exultingly and are glad when they find the grave? —</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> To the man whose way is hidden, and whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.23 Eloah">b</a></sup>God hath hedged in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> For my sighing cometh before my bread, and my groanings are poured out like the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> For I feared a fear, and it hath come upon me, and that which I dreaded hath come to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I was not in safety, neither had I quietness, neither was I at rest, and trouble came.</span>
