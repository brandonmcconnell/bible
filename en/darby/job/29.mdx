<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> When his lamp shone over my head, &#91;and&#93; by his light I walked through darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I was in the days of my youth, when the secret counsel of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="29.4 Eloah">b</a></sup>God was over my tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When the Almighty was yet with me, my young men round about me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When my steps were bathed in milk, and the rock poured out beside me rivers of oil! ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I went out to the gate by the city, when I prepared my seat on the broadway,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The young men saw me, and hid themselves; and the aged arose &#91;and&#93; stood up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> Princes refrained from talking, and laid the hand on their mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The voice of the nobles was hushed, and their tongue cleaved to their palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> When the ear heard &#91;me&#93;, then it blessed me, and when the eye saw &#91;me&#93;, it gave witness to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> For I delivered the afflicted that cried, and the fatherless who had no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of him that was perishing came upon me, and I caused the widow's heart to sing for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; my justice was as a mantle and a turban.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was eyes to the blind, and feet was I to the lame;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was a father to the needy, and the cause which I knew not I searched out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> And I broke the jaws of the unrighteous, and plucked the spoil out of his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> And I said, I shall die in my nest, and multiply my days as the sand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root shall be spread out to the waters, and the dew will lie all night on my branch;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory shall be fresh in me, and my bow be renewed in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> Unto me they listened, and waited, and kept silence for my counsel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> After my words they spoke not again, and my speech dropped upon them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> And they waited for me as for the rain, and they opened their mouth wide as for the latter rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> &#91;If&#93; I laughed on them, they believed &#91;it&#93; not; and they troubled not the serenity of my countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I chose their way, and sat as chief, and dwelt as a king in the army, as one that comforteth mourners.</span>
