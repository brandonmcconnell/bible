<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered Jehovah and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> I know that thou canst do everything, and that thou canst be hindered in no thought of thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 131:1; Psalms 139:6; Job 38:2"> Who is he that obscureth counsel without knowledge? therefore have I uttered what I did not understand; things too wonderful for me, which I knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> Hear, I beseech thee, and I will speak: I will demand of thee, and inform me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> I had heard of thee by the hearing of the ear, but now mine eye seeth thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; James 4:7–10"> Wherefore I abhor &#91;myself&#93;, and repent in dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> And it came to pass after Jehovah had spoken these words to Job, that Jehovah said to Eliphaz the Temanite, Mine anger is kindled against thee, and against thy two friends; for ye have not spoken rightly of me, like my servant Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalms 103:10"> And now, take for yourselves seven bullocks and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer up for yourselves a burnt-offering; and my servant Job shall pray for you, for him will I accept: lest I deal with you &#91;after your&#93; folly, for ye have not spoken of me rightly, like my servant Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite, and Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite, went and did according as Jehovah had said unto them; and Jehovah accepted Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> And Jehovah turned the captivity of Job, when he had prayed for his friends; and Jehovah gave Job twice as much as he had before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> And all his brethren, and all his sisters, and all they that had been of his acquaintance before, came to him, and they ate bread with him in his house, and they condoled with him, and comforted him concerning all the evil that Jehovah had brought upon him; and every one gave him a piece of money, and every one a golden ring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> And Jehovah blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning; and he had fourteen thousand sheep, and six thousand camels, and a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand she-asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3"> And he had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he called the name of the first, Jemimah; and the name of the second, Keziah; and the name of the third, Keren-happuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalms 144:12; Acts 7:20"> And in all the land were no women found &#91;so&#93; fair as the daughters of Job; and their father gave them inheritance among their brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalms 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> And Job lived after this a hundred and forty years, and saw his sons, and his sons' sons, four generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> And Job died, old and full of days.</span>
