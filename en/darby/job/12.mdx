<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> Truly ye are the people, and wisdom shall die with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> I also have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you; and who knoweth not such things as these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> I am to be one that is a derision to his friend, I who call upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.4 Eloah">a</a></sup>God, and whom he will answer: a derision is the just upright &#91;man&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> He that is ready to stumble with the foot is a lamp despised in the thought of him that is at ease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> The tents of desolators are in peace, and they that provoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.6 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God are secure; into whose hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.6 Eloah">c</a></sup>God bringeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee; and the fowl of the heavens, and they shall tell thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Or speak to the earth, and it shall teach thee; and the fishes of the sea shall declare unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who knoweth not in all these, that the hand of Jehovah hath wrought this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the spirit of all flesh of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> Doth not the ear try words, as the palate tasteth food?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> With the aged is wisdom, and in length of days understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> With him is wisdom and might; he hath counsel and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> Behold, he breaketh down, and it is not built again; he shutteth up a man, and there is no opening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> Behold, he withholdeth the waters, and they dry up; and he sendeth them out, and they overturn the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> With him is strength and effectual knowledge; the deceived and the deceiver are his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He leadeth counsellors away spoiled, and judges maketh he fools;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He weakeneth the government of kings, and bindeth their loins with a fetter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He leadeth priests away spoiled, and overthroweth the mighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He depriveth of speech the trusty, and taketh away the judgment of the elders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He poureth contempt upon nobles, and slackeneth the girdle of the mighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth out into light the shadow of death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> He increaseth the nations, and destroyeth them; he spreadeth out the nations, and bringeth them in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He taketh away the understanding of the chiefs of the people of the earth, and causeth them to wander in a pathless waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They grope in the dark without light, and he maketh them to stagger like a drunkard.</span>
