<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Job 14:5–6; Job 5:7"> Hath not man a life of labour upon earth? and are not his days like the days of a hireling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; James 5:4"> As a bondman earnestly desireth the shadow, and a hireling expecteth his wages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Psalms 39:5"> So am I made to possess months of vanity, and wearisome nights are appointed to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:67; Job 7:13–14; Job 17:12"> If I lie down, I say, When shall I rise up, and the darkness be gone? and I am full of tossings until the dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:14; Isaiah 14:11; Job 2:7–8"> My flesh is clothed with worms and clods of dust; my skin is broken, and suppurates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:25; Job 17:15; Job 17:11"> My days are swifter than a weaver's shuttle, and are spent without hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; Jeremiah 15:15"> Remember thou that my life is wind; mine eye shall no more see good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 20:9; Job 8:18; Psalms 37:36"> The eye of him that hath seen me shall behold me no &#91;more&#93;: thine eyes are upon me, and I am not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 30:15; Job 10:21; 2 Samuel 14:14"> The cloud consumeth and vanisheth away; so he that goeth down to Sheol shall not come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:18; Job 20:9; Psalms 103:16"> He shall return no more to his house, neither shall his place know him again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 40:9; 1 Samuel 1:10; Job 10:1"> Therefore I will not restrain my mouth: I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2–3; Lamentations 3:7; Job 38:6–11"> Am I a sea, or a sea-monster, that thou settest a watch over me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 77:4; Job 9:27–28"> When I say, My bed shall comfort me, my couch shall ease my complaint;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Genesis 41:8; Genesis 40:5–7"> Then thou scarest me with dreams, and terrifiest me through visions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; 2 Samuel 17:23"> So that my soul chooseth strangling, death, rather than my bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Job 10:1; Job 9:21"> I loathe it; I shall not live always: let me alone, for my days are a breath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:6; Psalms 8:4; Psalms 144:3"> What is man, that thou makest much of him? and that thou settest thy heart upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:12–13; Jeremiah 9:7; 1 Peter 1:7"> And that thou visitest him every morning, triest him every moment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:18; Job 14:6; Psalms 13:1–3"> How long wilt thou not look away from me, nor let me alone till I swallow down my spittle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 3:12; Job 7:11–12; Job 3:24"> Have I sinned, what do I unto thee, thou Observer of men? Why hast thou set me as an object of assault for thee, so that I am become a burden to myself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Job 10:14; 1 John 1:9"> And why dost not thou forgive my transgression and take away mine iniquity? for now shall I lie down in the dust, and thou shalt seek me early, and I shall not be.</span>
