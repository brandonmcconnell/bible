<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> How long will ye vex my soul, and crush me with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> These ten times have ye reproached me; ye are not ashamed to stupefy me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> And be it &#91;that&#93; I have erred, mine error remaineth with myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> If indeed ye will magnify yourselves against me, and prove against me my reproach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> Know now that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.6 Eloah">a</a></sup>God hath overthrown me, and hath surrounded me with his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Behold, I cry out of wrong, and I am not heard; I cry aloud, but there is no judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> He hath hedged up my way that I cannot pass, and he hath set darkness in my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> He hath stripped me of my glory, and taken the crown from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He breaketh me down on every side, and I am gone; and my hope hath he torn up as a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> And he hath kindled his anger against me, and hath counted me unto him as one of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> His troops have come together and cast up their way against me, and have encamped round about my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> He hath put my brethren far from me, and mine acquaintance are quite estranged from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My kinsfolk have failed, and my known friends have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> The sojourners in my house and my maids count me as a stranger; I am an alien in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I called my servant, and he answered not; I entreated him with my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My breath is strange to my wife, and my entreaties to the children of my &#91;mother's&#93; womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Even young children despise me; I rise up, and they speak against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> All my intimate friends abhor me, and they whom I loved are turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> My bones cleave to my skin and to my flesh, and I am escaped with the skin of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Have pity upon me, have pity upon me, ye my friends; for the hand of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.21 Eloah">b</a></sup>God hath touched me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why do ye persecute me as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="19.22 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and are not satisfied with my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> Oh would that my words were written! oh that they were inscribed in a book!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> That with an iron style and lead they were graven in the rock for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> And &#91;as for&#93; me, I know that my Redeemer liveth, and the Last, he shall stand upon the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> And &#91;if&#93; after my skin this shall be destroyed, yet from out of my flesh shall I see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="19.26 Eloah">d</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> Whom I shall see for myself, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another: — my reins are consumed within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> If ye say, How shall we persecute him? when the root of the matter is found in me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Be ye yourselves afraid of the sword! for the sword is fury against misdeeds, that ye may know there is a judgment.</span>
