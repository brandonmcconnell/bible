<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> Can a man be profitable to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="22.2 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God? surely it is unto himself that the wise man is profitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it any pleasure to the Almighty if thou art righteous? And is it gain &#91;to him&#93; that thou makest thy ways perfect?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Will he reason with thee for fear of thee? Will he enter with thee into judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not thy wickedness great? and thine iniquities without end?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For thou hast taken a pledge of thy brother for nought, and stripped off the clothing of the naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> Thou hast not given water to the fainting to drink, and thou hast withholden bread from the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> But the powerful man, he had the land; and the man of high rank dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> Widows hast thou sent empty away, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore snares are round about thee, and sudden fear troubleth thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Or darkness, that thou canst not see, and floods of waters cover thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="22.12 Eloah">b</a></sup>God in the height of the heavens? And behold the summit of the stars: how exalted are they!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> And thou sayest, What doth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="22.13 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God know? will he judge through the dark cloud?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> Thick clouds are a covering to him, that he seeth not; and he walketh on the vault of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Dost thou mark the ancient path which wicked men have trodden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who were carried off before the time, whose foundation was overflowed with a flood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Who said unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="22.17 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God, Depart from us! and what could the Almighty do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> Yet he filled their houses with good. But the counsel of the wicked is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The righteous see it, and are glad; and the innocent laugh them to scorn:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> Is not he who rose against us destroyed, and doth not the fire consume his residue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Reconcile thyself now with him, and be at peace: thereby good shall come unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Receive, I pray thee, instruction from his mouth, and lay up his words in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up. If thou remove unrighteousness far from thy tents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> And put the precious ore with the dust, and &#91;the gold of&#93; Ophir among the stones of the torrents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> Then the Almighty will be thy precious ore, and silver heaped up unto thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> Yea, then shalt thou delight thyself in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="22.26 Eloah">e</a></sup>God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> Thou shalt make thy prayer unto him, and he will hear thee, and thou shalt pay thy vows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> And thou shalt decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee; and light shall shine upon thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> When they are made low, then thou shalt say, Rise up! and he shall save him that is of downcast eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> &#91;Even&#93; him that is not innocent shall he deliver; yea, he shall be delivered by the pureness of thy hands.</span>
