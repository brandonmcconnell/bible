<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:11; Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20"> There was a man in the land of Uz whose name was Job; and this man was perfect and upright, and one that feared<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God and abstained from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Job 42:13; Psalms 128:3"> And there were born to him seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 29:25; Job 42:12; Judges 6:3"> And his substance was seven thousand sheep, and three thousand camels, and five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred she-asses, and very many servants; and this man was greater than all the children of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Psalms 133:1"> And his sons went and made a feast in the house of each one on his day; and they sent and invited their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 42:8; Genesis 8:20; 1 Samuel 16:5"> And it was so, when the days of the feasting were gone about, that Job sent and hallowed them; and he rose up early in the morning, and offered up burnt-offerings &#91;according to&#93; the number of them all; for Job said, It may be that my children have sinned, and cursed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God in their hearts. Thus did Job continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 38:7; Zechariah 3:1"> And there was a day when the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God came to present themselves before Jehovah; and Satan came also among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:12–17; Matthew 12:43"> And Jehovah said to Satan, Whence comest thou? And Satan answered Jehovah and said, From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:3; Job 1:1; Numbers 12:7–8"> And Jehovah said to Satan, Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him on the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God and abstaineth from evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 4:8; Job 2:10"> And Satan answered Jehovah and said, Doth Job fear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="1.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God for nought?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 2:5; 1 Peter 1:5"> Hast not thou made a hedge about him, and about his house, and about all that he hath on every side? Thou hast blessed the work of his hands, and his substance is spread abroad in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 19:21; Revelation 16:11"> But put forth thy hand now and touch all that he hath, &#91;and see&#93; if he will not curse thee to thy face!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:31–32; John 3:35–36; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> And Jehovah said to Satan, Behold, all that he hath is in thy hand; only upon himself put not forth thy hand. So Satan went forth from the presence of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Proverbs 27:1; Job 1:4"> And there was a day when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother, the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:13; Jeremiah 51:31; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And there came a messenger to Job and said, The oxen were ploughing, and the asses feeding beside them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Job 6:19; Isaiah 45:14"> and &#91;they of&#93; Sheba fell &#91;upon them&#93; and took them, and the servants have they smitten with the edge of the sword; and I only am escaped, alone, to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:12; Genesis 19:24; 1 Kings 18:38"> While he was yet speaking, there came another and said, The fire of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God fell from heaven and burned up the sheep and the servants, and consumed them; and I only am escaped, alone, to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 11:28; Habakkuk 1:6; Genesis 11:31"> While he was yet speaking, there came another and said, The Chaldeans made three bands, and fell upon the camels and took them, and the servants have they smitten with the edge of the sword; and I only am escaped, alone, to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 1:4; Job 1:13; Job 27:14"> While he was yet speaking, there came another and said, Thy sons and thy daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother, the firstborn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:36; Luke 13:1–5; Judges 16:30"> and behold, there came a great wind from over the wilderness, and smote the four corners of the house, and it fell upon the young men, and they died; and I only am escaped, alone, to tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 2 Samuel 12:16–20; Genesis 37:34"> And Job rose up, and rent his mantle, and shaved his head, and fell down on the ground, and worshipped;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:15; 1 Timothy 6:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> and he said, Naked came I out of my mother's womb, and naked shall I return thither: Jehovah gave, and Jehovah hath taken away; blessed be the name of Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 2:10; 1 Peter 1:7"> In all this Job sinned not, nor ascribed anything unseemly to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="1.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God.</span>
