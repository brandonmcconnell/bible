<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> Of a truth I know it is so; but how can man be just with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9.2 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If he shall choose to strive with him, he cannot answer him one thing of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> He is wise in heart and mighty in strength: who hath hardened himself against him, and had peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> Who removeth mountains, and they know it not, when he overturneth them in his anger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Who shaketh the earth out of its place, and the pillars thereof tremble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Who commandeth the sun, and it riseth not, and he sealeth up the stars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Who alone spreadeth out the heavens, and treadeth upon the high waves of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Who maketh the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the chambers of the south;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Who doeth great things past finding out, and wonders without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> Lo, he goeth by me, and I see &#91;him&#93; not; and he passeth along, and I perceive him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> Behold, he taketh away: who will hinder him? Who will say unto him, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="9.13 Eloah">b</a></sup>God withdraweth not his anger; the proud helpers stoop under him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How much less shall I answer him, choose out my words &#91;to strive&#93; with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Whom, though I were righteous, &#91;yet&#93; would I not answer; I would make supplication to my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> If I had called, and he had answered me, I would not believe that he hearkened to my voice, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> He, who crusheth me with a tempest, and multiplieth my wounds without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> He suffereth me not to take my breath, for he filleth me with bitternesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> Be it a question of strength, lo, &#91;he is&#93; strong; and be it of judgment, who will set me a time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> If I justified myself, mine own mouth would condemn me; were I perfect, he would prove me perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> Were I perfect, &#91;yet&#93; would I not know my soul: I would despise my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> It is all one; therefore I said, he destroyeth the perfect and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> If the scourge kill suddenly, he mocketh at the trial of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> The earth is given over into the hand of the wicked &#91;man&#93;; he covereth the faces of its judges. If not, who then is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> And my days are swifter than a runner: they flee away, they see no good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They pass by like skiffs of reed; as an eagle that swoops upon the prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I say, I will forget my complaint, I will leave off my &#91;sad&#93; countenance, and brighten up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I am afraid of all my sorrows; I know that thou wilt not hold me innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> Be it that I am wicked, why then do I labour in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I washed myself with snow-water, and cleansed my hands in purity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Then wouldest thou plunge me in the ditch, and mine own clothes would abhor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For he is not a man, as I am, that I should answer him; that we should come together in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> There is not an umpire between us, who should lay his hand upon us both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let him take his rod away from me, and let not his terror make me afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> &#91;Then&#93; I will speak, and not fear him; but it is not so with me.</span>
