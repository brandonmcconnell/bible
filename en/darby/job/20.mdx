<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> And Zophar the Naamathite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> Therefore do my thoughts give me an answer, and for this is my haste within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> I hear a reproof putting me to shame; and &#91;my&#93; spirit answereth me by mine understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> Knowest thou &#91;not&#93; this, that of old, since man was placed upon earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> The exultation of the wicked is short, and the joy of the ungodly man but for a moment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–14; Obadiah 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> Though his height mount up to the heavens, and his head reach unto the clouds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; 1 Kings 14:10; Job 4:20"> Like his own dung doth he perish for ever; they which have seen him shall say, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> He flieth away as a dream, and is not found; and is chased away as a vision of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> The eye which saw him shall &#91;see him&#93; not again; and his place beholdeth him no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> His children shall seek the favour of the poor, and his hands restore his wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> His bones were full of his youthful strength; but it shall lie down with him in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> Though wickedness be sweet in his mouth &#91;and&#93; he hide it under his tongue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> &#91;Though&#93; he spare it, and forsake it not, but keep it within his mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> His food is turned in his bowels; it is the gall of asps within him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> He hath swallowed down riches, but he shall vomit them up again:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20.15 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God shall cast them out of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> He shall suck the poison of asps; the viper's tongue shall kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> He shall not see streams, rivers, brooks of honey and butter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremiah 51:44"> That which he laboured for shall he restore, and not swallow down; its restitution shall be according to the value, and he shall not rejoice &#91;therein&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> For he hath oppressed, hath forsaken the poor; he hath violently taken away a house that he did not build.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Isaiah 57:20–21"> Because he knew no rest in his craving, he shall save nought of what he most desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> Nothing escaped his greediness; therefore his prosperity shall not endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; 2 Kings 24:2"> In the fulness of his sufficiency he shall be in straits; every hand of the wretched shall come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> It shall be that, to fill his belly, he will cast his fierce anger upon him, and will rain it upon him into his flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 24:18; Amos 5:19; 2 Samuel 22:35"> If he have fled from the iron weapon, the bow of brass shall strike him through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> He draweth it forth; it cometh out of his body, and the glittering point out of his gall: terrors are upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Isaiah 30:33"> All darkness is laid up for his treasures: a fire not blown shall devour him; it shall feed upon what is left in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> The heavens shall reveal his iniquity, and the earth shall rise up against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> The increase of his house shall depart, flowing away in the day of his anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> This is the portion of the wicked man from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and the heritage appointed to him by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="20.29 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
