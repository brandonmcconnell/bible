<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elihu proceeded and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Suffer me a little, and I will shew thee that I have yet words for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="36.2 Eloah">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will fetch my knowledge from afar, and will ascribe righteousness to my Creator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> For truly my words shall be no falsehood: one perfect in knowledge is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> Lo,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="36.5 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God is mighty, but despiseth not &#91;any&#93;; mighty in strength of understanding:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He saveth not the wicked alive; but he doeth justice to the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous, but with kings on the throne doth he even set them for ever; and they are exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> And if, bound in fetters, they be held in cords of affliction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Then he sheweth them their work, and their transgressions, because they have increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> And he openeth their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they hearken and serve &#91;him&#93;, they shall accomplish their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> But if they hearken not, they shall pass away by the sword, and expire without knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> But the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="36.13 Elohim">c</a></sup>godless in heart heap up anger; they cry not when he bindeth them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> Their soul dieth in youth, and their life is among the unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> But he delivereth the afflicted in his affliction, and openeth their ear in &#91;their&#93; oppression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> Even so would he have allured thee out of the jaws of distress into a broad place, where there is no straitness; and the supply of thy table &#91;would be&#93; full of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> But thou art full of the judgments of the wicked: judgment and justice take hold &#91;on thee&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> Because there is wrath, &#91;beware&#93; lest it take thee away through chastisement: then a great ransom could not avail thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Will he esteem thy riches? Not gold, nor all the resources of strength!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Desire not the night, when peoples are cut off from their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Take heed, turn not to iniquity; for this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Lo,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="36.22 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God is exalted in his power: who teacheth as he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who hath appointed him his way? or who hath said, Thou hast wrought unrighteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Remember that thou magnify his work, which men celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> All men look at it; man beholdeth &#91;it&#93; afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Lo,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="36.26 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God is great, and we comprehend &#91;him&#93; not, neither can the number of his years be searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> For he draweth up the drops of water: they distil in rain from the vapour which he formeth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> Which the skies pour down &#91;and&#93; drop upon man abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> But can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, &#91;or&#93; the crashing of his pavilion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> Lo, he spreadeth his light around him, and covereth the bottom of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> For with them he judgeth the peoples; he giveth food in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> &#91;His&#93; hands he covereth with lightning, and commandeth it where it is to strike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> His thundering declareth concerning him; the cattle even, concerning its coming.</span>
