<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 15:15; Job 15:8–10; Job 4:18"> Call, I pray thee! Is there any that answereth thee? and to which of the holy ones wilt thou turn?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Hosea 7:11; Genesis 30:1"> For vexation killeth the foolish man, and envy slayeth the simple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 92:7"> I myself saw the foolish taking root, but suddenly I cursed his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:5; Job 18:16–19; Psalms 119:155"> His children are far from safety, and they are crushed in the gate, and there is no deliverer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Hosea 8:7; Jeremiah 51:44"> Whose harvest the hungry eateth up, and taketh even out of the thorns; and the snare gapeth for his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; 1 Samuel 6:9"> For evil cometh not forth from the dust, neither doth trouble spring out of the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> For man is born to trouble, as the sparks fly upwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Job 22:21; Psalms 37:5"> But as for me I will seek unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.8 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God commit my cause;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Job 9:10; Psalms 86:10"> Who doeth great things and unsearchable, marvellous things without number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:24; Acts 14:17; Psalms 147:8"> Who giveth rain on the face of the earth, and sendeth waters on the face of the fields;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> Setting up on high those that are low; and mourners are exalted to prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:10–11; Psalms 21:11; Isaiah 8:10"> He disappointeth the devices of the crafty, and their hands carry not out the enterprise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; Psalms 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> He taketh the wise in their own craftiness; and the counsel of the wily is carried headlong:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> They meet with darkness in the daytime, and grope at midday as in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 140:12; Psalms 109:31"> And he saveth the needy from the sword, from their mouth, and from the hand of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 107:42; 1 Samuel 2:8–9; Psalms 63:11"> So the poor hath what he hopeth for, and unrighteousness stoppeth her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Behold, happy is the man whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.17 Eloah">c</a></sup>God correcteth; therefore despise not the chastening of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 30:26"> For he maketh sore, and bindeth up; he woundeth, and his hands make whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> He will deliver thee in six troubles, and in seven there shall no evil touch thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Hosea 13:14; Psalms 37:19"> In famine he will redeem thee from death, and in war from the power of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:20; Psalms 91:5–7; Jeremiah 18:18"> Thou shalt be hidden from the scourge of the tongue; and thou shalt not be afraid of destruction when it cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Isaiah 35:9; Psalms 91:13"> At destruction and famine thou shalt laugh, and of the beasts of the earth thou shalt not be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Hosea 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> For thou shalt be in league with the stones of the field, and the beasts of the field shall be at peace with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 121:7–8; Psalms 91:10; Isaiah 4:5–6"> And thou shalt know that thy tent is in peace; and thou wilt survey thy fold, and miss nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> And thou shalt know that thy seed is numerous, and thine offspring as the herb of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; Psalms 91:16"> Thou shalt come to the grave in a ripe age, as a shock of corn is brought in in its season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> Behold this, we have searched it out, so it is; hear it, and know thou it for thyself.</span>
