<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> Then he will be gracious unto him, and say, Deliver him from going down to the pit: I have found a ransom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 103:5; 2 Kings 5:14; Joshua 14:10–11"> His flesh shall be fresher than in childhood; he shall return to the days of his youth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 14:2"> He will sing before men, and say, I have sinned, and perverted what was right, and it hath not been requited to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:24; Job 3:16; Job 33:22"> He hath delivered my soul from going into the pit, and my life shall see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:11; 1 Corinthians 12:6; Philippians 2:13"> Lo, all these &#91;things&#93; worketh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="33.29 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">f</a></sup>God twice, thrice, with man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Isaiah 38:17; Job 33:28"> To bring back his soul from the pit, that he may be enlightened with the light of the living.</span>
