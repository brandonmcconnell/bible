<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; 1 Samuel 24:2; Psalms 104:18"> Knowest thou the time when the wild goats of the rock bring forth? dost thou mark the calving of the hinds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24"> Dost thou number the months that they fulfil? and knowest thou the time when they bring forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They bow themselves, they give birth to their young ones, they cast out their pains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their young ones become strong, they grow up in the open field, they go forth, and return not unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who hath sent out the wild ass free? and who hath loosed the bands of the onager,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Whose house I made the wilderness, and the salt plain his dwellings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He laugheth at the tumult of the city, and heareth not the shouts of the driver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> The range of the mountains is his pasture, and he searcheth after every green thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Will the buffalo be willing to serve thee, or will he lodge by thy crib?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Canst thou bind the buffalo with his cord in the furrow? or will he harrow the valleys after thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Wilt thou put confidence in him, because his strength is great? and wilt thou leave thy labour to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Wilt thou trust him to bring home thy seed, and gather it into thy threshing-floor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> The wing of the ostrich beats joyously — But is it the stork's pinion and plumage?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For she leaveth her eggs to the earth, and warmeth them in the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And forgetteth that the foot may crush them, or that the beast of the field may trample them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> She is hardened against her young ones, as though they were not hers; her labour is in vain, without her concern.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="39.17 Eloah">a</a></sup>God hath deprived her of wisdom, and hath not furnished her with understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> What time she lasheth herself on high, she scorneth the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Hast thou given strength to the horse? hast thou clothed his neck with the quivering mane?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Dost thou make him to leap as a locust? His majestic snorting is terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> He paweth in the valley, and rejoiceth in &#91;his&#93; strength; he goeth forth to meet the armed host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He laugheth at fear, and is not affrighted; neither turneth he back from before the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> The quiver rattleth upon him, the glittering spear and the javelin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> He swalloweth the ground with fierceness and rage, and cannot contain himself at the sound of the trumpet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> At the noise of the trumpets he saith, Aha! and he smelleth the battle afar off, the thunder of the captains, and the shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> Doth the hawk fly by thine intelligence, &#91;and&#93; stretch his wings toward the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> Doth the eagle mount up at thy command, and make his nest on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> He inhabiteth the rock and maketh his dwelling on the point of the cliff, and the fastness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From thence he spieth out the prey, his eyes look into the distance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> And his young ones suck up blood; and where the slain are, there is he.</span>
