<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 43:7; Job 10:3"> Thy hands have bound me together and made me as one, round about; yet dost thou swallow me up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; Genesis 3:19"> Remember, I beseech thee, that thou hast made me as clay, and wilt bring me into dust again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 139:14–16"> Hast thou not poured me out as milk, and curdled me like cheese?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4–8; Ephesians 4:16; Job 40:17–18"> Thou hast clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and sinews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> Thou hast granted me life and favour, and thy care hath preserved my spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 23:13; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 45:15"> And these things didst thou hide in thy heart; I know that this was with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; Psalms 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> If I sinned, thou wouldest mark me, and thou wouldest not acquit me of mine iniquity.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> And it increaseth: thou huntest me as a fierce lion; and ever again thou shewest thy marvellous power upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> Thou renewest thy witnesses before me and increasest thy displeasure against me; successions &#91;of evil&#93; and a time of toil are with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 11:20"> And wherefore didst thou bring me forth out of the womb? I had expired, and no eye had seen me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:8"> I should be as though I had not been; I should have been carried from the womb to the grave.</span>
