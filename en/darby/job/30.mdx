<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now they that are younger than I have me in derision, whose fathers I would have disdained to set with the dogs of my flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Yea, whereto &#91;should&#93; the strength of their hands &#91;profit&#93; me, &#91;men&#93; in whom vigour hath perished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> Withered up through want and hunger, they flee into waste places long since desolate and desert:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> They gather the salt-wort among the bushes, and the roots of the broom for their food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> They are driven forth from among &#91;men&#93; — they cry after them as after a thief —</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> To dwell in gloomy gorges, in caves of the earth and the rocks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They bray among the bushes; under the brambles they are gathered together:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> Sons of fools, and sons of nameless sires, they are driven out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now I am their song, yea, I am their byword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They abhor me, they stand aloof from me, yea, they spare not to spit in my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> For he hath loosed my cord and afflicted me; so they cast off the bridle before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> At &#91;my&#93; right hand rise the young brood; they push away my feet, and raise up against me their pernicious ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They mar my path, they set forward my calamity, without any to help them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> They come in as through a wide breach: amid the confusion they roll themselves onward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Terrors are turned against me; they pursue mine honour as the wind; and my welfare is passed away like a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my soul is poured out in me; days of affliction have taken hold upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> The night pierceth through my bones &#91;and detacheth them&#93; from me, and my gnawing pains take no rest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> By their great force they have become my raiment; they bind me about as the collar of my coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He hath cast me into the mire, and I have become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I cry unto thee, and thou answerest me not; I stand up, and thou lookest at me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou art changed to a cruel one to me; with the strength of thy hand thou pursuest me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou liftest me up to the wind; thou causest me to be borne away, and dissolvest my substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> For I know that thou wilt bring me to death, and into the house of assemblage for all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> Indeed, no prayer &#91;availeth&#93; when he stretcheth out &#91;his&#93; hand: though they cry when he destroyeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did not I weep for him whose days were hard? was not my soul grieved for the needy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> For I expected good, and there came evil; and I waited for light, but there came darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels well up, and rest not; days of affliction have confronted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I go about blackened, but not by the sun; I stand up, I cry in the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I am become a brother to jackals, and a companion of ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin is become black &#91;and falleth&#93; off me, and my bones are parched with heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> My harp also is &#91;turned&#93; to mourning, and my pipe into the voice of weepers.</span>
