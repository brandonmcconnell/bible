<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover Elihu answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Hear my words, ye wise &#91;men&#93;; and give ear unto me, ye that have knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For the ear trieth words, as the palate tasteth food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us choose for ourselves what is right; let us know among ourselves what is good!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job hath said, I am righteous, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="34.5 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath taken away my judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> Should I lie against my right? My wound is incurable without transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> What man is like Job? he drinketh up scorning like water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> And goeth in company with workers of iniquity, and walketh with wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachi 3:14"> For he hath said, It profiteth not a man if he delight himself in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="34.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Therefore hearken unto me, ye men of understanding: Far be wickedness from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="34.10 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and wrong from the Almighty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> For a man's work will he render to him, and cause every one to find according to &#91;his&#93; way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:17"> Yea, surely,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="34.12 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God acteth not wickedly, and the Almighty perverteth not judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> Who hath entrusted to him the earth? and who hath disposed the whole world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> If he only thought of himself, &#91;and&#93; gathered unto him his spirit and his breath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Isaiah 57:16"> All flesh would expire together, and man would return to the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> If now &#91;thou hast&#93; understanding, hear this: give ear to the voice of my words!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; 2 Samuel 23:3"> Should he that hateth right indeed govern? and wilt thou condemn the All-just?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> Shall one say to a king, Belial? to nobles, Wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> &#91;How then to him&#93; that accepteth not the persons of princes, nor regardeth the rich man more than the poor? for they are all the work of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Samuel 25:37–39; 1 Samuel 26:10"> In a moment they die, even at midnight the people are convulsed and pass away; and the strong are taken away without hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremiah 16:17"> For his eyes are upon the ways of man, and he seeth all his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where the workers of iniquity may hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For he doth not long consider a man, to bring him before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="34.23 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> He breaketh in pieces mighty men without inquiry, and setteth others in their stead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> Since he knoweth their actions; and he overthroweth &#91;them&#93; in the night, and they are crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Isaiah 66:24"> He striketh them as wicked men in the open sight of others,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 28:5; 1 Samuel 15:11; Isaiah 5:12"> Because they have turned back from him, and would consider none of his ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> So that they cause the cry of the poor to come unto him, and he heareth the cry of the afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalms 27:9"> When he giveth quietness, who then will disturb? and when he hideth &#91;his&#93; face, who shall behold him? and this towards a nation, or towards a man alike;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; 2 Kings 21:9"> That the ungodly man reign not, that the people be not ensnared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> For hath he said unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="34.31 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">f</a></sup>God, I bear &#91;chastisement&#93;, I will not offend;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 139:23–24"> What I see not, teach thou me; if I have done wrong, I will do so no more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Isaiah 45:9; Psalms 135:6"> Shall he recompense according to thy mind? for thou hast refused &#91;his judgment&#93;; for thou so choosest, and not I; speak then what thou knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> Men of understanding will say to me, and a wise man who heareth me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> Job hath spoken without knowledge, and his words were not with intelligence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> Would that Job may be tried unto the end, because of &#91;his&#93; answers after the manner of evil men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Isaiah 1:19–20; 1 Samuel 15:23"> For he addeth rebellion unto his sin, he clappeth &#91;his hands&#93; among us, and multiplieth his words against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="34.37 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">g</a></sup>God.</span>
