<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> Should a wise man answer with windy knowledge, and fill his belly with the east wind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> Reasoning with unprofitable talk, and with speeches which do no good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Yea, thou makest piety of none effect, and restrainest meditation before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.4 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> For thy mouth uttereth thine iniquity, and thou hast chosen the tongue of the crafty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Thine own mouth condemneth thee, and not I; and thy lips testify against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Art thou the first man that was born? and wast thou brought forth before the hills?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Hast thou listened in the secret council of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.8 Eloah">b</a></sup>God? And hast thou absorbed wisdom for thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What knowest thou that we know not? &#91;what&#93; understandest thou which is not in us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> Both the greyheaded and the aged are with us, older than thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Are the consolations of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.11 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">c</a></sup>God too small for thee? and the word gently spoken to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why doth thy heart carry thee away? and why do thine eyes wink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> That thou turnest thy spirit against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.13 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and lettest words go out of thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What is man, that he should be pure? and he that is born of a woman, that he should be righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Behold, he putteth no trust in his holy ones, and the heavens are not pure in his sight:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> How much less the abominable and corrupt, — man, that drinketh unrighteousness like water!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> I will shew thee, listen to me; and what I have seen I will declare;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> Which wise men have told from their fathers, and have not hidden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> Unto whom alone the earth was given, and no stranger passed among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> All his days the wicked man is tormented, and numbered years are allotted to the violent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> The sound of terrors is in his ears: in prosperity the destroyer cometh upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He believeth not that he shall return out of darkness, and he is singled out for the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He wandereth abroad for bread, — where may it be? He knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Distress and anguish make him afraid; they prevail against him, as a king ready for the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> For he hath stretched out his hand against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.25 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God, and strengthened himself against the Almighty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> He runneth against him, with &#91;outstretched&#93; neck, with the thick bosses of his bucklers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> For he hath covered his face with his fatness, and gathered fat upon &#91;his&#93; flanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> And he dwelleth in desolate cities, in houses that no man inhabiteth, which are destined to become heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He shall not become rich, neither shall his substance continue, and their possessions shall not extend upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He shall not depart out of darkness; the flame shall dry up his branches; and by the breath of his mouth shall he go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> Let him not trust in vanity: he is deceived, for vanity shall be his recompense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> It shall be complete before his day, and his branch shall not be green.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He shall shake off his unripe grapes as a vine, and shall cast his flower as an olive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For the family of the ungodly shall be barren, and fire shall consume the tents of bribery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> They conceive mischief, and bring forth iniquity, and their belly prepareth deceit.</span>
