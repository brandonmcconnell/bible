<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If I have withheld the poor from &#91;their&#93; desire, or caused the eyes of the widow to fail;</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I have made gold my hope, or said to the fine gold, My confidence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> If I rejoiced because my wealth was great, and because my hand had gotten much;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> If I beheld the sun when it shone, or the moon walking in brightness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> And my heart have been secretly enticed, so that my mouth kissed my hand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> This also would be an iniquity for the judge, for I should have denied the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="31.28 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God who is above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> If I rejoiced at the destruction of him that hated me, and exulted when evil befell him;</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> If the men of my tent said not, Who shall find one that hath not been satisfied with his meat? —</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> The stranger did not lodge without; I opened my doors to the pathway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> If I covered my transgressions as Adam, by hiding mine iniquity in my bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> Because I feared the great multitude, and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I kept silence, and went not out of the door, ...</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> Oh that I had one to hear me! Behold my signature: let the Almighty answer me! And let mine opponent write an accusation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> Would I not take it upon my shoulder? I would bind it on to me &#91;as&#93; a crown;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> I would declare unto him the number of my steps; as a prince would I come near to him.</span>
