<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Yea, the light of the wicked shall be put out, and the flame of his fire shall not shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> The light shall become dark in his tent, and his lamp over him shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> The steps of his strength shall be straitened, and his own counsel shall cast him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> For he is sent into the net by his own feet, and he walketh on the meshes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> The gin taketh &#91;him&#93; by the heel, the snare layeth hold on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> A cord is hidden for him in the ground, and his trap in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Terrors make him afraid on every side, and chase him at his footsteps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> His strength is hunger-bitten, and calamity is ready at his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> The firstborn of death devoureth the members of his body; it will devour his members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> His confidence shall be rooted out of his tent, and it shall lead him away to the king of terrors:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> They who are none of his shall dwell in his tent; brimstone shall be showered upon his habitation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> His roots shall be dried up beneath, and above shall his branch be cut off;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> His remembrance shall perish from the earth, and he shall have no name on the pasture-grounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> He is driven from light into darkness, and chased out of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He hath neither son nor grandson among his people, nor any remaining in the places of his sojourn.</span>
