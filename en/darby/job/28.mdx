<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Numbers 31:22; Genesis 4:22"> Iron is taken out of the dust, and copper is molten out of the stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Habakkuk 2:13; Matthew 6:33"> &#91;Man&#93; putteth an end to the darkness, and exploreth to the utmost limit, the stones of darkness and of the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He openeth a shaft far from the inhabitants &#91;of the earth&#93;: forgotten of the foot, they hang suspended; away below men they hover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:25–29; Psalms 104:14–15; Genesis 1:11–12"> As for the earth, out of it cometh bread, and underneath it is turned up as by fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:16; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11"> The stones of it are the place of sapphires, and it hath dust of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:6; Job 28:21–23; Job 38:24"> It is a path no bird of prey knoweth, and the vulture's eye hath not seen it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The proud beasts have not trodden it, nor the fierce lion passed over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:4–6"> &#91;Man&#93; putteth forth his hand upon the flinty rock, he overturneth the mountains by the root.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:9; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 24:4"> He cutteth out channels in the rocks, and his eye seeth every precious thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Isaiah 45:2–3; Isaiah 44:27"> He bindeth the streams that they drip not, and what is hidden he bringeth forth to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–6; Job 28:20; James 1:5"> But wisdom, where shall it be found? and where is the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 8:18–19; Proverbs 3:14–15"> Man knoweth not the value thereof; and it is not found in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34"> The deep saith, It is not in me; and the sea saith, It is not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:10–11; Proverbs 8:19"> Choice gold cannot be given for it, nor silver be weighed for its price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; Ezekiel 28:13; Psalms 45:9"> It is not set in the balance with gold of Ophir, with the precious onyx, and the sapphire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> Gold and glass cannot be compared to it, nor vessels of fine gold be its exchange.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> Corals and crystal are no more remembered; yea, the acquisition of wisdom is above rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 8:19; Exodus 39:10; Ezekiel 28:13"> The topaz of Ethiopia shall not be compared to it, neither shall it be set in the balance with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> Whence then cometh wisdom? and where is the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> For it is hidden from the eyes of all living, and concealed from the fowl of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:10–12; Job 26:6; Job 28:14"> Destruction and death say, We have heard its report with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="28.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God understandeth the way thereof, and he knoweth its place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zechariah 4:10; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For he looketh to the ends of the earth, he seeth under the whole heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Isaiah 40:12; Job 12:15"> In making a weight for the wind, and meting out the waters by measure,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 38:25; Job 37:3; Zechariah 10:1"> In appointing a statute for the rain, and a way for the thunder's flash:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then did he see it, and declare it; he established it, yea, and searched it out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 9:10"> And unto man he said, Lo, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding.</span>
