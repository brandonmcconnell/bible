<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:4–5; Psalms 69:26; Job 11:2–3"> I have heard many such things: grievous comforters are ye all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; Titus 2:8"> Shall words of wind have an end? or what provoketh thee that thou answerest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:25; Psalms 22:7; Lamentations 2:15"> I also could speak as ye: if your soul were in my soul's stead, I could join together words against you, and shake my head at you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:9; Galatians 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> &#91;But&#93; I would encourage you with my mouth, and the solace of my lips should assuage &#91;your pain&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 88:15–18; Psalms 77:1–9"> If I speak, my pain is not assuaged; and if I forbear, what am I eased?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalms 6:6–7"> But now he hath made me weary; ...thou hast made desolate all my family;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 10:17; Job 19:20; Ruth 1:21"> Thou hast shrivelled me up! it is become a witness; and my leanness riseth up against me, it beareth witness to my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:16; Job 13:24; Lamentations 2:16"> His anger teareth and pursueth me; he gnasheth with his teeth against me; &#91;as&#93; mine adversary he sharpeneth his eyes at me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:15; Psalms 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> They gape upon me with their mouth; they smite my cheeks reproachfully; they range themselves together against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 31:8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.11 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath delivered me over to the iniquitous man, and hurled me into the hands of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 7:20; Lamentations 3:11–12; Lamentations 3:4"> I was at rest, but he hath shattered me; he hath taken me by the neck and shaken me to pieces, and set me up for his mark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> His arrows encompass me round about, he cleaveth my reins asunder and doth not spare; he poureth out my gall upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> He breaketh me with breach upon breach; he runneth upon me like a mighty man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalms 7:5; Psalms 75:5"> I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and rolled my horn in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalms 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> My face is red with weeping, and on my eyelids is the shadow of death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalms 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> Although there is no violence in my hands, and my prayer is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> O earth, cover not my blood, and let there be no place for my cry!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> Even now, behold, my Witness is in the heavens, and he that voucheth for me is in the heights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 142:2; Job 16:4"> My friends are my mockers; mine eye poureth out tears unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.20 Eloah">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> Oh that there were arbitration for a man with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.21 Eloah">c</a></sup>God, as a son of man for his friend!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> For years &#91;few&#93; in number shall pass, — and I shall go the way &#91;whence&#93; I shall not return.</span>
