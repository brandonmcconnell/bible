<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Hear attentively my speech, and let this replace your consolations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Suffer me and I will speak; and after I have spoken, mock on!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> As for me, is my complaint to a man? or wherefore should not my spirit be impatient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Mark me, and be astonished, and lay the hand upon the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> Even when I think &#91;thereon&#93;, I am affrighted, and trembling taketh hold of my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Wherefore do the wicked live, grow old, yea, become mighty in power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their seed is established with them in their sight, and their offspring before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses are safe from fear, neither is the rod of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="21.9 Eloah">a</a></sup>God upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their bull gendereth, and faileth not; their cow calveth, and casteth not her calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> They send forth their little ones like a flock, and their children dance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They shout to the tambour and harp, and rejoice at the sound of the pipe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> They spend their days in prosperity, and in a moment go down to Sheol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> And they say unto<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="21.14 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">b</a></sup>God, Depart from us, for we desire not the knowledge of thy ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> What is the Almighty that we should serve him? and what are we profited if we pray unto him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> Behold, their prosperity is not in their hand. The counsel of the wicked be far from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How often is the lamp of the wicked put out, and cometh their calamity upon them? Doth he distribute sorrows &#91;to them&#93; in his anger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> Do they become as stubble before the wind, and as chaff that the storm carrieth away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="21.19 Eloah">c</a></sup>God layeth up &#91;the punishment of&#93; his iniquity for his children; he rewardeth him, and he shall know &#91;it&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> His eyes shall see his destruction, and he shall drink of the fury of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For what pleasure should he have in his house after him, when the number of his months is cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Can any teach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="21.22 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">d</a></sup>God knowledge? And he it is that judgeth those that are high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> One dieth in his full strength, being wholly at ease and quiet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> His sides are full of fat, and the marrow of his bones is moistened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> And another dieth in bitterness of soul, and hath not tasted good:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> Together they lie down in the dust, and the worms cover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> Lo, I know your thoughts, and the devices ye wrongfully imagine against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For ye say, Where is the house of the noble? and where the tent of the dwellings of the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> Have ye not asked the wayfarers? and do ye not regard their tokens:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> That the wicked is reserved for the day of calamity? They are led forth to the day of wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who shall declare his way to his face? and who shall repay him what he hath done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> Yet is he carried to the graves, and watch is kept over the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> The clods of the valley are sweet unto him; and every man followeth suit after him, as there were innumerable before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> How then comfort ye me in vain? Your answers remain perfidious.</span>
