<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 8:1; Job 22:1; Job 15:1"> And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 6:11"> If a word were essayed to thee, wouldest thou be grieved? But who can refrain from speaking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> Thy words have upholden him that was stumbling, and thou hast braced up the bending knees:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now it is come upon thee, and thou grievest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Hath not thy piety been thy confidence, and the perfection of thy ways thy hope?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Remember, I pray thee, who that was innocent has perished? and where were the upright cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> Even as I have seen, they that plough iniquity and sow mischief, reap the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> By the breath of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="4.9 Eloah">a</a></sup>God they perish, and by the blast of his nostrils are they consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> The roar of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the whelps of the lioness are scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Now to me a word was secretly brought, and mine ear received a whisper thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> In thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men: —</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> Fear came on me, and trembling, and made all my bones to shake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> And a spirit passed before my face — the hair of my flesh stood up —</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> It stood still; I could not discern the appearance thereof: a form was before mine eyes; I heard a slight murmur and a voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> Shall &#91;mortal&#93; man be more just than<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="4.17 Eloah">b</a></sup>God? Shall a man be purer than his Maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Lo, he trusteth not his servants, and his angels he chargeth with folly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> How much more them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed as the moth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> From morning to evening are they smitten: without any heeding it, they perish for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; Psalms 39:11; Job 8:22"> Is not their tent-cord torn away in them? they die, and without wisdom.</span>
