<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Even to-day is my complaint bitter: my stroke is heavier than my groaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> Oh that I knew where I might find him, that I might come to his seat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would order the cause before him, and fill my mouth with arguments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I would know the words he would answer me, and understand what he would say unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Would he plead against me with &#91;his&#93; great power? Nay; but he would give heed unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> There would an upright man reason with him; and I should be delivered for ever from my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> Lo, I go forward, but he is not there; and backward, but I do not perceive him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> On the left hand, where he doth work, but I behold &#91;him&#93; not; he hideth himself on the right hand, and I see &#91;him&#93; not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> But he knoweth the way that I take; he trieth me, I shall come forth as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My foot hath held to his steps; his way have I kept, and not turned aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> Neither have I gone back from the commandment of his lips; I have laid up the words of his mouth more than the purpose of my own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> But he is in one &#91;mind&#93;, and who can turn him? And what his soul desireth, that will he do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> For he will perform &#91;what&#93; is appointed for me; and many such things are with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore am I troubled at his presence; I consider, and I am afraid of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.16 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath made my heart soft, and the Almighty troubleth me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither hath he hidden the gloom from me.</span>
