<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timotheus the brother, to Philemon the beloved and our fellow-workman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> and to the sister Apphia and to Archippus our fellow-soldier, and to the assembly which &#91;is&#93; in thine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace to you and peace from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.3 Elohim">a</a></sup>God our Father, and &#91;the&#93; Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I thank my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.4 Elohim">b</a></sup>God, always making mention of thee at my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> hearing of thy love and the faith which thou hast towards the Lord Jesus, and towards all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> in such sort that thy participation in the faith should become operative in the acknowledgment of every good thing which is in us towards Christ &#91;Jesus&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> For we have great thankfulness and encouragement through thy love, because the bowels of the saints are refreshed by thee, brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Wherefore having much boldness in Christ to enjoin thee what is fitting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> for love's sake I rather exhort, being such a one as Paul the aged, and now also prisoner of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I exhort thee for my child, whom I have begotten in &#91;my&#93; bonds, Onesimus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> once unserviceable to thee, but now serviceable to thee and to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> whom I have sent back to thee: &#91;but do thou receive&#93; him, that is, my bowels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> whom I was desirous of keeping with myself, that for thee he might minister to me in the bonds of the glad tidings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> but I have wished to do nothing without thy mind, that thy good might not be as of necessity but of willingness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> for perhaps for this reason he has been separated &#91;from thee&#93; for a time, that thou mightest possess him fully for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> not any longer as a bondman, but above a bondman, a beloved brother, specially to me, and how much rather to thee, both in &#91;the&#93; flesh and in &#91;the&#93; Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If therefore thou holdest me to be a partner &#91;with thee&#93;, receive him as me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> but if he have wronged thee anything or owe anything &#91;to thee&#93;, put this to my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I Paul have written &#91;it&#93; with mine own hand; I will repay &#91;it&#93;: that I say not to thee that thou owest even thine own self also to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yea, brother, I would have profit of thee in &#91;the&#93; Lord: refresh my bowels in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Being confident of thine obedience, I have written to thee, knowing that thou wilt do even more than I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> But withal prepare me also a lodging; for I hope that I shall be granted to you through your prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras salutes thee, my fellow-prisoner in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow-workmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;be&#93; with your spirit.</span>
