<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:19–20; Jeremiah 30:3; Ezekiel 39:25"> For behold, in those days, and in that time, when I shall turn again the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–4; Isaiah 66:16; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will also gather all the nations, and will bring them down into the valley of Jehoshaphat, and I will enter into judgment with them there on account of my people and mine inheritance, Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations: and they have parted my land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Obadiah 1:11; Amos 2:6"> and they have cast lots for my people, and have given a boy for a harlot, and sold a girl for wine, and have drunk &#91;it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 1:6–10; Jeremiah 47:4; Isaiah 59:18"> Yea also, what have ye to do with me, O Tyre and Zidon, and all the districts of Philistia? Will ye render me a recompence? But if ye recompense me, swiftly &#91;and&#93; speedily will I bring your recompence upon your own head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:18"> because ye have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried into your temples my beautiful pleasant things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Joel 3:3; Deuteronomy 28:68"> and the children of Judah and the children of Jerusalem have ye sold unto the children of the Greeks, that ye might remove them far from their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Jeremiah 23:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:6"> Behold, I will raise them up out of the place whither ye have sold them, and will bring your recompence upon your own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 60:14; Job 1:15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the children of Judah, and they shall sell them to the Sabeans, to a nation far off: for Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:3–4; Isaiah 8:9–10; Ezekiel 38:7"> Proclaim this among the nations: prepare war, arouse the mighty men, let all the men of war draw near, let them come up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:8; Micah 4:3; 2 Chronicles 25:8"> Beat your ploughshares into swords, and your pruning-knives into spears; let the weak say, I am strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 38:9–18"> Haste ye and come, all ye nations round about, and gather yourselves together. Thither cause thy mighty ones to come down, O Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Isaiah 2:4; Psalms 96:13"> Let the nations rouse themselves, and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat; for there will I sit to judge all the nations round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:15–20; Mark 4:29; Hosea 6:11"> Put in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe: come, get you down, for the press is full, the vats overflow; for their wickedness is great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 34:2–8"> Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision! For the day of Jehovah is at hand in the valley of decision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31"> The sun and the moon shall be darkened, and the stars shall withdraw their shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 2:10–11; Haggai 2:6"> And Jehovah will roar from Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heavens and the earth shall shake: and Jehovah will be a shelter for his people, and the refuge of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 52:1"> And ye shall know that I, Jehovah, &#91;am&#93; your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.17 Elohim">a</a></sup>God, dwelling in Zion, my holy mountain; and Jerusalem shall be holy, and no strangers shall pass through her any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:1–12; Zechariah 14:8; Revelation 22:1–2"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the mountains shall drop down new wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the water-courses of Judah shall flow with waters; and a fountain shall come forth from the house of Jehovah, and shall water the valley of Shittim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:35; Zechariah 14:18–19"> Egypt shall be a desolation, and Edom shall be a desolate wilderness, for the violence against the children of Judah, in that they have shed innocent blood in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 33:20"> But Judah shall abide for ever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> And I will purge them from the blood from which I had not purged them: for Jehovah dwelleth in Zion.</span>
