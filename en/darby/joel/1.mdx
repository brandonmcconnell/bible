<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:16; Jeremiah 1:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> The word of Jehovah that came to Joel the son of Pethuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Hosea 5:1; Job 8:8"> Hear this, ye old men, and give ear, all ye inhabitants of the land. Hath this been in your days, or even in the days of your fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Exodus 13:14; Psalms 145:4"> Tell your children of it, and &#91;let&#93; your children &#91;tell&#93; their children, and their children another generation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Amos 4:9; Isaiah 33:4"> that which the palmer-worm hath left hath the locust eaten; and that which the locust hath left hath the cankerworm eaten; and that which the cankerworm hath left hath the caterpillar eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:1; Isaiah 32:10–12; Luke 21:34–36"> Awake, ye drunkards, and weep; and howl, all ye drinkers of wine, because of the new wine: for it is cut off from your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Revelation 9:7–10; Proverbs 30:14"> For a nation is come up upon my land, strong and without number: his teeth are the teeth of a lion, and he hath the cheek teeth of a lioness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Amos 4:9; Joel 1:12"> He hath made my vine a desolation, and barked my fig-tree; he hath made it clean bare, and cast it away: its branches are made white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Malachi 2:15; Jeremiah 3:4"> Wail like a virgin girded with sackcloth for the husband of her youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Joel 2:14; Hosea 9:4"> The oblation and the drink-offering are cut off from the house of Jehovah; the priests, Jehovah's ministers, mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Hosea 9:2; Jeremiah 12:11"> The field is laid waste, the land mourneth; for the corn is wasted, the new wine is dried up, the oil languisheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Isaiah 17:11; Jeremiah 9:12"> Be ashamed, ye husbandmen; howl, ye vinedressers, for the wheat and for the barley: because the harvest of the field hath perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Isaiah 24:11; Song of Solomon 2:3"> The vine is dried up, and the fig-tree languisheth; the pomegranate-tree, the palm also and the apple-tree; all the trees of the field are withered, yea, joy is withered away from the children of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 1:8–9; Jeremiah 4:8; Joel 2:17"> Gird yourselves, and lament, ye priests; howl, ministers of the altar; come, lie all night in sackcloth, ye ministers of my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God: for the oblation and the drink-offering are withholden from the house of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 29:10–11; 2 Chronicles 20:13"> Hallow a fast, proclaim a solemn assembly, gather the elders, &#91;and&#93; all the inhabitants of the land to the house of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and cry unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6–9; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Jeremiah 30:7"> Alas for the day! for the day of Jehovah is at hand, and as destruction from the Almighty shall it come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:6–7; Psalms 43:4; Isaiah 62:8–9"> Is not the food cut off before our eyes, joy and gladness from the house of our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Genesis 23:16"> The seeds are rotten under their clods, the granaries are laid desolate, the barns are broken down; for the corn is withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 14:5–6"> How do the beasts groan! The herds of cattle are bewildered, for they have no pasture; the flocks of sheep also are in suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 9:10; Amos 7:4"> To thee, Jehovah, do I cry; for the fire hath devoured the pastures of the wilderness, and the flame hath burned up all the trees of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; 1 Kings 17:7; Job 38:41"> The beasts of the field also cry unto thee; for the water-courses are dried, and the fire hath devoured the pastures of the wilderness.</span>
