<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:15; Joel 1:15; 1 Peter 4:7"> Blow the trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm in my holy mountain; let all the inhabitants of the land tremble: for the day of Jehovah cometh, for it is at hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:6; Zephaniah 1:14–15; Joel 2:10–11"> a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and gross darkness, as the dawn spread upon the mountains; — a great people and a strong; there hath not been ever the like, neither shall be any more after them, to the years of generations and generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 1:19–20; Genesis 2:8"> A fire devoureth before them, and behind them a flame burneth; the land is as a garden of Eden before them, and behind them a desolate wilderness: yea, and nothing escapeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:7"> The appearance of them is as the appearance of horses; and as horsemen, so they run.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:9; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 30:30"> Like the noise of chariots, on the tops of the mountains, they leap; like the noise of a flame of fire that devoureth the stubble, as a strong people set in battle array.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 30:6"> Before them the peoples are in anguish: all faces turn pale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 30:27; 2 Samuel 1:23; 2 Samuel 2:18–19"> They run like mighty men; they climb the wall like men of war; and they march every one on his ways, and break not their ranks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:10; Job 33:18; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> Neither doth one press upon another; they march every one in his path; and fall amid weapons, but are not wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:21; John 10:1; Exodus 10:6"> They spread themselves over the city; they run upon the wall; they climb up into the houses; they enter in by the windows like a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Isaiah 13:10; Joel 3:15–16"> The earth quaketh before them; the heavens tremble; the sun and the moon are darkened, and the stars withdraw their shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachi 3:2; Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 30:7"> And Jehovah uttereth his voice before his army; for his camp is very great; for strong is he that executeth his word: for the day of Jehovah is great and very terrible; and who can bear it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:8–9; Isaiah 55:6–7; 2 Chronicles 7:13–14"> Yet even now, saith Jehovah, turn to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Psalms 34:18; Exodus 34:6–7"> and rend your heart, and not your garments, and turn unto Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God: for he is gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and of great loving-kindness, and repenteth him of the evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Haggai 2:19; 2 Kings 19:4"> Who knoweth? He might return and repent, and leave a blessing behind him, an oblation and a drink-offering for Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Joel 2:1; 1 Kings 21:12"> Blow the trumpet in Zion, hallow a fast, proclaim a solemn assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; 2 Chronicles 20:13; 1 Corinthians 7:5"> gather the people, hallow the congregation, assemble the elders, gather the children, and those that suck the breasts; let the bridegroom go forth from his chamber, and the bride from her closet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 115:2; Ezekiel 8:16; Psalms 79:10"> Let the priests, the ministers of Jehovah, weep between the porch and the altar, and let them say, Spare, O Jehovah, thy people, and give not thine inheritance to reproach, that they should be a byword of the nations. Wherefore should they say among the peoples, Where is their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:2; Psalms 103:13; Zechariah 1:14"> Then Jehovah will be jealous for his land, and will have pity on his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Ezekiel 36:15; Isaiah 62:8–9"> And Jehovah will answer and say unto his people, Behold, I send you corn, and new wine, and oil, and ye shall be satisfied therewith; and I will no more make you a reproach among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 14:8; Amos 4:10; Isaiah 34:3"> And I will remove far off from you him &#91;that cometh&#93; from the north, and will drive him into a land barren and desolate, his face toward the eastern sea, and his rear toward the hinder sea; and his stench shall come up, and his ill odour shall come up, for he hath exalted himself to do great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:16–17; Isaiah 54:4; Isaiah 41:10"> — Fear not, O land; be glad and rejoice: for Jehovah doeth great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalms 65:12; Psalms 67:6"> Be not afraid, ye beasts of the field: for the pastures of the wilderness do spring; for the tree beareth its fruit; the fig-tree and the vine yield full increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 5:7–8; Philippians 4:4; Leviticus 26:4"> And ye, children of Zion, be glad and rejoice in Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God; for he giveth you the early rain in due measure, and he causeth to come down for you the rain, the early rain, and the latter rain at the beginning &#91;of the season&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Amos 9:13; Joel 3:18"> And the floors shall be full of corn, and the vats shall overflow with new wine and oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zechariah 10:6; Joel 1:4–7; Joel 2:2–11"> And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten, the cankerworm, and the caterpillar, and the palmer-worm, my great army which I sent among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 22:26; Romans 5:5"> And ye shall eat in plenty, and be satisfied, and praise the name of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, who hath dealt wondrously with you: and my people shall never be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:26–28; Leviticus 26:11–12; Zephaniah 3:17"> And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I, Jehovah, &#91;am&#93; your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, and there is none else: and my people shall never be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:16–21; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 32:15"> And it shall come to pass afterwards &#91;that&#93; I will pour out my Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11"> Yea, even upon the bondmen and upon the handmaids in those days will I pour out my Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Luke 21:11; Matthew 24:29"> And I will shew wonders in the heavens and on the earth, blood, and fire, and pillars of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 6:12–13; Matthew 24:29; Malachi 4:1"> The sun shall be changed to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and terrible day of Jehovah come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 10:11–14; Acts 2:20–21; Isaiah 46:13"> And it shall be that whosoever shall call upon the name of Jehovah shall be saved: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as Jehovah hath said, and for the residue whom Jehovah shall call.</span>
