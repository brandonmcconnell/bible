<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:40"> And it shall be if the wicked man have deserved to be beaten, that the judge shall cause him to lie down, and be beaten before his face, according to the measure of his wickedness with a certain number &#91;of stripes&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 18:3; James 2:2–3; Luke 15:30"> With forty &#91;stripes&#93; shall they beat him; they shall not exceed, lest, if they continue to beat him with many stripes above these, thy brother become despicable in thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Proverbs 12:10"> Thou shalt not muzzle the ox when he treadeth out &#91;the corn&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:24; Luke 20:28; Mark 12:19"> If brethren dwell together, and one of them die, and have no son, the wife of the dead shall not marry a stranger abroad: her husband's brother shall go in unto her, and take her to him as wife, and perform the duty of a husband's brother unto her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> And it shall be, that the firstborn that she beareth shall stand in the name of his brother who is dead, that his name be not blotted out from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> But if the man like not to take his brother's wife, his brother's wife shall go up to the gate unto the elders, and say, My husband's brother refuseth to raise up unto his brother a name in Israel: he will not perform for me the duty of a husband's brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> Then the elders of his city shall call him and speak unto him; and if he stand to it and say, I like not to take her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Isaiah 50:6"> then shall his brother's wife come near to him before the eyes of the elders, and draw his sandal from his foot, and spit in his face, and shall answer and say, So shall it be done unto the man that will not build up his brother's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And his name shall be called in Israel, The house of him that hath his shoe drawn off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> When men fight together one with another, and the wife of the one come near to rescue her husband out of the hand of him that smiteth him, and stretch out her hand, and seize him by his secret parts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> thou shalt cut off her hand; thine eye shall not spare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> Thou shalt not have in thy bag divers weights, a great and a small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou shalt not have in thy house divers ephahs, a great and a small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> A perfect and just weight shalt thou have; a perfect and just ephah shalt thou have; that thy days may be prolonged in the land that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="25.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For every one that doeth such things, every one that doeth unrighteousness, is an abomination to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what Amalek did unto thee on the way, when ye came forth out of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 16:6"> how he met thee on the way, and smote the hindmost of thee, all the feeble that lagged behind thee, when thou wast faint and weary, and he feared not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="25.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> And it shall be, when Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="25.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God shall have given thee rest from all thine enemies round about, in the land that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="25.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God giveth thee for an inheritance to possess it, that thou shalt blot out the remembrance of Amalek from under the heavens; thou shalt not forget it.</span>
