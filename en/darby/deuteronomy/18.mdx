<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–9; Deuteronomy 10:9; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> The priests, the Levites, &#91;and&#93; the whole tribe of Levi, shall have no portion nor inheritance with Israel: Jehovah's offerings by fire, and his inheritance shall they eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 1:5–6; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 2:5"> but they shall have no inheritance among their brethren: Jehovah, he is their inheritance, as he hath said unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Deuteronomy 12:27"> And this shall be the priest's due from the people, from them that sacrifice a sacrifice, whether ox, or sheep: they shall give unto the priest the shoulder, and the jawbones, and the maw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; Numbers 18:12–24; 2 Chronicles 31:4–10"> The firstfruits &#91;also&#93; of thy corn, of thy new wine, and of thine oil, and the firstfruits of the shearing of thy sheep, shalt thou give him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 3:10; Exodus 28:1–14"> for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="18.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath chosen him out of all thy tribes, that he may stand to serve in the name of Jehovah, he and his sons continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:2–3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 16:2"> And if the Levite shall come from one of thy gates out of all Israel, where he sojourneth, and shall come according to all the desire of his soul unto the place which Jehovah will choose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2–4"> and shall serve in the name of Jehovah his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="18.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, as all his brethren the Levites that stand there before Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; 2 Chronicles 31:4"> — they shall have like portions to eat, besides that which he hath sold of his patrimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> When thou art come into the land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="18.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God giveth thee, thou shalt not learn to do according to the abominations of those nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> There shall not be found among you he that maketh his son or his daughter to pass through the fire, that useth divination, that useth auguries, or an enchanter, or a sorcerer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 28:11–14"> or a charmer, or one that inquireth of a spirit of Python, or a soothsayer, or one that consulteth the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> For every one that doeth these things is an abomination to Jehovah, and because of these abominations Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="18.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God doth dispossess them from before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> Thou shalt be perfect with Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="18.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> For these nations, which thou shalt dispossess, hearkened unto those that use auguries, and that use divination; but as for thee, Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="18.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God hath not suffered thee &#91;to do&#93; so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="18.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God will raise up unto thee a prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him shall ye hearken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> according to all that thou desiredst of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="18.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, saying, Let me not hear again the voice of Jehovah my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="18.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, neither let me see this great fire any more, that I die not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> And Jehovah said unto me, They have well spoken that which they have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> A prophet will I raise up unto them from among their brethren, like unto thee, and will put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak unto them all that I shall command him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> And it shall come to pass that the man who hearkeneth not unto my words which he shall speak in my name, I will require it of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:13–15"> But the prophet who shall presume to speak a word in my name that I have not commanded him to speak, or that shall speak in the name of other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="18.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>gods, that prophet shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> And if thou say in thy heart, How shall we know the word that Jehovah hath not spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; 2 Kings 20:1; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> When a prophet speaketh in the name of Jehovah, and the thing followeth not, nor cometh to pass, that is the word which Jehovah hath not spoken; the prophet hath spoken it presumptuously: be not afraid of him.</span>
