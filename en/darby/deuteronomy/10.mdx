<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:1–2; Exodus 25:10–15; Deuteronomy 10:3–4"> At that time Jehovah said unto me, Hew for thyself two tables of stone like the first, and come up unto me into the mountain, and make thee an ark of wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 8:9; Deuteronomy 10:5; Deuteronomy 4:13"> and I will write on the tables the words that were on the first tables which thou didst break, and thou shalt lay them in the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:4; Exodus 37:1–9; Exodus 25:10"> And I made an ark of acacia-wood, and hewed two tables of stone like the first, and went up the mountain with the two tables in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:10; Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 12:18–19"> And he wrote on the tables, as the first writing, the ten words which Jehovah spoke unto you on the mountain, from the midst of the fire, on the day of the assembly, and Jehovah gave them unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:29; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 10:2"> And I turned and came down from the mountain, and put the tables in the ark which I had made; — and they are there, as Jehovah commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:23–28; Numbers 33:30–33"> (And the children of Israel took their journey from Beeroth-Bene-Jaakan to Moserah: there Aaron died, and there he was buried; and Eleazar his son exercised the priesthood in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:32–34"> From thence they journeyed unto Gudgodah; and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land of water-brooks.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Deuteronomy 18:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> At that time Jehovah separated the tribe of Levi, to bear the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, to stand before Jehovah to do service unto him, and to bless in his name, unto this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Therefore Levi has no portion nor inheritance with his brethren; Jehovah is his inheritance, according as Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 34:28"> But I stood upon the mountain according to the former days, forty days and forty nights; and Jehovah listened unto me also at that time: Jehovah would not destroy thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:1; Exodus 32:34"> And Jehovah said unto me, Rise up, take thy journey before the people, that they may enter in and possess the land, which I swore unto their fathers to give unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:5; Luke 10:27"> And now, Israel, what doth Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="10.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God require of thee, but to fear Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="10.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, to walk in all his ways, and to love him, and to serve Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="10.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God with all thy heart and with all thy soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Deuteronomy 6:24; James 1:25"> to keep the commandments of Jehovah, and his statutes, which I command thee this day, for thy good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Psalms 24:1; Psalms 115:16"> Behold, the heaven and the heaven of heavens belong to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="10.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God; the earth and all that is therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:37; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 9:13–23"> Only, Jehovah took pleasure in thy fathers, to love them, and he chose their seed after them, &#91;even&#93; you, out of all the peoples, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Deuteronomy 30:6; Leviticus 26:41"> Circumcise then the foreskin of your heart, and stiffen your neck no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; Daniel 2:47"> For Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="10.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="10.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="10.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>gods, and the Lord of lords, the great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="10.17 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">i</a></sup>God, the mighty and the terrible, who regardeth not persons, nor taketh reward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5; Jeremiah 49:11"> who executeth the judgment of the fatherless and the widow, and loveth the stranger, to give him food and clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Leviticus 19:33–34; Luke 17:18"> And ye shall love the stranger; for ye have been strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 13:4; Luke 4:8"> Thou shalt fear Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="10.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God; him thou shalt serve, and unto him shalt thou cleave, and swear by his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:14; Psalms 106:21–22; Exodus 15:2"> He is thy praise, and he is thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="10.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, who hath done for thee these great and terrible things, which thine eyes have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5; Genesis 15:5"> With seventy souls thy fathers went down into Egypt; and now Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="10.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God hath made thee as the stars of heaven for multitude.</span>
