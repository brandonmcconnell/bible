<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Exodus 12:2–20; Exodus 13:4"> Keep the month of Abib, and celebrate the passover to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="16.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God; for in the month of Abib Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="16.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God brought thee forth out of Egypt by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 12:26; Luke 22:15"> And thou shalt sacrifice the passover to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="16.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, of the flock and of the herd, in the place which Jehovah will choose to cause his name to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:39; Exodus 34:18; Exodus 12:19–20"> Thou shalt eat no leavened bread along with it; seven days shalt thou eat unleavened bread with it, bread of affliction; for thou camest forth out of the land of Egypt in haste, — that thou mayest remember the day when thou camest forth out of the land of Egypt, all the days of thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Exodus 12:10; Exodus 13:7"> And there shall be no leaven seen with thee in all thy borders seven days; neither shall any of the flesh, which thou sacrificedst at even on the first day, be left over night until the morning. —</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; Deuteronomy 12:5–6"> Thou mayest not sacrifice the passover in one of thy gates, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="16.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God giveth thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Exodus 12:6–9; Numbers 9:11"> but at the place that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="16.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God will choose, to cause his name to dwell in, there thou shalt sacrifice the passover at even, at the going down of the sun, at the time that thou camest forth out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Exodus 12:8–9; John 2:23"> And thou shalt cook and eat it at the place which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="16.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God will choose; and in the morning shalt thou turn and go unto thy tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 13:6–8; Leviticus 23:6–8; Leviticus 23:36"> Six days thou shalt eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day is a solemn assembly to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="16.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God; thou shalt do no work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 34:22; Exodus 23:16; Acts 2:1"> Seven weeks shalt thou count: from the beginning of putting the sickle into the corn shalt thou begin to count seven weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Leviticus 12:8; Malachi 3:10–11"> And thou shalt hold the feast of weeks to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="16.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God with a tribute of a voluntary-offering of thy hand, which thou shalt give, according as Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="16.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God hath blessed thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 12:18"> and thou shalt rejoice before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="16.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy bondman, and thy handmaid, and the Levite that is in thy gates, and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow that are in thy midst in the place that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="16.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God will choose to cause his name to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:15; Lamentations 3:19–20; Romans 6:17–18"> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in Egypt, and thou shalt keep and do these statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; John 7:2; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> The feast of tabernacles shalt thou hold seven days, when thou hast gathered in &#91;the produce&#93; of thy floor and of thy winepress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9–12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Isaiah 30:29"> And thou shalt rejoice in thy feast, thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy bondman, and thy handmaid, and the Levite, and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, that are in thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36–42; Deuteronomy 30:16; Deuteronomy 16:10"> Seven days shalt thou hold a feast to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="16.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God in the place which Jehovah will choose; for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="16.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God will bless thee in all thy produce, and in all the work of thy hands, and thou shalt be wholly joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Psalms 96:8; Haggai 1:9"> Three times in the year shall all thy males appear before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="16.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God in the place which he will choose, at the feast of unleavened bread, and at the feast of weeks, and at the feast of tabernacles; and they shall not appear before Jehovah empty:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–7; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Deuteronomy 16:10"> each &#91;shall give&#93; according to that which is in his power to give, according to the blessing of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="16.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God which he hath given thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Exodus 18:25–26; Romans 13:1–6"> Judges and officers shalt thou make thee in all thy gates, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="16.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God giveth thee, throughout thy tribes, that they may judge the people with just judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:23"> Thou shalt not wrest judgment; thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a bribe; for the bribe blindeth the eyes of the wise, and perverteth the words of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Philippians 4:8; Ezekiel 18:9; Ezekiel 18:5"> Perfect justice shalt thou follow, that thou mayest live, and possess the land that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="16.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:3; 2 Kings 17:16; Exodus 34:13"> Thou shalt not plant thyself an Asherah of any wood near unto the altar of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="16.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">r</a></sup>God, which thou shalt make thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:4; Zechariah 8:17"> Neither shalt thou set thee up a statue, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="16.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">s</a></sup>God hateth.</span>
