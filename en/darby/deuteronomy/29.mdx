<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:2–3; Hebrews 8:9; Exodus 24:2–8"> These are the words of the covenant that Jehovah commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant that he made with them in Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Exodus 8:12; Joshua 24:5–6"> And Moses called to all Israel, and said unto them, Ye have seen all that Jehovah did before your eyes in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, and to all his bondmen, and to all his land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:32–35; Nehemiah 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 7:18–19"> the great trials that thine eyes have seen, those great signs and wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 6:9–10; Proverbs 20:12"> But Jehovah hath not given you a heart to perceive, and eyes to see, and ears to hear, to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:4; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And I have led you forty years in the wilderness; your clothes are not grown old upon you, and thy sandal is not grown old upon thy foot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25"> ye have not eaten bread, neither have ye drunk wine or strong drink, that ye might know that I am Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="29.6 Elohim">a</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:24–17; Numbers 32:33–42; Psalms 135:10–12"> And ye came to this place; and Sihon the king of Heshbon and Og the king of Bashan came out against us for battle, and we smote them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:12–13; Numbers 32:33"> And we took their land, and gave it for an inheritance to the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to the half tribe of the Manassites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:6; Luke 11:28"> Ye shall keep then the words of this covenant, and do them, that ye may prosper in all that ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:16–17; Revelation 20:12; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> Ye stand this day all of you before Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="29.10 Elohim">b</a></sup>God: your chiefs &#91;of&#93; your tribes, your elders, and your officers, all the men of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:38; Joshua 9:21–27; Exodus 12:48–49"> your little ones, your wives, and thy stranger that is in thy camp, as well the hewer of thy wood as the drawer of thy water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:12–15; 2 Kings 11:17; Deuteronomy 5:2–3"> that thou mayest enter into the covenant of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="29.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, and into his oath, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="29.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God maketh with thee this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Genesis 17:7; Deuteronomy 28:9"> that he may establish thee this day for a people unto himself, and &#91;that&#93; he may be to thee a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="29.13 Elohim">e</a></sup>God, as he hath said unto thee, and as he hath sworn unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 8:7–12"> Neither with you only do I make this covenant and this oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:39; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Jeremiah 50:5"> but with him that standeth here with us this day before Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="29.15 Elohim">f</a></sup>God, and with him that is not here with us this day</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Deuteronomy 2:4"> (for ye know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt, and how we came through the nations which ye passed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36"> and ye have seen their abominations, and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which were among them);</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Deuteronomy 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 13:1–15"> lest there should be among you man, or woman, or family, or tribe, whose heart turneth away this day from Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="29.18 Elohim">g</a></sup>God, to go and serve the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="29.18 Elohim">h</a></sup>gods of these nations; lest there should be among you a root that beareth gall and wormwood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Numbers 15:30; Numbers 15:39"> and it come to pass, when he heareth the words of this curse, that he bless himself in his heart, saying, I shall have peace, though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart, to sweep away the drunken with the thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 79:5; Deuteronomy 9:14"> Jehovah will not pardon him, but the anger of Jehovah and his jealousy will then smoke against that man, and all the curse shall be upon him that is written in this book; and Jehovah will blot out his name from under the heavens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Joshua 7:1–26; Ezekiel 13:9"> and Jehovah will separate him for mischief out of all the tribes of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant that is written in this book of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:8"> And the generation to come, your children who shall rise up after you, and the foreigner that shall come from a far land, shall say, when they see the plagues of that land, and its sicknesses wherewith Jehovah hath visited it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 17:6; Isaiah 34:9"> &#91;that&#93; the whole ground thereof is brimstone and salt, &#91;and&#93; burning, that it is not sown, nor beareth, and no grass groweth in it, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboim, which Jehovah overthrew in his anger and in his fury:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:8–9; Romans 2:5"> even all nations shall say, Why has Jehovah done thus to this land? whence the heat of this great anger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:9; Isaiah 47:6; Jeremiah 50:7"> And men shall say, Because they have forsaken the covenant of Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="29.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God of their fathers, which he had made with them when he brought them forth out of the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Judges 2:12–13; Judges 5:8"> and they went and served other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="29.26 Elohim">j</a></sup>gods, and bowed down to them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="29.26 Elohim">k</a></sup>gods whom they knew not, and whom he had not assigned to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 27:15–26"> And the anger of Jehovah was kindled against this land, to bring upon it all the curse that is written in this book;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:20; 1 Kings 14:15; Psalms 52:5"> and Jehovah rooted them out of their land in anger, and in fury, and in great indignation, and cast them into another land, as &#91;it appears&#93; this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Acts 1:7; Romans 11:33–34"> The hidden things belong to Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="29.29 Elohim">l</a></sup>God; but the revealed ones are ours and our children's for ever, to do all the words of this law.</span>
