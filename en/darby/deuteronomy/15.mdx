<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10; Exodus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:2–4"> At the end of seven years thou shalt make a release,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 7:42; James 2:13; Luke 6:34–38"> and this is the manner of the release: Every creditor shall relax his hand from the loan which he hath lent unto his neighbour; he shall not demand it of his neighbour, or of his brother; for a release to Jehovah hath been proclaimed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:20; Matthew 17:25–26; 1 Corinthians 6:6–7"> Of the foreigner thou mayest demand it; but what is thine with thy brother thy hand shall release;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 58:10–11; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:24–25"> save when there shall be no one in need among you; for Jehovah will greatly bless thee in the land that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="15.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God giveth thee for an inheritance to possess it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–15; Isaiah 1:19–20; Joshua 1:7"> if thou only diligently hearken unto the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, to take heed to do all this commandment which I command thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Luke 6:35; Nehemiah 9:27"> For Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="15.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God will bless thee, as he promised thee; and thou shalt lend on pledge to many nations, but thou shalt not borrow; and thou shalt rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:16–17; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 21:13"> If there be amongst you a poor man, any one of thy brethren in one of thy gates, in thy land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="15.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God giveth thee, thou shalt not harden thy heart, nor shut thy hand from thy brother in need;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:34; Matthew 5:42"> but thou shalt open thy hand bountifully unto him, and shalt certainly lend him on pledge what is sufficient for his need, &#91;in that&#93; which he lacketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; Matthew 20:15; Jeremiah 17:10"> Beware that there be not a wicked thought in thy heart, saying, The seventh year, the year of release, is at hand; and thine eye be evil against thy poor brother, and thou givest him nought; and he cry against thee to Jehovah, and it be sin in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:9; Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:10"> Thou shalt bountifully give unto him, and thy heart shall not be evil-disposed when thou givest unto him; because for this thing Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="15.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God will bless thee in all thy works, and in all the business of thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11; John 12:8"> For the needy shall never cease from within the land; therefore I command thee, saying, Thou shalt open thy hand bountifully unto thy brother, to thy poor and to thy needy, in thy land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:14; Leviticus 25:39–41; Exodus 21:2–6"> If thy brother, a Hebrew man, or a Hebrew woman, have been sold unto thee, he shall serve thee six years, and in the seventh year thou shalt let him go free from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 22:13; Proverbs 3:27–28"> And when thou sendest him out free from thee, thou shalt not let him go away empty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Proverbs 10:22; Psalms 68:10"> thou shalt certainly furnish him from thy sheep, and out of thy floor, and out of thy winepress: of what Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="15.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God hath blessed thee with shalt thou give unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:12; Matthew 18:32–33; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in the land of Egypt, and that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="15.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God redeemed thee; therefore I command thee this thing to-day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:5–6; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 40:6"> And it shall be, if he say unto thee, I will not go away from thee, — because he loveth thee and thy house, because he is well with thee, —</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:22; Leviticus 25:39–42"> then thou shalt take an awl, and thrust it through his ear and into the door; and he shall be thy bondman for ever. And also unto thy handmaid thou shalt do likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 17:7–8; Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 21:16"> Let it not seem hard unto thee, when thou sendest him away free from thee; for double the worth of a hired servant hath he been to thee, &#91;in&#93; serving thee six years; and Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="15.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God will bless thee in all that thou doest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12; Leviticus 27:26"> Every firstling that is born among thy kine and among thy sheep that is a male, thou shalt hallow to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="15.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God: thou shalt do no work with the firstling of thy kine, nor shear the firstling of thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Thou shalt eat it before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="15.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, year by year, in the place which Jehovah will choose, thou and thy household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:7–8; Leviticus 22:19–25"> But if there be a defect therein, &#91;if it be&#93; lame, or blind, &#91;or have&#93; any evil defect, thou shalt not sacrifice it to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="15.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21–22; Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> In thy gates shalt thou eat it; the unclean and the clean &#91;shall eat it&#93; alike, as the gazelle and as the hart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Leviticus 7:26; Deuteronomy 12:23"> Only thou shalt not eat the blood thereof: thou shalt pour it upon the earth as water.</span>
