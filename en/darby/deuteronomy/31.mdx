<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> and he said unto them, I am a hundred and twenty years old this day, I can no more go out and come in; and Jehovah hath said unto me, Thou shalt not go over this Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 3:28"> Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="31.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, he will go over before thee, he will destroy these nations from before thee, that thou mayest take possession of them: Joshua, he shall go over before thee, as Jehovah hath said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:28–31; Deuteronomy 2:33; Deuteronomy 3:3–11"> And Jehovah will do to them as he did to Sihon and to Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land; whom he destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 23:32–33"> And when Jehovah giveth them up before you, ye shall do to them according to all the commandment which I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10; Psalms 27:14"> Be strong and courageous, fear them not, neither be afraid of them; for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="31.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, he it is that goeth with thee; he will not leave thee, nor forsake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:38"> And Moses called to Joshua, and said to him in the sight of all Israel, Be strong and courageous, for thou must go with this people into the land which Jehovah hath sworn unto their fathers to give them; and thou shalt cause them to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Deuteronomy 31:6; Joshua 1:9"> And Jehovah, he it is that goeth before thee: he will be with thee; he will not leave thee, nor forsake thee; fear not, neither be dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Joshua 3:3; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And Moses wrote this law, and delivered it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who bore the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, and to all the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34–43; Deuteronomy 15:1–2"> And Moses commanded them, saying, At the end of every seven years, at the set time of the year of release, at the feast of tabernacles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:2; Joshua 8:34–35; Deuteronomy 16:16–17"> when all Israel cometh to appear before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="31.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God in the place which he will choose, thou shalt read this law before all Israel in their ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Psalms 34:11–14; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Gather the people together, the men, and the women, and the children, and thy stranger that is within thy gates, that they may hear, and that they may learn, and fear Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="31.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and take heed to do all the words of this law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:2; Deuteronomy 6:7; Proverbs 22:6"> and that their children who do not know it may hear it and learn, that they may fear Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="31.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, as long as ye live in the land, whereunto ye pass over the Jordan to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Deuteronomy 31:23; Job 2:1"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Lo, the days are near for thee to die; call Joshua, and present yourselves at the tent of meeting, that I may give him a charge. And Moses and Joshua went and presented themselves at the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 40:38; Psalms 99:7; Exodus 33:9–10"> And Jehovah appeared at the tent in the pillar of cloud; and the pillar of cloud stood over the entrance to the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Judges 10:13; Judges 2:12"> And Jehovah said to Moses, Behold, thou shalt sleep with thy fathers; and this people will rise up, and go a whoring after the strange<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="31.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods of the land into which they enter, and will forsake me, and break my covenant which I have made with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:42; Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 104:29"> And my anger shall be kindled against them in that day, and I will forsake them, and I will hide my face from them, and they shall be devoured, and many evils and troubles shall befall them, and they will say in that day, Have not these evils befallen me because my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="31.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God is not in my midst?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> And I will entirely hide my face in that day for all the evils that they have wrought, because they turned unto other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="31.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 31:21–22; Deuteronomy 31:26"> And now, write ye this song, and teach it to the children of Israel; put it in their mouths, that this song may be a witness for me against the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Nehemiah 9:25–26"> For I shall bring them into the land which I swore unto their fathers, which floweth with milk and honey; and they will eat and fill themselves, and wax fat, and will turn unto other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="31.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>gods, and serve them, and despise me, and break my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:3; Psalms 139:2; John 2:24–25"> And it shall come to pass, when many evils and troubles have befallen them, that this song shall testify against them as a witness; for it shall not be forgotten out of the mouths of their seed; for I know their imagination which they are forming already this day, before I bring them into the land which I have sworn &#91;unto them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And Moses wrote this song the same day, and taught it to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:14; Acts 7:45"> And &#91;Jehovah&#93; commanded Joshua the son of Nun, and said, Be strong and courageous; for thou shalt bring the children of Israel into the land which I have sworn unto them; and I will be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And it came to pass, when Moses had ended writing the words of this law in a book, until their conclusion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9"> that Moses commanded the Levites, who bore the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; 2 Kings 22:8–11; Galatians 2:19"> Take this book of the law, and put it at the side of the ark of the covenant of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="31.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, that it may be there for a witness against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:20; Deuteronomy 9:24; Acts 7:51"> for I know thy rebellion, and thy stiff neck. Lo, while I am yet alive with you this day, ye have been rebellious against Jehovah; and how much more after my death!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 4:26; Deuteronomy 30:19"> Gather to me all the elders of your tribes, and your officers, that I may speak these words in their ears, and take heaven and earth to witness against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:19; Deuteronomy 32:5; Luke 21:24"> For I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt yourselves, and will turn aside from the way which I have commanded you; and mischief will befall you at the end of days; because ye do evil in the sight of Jehovah, to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2; Deuteronomy 4:5; John 12:49"> And Moses spoke in the ears of the whole congregation of Israel the words of this song, until their conclusion.</span>
