<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Romans 9:8; Galatians 3:26"> Ye are sons of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="14.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God: ye shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for a dead person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Leviticus 20:26; Exodus 19:5–6"> For thou art a holy people unto Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="14.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and thee hath Jehovah chosen for a people of possession unto himself, out of all the peoples that are upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:14; Romans 14:14; Leviticus 20:25"> Thou shalt not eat any abominable thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:2–45; Acts 10:14; 1 Kings 4:23"> These are the beasts which ye shall eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> the hart, and the gazelle, and the stag, and the wild goat, and the dishon and the oryx, and the wild sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 18:1; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Psalms 1:1–2"> And every beast that hath cloven hoofs, and the feet quite split open into double hoofs, &#91;and&#93; which cheweth the cud, among the beasts, that ye shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:22–23; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Only these ye shall not eat of those that chew the cud, or of those with hoofs cloven and split open: the camel, and the hare, and the rock-badger; for they chew the cud, but have not cloven hoofs — they shall be unclean unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 11:26–27; Isaiah 66:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> and the swine, for it hath cloven hoofs, yet cheweth not the cud — it shall be unclean unto you. Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 11:9–12"> These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever hath fins and scales shall ye eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> but whatsoever hath not fins and scales ye shall not eat: it shall be unclean unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All clean birds shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 11:13–19"> But these are they of which ye shall not eat: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the sea-eagle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the falcon, and the kite, and the black kite after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and every raven after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 30:29"> and the female ostrich, and the male ostrich, and the sea-gull, and the hawk after its kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the owl, and the ibis and the swan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the pelican, and the carrion vulture, and the gannet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the stork, and the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Leviticus 11:20–23"> And every winged crawling thing shall be unclean unto you; they shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> All clean fowls shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26; Leviticus 22:8"> Ye shall eat of no carcase; thou shalt give it unto the stranger that is within thy gates, that he may eat it, or sell it unto a foreigner; for thou art a holy people to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="14.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God. Thou shalt not boil a kid in its mother's milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> Thou shalt truly tithe all the increase of thy seed, the produce of the field, year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Deuteronomy 4:10; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> And thou shalt eat before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="14.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, in the place which he will choose to cause his name to dwell there, the tithe of thy corn, of thy new wine, and of thine oil, and the firstlings of thy herds and of thy flocks; that thou mayest learn to fear Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="14.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 11:24"> And if the way be too long for thee, so that thou art not able to carry it, because the place is too far from thee, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="14.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God will choose to set his name there, when Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="14.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God blesseth thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> then shalt thou give it for money, and bind the money together in thy hand, and go to the place which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="14.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God will choose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Deuteronomy 12:7; Matthew 21:12"> and thou shalt give the money for whatever thy soul desireth, for oxen, or for sheep, or for wine, or for strong drink, or for whatever thy soul asketh of thee; and thou shalt eat there before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="14.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, and thou shalt rejoice, thou, and thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 14:29; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And thou shalt not forsake the Levite that is within thy gates; for he hath no portion nor inheritance with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:22; Amos 4:4; Deuteronomy 26:12–15"> At the end of three years thou shalt bring forth all the tithe of thine increase the same year, and shalt lay it up within thy gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 14:27; Proverbs 19:17"> and the Levite — for he hath no portion nor inheritance with thee — and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, that are within thy gates, shall come, and shall eat and be satisfied; that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="14.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God may bless thee in all the work of thy hand which thou doest.</span>
</div>
