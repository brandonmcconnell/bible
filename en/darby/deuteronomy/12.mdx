<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 8:40; Job 7:1; Deuteronomy 4:19"> These are the statutes and ordinances, which ye shall take heed to do in the land, which Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="12.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God of thy fathers is giving thee to possess it, all the days that ye live upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; 2 Kings 16:4; Numbers 33:51–52"> Ye shall utterly destroy all the places wherein the nations which ye shall dispossess have served their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="12.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>gods, upon the high mountains, and upon the hills, and under every green tree;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 2:2; Zechariah 13:2; Numbers 33:52"> and ye shall break down their altars, and shatter their statues, and burn their Asherahs with fire; and ye shall hew down the graven images of their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods, and ye shall destroy the names of them out of that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 20:18; Deuteronomy 16:21–22"> Ye shall not do so unto Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="12.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:11; Deuteronomy 26:2; 2 Chronicles 7:12"> but unto the place which Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="12.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God will choose out of all your tribes to set his name there, his habitation shall ye seek, and thither thou shalt come;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:19–20; Malachi 3:10; Leviticus 27:32–33"> and thither ye shall bring your burnt-offerings and your sacrifices, and your tithes, and the heave-offering of your hand, and your vows, and your voluntary-offerings, and the firstlings of your kine and of your sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:18; Leviticus 23:40; Deuteronomy 14:26"> and ye shall eat there before Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="12.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, and ye shall rejoice, ye and your households, in all the business of your hand, wherein Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="12.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God hath blessed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 21:25; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 21:2"> Ye shall not do after all that we do here this day, each one whatever is right in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Psalms 95:11; Deuteronomy 25:19"> For ye are not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="12.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:11; Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 3:27"> But when ye have gone over the Jordan, and dwell in the land which Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="12.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God causeth you to inherit, and when he hath given you rest from all your enemies round about, and ye dwell in safety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 15:20; Deuteronomy 17:8"> then there shall be a place which Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="12.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God will choose to cause his name to dwell there; thither shall ye bring all that I command you: your burnt-offerings, and your sacrifices, your tithes, and the heave-offering of your hand, and all your choice vows which ye shall vow to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 10:9; Deuteronomy 26:12"> And ye shall rejoice before Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="12.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, ye, and your sons, and your daughters, and your bondmen, and your handmaids, and the Levite that is within your gates; for he hath no portion nor inheritance with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28–32; 1 Kings 15:34; 2 Chronicles 15:17"> Take heed to thyself that thou offer not thy burnt-offerings in every place that thou seest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:11; 2 Corinthians 5:19; Deuteronomy 12:5"> but in the place which Jehovah will choose in one of thy tribes, there thou shalt offer thy burnt-offerings, and there thou shalt do all that I command thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:5; Deuteronomy 15:22–23; Deuteronomy 14:26"> Nevertheless, according to all the desire of thy soul thou mayest slay and eat flesh in all thy gates, according to the blessing of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="12.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God which he hath given thee: the unclean and the clean may eat thereof, as of the gazelle, and the hart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:23–24"> Only, ye shall not eat the blood; ye shall pour it upon the earth as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:14; Deuteronomy 12:6; Deuteronomy 14:22–29"> Thou mayest not eat within thy gates the tithe of thy corn, or of thy new wine, or of thine oil, or the firstlings of thy kine or of thy sheep, nor any of thy vows which thou vowest, nor thy voluntary-offerings, nor the heave-offering of thy hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5"> but before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="12.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God shalt thou eat them in the place which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="12.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God will choose, thou and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy bondman, and thy handmaid, and the Levite that is within thy gates; and thou shalt rejoice before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="12.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God in all the business of thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:4–21; Nehemiah 10:34–39; Deuteronomy 14:27–29"> Take heed to thyself that thou forsake not the Levite all the days thou shalt be in thy land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8; Deuteronomy 11:24; Exodus 34:24"> When Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="12.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God shall enlarge thy border, as he promised thee, and thou say, I will eat flesh, because thy soul longeth to eat flesh, thou mayest eat flesh, according to all the desire of thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; 1 Kings 14:21; Deuteronomy 16:11"> If the place which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="12.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God will choose to set his name there be too far from thee, then thou shalt slay of thy kine and of thy sheep which Jehovah hath given thee, as I have commanded thee, and thou shalt eat in thy gates according to all the desire of thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> Even as the gazelle and the hart is eaten, so thou shalt eat them: the unclean and the clean alike may eat of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 17:13–14"> Only, be sure that thou eat not the blood; for the blood is the life, and thou mayest not eat the life with the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Deuteronomy 15:23"> thou shalt not eat it; thou shalt pour it upon the earth as water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 11:38; Deuteronomy 4:40; Isaiah 3:10"> thou shalt not eat it; that it may go well with thee, and with thy children after thee, when thou shalt do what is right in the eyes of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Numbers 5:9–10; Deuteronomy 12:11"> But thy hallowed things which thou hast, and what thou hast vowed, thou shalt take, and come to the place which Jehovah will choose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 1:9"> and thou shalt offer thy burnt-offerings, the flesh and the blood, upon the altar of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="12.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">r</a></sup>God; and the blood of thy sacrifices shall be poured out upon the altar of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="12.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">s</a></sup>God, and the flesh shalt thou eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; John 15:14; John 15:10"> Take heed to hear all these words which I command thee, that it may be well with thee, and with thy children after thee for ever, when thou doest what is good and right in the eyes of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="12.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">t</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 23:4; Deuteronomy 19:1; Deuteronomy 9:3"> When Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="12.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">u</a></sup>God cutteth off from before thee the nations whither thou goest, to take possession of them, and thou hast dispossessed them, and dwellest in their land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:2; Ezekiel 20:32; Deuteronomy 7:16"> take heed to thyself that thou be not ensnared &#91;to follow&#93; after them, after that they are destroyed from before thee; and that thou inquire not after their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="12.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">v</a></sup>gods, saying, How did these nations serve their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="12.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">w</a></sup>gods? even so will I do likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:35; Deuteronomy 18:9–10; 2 Kings 17:15–17"> Thou shalt not do so to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="12.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">x</a></sup>God; for every &#91;thing that is&#93; abomination to Jehovah, which he hateth, have they done unto their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="12.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">y</a></sup>gods; for even their sons and their daughters have they burned in the fire to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="12.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">z</a></sup>gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19; Joshua 1:7"> Everything that I command you, ye shall take heed to do it; thou shalt not add thereto, nor take from it.</span>
