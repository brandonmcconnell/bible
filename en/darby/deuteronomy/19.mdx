<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29; Deuteronomy 6:10; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> When Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="19.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath cut off the nations whose land Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="19.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God giveth thee, and thou hast dispossessed them, and dwellest in their cities and in their houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2–7; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> thou shalt separate three cities for thyself in the midst of thy land, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="19.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God giveth thee to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Hebrews 12:13; Isaiah 35:8"> Thou shalt prepare thee the way, and divide the territory of thy land, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="19.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God giveth thee to inherit, into three parts, so that every slayer may flee thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 35:15–24; Deuteronomy 4:42; Isaiah 30:33"> And this is the case of the slayer who shall flee thither that he may live: he that smiteth his neighbour unwittingly, whom he hated not previously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Proverbs 27:12; 2 Kings 6:5–7"> as when he goeth into the wood with his neighbour to hew wood, and his hand fetcheth a stroke with the axe to cut down the tree, and the iron slippeth from the handle, and lighteth upon his neighbour, that he die; such an one shall flee unto one of these cities, and live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; 2 Samuel 14:7; Deuteronomy 21:22"> lest the avenger of blood pursue the manslayer, while his heart is hot, and overtake him, because the way is long, and smite him mortally; whereas he was not worthy of death, since he hated him not previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore I command thee, saying, Thou shalt separate three cities for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:20; Exodus 34:24; Exodus 23:31"> And if Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="19.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God enlarge thy border, as he hath sworn unto thy fathers, and give thee all the land which he promised to give unto thy fathers</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 20:7–8; Deuteronomy 11:22–25; Deuteronomy 12:32"> (if thou keep all this commandment to do it, which I command thee this day, to love Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="19.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, and to walk in his ways continually), then shalt thou add three cities more for thyself to these three,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 27:4–5"> that innocent blood be not shed in the midst of thy land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="19.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God giveth thee for an inheritance, and blood come not upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:16–21; Deuteronomy 27:24; Genesis 9:6"> But if a man hate his neighbour, and lie in wait for him, and rise up against him, and smite him mortally that he die, and he flee into one of these cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; 1 Kings 2:5–6"> then the elders of his city shall send and fetch him thence, and deliver him into the hand of the avenger of blood, that he may die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 2:31; Deuteronomy 21:9; Deuteronomy 7:16"> Thine eye shall not spare him; and thou shalt put away innocent blood from Israel, that it may be well with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10"> Thou shalt not remove thy neighbour's landmark, which they of old time have fixed in thine inheritance, which thou shalt inherit in the land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="19.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God giveth thee to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Deuteronomy 17:6; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, and for any sin, in any sin that he sinneth: at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall a matter be established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11; Exodus 23:1–7"> If an unrighteous witness rise up against any man to testify against him of an offence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:9; Deuteronomy 21:5; Malachi 2:7"> then both the men between whom the controversy is shall stand before Jehovah, before the priests and the judges that shall be in those days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 17:4; 2 Chronicles 19:6–7"> and the judges shall make thorough inquiry; and if the witness be a false witness, and he have testified falsely against his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9; Daniel 6:24"> then shall ye do unto him as he had thought to have done unto his brother; and thou shalt put evil away from thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Deuteronomy 17:13; Deuteronomy 13:11"> And those that remain shall hear, and fear, and shall henceforth commit no more any such evil in thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:38–39; Exodus 21:23–25; Deuteronomy 19:13"> And thine eye shall not spare: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.</span>
