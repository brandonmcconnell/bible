<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:33–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> And we turned, and went up the way to Bashan; and Og the king of Bashan came out against us, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Chronicles 20:17; Numbers 21:23–25"> And Jehovah said to me, Fear him not; for into thy hand have I given him, and all his people, and his land; and thou shalt do unto him as thou didst unto Sihon the king of the Amorites, who dwelt at Heshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 21:35; Joshua 13:12; Joshua 13:30"> And Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God gave into our hand Og the king of Bashan also, and all his people; and we smote him until none was left to him remaining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 12:4; Joshua 13:30–31"> And we took all his cities at that time: there was not a town which we took not from them, sixty cities, the whole region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Hebrews 11:30"> All these cities were fortified with high walls, gates, and bars; besides unwalled towns very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:34; Psalms 135:10–12; Deuteronomy 2:24"> And we utterly destroyed them, as we had done to Sihon the king of Heshbon, utterly destroying every city, men, women and little ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:35; Joshua 8:27; Joshua 11:11–14"> But all the cattle and the spoil of the cities we took as booty for ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:9–12; Joshua 12:2–6; Numbers 32:33–42"> And we took at that time the land out of the hand of the two kings of the Amorites, that were on this side the Jordan, from the river Arnon to mount Hermon</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:23; Psalms 29:6; Ezekiel 27:5"> (the Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir):</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 13:31; Joshua 13:11–12; Deuteronomy 4:49"> all the cities of the plateau, and all Gilead, and all Bashan, as far as Salchah and Edrei, the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Samuel 12:26"> For only Og the king of Bashan remained of the residue of giants: behold, his bedstead was a bedstead of iron; is it not in Rabbah of the children of Ammon? its length was nine cubits, and its breadth four cubits, after the cubit of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:32–38; Joshua 13:8–28"> And this land we took in possession at that time. From Aroer, which is by the river Arnon, and the half of mount Gilead, and its cities, I gave to the Reubenites and to the Gadites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:39–42; Joshua 13:29–32; 1 Chronicles 5:23–26"> and the rest of Gilead, and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to half the tribe of Manasseh. (The whole region of Argob, even all Bashan, is called a land of giants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Joshua 13:13; 2 Samuel 3:3"> Jair the son of Manasseh took the whole region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and Maachathites, and called Bashan after his own name, Havoth-Jair, to this day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:39–40; Joshua 17:1–3; Joshua 22:7"> And I gave Gilead to Machir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 2:37; 2 Samuel 24:5"> And to the Reubenites and to the Gadites I gave from Gilead even to the river Arnon, the middle of the ravine and its border, as far as the river Jabbok, the border of the children of Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Genesis 14:3; Joshua 3:16"> the plain also, and the Jordan, and &#91;its&#93; border from Chinnereth as far as the sea of the plain, the salt sea, under the slopes of Pisgah eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–24; Joshua 4:12–13; Joshua 1:12–15"> And I commanded you at that time, saying, Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="3.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God hath given you this land to take possession of it: ye shall pass over armed before your brethren the children of Israel, all &#91;who are&#93; combatants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Only your wives, and your little ones, and your cattle, — I know that ye have much cattle, — shall abide in your cities which I have given you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 22:4; Joshua 22:8"> until Jehovah give rest to your brethren, as well as to you, and they also take possession of the land that Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="3.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God giveth them beyond the Jordan; then shall ye return, each man to his possession, which I have given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 27:18–23; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Psalms 9:10"> And I commanded Joshua at that time, saying, Thine eyes have seen all that Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="3.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God hath done to these two kings: so will Jehovah do to all the kingdoms to which thou shalt go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 20:4; Deuteronomy 1:30"> Ye shall not fear them; for Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, he will fight for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And I besought Jehovah at that time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 11:2; Psalms 71:19"> Lord Jehovah, thou hast begun to shew thy servant thy greatness, and thy powerful hand; for what<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="3.24 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">f</a></sup>God is in the heavens or in the earth that can do like to thy works, and like to thy might?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:21–22; Ezekiel 20:6; Exodus 3:8"> Let me go over, I pray thee, and see the good land that is beyond the Jordan, that goodly mountain, and Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Deuteronomy 31:2; 1 Chronicles 22:7–9"> But Jehovah was wroth with me on your account, and did not hear me; and Jehovah said to me, Let it suffice thee; speak no more unto me of this matter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Genesis 13:14–15"> Go up to the top of Pisgah, and lift up thine eyes westward, and northward, and southward, and eastward, and behold it with thine eyes; for thou shalt not go over this Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:3; Deuteronomy 31:7; Deuteronomy 1:38"> But charge Joshua, and encourage him and strengthen him; for he shall go over before this people, and he shall put them in possession of the land which thou shalt see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:6; Deuteronomy 4:46; Numbers 25:3"> And we abode in the valley opposite to Beth-Peor.</span>
