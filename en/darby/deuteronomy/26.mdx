<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14; Deuteronomy 13:9; Deuteronomy 6:1–10"> And it shall be when thou comest into the land that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="26.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God giveth thee for an inheritance, and possessest it, and dwellest therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 23:16; Ezekiel 44:30"> that thou shalt take of the first of all the fruit of the ground, which thou shalt bring of thy land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="26.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God giveth thee, and shalt put it in a basket, and shalt go unto the place that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="26.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God will choose to cause his name to dwell there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 105:9–10; Deuteronomy 19:17; Genesis 26:3"> and thou shalt come unto the priest that shall be in those days, and say unto him, I profess this day unto Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="26.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, that I am come unto the land that Jehovah swore unto our fathers to give us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Matthew 5:23–24; Hebrews 13:10–12"> And the priest shall take the basket out of thy hand, and set it down before the altar of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="26.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 31:20"> And thou shalt speak and say before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="26.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, A perishing Aramaean was my father, and he went down to Egypt with a few, and sojourned there, and became there a nation, great, mighty, and populous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 5:23"> And the Egyptians evil-entreated us, and afflicted us, and laid upon us hard bondage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–4; Ephesians 3:20–21; Exodus 3:9"> and we cried to Jehovah, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="26.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God of our fathers, and Jehovah heard our voice, and looked on our affliction, and our labour, and our oppression;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Deuteronomy 5:15; Psalms 78:12–13"> and Jehovah brought us forth out of Egypt with a powerful hand, and with a stretched-out arm, and with great terribleness, and with signs, and with wonders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Psalms 105:44; Psalms 107:7–8"> and he hath brought us into this place, and hath given us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Deuteronomy 18:4; Revelation 22:9"> And now, behold, I have brought the first of the fruits of the land, which thou, Jehovah, hast given me. And thou shalt set it down before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="26.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, and worship before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="26.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7; Philippians 4:4"> And thou shalt rejoice in all the good that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="26.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God hath given to thee, and to thy house, thou, and the Levite, and the stranger that is in thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; Numbers 18:24; Philippians 4:18–19"> When thou hast made an end of tithing all the tithes of thy produce in the third year, the year of tithing, thou shalt give it to the Levite, to the stranger, to the fatherless, and to the widow, that they may eat in thy gates, and be filled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:176; Psalms 119:153; Psalms 119:141"> and thou shalt say before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="26.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, I have brought away the hallowed things out of the house, and also have given them to the Levite, and to the stranger, to the fatherless, and to the widow, according to all thy commandment which thou hast commanded me; I have not transgressed nor forgotten &#91;any&#93; of thy commandments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20; Leviticus 21:11; Hosea 9:4"> I have not eaten thereof in my mourning, neither have I brought away thereof in uncleanness, nor given thereof for a dead person; I have hearkened to the voice of Jehovah my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="26.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God; I have done according to all that thou hast commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Zechariah 2:13; Jeremiah 31:23"> Look down from thy holy habitation, from the heavens, and bless thy people Israel, and the land that thou hast given us as thou didst swear unto our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:1; John 14:15; 1 John 5:2–3"> This day Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="26.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God hath commanded thee to do these statutes and ordinances; and thou shalt keep and do them with all thy heart and with all thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Isaiah 12:2; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> Thou hast this day accepted Jehovah to be thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="26.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God, and to walk in his ways, and keep his statutes, and his commandments, and his ordinances, and to hearken unto his voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:9"> and Jehovah hath accepted thee this day to be a people of possession to him, as he hath told thee, and that thou shouldest keep all his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 28:1"> so that he should make thee high above all the nations which he hath made, in praise and in name and in honour; and that thou shouldest be a holy people to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="26.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God, as he hath said.</span>
