<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:25–28; 1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8"> If there arise among you a prophet, or one that dreameth dreams, and he give thee a sign or a wonder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Matthew 7:22–23; Revelation 13:13–14"> and the sign or the wonder come to pass that he told unto thee, when he said, Let us go after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="13.2 Elohim">a</a></sup>gods, whom thou hast not known, and let us serve them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Psalms 66:10"> — thou shalt not hearken unto the words of that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams; for Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="13.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God proveth you, to know whether ye love Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="13.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Deuteronomy 10:20; 1 Corinthians 6:17"> Ye shall walk after Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="13.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, and ye shall fear him, and his commandments shall ye keep, and his voice shall ye hear; and ye shall serve him, and unto him shall ye cleave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:13; Deuteronomy 18:20; Jeremiah 14:15"> And that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death; for he hath spoken revolt against Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="13.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God who brought you out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, — to draw thee out of the way that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="13.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God commanded thee to walk in; and thou shalt put evil away from thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; 2 Peter 2:1; Ephesians 4:14"> If thy brother, the son of thy mother, or thy son, or thy daughter, or the wife of thy bosom, or thy friend, who is to thee as thy soul, entice thee secretly, saying, Let us go and serve other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="13.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>gods (whom thou hast not known, thou, nor thy fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="13.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>gods of the peoples which are round about you, near unto thee, or far from thee, from one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Proverbs 1:10; Deuteronomy 7:16"> thou shalt not consent unto him, nor hearken unto him; neither shall thine eye spare him, neither shalt thou pity him, neither shalt thou screen him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Luke 14:26; Matthew 10:37"> but thou shalt in any case kill him: thy hand shall be the first against him to put him to death, and afterwards the hands of all the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; Leviticus 24:14–16; Exodus 20:2"> and thou shalt stone him with stones, that he die; for he hath sought to draw thee away from Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="13.10 Elohim">i</a></sup>God who brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:20; Deuteronomy 17:13; 1 Timothy 5:20"> and all Israel shall hear, and fear, and shall do no more any such wicked thing as this in thy midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–17; Joshua 22:11–34"> If in one of thy cities, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="13.12 Elohim">j</a></sup>God hath given thee to dwell there, thou hearest, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Deuteronomy 13:2; 2 Corinthians 6:15"> There are men, children of Belial, gone out from among you, and they have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, Let us go and serve other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="13.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>gods, whom ye have not known;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:4; Isaiah 11:3–4; John 7:24"> then shalt thou inquire, and make search, and ask diligently; and if it be truth, &#91;and&#93; the thing be certain, that this abomination hath happened in the midst of thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Leviticus 27:28; Deuteronomy 7:2"> thou shalt surely smite the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword, devoting it to destruction, and all that is therein, and the cattle thereof, with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:2; Joshua 8:28; Isaiah 25:2"> And all the spoil of it shalt thou gather into the midst of the open place thereof, and shalt burn the city with fire, and all the spoil thereof, wholly to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="13.16 Elohim">l</a></sup>God; and it shall be a heap for ever; it shall not be built again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 26:4; Deuteronomy 7:26; Joshua 7:26"> And thou shalt not let anything cleave to thy hand of the devoted thing; that Jehovah may turn from the fierceness of his anger, and shew thee mercy, and have compassion upon thee, and multiply thee, as he hath sworn unto thy fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:28; Matthew 7:21; Deuteronomy 12:25"> when thou hearkenest to the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="13.18 Elohim">m</a></sup>God, to keep all his commandments which I command thee this day, that thou mayest do what is right in the eyes of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="13.18 Elohim">n</a></sup>God.</span>
</div>
