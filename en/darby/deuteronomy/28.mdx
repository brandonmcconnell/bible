<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Exodus 23:22; Leviticus 26:3–13"> And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="28.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, to take heed to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="28.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God will set thee supreme above all nations of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:15; 1 Timothy 4:8"> and all these blessings shall come on thee and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="28.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:12–15; Genesis 39:5; Haggai 2:19"> Blessed shalt thou be in the city, and blessed shalt thou be in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 49:25; Deuteronomy 7:13"> Blessed shall be the fruit of thy womb, and the fruit of thy ground, and the fruit of thy cattle, the offspring of thy kine, and the increase of thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Blessed shall be thy basket and thy kneading-trough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 121:8; Numbers 27:17; 2 Chronicles 1:10"> Blessed shalt thou be in thy coming in, and blessed shalt thou be in thy going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:38–41; Leviticus 26:7–8; Psalms 89:23"> Jehovah will give up, smitten before thee, thine enemies that rise up against thee; they shall come out against thee one way, and by seven ways shall they flee before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Leviticus 26:10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Jehovah will command blessing on thee in thy granaries, and in all the business of thy hand; and he will bless thee in the land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="28.8 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18–19"> Jehovah will establish thee unto himself a holy people as he hath sworn unto thee, if thou keep the commandments of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="28.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, and walk in his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Deuteronomy 11:25; Jeremiah 33:9"> And all peoples of the earth shall see that thou art called by the name of Jehovah, and they shall be afraid of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:9; Proverbs 10:22; Leviticus 26:9"> And Jehovah will give thee abundance of good, in the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy ground, in the land that Jehovah swore unto thy fathers to give thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:6; Joel 2:23–24; Deuteronomy 15:10"> Jehovah will open to thee his good treasure, the heavens, to give rain unto thy land in its season, and to bless all the work of thy hand; and thou shalt lend unto many nations, but thou shalt not borrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1; Deuteronomy 4:6–9; Philippians 1:27"> And Jehovah will make thee the head, and not the tail; and thou shalt be above only, and thou shalt not be beneath; if thou hearken unto the commandments of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="28.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, which I command thee this day, to keep and to do them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Deuteronomy 5:32; Deuteronomy 11:16"> and if thou turn not aside from any of the words that I command thee this day, to the right hand or to the left, to go after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="28.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>gods to serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Deuteronomy 28:2; Deuteronomy 29:20"> But it shall come to pass if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="28.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, to take heed to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day, that all these curses shall come upon thee and overtake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Malachi 3:9–12; Proverbs 3:33; Joel 1:4"> Cursed shalt thou be in the city, and cursed shalt thou be in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:5; Psalms 69:22"> Cursed shall be thy basket and thy kneading-trough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Leviticus 26:19–20; Job 18:16–19"> Cursed shall be the fruit of thy womb, and the fruit of thy ground, the offspring of thy kine, and the increase of thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Judges 5:6–7; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> Cursed shalt thou be in thy coming in, and cursed shalt thou be in thy going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15; Malachi 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:26"> Jehovah will send upon thee cursing, confusion, and rebuke, in all the business of thy hand which thou doest, until thou be destroyed and until thou perish quickly, because of the wickedness of thy doings, whereby thou hast forsaken me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Jeremiah 24:10; Jeremiah 16:4"> Jehovah will make the pestilence cleave unto thee, until he have consumed thee from off the land whither thou goest to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Amos 4:9; Leviticus 26:16; Haggai 2:17"> Jehovah will smite thee with consumption, and with fever, and with inflammation, and with burning ague, and with drought, and with blight, and with mildew, and they shall pursue thee until thou perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 14:1–6; 1 Kings 18:2"> And thy heavens which are over thy head shall be brass, and the earth which is under thee, iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Isaiah 5:24; Amos 4:11"> Jehovah will give as the rain of thy land powder and dust; from the heavens shall it come down upon thee until thou be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 30:17; Ezekiel 23:46; Jeremiah 24:9"> Jehovah will give thee up smitten before thine enemies; thou shalt go out against them one way, and by seven ways shalt thou flee before them; and thou shalt be driven hither and thither into all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 7:33"> And thy carcase shall be meat unto all the fowl of the air, and unto the beasts of the earth, and there shall be no man to scare them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Deuteronomy 28:35; Leviticus 21:20"> Jehovah will smite thee with the ulcers of Egypt, and with boils, and with the scab, and with the itch, whereof thou canst not be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Isaiah 19:11–17; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Jehovah will smite thee with madness, and with blindness, and with astonishment of heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 59:10; Job 5:14; Judges 6:1–6"> and thou shalt grope at noonday, as the blind gropeth in darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways; and thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled continually, and there shall be none to save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Jeremiah 8:10; Job 31:10"> Thou shalt betroth a wife, and another man shall lie with her; thou shalt build a house, and thou shalt not dwell therein; thou shalt plant a vineyard, and shalt not eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 1:14–15; Judges 6:1"> Thine ox shall be slaughtered before thine eyes, and thou shalt not eat thereof; thine ass shall be snatched away from before thy face, and shall not return to thee; thy sheep shall be given unto thine enemies, and thou shalt have none to recover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joel 3:6; Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 17:5"> Thy sons and thy daughters shall be given unto another people, and thine eyes shall look, and languish for them all the day long; and there shall be no power in thy hand &#91;to help it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:29–30; Isaiah 1:7"> The fruit of thy ground and all thy labour, shall a people that thou knowest not eat up; and thou shalt be only oppressed and crushed continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 16:10–11; Deuteronomy 28:68; Isaiah 33:14"> And thou shalt be mad through the sight of thine eyes which thou shalt see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27; Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 3:17"> Jehovah will smite thee in the knees and in the legs with evil ulcers, whereof thou canst not be healed, from the sole of thy foot unto the top of thy head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 4:28; 2 Kings 25:11"> Jehovah will bring thee, and thy king whom thou shalt set over thee, unto a nation that neither thou nor thy fathers have known, and there shalt thou serve other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="28.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>gods, wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; 1 Kings 9:7–8; Jeremiah 24:9"> And thou shalt become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword, among all the peoples whither Jehovah shall lead thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Micah 6:15; Joel 1:4"> Thou shalt carry much seed out into the field, and shalt gather little in; for the locust shall devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Joel 2:2–4; Jonah 4:7"> Thou shalt plant and till vineyards, but shalt drink no wine, nor gather &#91;the fruit&#93;; for the worms shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Psalms 23:5; Psalms 104:15"> Olive-trees shalt thou have throughout all thy borders, but thou shalt not anoint thyself with oil; for thine olive-tree shall cast its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:32; Lamentations 1:5; 2 Kings 24:14"> Sons and daughters shalt thou beget, but thou shalt not have them &#91;to be with thee&#93;; for they shall go into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 7:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:38–39"> All thy trees and the fruit of thy ground shall the locust possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:13; 1 Samuel 13:19–23; 2 Kings 17:20"> The sojourner that is in thy midst shall rise above thee higher and higher, and thou shalt sink down lower and lower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Lamentations 1:5"> He shall lend to thee, but thou shalt not lend to him: he shall be the head, and thou shalt be the tail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15; Isaiah 1:20; Jeremiah 24:9–10"> And all these curses shall come upon thee, and shall pursue thee, and overtake thee, until thou be destroyed; because thou hearkenedst not unto the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="28.45 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, to keep his commandments and his statutes which he commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Deuteronomy 28:37; Ezekiel 14:8"> And they shall be upon thee for a sign and for a wonder, and upon thy seed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7–12; Nehemiah 9:35–37"> Because thou servedst not Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="28.47 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God with joyfulness, and with gladness of heart, for the abundance of everything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:13–14; Lamentations 5:2–6; Ezekiel 4:16–17"> thou shalt serve thine enemies whom Jehovah will send against thee, in hunger, and in thirst, and in nakedness, and in want of everything; and he shall put a yoke of iron upon thy neck, until he have destroyed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Lamentations 4:19; Jeremiah 49:22; Jeremiah 48:40"> Jehovah will bring a nation against thee from afar, from the end of the earth, like as the eagle flieth, a nation whose tongue thou understandest not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Luke 21:23–24"> a nation of fierce countenance, which regardeth not the person of the old, nor is kind to the young;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Isaiah 62:8; Leviticus 26:26"> and he shall eat the fruit of thy cattle, and the fruit of thy ground, until thou be destroyed; for he shall not leave thee corn, new wine, or oil, offspring of thy kine, or increase of thy sheep, until he have destroyed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:4–7; Ezekiel 4:1–8; Matthew 24:15–16"> And he shall besiege thee in all thy gates, until thy high and strong walls wherein thou trustedst come down, throughout all thy land; and he shall besiege thee in all thy gates in all thy land, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="28.52 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God hath given thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Jeremiah 19:9; Leviticus 26:29"> And in the siege, and in the straitness, wherewith thine enemies shall distress thee, thou shalt eat the fruit of thine own body, the flesh of thy sons and of thy daughters whom Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="28.53 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God hath given thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:9; Matthew 20:15; Deuteronomy 13:6"> The eye of the man in thy midst that is tender and very luxurious shall be evil towards his brother, and the wife of his bosom, and the residue of his children which he hath left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2; Jeremiah 52:6; Jeremiah 5:10"> so that he will not give to any of them of the flesh of his children that he eateth, because he hath nothing left him in the siege and in the straitness wherewith thine enemies shall distress thee in all thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:54; Isaiah 3:16; Lamentations 4:3–6"> The eye of the tender and luxurious woman in thy midst who would not attempt to set the sole of her foot upon the ground from luxuriousness and from tenderness, shall be evil toward the husband of her bosom, and her son, and her daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 49:10"> because of her afterbirth which hath come out between her feet, and her children whom she shall bear; for she shall secretly eat them for want of everything in the siege and in the straitness wherewith thine enemy shall distress thee in thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Jeremiah 7:9–10; Malachi 1:14"> If thou wilt not take heed to do all the words of this law that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and fearful name, JEHOVAH THY GOD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Mark 13:19; Deuteronomy 28:46; Daniel 9:12"> then Jehovah will make thy plagues wonderful, and the plagues of thy seed, great and persistent plagues and evil and persistent sicknesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 28:27"> and he will bring upon thee all the diseases of Egypt which thou art afraid of, and they shall cleave unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:25–26"> Also every sickness and every plague which is not written in the book of this law, them will Jehovah bring upon thee, until thou be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Nehemiah 9:23; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And ye shall be left a small company, whereas ye were as the stars of heaven for multitude; because thou hast not hearkened to the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="28.62 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Jeremiah 32:41; Deuteronomy 30:9"> And it shall come to pass, that as Jehovah rejoiced over you to do you good and to multiply you, so Jehovah will rejoice over you to cause you to perish, and to destroy you; and ye shall be plucked from off the land whereunto thou goest to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27–28; Leviticus 26:33; Jeremiah 16:13"> And Jehovah will scatter thee among all peoples, from one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth; and thou shalt there serve other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="28.64 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>gods, whom thou hast not known, neither thou nor thy fathers, wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Romans 11:10; Amos 9:4"> And among these nations shalt thou have no rest, neither shall the sole of thy foot have a resting-place, and Jehovah shall give thee there a trembling heart, languishing of the eyes, and pining of the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Hebrews 10:27; Revelation 6:15–17"> And thy life shall hang in suspense before thee; and thou shalt be in terror day and night and shalt be afraid of thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:34; Job 7:3–4; Revelation 9:6"> In the morning thou shalt say, Would that it were even! and in the evening thou shalt say, Would that it were morning! through the fright of thy heart wherewith thou shalt be in terror, and through the sight of thine eyes which thou shalt see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:12; Jeremiah 43:7; Hosea 8:13"> And Jehovah will bring thee into Egypt again with ships, by the way whereof I said unto thee, Thou shalt see it again no more; and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and there shall be no man to buy &#91;you&#93;.</span>
