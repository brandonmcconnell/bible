<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5; Deuteronomy 10:12; Luke 1:74–75"> Thou shalt love then Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="11.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God, and keep his charge, and his statutes, and his ordinances, and his commandments continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2–5; Deuteronomy 29:10; Acts 26:22"> And know ye this day...; for &#91;I speak&#93; not with your children who have not known, and who have not seen the chastisement of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="11.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, his greatness, his powerful hand, and his stretched-out arm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 105:27–45"> and his signs and his acts which he did in the midst of Egypt unto Pharaoh the king of Egypt, and unto all his land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 106:11; Exodus 14:23–31; Exodus 15:19"> and what he did unto the army of Egypt, unto their horses, and unto their chariots, over which he made the water of the Red sea flow as they pursued after you, and Jehovah destroyed them unto this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; Psalms 106:12–48; Psalms 78:14–72"> — and what he did unto you in the wilderness, until ye came to this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:9–10; Psalms 106:17; Numbers 16:1–35"> and what he did unto Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, the son of Reuben, — how the earth opened its mouth, and swallowed them up, with their households, and their tents, and all the living substance that belonged to them, in the midst of all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–6; Psalms 145:12; Psalms 150:2"> For your eyes have seen all the great work of Jehovah which he hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:6–7; Ephesians 3:16"> Keep then all the commandment which I command you this day, that ye may be strong, and enter in and possess the land, whither ye pass over to possess it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 6:2"> and that ye may prolong your days in the land which Jehovah swore unto your fathers to give unto them and unto their seed, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 14:18"> For the land, whither thou enterest in to possess it, is not as the land of Egypt, from whence ye came out, where thou sowedst thy seed, and wateredst it with thy foot, as a garden of herbs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Psalms 65:12–13"> but the land, whereunto ye are passing over to possess it, is a land of mountains and valleys, which drinketh water of the rain of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Psalms 33:18; 1 Kings 9:3"> a land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="11.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God careth for; the eyes of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="11.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God are constantly upon it, from the beginning of the year even unto the end of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Deuteronomy 4:29; Deuteronomy 6:17"> And it shall come to pass, if ye hearken diligently unto my commandments which I command you this day, to love Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="11.13 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 5:7; Leviticus 26:4; Ezekiel 34:26"> that I will give rain to your land in its season, the early rain and the latter rain; and thou shalt gather in thy corn, and thy new wine, and thine oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Deuteronomy 6:11; Joel 2:19"> and I will give grass in thy field for thy cattle; and thou shalt eat and be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:27; Hebrews 3:12; Hebrews 2:1"> Take heed to yourselves, that your heart be not deceived, and ye turn aside and serve other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="11.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>gods, and bow down to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Deuteronomy 6:15; 2 Chronicles 6:26"> and Jehovah's wrath kindle against you, and he shut up the heavens, that there be no rain, and that the ground yield not its produce, and ye perish quickly from off the good land which Jehovah is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Proverbs 3:1"> And ye shall lay up these my words in your heart and in your soul, and bind them for a sign upon your hand, and they shall be for frontlets between your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Isaiah 38:19; Deuteronomy 4:9–10"> And ye shall teach them unto your children, speaking of them when thou sittest in thy house, and when thou goest on the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:9"> and write them upon the posts of thy house, and upon thy gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 3:2; Proverbs 9:11"> that your days may be multiplied, and the days of your children, in the land which Jehovah swore unto your fathers to give them, as the days of the heavens &#91;which are&#93; above the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:17; Deuteronomy 11:13; Deuteronomy 10:20"> For if ye diligently keep all this commandment which I command you &#91;this day&#93; to do it, to love Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="11.22 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, to walk in all his ways, and to cleave unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> then will Jehovah dispossess all these nations from before you, and ye shall take possession of nations greater and mightier than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 14:9; Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21"> Every place whereon the sole of your foot shall tread shall be yours; from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the river Euphrates, even unto the hinder sea shall your border be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Exodus 23:27; Joshua 1:5"> No man shall be able to stand before you: the fear of you and the dread of you will Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="11.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God lay upon all the land that ye shall tread upon, as he hath said unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:1; Deuteronomy 30:15–20; Galatians 3:10"> See, I set before you this day a blessing and a curse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Isaiah 3:10"> a blessing, if ye obey the commandments of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="11.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, which I command you this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 3:10"> and a curse, if ye will not obey the commandments of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="11.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God, but turn aside out of the way which I command you this day, to go after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="11.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>gods which ye have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:12–26; Joshua 8:30–35"> And it shall come to pass, when Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="11.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God hath brought thee into the land whither thou enterest in to possess it, that thou shalt put the blessing upon mount Gerizim, and the curse upon mount Ebal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Judges 7:1; Joshua 4:19"> Are they not on the other side of the Jordan, beyond the way toward the going down of the sun, in the land of the Canaanites that dwell on the plain opposite to Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 1:11; Deuteronomy 9:1; Joshua 3:13–17"> For ye pass over the Jordan to enter in to possess the land which Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="11.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God giveth you, and ye shall take possession of it, and dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Deuteronomy 12:32; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> And ye shall take heed to do all the statutes and ordinances which I set before you this day.</span>
