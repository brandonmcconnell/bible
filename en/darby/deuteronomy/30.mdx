<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; 1 Kings 8:47–48; Deuteronomy 30:19"> And it shall come to pass, when all these things are come upon thee, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before thee, and thou shalt take them to heart among all the nations whither Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="30.1 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath driven thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Nehemiah 1:9; Deuteronomy 4:28–31"> and shalt return to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="30.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God, and shalt hearken to his voice according to all that I command thee this day, thou and thy sons, with all thy heart and with all thy soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Ezekiel 36:24; Psalms 147:2"> that then Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="30.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God will turn thy captivity, and have compassion upon thee, and will gather thee again from all the peoples whither Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="30.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God hath scattered thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:9; Ezekiel 39:25–29; Deuteronomy 28:64"> Though there were of you driven out unto the end of the heavens, from thence will Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="30.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God gather thee, and from thence will he fetch thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14"> and Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="30.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God will bring thee into the land that thy fathers possessed, and thou shalt possess it; and he will do thee good, and multiply thee above thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 32:39"> And Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="30.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God will circumcise thy heart, and the heart of thy seed, to love Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="30.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God with all thy heart and with all thy soul, that thou mayest live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Lamentations 4:21–22; Jeremiah 51:24–26"> And Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="30.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God will put all these curses on thine enemies, and on them that hate thee, who have persecuted thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:16; Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> But thou shalt return and hearken to the voice of Jehovah, and do all his commandments which I command thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:11–14; Jeremiah 32:41; Jeremiah 33:9"> And Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="30.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God will make thee abound in every work of thy hand, in the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy ground, for good; for Jehovah will again rejoice over thee for good, as he rejoiced over thy fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Deuteronomy 4:29; Nehemiah 1:9"> if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="30.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, to keep his commandments and his statutes which are written in this book of the law; if thou turn to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="30.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God with all thy heart and with all thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:19; Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 16:25–26"> For this commandment which I command thee this day is not too wonderful for thee, neither is it far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 10:6–8; Proverbs 30:4; John 3:13"> It is not in the heavens, that thou shouldest say, Who shall go up for us to the heavens, and bring it to us, that we should hear it and do it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:33; Matthew 12:42; Proverbs 2:1–5"> And it is not beyond the sea, that thou shouldest say, Who shall go over the sea for us, and bring it to us, that we should hear it and do it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 10:8–10; Jeremiah 12:2; Matthew 7:21"> For the word is very near to thee, in thy mouth and in thy heart, that thou mayest do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:26; Deuteronomy 30:19; 1 John 5:11–12"> See, I have set before thee this day life and good, and death and evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Matthew 22:37–38; 1 John 5:2–3"> in that I command thee this day to love Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="30.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God, to walk in his ways, and to keep his commandments and his statutes and his ordinances, that thou mayest live and multiply, and that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="30.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God may bless thee in the land whither thou goest to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Hebrews 3:12; Proverbs 1:32"> But if thy heart turn away, so that thou wilt not hear, but shalt be drawn away, and thou shalt bow down to other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="30.17 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>gods and serve them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19–20; Isaiah 63:17–18; Deuteronomy 4:26"> I denounce unto you this day that ye shall surely perish; ye shall not prolong your days upon the land whereunto thou passest over the Jordan to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26; Psalms 119:30"> I call heaven and earth to witness this day against you: life and death have I set before you, blessing and cursing: choose then life, that thou mayest live, thou and thy seed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 17:3"> in loving Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="30.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God, in hearkening to his voice, and in cleaving to him — for this is thy life and the length of thy days — that thou mayest dwell in the land which Jehovah swore unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give them.</span>
