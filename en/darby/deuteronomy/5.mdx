<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Matthew 23:3; Deuteronomy 29:10"> And Moses called to all Israel, and said to them, Hear, Israel, the statutes and the ordinances that I speak in your ears this day, and learn them, and keep them to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:23; Exodus 19:5–8; Exodus 24:8"> Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="5.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God made a covenant with us in Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:10–15; Genesis 17:21; Genesis 17:7"> Not with our fathers did Jehovah make this covenant, but with us, &#91;even&#93; us, those &#91;who are&#93; here alive all of us this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 19:18–19; Deuteronomy 34:10; Exodus 19:9"> Face to face on the mountain from the midst of the fire Jehovah spoke with you</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; Exodus 19:16; Exodus 20:18–21"> (I stood between Jehovah and you at that time, to declare to you the word of Jehovah; for ye were afraid by reason of the fire, and went not up to the mountain), saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 20:2–17; Psalms 81:5–10; Leviticus 26:1–2"> I am Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="5.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God who have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:3; Matthew 4:10; 1 John 5:21"> Thou shalt have no other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="5.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>gods before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> Thou shalt not make thyself any graven image, any form of what is in the heavens above, or what is in the earth beneath, or what is in the waters under the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:4–6; Exodus 34:7; Daniel 9:4–9"> thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them; for I, Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="5.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, am a jealous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="5.9 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">e</a></sup>God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the sons, and upon the third and upon the fourth &#91;generation&#93; of them that hate me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 John 1:7; Jeremiah 32:18"> and shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me and keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 19:12; Deuteronomy 6:13"> Thou shalt not idly utter the name of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="5.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God; for Jehovah will not hold him guiltless that idly uttereth his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:8–11; Isaiah 58:13; Isaiah 56:6"> Keep the sabbath day to hallow it, as Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="5.12 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God hath commanded thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Luke 13:14–16; Exodus 23:12"> Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Genesis 2:2; Exodus 23:12"> but the seventh day is the sabbath of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="5.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God: thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy bondman, nor thy handmaid, nor thine ox, nor thine ass, nor any of thy cattle, nor thy sojourner that is within thy gates; that thy bondman and thy handmaid may rest as well as thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Deuteronomy 15:15; Deuteronomy 16:12"> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in the land of Egypt, and that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="5.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God brought thee out thence with a powerful hand and with a stretched-out arm; therefore Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="5.15 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God hath commanded thee to observe the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20"> Honour thy father and thy mother, as Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="5.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God hath commanded thee; that thy days may be prolonged, and that it may be well with thee in the land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="5.16 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God giveth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 20:13; Romans 13:9; Matthew 5:21–22"> Thou shalt not kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Luke 18:20; Leviticus 20:10"> Neither shalt thou commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:15; Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28"> Neither shalt thou steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:16; Exodus 23:1; Proverbs 19:9"> Neither shalt thou bear false witness against thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 20:17; Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15"> Neither shalt thou desire thy neighbour's wife, neither shalt thou covet thy neighbour's house, his field, nor his bondman, nor his handmaid, his ox, nor his ass, nor anything that is thy neighbour's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:12; Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 4:12–15"> These words Jehovah spoke to all your congregation on the mountain from the midst of the fire, of the cloud, and of the obscurity, with a great voice, and he added no more; and he wrote them on two tables of stone, and gave them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 20:18–19; Hebrews 12:18–21"> And it came to pass, when ye heard the voice from the midst of the darkness, and the mountain burned with fire, that ye came near to me, all the heads of your tribes, and your elders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:33; Exodus 19:19; Exodus 33:20"> and ye said, Behold, Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="5.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God has shewn us his glory and his greatness, and we have heard his voice from the midst of the fire: we have seen this day that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="5.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">n</a></sup>God talks with man, and he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–9"> And now, why should we die? for this great fire will consume us. If we hear the voice of Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="5.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God any more, we shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:33; Romans 3:20; Matthew 26:63"> For who is there of all flesh, that has heard the voice of the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="5.26 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>God speaking from the midst of the fire, as we, and has lived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Hebrews 12:19"> Come thou near, and hear all that Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="5.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God will say; and speak thou to us all that Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="5.27 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">r</a></sup>God will speak to thee; and we will hear it, and do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:17; Numbers 36:5; Numbers 27:7"> And Jehovah heard the voice of your words, when ye spoke to me; and Jehovah said unto me, I have heard the voice of the words of this people that have spoken to thee: they have well spoken all that they have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Ezekiel 33:31–32; Deuteronomy 11:1"> Oh that there were such a heart in them, that they would fear me, and keep all my commandments continually, that it might be well with them and with their sons for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Go, say unto them, Get you into your tents again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; Ezekiel 20:11; Deuteronomy 5:1"> But as for thee, stand thou here by me, and I will speak unto thee all the commandments, and the statutes, and the ordinances, which thou shalt teach them, that they may do them in the land which I give them to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 23:6; Proverbs 4:27; Joshua 1:7"> Take heed then to do as Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="5.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">s</a></sup>God hath commanded you: turn not aside to the right hand or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Deuteronomy 4:40; Luke 1:6"> In all the way that Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="5.33 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">t</a></sup>God hath commanded you shall ye walk, that ye may live, and that it may be well with you, and that ye may prolong your days in the land which ye shall possess.</span>
