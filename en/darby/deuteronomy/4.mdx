<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; Psalms 106:28–29; Joshua 22:17"> Your eyes have seen what Jehovah did because of Baal-Peor; for all the men that followed Baal-Peor, Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God hath destroyed them from among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 23:8; John 6:67–69"> but ye that did cleave to Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="4.4 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God are alive every one of you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2; Hebrews 3:5"> See, I have taught you statutes and ordinances, even as Jehovah my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="4.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God commanded me, that ye may do so in the land into which ye enter to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10; 2 Timothy 3:15"> And ye shall keep and do them; for that will be your wisdom and your understanding before the eyes of the peoples that shall hear all these statutes, and say, Verily this great nation is a wise and understanding people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; 2 Samuel 7:23; Psalms 34:18"> For what great nation is there that hath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="4.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God near to them as Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="4.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God is in everything we call upon him for?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 7:12–14; Psalms 119:127–128"> And what great nation is there that hath righteous statutes and ordinances, as all this law which I set before you this day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 78:3–8"> Only take heed to thyself, and keep thy soul diligently, lest thou forget the things that thine eyes have seen (and lest they depart from thy heart all the days of thy life; but thou shalt make them known to thy sons and to thy sons' sons),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Hebrews 12:18–19; Exodus 19:16"> the day that thou stoodest before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="4.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God in Horeb, when Jehovah said to me, Gather me the people together, that I may cause them to hear my words, that they may learn them, and fear me all the days that they live upon the earth, and teach them to their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:23; Exodus 19:16–18; Hebrews 12:18–19"> And ye came near and stood under the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire to the heart of heaven, with darkness, clouds, and obscurity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:15; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And Jehovah spoke to you from the midst of the fire: ye heard the voice of the words, but ye saw no form; only &#91;ye heard&#93; a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 9:9–11"> And he declared to you his covenant, which he commanded you to do, the ten words; and he wrote them on two tables of stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:44–45; Ezekiel 21:1–23"> And Jehovah commanded me at that time to teach you statutes and ordinances, that ye might do them in the land whither ye are passing over to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:12; Isaiah 40:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> And take great heed to your souls (for ye saw no form on the day that Jehovah spoke to you in Horeb from the midst of the fire),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Deuteronomy 5:8; Exodus 20:4–5"> lest ye corrupt yourselves, and make you a graven image, the form of any figure, the pattern of male or female,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:23"> the pattern of any beast that is on the earth, the pattern of any winged fowl that flieth in the heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the pattern of anything that creepeth on the ground, the pattern of any fish that is in the waters under the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:25; Deuteronomy 17:3; 2 Kings 21:3"> and lest thou lift up thine eyes to the heavens, and see the sun, and the moon, and the stars, the whole host of heaven, and be drawn away and bow down to them and serve them, which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="4.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God hath assigned unto all peoples under the whole heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 8:51; Jeremiah 11:4; Exodus 19:5–6"> But you hath Jehovah taken, and hath brought you forth out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, that ye might be to him a people of inheritance, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:12; Psalms 106:32–33"> And Jehovah was angry with me on your account, and swore that I should not go over the Jordan, and that I should not enter in to that good land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="4.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>God giveth thee &#91;for&#93; an inheritance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:25; Deuteronomy 3:27; 2 Peter 1:13–15"> for I shall die in this land, I shall not go over the Jordan; but ye shall go over, and possess this good land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:9; Hebrews 3:12; Joshua 23:16"> Take heed to yourselves lest ye forget the covenant of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="4.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God, which he made with you, and make yourselves a graven image, the form of anything which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="4.23 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">l</a></sup>God hath forbidden thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Nahum 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:3"> For Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="4.24 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">m</a></sup>God is a consuming fire, a jealous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="4.24 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">n</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:16; Hosea 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> When thou begettest sons, and sons' sons, and ye have remained long in the land, and shall corrupt yourselves, and make a graven image, the form of anything, and do evil in the sight of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="4.25 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">o</a></sup>God, to provoke him to anger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 6:2; Jeremiah 22:29"> I call heaven and earth to witness against you this day, that ye shall soon utterly perish from off the land whereunto ye pass over the Jordan to possess it: ye shall not prolong your days on it, but shall be utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:62–64; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 12:15"> And Jehovah will scatter you among the peoples, and ye shall be left a small company among the nations to which Jehovah will lead you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremiah 16:13; Deuteronomy 28:36"> And ye shall there serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="4.28 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">p</a></sup>gods, the work of men's hands, wood and stone, which neither see, nor hear, nor eat, nor smell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Jeremiah 29:12–14; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> And from thence ye shall seek Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="4.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">q</a></sup>God, and thou shalt find him, if thou shalt seek him with thy whole heart and with thy whole soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5"> In thy tribulation, and when all these things shall come upon thee, at the end of days, thou shalt return to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="4.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">r</a></sup>God, and shalt hearken to his voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Psalms 116:5; Nehemiah 9:31"> — for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="4.31 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">s</a></sup>God is a merciful<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="4.31 El|strong=&quot;H0410&quot;">t</a></sup>God, — he will not forsake thee, neither destroy thee, nor forget the covenant of thy fathers which he swore unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 8:8; Matthew 24:31; Psalms 44:1"> For ask now of the days that are past, which were before thee, since the day that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="4.32 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">u</a></sup>God created man on the earth, and from one end of the heavens to the other end of the heavens, whether there hath been anything as this great thing is, or if anything hath been heard like it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Deuteronomy 5:24; Exodus 33:20"> Did &#91;ever&#93; people hear the voice of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="4.33 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">v</a></sup>God speaking from the midst of the fire, as thou hast heard, and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 6:6"> Or hath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="4.34 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">w</a></sup>God essayed to come to take him a nation from the midst of a nation, by trials, by signs, and by wonders, and by war, and by a powerful hand, and by a stretched-out arm, and by great terrors, according to all that Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="4.34 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">x</a></sup>God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 45:18; 1 Samuel 2:2; Isaiah 45:5"> Unto thee it was shewn, that thou mightest know that Jehovah, he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="4.35 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">y</a></sup>God — there is none other besides him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Exodus 19:19; Deuteronomy 4:33"> From the heavens he made thee hear his voice, that he might instruct thee; and on the earth he shewed thee his great fire; and thou heardest his words from the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Isaiah 63:9; Exodus 13:9"> And because he loved thy fathers, and chose their seed after them, he brought thee out with his countenance, with his great power, out of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Deuteronomy 9:1–5; Exodus 23:27–28"> to dispossess nations from before thee greater and mightier than thou art, to bring thee in, to give thee their land for an inheritance, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:35; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> Thou shalt know therefore this day, and consider it in thy heart, that Jehovah, he is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="4.39 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">z</a></sup>God in the heavens above, and on the earth beneath: &#91;there is&#93; none else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:3; Ephesians 6:3; Deuteronomy 12:28"> And thou shalt keep his statutes, and his commandments, which I command thee this day, that it may be well with thee and with thy sons after thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy days on the land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="4.40 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">aa</a></sup>God giveth thee, for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:6; Numbers 35:14–15; Joshua 20:2–9"> Then Moses separated three cities on this side the Jordan toward the sun-rising,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Deuteronomy 19:1–10; Numbers 35:15–28"> that the manslayer might flee thither, who should kill his neighbour unawares, and hated him not previously, that fleeing to one of these cities, he might live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; 1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 21:27"> Bezer in the wilderness, in the plateau, of the Reubenites, and Ramoth in Gilead, of the Gadites, and Golan in Bashan, of the Manassites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:3; Leviticus 27:34; Malachi 4:4"> And this is the law which Moses set before the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:1; Psalms 119:7"> these are the testimonies, and the statutes, and the ordinances that Moses declared to the children of Israel, when they came out of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:29; Deuteronomy 4:47; Deuteronomy 1:4–5"> on this side the Jordan, in the valley opposite to Beth-Peor, in the land of Sihon the king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, whom Moses and the children of Israel smote when they came out of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:1–14; Numbers 21:33–35; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> and they took possession of his land, and the land of Og the king of Bashan, two kings of the Amorites, who were on this side the Jordan, toward the sun-rising;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Deuteronomy 3:12"> from Aroer, which is on the bank of the river Arnon, as far as mount Sion, which is Hermon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 13:20; Deuteronomy 34:1"> and all the plain on this side the Jordan, eastward, and as far as the sea of the plain, under the slopes of Pisgah.</span>
