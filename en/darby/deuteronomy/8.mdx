<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> Every commandment which I command thee this day shall ye take heed to do, that ye may live, and multiply, and enter in and possess the land which Jehovah swore unto your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:3; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:16"> And thou shalt remember all the way which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="8.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God led thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thy heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:4; Luke 4:4; Hebrews 13:5–6"> And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with the manna, which thou hadst not known, and which thy fathers knew not; that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread alone, but by everything that goeth out of the mouth of Jehovah doth man live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 29:5; Matthew 26:25–30"> Thy clothing grew not old upon thee, neither did thy foot swell, these forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> And know in thy heart that, as a man chasteneth his son, so Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="8.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God chasteneth thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:31; Deuteronomy 5:33; 1 Samuel 12:24"> and thou shalt keep the commandments of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="8.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God, to walk in his ways, and to fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:9–12; Exodus 3:8; Ezekiel 20:6"> For Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="8.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God bringeth thee into a good land, a land of water-brooks, of springs, and of deep waters, that gush forth in the valleys and hills;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 6:13; Psalms 147:14; Deuteronomy 32:14"> a land of wheat, and barley, and vines, and fig-trees, and pomegranates; a land of olive-trees and honey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:14; Deuteronomy 33:25; Joshua 22:8"> a land wherein thou shalt eat bread without scarceness, where thou shalt lack nothing; a land whose stones are iron, and out of whose mountains thou wilt dig copper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 103:2; Romans 14:6; Proverbs 3:9"> And thou shalt eat and be filled, and shalt bless Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="8.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God for the good land which he hath given thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Hosea 2:8–9; Proverbs 1:32"> Beware that thou forget not Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="8.11 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God, in not keeping his commandments, and his ordinances, and his statutes, which I command thee this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:15"> lest when thou hast eaten and art full, and hast built and inhabited fine houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:3; Genesis 13:1–5; Luke 12:13–21"> and thy herds and thy flocks multiply, and thy silver and thy gold is multiplied, and all that thou hast is multiplied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; 1 Corinthians 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:11"> then thy heart be lifted up, and thou forget Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="8.14 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God, who brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:6; Numbers 20:11; Jeremiah 2:6"> who led thee through the great and terrible wilderness, &#91;a wilderness of&#93; fiery serpents, and scorpions, and drought, where there is no water; who brought thee forth water out of the rock of flint;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; James 1:12; Exodus 16:15"> who fed thee in the wilderness with manna, which thy fathers knew not, that he might humble thee, and that he might prove thee, to do thee good at thy latter end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:4; 1 Corinthians 4:7; Daniel 4:30"> — and thou say in thy heart, My power and the might of my hand has procured me this wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Hosea 2:8; Deuteronomy 7:8"> But thou shalt remember Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="8.18 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, that it is he who giveth thee power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant which he swore unto thy fathers, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:26; 1 Samuel 12:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> And it shall be, if thou do at all forget Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="8.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, and go after other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="8.19 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">j</a></sup>gods, and serve them, and bow down to them, I testify against you this day that ye shall utterly perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:16–17"> As the nations which Jehovah is causing to perish before you, so shall ye perish; because ye would not hearken unto the voice of Jehovah your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="8.20 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">k</a></sup>God.</span>
</div>
