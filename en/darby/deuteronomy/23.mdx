<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Isaiah 56:3–4; Leviticus 21:17–21"> He that is a eunuch, whether he have been crushed or cut, shall not come into the congregation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 9:6; Isaiah 57:3; Hebrews 12:8"> A bastard shall not come into the congregation of Jehovah; even his tenth generation shall not come into the congregation of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> An Ammonite or Moabite shall not come into the congregation of Jehovah; even their tenth generation shall not come into the congregation of Jehovah for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; 1 Kings 18:4"> because they met you not with bread and with water on the way, when ye came forth out of Egypt, and because they hired against thee Balaam the son of Beor, of Pethor of Mesopotamia, to curse thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> But Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="23.5 Elohim">a</a></sup>God would not listen to Balaam; and Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="23.5 Elohim">b</a></sup>God turned the curse into blessing unto thee, because Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="23.5 Elohim">c</a></sup>God loved thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 Samuel 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Thou shalt not seek their peace nor their prosperity all thy days for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> Thou shalt not abhor an Edomite; for he is thy brother. Thou shalt not abhor an Egyptian; because thou wast a sojourner in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> Children that are born to them shall come into the congregation of Jehovah in the third generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> When thou goest forth into camp against thine enemies, then keep thee from every evil thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If there be with thee a man that is not clean from what hath happened in the night, then shall he go outside the camp; he shall not come inside the camp;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> and it shall be, towards evening, he shall bathe in water: and at the going down of the sun he may come inside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Thou shalt have a place also outside the camp, and shalt go forth thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> And thou shalt have a shovel amongst thy weapons, and it shall be, when thou sittest down abroad, thou shalt dig therewith, and shalt turn back and cover that which is come from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> For Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="23.14 Elohim">d</a></sup>God walketh in the midst of thy camp, to deliver thee, and to give up thine enemies before thee; and thy camp shall be holy, that he see nothing unseemly with thee, and turn away from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:15; Obadiah 1:14"> Thou shalt not hand over to his master a bondman that hath escaped from his master unto thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zechariah 7:10; Jeremiah 7:6"> he shall dwell with thee, even in thy midst, in the place that he shall choose in one of thy gates, where it seemeth good to him; thou shalt not oppress him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; 1 Kings 14:24; Leviticus 19:29"> There shall be no prostitute amongst the daughters of Israel, nor any Sodomite amongst the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> Thou shalt not bring the hire of a harlot, or the price of a dog, into the house of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="23.18 Elohim">e</a></sup>God for any vow; for even both these are an abomination to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="23.18 Elohim">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> Thou shalt take no interest of thy brother, interest of money, interest of victuals, interest of anything that can be lent upon interest:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> of a foreigner thou mayest take interest, but of thy brother thou shalt not take interest; that Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="23.20 Elohim">g</a></sup>God may bless thee in all the business of thy hand in the land whither thou goest to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> When thou vowest a vow to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="23.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God, thou shalt not delay to perform it; for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="23.21 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God will certainly require it of thee, and it shall be sin in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if thou forbear to vow, it shall be no sin in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> What is gone out of thy lips thou shalt keep and do, as thou hast vowed to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="23.23 Elohim">j</a></sup>God, the voluntary-offering that thou hast promised with thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:5"> When thou comest into thy neighbour's vineyard, thou mayest eat grapes thy fill, according to thy desire, but thou shalt not put any in thy vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–2; Mark 2:23; Luke 6:1–2"> When thou comest into the standing corn of thy neighbour, thou mayest pluck ears with thy hand; but thou shalt not wave the sickle against thy neighbour's standing corn.</span>
