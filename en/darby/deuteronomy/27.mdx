<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 15:14; Luke 11:28; James 2:10"> And Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, Keep all the commandment which I command you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> And it shall be on the day when ye pass over the Jordan unto the land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="27.2 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God giveth thee, that thou shalt set thee up great stones, and plaster them with plaster:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> and thou shalt write upon them all the words of this law, when thou goest over that thou mayest enter into the land which Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="27.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God giveth thee, a land flowing with milk and honey, as Jehovah the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="27.3 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God of thy fathers hath promised thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29–30; Joshua 8:30–33"> And it shall be when ye go over the Jordan, that ye shall set up these stones, as I command you this day, on mount Ebal, and thou shalt plaster them with plaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> And there shalt thou build an altar to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="27.5 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God, an altar of stones; thou shalt not lift up an iron &#91;tool&#93; upon them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> of whole stones shalt thou build the altar of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="27.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God; and thou shalt offer up burnt-offerings thereon to Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="27.6 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalms 100:1–2"> And thou shalt sacrifice peace-offerings, and shalt eat there, and rejoice before Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="27.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:2; John 16:25; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> And thou shalt write upon the stones all the words of this law very plainly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10–11; Deuteronomy 26:16–18; Ephesians 5:8–9"> And Moses and the priests, the Levites, spoke to all Israel, saying, Be silent and hearken, Israel! this day thou art become the people of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="27.9 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">h</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Matthew 5:48"> And thou shalt hearken unto the voice of Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="27.10 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">i</a></sup>God, and do his commandments and his statutes, which I command thee this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moses gave commandment to the people the same day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> These shall stand to bless the people upon mount Gerizim, when ye have gone over the Jordan: Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Joseph, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> And these shall stand upon mount Ebal to curse: Reuben, Gad, and Asher, and Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> And the Levites shall declare and say unto all the men of Israel with a loud voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> Cursed be the man that maketh a graven or molten image, an abomination to Jehovah, a work of the craftsman's hand, and putteth it up secretly! And all the people shall answer and say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> Cursed be he that slighteth his father or his mother! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> Cursed be he that removeth his neighbour's landmark! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> Cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of the way! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> Cursed be he that lieth with his father's wife; for he uncovereth his father's skirt! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> Cursed be he that lieth with any manner of beast! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> Cursed be he that lieth with his sister, the daughter of his father, or the daughter of his mother! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> Cursed be he that lieth with his mother-in-law! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> Cursed be he that smiteth his neighbour secretly! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> Cursed be he that taketh reward to smite mortally &#91;shedding&#93; innocent blood! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> Cursed be he that confirmeth not the words of this law to do them! And all the people shall say, Amen.</span>
