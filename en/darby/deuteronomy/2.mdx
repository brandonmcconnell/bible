<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 14:25"> And we turned, and took our journey into the wilderness by the way of the Red sea, as Jehovah had said unto me; and we went round mount Seir many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Jehovah spoke to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Deuteronomy 2:14; Deuteronomy 2:7"> Ye have gone round this mountain long enough: turn you northward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Exodus 15:15; Deuteronomy 23:7"> And command the people, saying, Ye are to pass through the border of your brethren the children of Esau, who dwell in Seir; and they will be afraid of you; and ye shall be very guarded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Joshua 24:4; Jeremiah 27:5"> attack them not; for I will not give you of their land, no, not so much as a foot-breadth; for I have given mount Seir as a possession unto Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Numbers 20:19; Romans 12:17"> Ye shall buy of them food for money, that ye may eat; and water shall ye also buy of them for money, that ye may drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2–4"> for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">a</a></sup>God hath blessed thee in all the work of thy hand. He hath known thy walking through this great wilderness: these forty years hath Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="2.7 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">b</a></sup>God been with thee; thou hast lacked nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; Judges 11:18; 2 Kings 16:6"> And we passed by from our brethren the children of Esau, who dwelt in Seir, by the plain, by Elath, and by Ezion-geber, and we turned and passed by the way of the wilderness of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 19:36–37; Numbers 21:15; Numbers 21:28"> And Jehovah said to me, Distress not the Moabites, neither engage with them in battle; for I will not give thee of their land a possession; for unto the children of Lot have I given Ar as a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:11; Numbers 13:22"> (The Emim dwelt therein in times past, a people great, and many, and tall as the Anakim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:28; Genesis 14:5"> They also are reckoned as giants like the Anakim; but the Moabites call them Emim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:22; Genesis 14:6; Deuteronomy 2:32–11"> And in Seir dwelt the Horites in times past; and the children of Esau dispossessed them, and destroyed them from before them, and dwelt in their stead; as Israel did to the land of their possession, which Jehovah gave to them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 21:12; Numbers 13:23"> Now rise up, and pass over the torrent Zered. And we passed over the torrent Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:28–35; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> Now the days in which we came from Kadesh-barnea, until we had come over the torrent Zered, were thirty-eight years; until the whole generation of the men of war was consumed from the midst of the camp, as Jehovah had sworn unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:26; Isaiah 66:14; Psalms 32:4"> Moreover the hand of Jehovah was against them to destroy them from the midst of the camp, until they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And it came to pass when all the men of war were consumed, having died off from among the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> that Jehovah spoke to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Isaiah 15:1; Numbers 21:15"> Thou art to pass this day over the border of Moab, &#91;which is&#93; Ar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Judges 11:13–27; 2 Chronicles 20:10"> and come near over against the children of Ammon; thou shalt not distress them nor attack them; for I will not give thee of the land of the children of Ammon a possession; for unto the children of Lot have I given it as a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 14:5"> (That also is reckoned a land of giants: giants dwelt therein in time past, and the Ammonites call them Zamzummim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:7–8; Deuteronomy 1:28; Deuteronomy 2:22"> a people great, and many, and tall as the Anakim; and Jehovah destroyed them before them, and they dispossessed them, and dwelt in their stead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Genesis 36:20–30; Deuteronomy 2:12"> as he did to the children of Esau, who dwelt in Seir, from before whom he destroyed the Horites; and they dispossessed them, and dwelt in their stead, even to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 10:14; Joshua 13:3; Amos 9:7"> And the Avvites who dwelt in the hamlets as far as Gazah — the Caphtorim, who came out of Caphtor, destroyed them, and dwelt in their stead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 4:17; Judges 11:18–21"> Rise up, take your journey, and pass over the river Arnon. Behold, I have given into thy hand Sihon the king of Heshbon, the Amorite, and his land: begin, take possession, and engage with him in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Exodus 15:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:10"> This day will I begin to put the dread of thee and the fear of thee upon the peoples under the whole heaven; who will hear report of thee, and will tremble, and quake because of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; Luke 10:10–12; Joshua 13:18"> And I sent messengers out of the wilderness of Kedemoth unto Sihon the king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 11:19; Deuteronomy 2:6; Numbers 21:21–23"> Let me pass through thy land: by the highway alone will I go; I will neither turn to the right hand nor to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 20:19"> Thou shalt sell me food for money that I may eat; and thou shalt give me water for money that I may drink; I will only pass through on my feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 20:18; Judges 11:17–18; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> — as the children of Esau who dwell in Seir, and the Moabites who dwell in Ar, did to me, — until I shall pass over the Jordan into the land which Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="2.29 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">c</a></sup>God giveth us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Exodus 4:21; Joshua 11:19–20"> But Sihon the king of Heshbon would not let us pass by him; for Jehovah thy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="2.30 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">d</a></sup>God hardened his spirit, and made his heart obdurate, that he might give him into thy hand, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:8; Deuteronomy 2:24"> And Jehovah said to me, Behold, I begin to give Sihon and his land before thee: begin, take possession, that thou mayest possess his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 21:23–30; Judges 11:20–23; Psalms 136:19"> And Sihon came out against us for battle, he and all his people, to Jahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 21:44; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> But Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="2.33 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">e</a></sup>God gave him up before us; and we smote him, and his sons, and his whole people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; 1 Samuel 15:3; Deuteronomy 3:6"> And we took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed every city, men, and women, and little ones: we let none escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 8:27; Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9–11"> Only the cattle we took as booty for ourselves, and the spoil of the cities which we took.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 4:48; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer, which is on the bank of the river Arnon, and the city that is in the ravine even to Gilead, there was not one city too strong for us: Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="2.36 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">f</a></sup>God delivered all before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16; Genesis 32:22"> Only thou didst not approach the land of the children of Ammon, the whole border of the river Jabbok, nor the cities of the mountain, nor to whatsoever Jehovah our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="2.37 Elohim|strong=&quot;H0430&quot;">g</a></sup>God had forbidden us.</span>
