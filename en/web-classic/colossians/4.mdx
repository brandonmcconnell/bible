<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17–18; Philippians 4:6; Ephesians 6:18"> Continue steadfastly in prayer, watching in it with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; Acts 14:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:1–2"> praying together for us also, that God may open to us a door for the word, to speak the mystery of Christ, for which I am also in bonds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 4:6"> that I may reveal it as I ought to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15–17; 1 Thessalonians 4:12; James 3:13"> Walk in wisdom toward those who are outside, redeeming the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; 1 Peter 3:15; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let your speech always be with grace, seasoned with salt, that you may know how you ought to answer each one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21–23; Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12"> All my affairs will be made known to you by Tychicus, the beloved brother, faithful servant, and fellow bondservant in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:22; Colossians 2:2; Philippians 2:28"> I am sending him to you for this very purpose, that he may know your circumstances and comfort your hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philemon 1:10–19; Colossians 4:7"> together with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will make known to you everything that is going on here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 19:29"> Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you, and Mark the cousin of Barnabas (concerning whom you received instructions, “if he comes to you, receive him”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 11:2; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 10:45"> and Jesus who is called Justus. These are my only fellow workers for God’s Kingdom who are of the circumcision, men who have been a comfort to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:30; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:7"> Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ, salutes you, always striving for you in his prayers, that you may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 2:1; Romans 10:2; Revelation 1:11"> For I testify about him that he has great zeal for you, and for those in Laodicea, and for those in Hierapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:10–11"> Luke the beloved physician and Demas greet you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:5; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Greet the brothers who are in Laodicea, with Nymphas and the assembly that is in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:14"> When this letter has been read among you, cause it to be read also in the assembly of the Laodiceans, and that you also read the letter from Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philemon 1:2; Acts 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> Tell Archippus, “Take heed to the ministry which you have received in the Lord, that you fulfill it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; 2 Timothy 4:22; 1 Timothy 6:21"> I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you. Amen.</span>
