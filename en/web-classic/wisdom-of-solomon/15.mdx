<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For even if we sin, we are yours, knowing your dominion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but we will not sin, knowing that we have been accounted yours.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For to be acquainted with you is</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. entire.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">perfect righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to know your dominion is the root of immortality.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For we weren’t led astray by any evil plan of men’s,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor yet by painters’ fruitless labor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a form stained with varied colors,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">the sight of which leads fools into</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some authorities read reproach.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lust.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their desire is for the breathless form of a dead image.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lovers of evil things, and worthy of such hopes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are those who make, desire, and worship them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For a potter, kneading soft earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">laboriously molds each article for our service.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He fashions out of the same clay</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">both the vessels that minister to clean uses, and those of a contrary sort,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all in like manner.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What shall be the use of each article of either sort,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the potter is the judge.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Also, laboring to an evil end, he molds a vain god out of the same clay,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he who, having but a little before been made of earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after a short space goes his way to the earth out of which he was taken,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when he is required to give back the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, life">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">soul which was lent him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">However he has anxious care,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not because his powers must fail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor because his span of life is short;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But he compares himself with goldsmiths and silversmiths,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he imitates molders in</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, copper">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">brass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and considers it great that he molds counterfeit gods.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">His heart is ashes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His hope is of less value than earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His life is of less honor than clay,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">because he was ignorant of him who molded him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and of him that inspired into him</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. a soul that moves to activity.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an active</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, life">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">soul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and breathed into him a vital spirit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some authorities read they accounted.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he accounted our life to be a game,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and our</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or, way of life">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lifetime a festival for profit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for, he says, one must get gain however one can, even if it is by evil.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this man, beyond all others, knows that he sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">out of earthy matter making brittle vessels and engraved images.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But most foolish and more miserable than a baby,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are the enemies of your people, who oppressed them;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">because they even considered all the idols of the nations to be gods,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which have neither the use of eyes for seeing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor nostrils for drawing breath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor ears to hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor fingers for handling,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their feet are helpless for walking.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For a man made them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and one whose own spirit is borrowed molded them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for no one has power as a man to mold a god like himself.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, being mortal, he makes a dead thing by the work of lawless hands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for he is better than the objects of his worship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">since he indeed had life, but they never did.</span></span>
</div>
