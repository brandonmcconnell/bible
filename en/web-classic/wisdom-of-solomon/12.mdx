<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> <span className="pl pl-1">For your incorruptible spirit is in all things.</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For truly the old inhabitants of your holy land,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-2">hating them because they practiced detestable works of enchantments and unholy rites—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-2">merciless slaughters of children</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sacrificial banquets of men’s flesh and of blood—</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-2">allies in an impious fellowship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and murderers of their own helpless babes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it was your counsel to destroy by the hands of our fathers;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-2">that the land which in your sight is most precious of all</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">might receive a worthy colony of God’s servants.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, children">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nevertheless you even spared these as men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you sent hornets</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, wasps">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as forerunners of your army,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to cause them to perish little by little.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Not that you were unable to subdue the ungodly under the hand of the righteous in battle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or by terrible beasts or by a stern word to make away with them at once,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">but judging them little by little you gave them a chance to repent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not being ignorant that their nature by birth was evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their wickedness inborn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that their manner of thought would never be changed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they were a cursed seed from the beginning.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It wasn’t through fear of any that you left them unpunished for their sins.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who will say, “What have you done?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or “Who will withstand your judgment?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who will accuse you for the perishing of nations which you caused?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or who will come and stand before you as an avenger for unrighteous men?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there isn’t any God beside you that cares for all,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you might show that you didn’t judge unrighteously.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">No king or prince will be able to confront you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">about those whom you have punished.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But being righteous, you rule all things righteously,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">deeming it a thing alien from your power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to condemn one who doesn’t deserve to be punished.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For your strength is the source of righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your sovereignty over all makes you to forbear all.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, being sovereign in strength, judge in gentleness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and with great forbearance you govern us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the power is yours whenever you desire it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you taught your people by such works as these,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how the righteous must be kind.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You made your sons to have good hope,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you give repentance when men have sinned.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For if on those who were enemies of your servants</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, children">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and deserving of death,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you took vengeance with so great deliberation and indulgence,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">giving them times and opportunities when they might escape from their wickedness,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">with how great care you judged your sons,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to whose fathers you gave oaths and covenants of good promises!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore while you chasten us, you scourge our enemies ten thousand times more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the intent that we may ponder your goodness when we judge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and when we are judged may look for mercy.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore also the unrighteous that lived in a life of folly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you tormented through their own abominations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For truly they went astray very far in the ways of error,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Taking as gods those animals</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. living creatures: and so elsewhere in this book.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which even among their enemies were held in dishonor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">deceived like foolish babes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, as to unreasoning children, you sent your judgment to mock them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But those who would not be admonished by mild correction</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will experience the deserved judgment of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For through the sufferings they were indignant of,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">being punished in these creatures which they supposed to be gods,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they saw and recognized as the true God him whom they previously refused to know.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Therefore also the result of extreme condemnation came upon them.</span></span>
