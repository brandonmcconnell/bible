<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Amos 8:1; Joel 2:25"> Thus the Lord Yahweh showed me: behold, he formed locusts in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; and behold, it was the latter growth after the king’s harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; Jeremiah 42:2"> When they finished eating the grass of the land, then I said, “Lord Yahweh, forgive, I beg you! How could Jacob stand? For he is small.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 3:10; Hosea 11:8"> Yahweh relented concerning this. “It shall not be,” says Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; Hebrews 1:7"> Thus the Lord Yahweh showed me: behold, the Lord Yahweh called for judgment by fire; and it dried up the great deep, and would have devoured the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; Jeremiah 30:19"> Then I said, “Lord Yahweh, stop, I beg you! How could Jacob stand? For he is small.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 7:3; Judges 2:18; Psalms 90:13"> Yahweh relented concerning this. “This also shall not be,” says the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Revelation 21:15; Isaiah 28:17"> Thus he showed me: behold, the Lord stood beside a wall made by a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Lamentations 2:8; Ezekiel 7:2–9"> Yahweh said to me, “Amos, what do you see?” I said, “A plumb line.” Then the Lord said, “Behold, I will set a plumb line in the middle of my people Israel. I will not again pass by them any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8–10; Genesis 46:1; Amos 8:14"> The high places of Isaac will be desolate, the sanctuaries of Israel will be laid waste; and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; 1 Kings 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, “Amos has conspired against you in the middle of the house of Israel. The land is not able to bear all his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Psalms 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> For Amos says, ‘Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel shall surely be led away captive out of his land.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; 1 Samuel 9:9"> Amaziah also said to Amos, “You seer, go, flee away into the land of Judah, and there eat bread, and prophesy there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32; Amos 2:12; 1 Kings 12:29"> but don’t prophesy again any more at Bethel; for it is the king’s sanctuary, and it is a royal house!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 2:5"> Then Amos answered Amaziah, “I was no prophet, neither was I a prophet’s son, but I was a herdsman, and a farmer of sycamore figs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> and Yahweh took me from following the flock, and Yahweh said to me, ‘Go, prophesy to my people Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> Now therefore listen to Yahweh’s word: ‘You say, Don’t prophesy against Israel, and don’t preach against the house of Isaac.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; 2 Kings 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> Therefore Yahweh says: ‘Your wife shall be a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword, and your land shall be divided by line; and you yourself shall die in a land that is unclean, and Israel shall surely be led away captive out of his land.’”</span>
