<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Luke 4:1–13; Mark 1:12–15"> Then Jesus was led up by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Exodus 34:28; 1 Kings 19:8"> When he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was hungry afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> The tempter came and said to him, “If you are the Son of God, command that these stones become bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> But he answered,<span className="woj">“It is written, ‘Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds out of God’s mouth.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Then the devil took him into the holy city. He set him on the pinnacle of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 34:7"> and said to him, “If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down, for it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘He will command his angels concerning you,’ and,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘On their hands they will bear you up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that you don’t dash your foot against a stone.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalms 78:18"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Again, it is written, ‘You shall not test the Lord, your God.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; Psalms 49:16–17"> Again, the devil took him to an exceedingly high mountain, and showed him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> He said to him, “I will give you all of these things, if you will fall down and worship me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> Then Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Get behind me,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR and NU read “Go away” instead of “Get behind me”">a</a></sup><span className="woj">Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and you shall serve him only.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Then the devil left him, and behold, angels came and served him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> Now when Jesus heard that John was delivered up, he withdrew into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> Leaving Nazareth, he came and lived in Capernaum, which is by the sea, in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">toward the sea, beyond the Jordan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Galilee of the Gentiles,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">the people who sat in darkness saw a great light;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to those who sat in the region and shadow of death,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to them light has dawned.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From that time, Jesus began to preach, and to say,<span className="woj">“Repent! For the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> Walking by the sea of Galilee, he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR reads “Jesus” instead of “he”">b</a></sup>saw two brothers: Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew, his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishermen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Come after me, and I will make you fishers for men.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> They immediately left their nets and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> Going on from there, he saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets. He called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> They immediately left the boat and their father, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> Jesus went about in all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the Good News of the Kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> The report about him went out into all Syria. They brought to him all who were sick, afflicted with various diseases and torments, possessed with demons, epileptics, and paralytics; and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> Great multitudes from Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and from beyond the Jordan followed him.</span>
