<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="woj">For the Son of Man came to save that which was lost.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU omits verse 11.">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:39–40; 2 Peter 3:9"> <span className="woj">Even so it is not the will of your Father who is in heaven that one of these little ones should perish.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Luke 17:3–4; Galatians 6:1"> <span className="woj">“If your brother sins against you, go, show him his fault between you and him alone. If he listens to you, you have gained back your brother.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1; John 8:17"> <span className="woj">But if he doesn’t listen, take one or two more with you, that at the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:9–7; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15"> <span className="woj">If he refuses to listen to them, tell it to the assembly. </div>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 145:8; Nehemiah 9:17; Psalms 86:15"> <span className="woj">The lord of that servant, being moved with compassion, released him and forgave him the debt.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Ezekiel 45:9; Deuteronomy 15:2"> <span className="woj">“But that servant went out and found one of his fellow servants who owed him one hundred denarii,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="100 denarii was about one sixtieth of a talent, or about 500 grams (1.1 pounds) of silver.">d</a></sup><span className="woj">and he grabbed him and took him by the throat, saying, ‘Pay me what you owe!’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
