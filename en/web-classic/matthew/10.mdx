<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> He called to himself his twelve disciples, and gave them authority over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal every disease and every sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> Now the names of the twelve apostles are these. The first, Simon, who is called Peter; Andrew, his brother; James the son of Zebedee; John, his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> Philip; Bartholomew; Thomas; Matthew the tax collector; James the son of Alphaeus; Lebbaeus, who was also called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU omits “Lebbaeus, who was also called”">a</a></sup>Thaddaeus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Simon the Zealot; and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> Jesus sent these twelve out and commanded them, saying,<span className="woj">“Don’t go among the Gentiles, and don’t enter into any city of the Samaritans.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> <span className="woj">Rather, go to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> <span className="woj">As you go, preach, saying, ‘The Kingdom of Heaven is at hand!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> <span className="woj">Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “raise the dead,”">b</a></sup><span className="woj">and cast out demons. Freely you received, so freely give.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> <span className="woj">Don’t take any gold, silver, or brass in your money belts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> <span className="woj">Take no bag for your journey, neither two coats, nor sandals, nor staff: for the laborer is worthy of his food.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> <span className="woj">Into whatever city or village you enter, find out who in it is worthy, and stay there until you go on.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> <span className="woj">As you enter into the household, greet it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalms 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> <span className="woj">If the household is worthy, let your peace come on it, but if it isn’t worthy, let your peace return to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> <span className="woj">Whoever doesn’t receive you or hear your words, as you go out of that house or that city, shake the dust off your feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> <span className="woj">Most certainly I tell you, it will be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah in the day of judgment than for that city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> <span className="woj">“Behold, I send you out as sheep among wolves. Therefore be wise as serpents and harmless as doves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> <span className="woj">But beware of men, for they will deliver you up to councils, and in their synagogues they will scourge you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalms 2:1–6"> <span className="woj">Yes, and you will be brought before governors and kings for my sake, for a testimony to them and to the nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> <span className="woj">But when they deliver you up, don’t be anxious how or what you will say, for it will be given you in that hour what you will say.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> <span className="woj">For it is not you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father who speaks in you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> <span className="woj">“Brother will deliver up brother to death, and the father his child. Children will rise up against parents and cause them to be put to death.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> <span className="woj">You will be hated by all men for my name’s sake, but he who endures to the end will be saved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> <span className="woj">But when they persecute you in this city, flee into the next, for most certainly I tell you, you will not have gone through the cities of Israel until the Son of Man has come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> <span className="woj">“A disciple is not above his teacher, nor a servant above his lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> <span className="woj">It is enough for the disciple that he be like his teacher, and the servant like his lord. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally, Lord of the Flies, or the devil">c</a></sup><span className="woj">how much more those of his household!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> <span className="woj">Therefore don’t be afraid of them, for there is nothing covered that will not be revealed, or hidden that will not be known.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> <span className="woj">What I tell you in the darkness, speak in the light; and what you hear whispered in the ear, proclaim on the housetops.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> <span className="woj">Don’t be afraid of those who kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul. Rather, fear him who is able to destroy both soul and body in Gehenna.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, Hell.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalms 104:27–30"> <span className="woj">“Aren’t two sparrows sold for an assarion coin?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="An assarion is a small coin worth one tenth of a drachma or a sixteenth of a denarius. An assarion is approximately the wages of one half hour of agricultural labor.">e</a></sup><span className="woj">Not one of them falls to the ground apart from your Father’s will.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> <span className="woj">But the very hairs of your head are all numbered.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalms 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> <span className="woj">Therefore don’t be afraid. You are of more value than many sparrows.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> <span className="woj">Everyone therefore who confesses me before men, I will also confess him before my Father who is in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> <span className="woj">But whoever denies me before men, I will also deny him before my Father who is in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> <span className="woj">“Don’t think that I came to send peace on the earth. I didn’t come to send peace, but a sword.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> <span className="woj">For I came to set a man at odds against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 41:9"> <span className="woj">A man’s foes will be those of his own household.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> <span className="woj">He who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me; and he who loves son or daughter more than me isn’t worthy of me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> <span className="woj">He who doesn’t take his cross and follow after me isn’t worthy of me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> <span className="woj">He who seeks his life will lose it; and he who loses his life for my sake will find it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> <span className="woj">“He who receives you receives me, and he who receives me receives him who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> <span className="woj">He who receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward. He who receives a righteous man in the name of a righteous man will receive a righteous man’s reward.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> <span className="woj">Whoever gives one of these little ones just a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, most certainly I tell you, he will in no way lose his reward.”</span></span>
