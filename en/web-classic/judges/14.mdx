<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> Samson went down to Timnah, and saw a woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> He came up, and told his father and his mother, saying, “I have seen a woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines. Now therefore get her for me as my wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 14:6"> Then his father and his mother said to him, “Isn’t there a woman among your brothers’ daughters, or among all my people, that you go to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines?”<br />Samson said to his father, “Get her for me, for she pleases me well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> But his father and his mother didn’t know that it was of Yahweh; for he sought an occasion against the Philistines. Now at that time the Philistines ruled over Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Samson went down to Timnah with his father and his mother, and came to the vineyards of Timnah; and behold, a young lion roared at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6"> Yahweh’s Spirit came mightily on him, and he tore him as he would have torn a young goat with his bare hands, but he didn’t tell his father or his mother what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He went down and talked with the woman, and she pleased Samson well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> After a while he returned to take her, and he went over to see the carcass of the lion; and behold, there was a swarm of bees in the body of the lion, and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> He took it into his hands, and went on, eating as he went. He came to his father and mother and gave to them, and they ate, but he didn’t tell them that he had taken the honey out of the lion’s body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> His father went down to the woman; and Samson made a feast there, for the young men used to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; 1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 10:23"> When they saw him, they brought thirty companions to be with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> Samson said to them, “Let me tell you a riddle now. If you can tell me the answer within the seven days of the feast, and find it out, then I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> but if you can’t tell me the answer, then you shall give me thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothing.”<br />They said to him, “Tell us your riddle, that we may hear it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; 1 Kings 17:6; Romans 8:37"> He said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Out of the eater came out food.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Out of the strong came out sweetness.”</span><br />They couldn’t in three days declare the riddle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> On the seventh day, they said to Samson’s wife, “Entice your husband, that he may declare to us the riddle, lest we burn you and your father’s house with fire. Have you called us to impoverish us? Isn’t that so?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> Samson’s wife wept before him, and said, “You just hate me, and don’t love me. You’ve told a riddle to the children of my people, and haven’t told it to me.”<br />He said to her, “Behold, I haven’t told my father or my mother, so why should I tell you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> She wept before him the seven days, while their feast lasted; and on the seventh day, he told her, because she pressed him severely; and she told the riddle to the children of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The men of the city said to him on the seventh day before the sun went down, “What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?”<br />He said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If you hadn’t plowed with my heifer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you wouldn’t have found out my riddle.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> Yahweh’s Spirit came mightily on him, and he went down to Ashkelon and struck thirty men of them. He took their plunder, then gave the changes of clothing to those who declared the riddle. His anger burned, and he went up to his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> But Samson’s wife was given to his companion, who had been his friend.</span>
</div>
