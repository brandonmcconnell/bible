<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 38:17; Genesis 29:21; Luke 15:29"> But after a while, in the time of wheat harvest, Samson visited his wife with a young goat. He said, “I will go in to my wife’s room.”<br />But her father wouldn’t allow him to go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 14:20; Judges 14:16; Genesis 38:14"> Her father said, “I most certainly thought that you utterly hated her; therefore I gave her to your companion. Isn’t her younger sister more beautiful than she? Please, take her instead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 14:15"> Samson said to them, “This time I will be blameless in the case of the Philistines when I harm them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:15; Psalms 63:10; Lamentations 5:18"> Samson went and caught three hundred foxes, and took torches, and turned tail to tail, and put a torch in the middle between every two tails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30; Exodus 22:6"> When he had set the torches on fire, he let them go into the standing grain of the Philistines, and burned up both the shocks and the standing grain, and also the olive groves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:15; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Proverbs 22:8"> Then the Philistines said, “Who has done this?”<br />They said, “Samson, the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he has taken his wife and given her to his companion.” The Philistines came up, and burned her and her father with fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 14:4; Romans 12:19; Judges 14:19"> Samson said to them, “If you behave like this, surely I will take revenge on you, and after that I will cease.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Isaiah 63:6; Isaiah 25:10"> He struck them hip and thigh with a great slaughter; and he went down and lived in the cave in Etam’s rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 15:17; Judges 15:19; Judges 15:14"> Then the Philistines went up, encamped in Judah, and spread themselves in Lehi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The men of Judah said, “Why have you come up against us?”<br />They said, “We have come up to bind Samson, to do to him as he has done to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> Then three thousand men of Judah went down to the cave in Etam’s rock, and said to Samson, “Don’t you know that the Philistines are rulers over us? What then is this that you have done to us?”<br />He said to them, “As they did to me, so I have done to them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Acts 7:25; 1 Kings 2:25"> They said to him, “We have come down to bind you, that we may deliver you into the hand of the Philistines.”<br />Samson said to them, “Swear to me that you will not attack me yourselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They spoke to him, saying, “No, but we will bind you securely and deliver you into their hands; but surely we will not kill you.” They bound him with two new ropes, and brought him up from the rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 14:19; Judges 3:10; Psalms 118:11"> When he came to Lehi, the Philistines shouted as they met him. Then Yahweh’s Spirit came mightily on him, and the ropes that were on his arms became as flax that was burned with fire; and his bands dropped from off his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:8; Judges 3:31"> He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey, put out his hand, took it, and struck a thousand men with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Samson said, “With the jawbone of a donkey, heaps on heaps; with the jawbone of a donkey I have struck a thousand men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> When he had finished speaking, he threw the jawbone out of his hand; and that place was called Ramath Lehi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Ramath” means “hill” and “Lehi” means “jawbone”.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–8; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 8:4"> He was very thirsty, and called on Yahweh and said, “You have given this great deliverance by the hand of your servant; and now shall I die of thirst, and fall into the hands of the uncircumcised?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 45:27; Psalms 34:6"> But God split the hollow place that is in Lehi, and water came out of it. When he had drunk, his spirit came again, and he revived. Therefore its name was called En Hakkore, which is in Lehi, to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 16:31; Judges 13:5"> He judged Israel twenty years in the days of the Philistines.</span>
</div>
