<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> Then what advantage does the Jew have? Or what is the profit of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much in every way! Because first of all, they were entrusted with the revelations of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> For what if some were without faith? Will their lack of faith nullify the faithfulness of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Psalms 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> May it never be! Yes, let God be found true, but every man a liar. As it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“that you might be justified in your words,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and might prevail when you come into judgment.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness commends the righteousness of God, what will we say? Is God unrighteous who inflicts wrath? I speak like men do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> May it never be! For then how will God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> For if the truth of God through my lie abounded to his glory, why am I also still judged as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> Why not (as we are slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say), “Let’s do evil, that good may come?” Those who say so are justly condemned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? Are we better than they? No, in no way. For we previously warned both Jews and Greeks that they are all under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> As it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There is no one righteous;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no, not one.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; Titus 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one who understands.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is no one who seeks after God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have all turned away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They have together become unprofitable.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is no one who does good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no, not so much as one.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their throat is an open tomb.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">With their tongues they have used deceit.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The poison of vipers is under their lips.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> <span className="pl pl-2">“Their mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their feet are swift to shed blood.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-2">Destruction and misery are in their ways.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> <span className="pl pl-2">The way of peace, they haven’t known.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“There is no fear of God before their eyes.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever things the law says, it speaks to those who are under the law, that every mouth may be closed, and all the world may be brought under the judgment of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> Because by the works of the law, no flesh will be justified in his sight; for through the law comes the knowledge of sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now apart from the law, a righteousness of God has been revealed, being testified by the law and the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> even the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ to all and on all those who believe. For there is no distinction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> whom God sent to be an atoning sacrifice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, a propitiation">a</a></sup>through faith in his blood, for a demonstration of his righteousness through the passing over of prior sins, in God’s forbearance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> to demonstrate his righteousness at this present time, that he might himself be just and the justifier of him who has faith in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Where then is the boasting? It is excluded. By what kind of law? Of works? No, but by a law of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> We maintain therefore that a man is justified by faith apart from the works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Or is God the God of Jews only? Isn’t he the God of Gentiles also? Yes, of Gentiles also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> since indeed there is one God who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Do we then nullify the law through faith? May it never be! No, we establish the law.</span>
</div>
</div>
