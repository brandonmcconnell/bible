<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> For you know what instructions we gave you through the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> For this is the will of God: your sanctification, that you abstain from sexual immorality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:9; Romans 12:1; Hebrews 13:4"> that each one of you know how to control his own body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="literally, possess his own vessel">a</a></sup>in sanctification and honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Acts 17:30–31; Ephesians 4:17–19"> not in the passion of lust, even as the Gentiles who don’t know God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Exodus 20:17; Romans 12:19"> that no one should take advantage of and wrong a brother or sister in this matter; because the Lord is an avenger in all these things, as also we forewarned you and testified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 2:9–12"> For God called us not for uncleanness, but in sanctification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Luke 10:16; John 12:48"> Therefore he who rejects this doesn’t reject man, but God, who has also given his Holy Spirit to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:23"> But concerning brotherly love, you have no need that one write to you. For you yourselves are taught by God to love one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:12; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> for indeed you do it toward all the brothers who are in all Macedonia. But we exhort you, brothers, that you abound more and more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Peter 3:4"> and that you make it your ambition to lead a quiet life, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands, even as we instructed you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; 1 Peter 2:12; Romans 12:17"> that you may walk properly toward those who are outside, and may have need of nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> But we don’t want you to be ignorant, brothers, concerning those who have fallen asleep, so that you don’t grieve like the rest, who have no hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Philippians 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, even so God will bring with him those who have fallen asleep in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–53; 2 Corinthians 4:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:19"> For this we tell you by the word of the Lord, that we who are alive, who are left until the coming of the Lord, will in no way precede those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> For the Lord himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel and with God’s trumpet. The dead in Christ will rise first,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:12; John 14:3"> then we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. So we will be with the Lord forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Luke 21:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore comfort one another with these words.</span>
