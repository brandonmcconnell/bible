<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Acts 28:17; Acts 13:26"> “Brothers and fathers, listen to the defense which I now make to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:40"> When they heard that he spoke to them in the Hebrew language, they were even more quiet.<br />He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:5–6; Acts 9:11"> “I am indeed a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia, but brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, instructed according to the strict tradition of the law of our fathers, being zealous for God, even as you all are today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 22:19–20; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 26:9–11"> I persecuted this Way to the death, binding and delivering into prisons both men and women,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:1–2; Luke 22:66; Acts 5:21"> as also the high priest and all the council of the elders testify, from whom also I received letters to the brothers, and traveled to Damascus to bring them also who were there to Jerusalem in bonds to be punished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:3–8; Acts 26:12–18; Revelation 1:16"> “As I made my journey and came close to Damascus, about noon suddenly a great light shone around me from the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:9; Matthew 25:45; Genesis 22:11"> I fell to the ground and heard a voice saying to me,<span className="woj">‘Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:9; 1 Samuel 8:7; Exodus 16:7–8"> I answered, ‘Who are you, Lord?’ He said to me,<span className="woj">‘I am Jesus of Nazareth, whom you persecute.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13"> “Those who were with me indeed saw the light and were afraid, but they didn’t understand the voice of him who spoke to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 22:12–16; Acts 2:37"> I said, ‘What shall I do, Lord?’ The Lord said to me,<span className="woj">‘Arise, and go into Damascus. There you will be told about all things which are appointed for you to do.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 9:8–9; Acts 13:11; Isaiah 42:16"> When I couldn’t see for the glory of that light, being led by the hand of those who were with me, I came into Damascus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:22; Acts 6:3; 3 John 1:12"> “One Ananias, a devout man according to the law, well reported of by all the Jews who lived in Damascus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:17–18; Philemon 1:16"> came to me, and standing by me said to me, ‘Brother Saul, receive your sight!’ In that very hour I looked up at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Corinthians 9:1"> He said, ‘The God of our fathers has appointed you to know his will, and to see the Righteous One, and to hear a voice from his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Acts 4:20; John 15:27"> For you will be a witness for him to all men of what you have seen and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 2:21; Titus 3:5"> Now why do you wait? Arise, be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling on the name of the Lord.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:1–4; Acts 10:9–10; Acts 9:26–28"> “When I had returned to Jerusalem and while I prayed in the temple, I fell into a trance</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:29; Luke 21:21; Ezekiel 3:6–7"> and saw him saying to me,<span className="woj">‘Hurry and get out of Jerusalem quickly, because they will not receive testimony concerning me from you.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 22:4; Matthew 10:17; Acts 8:3"> I said, ‘Lord, they themselves know that I imprisoned and beat in every synagogue those who believed in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Acts 8:1; Romans 1:32"> When the blood of Stephen, your witness, was shed, I also was standing by, consenting to his death, and guarding the cloaks of those who killed him.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:6–8"> “He said to me,<span className="woj">‘Depart, for I will send you out far from here to the Gentiles.’</span>”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:24; Acts 21:36; John 19:15"> They listened to him until he said that; then they lifted up their voice and said, “Rid the earth of this fellow, for he isn’t fit to live!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:13; Acts 26:11; Ecclesiastes 10:3"> As they cried out, threw off their cloaks, and threw dust into the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:31–32; Acts 21:34; John 19:1"> the commanding officer commanded him to be brought into the barracks, ordering him to be examined by scourging, that he might know for what crime they shouted against him like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:37; Acts 25:16; Acts 27:43"> When they had tied him up with straps, Paul asked the centurion who stood by, “Is it lawful for you to scourge a man who is a Roman, and not found guilty?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Acts 22:29"> When the centurion heard it, he went to the commanding officer and told him, “Watch what you are about to do, for this man is a Roman!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The commanding officer came and asked him, “Tell me, are you a Roman?”<br />He said, “Yes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The commanding officer answered, “I bought my citizenship for a great price.”<br />Paul said, “But I was born a Roman.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Acts 22:24–26; Acts 16:38–39"> Immediately those who were about to examine him departed from him, and the commanding officer also was afraid when he realized that he was a Roman, because he had bound him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:28; Acts 21:33; Acts 21:11"> But on the next day, desiring to know the truth about why he was accused by the Jews, he freed him from the bonds and commanded the chief priests and all the council to come together, and brought Paul down and set him before them.</span>
</div>
</div>
