<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The kindred, the Jews who are in Jerusalem and those who are in the country of Judea, send greetings and good peace to the kindred, the Jews who are throughout Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> May God do good to you, and remember his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, his faithful servants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and give you all a heart to worship him and do his will with a strong heart and a willing soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> May God open your heart to his law and his statutes, and make peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and listen to your requests, and be reconciled with you, and not forsake you in an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Now we are praying for you here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> In the reign of Demetrius, in the one hundred sixty-ninth year, we the Jews have already written to you in the suffering and in the distress that has come upon us in these years, from the time that Jason and his company revolted from the holy land and the kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and set the gate on fire, and shed innocent blood. We prayed to the Lord, and were heard. We offered sacrifices and meal offerings. We lit the lamps. We set out the show bread.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. loaves">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Now see that you keep the days of the feast of tabernacles in the month Chislev in the one hundred eighty-eighth year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The people of Jerusalem and those who are in Judea, with the senate and Judas, to Aristobulus, King Ptolemy’s teacher, who is also of the stock of the anointed priests, and to the Jews who are in Egypt, we send greetings and health.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Having been saved by God out of great perils, as men arrayed against a king, we thank him greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For he threw out into Persia those who fought against us in the holy city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For when the prince had come there, with an army that seemed irresistible, they were cut to pieces in the temple of Nanaea by the treachery of Nanaea’s priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For Antiochus, on the pretense that he would marry her, came into the place, he and his friends who were with him, that they might take a large part of the treasures as a dowry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And when the priests of Nanaea’s temple had set the treasures out, and he had come there with a small company within the wall of the sacred precinct, they locked the temple when Antiochus had come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Opening the secret door of the paneled ceiling, they threw stones and struck down the prince. They cut him and his company in pieces, and cut off their heads, and threw them to the people who were outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Blessed be our God in all things, who handed over those who had committed impiety.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Since we are now about to celebrate the purification of the temple in the month Chislev, on the twenty-fifth day, we thought it necessary to notify you, so that you may also keep a feast of tabernacles, and remember the fire which was given when Nehemiah offered sacrifices, after he had built both the temple and the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For indeed when our fathers were about to be led into the land of Persia, the godly priests of that time took some of the fire of the altar, and hid it secretly in the hollow of a well that was without water, where they made sure that the place was unknown to anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Now after many years, when it pleased God, Nehemiah, having received a charge from the king of Persia, sent in quest of the fire the descendants of the priests who hid it. When they declared to us that they had found no fire, but thick liquid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> he commanded them to draw some of it out and bring it to him. When the sacrifices had been offered, Nehemiah commanded the priests to sprinkle with that liquid both the wood and the things laid on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> When that was done and some time had passed, and the sun shone out, which before was hidden with clouds, a great blaze was kindled, so that all men marveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The priests made a prayer while the sacrifice was being consumed—both the priests and all the others. Jonathan led and the rest responded, as Nehemiah did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The prayer was like this: “O Lord, Lord God, the Creator of all things, you are awesome, strong, righteous, and merciful, you alone are King and gracious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> you alone supply every need, you alone are righteous, almighty, and eternal, you who save Israel out of all evil, who chose the ancestors and sanctified them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> accept the sacrifice for all your people Israel, and preserve your own portion, and consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Gather together our scattered people, set at liberty those who are in bondage among the heathen, look upon those who are despised and abhorred, and let the heathen know that you are our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Punish those who oppress us and in arrogance shamefully entreat us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Plant your people in your holy place, even as Moses said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Then the priests sang the hymns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> As soon as the sacrifice was consumed, then Nehemiah commanded that the rest of the liquid be poured on large stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> When this was done, a flame was kindled; but when the light from the altar shone back, it went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> When the matter became known, and king of the Persians was told that in the place where the priests who were led away had hid the fire, the liquid appeared which Nehemiah and those who were with him purified the sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> then the king enclosed the place and made it sacred after he had investigated the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> When the king would show favor to any, he would exchange many presents and give them some of this liquid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Nehemiah and those who were with him called this thing “Nephthar”, which is by interpretation, “Cleansing”; but most men call it Nephthai.</span>
</div>
</div>
