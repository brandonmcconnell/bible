<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> It came to pass that seven brothers and their mother were at the king’s command taken and shamefully handled with scourges and cords, to compel them to taste of the abominable swine’s flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> One of them made himself the spokesman and said, “What would you ask and learn from us? For we are ready to die rather than transgress the laws of our ancestors.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The king fell into a rage, and commanded that pans and caldrons be heated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> When these were immediately heated, he gave orders to cut out the tongue of him who had been their spokesman, and to scalp him, and to cut off his extremities, with the rest of his brothers and his mother looking on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when he was utterly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. useless.">a</a></sup>maimed, the king gave orders to bring him to the fire, being yet alive, and to fry him in the pan. And as the smoke from the pan spread far, they and their mother also exhorted one another to die nobly, saying this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “The Lord God sees, and in truth is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, comforted in">b</a></sup>entreated for us, as Moses declared in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 31:21 and 32:36.">c</a></sup>his song, which witnesses against the people to their faces, saying, ‘And he will have compassion on his servants.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And when the first had died like this, they brought the second to the mocking; and they pulled off the skin of his head with the hair and asked him, “Will you eat, before your body is punished in every limb?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But he answered in the language of his ancestors and said to them, “No.” Therefore he also underwent the next torture in succession, as the first had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When he was at the last gasp, he said, “You, miscreant, release us out of this present life, but the King of the world will raise us who have died for his laws up to an everlasting renewal of life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> After him, the third was made a victim of their mocking. When he was required, he quickly put out his tongue, and stretched out his hands courageously,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and nobly said, “I got these from heaven. For his laws’ sake I treat these with contempt. From him, I hope to receive these back again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As a result, the king himself and those who were with him were astonished at the young man’s soul, for he regarded the pains as nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When he too was dead, they shamefully handled and tortured the fourth in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Being near to death he said this: “It is good to die at the hands of men and look for the hope which is given by God, that we will be raised up again by him. For as for you, you will have no resurrection to life.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Next after him, they brought the fifth and shamefully handled him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But he looked toward<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. him.">d</a></sup>the king and said, “Because you have authority among men, though you are corruptible, you do what you please. But don’t think that our race has been forsaken by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But hold on to your ways, and see how his sovereign majesty will torture you and your descendants!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> After him they brought the sixth. When he was about to die, he said, “Don’t be vainly deceived, for we suffer these things for our own doings, as sinning against our own God. Astounding things have come to pass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> but don’t think that you will be unpunished, having tried to fight against God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But above all, the mother was marvelous and worthy of honorable memory; for when she watched seven sons perishing within the space of one day, she bore the sight with a good courage because of her hope in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> She exhorted each one of them in the language of their fathers, filled with a noble spirit and stirring up her woman’s thoughts with manly courage, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> “I don’t know how you came into my womb. It wasn’t I who gave you your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, breath">e</a></sup>spirit and your life. It wasn’t I who brought into order the first elements of each one of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Therefore the Creator of the world, who shaped the first origin of man and devised the first origin of all things, in mercy gives back to you again both your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, breath">f</a></sup>spirit and your life, as you now treat yourselves with contempt for his laws’ sake.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But Antiochus, thinking himself to be despised, and suspecting the reproachful voice, while the youngest was yet alive didn’t only make his appeal to him by words, but also at the same time promised with oaths that he would enrich him and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. make him one that is counted happy.">g</a></sup>raise him to high honor if he would turn from the ways of his ancestors, and that he would take him for his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See 2 Maccabees 8:9.">h</a></sup>friend and entrust him with public affairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But when the young man would in no way listen, the king called to him his mother, and urged her to advise the youth to save himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When he had urged her with many words, she undertook to persuade her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But bending toward him, laughing the cruel tyrant to scorn, she spoke this in the language of her fathers: “My son, have pity upon me who carried you nine months in my womb, and nursed you three years, and nourished and brought you up to this age, and sustained you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> I beg you, my child, to lift your eyes to the sky and the earth, and to see all things that are in it, and thus to recognize that God made them not of things that were, and that the race of men in this way comes into being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Don’t be afraid of this butcher, but, proving yourself worthy of your brothers, accept your death, that in God’s mercy I may receive you again with your brothers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> But before she had finished speaking, the young man said, “What are you all waiting for? I don’t obey the commandment of the king, but I listen to the commandment of the law that was given to our fathers through Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But you, who have devised all kinds of evil against the Hebrews, will in no way escape God’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For we are suffering because of our own sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> If for rebuke and chastening, our living Lord has been angered a little while, yet he will again be reconciled with his own servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> But you, O unholy man and of all most vile, don’t be vainly lifted up in your wild pride with uncertain hopes, raising your hand against the heavenly children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For you have not yet escaped the judgment of the Almighty God who sees all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For these our brothers, having endured a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. short pain of ever-flowing life.">i</a></sup>short pain that brings everlasting life, have now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. fallen. By the omission of one Greek letter the words would signify having endured a short pain, have now drunk of ever-flowing life under God’s covenant.">j</a></sup>died under God’s covenant. But you, through God’s judgment, will receive in just measure the penalties of your arrogance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But I, as my brothers, give up both body and soul for the laws of our fathers, calling upon God that he may speedily become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. propitious.">k</a></sup>gracious to the nation, and that you, amidst trials and plagues, may confess that he alone is God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and that in me and my brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Some authorities read may be stayed.">l</a></sup>you may bring to an end the wrath of the Almighty which has been justly brought upon our whole race.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But the king, falling into a rage, handled him worse than all the rest, being exasperated at his mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So he also died pure, putting his whole trust in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Last of all, after her sons, the mother died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Let it then suffice to have said thus much concerning the sacrificial feasts and the extreme tortures.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
