<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Kings 11:21; 2 Kings 11:1–4"> Jehoash began to reign in the seventh year of Jehu, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Zibiah of Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:17–22; 2 Chronicles 26:4; 2 Chronicles 25:2"> Jehoash did that which was right in Yahweh’s eyes all his days in which Jehoiada the priest instructed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 1 Kings 22:43; 1 Kings 15:14"> However, the high places were not taken away. The people still sacrificed and burned incense in the high places.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 22:4; Exodus 35:5; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9"> Jehoash said to the priests, “All the money of the holy things that is brought into Yahweh’s house, in current money, the money of the people for whom each man is evaluated, and all the money that it comes into any man’s heart to bring into Yahweh’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; 1 Kings 11:27; 2 Kings 22:5–6"> let the priests take it to them, each man from his donor; and they shall repair the damage to the house, wherever any damage is found.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 1 Peter 5:2; Isaiah 56:10–12"> But it was so, that in the twenty-third year of King Jehoash the priests had not repaired the damage to the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:16; 2 Chronicles 24:5–14; 2 Kings 11:4"> Then King Jehoash called for Jehoiada the priest, and for the other priests, and said to them, “Why aren’t you repairing the damage to the house? Now therefore take no more money from your treasurers, but deliver it for repair of the damage to the house.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The priests consented that they should take no more money from the people, and not repair the damage to the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 12:41; Psalms 84:10; Jeremiah 35:4"> But Jehoiada the priest took a chest and bored a hole in its lid, and set it beside the altar, on the right side as one comes into Yahweh’s house; and the priests who kept the threshold put all the money that was brought into Yahweh’s house into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Kings 22:12; 2 Kings 19:2"> When they saw that there was much money in the chest, the king’s scribe and the high priest came up, and they put it in bags and counted the money that was found in Yahweh’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 22:5–6; 2 Chronicles 24:11–12; 2 Chronicles 34:9–11"> They gave the money that was weighed out into the hands of those who did the work, who had the oversight of Yahweh’s house; and they paid it out to the carpenters and the builders who worked on Yahweh’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 22:5–6; 1 Kings 5:17–18; Ezra 3:7"> and to the masons and the stone cutters, and for buying timber and cut stone to repair the damage to Yahweh’s house, and for all that was laid out for the house to repair it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:14; 1 Kings 7:48–50; Ezra 1:9–11"> But there were not made for Yahweh’s house cups of silver, snuffers, basins, trumpets, any vessels of gold or vessels of silver, of the money that was brought into Yahweh’s house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> for they gave that to those who did the work, and repaired Yahweh’s house with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 22:7; 3 John 1:5; 1 Corinthians 4:2–3"> Moreover they didn’t demand an accounting from the men into whose hand they delivered the money to give to those who did the work; for they dealt faithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 5:15–18; Leviticus 4:24"> The money for the trespass offerings and the money for the sin offerings was not brought into Yahweh’s house. It was the priests’.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:23–24; Luke 9:53; Jeremiah 42:15"> Then Hazael king of Syria went up and fought against Gath, and took it; and Hazael set his face to go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Kings 18:15–16; 2 Kings 12:4"> Jehoash king of Judah took all the holy things that Jehoshaphat and Jehoram and Ahaziah, his fathers, kings of Judah, had dedicated, and his own holy things, and all the gold that was found in the treasures of Yahweh’s house, and of the king’s house, and sent it to Hazael king of Syria; and he went away from Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:19; 1 Kings 11:41"> Now the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:24–27; 2 Kings 14:5; 2 Samuel 5:9"> His servants arose and made a conspiracy, and struck Joash at the house of Millo, on the way that goes down to Silla.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 14:1; 2 Chronicles 24:26–27"> For Jozacar the son of Shimeath, and Jehozabad the son of Shomer, his servants, struck him, and he died; and they buried him with his fathers in David’s city; and Amaziah his son reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
