We saw his glory, such glory as of the only born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενους”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">b</a></sup>Son of the Father, full of grace and truth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; Romans 5:2; Ephesians 2:5–10"> From his fullness we all received grace upon grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:14; John 14:6; Romans 3:19–26"> For the law was given through Moses. Grace and truth were realized through Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">c</a></sup></span>

The only born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενη”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">d</a></sup>Son,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="NU reads “God”">e</a></sup>who is in the bosom of the Father, has declared him.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> This is John’s testimony, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, “Who are you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> He declared, and didn’t deny, but he declared, “I am not the Christ.”</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> He said, “I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, ‘Make straight the way of the Lord,’ as Isaiah the prophet said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John answered them, “I baptize in water, but among you stands one whom you don’t know.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> These things were done in Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> I didn’t know him, but for this reason I came baptizing in water, that he would be revealed to Israel.”</span>

You shall be called Cephas”</span>(which is by interpretation, Peter).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“Cephas” (Aramaic) and “Peter” (Greek) both mean “Rock”.">i</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> On the next day, he was determined to go out into Galilee, and he found Philip. Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Follow me.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip found Nathanael, and said to him, “We have found him of whom Moses in the law and also the prophets, wrote: Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> Nathanael said to him, “Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?”<br />Philip said to him, “Come and see.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalms 32:2; Psalms 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and said about him,<span className="woj">“Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalms 139:1–2"> Nathanael said to him, “How do you know me?”<br />Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> Nathanael answered him, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are King of Israel!”</span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“Because I told you, ‘I saw you underneath the fig tree,’ do you believe? You will see greater things than these!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> He said to him,<span className="woj">“Most certainly, I tell you all, hereafter you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.”</span></span>
