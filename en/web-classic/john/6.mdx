<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 6:15; Luke 9:28; Luke 6:12–13"> Jesus went up into the mountain, and he sat there with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 2:13; John 11:55; John 5:1"> Now the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Luke 9:12–13; Matthew 14:14–15"> Jesus therefore, lifting up his eyes and seeing that a great multitude was coming to him, said to Philip,<span className="woj">“Where are we to buy bread, that these may eat?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Deuteronomy 13:3; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> He said this to test him, for he himself knew what he would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 11:21–22; Mark 6:37; John 12:5"> Philip answered him, “Two hundred denarii<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A denarius was a silver coin worth about a day’s wages for an agricultural laborer, so 200 denarii would be between 6 and 7 month’s pay.">a</a></sup>worth of bread is not sufficient for them, that every one of them may receive a little.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:40–44; Matthew 4:18"> One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 14:17; Mark 6:38"> “There is a boy here who has five barley loaves and two fish, but what are these among so many?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Matthew 15:35–36; Mark 6:39–41"> Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Have the people sit down.”</span>Now there was much grass in that place. So the men sat down, in number about five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> Jesus took the loaves, and having given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to those who were sitting down, likewise also of the fish as much as they desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> When they were filled, he said to his disciples,<span className="woj">“Gather up the broken pieces which are left over, that nothing be lost.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; 2 Kings 4:2–7; 2 Corinthians 9:8–9"> So they gathered them up, and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces from the five barley loaves, which were left over by those who had eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:37"> When therefore the people saw the sign which Jesus did, they said, “This is truly the prophet who comes into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:36; John 6:15–21; John 7:3–4"> Jesus therefore, perceiving that they were about to come and take him by force to make him king, withdrew again to the mountain by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When evening came, his disciples went down to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> They entered into the boat, and were going over the sea to Capernaum. It was now dark, and Jesus had not come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:25; Psalms 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> The sea was tossed by a great wind blowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> When therefore they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="25 to 30 stadia is about 5 to 6 kilometers or about 3 to 4 miles">b</a></sup>they saw Jesus walking on the sea and drawing near to the boat; and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> But he said to them,<span className="woj">“It is I.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, I AM">c</a></sup><span className="woj">Don’t be afraid.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> They were willing therefore to receive him into the boat. Immediately the boat was at the land where they were going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> On the next day, the multitude that stood on the other side of the sea saw that there was no other boat there, except the one in which his disciples had embarked, and that Jesus hadn’t entered with his disciples into the boat, but his disciples had gone away alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> However, boats from Tiberias came near to the place where they ate the bread after the Lord had given thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> When the multitude therefore saw that Jesus wasn’t there, nor his disciples, they themselves got into the boats and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> When they found him on the other side of the sea, they asked him, “Rabbi, when did you come here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, you seek me, not because you saw signs, but because you ate of the loaves and were filled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> <span className="woj">Don’t work for the food which perishes, but for the food which remains to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give to you. For God the Father has sealed him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> They said therefore to him, “What must we do, that we may work the works of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“This is the work of God, that you believe in him whom he has sent.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> They said therefore to him, “What then do you do for a sign, that we may see and believe you? What work do you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24–25"> Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness. As it is written, ‘He gave them bread out of heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Greek and Hebrew use the same word for “heaven”, “the heavens”, “the sky”, and “the air”.">d</a></sup>to eat.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Jesus therefore said to them,<span className="woj">“Most certainly, I tell you, it wasn’t Moses who gave you the bread out of heaven, but my Father gives you the true bread out of heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> <span className="woj">For the bread of God is that which comes down out of heaven and gives life to the world.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; Psalms 4:6"> They said therefore to him, “Lord, always give us this bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to me will not be hungry, and whoever believes in me will never be thirsty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> <span className="woj">But I told you that you have seen me, and yet you don’t believe.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> <span className="woj">All those whom the Father gives me will come to me. He who comes to me I will in no way throw out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> <span className="woj">For I have come down from heaven, not to do my own will, but the will of him who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> <span className="woj">This is the will of my Father who sent me, that of all he has given to me I should lose nothing, but should raise him up at the last day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> <span className="woj">This is the will of the one who sent me, that everyone who sees the Son and believes in him should have eternal life; and I will raise him up at the last day.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> The Jews therefore murmured concerning him, because he said,<span className="woj">“I am the bread which came down out of heaven.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> Therefore Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Don’t murmur among yourselves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> <span className="woj">No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him; and I will raise him up in the last day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> <span className="woj">It is written in the prophets, ‘They will all be taught by God.’</span><span className="woj">Therefore everyone who hears from the Father and has learned, comes to me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> <span className="woj">Not that anyone has seen the Father, except he who is from God. He has seen the Father.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> <span className="woj">Most certainly, I tell you, he who believes in me has eternal life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> <span className="woj">I am the bread of life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> <span className="woj">Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness and they died.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> <span className="woj">This is the bread which comes down out of heaven, that anyone may eat of it and not die.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> <span className="woj">I am the living bread which came down out of heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever. Yes, the bread which I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> The Jews therefore contended with one another, saying, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> Jesus therefore said to them,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you don’t have life in yourselves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> <span className="woj">He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> <span className="woj">For my flesh is food indeed, and my blood is drink indeed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> <span className="woj">He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood lives in me, and I in him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> <span className="woj">As the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father, so he who feeds on me will also live because of me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> <span className="woj">This is the bread which came down out of heaven—not as our fathers ate the manna and died. He who eats this bread will live forever.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> He said these things in the synagogue, as he taught in Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> Therefore many of his disciples, when they heard this, said, “This is a hard saying! Who can listen to it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> But Jesus knowing in himself that his disciples murmured at this, said to them,<span className="woj">“Does this cause you to stumble?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> <span className="woj">Then what if you would see the Son of Man ascending to where he was before?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalms 119:93"> <span className="woj">It is the spirit who gives life. The flesh profits nothing. The words that I speak to you are spirit, and are life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> <span className="woj">But there are some of you who don’t believe.”</span>For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were who didn’t believe, and who it was who would betray him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> He said,<span className="woj">“For this cause I have said to you that no one can come to me, unless it is given to him by my Father.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Luke 9:62; 1 John 2:19; Hebrews 10:38"> At this, many of his disciples went back and walked no more with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> Jesus said therefore to the twelve,<span className="woj">“You don’t also want to go away, do you?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Simon Peter answered him, “Lord, to whom would we go? You have the words of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="John 11:27; Luke 9:20; Mark 8:29"> We have come to believe and know that you are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="John 13:27; John 13:2; John 17:12"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Didn’t I choose you, the twelve, and one of you is a devil?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:14–16; Jude 1:4; Acts 1:16–20"> Now he spoke of Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot, for it was he who would betray him, being one of the twelve.</span>
