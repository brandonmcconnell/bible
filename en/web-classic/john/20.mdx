</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Therefore Peter and the other disciple went out, and they went toward the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> They both ran together. The other disciple outran Peter and came to the tomb first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> Stooping and looking in, he saw the linen cloths lying there; yet he didn’t enter in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Then Simon Peter came, following him, and entered into the tomb. He saw the linen cloths lying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> and the cloth that had been on his head, not lying with the linen cloths, but rolled up in a place by itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> So then the other disciple who came first to the tomb also entered in, and he saw and believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> For as yet they didn’t know the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> So the disciples went away again to their own homes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary was standing outside at the tomb weeping. So as she wept, she stooped and looked into the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> and she saw two angels in white sitting, one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> They asked her, “Woman, why are you weeping?”<br />She said to them, “Because they have taken away my Lord, and I don’t know where they have laid him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> When she had said this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing, and didn’t know that it was Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Woman, why are you weeping? Who are you looking for?”</span><br />She, supposing him to be the gardener, said to him, “Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him away.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Mary.”</span><br />She turned and said to him, “Rabboni!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Rabboni is a transliteration of the Aramaic word for “great teacher.”">a</a></sup>which is to say, “Teacher!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, Master">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Don’t hold me, for I haven’t yet ascended to my Father; but go to my brothers and tell them, ‘I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary Magdalene came and told the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that he had said these things to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> When therefore it was evening on that day, the first day of the week, and when the doors were locked where the disciples were assembled, for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the middle and said to them,<span className="woj">“Peace be to you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> When he had said this, he showed them his hands and his side. The disciples therefore were glad when they saw the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> Jesus therefore said to them again,<span className="woj">“Peace be to you. As the Father has sent me, even so I send you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> When he had said this, he breathed on them, and said to them,<span className="woj">“Receive the Holy Spirit!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> <span className="woj">If you forgive anyone’s sins, they have been forgiven them. If you retain anyone’s sins, they have been retained.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> But Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, Twin">c</a></sup>wasn’t with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> The other disciples therefore said to him, “We have seen the Lord!”<br />But he said to them, “Unless I see in his hands the print of the nails, put my finger into the print of the nails, and put my hand into his side, I will not believe.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> After eight days, again his disciples were inside and Thomas was with them. Jesus came, the doors being locked, and stood in the middle, and said,<span className="woj">“Peace be to you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then he said to Thomas,<span className="woj">“Reach here your finger, and see my hands. Reach here your hand, and put it into my side. Don’t be unbelieving, but believing.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> Thomas answered him, “My Lord and my God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Because you have seen me,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="TR adds “Thomas,”">d</a></sup><span className="woj">you have believed. Blessed are those who have not seen and have believed.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Therefore Jesus did many other signs in the presence of his disciples, which are not written in this book;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> but these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name.</span>
</div>
