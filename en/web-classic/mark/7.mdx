<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 15:1–20; Mark 3:22; Luke 5:17"> Then the Pharisees and some of the scribes gathered together to him, having come from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 7:3–5; Romans 14:14"> Now when they saw some of his disciples eating bread with defiled, that is unwashed, hands, they found fault.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> (For the Pharisees and all the Jews don’t eat unless they wash their hands and forearms, holding to the tradition of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:10; Matthew 23:25; Luke 11:38–39"> They don’t eat when they come from the marketplace unless they bathe themselves, and there are many other things which they have received to hold to: washings of cups, pitchers, bronze vessels, and couches.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 21:21; Matthew 15:2"> The Pharisees and the scribes asked him, “Why don’t your disciples walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat their bread with unwashed hands?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 15:7–9"> He answered them,<span className="woj">“Well did Isaiah prophesy of you hypocrites, as it is written,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘This people honors me with their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">but their heart is far from me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:9; James 1:26"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">They worship me in vain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Isaiah 1:12"> <span className="woj">“For you set aside the commandment of God, and hold tightly to the tradition of men—the washing of pitchers and cups, and you do many other such things.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Galatians 2:21; Isaiah 29:13"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Full well do you reject the commandment of God, that you may keep your tradition.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16"> <span className="woj">For Moses said, ‘Honor your father and your mother;’</span><span className="woj">and, ‘He who speaks evil of father or mother, let him be put to death.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 23:18; 1 Timothy 5:4–8; Matthew 15:5"> <span className="woj">But you say, ‘If a man tells his father or his mother, “Whatever profit you might have received from me is Corban,”’”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Corban is a Hebrew word for an offering devoted to God.">a</a></sup>that is to say, given to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="woj">“then you no longer allow him to do anything for his father or his mother,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:9; Titus 1:14; Matthew 5:17–20"> <span className="woj">making void the word of God by your tradition which you have handed down. You do many things like this.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> He called all the multitude to himself and said to them,<span className="woj">“Hear me, all of you, and understand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> <span className="woj">There is nothing from outside of the man that going into him can defile him; but the things which proceed out of the man are those that defile the man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="woj">If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU omits verse 16.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> When he had entered into a house away from the multitude, his disciples asked him about the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Are you also without understanding? Don’t you perceive that whatever goes into the man from outside can’t defile him,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> <span className="woj">because it doesn’t go into his heart, but into his stomach, then into the latrine, making all foods clean?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NU ends Jesus’ direct quote and question after “latrine”, ending the verse with “Thus he declared all foods clean.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> He said,<span className="woj">“That which proceeds out of the man, that defiles the man.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> <span className="woj">For from within, out of the hearts of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, sexual sins, murders, thefts,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> <span className="woj">covetings, wickedness, deceit, lustful desires, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, and foolishness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> <span className="woj">All these evil things come from within and defile the man.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> From there he arose and went away into the borders of Tyre and Sidon. He entered into a house and didn’t want anyone to know it, but he couldn’t escape notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> For a woman whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, having heard of him, came and fell down at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> Now the woman was a Greek, a Syrophoenician by race. She begged him that he would cast the demon out of her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> But Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Let the children be filled first, for it is not appropriate to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> But she answered him, “Yes, Lord. Yet even the dogs under the table eat the children’s crumbs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> He said to her,<span className="woj">“For this saying, go your way. The demon has gone out of your daughter.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> She went away to her house, and found the child having been laid on the bed, with the demon gone out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> Again he departed from the borders of Tyre and Sidon, and came to the sea of Galilee through the middle of the region of Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> They brought to him one who was deaf and had an impediment in his speech. They begged him to lay his hand on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> He took him aside from the multitude privately and put his fingers into his ears; and he spat and touched his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> Looking up to heaven, he sighed, and said to him,<span className="woj">“Ephphatha!”</span>that is,<span className="woj">“Be opened!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> Immediately his ears were opened, and the impediment of his tongue was released, and he spoke clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> He commanded them that they should tell no one, but the more he commanded them, so much the more widely they proclaimed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> They were astonished beyond measure, saying, “He has done all things well. He makes even the deaf hear and the mute speak!”</span>
