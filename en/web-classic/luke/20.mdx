<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> On one of those days, as he was teaching the people in the temple and preaching the Good News, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “chief”">a</a></sup>priests and scribes came to him with the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> They asked him, “Tell us: by what authority do you do these things? Or who is giving you this authority?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> He answered them,<span className="woj">“I also will ask you one question. Tell me:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> <span className="woj">the baptism of John, was it from heaven, or from men?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> They reasoned with themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ he will say, ‘Why didn’t you believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But if we say, ‘From men,’ all the people will stone us, for they are persuaded that John was a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> They answered that they didn’t know where it was from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> He began to tell the people this parable:<span className="woj">“A</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU (in brackets) and TR add “certain”">b</a></sup><span className="woj">man planted a vineyard and rented it out to some farmers, and went into another country for a long time.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> <span className="woj">At the proper season, he sent a servant to the farmers to collect his share of the fruit of the vineyard. But the farmers beat him and sent him away empty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> <span className="woj">He sent yet another servant, and they also beat him and treated him shamefully, and sent him away empty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="woj">He sent yet a third, and they also wounded him and threw him out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> <span className="woj">The lord of the vineyard said, ‘What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. It may be that seeing him, they will respect him.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> <span className="woj">“But when the farmers saw him, they reasoned among themselves, saying, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> <span className="woj">Then they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. What therefore will the lord of the vineyard do to them?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> <span className="woj">He will come and destroy these farmers, and will give the vineyard to others.”</span>When they heard that, they said, “May that never be!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> But he looked at them and said,<span className="woj">“Then what is this that is written,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">was made the chief cornerstone’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">but it will crush whomever it falls on to dust.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> The chief priests and the scribes sought to lay hands on him that very hour, but they feared the people—for they knew he had spoken this parable against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> They watched him and sent out spies, who pretended to be righteous, that they might trap him in something he said, so as to deliver him up to the power and authority of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> They asked him, “Teacher, we know that you say and teach what is right, and aren’t partial to anyone, but truly teach the way of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it lawful for us to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But he perceived their craftiness, and said to them,<span className="woj">“Why do you test me?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> <span className="woj">Show me a denarius. Whose image and inscription are on it?”</span>They answered, “Caesar’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Then give to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> They weren’t able to trap him in his words before the people. They marveled at his answer and were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> Some of the Sadducees came to him, those who deny that there is a resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> They asked him, “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man’s brother dies having a wife, and he is childless, his brother should take the wife and raise up children for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> There were therefore seven brothers. The first took a wife, and died childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The second took her as wife, and he died childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The third took her, and likewise the seven all left no children, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> Afterward the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> Therefore in the resurrection whose wife of them will she be? For the seven had her as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“The children of this age marry and are given in marriage.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> <span className="woj">But those who are considered worthy to attain to that age and the resurrection from the dead neither marry nor are given in marriage.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> <span className="woj">For they can’t die any more, for they are like the angels and are children of God, being children of the resurrection.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> <span className="woj">But that the dead are raised, even Moses showed at the bush, when he called the Lord ‘The God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> <span className="woj">Now he is not the God of the dead, but of the living, for all are alive to him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> Some of the scribes answered, “Teacher, you speak well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> They didn’t dare to ask him any more questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Why do they say that the Christ is David’s son?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> <span className="woj">David himself says in the book of Psalms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">“Sit at my right hand,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalms 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> <span className="pl pl-2 woj">until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet.”’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> <span className="woj">“David therefore calls him Lord, so how is he his son?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> In the hearing of all the people, he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> <span className="woj">“Beware of those scribes who like to walk in long robes, and love greetings in the marketplaces, the best seats in the synagogues, and the best places at feasts;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> <span className="woj">who devour widows’ houses, and for a pretense make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation.”</span></span>
