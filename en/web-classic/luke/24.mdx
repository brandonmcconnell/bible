<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> They remembered his words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> returned from the tomb, and told all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> Now they were Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Mary the mother of James. The other women with them told these things to the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> These words seemed to them to be nonsense, and they didn’t believe them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. Stooping and looking in, he saw the strips of linen lying by themselves, and he departed to his home, wondering what had happened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> Behold, two of them were going that very day to a village named Emmaus, which was sixty stadia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="60 stadia = about 11 kilometers or about 7 miles.">a</a></sup>from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> They talked with each other about all of these things which had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> While they talked and questioned together, Jesus himself came near, and went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> But their eyes were kept from recognizing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“What are you talking about as you walk, and are sad?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> One of them, named Cleopas, answered him, “Are you the only stranger in Jerusalem who doesn’t know the things which have happened there in these days?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“What things?”</span><br />They said to him, “The things concerning Jesus the Nazarene, who was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered him up to be condemned to death, and crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we were hoping that it was he who would redeem Israel. Yes, and besides all this, it is now the third day since these things happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> Also, certain women of our company amazed us, having arrived early at the tomb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and when they didn’t find his body, they came saying that they had also seen a vision of angels, who said that he was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> Some of us went to the tomb and found it just like the women had said, but they didn’t see him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Foolish people, and slow of heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> <span className="woj">Didn’t the Christ have to suffer these things and to enter into his glory?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> Beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, he explained to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> They came near to the village where they were going, and he acted like he would go further.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> They urged him, saying, “Stay with us, for it is almost evening, and the day is almost over.”<br />He went in to stay with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> When he had sat down at the table with them, he took the bread and gave thanks. Breaking it, he gave it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> Their eyes were opened and they recognized him; then he vanished out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> They said to one another, “Weren’t our hearts burning within us while he spoke to us along the way, and while he opened the Scriptures to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> They rose up that very hour, returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven gathered together, and those who were with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> saying, “The Lord is risen indeed, and has appeared to Simon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> They related the things that happened along the way, and how he was recognized by them in the breaking of the bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> As they said these things, Jesus himself stood among them, and said to them,<span className="woj">“Peace be to you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But they were terrified and filled with fear, and supposed that they had seen a spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Why are you troubled? Why do doubts arise in your hearts?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> <span className="woj">See my hands and my feet, that it is truly me. Touch me and see, for a spirit doesn’t have flesh and bones, as you see that I have.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> When he had said this, he showed them his hands and his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> While they still didn’t believe for joy, and wondered, he said to them,<span className="woj">“Do you have anything here to eat?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> They gave him a piece of a broiled fish and some honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> He took them, and ate in front of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“This is what I told you while I was still with you, that all things which are written in the law of Moses, the prophets, and the psalms concerning me must be fulfilled.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then he opened their minds, that they might understand the Scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Thus it is written, and thus it was necessary for the Christ to suffer and to rise from the dead the third day,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> <span className="woj">and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name to all the nations, beginning at Jerusalem.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> <span className="woj">You are witnesses of these things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> <span className="woj">Behold, I send out the promise of my Father on you. But wait in the city of Jerusalem until you are clothed with power from on high.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> He led them out as far as Bethany, and he lifted up his hands and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> While he blessed them, he withdrew from them and was carried up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> They worshiped him and returned to Jerusalem with great joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> and were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.</span>
</div>
