<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">A stingy man hurries after riches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and doesn’t know that poverty waits for him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">One who rebukes a man will afterward find more favor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than one who flatters with the tongue.</span></span>
