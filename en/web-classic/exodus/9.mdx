<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 8:1; Exodus 10:3; Exodus 8:20"> Then Yahweh said to Moses, “Go in to Pharaoh, and tell him, ‘This is what Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews, says: “Let my people go, that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 8:2; Exodus 4:23; Leviticus 26:27–28"> For if you refuse to let them go, and hold them still,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:4; Acts 13:11; 1 Samuel 6:9"> behold, Yahweh’s hand is on your livestock which are in the field, on the horses, on the donkeys, on the camels, on the herds, and on the flocks with a very grievous pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 8:22; Exodus 10:23; Malachi 3:18"> Yahweh will make a distinction between the livestock of Israel and the livestock of Egypt; and nothing shall die of all that belongs to the children of Israel.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 8:23; Exodus 10:4; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> Yahweh appointed a set time, saying, “Tomorrow Yahweh shall do this thing in the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:48; Exodus 9:25; Psalms 78:50"> Yahweh did that thing on the next day; and all the livestock of Egypt died, but of the livestock of the children of Israel, not one died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 7:14; Exodus 8:32; Isaiah 48:4"> Pharaoh sent, and, behold, there was not so much as one of the livestock of the Israelites dead. But the heart of Pharaoh was stubborn, and he didn’t let the people go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 8:16"> Yahweh said to Moses and to Aaron, “Take handfuls of ashes of the furnace, and let Moses sprinkle it toward the sky in the sight of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:2; Deuteronomy 28:27; Job 2:7"> It shall become small dust over all the land of Egypt, and shall be boils and blisters breaking out on man and on animal, throughout all the land of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27"> They took ashes of the furnace, and stood before Pharaoh; and Moses sprinkled it up toward the sky; and it became boils and blisters breaking out on man and on animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 7:11–12; Exodus 8:18–19; Revelation 16:2"> The magicians couldn’t stand before Moses because of the boils; for the boils were on the magicians and on all the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 4:21; Exodus 7:13–14; Psalms 81:11–12"> Yahweh hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he didn’t listen to them, as Yahweh had spoken to Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 8:20; Exodus 7:15; Exodus 9:1"> Yahweh said to Moses, “Rise up early in the morning, and stand before Pharaoh, and tell him, ‘This is what Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews, says: “Let my people go, that they may serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 8:10; Leviticus 26:28; 1 Chronicles 17:20"> For this time I will send all my plagues against your heart, against your officials, and against your people; that you may know that there is no one like me in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:20; Exodus 9:3; Exodus 9:16"> For now I would have stretched out my hand, and struck you and your people with pestilence, and you would have been cut off from the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:17; Proverbs 16:4; Psalms 83:17–18"> but indeed for this cause I have made you stand: to show you my power, and that my name may be declared throughout all the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 10:15; Isaiah 37:23–24"> because you still exalt yourself against my people, that you won’t let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 20:6; Psalms 83:15; Exodus 9:22–25"> Behold, tomorrow about this time I will cause it to rain a very grievous hail, such as has not been in Egypt since the day it was founded even until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2"> Now therefore command that all of your livestock and all that you have in the field be brought into shelter. The hail will come down on every man and animal that is found in the field, and isn’t brought home, and they will die.”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:13; Proverbs 22:3; Hebrews 11:7"> Those who feared Yahweh’s word among the servants of Pharaoh made their servants and their livestock flee into the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Job 34:14; 1 Chronicles 22:19"> Whoever didn’t respect Yahweh’s word left his servants and his livestock in the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> Yahweh said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand toward the sky, that there may be hail in all the land of Egypt, on man, and on animal, and on every herb of the field, throughout the land of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 18:13; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 30:30"> Moses stretched out his rod toward the heavens, and Yahweh sent thunder and hail; and lightning flashed down to the earth. Yahweh rained hail on the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 9:23; Matthew 24:21; Exodus 10:6"> So there was very severe hail, and lightning mixed with the hail, such as had not been in all the land of Egypt since it became a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Psalms 105:32–33"> The hail struck throughout all the land of Egypt all that was in the field, both man and animal; and the hail struck every herb of the field, and broke every tree of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Exodus 9:4; Exodus 10:23"> Only in the land of Goshen, where the children of Israel were, there was no hail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 1:18; Psalms 129:4; Exodus 10:16"> Pharaoh sent and called for Moses and Aaron, and said to them, “I have sinned this time. Yahweh is righteous, and I and my people are wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Exodus 10:17; Exodus 8:28"> Pray to Yahweh; for there has been enough of mighty thunderings and hail. I will let you go, and you shall stay no longer.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; 1 Kings 8:38; Isaiah 1:15"> Moses said to him, “As soon as I have gone out of the city, I will spread out my hands to Yahweh. The thunders shall cease, and there will not be any more hail; that you may know that the earth is Yahweh’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Proverbs 16:6; Isaiah 63:17"> But as for you and your servants, I know that you don’t yet fear Yahweh God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Ruth 2:23; Habakkuk 3:17"> The flax and the barley were struck, for the barley had ripened and the flax was blooming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 10:22"> But the wheat and the spelt were not struck, for they had not grown up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 9:29; Exodus 8:12; James 5:17–18"> Moses went out of the city from Pharaoh, and spread out his hands to Yahweh; and the thunders and hail ceased, and the rain was not poured on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 7:14; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Exodus 4:21"> When Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunders had ceased, he sinned yet more, and hardened his heart, he and his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 4:21"> The heart of Pharaoh was hardened, and he didn’t let the children of Israel go, just as Yahweh had spoken through Moses.</span>
</div>
