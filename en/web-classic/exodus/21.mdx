<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:14; Deuteronomy 6:20; Exodus 24:3–4"> “Now these are the ordinances which you shall set before them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–15; Exodus 12:44; Deuteronomy 15:18"> “If you buy a Hebrew servant, he shall serve six years, and in the seventh he shall go out free without paying anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12–14"> If he comes in by himself, he shall go out by himself. If he is married, then his wife shall go out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If his master gives him a wife and she bears him sons or daughters, the wife and her children shall be her master’s, and he shall go out by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:14–15; Deuteronomy 15:16–17; Isaiah 26:13"> But if the servant shall plainly say, ‘I love my master, my wife, and my children. I will not go out free;’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:17; Leviticus 25:40; Deuteronomy 19:17–18"> then his master shall bring him to God, and shall bring him to the door or to the doorpost, and his master shall bore his ear through with an awl, and he shall serve him forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; Exodus 21:2–3"> “If a man sells his daughter to be a female servant, she shall not go out as the male servants do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:8; Deuteronomy 20:7; Malachi 2:11–15"> If she doesn’t please her master, who has married her to himself, then he shall let her be redeemed. He shall have no right to sell her to a foreign people, since he has dealt deceitfully with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If he marries her to his son, he shall deal with her as a daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1–6"> If he takes another wife to himself, he shall not diminish her food, her clothing, and her marital rights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> If he doesn’t do these three things for her, she may go free without paying any money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; Matthew 26:52; Leviticus 24:17"> “One who strikes a man so that he dies shall surely be put to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 20:2–9; 1 Samuel 24:18; Deuteronomy 4:41–43"> but not if it is unintentional, but God allows it to happen; then I will appoint you a place where he shall flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; Hebrews 10:26; Numbers 15:30–31"> If a man schemes and comes presumptuously on his neighbor to kill him, you shall take him from my altar, that he may die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:18–21; Proverbs 30:17; 1 Timothy 1:9"> “Anyone who attacks his father or his mother shall be surely put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:4"> “Anyone who kidnaps someone and sells him, or if he is found in his hand, he shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:20; Mark 7:10–11; Deuteronomy 27:16"> “Anyone who curses his father or his mother shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Deuteronomy 25:11; 2 Samuel 14:6"> “If men quarrel and one strikes the other with a stone, or with his fist, and he doesn’t die, but is confined to bed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:29; Zechariah 8:4"> if he rises again and walks around with his staff, then he who struck him shall be cleared; only he shall pay for the loss of his time, and shall provide for his healing until he is thoroughly healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:19; Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:19"> “If a man strikes his servant or his maid with a rod, and he dies under his hand, the man shall surely be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 25:44–46"> Notwithstanding, if his servant gets up after a day or two, he shall not be punished, for the servant is his property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:18–19; Exodus 21:30; Deuteronomy 16:18"> “If men fight and hurt a pregnant woman so that she gives birth prematurely, and yet no harm follows, he shall be surely fined as much as the woman’s husband demands and the judges allow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:19"> But if any harm follows, then you must take life for life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38–40; Matthew 7:2"> eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> burning for burning, wound for wound, and bruise for bruise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 21:20; Job 31:13–15; Psalms 72:12–14"> “If a man strikes his servant’s eye, or his maid’s eye, and destroys it, he shall let him go free for his eye’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> If he strikes out his male servant’s tooth, or his female servant’s tooth, he shall let the servant go free for his tooth’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Leviticus 20:15–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> “If a bull gores a man or a woman to death, the bull shall surely be stoned, and its meat shall not be eaten; but the owner of the bull shall not be held responsible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:1–9"> But if the bull had a habit of goring in the past, and this has been testified to its owner, and he has not kept it in, but it has killed a man or a woman, the bull shall be stoned, and its owner shall also be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 21:22; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 35:31–33"> If a ransom is imposed on him, then he shall give for the redemption of his life whatever is imposed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Whether it has gored a son or has gored a daughter, according to this judgment it shall be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 27:3–9"> If the bull gores a male servant or a female servant, thirty shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces, so 30 shekels is about 300 grams or about 10.6 ounces.">a</a></sup>of silver shall be given to their master, and the ox shall be stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Jeremiah 18:20; Ecclesiastes 10:8"> “If a man opens a pit, or if a man digs a pit and doesn’t cover it, and a bull or a donkey falls into it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 22:6; Exodus 21:29–30; Exodus 22:14"> the owner of the pit shall make it good. He shall give money to its owner, and the dead animal shall be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> “If one man’s bull injures another’s, so that it dies, then they shall sell the live bull, and divide its price; and they shall also divide the dead animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Or if it is known that the bull was in the habit of goring in the past, and its owner has not kept it in, he shall surely pay bull for bull, and the dead animal shall be his own.</span>
