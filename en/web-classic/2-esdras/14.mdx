<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> It came to pass upon the third day, I sat under an oak, and, behold, a voice came out of a bush near me, and said, “Esdras, Esdras!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I said, “Here I am, Lord,” and I stood up on my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then he said to me, “I revealed myself in a bush and talked with Moses when my people were in bondage in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I sent him, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Another reading is. I.">a</a></sup>he led my people out of Egypt. I brought him up to Mount Sinai, where I kept him with me for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> I told him many wondrous things, and showed him the secrets of the times and the end of the seasons. I commanded him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> ‘You shall publish these openly, and these you shall hide.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now I say to you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Lay up in your heart the signs that I have shown, the dreams that you have seen, and the interpretations which you have heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> for you will be taken away from men, and from now on you will live with my Son and with those who are like you, until the times have ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the world has lost its youth, and the times begin to grow old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Verses 11, 12 are omitted in the Syriac. The Aethiopic has For the age is divided into ten parts, and is come to the tenth: and half of the tenth remains. Now etc.">b</a></sup>For the age is divided into twelve parts, and ten parts of it are already gone,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lat. and.">c</a></sup>even the half of the tenth part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There remain of it two parts after the middle of the tenth part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now therefore set your house in order, reprove your people, comfort the lowly among them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Latin alone omits and...wise.">d</a></sup>and instruct those of them who are wise, and now renounce the life that is corruptible,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and let go of the mortal thoughts, cast away from you the burdens of man, put off now your weak nature,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> lay aside the thoughts that are most grievous to you, and hurry to escape from these times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For worse evils than those which you have seen happen will be done after this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For look how much the world will be weaker through age, so much that more evils will increase on those who dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For the truth will withdraw itself further off, and falsehood will be near. For now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="So the Oriental versions.">e</a></sup>the eagle which you saw in vision hurries to come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then I answered and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Latin omits I will speak.">f</a></sup>“Let me speak in your presence, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Behold, I will go, as you have commanded me, and reprove the people who now live, but who will warn those who will be born afterward? For the world is set in darkness, and those who dwell in it are without light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For your law has been burned, therefore no one knows the things that are done by you, or the works that will be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if I have found favor before you, send the Holy Spirit to me, and I will write all that has been done in the world since the beginning, even the things that were written in your law, that men may be able to find the path, and that those who would live in the latter days may live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He answered me and said, “Go your way, gather the people together, and tell them not to seek you for forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But prepare for yourself many tablets, and take with you Sarea, Dabria, Selemia, Ethanus, and Asiel, these five, which are ready to write swiftly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and come here, and I will light a lamp of understanding in your heart which will not be put out until the things have ended about which you will write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When you are done, some things you shall publish openly, and some things you shall deliver in secret to the wise. Tomorrow at this hour you will begin to write.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then went I out, as he commanded me, and gathered all the people together, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> “Hear these words, O Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Our fathers at the beginning were foreigners in Egypt, and they were delivered from there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and received the law of life, which they didn’t keep, which you also have transgressed after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then the land of Zion was given to you for a possession; but you yourselves and your ancestors have done unrighteousness, and have not kept the ways which the Most High commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Because he is a righteous judge, in due time, he took from you what he had given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Now you are here, and your kindred are among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Therefore if you will rule over your own understanding and instruct your hearts, you will be kept alive, and after death you will obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For after death the judgment will come, when we will live again. Then the names of the righteous will become manifest, and the works of the ungodly will be declared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Let no one therefore come to me now, nor seek me for forty days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So I took the five men, as he commanded me, and we went out into the field, and remained there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> It came to pass on the next day that, behold, a voice called me, saying, “Esdras, open your mouth, and drink what I give you to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then opened I my mouth, and behold, a full cup was handed to me. It was full of something like water, but its color was like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> I took it, and drank. When I had drunk it, my heart uttered understanding, and wisdom grew in my chest, for my spirit retained its memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> My mouth was opened, and shut no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The Most High gave understanding to the five men, and they wrote by course the things that were told them, in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="So the Oriental versions.">g</a></sup>characters which they didn’t know, and they sat forty days. Now they wrote in the day-time, and at night they ate bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> As for me, I spoke in the day, and by night I didn’t hold my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> So in forty days, ninety-four books were written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> It came to pass, when the forty days were fulfilled, that the Most High spoke to me, saying, “The first books that you have written, publish openly, and let the worthy and unworthy read them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> but keep the last seventy, that you may deliver them to those who are wise among your people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> for in them is the spring of understanding, the fountain of wisdom, and the stream of knowledge.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> I did so.</span>
</div>
