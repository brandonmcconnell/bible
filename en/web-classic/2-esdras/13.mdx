<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> It came to pass after seven days, I dreamed a dream by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Behold, a wind arose from the sea that moved all its waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I saw, and behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The words in brackets are added from the Syriac.">a</a></sup>&#91;this wind caused to come up from the midst of the sea something like the appearance of a man. I saw, and behold,&#93; that man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin has grew strong">b</a></sup>flew with the clouds of heaven. When he turned his face to look, everything that he saw trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Whenever the voice went out of his mouth, all who heard his voice melted, like the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="So the Syriac and other Oriental versions.">c</a></sup>wax melts when it feels the fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> After this I saw, and behold, an innumerable multitude of people was gathered together from the four winds of heaven to make war against the man who came out of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> I saw, and behold, he carved himself a great mountain, and flew up onto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> I tried to see the region or place from which the mountain was carved, and I couldn’t.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> After this I saw, and behold, all those who were gathered together to fight against him were very afraid, and yet they dared to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Behold, as he saw the assault of the multitude that came, he didn’t lift up his hand, or hold a spear or any weapon of war;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> but I saw only how he sent out of his mouth something like a flood of fire, and out of his lips a flaming breath, and out of his tongue he shot out a storm of sparks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="So the Syriac and Arabic.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> These were all mixed together: the flood of fire, the flaming breath, and the great storm, and fell upon the assault of the multitude which was prepared to fight, and burned up every one of them, so that all of a sudden an innumerable multitude was seen to be nothing but the dust of ashes and the smell of smoke. When I saw this, I was amazed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Afterward, I saw the same man come down from the mountain, and call to himself another multitude which was peaceful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Many people came to him. Some of them were glad. Some were sorry. Some of them were bound, and some others brought some of those as offerings. Then through great fear I woke up and prayed to the Most High, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> “You have shown your servant these wonders from the beginning, and have counted me worthy that you should receive my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Now show me also the interpretation of this dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For as I conceive in my understanding, woe to those who will be left in those days! Much more woe to those who are not left!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For those who were not left will be in heaviness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> understanding the things that are laid up in the latter days, but not attaining to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But woe to them also who are left, because they will see great perils and much distress, like these dreams declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Yet is it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Latin easier.">e</a></sup>better for one to be in peril and to come into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="So the Syriac.">f</a></sup>these things, than to pass away as a cloud out of the world, and not to see the things that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="So the Syriac.">g</a></sup>will happen in the last days.”<br />He answered me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “I will tell you the interpretation of the vision, and I will also open to you the things about which you mentioned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> You have spoken of those who are left behind. This is the interpretation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He that will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="So the Syriac.">h</a></sup>endure the peril in that time will protect those who fall into danger, even those who have works and faith toward the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Know therefore that those who are left behind are more blessed than those who are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> These are the interpretations of the vision: Whereas you saw a man coming up from the midst of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> this is he whom the Most High has been keeping for many ages, who by his own self will deliver his creation. He will direct those who are left behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Whereas you saw that out of his mouth came wind, fire, and storm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and whereas he held neither spear, nor any weapon of war, but destroyed the assault of that multitude which came to fight against him, this is the interpretation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Behold, the days come when the Most High will begin to deliver those who are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Astonishment of mind will come upon those who dwell on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> One will plan to make war against another, city against city, place against place, people against people, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> It will be, when these things come to pass, and the signs happen which I showed you before, then my Son will be revealed, whom you saw as a man ascending.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> It will be, when all the nations hear his voice, every man will leave his own land and the battle they have against one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> An innumerable multitude will be gathered together, as you saw, desiring to come and to fight against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> But he will stand on the top of Mount Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Zion will come, and will be shown to all men, being prepared and built, like you saw the mountain carved without hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> My Son will rebuke the nations which have come for their wickedness, with plagues that are like a storm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and will rebuke them to their face with their evil thoughts, and the torments with which they will be tormented, which are like a flame. He will destroy them without labor by the law, which is like fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Whereas you saw that he gathered to himself another multitude that was peaceful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> these are the ten tribes which were led away out of their own land in the time of Osea the king, whom Salmananser the king of the Assyrians led away captive, and he carried them beyond the River, and they were taken into another land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> But they made this plan among themselves, that they would leave the multitude of the heathen, and go out into a more distant region, where mankind had never lived,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> that there they might keep their statutes which they had not kept in their own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> They entered by the narrow passages of the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For the Most High then did signs for them, and stopped the springs of the River until they had passed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> For through that country there was a long way to go, namely, of a year and a half. The same region is called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, another land. See Deuteronomy 29:28.">i</a></sup>Arzareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Then they lived there until the latter time. Now when they begin to come again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> the Most High stops the springs of the River again, that they may go through. Therefore you saw the multitude gathered together with peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> But those who are left behind of your people are those who are found within my holy border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> It will be therefore when he will destroy the multitude of the nations that are gathered together, he will defend the people who remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Then he will show them very many wonders.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Then I said, “O sovereign Lord, explain this to me: Why have I seen the man coming up from the midst of the sea?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> He said to me, as no one can explore or know what is in the depths of the sea, even so no man on earth can see my Son, or those who are with him, except in the time of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="So the Oriental versions. The Latin omits his.">j</a></sup>his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> This is the interpretation of the dream which you saw, and for this only you are enlightened about this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> for you have forsaken your own ways, and applied your diligence to mine, and have searched out my law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> You have ordered your life in wisdom, and have called understanding your mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Therefore I have shown you this, for there is a reward laid up with the Most High. It will be, after another three days I will speak other things to you, and declare to you mighty and wondrous things.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then I went out and passed into the field, giving praise and thanks greatly to the Most High because of his wonders, which he did from time to time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> and because he governs the time, and such things as happen in their seasons. So I sat there three days.</span>
</div>
</div>
