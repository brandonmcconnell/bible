<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He said to me, “In the beginning, when the earth was made, before the portals of the world were fixed and before the gatherings of the winds blew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> before the voices of the thunder sounded and before the flashes of the lightning shone, before the foundations of paradise were laid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> before the fair flowers were seen, before the powers of the earthquake were established, before the innumerable army of angels were gathered together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> before the heights of the air were lifted up, before the measures of the firmament were named, before the footstool of Zion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="So the Syriac.">a</a></sup>was established,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> before the present years were reckoned, before the imaginations of those who now sin were estranged, and before they were sealed who have gathered faith for a treasure—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> then I considered these things, and they all were made through me alone, and not through another; just as by me also they will be ended, and not by another.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then I answered, “What will be the dividing of the times? Or when will be the end of the first and the beginning of the age that follows?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He said to me, “From Abraham to Isaac, because Jacob and Esau were born to him, for Jacob’s hand held Esau’s heel from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For Esau is the end of this age, and Jacob is the beginning of the one that follows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="So the Syriac, etc. The Latin is defective.">b</a></sup>The beginning of a man is his hand, and the end of a man is his heel. Seek nothing else between the heel and the hand, Esdras!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then I answered, “O sovereign Lord, if I have found favor in your sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> I beg you, show your servant the end of your signs which you showed me part on a previous night.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> So he answered, “Stand up upon your feet, and you will hear a mighty sounding voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If the place you stand on is greatly moved</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> when it speaks don’t be afraid, for the word is of the end, and the foundations of the earth will understand</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> that the speech is about them. They will tremble and be moved, for they know that their end must be changed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> It happened that when I had heard it, I stood up on my feet, and listened, and, behold, there was a voice that spoke, and its sound was like the sound of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> It said, “Behold, the days come when I draw near to visit those who dwell upon the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and when I investigate those who have caused harm unjustly with their unrighteousness, and when the affliction of Zion is complete,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and when the seal will be set on the age that is to pass away, then I will show these signs: the books will be opened before the firmament, and all will see together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The children a year old will speak with their voices. The women with child will deliver premature children at three or four months, and they will live and dance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Suddenly the sown places will appear unsown. The full storehouses will suddenly be found empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The trumpet will give a sound which when every man hears, they will suddenly be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> At that time friends will make war against one another like enemies. The earth will stand in fear with those who dwell in it. The springs of the fountains will stand still, so that for three hours they won’t flow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> “It will be that whoever remains after all these things that I have told you of, he will be saved and will see my salvation, and the end of my world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They will see the men who have been taken up, who have not tasted death from their birth. The heart of the inhabitants will be changed and turned into a different spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For evil will be blotted out and deceit will be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Faith will flourish. Corruption will be overcome, and the truth, which has been so long without fruit, will be declared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> When he talked with me, behold, little by little, the place I stood on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="After the Oriental versions. The Latin is corrupt.">c</a></sup>rocked back and forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> He said to me, “I came to show you these things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin is corrupt.">d</a></sup>tonight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If therefore you will pray yet again, and fast seven more days, I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Latin has tell you by day.">e</a></sup>again tell you greater things than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For your voice has surely been heard before the Most High. For the Mighty has seen your righteousness. He has also seen your purity, which you have maintained ever since your youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore he has sent me to show you all these things, and to say to you, ‘Believe, and don’t be afraid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Don’t be hasty to think vain things about the former times, that you may not hasten in the latter times.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> It came to pass after this, that I wept again, and fasted seven days in like manner, that I might fulfill the three weeks which he told me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> On the eighth night, my heart was troubled within me again, and I began to speak in the presence of the Most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> For my spirit was greatly aroused, and my soul was in distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> I said, “O Lord, truly you spoke at the beginning of the creation, on the first day, and said this: ‘Let heaven and earth be made,’ and your word perfected the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then the spirit was hovering, and darkness and silence were on every side. The sound of man’s voice was not yet there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Latin adds from you.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Then you commanded a ray of light to be brought out of your treasuries, that your works might then appear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> “On the second day, again you made the spirit of the firmament and commanded it to divide and to separate the waters, that the one part might go up, and the other remain beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> “On the third day, you commanded that the waters should be gathered together in the seventh part of the earth. You dried up six parts and kept them, to the intent that of these some being both planted and tilled might serve before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> For as soon as your word went out, the work was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Immediately, great and innumerable fruit grew, with many pleasant tastes, and flowers of inimitable color, and fragrances of most exquisite smell. This was done the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> “On the fourth day, you commanded that the sun should shine, the moon give its light, and the stars should be in their order;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and gave them a command to serve mankind, who was to be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> “On the fifth day, you said to the seventh part, where the water was gathered together, that it should produce living creatures, fowls and fishes; and so it came to pass</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> that the mute and lifeless water produced living things as it was told, that the nations might therefore praise your wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> “Then you preserved two living creatures. The one you called Behemoth, and the other you called Leviathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> You separated the one from the other; for the seventh part, namely, where the water was gathered together, might not hold them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> To Behemoth, you gave one part, which was dried up on the third day, that he should dwell in it, in which are a thousand hills;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> but to Leviathan you gave the seventh part, namely, the watery part. You have kept them to be devoured by whom you wish, when you wish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> “But on the sixth day, you commanded the earth to produce before you cattle, animals, and creeping things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Over these, you ordained Adam as ruler over all the works that you have made. Of him came all of us, the people whom you have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> “All this have I spoken before you, O Lord, because you have said that for our sakes you made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin has the firstborn world.">g</a></sup>this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> As for the other nations, which also come from Adam, you have said that they are nothing, and are like spittle. You have likened the abundance of them to a drop that falls from a bucket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Now, O Lord, behold these nations, which are reputed as nothing, being rulers over us and devouring us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> But we your people, whom you have called your firstborn, your only children, and your fervent lover, are given into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Now if the world is made for our sakes, why don’t we possess our world for an inheritance? How long will this endure?”</span>
</div>
