<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:8; Jeremiah 40:6; Psalms 41:9"> Now in the seventh month, Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, the son of Elishama, of the royal offspring and one of the chief officers of the king, and ten men with him, came to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam to Mizpah; and there they ate bread together in Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 25:25; Jeremiah 40:5; Psalms 109:5"> Then Ishmael the son of Nethaniah arose, and the ten men who were with him, and struck Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan with the sword and killed him, whom the king of Babylon had made governor over the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:25; Lamentations 1:2; Jeremiah 41:11–12"> Ishmael also killed all the Jews who were with Gedaliah at Mizpah, and the Chaldean men of war who were found there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Psalms 52:1–2"> The second day after he had killed Gedaliah, and no man knew it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 16:24; Deuteronomy 14:1; Joshua 18:1"> men came from Shechem, from Shiloh, and from Samaria, even eighty men, having their beards shaved and their clothes torn, and having cut themselves, with meal offerings and frankincense in their hand, to bring them to Yahweh’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; Jeremiah 50:4; 2 Samuel 1:2–16"> Ishmael the son of Nethaniah went out from Mizpah to meet them, weeping all along as he went, and as he met them, he said to them, “Come to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalms 55:23; Ezekiel 22:27"> It was so, when they came into the middle of the city, that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah killed them, and cast them into the middle of the pit, he, and the men who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:4; Proverbs 13:8; Philippians 3:7–9"> But ten men were found among those who said to Ishmael, “Don’t kill us; for we have stores hidden in the field, of wheat, and of barley, and of oil, and of honey.” So he stopped, and didn’t kill them among their brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:2; 1 Kings 15:17–22; 1 Samuel 13:6"> Now the pit in which Ishmael cast all the dead bodies of the men whom he had killed, by the side of Gedaliah (this was that which Asa the king had made for fear of Baasha king of Israel), Ishmael the son of Nethaniah filled it with those who were killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:14; Jeremiah 40:7; Nehemiah 2:10"> Then Ishmael carried away captive all of the people who were left in Mizpah, even the king’s daughters, and all the people who remained in Mizpah, whom Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard had committed to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam. Ishmael the son of Nethaniah carried them away captive, and departed to go over to the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:13–16; Jeremiah 40:7–8; Jeremiah 42:1"> But when Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces who were with him, heard of all the evil that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah had done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:13; Genesis 14:14–16; 1 Samuel 30:1–8"> then they took all the men, and went to fight with Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and found him by the great waters that are in Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now when all the people who were with Ishmael saw Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces who were with him, then they were glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So all the people who Ishmael had carried away captive from Mizpah turned about and came back, and went to Johanan the son of Kareah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 21:30; Proverbs 28:17; 1 Kings 20:20"> But Ishmael the son of Nethaniah escaped from Johanan with eight men, and went to the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:4–7; Jeremiah 42:8; Jeremiah 41:10"> Then Johanan the son of Kareah and all the captains of the forces who were with him took all the remnant of the people whom he had recovered from Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, from Mizpah, after he had killed Gedaliah the son of Ahikam—the men of war, with the women, the children, and the eunuchs, whom he had brought back from Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:37–38; Jeremiah 42:14; Jeremiah 43:7"> They departed and lived in Geruth Chimham, which is by Bethlehem, to go to enter into Egypt</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5; Luke 12:4–5; Jeremiah 42:16"> because of the Chaldeans; for they were afraid of them, because Ishmael the son of Nethaniah had killed Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon made governor over the land.</span>
