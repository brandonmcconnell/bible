</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Yahweh’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremiah 18:4"> “House of Israel, can’t I do with you as this potter?” says Yahweh. “Behold, as the clay in the potter’s hand, so are you in my hand, house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 12:14–17; Jeremiah 45:4"> At the instant I speak concerning a nation, and concerning a kingdom, to pluck up and to break down and to destroy it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:21; Jeremiah 26:13; Jonah 3:9–10"> if that nation, concerning which I have spoken, turns from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremiah 1:10"> At the instant I speak concerning a nation, and concerning a kingdom, to build and to plant it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:30; Ezekiel 18:24"> if they do that which is evil in my sight, that they don’t obey my voice, then I will repent of the good with which I said I would benefit them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> “Now therefore, speak to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying, ‘Yahweh says: “Behold, I frame evil against you, and devise a plan against you. Everyone return from his evil way now, and amend your ways and your doings.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> But they say, ‘It is in vain; for we will walk after our own plans, and we will each follow the stubbornness of his evil heart.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30; Hosea 6:10; Jeremiah 23:14"> Therefore Yahweh says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Ask now among the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Who has heard such things?’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The virgin of Israel has done a very horrible thing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will the snow of Lebanon fail from the rock of the field?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will the cold waters that flow down from afar be dried up?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Jeremiah 2:32; Hosea 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my people have forgotten me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They have burned incense to false gods.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have been made to stumble in their ways</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the ancient paths,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to walk in byways, in a way not built up,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 19:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">to make their land an astonishment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a perpetual hissing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who passes by it will be astonished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shake his head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:24; Jeremiah 2:27; Job 27:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will scatter them as with an east wind before the enemy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will show them the back, and not the face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of their calamity.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> Then they said, “Come! Let’s devise plans against Jeremiah; for the law won’t perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, and let’s strike him with the tongue, and let’s not give heed to any of his words.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give heed to me, Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and listen to the voice of those who contend with me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should evil be recompensed for good?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For they have dug a pit for my soul.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Remember how I stood before you to speak good for them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to turn away your wrath from them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore deliver up their children to the famine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and give them over to the power of the sword.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let their wives become childless and widows.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let their men be killed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their young men struck by the sword in battle.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let a cry be heard from their houses</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when you bring a troop suddenly on them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they have dug a pit to take me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hidden snares for my feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet, Yahweh, you know all their counsel against me to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Don’t forgive their iniquity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Don’t blot out their sin from your sight,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let them be overthrown before you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Deal with them in the time of your anger.</span></span>
