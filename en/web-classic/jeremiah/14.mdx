<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; Job 39:1–4; Isaiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, the doe in the field also calves and forsakes her young,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there is no grass.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Job 39:5–6; Joel 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wild donkeys stand on the bare heights.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They pant for air like jackals.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their eyes fail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there is no vegetation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 25:11; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Though our iniquities testify against us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">work for your name’s sake, Yahweh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for our rebellions are many.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We have sinned against you.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:19; Isaiah 59:1; Jeremiah 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why should you be like a scared man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as a mighty man who can’t save?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet you, Yahweh, are in the middle of us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we are called by your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Don’t leave us.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10; 2 Peter 2:1"> Then I said, “Ah, Lord Yahweh! Behold, the prophets tell them, ‘You will not see the sword, neither will you have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 12:24; Jeremiah 29:8–9"> Then Yahweh said to me, “The prophets prophesy lies in my name. I didn’t send them. I didn’t command them. I didn’t speak to them. They prophesy to you a lying vision, divination, and a thing of nothing, and the deceit of their own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–13; Ezekiel 14:10; Jeremiah 23:14–15"> Therefore Yahweh says concerning the prophets who prophesy in my name, but I didn’t send them, yet they say, ‘Sword and famine will not be in this land.’ Those prophets will be consumed by sword and famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Proverbs 1:31; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> The people to whom they prophesy will be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem because of the famine and the sword. They will have no one to bury them—them, their wives, their sons, or their daughters, for I will pour their wickedness on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:21; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 13:17"> “You shall say this word to them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘Let my eyes run down with tears night and day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let them not cease;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for the virgin daughter of my people is broken with a great breach,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with a very grievous wound.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Jeremiah 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I go out into the field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then behold, the slain with the sword!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If I enter into the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then behold, those who are sick with famine!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For both the prophet and the priest go about in the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have no knowledge.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Lamentations 5:22; Jeremiah 8:15; Jeremiah 6:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you utterly rejected Judah?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Has your soul loathed Zion?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why have you struck us, and there is no healing for us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We looked for peace, but no good came;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and for a time of healing, and behold, dismay!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 32:5; 1 John 1:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">We acknowledge, Yahweh, our wickedness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the iniquity of our fathers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for we have sinned against you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 3:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not abhor us, for your name’s sake.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do not disgrace the throne of your glory.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Remember, and don’t break your covenant with us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:23; Jeremiah 5:24; Psalms 135:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Are there any among the vanities of the nations that can cause rain?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or can the sky give showers?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Aren’t you he, Yahweh our God?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore we will wait for you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for you have made all these things.</span></span>
