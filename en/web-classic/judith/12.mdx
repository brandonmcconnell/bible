<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Judith said, “I can’t eat of it, lest there be an occasion of stumbling; but provision will be made for me from the things that have come with me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Holofernes said to her, “But if the things that are with you should run out, from where will we be able to give you more like it? For there is none of your race with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Judith said to him, “As your soul lives, my lord, your servant will not use up those things that are with me until the Lord works by my hand the things that he has determined.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Holofernes’ servants brought her into the tent, and she slept until midnight. Then she rose up toward the morning watch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and sent to Holofernes, saying, “Let my lord now command that they allow your servant to go out to pray.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Holofernes commanded his guards that they should not stop her. She stayed in the camp three days, and went out every night into the valley of Bethulia and washed herself at the fountain of water in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when she came up, she implored the Lord God of Israel to direct her way to the triumph of the children of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> She came in clean and remained in the tent until she ate her food toward evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> It came to pass on the fourth day, that Holofernes made a feast for his own servants only, and called none of the officers to the banquet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he said to Bagoas the eunuch, who had charge over all that he had, “Go now, and persuade this Hebrew woman who is with you that she come to us, and eat and drink with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For behold, it would be a disgrace if we shall let such a woman go, not having had her company; for if we don’t draw her to ourselves, she will laugh us to scorn.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Bagoas went from the presence of Holofernes, and came in to her, and said, “Let this fair lady not fear to come to my lord, and to be honored in his presence, and to drink wine and be merry with us, and to be made this day as one of the daughters of the children of Asshur who serve in Nebuchadnezzar’s palace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Judith said to him, “Who am I, that I should contradict my lord? For whatever would be pleasing in his eyes, I will do speedily, and this will be my joy to the day of my death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> She arose, and decked herself with her apparel and all her woman’s attire; and her servant went and laid fleeces on the ground for her next to Holofernes, which she had received from Bagoas for her daily use, that she might sit and eat upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Judith came in and sat down, and Holofernes’ heart was ravished with her. His passion was aroused, and he exceedingly desired her company. He was watching for a time to deceive her from the day that he had seen her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Holofernes said to her, “Drink now, and be merry with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Judith said, “I will drink now, my lord, because my life is magnified in me this day more than all the days since I was born.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then she took and ate and drank before him what her servant had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Holofernes took great delight in her, and drank exceedingly much wine, more than he had drunk at any time in one day since he was born.</span>
</div>
