<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:31–32; Jeremiah 9:3; Psalms 78:40"> Why does Yahweh bring us to this land, to fall by the sword? Our wives and our little ones will be captured or killed! Wouldn’t it be better for us to return into Egypt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:39; Nehemiah 9:16–17; Deuteronomy 17:16"> They said to one another, “Let’s choose a leader, and let’s return into Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:22; Numbers 16:45"> Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before all the assembly of the congregation of the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:38"> Joshua the son of Nun and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, who were of those who spied out the land, tore their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 1:25; Deuteronomy 8:7–9"> They spoke to all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, “The land, which we passed through to spy it out, is an exceedingly good land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:20; Isaiah 62:4; Numbers 13:27"> If Yahweh delights in us, then he will bring us into this land, and give it to us: a land which flows with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:18; Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Only don’t rebel against Yahweh, neither fear the people of the land; for they are bread for us. Their defense is removed from over them, and Yahweh is with us. Don’t fear them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 17:4; 1 Samuel 30:6; Leviticus 9:23"> But all the congregation threatened to stone them with stones.<br />Yahweh’s glory appeared in the Tent of Meeting to all the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:32; Psalms 106:24; Psalms 78:22"> Yahweh said to Moses, “How long will this people despise me? How long will they not believe in me, for all the signs which I have worked among them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:10; Numbers 25:9; 2 Samuel 24:12–15"> I will strike them with the pestilence, and disinherit them, and will make of you a nation greater and mightier than they.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:23; Ezekiel 20:9; Deuteronomy 32:27"> Moses said to Yahweh, “Then the Egyptians will hear it; for you brought up this people in your might from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:14; Exodus 33:11; Deuteronomy 5:4"> They will tell it to the inhabitants of this land. They have heard that you Yahweh are among this people; for you Yahweh are seen face to face, and your cloud stands over them, and you go before them, in a pillar of cloud by day, and in a pillar of fire by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:16"> Now if you killed this people as one man, then the nations which have heard the fame of you will speak, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9; Joshua 7:7"> ‘Because Yahweh was not able to bring this people into the land which he swore to them, therefore he has slain them in the wilderness.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Matthew 9:8; Micah 3:8"> Now please let the power of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word translated “Lord” is “Adonai.”">a</a></sup>be great, according as you have spoken, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Psalms 145:8; Exodus 20:5"> ‘Yahweh is slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness, forgiving iniquity and disobedience; and he will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children, on the third and on the fourth generation.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 34:9; Psalms 78:38; Psalms 106:45"> Please pardon the iniquity of this people according to the greatness of your loving kindness, and just as you have forgiven this people, from Egypt even until now.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; James 5:16; Psalms 106:23"> Yahweh said, “I have pardoned according to your word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 72:19; Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 6:3"> but in very deed—as I live, and as all the earth shall be filled with Yahweh’s glory—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:3; Genesis 31:7; Exodus 32:1"> because all those men who have seen my glory and my signs, which I worked in Egypt and in the wilderness, yet have tempted me these ten times, and have not listened to my voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 32:11; Numbers 26:64; Ezekiel 20:15"> surely they shall not see the land which I swore to their fathers, neither shall any of those who despised me see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 14:6–9; Joshua 14:6–14; Ephesians 6:6"> But my servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him, and has followed me fully, him I will bring into the land into which he went. His offspring shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 13:29; Psalms 81:11–13"> Since the Amalekite and the Canaanite dwell in the valley, tomorrow turn and go into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 16:28"> “How long shall I bear with this evil congregation that complain against me? I have heard the complaints of the children of Israel, which they complain against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Hebrews 3:17; Numbers 14:2"> Tell them, ‘As I live, says Yahweh, surely as you have spoken in my ears, so I will do to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:45; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:17"> Your dead bodies shall fall in this wilderness; and all who were counted of you, according to your whole number, from twenty years old and upward, who have complained against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 14:38; Deuteronomy 1:36–38"> surely you shall not come into the land concerning which I swore that I would make you dwell therein, except Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:39; Psalms 106:24; Numbers 26:64"> But I will bring in your little ones that you said should be captured or killed, and they shall know the land which you have rejected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:29; Hebrews 3:17"> But as for you, your dead bodies shall fall in this wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Numbers 32:13; Deuteronomy 2:14"> Your children shall be wanderers in the wilderness forty years, and shall bear your prostitution, until your dead bodies are consumed in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 13:25; Jeremiah 18:9–10"> After the number of the days in which you spied out the land, even forty days, for every day a year, you will bear your iniquities, even forty years, and you will know my alienation.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Hebrews 3:19"> I, Yahweh, have spoken. I will surely do this to all this evil congregation who are gathered together against me. In this wilderness they shall be consumed, and there they shall die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 13:31–33; Numbers 13:4–16"> The men whom Moses sent to spy out the land, who returned and made all the congregation to murmur against him by bringing up an evil report against the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Numbers 16:49; Hebrews 3:17"> even those men who brought up an evil report of the land, died by the plague before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 14:6–10; Numbers 26:65"> But Joshua the son of Nun and Caleb the son of Jephunneh remained alive of those men who went to spy out the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 33:4; Matthew 8:12; Hebrews 12:17"> Moses told these words to all the children of Israel, and the people mourned greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41; Matthew 7:21–23; Luke 13:25"> They rose up early in the morning and went up to the top of the mountain, saying, “Behold, we are here, and will go up to the place which Yahweh has promised; for we have sinned.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; Job 4:9; Jeremiah 2:37"> Moses said, “Why now do you disobey the commandment of Yahweh, since it shall not prosper?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:42; Joshua 7:8; Joshua 7:12"> Don’t go up, for Yahweh isn’t among you; that way you won’t be struck down before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 13:29; Numbers 14:25; 2 Chronicles 15:2"> For there the Amalekite and the Canaanite are before you, and you will fall by the sword because you turned back from following Yahweh; therefore Yahweh will not be with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:43; 1 Samuel 4:3–11; Numbers 31:6"> But they presumed to go up to the top of the mountain. Nevertheless, the ark of Yahweh’s covenant and Moses didn’t depart out of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:17; Deuteronomy 1:44"> Then the Amalekites came down, and the Canaanites who lived in that mountain, and struck them and beat them down even to Hormah.</span>
</div>
