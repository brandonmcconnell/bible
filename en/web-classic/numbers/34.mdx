<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> “Command the children of Israel, and tell them, ‘When you come into the land of Canaan (this is the land that shall fall to you for an inheritance, even the land of Canaan according to its borders),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> then your south quarter shall be from the wilderness of Zin along by the side of Edom, and your south border shall be from the end of the Salt Sea eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> Your border shall turn about southward of the ascent of Akrabbim, and pass along to Zin; and it shall pass southward of Kadesh Barnea; and it shall go from there to Hazar Addar, and pass along to Azmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> The border shall turn about from Azmon to the brook of Egypt, and it shall end at the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> “‘For the western border, you shall have the great sea and its border. This shall be your west border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> “‘This shall be your north border: from the great sea you shall mark out for yourselves Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> From Mount Hor you shall mark out to the entrance of Hamath; and the border shall pass by Zedad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> Then the border shall go to Ziphron, and it shall end at Hazar Enan. This shall be your north border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “‘You shall mark out your east border from Hazar Enan to Shepham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> The border shall go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain. The border shall go down, and shall reach to the side of the sea of Chinnereth eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> The border shall go down to the Jordan, and end at the Salt Sea. This shall be your land according to its borders around it.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying, “This is the land which you shall inherit by lot, which Yahweh has commanded to give to the nine tribes, and to the half-tribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> for the tribe of the children of Reuben according to their fathers’ houses, the tribe of the children of Gad according to their fathers’ houses, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> The two tribes and the half-tribe have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan at Jericho eastward, toward the sunrise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> “These are the names of the men who shall divide the land to you for inheritance: Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> You shall take one prince of every tribe, to divide the land for inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> These are the names of the men: Of the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Jephunneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> Of the tribe of the children of Simeon, Shemuel the son of Ammihud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> Of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad the son of Chislon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Of the tribe of the children of Dan a prince, Bukki the son of Jogli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Of the children of Joseph: of the tribe of the children of Manasseh a prince, Hanniel the son of Ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of the tribe of the children of Ephraim a prince, Kemuel the son of Shiphtan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the tribe of the children of Zebulun a prince, Elizaphan the son of Parnach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of the tribe of the children of Issachar a prince, Paltiel the son of Azzan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Of the tribe of the children of Asher a prince, Ahihud the son of Shelomi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Of the tribe of the children of Naphtali a prince, Pedahel the son of Ammihud.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These are they whom Yahweh commanded to divide the inheritance to the children of Israel in the land of Canaan.</span>
