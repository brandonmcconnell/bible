<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 19:30; 1 Kings 20:30"> Now the Philistines gathered together all their armies to Aphek; and the Israelites encamped by the spring which is in Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:1–2; Joshua 13:3; 1 Samuel 29:6–7"> The lords of the Philistines passed on by hundreds and by thousands; and David and his men passed on in the rear with Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:5; 1 Samuel 27:7; John 19:6"> Then the princes of the Philistines said, “What about these Hebrews?” Achish said to the princes of the Philistines, “Isn’t this David, the servant of Saul the king of Israel, who has been with me these days, or rather these years? I have found no fault in him since he fell away until today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:21; 1 Chronicles 12:19; Luke 16:8"> But the princes of the Philistines were angry with him; and the princes of the Philistines said to him, “Make the man return, that he may go back to his place where you have appointed him, and let him not go down with us to battle, lest in the battle he become an adversary to us. For with what should this fellow reconcile himself to his lord? Should it not be with the heads of these men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 18:6–7; Proverbs 27:14"> Isn’t this David, of whom people sang to one another in dances, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Saul has slain his thousands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and David his ten thousands’?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:25; 2 Kings 19:27; 1 Samuel 20:3"> Then Achish called David and said to him, “As Yahweh lives, you have been upright, and your going out and your coming in with me in the army is good in my sight; for I have not found evil in you since the day of your coming to me to this day. Nevertheless, the lords don’t favor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 22:34"> Therefore now return, and go in peace, that you not displease the lords of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Psalms 34:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:2"> David said to Achish, “But what have I done? What have you found in your servant so long as I have been before you to this day, that I may not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:27; 2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:20"> Achish answered David, “I know that you are good in my sight, as an angel of God. Notwithstanding, the princes of the Philistines have said, ‘He shall not go up with us to the battle.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:19; 1 Samuel 30:1–2; 1 Chronicles 12:22"> Therefore now rise up early in the morning with the servants of your lord who have come with you; and as soon as you are up early in the morning and have light, depart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 2 Samuel 4:4; Joshua 19:18"> So David rose up early, he and his men, to depart in the morning, to return into the land of the Philistines; and the Philistines went up to Jezreel.</span>
