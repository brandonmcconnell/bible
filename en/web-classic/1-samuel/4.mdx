<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 5:1"> The word of Samuel came to all Israel. Now Israel went out against the Philistines to battle, and encamped beside Ebenezer; and the Philistines encamped in Aphek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Samuel 17:8; Joshua 7:5–8"> The Philistines put themselves in array against Israel. When they joined battle, Israel was defeated by the Philistines, who killed about four thousand men of the army in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Numbers 10:35; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5"> When the people had come into the camp, the elders of Israel said, “Why has Yahweh defeated us today before the Philistines? Let’s get the ark of Yahweh’s covenant out of Shiloh and bring it to us, that it may come among us and save us out of the hand of our enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:2; Numbers 7:89; Psalms 80:1"> So the people sent to Shiloh, and they brought from there the ark of the covenant of Yahweh of Armies, who sits above the cherubim; and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of the covenant of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:5; Job 20:5; Amos 6:3"> When the ark of Yahweh’s covenant came into the camp, all Israel shouted with a great shout, so that the earth resounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 32:17–18"> When the Philistines heard the noise of the shout, they said, “What does the noise of this great shout in the camp of the Hebrews mean?” They understood that Yahweh’s ark had come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Exodus 14:25; Exodus 15:14–16"> The Philistines were afraid, for they said, “God has come into the camp.” They said, “Woe to us! For there has not been such a thing before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:14; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 78:43–51"> Woe to us! Who shall deliver us out of the hand of these mighty gods? These are the gods that struck the Egyptians with all kinds of plagues in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Judges 13:1; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Be strong and behave like men, O you Philistines, that you not be servants to the Hebrews, as they have been to you. Strengthen yourselves like men, and fight!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 14:12; Deuteronomy 28:25"> The Philistines fought, and Israel was defeated, and each man fled to his tent. There was a very great slaughter; for thirty thousand footmen of Israel fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:34; Psalms 78:64; 1 Samuel 2:32"> God’s ark was taken; and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Nehemiah 9:1; 2 Samuel 15:32"> A man of Benjamin ran out of the army and came to Shiloh the same day, with his clothes torn and with dirt on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:9; 1 Samuel 4:18; Joshua 7:9"> When he came, behold, Eli was sitting on his seat by the road watching, for his heart trembled for God’s ark. When the man came into the city and told about it, all the city cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Eli heard the noise of the crying, he said, “What does the noise of this tumult mean?” The man hurried, and came and told Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Psalms 90:10; 1 Kings 14:4"> Now Eli was ninety-eight years old. His eyes were set, so that he could not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:4; 1 Samuel 3:6; Joshua 7:19"> The man said to Eli, “I am he who came out of the army, and I fled today out of the army.” He said, “How did the matter go, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> He who brought the news answered, “Israel has fled before the Philistines, and there has been also a great slaughter among the people. Your two sons also, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead, and God’s ark has been captured.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:21–22; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Psalms 69:9"> When he made mention of God’s ark, Eli fell from off his seat backward by the side of the gate; and his neck broke, and he died, for he was an old man and heavy. He had judged Israel forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> His daughter-in-law, Phinehas’ wife, was with child, near to giving birth. When she heard the news that God’s ark was taken and that her father-in-law and her husband were dead, she bowed herself and gave birth; for her pains came on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:17–18; Psalms 77:2; John 16:21"> About the time of her death the women who stood by her said to her, “Don’t be afraid, for you have given birth to a son.” But she didn’t answer, neither did she regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Jeremiah 2:11; Psalms 78:61"> She named the child Ichabod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Ichabod” means “no glory”.">a</a></sup>saying, “The glory has departed from Israel!” because God’s ark was taken, and because of her father-in-law and her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:5–6; John 2:17"> She said, “The glory has departed from Israel; for God’s ark has been taken.”</span>
