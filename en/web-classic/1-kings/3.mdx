<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 11:1; 1 Kings 6:1–15"> Solomon made a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt. He took Pharaoh’s daughter and brought her into David’s city until he had finished building his own house, Yahweh’s house, and the wall around Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:43; 2 Chronicles 33:17; Deuteronomy 12:2–5"> However, the people sacrificed in the high places, because there was not yet a house built for Yahweh’s name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Psalms 31:23; Deuteronomy 30:16"> Solomon loved Yahweh, walking in the statutes of David his father, except that he sacrificed and burned incense in the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:39; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 2 Chronicles 7:5"> The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, for that was the great high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 9:2; James 1:5–6; Numbers 12:6"> In Gibeon, Yahweh appeared to Solomon in a dream by night; and God said, “Ask for what I should give you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 1 Kings 1:48; 2 Corinthians 9:11"> Solomon said, “You have shown to your servant David my father great loving kindness, because he walked before you in truth, in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart with you. You have kept for him this great loving kindness, that you have given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:1; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 1:6"> Now, Yahweh my God, you have made your servant king instead of David my father. I am just a little child. I don’t know how to go out or come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 13:16; 1 Chronicles 21:2"> Your servant is among your people which you have chosen, a great people, that can’t be numbered or counted for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 16:16; Hebrews 5:14"> Give your servant therefore an understanding heart to judge your people, that I may discern between good and evil; for who is able to judge this great people of yours?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8"> This request pleased the Lord, that Solomon had asked this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:26; James 4:2–3; Proverbs 16:31"> God said to him, “Because you have asked this thing, and have not asked for yourself long life, nor have you asked for riches for yourself, nor have you asked for the life of your enemies, but have asked for yourself understanding to discern justice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Ecclesiastes 1:16; Matthew 12:42"> behold, I have done according to your word. Behold, I have given you a wise and understanding heart, so that there has been no one like you before you, and after you none will arise like you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:16"> I have also given you that which you have not asked, both riches and honor, so that there will not be any among the kings like you for all your days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16; 1 Kings 15:5"> If you will walk in my ways, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as your father David walked, then I will lengthen your days.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 41:7; 1 Kings 8:65; Esther 1:3"> Solomon awoke; and behold, it was a dream. Then he came to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of Yahweh’s covenant, and offered up burnt offerings, offered peace offerings, and made a feast for all his servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:16; Judges 11:1; Numbers 27:2"> Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king, and stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Genesis 43:20"> The one woman said, “Oh, my lord, I and this woman dwell in one house. I delivered a child with her in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The third day after I delivered, this woman delivered also. We were together. There was no stranger with us in the house, just us two in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> This woman’s child died in the night, because she lay on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 13:25; Psalms 139:11"> She arose at midnight, and took my son from beside me while your servant slept, and laid it in her bosom, and laid her dead child in my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; 1 Samuel 1:23; Lamentations 4:3–4"> When I rose in the morning to nurse my child, behold, he was dead; but when I had looked at him in the morning, behold, it was not my son whom I bore.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 3:23–24"> The other woman said, “No! But the living one is my son, and the dead one is your son.”<br />The first one said, “No! But the dead one is your son, and the living one is my son.” They argued like this before the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the king said, “One says, ‘This is my son who lives, and your son is the dead one;’ and the other says, ‘No! But your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The king said, “Get me a sword.” So they brought a sword before the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 25:8"> The king said, “Divide the living child in two, and give half to the one, and half to the other.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Jeremiah 31:20; 1 John 3:17"> Then the woman whose the living child was spoke to the king, for her heart yearned over her son, and she said, “Oh, my lord, give her the living child, and in no way kill him!”<br />But the other said, “He shall be neither mine nor yours. Divide him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then the king answered, “Give the first woman the living child, and definitely do not kill him. She is his mother.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; 1 Kings 3:9–12; Colossians 2:3"> All Israel heard of the judgment which the king had judged; and they feared the king, for they saw that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice.</span>
</div>
</div>
