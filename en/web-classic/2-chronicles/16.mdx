<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; 2 Chronicles 15:5; 1 Kings 12:27"> In the thirty-sixth year of Asa’s reign, Baasha king of Israel went up against Judah, and built Ramah, that he might not allow anyone to go out or come in to Asa king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 12:18; 2 Chronicles 28:21; 2 Kings 18:15"> Then Asa brought out silver and gold out of the treasures of Yahweh’s house and of the king’s house, and sent to Ben Hadad king of Syria, who lived at Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> “Let there be a treaty between me and you, as there was between my father and your father. Behold, I have sent you silver and gold. Go, break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, that he may depart from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Kings 15:20"> Ben Hadad listened to King Asa, and sent the captains of his armies against the cities of Israel; and they struck Ijon, Dan, Abel Maim, and all the storage cities of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When Baasha heard of it, he stopped building Ramah, and let his work cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 15:38; Isaiah 10:29"> Then Asa the king took all Judah, and they carried away the stones and timber of Ramah, with which Baasha had built; and he built Geba and Mizpah with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:1; Psalms 146:3–6"> At that time Hanani the seer came to Asa king of Judah, and said to him, “Because you have relied on the king of Syria, and have not relied on Yahweh your God, therefore the army of the king of Syria has escaped out of your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; 2 Chronicles 13:16; 2 Chronicles 16:7"> Weren’t the Ethiopians and the Lubim a huge army, with chariots and exceedingly many horsemen? Yet, because you relied on Yahweh, he delivered them into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> For Yahweh’s eyes run back and forth throughout the whole earth, to show himself strong in the behalf of them whose heart is perfect toward him. You have done foolishly in this; for from now on you will have wars.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:26; Acts 16:23–24; 2 Samuel 12:13"> Then Asa was angry with the seer, and put him in the prison; for he was in a rage with him because of this thing. Asa oppressed some of the people at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 35:27"> Behold, the acts of Asa, first and last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 28:22; 1 Chronicles 10:14"> In the thirty-ninth year of his reign, Asa was diseased in his feet. His disease was exceedingly great; yet in his disease he didn’t seek Yahweh, but just the physicians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24"> Asa slept with his fathers, and died in the forty-first year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:2; 2 Chronicles 21:19; Jeremiah 34:5"> They buried him in his own tomb, which he had dug out for himself in David’s city, and laid him in the bed which was filled with sweet odors and various kinds of spices prepared by the perfumers’ art; and they made a very great fire for him.</span>
