<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now this one endured this praiseworthy death. The third was brought along, and exhorted by many to taste and save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But he cried out and said, “Don’t you know that the father of those who are dead is my father also, and that the same mother bore me, and that I was brought up in the same way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I don’t renounce the noble relationship of my kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Now then, whatever instrument of vengeance you have, apply it to my body, for you aren’t able to touch my soul, even if you want to.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But they, highly incensed at his boldness of speech, dislocated his hands and feet with racking engines, and wrenching them from their sockets, dismembered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They dragged around his fingers, his arms, his legs, and his ankles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Not being able by any means to strangle him, they tore off his skin, together with the extreme tips of his fingers, and then dragged him to the wheel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> around which his vertebral joints were loosened, and he saw his own flesh torn to shreds, and streams of blood flowing from his entrails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When about to die, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “We, O accursed tyrant, suffer this for the sake of Divine education and virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But you, for your impiety and blood shedding, will endure unceasing torments.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Thus having died worthily of his kindred, they dragged forward the fourth, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “Don’t share the madness of your kindred, but respect the king and save yourself.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But he said to them, “You don’t have a fire so scorching as to make me play the coward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> By the blessed death of my kindred, and the eternal punishment of the tyrant, and the glorious life of the pious, I will not repudiate the noble brotherhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Invent, O tyrant, tortures, that you may learn, even through them, that I am the brother of those tormented before.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> When he had said this, the blood-thirsty, murderous, and unholy Antiochus ordered his tongue to be cut out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But he said, “Even if you take away the organ of speech, God still hears the silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Behold, my tongue is extended, cut it off; for in spite of that you won’t silence our reasoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> We gladly lose our limbs on behalf of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But God will speedily find you, since you cut off the tongue, the instrument of divine melody.”</span>
</div>
