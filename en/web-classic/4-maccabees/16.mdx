<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> If, then, even a woman, and that an aged one, and the mother of seven children, endured to see her children’s torments even to death, it must be admitted that religious reasoning is master even of the emotions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I have proved, then, that not only men have obtained the mastery of their emotions, but also that a woman despised the greatest torments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The lions around Daniel were not so fierce, nor the furnace of Misael burning with most vehement fires as that natural love of children burned within her, when she saw her seven sons tortured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But with the reasoning of religion the mother quenched emotions so great and powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For we must consider also this: that, had the woman been faint hearted, as being their mother, she would have lamented over them, and perhaps might have spoken thus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “Ah! I am wretched and many times miserable, who having born seven sons, have become the mother of none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> O seven useless childbirths, and seven profitless periods of labor, and fruitless givings of suck, and miserable nursings at the breast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Vainly, for your sakes, O sons, have I endured many pangs, and the more difficult anxieties of rearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Alas, of my children, some of you unmarried, and some who have married to no profit, I will not see your children, nor have the joy of being a grandmother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ah, that I who had many and fair children, should be a lone widow full of sorrows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Nor, should I die, will I have a son to bury me.” But with such a lament as this, the holy and God-fearing mother wept for none of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Nor did she divert any of them from death, nor grieve for them as for the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But as one possessed with an adamant mind, and as one bringing forth again her full number of sons to immortality, she rather urged them to death on behalf of religion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> O woman, soldier of God for religion, you, aged and a female, have conquered through endurance even a tyrant; and even though weak, have been found more powerful in deeds and words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For when you were seized along with your children, you stood looking at Eleazar in torture, and said to your sons in the Hebrew tongue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> “O sons, the contest is noble, to which you being called as a witness for the nation, strive zealously for the laws of your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For it would be disgraceful if this old man endured pains for the sake of righteousness, and that you who are younger would be afraid of the tortures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Remember that through God, you obtained existence and have enjoyed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore, you ought to bear every affliction because of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For him also our father Abraham was zealous to sacrifice Isaac our progenitor, and didn’t shudder at the sight of his own paternal hand descending down with the sword upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The righteous Daniel was cast to the lions; and Ananias, Azarias, and Misael were hurled into a fiery furnace, yet they endured through God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> You, then, having the same faith toward God, don’t be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For it is unreasonable that they who know religion wouldn’t stand up against troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> With these arguments, the mother of seven, exhorting each of her sons, encouraged and persuaded them not to transgress God’s commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They saw this, too, that those who die for God, live to God, like Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all the patriarchs.</span>
