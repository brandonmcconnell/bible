<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> When he, too, had undergone blessed martyrdom, and died in the cauldron into which he had been thrown, the seventh, the youngest of all, came forward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> whom the tyrant pitying, though he had been dreadfully reproached by his kindred,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> seeing him already encompassed with chains, had him brought nearer, and endeavored to counsel him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> “You see the end of the madness of your kindred, for they have died in torture through disobedience. You, if disobedient, having been miserably tormented, will yourself perish prematurely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But if you obey, you will be my friend, and have a charge over the affairs of the kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Having thus exhorted him, he sent for the boy’s mother, that, by showing compassion to her for the loss of so many sons, he might incline her, through the hope of safety, to make the survivor obedient.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He, after his mother had urged him on in the Hebrew tongue, (as we will soon relate) said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> “Release me that I may speak to the king and all his friends.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They, rejoicing exceedingly at the promise of the youth, quickly let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He, running up to the pans, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “Impious tyrant, and most blasphemous man, were you not ashamed, having received prosperity and a kingdom from God, to kill His servants, and to rack the doers of godliness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore the divine vengeance is reserving you for eternal fire and torments, which will cling to you for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Weren’t you ashamed, man as you are, yet most savage, to cut out the tongues of men of like feeling and origin, and having thus abused to torture them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But they, bravely dying, fulfilled their religion toward God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But you will groan as you deserve for having slain without cause the champions of virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Therefore,” he continued, “I myself, being about to die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> will not forsake my kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> I call upon the God of my fathers to be merciful to my race.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But you, both living and dead, he will punish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Thus having prayed, he hurled himself into the pans; and so expired.</span>
</div>
