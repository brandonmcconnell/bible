<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Jonathan saw that the time was favorable for him, so he chose men and sent them to Rome to confirm and renew the friendship that they had with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He also sent similar letters to the Spartans, and to other places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They went to Rome, entered into the senate house, and said, “Jonathan the high priest and the nation of the Jews have sent us to renew for them the friendship and the alliance, as in former time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> They gave them letters to the men in every place, that they should provide safe conduct for them on their way to the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> This is the copy of the letters which Jonathan wrote to the Spartans:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “Jonathan the high priest, and the senate of the nation, and the priests, and the rest of the people of the Jews, to their kindred the Spartans, greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Even before this time letters were sent to Onias the high priest from Arius,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="So the old Latin versions and Josephus: compare also ver. 20. All the other authorities read Darius in this place.">a</a></sup>who was reigning among you, to signify that you are our kindred, as the copy written below shows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Onias welcomed honorably the man who was sent and received the letters, wherein declaration was made of alliance and friendship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore we also, even though we need none of these things, having for our encouragement the holy books which are in our hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> have undertaken to send that we might renew our brotherhood and friendship with you, to the end that we should not become estranged from you altogether; for a long time has passed since you sent your letter to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> We therefore at all times without ceasing, both in our feasts, and on the other convenient days, remember you in the sacrifices which we offer, and in our prayers, as it is right and proper to be mindful of kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Moreover, we are glad for your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But as for ourselves, many afflictions and many wars have encompassed us, and the kings who are around us have fought against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> We were unwilling to be troublesome to you, and to the rest of our allies and friends, in these wars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> for we have the help which is from heaven to help us, and we have been delivered from our enemies, and our enemies have been humbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> We chose therefore Numenius the son of Antiochus and Antipater the son of Jason, and have sent them to the Romans, to renew the friendship that we had with them, and the former alliance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> We commanded them therefore to go also to you, and to salute you, and to deliver you our letters concerning the renewing of friendship and our brotherhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And now you will do well if you give us a reply.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And this is the copy of the letters which they sent to Onias:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> “Arius king of the Spartans to Onias the chief priest, greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> It has been found in writing, concerning the Spartans and the Jews, that they are kindred, and that they are of the descendants of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Now, since this has come to our knowledge, you will do well to write to us of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. peace">b</a></sup>prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> We moreover write on our part to you, that your livestock and goods are ours, and ours are yours. We do command therefore that they make report to you accordingly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now Jonathan heard that Demetrius’ princes had returned to fight against him with a greater army than before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> so he marched away from Jerusalem, and met them in the country of Hamath; for he gave them no opportunity to set foot in his country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He sent spies into his camp, and they came again, and reported to him that they were preparing to attack them at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But as soon as the sun was down, Jonathan commanded his men to watch, and to be armed, that all the night long they might be ready for battle. He stationed sentinels around the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The adversaries heard that Jonathan and his men were ready for battle, and they feared, and trembled in their hearts, and they kindled fires in their camp then withdrew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But Jonathan and his men didn’t know it until the morning; for they saw the fires burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Jonathan pursued after them, but didn’t overtake them; for they had gone over the river Eleutherus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then Jonathan turned toward the Arabians, who are called Zabadaeans, and struck them, and took their spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> He came out from there, and came to Damascus, and took his journey through all the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Simon went out, and took his journey as far as Ascalon, and the strongholds that were near it. Then he turned toward Joppa and took possession of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> for he had heard that they were planning to hand over the stronghold to Demetrius’ men. He set a garrison there to guard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then Jonathan returned and called the elders of the people together. He planned with them to build strongholds in Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and to make the walls of Jerusalem higher, and to raise a great mound between the citadel and the city, to separate it from the city, that so it might be isolated, that its garrison might neither buy nor sell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They were gathered together to build the city. Part of the wall of the brook that is on the east side fell down, and he repaired the section called Chaphenatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Simon also built Adida in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. Sephela.">c</a></sup>plain country, made it strong, and set up gates and bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Tryphon sought to reign over Asia and to crown himself, and to stretch out his hand against Antiochus the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> He was afraid that Jonathan wouldn’t allow him, and that he would fight against him; and he sought a way to seize him, that he might destroy him. So he marched out and came to Bethshan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Jonathan came out to meet him with forty thousand men chosen for battle, and came to Bethshan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Tryphon saw that he came with a great army, and he was afraid to stretch out his hand against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> He received him honorably, and commended him to all his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 1 Maccabees 2:18.">d</a></sup>friends, and gave him gifts, and commanded his forces to be obedient to him, as to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> He said to Jonathan, “Why have you put all this people to so much trouble, seeing there is no war between us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Now send them away to their homes, but choose for yourself a few men who shall be with you, and come with me to Ptolemais, and I will give it to you, and the rest of the strongholds and the rest of the forces, and all the king’s officers. Then I will turn around and depart; for this is why I came.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> He put his trust in him, and did even as he said, and sent away his forces, and they departed into the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> But he reserved to himself three thousand men, of whom he left two thousand in Galilee, but one thousand went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Now as soon as Jonathan entered into Ptolemais, the people of Ptolemais shut the gates and seized him. They killed all those who came in with him with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Tryphon sent troops and cavalry into Galilee, and into the Great Plain, to destroy all Jonathan’s men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> They perceived that he had been seized and had perished, along with those who were with him. They encouraged one another and went on their way close together, prepared to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Those who followed them saw that they were ready to fight for their lives, and turned back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> They all came in peace into the land of Judah, and they mourned for Jonathan and those who were with him, and they were very afraid. All Israel mourned with a great mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And all the Gentiles who were around them sought to destroy them utterly; for they said, “They have no ruler nor anyone to help them. Now therefore let’s fight against them, and take away their memory from among men.”</span>
