<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In those days Mattathias the son of John, the son of Simeon, a priest of the sons of Joarib, from Jerusalem rose up; and he lived at Modin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he had five sons:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. Joannes.">a</a></sup>John, who was surnamed Gaddis;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Simon, who was called Thassi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Judas, who was called Maccabaeus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Eleazar, who was called Avaran; and Jonathan, who was called Apphus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He saw the blasphemies that were committed in Judah and in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and he said, “Woe is me! Why was I born to see the destruction of my people and the destruction of the holy city, and to dwell there when it was given into the hand of the enemy, the sanctuary into the hand of foreigners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Her temple has become like a man who was glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Her vessels of glory are carried away into captivity. Her infants are slain in her streets. Her young men are slain with the enemy’s sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> What nation has not inherited her palaces and taken possession of her spoils?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Her adornment has all been taken away. Instead of a free woman, she has become a slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Behold, our holy things, our beauty, and our glory are laid waste. The Gentiles have profaned them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Why should we live any longer?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Mattathias and his sons tore their clothes, put on sackcloth, and mourned exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the king’s officers who were enforcing the apostasy came into the city Modin to sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Many of Israel came to them, and Mattathias and his sons were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The king’s officers answered and spoke to Mattathias, saying, “You are a ruler and an honorable and great man in this city, and strengthened with sons and kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Now therefore come first and do the commandment of the king, as all the nations have done, including the men of Judah and those who remain in Jerusalem. You and your house will be numbered among the king’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 Maccabees 3:38; 10:10, etc.; Compare 1 Maccabees 10:65; 11:27; 2 Maccabees 8:9.">b</a></sup>friends, and you and your sons will be honored with silver and gold and many gifts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Mattathias answered and said with a loud voice, “If all the nations that are in the house of the king’s dominion listen to him, to fall away each one from the worship of his fathers, and have made choice to follow his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> yet I and my sons and my kindred will walk in the covenant of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Far be it from us that we should forsake the law and the ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> We will not listen to the king’s words, to turn aside from our worship, to the right hand, or to the left.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When he had finished speaking these words, a Jew came in the sight of all to sacrifice on the altar which was at Modin, according to the king’s commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Mattathias saw it, so his zeal was kindled, and his guts trembled, and he vented his wrath according to judgment, and ran and killed him upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He killed the king’s officer, who compelled men to sacrifice, at the same time, and pulled down the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He was zealous for the law, even as Phinehas did to Zimri the son of Salu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Mattathias cried out in the city with a loud voice, saying, “Whoever is zealous for the law and maintains the covenant, let him follow me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He and his sons fled into the mountains, and left all that they had in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then many who sought justice and judgment went down into the wilderness to live there—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> they, their children, their wives, and their livestock—because evils were multiplied upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> It was told the king’s officers and the forces that were in Jerusalem, the city of David, that certain men who had broken the king’s commandment had gone down into the secret places in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and many pursued after them, and having overtaken them, they encamped against them and set the battle in array against them on the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> They said to them, “Enough of this! Come out and do according to the word of the king, and you will all live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> They said, “We won’t come out. We won’t do the word of the king, to profane the Sabbath day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then the enemy hurried to attack them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> They didn’t answer them. They didn’t cast a stone at them, or block their secret places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> saying, “Let’s all die in our innocence. Heaven and earth testify for us, that you put us to death unjustly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> So they attacked them on the Sabbath, and they died—they, their wives, their children, and their livestock—in number a thousand souls of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> When Mattathias and his friends found out about it, and they mourned over them exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> One said to another, “If we all do as our kindred have done, and don’t fight against the Gentiles for our lives and our ordinances, they will quickly destroy us from off the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> So they decided that day, saying, “Whoever comes against us to battle on the Sabbath day, let’s fight against him, and we will in no way all die, as our kindred died in the secret places.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then a company of the Hasidaeans,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, Chasidim.">c</a></sup>mighty men of Israel, everyone who offered himself willingly for the law, were gathered together to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> All those who fled from the evils were added to them, and supported them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> They mustered an army, and struck sinners in their anger, and lawless men in their wrath. The rest fled to the Gentiles for safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And Mattathias and his friends went around and pulled down the altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> They forcibly circumcised the boys who were uncircumcised, as many as they found in the coasts of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> They pursued the arrogant, and the work prospered in their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> They rescued the law out of the hand of the Gentiles and out of the hand of the kings. They never allowed the sinner to triumph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> The days of Mattathias drew near that he should die, and he said to his sons, “Now pride and scorn have gained strength. It is a season of overthrow and indignant wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Now, my children, be zealous for the law, and give your lives for the covenant of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Call to remembrance the deeds of our fathers which they did in their generations; and receive great glory and an everlasting name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Wasn’t Abraham found faithful in temptation, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> Joseph in the time of his distress kept the commandment, and became lord of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Phinehas our father, because he was exceedingly zealous, obtained the covenant of an everlasting priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> Joshua became a judge in Israel for fulfilling the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Caleb obtained a heritage in the land for testifying in the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> David inherited the throne of a kingdom forever and ever for being merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Elijah was taken up into heaven because he was exceedingly zealous for the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Hananiah, Azariah, and Mishael believed, and were saved out of the flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> Daniel was delivered from the mouth of lions for his innocence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> “Thus consider from generation to generation that no one who put their trust in him will lack for strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> Don’t be afraid of the words of a sinful man; for his glory will be dung and worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> Today he will be lifted up, and tomorrow he will in no way be found, because he has returned to dust, and his thought has perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> You, my children, be strong, and show yourselves men on behalf of the law; for in it you will obtain glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> Behold, Simon your brother, whom I know to be a man of counsel. Always listen to him. He shall be a father to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> Judas Maccabaeus has been strong and mighty from his youth. He shall be your captain and shall fight<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some ancient authorities read you shall fight.">d</a></sup>the battle of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> Rally around all the doers of the law, and avenge the wrong done to your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> Repay the Gentiles, and obey the commandments of the law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> He blessed them, and was gathered to his ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> He died in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="circa B.C. 167.">e</a></sup>the one hundred forty sixth year, and his sons buried him in the tombs of his ancestors at Modin. All Israel made great lamentation for him.</span>
</div>
