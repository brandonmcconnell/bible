<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Therefore leaving the teaching of the first principles of Christ, let’s press on to perfection—not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works, of faith toward God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> of the teaching of baptisms, of laying on of hands, of resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> This will we do, if God permits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> For concerning those who were once enlightened and tasted of the heavenly gift, and were made partakers of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> and tasted the good word of God and the powers of the age to come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and then fell away, it is impossible to renew them again to repentance; seeing they crucify the Son of God for themselves again, and put him to open shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For the land which has drunk the rain that comes often on it and produces a crop suitable for them for whose sake it is also tilled, receives blessing from God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> but if it bears thorns and thistles, it is rejected and near being cursed, whose end is to be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> But, beloved, we are persuaded of better things for you, and things that accompany salvation, even though we speak like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> For God is not unrighteous, so as to forget your work and the labor of love which you showed toward his name, in that you served the saints, and still do serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> We desire that each one of you may show the same diligence to the fullness of hope even to the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> that you won’t be sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and perseverance inherited the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> For when God made a promise to Abraham, since he could swear by no one greater, he swore by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, “Surely blessing I will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> Thus, having patiently endured, he obtained the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> For men indeed swear by a greater one, and in every dispute of theirs the oath is final for confirmation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> In this way God, being determined to show more abundantly to the heirs of the promise the immutability of his counsel, interposed with an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> that by two immutable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we may have a strong encouragement, who have fled for refuge to take hold of the hope set before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> This hope we have as an anchor of the soul, a hope both sure and steadfast and entering into that which is within the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> where as a forerunner Jesus entered for us, having become a high priest forever after the order of Melchizedek.</span>
