<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> Therefore we ought to pay greater attention to the things that were heard, lest perhaps we drift away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the word spoken through angels proved steadfast, and every transgression and disobedience received a just penalty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> how will we escape if we neglect so great a salvation—which at the first having been spoken through the Lord, was confirmed to us by those who heard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> God also testifying with them, both by signs and wonders, by various works of power, and by gifts of the Holy Spirit, according to his own will?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For he didn’t subject the world to come, of which we speak, to angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But one has somewhere testified, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is man, that you think of him?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or the son of man, that you care for him?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made him a little lower than the angels.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You crowned him with glory and honor.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “and set him over the works of your hands”">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-2">You have put all things in subjection under his feet.”</span><br />For in that he subjected all things to him, he left nothing that is not subject to him. But now we don’t yet see all things subjected to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> But we see him who has been made a little lower than the angels, Jesus, because of the suffering of death crowned with glory and honor, that by the grace of God he should taste of death for everyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it became him, for whom are all things and through whom are all things, in bringing many children to glory, to make the author of their salvation perfect through sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both he who sanctifies and those who are sanctified are all from one, for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; John 18:20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will declare your name to my brothers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Among the congregation I will sing your praise.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> Again, “I will put my trust in him.” Again, “Behold, here I am with the children whom God has given me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Since then the children have shared in flesh and blood, he also himself in the same way partook of the same, that through death he might bring to nothing him who had the power of death, that is, the devil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> and might deliver all of them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For most certainly, he doesn’t give help to angels, but he gives help to the offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, seed">c</a></sup>of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> Therefore he was obligated in all things to be made like his brothers, that he might become a merciful and faithful high priest in things pertaining to God, to make atonement for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> For in that he himself has suffered being tempted, he is able to help those who are tempted.</span>
</div>
