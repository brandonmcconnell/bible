<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> <span className="woj">“The Kingdom of Heaven is like a certain king, who made a wedding feast for his son,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> <span className="woj">and sent out his servants to call those who were invited to the wedding feast, but they would not come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> <span className="woj">Again he sent out other servants, saying, ‘Tell those who are invited, “Behold, I have prepared my dinner. My cattle and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready. Come to the wedding feast!”’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> <span className="woj">But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his own farm, another to his merchandise;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> <span className="woj">and the rest grabbed his servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> <span className="woj">When the king heard that, he was angry, and sent his armies, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> <span className="woj">“Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding is ready, but those who were invited weren’t worthy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> <span className="woj">Go therefore to the intersections of the highways, and as many as you may find, invite to the wedding feast.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> <span className="woj">Those servants went out into the highways and gathered together as many as they found, both bad and good. The wedding was filled with guests.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> <span className="woj">“But when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man who didn’t have on wedding clothing,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> <span className="woj">and he said to him, ‘Friend, how did you come in here not wearing wedding clothing?’ He was speechless.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> <span className="woj">Then the king said to the servants, ‘Bind him hand and foot, take him away, and throw him into the outer darkness. That is where the weeping and grinding of teeth will be.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> <span className="woj">For many are called, but few chosen.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then the Pharisees went and took counsel how they might entrap him in his talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> They sent their disciples to him, along with the Herodians, saying, “Teacher, we know that you are honest, and teach the way of God in truth, no matter whom you teach; for you aren’t partial to anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Tell us therefore, what do you think? Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said,<span className="woj">“Why do you test me, you hypocrites?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> <span className="woj">Show me the tax money.”</span>They brought to him a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> He asked them,<span className="woj">“Whose is this image and inscription?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They said to him, “Caesar’s.” Then he said to them,<span className="woj">“Give therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> When they heard it, they marveled, and left him and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> On that day Sadducees (those who say that there is no resurrection) came to him. They asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, “Teacher, Moses said, ‘If a man dies, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife and raise up offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, seed">a</a></sup>for his brother.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were with us seven brothers. The first married and died, and having no offspring left his wife to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> In the same way, the second also, and the third, to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> After them all, the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the resurrection therefore, whose wife will she be of the seven? For they all had her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> But Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“You are mistaken, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> <span className="woj">For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like God’s angels in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> <span className="woj">But concerning the resurrection of the dead, haven’t you read that which was spoken to you by God, saying,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> <span className="woj">‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’?</span><span className="woj">God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> When the multitudes heard it, they were astonished at his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> But the Pharisees, when they heard that he had silenced the Sadducees, gathered themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, testing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> “Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the law?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> <span className="woj">This is the first and great commandment.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> <span className="woj">A second likewise is this, ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> <span className="woj">The whole law and the prophets depend on these two commandments.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them a question,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> saying,<span className="woj">“What do you think of the Christ? Whose son is he?”</span>They said to him, “Of David.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">sit on my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> <span className="woj">“If then David calls him Lord, how is he his son?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> No one was able to answer him a word, neither did any man dare ask him any more questions from that day forward.</span>
