<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Messiah (Hebrew) and Christ (Greek) both mean “Anointed One”">a</a></sup>the son of David, the son of Abraham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> Abraham became the father of Isaac. Isaac became the father of Jacob. Jacob became the father of Judah and his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> Judah became the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar. Perez became the father of Hezron. Hezron became the father of Ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> Ram became the father of Amminadab. Amminadab became the father of Nahshon. Nahshon became the father of Salmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> Salmon became the father of Boaz by Rahab. Boaz became the father of Obed by Ruth. Obed became the father of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> Jesse became the father of King David. David the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU omits “the king”.">b</a></sup>became the father of Solomon by her who had been Uriah’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> Solomon became the father of Rehoboam. Rehoboam became the father of Abijah. Abijah became the father of Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> Asa became the father of Jehoshaphat. Jehoshaphat became the father of Joram. Joram became the father of Uzziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> Uzziah became the father of Jotham. Jotham became the father of Ahaz. Ahaz became the father of Hezekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> Hezekiah became the father of Manasseh. Manasseh became the father of Amon. Amon became the father of Josiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> Josiah became the father of Jechoniah and his brothers at the time of the exile to Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> After the exile to Babylon, Jechoniah became the father of Shealtiel. Shealtiel became the father of Zerubbabel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Zerubbabel became the father of Abiud. Abiud became the father of Eliakim. Eliakim became the father of Azor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Azor became the father of Zadok. Zadok became the father of Achim. Achim became the father of Eliud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Eliud became the father of Eleazar. Eleazar became the father of Matthan. Matthan became the father of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> Jacob became the father of Joseph, the husband of Mary, from whom was born Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Jesus” means “Salvation”.">c</a></sup>who is called Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> So all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations; from David to the exile to Babylon fourteen generations; and from the carrying away to Babylon to the Christ, fourteen generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> Now the birth of Jesus Christ was like this: After his mother, Mary, was engaged to Joseph, before they came together, she was found pregnant by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> Joseph, her husband, being a righteous man, and not willing to make her a public example, intended to put her away secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> But when he thought about these things, behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">d</a></sup>an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, “Joseph, son of David, don’t be afraid to take to yourself Mary as your wife, for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> She shall give birth to a son. You shall name him Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“Jesus” means “Salvation”.">e</a></sup>for it is he who shall save his people from their sins.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> Now all this has happened that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the virgin shall be with child,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shall give birth to a son.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They shall call his name Immanuel,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which is, being interpreted, “God with us.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> Joseph arose from his sleep, and did as the angel of the Lord commanded him, and took his wife to himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> and didn’t know her sexually until she had given birth to her firstborn son. He named him Jesus.</span>
</div>
