<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> It is better to go to the house of mourning than to go to the house of feasting; for that is the end of all men, and the living should take this to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> Sorrow is better than laughter; for by the sadness of the face the heart is made good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; 1 Samuel 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning; but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise than for a man to hear the song of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 58:9; Psalms 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of the fool. This also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> Surely extortion makes the wise man foolish; and a bribe destroys the understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 126:5–6"> Better is the end of a thing than its beginning.<br />The patient in spirit is better than the proud in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Don’t be hasty in your spirit to be angry, for anger rests in the bosom of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; Psalms 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> Don’t say, “Why were the former days better than these?” For you do not ask wisely about this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Wisdom is as good as an inheritance. Yes, it is more excellent for those who see the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> For wisdom is a defense, even as money is a defense; but the excellency of knowledge is that wisdom preserves the life of him who has it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; Psalms 107:43"> Consider the work of God, for who can make that straight which he has made crooked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> In the day of prosperity be joyful, and in the day of adversity consider; yes, God has made the one side by side with the other, to the end that man should not find out anything after him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> All this I have seen in my days of vanity: there is a righteous man who perishes in his righteousness, and there is a wicked man who lives long in his evildoing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Don’t be overly righteous, neither make yourself overly wise. Why should you destroy yourself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> Don’t be too wicked, neither be foolish. Why should you die before your time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> It is good that you should take hold of this. Yes, also don’t withdraw your hand from that; for he who fears God will come out of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Wisdom is a strength to the wise man more than ten rulers who are in a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> Surely there is not a righteous man on earth who does good and doesn’t sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Also don’t take heed to all words that are spoken, lest you hear your servant curse you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> for often your own heart knows that you yourself have likewise cursed others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> All this I have proved in wisdom. I said, “I will be wise;” but it was far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> That which is, is far off and exceedingly deep. Who can find it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> I turned around, and my heart sought to know and to search out, and to seek wisdom and the scheme of things, and to know that wickedness is stupidity, and that foolishness is madness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> I find more bitter than death the woman whose heart is snares and traps, whose hands are chains. Whoever pleases God shall escape from her; but the sinner will be ensnared by her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> “Behold, I have found this,” says the Preacher, “to one another, to find an explanation</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; 1 Kings 11:1–3; Psalms 12:1"> which my soul still seeks, but I have not found. I have found one man among a thousand, but I have not found a woman among all those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> Behold, I have only found this: that God made mankind upright; but they search for many inventions.”</span>
