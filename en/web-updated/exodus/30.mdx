<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:3; Exodus 37:25–28; Exodus 40:5"> “You shall make an altar to burn incense on. You shall make it of acacia wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 27:2"> Its length shall be a cubit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>and its width a cubit. It shall be square, and its height shall be two cubits. Its horns shall be of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Exodus 25:24"> You shall overlay it with pure gold, its top, its sides around it, and its horns; and you shall make a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:27; Exodus 26:29; Exodus 27:7"> You shall make two golden rings for it under its molding; on its two ribs, on its two sides you shall make them; and they shall be for places for poles with which to bear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:13; Exodus 25:27"> You shall make the poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:21–22; Exodus 30:36; Leviticus 16:13"> You shall put it before the veil that is by the ark of the covenant, before the mercy seat that is over the covenant, where I will meet with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Exodus 27:20–21"> Aaron shall burn incense of sweet spices on it every morning. When he tends the lamps, he shall burn it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Hebrews 7:25"> When Aaron lights the lamps at evening, he shall burn it, a perpetual incense before the LORD throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1"> You shall offer no strange incense on it, nor burnt offering, nor meal offering; and you shall pour no drink offering on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:18; Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:29–30"> Aaron shall make atonement on its horns once in the year; with the blood of the sin offering of atonement once in the year he shall make atonement for it throughout your generations. It is most holy to the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Exodus 38:25–26"> “When you take a census of the children of Israel, according to those who are counted among them, then each man shall give a ransom for his soul to the LORD when you count them, that there be no plague among them when you count them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 3:47; Ezekiel 45:12"> They shall give this, everyone who passes over to those who are counted, half a shekel according to the shekel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">b</a></sup>of the sanctuary (the shekel is twenty gerahs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="a gerah is about 0.5 grams or about 7.7 grains">c</a></sup>); half a shekel for an offering to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:20; Numbers 1:18; Numbers 1:3"> Everyone who passes over to those who are counted, from twenty years old and upward, shall give the offering to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:50; Leviticus 17:11; Exodus 30:12"> The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less, than the half shekel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">d</a></sup>when they give the offering of the LORD, to make atonement for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 38:25–31"> You shall take the atonement money from the children of Israel, and shall appoint it for the service of the Tent of Meeting; that it may be a memorial for the children of Israel before the LORD, to make atonement for your souls.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 40:7; Exodus 38:8; 1 Kings 7:23"> “You shall also make a basin of bronze, and its base of bronze, in which to wash. You shall put it between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and you shall put water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Exodus 40:31–32; Psalms 26:6"> Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; Hebrews 12:28–29; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> When they go into the Tent of Meeting, they shall wash with water, that they don’t die; or when they come near to the altar to minister, to burn an offering made by fire to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Exodus 27:21"> So they shall wash their hands and their feet, that they not die. This shall be a statute forever to them, even to him and to his descendants throughout their generations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moreover the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:19; Song of Solomon 4:14; Ezekiel 27:22"> “Also take fine spices: of liquid myrrh, five hundred shekels;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces, so 500 shekels is about 5 kilograms or about 11 pounds.">e</a></sup>and of fragrant cinnamon half as much, even two hundred and fifty; and of fragrant cane, two hundred and fifty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Exodus 29:40; Numbers 3:47"> and of cassia five hundred, according to the shekel of the sanctuary; and a hin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A hin is about 6.5 liters or 1.7 gallons.">f</a></sup>of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; 1 Chronicles 9:30; Exodus 40:9"> You shall make it into a holy anointing oil, a perfume compounded after the art of the perfumer: it shall be a holy anointing oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; 1 John 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> You shall use it to anoint the Tent of Meeting, the ark of the covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> the table and all its articles, the lamp stand and its accessories, the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the basin with its base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Exodus 29:37; Leviticus 6:18"> You shall sanctify them, that they may be most holy. Whatever touches them shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Exodus 28:3; Leviticus 8:30"> You shall anoint Aaron and his sons, and sanctify them, that they may minister to me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Leviticus 21:10; Psalms 89:20"> You shall speak to the children of Israel, saying, ‘This shall be a holy anointing oil to me throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 30:25; Exodus 30:37–38; Leviticus 21:10"> It shall not be poured on man’s flesh, and do not make any like it, according to its composition. It is holy. It shall be holy to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 30:38; Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15"> Whoever compounds any like it, or whoever puts any of it on a stranger, he shall be cut off from his people.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 25:6; Exodus 30:23"> The LORD said to Moses, “Take to yourself sweet spices, gum resin, onycha, and galbanum: sweet spices with pure frankincense. There shall be an equal weight of each.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 2:13; Exodus 30:25; John 12:3"> You shall make incense of it, a perfume after the art of the perfumer, seasoned with salt, pure and holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Exodus 30:6; Exodus 16:34"> You shall beat some of it very small, and put some of it before the covenant in the Tent of Meeting, where I will meet with you. It shall be to you most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:32–33"> You shall not make this incense, according to its composition, for yourselves: it shall be to you holy for the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 30:33"> Whoever shall make any like that, to smell of it, he shall be cut off from his people.”</span>
</div>
