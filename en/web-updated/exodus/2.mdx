<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 23:12–14; Exodus 6:16–20"> A man of the house of Levi went and took a daughter of Levi as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Acts 7:20; Psalms 112:5"> The woman conceived and bore a son. When she saw that he was a fine child, she hid him three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Acts 7:19; Genesis 6:14"> When she could no longer hide him, she took a papyrus basket for him, and coated it with tar and with pitch. She put the child in it, and laid it in the reeds by the river’s bank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 15:20; Micah 6:4"> His sister stood far off, to see what would be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 7:21; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 9:9"> Pharaoh’s daughter came down to bathe at the river. Her maidens walked along by the riverside. She saw the basket among the reeds, and sent her servant to get it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; 1 Peter 3:8; Acts 7:21"> She opened it, and saw the child, and behold, the baby cried. She had compassion on him, and said, “This is one of the Hebrews’ children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:4; Numbers 26:59; Numbers 12:1"> Then his sister said to Pharaoh’s daughter, “Should I go and call a nurse for you from the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Psalms 27:10; Exodus 6:20"> Pharaoh’s daughter said to her, “Go.” The young woman went and called the child’s mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Pharaoh’s daughter said to her, “Take this child away, and nurse him for me, and I will give you your wages.” The woman took the child, and nursed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Genesis 16:11; 1 Samuel 1:20"> The child grew, and she brought him to Pharaoh’s daughter, and he became her son. She named him Moses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Moses” sounds like the Hebrew for “draw out”.">a</a></sup>and said, “Because I drew him out of the water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 7:22–24; Exodus 1:11"> In those days, when Moses had grown up, he went out to his brothers and saw their burdens. He saw an Egyptian striking a Hebrew, one of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 7:24–26"> He looked this way and that way, and when he saw that there was no one, he killed the Egyptian, and hid him in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:26–28; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8"> He went out the second day, and behold, two men of the Hebrews were fighting with each other. He said to him who did the wrong, “Why do you strike your fellow?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:14; Matthew 21:23; Genesis 19:9"> He said, “Who made you a prince and a judge over us? Do you plan to kill me, as you killed the Egyptian?” Moses was afraid, and said, “Surely this thing is known.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 7:29; Hebrews 11:27; Genesis 24:11"> Now when Pharaoh heard this thing, he sought to kill Moses. But Moses fled from the face of Pharaoh, and lived in the land of Midian, and he sat down by a well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 3:1; 1 Samuel 9:11"> Now the priest of Midian had seven daughters. They came and drew water, and filled the troughs to water their father’s flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 29:10; Genesis 21:25; Genesis 26:15–22"> The shepherds came and drove them away; but Moses stood up and helped them, and watered their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:29; Exodus 4:18"> When they came to Reuel, their father, he said, “How is it that you have returned so early today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:11; Genesis 29:10"> They said, “An Egyptian delivered us out of the hand of the shepherds, and moreover he drew water for us, and watered the flock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 43:25; Hebrews 13:2"> He said to his daughters, “Where is he? Why is it that you have left the man? Call him, that he may eat bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 31:38–40; Hebrews 13:5; Hebrews 11:25"> Moses was content to dwell with the man. He gave Moses Zipporah, his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13–14; Acts 7:29; Exodus 22:21"> She bore a son, and he named him Gershom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Gershom” sounds like the Hebrew for “an alien there”.">b</a></sup>for he said, “I have lived as a foreigner in a foreign land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:4; Exodus 3:7–9"> In the course of those many days, the king of Egypt died, and the children of Israel sighed because of the bondage, and they cried, and their cry came up to God because of the bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Psalms 105:42; Exodus 6:5"> God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Psalms 55:22; Exodus 3:7–8"> God saw the children of Israel, and God understood.</span>
