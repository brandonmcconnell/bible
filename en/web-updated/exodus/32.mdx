<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:40; Deuteronomy 9:9; Exodus 24:18"> When the people saw that Moses delayed coming down from the mountain, the people gathered themselves together to Aaron, and said to him, “Come, make us gods, which shall go before us; for as for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we don’t know what has become of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:24–27; Exodus 12:35–36; Ezekiel 16:17"> Aaron said to them, “Take off the golden rings, which are in the ears of your wives, of your sons, and of your daughters, and bring them to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Isaiah 40:19–20; Jeremiah 10:9"> All the people took off the golden rings which were in their ears, and brought them to Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16; 1 Kings 12:28"> He received what they handed him, fashioned it with an engraving tool, and made it a molded calf. Then they said, “These are your gods, Israel, which brought you up out of the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 10:20; Leviticus 23:2; Leviticus 23:37"> When Aaron saw this, he built an altar before it; and Aaron made a proclamation, and said, “Tomorrow shall be a feast to the LORD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:7; Numbers 25:2; Acts 7:41–42"> They rose up early on the next day, and offered burnt offerings, and brought peace offerings; and the people sat down to eat and to drink, and rose up to play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Hosea 9:9; Judges 2:19"> The LORD spoke to Moses, “Go, get down; for your people, whom you brought up out of the land of Egypt, have corrupted themselves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Exodus 20:23; Exodus 20:3–4"> They have turned away quickly out of the way which I commanded them. They have made themselves a molded calf, and have worshiped it, and have sacrificed to it, and said, ‘These are your gods, Israel, which brought you up out of the land of Egypt.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:13; Isaiah 48:4"> The LORD said to Moses, “I have seen these people, and behold, they are a stiff-necked people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:14; Numbers 14:12; Deuteronomy 9:19"> Now therefore leave me alone, that my wrath may burn hot against them, and that I may consume them; and I will make of you a great nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–20; Deuteronomy 9:26–29; Psalms 106:23"> Moses begged the LORD his God, and said, “LORD, why does your wrath burn hot against your people, that you have brought out of the land of Egypt with great power and with a mighty hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 14:13–16; Deuteronomy 9:28; Joshua 7:9"> Why should the Egyptians talk, saying, ‘He brought them out for evil, to kill them in the mountains, and to consume them from the surface of the earth’? Turn from your fierce wrath, and turn away from this evil against your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 22:16"> Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, your servants, to whom you swore by your own self, and said to them, ‘I will multiply your offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, seed">a</a></sup>as the stars of the sky, and all this land that I have spoken of I will give to your offspring, and they shall inherit it forever.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonah 3:10; Psalms 106:45; Jeremiah 26:13"> So the LORD turned away from the evil which he said he would do to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:15; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 5:22"> Moses turned, and went down from the mountain, with the two tablets of the covenant in his hand; tablets that were written on both their sides. They were written on one side and on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:1"> The tablets were the work of God, and the writing was the writing of God, engraved on the tablets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Exodus 17:9; Joshua 6:20"> When Joshua heard the noise of the people as they shouted, he said to Moses, “There is the noise of war in the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–18; Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23"> He said, “It isn’t the voice of those who shout for victory. It is not the voice of those who cry for being overcome; but the noise of those who sing that I hear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:16–17; Jeremiah 31:32; Lamentations 5:15"> As soon as he came near to the camp, he saw the calf and the dancing. Then Moses’ anger grew hot, and he threw the tablets out of his hands, and broke them beneath the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:21; 2 Kings 23:6; 2 Kings 23:15"> He took the calf which they had made, and burned it with fire, ground it to powder, and scattered it on the water, and made the children of Israel drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 20:9; Joshua 7:19–26; 1 Kings 21:22"> Moses said to Aaron, “What did these people do to you, that you have brought a great sin on them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:24; Exodus 15:24; Exodus 16:20"> Aaron said, “Don’t let the anger of my lord grow hot. You know the people, that they are set on evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 32:1–4; Exodus 32:8"> For they said to me, ‘Make us gods, which shall go before us. As for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we don’t know what has become of him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 32:4; Luke 10:29; Romans 3:10"> I said to them, ‘Whoever has any gold, let them take it off.’ So they gave it to me; and I threw it into the fire, and out came this calf.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:15"> When Moses saw that the people were out of control, (for Aaron had let them lose control, causing derision among their enemies),</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Matthew 12:30; 2 Samuel 20:11"> then Moses stood in the gate of the camp, and said, “Whoever is on the LORD’s side, come to me!” All the sons of Levi gathered themselves together to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 25:5; Luke 14:26; Deuteronomy 33:8–9"> He said to them, “The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘Every man put his sword on his thigh, and go back and forth from gate to gate throughout the camp, and every man kill his brother, and every man his companion, and every man his neighbor.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:9; Malachi 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 10:8"> The sons of Levi did according to the word of Moses. About three thousand men fell of the people that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Matthew 10:37; Zechariah 13:3"> Moses said, “Consecrate yourselves today to the LORD, for every man was against his son and against his brother, that he may give you a blessing today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:20; Numbers 25:13; 2 Kings 17:21"> On the next day, Moses said to the people, “You have sinned a great sin. Now I will go up to the LORD. Perhaps I shall make atonement for your sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 20:23; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:8"> Moses returned to the LORD, and said, “Oh, this people have sinned a great sin, and have made themselves gods of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 69:28; Romans 9:3; Revelation 21:27"> Yet now, if you will, forgive their sin—and if not, please blot me out of your book which you have written.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Ezekiel 18:4; Revelation 20:12"> The LORD said to Moses, “Whoever has sinned against me, I will blot him out of my book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Deuteronomy 32:35; Matthew 23:35"> Now go, lead the people to the place of which I have spoken to you. Behold, my angel shall go before you. Nevertheless, in the day when I punish, I will punish them for their sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:41; Exodus 32:25; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> The LORD struck the people, because of what they did with the calf, which Aaron made.</span>
