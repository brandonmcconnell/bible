<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 10:9; Acts 4:29; Matthew 10:28"> Afterward Moses and Aaron came, and said to Pharaoh, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘Let my people go, that they may hold a feast to me in the wilderness.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 3:19; Job 21:15; 2 Kings 18:35"> Pharaoh said, “Who is the LORD, that I should listen to his voice to let Israel go? I don’t know the LORD, and moreover I will not let Israel go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Zechariah 14:16–19; Ezekiel 6:11"> They said, “The God of the Hebrews has met with us. Please let us go three days’ journey into the wilderness, and sacrifice to the LORD, our God, lest he fall on us with pestilence, or with the sword.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Acts 24:5; Amos 7:10"> The king of Egypt said to them, “Why do you, Moses and Aaron, take the people from their work? Get back to your burdens!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:7–11; Proverbs 14:28"> Pharaoh said, “Behold, the people of the land are now many, and you make them rest from their burdens.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 5:10; Exodus 5:19; Deuteronomy 16:18"> The same day Pharaoh commanded the taskmasters of the people and their officers, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:19; Genesis 24:25"> “You shall no longer give the people straw to make brick, as before. Let them go and gather straw for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:41"> You shall require from them the number of the bricks which they made before. You shall not diminish anything of it, for they are idle. Therefore they cry, saying, ‘Let’s go and sacrifice to our God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:20; Jeremiah 43:2; Job 16:3"> Let heavier work be laid on the men, that they may labor in it. Don’t let them pay any attention to lying words.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 3:7; Proverbs 29:12"> The taskmasters of the people went out with their officers, and they spoke to the people, saying, “This is what Pharaoh says: ‘I will not give you straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 5:13–14"> Go yourselves, get straw where you can find it, for nothing of your work shall be diminished.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:7; Isaiah 5:24; Isaiah 47:14"> So the people were scattered abroad throughout all the land of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The taskmasters were urgent saying, “Fulfill your work quota daily, as when there was straw!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:24"> The officers of the children of Israel, whom Pharaoh’s taskmasters had set over them, were beaten, and were asked, “Why haven’t you fulfilled your quota both yesterday and today, in making brick as before?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the officers of the children of Israel came and cried to Pharaoh, saying, “Why do you deal this way with your servants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> No straw is given to your servants, and they tell us, ‘Make brick!’ and behold, your servants are beaten; but the fault is in your own people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:8; John 6:27; Exodus 5:8"> But Pharaoh said, “You are idle! You are idle! Therefore you say, ‘Let’s go and sacrifice to the LORD.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:18; Daniel 2:9–13"> Go therefore now, and work; for no straw shall be given to you; yet you shall deliver the same number of bricks!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Deuteronomy 32:36"> The officers of the children of Israel saw that they were in trouble when it was said, “You shall not diminish anything from your daily quota of bricks!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They met Moses and Aaron, who stood along the way, as they came out from Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Chronicles 19:6"> They said to them, “May the LORD look at you and judge, because you have made us a stench to be abhorred in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his servants, to put a sword in their hand to kill us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:7; 1 Kings 19:10; Numbers 11:14–15"> Moses returned to the LORD, and said, “Lord, why have you brought trouble on this people? Why is it that you have sent me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:21; Hebrews 10:36–37; Isaiah 28:16"> For since I came to Pharaoh to speak in your name, he has brought trouble on this people. You have not rescued your people at all!”</span>
</div>
</div>
