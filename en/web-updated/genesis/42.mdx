<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Mark 9:43–45; Genesis 37:23–28"> They said to one another, “We are certainly guilty concerning our brother, in that we saw the distress of his soul, when he begged us, and we wouldn’t listen. </div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They came to Jacob their father, to the land of Canaan, and told him all that had happened to them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 42:7"> “The man, the lord of the land, spoke roughly with us, and took us for spies of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 42:11"> We said to him, ‘We are honest men. We are no spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We are twelve brothers, sons of our father; one is no more, and the youngest is today with our father in the land of Canaan.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 42:19–20; Genesis 42:15"> The man, the lord of the land, said to us, ‘By this I will know that you are honest men: leave one of your brothers with me, and take grain for the famine of your houses, and go your way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 34:21; 1 Kings 10:15"> Bring your youngest brother to me. Then I will know that you are not spies, but that you are honest men. So I will deliver your brother to you, and you shall trade in the land.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
