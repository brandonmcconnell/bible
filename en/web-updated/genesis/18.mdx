</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:14; Luke 15:27; Luke 15:30"> Abraham ran to the herd, and fetched a tender and good calf, and gave it to the servant. He hurried to dress it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> He took butter, milk, and the calf which he had dressed, and set it before them. He stood by them under the tree, and they ate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> They asked him, “Where is Sarah, your wife?”<br />He said, “There, in the tent.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> He said, “I will certainly return to you at about this time next year; and behold, Sarah your wife will have a son.”<br />Sarah heard in the tent door, which was behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke 1:36"> Now Abraham and Sarah were old, well advanced in age. Sarah had passed the age of childbearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:18–20"> Sarah laughed within herself, saying, “After I have grown old will I have pleasure, my lord being old also?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 2:25"> The LORD said to Abraham, “Why did Sarah laugh, saying, ‘Will I really bear a child when I am old?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27"> Is anything too hard for the LORD? At the set time I will return to you, when the season comes around, and Sarah will have a son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> Then Sarah denied it, saying, “I didn’t laugh,” for she was afraid.<br />He said, “No, but you did laugh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> The men rose up from there, and looked toward Sodom. Abraham went with them to see them on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Psalms 25:14; John 15:15"> The LORD said, “Will I hide from Abraham what I do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> since Abraham will surely become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> For I have known him, to the end that he may command his children and his household after him, that they may keep the way of the LORD, to do righteousness and justice; to the end that the LORD may bring on Abraham that which he has spoken of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 3:9"> The LORD said, “Because the cry of Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and because their sin is very grievous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Zephaniah 1:12"> I will go down now, and see whether their deeds are as bad as the reports which have come to me. If not, I will know.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Ezekiel 22:30"> The men turned from there, and went toward Sodom, but Abraham stood yet before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; 2 Samuel 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> Abraham came near, and said, “Will you consume the righteous with the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Isaiah 1:9; Matthew 7:13–14"> What if there are fifty righteous within the city? Will you consume and not spare the place for the fifty righteous who are in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Isaiah 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> May it be far from you to do things like that, to kill the righteous with the wicked, so that the righteous should be like the wicked. May that be far from you. Shouldn’t the Judge of all the earth do right?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Matthew 24:22; Isaiah 65:8"> The LORD said, “If I find in Sodom fifty righteous within the city, then I will spare the whole place for their sake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Isaiah 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> Abraham answered, “See now, I have taken it on myself to speak to the Lord, although I am dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; 1 Kings 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> What if there will lack five of the fifty righteous? Will you destroy all the city for lack of five?”<br />He said, “I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> He spoke to him yet again, and said, “What if there are forty found there?”<br />He said, “I will not do it for the forty’s sake.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> He said, “Oh don’t let the Lord be angry, and I will speak. What if there are thirty found there?”<br />He said, “I will not do it if I find thirty there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke 18:1"> He said, “See now, I have taken it on myself to speak to the Lord. What if there are twenty found there?”<br />He said, “I will not destroy it for the twenty’s sake.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew 7:7"> He said, “Oh don’t let the Lord be angry, and I will speak just once more. What if ten are found there?”<br />He said, “I will not destroy it for the ten’s sake.”</span>
</div>
