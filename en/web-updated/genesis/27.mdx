<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> She put the skins of the young goats on his hands, and on the smooth of his neck.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He came to his father, and said, “My father?” He said, “Here I am. Who are you, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zechariah 13:3–4"> Jacob said to his father, “I am Esau your firstborn. I have done what you asked me to do. Please arise, sit and eat of my venison, that your soul may bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> Isaac said to his son, “How is it that you have found it so quickly, my son?” He said, “Because the LORD your God gave me success.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Jacob went near to Isaac his father. He felt him, and said, “The voice is Jacob’s voice, but the hands are the hands of Esau.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> He didn’t recognize him, because his hands were hairy, like his brother Esau’s hands. So he blessed him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let peoples serve you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and nations bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be lord over your brothers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let your mother’s sons bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cursed be everyone who curses you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Blessed be everyone who blesses you.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> He also made savory food, and brought it to his father. He said to his father, “Let my father arise, and eat of his son’s venison, that your soul may bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> Isaac his father said to him, “Who are you?” He said, “I am your son, your firstborn, Esau.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> Isaac answered Esau, “Behold, I have made him your lord, and all his brothers I have given to him for servants. <span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Isaiah 65:14"> Esau said to his father, “Do you have just one blessing, my father? Bless me, even me also, my father.” Esau lifted up his voice, and wept.</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will live by your sword, and you will serve your brother.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will happen, when you will break loose,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you will shake his yoke from off your neck.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> Now therefore, my son, obey my voice. Arise, flee to Laban, my brother, in Haran.</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Genesis 4:8–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> until your brother’s anger turns away from you, and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send, and get you from there. Why should I be bereaved of you both in one day?”</span>
