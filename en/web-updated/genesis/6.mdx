<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 1:28"> When men began to multiply on the surface of the ground, and daughters were born to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; 1 John 2:16; 2 Peter 2:14"> God’s sons saw that men’s daughters were beautiful, and they took any that they wanted for themselves as wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:30; Isaiah 63:10; Jeremiah 11:11"> The LORD said, “My Spirit will not strive with man forever, because he also is flesh; so his days will be one hundred twenty years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 13:33; 2 Samuel 21:15–22; 1 Samuel 17:4"> The Nephilim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, giants">a</a></sup>were on the earth in those days, and also after that, when God’s sons came in to men’s daughters and had children with them. Those were the mighty men who were of old, men of renown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Romans 1:28–31; Mark 7:21–23"> The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great on the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of man’s heart was continually only evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; 1 Samuel 15:11; Ephesians 4:30"> The LORD was sorry that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:3; Psalms 37:20; Jeremiah 12:3–4"> The LORD said, “I will destroy man whom I have created from the surface of the ground—man, along with animals, creeping things, and birds of the sky—for I am sorry that I have made them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Hebrews 4:16; Luke 1:30"> But Noah found favor in the LORD’s eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:6; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5"> This is the history of the generations of Noah: Noah was a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time. Noah walked with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 5:32"> Noah became the father of three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:17; Hosea 4:1–2; Habakkuk 2:8"> The earth was corrupt before God, and the earth was filled with violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 53:2–3; Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 14:1–3"> God saw the earth, and saw that it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted their way on the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:1–2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:2–6"> God said to Noah, “I will bring an end to all flesh, for the earth is filled with violence through them. Behold, I will destroy them and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:20; Luke 17:27; Matthew 24:38"> Make a ship of gopher wood. You shall make rooms in the ship, and shall seal it inside and outside with pitch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11; Genesis 7:20"> This is how you shall make it. The length of the ship shall be three hundred cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">b</a></sup>its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 8:6; Genesis 7:16; Ezekiel 41:16"> You shall make a roof in the ship, and you shall finish it to a cubit upward. You shall set the door of the ship in its side. You shall make it with lower, second, and third levels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; 2 Peter 2:5; Genesis 7:21–23"> I, even I, will bring the flood of waters on this earth, to destroy all flesh having the breath of life from under the sky. Everything that is on the earth will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 7:7; Genesis 17:7"> But I will establish my covenant with you. You shall come into the ship, you, your sons, your wife, and your sons’ wives with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 7:15–16; Genesis 7:8–9; Genesis 8:17"> Of every living thing of all flesh, you shall bring two of every sort into the ship, to keep them alive with you. They shall be male and female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 10:11–12; Genesis 2:19; Genesis 7:8–16"> Of the birds after their kind, of the livestock after their kind, of every creeping thing of the ground after its kind, two of every sort will come to you, to keep them alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 1:29–30; Psalms 145:16; Psalms 136:25"> Take with you some of all food that is eaten, and gather it to yourself; and it will be for food for you, and for them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; John 15:14; Matthew 7:24–27"> Thus Noah did. He did all that God commanded him.</span>
</div>
