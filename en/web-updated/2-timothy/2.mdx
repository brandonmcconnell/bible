<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> You therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> The things which you have heard from me among many witnesses, commit the same things to faithful men who will be able to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> You therefore must endure hardship as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No soldier on duty entangles himself in the affairs of life, that he may please him who enrolled him as a soldier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Also, if anyone competes in athletics, he isn’t crowned unless he has competed by the rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The farmer who labors must be the first to get a share of the crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Consider what I say, and may the Lord give you understanding in all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, of the offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, seed">a</a></sup>of David, according to my Good News,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> in which I suffer hardship to the point of chains as a criminal. But God’s word isn’t chained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> Therefore I endure all things for the chosen ones’ sake, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> This saying is trustworthy:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For if we died with him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will also live with him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we endure,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will also reign with him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If we deny him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he also will deny us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we are faithless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he remains faithful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for he can’t deny himself.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Remind them of these things, charging them in the sight of the Lord that they don’t argue about words to no profit, to the subverting of those who hear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Give diligence to present yourself approved by God, a workman who doesn’t need to be ashamed, properly handling the Word of Truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But shun empty chatter, for it will go further in ungodliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and those words will consume like gangrene, of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> men who have erred concerning the truth, saying that the resurrection is already past, and overthrowing the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> However, God’s firm foundation stands, having this seal: “The Lord knows those who are his,” and, “Let every one who names the name of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR reads “Christ” instead of “the Lord”">b</a></sup>depart from unrighteousness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> Now in a large house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of clay. Some are for honor and some for dishonor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> If anyone therefore purges himself from these, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified, and suitable for the master’s use, prepared for every good work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> Flee from youthful lusts; but pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But refuse foolish and ignorant questionings, knowing that they generate strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> The Lord’s servant must not quarrel, but be gentle toward all, able to teach, patient,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> in gentleness correcting those who oppose him. Perhaps God may give them repentance leading to a full knowledge of the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> and they may recover themselves out of the devil’s snare, having been taken captive by him to do his will.</span>
</div>
