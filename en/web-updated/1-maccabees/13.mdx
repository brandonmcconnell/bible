<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Simon heard that Tryphon had gathered together a mighty army to come into the land of Judah and destroy it utterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He saw that the people were trembling in great fear. So he went up to Jerusalem and gathered the people together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He encouraged them, and said to them, “You yourselves know all the things that I, my kindred, and my father’s house have done for the laws and the sanctuary, and the battles and the distresses which we have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Because of this, all my brothers have perished for Israel’s sake, and I am left alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now be it far from me, that I should spare my own life in any time of affliction, for I am not better than my kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> However, I will take vengeance for my nation, for the sanctuary, and for our wives and children, because all the Gentiles have gathered out of hatred to destroy us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The spirit of the people revived as soon as they heard these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They answered with a loud voice, saying, “You are our leader in the place of Judas and Jonathan your brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Fight our battles, and we will do all that you tell us to do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He gathered together all the men of war, and hurried to finish the walls of Jerusalem. He fortified it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He sent Jonathan the son of Absalom, and with him a great army, to Joppa. He threw out those who were in it, and lived there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Tryphon left Ptolemais with a mighty army to enter into the land of Judah, and Jonathan was with him under guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But Simon encamped at Adida, near the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Tryphon knew that Simon had risen up in the place of his brother Jonathan, and meant to join battle with him, so he sent ambassadors to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “It is for money which Jonathan your brother owed to the king’s treasury, by reason of the offices which he had, that we are detaining him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now send one hundred talents of silver and two of his sons for hostages, so that when he is released he may not revolt against us, and we will release him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Simon knew that they spoke to him deceitfully, but he sent to get the money and the children, lest perhaps he would arouse great hostility among the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> who might say, “Because I didn’t send him the money and the children, he perished.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> So he sent the children and the hundred talents, but Tryphon lied, and didn’t release Jonathan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> After this, Tryphon came to invade the land and destroy it, and he went around by the way that leads to Adora. Simon and his army marched near him to every place, wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Now the people of the citadel sent to Tryphon ambassadors, urging him to come to them through the wilderness, and to send them food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Tryphon prepared all his cavalry to come, but on that night a very heavy snow fell, and he didn’t come because of the snow. He marched off and went into the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When he came near to Bascama, he killed Jonathan, and he was buried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Then Tryphon turned back, and went away into his own land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Simon sent, and took the bones of Jonathan his brother, and buried him at Modin, the city of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> All Israel made great lamentation over him, and mourned for him many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Simon built a monument on the tomb of his father and his kindred, and raised it high so that it could be seen, with polished stone on the front and back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He also set up seven pyramids, one near another, for his father, his mother, and his four brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For these, he made an elaborate setting, erecting great pillars around them, and upon the pillars he made suits of armor for a perpetual memorial, and beside the suits of armor, he carved ships, so that they could be seen by all who sail on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> This is the tomb which he made at Modin. It remains to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Now Tryphon deceived the young King Antiochus and killed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and reigned in his place. He put on himself the crown of Asia and brought a great calamity upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Simon built up the strongholds of Judea, and walled them all around with high towers, great walls, gates, and bars; and he stored food in the strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Simon chose men, and sent to King Demetrius with a request that he grant the country an immunity, because all that Tryphon did was to plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> King Demetrius sent to him according to these words, and answered him, and wrote a letter to him, as follows:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> “King Demetrius to Simon the high priest and friend<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 1 Maccabees 2:18.">a</a></sup>of kings, and to the elders and nation of the Jews, greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> The golden crown and the palm branch, which you sent, we have received. We are ready to make a steadfast peace with you, yes, and to write to our officers to release you from tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Whatever things we confirmed to you, they are confirmed. The strongholds, which you have built, let them be your own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> As for any oversights and faults committed to this day, we forgive them, and the crown tax which you owed us. If there were any other tax collected in Jerusalem, let it be collected no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> If any among you are qualified to be enrolled in our court, let them be enrolled, and let there be peace between us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> In the one hundred seventieth year,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="circa B.C. 143.">b</a></sup>the yoke of the Gentiles was taken away from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The people began to write in their instruments and contracts, “In the first year of Simon, the great high priest and captain and leader of the Jews.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> In those days Simon encamped against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 1 Maccabees 13:53 (compare 1 Maccabees 13:48); 1 Maccabees 14:7, 34; 15:28; 16:1: also Josephus. All the authorities read Gaza in this verse.">c</a></sup>Gazara, and surrounded it with troops. He made a siege engine, and brought it up to the city, and struck a tower, and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Those who were in the engine leaped out into the city; and there was a great uproar in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> The people of the city tore their clothes, and went up on the walls with their wives and children, and cried with a loud voice, asking Simon to give them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. right hands.">d</a></sup>his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> They said, “Don’t deal with us according to our wickednesses, but according to your mercy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> So Simon was reconciled to them, and didn’t fight against them; but he expelled them from the city and cleansed the houses where the idols were, and then entered into it with singing and giving praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> He removed all uncleanness out of it, and placed in it men who would keep the law, and made it stronger than it was before, and built a dwelling place for himself in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> But the people of the citadel in Jerusalem were hindered from going out and from going into the country, and from buying and selling. So they were very hungry, and a great number of them perished from famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Then they cried out to Simon, that he should give them his right hand; and he gave it to them; but he expelled them from there, and he cleansed the citadel from its pollutions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> He entered into it on the twenty-third day of the second month, in the one hundred seventy-first year,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="circa B.C. 142.">e</a></sup>with praise and palm branches, with harps, with cymbals, and with stringed instruments, with hymns, and with songs, because a great enemy had been destroyed out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Simon ordained that they should keep that day every year with gladness. He made the hill of the temple that was by the citadel stronger than before, and he lived there with his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> Simon saw that his son John was a man, so he made him leader of all his forces; and he lived in Gazara.</span>
</div>
</div>
