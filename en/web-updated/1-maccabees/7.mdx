<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the one hundred fifty first year,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="circa B.C. 162.">a</a></sup>Demetrius the son of Seleucus came out of Rome, and went up with a few men to a city by the sea, and reigned there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> It came to pass, when he would go into the house of the kingdom of his fathers, that the army laid hands on Antiochus and Lysias, to bring them to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The thing became known to him, and he said, “Don’t show me their faces!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So the army killed them. Then Demetrius sat upon the throne of his kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> All the lawless and ungodly men of Israel came to him. Alcimus was their leader, desiring to be high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They accused the people to the king, saying, “Judas and his kindred have destroyed all your friends, and have scattered us from our own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now therefore send a man whom you trust, and let him go and see all the destruction which he has brought on us and the king’s country, and how he has punished them and all who helped them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So the king chose Bacchides, one of the king’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 Maccabees 2:18.">b</a></sup>friends, who was ruler in the country beyond the river, and was a great man in the kingdom, and faithful to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He sent him and that ungodly Alcimus, whom he made the high priest; and he commanded him to take vengeance upon the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> They marched away and came with a great army into the land of Judah. He sent messengers to Judas and his kindred with words of peace deceitfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They paid no attention to their words; for they saw that they had come with a great army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> A group of scribes gathered together to Alcimus and Bacchides to seek just terms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, Chasidim.">c</a></sup>Hasidaeans were the first among the children of Israel who sought peace from them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> for they said, “One who is a priest of the seed of Aaron has come with the army, and he will do us no wrong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He spoke with them words of peace, and swore to them, saying, “We won’t seek to harm you or your friends.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They trusted him. Then he seized sixty men of them, and killed them in one day, according to the word which was written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Psalms 79:2, 3.">d</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">The flesh of your saints</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their blood was shed all around Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there was no one to bury them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The fear and the dread of them fell upon all the people, for they said, “There is neither truth nor justice in them; for they have broken the covenant and the oath which they swore.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Bacchides withdrew from Jerusalem, and encamped in Bezeth. He sent and seized many of the deserters who were with him, and some of the people, and he killed them, throwing them into a large pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He placed Alcimus in charge of the country and left with him a force to aid him. Then Bacchides went away to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Alcimus struggled to maintain his high priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> All those who troubled their people joined him, and they took control of the land of Judah, and did great damage in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Judas saw all the wrongs that Alcimus and his company had done among the children of Israel, even more than the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He went out into all the borders of Judea and took vengeance on the men who had deserted from him, and they were restrained from going out into the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But when Alcimus saw that Judas and his company had grown strong, and knew that he was not able to withstand them, he returned to the king, and brought evil accusations against them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See 2 Maccabees 14:12.">e</a></sup>Then the king sent Nicanor, one of his honorable princes, a man who hated Israel and was their enemy, and commanded him to destroy the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Nicanor came to Jerusalem with a great army. He sent to Judas and his kindred deceitfully with words of peace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> “Let there be no battle between me and you; I will come with a few men, that I may see your faces in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> He came to Judas, and they saluted one another peaceably. The enemies were ready to seize Judas by violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> This was known to Judas, that he came to him with deceit, and he was very afraid of him, and would see his face no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Nicanor found out that his plan was disclosed; and he went out to meet Judas in battle beside Capharsalama.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> About five hundred men of Nicanor’s army fell, and the rest fled into the city of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> After these things, Nicanor went up to mount Zion. Some of the priests came out of the sanctuary, with some of the elders of the people, to salute him peaceably, and to show him the whole burned sacrifice that was being offered for the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> He mocked them, laughed at them, derided them shamefully,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. polluted them.">f</a></sup>spoke arrogantly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and swore in a rage, saying, “Unless Judas and his army are now delivered into my hands, it shall be that, if I return safely, I will burn up this house!” And he went out in a great rage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The priests entered in, and stood before the altar and the temple; and they wept, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> “You chose this house to be called by your name, to be a house of prayer and supplication for your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Take vengeance on this man and his army, and let them fall by the sword. Remember their blasphemies, and don’t allow them to live any longer.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then Nicanor went out from Jerusalem and encamped in Bethhoron, and there the Syrian army met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Judas encamped in Adasa with three thousand men. Judas prayed and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> “When those who came from the king blasphemed, your angel went out, and struck among them one hundred eighty-five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Even so, crush this army before us today, and let all the rest know that he has spoken wickedly against your sanctuary. Judge him according to his wickedness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> On the thirteenth day of the month Adar, the armies met in battle. Nicanor’s army was defeated, and he himself was the first to fall in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Now when his army saw that Nicanor had fallen, they threw away their weapons and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> They pursued them a day’s journey from Adasa until you come to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. Gazera.">g</a></sup>Gazara, and they sounded an alarm after them with the signal trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Men came out of all the surrounding villages of Judea, and outflanked them. These turned them back on those, and they all fell by the sword. There wasn’t one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> The Jews took the spoils and the booty, and they cut off Nicanor’s head and his right hand, which he had stretched out so arrogantly, and brought them, and hung them up beside Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> The people were exceedingly glad, and they kept that day as a day of great gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See 2 Maccabees 15:36.">h</a></sup>They ordained to keep this day year by year on the thirteenth day of Adar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> So the land of Judah had rest a few days.</span>
</div>
