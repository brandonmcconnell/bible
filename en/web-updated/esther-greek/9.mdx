<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now in the twelfth month, on the thirteenth day of the month, which is Adar, the letters written by the king arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> In that day, the adversaries of the Jews perished; for no one resisted, through fear of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For the chiefs of the local governors, and the princes and the royal scribes, honored the Jews; for the fear of Mordecai was upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For the order of the king was in force, that he should be celebrated in all the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In the city Susa the Jews killed five hundred men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> including Pharsannes, Delphon, Phasga,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Pharadatha, Barea, Sarbaca,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Marmasima, Ruphaeus, Arsaeus, and Zabuthaeus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> the ten sons of Haman the son of Hammedatha the Bugaean, the enemy of the Jews; and they plundered their property on the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The number of those who perished in Susa was reported to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then the king said to Esther, “The Jews have slain five hundred men in the city Susa. What do you think they have done in the rest of the country? What more do you ask, that it may be done for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Esther said to the king, “Let it be granted to the Jews to do the same to them tomorrow. Also, hang the bodies of the ten sons of Haman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He permitted it to be done; and he gave up to the Jews of the city the bodies of the sons of Haman to hang.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The Jews assembled in Susa on the fourteenth day of Adar and killed three hundred men, but plundered no property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The rest of the Jews who were in the kingdom assembled, and helped one another, and obtained rest from their enemies; for they destroyed fifteen thousand of them on the thirteenth day of Adar, but took no spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They rested on the fourteenth of the same month, and kept it as a day of rest with joy and gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The Jews in the city of Susa assembled also on the fourteenth day and rested; and they also observed the fifteenth with joy and gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> On this account then, the Jews dispersed in every foreign land keep the fourteenth of Adar as a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek good day.">a</a></sup>holy day with joy, each sending gifts of food to his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Mordecai wrote these things in a book and sent them to the Jews, as many as were in the kingdom of Ahasuerus, both those who were near and those who were far away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> to establish these as joyful days and to keep the fourteenth and fifteenth of Adar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> for on these days the Jews obtained rest from their enemies; and in that month, which was Adar, in which a change was made for them from mourning to joy, and from sorrow to a holiday, to spend the whole of it in good days of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greek weddings.">b</a></sup>feasting and gladness, sending portions to their friends and to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the Jews consented to this as Mordecai wrote to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> showing how Haman the son of Hammedatha the Macedonian fought against them, how he made a decree and cast<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek lot.">c</a></sup>lots to destroy them utterly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> also how he went in to the king, telling him to hang Mordecai; but all the calamities he tried to bring upon the Jews came upon himself, and he was hanged, along with his children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Therefore these days were called Purim, because of the lots (for in their language they are called Purim) because of the words of this letter, and because of all they suffered on this account, and all that happened to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Mordecai established it, and the Jews took upon themselves, upon their offspring, and upon those who were joined to them to observe it, neither would they on any account behave differently; but these days were to be a memorial kept in every generation, city, family, and province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> These days of Purim shall be kept forever, and their memorial shall not fail in any generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Queen Esther the daughter of Aminadab and Mordecai the Jew wrote all that they had done, and gave the confirmation of the letter about Purim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Mordecai and Esther the queen established this decision on their own, pledging their own well-being to their plan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And Esther established it by a command forever, and it was written for a memorial.</span>
