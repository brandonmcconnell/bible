<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> She answered and said, “If I have found favor in the sight of the king, let my life be granted as my petition, and my people as my request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For both I and my people are sold for destruction, pillage, and genocide. If both we and our children were sold for male and female slaves, I would not have bothered you, for this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see Hebrew: slanderer">a</a></sup>isn’t worthy of the king’s palace.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Esther said, “The enemy is Haman, this wicked man!”<br />Then Haman was terrified in the presence of the king and the queen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The king rose up from the banquet to go into the garden. Haman began to beg the queen for mercy, for he saw that he was in serious trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The king returned from the garden; and Haman had fallen upon the couch, begging the queen for mercy. The king said, “Will you even assault my wife in my house?”<br />And when Haman heard it, he changed countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Bugathan, one of the chamberlains, said to the king, “Behold, Haman has also prepared a gallows for Mordecai, who spoke concerning the king, and a fifty cubit high gallows has been set up on Haman’s property.”<br />The king said, “Let him be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, impaled.">b</a></sup>hanged on it!”</span>
