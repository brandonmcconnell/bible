<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 20:22–23; Acts 21:10–12; Acts 20:6–7"> Having found disciples, we stayed there seven days. These said to Paul through the Spirit that he should not go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 20:36; Acts 9:40; Psalms 95:6"> When those days were over, we departed and went on our journey. They all, with wives and children, brought us on our way until we were out of the city. Kneeling down on the beach, we prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:27; John 1:11; 2 Corinthians 2:13"> After saying goodbye to each other, we went on board the ship, and they returned home again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:24; 1 Samuel 10:4; Acts 28:12"> When we had finished the voyage from Tyre, we arrived at Ptolemais. We greeted the brothers and stayed with them one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 8:26–40; Ephesians 4:11"> On the next day, we who were Paul’s companions departed and came to Caesarea. We entered into the house of Philip the evangelist, who was one of the seven, and stayed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Revelation 2:20; Joel 2:28"> Now this man had four virgin daughters who prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 11:28; Acts 21:4; Acts 20:16"> As we stayed there some days, a certain prophet named Agabus came down from Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Jeremiah 13:1–11; 1 Samuel 15:27–28"> Coming to us and taking Paul’s belt, he bound his own feet and hands, and said, “The Holy Spirit says: ‘So the Jews at Jerusalem will bind the man who owns this belt, and will deliver him into the hands of the Gentiles.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:21–23; Acts 20:22; Acts 21:4"> When we heard these things, both we and the people of that place begged him not to go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Philippians 2:17; Philippians 1:20–21"> Then Paul answered, “What are you doing, weeping and breaking my heart? For I am ready not only to be bound, but also to die at Jerusalem for the name of the Lord Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 26:39"> When he would not be persuaded, we ceased, saying, “The Lord’s will be done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 25:1; Acts 25:6; Acts 25:9"> After these days we took up our baggage and went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Acts 21:8; Psalms 92:14"> Some of the disciples from Caesarea also went with us, bringing one Mnason of Cyprus, an early disciple, with whom we would stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 15:4; Romans 15:7; Hebrews 13:1–2"> When we had come to Jerusalem, the brothers received us gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 1:19; Galatians 2:9; Acts 15:13"> The day following, Paul went in with us to James; and all the elders were present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 1:17; Romans 15:18–19"> When he had greeted them, he reported one by one the things which God had worked among the Gentiles through his ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2; Galatians 1:14; Acts 22:3"> They, when they heard it, glorified God. They said to him, “You see, brother, how many thousands there are among the Jews of those who have believed, and they are all zealous for the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:12–15; 1 Corinthians 9:19–21; Galatians 5:1–6"> They have been informed about you, that you teach all the Jews who are among the Gentiles to forsake Moses, telling them not to circumcise their children and not to walk after the customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Acts 15:22; Acts 19:32"> What then? The assembly must certainly meet, for they will hear that you have come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 18:18; Numbers 6:2–7"> Therefore do what we tell you. We have four men who have taken a vow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:26; Acts 24:18; Acts 18:18"> Take them and purify yourself with them, and pay their expenses for them, that they may shave their heads. Then all will know that there is no truth in the things that they have been informed about you, but that you yourself also walk keeping the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Acts 15:19–20"> But concerning the Gentiles who believe, we have written our decision that they should observe no such thing, except that they should keep themselves from food offered to idols, from blood, from strangled things, and from sexual immorality.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 24:18; Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:13–20"> Then Paul took the men, and the next day purified himself and went with them into the temple, declaring the fulfillment of the days of purification, until the offering was offered for every one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 24:18; Acts 13:50"> When the seven days were almost completed, the Jews from Asia, when they saw him in the temple, stirred up all the multitude and laid hands on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 24:5–6; Acts 21:21; Acts 26:20–21"> crying out, “Men of Israel, help! This is the man who teaches all men everywhere against the people, and the law, and this place. Moreover, he also brought Greeks into the temple and has defiled this holy place!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 18:19"> For they had seen Trophimus the Ephesian with him in the city, and they supposed that Paul had brought him into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:21; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 16:19–22"> All the city was moved and the people ran together. They seized Paul and dragged him out of the temple. Immediately the doors were shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–33; Acts 17:5; Acts 26:9–10"> As they were trying to kill him, news came up to the commanding officer of the regiment that all Jerusalem was in an uproar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Isaiah 3:15; Acts 5:40"> Immediately he took soldiers and centurions and ran down to them. They, when they saw the chief captain and the soldiers, stopped beating Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Acts 21:11; Acts 20:23"> Then the commanding officer came near, arrested him, commanded him to be bound with two chains, and inquired who he was and what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:32; Acts 23:10; Acts 22:24"> Some shouted one thing and some another, among the crowd. When he couldn’t find out the truth because of the noise, he commanded him to be brought into the barracks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Psalms 55:9; Psalms 58:2"> When he came to the stairs, he was carried by the soldiers because of the violence of the crowd;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 19:15; Acts 22:22; Luke 23:18"> for the multitude of the people followed after, crying out, “Away with him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Luke 21:15; Acts 19:30"> As Paul was about to be brought into the barracks, he asked the commanding officer, “May I speak to you?” He said, “Do you know Greek?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 24:26; Acts 5:36–37; Matthew 5:11"> Aren’t you then the Egyptian who before these days stirred up to sedition and led out into the wilderness the four thousand men of the Assassins?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Acts 9:11; Acts 23:27"> But Paul said, “I am a Jew from Tarsus in Cilicia, a citizen of no insignificant city. I beg you, allow me to speak to the people.”</span>
