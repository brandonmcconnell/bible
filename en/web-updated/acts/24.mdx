<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:11; Acts 23:2; Acts 25:2"> After five days, the high priest, Ananias, came down with certain elders and an orator, one Tertullus. They informed the governor against Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:5; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 24:26–27"> When he was called, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying, “Seeing that by you we enjoy much peace, and that prosperity is coming to this nation by your foresight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> we accept it in all ways and in all places, most excellent Felix, with all thankfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32"> But that I don’t delay you, I entreat you to bear with us and hear a few words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 28:22; Acts 24:14; Acts 26:5"> For we have found this man to be a plague, an instigator of insurrections among all the Jews throughout the world, and a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 21:27–32; John 18:31"> He even tried to profane the temple, and we arrested him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “We wanted to judge him according to our law,”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “but the commanding officer, Lysias, came by and with great violence took him out of our hands,”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:15–16; Acts 25:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR adds “commanding his accusers to come to you.”">c</a></sup>By examining him yourself you may ascertain all these things of which we accuse him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Micah 7:2–3; Psalms 4:2"> The Jews also joined in the attack, affirming that these things were so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> When the governor had beckoned to him to speak, Paul answered, “Because I know that you have been a judge of this nation for many years, I cheerfully make my defense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> seeing that you can verify that it is not more than twelve days since I went up to worship at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> In the temple they didn’t find me disputing with anyone or stirring up a crowd, either in the synagogues or in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> Nor can they prove to you the things of which they now accuse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> But this I confess to you, that according to the Way, which they call a sect, so I serve the God of our fathers, believing all things which are according to the law, and which are written in the prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> having hope toward God, which these also themselves look for, that there will be a resurrection of the dead, both of the just and unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> In this I also practice always having a conscience void of offense toward God and men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> Now after some years, I came to bring gifts for the needy to my nation, and offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> amid which certain Jews from Asia found me purified in the temple, not with a mob, nor with turmoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> They ought to have been here before you and to make accusation if they had anything against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Or else let these men themselves say what injustice they found in me when I stood before the council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> unless it is for this one thing that I cried standing among them, ‘Concerning the resurrection of the dead I am being judged before you today!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> But Felix, having more exact knowledge concerning the Way, deferred them, saying, “When Lysias, the commanding officer, comes down, I will decide your case.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> He ordered the centurion that Paul should be kept in custody and should have some privileges, and not to forbid any of his friends to serve him or to visit him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> After some days, Felix came with Drusilla his wife, who was a Jewess, and sent for Paul and heard him concerning the faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> As he reasoned about righteousness, self-control, and the judgment to come, Felix was terrified, and answered, “Go your way for this time, and when it is convenient for me, I will summon you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> Meanwhile, he also hoped that money would be given to him by Paul, that he might release him. Therefore also he sent for him more often and talked with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> But when two years were fulfilled, Felix was succeeded by Porcius Festus, and desiring to gain favor with the Jews, Felix left Paul in bonds.</span>
</div>
</div>
