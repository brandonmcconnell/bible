<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> This is a faithful saying: someone who seeks to be an overseer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, superintendent, or bishop">a</a></sup>desires a good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> The overseer therefore must be without reproach, the husband of one wife, temperate, sensible, modest, hospitable, good at teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not a drinker, not violent, not greedy for money, but gentle, not quarrelsome, not covetous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> one who rules his own house well, having children in subjection with all reverence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> (for how could someone who doesn’t know how to rule his own house take care of God’s assembly?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> not a new convert, lest being puffed up he fall into the same condemnation as the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> Moreover he must have good testimony from those who are outside, to avoid falling into reproach and the snare of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Servants,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, Deacons.">b</a></sup>in the same way, must be reverent, not double-tongued, not addicted to much wine, not greedy for money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> Let them also first be tested; then let them serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, serve as deacons">c</a></sup>if they are blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Their wives in the same way must be reverent, not slanderers, temperate, and faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Let servants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, deacons">d</a></sup>be husbands of one wife, ruling their children and their own houses well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> For those who have served well<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="or, served well as deacons">e</a></sup>gain for themselves a good standing and great boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> These things I write to you, hoping to come to you shortly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> but if I wait long, that you may know how men ought to behave themselves in God’s house, which is the assembly of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> Without controversy, the mystery of godliness is great:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">God</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="NU replaces “God” with “who”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was revealed in the flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">justified in the spirit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">seen by angels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">preached among the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">believed on in the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and received up in glory.</span></span>
