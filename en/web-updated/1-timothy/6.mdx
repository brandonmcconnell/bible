<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Peter 2:17–20; Titus 2:8–10"> Let as many as are bondservants under the yoke count their own masters worthy of all honor, that the name of God and the doctrine not be blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; 1 Timothy 4:11; Genesis 16:4–5"> Those who have believing masters, let them not despise them because they are brothers, but rather let them serve them, because those who partake of the benefit are believing and beloved. Teach and exhort these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:3; Romans 16:17; 1 Timothy 1:10"> If anyone teaches a different doctrine and doesn’t consent to sound words, the words of our Lord Jesus Christ, and to the doctrine which is according to godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 3:4; 1 Timothy 3:6"> he is conceited, knowing nothing, but obsessed with arguments, disputes, and word battles, from which come envy, strife, insulting, evil suspicions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3"> constant friction of people of corrupt minds and destitute of the truth, who suppose that godliness is a means of gain. Withdraw yourself from such.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU omits “Withdraw yourself from such.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:8; Proverbs 16:8"> But godliness with contentment is great gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 1:21; Psalms 49:17; Luke 12:20–21"> For we brought nothing into the world, and we certainly can’t carry anything out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:25–33"> But having food and clothing, we will be content with that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 3:7"> But those who are determined to be rich fall into a temptation, a snare, and many foolish and harmful lusts, such as drown men in ruin and destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 1:19; 1 Timothy 6:9; Psalms 32:10"> For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. Some have been led astray from the faith in their greed, and have pierced themselves through with many sorrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 3:17; 1 Timothy 4:12"> But you, man of God, flee these things, and follow after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, perseverance, and gentleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25–26"> Fight the good fight of faith. Take hold of the eternal life to which you were called, and you confessed the good confession in the sight of many witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:36–37; Matthew 27:11; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I command you before God who gives life to all things, and before Christ Jesus who before Pontius Pilate testified the good confession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> that you keep the commandment without spot, blameless until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 1:17"> which at the right time he will show, who is the blessed and only Ruler, the King of kings and Lord of lords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; John 1:18; 1 John 1:5"> He alone has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light, whom no man has seen nor can see, to whom be honor and eternal power. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; Acts 14:17; Proverbs 27:24"> Charge those who are rich in this present age that they not be arrogant, nor have their hope set on the uncertainty of riches, but on the living God, who richly provides us with everything to enjoy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 John 3:17; Psalms 37:3"> that they do good, that they be rich in good works, that they be ready to distribute, willing to share;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; 1 Timothy 6:12; 2 Timothy 2:19"> laying up in store for themselves a good foundation against the time to come, that they may lay hold of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 2 Timothy 2:16; 2 Timothy 1:12–14"> Timothy, guard that which is committed to you, turning away from the empty chatter and oppositions of what is falsely called knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:18; 1 Timothy 1:6; 2 Timothy 4:22"> which some profess, and thus have wandered from the faith. Grace be with you. Amen.</span>
