<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Maccabaeus and those who were with him, the Lord leading them on, recovered the temple and the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> They pulled down the altars that had been built in the marketplace by the foreigners, and also the sacred enclosures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Having cleansed the sanctuary, they made another altar of sacrifice.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. firing.">a</a></sup>Striking flint and starting a fire, they offered sacrifices after they had ceased for two years, burned incense, lit lamps, and set out the show bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> When they had done these things, they fell prostrate and implored the Lord that they might fall no more into such evils; but that, if they ever did sin, they might be chastened by him with forbearance, and not be delivered to blaspheming and barbarous heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now on the same day that the sanctuary was profaned by foreigners, upon that very day it came to pass that the sanctuary was cleansed, even on the twenty-fifth day of the same month, which is Chislev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They observed eight days with gladness in the manner of the feast of tabernacles, remembering how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, not long before they kept the feast of tabernacles by wandering">b</a></sup>not long before, during the feast of tabernacles, they were wandering in the mountains and in the caves like wild animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore carrying wands wreathed with leaves, and beautiful branches, and palm fronds also, they offered up hymns of thanksgiving to him who had successfully brought to pass the cleansing of his own place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They ordained also with a public statute and decree, for all the nation of the Jews, that they should observe these days every year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Such were the events of the end of Antiochus, who was called Epiphanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Now we will declare what came to pass under Antiochus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, son of a good father.">c</a></sup>Eupator, who proved himself a son of that ungodly man, and will summarize the main evils of the wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For this man, when he succeeded to the kingdom, appointed one Lysias to be chancellor and supreme governor of Coelesyria and Phoenicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For Ptolemy who was called Macron, setting an example of observing justice toward the Jews because of the wrong that had been done to them, endeavored to deal with them on peaceful terms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Whereupon being accused by the king’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 2 Maccabees 8:9">d</a></sup>friends before Eupator, and hearing himself called traitor at every turn because he had abandoned Cyprus which Philometor had entrusted to him, and had withdrawn himself to Antiochus Epiphanes, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Greek text here is corrupt.">e</a></sup>failing to uphold the honor of his office, he took poison and did away with himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But when Gorgias was made governor of the district, he maintained a force of mercenaries, and at every turn kept up war with the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Together with him the Idumaeans also, being masters of important strongholds, harassed the Jews; and received those who had taken refuge from Jerusalem, they endeavored to keep up the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But Maccabaeus and his men, having made solemn supplication and having implored God to fight on their side, rushed upon the strongholds of the Idumaeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Assaulting them vigorously, they took control of the positions, and kept off all who fought upon the wall, and killed those whom they encountered, killing no fewer than twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Because no fewer than nine thousand had fled into two very strong towers having everything needed for a seige,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Maccabaeus, having left Simon and Joseph, and also Zacchaeus and those who were with him, a force sufficient to besiege them, departed himself to places where he was most needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But Simon and those who were with him, yielding to covetousness, were bribed by some of those who were in the towers, and receiving seventy thousand drachmas, let some of them slip away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But when word was brought to Maccabaeus of what was done, he gathered the leaders of the people together, and accused those men of having sold their kindred for money by setting their enemies free to fight against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So he killed these men for having turned traitors, and immediately took possession of the two towers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Prospering with his weapons in everything he undertook, he destroyed more than twenty thousand in the two strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now Timotheus, who had been defeated by the Jews before, having gathered together foreign forces in great multitudes, and having collected the cavalry which belonged to Asia, not a few, came as though he would take Judea by force of weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But as he drew near, Maccabaeus and his men sprinkled dirt on their heads and girded their loins with sackcloth, in supplication to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and falling down upon the step in front of the altar, implored him to become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. propitious.">f</a></sup>gracious to them, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Exodus 23:22.">g</a></sup>be an enemy to their enemies and an adversary to their adversaries, as the law declares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Rising from their prayer they took up their weapons, and advanced some distance from the city. When they had come near to their enemies, they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. were by themselves.">h</a></sup>halted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When the dawn was now breaking, the two armies joined in battle, the one part having this, beside virtue, for a pledge of success and victory, that they had fled to the Lord for refuge, the others making their passion their leader in the fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> When the battle became strong, there appeared out of heaven to their adversaries five splendid men on horses with bridles of gold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some authorities read and leading on the Jews; who also, taking.">i</a></sup>and two of them, leading on the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and taking Maccabaeus in the midst of them, and covering him with their own armor, guarded him from wounds, while they shot arrows and thunderbolts at the enemies. For this reason, they were blinded and thrown into confusion, and were cut to pieces, filled with bewilderment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Twenty thousand five hundred were slain, beside six hundred cavalry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Timotheus himself fled into a stronghold called Gazara, a fortress of great strength,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See ver. 37.">j</a></sup>where Chaereas was in command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Then Maccabaeus and his men were glad and laid siege to the fortress for four days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Those who were within, trusting in the strength of the place, blasphemed exceedingly, and hurled out impious words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> But at dawn of the fifth day, certain young men of Maccabaeus’ company, inflamed with anger because of the blasphemies, assaulted the wall with masculine force and with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. passion as of wild animals.">k</a></sup>furious anger, and cut down whoever came in their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Others climbing up in the same way, while the enemies were distracted with those who had made their way within, set fire to the towers, and kindled fires that burned the blasphemers alive, while others broke open the gates, and, having given entrance to the rest of the band, occupied the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They killed Timotheus, who was hidden in a cistern, and his brother Chaereas, and Apollophanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When they had accomplished these things, they blessed the Lord with hymns and thanksgiving, blessing him who provides great benefits to Israel and gives them the victory.</span>
