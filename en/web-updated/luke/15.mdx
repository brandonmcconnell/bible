<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He told them this parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> <span className="woj">“Which of you men, if you had one hundred sheep and lost one of them, wouldn’t leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness and go after the one that was lost, until he found it?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> <span className="woj">When he has found it, he carries it on his shoulders, rejoicing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> <span className="woj">When he comes home, he calls together his friends and his neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep which was lost!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> <span className="woj">I tell you that even so there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents, than over ninety-nine righteous people who need no repentance.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> <span className="woj">“Or what woman, if she had ten drachma</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A drachma coin was worth about a days wages for an agricultural laborer.">a</a></sup><span className="woj">coins, if she lost one drachma coin, wouldn’t light a lamp, sweep the house, and seek diligently until she found it?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> <span className="woj">When she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found the drachma which I had lost!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> <span className="woj">Even so, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner repenting.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> He said,<span className="woj">“A certain man had two sons.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> <span className="woj">The younger of them said to his father, ‘Father, give me my share of your property.’ So he divided his livelihood between them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> <span className="woj">Not many days after, the younger son gathered all of this together and traveled into a far country. There he wasted his property with riotous living.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> <span className="woj">When he had spent all of it, there arose a severe famine in that country, and he began to be in need.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> <span className="woj">He went and joined himself to one of the citizens of that country, and he sent him into his fields to feed pigs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> <span className="woj">He wanted to fill his belly with the pods that the pigs ate, but no one gave him any.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> <span className="woj">But when he came to himself, he said, ‘How many hired servants of my father’s have bread enough to spare, and I’m dying with hunger!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> <span className="woj">I will get up and go to my father, and will tell him, “Father, I have sinned against heaven and in your sight.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> <span className="woj">I am no more worthy to be called your son. Make me as one of your hired servants.”’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> <span className="woj">“He arose and came to his father. But while he was still far off, his father saw him and was moved with compassion, and ran, fell on his neck, and kissed him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> <span className="woj">The son said to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and in your sight. I am no longer worthy to be called your son.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> <span className="woj">“But the father said to his servants, ‘Bring out the best robe and put it on him. Put a ring on his hand and sandals on his feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> <span className="woj">Bring the fattened calf, kill it, and let’s eat and celebrate;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> <span className="woj">for this, my son, was dead and is alive again. He was lost and is found.’ Then they began to celebrate.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> <span className="woj">“Now his elder son was in the field. As he came near to the house, he heard music and dancing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="woj">He called one of the servants to him and asked what was going on.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> <span className="woj">He said to him, ‘Your brother has come, and your father has killed the fattened calf, because he has received him back safe and healthy.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> <span className="woj">But he was angry and would not go in. Therefore his father came out and begged him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> <span className="woj">But he answered his father, ‘Behold, these many years I have served you, and I never disobeyed a commandment of yours, but you never gave me a goat, that I might celebrate with my friends.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> <span className="woj">But when this your son came, who has devoured your living with prostitutes, you killed the fattened calf for him.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> <span className="woj">“He said to him, ‘Son, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> <span className="woj">But it was appropriate to celebrate and be glad, for this, your brother, was dead, and is alive again. He was lost, and is found.’”</span></span>
