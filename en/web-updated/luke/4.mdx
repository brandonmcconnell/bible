<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Acts 10:38; Mark 1:12–13"> Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 4:15; Deuteronomy 9:9"> for forty days, being tempted by the devil. He ate nothing in those days. Afterward, when they were completed, he was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:3; Luke 3:22"> The devil said to him, “If you are the Son of God, command this stone to become bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4; Matthew 6:31"> Jesus answered him, saying,<span className="woj">“It is written, ‘Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Matthew 4:8"> The devil, leading him up on a high mountain, showed him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 5:19; John 14:30"> The devil said to him, “I will give you all this authority and their glory, for it has been delivered to me, and I give it to whomever I want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 8:28; Psalms 72:11; Isaiah 45:14"> If you therefore will worship before me, it will all be yours.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“Get behind me, Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and you shall serve him only.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 4:5–6; Luke 4:3; Matthew 8:29"> He led him to Jerusalem and set him on the pinnacle of the temple, and said to him, “If you are the Son of God, cast yourself down from here,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Corinthians 11:14"> for it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘He will put his angels in charge of you, to guard you;’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 91:12"> and,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘On their hands they will bear you up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest perhaps you dash your foot against a stone.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Hebrews 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> Jesus answering, said to him,<span className="woj">“It has been said, ‘You shall not tempt the Lord your God.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; James 4:7; Matthew 4:11"> When the devil had completed every temptation, he departed from him until another time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–25; Acts 10:37; Matthew 4:12"> Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee, and news about him spread through all the surrounding area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 9:8; Matthew 9:35"> He taught in their synagogues, being glorified by all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:2; Acts 13:14–16; Matthew 2:23"> He came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up. He entered, as was his custom, into the synagogue on the Sabbath day, and stood up to read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> The book of the prophet Isaiah was handed to him. He opened the book, and found the place where it was written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; Psalms 146:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">“The Spirit of the Lord is on me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">because he has anointed me to preach good news to the poor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">He has sent me to heal the broken hearted,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU omits “to heal the broken hearted”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">to proclaim release to the captives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">recovering of sight to the blind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">to deliver those who are crushed,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 61:2; Isaiah 63:4; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> <span className="pl pl-2 woj">and to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 4:17; John 8:2; Luke 19:48"> He closed the book, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of all in the synagogue were fastened on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> He began to tell them,<span className="woj">“Today, this Scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; Psalms 45:2; Acts 6:10"> All testified about him and wondered at the gracious words which proceeded out of his mouth; and they said, “Isn’t this Joseph’s son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Doubtless you will tell me this proverb, ‘Physician, heal yourself! Whatever we have heard done at Capernaum, do also here in your hometown.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; John 4:44; Mark 6:4–5"> He said,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, no prophet is acceptable in his hometown.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 5:17; 1 Kings 17:1; Matthew 20:15"> <span className="woj">But truly I tell you, there were many widows in Israel in the days of Elijah, when the sky was shut up three years and six months, when a great famine came over all the land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> <span className="woj">Elijah was sent to none of them, except to Zarephath, in the land of Sidon, to a woman who was a widow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 5:1–27; John 17:12; Matthew 12:4"> <span className="woj">There were many lepers in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet, yet not one of them was cleansed, except Naaman, the Syrian.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> They were all filled with wrath in the synagogue as they heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:59; Acts 21:28–32; Numbers 15:35"> They rose up, threw him out of the city, and led him to the brow of the hill that their city was built on, that they might throw him off the cliff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> But he, passing through the middle of them, went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> He came down to Capernaum, a city of Galilee. He was teaching them on the Sabbath day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> and they were astonished at his teaching, for his word was with authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> In the synagogue there was a man who had a spirit of an unclean demon; and he cried out with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> saying, “Ah! what have we to do with you, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> Jesus rebuked him, saying,<span className="woj">“Be silent and come out of him!”</span>When the demon had thrown him down in the middle of them, he came out of him, having done him no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> Amazement came on all and they spoke together, one with another, saying, “What is this word? For with authority and power he commands the unclean spirits, and they come out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> News about him went out into every place of the surrounding region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> He rose up from the synagogue and entered into Simon’s house. Simon’s mother-in-law was afflicted with a great fever, and they begged him to help her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> He stood over her and rebuked the fever, and it left her. Immediately she rose up and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> When the sun was setting, all those who had any sick with various diseases brought them to him; and he laid his hands on every one of them, and healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> Demons also came out of many, crying out and saying, “You are the Christ, the Son of God!” Rebuking them, he didn’t allow them to speak, because they knew that he was the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> When it was day, he departed and went into an uninhabited place and the multitudes looked for him, and came to him, and held on to him, so that he wouldn’t go away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> But he said to them,<span className="woj">“I must preach the good news of God’s Kingdom to the other cities also. For this reason I have been sent.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> He was preaching in the synagogues of Galilee.</span>
