<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord says, “I brought this people out of bondage. I gave them my commandments by my servants the prophets, whom they would not listen to, but made my counsels void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The mother who bore them says to them, ‘Go your way, my children, for I am a widow and forsaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I brought you up with gladness, and I have lost you with sorrow and heaviness, for you have sinned before the Lord God, and done that which is evil before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But now what can I do for you? For I am a widow and forsaken. Go your way, my children, and ask for mercy from the Lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> As for me, O father, I call upon you for a witness in addition to the mother of these children, because they would not keep my covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> that you may bring them to confusion, and their mother to ruin, that they may have no offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Let them be scattered abroad among the heathen. Let their names be blotted out of the earth, for they have despised my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Woe to you, Assur, you who hide the unrighteous with you! You wicked nation, remember what I did to Sodom and Gomorrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> whose land lies in lumps of pitch and heaps of ashes. That is what I will also do to those who have not listened to me,” says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The Lord says to Esdras, “Tell my people that I will give them the kingdom of Jerusalem, which I would have given to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I will also take their glory back to myself, and give these the everlasting tabernacles which I had prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They will have the tree of life for fragrant perfume. They will neither labor nor be weary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Ask, and you will receive. Pray that your days may be few, that they may be shortened. The kingdom is already prepared for you. Watch!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Call heaven and earth to witness. Call them to witness, for I have left out evil, and created the good, for I live, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “Mother, embrace your children. I will bring them out with gladness like a dove does. Establish their feet, for I have chosen you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I will raise those who are dead up again from their places, and bring them out from their tombs, for I recognize my name in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Don’t be afraid, you mother of children, for I have chosen you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For your help, I will send my servants Esaias and Jeremy, after whose counsel I have sanctified and prepared for you twelve trees laden with various fruits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and as many springs flowing with milk and honey, and seven mighty mountains, on which roses and lilies grow, with which I will fill your children with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Do right to the widow. Secure justice for the fatherless. Give to the poor. Defend the orphan. Clothe the naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Heal the broken and the weak. Don’t laugh a lame man to scorn. Defend the maimed. Let the blind man have a vision of my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Protect the old and young within your walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Wherever you find the dead, set a sign upon them and commit them to the grave, and I will give you the first place in my resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Stay still, my people, and take your rest, for your rest will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Nourish your children, good nurse, and establish their feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> As for the servants whom I have given you, not one of them will perish, for I will require them from among your number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Don’t be anxious, for when the day of suffering and anguish comes, others will weep and be sorrowful, but you will rejoice and have abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The nations will envy you, but they will be able to do nothing against you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> My hands will cover you, so that your children don’t see Gehenna.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, Hell.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Be joyful, mother, with your children, for I will deliver you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Remember your children who sleep, for I will bring them out of the secret places of the earth and show mercy to them, for I am merciful, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Embrace your children until I come, and proclaim mercy to them, for my wells run over, and my grace won’t fail.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> I, Esdras, received a command from the Lord on Mount Horeb to go to Israel, but when I came to them, they rejected me and rejected the Lord’s commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Therefore I say to you, O nations that hear and understand, “Look for your shepherd. He will give you everlasting rest, for he is near at hand who will come at the end of the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Be ready for the rewards of the kingdom, for the everlasting light will shine on you forevermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Flee the shadow of this world, receive the joy of your glory. I call to witness my savior openly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Receive that which is given to you by the Lord, and be joyful, giving thanks to him who has called you to heavenly kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Arise and stand up, and see the number of those who have been sealed at the Lord’s feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Those who withdrew themselves from the shadow of the world have received glorious garments from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Take again your full number, O Zion, and make up the reckoning of those of yours who are clothed in white, which have fulfilled the law of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> The number of your children, whom you long for, is fulfilled. Ask the power of the Lord, that your people, which have been called from the beginning, may be made holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> I, Esdras, saw upon Mount Zion a great multitude, whom I could not number, and they all praised the Lord with songs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> In the midst of them, there was a young man of a high stature, taller than all the rest, and upon every one of their heads he set crowns, and he was more exalted than they were. I marveled greatly at this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> So I asked the angel, and said, “What are these, my Lord?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> He answered and said to me, “These are those who have put off the mortal clothing, and put on the immortal, and have confessed the name of God. Now they are crowned, and receive palms.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Then said I to the angel, “Who is the young man who sets crowns on them, and gives them palms in their hands?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> So he answered and said to me, “He is the Son of God, whom they have confessed in the world.” Then I began to praise those who stood so valiantly for the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Then the angel said to me, “Go your way, and tell my people what kind of things, and how great wonders of the Lord God you have seen.”</span>
