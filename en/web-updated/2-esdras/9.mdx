<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He answered me, “Measure diligently within yourself. When you see that a certain part of the signs are past, which have been told you beforehand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> then will you understand that it is the very time in which the Most High will visit the world which was made by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> When earthquakes, tumult of peoples, plans of nations, wavering of leaders, and confusion of princes are seen in the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> then will you understand that the Most High spoke of these things from the days that were of old, from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For just as with everything that is made in the world, the beginning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="So the Syriac. The Latin is corrupt.">a</a></sup>is evident and the end manifest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> so also are the times of the Most High: the beginnings are manifest in wonders and mighty works, and the end in effects and signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Everyone who will be saved, and will be able to escape by his works, or by faith by which they have believed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> will be preserved from the said perils, and will see my salvation in my land and within my borders, which I have sanctified for myself from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then those who now have abused my ways will be amazed. Those who have cast them away despitefully will live in torments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For as many as in their life have received benefits, and yet have not known me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and as many as have scorned my law, while they still had liberty and when an opportunity to repent was open to them, didn’t understand, but despised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, me">b</a></sup>it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> must know<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, me">c</a></sup>it in torment after death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Therefore don’t be curious any longer how the ungodly will be punished, but inquire how the righteous will be saved,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="So the Syriac and other versions. The Latin has and whose... created, and when.">d</a></sup>those who the world belongs to, and for whom the world was created.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “I have said before, and now speak, and will say it again hereafter, that there are more of those who perish than of those who will be saved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> like a wave is greater than a drop.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He answered me, “Just as the field is, so also the seed. As the flowers are, so are the colors. As the work is, so also is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="So the Aethiopic and Arabic. The Latin has creation.">e</a></sup>judgment on it. As is the farmer, so also is his threshing floor. For there was a time in the world</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> when I was preparing for those who now live, before the world was made for them to dwell in. Then no one spoke against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="So the Syriac.">f</a></sup>no one existed. But now those who are created in this world that is prepared, both<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="So the Syriac.">g</a></sup>with a table that doesn’t fail and a law which is unsearchable, are corrupted in their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So I considered my world, and behold, it was destroyed, and my earth, and behold, it was in peril, because of the plans that had come into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> I saw and spared them, but not greatly, and saved myself a grape out of a cluster, and a plant out of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="So the Syriac and other versions. The Latin has great tribes.">h</a></sup>a great forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let the multitude perish then, which were born in vain. Let my grape be saved, and my plant, for I have made them perfect with great labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Nevertheless, if you will wait seven more days—however don’t fast in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> but go into a field of flowers, where no house is built, and eat only of the flowers of the field, and you shall taste no flesh, and shall drink no wine, but shall eat flowers only—</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and pray to the Most High continually, then I will come and talk with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So I went my way, just as he commanded me, into the field which is called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The Syriac and Aethiopic have Arphad.">i</a></sup>Ardat. There I sat among the flowers, and ate of the herbs of the field, and this food satisfied me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> It came to pass after seven days that I lay on the grass, and my heart was troubled again, like before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> My mouth was opened, and I began to speak before the Lord Most High, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> “O Lord, you showed yourself among us, to our fathers in the wilderness, when they went out of Egypt, and when they came into the wilderness, where no man treads and that bears no fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> You said, ‘Hear me, O Israel. Heed my words, O seed of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For behold, I sow my law in you, and it will bring forth fruit in you, and you will be glorified in it forever.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But our fathers, who received the law, didn’t keep it, and didn’t observe the statutes. The fruit of the law didn’t perish, for it couldn’t, because it was yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Yet those who received it perished, because they didn’t keep the thing that was sown in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Behold, it is a custom that when the ground has received seed, or the sea a ship, or any vessel food or drink, and when it comes to pass that that which is sown, or that which is launched,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> or the things which have been received, should come to an end, these come to an end, but the receptacles remain. Yet with us, it doesn’t happen that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For we who have received the law will perish by sin, along with our heart which received it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Notwithstanding the law doesn’t perish, but remains in its honor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When I spoke these things in my heart, I looked around me with my eyes, and on my right side I saw a woman, and behold, she mourned and wept with a loud voice, and was much grieved in mind. Her clothes were torn, and she had ashes on her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then let I my thoughts go in which I was occupied, and turned myself to her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and said to her, “Why are you weeping? Why are you grieved in your mind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> She said to me, “Leave me alone, my Lord, that I may weep for myself and add to my sorrow, for I am very troubled in my mind, and brought very low.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> I said to her, “What ails you? Tell me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> She said to me, “I, your servant, was barren and had no child, though I had a husband thirty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Every hour and every day these thirty years I made my prayer to the Most High day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> It came to pass after thirty years that God heard me, your handmaid, and saw my low estate, and considered my trouble, and gave me a son. I rejoiced in him greatly, I and my husband, and all my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lat. townsmen.">j</a></sup>neighbors. We gave great honor to the Mighty One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> I nourished him with great care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> So when he grew up, and I came to take him a wife, I made him a feast day.</span>
