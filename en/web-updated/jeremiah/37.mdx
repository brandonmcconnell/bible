<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24; 2 Kings 24:17; 2 Kings 24:12"> Zedekiah the son of Josiah reigned as king instead of Coniah the son of Jehoiakim, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made king in the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:19–20; Proverbs 29:12; 2 Chronicles 36:12–16"> But neither he, nor his servants, nor the people of the land, listened to the LORD’s words, which he spoke by the prophet Jeremiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:1–2; Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 42:20"> Zedekiah the king sent Jehucal the son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah, the priest, to the prophet Jeremiah, saying, “Pray now to the LORD our God for us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:15; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> Now Jeremiah came in and went out among the people, for they had not put him into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Jeremiah 37:11; Jeremiah 34:21"> Pharaoh’s army had come out of Egypt; and when the Chaldeans who were besieging Jerusalem heard news of them, they withdrew from Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then the LORD’s word came to the prophet Jeremiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 4:17; 2 Kings 22:18; Ezekiel 17:17"> “The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘You shall tell the king of Judah, who sent you to me to inquire of me: “Behold, Pharaoh’s army, which has come out to help you, will return to Egypt into their own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2–8; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 34:21–22"> The Chaldeans will come again, and fight against this city. They will take it and burn it with fire.”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:3; Jeremiah 29:8; Galatians 6:3"> “The LORD says, ‘Don’t deceive yourselves, saying, “The Chaldeans will surely depart from us;” for they will not depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Isaiah 30:17; Joel 2:11"> For though you had struck the whole army of the Chaldeans who fight against you, and only wounded men remained among them, they would each rise up in his tent and burn this city with fire.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5"> When the army of the Chaldeans had withdrawn from Jerusalem for fear of Pharaoh’s army,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Nehemiah 6:11; 1 Kings 19:3"> then Jeremiah went out of Jerusalem to go into the land of Benjamin, to receive his portion there, in the middle of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; Jeremiah 38:7; Luke 23:2"> When he was in Benjamin’s gate, a captain of the guard was there, whose name was Irijah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Hananiah; and he seized Jeremiah the prophet, saying, “You are defecting to the Chaldeans!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–2; Jeremiah 40:4–6; Psalms 27:12"> Then Jeremiah said, “That is false! I am not defecting to the Chaldeans.”<br />But he didn’t listen to him; so Irijah seized Jeremiah, and brought him to the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:26; Jeremiah 20:1–3; Jeremiah 26:16"> The princes were angry with Jeremiah, and struck him, and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the scribe; for they had made that the prison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Jeremiah 38:10–13; Lamentations 3:53"> When Jeremiah had come into the dungeon house and into the cells, and Jeremiah had remained there many days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:7; Jeremiah 24:8; 1 Kings 14:1–4"> then Zedekiah the king sent and had him brought out. The king asked him secretly in his house, “Is there any word from the LORD?”<br />Jeremiah said, “There is.” He also said, “You will be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 25:8; John 10:32; Daniel 6:22"> Moreover Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, “How have I sinned against you, against your servants, or against this people, that you have put me in prison?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Jeremiah 28:10–17; Jeremiah 6:14"> Now where are your prophets who prophesied to you, saying, ‘The king of Babylon will not come against you, nor against this land’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Jeremiah 38:6–9; Acts 28:18–19"> Now please hear, my lord the king: please let my supplication be presented before you, that you not cause me to return to the house of Jonathan the scribe, lest I die there.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:13; Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 38:9"> Then Zedekiah the king commanded, and they committed Jeremiah into the court of the guard. They gave him daily a loaf of bread out of the bakers’ street, until all the bread in the city was gone. Thus Jeremiah remained in the court of the guard.</span>
</div>
