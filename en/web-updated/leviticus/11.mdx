<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> “Speak to the children of Israel, saying, ‘These are the living things which you may eat among all the animals that are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Whatever parts the hoof, and is cloven-footed, and chews the cud among the animals, that you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> “‘Nevertheless these you shall not eat of those that chew the cud, or of those who part the hoof: the camel, because it chews the cud but doesn’t have a parted hoof, is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> The hyrax,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or rock badger, or cony">a</a></sup>because it chews the cud but doesn’t have a parted hoof, is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> The hare, because it chews the cud but doesn’t have a parted hoof, is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> The pig, because it has a split hoof, and is cloven-footed, but doesn’t chew the cud, is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> You shall not eat their meat. You shall not touch their carcasses. They are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> “‘You may eat of all these that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, that you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> All that don’t have fins and scales in the seas and rivers, all that move in the waters, and all the living creatures that are in the waters, they are an abomination to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and you shall detest them. You shall not eat of their meat, and you shall detest their carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Whatever has no fins nor scales in the waters is an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> “‘You shall detest these among the birds; they shall not be eaten because they are an abomination: the eagle, the vulture, the black vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> the red kite, any kind of black kite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> any kind of raven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> the horned owl, the screech owl, the gull, any kind of hawk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the white owl, the desert owl, the osprey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> “‘All flying insects that walk on all fours are an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yet you may eat these: of all winged creeping things that go on all fours, which have long, jointed legs for hopping on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> Even of these you may eat: any kind of locust, any kind of katydid, any kind of cricket, and any kind of grasshopper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But all winged creeping things which have four feet are an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> “‘By these you will become unclean: whoever touches their carcass shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> Whoever carries any part of their carcass shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “‘Every animal which has a split hoof that isn’t completely divided, or doesn’t chew the cud, is unclean to you. Everyone who touches them shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> Whatever goes on its paws, among all animals that go on all fours, they are unclean to you. Whoever touches their carcass shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> He who carries their carcass shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the evening. They are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> “‘These are they which are unclean to you among the creeping things that creep on the earth: the weasel, the rat, any kind of great lizard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> the gecko, and the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the skink, and the chameleon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These are they which are unclean to you among all that creep. Whoever touches them when they are dead shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> Anything they fall on when they are dead shall be unclean; whether it is any vessel of wood, or clothing, or skin, or sack, whatever vessel it is, with which any work is done, it must be put into water, and it shall be unclean until the evening. Then it will be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> Every earthen vessel into which any of them falls and all that is in it shall be unclean. You shall break it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> All food which may be eaten which is soaked in water shall be unclean. All drink that may be drunk in every such vessel shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> Everything whereupon part of their carcass falls shall be unclean; whether oven, or range for pots, it shall be broken in pieces. They are unclean, and shall be unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Nevertheless a spring or a cistern in which water is gathered shall be clean, but that which touches their carcass shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> If part of their carcass falls on any sowing seed which is to be sown, it is clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But if water is put on the seed, and part of their carcass falls on it, it is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> “‘If any animal of which you may eat dies, he who touches its carcass shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> He who eats of its carcass shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the evening. He also who carries its carcass shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> “‘Every creeping thing that creeps on the earth is an abomination. It shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Whatever goes on its belly, and whatever goes on all fours, or whatever has many feet, even all creeping things that creep on the earth, them you shall not eat; for they are an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> You shall not make yourselves abominable with any creeping thing that creeps. You shall not make yourselves unclean with them, that you should be defiled by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am the LORD your God. Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be holy; for I am holy. You shall not defile yourselves with any kind of creeping thing that moves on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am the LORD who brought you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God. You shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> “‘This is the law of the animal, and of the bird, and of every living creature that moves in the waters, and of every creature that creeps on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> to make a distinction between the unclean and the clean, and between the living thing that may be eaten and the living thing that may not be eaten.’”</span>
