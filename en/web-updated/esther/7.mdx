<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 1:12; Proverbs 19:12; Revelation 3:9"> The king arose in his wrath from the banquet of wine and went into the palace garden. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Esther 6:12; Job 9:24"> Then the king returned out of the palace garden into the place of the banquet of wine; and Haman had fallen on the couch where Esther was. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 11:5–6; Esther 1:10"> Then Harbonah, one of the eunuchs who were with the king, said, “Behold, the gallows fifty cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>high, which Haman has made for Mordecai, who spoke good for the king, is standing at Haman’s house.”<br />The king said, “Hang him on it!”</span>
