<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> “Son of man, tell a riddle, and speak a parable to the house of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> and say, ‘The Lord GOD says: “A great eagle with great wings and long feathers, full of feathers which had various colors, came to Lebanon and took the top of the cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> He cropped off the topmost of its young twigs, and carried it to a land of traffic. He planted it in a city of merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> “‘“He also took some of the seed of the land and planted it in fruitful soil. He placed it beside many waters. He set it as a willow tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> It grew and became a spreading vine of low stature, whose branches turned toward him, and its roots were under him. So it became a vine, produced branches, and shot out sprigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> “‘“There was also another great eagle with great wings and many feathers. Behold, this vine bent its roots toward him, and shot out its branches toward him, from the ground where it was planted, that he might water it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> It was planted in a good soil by many waters, that it might produce branches and that it might bear fruit, that it might be a good vine.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> “Say, ‘The Lord GOD says: “Will it prosper? Won’t he pull up its roots and cut off its fruit, that it may wither, that all its fresh springing leaves may wither? It can’t be raised from its roots by a strong arm or many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> Yes, behold, being planted, will it prosper? Won’t it utterly wither when the east wind touches it? It will wither in the ground where it grew.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Moreover the LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> “Say now to the rebellious house, ‘Don’t you know what these things mean?’ Tell them, ‘Behold, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem, and took its king, and its princes, and brought them to him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> He took one of the royal offspring,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, seed">a</a></sup>and made a covenant with him. He also brought him under an oath, and took away the mighty of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> that the kingdom might be brought low, that it might not lift itself up, but that by keeping his covenant it might stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> But he rebelled against him in sending his ambassadors into Egypt, that they might give him horses and many people. Will he prosper? Will he who does such things escape? Will he break the covenant, and still escape?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> “‘As I live,’ says the Lord GOD, ‘surely in the place where the king dwells who made him king, whose oath he despised, and whose covenant he broke, even with him in the middle of Babylon he will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> Pharaoh with his mighty army and great company won’t help him in the war, when they cast up mounds and build forts to cut off many persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> For he has despised the oath by breaking the covenant; and behold, he had given his hand, and yet has done all these things. He won’t escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> “Therefore the Lord GOD says: ‘As I live, I will surely bring on his own head my oath that he has despised and my covenant that he has broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> I will spread my net on him, and he will be taken in my snare. I will bring him to Babylon, and will enter into judgment with him there for his trespass that he has trespassed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> All his fugitives in all his bands will fall by the sword, and those who remain will be scattered toward every wind. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> “The Lord GOD says: ‘I will also take some of the lofty top of the cedar, and will plant it. I will crop off from the topmost of its young twigs a tender one, and I will plant it on a high and lofty mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> I will plant it in the mountain of the height of Israel; and it will produce boughs, and bear fruit, and be a good cedar. Birds of every kind will dwell in the shade of its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> All the trees of the field will know that I, the LORD, have brought down the high tree, have exalted the low tree, have dried up the green tree, and have made the dry tree flourish. “‘I, the LORD, have spoken and have done it.’”</span>
