<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Revelation 4:2–3; Revelation 1:13"> Then I looked, and see, in the expanse that was over the head of the cherubim there appeared above them as it were a sapphire<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, lapis lazuli">a</a></sup>stone, as the appearance of the likeness of a throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:5; Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 1:13"> He spoke to the man clothed in linen, and said, “Go in between the whirling wheels, even under the cherub, and fill both your hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim, and scatter them over the city.”<br />He went in as I watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:4; Ezekiel 9:3; Ezekiel 8:16"> Now the cherubim stood on the right side of the house when the man went in; and the cloud filled the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Ezekiel 9:3; Exodus 40:35"> The LORD’s glory mounted up from the cherub, and stood over the threshold of the house; and the house was filled with the cloud, and the court was full of the brightness of the LORD’s glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Job 40:9; Exodus 19:16"> The sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard even to the outer court, as the voice of God Almighty when he speaks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 80:1"> It came to pass, when he commanded the man clothed in linen, saying, “Take fire from between the whirling wheels, from between the cherubim,” that he went in and stood beside a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 13:41–42; Ezekiel 1:13; Ezekiel 41:23–26"> The cherub stretched out his hand from between the cherubim to the fire that was between the cherubim, and took some of it, and put it into the hands of him who was clothed in linen, who took it and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:8; Ezekiel 10:21; Isaiah 6:6"> The form of a man’s hand appeared here in the cherubim under their wings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:15–17; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 21:20"> I looked, and behold, there were four wheels beside the cherubim, one wheel beside one cherub, and another wheel beside another cherub. The appearance of the wheels was like a beryl stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Psalms 36:6; Psalms 104:24"> As for their appearance, the four of them had one likeness, like a wheel within a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:17; Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 1:20"> When they went, they went in their four directions. They didn’t turn as they went, but to the place where the head looked they followed it. They didn’t turn as they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Revelation 4:8; Revelation 4:6"> Their whole body, including their backs, their hands, their wings, and the wheels, were full of eyes all around, even the wheels that the four of them had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As for the wheels, they were called in my hearing, “the whirling wheels”.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Ezekiel 10:21; 1 Kings 7:36"> Every one of them had four faces. The first face was the face of the cherub. The second face was the face of a man. The third face was the face of a lion. The fourth was the face of an eagle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:5; Hosea 9:12; Ezekiel 11:22"> The cherubim mounted up. This is the living creature that I saw by the river Chebar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:19–21"> When the cherubim went, the wheels went beside them; and when the cherubim lifted up their wings to mount up from the earth, the wheels also didn’t turn from beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:20–21; Romans 8:2; Ezekiel 1:12"> When they stood, these stood. When they mounted up, these mounted up with them; for the spirit of the living creature was in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 18:10; Matthew 23:37–39; Psalms 78:60–61"> The LORD’s glory went out from over the threshold of the house and stood over the cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 1:17–21; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> The cherubim lifted up their wings and mounted up from the earth in my sight when they went out, with the wheels beside them. Then they stood at the door of the east gate of the LORD’s house; and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Ezekiel 10:15; 1 Kings 7:36"> This is the living creature that I saw under the God of Israel by the river Chebar; and I knew that they were cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:6; Ezekiel 41:18–19; Ezekiel 10:14"> Every one had four faces, and every one four wings. The likeness of the hands of a man was under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 1:12"> As for the likeness of their faces, they were the faces which I saw by the river Chebar, their appearances and themselves. They each went straight forward.</span>
</div>
