<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> In the four hundred and eightieth year after the children of Israel had come out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign over Israel, in the month Ziv, which is the second month, he began to build the LORD’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> The house which King Solomon built for the LORD had a length of sixty cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>and its width twenty, and its height thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> The porch in front of the temple of the house had a length of twenty cubits, which was along the width of the house. Ten cubits was its width in front of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> He made windows of fixed lattice work for the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> Against the wall of the house, he built floors all around, against the walls of the house all around, both of the temple and of the inner sanctuary; and he made side rooms all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lowest floor was five cubits wide, and the middle was six cubits wide, and the third was seven cubits wide; for on the outside he made offsets in the wall of the house all around, that the beams should not be inserted into the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> The house, when it was under construction, was built of stone prepared at the quarry; and no hammer or ax or any tool of iron was heard in the house while it was under construction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The door for the middle side rooms was in the right side of the house. They went up by winding stairs into the middle floor, and out of the middle into the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> So he built the house and finished it; and he covered the house with beams and planks of cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He built the floors all along the house, each five cubits high; and they rested on the house with timbers of cedar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The LORD’s word came to Solomon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> “Concerning this house which you are building, if you will walk in my statutes, and execute my ordinances, and keep all my commandments to walk in them, then I will establish my word with you, which I spoke to David your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will not forsake my people Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> So Solomon built the house and finished it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> He built the walls of the house within with boards of cedar; from the floor of the house to the walls of the ceiling, he covered them on the inside with wood. He covered the floor of the house with cypress boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> He built twenty cubits of the back part of the house with boards of cedar from the floor to the ceiling. He built this within, for an inner sanctuary, even for the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> In front of the temple sanctuary was forty cubits long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> There was cedar on the house within, carved with buds and open flowers. All was cedar. No stone was visible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalms 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> He prepared an inner sanctuary in the middle of the house within, to set the ark of the LORD’s covenant there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> Within the inner sanctuary was twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in width, and twenty cubits in its height. He overlaid it with pure gold. He covered the altar with cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> So Solomon overlaid the house within with pure gold. He drew chains of gold across before the inner sanctuary, and he overlaid it with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> He overlaid the whole house with gold, until all the house was finished. He also overlaid the whole altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Cherubim” is plural of “cherub”, an angelic being.">b</a></sup>of olive wood, each ten cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Five cubits was the length of one wing of the cherub, and five cubits was the length of the other wing of the cherub. From the tip of one wing to the tip of the other was ten cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The other cherub was ten cubits. Both the cherubim were of one measure and one form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> One cherub was ten cubits high, and so was the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> He set the cherubim within the inner house. The wings of the cherubim were stretched out, so that the wing of the one touched the one wall and the wing of the other cherub touched the other wall; and their wings touched one another in the middle of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He overlaid the cherubim with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalms 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> He carved all the walls of the house around with carved figures of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, inside and outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> He overlaid the floor of the house with gold, inside and outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> For the entrance of the inner sanctuary, he made doors of olive wood. The lintel and door posts were a fifth part of the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> So he made two doors of olive wood; and he carved on them carvings of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold. He spread the gold on the cherubim and on the palm trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He also made the entrance of the temple door posts of olive wood, out of a fourth part of the wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> and two doors of cypress wood. The two leaves of the one door were folding, and the two leaves of the other door were folding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers; and he overlaid them with gold fitted on the engraved work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> He built the inner court with three courses of cut stone and a course of cedar beams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> The foundation of the LORD’s house was laid in the fourth year, in the month Ziv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> In the eleventh year, in the month Bul, which is the eighth month, the house was finished throughout all its parts and according to all its specifications. So he spent seven years building it.</span>
</div>
