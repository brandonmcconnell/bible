<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 20:34"> They continued three years without war between Syria and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; 1 Kings 22:1; 1 Kings 22:41"> In the third year, Jehoshaphat the king of Judah came down to the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:43; 1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 21:38"> The king of Israel said to his servants, “You know that Ramoth Gilead is ours, and we do nothing, and don’t take it out of the hand of the king of Syria?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 3:7; Ephesians 5:11; 2 John 1:11"> He said to Jehoshaphat, “Will you go with me to battle to Ramoth Gilead?” Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, “I am as you are, my people as your people, my horses as your horses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; 2 Chronicles 18:4–5; Jeremiah 42:2–6"> Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, “Please inquire first for the LORD’s word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 18:19; Jeremiah 23:14–17; Ezekiel 13:7–16"> Then the king of Israel gathered the prophets together, about four hundred men, and said to them, “Should I go against Ramoth Gilead to battle, or should I refrain?” They said, “Go up; for the Lord will deliver it into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:11–13; 2 Chronicles 18:6–7"> But Jehoshaphat said, “Isn’t there here a prophet of the LORD, that we may inquire of him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 5:10; John 3:19–21; John 7:7"> The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “There is yet one man by whom we may inquire of the LORD, Micaiah the son of Imlah; but I hate him, for he does not prophesy good concerning me, but evil.” Jehoshaphat said, “Don’t let the king say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Isaiah 39:7; 1 Kings 22:26–27"> Then the king of Israel called an officer, and said, “Quickly get Micaiah the son of Imlah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 5:1; Esther 6:8–9; 1 Kings 18:29"> Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah were sitting each on his throne, arrayed in their robes, in an open place at the entrance of the gate of Samaria; and all the prophets were prophesying before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:18–21; Deuteronomy 33:17; Ezekiel 22:27–28"> Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah made himself horns of iron, and said, “The LORD says, ‘With these you will push the Syrians, until they are consumed.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; 1 Kings 22:6–15; 1 Kings 22:32–36"> All the prophets prophesied so, saying, “Go up to Ramoth Gilead and prosper; for the LORD will deliver it into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 2:6–7; Psalms 11:1; Isaiah 30:10–11"> The messenger who went to call Micaiah spoke to him, saying, “See now, the prophets declare good to the king with one mouth. Please let your word be like the word of one of them, and speak good.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; 2 Chronicles 18:12–13"> Micaiah said, “As the LORD lives, what the LORD says to me, that I will speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:27; Judges 10:14; Matthew 26:45"> When he had come to the king, the king said to him, “Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth Gilead to battle, or shall we forbear?” He answered him, “Go up and prosper; and the LORD will deliver it into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 19:13; Matthew 26:63; Joshua 6:26"> The king said to him, “How many times do I have to adjure you that you speak to me nothing but the truth in the LORD’s name?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; Matthew 9:36; 1 Kings 22:34–36"> He said, “I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains, as sheep that have no shepherd. The LORD said, ‘These have no master. Let them each return to his house in peace.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:8; Proverbs 10:24; Luke 11:45"> The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “Didn’t I tell you that he would not prophesy good concerning me, but evil?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 1:6; Daniel 7:9–10"> Micaiah said, “Therefore hear the LORD’s word. I saw the LORD sitting on his throne, and all the army of heaven standing by him on his right hand and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16; Jeremiah 4:10"> The LORD said, ‘Who will entice Ahab, that he may go up and fall at Ramoth Gilead?’ One said one thing, and another said another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 2:1; 1 Kings 22:23; Job 1:6–7"> A spirit came out and stood before the LORD, and said, ‘I will entice him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 9:23; 1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 16:14"> The LORD said to him, ‘How?’ He said, ‘I will go out and will be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.’ He said, ‘You will entice him, and will also prevail. Go out and do so.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Matthew 24:24–25; Isaiah 44:20"> Now therefore, behold, the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all these your prophets; and the LORD has spoken evil concerning you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 23:2; Lamentations 3:30; Micah 5:1"> Then Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek, and said, “Which way did the LORD’s Spirit go from me to speak to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30; 2 Peter 2:1; Revelation 19:20"> Micaiah said, “Behold, you will see on that day when you go into an inner room to hide yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The king of Israel said, “Take Micaiah, and carry him back to Amon the governor of the city and to Joash the king’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; 2 Chronicles 18:25–27; Revelation 2:10"> Say, ‘The king says, “Put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction, until I come in peace.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 16:29; Micah 1:2; Acts 13:10–11"> Micaiah said, “If you return at all in peace, the LORD has not spoken by me.” He said, “Listen, all you people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:28; 1 Kings 22:2–9"> So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah went up to Ramoth Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; Jeremiah 23:24; 1 Samuel 28:8"> The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself and go into the battle, but you put on your robes.” The king of Israel disguised himself and went into the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 20:24; 2 Chronicles 18:30; Jeremiah 16:6"> Now the king of Syria had commanded the thirty-two captains of his chariots, saying, “Don’t fight with small nor great, except only with the king of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Jonah 2:1–2; Psalms 130:1–4"> When the captains of the chariots saw Jehoshaphat, they said, “Surely that is the king of Israel!” and they came over to fight against him. Jehoshaphat cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; 1 Kings 22:31"> When the captains of the chariots saw that it was not the king of Israel, they turned back from pursuing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; Micah 6:13; 1 Samuel 17:49"> A certain man drew his bow at random, and struck the king of Israel between the joints of the armor. Therefore he said to the driver of his chariot, “Turn around, and carry me out of the battle, for I am severely wounded.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 22:28; 1 Kings 20:42"> The battle increased that day. The king was propped up in his chariot facing the Syrians, and died at evening. The blood ran out of the wound into the bottom of the chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 14:12; 1 Kings 22:31; Judges 21:24"> A cry went throughout the army about the going down of the sun, saying, “Every man to his city, and every man to his country!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So the king died, and was brought to Samaria; and they buried the king in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Matthew 24:35; Jeremiah 44:21–23"> They washed the chariot by the pool of Samaria; and the dogs licked up his blood where the prostitutes washed themselves, according to the LORD’s word which he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Ezekiel 27:6; Psalms 45:8"> Now the rest of the acts of Ahab, and all that he did, and the ivory house which he built, and all the cities that he built, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12; 2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 1:2"> So Ahab slept with his fathers; and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:31; 1 Kings 22:2; 2 Chronicles 17:1"> Jehoshaphat the son of Asa began to reign over Judah in the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 15:2; 1 Kings 15:10"> Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he began to reign; and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Azubah the daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 15:14; 2 Kings 12:3; Proverbs 4:27"> He walked in all the way of Asa his father. He didn’t turn away from it, doing that which was right in the LORD’s eyes. However, the high places were not taken away. The people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Kings 22:2; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 2 Kings 8:18"> Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 20:34"> Now the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, and his might that he showed, and how he fought, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Kings 15:12; 1 Kings 14:24; Deuteronomy 23:17"> The remnant of the sodomites, that remained in the days of his father Asa, he put away out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:20"> There was no king in Edom. A deputy ruled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; 1 Kings 10:22; 1 Kings 9:28"> Jehoshaphat made ships of Tarshish to go to Ophir for gold, but they didn’t go, for the ships wrecked at Ezion Geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Then Ahaziah the son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, “Let my servants go with your servants in the ships.” But Jehoshaphat would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:1; 1 Kings 22:40; 1 Kings 15:24"> Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in his father David’s city. Jehoram his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 22:40; 1 Kings 15:25"> Ahaziah the son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned two years over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 21:25; Revelation 3:20"> He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, and walked in the way of his father, and in the way of his mother, and in the way of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, in which he made Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 16:30–32; Isaiah 65:3; 2 Kings 3:2"> He served Baal and worshiped him, and provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger in all the ways that his father had done so.</span>
