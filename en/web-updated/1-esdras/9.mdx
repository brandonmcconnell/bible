<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Esdras rose up from the court of the temple and went to the chamber of Jonas the son of Eliasib,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and lodged there, and ate no bread and drank no water, mourning for the great iniquities of the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A proclamation was made in all Judea and Jerusalem to all those who returned from captivity, that they should be gathered together at Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and that whoever didn’t meet there within two or three days, in accordance with the ruling of the elders, that their livestock would be seized for the use of the temple, and they would be expelled from the multitude of those who returned from captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Within three days, all those of the tribe of Judah and Benjamin gathered together at Jerusalem. This was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> All the multitude sat together shivering in the broad place before the temple because of the present foul weather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So Esdras arose up and said to them, “You have transgressed the law and married foreign wives, increasing the sins of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Now make confession and give glory to the Lord, the God of our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and do his will, and separate yourselves from the heathen of the land, and from the foreign women.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the whole multitude cried out, and said with a loud voice, “Just as you have spoken, so we will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But because the multitude is great, and it is foul weather, so that we can’t stand outside, and this is not a work of one day or two, seeing our sin in these things has spread far,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> therefore let the rulers of the multitude stay, and let all those of our settlements that have foreign wives come at the time appointed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and with them the rulers and judges of every place, until we turn away the wrath of the Lord from us for this matter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So Jonathan the son of Azael and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Another reading is, Ezias.">a</a></sup>Ezekias the son of Thocanus took the matter on themselves. Mosollamus and Levis and Sabbateus were judges with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Those who returned from captivity did according to all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Esdras the priest chose for himself principal men of their families, all by name. On the new moon of the tenth month they met together to examine the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So their cases of men who had foreign wives was brought to an end by the new moon of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Of the priests who had come together and had foreign wives, there were found</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> of the sons of Jesus the son of Josedek, and his kindred,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Maaseiah.">b</a></sup>Mathelas, Eleazar, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jarib.">c</a></sup>Joribus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gedaliah">d</a></sup>Joadanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They gave their hands to put away their wives, and to offer rams to make reconciliation for their error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Of the sons of Emmer: Ananias, Zabdeus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Harim">e</a></sup>Manes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Maaseiah.">f</a></sup>Sameus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jehiel.">g</a></sup>Hiereel, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Uzziah.">h</a></sup>Azarias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Pashhur.">i</a></sup>Phaisur: Elionas, Massias, Ishmael, Nathanael,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Jozabad.">j</a></sup>Ocidelus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Elasah.">k</a></sup>Saloas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Of the Levites: Jozabdus, Semeis,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Kelaiah.">l</a></sup>Colius who was called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Kelita.">m</a></sup>Calitas,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Pethahiah.">n</a></sup>Patheus, Judas, and Jonas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of the holy singers:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Eliashib.">o</a></sup>Eliasibus and Bacchurus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Of the gatekeepers: Sallumus and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Telem.">p</a></sup>Tolbanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Of Israel, of the sons of Phoros:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Parosh.">q</a></sup>Hiermas,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Ramiah">r</a></sup>Ieddias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Izziah. Another reading is, Iezias">s</a></sup>Melchias, Maelus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Mijamin.">t</a></sup>Eleazar, Asibas,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Malchijah.">u</a></sup>and Banneas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Of the sons of Ela: Matthanias, Zacharias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Jehiel.">v</a></sup>Jezrielus, Oabdius, Hieremoth, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Abdi.">w</a></sup>Aedias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Zattu.">x</a></sup>Zamoth:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Elioenai.">y</a></sup>Eliadas,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Eliashib.">z</a></sup>Eliasimus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Mattaniah.">aa</a></sup>Othonias, Jarimoth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Zabad.">ab</a></sup>Sabathus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Aziza.">ac</a></sup>Zardeus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Of the sons of Bebai: Joannes, Ananias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Zabbai.">ad</a></sup>Jozabdus, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Athlai.">ae</a></sup>Ematheis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Of the sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Bani.">af</a></sup>Mani:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Meshullam.">ag</a></sup>Olamus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Malluch.">ah</a></sup>Mamuchus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Adaiah.">ai</a></sup>Jedeus, Jasubas,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Sheal.">aj</a></sup>Jasaelus, and Hieremoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Of the sons of Addi: Naathus, Moossias, Laccunus, Naidus, Matthanias, Sesthel, Balnuus, and Manasseas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Of the sons of Annas: Elionas, Aseas, Melchias, Sabbeus, and Simon Chosameus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Of the sons of Asom:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Mattenai.">ak</a></sup>Maltanneus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Mattattah.">al</a></sup>Mattathias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Zabad.">am</a></sup>Sabanneus, Eliphalat, Manasses, and Semei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Of the sons of Baani: Jeremias, Momdis, Ismaerus, Juel, Mamdai, Pedias, Anos, Carabasion, Enasibus, Mamnitamenus, Eliasis, Bannus, Eliali, Someis, Selemias, and Nathanias. Of the sons of Ezora: Sesis, Ezril, Azaelus, Samatus, Zambri, and Josephus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Of the sons of Nooma: Mazitias, Zabadeas, Edos, Juel, and Banaias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> All these had taken foreign wives, and they put them away with their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> The priests and Levites, and those who were of Israel, lived in Jerusalem and in the country, on the new moon of the seventh month, and the children of Israel in their settlements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> The whole multitude gathered together with one accord into the broad place before the porch of the temple toward the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> They said to Esdras the priest and reader, “Bring the law of Moses that was given by the Lord, the God of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So Esdras the chief priest brought the law to the whole multitude both of men and women, and to all the priests, to hear the law on the new moon of the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> He read in the broad place before the porch of the temple from morning until midday, before both men and women; and all the multitude gave attention to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Esdras the priest and reader of the law stood up upon the pulpit of wood which had been prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Beside him stood Mattathias, Sammus, Ananias, Azarias, Urias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hilkiah.">an</a></sup>Ezekias, and Baalsamus on the right hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and on his left hand,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Pedaiah.">ao</a></sup>Phaldeus, Misael, Melchias,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Hashuin.">ap</a></sup>Lothasubus, Nabarias, and Zacharias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Then Esdras took the book of the law before the multitude, and sat honorably in the first place before all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> When he opened the law, they all stood straight up. So Esdras blessed the Lord God Most High, the God of armies, the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> All the people answered, “Amen.” Lifting up their hands, they fell to the ground and worshiped the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Also Jesus, Annus, Sarabias, Iadinus, Jacubus, Sabateus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Hodiah.">aq</a></sup>Auteas, Maiannas, Calitas, Azarias, Jozabdus, Ananias, and Phalias, the Levites, taught the law of the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Some authorities omit and read...Lord.">ar</a></sup>and read to the multitude the law of the Lord, explaining what was read.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Then Attharates said to Esdras the chief priest and reader, and to the Levites who taught the multitude, even to all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> “This day is holy to the Lord—now they all wept when they heard the law—</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> go then, eat the fat, drink the sweet, and send portions to those who have nothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> for the day is holy to the Lord. Don’t be sorrowful, for the Lord will bring you to honor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> So the Levites commanded all things to the people, saying, “This day is holy. Don’t be sorrowful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Then they went their way, every one to eat, drink, enjoy themselves, to give portions to those who had nothing, and to rejoice greatly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> because they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or, were inspired by">as</a></sup>understood the words they were instructed with, and for which they had been assembled.</span>
