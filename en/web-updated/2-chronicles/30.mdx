<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 11:13; Hosea 5:4"> Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters also to Ephraim and Manasseh, that they should come to the LORD’s house at Jerusalem, to keep the Passover to the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 9:10–11; 1 Chronicles 13:1–3; 2 Chronicles 30:13"> For the king had taken counsel with his princes and all the assembly in Jerusalem to keep the Passover in the second month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18"> For they could not keep it at that time, because the priests had not sanctified themselves in sufficient number, and the people had not gathered themselves together to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:4"> The thing was right in the eyes of the king and of all the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Leviticus 23:2; Esther 8:8–10"> So they established a decree to make proclamation throughout all Israel, from Beersheba even to Dan, that they should come to keep the Passover to the LORD, the God of Israel, at Jerusalem, for they had not kept it in great numbers in the way it is written.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Esther 8:14; 2 Kings 15:19"> So the couriers went with the letters from the king and his princes throughout all Israel and Judah, according to the commandment of the king, saying, “You children of Israel, turn again to the LORD, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, that he may return to the remnant of you that have escaped out of the hand of the kings of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Ezekiel 20:13–18; Zechariah 1:3–4"> Don’t be like your fathers and like your brothers, who trespassed against the LORD, the God of their fathers, so that he gave them up to desolation, as you see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 32:9; 2 Chronicles 29:10; Deuteronomy 10:16"> Now don’t be stiff-necked, as your fathers were, but yield yourselves to the LORD, and enter into his sanctuary, which he has sanctified forever, and serve the LORD your God, that his fierce anger may turn away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18; Psalms 86:5"> For if you turn again to the LORD, your brothers and your children will find compassion with those who led them captive, and will come again into this land, because the LORD your God is gracious and merciful, and will not turn away his face from you if you return to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 Chronicles 30:6; Nehemiah 2:19"> So the couriers passed from city to city through the country of Ephraim and Manasseh, even to Zebulun, but people ridiculed them and mocked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:18; 2 Chronicles 30:21; 2 Chronicles 30:25"> Nevertheless some men of Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 24:7; Philippians 2:13"> Also the hand of God came on Judah to give them one heart, to do the commandment of the king and of the princes by the LORD’s word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2; Psalms 84:7"> Many people assembled at Jerusalem to keep the feast of unleavened bread in the second month, a very great assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; 2 Samuel 15:23; 2 Chronicles 15:16"> They arose and took away the altars that were in Jerusalem, and they took away all the altars for incense and threw them into the brook Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 5:11; Exodus 19:22"> Then they killed the Passover on the fourteenth day of the second month. The priests and the Levites were ashamed, and sanctified themselves, and brought burnt offerings into the LORD’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; 2 Chronicles 35:15; 2 Kings 11:14"> They stood in their place after their order, according to the law of Moses the man of God. The priests sprinkled the blood which they received of the hand of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:3–6; Exodus 12:6"> For there were many in the assembly who had not sanctified themselves; therefore the Levites were in charge of killing the Passovers for everyone who was not clean, to sanctify them to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:28; James 5:15–16; Daniel 9:19"> For a multitude of the people, even many of Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun, had not cleansed themselves, yet they ate the Passover other than the way it is written. For Hezekiah had prayed for them, saying, “May the good LORD pardon everyone</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; Proverbs 23:26; Job 11:13"> who sets his heart to seek God, the LORD, the God of his fathers, even if they aren’t clean according to the purification of the sanctuary.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; James 5:15–16; Psalms 103:3"> The LORD listened to Hezekiah, and healed the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 13:6; Deuteronomy 12:12"> The children of Israel who were present at Jerusalem kept the feast of unleavened bread seven days with great gladness. The Levites and the priests praised the LORD day by day, singing with loud instruments to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:6; Nehemiah 9:3; Isaiah 40:1–2"> Hezekiah spoke encouragingly to all the Levites who had good understanding in the service of the LORD. So they ate throughout the feast for the seven days, offering sacrifices of peace offerings and making confession to the LORD, the God of their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 7:9"> The whole assembly took counsel to keep another seven days, and they kept another seven days with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; Ephesians 4:8"> For Hezekiah king of Judah gave to the assembly for offerings one thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep; and the princes gave to the assembly a thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep; and a great number of priests sanctified themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:11; 2 Chronicles 30:18; 1 Chronicles 16:10–11"> All the assembly of Judah, with the priests and the Levites, and all the assembly who came out of Israel, and the foreigners who came out of the land of Israel and who lived in Judah, rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> So there was great joy in Jerusalem; for since the time of Solomon the son of David king of Israel there was nothing like this in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 68:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> Then the Levitical priests arose and blessed the people. Their voice was heard, and their prayer came up to his holy habitation, even to heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>
