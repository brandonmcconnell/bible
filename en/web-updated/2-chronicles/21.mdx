<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 22:50; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in David’s city; and Jehoram his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:6; 2 Chronicles 23:2; 2 Chronicles 24:5"> He had brothers, the sons of Jehoshaphat: Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah. All these were the sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:23; Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 2 Chronicles 11:5"> Their father gave them great gifts of silver, of gold, and of precious things, with fortified cities in Judah; but he gave the kingdom to Jehoram, because he was the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Chronicles 21:17; Genesis 4:8"> Now when Jehoram had risen up over the kingdom of his father, and had strengthened himself, he killed all his brothers with the sword, and also some of the princes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 8:17–22"> Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; 1 Kings 12:28–30; 2 Kings 8:18"> He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as did Ahab’s house, for he had Ahab’s daughter as his wife. He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; Psalms 132:17–18; 2 Samuel 7:12–17"> However the LORD would not destroy David’s house, because of the covenant that he had made with David, and as he promised to give a lamp to him and to his children always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:10; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23; Genesis 27:40"> In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and made a king over themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Jehoram went there with his captains and all his chariots with him. He rose up by night and struck the Edomites who surrounded him, along with the captains of the chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Jeremiah 2:13; 2 Chronicles 13:10"> So Edom has been in revolt from under the hand of Judah to this day. Then Libnah revolted at the same time from under his hand, because he had forsaken the LORD, the God of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:5; 1 Kings 11:7; Leviticus 17:7"> Moreover he made high places in the mountains of Judah, and made the inhabitants of Jerusalem play the prostitute, and led Judah astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 2 Chronicles 17:3–4; 2 Kings 2:1"> A letter came to him from Elijah the prophet, saying, “the LORD, the God of David your father, says, ‘Because you have not walked in the ways of Jehoshaphat your father, nor in the ways of Asa king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:4; 2 Chronicles 21:11; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> but have walked in the way of the kings of Israel, and have made Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to play the prostitute like Ahab’s house did, and also have slain your brothers of your father’s house, who were better than yourself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Exodus 20:5; Hosea 5:11"> behold, the LORD will strike your people with a great plague, including your children, your wives, and all your possessions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 1:18; Deuteronomy 28:67"> and you will have great sickness with a disease of your bowels, until your bowels fall out by reason of the sickness, day by day.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:11; 2 Chronicles 33:11; 1 Kings 11:14"> The LORD stirred up against Jehoram the spirit of the Philistines and of the Arabians who are beside the Ethiopians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; 2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> and they came up against Judah, broke into it, and carried away all the possessions that were found in the king’s house, including his sons and his wives, so that there was no son left to him except Jehoahaz, the youngest of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:15; Acts 12:23; 2 Kings 9:29"> After all this the LORD struck him in his bowels with an incurable disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5"> In process of time, at the end of two years, his bowels fell out by reason of his sickness, and he died of severe diseases. His people made no burning for him, like the burning of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 28:27; Jeremiah 22:28"> He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem eight years. He departed with no one’s regret. They buried him in David’s city, but not in the tombs of the kings.</span>
