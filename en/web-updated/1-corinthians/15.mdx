<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> Now I declare to you, brothers, the Good News which I preached to you, which also you received, in which you also stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> by which also you are saved, if you hold firmly the word which I preached to you—unless you believed in vain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; Psalms 16:10–11"> that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> Then he appeared to over five hundred brothers at once, most of whom remain until now, but some have also fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> Then he appeared to James, then to all the apostles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> and last of all, as to the child born at the wrong time, he appeared to me also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> For I am the least of the apostles, who is not worthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the assembly of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> But by the grace of God I am what I am. His grace which was given to me was not futile, but I worked more than all of them; yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> Whether then it is I or they, so we preach, and so you believed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> Now if Christ is preached, that he has been raised from the dead, how do some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> But if there is no resurrection of the dead, neither has Christ been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> If Christ has not been raised, then our preaching is in vain and your faith also is in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> Yes, we are also found false witnesses of God, because we testified about God that he raised up Christ, whom he didn’t raise up if it is true that the dead are not raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if the dead aren’t raised, neither has Christ been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> If Christ has not been raised, your faith is vain; you are still in your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> Then they also who are fallen asleep in Christ have perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> If we have only hoped in Christ in this life, we are of all men most pitiable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> But now Christ has been raised from the dead. He became the first fruit of those who are asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> For since death came by man, the resurrection of the dead also came by man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> For as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> But each in his own order: Christ the first fruits, then those who are Christ’s at his coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> Then the end comes, when he will deliver up the Kingdom to God the Father, when he will have abolished all rule and all authority and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> For he must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> The last enemy that will be abolished is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> For, “He put all things in subjection under his feet.” But when he says, “All things are put in subjection”, it is evident that he is excepted who subjected all things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> When all things have been subjected to him, then the Son will also himself be subjected to him who subjected all things to him, that God may be all in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> Or else what will they do who are baptized for the dead? If the dead aren’t raised at all, why then are they baptized for the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> Why do we also stand in jeopardy every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> I affirm, by the boasting in you which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord, I die daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> If I fought with animals at Ephesus for human purposes, what does it profit me? If the dead are not raised, then “let’s eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Don’t be deceived! “Evil companionships corrupt good morals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Wake up righteously and don’t sin, for some have no knowledge of God. I say this to your shame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> But someone will say, “How are the dead raised?” and, “With what kind of body do they come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> You foolish one, that which you yourself sow is not made alive unless it dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> That which you sow, you don’t sow the body that will be, but a bare grain, maybe of wheat, or of some other kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> But God gives it a body even as it pleased him, and to each seed a body of its own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> All flesh is not the same flesh, but there is one flesh of men, another flesh of animals, another of fish, and another of birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> There are also celestial bodies and terrestrial bodies; but the glory of the celestial differs from that of the terrestrial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; Psalms 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> There is one glory of the sun, another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for one star differs from another star in glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> So also is the resurrection of the dead. The body is sown perishable; it is raised imperishable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> It is sown in dishonor; it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness; it is raised in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> It is sown a natural body; it is raised a spiritual body. There is a natural body and there is also a spiritual body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> So also it is written, “The first man Adam became a living soul.” The last Adam became a life-giving spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> However, that which is spiritual isn’t first, but that which is natural, then that which is spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> The first man is of the earth, made of dust. The second man is the Lord from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> As is the one made of dust, such are those who are also made of dust; and as is the heavenly, such are they also that are heavenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> As we have borne the image of those made of dust, let’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU, TR read “we will” instead of “let’s”">a</a></sup>also bear the image of the heavenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> Now I say this, brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>that flesh and blood can’t inherit God’s Kingdom; neither does the perishable inherit imperishable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>I tell you a mystery. We will not all sleep, but we will all be changed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we will be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> For this perishable body must become imperishable, and this mortal must put on immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> But when this perishable body will have become imperishable, and this mortal will have put on immortality, then what is written will happen: “Death is swallowed up in victory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Psalms 89:48; Acts 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Death, where is your sting?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Hades,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, Hell">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where is your victory?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> Therefore, my beloved brothers, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the Lord’s work, because you know that your labor is not in vain in the Lord.</span>
</div>
