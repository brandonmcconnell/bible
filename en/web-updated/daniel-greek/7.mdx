<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream and visions of his head on his bed. Then he wrote the dream and told the sum of the matters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Daniel spoke and said, “I saw in my vision by night and behold, the four winds of the sky broke out on the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Four great animals came up from the sea, different from one another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> “The first was like a lion, and had eagle’s wings. I watched until its wings were plucked, and it was lifted up from the earth, and made to stand on two feet as a man. A man’s heart was given to it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> “Behold, there was another animal, a second, like a bear. It was raised up on one side, and three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth. They said this to it: ‘Arise! Devour much flesh!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “After this I saw, and behold, another, like a leopard, which had on its back four wings of a bird. The animal also had four heads; and dominion was given to it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> “After this I saw in the night visions, and, behold, there was a fourth animal, awesome and powerful, and exceedingly strong. It had great iron teeth. It devoured and broke in pieces, and stamped the residue with its feet. It was different from all the animals that were before it. It had ten horns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> “I considered the horns, and behold, another horn came up among them, a little one, before which three of the first horns were plucked up by the roots: and behold, in this horn were eyes like the eyes of a man, and a mouth speaking great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I watched until thrones were placed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and one who was Ancient of Days sat.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His clothing was white as snow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the hair of his head like pure wool.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His throne was fiery flames,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its wheels burning fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">A fiery stream issued and came out from before him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Thousands of thousands ministered to him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The judgment was set.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The books were opened.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “I watched at that time because of the voice of the great words which the horn spoke. I watched even until the animal was slain, its body destroyed, and it was given to be burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As for the rest of the animals, their dominion was taken away; yet their lives were prolonged for a season and a time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “I saw in the night visions, and behold, one like a son of man came with the clouds, and he came to the Ancient of Days, and they brought him near before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Dominion was given him, with glory and a kingdom, that all the peoples, nations, and languages should serve him. His dominion is an everlasting dominion, which will not pass away, and his kingdom will not be destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “As for me, Daniel, my spirit was grieved within my body, and the visions of my head troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I came near to one of those who stood by, and asked him the truth concerning all this.<br />“So he told me, and made me know the interpretation of the things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> ‘These great animals, which are four, are four kings, who will arise out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But the saints of the Most High will receive the kingdom, and possess the kingdom forever, even forever and ever.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> “Then I desired to know the truth concerning the fourth animal, which was different from all of them, exceedingly terrible, whose teeth were of iron, and its nails of bronze; which devoured, broke in pieces, and stamped the residue with its feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and concerning the ten horns that were on its head, and the other horn which came up, and before which three fell, even that horn that had eyes, and a mouth that spoke great things, whose look was more stout than its fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> I saw, and the same horn made war with the saints and prevailed against them</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> until the Ancient of Days came, and judgment was given to the saints of the Most High, and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> “So he said, ‘The fourth animal will be a fourth kingdom on earth, which will be different from all the kingdoms, and will devour the whole earth, and will tread it down, and break it in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> As for the ten horns, ten kings will arise out of this kingdom. Another will arise after them; and he will be different from the former, and he will put down three kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He will speak words against the Most High, and will wear out the saints of the Most High. He will plan to change the times and the law; and they will be given into his hand until a time and times and half a time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “‘But the judgment will be set, and they will take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy it to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The kingdom and the dominion, and the greatness of the kingdoms under the whole sky, will be given to the people of the saints of the Most High. His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions will serve and obey him.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> “Here is the end of the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts troubled me greatly, and my face was changed in me; but I kept the matter in my heart.”</span>
</div>
