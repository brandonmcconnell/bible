<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> “At that time Michael will stand up, the great prince who stands for the children of your people. <span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Those who are wise will shine as the brightness of the expanse. Those who turn many to righteousness will shine like the stars forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But you, Daniel, shut up the words and seal the book, even to the time of the end. Many will run back and forth, and knowledge will be increased.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then I, Daniel, looked, and behold, two others stood, one on the river bank on this side, and the other on the river bank on that side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> One said to the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the river, “How long will it be to the end of these wonders?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> I heard the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the river, when he held up his right hand and his left hand to heaven, and swore by him who lives forever that it will be for a time, times, and a half; and when they have finished breaking in pieces the power of the holy people, all these things will be finished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> I heard, but I didn’t understand. Then I said, “My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He said, “Go your way, Daniel; for the words are shut up and sealed until the time of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Many will purify themselves, make themselves white, and be refined; but the wicked will do wickedly. None of the wicked will understand; but those who are wise will understand.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “From the time that the continual burnt offering is taken away and the abomination that makes desolate set up, there will be one thousand two hundred ninety days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Blessed is he who waits and comes to the one thousand three hundred thirty-five days.</span>
</div>
