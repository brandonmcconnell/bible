<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; 2 Kings 5:14; Acts 27:24"> The king of Israel sent to the place which the man of God told him and warned him of; and he saved himself there, not once or twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 18:7–11; Isaiah 57:20–21; Matthew 2:3–12"> The king of Syria’s heart was very troubled about this. He called his servants, and said to them, “Won’t you show me which of us is for the king of Israel?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:28–30; Amos 3:7; 2 Kings 6:9–10"> One of his servants said, “No, my lord, O king; but Elisha, the prophet who is in Israel, tells the king of Israel the words that you speak in your bedroom.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 37:17; John 11:47–53; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> He said, “Go and see where he is, that I may send and get him.”<br />He was told, “Behold, he is in Dothan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; 1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 23:26"> Therefore he sent horses, chariots, and a great army there. They came by night and surrounded the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; 2 Chronicles 20:12; Psalms 53:5"> When the servant of the man of God had risen early and gone out, behold, an army with horses and chariots was around the city. His servant said to him, “Alas, my master! What shall we do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Romans 8:31; 1 John 4:4"> He answered, “Don’t be afraid, for those who are with us are more than those who are with them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 6:1–7"> Elisha prayed, and said, “LORD, please open his eyes, that he may see.” the LORD opened the young man’s eyes, and he saw; and behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire around Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 13:11; John 12:40; Genesis 19:11"> When they came down to him, Elisha prayed to the LORD, and said, “Please strike this people with blindness.”<br />He struck them with blindness according to Elisha’s word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 24:16; 2 Samuel 16:18–19; Luke 9:23"> Elisha said to them, “This is not the way, neither is this the city. Follow me, and I will bring you to the man whom you seek.” He led them to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Luke 24:31; Luke 16:23"> When they had come into Samaria, Elisha said, “LORD, open these men’s eyes, that they may see.”<br />The LORD opened their eyes, and they saw; and behold, they were in the middle of Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 8:9; 2 Kings 2:12"> The king of Israel said to Elisha, when he saw them, “My father, shall I strike them? Shall I strike them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Genesis 48:22; Hosea 2:18"> He answered, “You shall not strike them. Would you strike those whom you have taken captive with your sword and with your bow? Set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink, then go to their master.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2; 2 Kings 24:2; Luke 6:35"> He prepared a great feast for them. After they ate and drank, he sent them away and they went to their master. So the bands of Syria stopped raiding the land of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 22:31; Ecclesiastes 9:14"> After this, Benhadad king of Syria gathered all his army, and went up and besieged Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 6:28–29; Jeremiah 14:18; Jeremiah 14:13–15"> There was a great famine in Samaria. <span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:4; Isaiah 10:3; Luke 18:3"> As the king of Israel was passing by on the wall, a woman cried to him, saying, “Help, my lord, O king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 118:8–9; Psalms 124:1–3; Psalms 60:11"> He said, “If the LORD doesn’t help you, where could I get help for you? From the threshing floor, or from the wine press?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:5; Judges 18:23; Lamentations 4:10"> Then the king asked her, “What is your problem?”<br />She answered, “This woman said to me, ‘Give your son, that we may eat him today, and we will eat my son tomorrow.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 26:29; Deuteronomy 28:53; Deuteronomy 28:57"> So we boiled my son and ate him; and I said to her on the next day, ‘Give up your son, that we may eat him;’ and she has hidden her son.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 5:7; 2 Kings 19:1"> When the king heard the words of the woman, he tore his clothes. Now he was passing by on the wall, and the people looked, and behold, he had sackcloth underneath on his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 1 Kings 19:2; John 11:50"> Then he said, “God do so to me, and more also, if the head of Elisha the son of Shaphat stays on him today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

Behold, when the messenger comes, shut the door, and hold the door shut against him. Isn’t the sound of his master’s feet behind him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 2:9; Isaiah 8:21; Genesis 4:13"> While he was still talking with them, behold, the messenger came down to him. Then he said, “Behold, this evil is from the LORD. Why should I wait for the LORD any longer?”</span>
