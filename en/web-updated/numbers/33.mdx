<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Isaiah 19:1; Exodus 18:11"> while the Egyptians were burying all their firstborn, whom the LORD had struck among them. The LORD also executed judgments on their gods.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> They traveled from Kehelathah, and encamped in Mount Shepher.</span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They traveled from Haradah, and encamped in Makheloth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They traveled from Mithkah, and encamped in Hashmonah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Aaron was one hundred twenty-three years old when he died in Mount Hor.</span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 21:4"> They traveled from Mount Hor, and encamped in Zalmonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> They traveled from Zalmonah, and encamped in Punon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 21:10"> They traveled from Punon, and encamped in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 21:11"> They traveled from Oboth, and encamped in Iye Abarim, in the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> They traveled from Iyim, and encamped in Dibon Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 6:14; Isaiah 15:2"> They traveled from Dibon Gad, and encamped in Almon Diblathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:49; Deuteronomy 34:1; Numbers 27:12"> They traveled from Almon Diblathaim, and encamped in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They traveled from the mountains of Abarim, and encamped in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Joshua 2:1; Joshua 13:20; Numbers 25:1–9"> They encamped by the Jordan, from Beth Jeshimoth even to Abel Shittim in the plains of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 33:48–49"> The LORD spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1"> Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them, “When you pass over the Jordan into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> then you shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, destroy all their stone idols, destroy all their molten images, and demolish all their high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:31; Psalms 115:16; Deuteronomy 32:8"> You shall take possession of the land, and dwell therein; for I have given the land to you to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Joshua 18:11–48; Joshua 15:1–12"> You shall inherit the land by lot according to your families; to the larger groups you shall give a larger inheritance, and to the smaller you shall give a smaller inheritance. Wherever the lot falls to any man, that shall be his. You shall inherit according to the tribes of your fathers.</span>
</div>
