<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> “Avenge the children of Israel on the Midianites. Afterward you shall be gathered to your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> Moses spoke to the people, saying, “Arm men from among you for war, that they may go against Midian, to execute the LORD’s vengeance on Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> You shall send one thousand out of every tribe, throughout all the tribes of Israel, to the war.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So there were delivered, out of the thousands of Israel, a thousand from every tribe, twelve thousand armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> Moses sent them, one thousand of every tribe, to the war with Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, to the war, with the vessels of the sanctuary and the trumpets for the alarm in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> They fought against Midian, as the LORD commanded Moses. They killed every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> They killed the kings of Midian with the rest of their slain: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the five kings of Midian. They also killed Balaam the son of Beor with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> The children of Israel took the women of Midian captive with their little ones; and all their livestock, all their flocks, and all their goods, they took as plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> All their cities in the places in which they lived, and all their encampments, they burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> They took all the captives, and all the plunder, both of man and of animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They brought the captives with the prey and the plunder, to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the congregation of the children of Israel, to the camp at the plains of Moab, which are by the Jordan at Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> Moses and Eleazar the priest, with all the princes of the congregation, went out to meet them outside of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> Moses was angry with the officers of the army, the captains of thousands and the captains of hundreds, who came from the service of the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> Moses said to them, “Have you saved all the women alive?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Behold, these caused the children of Israel, through the counsel of Balaam, to commit trespass against the LORD in the matter of Peor, and so the plague was among the congregation of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Now therefore kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman who has known man by lying with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> But all the girls, who have not known man by lying with him, keep alive for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> “Encamp outside of the camp for seven days. Whoever has killed any person, and whoever has touched any slain, purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> You shall purify every garment, and all that is made of skin, and all work of goats’ hair, and all things made of wood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> Eleazar the priest said to the men of war who went to the battle, “This is the statute of the law which the LORD has commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> However the gold, and the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything that may withstand the fire, you shall make to go through the fire, and it shall be clean; nevertheless it shall be purified with the water for impurity. All that doesn’t withstand the fire you shall make to go through the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> You shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and you shall be clean. Afterward you shall come into the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “Count the plunder that was taken, both of man and of animal, you, and Eleazar the priest, and the heads of the fathers’ households of the congregation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> and divide the plunder into two parts: between the men skilled in war, who went out to battle, and all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> Levy a tribute to the LORD of the men of war who went out to battle: one soul of five hundred; of the persons, of the cattle, of the donkeys, and of the flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Take it from their half, and give it to Eleazar the priest, for the LORD’s wave offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> Of the children of Israel’s half, you shall take one drawn out of every fifty, of the persons, of the cattle, of the donkeys, and of the flocks, of all the livestock, and give them to the Levites, who perform the duty of the LORD’s tabernacle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Now the plunder, over and above the booty which the men of war took, was six hundred seventy-five thousand sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> seventy-two thousand head of cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> sixty-one thousand donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and thirty-two thousand persons in all, of the women who had not known man by lying with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The half, which was the portion of those who went out to war, was in number three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and the LORD’s tribute of the sheep was six hundred seventy-five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> The cattle were thirty-six thousand, of which the LORD’s tribute was seventy-two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> The donkeys were thirty thousand five hundred, of which the LORD’s tribute was sixty-one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> The persons were sixteen thousand, of whom the LORD’s tribute was thirty-two persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> Moses gave the tribute, which was the LORD’s wave offering, to Eleazar the priest, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Of the children of Israel’s half, which Moses divided off from the men who fought</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> (now the congregation’s half was three hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> thirty-six thousand head of cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> thirty thousand five hundred donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and sixteen thousand persons),</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> even of the children of Israel’s half, Moses took one drawn out of every fifty, both of man and of animal, and gave them to the Levites, who performed the duty of the LORD’s tabernacle, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> The officers who were over the thousands of the army, the captains of thousands, and the captains of hundreds, came near to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> They said to Moses, “Your servants have taken the sum of the men of war who are under our command, and there lacks not one man of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> We have brought the LORD’s offering, what every man found: gold ornaments, armlets, bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and necklaces, to make atonement for our souls before the LORD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> Moses and Eleazar the priest took their gold, even all worked jewels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> All the gold of the wave offering that they offered up to the LORD, of the captains of thousands, and of the captains of hundreds, was sixteen thousand seven hundred fifty shekels.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces, so 16,750 shekels is about 167.5 kilograms or about 368.5 pounds.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> The men of war had taken booty, every man for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold of the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and brought it into the Tent of Meeting for a memorial for the children of Israel before the LORD.</span>
</div>
