<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> Paul, an apostle—not from men, nor through man, but through Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>and God the Father, who raised him from the dead—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>who are with me, to the assemblies of Galatia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> who gave himself for our sins, that he might deliver us out of this present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; Psalms 72:19"> to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I marvel that you are so quickly deserting him who called you in the grace of Christ to a different “good news”,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> but there isn’t another “good news.” Only there are some who trouble you and want to pervert the Good News of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> But even though we, or an angel from heaven, should preach to you any “good news” other than that which we preached to you, let him be cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As we have said before, so I now say again: if any man preaches to you any “good news” other than that which you received, let him be cursed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> For am I now seeking the favor of men, or of God? Or am I striving to please men? For if I were still pleasing men, I wouldn’t be a servant of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> But I make known to you, brothers, concerning the Good News which was preached by me, that it is not according to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> For I didn’t receive it from man, nor was I taught it, but it came to me through revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> For you have heard of my way of living in time past in the Jews’ religion, how that beyond measure I persecuted the assembly of God and ravaged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> I advanced in the Jews’ religion beyond many of my own age among my countrymen, being more exceedingly zealous for the traditions of my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when it was the good pleasure of God, who separated me from my mother’s womb and called me through his grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> to reveal his Son in me, that I might preach him among the Gentiles, I didn’t immediately confer with flesh and blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away into Arabia. Then I returned to Damascus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Peter, and stayed with him fifteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> But of the other apostles I saw no one except James, the Lord’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> Now about the things which I write to you, behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>before God, I’m not lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Then I came to the regions of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> I was still unknown by face to the assemblies of Judea which were in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> but they only heard, “He who once persecuted us now preaches the faith that he once tried to destroy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> So they glorified God in me.</span>
</div>
</div>
