<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 15:14; Luke 11:28; James 2:10"> Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, “Keep all the commandment which I command you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 8:31; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 11:19"> It shall be on the day when you shall pass over the Jordan to the land which the LORD your God gives you, that you shall set yourself up great stones, and coat them with plaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 8:32; Deuteronomy 26:9; 2 Corinthians 3:2–3"> You shall write on them all the words of this law, when you have passed over, that you may go in to the land which the LORD your God gives you, a land flowing with milk and honey, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29–30; Joshua 8:30–33"> It shall be, when you have crossed over the Jordan, that you shall set up these stones, which I command you today, on Mount Ebal, and you shall coat them with plaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:25; Exodus 24:4; Joshua 8:30–31"> There you shall build an altar to the LORD your God, an altar of stones. You shall not use any iron tool on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 1:1–17"> You shall build the LORD your God’s altar of uncut stones. You shall offer burnt offerings on it to the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; Psalms 100:1–2"> You shall sacrifice peace offerings, and shall eat there. You shall rejoice before the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:2; John 16:25; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> You shall write on the stones all the words of this law very plainly.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10–11; Deuteronomy 26:16–18; Ephesians 5:8–9"> Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, “Be silent and listen, Israel! Today you have become the people of the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:1; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Matthew 5:48"> You shall therefore obey the LORD your God’s voice, and do his commandments and his statutes, which I command you today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Moses commanded the people the same day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 9:7; Genesis 30:24; Genesis 35:18"> “These shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people, when you have crossed over the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 30:20"> These shall stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> With a loud voice, the Levites shall say to all the men of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> ‘Cursed is the man who makes an engraved or molten image, an abomination to the LORD, the work of the hands of the craftsman, and sets it up in secret.’<br />All the people shall answer and say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> ‘Cursed is he who dishonors his father or his mother.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> ‘Cursed is he who removes his neighbor’s landmark.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> ‘Cursed is he who leads the blind astray on the road.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> ‘Cursed is he who withholds justice from the foreigner, fatherless, and widow.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> ‘Cursed is he who lies with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e., has sexual relations with">a</a></sup>his father’s wife, because he dishonors his father’s bed.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> ‘Cursed is he who lies with any kind of animal.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> ‘Cursed is he who lies with his sister, his father’s daughter or his mother’s daughter.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> ‘Cursed is he who lies with his mother-in-law.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> ‘Cursed is he who secretly kills his neighbor.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> ‘Cursed is he who takes a bribe to kill an innocent person.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> ‘Cursed is he who doesn’t uphold the words of this law by doing them.’<br />All the people shall say, ‘Amen.’”</span>
</div>
