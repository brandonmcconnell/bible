<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14; Deuteronomy 13:9; Deuteronomy 6:1–10"> It shall be, when you have come in to the land which the LORD your God gives you for an inheritance, possess it, and dwell in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Exodus 23:16; Ezekiel 44:30"> that you shall take some of the first of all the fruit of the ground, which you shall bring in from your land that the LORD your God gives you. You shall put it in a basket, and shall go to the place which the LORD your God shall choose to cause his name to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 105:9–10; Deuteronomy 19:17; Genesis 26:3"> You shall come to the priest who shall be in those days, and tell him, “I profess today to the LORD your God, that I have come to the land which the LORD swore to our fathers to give us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Matthew 5:23–24; Hebrews 13:10–12"> The priest shall take the basket out of your hand, and set it down before the LORD your God’s altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 31:20"> You shall answer and say before the LORD your God, “My father<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, forefather">a</a></sup>was a Syrian ready to perish. He went down into Egypt, and lived there, few in number. There he became a great, mighty, and populous nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 1:11; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 5:23"> The Egyptians mistreated us, afflicted us, and imposed hard labor on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–4; Ephesians 3:20–21; Exodus 3:9"> Then we cried to the LORD, the God of our fathers. The LORD heard our voice, and saw our affliction, our toil, and our oppression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Deuteronomy 5:15; Psalms 78:12–13"> The LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand, with an outstretched arm, with great terror, with signs, and with wonders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Psalms 105:44; Psalms 107:7–8"> and he has brought us into this place, and has given us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Deuteronomy 18:4; Revelation 22:9"> Now, behold, I have brought the first of the fruit of the ground, which you, the LORD, have given me.” You shall set it down before the LORD your God, and worship before the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7; Philippians 4:4"> You shall rejoice in all the good which the LORD your God has given to you, and to your house, you, and the Levite, and the foreigner who is among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30; Numbers 18:24; Philippians 4:18–19"> When you have finished tithing all the tithe of your increase in the third year, which is the year of tithing, then you shall give it to the Levite, to the foreigner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, that they may eat within your gates and be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:176; Psalms 119:153; Psalms 119:141"> You shall say before the LORD your God, “I have put away the holy things out of my house, and also have given them to the Levite, to the foreigner, to the fatherless, and to the widow, according to all your commandment which you have commanded me. I have not transgressed any of your commandments, neither have I forgotten them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20; Leviticus 21:11; Hosea 9:4"> I have not eaten of it in my mourning, neither have I removed any of it while I was unclean, nor given of it for the dead. I have listened to the LORD my God’s voice. I have done according to all that you have commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Zechariah 2:13; Jeremiah 31:23"> Look down from your holy habitation, from heaven, and bless your people Israel, and the ground which you have given us, as you swore to our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:1; John 14:15; 1 John 5:2–3"> Today the LORD your God commands you to do these statutes and ordinances. You shall therefore keep and do them with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Isaiah 12:2; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> You have declared today that the LORD is your God, and that you would walk in his ways, keep his statutes, his commandments, and his ordinances, and listen to his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:9"> The LORD has declared today that you are a people for his own possession, as he has promised you, and that you should keep all his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 28:1"> He will make you high above all nations that he has made, in praise, in name, and in honor, and that you may be a holy people to the LORD your God, as he has spoken.</span>
