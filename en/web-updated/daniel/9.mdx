<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 11:1; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 5:31–1"> In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the offspring of the Medes, who was made king over the realm of the Chaldeans—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> in the first year of his reign I, Daniel, understood by the books the number of the years about which the LORD’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">a</a></sup>word came to Jeremiah the prophet for the accomplishing of the desolations of Jerusalem, even seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:10–13; James 5:16–18"> I set my face to the Lord God, to seek by prayer and petitions, with fasting and sackcloth and ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Exodus 20:6; Deuteronomy 5:10"> I prayed to the LORD my God, and made confession, and said,<br />“Oh, Lord, the great and dreadful God, who keeps covenant and loving kindness with those who love him and keep his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:15"> we have sinned, and have dealt perversely, and have done wickedly, and have rebelled, even turning aside from your precepts and from your ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Luke 20:10–12"> We haven’t listened to your servants the prophets, who spoke in your name to our kings, our princes, and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:15; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> “Lord, righteousness belongs to you, but to us confusion of face, as it is today; to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to all Israel, who are near and who are far off, through all the countries where you have driven them, because of their trespass that they have trespassed against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 9:6–7; Lamentations 1:7–8; Lamentations 1:18"> Lord, to us belongs confusion of face, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, because we have sinned against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–8; Daniel 9:7; Daniel 9:5"> To the Lord our God belong mercies and forgiveness, for we have rebelled against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:6; 2 Kings 18:12; Nehemiah 9:13–17"> We haven’t obeyed the LORD our God’s voice, to walk in his laws, which he set before us by his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5–10; Isaiah 1:4–6; Deuteronomy 28:15–68"> Yes, all Israel have transgressed your law, turning aside, and not obeying your voice.<br />“Therefore the curse and the oath written in the law of Moses the servant of God has been poured out on us, for we have sinned against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:9; Lamentations 1:12; Job 12:17"> He has confirmed his words, which he spoke against us and against our judges who judged us, by bringing on us a great evil; for under the whole sky, such has not been done as has been done to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Isaiah 9:13; Daniel 9:11"> As it is written in the law of Moses, all this evil has come on us. Yet we have not entreated the favor of the LORD our God, that we should turn from our iniquities and have discernment in your truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:7; Nehemiah 9:33; Psalms 51:14"> Therefore the LORD has watched over the evil, and brought it on us; for the LORD our God is righteous in all his works which he does, and we have not obeyed his voice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 32:11; Nehemiah 1:10"> “Now, Lord our God, who has brought your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand, and have gotten yourself renown, as it is today, we have sinned. We have done wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Joel 3:17; Psalms 87:1–3"> Lord, according to all your righteousness, please let your anger and your wrath be turned away from your city Jerusalem, your holy mountain; because for our sins and for the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and your people have become a reproach to all who are around us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Lamentations 5:18; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> “Now therefore, our God, listen to the prayer of your servant and to his petitions, and cause your face to shine on your sanctuary that is desolate, for the Lord’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 37:17; Jeremiah 36:7; 2 Kings 19:16"> My God, turn your ear and hear. Open your eyes and see our desolations, and the city which is called by your name; for we do not present our petitions before you for our righteousness, but for your great mercies’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 9:18; Ephesians 1:12; Amos 7:2"> Lord, hear. Lord, forgive. Lord, listen and do. Don’t defer, for your own sake, my God, because your city and your people are called by your name.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Daniel 9:3–4; Romans 3:23"> While I was speaking, praying, and confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and presenting my supplication before the LORD my God for the holy mountain of my God—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Daniel 8:16; 1 Kings 18:36"> yes, while I was speaking in prayer—the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning, being caused to fly swiftly, touched me about the time of the evening offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:24–27; Daniel 10:21; Daniel 8:16"> He instructed me and talked with me, and said, “Daniel, I have now come to give you wisdom and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 10:11–12"> At the beginning of your petitions the commandment went out, and I have come to tell you, for you are greatly beloved. Therefore consider the matter and understand the vision.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:11–14; Romans 5:10; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> “Seventy weeks are decreed on your people and on your holy city, to finish disobedience, to make an end of sins, to make reconciliation for iniquity, to bring in everlasting righteousness, to seal up vision and prophecy, and to anoint the most holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 4:24; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 55:4"> “Know therefore and discern that from the going out of the commandment to restore and build Jerusalem to the Anointed One,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Anointed One” can also be translated “Messiah” (same as “Christ”).">b</a></sup>the prince, will be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks. It will be built again, with street and moat, even in troubled times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Nahum 1:8; Mark 13:2"> After the sixty-two weeks the Anointed One<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Anointed One” can also be translated “Messiah” (same as “Christ”).">c</a></sup>will be cut off, and will have nothing. The people of the prince who come will destroy the city and the sanctuary. Its end will be with a flood, and war will be even to the end. Desolations are determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:15; Luke 21:20; Daniel 12:11"> He will make a firm covenant with many for one week. In the middle of the week he will cause the sacrifice and the offering to cease. On the wing of abominations will come one who makes desolate; and even to the decreed full end, wrath will be poured out on the desolate.”</span>
</div>
