<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 18:21–22; Daniel 5:31; Esther 1:1"> It pleased Darius to set over the kingdom one hundred twenty local governors, who should be throughout the whole kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 2:48–49; Daniel 5:29; Esther 7:4"> and over them three presidents, of whom Daniel was one, that these local governors might give account to them, and that the king should suffer no loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 5:14; Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:38–41"> Then this Daniel was distinguished above the presidents and the local governors, because an excellent spirit was in him; and the king thought to set him over the whole realm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Philippians 2:15; 1 Peter 2:12"> Then the presidents and the local governors sought to find occasion against Daniel as touching the kingdom; but they could find no occasion or fault, because he was faithful. There wasn’t any error or fault found in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 24:13–16; Esther 3:8; Acts 24:20–21"> Then these men said, “We won’t find any occasion against this Daniel, unless we find it against him concerning the law of his God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Nehemiah 2:3; Daniel 6:21"> Then these presidents and local governors assembled together to the king, and said this to him, “King Darius, live forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; Psalms 62:4; Daniel 3:27"> All the presidents of the kingdom, the deputies and the local governors, the counselors and the governors, have consulted together to establish a royal statute and to make a strong decree, that whoever asks a petition of any god or man for thirty days, except of you, O king, he shall be cast into the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:15; Esther 1:19; Daniel 6:12"> Now, O king, establish the decree and sign the writing, that it not be changed, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which doesn’t alter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 118:9; Psalms 62:9–10"> Therefore King Darius signed the writing and the decree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Psalms 55:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:17–18"> When Daniel knew that the writing was signed, he went into his house (now his windows were open in his room toward Jerusalem) and he kneeled on his knees three times a day, and prayed, and gave thanks before his God, as he did before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:32–33; Psalms 10:9; Daniel 6:6"> Then these men assembled together, and found Daniel making petition and supplication before his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:8; Daniel 3:8–12; Esther 1:19"> Then they came near, and spoke before the king concerning the king’s decree: “Haven’t you signed a decree that every man who makes a petition to any god or man within thirty days, except to you, O king, shall be cast into the den of lions?” The king answered, “This thing is true, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which doesn’t alter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 3:12; Acts 5:29; Daniel 2:25"> Then they answered and said before the king, “That Daniel, who is of the children of the captivity of Judah, doesn’t respect you, O king, nor the decree that you have signed, but makes his petition three times a day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:26; Matthew 27:17–24; 2 Samuel 3:28–29"> Then the king, when he heard these words, was very displeased, and set his heart on Daniel to deliver him; and he labored until the going down of the sun to rescue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 6:12; Psalms 94:20–21; Daniel 6:8"> Then these men assembled together to the king, and said to the king, “Know, O king, that it is a law of the Medes and Persians, that no decree nor statute which the king establishes may be changed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:39–40; Job 5:19; Psalms 91:14–16"> Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel and cast him into the den of lions. The king spoke and said to Daniel, “Your God whom you serve continually, he will deliver you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:53; Acts 12:4; Matthew 27:60–66"> A stone was brought, and laid on the mouth of the den; and the king sealed it with his own signet, and with the signet of his lords; that nothing might be changed concerning Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esther 6:1; Daniel 2:1; 2 Samuel 12:16–17"> Then the king went to his palace, and passed the night fasting. No musical instruments were brought before him; and his sleep fled from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Mark 16:2; Matthew 28:1"> Then the king arose very early in the morning, and went in haste to the den of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:11; Hebrews 7:25; Daniel 6:27"> When he came near to the den to Daniel, he cried with a troubled voice. The king spoke and said to Daniel, “Daniel, servant of the living God, is your God, whom you serve continually, able to deliver you from the lions?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 6:6; Nehemiah 2:3"> Then Daniel said to the king, “O king, live forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Hebrews 11:33; Acts 12:11"> My God has sent his angel, and has shut the lions’ mouths, and they have not hurt me, because innocence was found in me before him; and also before you, O king, I have done no harm.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; Mark 9:23; Daniel 3:27–28"> Then the king was exceedingly glad, and commanded that they should take Daniel up out of the den. So Daniel was taken up out of the den, and no kind of harm was found on him, because he had trusted in his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Esther 7:10; Deuteronomy 24:16"> The king commanded, and they brought those men who had accused Daniel, and they cast them into the den of lions—them, their children, and their wives; and the lions mauled them, and broke all their bones in pieces before they came to the bottom of the den.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Ezra 1:1–2; Esther 3:12"> Then King Darius wrote to all the peoples, nations, and languages who dwell in all the earth: “Peace be multiplied to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 4:34; Luke 1:33; Daniel 3:29"> “I make a decree that in all the dominion of my kingdom men tremble and fear before the God of Daniel.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“For he is the living God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and steadfast forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His kingdom is that which will not be destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His dominion will be even to the end.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 4:2–3; Psalms 18:50; Acts 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">He delivers and rescues.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He works signs and wonders in heaven and in earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who has delivered Daniel from the power of the lions.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 1:21; 2 Chronicles 36:22–2; Isaiah 44:28–1"> So this Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius and in the reign of Cyrus the Persian.</span>
