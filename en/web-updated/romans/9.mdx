<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I tell the truth in Christ. I am not lying, my conscience testifying with me in the Holy Spirit</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> that I have great sorrow and unceasing pain in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> For I could wish that I myself were accursed from Christ for my brothers’ sake, my relatives according to the flesh</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalms 147:19; Romans 9:6"> who are Israelites; whose is the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the giving of the law, the service, and the promises;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:30; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–19"> of whom are the fathers, and from whom is Christ as concerning the flesh, who is over all, God, blessed forever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> But it is not as though the word of God has come to nothing. For they are not all Israel that are of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> Neither, because they are Abraham’s offspring, are they all children. But, “your offspring will be accounted as from Isaac.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:13; Romans 4:11–16; Galatians 3:26–29"> That is, it is not the children of the flesh who are children of God, but the children of the promise are counted as heirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this is a word of promise: “At the appointed time I will come, and Sarah will have a son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> Not only so, but Rebekah also conceived by one, by our father Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> For being not yet born, neither having done anything good or bad, that the purpose of God according to election might stand, not of works, but of him who calls,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU puts the phrase “not of works, but of him who calls” at the beginning of verse 12 instead of the end of verse 11.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> it was said to her, “The elder will serve the younger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> Even as it is written, “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What shall we say then? Is there unrighteousness with God? May it never be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For he said to Moses, “I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> So then it is not of him who wills, nor of him who runs, but of God who has mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the Scripture says to Pharaoh, “For this very purpose I caused you to be raised up, that I might show in you my power, and that my name might be proclaimed in all the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> So then, he has mercy on whom he desires, and he hardens whom he desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> You will say then to me, “Why does he still find fault? For who withstands his will?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> But indeed, O man, who are you to reply against God? Will the thing formed ask him who formed it, “Why did you make me like this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Or hasn’t the potter a right over the clay, from the same lump to make one part a vessel for honor, and another for dishonor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> What if God, willing to show his wrath and to make his power known, endured with much patience vessels of wrath prepared for destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> and that he might make known the riches of his glory on vessels of mercy, which he prepared beforehand for glory—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> us, whom he also called, not from the Jews only, but also from the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As he says also in Hosea,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will call them ‘my people,’ which were not my people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her ‘beloved,’ who was not beloved.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">“It will be that in the place where it was said to them, ‘You are not my people,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there they will be called ‘children of the living God.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> Isaiah cries concerning Israel,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If the number of the children of Israel are as the sand of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is the remnant who will be saved;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">for he will finish the work and cut it short in righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the Lord will make a short work upon the earth.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> As Isaiah has said before,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Unless the Lord of Armies</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greek: Sabaoth (or Hebrew: Tze’va’ot)">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">had left us a seed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we would have become like Sodom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and would have been made like Gomorrah.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What shall we say then? That the Gentiles, who didn’t follow after righteousness, attained to righteousness, even the righteousness which is of faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> but Israel, following after a law of righteousness, didn’t arrive at the law of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Why? Because they didn’t seek it by faith, but as it were by works of the law. They stumbled over the stumbling stone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> even as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I lay in Zion a stumbling stone and a rock of offense;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one who believes in him will be disappointed.”</span></span>
