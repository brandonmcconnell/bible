<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> As he passed by, he saw a man blind from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> His disciples asked him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“This man didn’t sin, nor did his parents, but that the works of God might be revealed in him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> <span className="woj">I must work the works of him who sent me while it is day. The night is coming, when no one can work.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> <span className="woj">While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> When he had said this, he spat on the ground, made mud with the saliva, anointed the blind man’s eyes with the mud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> and said to him,<span className="woj">“Go, wash in the pool of Siloam”</span>(which means “Sent”). So he went away, washed, and came back seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> Therefore the neighbors and those who saw that he was blind before said, “Isn’t this he who sat and begged?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Others were saying, “It is he.” Still others were saying, “He looks like him.” He said, “I am he.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They therefore were asking him, “How were your eyes opened?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered, “A man called Jesus made mud, anointed my eyes, and said to me,<span className="woj">‘Go to the pool of Siloam and wash.’</span>So I went away and washed, and I received sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> Then they asked him, “Where is he?” He said, “I don’t know.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They brought him who had been blind to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> It was a Sabbath when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Again therefore the Pharisees also asked him how he received his sight. He said to them, “He put mud on my eyes, I washed, and I see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Some therefore of the Pharisees said, “This man is not from God, because he doesn’t keep the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a man who is a sinner do such signs?” So there was division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> Therefore they asked the blind man again, “What do you say about him, because he opened your eyes?” He said, “He is a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jews therefore didn’t believe concerning him, that he had been blind and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him who had received his sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> and asked them, “Is this your son, whom you say was born blind? How then does he now see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents answered them, “We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but how he now sees, we don’t know; or who opened his eyes, we don’t know. He is of age. Ask him. He will speak for himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> His parents said these things because they feared the Jews; for the Jews had already agreed that if any man would confess him as Christ, he would be put out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> Therefore his parents said, “He is of age. Ask him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> So they called the man who was blind a second time, and said to him, “Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He therefore answered, “I don’t know if he is a sinner. One thing I do know: that though I was blind, now I see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They said to him again, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, “I told you already, and you didn’t listen. Why do you want to hear it again? You don’t also want to become his disciples, do you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They insulted him and said, “You are his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God has spoken to Moses. But as for this man, we don’t know where he comes from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered them, “How amazing! You don’t know where he comes from, yet he opened my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God doesn’t listen to sinners, but if anyone is a worshiper of God and does his will, he listens to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since the world began it has never been heard of that anyone opened the eyes of someone born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered him, “You were altogether born in sins, and do you teach us?” Then they threw him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, and finding him, he said,<span className="woj">“Do you believe in the Son of God?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered, “Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“You have both seen him, and it is he who speaks with you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> He said, “Lord, I believe!” and he worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> Jesus said,<span className="woj">“I came into this world for judgment, that those who don’t see may see; and that those who see may become blind.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Those of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and said to him, “Are we also blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“If you were blind, you would have no sin; but now you say, ‘We see.’ Therefore your sin remains.</span></span>
