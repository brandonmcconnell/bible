<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> He came to Jesus by night and said to him, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher come from God, for no one can do these signs that you do, unless God is with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, unless one is born anew,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word translated “anew” here and in John 3:7 (ἄνωθεν) also means “again” and “from above”.">a</a></sup><span className="woj">he can’t see God’s Kingdom.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> Nicodemus said to him, “How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother’s womb and be born?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, unless one is born of water and Spirit, he can’t enter into God’s Kingdom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> <span className="woj">That which is born of the flesh is flesh. That which is born of the Spirit is spirit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> <span className="woj">Don’t marvel that I said to you, ‘You must be born anew.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> <span className="woj">The wind</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The same Greek word (πνεῦμα) means wind, breath, and spirit.">b</a></sup><span className="woj">blows where it wants to, and you hear its sound, but don’t know where it comes from and where it is going. So is everyone who is born of the Spirit.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> Nicodemus answered him, “How can these things be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“Are you the teacher of Israel, and don’t understand these things?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> <span className="woj">Most certainly I tell you, we speak that which we know and testify of that which we have seen, and you don’t receive our witness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> <span className="woj">If I told you earthly things and you don’t believe, how will you believe if I tell you heavenly things?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> <span className="woj">No one has ascended into heaven but he who descended out of heaven, the Son of Man, who is in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> <span className="woj">As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of Man be lifted up,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> <span className="woj">that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> <span className="woj">For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενη”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">c</a></sup><span className="woj">Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> <span className="woj">For God didn’t send his Son into the world to judge the world, but that the world should be saved through him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> <span className="woj">He who believes in him is not judged. He who doesn’t believe has been judged already, because he has not believed in the name of the only born Son of God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> <span className="woj">This is the judgment, that the light has come into the world, and men loved the darkness rather than the light, for their works were evil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> <span className="woj">For everyone who does evil hates the light and doesn’t come to the light, lest his works would be exposed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Psalms 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> <span className="woj">But he who does the truth comes to the light, that his works may be revealed, that they have been done in God.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> After these things, Jesus came with his disciples into the land of Judea. He stayed there with them and baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> John also was baptizing in Enon near Salim, because there was much water there. They came and were baptized;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> for John was not yet thrown into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> Therefore a dispute arose on the part of John’s disciples with some Jews about purification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> They came to John and said to him, “Rabbi, he who was with you beyond the Jordan, to whom you have testified, behold, he baptizes, and everyone is coming to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> John answered, “A man can receive nothing unless it has been given him from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> You yourselves testify that I said, ‘I am not the Christ,’ but, ‘I have been sent before him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> He who has the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears him, rejoices greatly because of the bridegroom’s voice. Therefore my joy is made full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Psalms 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> He must increase, but I must decrease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> “He who comes from above is above all. He who is from the earth belongs to the earth and speaks of the earth. He who comes from heaven is above all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> What he has seen and heard, of that he testifies; and no one receives his witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> He who has received his witness has set his seal to this, that God is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> For he whom God has sent speaks the words of God; for God gives the Spirit without measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The Father loves the Son, and has given all things into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> One who believes in the Son has eternal life, but one who disobeys<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The same word can be translated “disobeys” or “disbelieves” in this context.">d</a></sup>the Son won’t see life, but the wrath of God remains on him.”</span>
