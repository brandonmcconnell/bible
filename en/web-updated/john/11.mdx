<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now a certain man was sick, Lazarus from Bethany, of the village of Mary and her sister, Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> It was that Mary who had anointed the Lord with ointment and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> The sisters therefore sent to him, saying, “Lord, behold, he for whom you have great affection is sick.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> But when Jesus heard it, he said,<span className="woj">“This sickness is not to death, but for the glory of God, that God’s Son may be glorified by it.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> When therefore he heard that he was sick, he stayed two days in the place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after this he said to the disciples,<span className="woj">“Let’s go into Judea again.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples asked him, “Rabbi, the Jews were just trying to stone you. Are you going there again?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Aren’t there twelve hours of daylight? If a man walks in the day, he doesn’t stumble, because he sees the light of this world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> <span className="woj">But if a man walks in the night, he stumbles, because the light isn’t in him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> He said these things, and after that, he said to them,<span className="woj">“Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep, but I am going so that I may awake him out of sleep.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The disciples therefore said, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> Now Jesus had spoken of his death, but they thought that he spoke of taking rest in sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> So Jesus said to them plainly then,<span className="woj">“Lazarus is dead.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> <span className="woj">I am glad for your sakes that I was not there, so that you may believe. Nevertheless, let’s go to him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Thomas therefore, who is called Didymus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Didymus” means “Twin”.">a</a></sup>said to his fellow disciples, “Let’s also go, that we may die with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> So when Jesus came, he found that he had been in the tomb four days already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="15 stadia is about 2.8 kilometers or 1.7 miles">b</a></sup>away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> Many of the Jews had joined the women around Martha and Mary, to console them concerning their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Then when Martha heard that Jesus was coming, she went and met him, but Mary stayed in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Therefore Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if you would have been here, my brother wouldn’t have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> Even now I know that whatever you ask of God, God will give you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Your brother will rise again.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha said to him, “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will still live, even if he dies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> <span className="woj">Whoever lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She said to him, “Yes, Lord. I have come to believe that you are the Christ, God’s Son, he who comes into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> When she had said this, she went away and called Mary, her sister, secretly, saying, “The Teacher is here and is calling you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> When she heard this, she arose quickly and went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> Now Jesus had not yet come into the village, but was in the place where Martha met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> Then the Jews who were with her in the house and were consoling her, when they saw Mary, that she rose up quickly and went out, followed her, saying, “She is going to the tomb to weep there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Therefore when Mary came to where Jesus was and saw him, she fell down at his feet, saying to him, “Lord, if you would have been here, my brother wouldn’t have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus therefore saw her weeping, and the Jews weeping who came with her, he groaned in the spirit and was troubled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> and said,<span className="woj">“Where have you laid him?”</span>They told him, “Lord, come and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> The Jews therefore said, “See how much affection he had for him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> Some of them said, “Couldn’t this man, who opened the eyes of him who was blind, have also kept this man from dying?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus therefore, again groaning in himself, came to the tomb. Now it was a cave, and a stone lay against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Take away the stone.”</span>Martha, the sister of him who was dead, said to him, “Lord, by this time there is a stench, for he has been dead four days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Didn’t I tell you that if you believed, you would see God’s glory?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> So they took away the stone from the place where the dead man was lying.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NU omits “from the place where the dead man was lying.”">c</a></sup>Jesus lifted up his eyes and said,<span className="woj">“Father, I thank you that you listened to me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> <span className="woj">I know that you always listen to me, but because of the multitude standing around I said this, that they may believe that you sent me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> When he had said this, he cried with a loud voice,<span className="woj">“Lazarus, come out!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> He who was dead came out, bound hand and foot with wrappings, and his face was wrapped around with a cloth. Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Free him, and let him go.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Therefore many of the Jews who came to Mary and saw what Jesus did believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went away to the Pharisees and told them the things which Jesus had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> The chief priests therefore and the Pharisees gathered a council, and said, “What are we doing? For this man does many signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we leave him alone like this, everyone will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> But a certain one of them, Caiaphas, being high priest that year, said to them, “You know nothing at all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> nor do you consider that it is advantageous for us that one man should die for the people, and that the whole nation not perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> Now he didn’t say this of himself, but being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus would die for the nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not for the nation only, but that he might also gather together into one the children of God who are scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> So from that day forward they took counsel that they might put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Jesus therefore walked no more openly among the Jews, but departed from there into the country near the wilderness, to a city called Ephraim. He stayed there with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> Now the Passover of the Jews was at hand. Many went up from the country to Jerusalem before the Passover, to purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> Then they sought for Jesus and spoke with one another as they stood in the temple, “What do you think—that he isn’t coming to the feast at all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had commanded that if anyone knew where he was, he should report it, that they might seize him.</span>
