<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Genesis 12:6"> Then Jerubbaal, who is Gideon, and all the people who were with him, rose up early and encamped beside the spring of Harod. Midian’s camp was on the north side of them, by the hill of Moreh, in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> The LORD said to Gideon, “The people who are with you are too many for me to give the Midianites into their hand, lest Israel brag against me, saying, ‘My own hand has saved me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Revelation 17:14; Revelation 21:8"> Now therefore proclaim in the ears of the people, saying, ‘Whoever is fearful and trembling, let him return and depart from Mount Gilead.’” So twenty-two thousand of the people returned, and ten thousand remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 33:16; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 14:6"> The LORD said to Gideon, “There are still too many people. Bring them down to the water, and I will test them for you there. It shall be, that those whom I tell you, ‘This shall go with you,’ shall go with you; and whoever I tell you, ‘This shall not go with you,’ shall not go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So he brought down the people to the water; and the LORD said to Gideon, “Everyone who laps of the water with his tongue, like a dog laps, you shall set him by himself; likewise everyone who bows down on his knees to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The number of those who lapped, putting their hand to their mouth, was three hundred men; but all the rest of the people bowed down on their knees to drink water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:6; Isaiah 41:14–16; Judges 7:18–22"> The LORD said to Gideon, “I will save you by the three hundred men who lapped, and deliver the Midianites into your hand. Let all the other people go, each to his own place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:20; Numbers 10:9; Joshua 6:4"> So the people took food in their hand, and their trumpets; and he sent all the rest of the men of Israel to their own tents, but retained the three hundred men; and the camp of Midian was beneath him in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 46:2–3; Judges 3:28; Acts 18:9–10"> That same night, the LORD said to him, “Arise, go down into the camp, for I have delivered it into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Judges 4:8–9"> But if you are afraid to go down, go with Purah your servant down to the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Judges 7:13–15; Isaiah 35:3–4"> You will hear what they say; and afterward your hands will be strengthened to go down into the camp.” Then went he down with Purah his servant to the outermost part of the armed men who were in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:10; Judges 6:5; Joshua 11:4"> The Midianites and the Amalekites and all the children of the east lay along in the valley like locusts for multitude; and their camels were without number, as the sand which is on the seashore for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; Isaiah 41:14–15; Judges 4:9"> When Gideon had come, behold, there was a man telling a dream to his fellow. He said, “Behold, I dreamed a dream; and behold, a cake of barley bread tumbled into the camp of Midian, came to the tent, and struck it so that it fell, and turned it upside down, so that the tent lay flat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6–7; Joshua 2:9; Joshua 2:24"> His fellow answered, “This is nothing other than the sword of Gideon the son of Joash, a man of Israel. God has delivered Midian into his hand, with all the army.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–6; Genesis 40:8; 2 Chronicles 20:18–19"> It was so, when Gideon heard the telling of the dream and its interpretation, that he worshiped. Then he returned into the camp of Israel and said, “Arise, for the LORD has delivered the army of Midian into your hand!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7"> He divided the three hundred men into three companies, and he put into the hands of all of them trumpets and empty pitchers, with torches within the pitchers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; Matthew 16:24; Hebrews 13:7"> He said to them, “Watch me, and do likewise. Behold, when I come to the outermost part of the camp, it shall be that, as I do, so you shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:15–17; 1 Samuel 17:47"> When I blow the trumpet, I and all who are with me, then blow the trumpets also on every side of all the camp, and shout, ‘For the LORD and for Gideon!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Exodus 14:24; Matthew 25:6"> So Gideon and the hundred men who were with him came to the outermost part of the camp in the beginning of the middle watch, when they had but newly set the watch. Then they blew the trumpets and broke in pieces the pitchers that were in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 27:13; 2 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> The three companies blew the trumpets, broke the pitchers, and held the torches in their left hands and the trumpets in their right hands with which to blow; and they shouted, “The sword of the LORD and of Gideon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; Proverbs 28:1; 2 Kings 7:6–7"> They each stood in his place around the camp, and all the army ran; and they shouted, and put them to flight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Joshua 6:20; Joshua 6:4"> They blew the three hundred trumpets, and the LORD set every man’s sword against his fellow and against all the army; and the army fled as far as Beth Shittah toward Zererah, as far as the border of Abel Meholah, by Tabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 6:35; 1 Samuel 14:21–22"> The men of Israel were gathered together out of Naphtali, out of Asher, and out of all Manasseh, and pursued Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 3:27–28; Philippians 1:27; Romans 15:30"> Gideon sent messengers throughout all the hill country of Ephraim, saying, “Come down against Midian and take the waters before them as far as Beth Barah, even the Jordan!” So all the men of Ephraim were gathered together and took the waters as far as Beth Barah, even the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:26; Judges 8:3–4; Psalms 83:11–12"> They took the two princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb. They killed Oreb at Oreb’s rock, and Zeeb they killed at Zeeb’s wine press, as they pursued Midian. Then they brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon beyond the Jordan.</span>
