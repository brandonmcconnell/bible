<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> The men of Ephraim said to him, “Why have you treated us this way, that you didn’t call us when you went to fight with Midian?” They rebuked him sharply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> He said to them, “What have I now done in comparison with you? Isn’t the gleaning of the grapes of Ephraim better than the vintage of Abiezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> God has delivered into your hand the princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb! What was I able to do in comparison with you?” Then their anger was abated toward him when he had said that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> Gideon came to the Jordan and passed over, he and the three hundred men who were with him, faint, yet pursuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Psalms 60:6; 1 Samuel 25:18"> He said to the men of Succoth, “Please give loaves of bread to the people who follow me; for they are faint, and I am pursuing after Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> The princes of Succoth said, “Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand, that we should give bread to your army?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> Gideon said, “Therefore when the LORD has delivered Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, then I will tear your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness and with briers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; 1 Kings 12:25"> He went up there to Penuel, and spoke to them in the same way; and the men of Penuel answered him as the men of Succoth had answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; 1 Kings 22:27–28"> He spoke also to the men of Penuel, saying, “When I come again in peace, I will break down this tower.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor, and their armies with them, about fifteen thousand men, all who were left of all the army of the children of the east; for there fell one hundred twenty thousand men who drew sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> Gideon went up by the way of those who lived in tents on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and struck the army; for the army felt secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> Zebah and Zalmunna fled and he pursued them. He took the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and confused all the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Gideon the son of Joash returned from the battle from the ascent of Heres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; 1 Samuel 30:11–15"> He caught a young man of the men of Succoth, and inquired of him; and he described for him the princes of Succoth, and its elders, seventy-seven men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> He came to the men of Succoth, and said, “See Zebah and Zalmunna, concerning whom you taunted me, saying, ‘Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand, that we should give bread to your men who are weary?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> He took the elders of the city, and thorns of the wilderness and briers, and with them he taught the men of Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; 1 Kings 12:25"> He broke down the tower of Penuel, and killed the men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; Psalms 89:12"> Then he said to Zebah and Zalmunna, “What kind of men were they whom you killed at Tabor?” They answered, “They were like you. They all resembled the children of a king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He said, “They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. As the LORD lives, if you had saved them alive, I would not kill you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 149:9; 1 Samuel 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> He said to Jether his firstborn, “Get up and kill them!” But the youth didn’t draw his sword; for he was afraid, because he was yet a youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Judges 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> Then Zebah and Zalmunna said, “You rise and fall on us; for as the man is, so is his strength.” Gideon arose, and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and took the crescents that were on their camels’ necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; 1 Samuel 12:12"> Then the men of Israel said to Gideon, “Rule over us, both you, your son, and your son’s son also; for you have saved us out of the hand of Midian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 10:19"> Gideon said to them, “I will not rule over you, neither shall my son rule over you. The LORD shall rule over you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> Gideon said to them, “I do have a request: that you would each give me the earrings of his plunder.” (For they had golden earrings, because they were Ishmaelites.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They answered, “We will willingly give them.” They spread a garment, and every man threw the earrings of his plunder into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> The weight of the golden earrings that he requested was one thousand and seven hundred shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.32 Troy ounces, so 1700 shekels is about 17 kilograms or 37.4 pounds.">a</a></sup>of gold, in addition to the crescents, and the pendants, and the purple clothing that was on the kings of Midian, and in addition to the chains that were about their camels’ necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; Psalms 106:39"> Gideon made an ephod out of it, and put it in Ophrah, his city. Then all Israel played the prostitute with it there; and it became a snare to Gideon and to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> So Midian was subdued before the children of Israel, and they lifted up their heads no more. The land had rest forty years in the days of Gideon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; 1 Samuel 12:11"> Jerubbaal the son of Joash went and lived in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; 1 Kings 11:3"> Gideon had seventy sons conceived from his body, for he had many wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> His concubine who was in Shechem also bore him a son, and he named him Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> Gideon the son of Joash died in a good old age, and was buried in the tomb of Joash his father, in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> As soon as Gideon was dead, the children of Israel turned again and played the prostitute following the Baals, and made Baal Berith their god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 78:42; Psalms 106:21; Psalms 78:11"> The children of Israel didn’t remember the LORD their God, who had delivered them out of the hand of all their enemies on every side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> neither did they show kindness to the house of Jerubbaal, that is, Gideon, according to all the goodness which he had shown to Israel.</span>
