<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1; 2 Chronicles 11:16; Nehemiah 11:4–9"> In Jerusalem, there lived of the children of Judah, of the children of Benjamin, and of the children of Ephraim and Manasseh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:5; Genesis 46:12; Numbers 26:20"> Uthai the son of Ammihud, the son of Omri, the son of Imri, the son of Bani, of the children of Perez the son of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Nehemiah 11:5"> Of the Shilonites: Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; 1 Chronicles 2:6; Genesis 38:30"> Of the sons of Zerah: Jeuel and their brothers, six hundred ninety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:20; Nehemiah 11:7; Nehemiah 8:4"> Of the sons of Benjamin: Sallu the son of Meshullam, the son of Hodaviah, the son of Hassenuah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and Ibneiah the son of Jeroham, and Elah the son of Uzzi, the son of Michri; and Meshullam the son of Shephatiah, the son of Reuel, the son of Ibnijah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:8"> and their brothers, according to their generations, nine hundred fifty-six. All these men were heads of fathers’ households by their fathers’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:19; Nehemiah 11:10–24"> Of the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:11; 2 Kings 25:18; Numbers 4:33"> and Azariah the son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok, the son of Meraioth, the son of Ahitub, the ruler of God’s house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:40; Ezra 2:37; Nehemiah 11:12–13"> and Adaiah the son of Jeroham, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah; and Maasai the son of Adiel, the son of Jahzerah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Meshillemith, the son of Immer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:14; 1 Chronicles 26:30; 1 Chronicles 26:32"> and their brothers, heads of their fathers’ houses, one thousand seven hundred sixty; they were very able men for the work of the service of God’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:57; Nehemiah 10:11; 1 Chronicles 6:29"> Of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hasshub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:22; Nehemiah 11:17; 1 Chronicles 11:17"> and Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah the son of Mica, the son of Zichri, the son of Asaph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1; 2 Chronicles 35:15; 1 Chronicles 12:25"> and Obadiah the son of Shemaiah, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah the son of Asa, the son of Elkanah, who lived in the villages of the Netophathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:1–32; 1 Chronicles 23:5; Nehemiah 11:19"> The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their brothers (Shallum was the chief),</span>

Their fathers had been over the LORD’s camp, keepers of the entry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 31:6; Numbers 3:32; Numbers 4:33"> Phinehas the son of Eleazar was ruler over them in time past, and the LORD was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:2"> Zechariah the son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper of the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Chronicles 28:13; 1 Chronicles 23:1–32"> All these who were chosen to be gatekeepers in the thresholds were two hundred twelve. These were listed by genealogy in their villages, whom David and Samuel the seer ordained in their office of trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:14; 2 Chronicles 23:19; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> So they and their children had the oversight of the gates of the LORD’s house, even the house of the tent, as guards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14–18"> On the four sides were the gatekeepers, toward the east, west, north, and south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 11:5; 2 Chronicles 23:8; 2 Kings 11:7"> Their brothers, in their villages, were to come in every seven days from time to time to be with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:5; Nehemiah 10:38–39; 1 Chronicles 26:20–27"> for the four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were in an office of trust, and were over the rooms and over the treasuries in God’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:30–32; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 3:15"> They stayed around God’s house, because that was their duty; and it was their duty to open it morning by morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:4–5; Nehemiah 12:44; 1 Chronicles 26:22–26"> Certain of them were in charge of the vessels of service, for these were brought in by count, and these were taken out by count.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:29; Exodus 30:23–38; Exodus 27:20"> Some of them also were appointed over the furniture, and over all the vessels of the sanctuary, over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense, and the spices.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:31–33; Psalms 134:1–2; 1 Chronicles 16:4–6"> These are the singers, heads of fathers’ households of the Levites, who lived in the rooms and were free from other service, for they were employed in their work day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:1–15; 1 Chronicles 9:13"> These were heads of fathers’ households of the Levites, throughout their generations, chief men. They lived at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:23–24; 1 Chronicles 2:50–52; 1 Chronicles 8:29–40"> Jeiel the father of Gibeon, whose wife’s name was Maacah, lived in Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:39; 1 Chronicles 8:33"> His firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:31"> Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:22; 1 Chronicles 8:33–38; 1 Chronicles 10:2"> Ner became the father of Kish. Kish became the father of Saul. Saul became the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:4; 1 Chronicles 8:34–36"> The son of Jonathan was Merib-baal. Merib-baal became the father of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:35"> The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tahrea, and Ahaz.</span>
