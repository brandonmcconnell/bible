<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:61; Deuteronomy 12:32; Leviticus 16:12"> And Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, took each of them his censer, and put fire in it, and put incense on it, and offered strange fire before the LORD, which he commanded them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:61; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 9:24"> And there went out fire from the LORD, and devoured them, and they died before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:22; Leviticus 21:6; Isaiah 49:3"> Then Moses said to Aaron, This is what the LORD spoke, saying, I will be sanctified in them that come nigh me, and before all the people I will be glorified. And Aaron held his peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:22; Exodus 6:18; Acts 5:6"> And Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron, and said to them, Come near, carry your brethren from before the sanctuary out of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 8:13"> So they went near, and carried them in their coats out of the camp; as Moses had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 22:18; Joshua 7:1; Numbers 16:22"> And Moses said to Aaron, and to Eleazar and to Ithamar, his sons, Uncover not your heads, neither rend your clothes; lest ye die, and lest wrath come upon all the people: but let your brethren, the whole house of Israel, bewail the burning which the LORD hath kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Leviticus 8:30; Acts 10:38"> And ye shall not go out from the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, lest ye die: for the anointing oil of the LORD is upon you. And they did according to the word of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the LORD spoke to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 3:3; Ephesians 5:18"> Do not drink wine nor strong drink, thou, nor thy sons with thee, when ye go into the tabernacle of the congregation, lest ye die: it shall be a statute for ever throughout your generations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Leviticus 11:47"> And that ye may make a difference between holy and unholy, and between unclean and clean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Malachi 2:7; Nehemiah 8:8"> And that ye may teach the children of Israel all the statutes which the LORD hath spoken to them by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 7:9"> And Moses spoke to Aaron, and to Eleazar and to Ithamar, his sons that were left, Take the meat-offering that remaineth of the offerings of the LORD made by fire, and eat it without leaven beside the altar: for it is most holy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 6:16; Numbers 18:10"> And ye shall eat it in the holy place, because it is thy due, and thy sons' due, of the sacrifices of the LORD made by fire: for so I am commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:11; Leviticus 7:29–34; Exodus 29:24–28"> And the wave-breast and heave-shoulder shall ye eat in a clean place; thou, and thy sons, and thy daughters with thee: for they are thy due, and thy sons' due, which are given out of the sacrifices of peace-offerings of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 7:34; Genesis 17:13; Leviticus 7:29–30"> The heave-shoulder and the wave-breast shall they bring, with the offerings made by fire of the fat, to wave it for a wave-offering before the LORD; and it shall be thine, and thy sons' with thee, by a statute for ever; as the LORD hath commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:3; Leviticus 9:15; Leviticus 6:26"> And Moses diligently sought the goat of the sin-offering, and behold, it was burnt: and he was angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron who were left alive , saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6–7; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 6:24–30"> Why have ye not eaten the sin-offering in the holy place, seeing it is most holy, and God hath given it you to bear the iniquity of the congregation, to make atonement for them before the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:30; Leviticus 6:26"> Behold, the blood of it was not brought within the holy place : ye should indeed have eaten it in the holy place , as I commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12; Leviticus 9:8; Jeremiah 14:12"> And Aaron said to Moses, Behold, this day have they offered their sin-offering, and their burnt-offering before the LORD; and such things have befallen me: and if I had eaten the sin-offering to day, would it have been accepted in the sight of the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 7:8–9; Matthew 12:20; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> And when Moses heard that , he was content.</span>
