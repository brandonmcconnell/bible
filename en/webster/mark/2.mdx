<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:47; Luke 5:18; Luke 18:35–38"> And again he entered into Capernaum, after some days; and it was understood that he was in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Mark 1:45; Psalms 40:9"> And forthwith many were assembled, so that there was no room to receive them , no not so much as about the door: and he preached the word to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:18–26; Matthew 9:1–8; Matthew 4:24"> And they come to him, bringing one sick with the palsy, who was borne by four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 5:19; Deuteronomy 22:8"> And when they could not come nigh to him by reason of the crowd, they uncovered the roof where he was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed on which the sick with the palsy lay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; James 5:15; Mark 2:9–10"> When Jesus saw their faith, he said to the sick with the palsy, Son, thy sins are forgiven thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:21–22; Mark 8:17; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> But there were certain of the scribes sitting there, and reasoning in their hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Luke 5:21; Micah 7:18"> Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Acts 8:22; Luke 5:22"> And immediately, when Jesus perceived in his spirit that they so reasoned within themselves, he said to them, Why reason ye these things in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:22–25; Matthew 9:5"> Which is easier, to say to the sick with the palsy, Thy sins are forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and take up thy bed, and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Matthew 9:6–8; John 5:20–27"> But that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins, he saith to the sick with the palsy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 1:41; John 5:8–10; John 6:63"> I say to thee, Arise, and take up thy bed, and go into thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 9:33; Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:16"> And immediately he arose, took up the bed, and went forth before them all; so that they were all amazed, and glorified God, saying, We never saw it on this fashion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 4:1; Proverbs 1:20–22; Luke 19:48"> And he went forth again by the sea -side ; and all the multitude resorted to him, and he taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:27–32; Luke 6:15; Matthew 9:9–13"> And as he passed by, he saw Levi the son of Alpheus, sitting at the receipt of custom, and said to him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:1; Luke 5:29–30; Matthew 9:10–11"> And it came to pass, that as Jesus sat at table in his house, many publicans and sinners sat also together with Jesus and his disciples; for there were many, and they followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 19:7; Luke 18:11"> And when the scribes and Pharisees saw him eating with publicans and sinners, they said to his disciples, How is it that he eateth and drinketh with publicans and sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:12–13; Luke 15:7; Luke 5:31–32"> When Jesus heard it , he saith to them, They that are in health, have no need of a physician, but they that are sick; I came not to call the righteous, but sinners, to repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:12; Matthew 6:18; Matthew 9:14–17"> And the disciples of John, and of the Pharisees, used to fast: and they come, and say to him, Why do the disciples of John, and of the Pharisees fast, but thy disciples fast not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 25:1–10; Genesis 29:22; Judges 14:10–11"> And Jesus said to them, Can the children of the bride-chamber fast, while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them, they cannot fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Revelation 21:9; Isaiah 54:5"> But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then they will fast in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:16; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Isaiah 57:16"> No man also seweth a piece of new cloth on an old garment: else the new piece that filled it up, taketh away from the old, and the rent is made worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:17; Psalms 119:83; Luke 5:37–38"> And no man putteth new wine into old bottles, else the new wine doth burst the bottles, and the wine is spilled, and the bottles will be marred: but new wine must be put into new bottles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–8; Deuteronomy 23:24–25; Luke 6:1–5"> And it came to pass, that he went through the corn-fields on the sabbath; and his disciples began, as they went, to pluck the ears of corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:10; Hebrews 12:3; Matthew 12:2"> And the Pharisees said to him, Behold, why do they on the sabbath that which is not lawful?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 10:26; Matthew 22:31"> And he said to them, Have ye never read what David did, when he had need, and was hungry, he and they that were with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; Leviticus 24:5–9; 1 Samuel 21:1"> How he went into the house of God, in the days of Abiathar the high priest, and ate the show-bread, which it is not lawful to eat, but for the priests, and gave also to them who were with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 2:16; Luke 6:9; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And he said to them, The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 12:8; Revelation 1:10; John 5:17"> Therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath.</span>
