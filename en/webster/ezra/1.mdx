<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; 2 Chronicles 36:22–23; Jeremiah 25:12–14"> Now in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, that the word of the LORD by the mouth of Jeremiah might be fulfilled, the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, that he made a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and put it also in writing, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12–13; Isaiah 44:26–1; Daniel 5:23"> Thus saith Cyrus king of Persia, The LORD God of heaven hath given me all the kingdoms of the earth; and he hath charged me to build him a house at Jerusalem, which is in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:26; Jeremiah 10:10; Isaiah 45:5"> Who is there among you of all his people? his God be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and build the house of the LORD God of Israel, (he is the God,) who is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:3; Ezra 7:16–18; Ezra 2:68–70"> And whoever remaineth in any place where he sojourneth, let the men of his place help him with silver, and with gold, and with goods, and with beasts, besides the free-will-offering for the house of God that is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 36:22; Philippians 2:13"> Then arose the chief of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests, and the Levites, with all them whose spirit God had raised, to go up to build the house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Ezra 1:4; Ezra 8:33"> And all they that were about them strengthened their hands with vessels of silver, with gold, with goods, and with beasts, and with precious things, besides all that was willingly offered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; Ezra 5:14; 2 Kings 24:13"> Also Cyrus the king brought forth the vessels of the house of the LORD, which Nebuchadnezzar had brought from Jerusalem, and had put them in the house of his gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 5:14; Zechariah 4:6–10; Ezra 1:11"> Even those did Cyrus king of Persia bring forth by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and numbered them to Sheshbazzar, the prince of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 7:50; Numbers 7:19–89; 2 Chronicles 4:8"> And this is the number of them: thirty chargers of gold, a thousand chargers of silver, nine and twenty knives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Thirty basins of gold, silver basins of a second sort four hundred and ten, and other vessels a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19–21; Romans 9:23; Matthew 1:11–12"> All the vessels of gold and of silver were five thousand and four hundred. All these did Sheshbazzar bring with them of the captivity that were brought from Babylon to Jerusalem.</span>
