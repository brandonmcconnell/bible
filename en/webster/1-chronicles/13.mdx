<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:20; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 2 Chronicles 29:20"> And David consulted with the captains of thousands, and of hundreds, and with every leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 4:4–20; 1 Samuel 31:1; 1 Chronicles 10:7"> And David said to all the congregation of Israel, If it seemeth good to you, and that it is of the LORD our God, let us send abroad to our brethren every where, that are left in all the land of Israel, and with them also to the priests and Levites who are in their cities and suburbs, that they may assemble to us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:1–2; 1 Samuel 23:2; 1 Samuel 14:18"> And let us bring again to us the ark of our God: for we inquired not at it in the days of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 8:5; 2 Samuel 3:36; 1 Samuel 18:20"> And all the congregation said that they would do so: for the thing was right in the eyes of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 1 Kings 8:65; 2 Samuel 6:1"> So David assembled all Israel, from Shihor of Egypt even to the entrance of Hemath, to bring the ark of God from Kirjath-jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Joshua 15:9; 2 Kings 19:15"> And David went up, and all Israel, to Baalah, that is , to Kirjath-jearim, which belonged to Judah, to bring up thence the ark of God the LORD, that dwelleth between the cherubim, whose name is called on it .</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 1 Samuel 6:7; 1 Chronicles 15:2"> And they carried the ark of God in a new cart out of the house of Abinadab: and Uzza and Ahio drove the cart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–5; Psalms 68:25–27; Amos 6:5"> And David and all Israel played before God with all their might, and with singing, and with harps, and with psalteries, and with timbrels, and with cymbals, and with trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6"> And when they came to the threshing-floor of Chidon, Uzza put forth his hand to hold the ark; for the oxen stumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Numbers 4:15"> And the anger of the LORD was kindled against Uzza, and he smote him, because he put his hand to the ark: and there he died before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:7; 2 Samuel 6:9; Genesis 32:32"> And David was displeased, because the LORD had made a breach upon Uzza: wherefore that place is called Perez-uzza to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Kings 8:27; Job 25:5–6"> And David was afraid of God that day, saying, How shall I bring the ark of God home to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 2 Samuel 4:3"> So David brought not the ark to himself to the city of David, but carried it aside into the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–5; Proverbs 10:22; Proverbs 3:9–10"> And the ark of God remained with the family of Obed-edom in his house three months. And the LORD blessed the house of Obed-edom, and all that he had.</span>
