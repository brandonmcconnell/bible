<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; Ephesians 6:10; Matthew 1:8"> And Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his stead, and strengthened himself against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; 2 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Chronicles 11:11–12"> And he placed forces in all the fortified cities of Judah, and set garrisons in the land of Judah, and in the cities of Ephraim, which Asa his father had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> And the LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the first ways of his father David, and sought not to Baalim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Luke 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> But sought to the LORD God of his father, and walked in his commandments, and not after the doings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalms 127:1"> Therefore the LORD established the kingdom in his hand: and all Judah brought to Jehoshaphat presents; and he had riches and honor in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 2 Chronicles 20:33"> And his heart was lifted up in the ways of the LORD: moreover he took away the high places and groves out of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; Ecclesiastes 12:9–10"> Also in the third year of his reign he sent to his princes, even to Ben-hail, and to Obadiah, and to Zechariah, and to Nethaneel, and to Michaiah, to teach in the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 Chronicles 19:8; Malachi 2:7"> And with them he sent Levites, even Shemaiah, and Nethaniah, and Zebadiah, and Asahel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehonathan, and Adonijah, and Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah, Levites; and with them Elishama and Jehoram, priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> And they taught in Judah, and had the book of the law of the LORD with them, and went about throughout all the cities of Judah, and taught the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> And the fear of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were around Judah, so that they made no war against Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:8; 2 Chronicles 9:14; 2 Samuel 8:2"> Also some of the Philistines brought Jehoshaphat presents, and tribute silver; and the Arabians brought him flocks, seven thousand and seven hundred rams, and seven thousand and seven hundred he-goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And Jehoshaphat became exceedingly great; and he built in Judah castles, and cities of store.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 26:10–15"> And he had much business in the cities of Judah: and the men of war, mighty men of valor, were in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; Genesis 15:5; Numbers 1:18"> And these are the numbers of them according to the house of their fathers: of Judah, the captains of thousands; Adnah the chief, and with him mighty men of valor three hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And next to him was Jehohanan the captain, and with him two hundred and eighty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:9; Judges 5:2; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> And next to him was Amasiah the son of Zichri, who willingly offered himself to the LORD; and with him two hundred thousand mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Samuel 1:21–22"> And of Benjamin; Eliada a mighty man of valor, and with him armed men with bow and shield two hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And next to him was Jehozabad, and with him a hundred and eighty thousand ready prepared for the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:2; 2 Chronicles 11:12; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> These waited on the king, besides those whom the king put in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.</span>
