<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:13–37; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Chronicles 20:1–2"> After these things, and their establishment, Sennacherib king of Assyria came, and entered into Judah, and encamped against the fortified cities, and thought to win them for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Luke 9:51; 2 Kings 12:17"> And when Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come, and that he purposed to fight against Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> He took counsel with his princes and his mighty men to stop the waters of the fountains which were without the city: and they helped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 2 Kings 18:9; 1 Kings 19:21"> So there were assembled many people, who stopped all the fountains, and the brook that ran through the midst of the land, saying, Why should the kings of Assyria come, and find much water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:23; 1 Kings 9:24; 2 Kings 25:4"> Also he strengthened himself, and built up all the wall that was broken, and raised it up to the towers, and another wall without, and repaired Millo in the city of David, and made darts and shields in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22; Nehemiah 8:16"> And he set captains of war over the people, and assembled them to him in the street of the gate of the city, and encouraged them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 6:10"> Be strong and courageous, be not afraid nor dismayed for the king of Assyria, nor for all the multitude that is with him: for there are more with us than with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 20:17; Isaiah 41:10"> With him is an arm of flesh; but with us is the LORD our God to help us, and to fight our battles. And the people rested themselves upon the words of Hezekiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Joshua 10:31; Isaiah 36:2"> After this Sennacherib king of Assyria sent his servants to Jerusalem, (but he himself laid siege against Lachish, and all his power with him,) to Hezekiah king of Judah, and to all Judah that were at Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:19; Isaiah 36:4"> Thus saith Sennacherib king of Assyria, On what do ye trust, that ye abide in the siege in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:43; Psalms 11:1–3; Isaiah 36:18"> Doth not Hezekiah persuade you to give over yourselves to die by famine and by thirst, saying, The LORD our God will deliver us out of the hand of the king of Assyria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Deuteronomy 12:26–27"> Hath not the same Hezekiah taken away his high places and his altars, and commanded Judah and Jerusalem, saying, Ye shall worship before one altar, and burn incense upon it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:33–35; Isaiah 44:8–10; Daniel 4:30"> Know ye not what I and my fathers have done to all the people of other lands? were the gods of the nations of those lands any ways able to deliver their lands out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:11–12; Exodus 15:9–11; Isaiah 42:8"> Who was there among all the gods of those nations that my fathers utterly destroyed, that could deliver his people out of my hand, that your God should be able to deliver you out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Daniel 3:15; Isaiah 36:18"> Now therefore let not Hezekiah deceive you, nor persuade you in this manner, neither yet believe him: for no god of any nation or kingdom hath been able to deliver his people out of my hand, and out of the hand of my fathers: how much less shall your God deliver you out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 73:9; John 15:21; Job 15:25–26"> And his servants spoke yet more against the LORD God, and against his servant Hezekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; 2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:28–29"> He wrote also letters to rail at the LORD God of Israel, and to speak against him, saying, As the gods of the nations of other lands have not delivered their people out of my hand, so shall not the God of Hezekiah deliver his people out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; 2 Kings 18:26–28; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Then they cried with a loud voice in the Jews' speech to the people of Jerusalem that were on the wall, to affright them, and to fill them with consternation; that they might take the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 2:8; Jeremiah 32:30"> And they spoke against the God of Jerusalem, as against the gods of the people of the earth, the work of the hands of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–15; Isaiah 37:14–20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And for this cause Hezekiah the king, and the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz, prayed and cried to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Psalms 76:5; Psalms 18:50"> And the LORD sent an angel, who cut off all the mighty men of valor, and the leaders and captains in the camp of the king of Assyria. So he returned with shame of face to his own land. And when he had come into the house of his god, they that came forth from his own bowels slew him there with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4–5; Psalms 18:48–50; 2 Thessalonians 3:5"> Thus the LORD saved Hezekiah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib the king of Assyria, and from the hand of all others , and guided them on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; Matthew 2:11; 2 Chronicles 1:1"> And many brought gifts to the LORD to Jerusalem, and presents to Hezekiah king of Judah: so that from thenceforth he was magnified in the sight of all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Kings 20:1–11; Isaiah 38:21–22"> In those days Hezekiah was sick and near to death, and he prayed to the LORD: and he spoke to him, and he gave him a sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:18; 2 Chronicles 26:16; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> But Hezekiah rendered not again according to the benefit done to him; for his heart was lifted up: therefore there was wrath upon him, and upon Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18–19; 2 Chronicles 34:27–28; 2 Chronicles 33:12"> Notwithstanding, Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, both he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of the LORD came not upon them in the days of Hezekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 1:12; Proverbs 10:22"> And Hezekiah had very great riches and honor: and he made himself treasuries for silver, and for gold, and for precious stones, and for spices, and for shields, and for all manner of pleasant jewels:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Kings 4:26"> Store-houses also for the increase of corn, and wine, and oil; and stalls for all manner of beasts, and cotes for flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:12; Proverbs 10:22; 1 Samuel 2:7"> Moreover, he provided for himself cities, and possessions of flocks and herds in abundance: for God had given him substance in great abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:33; Isaiah 22:9–11; Psalms 1:1–3"> This same Hezekiah also stopped the upper watercourse of Gihon, and brought it straight down to the west side of the city of David. And Hezekiah prospered in all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Proverbs 17:3; Genesis 22:1"> But, in the business of the embassadors of the princes of Babylon, who sent to him to inquire of the wonder that was done in the land, God left him, to try him, that he might know all that was in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 36:1–22; 2 Kings 18:1–20; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21"> Now the rest of the acts of Hezekiah, and his goodness, behold, they are written in the vision of Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, and in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 1:21; 2 Chronicles 16:14"> And Hezekiah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the chief of the sepulchers of the sons of David: and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem did him honor at his death: and Manasseh his son reigned in his stead.</span>
