<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Acts 4:35; Acts 2:47"> And in those days, when the number of the disciples was multiplied, there arose a murmuring of the Grecians against the Hebrews, because their widows were neglected in the daily ministration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:9–14; Exodus 18:17–26; 2 Timothy 2:4"> Then the twelve called the multitude of the disciples, and said, It is not reason that we should leave the word of God, and serve tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7–15; James 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Wherefore, brethren, look ye out among you seven men of honest report, full of the Holy Spirit and wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:14; 1 Timothy 4:13–16; Philippians 1:9–11"> But we will give ourselves continually to prayer, and to the ministry of the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 11:24; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:5–40"> And the saying pleased the whole multitude: and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolas a proselyte of Antioch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; Numbers 8:10; Acts 1:24"> Whom they set before the apostles: and when they had prayed, they laid their hands on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Acts 12:24; Colossians 1:6"> And the word of God increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem greatly; and a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> And Stephen, full of faith and power, performed great wonders and miracles among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> Then there arose certain of the synagogue, which is called the synagogue of the Libertines, and Cyrenians, and Alexandrians, and of them of Cilicia, and of Asia, disputing with Stephen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And they were not able to resist the wisdom and the spirit by which he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Then they suborned men, who said, We have heard him speak blasphemous words against Moses, and against God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> And they stirred up the people, and the elders, and the scribes, and came upon him , and caught him, and brought him to the council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> And set up false witnesses, who said, This man ceaseth not to speak blasphemous words against this holy place, and the law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; Hebrews 12:26–28; Acts 21:21"> For we have heard him say, that this Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place, and will change the customs which Moses delivered to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Matthew 17:2"> And all that sat in the council, looking steadfastly on him, saw his face as it had been the face of an angel.</span>
