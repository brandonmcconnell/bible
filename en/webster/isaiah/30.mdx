<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2; Isaiah 1:2; Hosea 7:13"> Woe to the rebellious children, saith the LORD, that take counsel, but not from me; and that cover with a covering, but not of my spirit, that they may add sin to sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Numbers 27:21; Deuteronomy 28:68"> That walk to go down into Egypt, and have not asked at my mouth; to strengthen themselves in the strength of Pharaoh, and to trust in the shade of Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 20:5; Romans 10:11; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> Therefore shall the strength of Pharaoh be your shame, and the trust in the shade of Egypt your confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Isaiah 57:9; Jeremiah 43:7"> For his princes were at Zoan, and his embassadors came to Hanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:36; Isaiah 30:7; Isaiah 30:16"> They were all ashamed of a people that could not profit them, nor be a help nor profit, but a shame, and also a reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 11:4"> The burden of the beasts of the south: into the land of trouble and anguish, from whence come the young and the old lion, the viper and flying serpent, they will carry their riches upon the shoulders of young asses, and their treasures upon the bunches of camels, to a people that shall not profit them .</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:22; Isaiah 7:4; Psalms 118:8–9"> For the Egyptian shall help in vain, and to no purpose: therefore have I cried concerning this, Their strength is to sit still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:22; Habakkuk 2:2; Isaiah 8:1"> Now go, write it before them in a table, and note it in a book, that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Acts 7:51; Zechariah 1:4–6"> That this is a rebellious people, lying children, children that will not hear the law of the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–10; Romans 16:18; Amos 2:12"> Who say to the seers, See not; and to the prophets, Prophesy not to us right things, speak to us smooth things, prophesy deceits:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Romans 1:30; John 15:23–24"> Withdraw from the way, turn aside from the path, cause the Holy One of Israel to cease from before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Isaiah 5:24; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Wherefore thus saith the Holy One of Israel, Because ye despise this word, and trust in oppression and perverseness, and lean upon it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 29:5; Ezekiel 13:10–15; 1 Kings 20:30"> Therefore this iniquity shall be to you as a breach ready to fall, swelling out in a high wall, whose breaking cometh suddenly at an instant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:10–11; 2 Peter 2:4–5"> And he shall break it as the breaking of the potter's vessel that is broken in pieces; he shall not spare: so that there shall not be found in the bursting of it a piece to take fire from the hearth, or to take water out of the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Isaiah 32:17; Jeremiah 3:22–23"> For thus saith the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel; In returning and rest shall ye be saved; in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength: and ye would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1; Isaiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 28:25"> But ye said, No; for we will flee upon horses; therefore shall ye flee: and, We will ride upon the swift; therefore shall they that pursue you be swift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 32:30"> One thousand shall flee at the rebuke of one; at the rebuke of five shall ye flee: till ye be left as a beacon upon the top of a mountain, and as an ensign on a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Peter 3:9; Proverbs 16:20"> And therefore will the LORD wait, that he may be gracious to you, and therefore will he be exalted, that he may have mercy upon you: for the LORD is a God of judgment: blessed are all they that wait for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:20; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> For the people shall dwell in Zion at Jerusalem: thou shalt weep no more: he will be very gracious to thee at the voice of thy cry; when he shall hear it, he will answer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; Psalms 80:5; Amos 8:11–12"> And though the LORD shall give you the bread of adversity, and the water of affliction, yet shall not thy teachers be removed into a corner any more, but thy eyes shall see thy teachers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 58:11"> And thy ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it, when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 17:3–4; Isaiah 31:7; Zechariah 13:2"> Ye shall defile also the covering of thy graven images of silver, and the ornament of thy molten images of gold: thou shalt cast them away as a polluted cloth; thou shalt say to it, Be gone from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:11–12; Isaiah 32:20; Psalms 65:9–13"> Then shall he give the rain of thy seed, with which thou shalt sow the ground; and bread of the increase of the earth, and it shall be fat and plenteous: in that day shall thy cattle feed in large pastures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 45:6; Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Samuel 8:12"> The oxen likewise and the young asses that plow the ground shall eat clean provender, which hath been winnowed with the shovel and with the fan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19–20; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 2:14–15"> And there shall be upon every high mountain, and upon every high hill, rivers and streams of waters in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 22:5"> Moreover the light of the moon shall be as the light of the sun, and the light of the sun shall be seven-fold, as the light of seven days, in the day that the LORD bindeth up the breach of his people, and healeth the stroke of their wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Nahum 1:5–6; Psalms 18:7–9"> Behold, the name of the LORD cometh from far, burning with his anger, and the burden of it is heavy: his lips are full of indignation, and his tongue as a devouring fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 11:4; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And his breath, as an overflowing stream, shall reach to the midst of the neck, to sift the nations with the sieve of vanity: and there shall be a bridle in the jaws of the people, causing them to err.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 95:1–2; Exodus 15:1–21"> Ye shall have a song, as in the night, when a holy solemnity is kept; and gladness of heart, as when one goeth with a pipe to come upon the mountain of the LORD, to the mighty One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:13–14; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 29:6"> And the LORD shall cause his glorious voice to be heard, and shall show the lighting down of his arm, with the indignation of his anger, and with the flame of a devouring fire, with scattering, and tempest, and hailstones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 9:4; Micah 5:5–6"> For through the voice of the LORD shall the Assyrian be beaten down, who smote with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 11:15; Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And in every place where the grounded staff shall pass, which the LORD shall lay upon him, it shall be with tabrets and harps: and in battles of shaking will he fight with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Genesis 19:24; Jeremiah 19:6"> For Tophet is ordained of old; yes, for the king it is prepared; he hath made it deep and large: the pile of it is fire and much wood; the breath of the LORD, like a stream of brimstone, doth kindle it.</span>
