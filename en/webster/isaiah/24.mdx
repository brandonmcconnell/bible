<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Behold, the LORD maketh the earth empty, and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest; as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the taker of interest, so with the giver of interest to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The land shall be utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled: for the LORD hath spoken this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> The earth mourneth and fadeth away, the world languisheth and fadeth away, the haughty people of the earth do languish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> The earth also is defiled under its inhabitants; because they have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> Therefore hath the curse devoured the earth, and they that dwell in it are desolate: therefore the inhabitants of the earth are burned, and few men left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine mourneth, the vine languisheth, all the merry-hearted do sigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> The mirth of tabrets ceaseth, the noise of them that rejoice endeth, the joy of the harp ceaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> They shall not drink wine with a song; strong drink shall be bitter to them that drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> The city of confusion is broken down: every house is shut up, that no man may enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> There is a crying for wine in the streets; all joy is darkened, the mirth of the land is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> In the city is left desolation, and the gate is smitten with destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> When thus it shall be in the midst of the land among the people, there shall be as the shaking of an olive-tree, and as the gleaning grapes when the vintage is done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> They shall lift up their voice, they shall sing for the majesty of the LORD, they shall cry aloud from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> Wherefore glorify ye the LORD in the fires, even the name of the LORD God of Israel in the isles of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> From the uttermost part of the earth have we heard songs, even glory to the righteous. But I said, My leanness, my leanness, woe to me! the treacherous dealers have dealt treacherously; yes, the treacherous dealers have dealt very treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Fear, and the pit, and the snare, are upon thee, O inhabitant of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> And it shall come to pass, that he who fleeth from the noise of the fear shall fall into the pit; and he that cometh up out of the midst of the pit shall be taken in the snare: for the windows from on high are open, and the foundations of the earth do shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth is utterly broken down, the earth is entirely dissolved, the earth is exceedingly moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth shall reel to and fro like a drunkard, and shall be removed like a cottage; and the transgression of it shall be heavy upon it; and it shall fall, and not rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD shall punish the host of the high ones that are on high, and the kings of the earth upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> And they shall be gathered, as prisoners are gathered in the pit, and shall be shut up in the prison, and after many days shall they be visited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> Then the moon shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed, when the LORD of hosts shall reign on mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, and before his ancients gloriously.</span>
