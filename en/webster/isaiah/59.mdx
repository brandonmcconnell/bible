<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Isaiah 50:2; Numbers 11:23"> Behold, the LORD'S hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:25; Proverbs 15:29; Micah 3:4"> But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 2:34"> For your hands are defiled with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken lies, your tongue hath uttered perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Micah 7:2–5"> None calleth for justice, nor any pleadeth for truth: they trust in vanity, and speak lies; they conceive mischief, and bring forth iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Isaiah 14:29; Proverbs 23:32"> They hatch cockatrice eggs, and weave the spider's web: he that eateth of their eggs dieth, and that which is crushed breaketh out into a viper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 57:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Jeremiah 6:7"> Their webs shall not become garments, neither shall they cover themselves with their works: their works are works of iniquity, and the act of violence is in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:15–17; Proverbs 6:17; Mark 7:21–22"> Their feet run to evil, and they make haste to shed innocent blood: their thoughts are thoughts of iniquity; wasting and destruction are in their paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Romans 3:17; Luke 1:79"> The way of peace they know not; and there is no judgment in their goings: they have made them crooked paths: whoever goeth therein shall not know peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; Job 30:26; Lamentations 5:16–17"> Therefore is judgment far from us, neither doth justice overtake us: we wait for light, but behold obscurity; for brightness, but we walk in darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:29; Job 5:14; Lamentations 3:6"> We grope for the wall like the blind, and we grope as if we had no eyes: we stumble at noon day as in the night; we are in desolate places as dead men .</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Ezekiel 7:16; Jeremiah 9:1"> We all roar like bears, and mourn bitterly like doves: we look for judgment, but there is none; for salvation, but it is far from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Hosea 5:5; Jeremiah 14:7"> For our transgressions are multiplied before thee, and our sins testify against us: for our transgressions are with us; and as for our iniquities, we know them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; James 3:6; Hebrews 3:12"> In transgressing and lying against the LORD, and departing away from our God, speaking oppression and revolt, conceiving and uttering from the heart words of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:4; Ecclesiastes 3:16; Jeremiah 5:31"> And judgment is turned away backward, and justice standeth afar off: for truth is fallen in the street, and equity cannot enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 7:2; Jeremiah 7:28; 1 John 3:11–12"> Yes, truth faileth; and he that departeth from evil maketh himself a prey: and the LORD saw it , and it displeased him that there was no judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 64:7"> And he saw that there was no man, and wondered that there was no intercessor: therefore his arm brought salvation to him; and his righteousness, it sustained him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:17; Ephesians 6:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> For he put on righteousness as a breast-plate, and a helmet of salvation upon his head; and he put on the garments of vengeance for clothing, and was clad with zeal as a cloke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Isaiah 63:6; Romans 2:6"> According to their deeds, accordingly he will repay, fury to his adversaries, recompense to his enemies; to the isles he will repay recompense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Psalms 113:3; Zechariah 4:6"> So shall they fear the name of the LORD from the west, and his glory from the rising of the sun. When the enemy shall come in like a flood, the Spirit of the LORD will lift up a standard against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:26–27; Titus 2:11–14; Acts 3:19"> And the Redeemer will come to Zion, and to them that turn from transgression in Jacob, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> As for me, this is my covenant with them, saith the LORD; My spirit that is upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart out of thy mouth, nor out of the mouth of thy seed, nor out of the mouth of thy seed's seed, saith the LORD, from henceforth and for ever.</span>
