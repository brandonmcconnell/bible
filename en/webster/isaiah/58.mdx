<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:15; Hosea 8:1; Acts 20:26–27"> Cry aloud, spare not, lift up thy voice like a trumpet, and show my people their transgression, and the house of Jacob their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:7–9"> Yet they seek me daily, and delight to know my ways, as a nation that did righteousness, and forsook not the ordinance of their God: they ask of me the ordinances of justice; they take delight in approaching to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Malachi 3:14; Leviticus 16:29"> Why have we fasted, say they , and thou seest not? why have we afflicted our soul, and thou takest no knowledge? Behold, in the day of your fast ye find pleasure, and exact all your labors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 21:9–13; Isaiah 59:2; Proverbs 21:27"> Behold, ye fast for strife and debate, and to smite with the fist of wickedness: ye shall not fast as ye do this day, to make your voice to be heard on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–19; Zechariah 7:5; Isaiah 49:8"> Is it such a fast that I have chosen? a day for a man to afflict his soul? is it to bow down his head as a bulrush, and to spread sackcloth and ashes under him ? wilt thou call this a fast, and an acceptable day to the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:10–12; Isaiah 58:9; Jeremiah 34:8–11"> Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–45; Luke 3:11; 1 John 3:17–18"> Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou shouldst bring the poor that are cast out to thy house? when thou seest the naked, that thou shouldst cover him; and that thou shouldst not hide thyself from thy own flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:12; Jeremiah 33:6; Psalms 37:6"> Then shall thy light break forth as the morning, and thy health shall spring forth speedily: and thy righteousness shall go before thee; the glory of the LORD shall be thy rear-ward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> Then shalt thou call, and the LORD will answer; thou shalt cry, and he will say, Here I am . If thou shalt take away from the midst of thee the yoke, the putting forth of the finger, and speaking vanity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Isaiah 58:7–8; Isaiah 42:16"> And if thou shalt draw out thy soul to the hungry, and satisfy the afflicted soul; then shall thy light rise in obscurity, and thy darkness be as the noon day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 4:14; Jeremiah 31:12; Psalms 32:8"> And the LORD will guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drouth, and make fat thy bones: and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Ezekiel 36:33; Amos 9:14"> And they that shall be of thee shall build the old waste places: thou shalt raise up the foundations of many generations; and thou shalt be called, The repairer of the breach, The restorer of paths to dwell in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–27; Psalms 92:1–2; Nehemiah 13:15–22"> If thou shalt turn away thy foot from the sabbath, from doing thy pleasure on my holy day; and call the sabbath a delight, the holy of the LORD, honorable; and shalt honor him, not doing thy own ways, nor finding thy own pleasure, nor speaking thy own words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Job 22:26; Isaiah 33:16"> Then shalt thou delight thyself in the LORD; and I will cause thee to ride upon the high places of the earth, and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father: for the mouth of the LORD hath spoken it .</span>
