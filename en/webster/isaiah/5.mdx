<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:1; Luke 20:9; John 15:1"> Now will I sing to my well-beloved a song of my beloved concerning his vineyard. My well-beloved hath a vineyard in a very fruitful hill:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Mark 11:13"> And he fenced it, and gathered out the stones of it, and planted it with the choicest vine, and built a tower in the midst of it, and also made a wine-press therein: and he expected that it would bring forth grapes, and it brought forth wild grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Psalms 51:4; Micah 6:2–3"> And now, O inhabitants of Jerusalem, and men of Judah, judge, I pray you, betwixt me and my vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:37; Micah 6:3–4; Jeremiah 2:5"> What more could have been done to my vineyard, that I have not done in it? Why, when I expected that it would bring forth grapes, brought it forth wild grapes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Luke 21:24; Isaiah 28:18"> And now come; I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard: I will take away its hedge, and it shall be eaten up; and break down the wall of it, and it shall be trodden down:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23–25; Jeremiah 25:11; Isaiah 24:1–3"> And I will lay it waste: it shall not be pruned, nor digged; but there shall come up briers and thorns: I will also command the clouds that they rain no rain upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Jeremiah 12:10; James 5:4"> For the vineyard of the LORD of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah his pleasant plant: and he looked for judgment, but behold oppression; for righteousness, but behold a cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 2:2; Habakkuk 2:9–12; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> Woe to them that join house to house, that lay field to field, till there is no place, that they may be placed alone in the midst of the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 23:38; Isaiah 22:14; Amos 5:11"> In my ears said the LORD of hosts, Of a truth many houses shall be desolate, even great and fair, without inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Haggai 2:16; Haggai 1:6"> Yes, ten acres of vineyard shall yield one bath, and the seed of a homer shall yield an ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–30; Romans 13:13; Proverbs 20:1"> Woe to them that rise early in the morning, that they may follow strong drink; that continue until night, till wine inflameth them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:11–14; Psalms 28:5; Amos 6:4–6"> And the harp, and the viol, the tabret, and pipe, and wine, are in their feasts: but they regard not the work of the LORD, neither consider the operation of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 27:11"> Therefore my people have gone into captivity, because they have no knowledge: and their honorable men are famished, and their multitude dried up with thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 30:16; Luke 21:34; Habakkuk 2:5"> Therefore hell hath enlarged herself, and opened her mouth without measure: and their glory, and their multitude, and their pomp, and he that rejoiceth, shall descend into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 2:9; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> And the mean man shall be brought down, and the mighty man shall be humbled, and the eyes of the lofty shall be abased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; Isaiah 12:4; 1 Chronicles 29:11"> But the LORD of hosts shall be exalted in judgment, and God that is holy shall be sanctified in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:6; Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 7:25"> Then shall the lambs feed after their manner, and the waste places of the fat ones shall strangers eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Ezekiel 13:22; Jeremiah 5:31"> Woe to them that draw iniquity with cords of vanity, and sin as it were with a cart-rope:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:22; Jeremiah 17:15; Jeremiah 23:36"> That say, Let him make speed, and hasten his work, that we may see it : and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel draw nigh and come, that we may know it !</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Timothy 3:1–5; Luke 16:15"> Woe to them that call evil good, and good evil; that put darkness for light, and light for darkness; that put bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:18–20; Proverbs 3:7; Romans 12:16"> Woe to them that are wise in their own eyes, and prudent in their own sight!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11; Habakkuk 2:15; Isaiah 28:7"> Woe to them that are mighty to drink wine, and men of strength to mingle strong drink:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; James 5:6; Psalms 94:21"> Who justify the wicked for reward, and take away the righteousness of the righteous from him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 30:12; Job 18:16; Joel 2:5"> Therefore as the fire devoureth the stubble, and the flame consumeth the chaff, so their root shall be as rottenness, and their blossom shall go up as dust: because they have cast away the law of the LORD of hosts, and despised the word of the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:4; Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 9:17"> Therefore is the anger of the LORD kindled against his people, and he hath stretched forth his hand against them, and hath smitten them: and the hills trembled, and their carcasses were torn in the midst of the streets. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Zechariah 10:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 7:18"> And he will lift up an ensign to the nations from afar, and will hiss to them from the end of the earth: and behold, they shall come with speed swiftly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:7–8; Job 12:18; Job 12:21"> None shall be weary nor stumble among them; none shall slumber nor sleep; neither shall the girdle of their loins be loosed, nor the latchet of their shoes be broken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Nahum 2:3–4; Ezekiel 21:9–11"> Whose arrows are sharp, and all their bows bent, their horses' hoofs shall be counted like flint, and their wheels like a whirlwind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 42:22; Zechariah 11:3; Micah 5:8"> Their roaring shall be like a lion, they shall roar like young lions: yes, they shall roar, and lay hold of the prey, and shall carry it away safe, and none shall deliver it .</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 8:22; Joel 2:10; Jeremiah 4:23–28"> And in that day they shall roar against them like the roaring of the sea: and if one looketh to the land, behold darkness and sorrow, and the light is darkened in its heavens.</span>
