<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Chronicles 32:24; 2 Kings 20:1–11"> In those days was Hezekiah sick with a mortal disease. And Isaiah the prophet the son of Amoz came to him, and said to him, Thus saith the LORD, Set thy house in order: for thou shalt die, and not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Psalms 91:15; 1 Kings 8:30"> Then Hezekiah turned his face towards the wall, and prayed to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 18:20–27; Hebrews 6:10"> And said, Remember now, O LORD, I beseech thee, how I have walked before thee in truth and with a perfect heart, and have done that which is good in thy sight. And Hezekiah wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then came the word of the LORD to Isaiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:5–6; 1 John 5:14–15; Revelation 7:17"> Go and say to Hezekiah, Thus saith the LORD, the God of David thy father, I have heard thy prayer, I have seen thy tears: behold, I will add to thy days fifteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; Isaiah 31:4–5; Isaiah 12:6"> And I will deliver thee and this city from the hand of the king of Assyria: and I will defend this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:37–39; Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 7:11–14"> And this shall be a sign to thee from the LORD, that the LORD will do this thing that he hath spoken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 20:9–11; Joshua 10:12–14; Matthew 16:1"> Behold, I will bring again the shadow of the degrees, which hath gone down on the sun-dial of Ahaz ten degrees backward. So the sun returned ten degrees, by which degrees it had gone down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:1; Exodus 15:1–21; Job 5:18"> The writing of Hezekiah king of Judah, when he had been sick, and had recovered from his sickness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:24; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 107:18"> I said in the cutting off of my days, I shall go to the gates of the grave: I am deprived of the residue of my years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Psalms 116:8–9; Psalms 31:22"> I said, I shall not see the LORD, even the LORD, in the land of the living: I shall behold man no more with the inhabitants of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 73:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 1:12"> My age hath departed, and is removed from me as a shepherd's tent: I have cut off like a weaver my life: he will cut me off with pining sickness: from day even to night wilt thou make an end of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 51:8; Daniel 6:24; Psalms 50:22"> I reckoned till morning, that , as a lion, so will he break all my bones: from day even to night wilt thou make an end of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 123:1–4"> Like a crane or a swallow, so I chattered: I mourned as a dove: my eyes fail with looking upward: O LORD, I am oppressed; undertake for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; Job 7:11; Job 10:1"> What shall I say? he hath both spoken to me, and himself hath done it : I shall go softly all my years in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Psalms 119:25"> O LORD, by these things men live, and in all these things is the life of my spirit: so wilt thou recover me, and make me to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:18–19; Jonah 2:6"> Behold, for peace I had great bitterness: but thou hast in love to my soul delivered it from the pit of corruption: for thou hast cast all my sins behind thy back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 30:9; Psalms 6:5"> For the grave cannot praise thee, death can not celebrate thee: they that go down into the pit cannot hope for thy truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Psalms 119:175; Psalms 78:3–6"> The living, the living, he shall praise thee, as I do this day: the father to the children shall make known thy truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 150:4; Psalms 145:2"> The LORD was ready to save me: therefore we will sing my songs to the stringed instruments all the days of our life in the house of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 20:7–8; Mark 7:33; John 9:6"> For Isaiah had said, Let them take a lump of figs, and lay it for a plaster upon the boil, and he will recover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 118:18–19; 2 Kings 20:8; John 5:14"> Hezekiah also had said, What is the sign that I shall go up to the house of the LORD?</span>
