<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:111; Luke 10:42; Isaiah 27:5"> For thus saith the LORD to the eunuchs that keep my sabbaths, and choose the things that please me, and take hold of my covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:12; 1 John 3:1; Revelation 3:5"> Even to them will I give in my house and within my walls a place and a name better than of sons and of daughters: I will give them an everlasting name, that shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 56:2–4; Isaiah 58:13"> Also the sons of the stranger, that join themselves to the LORD, to serve him, and to love the name of the LORD, to be his servants, every one that keepeth the sabbath from profaning it, and taketh hold of my covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 11:17; Isaiah 2:2–3; Malachi 1:11"> Even them will I bring to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer: their burnt-offerings and their sacrifices shall be accepted upon my altar; for my house shall be called a house of prayer for all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 54:7; Isaiah 60:3–11"> The Lord GOD who gathereth the outcasts of Israel, Yet will I gather others to him, besides those that are gathered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 18:6"> All ye beasts of the field, come to devour, yes, all ye beasts in the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 58:1"> His watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant, they are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark; sleeping, lying down, loving to slumber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–3; Ezekiel 13:19; Micah 3:11"> Yes, they are greedy dogs which can never have enough, and they are shepherds that cannot understand: they all look to their own way, every one for his gain, from his quarter.</span>
