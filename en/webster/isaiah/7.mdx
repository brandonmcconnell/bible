<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; 2 Kings 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> And it came to pass in the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, king of Judah, that Rezin the king of Syria, and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up towards Jerusalem to war against it, but could not prevail against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> And it was told the house of David, saying, Syria is confederate with Ephraim. And his heart was moved, and the heart of his people, as the trees of the wood are moved with the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; Romans 9:27"> Then said the LORD to Isaiah, Go forth now to meet Ahaz, thou, and Shear-jashub thy son, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller's field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> And say to him, Take heed, and be quiet; fear not, neither be faint-hearted for the two tails of these smoking fire-brands, for the fierce anger of Rezin with Syria, and of the son of Remaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–4; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 2:2"> Because Syria, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah, have taken evil counsel against thee, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Let us go up against Judah, and harass it, and let us make a breach therein for us, and set a king in the midst of it, even the son of Tabeal:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> Thus saith the Lord GOD, It shall not stand, neither shall it come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; 2 Samuel 8:6"> For the head of Syria is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin; and within sixty five years shall Ephraim be broken, that it be not a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; 2 Chronicles 20:20"> And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah's son. If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> Moreover, the LORD spoke again to Ahaz, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> Ask thee a sign from the LORD thy God; ask it either in the depth, or in the hight above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Malachi 3:15"> But Ahaz said, I will not ask, neither will I tempt the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 34:18"> And he said, Hear ye now, O house of David? Is it a small thing for you to weary men, but will ye weary my God also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; Luke 1:31"> Therefore the LORD himself will give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> Butter and honey shall he eat, that he may know to refuse the evil, and choose the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> For before the child shall know to refuse the evil, and choose the good, the land that thou abhorrest shall be forsaken by both her kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Chronicles 28:19–21"> The LORD will bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon thy father's house, days that have not come, from the day that Ephraim departed from Judah; even the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD will hiss for the fly that is in the uttermost part of the rivers of Egypt, and for the bee that is in the land of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Jeremiah 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> And they shall come, and shall rest all of them in the desolate valleys, and in the holes of the rocks, and upon all thorns, and upon all bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Ezekiel 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> In the same day will the LORD shave with a razor that is hired, namely , by them beyond the river, by the king of Assyria, the head, and the hair of the feet: and it shall also consume the beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; Jeremiah 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that a man shall nourish a young cow, and two sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Matthew 3:4; 2 Samuel 17:29"> And it shall come to pass, for the abundance of milk that they shall give, he shall eat butter: for butter and honey shall every one eat that is left in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that every place shall be, where there were a thousand vines at a thousand pieces of silver, it shall even be for briers and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> With arrows and with bows shall men come thither; because all the land shall become briers and thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> And on all hills that shall be digged with the mattock, there shall not come thither the fear of briers and thorns: but it shall be for the sending forth of oxen, and for the treading of lesser cattle.</span>
