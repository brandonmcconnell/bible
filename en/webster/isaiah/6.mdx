<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:41; Revelation 15:8; Revelation 7:15–17"> In the year that king Uzziah died I saw also the LORD sitting upon a throne, high and elevated, and his train filled the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:8; Ezekiel 1:11; Psalms 104:4"> Above it stood seraphim: each one had six wings; with two he covered his face, and with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; Psalms 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> And one cried to another, and said, Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Exodus 40:34; 2 Chronicles 5:13–1"> And the posts of the door moved at the voice of him that cried, and the house was filled with smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 5:8–9; James 3:6–10; Jeremiah 9:3–8"> Then said I, Woe is me! for I am undone; because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips: for my eyes have seen the King, the LORD of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Ezekiel 10:2"> Then one of the seraphim flew to me, having a live coal in his hand, which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Isaiah 43:25"> And he laid it upon my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips; and thy iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> Also I heard the voice of the LORD, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here am I; send me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> And he said, Go, and tell this people, Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremiah 5:21"> Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes; lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and convert, and be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1–12; Psalms 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> Then said I, Lord, how long? And he answered, Until the cities shall be wasted without inhabitant, and the houses without man, and the land be utterly desolate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 25:11"> And the LORD shall have removed men far away, and there be a great forsaking in the midst of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Romans 11:5–6; Genesis 22:18"> But yet in it shall be a tenth, and it shall return, and shall be eaten: as a teil-tree, and as an oak whose substance is in them, when they cast their leaves : so the holy seed shall be the substance of it.</span>
