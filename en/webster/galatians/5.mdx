<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:13; 1 Peter 2:16"> Stand fast therefore in the liberty with which Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:1; Romans 9:31–32"> Behold, I Paul say to you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 2:25; James 2:10–11"> For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:31–32; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Romans 11:6"> Christ is become of no effect to you, whoever of you are justified by the law; ye have fallen from grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 130:5; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Psalms 62:5"> For we through the Spirit wait for the hope of righteousness by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 7:19; James 2:14–26"> For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision; but faith which worketh by love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; Galatians 3:1; Hebrews 12:1"> Ye did run well, who hindered you that ye should not obey the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Romans 8:28"> This persuasion cometh not from him that calleth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Mark 8:15"> A little leaven leaveneth the whole lump.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Galatians 1:7"> I have confidence in you through the Lord, that ye will be no otherwise minded: but he that troubleth you shall bear his judgment, whoever he may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Galatians 6:12; Galatians 4:29"> And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? then hath the offense of the cross ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Titus 3:10"> I would they were even cut off who trouble you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 9:19; 1 Corinthians 8:9"> For, brethren, ye have been called to liberty; only use not liberty for an occasion to the flesh, but by love serve one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 6:2"> For all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this, Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; James 3:14–3; Galatians 5:26"> But if ye bite and devour one another, take heed that ye be not consumed one by another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:24–25; Romans 8:12–14; Galatians 6:8"> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye will not fulfill the lust of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:5–8"> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other; so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:14; Romans 6:14–15; Galatians 5:25"> But if ye are led by the Spirit, ye are not under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Colossians 3:5–8; Matthew 15:18–19"> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these , Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Revelation 21:8"> Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Romans 8:13; Romans 13:13"> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revelings, and such like: of which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they who do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; John 15:5"> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, long-suffering, gentleness, goodness, faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9; Acts 24:25; Titus 2:2"> Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Romans 13:14; Romans 8:13"> And they that are Christ's have crucified the flesh, with the affections and lusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 8:4–5; Romans 8:2"> If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 3:14–16; Philippians 2:1–3; James 4:16"> Let us not be desirous of vain-glory, provoking one another, envying one another.</span>
