<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:10; Romans 10:7; Revelation 9:2"> And the fifth angel sounded, and I saw a star fall from heaven to the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Genesis 19:28; Joel 2:2"> And he opened the bottomless pit; and there arose a smoke out of the pit, as the smoke of a great furnace; and the sun and the air were darkened by reason of the smoke of the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Revelation 9:5; Joel 2:25"> And there came out of the smoke locusts upon the earth: and to them was given power, as the scorpions of the earth have power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 7:2–4; Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 14:1"> And it was commanded them that they should not hurt the grass of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any tree; but only those men who have not the seal of God in their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Job 2:6; Revelation 9:3"> And to them it was given that they should not kill them, but that they should be tormented five months: and their torment was as the torment of a scorpion, when he striketh a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Jeremiah 8:3; Revelation 6:16"> And in those days shall men seek death, and shall not find it; and shall desire to die, and death shall flee from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Nahum 3:17; Joel 2:4–5"> And the shapes of the locusts were like to horses prepared for battle; and on their heads were as it were crowns like gold, and their faces were as the faces of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 1:6; 1 Corinthians 11:14–15; 1 Peter 3:3"> And they had hair as the hair of women, and their teeth were as the teeth of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:5–8; Nahum 2:4–5; Job 39:25"> And they had breast-plates, as it were breast-plates of iron; and the sound of their wings was as the sound of chariots of many horses running to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:5; Revelation 9:3; Revelation 9:19"> And they had tails like scorpions, and there were stings in their tails: and their power was to hurt men five months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Revelation 9:1–2; Ephesians 2:2"> And they had a king over them, who is the angel of the bottomless pit, whose name in the Hebrew language is Abaddon, but in the Greek language he hath his name Apollyon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:14; Revelation 8:13–2; Revelation 9:13–21"> One woe is past; and behold, there come two woes more hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Exodus 30:2–3; Revelation 9:1"> And the sixth angel sounded, and I heard a voice from the four horns of the golden altar which is before God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Revelation 9:15; 2 Samuel 8:3"> Saying to the sixth angel who had the trumpet, Loose the four angels who are bound in the great river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:9"> And the four angels were loosed, who were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, to slay the third part of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Psalms 68:17; Daniel 7:10"> And the number of the army of the horsemen was two hundred thousand thousand: and I heard the number of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 14:10; 1 Chronicles 12:8"> And thus I saw the horses in the vision, and them that sat on them, having breast-plates of fire, and of jacinth, and brimstone: and the heads of the horses were as the heads of lions: and out of their mouths issued fire, and smoke, and brimstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 9:17; Revelation 9:15"> By these three was the third part of men killed, by the fire, and by the smoke, and by the brimstone, which issued out of their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Revelation 9:10; Ephesians 4:14"> For their power is in their mouth, and in their tails: for their tails were like to serpents, and had heads, and with them they do hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Deuteronomy 31:29; 1 Timothy 4:1"> And the rest of the men who were not killed by these plagues yet repented not of the works of their hands, that they should not worship demons, and idols of gold, and silver, and brass, and stone, and of wood: which neither can see, nor hear, nor walk:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 2 Corinthians 12:21; Revelation 17:5"> Neither repented they of their murders, nor of their sorceries, nor of their lewdness, nor of their thefts.</span>
