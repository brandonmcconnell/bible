<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:1–3; 2 Peter 1:16–19; John 20:31"> Forasmuch as many have taken in hand to set forth in order a declaration of those things which are most surely believed among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:27; Acts 26:16; Hebrews 2:3"> Even as they delivered them to us, who from the beginning were eye-witnesses, and ministers of the word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 1:1; Acts 26:25; Acts 11:4"> It seemed good to me also, having had perfect understanding of all things from the very first, to write to thee in order, most excellent Theophilus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> That thou mayest know the certainty of those things in which thou hast been instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> There was in the days of Herod the king of Judea, a certain priest named Zacharias, of the course of Abia: and his wife was of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elisabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> And they were both righteous before God, walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> And they had no child, because Elisabeth was barren, and they both were far advanced in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> And it came to pass, that, while he executed the priests' office before God in the order of his course,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> According to the custom of the priests' office, his lot was to burn incense when he went into the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> And the whole multitude of the people were praying without, at the time of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> And there appeared to him an angel of the Lord, standing on the right side of the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> And when Zacharias saw him , he was troubled, and fear fell upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said to him, Fear not, Zacharias: for thy prayer is heard; and thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> And thou shalt have joy and gladness, and many shall rejoice at his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> For he shall be great in the sight of the Lord, and shall drink neither wine nor strong drink; and he shall be filled with the Holy Spirit, even from his mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> And many of the children of Israel shall he turn to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> And he shall go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to make ready a people prepared for the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> And Zacharias said to the angel, By what shall I know this? for I am an old man, and my wife far advanced in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> And the angel answering, said to him, I am Gabriel, who stand in the presence of God; and am sent to speak to thee, and to show thee these glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> And behold, thou shalt be dumb, and not able to speak, until the day that these things shall be performed, because thou believest not my words which shall be fulfilled in their season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> And the people waited for Zacharias, and marveled that he tarried so long in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> And when he came out, he could not speak to them: and they perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple; for he beckoned to them, and remained speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> And it came to pass, that as soon as the days of his ministration were accomplished, he departed to his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And after those days his wife Elisabeth conceived, and secluded herself five months, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> Thus hath the Lord dealt with me in the days in which he looked on me , to take away my reproach among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> And in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee, named Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> To a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name was Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> And the angel came to her, and said, Hail, thou that art highly favored, the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> And when she saw him , she was troubled at his saying, and cast in her mind what manner of salutation this should be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> And the angel said to her, Fear not, Mary: for thou hast found favor with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> And behold, thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son, and shalt call his name JESUS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> He will be great, and will be called the Son of the Highest; and the Lord God will give to him the throne of his father David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> And he will reign over the house of Jacob for ever, and of his kingdom there will be no end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> Then said Mary to the angel, How shall this be, seeing I know not a man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> And the angel answered and said to her, The Holy Spirit will come upon thee, and the power of the Highest will overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And behold, thy cousin Elisabeth, she hath also conceived a son in her old age; and this is the sixth month with her who was called barren:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> For with God nothing will be impossible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> And Mary said, Behold the handmaid of the Lord, be it to me according to thy word. And the angel departed from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> And Mary arose in those days, and went into the hill-country with haste, into a city of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And entered into the house of Zacharias, and saluted Elisabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> And it came to pass, that when Elisabeth heard the salutation of Mary, the babe leaped in her womb: and Elisabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 1:28; Judges 5:24; Genesis 22:18"> And she spoke with a loud voice, and said, Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> And whence is this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For lo, as soon as the voice of thy salutation sounded in my ears, the babe leaped in my womb for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> And blessed is she that believed that there will be a performance of those things which were told her from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:1–10; Psalms 34:2–3; Psalms 35:9"> And Mary said, My soul doth magnify the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 35:9; 1 Timothy 2:3; Habakkuk 3:18"> And my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Malachi 3:12; 1 Corinthians 1:26–28"> For he hath regarded the low estate of his handmaiden: for behold, from henceforth all generations will call me blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Psalms 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> For he that is mighty hath done to me great things, and holy is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> And his mercy is on them that fear him, from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> He hath shown strength with his arm; he hath scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; James 4:10; Psalms 107:40–41"> He hath put down the mighty from their seats, and exalted them of low degree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; Psalms 34:10"> He hath filled the hungry with good things, and the rich he hath sent away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> He hath sustained his servant Israel, in remembrance of his mercy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; Psalms 132:11–17"> As he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham, and to his seed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And Mary abode with her about three months, and returned to her own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> Now Elisabeth's full time came that she should be delivered; and she brought forth a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> And her neighbors and her cousins heard that the Lord had shown great mercy upon her; and they rejoiced with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> And it came to pass, that on the eighth day they came to circumcise the child; and they called him Zacharias, after the name of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> And his mother answered and said, Not so ; but he shall be called John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they said to her, There is none of thy kindred that is called by this name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> And they made signs to his father, how he would have him called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> And he asked for a writing-table, and wrote, saying, His name is John. And they all wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> And his mouth was opened immediately, and his tongue loosed , and he spoke, and praised God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> And fear came on all that dwelt around them; and all these sayings were noised abroad throughout all the hill-country of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And all they that had heard them , laid them up in their hearts, saying, What manner of child will this be! And the hand of the Lord was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Luke 1:41; Luke 1:15"> And his father Zacharias was filled with the Holy Spirit, and prophesied, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Luke 7:16; Psalms 41:13"> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Samuel 22:3"> And hath raised up a horn of salvation for us, in the house of his servant David:</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> As he spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets, who have been since the world began:</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Psalms 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> That we should be saved from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–18; Micah 7:20; Psalms 106:45"> To perform the mercy promised to our fathers, and to remember his holy covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Psalms 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> The oath which he swore to our father Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> That he would grant to us, that we being delivered out of the hand of our enemies, might serve him without fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> In holiness and righteousness before him, all the days of our life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> And thou, child, shalt be called the prophet of the Highest, for thou shalt go before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Acts 2:38; Acts 10:43"> To give knowledge of salvation to his people, by the remission of their sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="Malachi 4:2; 2 Peter 1:19; Revelation 22:16"> Through the tender mercy of our God; by which the day-spring from on high hath visited us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Matthew 4:16; Acts 26:18"> To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shades of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Mark 1:3–4; Luke 2:52"> And the child grew, and became strong in spirit, and was in the deserts till the day of his showing to Israel.</span>
