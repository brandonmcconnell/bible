<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:8; Psalms 20:7"> When thou goest out to battle against thy enemies, and seest horses, and chariots, and a people more than thou, be not afraid of them: for the LORD thy God is with thee, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 31:6; 2 Chronicles 13:12; Numbers 10:8–9"> And it shall be when ye are come nigh to the battle, that the priest shall approach and speak to the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 3:6; Revelation 2:10"> And shall say to them, Hear, O Israel, ye approach this day to battle against your enemies: let not your hearts faint, fear not, and do not tremble, neither be ye terrified because of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 3:22; Deuteronomy 1:30"> For the LORD your God is he that goeth with you, to fight for you against your enemies, to save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:27; Numbers 31:14; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And the officers shall speak to the people, saying, What man is there that hath built a new house, and hath not dedicated it? let him go and return to his house, lest he should die in the battle, and another man should dedicate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:1–30; Jeremiah 31:5"> And what man is he that hath planted a vineyard, and hath not yet eaten of it? let him also go and return to his house, lest he should die in the battle, and another man should eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; Deuteronomy 28:30; Luke 14:18–20"> And what man is there that hath betrothed a wife, and hath not taken her? let him go and return to his house, lest he should die in battle, and another man should take her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 7:3; Deuteronomy 1:28; Revelation 21:8"> And the officers shall speak further to the people, and they shall say, What man is there that is fearful and faint-hearted? let him go and return to his house, lest his brethren's heart should faint as well as his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And it shall be, when the officers have made an end of speaking to the people, that they shall make captains of the armies to lead the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:18–1; Ephesians 2:17"> When thou comest nigh to a city to fight against it, then proclaim peace to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Joshua 11:19–20; Joshua 16:10"> And it shall be, if it shall make thee answer of peace, and open to thee, then it shall be, that all the people that are found therein, shall be tributaries to thee, and they shall serve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And if it will make no peace with thee, but will make war against thee, then thou shalt besiege it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 2:6–12; 1 Kings 11:15–16; Psalms 110:1"> And when the LORD thy God hath delivered it into thy hands, thou shalt smite every male of it with the edge of the sword:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:9"> But the women, and the little ones, and the cattle, and all that is in the city, even all the spoil of it, shalt thou take to thyself: and thou shalt eat the spoil of thy enemies, which the LORD thy God hath given thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Thus shalt thou do to all the cities which are very distant from thee, which are not of the cities of these nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 11:14; Numbers 21:2–3; Numbers 33:52"> But of the cities of these people which the LORD thy God doth give thee for an inheritance, thou shalt save alive nothing that breatheth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Jeremiah 50:35–40; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> But thou shalt utterly destroy them, namely , the Hittites, and the Amorites, the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, as the LORD thy God hath commanded thee:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Exodus 23:33; Ephesians 5:11"> That they may not teach you to do after all their abominations which they have done to their gods; so would ye sin against the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; John 15:2–8; Deuteronomy 26:6"> When thou shalt besiege a city a long time in making war against it to take it, thou shalt not destroy the trees of it by forcing an ax against them; for thou mayest eat of them: and thou shalt not cut them down (for the tree of the field is man's life ) to employ them in the siege:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:15; Jeremiah 33:4; Ecclesiastes 9:14"> Only the trees which thou knowest that they are not trees for food, thou shalt destroy and cut them down; and thou shalt build bulwarks against the city that maketh war with thee, until it shall be subdued.</span>
