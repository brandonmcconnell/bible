<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 14:6; Psalms 90:1; Genesis 27:4"> And this is the with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the children of Israel before his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Habakkuk 3:3; Revelation 5:11"> And he said, the LORD came from Sinai, and rose up from Seir to them; he shined forth from mount Paran, and he came with ten thousands of saints: from his right hand went a fiery law for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Malachi 1:2; Deuteronomy 7:6–8"> Yea, he loved the people; all his saints are in thy hand: and they sat down at thy feet; every one shall receive of thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:17; Psalms 119:111; John 7:19"> Moses commanded us a law; even the inheritance of the congregation of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 17:6; Numbers 23:21"> And he was king in Jeshurun, when the heads of the people and the tribes of Israel were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:8; Numbers 32:31–32; Joshua 22:1–9"> Let Reuben live, and not die; and let not his men be few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–12; Psalms 20:2; 2 Samuel 7:9–12"> And this is the blessing of Judah: and he said, Hear, LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people: let his hands be sufficient for him, and be thou a help to him from his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Exodus 28:30; Numbers 20:13"> And of Levi he said, Let thy Thummim and thy Urim be with thy holy one, whom thou didst prove at Massah, and with whom thou didst strive at the waters of Meribah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Exodus 32:25–29; Matthew 12:48"> Who said to his father and to his mother, I have not seen him, neither did he acknowledge his brethren, nor know his own children: for they have observed thy word, and kept thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:11; Psalms 51:19; Deuteronomy 17:9–11"> They shall teach Jacob thy judgments, and Israel thy law; they shall put incense before thee, and whole burnt sacrifice upon thy altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:23; Ezekiel 43:27; Ezekiel 20:40–41"> Bless, LORD, his substance, and accept the work of his hands: smite through the loins of them that rise against him, and of them that hate him, that they rise not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalms 91:4; Matthew 23:37"> And of Benjamin he said, The beloved of the LORD shall dwell in safety by him; and the LORD shall cover him all the day long, and he shall dwell between his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Micah 5:7; Isaiah 18:4"> And of Joseph he said, Blessed of the LORD be his land, for the precious things of heaven, for the dew, and for the deep that coucheth beneath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 8:3"> And for the precious fruits brought forth by the sun, and for the precious things put forth by the moon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:6; Genesis 49:26; James 5:7"> And for the chief things of the ancient mountains, and for the precious things of the lasting hills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Psalms 24:1; Exodus 3:2–4"> And for the precious things of the earth and fullness of it, and for the good will of him that dwelt in the bush; let the blessing come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the top of the head of him that was separated from his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 22:11; Numbers 23:22; Psalms 44:5"> His glory is like the firstling of his bullock, and his horns are like the horns of unicorns: with them he shall push the people together to the ends of the earth: and they are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and they are the thousands of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:13–15; Joshua 19:11; Judges 5:14"> And of Zebulun he said, Rejoice, Zebulun, in thy going out; and Issachar, in thy tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 4:5"> They shall call the people to the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of righteousness: for they shall suck of the abundance of the seas, and of treasures hid in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Genesis 9:26–27; 1 Chronicles 5:18–21"> And of Gad he said: Blessed be he that enlargeth Gad: he dwelleth as a lion, and teareth the arm with the crown of the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 32:16–42; Judges 5:11; Judges 5:2"> And he provided the first part for himself, because there, in a portion of the lawgiver, was he seated: and he came with the heads of the people, he executed the justice of the LORD, and his judgments with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Joshua 19:47; Judges 15:8"> And of Dan he said, Dan is a lion's whelp: he shall leap from Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Psalms 36:8; Matthew 4:16"> And of Naphtali he said, O Naphtali, satisfied with favor, and full with the blessing of the LORD: possess thou the west and the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; Job 29:6; Romans 14:18"> And of Asher he said, Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 138:3; Philippians 4:13"> Thy shoes shall be iron and brass; and as thy days, so shall thy strength be .</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Isaiah 43:11–13; Jeremiah 10:6"> There is none like to the God of Jeshurun, who rideth upon the heaven in thy help, and in his excellence on the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 90:1–2; Psalms 46:1"> The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms: and he shall thrust out the enemy from before thee, and shall say, Destroy them .</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:9; Genesis 27:28; Exodus 33:16"> Israel then shall dwell in safety alone; the fountain of Jacob shall be upon a land of corn and wine, also his heavens shall drop down dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 115:9–11"> Happy art thou, O Israel: who is like to thee, O people saved by the LORD, the shield of thy help, and who is the sword of thy excellence! and thy enemies shall be found liars to thee; and thou shalt tread upon their high places.</span>
