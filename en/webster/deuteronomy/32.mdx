<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 4:26; Jeremiah 22:29"> Give ear, O ye heavens, and I will speak; and hear, O earth, the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10–11; Psalms 72:6; Micah 5:7"> My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distill as the dew, as the small rain upon the tender herb, and as the showers upon the grass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:6; Psalms 150:2; Ephesians 1:19"> Because I will publish the name of the LORD: ascribe ye greatness to our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:30–31; Psalms 92:15; Isaiah 26:4"> He is the Rock, his work is perfect: for all his ways are judgment: a God of truth and without iniquity: just and right is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Philippians 2:15; Matthew 17:17"> They have corrupted themselves, their spot is not the spot of his children: they are a perverse and crooked generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:16; 1 John 3:1; Isaiah 44:2"> Do ye thus requite the LORD, O foolish people and unwise? is not he thy father that hath bought thee? hath he not made thee, and established thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–4; Job 8:8–10; Psalms 44:1"> Remember the days of old, consider the years of many generations: ask thy father, and he will show thee; thy elders, and they will tell thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Genesis 11:8–9; Daniel 4:17"> When the Most High divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Exodus 19:5–6"> For the LORD'S portion is his people; Jacob is the lot of his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Psalms 17:8; Hosea 13:5"> He found him in a desert land, and in the waste howling wilderness; he led him about, he instructed him, he kept him as the apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 63:9"> As an eagle stirreth up her nest, fluttereth over her young, spreadeth abroad her wings, taketh them, beareth them on her wings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 78:52–53; Isaiah 46:4; Isaiah 44:7–8"> So the LORD alone did lead him, and there was no strange God with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 58:14; Job 29:6; Psalms 81:16"> He made him ride on the high places of the earth, that he might eat the increase of the fields; and he made him to suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the flinty rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 49:11; Psalms 81:16; Psalms 147:14"> Butter of cows, and milk of sheep, with fat of lambs, and rams of the breed of Bashan, and goats, with the fat of kidneys of wheat; and thou didst drink the pure blood of the grape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Hosea 13:6; Deuteronomy 33:5"> But Jeshurun waxed fat, and kicked: thou hast waxed fat, thou art grown thick, thou art covered with fatness ; then he forsook God who made him, and lightly esteemed the Rock of his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Nahum 1:1–2"> They provoked him to jealousy with strange gods , with abominations they provoked him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 17:7"> They sacrificed to devils, not to God; to gods which they knew not, to new gods that came newly up, whom your fathers feared not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 106:21"> Of the Rock that begat thee thou art unmindful, and hast forgotten God that formed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Psalms 5:4; Isaiah 1:2"> And when the LORD saw it , he abhorred them , because of the provoking of his sons, and of his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:17–18; Luke 7:31–32; Deuteronomy 32:5"> And he said, I will hide my face from them, I will see what their end will be : for they are a very froward generation, children in whom is no faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:19; Romans 9:25; Romans 11:11–14"> They have moved me to jealousy with that which is not God; they have provoked me to anger with their vanities: and I will move them to jealousy with those who are not a people; I will provoke them to anger with a foolish nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Lamentations 4:11; Jeremiah 15:14; Micah 1:4"> For a fire is kindled in my anger, and shall burn to the lowest hell, and shall consume the earth with her increase, and set on fire the foundations of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Psalms 7:12–13; Isaiah 24:17–18"> I will heap mischiefs upon them; I will spend my arrows upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:22; Ezekiel 5:17; Isaiah 65:25"> They shall be burnt with hunger, and devoured with burning heat, and with bitter destruction: I will also send the teeth of beasts upon them, with the poison of serpents of the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:15; Lamentations 1:20; Isaiah 30:16"> The sword without, and terror within, shall destroy both the young man and the virgin, the suckling also with the man of gray hairs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I said, I would scatter them into corners, I would make the remembrance of them to cease from among men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 14:15–16; Exodus 32:12; Zechariah 1:14–15"> Were it not that I feared the wrath of the enemy, lest their adversaries should behave themselves strangely, and lest they should say, Our hand is high, and the LORD hath not done all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 27:11; Job 28:28"> For they are a nation void of counsel, neither is there any understanding in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 81:13; Deuteronomy 5:29; Hosea 14:9"> O that they were wise, that they understood this, that they would consider their latter end!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; Joshua 23:10; Isaiah 30:17"> How should one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, except their Rock had sold them, and the LORD had shut them up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 1 Samuel 2:2; Daniel 2:47"> For their rock is not as our Rock, even our enemies themselves being judges:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:18; Ezekiel 16:45–51; Isaiah 1:10"> For their vine is of the vine of Sodom, and of the fields of Gomorrah: their grapes are grapes of gall, their clusters are bitter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 58:4; Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3"> Their wine is the poison of dragons, and the cruel venom of asps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 13:12; Job 14:17; Jeremiah 2:22"> Is not this laid up in store with me, and sealed up among my treasures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Hebrews 10:30; Nahum 1:2"> To me belongeth vengeance, and recompense; their foot shall slide in due time: for the day of their calamity is at hand, and the things that shall come upon them make haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 135:14; Psalms 106:45; Judges 2:18"> For the LORD shall judge his people, and repent for his servants: when he seeth that their power is gone, and there is none shut up, or left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Judges 10:14; 2 Kings 3:13"> And he shall say, Where are their gods, their rock in whom they trusted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18–19; Psalms 50:13; Judges 10:14"> Which ate the fat of their sacrifices, and drank the wine of their drink-offerings? let them rise up and help you, and be your protection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:6; Hosea 6:1; Job 5:18"> See now that I, even I am he, and there is no god with me: I kill, and I make alive; I wound, and I heal: neither is there any that can deliver out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17–18; Genesis 14:22; Revelation 10:5–6"> For I lift up my hand to heaven, and say, I live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Isaiah 34:5–6; Isaiah 27:1"> If I shall whet my glittering sword, and my hand take hold on judgment; I will render vengeance to my enemies, and will reward them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Jeremiah 46:10; Ezekiel 38:21–22"> I will make my arrows drunk with blood, and my sword shall devour flesh; and that with the blood of the slain and of the captives from the beginning of revenges upon the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Romans 12:19; Revelation 6:10"> Rejoice, O ye nations, with his people: for he will avenge the blood of his servants, and will render vengeance to his adversaries, and will be merciful to his land, and to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Numbers 13:16; Deuteronomy 31:30"> And Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the ears of the people, he and Hoshea the son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And Moses made an end of speaking all these words to all Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:19; Ezekiel 40:4; Proverbs 3:1–4"> And he said to them, Set your hearts to all the words which I testify among you this day, which ye shall command your children to observe to do, all the words of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Leviticus 18:5; 2 Peter 1:16"> For it is not a vain thing for you: because it is your life; and through this thing ye shall prolong your days in the land whither ye go over Jordan to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 27:12–13"> And the LORD spoke to Moses that same day, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Isaiah 33:17; Deuteronomy 34:1–5"> Ascend this mountain Abarim, to mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, that is over against Jericho; and behold the land of Canaan which I give to the children of Israel for a Possession:</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:24–29"> And die in the mount whither thou goest, and be gathered to thy people; as Aaron thy brother died in mount Hor, and was gathered to his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 27:14; Numbers 20:11–14; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> Because ye trespassed against me among the children of Israel at the waters of Meribah-Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin; because ye sanctified me not in the midst of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:27; Hebrews 11:39"> Yet thou shalt see the land before thee , but thou shalt not go thither to the land which I give the children of Israel.</span>
