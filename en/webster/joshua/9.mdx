<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 23:23"> And it came to pass, when all the kings who were on this side Jordan, in the hills, and in the valleys, and in all the coasts of the great sea over against Lebanon, the Hittite, and the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite heard these things ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Joel 3:9–13; Psalms 2:1–2"> That they assembled themselves, to fight with Joshua and with Israel, with one accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 21:1–2"> And when the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and to Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Matthew 10:16; Mark 2:22"> They did work craftily, and went and made as if they had been embassadors, and took old sacks upon their asses, and wine-bottles, old, and rent, and bound up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 29:5"> And old shoes and patched upon their feet, and old garments upon them; and all the bread of their provision was dry and moldy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; 2 Kings 20:14; Joshua 9:9"> And they went to Joshua to the camp at Gilgal, and said to him, and to the men of Israel, We have come from a far country: now therefore make ye a league with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 11:19; Judges 2:2; Exodus 34:12"> And the men of Israel said to the Hivites, It may be ye dwell among us; and how shall we make a league with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:11; 2 Kings 10:5; Joshua 9:11"> And they said to Joshua, we are thy servants. And Joshua said to them, Who are ye? and whence come ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15; Joshua 9:24; Isaiah 55:5"> And they said to him, From a very far country thy servants have come because of the name of the LORD thy God: for we have heard the fame of him, and all that he did in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 12:4"> And all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites, that were beyond Jordan, to Sihon king of Heshbon, and to Og king of Bashan, who was at Ashtaroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 9:8; Esther 8:17; Joshua 1:11"> Wherefore our elders, and all the inhabitants of our country spoke to us, saying, Take provisions with you for the journey, and go to meet them, and say to them, We are your servants: therefore now make ye a league with us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 9:4–5"> This our bread we took hot for our provision out of our houses on the day we came forth to go to you; but now, behold, it is dry, and it is moldy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And these bottles of wine which we filled, were new, and behold, they are rent: and these our garments and our shoes are become old by reason of the very long journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Numbers 27:21; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14"> And the men took of their provisions, and asked not counsel at the mouth of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Deuteronomy 20:10–11; Joshua 11:19"> And Joshua made peace with them, and made a league with them, to let them live: and the princes of the congregation swore to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 12:19"> And it came to pass at the end of three days after they had made a league with them, that they heard that they were their neighbors, and that they dwelt among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:25; Joshua 18:25–28; Joshua 18:14"> And the children of Israel journeyed, and came to their cities on the third day. Now their cities were Gibeon, and Chephirah, and Beeroth, and Kirjath-jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 15:4; 2 Samuel 21:7; Ecclesiastes 5:6"> And the children of Israel smote them not, because the princes of the congregation had sworn to them by the LORD God of Israel. And all the congregation murmured against the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Joshua 9:20; Ecclesiastes 9:2"> But all the princes said to all the congregation, We have sworn to them by the LORD God of Israel: now therefore we may not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 20:25; 1 Timothy 1:10"> This we will do to them; we will even let them live, lest wrath be upon us, because of the oath which we swore to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:11; Joshua 9:27; Joshua 9:23"> And the princes said to them, Let them live; but let them be hewers of wood, and drawers of water to all the congregation; as the princes had promised them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 9:16; Genesis 3:13–14"> And Joshua called for them, and he spoke to them, saying, Why have ye deceived us, saying, We are very far from you; when ye dwell among us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 9:21; Genesis 9:25–26; Joshua 9:27"> Now therefore ye are cursed, and there shall none of you be freed from being bond-men, and hewers of wood and drawers of water for the house of my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15–17; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; Exodus 23:31–33"> And they answered Joshua, and said, Because it was certainly told thy servants, how the LORD thy God commanded his servant Moses to give you all the land, and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land from before you, therefore we were greatly afraid for our lives because of you, and have done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 16:6; Judges 10:15; Jeremiah 26:14"> And now, behold, we are in thy hand: do as it seemeth good and right to thee to do to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And so did he to them, and delivered them out of the hand of the children of Israel, that they slew them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5"> And Joshua made them that day hewers of wood and drawers of water for the congregation, and for the altar of the LORD, even to this day, in the place which he should choose.</span>
