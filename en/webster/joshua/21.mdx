<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Numbers 35:2–8; Matthew 10:10"> And they spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to dwell in, with their suburbs for our cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:7; Deuteronomy 33:8–10; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> And the children of Israel gave to the Levites out of their inheritance, at the commandment of the LORD, these cities and their suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:54–60; Joshua 24:33; Joshua 21:8–19"> And the lot came out for the families of the Kohathites: and the children of Aaron the priest, who were of the Levites, had by lot out of the tribe of Judah, and out of the tribe of Simeon, and out of the tribe of Benjamin, thirteen cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:20–26; Exodus 6:16–25; 1 Chronicles 6:66–70"> And the rest of the children of Kohath had by lot out of the families of the tribe of Ephraim, and out of the tribe of Dan, and out of the half-tribe of Manasseh, ten cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:27–33; 1 Chronicles 6:71–76; Exodus 6:16–17"> And the children of Gershon had by lot out of the families of the tribe of Issachar, and out of the tribe of Asher, and out of the tribe of Naphtali, and out of the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan, thirteen cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:34–40; 1 Chronicles 6:77–81; Exodus 6:19"> The children of Merari by their families had out of the tribe of Reuben, and out of the tribe of Gad, and out of the tribe of Zebulun, twelve cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 21:3; Joshua 18:6; Numbers 33:54"> And the children of Israel gave by lot to the Levites these cities with their suburbs, as the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:65; Joshua 21:13–18"> And they gave out of the tribe of the children of Judah, and out of the tribe of the children of Simeon, these cities which are here mentioned by name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 21:4; Numbers 3:27; Exodus 6:20–26"> Which the children of Aaron, being of the families of the Kohathites, who were of the children of Levi, had: for theirs was the first lot.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 14:13–15; 1 Chronicles 6:55–57"> But the fields of the city, and its villages, they gave to Caleb the son of Jephunneh for his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 15:42; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 15:54"> Thus they gave to the children of Aaron the priest, Hebron with its suburbs, to be a city of refuge for the slayer; and Libnah with its suburbs,</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:24–25; Joshua 9:3; 1 Chronicles 6:60"> And out of the tribe of Benjamin, Gibeon with its suburbs, Geba with its suburbs,</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> All the cities of the children of Aaron, the priests, were thirteen cities with their suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:66; Joshua 21:5"> And the families of the children of Kohath, the Levites who remained of the children of Kohath, even they had the cities of their lot out of the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 20:7; Judges 9:1; 1 Kings 12:1"> For they gave them Shechem with its suburbs in mount Ephraim, to be a city of refuge for the slayer; and Gezer with its suburbs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:68; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 18:13–14"> And Kibzaim with its suburbs, and Beth-horon with its suburbs; four cities.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Joshua 10:12; 1 Chronicles 6:69"> Ajalon with its suburbs, Gath-rimmon with its suburbs; four cities.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> All the cities were ten with their suburbs, for the families of the children of Kohath that remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:71; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 20:8"> And to the children of Gershon, of the families of the Levites, out of the other half-tribe of Manasseh they gave Golan in Bashan with its suburbs, to be a city of refuge for the slayer, and Beesh-terah with its suburbs; two cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 19:12; 1 Chronicles 6:72–73"> And out of the tribe of Issachar, Kishon with its suburbs, Dabareh with its suburbs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 10:23; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:3"> Jarmuth with its suburbs, En-gannim with its suburbs; four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:74–75; Joshua 19:25–28"> And out of the tribe of Asher, Mishal with its suburbs, Abdon with its suburbs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 18:21; 1 Chronicles 6:75; Judges 1:31"> Helkath with its suburbs, and Rehob with its suburbs; four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:76; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 19:37"> And out of the tribe of Naphtali, Kedesh in Galilee with its suburbs, to be a city of refuge for the slayer; and Hammoth-dor with its suburbs, and Kartan with its suburbs; three cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> All the cities of the Gershonites, according to their families, were thirteen cities with their suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:77; Joshua 19:11; Joshua 21:7"> And to the families of the children of Merari, the rest of the Levites, out of the tribe of Zebulun, Jokneam with its suburbs, and Kartah with its suburbs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Dimnah with its suburbs, Nahalal with its suburbs; four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Deuteronomy 4:43; Numbers 21:23"> And out of the tribe of Reuben, Bezer with its suburbs, and Jahazah with its suburbs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Kedemoth with its suburbs, and Mephaath with its suburbs; four cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Chronicles 6:80"> And out of the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Gilead with its suburbs to be a city of refuge for the slayer; and Mahanaim with its suburbs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:1"> Heshbon with its suburbs, Jazer with its suburbs; four cities in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So all the cities for the children of Merari by their families, which were remaining of the families of the Levites, were by their lot twelve cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:1–8; Deuteronomy 33:10; Genesis 49:7"> All the cities of the Levites within the possession of the children of Israel were forty and eight cities with their suburbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> These cities were every one with their suburbs around them. Thus were all these cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Psalms 106:42–45; Exodus 23:27–31"> And the LORD gave to Israel all the land which he swore to give to their fathers: and they possessed it, and dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Hebrews 4:9; Deuteronomy 31:3–5"> And the LORD gave them rest on all sides, according to all that he swore to their fathers: and there stood not a man of all their enemies before them; the LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 8:56; Joshua 23:14–15; Numbers 23:19"> There failed not aught of any good thing which the LORD had spoken to the house of Israel; all came to pass.</span>
