<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance. O LORD, rebuke me not in thy wrath: neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; Psalms 32:4; Psalms 64:7"> For thy arrows stick fast in me, and thy hand falleth heavy upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> There is no soundness in my flesh because of thy anger; neither is there any rest in my bones because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> For my iniquities have gone over my head: as a heavy burden they are too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> My wounds are offensive, and are corrupt because of my foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> For my loins are filled with a lothsome disease : and there is no soundness in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> I am feeble and grievously broken: I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> LORD, all my desire is before thee; and my groaning is not hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> My heart panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of my eyes, that also is gone from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my affliction; and my kinsmen stand afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> They also that seek after my life lay snares for me : and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and imagine deceits all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> But I, as a deaf man , heard not; and I was as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> Thus I was as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> For in thee, O LORD, do I hope: thou wilt hear, O LORD my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> For I said, Hear me , lest otherwise they should rejoice over me: when my foot slippeth, they magnify themselves against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> For I am ready to halt, and my sorrow is continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> For I will declare my iniquity; I will be sorry for my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> But my enemies are lively, and they are strong: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> They also that render evil for good are my adversaries; because I follow the thing that is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> Forsake me not, O LORD: O my God, be not far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> Make haste to help me, O LORD my salvation.</span>
