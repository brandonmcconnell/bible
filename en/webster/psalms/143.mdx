<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> For the enemy hath persecuted my soul; he hath smitten my life down to the ground; he hath made me to dwell in darkness, as those that have been long dead.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> I stretch forth my hands to thee: my soul thirsteth after thee, as a thirsty land. Selah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> Deliver me, O LORD, from my enemies: I flee to thee to hide me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: thy spirit is good; lead me into the land of uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> Revive me, O LORD, for thy name's sake: for thy righteousness' sake bring my soul out of trouble.</span>
