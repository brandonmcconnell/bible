<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Psalms 35:22; Psalms 50:3"> A song, or Psalm of Asaph. Keep not thou silence, O God: hold not thy peace, and be not still, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Matthew 27:24; Judges 8:28"> For lo, thy enemies make a tumult: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:2; Psalms 27:5; Psalms 31:20"> They have taken crafty counsel against thy people, and consulted against thy hidden ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Psalms 74:8"> They have said, Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:13; Isaiah 8:9–10"> For they have consulted together with one consent: they are confederate against thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 20:1; Psalms 137:7"> The tabernacles of Edom, and the Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the Hagarenes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 27:9"> Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; Genesis 25:3; 2 Kings 15:19"> Assur also is joined with them: they have helped the children of Lot. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 4:15–24; Isaiah 9:4; Judges 5:21"> Do to them as to the Midianites; as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the brook of Kison:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:17; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 28:7"> Who perished at En-dor: they became as dung for the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:25; Judges 8:12–21"> Make their nobles like Oreb, and like Zeeb: yes, all their princes as Zebah, and as Zalmunna:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:11; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 74:7–8"> Who said, Let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:18; Psalms 35:5; Jeremiah 13:24"> O my God, make them like a wheel; as the stubble before the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:22; Isaiah 9:18; Malachi 4:1"> As the fire burneth a wood, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 9:17; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 58:9"> So persecute them with thy tempest, and make them afraid with thy storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 9:19–20; Psalms 6:10; Psalms 109:29"> Fill their faces with shame; that they may seek thy name, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 40:14–15; Psalms 35:26"> Let them be confounded and troubled for ever; yes, let them be put to shame, and perish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Psalms 59:13; Jeremiah 16:21"> That men may know that thou, whose name alone is JEHOVAH, art the most high over all the earth.</span>
