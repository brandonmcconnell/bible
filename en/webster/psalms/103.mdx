<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 103:12"> Who redeemeth thy life from destruction; who crowneth thee with loving-kindness and tender mercies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things ; so that thy youth is renewed like the eagle's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> The LORD executeth righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> He made known his ways to Moses, his acts to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> The LORD is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in mercy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:10; Luke 1:50; Psalms 36:5"> For as the heaven is high above the earth, so great is his mercy towards them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Micah 7:18"> As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> Like as a father pitieth his children, so the LORD pitieth them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> For he knoweth our frame; he remembereth that we are dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Isaiah 51:12"> As for man, his days are as grass: as a flower of the field, so he flourisheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> For the wind passeth over it, and it is gone; and its place shall know it no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> But the mercy of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear him, and his righteousness to children's children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> To such as keep his covenant, and to those that remember his commandments to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; Psalms 9:7"> The LORD hath prepared his throne in the heavens; and his kingdom ruleth over all.</span>
