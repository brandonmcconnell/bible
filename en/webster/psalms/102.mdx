<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 130:1–2; Psalms 142:2–3"> A Prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed, and poureth out his complaint before the LORD. Hear my prayer, O LORD, and let my cry come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 27:9; Isaiah 8:17"> Hide not thy face from me in the day when I am in trouble; incline thy ear to me: in the day when I call, answer me speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 37:20; Job 30:30"> For my days are consumed like smoke, and my bones are burned as an hearth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7; Psalms 37:2; Psalms 77:3"> My heart is smitten, and withered like grass; so that I forget to eat my bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:20; Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 6:8"> By reason of the voice of my groaning, my bones cleave to my skin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:14; Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 34:11–15"> I am like a pelican of the wilderness: I am like an owl of the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 77:4; Psalms 38:11; Deuteronomy 28:66–67"> I watch, and am as a sparrow alone upon the house top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:11; Psalms 69:9–10; Isaiah 65:15"> My enemies reproach me all the day; and they that are enraged against me are sworn against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Psalms 80:5; Micah 1:10"> For I have eaten ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 38:3; Psalms 90:7–9; Psalms 147:6"> Because of thy indignation and thy wrath: for thou hast lifted me up, and cast me down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 109:23; Psalms 144:4"> My days are like a shadow that declineth; and I am withered like grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 135:13; Lamentations 5:19; Exodus 3:15"> But thou, O LORD, shalt endure for ever; and thy remembrance to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Galatians 4:4; Isaiah 40:2"> Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Zion: for the time to favor her, yes, the set time, is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2; Ezra 7:27; Ezra 3:1–3"> For thy servants take pleasure in her stones, and favor the dust thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:43; Psalms 138:4; Isaiah 60:3–22"> So the heathen shall fear the name of the LORD: and all the kings of the earth thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Psalms 147:2; Jeremiah 33:7"> When the LORD shall build up Zion, he will appear in his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Psalms 22:24; Jeremiah 29:11–14"> He will regard the prayer of the destitute, and not despise their prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:30–31; Romans 15:4; Isaiah 43:21"> This shall be written for the generation to come: and the people which shall be created shall praise the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 14:2; 1 Kings 8:43"> For he hath looked down from the hight of his sanctuary; from heaven hath the LORD beheld the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 79:11; Psalms 146:7; Exodus 3:7"> To hear the groaning of the prisoner; to loose those that are appointed to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Isaiah 51:11; Psalms 79:13"> To declare the name of the LORD in Zion, and his praise in Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:20–23; Matthew 24:14; Psalms 72:8–11"> When the people are assembled, and the kingdoms, to serve the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 89:38–47; 1 Timothy 4:1–3; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–12"> He weakened my strength in the way; he shortened my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 39:13; Habakkuk 1:12; Psalms 102:12"> I said, O my God, take me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are throughout all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Exodus 20:11; Hebrews 1:10–12"> Of old hast thou laid the foundation of the earth: and the heavens are the work of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 34:4"> They shall perish, but thou wilt endure: yes, all of them shall grow old like a garment; as a vesture wilt thou change them, and they shall be changed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> But thou art the same, and thy years will have no end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:35–36; Psalms 45:16–17; Psalms 22:30–31"> The children of thy servants shall continue, and their seed shall be established before thee.</span>
