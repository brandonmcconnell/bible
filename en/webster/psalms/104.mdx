<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 96:6"> Bless the LORD, O my soul. O LORD my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with honor and majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 45:12"> Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment: who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; 2 Samuel 22:11; Isaiah 19:1"> Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; 2 Kings 2:11; 2 Kings 6:17"> Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flaming fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 24:2; Psalms 33:9"> Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a garment: the waters stood above the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> At thy rebuke they fled; at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalms 33:7"> They go up by the mountains; they go down by the valleys to the place which thou hast founded for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; that they turn not again to cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> He sendeth the springs into the valleys, which run among the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> They give drink to every beast of the field: the wild asses quench their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> By them shall the fowls of the heaven have their habitation, which sing among the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> He watereth the hills from his chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man: that he may bring forth food out of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine, and bread which strengtheneth man's heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalms 92:2; Psalms 29:5"> The trees of the LORD are full of sap ; the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> Where the birds make their nests: as for the stork, the fir-trees are her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; and the rocks for the conies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 136:7–9"> He appointeth the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> Thou makest darkness, and it is night: in which all the beasts of the forest do creep forth .</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalms 34:10"> The young lions roar after their prey, and seek their food from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Nahum 3:17; John 3:20"> The sun ariseth, they collect, and lay themselves down in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Man goeth forth to his work and to his labor until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> O LORD, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> So is this great and wide sea, in which are creeping animals innumerable, both small and great beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> There go the ships: there is that leviathan, which thou hast made to play therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> These wait all upon thee; that thou mayest give them their food in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> That which thou givest them, they gather: thou openest thy hand, they are filled with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled: thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created: and thou renewest the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> The glory of the LORD shall endure for ever: the LORD shall rejoice in his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth: he toucheth the hills, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> I will sing to the LORD as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> My meditation of him shall be sweet: I will be glad in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> Let sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless thou the LORD, O my soul. Praise ye the LORD.</span>
