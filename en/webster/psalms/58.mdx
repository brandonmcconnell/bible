<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:20; Isaiah 10:1; Ezekiel 22:27"> Yes, in heart ye work wickedness; ye weigh the violence of your hands in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 51:5; Isaiah 48:8; Proverbs 22:15"> The wicked are estranged from their birth: they go astray as soon as they are born, speaking lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> Their poison is like the poison of a serpent: they are like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> Which will not hearken to the voice of charmers, charming never so wisely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Job 4:10–11; Hosea 5:14"> Break their teeth, O God, in their mouth: break out the great teeth of the young lions, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalms 64:3"> Let them melt away as waters which run continually: when he bendeth his bow to shoot his arrows, let them be as cut in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalms 37:35–36"> As a snail which melteth, let every one of them pass away: like the untimely birth of a woman, that they may not see the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> Before your pots can feel the thorns, he shall take them away as with a whirlwind, both living, and in his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 68:23; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 64:10"> The righteous shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: he shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; Psalms 64:9"> So that a man shall say, Verily there is a reward for the righteous: verily he is a God that judgeth in the earth.</span>
