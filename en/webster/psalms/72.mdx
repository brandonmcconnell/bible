<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; 1 Chronicles 22:12–13; Isaiah 11:2"> A Psalm for Solomon. Give the king thy judgments, O God, and thy righteousness to the king's son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:1; Psalms 72:12–14; Revelation 19:11"> He shall judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> The mountains shall bring peace to the people, and the little hills, by righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> He shall judge the poor of the people, he shall save the children of the needy, and shall break in pieces the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:36–37; Psalms 72:17; Daniel 7:14"> They shall fear thee as long as the sun and moon endure, throughout all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Deuteronomy 32:2; 2 Samuel 23:4"> He shall come down like rain upon the mown grass: as showers that water the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:4; Malachi 4:2; Psalms 92:12"> In his days shall the righteous flourish; and abundance of peace so long as the moon endureth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zechariah 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> He shall have dominion also from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Micah 7:17; Luke 19:27"> They that dwell in the wilderness shall bow before him; and his enemies shall lick the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Isaiah 49:7"> The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall bring presents: the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 49:22–23; Revelation 11:15"> Yes, all kings shall fall down before him: all nations shall serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 29:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Psalms 102:17"> For he shall deliver the needy when he crieth; the poor also, and him that hath no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> He shall spare the poor and needy, and shall save the souls of the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> He shall redeem their soul from deceit and violence: and precious shall be their blood in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Sheba: prayer also shall be made for him continually; and daily shall he be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Isaiah 30:23"> There shall be a handful of corn in the earth upon the top of the mountains; the fruit of it shall shake like Lebanon: and they of the city shall flourish like grass of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23; Psalms 89:36"> His name shall endure for ever: his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and men shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who only doeth wondrous things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> And blessed be his glorious name for ever: and let the whole earth be filled with his glory; Amen, and amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremiah 51:64"> The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.</span>
