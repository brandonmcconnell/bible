<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 10:35; Isaiah 51:9–10; Isaiah 42:13–14"> To the chief Musician, A Psalm or Song of David. Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered: let them also that hate him flee before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:4; Psalms 37:20; Isaiah 9:18"> As smoke is driven away, so drive them away: as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Psalms 100:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 5:16"> But let the righteous be glad; let them rejoice before God: yes, let them exceedingly rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:26; Isaiah 12:4–6; Psalms 66:4"> Sing to God, sing praises to his name: extol him that rideth upon the heavens by his name JAH, and rejoice before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Jeremiah 49:11; Psalms 82:3–4"> A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is God in his holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:33; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 107:10"> God setteth the solitary in families: he bringeth out those who are bound with chains: but the rebellious dwell in a dry land .</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Judges 5:4; Judges 4:14"> O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people, when thou didst march through the wilderness; Selah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 5:23–25"> The earth shook, the heavens also dropped at the presence of God: even Sinai itself was moved at the presence of God, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Psalms 65:9–13; Psalms 78:24–27"> Thou, O God, didst send a plentiful rain, by which thou didst confirm thy inheritance, when it was weary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:9–10; Psalms 74:19; Matthew 11:5"> Thy congregation hath dwelt therein: thou, O God, hast prepared of thy goodness for the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> The LORD gave the word: great was the company of those that published it .</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> Kings of armies fled apace: and she that tarried at home divided the spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 105:37; Genesis 49:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> Though ye have lain among the pots, yet shall ye be as the wings of a dove covered with silver, and her feathers with yellow gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Psalms 51:7; Isaiah 1:18"> When the Almighty scattered kings in it, it was white as snow in Salmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> The hill of God is as the hill of Bashan; a high hill as the hill of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Psalms 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> Why leap ye, ye lofty hills? this is the hill which God desireth to dwell in; yes, the LORD will dwell in it for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> The chariots of God are twenty thousand, even thousands of angels: the LORD is among them, as in Sinai, in the holy place .</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8–10; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3"> Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led captivity captive: thou hast received gifts for men; yes, for the rebellious also, that the LORD God might dwell among them .</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Lamentations 3:23; Psalms 55:22"> Blessed be the LORD, who daily loadeth us with benefits , even the God of our salvation. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 1:18; 1 Samuel 2:6"> He that is our God is the God of salvation; and to God the LORD belong the issues from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> But God will wound the head of his enemies, and the hairy scalp of such one as goeth on still in his trespasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> The LORD said, I will bring again from Bashan, I will bring my people again from the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Psalms 58:10; 1 Kings 22:38"> That thy foot may be dipped in the blood of thy enemies, and the tongue of thy dogs in the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> They have seen thy goings, O God; even the goings of my God, my King, in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> The singers went before, the players on instruments followed after; among them were the damsels playing with timbrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> Bless ye God in the congregations, even the LORD, from the fountain of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> There is little Benjamin with their ruler, the princes of Judah and their counsel, the princes of Zebulun, and the princes of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> Thy God hath commanded thy strength: strengthen, O God, that which thou hast wrought for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> Because of thy temple at Jerusalem shall kings bring presents to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> Rebuke the company of spearmen, the multitude of the bulls, with the calves of the people, till every one shall submit himself with pieces of silver: scatter thou the people that delight in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> Princes shall come out of Egypt; Cush shall soon stretch out her hands to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> Sing to God, ye kingdoms of the earth; O sing praises to the LORD; Selah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Psalms 68:4"> To him that rideth upon the heavens of heavens, which were of old; lo, he doth send out his voice, and that a mighty voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 19:6; 2 Peter 1:17; 1 Chronicles 16:28–29"> Ascribe ye strength to God: his excellence is over Israel, and his strength is in the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13"> O God, thou art terrible out of thy holy places: the God of Israel is he that giveth strength and power to his people. Blessed be God.</span>
