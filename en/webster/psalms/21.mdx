<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> For thou hast met him with the blessings of goodness: thou hast set a crown of pure gold on his head.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalms 45:7; Psalms 16:11"> For thou hast made him most blessed for ever: thou hast made him exceeding glad with thy countenance.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> Therefore shalt thou make them turn their back, when thou shalt make ready thy arrows upon thy strings against the face of them.</span>
