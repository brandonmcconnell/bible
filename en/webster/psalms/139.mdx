<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> Thou compassest my path and my lying down, and art acquainted with all my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalms 50:19–21"> For there is not a word on my tongue, but lo, O LORD, thou knowest it altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> Thou hast beset me behind and before, and laid thy hand upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; it is high, I cannot attain to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 1:10"> Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> If I ascend into heaven, thou art there: if I make my bed in hell, behold, thou art there .</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 19:6; Psalms 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> If I take the wings of the morning, and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 73:23"> Even there shall thy hand lead me, and thy right hand shall hold me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; Psalms 94:7"> If I say, Surely the darkness shall cover me; even the night shall be light about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> Yes, the darkness hideth not from thee; but the night shineth as the day: the darkness and the light are both alike to thee .</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 44:2"> For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 111:2; Psalms 104:24"> I will praise thee: for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: wonderful are thy works; and that my soul well knoweth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 139:13; Job 10:8–11"> My substance was not hid from thee when I was made in secret, and curiously formed in the lowest parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 56:8; Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12"> Thy eyes saw my substance, yet being imperfect; and in thy book all my members were written, which in continuance were fashioned, when as yet there was none of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Isaiah 55:8–9"> How precious also are thy thoughts to me, O God! how great is the sum of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 139:3; Psalms 40:5"> If I should count them, they are more in number than the sand: when I awake, I am still with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Psalms 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> Surely thou wilt slay the wicked, O God: depart from me therefore, ye bloody men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; Psalms 74:18"> For they speak against thee wickedly, and thy enemies take thy name in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Psalms 31:6; Psalms 26:5"> Do not I hate them, O LORD, that hate thee? and am not I grieved with those that rise up against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> I hate them with perfect hatred: I count them my enemies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Jeremiah 17:9–10; Psalms 25:8–9"> And see if there is any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.</span>
