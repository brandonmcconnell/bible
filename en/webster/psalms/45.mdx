<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 49:3; 2 Samuel 23:2; Job 33:3"> To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim, for the sons of Korah, Maschil, A Song of loves. My heart is inditing a good matter: I speak of the things which I have made concerning the king: my tongue is the pen of a ready writer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 4:22; John 7:46; Hebrews 7:26"> Thou art fairer than the children of men: grace is poured into thy lips: therefore God hath blessed thee for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Isaiah 49:2"> Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O most mighty, with thy glory and thy majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 65:5; Revelation 6:2; Revelation 19:11"> And in thy majesty ride prosperously because of truth and meekness and righteousness; and thy right hand shall teach thee terrible things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Numbers 24:8; Psalms 38:2"> Thy arrows are sharp in the heart of the king's enemies; by which the people fall under thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:8–9; Psalms 93:2; John 1:1"> Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: the scepter of thy kingdom is a scepter of justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 1:9; Psalms 21:6; Isaiah 61:1–3"> Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:3; 2 Corinthians 2:14–16; John 19:39"> All thy garments smell of myrrh, and aloes, and cassia, out of the ivory palaces, by which they have made thee glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:19; Revelation 21:9; Ephesians 5:26–27"> Kings daughters were among thy honorable women: upon thy right hand stood the queen in gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10–13; Genesis 12:1; Matthew 10:37"> Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thy ear; forget also thy own people, and thy father's house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Isaiah 54:5; Song of Solomon 4:1–5"> So shall the king greatly desire thy beauty: for he is thy lord; and worship thou him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:29; Isaiah 60:3; Isaiah 49:23"> And the daughter of Tyre shall be there with a gift; even the rich among the people shall entreat thy favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–8; Isaiah 61:10; Romans 3:22"> The king's daughter is all glorious within: her clothing is of wrought gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Song of Solomon 8:13; Exodus 28:39"> She shall be brought to the king in raiment of needlework: the virgins her companions that follow her shall be brought to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 7:15–17; Jude 1:24; Isaiah 55:12–13"> With gladness and rejoicing shall they be brought: they shall enter into the king's palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6"> Instead of thy fathers shall be thy children, whom thou mayest make princes in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Malachi 1:11; Psalms 72:17–19"> I will make thy name to be remembered in all generations: therefore shall the people praise thee for ever and ever.</span>
