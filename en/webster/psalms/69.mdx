<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim, A Psalm of David. Save me, O God; for the waters are come in to my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing: I am come into deep waters, where the floods overflow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> I am weary of my crying: my throat is dried: my eyes fail while I wait for my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 35:19; Psalms 109:3–5"> They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head: they that would destroy me, being my enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored that which I took not away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 38:9; Psalms 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my sins are not hid from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 25:3; Psalms 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> Let not them that wait on thee, O LORD God of hosts, be ashamed for my sake: let not those that seek thee be confounded for my sake, O God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> I am become a stranger to my brethren, and an alien to my mother's children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalms 119:139"> For the zeal of thy house hath eaten me up; and the reproaches of them that reproached thee have fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:13; Psalms 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> When I wept, and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> I made sackcloth also my garment; and I became a proverb to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> They that sit in the gate speak against me; and I was the song of the drunkards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalms 55:16–17"> But as for me, my prayer is to thee, O LORD, in an acceptable time: O God, in the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:7; Psalms 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink: let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> Let not the water-flood overflow me, neither let the deep swallow me up, and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 109:21; Psalms 25:16; Psalms 63:3"> Hear me, O LORD; for thy loving-kindness is good: turn to me according to the multitude of thy tender mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 27:9; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 70:1"> And hide not thy face from thy servant; for I am in trouble: hear me speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:1; Psalms 111:9"> Draw nigh to my soul, and redeem it: deliver me because of my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> Thou hast known my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonor: my adversaries are all before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalms 142:4; Mark 14:50"> Reproach hath broken my heart; and I am full of heaviness: and I looked for some to take pity, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> They gave me also gall for my food; and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> Let their table become a snare before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not; and make their loins continually to shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> Pour out thy indignation upon them, and let thy wrathful anger take hold of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> Let their habitation be desolate; and let none dwell in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> For they persecute him whom thou hast smitten; and they talk to the grief of those whom thou hast wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Add iniquity to their iniquity: and let them not come into thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–16; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 20:1"> But I am poor and sorrowful: let thy salvation, O God, set me up on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 34:3; Psalms 118:28–29"> I will praise the name of God with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 50:13–14; Psalms 50:23"> This also shall please the LORD better than an ox or bullock that hath horns and hoofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:26; Psalms 34:2; John 16:22"> The humble shall see this , and be glad: and your heart shall live that seek God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> For the LORD heareth the poor, and despiseth not his prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 150:6"> Let the heaven and earth praise him, the seas, and every thing that moveth therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalms 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: that they may dwell there, and have it in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 102:28; Psalms 37:29; Acts 2:39"> The seed also of his servants shall inherit it: and they that love his name shall dwell therein.</span>
