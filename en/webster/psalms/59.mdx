<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:11–24; Psalms 18:48; Psalms 57:1"> To the chief Musician, Al-taschith, Michtam of David; when Saul sent, and they watched the house to kill him. Deliver me from my enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:19; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 55:23"> Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 56:6; Psalms 69:4; 1 Samuel 24:11"> For lo, they lie in wait for my soul: the mighty are gathered against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 35:23; Isaiah 51:9; Proverbs 1:16"> They run and prepare themselves without my fault: awake to help me, and behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 84:8; Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:15"> Thou therefore, O LORD God of hosts, the God of Israel, awake to visit all the heathen: be not merciful to any wicked transgressors. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 59:14; Psalms 22:16; 1 Samuel 19:11"> They return at evening: they make a noise like a dog, and go about the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 15:2; Psalms 10:11"> Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords are in their lips: for who, say they , doth hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Psalms 37:13; Psalms 2:4"> But thou, O LORD, shalt laugh at them; thou shalt have all the heathen in derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 62:2; Psalms 27:14"> Because of his strength will I wait upon thee: for God is my defense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:7; Psalms 59:17; 1 Peter 5:10"> The God of my mercy will succor me: God will let me see my desire upon my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Revelation 9:6; Job 40:12"> Slay them not, lest my people forget: scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O LORD our shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 18:7; Psalms 10:7"> For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips let them even be taken in their pride: and for cursing and lying which they speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 7:9; Ezekiel 39:7"> Consume them in wrath, consume them , that they may not be : and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob to the ends of the earth. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 59:6"> And at evening let them return; and let them make a noise like a dog, and go about the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 15:23; Psalms 109:10; Job 30:1–7"> Let them wander up and down for food, and grudge if they are not satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 21:13; Ephesians 3:20; Romans 15:9"> But I will sing of thy power; yes, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast been my defense and refuge in the day of my trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 59:9–10; Psalms 46:1; Psalms 18:1"> To thee, O my strength, will I sing: for God is my defense, and the God of my mercy.</span>
