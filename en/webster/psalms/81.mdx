<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:7; Psalms 66:1; Psalms 100:1–2"> To the chief Musician upon Gittith, A Psalm of Asaph. Sing aloud to God our strength: make a joyful noise to the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 149:1–3; Psalms 92:3; Colossians 3:16"> Take a psalm, and bring hither the timbrel, the pleasant harp with the psaltery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 10:1–10; Numbers 15:3; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> Blow the trumpet in the new moon, in the time appointed, on our solemn feast day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For this was a statute for Israel, and a law of the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 114:1; Deuteronomy 28:49; 1 Corinthians 14:21–22"> This he ordained in Joseph for a testimony, when he went out through the land of Egypt: where I heard a language that I understood not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 10:27; Exodus 6:6"> I removed his shoulder from the burden: his hands were delivered from the pots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 19:19; Numbers 20:13; Exodus 2:23"> Thou calledst in trouble, and I delivered thee; I answered thee in the secret place of thunder: I proved thee at the waters of Meribah. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:7; John 3:11; Isaiah 1:19"> Hear, O my people, and I will testify to thee; O Israel, if thou wilt hearken to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:12; Deuteronomy 32:12; Deuteronomy 6:14"> There shall no strange god be in thee; neither shalt thou worship any strange god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 107:9; Exodus 20:2"> I am the LORD thy God who brought thee out of the land of Egypt: open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 2:11–13"> But my people would not hearken to my voice; and Israel would not obey me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:24; Jeremiah 7:24; Isaiah 30:1"> So I gave them up to their own hearts lust: and they walked in their own counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 5:29; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> O that my people had hearkened to me, and Israel had walked in my ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 2:20–23; Numbers 14:9"> I should soon have subdued their enemies, and turned my hand against their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 1:30; Exodus 20:5"> The haters of the LORD should have submitted themselves to him: and their time should have endured for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 147:14; 1 Samuel 14:25–26"> He would have fed them also with the finest of the wheat: and with honey out of the rock I should have satisfied thee.</span>
