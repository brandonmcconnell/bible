<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:34; 2 Samuel 22:35; Psalms 18:2"> A Psalm of David. Blessed be the LORD my strength, who teacheth my hands to war, and my fingers to fight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:2–3; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:2"> My goodness, and my fortress; my high tower, and my deliverer; my shield, and he in whom I trust; who subdueth my people under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Job 7:17; Hebrews 2:6"> LORD, what is man, that thou takest knowledge of him! or the son of man, that thou makest account of him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:11; Psalms 39:5–6; Psalms 109:23"> Man is like to vanity: his days are as a shadow that passeth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:32; Psalms 18:9; Exodus 19:18"> Bow thy heavens, O LORD, and come down: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Psalms 18:13–14; Deuteronomy 32:42"> Cast forth lightning, and scatter them: shoot thy arrows, and destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Psalms 18:16; Psalms 54:3"> Send thy hand from above; rid me, and deliver me out of great waters, from the hand of strange children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 41:6; Psalms 62:4"> Whose mouth speaketh vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:2–3; Psalms 40:3; Psalms 149:1"> I will sing a new song to thee, O God: upon a psaltery and an instrument of ten strings will I sing praises to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:50; Psalms 140:7; Psalms 33:16–18"> It is he that giveth salvation to kings: who delivereth David his servant from the hurtful sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:7–8; Isaiah 44:20; Psalms 12:2"> Rid me, and deliver me from the hand of strange children, whose mouth speaketh vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of falsehood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; 1 Peter 3:3–6; Isaiah 44:3–5"> That our sons may be as plants grown up in their youth; that our daughters may be as corner stones, polished after the similitude of a palace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:13–14; Leviticus 26:10; Malachi 3:10"> That our granaries may be full, affording all manner of store; that our sheep may bring forth thousands and ten thousands in our streets:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3–5; Deuteronomy 28:7; Isaiah 24:11"> That our oxen may be strong to labor; that there be no breaking in, nor going out; that there be no complaining in our streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 146:5"> Happy is that people, that is in such a case: yes , happy is that people, whose God is the LORD.</span>
