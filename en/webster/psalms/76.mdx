<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Ezekiel 39:3–4; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> There he broke the arrows of the bow, the shield, and the sword, and the battle. Selah.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 3:18; Psalms 13:3; Isaiah 46:12"> The stout-hearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep: and none of the men of might have found their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Psalms 80:16; Exodus 15:21"> At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, both the chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:29–30; Habakkuk 2:20; Judges 5:20"> Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from heaven; the earth feared, and was still,</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Psalms 50:14; Psalms 68:29"> Vow, and pay to the LORD your God: let all that are about him bring presents to him that ought to be feared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:6; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Psalms 68:12"> He shall cut off the spirit of princes: he is terrible to the kings of the earth.</span>
