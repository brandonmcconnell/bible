<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 106:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 1:74; Galatians 3:13"> Let the redeemed of the LORD say so , whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:47; Isaiah 43:5–6; Revelation 5:9"> And gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Ezekiel 34:6"> They wandered in the wilderness in a solitary way; they found no city to dwell in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:2–3; 1 Samuel 30:11–12; Lamentations 2:19"> Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 91:15; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 77:20; Hebrews 11:9–10"> And he led them forth by the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 107:31; Psalms 147:1; Daniel 4:2–3"> Oh that men would praise the LORD for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 34:10"> For he satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Micah 7:8; Romans 6:20–21"> Such as sit in darkness and in the shades of death, being bound in affliction and iron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:43; Psalms 78:40; Romans 1:28"> Because they rebelled against the words of God, and contemned the counsel of the Most High:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Exodus 5:18–19; Exodus 2:23"> Therefore he brought down their heart with labor; they fell down, and there was none to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 107:6; Judges 4:3"> Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he saved them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 42:16; Ephesians 5:8; Psalms 116:16"> He brought them out of darkness and the shades of death, and broke their bands asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:21; Psalms 107:31; Psalms 107:8"> Oh that men would praise the LORD for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; Judges 16:3; Micah 2:13"> For he hath broken the gates of brass, and cut the bars of iron asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:39; Proverbs 1:22; Psalms 38:1–8"> Fools, because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:13; Psalms 88:3; Job 33:19–22"> Their soul abhorreth all manner of food; and they draw near to the gates of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–35; Psalms 34:4–6; Psalms 107:13"> Then they cry to the LORD in their trouble, he saveth them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:8; Psalms 103:3–4; Psalms 147:15"> He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 107:15; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Oh that men would praise the LORD for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Psalms 118:17; Psalms 9:11"> And let them sacrifice the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his works with rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:17; Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 27:26"> They that go down to the sea in ships, that do business on great waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–27; Psalms 95:5; Job 38:8–11"> These see the works of the LORD, and his wonders in the deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4; Matthew 8:24; Psalms 148:8"> For he commandeth, and raiseth the stormy wind, which lifteth up its waves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Psalms 119:28; Isaiah 13:7"> They mount up to the heaven, they go down again to the depths: their soul is melted because of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 29:9; Isaiah 19:3"> They reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, and are at their wit's end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:25; Psalms 107:13; Psalms 107:19"> Then they cry to the LORD in their trouble, and he bringeth them out of their distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Psalms 65:7; Luke 8:23–25"> He maketh the storm a calm, so that the waves of the sea are still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:21"> Then are they glad because they are quiet; so he bringeth them to their desired haven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 107:8; Psalms 103:2; Psalms 107:21"> Oh that men would praise the LORD for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Psalms 22:22; Psalms 99:5"> Let them exalt him also in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the assembly of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Isaiah 42:15; Amos 4:7–8"> He turneth rivers into a wilderness, and the water-springs into dry ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10; Ezekiel 47:11"> A fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 114:8; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 41:17–19"> He turneth the wilderness into a standing water, and dry ground into water-springs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 107:7; Luke 1:53; Acts 17:26"> And there he maketh the hungry to dwell, that they may prepare a city for habitation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 2 Corinthians 9:10; Jeremiah 29:5"> And sow the fields, and plant vineyards, which may yield fruits of increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 1:7; Genesis 12:2; Genesis 17:20"> He blesseth them also, so that they are multiplied greatly; and suffereth not their cattle to decrease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; 2 Kings 10:32; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Again, they are diminished and brought low through oppression, affliction, and sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Job 12:24; Job 12:21; Daniel 4:33"> He poureth contempt upon princes, and causeth them to wander in the wilderness, where there is no way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; Job 21:11; Esther 8:15–17"> Yet he setteth the poor on high from affliction, and maketh him families like a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 63:11; Job 22:19; Romans 3:19"> The righteous shall see it , and rejoice: and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Jeremiah 9:12; Psalms 64:9"> Whoever is wise, and will observe these things , even they shall understand the loving-kindness of the LORD.</span>
