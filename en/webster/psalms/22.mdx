<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> To the chief Musician upon Aijeleth Shahar, A Psalm of David. My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> O my God, I cry in the day-time, but thou hearest not; and in the night season, and am not silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> Our fathers trusted in thee: they trusted, and thou didst deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> They cried to thee, and were delivered: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised by the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalms 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> All they that see me deride me: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalms 91:14"> He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him: let him deliver him, seeing he delighted in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalms 71:17"> But thou art he that brought me forth into life: thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother's breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> I was cast upon thee from my birth: thou art my God from the time I was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:12; Psalms 72:12; Psalms 10:1"> Be not far from me; for trouble is near; for there is none to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalms 68:30; Acts 4:27"> Many bulls have compassed me: strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> They gaped upon me with their mouths, as a ravening and a roaring lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint: my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 69:21"> My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> I may number all my bones: they look and stare upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Psalms 40:13; Psalms 40:17"> But be not thou far from me, O LORD: O my strength, haste thee to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalms 17:13"> Deliver my soul from the sword; my darling from the power of the dog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> Save me from the lion's mouth: for thou hast heard me from the horns of the unicorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalms 40:9–10"> I will declare thy name to my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalms 107:1–2"> Ye that fear the LORD, praise him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; and fear him, all ye the seed of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalms 116:3–6"> For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; neither hath he hid his face from him; but when he cried to him, he heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:9–10; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 111:1"> My praise shall be of thee in the great congregation: I will pay my vows before them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 69:32; Psalms 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> The meek shall eat and be satisfied: they shall praise the LORD that seek him: your heart shall live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalms 2:8"> All the ends of the world shall remember and turn to the LORD: and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalms 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> For the kingdom is the LORD'S: and he is the governor among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 45:12"> All they that are fat upon earth shall eat and worship: all they that go down to the dust shall bow before him: and none can keep alive his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalms 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the LORD for a generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 78:6; Psalms 86:9; Romans 1:17"> They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness to a people that shall be born, that he hath done this .</span>
