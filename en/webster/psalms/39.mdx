<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalms 141:3; James 1:26"> To the chief Musician, even to Jeduthun, A Psalm of David. I said, I will take heed to my ways, that I sin not with my tongue: I will keep my mouth with a bridle, while the wicked is before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> I was dumb with silence, I held my peace, even from good; and my sorrow was stirred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> My heart was hot within me, while I was musing the fire burned: then I spoke with my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> LORD, make me to know my end, and the measure of my days, what it is ; that I may know how frail I am .</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> Behold, thou hast made my days as a hand-breadth; and my age is as nothing before thee: verily every man at his best state is altogether vanity. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Surely every man walketh in a vain show: surely they are disquieted in vain: he heapeth up riches , and knoweth not who shall gather them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> And now, LORD, what wait I for? my hope is in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> Deliver me from all my transgressions: make me not the reproach of the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> I was dumb, I opened not my mouth; because thou didst it .</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> Remove thy stroke away from me; I am consumed by the blow of thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> When thou with rebukes dost correct man for iniquity, thou makest his beauty to consume away like a moth: surely every man is vanity. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear to my cry; hold not thy peace at my tears: for I am a stranger with thee, and a sojourner, as all my fathers were .</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> O spare me, that I may recover strength, before I go hence, and be no more.</span>
