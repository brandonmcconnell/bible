<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–6; Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 6:20"> To the chief Musician for the sons of Korah, Maschil. We have heard with our ears, O God, our fathers have told us, what work thou didst in their days, in the times of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> How thou didst drive out the heathen with thy hand, and didst plant them; how thou didst afflict the people, and cast them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> For they obtained not the land in possession by their own sword, neither did their own arm save them: but thy right hand, and thy arm, and the light of thy countenance, because thou hadst a favor to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 74:12; Psalms 42:8; Psalms 149:2"> Thou art my King, O God: command deliverances for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:13; Psalms 60:12; Psalms 118:10–13"> Through thee will we push down our enemies: through thy name we will tread them under that rise up against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Hosea 1:7; 1 Samuel 17:47"> For I will not trust in my bow, neither shall my sword save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 136:24"> But thou hast saved us from our enemies, and hast put them to shame that hated us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremiah 9:24"> In God we boast all the day long, and praise thy name for ever. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 60:10; Psalms 60:1"> But thou hast cast off, and put us to shame; and goest not forth with our armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> Thou makest us to turn back from the enemy: and they who hate us plunder for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> Thou hast given us like sheep appointed for food; and hast scattered us among the heathen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Isaiah 52:3–4"> Thou sellest thy people for naught, and dost not increase thy wealth by their price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Psalms 80:6; Psalms 89:41"> Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbors, a scorn and a derision to them that are around us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 19:21; Job 16:4"> Thou makest us a by-word among the heathen, a shaking of the head among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 69:7; Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 89:45"> My confusion is continually before me, and the shame of my face hath covered me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Psalms 74:10; Psalms 74:18"> For the voice of him that reproacheth and blasphemeth; by reason of the enemy and avenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> All this is come upon us; yet we have not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt falsely in thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:51; Psalms 119:157; Psalms 78:57"> Our heart is not turned back, neither have our steps declined from thy way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 3:5; Psalms 23:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> Though thou hast severely broke us in the place of dragons, and covered us with the shades of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 81:9; Job 11:13; Deuteronomy 6:14"> If we have forgotten the name of our God, or stretched out our hands to a strange god;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 23:24; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Will not God search out this? for he knoweth the secrets of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:36; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 44:11"> Yes, for thy sake are we killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:65; Psalms 7:6; Psalms 35:23"> Awake, why sleepest thou, O LORD? arise, cast us not off for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:24; Deuteronomy 32:20; Psalms 42:9"> Why hidest thou thy face, and forgettest our affliction and our oppression?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:25; Lamentations 4:5; Isaiah 51:23"> For our soul is bowed down to the dust: our belly cleaveth to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 130:7–8; Psalms 25:22; Psalms 26:11"> Arise for our help, and redeem us for thy mercies' sake.</span>
