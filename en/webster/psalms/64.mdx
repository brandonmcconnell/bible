<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 143:1–3; Lamentations 3:55–56"> To the chief Musician, a Psalm of David. Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 143:9; Psalms 59:2; Psalms 109:2–3"> Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:7; James 3:6–8"> Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:19; Psalms 11:2; 1 Peter 2:22–23"> That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who will see them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9–10; Matthew 26:59; Psalms 5:9"> They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them , and the heart, is deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Proverbs 6:15"> But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13; Psalms 140:9"> So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:10; Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 40:3"> And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 32:11–1; Psalms 40:3; 1 Corinthians 1:30–31"> The righteous shall be glad in the LORD, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.</span>
