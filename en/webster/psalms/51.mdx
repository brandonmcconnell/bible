<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 1:7–9; Psalms 51:7; Hebrews 10:21–22"> Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 59:12; Psalms 38:18"> For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13–14; Luke 15:21; Genesis 20:6"> Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight: that thou mayest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Job 14:4; Psalms 58:3"> Behold, I was shapen in iniquity; and in sin did my mother conceive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Peter 3:4"> Behold, thou desirest truth in the inward parts: and in the hidden part thou shalt make me to know wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Isaiah 1:18; Hebrews 9:13–14"> Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:17–18; Hosea 6:1–2; Matthew 5:4"> Make me to hear joy and gladness; that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:1"> Hide thy face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 4:22–24"> Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Kings 13:23; Isaiah 63:10–11"> Cast me not away from thy presence; and take not thy holy spirit from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 13:5; Isaiah 61:10"> Restore to me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:32; Acts 26:18–20; Acts 3:19"> Then will I teach transgressors thy ways; and sinners shall be converted to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; 2 Samuel 12:9; Psalms 26:9"> Deliver me from blood-guiltiness, O God, thou God of my salvation: and my tongue shall sing aloud of thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 63:3–5; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 9:14"> O LORD, open thou my lips; and my mouth shall show forth thy praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 40:6; Hosea 6:6; Hebrews 10:5–6"> For thou desirest not sacrifice: else would I give it : thou delightest not in burnt-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:2; Psalms 147:3"> The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 122:6–9; Luke 12:32"> Do good in thy good pleasure to Zion: build thou the walls of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 4:5; Ephesians 5:2; Malachi 3:3"> Then shalt thou be pleased with the sacrifices of righteousness, with burnt-offering and whole burnt-offering: then shall they offer bullocks upon thy altar.</span>
