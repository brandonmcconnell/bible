<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:2; Psalms 71:1–3"> To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David. In thee, O LORD, do I put my trust; let me never be ashamed: deliver me in thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 71:2–3; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 86:1"> Bow down thy ear to me; deliver me speedily: be thou my strong rock, for a house of defense to save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 143:10–11; Luke 1:79; Psalms 79:9"> For thou art my rock and my fortress; therefore for thy name's sake lead me, and guide me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:15; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 19:14"> Pull me out of the net that they have laid privily for me: for thou art my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 23:46; Acts 7:59; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Into thy hand I commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O LORD God of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 2:8; Psalms 24:4; Jeremiah 10:15"> I have hated them that regard lying vanities: but I trust in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 119:153"> I will be glad and rejoice in thy mercy: for thou hast considered my trouble; thou hast known my soul in adversities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 36:16; Deuteronomy 32:30; Psalms 18:19"> And hast not shut me up into the hand of the enemy: thou hast set my foot in a large room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 73:26"> Have mercy upon me, O LORD, for I am in trouble: my eye is consumed with grief, yes , my soul and my belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 88:15; Psalms 32:3–4; Psalms 38:3"> For my life is spent with grief, and my years with sighing: my strength faileth because of my iniquity, and my bones are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18"> I was a reproach among all my enemies, but especially among my neighbors, and a fear to my acquaintance: they that saw me without fled from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; Psalms 2:9; Romans 9:21–22"> I am forgotten as a dead man out of mind: I am like a broken vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Matthew 27:1; Lamentations 2:22"> For I have heard the slander of many: fear was on every side: while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 18:2; Psalms 43:5"> But I trusted in thee, O LORD: I said, Thou art my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:1–8; John 17:1"> My times are in thy hand: deliver me from the hand of my enemies, and from them that persecute me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; Psalms 6:4; Daniel 9:9"> Make thy face to shine upon thy servant: save me for thy mercies' sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 25:2–3; Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 34:5"> Let me not be ashamed, O LORD; for I have called upon thee: let the wicked be ashamed, and let them be silent in the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Psalms 94:4; 1 Samuel 2:3"> Let the lying lips be put to silence; which speak grievous things proudly and contemptuously against the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; Lamentations 3:23–25"> Oh how great is thy goodness, which thou hast laid up for them that fear thee; which thou hast wrought for them that trust in thee before the sons of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 27:5; Psalms 32:7; Psalms 91:1–4"> Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the pride of man: thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:7–13; Psalms 17:7; 1 Peter 2:9"> Blessed be the LORD: for he hath showed me his wonderful kindness in a strong city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 116:11; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 88:16"> For I said in my haste, I am cut off from before thy eyes: nevertheless thou heardest the voice of my supplications when I cried to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Psalms 94:2; Psalms 145:20"> O love the LORD, all ye his saints: for the LORD preserveth the faithful, and plentifully rewardeth the proud doer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 146:5; Colossians 1:11"> Be of good courage, and he will strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the LORD.</span>
