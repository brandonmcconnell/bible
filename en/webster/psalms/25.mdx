<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Colossians 3:12; Luke 1:50"> Remember, O LORD, thy tender mercies and thy loving kindnesses; for they have been ever of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 38:17; Job 13:26"> Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions: according to thy mercy remember thou me for thy goodness' sake, O LORD.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 141:8; Psalms 124:7–8; Psalms 31:4"> My eyes are ever towards the LORD; for he will pluck my feet out of the net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 86:16; Daniel 9:17; Micah 7:19"> Turn thee to me, and have mercy upon me; for I am desolate and afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:6; Psalms 34:19; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> The troubles of my heart are enlarged: O bring thou me out of my distresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Psalms 119:132; Psalms 51:8–9"> Look upon my affliction and my pain; and forgive all my sins.</span>
