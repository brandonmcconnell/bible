<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:1; Psalms 89:8; Psalms 119:90"> Maschil of Ethan the Ezrahite. I will sing of the mercies of the LORD for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; Psalms 89:5"> For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness wilt thou establish in the very heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 2 Samuel 7:10–16; Psalms 89:34–35"> I have made a covenant with my chosen, I have sworn to David my servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 132:12"> Thy seed will I establish for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 19:1; Isaiah 44:23"> And the heavens shall praise thy wonders, O LORD; thy faithfulness also in the congregation of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:8; Psalms 71:19; Psalms 113:5"> For who in the heaven can be compared to the LORD? who among the sons of the mighty can be likened to the LORD?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Psalms 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> God is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints, and to be had in reverence by all them that are about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:19; 1 Samuel 2:2; Jeremiah 32:17"> O LORD God of hosts, who is a strong LORD like to thee? or to thy faithfulness around thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when its waves arise, thou stillest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 87:4; Psalms 68:30; Psalms 144:6"> Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; thou hast scattered thy enemies with thy strong arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 115:16"> The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine: as for the world, and the fullness of it, thou hast founded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> The north and the south thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; Psalms 89:10; Psalms 62:11"> Thou hast a mighty arm: strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 45:6–7"> Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Job 29:3; Psalms 4:6"> Blessed are the people that know the joyful sound: they shall walk, O LORD, in the light of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> In thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 148:14"> For thou art the glory of their strength: and in thy favor our horn shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 47:9; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 33:22"> For the LORD is our defense; and the Holy One of Israel is our king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; 1 Kings 11:34; Psalms 89:3"> Then thou didst speak in vision to thy holy one, and say, I have laid help upon one that is mighty; I have exalted one chosen out of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; Isaiah 61:1–3; 1 Samuel 16:1"> I have found David my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 18:32–39; Psalms 89:13"> With whom my hand shall be established: my arm also shall strengthen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:9; 2 Samuel 7:10; Matthew 4:1–10"> The enemy shall not exact upon him; nor the son of wickedness afflict him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 3:1; Psalms 18:40"> And I will beat down his foes before his face, and afflict them that hate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 91:14"> But my faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Psalms 2:8; Psalms 72:8–11"> I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 95:1"> He shall cry to me, Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Colossians 1:18; Psalms 2:7"> Also I will make him my first-born, higher than the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 33:20–21; Acts 13:32–34"> My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant shall stand fast with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 89:4; Deuteronomy 11:21; Psalms 89:36"> His seed also will I make to endure for ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 119:53"> If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 55:20"> If they break my statutes, and keep not my commandments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; Hebrews 12:6–11; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Then will I visit their transgression with the rod, and their iniquity with stripes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; 2 Samuel 7:15; Lamentations 3:31–32"> Nevertheless my loving-kindness will I not utterly take from him, nor suffer my faithfulness to fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35; James 1:17"> My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that hath gone out of my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Hebrews 6:13; Titus 1:2"> Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 72:5; Psalms 89:29; Isaiah 59:21"> His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 104:19"> It shall be established for ever as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 32:19; Psalms 106:40"> But thou hast cast off and abhorred, thou hast been wroth with thy anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Lamentations 5:16; Psalms 74:7; Psalms 89:34–36"> Thou hast made void the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned his crown by casting it to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 80:12; Lamentations 2:2; Lamentations 2:5"> Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast brought his strong holds to ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Lamentations 5:1; Ezekiel 5:14–15"> All that pass by the way plunder him: he is a reproach to his neighbors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Revelation 11:10; Deuteronomy 28:43"> Thou hast set up the right hand of his adversaries; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:8–12; Numbers 14:42; Psalms 44:10"> Thou hast also turned the edge of his sword, and hast not made him to stand in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 4:1–2; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–10; Psalms 89:39"> Thou hast made his glory to cease, and cast his throne down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 44:15; Micah 7:10"> The days of his youth hast thou shortened: thou hast covered him with shame. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 78:63; Psalms 88:14"> How long, LORD, wilt thou hide thyself? for ever? shall thy wrath burn like fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 10:9; Job 7:7"> Remember how short my time is: why hast thou made all men in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Hebrews 11:5; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> What man is he that liveth, and shall not see death? shall he deliver his soul from the hand of the grave? Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:15; Hebrews 7:21; Isaiah 55:3"> LORD, where are thy former loving-kindnesses, which thou didst swear to David in thy truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 74:22; Psalms 74:18; Psalms 69:9"> Remember, LORD, the reproach of thy servants; how I do bear in my bosom the reproach of all the mighty people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14–16; Psalms 74:10; 2 Samuel 16:7–8"> With which thy enemies have reproached, O LORD; with which they have reproached the footsteps of thy anointed</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Psalms 41:13; 1 Timothy 1:17"> Blessed be the LORD for evermore. Amen and amen.</span>
