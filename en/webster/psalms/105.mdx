<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 145:11–12; Romans 10:13"> O give thanks to the LORD; call upon his name: make known his deeds among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:27; Psalms 98:5; Isaiah 12:5–6"> Sing to him, sing psalms to him: talk ye of all his wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:29"> Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 27:8; Zephaniah 2:2–3; 2 Chronicles 6:41"> Seek the LORD, and his strength: seek his face evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:11; Psalms 119:13; Deuteronomy 32:7"> Remember his wonderful works that he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; John 15:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> O ye seed of Abraham his servant, ye children of Jacob his chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:9; Deuteronomy 26:17–18; Exodus 20:2"> He is the LORD our God: his judgments are in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 106:45; Psalms 111:5"> He hath remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded to a thousand generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:2; Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 26:3"> Which covenant he made with Abraham, and his oath to Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; 2 Samuel 23:5; Genesis 28:13–15"> And confirmed the same to Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 15:18; Psalms 78:55"> Saying, To thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Hebrews 11:9"> When they were a few men in number; yes, very few, and strangers in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When they went from one nation to another, from one kingdom to another people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:5; Exodus 7:16–17; Genesis 26:14–33"> He suffered no man to do them wrong: yes, he reproved kings for their sakes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 26:11; Genesis 20:7"> Saying , Touch not my anointed, and do my prophets no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Genesis 41:54; Isaiah 3:1"> Moreover he called for a famine upon the land: he broke the whole staff of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 45:4–5"> He sent a man before them, even Joseph, who was sold for a servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 16:24; Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:15"> Whose feet they hurt with fetters: he was laid in iron:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Proverbs 21:1; Genesis 40:20–21"> Until the time that his word came: the word of the LORD tried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:14"> The king sent and loosed him; even the ruler of the people, and let him go free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:40–44; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 41:55"> He made him lord of his house, and ruler of all his substance:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:38"> To bind his princes at his pleasure; and teach his senators wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 7:11–15; Acts 13:17; Psalms 78:51"> Israel also came into Egypt; and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7–9; Acts 7:17"> And he increased his people greatly; and made them stronger than their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 9:16"> He turned their heart to hate his people, to deal subtilly with his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 29:5–46; Exodus 28:12"> He sent Moses his servant; and Aaron whom he had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:43–51; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Psalms 105:23"> They showed his signs among them, and wonders in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:2"> He sent darkness, and made it dark; and they rebelled not against his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 7:20–21; Psalms 78:44; Isaiah 50:2"> He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:13–14; Exodus 8:3–14; Psalms 78:45"> Their land brought forth frogs in abundance, in the chambers of their kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 8:16–18; Exodus 8:21–24; Isaiah 7:18"> He spoke, and there came divers sorts of flies, and lice in all their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:47–48; Exodus 9:18–28; Revelation 8:7"> He gave them hail for rain, and flaming fire in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Revelation 9:4"> He smote their vines also and their fig-trees; and broke the trees of their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:4–7"> He spoke, and the locusts came, and caterpillars, and that without number,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And ate up all the herbs in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Exodus 12:29–30; Psalms 78:51"> He smote also all the first-born in their land, the chief of all their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Exodus 3:22; Acts 13:17"> He brought them forth also with silver and gold: and there was not one feeble person among their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 15:16; Genesis 35:5"> Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them fell upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 78:14; Isaiah 4:5"> He spread a cloud for a covering; and fire to give light in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Numbers 11:31–33; Joshua 5:12"> The people asked, and he brought quails, and satisfied them with the bread of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; 1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6"> He opened the rock, and the waters gushed out; they ran in the dry places like a river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Luke 1:72–73; Genesis 12:7"> For he remembered his holy promise, and Abraham his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Acts 7:36; Jeremiah 31:11–12"> And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–11; Joshua 24:13; Psalms 78:55"> And gave them the lands of the heathen: and they inherited the labor of the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Titus 2:14; Ephesians 2:8–10"> That they might observe his statutes, and keep his laws. Praise ye the LORD.</span>
