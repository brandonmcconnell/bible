<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Psalms 66:19; Psalms 77:3"> Attend to me, and hear me: I mourn in my complaint, and make a noise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:7–8; Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11"> Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked: for they cast iniquity upon me, and in wrath they hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Mark 14:33–34; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> My heart is severely pained within me: and the terrors of death have fallen upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Job 21:6; 2 Samuel 15:14"> Fearfulness and trembling have come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Revelation 12:14; Psalms 139:9"> And I said, O that I had wings like a dove! for then I would fly away, and be at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; 2 Samuel 17:21–22; Jeremiah 37:12"> Lo, then I would wander far off, and remain in the wilderness. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 4:6; Matthew 7:25–27; Isaiah 17:12–13"> I would hasten my escape from the windy storm and tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:7; Genesis 11:7–9; Jeremiah 23:14"> Destroy, O LORD, and divide their tongues: for I have seen violence and strife in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 59:14–15; Isaiah 59:6–15; Ezekiel 9:4"> Day and night they go about it upon its walls: mischief also and sorrow are in the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–27; Psalms 10:7; Psalms 5:9"> Wickedness is in the midst of it: deceit and guile depart not from her streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Matthew 26:21–23"> For it was not an enemy that reproached me; then I could have borne it : neither was it he that hated me that magnified himself against me; then I would have hid myself from him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; Micah 7:5; Jeremiah 9:4"> But it was thou, a man my equal, my guide, and my acquaintance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Isaiah 2:3; Ezekiel 33:31"> We took sweet counsel together, and walked to the house of God in company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 59:13; Psalms 64:7; Psalms 9:17"> Let death seize upon them, and let them go down quick into hell: for wickedness is in their dwellings, and among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 109:4; Psalms 73:28"> As for me, I will call upon God: and the LORD will save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Psalms 141:2"> Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he will hear my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:3; Psalms 3:6–7; 2 Samuel 22:1"> He hath delivered my soul in peace from the battle that was against me: for there were many with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Revelation 6:10–11; Jeremiah 48:11"> God will hear and afflict them, even he that abideth of old. Selah. Because they have no changes, therefore they fear not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:1; Psalms 89:34; Psalms 7:4"> He hath put forth his hands against such as are at peace with him: he hath broken his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 28:3; Proverbs 5:3–4"> The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet were they drawn swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 37:5; Philippians 4:6–7"> Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he will sustain thee: he will never suffer the righteous to be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 5:6; Job 15:32"> But thou, O God, wilt bring them down into the pit of destruction: bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee.</span>
