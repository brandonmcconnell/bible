<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19"> There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 10:18; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 98:3"> Their line hath gone out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world. In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Philippians 3:13–14; 1 Corinthians 9:24–26"> Which is as a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, and rejoiceth as a strong man to run a race.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:5; Job 22:14; Colossians 1:23"> His going forth is from the end of the heaven, and his circuit to the ends of it: and there is nothing hid from his heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:130; Psalms 111:7; Colossians 3:16"> The law of the LORD is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; Psalms 12:6; Jeremiah 15:16"> The statutes of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Psalms 119:142; Psalms 119:137–138"> The fear of the LORD is clean, enduring for ever: the judgments of the LORD are true and righteous altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:103; Proverbs 3:13–15; Psalms 119:72"> More to be desired are they than gold, yes, than much fine gold: sweeter also than honey and the honey-comb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 2 John 1:8; Hebrews 11:6–7"> Moreover, by them is thy servant warned: and in keeping of them there is great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 90:8; Job 6:24; Psalms 139:23–24"> Who can understand his errors? cleanse thou me from secret faults .</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:133; Romans 6:12–14; 1 Samuel 25:39"> Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins ; let them not have dominion over me: then shall I be upright, and I shall be innocent from the great transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:34; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 119:108"> Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O LORD, my strength, and my redeemer.</span>
