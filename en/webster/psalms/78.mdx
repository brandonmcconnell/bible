<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:4; Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 74:1"> Maschil of Asaph. Give ear, O my people, to my law: incline your ear to the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 4:34; Matthew 13:11–13"> I will open my mouth in a parable: I will utter dark sayings of old:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> Which we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 145:4–6; Deuteronomy 4:9"> We will not hide them from their children, showing to the generation to come the praises of the LORD, and his strength, and his wonderful works that he hath done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 4:45"> For he established a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known to their children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 102:18; Joel 1:3; Esther 9:28"> That the generation to come might know them , even the children who should be born; who should arise and declare them to their children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Revelation 14:12; 1 John 5:3"> That they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:37; Exodus 32:9; 2 Kings 17:14"> And might not be as their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation; a generation that set not their heart aright, and whose spirit was not steadfast with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; 1 Samuel 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> The children of Ephraim, being armed, and carrying bows, turned back in the day of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> They kept not the covenant of God, and refused to walk in his law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:13; Deuteronomy 32:18; Psalms 106:21–22"> And forgot his works, and his wonders that he had shown them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 19:11"> Marvelous things did he in the sight of their fathers, in the land of Egypt, in the field of Zoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> He divided the sea, and caused them to pass through; and he made the waters to stand as a heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:39; Exodus 14:24; Exodus 13:21–22"> In the day-time also he led them with a cloud, and all the night with a light of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:41; Numbers 20:11; Exodus 17:6"> He cleaved the rocks in the wilderness, and gave them drink as out of the great depths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Numbers 20:10–11; Deuteronomy 8:15"> He brought streams also out of the rock, and caused waters to run down like rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Hebrews 3:16–19; Deuteronomy 9:12–22"> And they sinned yet more against him by provoking the Most High in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> And they tempted God in their heart by asking food for their desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 11:4; Exodus 16:3"> Yes, they spoke against God; they said, Can God furnish a table in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Numbers 11:21–23; Psalms 78:41"> Behold, he smote the rock, that the waters gushed out, and the streams overflowed; can he give bread also? can he provide flesh for his people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Numbers 11:1–3"> Therefore the LORD heard this , and was wroth: so a fire was kindled against Jacob, and anger also came up against Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:18–19; Hebrews 3:12; Jude 1:5"> Because they believed not in God, and trusted not in his salvation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10; Psalms 33:9"> Though he had commanded the clouds from above, and opened the doors of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; Psalms 105:40; John 6:31–71"> And had rained down manna upon them to eat, and had given them of the corn of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Matthew 15:37; Psalms 103:20"> Man ate angels' food: he sent them food to the full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 11:31; Psalms 135:7"> He caused an east wind to blow in the heaven: and by his power he brought in the south wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 11:18–19; Exodus 16:12–13; Numbers 11:32"> He rained flesh also upon them as dust, and feathered fowls like as the sand of the sea:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he let it fall in the midst of their camp, around their habitations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 11:19–20"> So they ate, and were well filled: for he gave them their own desire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 11:33–34; Luke 16:19–23; Numbers 22:20–22"> They were not estranged from their desire: but while their meat was yet in their mouths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> The wrath of God came upon them, and slew the fattest of them, and smote down the chosen men of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 78:22; Psalms 78:11"> For all this they sinned still, and believed not for his wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 14:35; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65"> Therefore their days did he consume in vanity, and their years in trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Numbers 21:7; Judges 3:12–15"> When he slew them, then they sought him: and they returned and inquired early after God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 41:14; Exodus 15:13"> And they remembered that God was their rock, and the high God their redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 29:13; Hosea 11:12"> Nevertheless they flattered him with their mouth, and they lied to him with their tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 8:21; Psalms 78:8; Psalms 51:10"> For their heart was not right with him, neither were they steadfast in his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Numbers 14:18–20; Ezekiel 20:17"> But he, being full of compassion, forgave their iniquity, and destroyed them not: yes, many a time he turned his anger away, and did not stir up all his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 6:3; James 4:14; Job 7:7"> For he remembered that they were but flesh; a wind that passeth away, and cometh not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 95:8–10; Hebrews 3:15–17"> How oft did they provoke him in the wilderness, and grieve him in the desert!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Hebrews 3:8–11; Numbers 14:22; Acts 7:39"> Yes, they turned back and tempted God, and limited the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 8:34; Psalms 78:11; Jeremiah 32:21"> They remembered not his hand, nor the day when he delivered them from the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:22; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 3:19–20"> How he had wrought his signs in Egypt, and his wonders in the field of Zoan:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:29; Revelation 16:3–6; Exodus 7:17–21"> And had turned their rivers into blood; and their floods, that they could not drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 8:21–24; Psalms 105:30–31; Exodus 8:2–15"> He sent divers sorts of flies among them, which devoured them; and frogs, which destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 105:34–35; Amos 7:1–2"> He gave also their increase to the caterpillar, and their labor to the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 105:32–33; Exodus 9:18–34"> He destroyed their vines with hail, and their sycamore trees with frost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 9:19; Exodus 9:28"> He gave up their cattle also to the hail, and their flocks to hot thunderbolts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Exodus 12:13; 1 Kings 22:21–22; Lamentations 4:11"> He cast upon them the fierceness of his anger, wrath, and indignation, and trouble, by sending evil angels among them .</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; 2 Peter 2:4–5; Romans 8:32"> He made a way to his anger; he spared not their soul from death, but gave their life over to the pestilence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 135:8; Psalms 106:22"> And smote all the first-born in Egypt; the chief of their strength in the tabernacles of Ham:</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 77:20; John 10:11–42"> But made his own people to go forth like sheep, and guided them in the wilderness like a flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Exodus 14:19–20; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:27–28"> And he led them on safely, so that they feared not: but the sea overwhelmed their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Exodus 15:17; Isaiah 11:9"> And he brought them to the border of his sanctuary, even to this mountain, which his right hand had purchased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Joshua 13:7; Psalms 44:2; Nehemiah 9:22–25"> He cast out the heathen also before them, and divided them an inheritance by line, and made the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Judges 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 32:15–21; Psalms 78:40–41"> Yet they tempted and provoked the most high God, and kept not his testimonies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Ezekiel 20:27–28; Psalms 78:8"> But turned back, and dealt unfaithfully like their fathers: they were turned aside like a deceitful bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Leviticus 26:30; 1 Kings 12:31"> For they provoked him to anger with their high places, and moved him to jealousy with their graven images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Lamentations 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:19"> When God heard this , he was wroth, and greatly abhorred Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:4–11; Jeremiah 26:6–9"> So that he forsook the tabernacle of Shiloh, the tent which he placed among men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Samuel 4:21–2; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And delivered his strength into captivity, and his glory into the enemy's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Psalms 89:38; Isaiah 64:9; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> He gave over his people also to the sword; and was wroth with his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Jeremiah 16:9; Deuteronomy 32:22"> The fire consumed their young men; and their maidens were not given to marriage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:17; Job 27:15; Ezekiel 24:23"> Their priests fell by the sword; and their widows made no lamentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> Then the LORD awaked as one out of sleep, and like a mighty man that shouteth by reason of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 23:40; Job 40:12"> And he smote his enemies in the hinder part: he put them to a perpetual reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 2 Samuel 6:2; 2 Samuel 6:17"> Moreover he refused the tabernacle of Joseph, and chose not the tribe of Ephraim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 87:2; Psalms 132:12–14; Ruth 4:17–22"> But chose the tribe of Judah, the mount Zion which he loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Job 26:7; 1 Chronicles 29:19; 1 Samuel 2:8"> And he built his sanctuary like high palaces , like the earth which he hath established for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11–12; Acts 13:22; Exodus 3:1"> He chose David also his servant, and took him from the sheep-folds:</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 11:2; Ezekiel 34:23–24"> From following the ewes great with young he brought him to feed Jacob his people, and Israel his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 2 Timothy 2:15; Isaiah 11:2–4"> So he fed them according to the integrity of his heart; and guided them by the skillfulness of his hands.</span>
