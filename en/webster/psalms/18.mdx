<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:1–51; Colossians 1:11; 1 John 4:19"> To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD, who spoke to the LORD the words of this song in the day that the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul: And he said, I will love thee, O LORD, my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 19:14"> The LORD is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 91:15; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 62:8"> I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Matthew 27:24–25; Mark 14:33–34"> The sorrows of death compassed me, and the floods of ungodly men made me afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 88:3–8; Acts 2:24"> The sorrows of hell encompassed me: the snares of death seized me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; 2 Samuel 22:7; Psalms 34:15"> In my distress I called upon the LORD, and cried to my God: he heard my voice out of his temple, and my cry came before him, even into his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Psalms 68:7–8; Acts 16:25–26"> Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations also of the hills moved and were shaken, because he was wroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 11:6; Nahum 1:5–6"> There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–15; Deuteronomy 33:26; 2 Samuel 22:10"> He bowed the heavens also, and came down: and darkness was under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; 2 Samuel 22:11–12; Psalms 99:1"> And he rode upon a cherub, and flew; yes, he flew upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Deuteronomy 4:11; Psalms 91:1"> He made darkness his secret place; his pavilion around him were dark waters and thick clouds of the skies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:21; Matthew 17:2"> At the brightness that was before him his thick clouds passed, hail stones and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:7; 1 Samuel 7:10; Psalms 140:10"> The LORD also thundered in the heavens, and the Highest gave his voice; hail stones and coals of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:6; Habakkuk 3:11; Numbers 24:8"> Yes, he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; and he shot out lightnings, and discomfited them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 106:9; Exodus 15:8; Micah 6:2"> Then the channels of waters were seen, and the foundations of the world were uncovered at thy rebuke, O LORD, at the blast of the breath of thy nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:17; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 57:3"> He sent from above, he took me, he drew me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14–15; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 142:6"> He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them who hated me: for they were too strong for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10–14; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 46:11"> They attacked me in the day of my calamity: but the LORD was my stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 118:5; Psalms 31:8; Psalms 18:36"> He brought me forth also in a large place; he delivered me, because he delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Matthew 6:4; Psalms 58:11"> The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:102; Psalms 17:4; Psalms 26:1"> For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:13; Psalms 119:112; Psalms 119:30"> For all his judgments were before me, and I did not put away his statutes from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:8–9; Psalms 11:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> I was also upright before him, and I kept myself from my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:41–42; Ruth 2:12; 1 Samuel 26:23"> Therefore hath the LORD recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Psalms 62:12; Matthew 18:33–35"> With the merciful thou wilt show thyself merciful; with an upright man thou wilt show thyself upright;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:34; Leviticus 26:23–24; James 2:13"> With the pure thou wilt show thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt contend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; James 2:5; Isaiah 57:15"> For thou wilt save the afflicted people; but wilt bring down high looks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:29; Job 29:3; Isaiah 42:16"> For thou wilt light my candle: the LORD my God will enlighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:30; Ephesians 6:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> For by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; 2 Samuel 22:31"> As for God, his way is perfect: the word of the LORD is tried: he is a buckler to all those that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:31; Psalms 86:8; 2 Samuel 22:32"> For who is God save the LORD? or who is a rock save our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 28:7; 2 Samuel 22:33"> It is God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:19; Deuteronomy 32:13; Deuteronomy 33:29"> He maketh my feet like hinds' feet , and setteth me upon my high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 46:9; 2 Samuel 22:36"> He teacheth my hands to war, so that a bow of steel is broken by my arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:36; James 3:17–18; Psalms 28:7"> Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath held me up and thy gentleness hath made me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; 2 Samuel 22:37; Job 36:16"> Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, that my feet did not slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:10–12; Psalms 44:5; Psalms 35:2"> I have pursued my enemies, and overtaken them: neither did I turn again till they were consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:1–25; 2 Samuel 22:39; 1 Samuel 23:5"> I have wounded them that they were not able to rise: they have fallen under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:40–41; Psalms 18:32; 1 Corinthians 15:25–28"> For thou hast girded me with strength to battle: thou hast subdued under me those that rose up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 21:12; Psalms 94:23"> Thou hast also given me the necks of my enemies; that I might destroy them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Job 27:9; Job 35:12–13"> They cried, but there was none to save them : even to the LORD, but he answered them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; 2 Kings 13:7"> Then I beat them small as the dust before the wind: I cast them out as the dirt in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1; Isaiah 52:15"> Thou hast delivered me from the strivings of the people; and thou hast made me the head of the heathen: a people whom I have not known shall serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 66:3; Psalms 81:15; Deuteronomy 33:29"> As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me: the strangers shall submit themselves to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 24:4"> The strangers shall fade away, and be afraid from their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 57:5; Psalms 18:2"> The LORD liveth; and blessed be my rock; and let the God of my salvation be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 47:3; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> It is God that avengeth me, and subdueth the people under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; Psalms 140:4; Psalms 86:14"> He delivereth me from my enemies: yes, thou liftest me up above those that rise up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 15:9; 2 Samuel 22:50–51; Psalms 72:18–19"> Therefore will I give thanks to thee, O LORD, among the heathen, and sing praises to thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 144:10; Psalms 132:10; 2 Samuel 7:13"> He giveth great deliverance to his king; and showeth mercy to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for ever.</span>
