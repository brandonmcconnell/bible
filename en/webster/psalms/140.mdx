<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> I said to the LORD, Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications, O LORD.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:21; Proverbs 18:21; Isaiah 3:11"> Let not an evil speaker be established on the earth: evil shall hunt the violent man to overthrow him .</span>
