<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 5:11; Psalms 7:17"> I will be glad and rejoice in thee: I will sing praise to thy name, O thou most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:9; Revelation 6:12–17; Psalms 80:16"> When my enemies are turned back, they shall fall and perish at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; 1 Peter 2:23; Psalms 98:9"> For thou hast maintained my right and my cause; thou sattest on the throne judging right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Deuteronomy 9:14; Proverbs 13:9"> Thou hast rebuked the heathen, thou hast destroyed the wicked, thou hast put out their name for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:22–23; Exodus 14:13; Micah 7:8"> O thou enemy, destructions are come to a perpetual end: and thou hast destroyed cities; their memorial hath perished with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 103:19; Hebrews 13:8; Psalms 102:12"> But the LORD shall endure for ever: he hath prepared his throne for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Psalms 98:9; Revelation 20:12–13"> And he shall judge the world in righteousness, he shall minister judgment to the people in uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 91:1–2; Nahum 1:7"> The LORD also will be a refuge for the oppressed, a refuge in times of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:14; Proverbs 18:10; Isaiah 26:3–4"> And they that know thy name will put their trust in thee: for thou, LORD, hast not forsaken them that seek thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:22; Psalms 96:1–2; Psalms 148:1–5"> Sing praises to the LORD, who dwelleth in Zion: declare among the people his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Exodus 3:7; Revelation 16:6"> When he maketh inquisition for blood, he remembereth them: he forgetteth not the cry of the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 30:3; Psalms 107:18"> Have mercy upon me, O LORD; consider my trouble which I suffer from them that hate me, thou that liftest me up from the gates of death:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Psalms 51:12; Psalms 106:2"> That I may show forth all thy praise in the gates of the daughter of Zion: I will rejoice in thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 35:8"> The heathen are sunk down in the pit that they made: in the net which they hid is their own foot taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Judges 1:7; Proverbs 12:13"> The LORD is known by the judgment which he executeth: the wicked is snared in the work of his own hands. Higgaion. Selah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Job 8:13; Romans 2:8–9"> The wicked shall be turned into hell, and all the nations that forget God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Proverbs 23:18; Proverbs 24:14"> For the needy shall not always be forgotten: the expectation of the poor shall not perish for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> Arise, O LORD; let not man prevail: let the heathen be judged in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Psalms 82:6–7; Isaiah 31:3"> Put them in fear, O LORD: that the nations may know themselves to be but men. Selah.</span>
