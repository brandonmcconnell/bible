<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:1; Psalms 80:8; Jeremiah 2:21"> And the vineyard which thy right hand planted, and the branch that thou madest strong for thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; John 15:6; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> It is burnt with fire, it is cut down: they perish at the rebuke of thy countenance.</span>
