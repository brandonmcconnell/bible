<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 27:13–14; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David. I waited patiently for the LORD; and he inclined to me, and heard my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 119:133; Psalms 27:5"> He brought me up also out of a horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 103:1–5; Psalms 34:1–6"> And he hath put a new song in my mouth, even praise to our God: many shall see it , and fear, and shall trust in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> Blessed is that man that maketh the LORD his trust, and respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> Many, O LORD, my God, are thy wonderful works which thou hast done, and thy thoughts which are toward us, they cannot be reckoned up in order to thee: if I would declare and speak of them , they are more than can be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> Sacrifice and offering thou didst not desire; my ears hast thou opened: burnt-offering and sin-offering hast thou not required.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> Then said I, Lo, I come: in the volume of the book it is written of me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> I have preached righteousness in the great congregation: lo, I have not refrained my lips, O LORD, thou knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> I have not hid thy righteousness within my heart; I have declared thy faithfulness and thy salvation: I have not concealed thy loving-kindness and thy truth from the great congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> Withhold not thou thy tender mercies from me, O LORD: let thy loving-kindness and thy truth continually preserve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> For innumerable evils have encompassed me: my iniquities have taken hold upon me, so that I am not able to look up; they are more than the hairs of my head: therefore my heart faileth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me: O LORD, make haste to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> Let them be ashamed and confounded together that seek after my soul to destroy it; let them be driven backward and put to shame that wish me evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> Let them be desolate for a reward of their shame that say to me, Aha, aha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:27; Psalms 68:3; Acts 19:17"> Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: let such as love thy salvation say continually, The LORD be magnified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> But I am poor and needy; yet the LORD thinketh upon me: thou art my help and my deliverer; make no delay, O my God.</span>
