<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 83:1"> To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David. Hold not thy peace, O God of my praise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 52:4; Proverbs 6:17; Acts 6:13"> For the mouth of the wicked and the mouth of the deceitful are opened against me: they have spoken against me with a lying tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 35:20"> They encompassed me also with words of hatred; and fought against me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:20; Psalms 69:12–13; Psalms 35:7"> For my love they are my adversaries: but I give myself to prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Genesis 44:4; Psalms 38:20"> And they have rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Matthew 5:25; Matthew 27:4"> Set thou a wicked man over him: and let Satan stand at his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> When he shall be judged, let him be condemned: and let his prayer become sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Acts 1:16–26; Matthew 27:5"> Let his days be few; and let another take his office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 18:21; Lamentations 5:3"> Let his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; Genesis 4:12–14; Isaiah 16:2"> Let his children be continually vagabonds, and beg: let them seek their bread also out of their desolate places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:5; Job 20:18; Job 18:9–19"> Let the extortioner catch all that he hath; and let strangers spoil his labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Psalms 137:8–9; James 2:13"> Let there be none to extend mercy to him: neither let there be any to favor his fatherless children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 37:28; Job 18:19"> Let his posterity be cut off; and in the generation following let their name be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Nehemiah 4:5; Jeremiah 18:23"> Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered with the LORD; and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Psalms 90:8; Job 18:17"> Let them be before the LORD continually, that he may cut off the memory of them from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; James 2:13; Psalms 37:14"> Because that he remembered not to show mercy, but persecuted the poor and needy man, that he might even slay the broken in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 14:14; Ezekiel 35:6"> As he loved cursing, so let it come to him: as he delighted not in blessing, so let it be far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:6; Numbers 5:22; Matthew 27:3–5"> As he clothed himself with cursing like as with his garment, so let it come into his bowels like water, and like oil into his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> Let it be to him as the garment which covereth him, and for a girdle with which he is girded continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 94:23"> Let this be the reward of my adversaries from the LORD, and of them that speak evil against my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> But do thou for me, O GOD the Lord, for thy name's sake: because thy mercy is good, deliver thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:1; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 102:4"> For I am poor and needy, and my heart is wounded within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> I am gone like the shadow when it declineth: I am tossed up and down as the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:3–4; Hebrews 12:12; Matthew 4:2"> My knees are weak through fasting; and my flesh faileth of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> I became also a reproach to them: when they looked upon me they shook their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:86; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 69:13"> Help me, O LORD my God: O save me according to thy mercy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 37:7; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Exodus 8:19"> That they may know that this is thy hand; that thou, LORD, hast done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:20; John 16:22; Psalms 109:17"> Let them curse, but bless thou: when they arise, let them be ashamed; but let thy servant rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22"> Let my adversaries be clothed with shame, and let them cover themselves with their own confusion, as with a mantle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 22:22–25"> I will greatly praise the LORD with my mouth; yes, I will praise him among the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 121:5; Psalms 16:8; Psalms 140:12"> For he will stand at the right hand of the poor, to save him from those that condemn his soul.</span>
