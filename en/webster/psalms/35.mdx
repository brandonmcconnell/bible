<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 119:154; Jeremiah 51:36"> A Psalm of David. Plead my cause , O LORD, with them that strive with me: fight against them that fight against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 91:4; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 42:13"> Take hold of shield and buckler, and stand up for my help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 27:2; Psalms 51:12"> Draw out also the spear, and stop the way against them that persecute me: say to my soul, I am thy salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 70:2–3; Jeremiah 46:5; Psalms 35:26"> Let them be confounded and put to shame that seek after my soul: let them be turned back and brought to confusion that devise my hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:18; Isaiah 29:5; Isaiah 37:36"> Let them be as chaff before the wind: and let the angel of the LORD chase them .</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Psalms 73:18; Jeremiah 13:16"> Let their way be dark and slippery: and let the angel of the LORD persecute them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 140:5; Psalms 9:15"> For without cause they have hid for me their net in a pit, which without cause they have digged for my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:9–10; Proverbs 5:22; Luke 21:34"> Let destruction come upon him at unawares; and let his net that he hath hid catch himself: into that very destruction let him fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Psalms 58:10–11; Luke 1:46–47"> And my soul shall be joyful in the LORD: it shall rejoice in his salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 140:12; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 51:8"> All my bones shall say, LORD, who is like thee, who deliverest the poor from him that is too strong for him, even the poor and the needy from him that spoileth him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:5–6"> False witnesses arose; they laid to my charge things that I knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 10:32; Jeremiah 18:20; Psalms 38:20"> They rewarded me evil for good to the spoiling of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 69:10–11; Job 30:25; Matthew 5:44"> But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth: I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer returned into my own bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; 2 Samuel 1:11–12; Genesis 24:67"> I behaved myself as though he had been my friend or brother: I bowed down heavily, as one that mourneth for his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Psalms 7:2; Psalms 35:25–26"> But in my adversity they rejoiced, and assembled themselves: yes , the abjects assembled themselves against me, and I knew it not; they did tear me , and ceased not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Job 16:9; Psalms 37:12"> With hypocritical mockers in feasts, they gnashed upon me with their teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:13; Psalms 22:20–21; Psalms 57:4"> LORD, how long wilt thou look on? rescue my soul from their destructions, my darling from the lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:12; Psalms 22:22–25; Psalms 116:18"> I will give thee thanks in the great congregation: I will praise thee among many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; John 15:25; Proverbs 6:13"> Let not them that are my enemies wrongfully rejoice over me: neither let them wink with the eye that hate me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 120:5–7; Matthew 12:19; Acts 23:15"> For they speak not peace: but they devise deceitful matters against them that are quiet in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:13; Psalms 40:15; Psalms 70:3"> Yes, they opened their mouth wide against me, and said, Aha, aha, our eye hath seen it .</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Psalms 10:1; Psalms 28:1"> This thou hast seen, O LORD: keep not silence: O LORD, be not far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> Stir up thyself, and awake to my judgment, even to my cause, my God and my LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 43:1; Psalms 26:1; Psalms 7:8"> Judge me, O LORD my God, according to thy righteousness; and let them not rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Psalms 124:3; 2 Samuel 20:19"> Let them not say in their hearts, Ah, so would we have it: let them not say, We have swallowed him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 38:16; Job 19:5; Psalms 132:18"> Let them be ashamed and brought to confusion together that rejoice at my hurt: let them be clothed with shame and dishonor that magnify themselves against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40–41; Psalms 149:4; Psalms 40:16"> Let them shout for joy, and be glad, that favor my righteous cause: yes, let them say continually, Let the LORD be magnified, who hath pleasure in the prosperity of his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:21; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:5"> And my tongue shall speak of thy righteousness and of thy praise all the day long.</span>
