<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Ezekiel 20:4; Isaiah 58:1"> Son of man, cause Jerusalem to know her abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:45; Isaiah 51:1–2; Deuteronomy 20:17"> And say, Thus saith the Lord GOD to Jerusalem; Thy birth and thy nativity is of the land of Canaan; thy father was an Amorite, and thy mother a Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 2:3; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 5:16–21"> And as for thy nativity, in the day thou wast born thy navel was not cut, neither wast thou washed in water to supple thee ; thou wast not salted at all, nor swaddled at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Genesis 21:10; Exodus 1:22"> No eye pitied thee, to do any of these to thee, to have compassion upon thee; but thou wast cast out in the open field, to the lothing of thy person, in the day that thou wast born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; John 5:25; Titus 3:3–7"> And when I passed by thee, and saw thee polluted in thy own blood, I said to thee when thou wast in thy blood, Live; yes, I said to thee when thou wast in thy blood, Live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Exodus 1:7; Isaiah 62:3"> I have caused thee to multiply as the bud of the field, and thou hast increased and become great, and thou art come to excellent ornaments: thy breasts are fashioned, and thy hair is grown, whereas thou wast naked and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 3:9; Jeremiah 2:2–3; Romans 5:8"> Now when I passed by thee, and looked upon thee, behold, thy time was the time of love; and I spread my skirt over thee, and covered thy nakedness: yes, I swore to thee, and entered into a covenant with thee, saith the Lord GOD, and thou becamest mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ruth 3:3; 1 John 2:27"> Then I washed thee with water; yes, I thoroughly washed away thy blood from thee, and I anointed thee with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:18; Ezekiel 16:13; Isaiah 61:10"> I clothed thee also with broidered work, and shod thee with badgers' skin, and I girded thee with fine linen, and I covered thee with silk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Isaiah 3:19; Genesis 41:42"> I decked thee also with ornaments, and I put bracelets upon thy hands, and a chain on thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:21; Isaiah 28:5; Jeremiah 13:18"> And I put a jewel on thy forehead, and ear-rings in thy ears, and a beautiful crown upon thy head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 50:2; 1 Kings 4:21"> Thus wast thou decked with gold and silver; and thy raiment was of fine linen, and silk, and broidered work; thou didst eat fine flour, and honey, and oil: and thou wast exceeding beautiful, and thou didst prosper into a kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 10:24; Lamentations 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> And thy renown went forth among the heathen for thy beauty: for it was perfect through my comeliness, which I had put upon thee, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 57:8; Jeremiah 2:20; Ezekiel 16:25"> But thou didst trust in thy own beauty, and playedst the harlot because of thy renown, and pouredst out thy fornications on every one that passed by; his it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 23:7; Ezekiel 7:20; 2 Chronicles 28:24"> And of thy garments thou didst take, and deck thy high places with divers colors, and play the harlot upon them: the like things shall not come, neither shall it be so .</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–21; Jeremiah 3:9; Ezekiel 16:11"> Thou hast also taken thy fair jewels of my gold and of my silver, which I had given thee, and hast made to thyself images of men, and hast committed lewdness with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And hast taken thy broidered garments, and covered them: and thou hast set my oil and my incense before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:13; Hosea 2:8–13; Genesis 8:21"> My provisions also which I gave thee, fine flour, and oil, and honey, with which I fed thee, thou hast even set before them for a sweet savor: and thus it was, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:37; Ezekiel 20:31; Jeremiah 7:31"> Moreover thou hast taken thy sons and thy daughters, whom thou hast borne to me, and these hast thou sacrificed to them to be devoured. Is this of thy lewdness a small matter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 18:10"> That thou hast slain my children, and delivered them to cause them to pass through the fire for them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 11:1; Ezekiel 16:43"> And in all thy abominations and thy deeds of lewdness thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, when thou wast naked and bare, and wast polluted in thy blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 12:12; Ezekiel 2:10; Ezekiel 24:6"> And it came to pass after all thy wickedness, (woe, woe to thee! saith the Lord GOD;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; Ezekiel 16:39; Ezekiel 16:31"> That thou hast also built to thee an eminent place, and hast made thee a high place in every street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:31; Jeremiah 3:2; Genesis 38:21"> Thou hast built thy high place at every head of the way, and hast made thy beauty to be abhorred, and hast prostituted thyself to every one that passed by, and multiplied thy lewd deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19–21; Ezekiel 20:7–8; Ezekiel 8:10"> Thou hast also committed fornication with the Egyptians thy neighbors, great of flesh; and hast multiplied thy lewd deeds, to provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:12; Ezekiel 16:57; Ezekiel 16:37"> Behold, therefore I have stretched out my hand over thee, and have diminished thy ordinary food , and delivered thee to the will of them that hate thee, the daughters of the Philistines, who are ashamed of thy lewd way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 16:7; Hosea 10:6; 2 Kings 16:10–18"> Thou hast played the harlot also with the Assyrians, because thou wast insatiable; yes, thou hast played the harlot with them, and yet couldst not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 21:9; Judges 2:12–19; Ezekiel 13:14–23"> Thou hast moreover multiplied thy fornication in the land of Canaan to Chaldea; and yet with this thou wast not satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 1:3; Isaiah 3:9"> How weak is thy heart, saith the Lord GOD, seeing thou doest all these things , the work of an imperious lewd woman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:33–34; Isaiah 52:3; Ezekiel 16:39"> In that thou buildest thy eminent place in the head of every way, and makest thy high place in every street; and hast not been as a harlot, in that thou scornest hire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Ezekiel 23:37; Jeremiah 2:25"> But as a wife that committeth adultery, who taketh strangers instead of her husband!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:9; Hosea 8:9–10; Ezekiel 16:41"> They give gifts to all lewd women: but thou givest thy gifts to all thy lovers, and hirest them, that they may come to thee on every side for thy lewdness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And the contrary is in thee from other women in thy unlawful commerce, whereas none followeth thee to commit lewdness: and in that thou givest a reward, and no reward is given to thee, therefore thou art contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 4:18; 1 Kings 22:19; John 4:10"> Wherefore, O harlot, hear the word of the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:10; Ezekiel 24:13; Zephaniah 3:1"> Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thy filthiness was poured out, and thy nakedness exposed through thy carnal connection with thy lovers, and with all the idols of thy abominations, and by the blood of thy children, which thou didst give to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hosea 2:10; Jeremiah 13:22; Hosea 2:3"> Behold therefore, I will gather all thy lovers, with whom thou hast taken pleasure, and all them that thou hast loved, with all them that thou hast hated; I will even gather them on every side against thee, and will uncover thy nakedness to them, that they may see all thy nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Genesis 9:6; Zephaniah 1:17"> And I will judge thee, as women that break wedlock and shed blood are judged; and I will give thee blood in fury and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:26; Hosea 2:3; Ezekiel 16:24–25"> And I will also give thee into their hand, and they shall throw down thy eminent place, and shall break down thy high places: they shall strip thee also of thy clothes, and shall take thy fair jewels, and leave thee naked and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Habakkuk 1:6–10; John 8:5–7"> They shall also bring up a company against thee, and they shall stone thee with stones, and thrust thee through with their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:48; Jeremiah 52:13; Jeremiah 39:8"> And they shall burn thy houses with fire, and execute judgments upon thee in the sight of many women: and I will cause thee to cease from playing the harlot, and thou also shalt give no hire any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 40:1–2"> So will I make my fury towards thee to rest, and my jealousy shall depart from thee, and I will be quiet, and will be no more angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:22; Ezekiel 11:21; Psalms 78:42"> Because thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, but hast provoked me in all these things ; behold therefore, I also will recompense thy way upon thy head, saith the Lord GOD: and thou shalt not commit this lewdness above all thy abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:2–3; Psalms 106:35–38; 1 Samuel 24:13"> Behold, every one that useth proverbs shall use this proverb against thee, saying, As is the mother, so is her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Isaiah 1:4; Ezekiel 23:37–39"> Thou art thy mother's daughter, that lotheth her husband and her children; and thou art the sister of thy sisters, who lothed their husbands and their children: your mother was a Hittite, and your father an Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:4; Ezekiel 16:48–49; Ezekiel 16:53–56"> And thy elder sister is Samaria, she and her daughters that dwell at thy left hand: and thy younger sister, that dwelleth at thy right hand, is Sodom and her daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Kings 21:9; Ezekiel 16:51; Ezekiel 5:6–7"> Yet hast thou not walked after their ways, nor done after their abominations: but as if that were a very little thing , thou wast corrupted more than they in all thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Luke 10:12; Acts 7:52"> As I live, saith the Lord GOD, Sodom thy sister hath not done, she nor her daughters, as thou hast done, thou and thy daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Ezekiel 28:2; Genesis 13:10"> Behold, this was the iniquity of thy sister Sodom, pride, fullness of bread, and abundance of idleness was in her and in her daughters, neither did she strengthen the hand of the poor and needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 19:24; Genesis 18:20; Genesis 19:5"> And they were haughty, and committed abomination before me: therefore I took them away as I saw good .</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:8–11; Luke 12:47–48; Romans 3:9–20"> Neither hath Samaria committed half of thy sins; but thou hast multiplied thy abominations more than they, and hast justified thy sisters in all thy abominations which thou hast done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Ezekiel 36:15; Ezekiel 16:56"> Thou also, who hast judged thy sisters, bear thy own shame for thy sins that thou hast committed more abominable than they: they are more righteous than thou: yes, be thou confounded also, and bear thy shame, in that thou hast justified thy sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Jeremiah 12:16; Ezekiel 39:25"> When I shall bring again their captivity, the captivity of Sodom and her daughters, and the captivity of Samaria and her daughters, then will I bring again the captivity of thy captives in the midst of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:22–23; Jeremiah 2:26; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> That thou mayest bear thy own shame, and mayest be confounded in all that thou hast done, in that thou art a comfort to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:11; Malachi 3:4; Ezekiel 16:53"> When thy sisters, Sodom and her daughters, shall return to their former state, and Samaria and her daughters shall return to their former state, then thou and thy daughters shall return to your former state.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Isaiah 65:5; Luke 15:28–30; Zephaniah 3:11"> For thy sister Sodom was not mentioned by thy mouth in the day of thy pride,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Kings 16:5–7; Ezekiel 16:36–37; 2 Chronicles 28:5–6"> Before thy wickedness was disclosed, as at the time of thy reproach of the daughters of Syria, and all that are around her, the daughters of the Philistines, who despise thee on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Lamentations 5:7; Genesis 4:13"> Thou hast borne thy lewdness and thy abominations, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5; Ezekiel 17:19; Deuteronomy 29:25"> For thus saith the Lord GOD; I will even deal with thee as thou hast done, who hast despised the oath in breaking the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Hebrews 12:24; Ezekiel 16:8"> Nevertheless I will remember my covenant with thee in the days of thy youth, and I will establish to thee an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Hebrews 8:13; Galatians 4:26–31; Isaiah 66:7–12"> Then thou shalt remember thy ways, and be ashamed, when thou shalt receive thy sisters, thy elder and thy younger: and I will give them to thee for daughters, but not by thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 39:22"> And I will establish my covenant with thee; and thou shalt know that I am the LORD:</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 39:9; Romans 3:19; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> That thou mayest remember, and be confounded, and never open thy mouth any more because of thy shame, when I am pacified towards thee for all that thou hast done, saith the Lord GOD.</span>
