<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:16; Jeremiah 1:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> The word of the LORD that came to Joel the son of Pethuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Hosea 5:1; Job 8:8"> Hear this, ye old men, and give ear, all ye inhabitants of the land. Hath this been in your days, or even in the days of your fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Exodus 13:14; Psalms 145:4"> Tell ye your children of it, and let your children tell their children, and their children another generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Amos 4:9; Isaiah 33:4"> That which the palmer-worm hath left hath the locust eaten; and that which the locust hath left hath the canker-worm eaten; and that which the canker-worm hath left hath the caterpillar eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:1; Isaiah 32:10–12; Luke 21:34–36"> Awake, ye drunkards, and weep; and howl, all ye drinkers of wine, because of the new wine; for it is cut off from your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Revelation 9:7–10; Proverbs 30:14"> For a nation is come up upon my land, strong, and without number, whose teeth are the teeth of a lion, and he hath the cheek teeth of a great lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Amos 4:9; Joel 1:12"> He hath laid my vine waste, and barked my fig-tree: he hath made it clean bare, and cast it away; its branches are made white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Malachi 2:15; Jeremiah 3:4"> Lament like a virgin girded with sackcloth for the husband of her youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Joel 2:14; Hosea 9:4"> The meat-offering and the drink-offering is cut off from the house of the LORD; the priests, the LORD'S ministers, mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Hosea 9:2; Jeremiah 12:11"> The field is wasted, the land mourneth; for the corn is wasted; the new wine is dried up, the oil languisheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Isaiah 17:11; Jeremiah 9:12"> Be ye ashamed, O ye husbandmen; howl, O ye vine-dressers, for the wheat and for the barley; because the harvest of the field hath perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Isaiah 24:11; Song of Solomon 2:3"> The vine is dried up, and the fig-tree languisheth; the pomegranate-tree, the palm-tree also, and the apple-tree, even all the trees of the field, are withered: because joy is withered away from the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 1:8–9; Jeremiah 4:8; Joel 2:17"> Gird yourselves, and lament, ye priests: howl, ye ministers of the altar: come, lie all night in sackcloth, ye ministers of my God: for the meat-offering and the drink-offering is withheld from the house of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 29:10–11; 2 Chronicles 20:13"> Sanctify ye a fast, call a solemn assembly, gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land into the house of the LORD your God, and cry to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6–9; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Jeremiah 30:7"> Alas for the day! for the day of the LORD is at hand, and as a destruction from the Almighty shall it come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:6–7; Psalms 43:4; Isaiah 62:8–9"> Is not the food cut off before our eyes, and joy and gladness from the house of our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; Genesis 23:16"> The seed hath perished under their clods, the granaries are laid desolate, the barns are broken down; for the corn is withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 14:5–6"> How do the beasts groan! the herds of cattle are perplexed, because they have no pasture; yes, the flocks of sheep are made desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 9:10; Amos 7:4"> O LORD, to thee will I cry: for the fire hath devoured the pastures of the wilderness, and the flame hath burned all the trees of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:21; 1 Kings 17:7; Job 38:41"> The beasts of the field cry also to thee: for the rivers of waters are dried up, and the fire hath devoured the pastures of the wilderness.</span>
