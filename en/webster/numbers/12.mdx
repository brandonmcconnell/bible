<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> And Aaron said to Moses, Alas, my lord, I beseech thee, lay not the sin upon us, in which we have done foolishly, and in which we have sinned.</span>
