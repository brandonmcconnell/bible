<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 16:35; 2 Kings 1:12"> And when the people complained, it displeased the LORD; and the LORD heard it : and his anger was kindled; and the fire of the LORD burnt among them, and consumed them that were in the uttermost parts of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 21:7; James 5:16; Numbers 16:45–48"> And the people cried to Moses; and when Moses prayed to the LORD, the fire was quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:22"> And he called the name of the place Taberah; because the fire of the LORD burnt among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Exodus 12:38; Psalms 106:14"> And the mixed multitude that was among them fell to lusting: and the children of Israel also wept again, and said, Who shall give us flesh to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:3; Psalms 17:14; Philippians 3:19"> We remember the fish which we ate in Egypt freely: the cucumbers, and the melons, and the leeks, and the onions, and the garlic:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; 2 Samuel 13:4"> But now our soul is dried away; there is nothing at all, besides this manna, before our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 16:31; Genesis 2:12; Exodus 16:14–15"> And the manna was as coriander-seed, and the color of it as the color of bdellium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 16:31; Exodus 16:16–18; Exodus 16:23"> And the people went about, and gathered it and ground it in mills, or beat it in a mortar, and baked it in pans, and made cakes of it: and the taste of it was as the taste of fresh oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 16:13–14; Psalms 78:23–25; Deuteronomy 32:2"> And when the dew fell upon the camp in the night, the manna fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 78:21; Numbers 16:27; Numbers 14:1–2"> Then Moses heard the people weeping throughout their families, every man in the door of his tent: and the anger of the LORD was kindled greatly: Moses also was displeased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:12; Exodus 5:22; Jeremiah 15:10"> And Moses said to the LORD, Why hast thou afflicted thy servant? and why have I not found favor in thy sight, that thou layest the burden of all this people upon me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 40:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> Have I conceived all this people? have I begotten them, that thou shouldest say to me, Carry them in thy bosom, as a nursing-father beareth the sucking child, to the land which thou sworest to their fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Mark 8:4; John 6:5–9"> Whence should I have flesh to give to all this people? for they weep to me, saying, Give us flesh, that we may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Deuteronomy 1:9–12; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> I am not able to bear all this people alone, because it is too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3"> And if thou dealest thus with me, kill me, I pray thee, out of hand, if I have found favor in thy sight; and let me not see my wretchedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:1; Exodus 24:9; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And the LORD said to Moses, Gather to me seventy men of the elders of Israel, whom thou knowest to be the elders of the people, and officers over them; and bring them to the tabernacle of the congregation, that they may stand there with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; 1 Samuel 10:6; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> And I will come down and talk with thee there: and I will take of the spirit which is upon thee, and will put it upon them; and they shall bear the burden of the people with thee, that thou mayest not bear it thyself alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:10; Acts 7:39; Exodus 16:3–7"> And say thou to the people, Sanctify yourselves against to-morrow, and ye shall eat flesh; for ye have wept in the ears of the LORD, saying, Who shall give us flesh to eat? for it was well with us in Egypt: therefore the LORD will give you flesh, and ye shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Ye shall not eat one day, nor two days, nor five days, neither ten days, nor twenty days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 21:5; Psalms 78:27–30"> But even a whole month, until it shall come out at your nostrils, and it be lothsome to you; because ye have despised the LORD who is among you, and have wept before him, saying, Why came we forth out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 1:46; Genesis 12:2"> And Moses said, The people among whom I am , are six hundred thousand footmen; and thou hast said, I will give them flesh, that they may eat a whole month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; 2 Kings 7:2; John 6:9"> Shall the flocks and the herds be slain for them, to suffice for them? or shall all the fish of the sea be gathered for them, to suffice for them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:1; Isaiah 50:2; Genesis 18:14"> And the LORD said to Moses, Is the LORD'S hand shortened? thou shalt see now whether my word shall come to pass to thee, or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 11:16; Numbers 11:26"> And Moses went out, and told the people the words of the LORD, and gathered the seventy men of the elders of the people, and set them around the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Joel 2:28–29; 1 Samuel 10:10"> And the LORD came down in a cloud, and spoke to him, and took of the spirit that was upon him, and gave it to the seventy elders: and it came to pass, that when the spirit rested upon them; they prophesied, and did not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:5; 1 Samuel 20:26; 1 Samuel 10:22"> But there remained two of the men in the camp, the name of the one was Eldad, and the name of the other Medad: and the spirit rested upon them, and they were of them that were described, but went not out to the tabernacle: and they prophesied in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And there ran a young man, and told Moses, and said, Eldad and Medad do prophesy in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Luke 9:49–50; John 3:26"> And Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of Moses, one of his young men, answered and said, My lord, Moses, forbid them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:5; Matthew 9:37–38; Luke 10:2"> And Moses said to him, Enviest thou for my sake? I would that all the LORD'S people were prophets, and that the LORD would put his spirit upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Moses withdrew into the camp, he and the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 16:13; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:26–29"> And there went forth a wind from the LORD, and brought quails from the sea, and let them fall by the camp, as it were a day's journey on this side, and as it were a day's journey on the other side, around the camp, and as it were two cubits high upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11; Exodus 16:36"> And the people stood up all that day, and all that night, and all the next day, and they gathered the quails: he that gathered least gathered ten homers: and they spread them all abroad for themselves around the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Psalms 106:14–15; Numbers 25:9"> And while the flesh was yet between their teeth, ere it was chewed, the wrath of the LORD was kindled against the people, and the LORD smote the people with a very great plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:22; 1 Corinthians 10:6; Numbers 33:16"> And he called the name of that place Kibroth-hattaavah: because there they buried the people that lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 33:17; Numbers 12:16; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And the people journeyed from Kibroth-hattaavah to Hazeroth: and abode at Hazeroth.</span>
