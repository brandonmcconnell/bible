<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> And when king Arad the Canaanite, who dwelt in the south, heard that Israel came by the way of the spies; then he fought against Israel, and took some of them prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> And Israel vowed a vow to the LORD, and said, If thou wilt indeed deliver this people into my hand, then I will utterly destroy their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; Psalms 10:17"> And the LORD hearkened to the voice of Israel, and delivered up the Canaanites; and they utterly destroyed them and their cities: and he called the name of the place Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> And they journeyed from mount Hor by the way of the Red sea, to compass the land of Edom: and the soul of the people was much discouraged because of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> And the people spoke against God, and against Moses, Why have ye brought us out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? for there is no bread, neither is there any water; and our soul lotheth this light bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And the LORD sent fiery serpents among the people, and they bit the people; and many people of Israel died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; Psalms 78:34"> Therefore the people came to Moses, and said, We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD, and against thee; pray to the LORD, that he take away the serpents from us. And Moses prayed for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; Psalms 106:43–45; Psalms 145:8"> And the LORD said to Moses, Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> And Moses made a serpent of brass, and put it upon a pole, and it came to pass, that if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> And the children of Israel moved forward, and pitched in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they journeyed from Oboth, and pitched at Ije-abarim, in the wilderness which is before Moab, towards the sun-rising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> From thence they removed, and pitched in the valley of Zared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> From thence they removed, and pitched on the other side of Arnon, which is in the wilderness that cometh out of the coasts of the Amorites: for Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> Wherefore it is said in the book of the wars of the LORD, What he did in the Red sea, and in the brooks of Arnon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> And at the stream of the brooks that goeth down to the dwelling of Ar, and lieth upon the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> And from thence they went to Beer: that is the well of which the LORD spoke to Moses, Assemble the people, and I will give them water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; Psalms 106:12"> Then Israel sung this song, Spring up, O well; sing ye to it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> The princes digged the well, the nobles of the people digged it, by the direction of the lawgiver, with their staffs. And from the wilderness they went to Mattanah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And from Mattanah to Nahaliel: and from Nahaliel to Bamoth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> And from Bamoth in the valley, that is in the country of Moab, to the top of Pisgah, which looketh towards Jeshimon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> And Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> Let me pass through thy land: we will not turn into the fields, or into the vineyards; we will not drink of the waters of the well: but we will go along by the king's high -way, until we have past thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> And Sihon would not suffer Israel to pass through his border: but Sihon collected all his people, and went out against Israel into the wilderness: and he came to Jahaz, and fought against Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> And Israel smote him with the edge of the sword, and possessed his land from Arnon to Jabbok, even to the children of Ammon: for the border of the children of Ammon was strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> And Israel took all these cities: and Israel dwelt in all the cities of the Amorites, in Heshbon, and in all its villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For Heshbon was the city of Sihon the king of the Amorites, who had fought against the former king of Moab, and taken all his land out of his hand, even to Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> Wherefore they that speak in proverbs say, Come into Heshbon, let the city of Sihon be built and prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> For there has a fire gone out of Heshbon, a flame from the city of Sihon: it hath consumed Ar of Moab, and the lords of the high places of Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> Woe to thee, Moab! thou art undone, O people of Chemosh: he hath given his sons that escaped, and his daughters, into captivity to Sihon king of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> We have shot at them; Heshbon has perished even to Dibon, and we have laid them waste even to Nophah, which reacheth to Medeba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> Thus Israel dwelt in the land of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> And Moses sent to explore Jaazer, and they took its villages, and drove out the Amorites that were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> And they turned and went up by the way of Bashan: and Og the king of Bashan went out against them, he, and all his people, to the battle at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And the LORD said to Moses, Fear him not: for I have delivered him into thy hand, and all his people, and his land; and thou shalt do to him as thou didst to Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt at Heshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> So they smote him, and his sons, and all his people, until there was none left to him alive: and they possessed his land.</span>
