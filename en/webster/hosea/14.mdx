<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Joel 2:12–13; Hosea 12:6"> O Israel, return to the LORD thy God; for thou hast fallen by thy iniquity.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 10:14; Isaiah 31:1"> Ashur shall not save us; we will not ride upon horses: neither will we say any more to the work of our hands, Ye are our gods: for in thee the fatherless findeth mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 57:18; Ephesians 2:4–9; Jeremiah 3:22"> I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely: for my anger is turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 27:6; Ephesians 3:17"> I will be as the dew to Israel: he shall grow as the lily and cast forth his roots as Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:8; John 15:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14–15"> His branches shall spread, and his beauty shall be as the olive-tree, and his smell as Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 85:6; 1 Corinthians 15:36–38"> They that dwell under his shade shall return; they shall revive as the corn, and grow as the vine: the scent of it shall be as the wine of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 34:32; Isaiah 55:13; James 1:17"> Ephraim shall say , What have I to do any more with idols? I have heard him , and observed him: I am like a green fir-tree. From me is thy fruit found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Psalms 107:43; John 8:47"> Who is wise, and he shall understand these things ? prudent, and he shall know them? for the ways of the LORD are right, and the just shall walk in them: but the transgressors shall fall in them.</span>
