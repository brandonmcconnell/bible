<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:13; Micah 7:3–7; Hosea 6:4"> When I would have healed Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim was disclosed, and the wickedness of Samaria: for they commit falsehood; and the thief cometh in, and the troop of robbers strippeth without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:2; Amos 8:7; Jeremiah 14:10"> And they consider not in their hearts that I remember all their wickedness: now their own doings have beset them around; they are before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Romans 1:32; Jeremiah 28:1–4"> They make the king glad with their wickedness, and the princes with their lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:2; James 4:4; Jeremiah 5:7–8"> They are all adulterers, as an oven heated by the baker, who ceaseth from raising after he hath kneaded the dough, until it is leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 28:1; Matthew 14:6; Genesis 40:20"> In the day of our king the princes have made him sick with bottles of wine; he stretched out his hand with scorners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 7:7; Hosea 7:4; 1 Samuel 19:11–15"> For they have made ready their heart like an oven, while they lie in wait: their baker sleepeth all the night; in the morning it burneth as a flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 15:25; 2 Kings 15:30; 2 Kings 15:10"> They are all hot as an oven, and have devoured their judges; all their kings are fallen: there is none among them that calleth to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:35; Ezra 9:12; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Ephraim, he hath mixed himself among the people; Ephraim is a cake not turned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 8:7; Isaiah 42:22–25; 2 Kings 13:3–7"> Strangers have devoured his strength, and he knoweth it not: yes, gray hairs are here and there upon him, yet he knoweth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 5:5; Isaiah 9:13; Amos 4:6–13"> And the pride of Israel testifieth to his face: and they do not return to the LORD their God, nor seek him for all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 5:13; Hosea 11:11"> Ephraim also is like a silly dove without heart: they call to Egypt, they go to Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 31:16–29"> When they shall go, I will spread my net upon them; I will bring them down as the fowls of the heaven; I will chastise them, as their congregation hath heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:12; Hosea 11:12; Isaiah 63:8"> Woe to them! for they have fled from me: destruction to them! because they have transgressed against me: though I have redeemed them, yet they have spoken lies against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:3; Job 35:9–10; Jeremiah 3:10"> And they have not cried to me with their heart, when they howled upon their beds: they assemble themselves for corn and wine, and they rebel against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; Psalms 62:3; Jeremiah 17:9"> Though I have bound and strengthened their arms, yet do they imagine mischief against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:57; Hosea 9:3; Ezekiel 23:32"> They return, but not to the most High: they are like a deceitful bow: their princes shall fall by the sword for the rage of their tongue: this shall be their derision in the land of Egypt.</span>
