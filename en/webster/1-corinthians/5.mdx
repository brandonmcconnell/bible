<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:23; Colossians 3:17; Matthew 16:19"> In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are assembled, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 12:15"> Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Corinthians 6:14"> I have written to you in this epistle, not to associate with persons guilty of lewdness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; John 17:15–16; Matthew 5:14–16"> Yet not altogether with lewd persons of this world, or with the covetous, or extortioners, or with idolaters: for then ye must needs go out of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But now I have written to you not to keep company, if any man that is called a brother is a lewd person, or covetous, or an idolater, or a railer, or a drunkard, or an extortioner: with such person no not to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:11; 1 Timothy 3:7; Luke 12:14"> For what have I to do to judge them also that are without? do ye not judge them that are within?</span>
