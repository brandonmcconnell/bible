<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:3; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 31:1–6"> Then wrought Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every wise-hearted man, in whom the LORD put wisdom and understanding, to know how to work all manner of work for the service of the sanctuary, according to all that the LORD had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:17; Hebrews 5:4; Exodus 31:6"> And Moses called Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every wise-hearted man, in whose heart the LORD had put wisdom, even every one whose heart excited him to come to the work to do it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:27; Exodus 35:5–21"> And they received from Moses all the offering which the children of Israel had brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary, to make it. And they brought yet to him free-offerings every morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10; Luke 12:42; Matthew 24:45"> And all the wise men, that wrought all the work of the sanctuary, came every man from his work which they made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:6–10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 2 Chronicles 24:14"> And they spoke to Moses, saying, The people bring much more than enough for the service of the work, which the LORD commanded to make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses gave commandment, and they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp, saying, Let neither man nor woman make any more work for the offering of the sanctuary. So the people were restrained from bringing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:10"> For the stuff they had was sufficient for all the work to make it, and too much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23; Exodus 26:1–37; Ezekiel 1:5–28"> And every wise-hearted man among them that wrought the work of the tabernacle made ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet: he made them with cherubim of curious work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The length of one curtain was twenty and eight cubits, and the breadth of one curtain four cubits: the curtains were all of one size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; Exodus 26:3; Psalms 122:3"> And he coupled the five curtains one to another: and the other five curtains he coupled one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:4"> And he made loops of blue on the edge of one curtain from the selvedge in the coupling: likewise he made in the extremity of another curtain, in the coupling of the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 26:5; Exodus 26:10"> Fifty loops he made in one curtain, and fifty loops he made in the edge of the curtain which was in the coupling of the second: the loops held one curtain to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 12:20"> And he made fifty buttons of gold, and coupled the curtains one to another with the buttons. So it became one tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 26:7–13"> And he made curtains of goats' hair for the tent over the tabernacle: eleven curtains he made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length of one curtain was thirty cubits, and four cubits was the breadth of one curtain: the eleven curtains were of one size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And he coupled five curtains by themselves, and six curtains by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he made fifty loops upon the outermost edge of the curtain in the coupling, and fifty loops made he upon the edge of the curtain which coupleth the second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he made fifty buttons of brass to couple the tent together, that it might be one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:14"> And he made a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of badgers' skins above that .</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 25:5; Numbers 25:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> And he made boards for the tabernacle of shittim wood, standing up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The length of a board was ten cubits, and the breadth of a board one cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> One board had two tenons, equally distant one from another; thus did he make for all the boards of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he made boards for the tabernacle; twenty boards for the south side southward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And forty sockets of silver he made under the twenty boards; two sockets under one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under another board for its two tenons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And for the other side of the tabernacle which is towards the north corner, he made twenty boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And their forty sockets of silver; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 26:22; Exodus 26:27"> And for the sides of the tabernacle westward he made six boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And two boards he made for the corners of the tabernacle in the two sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:32; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And they were coupled beneath, and coupled together at the head thereof, to one ring: thus he did to both of them in both the corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 26:25"> And there were eight boards; and their sockets were sixteen sockets of silver, under every board two sockets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 26:26–29; Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:5"> And he made bars of shittim wood; five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:26"> And five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the tabernacle for the sides westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And he made the middle bar to shoot through the boards from the one end to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he overlaid the boards with gold, and made their rings of gold to be places for the bars, and overlaid the bars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:20; Exodus 40:21"> And he made a vail of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen: with cherubim he made it of curious work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18"> And he made to it four pillars of shittim wood , and overlaid them with gold: their hooks were of gold; and he cast for them four sockets of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 26:36–37; Exodus 40:28"> And he made a hanging for the tabernacle-door of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, of needle-work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 27:10"> And the five pillars of it, with their hooks: and he overlaid their capitals and their fillets with gold: but their five sockets were of brass.</span>
