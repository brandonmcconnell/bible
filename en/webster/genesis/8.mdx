<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Matthew 8:26–27; Proverbs 8:28"> The fountains also of the deep, and the windows of heaven were stopped, and the rain from heaven was restrained;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 7:24; Genesis 7:11"> And the waters returned from off the earth continually: and after the end of the hundred and fifty days the waters were abated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; Jeremiah 51:27; Isaiah 37:38"> And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And the waters decreased continually, till the tenth month: in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, were the tops of the mountains seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 6:16; Daniel 6:10"> And it came to pass at the end of forty days, that Noah opened the window of the ark which he had made:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 147:9; 1 Kings 17:6; 1 Kings 17:4"> And he sent forth a raven, which went forth to and fro, till the waters were dried from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 1:15; Matthew 10:16"> Also he sent forth a dove from him, to see if the waters were abated from off the face of the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Psalms 116:7; Deuteronomy 28:65"> But the dove found no rest for the sole of her foot, and she returned to him into the ark; for the waters were on the face of the whole earth. Then he put forth his hand, and took her, and pulled her in to him into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 7:4; Genesis 7:10; Genesis 8:12"> And he stayed yet other seven days, and again he sent forth the dove out of the ark;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:15; Zechariah 4:12–14; Romans 10:15"> And the dove came in to him in the evening, and lo, in her mouth was an olive-leaf plucked off: So Noah knew that the waters were abated from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Isaiah 30:18; Genesis 8:10"> And he stayed yet other seven days, and sent forth the dove; which returned not again to him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 7:11"> And it came to pass in the six hundredth and first year, in the first month, the first day of the month, the waters were dried from off the earth: and Noah removed the covering of the ark, and looked, and behold, the face of the ground was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 7:13–14; Genesis 7:11"> And in the second month, on the seven and twentieth day of the month, was the earth dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And God spoke to Noah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 7:13; Psalms 121:8; Psalms 91:11"> Go forth from the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and thy sons' wives with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:22; Genesis 9:1; Jeremiah 31:27–28"> Bring forth with thee every living animal that is with thee, of all flesh, of fowl, and of cattle, and of every creeping animal that creepeth upon the earth; that they may breed abundantly on the earth, and be fruitful, and multiply upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:8"> And Noah went forth, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every beast, every creeping animal, and every fowl, and whatsoever creepeth upon the earth, after their kinds, went out of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15–16; Romans 12:1; Genesis 22:9"> And Noah built an altar to the LORD, and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt-offerings on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:15; Ephesians 5:2; Isaiah 54:9–10"> And the LORD smelled a sweet savor; and the LORD said in his heart, I will not again curse the ground any more for man's sake; for the imagination of man's heart is evil from his youth: neither will I again smite any more every living animal as I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:7; Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 5:24"> While the earth remaineth, seed-time and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease.</span>
