<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 13:18"> And the LORD appeared to him in the plains of Mamre: and he sat in the tent door in the heat of the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 18:22; Genesis 19:1"> And he lifted up his eyes and looked, and lo, three men stood by him: and when he saw them, he ran to meet them from the tent door, and bowed himself toward the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 32:5"> And said, My Lord, if now I have found favor in thy sight, pass not away, I pray thee, from thy servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 24:32; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 43:24"> Let a little water, I pray you, be brought, and wash your feet, and rest yourselves under the tree:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Judges 13:15; Judges 6:18"> And I will bring a morsel of bread, and comfort ye your hearts; after that you shall pass on: for therefore are ye come to your servant. And they said, So do, as thou hast said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:15"> And Abraham hastened into the tent to Sarah, and said, Make ready quickly three measures of fine meal, knead it, and make cakes upon the hearth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:14; Luke 15:27; Luke 15:30"> And Abraham ran to the herd, and brought a calf tender and good, and gave it to a young man; and he hasted to dress it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> And he took butter, and milk, and the calf which he had dressed, and set it before them; and he stood by them under the tree, and they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> And they said to him, Where is Sarah thy wife? And he said, Behold, in the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> And he said, I will certainly return to thee according to the time of life; and lo, Sarah thy wife shall have a son. And Sarah heard it in the tent door, which was behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke 1:36"> Now Abraham and Sarah were old and far advanced in age; and it ceased to be with Sarah after the manner of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:18–20"> Therefore Sarah laughed within herself, saying, After I am become old shall I have pleasure, my lord being old also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 2:25"> And the LORD said to Abraham, Why did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I certainly bear a child, who am old?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27"> Is any thing too hard for the LORD? At the time appointed I will return to thee, according to the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> Then Sarah denied, saying, I laughed not; for she was afraid. And he said, Nay; but thou didst laugh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> And the men rose up from thence, and looked toward Sodom: and Abraham went with them to bring them on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Psalms 25:14; John 15:15"> And the LORD said, Shall I hide from Abraham that thing which I do;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> Seeing that Abraham shall surely become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth shall be blessed in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> For I know him, that he will command his children and his household after him, and they will keep the way of the LORD, to do justice and judgment; that the LORD may bring upon Abraham that which he hath spoken of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 3:9"> And the LORD said, Because the cry of Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and because their sin is very grievous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Zephaniah 1:12"> I will go down now, and see whether they have done altogether according to the cry of it, which is come to me; and if not, I will know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Ezekiel 22:30"> And the men turned their faces from thence, and went towards Sodom: but Abraham stood yet before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; 2 Samuel 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> And Abraham drew near, and said, Wilt thou also destroy the righteous with the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Isaiah 1:9; Matthew 7:13–14"> Peradventure there are fifty righteous within the city: wilt thou also destroy and not spare the place for the fifty righteous that are in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Isaiah 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> That be far from thee to do after this manner, to slay the righteous with the wicked: and that the righteous should be as the wicked, that be far from thee: Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:1; Matthew 24:22; Isaiah 65:8"> And the LORD said, If I find in Sodom fifty righteous within the city, then I will spare all the place for their sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Isaiah 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> And Abraham answered and said, Behold now, I, who am dust and ashes, have taken upon me to speak to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; 1 Kings 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> Peradventure there will lack five of the fifty righteous: wilt thou destroy all the city for lack of five? And he said, If I find there forty and five, I will not destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> And he spoke to him yet again, and said, Peradventure there will be forty found there. And he said, I will not do it for forty's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> And he said, Oh, let not the LORD be angry, and I will speak: Peradventure there will thirty be found there. And he said, I will not do it, if I find thirty there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke 18:1"> And he said, Behold now, I have taken upon me to speak to the LORD: Peradventure there will be twenty found there. And he said, I will not destroy it for twenty's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew 7:7"> And he said, Oh let not the LORD be angry, and I will speak yet but this once: Peradventure ten will be found there. And he said, I will not destroy it for ten's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 32:26; Genesis 31:55"> And the LORD went his way, as soon as he had left communing with Abraham: and Abraham returned to his place.</span>
