<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now a certain man was sick, named Lazarus, of Bethany, the town of Mary and her sister Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> (It was that Mary who anointed the Lord with ointment, and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Therefore his sisters sent to him, saying, Lord, behold, he whom thou lovest is sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> When Jesus heard that , he said, This sickness is not to death, but for the glory of God, that the Son of God may be glorified by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> When therefore he had heard that he was sick, he abode two days still in the same place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after that he saith to his disciples, Let us ho into Judea again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> His disciples say to him, Master, the Jews of late sought to stone thee; and goest thou thither again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus answered, Are there not twelve hours in the day? If any man walketh in the day, he stumbleth not, because he seeth the light of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> But if a man walketh in the night, he stumbleth, because there is no light in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> These things he said: and after that he saith to them, Our friend Lazarus sleepeth; but I go that I may awake him out of sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then said his disciples, Lord, if he sleepeth he will do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> Now Jesus spoke of his death: but they thought that he had spoken of taking rest in sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Then said Jesus to them plainly, Lazarus is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> And I am glad for your sakes that I was not there, to the intent ye may believe; nevertheless, let us go to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Then said Thomas, who is called Didymus, to his fellow-disciples, Let us also go, that we may die with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> Then when Jesus came, he found that he had lain in the grave four days already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> (Now Bethany was nigh to Jerusalem, about fifteen furlongs distant.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> And many of the Jews came to Martha and Mary, to comfort them concerning their brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Then Martha, as soon as she heard that Jesus was coming, went and met him: but Mary sat in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Then said Martha to Jesus, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> But I know that even now, whatever thou wilt ask of God, God will give it thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus saith to her, Thy brother shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha saith to him, I know that he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> And whoever liveth, and believeth in me, shall never die. Believest thou this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She saith to him, Yes, Lord, I believe that thou art the Christ, the Son of God, who was to come into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And when she had so said, she went and called Mary her sister secretly, saying, The Master is come, and calleth for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> As soon as she heard that , she arose quickly, and came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> Now Jesus had not yet come into the town, but was in that place where Martha met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> The Jews then who were with her in the house, and comforted her, when they saw Mary that she rose hastily, and went out, followed her, saying, She goeth to the grave to weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Then when Mary had come where Jesus was, and saw him, she fell down at his feet, saying to him, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus therefore saw her weeping, and the Jews also weeping who came with her, he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> And said, Where have ye laid him? They say to him, Lord, come and see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> Then said the Jews, Behold how he loved him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> And some of them said, Could not this man, who opened the eyes of the blind, have caused that even this man should not have died?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus therefore again groaning in himself, cometh to the grave. It was a cave, and a stone lay upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus said, Take ye away the stone. Martha, the sister of him that was dead, saith to him, Lord, by this time his body is offensive: for he hath been dead four days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus saith to her, Said I not to thee, that if thou wouldest believe, thou shouldest see the glory of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> Then they took away the stone from the place where the dead was laid. And Jesus lifted up his eyes, and said, Father, I thank thee that thou hast heard me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> And I knew that thou hearest me always: but because of the people who stand by, I said it , that they may believe that thou hast sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> And when he had thus spoken, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> And he that was dead came forth, bound hand and foot with grave-clothes: and his face was bound about with a napkin. Jesus saith to them, Loose him, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Then many of the Jews who came to Mary, and had seen the things which Jesus did, believed on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went to the Pharisees, and told them what things Jesus had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> Then the chief priests and the Pharisees convened a council, and said, What do we? for this man performeth many miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him: and the Romans will come, and take away both our place and nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> And one of them, named Caiaphas, being the high priest that same year, said to them, Ye know nothing at all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> Nor consider that it is expedient for us, that one man should die for the people, and that the whole nation perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> And this he spoke not of himself: but being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for that nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> And not for that nation only, but that also he should assemble in one the children of God that were scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> Then from that day forth they took counsel together to put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Jesus therefore walked no more openly among the Jews; but went thence to a country near to the wilderness, into a city called Ephraim, and there continued with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> And the Jews' passover was nigh at hand: and many went out of the country to Jerusalem before the passover, to purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> Then they sought for Jesus, and spoke among themselves, as they stood in the temple, What think ye, that he will not come to the feast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> Now both the chief priests and the Pharisees had given a commandment, that, if any man knew where he was, he should show it , that they might take him.</span>
