<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:3; Psalms 48:1; Psalms 96:4"> I will call on the LORD, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Revelation 17:15"> When the waves of death compassed me, the floods of ungodly-men made me afraid;</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 26:11; Psalms 97:4; Judges 5:4"> Then the earth shook and trembled: the foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was wroth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; 1 Samuel 4:4"> And he rode upon a cherub, and flew: and he was seen upon the wings of the wind.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; 1 Samuel 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> The LORD thundered from heaven, and the most High uttered his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Habakkuk 3:11"> And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 1:4; Habakkuk 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And the channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were laid open, at the rebuking of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He sent from above, he took me; he drew me out of many waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivered me from my strong enemy, from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> They fell upon me in the day of my calamity: but the LORD was my support.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; 2 Samuel 15:26"> He brought me forth also into a large place: he delivered me, because he delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness: according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all his judgments were before me: and as for his statutes, I did not depart from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> I was also upright before him, and have kept myself from my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> Therefore the LORD hath recompensed me according to my righteousness; according to my cleanness in his eye-sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the merciful thou wilt show thyself merciful, and with the upright man thou wilt show thyself upright.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Isaiah 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And the afflicted people thou wilt save: but thy eyes are upon the haughty, that thou mayest bring them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For thou art my lamp, O LORD: and the LORD will lighten my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For by thee I have run through a troop: by my God have I leaped over a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> As for God, his way is perfect; the word of the LORD is tried: he is a buckler to all them that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremiah 10:6–7"> For who is God, save the LORD? and who is a rock, save our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Isaiah 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> God is my strength and power: and he maketh my way perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Isaiah 58:14; Habakkuk 3:19"> He maketh my feet like hinds' feet : and setteth me upon my high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> He teacheth my hands to war; so that a bow of steel is broken by my arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy gentleness hath made me great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; so that my feet have not slipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:18–25; 2 Samuel 10:14"> I have pursued my enemies, and destroyed them; and turned not again until I had consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Malachi 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> And I have consumed them, and wounded them, that they could not arise: yea, they have fallen under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Isaiah 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> For thou hast girded me with strength to battle: them that rose up against me hast thou subdued under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> Thou hast also given me the necks of my enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; 1 Samuel 28:6; Micah 3:4"> They looked, but there was none to save; even to the LORD, but he answered them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Isaiah 10:6; 2 Kings 13:7"> Then I beat them as small as the dust of the earth, I stamped them as the mire of the street, and spread them abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–14; Isaiah 55:5; 2 Samuel 3:1"> Thou also hast delivered me from the strivings of my people, thou hast kept me to be head of the heathen: a people which I knew not shall serve me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Isaiah 56:3"> Strangers shall submit themselves to me: as soon as they hear, they shall be obedient to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Micah 7:17; James 1:11; Isaiah 64:6"> Strangers shall fade away, and they shall tremble from their close places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; 2 Samuel 22:3; Job 19:25"> The LORD liveth; and blessed be my rock; and exalted be the God of the rock of my salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; 2 Samuel 18:31"> It is God that avengeth me, and that bringeth down the people under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> And that bringeth me forth from my enemies: thou also hast lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.</span>
