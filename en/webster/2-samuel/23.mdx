<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Deuteronomy 32:4; Exodus 18:21"> The God of Israel said, the Rock of Israel spoke to me, He that ruleth over men must be just, ruling in the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Psalms 72:6; Hosea 6:5"> And he shall be as the light of the morning, when the sun riseth, even a morning without clouds; as the tender grass springing out of the earth by clear shining after rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 7:14–16; Isaiah 9:6–7"> Although my house is not so with God; yet he hath made with me an everlasting covenant, ordered in all things , and sure: for this is all my salvation, and all my desire, although he maketh it not to grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 33:12; Deuteronomy 13:13"> But the sons of Belial shall be all of them as thorns thrust away, because they cannot be taken with hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 6:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Isaiah 27:4"> But the man that shall touch them must be fenced with iron and the staff of a spear; and they shall be utterly burned with fire in the same place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These are the names of the mighty men whom David had: The Tachmonite that sat in the seat, chief among the captains; the same was Adino the Eznite: he lifted up his spear against eight hundred, whom he slew at one time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 1 Chronicles 11:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:26"> And after him was Eleazar the son of Dodo the Ahohite, one of the three mighty men with David, when they defied the Philistines that were there assembled to battle, and the men of Israel had gone away:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> He arose, and smote the Philistines until his hand was weary, and his hand adhered to the sword: and the LORD wrought a great victory that day; and the people returned after him only to plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> And after him was Shammah the son of Agee the Hararite. And the Philistines were collected into a troop, where was a piece of ground full of lentils: and the people fled from the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalms 3:8"> But he stood in the midst of the ground, and defended it, and slew the Philistines: and the LORD wrought a great victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> And three of the thirty chiefs went down, and came to David in the harvest time to the cave of Adullam: and the troop of the Philistines encamped in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> And David was then in a hold, and the garrison of the Philistines was then in Beth-lehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> And David longed, and said, Oh that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Beth-lehem, which is by the gate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> And the three mighty men broke through the host of the Philistines, and drew water out of the well of Beth-lehem, that was by the gate, and took it , and brought it to David: nevertheless he would not drink of it, but poured it out to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> And he said, Be it far from me, O LORD, that I should do this: is not this the blood of the men that went in jeopardy of their lives? therefore he would not drink it. These things did these three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> And Abishai, the brother of Joab, the son of Zeruiah, was chief among three. And he lifted up his spear against three hundred, and slew them , and had the name among three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> Was he not most honorable of three? therefore he was their captain: yet he attained not to the first three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21; 2 Samuel 20:23"> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a valiant man, of Kabzeel, who had done many acts, he slew two lion-like men of Moab: he went down also and slew a lion in the midst of a pit in time of snow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> And he slew an Egyptian, a goodly man: and the Egyptian had a spear in his hand; but he went down to him with a staff, and plucked the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, and slew him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These things did Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and had a name among three mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23; 1 Chronicles 27:6; 1 Samuel 22:14"> He was more honorable than the thirty, but he attained not to the first three. And David set him over his guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 1 Chronicles 27:7; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> Asahel the brother of Joab was one of the thirty; Elhanan the son of Dodo of Beth-lehem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 7:1; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28"> Shammah the Harodite, Elika the Harodite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Chronicles 11:27–28; 1 Chronicles 27:9–10"> Helez the Paltite, Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:19; 1 Chronicles 11:28"> Abiezer the Anethothite, Mebunnai the Hushathite,</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:3; 2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Eliphelet the son of Ahasbai, the son of the Maachathite, Eliam the son of Ahithophel the Gilonite,</span>
