<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> He that loveth pureness of heart, for the grace of his lips the king shall be his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> The eyes of the LORD preserve knowledge, and he overthroweth the words of the transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> The slothful man saith, There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The mouth of strange women is a deep pit: he that is abhorred by the LORD shall fall therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> He that oppresseth the poor to increase his riches , and he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Bow down thy ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply thy heart to my knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> For it is a pleasant thing if thou keepest them within thee; they shall withal be fitted in thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> That thy trust may be in the LORD, I have made known to thee this day, even to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> That I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth; that thou mightest answer the words of truth to them that send to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Rob not the poor, because he is poor: neither oppress the afflicted in the gate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> For the LORD will plead their cause, and spoil the soul of those that spoiled them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; 2 Kings 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> If thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed from under thee?</span>
