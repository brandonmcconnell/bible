<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:7"> He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: but he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:24; Proverbs 12:11; 2 Peter 1:5–11"> The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is lothsome, and cometh to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Proverbs 11:5–6; Psalms 140:11"> Righteousness keepeth him that is upright in the way: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 18:11–14; Revelation 3:17; Proverbs 11:24"> There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 49:6–10"> The ransom of a man's life is his riches: but the poor heareth not rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> The light of the righteous rejoiceth: but the lamp of the wicked shall be extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well-advised is wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labor shall increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> Whoever despiseth the word shall be destroyed: but he that feareth the commandment shall be rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The law of the wise is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> Good understanding giveth favor: but the way of transgressors is hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> Every prudent man dealeth with knowledge: but a fool layeth open his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> A wicked messenger falleth into mischief: but a faithful embassador is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Poverty and shame shall be to him that refuseth instruction: but he that regardeth reproof shall be honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul: but it is abomination to fools to depart from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:10; Isaiah 3:10–11; Romans 2:7–10"> Evil pursueth sinners: but to the righteous good shall be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:26; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children: and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 28:19; Ecclesiastes 5:9"> Much food is in the tillage of the poor: but there is that is destroyed for want of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:17; Proverbs 23:13–14; Proverbs 29:15"> He that spareth his rod hateth his son: but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes.</span>
