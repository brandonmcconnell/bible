<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Be not thou envious against evil men, neither desire to be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For their heart studieth destruction, and their lips talk of mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> Through wisdom is a house built; and by understanding it is established:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> A wise man is strong; and a man of knowledge increaseth strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For by wise counsel thou shalt make thy war: and in multitude of counselors there is safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom is too high for a fool: he openeth not his mouth in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> He that deviseth to do evil shall be called a mischievous person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The thought of foolishness is sin: and the scorner is an abomination to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> If thou faintest in the day of adversity, thy strength is small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> If thou forbearest to deliver them that are drawn to death, and those that are ready to be slain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> If thou sayest, Behold, we knew it not; doth not he that pondereth the heart consider it ? and he that keepeth thy soul, doth not he know it ? and will not he render to every man according to his works?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> My son, eat thou honey, because it is good; and the honey-comb, which is sweet to thy taste:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> So shall the knowledge of wisdom be to thy soul: when thou hast found it , then there shall be a reward, and thy expectation shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Lay not wait, O wicked man , against the dwelling of the righteous; destroy not his resting-place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> Rejoice not when thy enemy falleth, and let not thy heart be glad when he stumbleth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> Lest the LORD see it , and it displease him, and he turn away his wrath from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Fret not thyself because of evil men , neither be thou envious at the wicked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For there shall be no reward to the evil man ; the candle of the wicked shall be extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My son, fear thou the LORD and the king: and meddle not with them that are given to change:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For their calamity shall rise suddenly; and who knoweth the ruin of them both?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These things also belong to the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> He that saith to the wicked, Thou art righteous; him shall the people curse, nations shall abhor him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> But to them that rebuke him shall be delight, and a good blessing shall come upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Every man shall kiss his lips that giveth a right answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Prepare thy work without, and make it fit for thyself in the field; and afterwards build thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Be not a witness against thy neighbor without cause; and deceive not with thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, I will do so to him as he hath done to me: I will render to the man according to his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> And lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face of it, and its stone wall was broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Then I saw, and considered it well: I looked upon it , and received instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> So shall thy poverty come as one that traveleth; and thy want as an armed man.</span>
