<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 6:9"> Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise:</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Jeremiah 4:14; Ephesians 5:14"> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; Proverbs 6:6; Proverbs 23:33–34"> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 24:34"> So shall thy poverty come as one that traveleth, and thy want as an armed man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Timothy 5:13; Proverbs 16:27"> A naughty person, a wicked man, walketh with a froward mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 10:10; Job 15:12"> He winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet, he teacheth with his fingers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Micah 2:1; Hosea 8:7"> Frowardness is in his heart, he deviseth mischief continually; he soweth discord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Jeremiah 19:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly; suddenly shall he be broken without remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 11:5; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 11:20"> These six things doth the LORD hate: yes, seven are an abomination to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 12:22; Psalms 101:5"> A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 6:5; Proverbs 1:16; Romans 3:15"> A heart that deviseth wicked imaginations, feet that are swift in running to mischief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 6:14; James 3:18"> A false witness that speaketh lies, and him that soweth discord among brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1; Deuteronomy 27:16; Proverbs 7:1–4"> My son, keep thy father's commandment, and forsake not the law of thy mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:3–4; 2 Corinthians 3:3"> Bind them continually upon thy heart, and tie them about thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Proverbs 3:23–24; Psalms 119:24"> When thou goest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and when thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; 2 Peter 1:19; Psalms 19:8"> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 7:5; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> To keep thee from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:14–15; Matthew 5:28; 2 Kings 9:30"> Lust not after her beauty in thy heart; neither let her take thee with her eyelids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 15:30; Proverbs 29:3; 1 Samuel 2:36"> For by means of a lewd woman a man is brought to a piece of bread: and the adulteress will hunt for the precious life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 31:9–12; Hosea 7:4–7; James 3:5"> Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his clothes not be burned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Can one go upon hot coals, and his feet not be burned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; Malachi 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:9–10"> So he that goeth in to his neighbor's wife; whoever toucheth her shall not be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:39"> Men do not despise a thief, if he stealeth to satisfy his soul when he is hungry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 19:8; Job 20:18; 2 Samuel 12:6"> But if he is found, he shall restore seven-fold; he shall give all the substance of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 7:7; Hebrews 13:4; Romans 1:22–24"> But whoever committeth adultery with a woman lacketh understanding: he that doeth it destroyeth his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 5:9–11; Psalms 51:8; Judges 16:19–21"> A wound and dishonor shall he get; and his reproach shall not be wiped away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Numbers 5:14; Song of Solomon 8:6"> For jealousy is the rage of a man: therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance.</span>
