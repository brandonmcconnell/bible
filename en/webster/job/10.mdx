<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 90:2–4; Psalms 102:24–27"> Are thy days as the days of man? are thy years as man's days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:16; Psalms 10:15; Zephaniah 1:12"> That thou inquirest after my iniquity, and searchest after my sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Daniel 3:15; Psalms 139:1–2"> Thou knowest that I am not wicked; and there is none that can deliver out of thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 43:7; Job 10:3"> Thy hands have made me and fashioned me in all my parts; yet thou dost destroy me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; Genesis 3:19"> Remember, I beseech thee, that thou hast made me as the clay; and wilt thou bring me into dust again?</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4–8; Ephesians 4:16; Job 40:17–18"> Thou hast clothed me with skin and flesh, and hast fenced me with bones and sinews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> Thou hast granted me life and favor, and thy visitation hath preserved my spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 23:13; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 45:15"> And these things hast thou hid in thy heart: I know that this is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; Psalms 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> If I sin, then thou markest me, and thou wilt not acquit me from my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Job 9:15; Job 9:20–21"> If I be wicked, woe to me; and if I be righteous, yet will I not lift up my head. I am full of confusion; therefore see thou my affliction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> For it increaseth. Thou huntest me as a fierce lion: and again thou showest thyself wonderful upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> Thou renewest thy witnesses against me, and increasest thy indignation upon me; changes and war are against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 11:20"> Why then hast thou brought me forth from the womb? O that I had expired, and no eye had seen me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:8"> I should have been as though I had not been; I should have been carried from the womb to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:1; Psalms 39:13; Psalms 103:15–16"> Are not my days few? cease then , and let me alone, that I may take comfort a little,</span>
