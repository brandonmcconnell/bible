<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> But Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> Oh that my grief were thoroughly weighed, and my calamity laid in the balances together!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> For now it would be heavier than the sand of the sea: therefore my words are swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Almighty are within me, the poison of which drinketh up my spirit: the terrors of God set themselves in array against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Doth the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or loweth the ox over his fodder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Can that which is unsavory be eaten without salt? or is there any taste in the white of an egg?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> The things that my soul refused to touch are as my sorrowful food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> Oh that I might have my request; and that God would grant me the thing that I long for!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> Even that it would please God to destroy me; that he would let loose his hand, and cut me off!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> Then should I yet have comfort; yes, I would harden myself in sorrow: let him not spare; for I have not concealed the words of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> What is my strength, that I should hope? and what is my end, that I should prolong my life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones? or is my flesh of brass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Is not my help in me? and is wisdom driven quite from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> To him that is afflicted pity should be shown from his friend; but he forsaketh the fear of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My brethren have dealt deceitfully as a brook, and as the stream of brooks they pass away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Which are blackish by reason of the ice, and in which the snow is hid:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> In the time when they become warm, they vanish: when it is hot, they are consumed out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The paths of their way are turned aside; they go to nothing, and perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> The troops of Tema looked, the companies of Sheba waited for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They were confounded because they had hoped; they came thither, and were ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> For now ye are nothing: ye see my casting down, and are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Did I say, Bring to me? or Give a reward for me of your substance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> Or, Deliver me from the enemy's hand? or, Redeem me from the hand of the mighty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Teach me, and I will hold my tongue: and cause me to understand in what I have erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> How forcible are right words! but what doth your arguing reprove?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Do ye imagine to reprove words, and the speeches of one that is desperate, which are as wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Yes, ye overwhelm the fatherless, and ye dig a pit for your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> Now therefore be content, look upon me; for it is evident to you if I lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Return, I pray you, let it not be iniquity; yes, return again, my righteousness is in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> Is there iniquity in my tongue? cannot my taste discern perverse things?</span>
