<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:7; 2 Corinthians 11:10; John 8:55"> My lips shall not speak wickedness, nor my tongue utter deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:9; Job 13:15; Job 42:7"> Far be it from me that I should justify you: till I die I will not remove my integrity from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:3; 1 John 3:20–21; Acts 24:16"> My righteousness I hold fast, and will not let it go: my heart shall not reproach me so long as I live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 4:19; 1 Samuel 25:26; 2 Samuel 18:32"> Let my enemy be as the wicked, and he that riseth up against me as the unrighteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 8:13; Job 11:20"> For what is the hope of the hypocrite, though he hath gained, when God taketh away his soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:28; Jeremiah 14:12"> Will God hear his cry when trouble cometh upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 22:26–27; Psalms 37:4; Habakkuk 3:18"> Will he delight himself in the Almighty? will he always call upon God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:17; Isaiah 8:11; Job 4:3–4"> I will teach you by the hand of God: that which is with the Almighty will I not conceal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Behold, all ye yourselves have seen it ; why then are ye thus altogether vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:19–29; Job 15:20–35; Isaiah 3:11"> This is the portion of a wicked man with God, and the heritage of oppressors, which they shall receive of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 20:10; Luke 23:29"> If his children are multiplied, it is for the sword: and his offspring shall not be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 22:18; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> Those that remain of him shall be buried in death: and his widows shall not weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3; Matthew 6:19; Habakkuk 2:6"> Though he should heap up silver as the dust, and prepare raiment as the clay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26; Proverbs 28:8"> He may prepare it , but the just shall put it on, and the innocent shall divide the silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Isaiah 51:8"> He buildeth his house as a moth, and as a booth that the keeper maketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 58:9; Psalms 73:19–20"> The rich man shall lie down, but he shall not be gathered: he openeth his eyes, and he is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:21; Job 18:11; Job 21:18"> Terrors take hold on him as waters, a tempest stealeth him away in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 83:15; Psalms 58:9"> The east wind carrieth him away, and he departeth: and as a storm hurleth him out of his place.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Lamentations 2:15; 1 Kings 9:8; Zephaniah 2:15"> Men shall clap their hands at him, and shall hiss him out of his place.</span>
