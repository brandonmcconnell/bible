<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Isaiah 11:12"> He directeth it under the whole heaven, and his lightning to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> After it a voice roareth: he thundereth with the voice of his excellence; and he will not stay them when his voice is heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Isaiah 40:28"> God thundereth marvelously with his voice; great things he doeth, which we cannot comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Ezekiel 13:13"> For he saith to the snow, Be thou on the earth; likewise to the small rain, and to the great rain of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Isaiah 26:11"> He sealeth up the hand of every man; that all men may know his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> Then the beasts go into dens, and remain in their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Isaiah 21:1"> From the south cometh the whirlwind: and cold from the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> By the breath of God frost is given: and the breadth of the waters is straitened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Isaiah 18:4"> Also by watering he wearieth the thick cloud: he scattereth his bright cloud:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And it is turned around by his counsels: that they may do whatever he commandeth them upon the face of the world on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:45; Job 38:26–27; 1 Samuel 12:18–19"> He causeth it to come, whether for correction, or for his land, or for mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Hearken to this, O Job: stand still, and consider the wondrous works of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Isaiah 40:26; Job 37:11"> Dost thou know when God disposed them, and caused the light of his cloud to shine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Isaiah 40:22"> Dost thou know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him who is perfect in knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> How thy garments are warm, when he quieteth the earth by the south wind ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Isaiah 40:22"> Hast thou with him spread out the sky which is strong, and as a molten looking-glass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Teach us what we shall say to him; for we cannot order our speech by reason of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> Shall it be told him that I speak? If a man shall speak, surely he will be swallowed up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> And now men see not the bright light which is in the clouds: but the wind passeth, and cleanseth them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> Touching the Almighty, we cannot find him out: he is excellent in power, and in judgment, and in abundance of justice: he will not afflict.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> Men therefore fear him: he respecteth not any that are wise of heart.</span>
