<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Elihu also proceeded, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Suffer me a little, and I will show thee that I have yet to speak on God's behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will bring my knowledge from afar, and will ascribe righteousness to my Maker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> For truly my words shall not be false: he that is perfect in knowledge is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> Behold, God is mighty, and despiseth not any : he is mighty in strength and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He preserveth not the life of the wicked: but giveth right to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous: but with kings are they on the throne; yes, he doth establish them for ever, and they are exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> And if they are bound in fetters, and are held in cords of affliction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Then he showeth them their work, and their transgressions that they have exceeded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> He openeth also their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they obey and serve him , they shall spend their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> But if they obey not, they shall perish by the sword, and they shall die without knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> But the hypocrites in heart heap up wrath: they cry not when he bindeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> They die in youth, and their life is among the unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> He delivereth the poor in his affliction, and openeth their ears in oppression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> Even so would he have removed thee out of the strait into a broad place, where there is no straitness; and that which should be set on thy table would be full of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> But thou hast fulfilled the judgment of the wicked: judgment and justice take hold on thee .</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> Because there is wrath, beware lest he take thee away with his stroke: then a great ransom cannot deliver thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Will he esteem thy riches? no , not gold, nor all the forces of strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Desire not the night, when people are cut off in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Take heed, regard not iniquity: for this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Behold, God exalteth by his power: who teacheth like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who hath enjoined him his way? or who can say, Thou hast wrought iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Remember that thou magnify his work, which men behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Every man may see it; man may behold it afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Behold, God is great, and we know him not, neither can the number of his years be searched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> For he maketh small the drops of water: they pour down rain according to the vapor of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> Which the clouds do drop and distill upon man abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> Also can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, or the noise of his tabernacle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> Behold, he spreadeth his light upon it, and covereth the bottom of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> For by them he judgeth the people; he giveth food in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> With clouds he covereth the light; and commandeth it not to shine by the intervening cloud .</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> The noise of it showeth concerning it, the cattle also concerning the vapor.</span>
