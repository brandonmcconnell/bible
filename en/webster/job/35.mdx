<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Elihu spoke moreover, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> Thinkest thou this to be right, that thou saidst, My righteousness is more than God's?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For thou saidst, What advantage will it be to thee? and , What profit shall I have, if I be cleansed from my sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I will answer thee, and thy companions with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Look to the heavens, and see; and behold the clouds which are higher than thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If thou sinnest, what doest thou against him? or if thy transgressions are multiplied, what doest thou to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If thou art righteous, what givest thou to him? or what receiveth he from thy hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Thy wickedness may hurt a man as thou art : and thy righteousness may profit the son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> By reason of the multitude of oppressions they make the oppressed to cry: they cry out by reason of the arm of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> But none saith, Where is God my maker, who giveth songs in the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and maketh us wiser than the fowls of heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There they cry, but none giveth answer, because of the pride of evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> Surely God will not hear vanity, neither will the Almighty regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Although thou sayest thou shalt not see him, yet judgment is before him; therefore trust thou in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> But now, because it is not so , he hath visited in his anger; yet he knoweth it not in great extremity:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> Therefore doth Job open his mouth in vain; he multiplieth words without knowledge.</span>
