<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Ecclesiastes 3:17; 2 Peter 3:7–8"> Why, seeing times are not hid from the Almighty, do they that know him not see his days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 23:10"> Some remove the landmarks: they violently take away flocks, and their feed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:6; Deuteronomy 24:10–13; 1 Samuel 12:3"> They drive away the ass of the fatherless, they take the widow's ox for a pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 28:28; Job 24:14; Ezekiel 22:29"> They turn the needy out of the way: the poor of the earth hide themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Job 39:5–7; Psalms 104:23"> Behold, as wild asses in the desert, they go forth to their work; rising betimes for a prey: the wilderness yieldeth food for them and for their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:51; Deuteronomy 28:33"> They reap every one his corn in the field: and they gather the vintage of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 22:26–27; Job 22:6; Deuteronomy 24:11–13"> They cause the naked to lodge without clothing, that they have no covering in the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Hebrews 11:38"> They are wet with the showers of the mountains, and embrace the rock for want of a shelter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:5; 2 Kings 4:1"> They pluck the fatherless from the breast, and take a pledge of the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 2:7–8; Deuteronomy 24:19; Amos 5:11–12"> They cause him to go naked without clothing, and they take away the sheaf from the hungry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:4; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 25:4"> Who make oil within their walls, and tread their wine-presses, and suffer thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Malachi 2:17; Romans 2:4–5"> Men groan from out of the city, and the soul of the wounded crieth out: yet God layeth not folly to them .</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Jude 1:6"> They are of those that rebel against the light; they know not its ways, nor abide in its paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 2:1–2; Psalms 10:8–10; Revelation 3:3"> The murderer rising with the light killeth the poor and needy, and in the night is as a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 10:11; Proverbs 7:9–10; Psalms 94:7"> The eye also of the adulterer waiteth for the twilight, saying, No eye shall see me: and disguiseth his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 3:20; Ephesians 5:11–13; Matthew 24:43"> In the dark they dig through houses, which they had marked for themselves in the day-time: they know not the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 3:5; Jeremiah 2:26; Psalms 73:18–19"> For the morning is to them even as the shades of death: if one knoweth them , they are in the terrors of the shades of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Psalms 58:7; Job 9:26"> He is swift as the waters; their portion is cursed in the earth: he beholdeth not the way of the vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:14; Luke 16:22; Proverbs 14:32"> Drouth and heat consume the snow-waters: so doth the grave those who have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Daniel 4:14; Job 18:16–17"> The womb shall forget him; the worm shall feed sweetly on him; he shall be no more remembered; and wickedness shall be broken as a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:6–7; Job 22:9; Job 29:13"> He oppresseth the barren that beareth not: and doeth not good to the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 6:4–9; Esther 3:8–10; John 19:12–16"> He draweth also the mighty with his power: he riseth up, and no man is sure of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 2:23"> Though it is given him to be in safety, on which he resteth; yet his eyes are upon their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Psalms 37:35–36; James 5:1–3"> They are exalted for a little while, but are gone and brought low; they are taken out of the way as all others , and cut off as the tops of the ears of corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:24; Job 11:2–3; Job 6:28"> And if it is not so now, who will make me a liar, and make my speech of no worth?</span>
