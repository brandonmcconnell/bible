<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> Who can discover the face of his garment? or who can come to him with his double bridle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> Who can open the doors of his face? his teeth are terrible around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> His scales are his pride, shut together as with a close seal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> One is so near to another, that no air can come between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They are joined one to another, they stick together, that they cannot be sundered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> His sneezings flash light, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> Out of his mouth go burning lamps, and sparks of fire dart forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> Out of his nostrils issueth smoke, as out of a seething pot or caldron.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> In his neck remaineth strength, and sorrow is turned into joy before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The flakes of his flesh are joined together: they are firm in themselves; they cannot be moved.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He esteemeth iron as straw, and brass as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> The arrow cannot make him flee: sling-stones are turned with him into stubble.</span>
