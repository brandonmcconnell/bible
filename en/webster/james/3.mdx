<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> My brethren, be not many teachers, knowing that we shall receive the greater condemnation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For in many things we all offend. If any man offendeth not in word, the same is a perfect man, able also to bridle the whole body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> Behold, we put bits in the mouths of horses, that they may obey us; and we turn about their whole body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Behold also the ships, which though they are so great, and are driven by fierce winds, yet they are turned about with a very small helm, withersoever the governor willeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> Even so the tongue is a little member, and boasteth great things. Behold, how great a matter a little fire kindleth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire, a world of iniquity: so is the tongue among our members, that it defileth the whole body, and setteth on fire the course of nature; and it is set on fire from hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every kind of beasts, and of birds, and of serpents, and of animals in the sea, is tamed, and hath been tamed by mankind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> But the tongue can no man tame; it is an unruly evil, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> With this we bless God, even the Father; and with this we curse men, who are made after the similitude of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> Out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing. My brethren, these things ought not so to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Doth a fountain send forth at the same place sweet water and bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> Can the fig-tree, my brethren, bear olive-berries? or a vine, figs? so no fountain can yield both salt water and fresh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who is a wise man and endued with knowledge among you? let him show by a good deportment his works with meekness of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if ye have bitter envying and strife in your hearts, glory not, and lie not against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This wisdom descendeth not from above, but is earthly, sensual, demoniacal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where envying and strife is , there is confusion and every evil work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be entreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by them that make peace.</span>
