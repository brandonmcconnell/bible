<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> There is one lawgiver, who is able to save, and to destroy: who art thou that judgest another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Come now, ye that say, To-day or to-morrow we will go into such a city, and continue there a year, and buy, and sell, and get gain:</span>
