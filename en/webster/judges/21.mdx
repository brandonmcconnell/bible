<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Mark 6:23; Acts 23:12"> Now the men of Israel had sworn in Mizpeh, saying, There shall not any of us give his daughter to Benjamin for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 20:26; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 30:4"> And the people came to the house of God, and abode there till evening before God, and lifted up their voices, and wept bitterly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Joshua 7:7–9; Isaiah 63:17"> And said, O LORD God of Israel, why is this come to pass in Israel, that there should be to-day one tribe lacking in Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; 1 Kings 8:64; Psalms 78:34–35"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that the people rose early, and built there an altar, and offered burnt-offerings, and peace-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:1"> And the children of Israel said, Who is there among all the tribes of Israel that came not up with the congregation to the LORD? For they had made a great oath concerning him that came not up to the LORD to Mizpeh, saying, He shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 21:15; Judges 20:23; Luke 19:41–42"> And the children of Israel repented for Benjamin their brother, and said, There is one tribe cut off from Israel this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> How shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing we have sworn by the LORD, that we will not give them of our daughters for wives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 2:5–6"> And they said, What one is there of the tribes of Israel that came not up to Mizpeh to the LORD? and behold, there came none to the camp from Jabesh-gilead to the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For the people were numbered, and behold, there were none of the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:5; 1 Samuel 11:7"> And the congregation sent thither twelve thousand men of the most valiant, and commanded them, saying, Go and smite the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the edge of the sword, with the women and the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 31:17–18; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And this is the thing that ye shall do, Ye shall utterly destroy every male, and every woman that hath lain by man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 7:12; Psalms 78:60"> And they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young virgins that had known no man by lying with any male: and they brought them to the camp to Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 20:47; Deuteronomy 20:10; Ephesians 2:17"> And the whole congregation sent some to speak to the children of Benjamin that were in the rock Rimmon, and to call peaceably to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 21:12; 1 Corinthians 7:2; Judges 20:47"> And Benjamin came again at that time; and they gave them wives which they had saved alive of the women of Jabesh-gilead: and yet so they sufficed them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 21:6; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Isaiah 58:12"> And the people repented for Benjamin, because the LORD had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the elders of the congregation said, How shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing the women are destroyed out of Benjamin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:55; Numbers 36:7"> And they said, There must be an inheritance for them that have escaped of Benjamin, that a tribe be not destroyed out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 11:35"> But, we may not give them wives of our daughters: for the children of Israel have sworn, saying, Cursed be he that giveth a wife to Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:10; Deuteronomy 16:1; Deuteronomy 16:13"> Then they said, Behold, there is a feast of the LORD in Shiloh yearly in a place which is on the north side of Beth-el, on the east side of the highway that goeth up from Beth-el to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore they commanded the children of Benjamin, saying, Go, and lie in wait in the vineyards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> And see, and behold, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in dances, then come ye out of the vineyards, and catch you every man his wife of the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:14"> And it shall be, when their fathers or their brethren come to us to complain, that we will say to them, Be favorable to them for our sakes: because we reserved not to each man his wife in the war: for ye did not give to them at this time, that ye should be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:48"> And the children of Benjamin did so, and took them wives, according to their number, of them that danced, whom they caught: and they went and returned to their inheritance, and repaired the cities and dwelt in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the children of Israel departed thence at that time, every man to his tribe and to his family, and they went from thence every man to his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 3:5"> In those days there was no king in Israel: every man did that which was right in his own eyes.</span>
