<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Zechariah 2:8; Jeremiah 4:7"> The word of the LORD which came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Gentiles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> Against Egypt, against the army of Pharaoh-necho king of Egypt, who was by the river Euphrates in Carshemish, whom Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon smote in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> Order ye the buckler and shield, and draw near to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> Harness the horses; and mount, ye horsemen, and stand forth with your helmets; furbish the spears, and put on the coats of mail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> Why have I seen them dismayed and turned away back? and their mighty ones are beaten down, and have fled apace, and look not back: for fear was on all sides, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> Let not the swift flee away, nor the mighty man escape! they shall stumble, and fall towards the north by the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> Who is this that cometh up as a flood, whose waters are moved as the rivers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> Egypt riseth up like a flood, and his waters are moved like the rivers; and he saith, I will go up, and will cover the earth; I will destroy the city and its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> Come up, ye horses; and rage, ye chariots; and let the mighty men come forth: the Cushites and the Libyans, that handle the shield; and the Lydians that handle and bend the bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> For this is the day of the Lord GOD of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may avenge him of his adversaries: and the sword shall devour, and it shall be satiated and made drunk with their blood: for the Lord GOD of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> Go up into Gilead, and take balm, O virgin, the daughter of Egypt: in vain shalt thou use many medicines; for thou shalt not be cured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> The nations have heard of thy shame, and thy cry hath filled the land: for the mighty man hath stumbled against the mighty, and they have fallen both together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> The word that the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet, how Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon should come and smite the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> Declare ye in Egypt, and publish in Migdol, and publish in Noph and in Tahpanhes: say ye, Stand fast, and prepare thee; for the sword shall devour around thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> Why are thy valiant men swept away? they stood not, because the LORD did drive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> He made many to fall, yes, one fell upon another: and they said, Arise, and let us go again to our own people, and to the land of our nativity, from the oppressing sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> They cried there, Pharaoh king of Egypt is but a noise; he hath passed the time appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> As I live, saith the king, whose name is the LORD of hosts, Surely as Tabor is among the mountains, and as Carmel by the sea, so shall he come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> O thou daughter dwelling in Egypt, furnish thyself to go into captivity: for Noph shall be waste and desolate without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> Egypt is like a very fair heifer, but destruction cometh; it cometh out of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> Also her hired men are in the midst of her like fatted bullocks; for they also have turned back, and have fled away together: they did not stand, because the day of their calamity had come upon them, and the time of their visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> The voice thereof shall go like a serpent; for they shall march with an army, and come against her with axes, as hewers of wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> They shall cut down her forest, saith the LORD, though it cannot be searched; because they are more than the grasshoppers, and are innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> The daughter of Egypt shall be confounded; she shall be delivered into the hand of the people of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> The LORD of hosts, the God of Israel saith: Behold, I will punish the multitude of No, and Pharaoh, and Egypt, with their gods, and their kings; even Pharaoh, and all them that trust in him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> And I will deliver them into the hand of those that seek their lives, and into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of his servants: and afterward it shall be inhabited, as in the days of old, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> But fear not thou, O my servant Jacob, and be not dismayed, O Israel: for behold, I will save thee from afar, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and be in rest and at ease, and none shall make him afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> Fear thou not, O Jacob my servant, saith the LORD: for I am with thee; for I will make a full end of all the nations whither I have driven thee: but I will not make a full end of thee, but correct thee in measure; yet will I not leave thee wholly unpunished.</span>
