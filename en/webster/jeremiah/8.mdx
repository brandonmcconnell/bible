<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:5; 2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 7:32–34"> At that time, saith the LORD, they shall bring the bones of the kings of Judah, and the bones of his princes, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, out of their graves:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:5; Jeremiah 9:22; Deuteronomy 4:19"> And they shall spread them before the sun, and the moon, and all the host of heaven, whom they have loved, and whom they have served, and after whom they have walked, and whom they have sought, and whom they have worshiped: they shall not be gathered, nor be buried; they shall be for dung upon the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:3"> And death shall be chosen rather than life by all the residue of them that remain of this evil family, who remain in all the places whither I have driven them, saith the LORD of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Micah 7:8; Hosea 6:1"> Moreover, thou shalt say to them, Thus saith the LORD; Shall they fall, and not arise? shall he turn away, and not return?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:6; Jeremiah 5:3; Zechariah 7:11"> Why then hath this people of Jerusalem slidden back by a perpetual backsliding? they hold fast deceit, they refuse to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 14:2; Ezekiel 18:28; Luke 15:17–19"> I hearkened and heard, but they spoke not aright: no man repented of his wickedness, saying, What have I done? every one turned to his course, as the horse rusheth to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Jeremiah 5:4–5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Yes, the stork in the heaven knoweth her appointed times; and the turtle and the crane and the swallow observe the time of their coming; but my people know not the judgment of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 9:41; Job 5:12–13; Isaiah 10:1–2"> How do ye say, We are wise, and the law of the LORD is with us? Lo, certainly in vain he hath made it ; the pen of the scribes is in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:15; Psalms 119:98–100; Job 5:12"> The wise men are ashamed, they are dismayed and taken: lo, they have rejected the word of the LORD; and what wisdom is in them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12–13; Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 56:10–12"> Therefore will I give their wives to others, and their fields to them that shall inherit them : for every one from the least even to the greatest is given to covetousness, from the prophet even to the priest every one dealeth falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Lamentations 2:14"> For they have healed the hurt of the daughter of my people slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when there is no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:3; Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 6:15"> Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? no, they were not at all ashamed, neither could they blush: therefore shall they fall among them that fall: in the time of their visitation they shall be cast down, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Joel 1:7; Jeremiah 17:8"> I will surely consume them, saith the LORD: there shall be no grapes on the vine, nor figs on the fig-tree, and the leaf shall fade; and the things that I have given to them shall pass away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:19"> Why do we sit still? assemble yourselves, and let us enter into the fortified cities, and let us be silent there: for the LORD our God hath put us to silence, and given us water of gall to drink, because we have sinned against the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Jeremiah 4:10; Jeremiah 8:11"> We looked for peace, but no good came ; and for a time of health, and behold, trouble!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Judges 5:22; Jeremiah 4:15–16"> The snorting of his horses was heard from Dan: the whole land trembled at the sound of the neighing of his strong ones; for they have come, and have devoured the land, and all that is in it; the city, and those that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:11; Psalms 58:4–5; Deuteronomy 32:24"> For behold, I will send serpents, cockatrices, among you, which will not be charmed, and they shall bite you, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 1:16–17; Jeremiah 6:24; Isaiah 22:4"> When I would comfort myself against sorrow, my heart is faint in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 39:3; Isaiah 13:5; Psalms 135:21"> Behold the voice of the cry of the daughter of my people because of them that dwell in a distant country: Is not the LORD in Zion? is not her king in her? Why have they provoked me to anger with their graven images, and with strange vanities?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Matthew 25:1–12; Luke 13:25"> The harvest is past, the summer is ended, and we are not saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Joel 2:6; Nahum 2:10"> For the hurt of the daughter of my people am I hurt; I am black; astonishment hath taken hold on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Genesis 37:25; Jeremiah 51:8"> Is there no balm in Gilead: is there no physician there? why then is not the health of the daughter of my people recovered.</span>
