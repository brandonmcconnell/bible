<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again the word of the LORD of hosts came to me , saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Zechariah 1:14–16; Isaiah 42:13–14"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts; I was jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I was jealous for her with great fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:16; Zechariah 2:10–11; John 1:14"> Thus saith the LORD; I have returned to Zion, and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and Jerusalem shall be called a city of truth; and the mountain of the LORD of hosts the holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 65:20–22; Job 5:26; Lamentations 5:11–15"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts; There shall yet old men and old women dwell in the streets of Jerusalem, and every man with his staff in his hand for very age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:13; Matthew 11:16–17; Jeremiah 30:19–20"> And the streets of the city shall be full of boys and girls playing in its streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Romans 4:20–21; Numbers 11:22–23"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts; If it is marvelous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in these days, should it also be marvelous in my eyes? saith the LORD of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Romans 11:25–27; Malachi 1:11"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Behold, I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:28; Ezekiel 11:20; Jeremiah 31:33"> And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; 1 Chronicles 22:13; Ezra 5:1–2"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Let your hands be strong, ye that hear in these days these words by the mouth of the prophets, who were in the day when the foundation of the house of the LORD of hosts was laid, that the temple might be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:16–18; Amos 3:6"> For before these days there was no hire for man, nor any hire for beast; neither was there any peace to him that went out or came in because of the affliction: for I set all men every one against his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; Haggai 2:19; Zechariah 8:8–9"> But now I will not be to the residue of this people as in the former days, saith the LORD of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 26:4–5; Deuteronomy 33:13; Genesis 27:28"> For the seed shall be prosperous; the vine shall give her fruit, and the ground shall give her increase, and the heavens shall give their dew; and I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things .</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:20; Zechariah 8:9; Psalms 72:17"> And it shall come to pass, that as ye were a curse among the heathen, O house of Judah, and house of Israel; so will I save you, and ye shall be a blessing: fear not, let your hands be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; Ezekiel 24:14; Jeremiah 4:28"> For thus saith the LORD of hosts; As I thought to punish you, when your fathers provoked me to wrath, saith the LORD of hosts, and I repented not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 8:13; Micah 7:18–20; Luke 12:32"> So again have I thought in these days to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah: fear ye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 7:9; Psalms 15:2"> These are the things that ye shall do; Speak ye every man the truth to his neighbor; execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Micah 2:1–3; Jeremiah 44:4"> And let none of you imagine evil in your hearts against his neighbor; and love no false oath: for all these are things that I hate, saith the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the word of the LORD of hosts came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 39:2; Zechariah 7:5"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts; The fast of the fourth month , and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth, shall be to the house of Judah joy and gladness, and cheerful feasts; therefore love the truth and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2–3; Micah 4:1–2; Zechariah 2:11"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts: It shall yet come to pass , that there shall come people, and the inhabitants of many cities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 7:2; Hosea 6:3; Psalms 146:1–2"> And the inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying, Let us go speedily to pray before the LORD, and to seek the LORD of hosts: I will go also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 8:21; Isaiah 60:3–22; Revelation 21:24"> And many people and strong nations shall come to seek the LORD of hosts in Jerusalem, and to pray before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Isaiah 60:3"> Thus saith the LORD of hosts: In those days it shall come to pass , that ten men out of all languages of the nations, shall take hold, shall even take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, We will go with you: for we have heard that God is with you.</span>
