<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Ezekiel 34:7–10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> My anger was kindled against the shepherds, and I punished the goats: for the LORD of hosts hath visited his flock the house of Judah, and hath made them as his goodly horse in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:10; Zechariah 9:8; Numbers 24:17"> Out of him came forth the corner, out of him the nail, out of him the battle-bow, out of him every oppressor together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; Zechariah 9:13; Psalms 20:7"> And they shall be as mighty men , who tread down their enemies in the mire of the streets in the battle: and they shall fight, because the LORD is with them, and the riders on horses shall be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Zechariah 10:12; Jeremiah 3:18"> And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will bring them again to place them; for I have mercy upon them: and they shall be as though I had not cast them off: for I am the LORD their God, and will hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:15; Zechariah 9:17; 1 Samuel 2:1"> And they of Ephraim shall be like a mighty man , and their heart shall rejoice as through wine: yes, their children shall see it , and be glad; their heart shall rejoice in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 7:18; Jeremiah 33:22"> I will hiss for them, and gather them: for I have redeemed them: and they shall increase as they have increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 2:23; Esther 8:17"> And I will sow them among the people: and they shall remember me in remote countries; and they shall live with their children, and turn again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 11:11; Micah 7:14; Zechariah 8:7"> I will bring them again also out of the land of Egypt, and gather them out of Assyria; and I will bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon; and place shall not be found for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Micah 5:5–6; Isaiah 43:2"> And he shall pass through the sea with affliction, and shall smite the waves in the sea, and all the deeps of the river shall dry up: and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the scepter of Egypt shall depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Isaiah 2:5; Zechariah 10:6"> And I will strengthen them in the LORD; and they shall walk up and down in his name, saith the LORD.</span>
