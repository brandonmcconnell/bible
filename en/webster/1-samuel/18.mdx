<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:3"> And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking to Saul, that the soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:15; 1 Samuel 16:21–23"> And Saul took him that day, and would let him go no more home to his father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8–17; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Samuel 21:7"> Then Jonathan and David made a covenant, because he loved him as his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Luke 15:22; Esther 6:8–9"> And Jonathan stripped himself of the robe that was upon him, and gave it to David, and his garments, even to his sword, and to his bow, and to his girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:30; 1 Samuel 18:14–15; Colossians 4:5"> And David went out whithersoever Saul sent him, and behaved himself wisely: and Saul set him over the men of war, and he was accepted in the sight of all the people, and also in the sight of Saul's servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Judges 11:34; Psalms 68:25"> And it came to pass as they came, when David had returned from the slaughter of the Philistine, that the women came out of all the cities of Israel, singing and dancing, to meet king Saul, with tabrets, with joy, and with instruments of music.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 29:5; Exodus 15:21"> And the women answered one another as they played, and said, Saul hath slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Proverbs 13:10; James 4:5"> And Saul was very wroth, and the saying displeased him; and he said, They have ascribed to David ten thousands, and to me they have ascribed thousands; and what can he have more but the kingdom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:22; Genesis 31:2; Matthew 20:15"> And Saul eyed David from that day and forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 18:29"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that the evil spirit from God came upon Saul, and he prophesied in the midst of the house: and David played with his hand, as at other times: and there was a javelin in Saul's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; Isaiah 54:17; Luke 4:30"> And Saul cast the javelin; for he said, I will smite David even to the wall. And David escaped from his presence twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:15; 1 Samuel 18:29; 1 Samuel 16:13–14"> And Saul was afraid of David, because the LORD was with him, and had departed from Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 18:16–17"> Therefore Saul removed him from him, and made him his captain over a thousand; and he went out and came in before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 39:23; Matthew 28:20; 1 Samuel 16:18"> And David behaved himself wisely in all his ways; and the LORD was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; Colossians 4:5; James 1:5"> Wherefore when Saul saw that he behaved himself very wisely, he was afraid of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> But all Israel and Judah loved David, because he went out and came in before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 25:28; 1 Samuel 18:25"> And Saul said to David, Behold, my elder daughter Merab, her will I give thee for a wife: only be thou valiant for me, and fight the LORD'S battles. For Saul said, Let not my hand be upon him, but let the hand of the Philistines be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 18:23"> And David said to Saul, Who am I? and what is my life, or my father's family in Israel, that I should be son-in-law to the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Samuel 21:8; Judges 14:20"> But it came to pass at the time when Merab Saul's daughter should have been given to David, that she was given to Adriel the Meholathite to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:28"> And Michal Saul's daughter loved David: and they told Saul, and the thing pleased him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; Exodus 10:7; Jeremiah 9:8"> And Saul said, I will give her to him, that she may be a snare to him, and that the hand of the Philistines may be against him. Wherefore Saul said to David, Thou shalt this day be my son-in-law, in the one of the two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 36:1–3; Psalms 55:21; 2 Samuel 13:28–29"> And Saul commanded his servants, saying , Commune with David secretly, and say, Behold, the king hath delight in thee, and all his servants love thee: now therefore be the king's son-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:141; Proverbs 14:20; Ecclesiastes 9:15–16"> And Saul's servants spoke those words in the ears of David. And David said, Seemeth it to you a light thing to be a king's son-in-law, seeing that I am a poor man, and lightly esteemed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the servants of Saul told him, saying, On this manner spoke David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 14:24"> And Saul said, Thus shall ye say to David, The king desireth not any dower, but a hundred foreskins of the Philistines, to be avenged of the king's enemies. But Saul thought to make David fall by the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21"> And when his servants told David these words, it pleased David well to be the king's son-in-law: and the days had not expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; Judges 14:19; 1 Samuel 18:13"> Wherefore David arose and went, he and his men, and slew of the Philistines two hundred men; and David brought their foreskins, and they gave them in full number to the king, that he might be the king's son-in-law. And Saul gave him Michal his daughter for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:20; 1 Samuel 26:25; Genesis 37:8–11"> And Saul saw and knew that the LORD was with David, and that Michal, Saul's daughter, loved him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; John 11:53; James 2:19"> And Saul was yet the more afraid of David; and Saul became David's enemy continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> Then the princes of the Philistines went forth: and it came to pass after they went forth, that David behaved himself more wisely than all the servants of Saul; so that his name was much esteemed.</span>
