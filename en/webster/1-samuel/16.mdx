<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:12; 1 Samuel 20:29; Matthew 10:16"> And Samuel said, How can I go? if Saul shall hear it , he will kill me. And the LORD said, Take a heifer with thee, and say, I have come to sacrifice to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 15:11"> And call Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will show thee what thou shalt do: and thou shalt anoint to me him whom I name to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:13; 1 Chronicles 12:17–18; 1 Samuel 21:1"> And Samuel did that which the LORD spoke, and came to Beth-lehem. And the elders of the town trembled at his coming, and said, Comest thou peaceably?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Leviticus 20:7–8; Exodus 19:10"> And he said, Peaceably: I have come to sacrifice to the LORD: sanctify yourselves, and come with me to the sacrifice. And he sanctified Jesse and his sons, and called them to the sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 17:22"> And it came to pass when they had come, that he looked on Eliab, and said, Surely the LORD'S anointed is before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:24; Jeremiah 17:10; Luke 16:15"> But the LORD said to Samuel, Look not on his countenance, or on the hight of his stature; because I have refused him: for the LORD seeth not as man seeth; for man looketh on the outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13"> Then Jesse called Abinadab, and made him pass before Samuel. And he said, Neither hath the LORD chosen this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Then Jesse made Shammah to pass by. And he said, Neither hath the LORD chosen this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–15"> Again, Jesse made seven of his sons to pass before Samuel. And Samuel said to Jesse, The LORD hath not chosen these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Samuel 13:3"> And Samuel said to Jesse, Are here all thy children? And he said, There remaineth yet the youngest, and behold, he keepeth the sheep. And Samuel said to Jesse, Send and bring him: for we will not sit down till he hath come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:7; Psalms 89:19–20; Song of Solomon 5:10"> And he sent, and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, and also of a beautiful countenance, and a good appearance. And the LORD said, Arise, anoint him: for this is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:6; Hebrews 1:9; John 3:34"> Then Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward. So Samuel arose, and went to Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:11; 1 Samuel 18:12; Judges 9:23"> But the Spirit of the LORD departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the LORD troubled him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Saul's servants said to him, Behold now, an evil spirit from God troubleth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Kings 10:8; 1 Samuel 19:9"> Let our lord now command thy servants, who are before thee, to seek a man who is a skillful player on a harp: and it shall come to pass, when the evil spirit from God is upon thee, that he will play with his hand, and thou wilt be well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Saul said to his servants, Provide me now a man that can play well, and bring him to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 39:23; Genesis 39:2"> Then answered one of the servants, and said, Behold, I have seen a son of Jesse the Beth-lehemite, that is skillful in playing, and a mighty valiant man, and a man of war, and prudent in matters, and a comely person, and the LORD is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 17:15; Psalms 78:70–72"> Wherefore Saul sent messengers to Jesse, and said, Send to me David thy son, who is with the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Proverbs 18:16; 1 Samuel 17:18"> And Jesse took an ass laden with bread, and a bottle of wine, and a kid, and sent them by David his son to Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:46; Proverbs 22:29; Psalms 146:3"> And David came to Saul, and stood before him: and he loved him greatly; and he became his armor-bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Saul sent to Jesse, saying, Let David, I pray thee, stand before me; for he hath found favor in my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14–16; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; Luke 11:24–26"> And it came to pass, when the evil spirit from God was upon Saul, that David took a harp, and played with his hand: so Saul was refreshed, and was well, and the evil spirit departed from him.</span>
