<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:15; Psalms 90:10; Genesis 27:1"> And it came to pass at that time, when Eli was laid down in his place, and his eyes began to grow dim, that he could not see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Leviticus 24:2–4; Exodus 27:20–21"> And ere the lamp of God went out in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was , and Samuel was laid down to sleep ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 12:6–11; Exodus 3:4"> That the LORD called Samuel: and he answered, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he ran to Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou calledst me. And he said, I called not; lie down again. And he went and lay down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; 1 Samuel 4:16; 2 Samuel 18:22"> And the LORD called yet again, Samuel. And Samuel arose and went to Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou didst call me. And he answered, I called not, my son; lie down again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:2; Jeremiah 9:24"> Now Samuel did not yet know the LORD, neither was the word of the LORD yet revealed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 33:14–15; 1 Corinthians 13:11–12"> And the LORD called Samuel again the third time. And he arose and went to Eli, and said, Here am I; for thou didst call me. And Eli perceived that the LORD had called the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; Psalms 85:8"> Therefore Eli said to Samuel, Go, lie down: and it shall be, if he should call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, LORD; for thy servant heareth. So Samuel went and lay down in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:4–6"> And the LORD came, and stood and called as at other times, Samuel, Samuel. Then Samuel answered, Speak; for thy servant heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Jeremiah 19:3; 2 Kings 21:12"> And the LORD said to Samuel, Behold, I will do a thing in Israel, at which both the ears of every one that heareth it shall tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:27–36; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:15"> In that day I will perform against Eli all things which I have spoken concerning his house: when I begin, I will also make an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Matthew 10:37; 1 Samuel 2:12"> For I have told him, that I will judge his house for ever, for the iniquity which he knoweth: because his sons made themselves vile, and he restrained them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 15:1"> And therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli, that the iniquity of Eli's house shall not be purged with sacrifice nor offering for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6–8; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 1:9"> And Samuel lay until the morning, and opened the doors of the house of the LORD: and Samuel feared to show Eli the vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Eli called Samuel, and said, Samuel, my son. And he answered, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; Ruth 1:17; Psalms 141:5"> And he said, What is the thing that the LORD hath said to thee? I pray thee hide it not from me: God do so to thee, and more also, if thou shalt hide any thing from me, of all the things that he said to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; Judges 10:15"> And Samuel told him every whit, and hid nothing from him. And he said, It is the LORD: let him do what seemeth him good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; 1 Samuel 9:6; 1 Kings 8:56"> And Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him, and let none of his words fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 3:10; 1 Timothy 1:12"> And all Israel, from Dan even to Beer-sheba, knew that Samuel was established to be a prophet of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Genesis 12:7; 1 Samuel 3:4"> And the LORD appeared again in Shiloh: for the LORD revealed himself to Samuel in Shiloh by the word of the LORD.</span>
