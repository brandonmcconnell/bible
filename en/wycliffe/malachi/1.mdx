<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:1; ISAIAH 13:1; HAGGAI 1:1"> The burden of the word of the Lord to Israel, in the hand of Malachi, the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:8–9; JEREMIAH 31:3; DEUTERONOMY 10:15"> I loved you, saith the Lord, and ye said, In what thing lovedest thou us? Whether Esau was not the brother of Jacob, saith the Lord, and I loved Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:10; EZEKIEL 35:3–4; JEREMIAH 49:16–18"> but I hated Esau? And I have put Seir, the hills of him, into wilderness, and his heritage into dragons of desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:14; EZEKIEL 35:9; EZEKIEL 11:10"> That if Idumea saith, We be destroyed, but we shall turn again, and build those things that be destroyed; the Lord of hosts saith these things, These men shall build, and I shall destroy; and they shall be called the terms of wickedness, and a people to whom the Lord is wroth, till into without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:7; PSALMS 58:10–11; EZEKIEL 38:16"> And your eyes shall see, and ye shall say, The Lord be magnified on the term of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 6:46; ISAIAH 1:2; JOHN 13:13–17"> The son honoureth the father, and the servant shall dread his lord; therefore if I am the father, where is mine honour? and if I am the lord, where is my dread? saith the Lord of hosts. A! ye priests, to you that despise my name; and ye say, Wherein have we despised thy name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MALACHI 1:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:27–32; MALACHI 1:8"> Ye offer on mine altar defouled, either unclean, bread, and ye say, Wherein have we defouled thee? In that thing that ye say, The board of the Lord is despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:21; LEVITICUS 22:19–25; HOSEA 8:13"> If ye offer a blind beast to be sacrificed, whether it is not evil? And if ye offer a crooked and sick beast, whether it is not evil? Offer thou it to thy duke, if it shall please him, either if he shall receive thy face, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 32:11; JEREMIAH 27:18; 1ST PETER 1:17"> And now beseech ye the cheer of the Lord, that he have mercy on you; for of your hand this thing is done, if in any manner he receive your faces, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; AMOS 5:21–24; JEREMIAH 6:20"> Who is in you that closeth doors, and burneth mine altar of his own will, either freely? Will or Delight is not to me in you, saith the Lord of hosts; and I shall not receive a gift of your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:6; ISAIAH 54:5; ISAIAH 66:19–20"> For from the rising of the sun till to the going down, my name is great among heathen men; and in each place a clean offering is sacrificed, and offered to my name; for my name is great among heathen men, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MALACHI 1:6–8; MALACHI 2:8; AMOS 2:7"> And ye have defouled it in that that ye say, The board of the Lord is defouled, and that that is put above is contemptible, or worthy for to be despised, with &#91;the&#93; fire that devoureth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MICAH 6:3; LEVITICUS 22:19–23; MARK 14:37–38"> And ye said, Lo! of travail; and ye have blown it away, saith the Lord of hosts. And ye brought in of ravens a crooked thing, and sick, and brought in a gift; whether I shall receive it of your hand? saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:1–10; ECCLESIASTES 5:4–5"> Cursed is the guileful, that hath in his flock a male beast, and he making a vow, offereth a feeble beast to the Lord; for I am a great king, saith the Lord of hosts, and my name is dreadful among folks.</span>
