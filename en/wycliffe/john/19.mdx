<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 23:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:36; JOHN 19:6"> But they cried, and said, Take away, take away, &#91;or Do away, do away&#93;; crucify him. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 23:33; LUKE 14:27; LUKE 23:26"> And he bare to himself a cross, and went out into that place, that is said Calvary, in Hebrew Golgotha;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:12; MARK 15:24–27; GALATIANS 3:13"> where they crucified him, and others twain with him, one on this side and one on that side, and Jesus in the middle.</span>

And the knights did these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 23:49; MATTHEW 27:55–56; JOHN 20:1"> But beside the cross of Jesus stood his mother, and the sister of his mother, Mary Cleophas, and Mary Magdalene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 21:24; JOHN 20:2; JOHN 2:4"> Therefore when Jesus had seen his mother, and the disciple standing, whom he loved, he saith to his mother, Woman, lo! thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:48–50; JOHN 16:32; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:2–4"> Afterward he saith to the disciple, Lo! thy mother. And from that hour the disciple took her into his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; PSALMS 22:15; JOHN 19:30"> Afterward Jesus witting, that now all things be ended, that the scripture were fulfilled, he saith, I thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:18; LUKE 23:36; MARK 15:36"> And a vessel was set full of vinegar or eisell. <span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 17:4; PHILIPPIANS 2:8; COLOSSIANS 2:14–17"> Therefore when Jesus had taken the vinegar, he said, It is ended. <span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 19:18; LUKE 23:39–43"> Therefore knights came, and they brake the thighs of the first, and of the other, that was crucified with him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST JOHN 5:6; ZECHARIAH 12:10; ZECHARIAH 13:1"> but one of the knights opened his side with a spear, and anon blood and water went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 21:24; HEBREWS 2:3–4; 1ST JOHN 1:1–3"> And he that saw, bare witnessing &#91;thereof&#93;, and his witnessing is true; and he knoweth that he saith true things, that ye believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 34:20; NUMBERS 9:12; EXODUS 12:46"> And these things were done, that the scripture should be fulfilled, Ye shall not break a bone of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 12:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:7; PSALMS 22:16–17"> And again another scripture saith, They shall see in whom they pierced through.</span>

And Pilate suffered. And so he came, and took away the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 7:50–52; SONG OF SONGS 4:14; JOHN 3:1–21"> And Nicodemus came also, that had come to him first by night, &#91;or that had come to Jesus first in the night&#93;, and brought a meddling of myrrh and aloes, as it were an hundred pound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 11:44; JOHN 20:5–7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:6"> And they took the body of Jesus, and bound it in linen clothes with sweet smelling ointments &#91;or spices&#93;, as it is custom to Jews for to bury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 23:53; MATTHEW 27:60; JOHN 20:15"> And in the place where he was crucified, was a garden, and in the garden a new grave, in which yet no man was laid &#91;or put&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JOHN 19:31; JOHN 19:14; ISAIAH 53:9"> Therefore there they put Jesus, for the vigil of Jews’ feast &#91;or for the making ready of Jews&#93;, for the sepulchre was nigh.</span>
