<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 6:1; JOHN 6:23; MATTHEW 28:16"> Afterward Jesus again showed him to his disciples, at the sea of Tiberias. And he showed him thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:21–22; JOHN 1:45–1; JOHN 4:46"> There were together Simon Peter, and Thomas, that is said Didymus, and Nathanael, that was of the Cana of Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and twain other of his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 5:5; MATTHEW 4:18–20; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:7–9"> Simon Peter saith to them, I go to fish. They say to him, And we come with thee. And they went out, and went into a boat. And in that night they took nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 20:14; LUKE 24:15–16; LUKE 24:31"> But when the morrow was come, Jesus stood in the brink; nevertheless the disciples knew not, that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:19; PHILIPPIANS 4:11–13; 1ST JOHN 2:13"> Therefore Jesus saith to them, Children, whether ye have any supping thing? They answered to him, Nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 5:4–7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:41; JOHN 2:5"> He said to them, Put &#91;or Send&#93; ye the net into the right half of the rowing, and ye shall find. And they putted &#91;or sent&#93; the net; and then they might not draw it for multitude of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 13:23; JOHN 20:2; MATTHEW 14:28–29"> Therefore that disciple, whom Jesus loved, said to Peter, It is the Lord. Simon Peter, when he had heard that it is the Lord, girt him with a coat, for he was naked, and went into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> But the other disciples came by boat, for they were not far from the land, but as a two hundred cubits &#91;or as it were two hundred cubits&#93;, drawing the net of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 21:13; 1ST KINGS 19:5–6; JOHN 21:10"> And as they came down into the land, they saw coals lying, and fish laid on, and bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus saith to them, Bring ye of the fishes, which ye have taken now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 5:6–8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:41"> Simon Peter went up, and drew the net into the land, full of great fishes, an hundred fifty and three; and when they were so many, the net was not broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:41; LUKE 9:45; JOHN 16:19"> Jesus saith to them, Come ye, and eat ye. And no man of them that sat at the meat, durst ask him, Who art thou, witting that it is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 21:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:41; LUKE 24:42–43"> And Jesus came, and took bread, and gave to them, and fish also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 20:26; JOHN 20:19"> Now this &#91;is the&#93; third time Jesus was showed to his disciples, when he had risen again from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:11; 1ST JOHN 4:19; ROMANS 15:1"> And when they had eaten, Jesus saith to Simon Peter, Simon of John, lovest thou me more than these? He saith to him, Yea, Lord, thou knowest that I love thee. Jesus saith to him, Feed thou my lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:28; 1ST PETER 5:2; 1ST PETER 2:25"> Again he saith to him, Simon of John, lovest thou me? He saith to him, Yea, Lord, thou knowest that I love thee. He saith to him, Feed thou my lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 13:38; JOHN 16:30; MARK 14:72"> He saith to him the third time, Simon of John, lovest thou me? Peter was heavy, &#91;or sorry&#93;, for he said to him the third time, Lovest thou me, and he saith to him, Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee. Jesus saith to him, Feed my sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 13:36; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:3–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:11"> Truly, truly, I say to thee, when thou were younger, thou girdedest thee, and wanderedest where thou wouldest; but when thou shalt wax older, thou shalt hold forth thine hands, and another shall gird thee, and shall lead thee whither thou wilt not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND PETER 1:14; JOHN 21:22; MATTHEW 10:38"> He said this thing, signifying by what death he should glorify God. And when he had said these things, he saith to him, Pursue thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 21:7; JOHN 20:2; JOHN 21:24"> Peter turned, and saw that disciple pursuing, whom Jesus loved, which also rested in the supper on his breast, and said to him, Lord, who is it, that shall betray thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 13:23–24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:6–7; MATTHEW 24:3–4"> Therefore when Peter had seen this disciple, he saith to Jesus, Lord, but what this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:25; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:11; MARK 9:1"> Jesus saith to him, So I will, that he dwell till I come, what to thee? pursue thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:29; JOB 33:13; DANIEL 4:35"> Therefore this word went out among the brethren, that that disciple dieth not. And Jesus said not to him, that he dieth not, but, So I will, that he dwell till I come, what to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 19:35; JOHN 15:27; 1ST JOHN 5:6"> This is that disciple, that beareth witnessing of these things, and wrote them; and we know, that his witness-ing is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 40:5; JOHN 20:30–31; PSALMS 71:15"> And there be also many other things that Jesus did, which if they be written each by itself, I deem that the world itself shall not &#91;be able to&#93; take the books, that be to be written. &#91;Amen.&#93;</span>
