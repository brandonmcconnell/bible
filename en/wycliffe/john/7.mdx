<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 5:16–18; JOHN 4:3; MATTHEW 21:38"> After these things Jesus walked into Galilee, for he would not walk into Judea, for the Jews sought to slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 14:16–19; DEUTERONOMY 16:13–16; EXODUS 23:16–17"> And there was nigh a feast day of the Jews, Scenopegia, &#91;that is, a feast of tabernacles&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:46; JOHN 7:5; LUKE 8:19"> And his brethren said to him, Pass from hence, and go into Judea, that also thy disciples see thy works that thou doest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 18:20; PROVERBS 18:1–2; MATTHEW 23:5"> for no man doeth anything in huddles, and himself seeketh to be open, &#91;+or forsooth no man doeth anything in hid place, or privy, and he seeketh to be in open&#93;. If thou doest these things, show thyself to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARK 3:21; JOHN 1:11–13; MICAH 7:5–6"> For neither his brethren believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 7:8; JOHN 2:4; JOHN 7:30"> Therefore Jesus saith to them, My time came not yet, but your time is evermore ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 15:18–19; JOHN 3:19; GALATIANS 4:16"> The world may not hate you, soothly it hateth me; for I bear witnessing thereof, that the works of it be evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 7:6; JOHN 8:20; JOHN 7:30"> Go ye up to this feast day, but I shall not go up to this feast day, for my time is not yet fulfilled &#91;or full-filled&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When he had said these things, he dwelt in Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 5:13; MATTHEW 10:16; ISAIAH 42:2–3"> And after that his brethren were gone up, then he went up to the feast day, not openly, but as in private.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 11:56"> Therefore the Jews sought him in the feast day, and said, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 7:40–43; JOHN 9:16; JOHN 7:32"> And much grutching was of him among the people. For some said, That he is good; and others said, Nay, but he deceiveth the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 9:22; JOHN 19:38; JOHN 20:19"> nevertheless no man spake openly of him, for dread of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 7:28; LUKE 19:47; JOHN 7:37"> But when the middle feast day came, Jesus went up into the temple, and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 4:22; MATTHEW 7:28–29; MATTHEW 13:54"> And the Jews wondered, and said, How knoweth this man letters, since he hath not learned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 14:10; JOHN 14:24; JOHN 17:8"> Jesus answered to them, and said, My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 8:31–32; JOHN 8:43; JEREMIAH 31:33–34"> If any man will do his will, he shall know of the teaching, whether it be of God, or I speak of myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 5:41; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:31–33; JOHN 13:31–32"> He that speaketh of himself, seeketh his own glory; but he that seeketh the glory of him that sent him, is soothfast, and unrightwiseness is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 1:17; JOHN 11:53; MATTHEW 12:14"> Whether Moses gave not to you a law, and none of you doeth &#91;or keep-eth&#93; the law? What seek ye to slay me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 10:20; JOHN 8:48; JOHN 8:52"> And the people answered, and said, Thou hast a devil; who seeketh to slay thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 7:23; JOHN 5:2–11"> Jesus answered, and said to them, I have done one work, and all ye wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:3; GENESIS 17:10–14; ROMANS 4:9–11"> Therefore Moses gave to you circumcision; not for it is of Moses, but of the fathers; and in the sabbath ye circumcise a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:5; JOHN 5:14–16; MATTHEW 12:2"> If a man take circumcision in the sabbath, that the law of Moses be not broken, have ye indignation, &#91;or wrath&#93;, to me, for I made all a man whole in the sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:3–4; JAMES 2:9; JOHN 8:15"> Do not ye deem after the face, but deem ye a rightful doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 7:10–11"> Therefore some of Jerusalem said, Whether this is not he, whom the Jews seek to slay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:7–8; JOHN 7:48; JOHN 12:42"> and lo! he speaketh openly, and they say nothing to him. Whether the princes know verily that this is Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MICAH 5:2; JOHN 7:41–42; JOHN 6:42"> But we know this man, of whence he is; but when Christ shall come, no man knoweth of whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 8:42; JOHN 8:19; JOHN 8:26"> Therefore Jesus cried in the temple teaching, and said, Ye know me, and ye know of whence I am; and I came not of myself, but he is true that sent me, whom ye know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 8:55; MATTHEW 11:27; JOHN 17:18"> I know him, and if I say that I know him not, I shall be like to you, a liar; but I know him, for of him I am, and he sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 8:20; JOHN 10:39; JOHN 7:6"> Therefore they sought to take him, and no man set on him hands, for his hour came not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 12:42; MATTHEW 12:23; JOHN 10:41–42"> And many of the people believed in him, and said, When Christ shall come, whether he shall do more tokens than those that this doeth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 18:3; LUKE 22:52–53; JOHN 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the people musing of him, these things; and the princes and the Pharisees sent min-isters, to take him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 16:5; JOHN 13:33; MARK 16:19"> Therefore Jesus said to them, Yet a little time I am with you, and I go to the Father, that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:24–31; JOHN 13:33–36; JOHN 14:6"> Ye shall seek me, and ye shall not find me; and where I am, ye may not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JAMES 1:1; ISAIAH 11:12; ZEPHANIAH 3:10"> Therefore the Jews said to them-selves, Whither shall this go, for we shall not find him? whether he will go into the scattering of heathen men, and will teach the heathen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 3:9; JOHN 7:34; JOHN 12:34"> What is this word, which he said, Ye shall seek me, and ye shall not find me; and where I am, ye may not come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:1; JOHN 4:14; JOHN 6:35"> But in the last day of the great feast, Jesus stood, and cried, and said, If any man thirsteth, come he to me, and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; ISAIAH 44:3; ISAIAH 58:11"> He that believeth in me, as the scripture saith, Floods of quick water shall flow out of his womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:17; JOEL 2:28; JOHN 14:16–17"> But he said this thing of the Spirit &#91;or of the Holy Ghost&#93;, whom men that believed in him should take; for the Spirit was not yet given, for Jesus was not yet glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:11; JOHN 1:21; JOHN 6:14"> Therefore of that company, when they had heard these words of him, they said, This is verily a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 7:52; JOHN 1:46; JOHN 1:49"> Others said, This is Christ. But some said, Whether Christ cometh from Galilee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MICAH 5:2; MATTHEW 2:5; LUKE 2:4"> Whether the scripture saith not, that of the seed of David, and of the castle of Bethlehem, where David was, Christ cometh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JOHN 9:16; JOHN 10:19; JOHN 7:12"> Therefore dissension was made among the people for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="JOHN 7:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:11; JOHN 8:20"> And some of them would have taken him, but no man set hands on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="JOHN 7:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:21–27"> Therefore the ministers came to the bishops, and Pharisees, and they said to them, Why brought ye not him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:28–29; LUKE 4:22; JOHN 7:26"> The ministers answered, Never man spake so, as this man speaketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JOHN 7:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:8; 2ND KI 18:32"> Therefore the Pharisees answered to them, Whether ye be deceived also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JOHN 12:42; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:8"> whether any of the princes, or of the Pharisees believed in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20–21; JOHN 9:40; JOHN 9:34"> But this people, that knoweth not the law, be cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JOHN 19:39; JOHN 3:1–2"> Nicodemus saith to them, he that came to him by night, that was one of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:13; DEUTERONOMY 1:17; DEUTERONOMY 19:15–19"> Whether our law deemeth a man, but it have first heard of him &#91;or no but first it have heard of him&#93;, and know what he doeth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="JOHN 7:41; JOHN 1:46; ISAIAH 9:1–2"> They answered, and said to him, Whether thou art a man of Galilee also? Seek thou scriptures, and see thou, that a prophet riseth not of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="PSALMS 33:10; JOB 5:12–13; PSALMS 76:10"> And they turned again, each into his house.</span>
