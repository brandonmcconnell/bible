<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 3:26; JOHN 3:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:5"> Therefore as Jesus knew, that the Pharisees heard, that Jesus maketh and baptizeth more disciples than John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:48; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:13–17"> though Jesus baptized not, but his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:23; JOHN 10:40; JOHN 2:11"> he left Judea, and went again into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 17:11; LUKE 2:49; LUKE 9:51–52"> And it behooved him to pass by Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:32; GENESIS 33:19; GENESIS 48:22"> Therefore Jesus came into a city of Samaria, that is called Sychar, beside the place &#91;or the field&#93; that Jacob gave to Joseph, his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:15; HEBREWS 2:17; MATTHEW 8:24"> And the well of Jacob was there; and Jesus was weary of the journey, and sat thus upon the well. And the hour was, as it were the sixth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:42; 1ST KINGS 17:10; GENESIS 24:43"> And a woman came from Samaria, to draw water. And Jesus saith to her, Give me drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 9:13; JOHN 4:39; JOHN 4:5"> And his disciples were gone into the city, to buy meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:28; MATTHEW 10:5; 2ND KI 17:24–41"> Therefore that woman of Samaria saith to him, How thou, that art a Jew, askest of me a drink, that am a woman of Samaria? for &#91;the&#93; Jews used not to deal with &#91;the&#93; Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:17; JOHN 7:37–39"> Jesus answered, and said to her, If thou knewest the gift of God, and who it is, that saith to thee, Give me drink, thou peradventure wouldest have asked of him, and he should have given to thee quick water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; JOHN 3:4"> The woman saith to him, Sire, thou hast not wherein to draw, and the pit is deep; whereof then hast thou quick water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 4:6; JOHN 8:53; MATTHEW 12:42"> Whether thou art greater than our father Jacob, that gave to us the pit? and he drank thereof, and his sons, and his beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 6:27; ISAIAH 65:13–14; LUKE 16:24"> Jesus answered, and said to her, Each man that drinketh of this water, shall thirst again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 7:38–39; JOHN 6:35; ISAIAH 49:10"> but he that drinketh of the water that I shall give him, shall not thirst without end; but the water that I shall give him, shall be made in him a well of water, springing up into everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 6:34; ROMANS 8:5; PSALMS 4:6"> The woman saith to him, Sire, give me this water, that I thirst not, neither come hither to draw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:23; JOHN 4:18"> Jesus saith to her, Go, call thine husband, and come hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The woman answered, and said, I have none husband &#91;or I have not an husband&#93;. Jesus saith to her, Thou saidest well, That I have none husband &#91;or I have not an husband&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:10–11; ROMANS 7:3"> for thou hast had five husbands, and he that thou hast &#91;now&#93;, is not thine husband. This thing thou saidest soothly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 7:39; JOHN 6:14; JOHN 9:17"> The woman saith to him, Lord, I see, that thou art a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 12:6–7; DEUTERONOMY 27:12; DEUTERONOMY 11:29"> Our fathers worshipped in this hill, and ye say, that at Jerusalem is a place, where it behooveth to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:8; MALACHI 1:11; JOHN 4:23"> Jesus saith to her, Woman, believe thou to me, for the hour shall come, when neither in this hill, neither in Jerusalem, ye shall worship the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:23; ISAIAH 2:3; ROMANS 9:4–5"> Ye worship that that ye know not; we worship that that we know; for health is of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:3; JOHN 5:25; MATTHEW 15:7–9"> But the time is come, and now it is, when true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and truth; for also the Father seeketh such, that worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:17; MATTHEW 15:8–9; PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> God is a Spirit, and it behooveth them that worship him, to worship in spirit and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 1:49; DANIEL 9:24–26; JOHN 1:41–42"> The woman saith to him, I know that Messiah is come, that is said Christ; therefore when he cometh, he shall tell us all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 8:24; MARK 14:61–62; ROMANS 10:20–21"> Jesus saith to her, I am he &#91;or I am&#93;, that speaketh with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 7:39; JOHN 4:8–9"> And anon his disciples came, and wondered, that he spake with the woman; nevertheless no man said to him, What seekest thou, or, What speakest thou with her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 4:7; LUKE 24:33; MARK 16:8–10"> Therefore the woman left her water pot, and went into the city, and said to the men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:24–25; JOHN 4:25"> Come ye, and see a man, that said to me all things that I have done; whether he be Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ROMANS 5:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:5–8; LUKE 17:16–18"> And they went out of the city, and came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 1:38; GENESIS 24:33; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:30–34"> In the meanwhile his disciples prayed him, and said, Master &#91;or Rabbi&#93;, eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOB 23:12; PSALMS 119:103; JOHN 4:34"> But he said to them, I have meat to eat, that ye know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:6–11; LUKE 9:45"> Therefore the disciples said together, Whether any man hath brought him meat to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOHN 6:38; JOHN 5:30; JOB 23:12"> Jesus saith to them, My meat is that I do the will of him that sent me, &#91;and&#93; that I perform the work of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKE 10:2–3; MATTHEW 9:37–38; JOHN 4:30"> Whether ye say not, that yet four months be, and ripe corn cometh? Lo! I say to you, lift up your eyes, and see ye the fields, for now they be white to reap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JAMES 5:19–20; ROMANS 1:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:5–9"> And he that reapeth taketh hire, and gathereth fruit into everlasting life; that both he that soweth, and he that reapeth, have joy together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MICAH 6:15; JOB 31:8; LUKE 19:21"> In this thing is the word true, For one is that soweth, and another that reapeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 1:7; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:42–43"> I sent you to reap, that that ye have not travailed; other men have travailed, and ye have entered into their travails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 4:29; JOHN 4:5; JOHN 10:41–42"> And of that city many &#91;of the&#93; Samaritans believed in him, for the word of the woman, that bare witnessing, That he said to me all things that I have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:15; LUKE 24:29"> Therefore when Samaritans came to him, they prayed him to dwell there; and he dwelt there two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:4–5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:12"> And many more believed for his word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:14; JOHN 1:29; 1ST JOHN 5:20"> and said to the woman, That now not for thy speech we believe; for we have heard, and we know, that this is verily the Saviour of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:13; ROMANS 15:8; MARK 7:27–28"> And after two days he went out from thence, and went into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:57; LUKE 4:24; MARK 6:4"> And he bare witnessing, that a prophet in his own country hath none honour, &#91;or worship&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="JOHN 2:23; JOHN 2:13–16; DEUTERONOMY 16:16"> Therefore when he came into Galilee, men of Galilee received him, when they had seen all things that he had done in Jerusalem in the feast day; for also they had come to the feast day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="JOHN 2:1–11; LUKE 7:2; HOSEA 5:15"> Therefore he came again into the Cana of Galilee, where he made the water &#91;into&#93; wine. And there was a little king, whose son was sick at Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JOHN 4:3; JOHN 11:21; JOHN 11:32"> When this had heard, that Jesus should come from Judea into Galilee, he went to him, and prayed him, that he should come down, and heal his son; for he began to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JOHN 20:29; HEBREWS 2:4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:22"> Therefore Jesus said to him, But ye see tokens, and great wonders, ye believe not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARK 5:23; PSALMS 88:10–12; PSALMS 40:17"> The little king saith to him, Lord, come down, before that my son die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:13; MARK 7:29–30; ROMANS 4:20–21"> Jesus saith to him, Go, thy son liveth. The man believed to the word, that Jesus said to him, and he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1ST KINGS 17:23; JOHN 4:50; JOHN 4:53"> And now when he came down, the servants came to meet him, and told to him, and said, That his son lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And he asked of them the hour, in which he was amended. And they said to him, For yesterday in the seventh hour the fever left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; LUKE 19:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:34"> Therefore the father knew, that that hour it was, in which Jesus said to him, Thy son liveth; and he believed, and all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="JOHN 4:45; JOHN 2:1–11"> Jesus did again this second token, when he came from Judea into Galilee.</span>
