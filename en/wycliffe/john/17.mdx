<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 109:6–19; JOHN 10:27–28; JOHN 18:9"> While I was with them, I kept them in thy name; those that thou gavest to me, I kept, and none of them perished, but the son of perdition, that the scripture be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:2; GALATIANS 5:22; JOHN 15:11"> But now I come to thee, and I speak these things in the world, that they have my joy fulfilled in them-selves.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 121:7; 1ST JOHN 5:18; MATTHEW 6:13"> I pray not, that thou take them away from the world, but that thou keep them from evil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:18; JOHN 14:20; MARK 16:17–20"> And I have given to them the clearness, that thou hast given to me, that they be one, as we be one;</span>
