<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:15; JOHN 10:8–10; ROMANS 16:18"> Truly, truly, I say to you, he that cometh not in by the door into the fold of &#91;the&#93; sheep, but ascendeth &#91;or goeth up&#93; by another way, is a night thief and a day thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 10:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:28; 1ST PETER 5:4"> But he that entereth by the door, is the shepherd of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:11–16; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:19; JOHN 10:4"> To this the porter openeth, and the sheep hear his voice, and he calleth his own sheep by name, and leadeth them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:30; JOHN 10:16; JOHN 12:26"> And when he hath done out &#91;or hath sent out&#93; his own sheep, he goeth before them, and the sheep pursue him; for they know his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:5–6; COLOSSIANS 2:6–10; MARK 4:24"> But they pursue not an alien, but flee from him; for they have not known the voice of aliens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:11; PSALMS 82:5; PROVERBS 28:5"> Jesus said to them this proverb; but they knew not what he spake to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 14:6; EPHESIANS 2:18; JOHN 10:1"> Therefore Jesus said to them again, Truly, truly, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:2; JEREMIAH 23:1; JOHN 10:1"> As many as have come, were night thieves and day thieves, but the sheep heard not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 14:6; EPHESIANS 2:18; JOHN 10:7"> I am the door. If any man shall enter by me, he shall be saved; and he shall go in, and shall go out, and he shall find pastures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; JOHN 6:51; JOHN 12:47"> A night thief cometh not, but that he steal, slay, and lose; and I came, that they have life, and have more plenteously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 10:14–15; PSALMS 23:1; JOHN 15:13"> I am a good shepherd; a good shepherd giveth his life for his sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:29; ZECHARIAH 11:16–17; EZEKIEL 34:2–6"> But an hired hind, and that is not the shepherd, whose be not the sheep his own &#91;or whose the sheep be not his own&#93;, seeth a wolf coming, and he leaveth the sheep, and fleeth; and the wolf ravisheth, and disperseth, &#91;or scattereth&#93;, the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:20; JOHN 12:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:17"> And the hired hind fleeth, for he is an hired hind, and it pertaineth not to him of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 10:27; JOHN 10:11; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:19"> I am a good shepherd, and I know my sheep, and my sheep know me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:4; JOHN 10:11; ISAIAH 53:10"> As the Father hath known me, I know the Father; and I put my life for my sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:8; JOHN 11:52; 1ST PETER 2:25"> I have other sheep, that be not of this fold, and it behooveth me to bring them together, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be made one fold and one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 10:18; JOHN 10:11; HEBREWS 2:9"> Therefore the Father loveth me, for I put my life, that again I take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:9; TITUS 2:14; JOHN 2:19–21"> No man taketh it from me, but I put it of myself. I have power to put it, and I have power to take it again. This commandment I have taken of my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 9:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:3; JOHN 7:40–43"> Again dissension was made among the Jews for these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 7:20; MARK 3:21; JOHN 8:47–48"> And many of them said, He hath a devil, and maddeth, &#91;or waxeth mad&#93;; what hear ye him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 4:11; ISAIAH 35:5–6; PSALMS 94:9"> Other men said, These words be not of a man that hath a devil. Whether the devil &#91;or a devil&#93; may open the eyes of blind men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But the feasts of hallowing of the temple were made in Jerusalem, and it was winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:11"> And Jesus walked in the temple, in the porch of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 22:67–70; JOHN 1:19; JOHN 8:25"> Therefore the Jews came about him, and said to him, How long takest thou away our soul? if thou art Christ, say thou to us openly &#91;or plainly&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 10:38; JOHN 8:58; JOHN 14:11"> Jesus answered to them, I speak to you, and ye believe not; the works that I do in the name of my Father, bear witnessing of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 8:47; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:3–4; JOHN 12:37–40"> But ye believe not, for ye be not of my sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 10:16; JOHN 10:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:20"> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they pursue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 6:39–40; JOHN 6:37; JOHN 3:15–16"> And I give to them everlasting life, and they shall not perish without end, and none shall ravish them out of mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 6:37; JOHN 17:2; JOHN 17:9"> That thing that my Father gave to me, is more than all things; and no man may ravish from my Father’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 1:1–2; JOHN 14:9; JOHN 17:21–23"> I and the Father be one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 5:18; JOHN 8:59; 1ST SAMUEL 30:6"> The Jews took up stones, to stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:12; JOHN 5:36; PSALMS 35:12"> Jesus answered to them, I have showed to you many good works of my Father, for which work of them stone ye me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 5:18; LEVITICUS 24:16; JOHN 10:30"> The Jews answered to him, We stone thee not of good work, but of blasphemy, and for thou, since thou art a man, makest thyself God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 82:6–7; EXODUS 7:1; PSALMS 82:1"> Jesus answered to them, Whether it is not written in your law, That I said, Ye be gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:35; ROMANS 13:1; LUKE 16:17"> If he said that they were gods, to whom the word of God was made, and the scripture may not be undone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 5:17–18; JOHN 3:17; JEREMIAH 1:5"> that that the Father hath hallowed, and hath sent into the world, ye say, That thou blasphemest, for I said, I am God’s Son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 15:24; JOHN 10:25; JOHN 12:37–40"> If I do not the works of my Father, do not ye believe to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 14:20; JOHN 10:25; JOHN 10:30"> but if I do, though ye will not believe to me, believe ye to the works; that ye know and believe, that the Father is in me, and I in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 7:30; JOHN 8:59; JOHN 7:44"> Therefore they sought to take him, and he went out of their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 1:28; JOHN 11:54; JOHN 7:1"> And he went again over Jordan, into that place where John was first baptizing, and he dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 1:33–34; JOHN 1:29–30; MARK 1:37"> And many came to him, and said, For John did no miracle &#91;or sign&#93;; and all things whatever John said of this, were sooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JOHN 4:41; JOHN 2:23; JOHN 7:31"> And many believed in him.</span>
