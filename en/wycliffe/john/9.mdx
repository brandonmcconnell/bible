<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Jesus passing, saw a man blind from the birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 13:2; JOHN 9:34; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:4"> And his disciples asked him, Master, who sinned, this man, or his elders, &#91;or Rabbi, who sinned, this man, or his father and mother&#93;, that he should be born blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 11:4; JOHN 11:40; ECCLESIASTES 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, Neither this man sinned, neither his elders &#91;or neither his father and mother&#93;; but that the works of God be showed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 12:35; EPHESIANS 5:16; JOHN 4:34"> It behooveth me to work the works of him that sent me, as long as the day is &#91;or the while the day is&#93;; the night shall come, when no man may work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; JOHN 12:46; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:47"> As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 8:23; MARK 7:33; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:18"> When he had said these things, he spat into the earth, and made clay of the spittle, and anointed the clay on his eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 35:5; JOHN 9:11; JOHN 11:37"> and said to him, Go, and be thou washed in the water, &#91;or cistern&#93;, of Siloam, that is to say, Sent. Then he went, and washed, and came seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:8; 1ST SAMUEL 21:11; LUKE 18:35"> And so neighbours, and they that had seen him before, for he was a beggar, said, Whether this is not he, that sat, and begged?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Other men said, That this it is; and other men said, Nay, but he is like him. But he said, That I am &#91;he&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 9:26; JOHN 9:21; ECCLESIASTES 11:5"> Therefore they said to him, How be thine eyes opened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 9:6–7; JOHN 9:27; JEREMIAH 36:17"> He answered, That man, that is said Jesus, made clay, and anointed mine eyes, and said to me, Go thou to the water, &#91;or cistern&#93;, of Siloam, and wash; and I went, and washed, and saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 5:11–13; EXODUS 2:18–20; JOHN 7:11"> And they said to him, Where is he? He said, I know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 11:57; JOHN 11:46–47; JOHN 12:42"> They led him that was blind to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 5:9; LUKE 13:10–17; MATTHEW 12:1–14"> And it was sabbath, when Jesus made clay, and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 9:26–27; JOHN 9:10–11"> Again the Pharisees asked him, how he had seen. And he said to them, He laid &#91;or put&#93; to me clay on the eyes; and I washed, and I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 7:43; JOHN 10:19; MATTHEW 12:2"> Therefore some of the Pharisees said, This man is not of God, that keepeth not the sabbath. Other men said, How may a sinful man do these signs, &#91;or miracles&#93;. And strife &#91;or division&#93; was among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 4:19; JOHN 6:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:22–26"> Therefore they said again to the blind man, What sayest thou of him, that opened thine eyes? And he said, That he is a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:15–19; JOHN 5:44; ISAIAH 26:11"> Therefore the Jews believed not of him, that he was blind, and had seen, till they called his father and mother, that had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 9:8–9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:10"> And they asked them, and said, Is this your son, which ye say was born blind? how then seeth he now?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His father and mother answered to them, and said, We know, that this is our son, and that he was born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but how he seeth now, we know not, or who opened his eyes, we know not; ask ye him, he hath age, speak he of himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 7:13; JOHN 16:2; LUKE 6:22"> His father and mother said these things, for they dreaded the Jews; for then the Jews had conspired, that if any man acknowledged him Christ, he should be done out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 9:21"> Therefore his father and mother said, That he hath age, ask ye him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:19; JOHN 9:16; JOHN 8:49"> Therefore again they called the man, that was blind, and said to him, Give thou glory to God; we know, that this man is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST JOHN 5:10; JOHN 9:30; JOHN 5:11"> Then he said, If he is a sinner, I know not; one thing I know, that when I was blind, now I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Therefore they said to him, What did he to thee? how opened he thine eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 22:67; JOHN 9:10–15"> He answered to them, I said to you now, and ye heard; what will ye again hear? whether ye will be made his disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ROMANS 2:17; JOHN 7:19; MATTHEW 27:39"> Therefore they cursed him, and said, Be thou his disciple; we be &#91;the&#93; disciples of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 8:14; NUMBERS 12:2–7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:35"> We know, that God spake to Moses; but we know not this, of whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 12:37; PSALMS 119:18; ISAIAH 29:18"> That man answered, and said to them, For in this is a wonderful thing, that ye know not, of whence he is, and he hath opened mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:29; ISAIAH 1:15; PROVERBS 28:9"> And we know, that God heareth not sinful men, but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, he heareth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 64:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:18; JOB 20:4"> From the world it is not heard, that any man opened the eyes of a blind-born man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 9:16; JOHN 3:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:38–39"> but this man were of God, he might not do anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOHN 9:22; JOHN 9:2; ISAIAH 66:5"> They answered, and said to him, Thou art all born in sins, and teachest thou us? &#91;or thou teachest us?&#93; And they put him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 20:31; 1ST JOHN 5:13; JOHN 10:36"> Jesus heard, that they had put him out; and when he had found him, he said to him, Believest thou in the Son of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ROMANS 10:14; SONG OF SONGS 5:9; JOHN 1:38"> He answered, and said, Lord, who is he, that I believe in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 4:26; PSALMS 25:8–9; JOHN 14:21–23"> And Jesus said to him, And thou hast seen him, and he it is, that speaketh with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:33; MATTHEW 28:9; LUKE 24:52"> And he said, Lord, I believe. And he fell down, and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 3:19; JOHN 12:46; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18"> Therefore Jesus said to him, I came into this world, in doom, that they that see not, see, and they that see, be made blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17; MATTHEW 15:12–14; JOHN 7:47–52"> And some of the Pharisees heard, that were with him, and they said to him, Whether we be blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:21; JOHN 15:22–24; 1ST JOHN 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, If ye were blind, ye should not have sin; but now ye say, That we see, your sin dwelleth still.</span>
