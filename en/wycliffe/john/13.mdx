<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 51:7; PSALMS 51:2; 1ST PETER 3:21"> Simon Peter saith to him, Lord, not only my feet, but both the hands and the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 15:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:1; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17"> Jesus saith to him, He that is washed, hath no need but that he wash the feet, but he is all clean; and ye be clean, but not all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 13:26; JOHN 13:2; JOHN 13:21"> For he knew, who it was that should betray him; therefore he said, Ye be not all clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 13:7; JOHN 13:4; EZEKIEL 24:24"> And so after that he had washed their feet, he took his clothes; and when he was set to meat again, again he said to them, Ye know what I have done to you, &#91;or Know ye what I have done to you?&#93;.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:6; MATTHEW 11:29; 1ST PETER 3:17–18"> for I have given to you ensample, that as I have done to you, so do ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 6:40; JOHN 15:20; MATTHEW 10:24–25"> Truly, truly, I say to you, the servant is not greater than his lord, neither an apostle is greater than he that sent him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:10; JOHN 14:29; JOHN 16:4"> Truly, I say to you before it be done, that when it is done, ye believe that I am.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:17; LUKE 1:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:16"> Therefore Simon Peter beckoned to him, and said to him, Who is it, of whom he saith?</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:23; JOHN 13:30; MARK 14:19–20"> Jesus answered, He it is, to whom I shall areach a sop of bread. And when he had wet bread &#91;or had dipped in bread&#93;, he gave to Judas of Simon Iscariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 22:3; JOHN 13:2; ECCLESIASTES 9:3"> And after the morsel, then Satan entered into him. And Jesus saith to him, That thing that thou doest, do thou swiftly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And none of them that sat at the meat knew, whereto &#91;or what thing&#93; he said to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 12:5–6; JOHN 13:1; EPHESIANS 4:28"> For some guessed, for Judas had purses, that Jesus had said to him, Buy thou those things, that be needful to us to the feast day, or that he should give something to needy men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:7; JOB 24:13–15; PROVERBS 4:16"> Therefore when he had taken the morsel, he went out anon; and it was night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 14:13; 1ST PETER 4:11; JOHN 7:39"> Therefore when he was gone out, Jesus said, Now man’s Son is clarified, and God is clarified in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:1; ISAIAH 53:10–12; JOHN 17:4–6"> If God is clarified in him, and God shall clarify him in himself, and anon he shall clarify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 7:33–34; JOHN 8:21–24; JOHN 16:16–22"> Little sons, yet a little I am with you; ye shall seek me, and, as I said to the Jews, Whither I go, ye may not come; and to you I say now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:2; LEVITICUS 19:18; JOHN 15:17"> I give to you a new commandment, that ye love together, as I have loved you, and that ye love together.</span>
