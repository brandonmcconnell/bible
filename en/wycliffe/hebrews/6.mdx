<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:12–14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:1; HEBREWS 9:14"> Therefore we bringing in a word of the beginning of Christ, be we born to the perfection of him, not again laying the foundament of penance from dead works, and of the faith to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:13–57; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31–32; ROMANS 6:3–5"> and of teaching of baptisms, and of laying on of hands, and of rising again of dead men, and of everlasting doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:19"> And this thing we shall do, if God shall suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:26–29; 2ND PETER 2:20–22; MATTHEW 5:13"> But it is impossible, that they that be once enlightened &#91;or illumined&#93;, and have tasted also an heavenly gift, and be made partners of the Holy Ghost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:3; LUKE 8:13; MATTHEW 13:20–21"> and nevertheless have tasted the good word of God, and the virtues of the world to coming &#91;or to come&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:21; HEBREWS 10:29; HEBREWS 6:4"> and be slid far away, that they be renewed again to penance. Which again crucify to themselves the Son of God, and have him to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSEA 10:12; ISAIAH 44:3; PSALMS 65:9–13"> For the earth that drinketh rain oft coming on it, and bringeth forth covenable herb, to them of which it is tilled, taketh blessing of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 3:17–18; JOHN 15:6; LUKE 13:7–9"> But that that is bringing forth thorns and briars, is reprovable, and next to curse, whose ending shall be into burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:39; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:10; HEBREWS 6:4–6"> But, ye most dearworthy, we trust of you better things, and nearer to health, though we speak so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:42; HEBREWS 13:16; GALATIANS 6:10"> For God is not unjust, that he forget your work and love, which ye have showed in his name; for ye have ministered to saints, and minister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND PETER 1:5–8; HEBREWS 3:6; ROMANS 5:2–5"> And we covet that each of you show the same busyness to the &#91;full&#93;-filling of hope into the end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:7; HEBREWS 10:36; 1ST JOHN 2:25"> that ye be not made slow, but also pursuers of them, which by faith and patience shall inherit the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 22:15–18; LUKE 1:73; JEREMIAH 22:5"> For God promising to Abraham, for he had none greater, by whom he should swear, swore by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; DEUTERONOMY 1:10; NEHEMIAH 9:23"> and said, I blessing shall bless thee, and I multiplying shall multiply thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 12:2–3; ROMANS 4:17–25; EXODUS 1:7"> and so he long abiding had the promise &#91;or got repromission&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 22:11; HEBREWS 6:13; GENESIS 31:53"> For men swear by a greater than themselves, and the end of all their plea, &#91;or all their controversy, or debate&#93;, is an oath to confirmation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:18; PROVERBS 19:21; ISAIAH 55:11"> In which thing God willing to show plenteouslier, to the heirs of his promise the firmness &#91;or the unmoveableness&#93; of his counsel, put betwixt an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; TITUS 1:2; 1ST SAMUEL 15:29"> that by two things unmoveable, by which it is impossible that God lie, we have strongest solace, &#91;or comfort&#93;, we that flee together to hold the hope that is put forth to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 43:5; ROMANS 5:5–10; HEBREWS 9:7"> Which hope as an anchor we have secure to the soul, and firm, and going into the inner things of hiding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:14; HEBREWS 3:1; HEBREWS 5:6"> where the before-goer, Jesus, that is made bishop without end by the order of Melchizedek, entered for us.</span>
