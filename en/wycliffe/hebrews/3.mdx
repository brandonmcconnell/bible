<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:14; HEBREWS 4:14–15"> Therefore, holy brethren, and partners of heavenly calling, behold ye the apostle and the bishop of our confession, Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:7; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:15; EPHESIANS 2:22"> which is true to him that made him, as also Moses in all the house of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:9; 1ST PETER 2:5–7; ZECHARIAH 6:12–13"> But this bishop is had worthy of more glory than Moses, by as much as he hath more honour of the house, that made the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:3; HEBREWS 1:2; ESTHER 2:10"> For each house is made of some man; he that made all things of nought is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:7; HEBREWS 3:2; EXODUS 14:31"> And Moses was true in all his house, as a servant, into witnessing of those things that were to be said;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16; HEBREWS 10:35"> but Christ as a son in his house. Which house we be, if we hold firm trust and glory of hope into the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 95:7–11; HEBREWS 3:15; ISAIAH 55:3"> Wherefore as the Holy Ghost saith, Today, if ye have heard his voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:14; EXODUS 17:7; ZECHARIAH 7:11–12"> do not ye harden your hearts, as in wrathing, like the day of temptation in desert;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:36; DEUTERONOMY 4:9; EXODUS 19:4"> where your fathers tempted me, and proved, and saw my works forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 95:10; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:10–12; PSALMS 67:2"> Wherefore I was wroth to this generation, and I said, Evermore they err in heart, for they knew not my ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:34–35; HEBREWS 4:3; NUMBERS 32:10–13"> to which I swore in my wrath, they shall not enter into my rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:5; JEREMIAH 7:24; JEREMIAH 17:9"> Brethren, see ye, lest peradven-ture in any of you be an evil heart of unbelief, to depart from the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:11; HEBREWS 10:24–25; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:2"> But admonish yourselves by all days, the while today is named, that none of you be hardened by fallacy &#91;or falseness&#93; of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:6; HEBREWS 6:11; HEBREWS 12:10"> For we be made partners of Christ, if nevertheless we hold the beginning of his substance firm into the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:7–8; PSALMS 95:7; HEBREWS 10:38"> While it is said, today, if ye have heard the voice of him, do not ye harden your hearts, as in that wrathing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:2; NUMBERS 14:30; NUMBERS 26:65"> For some men hearing wrathed, but not all they that went out of Egypt by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:29; NUMBERS 26:64–65; NUMBERS 14:32–33"> But to whom was he wrathed forty years? Whether not to them that sinned, whose carrions were cast down in desert?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:30; HEBREWS 4:6; DEUTERONOMY 1:34–35"> And to whom swore he, that they should not enter into the rest of him &#91;or into his rest&#93;, no but to them that were unbelieveful?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JUDE 1:5; JOHN 3:36; MARK 16:16"> And we see, that they might not enter into the rest of him for unbelief.</span>
