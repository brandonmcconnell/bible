<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:3; HEBREWS 10:11; HEBREWS 7:27"> For each bishop taken of men, is ordained for men in these things that be to God, that he offer gifts and sacrifices for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:15; HEBREWS 2:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> Which may together sorrow with them, that be unknowing, and err; for also he is environed with infirmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:7; HEBREWS 9:7; LEVITICUS 16:6"> And therefore he oweth, as for the people, so also for himself, to offer for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 28:1; NUMBERS 16:40; NUMBERS 16:5"> Neither any man taketh to him honour, but he that is called of God, as Aaron was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; JOHN 8:54; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:33"> So Christ clarified not himself, that he were bishop, but he that spake to him, Thou art my Son, today I engendered thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 110:4; HEBREWS 7:17; HEBREWS 7:21"> As in another place he saith, Thou art a priest without end, after the order of Melchizedek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 14:32–39; LUKE 22:41–44; JOHN 17:1"> Which in the days of his flesh offered, with great cry and tears, prayers and beseechings to him that might make him safe from death, and was heard for his reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:8; HEBREWS 10:5–9; JOHN 4:34"> And when he was God’s Son, he learned obedience of these things that he suffered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:10; HEBREWS 9:12; HEBREWS 9:15"> and he brought to the end &#91;or he led to perfection&#93; is made cause of everlasting health to all that obey him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:5–6; HEBREWS 6:20"> and is called of God a bishop, by the order of Melchizedek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:27; 2ND PETER 3:16"> Of whom there is to us a great word for to say, and able to be expounded, for ye be made feeble to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:1–3; 1ST PETER 2:2"> For when ye ought to be masters for a time, again ye need that ye be taught, which be the letters &#91;or the elements&#93; of the beginning of God’s words. And ye be made those, to whom is need of milk, and not &#91;of&#93; firm meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:20; 1ST PETER 2:2; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:16"> For each that is partner of milk, is without part of the word of rightwiseness, for he is a little child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14–15; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:21; EPHESIANS 1:18"> But of perfect men is firm meat, of them that for custom have &#91;their&#93; wits exercised to discretion of good and of evil.</span>
