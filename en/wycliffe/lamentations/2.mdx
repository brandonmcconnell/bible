<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:2; PSALMS 132:7; MATTHEW 11:23"> How hath the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with darkness in his strong vengeance? he hath cast down from heaven into earth the noble city of Israel; and bethought not on the stool of his feet, in the day of his strong vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:17; PSALMS 89:39–40; LAMENTATIONS 3:43"> The Lord casted down, and spared not, all the fair things of Jacob; he destroyed in his strong vengeance the strengths or strongholds of the virgin of Judah, and casted down into &#91;the&#93; earth; he defouled the realm, and the princes thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 74:11; PSALMS 75:10; PSALMS 75:5"> He brake in the ire &#91;or the wrath&#93; of his strong vengeance all the horn of Israel; he turned aback his right hand from the face of the enemy; and he kindled in Jacob, as fire of flame devouring in compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:20; JEREMIAH 21:5; LAMENTATIONS 3:12–13"> He as an enemy bent his bow, he as an adversary made steadfast his right hand; and he killed all thing that was fair in sight in the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion; he shedded &#91;or poured&#93; out his indignation as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:2; JEREMIAH 30:14; JEREMIAH 52:13"> The Lord is made as an enemy; he casted down Israel, he casted down all the walls thereof; he des-troyed the strengths thereof, and filled in the daughter of Judah a man made low, and a woman made low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:4; ZEPHANIAH 3:18; LAMENTATIONS 4:16"> And he scattered his tent as a garden, he destroyed his tabernacle; the Lord gave to forgetting in Zion a feast day, and sabbath; and put the king and priest into shame, and into the indignation of his strong venge-ance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:21; AMOS 2:5; PSALMS 74:3–8"> The Lord putted away his altar, he cursed his hallowing; he betook into the hands of the enemy the walls of the towers thereof; they gave voice in the house of the Lord, as in a solemn day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND KI 21:13; ISAIAH 34:11; ISAIAH 3:26"> The Lord thought to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion; he stretched forth his cord, and turned not away his hand from perdition; the forewall, either the outerward, mourned, and the wall was destroyed altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:26; HOSEA 3:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:3"> The gates thereof be pitched in the earth, he lost and all-brake the bars thereof; the king thereof and the princes thereof be among heathen men; the law is not, and the prophets thereof found not of the Lord a vision, either revelation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:26; ISAIAH 15:3; JOB 2:12–13"> They sat in &#91;the&#93; earth, the eld &#91;or old&#93; men of the daughter of Zion were still; they besprinkled their heads with ashes, the elder men of Judah be girt with hair-shirts; the virgins of Judah casted down to the earth their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:20; JOB 16:13; LAMENTATIONS 1:16"> Mine eyes failed for tears, mine entrails were troubled or disturbed; my maw was shed &#91;or poured&#93; out in &#91;the&#93; earth upon the sorrow of the daughter of my people; when a little child and &#91;the&#93; sucking infant failed in the streets of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:12; EZEKIEL 30:24"> They said to their mothers, Where is wheat, and wine? when they failed as wounded men in the streets of the city; yea, when they sent out their souls in the bosom of their mothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:12; JEREMIAH 8:22; 2ND SAMUEL 5:20"> To whom shall I comparison thee? either to whom shall I liken thee, thou daughter of Jerusalem? to whom shall I make thee even, and shall I comfort thee, thou virgin, the daughter of Zion? for why thy sorrow is great as the sea; who shall do medicine to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:28; ISAIAH 58:1; JEREMIAH 5:31"> Thy prophets saw to thee false things, and fond &#91;or foolish&#93;; and they opened not thy wickedness, that they should stir thee to penance; but they saw to thee false takings, and castings out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:16; PSALMS 48:2; PSALMS 50:2"> All men passing on the way clapped with hands on thee; they hissed, and moved their head on the daughter of Jerusalem; and said, This is the city of perfect fairness, the joy of all earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 35:21; LAMENTATIONS 3:46; JOB 16:9–10"> All thine enemies opened their mouth on thee; they hissed, and gnashed with their teeth, and said, We shall devour; lo! this is the day which we abided, we found, we saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 89:42; EZEKIEL 5:11; DEUTERONOMY 31:16–17"> The Lord did those things which he thought, he &#91;ful&#93; filled his word which he had commanded from &#91;the&#93; eld &#91;or old&#93; days; he destroyed, and spared not; and made glad the enemy on thee; and enhanced the horn of thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:145; JEREMIAH 9:1; LAMENTATIONS 2:8"> The heart of them cried to the Lord, on the walls of the daughter of Zion; lead thou forth tears as a strand &#91;or a stream&#93;, by day and night; give thou not rest to thee, neither the apple of thine eye be still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; PSALMS 119:147–148; ISAIAH 26:9"> Rise thou together, praise thou in the night, in the beginning of the wakings &#91;or watches&#93;; shed &#91;or pour&#93; out thine heart as water, before the sight of the Lord; raise thine hands to him for the souls of thy little children, that failed for hunger in the head of all meetings of ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:9; LAMENTATIONS 4:10; PSALMS 78:64"> See thou, Lord, and behold, whom thou hast made so bare; therefore whether women shall eat their fruit, their little children at the measure of an hand? for a priest and prophet is slain in the saintuary of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:17; LAMENTATIONS 3:43; ZECHARIAH 11:6"> A child and an eld &#91;or old&#93; man lie on the earth withoutforth; my virgins and my young men fell down by sword; thou hast slain them in the day of thy strong vengeance, thou smotest and didest no mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:25; PSALMS 31:13; JEREMIAH 16:2–4"> Thou calledest, as to a solemn day, them that made me afeared of compass; and none was that escaped in the day of the strong vengeance of the Lord, and was left; mine enemy wasted them, which I fed, and nourished up.</span>
