</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 6:6; MICAH 7:5; PSALMS 77:2–6"> It<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Throughout Chapter 1, where the “Later Version” uses the impersonal ‘it’ and ‘thereof’, the “Early Version” and most other translations use ‘she’ and ‘her’.">a</a></sup>weeping wept in the night, and the tears thereof be in the cheeks thereof; there is none of all the dear-worthy thereof, that comforteth it; all the friends thereof forsook it, and be made enemies to it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:64–67; LAMENTATIONS 2:9; AMOS 9:1–4"> Judah passed from torment and multitude of servage, it dwelled among heathen men, and found no rest; all the pursuers thereof took it among anguishes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:11; JOEL 1:8–13; JEREMIAH 10:22"> The ways of Zion mourn, for no men come to the solemnity; all the gates thereof be destroyed, the priests thereof wail; the virgins thereof be defouled, and it is oppressed with bitterness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:15–27; JEREMIAH 30:14–15; EZEKIEL 9:9"> The enemies thereof be made in the head, and the enemies thereof be made rich, for the Lord spake on it. For the multitude of wickednesses thereof the little children thereof be led into captivity, before the face of the troubler.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:25; JEREMIAH 13:18; DEUTERONOMY 28:25"> And all the fairness of the daughter of Zion went out from the daughter of Zion; the princes thereof be made as rams not finding pastures; and went forth without strength before the face of the pursuer &#91;or of the follower&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 79:4; DEUTERONOMY 4:34–37; PSALMS 42:4"> And Jerusalem bethought on the days of her affliction and of trespassing, and on all her desirable things which it had from &#91;the&#93; eld &#91;or old&#93; days; when the people thereof fell down in the hand of enemies, and none helper was; &#91;the&#93; enemies saw it, and scorned the sabbaths thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:2–13; LAMENTATIONS 1:20–22; JEREMIAH 13:26"> Jerusalem sinned a sin, therefore it was made unsteadfast; all that glorified it forsook it, for they saw the shame thereof; forsooth it wailed, and was turned aback.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:29; ISAIAH 47:7; PSALMS 25:18"> The filths thereof be in the feet thereof, and it had no mind of her end; it was put down greatly, and had no comforter; Lord, see thou my torment, for the enemy is raised &#91;up&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:3; JEREMIAH 51:51; PSALMS 74:4–8"> The enemy put his hand to all the desirable things thereof; for it saw heathen men entered into thy saintuary, of which thou haddest commanded, that they should not enter into thy church.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:6; JEREMIAH 38:9; LAMENTATIONS 2:12"> All the people thereof was wailing and seeking bread, they gave all precious things for meat, to strengthen the soul; and the city saith, See thou, Lord, and behold, for I am made vile.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 23:28–31; DANIEL 9:12; JEREMIAH 30:24"> A! all ye that pass by the way, perceive, and see, if any sorrow is as my sorrow; for he gathered away my grapes from me, as the Lord spake in the day of wrath of his strong venge-ance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 30:30; HABAKKUK 3:16; EZEKIEL 17:20"> From on high he sent fire in my bones, and taught me; he spreaded abroad a net to my feet, he turned me aback; he setted me desolate, meddled together all day with mourning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:48; ISAIAH 47:6; PROVERBS 5:22"> The yoke of my wickednesses watched in the hand of him, those be folded together, and put on my neck; my strength is made feeble; the Lord gave me in the hand, from which I shall not be able to rise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MALACHI 4:3; ISAIAH 28:18; ISAIAH 63:3"> The Lord took away all my wor-shipful men from the midst of me; he called time against me, that he should all-foul my chosen men; the Lord stamped a presser &#91;or the press trod&#93; to the virgin, the daughter of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:2; JEREMIAH 13:17; PSALMS 119:136"> Therefore I am weeping, and mine eye is leading down water; for a comforter, converting my soul, is made far from me; my sons be made lost, for the enemy had the mastery.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:15; JEREMIAH 4:31; LAMENTATIONS 1:9"> Zion spreaded abroad his hands, none is that comforteth it; the Lord sent against Jacob enemies thereof, in the compass thereof; Jerusalem is made as defouled with unclean blood among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 12:14–15; ZEPHANIAH 3:5; PSALMS 119:75"> And the city saith, The Lord is just &#91;or rightwise&#93;, for I stirred his mouth to wrathfulness &#91;or wrath&#93;; all peoples, I beseech, hear ye, and see my sorrow; my virgins and my young men went forth into captivity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:2; LAMENTATIONS 1:11; LAMENTATIONS 2:20"> I called my friends, and they deceived me; my priests and mine eld &#91;or old&#93; men in the city be wasted; for they sought meat to themselves, to comfort their life &#91;or refresh their soul&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:11; DEUTERONOMY 32:25; ISAIAH 16:11"> See thou, Lord, for I am troubled, my womb is disturbed; mine heart is destroyed in myself, for I am full of bitterness; &#91;the&#93; sword slayeth without-forth, and like death is at home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:8; LAMENTATIONS 2:15; LAMENTATIONS 1:16"> They heard, that I make inward wailing, and none is that comforteth me; all mine enemies heard of mine evil, they be glad, for thou hast done it; thou hast brought a day of comfort, and they shall be made like me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:18; PSALMS 109:14–15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:10"> All the evil of them enter before thee, and gather thou grapes away from them, as thou hast gathered grapes away from me, for my wicked-nesses; for my wailings be many, and mine heart is mourning.</span>
</div>
