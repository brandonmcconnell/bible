<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:10; LUKE 23:28–29; LAMENTATIONS 2:20"> But also lamias or lamiae made naked their teats, gave milk to their whelps; the daughter of my people is cruel, as an ostrich in desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 22:15; LAMENTATIONS 2:11–12; MATTHEW 7:9–11"> The tongue of the sucking child cleaved to his palate in thirst; little children asked for bread, and none was that brake to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="AMOS 6:3–7; LUKE 16:19; LUKE 15:16"> They that ate lustfully, perished in ways; they that were nourished in cradles, embraced turds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 19:25; LUKE 12:47; LUKE 10:12"> And the wickedness of the daughter of my people is made more than the sin of men of Sodom, that was destroyed in a moment, and hands took not therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 51:7; SONG OF SONGS 5:10; PSALMS 144:12"> Nazarites thereof were whiter than snow, shininger than milk; ruddier than eld &#91;or old&#93; ivory, fairer than sapphire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:10; PSALMS 102:3–5; JOB 30:30"> The face of them was made blacker than coals, and they were not known in streets; the skin cleaved to their bones, it dried, and was made as a stick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:23; LEVITICUS 26:39; JEREMIAH 16:4"> It was better to men slain with sword, than to men slain with hunger; for these men waxed rotten, they were wasted of the barrenness of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:20; ISAIAH 49:15; 2ND KI 6:26–29"> The hands of merciful women seethed their children; they were made the meats of those women in the sorrow of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:20; EZEKIEL 22:31; JEREMIAH 17:27"> The Lord &#91;ful&#93; filled his strong vengeance, he shedded out the ire &#91;or he poured out the wrath&#93; of his indignation; and the Lord kindled a fire in Zion, and it devoured the foundaments thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:13; DEUTERONOMY 29:24–28; PSALMS 48:4–6"> The kings of &#91;the&#93; earth, and all the dwellers of the world believed not, that an adversary and &#91;the&#93; enemy should enter in by the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:31; JEREMIAH 6:13; JEREMIAH 26:8–9"> For the sins of the prophets thereof, and for &#91;the&#93; wickednesses of priests thereof, that shedded out the blood of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:34; ISAIAH 56:10; ISAIAH 1:15"> Blind men erred in streets, they were defouled in blood; and when they might not go, they held their hems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:45–46"> They cried to them, Depart away, ye defouled men, depart ye, go ye away, do not ye touch; forsooth they chided, and were stirred; they said among heathen men, God shall no more add to, that he dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:12; ISAIAH 9:14–16; DEUTERONOMY 28:64–65"> The face of the Lord parted them, he shall no more lay to, that he behold them; they were not ashamed of the faces of priests, neither they had mercy on eld &#91;or old&#93; men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:16; ISAIAH 20:5; 2ND KI 24:7"> The while we stood yet, our eyes failed to our vain help; when we beheld attentive to a folk, that might not save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="AMOS 8:2; EZEKIEL 7:2–12; LAMENTATIONS 3:52"> Our steps were slidery in the way of our streets; our end nighed, our days were &#91;ful&#93; filled, for our end came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:8; JEREMIAH 4:13; ISAIAH 5:26–28"> Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of heaven; they pursued us on &#91;the&#93; hills &#91;or mountains&#93;, they setted ambushments to us in desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 19:21; EZEKIEL 12:13; JEREMIAH 39:5"> The spirit of our mouth, Christ the Lord, was taken in our sins; to whom we said, We shall live in thy shadow among heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="AMOS 1:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:15; JOB 1:1"> Thou daughter of Edom, make joy, and be glad, that dwellest in the land of Uz; the cup shall come also to thee, thou shalt be made drunken, and shalt be made bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:2; PSALMS 137:7; JEREMIAH 50:20"> Thou daughter of Zion, thy wickedness is &#91;ful&#93; filled; he shall not add more, that he make thee to pass over again into captivity; thou daughter of Edom, he shall visit thy wicked-ness, he shall uncover thy sins.</span>
