<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 32:17; ROMANS 15:13; JOHN 16:33"> Therefore we, justified of faith, have we peace at God by our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:18; EPHESIANS 3:12; ROMANS 12:12"> By whom we have nigh going to &#91;or access&#93;, by faith into this grace, in which we stand, and have glory in the hope of the glory of God’s children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JAMES 1:2–3; JAMES 1:12; 1ST PETER 3:14"> And not this only, but also we glory in tribulations, witting that tribulation worketh patience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:10; JAMES 1:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:8–12"> and patience proving, and proving hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:6; EPHESIANS 3:16–19; PHILIPPIANS 1:20"> And hope confoundeth not, for the charity of God is spread abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost, that is given to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:1–5; ROMANS 5:8; COLOSSIANS 2:13"> And while that we were sick after the time, what died Christ for wicked men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 15:13; 1ST JOHN 3:16; ROMANS 16:4"> For scarcely dieth any man for the just man; and yet for a good man peradventure some man dare die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 15:13; JOHN 3:16; ROMANS 5:6"> But God commendeth his charity in us; for if when we were yet sinners, after the time Christ was dead for us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 1:10; ROMANS 8:1; EPHESIANS 2:13"> then much more now we justified in his blood, shall be safe from wrath by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:18–19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:10–11; ROMANS 8:32"> For if when we were enemies, we be reconciled to God by the death of his Son, much more we reconciled shall be safe in the life of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:18–19; ROMANS 5:10; ISAIAH 61:10"> And not only this, but also we glory in God, by our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom we have received now reconciling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:21–22; ROMANS 5:19; ROMANS 6:23"> Therefore as by one man sin entered into this world, and by sin death, and so death passed forth into all men, in which man all men sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 4:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:56; 1ST JOHN 3:4"> For unto the law sin was in the world; but sin was not reckoned, when &#91;the&#93; law was not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:45; ROMANS 5:17; HEBREWS 9:27"> But death reigned from Adam unto Moses, also into them that sinned not in likeness of the trespassing of Adam, the which is likeness of Christ to coming &#91;or to come&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 6:23; EPHESIANS 2:8; ROMANS 5:12"> But not as &#91;the&#93; guilt &#91;or the trespass&#93;, so the gift; for if through the guilt &#91;or the trespass&#93; of one many be dead, much more the grace of God and the gift in the grace of one man Jesus Christ hath abounded into many men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:38–39; ISAIAH 1:18; ISAIAH 44:22"> And not as by one sin, so by the gift; for the doom, of one into condemnation, but the grace of many guilts &#91;or trespassings&#93; into justification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:21–22; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:49; ROMANS 5:12"> For if in the guilt of one death reigned through one, much more men that take plenty of grace, and of giving, and of rightwiseness, shall reign in life by one Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:22; ROMANS 5:12; ROMANS 5:15"> Therefore as by the guilt of one into all men into condemnation, so by the rightwiseness of one into all men into justifying of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 5:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21; PHILIPPIANS 2:8"> For as by unobedience of one man many be made sinners, so by the obedience of one many shall be &#91;ordained&#93; just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 6:1; TITUS 3:3–7; ROMANS 6:14"> And the law entered, that guilt should be plenteous; but where guilt was plenteous, grace was more plenteous &#91;or abounded&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="TITUS 2:11; ROMANS 5:14; ROMANS 8:10"> That as sin reigned into death, so grace reign by rightwiseness into ever-lasting life, by Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
