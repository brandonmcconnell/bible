</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30"> As David saith the blessedness of a man, whom God accepteth, he giveth to him rightwiseness without works of the law,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 32:1–2; PSALMS 130:3–4; LUKE 7:47–50"> Blessed be they, whose wicked-nesses be forgiven, and whose sins be hid &#91;or be covered&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 32:2; 1ST PETER 2:24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:19–20"> Blessed is that man, to whom God areckoned not sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:14; COLOSSIANS 3:11; GALATIANS 3:26–28"> Then whether dwelleth this blessedness only in circumcision, or also in prepuce? For we say, that the faith was areckoned to Abraham to rightwiseness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:15; GALATIANS 5:6; GENESIS 17:10"> How then was it areckoned? in circumcision, or in prepuce? Not in circumcision, but in prepuce.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 3:22; LUKE 19:9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:22"> And he took a sign of circumcision, a token &#91;or a marking&#93; of rightwiseness of the faith which is in prepuce, that he be father of all men believing by prepuce, that it be areckoned also to them to rightwise-ness;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 1:8; JOHN 8:39–40; PROVERBS 2:20"> and that he be father of circumcision, not only to them that be of circumcision, but also to them that pursue the steps of the faith, which faith is in prepuce of our father Abraham, &#91;or but and to them that pursue the steps of the faith of our father Abraham, that is in prepuce&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:29; GENESIS 22:17–18; GENESIS 28:14"> For not by the law is &#91;the&#93; promise to Abraham, or to his seed, that he should be &#91;the&#93; heir of the world, but by the rightwiseness of faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:18–24; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; ROMANS 4:16"> For if they that be of the law, be heirs, faith is destroyed, promise is done away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 5:13; GALATIANS 3:10; ROMANS 3:19–20"> For the law worketh wrath; for where is no law, there is no trespass, neither is trespassing &#91;or prevarication&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 3:24–26; EPHESIANS 2:8; GALATIANS 3:22"> Therefore rightwiseness is of faith, that by grace promise be stable &#91;or be steadfast&#93; to each seed, not to that seed only that is of the law, but to that that is of the faith of Abraham, which is father of us all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:28; GENESIS 17:4–5; JOHN 5:21"> As it is written, For I have set thee father of many folks, before God to whom thou hast believed. The which God quickeneth dead men &#91;or The which quickeneth the dead&#93;, and calleth those things that be not, as those that be.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 8:24; GENESIS 15:5–6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:25"> &#91;The&#93; Which Abraham against hope believed into hope, that he should be made father of many folks, as it was said to him, Thus shall thy seed be, as the stars of heaven, and as the gravel &#91;or sand&#93; that is in the brink of the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:11–19; ROMANS 4:20–21; GENESIS 17:17"> And he was not made unsteadfast in the belief, neither he beheld his body then nigh dead, when he was almost of an hundred years, nor the womb of Sarah nigh dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 1:45; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:13; DANIEL 10:19"> Also in the promise of God he doubted not with untrust; but he was comforted in belief, giving glory to God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 1:37; JEREMIAH 32:27; GENESIS 18:14"> witting most fully that whatever things God hath promised, he is mighty also to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 4:6; ROMANS 4:3"> Therefore it was areckoned to him to rightwiseness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:11; ROMANS 15:4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:6"> And it is not written only for him, that it was areckoned to him to rightwiseness,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:24; 1ST PETER 1:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:30"> but also for us, to whom it shall be areckoned, that believe in him that raised our Lord Jesus Christ from death.</span>
