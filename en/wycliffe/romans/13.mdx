<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TITUS 3:1; 1ST PETER 2:13–17; DANIEL 2:21"> Every soul be subject to higher powers. For there is no power but of God, and those things that be of God, be ordained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:13; TITUS 3:1; ROMANS 13:5"> Therefore he that against-standeth power, against-standeth the ordinance of God; and they that against-stand, get to themselves damnation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:4–6; 1ST PETER 2:13–14; PROVERBS 20:2"> For princes be not to the dread of good work, but of evil. But wilt thou, that thou dread not power? Do thou good thing, and thou shalt have praising of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 12:19; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:6; PROVERBS 16:14"> for he is the minister of God to thee into good. But if thou doest evil, dread thou; for not without cause he beareth the sword, for he is the minister of God, avenger into wrath to him that doeth evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 8:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:16; 1ST PETER 2:19"> And therefore by need be ye subject, not only for wrath, but also for conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:17–21; MARK 12:14–17; MATTHEW 17:24–27"> For therefore ye give tributes, they be the ministers of God, and serve for this same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:21; 1ST PETER 2:17–18; LUKE 20:25"> Therefore yield ye to all men debts, to whom tribute, tribute, to whom toll, &#91;or custom for things borne about&#93;, toll, &#91;or such custom&#93;, to whom dread, dread, to whom honour, honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 13:10; MATTHEW 22:39–40; GALATIANS 5:14"> To no man owe ye anything, but that ye love together. For he that loveth his neighbour, hath fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12–17; MATTHEW 22:39; LUKE 10:27"> For, Thou shalt do no lechery, Thou shalt not slay, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not say false witnessing, Thou shalt not covet the thing of thy neighbour, and if there be any other commandment, it is enstored, &#91;or enclosed&#93;, or included, in this word, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:39–40; ROMANS 13:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:4–7"> The love of thy neighbour worketh not evil; therefore love is the fulfilling of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:5–8; 1ST PETER 4:7; EPHESIANS 5:14"> And we know this time, that the hour is now, that we rise from sleep; for now is our health near &#91;or nearer&#93;, than when we believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:31–32; EPHESIANS 5:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:7"> The night went before, but the day hath nighed. Therefore cast we away the works of darknesses, and be we clothed in &#91;or with&#93; the armours of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:21; EPHESIANS 5:18; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:3–5"> As in &#91;the&#93; day wander we honestly, not in superfluous feasts &#91;or in oft eatings&#93;, and drunkennesses, not in beds and unchastities, not in strife and in envy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:24; GALATIANS 5:16–17; GALATIANS 3:27"> but be ye clothed in the Lord Jesus Christ, and do ye not the busyness &#91;or the cares&#93; of flesh in desires.</span>
