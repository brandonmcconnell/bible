<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 6:37; ROMANS 2:3; JOHN 8:7–9"> Therefore thou art unexcusable, each man that deemest, for in what thing thou deemest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou doest the same things which thou deemest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 9:7–8; ZEPHANIAH 3:5; PSALMS 145:17"> And we know, that the doom of God is after truth against them, that do such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:5; LUKE 12:14; ROMANS 1:32–1"> But guessest thou, man, that deemest them that do such things, and thou doest those things, that thou shalt escape the doom of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:9; ISAIAH 30:18; EXODUS 34:6"> Whether thou despisest the riches of his goodness, and the patience, and the long abiding? Knowest thou not, that the benignity, &#91;or good will&#93;, of God leadeth thee to repenting &#91;or to penance&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:14; ROMANS 1:18; ROMANS 2:2–3"> But after thine hardness and unrepentant heart, thou treasurest to thee wrath in&#91;to&#93; the day of wrath, and of showing of the rightful doom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 62:12; JEREMIAH 32:19; MATTHEW 16:27"> that shall yield to each man after his works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:7–8; LUKE 8:15; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:10"> soothly to them that be by patience of good work, glory, and honour, and uncorruption, to them that seek everlasting life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 3:18–21; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:8; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:3–4"> but to them that be of strife, and that assent not to truth, but believe to wickedness, wrath and indignation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:17; EZEKIEL 18:4; ROMANS 1:16"> tribulation and anguish, into each soul of man that worketh evil, to the Jew first, and to the Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 32:17; ISAIAH 26:12; NUMBERS 6:26"> but glory, and honour, and peace, to each man that worketh good thing, to the Jew first, and to the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:34; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:7"> For acception of persons, &#91;that is, to put one before another without desert&#93;, is not with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:21; GALATIANS 3:10; GALATIANS 2:16–19"> For whoever have sinned without the law, shall perish without the law; and whoever have sinned in the law, they shall be deemed by the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 1:22–25; 1ST JOHN 3:7; JAMES 2:21–25"> For the hearers of the law be not just with God, but the doers of the law shall be made just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 2:12; ROMANS 1:19–20; ROMANS 2:27"> For when heathen men that have not law, do kindly those things that be of the law, they not having such manner &#91;of&#93; law, be law to themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:12; ROMANS 1:18–19"> that show the work of the law written in their hearts. For the conscience of them yieldeth to them a witnessing betwixt themselves of thoughts that be accusing or defending,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31"> in the day when God shall deem the privy things of men after my gospel, by Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 2:23; MICAH 3:11; ISAIAH 48:1–2"> But if thou art named a Jew, and restest in the law, and hast glory in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:21; DEUTERONOMY 4:8; JAMES 4:17"> and hast known his will, and thou learned by the law approvest &#91;or hast proved&#93; the more profitable things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17–18; MATTHEW 15:14; ISAIAH 49:6"> and trustest thyself to be a leader of blind men, the light of them that be in darknesses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:5; TITUS 1:16; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:13"> a teacher of unwise men, a master of young children, that hast the form of knowing &#91;or of science&#93;, and of truth in the law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:27; MATTHEW 23:3–28; LUKE 12:47"> what then teachest thou another, and teachest not thyself? Thou that preachest that me/that men shall not steal, stealest? &#91;or Thou that preachest to not steal, stealest?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MALACHI 3:8; MATTHEW 12:39; MARK 11:17"> Thou that teachest that me/that men shall not do lechery, doest lechery? &#91;or Thou that sayest to not do lechery, doest lechery?&#93; Thou that loathest maumets &#91;or idols&#93;, doest sacrilege?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JAMES 4:16–17; ROMANS 2:17; ROMANS 3:2"> Thou that hast glory in the law, unworshippest God by breaking of the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:20–23; ISAIAH 52:5; 2ND PETER 2:2"> For the name of God is blasphemed by you among heathen men, as &#91;it&#93; is written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:4; GALATIANS 5:3–6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51"> For circumcision profiteth, if thou keep the law; but if thou be a trespasser against the law, thy circumcision is made prepuce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:18–19; ROMANS 8:4"> Therefore if prepuce keep the rightwiseness of the law, whether his prepuce shall not be areckoned into circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:41–42; ROMANS 2:29; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:6"> And the prepuce of kind that fulfilleth the law, shall deem thee, that by letter and circumcision art a trespasser against the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:15; MATTHEW 3:9; ROMANS 9:6–8"> For he that is in open is not a Jew, neither it is &#91;the&#93; circumcision that is openly in the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:6; PHILIPPIANS 3:3; DEUTERONOMY 10:16"> but he that is a Jew in hid, and the circumcision of heart, in spirit, not by &#91;or in&#93; the letter, whose praising is not of men, but of God.</span>
