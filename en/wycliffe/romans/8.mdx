<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 3:18–19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17; GALATIANS 3:13"> Therefore now nothing of condemnation is to them that be in Christ Jesus, which wander not after the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 6:14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:17; ROMANS 6:18"> For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath delivered me from the law of sin, and of death, &#91;or the law of sin, and death&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:39; GALATIANS 4:4–5; HEBREWS 7:18–19"> For that that was impossible to the law, in what thing it was sick by flesh, God sent his Son into the likeness of flesh of sin, and of sin condemned sin in flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:16; GALATIANS 5:22–25; COLOSSIANS 1:22"> that the justifying of the law were fulfilled in us, that &#91;we&#93; go not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; GALATIANS 5:19–25; JOHN 3:6"> For they that be after the flesh, understand &#91;or savour&#93; those things that be of the flesh; but they that be after the Spirit, feel those things that be of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:8; ROMANS 8:7; ROMANS 8:13"> For the prudence of flesh is death; but the prudence of Spirit is life and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; 1ST JOHN 2:15–16; JAMES 4:4"> For the wisdom of the flesh is enemy to God; for it is not subject to the law of God, for neither it may &#91;be subject to the law&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 3:5–6; HEBREWS 11:5–6; ROMANS 8:9"> And they that be in flesh, may not please to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:14"> But ye be not in flesh, but in Spirit; if nevertheless the Spirit of God dwelleth in you. But if any man hath not the Spirit of Christ, this is not his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EPHESIANS 3:17; GALATIANS 2:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21"> For if Christ is in you, the body is dead for sin &#91;or from sin&#93;, but the Spirit liveth for justifying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:14; ROMANS 6:4–5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:11"> And if the Spirit of him that raised Jesus Christ from death dwelleth in you, he that raised Jesus Christ from death, shall quicken also your deadly bodies, for the Spirit of him that dwelleth in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 6:2–15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:19–20; 1ST PETER 4:2–3"> Therefore, brethren, we be debtors, not to the flesh, that we live after the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:8; 1ST PETER 2:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:27"> For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die; but if ye by the Spirit slay the deeds of the flesh, ye shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:18; JOHN 1:12; GALATIANS 3:26"> For whoever be led by the Spirit of God, these be the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:5–7; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:12"> For ye have not taken again the spirit of servage in dread, but ye have taken the Spirit of adoption of sons, that is, sons of God by grace, in which we cry, Abba, Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:5"> And that Spirit yieldeth witnessing to our spirit, that we be the sons of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:7; GALATIANS 3:29; EPHESIANS 3:6"> if sons, and heirs, heirs forsooth of God, and heirs together with Christ; if nevertheless we suffer together, that also we be glorified together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:17–18; 1ST PETER 4:13; 1ST PETER 1:5–7"> And I deem, that the passions of this time be not even worthy, to the glory to coming &#91;or to come&#93;, that shall be showed in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MALACHI 3:17–18; ROMANS 8:23; 1ST JOHN 3:2"> For the abiding of creature abideth the showing of the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 3:17–19; GENESIS 5:29; ROMANS 8:22"> But the creature is subject to vanity, not willingly, but for him that made it subject in hope;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:3–5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:21; 2ND PETER 3:13"> for that creature shall be delivered from servage of corruption into the liberty of the glory of the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 12:4; JEREMIAH 12:11; JOHN 16:21"> And we know, that each creature sorroweth, and travaileth with pain till yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:20–21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:2–5; HEBREWS 9:28"> And not only it, but also we us-selves, that have the first fruits of the Spirit, and we us-selves sorrow within us for the adoption of God’s sons, abiding the again-buying of our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:7; HEBREWS 11:1; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:18"> But by hope we be made safe. For hope that is seen, is not hope; for who hopeth that thing, that he seeth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 12:12; HEBREWS 10:36; PSALMS 27:14"> And if we hope that thing that we see not, we abide by patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:20; EPHESIANS 6:18; GALATIANS 4:6"> And also the Spirit helpeth our infirmity; for what we shall pray, as it behooveth, we know not, but that Spirit asketh for us with sorrowings, that may not be told out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:10; 1ST JOHN 5:14–15; EPHESIANS 2:18"> For he that seeketh the hearts, knoweth what the Spirit desireth, for by God, &#91;that is, after God’s will&#93;, he asketh for holy men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:10; JAMES 1:12; GENESIS 50:20"> And we know, that to men that love God, all things work together into good, to them that after purpose be called saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:4–5; JEREMIAH 1:5; EPHESIANS 1:11"> For those that he knew before, he before-ordained by grace to be made like to the image of his Son, that he be the first begotten among many brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:14; ROMANS 8:28; EPHESIANS 1:11"> And those that he before-ordained to bless, them he called; and whom he called, them he justified; and whom he justified, them he glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 118:6; JEREMIAH 1:19; PSALMS 56:11"> What then shall we say to these things? If God be for us, who is against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 3:16; ROMANS 8:28; PSALMS 84:11"> Which also spared not his own Son, but betook him for us all, how also gave he not to us all things with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:10–11; ISAIAH 50:8–9; ISAIAH 54:17"> Who shall accuse against the chosen men of God? It is God that justifieth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:25; ROMANS 8:27; HEBREWS 9:24"> who is it that condemneth? It is Jesus Christ that was dead, yea, the which rose again, the which is on the right half of God, and the which prayeth for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 10:28; PSALMS 103:17; JOHN 16:33"> Who then shall part us from the charity of Christ? tribulation, or anguish, or hunger, or nakedness, or persecution, or peril, or sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 44:22; ISAIAH 53:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:30"> As it is written, For we be slain all day for thee; we be guessed as sheep of slaughter &#91;or to slaughter&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:57; 1ST JOHN 4:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:14"> But in all these things we overcome, for him that loved us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 10:28; ROMANS 14:8; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:12"> But I am certain, that neither death, neither life, neither angels, neither principalities, neither virtues, neither present things, neither things to coming &#91;or to come&#93;, neither strength,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 10:28–30; ROMANS 8:35; ROMANS 5:8"> neither height, neither deepness, neither any other creature, may part us from the charity of God, that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
