<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:7; ROMANS 1:9; GALATIANS 1:20"> I say truth in Christ Jesus, I lie not, for my conscience beareth witnessing to me in the Holy Ghost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:18; PSALMS 119:136; JEREMIAH 13:17"> for great heaviness is to me, and continual sorrow to my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 32:32; ESTHER 8:6; ROMANS 11:14"> For I myself desired to be parted &#91;or cursed&#93; from Christ for my brethren, that be my cousins after the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:12; PSALMS 147:19; ROMANS 9:6"> that be men of Israel &#91;or Israelites&#93;; whose is adoption of sons, and glory, and testament, and giving of the law, and service, and promises;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 10:30; JOHN 1:1–3; COLOSSIANS 1:16–19"> whose be the fathers, and of which is Christ after the flesh, that is God above all things, blessed into worlds. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ROMANS 2:28–29; GALATIANS 6:16; ROMANS 11:1–2"> But not that the word of God hath fallen down, &#91;or failed unfulfilled&#93;. For not all that be of Israel, these be Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 21:12; HEBREWS 11:18; JOHN 8:37–39"> Neither they that be &#91;the&#93; seed of Abraham, all be sons; but in Isaac the seed shall be called to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 1:13; ROMANS 4:11–16; GALATIANS 3:26–29"> that is to say, not they that be sons of the flesh, be sons of God, but they that be sons of &#91;the&#93; promise be deemed in the seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 18:10; GENESIS 18:14; GENESIS 17:21"> For why this is the word of promise, After this time I shall come, and a son shall be to Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 25:21–23; LUKE 16:26; ROMANS 5:11"> And not only she, but also Rebecca had two sons of one lying-by, &#91;or of one knowing of man&#93;, of Isaac, our father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:9; EPHESIANS 1:4–5; 2ND PETER 1:10"> And when they were not yet born, neither had done anything of good either evil, that the purpose of God should dwell by election, not of works, but of God calling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 25:22–23; 2ND SAMUEL 8:14; 1ST KINGS 22:47"> it was said to him &#91;or to her&#93;, That the more should serve the less,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MALACHI 1:2–3; DEUTERONOMY 21:15; GENESIS 29:33"> as it is written, I loved Jacob, but I hated Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 19:7; PSALMS 145:17; DEUTERONOMY 32:4"> What therefore shall we say? Whether wickedness be with God? God forbid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 33:19; ROMANS 9:16; MICAH 7:18"> For he saith to Moses, I shall have mercy on whom I shall have mercy; and I shall give mercy on whom I shall have mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:8; MATTHEW 11:25–26; 1ST PETER 2:9–10"> Therefore it is not neither of &#91;a&#93; man willing, neither running, but of God having mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 9:16; PROVERBS 16:4; EXODUS 14:17–18"> And the scripture saith to Pharaoh, For to this thing I have stirred thee, that I show in thee my virtue, and that my name be told in all &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:20; EXODUS 4:21; ROMANS 9:15–16"> Therefore of whom God will, he hath mercy; and whom he will, he endureth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 4:35; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:12"> Then sayest thou to me, What is sought yet? &#91;or What thing is yet sought?&#93; for who withstandeth his will?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:16; ISAIAH 45:9–11; ISAIAH 64:8"> O! man, who art thou, that answerest to God? Whether a made thing saith to him that made it, What hast thou made me so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 64:8; PROVERBS 16:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:20–21"> Whether a potter of clay hath not power to make of the same gobbet one vessel into honour, another into despite, &#91;or low office&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:4; JUDE 1:4; ROMANS 9:21"> And if God willing to show his wrath, and to make his power known, hath suffered in great patience vessels of wrath able into death, &#91;or into perdition, or damnation&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ROMANS 2:4; 1ST PETER 1:2–5; COLOSSIANS 1:27"> to show the riches of his glory into vessels of mercy, which he made ready into glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:47–48; EPHESIANS 3:6–8"> Which also he called &#91;us&#93;, not only of the Jews, but also of heathen men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HOSEA 2:23; 1ST PETER 2:10; JOHN 16:27"> as he saith in Hosea, I shall call not my people my people, and not my loved my loved, and not getting mercy getting mercy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSEA 1:9–10; 1ST JOHN 3:1–3; GALATIANS 3:26"> and it shall be in the place, where it is said to them, Not ye my people, there they shall be called the sons of living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:20–23; ROMANS 11:4–6; ISAIAH 1:9"> But Isaiah crieth for Israel, If the number of children of Israel shall be as &#91;the&#93; gravel of the sea, the remnants shall be made safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:23; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31"> Forsooth a word making an end, and abridging in equity, for the Lord shall make a word abridged, &#91;or made short&#93;, on all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:9; ISAIAH 13:19; AMOS 4:11"> And as Isaiah before-said, But God of hosts had left to us seed, we had been made as Sodom, and we had been like as Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:9; HEBREWS 11:7; GALATIANS 2:16"> Therefore what shall we say? That heathen men that pursued not right-wiseness, have gotten &#91;or have caught&#93; rightwiseness, yea, the right-wiseness that is of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ROMANS 11:7; ROMANS 3:20; JAMES 2:10–11"> But Israel pursuing the law of rightwiseness, came not perfectly to the law of rightwiseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:23; 1ST PETER 2:8; LUKE 7:23"> Why? For not of faith, but as of works. And they spurned against the stone of offence, &#91;or spurning&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:16; ISAIAH 8:14–15; ROMANS 10:11"> as it is written, Lo! I put a stone of offence in Zion, and a stone of stumbling; and each that shall believe in it, shall not be confounded, &#91;or shamed&#93;.</span>
