<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 2:25–29; GENESIS 25:32; ECCLESIASTES 6:11"> What then is more to a Jew, or what profit of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 9:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:38; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:17"> Much by all wise &#91;or by all manner&#93;; first, for the speakings of God were betaken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:13; HEBREWS 4:2; ROMANS 9:6"> And what if some of them believed not? Whether the unbelief of them hath voided the faith of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; PSALMS 116:11; GALATIANS 2:17"> God forbid. For God is soothfast, &#91;or true&#93;, but each man a liar; as it is written, That thou be justified in thy words, and overcome, when thou art deemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 6:19; GALATIANS 3:15; ROMANS 9:18–20"> But if our wickedness commend the rightwiseness of God, what shall we say? Whether God is wicked, that bringeth in wrath? After man I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 18:25; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31; JOB 8:3"> God forbid. Else how shall God deem this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 3:4; ROMANS 9:19–20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:27–29"> For if the truth of God hath abounded in my leasing, into the glory of him, what yet am I deemed as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 6:1; ROMANS 6:15; ROMANS 5:20"> And not as we be blasphemed, and as some say that we say, Do we evil things, that good things come. Whose damnation is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:22; ROMANS 6:15; ISAIAH 65:5"> What then? Surpass we them? Nay; for we have showed by skill, that all both Jews and Greeks be under sin &#91;or the Jews and Greeks all to be under sin&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 53:1–3; PSALMS 14:1–3; ROMANS 3:23"> as it is written, For there is no man just;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 14:2–4; PSALMS 53:2; TITUS 3:3"> there is no man understanding, neither seeking God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 14:3; ECCLESIASTES 7:20; ISAIAH 64:6"> All bowed away, together they be made unprofitable; there is none that doeth good thing, there is none till to one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 5:9; PSALMS 140:3; MATTHEW 23:27–28"> The throat of them is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they did guilefully, &#91;or treacherously&#93;; the venom of snakes is under their lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 109:17–18; PSALMS 59:12"> The mouth of whom is full of cursing and bitterness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:7–8; PROVERBS 1:16; PROVERBS 6:18"> the feet of them be swift to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Sorrow and cursedness be in the ways of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:8; ROMANS 5:1; LUKE 1:79"> and they knew not the way of peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 36:1; PROVERBS 23:17; PROVERBS 16:6"> the dread of God is not before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:10; GALATIANS 3:22–23; ROMANS 2:12–18"> And we know, that whatever things the law speaketh, it speaketh to them that be in the law, that each mouth be stopped, and each world be made subject to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:39; ROMANS 3:28"> For of the works of the law each flesh shall not be justified before him; for by the law there is knowing of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:43; JEREMIAH 33:16; PHILIPPIANS 3:9"> But now without the law the rightwiseness of God is showed, that is witnessed of the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 10:12; GALATIANS 3:28; ROMANS 8:1"> And the rightwiseness of God is by the faith of Jesus Christ into all men and on all men that believe in him; for there is no parting &#91;or distinction&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:20; 1ST JOHN 1:8–10; ROMANS 3:9"> For all men sinned, and have need to the glory of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:18–19; TITUS 3:5–7; COLOSSIANS 1:14"> and be justified freely by his grace, by the again-buying &#91;or the redemption&#93; that is in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 3:23–24; 1ST PETER 1:18–20; 1ST JOHN 4:10"> Whom God ordained forgiver &#91;or purposed an helper&#93;, by faith in his blood, to the showing of his right-wiseness, for &#91;the&#93; remission of before-going sins, in the bearing up of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; ROMANS 4:5; ROMANS 3:30"> to the showing of his rightwise-ness in this time, that he be just, and justifying him that is of the faith of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ROMANS 4:2; ROMANS 2:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:29–31"> Where then is thy glorying? It is excluded. By what law? Of deeds doing? Nay, but by the law of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:16; GALATIANS 3:8; TITUS 3:7"> For we deem a man to be justified by faith, without works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:11; EPHESIANS 3:6; ZECHARIAH 2:11"> Whether of the Jews is God only? Whether he is not also of heathen men? Yes, and of heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:8; GALATIANS 3:20; PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> For there is one God, that justifieth circumcision by faith, and prepuce by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:21; MATTHEW 5:17; ROMANS 13:8–10"> Destroy we therefore the law by faith? God forbid; but we stablish the law.</span>
