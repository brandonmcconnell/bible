<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 6:14; ROMANS 7:6; ROMANS 6:3"> Brethren, whether ye know not; for I speak to men that know the law; for the law hath lordship in a man, as long time as he liveth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 2:23–24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:39; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:4"> For that woman that is under an husband, is bound to the law, while the husband liveth; but if her husband is dead, she is delivered &#91;or is unbound&#93; from the law of the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 20:14; MATTHEW 5:32; MARK 10:6–12"> Therefore she shall be called adulteress, if she be with another man, while the husband liveth; but if her husband is dead, she is delivered from the law of the husband, that she be not adulteress, if she be with another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:24; GALATIANS 5:22–23; ISAIAH 54:5"> And so, my brethren, ye be made dead to the law by &#91;or through&#93; the body of Christ, that ye be of another, that rose again from death, that ye bear fruit to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:3; GALATIANS 5:16–17; GALATIANS 5:24"> For when we were in flesh, passions of sins, that were by the law, wrought in our members, to bear fruit to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:23–25; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:6; ROMANS 7:4"> But now we be unbound from the law of death, in which we were held, so that we serve in newness of spirit, and not in oldness of letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 3:20; ROMANS 4:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:56"> What therefore shall we say? The law is sin? God forbid. But I knew not sin, but by &#91;the&#93; law; for I knew not that coveting was sin, but for the law said, Thou shalt not covet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 7:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:56; ROMANS 4:15"> And through occasion taken, sin by the commandment hath wrought in me all covetousness; for without the law, sin was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:10; ROMANS 8:7; JAMES 2:10–11"> And I lived without the law sometime; but when the commandment was come &#91;or had come&#93;, sin lived again. But I was dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:7; ROMANS 10:5; LEVITICUS 18:5"> and this commandment that was to life, was found to me, to be to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 3:13; ROMANS 7:8; HEBREWS 3:13"> For sin, through occasion taken by the commandment, deceived me, and by that it slew me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:8; PSALMS 119:137; PSALMS 19:7–12"> Therefore the law is holy, and the commandment is holy, and just, and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 1:13–15; GALATIANS 3:21; ROMANS 8:3"> Is then that thing that is good, made death to me? God forbid. But sin, that it seem sin, through good thing wrought death to me, that me or that men sin over-manner through the commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST KINGS 21:25; 2ND KI 17:17; ROMANS 7:18"> And we know, that the law is spiritual; but I am fleshly, sold under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:17; ROMANS 7:19–20; ECCLESIASTES 7:20"> For I understand not that that I work; for I do not the good thing that I will, but I do that evil thing that I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 7:12; ROMANS 7:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:8"> And if I do that thing that I will not, I consent to the law, that it is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 7:20; PHILIPPIANS 3:8–9; ROMANS 7:18"> But now I work not it now, but the sin that dwelleth in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 3:6; PSALMS 51:5; GALATIANS 5:17"> But I know, that in me, that is, in my flesh, dwelleth no good; for will lieth to me, but I find not to perform good thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 7:15"> For I do not that good thing that I will, but I do that evil thing that I will not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 7:17"> And if I do that evil thing that I will not, I work not it, but the sin that dwelleth in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 6:14; ROMANS 7:23; PSALMS 119:133"> Therefore I find the law &#91;or a law&#93; to me willing to do good thing, for evil thing lieth to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 1:2; PSALMS 40:8; PSALMS 119:35"> For I delight together to the law of God, after the inner man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:17; JAMES 4:1; ROMANS 8:2"> But I see another law in my members, fighting against the law of my soul, and making me captive in the law of sin, that is in my members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 6:6; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:18; TITUS 2:14"> I am an unhappy &#91;or a woeful&#93; man; who shall deliver me from the body of this sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:17–24; COLOSSIANS 3:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> The grace of God, by Jesus Christ our Lord. Therefore I myself by the soul serve to the law of God; but by the flesh to the law of sin.</span>
