<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND KI 15:30"> In the twelfth year of Ahaz, king of Judah, Hoshea, the son of Elah, reigned in Samaria upon Israel nine years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND KI 3:2; 2ND KI 13:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:5–11"> And he did evil before the Lord, but not as the kings of Israel, that were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 10:14; 2ND KI 15:19; 2ND KI 19:36–37"> Shalmaneser, king of Assyrians, went up against Hoshea, and Hoshea was made servant to him, and yielded tributes to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND KI 18:21; 2ND KI 24:20; EZEKIEL 17:13–19"> And when the king of Assyrians had perceived, that Hoshea enforced or endeavoured to rebel, and had sent messengers to So, king of Egypt, that he should not give tributes to the king of Assyrians, as he was wont by all years, the king of Assyrians besieged him, and sent him bound into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HOSEA 13:16; 2ND KI 25:1–3; 2ND KI 18:9"> And Shalmaneser went through-&#91;out&#93; all the land, and he went up to Samaria, and besieged it three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 5:26; HOSEA 13:16; DEUTERONOMY 28:64"> Forsooth in the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyrians took Samaria, and translated or brought over Israel into Assyrians; and he put them in Halah, and in Habor, beside the flood &#91;of&#93; Gozan, in the cities of Medes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:16; PSALMS 106:35–41; NEHEMIAH 9:26"> Forsooth it was done, when the sons of Israel had sinned before their Lord God, that led them out of the land of Egypt, from under the hand of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, they worship-ped alien gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:3; 2ND KI 16:3; DEUTERONOMY 18:9"> and went by the custom of heathen men, which the Lord had wasted in the sight of the sons of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, for they had done in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND KI 18:8; JOB 31:27; DEUTERONOMY 13:6"> And the sons of Israel offended their Lord God by words not rightful &#91;or right&#93;, and they builded to them-selves high things in all their cities, from the tower of keepers unto a strengthened city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST KINGS 14:23; MICAH 5:14; EXODUS 34:13"> And they made to them images, and maumet woods, in each high hill, and under each tree full of boughs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 28:25; 2ND KI 21:6; JEREMIAH 44:17"> and they burnt there incense on the altars, by the custom of heathen men, which the Lord had translated or brought over from the face of them. And they did &#91;the&#93; worst words, that is, worst works, and they wrathed the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:4; EXODUS 20:3–5; DEUTERONOMY 4:23–25"> and &#91;they&#93; worshipped &#91;the&#93; unclean-nesses, of which the Lord commanded to them, that they should not do this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 35:15; 1ST SAMUEL 9:9; JEREMIAH 18:11"> And the Lord witnessed in Israel and in Judah, by the hand of all prophets, and &#91;the&#93; seers, and said, Turn ye again from your worst or your full evil ways, and keep my commandments, and &#91;my&#93; ceremonies, by all the law which I commanded to your fathers, and as I sent to you in the hand of my servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; PSALMS 106:24"> Which heard not, but made hard their noll by the noll of their fathers, that would not obey to their Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:30–31; DEUTERONOMY 32:21; ROMANS 1:21–23"> And they casted away the lawful things of him, and the covenant that he covenanted with their fathers, and the witnessings by which he witnessed to them; and they pursued &#91;or followed&#93; vanities, that is, idols, and did vainly; and pursued &#91;or followed&#93; heathen men, that were about them; of which things the Lord commanded to them, that they should not do as also those heathen men did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 14:23; 1ST KINGS 16:31; 1ST KINGS 14:15"> And they forsook all the command-ments of their Lord God, and they made to them two molten calves, and maumet woods, and worshipped all the knighthood of heaven, that is, sun, and moon, and other planets; and they served Baal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 21:6; 2ND KI 16:3; DEUTERONOMY 18:10–12"> and &#91;they&#93; hallowed to him their sons, and their daughters, through fire, and they served to false divining, and to divining by chittering of birds; and they gave themselves to do evil before the Lord, and they wrathed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:20; 1ST KINGS 11:13; HOSEA 11:12"> And the Lord was wroth greatly to Israel; and he took away them from his sight, and none was left, no but the lineage of Judah only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND KI 16:3; 1ST KINGS 14:22–23; EZEKIEL 16:51–52"> But neither Judah himself kept the behests of the Lord his God, but nevertheless he erred, and went in the error of Israel, which it wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND KI 15:29; JEREMIAH 6:30; 2ND KI 13:3"> And the Lord casted away all the seed of Israel, and tormented them, and betook them in the hand of raveners; till he had cast away them from his face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:11; 1ST KINGS 11:31; 1ST KINGS 14:16"> from that time in which Israel was parted from the house of David, and &#91;they&#93; made to them a king, Jeroboam, the son of Nebat. For Jeroboam separated Israel from the Lord, and made them to do great sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND KI 3:3; 2ND KI 13:6; 2ND KI 13:11"> And the sons of Israel went in all the sins of Jeroboam, which he had done; and they departed not from those sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND KI 17:6; 2ND KI 17:13; 2ND KI 17:20"> till the Lord did away Israel from his face, as he spake in the hand of all his servants the prophets; and Israel was translated or brought over from his land into Assyrians till into this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND KI 18:34; 2ND KI 17:30–31; MATTHEW 10:5"> Forsooth the king of Assyrians brought people from Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from Ava, and from Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and set them in the cities of Samaria for the sons of Israel; and these had in possession Samaria, and they dwelled in the cities thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND KI 17:32; 2ND KI 17:34; 2ND KI 17:41"> And when they began to dwell there, they dreaded not the Lord; and the Lord sent to them lions, the which killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND KI 17:27; 1ST SAMUEL 8:9; AMOS 8:14"> And it was told to the king of Assyrians, and was said, The folks which thou hast translated or brought over, and madest to dwell in the cities of Samaria, know not the lawful things of &#91;the&#93; God of the land; and the Lord hath sent lions into them, and lo! those &#91;or they&#93; slay them; for they know not the custom of &#91;the&#93; God of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 17:13; 1ST KINGS 12:31; 1ST KINGS 13:2"> Soothly the king of Assyrians commanded, and said, Lead ye thither one of the priests, which ye brought as prisoners from thence, that he go, and dwell with them, and teach them the lawful things of God of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:29–32; ISAIAH 29:13; MATTHEW 15:14"> Therefore when one of these priests had come, that were led away as prisoners from Samaria, he dwelled in Bethel, and taught them, how they should worship the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:31; MICAH 4:5; 1ST KINGS 13:32"> And each folk made his god, and they setted &#91;or put&#93; those gods in the high temples, which the men of Samaria had made, folk and folk in their cities, in which they dwelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND KI 17:24"> For men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth; and men of Cuth made Nergal; and men of Hamath made Ashima;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND KI 17:24; 2ND KI 17:17; 2ND KI 19:37"> and Avites made Nibhaz and Tartak; soothly they that were of Sepharvaim burnt their sons in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:31; 2ND KI 17:29; 1ST KINGS 13:33"> And nevertheless they worshipped the Lord; forsooth of the last men, that is, of vile persons, that were not of priests’ kin, by the law of Moses, they made priests of the high things, and setted &#91;or put&#93; them in high temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND KI 17:41; LUKE 16:13; MATTHEW 6:24"> And when they worshipped God, they served also their gods, by the custom of heathen men, from which they were translated or brought over to Samaria;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 32:28; GENESIS 35:10; 2ND KI 17:27–28"> till to this present day they pursue &#91;or follow&#93; the eld &#91;or old&#93; custom; they dreaded not the Lord, neither they keep his ceremonies, and dooms, and law, and commandment, which the Lord commanded to the sons of Jacob, whom he named Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JUDGES 6:10; JOSHUA 23:16; JOSHUA 23:7"> and the Lord &#91;had&#93; smote a covenant with them, and &#91;had&#93; commanded to them, and said, Do not ye dread alien gods, and honour ye not outwardly them, neither worship ye inwardly them, and make ye not sacrifice to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 6:6; DEUTERONOMY 6:13; LEVITICUS 19:32"> but your Lord God, that led you out of the land of Egypt in great strength, and in an arm stretched out, dread ye him, and worship ye him, and make ye sacrifice to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:37; DEUTERONOMY 5:31–2; DEUTERONOMY 4:44–45"> Also keep ye the ceremonies, and &#91;the&#93; dooms, and the law, and the commandment, which he wrote to you, that ye do it in all days; and dread ye not alien gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:23; DEUTERONOMY 6:12; DEUTERONOMY 8:14–18"> And do not ye forget the covenant, which he/the Lord smote with you, neither worship ye alien gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 1:74–75; 1ST SAMUEL 12:24; MATTHEW 10:28"> but dread ye your Lord God, and he shall deliver you from the hand of all your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:28; 2ND KI 17:8; JEREMIAH 13:23"> Forsooth they heard not, but did by their former custom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:24; ZEPHANIAH 1:5; 1ST KINGS 18:21"> Therefore soothly these heathen men dreaded God; but nevertheless they served also their idols, for both their sons and the sons of their sons do so, till into this present day, as their fathers did.</span>
