<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST KINGS 21:27; 2ND KI 18:37; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:20–22"> And when king Hezekiah had heard these things, he rent his clothes, and was covered with a sackcloth; and he entered into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:1; ISAIAH 2:1; MATTHEW 4:14"> And he sent Eliakim, &#91;the&#93; sovereign of the house, and Shebna, the scribe, and &#91;the&#93; eld &#91;or old&#93; men of the priests, covered with sackcloths, to Isaiah, the prophet, the son of Amoz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:5–7; ISAIAH 66:9; HOSEA 13:13"> The which said to him, Hezekiah saith these things, This day is a day of tribulation, and of blaming, and of blasphemy; sons came unto the birth, and the mother travailing hath not strength thereto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:12; ROMANS 9:27; JOSHUA 14:12"> If peradventure thy Lord God hear all the words of Rabshakeh, whom the king of Assyrians, his lord hath sent, that he should despise the Lord living, and reprove by words, which thy Lord God heard; and make thou prayer for these remnants of the people, that be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Therefore the servants of king Hezekiah came to Isaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND KI 18:35; 2ND KI 18:17; ISAIAH 37:6–38"> and Isaiah said to them, Say ye these things to your lord, The Lord saith these things, Do not thou dread of the face, or showing, of the words, that thou heardest, by which the children of the king of Assyrians blasphemed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:1; 2ND KI 7:6; PSALMS 18:14–15"> Lo! I shall send to him a spirit, and he shall hear a messenger, and he shall turn again into his land; and I shall cast him down by sword in his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND KI 18:14; JOSHUA 10:29; JOSHUA 12:11"> Therefore Rabshakeh turned again, and found the king of Assyrians fighting against Libnah; for he had heard, that the king had gone away from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 23:27; ISAIAH 37:9; 2ND KI 18:17"> And when he had heard of Tirhakah, king of Ethiopia, men saying, Lo! he went out, that he fight against thee; that he should go against that king, he sent messengers to Hezekiah, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND KI 18:5; 2ND KI 18:29–30; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:15–19"> Say ye these things to Hezekiah, king of Judah, Thy Lord God, in whom thou hast trust, deceive not thee, neither say thou, Jerusalem shall not be betaken into the hands of the king of Assyrians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:8–11; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:13–14; 2ND KI 19:17–18"> for thou thyself hast heard what things the kings of Assyrians have done in all lands, how they have wasted them; whether therefore thou alone mayest be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND KI 17:6; GENESIS 11:31; EZEKIEL 27:23"> Whether the gods of heathen men delivered all men which my fathers destroyed, that is, Gozan, and Haran, and Rezeph, and the sons of Eden, that were in Thelasar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND KI 18:34; 2ND KI 17:24; ISAIAH 11:11"> Where is the king of Hamath, and the king of Arpad? and the king of the city of Sepharvaim, of Hena, and of Ivah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:14; PSALMS 91:1–2; PSALMS 123:1–4"> Therefore when Hezekiah had taken the letters from the hand of the messengers, and had read them, he went up into the house of the Lord, and spreaded abroad those letters before the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:6; 2ND KI 5:15; DANIEL 4:34–35"> and prayed in his sight, and said, Lord God of Israel, that sittest upon cherubim, thou art God alone of all kings of &#91;the&#93; earth; thou madest heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 6:40; PSALMS 31:2; 2ND KI 19:4"> Bow &#91;down&#93; thine ear, and hear; open thine eyes, Lord, and see; and hear all the words of Sennacherib, the which hath sent to us, that he would despise the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 5:26; JOB 9:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:25"> Truly, Lord, the kings of Assyrians have destroyed heathen men, and the lands of all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:29; ISAIAH 44:9–20; JEREMIAH 10:3–9"> and they have sent the gods of them into fire; for they were not gods, but &#91;the&#93; works of men’s hands, of wood and of stone; and they destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 83:18; 1ST KINGS 8:43; PSALMS 67:1–2"> Now therefore, our Lord God, make us safe from the hand of them, that all the realms of &#91;the&#93; earth know that thou art the Lord God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND KI 20:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:20–21; JEREMIAH 33:3"> Forsooth Isaiah, the son of Amoz, sent to Hezekiah, and said, The Lord God of Israel saith these things, I have heard those things, which thou prayedest me on Sennacherib, king of Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:13; JOB 16:4; JEREMIAH 14:17"> This is the word, that the Lord hath spoken of him, Thou virgin the daughter of Zion, the king of Assyria hath despised thee, and scorned thee; thou daughter of Jerusalem, he moved his head after thy back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:24; PSALMS 71:22; ISAIAH 30:11–12"> O! Sennacherib, whom hast thou despised, and whom hast thou blasphemed? Against whom hast thou raised thy voice, and hast raised thine eyes on high? Against the Holy of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND KI 18:17; PSALMS 20:7; ISAIAH 10:18"> By the hand of thy servants thou hast despised the Lord, and saidest, In the multitude of my chariots I went up into the high things of hills, in the highness of Lebanon, and &#91;I&#93; cutted down the high cedars thereof, and the chosen box trees thereof; and I entered unto the terms, or uttermost coasts, thereof, and I cutted down the forest of Carmel thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:6; EXODUS 15:9; 2ND SAMUEL 17:13"> and I drank alien waters, and I made dry with the steps of my feet all &#91;the&#93; waters enclosed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:7; ISAIAH 10:5–6; ISAIAH 37:26–27"> Whether thou heardest not, what I made at the beginning? From &#91;the&#93; eld &#91;or old&#93; days I made it, and now I have brought it forth; and strengthened cities of fighters shall be into falling of hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:30; PSALMS 102:11; JEREMIAH 51:32"> And they that sit meek in hand in those cities, trembled together, and be shamed; they be made as the hay of the field, and as green herb of roofs, which dried, or withered, before that it came to ripeness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:28–29; JEREMIAH 23:23–24; PSALMS 139:1–11"> And I knew thy dwelling &#91;place&#93;, and thy going out, and thine entering or thy going in, and thy way, and thy strong vengeance against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:4; EZEKIEL 38:4; 2ND KI 19:33"> Thou were wroth against me, and thy pride went up into mine ears; therefore I shall put a ring in thy nostrils, and a barnacle in thy lips, and I shall lead thee again into the way by which thou camest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:20–22; 2ND KI 20:8–9; EXODUS 3:12"> Forsooth Hezekiah, this shall be a sign to thee; eat thou in this year that, that thou findest; forsooth in the second year, those things that grow by their own will; soothly in the third year, sow ye, and reap ye, and plant ye vineries &#91;or vines&#93;, and eat the fruits of those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND KI 19:4; ISAIAH 27:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:22–23"> And whatever thing shall be residue, or left over, of the house of Judah, it shall send root downward, and shall make fruit upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:7; ROMANS 11:5; ROMANS 9:27"> For the relics, or folk left, shall go out of Jerusalem, and those who shall be saved, shall go out of the hill of Zion; the fervent love of the Lord of hosts shall do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:7–10; 2ND SAMUEL 20:15; ISAIAH 37:33–35"> Wherefore the Lord saith these things of the king of Assyrians, He shall not enter into this city Jerusalem, neither he shall send an arrow into it, neither shield of him shall occupy it, neither stronghold, either besieging, shall encompass it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND KI 19:28; 2ND KI 19:36"> He shall turn again by the way by which he came, and he shall not enter into this city, saith the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:12–13; 2ND KI 20:6; 1ST KINGS 15:4"> and I shall defend this city, and I shall save it for myself, and for David, my servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 32:21–22; 2ND SAMUEL 24:16; EXODUS 12:29–30"> Therefore it was done, in that night the angel of the Lord came, and smote in the castles &#91;or tents&#93; of the Assyrians an hundred fourscore and five thousand. And when Sennacherib had risen early, he saw all the bodies of &#91;the&#93; dead men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JONAH 1:2; 2ND KI 19:7; 2ND KI 19:33"> and he departed, and went away. And Sennacherib, king of Assyrians, turned again, and dwelled in Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EZRA 4:2; GENESIS 8:4; 2ND KI 19:7"> And when he worshipped in the temple Nisroch his god, Adrammelech and Sharezer, his sons, killed him with sword; and they fled into the land of Armenia; and Esarhaddon, his son, reigned for him.</span>
