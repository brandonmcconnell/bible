<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND KI 20:3; 1ST KINGS 11:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:20–21"> And he did that, that was good before the Lord, by all things that David, his father, had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 31:1; NUMBERS 21:8–9; 2ND KI 18:22"> And he destroyed &#91;the&#93; high places, and all-brake &#91;the&#93; images, and cut down &#91;the maumet&#93; woods, and he brake the brazen serpent, whom Moses had made; for unto that time the sons of Israel burnt incense to it; and he called the name of it Nehushtan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND KI 23:25; 2ND KI 19:10; MATTHEW 27:43"> And he hoped in the Lord God of Israel; therefore after him none was like him of all the kings of Judah, but neither also in the kings that were before him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:8; DEUTERONOMY 10:20; JOHN 14:15"> And he cleaved to the Lord, and went not away from his steps, and he did the commandments of the Lord, which the Lord commanded to Moses;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; GENESIS 39:2–3; 2ND KI 16:7"> wherefore and the Lord was with him, and he governed wisely himself in all things, to which he went forth. Also he rebelled against the king of Assyrians, and therefore he served not to him;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:29; 2ND KI 17:9; 2ND CHRONICLES 28:18"> and he smote &#91;the&#93; Philistines till to Gaza, and all the terms of them, from the tower of the keepers unto a city made strong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSEA 10:14; 2ND KI 17:3–23; 2ND KI 18:1"> In the fourth year of king Heze-kiah, that was the seventh year of Hoshea, the son of Elah, king of Israel, Shalmaneser, king of Assyrians, went up to Samaria, and fought against it,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 6:7; AMOS 9:1–4; MICAH 1:6–9"> and took it. For after three years, in the sixth year of Hezekiah, that is, in the ninth year of Hoshea, king of Israel, Samaria was taken;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 5:26; 2ND KI 17:6; ISAIAH 37:12"> and the king of Assyrians translated or brought over Israel into Assyrians, and he set them in Halah, and in Habor, rivers of Gozan, in the cities of Medes;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:17; 1ST PETER 2:8; MICAH 3:4"> for they heard not the voice of their Lord God, but they brake his covenant; they heard not, neither did all things, which Moses, the servant of the Lord, &#91;had&#93; commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:1–22; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:1–23; ISAIAH 7:17–25"> In the fourteenth year of king Hezekiah, Sennacherib, king of Assyr-ians, went up to all the strengthened cities of Judah, and took them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND KI 18:7; LUKE 14:32; 1ST KINGS 20:4"> Then Hezekiah, king of Judah, sent messengers to the king of Assyrians into Lachish, and said, I have sinned; go away from me, and I shall bear all things, that thou shalt put to me. Therefore the king of Assyrians putted on Hezekiah, king of Judah, a fine of three hundred talents of silver, and thirty talents of gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND KI 16:8; 1ST KINGS 15:18–19; 2ND KI 12:18"> And Hezekiah gave all the silver, that was found in the house of the Lord, and in the king’s treasures &#91;or treasuries&#93;, to the king of Assyrians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 29:3; 1ST KINGS 6:31–35"> In that time Hezekiah brake the gates of the temple of the Lord, and the plates of gold, which he had fastened to them, and he gave those &#91;or them&#93; to the king of Assyrians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 20:1; 2ND KI 20:20; ISAIAH 7:3"> Forsooth the king of Assyrians sent Tartan, and Rabsaris, and Rabshakeh, from Lachish to king Hezekiah, with strong hand to Jerusalem; and when they had gone up, they came to Jerusalem, and stood beside the water conduit of the higher cistern, which is in the way of the fuller, either tucker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND KI 19:2; ISAIAH 36:3; 2ND SAMUEL 8:16–17"> And they called the king; soothly Eliakim, the son of Hilkiah, the sovereign of the house, and Shebna, the scribe, and Joah, chancellor, the son of Asaph, went out to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:4; 2ND KI 19:10; ISAIAH 10:8–14"> And Rabshakeh said to them, Speak ye to Hezekiah, The great king, the king of Assyrians, saith these things, What is this trust, in which thou enforcest or endeavourest thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30–31"> In hap thou hast taken counsel, that thou wouldest make thee ready to battle. In whom trustest thou, that thou be fool-hardy to rebel against Sennacherib?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:7; EZEKIEL 29:6–7; ISAIAH 30:2–3"> Whether thou hopest in a staff of a reed and broken, that is, upon Egypt, on which, if a man leaneth, it shall be broken, and shall enter into his hand, and shall pierce it. So is Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to all men that trust in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 31:1; 2ND KI 18:4–5; MATTHEW 27:43"> That if thou sayest to me, We have trust in the Lord our God; whether this is not he, whose high things and altars Hezekiah took away, and commanded to Judah and to Jerusalem, saying, Ye shall worship before this altar in Jerusalem?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 17:42; 1ST KINGS 20:10; NEHEMIAH 4:2–5"> Now therefore, give ye weds to my lord, the king of Assyrians, and I shall give to you two thousand of horses, and see ye, whether ye may have riders of them?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:8; DEUTERONOMY 17:16; ISAIAH 36:6"> And how may ye withstand before one prince of the least servants of my lord? Whether thou hast trust in Egypt, for chariots and knights thereof?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND KI 19:6; ISAIAH 10:5–6; 2ND KI 19:22–37"> Whether I ascended &#91;or went&#93; up without God’s will to this place, that I should destroy it? The Lord said to me, Ascend thou &#91;or Go ye up&#93; to this land, and destroy thou it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZRA 4:7; DANIEL 2:4; ISAIAH 36:11–12"> Forsooth Eliakim, the son of Hilkiah, and Shebna, and Joah, said to Rabshakeh, We pray thee, that thou speak by the language of Syria to us, thy servants; for we understand this language; and that thou speak not to us by the language of Jews, while the people heareth, which is on the wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 73:8; LAMENTATIONS 4:5; EZEKIEL 4:15"> And Rabshakeh answered, and said, Whether my lord sent me to thy lord and to thee, that I should speak these words, and not rather to the men that sit on the wall, that they eat their turds, and drink their piss with you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:13–18; ISAIAH 10:8–13; EZEKIEL 29:3"> Therefore Rabshakeh stood, and cried with &#91;a&#93; great voice by language of Jews, and said, Hear ye the words of the great king, the king of Assyrians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 32:15; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:11"> The king saith these things, Hezekiah deceive not you, for he may not deliver you from mine hand;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 125:1–2; LUKE 23:35; 2ND KI 19:32–34"> neither give he trust to you on the Lord, and say, The Lord delivering shall deliver us, and this city shall not be betaken in the hand of the king of Assyrians;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST KINGS 4:25; 1ST KINGS 4:20; ZECHARIAH 3:10"> do not ye hear Hezekiah. For the king of Assyrians saith these things, Do ye with me that, that is profitable to you, and go ye out to me; and each man shall eat of his vinery &#91;or vine&#93;, and of his fig tree, and ye shall drink waters of your cisterns,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:7–9; EXODUS 3:8; DEUTERONOMY 11:12"> till I come, and translate you, or bear you over, into a land which is like your land, into a fruitful land, and plenteous of wine, a land of bread, and of vineries &#91;or vines&#93;, a land of olive trees, and of oil, and of honey; and ye shall live, and ye shall not die. Do not ye hear Hezekiah, that deceiveth you, and saith, The Lord shall deliver you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:10–11; 2ND KI 19:12–13; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:19"> Whether the gods of heathen men delivered their land from the hand of the king of Assyrians?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:9; 2ND KI 17:23–33; JEREMIAH 49:23"> Where is &#91;the&#93; god of Hamath, and of Arpad? Where is &#91;the&#93; god of Sepharvaim, of Hena, and of Ivah? Whether they delivered Samaria from mine hand?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DANIEL 3:15; ISAIAH 10:15; EXODUS 5:2"> For who be they in all &#91;the&#93; gods of lands, that delivered their country from mine hand, that the Lord may deliver Jerusalem from mine hand?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="AMOS 5:13; PROVERBS 9:7; MATTHEW 7:6"> Therefore the people was still, and answered not anything to him; for they had taken commandment of the king, that they should not answer to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2ND KI 22:11; GENESIS 37:29; 2ND KI 5:7"> And Eliakim, the son of Hilkiah, the sovereign of the house, and Shebna, the scribe, and Joah, the chancellor, the son of Asaph, came with rent clothes to Hezekiah; and told to him the words of Rabshakeh.</span>
