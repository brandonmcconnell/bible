<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 20:2; GENESIS 17:7–8; LEVITICUS 26:42"> And the angel of the Lord went up from Gilgal to the place of Weepers, and said, I led you out of Egypt, and I brought you into the land, for which I swore to your fathers, and promised, that I should not make void my covenant with you into without end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:2–4; JEREMIAH 2:36; EZRA 9:1–3"> so only that ye should not smite bond of peace with the dwellers of this land, and that ye destroy their altars; and you would not hear my voice. Why did ye these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:13; NUMBERS 33:55; DEUTERONOMY 7:16"> Wherefore I would not do them away from your face, that ye have them enemies, and that their gods be to you into falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:9; PROVERBS 17:10; ZECHARIAH 12:10"> And when the angel of the Lord spake these words to all the sons of Israel, they raised &#91;up&#93; their voice, and wept;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 7:9; GENESIS 35:8; JOSHUA 7:26"> and the name of the place was called, the place of Weepers, either of tears; and they offered there sacrifices to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:28–31; JOSHUA 22:6"> Then Joshua let the people go; and the sons of Israel went forth, each man into his possession, that they should get it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:12; JOSHUA 24:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:2"> And they served the Lord in all the days of Joshua, and of the elder men that lived after him long time, and knew all the great works of the Lord, which he had done with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:29–30"> Forsooth Joshua, &#91;the&#93; son of Nun, &#91;the&#93; servant of the Lord, was dead of an hundred years and ten;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:50; JOSHUA 24:30"> and they buried him in the ends of his possession, in Timnath of Heres, in the hill of Ephraim, at the north coast of the hill Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:12; TITUS 1:16; EXODUS 5:2"> And all that generation was gathered to their fathers; and other men of Israel rose up, that knew not the Lord, and the works which he had done with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 4:1; JUDGES 6:1; JUDGES 3:7"> And the sons of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and they served Baalim and Ashtaroth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5; DEUTERONOMY 13:5; DEUTERONOMY 6:14–15"> and forsook the Lord God of their fathers, that led them out of the land of Egypt; and they pursued &#91;or followed&#93; alien gods, the gods of peoples, that dwelled in the compass of them, and worshipped those gods, and they stirred the Lord to great wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 10:6; PSALMS 106:36; JUDGES 3:7"> and they forsook him, and served Baal and Ashtaroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND KI 17:20; PSALMS 106:40–42; DEUTERONOMY 32:30"> And the Lord was wroth against Israel, and he betook them into the hands of ravishers, the which took them, and sold them to enemies, that dwelled by compass; and they might not against-stand their adversaries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68; JOSHUA 23:15–16; LEVITICUS 26:14–46"> but whither ever they would go, the hand of the Lord was on them, that is, to torment them, as he spake and swore to them; and they were tormented greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:20; PSALMS 106:43–45; 1ST SAMUEL 12:11"> And the Lord raised judges, that delivered them from the hands of destroyers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 9:12; JUDGES 2:7"> but they would not hear them, and they did fornication, that is, idolatry, with alien gods, and worshipped them. Soon they forsook the way, by which their fathers entered; and they heard the commandments of the Lord, and did all things contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:36; JOSHUA 1:5; EXODUS 2:24"> And when the Lord raised up judges in their days, he was bowed by mercy, and he heard the wailings of them that were tormented, and he delivered them from the slaying of their destroyers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JUDGES 8:33; JUDGES 4:1; MATTHEW 23:32"> Soothly after that the judge was dead, they turned again, and did many things greater in evil than their fathers did; and they pursue &#91;or following&#93; alien gods, and served them, and worshipped them; they left not their own findings, and the hardest way by which they were wont to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 2:14; JOSHUA 23:16; EXODUS 32:10–11"> And the strong vengeance of the Lord was wroth against Israel, and he said, For this people hath made void my covenant which I covenanted with their fathers, and have despised to hear my voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:13; JUDGES 2:3; JUDGES 3:3"> also I shall not do away the folks, which Joshua left, and was dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:2; DEUTERONOMY 8:16; DEUTERONOMY 13:3"> that in them I assay Israel, whether they keep the way of the Lord, and go therein, as their fathers kept it, either nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Therefore the Lord left alive all these nations, and he would not destroy them soon, neither he betook them into the hands of Joshua.</span>
