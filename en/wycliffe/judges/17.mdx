<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 18:2; JOSHUA 17:14–18; JUDGES 10:1"> In that time was a man, that was called Micah, of the hill of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:25; RUTH 3:10; 1ST SAMUEL 23:21"> And he said to his mother, Lo! I have a thousand and an hundred plates of silver, which thou separatedest to thee, and on which thou cursedest, while I heard; and those be with me. To whom she answered, Blessed be my son of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 20:4; EXODUS 20:23; LEVITICUS 19:4"> Therefore he yielded those &#91;or them&#93; to his mother; and she said to him, I hallowed and avowed this silver to the Lord, that my son receive of mine hand, and make a graven image and a molten image; and now I give it to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:6–7; JEREMIAH 10:9–10"> Therefore he yielded to his mother; and she took two hundred plates of silver, and gave those &#91;or them&#93; to a workman of silver, that he should make of those &#91;or them&#93; a graven image and molten image, that was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 18:14; JUDGES 8:27; GENESIS 31:19"> And Micah also separated a little house, or an oratory, therein to God; and made ephod, and teraphim, that is, a priest’s cloth, and idols; and he filled the hand of one of his sons, and he was made a priest to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:8; PROVERBS 14:12; JUDGES 21:25"> In those days was no king in Israel, but each man did that, that seemed rightful &#91;or right&#93; to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MICAH 5:2; MATTHEW 2:1; RUTH 1:1–2"> Also another young waxing man was of Bethlehem of Judah, of the kindred of Judah, and he was a deacon &#91;or Levite&#93;, and dwelled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:10–11; JUDGES 17:11; JOSHUA 24:33"> And he went out of the city of Bethlehem, and would be a pilgrim, wherever he found profitable to him-self. And when he made journey, and had come into the hill of Ephraim, and had bowed &#91;down&#93; a little into the house of Micah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Micah asked him, From whence comest thou? Which answered, I am a deacon &#91;or Levite&#93; of Bethlehem of Judah, and I go, that I dwell where I may, and where I see that it is profitable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:19; JUDGES 18:19–20; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:10"> And Micah said, Dwell thou with me, and be thou to me a father and priest; and I shall give to thee by each year ten plates of silver, and a double clothing, and those things that be necessary to lifelode. &#91;And&#93; He assented,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and dwelled with that man; and he was to that man as one of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 18:30; NUMBERS 16:8–10; NUMBERS 16:5"> And Micah filled his hand, and he had the young man priest with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 10:2–3; PROVERBS 14:12; JOHN 16:2"> and he said, Now I know, that God shall do well to me, having a priest of the kin of Levi.</span>
