</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 28:20; 1ST SAMUEL 1:11; ECCLESIASTES 5:1–2"> and he made a vow to the Lord, and said, If thou shalt betake the sons of Ammon into mine hands,</span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:19; PHILIPPIANS 2:30; ROMANS 16:4"> To whom she answered, My father, if thou openedest thy mouth to the Lord, do to me whatever thing thou promisedest, while vengeance and victory of thine enemies be granted to thee.</span>
