<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 2:21–22; DEUTERONOMY 7:22; PROVERBS 17:3"> These be the folks which the Lord left alive, that in them he should teach Israel, and all men that knew not the battles of Canaanites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 3:5; GENESIS 3:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:26–27"> and that afterward the sons of them should learn to fight with enemies, and to have custom of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:3; JOSHUA 13:5; JUDGES 10:7"> He left five princes of the Philistines, and all Canaanites, and the people of Sidon, and Hivites that dwelled in the hill Lebanon, from the hill Baalhermon till to the entering of Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 3:1; JUDGES 2:22; EXODUS 15:25"> And he left them, that in them he should assay Israel, whether they would hear the behests of the Lord, which he commanded to their fathers by the hand of Moses, either nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34–38; GENESIS 10:15–18; EXODUS 3:8"> And so the sons of Israel dwelled in the midst of Canaanites, of Hittites, and of Amorites, and of Perizzites, and of Hivites, and of Jebusites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:3–4; EXODUS 34:16; 1ST KINGS 11:1–5"> and they wedded wives, the daughters of them; and the sons of Israel gave their daughters to their sons, and they served to their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 34:13; 2ND KI 23:6; 1ST KINGS 18:19"> And the sons of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and forgat their Lord God, and served Baalim, and Asheroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 2:14; HABAKKUK 3:7; JUDGES 4:9"> And the Lord was wroth against Israel, and he betook them into the hands of Chushanrishathaim, king of Mesopotamia, and they served him eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 3:15; JUDGES 1:13; PSALMS 22:5"> And they cried to the Lord, and he raised to them a saviour, and delivered them, that is, Othniel, the son of Kenaz, and the younger brother of Caleb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 13:25; 1ST SAMUEL 11:6; JUDGES 14:19"> And the spirit of the Lord was in him, and he deemed Israel. And he went out to battle, and the Lord betook into his hand Chushanrishathaim, king of Syria; and Othniel oppressed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 5:31; JUDGES 8:28; JOSHUA 11:23"> And the land rested forty years; and Othniel, the son of Kenaz, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 12:9; JUDGES 2:19; JUDGES 2:14"> Forsooth the sons of Israel added to do evil in the sight of the Lord; and he comforted against them Eglon, the king of Moab, for they did evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; DEUTERONOMY 34:3; JUDGES 5:14"> And the Lord coupled to him the sons of Ammon and Amalek; and he went, and smote Israel, and had in possession the city of Palms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:47–48; DEUTERONOMY 28:40; LEVITICUS 26:23–25"> And the sons of Israel served Eglon, the king of Moab, eighteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 3:9; PSALMS 78:34; ISAIAH 36:16"> And afterward they cried to the Lord; and he raised to them a saviour, Ehud by name, the son of Gera, son of Benjamin, the which Ehud used ever either hand for the right hand. And the sons of Israel sent by him gifts, that is, tribute, to Eglon, king of Moab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 3:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:12; PSALMS 149:6"> &#91;the&#93; which Ehud made to him a sword carving on ever either side, of the length of the palm of an hand; and he was girded therewith under the say, that is, a knight’s mantle, in the right hip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:20; JOB 15:27; JEREMIAH 50:11"> And he brought gifts to Eglon, king of Moab; and Eglon was full fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when he had given gifts to the king, he pursued &#91;or followed&#93; forth after his fellows that came with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:20; JUDGES 3:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:18–19"> and he turned again from Gilgal, where the graven idols were, and he said to the king, O king, I have a privy word to thee. And the king commanded silence. And when all men were gone out, that were about him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:7; AMOS 3:15; PSALMS 29:1"> Ehud entered to him; and the king sat alone in a summer parlour. And Ehud said, I have the word of God to thee. The which rose anon from his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:3; JOB 20:25; 1ST SAMUEL 15:33"> And Ehud held forth his left hand, and took his sword from his right hip; and he put it into the king’s womb so strongly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> that the pommel, either hilt, pursued &#91;or followed&#93; the iron in the wound, and was holden strait in the thickest fatness within; and Ehud drew not out the sword, but so as he had smitten Eglon, he left it in his body; and anon by the privates of mankind, the turds of the womb burst out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Forsooth when the doors of the parlour were closed most diligently, and fastened with a lock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 24:3"> Ehud went out by a porch. And the king’s servants entered, not into the parlour, but into the porch, and they saw the doors of the parlour closed, and they said, In hap he purgeth the womb in the summer parlour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they abode so long, till they were ashamed; and they saw that no man opened the door, and they took the key, and they opened, and they found their lord lying dead on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And while they were troubled, Ehud fled out, and passed the place of the graven idols, from whence he turned again; and he came into Seirath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 6:34; 1ST SAMUEL 13:3; JUDGES 5:14"> And anon he sounded with a clarion in the hill of Ephraim; and the sons of Israel came down with him, and he went in the front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JUDGES 12:5; JUDGES 7:9; JUDGES 7:15"> Which said to them, Follow ye me, for the Lord hath betaken our enemies, Moabites, into our hands. And they came down after him, and occupied the fords of Jordan, that led over into Moab. And they suffered not any man to pass &#91;over&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:15; JUDGES 3:17; PSALMS 17:10"> but they smote in that time about ten thousand Moabites, all mighty men and strong; no man of them might escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JUDGES 3:11; JUDGES 5:31"> And Moab was made low in that day under the hand of Israel, and the land rested eighty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JUDGES 5:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:17; 1ST SAMUEL 17:50"> After him was Shamgar, the son of Anath, that smote of Philistines six hundred men with a goad of ox; and he also defended Israel.</span>
