<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 20:18; NUMBERS 27:21; JUDGES 20:28"> After the death of Joshua the sons of Israel counselled with the Lord, and said, Who shall go up before us against Canaanites, and shall be duke of the battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:5; HEBREWS 7:14; NUMBERS 2:3"> And the Lord said, Judah shall go up; lo! I have given the land into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 10:11; JOSHUA 19:1; JUDGES 1:17"> And Judah said to Simeon, his brother, Go thou up with me in my lot, and fight thou against Canaanites, that I go with thee in thy lot. And Simeon went with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 11:8; EXODUS 23:28–29; JOSHUA 11:6–8"> and Judah went up. And the Lord betook Canaanites and Perizzites into their hands, and they killed in Bezek ten thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they found Adonibezek in Bezek, and they fought against him, and they overcame Canaanites, and Perizzites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Adonibezek fled, whom they pursued, and took, and they cut off the ends of his hands and of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:33; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:10; LUKE 6:37–38"> And Adonibezek said, Seventy kings, when the ends of their hands and of their feet were cut away, gathered remnants of meats under my board; as I have done, so God hath yielded to me. And they brought him into Jerusalem, and there he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:63; JUDGES 1:21"> Then the sons of Judah fought against Jerusalem, and they took it, and they smote it by the sharpness of sword, and they betook all the city to burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:13–20; JOSHUA 11:21; JOSHUA 10:36"> And afterward they went down, and fought against Canaanites, that dwelled in the hilly places, and at the south, in &#91;the&#93; wild fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:22; JOSHUA 14:15; JEREMIAH 9:23"> And Judah went against Canaanites, that dwelled in Hebron, whose name was by eld &#91;or old&#93; time Kiriatharba; and Judah killed Sheshai, and Ahiman, and Talmai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:15; JOSHUA 10:38–39"> And from thence he went forth, and he came to the dwellers of Debir, whose eld &#91;or old&#93; name was Kiriath-sepher, that is, the city of letters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:16–17; 1ST SAMUEL 17:25; 1ST SAMUEL 18:23"> And Caleb said, I shall give Achsah, my daughter, wife to him that shall smite Kiriathsepher, and shall waste it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 3:9"> And when Othniel, the son of Kenaz, the younger brother of Caleb, had taken it, Caleb gave Achsah, his daughter, wife to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:18–19"> And her husband stirred her, going in the way, that she should ask of her father a field; and when she had sighed, sitting on the ass, Caleb said to her, What hast thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:5; 1ST PETER 3:9"> And she answered, Give thou &#91;a&#93; blessing to me, for thou hast given a dry land to me; give thou to me also a moist land with waters. And Caleb gave to her the moist land above, and the moist land beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:29–32; JUDGES 4:11; 1ST SAMUEL 15:6"> Forsooth the sons of Kenite, the father of Moses’ wife, went up from the city of Palms with the sons of Judah, into the desert of his lot, which desert is at the south of Arad; and &#91;they&#93; dwelled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:3; JUDGES 1:3; JOSHUA 19:4"> And Judah went with Simeon, his brother; and they smote altogether Canaanites, that dwelled in Zephath, and they killed him; and the name of that city was called Hormah, that is, cursing, either perfect destroying, for that city was destroyed utterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:22; JUDGES 3:3; JOSHUA 15:45–47"> And Judah took Gaza with his coasts, and Askelon, and Ekron with his terms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; JUDGES 1:2; PSALMS 60:12"> And the Lord was with Judah, and he had in possession the hilly places; and he might not do away the dwellers of the valley, for they were plenteous in iron chariots, full of weapons, sharp as scythes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 1:10; JOSHUA 15:13–14; NUMBERS 14:24"> And the sons of Israel gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had said, and Caleb did away from it three sons of Anak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:63; JUDGES 19:10–12; JUDGES 1:8"> But the sons of Benjamin did not away Jebusites, the dwellers of Jerusalem; and Jebusites dwelled with the sons of Benjamin in Jerusalem unto this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JUDGES 1:19; 2ND KI 18:7; NUMBERS 1:32"> Also the house of Joseph went up into Bethel, and the Lord was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 28:19; GENESIS 35:6; GENESIS 48:3"> For when they besieged the city, that was called Luz before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:12–14; 1ST SAMUEL 30:15"> they saw a man going out of the city, and they said to him, Show thou to us the entering of the city, and we shall do mercy with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:22–25"> And when he had showed to them, they smote the city by sharpness of sword; and they delivered that man and all his kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND KI 7:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 1:17"> And when he was delivered, he went into the land of Hittites, and builded there a city, and called it Luz; which is called so till into this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:11–13; JEREMIAH 48:10; PSALMS 106:34–35"> Also Manasseh did not away Beth-shean and Taanach with their towns, and the dwellers of Dor, and Ibleam, and Megiddo, with their towns; and Canaanites began to dwell with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Soothly after that Israel was comforted, he made them tributaries, either to pay tribute, and would not do away them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 16:10; 1ST KINGS 9:16"> Soothly Ephraim killed not Canaanites that dwelled in Gezer, but dwelled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:15"> Zebulun did not away the dwellers of Kitron, and of Nahalol; but Canaanites dwelled in the midst of him, and was made tributary to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:24–30"> Also Asher did not away the dwellers of Accho, and of Sidon, of Ahlab, and of Achzib, and of Helbah, and of Aphik, and of Rehob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34–35"> and Asher dwelled in the midst of Canaanites, the dwellers of that land, and Asher killed not him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:32–38; JUDGES 1:30; PSALMS 18:24"> Naphtali did not away the dwellers of Bethshemesh, and of Bethanath; and he dwelled among Canaanites, the dwellers of the land; and Bethshe-mesh and Bethanath were tributaries to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:47; JUDGES 18:1"> And Amorites held strait the sons of Dan in the hill, and he gave not place to them to go down to &#91;the&#93; plainer places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:42; JUDGES 12:12; JOSHUA 10:12"> and he dwelled in the hill of Heres, which is interpreted, Witnessing, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim. And the hand of the house of Joseph was made heavy, and he was made tributary to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:4; JOSHUA 15:2–3"> And the term of Amorites was from the ascending or going up of Scorpion, and from the stone, and &#91;the&#93; higher places.</span>
