<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 17:6; JUDGES 19:1; JUDGES 1:34"> In those days was no king in Israel; and the lineage of Dan sought possession to itself, to dwell therein; for till to that day it had not taken heritage among other lineages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 13:25; JOSHUA 2:1; JUDGES 18:11"> Therefore the sons of Dan sent five the strongest men of their generation, and meine, from Zorah and Eshtaol, that they should espy the land, and behold diligently. And they said to them, Go ye, and behold the land. And when they going forth had come into the hill country of Ephraim, and had entered into the house of Micah, they rested there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 12:6; GENESIS 27:22; MATTHEW 26:73"> And they knew the voice of the young waxing deacon &#91;or Levite&#93;; and they rested in his place, and said to him, Who brought thee hither? What doest thou here? For what cause wouldest thou come hither?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 17:10; EZEKIEL 13:19; PROVERBS 28:21"> The which answered, Micah hath given to me these things and these, and he hath hired me for meed, that I be priest to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:10; ISAIAH 30:1; 1ST KINGS 22:5"> And they prayed him, that he should counsel with the Lord, and that they might know, whether they went in the way of prosperity, and that the thing of their purpose should have effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:6; 1ST KINGS 22:15; PSALMS 33:18"> Which answered to them, Go ye with peace, the Lord beholdeth your way, and the journey whither ye go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:47; JUDGES 18:27–28; 1ST SAMUEL 3:13"> Then those five men went forth, and came to Laish; and they saw the people dwelling therein without any dread, by the custom of Sidonians, secure and restful, for no man utterly against-stood them, and they were full rich, and dwelled far from Sidon, and were parted from all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 18:11; JUDGES 18:2; JUDGES 13:2"> And they turned again to their brethren in Zorah and Eshtaol; and they answered to brethren, asking what they had done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:23–24; NUMBERS 13:30; JOSHUA 18:3"> and said, Rise ye, and go we up to them of Laish, for we have seen the land full rich and plenteous; do not ye be negligent, do not ye cease, go we forth, and have we it into possession;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 18:27; JUDGES 18:7; DEUTERONOMY 2:29"> no travail shall be to us; we shall enter to secure men, into a full large country; and the Lord shall betake to us a place, wherein is not poverty of anything of those things that be brought forth in &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 18:16"> Therefore six hundred men girded with arms of battle went forth of the kindred of Dan, that is, from Zorah and Eshtaol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 13:25; JOSHUA 15:60; 2ND CHRONICLES 1:4"> And they went up, and dwelled in Kiriathjearim of Judah, the which place took from that time the name of &#91;the&#93; Tents of Dan, and it is behind the back of Kiriathjearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:30; JUDGES 19:1; JUDGES 18:2–3"> From thence they passed into the hill country of Ephraim; and when they had come to the house of Micah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 17:5; JUDGES 18:3–4; 1ST SAMUEL 14:28"> the five men, that were sent before to behold the land of Laish, said to their other brethren, Know ye, that ephod, and teraphim, and a graven image or idol, and a molten image is in these houses; see ye what pleaseth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 43:27; 1ST SAMUEL 17:22; JOHN 14:27"> And when they had bowed a little aside, they entered into the house of the young deacon, that was in the house of Micah, and they greeted him with peaceable words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 18:11"> And six hundred men stood before the door, so as they were armed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDGES 18:14; JUDGES 17:4–5; JUDGES 18:2"> And they, that entered into the house of the young man, enforced or endeavoured to take away the graven image, and the ephod, and teraphim, and the molten image; and the priest stood before the door, while six hundred full strong men abode not far thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Therefore they that entered took the graven image, ephod, and idols, and the molten image; to whom the priest said, What do ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 29:9; JOB 21:5; JUDGES 17:10"> To whom they answered, Be thou still, and put thy finger on thy mouth, and come with us, that we have thee father and priest. What is better to thee, that thou be priest in the house of one man, either in a lineage and meine of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:11; EZEKIEL 13:19; PROVERBS 30:15"> And when he had heard this, he assented to their words, and he took the ephod, and idols, and the graven image, and went forth with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And when they went forth, and had made their little children, and their work beasts, and all thing that was precious, to go before them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and when they were now far from the house of Micah, &#91;the&#93; men that dwelled in the houses of or near Micah cried together, and pursued &#91;or followed&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 114:5; 2ND SAMUEL 14:5; 1ST SAMUEL 11:5"> and began to cry after the backs. Which when they had beheld, &#91;they&#93; said to Micah, What wilt thou to thee? why criest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:18–19; ISAIAH 44:18–20; PSALMS 115:8"> Which answered, Ye have taken away my gods, which I made to me, and the priest, and what dwelleth over? and ye say, What is to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOB 27:2; JOB 3:5; 1ST SAMUEL 30:6"> And the sons of Dan said to him, Beware, lest thou speak &#91;any&#93; more to us, and men stirred in soul come to thee, and thou perish with all thine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And so Dan went forth in the journey begun. And Micah saw, that they were stronger than he, and &#91;he&#93; turned again into his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:47; JUDGES 18:7; JUDGES 18:10"> Forsooth six hundred men took the priest, and the things which we before-said, and came into Laish to the people resting and secure; and they smited them by the sharpness of sword, and betook the city to burning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 10:6; JUDGES 18:7; NUMBERS 13:21"> while no man utterly gave help, for they dwelled far from Sidon, and had not anything of fellowship and cause with any men. And the city was set in the countryside of Bethrehob; the which city Dan builded again, and dwelled therein;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:47; GENESIS 14:14; 1ST KINGS 15:20"> and the name of the city was called Dan, by the name of their father, whom Israel had begat, the which city was called Laish before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 4:10–11; JUDGES 13:1; JUDGES 17:5"> And Dan setted there the graven image, and Jonathan, the son of Gershom, &#91;the&#93; son of Moses, and Jonathan’s sons, the priests, in the lineage of Dan, till into the day of their captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:1; 1ST SAMUEL 1:3; JUDGES 19:18"> And the idol of Micah dwelled with them, in all &#91;the&#93; time that the house of God was in Shiloh.</span>
