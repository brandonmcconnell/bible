<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the same time Antiochus turned again unhonestly or dishonoured from Persia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For he had entered into that city, that is said Persepolis, that is, the chief city of Persia, and he attempted for to rob the temple, and oppress the city; but for the multitude ran together to arms, they were turned into flight; and so it befell, that Antiochus after flight vile-like &#91;or lowly&#93;, turned again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And when he came about Ecbatana, he knew what things were done against Nicanor and Timothy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Forsooth he was enhanced in wrath, and deemed that he might turn into Jews the wrong of them, that had driven him. And therefore he bade the chariot to be led in haste, doing journey without ceasing; for why heavenly doom drove, or constrained, him, for that he spake so proudly, that he shall come to Jerusalem, and to make it a gathering of sepulchres of Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But the Lord God of Israel, that beholdeth all things, smote him with a wound incurable and invisible; for as he ended this same word, an hard sorrow of entrails took him, and bitter torments of inward things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And soothly justly enough, for he that had tormented the entrails of other men, with many and new torments,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> though he in no manner ceased of his malice. Forsooth over this he was filled with pride, and breathed fire in soul against Jews, and commanding the need for to be hasted, it befell, that he going in fierceness fell down off the chariot, and that the members were travailed with the grievous hurtling altogether of body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he that seemed to himself for to command also to waves of the sea, and over man’s manner was filled with pride, and for to weigh in balance the heights of hills, was then made low &#91;or meeked&#93; to earth, and was borne in a bier, either horse-litter, and witnessed in himself the open virtue of God;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> so that worms boiled out of the body of the unpious man, and the quick fleshes of him floated out in sorrows. Also with the savour of him, and stinking, his host was grieved;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and no man might bear him, for unsuffering of stink, that a little before deemed him for to touch the stars of heaven. &#91;and he, that a little before deemed him to touch the stars of heaven, no man might bear him, for unsuffering of stink.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore hereby he was led down from grievous pride, and began for to come to knowing of himself, and was warned by God’s vengeance, for by all moments his sorrows took increases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And when he might not then &#91;or now&#93; suffer his stink, thus he said, It is just for to be subject to God, and that a deadly man feel not even things to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Forsooth the cursed man prayed the Lord of these things, of whom he should not get mercy or should get no mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And now he desireth to yield free the city, to which he came hasting, for to draw down it &#91;or to lead it down&#93; to earth, and for to make a sepulchre of things borne together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And now he promiseth to make the Jews even to men of Athens, which Jews he said that he should not have worthy, yea, of sepulchre, but to betake to fowls and wild beasts, for to be drawn, and to destroy with little children; &#91;And the Jews, whom he said himself to having neither soothly worthy of sepulchre, but to betaking to fowls and wild beasts, for to be drawn, and to destroying with little children, now he promiseth to making even to Athenians, or men of Athens;&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> also to adorn with best gifts the holy temple, which he robbed before, and to multiply holy vessels, and to giving of his rents costs pertaining to sacrifices;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> over these things and that he shall be made a Jew, and to walk by each place of the land, and to preach the power of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But, for sorrows ceased not, the just doom of God had above come on him, he despaired, and wrote to Jews, by manner of beseeching, an epistle, containing these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> To the best citizens, Jews, most health, and welfare, and to be rich, or in prosperity, the king and prince Antiochus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> If ye fare well, and your sons, and all things be to you of sentence, that is, befall at your will, we do most thankings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I am ordained in sickness, and soothly I am mindful benignly of you, and I turned again from places of Persia, and am caught with grievous infirmity, and I led needful for to have care for common profit;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and I despair not of myself, but I have much hope to escape &#91;the&#93; sick-ness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For I behold that also my father, in what times he led host in higher places, showed, who after him should receive princehood;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> if that any contrary thing befell, or hard thing were told, these that were in countries, shall know to whom the sum, or charge, of &#91;all&#93; things was left, and should not be troubled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> To these things I beheld of next, that all the mighty men and neighbours espy times, and abide coming, and I have ordained my son Antiochus king, whom I, running again often into high realms, commended to many of you, and I wrote to him what things be subject.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Therefore I pray you, and ask, that ye be mindful of benefices openly and privily, and that each of you keep faith to me, and to my son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For I trust, that he shall do mildly, and manly, or courteously, and pursue my purpose, and be common, or treatable, to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Therefore the man-queller and blasphemer was smitten worst, and as he had treated others, he died in pilgrimage in mountains, in wretched-ful death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Forsooth Philip, his even-sucker, translated &#91;or transferred&#93;, or bare over, the body; which dreaded the son of Antiochus, and went to Ptolemy Philometor, into Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>
