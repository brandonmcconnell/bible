<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he said, And I am mighty on earth, which command arms for to be taken, and needs of the king for to be fulfilled. Nevertheless he got not, for to perform counsel.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and he admonished his, that they should not inwardly dread at the coming to of nations, but should have in mind the helps done to them of heaven, and now should hope that the victory should come to them of the Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he spake to them of the law, and prophets, and admonished of battles which they did before, and ordained, &#91;or confirmed&#93;, them readier.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And so when the souls of them were raised, he showed altogether the falseness of heathen men, and breaking of oaths.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Forsooth he armed each of them, not by strengthening of shield and shaft, but with best words and admonishings, and expounded a sweven worthy of believe, by which he gladded all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Soothly the vision was such. Judas saw Onias, that was highest priest, a good man and benign, shamefast in sight, and mild in manners, and fair in speech, and which was exercised in virtues from a child, holding forth the hands for to pray for all the people of Jews.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After this thing, that also another man appeared, wonderful in age and glory, and in having of great fairness about him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsooth he saw Onias answering for to have said, This is the lover of brethren, and of the people of Israel; this is he, that much prayeth for the people, and &#91;for&#93; all the holy city, Jeremy, the prophet of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Forsooth he saw that Jeremy hath stretched forth the right hand, and hath given a golden sword to Judas, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Take thou the holy sword, a gift of God, in which thou shalt cast down the adversaries of my people Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Therefore they were admonished with full great words of Judas, of which fierceness might be enhanced, and souls of young men be comforted, and they ordained for to fight, and torment altogether strongly, that virtue should deem of needs, or causes, for that the holy city, and temple were in peril.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For why &#91;or Soothly&#93; for wives, and sons, and also for brethren, and cousins, was less busyness, but the most and first dread was &#91;for&#93; holiness of the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But not least busyness had them that were in the city, for these that should assail, or fight, together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And when now all men hoped doom to be, and enemies come &#91;or came&#93;, and the host was ordained, beasts and horsemen put together in covenable places,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Maccabeus beheld the coming of multitude, and diverse apparel of arms, and fierceness of beasts, and he stretched out the hands to heaven, and called to help the Lord doing great wonders, which not by &#91;or after&#93; power of arms, but as it pleaseth to him, giveth victory to worthy men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Forsooth he said, calling to help in this manner, Thou Lord, that sentest thine angel under Hezekiah, king of Judea, and hast slain of the tents, either hosts, of Sennacherib, an hundred thousand fourscore and five thousand &#91;or an hundred fourscore and five thousand&#93;;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and now, lordshipper of heavens, send thou thy good angel before us, in dread and trembling of greatness of thine arm,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> that they dread, that come with blasphemy against thine holy people. And soothly thus he perfectly prayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Forsooth Nicanor, and they that were with him, moved to with trumps and songs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Judas forsooth, and they that were with him, called God to help by prayers &#91;of acknowledging&#93;, and went together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Soothly they fighting with hands, but praying God in hearts, casted down five and thirty thousand, not less, and delighted greatly by presence of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And when they had ceased, and with joy turned again, they knew that Nicanor had fallen, with his armours.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Therefore when cry was made, and perturbation was stirred, by country voice they blessed the Lord Almighty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Whither when he fully came, when men of his lineage were called together, and priests to the altar, he called also them that were in the high tower.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And when the head of Nicanor was showed, and the cursed hand, which he holding forth against the holy house of Almighty God greatly gloried,</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> also he commanded the tongue of unpious Nicanor cut off, for to be given to birds gobbet-meal; forsooth he commanded the hand of the mad man for to be hanged up against the temple.</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Therefore all blessed the Lord of heaven, and said, Blessed be the Lord, that kept his place undefouled.</span>

</div>
