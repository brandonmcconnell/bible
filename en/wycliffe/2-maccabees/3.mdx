<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Therefore when the holy city was inhabited in all peace, laws also yet were best kept, for the faithfulness of Onias, bishop, &#91;or for ordinance and piety of Onias, priest&#93;, and for souls hating evil things,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> it was made, that both they kings and princes led the place worthy highest honour, and lighted the temple with greatest gifts;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> so that Seleucus, king of Asia, gave of his rents all expenses pertain-ing to the service of sacrifices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Forsooth Simon, of the lineage of Benjamin, that was ordained sovereign &#91;or provost&#93; of the temple, when the prince of priests against-stood him, strove for to cast some wicked thing in the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But when he might not overcome Onias, he came to Apollonius, son of Tarsus, that in that time was duke of Coelesyria and Phoenicia;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and told to him, that the treasury in Jerusalem was full with riches &#91;or with monies&#93; unnumberable; and that common riches be great, which pertain not to the reason of sacrifices; forsooth that it was possible, that all things fall under power of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And when Apollonius had told to the king of riches &#91;or monies&#93; that were borne in, he sent Heliodorus called, that was on his needs, with commandments for to bear out the foresaid money.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And anon Heliodorus took the way, soothly by form as if he were to passing by Coelesyria and Phoenicia cities, but in true thing to performing the king’s purpose.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But when he came to Jerusalem, and was received benignly of the highest priest in the city, he told of doom given of the riches &#91;or of monies&#93;, and opened for cause of what thing he came; forsooth he asked, if verily these things were so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the highest priest showed, that these things were kept to the lifelodes &#91;or the livelodes&#93; of widows, and of fatherless either motherless children;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> that some soothly were of Hyrcanus son of Tobias, a man full noble in these things, that unpious Simon had told; forsooth that all the talents of silver were four hundred, and of gold two hundred;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> for that it was impossible on all manner, that they be deceived, that betook their things to be kept to the place and temple, that by all the world was honoured for his worship-ping, and holiness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he said, for these things that he had in commandments of the king, That in all kind those should be borne to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsooth in the day ordained Heliodorus entered, to ordain of these things; forsooth there was not a little trembling through all the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Forsooth priests casted themselves before the altar, with priests’ stoles, and called to help from heaven him that gave law of things put in keeping, that he should keep those things safe to them that had put those &#91;or had put them&#93; in keeping.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now forsooth he that saw the cheer of the highest priest, was wounded in soul; for the face and colour was changed, and declared the inward sorrow of soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For some sorrowfulness was shed about to the man, and hideousness of body, by which the sorrow of heart was made known to men beholding.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Also other men gathered together flock-meal, and came out of houses, beseeching with open beseeching, for that that the place was to coming into despite.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And women were gird on the breast with hair-shirts, and flowed together by streets; but and virgins, that were closed altogether, ran to Onias; others forsooth to the walls, some soothly beheld by windows.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Forsooth all held forth hands into heaven, and besought;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> for there was a wretched abiding of multitude mingled, and of the highest priest ordained in strife, or anguish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And these soothly called Almighty God to help, that things taken in keeping should be kept in all holiness, to them that had put those &#91;or had put them&#93; in keeping.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Forsooth Heliodorus performed that thing, that he had deemed,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and he was present with his knights in the same place about the treasury. But the Spirit of Almighty God made great evidence of his showing, so that all that were hardy for to obey to him, fell down by virtue of God, and were converted into feebleness, &#91;or unstrength&#93;, and inward dread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For an horse appeared to them, and had a dreadful sitter, adorned with best coverings; and he with fierceness rushed the former feet or forefeet to Heliodorus; forsooth he that sat on him, seemed &#91;or was seen&#93; for to have golden armours.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Also two other young men appeared, fair in virtue, best in glory, and fair in clothing, that stood about him, and on each side scourged him without ceasing, and beat with many wounds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Soothly forsooth Heliodorus fell down to the earth, and they ravished him shed about with much darkness, and casted out him &#91;or casted him out&#93;, put in a pack saddle, either horse’s litter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he that entered with many runners and knights into the foresaid treasury, was borne, when no man helped him, for the open virtue of God was known;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and forsooth by God’s virtue he lay dumb, and deprived of all hope and health.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Forsooth these Jews blessed the Lord, for he magnified his place; and the temple, that a little before was full of dread and noise, is &#91;full&#93;-filled with joy and gladness, for the Lord Almighty appeared.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then forsooth some of Heliodorus’ friends prayed anon Onias, for to call to help the Highest, and for to give life to him, that was set in the last spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Soothly the highest priest beheld, lest peradventure the king would suppose any malice fully done of Jews about Heliodorus, and offered for health of the man a wholeful or an healthful sacrifice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And when the highest priest prayed, the same younglings, clothed in the same clothes, stood nigh &#91;to&#93; Heliodorus, and said, Do thou thankings to Onias, the priest; for why for him the Lord hath given life to thee;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> thou soothly, that art scourged of God, tell to all men the great doings and power of God. And when these things were said, they appeared not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Heliodorus soothly, when a sacrifice was offered to God, and great avows were promised to him, that granted him for to live, and did thankings or thanks to Onias; and when his host was received, he went again to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Soothly he witnessed to all men the works of great God, which he saw under his eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Forsooth when the king asked Heliodorus, who was able to be sent yet once to Jerusalem, he said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> If thou hast any enemy, either traitor of thy realm, send thither, and thou shalt receive him beaten, if nevertheless he shall escape; for some virtue of God is verily in the place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> For why he that hath dwelling in heavens, is visitor and helper of that place; and he smiteth and loseth them, that come to mis-do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Therefore of Heliodorus, and keeping of the treasury, thus the thing hath itself.</span>
</div>
