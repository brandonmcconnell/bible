<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But a little time after, Lysias, the procurator of the king, and kinsman, and sovereign of offices &#91;or provost of needs&#93;, bare grievously of these things that befell,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and gathered fourscore thousands, and all the multitude of horsemen, and came against Jews, and deemed himself to make the city taken a dwelling to heathen men,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> forsooth to have the temple into winning of money, as other temples of heathen men, and priesthood set to sale by each year;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and bethought not on the power of God, but in mind, or understanding, he was made without bridle, and trusted in multitude of footmen, and in thousands of horsemen, and in fourscore elephants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Soothly he went into Judea, and came nigh to Bethsura, that was in a strait place, from Jerusalem in space of five furlongs, and fought against that strength.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Soothly when Maccabeus, and they that were with him, knew that strengths were impugned, with weeping and tears they prayed the Lord, and all the company together, for to send a good angel to the health of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Maccabeus himself took first arms, and admonished others to take together peril with him, and bear help to their brethren.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when they went forth together with ready will from Jerusalem, an horseman, that is, an angel in the likeness of an horseman, or knight, appeared going before them in white clothes, in golden armours or arms, and flourishing a shaft.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then all together blessed the merciful Lord, and waxed strong in souls; and were ready for to pierce not only men, but and most fierce beasts, and iron walls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Therefore they went ready, having an helper of heaven, and the Lord having mercy on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Soothly by custom of lions, in fierceness they hurled into enemies, and casted down of them eleven thousands &#91;or eleven thousand&#93; of footmen, and a thousand and six hundred of horsemen. Soothly they turned all into flight;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> forsooth many of them wounded, escaped naked, but and Lysias himself foully fleeing escaped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for he was not witless, he areckoned with himself the making less done against him, and understood that Hebrews be unovercome, and trust to help of Almighty God;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and he sent to them, and promised him to consent to all things that be just, and to compel the king for to be made friend.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Forsooth Maccabeus granted to the prayers of Lysias, and counselled to profit in all things; and whatever things Maccabeus wrote of Jews to Lysias &#91;or wrote to Lysias of Jews&#93;, the king granted those things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For why epistles were written to Jews from Lysias, containing this manner. Lysias to the people of Jews, health.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> John and Absalom, that were sent from you, betook writs, and asked, that I should &#91;ful&#93; fill those things that were signified by them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Therefore whatever things might be brought forth to the king, I expounded, and which the thing suffered, that is, was covenable and just, he granted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore if in needs ye &#91;shall&#93; keep faith, also from henceforth I shall enforce or endeavour, or attempt for to be cause of good things to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Of other things soothly I commanded by all words, both to these and to them that be sent of me, for to speak together with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Fare ye well. In the hundred year and eight and fortieth, in the four and twentieth day of the month Dioscorinthius, that is, in June.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Forsooth the epistle of the king contained these things. King Antiochus to Lysias, brother, health.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For our father is translated among gods, we will that they that be in our realm do without noise, and give diligence to their things;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> we have heard that Jews assented not to the father, to be translated to the custom of Greeks, but will &#91;for to&#93; hold their ordinance, and that there-fore they ask of us, that their lawful things be granted to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Therefore we will that also this folk be quiet, and have ordained and deemed, that the temple be restored to them, that they should do by &#91;or after&#93; the custom of their greater men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Therefore thou shalt do well, if thou shalt send to them, and shalt give right hand; that, when our will is known, they be in good comfort, and serve to their own profits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Soothly to the Jews the king’s epistle was such. King Antiochus to the senate, or elder men, of Jews, and to other Jews, health.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> If ye fare well, so it is as we will, but and we &#91;our&#93; selves fare well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Menelaus came to us, and said, that ye will go down to yours, that be with us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Therefore to these that go together, we give right hands of secureness till to the thirtieth day of the month Xanthicus, that is, April,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> that Jews use their meats, and laws, as and before &#91;or as before&#93;; and no man of them in any manner suffer dis-ease of these things, that be done by ignorance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Soothly we sent also Menelaus, that shall speak to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Fare ye well. In the hundred year and eight and fortieth, the fifteenth day of the month Xanthicus,</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> also Romans sent an epistle, having it thus. Quintus Memmius, and Titus Manlius, legates of Romans, to the people of Jews, health.</span>
