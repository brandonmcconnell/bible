<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:1; EPHESIANS 3:1; PHILEMON 1:9"> Paul, the bound of Christ Jesus, and Timothy, brother, to Philemon, beloved, and our helper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:25; COLOSSIANS 4:17; ROMANS 16:5"> and to Apphia, most dear sister, and to Archippus, our even-knight, and to the church that is in thine house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 1:7; EPHESIANS 1:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:14"> grace be to you, and peace of God our Father, and of the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 1:8; EPHESIANS 1:16; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:3"> I do thankings to my God, ever-more making mind of thee, in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:4; EPHESIANS 1:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:1"> hearing thy charity, and faith, that thou hast in the Lord Jesus, and to all holy men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:9–11; 2ND PETER 1:8; COLOSSIANS 1:9"> that the communing of thy faith be made open, in knowing of all good thing in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 7:13; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:4"> And I had great joy and comfort in thy charity, for the entrails of holy men rested &#91;or were refreshed&#93; by thee, brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:6; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:12"> For which thing I having much trust in Christ Jesus, to command to thee that that pertaineth to profit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:20; EPHESIANS 4:1; ROMANS 12:1"> but I beseech more for charity, since thou art such as the old Paul, and now the bound of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:15; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:2"> I beseech thee for my son Onesimus, whom I in bonds begat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:11; JOB 30:1–2; 1ST PETER 2:10"> which sometime was unprofitable to thee, but now profitable, both to thee and to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:14–15; JEREMIAH 31:20; MARK 11:25"> whom I sent again to thee. And receive thou him as mine entrails;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:7; EPHESIANS 3:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:17"> whom I would withhold with me, that he should serve for thee to me in bonds of the gospel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:7; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:17; 1ST PETER 5:2–3"> but without thy counsel I would not do anything, that thy good should not be as of need, but willful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 50:20; GENESIS 45:5–8; ISAIAH 20:6"> For peradventure therefore he departed from thee for a time, that thou shouldest receive him without end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:22; MATTHEW 23:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:22"> now not as a servant, but for a servant a most dear brother &#91;or but more than a servant, most dear brother&#93;, most to me; and how much more to thee, both in &#91;the&#93; flesh and in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:23; MATTHEW 10:40; PHILEMON 1:12"> Therefore if thou hast me a fellow, receive him as me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:4–7"> for if he hath anything annoyed thee, either oweth, areckon thou this thing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:11; GALATIANS 5:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:15"> I, Paul, wrote with mine hand, I shall yield; that I say not to thee, that also thou owest to me thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3RD JOHN 1:4; PHILEMON 1:7; PHILEMON 1:12"> So, brother, I shall use thee in the Lord; &#91;full&#93;-fill thou mine entrails in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:16"> I trusting of thine obedience wrote to thee, witting that thou shalt do over that that I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 1:11; PHILIPPIANS 2:24; 2ND JOHN 1:12"> Also make thou ready to me an house to dwell in; for I hope that by your prayers I shall be given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:7; ROMANS 16:7; COLOSSIANS 4:12"> Epaphras, prisoner with me in Christ Jesus, greeteth thee well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:10–11; COLOSSIANS 4:14; COLOSSIANS 4:10"> and Marcus, Aristarchus, Demas, Lucas, mine helpers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:22; ROMANS 16:23; ROMANS 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</span>
</div>
