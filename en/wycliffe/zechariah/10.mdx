<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:36; JEREMIAH 27:9; EZEKIEL 34:5"> For simulacra spake unprofitable things, and diviners saw leasing; and dreamers spake vainly, idly they comforted; therefore they be led away as a flock, they shall be tormented, for a shepherd is not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:2; EZEKIEL 34:7–10; JEREMIAH 23:1–2"> On shepherds my strong vengeance is wroth, and on the bucks of goats I shall visit; for the Lord of hosts hath visited his flock, the house of Judah, and hath put them as an horse of his glory in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:10; ZECHARIAH 9:8; NUMBERS 24:17"> Of him shall be a cornerstone, and of him a little pale, of him a bow of battle, and of him each exactor, either unjust asker, shall go out together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HAGGAI 2:22; ZECHARIAH 9:13; PSALMS 20:7"> And they shall be as strong men, defouling clay of ways in battle, and they shall fight, for the Lord is with them; and riders of horses shall be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:9; ZECHARIAH 10:12; JEREMIAH 3:18"> And I shall comfort the house of Judah, and I shall save the house of Joseph; and I shall convert them, for I shall have mercy on them; and they shall be as they were, when I had not cast away them; for I shall be the Lord God of them, and I shall graciously hear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:15; ZECHARIAH 9:17; 1ST SAMUEL 2:1"> And they shall be as the strong men of Ephraim, and the heart of them shall be glad, as of wine; and the sons of them shall see, and be glad, and the heart of them shall make joy withoutforth in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:26; ISAIAH 7:18; JEREMIAH 33:22"> I shall hiss to them, and I shall gather them, for I again-bought them, and I shall multiply them, as they were multiplied before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:9; HOSEA 2:23; ESTHER 8:17"> And I shall sow them among peoples, and from far they shall bethink of me; and they shall live with their sons, and shall turn again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSEA 11:11; MICAH 7:14; ZECHARIAH 8:7"> And I shall bring again them from the land of Egypt, and I shall gather them from Assyrians; and I shall bring them to the land of Gilead and of Lebanon, and place shall not be found to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:13; MICAH 5:5–6; ISAIAH 43:2"> And he shall pass in the wave of the sea, and shall smite waves in the sea, and all depths of flood shall be confounded; and the pride of Assur shall be meeked, and the sceptre of Egypt shall go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; ISAIAH 2:5; ZECHARIAH 10:6"> I shall comfort them in the Lord, and they shall walk in the name of him, saith the Lord.</span>
