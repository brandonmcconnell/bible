<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 21:34–35; MATTHEW 24:37–39; LUKE 17:26–30"> For when they shall say peace is, and secureness, then sudden death &#91;or suddenly perishing&#93; shall come on them, as sorrow to a woman that is with child, and they shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:8; 2ND PETER 3:10; DEUTERONOMY 19:6"> But, brethren, ye be not in darknesses, that that day as a thief catch you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; JOHN 12:36"> For all ye be the sons of light, and sons of &#91;the&#93; day; we be not of night, neither of darknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:13; MATTHEW 24:42; LUKE 22:46"> Therefore sleep we not as others; but wake we, and be we sober.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 13:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:34; 2ND PETER 2:13"> For they that sleep, sleep in the night, and they that be drunken, be drunken in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 13:12–13; EPHESIANS 6:11; 1ST PETER 1:13"> But we that be of the day, be sober, clothed in the habergeon of faith and of charity, and in the helmet of hope of health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 1:10; 1ST PETER 2:10; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:10"> For God putted not us into wrath, but into the purchasing of health, by our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 14:8–9; 1ST PETER 3:18; ROMANS 8:34"> that was dead for us; that whether we wake, whether we sleep, we live together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:29; HEBREWS 10:25; ROMANS 15:2"> For which thing comfort ye together, and edify ye each other, as ye do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:17; HEBREWS 13:7; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:17–18"> And, brethren, we pray you, that ye know them that travail among you, and be sovereigns &#91;or be before&#93; to you in the Lord, and teach you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARK 9:50; HEBREWS 12:14; GALATIANS 6:6"> that ye have them more abun-dantly in charity; and for the work of them, have ye peace with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:2; ROMANS 14:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:35"> And, brethren, we pray you, reprove, &#91;or chastise&#93;, unpeaceable &#91;or unquiet&#93; men. Comfort ye men of little heart, receive ye sick men, be ye patient to all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:9; GALATIANS 6:10; LEVITICUS 19:18"> See ye, that no man yield evil for evil to any man; but evermore pursue ye that that is good, each to other, and to all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 12:12; PHILIPPIANS 4:4; MATTHEW 5:12"> Evermore joy ye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 12:12; COLOSSIANS 4:2; EPHESIANS 6:18"> without ceasing pray ye;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:20; PHILIPPIANS 4:6; COLOSSIANS 3:17"> in all things do ye thankings. For this is the will of God in Christ Jesus, in all you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:6"> Do not ye quench the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:3–6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:1; NUMBERS 11:25–29"> do not ye despise prophecies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:1; ROMANS 12:2; EPHESIANS 5:10"> But prove ye all things, and hold ye that thing that is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:3; PHILIPPIANS 4:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:31–33"> Abstain &#91;ye&#93; you from all evil species, &#91;or likeness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JUDE 1:24; HEBREWS 4:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:8–9"> And God himself of peace make you holy by all things, that your spirit be kept whole, and soul, and body, without complaint, in the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:3; NUMBERS 23:19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:9"> God is true, that called you, which also shall do &#91;work of grace in you&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:18–19; PHILIPPIANS 1:19; PHILEMON 1:22"> Brethren, pray ye for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:20"> Greet ye well all brethren in holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:13; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:3"> I charge you by the Lord, that this epistle be read to all holy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ROMANS 16:20; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:18; ROMANS 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.</span>
