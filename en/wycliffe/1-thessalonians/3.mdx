<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:22; ROMANS 5:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:16"> that no man be moved in these tribulations. For &#91;ye&#93; yourselves know, that in this thing we be set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:1–2; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:2"> For when we were at you, we before-said to you, that we should suffer tribulations; as it is done, and ye know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 1:13–14; MATTHEW 4:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:11"> Therefore I, Paul, no longer abiding, sent to know your faith, lest peradventure he that tempteth &#91;shall&#93; tempt you, and your travail &#91;or our travail&#93; be made vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:2; ISAIAH 52:7; PROVERBS 25:25"> But now, when Timothy shall come to us from you, and tell to us your faith and charity, and that ye have good mind of us, ever&#91;more&#93; desiring to see us, as we also you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 1:4; 2ND JOHN 1:4; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:8–9"> therefore, brethren, we be comforted in you, in all our need and tribulation, by your faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:11"> For now we live, if ye stand in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 71:14–15; PSALMS 96:12–13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:15"> For what doing of thankings may we yield to God for you, in all joy, in which we joy for you before our Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:9; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:11; PHILIPPIANS 1:25"> night and day more plenteously praying, that we see your face, and fulfill those things that fail to &#91;or of&#93; your faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5–6; MATTHEW 6:32; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:16"> But God himself and our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ, address our way to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:9; 1ST JOHN 4:7–16; MATTHEW 22:39"> And the Lord multiply you, and make your charity to be plenteous of each to other &#91;or for to abound together&#93;, and into all men, as also we in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:22; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:23; EPHESIANS 5:27"> that your hearts be confirmed without complaint in holiness, before God and our Father, in the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ with all his saints. Amen.</span>
