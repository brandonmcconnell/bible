<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:1–2; LUKE 23:1–5; LUKE 22:66"> And anon in the morrowtide the high priests made a counsel with the elder men, and the scribes, and with all the council, and bound Jesus and led, and betook him to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:2; MATTHEW 27:11–14; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:13"> And Pilate asked him, Art thou king of Jews? And Jesus answered, and said to him, Thou sayest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:7; MARK 15:5; JOHN 18:29–31"> And the high priests accused him in many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 19:10; MATTHEW 27:13; MATTHEW 26:62"> But Pilate again asked him, and said, Answerest thou nothing? Seest thou in how many things they accuse thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:7; ZECHARIAH 3:8; PSALMS 71:7"> But Jesus answered no&#91;thing&#93; more, so that Pilate wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 18:39–40; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:27"> But by the feast day he was wont to let go to them, one of the men bound &#91;or one of the prisoners&#93;, whomever they asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 23:18–19; MATTHEW 27:16; LUKE 23:25"> And there was one that was said Barabbas, that was bound with men of dissension, that had done man-slaughter in &#91;the&#93; sedition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when the people was gone up, he began to pray, as he evermore did to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 19:14–16; JOHN 19:4–5; JOHN 18:39"> And Pilate answered to them, and said, Will ye that I let go to you the king of Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:4; TITUS 3:3; GENESIS 4:4–6"> For he knew, that the high priests had taken him by envy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:14; JOHN 18:40; HOSEA 5:1"> But the bishops stirred &#91;or excited&#93; the people, that he should rather let go to them Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:5–6; ISAIAH 9:6–7; ZECHARIAH 9:9"> And again Pilate answered, and said to them, What then will ye that I shall do to the king of Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they again cried, Crucify him, &#91;that is, put him on the cross&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 23:41; LUKE 23:23–24; ISAIAH 53:3"> But Pilate said to them, What evil hath he done? And they cried the more, Crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 19:1; MATTHEW 27:26; ISAIAH 50:6"> And Pilate, willing to make satisfaction to the people, let go to them Barabbas, and he betook to them Jesus, beaten with scourges, to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 19:9; MATTHEW 27:27–31; JOHN 18:28"> And knights led him withinforth, into the porch of the moot hall. And they called together all the company of knights,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:28–30; JOHN 19:2–5; LUKE 23:11"> and they clothed him with purple. And they wreathed a crown of thorns, and put on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:42–43; MARK 15:2; GENESIS 37:10"> And they began to greet him, and said, Hail, king of Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 14:65; ISAIAH 53:3–5; LUKE 23:11"> And they smote his head with a reed, and bespat him &#91;or spat on him&#93;; and they kneeled, and worship-ped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 19:16; MATTHEW 27:31"> And after that they had scorned him, they unclothed him of the purple, and clothed him with his clothes, and led out him, to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:32; LUKE 23:26; ROMANS 16:13"> And they compelled a man that passed by the way, that came from the town, Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and of Rufus, to bear his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:33–44; JOHN 19:17–27; LUKE 23:27–38"> And they led him into a place Golgotha &#91;or the place of Golgotha&#93;, that is to say, the place of Calvary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:34; JOHN 19:28–30; LUKE 23:36"> And they gave to him to drink wine meddled with myrrh, and he took not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 23:34; JOHN 19:23–24; MATTHEW 27:35–36"> And they crucified him, and parted his clothes, and cast lot on those, who should take what.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 19:14; MARK 15:33; MATTHEW 27:45"> And it was the third hour, and they crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:37; MATTHEW 2:2; JOHN 19:18–22"> And the title of his cause was written, King of Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 23:32–33; MATTHEW 27:38; JOHN 19:18"> And they crucified with him two thieves, one at the right half, and one at his left half, &#91;or one on his right half, and one on his left half&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the scripture was fulfilled that saith, And he is ordained &#91;or areckoned&#93; with wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARK 14:58; PSALMS 109:25; LAMENTATIONS 1:12"> And as they passed forth, they blasphemed him, moving their heads, and saying, Vath! &#91;or Fie!&#93; thou that destroyest the temple of God, and in three days buildest it again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> come adown from the cross, and make thyself safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 12:23–24; MATTHEW 27:41–43; LUKE 23:35–37"> Also the high priests scorned him each to other, with the scribes, and said, He hath made other men safe, he may not save himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:44; LUKE 23:39–43; ZECHARIAH 9:9"> Christ, &#91;the&#93; king of Israel, come down now from the cross, that we see, and believe. And they that were crucified with him, despised him &#91;or put false reproof to him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:45; LUKE 23:44–45; AMOS 8:9–10"> And when the sixth hour was come, darknesses were made on all the earth, till into the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1; MATTHEW 27:46; HEBREWS 5:7"> And in the ninth hour, Jesus cried with a great voice, and said, Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani, that is to say, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:47–49; MARK 9:11–13; MATTHEW 17:11–13"> And some of the men that stood about heard, and said, Lo! he calleth Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; MARK 15:23; JOHN 19:28–30"> And one ran, and filled a sponge with vinegar, and putted about to a reed, and gave him to drink, and said, Suffer ye, see we, if Elijah come to do him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 19:30; LUKE 23:46; MATTHEW 27:50"> And Jesus gave out a great cry, and died &#91;or sent out the spirit&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:19–23; LUKE 23:45; HEBREWS 9:3–12"> And the veil of the temple was rent &#91;or cut&#93; atwo, from the highest to beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:54; LUKE 23:47–48; MATTHEW 27:43"> But the centurion that stood over against saw, that he so crying had died, and he said, Verily, this man was God’s Son &#91;or this man was the Son of God&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 23:49; LUKE 8:2–3; MATTHEW 27:55–56"> And there were also women beholding from afar, among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary, the mother of James the less, and of Joseph, and of Salome &#91;or and Salome&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 8:2–3; MATTHEW 27:55–56"> And when Jesus was in Galilee, they followed him, and ministered to him, and many other women, that came up together with him to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 23:50–56; JOHN 19:38–42; JOHN 19:31"> And when the eventide was come, for it was the eventide that is before the sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 2:38; LUKE 2:25; LUKE 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathaea, the noble decurion, came, and he abode the realm of God; and boldly he entered to Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="JOHN 19:31–37"> But Pilate wondered, if he were now dead. And when the centurion was called, he asked him, if he were &#91;now&#93; dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:58; JOHN 19:38; MARK 15:39"> and when he knew of the centurion, he granted &#91;or gave&#93; the body of Jesus to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:9; MATTHEW 27:59–60; ISAIAH 22:16"> And Joseph bought linen cloth &#91;or sendal&#93;, and took him down, and wrapped &#91;him&#93; in the linen cloth, and laid him in a sepulchre that was hewn of a stone &#91;or put him in a new sepulchre that was hewn in a stone&#93;, and wallowed a stone to the door of the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARK 15:40; MARK 16:1; LUKE 23:55–2"> And Mary Magdalene and Mary of Joseph beheld, where he was laid.</span>
