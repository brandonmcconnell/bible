<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:1–51; EZEKIEL 11:22–23; EZEKIEL 10:19"> And when he went out of the temple, one of his disciples said to him, Master, behold, what manner stones, and what manner buildings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 21:6; MATTHEW 24:2; 1ST KINGS 9:7–8"> And Jesus answered, and said to him, Seest thou all these great buildings? there shall not be left a stone on a stone, which shall not be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:1; MARK 4:34; MARK 14:33"> And when he sat in the mount of Olives against the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him by themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:6–7; MATTHEW 24:3; DANIEL 12:6"> Say thou to us, when these things shall be done, and what token &#91;or sign&#93; shall be, when all these things shall begin to be ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:8; EPHESIANS 5:6; JEREMIAH 29:8"> And Jesus answered, and began to say to them, Look &#91;or See&#93; ye, that no man deceive you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 13:22; JEREMIAH 23:21–25; JOHN 8:24"> for many shall come in my name, saying, That I am; and they shall deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 21:9–11; JOHN 14:27; MATTHEW 24:6–7"> And when ye hear battles and opinions of battles, dread ye not; for it behooveth these things to be done, but not yet anon is the end, &#91;or for it behooveth that these things be done, but the end is not yet&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:8; ISAIAH 37:3; PSALMS 48:6"> For folk shall rise on folk, and realm on realm, and earth-movings and hunger shall be by places; these things shall be beginnings of sorrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:17–18; JOHN 16:2; PHILIPPIANS 1:29"> But see ye yourselves, for they shall take you in councils, and ye shall be beaten in synagogues; and ye shall stand before kings and dooms-men for me, in witnessing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:14; COLOSSIANS 1:23; ROMANS 10:18"> And it behooveth, that the gospel be first preached among all folk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 21:14–15; LUKE 12:11–12; MATTHEW 10:19–21"> And when they take you, and lead you forth, do not ye before-think what ye shall speak, but speak ye that thing that shall be given to you in that hour; for ye be not the speakers &#91;or soothly ye be not speaking&#93;, but the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:21; LUKE 12:52–53; LUKE 21:16"> For the brother shall betake the brother into death &#91;or a brother shall betray a brother into death&#93;, and the father the son, and sons shall rise together against fathers and mothers, and punish them by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 15:18–19; MATTHEW 10:22; MATTHEW 24:13"> And ye shall be in hate &#91;or in hatred&#93; to all men for my name; but he that lasteth &#91;or shall sustain&#93; into the end, shall be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 9:27; DANIEL 12:11; DANIEL 11:31"> But when ye shall see the abomination of discomfort, standing where it oweth not; he that readeth, understand; then they that be in Judea, flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 17:31–33; HEBREWS 11:7; PHILIPPIANS 3:7–8"> And he that is above in the roof, come not down into the house, neither enter he, to take anything of his house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and he that shall be in the field, turn not again behind to take his cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:10; LUKE 23:29; HOSEA 9:14"> But woe to them that be with child, and nourishing &#91;or nursing&#93; in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Therefore pray ye, that those things, &#91;or that your fleeing&#93;, be not done in winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 12:1; JOEL 2:2; MATTHEW 24:21"> But those days of tribulation shall be such, which manner were not from the beginning of creature, which God hath made, till now, neither shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:22; ZECHARIAH 13:8–9; ISAIAH 65:8–9"> And but the Lord had abridged those days, all flesh, &#91;or mankind&#93;, had not be safe; but for the chosen which he chose, the Lord hath made short the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 21:8; MATTHEW 24:23–25; MATTHEW 24:5"> And then if any man say to you, Lo! here is Christ, lo! there, believe ye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:24; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:8–14; MARK 13:6"> For false Christs and false prophets shall rise, and shall give tokens &#91;or signs&#93; and wonders, to deceive, if it may be done, yea, them that be chosen, &#91;or yea, the chosen&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:17; MARK 13:5; JOHN 14:29"> Therefore take ye keep; lo! I have before-said to you all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 13:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:19–20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:12–14"> But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun shall be made dark, and the moon shall not give her light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 34:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:13"> and the stars of heaven shall fall down, and the virtues that be in heavens shall be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:7; MATTHEW 16:27; MATTHEW 24:30"> And then they shall see man’s Son coming in the clouds of heaven, with great virtue and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:6; MATTHEW 24:31; DEUTERONOMY 30:4"> And then he shall send his angels, and shall gather his chosen from the four winds, from the highest &#91;or the lowest&#93; thing of earth till to &#91;or unto&#93; the highest thing of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 21:29–31; MATTHEW 24:32–33"> But of the fig tree learn ye the parable. When now his branch is tender, and leaves be sprung out, ye know that summer is nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:17–18; JAMES 5:9; EZEKIEL 12:25–28"> So when ye see these things be done, know ye, that it is nigh in the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:34; LUKE 21:32; MATTHEW 16:28"> Truly I say to you, that this generation shall not pass away, till all these things be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:8; ISAIAH 51:6; MATTHEW 24:35"> Heaven and earth shall pass, but my words shall not pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:7; MATTHEW 24:36–42; MATTHEW 25:13"> But of that day or hour no man knoweth, neither angels in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:18; LUKE 12:40; ROMANS 13:11–12"> See ye, wake ye, and pray ye; for ye know not, when the time is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:14–30; JOHN 10:3; LUKE 12:36–40"> For as a man that is gone far in pilgrimage, left his house, and gave to his servants power of every work, and commanded to the porter, that he &#91;should&#93; wake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MARK 13:33; MATTHEW 24:42; MATTHEW 24:44"> Therefore wake ye, for ye know not, when the lord of the house cometh, in the eventide, or at midnight, or at cock’s crowing, or in the morning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:6–7; ROMANS 13:11–14; MATTHEW 24:48–51"> lest when he come suddenly, he find you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MARK 13:33; LUKE 12:41–46; MARK 13:35"> Forsooth that that I say to you, I say to all, Wake ye.</span>
