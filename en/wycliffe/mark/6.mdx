<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 4:16–30; MATTHEW 13:54–58; MATTHEW 2:23"> And he went out from thence, and went into his own country; and his disciples followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 7:15; JOHN 6:42; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:13–14"> And when the sabbath was come, Jesus began to teach in a synagogue. And many heard him, and wondered in his teaching, and said, Of whence cometh to this &#91;man&#93; all these things? and what is the wisdom that is given to him, and such virtues, which be made by his hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:46; MATTHEW 13:55–57; JOHN 6:42"> Whether this is not a carpenter, the son of Mary, the brother of James and of Joseph and of Judas and of Simon? whether his sisters be not here with us? And they were offended in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 4:44; LUKE 4:24; MATTHEW 13:57"> And Jesus said to them, That a prophet is not without honour, but in his own country, and among his kin, and in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:58; MARK 9:23; MARK 5:23"> And he might not do there any virtue, save that he healed a few sick men, laying on them his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:35; MATTHEW 8:10; LUKE 13:22"> And he wondered for the unbelief of them. And he went about castles on each side, and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:9–10; LUKE 10:17–20; MATTHEW 10:1–5"> And he called together twelve, and began to send them by two together; and gave to them power of unclean spirits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 9:3; MATTHEW 10:9–10; LUKE 10:4"> and commanded them, that they should not take anything in the way, but a staff &#91;or a rod&#93; only, not a scrip, nor bread, neither money in the girdle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:8; EPHESIANS 6:15"> but shod with sandals, and that they should not be clothed with two coats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 10:7–8; LUKE 9:4; MATTHEW 10:11–13"> And he said to them, Whither ever ye enter into an house, dwell ye there, till ye go out from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:14–15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:6; LUKE 10:10–15"> And whoever receive you not, nor hear you, go ye out from thence, and shake away the powder from your feet, into witnessing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:17; LUKE 13:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:21"> And they went forth, and preached, that men should do penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 5:14–15; MARK 6:7; LUKE 10:17"> And they casted out many fiends, and anointed with oil many sick men, and they were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 9:7–9; MARK 6:14–29; LUKE 23:7–12"> And king Herod heard, for his name was made open, and he said, That John Baptist hath risen again from death, and therefore virtues work in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:14; MALACHI 4:5; MATTHEW 21:11"> Others said, That it is Elijah; but others said, That it is a prophet, as one of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:10–13; MATTHEW 27:4; LUKE 9:9"> And when this thing was heard, Herod said, This is John, whom I have beheaded, he is risen again from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 3:19–20; MATTHEW 11:2; LUKE 3:1"> For that Herod sent, and held John, and bound him into prison, for Herodias, the wife of Philip, his brother; for he had wedded her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:21; LEVITICUS 18:16; 1ST KINGS 22:14"> For John said to Herod, It is not leaveful to thee, to have the wife of thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:9; EPHESIANS 4:26–27; GENESIS 39:17–20"> And Herodias laid ambush to him, and would slay him, and might not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:26; MATTHEW 14:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:15–22"> And Herod dreaded John, and knew him a just man and holy, and kept him. And Herod heard him, and he did many things, and gladly heard him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ESTHER 2:18; GENESIS 40:20; 1ST PETER 4:3"> And when a covenable day was fallen, Herod in his birthday made a supper to the princes, and tribunes, and to the greatest of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ESTHER 1:10–12; MATTHEW 14:6; DANIEL 5:2"> And when the daughter of that Herodias was come in, and danced, and pleased to Herod &#91;or and pleased Herod&#93;, and also to men that sat at the meat, the king said to the damsel, Ask thou of me what thou wilt, and I shall give to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ESTHER 7:2; ESTHER 5:3; ESTHER 5:6"> And he swore to her, That what-ever thou ask, I shall give to thee, though it be half of my kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOB 31:31; EZEKIEL 19:2–3; GENESIS 27:8–11"> And when she had gone out, she said to her mother, What shall I ask? And she said, The head of John &#91;the&#93; Baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 3:15; NUMBERS 7:19–89; NUMBERS 7:13"> And when she was come in anon with haste to the king, she asked, and said, I will that anon, thou give to me in a dish the head of John Baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:24–25; MATTHEW 14:9; MATTHEW 27:3–5"> And the king was sorry &#91;or sorrowful&#93; for the oath, and for men that sat together at the meat, he would not make her sorry &#91;or heavy&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:10–11"> but he sent a man-queller and commanded, that John’s head were brought in a dish. And he beheaded him in the prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and brought his head in a dish, and gave it to the damsel, and the damsel gave it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:16; MATTHEW 27:57–60"> And when this thing was heard, his disciples came, and took his body, and laid it in a burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 9:10; LUKE 10:17; MATTHEW 10:2"> And the apostles came together to Jesus, and told to him all things, that they had done, and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARK 3:20; JOHN 6:1; MARK 1:45"> And he said to them, Come ye by yourselves into a desert place; and rest ye a little. For there were many that came, and went again, and they had not space &#91;for&#93; to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 8:2–9; MARK 6:45; MARK 4:36"> And they went into a boat, and went into a desert place by them-selves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 6:2; MATTHEW 15:29–31; MARK 6:54–55"> And they saw them go away, and many knew, and they went afoot from all cities, and ran &#91;together&#93; thither, and came before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:36; LUKE 9:11; NUMBERS 27:17"> And Jesus went out, and saw much people, and had ruth &#91;or mercy&#93; on them, for they were as sheep not having a shepherd. And he began to teach them many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 6:5–15; LUKE 9:12–17; MATTHEW 14:15–21"> And when it was late in the day, his disciples came to him, and said, This is a desert place, and the time is now passed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:23; MARK 3:21; MARK 5:31"> let them go into the next towns and villages, to buy them meat to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2ND KI 4:42–44; MATTHEW 15:32–33; MARK 8:2–3"> And he answered, and said to them, Give ye to them for to eat. And they said to him, Go we, and buy we loaves with two hundred pence, and we shall give to them for to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:34; MARK 8:5; JOHN 6:9"> And he saith to them, How many loaves have ye? Go ye, and see. And when they had known, they say, Five, and two fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:40; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:33; MATTHEW 15:35"> And he commanded to them, that they should make all men sit to meat by companies, on &#91;the&#93; green hay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 9:14–15"> And they sat down by parts &#91;or by parties&#93;, by hundreds, and by fifties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:19; MARK 7:34; LUKE 9:16"> And when he had taken the five loaves, and two fishes, he beheld into heaven, and blessed, and brake &#91;the&#93; loaves, and gave to his disciples, that they should set before them. And he parted two fishes to all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 9:17; MARK 8:8–9; MATTHEW 15:37–38"> and all ate, and were full-filled &#91;or fulfilled&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MARK 8:19–20"> And they took the remnants of broken meats, twelve coffins full, and of the fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they that ate, were five thousand of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:22–33; JOHN 6:15–21; MARK 6:32"> And anon he made his disciples to go up into a boat, to pass before him over the sea to Bethsaida, while he let the people go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MARK 1:35; MATTHEW 6:6; LUKE 6:12"> And when he had left them, he went into an high hill, to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:23; JOHN 6:16–17"> And when it was even, the boat was in the middle &#91;or in the midst&#93; of the sea, and he alone in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKE 24:28; LUKE 12:38; JOB 9:8"> and he saw them travailing in rowing; for the wind was contrary to them. And about the fourth waking of the night, he wandering on the sea, came to them, and would pass them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:25–26; LUKE 24:37; JOB 9:8"> And as they saw him wandering on the sea, they guessed that it were a phantom, and cried out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:27; JOHN 6:19–20; ISAIAH 43:2"> for all saw him, and they were afraid, &#91;or they were troubled, or disturbed&#93;. And anon he spake with them, and said to them, Trust ye, I am; do not ye dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PSALMS 93:3–4; MARK 4:39; JOHN 6:21"> And he came up to them into the boat, and the wind ceased. And they wondered more within themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MARK 8:17–21; LUKE 24:25; ISAIAH 63:17"> for they understood not of the loaves; for their heart was blinded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:34–36; JOHN 6:24–25; LUKE 5:1"> And when they were passed over the sea, they came into the land of Gennesaret, and setted to land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="PSALMS 9:10; PHILIPPIANS 3:10"> And when they were gone out of the boat, anon they knew him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:24; MARK 2:1–3; MARK 3:7–11"> And they ran through all that country, and began to bring sick men in beds on each side, where they heard that he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:20; LUKE 6:19; NUMBERS 15:38–39"> And whither ever he entered into villages, or into towns, or into cities, they set sick men in streets, and prayed him, that they should touch namely the hem of his cloth; and how many that touched him, were made safe.</span>
