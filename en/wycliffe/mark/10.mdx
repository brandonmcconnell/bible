<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 10:40; MARK 4:2; JOHN 11:7"> And Jesus rose up from thence, and came into the coasts of Judea over Jordan; and again the people came together to him, and as he was wont, again he taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:10–11; LUKE 11:53–54; JOHN 8:6"> And the Pharisees came &#91;nigh&#93;, and asked him, Whether it be leaveful to a man to leave &#91;or to forsake&#93; his wife? and they tempted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:20; GALATIANS 4:21; LUKE 10:25"> And he answered, and said to them, What commanded Moses to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:1–4; MATTHEW 5:31–32; ISAIAH 50:1"> And they said, Moses suffered to write a libel of forsaking, and to forsake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:8; NEHEMIAH 9:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51"> And Jesus answered, and said to them, For the hardness of your heart Moses wrote to you this command-ment &#91;or this precept&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 1:27; GENESIS 5:2; GENESIS 1:1"> But from the beginning of creature, God made them male and female;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 2:24; MATTHEW 19:5–6; EPHESIANS 5:31"> and said, For this thing a man shall leave his father and mother, and shall draw &#91;or cleave&#93; to his wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:28; GENESIS 2:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:16"> and they shall be twain in one flesh. And so now they be not twain, but one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 7:1–3; 1ST CHRONICLES 7:10–13"> Therefore that thing that God hath joined together, no man separate &#91;or part&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARK 9:33; MARK 9:28; MARK 4:10"> And again in the house his disciples asked him of the same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:31–32; MATTHEW 19:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:10–11"> And he said to them, Whoever leaveth his wife, and weddeth another, he doeth adultery on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:13"> And if the wife leave her husband, and be wedded to another, she doeth lechery &#91;or doeth adultery&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:13–15; LUKE 18:15–17; EXODUS 10:9–11"> And they brought to him little children, that he should touch them; and the disciples threatened the men, that brought them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:14; MATTHEW 18:10; MATTHEW 18:4"> And when Jesus had seen them, he bare heavy, and said to them, Suffer ye little children to come to me, and forbid ye them not, for of such is the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:3; LUKE 18:17; JOHN 3:3–6"> Truly I say to you, whoever receiveth not the kingdom of God as a little child, he shall not enter into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARK 9:36; ISAIAH 40:11; JOHN 21:15–17"> And he embraced them, and laid his hands on them, and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:16–30; LUKE 18:18–30; ROMANS 2:7"> And when Jesus was gone out into the way, a man ran before, and kneeled before him, and prayed him, and said, Good master, what shall I do, that I receive everlasting life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 18:19; MATTHEW 19:17; ROMANS 3:12"> And Jesus said to him, What sayest thou that I am good? There is no man good, but God himself, &#91;or None is good, no but God alone&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12–17; ROMANS 13:9; JAMES 2:11"> Thou knowest the commandments, do thou none adultery, slay not, steal not, say not false witnessing, do no fraud, worship thy father and thy mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:31–32; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:5; PHILIPPIANS 3:6"> And he answered, and said to him, Master, I have kept all these things from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:19–21; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:4; LUKE 18:22"> And Jesus beheld him, and loved him, and said to him, One thing faileth to thee; go thou, and sell all things that thou hast, and give to poor men, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven; and come, pursue thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:15–16; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:9–10; LUKE 12:15"> And he was full sorry &#91;or made sorrowful&#93; in the word, and went away mourning, for he had many possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:26; LUKE 18:24; MARK 3:5"> And Jesus beheld about, and said to his disciples, How hard they that have riches &#91;or money&#93; shall enter into the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JAMES 5:1–3; PROVERBS 11:28; PSALMS 52:7"> And the disciples were astonied in his words. And Jesus again answered, and said to them, Ye little children &#91;or Little sons&#93;, how hard it is for men that trust in riches to enter into the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:24–25; LUKE 18:25; MATTHEW 23:24"> It is lighter a camel to pass through a needle’s eye &#91;or It is easier that a camel pass through a needle’s eye&#93;, than a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:31; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:23; ROMANS 10:9–13"> And they wondered more, and said among themselves, And who may be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:26; JEREMIAH 32:27; JOB 42:2"> And Jesus beheld them, and said, With men it is impossible, but not with God; for all things be possible with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:27–30; LUKE 18:28–30; LUKE 14:33"> And Peter began to say to him, Lo! we have left all things, and have pursued thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:23; MATTHEW 5:10–11; MARK 8:35"> Jesus answered, and said, Truly I say to you, there is no man that leaveth house, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or children, or fields for me and for the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 18:30; PHILIPPIANS 3:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:10"> which shall not take an hundredfold so much now in this time, houses, and brethren, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and fields, with persecutions, and in the world to coming &#91;or to come&#93; everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:30; LUKE 13:30; MATTHEW 20:16"> But many shall be, the first the last, and the last the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:17–19; LUKE 18:31–34; LUKE 9:51"> And they were in the way going up to Jerusalem; and Jesus went before them, and they wondered, and followed, and dreaded. And again Jesus took the twelve, and began to say to them, what things were to come to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:2; MATTHEW 20:17–19; MARK 8:31"> For lo! we go up to Jerusalem, and man’s Son shall be betrayed to the princes of priests, and to &#91;the&#93; scribes, and to the elder men; and they shall condemn him by death, and they shall &#91;be&#93; take him to heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:67; MATTHEW 16:21; MARK 14:65"> And they shall scorn him, and bespit him, and beat him; and they shall slay him, and in the third day he shall rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:20–28; 2ND SAMUEL 14:4–11; 1ST KINGS 2:20"> And James and John, Zebedee’s sons, came to him, and said, Master, we will, that whatever we ask, thou do to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 15:7; 1ST KINGS 3:5–15; MARK 10:51"> And he said to them, What will ye that I do to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:28; MARK 16:19; LUKE 24:26"> And they said, Grant to us, that we sit the one at thy right half, and the other at thy left half, in thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKE 12:50; MATTHEW 20:21–22; PSALMS 75:8"> And Jesus said to them, Ye know not what ye ask; may ye drink the cup, which I shall drink, or be washed with the baptism, in which I am baptized?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:9; JOHN 17:14"> And they said to him, We may. And Jesus said to them, &#91;Truly&#93; Ye shall drink the cup that I drink, and ye shall be washed with the baptism, in which I am baptized;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:23; MATTHEW 25:34; JOHN 17:24"> but to sit at my right half or left half is not mine to give to you, but to whom it is made ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:24; PROVERBS 13:10; ROMANS 12:10"> And the ten heard, and began to have indignation of James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:3; LUKE 22:25; MATTHEW 20:25"> But Jesus called them, and said to them, Ye know, that they that seem &#91;or be seen&#93; to have princehood of folks, be lords of them, and the princes of them have power of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:8–12; LUKE 14:11; MARK 9:35"> But it is not so among you, but whoever will be made &#91;the&#93; greater, shall be your minister;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and whoever will be the first among you, shall be servant of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:28; PHILIPPIANS 2:5–8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:9"> For why man’s Son came not, that it should be ministered to him, but that he should minister, and give his life again-buying &#91;or as a redemp-tion&#93; for many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 18:35–1; MATTHEW 20:29–34; JOHN 9:8"> And they came to Jericho; and when he went forth from Jericho, and his disciples, and a full much people, Bartimaeus, a blind man, the son of Timaeus, sat beside the way, and begged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARK 1:24; MATTHEW 9:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:16"> And when he heard, that it was Jesus of Nazareth, he began to cry, and say, Jesus, the son of David, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:13; MARK 7:26–29; HEBREWS 5:7"> And many threatened him, that he should be still; and he cried much the more &#91;or he cried much more, saying&#93;, Jesus, the son of David, have mercy on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LUKE 18:40; MATTHEW 20:32–34; JOHN 11:28"> And Jesus stood, and commanded him to be called; and they called the blind man, and said to him, Be thou of better heart, rise up, he calleth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:7–9; HEBREWS 12:1"> And he cast away his cloth, and skipped, and came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LUKE 18:41–43; MARK 10:36; MATTHEW 7:7"> And Jesus answered, and said to him, What wilt thou, that I shall do to thee? The blind man said to him, Master, that I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; MARK 8:25"> Jesus said to him, Go thou, thy faith hath made thee safe. And anon he saw, and pursued him in the way.</span>
