<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 4:47; LUKE 5:18; LUKE 18:35–38"> And again he entered into Capernaum, after eight days. And it was heard, that he was in an house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARK 2:13; MARK 1:45; PSALMS 40:9"> and many came together, so that they might not be in the house, nor at the gate. And he spake to them the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 5:18–26; MATTHEW 9:1–8; MATTHEW 4:24"> And there came to him men that brought a man sick in the palsy, which was borne of four. &#91;And there came to him men bringing or bearing a sick man in palsy, the which was borne of four men.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 5:19; DEUTERONOMY 22:8"> And when they might not bring him to Jesus for the people, they uncovered the roof where he was, and opened it, and they let down the bed in which the sick man in palsy lay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:2; JAMES 5:15; MARK 2:9–10"> And when Jesus had seen the faith of them, he said to the sick man in palsy, Son, thy sins be forgiven to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 5:21–22; MARK 8:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:5"> But there were some of the scribes sitting, and thinking in their hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:25; LUKE 5:21; MICAH 7:18"> What speaketh he thus? He blasphemeth; who may forgive sins, but God alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 139:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:22; LUKE 5:22"> And &#91;anon&#93; when Jesus had known this by the Holy Ghost, that they thought so within themselves, he saith to them, What think ye these things in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 5:22–25; MATTHEW 9:5"> What is lighter to say to the sick man in palsy, Sins be forgiven to thee, or to say, Rise, take thy bed, and walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13–14; MATTHEW 9:6–8; JOHN 5:20–27"> But that ye know that man’s Son hath power in earth to forgive sins, he said to the sick man in palsy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARK 1:41; JOHN 5:8–10; JOHN 6:63"> I say to thee, rise up, take thy bed, and go into thine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:33; MATTHEW 9:8; LUKE 7:16"> And anon he rose up, and when he had taken the bed, he went before all men, so that all men wondered, and honoured God, and said, For we saw never so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARK 4:1; PROVERBS 1:20–22; LUKE 19:48"> And he went out again to the sea, and all the people came to him; and he taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 5:27–32; LUKE 6:15; MATTHEW 9:9–13"> And when he passed, he saw Levi of Alphaeus sitting at the tollbooth, and he said to him, Pursue me. And he rose, and pursued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 15:1; LUKE 5:29–30; MATTHEW 9:10–11"> And it was done, when he sat at the meat in his house, many publicans and sinful men sat together at the meat with Jesus and his disciples; for there were many that followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; LUKE 19:7; LUKE 18:11"> And scribes and Pharisees seeing, that he ate with publicans and sinful men, said to his disciples, Why eateth and drinketh your master with publicans and sinners?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:12–13; LUKE 15:7; LUKE 5:31–32"> When this was heard, Jesus said to them, Whole men have no need to a leech, but they that be evil-at-ease &#91;or they that have evil&#93;; for I came not to call just men, but sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 18:12; MATTHEW 6:18; MATTHEW 9:14–17"> And the disciples of John and the Pharisees were fasting; and they came, and said to him, Why fast the disciples of John, and the Pharisees fast, but thy disciples fast not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:1–10; GENESIS 29:22; JUDGES 14:10–11"> And Jesus said to them, Whether the sons of the spousals &#91;or of the weddings&#93; may fast, as long as the spouse is with them? As long time as they have the spouse with them, they may not fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 17:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:9; ISAIAH 54:5"> But days shall come, when the spouse shall be taken away from them, and then they shall fast in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:13; ISAIAH 57:16"> No man seweth a patch of new cloth to an old cloth, else he taketh away the new patch from the old, and a more breaking is made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:17; PSALMS 119:83; LUKE 5:37–38"> And no man putteth new wine into old bottles, else the wine shall burst the bottles, and the wine shall be shed out, and the bottles shall perish. But new wine shall be put into new bottles. &#91;And no man putteth new wine into old wine vessels, else the wine shall burst or shall break the wine vessels, and the wine shall be poured out, and the wine vessels shall perish. But new wine oweth to be put into new wine vessels.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:1–8; DEUTERONOMY 23:24–25; LUKE 6:1–5"> And it was done again, when the Lord walked in the sabbaths by the corns, and his disciples began to pass &#91;or to go&#93; forth, and &#91;to&#93; pluck ears of the corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 20:10; HEBREWS 12:3; MATTHEW 12:2"> And the Pharisees said to him, Lo! what thy disciples do in the sabbaths, that is not leaveful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 21:3–6; LUKE 10:26; MATTHEW 22:31"> And he said to them, Read ye never what David did, when he had need, and he hungered, and they that were with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:17; LEVITICUS 24:5–9; 1ST SAMUEL 21:1"> How he went into the house of God, under Abiathar, prince of priests, and ate loaves of proposition, &#91;either of setting forth&#93;, which it was not leave-ful to eat, but to priests alone, and he gave to them that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:16; LUKE 6:9; DEUTERONOMY 5:14"> And he said to them, The sabbath is made for man, and not man for the sabbath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:10; JOHN 5:17"> and so man’s Son is Lord also of the sabbath.</span>
