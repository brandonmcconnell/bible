<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NAHUM 1:1; ISAIAH 22:1"> The burden that Habakkuk, the prophet, saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1–2; JEREMIAH 14:9; PSALMS 13:1–2"> How long, Lord, shall I cry, and thou shalt not hear? I suffering violence shall cry on high to thee, and thou shalt not save?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 55:9–11; JEREMIAH 9:2–6; JEREMIAH 20:8"> Why showedest thou to me wickedness and travail, for to see prey and unrightwiseness against me? Why beholdest thou despisers, and art still, the while the unpious man defouleth a right-fuller than himself? And thou shalt make men as fishes of the sea, and as creeping things not having a leader; and doom is made, and against-saying is more mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 119:126; ISAIAH 1:21–23; JOB 21:7"> For this thing law is broken, and doom cometh not till to the end; for the unpious man hath might against the just, therefore wayward doom shall go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:40–41; LAMENTATIONS 4:12; ISAIAH 29:14"> Behold ye in heathen men, and see ye, and wonder ye, and greatly dread ye; for a work is done in your days, which no man shall believe, when it shall be told.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND KI 24:2; JEREMIAH 5:15; ISAIAH 23:13"> For lo! I shall raise Chaldees, a bitter folk and swift, going on the breadth of earth, that he wield tabernacles not his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:5–9; JEREMIAH 52:9–11; DEUTERONOMY 5:27"> It is horrible, and dreadful; the doom and the burden thereof shall go out of itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:13; ZEPHANIAH 3:3; HOSEA 8:1"> His horses be lighter than leopards, and swifter than eventide wolves, and his horsemen shall be scattered abroad; for why his horsemen shall come from far, they shall fly as an eagle hasting to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:9; HOSEA 13:15; HOSEA 1:10"> All these men shall come to or for prey, the faces of them is as a burning wind; and he shall gather as gravel the captivity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:6; JEREMIAH 32:24; ISAIAH 14:16"> and he shall have victory of kings, and tyrants shall be of his scorning. He shall laugh on all strength or stronghold, and shall bear together &#91;an&#93; heap of earth, and shall take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 5:20; DANIEL 4:30–34; JEREMIAH 4:11–12"> Then the spirit &#91;of him&#93; shall be changed, and he shall pass forth, and fall down; this is the strength of him, of his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; JEREMIAH 30:11; JEREMIAH 46:28"> Whether thou art not from the beginning, thou, Lord my God, mine Holy, and we shall not die? Lord, into doom thou hast set him, and thou groundedest him strong, that thou shouldest chastise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 5:4–5; PSALMS 34:15–16; JEREMIAH 12:1–2"> Thine eyes be clean, see thou not evil, and thou shalt not be able to behold to wickedness. Why beholdest thou not on men doing wickedly, and thou art still, while the unpious man devoureth a more just man than himself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:7"> And thou shalt make men as fishes of the sea, and as a creeping thing not having a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="AMOS 4:2; JEREMIAH 16:16; PSALMS 10:9"> He shall lift up all in the hook; he drew it in his great net, and gathered into his net; on this thing he shall be glad, and make joy withoutforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:11; DEUTERONOMY 8:17; DANIEL 4:30"> Therefore he shall offer to his great net, and shall make sacrifice to his net; for in them his part is made fat, and his meat is chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:5–8; EZEKIEL 25:1–17; JEREMIAH 25:9–26"> Therefore for this thing he spread-eth abroad his great net, and ever-more he ceaseth not for to slay folks.</span>
