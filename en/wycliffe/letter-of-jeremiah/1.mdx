<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Here beginneth a sample of the epistle of the same Baruch, which epistle Jeremy sent to the Jews, that were led away prisoners into Babylon, of the king of Babylonians, that he should tell to them, by that that was commanded of God to him. &#91;Here beginneth the ensample of the same epistle, which Jeremy sent to the captives led away into Babylon, of the king of men of Babylon, that he should show to them, after that it is commanded to him of God.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For the sins which ye sinned before God, ye shall be led prisoners &#91;or captives&#93; into Babylon, of Nebuchad-nezzar, king of Babylonians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Therefore ye shall enter &#91;or go&#93; into Babylon, and ye shall be there full many years, and into long time, till to seven generations; forsooth after this I shall lead out you from thence with peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But now ye shall see in Babylon gods of gold, and of silver, and of stone, and of tree, to be borne on shoulders, showing dread to heathen men &#91;or to folks&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Therefore see ye, lest also ye be made like &#91;to&#93; alien deeds, and lest ye dread, and &#91;that&#93; dread take you in them. Therefore when ye see a company behind and before, worship ye God, &#91;or And so the company of people seen from behind and from before worshipping&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> say in your hearts, Lord God, it behooveth that thou be worshipped. &#91;say in your hearts, Lord God, it behooveth thee for to be worshipped.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Forsooth mine angel is with you, but I shall seek &#91;out&#93; your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For why the trees of them be polished of a carpenter; also they be arrayed with gold, and arrayed with silver, and be false, and may not speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And as to a virgin &#91;or a maiden&#93; loving ornaments, so, when gold is taken, ornaments be made to idols. Certainly the gods of them have golden crowns on their heads;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> wherefore priests withdraw from those gods gold and silver, and spend it in themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Soothly they give also of that to whores, and array &#91;or adorn&#93; whores; and again when they receive that of whores, they array &#91;or adorn&#93; their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But those &#91;or these&#93;gods be not delivered from rust and moths. Forsooth when they be covered with a cloth of purple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> priests shall wipe the faces of them, for dust of the house, which is full much among those gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsooth idols have a sceptre, &#91;or king’s rod&#93;, as a man hath; as the judge of a country, that slayeth not a man sinning against himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Also they have in the hand a sword, and an ax; but they deliver not themselves from battle and from thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Wherefore be it known to you, that they be not gods; therefore worship ye not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For as a broken vessel of a man is made unprofitable, such also be the gods of them. When they be set in the house, the eyes of them be full of dust, of the feet of men entering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And as &#91;the&#93; gates be set about a man, that offended the king, either as when a dead man is brought to the sepulchre, so priests keep securely the doors with closings, and locks, lest they be robbed of thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They tend lanterns to them, and soothly many lanterns, of which they may see none;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> forsooth they be as beams in an house &#91;or in the house&#93;. Soothly men say that serpents, that be of earth, lick out the hearts of them; while the serpents eat them, and their clothes &#91;or clothing&#93;, and they feel it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The faces of them be black of the smoke, which is made in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Night crows &#91;or bats&#93; and swallows fly above the body of them, and above the head of them, and birds also, and cats in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Wherefore know ye, that they be not gods; therefore dread ye not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Also the gold which they have, is to fairness; no but some man wipe away the rust, they shall not shine. For they feeled not, the while those were welled together &#91;or they were molten together&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They be bought of all price, in which &#91;things&#93; no spirit is in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They without feet be borne on shoulders of men, and show openly their unnobility to men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> be they shamed also that worship them. Therefore if they fall down to earth, they shall not rise of them-selves; and if any man setteth that idol upright, it shall not stand by itself, but as to dead things &#91;or dead men&#93; shoulders shall be put to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The priests of them sell the sacrifices of them, and mis-use; in like manner and the women of them ravish &#91;or taking&#93; away, neither to a sick man, neither to a beggar, they give anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Of their sacrifices foul women, and in unclean blood &#91;or menstruate&#93;, touch. Therefore know ye by these things, that they be not gods, and dread ye not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For whereof be they called gods? For women set sacrifices to gods of silver, and of gold, and of tree;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and priests that have coats rent &#91;or torn&#93;, and heads and beards shaven, whose heads be naked, sit in the houses of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Soothly they roar and cry against their gods, as in the supper of a dead man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Priests take away the clothes of them, and clothe their wives, and their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And if they suffer anything of evil of any man, or if they suffer anything of good, they may not yield back. Neither they may ordain &#91;or make&#93; a king, neither do away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> In like manner they may neither give riches, neither yield evil &#91;thing&#93;. If any man maketh a vow to them, and yieldeth not, they ask &#91;or require&#93; not this &#91;thing&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> They deliver not a man from death, neither ravish a sick man from a mightier &#91;or neither deliver a sick man from a more mighty&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They restore not a blind man to sight; they shall not deliver a man from need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> They shall not have mercy on a widow, neither they shall do good to fatherless children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Their gods of tree, and of stone, and of gold, and of silver, be like stones of the mountain; forsooth they that worship them, shall be shamed &#91;or confounded&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> How therefore is it to guess, either to say, that they be gods? for why yet when Chaldeans honour not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Which when they hear that a dumb man may not speak, offer him to Bel, and ask of him to speak; as if they that have no stirring, may feel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they, when they shall under-stand, shall forsake those idols; for those gods of them have no wit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Forsooth women gird with ropes &#91;or cords&#93; sit in ways, and kindled bones of olives. Soothly when any of these women is drawn away of any man passing, and sleepeth with him, she despiseth her neighbouress, that she is not had worthy as herself, neither her rope &#91;or her cord&#93; is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Forsooth all things that be done to them, be false. How therefore is it to guess, either to say, that those be gods? &#91;or How is it to be guessed, or to be said, them to be gods?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Forsooth those idols be made of smiths &#91;or carpenters&#93;, and of gold-smiths. They shall be none other thing, no but that that priests will that they be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Also those goldsmiths that make them, be not of much time; therefore whether those things &#91;or the things&#93; that be made of them, may be gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Soothly they left false things, and shame to men to coming afterward &#91;or coming after.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> For why when battles and evils come on them, priests think, where they shall hide themselves with those &#91;or with them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> How therefore owe those &#91;or they&#93; to be deemed, that they be gods, which neither deliver themselves from battle, neither deliver themselves from evils?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> For why when those be of tree, and of stone, and of gold, and of silver, it shall be known afterward,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> of all folks, and kings, that those things be false, that be made open; &#91;or known&#93;, for those &#91;or they&#93; be not gods, but the works of hands of men &#91;or of men’s hands&#93;, and no work of God is with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Whereof therefore it is known, that they be not gods, but the works of hands of men &#91;or of men’s hands&#93;, and no work of God is in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> They raise not a king to a country, neither shall give rain to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Also they shall not deem, &#91;or judge&#93;, &#91;a&#93; doom, neither they shall deliver the country from wrong. For those may do nothing, as little crows betwixt the midst &#91;or the middle&#93; of heaven and of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> For when fire falleth into the house of gods of tree, and of gold, and of silver, soothly the priests of those &#91;or of them&#93; shall flee, and shall be delivered; but they shall be burnt as beams in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Forsooth they shall not against-stand a king and battle. How there-fore is it to guess, or to receive, &#91;or to be guessed, or to be received&#93;, that they be gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Gods of tree, and of stone, and of gold, and of silver, shall not deliver themselves from night thieves, neither from day thieves, and wicked men be stronger than those gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> They shall take away gold, and silver, and clothes, by which those &#91;or they&#93; be covered, and they shall go away; neither they help themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Therefore it is better to be a king showing his virtue, either a profitable vessel in the house, in which he shall have glory that wieldeth it, than false gods; either a door in the house, that keepeth those things that be in it, is better than false gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> Forsooth the sun, and moon, and stars, when they be bright &#91;or be shining&#93;, and sent out to profits, obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> In like manner and &#91;the&#93; lightning, when it appeareth, is clear. Soothly the same thing and wind breatheth in each country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And clouds, to which, when it is commanded of God to go through all the world, perform that, that is commanded to those &#91;or do what thing is commanded to them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> Also fire sent from above, to waste mountains and woods, doeth that, that is commanded to it; but these idols be not like to one of those things, neither by forms, neither by virtues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> Wherefore it is neither to guess, neither to say, &#91;or to be guessed, either to be said&#93;, that they be gods, when they may not neither deem doom, neither do to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> Therefore know ye that those &#91;or they&#93; be not gods, and dread ye not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> For they shall neither curse, neither bless kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> Also they show not to heathen men signs in heavens, neither those &#91;or they&#93; shall shine as the sun, neither they shall give light as the moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> Beasts that may flee under a roof, and do profit to themselves, be better than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> Therefore by no manner it is open to you, that they be gods. For which thing, dread ye not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> For why as a man of rags, or shoeless, in a place where gourds, &#91;or cucumbers, that be bitter herbs&#93;, waxed, keepeth nothing, so be their gods of tree, and of silver, and of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71"> In the same manner and a white-thorn in a garden &#91;or a yard&#93;keepeth nothing, on which thorn each bird sitteth, in like manner and their gods of tree, and of gold, and of silver, be like &#91;to&#93; a dead man cast forth in darknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72"> Also of &#91;the&#93; purple and of marble, which they hold above it; therefore ye shall know, that they be not gods. Also those be eaten at the last &#91;or at the last they themselves be eaten&#93;, and it shall be into shame &#91;or reproof&#93; in the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73"> Better is a just man, that hath no simulacra, for why he shall be far from shames.</span>
