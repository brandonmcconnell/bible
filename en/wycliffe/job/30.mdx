<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 12:4; PSALMS 35:15–16; PSALMS 69:12"> But now younger men in time scorn me, whose fathers I deigned not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This disdain came not of despite, neither of pride, but of worthy beholding of vilety or vileness.">a</a></sup>to set with the dogs of my flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Of which men the strength of their hands was for nought to me, and they were guessed unworthy to that life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 24:5; HEBREWS 11:38; JOB 24:13–16"> They were barren for neediness and hunger; they gnawed in wilder-ness, and were pale for poverty and wretchedness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 15:16; 2ND KI 4:38–39; AMOS 7:14"> and they ate herbs, and the rinds of trees; and the root of junipers was their meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 4:25; DANIEL 4:32–33; PSALMS 109:10"> The which men ravished these things from great valleys; and when they had found any of all these things, they ran with a cry to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 22:1–2; JUDGES 6:2; ISAIAH 2:19"> They dwelled in deserts of strands &#91;or streams&#93;, and in caves of &#91;the&#93; earth, either on gravel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 6:5; JOB 11:12; GENESIS 16:12"> Which were glad among such things, and they areckoned as delights to be under bushes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:22; JOB 40:4; ISAIAH 32:6"> These were the sons of fools, and of unnoble men, and utterly appearing not on &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:63; JOB 17:6; LAMENTATIONS 3:14"> But now I am turned into the song of them, and I am made a proverb to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:67; ISAIAH 50:6; NUMBERS 12:14"> They hold me abominable, and they flee far from me, and dread not to spit on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 12:21; RUTH 1:21; JOB 12:18"> For God hath opened his arrow case, and he hath tormented me, and he hath put a bridle into my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 19:12; ISAIAH 3:5; JOB 19:18"> At the right side of the east my wretchednesses have risen up anon; they turned upside down my feet, and they oppressed me with their paths as with floods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:12; ZECHARIAH 1:15; PSALMS 69:26"> They destroyed my ways; they setted treason to me, and they had the mastery; and there was none that helped me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 22:16; PSALMS 18:4; PSALMS 69:14–15"> They felled in upon me as by a broken wall, and by gate opened, and were stretched forth to my wretched-nesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HOSEA 13:3; JOB 6:4; ISAIAH 44:22"> I am driven into nought; he took away my desire as &#91;the&#93; wind, and mine help passed away as a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; PSALMS 22:14; ISAIAH 53:12"> But now my soul fadeth in myself, and &#91;the&#93; days of torment hold me steadfastly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 7:4; JOB 33:19–21; ISAIAH 38:13"> In &#91;the&#93; night my bone is pierced with sorrows; and they, that eat me, sleep not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 7:5; JOB 19:20; JOB 2:7"> In the multitude of those &#91;or them&#93; my cloth is wasted, and they have girded me as with the collar of a coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 9:31; JOB 42:6; GENESIS 18:27"> I am comparisoned to clay, and I am made like to a dead spark and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 19:7; PSALMS 80:4–5; PSALMS 22:2"> I shall cry to thee, and thou shalt not hear me; I stand, and thou behold-est not me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 10:14–17; JOB 16:9–14; JOB 19:6–9"> Thou art changed into cruel to me, and in the hardness of thine hand thou art adversary to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 27:21; PSALMS 1:4; HOSEA 4:19"> Thou hast raised me, and hast set me as on wind; and hast hurtled me down strongly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:5–7; HEBREWS 9:27; ECCLESIASTES 9:5"> I know, that thou shalt betake me to death, where an house is ordained to each living man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JUDGES 5:31; MATTHEW 27:39–44; JOB 19:7"> Nevertheless thou sendest not out thine hand to the wasting of them; and if they fall down, thou shalt save them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; PSALMS 35:13–14; JEREMIAH 13:17"> I wept sometime on him that was tormented, and my soul had compas-sion on a poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:15; JOB 3:25–26; JEREMIAH 14:19"> I abode goods, and evils be come to me; I abode light, and darknesses brake out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:11; JEREMIAH 31:20; JEREMIAH 4:19"> Mine inner things boiled out with-out my rest; and &#91;the&#93; days of torment came before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 42:9; PSALMS 38:6; PSALMS 43:2"> I went mourning, and I rose up without strong vengeance in the company, and I cried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MICAH 1:8; PSALMS 102:6; PSALMS 44:19"> I was the brother of dragons, and the fellow of ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:8; PSALMS 102:3; PSALMS 119:83"> My skin was made black upon me, and my bones dried for heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:15; ISAIAH 21:4; ISAIAH 22:12"> Mine harp is turned into mourning, and mine organ into the voice of weepers.</span>
