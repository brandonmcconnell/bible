<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 10:7; JOB 33:9; JOB 10:2"> Forsooth these three men left off to answer Job, for he seemed a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 22:21; JOB 40:8; JOB 27:2"> And Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram, was wroth, and had indignation; and he was wroth against Job, for he said himself to be just before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 8:6; JOB 24:25; JOB 22:5–30"> And also Elihu had indignation against the three friends of Job, for they had found no reasonable answer, but only had condemned Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 32:11–12; PROVERBS 18:13"> Therefore Elihu abode Job speak-ing, for they, that spake, were elder men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 32:2; EXODUS 32:19"> But when he had seen, that these three men might not answer Job, he was wroth greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 15:10; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:1; TITUS 2:6"> And Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, answered, and said, I am younger in time, and ye be elder; therefore with head holden down, I dreaded to show to you my sentence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:31; JOB 12:12; HEBREWS 5:12"> For I hoped that &#91;the&#93; longer age should speak, and that the multitude of years should teach wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 38:36; JOB 33:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:16"> But as I see now, a spirit is in men, and the inspiration, either revelation, of Almighty God giveth understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:25; JOB 12:20; ECCLESIASTES 4:13"> Men of long life be not always wise, and eld &#91;or old&#93; men understand not doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:40"> Therefore I shall say, Hear ye me, and I also shall show my knowing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 29:21; JOB 5:27; PROVERBS 28:11"> For I abode your words, I heard your prudence, as long as ye disputed in your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 32:3; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:7"> And as long as I guessed you to say anything, I beheld; but as I perceive, there is none of you, that may reprove Job, and answer to his words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:27–29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18"> lest peradventure ye say, We have found wisdom; God, and not man, hath cast him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Job spake nothing to me, and I not by your words shall answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 6:24–25; MATTHEW 22:46; JOB 29:22"> They &#91;much&#93; dreaded, and answer-ed no more, and took away speech from themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; JOB 13:5; AMOS 5:13"> Therefore since I abode, and they spake not, they stood, and answered no more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 35:3–4; JOB 33:12; JOB 32:10"> also I shall answer my part, and I shall show my knowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:13–14"> For I am full of words, and the spirit of my womb, that is, my mind, constraineth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:17"> Lo! my womb is as must without a spigot or faucet, either a venting, that bursteth new vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:6–7; JOB 21:3; JOB 13:13"> I shall speak, and breathe again a little; I shall open my lips, and I shall answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:15; JOB 13:8; MATTHEW 22:16"> I shall not take the person of a man, and I shall not make God even to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2–3; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:5; PROVERBS 29:5"> For I know not how long I shall abide alive, and if my Maker will take me away after a little time.</span>
