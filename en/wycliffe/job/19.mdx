<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth Job answered, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 64:3; JOB 18:2; JOB 27:2"> How long torment ye my soul, and all-break me with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 31:7; NEHEMIAH 4:12; JOB 18:4–21"> Lo! ten times ye have shamed me, and ye be not ashamed, oppressing me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10; PROVERBS 9:12; EZEKIEL 18:4"> Forsooth and if I know not &#91;or if I am uncunning&#93;, mine unknowing shall be with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 35:26; PSALMS 38:16; PSALMS 55:12"> And ye be raised against me, and reprove me with my shames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 18:8–10; JOB 27:2; JOB 16:11–14"> Namely now understand ye, that God hath tormented me not by even doom, and hath encompassed me with his beatings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:2–3; LAMENTATIONS 3:8; JOB 31:35–36"> Lo! I suffering violence shall cry, and no man shall hear me; I shall cry loud, and there is none that deemeth me worthy to be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:7; JOB 3:23; PROVERBS 4:19"> The Lord hath beset about my path, and I may not go; and he hath set darknesses in my way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 89:44; JOB 29:7–14; PSALMS 89:39"> He hath spoiled me of my glory, and hath taken away the crown from mine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 24:20; PSALMS 37:35–36; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:8–9"> He hath destroyed me on each side, and I perished; and he hath taken away mine hope, as from a tree pulled up by the root.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 13:24; JOB 16:9; JOB 33:10"> His strong vengeance was wroth against me; and he had me so as his enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 30:12; JOB 16:13; ISAIAH 51:23"> His thieves came together, and made to them a way by me; and besieged my tabernacle in compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 88:8; PSALMS 88:18; PSALMS 69:8"> He made &#91;a&#93; far my brethren from me; and my known as aliens went away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 38:11; JEREMIAH 20:10; 2ND SAMUEL 16:23"> My neighbours have forsaken me; and they that knew me have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 31:31–32; PSALMS 123:3"> The tenants of mine house, and mine handmaids, had me as a stranger; and I was as a pilgrim before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 1:15–17; JOB 1:19"> I called my servant, and he answered not to me; with mine own mouth I prayed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 2:9–10; JOB 17:1"> My wife loathed my breath; and I prayed the sons of my womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND KI 2:23; JOB 30:12; JOB 30:1"> Also fools despised me; and when I was gone away from them, they backbited me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; PSALMS 109:4–5; PSALMS 55:12–14"> They, that were my counsellors sometime, had abomination of me; and he, whom I loved most, was adversary to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 102:5; LAMENTATIONS 4:8; JOB 33:19–22"> When my fleshes were wasted, my bones cleaved to my skin; and only &#91;the&#93; lips be left about my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 1:11; PSALMS 38:2; JOB 2:5"> Have ye mercy on me, have ye mercy on me, namely, ye my friends; for the hand of the Lord hath touched me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 69:26; JOB 31:31; JOB 16:13–14"> Why pursue ye me, as God pursueth; and ye be fulfilled with my fleshes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:8; JOB 31:35; ISAIAH 8:1"> Who giveth, or granteth, to me, that my words be written? Who giveth to me, that those &#91;or they&#93; be written in a book,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:1; EXODUS 28:21; EXODUS 32:16"> with an iron pointel, either with a piece of lead; either with a chisel those &#91;or they&#93; be engraved in a flint?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:7; ISAIAH 54:5; PSALMS 78:35"> For I know, that mine again-buyer liveth, and in the last day I shall rise from the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:8; 1ST JOHN 3:2; PSALMS 17:15"> and again I shall be encompassed with my skin, and in my flesh I shall see God, my saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 73:26; ISAIAH 26:19; NUMBERS 24:17"> Whom I myself shall see, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another man. This mine hope is kept in my bosom, that is, in mine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 19:22; PSALMS 69:26; 1ST KINGS 14:13"> Why therefore say ye now, Pursue we him, and find we the root of a word against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:14; ECCLESIASTES 11:9; PSALMS 58:10–11"> Therefore flee ye from the face of the sword; for the sword is the avenger of wickednesses, and know ye, that doom shall be.</span>
