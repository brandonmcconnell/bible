<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 38:29–30; PSALMS 147:16–18; PSALMS 78:47"> When God maketh blowing, frost waxeth altogether; and again full broad waters be poured out thereof.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:45; JOB 38:26–27; 1ST SAMUEL 12:18–19"> whether in one lineage, either in his land, either in whatever place of his mercy he commandeth those &#91;or them&#93; to be found.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 36:30–32; ISAIAH 40:26; JOB 37:11"> Whether thou knowest, when God commanded to the rains, that those &#91;or they&#93; shall show the light of his clouds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 36:4; PROVERBS 3:19–20; ISAIAH 40:22"> Whether thou knowest the great ways of the clouds, and the perfect knowings of those?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 147:18; JOB 6:17; JOB 38:31"> Whether thy clothes be not hot, when the earth is blown with the south?</span>
