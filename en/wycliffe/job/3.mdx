<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:14–15; PSALMS 106:33; PSALMS 39:2–3"> After these things Job opened his mouth, and cursed his day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 18:14"> and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:14–18; JEREMIAH 15:10; JOB 10:18–19"> Perish the day in which I was born, and the night in which it was said, A man is conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:20; EXODUS 10:22–23; JOEL 2:2"> That day be turned into darknesses; God seek not it &#91;from&#93; above, and be it not in mind, neither be it lightened with light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 10:21–22; PSALMS 23:4; JOB 28:3"> Darkness make it dark, and the shadow of death and of mist occupy it; and be it wrapped with bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Dark whirlwind hold that night; be it not reckoned among the days of the year, neither be it numbered among the months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 24:8; JEREMIAH 7:34; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:22–23"> That night be sullen, and not worthy of praising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 41:1; JOB 41:10; MARK 5:38"> Curse they it, that curse the day, that be ready to raise Leviathan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Devil, as Alcuin saith.">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 41:18; JEREMIAH 13:16; JOB 30:26"> &#91;The&#93; Stars of that night be they made dark with the darkness thereof; abide it light, and see it not, neither see it the beginning of the morrow-tide rising up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:17; ECCLESIASTES 6:3–5; GENESIS 20:18"> For it closed not the doors of the womb, that bare me, neither it took away evil from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 10:18–19; ISAIAH 46:3; PSALMS 71:6"> Why was not I dead in the womb? why went I out of the womb, and perished not anon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:12; GENESIS 30:3; GENESIS 50:23"> Why was I taken on knees? why was I given suck with teats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:10; JOB 21:13; JOB 14:10–12"> For now I sleeping should be still, and I should rest in my sleep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 15:28; PSALMS 89:48; PSALMS 49:14"> with kings and counsellors of the earth, which build to them sullen places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 10:27; JOB 22:25; ZEPHANIAH 1:18"> either with princes that have gold in possession, and fill their houses with silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 58:8; ECCLESIASTES 6:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:8"> either as a child, or a beast, born before the time, and hid, I should not have been; either as they that be conceived, and saw not light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:13; JOB 17:16; HEBREWS 4:11"> There wicked men ceased of making noise, and there men made weary of strength rested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 39:7; ISAIAH 14:3–4; EXODUS 5:6–8"> And sometime men bound together now be without dis-ease, they heard not the voice of the wrongful asker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 8:8; JOB 30:23; ECCLESIASTES 12:7"> A little man and a great man be there, and a servant is free from his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:18; JOB 7:15–16; 1ST SAMUEL 1:10"> Why is light given to the wretch, and life to them that be in bitterness of soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:6; PROVERBS 2:4; 1ST KINGS 19:4"> Which abide death, and it cometh not; as men that dig out treasure,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and joy greatly, when they have found a sepulchre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 19:8; LAMENTATIONS 3:7; ISAIAH 40:27"> Why is light given to a man, whose way is hid, and God hath encompassed him with darknesses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 102:9; PSALMS 42:3–4; PSALMS 80:5"> Before that I eat, I sigh; and as of water flowing, so is my roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOB 1:5; JOB 30:15; JOB 31:23"> For the dread, which I dreaded, hath come to me; and that thing, that I shamed &#91;of&#93;, hath befallen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOB 7:14; JOB 27:9; PSALMS 143:11"> Whether I dissembled or feigned not? whether I was not still? whether I rested not? and yet indignation hath come &#91;up&#93; on me.</span>
