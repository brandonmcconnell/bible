</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:1; PSALMS 133:1"> And his sons went, and made feasts by their houses, each man in his day; and they sent, and called their three sisters, to eat, and to drink wine with them.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:12–17; MATTHEW 12:43"> To whom the Lord said, From whence comest thou? And Satan answered, and said, I have compassed the earth, and I have walked through it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 2:3; JOB 1:1; NUMBERS 12:7–8"> And the Lord said to him, Whether thou hast beheld my servant Job, that there is no man in &#91;the&#93; earth like him; he is a simple man, and rightful &#91;or right&#93;, and dreading God, and going away from evil?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:8; JOB 2:10"> To whom Satan answered, Whether Job dreadeth God vainly?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 34:7; ZECHARIAH 2:5; 1ST PETER 1:5"> Whether thou hast not encom-passed him, and his house, and all his chattel by compass? Thou hast blessed the works of his hands, and his possession is increased on &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 2:5; JOB 19:21; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:11"> But stretch forth thine hand a little, and touch thou all things that he hath in possession, taking them away, or extinguishing them; and if he curse not thee in the face, he is verily simple, and rightwise, and dreading thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 22:31–32; JOHN 3:35–36; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:7"> Therefore the Lord said to Satan, Lo! all things, which he hath, be in thine hand; only stretch thou not forth thine hand into him. And Satan went out from the face of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 21:34; PROVERBS 27:1; JOB 1:4"> And when in a day the sons of Job and his daughters ate, and drank wine in the house of their first begot-ten brother,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:13; JEREMIAH 51:31; 1ST SAMUEL 4:17"> a messenger came to Job, and said to him, Thine oxen eared, and thy female asses &#91;were&#93; pastured beside them;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 10:7; JOB 6:19; ISAIAH 45:14"> and Sabeans felled in, and took away all things, and smited the children or servants with sword; and I alone escaped to tell to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND KI 1:12; GENESIS 19:24; 1ST KINGS 18:38"> And when he spake yet, another came, and said, &#91;The&#93; Fire of God came down from heaven, and wasted &#91;the&#93; sheep, and smote thy children or servants; and I alone escaped for to tell to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 11:28; HABAKKUK 1:6; GENESIS 11:31"> But yet the while he spake, also another came, and said, Chaldees made three companies, and assailed the camels, and took those &#91;or them&#93; away, and they have killed thy servants with sword; and I alone escaped to tell to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 1:4; JOB 1:13; JOB 27:14"> And yet while he spake, lo! another entered, and said, While thy sons and daughters ate, and drank wine in the house of their first begotten brother,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 42:36; LUKE 13:1–5; JUDGES 16:30"> a great wind felled in suddenly from the coast of desert, and shook &#91;the&#93; four corners of the house, which felled down, and oppressed thy children, and they be dead; and I alone fled to tell to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:6; 2ND SAMUEL 12:16–20; GENESIS 37:34"> Then Job rose up, and he rent his clothes, and when his head was polled, he felled down upon the earth, and he worshipped God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:15; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:7; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:18"> and said, I went naked out of the womb of my mother, and naked I shall turn again thither; the Lord gave, and the Lord hath taken away; as it pleased the Lord, so be it done; the name of the Lord be blessed.</span>
</div>
