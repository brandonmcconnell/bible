<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 13:6; MARK 4:9; PSALMS 49:1–3"> Therefore, Job, hear thou my speeches, and hearken to all my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 3:1; MATTHEW 5:2; PSALMS 78:2"> Lo! I have opened my mouth, my tongue shall speak in my cheeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 27:4; PROVERBS 20:15; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:3–4"> Of simple heart be my words, and my lips shall speak clean sentence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 32:8; GENESIS 2:7; JOB 27:3"> The spirit of God made me, and the breathing of Almighty God quick-ened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 33:32–33; JOB 32:1; PSALMS 50:21"> If thou mayest, answer thou to me, and stand thou against my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 4:19; JOB 10:9; JOB 13:12"> Lo! God made me as and thee; and also I am formed of the same clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 13:21; JOB 9:34; PSALMS 32:4"> Nevertheless my miracle, that is, knowing given of God, either by miracle, to me, make thee not afeared, and mine eloquence be not grievous to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:14; JEREMIAH 29:23"> Thou saidest in mine hearing, and I heard the voice of thy words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 10:7; JOB 16:17; JOB 9:21"> I am clean, and without guilt, and unwemmed, and wickedness is not in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 19:11; JOB 13:24–25; JOB 9:30–31"> For God found quarrels in me, therefore he deemed me enemy to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 13:27; JOB 31:4; DANIEL 4:35"> He hath set &#91;or put&#93; my feet in a stock; he kept all my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:25; JOB 36:5; JOB 1:22"> Therefore this thing it is, in which thou art not made just; I shall answer to thee, that God is more than man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 40:2; ISAIAH 45:9; ROMANS 11:34"> Thou, Job, strivest against God, that not at all thy words &#91;he&#93; answered to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:24; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:10; JOB 33:29"> God speaketh once, and the second time he rehearseth not the same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:6; JOB 4:13; GENESIS 20:3"> God speaketh by a dream in the vision of night, when sleep falleth on men, and when they sleep in their bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 36:10; JOB 36:15; PSALMS 40:6"> Then he openeth the ears of men, and he teacheth them, and teacheth prudence or discipline;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 2:11; JAMES 4:10; GENESIS 20:6"> that he turn away a man from these things which he made, and deliver him from pride;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 33:30; JOB 33:28; 2ND PETER 3:9"> and that he deliver his soul from corruption, and his life, that it go not into sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 30:17; JOB 5:17–18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:32"> Also God blameth a man by sorrow in his bed, and he maketh all the bones of him for to wax rotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 107:17–18; JOB 3:24; GENESIS 3:6"> Bread is made abominable to him in his life, and the meat, that before was to him desirable, loathed to his soul after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 19:20; JOB 13:28; PROVERBS 5:11"> His flesh shall fail for rot, and his bones, that were covered, shall be made naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 33:28; 2ND SAMUEL 24:16; JOB 17:13–16"> His soul shall nigh to corruption, and his life to things bringing death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; ECCLESIASTES 7:28; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:20"> If an angel, one of a thousand, is speaking for him, that he show the equity of man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–8; MATTHEW 20:28; 1ST PETER 1:18–19"> God shall have mercy on him, and shall say, Deliver thou him, that he go not down into corruption; I have found in what thing I shall do mercy to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 103:5; 2ND KI 5:14; JOSHUA 14:10–11"> His flesh is wasted with torments; turn he again to the days of his young waxing age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 30:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:11"> He shall beseech God, and he shall be quemeful to him; and he shall see his face in perfect joy, and he shall yield to man his rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ROMANS 6:21; 2ND SAMUEL 12:13; PSALMS 14:2"> He shall behold men, and he shall say, I have sinned, and verily I have trespassed; and I have not received, as I was worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 33:24; JOB 3:16; JOB 33:22"> Forsooth he hath delivered his soul, that it should not go into perish-ing, but that he living should see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:6; PHILIPPIANS 2:13"> Lo! God worketh all these things in three times by all men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 56:13; ISAIAH 38:17; JOB 33:28"> that he again-call their souls from corruption, and enlighten them in the light of living men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOB 18:2; JOB 13:6; JOB 21:2"> Job, take heed, and hear thou me, and be thou still, while I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOB 21:27; JOB 15:4–5; JOB 22:5–9"> But if thou hast ready what thou shalt speak, answer thou to me, speak; for I will or desire, that thou appear just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 34:11; PROVERBS 5:1–2; PSALMS 49:3"> That if thou hast not, hear thou me; be thou still, and I shall teach thee wisdom.</span>
