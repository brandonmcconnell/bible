</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:11–16; LEVITICUS 15:1–33; LEVITICUS 12:1–8"> And if he toucheth anything of the uncleanness of man, by all the uncleanness by which he is wont to be defouled, and he forgetteth it, and knoweth this afterward, he shall be subject to that trespass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:2–6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:12; JUDGES 11:31"> A soul that sweareth, and bringeth forth with his lips, that he should do either evil, or well, and doeth it not, and confirmeth the same thing with an oath, either with a word, and forgetteth what he swore, or said, and afterward understandeth his trespass,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; NUMBERS 5:7; LEVITICUS 26:40"> do he penance for his sin,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:28; LEVITICUS 4:32; LEVITICUS 4:20"> and offer he of the flocks a female lamb, either a goat; and the priest shall pray for him, and for his sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:8; LEVITICUS 14:21–22; MATTHEW 3:16"> But if he may not offer a beast, offer he two turtles, either two culver birds to the Lord, one for &#91;the&#93; sin, and the tother into burnt sacrifice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:15; LEVITICUS 1:17; ROMANS 4:25"> And he shall give those &#91;or them&#93; to the priest, which shall offer the first for &#91;the&#93; sin, and shall fold again the head thereof to the wings, so that it cleave to the neck, and be not broken utterly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:30; LEVITICUS 4:34; LEVITICUS 4:18"> And the priest shall sprinkle the wall of the altar, of the blood thereof; soothly whatever is residue, he shall make to drop down at the foundament of the altar, for it is for sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:14–17; LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:20"> Soothly he shall burn the tother bird into burnt sacrifice, as it is wont to be done; and the priest shall pray for him, and for his sin, and it shall be forgiven to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:15; LEVITICUS 2:1–2; EXODUS 16:36"> That if his hand for poverty may not offer two turtles, either two culver birds, he shall offer for his sin the tenth part of ephah of tried wheat flour; he shall not put oil into it, neither he shall put anything of incense, for it is for sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:35; LEVITICUS 2:9; LEVITICUS 2:2"> And he shall give it to the priest, which priest shall take up an handful thereof, and shall burn it on the altar, into mind of him that offered it,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; LEVITICUS 4:26; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:13"> and the priest shall pray for him, and cleanse him; forsooth the priest shall have the tother part into gift.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; LEVITICUS 4:2; LEVITICUS 6:6"> If a soul, that is, a man, breaketh &#91;the&#93; ceremonies by error, and sin in these things that be hallowed to the Lord, he shall offer for his trespass a ram without wem of the flocks, that may be bought for two shekels, at the weight of the saintuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:14; NUMBERS 5:7; LEVITICUS 27:27"> And he shall restore that harm that he did, and he shall put the fifth part thereof above, and he shall give it to the priest, which priest shall pray for him, and offer the ram, and it shall be forgiven to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:27; LEVITICUS 4:13; LEVITICUS 4:2–4"> A soul, that is, a man, that sinneth by ignorance, and doeth one of these things that be forbidden in the law of the Lord, and is guilty of &#91;the&#93; sin, and understandeth his wickedness,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:15–16; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:5–6; LEVITICUS 1:4"> he shall offer to the priest, a ram without wem of the flocks, by the measure and estimation, or value, of the sin; and the priest shall pray for him, for he did unwittingly, and it shall be forgiven to him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; MALACHI 3:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:19–21"> for by error he trespassed against the Lord.</span>
