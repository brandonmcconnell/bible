<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:2; 1ST SAMUEL 1:28; JUDGES 11:30–31"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, A man that maketh a vow, and promiseth his soul to God, he shall give the price under value, either appraising thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; LEVITICUS 27:25; NUMBERS 18:16"> If it is a male, from the twentieth year till to the sixtieth year, he shall give fifty shekels of silver, at the measure of the saintuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:9–10; ZECHARIAH 11:12–13; MATTHEW 26:15"> if it is a woman, she shall give thirty shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> forsooth from the fifth year till to the twentieth year, a male shall give twenty shekels, a woman shall give ten shekels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:40–43; NUMBERS 18:14–16"> from one month till to the fifth year, five shekels shall be given for a male, three shekels for a woman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 90:10"> a male of sixty years and over shall give fifteen shekels, a woman shall give ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 14:7; LEVITICUS 14:21–22; LUKE 21:1–4"> If it is a poor man, and &#91;he&#93; may not yield the value, he shall stand before the priest, and as much as the priest appraiseth, and seeth that the poor man may yield, so much he shall give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Soothly if any man avoweth a beast, that may be offered to the Lord, it shall be holy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:15–33; JAMES 1:8"> and it shall not be able to be changed, that is, neither a better for a worse, neither an evil for a good; and if he changeth it, both that, that is changed, and that, for which it is changed, shall be hallowed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:18; MALACHI 1:14"> Soothly if any man avoweth an unclean beast, that may not be offered to the Lord, it shall be brought before the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and the priest shall deem whether it is good either evil, and he shall set the price;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:19; LEVITICUS 27:15; LEVITICUS 22:14"> which price, if he that offereth will not give, he shall add the fifth part over the value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:14; LEVITICUS 25:29–31; LEVITICUS 27:21"> If a man avoweth his house, and halloweth it to the Lord, the priest shall behold, whether it is good either evil, and by the price which is ordained of him, it shall be sold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:13"> soothly if he that avowed will again-buy it, he shall give the fifth part of the value above, and he shall have the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:11–14; HOSEA 3:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:34–37"> That if a man avoweth the field of his possession, and halloweth it to the Lord, the price shall be deemed by the measure of &#91;the&#93; seed; if the field is sown with thirty bushels of barley, it shall be sold for fifty shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he avoweth the field anon from the year of &#91;the&#93; beginning of the jubilee, as much as it may be worth, by so much it shall be appraised;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:15–16; LEVITICUS 25:51–52; LEVITICUS 25:27"> but if it be after some part of time, the priest shall reckon, either determine, the money by the number of the years that be left till to the jubilee, and it shall be withdrawn of the price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:13"> That if he that avowed will again-buy the field, he shall add the fifth part of the money that is appraised, and he shall wield it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> but if he will not again-buy it, but it is sold to any other man, he that avowed it shall never be able to again-buy it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:29; NUMBERS 18:14; LEVITICUS 25:31"> for when the day of jubilee cometh, that field shall be hallowed to the Lord, and the possession hallowed pertaineth to the right of priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:10; LEVITICUS 25:25"> If the field is bought, and is not of the possession of greater men, that is, of ancestors, and it is hallowed to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:18; LEVITICUS 27:12"> the priest shall determine the price by the number of years till to the jubilee, and he that avowed the field shall give the price thereof to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:28; LEVITICUS 27:20"> forsooth in the jubilee it shall turn again to the former lord that sold it, and he shall have it into the heritage of his possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; NUMBERS 18:16; NUMBERS 3:47"> All the appraising, or value, shall be weighed by the shekel of the saintuary; a shekel hath twenty half-pence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 13:2; EXODUS 13:12–13; NUMBERS 18:17"> No man may hallow and avow the first engendered things that pertain to the Lord, whether it is ox, or sheep, they be the Lord’s part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:11"> That if the beast is unclean that is avowed, he that offered it shall again-buy it after the value that it is appraised, and he shall add to the fifth part of the price; if he will not again-buy it, it shall be sold to another man, for as much as it is appraised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:17–19; LEVITICUS 27:21; EXODUS 22:20"> All thing that is hallowed to the Lord, whether it is man, or beast, whether field of his heritage, it shall not be sold, neither it shall be able to be again-bought; whatever thing is hallowed once, it shall be holy of holy things to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:18–23; NUMBERS 21:2–3"> and each hallowing which is offered of man, shall not be again-bought, but it shall die by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:23; NEHEMIAH 13:12; GENESIS 28:22"> All the tithes of &#91;the&#93; earth, whether of fruits of corn, whether of apples of trees, be the Lord’s part, and be hallowed to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> soothly if any man will again-buy his tithes, he shall add to the fifth part of those &#91;or them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:13; EZEKIEL 20:37; MICAH 7:14"> of all the tithes of sheep, and of oxen, and of goats, that pass under the shepherd’s rod, whatever thing cometh to the tenth part, it shall be hallowed to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:10"> it shall not be chosen, neither good, neither evil; neither it shall be changed for another; if any man changeth it, both that, that is changed, and that, for which it is changed, shall be hallowed to the Lord, and it shall not be again-bought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:46; NUMBERS 1:1; HEBREWS 12:18–25"> These be the commandments which the Lord commanded to Moses, and to the sons of Israel, in the hill &#91;or mount&#93; of Sinai.</span>
