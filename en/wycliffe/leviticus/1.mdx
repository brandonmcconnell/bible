<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 19:3; EXODUS 25:22; EXODUS 29:42"> Forsooth the Lord called Moses, and spake to him from the tabernacle of witnessing, saying,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:18–19; ROMANS 12:6; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:29"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, A man of you, that offereth to the Lord a sacrifice of beasts, that is, of oxen and of sheep, and offereth slain sacrifices,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:21; HEBREWS 9:14; LEVITICUS 22:19–24"> if his offering is burnt sacrifice, and of the drove of oxen, he shall offer a male beast without wem at the door of the tabernacle of witnessing, to make the Lord pleased to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 29:10; EXODUS 29:15; LEVITICUS 4:20"> And he shall set his hands on the head of the sacrifice, and it shall be acceptable, and profiting into the cleansing of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:11; LEVITICUS 3:8; LEVITICUS 3:2"> And he shall offer a calf before the Lord, and the sons of Aaron, &#91;the&#93; priests, shall offer the blood thereof, and they shall shed it by compass of the altar, that is before the door of the tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:8; GENESIS 3:21"> And when the skin of the sacrifice is drawn away, they shall cut the members into gobbets;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:12–13; GENESIS 22:9; 1ST CHRONICLES 21:26"> and they shall put under the altar the fire, and they shall make an heap of wood ready before;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:13–14; LEVITICUS 1:12; 1ST KINGS 18:33"> and they shall ordain above that wood those things that be cut, that is, the head, and all things that cleave to the maw,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 8:21; LEVITICUS 1:13; EPHESIANS 5:2"> when the entrails and the feet be washed with water; and the priest shall burn those &#91;or them&#93; on the altar, into burnt sacrifice, and to sweet odour to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:6–7; LEVITICUS 1:2–3; GENESIS 8:20"> That if the offering is of little beasts, a burnt sacrifice of sheep, either of goats, he shall offer a male beast without wem,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:5; EXODUS 40:22; LEVITICUS 7:2"> and he shall offer that at the side of the altar that beholdeth to the north, before the Lord. Soothly the sons of Aaron shall pour the blood thereof on the altar by compass,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and they shall part the members, the head, and all things that cleave to the maw, and they shall put them on the wood, under which the fire shall be set &#91;or put&#93;;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:9"> soothly they shall wash in water the entrails and &#91;the&#93; feet; and the priest shall burn all things offered on the altar, into burnt sacrifice, and sweetest odour to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:7; LUKE 2:24; LEVITICUS 12:8"> Forsooth if the offering of burnt sacrifice to the Lord is of birds, of turtles, or of culver birds,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:8–9; PSALMS 22:21; 1ST JOHN 2:27"> the priest shall offer it at the altar; and when the head is writhed to the neck, and the place of the wound is broken, he shall make the blood run down on the brink of the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:2; LUKE 1:35; LEVITICUS 16:27"> Soothly he shall cast forth the little bladder of the throat, or the crop, and the feathers beside the altar, at the east coast, in the place in the which the ashes be wont to be cast out;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 15:10; JOHN 19:30; LEVITICUS 1:9–10"> and the priest shall break the wings thereof, and he shall not carve it, neither part it with iron; and he shall burn it on the altar, when fire is put under the wood; it is a burnt sacrifice, and an offering of sweetest odour to the Lord.</span>
</div>
