<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:25; LEVITICUS 21:11; LEVITICUS 10:6–7"> And the Lord said to Moses, Speak thou to &#91;the&#93; priests, the sons of Aaron, and thou shalt say to them, A priest be not defouled in the dead men of his citizens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 4:13; LEVITICUS 18:6"> no but only in his kinsmen, and nigh of blood, that is, on father, and mother, and son, and daughter, and brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and sister, a virgin, that is not wedded to man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:16–17"> but neither he shall be defouled in the prince of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:1; EZEKIEL 44:20; LEVITICUS 19:27–28"> Priests shall not shave their head, neither beard, neither they shall make carvings in their fleshes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:11; LEVITICUS 18:21; 1ST PETER 2:9"> they shall be holy to their God, and they shall not defoul his name; for they offer incense of the Lord, and the loaves of their God, and therefore they shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:13–14; EZEKIEL 44:22; LEVITICUS 21:8"> A priest shall not wed a wife a corrupt woman, and a foul whore, neither he shall wed her that is forsaken of the husband, for he is hallowed to his God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:7–8; LEVITICUS 19:2; LEVITICUS 11:44–45"> and offereth the loaves of setting forth; therefore be he holy, for I am the holy Lord that halloweth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 38:24; LEVITICUS 19:29; EZEKIEL 9:6"> If the daughter of a priest is taken in defouling of virginity, and defouleth the name of her father, she shall be burnt in fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:6–7; LEVITICUS 16:32; MATTHEW 26:65"> The bishop, that is the most priest among his brethren, upon whose head the oil of anointing is poured, and whose hands be made sacred in priesthood, and he is clothed in holy clothes, shall not discover his head, he shall not tear his clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:14; LEVITICUS 21:1–2; NUMBERS 6:7"> and utterly he shall not enter &#91;in&#93; to any dead man; and he shall not be defouled on his father, and mother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:7; EXODUS 29:6–7; LEVITICUS 8:9–12"> neither he shall go out of &#91;the&#93; holy places, lest he defoul the saintuary of the Lord, for the oil of holy anointing of his God is on him; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:4"> He shall wed to wife a virgin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:7; EZEKIEL 44:22"> he shall not take a widow, and a forsaken woman, and a foul woman, and whore, but a virgin of his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 11:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:14; NEHEMIAH 13:23–29"> meddle &#91;or mingle&#93; he not the generation of his kin to the common people of his folk, for I am the Lord, that halloweth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 65:4; NUMBERS 16:5; LEVITICUS 10:3"> Speak thou to Aaron; a man of thy seed, by meines, that hath a wem, that is, a notable foulness, shall not offer bread to his God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 3:2–3; LEVITICUS 22:19–25; TITUS 1:7"> neither shall nigh to his service; if he is blind; if he is crooked; if he is either of little, either of great, or wrong nose;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> if he is of broken foot, either hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:1; ISAIAH 56:3"> if he hath a botch, or a bulge, on his back; either if he is bleary-eyed; if he hath white colour, or a pearl, in his eye, that hindereth his sight; if he hath continual scab; if he hath a dry scab in his body; either if he be bruised in privy members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:6; LEVITICUS 21:8; LEVITICUS 21:17"> Each man of the seed of Aaron the priest, that hath a wem, shall not nigh to offer sacrifices to the Lord, neither to offer loaves to his God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; NUMBERS 18:19; LEVITICUS 24:8–9"> nevertheless he shall eat the loaves that be offered in the saintuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:12; EZEKIEL 44:9–14; EXODUS 30:6–8"> so only that he enter not within the veil; he shall not nigh to the altar, for he hath a wem, and he shall not defoul my saintuary; I am the Lord, that hallow them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:18; MALACHI 2:1–7; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:2"> Therefore Moses spake to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all Israel, all things that were commanded to him.</span>
