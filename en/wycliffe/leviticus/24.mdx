<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> without the veil of witnessing, in the tabernacle of &#91;the&#93; bond of peace; and Aaron shall array those lanterns from eventide till to eventide before the Lord, by religion and custom everlasting in your generations;</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 25:30; 1ST KINGS 18:31; EXODUS 40:23"> Also thou shalt take &#91;tried&#93; wheat flour, and thou shalt bake thereof twelve loaves, which shall have each by themselves two tenth parts,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:48; EXODUS 25:23–24; 2ND CHRONICLES 4:19"> of which thou shalt set six on ever either side, on a full clean board before the Lord;</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:3–4; GENESIS 9:16"> and thou shalt set &#91;or put&#93; clearest incense upon those loaves, that the loaves be into mind of &#91;the&#93; offering of the Lord;</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:31; LUKE 6:4; MARK 2:26"> and they shall be Aaron’s and his sons’, that they eat those &#91;or them&#93; in the holy place, for it is holy of holy things, of the sacrifices of the Lord, by everlasting law.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 20:7; LEVITICUS 24:15–16; EXODUS 3:15"> and when he had blasphemed the name of the Lord, and had cursed the Lord, he was brought to Moses; soothly his mother was called Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the lineage of Dan;</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:13; EXODUS 22:28; LEVITICUS 5:1"> And thou shalt speak to the sons of Israel, A man that curseth his God, shall bear his sin,</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:31; JAMES 2:7; JOHN 10:33–36"> and he that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, die he by death; all the multitude of the people shall oppress him with stones; whether he that blasphemed the name of the Lord is a citizen, or a pilgrim, die he by death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 9:5–6; DEUTERONOMY 19:11–12; DEUTERONOMY 27:24"> He that smiteth and slayeth a man, die he by death;</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:21; EXODUS 21:34–36"> he that smiteth a beast, yield one in his stead, that is, life for life.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:38; MATTHEW 7:2; DEUTERONOMY 19:21"> If a man giveth a wem to any of his citizens, as he did, so be it done to him;</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:21; MATTHEW 5:38; EXODUS 21:23–25"> he shall restore breaking for breaking, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; what manner wem he gave, he shall be compelled to suffer such a wem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:17–18; EXODUS 21:33"> He that smiteth a work beast, yield he another; he that smiteth a man, shall die.</span>
