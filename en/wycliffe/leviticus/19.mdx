<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:44–45; LEVITICUS 20:7; 1ST PETER 1:15–16"> Speak thou to all the company of the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, Be ye holy, for I am holy, your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12; EXODUS 20:8; HEBREWS 12:9"> Each man dread his father, and his mother. Keep ye my sabbaths; I am your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:1; EXODUS 20:23; PSALMS 115:4–7"> Do not ye be turned to idols, neither ye shall make to you molten gods; I am your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:19; LEVITICUS 22:29; LEVITICUS 7:16"> If ye offer a sacrifice of peaceable things to the Lord, that it be quemeful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:11–17"> ye shall eat it in that day, in which it is offered, and in the tother day; soothly whatever thing is left into the third day, ye shall burn it in fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:18–21; LEVITICUS 22:25; JEREMIAH 16:18"> If any man eateth thereof after two days, he shall be unholy, and guilty of unfaithfulness, either wicked-ness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:15; LEVITICUS 5:1"> and he shall bear his wickedness, for he defouled the holy thing of the Lord, and his soul shall perish from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:22; DEUTERONOMY 24:19–22; RUTH 2:2"> When thou shalt reap the fruits of thy land, thou shalt not cut till to the ground the corns of the land, neither thou shalt gather the ears of corn that be left;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 17:6; LEVITICUS 25:6; ISAIAH 24:13"> neither in thy vineyard thou shalt gather the raisins and the grains falling down, but thou shalt leave them to be gathered of poor men and of pilgrims; I am your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 101:7; EPHESIANS 4:25; COLOSSIANS 3:9"> Ye shall not do theft. Ye shall not lie, and no man deceive his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 20:7; DEUTERONOMY 5:11; LEVITICUS 18:21"> Thou shalt not forswear in my name, neither thou shalt defoul the name of thy God; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 5:4; MALACHI 3:5; DEUTERONOMY 24:14–15"> Thou shalt not make false challenge to thy neighbour, neither thou shalt oppress him by violence. The hire of thy workman shall not dwell with thee unto the morrowtide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:18; LEVITICUS 19:32; LEVITICUS 25:17"> Thou shalt not curse a deaf man, neither thou shalt set &#91;or put&#93; an hurting before a blind man; but thou shalt dread thy Lord God, for I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:17; PROVERBS 24:23; DEUTERONOMY 27:19"> Thou shalt not do that, that is wicked, neither thou shalt deem unjustly; behold thou not the person of a poor man, neither honour thou the face of a mighty man; deem thou justly to thy neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 23:1; EXODUS 23:7; PROVERBS 20:19"> Thou shalt not be a slanderer, that is, a false accuser, neither a privy backbiter in the people; thou shalt not stand against the blood of thy neighbour; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:9; 1ST JOHN 2:11; LUKE 17:3"> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart, but reprove thou him openly, lest thou have sin on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 13:9; MATTHEW 22:39–40; MARK 12:31–34"> Thou shalt not seek vengeance, neither thou shalt be mindful of the wrong of thy citizens; thou shalt love thy friend as thyself; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14–17; DEUTERONOMY 22:9–11; MATTHEW 9:16–17"> Keep ye my laws. Thou shalt not make thy beasts to engender with the beasts of another kind. Thou shalt not sow a field with diverse seed. Thou shalt not be clothed in a cloth which is woven of two things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:23–24; EXODUS 21:20–21"> If a man sleepeth with a woman by fleshly knowing of seed, which woman is an handmaid, or bond, yea, a noble woman of kin, and nevertheless is not again-bought by price, neither rewarded with freedom, she shall be beaten &#91;or both shall be scourged&#93;, and they shall not die, for she was not free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:6–7; LEVITICUS 5:1–6; LEVITICUS 5:15"> Soothly the man for his trespass shall offer a ram to the Lord, at the door of the tabernacle of witnessing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:20"> and the priest shall pray for him, and for his trespass, before the Lord; and the Lord shall be merciful to him, and the sin shall be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; JEREMIAH 9:25–26"> When ye have entered into the land of promise, and have planted therein apple trees, ye shall do away the first fruits; the apples which those trees bring forth shall be unclean to you, neither ye shall eat of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:9; DEUTERONOMY 18:4; NUMBERS 18:12–13"> Forsooth in the fourth year all the fruit of the trees shall be hallowed and be praiseable to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HAGGAI 1:9–11; MALACHI 3:8–10; LEVITICUS 26:3–4"> forsooth in the fifth year ye shall eat the fruits, and ye shall gather apples, which those trees bring forth; I am your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:10–14; 2ND KI 17:17; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:6"> Ye shall not eat flesh with &#91;the&#93; blood. Ye shall not make vain divining, neither ye shall keep dreams;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:5; DEUTERONOMY 14:1; EZEKIEL 44:20"> neither ye shall clip the hair round, neither ye shall shave the beard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:1; LEVITICUS 21:5; 1ST KINGS 18:28"> and on dead men ye shall not cut your flesh, neither ye shall make to you any figures, either marks in your flesh; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:17–18; LEVITICUS 21:7; HOSEA 4:12–14"> Set thou not thy daughter to do lechery for hire, and the land be defouled, and be &#91;full-&#93;filled with sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:2; LEVITICUS 19:3; ECCLESIASTES 5:1"> Keep ye my sabbaths, and dread ye my saintuary; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:19; LEVITICUS 20:27; LEVITICUS 20:6–7"> Bow ye not to astronomers or astrologers, neither ask ye anything of false diviners, that ye be defouled by them; I am your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:1; LAMENTATIONS 5:12; PROVERBS 16:31"> Rise thou before an hoar head, and honour thou the person of an eld &#91;or old&#93; man, and dread thou thy Lord God; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 23:9; EXODUS 22:21; MALACHI 3:5"> If a comeling dwelleth in your land, and abideth among you, despise ye not him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:19; LEVITICUS 19:18; EXODUS 12:48–49"> but be he among you as a man born in the land; and ye shall love him as yourself, for also ye were comelings in the land of Egypt; I am your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:15; DEUTERONOMY 25:13; PROVERBS 16:11"> Do not ye do any wicked thing in doom, in rule, in weight, and in measure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:1; EXODUS 20:2; DEUTERONOMY 25:13–15"> the balance be just &#91;or right&#93;, and the weights be even, the bushel be just &#91;or right&#93;, and the pint be even; I am your Lord God, that led you out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:1; LEVITICUS 18:4–5; DEUTERONOMY 5:1"> Keep ye all my behests, and all my dooms, and do ye them; I am the Lord.</span>
