</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:23; EXODUS 23:24; 1ST PETER 4:2–4"> ye shall not do by the custom of the land of Egypt, in which ye dwelled; ye shall not do by the custom of the land of Canaan, to which I shall bring you in, neither ye shall go in the lawful things of them, that is, in their custom of worshipping.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:2; LEVITICUS 18:26; EZEKIEL 20:19"> Ye shall do my dooms, and ye shall keep my behests, and ye shall go in them; I am your Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 10:5; EZEKIEL 20:11; GALATIANS 3:12"> Keep ye my laws and dooms, which a man shall do, and he shall live in those &#91;or them&#93;; I am your Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:17–21; LEVITICUS 18:7–19; LEVITICUS 20:11–12"> A man shall not nigh to a nigh woman of his blood, that he show her filthhood or nakedness; I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:11; EZEKIEL 22:10; LEVITICUS 18:8–16"> Thou shalt not discover the filth-hood of thy father, and the filthhood of thy mother; she is thy mother, thou shalt not show her filthhood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:1; DEUTERONOMY 27:20"> Thou shalt not uncover the filth-hood of the wife of thy father, for it is the filthhood of thy father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:17; DEUTERONOMY 27:22; EZEKIEL 22:11"> Thou shalt not show the filthhood of thy sister, of father, either of mother, which sister is begotten at home, that is, in wedlock, either withoutforth, that is, out of wedlock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Thou shalt not show the filthhood of the daughter of thy son, either of thy niece, that is, the daughter of thy daughter, for it is thy filthhood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thou shalt not show the filthhood of the daughter of the wife of thy father, which she childed to thy father, and she is thy sister.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:19; EXODUS 6:20"> Thou shalt not open the filthhood of thy father’s sister, for she is the flesh of thy father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou shalt not show the filthhood of the sister of thy mother, for she is the flesh of thy mother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:20"> Thou shalt not show the filthhood of the brother of thy father, neither thou shalt nigh to his wife, that is joined to thee by affinity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:12; GENESIS 38:26; EZEKIEL 22:11"> Thou shalt not show the filthhood of thy son’s wife, for she is the wife of thy son, neither thou shalt discover her shame; and no man take his brother’s wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5; LEVITICUS 20:21; MATTHEW 22:24"> Thou shalt not show the filth-hood of thy brother’s wife, for it is the filthhood of thy brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:14; AMOS 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 27:23"> Thou shalt not show the filthhood of a woman, and of her daughter; thou shalt not take the daughter of her son, and the daughter of her daughter, that thou show her shame; they be the flesh of her, and such lechery is incest, that is, lechery of them that be kin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MALACHI 2:15; GENESIS 30:15; GENESIS 29:28"> Thou shalt not take the sister of thy wife, to anguish of her, neither thou shalt show her filthhood, while thy wife liveth yet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:18; LEVITICUS 15:24; EZEKIEL 22:10"> Thou shalt not nigh to a woman that suffereth the running of blood of month, neither thou shalt show her filthhood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 20:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9; HEBREWS 13:4"> Thou shalt not do lechery with thy neighbour’s wife, neither thou shalt be defouled with meddling &#91;or mingling together&#93; of seed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:31; LEVITICUS 20:2–5; DEUTERONOMY 18:10"> Thou shalt not give of thy seed, that it be offered to the idol Moloch, neither thou shalt defoul the name of thy God; I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:13; ROMANS 1:26–27; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9"> Thou shalt not be meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; with a man, by lechery of a woman, for it is abomination.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 22:19; LEVITICUS 20:15–16; LEVITICUS 20:12"> Thou shalt not do lechery with any beast, neither thou shalt be defouled with it. A woman shall not lie under a beast, neither shall be meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; therewith, that is, defouled by fleshly knowing thereof, for it is great sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:12; LEVITICUS 18:30; LEVITICUS 20:22–23"> Be ye not defouled in all these things, in which all folks, either heathen men, be defouled, which folks I shall cast out before your sight,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:28; LEVITICUS 20:22–23; JEREMIAH 2:7"> of whom the land is defouled, of which land I shall visit the great sins, that it vomit, or throw out, his dwellers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:5; LEVITICUS 17:10; LEVITICUS 18:30"> Keep ye my lawful things, and my dooms, that ye do not any of all these abominations, as well a man born in the land, as a comeling that is a pilgrim with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:14; LEVITICUS 18:24; DEUTERONOMY 23:18"> For the dwellers of the land, that were before you, did all these abominations, and defouled that land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:16; LEVITICUS 20:22; LEVITICUS 18:25"> Therefore beware, lest it cast out vilely you in the same manner, when ye shall do such sins, as it casted out vilely the folk, that was before you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:6; EXODUS 12:15; LEVITICUS 17:10"> Each man that shall do anything of these abominations, shall perish from the midst of his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:23; DEUTERONOMY 18:9–12; LEVITICUS 22:9"> Keep ye my behests; do not ye do those things, which they that were before you did, and be ye not defouled in those &#91;or them&#93;; I am your Lord God.</span>
</div>
