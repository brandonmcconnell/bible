<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses and Aaron, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:11; LEVITICUS 11:11; HEBREWS 9:10"> Say ye to the sons of Israel, Keep ye all things which I wrote to you, that I be your God. These be the beasts, which ye shall eat, of all the living beasts of earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:17; DEUTERONOMY 6:6–7"> ye shall eat all things among beasts that have the claw parted, and cheweth the cud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> soothly whatever thing cheweth cud, and hath a claw, but parteth not it, as a camel, and other beasts, ye shall not eat it, and ye shall areckon it among unclean things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 104:18; PROVERBS 30:26; MATTHEW 7:26"> A coney, which cheweth cud, and parteth not the claw, is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:7"> and an hare, for also he cheweth cud, but parteth not the claw;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:17; ISAIAH 65:4; ISAIAH 66:3"> and a swine, that cheweth not the cud, though he parteth the claw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:11; COLOSSIANS 2:16; HEBREWS 9:10"> Ye shall not eat the flesh of these beasts, neither ye shall touch their dead bodies, for those &#91;or they&#93; be unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:6; JAMES 2:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:21"> Also these things be that be engendered in waters, and &#91;it&#93; is leaveful to eat; ye shall eat all things that have fins and scales, as well in the sea, as in &#91;the&#93; fresh floods, and standing waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:27; LEVITICUS 7:18; PSALMS 139:21–22"> soothly whatever thing of them that be moved and live in waters, hath not fins and scales, shall be abominable, and loathsome to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and ye shall not eat the flesh of those &#91;or them&#93;, and ye shall eschew their bodies dead by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> All things in &#91;the&#93; waters that have not fins and scales, shall be polluted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 39:27–30; JEREMIAH 4:22; TITUS 3:3"> These things be of fowls which ye shall not eat, and shall be eschewed &#91;or shunned&#93; of you; an eagle, and a gripe, &#91;and&#93; an aliet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and a kite, and a vulture by his kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 12:24; 1ST KINGS 17:6; PROVERBS 30:17"> and all the kind of ravens by his likeness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 34:11–15; EPHESIANS 5:7–11; EPHESIANS 2:2–3"> a struthio, and a night crow, &#91;and&#93; a lari, or a coot, and a hawk by his kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> an owl, and a dipper, and ciconia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> a swan, and a cormorant, and a pelican;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> a falcon, &#91;and&#93; a jay by his kind; &#91;and&#93; a lapwing, and a rearmouse, or a bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:10; PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; LEVITICUS 11:23"> All thing of fowls or insects that goeth on four feet, shall be abominable to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> soothly whatever thing goeth on four feet, but hath longer hips behind, by which it skippeth on the earth, ye shall eat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:4; MARK 1:6; EXODUS 10:4–5"> as is a bruchus, that is, the fruit of locusts before it hath wings, in his kind, and accatus, that is, the fruit of locusts when it beginneth to have wings, and ophimachus, &#91;that is, a foul enemy to serpents&#93;, and a locust, all by their kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Forsooth whatever thing of birds or insects hath four feet only, it shall be abominable to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33; EPHESIANS 2:1–3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:17"> and whoever toucheth their bodies dead by themselves, shall be polluted, or defouled, and shall be unclean till to eventide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:5; LEVITICUS 14:8; NUMBERS 31:24"> and if it is need, that he bear any dead thing of these &#91;or them&#93;, he shall wash his clothes, and he shall be unclean till to the going down of the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Soothly each beast that hath a claw, but parteth not it, neither cheweth cud, shall be unclean; and whatever thing toucheth it, shall be defouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:23; LEVITICUS 11:20"> That that goeth on hands, of all beasts that go on four feet, shall be unclean; he that toucheth their bodies dead by themselves, shall be defouled till to eventide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:24–25"> and he, that beareth such dead bodies, shall wash his clothes, and he shall be unclean till to eventide; for all these things be unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:41–42; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:2–5; PSALMS 10:3"> Also these things shall be areckoned among defouled things, of these things that be moved on earth; a weasel, and a mouse, and a crocodile, each after his kind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> a migale, a chameleon, and &#91;a&#93; stellion, and a lacert, and a mouldwarp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:24–25; LEVITICUS 11:8"> All these be unclean; he that toucheth their bodies dead by them-selves, shall be unclean till to eventide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:12; TITUS 3:5; TITUS 2:14"> and that thing shall be defouled, on which anything of their bodies dead by themselves falleth, as well a vessel of wood, and a cloth, as skins, or pilches, either hair-shirts; and in whatever thing work is made, it shall be dipped in water, and those things shall be defouled till to eventide, and so afterward they shall be cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:28; LEVITICUS 15:12; LEVITICUS 14:45"> Soothly a vessel of earth, in which anything of these falleth within, shall be defouled, and therefore it shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="TITUS 1:15; PROVERBS 21:4; PROVERBS 21:27"> Each meat, that ye shall eat, shall be unclean, if water of such a vessel is poured out thereon; and each flowing thing, that is drunken of such a vessel, shall be unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:12; LEVITICUS 6:28; LEVITICUS 11:33"> and whatever thing of such dead bodies by themselves shall fall upon, it shall be unclean, whether furnaces, or kettles standing upon three feet, they shall be destroyed, and shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:1; JOHN 4:14"> Soothly wells and cisterns, and all the gatherings together of waters, shall be clean. He that toucheth their body dead by itself, shall be defouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:37; 1ST PETER 1:23; 1ST JOHN 5:18"> If it falleth upon seed, it shall not defoul the seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> soothly if any man sheddeth &#91;or pour&#93; out &#91;the&#93; seed with water, and afterward the water is touched with dead bodies by themselves, it shall be defouled anon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:40; LEVITICUS 11:24; LEVITICUS 15:7"> If a beast is dead, which it is leaveful to you to eat, he that toucheth the dead body thereof shall be unclean till to eventide;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:8; EZEKIEL 44:31; DEUTERONOMY 14:21"> and he that eateth thereof anything, either beareth it, shall wash his clothes, and shall be unclean till to eventide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:29; LEVITICUS 11:20; LEVITICUS 11:23"> All thing that creepeth upon earth, shall be abominable, neither it shall be into meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="TITUS 1:12; ISAIAH 65:25; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:3"> Whatever thing goeth upon the breast, and on four feet, and hath many feet, either is drawn by the earth, ye shall not eat it, for it is abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:25; LEVITICUS 11:41–42"> Do not ye defoul your souls, neither touch ye anything of them, lest ye be unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="EXODUS 20:2; 1ST PETER 1:15–16; LEVITICUS 19:2"> for I am your Lord God; be ye holy, for I am holy. Defoul ye not your souls in each creeping thing that is moved upon earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:44; EXODUS 20:2; EXODUS 6:7"> for I am the Lord, that led you out of the land of Egypt, that I should be to you into God; ye shall be holy, for I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:54; LEVITICUS 15:32; EZEKIEL 43:12"> This is the law of living beasts, and of fowls, and of each living thing that is moved in water, and creepeth in earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10; EZEKIEL 44:23; ROMANS 14:13–23"> that ye know the differences of clean thing and unclean, and that ye know what ye shall eat, and what ye owe to forsake.</span>
