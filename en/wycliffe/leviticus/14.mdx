<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 17:14; LUKE 5:12–14; MARK 1:40–44"> This is the custom of a leprous man, when he shall be cleansed. He shall be brought to the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:46; LUKE 7:22; LUKE 17:15–19"> the which priest shall go out of the tents, and when he shall find that the leprosy is cleansed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:6; LEVITICUS 14:6; HEBREWS 9:19"> he shall command to the man that is cleansed, that he offer for himself two quick sparrows, which is leaveful to eat, and cedar wood, and vermilion, that is, a red thread, and hyssop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:50; HEBREWS 2:14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:1"> And the priest shall command that one of the sparrows be offered in an earthen vessel upon quick waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:5; ZECHARIAH 13:1; PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11"> soothly he shall dip the tother sparrow quick, with the cedar wood, and with the red thread, and hyssop, in the blood of the sparrow offered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 5:14; EZEKIEL 36:25; ISAIAH 52:15"> with which he shall sprinkle seven times him that shall be cleansed, that he be purged rightfully; and he shall deliver the quick sparrow, that it fly &#91;away&#93; into the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; NUMBERS 8:7; 1ST PETER 3:21"> And when the man hath washed his clothes, he shall shave all the hairs of his body, and he shall be washed in water, and he shall be cleansed, and he shall enter into the tents; so only that he dwell without his tabernacle by seven days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:9; LEVITICUS 14:8; NUMBERS 8:7"> and that in the seventh day, he shave the hairs of the head, and his beard, and his brows, and the hairs of all his body. And when his clothes and his body be washed again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:1; MATTHEW 8:4; LUKE 5:14"> in the eighth day he shall take two lambs without wem, and a sheep of one year without wem, and three dimes, or three tenth parts, of &#91;tried&#93;wheat flour, into sacrifice, which be sprinkled with oil, and by itself a sextary, or a pint, of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:26–27; EXODUS 29:1–4; NUMBERS 8:21"> And when the priest that purgeth the man, hath set him and all his things before the Lord, in the door of the tabernacle of witnessing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 29:24; LEVITICUS 6:6–7; LEVITICUS 8:27–29"> he shall take a lamb, and shall offer it for trespass, and shall offer the pint of oil; and when all things be offered before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 29:11; LEVITICUS 1:11; LEVITICUS 4:24"> he shall offer the lamb, where the sacrifice for sin and the burnt sacrifice is wont to be offered, that is, in the holy place; for as for sin, so and for trespass, the offering pertaineth to the priest; it is holy of holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 29:20; LEVITICUS 8:23–24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:1"> And the priest shall take of the blood of &#91;the&#93; sacrifice which is offered for trespass, and shall put on the last part of the right ear of him which is cleansed, and on the thumbs of the right hand and foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 45:7; JOHN 3:34; 1ST JOHN 2:20"> And he shall put of the pint of oil into his left hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:31; LEVITICUS 4:6"> and the priest shall dip his right finger therein, and he shall sprinkle it seven times before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:2; EZEKIEL 36:27; LEVITICUS 14:14"> Soothly he shall pour that that is left of the oil in the left hand, on the last part of the right ear of him which is cleansed, and on the thumbs of the right hand and foot, and on the blood which is shed for trespass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:21–22; EPHESIANS 1:17–18"> and on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:12; LEVITICUS 5:1; LEVITICUS 12:6–8"> And the priest shall pray for him before the Lord, and shall make sacrifice for sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:10; EPHESIANS 5:2; LEVITICUS 14:8"> then the priest shall offer the burnt sacrifice, and he shall put it in the altar with his flowing sacrifices, and the man shall be cleansed rightfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:7; LEVITICUS 12:8; PROVERBS 17:5"> That if he is poor, and his hand may not find those things that be said, he shall take for his trespass a lamb to &#91;the&#93; offering, that the priest pray for him, and the tenth part of &#91;tried&#93;wheat flour sprinkled altogether with oil, into sacrifice, and a pint of oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:16; ISAIAH 59:11; JEREMIAH 48:28"> and two turtles, either two culver birds, of which one shall be for sin, and the tother into burnt sacrifice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:10–11"> and he shall offer those &#91;or them&#93; in the eighth day of his cleansing to the priest, at the door of the tabernacle of witnessing, before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:12; LEVITICUS 14:10"> And the priest shall take the lamb offered for trespass, and the pint of oil, and he shall raise or rear them up together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:1; PSALMS 40:6; LEVITICUS 14:14–20"> and when the lamb is offered, he shall put of the blood thereof on the last part of the right ear of him that is cleansed, and on the thumbs of his right hand and foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Soothly the priest put the part of oil into his own left hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> in which he shall dip the finger of his right hand, and he shall sprinkle it seven times against or before the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the priest shall touch the last part of the right ear of him that is cleansed, and the thumbs of the right hand and foot, in the place of &#91;the&#93; blood which is shed out for trespass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:18; 1ST JOHN 2:1–2; EXODUS 30:15–16"> Soothly the priest shall put the tother part of &#91;the&#93; oil, that is in his left hand, upon the head of the man that is cleansed, that he please the Lord for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:22; ROMANS 8:3; LEVITICUS 12:8"> And he shall offer a turtle, or a culver bird,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:18; LEVITICUS 5:7"> one for trespass, and the tother into burnt sacrifice, with their flowing offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:10; LEVITICUS 14:21; MATTHEW 11:5"> This is the sacrifice of a leprous man, that may not have all things into the cleansing of himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And the Lord spake to Moses and Aaron, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:49; GENESIS 17:8; NUMBERS 32:22"> When ye have entered into the land of Canaan, which I shall give to you into possession, if the wound of leprosy is in the houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 91:10; DEUTERONOMY 7:26; ZECHARIAH 5:4"> he shall go, whose the house is, and shall tell to the priest, and shall say, It seemeth to me, that as it were a wound of leprosy is in mine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:4; HEBREWS 12:15; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:17–18"> And the priest shall command, that they bear out of the house all things, before that he enter into it, that he may see whether it be leprosy, lest all things that be in the house be made unclean; and the priest shall enter afterward, that he see the leprosy of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:42; LEVITICUS 13:3; LEVITICUS 13:19–20"> And when he seeth in the walls thereof as little valleys, or crevices, defouled with paleness, either with redness, and lower than the tother higher part,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:50"> he shall go out at the door of the house, and anon he shall enclose it by seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:36; LEVITICUS 13:27; LEVITICUS 13:22"> And he shall turn again in the seventh day, and shall see it; &#91;and&#93; if he findeth that the leprosy hath increased,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:20; TITUS 3:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:5–6"> he shall command that the stones be cast out, in which the leprosy is, and that those stones be cast out of the city into an unclean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:15; MATTHEW 24:51"> Soothly he shall command that that house be razed within by compass, and that the dust of the razing be sprinkled without the city, in an unclean place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:2; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:21–22; 2ND CHRONICLES 17:7–9"> and that other stones be put again for these, that be taken away, and that the house be daubed with other mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:28–30; JUDE 1:12; 2ND PETER 2:20"> But if after that the stones be taken away, and the dust is borne out, and &#91;with&#93; other earth is daubed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:51–52; ZECHARIAH 5:4"> the priest entereth, and seeth the leprosy turned again, and the walls sprinkled with spots, the leprosy is then steadfastly dwelling, and the house is unclean;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2ND KI 10:27; MATTHEW 24:2; 1ST KINGS 9:6–9"> which house they shall destroy anon, and they shall cast out of the city, into an unclean place, the stones thereof, and the wood, and all the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:21–22; LEVITICUS 22:6; LEVITICUS 11:28"> He that entereth into the house, when it is shut, shall be unclean till to eventide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:8–9; LEVITICUS 11:25"> and he that sleepeth &#91;in it&#93;, and eateth anything therein, he shall wash his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:11; JOB 5:18; MARK 5:29"> That if the priest entereth, and seeth that the leprosy increased not in the house, after that it was daubed the second time, the priest shall cleanse it; for health is yielded &#91;again&#93;thereto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:4"> And to the cleansing thereof, the priest shall take two sparrows, and cedar wood, and vermilion, that is, a red thread, and hyssop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And when one sparrow is offered in a vessel of earth, on quick waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PSALMS 51:7"> the priest shall take the cedar wood, and hyssop, and the red thread, and the quick sparrow, and he shall dip, or wet, all these things in the blood of the sparrow offered, or slain, and in the quick waters; and he shall sprinkle the house seven times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> and he shall cleanse it as well in the blood of the sparrow, as in the living waters, and in the quick sparrow, and in the cedar wood, and in the hyssop, and red thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:20; LEVITICUS 14:7"> And when he hath let go the sparrow to fly away into the field freely, he shall pray for the house, and it shall be cleansed rightfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:32; LEVITICUS 14:32; LEVITICUS 6:9"> This is the law of all leprosy, and of smiting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:47–59"> &#91;and&#93; of leprosy of clothes, and of houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:2"> &#91;and&#93; of the sign of wound, and of little whelks breaking out, &#91;and&#93; of spot shining, and in colours changed into diverse spots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10; EZEKIEL 44:23; JEREMIAH 15:19"> that it may be known, what is clean, or unclean.</span>
