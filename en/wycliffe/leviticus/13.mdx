<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord spake to Moses and Aaron, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:8; LEVITICUS 14:56; LEVITICUS 14:3"> A man in whose skin and flesh riseth diverse colour, either whelk, either as some shining thing, that is, a wound &#91;or a plague&#93; of leprosy, he shall be brought to Aaron the priest, either to one of any of his sons;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 3:19–20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:28; MALACHI 2:7"> and when he seeth the leprosy, or meselry, in the skin, and the hair changed into white colour, and that the species of leprosy is lower than the other skin and the flesh, it is a wound &#91;or a plague&#93; of leprosy, and he shall be separated at the doom of the priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:24"> Soothly if the shining whiteness that is in the skin, neither is lower than the tother flesh, and the hairs be of the former colour, the priest shall enclose him seven days;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and the priest shall behold him in the seventh day, and soothly if the leprosy wax not further, neither passeth the former terms in the flesh, again the priest shall enclose him again seven other days;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; LEVITICUS 14:8; DEUTERONOMY 32:5"> and he shall behold him in the seventh day; if the leprosy is then dark, and waxeth not in the flesh, the priest shall cleanse him, that is, shall deem him to be clean, for it is a scab; and the man shall wash his clothes, and he shall be clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 38:3; LEVITICUS 13:35–36; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:16–17"> That if the leprosy waxeth again, after that he is seen of the priest, and is yielded to cleanness, he shall be brought again to the priest,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:7–8; PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:21"> and he shall be deemed to be of uncleanness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If the wound &#91;or the plague&#93; of leprosy is in a man, he shall be brought to the priest,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND KI 5:27; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:19–20; LEVITICUS 13:14–15"> and he shall see the man; and when white colour is in the flesh, and it changeth the sight, or former colour, of &#91;the&#93; hairs, and that flesh appeareth quick, or waxing,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> it shall be deemed the eldest &#91;or most old&#93; leprosy, and grown to the skin; therefore the priest shall defoul him, that is, deem him to be foul, and the priest shall not enclose him again, for it is of open uncleanness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:8–10; JOB 42:6; ROMANS 7:14"> But if the leprosy running about in the skin flowereth out, and covereth all the flesh, from the head till to the feet, whatever thing falleth under the sight of eyes;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 9:41; ISAIAH 64:6"> the priest shall behold him, and he shall deem him to be holden with the cleanest leprosy, for all the skin is turned into whiteness, and therefore the man shall be clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Soothly when quick flesh appeareth in him, he shall be defouled</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> by the doom of the priest, and he shall be areckoned among unclean men; for quick flesh is unclean, if it is sprinkled with leprosy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:14–16; ROMANS 7:14–24; PHILIPPIANS 3:6–8"> That if the flesh is turned again into whiteness, and covereth all the man,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the priest shall behold him, and shall deem that he is clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 9:9; ISAIAH 38:21; PSALMS 38:3–7"> The flesh and the skin, in which a botch is bred, and is healed,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:24"> and the place of the botch, or a fell sore, appeareth white, either red, the man shall be brought to the priest;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 5:14; LEVITICUS 13:3; 2ND PETER 2:20"> and when the priest seeth the place of the leprosy lower than the other flesh, and the hairs turned into whiteness, the priest shall defoul him, that is, deem him foul; for the wound &#91;or the plague&#93; of leprosy is bred &#91;or sprung&#93; in the botch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 5:5"> That if the hair is of the former colour, and the sign of the wound is some-deal dark, and is not lower than the flesh beside, the priest shall enclose the man seven days;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and soothly, if his sore waxeth, the priest shall deem the man to be a leper;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:1; JOB 34:31–32; MATTHEW 26:75"> forsooth if it standeth in his place, it is a sign of a botch, and the man shall be clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:24"> Flesh, and skin, which the fire hath burnt, and is healed, and hath a white, either red, sign of wound, the priest shall behold it,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:18–20; LEVITICUS 13:4"> and lo! if it is turned into white-ness, and the place thereof is lower than the tother skin, the priest shall defoul the man, for a wound &#91;or the plague&#93; of leprosy is bred &#91;or sprung&#93; in the sign of wound.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:23; LEVITICUS 13:4–5"> That if the colour of &#91;the&#93; hairs is not changed, neither the wound, or soreness, is lower than the tother flesh, and that species of leprosy is some-deal dark, the priest shall enclose the man seven days;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and in the seventh day he shall behold him; if the leprosy waxeth in the flesh, the priest shall defoul the man;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> else if the whiteness standeth in his place, and is not clear enough, it is a wound, or soreness, of burning, and therefore the man shall be cleansed, for it is a sign of burning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:15; MATTHEW 13:14–15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:3–4"> A man or a woman, in whose head or beard leprosy burgeoneth,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:34–37; LEVITICUS 14:54"> the priest shall see them; and if the place is lower than the tother flesh, and the hair is white, and is subtler, either smaller, than it is wont, the priest shall defoul them, for it is leprosy of the head, and of the beard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Else if he seeth the place of the wem, or the sore, even with the nigh flesh, and the hair black, the priest shall enclose them seven days,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:30; MATTHEW 23:5; LUKE 18:9–12"> and he shall see them in the seventh day; if the wem waxeth not, and the hair is of his colour, and the place of wound is even with the tother flesh,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> the man shall be shaven, without the place of the wem, and he shall be enclosed again by seven other days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JUDE 1:22; 1ST JOHN 4:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:2"> If in the seventh day the wound, or soreness, is seen to have stand in his place, neither is lower than the tother flesh, the priest shall cleanse the man; and when his clothes be washed, he shall be clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:27; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:16–17; LEVITICUS 13:7"> Else if after the cleansing, a spot waxeth again in the skin,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> the priest shall no more inquire, whether the hair is changed into white colour, for apertly he is unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Soothly if the spot standeth still, and the hairs be black, know then the priest that the man is healed, and trustily pronounce he the man clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> A man or a woman, in whose skin whiteness appeareth,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ROMANS 7:22–25; JAMES 3:2; ECCLESIASTES 7:20"> the priest shall behold them; if he perceiveth, that whiteness some-deal dark shineth in the skin, know he, that it is no leprosy, but a spot of white colour, and that the man is clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:13; ROMANS 8:10; SONG OF SONGS 5:11"> A man of whose head the hairs float away, he is bald, and clean;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> and if the hairs fall from the forehead, he is bald, and is clean;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> else if in the baldness before, either in the baldness behind, white either red colour is bred, or is sprung up,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and the priest seeth this, he shall condemn the man, without doubt of leprosy, which is bred in the baldness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:1–2; MATTHEW 6:23; ISAIAH 1:5"> Therefore whoever is defouled with leprosy, and is separated from other men, at the doom of the priest,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MICAH 3:7; LEVITICUS 10:6; EZEKIEL 24:17"> he shall have his clothes unsewed, and his head bare, and his mouth covered with a cloth, &#91;and&#93; he shall cry himself defouled, and vile;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2ND KI 7:3; 2ND KI 15:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:21"> in all the time that he is leprous and unclean, he shall dwell alone, without the tents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JUDE 1:23; ROMANS 13:12; EZEKIEL 16:16"> A woollen cloth, either linen,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:51; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:4; DEUTERONOMY 8:11"> that hath leprosy in the warp, either woof, either certainly a skin, or a pilch, either whatever thing is made of skin,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> if it is corrupted with a white spot, either red, it shall be areckoned leprosy, and it shall be showed to the priest;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:23"> the which when he hath beheld, shall enclose it up seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:44"> And again he shall behold it in the seventh day, and if he perceiveth, that the leprosy therein hath waxed, it shall be deemed &#91;a&#93; continual leprosy; he shall deem that cloth defouled, and all thing in which it is found;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:35; LEVITICUS 11:33; LEVITICUS 14:44–45"> and therefore the cloth shall be burnt with flames of fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And if the priest seeth that the spot hath waxen not,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> he shall command, and they shall wash that thing wherein the leprosy is, and he shall enclose it again seven other days;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:13; HEBREWS 6:4–8; 2ND PETER 2:20–22"> and when he seeth the former like-ness not changed again, nevertheless that neither the leprosy hath waxed, he shall deem that thing unclean, and he shall burn it in fire, for the leprosy is shed &#91;or spread&#93; in the over-part of that cloth, either through&#91;out&#93; it all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Else if the place of &#91;the&#93; leprosy is darker, after that the cloth is washed, he shall break away that dark place, and he shall part it from the whole.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8; MATTHEW 3:12"> That if fleeing leprosy and unstead-fast appeareth furthermore in these places, that were unwemmed before, it oweth to be burnt in fire;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="2ND KI 5:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:5; PSALMS 51:2"> if it ceaseth, he shall wash the second time those things that be clean, and they shall be clean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> This is the law of leprosy of a cloth, woollen and linen, of warp and woof, and of all purtenance of skin, how it oweth to be cleansed, either to be defouled.</span>
</div>
