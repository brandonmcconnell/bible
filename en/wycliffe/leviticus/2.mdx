<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:14–18; LEVITICUS 9:17; ISAIAH 66:20"> When a soul, that is, a poor man, offereth an offering of sacrifice to the Lord, &#91;tried&#93; flour of wheat shall be his offering. And he shall pour oil thereon, and he shall put incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:12; LEVITICUS 6:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:4"> and he shall bear it to the sons of Aaron, &#91;the&#93; priest, of the which sons one of them shall take an handful of tried wheat flour, and of oil, and all the incense; and he shall put these as a memorial on the altar, into the sweetest odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:12–13; NUMBERS 18:9; LEVITICUS 21:22"> Forsooth that that is left of the sacrifice shall be Aaron’s and his sons’, the holy of holy things of offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 29:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:7–8; PSALMS 22:14"> Forsooth when thou offerest a sacrifice baked in an oven, of tried wheat flour, that is, loaves without sourdough, sprinkled with oil, and therf bread sodden in water, balmed with oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:21; LEVITICUS 7:9"> if thine offering is of tried wheat flour sprinkled with oil, and without sourdough, taken of the frying pan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 18:1–19; PSALMS 22:1–21; MARK 14:1–15"> thou shalt part it in small parts, and thou shalt pour oil thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:1–2; LEVITICUS 7:9"> Else if the sacrifice is taken of the griddle, evenly the &#91;tried&#93;wheat flour shall be sprinkled with oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> the which wheat flour thou shalt offer to the Lord, and thou shalt betake it in the hands of the priest. And when he hath offered it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:2; PHILIPPIANS 4:18; LEVITICUS 6:15"> he shall take a memorial of the sacrifice, and he shall burn it on the altar, into odour of sweetness to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3"> Soothly whatever thing is left, it shall be Aaron’s and his sons, the holy of holy things of the offerings to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:9; LUKE 12:1; EXODUS 23:18"> Each offering which is offered to the Lord, shall be without sourdough, neither anything of sourdough, and of honey, shall be burnt in the sacrifice of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 23:19; GENESIS 23:10–11; EXODUS 22:29"> Ye shall offer only the first fruits of those &#91;or them&#93;, and gifts; soothly those &#91;or they&#93; shall not be put on the altar, into odour of sweetness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:24; MATTHEW 5:13; COLOSSIANS 4:6"> Whatever thing of sacrifice thou shalt offer, thou shalt make it savory with salt, neither thou shalt take away the salt of the bond of peace of thy God from thy sacrifice; in each offering thou shalt offer salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:10; MALACHI 1:11; NUMBERS 28:2"> Forsooth if thou offerest a gift of the first things of thy fruits to the Lord, of ears of corn yet green, thou shalt scorch, or singe, them in fire, and thou shalt break them in the manner of bruised corn; and so thou shalt offer thy first fruits to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and thou shalt pour oil there-upon, and thou shalt put incense, for it is the offering of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:7; ROMANS 8:26–27; ISAIAH 61:1"> Of which the priest shall burn, into mind of the gift, a part of the bruised corn, and of the oil, and all the incense.</span>
