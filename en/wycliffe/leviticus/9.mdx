<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:1; EZEKIEL 43:26–27; LEVITICUS 15:14"> Forsooth when the eighth day was made, Moses called Aaron, and his sons, and the greater men in birth of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:3; HEBREWS 10:10–14; LEVITICUS 8:14"> and he said to Aaron, Take thou of the drove a calf for sin, and a ram for burnt sacrifice, ever either without wem, and offer thou them before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZRA 6:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:9; TITUS 2:14"> And thou shalt speak to the sons of Israel, Take ye a buck of goats for sin, and a calf, and a lamb, of one year, and without wem, into burnt sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 29:43; EZEKIEL 43:2; LEVITICUS 9:23"> an ox and a ram for peaceable things; and offer ye them before the Lord, and offer ye &#91;tried&#93;wheat flour sprinkled with oil in the sacrifice of each of them; for today the Lord shall appear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 5:2–3; EXODUS 19:17; NEHEMIAH 8:1"> Therefore they took all things, which Moses commanded, to the door of the tabernacle, where, when all the multitude stood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:23; EZEKIEL 43:2; EXODUS 24:16"> Moses said, This is the word which the Lord commanded, do ye it, and his glory shall appear to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:3; HEBREWS 5:1; LEVITICUS 4:3"> And Moses said to Aaron, Nigh thou to the altar, and offer thou for thy sin; offer thou burnt sacrifice, and pray for thee, and for the people; and when thou hast slain the sacrifice of the people, pray thou for them, as the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:1–12; LEVITICUS 1:4–5; LEVITICUS 4:29"> And anon Aaron nighed to the altar, and offered a calf for his sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:6–7; LEVITICUS 8:15; LEVITICUS 4:25"> whose blood his sons offered, or brought, to him, in which blood Aaron dipped his finger, and he touched the horns of the altar, and he poured the blood that was left at the foundament of the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:16–17; PROVERBS 23:26; LEVITICUS 3:9–11"> and he burnt upon the altar the inner fatness, and the little reins, and the caul of the maw, as the Lord commanded to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:17; LEVITICUS 4:11–12; LEVITICUS 16:27–28"> Forsooth Aaron burnt with fire without the tents the flesh and the skin thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:2; EPHESIANS 5:25–27; LEVITICUS 1:1–17"> And he offered the beast of burnt sacrifice, and his sons brought to him the blood thereof, which he shedded &#91;or poured&#93; by compass of the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> they offered also that sacrifice cut into gobbets, with the head, and all the members; and he burnt by fire all these things upon the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:21"> when the entrails and the feet were washed before with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:27–31; HEBREWS 5:3; LEVITICUS 9:3"> And he offered and killed a buck of goats, for the sin of the people; and when the altar was cleansed, he made burnt sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:12–14; LEVITICUS 8:18–21; HEBREWS 10:1–22"> and he added into the sacrifice flowing offerings, that be offered together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:1–2; JOHN 6:53; LEVITICUS 9:1"> and he burnt those &#91;or them&#93; on the altar, without the ceremonies of &#91;the&#93; burnt sacrifice of the morrowtide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:20; ROMANS 5:1; EPHESIANS 2:14–17"> He offered also an ox, and a ram, &#91;the&#93; peaceable sacrifices of the people; and his sons offered to him the blood, the which he poured by compass of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:16; LEVITICUS 9:10; LEVITICUS 3:9"> Forsooth they putted &#91;or put&#93; on the breasts the inner fatness of the ox, and the tail of the ram, and the little reins with their fatnesses, and the caul of the maw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:14–17; LEVITICUS 7:29–34"> And when the inner fatnesses were burnt upon the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:30–34; LEVITICUS 7:24; EXODUS 29:24"> Aaron separated the breasts, and the right shoulders of them, and raised them before the Lord, as Moses commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:5; LUKE 24:50; NUMBERS 6:23–27"> And he stretched forth his hands to the people, and blessed it; and so when the sacrifices for sin, and &#91;the&#93; burnt sacrifices, and &#91;the&#93; peaceable sacrifices, were fulfilled, Aaron came down from the place of sacrificing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:6; NUMBERS 16:19; LUKE 1:21–22"> Soothly Moses and Aaron entered into the tabernacle of witnessing, and went out afterward, and blessed the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JUDGES 6:21; 1ST KINGS 18:38–39; 1ST CHRONICLES 21:26"> and the glory of the Lord appeared to all the multitude. And lo! fire went out from the Lord, and devoured the burnt sacrifice, and the inner fatnesses that were upon the altar; and when the companies had seen this thing, they praised the Lord, and felled on their faces.</span>
