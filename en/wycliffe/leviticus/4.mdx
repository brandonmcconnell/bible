<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 19:12; HEBREWS 9:7; LEVITICUS 4:27"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, When a soul, that is, a man, hath done sin by ignorance, and hath done anything of all the commandments of the Lord, which he commanded that those &#91;or they&#93; shall not be done, thou shalt follow these ordinances;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:27–28; LEVITICUS 4:14; LEVITICUS 9:2"> if a priest that is anointed, hath done sin, making the people to trespass, he shall offer for his sin a calf without wem to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:3–4; DANIEL 9:26; ISAIAH 53:6"> And he shall bring it to the door of the tabernacle of witnessing, before the Lord, and he shall put his hand on the head thereof, and he shall offer it to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:14; NUMBERS 19:4; LEVITICUS 4:16–17"> And he shall take up of the blood of the calf, and shall bring it into the tabernacle of witnessing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:14; LEVITICUS 14:18; LEVITICUS 26:28"> And when he hath dipped his finger into the blood, he shall sprinkle it seven times before the Lord, against the veil of the saintuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:15; LEVITICUS 9:9; LEVITICUS 16:18"> And he shall put of the same blood on the corners of the altar of incense most acceptable to the Lord, which altar is in the tabernacle of witnessing; soothly he shall pour all the tother blood into the foundament of the altar of burnt sacrifice in the entering of the tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:3–5; LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:31"> And he shall offer for his sin the inner fatness of the calf, as well it that covereth the entrails, as all things that be within,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:4"> two little reins, and the caul, which is on them, beside &#91;the&#93; ileum, and the fatness of the maw, with the little reins,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> as it is offered of the calf of the sacrifice of peaceable things; and the priest shall burn those things on the altar of burnt sacrifice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:5; EXODUS 29:14; LEVITICUS 9:8–11"> Soothly the priest shall bear out of the tents, the skin, and all the flesh, with the head, and the feet, and &#91;the&#93; entrails, and the dung,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:11; EXODUS 29:14; LEVITICUS 16:27"> and the body that is left, into a clean place, where &#91;the&#93; ashes be wont to be poured out; and he shall burn those things upon the heap of wood, the which shall be burnt in the place of ashes poured out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:17; LEVITICUS 5:2–5; HOSEA 5:15"> That if all the company of the sons of Israel knoweth not, and doeth by unknowing that that is against the commandment of the Lord,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:23; LEVITICUS 4:3; LEVITICUS 4:28"> and afterward understandeth his sin, he shall offer a calf for that sin, and he shall bring the calf to the door of the tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:4; LEVITICUS 16:21; LEVITICUS 8:14"> And the elder men of the people shall set &#91;or put&#93; hands on the head thereof before the Lord; and when the calf is offered in the sight of the Lord,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:5–12; HEBREWS 9:12–14"> the priest that is anointed shall bear in of his blood into the tabernacle of witnessing;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:6–7"> and when he hath dipped his finger, he shall sprinkle the blood seven times against the veil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:7"> And he shall put of the same blood in the horns of the altar, which is before the Lord in the tabernacle of witnessing; soothly he shall pour the blood that leaveth beside the foundament of the altar of burnt sacrifice, which is in the door of the tabernacle of witnessing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:6; LEVITICUS 4:26; NUMBERS 15:25"> And he shall take all the fatness thereof, and shall burn it on the altar;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:25; HEBREWS 10:10–12; LEVITICUS 12:8"> and he shall do also of this calf, as he did before of the tother; and when the priest shall pray for them, the Lord shall be merciful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:15; MATTHEW 20:28; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21"> Forsooth he shall bear out &#91;of the tents&#93; that calf, and he shall burn it, as he did also the former calf, for it is for the sin of the multitude.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:2; LEVITICUS 4:13; NUMBERS 31:13"> If a prince sinneth, and doeth by ignorance one thing of many, which is forbidden in the law of the Lord,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:14; ROMANS 8:3; NUMBERS 7:22"> and afterward understandeth his sin, he shall offer to the Lord a sacrifice, a goat buck, without wem;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:13; LEVITICUS 7:2; LEVITICUS 4:3–35"> and he shall set his hand on the head thereof. And when he hath offered it in the place, where &#91;the&#93; burnt sacrifice is wont to be slain, before the Lord, for it is for sin;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:7; LEVITICUS 4:18; LEVITICUS 4:30"> the priest shall dip his finger in the blood of &#91;the&#93; sacrifice for sin, and he shall touch with his bloody finger the corners of the altar of burnt sacrifice, and he shall pour the blood that leaveth at the foundament thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:20; LEVITICUS 4:31; LEVITICUS 4:35"> Soothly the priest shall burn the inner fatness above the altar, as it is wont to be done in the sacrifice of peaceable things, and the priest shall pray for him, and for his sin, and it shall be forgiven to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:2; NUMBERS 15:27; LEVITICUS 4:13"> That if a soul, that is, a singular man, of the people of the land sinneth by ignorance, that he do anything of these &#91;things&#93; that be forbidden in the law of the Lord, and trespasseth,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:23; LEVITICUS 4:3; LEVITICUS 4:32"> and knoweth his sin, he shall offer a she-goat without wem;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:4; LEVITICUS 4:24; LEVITICUS 1:4"> and he shall set his hand upon the head of the sacrifice which is for &#91;the&#93; sin, and he shall offer it in the place of &#91;the&#93; burnt sacrifice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:7; LEVITICUS 4:25; LEVITICUS 4:34"> And the priest shall take of the blood thereof upon his finger, and he shall touch the horns of the altar of burnt sacrifice, and he shall pour the blood that is left at the foundament of the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:35; LEVITICUS 1:9"> Soothly he shall take away all the inner fatness, as it is wont to be done away of the sacrifices of peaceable things, and he shall burn it on the altar, into odour of sweetness to the Lord; and the priest shall pray for him, and it shall be forgiven to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:28; 1ST PETER 3:18; 1ST PETER 2:22"> Soothly if he offereth of little beasts a sacrifice for sin, that is, a sheep without wem,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:29–31; LEVITICUS 4:4"> he shall put his hand on the head thereof, and he shall offer it in the place where the beasts of burnt sacrifices be wont to be slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:30; LEVITICUS 4:25; LEVITICUS 4:7"> And the priest shall take of the blood thereof in his finger, and he shall touch therewith the horns of the altar of burnt sacrifice, and he shall pour the blood that is left at the foundament of the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:20; LEVITICUS 14:18"> And he shall do away all the inner fatness, as the inner fatness of the ram, that is offered for peaceable things, is wont to be done away, and the priest shall burn it upon the altar of incense of the Lord; and the priest shall pray for him, and for his sin, and it shall be forgiven to him.</span>
</div>
