<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:21; LEVITICUS 25:20–23; EXODUS 23:10–11"> forsooth in the seventh year shall be the sabbath of the earth, of &#91;the&#93; resting of the Lord; thou shalt not sow the field, and thou shalt not cut the vineyard,</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:24; LEVITICUS 23:27; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:8"> and thou shalt sound with a clarion in the seventh month, in the tenth day of the month, in the time of propitiation, that is, mercy, in all your land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:8; ISAIAH 63:4; 1ST PETER 2:16"> And thou shalt hallow the fiftieth year, and thou shalt call it remission, or forgiveness, to all the dwellers of thy land; for that year is &#91;the&#93; jubilee, that is, the joyful year; a man shall turn again to his possession, and each man shall go again to his first meine,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:17; LEVITICUS 25:4–5"> for it is the jubilee, and the fiftieth year. Ye shall not sow, neither ye shall reap those things, that come forth freely in the field, and ye shall not gather the first fruits of &#91;the&#93; vintage,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:6–7"> for the hallowing of &#91;the&#93; jubilee; but anon ye shall eat things taken away;</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:10; LEVITICUS 27:17–24; NUMBERS 36:4"> in the year of jubilee, all men go again to their possessions.</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOEL 2:26; PSALMS 85:12; LEVITICUS 26:5"> and that the earth bring forth his fruits to you, which ye shall eat till to fullness, and dread not the assailing of any man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:4; LUKE 12:29; NUMBERS 11:4"> That if ye say, What shall we eat in the seventh year, if we sow not, neither gather our fruits?</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:8; PROVERBS 10:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:10"> I shall give my blessing to you in the sixth year, and it shall make &#91;the&#93; fruits of three years;</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND KI 19:29; LEVITICUS 26:10; ISAIAH 37:30"> and ye shall sow in the eighth year, and ye shall eat eld &#91;or old&#93; fruits till to the ninth year; till new things come forth, ye shall eat the eld &#91;or old&#93; things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:11; PSALMS 39:12; EZEKIEL 48:14"> Also the land shall not be sold into without end, for it is mine, and ye be my comelings, and &#91;my&#93; tenants;</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:7; LEVITICUS 25:51–53; LEVITICUS 25:27"> wherefore all the country of your possession shall be sold under the condition of again-buying.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:7"> soothly if he hath no nigh kinsman, and he may find &#91;the&#93; price to again-buy,</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–8"> Forsooth if the house is in a town that hath not walls, it shall be sold by the law of &#91;the&#93; fields; soothly if it is not again-bought in the jubilee, it shall turn again to the lord thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:1–45; NUMBERS 35:1–8"> The houses of &#91;the&#93; deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, that be in &#91;the&#93; cities, may ever&#91;more&#93; be again-bought;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:20–24; DEUTERONOMY 18:1–2"> if those &#91;or they&#93; be not again-bought, those &#91;or they&#93; shall turn again in the jubilee to the lords; for the houses of the cities of deacons &#91;or Levites&#93; be for possessions among the sons of Israel;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:36–37; NUMBERS 35:2–5"> forsooth the suburbs of them shall not be sold, for it is everlasting possession.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:7–8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:29; LEVITICUS 25:25"> If thy brother is made poor, and feeble in power, and thou receivest him as a comeling, and a pilgrim, and he liveth with thee,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:45; NUMBERS 15:41; EXODUS 20:2"> I am your Lord God, that led you out of the land of Egypt, that I should give to you the land of Canaan, and that I should be your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 21:2; 1ST KINGS 9:22; 2ND KI 4:1"> If thy brother compelled by poverty selleth himself to thee, thou shalt not oppress him by servage of servants,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="EXODUS 12:44; PSALMS 2:8–9; ISAIAH 14:1–2"> A servant and handmaid be to you of &#91;the&#93; nations that be in your compass, and of &#91;the&#93; comelings the which be pilgrims with you,</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:3–6"> either they that be born of comelings in your land; ye shall have these servants, and by right of heritage ye shall leave them to your after-comers, and ye shall wield them without end;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:2; LEVITICUS 25:43"> soothly oppress ye not by power your brethren, the sons of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; LEVITICUS 25:26; 1ST SAMUEL 2:7–8"> If the hand of a comeling or of a pilgrim waxeth strong at you, and thy brother is made poor, and selleth himself to that comeling, either to any of his kin,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 5:5; HEBREWS 2:11–13; LEVITICUS 25:35"> he may be again-bought after the selling; he that will of his brethren, again-buy him;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:26"> both his father’s brother, and the son of his father’s brother, and his kins-man, and his ally. Else if also he shall be able, he shall again-buy himself,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="ISAIAH 16:14; ISAIAH 21:16; LEVITICUS 25:53"> while the years be reckoned only from the time of his selling till into the year of jubilee; and while the money, for which he was sold, is reckoned by the number of years, and while the hire of an hired man is reckoned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> If more years be that dwell till to the jubilee, by these years he shall yield also the price;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> if few years be, he shall set reckoning with him by the number of these years; and he shall yield to the buyer that that is left of &#91;the&#93; years,</span>

<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="EXODUS 21:2–3; LEVITICUS 25:40–41; ISAIAH 49:9"> That if he may not be again-bought by this manner, he shall go out with his free children in the year of jubilee;</span>
