<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 5:7; ISAIAH 21:11–12; JOHN 20:15"> Watchmen, that keep the city, found me. I asked, Whether ye saw him, whom my soul loveth?</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 2:7; SONG OF SONGS 8:4; MICAH 4:8"> Ye daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you greatly, by the caprets, and harts of fields, that ye raise not, neither make to awake the dearworthy spousess, till she will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 8:5; SONG OF SONGS 1:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:14"> Who is this woman, that goeth up by the desert, as a rod of smoke of sweet smelling spices, of myrrh, and of incense, and of all powder of an ointment maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 3:9; 1ST KINGS 9:22; 1ST SAMUEL 14:52"> Lo! sixty strong men of the strongest men of Israel encompass the bed of Solomon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 45:3; PSALMS 91:5; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:6–8"> and all they hold swords, and be most witting to battles; the sword of each man is on his hip, for the dread of nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 3:7; 2ND SAMUEL 23:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:6"> King Solomon made to him a seat, of the wood of Lebanon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 3:15–16; EPHESIANS 3:18–19; PSALMS 87:3"> he made the pillars thereof of silver; he made a golden resting place, a going up of purple; and he arrayed the middle things with charity, for the daughters of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 62:5; JEREMIAH 2:2; HOSEA 2:19–20"> Ye daughters of Zion, go out, and see king Solomon in the diadem, with which his mother crowned him, in the day of his espousing, and in the day of the gladness of his heart.</span>
