<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:7–8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:3; GALATIANS 1:6"> O! unwitting Galatians, before whose eyes Jesus Christ is exiled, &#91;or O! ye witless men of Galatia, before whose eyes Jesus Christ is damned or condemned&#93;, and is crucified in you, who hath deceived you, that ye obey not to truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 10:16–17; GALATIANS 3:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:8"> This only I desire to learn of you, whether ye have received the Spirit of the works of the law, or of hearing of belief?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:4–8; GALATIANS 4:7–10; HEBREWS 7:16–19"> So ye be fools, that when ye have begun in Spirit, &#91;now&#93; ye be ended in flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND JOHN 1:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:2; HEBREWS 10:32–39"> So great things &#91;or So many things&#93; ye have suffered without cause, if it be without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:9–10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:11–12"> He that giveth to you &#91;the&#93; Spirit, and worketh virtues in you, whether of &#91;the&#93; works of the law, or of hearing of belief? &#91;or of faith?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 15:6; ROMANS 4:21–22; JAMES 2:23"> As it is written, Abraham believed to God, and it was reckoned to him to rightwiseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 9:7–8; GALATIANS 3:9; GALATIANS 3:26–29"> And therefore know ye, that these that be of belief &#91;or they that be of faith&#93;, be the sons of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 12:3; GENESIS 22:18; GENESIS 28:14"> And the scripture seeing afar, that God justifieth the heathen of belief, &#91;or Forsooth the scripture purveying, for God justifieth of faith heathen men&#93;, told before to Abraham, That in thee all the heathen &#91;or all folks&#93; shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 4:16; GALATIANS 3:29; ROMANS 4:24"> And therefore these that be of belief, &#91;or they that be of faith&#93;, shall be blessed with faithful Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:26; ROMANS 8:7; ROMANS 3:19–20"> For all that be of the works of the law, be under curse; for it is written, Each man is cursed, that abideth not &#91;or that dwelleth not&#93; in all things that be written in the book of the law, to do those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:4; ROMANS 1:17; HEBREWS 10:38"> And that no man is justified in the law before God, it is open, for a rightful man liveth of belief. &#91;Forsooth for no man is justified in the law with God, it is known, for a rightful man liveth by faith.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:5; ROMANS 10:5–6; NEHEMIAH 9:29"> But the law is not of belief, but he that doeth those things of the law, shall live in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:24; DEUTERONOMY 21:23; HEBREWS 9:12"> But Christ again-bought us &#91;or delivered us&#93; from the curse of the law, and was made accursed for us; for it is written, Each man is cursed that hangeth in the tree;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:28–29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:13; EZEKIEL 36:26–27"> that among the heathen the blessing of Abraham were made in Jesus Christ, that we receive the promise of Spirit through belief. &#91;that the blessing of Abraham in heathen men should be made in Christ Jesus, that we take the promise of Spirit by faith.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:17; ROMANS 6:19; ROMANS 3:5"> Brethren, I say after man, no man despiseth the testament of a man that is confirmed, or ordaineth above &#91;other thing&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 17:7–8; ROMANS 4:13; LUKE 1:55"> The promises were said to Abraham and to his seed; he saith not, In &#91;the&#93; seeds, as in many, but as in one, And to thy seed, that is, Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:39–40; GENESIS 15:13; ROMANS 4:13–14"> But I say, this testament is confirmed of God; the law that was made after four hundred and thirty years, maketh not the testament vain to void away the promise &#91;or maketh not void for to do away the promise&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:12–15; GALATIANS 2:21; ROMANS 4:13–16"> For if &#91;the&#93; heritage were of the law, it were not now of promise. But God granted &#91;or gave&#93; to Abraham through promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:53; ROMANS 7:7–13; HEBREWS 2:2"> What then the law? that is, Whereto is the law profitable? &#91;or What therefore profiteth the law?&#93; It was set for trespassing, till the seed came, to whom he had made the promise. Which law was ordained by angels, in the hand of a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:5; DEUTERONOMY 6:4; JOB 9:33"> But a mediator is not of one. But God is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:21; ROMANS 9:31; ROMANS 3:20–22"> Is then the law against the promises of God? God forbid. For if the law were given, that might quicken, verily were rightfulness of law, &#91;or verily rightwiseness were of law&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 11:32; HEBREWS 9:15; GALATIANS 3:23"> But the scripture hath concluded all things under sin, that the promise of the faith of Jesus Christ were given to them that believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:18; GALATIANS 3:24–25; GALATIANS 3:19"> And before that belief came, they were kept under the law, enclosed into that belief that was to be showed. &#91;Forsooth before that the faith came, we were kept under the law, shut together into that faith that was to be showed.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 10:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:38–39; ROMANS 7:24–25"> And so the law was our under-master in Christ, that we be justified of belief &#91;or of faith&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 6:14; ROMANS 7:4; HEBREWS 10:15–18"> But after that belief came, we be not now under the undermaster. &#91;But after that the faith came, now we be not under the little master.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:18; JOHN 1:12–13"> For all ye be the children of God through the belief of Jesus Christ. &#91;For all ye be the sons of God by faith in Christ Jesus.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ROMANS 13:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:13; ROMANS 6:3–4"> For all ye that be baptized, be clothed with Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:12–13; COLOSSIANS 3:11; ROMANS 3:29–30"> There is no Jew, nor Greek, no bondman, nor free man, no male, nor female; for all ye be one in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EPHESIANS 3:6; ROMANS 9:7–8; GALATIANS 3:7"> And if ye be one in Jesus Christ, then ye be the seed of Abraham, and heirs by promise.</span>
