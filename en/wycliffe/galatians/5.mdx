<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:17; GALATIANS 5:13; 1ST PETER 2:16"> Stand ye therefore, and do not ye again be held in the yoke of servage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:1; ROMANS 9:31–32"> Lo! I, Paul, say to you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall nothing profit to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:10; ROMANS 2:25; JAMES 2:10–11"> And I witness again to each man that circumciseth himself, that he is a debtor of all the law to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 9:31–32; 2ND PETER 2:20–22; ROMANS 11:6"> And ye be voided away from Christ, and ye that be justified in the law, ye have fallen away from grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 130:5; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:10; PSALMS 62:5"> For we through the Spirit of belief abide the hope of rightwiseness. &#91;For we by Spirit of faith abide the hope of rightwiseness.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:19; JAMES 2:14–26"> For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision is anything worth, neither prepuce, but the belief that worketh by charity &#91;or but faith that worketh by charity&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:24; GALATIANS 3:1; HEBREWS 12:1"> Ye ran well; who hindered you that ye obeyed not to the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:6; ROMANS 8:28"> Consent ye to no man; for this counsel is not of him that hath called you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:6–7; MARK 8:15"> A little sourdough impaireth &#91;or maketh sour&#93; all the gobbet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:3; GALATIANS 1:7"> I trust on you in our Lord, that ye should understand none other thing. And who that disturbeth or dis-troubleth you, shall bear doom, whoever he be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:23; GALATIANS 6:12; GALATIANS 4:29"> And, brethren, if I preach yet circumcision, what suffer I yet persecution? then the stumbling of the cross is avoided/is voided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:13; TITUS 3:10"> I would that they were cut away, that disturb you. &#91;I would that they that distrouble you, be also cut off.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:9"> For, brethren, ye be called into freedom &#91;or into liberty&#93;; only give ye not freedom &#91;or liberty&#93; into occasion of flesh, but by charity of &#91;the&#93; Spirit serve ye together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:12; LEVITICUS 19:18; GALATIANS 6:2"> For every law &#91;or all the law&#93; is fulfilled in one word, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:3; JAMES 3:14–3; GALATIANS 5:26"> And if ye bite, and eat each other, see ye, lest ye be wasted each from other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:24–25; ROMANS 8:12–14; GALATIANS 6:8"> And I say to you in Christ, walk ye in Spirit, and ye shall not perform the desires of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 8:13; JOHN 3:6; ROMANS 8:5–8"> For the flesh coveteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these be adversaries together, that ye do not all things that ye will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 8:14; ROMANS 6:14–15; GALATIANS 5:25"> That if ye be led by &#91;the&#93; Spirit, ye be not under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9–10; COLOSSIANS 3:5–8; MATTHEW 15:18–19"> And the works of the flesh be open, which be fornication, unclean-ness, unchastity, lechery,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TITUS 3:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8"> service of false gods &#91;or serving of idols&#93;, witchcrafts, enmities, strivings &#91;or strives&#93;, indignations, wraths, chidings, dissensions, sects &#91;or heresies&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9–10; ROMANS 8:13; ROMANS 13:13"> envies, manslayings, drunkennesses, unmeasurable eatings &#91;or gluttonies&#93;, and things like to these, which I say to you before, as I have told to you before, for they that do such things, shall not have the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:12–17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:4–7; JOHN 15:5"> But the fruit of the Spirit is charity, joy, peace, patience, long abiding, benignity, &#91;or good will&#93;, goodness, mildness, faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:25; TITUS 2:2"> temperance, continence, chastity; against such things is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 6:6; ROMANS 13:14; ROMANS 8:13"> And they that be of Christ, have crucified their flesh with vices and covetings &#91;or concupiscences&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:16; ROMANS 8:4–5; ROMANS 8:2"> If we live by Spirit, walk we by Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JAMES 3:14–16; PHILIPPIANS 2:1–3; JAMES 4:16"> be we not made covetous of vain glory, stirring each other to wrath, or having envy each to other.</span>
