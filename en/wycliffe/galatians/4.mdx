<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:23; GALATIANS 4:29; GENESIS 24:2–3"> But I say, as long time as the heir is a little child, he diverseth nothing from a servant, when he is lord of all things &#91;or lord of all&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> but he is under keepers and tutors, into the time determined of the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:20; COLOSSIANS 2:8; GALATIANS 4:9"> So we, when we were little children, we served under the elements of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:14; JOHN 1:14; ROMANS 1:3"> But after that the fulfilling of time came, God sent his Son, made of a woman, made under the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:15; EPHESIANS 1:5; 1ST PETER 1:18–20"> that he should again-buy them that were under the law, that we should receive the adoption of sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ROMANS 8:15–17; ROMANS 5:5; ROMANS 8:9"> And for ye be God’s sons, God sent his Spirit into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father. &#91;Forsooth for ye be the sons of God, God sent the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, that is, father.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:16–17; GALATIANS 3:29–2; GALATIANS 3:26"> And so there is not now a servant, but a son; and if he is a son, he is an heir by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 8:4; EPHESIANS 2:11–12; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:8"> But then ye unknowing God, served to them that in kind were not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 8:3; GALATIANS 3:3; ROMANS 8:3"> But now when ye have known God, and be known of God, how be ye turned again to the feeble &#91;or the sick&#93; and needy elements, to the which ye will again serve?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:16–17; ROMANS 14:5; LEVITICUS 23:1–44"> Ye take keep to days, &#91;or Ye keep, or wait on, days&#93;, and months, and times, and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 3:5; PHILIPPIANS 2:16; GALATIANS 2:2"> But I dread you, lest without cause, I have travailed among you, &#91;or lest peradventure I have travailed in you without cause&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:5; 1ST KINGS 22:4; GENESIS 34:15"> Be ye as I, for I am as ye. Brethren, I beseech you, ye have hurt me nothing &#91;or ye have nothing hurt me&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:7–10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:30"> But ye know, that by infirmity of flesh I have preached &#91;or I have evangelized&#93; to you now before;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:40; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:13; HEBREWS 13:2"> and ye despised not, neither forsook your temptation in my flesh, but ye received me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 5:2; 1ST JOHN 3:16–18; COLOSSIANS 4:13"> Where then is your blessing? &#91;or Where is therefore your blessedness, that ye had before time?&#93; For I bear you witness, that if it might have been done, ye would have put out your eyes, and have given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="AMOS 5:10; PROVERBS 9:8; JOHN 8:45"> Am I then made an enemy to you, saying to you the sooth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 16:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:13–15; 2ND PETER 2:3"> They love not you well &#91;or They love you not well&#93;, but they will exclude you, that ye pursue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; PHILIPPIANS 1:27"> But pursue ye the good evermore in good, and not only when I am present with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:13; ROMANS 8:29; ROMANS 13:14"> My small children, which I bear again, till that Christ be formed in you, &#91;My little sons, whom I child, or I bring forth by travail, again, till Christ be formed in you,&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:17–18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:19–21; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:9"> and I would now be at you, and change my voice, for I am confounded among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:10; GALATIANS 3:23–24; ROMANS 7:5–6"> Say to me, ye that will be under the law, have ye not read the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 16:15; GENESIS 21:10; GENESIS 21:1–2"> For it is written, that Abraham had two sons, one of a servant &#91;or a handmaiden&#93;, and one of a free woman &#91;or a wife&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:11; ROMANS 9:7–8; GALATIANS 4:28–29"> But he that was of the servant &#91;or the handmaiden&#93; was born after the flesh; but he that was of the free woman &#91;or the wife&#93; by a promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 21:9–13; HEBREWS 10:15–18; GENESIS 16:8"> The which things be said by another understanding &#91;or allegory&#93;. For these be two testaments; one in the hill of Sinai &#91;or mount Sinai&#93;, engendering into servage, which is Agar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 19:44; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:11; GALATIANS 1:17"> For Sinai is an hill that is in Arabia, which hill is joined to it that is now Jerusalem, and serveth with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:22; PHILIPPIANS 3:20; MICAH 4:1–2"> But that Jerusalem that is above, is free, which is our mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:1–5; PSALMS 113:9; RUTH 4:14–16"> For it is written, Be glad, thou barren, that bearest not; break out and cry, &#91;thou&#93; that bringest forth no children; for many sons be of her that is left of her husband, more than of her that hath an husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:23; ROMANS 9:8–9; ROMANS 4:13–18"> For, brethren, we be &#91;the&#93; sons of promise after Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 21:9; ROMANS 8:13; GALATIANS 5:11"> but now as this that was born after the flesh pursued him that was after the Spirit, so now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 21:10–12; JOHN 8:35; ROMANS 8:15–17"> But what saith the scripture? Cast out the servant &#91;or the handmaiden&#93; and her son, for the son of the servant shall not be heir with the son of the free wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 8:36; JOHN 1:12–13; GALATIANS 5:1"> And so, brethren, we be not sons of the servant &#91;or of the handmaiden&#93;, but of the free wife, by which freedom &#91;or liberty&#93; Christ hath made us free.</span>
