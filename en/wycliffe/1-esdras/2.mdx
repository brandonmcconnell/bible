<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Reigning Cyrus, king of Persia, in the fulfilling of the word of the Lord, in the mouth of Jeremy,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> the Lord raised up the spirit of Cyrus, king of Persia; and he preached in all his realm altogether by scripture,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> saying, These things saith Cyrus, king of Persia, The Lord of Israel, the high Lord, hath ordained me king to the world of earths;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and he signified to me to build to him an house in Jerusalem, that is in Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If there is any man of your kindred, his Lord go up with him into Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Therefore how many ever dwell in places about, help they them that dwell &#91;or that be&#93; in that place, in gold and silver,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> in gifts, with horses, and beasts, and with other things, the which after vows be laid up into the house of the Lord, that is in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the standing princes of lineages of towns of Judah, of the lineage of Benjamin, and priests and deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, whom the Lord stirred to wend &#91;or to go&#93; up, and to build up the house of the Lord, that is in Jerusalem;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and they, that were in the environ, or in compass, of them, should help in all silver and gold of it, and in beasts, and in many vows; and many others, of whom the wit is stirred, help they also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And king Cyrus brought forth the sacred vessels of the Lord, the which Nebuchadnezzar translated or borne over from Jerusalem, and made sacred them in his maumet temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Cyrus, king of Persia, bringing them forth, took those to Mithridates, that was upon the treasures of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Forsooth by him they be taken to Sanabassar, governor of Judea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Of these things forsooth this is the number; silvern hallowed vessels of liquors, two thousand and four hundred; thirty silvern drinking vessels; thirty golden vials; and two thousand and four hundred silvern vials; and a thousand other vessels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsooth all the golden and silvern vessels were four thousand and four hundred and eight and sixty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they be delivered out to Sanabassar, together with them, that were come into Jerusalem of the captivity, or thralldom, of Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Forsooth in the time of Artaxerxes, king of Persia, there wrote to him, of these that dwelled in Judea and in Jerusalem, Belemus, and Mithridates, and Tabellius, and Rathumus, Beelteth-mus, and Semellius, the scribe, and others dwelling in Samaria, and in other places, they wrote this subject letter to king Artaxerxes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Lord, thy children Rathumus, and Semellius, the scribe, and other dooms-men of thy court, of things that fall in Coelesyria and Phoenicia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And now be it known to the lord the king, that Jews, the which went up from you to us, coming into Jerusalem, a city of fleers-away &#91;or a city again-flown to&#93;, and a full evil city, they build up the ovens of it, and they set the walls, and rear the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> That if this city and walls were made up &#91;or full ended&#93;, they shall not suffer to yield tributes, but also they shall against-stand to kings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And for cause that that thing is done about the temple, to have it rightly we have deemed to not despise that same thing,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but to make known to the lord &#91;the&#93; king, that if it shall be seen pleasing to the king, be it sought in the books of thy fathers;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and thou shalt find in remem-brances written of them, and thou shalt know, that that city was again-flown, and kings and cities smiting altogether,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and Jews fleeing again, and making battles in it always; for the which cause this city was forsaken &#91;or is deserted&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now therefore we make known to the lord &#91;the&#93; king, that if this city were built up, and the walls of it were areared, there shall be no coming down to thee into Coelesyria and Phoenicia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then the king wrote again to Rathumus, that wrote those things that befell, and to Beeltethmus, and to Semellius, the scribe, and to others ordained sovereigns and dwelling in Syria, and in Phoenicia &#91;or Phenice&#93;, he wrote to them these things that be set under.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> I have read the letter, that thou sentest to me. Therefore I commanded it to be sought; and it was found, that that city was always withstanding to kings,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and men again-fugitive, and making battles in it; and most strong kings have been lordshipping in Jerusalem, and asking tributes of Coelesyria and Phoenicia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now therefore I command to prevent those men to build up the city, and to look &#91;or purvey&#93;, that anything be not made hereafter; but that they pass not into full much &#91;or but go they not forth into more&#93;,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> since they be of malice, so that grievances be not brought there to kings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Then after these things were rehearsed, that were written of Artaxerxes, the king, Rathumus, and Semellius, the scribe, and they that were with them ordained, enjoining, hieingly came into Jerusalem, with horsemen, and people, and with company; and they began to prevent the builders. And they were voided then from the building of the temple &#91;in Jerusalem&#93;, unto the second year of the realm of Darius, king of Persia.</span>
</div>
</div>
