<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> For which thing our Lord God &#91;or the Lord our God&#93; setted steadfastly his word, which he spake to us, and to our judges, that deemed in Israel, and to our kings, and to our princes, and to all Israel and Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> that the Lord should bring on us great evils, that were not done under heaven, as those be done in Jerusalem; by &#91;or after&#93; those things that be written in the law of Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> that a man should eat the fleshes &#91;or the flesh&#93; of his son, and the fleshes &#91;or the flesh&#93; of his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he gave them into the hand of all kings, that be in our compass, into shame &#91;or reproof&#93;, and into desolation in all peoples, among which the Lord scattered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And we be made beneath, and not above; for we sinned to our Lord God &#91;or to the Lord our God&#93;, in not obeying to the voice of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Rightfulness is to our Lord God &#91;or To the Lord our God rightwiseness&#93;, but shame of face is to us and to our fathers, as this day is.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And we besought not the face of our Lord God &#91;or the Lord our God&#93;, that we should turn again, each of us from our worst ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the Lord waked in evils, and brought those &#91;or them&#93; on us; for the Lord is just &#91;or rightwise&#93; in all his works, which he commanded to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And we heard not the voice of him, that we should go in the commandments of the Lord, which he gave before our face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And now, Lord God of Israel, that leddest &#91;or broughtest&#93; thy people out of the land of Egypt in a strong hand, and in miracles &#91;or signs&#93;, and in great wonders, and in thy great virtue, and in an high arm, and madest to thee a name, as this day is;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> we have sinned, we have done unfaithfully &#91;or unpiously&#93;, we have done wickedly, our Lord God, in all thy rightfulnesses, &#91;or Lord our God, in all thy rightwisenesses&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thy wrath be turned away from us; for we be left a few among heathen men, where thou hast scattered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Lord, hear thou our askings, and our prayers, and lead &#91;or bring&#93; us out for thee; and give thou to us to find grace before the face of them, that led us away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> that all earth know, that thou art our Lord God &#91;or thou art Lord our God&#93;, and that thy name is called to help on Israel, and on the kin of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Lord, behold thou from thine holy house on us, and bow down thine ear, and hear us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Open thine eyes, and see; for not dead men that be in hell, whose spirit is taken from their entrails, shall give honour &#91;or worship&#93; and justifying to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> but a soul which is sorry on the greatness &#91;or muchliness&#93; of evil, and goeth bowed, and sick &#91;or meeked&#93;, and eyes failing, and an hungry soul &#91;or the soul hungering&#93;, giveth glory to thee, and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For not by &#91;or after&#93; the rightwise-nesses of our fathers we poured out prayers, and asked mercy before thy sight, our Lord God &#91;or Lord our God&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> but for thou sentest thy wrath and thy strong vengeance on us, as thou spakest in the hands of thy children, prophets, and saidest,</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> That if ye do not, neither hear the voice of your Lord God &#91;or the Lord our God&#93;, to work to the king of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> I shall make your failing from the cities of Judah, and from the gates of Jerusalem; and I shall take away from you the voice of gladness &#91;or of mirth&#93;, and the voice of joy, and the voice of spouse, and the voice of spousess; and all the land shall be without step of them that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And they heard not thy voice, that they should work to the king of Babylon; and thou hast set steadfastly thy words, which thou spakest in the hands of thy children, prophets; that the bones of our kings, and the bones of our fathers should be borne over &#91;or translated&#93; from their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And lo! they be cast forth in the heat of the sun, and in the frost of the night; and men be dead &#91;or they be dead&#93; in the worst sorrows, in hunger, and in sword, and in sending out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou hast set the temple in which thy name was called to help, as this day showeth, for the wickedness of the house of Israel, and of the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And thou, our Lord God, hast done in us by all thy goodness, and by all that great merciful doing of thee, &#91;And thou hast done in us, Lord our God, after all thy goodness, and after all that thy great mercy doing,&#93;</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For I knew, that the people shall not hear me, for it is a people of &#91;an&#93; hard noll. And it shall turn to their heart in the land of their captivity;</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> &#91;And&#93; They shall turn away them-selves from their hard back, and from their wickednesses; for they shall have mind of &#91;or shall remember&#93; the way of their fathers, that sinned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And I shall again-call them into the land, which I swore to give to the fathers of them, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; and they shall be lords of it. And I shall multiply them, and they &#91;shall&#93; not be made less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And I shall ordain to them another testament everlasting, that I be to them into God, and they shall be to me into a people. And I shall no more move my people, the sons of Israel, from the land which I gave to them.</span>
