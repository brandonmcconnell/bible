<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 16:1–8; JOHN 20:1–10; MATTHEW 27:56"> But in the eventide of the sabbath, &#91;or holiday&#93;, that beginneth to shine in the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene came, and another Mary, to see the sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:26; JOHN 20:12–13; MARK 16:3–5"> And lo! there was made a great earth-shaking; for the angel of the Lord came down from heaven, and nighed, and turned away the stone, and sat thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 9:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:10; MARK 16:5"> And his looking was as lightning, and his clothes &#91;white&#93; as snow;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:17; DANIEL 10:7; MATTHEW 27:65–66"> and for dread of him the keepers were afeared, and they were made as dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:17–18; HEBREWS 1:14; ISAIAH 41:10"> But the angel answered, and said to the women, Do not ye dread, for I know that ye seek Jesus, that was crucified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 16:6; MATTHEW 12:40; LUKE 24:6–8"> he is not here, for he is risen, as he said; come ye, and see ye the place, where the Lord was laid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:32; LUKE 24:22–24; MATTHEW 28:10"> And go ye soon, and say ye to his disciples, that he is risen. And lo! he shall go before you into Galilee; there ye shall see him. Lo! I have before-said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 2:11; MARK 16:8; LUKE 24:36–41"> And they went out soon from the burials &#91;or the sepulchre&#93;, with dread, and great joy, running to tell to his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:17; JOHN 20:14–17; MATTHEW 14:33"> And lo! Jesus met them, and said, Hail ye. And they nighed, and held his feet, and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 8:29; JOHN 20:17; MATTHEW 28:7"> Then Jesus said to them, Do not ye dread; go ye, tell ye to my brethren, that they go into Galilee; there they shall see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:65–66; MATTHEW 28:4"> And when they were gone, lo! some of the keepers came into the city, and told to the princes of priests all things that were done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:3–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:5–22; MATTHEW 27:1–2"> And when they were gathered together with the elder men, and had taken their counsel, they gave to the knights much money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:64"> and said, Say ye, that his disciples came by night, and have stolen him, while ye slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:19"> And if this be heard of the justice &#91;or the president&#93;, we shall counsel him, and make you secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:10; MATTHEW 27:8; MATTHEW 26:15"> And when the money was taken, they did, as they were taught. And this word is published among the Jews, till into this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:32; MATTHEW 28:10; MATTHEW 28:7"> And the eleven disciples went into Galilee, into an hill, where Jesus had ordained to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 45:11; PSALMS 2:12; JOHN 5:23"> And they saw him, and worship-ped; but some of them doubted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 3:35; 1ST PETER 3:22; MATTHEW 11:27"> And Jesus came nigh, and spake to them, and said, All power in heaven and in earth is given to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 16:15–16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38–39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8"> Therefore go ye, and teach all folks, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; JOSHUA 1:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:9–10"> teaching them to keep all things, whatever things I have commanded to you; and lo! I am with you in all days, into the end of the world.</span>
