<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:34–38; MARK 8:17–21; MARK 8:5–9"> neither of seven loaves into four thousand of men, and how many baskets ye took?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:6; LUKE 12:56; MARK 8:21"> Why understand ye not, for I said not to you of bread, Be ye ware of the sourdough of Pharisees and of Sadducees?</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:18; ISAIAH 22:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:18"> And to thee I shall give the keys of the kingdom of heavens; and what-ever thou shalt bind on earth, shall be bound also in heavens; and whatever thou shalt unbind on earth, shall be unbound also in heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 8:30; LUKE 9:21; JOHN 1:41"> Then he commanded to his disciples, that they should say to no man, that he was Jesus Christ.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARK 8:32; JOHN 13:6–8; MATTHEW 16:16–17"> And Peter took him, and began to blame him, and said, Far be it from thee, Lord; this shall not be to thee.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:38; LUKE 14:27; 1ST PETER 4:1–2"> Then Jesus said to his disciples, If any man will come after me, deny he himself, and take his cross, and pursue me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:39; MARK 8:35; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:11"> for he that will make his life safe, shall lose it; and he that shall lose his life for me, shall find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARK 8:36–37; JOB 27:8; LUKE 9:25"> For what profiteth it to a man &#91;or what profiteth to a man&#93;, if he win all the world, and suffer impairing of his soul? or what exchanging shall a man give for his soul?</span>
