<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 3:1; MARK 8:15; LUKE 9:7–9"> In that time Herod tetrarch, &#91;that is, prince of the fourth part&#93;, heard the fame of Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:14; JOHN 10:41; MARK 8:28"> and said to his children, This is John Baptist, he is risen from death, and therefore virtues work in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 6:17; LUKE 3:19–20; MATTHEW 4:12"> For Herod had holden &#91;or held&#93; John, and bound him, and putted him in prison for Herodias, the wife of his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:21; LEVITICUS 18:16; MARK 6:18"> For John said to him, It is not leaveful to thee to have her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:26; MATTHEW 11:9; MARK 6:19–20"> And he willing to slay him, dreaded the people; for they had him as a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 6:21–23; GENESIS 40:20; HOSEA 1:5–6"> But in the day of Herod’s birth, the daughter of Herodias danced in the middle, and pleased Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ESTHER 5:3; ESTHER 5:6; ESTHER 7:2"> Wherefore with an oath he promised to give to her, whatever thing she asked of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 6:24; PROVERBS 29:10; PROVERBS 1:16"> And she before-warned &#91;or before admonished&#93; of her mother, said, Give thou to me here the head of John Baptist in a dish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 19:12–16; 1ST SAMUEL 14:28; MARK 6:26"> And the king was sorrowful, but for the oath, and for them that sat together at the meat, he commanded to be given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:12; MATTHEW 21:35–36; LUKE 9:9"> And he sent, and beheaded John in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:6; GENESIS 49:7; EZEKIEL 16:3–4"> And his head was brought in a dish, and it was given to the damsel, and she bare it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:2; MATTHEW 27:58–61"> And his disciples came, and took his body, and buried it; and they came, and told to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 9:10–17; MATTHEW 14:1–2; MARK 6:30–44"> And when Jesus had heard this thing, he went from thence in a boat, into a desert place beside. And when the people had heard, they followed him on their feet from cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:15; MATTHEW 9:36; HEBREWS 2:17"> And Jesus went out, and saw a great people &#91;or a great multitude&#93;, and had ruth on them, and he healed the sick men of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:23; MARK 8:3; MARK 6:35–36"> But when eventide was come, his disciples came to him, and said, The place is desert, and the time is now passed; let the people go into towns, to buy them meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND KI 4:42–44; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:2–3; ECCLESIASTES 11:2"> Jesus said to them, They have not need &#91;or no need&#93; to go; give ye them somewhat to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:33–34; MARK 6:37–38; LUKE 9:13"> They answered, We have not here, but five loaves and two fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he said to them, Bring ye them hither to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 8:6–7; LUKE 24:30; 1ST SAMUEL 9:13"> And when he had commanded the people to sit to &#91;the&#93; meat on the hay, he took five loaves and two fishes, and he beheld into heaven, and blessed, and brake, and gave &#91;loaves&#93; to his disciples; and the disciples gave to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 16:12; MARK 6:42–44; MATTHEW 16:8–10"> And all ate, and were fulfilled &#91;or filled&#93;. And they took the remnants of broken gobbets, twelve coffins full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:8–11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:4"> And the number of men that ate was five thousand of men, without women and little children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MARK 6:45–51; JOHN 6:15–21; MATTHEW 15:39"> And anon Jesus compelled the disciples to go up into a boat, and &#91;to&#93; go before him over the sea, while he let go the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 6:12; MARK 6:46; MATTHEW 26:36"> And when the people was let go, he went up alone into an hill to pray. But when the evening was come, he was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 6:18; MARK 6:48; MATTHEW 8:24"> And the boat in the middle of the sea was shogged &#91;or thrown&#93; with waves, for the wind was contrary to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOB 9:8; JOHN 6:19; MARK 6:48"> But in the fourth waking of the night, he came to them walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARK 6:49–50; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:17; LUKE 1:11–12"> And they, seeing him walking on the sea, were disturbed &#91;or distroubled&#93;, and said, That it is a phantom; and for dread they cried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:17–18; JOHN 16:33"> And anon Jesus spake to them, and said, Have ye trust, I am; do not ye dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 6:68; ROMANS 12:3; MATTHEW 26:33–35"> And Peter answered, and said, Lord, if thou art, command me to come to thee on the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; MARK 9:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:16"> And he said, Come thou. And Peter went down from the boat, and walked on the waters to come to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:54–57; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:16–17; MARK 14:38"> But he saw the wind strong, and was afeared; and when he began to drench or drown, he cried, and said, Lord, make me safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JAMES 1:6–8; PSALMS 138:7; GENESIS 22:14"> And anon Jesus held forth his hand, and took Peter, &#91;or caught him&#93;, and said to him, Thou of little faith, why hast thou doubted?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 107:29–30; MARK 4:41; JOHN 6:21"> And when he had gone &#91;up&#93; into the boat, the wind ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 11:27; PSALMS 2:7; ROMANS 1:4"> And they, that were in the boat, came, and worshipped him, and said, Verily, thou art God’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 6:53–56; LUKE 5:1; JOHN 6:24–25"> And when they had passed over the sea, they came into the land of Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:24–25; MARK 6:55; MARK 1:28–34"> And when men of that place had known him, they sent into all that country; and they brought to him all that had sicknesses &#91;or all having evil&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKE 6:19; MARK 3:10; EXODUS 28:33–43"> And they prayed him, that they should touch the hem of his clothing; and whoever touched were made safe.</span>
