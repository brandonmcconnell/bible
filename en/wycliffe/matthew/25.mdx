<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:9; LUKE 12:35; HEBREWS 4:1"> And the fools said to the wise, Give ye to us of your oil, for our lamps be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17–18; ISAIAH 55:6–7"> The prudent answered, and said, Lest peradventure it suffice not to us and to you, go ye rather to men that sell, and buy to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 13:25; MATTHEW 24:44; LUKE 12:36–37"> And while they went to buy, the spouse came; and those that were ready, entered with him to the weddings; and the gate was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:21–23; HEBREWS 12:16–17"> And at the last the other virgins came, and said, Lord, lord, open to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 8:3; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:19; JOHN 10:27"> And he answered, and said, Truly I say to you, I know you not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:42–44; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:15; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:6"> Therefore wake ye, for ye know not the day nor the hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 13:34; LUKE 19:12–27; 1ST PETER 4:9–11"> For as a man that goeth &#91;far&#93; in pilgrimage, called his servants, and betook to them his goods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:24; LUKE 19:13–14; LUKE 12:48"> and to one he gave five bezants or talents, to another twain, and to another one, to each after his own virtue; and he went forth anon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3RD JOHN 1:5–8; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:17–18; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:6"> And he that had five bezants &#91;or talents&#93; went forth, and wrought in them, and won other five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:9–10; EPHESIANS 5:16; 1ST PETER 4:10"> Also and he that had taken twain, won other twain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND PETER 1:8; HAGGAI 1:2–4; MALACHI 1:10"> But he that had taken one, went forth, and delved in the earth, and hid the money of his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:12–15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10; ROMANS 14:7–12"> But after long time, the lord of those servants came, and reckoned with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:24; LUKE 19:16–17; COLOSSIANS 1:29"> And he that had taken five bezants &#91;or talents&#93;, came, and brought other five, and said, Lord, thou betookest to me five bezants &#91;or talents&#93;, lo! I have gotten above five others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:23; LUKE 16:10; JOHN 12:26"> His lord said to him, Well be thou, good servant and faithful; for on few things thou hast been true &#91;or faithful&#93;, I shall ordain thee on many things; enter thou into the joy of thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:7–8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:1–3"> And he that had taken two bezants, or two talents, came, and said, Lord, thou betookest to me two bezants &#91;or talents&#93;; lo! I have won &#91;or gotten&#93; over other twain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:21; MARK 14:8–9; MARK 12:41–44"> His lord said to him, Well be thou, good servant and true &#91;or faithful&#93;; for on few things thou hast been true &#91;or faithful&#93;, I shall ordain thee on many things; enter thou into the joy of thy lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 19:20–22; LUKE 15:29; MALACHI 3:14–15"> But he that had taken one bezant &#91;or talent&#93;, came, and said, Lord, I know that thou art an hard man; thou reapest where thou hast not sown, and thou gatherest together where thou hast not spread abroad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:6–7; ROMANS 8:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8"> and I dreading, went, and hid thy bezant &#91;or talent&#93; in the earth; lo! thou hast that that is thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:32; JOB 15:5–6"> His lord answered, and said to him, Evil servant and slow, knewest thou that I reap where I sowed not, and gather together where I spreaded not abroad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20; ROMANS 3:19; JUDE 1:15"> Therefore it behooved thee to betake my money to exchangers, that when I came, I should have received that that is mine with usuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 19:24; LUKE 10:42"> Therefore take away from him the bezant &#91;or talent&#93;, and give to him that hath ten bezants &#91;or talents&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 8:18; MARK 4:25; MATTHEW 13:12"> For to every man that hath me shall give, and he shall increase &#91;+or For to every man having it shall be given, and he shall have plenty, or increase&#93;; but from him that hath not, also that that he seemeth to have, shall be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:15–16; MATTHEW 13:50"> And cast ye out the unprofitable servant into outer-more &#91;or uttermore&#93; darknesses; there shall be weeping, and grinding of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:7; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:7–8; MATTHEW 19:28"> When man’s Son shall come in his majesty, and all his angels with him, then he shall sit on the seat of his majesty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MALACHI 3:18; MATTHEW 13:49; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10"> and all folks shall be gathered before him, and he shall separate &#91;or part&#93; them atwain, as a shepherd separateth &#91;or parteth&#93; sheep from kids;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; JOHN 10:26–28; JOHN 21:15–17"> and he shall set the sheep on his right half, and the kids on the &#91;or his&#93; left half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:19–20; JAMES 2:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:7"> Then the king shall say to them, that shall be on his right half, Come ye, the blessed of my Father, take ye in possession the kingdom made ready to you from the making &#91;or the beginning&#93; of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:16–19; JAMES 2:15–16; DEUTERONOMY 15:7–11"> For I hungered &#91;or I was hungry&#93;, and ye gave me to eat; I thirsted, and ye gave me to drink; I was harbour-less, and ye harboured me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JAMES 1:27; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:16–18; JAMES 5:14–15"> naked, and ye covered me; sick, and ye visited me; I was in prison, and ye came to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:14; 1ST PETER 5:5–6"> Then just men shall answer to him, and say, Lord, when saw we thee hungry, and we fed thee; thirsty, and we gave to thee &#91;or we gave thee&#93; drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and when saw we thee harbour-less, and we harboured thee &#91;or we gathered thee to harbour&#93;; or naked, and we covered thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and we came to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; PROVERBS 14:31; MATTHEW 10:42"> And the king answering shall say to them, Truly I say to you, as long as ye did to one of these my least brethren, ye did to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:4; JUDE 1:6–7; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:9"> Then the king shall say also to them, that shall be on his left half, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, that is made ready to the devil and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JAMES 2:15–24; JOHN 5:23; MATTHEW 12:30"> For I hungered, and ye gave not &#91;to&#93; me to eat; I thirsted, and ye gave not &#91;to&#93; me to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> I was harbourless, and ye harboured not me &#91;or ye gathered not me to harbour&#93;; naked, and ye covered not me; sick, and in prison, and ye visited not me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:22; LUKE 10:29; MATTHEW 25:24–27"> Then and they shall answer to him, and shall say, Lord, when saw we thee hungering, or thirsting, or harbourless, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and we served not to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:13; PROVERBS 17:5; PROVERBS 14:31"> Then he shall answer to them, and say, Truly I say to you, as long as ye did not to one of these least, neither ye did to me &#91;or ye did not to me&#93;.</span>
