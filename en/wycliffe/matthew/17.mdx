<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 9:28–36; MATTHEW 26:37; MARK 5:37"> And after six days Jesus took Peter, and James, and John, his brother, and led them aside into an high hill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 104:2; MATTHEW 28:3; LUKE 9:29"> and was transfigured, &#91;or turned&#93;, into another likeness before them. And his face shone as the sun; and his clothes were made white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 24:44; MARK 9:4; MALACHI 4:5"> And lo! Moses and Elijah appeared to them, and spake with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARK 9:5–6; LUKE 9:33; JOHN 14:8–9"> And Peter answered, and said to Jesus, Lord, it is good us to be here. If thou wilt, make we here three tabernacles; to thee one, to Moses one, and one to Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:17; MATTHEW 12:18; DEUTERONOMY 18:15"> Yet while he spake, lo! a bright cloud overshadowed them; and lo! a voice out of the cloud, that said, This is my dearworthy Son, in whom I have well pleased to me; hear ye him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:14; 2ND PETER 1:18; DANIEL 8:17"> And the disciples heard, and felled down on their faces, and dreaded greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 10:18; MATTHEW 14:27; DANIEL 8:18"> And Jesus came, and touched them, and said to them, Rise up, and do not ye dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 9:36; MARK 9:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:10–11"> And they lifted up their eyes, and saw no man, but Jesus alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 8:30; MARK 9:9–13; MATTHEW 17:23"> And as they came down of the hill &#91;or down from the mountain&#93;, Jesus commanded to them, and said, Say ye to no man the vision, till man’s Son rise again from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5–6; MATTHEW 11:14; JOHN 1:21"> And his disciples asked him, and said, What then say the scribes, that it behooveth that Elijah come first?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:21; LUKE 1:16–17; MALACHI 4:6"> He answered, and said to them, Elijah shall come, and he shall restore all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:3–12; MATTHEW 16:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:24–28"> And I say to you, that Elijah is now come, and they knew him not, but they did in him whatever things they would; and so man’s Son shall suffer of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:14"> Then the disciples understood, that he said to them of John the Baptist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 9:14–29; MARK 1:40; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:25–26"> And when he came to the people, a man came to him, and felled down on his knees before him, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:24; JOB 1:10–19; MARK 9:20–22"> Lord, have mercy on my son; for he is lunatic, and suffereth evil, for oft times he falleth into the fire, and oft times into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:19–20; LUKE 9:40; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:15–16"> And I brought him to thy disciples, and they might not heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:11; MATTHEW 6:30; MARK 16:14"> Jesus answered, and said, A! thou generation unbelieveful, &#91;or out of the faith&#93;, and wayward; how long shall I be with you? how long shall I suffer you? Bring ye him hither to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:22; MARK 1:34; MARK 5:8"> And Jesus blamed him, and the devil went out from him; and the child was healed from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 9:28; MARK 4:10"> Then the disciples came to Jesus privily, and said to him, Why might not we cast him out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 17:6; MARK 11:23; LUKE 1:37"> Jesus saith to them, For your unbelief. Truly I say to you, if ye have faith, as a corn of sinapi, ye shall say to this hill, Pass thou &#91;from&#93; hence, and it shall pass; and nothing shall be impossible to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but this kind is not cast out, but by prayer and fasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21; LUKE 18:31–34; MATTHEW 17:23"> And whiles they were abiding together in Galilee, Jesus said to them, Man’s Son shall be betrayed into the hands of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21; ISAIAH 53:10–12; MARK 8:31"> and they shall slay him, and the third day he shall rise again to life. And they were &#91;made&#93; full sorry &#91;or were sorrowful greatly&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 38:26; EXODUS 30:13; MARK 9:33"> And when they came to Capernaum, they that took tribute, came to Peter, and said to him, Your master payeth not tribute?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:21; ROMANS 13:6–7; MATTHEW 22:17"> And he said, Yes. And when he was come into the house, Jesus came before him, and said, Simon, what seemeth to thee? Kings of the earth, of whom take they tribute? of their sons, either of aliens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he said, Of aliens. Jesus said to him, Then sons be free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:19–22"> But that we offend them not, go thou to the sea, and cast an hook, and take that fish that first cometh up; and, when his mouth is opened, thou shalt find a stater, &#91;that is, a certain of money&#93;; take it, and give &#91;to them&#93; for thee and for me.</span>
