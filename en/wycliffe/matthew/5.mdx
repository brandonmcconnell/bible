<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 3:13; MATTHEW 15:29; MARK 4:1"> And Jesus, seeing the people, went up into the hill; and when he was set &#91;or had sat&#93;, his disciples came to him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:2; PROVERBS 29:23; PSALMS 34:18"> Blessed be poor men in spirit, for the kingdom of heavens is theirs. &#91;Blessed be the poor in spirit, for the kingdom of heaven is theirs.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:9–10; JOHN 16:20–22"> Blessed be mild men, for they shall wield the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:11; ISAIAH 29:19; JAMES 3:13"> Blessed be they that mourn, for they shall be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; PSALMS 107:9; JOHN 6:27"> Blessed be they that hunger and thirst rightwiseness, for they shall be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:32–1; MATTHEW 6:14–15; 2ND SAMUEL 22:26"> Blessed be merciful men&#91;+or Blessed be the merciful&#93;, for they shall get mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 24:4; PSALMS 51:10; HEBREWS 10:22"> Blessed be they that be of clean heart, for they shall see God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; JAMES 3:16–18; ROMANS 12:18"> Blessed be peaceable men, for they shall be called God’s children. &#91;+Blessed be the peaceable, for they shall be called the sons of God.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; 1ST PETER 3:13–14; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:17"> Blessed be they that suffer persecution for rightwiseness, for the kingdom of heavens &#91;or the kingdom of heaven&#93; is theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:14; LUKE 21:17; MARK 13:13"> Blessed be ye, when men shall curse you, and shall pursue you, and shall say all evil against you lying, for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 6:23; JAMES 1:2; COLOSSIANS 3:24"> Joy ye, and be ye glad, for your meed is plenteous in heavens; for so they have pursued also prophets that were before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:6; LUKE 14:34–35; MARK 9:49–50"> Ye be salt of the earth; that if the salt vanish away, wherein shall it be salted? To nothing it is worth over, but that it be cast out, and be defouled of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:15; JOHN 8:12; EPHESIANS 5:8–14"> Ye be &#91;the&#93; light of the world; a city set on an hill may not be hid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 11:33; MARK 4:21; LUKE 8:16"> nor men tendeth a lantern, and putteth it under a bushel, but on a candlestick, that it give light to all that be in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:12; TITUS 2:7; JOHN 15:8"> So shine your light before men, that they see your good works, and glorify your Father that is in heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 10:4; LUKE 16:17; GALATIANS 4:4–5"> Do not ye deem, &#91;or guess&#93;, that I came to undo &#91;or to destroy&#93; the law, or the prophets; I came not to undo &#91;or to destroy&#93; the law, but to fulfill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 16:17; ISAIAH 40:8; 1ST PETER 1:25"> Forsooth I say to you, till heaven and earth pass, one letter, &#91;or one i, that is, the least letter&#93;, or one tittle shall not pass from the law, till all things be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:32; JAMES 2:10–11; MATTHEW 28:20"> Therefore he that breaketh one of these least commandments, and teach-eth thus men, shall be called the least in the realm of heavens; but he that doeth, and teacheth, shall be called great in the kingdom of heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 10:2–3; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; ROMANS 9:30–32"> And I say to you, that but your rightwiseness be more plenteous than of scribes and of Pharisees, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:17; EXODUS 20:13; NUMBERS 35:30–34"> Ye have heard that it was said to old men, Thou shalt not slay; and he that slayeth, shall be guilty to the doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:14–15; 1ST JOHN 4:20–21; 1ST JOHN 2:9"> But I say to you, that each man that is wroth to his brother, shall be guilty to doom; and he that saith to his brother, Fie!, &#91;that is, a word of scorn&#93;, shall be guilty to the council; but he that saith Fool, &#91;that is, a word of despising&#93;, shall be guilty to the fire of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARK 11:25; LEVITICUS 6:2–6; MATTHEW 5:24"> Therefore if thou offerest thy gift at the altar, and there thou bethinkest, that thy brother hath somewhat &#91;or something&#93; against thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JAMES 5:16; ROMANS 12:17–18; MATTHEW 18:15–17"> leave there thy gift before the altar, and go first to be reconciled to thy brother, and then thou shalt come, and shalt offer thy gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 12:58–59; PROVERBS 25:8; HEBREWS 3:13"> Be thou consenting to thine adversary soon, while thou art in the way with him, lest peradventure thine adversary take thee to the doomsman &#91;or the judge&#93;, and the doomsman take thee to the minister, and thou be sent into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:34; LUKE 12:59; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:9"> Truly I say to thee, Thou shalt not go out from thence, till thou yield the last farthing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:18; PROVERBS 6:32; EXODUS 20:14"> Ye have heard that it was said to old men, Thou shalt not do lechery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:16; JAMES 1:14–15; PROVERBS 6:25"> But I say to you, that every man that seeth a woman &#91;for&#93; to covet her, hath now done lechery by her in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ROMANS 8:13; MARK 9:43–48; COLOSSIANS 3:5"> That if thy right eye cause thee to stumble, pull it out, and cast it from thee; for it speedeth to thee, that one of thy members perish, than that all thy body go into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MARK 9:43; MATTHEW 5:29; 1ST PETER 2:8"> And if thy right hand cause thee to stumble, cut it away, and cast &#91;it&#93; from thee; for it speedeth to thee, that one of thy members perish, than that all thy body go into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:1; DEUTERONOMY 24:1–4; MATTHEW 19:7"> And it hath been said, Whoever leaveth his wife, give he to her a libel &#91;or a little book&#93; of forsaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ROMANS 7:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:10–11; LUKE 16:18"> But I say to you, that every man that leaveth his wife, except &#91;the&#93; cause of fornication, maketh her to do lechery, and he that weddeth the forsaken wife, doeth adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:21; LEVITICUS 19:12; ECCLESIASTES 5:4–6"> Again ye have heard, that it was said to old men, Thou shalt not forswear, but thou shalt yield thine oaths to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JAMES 5:12; ISAIAH 66:1; DEUTERONOMY 23:21–23"> But I say to you, that ye swear not for anything; neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 48:2; MALACHI 1:14; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:6"> neither by the earth, for it is the stool of his feet; neither by Jerusalem, for it is the city of a great &#91;or the great&#93; king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKE 12:25; MATTHEW 6:27; MATTHEW 23:16–21"> neither thou shalt swear by thine head, for thou mayest not make one hair white, or black;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JAMES 5:12; COLOSSIANS 4:6; COLOSSIANS 3:9"> but be your word, Yea, yea; Nay, nay; and that that is more than these, is of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:19–20; DEUTERONOMY 19:21; EXODUS 21:22–27"> Ye have heard that it hath been said, Eye for eye, and tooth for tooth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15; 1ST PETER 3:9; ROMANS 12:17–19"> But I say to you, that ye against-stand not an evil man, &#91;or to not against-stand evil&#93;; but if any smite thee in the right cheek, show &#91;or give&#93; to him also the other;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 6:29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:7"> and to him that will strive with thee in doom, and take away thy coat, leave thou to him also thy mantle &#91;or thine over-cloth&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 23:26; MATTHEW 27:32; MARK 15:21"> and whoever constraineth thee a thousand paces, go thou with him other twain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; HEBREWS 13:16; LUKE 6:30–36"> Give thou to him that asketh of thee, and turn thou not away from him that will borrow of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:18; PSALMS 139:21–22; GALATIANS 5:13–14"> Ye have heard that it was said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate thine enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="ROMANS 12:14; 1ST PETER 3:9; LUKE 6:27–28"> But I say to you, love ye your enemies, do ye well to them that hate you, and pray ye for them that pursue, and slander you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PSALMS 145:9; LUKE 6:35; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:17"> that ye be the sons of your Father that is in heavens, that maketh his sun to rise upon good and evil men, and raineth on just men and unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 6:32–35; 1ST PETER 2:20–23; MATTHEW 9:10–11"> For if ye love them that love you, what meed shall ye have? whether &#91;the&#93; publicans do not this thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:20; LUKE 6:32; 1ST PETER 2:20"> And if ye greet your brethren only, what shall ye do more&#91;over&#93;? do not heathen men &#91;or pagans&#93; this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:15–16; JAMES 1:4; LEVITICUS 19:2"> Therefore be ye perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect.</span>
