<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:18; LUKE 4:1–13; MARK 1:12–15"> Then Jesus was led of a spirit into desert, to be tempted of the fiend &#91;or the devil&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:18; EXODUS 34:28; 1ST KINGS 19:8"> And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, afterward he hungered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 3:1–5; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:5; NUMBERS 11:4–6"> And the tempter came nigh, and said to him, If thou be God’s Son &#91;or If thou art the Son of God&#93;, say that these stones be made loaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:3; LUKE 4:4; JOHN 6:63"> Which answered, and said to him, It is written, Not only in bread liveth man, but in each word that cometh forth of God’s mouth, &#91;or A man liveth not in bread alone, but in every word that cometh forth from the mouth of God&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 4:9; MATTHEW 27:53; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:2"> Then the fiend &#91;or the devil&#93; took him into the holy city, and setted him on the pinnacle of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 91:11–12; HEBREWS 1:14; PSALMS 34:7"> and said to him, If thou art God’s Son, send thee adown, &#91;or If thou art the Son of God, send thee down&#93;; for it is written, That to his angels he commanded of thee, and they shall take thee in hands, lest peradventure thou hurt thy foot at a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:9; PSALMS 78:18"> Again Jesus said to him, It is written, Thou shalt not tempt thy Lord God &#91;or the Lord thy God&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:15–16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15; PSALMS 49:16–17"> Again the fiend &#91;or the devil&#93; took him &#91;up&#93; into a full high hill, and showed to him all the realms of the world, and the joy &#91;or the glory&#93; of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4; DANIEL 5:18–19; JOHN 16:11"> and said to him, All these I shall give to thee, if thou fall down and worship me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:13–14; DEUTERONOMY 10:20; JOSHUA 24:14"> Then Jesus said to him, Go, Satan; for it is written, Thou shalt worship thy Lord God &#91;or the Lord thy God&#93;, and to him alone thou shalt serve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:53; HEBREWS 1:14; LUKE 22:43"> Then the fiend &#91;or the devil&#93; left him; and lo! angels came nigh, and served to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 1:14; LUKE 3:20; MATTHEW 14:3"> But when Jesus had heard that John was taken, he went into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 4:46; MARK 1:21; MATTHEW 11:23"> And he left the city of Nazareth, and came, and dwelt in the city of Capernaum, beside the sea, in the coasts of Zebulun and Naphtali,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:1–2; MATTHEW 2:23; LUKE 24:44"> that it should be fulfilled, that was said by Isaiah, the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:1; 2ND KI 15:29; JOSHUA 21:32"> The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, the way of the sea over Jordan, of Galilee of heathen men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:2; ISAIAH 42:6–7; ISAIAH 60:1–3"> the people that walked &#91;or that dwelt&#93; in darknesses saw great light, and while men sat in the country of &#91;the&#93; shadow of death, light arose to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; MARK 1:14–15"> From that time Jesus began to preach, and &#91;to&#93; say, Do ye penance, for the kingdom of heavens shall come nigh &#91;or the realm of heaven hath nighed&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 1:40–42; MATTHEW 10:2; LUKE 6:14"> And Jesus walked beside the sea of Galilee, and saw two brethren, Simon, that is called Peter, and Andrew, his brother, casting nets into the sea; for they were fishers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 1:17–18; JOHN 12:26; MATTHEW 16:24"> And he said to them, Come ye after me, and I shall make you to be made fishers of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 10:28–31; MATTHEW 19:27; LUKE 18:28–30"> And anon they left their nets, and pursued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARK 1:19–20; MATTHEW 10:2; MARK 3:17"> And he went forth from that place, and saw twain other brethren, James of Zebedee, and John, his brother, in a ship with Zebedee, their father, amending their nets, and he called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:37; LUKE 9:59–60; DEUTERONOMY 33:9–10"> And anon they left the nets and the father, and pursued him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:35; MARK 1:39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:38"> And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in the synagogues of them, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every languor, &#91;or all sorrow, or ache&#93;, and each sickness among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:6; MATTHEW 17:15; LUKE 5:15"> And his fame went into all Syria; and they brought to him all that were at mal-ease, and that were taken with diverse languors &#91;or sorrows&#93;, and torments, and them that had fiends &#91;or devils&#93;, and lunatic men, and men in &#91;the&#93; palsy, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 6:17; MARK 5:20; MARK 3:7–8"> And there pursued him much people of Galilee, and of Decapolis, and of Jerusalem, and of Judea, and of beyond Jordan.</span>
