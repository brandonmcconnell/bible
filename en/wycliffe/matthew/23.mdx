<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 12:1; LUKE 20:45–46; MATTHEW 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spake to the people, and to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 20:46; MARK 12:38; MALACHI 2:7"> and said, On the chair of Moses, scribes and Pharisees have sat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:5; TITUS 1:16; ROMANS 2:19–24"> Therefore keep ye, and do ye all things, whatever things they say to you. But do not ye do after their works; for they say, and do not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 11:46; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:10; GALATIANS 6:13"> And they bind grievous charges, and that may not be borne, and put on &#91;the&#93; shoulders of men; but with their finger they will not move them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:1–16; JOHN 12:43; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> Therefore they do all their works to be seen of men; for they draw abroad their phylacteries, and magnify &#91;their&#93; hems.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3RD JOHN 1:9; LUKE 11:43–54; MARK 12:38–39"> And they love the first sitting places in &#91;or at&#93; suppers, and the first chairs in synagogues;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 1:38; JOHN 1:49; JOHN 3:26"> and salutations in &#91;the&#93; chapping, and to be called of men, master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 3:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:12–13; 1ST PETER 5:3"> But do not ye be called master; for one is your master, and all ye be brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:1; HEBREWS 12:9; MALACHI 1:6"> And do not ye call to you a father on earth, for one is your Father, that is in heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Neither be ye called masters, for one is your master, Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:19; GALATIANS 5:13; JOHN 13:14–15"> He that is greatest among you, shall be your minister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:5; JAMES 4:6; PROVERBS 29:23"> For he that higheth himself, shall be meeked; and he that meeketh himself, shall be enhanced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 11:52; MATTHEW 23:29; MATTHEW 23:23"> But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that close the kingdom of heavens &#91;or the realm of heaven&#93; before men; and ye enter not, neither suffer other men entering to enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that eat the houses of widows, and pray by long prayer; for this thing ye shall take the more doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:43"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that go about the sea and the land, to make one proselyte; and when he is made, ye make him a son of hell, double more than ye be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:14; MATTHEW 5:33–35; JAMES 5:12"> Woe to you, blind leaders, that say, Whoever sweareth by the temple of God, it is nothing; but he that sweareth in the gold of the temple, is debtor &#91;or oweth&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:19; NUMBERS 16:38–39; PSALMS 94:8"> Ye fools and blind, for what is greater, the gold, or the temple that halloweth the gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And whoever sweareth in the altar, it is nothing; but he that sweareth in the gift that is on the altar, oweth &#91;or is a debtor&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 29:37; EXODUS 30:29"> Blind men, for what is more, the gift, or the altar that halloweth the gift?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore he that sweareth in the altar, sweareth in it, and in all things that be thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 26:8; 1ST KINGS 8:13; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:2"> And he that sweareth in the temple, sweareth in it, and in him that dwelleth in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:34; PSALMS 11:4; ISAIAH 66:1"> And he that sweareth in heaven, sweareth in the throne of God, and in him that sitteth thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 11:42; MICAH 6:8; HOSEA 6:6"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that tithe mint, anise, and cumin, and have left those things that be of more charge of the law, doom, and mercy, and faith. And it behooved &#91;or needed&#93; to do these things, and not to leave those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:24; MATTHEW 27:6–8; MATTHEW 15:2–6"> Blind leaders, cleansing a gnat, but swallowing a camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 11:39–40; MATTHEW 15:19–20; MARK 7:4–13"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that cleanse the cup and the platter withoutforth; but within ye be full of raven, and uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 7:1; JAMES 4:8; HEBREWS 10:22"> Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first the cup and the platter withinforth, that &#91;also&#93; that that is withoutforth be made clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 11:44; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:3; NUMBERS 19:16"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that be like to sepulchres whited &#91;or made white&#93;, which with-outforth seem fair to men; but within they be full of bones of dead men, and of all filth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 16:15; PSALMS 51:6; MATTHEW 23:5"> So ye withoutforth seem just &#91;or rightful&#93; to men; but within ye be full of hypocrisy and wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 11:47–48; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:29"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, that build &#91;the&#93; sepulchres of prophets, and make fair the burials of just men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:30; MATTHEW 23:34–35; MATTHEW 21:35–36"> and say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we should not have been their fellows in the blood of prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51–52; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:15–16; JOSHUA 24:22"> And so ye be in witnessing to yourselves, that ye be the sons of them that slew the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 15:16; NUMBERS 32:14; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:16"> And full-fill ye the measure of your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:7; MATTHEW 12:34; MATTHEW 5:22"> Ye adders, and adders’ brood &#91;or fruits of adders&#93;, how shall ye flee from the doom of hell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:16–17; MATTHEW 13:52; COLOSSIANS 1:28"> Therefore lo! I send to you prophets, and wise men, and scribes &#91;or writers&#93;; and of them ye shall slay and crucify, and of them ye shall scourge in your synagogues, and shall pursue from city into city &#91;or from city to city&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 24:20–22; ZECHARIAH 1:1; HEBREWS 11:4"> that all the just blood come on you, that was shed on the earth, from the blood of just Abel to the blood of Zechariah, the son of Barachiah, whom ye slew betwixt the temple and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:23; MATTHEW 24:34; MARK 13:30–31"> Truly I say to you, all these things shall come on this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 13:34–35; RUTH 2:12; PSALMS 91:4"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that slayest prophets, and stonest them that be sent to thee, how oft would I gather together thy children, as an hen gathereth together her chickens under her wings, and thou wouldest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:5; LUKE 13:35; LUKE 21:24"> Lo! your house shall be left to you desert &#91;or forsaken&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 118:26; MATTHEW 21:9; ROMANS 11:25"> And I say to you, ye shall not see me from henceforth, till ye say, Blessed is he, that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span>
