<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:3; MALACHI 3:1; JOHN 1:23"> For this is he, of whom it is said by Isaiah, the prophet, saying, A voice of a crier in desert, &#91;or A voice of a man crying in desert&#93;, Make ye ready the ways of the Lord; make ye right the paths of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND KI 1:8; ZECHARIAH 13:4; MARK 1:6"> And this John had clothing &#91;or a cloth&#93; of camel’s hairs, and a girdle of skin about his loins; and his meat was honeysuckles &#91;or locusts&#93;, and honey of the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARK 1:5; LUKE 3:7; JOHN 3:23"> Then Jerusalem went out to him, and all Judea, and all the country about Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:18; MATTHEW 3:13–16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38–41"> and they were washed &#91;or christened&#93; of him in Jordan, acknowledging their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:34; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:10; ROMANS 5:9"> But he saw many of the Pharisees and of Sadducees coming to his baptism, and said to them, Generation of adders, who showed to you to flee from the wrath that is to come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 3:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:20; 2ND PETER 1:4–8"> Therefore do ye worthy fruits of penance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 3:8; ROMANS 9:7–8; GALATIANS 3:27–29"> and do not ye say within you, We have Abraham to our father &#91;or We have the father Abraham&#93;; for I say to you, that God is mighty to raise up of these stones the sons of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 15:6; LUKE 3:9; MATTHEW 7:19"> And now the ax is put to the root of the tree; therefore every tree that maketh not good fruit, shall be cut down, and shall be cast into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 4:4; LUKE 3:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:5"> I wash you in water, into penance; but he that shall come after me is stronger than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear; he shall baptize &#91;or christen&#93; you in the Holy Ghost, and &#91;in&#93; fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MALACHI 4:1; MATTHEW 13:30; LUKE 3:17"> Whose winnowing cloth, &#91;or tool, or fan&#93;, is in his hand, and he shall fully cleanse his cornfloor, and shall gather his wheat into his barn; but the chaff he shall burn with fire that may not be quenched &#91;or that is unquench-able&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 1:32–34; MATTHEW 2:22; MARK 1:9–11"> Then Jesus came from Galilee into Jordan to John, to be baptized of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 3:3–7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:5–8; JOHN 13:6–8"> And John forbade him, and said, I owe to be baptized of thee, and thou comest to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 4:34; JOHN 15:10; PSALMS 40:7–8"> But Jesus answered, and said to him, Suffer now, for thus it falleth to us to fulfill all rightwiseness. Then John suffered him &#91;or let him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:2; MARK 1:10; LUKE 3:21–22"> And when Jesus was baptized, anon he went up from the water; and lo! heavens were opened to him, and he saw the Spirit of God coming down as a dove, and coming &#91;up&#93; on him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:1; PSALMS 2:7; MATTHEW 17:5"> and lo! a voice from heaven, saying, This is my &#91;be&#93; loved Son, in which I have well pleased to me.</span>
