<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:30; PSALMS 106:23; ISAIAH 58:12"> Ye ascended not &#91;or went not up&#93; even against, neither again-setted &#91;or again-putted&#93; a wall for the house of Israel, that ye should stand in battle in the day of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:8; EZEKIEL 22:28; JEREMIAH 28:15"> They see vain things, and divine a leasing, and say, The Lord saith, when the Lord sent not them; and they continued to confirm the word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:6; MATTHEW 24:23–24; EZEKIEL 13:2–3"> Whether ye saw not a vain vision, and spake false divining, and said, The Lord saith, when I spake not?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:8; EZEKIEL 21:3; EZEKIEL 28:22"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, For ye spake vain things, and saw a leasing, therefore lo! I to you, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 87:6; PSALMS 69:28; EZEKIEL 20:38"> And mine hand shall be on the prophets that see vain things, and divine leasings; they shall not be in the counsels of my people, and they shall not be written in the scripture of the house of Israel, neither they shall enter into the land of Israel; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:28; EZEKIEL 13:16; JEREMIAH 6:14"> For they deceived my people, and said, Peace, peace, and no peace is; and it builded a wall, but they pargeted it with fen without chaffs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:22; ISAIAH 28:2; NAHUM 1:7–8"> Say thou to them that parget without tempering, that it shall fall down; for a strong rain shall be overflowing, and I shall give full great stones falling from above, and I shall give a wind of tempest that destroyeth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND KI 3:13; LAMENTATIONS 2:14–15; JUDGES 9:38"> For lo! the wall fell down. Whether it shall not be said to you, Where is the pargeting, which ye pargeted?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:30; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:21; PSALMS 148:8"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, And I shall make the spirit of tempests to break out in mine indignation, and &#91;a&#93; strong rain flowing in my strong vengeance shall be, and great hailstones in &#91;my&#93; wrath into wasting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MICAH 1:6; JEREMIAH 14:15; JEREMIAH 6:15"> And I shall destroy the wall, which ye have pargeted without tempering, and I shall make it even with the earth; and the foundament thereof shall be showed, and it shall fall down, and it shall be wasted in the midst thereof; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:13; PSALMS 62:3; NEHEMIAH 4:3"> And I shall &#91;ful&#93; fill mine indignation in the wall, and in them that pargeted it without tempering; and I shall say to you, The wall is not, and they be not, that pargeted it,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:10; JEREMIAH 6:14; JEREMIAH 8:11"> that is, the prophets of Israel, that prophesy to Jerusalem, and see to it the vision of peace, and peace is not, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:2; 2ND KI 22:14; JUDGES 4:4"> And thou, son of man, set thy face against the daughters of thy people, that prophesy of their heart; and prophesy thou on them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:14; EZEKIEL 13:20; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:3"> and say thou, The Lord God saith these things, Woe to them that sew together cushions under each cubit of &#91;the&#93; hand, and make pillows under the head of each age, to take souls; and when they deceived the souls of my people, they quickened the souls of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MICAH 3:5; PROVERBS 28:21; JEREMIAH 23:17"> And they defouled me to my people, for an handful of barley, and for a gobbet of bread, that they should slay souls that die not, and quicken souls that live not; and they lied to my people, believing to leasings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:18; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:8–9; EZEKIEL 13:8–9"> For this thing the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I to your cushions, by which ye deceive souls flying; and I shall all-break those &#91;or them&#93; from your arms, and I shall deliver &#91;the&#93; souls which ye deceive, souls that ye make to fly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 91:3"> And I shall all-break your pillows, and I shall deliver my people from your hand; and they shall no more be in your hands, to be robbed; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:14; JEREMIAH 14:13–17; 2ND PETER 2:18–19"> For that that ye made falsely the heart of a just man to mourn, whom I made not sorry; and ye comforted the hands of a wicked man, that he should not turn again from his evil way, and live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:21; EZEKIEL 12:24; MICAH 3:6"> Therefore ye shall not see vain things, and ye shall no more divine false divinings; and I shall deliver my people from your hand, and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
