<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:1; EZEKIEL 20:1; 2ND KI 6:32"> And men of the elders of Israel came to me, and sat before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; 1ST KINGS 14:4"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:7; EZEKIEL 7:19; EZEKIEL 20:3"> Son of man, these men have set &#91;or put&#93; their uncleannesses in their hearts, and have set steadfastly the cause of stumbling of their wickedness against their faces. Whether I that am asked, shall answer to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:4; EZEKIEL 14:7; ISAIAH 3:11"> For this thing speak thou to them, and thou shalt say to them, These things saith the Lord God, A man, a man of the house of Israel, that setteth &#91;or hath put&#93; his uncleannesses in his heart, and setteth steadfastly the cause of stumbling of his wickedness against his face, and cometh to the prophet, and asketh me by him, I the Lord shall answer to him in the multitude of his uncleannesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:4; ZECHARIAH 11:8; ROMANS 1:28"> that the house of Israel be taken in their heart, by which they went away from me in all their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:30; ISAIAH 30:22; EZEKIEL 36:31–32"> Therefore say thou to the house of Israel, The Lord God saith these things, Be ye converted, and go ye away from your idols, and turn away your faces from all your filths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 12:48; ISAIAH 58:1–2; LEVITICUS 24:22"> For why a man, a man of the house of Israel, and of converts, who-ever is a comeling in Israel, if he is alienated from me, and setteth &#91;or shall put&#93; his idols in his heart, and setteth steadfastly the cause of stumbling of his wickedness against his face, and he cometh to the prophet, to ask me by him, I the Lord shall answer him by myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 15:7; JEREMIAH 44:11; EZEKIEL 5:15"> And I shall set my face on that man, and I shall make him into ensample, and into a proverb, and I shall lose him from the midst of my people; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:25; JEREMIAH 4:10; PSALMS 81:11–12"> And when a prophet erreth, and speaketh a word, I the Lord shall deceive that prophet; and I shall stretch forth mine hand &#91;up&#93; on him, and I shall do him away from the midst of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:49; EZEKIEL 14:4; JEREMIAH 8:11–12"> And they shall bear their wicked-ness; by the wickedness of the asker, so the wickedness of the prophet shall be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:23; EZEKIEL 44:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:7"> that the house of Israel err no more from me, neither be defouled in all their trespassings; but that it be into a people to me, and I be into God to them, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:26; EZEKIEL 4:16; ISAIAH 24:20"> Son of man, when the land sinneth against me, that it trespassing do trespass, I shall stretch forth mine hand on it, and I shall all-break the staff of bread thereof; and I shall send hunger into it, and I shall slay of it man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:20; JEREMIAH 15:1; EZEKIEL 28:3"> And if these three men, Noah, Daniel, and Job, be in the midst thereof, they by their rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; shall deliver their souls, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:17; LEVITICUS 26:22; JEREMIAH 15:3"> That if also I bring in worst beasts on the land, that I destroy it, and if it is without a way, for that no passer is for the beasts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 19:29; JAMES 5:16; EZEKIEL 33:11"> and these three men that be before-said, be therein, I live, saith the Lord God, for they shall neither deliver sons, neither daughters, but they alone shall be delivered; forsooth the land shall be made desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:25; EZEKIEL 21:3–4; ZEPHANIAH 1:3"> Either if I bring in a sword on that land, and I say to the sword, Pass thou through the land, and I slay of it man and beast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and these three men be in the midst thereof, I live, saith the Lord God, that they shall not deliver sons neither daughters, but they alone shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:22; EZEKIEL 5:12; JEREMIAH 14:12"> Forsooth if I bring in also pestilence on that land, and I shed out mine indignation on it in blood, that I do away from it man and beast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:14; 1ST JOHN 2:29; ISAIAH 3:10"> and Noah, and Daniel, and Job, be in the midst thereof, I live, saith the Lord God, for they shall not deliver a son and a daughter, but they by their rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; shall deliver their souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:27; EZEKIEL 5:17; EZEKIEL 14:13"> For the Lord God saith these things, That though I send in my four worst dooms, sword, and hunger, and evil beasts, and pestilence, into Jeru-salem, that I slay of it man and beast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:16; EZEKIEL 20:43; HEBREWS 12:6–11"> nevertheless salvation of them that led out sons and daughters, shall be left therein. Lo! they shall go out to you, and ye shall see the way of them, and the findings of them; and ye shall be comforted on the evil, which I brought in on Jerusalem, in all things which I bare in on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:4; DEUTERONOMY 8:2"> And they shall comfort you, when ye shall see the way of them, and the findings of them; and ye shall know, that not in vain I did all things, whatever things I did therein, saith the Lord Almighty.</span>
