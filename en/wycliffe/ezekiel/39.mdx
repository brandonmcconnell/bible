<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:2–3; EZEKIEL 35:3; NAHUM 3:5"> But prophesy thou, son of man, against Gog; and thou shalt say, The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I on thee, thou Gog, prince of the head of Meshech and of Tubal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 40:14; EZEKIEL 38:15; ISAIAH 37:29"> And I shall lead thee about, and I shall deceive thee, and I shall make thee to go up from the sides of the north, and I shall bring thee on the hills of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 76:3; HOSEA 1:5; JEREMIAH 21:4–5"> And I shall smite thy bow in thy left hand, and I shall cast down thine arrows from thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:17–20; EZEKIEL 33:27; EZEKIEL 38:21"> Thou shalt fall down on the hills of Israel, thou, and all thy companies, and peoples that be with thee; I gave thee for to be devoured to wild beasts, to birds, and to each volatile, and to the beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:19; EZEKIEL 32:4; JEREMIAH 8:2"> Thou shalt fall down on the face of the field; for I the Lord have spoken, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:16; AMOS 1:4; EZEKIEL 30:8"> And I shall send fire in Magog, and in them that dwell trustily in isles; and they shall know, that I am the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; EZEKIEL 38:23; EXODUS 20:7"> And I shall make mine holy name known in the midst of my people Israel, and I shall no more let them defoul mine holy name; and heathen men shall know, that I am the Lord God, the Holy of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:17; EZEKIEL 7:2–10"> Lo! it cometh, and it is done, saith the Lord God. This is the day of which I spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 46:9; ISAIAH 66:24; MALACHI 1:5"> And dwellers shall go out of the cities of Israel, and they shall set afire, and shall burn arms, shield and spear, bow and arrows, and staves of hand, and shafts without iron; and they shall burn those &#91;or them&#93; in fire by seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 33:1; ISAIAH 14:2; MICAH 5:8"> And they shall not bear trees of countries, neither shall cut down of &#91;the&#93; forests, for they shall burn arms by fire; and they shall take preys of them, to whom they were preys, and they shall ravish their wasters, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:18; NUMBERS 34:11; EZEKIEL 38:2"> And it shall be in that day, I shall give to Gog a named place, a sepulchre in Israel, the valley of way-goers at the east of the sea, that shall make them that pass forth for to wonder; and they shall bury there Gog, and all the multitude of him, and it shall be called The valley of the multitude of Gog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:23; EZEKIEL 39:14; EZEKIEL 39:16"> And the house of Israel shall bury them, that they cleanse the land in seven months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:22; ZEPHANIAH 3:19–20; JEREMIAH 33:9"> Forsooth all the people of the land shall bury them, and it shall be a named day to them, in which I am glorified, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:12; NUMBERS 19:11–19"> And they shall ordain busily men compassing the land, that shall bury and seek them that were left on the face of the land, that they cleanse it. Forsooth after seven months they shall begin to seek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 11:44"> and they shall compass going about the land; and when they shall see the bone of a man, they shall set a title, or a notable sign, beside it, till the buriers of carrions bury it in the valley of the multitude of Gog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Soothly the name of the city is Hamonah; and they shall cleanse the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:17–18; ISAIAH 34:6"> Forsooth, thou, son of man, the Lord God saith these things, Say thou to each bird, and to all fowls, and to all beasts of the field, Come ye together, and haste ye, run ye to-gether on each side to my sacrifice, which I slay to you, a great sacrifice on the hills of Israel, that ye eat fleshes, and drink blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 22:12; JEREMIAH 51:40; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:17–18"> Ye shall eat the fleshes of strong men, and ye shall drink the blood of princes of earth, of wethers, of lambs, and of bucks of goats, and of bulls, and of beasts made fat, and of all fat things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And ye shall eat the inner fatness into fullness, and ye shall drink the blood into drunkenness, of the sacrifice which I shall slay to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:18; HAGGAI 2:22; PSALMS 76:5–6"> And ye shall be filled on my board, of horse, and of strong horse-man, &#91;or knight&#93;, and of all men warriors, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; EZEKIEL 38:23; ISAIAH 37:20"> And I shall set my glory among heathen men, and all heathen men shall see my doom, which I have done, and mine hand, which I have set &#91;or put&#93; on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:7; EZEKIEL 28:26; EZEKIEL 39:28"> And the house of Israel shall know, that I am their Lord God, from that day and afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; ISAIAH 1:15; ISAIAH 59:2"> And heathen men shall know, that the house of Israel is taken in their wickedness, for that that they forsook me; and I hid my face from them, and I betook them into the hands of enemies, and all they fell down by sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:19; JEREMIAH 2:17; JEREMIAH 4:18"> By the uncleanness and great trespass of them I did to them, and I hid my face from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:13; ISAIAH 27:12–13; HOSEA 1:11"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, Now I shall lead again the captivity of Jacob, and I shall have mercy on all the house of Israel; and I shall take fervor for mine holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MICAH 4:4; 1ST KINGS 4:25; EZEKIEL 16:63"> And they shall bear their shame, and all their trespassing by which they trespassed against me, when they dwelled in their land trustily, and dreaded no man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; EZEKIEL 38:23; EZEKIEL 36:23–24"> and when I shall bring them again from peoples, and shall gather &#91;them&#93; from the lands of their enemies, and shall be hallowed in them, before the eyes of full many folks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:22; AMOS 9:9; ISAIAH 27:12"> And they shall know, that I am the Lord God of them, for that I translated them into nations, and have gathered them on their land, and I left not any of them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOEL 2:28; 1ST JOHN 3:24; ISAIAH 32:15"> And I shall no more hide my face from them, for I have shed out my spirit on all the house of Israel, saith the Lord God.</span>
