<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:7; ISAIAH 48:4; JEREMIAH 5:3"> And the sons be of hard face, and of unchastiseable heart, to whom I send thee. And thou shalt say to them, The Lord God saith these things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:33; EZEKIEL 3:27; MATTHEW 10:12–15"> if peradventure namely they hear, and if peradventure they rest, for it is an house stirring to wrath. And they shall know, that a prophet is in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:8; ISAIAH 51:12; 1ST PETER 3:14"> Therefore thou, son of man, dread not them, neither dread thou the words of them; for unbelieveful men and destroyers be with thee, and thou dwellest with scorpions. Dread thou not the words of them, and dread thou not the faces of them, for it is an house stirring to wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:28; EZEKIEL 3:17; JEREMIAH 1:7"> Therefore thou shalt speak my words to them, if peradventure they hear, and rest, for they be stirrers to wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 10:9; JEREMIAH 15:16; ISAIAH 50:5"> But thou, son of man, hear what-ever things I shall speak to thee; and do not thou be a stirrer to wrath, as the house of Israel is a stirrer to wrath. Open thy mouth, and eat whatever things I give to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:1–5; DANIEL 10:10"> And I saw, and lo! an hand was sent to me, in which a book was folded together.</span>
