<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:1; EZEKIEL 40:17; EZEKIEL 42:14"> And he turned me to the way of the gate of the outermore saintuary, which gate beheld to the east, and was closed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:2–4; EXODUS 24:10; ISAIAH 6:1–5"> And the Lord said to me, This gate shall be closed, and shall not be opened, and a man shall not pass through it; for the Lord God of Israel entered &#91;in&#93; by it, and it shall be closed to the prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:2; EZEKIEL 34:24; EZEKIEL 37:25"> The prince himself shall sit therein, that he eat bread before the Lord; he shall go in by the way of the gate of the porch, and he shall go out by the way thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:28; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:17; EZEKIEL 3:23"> And he led me by the way of the north gate, in the sight of the house of the Lord; and I saw, and lo! the glory of the Lord filled the house of the Lord; and I fell down on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 40:4; EZEKIEL 43:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 12:32"> And the Lord said to me, Thou, son of man, set thine heart, and see with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears all things which I speak to thee, of all the ceremonies of the house of the Lord, and of all the laws thereof; and thou shalt set thine heart in the ways of the temple, by all the goings out of the saintuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:9; EZEKIEL 45:9; 1ST PETER 4:3"> And thou shalt say to the house of Israel stirring me to wrath, The Lord God saith these things, Ye house of Israel, all your great trespasses suffice to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:25; JEREMIAH 9:26; EZEKIEL 44:9"> for ye bring in alien sons, uncir-cumcised in heart, and uncircumcised in flesh, that they be in my saintuary, and defoul mine house. And ye offer my loaves, inner fatness, and blood, and break my covenant in all your great trespasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:14; NUMBERS 18:3–5; EZEKIEL 40:45–46"> And ye kept not the command-ments of my saintuary, and ye setted keepers of my keepings in my saintuary to yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOEL 3:17; EZEKIEL 44:7; ZECHARIAH 14:21"> The Lord God saith these things, Each alien, uncircumcised in heart, and uncircumcised in flesh, shall not enter into my saintuary; yea, each alien son, which is in the midst of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:26; EZEKIEL 48:11; 2ND KI 23:8–9"> But also &#91;the&#93; Levites, &#91;or deacons&#93;, that went far away from me, in the error of the sons of Israel, and erred from me after their idols, and bare their wickedness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:9; 2ND CHRONICLES 29:34; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:17"> they shall be keepers of &#91;the&#93; houses in my saintuary, and porters of gates of the house, and ministers of the house; they shall slay burnt sacrifices, and sacrifices for victory of the people; and they shall stand in the sight of the priests for to minister to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND KI 16:10–16; PSALMS 106:26; EZEKIEL 14:3–4"> For that that they ministered to those &#91;or them&#93; in the sight of their idols, and were made to the house of Israel into offending, either hurting, of wickedness; therefore I raised mine hand on them, saith the Lord God, and they bare their wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:3; 2ND KI 23:9; EZEKIEL 16:61"> And they shall not nigh to me, that they use priesthood to me, neither they shall nigh to all my saintuary beside &#91;the&#93; holy of holy things, but they shall bear their shame, and their great trespasses which they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 23:28–32; NUMBERS 18:4"> And I shall make them porters of the house, in all the service thereof, and in all things that be done therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:8; EZEKIEL 48:11; EZEKIEL 44:7"> Forsooth priests and deacons, the sons of Zadok, that kept the ceremonies of my saintuary, when the sons of Israel erred from me, they shall nigh to me, for to minister to me; and they shall stand in my sight, that they offer to me inner fatness and blood, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 41:22; MALACHI 1:12; NUMBERS 18:7–8"> They shall enter into my saintuary, and they shall nigh to my board, that they minister to me, and keep my ceremonies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:8; EXODUS 28:43; EXODUS 39:27–29"> And when they shall enter into the gates of the inner foreyard, they shall be clothed with linen clothes, neither any woolen thing shall go on them, when they minister in the gates of the inner foreyard, and within;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 39:28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:4–10; ISAIAH 3:20"> linen caps, either mitres, shall be in the heads of them, and linen breeches shall be in the loins of them, and they shall not be gird in sweat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:20; LEVITICUS 6:27; LEVITICUS 6:10–11"> And when they shall go out at the outermore foreyard to the people, they shall despoil them of their clothes in which they ministered, and they shall lay those &#91;or them&#93; up in the chambers of the saintuary; and they shall clothe themselves in other clothes, and they shall not hallow my people in their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:14; NUMBERS 6:5; LEVITICUS 21:5–24"> Forsooth they shall not shave their head&#91;s&#93;, neither they shall nurse long hair, but they clipping shall clip their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:9; TITUS 1:7–8; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:23"> And each priest shall not drink wine, when he shall enter into the inner foreyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:7; LEVITICUS 21:13–14; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:2"> And priests shall not take for wives a widow, and a forsaken woman, but virgins of the seed of the house of Israel; but also they shall take a widow, which is the widow of a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:26; LEVITICUS 10:10–11; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:24–25"> And they shall teach my people, what is betwixt holy thing and defouled; and they shall show to them, what is betwixt clean thing and unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 23:4; EZEKIEL 22:26; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:8–10"> And when debate is, they shall stand in my dooms, and shall deem by my laws; and they shall keep my commandments in all my solemnities, and they shall hallow my sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:16; LEVITICUS 22:4; MATTHEW 8:21–22"> And they shall not enter to a dead man, lest they be defouled; no but to father, and mother, and to son, and daughter, and to brother, and sister that had not an husband, in which they shall be defouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:13–14; NUMBERS 19:11–14; NUMBERS 6:10–21"> And after that he is cleansed, seven days shall be numbered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:9–11; EZEKIEL 44:17; LEVITICUS 4:3–35"> And in the day of his entering into the saintuary, to the inner foreyard, that he minister to me in the saintuary, he shall offer for his sin, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:33; NUMBERS 18:20; DEUTERONOMY 18:1–2"> Forsooth none heritage shall be to them, I am the heritage of them; and ye shall not give to them a possession in Israel, for I am the possession of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:14; LEVITICUS 27:21; LEVITICUS 27:28"> They shall eat sacrifice, and that for sin, and that for trespass, and each avow &#91;or vow&#93; of Israel shall be theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:35–37; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:10; DEUTERONOMY 18:4"> And the first things of all first engendered things, and all moist sacrifices, of all things that be offered, shall be the priests’ part; and ye shall give the first things of your meats to the priest, that he lay up blessing to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:8; EXODUS 22:31; DEUTERONOMY 14:21"> Priests shall not eat anything dead by itself, and taken of a beast, of fowls, and of sheep.</span>
