<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:1; EZEKIEL 24:6; EZEKIEL 24:9"> And thou, son of man, whether thou deemest not the city of bloods? And thou shalt show to it all his abominations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:27; ROMANS 2:5; 2ND PETER 2:3"> and thou shalt say, The Lord God saith these things, This is a city shedding out blood in the midst of itself, that the time thereof come; and which made idols against itself, that it should be defouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND KI 21:16; PSALMS 44:13–14; EZEKIEL 22:2"> In thy blood which is shed out of thee, thou trespassedest, and thou art defouled in thine idols which thou madest; and thou madest thy days to nigh, and thou broughtest the time of thy years. Therefore I gave thee to be a shame to heathen men, and a scorning to all lands</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 22:2; JEREMIAH 15:2–3"> that be nigh thee, and that be far from thee; thou foul city, noble, great in perishing, they shall have victory of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:27; ISAIAH 1:23; ZECHARIAH 3:3"> Lo! &#91;the&#93; princes of Israel, all in their arm, were in thee, to shed out blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:16; EXODUS 22:21–22; LEVITICUS 20:9"> They punished with wrongs &#91;the&#93; father and mother in thee, they challenged falsely a comeling in the midst of thee, they made sorry a fatherless child, and a widow at thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:38–39; EZEKIEL 22:26; EZEKIEL 20:13"> Ye despised my saintuaries, and ye defouled my sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSEA 4:14; LEVITICUS 19:16; HOSEA 4:10"> Men backbiters were in thee, to shed out blood, and eat on hills &#91;or mountains&#93; in thee; they wrought great trespass in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:19; LEVITICUS 18:7–8; EZEKIEL 18:6"> They uncovered the shamefuller things of the father&#91;s&#93; in thee, they made low in thee the uncleanness of a woman in unclean blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:15; 2ND SAMUEL 13:14; LEVITICUS 20:17"> And each man wrought abomin-ation against the wife of his neigh-bour, and the father of the husband defouled his son’s wife unleavefully; a brother oppressed in thee his sister, the daughter of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:25; EZEKIEL 23:35; PSALMS 106:21"> They took gifts of thee, to shed out blood; thou tookest usury and over-abundance, and thou challengedest greedily thy neighbours, and thou hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:17; ISAIAH 33:15; AMOS 2:6–8"> Lo! I have smitten together mine hands on thine avarice, &#91;or greediness&#93;, which thou didest, and on the blood which is shed out in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:7; EZEKIEL 17:24; EZEKIEL 24:14"> Whether thine heart shall sustain, either thine hands shall have power, in the days which I shall make to thee? For I the Lord spake, and I shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 1:8; EZEKIEL 22:22; DEUTERONOMY 4:27"> And I shall scatter thee into nations, and I shall winnow, &#91;or blow&#93;, thee into other lands; and I shall make thine uncleanness to fail from thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:7; PSALMS 83:18; EZEKIEL 7:24"> and I shall wield thee in the sight of heathen men; and thou shalt know, that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:119; ISAIAH 1:22; JEREMIAH 6:28–30"> Thou, son of man, the house of Israel is turned to me into dross, either filth of iron; all these be brass, and tin, and iron, and lead, in the midst of a furnace; they be made the dross of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:30; EZEKIEL 24:3–6; MICAH 4:12"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, For that all ye be turned into dross, lo! I shall gather you together in the midst of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:11–12; ISAIAH 54:16; EZEKIEL 24:13"> by the gathering together of silver, and of latten, and of iron, and of tin, and of lead, in the midst of a furnace; and I shall kindle therein a fire, to well together; so I shall gather you together in my strong vengeance, and in my wrath, and I shall rest. And I shall well you together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 64:7; NAHUM 1:6; PSALMS 50:3"> and I shall gather you together, and I shall set you afire in the fire of my strong vengeance, and ye shall be welled together in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:8; EZEKIEL 20:33; HOSEA 5:10"> As silver is welled together in the midst of a furnace, so ye shall be in the midst thereof; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord, when I have shed out mine indignation &#91;up&#93; on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:14–16; JEREMIAH 2:30; 2ND CHRONICLES 28:22"> Son of man, say thou to it, Thou art a land unclean, and not berained in the day of strong vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HOSEA 6:9; JEREMIAH 2:34; EZEKIEL 13:19"> Swearing together, either conspiring, of prophets is in the midst thereof; as a lion roaring and taking prey, they devoured men, they took riches, and price; they multiplied widows thereof in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10; EZEKIEL 44:23; EZEKIEL 22:8"> &#91;The&#93; Priests thereof despised my law, and defouled my saintuaries; they had not difference betwixt holy thing and unholy, they understood not betwixt defouled thing and clean thing; and they turned away their eyes from my sabbaths, and I was defouled in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:13; ISAIAH 1:23; EZEKIEL 22:6"> The princes thereof in the midst thereof were as wolves ravishing prey, to shed out blood, and to lose men, and in following lucres greedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:6–7; EZEKIEL 13:10–16; JEREMIAH 23:25–32"> Forsooth the prophets thereof pargeted them without tempering, and saw vain things, and divined leasings to them, and said, The Lord God saith these things, when the Lord spake not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:7; EXODUS 23:9; EXODUS 22:21"> The peoples of the land challenged false challenge, and ravished by violence; they tormented a needy man and poor, and oppressed a comeling by false challenge, without doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:16; JEREMIAH 5:1; PSALMS 106:23"> And I sought of them a man, that should set &#91;or put&#93; an hedge betwixt, and stand set &#91;even&#93; against me for the land, that I should not destroy it, and I found not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:43; EZEKIEL 7:8–9; EZEKIEL 9:10"> And I shed out on them mine indignation, and I wasted them in the fire of my wrath; and I yielded the way of them on the head of them, saith the Lord God.</span>
