<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:15; PROVERBS 14:28; EZEKIEL 17:5"> Waters nourished him, the depth of waters enhanced him; his floods floated out in the compass of his roots, and he sent out his strands to all the trees of the country.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 4:11; EZEKIEL 17:5; PSALMS 1:3"> Therefore his highness was enhanced over all &#91;the&#93; trees of the country, and his trees were multiplied, and his branches were raised &#91;up&#93;, for many waters. And when he had stretched forth his shadow,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:23; MATTHEW 13:32; DANIEL 4:12"> all the volatiles of the air made nests in his branches; and all the beasts of forests engendered under his boughs, and the company of full many folks dwelled under the shadowing place of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he was full fair in his greatness, and in alarging of his trees; for the root of him was beside many waters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:13; GENESIS 13:10; ISAIAH 51:3"> Cedars in the paradise of God were not higher than he; fir trees attained not evenly to the highness of him, and plane trees were not even with the boughs of him. Each tree of paradise of God was not made like him in his fairness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:13; DANIEL 2:37–38; GENESIS 13:10"> For I made him fair, and with many and thick boughs; and all the trees of lust or Eden, that were in the paradise of God, had envy to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:17; DANIEL 5:20; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:25"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, For that that he was raised in highness, and he gave his highness green and thick, and his heart was raised &#91;up&#93; in his highness;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:11–12; NAHUM 3:18; DANIEL 5:18–19"> now I have given him into the hands of the strongest man of heathen men. And he doing shall do to that Assur; after the unfaithfulness of him I casted him out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:7; EZEKIEL 35:8; EZEKIEL 30:11"> And aliens, and the most cruel men of nations, shall cut him down, and shall cast him forth on hills &#91;or mountains&#93;. And his branches shall fall down in all the great valleys, and his trees shall be broken in all rocks of stone of earth. And all the peoples of earth shall go away from his shadowing place, and shall forsake him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:4; EZEKIEL 29:5; ISAIAH 18:6"> All the volatiles of the air dwelled in the falling of him, and all beasts of the country were in the branches of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 63:9–10; NEHEMIAH 13:18; 2ND PETER 2:6"> Wherefore all the trees of waters shall not be raised &#91;up&#93; in their highness, neither shall set their highness among places full of woods, and full of boughs, and all trees that be moisted of waters shall not stand in the highness of those &#91;or them&#93;. For all they be given into death, to the farthest land in the midst of the sons of men, to them that go down into the pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MALACHI 3:4; NAHUM 2:8–10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:9–11"> The Lord God saith these things, In the day when he went down to hells, I brought in mourning; I covered him with depth of waters, and I forbade his floods, and I refrained many waters. The Lebanon was sorry on him, and all the trees of the field were shaken of the sound of his falling.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:15; ISAIAH 14:8; EZEKIEL 26:15"> I moved altogether heathen men, when I led him down to hell, with them that went down into the pit. And all trees of liking, noble trees, and full clear in the Lebanon, all that were moisted with waters, were comforted in the lowest land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 9:17; EZEKIEL 31:6; EZEKIEL 31:3"> For why also they shall go down with him to hell, to &#91;the&#93; slain men with sword; and the arm of each man, shall sit under the shadowing place of him, in the midst of nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:19; EZEKIEL 32:21; EZEKIEL 28:10"> To whom art thou likened, thou noble and high among the trees of liking? Lo! thou art led down with the trees of liking to the farthest land. In the midst of uncircumcised men thou shalt sleep, with them that be slain by sword. That is Pharaoh, and all the multitude of him, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
