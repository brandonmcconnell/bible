<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:22; EZEKIEL 1:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:39"> The hand of the Lord was made on me, and led me out in the spirit of the Lord; and he let me go in the midst of a field that was full of bones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 141:7; EZEKIEL 37:11; DEUTERONOMY 11:30"> and he led me about by them in compass. Forsooth those &#91;or there&#93; were full many on the face of the field, and dry greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 11:25–26; JOHN 5:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:39"> And he said to me, Guessest thou, son of man, whether these bones shall live? And I said, Lord God, thou knowest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 5:25; JOHN 5:28–29; MATTHEW 21:21"> And he said to me, Prophesy thou of these bones; and thou shalt say to them, Ye dry bones, hear the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:14; PSALMS 104:29–30; GENESIS 2:7"> The Lord God saith these things to these bones, Lo! I shall send into you a spirit, and ye shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOEL 2:27; ISAIAH 49:23; JOEL 3:17"> And I shall give sinews on you, and I shall make fleshes to wax on you, and I shall stretch forth above skin in you, and I shall give a spirit to you, and ye shall live; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:2; 1ST KINGS 19:11–13; JEREMIAH 13:5–7"> And I prophesied, as he com-manded to me; forsooth a sound was made, while I prophesied, and lo! a stirring together, and bones came to bones, each to his jointure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And I saw, and lo! sinews and fleshes waxed upon those &#91;or them&#93;, and skin was stretched forth above in them, and those &#91;or they&#93; had no spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 3:8; SONG OF SONGS 4:16; PSALMS 104:30"> And he said to me, Prophesy thou to the spirit, prophesy thou, son of man; and thou shalt say to the spirit, The Lord God saith these things, Come, thou spirit, from four winds, and blow thou on these slain men, and live they again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:11; PSALMS 104:30; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:4–5"> And I prophesied, as he com-manded to me; and the spirit entered into those bones, and they lived, and stood on their feet, a full great host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:1; PSALMS 77:7–9; EZEKIEL 39:25"> And the Lord said to me, Thou, son of man, all these bones is the house of Israel; they say, Our bones dried, and our hope perished, and we be cut away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:19; HOSEA 13:14; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:16"> Therefore prophesy thou, and thou shalt say to them, The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall open your graves, and I shall lead you out of your sepulchres, my people, and I shall lead you into your land &#91;of&#93; Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:6; EZEKIEL 16:62; PSALMS 126:2–3"> And ye shall know, that I am the Lord, when I shall open your sepulchres, and shall lead you out of your burials, my people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:27; JOEL 2:28–29; EZEKIEL 36:36"> and I shall give my spirit in you, and ye shall live. And I shall make you for to rest on your land; and ye shall know, that I the Lord spake, and did, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:9; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:19; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:17"> And thou, son of man, take to thee one stick, and write thou on it, To Judah, and to the sons of Israel, and to his fellows. And take thou another stick, and write on it, Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and of all the house of Israel, and of his fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSEA 1:11; JEREMIAH 50:4; ISAIAH 11:13"> And join thou those sticks one to the tother into one stick to thee; and those &#91;or they&#93; shall be into onement in thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:19; EZEKIEL 12:9; EZEKIEL 17:12"> Soothly when the sons of thy people that speak, shall say to thee, Whether thou showest not to us, what thou wilt to thee in these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:6; EZEKIEL 37:16–17; EPHESIANS 2:13–14"> thou shalt speak to them, The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall take the stick of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim, and the lineages of Israel, that be joined to him, and I shall give them together with the stick of Judah; and I shall make them into one stick, and they shall be one in the hand of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:3; NUMBERS 17:6–9; HOSEA 12:10"> Soothly the sticks on which thou hast written, shall be in thine hand before the eyes of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:24; JEREMIAH 29:14; JEREMIAH 23:8"> And thou shalt say to them, The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall take the sons of Israel from the midst of nations, to which they went forth; and I shall gather them together on each side. And I shall bring them to their land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:24; EZEKIEL 34:23–24; JEREMIAH 3:18"> and I shall make them one folk in the land, in the hills of Israel, and one king shall be commanding to all; and they shall no more be two folks, and they shall no more be parted into two realms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:3–4; EZEKIEL 36:28–29; 1ST JOHN 1:9"> And they shall no more be defouled in their idols, and their abominations, and in all their wicked-nesses. And I shall make them safe from all their seats, in which they sinned, and I shall cleanse them; and they shall be a people to me, and I shall be God to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:23–24; HOSEA 3:5; EZEKIEL 37:25"> And my servant David shall be king on them, and one shepherd shall be of all them; they shall go in my dooms, and they shall keep my commandments, and shall do those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="AMOS 9:15; EZEKIEL 37:24; EZEKIEL 28:25"> And they shall dwell on the land, which I gave to my servant Jacob, in which your fathers dwelled; and they shall dwell on that land, they, and the sons of them, and the sons of their sons, till into without end; and David, my servant, shall be the prince of them without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:3; JEREMIAH 30:19; EZEKIEL 34:25"> And I shall smite to them a bond of peace; it shall be a covenant ever-lasting to them, and I shall ‘found’ them, and I shall multiply &#91;them&#93;, and I shall give mine hallowing in the midst of them without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:3; JOHN 1:14; EZEKIEL 37:23"> And my tabernacle shall be among them, and I shall be God to them, and they shall be a people to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 17:17–19; EZEKIEL 20:12; EXODUS 31:13"> And heathen men shall know, that I am the Lord, hallower of Israel, when mine hallowing shall be in the midst of them without end.</span>
