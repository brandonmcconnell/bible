<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:4; EZEKIEL 34:14; EZEKIEL 37:22"> Forsooth thou, son of man, prophesy on the hills &#91;or mountains&#93; of Israel; and thou shalt say, Hills of Israel, hear ye the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:13; EZEKIEL 25:3; EZEKIEL 35:10"> The Lord God saith these things, For that that the enemy said of you, Well! everlasting highnesses be given to us into heritage;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 44:13–14; LAMENTATIONS 2:15–16; JEREMIAH 18:16"> therefore prophesy thou, and say, The Lord God saith these things, For that that ye be made desolate, and defouled by compass, and be made into heritage to other folks, and ye ascended &#91;or went up&#93; on the lip of tongue, and on the shame of people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:28; DEUTERONOMY 11:11; EZEKIEL 36:6"> therefore, hills of Israel, hear ye the word of the Lord God. The Lord God saith these things to the mountains, and little hills, to strands &#91;or streams&#93;, and to valleys, and to pieces of walls left, and to cities forsaken, that be made bare of peoples, and be scorned of other folks by compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MICAH 7:8; EZEKIEL 36:3; PSALMS 137:7"> therefore the Lord God saith these things, For in the fire of my fervor I spake of other folks, and of all Idumea, that gave my land into heritage to themselves with joy and all &#91;the&#93; heart, and of intent, and casted out it, to destroy it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 123:3–4; EZEKIEL 34:29; PSALMS 74:23"> therefore prophesy thou on the earth of Israel, and thou shalt say to mountains, and little hills, to the highness of hills, and to valleys, The Lord God saith these things, For that that ye be desolate, lo! I spake in my fervor and in my strong vengeance. For that that ye suffered shame of heathen men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:5; AMOS 1:1–15; EZEKIEL 20:15"> therefore the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I raised mine hand against heathen men, that be in your compass, that they bear their shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 27:6; HEBREWS 10:37; EZEKIEL 34:26–29"> Forsooth, ye hills of Israel, bring forth your branches, and bring ye fruit to my people Israel; for it is nigh that it come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 46:11; HOSEA 2:21–23; EZEKIEL 36:34"> For lo! I to you, and I shall turn to you, and ye shall be eared, and shall take seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:33; JEREMIAH 33:12; EZEKIEL 36:37"> And in you I shall multiply men, and all the house of Israel; and cities shall be inhabited, and ruinous things shall be repaired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:12; JEREMIAH 23:5–8; JEREMIAH 31:27"> And I shall fill you with men and beasts, and they shall be multiplied, and shall increase; and I shall make you to dwell as at the beginning, and I shall reward with more goods than ye had at the beginning; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:7; EZEKIEL 36:13; JEREMIAH 32:44"> And I shall bring men on you, my people Israel, and by heritage they shall wield thee, and thou shalt be to them into heritage; and thou shalt no more lay to, that thou be without them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:32"> The Lord God saith these things, For that that they say of you, Thou art a devouress of men, and stranglest thy folk;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:25–28; AMOS 9:15; ISAIAH 60:21"> therefore thou shalt no more eat men, and thou shalt no more slay thy folk, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:29; PSALMS 89:50; EZEKIEL 36:6"> And I shall no more make heard in thee the shame of heathen men, and thou shalt no more bear the shame of peoples, and thou shalt no more lose thy folk, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:7; NUMBERS 35:33–34; MICAH 2:10"> Thou, son of man, the house of Israel dwelled in their land, and they defouled it in their ways, and in their studies; by the uncleanness of a woman in rotten blood the way of them is made before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 34:21; EZEKIEL 7:8; LAMENTATIONS 2:4"> And I shedded out mine indignation on them, for &#91;the&#93; blood which they shedded on the land, and in their idols they defouled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:24; DEUTERONOMY 28:64; EZEKIEL 5:12"> And I scattered them among heathen men, and they were winnowed into lands; I deemed them by the ways and findings of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 2:24; ISAIAH 52:5; JEREMIAH 33:24"> And they entered to heathen men, to which they entered, and defouled mine holy name, when it was said to them, This is the people of the Lord, and they went out of the land of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:9; PSALMS 74:18; EZEKIEL 20:9"> And I spared them for the sake of mine holy name, which the house of Israel had defouled among heathen men, to which they entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 106:8; EZEKIEL 36:32; PSALMS 115:1–2"> Therefore thou shalt say to the house of Israel, The Lord God saith these things, O! ye house of Israel, not for you I shall do, but for mine holy name, which ye defouled among heathen men, to which ye entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:41; ISAIAH 5:16; EZEKIEL 39:7"> And I shall hallow my great name, which is defouled among heathen men, which ye defouled in the midst of them; that heathen men know, that I am the Lord, saith the Lord of hosts, when I shall be hallowed in you before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:21; EZEKIEL 34:13; AMOS 9:14–15"> For I shall take away you from heathen men, and I shall gather you from all lands, and I shall bring you into your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:22; JOHN 3:5; JEREMIAH 33:8"> And I shall pour out clean water &#91;up&#93; on you, and ye shall be cleansed from all your filths; and I shall cleanse you from all your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:19–20; PSALMS 51:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17"> And I shall give to you a new heart, and I shall set a new spirit in the midst of you; and I shall do away an heart of stone from your flesh, and I shall give to you an heart of flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TITUS 3:3–6; HEBREWS 13:21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16"> and I shall set my spirit in the midst of you. And I shall make that ye go in my commandments, and &#91;that ye&#93; keep and work my dooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:33; JEREMIAH 32:38; EZEKIEL 37:27"> And ye shall dwell in the land, which I gave to your fathers; and ye shall be into a people to me, and I shall be into God to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 1:7–9; MATTHEW 1:21; HOSEA 2:21–23"> And I shall save you from all your filths; and I shall call wheat, and I shall multiply it, and I shall not put hunger on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:27; JOEL 2:26; DEUTERONOMY 29:23–28"> And I shall multiply the fruit of tree, and the seeds of the field, that ye bear no more the shame of hunger among heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:9; EZEKIEL 20:43; NEHEMIAH 9:26–35"> And ye shall have mind on your worst ways, and on studies not good; and your wickednesses, and your great trespasses, shall displease you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:5; EZEKIEL 36:22; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:9"> Not for you I shall do, saith the Lord God, be it known to you; O! the house of Israel, be ye shamed, and be ashamed on your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:12; EZEKIEL 36:10; JEREMIAH 33:10"> The Lord God saith these things, In the day in which I shall cleanse you from all your wickednesses, and I shall make cities to be inhabited, and I shall repair ruinous things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:14; JEREMIAH 25:9–11; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:21"> and the desert land shall be tilled, that was sometime desolate, before the eyes of each way-goer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:3; JOEL 2:3; JEREMIAH 33:9"> they shall say, That land untilled is made as a garden of liking, and cities that forsaken and destitute and undermined sat, now be made strong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:35; NUMBERS 23:19; EZEKIEL 17:24"> and heathen men, which ever be left in your compass, shall know, that I the Lord have builded &#91;the&#93; destroyed things, and I have planted untilled things; I the Lord spake, and I did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST JOHN 5:14; HEBREWS 10:21–22; EZEKIEL 14:3"> The Lord God saith these things, Yet in this thing the house of Israel shall find me, that I do to them; I shall multiply them as the flock of men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:63; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:5–11; JOHN 10:16"> as an holy flock, as the flock of Jerusalem in the solemnities thereof, so the cities that be forsaken shall be full of the flocks of men; and they shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
