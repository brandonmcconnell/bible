<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:1; EZEKIEL 20:1; EZEKIEL 29:17"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, in the ninth year, and in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:1; JEREMIAH 52:4; 2ND KI 25:1"> Thou, son of man, write to thee the name of this day, in which the king of Babylon is confirmed in his attack against Jerusalem today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:3; EZEKIEL 17:2; EZEKIEL 20:49"> And thou shalt say by a proverb a parable to the house of Israel, stirrer to wrath, and thou shalt speak to them, The Lord God saith these things, Set thou a brazen pot, set thou it soothly, and put thou water into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 3:2–3; EZEKIEL 22:18–22; MATTHEW 7:2"> Take thou a beast full fat; gather thou together the gobbets thereof into it, each good part, and the hip, and the shoulder, chosen things and full of bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:24–27; JEREMIAH 52:10; JEREMIAH 39:6"> Also dress thou heaps of bones under it; and the seething thereof boiled out, and the bones thereof were sodden in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:2; NAHUM 3:1; OBADIAH 1:11"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, Woe to the city of bloods, to the pot whose rust is therein, and the rust thereof went not out of it; cast thou out it by parts, and by his parts; and the lot fell not on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:13; DEUTERONOMY 12:16; JOB 16:18"> For why the blood thereof is in the midst thereof; he shed it out on a full clear stone, he shed not it out on earth, that it may be covered with dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:21–22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:5–10; EZEKIEL 22:30–31"> that I should bring in mine indignation, and avenge by vengeance; I gave the blood thereof on a full clear stone, that it should not be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:6; HABAKKUK 2:12; ISAIAH 31:9"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, Woe to the city of bloods, whose burning I shall make great;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:16; JEREMIAH 17:3; LAMENTATIONS 1:10"> gather thou together &#91;the&#93; bones, which I shall kindle with fire; fleshes shall be wasted, and all the setting together shall be sodden, and bones shall fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:10; MALACHI 4:1; ISAIAH 1:25"> Also set thou it void on coals, that the metal thereof wax hot, and be melted, and that the filth thereof be welled together in the midst thereof, and the rust thereof be wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:5; JEREMIAH 2:13; JEREMIAH 5:3"> It was sweated by much travail, and the over-great rust thereof went not out thereof, neither by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:24; EZEKIEL 5:13; ZEPHANIAH 3:2"> Thine uncleanness is abominable; for I would cleanse thee, and thou art not cleansed from thy filths; but neither thou shalt be cleansed before, till I make mine indignation to rest in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:27; EZEKIEL 18:30; ISAIAH 3:11"> I the Lord spake; it shall come, and I shall make it, I shall not pass, neither I shall spare, neither I shall be pleased; by thy ways and by thy findings, I shall deem thee, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:10; JEREMIAH 13:17; SONG OF SONGS 7:10"> Thou, son of man, lo! I take away from thee the desirable thing of thine eyes in vengeance, and thou shalt not wail, neither weep, neither thy tears shall flow down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSEA 9:4; 2ND SAMUEL 15:30; LEVITICUS 10:6"> Wail thou while being still, thou shalt not make mourning of dead men; thy crown be bound about thine head, and thy shoes shall be in the feet, neither thou shalt cover the mouth with a cloth, neither thou shalt eat the meats of mourners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:29–30"> Therefore I spake to the people in the morrowtide, and my wife was dead in the eventide; and I did in the morrowtide, as he had commanded to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:9; EZEKIEL 37:18; MALACHI 3:7–8"> And the people said to me, Why showest thou not to us what these things signify, which thou doest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I said to them, The word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:47; EZEKIEL 23:25; PSALMS 27:4"> Speak thou to the house of Israel, The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall defoul my saintuary, the pride of your empire, and the desirable thing of your eyes, and on which your soul dreadeth; and your sons and your daughters, which ye left, shall fall by sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 78:64; JEREMIAH 16:4–7; EZEKIEL 24:16–17"> And ye shall do, as I did; ye shall not cover mouths with cloth, and ye shall not eat the meat of wailers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:10; EZEKIEL 4:17; LEVITICUS 26:39"> Ye shall have crowns in your heads, and shoes in the feet; ye shall not wail, neither ye shall weep, but ye shall fail in wretchedness, for your wickednesses; and each man shall wail to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:3; ISAIAH 20:3; LUKE 11:29–30"> And Ezekiel shall be to you into a sign of thing to coming, &#91;or to come&#93;; by all things which he did, ye shall do, when this thing shall come; and ye shall know, that I am the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:21; PSALMS 48:2; JEREMIAH 11:22"> And thou, son of man, lo! in the day in which I shall take away from them the strength of them, and the joy of dignity, and the desire of their eyes, on which the souls of them rest, cast away the sons and the daughters of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOB 1:15–19; EZEKIEL 33:21–22; 1ST SAMUEL 4:12–18"> in that day when a man fleeing shall come to thee, to tell to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:22; EZEKIEL 29:21; LUKE 21:15"> in that day soothly thou shalt open thy mouth with him that fled; and thou shalt speak, and shalt no more be still; and thou shalt be to them into a sign of thing to coming, and ye shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
