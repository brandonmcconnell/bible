<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:1; EZEKIEL 27:13; EZEKIEL 38:3"> Thou, son of man, set thy face against Gog, and against the land of Magog, the prince of the head of Meshech and of Tubal; and prophesy thou of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:8; EZEKIEL 39:1–10; EZEKIEL 29:3"> And thou shalt say to him, The Lord God saith these things, A! Gog, lo! I to thee, prince of the head of Meshech and of Tubal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:4; DANIEL 11:40; EZEKIEL 38:15"> and I shall lead thee about, and I shall set &#91;or put&#93; a bridle in thy cheeks, and I shall lead out thee, and all thine host, horses, and horsemen, all clothed with habergeons, a great multitude of men, taking spear, and shield, and sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:10; GENESIS 10:6; EZEKIEL 30:5"> Persians, Ethiopians, and Libyans with them, all be arrayed with shields and helmets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:14; GENESIS 10:2–3; 1ST CHRONICLES 1:5–6"> Gomer, and all the companies of him, the house of Togarmah, the sides of the north, and all the host thereof, and many peoples be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 25:8; JOEL 3:9–12; ISAIAH 37:22"> Make ready, and array thee, and all thy multitude which is gathered to thee, and be thou into commandment to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; ISAIAH 24:22; EZEKIEL 38:11–12"> After many days thou shalt be visited; in the last of years thou shalt come to the land, that &#91;is&#93; turned again from sword, and was gathered of many peoples, to the hills of Israel, that were desert full oft; this was led out of peoples, and all men dwelled trustily therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:2; JEREMIAH 4:13; JOEL 2:2"> Forsooth thou shalt ascend &#91;or go up&#93;, and shalt come as a tempest, and as a cloud, for to cover the land, thou, and all thy companies, and many peoples with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 36:4; MICAH 2:1; PSALMS 83:3–4"> The Lord God saith these things, In that day, words shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; on thine heart, and thou shalt think the worst thought;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:4–5; PROVERBS 3:29–30; JEREMIAH 49:31–32"> and shalt say, I shall go up to the land without walls, I shall come to them that rest, and dwell securely; all these dwell without walls, bars, &#91;or locks&#93;, and gates be not to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:6; EZEKIEL 38:8; EZEKIEL 29:19"> that thou ravish spoils, and assail prey; that thou bring in thine hand on them that were forsaken, and afterward restored, and on the people which is gathered of heathen men, that began to wield, and to be inhabiter of the navel of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:15; EZEKIEL 27:22–23; EZEKIEL 32:2"> Sheba, and Dedan, and the merchants of Tarshish, and all the lions thereof, shall say to thee, Whether thou comest to take spoils? Lo! to ravish prey thou hast gathered thy multitude, that thou take away gold and silver, and do away appurtenance of household and cattle, and that thou ravish preys without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:8; ZECHARIAH 2:5; JEREMIAH 23:6"> Therefore prophesy thou, son of man; and thou shalt say to Gog, The Lord God saith these things, Whether not in that day, when my people Israel shall dwell trustily, thou shalt know;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:2; EZEKIEL 38:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:8"> and shalt come from thy place, from the sides of the north, thou, and many peoples with thee, all riders of horses, a great company, and an huge host;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:21; EZEKIEL 38:23; HOSEA 3:5"> and thou as a cloud shalt ascend &#91;or go up&#93; &#91;up&#93; on my people Israel, that thou cover the earth? Thou shalt be in the last days, and I shall bring thee on my land, that my folks know, when I shall be hallowed in thee, thou Gog, before the eyes of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 11:40–45; ISAIAH 63:1–6; JOEL 3:9–14"> The Lord God saith these things, Therefore thou art he of whom I spake in eld &#91;or old&#93; days, in the hand of my servants, prophets of Israel, that prophesied in the days of those times, that I should bring thee on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:5–6; HEBREWS 12:29; PSALMS 89:46"> And it shall be, in that day, in the day of the coming of Gog on the land of Israel, saith the Lord God, mine indignation shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; in my strong vengeance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HAGGAI 2:6–7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:20"> and in my fervor; I spake in the fire of my wrath. For in that day shall be great earth-moving on the land of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HOSEA 4:3; NAHUM 1:4–6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:12–13"> and fishes of the sea, and beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, and birds of the air, and each creeping beast which is moved on earth, and all men that be on the face of &#91;the&#93; earth, shall be moved from my face; and hills shall be under-turned, and hedges shall fall down, and each wall shall fall down into the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HAGGAI 2:22; JUDGES 7:22; EZEKIEL 14:17"> And I shall call together a sword against him in all mine hills, saith the Lord God; the sword of each man shall be dressed against his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:16; PSALMS 11:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:21"> And then I shall deem him by pestilence, and blood, and great rain, and by great stones; I shall rain fire and brimstone on him, and on his host, and on many peoples that be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 9:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:1–6; EZEKIEL 38:16"> And I shall be magnified, and shall be hallowed, and I shall be known before the eyes of many folks; and they shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
