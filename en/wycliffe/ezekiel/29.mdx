</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND KI 18:21; ISAIAH 36:6; EXODUS 9:14"> And all the dwellers of Egypt shall know, that I am the Lord. For that that thou were a staff of a reed to the house of Israel,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:6; EZEKIEL 17:15–17; JEREMIAH 17:5–6"> when they took hold of thee with their hands, and thou were broken, and rentest each shoulder of them, and when they rested on thee, thou were made less, and thou hast loosed, either discomforted, all the reins of them;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:17; EZEKIEL 30:10; EZEKIEL 29:19–20"> therefore the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall bring a sword on thee, and I shall slay of thee man and beast;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:3; PROVERBS 16:18; EZEKIEL 29:10–12"> and the land of Egypt shall be into desert, and into wilderness, and they shall know, that I am the Lord. For that that thou saidest, The flood is mine, and I made it,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:12; EZEKIEL 30:6–9; EXODUS 14:2"> therefore lo! I to thee, and to thy floods. And I shall give into wilder-ness the land of Egypt, destroyed by sword, from the tower of Syene till to the terms of Ethiopia.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:19; EZEKIEL 30:7; EZEKIEL 30:23"> And I shall give the land of Egypt forsaken, in the midst of lands forsaken, and the cities thereof in the midst of a city destroyed, and those shall be desolate by forty years. And I shall scatter Egyptians into nations, and I shall winnow them into lands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:26; ISAIAH 19:22"> For the Lord God saith these things, After the end of forty years I shall gather together Egypt from peoples, among which they were scattered;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:14; ISAIAH 11:11; JEREMIAH 44:1"> and I shall bring again the captivity of Egypt. And I shall set them again in the land of Pathros, in the land of their birth; and they shall be there into a meek realm,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:11; EZEKIEL 30:13; EZEKIEL 17:14"> and among other realms it shall be most low, and it shall no more be raised over nations. And I shall make them less, that they reign not on heathen men;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HOSEA 8:13; ISAIAH 20:5; EZEKIEL 29:6–7"> and they shall no more be to the house of Israel in trust, teaching them wickedness, that they flee, and follow them; and they shall know, that I am the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:1; EZEKIEL 1:2; EZEKIEL 24:1"> And it was done in the seven and twentieth year, in the first month, in the first day of the month, the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:6; JEREMIAH 25:9; EZEKIEL 26:7–12"> Thou, son of man, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, made his host to serve by great service against Tyre; each head was made bald, and each shoulder was made bare of hair, and meed was not yielded of Tyre to him, neither to his host, for the service by which he served to me against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:24–25; JEREMIAH 43:10–13; EZEKIEL 32:11"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall give Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, in the land of Egypt, and he shall take the multitude thereof; and he shall take in prey the clothes thereof, and he shall ravish the spoils thereof, and meed shall be to his host,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:9; ISAIAH 10:6–7; ISAIAH 45:1–3"> and to the work for which he served to me against it; and I gave the land of Egypt to him, for that that he travailed to me, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
