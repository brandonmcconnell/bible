</div>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 9:8; EZEKIEL 14:19; EZEKIEL 20:8"> Now anon I shall shed &#91;or pour&#93; out mine ire on thee, and I shall &#91;ful&#93; fill my strong vengeance in thee; and I shall deem thee by thy ways, and I shall put to thee all thy great trespasses.</span>

</div>

They shall not &#91;ful&#93; fill their souls, and the wombs of them shall not be filled; for it is made the cause of stumbling out of their wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:21; JEREMIAH 7:30; EZEKIEL 8:15–16"> And they setted the ornament of their brooches into pride; and they made of it the images of their abominations and simulacra. For this thing I gave it to them, into uncleanness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND KI 24:13; PSALMS 74:2–8; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:18–19"> And I shall give it into the hands of aliens, to ravish, and to the unpious men of earth, into prey, and they shall defoul it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:17; PSALMS 74:18–23; PSALMS 74:10–11"> And I shall turn away my face from them, and they shall defoul my private place; and knaves shall enter into it, and shall defoul it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 9:9; JEREMIAH 27:2; EZEKIEL 11:6"> Make thou a closing together; for the land is full of doom of bloods, and the city is full of wickedness.</span>
