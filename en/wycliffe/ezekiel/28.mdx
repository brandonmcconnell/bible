<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:9; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:4; ISAIAH 14:13–14"> Son of man, say thou to the prince of Tyre, The Lord God saith these things, For thine heart was raised &#91;up&#93;, and thou saidest, I am God, and I sat in the chair of God, in the heart of the sea, since thou art man and not God, and thou gavest thine heart as the heart of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 1:20; DANIEL 2:22; DANIEL 2:27–28"> lo! thou art wiser than Daniel, each private is not hid from thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:17–18; PROVERBS 23:4–5; PROVERBS 18:11"> in thy wisdom and prudence thou madest to thee strength, and thou gattest to thee gold and silver in thy treasuries;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 62:10; PSALMS 52:7; EZEKIEL 28:2"> in the multitude of thy wisdom, and in thy merchandise thou multipliedest to thee strength, and thine heart was raised &#91;up&#93; in thy strength;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:2; EXODUS 9:17; JAMES 1:11"> therefore the Lord God saith these things, For thine heart was raised &#91;up&#93; as the heart of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:12; EZEKIEL 32:12; EZEKIEL 30:11"> therefore lo! I shall bring on thee aliens, the strongest of heathen. And they shall make naked their swords on the fairness of thy wisdom, and they shall defoul thy fairness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:26–27; EZEKIEL 27:34; PROVERBS 1:12"> They shall slay, and draw down thee into the pit; and thou shalt die by the death of uncircumcised men, in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:2; ISAIAH 31:3; PSALMS 82:7"> Whether thou shalt say, and speak, I am God, before them that slay thee; since thou art a man, and not God? In the hand of them that slay thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:18; EZEKIEL 32:19; PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> by death of uncircumcised men, thou shalt die in the hand of aliens; for I the Lord spake, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 19:1; EZEKIEL 26:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:22"> Son of man, raise thou wailing on the king of Tyre; and thou shalt say to him, The Lord God saith these things, Thou, a print of likeness, full of wisdom, perfect in fairness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:15; GENESIS 2:8; EZEKIEL 27:16"> were in the delights of paradise of God. Each precious stone was thy covering, sardius, topaz, and jasper, chrysolyte, and onyx, and beryl, sapphire, and carbuncle, and smaragdus; also gold was the work of thy fairness, and thine holes were made ready, in the day in which thou were made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:12–15; EXODUS 30:26; EZEKIEL 28:13"> Thou were with a cherub held forth, and covering; and I setted thee in the holy hill of God. In the midst of stones set afire thou wentest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:4; EZEKIEL 28:17–18; ROMANS 7:9"> perfect in thy ways from the day of thy making, till wickedness was found in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:9–10; HABAKKUK 2:8; HABAKKUK 2:17"> In the multitude of thy merchandise, thine inner things were filled of wickedness, and thou didest sin; and I casted thee out of the hill of God, and, thou cherub covering afar, I lost thee from the midst of stones set afire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:5; EZEKIEL 31:10; JAMES 4:6"> And thine heart was raised &#91;up&#93; in thy fairness, thou lostest thy wisdom in thy fairness. I casted thee down into earth, I gave thee before the face of kings, that they should see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MALACHI 4:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:8; AMOS 1:9–10"> In the multitude of thy wicked-nesses, and in the wickedness of thy merchandise, thou defouledest thine hallowing; therefore I shall bring forth fire of the midst of thee, that shall eat thee; and I shall give thee into ashes on &#91;the&#93; earth, in the sight of all men seeing thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:21; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:21; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:15–19"> All men that shall see thee among heathen men, shall be astonied on thee; thou art made nought, and thou shalt not be without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:2; GENESIS 10:15; JEREMIAH 25:22"> Thou, son of man, set thy face against Sidon, and thou shalt prophesy of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:13; EXODUS 14:17; EZEKIEL 20:41"> and shalt say, The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I to thee, Sidon, and I shall be glorified in the midst of thee; and they shall know, that I am the Lord, when I shall do dooms in it, and I shall be hallowed therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:22; EZEKIEL 26:6; EZEKIEL 25:11"> And I shall send pestilence into it, and blood in the streets thereof, and slain men by sword shall fall down in the midst thereof by compass; and they shall know, that I am the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:55; JOSHUA 23:13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:7"> And there shall no more be an hurting of bitterness to the house of Israel, and a thorn bringing in sorrow on each side, by the compass of them, that be adversaries to them; and they shall know, that I am the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:37; EZEKIEL 20:41; EZEKIEL 36:28"> The Lord God saith these things, When I shall gather together the house of Israel from peoples, among which they be scattered, I shall be hallowed in them before heathen men. And they shall dwell in their land, which I gave to my servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:24; EZEKIEL 28:22; JEREMIAH 32:15"> And they shall dwell secure therein, and they shall build houses, and they shall plant vines, and they shall dwell trustily, when I shall make dooms in all men that be adversaries to them by compass; and they shall know, that I am the Lord God of them.</span>
