<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 23:1–18; EZEKIEL 36:2; JOEL 3:4"> Thou, son of man, for that that Tyre said of Jerusalem, Well! the gates of peoples be broken, it is turned to me; I shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled, it is forsaken;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:42; ISAIAH 5:30; MICAH 4:11"> therefore the Lord God saith these things, Lo! Tyre, I on thee; and I shall make many folks to ascend &#91;or go up&#93; to thee, as the sea flowing ascendeth &#91;or goeth up&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 23:11; AMOS 1:10; EZEKIEL 26:9"> And they shall destroy the walls of Tyre, and they shall destroy the towers thereof; and I shall raze the dust thereof from it, and I shall give it into a most clear stone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:19; EZEKIEL 27:32; EZEKIEL 25:7"> Drying of nets &#91;it&#93; shall be in the midst of the sea, for I spake, saith the Lord God. And Tyre shall be into ravishing to heathen men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:46; EZEKIEL 26:8; JEREMIAH 49:2"> And the daughters thereof that be in the field, shall be slain by sword; and they shall know, that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZRA 7:12; DANIEL 2:37; EZEKIEL 23:24"> For why the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall bring to Tyre Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, from the north, the king of kings, with horses, and chariots, and knights, and with a company, and great people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:22; JEREMIAH 52:4; JEREMIAH 32:24"> He shall slay by sword thy daughters that be in the field, and he shall encompass thee with strongholds, and he shall bear together &#91;the heap of&#93; earth in compass. And he shall raise a shield against thee,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 26:15"> and he shall temper engines like vineries &#91;or vines&#93;, and engines that be called wethers against thy walls; and he shall destroy thy towers by his arms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:15; EZEKIEL 27:28; JEREMIAH 47:3"> By flowing of his horses, the dust of those &#91;or them&#93; shall cover thee; thy walls shall be moved of the sound of knights, and of wheels, and of chariots; when he shall enter by the gates, as by enterings of a city destroyed,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:28; ISAIAH 26:5; HABAKKUK 1:8"> with the claws of his horses he shall defoul all thy streets. He shall slay by sword thy people, and thy noble images shall fall down into earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 23:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:27; ZECHARIAH 9:3–4"> They shall waste thy riches, they shall ravish thy merchandise; and they shall destroy thy walls, and they shall destroy thine houses full clear, and thy stones, and thy trees, and they shall put thy dust in the midst of waters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:34; ISAIAH 23:16; ISAIAH 14:11"> And I shall make to rest the multitude of thy singers, and the sound of thine harps shall no more be heard;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 1:4; JOB 12:14; NUMBERS 23:19"> and I shall give thee into a most clear stone. Thou shalt be a place for the drying of nets, and thou shalt no more be builded, for I the Lord spake, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:35; JEREMIAH 49:21; EZEKIEL 26:18"> The Lord God saith these things of Tyre, Whether isles shall not be moved of the sound of thy fall, and of the wailing of thy slain men, when they be slain in the midst of thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:10; JONAH 3:6; JOB 8:22"> And all the princes of the sea shall go down off their seats, and they shall do away their mantles, either their spoils of slain enemies, and they shall cast away their diverse clothes, and shall be clothed with wondering. They shall sit in the earth, and shall be astonied, and shall wonder on thy sudden fall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:12; EZEKIEL 19:1; 2ND SAMUEL 1:25–27"> And they shall take wailing on thee, and shall say to thee, How perishedest thou, noble city, that dwellest in the sea, that were strong in the sea, with thy dwellers, which dwellers all men dreaded?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:15; ISAIAH 23:5–7; EZEKIEL 27:35"> Now ships shall wonder in the day of thy dread, and isles in the sea shall be troubled, for none goeth out of thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:7–8; EZEKIEL 26:3; DANIEL 11:40"> For the Lord God saith these things, When I shall give thee to be a city desolate, as the cities that be not inhabited, and I shall bring on thee the depth of waters, and many waters shall cover thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 27:13; LUKE 10:15; ZECHARIAH 2:8"> And I shall draw thee down with them that go down into a pit, to the people everlasting; and I shall set thee in the last land, as old wilder-nesses, with them that be led down into a pit, that thou be not inhabited. Certainly when I shall give glory in the land of livers,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:36; EZEKIEL 28:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:21"> I shall drive thee into nought, and thou shalt not be; and thou shalt be sought, and thou shalt no more be found without end, saith the Lord God.</span>
</div>
