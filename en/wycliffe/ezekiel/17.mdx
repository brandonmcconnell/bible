<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 8:1; JEREMIAH 48:40; EZEKIEL 17:7"> and thou shalt say, The Lord God saith these things. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:14; ISAIAH 47:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:11–19"> He pulled away the highness of boughs thereof, and bare it over into the land of Canaan, and setted it in the city of merchants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:4; DEUTERONOMY 8:7–9; JEREMIAH 37:1"> And he took of the seed of the land, and setted it in the land for seed, that it should make steadfast root on many waters; and he setted it in the higher part.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:14; PROVERBS 16:18–19"> And when it had grown, it increased into a larger vinery &#91;or a broad vineyard&#93;, in low stature; for the boughs thereof beheld to that eagle, and the roots thereof were under that eagle; therefore it was made a vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;, and it made fruit into scions, and sent out boughs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:4; EZEKIEL 17:15; 2ND KI 24:20"> And another great eagle was made, with great wings, and many feathers; and lo! this vinery &#91;or this vineyard&#93; as sending his roots to that eagle, stretched forth his scions to that eagle, that he should moist it out of the cornfloors of his seed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:5–6"> Which is planted in a good land on many waters, that it make boughs, and bear fruit, that it be into a great vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:10; JEREMIAH 32:5; ISAIAH 30:1–7"> Say thou, Ezekiel, The Lord God saith these things, Therefore whether he shall have prosperity? Whether Nebuchadnezzar shall not pull away the roots of him, and shall constrain the fruits of him? And he shall make dry all the scions of burgeoning thereof, and it shall be dry; and not in great arm, neither in much people, that he should draw it out by the roots.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSEA 13:15; JOHN 15:6; MATTHEW 21:19"> Lo! it is planted, therefore whether it shall have prosperity? Whether not when burning wind shall touch it, it shall be made dry, and shall wax dry in the cornfloors of his seed?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:19; EZEKIEL 17:3; EZEKIEL 1:2"> Say thou to the house of Israel stirring me to wrath, Know ye not what these things signify? Say thou, Lo! the king of Babylon cometh into Jerusalem; and he shall take the king and the princes thereof, and he shall lead them to himself into Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:13; 2ND KI 24:15–17; EZEKIEL 17:5"> And he shall take of the seed of the realm, and shall smite with it a bond of peace, and he shall take of it an oath; but also he shall take away the strong men of the land,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:14; DEUTERONOMY 28:43; 1ST SAMUEL 2:30"> that it be a meek realm, and be not raised &#91;up&#93;, but that it keep the covenant of him, and hold it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:3; JEREMIAH 38:18; EZEKIEL 17:7"> Which went away from him, and sent messengers into Egypt, that it should give to him horses and much people. Whether he that did these things, shall have prosperity, either shall get health? and whether he that breaketh &#91;a&#93; covenant, shall escape?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:13; JEREMIAH 52:11; EZEKIEL 17:18–19"> I live, saith the Lord God, for in the place of the king that made him king, whose oath he made void, and brake the covenant, which he had with him, in the midst of Babylon he shall die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:7; EZEKIEL 4:2; EZEKIEL 29:6–7"> And not in great host, neither in much people, Pharaoh shall make battle against him, in the casting up of &#91;an heap of&#93; earth, and in building of pales or posts, that he slay many persons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:24; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:8; LAMENTATIONS 5:6"> For he despised the oath, that he should break the bond of peace, and lo! he gave his hand; and when he hath done all these things, he shall not escape.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:11; JEREMIAH 5:9; JEREMIAH 5:2"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, I live, for I shall set &#91;or put&#93; on his head the oath which he despised, and the bond of peace which he brake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:13; EZEKIEL 32:3; JEREMIAH 2:35"> And I shall spread abroad my net &#91;up&#93; on him, and he shall be taken in my net, and I shall bring him into Babylon; and there I shall deem him in the trespassing, by which he despised me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND KI 25:5; 2ND KI 25:11; EZEKIEL 12:14"> And all his fleers-away with all his company shall fall down by sword, forsooth the remnants shall be scattered into each wind; and ye shall know, that I the Lord spake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:2; ZECHARIAH 6:12–13; ISAIAH 11:1–5"> The Lord God saith these things, And I shall take of the marrow of an high cedar, and I shall set a tender thing of the cop &#91;or top&#93; of his branches; I shall constrain, and I shall plant it on an high hill, and appearing far off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:32; EZEKIEL 31:6; ISAIAH 2:2"> In the high hill of Israel I shall plant it; and it shall break out into burgeoning, and it shall make fruit, and it shall be into a great cedar, and all birds shall dwell under it; each volatile shall make nest under the shadow of his boughs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="AMOS 9:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:27–28; EZEKIEL 24:14"> And all &#91;the&#93; trees of the country shall know, that I am the Lord; I made low the high tree, and I enhanced the low tree, and I made dry the green tree, and I made the dry tree to bring forth boughs; I the Lord have spoken, and I have done.</span>
</div>
