<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:1; JEREMIAH 3:15; ISAIAH 40:11"> Son of man, prophesy thou of the shepherds of Israel, prophesy thou; and thou shalt say to the shepherds, The Lord God saith these things, Woe to the shepherds of Israel, that fed himself &#91;or themselves&#93;; whether flocks be not fed of shepherds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:16; JEREMIAH 2:30; EZEKIEL 33:25–26"> Ye ate &#91;the&#93; milk, and were covered with &#91;the&#93; wools, and ye killed that that was fat; but ye fed not my flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:12–13; MATTHEW 9:36; EZEKIEL 34:16"> Ye made not firm that that was unsteadfast, and ye made not whole that that was sick; ye bound not up that that was broken, and ye brought not again that that was cast away, and ye sought not that that perished; but ye commanded to them with sternness, and with power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:36; 2ND CHRONICLES 18:16; JEREMIAH 23:2"> And my sheep were scattered, for no shepherd was; and they were made into devouring of all beasts of the field, and they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 142:4; 1ST PETER 2:25; JOHN 10:16"> My flocks erred in all mountains, and in each high hill, and my flocks were scattered on all the face of earth, and none was that sought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:2–3; MICAH 3:8–9; PSALMS 82:1–7"> Therefore, shepherds, hear ye the word of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:33; EZEKIEL 34:5–6"> I live, saith the Lord God, for why for that that my flocks be made into raven, and my sheep into devouring of all beasts of the field, for that that no shepherd was, for the shepherds sought not my flocks, but the shepherds fed themselves, and fed not my flocks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> therefore, shepherds, hear ye the word of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:2; ZECHARIAH 10:3; EZEKIEL 13:8"> The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I myself am over &#91;the&#93; shepherds; I shall seek my flock of the hand of them, and I shall make them to cease, that they feed no more my flock, and that the shepherds feed no more themselves. And I shall deliver my flock from the mouth of them, and it shall no more be into meat to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 10:16; LUKE 19:10; JEREMIAH 23:3"> For the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I myself shall seek my sheep, and I shall visit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:11; JOHN 10:11–12; LUKE 19:10"> As a shepherd visiteth his flock, in the day when he is in the midst of his sheep that be scattered, so I shall visit my sheep; and I shall deliver them from all places in which they were scattered, in the day of cloud, and of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:25–26; EZEKIEL 11:17; ISAIAH 11:11–16"> And I shall lead them out of peoples, and I shall gather them from lands, and I shall bring them into their land, and I shall feed them in the hills of Israel, in rivers, and in all seats of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 10:9; JEREMIAH 31:25; PSALMS 31:8–10"> I shall feed them in most plenteous pastures, and the pastures of them shall be in the high hills of Israel; there they shall rest in green herbs, and in fat pastures they shall be fed on the hills of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:15; PSALMS 23:1–2; JOHN 21:15"> I shall feed my sheep, and I shall make them to lie down, or to rest, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; EZEKIEL 34:4; EZEKIEL 34:11"> I shall seek that that perished, and I shall bring again that that was cast away; and I shall bind up that that was broken, and I shall make strong that that was sick; and I shall keep that that is fat and strong; and I shall feed them in doom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:20–22; MATTHEW 25:32–33; ZECHARIAH 10:3"> forsooth ye be my flocks. The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I deem betwixt beast and beast, and a wether and a buck of goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:13; LUKE 11:52; NUMBERS 16:13"> Whether it was not enough to you to devour good pastures? Further-more and ye defouled with your feet the remnants of your pastures, and when ye drank clearest water, ye disturbed the residue with your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And my sheep were fed with these things that were defouled with your feet; and they drank these things, that your feet had troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:31–46; EZEKIEL 34:10; PSALMS 22:12–16"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things to you, Lo! I myself deem betwixt a fat beast and a lean beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:5; DEUTERONOMY 33:17; LUKE 13:14–16"> For that that ye hurled with your sides, and shoulders, and winnowed with your horns all sick beasts, till those &#91;or they&#93; were scattered withoutforth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:2–3; PSALMS 72:12–14; EZEKIEL 34:10"> I shall save my flock, and it shall no more be into raven. And I shall deem betwixt beast and beast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:24–25; JEREMIAH 30:9; ISAIAH 40:11"> and I shall raise on those &#91;or them&#93; one shepherd, my servant David, that shall feed those &#91;or them&#93;; he shall feed them, and he shall be a shepherd to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:33; EZEKIEL 36:28; JEREMIAH 23:5–6"> Forsooth I the Lord shall be into God to them, and my servant David shall be prince in the midst of them; I the Lord spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:6–9; EZEKIEL 37:26; LEVITICUS 26:6"> And I shall make with them a covenant of peace, and I shall make &#91;the&#93; worst beasts to cease from &#91;the&#93; earth; and they that dwell in desert, shall sleep secure in forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:12; ISAIAH 44:3; LEVITICUS 26:4"> And I shall set them &#91;a&#93; blessing in the compass of my little hill, and I shall lead down rain in his time. And rains of blessing shall be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:8; LEVITICUS 26:4; LEVITICUS 26:13"> and the tree of the field shall give his fruit, and the earth shall give his seed. And they shall be in their land without dread; and they shall know, that I am the Lord, when I shall all-break the chains of their yoke, and shall deliver them from the hand of them that command to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:27; JEREMIAH 30:10; EZEKIEL 39:26"> And they shall no more be into raven into heathen men, neither the beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth shall devour them, but they shall dwell trustily without any dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:29; ISAIAH 4:2; EZEKIEL 34:26–27"> And I shall raise to them a just burgeoning named; and they shall no more be made less for hunger in earth, and they shall no more bear the shame of heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:27; EZEKIEL 34:24; PSALMS 46:11"> And they shall know, that I am their Lord God with them, and they be my people, the house of Israel, saith the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; JOHN 10:16; PSALMS 78:52"> Forsooth ye my flocks be men, the flocks of my pasture; and I am your Lord God, saith the Lord God.</span>
