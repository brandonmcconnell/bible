</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:3; EZEKIEL 46:8; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:10–12"> And the prince shall enter by the way of the porch of the gate without-forth, and he shall stand in the threshold of the gate; and priests shall make the burnt sacrifice of him, and the peaceable sacrifices of him; and he shall worship on the threshold of the gate, and he shall go out; forsooth the gate shall not be closed till to the eventide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 1:10; JOHN 10:9; HEBREWS 10:19–22"> And the people of the land shall worship at the door of that gate, in sabbaths, and in calends, before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:17; NUMBERS 28:9–10"> Forsooth the prince shall offer this burnt sacrifice to the Lord in the day of sabbath, six lambs without wem, and a wether without wem,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:24; EZEKIEL 46:7; DEUTERONOMY 16:17"> and the sacrifice of ephah by a wether; but in the lambs he shall offer the sacrifice which his hand shall give, and of oil the measure hin, by each ephah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:1"> But in the day of calends he shall offer a calf without wem of the drove; and six lambs, and wethers shall be without wem,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:5"> and ephah by a calf. Also he shall make the sacrifice, ephah by a wether; but of lambs as his hand findeth, and of oil the measure hin, by each ephah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:2; EZEKIEL 44:1–3; COLOSSIANS 1:18"> And when the prince shall enter, enter he by the way of the porch of the gate, and go he out by the same way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:16; EXODUS 23:14–17; 2ND PETER 2:20–21"> And when the people of the land shall enter in the sight of the Lord into solemnities, which people entereth by the gate of the north for to worship, go it out by the way of the south gate. Certainly the people that entereth by the way of the south gate, go out by the way of the north gate. It shall not turn again by the way of the gate by which it entered, but even against that way it shall go out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:20; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:22"> Forsooth the prince shall be in the midst of them; he shall enter with them that enter, and he shall go out with them that go out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:5; EZEKIEL 46:7; NUMBERS 15:1–41"> And in fairs and in solemnities, the sacrifice of ephah shall be by a calf, and ephah by a wether; in lambs shall be sacrifice as his hand findeth, and of oil the measure hin, by each ephah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:1–2; EZEKIEL 45:17; EZEKIEL 44:3"> Forsooth when the prince maketh a willful burnt sacrifice, either willful peaceable sacrifices to the Lord, the gate that beholdeth to the east shall be opened to him; and he shall make his burnt sacrifice, and his peaceable sacrifices, as it is wont to be done in the day of sabbath; and he shall go out, and the gate shall be closed after that he went out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 50:4; JOHN 1:29; EXODUS 12:5"> And he shall make burnt sacrifice each day to the Lord, a lamb without wem of the same year; ever he shall make it in the morrowtide,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:5"> and he shall make sacrifice on it full early; early he shall make the sixth part of ephah, and of oil the third part of hin, that it be meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; with the flour of wheat; it is a lawful sacrifice, continual and everlasting, to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:1–10; NUMBERS 28:6; EXODUS 29:42"> He shall make ready a lamb, and sacrifice, and oil, full early; he shall make ready early &#91;the&#93; burnt sacrifice everlasting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 21:3; GALATIANS 4:7; GENESIS 25:5–6"> The Lord God saith these things, If the prince giveth an house to any of his sons, the heritage of him shall be of his sons; they shall wield it by heritage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:10; LUKE 19:25–26; GALATIANS 4:30–31"> Forsooth if he giveth a bequest of his heritage to one of his servants, it shall be his till to the year of remission, and it shall turn again to the prince; forsooth the heritage of him shall be to his sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:8; 1ST KINGS 21:19; EZEKIEL 22:27"> And the prince shall not take by violence of the heritage of the people, and of the possession of them; but of his own possession he shall give heritage to his sons, that my people be not scattered, each man from his possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 42:9; EZEKIEL 44:4–5; EZEKIEL 40:44–46"> And he led me in by the entering, that was on the side of the gate, into the chambers of the saintuary to the priests, which chambers beheld to the north; and there was a place going to the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 35:13; EZEKIEL 44:29; EZEKIEL 44:19"> And he said to me, This is the place where priests shall seethe, both for sin &#91;of doing&#93;, and for trespass &#91;of leaving undone&#93;; where they shall seethe sacrifice, that they bear it not out into the outermore foreyard, and the people be hallowed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he led me out into the outermore foreyard, and led me about by the four corners of the foreyard; and lo! a little foreyard was in the corner of the foreyard, all little fore-yards by the corners of the foreyard;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> in four corners of the foreyard little foreyards were disposed, of forty cubits by length, and of thirty by breadth; four were of one measure;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and a wall by compass went about four little foreyards; and kitchens were made under the porches by compass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:20; JOHN 21:15–17; 1ST PETER 5:2"> And he said to me, This is the house of kitchens, in which the ministers of the house of the Lord shall seethe the sacrifice of the people.</span>
</div>
