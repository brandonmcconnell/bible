<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 8:11–12; PSALMS 74:9; 1ST SAMUEL 3:21"> Forsooth the child Samuel ministered to the Lord before Eli, and the word of the Lord was precious; in those days was none open revelation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 4:15; PSALMS 90:10; GENESIS 27:1"> Therefore it was done in a day, Eli lay in his bed, and his eyes dimmed, and he might not see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 27:4; LEVITICUS 24:2–4; EXODUS 27:20–21"> the lantern of the Lord was not yet quenched. And Samuel slept in the temple of the Lord, where the ark of God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:6–11; EXODUS 3:4"> And the Lord called Samuel; and he answered and said, Lo! I or Lo! I am ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he ran to Eli, and said to him, Lo! I; for thou calledest me. And Eli said, I called not thee; turn thou again and sleep. And he went and slept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:2; 1ST SAMUEL 4:16; 2ND SAMUEL 18:22"> And the Lord added again to call Samuel; and Samuel rose, and went to Eli, and said, Lo! I am here; for thou calledest me. And Eli answered, I called not thee, my son; turn thou again and sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:2; JEREMIAH 9:24"> Forsooth Samuel knew not yet the Lord, neither the word of the Lord was showed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 33:14–15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:11–12"> And the Lord added, and called yet Samuel the third time; the which rose up and went to Eli, and said, Lo! I; for thou calledest me. Then Eli understood, that the Lord had called the child;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 20:19; ISAIAH 6:8; PSALMS 85:8"> and Eli said to Samuel, Go thou and sleep; and if he calleth thee afterward, thou shalt say, Speak thou, Lord, for thy servant heareth. Then Samuel went and slept in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 3:8; 1ST SAMUEL 3:4–6"> And the Lord came, and stood, and called as he had called the second time, Samuel, Samuel. And Samuel said, Speak thou, Lord, for thy servant heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:5; JEREMIAH 19:3; 2ND KI 21:12"> And the Lord said to Samuel, Lo! I shall make a word, that is, a thing signified by a word, in Israel, which word whoever shall hear, both his ears shall ring, that is, he shall be astonied for wonder and dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:27–36; ZECHARIAH 1:6; JOSHUA 23:15"> In that day I shall raise up against Eli all things that I have spoken upon his house; I shall begin it, and I shall end it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:18; MATTHEW 10:37; 1ST SAMUEL 2:12"> For I before-said to him, that I should deem his house without end for the wickedness thereof; for he knew, that his sons did unworthily, and he chastised not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 22:14; 1ST SAMUEL 2:25; JEREMIAH 15:1"> Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli, that the wickedness of his meine, or house, shall not be cleansed with sacrifices and gifts till into without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:6–8; MALACHI 1:10; 1ST SAMUEL 1:9"> And then Samuel slept till the morrowtide, and he opened the doors of the house of the Lord; and Samuel dreaded to show the revelation to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Therefore Eli called Samuel, and said, Samuel, my son. And he answered and said, I am ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:35; RUTH 1:17; PSALMS 141:5"> And Eli asked him, What is the word that the Lord hath spoken to thee? I pray thee, hide it not from me; God do to thee these things, and increase these things, if thou hidest from me a word of all &#91;the&#93; words that be said to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 2:10; ISAIAH 39:8; JUDGES 10:15"> And Samuel showed to him all the words, and hid not anything from him. And Eli answered, He is the Lord; do he that, that is good in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 39:2; 1ST SAMUEL 9:6; 1ST KINGS 8:56"> Forsooth Samuel increased, and the Lord was with him, and none of all his words felled into &#91;the&#93; earth, that is, in vain, for all was fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; 2ND SAMUEL 3:10; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:12"> And all Israel from Dan to Beer-sheba knew, that faithful Samuel was a prophet of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 3:1; GENESIS 12:7; 1ST SAMUEL 3:4"> And the Lord added to appear again in Shiloh, for the Lord was showed to Samuel in Shiloh by the word of the Lord;</span>
