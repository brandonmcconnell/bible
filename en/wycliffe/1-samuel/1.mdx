<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 6:34; 1ST CHRONICLES 6:25–27; 1ST SAMUEL 1:19"> There was a man of Ramathaim in Zophim, of the hill of Ephraim, and his name was Elkanah, the son of Jeroham, son of Elihi, son of Tohu, son of Zuph, of Ephraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 16:1–2; JUDGES 13:2; GENESIS 29:23–29"> And Elkanah had two wives; the name to the one was Hannah, and the name of the second was Peninnah; and sons were to Peninnah; but Hannah had none free children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 2:41; JOSHUA 18:1; DEUTERONOMY 12:5–7"> And that man went up from his city in the days that were ordained, to worship and to offer sacrifice to the Lord of hosts in Shiloh. And &#91;the&#93; two sons of Eli were there, Hophni and Phinehas, priests of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:17–18; DEUTERONOMY 16:11; DEUTERONOMY 12:5–7"> Then the day came, and Elkanah offered, and he gave parts to Peninnah, his wife, and to all his sons and daughters;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 30:2; GENESIS 29:30–31; GENESIS 45:22"> forsooth he gave sorrowfully one part, either double, to Hannah, for he loved Hannah; forsooth the Lord had closed her womb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 6:14; JOB 24:21; LEVITICUS 18:18"> And her enemy Peninnah tormented her, and anguished her greatly, in so much that she upbraided her, that the Lord had closed her womb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:19"> And so Peninnah did each year, when the time came that they went up into the house of the Lord; and so she stirred Hannah. And then she wept, and took no meat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="RUTH 4:15; JOB 6:14; ISAIAH 54:6"> Therefore Elkanah, her husband, said to her, Hannah, why weepest thou, and why eatest thou not, and why is thine heart tormented? Whether I am not better to thee than be ten sons?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 3:3; PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 5:7"> Soothly Hannah rose, after that she had eaten and drunk in Shiloh. And the while Eli &#91;the priest&#93; was on his great seat before the doorposts of the house of the Lord,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 7:11; LUKE 22:44; JOB 10:1"> and when she was in bitter sorrow of soul, she prayed the Lord, and wept largely;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 13:5; NUMBERS 6:5; GENESIS 29:32"> and she made a vow to the Lord, and said, Lord God of hosts, if thou beholdest, and seest the torment of thy servantess, and if thou hast mind of me, and forgettest not thine handmaid, and givest a son to thy servantess, I shall give him to the Lord all the days of his life, and a razor shall not come upon his head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:17; JAMES 5:16; LUKE 18:1"> And it was done, when she multiplied her prayers before the Lord, that Eli espied her mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 8:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:13; GENESIS 24:42–45"> Forsooth Hannah spake in her heart, and only her lips were moved, and utterly her voice was not heard. Therefore Eli guessed her drunken,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:25; JOB 8:2; PROVERBS 6:9"> and he said to her, How long shalt thou be drunken? Avoid thou a little the wine, by which thou art moist.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; LAMENTATIONS 2:19; PSALMS 42:4"> Hannah answered, and said, Nay, my lord, for I am an unhappy woman; I have not drunk wine, neither any-thing that may make drunken, but I have poured out my soul in the Lord’s sight;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:12; JOB 6:2–3; MATTHEW 12:34–35"> guess thou not thine handmaid as one of the daughters of Belial, for of the multitude of my sorrow and of my mourning I have spoken unto this present time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 5:34; JUDGES 18:6; PSALMS 20:3–5"> Then Eli said to her, Go thou in peace, and God of Israel give to thee the asking that thou hast prayed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="RUTH 2:13; ROMANS 15:13; ECCLESIASTES 9:7"> And she said, I would that thine handmaid find grace in thine eyes. And the woman went into her way, and ate, and her cheers were no more changed diversely.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 21:1; GENESIS 30:22; 1ST SAMUEL 1:11"> And they rised early, and worship-ped before the Lord; and they turned again, and came into their house in Ramah. And Elkanah knew Hannah, his wife; and the Lord thought on her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 41:51–52; MATTHEW 1:21; EXODUS 2:10"> And it was done after the compass of days, Hannah conceived, and childed a son, and called his name Samuel; for she had asked him of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 1:3; DEUTERONOMY 12:11; JOSHUA 24:15"> And her husband Elkanah went up, and all his house, to offer a solemn sacrifice, and his avow &#91;or vow&#93; to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 1:28; 1ST SAMUEL 1:11; LUKE 2:22"> And Hannah went not up to that solemnity or feast, for she had said to her husband, I shall not go, till the young child be weaned, and till I lead him thither, and he appear before the sight of the Lord, and dwell there continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:25; LUKE 11:27; GENESIS 21:7–8"> And Elkanah, her husband, said to her, Do thou that that seemeth good to thee, and dwell thou still till thou have weaned him; and I beseech, that the Lord &#91;ful&#93; fill his word. There-fore the woman abode, and gave milk to her son, till the time she removed him from the milk.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:9–10; JOSHUA 18:1; 1ST SAMUEL 4:3–4"> And she brought him with her, after that she had weaned him, with three calves, and three bushels of meal, and an amphora, either a pot, of wine; and she brought him to the house of the Lord in Shiloh. And the child was yet full young.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 18:15–16; LUKE 2:22"> And they sacrificed a calf, and they offered the child to Eli.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND KI 4:30; 2ND KI 2:4; 2ND KI 2:6"> And Hannah said, My lord, I beseech thee, thy soul liveth; I am the woman, that stood before thee here, and prayed the Lord;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST JOHN 5:15; PSALMS 118:5; MATTHEW 7:7"> for this child I prayed, and the Lord gave to me mine asking which I asked of him;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 24:26; GENESIS 24:52; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:15"> therefore and I have given him to the Lord in all &#91;the&#93; days, in which he is given to the Lord. And they worshipped there the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
