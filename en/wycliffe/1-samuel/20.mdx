</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:17; ESTHER 7:7; DEUTERONOMY 1:23"> If he saith, Well, peace shall be to thy servant; forsooth if he is wroth, know thou, that his malice is filled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:32; 1ST SAMUEL 18:3; 1ST SAMUEL 23:18"> Therefore do thou mercy into thy servant, for thou hast made me thy servant to make with thee bond of peace of the Lord; but if any wicked-ness is in me, slay thou me, and bring thou not in me to thy father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:42; 1ST SAMUEL 19:2; 1ST SAMUEL 20:38"> And Jonathan said, Far be this from me, for it may not be done, that I tell not to thee, if I know certainly, that the malice of my father is filled against thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:14; 1ST SAMUEL 25:17; GENESIS 42:30"> And David answered to Jonathan, Who shall tell me, if in case thy father answereth hard &#91;to thee&#93; anything of me?</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:22; JOB 31:4; PROVERBS 25:2–3"> Jonathan said to David, Thou Lord God of Israel, if I inquire the sentence of my father tomorrow, either in the next day after, and any good thing be said of thee, David, and I send not at once to thee, and make it known to thee,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="RUTH 1:17; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:11; JOSHUA 1:5"> God do these things to Jonathan, and add these things. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 9:3; EPHESIANS 5:1–2"> And if I live, do thou the mercies of the Lord to me; forsooth if I am dead,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 21:7; 1ST SAMUEL 24:21; 2ND SAMUEL 9:1–7"> take thou not away thy mercy from mine house unto without end; and if I do it not, when the Lord shall draw out by the root the enemies of David, each man from the land, take he away Jonathan from his house, and seek the Lord of the hand of the enemies of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:22; 1ST SAMUEL 18:3; 2ND SAMUEL 21:8"> Therefore Jonathan made &#91;a&#93; bond of peace with the house of David, and the Lord sought it of the hand of &#91;the&#93; enemies of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:25; 1ST SAMUEL 20:5"> And Jonathan said to David, Tomorrow is the first day of the month, that is solemn, and thou shalt be sought after;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 19:2; 1ST SAMUEL 20:5"> and thy sitting shall be asked of till after the morrow. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I shall shoot three arrows beside that stone, and I shall cast as exercising, either playing me at a sign.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:2; AMOS 8:14; JEREMIAH 12:16"> I shall send my child, and I shall say to him, Go thou, and bring to me the arrows. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:37"> But if I speak thus to the child, Lo! the arrows be beyond thee; go thou in peace, for the Lord hath delivered thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:14–15; 1ST SAMUEL 20:42; GENESIS 31:50"> Certainly of the word that thou and I have spoken, that is, of the bond of peace betwixt us and our heirs, the Lord be witness betwixt me and thee till into without end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:17; ISAIAH 1:11–15; PROVERBS 21:3"> Therefore David was hid in the field; and the calends or solemn feast came, and the king sat to eat bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:18; JUDGES 16:20"> And when the king had set on his chair by custom, which chair was beside the wall, Jonathan rose, and sat after Abner, and Abner sat at the side of Saul, and the place of David appeared void.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:5; LEVITICUS 11:24; LEVITICUS 11:27"> And Saul spake not anything in that day; for he thought, that in hap it befelled to him, that he was not clean, neither purified.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

And Saul said to Jonathan his son, Why cometh not the son of Jesse, neither yesterday, neither today, to eat?</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:6"> And Jonathan answered to Saul, He prayed me meekly that he should go into Bethlehem;</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 17:28"> and he said, Suffer thou me, for solemn sacrifice is in my city; one of my brethren &#91;hath&#93; called me; now therefore, if I &#91;have&#93; found grace in thine eyes, I shall go soon, and I shall see my brethren; for this cause he cometh not to the table of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:24; JAMES 1:19–20; PROVERBS 25:28"> And Saul was wroth against Jonathan, and said to him, Thou son of the woman willfully ravishing a man, whether I know not, that thou lovest the son of Jesse into thy &#91;own&#93; confusion or shame, and into the confusion of thy shameful mother?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 19:6; 1ST SAMUEL 20:8; 1ST SAMUEL 19:11–15"> For in all the days in which the son of Jesse liveth on &#91;the&#93; earth, thou shalt not be stablished, neither thy realm; therefore right now or anon send thou, and bring him to me, for he is the son of death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:23; 1ST SAMUEL 19:5; LUKE 23:22"> And Jonathan answered to Saul his father, and said, Why shall he die? what hath he done?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:7; 1ST SAMUEL 18:11; 1ST SAMUEL 19:10"> And Saul took a spear, that he should smite him, and Jonathan under-stood, that it was determined of his father, that David should be slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:26; MARK 3:5; ECCLESIASTES 7:20"> Then Jonathan rose from the table in full fierce wrath, and he ate not bread in the second day of calends; for he was sorry for David, for his father had shamed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 20:5; 1ST SAMUEL 20:19"> And when the morrowtide had shined, Jonathan came into the field, and a little child with him, by the covenant made of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:20–22"> And Jonathan said to his child, Go thou, and bring to me the arrows that I shoot. And when the child had run forth, he shot another arrow beyond the child.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:22"> Therefore when the child came to the place of the arrow that Jonathan had shot, Jonathan cried behind the back of the child, and said, Lo! the arrow is not there, certainly it is beyond thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="PSALMS 55:6–9; PROVERBS 6:4–5; MARK 13:14–16"> And Jonathan cried again behind the back of the child, Haste thou swiftly, stand thou not. Soothly the child gathered up the arrows of Jonathan, and brought them to his lord,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and utterly the child knew not what was done; for only Jonathan and David knew the thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Then Jonathan gave his bow and arrows to the child, and said to him, Go thou, bear these into the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="GENESIS 29:11; 1ST SAMUEL 18:3; 2ND SAMUEL 9:6"> And when the child had gone, David rose from the place that went to the south; and he felled low upon the earth, and worshipped or honour-ed the third time, and they kissed themselves together, and wept together; but David wept more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 1:17; 1ST SAMUEL 23:18; 1ST SAMUEL 20:22–23"> Then Jonathan said to David, Go thou in peace; whatever things we both have sworn in the name of the Lord, and said, The Lord be betwixt me and thee, and betwixt my seed and thy seed, till into without end, be steadfast. And David rose up, and went forth, but and Jonathan went into the city.</span>
