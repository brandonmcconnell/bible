<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 2:26; NEHEMIAH 11:32; ISAIAH 10:32"> Forsooth David came into Nob to Ahimelech, the priest; and Ahimelech wondered, for David had come; and he said to David, Why art thou alone, and no man is with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:12; COLOSSIANS 3:9; 1ST SAMUEL 22:22"> And David said to Ahimelech the priest, The king hath commanded to me a word, and said, No man know this thing, for which thou art sent from me, and what manner behests I have given to thee; for I said also to my children, that they should go into that and that place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:6; JUDGES 9:29; 1ST SAMUEL 21:4"> now therefore if thou hast anything at hand, either five loaves, give thou to me, either whatever thing thou findest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 19:15; MATTHEW 12:3–4; EXODUS 25:30"> And the priest answered to David, and said to him, I have not lay, that is, common, loaves at hand, but only holy bread; whether the children be clean, and mostly of women?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:9; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:3–4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:20–21"> And David answered to the priest, and said to him, And soothly if it is done of women, we have abstained us from yesterday and the third day ago, when we went out, and the vessels, that is, bodies, of the children were clean; certainly this way is defouled, but and it shall be hallowed today in the vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 6:3–4; LEVITICUS 24:5–9; MATTHEW 12:3–4"> Therefore the priest gave to him hallowed bread, for none other bread was there, but only loaves of setting forth, that were taken away from the face of the Lord, that hot loaves shall be set forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 22:9; PSALMS 52:1–9; 1ST CHRONICLES 27:29"> And a man of the servants of Saul was there that day, within in the tabernacle of the Lord; and his name was Doeg of Idumea, the mightiest man of the herdsmen of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And David said to Ahimelech, If thou hast here at hand spear, either sword, give it to me; for I took not with me my sword, neither mine arms; for why the king’s word constrained me to go in haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 17:2; 1ST SAMUEL 17:50–54; EXODUS 28:6–14"> And the priest said, Lo! the sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom thou killedest in the valley of Terebinth, is wrapped in a cloth next after &#91;the&#93; ephod; if thou wilt take this, take it; for there is none other except that. And David said, None other is like this, give thou it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:21; 1ST KINGS 19:3; 1ST SAMUEL 27:1–2"> And so David rose up, and fled in that day from the face of Saul, and came to Achish, the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 29:5; 1ST SAMUEL 18:7–8; 1ST SAMUEL 16:1"> And the servants of Achish said to him, when they had seen David, Whether this is not David, &#91;the&#93; king of the land? Whether they sang not to him by quires &#91;or with dances&#93;, and said, Saul smote a thousand, and David smote ten thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 2:19; PSALMS 56:3; PSALMS 34:4"> And David took these words in his heart, and he dreaded greatly of the face of Achish, king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:25; ECCLESIASTES 7:7; PSALMS 34:1–22"> And David changed his mouth before Achish, and felled down betwixt their hands, and he painted on the doors of the gate, and his dribbles, that is, spittles, flowed down into his beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:7"> And Achish said to his servants, See ye the mad man? why brought ye him to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> whether mad men fail to us? why have ye brought in him, that he should be mad, while I am present? Deliver ye him from hence, lest he enter into mine house.</span>
