<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 1:7; ISAIAH 2:18–19; JEREMIAH 10:11"> And again they rose early in the tother day, and they found Dagon lying on his face upon the earth before the ark of the Lord. </div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:11; EXODUS 9:3"> Forsooth the hand of the Lord was made grievous upon &#91;the&#93; men of Ashdod, and he destroyed them, and he smote Ashdod and the coasts thereof in the privier part of &#91;the&#93; buttocks or in the more privy part of their tail ends.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 12:33; JEREMIAH 48:7; 1ST CHRONICLES 15:13"> And men of Ashdod saw such a vengeance, and they said, The ark of God of Israel dwell not with us; for his hand is hard on us, and on Dagon our god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:11; 1ST SAMUEL 17:4; ZECHARIAH 12:3"> And they sent, and gathered all the wise men, either princes, of Philistines to them, and said, What shall we do of the ark of God of Israel? And the men of Gath answered, The ark of God of Israel be led about; and they led about the ark of God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:6; 1ST SAMUEL 7:13; DEUTERONOMY 2:15"> And while they led it about, the hand of the Lord was made upon all the cities about, of full great slaughter; and he smote men of each city, from a little man till to the more, and the lower entrails of them waxed rotten, and came forth; and men of Gath took counsel, and they made to them-selves seats of skins, either cushions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 1:8; JUDGES 1:18; JOSHUA 15:45"> Therefore they sent the ark of the Lord into Ekron. And when the ark of the Lord had come into Ekron, men of Ekron cried &#91;out&#93;, and said, They have brought to us the ark of God of Israel, that he slay us and our people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:6; JEREMIAH 48:42–44; ISAIAH 13:7–9"> Then they sent, and gathered together all the wise men, either princes, of Philistines; which said, Deliver ye the ark of God of Israel, and turn it again into his place, and slay not us with our people. For dread of death was made in all &#91;the&#93; cities, and the hand of the Lord was full grievous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 12:30; AMOS 5:19; 1ST KINGS 19:17"> And the men, that were not dead, were smitten in the privy parts of their buttocks, and the yelling of each city went up into heaven.</span>
