<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:5; 1ST PETER 4:8; GALATIANS 5:22"> If I speak with tongues of men and of angels, and I have not charity, I am made as brass sounding, or a cymbal tinkling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:3; 1ST JOHN 4:20–21"> And if I have prophecy, and know all mysteries, and all knowing, &#91;or science&#93;, and if I have all faith, so that I move hills from their place, &#91;or from one place to another&#93;, and I have not charity, I am nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JAMES 2:14–17; JOHN 15:13; MATTHEW 23:5"> And if I part all my goods into the meats of poor men, and if I betake my body, so that I burn, and if I have not charity, it profiteth to me nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:8; EPHESIANS 4:32; GALATIANS 5:21–22"> Charity is patient, it is benign; charity envieth not, it doeth not wickedly, it is not blown &#91;with pride&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:24; PHILIPPIANS 2:3–5; 1ST JOHN 3:16–17"> it is not covetous, &#91;or ambitious, or covetous of worships&#93;, it seeketh not those things that be his own &#91;or her own&#93;, it is not stirred to wrath, it thinketh not evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ROMANS 12:9; 3RD JOHN 1:3–4; 2ND JOHN 1:4"> it joyeth not on &#91;or in&#93; wicked-ness, but it joyeth together to &#91;or with&#93; truth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:24"> it suffereth all things, it believeth all things, it hopeth all things, it sustaineth all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 13:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:1–2; GALATIANS 5:6"> Charity falleth never down, whether prophecies shall be voided, either languages shall cease, either science shall be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:2–3; EPHESIANS 3:18–19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:9"> For a part we know, and a part we prophesy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 13:12; ISAIAH 60:19–20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:7–8"> but when that shall come that is perfect, that thing that is of part shall be voided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:1–2; GALATIANS 4:1"> When I was a little child, I spake as a little child, I understood as a little child, I thought as a little child; but when I was made a man, I avoided/I voided those things that were of a little child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:9–10"> And we see now by a mirror in darkness, but then face to face; now I know of part, but then I shall know, as I am known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 16:14; COLOSSIANS 3:14; 1ST JOHN 4:7–18"> And now dwell faith, hope, charity, these three; but the most of these is charity.</span>
