<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:15–17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:38; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:33"> Dare any of you that hath a cause against another, be deemed at wicked men, and not at holy men &#91;or saints&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:28; DANIEL 7:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:4"> Whether ye know not, that saints shall deem this world? And if the world shall be deemed by you, be ye unworthy to deem the least things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDE 1:6; MATTHEW 25:41; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:4"> Know ye not, that we shall deem angels? how much more worldly things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 5:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:2–4"> Therefore if ye have worldly dooms, ordain ye those contemptible men, &#91;or of little reputation&#93;, that be in the church, to deem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:34; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:14; JAMES 3:13–18"> I say to make you ashamed &#91;or I say to your shame&#93;. So there is not any wise man, that may deem betwixt a brother and his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:14–15; 1ST JOHN 2:9–11; 1ST JOHN 3:11–15"> but brother with brother striveth in doom, and that among unfaithful men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 12:17–19; PROVERBS 20:22; 1ST PETER 2:19–23"> And now trespass is always among you, for ye have dooms among you. Why rather take ye not wrong? why rather suffer ye not deceit &#91;or fraud&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 4:6; COLOSSIANS 3:25; MARK 10:19"> But also ye do wrong, and do fraud &#91;or and defraud&#93;, and that to brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:9–10; GALATIANS 5:19–21; EPHESIANS 5:4–5"> Whether ye know not, that wicked men shall not wield the kingdom of God? Do not ye err; neither lechers, neither men that serve maumets &#91;or idols&#93;, neither adulterers, neither lechers against kind, neither they that do lechery with men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 5:11; GALATIANS 5:21; EZEKIEL 22:29"> neither thieves, neither avaricious &#91;or covetous&#93; men, neither men full of drunkenness, neither cursers, neither raveners, shall wield the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30; COLOSSIANS 3:5–7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:2"> And ye were sometime these things; but ye be washed, but ye be hallowed, but ye be justified in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, and in the Spirit of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:23–33; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:27; HEBREWS 12:15–16"> All things be leaveful to me, but not all things be speedful. All things be leaveful to me, but I shall not be brought down under any man’s power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16; ROMANS 6:12"> Meat to the womb, and the womb to meats; and God shall destroy both this and that. And the body not to fornication, but to the Lord, and the Lord to the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:14; ROMANS 8:11; EPHESIANS 1:19–20"> For God raised the Lord, and shall raise us by his virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:30; ROMANS 12:5; EPHESIANS 4:15–16"> Know ye not, that your bodies be members of Christ? Shall I then take the members of Christ, and shall I make them the members of a whore? God forbid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 2:24; EPHESIANS 5:31; MARK 10:8"> Whether ye know not, that he that cleaveth to a whore, is made one body? For he saith, There shall be twain in one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 17:21–23; GALATIANS 2:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:13"> And he that cleaveth to the Lord, is one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:3; COLOSSIANS 3:5"> Flee ye fornication; all sin what-ever sin a man doeth, is without the body; but he that doeth fornication, sinneth against his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:16; 1ST PETER 2:5"> Whether ye know not, that your members be the temple of the Holy Ghost, that is in you, whom ye have of God, and ye be not your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:23; ROMANS 12:1; 1ST PETER 2:9"> For ye be bought with great price. Glorify ye, and bear ye God in your body.</span>
