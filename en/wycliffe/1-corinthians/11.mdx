<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:17; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:16"> Be ye my followers, as I am of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:17; PROVERBS 31:28–31"> And, brethren, I praise you, that by all things ye be mindful of me; and as I betook to you my commandments, ye hold &#91;or ye keep&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 3:16; COLOSSIANS 1:18; COLOSSIANS 3:18"> But I will that ye know, that Christ is &#91;the&#93; head of each man; but the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:30; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:10"> Each man praying, or prophesying, when his head is covered, defouleth his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:12; LUKE 2:36; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:17"> But each woman praying, or prophesying, when her head is not covered, defouleth her head; for it is one, as if she were polled, &#91;or were made bald, or clipped&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:5; NUMBERS 5:18"> And if a woman be not covered &#91;or veiled&#93;, be she polled; and if it is foul thing to a woman to be polled, or to be made bald, cover she her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:3; GENESIS 5:1; PSALMS 8:6"> But a man shall not cover his head, for he is the image and glory of God; but a woman is the glory of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:13; GENESIS 2:21–23"> For a man is not of the woman, but the woman of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 2:18; GENESIS 2:23–24; GENESIS 2:20"> And the man is not made for the woman, but the woman for the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 24:64–65; HEBREWS 1:14; MATTHEW 18:10"> Therefore the woman shall have a covering on her head, also for angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:10–14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:12–22"> Nevertheless neither the man is without the woman, neither the woman is without &#91;the&#93; man, in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 11:36; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:6; HEBREWS 1:2–3"> For why as the woman is of the man, so the man is by the woman; but all things be of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 12:57; JOHN 7:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:15"> Deem ye yourselves; beseemeth &#91;or becometh&#93; it a woman not covered on the head to pray to God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:26; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:35"> Neither the kind itself teacheth us &#91;that&#93;, for if a man nourish long hair, it is shame to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> but if a woman nourish long hair, it is glory to her, for hairs be given to her for covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:17; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:3–4; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:14"> But if any man is seen to be full of strife, we have none such custom, neither the church of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:22"> But this thing I command, not praising, that ye come together not into the better, but into the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:10–12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:1"> First for when ye come together into the church, I hear that dissensions, either partings, be, and in part I believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:19; TITUS 3:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:30"> For it behooveth heresies to be, that they that be approved, be openly known in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore when ye come together into one, now it is not to eat the Lord’s supper;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:13; JUDE 1:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:23–25"> for why each man before taketh his supper to eat, and one is hungry, and another is drunken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:28; PROVERBS 17:5; JAMES 2:5–6"> Whether ye have not houses to eat and drink, or ye despise the church of God, and confound, &#91;or shame&#93;, them that have none? &#91;or that have not?&#93; What shall I say to you? I praise you, but herein &#91;or in this thing&#93; I praise you not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 22:19–20; MATTHEW 26:26–28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:23–25"> For I have taken of the Lord that thing, which I have betaken to you. For the Lord Jesus, in what night he was betrayed, took bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 6:53–58; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:27–28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:3–4"> and did thankings &#91;or graces&#93;, and brake, and said, Take ye, and eat ye; this is my body, which shall be betrayed for you; do ye this thing into my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 22:20; HEBREWS 9:15–20; HEBREWS 13:20"> Also &#91;he took&#93; the cup, after that he had supped, and said, This cup is the new testament in my blood; do ye this thing, as oft as ye shall drink &#91;it&#93;, into my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:28; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:7; JOHN 14:3"> For as oft as ye shall eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye shall tell &#91;or show&#93; the death of the Lord, till that he come &#91;or till he come&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 6:51; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:21; HEBREWS 10:29"> Therefore whoever eateth the bread, or drinketh the cup of the Lord unworthily, he shall be guilty of the body and of the blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:5; LAMENTATIONS 3:40"> But prove a man himself, and so eat he of that bread, and drink he of the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:27; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:30; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:32–34"> For he that eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh doom, &#91;or damnation&#93;, to him&#91;self&#93;, not wisely deeming the body of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; NUMBERS 20:12; AMOS 3:2"> Therefore among you many be sick and feeble &#91;or unstrong&#93;, and many sleep, &#91;or die&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:9; PSALMS 32:3–5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:5"> And if we deemed wisely us-selves, we should not be deemed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:5–11; PSALMS 118:18; PROVERBS 3:11–12"> but while we be deemed of the Lord, we be chastised, that we be not condemned with this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore, my brethren, when ye come together to eat, abide ye together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:21–22; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:19"> If any man hungereth, eat he at home, that ye come not together into doom. And I shall dispose other things, when I come.</span>
