<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:4–11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:37; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:1–18"> But of spiritual things, brethren, I will not that ye unknow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:3; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:9; PSALMS 115:5"> For ye know, that when ye were heathen men, how ye were led going to dumb maumets &#91;or dumb simulacra&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:2–3; MATTHEW 16:16–17; ROMANS 10:9"> Therefore I make known to you, that no man speaking in the Spirit of God, saith departing from Jesus; and no man may say the Lord Jesus &#91;is&#93;, but in the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:28; ROMANS 12:4–6"> And diverse graces there be, but it is all one Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:11–12; ROMANS 12:6–8; ROMANS 14:8–9"> and diverse services there be, but it is all one Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:11; JOHN 5:17"> and diverse workings there be, but it is all one God, that worketh all things in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:10–11; ROMANS 12:6–8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:19"> And to each man the showing of Spirit is given to profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:6–10; PSALMS 143:10"> And the word of wisdom is given to one by Spirit; to another the word of knowing, by the same Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 5:14–15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:13; EPHESIANS 2:8"> faith to another, in the same Spirit; to another, grace of healings &#91;or of healths&#93;, in one Spirit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:39; 1ST JOHN 4:1; MARK 16:17"> to another, the working of virtues; to another, prophecy; to another, very knowing &#91;or discretion&#93; of spirits; to another, kinds of languages &#91;or tongues&#93;; to another, expounding &#91;or interpreting&#93; of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:4; HEBREWS 2:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:13"> And one and the same Spirit worketh all these things, parting to each by themselves as he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 12:4–5; COLOSSIANS 3:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:27"> For as there is one body, and hath many members, and all the members of the body when those &#91;or they&#93; be many, be one body, so also Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:5; COLOSSIANS 3:11; GALATIANS 3:28"> For in one Spirit all we be baptized into one body, either Jews, either heathen, either servants, either free; and all we be filled with drink &#91;or have drunk&#93; in one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:27–28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:12; EPHESIANS 4:25"> For the body is not one member, but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 9:8–15; 2ND KI 14:9"> If the foot shall say, For I am not the hand, I am not of the body; not therefore it is not of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 12:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:22; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> And if the ear saith, For I am not the eye, I am not of the body; not therefore it is not of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:21; PSALMS 139:13–16; PROVERBS 20:12"> If all the body is the eye, where is &#91;the&#93; hearing? and if all the body is hearing, where is &#91;the&#93; smelling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:28; ROMANS 12:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:11"> But now God hath set &#91;or put&#93; members, and each of them in the body, as he would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And if all were one member, where were the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:12"> But now there be many members, but one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZRA 10:1–5; NEHEMIAH 4:16–21; JOB 29:11"> And the eye may not say to the hand, I have no need to thy works; or again the head to the feet, Ye be not necessary to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:28; ECCLESIASTES 9:14–15; ECCLESIASTES 4:9–12"> But much more those that be seen to be the lower members of the body, &#91;or the more sick&#93;, be more needful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 3:21; GENESIS 3:7"> and those that we guess to be the unworthier &#91;or the unnobler&#93; members of the body, to them we give more honour; and those members that be unhonest, have more honesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 3:11; GENESIS 2:25"> For our honest members have need of none; but God tempered the body, giving more worship to it, to whom it failed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:10–12; JOHN 17:21–26"> that debate be not in the body, but that the members be busy into the same thing each for other &#91;or for each other&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; GALATIANS 6:2; HEBREWS 13:3"> And if one member suffereth any-thing, all members suffer therewith; either if one member joyeth &#91;or glorieth&#93;, all members joy together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:12; COLOSSIANS 1:24; EPHESIANS 5:30"> And ye be the body of Christ, and members of member.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:11–13; ROMANS 12:6–8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:18"> But God set some men in the church, first apostles, the second time prophets &#91;or the second prophets&#93;, the third teachers, afterward virtues, afterward graces of healings, helpings, governings, kinds of languages, interpretations of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:4–11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:14–20"> Whether all &#91;be&#93; apostles? whether all &#91;be&#93; prophets? whether all teachers? whether all virtues?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:10"> whether all have grace of healings? whether all speak with languages? whether all expound &#91;or interpret&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 13:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:39; MATTHEW 5:6"> But pursue ye the better ghostly gifts. And yet I show to you a more excellent, &#91;or worthy&#93;, way.</span>
