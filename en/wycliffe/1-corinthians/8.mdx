<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 8:7; ISAIAH 5:21; ROMANS 14:3"> But of these things that be sacrificed to idols, we know, for all we have knowing. But knowing &#91;or science&#93;, bloweth &#91;with pride&#93;, charity edifieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 13:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18; PROVERBS 26:12"> But if any man guesseth, &#91;or deem&#93;, that he knoweth anything, he hath not yet known how it behooveth him to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:8–9; EXODUS 33:17; NAHUM 1:7"> And if any man loveth God, this is known of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:39; ISAIAH 41:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:19–20"> But of meats that be offered to idols, we know, that an idol is nothing in the world, and that there is no God but one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:4; JEREMIAH 2:28; GALATIANS 4:8"> For though there be some that be said gods, either in heaven, either in earth, as there be many gods, and many lords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ROMANS 11:36; JOHN 1:3; MALACHI 2:10"> nevertheless to us is one God, the Father, of whom be all things, and we in him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom be all things, and we by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 14:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:28–29; ROMANS 14:22–23"> But not in all men is knowing. For some men with conscience of idol, that is, they guess that the idol is some divine thing, till now eat as thing offered to idols; and their conscience is defouled, for it is sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 14:17; HEBREWS 13:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:13"> Meat commendeth us not to God; for neither we shall fail, if we eat not, neither if we eat, we shall have plenty &#91;or we shall abound&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:13; ROMANS 14:20–21; 1ST PETER 2:16"> But see ye, lest peradventure this your leave &#91;or license&#93; be made hurting to sick men, &#91;or frail&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:28–29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:20–21; ROMANS 14:23"> For if any man shall see him, that hath knowing, eating in a place where idols be worshipped, whether his conscience, since it is sick, shall not be edified to eat things offered to idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 14:15; ROMANS 15:1–3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:13"> And the sick, &#91;or unsteadfast&#93;, brother, for whom Christ died, shall perish in thy knowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:6; MATTHEW 25:45; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:12"> For thus ye sinning against brethren, and smiting their sick conscience, sin against Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 14:21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:29"> Wherefore if meat causeth my brother to stumble, I shall never eat flesh, lest I cause my brother to stumble.</span>
