<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:26–27; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:37–38"> But of those things that ye have written to me, it is good to a man to touch not a woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:28; EPHESIANS 5:33; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:9"> But for fornication each man have his own wife, and each woman have her own husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:7; EXODUS 21:10"> The husband yield debt to the wife, and also the wife to the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSEA 3:3; MATTHEW 19:9; MARK 10:11–12"> The woman hath not power of her body, but the husband; and the husband hath not power of his body, but the woman &#91;or the wife&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 19:15; JOEL 2:16; ZECHARIAH 12:12–14"> Do not ye defraud each to other, but peradventure of consent for a time, that ye give attention to prayer; and again turn again to the same thing, lest Satan tempt you for your uncontinence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:12"> But I say this thing as giving leave &#91;or by indulgence&#93;, not by command-ment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:11–12; ROMANS 12:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:5"> For I will, that all men be as myself. But each man hath his proper gift of God; one thus, and another thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:32; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:34–35; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:26–27"> But I say to them, that be not wedded, and to widows, it is good to them, if they dwell so as I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:2; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:11"> And if they contain not them-selves, &#91;or be not chaste&#93;, be they wedded; for it is better to be wedded, than to be burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:32; LUKE 16:18; MATTHEW 19:3–9"> But to them that be joined in matrimony, I command, not I, but the Lord, that the wife depart not from the husband;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:10; ISAIAH 50:1"> and that if she departeth, that she dwell unwedded, or be reconciled to her husband; and the husband forsake not the wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:25"> But to others I say, not the Lord. If any brother hath an unfaithful, &#91;or heathen&#93;, wife, and she consenteth to dwell with him, leave he her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if any woman hath an unfaithful husband, and this consenteth to dwell with her, leave she not the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 2:15–16; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:5; TITUS 1:15"> For the unfaithful husband is hallowed by the faithful woman, and the unfaithful woman is hallowed by the faithful husband. Else your children were unclean, but now they be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 14:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:11; JAMES 3:17–18"> That if the unfaithful departeth, depart he. For why the brother or sister is not subject to servage in such; for God hath called us in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:1–2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:22; ROMANS 11:14"> And whereof knowest thou, woman, if thou shalt make the man safe; or whereof knowest thou, man, if thou shalt make the woman safe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 4:17; ROMANS 12:3–8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:24"> But as the Lord hath parted to each, and as God hath called each man, so go he, as I teach in all churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:24"> A man circumcised is called, bring he not to prepuce. A man is called in prepuce, be he not circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:15; GALATIANS 5:6; 1ST JOHN 2:3–4"> Circumcision is nought, and prepuce is nought, but the keeping of the commandments of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:17; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:11; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:12"> Each man in what calling he is called, in that dwell he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:18–24; COLOSSIANS 3:11; LUKE 10:40–41"> Thou &#91;a&#93; servant art called, be it no charge to thee; but if thou mayest be made free, use it rather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:16; PHILEMON 1:16; COLOSSIANS 3:22–24"> He that is a servant, and is called in the Lord, is a free man of the Lord. Also he that is a free man, and is called, is the servant of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:20; 1ST PETER 1:18–19; TITUS 2:14"> With price ye be bought; do not ye be made servants of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:20; COLOSSIANS 3:23–24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:17"> Therefore each man in what thing he is called a brother, dwell he in this with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:40"> But of virgins I have no command-ment of God; but I give counsel, as he that hath gotten mercy of the Lord, that I be true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:8; 1ST PETER 4:17"> Therefore I guess, that this thing is good for the present need; for it is good to &#91;or for&#93; a man to be so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:12–14"> Thou art bound to a wife, do not thou seek unbinding; thou art unbound from a wife, do not thou seek a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:32–36; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:26"> But if thou hast taken a wife, thou hast not sinned; and if a maiden is wedded, she sinned not; nevertheless such shall have tribulation of flesh. But I spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:31; ROMANS 13:11–12"> Therefore, brethren, I say this thing, The time is short. Another is this, that they that have wives, be as though they had none;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 30:5; LUKE 6:25; LUKE 6:21"> and they that weep, as they wept not; and they that joy, as they joyed not; and they that buy, as they had not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:17; 1ST PETER 4:7; JAMES 4:14"> and they that use this world, as they that use &#91;it&#93; not. For why the figure, &#91;or fairness&#93;, of this world passeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:25–34; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:5; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> But I will, that ye be without busyness, for he that is without wife, is busy what things be of the Lord, how he shall please God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:7; COLOSSIANS 3:19; EPHESIANS 5:25–33"> But he that is with a wife, is busy what things be of the world, how he shall please the wife &#91;or his wife&#93;, and he is parted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 10:40–42; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:20; LUKE 2:36–37"> And a woman unwedded and &#91;a&#93; maiden thinketh what things be of the Lord, that she be holy in body and spirit. But she that is wedded, thinketh what things be of the world, how she shall please the husband &#91;or her husband&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:33–34; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:36; LUKE 8:14"> And I say these things to your profit, not that I cast to you a snare, but to that that is seemly, and that giveth easiness &#91;or facility&#93;, without hindering to make prayers, &#91;or to beseech&#93;, to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:37; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:9; 1ST SAMUEL 2:33"> And if any man guesseth himself to be seen foul on his virgin, that she is full waxen &#91;or is well old&#93;, and so it behooveth to be done, do she that that she will &#91;or what he will&#93;; she sinneth not, if she be wedded, &#91;+or s/he sinneth not, if s/he be wedded&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> For he that ordained stably, &#91;or steadfastly&#93;, in his heart, not having need, but having power of his will, and hath deemed in his heart this thing, to keep his virgin &#91;or his virginity&#93;, doeth well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:1–2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:8"> Therefore he that joineth his virgin in matrimony, doeth well; and he that joineth not, doeth better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ROMANS 7:2–3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14–16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:10"> The woman is bound to the law, as long time as her husband liveth; and if her husband is dead, she is delivered from the law of the husband, be she wedded to whom she will, only in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:35; 2ND PETER 3:15–16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:8"> But she shall be more blessed, if she dwelleth thus, after my counsel; and I ween, that I have the Spirit of God.</span>
