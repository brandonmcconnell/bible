<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 1:1; EPHESIANS 1:1; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:1"> Paul, called apostle of Jesus Christ, by the will of God, and Sosthenes, brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 1:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9–11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30"> to the church of God that is at Corinth, to them that be hallowed in Christ Jesus, and called saints, with all that inwardly call &#91;or in-call&#93; the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, in each place of them and of ours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 1:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:2; 1ST PETER 1:2"> grace to you and peace of God, our Father, and of the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 1:8; JOHN 14:26; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:14"> I do thankings to my God ever-more for you, in the grace of God that is given to you in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:7; COLOSSIANS 2:3"> For in all things ye be made rich in him, in each word, and in each knowing, &#91;or science&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:2; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:8; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:6"> as the witnessing of Christ is confirmed in you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:20; 2ND PETER 3:12; HEBREWS 9:28"> so that nothing fail to you in any grace, that abide the showing &#91;or the revelation&#93; of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:23–24; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:13; COLOSSIANS 1:22"> which also shall confirm you into the end without crime, &#91;or great sin&#93;, in the day of the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:3; HEBREWS 10:23; DEUTERONOMY 7:9"> A true God &#91;or Forsooth God is true&#93;, by whom ye be called into the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:1–4; EPHESIANS 4:1–7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:25"> But, brethren, I beseech you, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all say the same thing, and that dissensions, &#91;or schisms, or divisions, or discords&#93;, be not among you; but be ye perfect in the same wit, and in the same knowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:6; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:23–25"> For, my brethren, it is told to me of them that be at Chloe’s, that strives be among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:21–23; JOHN 1:42; MATTHEW 23:9–10"> And I say that, that each of you saith, For I am of Paul, and I am of Apollos, and I am of Cephas, &#91;that is, Peter&#93;, but I am of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38"> Whether Christ is parted? whether Paul was crucified for you, either ye be baptized in the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:8; ROMANS 16:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:4"> I do thankings to my God, that I baptized none of you, but Crispus and Gaius;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:2; JOHN 3:28–29; JOHN 7:18"> lest any man say, that ye be baptized in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 16:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:33"> And I baptized also the house of Stephanas, but I know not, that I baptized any other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:13; 2ND PETER 1:16"> For Christ sent me not to baptize, but to preach the gospel &#91;or to evangelize&#93;; not in wisdom of word, that the cross of Christ be not voided away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; ROMANS 1:16; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:10"> For the word of the cross is folly to them that perish; but to them that be made safe, that is to say, to us, it is the virtue of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:14; JEREMIAH 8:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19"> For it is written, I shall destroy the wisdom of wise men, and I shall reprove the prudence of prudent men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 1:22; JOB 12:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19"> Where is the wise man? where is the wise lawyer? &#91;or where is the writer, or the man of law?&#93; where is the purchaser of this world? Whether God hath not made the wisdom of this world fond? &#91;or foolish?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 10:21; ROMANS 1:20–22; MATTHEW 11:25"> For the world, in wisdom of God, knew not God by wisdom, &#91;or For why for in the wisdom of God, the world knew not God by wisdom&#93;, it pleased to God, by &#91;the&#93; folly of preaching, to make them safe that believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:1–4; MATTHEW 12:38–39; MARK 8:11"> For Jews seek signs, and Greeks seek wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:2; 1ST PETER 2:8"> but we preach Christ crucified, to the Jews cause of stumbling, and to heathen men folly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30; COLOSSIANS 2:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:18"> but to those Jews and Greeks that be called, we preach Christ the virtue of God, and the wisdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 11:33–36; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:18; ZECHARIAH 4:6–7"> For that that is folly thing of God, is wiser than men; and that that is the feeble, &#91;or frail&#93;, thing of God, is stronger than men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18–20; JAMES 1:9–11"> But, brethren, see ye your calling; for not many wise men after the flesh, not many mighty, not many noble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; MATTHEW 11:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20"> But God chose those things that be fond &#91;or foolish&#93; of the world, to confound wise men; and God chose the feeble, &#91;or frail&#93;, things of the world, to confound the strong things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 34:24; ROMANS 4:17; ISAIAH 2:11"> and God chose the unnoble things and despisable things of the world, and those things that be not, to destroy those things that be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ROMANS 3:27; JEREMIAH 9:23; EPHESIANS 2:9"> that each man have not glory in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:7; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; ROMANS 8:1"> But of him ye be in Christ Jesus, which is made of God to us wisdom, and rightwiseness, and holiness, and again-buying;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23–24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:17; PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> that, as it is written, He that glorieth, have glory in the Lord.</span>
