<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 4:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:33; TITUS 2:15"> Therefore no man despise him; but lead him forth in peace, that he come to me; for I abide him with brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:5; TITUS 3:4"> But, brethren, I make known to you of Apollos, that I prayed him much, that he should come to you, with brethren. But it was not his will to come now; but he shall come, when he shall have leisure &#91;or it shall be able to him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; EPHESIANS 6:13–18; EPHESIANS 6:10"> Walk ye, and stand ye in the faith; do ye manly, and be ye comforted in the Lord,</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:16; ROMANS 16:5; ROMANS 16:2"> And, brethren, I beseech you, ye know the house of Stephanas, and of Fortunatus, and Achaicus, for they be the first fruits of Achaia, and into ministry of saints they have ordained themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:17; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:12; 3RD JOHN 1:8"> that also ye be subjects to such, and to each working together and travailing.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:13; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:12"> for they have refreshed both my spirit and yours. Therefore know ye them, that be such manner men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:2; PHILEMON 1:2; ROMANS 16:3–5"> All the churches of Asia greet you well. Aquila and Priscilla, with their home-church, greet you much in the Lord, at the which also I am harboured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:26; 1ST PETER 5:14"> All brethren greet you well. Greet ye well together in holy kiss.</span>
