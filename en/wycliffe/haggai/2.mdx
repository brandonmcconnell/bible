<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HAGGAI 1:1; HAGGAI 2:20; HAGGAI 2:10"> In the seventh month, in the one and twentieth day of the month, the word of the Lord was made in the hand of Haggai, the prophet, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HAGGAI 1:1; NEHEMIAH 8:9; EZRA 2:63"> Speak thou to Zerubbabel, the son of Salathiel, the duke of Judah, and to Joshua, the great priest, the son of Josedech, and to others of the people, and say thou,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZRA 3:12; HAGGAI 2:9; EZEKIEL 7:20"> Who in you is left, that saw this house in his first glory? and what see ye this now? whether it is not thus, as if it be not before your eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:20; JOSHUA 1:9; ZECHARIAH 8:9"> And now, Zerubbabel, be thou strengthened, saith the Lord, and Joshua, the great priest, the son of Josedech, be thou comforted, and all the people of the land, be thou comforted, saith the Lord of hosts; and do ye, for I am with you, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 34:10; EXODUS 29:45–46; ISAIAH 41:13"> This is The word that I covenanted with you, when ye went out of the land of Egypt, and my Spirit shall be in the midst of you. Do not ye dread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:26–28; ISAIAH 10:25; MARK 13:24–26"> for the Lord of hosts saith these things, Yet one little thing is, and I shall move heaven, and earth, and sea, and dry land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; 1ST KINGS 8:11; JOHN 7:37–39"> and I shall move all folks, and the desired to all folks shall come; and I shall fill this house with glory, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 50:10–12; PSALMS 24:1; ISAIAH 60:17"> Mine is silver, and mine is gold, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 85:8–9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:9–10; COLOSSIANS 1:19–21"> The glory of this last house shall be great, more than of the first, saith the Lord of hosts. And in this place I shall give peace, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HAGGAI 2:20; HAGGAI 1:1; HAGGAI 1:15–1"> In the four and twentieth day of the ninth month, in the second year of king Darius, the word of the Lord was made to Haggai, the prophet, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10–11; MALACHI 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 33:10"> The Lord God of hosts saith these things, Ask thou priests the law, and say thou,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:27; MATTHEW 23:19; LEVITICUS 7:6"> If a man taketh hallowed flesh in the hem of his clothing, and toucheth of the highness thereof bread, either pottage, either wine, either oil, either any meat, whether it shall be hallowed? Soothly priests answered, and said, Nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:2–3; NUMBERS 19:11–22; LEVITICUS 22:4–6"> And Haggai said, If a man defouled in soul toucheth of all these things, whether it shall be defouled? And priests answered, and said, It shall be defouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:9; TITUS 1:15; ISAIAH 1:11–15"> And Haggai answered, and said, So is this people, and so is this folk before my face, saith the Lord, and so is all the work of their hands; and all things which they offer there shall be defouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HAGGAI 1:5; EZRA 3:10; EZRA 4:24"> And now put ye this on your hearts, from this day and above, before that a stone on a stone was put in the temple of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HAGGAI 1:6; MALACHI 2:2; PROVERBS 3:9–10"> when ye went to an heap of twenty bushels, and there were made ten; ye entered to the presser, that ye should press out fifty gallons, and there were made twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:22; HAGGAI 1:11; HAGGAI 1:9"> I smote you with burning wind, and with mildew, and hail, all the works of your hands; and there was none in you that turned again to me, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:9; HAGGAI 2:15; EZRA 5:1–2"> Put ye this on your hearts from this day, and into coming, from the four and twentieth day of the ninth month, from the day in which foundaments of the temple of the Lord be casted, put ye this on your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MALACHI 3:10; HABAKKUK 3:17–18; PROVERBS 3:9–10"> Whether now seed is in burgeoning? and yet vineyard, and fig tree, and pomegranate, and the tree of olive flowered not. From this day I shall bless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the word of the Lord was made the second time to Haggai, in the four and twentieth day of the month, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:6–10; HAGGAI 2:6–7; HAGGAI 1:1"> Speak thou to Zerubbabel, duke of Judah, and say thou, I shall move heaven and earth together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MICAH 5:10; JUDGES 7:22; ZEPHANIAH 3:8"> and I shall destroy the seat of realms, and I shall all-break the strength of &#91;the&#93; realms of heathen men, and I shall destroy a four-horsed cart, and the rider thereof; and &#91;the&#93; horses shall go down, and &#91;the&#93; riders of them, a man by sword of his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:10; ISAIAH 42:1; SONG OF SONGS 8:6"> In that day, saith the Lord of hosts, thou Zerubbabel, son of Salathiel, my servant, I shall take thee, saith the Lord; and I shall put thee as a signet, for I chose thee, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
