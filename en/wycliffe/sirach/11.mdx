<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The wisdom of a man made meek shall enhance his head; and shall make him to sit in the midst of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Praise thou not a man in his fairness; neither despise thou a man in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A bee is little among birds; and his fruit hath the beginning of sweet-ness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Have thou never glory in cloth-ing, and be thou not enhanced in the day of thine honour; for why the works of the Highest alone be wonderful, and his works be glorious, and hid, and unseen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Many tyrants have set in throne; and a man of whom was no supposing bare the diadem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many mighty men be oppressed strongly; and glorious men be given into the hands of other men. &#91;Many mighty men be oppressed greatly; and the glorious be taken into the hands of other men.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Before that thou ask, blame thou not any man; and when thou hast asked, blame thou justly &#91;or chastise thou rightly&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Before that thou hear, answer thou not a word; and in the midst of elder men add thou not to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Strive thou not, of that thing that dis-easeth &#91;or grieveth&#93; not thee; and stand thou not in the doom of sins &#91;or in the middle of sinners&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Son, thy deeds be not in many things; and if thou art rich, thou shalt not be without part of guilt &#91;or thou shalt not be guiltless from trespass&#93;. For if thou pursuest &#91;or shalt follow&#93;, thou shalt not &#91;over&#93;-take; and thou shalt not escape, if thou runnest before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> There is a man travailing, and hasting, and sorrowing, and unpious; and by so much the more he shall not have plenty &#91;or shall not abound&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There is a man fade, that is, feeble, failing more than others, needy of recovering, failing more in virtue, and plenteous in poverty; and the eye of God beheld him in good, and raised him from his lowness, &#91;There is a man withered, needing recovering, more failing in virtue, and abounding in poorness; and the eye of God beheld him in good, and reared him from his lowness;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and enhanced his head; and many men wondered &#91;or marvelled&#93; in him, and honoured &#91;or worship-ped&#93; God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Goods and evils, that is, prosperities and adversities, life and death, poverty and riches, be of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Wisdom, and learning, and knowing of the law be with the Lord; love and the ways of good men be at &#91;or with&#93; him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Error and darknesses be made together to sinners; forsooth they that make full out joy in evil, wax eld &#91;or old&#93; together into evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The gift of God dwelleth to just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men; and increasings of him shall have prosperities without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Some man there is made rich in doing scarcely &#91;or scarcely doing&#93;, and this is the part of his meed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> in that that he saith, I have found rest to me, and now I alone shall eat of my goods. And he know not that time passeth him, and death nigheth, and he shall leave all things to other men, and shall die &#91;or and die&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Stand thou in thy testament, and speak thou altogether in it; and wax thou eld &#91;or old&#93; in the work of thy behests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Dwell &#91;or Abide&#93; thou not in the works of sinners; but trust thou in God, and dwell in thy place. For it is easy in the eyes of God, suddenly to make rich a poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The blessing of God hasteth into the meed of a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man; and the going forth of him maketh fruit in swift honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Say thou not, What is need to me? and what goods shall be for me hereafter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Say thou not, I am sufficient, and what shall I be made worse hereafter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In the day of goods, be thou not unmindful of evils, and in the day of evils, be thou not unmindful of goods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> for it is easy before God to yield in the day of death, to each man after his ways. &#91;for light it is before God in the day of death, to yield to each after his ways.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The malice of one hour maketh forgetting of most lechery; and in the end of a man is making naked of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Praise thou not any man before his death; for why a man is known in his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Bring thou not each man into thine house; for why many treasons be of a guileful &#91;or treacherous&#93; man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For why as the entrails of stinking things break out, and as a partridge is led into a trap, either net, and as a capret is led into a snare, so and the heart of proud men; and as a beholder &#91;or the for-looker&#93; seeing the fall of his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For he turneth goods &#91;or good things&#93; into evils, and setteth treasons, and putteth a wem on chosen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Fire is increased of a sparkle, and blood is increased of a guileful &#91;or treacherous&#93; man; for why a sinful man setteth treason to blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Take heed to thee from a guileful man, for he maketh evils; lest per-adventure he bring in on thee scorning without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Receive thou an alien to thee, and he shall destroy thee in a whirlwind, and he shall make thee alienated from thine own ways.</span>
