<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> All wisdom is of the Lord God, and was ever with him, and is before the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Who numbered the gravel of the sea, and the drops of rain, and the days of the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Who measured the highness &#91;or height&#93; of heaven, and the breadth of earth, and the depth of the sea? Who ensearched the wisdom of God, that goeth before all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Wisdom was formed first of all things, and the understanding of prudence, from the world, that is, from without beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The well of wisdom is the son of God in high things &#91;or the word of God in heights&#93;; and the entering of that wisdom is everlasting command-ments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> To whom was the root of wisdom showed? and who knew the subtleties thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> To whom was the lore &#91;or the discipline&#93; of wisdom showed, and made open? and who understood the multiplying of the entering thereof, that is, of the work thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> One is the highest Creator &#91;or maker of nought&#93; of all things, almighty, and a mighty king, and worthy to be dreaded full much, sitting on the throne of that wisdom, and God having lordship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He formed it in the Holy Ghost, and he saw, and numbered, and he measured. And he shedded &#91;or poured&#93; out it on all his works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and on each flesh by &#91;or after&#93; his gift; he giveth it to them that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The dread of the Lord is glory, and glorying &#91;or joying&#93;, and glad-ness, and a crown of full out joying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The dread of the Lord shall delight the heart; and shall give gladness and joy into &#91;the&#93; length of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> To him that dreadeth God, it shall be well in the last things or days; and he shall be blessed in the day of his death. Forsooth they to whom wisdom appeareth in sight, that is, by revelation of prophecy, love it in sight, and in knowing of his great things The love of God is honourable wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The beginning of wisdom is the dread of the Lord; and it is formed together in the womb with faithful men, and it goeth with chosen women, and it is known with just &#91;or right-wise&#93; men and faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The dread of the Lord is religi-osity of knowing. Religiosity shall keep, and shall justify the heart; and shall give mirth and joy. It shall be well to him that dreadeth God; and he shall be blessed in the days of his comfort &#91;or in the days of ending of him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The fullness of wisdom is for to dread God; and fullness is of the fruits thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> It shall fill each gift &#91;or each house&#93; of him of generations, and receptacles of the treasures thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The crown of wisdom is the dread of the Lord, and filleth peace, and the fruit of health. And he saw, and numbered it; forsooth ever either be the gifts of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Wisdom shall part the knowing and understanding of prudence; and it enhanceth the glory of them, that hold it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The root of wisdom is for to dread God; forsooth the branches thereof be long enduring &#91;or long living&#93;. Understanding and religiosity of knowing be in the treasures of wisdom; but wisdom is abomination to sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The dread of the Lord putteth away sin, for he that is without dread of God, may not be justified;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> for why the wrathfulness of his pride &#91;or willfulness&#93; is the destroying of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A patient man shall suffer the dis-eases of a proud man till into time; and afterward there shall be yielding of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Good wit shall hide the words of him till into a time; and the lips of many men shall tell out the wit of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In the treasures of wisdom is signifying of knowing; but the wor-shipping of God is abomination to a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A! son, coveting wisdom, keep thou rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and God shall give it to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For why the dread of the Lord is wisdom, and knowing &#91;or discipline&#93;, and that that is well pleasant &#91;or well-pleased&#93; to him is faith and mildness; and God shall fill the treasures of him &#91;or it shall full-fill the treasures of him&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Be thou not rebel, and unbelieve-ful to the dread of the Lord; and nigh thou not to him in &#91;or with&#93; double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Be thou not an hypocrite in the sight of men; and be thou not caused to stumble in thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Take thou keep to those &#91;things&#93;, lest thou fall, and bring dishonor to thy soul; and lest God show thy privates, and hurtle thee down in the midst of the synagogue, that is, of gathering together of faithful men; for thou nighedest wickedly &#91;or malicious-ly&#93; to the Lord, and thine heart was full of guile and of falseness &#91;or treachery and deceit&#93;.</span>
