<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Vain hope and a leasing to an unwise man; and dreams &#91;or swevens&#93; enhance unprudent men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> As he that taketh &#91;or catcheth&#93; a shadow, and pursueth wind, so and he that taketh heed to leasings seen, or in vain dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> After this thing is the sight of dreams &#91;or swevens&#93;; before the face of a man is the likeness of another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> What shall be cleansed of him that is unclean, and what true thing shall be said of a liar? &#91;Of the unclean, what shall be cleansed, and of the liar, what sooth shall be said?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> False divining of error, and false divinings by chittering of birds, and dreams of witches, is vanity. And as the heart of a woman travailing of &#91;or bearing&#93; child, thine heart suffereth fantasies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> no but visitation is sent out of the Highest, give thou not thine heart in those dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For why dreams have made many men for to err, and men hoping in those &#91;or them&#93; fell down &#91;or fell away&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The word of the law of God and of his prophets, shall be made perfect without leasing; and wisdom in the mouth of a faithful man shall be made plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> What knoweth he, that is not assayed? A man assayed in many things, shall think many things; and he that learned many things, shall tell out understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He that is not assayed &#91;or Who Is not expert&#93;, knoweth few things; forsooth he that is a fool in many things, shall multiply malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> What manner things know he, that is not assayed? He that is not planted, that is, hath not set root of understanding, and desire in good, shall be plenteous in wickedness &#91;or abound shrewdness&#93;. I saw many things in telling out, and full many customs of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Sometime I was in peril unto death, for the cause of these things; and I was delivered by the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The spirit of them that dread God is sought, and shall be blessed in the beholding of him. For why the hope of them is into God saving them; and the eyes of the Lord be into them, that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He that dreadeth God, shall not tremble for anything, and he shall not dread; for why God is his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The soul of him that dreadeth the Lord, is blessed. To whom beholdeth he, and who is his strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The eyes of the Lord be on them that dread him. God is a defender of might, steadfastness &#91;or firmament&#93; of virtue, covering of heat, and a sha-dowing place of midday; beseeching of offending, that is, hearing beseech-ing for offenses, and help of falling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> enhancing the soul, and lighten-ing the eyes, and giving health, and life, and blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The offering of him that offereth of wicked thing, that is, of thing gotten unjustly, is defouled; and the scornings of unjust men be not well pleasant &#91;or well-pleasing&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The Lord alone is to them that abide him in the way of truth, and of rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;. The Highest approveth not the gifts of wicked men, neither beholdeth in the offerings of wicked men, neither in the multitude of their sacrifices he shall do &#91;or have mercy&#93; to their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He that offereth sacrifice of the chattel &#91;or substance&#93; of poor men, is as he that slayeth the son in the sight of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The bread of needy men is the life of a poor man; he that defraudeth him, is a man of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He that taketh away bread earned in sweat, is as he that slayeth his neighbour. He that sheddeth out blood, and he that doeth fraud to an hired man, be brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> One building, and one destroy-ing; what profiteth it to them, no but travail?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> One praying, and one cursing; whose voice shall the Lord hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> What profiteth the washing of him, that is washed for a dead body, and toucheth again a dead body? &#91;That is baptized from the dead, and again toucheth the dead, what profit-eth his washing?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So a man that fasteth in his sins, and again doing the same sins, what profiteth he in meeking himself? who shall hear his prayer?</span>
