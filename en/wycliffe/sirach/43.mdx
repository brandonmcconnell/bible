<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The firmament of highness is the fairness thereof &#91;or The firmament of the height is the fairness of him&#93;; the fairness of heaven in the sight of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The sun in beholding, telling in going out, is a wonderful vessel, the work of high God. &#91;The sun in the sight, showing out in the issue, a marvellous vessel, the work of the High.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In the time of midday it burneth the earth; and who shall be able to suffer in the sight of his heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Keeping a furnace in the works of heat; the sun burning hills in three manners, sending out beams of fire &#91;or blasting out fiery beams&#93;, and shining again with his beams, blind-eth eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The Lord is great &#91;or Great is the Lord&#93;, that made it; and in the words of him it hasted its journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the moon in all men in his time is showing of time, and a sign of the world. &#91;And the moon in all things in his time showing of time, and token of the spiritual world.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A sign of the feast &#91;or holy&#93; day is taken of the moon; the light which is made little in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The month is increasing &#91;or wax-ing&#93; by the name thereof, wonderfully into the ending. A vessel of castles &#91;or tents&#93; in high things, shining glorious-ly in the firmament of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The fairness of heaven is the glory of stars; the Lord on high lighteneth the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In the words of the Holy, those shall stand at the doom; and those shall not fail in their wakings &#91;or they shall not fail in their watches&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> See thou the rainbow, and bless thou him that made it; it is full fair in his shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> It went about heaven in the com-pass &#91;or the circuit&#93; of his glory; the hands of high God&#91;or of the High&#93; opened it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> By his commandment he hasted the snow; and he hasteth to send out the lightnings of his doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Therefore &#91;the&#93; treasures were opened, and &#91;the&#93; clouds fled out as bees &#91;or flew away as birds&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> In his greatness he setted &#91;or put the&#93; clouds; and stones of hail were broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> &#91;The&#93; Hills shall be moved in his sight; and the south wind shall blow in his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The voice of his thunder shall beat &#91;or beateth&#93; the earth; the tempest of the north, and the gathering together of wind. And as a bird putting down to sit sprinkleth snow, and the coming down of that snow is as a locust drowning down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The eye shall wonder &#91;or marvel&#93; on the fairness of whiteness thereof; and an heart dreadeth &#91;or quaketh&#93; on the rain thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He shall shed &#91;or pour&#93; out frost as salt &#91;up&#93; on the earth; and while the wind bloweth, it shall be made as cops or tops of a briar &#91;or bramble bush&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The cold northern wind blew, and crystal of water froze together &#91;or the crystal freezed from the water&#93;; it resteth on all the gathering together of waters, and it clotheth itself with waters, as with an habergeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And it shall devour hills, and it shall burn the desert; and it shall quench &#91;the&#93; green thing as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The medicine of all things is in the hasting &#91;or hieing&#93; of a cloud; a dew, meeting the heat coming of burning, shall make it low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The wind was still in the word of God; by his thought he made peaceable the depth of waters; and the Lord Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Though the author of this book, that was an Hebrew man, wrote this book in Greek, nevertheless he setted in here an Hebrew name. (Notwithstanding this gloss from the “Wycliffe Bible”, modern translations do not have this or any name here. T.P.N.)">a</a></sup>, that is, God, which is Saviour of all men, planted it. &#91;In his word the wind held his peace; by his thinking he shall peace the sea; and the Lord Jesus planted it.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They that sail in the sea, tell out the perils thereof; and we hearing with our ears, shall wonder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> There be full clear works, and wonderful &#91;or marvellous&#93;, diverse kinds of beasts, and of all little beasts, and the creatures of wonderful fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The end of way is confirmed for it; and all things be made in the word of him. &#91;For him is confirmed the end of the way; and in the word of him all things be made together.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> We say many things, and we fail in words; forsooth he is the &#91;full&#93; ending of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> To what thing shall we be mighty, that have glory in all things? for he is all-mighty above &#91;or over&#93; all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The Lord is fearedful, and full great; and his power is wonderful &#91;or marvellous&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Glorify ye the Lord as much as ever ye may, yet he shall be mightier; and his great doing is wonderful &#91;or marvellous&#93;. Ye blessing the Lord, enhance him as much as ye may; for he is more than all praising. Ye enhancing him shall be filled with virtue; travail ye not to know God perfectly in this life, for ye shall not take it perfectly, that is, for it is unpossible, &#91;or ye shall not full come&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Who saw him, and shall tell it out? and who shall magnify him, as he is from the beginning?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Many things greater than these be hid from us; for we have seen few things of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Forsooth the Lord made all things; and he gave wisdom to men doing faithfully &#91;or piously&#93;.</span>
