<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that keepeth the word of God, multiplieth prayer. Wholeful &#91;or Wholesome&#93; sacrifice is to take heed to the commandments, and to depart &#91;or go away&#93; from all wickedness. And to offer the pleasing of sacrifice for unrightfulnesses &#91;or unrightwise-nesses&#93;, and beseeching for sins, is to go away from unrightfulness &#91;or unrightwiseness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He that offereth purest &#91;or tried&#93; flour of wheat, shall yield grace, that is, shall give a pleasant service to God; and he that doeth mercy, offereth a sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> It is well pleasant &#91;or well-pleas-ing&#93; to the Lord, to go away from wickedness; and prayer is to go away from unrightfulness &#91;or unrightwise-ness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Thou shalt not appear void before the sight of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> for why all these things be done for the behests of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The offering of a just &#91;or right-wise&#93; man maketh fat the altar, and is odour of sweetness in the sight of the Highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sacrifice of a just &#91;or right-wise&#93; man is acceptable, and the Lord shall not forget the mind of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> With good will yield thou glory to God, and make thou not less the first fruits of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In each gift &#91;or all gifts&#93; make glad thy cheer, and in full out joying hallow thy tithes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Give thou to the Highest after his gift &#91;or Give to the Highest after the free gift of him&#93;, that is, after the quantity of thy goods, which thou hast of God; and with good eye make thou the findings of thine hands, that is, with rightful intent, ordain thy good works, to the praising of God and not of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For why the Lord is a yielder, and he shall yield sevenfold so much to thee &#91;or seven times as much he shall yield to thee&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not thou offer shrewd gifts; for he shall not receive those &#91;or them&#93;. And do not thou behold an unjust &#91;or unrightwise&#93; sacrifice; for the Lord is judge, and glory, or taking, of persons is not at &#91;or with&#93; him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The Lord shall not take or favour a person against a poor man; and he shall hear the prayer of him that is hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He shall not despise the prayers of a fatherless child, neither a widow, if she sheddeth &#91;or poured&#93; out speech of wailing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Whether the tears of a widow go not down to the cheek, and the crying of her on him that leadeth forth those tears? For why those ascend &#91;or they go up&#93; from the cheek unto heaven, and the Lord hearing shall not delight in those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He that worshippeth God in delighting, shall be received; and his prayer shall nigh unto the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The prayer of him that meeketh himself shall pierce clouds, and till it nigheth to God, he shall not be comforted, and he shall not go away, till the Highest behold. And the Lord shall not be far &#91;or long away&#93;, but he shall judge just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men, and shall make doom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the Strongest shall not have patience in those &#91;or them&#93;, that he trouble the backs of them. And he shall yield vengeance to folks, till he take away the fullness of proud men, &#91;or to Gentiles he shall yield venge-ance, to the time that he take away the plenty of proud men&#93;, and trouble altogether the sceptres of wicked men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> till he yield to men after their deeds, and after the works of Adam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="By Hebrews, this word ‘Adam’ signifieth man commonly.">a</a></sup>, and after the presumption of him; till he deem the doom of his people, and shall delight just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men in his mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The mercy of God is fair in the time of tribulation, as clouds of rain in the time of dryness &#91;or drought&#93;.</span>
