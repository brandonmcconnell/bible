<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="wycliffe" data-translation-id="eng_wyc2017" data-translation-label="Wycliffe" data-book="SIR" data-book-slug="sirach" data-book-name="ECCLESIASTICUS" data-chapter="13" data-verses="26">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that toucheth pitch, shall be defouled of it; and he that communeth with a proud man, shall be clothed with &#91;or in&#93; pride<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="For why men be inclined to undue desire of their own excellence, wherein pride standeth, wherefore it cleaveth lightly to a man.">a</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He raiseth a weight on himself &#91;or Burden upon him he taketh&#93;, that communeth with a more rich man than himself; and be thou not fellow to a man richer than thou. What shall a caldron commune to a pot? for when those &#91;or they&#93; hurtle them-selves together, the pot shall be broken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A rich man shall do unjustly &#91;or unrightwisely&#93;, and shall gnash, as ready yet to do worse; but a poor man hurt shall be still &#91;or shall hold his peace&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> If thou givest, he shall take thee; and if thou hast not, he shall forsake thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If thou hast, he shall live together with thee, and shall make thee void; and he shall not have sorrow on thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> If thou art needful &#91;or necessary&#93; to him, he shall deceive thee; and he shall flatter, and shall give hope, telling to thee all goods; and shall say, What is need to thee? &#91;or What need is to thee?&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he shall shame thee in his meats, till he annihilate or extinguish thee twice and thrice, and at the last he shall scorn thee; afterward he shall see, and shall forsake thee, and he shall move his head to thee. Be thou made meek to God, and abide thou his hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Take heed, lest thou be deceived, and be made low in folly. Do not thou be low in thy wisdom, lest thou be made low, and be deceived into folly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When thou art called of a mightier man, go thou away; for by this he shall more call thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Be thou not greatly pressing &#91;or too greedy&#93;, lest thou be hurtled down &#91;or be put again&#93;; and be thou not far from him, lest thou go into forgetting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Withhold thou not to speak with him evenly, that is, speak thou to him without reverence, and believe thou not to his many words; for of much speech he shall tempt thee, and he shall laugh privily, and shall ask thee of thine hid things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> His cruel soul shall keep thy words, and he shall not spare of &#91;or from&#93; malice, and of &#91;or from&#93; bonds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Beware to thee, and take heed diligently to thine hearing; for thou goest with thy destroying &#91;or thy turning upside-down&#93;. But thou hear-ing those things, see as in sleep, and thou shalt wake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> In all thy life love thou God &#91;or love God&#93;, and inwardly call thou him in thine health, that is, for thine health, temporal and everlasting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Each beast loveth a beast like itself; so and each man oweth to love his neighbour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Each flesh shall be joined to flesh like itself, and each man shall be fellowshipped to a man like himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> As a wolf shall commune some-time with a lamb, so a sinner with a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> What communing &#91;or communi-cation&#93; is of an holy man to a dog? either what good part is of a rich man to a poor man?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The hunting of a lion is a wild ass in desert &#91;or wilderness&#93;; so in the pastures of rich men be poor men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And as meekness is abomination to a proud man, so and a poor man is abomination of a rich man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> A rich man moved, that is, disturbed, either hurled, is confirmed of his friends; but a meek man, when he falleth, shall be cast out, yea, of known men &#91;or shall be put out also from known&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Many recoverers be to a rich man deceived; he spake proudly, and they justified him. A meek man is deceived, furthermore also he is reproved; he spake wisely, and no place was given to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The rich man spake, and all men were still &#91;or held their peace&#93;; and they shall bring &#91;or shall bear&#93; his word till to the clouds. A poor man spake, and they say, Who is this? and if he offendeth, they shall destroy him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Chattel is good to him, to whom is no sin in conscience; and the worst poverty is in the mouth of a wicked man. &#91;Good is substance, to whom is not sin in conscience; and most wicked is poorness in the mouth of the unpious.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The heart of a man changeth his face, either in good either in evil. Of hard and with travail, thou shalt find the step of a good heart, and a good face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> (This verse is omitted in the original text.)</span>
</div>
