<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Have thou mind on &#91;or Remem-ber&#93; the dread of the Lord, and be not wroth to the neighbour. Have thou mind on &#91;or Remember&#93; the testament of the Highest, and despise thou the ignorance of thy neighbour.</span>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and a sinful man, that is, a sower of discords, shall disturb or trouble friends, and he shall send in enmity in the midst &#91;or in the middle&#93; of men having peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For why after the trees of the wood, so fire shall burn on high; and after the might of a man, so his wrathfulness shall be, and after his chattel &#91;or substance&#93; he shall enhance his ire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hasty striving &#91;or strife&#93; shall kindle fire, and hasty chiding shall shed out blood; and a tongue bearing witnessing shall bring death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If thou blowest, as fire it shall burn on high; and if thou spittest thereon, it shall be quenched; ever either, that is, a word kindling ire, and a word refraining it, come forth of the mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> A privy backbiter, and a double-tongued man &#91;or The whispering grutcher, and the twisel tongue cursed&#93;, yea, he that speaketh fair before a man, and evil behind him, is cursed; for he disturbed or troubled many men having peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The third tongue hath stirred many men, and hath scattered them from folk into folk. It hath destroyed walled cities of rich men, and hath mined down or undermined the houses of great men. It hath cut down the virtues of peoples, and hath unknit strong folks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The third tongue hath cast out wedded, or honest, women, and hath deprived them of their travails.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He that beholdeth the third tongue &#91;or it&#93;, shall not have rest; neither shall have a friend, in whom he shall rest.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The wound of &#91;a&#93; beating &#91;or scourge&#93; maketh wanness; but the wound of &#91;a&#93; tongue shall make less &#91;or shall bruise or break&#93; the bones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Many men fell down by the sharpness of sword; but not so many as they that perished &#91;or died&#93; by their tongue.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He is blessed that is covered from a wicked &#91;or a shrewd&#93; tongue; and he that passed not in the wrathfulness thereof, and he that drew not the yoke thereof, and was not bound in the bonds thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For why the yoke thereof is an iron yoke, and the bond thereof is a brazen bond.</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
