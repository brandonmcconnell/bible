<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that dreadeth God, shall do good works; and he that holdeth rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, shall take it, that is, wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And it as a mother honoured shall meet him, and as a woman from virginity &#91;or maidenhood&#93; it shall take him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> It shall feed him with the bread of life, and of understanding; and it shall give drink to him with water of heal-ful &#91;or wholesome&#93; wisdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> it shall be made steadfast in him, and he shall not be bowed from the evenness of rightfulness. And it shall hold him, and he shall not be shamed &#91;or confounded&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and it shall enhance him at his neighbours. And in the midst of the church he &#91;or it&#93; shall open his mouth; and God shall fill him with the spirit of wisdom, and of under-standing, and shall clothe him with the stole of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> God shall treasure on him mirth, and full out joying; and shall inherit him with everlasting name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Fond men &#91;or fools&#93; shall not take that wisdom, and witting men shall meet it. Fond men &#91;or fools&#93; shall not see it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> for why it goeth away far from pride, and guile &#91;or treachery&#93;. Men leasing-mongers &#91;or liars&#93; shall not be mindful thereof, and soothfast men be found therein; and shall have prosperity unto the beholding of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Praising is not fair in the mouth of a sinner, for he is not sent of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For why wisdom went forth from God; forsooth praising shall stand nigh the wisdom of God, and it shall be plenteous &#91;or abound&#93; in a faithful mouth, and the Lord shall give it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Say thou not, It goeth away by God; for why do thou not those things, which God hateth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Say thou not, He made me for to err; for why wicked &#91;or unpious&#93; men be not needful to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The Lord hateth all cursedness of error, and it shall not be amiable &#91;or loveful&#93; to them, that dread him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> At &#91;or from&#93; the beginning God made man, and let him go in the hand of his counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He added his commandments, and laws; if thou wilt keep the commandments, those &#91;or they&#93; shall keep thee, and keep pleasant faith without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He hath set &#91;or put&#93; to thee water and fire; dress &#91;or put forth&#93; thine hand to that, that thou wilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Before man is life and death, good and evil; that, that pleaseth him, shall be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For why the wisdom of God is much, and he is strong in power &#91;or might&#93;, and seeth all men without ceasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The eyes of the Lord be to them, that dread him; and he knoweth all the travail &#91;or all the work&#93; of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He commanded not to any man to do wickedly &#91;or unpiously&#93;; and he gave not to any man space to do sin.</span>
