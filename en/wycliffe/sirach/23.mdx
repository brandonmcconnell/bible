<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Sons, hear ye the teaching &#91;or the doctrine&#93; of &#91;the&#93; mouth; and he that keepeth it, shall not perish by his lips, neither shall be caused to stumble in worst works.</span>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Forsooth the naming of God be not customable &#91;or continual&#93; in thy mouth, and be thou not meddled to &#91;or mingled with&#93; the names of saints; for thou shalt not be guiltless of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Also again-ward another speech is into death; be it not found in the heritage of Jacob. For why all these things shall be done away from merciful men; and they shall not delight in trespasses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thy mouth be not customable to unreverent speech; for why a word of sin is in it. &#91;To the undisciplined speech use not thy mouth; forsooth there is in it the word of sin.&#93;</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Have thou mind on thy father and mother; for thou standest in the midst of great men. <div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> A man customable in the words of shame, in all days shall not be taught. &#91;A man used in words of reproof, in all his days shall not be learned.&#93;</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Each bread is sweet to a lecherous man; he shall not be made weary, trespassing till to the end.</span>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
