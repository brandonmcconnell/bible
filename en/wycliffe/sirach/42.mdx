</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> of the word of fellows, and of way-goers, and of the giving of heritage of friends;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> of the evenness of balance, and of weights, of the getting of many things, and of few things;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> of corruption of buying, and of merchants, and of much chastising &#91;or discipline&#93; of sons; and of a worst servant, to make the side to bleed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A sealing, either enclosing, is good on a wicked woman. Where be many hands, enclose thou;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and whatever thing thou shalt betake, number thou, and weigh thou; forsooth describe thou, either write down, each gift, and taking.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Abstain thou from the teaching &#91;or discipline&#93; of an unwitting man, and &#91;the&#93; fool, and of elder men &#91;or elders&#93; that be deemed of young men; and thou shalt be learned in all things, and thou shalt be commend-able &#91;or approvable&#93; in the sight of all men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> An hid daughter of a father is waking and busyness of him; she shall take away sleep &#91;or The daughter of the father is hid, the watch and the busyness of her shall do away sleep&#93;; lest peradventure she be made adult-eress in her young waxing age, and lest she dwelling with the husband, be made hateful;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> lest anytime she be defouled in her virginity &#91;or polluted in her maidenhood&#93;, and be found with child in the keeping of her father; lest peradventure she dwelling with the husband, do trespass &#91;or she trespass&#93;, either certainly be made barren.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ordain thou keeping &#91;or ward&#93; on a lecherous daughter, lest any time she make thee to come into shame &#91;or reproof&#93; to &#91;thine&#93; enemies, of back-biting in the city, and of casting out of the people; and she make thee ashamed in the multitude of people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not thou take heed to each man in the fairness, that is, in delighting in the beholding of his fairness; and do not thou dwell in the midst of women.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For why a moth cometh forth of clothes, and &#91;the&#93; wickedness of a man cometh forth of a woman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For why the wickedness of a man is better, that is, less evil, than a woman doing well, and a woman shaming into shame &#91;or a woman confounded into reproof&#93;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See note for Chapter 25, verse 13.">a</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore be thou &#91;or be I&#93; mindful of the works of the Lord; and I shall tell the works of the Lord, which I saw, in the words of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The sun lightening beheld by all things &#91;or The sun shining through all things beheld&#93;; and the work thereof is full of the glory of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Whether the Lord made not holy men &#91;or saints&#93; to tell out all his marvels, which the Lord Almighty steadfast in his glory shall confirm?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He shall ensearch the depth, and the heart of men; and he shall think in the fellness, &#91;or the sly wit, or guile&#93;, of them. For the Lord knew all knowing, and beheld into the signs of the world;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> telling &#91;out&#93; those things that be passed, and those things that shall come; showing &#91;or opening&#93; the steps of hid things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And no thought passeth him by, and no word hideth itself from him.</span>

</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All these things live, and dwell into the world; and all things obey to him in all need.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> All things be double, one against one; and he made not anything to fail.</span>
