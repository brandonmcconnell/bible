<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> There is false reproving in the ire of a man full of despising &#91;or There is lying correction in wrath of the wrongful&#93;; and there is doom which is not proved to be good; and there is a still man, and he is prudent.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The covetousness &#91;or lust&#93; of a gelding hath defouled &#91;or deflowered&#93; the maidenhood of a young woman, so he that maketh wicked doom by violence &#91;or so he that doeth by force wicked doom&#93;.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> It is full good, that a man that is reproved, show openly penance &#91;or How good it is, the chastised to show penance&#93;; for so thou shalt escape willful sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> There is a still man, which is found wise; and he that is hateful, which is fool-hardy &#91;or greedy&#93; to speak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Soothly there is a still man, not having wit of speech; and there is a still man, knowing the season of covenable time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A wise man shall be still till to the right time; but a jolly man &#91;or the reckless, or wild&#93; and an unprudent man shall not keep time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that useth many words, hurteth his soul; and he that taketh power to himself unjustly &#91;or wrong-fully&#93;, shall be hated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> There is going forth in evils to a man unlearned &#91;or undisciplined&#93;; and there is finding into impairing &#91;or into harm&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There is a gift, which is not profit-able; and there is a gift, whose yielding is double.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> There is making less for glory; and there is a man, which shall raise the head from meekness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There is a man, that again-buyeth many sins &#91;or things&#93; for little price, and restoreth those &#91;or them&#93; in sevenfold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> A wise man in words maketh himself amiable &#91;or lovable&#93;; but the graces of fools shall be shed &#91;or poured&#93; out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The gift of an unwise man shall not be profitable to thee; for his eyes be sevenfold, that is, his intent is manyfold and diverse.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He shall give little things &#91;or Few things he shall give&#93;, and he shall upbraid many things; and the opening of his mouth is enflaming. Today a man lendeth, and tomorrow he asketh for &#91;it&#93;; and such a man is hateful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> A friend shall not be to a fool, and grace shall not be to his goods. For they that eat his bread, be of false tongue, that is, flatterers praising his follies;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> how often, and how many men shall scorn him? For he parteth &#91;or dealed&#93; not by even wit that, that was worthy to be had; in like manner and that, that was not worthy to be had.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The falling &#91;or sliding&#93; of a false tongue is as he that falleth in the pavement; so the falls of evil men shall come hastily.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> A man without grace is as a vain fable; and it shall be customable in the mouth of unlearned men. &#91;An unkind man as a vain fable; and it shall be often in the mouth of the undisciplined.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A parable, that is, a true sentence and great, shall be reproved when it cometh out of the mouth of a fool; for he saith not it &#91;or he saith it not&#93; in his time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> There is a man, that is forbidden to do sin, for poverty; and he shall not be pricked in his rest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> There is a man, that shall lose his soul for shame; and for the un-prudence of a person he shall lose it. Forsooth he shall lose himself for the taking or favouring of a person.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There is a man, that for shame promiseth to a friend; and he hath gotten him an enemy without cause.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Leasing is a wicked shame in a man; and it shall be customably &#91;or busily&#93; in the mouth of unlearned men &#91;or the undisciplined&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Better, that is, less evil, is a thief than the customableness of a man, a leasing-monger &#91;or a liar&#93;; forsooth both they shall inherit perdition.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The manners of men leasing-mongers &#91;or liars&#93; be without honour; and their shame is with them without ceasing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wise man in words shall bring forth himself; and a prudent man shall please great men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He that worketh his land, shall make high the heap of fruits; and he that worketh rightfulness &#91;or right-wiseness&#93;, shall be enhanced. Soothly he that pleaseth great men, shall escape wickedness.</span>
</div>
