<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In three things it is pleased to my spirit, which be approved before God and men; according &#91;or the accord&#93; of brethren, and love of neighbours, &#91;and&#93; a man and woman well con-senting to themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> My soul hated three species, and I am grieved greatly to the soul of them; a poor man proud, and a rich man that is a liar, and an eld &#91;or old&#93; man that is a fool and unwitting &#91;or doted&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> How shalt thou find in thine eld age those things, which thou gather-ed not in thy youth?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Doom of discretion is full fair in hoariness &#91;or How fair the doom in hoariness&#93;, either eld man, and to priests to know counsel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Wisdom is full fair to eld &#91;or old&#93; men, and glorious understanding, and counsel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The crown of eld &#91;or old&#93; men is in much knowing &#91;or wisdom&#93;; and the glory of them is the dread of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> I magnified nine things unsus-pected of the heart; and I shall say the tenth thing by tongue to men. A man which living is merry &#91;or joyed&#93; in sons, and seeing the destroying of his enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He is blessed &#91;or Blissful&#93; that dwelleth with a witting woman, and he that fell not &#91;or is not slidden&#93; by his tongue, and he that served not to men unworthy to himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He is blessed &#91;or Blissful&#93; that findeth a very friend, and he that telleth out rightfulness &#91;or rightwise-ness&#93; to an ear hearing &#91;or the hearing ear&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He is full great that findeth wisdom and knowing; but he is not above him that dreadeth God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The dread of God is the more good, and passeth all goods beforesaid.">a</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The dread of God hath set itself above all things. Blessed is the man to whom it is given to have the dread of God; to whom shall he be likened, that holdeth that dread?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The dread of God is the begin-ning of his love; forsooth the begin-ning of faith is to be fast-joined thereto &#91;or to be joined to him&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The sorrow of heart is each wound; and the wickedness of a woman is all &#91;or each&#93; malice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, as the sorrow of heart passeth each wound of body, so the malice of a woman passeth all the malice of a man.">b</a></sup>. A leech shall see each wound, and not the wound of heart; and all wicked-ness, and not the wickedness of a woman;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and each covering &#91;or all hid thing&#93;, that is, colouring of malice, and not the covering &#91;or hid thing&#93; of haters; and each &#91;or all&#93; vengeance, and not the vengeance of enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> None head is worse than &#91;or wickeder over&#93; the head of an adder dwelling in shadow; and none ire is above the ire of a woman &#91;or there is not wrath over the wrath of a woman&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It shall please more to dwell with a lion and a dragon, than to dwell with a wicked woman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The wickedness of a woman changeth her face; and she blinded her cheer as a bear doeth, and she shall show as a sackcloth in the midst of neighbours.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Her husband wailed; and his wicked wife heard, and sighed a little. &#91;The husband of her greatly wailed; and hearing, sighed a little.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> All malice is short on or little compared to the malice of a woman; the part &#91;or the lot&#93; of sinners, that is, the pain of hell, fall on her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> As a going-up full of gravel in the feet of an eld &#91;or old&#93; man, so is a woman that is a great jangler to a peaceable man &#91;or a tonguey woman to a quiet man&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Behold thou not the fairness of a woman, and covet thou not a woman for her fairness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The ire and unreverence of a woman to her husband is a great shame. If a woman hath the first dignity &#91;or mastery&#93;, either chief governail, she is contrary to her husband.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A low heart, and sorrowful face, and wound of death, is a wicked woman. Feeble hands and knees unbound, cometh from a woman that blesseth not her husband &#91;or a woman that maketh not blissful or blessful her husband&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The beginning of sin was made of a woman; and all we die by her &#91;or by her all we die&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Give thou not issue to thy water, yea, not a little issue; neither to a wicked woman freedom, &#91;or leave&#93;, of going forth &#91;or going out&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If she goeth not at thine hand, she shall shame thee in the sight of thine enemies. Cut her away from thy fleshes, lest ever&#91;more&#93; she mis-use thee.</span>
