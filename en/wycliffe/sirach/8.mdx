<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Chide &#91;or Strive&#93; thou not with a mighty man, lest thou fall into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Strive thou not with a rich man, lest peradventure he make play again-ward to thee &#91;or lest again-ward he set strife to thee&#93;. For why gold and silver hath lost many men; and it stretcheth forth till to the heart&#91;s&#93; of kings, and turneth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Chide &#91;or Strive&#93; thou not with a man, a jangler &#91;or a tonguey man&#93;, and lay thou not trees into his fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Commune thou not with an untaught man, lest he speak evil of thy kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Despise thou not a man turning away himself from sin, neither up-braid thou him &#91;nor put thou reproof to him&#93;; have thou mind, that all we be in corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Despise thou not a man in his eld age; for why some of us men wax eld &#91;or old&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Do not thou make joy of thine enemy being dead, witting that all we die, and will not to come into joy of our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Despise thou not the telling of wise priests, and be thou conversant in the proverbs of them &#91;or in the proverbs of them altogether dwell thou&#93;; for of them thou shalt learn wisdom, and teaching of understand-ing, and to serve without complaint to great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The telling of elder men pass not by thee &#91;or Pass not beside thee the telling of elders&#93;; for they have learned of their fathers. For of them thou shalt learn understanding; and in the time of need thou shalt give &#91;an&#93; answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Kindle thou not the coals of sinners, and reprove them; and be thou not burnt with the flame of &#91;the&#93; fire of their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Stand thou not against the face of a man full of despising &#91;or the face of the strifeful&#93;; lest he sit as an espyer to thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not thou lend to a man stronger than thou; that if thou hast lent, have thou it as lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Promise thou not above &#91;or over&#93; thy power or virtue; that if thou hast promised, bethink thou as yielding, for thou art holden to do thy might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Deem thou not against a judge; for he deemeth after that, that is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Go thou not in the way with an hardy man, lest peradventure he aggregate his evils in thee; for he goeth after his will, and thou shalt perish together with him through his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Make thou not chiding &#91;or jangling&#93; with a wrathful man, and go thou not into desert with an hardy man; for why blood, that is, shedding out of innocent blood, is as nought before him, and where none help is &#91;or and where is not help&#93;, he shall hurtle thee down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Have thou not counsel with fools; for they may not love, but those things that please them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Make thou not a counsel before a stranger; for thou knowest not, what he shall bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Make not thine heart known to each man &#91;or To all men thine heart open thou not&#93;, but only to a very friend, and proved; lest peradventure he bring to thee false grace, that is, feigned friendship, and despise &#91;or put reproof to&#93; thee.</span>
