<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Simon, the son of Onias, was a great priest, which in his life under-setted the house, and in his days strengthened the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Also the highness of the temple was builded of him, that is, repaired, the double building, and high walls of the temple. &#91;Also the height of the temple of him is founded, the double building, and the high walls of the temple.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In the days of him the pits of waters came forth; and as the sea, those &#91;or they&#93; were filled above measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Which Simon healed his folk, and delivered it from perdition. Which was mighty to alarge the city; &#91;That cured or cared for his folk, and delivered it from perdition. That had the mastery to make large the city;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> which got glory in the conversa-tion of folk; and alarged the entering of the house, and of the large compass about. &#91;that gat glory into the living of folk; and the in-coming of the house, and of the porch he made large.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As the day &#91;or the morrowtide&#93; star in the midst of a cloud, and as a full moon shineth in his days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and as the sun shining, so he shined in the temple of God; as a rainbow shining among the clouds of glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and as a flower of roses in the days of ver or spring, and as lilies, that be in the passing &#91;or goings&#93; of water, and as incense smelling in the days of summer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> as fire shining, and incense burn-ing in fire; as a firm vessel of gold, adorned with each &#91;or all manner of&#93; precious stone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> as an olive tree springing forth &#91;or burgeoning&#93;, and a cypress tree raising itself on high &#91;or into heights;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> while he took the stole of glory, and was clothed in the perfection of virtue. In the ascending &#91;or going up&#93; of the holy altar, the clothing &#91;or amice&#93; of holiness gave glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Forsooth in taking parts of the hands of priests, that is, the tithe of tithes, which the priests took of the people, and he stood beside the altar. The crown of brethren, as a planting of &#91;a&#93; cedar in the hill Lebanon, was about him; so they stood about him as boughs of palm trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and all the sons of Aaron stood in their glory. Soothly the offering of the Lord was in the hands of them, before all the synagogue &#91;or all the gather-ing&#93; of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and he used full ending on the altar, to alarge &#91;or make large&#93; the offering of the high King.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he dressed &#91;or straightened or stretched out&#93; his hand in moist &#91;or liquid&#93; sacrifice; and sacrificed in the blood of grape. He shedded &#91;or poured&#93; out in the foundament of the altar, the odour of God to the high Prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the sons of Aaron cried aloud &#91;or cried out&#93;; they sounded in trumps beaten out with hammers, and made a great voice heard into mind before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then all the people hasted together, and fell down on the face on the earth, for to worship their Lord God &#91;or the Lord their God&#93;, and to give prayers to Almighty God on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And men singing in their voices alarged, that is, praised God largely; and a sound full of sweetness was made in the great house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the people prayed the high Lord in prayer, till that the honour of the Lord was done perfectly, and they performed their gift, or his service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then Simon came down, and raised &#91;or put out&#93; his hands into all the congregation of the sons of Israel, to give glory to God by his lips, and to have glory in the name of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he rehearsed his prayer, willing to show the virtue of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he prayed more the Lord of all, that made great things in each land &#91;or in all the earth&#93;; which increased our days from the womb of our mother, and did with us by &#91;or after&#93; his mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Give he gladness of heart to us, and that peace be made in Israel by everlasting days; &#91;Give he to us in-ward joy of heart, and to be made peace in our days in Israel by ever-lasting days;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> that Israel believe, that God’s mercy is with us, that he deliver them in their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> My soul hateth two folks; but the third is not a folk, whom I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They that sit in the hill of Samaria, and the Philistines, and the fond &#91;or foolish&#93; people, that dwelleth in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Jesus, the son of Sirach, a man of Jerusalem, wrote in this book the teaching &#91;or doctrine&#93; of wisdom, and of knowing; and he renewed wisdom of his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He is blessed, that dwelleth &#91;or abideth&#93; in these goods; he that setteth &#91;or putteth&#93; those &#91;things&#93; in his heart, shall ever&#91;more&#93; be wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For if he doeth these things, he shall be mighty to all things; for why the light of God is the step of him.</span>
