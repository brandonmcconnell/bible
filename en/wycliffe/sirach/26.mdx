<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The husband of a good woman is blessed; for why the number of their years is double. &#91;Blessed is the man of a good woman; the number forsooth of the years of him double.&#93;</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A good woman is a good partner; in the good part of them that dread God, she shall be given to a man for &#91;his&#93; good deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Forsooth if the heart of a rich man and of a poor man is good; in all time their cheer is glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Mine heart dreaded of three things, and my face dreaded &#91;or was afeared&#93; in the fourth thing. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The sorrow of heart, and mourning, is a jealous woman. In a jealous woman is beating of tongue, and she communeth with all men &#91;or In a jealous woman scourge of tongue, to all communing&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> As a yoke of oxes &#91;or oxen&#93; which is moved, so and a wicked woman; he that holdeth her, is as he that taketh &#91;or caught&#93; a scorpion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A drunken woman is great ire &#91;or wrath&#93;, and despising &#91;or strife&#93;; and her filth&#91;hood&#93; shall not be covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The fornication of a woman is in the raising of her eyes; and &#91;she&#93; shall be known in the eyelids of her.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Be thou ware of all unreverence of her eyes; and wonder thou not, if she despiseth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As a way-goer thirsting shall open the mouth at a well, and shall drink of each water next; and the foresaid daughter shall sit against each pale, and shall open the arrow case against each arrow &#91;or against all arrows shall open the quiver&#93;, till she fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The grace of a busy woman shall delight her husband &#91;or her man&#93;; and shall make fat his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The knowing of her is the gift of God. A wise woman and a still is not &#91;the&#93; exchanging of a learned &#91;or the taught&#93; soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Grace &#91;up&#93; on grace is an holy woman, and shamefast. Forsooth all weighing is not worth a continent soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> As the sun rising in the world in the highest things of God, so the fairness of a good woman is into the ornament, or the adornment &#91;or adorning&#93; of her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> A lantern shining &#91;up&#93; on an holy candlestick, and the fairness of a face on steadfast &#91;or stable&#93; age, that is, as such a lantern lighteneth the church, and such a face maketh fair ripe age, so a good woman maketh fair her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Golden pillars on silvern founda-ments, and steadfast feet on the soles of a steadfast &#91;or stable&#93; woman. Ever-lasting foundaments on a firm stone, and the behests &#91;or commandments&#93; of God in the heart of an holy woman.</span>
