<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Love thou not jealously the woman of thy bosom &#91;or Be thou not jealous to the woman of thy bosom&#93;; lest she show on thee the malice of evil doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Give thou not to a woman the power of thy soul; lest she enter in thy virtue, and thou be shamed &#91;or confounded&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Behold thou not a woman of many wills, that coveteth now this man, now that man; lest perad-venture thou fall into the snares of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Be thou not customable with a danceress, &#91;or a leaperess, or tumbler&#93;, neither hire thou her; lest perad-venture thou perish in the speedy work of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Behold thou not a virgin &#91;or a maiden&#93;; lest peradventure thou be caused to stumble in the fairness of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Give thou not thy soul to whores in anything; lest thou lose thee, and thy soul, and thine heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Do not thou behold about in the lanes &#91;or ways&#93; of the city; neither err thou in the large streets thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Turn away thy face from a woman well arrayed; and behold thou not about the fairness of another or another man’s wife. Many men have perished for the fairness of a woman; and thereby covetousness &#91;or lust&#93; burneth on high as fire or concupiscence burneth out as fire. Each woman which is an whore, either customable to fornication, shall be defouled as a fen, or a turd, in the way, &#91;or Each woman that is lech-erous, as a thost, or dung, in the way shall be trodden&#93;. Many men won-dering on the fairness of an alien woman were made reprovable, for why the speech of her burneth on high as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Sit thou not in any manner with an alien woman, neither rest thou with her on a bed &#91;nor lie thou with her upon the arm&#93;; and jangle thou not with her in wine, lest per-adventure thine heart bow into her, and thou fall into perdition by thy blood &#91;or by thy blood thou slide into perdition&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Forsake thou not an eld &#91;or old&#93; friend; for a new friend shall not be like him. New wine is like a new friend; it shall wax eld &#91;or old&#93;, and thou shalt drink it with sweetness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Covet &#91;or Love&#93; thou not the glory and riches of a sinner; for thou knowest not, what destroying of him shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The wrong of unjust &#91;or unright-wise&#93; men please not thee, and know thou that a wicked man &#91;or the unpious&#93; shall not please till to hells &#91;or hell&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Be thou far from a man that hath power to slay, that is, from a cruel tyrant, and thou shalt not have suspicion of the dread of death; and if thou nighest to him, do not thou do any trespass &#91;or anything do amiss&#93;, lest peradventure he take away thy life. Know thou the communing of death; for thou shalt enter into the midst of snares, and thou shalt go on the arms of them that sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> By thy virtue, keep thee from thy neighbour that may speak against thee to a tyrant; and treat thou with wise men and prudent men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Just &#91;or Rightwise&#93; men be guests, or meat-frères, &#91;or meat-fellows&#93;, to thee; and thy glorying be in the dread of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the thought of God be to thee in wit, that is, apply thy wit to think on God; and all thy telling be in the behests of the Highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Works shall be praised in the hand of craftsmen, and the prince of the people in the wisdom of his word; forsooth in the wit of elder men &#91;or elders&#93; a word shall be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A man, a jangler &#91;or a tonguey man&#93;, is dreadful in his city; and a fool-hardy man in his word shall be hateful.</span>
