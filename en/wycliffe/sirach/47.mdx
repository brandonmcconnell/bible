<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> After these things Nathan, the prophet, rose, in the days of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And as inner fatness parted &#91;or tallow severed&#93; from the flesh, so David from the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He played with lions, as with lambs; he did in like manner with bears, as with lambs of sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Whether in his youth he killed &#91;or slew&#93; not a giant, and took away shame &#91;or reproof&#93; from the folk? In raising the hand in a stone of a sling, he casted down the full out joying of Goliath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> where he called to help &#91;or inwardly called&#93; the Lord Almighty; and he gave in his right hand to do away a strong man in battle, and to enhance the horn of his folk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So he glorified him, that is, made him to be praised, in ten thousand&#91;s&#93;, and he praised him in the blessings of the Lord, in offering to him the crown &#91;or a crown&#93; of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For he all-brake enemies on each side, and drew out &#91;or destroyed&#93; by the root &#91;the&#93; Philistines contrary &#91;or contrarious&#93;, unto this day; he all-brake the horn of them unto without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> David in each work gave ac-knowledging to holy God, and high in the word of glory, &#91;or In all work he gave acknowledging unto the Holy, and to the High in the word of glory&#93;. Of all his heart he praised God, and he loved the Lord that made him, and gave to him power &#91;or might&#93; against enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he made singers to stand against the altar; and he made sweet motets in the sound of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he gave fairness in hal-lowings, and he adorned times, or solemnities, unto the ending of life; that they should praise the holy name of the Lord, and make large early the holiness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="All modern translations have “The Lord” here.">a</a></sup>purged the sins of him, and enhanced his horn without end; and he gave to him the testament of kings, and the seat &#91;or a seat&#93; of glory in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> After him rose a witting son; and for him he, that is, God, casted down all the power &#91;or might&#93; of their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Solomon reigned in the days of peace, to whom God made subject all &#91;the&#93; enemies, that he should make an house in the name of God, and make ready holiness without end &#91;or into evermore&#93;, as he was learned &#91;or taught&#93; in his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he was &#91;full-&#93;filled with wisdom as a flood is filled; and his soul uncovered the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And thou, Solomon, filledest dark figurative speeches in likenesses &#91;or in comparisons dark proverbs&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and thy name was published to isles afar, and thou were loved in thy peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Lands, that is, men dwelling in those lands, wondered in songs, and in proverbs, and in likenesses &#91;or comparisons&#93;, and interpretings, either expositions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and in the name of the Lord, to whom the surname is God of Israel &#91;or to whom is the surname God of Israel&#93;. Thou gatheredest together gold as latten, and thou filledest silver as lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And thou bowedest thy thighs to women; thou haddest power in thy body to fill the lust of thy body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Thou hast given a wem in thy glory, and madest unholy &#91;or cursedest&#93; thy seed, to bring in wrathfulness to thy children, and thy folly in other men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> that thou shouldest make the realm parted into twain &#91;or the empire parted on two&#93;, and of Ephraim to command an hard commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But God shall not forsake his mercy, and shall not destroy, neither do away his works, neither he shall lose from &#91;the&#93; generation the sons’ sons of his chosen king David; and he shall not destroy the seed of him that loveth the Lord. Forsooth God gave remnant to Jacob, and to David of that generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Solomon had an end with his fathers. And he left after him of his seed Rehoboam, the folly of the folk, and made less &#91;or diminished&#93; from prudence; which Rehoboam turned away the folk by his counsel. And Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, that made Israel to do sin, and gave to Ephraim way to do sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and full many sins of them were plenteous, for they turned them away greatly from their land, that is, sins great and horrible made them to be prisoners far from their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the lineage of Ephraim sought all wickednesses, till defence came to them; and &#91;he&#93; delivered them from all sins.</span>
