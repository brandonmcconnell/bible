<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> They have set thee a governor, do not thou be enhanced; be thou among them as one of them. Have thou care of them, and so behold thou; and when all thy care is filled, sit thou to meat &#91;or rest&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> That thou be glad for them, and take the ornament of grace; and get crown, and dignity of congregation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Speak thou, the greater man in birth; for why the word of him that loveth knowing become thee first; and hinder thou not music.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Where hearing is not, shed &#91;or pour&#93; thou not out a word; and do not thou be enhanced uncovenably in thy wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> A gem of carbuncle in the ornament of gold; and comparison of musics in the feast of wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As in the making &#91;or forging&#93; of gold is a signet of smaragdus, so the number of musics is in merry and measurable &#91;or temperate&#93; wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Hear thou &#91;being&#93; still, and good grace shall come to thee for rever-ence. Young man, speak thou scarce-ly in thy cause, when need is. If thou art asked twice, the head have thine answer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> In many things be thou as unknowing, and hear thou &#91;being&#93; still altogether, and asking &#91;or seek-ing&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And presume thou not to speak in the midst of great men; and where eld &#91;or old&#93; men be, speak thou not much.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Lightning shall go before hail, and grace shall go before shamefast-ness, and good grace shall come &#91;or fall&#93; to thee for reverence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And in the hour of rising, trifle thee not, that is, make thee no tarrying in arraying, either trifling of hairs, as women do; forsooth run thou before first into thine house, and there call thou thee to answer, and there play thou.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And do thy conceits &#91;or con-ceivings&#93;, that is, perform thy good purpose conceived there, and not in sins, and in a proud word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> On all these things, bless thou the Lord, that made thee, and filling thee greatly of all his goods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He that dreadeth God, shall take his teaching, &#91;or Who dreadeth God, shall take the doctrine of him&#93;; and they that wake to him, shall find blessing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He that seeketh the law, shall be filled thereof; and he that doeth traitorously, shall be caused to stumble therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They that dread God, shall find just doom; and shall kindle rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; as light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> A sinful man, that is, obstinate in sin, shall eschew blaming &#91;or shun correction&#93;; and after his will, he shall find comparison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A man of counsel shall not lose &#91;or destroy&#93; understanding; a man alien and proud shall not dread dreading. Yea, after that he hath done with that dread without counsel, and he shall be reproved by his pursuings &#91;or followings&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Son, do thou nothing without counsel; and after the deed thou shalt not repent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Go thou not in the way of falling, and offend thou not against stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Betake thou not thee to a tra-vailous way, lest thou set cause of stumbling to thy soul;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and be thou ware of thy sons, and perceive thou &#91;or take heed&#93; of thy menials.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In all thy work believe thou by faith of thy soul; for why this is the keeping of commandments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that believeth to God, taketh heed to the commandments; and he that trusteth in him, shall not be made less, either deceived.</span>
</div>
