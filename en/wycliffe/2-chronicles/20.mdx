<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 9:3; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:3; 1ST SAMUEL 7:6"> Forsooth Jehoshaphat was afeared by dread, and gave himself all for to pray the Lord, and preached fasting to all Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 60:10–12; PSALMS 69:35"> And Judah was gathered together for to pray the Lord, and also all men came from their cities for to beseech him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND KI 19:15–19; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:12–13; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:31"> And when Jehoshaphat had stood in the midst of the company of Judah and of Jerusalem, in the house of the Lord, before the new large place of the temple,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:39; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:11–12; DANIEL 4:17"> he said, Lord God of our fathers, thou art God in heaven, and thou art Lord of all realms of folks; strength and power be in thine hand, and none may against-stand thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:8; JAMES 2:23; PSALMS 44:2"> Whether not thou, our God, hast slain all the dwellers of this land before thy people Israel, and hast given it to the seed of Abraham, thy friend, with-out end?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 6:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:4"> And they dwelled therein, and builded therein a saintuary to thy name, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 6:28–30; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:20; 1ST KINGS 8:33"> If evils come &#91;up&#93; on us, the sword of doom, pestilence, or hunger, we shall stand before this house without end in thy sight, in which house thy name is called, and we shall cry to thee in our tribulations; and thou shalt hear us, and shalt make us safe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:19; DEUTERONOMY 2:4–5; DEUTERONOMY 2:9"> Now therefore lo! the sons of Ammon, and of Moab, and the hill of Seir, by whom thou grantedest not to the sons of Israel for to pass through their lands, when they went out of Egypt, but they bowed away from them, and killed not them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 83:3–12; JUDGES 11:23–24; JEREMIAH 18:20"> but they do on the contrary, and endeavour to cast us out of the possession, which thou, our God, hast given to us;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 121:1–2; PSALMS 25:15; PSALMS 141:8"> therefore whether thou, Lord, shalt not deem them? Truly in us is not so great strength, that we may against-stand this multitude, that falleth in upon us; but since we know not what we owe to do, we, the residue, have this only, that we dress our eyes to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZRA 10:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:5; DEUTERONOMY 29:10"> And all Judah stood before the Lord, with their little children, and their wives, and with their free children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:31"> And Jahaziel, the son of Zechariah, the son of Benaiah, the son of Jeiel, the son of Mattaniah, was a deacon &#91;or Levite&#93;, and of the sons of Asaph, upon whom the Spirit of the Lord was made in the midst of the company,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 32:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 31:6; EXODUS 14:13–14"> and he said, All Judah, and ye that dwell in Jerusalem, and thou, king Jehoshaphat, perceive ye, or taketh heed, The Lord saith these things to you, Do not ye dread, neither be ye afeared of this multitude, for it is not your battle, but God’s battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Tomorrow ye shall go up against them; for they shall go up by the side of the hill, called Ziz by name, and ye shall find them in the height of the strand &#91;or stream&#93;, that is against the wilderness of Jeruel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 14:13–14; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:2; ISAIAH 41:10"> For it shall not be ye, that shall fight; but only stand ye trustily, and ye shall see the help of the Lord upon you. O! Judah and Jerusalem, do not ye dread, neither be ye afeared; tomorrow ye shall go out against them, and the Lord shall be with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 4:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:3; GENESIS 24:26"> Therefore Jehoshaphat, and Judah, and all the dwellers of Jerusalem, fell lowly upon the earth before the Lord, and worshipped him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 15:16–22; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:42; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:5"> And the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93; of the sons of Kohath, and of the sons of Korah, praised the Lord God of Israel with &#91;a&#93; great voice on high.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; JOHN 14:1; JOHN 11:40"> And when upon the morrow they had risen early, they went out by the desert of Tekoa; and when they had gone forth, Jehoshaphat stood in the midst of them, and said, Judah, and all the dwellers of Jerusalem, hear ye me; believe ye in the Lord your God, and ye shall be secure; believe ye to his prophets, and all prosperities shall come to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 107:1; PSALMS 106:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 5:13"> And he gave counsel to the people, and he ordained the singers of the Lord, that they should praise him in their companies, and that they should go before the host, and say with according voice, Acknowledge ye to the Lord, for he is good; for his mercy is without end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 14:20; ISAIAH 19:2; EZEKIEL 38:21"> And when they began to sing praisings, the Lord turned the ambush-ments of them against themselves, that is, of the sons of Ammon, and of Moab, and of the hill of Seir, which went out to fight against Judah; and they were slain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JUDGES 7:22; 1ST SAMUEL 14:20; EZEKIEL 35:2–3"> For why the sons of Ammon and of Moab rose together against the dwellers of the hill of Seir, to slay, and to do away them; and when they had done this thing in work, they were then also turned against them-selves, and they fell down together by wounds, each slaying other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:36; PSALMS 110:6; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:22"> Certainly when Judah was come to the den, that beholdeth, or is over against, the wilderness, he saw afar all the large country full of dead bodies, and that none was left, that might escape death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 30:19–20; 2ND KI 7:9–16; NUMBERS 31:15"> Therefore Jehoshaphat came, and all the people with him, to draw away the spoils of &#91;the&#93; dead men, and they found among the dead bodies diverse appurtenance of household, and clothes, and full precious vessels; and they ravished, or took those things away, in diverse manners, so that they might not bear all things, neither they might take away the spoils by three days, for the greatness of &#91;the&#93; prey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 17:15; GENESIS 32:20; 2ND CHRONICLES 5:9"> Soothly in the fourth day they were gathered together in the valley of Blessing; for-thy that they blessed the Lord there, they called that place the valley of Blessing, unto this present day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:43; HEBREWS 6:20; PSALMS 30:1"> And each man of Judah turned again, and the dwellers of Jerusalem, and Jehoshaphat went before them, into Jerusalem with great gladness; for the Lord God had given to them joy of their enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:2–3; PSALMS 57:8; PSALMS 92:3"> And they entered into Jerusalem with psalteries, and harps, and trumps, into the house of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 17:10; 2ND KI 7:6; EXODUS 23:27"> Forsooth the dread of the Lord felled &#91;or fell&#93; on all the realms of lands, when they had heard, that the Lord had fought against the enemies of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:15; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:6–7; 2ND SAMUEL 7:1"> And the realm of Jehoshaphat rested from war; and the Lord gave peace to him all about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:41–44"> And Jehoshaphat reigned upon Judah; and he was of five and thirty years, when he began to reign; and he reigned five and twenty years in Jerusalem; and the name of his mother was Azubah, the daughter of Shilhi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 14:2–5; 1ST KINGS 15:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:11–13"> And he went in the way of Asa his father, and bowed not from it, and he did whatever things were pleasant before the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 17:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:3; 2ND CHRONICLES 12:14"> Nevertheless he did not away the high places; and yet the people had not dressed their heart to the Lord God of their fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST KINGS 16:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:2; 1ST KINGS 16:7"> Forsooth the residue of the former and the last deeds of Jehoshaphat be written in the book of Jehu, the son of Hanani, which he ordained in the book of &#91;the&#93; kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:48–49; 2ND KI 1:2–16"> After these things Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, made friendships with Ahaziah, king of Israel, whose works were full evil or most evil;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1ST KINGS 10:22; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:21; 1ST KINGS 9:26"> and he was partner to him, and they made ships, which should go into Tarshish; and they made one ship to go into Eziongaber.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:21"> And Eliezer, the son of Dodavah, of Mareshah, prophesied to Jehosh-aphat, and said, For thou hast had bond of peace with Ahaziah, the Lord hath destroyed thy works; and the ships be broken, and &#91;they&#93; might not go into Tarshish.</span>
</div>
