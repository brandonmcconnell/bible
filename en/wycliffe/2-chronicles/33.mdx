<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND KI 21:1–18; 1ST CHRONICLES 3:13; ISAIAH 3:12"> Manasseh was of twelve years, when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem five and fifty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 28:3; DEUTERONOMY 18:9; 2ND KI 17:11"> And he did evil before the Lord after the abominations of heathen men, whom the Lord destroyed before the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:21; DEUTERONOMY 17:3; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:1"> And he turned, and restored the high places, which Hezekiah, his father, had destroyed. And he builded altars to Baalim, and made woods, and worshipped all the knighthood of heaven, and praised it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 7:16; 1ST KINGS 8:29; DEUTERONOMY 12:11"> And he builded altars in the house of the Lord, of which the Lord had said, My name shall be in Jeru-salem without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 4:9; JEREMIAH 32:34–35; EZEKIEL 8:7–18"> Soothly he builded those altars to all the knighthood of heaven in the two large places of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:21; LEVITICUS 20:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 28:3"> And he made his sons to pass through the fire in the valley of Ben-hinnom; he kept dreams; he pursued &#91;or followed&#93; false divining by chit-tering of birds; and he served witch-crafts; and he had with him astrono-mers and enchanters, either tregetours, that deceived men’s wits, and he wrought many evils before the Lord to stir him to wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 33:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:16; 1ST KINGS 11:13"> And he set a graven and a molten sign in the house of the Lord, of which house God spake to David, and to Solomon, his son, and said, I shall set my name without end in this house, and in Jerusalem, which I chose of all the lineages of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:10; DEUTERONOMY 5:31–1; LEVITICUS 8:36"> and I shall not make the foot of Israel to move from the land which I gave to their fathers, so only that they take heed to do those things that I have commanded to them, and all the law, and ceremonies, and dooms, by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND KI 24:3–4; PROVERBS 29:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:2"> But Manasseh deceived the men of Judah, and the dwellers of Jerusalem, so that they did evil, more than all heathen men, which the Lord had destroyed from the face of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:15–16; JEREMIAH 44:4–5; ZECHARIAH 1:4"> And the Lord spake to him, and to his people; and they would not take heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:36; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:6; NEHEMIAH 9:32"> Therefore the Lord brought upon them the princes of the host of the king of Assyrians; and they took Manasseh, and bound him with chains, and stocks, and led him into Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 10:3; PSALMS 50:15; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:26"> And after that he was anguished, he prayed the Lord his God, and did penance greatly before the God of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 5:20; DANIEL 4:25; MATTHEW 7:7–8"> And he prayed God, and beseech-ed him intently; and God heard his prayer, and brought him again into Jerusalem into his realm; and then Manasseh knew, that the Lord himself is God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 27:3; 1ST KINGS 1:33; NEHEMIAH 3:3"> After these things he builded the wall without or outside the city of David, at the west side of Gihon, in the valley, from the entering of the gate of fishes, by compass unto Ophel; and he raised it up greatly; and he ordained princes of the host in all the strong cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 33:3–7; HOSEA 14:1–3; 2ND KI 21:7"> And he did away alien gods and simulacra from the house of the Lord; and he did away the altars, which he had made in the hill of the house of the Lord, and in Jerusalem, and he casted them away all without the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 14:4; 1ST KINGS 18:30; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:9"> Certainly he restored the altar of the Lord, and offered thereon slain sacrifices, and peaceable sacrifices, and praising; and he commanded Judah to serve the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 32:12; 1ST KINGS 22:43; 2ND KI 15:4"> Nevertheless the people offered yet in high places to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 33:19; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:12–13; AMOS 7:12"> Forsooth the residue of &#91;the&#93; deeds of Manasseh, and his beseeching to his Lord God, and the words of &#91;the&#93; prophets, that spake to him in the name of the Lord God of Israel, be contained in the words of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:11; 1ST JOHN 1:9; DANIEL 5:22"> And his prayer, and the hearing that the Lord heard him, and all his sins, and all his despising, and also the places in which he builded high things, and made maumet woods and images, before that he did penance, these be written in the book of Hozai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:33; 2ND KI 21:18–25"> And Manasseh slept with his fathers, and they buried him in his house; and Amon, his son, reigned for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JAMES 4:13–15; LUKE 12:19–20; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:1"> Amon was of two and twenty years, when he began to reign; and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND KI 21:20; EZEKIEL 20:18; 2ND KI 21:1–11"> And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, as Manasseh, his father, had done; and he offered, and served to all the idols, which Manasseh had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 33:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:19; JEREMIAH 7:26"> And he reverenced not the face of the Lord, as Manasseh, his father, reverenced; and he did much greater trespasses than his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 11:22; 2ND CHRONICLES 25:27–28; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:25–26"> And when his servants had sworn together against him, they killed him in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:1"> Soothly the residue multitude of the people, after that they had slain them that had slain Amon, ordained Josiah, his son, king for him.</span>
