<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:10–28"> Forsooth when twenty years were &#91;ful&#93; filled, after that Solomon had builded the house of the Lord, and his own house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:11–18"> he builded the cities, which Hiram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Also known as Huram.">a</a></sup>had given to Solomon; and he made the sons of Israel to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:21; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:3; 2ND SAMUEL 8:3"> Also he went into Hamath of Zobah, and got it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:17–19"> And he builded Palmyra in desert, and he builded other full strong cities in Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 7:24; JOSHUA 16:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:7"> And he builded the higher Beth-horon and the lower Bethhoron, walled cities, having gates, and locks, and bars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 8:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 1:14"> also he builded Baalath, and all the strong cities that were of Solomon; and all the cities of carts, and the cities of knights, king Solomon builded, and disposed all things, whichever he would, in Jerusalem, and in Lebanon, and in all the land of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 15:18–21; DEUTERONOMY 7:1; 1ST KINGS 9:20–22"> And Solomon made subject into tributaries till into this day all the people that was left of Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites, that were not of the generations of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 16:10; 1ST KINGS 9:21; 1ST KINGS 4:6"> and of the sons of them, and of the after-comers of them, which the sons of Israel had not slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 8:11–12; EXODUS 19:5–6; GALATIANS 4:31"> For of the sons of Israel Solomon set not, that they should serve the works of the king; for they were men warriors, and the first, or chief, dukes, and princes of his chariots, and of his knights;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:23; 1ST KINGS 5:16; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:18"> and all the princes of the host of king Solomon were two hundred and fifty, that taught, or ruled, the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:1; 1ST KINGS 7:8; 1ST KINGS 9:24"> And Solomon translated or brought over the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David into an house, that he had builded to her; for the king said, My wife shall not dwell in the house of David, king of Israel, for it is hallowed, for the ark of the Lord entered into that house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 4:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:8; JOHN 10:23"> Then Solomon offered burnt sacrifices to the Lord on the altar of the Lord, which he had builded before the porch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:16; EXODUS 29:38–42; EZEKIEL 46:3–15"> that by all days offering should be offered in it, by the commandment of Moses, in sabbaths, and in calends, and in feast days, thrice by the year, that is, in the solemnity of therf loaves, and in the solemnity of weeks, and in the solemnity of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 9:17; EZRA 6:18; NEHEMIAH 12:36"> And he ordained by the ordinance of David, his father, the offices of priests in their services, and the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93; in their order, that they should praise and minister before &#91;the&#93; priests by the custom of each day; and he ordained&#91;the&#93; porters in their partings by gate and gate. For David, the man of God, had commanded so;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 30:12; 1ST CHRONICLES 9:29; 1ST KINGS 7:51"> and both priests and deacons &#91;or Levites&#93; passed not from the command-ments of the king of all things which he had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 5:18; 1ST KINGS 6:7"> And Solomon had all &#91;the&#93; costs, or dispenses, or expenses, made ready in the keepings of &#91;the&#93; treasuries, from that day in which he founded the house of the Lord, till into the day in which he performed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 14:22; DEUTERONOMY 2:8; NUMBERS 33:35"> Then Solomon went into Ezion-geber, and into Elath, at the brink of the Red Sea, which is in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:13; ECCLESIASTES 2:8"> Therefore Hiram sent to him, by the hands of his servants, ships, and shipmen knowing of the sea, and they went with the servants of Solomon into Ophir, and they took from thence four hundred and fifty talents of gold, and they brought it to king Solomon.</span>
