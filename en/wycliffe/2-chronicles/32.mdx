<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND KI 18:13–37; 2ND KI 17:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:1–2"> After which things and such truth, Sennacherib, the king of Assyrians, came and entered into Judah; and he besieged strong cities, and would take those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 9:53; LUKE 9:51; 2ND KI 12:17"> And when Hezekiah had heard this thing, that is, that Sennacherib had come, and that all the fierceness of his battle was turned against Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:6; PROVERBS 20:18; PROVERBS 15:22"> he took counsel with &#91;the&#93; princes and with most strong men, that they should stop the heads of wells, which were without the city; and when the sentence of all men deemed this profitable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 32:30; 2ND KI 18:9; 1ST KINGS 19:21"> he gathered together a full great multitude of men, and they stopped up all the wells, and the river, that flowed in the midst of the land; and said, Lest the kings of Assyrians come, and find abundance of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 25:23; 1ST KINGS 9:24; 2ND KI 25:4"> Also Hezekiah did wittingly, and he builded all the wall that was destroyed, and he builded towers on the wall, and another wall without-forth. And he repaired Millo in the city of David; and made armour or arms of all kind, and shields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:22; NEHEMIAH 8:16"> And he ordained princes of warriors in the host; and he called together all men in the street of the gate of the city, and spake to the hearts of them, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 6:16; ROMANS 8:31; EPHESIANS 6:10"> Do ye manly, and be ye comfort-ed; do not ye dread, neither be ye afeared of the king of Assyrians, nor of all the multitude that is with him; for many more be with us than with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:17; ISAIAH 41:10"> A fleshly arm is with him; and the Lord our God is with us, which is our helper, and shall fight for us. And the people was comforted with such words of Hezekiah, king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND KI 18:17; JOSHUA 10:31; ISAIAH 36:2"> And after that these things were done, Sennacherib, &#91;the king of Assyria&#93;, sent his servants to Jerusalem; for he himself, with all the host, besieged Lachish. He sent to Hezekiah, king of Judah, and to all the people that was in the city of Jerusalem, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND KI 18:19; ISAIAH 36:4"> Sennacherib, king of Assyrians, saith these things, In whom have ye trust, and sit besieged in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:43; PSALMS 11:1–3; ISAIAH 36:18"> Whether not Hezekiah deceiveth you, that ye betake you to death in hunger and thirst, and he affirmeth, that the Lord your God shall deliver you from the hand of the king of Assyrians?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 31:1; DEUTERONOMY 12:13–14; DEUTERONOMY 12:26–27"> Whether this is not Hezekiah, that destroyed high places, and altars of him, and commanded to Judah and Jerusalem, and said, Ye shall worship before one altar, and therein ye shall burn incense?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND KI 18:33–35; ISAIAH 44:8–10; DANIEL 4:30"> Whether ye know not what things I have done, and my fathers, to all the peoples of lands? Whether the gods of folks and of all lands might deliver their country from mine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:11–12; EXODUS 15:9–11; ISAIAH 42:8"> Who is, of all the gods of folks, which my fathers destroyed, that might deliver his people from mine hand, that also your God may deliver you from mine hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 5:2; DANIEL 3:15; ISAIAH 36:18"> Therefore Hezekiah deceive not you, neither scorn he you by vain counselling, neither believe ye to him; for if no god of all folks and countries might deliver his people from mine hand, and from the hand of my fathers, pursuingly &#91;or followingly&#93; neither your God shall be able to deliver you from this mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 73:9; JOHN 15:21; JOB 15:25–26"> But also his servants spake many other things against the Lord God, and against Hezekiah, his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:14; 2ND KI 19:12; ISAIAH 37:28–29"> Also he wrote epistles full of blas-phemy against the Lord God of Israel, and he spake against God, and said, As the gods of other folks might not deliver their people from mine hand, so and the God of Hezekiah may not deliver his people from mine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 17:10; 2ND KI 18:26–28; 1ST SAMUEL 17:26"> Furthermore, and with &#91;a&#93; great cry in the language of Jews, he sounded against the people, that sat on the walls of Jerusalem, to make them afeared, and to take the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND KI 19:18; ISAIAH 2:8; JEREMIAH 32:30"> And he spake against &#91;the&#93; God of Israel, as against the gods of the peoples of &#91;the&#93; earth, the works of men’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 91:14–15; ISAIAH 37:14–20; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:11"> Therefore Hezekiah, the king, and Isaiah, the prophet, the son of Amoz, prayed against this blasphemy, and cried &#91;out&#93; till into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:36–38; PSALMS 76:5; PSALMS 18:50"> And the Lord sent his angel, the which killed each strong man and warrior, and the prince of the host of the king of Assyrians; and he/Sen-nacherib turned again with shame to his land. And when he had entered into the house of his god, the sons, which went out of his womb, killed him there with sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 31:4–5; PSALMS 18:48–50; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:5"> And the Lord saved Hezekiah, and the dwellers of Jerusalem, from the hand of Sennacherib, king of Assyrians, and from the hand of all men; and he gave to them rest by compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 17:5; MATTHEW 2:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 1:1"> Also many men brought offerings and sacrifices to the Lord into Jeru-salem, and gifts to Hezekiah, king of Judah; which was enhanced after these things before all folks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 38:1–8; 2ND KI 20:1–11; ISAIAH 38:21–22"> In those days Hezekiah was sick unto the death, and he prayed the Lord; and he heard him, and gave to him a sign;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 24:18; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:16; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:31"> but he yielded not thankings to the Lord after the benefits which he had taken, for his heart was raised into pride; and wrath of the Lord was made against him, and against Judah, and against Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:18–19; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:27–28; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:12"> And he was meeked afterward, for-thy that his heart was raised; both he was meeked, and the dwellers of Jerusalem; and therefore the wrath of the Lord came not upon them in the days of Hezekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 27:25–31; 2ND CHRONICLES 1:12; PROVERBS 10:22"> And Hezekiah was rich, and full noble, and he gathered to himself full many treasures of silver, and of gold, and of precious stones, and of sweet smelling spices, and of armours of all kind, and of vessels of great price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:10; 1ST KINGS 4:26"> Also he builded large houses of wheat, &#91;and&#93; of wine, and of oil, and cratches of all beasts, and folds to sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:12; PROVERBS 10:22; 1ST SAMUEL 2:7"> and he builded six cities. And he had unnumberable flocks of sheep and of great beasts; for the Lord had given to him full much chattel &#91;or substance&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:33; ISAIAH 22:9–11; PSALMS 1:1–3"> That is Hezekiah, that stopped the higher well of the waters of Gihon, and he turned those &#91;or them&#93; away under the earth at the west side of the city of David; in all his works he did by prosperity, whatever thing he would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:2; PROVERBS 17:3; GENESIS 22:1"> Nevertheless in the message of the princes of Babylon, that were sent to him for to ask of the great wonder, that befelled on the land, God forsook him, that he were assayed, and that all things were known that were in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:1–22; 2ND KI 18:1–20; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:20–21"> Soothly the residue of &#91;the&#93; words of Hezekiah, and of his mercies, be written in the prophecy of Isaiah, the prophet, the son of Amoz, and in the book of kings of Judah and of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:7; 1ST KINGS 1:21; 2ND CHRONICLES 16:14"> And Hezekiah slept with his fathers, and they buried him above the sepulchres of the sons of David. And all Judah and all the dwellers of Jerusalem made solemn the services of his burying; and Manasseh, his son, reigned for him.</span>
