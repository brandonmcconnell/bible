<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:50; 2ND KI 8:16–17; 2ND CHRONICLES 12:16"> And Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers, and was buried with them in the city of David; and Jehoram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Also known as Joram.">a</a></sup>, his son, reigned for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 12:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 23:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:5"> And he had brethren, the sons of Jehoshaphat, Azariah, Jehiel, and Zech-ariah, and Azariah, and Michael, and Shephatiah; all these were the sons of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 11:23; DEUTERONOMY 21:15–17; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:5"> And their father gave to them many gifts of gold and of silver, and he gave them pensions, or rents, with full strong cities in Judah; but he gave the realm to Jehoram, for he was his first begotten son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 9:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 21:17; GENESIS 4:8"> And Jehoram rose up on the realm of his father; and when he had con-firmed himself in the realm, he slew all his brethren by sword, and also some of the princes of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND KI 8:17–22"> Jehoram was of two and thirty years, when he began to reign; and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 18:1; 1ST KINGS 12:28–30; 2ND KI 8:18"> And he went in the ways of the kings of Israel, as the house of Ahab had done, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:36; PSALMS 132:17–18; 2ND SAMUEL 7:12–17"> But the Lord would not destroy the house of David, for the covenant which he had made with David, and for he had promised to give to him a lantern, and to his sons, in all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 21:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:22–23; GENESIS 27:40"> In those days Edom rebelled, so that it was not subject to Judah, and it ordained a king to itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when Jehoram had passed forth with his princes, and all the multitude of knights, that was with him, he rose up by night, and smote Edom, that encompassed him, and all the dukes of his multitude of knights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:2; JEREMIAH 2:13; 2ND CHRONICLES 13:10"> Nevertheless Edom rebelled, that it was not under the lordship of Judah unto this day. In that time also Libnah went away, that it was not under the hand of him; for he had forsaken the Lord God of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:5; 1ST KINGS 11:7; LEVITICUS 17:7"> Furthermore he made high places in the cities of Judah, and made the dwellers of Jerusalem to do fornication, that is, idolatry, and Judah to break the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 14:2–5; 2ND CHRONICLES 17:3–4; 2ND KI 2:1"> And letters were brought to him from Elijah, the prophet, in which it was written, The Lord God of David, thy father, saith these things, For that thou hast not gone in the ways of Jehoshaphat, thy father, and in the ways of Asa, king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 21:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 21:11; 1ST KINGS 16:30–33"> but thou hast gone by the way of the kings of Israel, and thou hast made Judah and the dwellers of Jerusalem to do fornication, and thou hast pur-sued &#91;or following&#93; the fornication of the house of Ahab; furthermore and thou hast slain thy brethren in the house of thy father, that is, princes of the house of thy father, which were better than thou;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:21; EXODUS 20:5; HOSEA 5:11"> lo! the Lord shall smite thee with a great vengeance, and thy people, and thy sons, and thy wives, and all thy chattel &#91;or substance&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 21:18–19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:18; DEUTERONOMY 28:67"> and thou shalt be sick with the worst sorrow of thy womb, till that thine entrails go out little and little by each day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 17:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:11; 1ST KINGS 11:14"> Therefore the Lord raised up against Jehoram the spirit of Philistines, and of Arabians, that march with Ethiopians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 22:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 22:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 25:23"> and these went up into the land of Judah, and they wasted it, and they took away all the substance, that was found in the house of the king, furthermore and his sons, and his wives they took away; and no son was left to him, but Jehoahaz, that was his least or youngest son in birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 21:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:23; 2ND KI 9:29"> And over all these things the Lord smote him with uncurable sorrow of the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 16:14; JEREMIAH 34:5"> And when day came after day, and the spaces of time were turned about, the course of two years was fulfilled; and so he was wasted by long rot, so that he casted out also his own entrails, and so he wanted sorrow and life together, and he was dead in the worst sickness. And the people did not to him &#91;the&#93; service of dead men by the custom of burning, as it had done to his greaters, either ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:18; 2ND CHRONICLES 28:27; JEREMIAH 22:28"> He was of two and thirty years when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem, and he went not rightfully; and they buried him in the city of David, nevertheless not in the sepulchres of kings.</span>
