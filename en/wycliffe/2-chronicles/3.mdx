</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The wings of cherubims &#91;or cher-ubim&#93; were holden forth by twenty cubits, so that one wing had five cubits, and it touched the wall of the house; and the tother wing had five cubits, and it touched the wing of the other cherub.</span>

<div className="bp">
