</div>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:8–14; PSALMS 89:15; PSALMS 68:24–26"> Forsooth when all the company worshipped/And when all the company worshipped the Lord, &#91;the&#93; singers and they that held trumps were in their office, till the burnt sacrifice was filled.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

Therefore all the multitude offered with devout soul sacrifices, and praisings, and burnt sacrifices.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZRA 6:17; 1ST KINGS 3:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:21"> And this was the number of burnt sacrifices, which the multitude offered; seventy bulls, and an hundred rams, and two hundred lambs.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Also they hallowed to the Lord six hundred oxen, and three thousand sheep.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:5–10; EXODUS 29:13; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:37–42"> Therefore there were full many burnt sacrifices, and inner fatness of peaceable sacrifices, and the moist &#91;or liquor&#93; sacrifices of burnt sacrifices, and thereby the worship of the house of the Lord was &#91;ful&#93; filled.</span>
