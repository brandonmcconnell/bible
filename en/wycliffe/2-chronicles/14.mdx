<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:31; 1ST KINGS 2:10; 1ST KINGS 14:31"> And Abijah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; and Asa, his son, reigned for him. In whose days the land rested in peace ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST KINGS 15:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:20; 1ST KINGS 15:14"> And Asa did that, that was good and pleasant in the sight of his God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:5; EXODUS 34:13; 2ND KI 23:14"> and he destroyed the altars of strange worshipping, that is, of idolatry, and the high places, and brake alto-gether the images, and cutted down &#91;the&#93; maumet woods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 30:19; PSALMS 119:10; PSALMS 101:2–8"> and he commanded Judah to seek the Lord God of their fathers, and to do the law and all &#91;the&#93; commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 34:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:7"> And he took away from all the cities of Judah altars and temples of idols, and he reigned in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:15; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:9; JOB 34:29"> And he builded strong cities in Judah; for he was in rest, and no battles rose in his times, for the Lord gave him peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:6; JEREMIAH 29:12–14"> And Asa said to Judah, Build we &#91;up&#93; these cities, and encompass we them with walls, and strengthen we them with towers, and gates, and locks, as long as all things be restful from battle; for we have sought the Lord God of our fathers, and he hath given to us rest by compass. There-fore they builded, and there was no hindering in the building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 13:3; 2ND CHRONICLES 17:14–19; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:1"> And Asa had in his host three hundred thousand of men of Judah bearing shields and spears; and of Benjamin, he had two hundred thousand and fourscore thousand of shield-bearers and of archers; all these were full strong men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 16:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 12:2–3; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:8"> Forsooth Zerah of Ethiopia went out against them with his host of ten hundred thousand, and with three hundred chariots, and came unto Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 1:17; JOSHUA 19:4"> Certainly Asa went out against them, and they arrayed battle array in the valley of Zephathah, which is beside Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; PSALMS 22:5"> And Asa inwardly called the Lord God, and said, Lord, no diversity is with thee, whether thou help in few, either in many; our Lord God, help thou us, for we have trust in thee, and in thy name, and we came against this multitude; Lord, thou art our God, a man have not the mastery against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 13:15; PSALMS 136:17–18; DEUTERONOMY 28:7"> Therefore the Lord made afeared Ethiopians before Asa and Judah, and Ethiopians fled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 10:19; GENESIS 26:1; GENESIS 20:1"> and Asa and his people, that was with him, pursued them unto Gerar. And Ethiopians felled down to death, for they were all-broken by the Lord slaying, and by his host fighting. Then they took many spoils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 17:10; GENESIS 35:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:29"> and they smote all the cities about Gerar; for great dread had assailed all men. And they spoiled or rifled the cities, and bare away much prey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:30–47; NUMBERS 31:9; 1ST SAMUEL 30:20"> and also they destroyed the folds of sheep, and they took multitude without number of sheep and of camels, and they turned again into Jerusalem.</span>
