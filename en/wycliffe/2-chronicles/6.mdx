<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 18:8–11; LEVITICUS 16:2; EXODUS 20:21"> Then Solomon said, The Lord promised, that he would dwell in &#91;the&#93; darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 2:4–6; PSALMS 135:21; 1ST CHRONICLES 28:6"> and I have built an house to his name, that he should dwell therein without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:14; 2ND CHRONICLES 29:29; 1ST KINGS 8:55–61"> And Solomon turned his face, and blessed all the multitude of Israel; for all the company stood attentive;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 72:18–19; MATTHEW 24:35; PSALMS 68:32–35"> and he said, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, for he hath fulfilled in work that thing, that he spake to David, my father, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 20:24; 1ST KINGS 8:16; 1ST SAMUEL 13:13–14"> From the day in which I led my people out of the land of Egypt, I chose not a city of all the lineages of Israel, that an house should be builded therein to my name, neither I chose any other man, that he should be duke upon my people Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:4; PSALMS 132:13; 2ND CHRONICLES 12:13"> but I chose Jerusalem, that my name be therein, and I chose David, to ordain him upon my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:1; 1ST KINGS 5:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:7"> And when it was of the will of David, my father, to build an house to the name of the Lord God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; MARK 14:8; 1ST KINGS 8:18–21"> the Lord said to him, For this was thy will, to build an house to my name, soothly thou didest well, having such a will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:12–13; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:11–12"> but yet thou shalt not build an house to me; nevertheless the son, that shall go out of thy loins, he shall build an house to my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 28:5; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:11"> Therefore the Lord hath fulfilled his word, that he spake; and I rose up for David, my father, and I sat on the throne of Israel, as the Lord spake, and I have builded an house to the name of the Lord God of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 5:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 5:7; 1ST KINGS 8:21"> and I have put therein the ark, in which is the covenant of the Lord, which he covenanted with the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 63:4; ISAIAH 50:10; JOB 11:13"> Therefore Solomon stood before the altar of the Lord even against all the multitude of Israel, and stretched forth his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:54; NEHEMIAH 8:4; PSALMS 95:6"> For Solomon had made a brazen foundament, and had set &#91;or put&#93; it in the midst of the great house, and it had five cubits of length, and five cubits of breadth, and three cubits of height, and he stood there upon &#91;it&#93;; and from that time he kneeled against all the multitude of Israel, and he raised up his hands into heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 15:11; DEUTERONOMY 7:9; NEHEMIAH 1:5"> and said, Lord God of Israel, none is like thee; thou art God in heaven, and in earth, which keepest covenant and mercy with thy servants, that go before thee in all their heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 22:9–10; 2ND SAMUEL 7:12; 1ST KINGS 8:24"> thou hast given to David thy servant, my father, whatever thing thou hast spoken, or promised, to him; and thou hast fulfilled in work those things, which thou promisedest by mouth, as also this present time proveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:4; JOHN 15:14–15; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:18"> Now therefore, Lord God of Israel, fulfill thou to thy servant, my father David, whatever things thou hast spoken, saying, A man of thee shall not fail before me, that shall sit upon the throne of Israel; so nevertheless if thy sons keep my ways, and go in my law, as and thou hast gone before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:5; ISAIAH 41:17; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:4"> And now, Lord God of Israel, thy word be made steadfast, which thou spakest to thy servant David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 2:6; ISAIAH 66:1; PSALMS 139:7–10"> Therefore whether it is believeful, that the Lord dwell with men on earth? If heaven and the heavens of heavens may not take, either may not hold thee, Lord, how much more this house, which I have builded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:28; LUKE 18:1–7; JOHN 17:20"> But hereto only it is made, that thou, my Lord God, behold there the prayer of thy servant, and the beseeching of him, and that thou hear the prayers, which thy servant poureth &#91;out&#93; before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 34:15; DANIEL 6:10; PSALMS 121:5"> that thou open thine eyes upon this house by days and nights, upon the place in which thou promisedest, that thy name should be in-called, and that thou wouldest hear the prayer, which thy servant prayeth therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MICAH 7:18; DANIEL 9:19; ISAIAH 43:25"> Hear thou the prayers of thy servant, and of thy people Israel; who-ever prayeth in this place, hear thou from thy dwelling place, that is, from heaven, and do thou mercy to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 22:11; LEVITICUS 5:1; NUMBERS 5:19–22"> If any man sinneth against his neighbour, and cometh ready to swear against him, and bindeth himself with cursing before the altar in this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:10–11; PROVERBS 17:15; JEREMIAH 51:56"> thou shalt hear from heaven, and shalt do the doom of thy servants; so that thou yield to the wicked man his way into his own head, and that thou avenge the just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man, and yield to him after his rightwiseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:17; 2ND KI 17:7–18; JOSHUA 7:8"> If thy people Israel is overcome of enemies, for they shall do sin against thee, and if they converted do penance, and beseech thy name, and pray in this place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:6–13; EZRA 1:1–6; GENESIS 13:15"> thou shalt hear from heaven, and do thou mercy to the sin of thy people Israel, and bring them again into the land, which thou hast given to them, and to their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:23; DEUTERONOMY 11:17; LEVITICUS 26:19"> If when heaven is closed, rain come not down for the sin of thy people, and they beseech thee in this place, and acknowledge to thy name, and be turned from their sins, when thou hast tormented them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:35–36; JEREMIAH 5:24; ZECHARIAH 10:1"> hear thou, Lord, from heaven, and forgive thou the sins to thy servants, and to thy people Israel, and teach thou them a good way, by which they shall enter, and give thou rain to the land, which thou hast given to thy people to have in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:3–11; JOEL 2:25; RUTH 1:1"> If that hunger riseth in the land, and pestilence, and rust, and wind destroying corns, or crops, and if that a locust, and a bruchus cometh; and if enemies besiege the gates of the city, after that the countries be destroyed; and if in any manner vengeance and sickness oppresseth thy people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 32:2–6; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:12–13"> if any of thy people Israel beseech-eth, and knoweth his vengeance, that is, his sin wherefore he hath deserved vengeance, and sickness, and if he spreadeth abroad his hands in this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:9; 1ST SAMUEL 16:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:23"> thou shalt hear from heaven, that is, from thine high dwelling place, and do thou mercy, and yield thou to each man after his ways, which thou knowest, that he hath in his heart; for thou alone knowest the hearts of the sons of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:31; JOB 28:28; PSALMS 128:1"> that they dread thee, and go in thy ways in all days, in which they live on the face of &#91;the&#93; earth, which thou hast given to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:3–7; 1ST KINGS 8:41–43; MATTHEW 8:10–11"> Also thou shalt hear from heaven, thy most steadfast dwelling place, a stranger, which is not of thy people Israel, if he cometh from a far land for thy great name, and for thy strong hand, and thine arm stretched forth, and prayeth in this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 7:14; 1ST KINGS 8:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15"> and thou shalt do all things, for which that pilgrim inwardly calleth thee, that all the people of earth know thy name, and dread thee, as thy people Israel doeth; and that they know, that thy name is called on this house, which I have builded to thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 20:6–13; 2ND CHRONICLES 18:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:4"> If thy people goeth out to battle against his adversaries, by the way in which thou sendest them, they shall worship thee against the way in which this city is set, which thou hast chosen, and the house which I &#91;have&#93; builded to thy name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DANIEL 9:17–19; PSALMS 9:3–4; JEREMIAH 5:28"> that thou hear from heaven their prayers and their beseeching, and do thou vengeance to their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:20; JAMES 3:2; 1ST JOHN 1:8–10"> And if they sin against thee, for no man is alive that sinneth not, and if thou art wroth with them, and betakest them to their enemies; and enemies lead them prisoners into a far land, either certainly which land is nigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:18–20; DANIEL 9:5–11; ISAIAH 64:6–12"> and if they be converted in their heart in the land, to which they be led prisoners, and they do penance, and beseech thee in the land of their captivity, and say, We have sinned, we have done wickedly, we did unjustly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 33:11–13; DANIEL 6:10; DANIEL 9:3–4"> and if they turn again to thee in all their heart, and in all their soul, in the land of their captivity, to which they be led, and if they shall worship thee against the way of their land, which thou hast given to the fathers of them, and against the way of the city which thou hast chosen, and of the house which I &#91;have&#93; builded to thy name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MICAH 7:18–20; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:35; PSALMS 25:18"> that thou hear from heaven, that is, from thy steadfast dwelling place, the prayers of them, and that thou make doom, and forgive to thy people, though they be sinful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 7:15; PSALMS 116:2; NEHEMIAH 1:6"> for thou art my God; I beseech thee, be thine eyes opened, and thine ears be attentive to the prayer that is made in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="ISAIAH 61:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 28:2; PSALMS 132:8–10"> Now therefore, Lord God, rise up into thy rest, thou and the ark of thy strength; Lord God, thy priests be clothed with health, and thy holy men be glad in good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:3; PSALMS 132:1; ISAIAH 61:1"> Lord God, turn thou not away the face of thy christ or thine anointed; have thou mind on the mercies of David thy servant.</span>
