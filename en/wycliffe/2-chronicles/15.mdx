<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 24:20; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:14; NUMBERS 24:2"> Forsooth Azariah, the son of Oded, when the spirit of the Lord was come into him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:20; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:4"> he went out into the meeting of Asa; and said to him, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin, hear ye me; the Lord is with you, for ye were with him; if ye seek him, ye shall find him; soothly if ye forsake him, he shall forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 8:9; LEVITICUS 10:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 17:8–9"> Forsooth many days shall pass in Israel without very God, and without priest, and without teacher, and without law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:6; DEUTERONOMY 4:29–30; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:15"> And when they turn again in their anguish, and cry to the Lord God of Israel, and seek him, they shall find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 5:6; PSALMS 121:8; 1ST SAMUEL 13:6"> In that time &#91;there&#93; shall not be peace to go out and to go in, but dreads on all sides on all the dwellers of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 21:22–24; MATTHEW 24:7; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:11"> For folk shall fight against folk, and a city against a city, for the Lord shall disturb &#91;or trouble&#93; them in all anguish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:58; JOSHUA 1:7; JOSHUA 1:9"> but be ye comforted, and your hands be not slacked; for meed shall be to your work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 8:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 13:19; 2ND CHRONICLES 4:1"> And when Asa had heard this thing, that is, the words and &#91;the&#93; prophecy of Azariah, the son of Oded, the prophet, he was comforted, and he did away all the idols from all the land of Judah and of Benjamin, and from the cities which he had taken of the hill of Ephraim. And he hallowed the altar of the Lord, that was before the porch of the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 11:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:31; ZECHARIAH 8:21–23"> And he gathered together all Judah and Benjamin, and with them the comelings of Ephraim, and of Manasseh, and of Simeon; for many of Israel, seeing that his Lord God was with him, fled over to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ESTHER 8:9"> And when they had come into Jerusalem, in the third month, in the fifteen year of the realm of Asa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 14:13–15; NUMBERS 31:28–29; NUMBERS 31:50"> they offered to the Lord in that day, both of the spoils and of the prey, which they had brought, seven hundred oxen, and seven thousand wethers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:14; 2ND CHRONICLES 23:16; DEUTERONOMY 10:12"> And Asa entered by custom to make strong the bond of peace, that they should seek the Lord God of their fathers in all their heart, and in all their soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 22:20; PSALMS 115:13; 1ST KINGS 18:40"> And the king said, If any man seeketh not the Lord God of Israel, die he, from the least unto the most, from man unto woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 5:13; PSALMS 81:1–4; NEHEMIAH 10:29"> And all that were in Judah swore with cursing to the Lord, that is, oblig-ing themselves to cursing and pain of death, if they did against the oath, with &#91;a&#93; great voice, in hearty song, and in sound of trump, and in sound of clarions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 14:7; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:12; ISAIAH 26:8"> for they swore in all their heart, and in all their will they sought him, and found him; and the Lord gave to them rest by compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 15:13–24; 1ST KINGS 15:10; 2ND KI 23:15"> But also he put down Maachah, the grandmother of Asa the king, that is, his own grandmother, from the strait empire, for she had made in a wood a simulacrum, or a likeness, of a man’s rod; and he all-brake that simulacrum, and pounded it into gobbets, and burnt it in the strand &#91;or stream&#93; of Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 14:4; 2ND KI 12:3; 1ST KINGS 11:4"> But yet &#91;the&#93; high places were left in Israel; nevertheless the heart of Asa was rightful in all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:51; 1ST CHRONICLES 26:20–26; 1ST KINGS 15:14–15"> And he brought into the house of the Lord those things that his father &#91;had&#93; avowed, silver and gold, and diverse appurtenance of vessels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST KINGS 15:31; 1ST KINGS 15:33; 1ST KINGS 15:16–17"> and battle was not unto the five and thirtieth year of the realm of Asa.</span>
