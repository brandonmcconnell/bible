<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 69:4; PSALMS 35:7; PSALMS 35:20"> and they encompassed me with words of hatred; and fought against me without cause.</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 34:16; PSALMS 90:8; JOB 18:17"> Be they made ever&#91;more&#93; against the Lord; and the mind of them perish from earth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:2; PROVERBS 14:14; EZEKIEL 35:6"> And he loved cursing, and it shall come to him; and he would not give blessing, and it shall be made far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 73:6; NUMBERS 5:22; MATTHEW 27:3–5"> And he clothed cursing as a cloth, and it entered as water into his inner things; and as oil in his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 109:29; PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 109:18"> Be it made to him as a cloth, with which he is covered; and as a girdle, with which he is ever&#91;more&#93; girded.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 31:3; PSALMS 69:16; PSALMS 79:9–10"> And thou, Lord God, do with me for thy name; for thy mercy is sweet. Deliver thou me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 86:1; PSALMS 40:17; PSALMS 102:4"> for I am needy and poor; and mine heart is troubled within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 10:19; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:15; PSALMS 102:10–11"> I am taken away as a shadow, when it boweth away; and I am shaken away as locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 32:3–4; HEBREWS 12:12; MATTHEW 4:2"> My knees be made feeble with fasting; and my flesh was changed for oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 22:6–7; PSALMS 69:19–20; ROMANS 15:3"> And I am made a shame to them; they saw me, and moved their heads.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOB 37:7; 1ST KINGS 18:36–37; EXODUS 8:19"> And they shall know, that this is thine hand; and thou, Lord, hast done it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 111:1; PSALMS 35:18; PSALMS 22:22–25"> I shall acknowledge to the Lord greatly with my mouth; and I shall praise him in the middle of many men.</span>
