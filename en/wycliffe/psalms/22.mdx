<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 15:34; MATTHEW 27:46; HEBREWS 5:7"> To the overcomer, for the morrow-tide hind, the psalm of David. God, my God, behold thou on me, why hast thou forsaken me? the words of my trespasses be far from mine health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 42:3; LUKE 18:7; LUKE 22:41–46"> My God, I shall cry by day, and thou shalt not hear; and by night, and not to unwisdom to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 50:23; DEUTERONOMY 10:21; ISAIAH 6:3"> Forsooth thou, the praising of Israel, dwellest in holiness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 14:31; EXODUS 14:13–14; HEBREWS 11:8–32"> our fathers hoped in thee; they hoped, and thou deliveredest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 10:11; ISAIAH 49:23; ROMANS 9:33"> They cried to thee, and they were made safe; they hoped in thee, and they were not shamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:3; JOB 25:6; ISAIAH 49:7"> But I am a worm, and not a man; the shame of men, and the out-casting of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 15:29; PSALMS 109:25; MATTHEW 27:39–40"> All men seeing me scorned me; they spake with lips, and wagged the head, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:42–43; MARK 15:30–32; PSALMS 91:14"> He hoped in the Lord, deliver he him; make he him safe, for he will or delights in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 71:6; ISAIAH 49:1–2; PSALMS 71:17"> For thou it art that drewest me out of the womb, that art mine hope from the teats of my mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:15; JEREMIAH 1:5; ISAIAH 49:1"> into thee I am cast forth from the womb. From the womb of my mother thou art my God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 71:12; PSALMS 72:12; PSALMS 10:1"> depart thou not from me. For tribulation is next; for none there is that helpeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 4:1–3; PSALMS 68:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:27"> Many calves encompassed me; fat bulls besieged me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8; PSALMS 35:21; LAMENTATIONS 3:46"> They opened their mouth on me; as a lion ravishing and roaring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 31:10; DANIEL 5:6; JOB 30:16"> I am poured out as water; and all my bones be scattered. Mine heart is made, as wax floating abroad &#91;or melting&#93;; in the midst of my womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 19:28; PSALMS 69:3; PSALMS 69:21"> My virtue dried as a tilestone, and my tongue cleaved to my cheeks; and thou hast brought forth me into the dust of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 19:37; ZECHARIAH 12:10; MATTHEW 27:35"> For many dogs encompassed me; the council of wicked men besieged me. They delved mine hands and my feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 23:35; JOB 33:21; MATTHEW 27:39–41"> they numbered all my bones. Soothly they looked, and beheld me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:35; LUKE 23:34; MARK 15:24"> they parted my clothes to them-selves, and they sent lot on my cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 22:11; PSALMS 40:13; PSALMS 40:17"> But thou, Lord, delay not thine help from me; behold thou to my defence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 35:17; ZECHARIAH 13:7; PSALMS 17:13"> God, deliver thou my life from sword; and deliver thou mine one alone from the hand, or power, of the dog.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:17; NUMBERS 23:22; 1ST PETER 5:8"> Make thou me safe from the mouth of a lion; and my meekness from the horns of unicorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:11–12; ROMANS 8:29; PSALMS 40:9–10"> I shall tell thy name to my brethren; I shall praise thee in the midst of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 135:19–20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:4; PSALMS 107:1–2"> Ye that dread the Lord, praise him; all the seed of Jacob, glorify him. All the seed of Israel, dread him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:7; ISAIAH 50:6–9; PSALMS 116:3–6"> for he forsook not, neither despised the prayer of a poor man. Neither he turned away his face from me; and when I cried to him, he heard me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 40:9–10; PSALMS 35:18; PSALMS 111:1"> My praising is with thee in a great church; I shall yield my vows in the sight of men dreading him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 69:32; PSALMS 40:16; ISAIAH 65:13"> Poor men shall eat, and shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled, and they shall praise the Lord, that seek him; the hearts of them shall live into the world of world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; ISAIAH 45:22; PSALMS 2:8"> All the ends of earth shall bethink; and shall be converted to the Lord. And all the meines of heathen men, shall worship in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 14:9; PSALMS 47:7–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15"> For the realm is the Lord’s; and he shall be Lord of heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:10; ISAIAH 26:19; PSALMS 45:12"> All the fat men of earth ate and worshipped; all men, that go down into earth, shall fall down in his sight. And my soul shall live to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:13; PSALMS 14:5; 1ST PETER 2:9"> and my seed shall serve him. A generation to coming shall be told to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 78:6; PSALMS 86:9; ROMANS 1:17"> and heavens shall tell his rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; to the people that shall be born, whom the Lord made.</span>
