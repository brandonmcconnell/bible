<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 124:7; PSALMS 141:9; JOB 5:10–22"> For he delivered me from the snare of hunters; and from a sharp word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 61:4; PSALMS 57:1; PSALMS 17:8"> With his shoulders he shall make shadow to thee; and thou shalt have hope under his feathers. His truth shall encompass thee with a shield;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:2; JOB 5:19–27; PROVERBS 3:23–25"> thou shalt not dread of the night’s dread. Of an arrow flying in the day,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 71:3; PSALMS 90:1; PSALMS 91:1–2"> For thou, Lord, art mine hope; thou hast set thine help to be the alder Highest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:21; PSALMS 121:7; DEUTERONOMY 7:15"> Evil shall not come to thee; and a scourge shall not come nigh to thy tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 4:10–11; PSALMS 34:7; HEBREWS 1:14"> For God hath commanded to his angels of thee; that they keep thee in all thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:24; PROVERBS 3:23; LUKE 4:11"> They shall bear thee in the hands; lest peradventure thou hurt thy foot at a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 10:19; DANIEL 6:22; MARK 16:18"> Thou shalt go upon a snake, and a cockatrice; and thou shalt defoul a lion, and a dragon.</span>
