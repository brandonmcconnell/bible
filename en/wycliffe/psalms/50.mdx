<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 113:3; JOSHUA 22:22; MATTHEW 25:32"> The psalm of Asaph. God, the Lord of gods or God of gods, spake; and called the earth, from the rising of the sun till to the going down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 48:2; PSALMS 80:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:23"> The shape of his fairness from Zion, God shall come openly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 7:10; NUMBERS 16:35; LEVITICUS 10:2"> our God, and he shall not be still. Fire shall burn on high in his sight; and a strong tempest in his compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:1; ISAIAH 1:2; DEUTERONOMY 31:28"> He called heaven above; and the earth, to witness him deem his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:28; EXODUS 24:3–8; HEBREWS 13:20"> Gather ye to him his saints; that ordain his testament above sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 97:6; PSALMS 75:7; PSALMS 89:5"> And heavens shall show his rightwiseness or tell his rightfulness; for God is the judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 20:2; PSALMS 81:8; JEREMIAH 2:9"> My people, hear thou, and I shall speak to Israel; and I shall witness to thee, I am God, thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:11–31; HEBREWS 10:4–10; HOSEA 6:6"> I shall not reprove thee in thy sacrifices; and thy burnt sacrifices be ever&#91;more&#93; before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MICAH 6:6–8; HEBREWS 10:4–6; PSALMS 69:31"> I shall not take calves of thine house; neither goat bucks of thy flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:5–6; PSALMS 8:6–8; PSALMS 104:14"> For all the wild beasts of woods be mine; work beasts, and oxes &#91;or oxen&#93; in hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 1:20–22; MATTHEW 6:26; LUKE 12:24"> I have known all the volatiles of the firmament; and the fairness of the field is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5; DEUTERONOMY 10:14; PSALMS 24:1–2"> If I shall be hungry, I shall not say to thee; for the world and the fullness thereof is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Whether I shall eat the flesh of bulls? either shall I drink the blood of goat bucks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:15; PSALMS 56:12; DEUTERONOMY 23:21"> Offer thou to God the sacrifice of praising; and yield thine avows &#91;or vows&#93; to the highest God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 107:28; PSALMS 91:15; PSALMS 34:3–4"> And inwardly call thou me in the day of tribulation; and I shall deliver thee, and thou shalt honour me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:13; PSALMS 78:36–38; ROMANS 2:17–24"> But God said to the sinner, Why tellest thou out my rightfulnesses; and takest my testament by thy mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:26; PROVERBS 12:1; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:3–4"> Soothly thou hatedest lore, or discipline; and hast cast away my words behind thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 1:32; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:22; HEBREWS 13:4"> If thou sawest a thief, thou rannest with him; and thou settedest thy part with adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 52:2–4; PSALMS 55:21"> Thy mouth was plenteous of malice; and thy tongue meddled together guiles &#91;or treacheries&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:21; PROVERBS 10:18; LEVITICUS 19:16"> Thou sitting spakest against thy brother, and thou settedest slander against the son of thy mother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 2:4–5; PROVERBS 29:1; ISAIAH 57:11"> thou didest these things, and I was still. Thou guessedest wickedly, that I shall be like thee; I shall reprove thee, and I shall set against thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 8:13; PSALMS 9:17; HOSEA 4:6"> Ye that forget God, understand these things; lest sometime he ravish, and none be that shall deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 91:16; JAMES 3:13; PSALMS 24:4–5"> The sacrifice of praising shall honour me; and there is the way, wherein I shall show to him the health of God.</span>
