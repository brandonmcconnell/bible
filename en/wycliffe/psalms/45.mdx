<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 49:3; 2ND SAMUEL 23:2; JOB 33:3"> To the overcomer, for the lilies, the most loved song of learning of the sons of Korah. Mine heart hath told out a good word; I say my works to the king. My tongue is the pen of a writer; writing swiftly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 4:22; JOHN 7:46; HEBREWS 7:26"> Christ, thou art fairer in shape than the sons of men; grace is spread abroad in thy lips; therefore God blessed thee without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:16; ISAIAH 49:2"> Be thou gird with thy sword; on thy hip most mightily. Behold thou in thy shapeliness and thy fairness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 65:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:11"> come thou forth with prosperity, and reign thou. For truth, and mildness, and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;; and thy right hand shall lead forth thee wonderfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 21:12; NUMBERS 24:8; PSALMS 38:2"> Thy sharp arrows shall fall into the hearts of the enemies of the king; peoples shall be under thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:8–9; PSALMS 93:2; JOHN 1:1"> God, thy seat is into the world of world; the rod of thy realm is a rod of right ruling, or of equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:9; PSALMS 21:6; ISAIAH 61:1–3"> Thou lovedest rightfulness &#91;or right-wiseness&#93;, and hatedest wickedness; therefore thou, God, thy God, anointed thee with the oil of gladness, more than thy fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 1:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:14–16; JOHN 19:39"> Myrrh, and gum, and cassia, of thy clothes, out of the ivory houses or the houses of ivory; of which the daughters of kings delighted thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:9; EPHESIANS 5:26–27"> A queen stood nigh on thy right side, in clothing overgilded; compassed with diversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 2:10–13; GENESIS 12:1; MATTHEW 10:37"> Daughter, hear thou, and see, and bow down thine ear; and forget thy people, and the house of thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 95:6; ISAIAH 54:5; SONG OF SONGS 4:1–5"> And the king shall covet thy fair-ness; for he is thy Lord God, and they shall worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 22:29; ISAIAH 60:3; ISAIAH 49:23"> And the daughters of Tyre in gifts; all the rich men of the people shall beseech thy cheer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:7–8; ISAIAH 61:10; ROMANS 3:22"> All the glory of that daughter of the king is within, adorned in golden hems;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:2; SONG OF SONGS 8:13; EXODUS 28:39"> she is clothed about with diversities. Virgins shall be brought to the king after her; her neighbouresses shall be brought to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:15–17; JUDE 1:24; ISAIAH 55:12–13"> They shall be brought in gladness, and full out joying; they shall be brought into the temple of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:10; 1ST PETER 2:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:6"> Sons be born to thee, for thy fathers; thou shalt ordain them princes on all earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 62:3; MALACHI 1:11; PSALMS 72:17–19"> Lord, they shall be mindful of thy name; in each generation, and into generation. Therefore peoples shall acknowledge to thee without end; and into the world of world.</span>
