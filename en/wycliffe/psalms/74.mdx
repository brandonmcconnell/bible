<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:3; PSALMS 78:65–66; PSALMS 44:23"> Why turnest thou away thine hand, to not draw out thy right hand from the midst of thy bosom, till into the end?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 44:4; EXODUS 19:5–6; ISAIAH 33:22"> Forsooth God our king before worlds, wrought health in the midst of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 14:21; PSALMS 78:13; EZEKIEL 29:3"> Thou madest firm the sea by thy virtue; thou hast troubled the heads of the dragons in waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 27:1; PSALMS 72:9; PSALMS 104:25–26"> Thou hast broken the heads of the dragon; thou hast given him to be meat to the peoples of Ethiopians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:11; ISAIAH 48:21; PSALMS 105:41"> Thou hast broken wells, and strands &#91;or streams&#93;; thou madest dry the floods of Eitan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 136:7–9; MATTHEW 5:45; PSALMS 104:19"> The day is thine, and the night is thine; thou madest the morrowtide and the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:26; GENESIS 8:22"> Thou madest all the ends of the earth; summer, and ver time, either springing time, thou formedest those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:19; DEUTERONOMY 32:6"> Be thou mindful of this thing, the enemy hath said shame to the Lord; and the unwise people hath excited to ire thy name.</span>
