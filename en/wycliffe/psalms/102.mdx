<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JAMES 4:14; PSALMS 37:20; JOB 30:30"> For my days have failed as smoke; and my bones have dried up as croutons or cracklings, either leavings of frying.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:11; PSALMS 69:9–10; ISAIAH 65:15"> All day mine enemies despised me; and they that praised me swore against me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 14:2; PSALMS 109:23; PSALMS 144:4"> My days bowed away as a shadow; and I waxed dry as hay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 135:13; LAMENTATIONS 5:19; EXODUS 3:15"> But, Lord, thou dwellest without end; and thy memorial in generation and into generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:1; GALATIANS 4:4; ISAIAH 40:2"> Lord, thou rising up, shalt have mercy on Zion; for the time to have mercy thereof cometh, for the time cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 4:2; EZRA 7:27; EZRA 3:1–3"> For the stones thereof pleased thy servants; and they shall have mercy on the land thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:43; PSALMS 138:4; ISAIAH 60:3–22"> And, Lord, heathen men shall dread thy name; and all kings of earth shall dread thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:1–2; PSALMS 147:2; JEREMIAH 33:7"> For the Lord hath built &#91;up&#93; Zion; and he shall be seen in his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 1:6; PSALMS 22:24; JEREMIAH 29:11–14"> He beheld on the prayer of meek men; and he despised not the prayer of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 22:30–31; ROMANS 15:4; ISAIAH 43:21"> Be these things written in another generation; and the people that shall be made shall praise the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:15; PSALMS 14:2; 1ST KINGS 8:43"> For he beheld from his high holy place; the Lord looked from heaven into earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 79:11; PSALMS 146:7; EXODUS 3:7"> For to hear the wailings of fettered men; and for to unbind the sons of slain men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 22:22; ISAIAH 51:11; PSALMS 79:13"> That they tell in Zion the name of the Lord; and his praising in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:20–23; MATTHEW 24:14; PSALMS 72:8–11"> In gathering together peoples into one; and kings, that they serve the Lord.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 39:13; HABAKKUK 1:12; PSALMS 102:12"> Again-call thou not me in the middle of my days; thy years be in generation and into generation.</span>
