<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 107:22; PSALMS 96:1–2; PSALMS 148:1–5"> Sing ye &#91;psalms&#93; to the Lord, that dwelleth in Zion; tell ye his studies among heathen men.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 13:5; PSALMS 51:12; PSALMS 106:2"> that I tell all thy praisings in the gates of the daughter of Zion. I shall be fully joyful or joy fully in thine health;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 7:15–16; PSALMS 57:6; PSALMS 35:8"> heathen men be fast-set in the perishing, which they made. In this snare, which they hid, the foot of them is caught.</span>
