<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 31:1–3; PSALMS 25:2–3; 1ST PETER 2:6"> Lord, I hoped in thee; be I not shamed without end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 31:1; PSALMS 17:6; PSALMS 116:1–2"> in thy rightwiseness deliver thou me, and ravish me out. Bow down thine ear to me; and make me safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 18:2; PSALMS 91:9; PROVERBS 18:10"> Be thou to me into God a defender; and into a strengthened place, that thou make me safe. For thou art my steadfastness; and my refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 140:1–4; PSALMS 59:1–2; 2ND SAMUEL 17:1–2"> My God, deliver thou me from the hand of the sinner; and from the hand of a man doing against the law, and of the wicked man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:7; PSALMS 39:7; PSALMS 119:81"> For thou, Lord, art my patience; Lord, thou art mine hope from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 22:9–10; JEREMIAH 1:5; PSALMS 139:15–16"> In thee I am confirmed, that is, defended, from the womb; thou art my defender from the womb of my mother. My singing is ever&#91;more&#93; in thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:13"> I am made as a great wonder to many men; and thou art a strong helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 35:28; PSALMS 146:2; PSALMS 104:1"> My mouth be filled with praising; that I sing thy glory, all day thy great-ness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:12; PSALMS 71:18"> Cast thou not away me in the time of eld age /in the time of oldness; when my strength faileth, forsake thou not me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 83:3; PSALMS 56:6; JEREMIAH 20:10"> For mine enemies said of me; and they that kept my life made counsel together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 7:2; PSALMS 3:2; MATTHEW 27:46"> Saying, God hath forsaken him; pursue ye, and take him; for none there is that shall deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:22; PSALMS 38:21–22; PSALMS 70:5"> God, be thou not made afar from me; my God, behold thou into mine help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:11; PSALMS 71:24; PSALMS 35:26"> Men that backbite my soul, be shamed, and fail they; and be they covered with reproof and shame, that seek evils to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 130:7; 1ST JOHN 3:3; PSALMS 71:6"> But I shall hope ever&#91;more&#93;; and I shall add to ever over all thy praising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 40:5; PSALMS 35:28; PSALMS 71:8"> My mouth shall tell thy rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;; all day thine health. For I knew not by literature, that is, by man’s teaching, but by God’s revelation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; PSALMS 51:14; PHILIPPIANS 3:9"> I shall enter into the powers of the Lord; Lord, I shall bethink on thy rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 71:5; PSALMS 119:9; PSALMS 119:102"> God, thou hast taught me from my youth, and till to now; I shall tell out thy marvels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:4; PSALMS 71:9; PSALMS 78:4"> And till into eld age /into oldness, and the last age; God, forsake thou not me. Till I tell thine arm, or power, to each generation that shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 35:10; PSALMS 36:5–6; PSALMS 57:10"> Till I tell of thy might, and thy rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, God, till into the highest great deeds which thou hast done; God, who is like thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 138:7; PSALMS 60:3; PSALMS 86:13"> How great tribulations, many and evil, hast thou showed to me; and thou converted, hast quickened me, and hast again-brought me again from the depths of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 12:1; 2ND SAMUEL 3:1; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:14"> Thou hast multiplied thy great doing; and thou converted, hast comforted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 92:1–3; 2ND KI 19:22; PSALMS 33:2"> For why and I shall acknowledge to thee, thou God, thy truth in the instruments of psalm; I shall sing in an harp to thee, that art the holy of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 104:33; PSALMS 103:4; LUKE 1:46–47"> My lips shall make fully joy &#91;or full out joy&#93;, when I shall sing to thee; and my soul, which thou again-boughtest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 71:13; PSALMS 18:37–43; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:25"> But and my tongue shall think all day on thy rightfulness &#91;or rightwise-ness&#93;; when they shall be shamed and ashamed, that seek evils to me.</span>
