<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 78:55; EXODUS 15:17; JOSHUA 11:23"> Thine hand lost heathen men, and thou plantedest them; thou tormentedest peoples, and castedest them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 8:17–18; JOSHUA 24:12"> For the children of Israel wielded the land not by their sword; and the arm of them saved not them. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 74:12; PSALMS 42:8; PSALMS 149:2"> Thou art thyself, my king, and my God; that sendest healths to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 108:13; PSALMS 60:12; PSALMS 118:10–13"> By thee we shall winnow our enemies with &#91;the&#93; horn; and in thy name we shall despise them, that rise against us.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 136:24"> For thou hast saved us from men tormenting us; and thou hast shamed men hating us.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 74:1; PSALMS 60:10; PSALMS 60:1"> But now thou hast put us aback, and hast shamed us; and thou, Lord, shalt not go out in our virtues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:8; LEVITICUS 26:17; JOSHUA 7:12"> Thou hast turned us away behind after our enemies; and they, that hated us, ravished diversely to themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:27; DEUTERONOMY 28:64; ROMANS 8:36"> Thou hast given us as sheep of meats; and among heathen men thou hast scattered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:13; DEUTERONOMY 32:30; ISAIAH 52:3–4"> Thou hast sold thy people without price; and multitude there was not in the exchangings of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 79:4; PSALMS 80:6; PSALMS 89:41"> Thou hast set &#91;or put&#93; us as a shame to our neighbours; mocking and scorn to them that be in our compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:9; 2ND KI 19:21; JOB 16:4"> Thou hast set &#91;or put&#93; us into like-ness to heathen men; and wagging of the head among peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 69:7; JEREMIAH 3:25; PSALMS 89:45"> All day my shame is against me; and the shame of my face covered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 8:2; PSALMS 74:10; PSALMS 74:18"> From the voice of &#91;the&#93; despiser, and evil speaker; from the face of the enemy, and &#91;the&#93; pursuer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 9:13; DEUTERONOMY 8:14; DEUTERONOMY 6:12"> All these things came on us, and we have not forgotten thee; and we did not wickedly in thy testament.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:51; PSALMS 119:157; PSALMS 78:57"> And our heart went not away behind; and thou hast not bowed away our paths from thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 3:5; PSALMS 23:4; JEREMIAH 14:17"> For thou hast made us low in the place of torment; and the shadow of death covered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 81:9; JOB 11:13; DEUTERONOMY 6:14"> If we forgat the name of our God; and if we held forth our hands to an alien God.</span>
