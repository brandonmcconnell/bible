<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1; PSALMS 27:9; PSALMS 13:1–3"> Lord, why hast thou gone far away? thou despisest us in covenable times in tribulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 59:12; PSALMS 36:11; PSALMS 7:16"> While the wicked man waxeth proud, the poor man is burnt; they be taken in the wicked counsels, which they thinked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 94:4; LUKE 12:19; ZECHARIAH 11:5–8"> For why the sinner is praised in the desires of his soul; and the wicked is blessed. The sinner hath stirred the Lord to wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 14:1–2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:22; ROMANS 1:28"> after the multitude of his wrath, he shall not seek after God. God is not in his sight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 92:5–6; ISAIAH 26:11; PSALMS 12:5"> his ways be defouled in all time. Thy dooms be taken away from his face; he shall be lord of all his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 8:11; MATTHEW 24:48; ISAIAH 56:12"> For he said in his heart, I shall not be moved, from generation into gener-ation without evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 7:14; PSALMS 140:9; PSALMS 140:3"> Whose mouth is full of cursing, and of bitterness, and of guile; travail and sorrow is under his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:14; 1ST SAMUEL 22:18; JEREMIAH 22:17"> He sitteth in ambushes with rich men in privates; to slay the innocent man. His eyes behold cruelly on the poor man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 17:12; PSALMS 59:3; MICAH 7:2"> he setteth ambushes in hid place, as a lion in his den. He setteth ambushes, for to ravish a poor man; for to ravish a poor man, while he draweth in the poor man. In his snare he shall make meek the poor man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 23:21–22; 1ST SAMUEL 18:21–26; 2ND SAMUEL 15:5"> he shall bow himself down, and he shall fall, when he hath been lord of poor men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:12; PSALMS 94:7; PSALMS 73:11"> For he said in his heart, God hath forgotten; he hath turned away his face, that he see not into the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MICAH 5:9; PSALMS 9:12; PSALMS 94:2"> Lord God, rise thou up, and thine hand be enhanced; forget thou not poor men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:9–10; PSALMS 74:10; GENESIS 9:5"> For what thing stirred the wicked man God to wrath? for he said in his heart, God shall not seek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 68:5; PSALMS 146:9; PSALMS 55:22"> Thou seest, for thou beholdest travail and sorrow; that thou take them into thine hands. The poor man is left to thee; thou shalt be an helper to the fatherless and motherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:12; PSALMS 37:17; JOB 38:15"> All-break thou the arm of the sinner, and evil-willed; his sin shall be sought, and it shall not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 29:10; JEREMIAH 10:10; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:17"> The Lord shall reign without end, and into the world of world; folks, ye shall perish from the land of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 145:19; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:27; PSALMS 34:15"> The Lord hath heard the desire of poor men; thine ear hath heard the making ready of their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 82:3; ISAIAH 11:4"> To deem for the motherless and meek; that a man presume no more to make himself great on earth.</span>
