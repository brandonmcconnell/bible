<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 32:1; PSALMS 72:12–14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:11"> To deem thy people in rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;; and thy poor men in doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:7; PSALMS 96:11–13; PSALMS 72:16"> Mountains receive peace to the people; and little hills receive rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:4; EZEKIEL 34:15–16; PSALMS 109:31"> He shall deem the poor men of the people, and he shall make safe the sons of poor men; and he shall make low the false challenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 89:36–37; PSALMS 72:17; DANIEL 7:14"> And he shall dwell with the sun, and before the moon, that is, without beginning, and end; in generation and into generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 6:3; DEUTERONOMY 32:2; 2ND SAMUEL 23:4"> He shall come down as rain into a fleece; and as gutters dropping on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 2:4; MALACHI 4:2; PSALMS 92:12"> Rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; shall come forth in his days; and the abun-dance of peace, till the moon be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 23:31; ZECHARIAH 9:10; PSALMS 80:11"> And he shall be lord from the sea till to the sea; and from the flood till to the ends of the world.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:6; PSALMS 68:29; ISAIAH 49:7"> The kings of Tarshish and isles shall offer gifts; the kings of Arabia and of Seba shall bring gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; ISAIAH 49:22–23; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15"> And all kings shall worship him; all folks shall serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 29:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17–18; PSALMS 102:17"> For he shall deliver a poor man from the mighty; and a poor man to whom there was none helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:16; PSALMS 109:31; MATTHEW 5:3"> He shall spare a poor man and needy; and he shall make safe the souls of poor men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 116:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:2; PSALMS 69:18"> He shall again-buy the souls of them from usuries, and wickedness; and the name of them is honourable before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 10:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:8–14; JUDE 1:25"> And he shall live, and men shall give to him of the gold of Arabia; and they shall ever worship of him, all day they shall bless him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:9; PSALMS 92:12–14; ISAIAH 30:23"> Steadfastness shall be in the earth, in the highest place of mountains; the fruit thereof shall be enhanced above the Lebanon; and they shall blossom from the city, as the hay of earth doeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:14; MATTHEW 1:23; PSALMS 89:36"> His name be blessed into worlds; his name dwell before the sun. And all the lineages of earth shall be blessed in him; all folks shall magnify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 41:13; PSALMS 106:48; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:10"> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; which alone maketh marvels.</span>
