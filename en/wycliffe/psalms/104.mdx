<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 103:22; PSALMS 145:3; PSALMS 96:6"> My soul, bless thou the Lord; my Lord God, thou art magnified greatly. Thou hast clothed acknowledging and fairness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:22; DANIEL 7:9; ISAIAH 45:12"> and thou art clothed with light, as with a cloth. And thou stretchest forth heaven as a skin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 9:6; 2ND SAMUEL 22:11; ISAIAH 19:1"> and thou coverest with waters the higher parts thereof. Which settest a cloud thy ascending; which goest on the feathers of winds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:7; 2ND KI 2:11; 2ND KI 6:17"> Which makest spirits thine angels; and thy ministers a burning fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 26:7; PSALMS 24:2; PSALMS 33:9"> Which hast founded the earth on his stableness; it shall not be bowed into the world of world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 7:19; 2ND PETER 3:5; GENESIS 1:2–10"> The depth of waters as a cloth is the clothing thereof; waters shall stand on hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 18:15; GENESIS 8:1; MARK 4:39"> Those &#91;or they&#93; shall flee from thy blaming; men shall be afeared of the voice of thy thunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 8:5; PSALMS 33:7"> Hills go up, and fields go down; into the place which thou hast founded to those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 38:10–11; GENESIS 9:11–15; JEREMIAH 5:22"> Thou hast set a term, which they shall not pass; neither those &#91;or they&#93; shall be turned, for to cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:18; PSALMS 107:35; ISAIAH 35:7"> And thou sendest out wells into great valleys; waters shall pass betwixt the midst of hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 145:16; JOB 39:5–8; PSALMS 104:13"> All the beasts of the field shall drink; wild asses shall abide in their thirst, that is, to be filled in their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 50:11; PSALMS 148:10; MATTHEW 8:20"> &#91;The&#93; Birds of the air shall dwell on those; from the midst of the stones they shall give voices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 147:8; JEREMIAH 10:13; JEREMIAH 14:22"> And thou moistest &#91;the&#93; hills of their higher things; the earth shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled of the fruit of thy works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 136:25; JOB 28:5; PSALMS 147:8–9"> And thou bringest forth hay to beasts; and herb to the service of men. That thou bring forth bread of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 23:5; ECCLESIASTES 10:19; ECCLESIASTES 9:7"> and that wine make glad the heart of men. That he make glad the face with oil; and that bread make steadfast the heart of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:6; PSALMS 92:2; PSALMS 29:5"> The trees of the field shall be &#91;full-&#93;filled, and the cedars of the Lebanon, which he planted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 104:12; LEVITICUS 11:19; DANIEL 4:21"> sparrows shall make nest there. The house of the gyrfalcon is the leader of those &#91;or them&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:26; JOB 39:1; LEVITICUS 11:5"> high hills be refuge to harts; the stone is refuge to urchins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:35; GENESIS 1:14–18; PSALMS 136:7–9"> He made the moon into times; the sun knew his going down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:7; PSALMS 74:16; AMOS 1:13"> Thou hast set darknesses, and night is made; all beasts of the wood shall go therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 38:39; JOEL 1:20; PSALMS 34:10"> Lions’ whelps roaring for to ravish; and to seek of God meat to them-selves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 37:8; NAHUM 3:17; JOHN 3:20"> The sun is risen, and those &#91;or they&#93; be gathered together; and those &#91;or they&#93; shall be set in their couches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 3:19; ECCLESIASTES 5:12; EPHESIANS 4:28"> A man shall go out to his work; and to his working, till to the eventide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:12; PSALMS 40:5; PSALMS 50:10–12"> Lord, thy works be magnified full much, thou hast made all things in wisdom; the earth is filled with thy possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 69:34; PSALMS 95:4–5; GENESIS 1:28"> This sea is great and large to hands; there be creeping beasts, of which is no number. Little beasts with &#91;the&#93; great;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 107:23; ISAIAH 27:1; PSALMS 74:14"> ships shall pass there. This dragon which thou hast formed, for to scorn him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 136:25; PSALMS 147:9; JOB 38:41"> All things abide of thee; that thou give to them meat in time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 145:16"> When thou shalt give to them, they shall gather; when thou shalt open thine hand, all things shall be filled with goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 34:14–15; ECCLESIASTES 12:7; PSALMS 146:4"> But when thou shalt turn away thy face, they shall be troubled; thou shalt take away the spirit of them, and they shall fail; and they shall turn again into their dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOB 33:4; EZEKIEL 37:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:5"> Send out thy spirit, and they shall be formed of the new or reformed of new; and thou shalt renew the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 1:31; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:18; EPHESIANS 3:21"> The glory of the Lord be into the world; the Lord shall be glad in his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 144:5; PSALMS 97:4–5; EXODUS 19:18"> Which beholdeth the earth, and maketh it to tremble; which toucheth hills, and those &#91;or they&#93; smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 63:4; PSALMS 146:2; PSALMS 145:1–2"> I shall sing to the Lord in my life; I shall say psalm to my God, as long as I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:17–18; PHILIPPIANS 4:4; PSALMS 1:2"> My speech be merry or mirth to him; forsooth I shall delight in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 103:22–1; PSALMS 37:38; PSALMS 106:48"> Sinners fail from the earth, and wicked men fail, so that they be not; my soul, bless thou the Lord.</span>
