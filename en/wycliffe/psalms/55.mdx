<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:7–8; PSALMS 27:12; PSALMS 35:11"> and I am disturbed of the face of the enemy, and of the tribulation of the sinner. For they bowed wicked-nesses into me; and in ire they were dis-easeful to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 116:3; MARK 14:33–34; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> Mine heart was troubled in me; and the dread of death felled on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 119:120; JOB 21:6; 2ND SAMUEL 15:14"> Dread and trembling came on me; and darknesses covered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 11:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:14; PSALMS 139:9"> And I said, Who shall give to me feathers, as of a culver; and I shall fly, and shall take rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:2; 2ND SAMUEL 17:21–22; JEREMIAH 37:12"> Lo! I went far away, and fled; and I dwelled in wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 4:6; MATTHEW 7:25–27; ISAIAH 17:12–13"> I abode him, that made me safe from the littleness, either dread, of spirit; and from tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:7; GENESIS 11:7–9; JEREMIAH 23:14"> Lord, cast thou down, &#91;and&#93; part thou the tongues of them; for I saw wickedness and against-saying in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 59:14–15; ISAIAH 59:6–15; EZEKIEL 9:4"> By day and night wickedness shall encompass it on the walls thereof; and travail and unrightfulness be in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:26–27; PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 5:9"> And usury and guile &#91;or treachery&#93; failed not; from the streets thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; JOHN 13:18; MATTHEW 26:21–23"> For if mine enemy had cursed me; soothly I had suffered. And if he, that hated me, had spoken great things on me; in hap I had hid me from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:12; MICAH 7:5; JEREMIAH 9:4"> But thou art a man of one will; my leader, and my known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; ISAIAH 2:3; EZEKIEL 33:31"> Which tookest together sweet meats and fellowship with me; we went with consent in the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 59:13; PSALMS 64:7; PSALMS 9:17"> Death come on them; and go they down quick into hell. For way-wardnesses be in the dwelling places of them; in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 109:4; PSALMS 73:28"> But I cried to thee, Lord; and the Lord saved me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:18; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:17; PSALMS 141:2"> In the eventide, and &#91;the&#93; morrow-tide, and in midday, I shall tell, and show; and he shall hear my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 57:3; PSALMS 3:6–7; 2ND SAMUEL 22:1"> He shall again-buy my soul in peace from them, that nigh to me; for among many they were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:10–11; JEREMIAH 48:11"> God shall hear; and he that is before the worlds shall make them low. For changing is not to them, and they dreaded not God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:1; PSALMS 89:34; PSALMS 7:4"> he holdeth forth his hand in yielding. They defouled his testament,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 28:3; PROVERBS 5:3–4"> the cheers thereof were parted from ire; and his heart nighed. The words thereof were softer than oil; and they be darts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:7; PSALMS 37:5; PHILIPPIANS 4:6–7"> Cast thy care, or thought, &#91;or thy busyness&#93;, on the Lord, and he shall fully nourish thee; and he shall not give without end fluttering to a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:27; PSALMS 5:6; JOB 15:32"> But thou, God, shalt lead them forth; into the pit of death. Men-quellers and beguilers shall not have half their days; but, Lord, I shall hope in thee.</span>
