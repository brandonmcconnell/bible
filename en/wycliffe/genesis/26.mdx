</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 12:7; PSALMS 37:3; GENESIS 17:1"> And the Lord appeared to him, and said, Go not down into Egypt, but rest thou in the land which I shall say to thee,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 28:15; PSALMS 105:9; GENESIS 17:8"> and be thou a pilgrim therein; and I shall be with thee, and I shall bless thee; for I shall give all these countries to thee, and to thy seed, and I shall &#91;ful&#93; fill the oath which I promised to Abraham, thy father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:25; GENESIS 15:5; GENESIS 22:17–18"> And I shall multiply thy seed as the stars of heaven, and I shall give all these countries to thine heirs, and all folks of the earth shall be blessed in thy seed,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:24; GENESIS 22:18; GENESIS 22:16"> for Abraham obeyed to my voice, and kept my behests, and my commandments, and my ceremonies, and my laws.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 20:1"> And so Isaac dwelled in Gerar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:25; GENESIS 12:13; GENESIS 20:2"> And when he was asked of &#91;the&#93; men of that place of his wife, he answered, She is my sister; for he dreaded to acknowledge that she was fellowshipped to him in matrimony, and he guessed lest peradventure they would slay him for the fairness of her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 62:5; PROVERBS 5:18–19; PROVERBS 7:6"> And when full many days were passed, and he dwelled there, Abimelech, king of Palestines, beheld by a window, and saw him playing with Rebecca, his wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when Isaac was called, the king said, It is open, that she is thy wife; why saidest thou, that she was thy sister? Isaac answered, For I dreaded, lest I should die for her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 20:9–10; GENESIS 12:18–19"> And Abimelech said, Why hast thou deceived us? Some man of the people might do lechery with thy wife, and thou haddest brought in grievous sin on us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 105:15; GENESIS 20:6; PROVERBS 6:29"> And the king commanded to all the people, and said, He that toucheth the wife of this man shall die by death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 4:8; GENESIS 24:1; GENESIS 26:3"> Forsooth Isaac sowed in that land, and he found an hundredfold increase in that year; and the Lord blessed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 112:3; PROVERBS 10:22; GENESIS 24:35"> And the man was made rich, and he went profiting and increasing, till he was made full great.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 37:11; GENESIS 13:2; PROVERBS 27:4"> Also he had possessions of sheep and of great beasts, and full much of menials. For this thing Palestines had envy to him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 21:30; GENESIS 21:25"> and they stopped in that time and filled with earth all the pits or wells which the servants of Abraham his father had digged,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 1:9"> in so much that Abimelech himself said to Isaac, Go thou away from us, for thou art made greatly mightier than we.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he went away, that he should come to the strand of Gerar, and dwelled there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:2; HOSEA 2:17; NUMBERS 32:38"> And he digged again other wells, which the servants of Abraham his father had digged, and which the Philistines had stopped sometime, when Abraham was dead; and he called those pits by the same names, by which his father had called before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 4:10–11; JOHN 7:38; SONG OF SONGS 4:15"> They digged in the strand, and they found quick, or welling up, water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 21:25"> But also strife of &#91;the&#93; shepherds of Gerar was there against the shep-herds of Isaac, and they said, The water is ours; wherefore of that strife that befelled, Isaac called the name of that well False Challenge, or Esek, or Quarrel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZRA 4:6"> And they digged another well, and they strived also for that, and Isaac called that well Enmities, or Sitnah, or Enmity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 17:6; EXODUS 1:7; PSALMS 118:5"> And he went forth from thence, and digged another pit, for which they strived not, &#91;and&#93; therefore he called the name of that well Breadth, either Largeness; and said, Now God hath alarged us, and hath made us to increase on &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; GENESIS 21:31; GENESIS 46:1"> Isaac forsooth went up from that place into Beersheba,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 15:1; EXODUS 3:6; ISAIAH 43:1–2"> where the Lord appeared to him in that night; and said, I am &#91;the&#93; God of Abraham, thy father; do not thou dread, for I am with thee, and I shall bless thee, and I shall multiply thy seed for my servant Abraham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 13:18; PSALMS 116:17; GENESIS 12:7–8"> And so Isaac builded there an altar to the Lord; and when the name of the Lord was inwardly called, he stretched forth a tabernacle; and he commanded his servants that they should dig pits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 21:22–32; GENESIS 20:3"> And when Abimelech, and Ahuzzath, one of his friends, and Phicol, &#91;the&#93; duke of knights, had come from Gerar to that place,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 26:16; JUDGES 11:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:14"> Isaac spake to them, What came ye to me, a man whom ye have hated, and putted away from you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 21:22–23; ISAIAH 45:14; ISAIAH 61:9"> Which answered, We saw that God is with thee, and therefore we said now, An oath be betwixt us, and make we a covenant of peace,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 24:31; GENESIS 21:22; GENESIS 22:17"> that thou do not any &#91;thing of&#93; evil to us, as we touched nothing of thine, neither did that that hurted thee, but with peace we let go thee increased by the blessings of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 19:3; ROMANS 12:18; HEBREWS 12:14"> Therefore Isaac made them a feast; and after meat and drink,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:16–17; HEBREWS 6:16; GENESIS 25:33"> they rose early, and swore each to other; and Isaac let go them peaceably into their place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:4; PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 2:4–5"> Lo! forsooth in that day the servants of Isaac came, telling to him of the pit which they had digged, and said, We have found water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 21:31; GENESIS 26:28"> Wherefore Isaac called that pit Abundance or Shebah; and the name of the city was set Beersheba till into this present day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 36:2; GENESIS 36:13; EXODUS 34:16"> Esau forsooth forty years eld &#91;or old&#93; wedded two wives, Judith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="She is also known as Oholibamah or Aholibamah.">a</a></sup>, the daughter of Beeri Hittite, and Bashemath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="She is also known as Adah.">b</a></sup>, the daughter of Elon, of the same place;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 27:46–2; GENESIS 28:8; GENESIS 6:2"> which both offended the soul of Isaac and of Rebecca.</span>
</div>
