<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 18:4; LUKE 24:28–29; HEBREWS 13:2"> and said, My lords, I beseech, bow ye &#91;down&#93; into the house of your servant, and dwell ye there; wash ye your feet, and in the morrowtide ye shall go into your way. Which said, Nay, but we shall dwell in the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 18:6–8; JUDGES 6:19; 1ST SAMUEL 28:24"> He constrained them greatly, that they should turn in to him. And when they entered into his house, he made a feast, and baked therf bread, and they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 13:13; PROVERBS 4:16; EXODUS 23:2"> Forsooth before that they went to sleep, men of the city compassed his house, from a child till to an eld &#91;or old&#93; man, all the people together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 19:22; ISAIAH 3:9; JUDE 1:7"> and they called Lot, and said to him, Where be the men that entered to thee tonight? bring them out hither, that we know them, that is, by lechery against kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 19:23"> And Lot went out to them behind the back, and closed the door,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:9–11; JUDGES 19:23; LEVITICUS 20:13"> and said, I beseech, do not ye, my brethren, do not ye do this evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 19:24; GENESIS 19:31–38; JUDGES 9:15"> I have two daughters, that knew not yet a man; I shall lead out them to you, and mis-use ye them as it pleaseth you, so that ye do none evil to these men, for they entered under the shadow of my roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 2:14; 2ND PETER 2:7–8; GENESIS 13:12"> And they said, Go thou from hence. And again they said, Thou enteredest &#91;in&#93; hither as a comeling; whether that thou shalt deem us? therefore we shall torment thee more than these. And they did violently to Lot full greatly. Then it was nigh that they would break the doors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and lo! the men put forth their hands, and led in Lot to them, and they closed the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND KI 6:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:11; DEUTERONOMY 28:28–29"> And they smote with blindness they that were withoutforth, from the least till to the most; so that they might not find the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 7:1; 2ND PETER 2:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:4"> Forsooth they said to Lot, Hast thou here any man of thine, husband of thy daughter, or sons, or daughters; if so, lead thou out of this city all men that be thine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 18:20; MATTHEW 13:49–50; JUDE 1:7"> for we shall do away this place, for the cry of them increased before the Lord, which sent us that we lose them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:21; JEREMIAH 51:6; NUMBERS 16:45"> And Lot went out, and spake to the husbands to be of his daughters, that should take his daughters, and said, Rise ye, and go ye out of this place; for the Lord shall do away this city. And he was seen to them to speak as playing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:24–27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:4; GENESIS 19:22"> And when the morrowtide was, the angels constrained Lot, and said, Rise thou, and take thy wife, and thy two daughters, which thou hast, lest also thou perish altogether in the sin of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 9:15–16; TITUS 3:5; JOHN 6:44"> While he dissembled, they took his hand, and the hand of his wife, and of his two daughters; for the Lord spared him. And they led out him, and set &#91;or put&#93;him without the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:16–18; LUKE 9:62; PHILIPPIANS 3:13–14"> There they spake to him, and said, Save thou thy life; do not thou behold behind thy back, neither stand thou in all the country about, but make thee safe in the hills; lest also thou perish altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 13:6–8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:14; GENESIS 32:26"> And Lot said to them, My Lord, I beseech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 27:1; PSALMS 103:1–22; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:14–16"> for thy servant hath found grace before thee, and thou hast magnified thy grace and mercy, which thou hast done to me, that thou shouldest save my life; I may not be saved in the hills, lest peradventure evil overtake me, and I die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 119:175; PROVERBS 3:5–7; GENESIS 19:30"> a little city is here beside, to which I may flee, and I shall be safe therein; whether it is not such a little city? and my soul shall live therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 145:19; PSALMS 34:15; PSALMS 102:17"> And he said to Lot, Lo! also in this I have received thy prayers, that I destroy not the city, for which thou hast spoken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 13:10; GENESIS 14:2; GENESIS 32:25–28"> haste thee, and be thou saved there, for I may not do anything till thou enter &#91;in&#93; thither. Therefore the name of that city was called Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The sun rose on &#91;the&#93; earth, and Lot entered into Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:23; ISAIAH 13:19; AMOS 4:11"> Therefore the Lord rained on Sodom and Gomorrah brimstone and fire, from the Lord of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; GENESIS 14:3; GENESIS 13:10"> and destroyed these cities, and all the country about; he destroyed all the dwellers of those cities, and all green things of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 17:31–32; GENESIS 19:17; PROVERBS 14:14"> And Lot’s wife looked aback, and she was turned into an image of salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:1; PSALMS 5:3; HEBREWS 2:1"> Forsooth Abraham rising early, went to where he stood before with the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8"> beheld Sodom and Gomorrah, and all the land of that country; and he saw a dead spark going up from the earth, as the smoke of a furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 8:1; DEUTERONOMY 9:5; GENESIS 18:23–33"> For when God destroyed the cities of that country, he had mind of Abraham, and delivered Lot from &#91;the&#93; destroying of the cities in which he dwelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 19:19; ISAIAH 15:5; JAMES 1:8"> And Lot went up from Zoar, and dwelled in the hills, and his two daughters with him, for he dreaded to dwell in Zoar; and he dwelled in a den, he and his two daughters with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5; ISAIAH 4:1; GENESIS 16:2"> And the more daughter said to the less, Our father is eld &#91;or old&#93;, and no man is left on earth that may enter &#91;in&#93; to us, by the custom of all earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:6–7; GENESIS 11:3; GENESIS 9:21"> come thou, make we him drunken of wine, and sleep we with him, that we may keep the seed of our father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:15–16; PROVERBS 23:29–35; PROVERBS 20:1"> And so they gave to their father to drink wine in that night, and the more, or the elder, daughter entered, and slept with her father; and he feeled not, neither when the daughter lay down, neither when she &#91;a&#93; rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:12; JEREMIAH 6:15; ISAIAH 3:9"> And the tother day the more daughter said to the less, or the younger, Lo! I slept yesterday with my father; give we to him to drink wine also in this night; and thou sleep with him, that we save the seed of our father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:16; PSALMS 8:4; 1ST PETER 4:7"> And they gave to their father also in that night to drink wine, and the less daughter entered, and slept with him; and soothly he feeled not then when she lay down, neither when she &#91;a&#93; rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:15; JUDGES 1:7; MATTHEW 7:2"> Therefore the two daughters of Lot conceived of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:9; 2ND KI 3:1–27; NUMBERS 21:29"> And the more daughter childed a son, and called his name Moab; he is the father of men of Moab unto this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:19; JUDGES 10:6–40; PSALMS 83:4–8"> And the less daughter childed a son, and called his name Benammi, that is, The son of my people; he is the father of men of Ammon till to &#91;this&#93; day.</span>
