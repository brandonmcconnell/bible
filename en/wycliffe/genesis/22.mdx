<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:17; JAMES 2:21; DEUTERONOMY 8:2"> And after that these things were done, God assayed Abraham, and said to him, Abraham! Abraham! He answered, I am present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 3:16; ROMANS 8:32; HEBREWS 11:17"> God said to him, Take thine one begotten son, whom thou lovest, Isaac; and go into the land of vision, and offer thou him there into burnt sacrifice on one of the hills which I shall show to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; MATTHEW 10:37; LUKE 14:26"> Therefore Abraham rose by night, and saddled his ass, and led with him two young men, and Isaac his son; and when he had hewn trees into burnt sacrifice, he went to the place which God had commanded to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 13:32; MATTHEW 17:23; HOSEA 6:2"> Forsooth in the third day, he raised &#91;up&#93; his eyes, and saw a place afar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:19; HEBREWS 12:1"> and he said to his young men, Abide ye here with the ass, I and the child<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="According to Jewish tradition, Isaac was probably 25 years old at this time (Gehman/Josephus).">a</a></sup>shall go thither; and after that we have worshipped, we shall turn again to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 19:17; 1ST PETER 2:24; ISAIAH 53:6"> And he took the wood of burnt sacrifice, and laid it on Isaac his son; forsooth he bare fire, and a sword in his hands. And when they twain &#91;or two&#93; went together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:15; MATTHEW 26:39; GENESIS 8:20"> Isaac said to his father, My father! And he answered, What wilt thou, son? He said, Lo! fire and wood, where is the beast of burnt sacrifice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 1:36; JOHN 1:29; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:12"> Abraham said, My son, God shall purvey to him the beast of burnt sacrifice. Therefore they went together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 2:21; HEBREWS 11:17–19; PHILIPPIANS 2:7–8"> and came to the place which God had showed to him; in which place Abraham builded an altar, and dressed &#91;the&#93; wood above; and when he had bound altogether Isaac, his son, he laid Isaac on the altar, upon the heap of wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 2:21–23; HEBREWS 11:17–19; ISAIAH 53:6–12"> And he held forth his hand, and took the sword to sacrifice his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 21:17; EXODUS 3:4; GENESIS 16:9–10"> And lo! the angel of the Lord cried from heaven, and said, Abraham! Abraham! Which answered, I am present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JAMES 2:21–22; JAMES 2:18; 1ST SAMUEL 15:22"> And the angel said to him, Hold thou not forth thine hand on the child, neither do thou anything of harm to him; now I know that thou dreadest God, and sparedest not thine one begotten son for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:13; PSALMS 40:6–8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:9–10"> Abraham raised &#91;up&#93; his eyes, and he saw behind him a ram cleaving by the horns among briars, which he took, and offered as burnt sacrifice for the son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 22:13; EXODUS 17:15; GENESIS 22:8"> And he called the name of that place The Lord seeth; wherefore it is said, till to this day, The Lord shall see in the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Forsooth the angel of the Lord called to Abraham the second time from heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:13–14; LUKE 1:73; PSALMS 105:9"> and said, The Lord saith, I have sworn by myself, for thou hast done this thing, and hast not spared thine one begotten son for me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 15:5; GENESIS 24:60; JEREMIAH 33:22"> I shall bless thee, and I shall multiply thy seed as the stars of heaven, and as the gravel, either sand, which is in the brink of the sea; thy seed shall wield the gates of his enemies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:25; GENESIS 12:3; GALATIANS 3:16"> and all the folks of &#91;the&#93; earth shall be blessed in thy seed, for thou obeyedest to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 21:31; JUDGES 20:1; JOSHUA 15:28"> Abraham turned again to his young men, and they went to Beersheba together, and he dwelled there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 11:29; GENESIS 24:24; PROVERBS 25:25"> And so when these things were done, it was told to Abraham that also Milcah had borne sons to Nahor his brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 1:1; JOB 32:2; PSALMS 60:1–12"> Huz the first begotten, and Buz his brother, and Kemuel the father of Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and Chesed, and Hazo, and Pildash, and Jidlaph, and Bethuel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 24:15; GENESIS 28:5; GENESIS 24:51"> of whom Rebecca was born; Milcah childed these eight to Nahor, the brother of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 16:3; GENESIS 25:6; PROVERBS 15:25"> Forsooth his concubine, or secon-dary wife, Reumah by name, childed Tebah, and Gaham, and Thahash, and Maachah.</span>
