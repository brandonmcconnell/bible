<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 32:6–7; GENESIS 27:41–42; GENESIS 32:16"> Forsooth Jacob raised up his eyes, and saw Esau coming, and four hundred men with him; and he parted the sons of Leah, and of Rachel, and of both the servantesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 29:30; GENESIS 30:22–24; GENESIS 37:3"> And he put ever either handmaid, and the free children of them, in the beginning; soothly he put Leah, and her sons, in the second place; forsooth he put Rachel and Joseph the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 42:6; LUKE 14:11; GENESIS 18:2"> And Jacob went before, and worshipped or honoured lowly to the earth seven times, till his brother nighed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 45:14–15; PSALMS 34:4; LUKE 15:20"> And so Esau ran against his brother, and embraced him, and Esau held his neck, and kissed him, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 127:3; ISAIAH 8:18; GENESIS 48:9"> And when Esau’s eyes were raised up, he saw the women, and the little children of them, and said, What will these mean to themselves? and whether they pertain to thee? Jacob answered, They be the little children, which God hath given to me, thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the handmaids and their sons nighed, and were bowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Also Leah nighed with her free children; and when they had worshipped in like manner, Joseph and Rachel last worshipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:5; GENESIS 39:5; GENESIS 32:13–20"> And Esau said, What be these companies, which I met? And Jacob answered, That I should find grace before my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 27:39; PHILEMON 1:7; GENESIS 27:41"> And he said, My brother, I have full many things, thy things be to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 33:26; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:4; GENESIS 32:30"> And Jacob said, I beseech thee, do not thou so, but if I have found grace in thine eyes, take thou a little gift of mine hands; for I saw so thy face as if I had seen the cheer of God; be thou merciful to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:27; PHILIPPIANS 4:18; GENESIS 30:43"> and receive the blessing which I have brought to thee, and which blessing God giving all things gave to me. Scarcely desiring it, while the brother compelled, he received,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and said, Go we together, and I shall be a fellow of thy way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:11; EZEKIEL 34:15–16; EZEKIEL 34:23–25"> And Jacob said, My lord, thou knowest that I have little children tender, and sheep, and kine with calves with me, and if I shall make them for to travail more in going, all the flocks shall die in one day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 32:3; ISAIAH 40:11; ROMANS 15:1"> my lord go before his servant, and I shall pursue &#91;or follow&#93; little and little his steps, as I see that my little children be able, till I come to my lord, into Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="RUTH 2:13; GENESIS 47:25; GENESIS 34:11"> Esau answered, I pray thee, that of the people which is with me, dwell they namely fellows of thy way. Jacob said, It is no need; I have need to this one thing only, that I find grace in thy sight, my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And so Esau turned again in that day in the way by which he came, into Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 60:6; JOSHUA 13:27; JUDGES 8:5"> And Jacob came into Succoth, where when he had builded an house, and had set tents, he called the name of that place Succoth, that is, taber-nacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 9:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:16; JOSHUA 24:1"> And Jacob passed into Shalem, a city of Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, after that he turned again from Mesopotamia of Syria, and he dwelled beside the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:16; JOHN 4:5; JOSHUA 24:32"> And he bought for an hundred lambs a part of the field, in which he set tabernacles, of the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 35:7; GENESIS 21:33; GENESIS 32:28"> And when he had raised an altar there, he inwardly called on it the full strong God of Israel.</span>
