<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 10:10; GENESIS 10:22; ISAIAH 37:12"> Forsooth it was done in that time, that Amraphel, king of Shinar, and Arioch, king of Ellasar, and Chedor-laomer, king of Elamites, and Tidal, king of folks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 13:10; DEUTERONOMY 29:23; GENESIS 10:19"> began battle against Bera, king of Sodom, and against Birsha, king of Gomorrah, and against Shinab, king of Admah, and against Shemeber, king of Zeboiim, and against the king of Bela, that Bela is Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:16; DEUTERONOMY 3:17; NUMBERS 34:12"> All these came together into the valley of wood, which is now the sea of salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 9:25–26; EZEKIEL 17:15"> For in twelve years they served Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:11; GENESIS 15:20; DEUTERONOMY 1:4"> Therefore Chedorlaomer came in the fourteenth year, and &#91;the&#93; kings that were with him, and they smited Rephaims in Ashteroth Karnaim, and Zuzims with them, and Emims in Shaveh Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:22; DEUTERONOMY 2:12; GENESIS 21:21"> and Horites in the hills of Seir, till to the field places of Elparan, which is in wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 20:2; GENESIS 16:14; GENESIS 20:1"> And they turned again, and came till to the well of Mishpat; that is Kadesh. And they smited all the country of men of Amalek, and Amorites, that dwelled in Hazazontamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 19:22; GENESIS 13:10; GENESIS 19:20"> And the king of Sodom, and the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Zeboiim, also and the king of Bela, which is Zoar, went out, and &#91;they&#93; dressed battle array against them in the valley of wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> that is, against Chedorlaomer, king of Elamites, and Tidal, king of folks or of Goiim, and Amraphel, king of Shinar, and Arioch, king of Ellasar; four kings against five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 19:17; GENESIS 19:30; ISAIAH 24:18"> Forsooth the valley of wood had many pits of pitch, either strong glue; and so the king of Sodom and the king of Gomorrah turned their backs, and felled down there; and they that were left fled to the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 14:21; GENESIS 14:16; DEUTERONOMY 28:31"> Soothly they took away all the chattel &#91;or substance&#93; of Sodom and Gomorrah, and all things that pertain&#91;ed&#93; to meat, and went away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 12:5; GENESIS 11:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19"> also and they took away Lot and his chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, the son of the brother of Abram, which Lot dwelled in Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 13:18; GENESIS 14:24; GENESIS 40:15"> And, lo! one that escaped, told to Abram the Hebrew, that dwelled in the valley of Mamre of Amorites, &#91;the&#93; brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner; for these &#91;had&#93; made covenant of peace with Abram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 18:29; GENESIS 15:3; GENESIS 17:27"> And when Abram had heard this thing, that is, Lot, the son of his brother, was taken, he numbered his born servants made ready, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued them till to Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:2; PSALMS 112:5; 1ST KINGS 15:18"> And when his fellows were separated, he felled on them in the night, and smote them, and pursued them till to Hobah, and Phenice, which is at the left side of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 30:18–19; 1ST SAMUEL 30:8; GENESIS 12:2"> And he brought again all the chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, and Lot, the son of his brother, with his chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, also women, and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:18; PROVERBS 19:4; HEBREWS 7:1"> Soothly the king of Sodom went out into the meeting of him, after that he turned again from &#91;the&#93; slaying of Chedorlaomer, and of the kings that were with him, in the valley of Shaveh, which is the valley of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:20–3; PSALMS 110:4; HEBREWS 5:6"> And soothly Melchizedek, king of Salem, brought forth bread and wine, for he was the priest of the highest God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:6–7; GENESIS 14:22; PSALMS 24:1"> and he blessed Abram, and said, Blessed be Abram of &#91;the&#93; high God, that made heaven and earth of nought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 28:22; HEBREWS 7:4–9; MALACHI 3:10"> and blessed be &#91;the&#93; high God, by whom defending, thine enemies be betaken into thine hands. And Abram gave tithes of all things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Forsooth the king of Sodom said to Abram, Give thou the men to me; take thou other things to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 12:7; DANIEL 4:34; DEUTERONOMY 32:40"> And Abram answered to him, I raise &#91;up&#93; mine hand to the high Lord God, Lord of heaven and of earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND KI 5:16; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:9–12; ESTHER 9:15–16"> that from the thread of &#91;the&#93; woof till to the lanyard of the hose, I shall not take anything of all things that be thine, lest thou say, I &#91;have&#93; made Abram rich;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 14:13; PROVERBS 3:27; MATTHEW 7:12"> except these things which the young men ate, and the parts of men that came with me, Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre; these men shall take their parts.</span>
