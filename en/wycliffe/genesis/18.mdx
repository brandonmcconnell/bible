<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 14:13; GENESIS 48:3; GENESIS 13:18"> Forsooth in the valley of Mamre the Lord appeared to Abraham, sitting in the door of his tabernacle, in that heat of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:2; GENESIS 18:22; GENESIS 19:1"> And when Abraham had raised up his eyes, three men appeared to him, and stood nigh &#91;to&#93; him. And when he had seen them, he ran from the door of his tabernacle into the meeting of them, and he worshipped on &#91;the&#93; earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 32:5"> and said, Lord, if I have found grace in thine eyes, pass thou not thy servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 24:32; GENESIS 19:2; GENESIS 43:24"> but I shall bring a little water, and your feet be washed, and rest ye under the tree;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; JUDGES 13:15; JUDGES 6:18"> and I shall set before you a morsel of bread, and your heart be comforted; afterward ye shall pass &#91;forth&#93;; for therefore be ye bowed aside to your servant. Which said, Do thou as thou hast spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:33; HEBREWS 13:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:15"> Abraham hasted into the tabernacle, to Sarah, and said to her, Haste thou, mix three half bushels of clean flour; and make thou loaves baken under ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MALACHI 1:14; LUKE 15:27; LUKE 15:30"> Forsooth he ran to the drove of beasts, and took thereof a calf most tender and best, and gave to a servant, which hasted, and seethed the calf;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 19:3; JUDGES 13:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:20"> and he took butter, and milk, and the calf which he had sodden, and set before them; forsooth Abraham stood beside them under the tree. And when they had eaten,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TITUS 2:5; GENESIS 24:67; GENESIS 4:9"> they said to him, Where is Sarah thy wife? He answered, Lo! she is in the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 21:2; GENESIS 17:21; ROMANS 9:8–9"> To whom the Lord said, I shall turn again, and I shall come to thee in this time, if I live; and Sarah, thy wife, shall have a son. When this was heard, Sarah laughed behind the door of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 17:17; HEBREWS 11:11–12; LUKE 1:36"> Forsooth both were old, and of great age, and woman’s terms ceased to be made to Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:6; GENESIS 17:17; LUKE 1:18–20"> And she laughed, saying privily, After that I waxed eld &#91;or old&#93;, and my lord is eld &#91;or old&#93;, shall I give diligence &#91;or busyness&#93; to lust?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 2:25"> Forsooth the Lord said to Abraham, Why laughed Sarah, thy wife, saying, Whether I an eld &#91;or old&#93; woman shall bear a child verily?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 1:37; MATTHEW 19:26; MARK 10:27"> whether anything is hard to God? By the promise I shall turn again to thee in this same time, if I live; and Sarah shall have a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:8; PROVERBS 28:13; EPHESIANS 4:23"> Sarah was afeared for dread, and denied, saying, I laughed not. Forsooth the Lord said, It is not so, but thou laughedest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:38"> Therefore when the men had risen from thence, they dressed the eyes against Sodom; and Abraham went together, leading them forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; PSALMS 25:14; JOHN 15:15"> And the Lord said, Whether I may cover from Abraham what things I shall do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:8; GENESIS 26:4; GALATIANS 3:14"> since he shall be into a great folk and most strong, and all nations of &#91;the&#93; earth shall be blessed in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:6–7; DEUTERONOMY 4:9–10; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:4–5"> For I know that Abraham shall command his children, and his house after him, that they keep the way of the Lord, and that they do rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; and doom, that the Lord bring for Abraham all things which he spake to Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 19:13; EZEKIEL 16:49–50; ISAIAH 3:9"> And so the Lord said, The cry of men of Sodom and of men of Gomorrah is multiplied, and their sin is egregious greatly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 3:8; GENESIS 11:5; ZEPHANIAH 1:12"> I shall come down, and see whether they have &#91;ful&#93; filled in work the cry that came to me, that I know whether it is not so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 19:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:55; EZEKIEL 22:30"> And they turned them&#91;selves&#93; from thence, and went to Sodom. Abraham soothly stood yet before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:22; 2ND SAMUEL 24:17; GENESIS 20:4"> and nighed, and said, Whether thou shalt lose a just man &#91;or the rightwise&#93; with the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:1; ISAIAH 1:9; MATTHEW 7:13–14"> if fifty just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men be in the city, shall they perish altogether, and shalt thou not spare that place for fifty just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men, if they be therein?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOB 8:3; ISAIAH 3:10–11; JOB 8:20"> Far be it from thee that thou do this thing, and slay the just &#91;or rightwise&#93; with the wicked, and that a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man be made as a wicked man; this is not thine that deemest all &#91;the&#93; earth; thou shalt not make this doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:1; MATTHEW 24:22; ISAIAH 65:8"> And the Lord said to him, If I shall find in Sodom fifty just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men in the midst of the city, I shall forgive to all the place for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 3:19; ISAIAH 6:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:47–48"> Abraham answered and said, For I began once, I shall speak to my Lord, since I am dust and ashes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 18:29; 1ST KINGS 20:32–33; GENESIS 18:26"> what if less than fifty just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men by five be, shalt thou do away all the city for five and forty? And the Lord said, I shall not do away, if I shall find five and forty there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:18; HEBREWS 4:16"> And again Abraham said to him, But if forty be there, what shalt thou do? The Lord said, I shall not smite for forty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 89:7; PSALMS 9:12; ESTHER 4:11–16"> Abraham said, Lord, I beseech, take thou not into indignation, if I speak; what if thirty be found there? The Lord answered, I shall not do, if I shall find thirty there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKE 11:8; GENESIS 18:27; LUKE 18:1"> Abraham said, For I began once, I shall speak to my Lord; what if twenty be found there? The Lord said, I shall not slay for twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JAMES 5:15–17; JUDGES 6:39; MATTHEW 7:7"> Abraham said, Lord, I beseech, be thou not wroth, if I speak yet once more; what if ten be found there? The Lord said, I shall not do away for ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 18:22; GENESIS 32:26; GENESIS 31:55"> The Lord went forth, after that he &#91;had&#93; ceased to speak to Abraham, and Abraham turned again into his place.</span>
