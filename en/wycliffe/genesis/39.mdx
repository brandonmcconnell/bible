<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 37:28; PSALMS 105:17; GENESIS 37:36"> Therefore Joseph was led into Egypt, and Potiphar, a gelding of Pharaoh, prince of the host, a man of Egypt, bought him of the hand of Ishmaelites, of which he was brought thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 18:14; PSALMS 91:15; JEREMIAH 15:20"> And the Lord was with him, and he was a man doing with prosperity in all things. And Joseph dwelled in the house of his lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 1:3; GENESIS 30:27; MATTHEW 5:16"> which knew full well that the Lord was with Joseph, and that all things which he did, were dressed of the Lord in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 39:21–22; PROVERBS 27:18; GENESIS 19:19"> And Joseph found grace before his lord, and he served him, of whom Joseph was made sovereign of all things, and he governed the house betaken to him, and all things that were betaken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 30:27; 2ND SAMUEL 6:11–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:24"> And the Lord blessed the Egyptian’s house for Joseph, and multiplied all his chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, as well in houses, as in fields;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:12; LUKE 19:17; PROVERBS 31:11"> neither he knew any other thing, but &#91;the&#93; bread which he ate. Forsooth Joseph was fair in face, and shapely in sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 13:11; EZEKIEL 16:34; 2ND PETER 2:14"> And so after many days the lady of his lord cast her eyes into Joseph, and said, Sleep thou with me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:26–28; PROVERBS 22:14; PROVERBS 9:13–18"> which assented not to the unleave-ful work, and said to her, Lo! while all things be betaken to me, my lord wot not what he hath in his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:13; GENESIS 20:6; GENESIS 42:18"> neither anything is, which is not in my power, either which he hath not betaken to me, except thee, which art his wife; how therefore may I do this evil, and do sin against my lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:11; GENESIS 39:8; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:22"> They spake such words by all days or by long time, and the woman was dis-easeful to the young man, and he forsook the adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:12; EPHESIANS 5:3; MALACHI 3:5"> Forsooth it befelled in a day, that Joseph entered into the house, and did some work without witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:22; PROVERBS 7:13–27; ECCLESIASTES 7:26"> And she took the hem of his cloth, and she said, Sleep thou with me; and he left the mantle in her hand, and he fled, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when the woman had seen the cloth in her hands, and that she was despised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 10:21; 1ST PETER 2:20; PSALMS 35:11"> she called to her the men of her house, and said to them, Lo! my lord hath brought in an Hebrew man, that he should scorn us; he entered to me to do lechery with me, and when I cried,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and he heard my voice, he left the mantle which I held, and he fled out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 37:32; JEREMIAH 4:22; JEREMIAH 9:3–5"> Therefore into proving of the truth, she showed the mantle, that she held, to her husband turning again home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 23:1; 1ST KINGS 18:17; PSALMS 37:14"> And she said, The Hebrew servant, whom thou broughtest in, entered to me to scorn me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and when he saw me cry, he left the mantle that I held, and he fled out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 4:5–6; PROVERBS 18:17; PROVERBS 6:34–35"> And when these things were heard, the lord believed over much to the words of the wife, and he was full wroth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 40:15; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:9; PSALMS 105:18–19"> and he betook Joseph into prison, where the bound men of the king were kept, and he was enclosed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 3:21; ISAIAH 41:10; 1ST PETER 3:17"> Forsooth the Lord was with Joseph, and had mercy on him, and gave grace to him, in the sight of the prince of the prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 40:3–4; GENESIS 39:4; 1ST SAMUEL 2:30"> which betook in the hand of Joseph all the prisoners that were holden in keeping, and whatever thing was done, it was done under Joseph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:2; GENESIS 39:2–3; PSALMS 1:3"> neither the prince knew anything, for all things were betaken to Joseph; for the Lord was with him, and dressed all his works.</span>
