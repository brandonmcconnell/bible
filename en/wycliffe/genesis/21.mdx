<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 12:6; GENESIS 18:10; GENESIS 18:14"> Forsooth God visited Sarah, as he promised, and &#91;ful&#93; filled those things, that he spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:22; HEBREWS 11:11; GENESIS 18:10"> And she conceived, and childed a son in her eld age, in the time wherein God before-said to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 17:19; ROMANS 9:7; GENESIS 21:6"> And Abraham called the name of his son, whom Sarah childed to him, Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:8; DEUTERONOMY 12:32; JOHN 7:22–23"> And Abraham circumcised him in the eighth day, as God commanded to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 17:17; ROMANS 4:19; GENESIS 17:1"> when he was of an hundred years; for Isaac was born in this age of the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2; ISAIAH 54:1; GENESIS 17:17"> And Sarah said, The Lord hath made laughing to me, and whoever shall hear shall laugh with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 18:11–12; ISAIAH 66:8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:10"> And again she said, Who should hear, and believe to Abraham, that Sarah should give sucking to a son, whom she childed to him, when he is now an eld &#91;or old&#93; man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 1:22; HOSEA 1:8; GENESIS 40:20"> Therefore the child increased, and was weaned; and Abraham made a great feast in the day of his weaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:29; GENESIS 16:15; GALATIANS 4:22"> And when Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, playing, or doing idolatry, with Isaac her son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 8:35; 1ST JOHN 2:19; GALATIANS 4:22–31"> she said to Abraham, Cast out this handmaid and her son; for the son of the handmaid shall not be heir with my son Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:11; GENESIS 17:18; MATTHEW 10:37"> Abraham took this heavily for his son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:18; ROMANS 9:7–8; GENESIS 17:19"> and God said to him, Be it not seen sharp to thee on the child, and on thine handmaid; all things which Sarah saith to thee, hear thou her voice, for in Isaac seed shall be called to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 16:10; GENESIS 17:20; GENESIS 21:18"> but also I shall make the son of the handmaid into a great folk, for he is thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:10; PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 16:7"> And so Abraham rose early, and took bread, and a bottle of water, and put it on Hagar’s shoulder, and he betook to her the child<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ishmael would be about 15 years old at this time.">a</a></sup>, and let go her; and when she had gone, she went out of the way in the wilderness of Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND KI 3:9; PSALMS 63:1; JEREMIAH 14:3"> And when the water in the bottle was ended, she cast away the child under a tree that was there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:15; GENESIS 29:11; 1ST SAMUEL 24:16"> and she went away, and she sat even against, as far as a bow may cast; for she said, I shall not see the child dying; and she sat against, and raised &#91;up&#93; her voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 16:11; PSALMS 50:15; EXODUS 3:7"> Forsooth the Lord heard the voice of the child, and the angel of the Lord called Hagar from heaven, and said, What doest thou, Hagar? do not thou dread, for God hath heard the voice of the child, from the place wherein he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 21:13; GENESIS 16:10; GENESIS 17:20"> Rise thou, and take the child, and hold his hand; for I shall make him into a great folk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:31; 2ND KI 6:17–20; ISAIAH 35:5–6"> And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water, and she went, and filled the bottle, and she gave drink to the child;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 28:15; GENESIS 39:21; GENESIS 16:12"> and God was with him, and he increased, and dwelled in wilderness, and he was made a young man an archer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 26:34–35; GENESIS 24:3–4; NUMBERS 12:16"> and dwelled in the desert of Paran; and his mother took to him a wife of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:7; GENESIS 26:26; GENESIS 26:28"> In the same time, Abimelech, and Phicol, prince of his host, said to Abraham, God is with thee in all things that thou doest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:12; GENESIS 20:14; HEBREWS 6:16"> therefore swear thou by God that thou harm not me, and mine heirs, and my kindred; but by the mercy which I did to thee, do thou to me, and to the land in which thou livedest as a comeling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:16; GENESIS 14:13; ROMANS 12:18"> And Abraham said, I shall swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 26:15–22; GENESIS 29:8; PROVERBS 25:9"> And he blamed Abimelech for the well of water, which his servants took away by violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 13:7; 2ND KI 5:20–24"> And Abimelech answered, I wist not who did this thing, but also thou showedest not to me, and I heard not except today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 14:22–23; ISAIAH 32:8; ROMANS 1:31"> And so Abraham took sheep and oxen, and gave to Abimelech, and both smote together a bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock asides half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:14; GENESIS 33:8; EXODUS 12:26"> And Abimelech said to him, What will these seven ewe lambs mean to themselves, which thou madest stand asides half?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 31:52; JOSHUA 24:27; JOSHUA 22:27–28"> And he said, Thou shalt take of mine hand seven ewe lambs, that those &#91;or they&#93; be into witnessing to me, for I digged this well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 26:33; GENESIS 21:14; 1ST KINGS 4:25"> Therefore that place was called Beersheba, that is, The Well of the Oath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Beersheba also means ‘the Well of the Seven’.">b</a></sup>, for ever either swore there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 13:17; GENESIS 21:27; JUDGES 13:1"> and they made bond of peace for the well of an oath. Forsooth Abimelech rose, and Phicol, the prince of his chivalry, and they turned again into the land of Palestines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:17; ISAIAH 40:28; PSALMS 90:2"> Soothly Abraham planted a wood in Beersheba, and inwardly called there the name of &#91;the&#93; everlasting God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:15; 1ST PETER 2:11; HEBREWS 11:9"> and he was an earth-tiller, or a comeling, of the land of Palestines in many days.</span>
