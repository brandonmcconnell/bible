<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:13; GENESIS 1:18; ECCLESIASTES 11:7"> And God saw the light, that it was good, and he parted the light from darknesses;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 74:16; ISAIAH 45:7; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:5"> and he called the light, day, and the darknesses, night. And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, one day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:15; PSALMS 33:6; JOB 37:18"> And God said, The firmament be made in the midst of waters, and part waters from waters.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 1:5; GENESIS 1:19; GENESIS 1:10"> And God called the firmament, heaven. And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the second day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:5; JONAH 1:9; JEREMIAH 5:22"> Forsooth God said, The waters, that be under heaven, be gathered into one place, and a dry place appear; and it was done so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; PSALMS 104:31; GENESIS 1:4"> And God called the dry place, earth; and he called the gatherings together of waters, the seas. And God saw that it was good;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14–17; HEBREWS 6:7; PSALMS 147:8"> and said, The earth bring forth green herb, and making seed, and an apple tree making fruit by his kind, whose seed be in itself, on &#91;the&#93; earth; and it was done so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 4:28; ISAIAH 55:10–11; MATTHEW 13:24–26"> And the earth brought forth green herb and making seed by his kind, and a tree making fruit, and each having seed by his kind. And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the third day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 104:19–20; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; JEREMIAH 31:35"> Forsooth God said, Lights be made in the firmament of heaven, and part they the day and night; and be they into signs, and times, and days, and years;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and shine those in the firmament of heaven, and lighten they the earth; and it was done so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 136:7–9; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; PSALMS 8:3"> And God made two great lights, the greater light that it should be before to the day, and the lesser light that it should be before to the night; and God made stars;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 8:3; PSALMS 8:1; JOB 38:12"> and setted them in the firmament of heaven, that they should shine on &#91;the&#93; earth,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:35; PSALMS 19:6"> and that they should be before to the day and &#91;to the&#93; night, and should part light and darkness. And God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And the eventide and the morrow-tide was made, the fourth day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 2:19; PSALMS 104:24–25; 1ST KINGS 4:33"> Also God said, The waters bring forth a reptile, either a creeping beast, of living soul, and a volatile, either a bird flying above &#91;the&#93; earth, under the firmament of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 104:24–26; GENESIS 1:25; GENESIS 8:19"> And God made of nought great whales, and each living soul and movable, which the waters have brought forth in their kinds; and God made of nought each volatile by his kind. And God saw that it was good;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 40:15; GENESIS 8:17; PSALMS 107:38"> and blessed them, and said, Wax ye, and be ye multiplied, and fill ye the waters of the sea, and &#91;the&#93; birds be multiplied on &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the fifth day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 50:9–10; GENESIS 6:20; GENESIS 8:19"> And God said, The earth bring forth a living soul in his kind, work beasts, and reptiles, either creeping beasts, and unreasonable beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, by their kinds; and it was done so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:5; JOB 12:8–10; GENESIS 2:19–20"> And God made unreasonable beasts of the earth by their kinds, and work beasts, and each creeping beast of the earth in his kind. And God saw that it was good;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:10; EPHESIANS 4:24; PSALMS 8:4–8"> and said, Make we man to our image and likeness, and be he sovereign to the fishes of the sea, and to the volatiles of heaven, and to &#91;the&#93; unreasonable beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, and to each creature, and to each creeping beast or each reptile, which is moved in &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:4; EPHESIANS 2:10; GENESIS 5:1–2"> And God made of nought a man to his image and likeness; God made of nought a man, to the image of God; God made of nought them, male and female.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 9:1; GENESIS 9:7; LEVITICUS 26:9"> And God blessed them, and said, Increase ye, and be ye multiplied, and fill ye the earth, and make ye it subject; and be ye lords to the fishes of the sea, and to &#91;the&#93; volatiles of heaven, and to all living beasts that be moved on &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 9:3; PSALMS 104:14–15; PSALMS 145:15–16"> And God said, Lo! I have given to you each herb bearing seed on &#91;the&#93; earth, and all trees that have in themselves the seed of their kind, that they be into meat to you;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14; PSALMS 145:15–16; PSALMS 147:9"> and to all living beasts of &#91;the&#93; earth, and to each bird of heaven, and to all things that be moved in &#91;the&#93; earth, and in which is a living soul, that they have to eat; and it was done so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 4:4; PSALMS 104:24; PSALMS 19:1–2"> And God saw all things which he made, and they were full good. And the eventide and the morrowtide was made, the sixth day.</span>
