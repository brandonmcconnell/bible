<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 17:8; GENESIS 23:4; GENESIS 36:7"> Forsooth Jacob dwelled in the land of Canaan, in which his father was a pilgrim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 35:25–26; GENESIS 35:22; 1ST SAMUEL 2:22–24"> and these were the generations of him. Joseph when he was of sixteen years, yet a child, kept a flock with his brethren, and he was with the sons of Bilhah and Zilpah, the wives of his father; and he accused his brethren at the father of the worst sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 3:35; GENESIS 37:32; 2ND SAMUEL 13:18"> Forsooth Israel loved Joseph above all his sons, for he had begotten him in his eld age; and he made to Joseph a coat of many colours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 27:41; 1ST JOHN 3:12; 1ST JOHN 4:20"> Forsooth his brethren saw that he was loved of the father more than all they, and they hated him, and might not speak anything peaceably to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 28:12; GENESIS 42:9; JOHN 17:14"> And it befelled that he told to his brethren a sweven that he saw, which cause was the seed of more hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 44:18; JUDGES 9:7"> And Joseph said to his brethren, Hear ye the sweven which I saw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 44:14; GENESIS 42:6; GENESIS 43:26"> I guessed that we bound together sheaves, or handfuls, &#91;in the field&#93;, and that as mine handful rose up, and stood upright, and that your handfuls stood about, and worshipped or honoured mine handful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 10:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:35; HEBREWS 10:29"> His brethren answered, Whether thou shalt be our king, either we shall be made subject to thy lordship? Therefore this cause of dreams and words ministered the nourishing of envy, and of hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:9–14; GENESIS 37:7; DANIEL 8:10"> Also Joseph saw another sweven, which he told to his brethren, and said, I saw a dream that as the sun, and the moon, and the eleven stars worshipped me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 27:29; PHILIPPIANS 2:10–11; ISAIAH 60:14"> And when he had told this dream to his father, and his brethren, his father blamed him, and said, What will this dream mean to itself that thou hast seen? Whether I, and thy mother, and thy brethren, shall worship thee on earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:9; LUKE 2:19; LUKE 2:51"> Therefore his brethren had envy to him. Forsooth the father beheld privily the thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 37:1; GENESIS 33:18; GENESIS 34:25–31"> and when his brethren dwelled in Shechem, about &#91;the&#93; keeping of &#91;the&#93; flocks of their father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:1–3; 1ST SAMUEL 3:8; 1ST SAMUEL 3:16"> Israel said to Joseph, Thy brethren keep sheep in Shechem; come thou, I shall send thee to them. And when Joseph answered, I am ready,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 35:27; LUKE 19:42; PSALMS 125:5"> Israel said, Go thou, and see whether all things be welsome with thy brethren, and the sheep; and then tell thou to me what is done. And so he was sent from the valley of Hebron, and came into Shechem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 4:27; JUDGES 4:22; GENESIS 21:14"> and a man found him erring in the field, and the man asked him, what he sought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; SONG OF SONGS 1:7"> And he answered, I seek my brethren; show thou to me where they keep their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 6:13"> And the man said to him, They went away from this place; forsooth I heard them saying, Go we into Dothan. And Joseph went after his brethren, and found them in Dothan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:12; PSALMS 37:12; PSALMS 37:32"> And when they had seen him afar, before he nighed to them, they thought to slay him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 28:12; GENESIS 37:11; GENESIS 37:5"> and they spake together, Lo! the dreamer cometh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TITUS 3:3; PROVERBS 27:4; MATTHEW 2:2–16"> come ye, slay we him, and put we him into an eld &#91;or old&#93; cistern, and we shall say, A wild beast full wicked hath devoured him; and then it shall appear what his dreams profit to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 42:22; GENESIS 35:22; JOSHUA 10:28"> Soothly Reuben heard this, and enforced or endeavoured to deliver him from their hands, and said, Slay we not the life of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 22:12; DEUTERONOMY 13:9; GENESIS 42:22"> neither shed we out his blood, but cast ye him into an eld &#91;or old&#93; cistern, which is in the wilderness, and keep ye your hands guiltless. Forsooth he said this, willing to deliver him from their hands, and to yield him to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 37:3; MATTHEW 27:28; GENESIS 42:21"> Therefore anon as Joseph came to his brethren, they despoiled him of his coat, that went down to the heel, and was of many colours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:6; PSALMS 130:1–2; ZECHARIAH 9:11"> and they put him in&#91;to&#93; an eld &#91;or old&#93; cistern, that had no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 37:28; GENESIS 43:11; JEREMIAH 8:22"> And they sat to eat bread; and they saw that Ishmaelite way-goers came from Gilead, and that their camels bare sweet smelling spiceries, and resin, and stacte, into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 37:20; GENESIS 4:10; GENESIS 25:32"> Therefore Judah said to his brethren, What shall it profit to us, if we shall slay our brother, and shall hide his blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; GENESIS 42:21; 1ST SAMUEL 18:17"> It is better that he be sold to Ishmaelites, and our hands be not defouled, for he is our brother and our flesh. His brethren assented to these words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:9; PSALMS 105:17; GENESIS 45:4–5"> and when &#91;the&#93; merchants of Midian passed thereforth, they drew Joseph out of the cistern, and sold him to Ishmaelites, for twenty pieces of silver; which led him into Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 1:20; GENESIS 37:34; GENESIS 44:13"> And Reuben turned again to the cistern, and found not the child; and he rent his clothes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 42:13; JEREMIAH 31:15; GENESIS 42:32"> and he went to his brethren, and said, The child appeareth not, and whither shall I go?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 37:23; PROVERBS 28:13; GENESIS 37:3"> Forsooth they took his coat, and dipped it in the blood of a kid, which they had slain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 15:30; GENESIS 37:3; GENESIS 44:20–23"> and they sent men that bare it to their father, and said, We have found this coat; see thou, whether it is the coat of thy son, or nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 37:20; GENESIS 44:28; JOHN 13:7"> And when their father had known it, he said, It is the coat of my son; a wild beast full wicked hath eaten him; a beast hath devoured Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 37:29; 2ND SAMUEL 3:31; PSALMS 69:11"> And he rent his clothes, and he was clothed with an hair-shirt, and bewailed his son in much time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 42:38; JOB 2:11; 2ND SAMUEL 12:17"> Soothly when his free children were gathered together, that they should appease the sorrow of their father, he would not take comfort; but said, I shall go down into hell, and shall bewail my son. And while Jacob continued in weeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GENESIS 40:4; GENESIS 37:28; 2ND KI 25:8"> Midianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar, a chaste and honest servant &#91;or the gelding&#93; of Pharaoh, master of the chivalry.</span>
</div>
