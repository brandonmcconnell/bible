<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 91:11; PSALMS 34:7; HEBREWS 1:4"> Forsooth Jacob went forth in the way in which he began, and the angels of the Lord met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 2:8; JOSHUA 5:14; JOSHUA 21:38"> And when he had seen them, he said, These be the castles of God; and he called the name of that place Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 25:30; JOSHUA 24:4; DEUTERONOMY 2:5"> Soothly Jacob sent before him also messengers to Esau, his brother, into the land of Seir, in the country of Edom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:6; LUKE 14:11; GENESIS 33:8"> and he commanded to them, and said, Thus speak ye to my lord Esau, Thy brother Jacob saith these things, I have been a pilgrim at Laban, and I was till into this present day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 33:8; GENESIS 33:15; GENESIS 33:11"> I have oxen, and asses, and sheep, and menservants, and handmaids, and I send now a message to my lord, that I find grace in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 33:1; GENESIS 32:8; GENESIS 32:11"> And the messengers turned again to Jacob, and said, We came to Esau, thy brother, and lo! he hasteth him into thy coming, with four hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 35:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:8–10; EXODUS 14:10"> Jacob dreaded greatly, and he was afeared, and he parted the people that was with him, and he parted the flocks, and sheep, and oxen, and camels, into two companies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:16; GENESIS 33:1–3"> and he said, If Esau shall come to one company, and shall smite it, the other company which is left unsmitten, shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 31:42; PSALMS 34:4–6; GENESIS 31:3"> And Jacob said, O! God of my father Abraham, and God of my father Isaac, O! Lord, that saidest to me, Turn thou again into thy land, and to the place of thy birth, and I shall do well to thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 18:35; GENESIS 24:27; 2ND SAMUEL 7:18"> I am less than all thy merciful doings, and than thy truth which thou hast &#91;ful&#93; filled to thy servant; with my staff I passed over this Jordan, and now I go again with two companies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 59:1–2; PROVERBS 18:19; MATTHEW 6:13"> deliver thou me from the hand of my brother Esau, for I dread him greatly, lest he come and smite or slay the mothers with the sons &#91;or with the children&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 28:13–15; HEBREWS 6:17; TITUS 1:2"> Thou spakest that thou shouldest do well to me, and wouldest alarge my seed as &#91;the&#93; gravel of the sea, that may not be numbered for muchliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:16; GENESIS 43:11; PROVERBS 19:6"> And when Jacob had slept there in that night, he separated of those things which he had as gifts to Esau, his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 1:3; DEUTERONOMY 8:18; JOB 42:12"> two hundred she goats, and twenty bucks of goats, two hundred sheep, and twenty rams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> camels full with their foals thirty, forty kine, and twenty bulls, twenty she-asses, and &#91;the&#93; ten foals of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 33:8–9; GENESIS 32:20; PROVERBS 2:11"> And he sent by the hands of his servants all the flocks by themselves; and he said to his servants, Go ye before me, and a space be betwixt flock and flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 33:3"> And he commanded to the former or first, and said, If thou shalt meet my brother Esau, and he shall ask thee, whose man thou art, or whither thou goest, or whose be these things which thou followest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 32:4–5"> thou shalt answer, Of thy servant Jacob; he hath sent gifts to his lord Esau, and he cometh after us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In like manner, he gave command-ments to the second, and to the third, and to all that pursued &#91;or followed&#93; the flocks; and said, Speak ye by the same words to Esau, when ye find him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 42:8–9; PROVERBS 21:14; GENESIS 43:11"> and ye shall add, Also Jacob himself thy servant pursueth &#91;or followeth&#93; our way. For Jacob said, I shall please Esau with gifts that go before, and afterward I shall see him; in hap he shall be merciful to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And so the gifts went before him; soothly he dwelled in that night in the tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:16; DEUTERONOMY 2:37; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:8"> And when Jacob had risen hastily, he took his two wives, and so many handmaids, with his eleven sons, and he passed over the ford of Jabbok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when all things that pertained to him were led over,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HOSEA 12:3–5; EPHESIANS 6:12; LUKE 13:24"> Jacob dwelled there alone, and, lo! a man came, and wrestled with him till to the morrowtide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:7–9; HOSEA 12:3–4; MATTHEW 26:41"> And when the man saw that he might not overcome Jacob, he touched the sinew of Jacob’s hip, and it dried anon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSEA 12:4; PSALMS 115:12–13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:8–9"> And he said to Jacob, Let go thou me, for the morrowtide goeth up now. Jacob answered, I shall not let go thee, no but thou bless me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Therefore he said, What name is to thee? He answered, Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:17; GENESIS 35:10; ISAIAH 62:2–4"> And the man said, Thy name shall no more be called Jacob, but Israel; for if thou were strong against God, how much more shalt thou have power against men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:6; JUDGES 13:16–18; JOB 11:7"> Jacob asked him, Say thou to me by what name thou art called? He answered, Why askest thou my name, which is wonderful? And he blessed Jacob in the same place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 24:10–11; NUMBERS 12:8; ISAIAH 6:5"> And Jacob called the name of that place Penuel, and said, I saw the Lord face to face, and my life is made safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:7; GENESIS 19:15; JUDGES 8:8"> And anon the sun rose to him, after that he had passed over from Penuel; forsooth he halted in the foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:5"> For which cause the sons of Israel eat not unto this present day the sinew, like that that dried in the hip of Jacob; for the man touched the sinew of Jacob’s hip, and it dried up.</span>
