<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 12:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:11–13; LAMENTATIONS 5:10"> In the meantime hunger oppressed greatly all the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:6–8; PROVERBS 16:18; PROVERBS 15:16"> and when the meats were wasted or consumed, which they &#91;had&#93; brought from Egypt, Jacob said to his sons, Turn ye again, and buy ye a little of meats to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 44:23; GENESIS 43:5; 2ND SAMUEL 3:13"> Judah answered, That man announced to us, under witnessing of an oath, and said, Ye shall not see my face, if ye shall not bring with you your least brother;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> therefore if thou wilt send him with us, we shall go down together, and we shall buy necessaries to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12; GENESIS 44:26; GENESIS 42:38"> else if thou wilt not, we shall not go down; for as we said oft, the man announced to us, and said, Ye shall not see my face without your least brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Forsooth Israel said to them, Ye did this into my wretchedness, that ye showed to him, that ye had also another brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 43:3; GENESIS 42:13; GENESIS 43:27"> And they answered, The man asked us by order our generation, if our father lived, if we had another brother; and we answered pursuingly to him, by that that he asked; whether we might know that he would say, Bring ye your brother with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 42:2; DEUTERONOMY 33:6; GENESIS 45:19"> And Judah said to his father, Send the child with me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Benjamin would have been over 30 years old at this time.">a</a></sup>, that we go, and may live, lest we die, and thou, and our little children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 42:37; PHILEMON 1:18–19; HEBREWS 7:22"> I shall take the child, require thou him again of mine hand; if I shall not bring him again, and betake him to thee, I shall be guilty of sin against thee in all time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 19:16"> if this delay, or tarrying, had not been, we had come thence now another time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 37:25; JEREMIAH 8:22; EZEKIEL 27:17"> Therefore Israel, their father, said to them, If it is need so to be, do ye that that ye will; take ye of the best fruits of the land in your vessels, and bear ye gifts to the man, a little of gum, and of honey, and of storax, that is, a precious gum, and of myrrh, that is, a bitter gum, and of terebinth, that is, best resins, and of almonds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 42:25; GENESIS 42:35; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:6"> and bear ye with you double money, and bear ye again that money which ye found in &#91;the&#93; bags, lest peradventure it be done by error, or unwittingly, or by negligence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 42:38"> but also take ye your brother, and go ye to the man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ESTHER 4:16; GENESIS 17:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:14"> forsooth my God Almighty make him peaceable or pleasable, or queme-ful, to you, and send he again your brother, whom he holdeth in bonds, and also this Benjamin; forsooth I shall be now as made bare without sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore the men took gifts, and double money, and Benjamin; and they went down into Egypt, and stood before Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 44:1; GENESIS 43:19; GENESIS 24:2–10"> And when he had seen them and Benjamin together, he commanded the dispenser of his house, and said, Lead these men into the house, and slay some beasts, and make a feast; for they shall eat with me today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He did as it was commanded or as it was bidden to him, and he led the men into the house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 42:35; ROMANS 7:8; GENESIS 42:21"> and there they were afeared, and they said together, We be brought in for the money which we bare again before in our sacks, that he put challenge against us, and make subject by violence to servage both us and our asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Wherefore they nighed in the gates, and spake to the dispenser,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 42:3; GENESIS 42:10; GENESIS 42:27"> Lord, we pray, that thou hear us; we came down now before that we should buy meats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 43:12; GENESIS 42:27–35; ROMANS 13:8"> when those were bought, and we headed home, when we came to the inn, we opened our bags, and we found the money in the mouths of our sacks, which money we have brought again now in the same weight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> but also we have brought other silver, that we can buy those things that be needful to us; it is not in our knowing who put the money in our purses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 20:26; GENESIS 42:24; JOHN 14:27"> And he answered, Peace be to you, do not ye dread; your God, and &#91;the&#93; God of your father, gave to you treasures in your bags; for I have the money proved, which ye gave to me. And he led out Simeon to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 18:4; GENESIS 24:32; LUKE 7:44"> and when they were brought into the house, he brought water, and they washed their feet, and he gave their asses meats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 43:11; GENESIS 43:16"> Soothly they made ready their gifts till Joseph entered at midday, for they had heard that they should eat bread there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:10–11; GENESIS 27:29; GENESIS 42:6"> Therefore Joseph entered into his house, and they offered gifts to him, and held them in their hands, and worshipped low to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 42:13; 1ST SAMUEL 25:5; 1ST SAMUEL 17:22"> And he greeted them again meekly; and he asked them, and said, Whether your father, the eld &#91;or old&#93; man, is safe, of whom ye said to me? liveth he yet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 37:7; GENESIS 43:26; 2ND SAMUEL 14:4"> Which answered, He is whole, thy servant our father liveth yet; and they were bowed, and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 42:13; NUMBERS 6:25; GENESIS 30:22–24"> Forsooth Joseph raised &#91;up&#93; his eyes, and saw Benjamin, his brother of the same womb, and he said, Is this your youngest brother, of whom ye said to me? And again Joseph said, My son, God have mercy on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 42:24; JEREMIAH 31:20; 1ST KINGS 3:26"> And Joseph hasted into another part of the house, for his entrails were moved on his brother, and tears burst out, and he entered into a closet, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 45:1; 1ST PETER 3:10; ISAIAH 42:14"> And again when his face was washed, he went out, and refrained himself from weeping, and said, Set ye forth loaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 46:34; EXODUS 8:26; GENESIS 43:16"> Which were set forth to Joseph by himself, and to his brethren by themselves, and to the Egyptians that ate together by themselves; for it is unleaveful to Egyptians to eat with Hebrews, and they guess such a feast unholy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore they sat before him, the first begotten by right of the first begotten, and so on down to the youngest by his age; and they wondered greatly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 45:22; 2ND SAMUEL 11:8; 1ST SAMUEL 1:5"> when the parts were taken which they had received of him, and the more part came to Benjamin, so that it passed the others in five parts; and they drank, and were &#91;ful&#93; filled with him.</span>
