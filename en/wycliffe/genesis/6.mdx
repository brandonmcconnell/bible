<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 1:28"> And when men began to be multiplied on &#91;the&#93; earth, and had begat daughters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 82:6–7; 1ST JOHN 2:16; 2ND PETER 2:14"> the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair, and took wives to them of all which they had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:30; ISAIAH 63:10; JEREMIAH 11:11"> And God said, My spirit shall not dwell in man without end, for he is flesh; and the days of him shall be an hundred and twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:33; 2ND SAMUEL 21:15–22; 1ST SAMUEL 17:4"> Soothly giants were on the earth in those days, forsooth after that the sons of God entered &#91;in&#93; to the daughters of men, and those daughters begat; these were mighty of the world and famous men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:19; ROMANS 1:28–31; MARK 7:21–23"> Soothly God saw that much malice of men was in &#91;the&#93; earth, and that all the thought of their heart was attentive, either given, to evil in all time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:10; 1ST SAMUEL 15:11; EPHESIANS 4:30"> and it repented him that he had made man in earth; and God was wary before against time to coming &#91;or to come&#93;, and was touched with sorrow of heart within;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:3; PSALMS 37:20; JEREMIAH 12:3–4"> and said, I shall do away man, whom I made of nought, from the face of the earth; from man till to living things, from creeping beast till to the birds of heaven; for it repenteth me that I made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 19:19; HEBREWS 4:16; LUKE 1:30"> Forsooth Noah found grace before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 1:6; HEBREWS 11:7; 2ND PETER 2:5"> These be the generations of Noah. Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations; Noah went with God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 5:32"> and begat three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:17; HOSEA 4:1–2; HABAKKUK 2:8"> Forsooth the earth was corrupt before God, and was filled with wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 53:2–3; PROVERBS 15:3; PSALMS 14:1–3"> And when God saw, that the earth was corrupt, for each flesh, or man, had corrupted his way on &#91;the&#93; earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 4:1–2; 1ST PETER 4:7; EZEKIEL 7:2–6"> he said to Noah, The end of all flesh is come before me; the earth is filled with wickedness of the face of them, and I shall destroy them with the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:20; LUKE 17:27; MATTHEW 24:38"> Make thou to thee a ship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Throughout Genesis, whenever the “Later Version” refers to Noah’s ‘ship’, the “Early Version” refers to Noah’s ‘ark’.">a</a></sup>of wood hewn and planed; thou shalt make dwelling places in the ship, and thou shalt anoint it with pitch within and withoutforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:11; GENESIS 7:20"> And so thou shalt make it. The length of the ship shall be of three hundred cubits, the breadth shall be of fifty cubits, and the highness thereof shall be of thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 8:6; GENESIS 7:16; EZEKIEL 41:16"> Thou shalt make a window in the ship, and thou shalt end the highness thereof in a cubit; soothly thou shalt set the door of the ship in the side beneath; thou shalt make solars or upper rooms, and places of three chambers in the ship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 7:4; 2ND PETER 2:5; GENESIS 7:21–23"> Lo! I shall bring waters of deluge, or great flood, on the earth, and I shall slay each flesh in which is the spirit of life under heaven, and all things that be in &#91;the&#93; earth, shall be wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:7; GENESIS 7:7; GENESIS 17:7"> And I shall set my covenant of peace with thee; and thou shalt enter into the ship, &#91;thou&#93;, and thy sons, and thy wife, and the wives of thy sons shall enter with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 7:15–16; GENESIS 7:8–9; GENESIS 8:17"> And of all living beasts of all flesh, thou shalt bring into the ship twain &#91;or two&#93; and twain, of male kind and female, that they live with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:11–12; GENESIS 2:19; GENESIS 7:8–16"> of birds by their kind, and of work beasts in their kind, and of each creeping beast of &#91;the&#93; earth, by their kind; twain &#91;or two&#93; and twain of all shall enter with thee, that they may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 1:29–30; PSALMS 145:16; PSALMS 136:25"> Therefore thou shalt take with thee of all meats that may be eaten, and thou shalt bear together at thee, and those shall be as well to thee as to the beasts into meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 40:16; JOHN 15:14; MATTHEW 7:24–27"> Therefore Noah did all things which God commanded to him.</span>
