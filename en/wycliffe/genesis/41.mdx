<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 20:3; GENESIS 29:14; EXODUS 4:9"> After two years Pharaoh saw a dream; he guessed that he stood on a river,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:6"> from which seven fair kine and full fat went up, and &#91;they&#93; were fed in the places of marshes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 41:20–21; GENESIS 41:4"> and another seven, foul and lean, came out of the river, and were fed in that brink of the water, in green places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:15"> and those foul and lean kine devoured those kine of which the fairness and comeliness of their bodies were wonderful. Pharaoh waked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:14"> and slept again, and he saw another dream; seven ears of corn, full and fair, came forth in one stalk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:10; HOSEA 13:15; EZEKIEL 19:12"> and others, as many ears of corn, thin and smitten with corruption of burning wind, came forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 37:5; GENESIS 20:3"> devouring all the fairness of the first. Pharaoh waked after this rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 4:7; EXODUS 7:22; MATTHEW 2:1"> and when the morrowtide was made, he was afeared by inward dread, and he sent to all the expounders of Egypt, and to all the wise men; and when they were called, he told the dream, and none was that expounded it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 40:14; GENESIS 40:23; GENESIS 40:1–3"> Then at the last, the master butler bethought to him, and said to Pharaoh, I acknowledge my sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 39:20; GENESIS 40:2–3; GENESIS 37:36"> the king was wroth to his servants, and commanded me and the master baker to be cast down into the prison of the prince of knights,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 40:5–8"> where we both saw a dream in one night, before-showing of things to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 40:12–19; GENESIS 37:36; GENESIS 39:1"> An Hebrew child, servant of the same duke of knights, was there, to whom we told the dreams, and heard whatever thing the befalling of &#91;the&#93; thing proved afterward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:3; GENESIS 40:20–22; GENESIS 40:12"> for I am restored to mine office, and he was hanged in a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 2:25; PSALMS 113:7–8; PSALMS 105:19–22"> Anon at the behest of the king, they polled Joseph, led him out of the prison, and when his clothing was changed, they brought him to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 5:16; DANIEL 5:12; PSALMS 25:14"> To whom the king said, I saw dreams, and none &#91;there&#93; is that expoundeth those things that I saw; I have heard that thou expoundest such things most prudently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:5; GENESIS 40:8; DANIEL 2:47"> Joseph answered, Without me, God shall answer prosperities to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Therefore Pharaoh told that that he saw; I guessed that I stood on the brink of the flood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:8; JEREMIAH 24:1–3; JEREMIAH 24:5"> and seven kine, full fair, with flesh able to eating, went up from the water, which kine gathered green sedges in the pasture of the marshes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and lo! seven other kine, so foul and lean, followed these, that I saw never such in the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and when the former kine were devoured and wasted of the lean kine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:3; PSALMS 37:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 10:9–10"> the lean kine gave no step, or token, of fullness, but were slow, or feeble, by like leanness and paleness. I waked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and again I was oppressed by sleep, and I saw a dream; seven ears of corn, full and most fair, came forth on one stalk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 129:6–7; HOSEA 13:15; 2ND KI 19:26"> and another seven, thin and smitten with &#91;a&#93; burning wind, came forth of the stubble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 4:7; GENESIS 41:8; EXODUS 8:19"> which devoured the fairness of the former; I told this dream to &#91;the&#93; expounders, and no man there is that expoundeth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 2:45; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:1; EPHESIANS 1:17"> Joseph answered, The dream of the king is one; God hath showed to Pharaoh what things he shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 41:47; GENESIS 41:2; GENESIS 41:53"> Seven fair kine, and seven full ears of corn, be seven years of plenty, and the same things comprehend the strength of the dream;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND KI 8:1; 2ND SAMUEL 24:19"> and &#91;the&#93; seven kine, thin and lean, that went up after the fair kine, and the seven thin ears of corn, and smitten with &#91;a&#93; burning wind, be seven years of hunger to coming &#91;or to come&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 41:25; GENESIS 41:16"> which shall be fulfilled by this order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 41:26; GENESIS 41:46–47; GENESIS 41:49"> Lo! seven years of great plenty in all the land of Egypt shall come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 47:13; GENESIS 41:54; JAMES 5:17"> and seven other years of so great barrenness shall pursue &#91;or follow&#93; those, that all the abundance before shall be given to forgetting; for hunger shall waste all the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 24:20; 1ST SAMUEL 5:6"> and the greatness of neediness shall waste the greatness of plenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; ISAIAH 46:10–11; ISAIAH 14:24–27"> Forsooth this that thou sawest the second time in a dream pertaining to the same thing, is a showing of firm-ness, that is, a confirming of the first, for the word of God shall be done, and it shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled full swiftly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 41:39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:3; DEUTERONOMY 1:13"> Now therefore purvey the king a wise man and a ready, and make the king him sovereign to the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:3; LUKE 16:5; PROVERBS 6:6–8"> which man ordain governors by all countries, and gather he into barns the fifth part of fruits by &#91;the&#93; seven years of plenty, that shall come now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 45:6–7; GENESIS 41:56; EXODUS 4:13"> and all the wheat be kept under the power of Pharaoh, and be it kept in &#91;the&#93; cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GENESIS 47:13–25"> and be it made ready to the hunger to coming &#91;or to come&#93; of the seven years that shall oppress Egypt, and the land be not wasted by poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10; 1ST KINGS 21:2; 2ND SAMUEL 3:36"> The counsel of Joseph pleased Pharaoh, and all his servants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOB 32:8; DANIEL 5:14; NUMBERS 27:18"> and he spake to them, Whether we be able to find such a man which is full of God’s spirit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 41:33; GENESIS 41:28; GENESIS 41:25"> Therefore Pharaoh said to Joseph, For God hath showed to thee all things which thou hast spoken, whether I may find a wiser man than thou, and like to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10; PSALMS 105:21–22"> Therefore thou shalt be over mine house, and all the people shall obey to the behest of thy mouth; I shall pass thee only by one throne of the realm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:18; PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11; GENESIS 42:6"> And again Pharaoh said to Joseph, Lo! I have ordained thee on all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DANIEL 5:29; DANIEL 5:7; ESTHER 3:10"> And Pharaoh took off the ring from his hand, and gave it in the hand of Joseph, and he clothed Joseph with a stole of bis, or of white silk, and he put a golden wreath about his neck;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10; GENESIS 45:8; GENESIS 45:26"> and Pharaoh made Joseph to go upon his second chariot, while a beadle cried, that all men should kneel before him, and should know that he was sovereign of all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 105:22; EXODUS 11:7"> And the king said to Joseph, I am Pharaoh, without thy behest no man shall stir hand either foot in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="GENESIS 46:20; EZEKIEL 30:17; EXODUS 2:16"> And Pharaoh turned the name of Joseph, and called him by the Egyptian language, The Saviour of the World<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In Hebrew, it is ‘showing privates’, as Jerome and Lira here say, (or it is ‘The one showing secrets’, or revealing mysteries, as Jerome and Nicholas of Lira say here).">a</a></sup>, or Zaphnathpaaneah; and he gave to Joseph a wife, Asenath, the daughter of Potipherah, a priest of Heliopolis, that is, The City of the Sun. And so Joseph went out to the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DANIEL 1:19; 1ST KINGS 12:6; 1ST SAMUEL 16:21"> Forsooth Joseph was of thirty years, when he stood in the sight of king Pharaoh, and compassed all the countries &#91;or regions&#93; of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="GENESIS 26:12; PSALMS 72:16"> And the plenty of &#91;the&#93; seven years came, and &#91;the&#93; ripe corns were bound into handfuls or sheaves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="GENESIS 41:34–36; GENESIS 47:21"> and they were gathered into the barns of Egypt, also all the abundance of ripe corns was kept in all cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="PSALMS 78:27; JUDGES 6:5; GENESIS 22:17"> and so great abundance was of wheat, that it was made even to the gravel, or the sand, of the sea, and the plenty passed measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="GENESIS 46:20; GENESIS 48:5; 2ND SAMUEL 8:18"> Soothly two sons were born to Joseph before that the hunger came, which Asenath, the daughter of Potipherah, a priest of Heliopolis, childed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PSALMS 30:5; PROVERBS 31:7; DEUTERONOMY 33:17"> And Joseph called the name of the first begotten son, Manasseh, and said, God hath made me to forget all my travails, and the house of my father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="GENESIS 49:22; GENESIS 50:23; GENESIS 17:6"> and he called the name of the second son Ephraim, and said, God hath made me to increase in the land of my poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="GENESIS 41:29–31; PSALMS 73:20; LUKE 16:25"> Therefore when seven years of plenty that were in Egypt were passed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="GENESIS 45:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:11; PSALMS 105:16"> &#91;the&#93; seven years of poverty began to come, which Joseph before-said, and hunger had the mastery in all the world; also hunger was in all the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:5; MATTHEW 3:17; JEREMIAH 14:1–6"> and when that land hungered, the people cried to Pharaoh, and asked for meats; to whom he answered, Go ye to Joseph, and do ye whatever thing he saith to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="GENESIS 42:6; ZECHARIAH 5:3; LUKE 21:35"> Forsooth hunger increased each day in all the land, and Joseph opened all the barns, and sold corn to the Egyptians, for also hunger oppressed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="GENESIS 41:54; GENESIS 42:5; GENESIS 41:56"> and all &#91;the&#93; provinces came into Egypt to buy corns, and to abate the evil of neediness.</span>
