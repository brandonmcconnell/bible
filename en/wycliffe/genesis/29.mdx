<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; ISAIAH 49:10; JOHN 4:6"> and he saw a pit or well in the field, and three flocks of sheep resting beside it, for why sheep were watered thereof, and the mouth thereof was closed with a great stone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the custom was that when all the sheep were gathered together, they should turn away the stone, and when the flocks were watered, they should put it again on the mouth of the pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 28:10; GENESIS 27:43; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:2"> And Jacob said to the shepherds, Brethren, of whence be ye? Which answered, Of Haran.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 24:24; GENESIS 24:29; GENESIS 31:53"> And he asked them and said, Whether ye know Laban, the son of Nahor? They said, We know him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 20:9; GENESIS 37:14; EXODUS 18:7"> Jacob said, Is he whole? They said, He is in good state; and lo! Rachel, his daughter, cometh with his flock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:16; GALATIANS 6:9–10"> And Jacob said, Yet much of the day is to come, and it is not time that the flocks be led again to the folds; soothly give ye drink to the sheep, and so lead ye them again to meat or feeding.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 16:3; LUKE 24:2; GENESIS 34:14"> Which answered, We may not till all the sheep be gathered together, and till we remove the stone from the mouth of the pit, to water the flocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 2:21; SONG OF SONGS 1:7–8; EXODUS 2:15–16"> Yet they spake, and lo! Rachel came with the sheep of her father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 2:17"> And when Jacob saw her, and knew her to be the daughter of his mother’s brother, and the sheep to be of Laban his uncle, he removed the stone with which the pit was closed; and when the flock was watered,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 33:4; GENESIS 45:14–15; GENESIS 45:2"> he kissed her, and he wept with voice raised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 24:28; GENESIS 13:8; GENESIS 14:14–16"> And Jacob showed to her that he was the brother of her father, and the son of Rebecca; and she hasted, and told to her father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 24:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:37; ROMANS 16:16"> And when he had heard, that Jacob, the son of his sister, came, he ran to meet him, and he embraced Jacob, and kissed him, and led him into his house. Forsooth when the causes of the journey were heard,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 19:12–13; GENESIS 2:23; JUDGES 9:2"> Laban answered, Thou art my bone and my flesh. And after that the days of a month were filled,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 30:28; GENESIS 31:7"> Laban said to Jacob, Whether for thou art my brother, thou shalt serve me freely? say thou what meed thou shalt take.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="RUTH 4:11; GENESIS 49:31; GENESIS 31:4"> Forsooth Laban had two daughters, the name of the elder was Leah, soothly the younger was called Rachel;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 12:11; GENESIS 46:19–22; GENESIS 24:16"> but Leah was bleary-eyed, and Rachel was of fair face, and lovely in sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HOSEA 12:12; EXODUS 22:16–17; GENESIS 34:12"> And Jacob loved Rachel, and said, I shall serve thee seven years for Rachel thy younger daughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:11; ISAIAH 6:5; PSALMS 12:2"> Laban answered, It is better that I give her to thee than to another man; dwell thou with me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 13:7; EPHESIANS 5:2; HOSEA 12:12"> Therefore Jacob served seven years for Rachel; and the days seemed few to him for the greatness of his love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 15:1; GENESIS 29:20; GENESIS 4:1"> And at last he said to Laban, Give thou my wife to me, for the time is fulfilled that I enter &#91;in&#93; to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:9; RUTH 4:10–13; JUDGES 14:10–18"> And when many companies of friends were called to the feast, he made &#91;the&#93; weddings,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MICAH 7:5; GENESIS 38:14–15; GENESIS 24:65"> and in the eventide Laban brought in to him Leah his daughter,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 16:1; GENESIS 30:9–12; GENESIS 46:18"> and gave an handmaid, Zilpah by name, to his daughter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:2; PROVERBS 11:31; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:13"> And when Jacob had entered &#91;in&#93; to her by custom, when the morrow-tide was made, he saw Leah, and he said to his wife’s father, What is it that thou wouldest do? whether I served not thee for Rachel? why hast thou deceived me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Laban answered, It is not custom in our place that we give first the younger daughter to weddings;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:18; JUDGES 14:12; GENESIS 2:2–3"> fulfill thou the week of days of this wedding, and I shall give to thee also this Rachel, for the work in which thou shalt serve me by other seven years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Jacob assented to the covenant, and when the week was passed, he wedded Rachel,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 35:22; GENESIS 29:24; GENESIS 30:3–8"> to whom her father had given Bilhah an handmaid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 31:41; GENESIS 29:20; GENESIS 31:15"> And at the last Jacob used the weddings desired, and set the love of the latter wife before the first; and Jacob served Laban seven other years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:21; MATTHEW 10:37; DEUTERONOMY 21:15"> Forsooth the Lord saw that Jacob despised Leah, that is, loved her less than Rachel, and he opened Leah’s womb, while her sister dwelled barren.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:7; EXODUS 4:31; EXODUS 3:7"> And Leah childed a son conceived, and she called his name Reuben, and said, The Lord hath seen my meek-ness; now mine husband shall love me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 34:25; GENESIS 49:5–6; GENESIS 34:30"> And again she conceived, and childed a son, and said, For the Lord saw that I was despised, he gave also this son to me; and she called his name Simeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 32:26–29; GENESIS 49:5–7; GENESIS 46:11"> And she conceived the third time, and childed another son, and she said also, Now mine husband shall be coupled to me, for I have childed three sons to him; and therefore she called his name Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:2; GENESIS 49:8–12"> The fourth time she conceived, and childed a son, and said, Now I shall acknowledge to the Lord; and therefore she called his name Judah; and ceased to child.</span>
</div>
