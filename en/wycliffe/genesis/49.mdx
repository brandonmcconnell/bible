<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:14; JEREMIAH 23:20; DANIEL 10:14"> Forsooth Jacob called his sons, and said to them, Be ye gathered together, that I tell what things shall come to you in the last days;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 34:11; PROVERBS 7:1; PROVERBS 23:22"> be ye gathered &#91;together&#93;, and hear, ye sons of Jacob, hear ye Israel your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:17; PSALMS 78:51; PSALMS 105:36"> Reuben, my first begotten son, thou art my strength, and the beginning of my sorrow; thou oughtest to be the former in gifts, the more in lordship;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 35:22; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:1; DEUTERONOMY 27:20"> thou art shed &#91;or poured&#93; out as water; wax thou not, for thou ascend-edest &#91;or went up&#93; on the bed of thy father, and defouledest his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 34:25–31; GENESIS 46:10–11; GENESIS 29:33–34"> Simeon and Levi, brethren, fighting vessels of wickedness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 34:30; PSALMS 26:9; PSALMS 16:9"> my soul come not into the counsel of them, and my glory be not in the congregation of them; for in their strong vengeance, they killed a man, and in their &#91;own&#93; will, they under-mined the wall;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:1–9; 1ST CHRONICLES 4:24–31; 1ST CHRONICLES 6:65"> cursed be the strong vengeance of them, for it is obstinate, and the indignation of them, for it is hard; I shall part them in Jacob, and I shall scatter them in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:14; GENESIS 27:29; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:2"> Judah, thy brethren shall praise thee, thine hands shall be in the nolls of thine enemies; the sons of thy father shall worship thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:9; NUMBERS 23:24; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:5"> Judah, the whelp of a lion; my son, thou hast gone up to the prey; thou restedest, and hast lain as a lion, and as a lioness, who shall raise him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 1:32–33; NUMBERS 24:17; ISAIAH 9:6"> The sceptre shall not be taken away from Judah, and a duke of his hip, till he come that shall be sent, and he shall be the abiding of heathen men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:1–3; JOEL 3:18; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:14"> and he shall tie his colt at the vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;, and his she-ass at the vine; O! my son, he shall wash his stole in wine, and his mantle in the blood of the grape;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:29"> his eyes be fairer than wine, and his teeth be whiter than milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:18–19; GENESIS 30:20; JOSHUA 19:10–16"> Zebulun shall dwell in the brink of the sea, and in the standing of ships; and he shall stretch till to Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 12:32; GENESIS 30:18; DEUTERONOMY 33:18"> Issachar, a strong ass, lying betwixt &#91;the&#93; terms,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 81:6; MATTHEW 23:4; JOSHUA 14:15"> saw rest, that it was good, and saw the land, that it was best, and he underset his shoulder to bear, and he was made serving to tributes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Either to rent, (Or to pay rent or taxes), as it is in Hebrew.">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 30:6; DEUTERONOMY 33:22; JUDGES 18:26–27"> Dan shall deem his people, as also another lineage in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 12:35; JUDGES 18:22–31; JUDGES 14:1–15"> Dan be made a serpent in the way, and a cerastes, that is, an horned adder, in the path, and bite he the feet of an horse, that the rider of him fall backward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:174; PSALMS 119:166; MICAH 7:7"> Lord, I shall abide thine health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 30:11; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:11–22; DEUTERONOMY 33:20–21"> Gad shall be girded, and he shall fight before him, and he shall be girded behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:24–25; GENESIS 30:13; GENESIS 46:17"> Asher, his bread shall be fat, and he shall give delights to kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:23; GENESIS 30:8; JOSHUA 19:32–39"> Naphtali shall be an hart sent out, and giving speeches of fairness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 41:52; PSALMS 128:3; PSALMS 1:1–3"> Joseph, a son increasing, a son increasing, and fair in beholding; &#91;the&#93; daughters run about on the wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 37:24; PSALMS 64:3; PSALMS 118:13"> but his brethren wrathed at him, and chided him, and they had darts, and had envy to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 132:2; ISAIAH 28:16; PSALMS 132:5"> His bow sat in the Strong, that is, the Lord, and the bonds of his arms and his hands were unbound by the hand of the mighty God of Jacob; of him a shepherd went out, the stone of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 28:13; GENESIS 35:11; PHILIPPIANS 4:19"> God of thy father shall be thine helper, and Almighty God shall bless thee, with blessings of heaven from above, and with blessings of the sea lying beneath, with blessings of teats, and of the womb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:15–16; HABAKKUK 3:6; ISAIAH 66:5"> the blessings of thy father be comforted, that is, be better than the blessings of his fathers, till the desire of everlasting hills came; blessings be made on the head of Joseph, and in the noll of Nazarite, that is, holy, among his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 20:25; GENESIS 35:18; EZEKIEL 22:27"> Benjamin, a ravishing wolf, shall eat the prey early, and in the eventide he shall part spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:4; ESTHER 8:11; 1ST KINGS 18:31"> All these were in &#91;the&#93; twelve kindreds of Israel; their father spake these things to them, and he blessed them all by proper blessings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 47:30; GENESIS 50:13; GENESIS 15:15"> and he commanded to them, and said, I am soon to be gathered to my people; bury ye me with my fathers in the double den, that is in the land of Ephron the Hittite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 23:20; GENESIS 23:16–18; GENESIS 23:8–9"> that is, in the den in the field at Machpelah, against Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which den Abraham bought with the field of Ephron the Hittite, into possession of a sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 25:9; GENESIS 35:29; GENESIS 50:13"> There they buried him, and Sarah his wife; also Isaac was buried there, with Rebecca his wife; there also Leah lieth buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 23:17–20"> (This verse is omitted in the original text.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 49:29; GENESIS 25:8; GENESIS 35:29"> And when the behests were ended, by which he taught his sons, he gathered together his feet on the bed, and died, and he was put to his people.</span>
