<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 50:26; 2ND CHRONICLES 16:14; JOHN 19:39–40"> and he commanded his servants, leeches, that they should anoint his father with sweet smelling spiceries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:29; DEUTERONOMY 34:8; GENESIS 50:10"> While they fulfilled his behests, forty days passed, for this was the custom of dead bodies that were anointed; and Egypt bewept him seventy days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 18:3; ESTHER 4:2"> And when the time of wailing was fulfilled, Joseph spake to the meine of Pharaoh, If I have found grace in your sight, speak ye in the ears of Pharaoh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 16:14; ISAIAH 22:16; MATTHEW 27:60"> for my father charged me &#91;with an oath&#93;, and said, Lo! I die; thou shalt bury me in my sepulchre which I digged to me in the land of Canaan; therefore I shall go up that I bury my father, and I shall turn again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 48:21"> And Pharaoh said to him, Go up, and bury thy father, as thou art charged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 14:16"> And when Joseph went up, all the elder men of the household of Pharaoh went with him, and all the greater men in birth of the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:24–27; EXODUS 10:26; EXODUS 10:8–9"> and all the household of Joseph with their brethren, without little children, and flocks, and great beasts, which they left in the land of Goshen, went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 14:28; 2ND KI 18:24; SONG OF SONGS 1:9"> And he had chariots, and horse-men, and fellowship with him, and the company was made not little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 2:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:2; 2ND SAMUEL 1:17"> And they came to the cornfloor of Atad, which is set over Jordan, where they made the service of the dead body, with great wailing and strong, and filled seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 10:15–19; GENESIS 24:6; DEUTERONOMY 11:30"> And when the dwellers of the land of Canaan had seen this, they said, This is a time of great wailing to the Egyptians; therefore they called the name of that place The wailing of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:16; GENESIS 49:29–32; EPHESIANS 6:1"> Therefore the sons of Jacob did, as he had commanded to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 49:29–31; GENESIS 23:16–18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:16"> and they bare him into the land of Canaan, and they buried him in the double den, which den with the field Abraham had bought of Ephron the Hittite, against the face of Mamre, into possession of a sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Joseph turned again into Egypt with his brethren and all the fellowship, when his father was buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:1; GENESIS 42:21–22; PSALMS 53:5"> And when their father was dead, the brethren of Joseph dreaded, and spake together, Lest peradventure he be mindful of the wrong which he suffered, and yield to us all the evil, that we did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:25"> And they sent to him, and said, Thy father commanded to us, before that he died,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:32; MATTHEW 6:14–15; GENESIS 45:8"> that we should say to thee these things by his words; I beseech thee, that thou forget the wickedness of thy brethren, and the sin, and &#91;the&#93; malice that they haunted against thee; also we pray thee, that thou forgive this wickedness which we did to thy father, the servant of God. When these things were heard, Joseph wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 37:7–11; GENESIS 42:6; GENESIS 45:3"> And his brethren came to him, and worshipped low to the earth, and said, We be thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 12:19; GENESIS 45:5; GENESIS 30:2"> To which he answered, Do not ye dread; whether we may against-stand God’s will?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 8:28; PSALMS 119:71; GENESIS 45:5–8"> Ye thought evil of me, and God turned it into good, that he should enhance me, as ye see in this present time, and that he should make safe many peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 47:12; 1ST PETER 3:9; GENESIS 45:10–11"> do not ye dread, I shall feed you and your little children. And he comforted them, and spake sweetly and lightly to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and Joseph dwelled in Egypt, with all the house of his father. And he lived an hundred &#91;and ten&#93; years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:39; GENESIS 30:3; JOB 42:16"> and he saw the sons of Ephraim till to the third generation; also the sons of Machir, the son of Manasseh, were borne in the knees of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 3:16–17; GENESIS 35:12; GENESIS 26:3"> When these things were done, Joseph spake to his brethren, After my death God shall visit you, and he shall make you to go up from this land to the land which he swore to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 13:19; JOSHUA 24:32; GENESIS 47:29–31"> And when Joseph had charged them with an oath, and had said, God shall visit you, bear ye out with you my bones from this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 50:2–3; GENESIS 47:9; JOSHUA 24:29"> he died, when an hundred and ten years of his life were filled; and he was anointed with sweet smelling spiceries, and he was kept in a bier in Egypt.</span>
