<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 17:17"> Forsooth Sarah lived an hundred and seven and twenty years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 23:19; GENESIS 13:18; 1ST CHRONICLES 6:57"> and died in the city of Arba, which is Hebron, in the land of Canaan; and Abraham came to bewail and beweep her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 10:15; GENESIS 23:7; 1ST SAMUEL 26:6"> And when he had risen from the office of the dead body, he spake to the sons of Heth, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:15; HEBREWS 11:9; GENESIS 17:8"> I am a comeling and a pilgrim with you; give ye to me right of &#91;a&#93; sepulchre with you, that I bury my dead body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the sons of Heth answered, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 24:35; GENESIS 14:14; EXODUS 32:22"> Lord, hear thou us; thou art the prince of God with us; bury thou thy dead body in our chosen sepulchres, and no man shall be able to forbid thee, that not thou bury thy dead body in the sepulchre of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; GENESIS 19:1; PROVERBS 18:24"> And Abraham &#91;a&#93; rose, and worship-ped or honoured the people of the land, that is, the sons of Heth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 25:9; 1ST JOHN 2:1–2; 1ST KINGS 2:17"> And he said to them, If it pleaseth your soul that I bury my dead body, hear ye me, and pray ye for me to Ephron, the son of Zohar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 13:8; ROMANS 12:17"> that he give to me the double cave, which he hath in the uttermost part of his field; for sufficient money give he it to me before you into possession of &#91;a&#93; sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 34:20; GENESIS 34:24; GENESIS 23:18"> Forsooth Ephron dwelled in the midst of the sons of Heth. And Ephron answered to Abraham, while all men heard that entered by the gate of that city, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 19:24; ISAIAH 32:8; DEUTERONOMY 19:15"> My lord, it shall not be done so, but more hearken thou to that that I say; I give to thee the field, and the cave which is therein, while the sons of my people be present; bury thou thy dead body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 23:7; GENESIS 18:2; GENESIS 19:1"> Abraham worshipped before the Lord, and before the people of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 13:8; 2ND SAMUEL 24:24; GENESIS 14:22–23"> and he spake to Ephron, while his people stood about, I beseech, that thou hear me; I shall give money for the field, receive thou it, and so I shall bury my dead body in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Ephron answered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:12; EXODUS 30:13; EXODUS 30:15"> My lord, hear thou me; the land which thou askest for is worth four hundred shekels of silver, that is the price betwixt me and thee; but how much is this? bury thou thy dead body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:12; JEREMIAH 32:9; EXODUS 30:13"> And when Abraham had heard this, he numbered out the money which Ephron asked for, while the sons of Heth heard, four hundred shekels of silver, and of proved common money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 25:9; GENESIS 50:13; GENESIS 23:20"> And the field that was sometime of Ephron, in which field was a double den, beholding to Mamre, as well that field, as the den, and all the trees thereof, in all the terms thereof by compass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="RUTH 4:1; JEREMIAH 32:12; GENESIS 34:20"> was confirmed to Abraham into possession, while the sons of Heth saw, and all men that entered by the gate of that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 50:13; ECCLESIASTES 12:7; ECCLESIASTES 12:5"> And so Abraham buried Sarah, his wife, in the double den of the field, that beheld to Mamre; this is Hebron in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:10–14; RUTH 4:7–10; GENESIS 49:31–32"> And the field, and the den that was therein, was confirmed of the sons of Heth to Abraham, into possession of a sepulchre.</span>
