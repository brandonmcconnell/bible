<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 5:2; GENESIS 29:31; GENESIS 37:11"> Forsooth Rachel saw, that she was unfruitful, and she had envy to her sister, and said to her husband, Give thou free children to me, else I shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 16:2; PSALMS 127:3; 2ND KI 5:7"> To whom Jacob was wroth, and answered, Whether I am for God, which have deprived thee from the fruit of thy womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 50:23; JOB 3:12; RUTH 4:11"> And she said, I have an handmaid Bilhah; enter thou &#91;in&#93; to her that she child on my knees, and that I have sons of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 35:22; GENESIS 21:10; 2ND SAMUEL 12:11"> And she gave to him Bilhah into matrimony; and when her husband had entered &#91;in&#93; to her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> she conceived, and childed a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:59; PSALMS 35:24; PSALMS 43:1"> And Rachel said, The Lord hath deemed to me, and hath heard my prayer, and gave a son to me; and therefore she called his name Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And again Bilhah conceived, and childed another son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:24–25; GENESIS 23:6; GENESIS 49:21"> for whom Rachel said, The Lord hath made me like my sister, and I &#91;have&#93; waxed strong; and she called him Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 30:4; GENESIS 30:17; GENESIS 29:35"> Leah feeled that she ceased to bear child, and she gave Zilpah, her handmaid, to her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And when Zilpah, after conceiving, childed a son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 49:19; ISAIAH 65:11; GENESIS 35:26"> Leah said, Blessedly; and therefore she called his name Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Also Zilpah childed another son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 1:48; PROVERBS 31:28; SONG OF SONGS 6:9"> and Leah said, This is for my bliss, for all women shall say me blessed; therefore she called him Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 7:13; GENESIS 25:30"> Forsooth Reuben went out into the field in the time of wheat harvest, and found mandrakes, which he brought to Leah, his mother. And Rachel said, Give thou to me a part of the mandrakes of thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:3; NUMBERS 16:9–10"> Leah answered, Whether it seemeth little to thee, that thou hast ravished my husband from me, no but thou take also the mandrakes of my son? Rachel said, The husband sleep with thee in this night for the mandrakes of thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when Jacob came again from the field at the eventide, Leah went out into his meeting, and said, Thou shalt enter &#91;in&#93; to me, for I have hired thee with hire for the mandrakes of my son. He slept with her in that night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 30:6; GENESIS 30:22; EXODUS 3:7"> and God heard her prayers, and she conceived, and childed the fifth son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 49:14–15; DEUTERONOMY 33:18; GENESIS 46:13"> and said, God hath given meed to me, for I gave mine handmaid to mine husband; and she called his name Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Again Leah conceived, and childed the sixth son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 35:23; MATTHEW 4:13; GENESIS 49:13"> and said, The Lord hath made me rich with a good dower; also in this time mine husband shall be with me, for I have engendered six sons to him; and therefore she called his name Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 34:1–3; GENESIS 34:26; GENESIS 46:15"> After whom she childed a daughter, Dinah by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 29:31; PSALMS 113:9; PSALMS 127:3"> Also the Lord had mind on Rachel, and he heard her, and opened her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 1:25; GENESIS 29:31; LUKE 1:27"> And she conceived, and childed a son, and said, God hath taken away my shame;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 49:22–26; GENESIS 35:24; GENESIS 37:2"> and she called his name Joseph, and said, The Lord give to me another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 24:54; GENESIS 24:56; GENESIS 28:15"> Soothly when Joseph was born, Jacob said to his wife’s father, Deliver thou me, that I turn again to my country, and to my land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 29:30; HOSEA 12:12; GENESIS 31:26"> Give thou to me my wives, and my free children, for which I have served thee, that I go; forsooth thou knowest the service by which I have served thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="RUTH 2:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10; ISAIAH 61:9"> Laban said to him, Find I grace in thy sight; I have learned by experience, that God hath blessed me for thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 29:15; GENESIS 29:19"> ordain thou the meed which I shall give to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 31:6; GENESIS 31:38–40; 1ST PETER 2:18"> And Jacob answered, Thou knowest how I have served thee, and how great thy possession was in mine hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:14; DEUTERONOMY 11:10"> thou haddest little before that I came to thee, and now thou art made rich, and the Lord &#91;hath&#93; blessed thee at mine entering; therefore it is just that I purvey sometime also for mine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 21:4–6; HEBREWS 13:5; PSALMS 118:8"> And Laban said, What shall I give to thee? And Jacob said, I will or desire nothing, that is, of thy gift, but if thou doest that that I ask, again I shall feed and keep thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 31:8; GENESIS 31:10; GENESIS 30:35"> Go about all thy flocks, and separate thou all diversely-coloured sheep, and of spotted fleeces, and whatever thing shall be of dun hue, and spotted, and diverse of colour, as well in sheep as in goats; that shall be my meed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 13:14; 2ND SAMUEL 22:21; 1ST SAMUEL 26:23"> And my rightfulness &#91;or rightwise-ness&#93; shall answer to me tomorrow, when the time of covenant shall come before thee; and all that be not diverse, and spotted, and dunned, as well in sheep as in goats, be found at me thou shalt reprove me of theft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 7:7; NUMBERS 22:29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:5"> And Laban said, I have it acceptable that that thou askest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 31:9"> And Laban separated in that day &#91;the&#93; goats, and sheep, goat bucks, and rams, diverse and spotted. Soothly he betook all the flock of one colour, that is, of white, and of black fleece, into the hands of his sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and he set the space of a way of three days betwixt his sons, and the husband of his daughters, that fed his other flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GENESIS 31:9–13; EZEKIEL 31:8"> Therefore Jacob took green rods of poplars, and of almonds, and of planes, and in part he did away the rind of them; and when the rinds were drawn away, either shaved, whiteness appeared in these that were made bare; soothly those that were whole dwelled green, and by this manner the colour was made diverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And Jacob put those rods in the troughs, where the water was poured out, that when the flocks should come to drink,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 12:35–36; GENESIS 31:9–12; GENESIS 31:40"> they should have the rods before their eyes, and they should conceive in &#91;the&#93; sight of the rods. And it was done that in that heat of riding, or engendering, the sheep should behold those rods, and that they should bring forth spotted beasts, and diverse, and besprinkled with diverse colour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Jacob separated the flock, and put the rods in the &#91;water&#93; troughs, before the eyes of the rams. Soothly all the white and &#91;the&#93; black were Laban’s; soothly all the others were Jacob’s; for the flocks were separated betwixt themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Therefore when the sheep were ridden in the first time, Jacob put the rods in the water troughs before the eyes of rams, and of ewe sheep, that they should conceive in the sight of the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Forsooth when the late mixing, or engendering, and the last conceivings were, Jacob put not those rods; and those that were late engendered, were made Laban’s, and those that were of the first time engendered, were Jacob’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GENESIS 24:35; GENESIS 30:30; GENESIS 26:13–14"> And Jacob was made full rich, and had many flocks, handmaids, and menservants, camels, and asses.</span>
