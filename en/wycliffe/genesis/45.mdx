<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:41; GENESIS 43:30–31"> Joseph might no longer abstain himself, while many men stood there before him; wherefore he commanded that all men should go out, and that none alien were present in the knowing of Joseph and his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 46:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:37; NUMBERS 14:1"> And Joseph raised up his voice with weeping, which the Egyptians heard, and all the household of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:5; ZECHARIAH 12:10"> And he said to his brethren, I am Joseph; liveth my father yet? His brethren might not answer, and were aghast with full much dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 37:28; MATTHEW 14:27; GENESIS 50:18"> To whom Joseph said meekly, Cometh nigh to me. And when they had nighed nigh, he said, I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 50:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:9–15; GENESIS 45:7–8"> do not ye dread, neither seem it to be hard to you, that ye sold me into these countries; for God hath sent me before you into Egypt for your health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 47:23; GENESIS 41:54; GENESIS 41:56"> For it is two years that hunger began to be in the land, yet five more years shall pursue, in which men shall not be able to ear, neither reap;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 44:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:35; PSALMS 18:50"> and God before-sent me, that ye be kept alive on earth, and may have meats to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 15:16; ROMANS 9:16; JOHN 19:11"> I was sent hither not by your counsel, but by God’s will, which hath made me as the father of Pharaoh, and lord of all his house, and prince in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 45:19–20; GENESIS 45:13"> Haste ye, and goeth up to my father, and ye shall say to him, Thy son Joseph sendeth these things to thee; God hath made me lord of all the land of Egypt; come down here to me, and tarry not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 9:26; EXODUS 8:22; GENESIS 46:34–6"> and dwell in the land of Goshen; and thou shalt be beside me, thou, and thy sons, and the sons of thy sons, thy sheep, and thy great beasts, and all things which thou wieldest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 47:12; GENESIS 47:6; MATTHEW 15:5–6"> and there I shall feed thee; for yet five years of hunger be left, lest both thou perish, and thine house, and all things which thou wieldest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 42:23; LUKE 24:39; JOHN 20:27"> Lo! your eyes, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin see, that my mouth speaketh to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:23; 1ST PETER 1:10–12"> tell ye to my father all my glory, and all things which ye saw in Egypt; haste ye, and bring ye him to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 33:4; GENESIS 29:11; GENESIS 46:29"> And when he had embraced, and felled into the neck of Benjamin, his brother, he wept, the while also Benjamin wept in like manner on the neck of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 15:20; 1ST SAMUEL 10:1; PSALMS 77:4"> And Joseph kissed all his brethren, and wept on them all; after which things they were hardy to speak to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 41:33; ESTHER 1:21; GENESIS 20:15"> And it was heard, and published by famous word in the king’s hall, The brethren of Joseph be come. And Pharaoh joyed, and all his household;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 42:25–26; GENESIS 44:1–2"> and Pharaoh said to Joseph, that he should command his brethren, and say to them, Charge ye your beasts, and go ye into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 27:28; NUMBERS 18:12; GENESIS 47:6"> and take ye from thence your father, and your kindred, and come ye back to me; and I shall give you all the goods of Egypt, that ye eat the marrow of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 46:5; GENESIS 45:27; GENESIS 31:17–18"> Command thou also, that they take wains of the land of Egypt to the carriage of their little children, and wives, and say thou, Take ye your father, and haste ye in coming soon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:19; LUKE 17:31; DEUTERONOMY 7:16"> neither leave ye anything of the appurtenance of your house, for all the riches of Egypt shall be yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 45:19; EZEKIEL 23:24; LAMENTATIONS 1:18"> The sons of Israel did as it was commanded to them; to which Joseph gave wains, by the behest of Pharaoh, and meats in the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 43:34; 2ND KI 5:5; JUDGES 14:12"> and he commanded two stoles to be brought forth to each; forsooth he gave to Benjamin three hundred pieces of silver, with five of the best stoles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 24:10; EXODUS 16:3; GENESIS 43:11"> and he sent to his father so much of silver, and of clothes, and he added to them ten male asses, that should bear of the riches of Egypt, and so many female asses, bearing wheat and loaves in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:13; PSALMS 133:1–3; GENESIS 42:21–22"> Therefore he let go his brethren, and said to them going forth, Be ye not wroth in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Which went up from Egypt, and came into the land of Canaan, to their father Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 44:28; GENESIS 37:35; LUKE 24:34"> and they told to him, and said, Joseph, thy son, liveth, and he is lord in all the land of Egypt. And when this was heard, Jacob waked as of a grievous sleep; nevertheless he believed not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 85:6; ISAIAH 57:15; GENESIS 45:19"> They told on the contrary to him all the order of the thing; and when Jacob had seen the wains, and all things which Joseph had sent, his spirit lived again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 2:28–30; JOHN 16:21–22; GENESIS 46:30"> and he said, It sufficeth to me, if Joseph my son liveth yet; I shall go and see him before that I die.</span>
