<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 40:13; GENESIS 39:20–23; ESTHER 6:1"> When these things were done, it befelled that two geldings, the butler and the baker of the king of Egypt, sinned to their lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:14; PROVERBS 19:12; PSALMS 76:10"> And Pharaoh was wroth against them, for the one was master butler, and the tother was master baker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 39:20; GENESIS 39:23"> And he sent them into the prison of the prince of knights, in which also Joseph was bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 39:1; PSALMS 37:5; GENESIS 37:36"> And the keeper of the prison betook them to Joseph, which also served, or kept, them. Somewhat of time passed, and they were holden in keeping,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 41:11; GENESIS 40:8; GENESIS 37:5–10"> and both saw a dream in one night, by covenable expounding to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 41:8; DANIEL 2:1–3; GENESIS 40:8"> And when Joseph had entered to them early, and had seen them sorry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 2:2; 1ST SAMUEL 1:8; LUKE 24:17"> he asked them, and said, Why is your cheer heavier today than it is wont to be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 2:47; GENESIS 41:15–16; DANIEL 2:28"> Which answered, We each saw a dream, and there is no man that expoundeth it to us. And Joseph said to them, Whether the expounding is not of God? Tell ye to me what ye have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 7:13–15; GENESIS 37:5–10; DANIEL 4:8"> The master butler told first his dream; I saw before me that a vine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> in which were three scions, waxed little and little into burgeonings, and that after the flowers, the grapes waxed ripe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 1:11–1; 1ST KINGS 10:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:4"> and the cup of Pharaoh was in mine hand; therefore I took the grapes, and pressed them out into the cup that I held, and I gave drink to Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 41:12; JUDGES 7:14; GENESIS 41:25–26"> Joseph answered, This is the expounding of the dream; three scions be yet three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3; JEREMIAH 52:31; 2ND KI 25:27"> after which Pharaoh shall have mind of thy service, and he shall restore thee into the first degree, and thou shalt give to him the cup, by thine office, as thou were wont to do before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:12; LUKE 23:42; 1ST SAMUEL 20:14–15"> Only have thou mind of me, when it is well to thee, and thou shalt do mercy with me, that thou make suggestion to Pharaoh, that he lead me out of this prison;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:17–18; GENESIS 39:20; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:10"> for thiefly, that is, by thievery, I am taken away from the land of Hebrews, and here I am sent innocent into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 40:1–2"> The master baker saw that Joseph had declared prudently the dream, and he said, And I saw a dream, that I had three baskets of meal on mine head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 49:20; 1ST CHRONICLES 12:20"> and I guessed that I bare in one basket, that was highest, all meats or baked foods that be made for Pharaoh by the craft of bakers, and that birds ate thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 40:12; GENESIS 41:26; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:24"> Joseph answered, This is the expounding of the dream; three baskets be yet three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 40:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:27; 2ND SAMUEL 21:6"> after which Pharaoh shall take away thine head, and he shall hang thee in a cross, and birds shall draw thy flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 40:19; GENESIS 40:13; MATTHEW 14:6"> From thence the third day was the day of the birth of Pharaoh, which made a great feast to his servants, and he had mind among the meats or during the meal, of the master butler, and of the prince of bakers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 40:13; NEHEMIAH 2:1"> and he restored the one into his place, that he should dress the cup, either drink, to the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 40:19; GENESIS 41:16; DANIEL 2:30"> and he hanged the tother in a gibbet, that the truth of Joseph declaring the dreams should be proved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 31:12; JOB 19:14; PSALMS 105:19"> And nevertheless when prosperities befelled to the master butler, he forgat Joseph that declared his dream.</span>
