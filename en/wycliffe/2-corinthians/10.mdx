<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:2; GALATIANS 5:16–25"> For I pray you, that lest I present be not bold &#91;or not hardy&#93; by the trust, in which I am guessed to be bold &#91;or hardy&#93; into some, that deem us, as if we wander after the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4; GALATIANS 2:20; 1ST PETER 4:1–2"> For we walking in the flesh, fight not after the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:7; EPHESIANS 6:13–18; JEREMIAH 1:10"> For the arms or armours of our knighthood be not fleshly, but mighty by God to the destruction of strengths &#91;or of wardings&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; ISAIAH 55:7; 1ST PETER 1:14–15"> And we destroy counsels, and all highness that higheth &#91;or raised&#93; itself against the science of God, and drive into captivity all understanding into the service of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:9; NUMBERS 16:26–30; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:15"> And we have ready to avenge all unobedience, when your obedience shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:12; JOHN 7:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:37"> See ye the things that be after the face. If any man trusteth to himself, that he is of Christ, think he this thing again with himself, for as he is Christ’s, so also we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:6; GALATIANS 1:1"> For if I shall glory, anything more of our power, which the Lord gave to us into edifying, and not into your destruction, I shall not be shamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:19–21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5"> But that I be not guessed as to fear you by epistles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:17; EXODUS 4:10; GALATIANS 4:13–14"> for they say, That epistles be grievous &#91;or be heavy&#93; and strong, but the presence of the body is feeble, and the word worthy to be despised &#91;or it is contemptible&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:2–3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:10"> He that is such one, think this, for such as we absent be in word by epistles, such we be present in deed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:2; PROVERBS 26:12; LUKE 18:11"> For we dare not put us among, or comparison us to some men, that commend themselves; but we measure us in us-selves, and comparison us-selves to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 12:6; ROMANS 15:20; 1ST PETER 4:10"> For we shall not have glory over-measure, but by the measure of the rule which God measured to us, the measure that stretcheth &#91;till&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 4:15; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:11; ROMANS 1:16"> For we overstretch not forth us, as not stretching to you. For to you we came in the gospel of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 15:20; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:13"> not glorying over-measure in other men’s travails. For we have hope of your faith that waxeth in you, to be magnified by our rule in abundance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 15:24–28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:21"> also to preach into those things that be beyond you, not to have glory in other man’s rule, in these things that be made ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23–24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:31; JAMES 1:9–10"> He that glorieth, have glory in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 16:15; PROVERBS 21:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5"> For not he that commendeth himself is approved, but whom God commendeth.</span>
