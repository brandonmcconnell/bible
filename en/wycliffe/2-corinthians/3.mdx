<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:12"> Begin we therefore again to praise us-selves? or whether we need, as some men, epistles &#91;or letters&#93; of praising to you, or of you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:1–2; 1ST THESSALONIANS 1:8; ROMANS 1:8"> Ye be our epistle, written in our hearts, which is known and read of all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:19; JEREMIAH 31:33; HEBREWS 8:10"> and &#91;ye be&#93; made open, for ye be the epistle of Christ ministered of us, and written, not with ink, but by the Spirit of the living God; not in stone tables, but in fleshly tables of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:6; EPHESIANS 3:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:14"> For we have such trust by Christ to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9; PHILIPPIANS 4:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:10"> not that we be sufficient to think anything of us-&#91;selves&#93;, as of us, but our sufficience is of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 6:63; ROMANS 7:6; JEREMIAH 31:31"> Which also made us able ministers of the new testament, not by letter, but by Spirit; for the letter slayeth, but the Spirit quickeneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:9–11; ROMANS 10:4; EXODUS 31:18"> And if the ministration of death written by letter in stones was in glory, so that the children of Israel might not behold into the face of Moses, for the glory of his face, which &#91;glory&#93; is voided,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:16; EPHESIANS 2:18; GALATIANS 3:14"> how shall not the ministration of the Spirit be more in glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 3:21–22; HEBREWS 12:18–21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21"> For if the ministration of condemnation was in glory, much more the ministration of rightwiseness is plenteous in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND PETER 1:17; HAGGAI 2:7–9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:13"> For neither that that was clear was glorified in this part for the excellent glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 5:20–21; HEBREWS 8:13; HEBREWS 7:21–25"> and if that that is voided was by glory, much more that that dwelleth still is in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 7:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:13; PHILIPPIANS 1:20"> Therefore we that have such hope, use much trust;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 34:33–35; HEBREWS 10:1–9; EPHESIANS 2:14–15"> and not as Moses laid &#91;or put&#93; a veil on his face, that the children of Israel should not behold into his face, which veil is voided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:3–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; ROMANS 11:25"> But the wits of them be astonied; for into this day the same veil in &#91;the&#93; reading of the old testament, dwelleth not showed, for it is voided in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:27–29"> but into this day, when Moses is read, the veil is put on their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 34:34; ISAIAH 29:18; ISAIAH 25:7"> But when Israel shall be converted to God, the veil shall be done away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 8:2; GALATIANS 5:13; JOHN 8:32"> And the Spirit is the Lord; and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom. &#91;Forsooth the Lord is a Spirit; forsooth where is the Spirit of God, there is liberty.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:6; EPHESIANS 4:22–24; ROMANS 12:2"> And all we that with open face see the glory of the Lord, be transformed into the same image, from clearness into clearness, as of the Spirit of the Lord.</span>
