<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:16; DEUTERONOMY 19:15; DEUTERONOMY 17:6"> Lo! this third time I come to you, and in the mouth of two or of three witnesses every word shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 1:23; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:20–21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:5"> I said before, and say before, as present twice, and now absent, to them that before have sinned, and to all others; for if I come again, I shall not spare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:12; LUKE 21:15"> Whether ye seek the proof &#91;or assaying&#93; of that Christ, that speaketh in me, which is not feeble in you, &#91;but is mighty in you&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:18; ROMANS 6:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:3–4"> For though he was crucified of infirmity, but he liveth of the virtue of God. For also we be sick in him, but we shall live with him of the virtue of God in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:40; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:28; PSALMS 139:23–24"> Assay yourselves, if ye be in the faith; ye yourselves prove. Whether ye know not yourselves, for Christ Jesus is in you? but in hap ye be reprovable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 12:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:3–4"> But I hope, that ye know, that we be not reprovable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:8–9"> And we pray the Lord, that ye do nothing of evil; not that we seem proved, but that ye do that that is good, and that we be as reprovable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30; MARK 9:39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:28–30"> For we may do nothing against truth, but, for the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:10; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:10"> For we joy, when we be sick, but ye be mighty; and we pray this thing, your perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:3; TITUS 1:13"> Therefore I absent write these things, that I present do not harder, by the power, which the Lord gave to me into edification, and not into your destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; ROMANS 12:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:10"> Brethren, henceforward joy ye, be ye perfect, excite ye &#91;or and teach ye&#93;; understand ye the same thing; have ye peace, and God of peace and of love shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; 1ST PETER 5:14; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:26"> Greet ye well together in holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; ROMANS 16:21–23; HEBREWS 13:24"> All holy men greet you well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:22; ROMANS 5:5; ROMANS 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the charity of God, and the communing of the Holy Ghost, be with you all. Amen.</span>
