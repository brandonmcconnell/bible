<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:13; GALATIANS 6:9; EPHESIANS 3:13"> Therefore we that have this administration, &#91;or office&#93;, after this that we have gotten mercy, fail we not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:17; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:3–5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:11"> but do we away the privy things of shame, not walking in subtle guile, neither doing adultery by the word of God &#91;or neither adulterating the word of God&#93;, but in showing of the truth commending us-selves to each conscience of men before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:18; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–11"> For if also our gospel is covered, or is hid, in these that perish it is covered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 12:40; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18"> in which &#91;the&#93; god of this world, that is, the devil, hath blinded the souls of unfaithful men, that the enlightening &#91;or the lighting&#93; of the gospel of the glory of Christ, which is the image of God, shine not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 1:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:23; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:5–6"> But we preach not us-selves, but our Lord Jesus Christ &#91;or but Jesus Christ our Lord&#93;; and us your servants by Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:2; GENESIS 1:3; 1ST PETER 2:9"> For God, that said &#91;the&#93; light to shine of darknesses, he hath given light in our hearts, to the enlightening &#91;or the illumining&#93; of the science of the clearness of God, in the face of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:5–6; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:1; EPHESIANS 2:8–9"> And we have this treasure in brittle vessels, that the worthiness &#91;or the highness&#93; be of God’s virtue, and not of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 1:2–4; ROMANS 8:35–37; 1ST PETER 4:12–14"> In all things we suffer tribulation, but we be not anguished, or annoyed; we be made poor, but we lack nothing &#91;or we be not destitute&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:2; MICAH 7:8; PSALMS 37:24–25"> we suffer persecution, but we be not forsaken; we be made low, but we be not confounded; we be cast down, but we perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 6:5; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:11; 1ST PETER 4:13"> And evermore we bear about the slaying &#91;or the mortifying&#93; of Jesus in our body, that also the life of Jesus be showed in our bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:49; ROMANS 8:36; PSALMS 44:22"> For evermore we that live, be taken into death, for Jesus, that the life of Jesus be showed in our deadly flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:24"> Therefore death worketh in us, but life in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 116:10; HEBREWS 11:1–40; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:12"> And we have the same spirit of faith, as it is written, I have believed, wherefore I have spoken; and we believe, wherefore also we speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 4:14; ROMANS 8:11; JUDE 1:24"> witting that he that raised Jesus, shall raise up also us with Jesus, and shall ordain &#91;us&#93; with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 8:28; EPHESIANS 3:20–21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:11–12"> And all things &#91;be done&#93; for you, that a plenteous grace by many thankings be plenteous into the glory of God. &#91;Soothly all things be done for you, that grace being plenteous by many in doing of thanks be plenteous into glory of God.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:31; COLOSSIANS 3:10; ISAIAH 40:29"> For which thing we fail not, for though our outer man be corrupted; nevertheless the inner man is renewed from day to day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 8:18; 1ST PETER 5:10; ROMANS 5:3–5"> But that light, &#91;or easy&#93;, thing of our tribulation that lasteth now, but as it were by a moment, worketh in us over-measure an everlasting burden &#91;or an everlasting weight&#93; into the highness of glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:7; ROMANS 8:24–25; HEBREWS 11:1"> while that we behold not those things that be seen, but those &#91;things&#93; that be not seen. For those things that be seen, be but during for a short time &#91;or temporal&#93;; but those things that be not seen, be everlasting &#91;or eternal&#93;.</span>
