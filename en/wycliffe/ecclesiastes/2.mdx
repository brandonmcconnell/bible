<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:1; ECCLESIASTES 1:17; PROVERBS 31:4–5"> I thought in mine heart to withdraw my flesh from wine, that I should lead over my soul to wisdom, and that I would eschew folly, till I should see, what were profitable to the sons of men; in which deed the number of days of their life under the sun is needful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:1–12; 2ND CHRONICLES 8:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:10"> I magnified, either made great, my works, I builded houses to me, and I planted vines;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 17:27–29; SONG OF SONGS 4:12–1; JEREMIAH 39:4"> I made yards &#91;or gardens&#93; and orchards, and I set those with trees of all kinds;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 2:14; PSALMS 1:3; JEREMIAH 17:8"> and I made cisterns of waters, for to water the wood of &#91;the&#93; trees growing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 3:4; GENESIS 15:3; 1ST KINGS 4:23"> I had in possession servants and handmaids; and I had much meine, and droves of great beasts, and great flocks of sheep, over all men that were before me in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST KINGS 10:10; 1ST KINGS 9:28; 2ND SAMUEL 19:35"> I gathered together to me silver and gold, and the castles of kings and of provinces; I made to me singers and singeresses, and &#91;the&#93; delights of the sons of men, and cups and vessels in service, to pour out wines;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:25; ECCLESIASTES 1:16; 1ST KINGS 10:23"> and I passed in riches all men that were before me in Jerusalem. Also wisdom dwelled stably with me,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:9; ECCLESIASTES 5:18; ECCLESIASTES 3:22"> and all things which mine eyes desired, I denied not to them; neither I refrained mine heart, that not it used all lust, and delighted itself in these things which I had made ready; and I deemed this my part, if I used my travail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:14; 1ST JOHN 2:16–17; ECCLESIASTES 1:3"> And when I had turned me to all the works which mine hands had made, and to the travails in which I had sweated in vain, I saw in all things vanity and torment of soul, and that nothing under &#91;the&#93; sun dwelleth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:17; ECCLESIASTES 7:25; ECCLESIASTES 1:9–10"> I passed forth to behold wisdom, and errors, and folly; I said, What is a man, that he may pursue &#91;or follow&#93; the king, his maker?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:8; ECCLESIASTES 7:11–12; MATTHEW 6:23"> And I saw, that wisdom went so much before folly, as much as light is diverse from darknesses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 49:10; PROVERBS 17:24; ECCLESIASTES 9:11"> The eyes of a wise man be in his head, a fool goeth in darknesses; and I learned, that one perishing was of ever either.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 6:8; ECCLESIASTES 1:14; ECCLESIASTES 1:2"> And I said in mine heart, If one death shall be both of the fool and of me, what profiteth it to me, that I gave more busyness to wisdom? And I spake with my soul, and perceived, that this also was vanity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:11; ECCLESIASTES 9:5; PSALMS 88:12"> For the mind of a wise man shall not be, in like manner as neither that of a fool, without end, and &#91;the&#93; times to coming &#91;or to come&#93; shall cover all things altogether with forgetting; a learned man dieth in like manner as an unlearned man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:11; JONAH 4:8; ECCLESIASTES 4:2"> And therefore it annoyed me of my life, seeing that all things under &#91;the&#93; sun be evil, and that all things be vanity and torment of the spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:6; PSALMS 49:10; LUKE 16:27–28"> Again I cursed all my busyness, by which I travailed most studiously under &#91;the&#93; sun; and I shall have an heir after me,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JAMES 3:17; ECCLESIASTES 3:22; ECCLESIASTES 9:13"> whom I know not, whether he shall be wise either a fool; and he shall be lord in my travails, for which I sweated greatly, and was busy; and is there anything so vain?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 43:14; JOB 17:11–15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> Wherefore I ceased, and mine heart forsook for to travail further under &#91;the&#93; sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:17–18; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:20–21; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:2"> For why when another man travaileth in wisdom, and teaching, and busyness, he leaveth things gotten to an idle man; and therefore this is vanity, and great evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 127:2; ECCLESIASTES 1:3; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> For why what shall it profit to a man of all his travail, and torment of spirit, with which he was tormented under &#91;the&#93; sun?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 14:1; JOB 5:7; PSALMS 6:6–7"> All his days be full of sorrows and mischiefs, and by night he resteth not in soul; and whether this is not vanity?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:22; ECCLESIASTES 8:15; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:17"> Whether it is not better to eat and drink, and to show to his soul &#91;the&#93; goods of his travails? and this thing is of the hand of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:1–12; 1ST KINGS 4:21–24"> Who shall devour so, and shall flow in delights, as I have?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:22; JOB 27:16–17; PROVERBS 28:8"> God gave wisdom, and knowing, and gladness to a good man in his sight; but he gave torment, and superfluous busyness to a sinner, that he increase, and gather together, and give to him that pleaseth God; but also this is vanity, and vain busyness of soul.</span>
</div>
