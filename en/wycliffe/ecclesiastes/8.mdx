<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 9:12; ROMANS 13:1–4; DANIEL 4:35"> and his word is full of power, and no man may say to him, Why doest thou so?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:14; 1ST PETER 3:13–14; PHILIPPIANS 1:9–10"> He that keepeth the commandment of God, shall not feel anything of evil; the heart of a wise man understandeth time and answer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:1; ECCLESIASTES 3:17; ECCLESIASTES 3:11"> Time and season is to each work; and much torment is of a man,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:14; ECCLESIASTES 9:12; PROVERBS 24:22"> for he knoweth not &#91;the&#93; things passed, and he may not know by any messenger &#91;the&#93; things to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 89:48; JOB 14:5; HEBREWS 9:27"> It is not in the power of man to forbid the spirit, that is, his soul, from going out from the body, neither he hath power in the day of death, neither he is suffered to have rest, when the battle nigheth; neither wickedness shall save a wicked man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:8; ECCLESIASTES 4:7–8; ECCLESIASTES 5:13"> I beheld all these things, and I gave mine heart in all works, that be done under the sun. Sometime a man is lord of a man, to his evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:5; ECCLESIASTES 2:16; PROVERBS 10:7"> I saw wicked men buried, which, when they lived yet, were in holy place; and they were praised in the city, as men of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; works; but also this is vanity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:10; PSALMS 50:21–22; ROMANS 2:4–5"> Forsooth for the sentence is not brought forth soon against evil men, the sons of men do evils without any dread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:11; 2ND PETER 2:9; LUKE 1:50"> Nevertheless of that, that a sinner doeth evil an hundred times, and is suffered by patience, I knew that good it shall be to men dreading God, that reverence his face, or presence.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 6:12; ISAIAH 3:11; JOB 14:2"> Good be not to the wicked man, neither his days be made long; but pass they as shadow, that dread not the face of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:15; ECCLESIASTES 2:14; MALACHI 3:15"> Also another vanity there is, that is done on earth. Just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men there be, to whom evils come, as if they did the works of wicked men; and wicked men there be, that be as secure, as if they had done the deeds of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men; but I deem also this most vain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:24; ECCLESIASTES 5:18; ECCLESIASTES 3:12–13"> Therefore I praised gladness, that no good was to a man under the sun, but to eat, and drink, and to be joyful; and that he should bear away with himself only this of his travail, in the days of his life, which God gave to him under the sun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:13; PSALMS 127:2; ECCLESIASTES 2:23"> And I setted mine heart to know wisdom, and to understand the parting, which is turned in earth. A man is, that by days and nights, taketh not sleep with his eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:11; PSALMS 73:16; ROMANS 11:33"> And I understood, that of all the works of God, a man may find no reason of those things, that be done under the sun; and inasmuch as he travaileth more to seek, by so much he shall find less; yea, though a wise man say that he knoweth, he shall not be able to find.</span>
</div>
