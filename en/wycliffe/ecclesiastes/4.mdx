<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:16; ECCLESIASTES 5:8; ISAIAH 5:7"> I turned me to other things, and I saw false challenges, that be done under the sun, and the tears of the guiltless, and no man a comforter; and that they destitute, either forsaken, of the help of all men, may not against-stand the violence of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 3:11–26; ECCLESIASTES 2:17; ECCLESIASTES 9:4–6"> And I praised more dead men than living men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 23:29; ECCLESIASTES 6:3–5; MATTHEW 24:19"> and I deemed him, that was not born yet, and saw not the evils that be done under the sun, to be more blessed than ever either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:12; ECCLESIASTES 1:14; ECCLESIASTES 2:26"> Again I beheld all the travails of men, and busynesses; and I perceived that those be open to &#91;the&#93; envy of the neighbour; and therefore in this is vanity, and superfluous busyness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:20; PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 6:10–11"> A fool foldeth together his hands, and eateth his flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:8; PROVERBS 15:16–17; PSALMS 37:16"> and saith, Better is an handful, with rest, than ever either hand full, with travail and torment of soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:1; PSALMS 78:33; ZECHARIAH 1:6"> I beheld and found also another vanity under the sun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:16; PROVERBS 27:20; LUKE 12:20"> one there is, and he hath not a second; neither a son, nor a brother; and nevertheless he ceaseth not to travail, neither his eyes be filled with riches; neither he bethinketh him, and saith, To whom travail I, and deceive my soul in goods? In this also is vanity, and the worst torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:17; GENESIS 2:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:18–21"> Therefore it is better, that two be together than one; for they have profit of their fellowship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:18; ISAIAH 35:3–4"> If one falleth down, he shall be underset of the tother; woe to him that is alone, for when he falleth, he hath none to raise him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:1–4"> And if twain sleep together, they shall be nourished together; how shall one be made hot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 3:16–17; EPHESIANS 4:3; 2ND SAMUEL 23:9"> And if any man hath &#91;the&#93; mastery against one, twain &#91;or two&#93; against-stand him; a threefold cord is broken of hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 25:16; 2ND CHRONICLES 16:9–10; PROVERBS 19:1"> A poor man and wise is better than an eld &#91;or old&#93; king and foolish, that cannot before-see into time to coming &#91;or after&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 113:7–8; GENESIS 41:14; JOB 5:11"> For sometime a man goeth out, both from prison and chains, to a realm; and another, born into a realm, is wasted by neediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:6"> I saw all men living that go under the sun, with the second young waxing man, that shall rise for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:14; 1ST KINGS 1:40; ECCLESIASTES 2:17"> The number of people, of all that were before him, is great without measure, and they that shall come afterward, shall not be glad in him; but also this is vanity and torment of the spirit.</span>
