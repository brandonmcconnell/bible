<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:13"> Also another evil there is, which I saw under the sun; and certainly it is oft used with men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:19; PSALMS 17:14; PSALMS 73:7"> A man is, to whom God gave riches, and chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, and honour; and nothing faileth to his soul of all things which he desireth; and God giveth not power to him, that he eat thereof, but a strange man shall devour it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="For God draweth him away suddenly from present life.">a</a></sup>. This is vanity, and a great wretchedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 3:16; JEREMIAH 22:19; ECCLESIASTES 4:3"> If a man engendereth an hundred free sons, and hath many days of age, and his soul useth not the goods of his chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, and wanteth burying; I pronounce of this man, that a dead-born child is better than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 109:13"> For he cometh in vain, and goeth to darknesses; and his name shall be done away by forgetting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 58:8; JOB 14:1; JOB 3:10–13"> He saw not the sun, neither knew the diversity of good and of evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:27; ECCLESIASTES 3:20; JOB 30:23"> also though he live two thousand years, and useth not &#91;the&#93; goods; whether all things hasten not to one place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 6:27; PROVERBS 16:26; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:6–8"> All the travail of a man is in his mouth, but the soul of him shall not be &#91;ful&#93; filled with goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:1; ECCLESIASTES 2:14–16; PSALMS 101:2"> What hath a wise man more than a fool? and what hath a poor man, but that he go thither, where is life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:14; JOB 31:7; ECCLESIASTES 1:2"> It is better to see that, that thou covetest, than to desire that, that thou knowest not; but also this is vanity, and presumption of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 9:32; ISAIAH 45:9–10; JOB 40:2"> The name of him that shall come, is called now, and it is known, that he is a man, and he may not strive in doom against a stronger than himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:16; ECCLESIASTES 5:7; ECCLESIASTES 2:3–11"> Words be full many, and have much vanity in disputing. What need is it to a man to seek greater things than himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JAMES 4:14; JOB 14:2; ECCLESIASTES 3:22"> since he knoweth not, what shall befall to him in his life, in the number of days of his pilgrimage, and in the time that passeth as shadow? either who may show to him, what thing under &#91;the&#93; sun shall come after him?</span>
