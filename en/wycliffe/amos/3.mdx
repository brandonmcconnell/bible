<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:6; EXODUS 19:5–6; DEUTERONOMY 10:15"> Only I knew you of all the kindreds of earth; therefore I shall visit on you all your wickednesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14–16; GENESIS 17:1; GENESIS 5:22"> Whether twain &#91;or two&#93; shall go together, no but it accord to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSEA 11:10; PSALMS 104:21; AMOS 1:2"> Whether a lion shall roar in a forest, no but he have prey? Whether the whelp of a lion shall give voice from his den, no but he take anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:12; JEREMIAH 31:28; DANIEL 9:14"> Whether a bird shall fall into a snare of earth, without a fowler? Whether a snare shall be taken away from earth, before that it take something?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:7; ZEPHANIAH 1:16; ISAIAH 14:24–27"> Whether a trump shall sound in a city, and the people shall not dread? Whether evil shall be in a city, which evil the Lord shall not make?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 15:15; GENESIS 18:17; PSALMS 25:14"> For the Lord God shall not make a word, no but he show his private plan to his servants prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:20; JEREMIAH 20:9; AMOS 3:4"> A lion shall roar, who shall not dread? the Lord God spake, who shall not prophesy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 4:1; AMOS 6:1; AMOS 1:8"> Make ye heard in the houses of Ashdod, and in the houses of the land of Egypt; and say ye, Be ye gathered together on the hills of Samaria, and see ye many strong vengeances in the midst thereof, and them that suffer false challenge in the privy places thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:22; ZEPHANIAH 1:9; ZECHARIAH 5:3–4"> And they could not or knew not how to do rightful &#91;or right&#93; thing, saith the Lord, and they treasured wickedness and raven in their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND KI 18:9–11; 2ND KI 17:3–6; 2ND KI 15:19"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, The land shall be troubled, and be encompassed; and thy strength shall be drawn down of thee, and thine houses shall be ravished &#91;or robbed&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 17:34–37; ROMANS 11:4–5; ISAIAH 31:4"> The Lord saith these things, As if a shepherd ravisheth from the mouth of a lion twain &#91;or two&#93; hips, either the last thing of the ear, so the children of Israel shall be ravished, that dwell in Samaria, in the country of a bed, and in the bed of Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:18–19; 2ND CHRONICLES 24:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:21"> Hear ye, and witness ye in the house of Jacob, saith the Lord God of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND KI 23:15; HOSEA 10:5–8; HOSEA 10:14–15"> For in the day, when I shall begin to visit the trespassings of Israel on him, I shall visit also on the altars of Bethel; and the horns of the altar shall be cut away, and shall fall down into the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:39; JEREMIAH 36:22; AMOS 6:11"> And I shall smite the winter house with the summer house, and the houses of ivory shall perish, and many houses shall be destroyed, saith the Lord.</span>
