<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:17; JEREMIAH 48:7; JEREMIAH 48:25"> And I shall lose a judge of the midst thereof, and I shall slay with it all the princes thereof, saith the Lord.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HOSEA 8:14; JEREMIAH 17:27; JEREMIAH 21:10"> And I shall send fire into Judah, and it shall devour the houses of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOEL 3:3; MICAH 3:2–3; AMOS 5:11–12"> The Lord saith these things, On three great trespasses of Israel, and on four, I shall not convert him, for that that he sold a just man for silver, and a poor man for shoes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 5:12; ISAIAH 10:2; EZEKIEL 22:11"> Which all-foul the heads of poor men on the dust of &#91;the&#93; earth, and bow away the way of meek men; and the son and his father went to a damsel, that they should defoul mine holy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:12–17; EXODUS 22:26–27; AMOS 6:6"> And they ate on clothes laid to wed beside each altar, and drank the wine of condemned men in the house of their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 18:16; MALACHI 4:1; DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> Forsooth I destroyed Amorite from the face of them, whose highness was the highness of cedars, and he was strong as an oak; and I all-brake the fruit of him above, and the roots of him beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:7; EXODUS 12:51; AMOS 3:1"> I am, that made you to go up from the land of Egypt, and led you out in desert by forty years, that ye should wield the land of Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:25; NUMBERS 6:2–3; 1ST KINGS 20:41"> And I raised of your sons into prophets, and Nazarites of your young men. Whether it is not so, ye sons of Israel? saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 7:13; ISAIAH 30:10; JEREMIAH 11:21"> And ye poured out wine to Nazarites, and commanded to prophets, and said, Prophesy ye not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:14; ISAIAH 43:24; EZEKIEL 16:43"> Lo! I shall creak under you, as a wain charged with hay creaketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23; PSALMS 33:16; ECCLESIASTES 9:11"> And flight shall perish from a swift man, and a strong man shall not hold his strength, and a stalworthy man shall not save his life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 33:16–17; EZEKIEL 39:3"> and he that holdeth a bow shall not stand, and a swift man shall not be saved by his feet; and the rider of an horse shall not save his life,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARK 14:52; JEREMIAH 48:41; 2ND KI 7:8–20"> and a strong man of heart shall flee naked among strong men in that day, saith the Lord.</span>
