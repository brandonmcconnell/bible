<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Tobias called to him the angel, whom soothly he guessed a man. And Tobias said to him, Azarias, brother, I ask of thee, that thou hearken my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> If I shall betake or Though I betake myself &#91;a&#93; servant to thee, yet I shall not by my knowing, be even worthy to thy purveyance, or wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Nevertheless I beseech thee, that thou take to thee beasts, either servants, and go thou to Gabael into Rages, a city of Media, and yield or betake thou to him his obligation &#91;or his writ&#93;; and take &#91;or receive&#93; of him the money that he oweth to my father, and pray him to come to my weddings &#91;or to my bridals&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For thou knowest, that my father numbereth the days of our journey, and if I &#91;shall&#93; tarry one day more, his soul &#91;or his life&#93; shall be made sorry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And certainly thou seest, how Raguel hath charged me to dwell here with him, whose charging I may not despise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Raphael took four of the servants of Raguel, and two camels, and went into Rages, a city of Media, and he found Gabael, and gave to him his obligation &#91;or writ&#93;, and received of him all the money;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and he showed to him of Tobias, the son of Tobit, and all things that were done. And he made Gabael to come with him to the weddings &#91;or to the bridals&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when Gabael entered into the house of Raguel, he found Tobias sitting at the meat; and he skipped up/and Tobias rose up anon, and they kissed themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Gabael wept, and blessed God, and said, The Lord God of Israel bless thee, for thou art the son of a full good man, and just &#91;or rightwise&#93;, and dreading God, and doing alms-deeds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and the blessing of God be brought or come upon thy wife, and upon your fathers and mothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and see ye your sons, and the sons of your sons, till into &#91;or unto&#93; the third and the fourth generation; and your seed be blessed of God of Israel, that reigneth into worlds of worlds or without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And when all men had said Amen, they went to the feast or to meat; and they used the feast of those weddings &#91;or bridals&#93; with the dread of the Lord.</span>
