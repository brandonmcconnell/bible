<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Tobias bethought him of the words of the angel, and brought forth or drew out of his scrip &#91;or his bag&#93;, a part of the maw of the fish, and he putted it upon quick coals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And then Raphael, the angel, took the fiend &#91;or caught the devil&#93;, and bound him fast in the desert of higher Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then Tobias admonished the virgin &#91;or the maiden&#93;, and said to her, Sarah, rise up, and pray we God today, and tomorrow, and the second morrow/ and after tomorrow; for in these three nights we be joined to God; and when the third night is passed, we shall be in our marriage &#91;or in our wedlock&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> for we be the children of holy men, and we may not be joined or coupled together as heathen men &#91;or Gentiles&#93;be, that know not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Soothly they rose up together, and they both prayed together busily, that health should be given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Tobias said, Lord God of our fathers, heavens, and all lands or earths, and the sea, and wells, and floods, and each creature of thine, that is in those, bless thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> thou madest Adam of the slime of earth, and thou gavest to him an helper, Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And now, Lord, thou knowest, that I take my sister to wife, not for cause &#91;or because&#93; of lechery, but for love alone of heirs or of begetting &#91;or of bringing forth&#93; of children, in which thy name be blessed into worlds of worlds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And then Sarah said, Lord, have thou mercy on us, have thou mercy on us, and wax we both eld together whole in body and soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And it was done about the cock’s crowing or the crowing of the cock’s, Raguel made &#91;or commanded&#93; his servants to be called, and they went with him to dig a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For he dreaded, lest it had befallen in like manner to Tobias, that befelled also to the other seven men, that entered into wedlock to Sarah before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And when they had made ready a pit, Raguel went again to his wife, and said to her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Send one of thine handmaids, and see she, whether Tobias is dead, that I bury him, before that light of day come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And she sent one of her hand-maids, which entered into the closet, or bed chamber, &#91;or into the bed-place&#93;, and found them safe and sound, sleeping together by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she turned again, and told good message. And they blessed the Lord, that is, Raguel and Edna, his wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and said, Lord God of Israel, we bless thee, for it hath not befallen to us, as we guessed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> for thou hast done thy mercy with us, and hast shut out from us the enemy pursuing us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Soothly thou hast done mercy with two alone. My Lord, make thou them to bless thee fullier &#91;or more fully&#93;, and for to offer to thee the sacrifice of thy praising, and of their health, that the university, or the multitude, of folks know, that thou art God alone in all earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And anon Raguel commanded his servants to fill the pit, which they had made, before that light of day came &#91;or before that it waxed light&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Raguel said to his wife, that she should array a feast, and make ready all things, that were needful to men making journey &#91;or were necessary to way-goers&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Also he made to be slain two fat kine, and four weathers, and &#91;plenteous&#93; meats to be made ready to all his neighbours, and to all his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Raguel made Tobias to swear or to promise him, that he should dwell two weeks at Raguel/that he should abide with him two weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And all things, that Raguel had in possession, he gave the half part to Tobias; and he made this scripture, that the half part, that was left of his chattel, should come to the lordship of Tobias after the death of him and his wife.</span>
