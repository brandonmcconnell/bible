<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth the elder Tobit opened his mouth, and blessed God, and said, Lord, thou art great without end, and thy realm is into all worlds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> for thou beatest &#91;or scourgest&#93;, and makest safe; thou leadest down to hells, and leadest again; for none is that escapeth thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Sons of Israel, acknowledge ye to the Lord, and praise ye him in the sight of heathen men &#91;or Gentiles&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> for therefore he hath scattered you among heathen men, that know not God, that ye tell out his marvels, and make them to know, that none other God is almighty besides him/ that there is none other God Almighty except him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He hath chastised us for our wickednesses; and he shall save us for his mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Therefore behold ye, what things he hath done with you, and acknowledge ye to him with dread and trembling; and enhance ye the King of worlds in your works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Forsooth I in the land of my captivity shall acknowledge to him; for he hath showed his majesty upon &#91;or into&#93; a sinful folk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore, ye sinners, be ye converted, that is, be ye altogether in heart, and word, and deed, turned from your sin, and do ye rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; before God, and then believe ye, that he shall do his mercy with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Soothly I and my soul shall be glad in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> All chosen of the Lord, bless ye him &#91;or Blesseth the Lord, ye all chosen of him&#93;; make ye the days of gladness, and acknowledge ye to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Jerusalem, the city of God, the Lord hath chastised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, shall chastise, for it was not destroyed in the time of Tobit.">a</a></sup>thee for the works of thine hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Acknowledge thou to God in thy goods, and bless thou God of worlds, that is, without ending, that he build again in thee his tabernacle, and again-call to thee all thy prisoners &#91;or call again to thee all thy captives&#93;; and that thou have joy into all worlds of worlds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Thou shalt shine with bright light, and all the coasts of the earth shall worship &#91;or shall honour&#93; thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Nations, that is, many men from diverse countries, shall come from &#91;a&#93; far to thee, and they shall bring gifts to thee, and shall worship the Lord in thee, and they shall have thy land into hallowing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> for they shall call in thee the great name./and in thee, Jerusalem, they shall inwardly call the great name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they shall be cursed, that despise thee, and they shall be damned or condemned, that blaspheme thee; and they shall be blessed, that build &#91;up&#93; thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Forsooth, Jerusalem, thou shalt be glad in thy sons, for all they shall be blessed &#91;or for all shall be blessed&#93;, and they shall be gathered together to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Blessed be all that love thee, and that have joy upon thy peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> My soul, bless thou the Lord, for the Lord our God hath delivered Jerusalem, his city, from all tribulations thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I shall be blessed, if the relics or the after-comers of my seed shall be abiding to see the clearness &#91;or the clarity&#93; of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The gates of Jerusalem shall be builded of sapphire, and of smaragdus, and of precious stones; all the compass of the walls thereof shall be of white and clean stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And all the streets thereof shall be strewed; and alleluia, that is, the praising of God, shall be sung through the streets thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Blessed be the Lord, that hath enhanced it, that his realm be upon it into worlds of worlds. Amen.</span>
