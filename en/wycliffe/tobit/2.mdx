<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth after these things, when a feast day of the Lord was, and a good meat was made in the house of Tobit, he said to his son,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Go thou, and bring some men of our lineage, that dread God, that they eat with us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And when he, young Tobias, was gone forth, he turned again, and told to his father, that one of the sons of Israel lay strangled in the street; and anon Tobit rose up from his sitting place, and left the meat, and came fasting to the body;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and he took it, and bare it to his house privily, for to bury him warily &#91;or slyly&#93;, when the sun was gone down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when he had hid the body, he ate bread with mourning and trembling,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and remembered the word, which the Lord said by Amos, the prophet, Your feast days shall be turned into mourning and lamentation, either wailing, &#91;or into wailing and sorrowing&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And when the sun was gone down, Tobit went, and buried him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Forsooth all his neighbours blamed &#91;or reproved&#93; him, and said, Now for the cause &#91;or because&#93; of this thing thou were commanded to be slain, and scarcely thou hast escaped the behest &#91;or the commandment&#93; of death, and again thou buriest dead men?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But Tobit dreaded more God than the king, and he took away the bodies of slain men, and hid them in his house, and buried those in the middle of nights &#91;or in the midnights he buried them&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And it befelled, that in a day Tobit was made weary of burying dead bodies; and he came home, and laid himself beside a wall, and slept there;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and while he slept, hot turds, or drit, fell down from the nest of swallows upon his eyes; and he was made blind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And therefore the Lord suffered this temptation to befall to him, that the ensample of his patience should be given to after-comers, as also it is of holy Job.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For why when Tobit dreaded God ever&#91;more&#93; from his young childhood, and kept his commandments, he was not sorry, or heavy, or grutching against God, for that the sickness of blindness came to him;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> but he dwelled unmovable in the dread of God, and did thankings to God in all the days of his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For why as kings upbraided saint or blessed Job, so it befelled to this Tobit, that his elders and kinsmen scorned his life, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Where is thine hope<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="These believed the rewarding of good and of evil is only in present life, as the friends of Job did.">a</a></sup>, for which thou didest alms-deeds and buryings?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Tobit blamed them, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Do not ye speak so, for we be the sons of holy men, and we abide that life, which God shall give to them that change never their faith from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Anna, his wife, went each day to the work of weaving, and she brought home the livelode &#91;or the lifelode&#93; which she might get of the travail of her hands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Whereof it befell, that she took a kid of goats, for her weaving, and she brought it home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And when her husband had heard the voice of this kid bleating, he said, Look ye, lest peradventure this kid be gotten of theft &#91;or it be stolen&#93;, but if it so be yield ye it again to his lords; for it is not leaveful, either to eat either to touch anything of theft.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> At these words the wife of Tobit was wroth, and answered, Now is openly thine hope made vain, and thine alms-deeds have appeared, that is, feigned and void, as done for hypocrisy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And by these and other such words she said shame &#91;or put reproof&#93; to him.</span>
</div>
</div>
