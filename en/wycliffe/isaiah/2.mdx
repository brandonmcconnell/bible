<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:29–30; ISAIAH 2:11; ISAIAH 13:11"> And all the highness of men shall be bowed down, and the highness of men shall be made low; and the Lord alone shall be raised &#91;up&#93; in that day,</span>
