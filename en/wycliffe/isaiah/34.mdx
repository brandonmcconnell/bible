</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:13–14; MATTHEW 24:29; EZEKIEL 32:7–8"> And all the chivalry of heavens shall fail, and heavens shall be folded together as a book, and all the knighthood of those shall float down, as the leaf of a vinery &#91;or a vine&#93; and of a fig tree falleth down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:10; MALACHI 1:4; ISAIAH 63:1"> For my sword is filled in heaven; lo! it shall come down on Idumea, and on the people of my slaying, to doom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:1; JEREMIAH 49:13; ISAIAH 34:5"> The sword of the Lord is filled of blood, it is made fat of the inner fatness of the blood of lambs and of bucks of goats, of the blood of rams full of marrow; for why the slain sacrifice of the Lord is in Bozrah, and great slaying is in the land of Edom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 68:30; NUMBERS 23:22; JEREMIAH 50:27"> And unicorns shall go down with them, and bulls with them that be mighty; the land of them shall be filled with blood, and the earth of them with &#91;the&#93; inner fatness of fat beasts;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:4; ISAIAH 61:2; JEREMIAH 46:10"> for it is a day of vengeance of the Lord, a year of yielding of the doom of Zion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:23; PSALMS 11:6; GENESIS 19:28"> And the strands &#91;or streams&#93; thereof shall be turned into pitch, and the earth thereof into brimstone; and the land thereof shall be into burning pitch, night and day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:3; ISAIAH 13:20; MALACHI 1:3–4"> It shall not be quenched without end, the smoke thereof shall go up from generation into generation, and it shall be desolate into worlds of worlds; none shall pass thereby.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:8; 2ND KI 21:13; ZEPHANIAH 2:14"> And an onocrotalus, and an urchin, shall wield it; and a capret, and a crow shall dwell therein; and a measure shall be stretched forth thereon, that it be driven to nought, and an hanging plummet into desolation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:6–8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:2; JEREMIAH 39:6"> The noble men thereof shall not be there; rather they shall call the king into help, and all the princes thereof shall be into nought.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:11; JEREMIAH 10:22; MALACHI 1:3"> And thorns and nettles shall grow in the houses thereof, and a teasel in the strongholds thereof; and it shall be the couch of dragons, and the pasture of ostriches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 13:21–22"> And fiends, and wonderful beasts, like men in the higher part, and like asses in the nether part, and an hairy, shall meet; one shall cry to another. Lamia shall lie there, and find rest there to herself;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:13"> there an urchin had ditches, and nourished out whelps, and digged about, and fostered in the shadow thereof; there kites were gathered together, one to another.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 33:9; JOSHUA 1:8; MATTHEW 5:18"> Seek ye diligently in the book of the Lord, and read ye; one of those things failed not, one sought not another; for he commanded that thing, that goeth forth of my mouth, and his spirit, he gathered them together.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 34:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:26; ISAIAH 17:14"> And he sent to them heritage, and his hand parted it in measure; till into without end they shall wield that land, in generation and into generation they shall dwell therein.</span>
</div>
