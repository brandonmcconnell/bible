<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:2; ISAIAH 1:2; HOSEA 7:13"> Woe! sons forsakers, saith the Lord, that ye shall make a counsel, and not of me; and weave a web, and not by my spirit, that ye should increase sin on sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:6; NUMBERS 27:21; DEUTERONOMY 28:68"> Which go, to go down into Egypt, and ye asked not my mouth; ye hoping help in the strength of Pharaoh, and ye having trust in the shadow of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 20:5; ROMANS 10:11; JEREMIAH 17:5–6"> And the strength of Pharaoh shall be to you into confusion, and the trust of the shadow of Egypt into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:11; ISAIAH 57:9; JEREMIAH 43:7"> For why thy princes were in Tanis, and thy messengers came till to Hanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:36; ISAIAH 30:7; ISAIAH 30:16"> All they were shamed on the people, that might not profit to them; they were not into help, and into any profit, but into shame and shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:15; ISAIAH 46:1–2; JEREMIAH 11:4"> The burden of &#91;the&#93; work beasts of the south. In the land of tribulation and of anguish, a lioness, and a lion, of them a serpent, and a &#91;flying&#93; cockatrice; they were bearing their riches on the shoulders of work beasts, and their treasures on the botch of camels, to a people that might not profit to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 2:22; ISAIAH 7:4; PSALMS 118:8–9"> For why Egypt shall help in vain, and idly. Therefore I cried on this thing, It is pride only; cease thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:22; HABAKKUK 2:2; ISAIAH 8:1"> Now therefore enter thou, and write to it on &#91;a&#93; box &#91;table&#93;, and write thou it diligently in a book; and it shall be in the last day into witnessing, till into without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; ZECHARIAH 1:4–6"> For it is a people stirring me to wrathfulness &#91;or wrath&#93;, and sons liars, sons that will not hear the law of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:7–10; ROMANS 16:18; AMOS 2:12"> Which say to prophets, Do not ye prophesy; and to beholders, Do not ye behold to us those things that be rightful &#91;or right&#93;; speak ye things pleasing to us, see ye errors to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 8:7; ROMANS 1:30; JOHN 15:23–24"> Do ye away from me the way, bow ye away from me the path; the Holy of Israel cease from our face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 31:1–3; ISAIAH 5:24; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:8"> Therefore the Holy of Israel saith these things, For that that ye reproved this word, and hoped on false challenge, and on noise, and trusted on it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:5; EZEKIEL 13:10–15; 1ST KINGS 20:30"> therefore this wickedness shall be to you as a breaking falling down, and sought in an high wall; for suddenly while it is not hoped, the breaking thereof shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 2:9; JEREMIAH 19:10–11; 2ND PETER 2:4–5"> And it shall be made less, as a vessel of a potter is broken with full strong breaking; and a shard shall not be found of the gobbets, either remnants, thereof, in which shard a little fire shall be borne of burning, either a little of water shall be drawn of the ditch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:3–4; ISAIAH 32:17; JEREMIAH 3:22–23"> For why the Lord God, the Holy of Israel, saith these things, If ye turn again, and rest, ye shall be safe; in stillness and in hope shall be your strength. And ye would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 31:1; ISAIAH 31:3; DEUTERONOMY 28:25"> And ye said, Nay, but we shall flee to horses; therefore ye shall flee. And we shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; on swift horses; therefore they shall be swifter, that shall pursue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:25; JOSHUA 23:10; DEUTERONOMY 32:30"> A thousand men shall flee from the face of the dread of one; and ye shall flee from the face of &#91;the&#93; dread of five, till ye be left as the mast of a ship in the top of a mountain, and as a sign on a little hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:25–26; 2ND PETER 3:9; PROVERBS 16:20"> Therefore the Lord abideth, that he have mercy on you, and therefore he shall be enhanced sparing you; for why God is Lord of doom, blessed be all they that abide him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:20; PSALMS 50:15; ISAIAH 65:24"> Forsooth the people of Zion shall dwell in Jerusalem; thou weeping shalt not weep, he doing mercy shall have mercy on thee; at the voice of thy cry, anon as he heareth, he shall answer to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:27; PSALMS 80:5; AMOS 8:11–12"> And the Lord shall give to thee strait bread, and short water, and shall no more make thy teacher to flee away from thee; and thine eyes shall be seeing thy commander,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; PROVERBS 3:5–6; ISAIAH 58:11"> and thine ears shall hear a word behind the back of him that admonisheth; This is the way, go ye therein, neither to the right half, neither to the left half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JUDGES 17:3–4; ISAIAH 31:7; ZECHARIAH 13:2"> And thou shalt defoul the plates of the graven images of thy silver, and the cloth of the molten image of thy gold; and thou shalt scatter them, as the uncleanness of a woman in unclean blood; Go thou out, and thou shalt say to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:11–12; ISAIAH 32:20; PSALMS 65:9–13"> And rain shall be given to thy seed, wherever thou shalt sow in &#91;the&#93; earth, and the bread of &#91;the&#93; fruits of &#91;the&#93; earth shall be most plenteous and fat; in that day a lamb shall be fed largely in thy possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 45:6; DEUTERONOMY 25:4; 1ST SAMUEL 8:12"> And thy bulls and colts of asses, that work the land, shall eat barley with chaff meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; together, as it is winnowed in the cornfloor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:19–20; ISAIAH 35:6–7; ISAIAH 2:14–15"> And strands &#91;or rivers&#93; of running waters shall be on each high mountain, and on each little hill raised, in the day of slaying of many men, when towers fall down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:19–20; DEUTERONOMY 32:39; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:5"> And the light of the moon shall be as the light of the sun, and the light of the sun shall be sevenfold, as the light of seven days, in the day in which the Lord shall bind together the wound of his people, and shall make whole the smiting of the wounds thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:8; NAHUM 1:5–6; PSALMS 18:7–9"> Lo! the name of the Lord cometh down from &#91;a&#93; far; his strong vengeance is burning, and grievous to bear; his lips be filled of indignation, and his tongue is as fire devouring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:29; ISAIAH 11:4; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:8"> His spirit is as a stiff stream, flowing till to the midst of the neck, to lose folks into nought, and the bridle of error, that was in the cheeks of peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; PSALMS 95:1–2; EXODUS 15:1–21"> Song shall be to you, as the voice of an hallowed solemnity; and gladness of heart, as he that goeth with a pipe, for to enter into the hill of the Lord, to the Strong of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 18:13–14; JOSHUA 10:11; ISAIAH 29:6"> And the Lord shall make heard the glory of his voice, and he shall show the fearedfulness of his arm in menacing &#91;or threatening&#93; of strong vengeance, and in flame of fire burning; he shall hurtle down in whirlwind, and in &#91;the&#93; stone of hail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:4; ISAIAH 9:4; MICAH 5:5–6"> For why Assur smitten with a rod shall dread of the voice of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:15; ISAIAH 19:16; ISAIAH 2:19"> and the passing of the rod shall be founded, which rod the Lord shall make for to rest on him. In tympans, and harps, and in sovereign battles he shall overcome them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND KI 23:10; GENESIS 19:24; JEREMIAH 19:6"> For why Tophet, that is, hell, deep and alarged, is made ready of the king from yesterday; the nourishings thereof be fire and many trees; the blast of the Lord, as a stream of brimstone, kindleth it.</span>
