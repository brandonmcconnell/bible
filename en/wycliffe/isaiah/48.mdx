<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; ROMANS 2:17; ISAIAH 52:1"> For they be called of the holy city, and be stablished on the God of Israel, the Lord of hosts is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:9; JOSHUA 21:45; ISAIAH 41:22"> From that time I told &#91;out&#93; the former things, and those went out of my mouth; and I made them known; suddenly I wrought, and those things came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; EXODUS 32:9; ZECHARIAH 7:11–12"> For I knew that thou art hard, and thy noll is a sinew of iron, and thy forehead is of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:3; LUKE 1:70; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:18"> I before-said to thee from that time; before that those things came, I showed to thee; lest peradventure thou wouldest say, Mine idols did these things, and my graven images, and my molten images, sent these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:1; ISAIAH 42:9; ROMANS 16:25–26"> which thou heardest. See thou all things, but ye told not. I made heard new things to thee from that time, and things be kept which thou knowest not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> now those be made of nought, and not from that time, and before the day, and thou heardest not those things; lest peradventure thou say, Lo! I knew those things.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 79:9; PSALMS 106:8; PSALMS 143:11"> For my name I shall make far my strong vengeance, and with my praising I shall refrain &#91;or bridle&#93; thee, lest thou perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:12; PROVERBS 17:3; JOB 23:10"> Lo! I have sodden thee, but not as silver; I chose thee in the chimney of poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:8; EZEKIEL 20:9; ISAIAH 37:35"> I shall do for me, that I be not blasphemed, and I shall not give my glory to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:8"> Jacob and Israel, whom I call, hear thou me; I myself, I am the first, and I am the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:10–12; PSALMS 102:25; ISAIAH 40:26"> And mine hand founded the earth, and my right hand meted &#91;or measured&#93; heavens; I shall call them, and they shall stand together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:9; JEREMIAH 50:21–29; ISAIAH 13:17–18"> All ye be gathered together, and hear; who of them told &#91;out&#93; these things? The Lord loved him, he shall do his will in Babylon, and his arm in Chaldees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:8; ISAIAH 45:1–2; PSALMS 45:4"> I, I spake, and called him; I brought him, and his way was addressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 18:20; JOHN 20:21–22; JOHN 3:34"> Nigh ye to me, and hear ye these things; at the beginning I spake not in huddles, either private; from time, before that things were made, I was there, and now the Lord God, and his Spirit, sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; PSALMS 25:12; JOB 22:21–22"> The Lord, thine again-buyer, the Holy of Israel, saith these things, I am thy Lord God, teaching thee profitable things, and I govern thee in the way, wherein thou goest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:29; ISAIAH 66:12; PSALMS 119:165"> I would that thou haddest perceived my commandments, thy peace had been made as &#91;a&#93; flood, and thy rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; as the swells of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:22; GENESIS 22:17; ROMANS 9:27"> and thy seed had been as gravel, and the generation of thy womb, as the little stones thereof; the name of it had not perished, and had not been all-broken from my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:45; JEREMIAH 50:8; ISAIAH 52:9"> Go ye out of Babylon, flee ye from Chaldees; tell ye in the voice of full out joying; make ye this heard, and bear ye it unto the last parts of &#91;the&#93; earth; say ye, The Lord again-bought his servant Jacob.</span>
