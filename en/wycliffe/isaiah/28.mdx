<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HOSEA 7:5; ISAIAH 28:7; 2ND KI 14:25–27"> Woe to the crown of pride, to the drunken men of Ephraim, and to the flower falling down of the glory of the full out joying thereof, that were in the top of the fattest valley, and erred of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:30; NAHUM 1:8; EZEKIEL 13:11"> Lo! the mighty and strong Lord, as the fierceness of hail, and whirlwind breaking altogether, as the fierceness of many waters flowing, and sent out on a large land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:6; HEBREWS 10:29; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:2"> The crown of pride of the drunken men of Ephraim shall be defouled with feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NAHUM 3:12; MICAH 7:1; ISAIAH 28:1"> and the flower of &#91;the&#93; glory of the full out joying of him, that is on the top of the valley of fat things, shall be falling down, as a timely thing before the ripeness of harvest; which when a man seeing beholdeth, anon as he taketh with hand, he shall devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:4; ISAIAH 62:3; ISAIAH 60:19"> In that day the Lord of hosts shall be a crown of glory, and a garland of full out joying, to the residue of his people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 18:32–34; JOHN 5:30; JOSHUA 1:9"> and a spirit of doom to him that sitteth on the throne, and strength to them that turn again from &#91;the&#93; battle to the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:10–12; LUKE 21:34; ISAIAH 19:14"> But also they knew not for wine, and erred for drunkenness; the priest and prophet knew not for drunken-ness; they were sopped up of wine, they erred in drunkenness; they knew not a prophet, they knew not doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:26; PROVERBS 26:11; HABAKKUK 2:15–16"> For why all boards were filled with spewing and filths, so that there was no more place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 131:2; JEREMIAH 6:10; JOHN 12:38"> Whom shall he teach knowing, and whom shall he make to understand &#91;the&#93; hearing? Men weaned from milk, men drawn away from teats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:13; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:7; JEREMIAH 11:7"> For why command thou, command thou again; command thou, command thou again; abide thou, abide thou again; abide thou, abide thou again; a little there, a little there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:21; DEUTERONOMY 28:49; ISAIAH 33:19"> For why in &#91;the&#93; speech of lip, and in other language, he shall speak to this people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:28–29; ISAIAH 30:15; JEREMIAH 6:16"> to which he said, This is my rest; refresh ye a weary man, and this is my refreshing; and they would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:14; ISAIAH 6:9–10; 2ND PETER 3:16"> And the word of the Lord shall be to them, Send thou, send thou again; send thou, send thou again; abide thou, abide thou again; abide thou, abide thou again; a little there, a little there; that they go, and fall backward, and be all-broken, and be snared, and be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:22; ISAIAH 29:20; ISAIAH 1:10"> For this thing, ye men scorners, that be lords over my people which is in Jerusalem, hear the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:7–8; EZEKIEL 13:22; AMOS 2:4"> For ye said, We have smitten a bond of peace with death, and we have made covenant with hell; a scourge flowing, when it shall pass, shall not come &#91;up&#93; on us, for we have set a leasing our hope, and we be covered with a leasing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:11–12; 1ST PETER 2:6–8"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall send in the foundaments of Zion a cornerstone precious, proved, founded in the foundament; he that believeth, shall not hasten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 21:13; AMOS 7:7–9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:21"> And I shall set doom in weight, and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; in measure; and hail shall destroy the hope of leasing, and waters shall flow on protection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:7; ISAIAH 8:10; ISAIAH 28:15"> And your bond of peace with death shall be done away, and your covenant with hell shall not stand; when the scourge flowing shall pass, ye shall be to it into defouling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:16; ISAIAH 36:22; 2ND KI 24:2"> Whenever it shall pass, it shall take away you; for why early in the gray morrowtide it shall pass, in day and night; and only travail alone shall give understanding to &#91;the&#93; hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 64:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:18–31; ROMANS 9:30–32"> Forsooth the bed is strait, so that the tother fall down; and a short mantle shall not cover ever either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 14:11; JOSHUA 10:12; 2ND SAMUEL 5:20"> For as in the hill of partings &#91;or divisions&#93; the Lord shall stand, as in the valley, which is in Gibeon, he shall be wroth, that he do his work; his work alien &#91;or alien is his work&#93;, that he work his work; his work is strange from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:22–23; ISAIAH 24:1–23; MATTHEW 27:44"> And now do not ye scorn, lest peradventure your bonds be made strait together; for I heard of the Lord God of hosts, ending and abridging on all earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:2; JEREMIAH 22:29; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:11"> Perceive ye with ears, and hear ye my voice; perceive ye, and hear ye my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:3; HOSEA 10:11–12"> Whether he that eareth, shall ear all day, for to sow, and shall he carve, and purge his land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:9; MATTHEW 23:23; EXODUS 9:31–32"> Whether when he hath made even the face thereof, shall he not sow gith, and sprinkle abroad cumin? and he shall not set wheat by order, and barley, and millet, and fitches in his coasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 31:3–6; EXODUS 36:2; JOB 39:17"> And his God shall teach him, in doom he shall teach him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:15; ISAIAH 27:7–8; JEREMIAH 46:28"> Forsooth gith shall not be threshed in saws, and a wheel of a wain shall not compass on &#91;the&#93; cumin; but gith shall be beaten out with a rod, and &#91;the&#93; cumin with a staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:9–10; MATTHEW 3:12; JOHN 12:24"> Soothly bread shall be made less, but he that thresheth shall not thresh it without end, neither shall travail it with a wheel of a wain, neither shall make it less with his claws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ROMANS 11:33; JEREMIAH 32:19; JOB 5:9"> And this thing went out of the Lord God of hosts, that he should make wonderful counsel, and magnify rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
