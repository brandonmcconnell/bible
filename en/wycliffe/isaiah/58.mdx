<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TITUS 2:15; HOSEA 8:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:26–27"> Cry thou, cease thou not; as a trump enhance thy voice, and show thou to my people their great trespasses, and to the house of Jacob their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TITUS 1:16; ISAIAH 29:13; MATTHEW 15:7–9"> For they seek me from day into day, and they will to know my ways; as a folk, that hath done rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and that hath not forsaken the doom of their God; they pray me dooms of rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and they will to nigh to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 18:9–12; MALACHI 3:14; LEVITICUS 16:29"> Why fasted we, and thou beheldest not; we meeked our souls, and thou knewest not? Lo! your will is found in the day of your fasting, and ye ask all your debtors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 21:9–13; ISAIAH 59:2; PROVERBS 21:27"> Lo! ye fast to chidings and strivings, and smite with the fist wickedly. Do not ye fast, as ye have unto this day, that your cry be heard on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 9:3–19; ZECHARIAH 7:5; ISAIAH 49:8"> Whether such is the fasting which I choose, a man to torment his soul by day? whether to bind his head as a circle, and to make ready a sackcloth and ashes? Whether thou shalt call this a fasting, and a day acceptable to the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 5:10–12; ISAIAH 58:9; JEREMIAH 34:8–11"> Whether not this is more the fasting, which I choose? Unbind thou the bindings together of unpity, either of cruelty, release thou &#91;the&#93; burdens pressing down; deliver thou them free, that be broken, and break thou each burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:35–45; LUKE 3:11; 1ST JOHN 3:17–18"> Break thy bread to the hungry man, and bring into thine house needy men and harbourless; when thou seest a naked man, cover thou him, and despise not thy flesh, that is, brother or sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:12; JEREMIAH 33:6; PSALMS 37:6"> Then thy light shall break out as the morrowtide, and thine health shall rise full soon; and thy rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; shall go before thy face, and the glory of the Lord shall gather thee to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:12–13; PSALMS 50:15; ISAIAH 65:24"> Then thou shalt call to help, and the Lord shall hear; thou shalt cry, and he shall say, Lo! I am present, for I am merciful, thy Lord God. If thou takest away the chain from the midst of thee, and ceasest to hold forth the finger, and to speak that that profiteth not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:24–25; ISAIAH 58:7–8; ISAIAH 42:16"> when thou sheddest &#91;or shalt pour&#93; out thy soul, either thy will, to an hungry man, and &#91;ful&#93; fillest a soul that is tormented, thy light shall rise in darknesses, and thy darknesses shall be as midday.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; JEREMIAH 31:12; PSALMS 32:8"> And the Lord thy God shall give ever rest to thee, and shall &#91;ful&#93; fill thy soul with shinings, and shall deliver thy bones; and thou shalt be as a watered garden, and as a well of waters, whose waters shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 61:4; EZEKIEL 36:33; AMOS 9:14"> And the forsaken things of world’s shall be builded in thee, and thou shalt raise the foundaments of generation and generation; and thou shalt be called, A builder of hedges, turning away the paths of wicked-nesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:21–27; PSALMS 92:1–2; NEHEMIAH 13:15–22"> If thou turnest away thy foot from the sabbath, to do thy &#91;own&#93; will in mine holy day; and callest the sabbath delicate, and holy, the glorious of the Lord; and glorifiest him, while thou doest not thy ways, and thy will is not found, that thou speak a word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:13; JOB 22:26; ISAIAH 33:16"> then thou shalt delight on the Lord, and I shall raise thee &#91;up&#93; on the highness of the earth, and I shall feed thee with the heritage of Jacob, thy father; for why the mouth of the Lord spake.</span>
