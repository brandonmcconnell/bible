</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND KI 18:25; ISAIAH 10:5–7; 1ST KINGS 13:18"> and now whether I ascended &#91;or went up&#93; to this land without the Lord, that I should destroy it? The Lord said to me, Ascend thou &#91;or Go up&#93; on this land, and destroy thou it.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND KI 18:27; LEVITICUS 26:29; LAMENTATIONS 4:9–10"> And Rabshakeh said to them, Whether my lord sent me to thy lord, and to thee, that I should speak all these words, and not rather to the men that sit on the wall, that they eat their turds, and drink the piss off their feet, with you?</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 18:32; DEUTERONOMY 8:7–9; JOB 20:17"> till I come, and take away you to a land which is as your land; to a land of wheat and of wine, to a land of loaves and of vineries &#91;or vines&#93;.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:9–11; JEREMIAH 49:23; 2ND SAMUEL 8:9"> Where is the god of Hamath, and of Arphad? Where is the god of Sepharvaim? Whether they delivered Samaria from mine hand?</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:4; PSALMS 38:13–15; MATTHEW 7:6"> And they were still, and answered not to him a word. For why the king commanded to them, and said, Answer ye not to him.</span>
