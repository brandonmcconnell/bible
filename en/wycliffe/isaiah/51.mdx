<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:7; PSALMS 94:15; HEBREWS 12:14"> Hear ye me, that follow that that is just, and seek the Lord. Take ye heed to the stone, from whence ye be hewn down, and to the cave of the pit, from which ye be cut down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:24; GALATIANS 3:9–14; ROMANS 4:1–5"> Take ye heed to Abraham, your father, and to Sarah, that childed you; for I called him when he was but one, or without an heir, and I blessed him, and I multiplied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:13; JOEL 2:3; ISAIAH 52:9"> Therefore the Lord shall comfort Zion, and he shall comfort all the fallings thereof; and he shall set the desert thereof in delights, and the wilderness thereof as a garden of the Lord; joy and gladness shall be found therein, the doing of thankings and the voice of praising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:9; ISAIAH 2:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:21"> My people, take ye heed to me, and, my lineage, hear ye me; for why a law shall go out from me, and my doom shall rest into the light of peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 10:17–18; ISAIAH 46:13; ISAIAH 42:4"> My just &#91;or rightwise&#93;man is nigh, my saviour is gone out, and mine arms shall deem peoples; isles shall abide me, and shall suffer mine arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:35; PSALMS 102:25–26; ISAIAH 34:4"> Raise your eyes to heaven, and see ye under earth beneath; for why heavens shall melt away as smoke, and the earth shall be all-broken as a cloth, and the dwellers thereof shall perish as these things; but mine health shall be without end, and my rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:11; PSALMS 37:31; EZEKIEL 2:6"> Ye people, that know the just &#91;or rightwise&#93;man, hear me, my law is in the heart of them; do not ye dread the shame of men, and dread ye not the blasphemies of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:6; ISAIAH 50:9; HOSEA 5:12"> For why a worm shall eat them so as a cloth, and a moth shall devour them so as wool; but mine health shall be without end, and my rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; into generations of generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 74:13–14; ISAIAH 52:1; PSALMS 89:10"> Rise thou, rise thou, arm of the Lord, be thou clothed in strength; rise thou, as in &#91;the&#93; eld days, in generations of worlds. Whether thou smitedest not the proud man, wound-edest not the dragon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:16; ISAIAH 63:11–12; ISAIAH 50:2"> Whether thou driedest not the sea, the water of the great depth, which settedest &#91;or puttest&#93; the depth of the sea to be a way, that men that were delivered, should pass &#91;over&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:17"> And now they that be again-bought of the Lord shall turn again, and shall come praising into Zion, and everlasting gladness on the heads of them; they shall hold joy and gladness, and sorrow and wailing shall flee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 118:6; 1ST PETER 1:24; ISAIAH 2:22"> I, I myself, that is, I am he, I am he, that shall comfort you; who art thou, that thou dreadest of a deadly man, and of the son of man, that shall wax dry so as hay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:12; ISAIAH 40:22; PSALMS 104:2"> And thou hast forgotten the Lord, thy Creator, that stretched abroad heavens, and founded the earth; and thou dreadedest continually all day of the face of his strong vengeance, that did tribulation to thee, and made ready for to lose. Where is now the strong vengeance of the troubler?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:20; ZECHARIAH 9:11; ISAIAH 52:2"> Soon he shall come, going for to open; and he shall not slay till to death, neither his bread shall fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:35; ISAIAH 54:5; AMOS 9:5–6"> Forsooth I am thy Lord God, that trouble the sea, and the waves thereof wax great; the Lord of hosts is my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:21; ISAIAH 49:2; JOHN 17:8"> I have put my words in thy mouth, and I defended thee in the shadow of mine hand; that thou plant heavens, and found the earth, and say to Zion, Thou art my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:10; PSALMS 60:3"> Be thou raised, be thou raised, rise thou, Jerusalem, that hast drunk of the hand of the Lord the cup of his wrath; thou hast drunk unto the bottom of the cup of sleep, thou hast drunk of unto the dregs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:21; PSALMS 88:18; PSALMS 142:4"> None there is that sustaineth it, of all the sons which it engendered; and none there is that taketh the hand thereof, of all the sons which it nourished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="AMOS 7:2; ISAIAH 47:9; EZEKIEL 14:21"> Two things there be that came to thee; who shall be sorry on thee? destroying, and defouling, and hunger, and sword. Who shall comfort thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:11–12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:9–11; EZEKIEL 39:19"> Thy sons be cast forth, they slept in the head of all ways, as the beast oryx, taken with a snare; they be full of &#91;the&#93; indignation of the Lord, of the blaming of thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:9; ISAIAH 54:11; ISAIAH 51:17"> Therefore thou poor, and drunken, not of wine, hear these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:23; ISAIAH 51:17; JEREMIAH 50:34"> The Lordly Governor, thy Lord, and thy God, that fought for his people, saith these things, Lo! I have taken from thine hand the cup of sleep, the bottom of the cup of mine indignation; I shall not lay to, that thou drink it any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:24; ZECHARIAH 12:2; ISAIAH 49:25–26"> And I shall set it in the hand of them that made thee low, and said to thy soul, Be thou bowed, that we pass; and thou hast set thy body as earth, and as a way to them that go forth.</span>
