<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:17; ISAIAH 50:2; NUMBERS 11:23"> Lo! the hand of the Lord is not abridged, that he may not save, neither his ear is made hard, that he hear not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:25; PROVERBS 15:29; MICAH 3:4"> but your wickednesses have parted betwixt you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he should not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:15; EZEKIEL 13:8; JEREMIAH 2:34"> For why your hands be defouled with blood, and your fingers with wickedness; your lips spake leasing, and your tongue speaketh wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 15:35; JAMES 1:15; MICAH 7:2–5"> None there is, that calleth rightful-ness to help, and none is, that deemeth verily; but they trust in nought, and speak vanities; they conceived travail, and childed wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:7; ISAIAH 14:29; PROVERBS 23:32"> The have broken eggs of snakes, and made webs of an araneid; he that eateth of the eggs of them, shall die, and that that is nursed, or hatched, shall break out into a cockatrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 57:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17–18; JEREMIAH 6:7"> The webs of them shall not be into cloth&#91;ing&#93;, neither they shall be covered with their works; the works of them be unprofitable works, and the work of wickedness is in the hands of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 3:15–17; PROVERBS 6:17; MARK 7:21–22"> The feet of them run to evil, and haste to shed out innocent blood; the thoughts of them be unprofitable thoughts; destroying and defouling be in the ways of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 57:20–21; ROMANS 3:17; LUKE 1:79"> They knew not the way of peace, and doom is not in the goings of them; the paths of them be bowed to them; each that treadeth in those &#91;or in them&#93;, knoweth not peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:30; JOB 30:26; LAMENTATIONS 5:16–17"> Therefore doom is made far from us, and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; shall not take us; we abided light, and lo! darknesses be; we abided shining, and we went in darknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:29; JOB 5:14; LAMENTATIONS 3:6"> We groped as blind men the wall, and we as without eyes touched; we stumbled in midday, as in darknesses, in dark places, as dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 38:14; EZEKIEL 7:16; JEREMIAH 9:1"> All we shall roar as bears, and we shall wail thinking as culvers; we abided doom, and none there is; we abided health, and it is made far from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZRA 9:6; HOSEA 5:5; JEREMIAH 14:7"> For why our wickednesses be multiplied before thee, and our sins answered to us; for our great trespasses be with us, and we knew our wickednesses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARK 7:21–22; JAMES 3:6; HEBREWS 3:12"> to do sin, and to lie against the Lord. And we be turned away, that we went not after the back of our God, that we speak false challenge, and trespassing. We conceived, and spake of heart, words of leasing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:4; ECCLESIASTES 3:16; JEREMIAH 5:31"> and doom was turned aback, and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; stood &#91;a&#93; far; for why truth fell down in the street, and equity, either evenness, might not enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MICAH 7:2; JEREMIAH 7:28; 1ST JOHN 3:11–12"> And truth was made into forgetting, and he that went away from evil, was open to prey, either robbing. And the Lord saw, and it appeared evil in his eyes, for there is no doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:30; PSALMS 106:23; ISAIAH 64:7"> And God saw, that a man is not, and he was anguished, for none there is that runneth to. And his arm shall save to himself, and his rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; itself shall confirm him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:17; EPHESIANS 6:14; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:8"> He is clothed with rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; as with an habergeon, and the helmet of health is in his head; he is clothed with &#91;the&#93; clothes of vengeance, and he is covered as with a mantle of fervent working.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:10; ISAIAH 63:6; ROMANS 2:6"> As to vengeance, as to yielding of indignation to his enemies, and to requiting of time to his adversaries, he shall yield while to &#91;the&#93; isles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 7:27; PSALMS 113:3; ZECHARIAH 4:6"> And they that be at the west, shall dread the name of the Lord, and they that be at the rising of the sun, shall dread the glory of him; when he shall come as a violent flood, whom the spirit of the Lord compelleth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 11:26–27; TITUS 2:11–14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:19"> When &#91;the&#93; again-buyer shall come to Zion, and to them that go again from wickedness in Jacob, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:31–34; HEBREWS 10:16; EZEKIEL 36:25–27"> This is my bond of peace with them, saith the Lord; My spirit which is in thee, and my words which I have set in thy mouth, shall not go away from thy mouth, and from the mouth of thy seed, saith the Lord, from henceforth and till into without end.</span>
