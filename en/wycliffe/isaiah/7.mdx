<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:15; MATTHEW 3:4; 2ND SAMUEL 17:29"> and for the plenty of milk he shall eat butter; for why each man that shall be left in the midst of the land, shall eat butter and honey.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:6; SONG OF SONGS 8:11–12; MATTHEW 21:33"> And it shall be, in that day each place where a thousand vineries &#91;or vines&#93; shall be worth a thousand pieces of silver, and shall be into thorns and briars,</span>
