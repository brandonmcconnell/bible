</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 9:29; ISAIAH 37:31–32; GENESIS 19:24"> If the Lord of hosts had not left seed to us, we had been as Sodom, and we had been like as Gomorrah.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:8; EZEKIEL 16:49; AMOS 9:7"> Ye princes of men of Sodom, hear the word of the Lord; and ye people of Gomorrah, perceive with ears the law of your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:22; AMOS 5:21; JEREMIAH 6:20"> Whereto offer ye to me &#91;or What to me&#93; the multitude of your sacrifices? saith the Lord. </div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:3; MALACHI 1:10; MATTHEW 15:9"> Offer ye no more sacrifice in vain; incense is abomination to me; I shall not suffer new moon, and sabbath, and other feasts. For your companies be wicked;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 5:21; ISAIAH 43:24; ISAIAH 7:13"> my soul hateth your calends and your solemnities; those be dis-easeful to me, I travailed with suffering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MICAH 3:4; ISAIAH 59:2–3; PROVERBS 1:28"> And when ye stretch forth your hands, I shall turn away mine eyes from you; and when ye multiply prayer, I shall not hear; for why your hands be full of blood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:11; ISAIAH 55:6–7; 1ST PETER 2:1"> Be ye washed, be ye clean; do ye away the evil of your thoughts from mine eyes; cease ye to do waywardly,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MICAH 6:8; JEREMIAH 22:3; PSALMS 82:3–4"> learn ye to do well. Seek ye doom, help ye him that is oppressed, deem ye to the fatherless and motherless child, defend ye a widow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:14; PSALMS 51:7"> And come ye, and prove ye me, saith the Lord. Though your sins be as blood-red, those shall be made white as snow; and though they be red as vermilion, they shall be white as wool.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:15–16; ISAIAH 3:10; JOEL 2:26"> If ye will, and hear me, ye shall eat the goods of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:12; ISAIAH 58:14; NUMBERS 23:19"> That if ye do not, and ye stir me to wrathfulness &#91;or wrath&#93;, sword shall devour you; for why the mouth of the Lord spake.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:1–23; ZECHARIAH 8:3; ZEPHANIAH 3:1–3"> How is the faithful city full of doom made an whore? rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; dwelled therein; but now men-quellers dwell therein.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:28–30; LAMENTATIONS 4:1–2; HOSEA 4:18"> Thy silver is turned into dross, either filth; thy wine is meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MICAH 7:3; EXODUS 23:8; ZECHARIAH 7:10"> Thy princes be unfaithful, the fellows of thieves; all love gifts, and follow yieldings, either meeds; they deem not to a fatherless child, and the cause of a widow entereth not to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:16; ISAIAH 35:4; DEUTERONOMY 28:63"> For this thing, saith the Lord God of hosts, the Strong of Israel, Alas! I shall be comforted on mine enemies, and I shall be avenged of mine enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MALACHI 3:3; JEREMIAH 9:7; ISAIAH 4:4"> And I shall turn mine hand to thee, and I shall seethe out thy filth to the clean, and I shall do away all thy tin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:7; ZECHARIAH 8:3; ISAIAH 62:1–2"> And I shall restore thy judges, as they were before, and thy counsellors, as in eld &#91;or old&#93; time. After these things thou shalt be called the city of the rightful &#91;or rightwise&#93;, a faithful city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 62:12; ISAIAH 45:21–25; ISAIAH 5:16"> Zion shall be again-bought in doom, and they shall bring it again into rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 1:8–9; PSALMS 1:6; ISAIAH 30:13"> and God shall all-break &#91;the&#93; cursed men and &#91;the&#93; sinners together, and they that forsake the Lord, shall be wasted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:3; ISAIAH 57:5; ISAIAH 66:17"> For they shall be ashamed of &#91;the&#93; idols, to which they made sacrifice; and ye shall be ashamed on the orchards, which ye choosed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:5–6; EZEKIEL 17:9–10; MATTHEW 21:19"> When ye shall be as an oak, when the leaves fall down, and as an orchard without water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:24; EZEKIEL 32:21; MALACHI 4:1"> And your strength shall be as a dead spark of stubble, either of hards of flax, and your work shall be as a quick spark; and ever either shall be burnt together, and none shall be that shall quench.</span>
