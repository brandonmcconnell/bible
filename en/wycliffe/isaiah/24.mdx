</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 34:4–10; DEUTERONOMY 11:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:11"> The earth shall be broken with breaking, the earth shall be defouled with defouling, the earth shall be moved with moving,</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
