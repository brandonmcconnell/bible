<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; MARK 1:2–5; JOHN 1:23"> The voice of a crier in desert &#91;or The voice of the one crying in desert&#93;, Make ye ready the way of the Lord, make ye rightful &#91;or right&#93; the paths of our God in wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 3:5; EZEKIEL 21:26; LUKE 18:14"> Each valley shall be enhanced, and each mountain and little hill shall be made low; and shrewd things shall be into straight things, and sharp things shall be into plain ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:14; JOEL 2:28; ISAIAH 52:10"> And the glory of the Lord shall be showed, and each man shall see together, that the mouth of the Lord hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 103:15–16; 1ST PETER 1:24–25; JAMES 1:10–11"> The voice of God, saying, Cry thou. And I said, What shall I cry? Each flesh is hay, and all the glory thereof is as the flower of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 41:21"> The hay is dried up, and the flower fell down, for the spirit of the Lord blew therein. Verily the people is hay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 13:31; 1ST PETER 1:25; MATTHEW 24:35"> the hay is dried up, and the flower fell down; but the word of the Lord dwelleth, either shall stand, without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:7–8; ISAIAH 12:2; ISAIAH 25:9"> Thou that preachest to Zion, go upon an high hill; thou that preachest to Jerusalem, enhance thy voice in strength; enhance thou, do not thou dread; say thou to the cities of Judah, Lo! your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:12; ISAIAH 62:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:14"> Lo! the Lord God shall come in strength, and his arm shall hold lordship; lo! his meed is with him, and his work is before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:17; MICAH 5:4; PSALMS 23:1–6"> As a shepherd he shall feed his flock, he shall gather &#91;the&#93; lambs in his arms, and he shall raise in his bosom; he shall bear &#91;the&#93; sheep with lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:4; HEBREWS 1:10–12; PSALMS 102:25–26"> Who meted &#91;or measured&#93; waters in a fist, and weighed heavens with a span? Who weighed the heaviness of the earth with three fingers, and weighed &#91;the&#93; mountains in a weigh, and &#91;the&#93; little hills in a balance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:16; ROMANS 11:34; JOB 21:22"> Who helped the Spirit of the Lord, either who was his counsellor, and showed to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 21:22; COLOSSIANS 2:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:4–6"> With whom took he counsel, and who learned him, and taught him the path of rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and learned him in knowing, and showed to him the way of prudence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:22; JOB 34:14–15; JEREMIAH 10:10"> Lo! folks be as a drop of a bucket, and be areckoned as the tongue of a balance; lo! isles be as a little dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MICAH 6:6–7; PSALMS 40:6; PSALMS 50:9–12"> and the Lebanon shall not suffice to burn his sacrifice, and the beasts thereof shall not suffice to burnt sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 62:9; DANIEL 4:34–35; ISAIAH 29:7"> All folks be so before him, as if they be not; and they be reckoned as nothing and vain thing to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:5; 1ST SAMUEL 2:2; ISAIAH 40:25"> To whom therefore made ye God like? either what image shall ye set to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:9; HABAKKUK 2:18–19; ISAIAH 46:6–7"> Whether a smith shall weld together an image, either a goldsmith shall figure it in gold, and a worker in silver shall dight it with pieces of silver?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:3–4; ISAIAH 41:7; ISAIAH 46:7"> A wise craftsman chooseth a strong tree, and unable to be rotten; he seeketh how he shall ordain a simulacrum, that shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:13; PSALMS 50:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:17"> Whether ye know not? whether ye heard not? whether it was not told to you from the beginning? whether ye understood not the foundaments of &#91;the&#93; earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:27; PSALMS 104:2; ISAIAH 42:5"> Which sitteth on the compass of &#91;the&#93; earth, and the dwellers thereof be as locusts; which stretcheth forth heavens as nought, and spreadeth abroad those as a tabernacle to dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 12:21; PSALMS 107:40; ISAIAH 34:12"> Which giveth the searchers of privates, as if they be not, and &#91;he&#93; made the judges of &#91;the&#93; earth as a vain thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:16; ISAIAH 17:13; HAGGAI 1:9"> And soothly when the stock of them is neither planted, neither is sown, neither is rooted in &#91;the&#93; earth, he blew suddenly on them, and they dried up, and a whirlwind shall take them away as stubble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:18; DEUTERONOMY 4:33; DEUTERONOMY 4:15–18"> And to what thing have ye likened me, and have made &#91;me&#93; even? saith the Holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:16–17; JEREMIAH 32:17–19; ISAIAH 48:13"> Raise &#91;up&#93; your eyes on high, and see ye, who made these things of nought; which leadeth out in number the knighthood of them, and calleth all by name, for the multitude of his strength, and stalworth&#91;y&#93; ness, and might; neither one residue thing was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 18:7–8; ISAIAH 49:4; ISAIAH 49:14"> Why sayest thou, Jacob, and speakest thou, Israel, My way is hid from the Lord, and my doom passed from my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ROMANS 11:33–34; PSALMS 147:5; ISAIAH 55:8–9"> Whether thou knowest not, either heardest thou not? God, everlasting Lord, that made of nought the ends of &#91;the&#93; earth, shall not fail, neither shall travail, neither ensearching of his wisdom is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; PHILIPPIANS 4:13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> That giveth strength to the weary, and strength to them that be not, and multiplieth stalworth&#91;y&#93; ness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="AMOS 2:14; PSALMS 34:10; PSALMS 33:16"> Young men shall fail, and shall travail, and young men shall fall down in their sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:25–26; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:16; PSALMS 27:13–14"> But they that hope in the Lord, shall change strength, they shall take feathers as eagles; they shall run, and shall not travail; they shall go, and shall not fail.</span>
