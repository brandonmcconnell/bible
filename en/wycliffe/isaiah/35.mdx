<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:35; ISAIAH 51:3; ISAIAH 55:12–13"> The forsaken Judah and without a way shall be glad, and &#91;the&#93; wilder-ness shall make full out joy, and shall flower as a lily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:9; ISAIAH 60:13; AMOS 9:13–15"> It burgeoning shall burgeon, and it glad and praising shall make full out joy. The glory of Lebanon is given to it, the fairness of Carmel and of Sharon; they shall see the glory of the Lord, and the fairness of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:12; JOB 4:3–4; LUKE 22:32"> Comfort ye &#91;the&#93; benumbed hands, and make ye strong &#91;the&#93; feeble knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JAMES 5:7–9; ISAIAH 41:10–14; HAGGAI 2:4"> Say ye, Men of little comfort, be ye comforted, and do not ye dread; lo! our God shall bring the vengeance of yielding, God himself shall come, and shall save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:6–7; LUKE 7:20–23; ISAIAH 29:18"> Then the eyes of blind men shall be opened, and the ears of deaf men shall be open&#91;ed&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 5:8–9; JOHN 7:37–39; LUKE 11:14"> Then a crooked man shall skip as an hart, and the tongue of dumb men shall be opened; for why waters be broken out in desert, and streams in wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; ISAIAH 49:10; JOHN 4:14"> And that that was dry, is made into a pond, and the thirsty is made into wells of waters. &#91;The&#93; Greenness of &#91;the&#93; reed, and of &#91;the&#93; spire shall grow in &#91;the&#93; dens, in which dwelled dragons before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:13–14; ISAIAH 42:16; JOHN 14:6"> And a path and a way shall be there, and it shall be called an holy way, he that is defouled shall not pass thereby; and this shall be a straight &#91;right&#93; way to you, so that fools err not thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 62:12; HOSEA 2:18; LEVITICUS 26:6"> A lion shall not be there, and an evil beast shall not ascend &#91;or go up&#93; thereby, neither shall be found there. And they shall go, that be delivered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:4; ISAIAH 25:8"> and again-bought of the Lord; and they shall be converted, and shall come into Zion with praising; and everlasting gladness shall be on the heads of them; they shall have joy and gladness, and sorrow and wailing shall flee away.</span>
