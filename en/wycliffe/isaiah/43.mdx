<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; ISAIAH 43:7; ZECHARIAH 13:9"> And now the Lord God, making of nought thee, Jacob, and forming thee, Israel, saith these things, Do not thou dread, for I again-bought thee, and I called thee by thy name; thou art my servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; JOSHUA 1:9; PSALMS 66:12"> When thou shalt go by waters, I shall be with thee, and floods shall not cover thee; when thou shalt go in fire, thou shalt not be burnt, and flame shall not burn in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:16; ISAIAH 45:15; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:9–14"> For I am thy Lord God, the Holy of Israel, thy saviour. I gave thy mercy Egypt; Ethiopia, and Seba, for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:6–8; 1ST PETER 2:9; JEREMIAH 31:3"> Since thou art made honourable, and glorious in mine eyes; I loved thee, and I shall give men for thee, and peoples for thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:3; ISAIAH 49:12; LUKE 13:29"> Do not thou dread, for I am with thee; I shall bring thy seed from the east, and I shall gather thee together from the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:17–18; JEREMIAH 3:14; JEREMIAH 3:18–19"> I shall say to the north, Give thou, and to the south, Do not thou forbid; bring thou my sons from afar, and my daughters from the last parts of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:10; ISAIAH 43:21; EPHESIANS 1:12"> And each that calleth my name to help, into my glory I made him of nought; I formed him, and made him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:9; EZEKIEL 12:2; ISAIAH 42:18–20"> Lead thou forth the blind people, and having eyes; the deaf people, and ears be to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:10; ISAIAH 44:7–9; ISAIAH 41:21–26"> All heathen men be gathered together, and lineages be gathered together. Who among you, who shall tell this, and shall make you to hear those things, that be the first? give they &#91;the&#93; witnesses of them, and be they justified, and hear they, and say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:4; ISAIAH 45:6; ISAIAH 46:8–9"> Verily ye be my witnesses, saith the Lord, and my servants, whom I chose; that ye know, and believe to me, and understand, for I myself am; before me is no God former &#91;or before me is not formed God&#93;, and after me shall none be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 13:4; ISAIAH 44:6; ISAIAH 45:21–22"> I am, I am the Lord, and without me is no Saviour &#91;or and there is not without me a saviour&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:12; PSALMS 81:9–10; ISAIAH 43:10"> I told, and saved; I made hearing, and none alien God was among you. Ye be my witnesses, saith the Lord; and I am God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:27; ISAIAH 46:10; PSALMS 90:2"> from the beginning, I myself am, and none there is that delivereth from mine hand; I shall work, and who shall destroy it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:6; ISAIAH 23:13; JEREMIAH 51:24"> The Lord, your again-buyer, the Holy of Israel, saith these things, For you I sent out into Babylon, and I drew down all &#91;the&#93; bars, and &#91;the&#93; Chaldees having glory in their ships.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:3; MATTHEW 25:34; ISAIAH 48:17"> I am the Lord, your Holy, your king, making Israel of nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:10; ISAIAH 43:2; PSALMS 77:19"> The Lord saith these things, that gave &#91;a&#93; way in the sea, and a path in running waters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 14:4–9; ISAIAH 1:31; EZEKIEL 38:8–18"> which led out a cart, and horse, a company, and strong man; they slept together, neither they shall rise again; they be all-broken as flax, and be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:12"> Think ye not on the former things, and behold ye not old things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:6; ISAIAH 42:9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17"> Lo! I make new things, and now those shall begin to be made; soothly ye shall know them. I shall set &#91;a&#93; way in desert, and floods in a land without a way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:17; ISAIAH 48:21; ISAIAH 49:10"> And a beast of the field shall glorify me, dragons and ostriches shall glorify me; for I gave waters in desert, and floods in the land without a way, that I should give drink to my people, to my chosen people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:9; HEBREWS 13:15; PSALMS 102:18"> I formed this people to me, it shall tell my praising.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MICAH 6:3; MALACHI 3:14; MALACHI 1:13"> Jacob, thou calledest not me to help; and thou, Israel, travailedest not for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="AMOS 5:25; ZECHARIAH 7:5–6; MALACHI 3:8"> Thou offeredest not to me the ram of thy burnt sacrifice, and thou glorifiedest not me with thy slain sacrifices. I made not thee to serve in offering, neither I gave to thee travail in incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:13; JEREMIAH 6:20; ISAIAH 1:14"> Thou boughtest not to me sweet smelling spicery for silver, and thou filledest not me with &#91;the&#93; fatness of thy slain sacrifices; nevertheless thou madest me to serve in thy sins, thou gavest travail to me in thy wicked-nesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:12; MICAH 7:18–19; HEBREWS 10:17"> I am, I myself am, that do away thy wickednesses for me, and I shall not have mind on thy sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:18; ISAIAH 43:9; ROMANS 8:33"> Bring me again into mind, and be we deemed together; tell thou, if thou hast anything, that thou be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:7; JEREMIAH 5:31; EZEKIEL 22:25–28"> Thy first father sinned, and thine interpreters trespassed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:9; ISAIAH 65:15; ZECHARIAH 8:13"> And I made foul holy princes, and I gave Jacob to death, and Israel into blasphemy.</span>
