<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:18; HABAKKUK 2:20; ZECHARIAH 2:13"> Isles, be still to me, and folks change strength; nigh they, and then speak they; nigh we together to doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:1; 2ND SAMUEL 22:43; EZRA 1:2"> Who raised the just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man from the east, and called him to follow himself? He shall give folks in his sight, and he shall wield kings; he shall give as dust to his sword, and as stubble ravished of the wind to his bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 5:24; ISAIAH 57:2"> He shall pursue them, he shall go in peace; a path shall not appear in his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:17"> Who wrought and did these things? calling generations at the beginning. I am the Lord; and I am the first, and the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:15–16; PSALMS 65:8; PSALMS 66:3"> Isles saw, and dreaded; the last parts of &#91;the&#93; earth were astonied; they came nigh, and nighed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:12; ISAIAH 40:19; DANIEL 3:1–7"> Each man shall help his neighbour, and shall say to his brother, Be thou comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:19–20; DANIEL 3:1–7; JUDGES 18:17–18"> A smith of metal smiting with an hammer comforted him that polished, either made fair, in that time, saying, It is good to &#91;the&#93; glue; and he fastened him with nails, that he should not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 2:23; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:7; DEUTERONOMY 14:2"> And thou, Israel, my servant, Jacob, whom I chose, the seed of Abraham, my friend,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 12:22; PSALMS 94:14; DEUTERONOMY 7:6–7"> in whom I took thee; from the last parts of &#91;the&#93; earth, and from the far parts thereof I called thee; and I said to thee, Thou art my servant; I chose thee, and casted not away thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; ISAIAH 43:1–2; ROMANS 8:31"> Dread thou not, for I am with thee; bow thou not away, for I am thy God. I comforted thee, and helped thee; and the right hand of my just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man up-took thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 23:22; ISAIAH 45:24; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:9"> Lo! all men shall be shamed, and shall be ashamed, that fight against thee; they shall be as if they be not, and men shall perish, that against-say thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:35–36; JOB 20:7–9; ISAIAH 17:14"> Thou shalt seek them, and thou shalt not find thy rebel men; they shall be as if they be not, and as the wasting of a man fighting against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10; PSALMS 73:23; PSALMS 63:8"> For I am thy Lord God, taking thine hand, and saying to thee, Dread thou not, I helped thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:14; PSALMS 22:6; ISAIAH 54:5"> Do not thou, worm of Jacob, dread, ye that be dead of Israel. I helped thee, saith the Lord, and thine again-buyer, the Holy of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MICAH 4:13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4–5; ZECHARIAH 4:7"> I have set thee as a new wain threshing, having sawing bills; thou shalt thresh mountains, and shalt make small, and thou shalt set little hills as dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:2; ISAIAH 61:10–11; ISAIAH 45:24–25"> Thou shalt winnow them, and the wind shall take them away, and a whirlwind shall scatter them; and thou shalt make full out joy in the Lord, and thou shalt be glad in the Holy of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:1; ISAIAH 30:19; ISAIAH 44:3"> Needy men and poor seek waters, and those be not; the tongue of them dried for thirst. I the Lord shall hear them, I God of Israel shall not forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 107:35; ISAIAH 35:6–7; ISAIAH 58:11"> I shall open floods in high hills, and wells in the midst of fields; I shall set the desert into ponds of waters, and the land without a way into rivers of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:13; ISAIAH 37:31–32; ISAIAH 35:1"> I shall give in wilderness a cedar, and a thorn, and a myrtle tree, and the tree of an olive; I shall set in the desert a fir tree, an elm, and a box tree together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 12:9; ISAIAH 44:23; ISAIAH 43:21"> That they see, and know, and bethink, and understand together; that the hand of the Lord did this thing, and the Holy of Israel made that of nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 23:3–4; JOB 38:3; MICAH 6:1–2"> Make ye nigh your doom, saith the Lord; bring ye, if in hap ye have anything, saith the King of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:21; ISAIAH 48:14; ISAIAH 44:7"> Nigh, and tell to us, whatever things shall come; tell ye the former things that were, and we shall set our heart, and shall know; show ye to us the last things of them, and those things that shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 13:19; ISAIAH 42:9; JEREMIAH 10:5"> Tell ye what things shall come in time to coming &#91;or to come&#93;, and we shall know, that ye be gods; also do ye well, either evil, if ye may; and speak we, and see we together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 115:8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:4; ISAIAH 41:29"> Lo! ye be of nought, and your work is of that that is not; he that choose you, is abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:2; 2ND SAMUEL 22:43; ISAIAH 10:6"> I raised from the north, and he shall come from the rising of the sun; he shall call my name. And he shall bring magistrates as clay, and as a potter defouling &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:7; ISAIAH 41:22; ISAIAH 45:21"> Who told from the beginning, that we know, and from the beginning, that we say, Thou art just? none is telling, neither before-saying, neither hearing your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:9; ISAIAH 52:7; ISAIAH 44:28"> The first shall say to Zion, Lo! I am present; and I shall give a gospeller to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:5; DANIEL 2:10–11; ISAIAH 40:13–14"> And I saw, and none was of these, that took counsel, and he that was asked, answered a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:13; ISAIAH 41:24; JEREMIAH 10:2–16"> Lo! all men be unjust, and their works be wind and vain; the simulacra of them be wind, and void thing.</span>
