<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 21:34–36; 1ST SAMUEL 25:36–38; DANIEL 5:1"> Mine heart faded, darknesses astonied me; Babylon, my darling, is set &#91;or put&#93; to me into miracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:11; JEREMIAH 51:57; JEREMIAH 51:39"> Set thou a board, behold thou into a looking place; rise, ye princes, eating and drinking, take ye &#91;the&#93; shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:2–7; EZEKIEL 3:17; HABAKKUK 2:1–2"> For why the Lord said these things to me, Go thou, and set a looker; and tell he, whatever thing he seeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 21:9; ISAIAH 37:24; HEBREWS 2:1"> And he saw the chariot of two horsemen, the rider of an ass, and the rider of a camel; and he beheld diligently with much looking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:1–2; ISAIAH 56:10; 1ST PETER 5:8"> and &#91;he&#93; cried as a lion, I stand continually by day on the looking place of the Lord, and I stand by all nights on my keeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:8; JEREMIAH 51:8"> Lo! this cometh, a man-rider of a cart of horsemen. And Isaiah cried, and said, Babylon fell down, fell down; and all the graven images of gods thereof be all-broken into &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:33; MICAH 4:13; 2ND KI 13:7"> My threshing, and the daughter of my cornfloor, I have told to you what things I heard of the Lord of hosts, of God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 25:14; OBADIAH 1:1–16; AMOS 1:6"> The burden of Dumah. It crieth from Seir to me, Keeper, what hour of the night? keeper, what hour of the night?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:30–32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:37–38; EZEKIEL 18:30–32"> The keeper said, The morrowtide cometh, and night; if ye seek, seek ye, and be ye converted, and come ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:23–24; 1ST CHRONICLES 1:32; EZEKIEL 27:15"> The burden of Arabia. In the forest at eventide ye shall sleep, in the paths of Dedanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 25:15; JOB 6:19; ROMANS 12:20"> Ye that dwell in the land of the south, run, and bear water to the thirsty; and run ye with loaves to him that fleeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 6:19–20; ISAIAH 13:14"> For they fled from the face of swords, from the face of &#91;the&#93; sword nighing, from the face of &#91;the&#93; bow bent, from the face of &#91;the&#93; grievous battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 120:5; ISAIAH 60:7; ISAIAH 16:14"> For the Lord saith these things to me, Yet in one year, as in the year of an hired man, and all the glory of Kedar shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:6; NUMBERS 23:19; MATTHEW 24:35"> And the remnants of the number of strong archers of the sons of Kedar shall be made less; for why the Lord God of Israel spake.</span>
