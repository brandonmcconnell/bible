<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 4:6; ISAIAH 13:11; JOB 40:11–12"> The Lord of hosts thought this thing, that he should draw down the pride of all glory, and that he should bring to shame all the noble men of earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 12:21; ISAIAH 23:14; PSALMS 18:32"> Thou daughter of the sea, pass &#91;over&#93; thy land as a flood; a girdle is no more to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:3–4; EXODUS 14:21; JEREMIAH 47:7"> It stretched forth his hand above the sea, and troubled realms. The Lord sent against Canaan, for to all-break the strong men thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:22; EZEKIEL 26:13–14; ISAIAH 23:1–2"> and he said, Thou maiden, the daughter of Sidon, that sufferest challenge, shalt no more add, that thou have glory. Rise thou, and pass over the sea into Chittim; there also no rest shall be to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:6; ISAIAH 10:5; 2ND KI 17:24"> Lo! the land of Chaldees, such a people was not; Assur founded that Tyre; they led over into captivity the strong men thereof; they &#91;under&#93; mined the houses thereof; they setted &#91;or put&#93; it into falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 23:1; ISAIAH 23:6; EZEKIEL 27:25–30"> Yell, ye ships of the sea, for your strength be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:22; JEREMIAH 25:9–11; DANIEL 7:14"> And it shall be, in that day, thou, Tyre, shalt be in forgetting by seventy years, as the days of one king; but after seventy years, as the song of a whore shall be to Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:14; PROVERBS 7:10–12"> Thou whore, given to forgetting, take an harp, compass the city; sing thou well, use thou oft a song, that mind be of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NAHUM 3:4; EZEKIEL 27:6–36; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:2"> And it shall be, after seventy years, the Lord shall visit Tyre, and shall bring it again to his hire; and again it shall be, when it shall do fornication with all &#91;the&#93; realms of &#91;the&#93; earth, on the face of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 72:10; DEUTERONOMY 26:12–14; PHILIPPIANS 4:17–18"> And the merchandises thereof and the meeds thereof shall be hallowed to the Lord; they shall not be hid, neither shall be laid up; for why the merchandise thereof shall be to them that dwell before the Lord, that they eat to fullness, and be clothed till to eldness &#91;or oldness&#93;.</span>
