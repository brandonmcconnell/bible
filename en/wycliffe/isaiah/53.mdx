<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 12:38; ROMANS 10:16–17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:18"> Who believed to our hearing? and to whom is the arm of the Lord showed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:6–7; ISAIAH 52:14; JOHN 1:10–14"> And he shall go up as a rod before him, and as a root from &#91;the&#93; thirsty land. And neither shape neither fairness was to him; and we saw him, and no beholding was; and we desired him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:67; JOHN 1:10–11; ISAIAH 53:10"> despised, and the last of men, a man of sorrows, and knowing sickness. And his cheer was as hid, and despised; wherefore and we areckoned not him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:24; MATTHEW 8:17; GALATIANS 3:13"> Verily he suffered our sicknesses, and he bare our sorrows; and we areckoned him as a mesel, and smitten of God, and made low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:24–25; 1ST PETER 3:18; MATTHEW 20:28"> Forsooth he was wounded for our wickednesses, he was defouled for our great trespasses; the learning of our peace was on him, and we be made whole by his wanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:25; 1ST PETER 3:18; PSALMS 119:176"> All we erred as sheep, each man bowed into his own way, and the Lord putted &#91;or put&#93; in him the wickedness of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:32–33; MATTHEW 26:63; 1ST PETER 2:23"> He was offered, for he would, and he opened not his mouth; as a sheep he shall be led to slaying, and he shall be dumb as a lamb before him that clippeth it, and he shall not open his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:33; MATTHEW 26:65–66"> He is taken away from anguish and from doom; who shall tell out the generation of him? For he was cut down from the land of livers. I smote him for the great trespass of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:57–60; 1ST PETER 2:22; 1ST JOHN 3:5"> And he shall give unfaithful men for burying, and rich men for his death; for he did not wickedness, neither guile was in his mouth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 8:32; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21; GALATIANS 3:13"> and the Lord would defoul him in sickness. If he putteth his life for sin, he shall see his seed long enduring, and the will of the Lord shall be addressed in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 5:18–19; 1ST PETER 2:24; ISAIAH 53:4–6"> For that that his soul travailed, he shall see, and shall be filled, &#91;or For-thy that he travailed, his soul shall see, and be fulfilled&#93;. That my just &#91;or rightwise&#93; servant shall justify many men in his knowing, and he shall bear the wickednesses of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 22:37; HEBREWS 9:28; LUKE 23:32–34"> Therefore I shall yield, either deal, to him full many men, and he shall part the spoils of the strong fiends; for that that he gave his life into death, and was areckoned with felonious men; and he did away the sin of many men, and he prayed for trespassers.</span>
