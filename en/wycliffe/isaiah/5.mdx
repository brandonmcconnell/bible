<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 12:1; LUKE 20:9; JOHN 15:1"> I shall sing for my darling the song of mine uncle’s son, of his vineyard. A vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93; was made to my darling, in the horn, that is, in an high place and excellent, in the son of oil, that is, in a place full of olives, of whose fruit is wrung out oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:32–33; MARK 11:13"> And he hedged it, and chose stones thereof, and planted a chosen vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;; and he builded a tower in the midst thereof, and reared a wine press therein; and he abode, that it should make grapes, and it made wild grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 2:5; PSALMS 51:4; MICAH 6:2–3"> Now therefore, ye dwellers of Jerusalem, and ye men of Judah, deem between me and my vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:37; MICAH 6:3–4; JEREMIAH 2:5"> What is it that I ought to do more to my vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;, and I did not to it? whether that I abode, that it should make grapes, and it made wild grapes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:15; LUKE 21:24; ISAIAH 28:18"> And now I shall show to you, what I shall do to my vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;. I shall take away the hedge thereof, and it shall be into ravishing; I shall cast down the wall thereof, and it shall be into defouling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:23–25; JEREMIAH 25:11; ISAIAH 24:1–3"> and I shall set it deserted, either forsaken. It shall not be cut, and it shall not be digged, and briars and thorns shall grow upon it; and I shall command to &#91;the&#93; clouds, that they rain not rain on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 80:8–11; JEREMIAH 12:10; JAMES 5:4"> Forsooth the vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93; of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah be the delightable burgeoning of him. I abode, that it shall make doom, and lo! wickedness; and that it should do rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and lo! cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MICAH 2:2; HABAKKUK 2:9–12; JEREMIAH 22:13–17"> Woe to you that join house to house, and couple field to field, till to the end of &#91;a&#93; place. Whether ye alone shall dwell in the midst of the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:38; ISAIAH 22:14; AMOS 5:11"> These things be in the ears of me, the Lord of hosts; If many houses be not forsaken, great and fair, without dweller, believe ye not to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:26; HAGGAI 2:16; HAGGAI 1:6"> For why ten acres of vines &#91;or vineyards&#93; shall make one pottle, and thirty bushels of seed shall make three bushels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:29–30; ROMANS 13:13; PROVERBS 20:1"> Woe to you that rise altogether early to follow drunkenness, and to drink till to eventide, that ye burn with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 21:11–14; PSALMS 28:5; AMOS 6:4–6"> Harp, and gittern, and tympan, and pipe, and wine be in your feasts; and ye behold not the work of the Lord, neither ye behold the works of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 4:6; ISAIAH 1:3; ISAIAH 27:11"> Therefore my people is led captive, either prisoner, for it had not knowing; and the noblemen thereof perished in hunger, and the multitude thereof was dry in thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:16; LUKE 21:34; HABAKKUK 2:5"> Therefore hell hath alarged his soul, and opened his mouth without any end; and the strong men thereof, and the people thereof, and the high men, and the glorious men thereof, shall go down to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 2:11; ISAIAH 2:9; JEREMIAH 5:4–5"> And a man shall be bowed down, and a man of age shall be made low; and the eyes of high men shall be pressed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 46:10; ISAIAH 12:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:11"> And the Lord of hosts shall be enhanced in doom, and &#91;the&#93; holy God shall be hallowed in rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 2:6; ZEPHANIAH 2:14; ISAIAH 7:25"> And lambs shall be fed by their order, and comelings shall eat of the desert places turned into plenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:14; EZEKIEL 13:22; JEREMIAH 5:31"> Woe to you that draw wickedness in the cords of vanity, and draw sin as the bond of a wain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:22; JEREMIAH 17:15; JEREMIAH 23:36"> and ye say, The work of him hasten, and come soon, that we see; and the counsel of the Holy of Israel nigh, and come, and we shall know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:15; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:1–5; LUKE 16:15"> Woe to you that say evil good, and good evil; and put darknesses light, and light darknesses; and put bitter thing into sweet, and sweet thing into bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:18–20; PROVERBS 3:7; ROMANS 12:16"> Woe to you that be wise men in your eyes, and be prudent before yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:11; HABAKKUK 2:15; ISAIAH 28:7"> Woe to you that be mighty to drink wine, and be strong to meddle &#91;or mingle&#93; drunkenness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:2; JAMES 5:6; PSALMS 94:21"> and ye justify a wicked man for gifts, and ye take away the rightful-ness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; of a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:12; JOB 18:16; JOEL 2:5"> For this thing, as the tongue of fire devoureth stubble, and the heat of flame burneth, so the root of them shall be as a dead spark, and the seed of them shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; as dust; for they casted away the law of the Lord of hosts, and blasphemed the speech of the Holy of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:4; ISAIAH 9:21; ISAIAH 9:17"> Therefore the strong vengeance of the Lord was wroth against his people, and he stretched forth his hand on it, and smote it; and hills were troubled &#91;or the mountains be disturbed&#93;, and the dead bodies of them were made as a turd in the midst &#91;or the middle&#93; of streets. In all these things the strong vengeance of him was not turned away, but yet his hand was stretched forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:8; DEUTERONOMY 28:49; ISAIAH 7:18"> And he shall raise &#91;up&#93; a sign among nations afar, and he shall hiss to him from the ends of &#91;the&#93; earth; and lo! he shall hasten, and shall come swiftly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOEL 2:7–8; JOB 12:18; JOB 12:21"> None is failing neither travailing in that host; he shall not nap, neither sleep, neither the girdle of his reins shall be undone, neither the lace of his shoe shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 45:5; NAHUM 2:3–4; EZEKIEL 21:9–11"> His arrows be sharp, and all his bows be bent; the hoofs of his horses be as flint, and his wheels be as the fierceness of tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:22; ZECHARIAH 11:3; MICAH 5:8"> His roaring shall be as of a lion; he shall roar as the whelps of lions; and he shall gnash, and shall hold prey, and shall embrace, and none shall be, that shall deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:22; JOEL 2:10; JEREMIAH 4:23–28"> And he shall sound on it in that day, as doeth the sound of the sea; we shall behold into the earth, and lo! darknesses of tribulation, and light is made dark in the darkness thereof.</span>
