</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; ISAIAH 61:1; MATTHEW 11:29"> and mild men shall increase gladness in the Lord, and poor men shall make full out joy in the Holy of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MICAH 2:1; ISAIAH 28:14–22; LUKE 16:14"> For he that had the mastery, failed, and the scorner is ended, and all they be cut down that waked on wickedness;</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="AMOS 5:10–12; JAMES 5:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:14"> which made men to do sin in word, and deceived a reprover in the gate, and bowed away in vain from a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man.</span>
