<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:18–21; LUKE 3:22; ISAIAH 61:1"> Lo! my servant, I shall up-take him; my chosen, my soul pleased to itself in him. I gave my spirit on him, he shall bring forth doom to heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:16–20; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:24; MATTHEW 11:29"> He shall not cry, neither he shall take a person, neither his voice shall be heard withoutforth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:16; JEREMIAH 31:25; PSALMS 147:3"> He shall not break a shaken reed, and he shall not quench smoking flax; he shall bring out doom in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:2–12; GENESIS 49:10; ISAIAH 52:13–15"> He shall not be sorrowful, neither troubled, till he set doom in &#91;the&#93; earth, and isles shall abide his law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:22; ISAIAH 45:12; JEREMIAH 10:12"> The Lord God saith these things, making heavens of nought, and stretching forth them, making steadfast the earth, and those things that burgeon &#91;out&#93; of it, giving breath to the people, that is on it, and giving spirit to them that tread on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 2:32; JOHN 8:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:47"> I the Lord have called thee in rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and I took thine hand, and kept thee, and I gave thee into a bond of peace of the people, and into light of folks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 61:1; EPHESIANS 1:17–18; ISAIAH 35:5"> That thou shouldest open the eyes of blind men; that thou shouldest lead out of enclosing together a bound man, from the house of prison men sitting in darknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:11; ISAIAH 43:11; EXODUS 34:14"> I am the Lord, this is my name; I shall not give my glory to another, and my praising to graven images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:19; ISAIAH 46:9–10; JOHN 13:19"> Lo! those things that were the first, be come, and I tell new things; I shall make heard to you, before that those &#91;or they&#93; begin to be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 33:3; PSALMS 40:3; ROMANS 15:9–11"> Sing ye a new song to the Lord; his praising is from the last parts of the earth; ye that go down into the sea, and the fullness thereof, isles, and the dwellers of those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 32:16; ISAIAH 60:7; ISAIAH 41:18–19"> The desert be raised &#91;up&#93;, and the cities thereof; he shall dwell in the houses of Kedar; ye dwellers of the stone, praise ye; they shall cry from the top of hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 15:9–11; PSALMS 117:1–2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:9–12"> They shall set glory to the Lord, and they shall tell his praising in isles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 15:1–3; PSALMS 78:65; ISAIAH 59:16–19"> The Lord as a strong man shall go out, as a man a warrior he shall raise fervent love; he shall speak, and shall cry; he shall be comforted on his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 83:1–2; ECCLESIASTES 8:11–12; 2ND PETER 3:15"> I was still, ever I held &#91;my&#93; silence; I was patient, I shall speak as a woman travailing of child; I shall scatter, and I shall swallow together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 50:2; NAHUM 1:4–6; ISAIAH 44:27"> I shall make desert high mountains and little hills, and I shall dry up all the burgeoning of them; and I shall set floods into isles, and I shall make ponds dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:21; LUKE 3:5; ISAIAH 60:19–20"> And I shall lead out blind men into the way, which they know not, and I shall make them to go in paths, which they knew not; I shall set the darknesses of them before them into light, and shrewd things into rightful &#91;or even&#93; things; I did these words to them, and I forsook not them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 97:7; ISAIAH 1:29; ISAIAH 44:11"> They be turned aback; be they shamed with shame, that trust in a graven image; which say to a molten image, Ye be our gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:8; PROVERBS 20:12; MARK 7:34–37"> Ye deaf men, hear; and ye blind men, behold to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4; EZEKIEL 12:2; JEREMIAH 5:21"> Who is blind, no but my servant? and deaf, but he to whom I sent my messengers? Who is blind, but he that is sold? and who is blind, but the servant of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:2–4; ROMANS 2:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:22–27"> Whether thou that seest many things, shalt not keep? Whether thou that hast open ears, shalt not hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:10; ROMANS 3:31; ISAIAH 46:12–13"> And the Lord would, that he should hallow it, and magnify the law, and enhance it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 102:20; JEREMIAH 50:17; ISAIAH 51:23"> But that people was ravished, and wasted; all they be the snare of young men, and be hid in the houses of prisons. They be made into raven, and none is that delivereth; into ravishing, and none there is that saith, Yield thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:22–23; ISAIAH 48:18; DEUTERONOMY 32:29"> Who is among you, that heareth this, perceiveth, and hearkeneth things to coming &#91;or to come&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 47:6; ISAIAH 10:5–6; MATTHEW 22:7"> Who gave Jacob into ravishing, and Israel to destroyers? Whether not the Lord? He it is, against whom they sinned; and they would not go in his ways, and they heard not his law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HOSEA 7:9; ISAIAH 57:11; MALACHI 2:2"> And he shedded &#91;or poured&#93; out on them the indignation of his strong vengeance, and strong battle; and he burnt it in compass, and it knew not; and he burnt it, and it understood not.</span>
