<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 12:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:7; JOSHUA 2:11"> The burden of Egypt. Lo! the Lord shall ascend on a light cloud, and he shall enter into Egypt; and the simulacra of Egypt shall be moved from his face, and the heart of Egypt shall fail in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 7:22; 1ST SAMUEL 14:20; MATTHEW 10:36"> And I shall make Egyptians to run together against Egyptians, and a man shall fight against his brother, and a man against his friend, a city against a city, and a realm against a realm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:19; DANIEL 2:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 10:13"> And the spirit of Egypt shall be broken in the entrails thereof, and I shall cast down the counsel thereof; and they shall ask their simulacra, and their false diviners, and their men that have unclean spirits speaking in the womb, and their diviners by sacrifices made on altars to fiends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 20:4; JEREMIAH 46:26; EZEKIEL 29:19"> And I shall betake Egypt into the hand of cruel lords, and a strong king shall be lord of them, saith the Lord God of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:36; EZEKIEL 30:12; ZECHARIAH 14:18"> And &#91;the&#93; water of the sea shall wax dry, and the flood shall be desolate, and shall be dried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:25; EXODUS 7:18; 2ND KI 19:24"> And the floods shall fail, and the strands &#91;or rivers&#93; of the fields shall be made thin, and shall be dried; a reed and spire shall fade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 32:20; JOEL 1:17–18; ISAIAH 23:3"> The bottom of water shall be made naked, and streams from their wells; and the moist place of all seed shall be dried, it shall wax dry, and it shall not be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:10; HABAKKUK 1:15; NUMBERS 11:5"> And &#91;the&#93; fishers shall mourn, and all that cast hook into the flood shall wail; and they that spread abroad a net on the face of waters shall fade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:7; PROVERBS 7:16; 1ST KINGS 10:28"> They shall be shamed, that wrought flax, folding and ordaining subtle things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:10; EXODUS 7:19; EXODUS 8:5"> And the water places thereof shall be dry; all that made ponds to take fishes, shall be shamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:22; 1ST KINGS 4:30"> The fond princes &#91;or fools&#93; of Tanis, the wise counsellors of Pharaoh, gave unwise counsel; how shall ye say to Pharaoh, I am the son of wise men, the son of eld &#91;or old&#93; kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:20; ISAIAH 14:24; JUDGES 9:38"> Where be now thy wise men? Tell they to thee, and show they, what the Lord of hosts thought on Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:13; JEREMIAH 2:16; JEREMIAH 46:14"> The princes of Tanis be made fools; the princes of Memphis faded; they deceived Egypt, a cornerstone of the peoples thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:11; 1ST KINGS 22:20–23; EZEKIEL 14:7–9"> The Lord meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; a spirit of error in the midst thereof; and they made Egypt for to err in all his work, as a drunken man and spewing erreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:14–15; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:11–12; PROVERBS 14:23"> And work shall not be to Egypt, that it make an head and tail bowing and refraining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:30; NAHUM 3:13; ISAIAH 11:15"> In that day Egypt shall be as women, and they shall be astonied, and shall dread of the face of the moving of the hand of the Lord of hosts, which he moved on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:24; DANIEL 4:35; EZEKIEL 29:6–7"> And the land of Judah shall be to Egypt into dread; each that shall think on it, shall dread of the face of the counsel of the Lord of hosts, which he thought on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:9; ZECHARIAH 2:11; ISAIAH 19:21"> In that day five cities shall be in the land of Egypt, and shall speak with the tongue of Canaan, and shall swear by the Lord of hosts; the city of the sun shall be called one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 28:18; JOSHUA 22:10; JOSHUA 22:26"> In that day the altar of the Lord shall be in the midst of the land of Egypt, and the title of the Lord shall be beside the end thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; TITUS 2:13; ISAIAH 45:21–22"> and it shall be into a sign and witnessing to the Lord of hosts, in the land of Egypt. For they shall cry to the Lord from the face of the troubler, and he shall send a saviour to them, and a for-fighter, that shall deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:9; MALACHI 1:11; JOHN 17:3"> And the Lord shall be known of Egypt, and &#91;the&#93; Egyptians shall know the Lord in that day; and they shall worship him in sacrifices and gifts, and they shall make vows to the Lord, and they shall pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:39; HEBREWS 12:11; HOSEA 14:1"> And the Lord shall smite Egypt with a wound, and shall make it whole; and &#91;the&#93; Egyptians shall turn again to the Lord, and he shall be pleased in them, and he shall make them whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:16; EPHESIANS 2:18–22; ISAIAH 35:8–10"> In that day a way shall be from Egypt into Assyrians, and Egyptians shall serve Assur; and Assur shall enter into Egypt, and Egypt into Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:8; GENESIS 12:2; GALATIANS 3:14"> In that day Israel shall be the third to Egypt and to Assur, the blessing in the middle of &#91;the&#93; earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:23; HOSEA 2:23; ROMANS 9:24–25"> whom the Lord of hosts blessed, saying, Blessed be my people of Egypt, and the work of mine hands be to Assyrians; but mine heritage be to Israel.</span>
