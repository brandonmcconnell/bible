Whether the earth shall travail of child in one day, either a folk shall be childed together? For why Zion travailed of child, and childed her sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:3; GENESIS 18:14"> Whether that I make others to bear child, shall not bear child myself? saith the Lord. Whether I that give gener-ation to other men, shall be barren? saith thy Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 26:8; DEUTERONOMY 32:43; PSALMS 137:6"> Be ye glad with Jerusalem, and all ye that love that, make full out joy therein; all ye that mourn on that Jerusalem, make ye joy with it in joy;</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 48:18; ISAIAH 60:16; ISAIAH 45:14"> For why the Lord saith these things, Lo! I shall bow down on it, as a flood of peace, and as a flowing stream, the glory of heathen men, which ye shall suck; ye shall be borne at teats, and on knees, they shall speak pleasantly to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 1:4; ISAIAH 51:3; ISAIAH 40:1"> As if a mother speaketh fair to any child, so I shall comfort you, and ye shall be comforted in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:7; EZRA 8:31; EZRA 7:9"> Ye shall see, and your heart shall have joy, and your bones shall burgeon as an herb. And the hand of the Lord shall be known in his servants, and he shall have indignation to his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:33; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:6–9; 2ND PETER 3:10–12"> For lo! the Lord shall come in fire, and as a whirlwind his chariots, to yield in indignation his strong vengeance, and his blaming in the flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:21–22; ISAIAH 30:30; EZEKIEL 39:2–10"> For why the Lord shall deem in fire, and in his sword to each flesh; and slain men of the Lord shall be multiplied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:3–4; DEUTERONOMY 14:3–8; ISAIAH 1:28–29"> that were hallowed, and guessed them clean, in gardens after one gate within; that eat swine’s flesh, and abomination, and a mouse, they shall be wasted together, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4–6"> Forsooth I come to gather together the works of them, and the thoughts of them, with all folks and languages; and they shall come, and shall see my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:15; ROMANS 15:21; ISAIAH 55:5"> And I shall set &#91;or put&#93; a sign in them, and I shall send of them that be saved to heathen men, into the sea, into Africa, and into Lydia, and to them that hold &#91;an&#93; arrow, into Italy, and Greek land &#91;or Greece&#93;, to isles far, to them that heard not of me, and saw not my glory. And they shall tell my glory to heathen men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:3–14; ISAIAH 65:11; ISAIAH 11:9"> and they shall bring all your brethren out of &#91;or from&#93; all folks as a gift to the Lord, in horses, and &#91;in&#93; chariots, and in litters, and in mules, and in carts, to mine holy hill &#91;or holy mountain&#93;, Jerusalem, saith the Lord; as if the sons of Israel bring a gift in a clean vessel into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:9; EXODUS 19:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:6"> And I shall take of them into priests and deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:17; 2ND PETER 3:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:1"> For as new heavens and new earth, which I make to stand before me, saith the Lord, so your seed shall stand, and your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:1; PSALMS 86:9; COLOSSIANS 2:16–17"> And a month shall be of month, and a sabbath of sabbath; each man shall come for to worship before my face, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:10–11; ISAIAH 14:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:15–16"> And they shall go out, and shall see the carrions of men, that trespassed against me; the worm of them shall not die, and the fire of them shall not be quenched; and they shall be unto filling of sight to each man.</span>
