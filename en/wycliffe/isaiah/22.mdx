</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND KI 25:4–7; ISAIAH 3:1–8; JEREMIAH 39:4–7"> All thy princes fled together, and were bound hard; all that were found, were bound together, they fled far.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:1; MATTHEW 2:18; PSALMS 77:2"> Therefore I said, Go ye away from me, I shall weep bitterly; do not ye be busy to comfort me on the destroying of the daughter of my people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MICAH 7:4; ISAIAH 37:3; LUKE 23:30"> For why a day of slaying, and of defouling, and of weepings, is ordained of the Lord God of hosts, in the valley of vision; and he searcheth the wall, and is worshipful on the hill &#91;or the mountain&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND KI 16:9; ISAIAH 21:2; AMOS 1:5"> And Elam took an arrow case, or a quiver, and the chariot of an horseman; and the shield made naked the wall.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:7–8; ISAIAH 10:28–32; JEREMIAH 39:1–3"> And thy chosen valleys, Jerusalem, shall be full of carts; and knights shall put their seats in the gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:2; 1ST KINGS 10:17; ISAIAH 36:1–3"> And the covering of Judah shall be showed; and thou shalt see in that day the place of armours of the house of the forest;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND KI 20:20; NEHEMIAH 3:16; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:1–6"> and ye shall see the crazings of the city of David, for those be multiplied. Ye gathered together the waters of the lower cistern,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and ye numbered the houses of Jerusalem, and ye destroyed &#91;the&#93; houses, to make strong the wall;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MICAH 7:7; JEREMIAH 33:2–3; ISAIAH 31:1"> and ye made a pit betwixt two walls, and ye restored the water of the eld &#91;or old&#93; cistern; and ye beheld not to him, that made that Jerusalem, and ye saw not the worker thereof afar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOEL 2:17; MICAH 1:16; JOEL 1:13"> And the Lord of hosts shall call in that day to weeping, and to mourning, and to baldness, and to a girdle of sackcloth;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:32; LUKE 17:26–29"> and lo! joy and gladness is to slay calves, and to strangle wethers, to eat flesh, and to drink wine; eat we, and drink we, for we shall die tomorrow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:13; 1ST SAMUEL 3:14; ISAIAH 5:9"> And the voice of the Lord of hosts is showed in mine ears, This wicked-ness shall not be forgiven to you, till ye die, saith the Lord God of hosts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:3; 2ND KI 18:18; 2ND KI 19:2"> The Lord God of hosts saith these things, Go thou, and enter to him that dwelleth in the tabernacle, to Shebna, the sovereign of the temple; and thou shalt say to him,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:60; 2ND CHRONICLES 16:14; 2ND SAMUEL 18:18"> What thou here, either as who here? for thou hast hewed &#91;out&#93; to thee a sepulchre here, thou hast hewed a memorial in high place diligently, a tabernacle in a stone to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:3; JOB 9:24; ESTHER 7:8"> Lo! the Lord shall make thee to be borne out, as a capon is borne out, and as a cloth, so he shall raise thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 17:13; AMOS 7:17"> He crowning shall crown thee with tribulation; he shall send thee as a ball into a large land and wide; there thou shalt die, and there shall be the chariot of thy glory, and the shame of the house of thy Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:24; PSALMS 75:6–7; JOB 40:11–12"> And I shall cast thee out of thy standing, and I shall put thee down of thy service.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:3; 2ND KI 18:18; ISAIAH 37:2"> And it shall be, in that day I shall call my servant Eliakim, the son of Hilkiah;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 45:8; ISAIAH 9:6–7; GENESIS 41:42–43"> and I shall clothe him in thy coat, and I shall comfort him with thy girdle, and I shall give thy power into the hands of him; and he shall be as a father to them that dwell in Jeru-salem, and to the house of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:18–19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:7; JOB 12:14"> And I shall give the key of the house of David on his shoulder; and he shall open, and none shall be that shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall be that shall open.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZRA 9:8; JOB 36:7; ZECHARIAH 10:4"> And I shall set him a stake, either a perch, in a faithful place, and he shall be into a seat of glory of the house of his father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 6:1–3; JOHN 20:21–23; MATTHEW 28:18"> And thou shalt hang on him all the glory of the house of his father, diverse kinds of vessels, each little vessel, from the vessels of cups, till to each vessel of musics.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:11; ESTHER 9:24–25; PSALMS 146:3"> In that day, saith the Lord of hosts, the stake that was set &#91;or fixed&#93; in the faithful place, shall be taken away, and it shall be broken, and shall fall down; and shall perish that hanged therein, for the Lord spake.</span>
</div>
