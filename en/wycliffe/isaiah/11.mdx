<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:16; ZECHARIAH 6:12"> And a rod shall go out of the root of Jesse, and a flower shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; of the root of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 61:1; JOHN 16:13; EPHESIANS 1:17–18"> And the Spirit of the Lord shall rest on him, the spirit of wisdom and of understanding, the spirit of counsel and of strength, the spirit of knowing and of piety;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 7:24; JOHN 8:15–16; LUKE 2:52"> and the spirit of the dread of the Lord shall fill him. He shall deem not by the sight of eyes, neither he shall reprove, either convict, by the hearing of ears;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:15; MALACHI 4:6"> but he shall deem in rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; poor men, and he shall reprove in equity, for the mild men of &#91;the&#93; earth. And he shall smite the land with the rod of his mouth, and with the spirit of his lips he shall slay the wicked man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:14; 1ST JOHN 1:9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:7"> And rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; shall be the girdle of his loins, and faith shall be the girding of his reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:25; HOSEA 2:18; EZEKIEL 34:25"> A wolf shall dwell with a lamb, and a leopard shall rest with a kid; a calf, and a lion, and a sheep shall dwell together, and a little child shall drive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A calf and a bear shall be pastured together; the whelps of them shall rest together, and a lion as an ox shall eat straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:5; PSALMS 140:3"> And a young sucking child from the teat shall delight on the hole of a snake, and he that is weaned shall put his hand in the cave of the cockatrice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:14; ISAIAH 52:10; PSALMS 98:2–3"> They shall not annoy, and shall not slay, in all mine holy hill &#91;or holy mountain&#93;; for why the earth is filled with the knowing of the Lord, as &#91;the&#93; waters of the sea covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 15:9–12; LUKE 2:32; ISAIAH 11:1"> In that day the root of Jesse, that standeth into the sign of peoples; heathen men shall beseech him, and his sepulchre shall be glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MICAH 7:12; ISAIAH 10:20; ZECHARIAH 10:8–12"> And it shall be in that day, the Lord shall add the second time his hand to have in possession the residue of his people that shall be left, of &#91;the&#93; Assyrian, and of Egypt, and of Pathros, and of Ethiopia, and of Elam, and of Shinar, and of Hamath, and of &#91;the&#93; isles of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 56:8; ISAIAH 43:6; ZEPHANIAH 3:10"> And he shall raise a sign to nations, and shall gather together the fleers-away of Israel; and he shall gather together the scattered men of Judah from &#91;the&#93; four coasts of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:18; HOSEA 1:11; EZEKIEL 37:16–24"> And the envy of Ephraim shall be done away, and the enemies of Judah shall perish; Ephraim shall not have envy to Judah, and Judah shall not fight against Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOEL 3:19; ISAIAH 25:10; JEREMIAH 49:28"> And they shall fly into the shoulders of Philistines by the sea, they shall take prey together of the sons of the east; Idumea and Moab shall be under the commandment of the hand of them, and the sons of Ammon shall be obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:12; ISAIAH 50:2"> And the Lord shall make desolate the tongue of the sea of Egypt, and he shall raise his hand on the flood in the strength of his spirit; and he shall smite, either part, it into seven rivers, so that shod men pass by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:23; ISAIAH 35:8–10; ISAIAH 11:11"> And a way shall be to my residue people that shall be left, of the Assyrians, as it was to Israel, in the day in which it ascended &#91;or went up&#93; from the land of Egypt.</span>
