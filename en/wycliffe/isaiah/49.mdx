<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:5; GALATIANS 1:15; ISAIAH 44:2"> Isles, hear ye, and peoples afar, perceive ye; the Lord called me from the womb, he thought on my name from the womb of my mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:16; ISAIAH 50:4"> And he hath set &#91;or put&#93; my mouth as a sharp sword, he defended me in the shadow of his hand, and setted &#91;or put&#93; me as a chosen arrow; he hid me in his arrow case,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:1; 1ST PETER 2:9; ISAIAH 44:23"> and said to me, Israel, thou art my servant, for I shall have glory in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 35:4; GALATIANS 4:11; ISAIAH 53:10–12"> And I said, I travailed in vain, I wasted my strength without cause, and vainly; therefore my doom is with the Lord, and my work is with my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:18; MATTHEW 17:5; JOHN 3:35"> And now the Lord, forming me a servant to himself from the womb, saith these things, that I bring again Jacob to him. And Israel shall not be gathered together; and I am glorified in the eyes of the Lord, and my God is made my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:47; ISAIAH 42:6; LUKE 2:32"> And he said, It is little, that thou be a servant to me, to raise the lineages of Jacob, and to convert the dregs of Israel; I gave thee into the light of heathen men, that thou be mine health till to the last part of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:67; 1ST PETER 2:4; ISAIAH 53:3"> The Lord, &#91;the&#93; again-buyer of Israel, the Holy thereof, saith these things to a despisable soul, and to a folk had in abomination, to the servant of lords, Kings shall see, and princes shall rise together, and shall worship, for the Lord, for he is faithful, and for the Holy of Israel, that chose thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:2; ISAIAH 42:6; PSALMS 69:13"> The Lord saith these things, In a pleasant time I heard thee, and in the day of health I helped thee; and I kept thee, and gave thee into a bond of peace of the people, that thou shouldest raise tillage upon the earth, and have in possession heritages, that be destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:7; ISAIAH 42:16; 1ST PETER 2:9"> that thou shouldest say to them that be bound, Go ye out, and to them that be in darknesses, Be ye showed. They shall be fed in ways, and the pastures of them shall be in all plain things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:16–17; MATTHEW 5:6; EZEKIEL 34:23"> They shall not hunger, and they shall no more thirst; and heat, and the sun shall not smite them; for the merciful doer of them shall govern them, and shall give drink to them at the wells of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:16; ISAIAH 62:10; ISAIAH 35:8–10"> And I shall set all mine hills &#91;or put my mountains&#93; into &#91;a&#93; way, and my paths shall be enhanced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:5–6; MATTHEW 8:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15"> Lo! these men shall come from &#91;a&#93; far, and lo! they shall come from the north, and from the sea, and these from the south land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:23; ISAIAH 51:3; PSALMS 96:11–13"> Heavens, praise ye, and thou earth, make full out joy; hills, sing ye heartily praising; for the Lord &#91;hath&#93; comforted his people, and shall have mercy on his poor men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:27; ROMANS 11:1–5; PSALMS 22:1"> And Zion said, The Lord hath forsaken me, and the Lord hath forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:21; PSALMS 103:13; MATTHEW 7:11"> Whether a woman may forget her young child, that she have not mercy on the son of her womb? though she forget, nevertheless I shall not forget thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HAGGAI 2:23; ISAIAH 62:6; ISAIAH 60:18"> Lo! I have written thee in mine hands; thy walls, be ever before mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:13; ISAIAH 62:5; EZEKIEL 28:24"> The builders be come; they that destroy thee, and scatter, shall go away from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:1–3; ISAIAH 66:12–13; ISAIAH 61:10"> Raise &#91;up&#93; thine eyes in compass, and see; all these men be gathered together, they be come to thee. I live, saith the Lord, for thou shalt be clothed with all these as with an ornament, and thou as a spousess shalt bind them to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:10; ISAIAH 54:1–2; JEREMIAH 30:18–19"> For why thy deserts, and thy wildernesses, and the land of thy falling, now shall be strait for &#91;the&#93; inhabiters; and they shall be driven away &#91;a&#93; far, that swallowed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HOSEA 1:10; JOSHUA 17:14–16; GALATIANS 4:26–28"> Yet the sons of thy barrenness shall say in thine ears, The place is strait to me, make thou a space to me for to dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:13; ISAIAH 3:26; LAMENTATIONS 1:1–3"> And thou shalt say in thine heart, Who engendered these sons to me? I am barren, not bearing child; I am led over, and prisoner; and who nourished these sons? I am destitute, and alone; and where were these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; LUKE 13:29; ISAIAH 14:2"> The Lord God saith these things, Lo! I raise mine hand to heathen men, and I shall enhance my sign to peoples; and they shall bring thy sons in arms, and they shall bear thy daughters on shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:14; ISAIAH 60:16; ISAIAH 60:3"> And kings shall be thy nursers or nourishers, and queens shall be thy nurses; with cheer cast down into &#91;the&#93; earth they shall worship thee, and they shall lick the dust of thy feet; and thou shalt know, that I am the Lord, on whom they shall not be shamed, that abide him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 11:21–22; PSALMS 124:6–7; MATTHEW 12:29"> Whether prey shall be taken away from a strong man? either that that is taken of a stalworthy man, may be safe?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:15–17; ISAIAH 35:4; ISAIAH 41:11–12"> For the Lord saith these things, Soothly and &#91;the&#93; captivity shall be taken away from the strong man, and that that is taken away of a stalworthy man, shall be saved. Forsooth I shall deem them that deemed thee, and I shall save thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 9:20; ISAIAH 41:14–20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:6"> And I shall feed thine enemies with their fleshes, and they shall be greatly filled with their blood, as with must; and each man shall know, that I am the Lord, saving thee, and thine again-buyer, the Strong of Jacob.</span>
