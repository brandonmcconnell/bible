<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ESTHER 9:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:23; HEBREWS 13:22"> And these be the words of the book, which Jeremy, the prophet, sent from Jerusalem to the residues of &#91;the&#93; elder men of &#91;the&#93; passing over, and to the priests, and to the prophets, and to all the people, whom Nebu-chadnezzar had led over from Jeru-salem into Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:24–28; JEREMIAH 27:20; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:9–10"> after that Jeconiah, the king, went out, and the lady, and the honest servants and chaste, and the princes of Judah went out of Jerusalem, and a subtle craftsman, and a goldsmith of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:11; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:20; JEREMIAH 39:14"> in the hand of Elasah, son of Shaphan, and of Gemariah, the son of Hilkiah, which Zedekiah, king of Judah, sent to Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, into Babylon. And Jeremy said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:5; ISAIAH 10:5–6; ISAIAH 45:7"> The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things to all the passing over, which I translated from Jerusalem into Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:28; JEREMIAH 29:10; EZEKIEL 28:26"> Build ye houses, and inhabit, and plant ye orchards, and eat ye &#91;the&#93; fruit of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 24:3–4; GENESIS 24:51; GENESIS 28:1–4"> take ye wives, and engender ye sons and daughters, and give ye wives to your sons, and give ye your daughters to husbands, and bear they sons and daughters; and be ye multiplied there, and do not ye be few in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:1–2; EZRA 6:10; 1ST PETER 2:13–17"> And seek ye &#91;the&#93; peace of the cities, to which I made you to pass over; and pray ye the Lord for it, for in the peace thereof shall be peace to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:6; JEREMIAH 5:31; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–11"> The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, Your prophets, that be in the midst of you, and your diviners, deceive you not; and take ye none heed to your dreams, which ye dream;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:15; JEREMIAH 29:31; JEREMIAH 29:23"> for they prophesy falsely to you in my name, and I sent not them, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 9:2; ZECHARIAH 7:5; JEREMIAH 24:6–7"> For the Lord saith these things, When seventy years begin to be &#91;ful&#93; filled in Babylon, I shall visit you, and I shall raise on you my good word, and I shall bring you again to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:8–12; PSALMS 33:11; MICAH 4:12"> For I know the thoughts which I think on you, saith the Lord, the thoughts of peace, and not of torment, that I give to you an end and patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:7–8; JEREMIAH 33:3; PSALMS 50:15"> And ye shall call me to help, and ye shall go, and shall worship me, and I shall hear you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:29–31; PSALMS 119:2; MATTHEW 7:7"> ye shall seek me, and ye shall find, when ye seek me in all your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:1–38; JEREMIAH 30:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 28:9"> And I shall be found of you, saith the Lord, and I shall bring again your captivity, and I shall gather you from all folks, and from all places, to which I casted out you &#91;or put you out&#93;, saith the Lord; and I shall make you to turn again from the place, to which I made you to pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:8–9; JEREMIAH 28:1–17; EZEKIEL 1:3"> For ye said, The Lord shall raise prophets to us in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:2–3; JEREMIAH 38:17–23; EZEKIEL 17:12–21"> For the Lord saith these things to the king, that sitteth on the seat of David, and to all the people, dweller of this city, to your brethren, that went not out with you into the passing over,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:8–10; JEREMIAH 29:18; JEREMIAH 24:1–3"> The Lord of hosts saith these things, Lo! I shall send among them sword, and hunger, and pestilence; and I shall set them as evil figs, that may not be eaten, for those be full evil &#91;or they be worst&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 29:8; JEREMIAH 15:4; JEREMIAH 42:18"> And I shall pursue them in sword, and in hunger, and in pestilence; and I shall give them into travailing in all realms of earth, into cursing, and into wondering, and into scorning, and into shame to all folks, to which I casted &#91;or cast&#93; them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:19; JEREMIAH 26:5; JEREMIAH 7:24–26"> For they heard not my words, saith the Lord, which I sent to them by my servants, prophets, and rose by night, and sent, and ye heard not, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:5; MICAH 4:10; EZEKIEL 3:15"> Therefore all the passing over, which I sent out from Jerusalem into Babylon, hear ye the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:14–15; LAMENTATIONS 2:14; JEREMIAH 29:8–9"> The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things to Ahab, the son of Kolaiah, and to Zedekiah, the son of Maaseiah, that prophesy to you leasing in my name, Lo! I shall betake them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and he shall smite them before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:15; DANIEL 3:6; DANIEL 3:21"> And cursing shall be taken of them to all the passing over of Judah, which is in Babylon, of men saying, The Lord set &#91;or put&#93; thee as Zedekiah, and as Ahab, which the king of Babylon fried in fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:14; HEBREWS 4:13; MALACHI 3:5"> for they did folly in Israel, and did adultery on the wives of their friends; and they spake a word falsely in my name, which I commanded not to them; I am judge and witness, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:31–32"> And thou shalt say to Shemaiah the Nehelamite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:3; JEREMIAH 29:29; JEREMIAH 52:24"> The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, For that that thou sentest books in my name to all the people, which is in Jerusalem, and to Zephaniah, the son of Maaseiah, the priest, and to all the priests, and saidest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:1–2; 2ND KI 9:11; JOHN 10:20"> The Lord gave thee priest for Jehoiada, the priest, that thou be duke in the house of the Lord on each man that is travailed of the fiend, and prophesying, that thou send him into stocks, and into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 25:16; JEREMIAH 43:2–3; JEREMIAH 29:26"> And now why blamest thou not Jeremy of Anathoth, that prophesieth to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:5; JEREMIAH 29:1; JEREMIAH 29:10"> For on this thing he sent to us into Babylon, and said, It is long; build ye houses, and inhabit, and plant ye orchards, and eat ye the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:25"> Therefore Zephaniah, the priest, read this book in the ears of Jeremy, the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the word of the Lord was made to Jeremy, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:9; JEREMIAH 29:23–24; JEREMIAH 14:14–15"> Send thou to all the passing over, and say, The Lord saith these things to Shemaiah the Nehelamite, For that that Shemaiah prophesied to you, and I sent not him, and he made you to trust in a leasing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 28:16; 2ND KI 7:19–20; JEREMIAH 17:6"> therefore the Lord saith these things, Lo! I shall visit on Shemaiah the Nehelamite, and on his seed; and no man sitting in the midst of this people shall be to him; and he shall not see the good, which I shall do to my people, saith the Lord, for he spake trespassing against the Lord.</span>
