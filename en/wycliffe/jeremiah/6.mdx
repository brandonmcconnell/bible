<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 3:14; JUDGES 1:21; JOSHUA 18:21–28"> Sons of Benjamin, be ye comforted in the middle of Jerusalem, and make ye noise with a clarion in Tekoa, and raise ye a banner on Bethhaccerem; for why evil and great sorrow is seen from the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:31; ISAIAH 1:8; ISAIAH 3:16–17"> I have likened the daughter of Zion to a fair woman and delicate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 19:43; JEREMIAH 39:1–3; 2ND KI 24:10–12"> Shepherds and their flocks shall come to it; they have pitched tents in it in compass; each man shall feed them, that be under his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:8; ZEPHANIAH 2:4; JOEL 3:9"> Hallow ye battle on it. Rise ye together, and ascend we &#91;or go we up&#93; in midday. Woe to us, for the day hath bowed down, for shadows be made longer in the eventide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:13; ISAIAH 32:14; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:19"> Rise ye, and ascend we &#91;or go we up&#93; in the night, and destroy we the houses thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:19–20; JEREMIAH 32:24"> For the Lord of hosts saith these things, Cut ye down the tree thereof, and shed ye earth about Jerusalem; this is the city of visitation; all false challenge is in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:8; EZEKIEL 7:23; EZEKIEL 7:11"> As a cistern maketh his water cold, so it made his malice cold; wickedness and destroying shall ever be heard therein before me, sickness and wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:18; HOSEA 9:12; JEREMIAH 17:23"> Jerusalem, be thou taught, lest peradventure my soul go away from thee; lest peradventure I set thee forsaken, a land unhabitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:9; JEREMIAH 16:16; OBADIAH 1:5–6"> The Lord of hosts saith these things, They shall gather till to a raisin, they shall gather the remnants of Israel as in a vinery &#91;or a vine&#93;; turn thine hand, as a gatherer of grapes to the basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; JEREMIAH 7:26; JOHN 7:7"> To whom shall I speak, and to whom shall I say witnessing, that he hear? Lo! the ears of them be uncircumcised, and they may not hear; lo! the word of the Lord is made to them into despite, and they shall not receive it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:21; JEREMIAH 20:9; MICAH 3:8"> Therefore I am full of the strong vengeance of the Lord, and I travailed suffering. Shed thou &#91;or Pour&#93; out vengeance on a little child withoutforth, and on the counsel of young men together; for a man with his wife shall be taken, and an eld &#91;or old&#93; man with him that is full of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:10; ISAIAH 5:25; DEUTERONOMY 28:39–43"> And the houses of them, the fields and wives together, shall go to other men; for I shall stretch forth mine hand on the dwellers of the land, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:11; JEREMIAH 8:10; MICAH 3:11"> For from the less unto the greater, all study to avarice; and all do guile, from the prophet unto the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:10; JEREMIAH 23:17; JEREMIAH 4:10"> And they healed the sorrow of the daughter of my people with evil fame, saying, Peace, peace, and no peace was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:12; MATTHEW 15:14; JEREMIAH 3:3"> They be shamed, that did abomination; yea, rather they were not shamed by confusion, and they could not be ashamed. Wherefore they shall fall down among them that shall fall down; they shall fall down in the time of their visitation, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:7; JEREMIAH 18:15"> The Lord saith these things, Stand ye on ways, and see ye, and ask ye of &#91;the&#93; eld &#91;or old&#93; paths, which is the good way; and go ye therein, and ye shall find refreshing to your souls. And they said, We shall not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:4; ISAIAH 58:1; ZECHARIAH 7:11"> And I ordained espyers, either beholders, on you, and I said, Hear ye the voice of a trump. And they said, We shall not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:10; JEREMIAH 31:10; MICAH 6:5"> Therefore, heathen men, hear ye, and, thou congregation, know, how great things I shall do to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:2; JEREMIAH 8:9; JEREMIAH 17:10"> Thou earth, hear, lo! I shall bring evils on this people, the fruit of their thoughts; for they heard not my words, and casted away my law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:11; AMOS 5:21–22; ISAIAH 60:6"> Whereto bring ye to me incense from Sheba, and a tree of spicery smelling sweetly from a far land? Your burnt sacrifices be not accepted, and your slain sacrifices pleased not me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:14; EZEKIEL 3:20; JEREMIAH 13:16"> Therefore the Lord God saith these things, Lo! I shall give fallings into this people, and fathers and sons together, a neighbour and kinsman, shall fall in them, and shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:41–43; JEREMIAH 10:22; JEREMIAH 6:1"> The Lord God saith these things, Lo! a people cometh from the land of the north, and a great folk shall rise together from the ends of &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 13:18; JEREMIAH 50:42; LUKE 21:25–26"> It shall take arrow and shield; it is cruel, and shall not have mercy; the voice thereof shall sound as the sea, and they made ready as a man to battle shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; on horses against thee, thou daughter of Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:31; JEREMIAH 50:43; JEREMIAH 49:24"> We &#91;have&#93; heard the fame thereof, our hands be made numb; tribulation hath taken us, sorrows have taken us as a woman travailing of child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:29; JEREMIAH 20:10; PSALMS 31:13"> Do not ye go out to the fields, and go ye not in the way, for the sword of the enemy, dread in compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:8; ZECHARIAH 12:10; ISAIAH 32:11"> The daughter of my people, be thou girded with hair-shirt, and be thou sprinkled together with ashes; make to thee mourning of &#91;the&#93; one alone begotten son, a bitter wailing, for why a waster shall come suddenly &#91;up&#93; on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:18; JEREMIAH 15:20; EZEKIEL 3:8–10"> I gave thee to be a strong prover in my people, and thou shalt know, and prove the way of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:4; JEREMIAH 5:23; JEREMIAH 18:18"> All these princes bowing away, going guilefully, be metal and iron; all be corrupt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:13; JEREMIAH 9:7; 1ST PETER 4:12"> The bellow failed, lead is wasted in the fire, the weller welled in vain; for the malices of them be not wasted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HOSEA 9:17; PSALMS 119:119; ISAIAH 1:22"> Call ye them reprovable silver, for the Lord hath cast them away.</span>
