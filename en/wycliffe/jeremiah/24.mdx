<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:10; 2ND KI 24:12–16"> The Lord showed to me, and lo! two panniers full of figs were set before the temple of the Lord, after that Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, translated Jeconiah, the son of Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, and the princes of him, and a subtle craftsman, and a goldsmith, from Jerusalem, and brought them into Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:7; ISAIAH 5:4; MICAH 7:1"> And one pannier had full good figs, as figs of the first time be wont to be; and one pannier had full evil figs, that might not be eaten, for those &#91;or they&#93; were evil figs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 8:2; ZECHARIAH 5:5–11; MATTHEW 25:32–33"> And the Lord said to me, Jeremy, what thing seest thou? And I said, Figs, good figs, full good; and evil figs, full evil, that may not be eaten, for those &#91;or they&#93; be evil figs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the word of the Lord was made to me, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:9; NAHUM 1:7; GALATIANS 4:9"> The Lord God of Israel saith these things, As these figs be good, so I shall know the transmigration of Judah, which I sent out from this place into the land of Chaldees, into good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:10; JEREMIAH 29:10; JEREMIAH 33:7"> And I shall set &#91;or put&#93; mine eyes on them to please, and I shall bring them again into this land; and I shall build them, and I shall not destroy them; and I shall plant them, and I shall not draw up by the root.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:10; JEREMIAH 30:22; EZEKIEL 36:24–28"> And I shall give to them an heart, that they know me, for I am the Lord; and they shall be into a people to me, and I shall be into God to them, for they shall turn again to me in all their heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:1; JEREMIAH 39:2–9; JEREMIAH 24:2"> And as the worst figs be, that may not be eaten, for those &#91;or they&#93; be evil figs, the Lord saith these things, So I shall give Zedekiah, the king of Judah, and the princes of him, and other men of Jerusalem, that dwell in this city, and that dwell in the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:4; JEREMIAH 29:18; DEUTERONOMY 28:37"> And I shall give them into travailing and torment in all realms of earth, into reproof, and into parable, and into a proverb, and into cursing, in all places to which I casted them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:19; EZEKIEL 5:12–17; JEREMIAH 16:4"> And I shall send in them sword, and hunger, and pestilence, till they be wasted from the land which I gave to them, and to the fathers of them.</span>
</div>
