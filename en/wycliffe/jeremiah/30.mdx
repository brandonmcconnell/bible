<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:15; JEREMIAH 1:1–2"> This is the word, that was made of the Lord to Jeremy, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 36:32; ISAIAH 30:8; HABAKKUK 2:2–3"> The Lord God of Israel saith these things, and speaketh, Write to thee in a book, all these words which I spake to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:14; JEREMIAH 16:15; JEREMIAH 30:18"> For lo! days come, saith the Lord, and I shall turn the turning of my people Israel and Judah, saith the Lord; and I shall turn them to the land which I gave to the fathers of them, and they shall have it in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And these be the words, which the Lord spake to Israel, and to Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:10–11; AMOS 8:10; ISAIAH 5:30"> For the Lord saith these things, We heard a word of dread; inward dread is, and peace is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NAHUM 2:10; JOEL 2:6; JEREMIAH 4:31"> Ask ye, and see, if a male beareth child; why therefore saw I the hand of each man on his loins, as of a woman travailing of child, and all faces be turned into yellow colour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 12:1; ZECHARIAH 14:1–2; MALACHI 4:1"> Woe! for that day is great, neither any is like it; and it is a time of tribulation to Jacob, and of him &#91;he&#93; shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NAHUM 1:13; EZEKIEL 34:27; ISAIAH 9:4"> And it shall be, in that day, saith the Lord of hosts, I shall all-break the yoke of him from thy neck, and I shall break his bonds; and aliens shall no more be lords of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSEA 3:5; ISAIAH 55:3–5; LUKE 1:69"> but they shall serve to their Lord God, and to David, their king, whom I shall raise for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:27–28; ISAIAH 43:5; JEREMIAH 29:14"> Therefore, Jacob, my servant, dread thou not, saith the Lord, and Israel, dread thou not; for lo! I shall save thee from a far land, and thy seed from the land of the captivity of them. And Jacob shall turn again, and shall rest, and shall flow with all goods; and none shall be whom he shall dread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:24; JEREMIAH 1:19; ISAIAH 8:10"> For I am with thee, saith the Lord, for to save thee. For I shall make &#91;full&#93; ending in all folks, in which I scattered thee; soothly I shall not make thee into &#91;full&#93; ending, but I shall chastise thee in doom, that thou be not seen to thee to be guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:18; JEREMIAH 30:15; JEREMIAH 14:17"> For the Lord saith these things, Thy breaking is uncurable, thy wound is the worst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:11; JEREMIAH 8:22; JEREMIAH 30:17"> None is, that deemeth thy doom to bind together; the profit of healings is not to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:6; JOB 30:21; LAMENTATIONS 1:2"> All thy lovers have forgotten thee, they shall not seek thee; for I have smitten thee with the wound of an enemy, with cruel chastising; for the multitude of thy wickedness, thy sins be made hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:14; JEREMIAH 30:12; JEREMIAH 6:6–7"> What criest thou on thy breaking? thy sorrow is uncurable; for the multitude of thy wickedness, and for thine hard sins, I have done these things to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:25; ISAIAH 33:1; EXODUS 23:22"> Therefore all that eat thee, shall be devoured, and all thine enemies shall be led into captivity; and they that destroy thee, shall be destroyed, and I shall give all thy robbers into raven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; JEREMIAH 33:6; 1ST PETER 2:24"> For I shall heal perfectly thy wound, and I shall make thee whole of thy wounds, saith the Lord; for thou, Zion, they called thee Cast out; this is it that had no seeker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HAGGAI 2:7–9; PSALMS 102:13; JEREMIAH 30:3"> The Lord saith these things, Lo! I shall turn the turning of the tabernacles of Jacob, and I shall have mercy on the houses of him; and the city shall be builded &#91;up&#93; in his highness, and the temple shall be founded by his order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:22; ISAIAH 51:11; ISAIAH 60:22"> And praising and the voice of players shall go out of them, and I shall multiply them, and they shall not be decreased; and I shall glorify them, and they shall not be made thin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:39; JEREMIAH 2:3; JEREMIAH 50:33–34"> And the sons thereof shall be as at the beginning, and the company thereof shall dwell before me; and I shall visit against all that do tribulation to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:5; EZEKIEL 34:23–24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:31"> And the duke thereof shall be of it, and a prince shall be brought forth of the midst thereof; and I shall join him, and he shall nigh to me; for who is this, that shall join his heart, that he nigh to me? saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:3; EZEKIEL 36:28; JEREMIAH 31:33"> And ye shall be into a people to me, and I shall be into God to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:19–20; ZECHARIAH 9:14; JEREMIAH 25:32"> Lo! the whirlwind of the Lord, a strong vengeance going out, a tempest falling down, shall rest in the head of wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:20; HOSEA 3:5; MICAH 4:1"> The Lord shall not turn away the ire &#91;or the wrath&#93; of indignation, till he do, and &#91;ful&#93; fill the thought of his heart; in the last days ye shall understand those things.</span>
