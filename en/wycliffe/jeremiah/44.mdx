<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:14; ISAIAH 11:11; EXODUS 14:2"> The word that was made to Jeremy, and to all the Jews, that dwelled in the land of Egypt, dwelling in Migdol, and in Tahpanhes, and in Memphis, and in the land of Pathros, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:11; JEREMIAH 9:11; JEREMIAH 34:22"> The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, Ye saw all this evil, which I brought on Jerusalem, and on all the cities of Judah; and lo! those &#91;or they&#93; be forsaken today, and no dweller is in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:26; DEUTERONOMY 13:6; DEUTERONOMY 32:17"> for the malice which they did, to stir me to wrathfulness &#91;or to wrath&#93;, and that they went, and made sacrifice, and worshipped alien gods, which they knew not, both ye, and they, and your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:5; JEREMIAH 7:25; JEREMIAH 7:13"> And I sent to you all my servants prophets; and I rose by night, and sent, and said, Do not ye do the word of such abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:10; JEREMIAH 19:13; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:16"> And they heard not, neither bowed down their ear, that they shall be converted from their evils, and should not make sacrifice to alien gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:20; JEREMIAH 21:5; ISAIAH 51:17"> And mine indignation and my strong vengeance is welled together, and is kindled in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem; and they be turned into wilderness, and wasteness, by this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:10; JEREMIAH 51:22; EZEKIEL 33:11"> And now the Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, Why do ye this great evil against your souls, that a man of you perish and a woman, a little child and a sucking infant, from the midst of Judah, neither any residue thing be left in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:18; JEREMIAH 25:6–7; JEREMIAH 44:3"> that stir me to wrath by the works of your hands, in making sacrifice to alien gods in the land of Egypt, into which ye entered, that ye dwell there, and that ye perish, and be into cursing, and into shame to all the folks of earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:17–18; JOSHUA 22:17–20; JEREMIAH 44:15–19"> Whether ye have forgotten the evils of your fathers, and the evils of the kings of Judah, and the evils of their wives, and your evils, and the evils of your wives, which they did in the land of Judah, and in the countries of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:14; 1ST PETER 5:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:4"> They be not cleansed unto this day, and they dreaded not, and they went not in the law of the Lord, and in my behests, which I gave before you, and before your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:10; LEVITICUS 26:17; LEVITICUS 20:5–6"> Therefore the Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, Lo! I shall set my face in you into evil, and I shall lose all Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:15–18; JEREMIAH 42:22; ISAIAH 65:15"> and I shall take the remnants of Judah, that setted their faces, to go into the land of Egypt, and to dwell there; and all shall be wasted in the land of Egypt, they shall fall down by sword, and shall be wasted in hunger, from the least unto the most, they shall die by sword and hunger, and shall be into swearing, and into miracle, or wonder, and into cursing, and into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:22; JEREMIAH 43:11; JEREMIAH 44:27–28"> And I shall visit on the dwellers of Egypt, as I visited on Jerusalem, in sword, and in hunger, and in pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 9:27; JEREMIAH 22:26–27; JEREMIAH 42:17"> And none shall be, that shall escape, and be &#91;the&#93; residue of the remnants of Jews, that go to be pilgrims in the land of Egypt, and to turn again to the land of Judah, to which they raise &#91;up&#93; their souls, that they turn again, and dwell there; they shall not turn again thither, no but they that fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:21; JEREMIAH 5:1–5; GENESIS 19:4"> Forsooth all men answered to Jeremy, and knew, that their wives made sacrifice to alien gods, and all &#91;the&#93; women, of which a great multitude stood, and all the people of dwellers in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 21:14–15; LUKE 19:27; JEREMIAH 16:15–17"> We shall not hear of thee the word which thou speakest to us in the name of our Lord God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:18; NEHEMIAH 9:34; JEREMIAH 44:21"> but we doing shall do each word that shall go out of our mouth, that we make sacrifice to the queen of heaven, and that we offer to it moist sacrifices, as we did, and our fathers, our kings, and our princes, in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem; and we were &#91;full-&#93;filled with loaves, and it was well to us, and we saw none evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MALACHI 3:13–15; NUMBERS 11:5–6; JOB 21:14–15"> But from that time, in which we ceased to make sacrifice to the queen of heaven, and to offer to it moist sacrifices, we had need to all things, and we were wasted by sword and hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:18; JEREMIAH 44:15; 1ST KINGS 21:25"> That if we make sacrifice to the queen of heaven, and offer to it moist sacrifices, whether without our hus-bands we made to it cakes, to worship it, and loaves to be offered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Jeremy said to all the people, against the men, and against the women, and against all the people, that answered to him the word, and he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:17; JEREMIAH 44:9; JEREMIAH 11:13"> Whether not the sacrifices which ye sacrificed in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, ye, and your fathers, your kings, and your princes, and the people of the land, stirred God to vengeance? The Lord had mind on these things, and it ascended &#91;or went up&#93; on his heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:18; ISAIAH 7:13; MALACHI 2:17"> and the Lord might no more bear, for the malice of your studies, and for the abominations which ye did. And your land is made into desolation, and into wondering, and into curse, for no dweller is, as this day is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:9; DANIEL 9:11–12; NEHEMIAH 13:18"> Therefore for ye made sacrifice to idols, and sinned to the Lord, and heard not the voice of the Lord, and went not in the law, and in the commandments, and in the witnesses of him, therefore these evils befell to you, as this day is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:7; JEREMIAH 44:15–16; JEREMIAH 42:15"> Forsooth Jeremy said to all the people, and to all the women, All Judah, that be in the land of Egypt, hear ye the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:12–15; ISAIAH 28:15"> The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, and speaketh, Ye and your wives spake with your mouths, and &#91;ful&#93; filled with your hands, and said, Make we our vows which we vowed, that we make sacrifice to the queen of heaven, and offer to it moist sacrifices; ye &#91;ful&#93; filled your vows, and did those &#91;or them&#93; in work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 50:16; EZEKIEL 20:39; HEBREWS 6:13"> Therefore, all Judah, that dwell in the land of Egypt, hear ye the word of the Lord; Lo! I swore in my great name, saith the Lord, that my name shall no more be called by the mouth of each man Jew, saying, The Lord God liveth, in all the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; 2ND KI 21:14; JEREMIAH 1:10"> Lo! I shall wake on them into evil, and not into good; and all the men of Judah, that be in the land of Egypt, shall be wasted, by sword and hunger, till they be wasted utterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:14; ISAIAH 27:12–13; ISAIAH 10:19"> And a few men that fled the sword, shall turn again from the land of Egypt into the land of Judah; and all the remnants of Judah, of them that enter into the land of Egypt, to dwell there, shall know, whose word shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled, mine either theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; MATTHEW 24:15–16; JEREMIAH 44:30"> And this shall be a sign to you, saith the Lord, that I shall visit on you in this place, that ye know, that verily my words shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled against you into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND KI 25:4–7; JEREMIAH 43:9–13; JEREMIAH 34:21"> The Lord saith these things, Lo! I shall betake Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, into the hand of his enemies, and into the hand of them that seek his life, as I betook Zedekiah, king of Judah, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, his enemy, and seeking his life.</span>
