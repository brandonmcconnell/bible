<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOEL 2:12; JEREMIAH 3:22; JEREMIAH 3:12"> Israel, if thou turnest again, saith the Lord, turn thou to me; if thou takest away thine offendings from my face, thou shalt not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:16; JEREMIAH 9:24; GENESIS 22:18"> And thou shalt swear, The Lord liveth, in truth, and in doom, and in rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;; and all folks shall bless him, and shall praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 10:12; LUKE 8:7; LUKE 8:14"> For the Lord God saith these things to a man of Judah, and to a dweller of Jerusalem, Make ye new to you a land tilled of new, either a fallow, and do not ye sow on thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:16; DEUTERONOMY 30:6; ROMANS 2:28–29"> Men of Judah, and dwellers of Jerusalem, be ye circumcised to the Lord, and do ye away the prepuces, either filths, of your hearts; lest peradventure mine indignation go out as fire, and be kindled, and none there be that quench, for the malice of your thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:14; JEREMIAH 6:1; JOSHUA 10:20"> Tell ye in Judah, and make ye heard in Jerusalem; speak ye, and sing ye with a trump in the land; cry ye strongly, and say ye, Be ye gathered together, and enter we into &#91;the&#93; strong cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:2; JEREMIAH 1:13–15; JEREMIAH 51:27"> Raise ye a sign in Zion, comfort ye, and do not ye stand; for I &#91;shall&#93; bring evil from the north, and a great sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:7; JEREMIAH 5:6; ISAIAH 6:11"> A lion shall go up from his den, and the robber of folks shall raise himself. He is gone out of his place, to set thy land into wilderness; thy cities shall be destroyed, abiding still without dweller.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 22:12; JEREMIAH 6:26; ISAIAH 10:4"> On this thing gird you with hair-shirts; wail ye, and yell, for the wrath of the strong vengeance of the Lord is not turned away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:9–16; ISAIAH 29:9–10; ISAIAH 22:3–5"> And it shall be, in that day, saith the Lord, the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of princes; and the priests shall wonder, and the prophets shall be astonied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:12; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–12; ISAIAH 37:35"> And I said, Alas! alas! alas! Lord God; therefore whether thou hast deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, Peace shall be to you, and lo! a sword is come unto the soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 13:15; EZEKIEL 17:10; JEREMIAH 23:19"> In that time it shall be said to this people and to Jerusalem, A burning wind in the ways that be in desert, be the ways of the daughter of my people, not to winnow, and not to purge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:16; EZEKIEL 5:8; EZEKIEL 6:11–13"> A spirit full of them shall come to me; and now I, but I shall speak my dooms with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:1; ISAIAH 66:15; HABAKKUK 1:8"> Lo! he shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; as a cloud, and his chariots as a tempest; his horses be swifter than eagles; woe to us, for we be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; ISAIAH 55:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:22"> Thou Jerusalem, wash thine heart from malice, that thou be made safe. How long shall harmful thoughts dwell in thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:16; JOSHUA 17:15; JUDGES 18:29"> For why the voice of a teller from Dan, and making known an idol from the hill or mount of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:15; EZEKIEL 21:22; ISAIAH 39:3"> Raise, ye folks; lo! it is heard in Jerusalem, that keepers be come from a far land, and give their voice on the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:23; 2ND KI 25:1–4; NEHEMIAH 9:30"> As the keepers of fields, they be made on it in compass; for it stirred me to wrathfulness &#91;or to wrath&#93;, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:19; PSALMS 107:17; PROVERBS 1:31"> Thy ways and thy thoughts have made this to thee; this malice of thee, for it is bitter, for it touched thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:16; ISAIAH 16:11; ISAIAH 22:4"> My womb acheth, my womb acheth; the wits of mine heart be troubled in me. I shall not be still, for my soul heard the voice of a trump, the cry of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 42:7; MATTHEW 10:28; JOEL 1:15"> Sorrow is called on sorrow, and all the land is destroyed; my taber-nacles be wasted suddenly, my skins be wasted suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:3; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:6–7"> How long shall I see them that flee, shall I hear the voice of a clarion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:20; ROMANS 16:19; JEREMIAH 5:21"> For my fond &#91;or foolish&#93; people knew not me; they be unwise sons, and cowards; they be wise to do evils, but they know not to do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:29; MARK 13:24–25; ISAIAH 13:10"> I beheld the land, and lo! it was void, and nought; and I beheld heavens, and no light there was in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:20; ISAIAH 5:25; NAHUM 1:5–6"> I saw mountains, and lo! they were moved, and all little hills were troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:2–3; JEREMIAH 12:4; JEREMIAH 9:10"> I looked, and no man there was, and each bird of heaven was gone away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; JEREMIAH 12:4; JEREMIAH 14:2–6"> I beheld, and lo! Carmel is forsaken, and all cities thereof be destroyed from the face of the Lord, and from the face of the ire &#91;or the wrath&#93; of his strong vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:28; JEREMIAH 5:10; JEREMIAH 5:18"> For the Lord saith these things, All the land shall be forsaken, but nevertheless, I shall not make a &#91;full&#93; ending.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; ISAIAH 50:3; HOSEA 4:3"> The earth shall mourn, and heavens above shall make sorrow, for that I spake; I thought, and it repented not me, neither I am turned away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 2:19–21; JEREMIAH 4:7; JEREMIAH 52:7"> Each city fled from the voice of a knight, and a man shooting an arrow; they entered into hard places, and ascended &#91;or went up&#93; into rocks of stone; all &#91;the&#93; cities be forsaken, and no man dwelleth in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND KI 9:30; LAMENTATIONS 1:2; LAMENTATIONS 1:19"> But what shalt thou destroyed, do? When thou shalt clothe thee with red scarlet, when thou shalt be adorned with a golden brooch, and shalt anoint thine eyes with woman’s ointment, thou shalt be arrayed in vain; thy lovers have despised thee, they shall seek thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:17; ISAIAH 1:15; JEREMIAH 13:21"> For I heard a voice as a woman travailing of child, the anguishes as of a woman childing; the voice of the daughter of Zion among them that die, and spread abroad her hands; Woe to me, for my soul failed for them that be slain.</span>
