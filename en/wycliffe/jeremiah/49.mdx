<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 1:13–15; ZEPHANIAH 2:8–11; EZEKIEL 25:2–10"> To the sons also of Ammon. The Lord saith these things. Whether no sons be of Israel, either an heir is not to it? why therefore wielded Malcham the heritage of Gad, and the people thereof dwelled in the cities of Gad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:19; EZEKIEL 21:20; DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> Lo! days come, saith the Lord, and I shall make the gnashing of battle heard on Rabbah of the sons of Ammon; and it shall be destroyed into noise, and the villages thereof shall be burnt with fire, and Israel shall wield his wielders, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:7; JEREMIAH 48:37; ISAIAH 15:2"> Yell, ye Heshbon, for Ai is destroyed; cry, ye daughters of Rabbah, gird you with hair-shirts, wail ye, and compass by hedges; for why Malcham shall be led into passing over, the priests thereof and princes thereof together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:17; JEREMIAH 9:23; JEREMIAH 21:13"> What hast thou glory in valleys? Thy valleys floated away &#91;or flowed down&#93;, thou delicate daughter, that haddest trust in thy treasures, and saidest, Who shall come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:5; JEREMIAH 49:29; JEREMIAH 15:8"> Lo! I shall bring in dread on thee, saith the Lord God of hosts, God of Israel, of all men that be in thy compass; and ye shall be scattered, each by himself, from your sight, and none shall be, that gather them that flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:39; JEREMIAH 48:47; EZEKIEL 16:53"> And after these things I shall make the fleers and &#91;the&#93; prisoners of the sons of Ammon to turn again, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 1:11–12; JEREMIAH 25:21; GENESIS 36:15"> To Idumea, the Lord of hosts saith these things. Whether wisdom is no more in Teman? Counsel perished from sons, the wisdom of them is made unprofitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:23; JEREMIAH 46:21; JEREMIAH 49:30"> Flee ye, and turn ye the backs; go down into a swallow, ye dwellers of Dedan, for I have brought the perdition of Esau on him, the time of his visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:5–6; ISAIAH 17:6"> If gatherers of grapes had come &#91;up&#93; on thee, they should have left a cluster; if thieves in the night, they should have ravished that that sufficed to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 17:14; MALACHI 1:3–4; ISAIAH 45:3"> Forsooth I have uncovered Esau, and I have showed the hid things of him, and he may not be able to be hid; his seed is destroyed, and his brethren, and his neighbours, and it shall not be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 68:5; HOSEA 14:3; JAMES 1:27"> Forsake thy fatherless children, and I shall make them to live, and thy widows shall hope in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:28–29; OBADIAH 1:16; JEREMIAH 25:15"> For the Lord saith these things, Lo! they drinking shall drink, to whom was no doom, that they should drink the cup. And shalt thou be left as innocent? thou shalt not be innocent, but thou drinking shalt drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 22:16; ISAIAH 34:6; GENESIS 36:33"> For I swore by myself, saith the Lord, that Bozrah shall be into wilderness, and into shame, and into forsaking, and into cursing; and all the cities thereof shall be into everlasting wildernesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:4; ISAIAH 37:7; EZEKIEL 7:25–26"> I heard an hearing of the Lord, and I am sent a messenger to heathen men; Be ye gathered together, and come ye against them, and rise we together into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 1:51; OBADIAH 1:2; MICAH 7:10"> For lo! I have given thee a little one among heathen men, despisable among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:25; AMOS 9:2; JOB 39:27"> Thy boast, and the pride of thine heart, hath deceived thee, that dwellest in the caves of stone, and enforcest or endeavourest to take the highness of a little hill; when thou as an eagle hast raised thy nest, from thence I shall draw thee down, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 35:7; JEREMIAH 50:13; JEREMIAH 49:13"> And Idumea shall be forsaken; each man that shall pass by it, shall wonder, and shall hiss on all the wounds thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:33; DEUTERONOMY 29:23; JEREMIAH 50:40"> as Sodom and Gomorrah is destroyed, and the nigh cities thereof, saith the Lord. A man shall not dwell there, and the son of man shall not inhabit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 12:5; JEREMIAH 30:21; JEREMIAH 50:44–46"> Lo! as a lion he shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; from the pride of Jordan to the strong fairness; for I shall make him run suddenly to it; and who shall be the chosen man whom I shall set &#91;or put&#93; before him? For who is like to me, and who shall suffer me? and who is this shepherd, that shall against-stand my cheer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:45; MALACHI 1:3–4; JEREMIAH 49:7"> Therefore hear ye the counsel of the Lord, which he took to Edom, and his thoughts, which he thought of the dwellers of Teman. If the little of the flock cast not them down, if they destroy not with them the dwelling of them, else no man give credence to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:46; EZEKIEL 32:10; ISAIAH 14:4–15"> The earth was moved of the voice of &#91;the&#93; falling of them; the cry of voice thereof was heard in the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:40–41; HOSEA 8:1; ISAIAH 13:8"> Lo! as an eagle he shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93;, and fly out, and he shall spread abroad his wings on Bozrah; and the heart of the strong men of Idumea shall be in that day, as the heart of a woman travailing of child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 10:9; 2ND KI 18:34; GENESIS 15:2"> To Damascus. Hamath is shamed, and Arpad, for they heard a full wicked hearing; they were troubled in the sea, for anguish they might not have rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Damascus was discomforted, it was turned into flight; trembling took it, anguishes and sorrows held it, as a woman travailing of child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:9; JEREMIAH 51:41; ISAIAH 1:26"> How forsook they a praiseable city, the city of gladness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:30; AMOS 4:10; JEREMIAH 11:22"> Therefore the young men thereof shall fall in the streets thereof, and all men of battle shall be stilled in that day, saith the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="AMOS 1:3–5; 1ST KINGS 15:18–20; JEREMIAH 43:12"> And I shall kindle fire in the wall of Damascus, and it shall devour the buildings of Benhadad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JUDGES 6:3; GENESIS 25:13; ISAIAH 11:14"> To Kedar, and to the realm&#91;s&#93; of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, smote, the Lord saith these things. Rise ye, and ascend &#91;or goeth up&#93; to Kedar, and destroy ye the sons of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:5; JEREMIAH 6:25; HABAKKUK 3:7"> They shall take the tabernacles of them, and the flocks of them; they shall take to them the skins of them, and all the vessels of them, and the camels of them; and they shall call on them inward dread in compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:9; JEREMIAH 27:6; ISAIAH 10:7"> Flee ye, go ye away greatly, ye that dwell in Hazor, sit in swallows, saith the Lord. For why Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, hath taken counsel against you, and he thought thoughts against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:11; NUMBERS 23:9; DEUTERONOMY 33:28"> Rise ye together, and ascend ye &#91;or goeth up&#93; to a peaceable folk, and dwelling trustily, saith the Lord; not doors nor bars be to it, and they dwell alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:26; JEREMIAH 25:23; JEREMIAH 49:36"> And the camels of them shall be into ravishing, and the multitude of their beasts into prey; and I shall scatter them into each wind, that be beclipped on the long hair, and by each coast of them I shall bring perishing on them, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:22; JEREMIAH 9:11; JEREMIAH 51:37"> And Hazor shall be into a dwelling place of dragons; it shall be forsaken till into without end; a man shall not dwell there, neither the son of man shall inhabit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 10:22; DANIEL 8:2; JEREMIAH 25:25"> The word of the Lord that was made to Jeremy, the prophet, against Elam, in the beginning of the realm of Zedekiah, king of Judah, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="ISAIAH 22:6; JEREMIAH 51:56; PSALMS 46:9"> The Lord of hosts, God of Israel, saith these things, Lo! I shall break the bow of Elam, and I shall take the strength of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:32; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:1; EZEKIEL 5:10"> And I shall bring on Elam four winds, from &#91;the&#93; four coasts of heaven, and I shall winnow them into all these winds, and no folk shall be, to whom the fleers of Elam shall not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:16; JEREMIAH 48:2; JEREMIAH 49:22"> And I shall make Elam for to dread before their enemies, and in the sight of men seeking the life of them; and I shall bring on them evil, the wrath of my strong vengeance, saith the Lord; and I shall send after them a sword, till I waste them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:10; DANIEL 7:9–14"> And I shall set my king’s seat in Elam, and I shall lose thereof kings, and princes, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:47; HOSEA 3:5; MICAH 4:1"> But in the last days I shall make the prisoners of Elam to turn again, saith the Lord.</span>
