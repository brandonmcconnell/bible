<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 1:11–12; JOEL 3:19; EZEKIEL 35:3–15"> &#91;The&#93; Vision of Obadiah. The Lord God saith these things to Edom. We heard an hearing of the Lord, and he sent a legate, either a messenger, to heathen men. Rise ye, and together rise we against him into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:18; ISAIAH 23:9; MICAH 7:10"> Lo! I gave thee little in heathen men, thou art full much worthy to be despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:13–15; PROVERBS 29:23; JEREMIAH 49:16"> The pride of thine heart enhanced thee, dwelling in the crazings of stones, araising thy seat. Which sayest in thine heart, Who shall draw me down into earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="AMOS 9:2; JOB 20:6–7; HABAKKUK 2:9"> Though thou shalt be raised as an eagle, and thou shalt put thy nest among stars, from thence I shall draw thee down, saith the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:9; DEUTERONOMY 24:21; ISAIAH 17:6"> If night thieves had entered to thee, if outlaws by night, how shouldest thou have been still? whether they should not have stolen things enough to them? If gatherers of grapes had entered to thee, whether they should have left namely raisins, or clusters, to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:10; PSALMS 139:1; ISAIAH 45:3"> How sought they Esau, searched the hid things of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 41:9; JEREMIAH 49:7; JEREMIAH 30:14"> Till to the terms they sent out thee; and all men of thy covenant of peace scorned, either deceived, thee, men of thy peace waxed strong against thee; they that shall eat with thee, shall set ambush, either treasons, under thee; there is no prudence in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:14; JOB 5:12–14; PSALMS 33:10"> Whether not in that day, saith the Lord, I shall lose the wise men of Idumea, and prudence of the mount of Esau?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:13; AMOS 1:12; ISAIAH 63:1–3"> And thy strong men shall dread of midday, either south, that a man of the hill of Esau perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 1:11; MALACHI 1:3–4; GENESIS 27:41"> For slaying and for wickedness against thy brother Jacob, confusion shall cover thee, and thou shalt perish without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 137:7; NAHUM 3:10; JOEL 3:3"> In the day when thou stoodest against him, when aliens took the host of him, and strangers entered into the gates of him, and sent lot on Jerusalem, thou were also as one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 35:15; MICAH 4:11; PROVERBS 17:5"> And thou shalt not despise in the day of thy brother, in the day of his pilgrimage, and thou shalt not be glad on the sons of Judah, in the day of perdition of them; and thou shalt not magnify thy mouth in the day of anguish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:15; EZEKIEL 35:5; EZEKIEL 35:10"> neither thou shalt enter into the gate of my people, in the day of falling of them; and thou shalt not despise in the evils of him, in the day of his destroying; and thou shalt not be sent out against his host, in the day of his destroying;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 1:6; PSALMS 31:8; OBADIAH 1:12"> neither thou shalt stand in the going out &#91;of the ways&#93;, that thou slay them that fled; and thou shalt not enclose together the residues, either left men, of him, in the day of tribulation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JAMES 2:13; HABAKKUK 2:8; EZEKIEL 30:3"> for the day of the Lord is nigh on all heathen men. As thou hast done, it shall be done to thee; he shall convert thy yielding into thine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOEL 3:17; JEREMIAH 49:12; JEREMIAH 25:15–16"> For as ye drank on mine holy hill, all heathen men shall drink busily, and they shall drink, and they shall swallow up; and they shall be as if they be not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:27; AMOS 9:11–15; ISAIAH 14:1–2"> And salvation shall be in the hill of Zion, and it shall be holy; and the house of Jacob shall wield them which wielded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 12:6; ISAIAH 10:17; OBADIAH 1:16"> And the house of Jacob shall be fire, and the house of Joseph shall be flame, and the house of Esau shall be stubble; and they shall be kindled in them, and they shall devour them; and remnants shall not be of the house of Esau, for the Lord spake,.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:44; ZEPHANIAH 2:4–7; AMOS 9:12"> And these that be at the south, shall inherit the hill of Esau; and they that be in the low fields, shall inherit Philistines; and they shall wield the country of Ephraim, and the country of Samaria; and Benjamin shall wield Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST KINGS 17:9–10; LUKE 4:26; JEREMIAH 32:44"> And transmigration, either passing over, of this host of sons of Israel shall wield all places of Canaanites, till to Zarephath; and the transmigration of Jerusalem, which is in Sepharad, shall wield the cities of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 14:9; JAMES 5:20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15"> And saviours shall ascend or go up into the hill of Zion, for to deem the hill of Esau, and a realm shall be to the Lord.</span>
