<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:17–29"> This is the possession that the sons of Israel wielded in the land of Canaan, which land Eleazar the priest, and Joshua, the son of Nun, and the princes of the meines of the lineages of Israel gave to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:54; NUMBERS 34:13; NUMBERS 26:55–56"> and these parted all things by lot, as the Lord commanded in the hand of Moses, to the nine lineages, and to the half lineage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:14; JOSHUA 13:8; JOSHUA 13:32–33"> For Moses had given to the two lineages and to the half lineage possession over Jordan; without the Levites, that took nothing of the land among their brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 48:5; 1ST CHRONICLES 5:1–2; NUMBERS 35:2–8"> but the sons of Joseph were parted into two lineages, of Manasseh, and of Ephraim, and were heirs into the place of them. And the Levites took none other part in the land, no but cities to dwell in, and the suburbs of those &#91;or them&#93; to their work beasts and their sheep to be fed in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:2; NUMBERS 35:1–2"> As the Lord commanded to Moses, so the sons of Israel did, and they parted the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:30; NUMBERS 13:6; NUMBERS 14:24"> And so the sons of Judah went to Joshua in Gilgal; and Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, of Kenaz, spake to him, Thou knowest, what the Lord spake to Moses, the man of God, of me and of thee in Kadeshbarnea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:6; NUMBERS 13:26–33; NUMBERS 14:6–10"> I was of forty years, when Moses, the servant of the Lord, sent me from Kadeshbarnea, that I should behold the land, and I told to him that, that seemed sooth to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:24; DEUTERONOMY 1:36; NUMBERS 13:31"> And my brethren, that went up with me, discomforted the heart of the people, and nevertheless I pursued &#91;or followed&#93; my Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:3; DEUTERONOMY 1:36; NUMBERS 14:22–24"> And Moses swore in that day, and said, The land, that thy foot hath trodden, shall be thy possession, and of thy sons without end; for thou pursuedest &#91;or hast followed&#93; thy Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:33–34; NUMBERS 14:30; JOSHUA 11:18"> Soothly the Lord granted life to me, as he promised, till into present day. Forty years and five be, since the Lord spake this word to Moses, when Israel went through wilderness. Today I am of fourscore years and five,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:7; PSALMS 103:5; DEUTERONOMY 31:2"> and I am as mighty, as I was mighty in that time, when I was sent to espy; the strength of that time dwelleth stably in me till to this day, as well to fight, as to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:33; PHILIPPIANS 4:13; NUMBERS 13:33"> Therefore give thou to me this hill, which the Lord promised to me, while also thou heardest, in which hill be Anakim, and great cities, and strengthened; if in hap the Lord is with me, and I may do them away, as he promised to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 1:20; JOSHUA 22:6; 1ST CHRONICLES 6:55–56"> And Joshua blessed Caleb, and he gave to him Hebron into possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:8–9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:58"> And from that time Hebron was of Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, of Kenaz, unto this present day; for he pursued &#91;or followed&#93; the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:23; GENESIS 23:2; JOSHUA 15:13"> The name of Hebron was called before Kiriatharba. Arba, the greatest, was set there in the land of Anakim; and the land ceased from battles.</span>
