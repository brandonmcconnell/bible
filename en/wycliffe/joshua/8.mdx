<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; DEUTERONOMY 7:18; DEUTERONOMY 1:21"> And the Lord said to Joshua, Neither dread thou, nor be thou afeared; take with thee all the multitude of fighting men, and rise thou, and go up into the city of Ai; lo, I have betaken into thine hand the king thereof, and the people, and the city, and the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:14; JOSHUA 6:21; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:22"> And thou shalt do to the city of Ai, and to the king thereof, as thou didest to Jericho, and the king thereof; soothly ye shall take to you the prey, and all &#91;the&#93; living beasts, set &#91;or put&#93; thou ambushes, either ambushments, to the city behind it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:39; MATTHEW 25:6; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:2"> And Joshua rose, and all the host of fighting men with him, for to go up into Ai; and by night he sent thirty chosen thousand of strong men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 20:29; JOSHUA 8:16; JUDGES 20:36"> and he commanded to them, and said, Set &#91;or Put&#93; ye ambushments behind the city, and go ye not further; and all ye shall be ready;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:5; MATTHEW 10:16; JUDGES 20:31–33"> forsooth I, and the tother multitude which is with me, shall come on the contrary side against the city; and when they shall go out against us, as we did before, we shall flee, and turn the backs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 20:32; EXODUS 15:9; ECCLESIASTES 8:11"> till they pursue us, and be drawn away further from the city; for they shall guess, that we shall flee them as we did before. Then while we shall flee, and while they pursue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30–31; 2ND KI 5:1; JOSHUA 8:1"> ye shall rise from the ambushments, and shall waste the city; and your Lord God shall betake it into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 13:28; JOSHUA 1:16; JOSHUA 1:9"> And when ye have taken it, burn ye it; do ye all things, as I have commanded to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:2; GENESIS 12:8; EZRA 2:28"> And Joshua let them go, and they went to the place of &#91;the&#93; ambushments, and sat betwixt Bethel and Ai, at the west coast of the city of Ai. Forsooth Joshua dwelled in that night in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:12; PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 22:3"> And he rose early, and numbered his fellows, and he went up with the elder men in the front of the host, and was compassed with the help of fighters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And when they had come, and had gone up against the city, they stood at the north coast of the city, betwixt the which city and them a valley was in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:2–3"> And Joshua had chosen five thousand men, and he had set them in &#91;the&#93; ambushments betwixt Bethel and Ai, in the west part of the same city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:12; JOSHUA 8:8"> And all the tother host dressed the battle array to the north, so &#91;that&#93; the last men of the multitude reached to the west coast of the city. Then Joshua went in that night, and stood in the midst of &#91;the&#93; valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:39; JUDGES 20:34–36; DEUTERONOMY 1:1"> and when the king of Ai had seen this, he hasted early, and went out with all the host of the city, and he dressed battle array against the desert; and he wist not that ambushments were hid behind his back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:12; JOSHUA 16:1; JUDGES 20:36"> Forsooth Joshua and all the multitude of Israel gave place, feigning dread, and fleeing by the way of wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 20:31; JUDGES 20:36–39; JOSHUA 8:5–6"> and the men cried together, and with gladness stirred themselves together, and they pursued the men of Israel. And when they had gone away from the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 5:13; JOSHUA 8:3; ISAIAH 19:11–13"> and soothly not one had &#91;or was&#93; left in the city of Ai and Bethel, that pursued not Israel, and they left the cities open, as they had broken out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:26; JOB 15:25; 1ST SAMUEL 17:41"> the Lord said to Joshua, Raise up the shield &#91;or the sword&#93; that is in thine hand, against the city of Ai; for I shall give it to thee. And when Joshua had raised up his banner or the shield &#91;or the sword&#93; against the city of Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the ambushments, that were hid, rose up anon; and they went to the city, and took &#91;it&#93;, and burnt it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 76:5; JOB 11:20; GENESIS 19:28"> Forsooth the men of the city, that pursued Joshua, beheld, and saw the smoke of the city ascend &#91;or go up&#93; till to heaven; and they might no more flee hither and thither; mostly since they that had feigned flight, and went to wilderness, withstood strongliest against the pursuers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Joshua saw, and all Israel, that the city was taken, and that the smoke of the city went up; and he turned again, and killed the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:2; JOSHUA 10:28; JOSHUA 6:21"> And also those men that had taken and burnt the city, went out of the city against their enemies, and they began to smite the middle men of their enemies; and when their adversaries were slain behind and before, so that no man of so great &#91;a&#93; multitude was saved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:8; JOSHUA 10:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:20"> they took also the king of Ai living, and they brought him to Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:10–14; JOSHUA 10:30–41; NUMBERS 21:24"> Therefore, when all the men were slain, that pursued Israel fleeing to desert, and had fallen by sword in the same place, the sons of Israel turned again, and destroyed the city of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:16–18"> Forsooth they that felled down in the same day, from man till to woman, were twelve thousand of men, all men of the city of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 17:11–12; JOSHUA 8:18"> For Joshua withdrew not his hand, which he had dressed on high holding up his banner or the shield &#91;or the sword&#93;, till that all the dwellers of Ai were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:2; NUMBERS 31:26; PSALMS 50:10"> And the sons of Israel parted to themselves the work beasts, and the prey of the city, as the Lord com-manded to Joshua;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:16; JEREMIAH 49:2; 2ND KI 19:25"> and Joshua burnt that city, and made it an everlasting burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:26; 2ND SAMUEL 18:17; DEUTERONOMY 21:22–23"> And he hanged the king thereof in a gibbet, till to the eventide, and the going down of the sun. And Joshua commanded, and they putted down his dead body from the cross; and they casted forth him in that entering of the city, and gathered on him a great heap of stones, which heap dwelleth till into present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:4–6; EXODUS 20:24; GENESIS 12:7–8"> Then Joshua builded an altar to the Lord God of Israel in the hill of Ebal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:5–7; EXODUS 20:24–25; JOSHUA 8:34–35"> as Moses, the servant of the Lord, commanded to the sons of Israel, and it is written in the book of Moses’ law, an altar of stones unpolished, that iron hath not touched. And he offered thereon burnt sacrifices to the Lord, and he offered also peaceable sacrifices;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:2–3; DEUTERONOMY 27:8"> and he wrote on the stones the Deuteronomy of Moses’ law, not all the book, but the ten behests only, which he had declared before the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:29; DEUTERONOMY 31:12; DEUTERONOMY 31:9"> And all the people, and the greater men in birth, and dukes, and judges, stood on either side of the ark, in the sight of priests and deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, that bare the ark of the bond of peace of the Lord; as a comeling, so and a man born in the land; the half part of them stood beside the hill Gerizim, and the half part of them stood beside the hill Ebal, as Moses, the servant of the Lord, commanded. And Joshua first blessed the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 8:2–3; NEHEMIAH 13:1; DEUTERONOMY 31:10–12"> And after these things he read all the words of blessing and of cursing, and all things that were written in the book of law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:12; NEHEMIAH 8:2; JEREMIAH 26:2"> Joshua left nothing untouched of these things that Moses commanded; but he declared all things before all the multitude of Israel, to women, and little children, and to comelings that dwelled among them.</span>
