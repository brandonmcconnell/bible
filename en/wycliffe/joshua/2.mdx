</div>
<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:20; EZEKIEL 47:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:25"> and when the gate was closed in darknesses, and they went out together, I know not whither they went; pursue ye them soon, and ye shall overtake them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:23; JAMES 2:25; COLOSSIANS 3:3"> Forsooth she had made the men to go up into the solar of her house, and she covered them with stubble, or sheaves, of flax, that was there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 3:28; JOSHUA 2:5; JUDGES 12:5"> And they, that were sent, pursued &#91;or followed&#93; after them by the way that leadeth to the fords of Jordan; and when they were gone out, anon the gate was closed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> &#91;And&#93; They that were hid, slept not yet, and lo! the woman went up to them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 23:27; DEUTERONOMY 2:25; JOSHUA 2:11"> and said, I know that the Lord hath betaken to you this land; for your fearedfulness is fallen into us, and all the dwellers of the land be abashed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:21–35; EXODUS 15:14–16; EXODUS 14:21–31"> We have heard, that the Lord hath dried up the waters of the Red Sea at your entering, when ye went out of Egypt; and what things ye did to the two kings of Amorites, that were beyond Jordan, to Sihon and Og, which ye killed;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:39; JOSHUA 5:1; JOSHUA 7:5"> and we heard these things, and we dreaded, and our heart was sick, and spirit dwelled not in us at your entering; for the Lord your God himself is God in heaven above, and in earth beneath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:18; JOSHUA 9:18–20; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:16–18"> Now therefore swear ye to me by the Lord God, that as I did mercy with you, so and ye do with the house of my father; and give ye to me a very sign,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> that ye save my father, and my mother, and my brethren, and &#91;my&#93; sisters, and all things that be theirs, and deliver our lives from death.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:7; JUDGES 1:24–25; GENESIS 24:49"> Which answered to her, Our life be for you into death, if nevertheless thou betrayest not us; and when the Lord hath betaken to us the land, we shall do mercy and truth with thee.</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JAMES 2:25; 1ST SAMUEL 23:14; PSALMS 11:1"> And she said to them, Go ye up to the hilly places &#91;or mountains&#93;, lest peradventure the men turning again meet you; and be ye hid there three days, till they come again; and so ye shall go by your way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 30:2; 2ND SAMUEL 21:7; GENESIS 24:3–8"> Which said to her, We shall be guiltless of this oath, by which thou hast charged us,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:23; JOSHUA 2:21; JOSHUA 2:12–13"> if, when we enter into the land, this red cord is not a sign, and thou bindest it not in the window, by which thou lettest us down; and thou gatherest not into thine house thy father, and mother, and brethren, and all thy kindred;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:31; 1ST KINGS 2:36–42; EXODUS 12:13"> the blood of him shall be on his head, that goeth out at the door of thine house, and we shall be guiltless; forsooth the blood of all men that be in the house with thee, shall turn into our head, if any man toucheth them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:13"> That if thou wilt betray us, and bring forth into the midst this word, we shall be clean of this oath, by which thou hast charged us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 2:5; JOSHUA 2:18; MATTHEW 7:24"> And she answered, As ye have spoken, so be it done. And she let them go, that they should go forth, and she hanged the red cord in her window.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 19:10–12; 2ND SAMUEL 17:20; PSALMS 32:6–7"> And they went forth, and came into the hilly places &#91;or mountains&#93;, and dwelled there three days, till they turned again that pursued them; for they sought them by each way, and found not them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And when the seekers entered into the city again, the spyers &#91;or spies&#93; turned again, and came down from the hill; and when they had passed &#91;over&#93; Jordan, they came to Joshua, the son of Nun; and they told to him all things that befelled to them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 23:31; JOSHUA 1:8; JOSHUA 2:9–11"> and said, The Lord hath betaken all the land into our hands, and all the dwellers thereof be cast down by dread.</span>
