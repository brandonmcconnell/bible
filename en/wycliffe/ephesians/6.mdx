<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:20–25; PROVERBS 23:22; PROVERBS 6:20"> Sons, obey ye to your father and mother, in the Lord; for this thing is rightful &#91;or is just&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12; DEUTERONOMY 27:16; MATTHEW 15:4–6"> Honour thou thy father and mother, that is the first commandment in &#91;the&#93; promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:16; DEUTERONOMY 4:40; DEUTERONOMY 12:28"> that it be well to thee, and that thou be long living on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:21; PROVERBS 22:6; PROVERBS 29:15"> And, fathers, do not ye provoke your sons to wrath; but nourish ye them in the teaching and chastising &#91;or in the discipline and correction&#93; of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:18–21; TITUS 2:9–10; COLOSSIANS 3:17–24"> Servants, obey ye to fleshly lords with dread and trembling, in simple-ness of your heart, as to Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:22–23; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:4; GALATIANS 1:10"> not serving at the eye, as pleasing to men, but as servants of Christ; doing the will of God by discretion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:23; EPHESIANS 6:5–6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:31"> with good will serving as to the Lord, and not as to men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:24; LUKE 14:14; PROVERBS 11:18"> witting that each man, whatever good thing he shall do, he shall receive this of the Lord, whether servant, whether free man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:25–1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:34; ROMANS 2:11"> And, ye lords, do the same things to them, forgiving menacings; witting that both their Lord and yours is in heavens, and the taking of persons is not with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; ISAIAH 40:31; JOSHUA 1:9"> Here afterward, brethren, be ye comforted in the Lord, and in the might of his virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4; ROMANS 13:12; EPHESIANS 6:13"> Clothe you with the armour of God, that ye may stand against the ambushings, &#91;or assailings&#93;, of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4; EPHESIANS 2:2"> For why striving &#91;or battle&#93; is not to us against flesh and blood, but against &#91;the&#93; princes and potentates, against governors of the world of these darknesses, against spiritual things of wickedness, in heavenly things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 10:4; LUKE 21:36; EPHESIANS 6:11–17"> Therefore take ye the armour of God, that ye may against-stand in the evil day; and in all things stand perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:17; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:8; ISAIAH 11:5"> Therefore stand ye, and be girded about your loins in soothfastness, and clothed with the habergeon of rightwiseness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:7; ROMANS 10:15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:18–21"> and your feet shod in making ready of the gospel of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8–9; 1ST JOHN 5:4–5; PROVERBS 18:10"> In all things take ye the shield of faith, in which ye may quench all the fiery darts of him that is most wicked or the worst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; ISAIAH 59:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:15"> And take ye the helmet of health, and the sword of the Ghost, that is, the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:17; COLOSSIANS 4:2; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> By all prayer and beseeching pray ye all time in Spirit, and in him waking in all busyness, and beseeching for all holy men &#91;or for all saints&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:29; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:1"> and for me; that word be given to me in opening of my mouth, with trust to make known the mystery of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:20; COLOSSIANS 4:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:9"> for which I am set in message in a chain; so that in it I be hardy to speak, as it behooveth me &#91;to speak out&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:12; TITUS 3:12"> And &#91;that&#93; ye know, what things be about me, what I do, Tychicus, my most dear brother, and true minister in the Lord, shall make all things known to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:25; COLOSSIANS 4:7–8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:17"> whom I sent to you for this same thing, that ye know what things be about us, and that he comfort your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:16; GALATIANS 5:6; 1ST PETER 5:14"> Peace to brethren, and charity, with faith of God our Father, and of the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:37; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; MATTHEW 6:13"> Grace with all men that love our Lord Jesus Christ in uncorruption. Amen, that is, So be it.</span>
