<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOEL 3:9–11; 2ND CHRONICLES 25:8; JEREMIAH 51:20–23"> He went up, that shall scatter before thee, that shall keep &#91;the&#93; besieging; behold thou the way, comfort thou loins, strengthen thou virtue greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 80:12–13; EZEKIEL 37:23; ZEPHANIAH 3:11"> For as the Lord yielded the pride of Jacob, so the pride of Israel; for destroyers scattered them, and destroyed the generations of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:14–15; ISAIAH 63:1–3; ZECHARIAH 6:2"> The shields of strong men of him be fiery, men of the host be in red clothes; reins of fire of &#91;the&#93; chariots, in the day of his making ready; and the leaders thereof be asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:13; EZEKIEL 26:10; NAHUM 3:2–3"> In ways they be troubled together, carts of four horses be hurtled together in streets; the sight of them as lamps, as lightnings running about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:12; JEREMIAH 51:27–28; JEREMIAH 50:29"> He shall bethink of his strong men, they shall fall in their ways; and swiftly they shall go up on the walls thereof, and shadowing place shall be made ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:1–2; 2ND PETER 3:10–11"> Gates of floods be opened, and the temple is broken down to &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 38:14; ISAIAH 59:11; LUKE 23:48"> And a knight is led away captive, and the handmaids thereof shall be driven sorrowing as culvers, grutching in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:1; JEREMIAH 46:5"> And Nineveh, as a cistern of waters the waters thereof; forsooth they fled; Stand ye, stand ye, and there is not that shall turn again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:12; DANIEL 11:8; NAHUM 2:12–13"> Ravish ye silver, ravish ye gold; and there is none end of riches, of all desirable vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOEL 2:6; PSALMS 22:14; DANIEL 5:6"> It is destroyed, and cut, and rent, or torn, and heart failing, and unknitting of small knees, and failing in all reins; and the face of all be as blackness of a pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:29; JEREMIAH 2:15; ISAIAH 31:4"> Where is the dwelling of lions, and &#91;the&#93; pastures of whelps of lions? To which city the lion went, that the whelp of the lion should enter thither, and there is not that shall make afeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:34; PSALMS 17:12; ISAIAH 10:6–14"> The lion took enough to his whelps, and slew to his lionesses; and filled their dens with prey, and his couch with raven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:13; PSALMS 46:9; NAHUM 3:5"> Lo! I to thee, saith the Lord God of hosts; and I shall burn thy carts of four horses till to the highest, and sword shall eat thy small lions; and I shall destroy thy prey from the land, and the voice of thy messengers shall no more be heard.</span>
