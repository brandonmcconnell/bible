<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:1; ZECHARIAH 7:1; ESTHER 1:2"> The words of Nehemiah, the son of Hachaliah. And it was done in the month of Chisleu, that is, November, in the twentieth year, and I was in the castle of Susa;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 7:2; EZEKIEL 6:9; JEREMIAH 44:14"> and Hanani, one of my brethren, came to me, he and men of Judah; and I asked them of the Jews, that were left, and were alive of the captivity, and of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 2:17; 2ND KI 25:10; NEHEMIAH 7:6"> And they said to me, They that be left of the captivity, and that dwelled there still in the province, be in great torment, and shame; and the wall of Jerusalem is destroyed, and the gates of it be burnt with fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 9:3; PSALMS 137:1; EZRA 10:1"> And when I had heard such words, I sat and wept, and mourned many days, and I fasted, and prayed before the face of God of heaven;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 20:6; DANIEL 9:4–19; DEUTERONOMY 7:21"> and I said, I beseech thee, Lord God of heaven, that art strong, great, and fearedful, that keepest covenant and mercy with them that love thee, and keep thy behests;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 9:20; DANIEL 9:4; PSALMS 34:15"> thine ear be made hearkening, and thine eyes opened, that thou hear the prayer of thy servant, by which prayer I pray before thee now, by night and day, for the sons of Israel, thy servants, and I acknowledge for the sins of the sons of Israel, by which they have sinned to thee; both I and the house of my father have sinned;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 9:5–6; DANIEL 9:11; DANIEL 9:13"> we were deceived by vanity, and we kept not thy commandments, and ceremonies, and dooms, which thou commandedest to Moses, thy servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:64; DEUTERONOMY 4:25–27; LEVITICUS 26:33–46"> Have mind of the word, which thou commandedest to thy servant Moses, and saidest, When ye have trespassed, I shall scatter you into peoples;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:11–14; DEUTERONOMY 30:2–5; DEUTERONOMY 4:29–31"> and if ye turn again to me, that ye keep my behests, and do those &#91;or them&#93;, yea, though ye be led away to the furthest things of heaven, from thence I shall gather you together, and I shall bring you into the place, that I have chosen, that my name should dwell there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:29; DANIEL 9:15–27; EXODUS 32:11"> And we be thy servants, and thy people, which thou hast again-bought in thy great strength, and in thy strong hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 1:6; NEHEMIAH 2:8; PROVERBS 21:1"> Lord, I beseech thee, thine ear be attentive to the prayer of thy servant, and to the prayer of thy servants, that will dread thy name; and dress thy servant today, and give thou mercy to him before this man, that is, Artaxerxes, king. For I was the bottler of the king.</span>
</div>
