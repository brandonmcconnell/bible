<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:5; JOSHUA 24:9–10; NUMBERS 23:8–11"> for they met not the sons of Israel with bread and water, and they hired against the sons of Israel Balaam, for to curse them; and our God turned the cursing into blessing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:2; EXODUS 12:38; NUMBERS 11:4"> And it was done, when the people had heard the law, they separated each alien from Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:44; NEHEMIAH 6:17–18; NEHEMIAH 13:28"> And upon these things was Eliashib, the priest, that was sovereign in the chamber of the house of our God, and was the neighbour of Tobiah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, friend, by the matrimony of his cousin, that had wedded the daughter of Sanballat.">a</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:44; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:11; NUMBERS 18:21–24"> Therefore he made to him a great chamber in the house of God; and there before him were men that kept the gifts that were offered, and incense, and diverse vessels, and the tithe of wheat, of wine, and of oil, and the parts of deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, and of singers, and of porters, and the first fruits of priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 5:14; NEHEMIAH 2:5–6; MATTHEW 13:25"> And in all these things I was not in Jerusalem; for in the two and thirtieth year of Artaxerxes, king of Babylon, I came to the king, and in the end of days I prayed the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:5; MATTHEW 21:12–13; LAMENTATIONS 1:10"> And I came into Jerusalem, and I understood the evil that Eliashib had done to Tobiah, to make to him a chamber in the porches of God’s house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 11:15–17; EZRA 10:1; PSALMS 69:9"> and to me it seemed full evil. And I casted forth the vessels of the house of Tobiah out of the chamber;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 29:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 29:15–19; NEHEMIAH 12:45"> and I commanded, and they cleansed the chambers; and I brought again there the vessels of God’s house, and the sacrifice thereof, and the incense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:37; MALACHI 3:8; MALACHI 1:6–14"> And I knew that the parts of the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93; were not given to them, and that each man of the deacons and of the singers, and of them that ministered in the temple, had gone away into his country;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:39; NEHEMIAH 13:17; NEHEMIAH 13:25"> and I did the cause against magi-strates, and I said, Why have ye forsaken the house of God? And I gathered them together, that is, deacons or Levites and the ministers, that had gone away, and I made them to stand in their standings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:37–39; MALACHI 3:10; NEHEMIAH 12:44"> And all Judah brought the tithe of wheat, of wine, and of oil, into the barns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND KI 22:7; NEHEMIAH 7:2; NEHEMIAH 12:44"> And we ordained on the barns, Shelemiah, the priest, and Zadok, the writer, and Pedaiah, one of the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, and besides them we ordained Hanan, the son of Zaccur, the son of Mattaniah; for they were proved faithful men, and the parts of their brethren were betaken to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 122:6–9; NEHEMIAH 13:22; NEHEMIAH 13:31"> My God, have mind of me for this thing, and do thou not away my merciful doings, which I have done in the house of my God, and in his ceremonies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:31; NEHEMIAH 13:21; EXODUS 34:21"> In those days I saw in Judah men treading pressers in the sabbath, men bringing in heaps, and charging or loading on asses wine, and grapes, and figs, and all burdens, and bringing into Jerusalem in the day of sabbath; and I witnessed to them, that they should sell in the day in which it was leaveful to sell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 23:12; DEUTERONOMY 5:14"> And men of Tyre dwelled in it, and brought in fishes, and all things set to sale, and they sold in the sabbaths to the sons of Judah, and of Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:11; NEHEMIAH 13:25; ISAIAH 1:10"> And I rebuked the principal men of Judah, and I said to them, What is this evil thing that ye do, and make unholy the day of the sabbath?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:21–23; JEREMIAH 44:9; NUMBERS 32:14"> Whether our fathers did not these things, and our God brought on us all this evil, and on this city? and ye increase wrathfulness on Israel, in defouling the sabbath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:32; EXODUS 31:14–17; JEREMIAH 17:19–22"> And it was done, when the gates of Jerusalem had rested in the day of sabbath, I said, Shut ye the gates; and they shutted the gates; and I com-manded, that they should not open the gates till after the sabbath. And of my children, or servants, I ordained numbers on the gates to keep shut, that no man should bring in a burden in the day of sabbath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And merchants, and men selling all things set to sale, dwelled without Jerusalem once or twice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 13:3–4; 1ST PETER 2:14; EZRA 7:26"> And I areasoned with them, and I said to them, Why dwell ye there even against, or on the other side of, the wall? If ye do this thing the second time, I shall set hand on you. Therefore from that time forth they came not thither in the sabbath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:30; NEHEMIAH 13:14; NEHEMIAH 13:31"> Also I said to the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, that they should be cleansed, and that they should come to keep the gates, and to hallow the day of sabbath. And therefore for this thing, my God, have mind of me, and spare me by the muchliness of thy merciful doings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZRA 9:1–2; EZRA 10:10; NEHEMIAH 10:30"> But also in those days I saw Jews wedding wives, women of Ashdod, and women of Ammonites, and women of Moabites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:9"> And their children spake half part by the speech of Ashdod, and could not speak by the speech of Jews, and they spake by the language of this people, and of that people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:3; NEHEMIAH 13:17; NEHEMIAH 10:29–30"> And I rebuked them, and cursed them; and I beat the men of them, and I made them bald, and I made them swear by the Lord, that they should not give their daughters to the sons of those aliens, and that they should not take of the daughters of those aliens to their sons, and to themselves;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:1–8; 1ST KINGS 3:13; ECCLESIASTES 7:26"> and I said, Whether Solomon, the king of Israel, sinned not in such things? And certainly in many folks there was no king like him, and he was loved of his God, and God setted &#91;or set&#93; him king upon all Israel, and alien women brought him to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZRA 10:2; 1ST SAMUEL 30:24"> Whether also we unobedient, should do all this great evil, that we trespass against our Lord God, and wed alien wives?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 2:19; NEHEMIAH 3:1; NEHEMIAH 12:22"> Forsooth Sanballat the Horonite had wedded a daughter of the sons of Joiada, son of Eliashib, the great priest, which Sanballat I drove away from me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 6:14; NUMBERS 25:12–13; MALACHI 2:4–8"> My Lord God, have mind against them, that defoul priesthood, and the right of priests, and of deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:30; NEHEMIAH 12:1–26; 1ST CHRONICLES 23:1–26"> Therefore I cleansed them from all aliens, and I ordained the orders of priests and of deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, each man in his service,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:14; NEHEMIAH 13:22; PSALMS 106:4"> and in offering, that is, dressing, of trees, or of wood, to burn with offered sacrifices, in times ordained, and in the first fruits. My God, have mind of me into good.</span>
</div>
