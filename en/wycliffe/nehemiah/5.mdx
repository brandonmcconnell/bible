<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:7; EXODUS 22:25–27; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:6–8"> And great cry of the people and of their wives was made against their brethren Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MALACHI 2:2; PSALMS 128:2–4; PSALMS 127:3–5"> And there were that said, Our sons and our daughters be full many; take we wheat for the price of them, and eat we, and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:35–39; MALACHI 3:8–11; GENESIS 47:15–25"> Also there were that said, Set we forth to sale our fields, and vineries &#91;or vines&#93;, and our houses, and take we wheat to eat in this hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZRA 4:13; EZRA 4:20; DEUTERONOMY 28:47–48"> And other men said, Take we money by borrowing, for usury, into the tributes of the king, and give our fields and vineries &#91;or vines&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND KI 4:1; LEVITICUS 25:39–43; ISAIAH 58:7"> And now as the fleshes of our brethren be, so and our fleshes be; and as be the sons of them, so and our sons be; lo! we have made subject our sons and our daughters into servage, and servantesses be of our daughters, and we have not whereof they may be again-bought; and other men have in possession our fields, and our vineries &#91;or vines&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 11:8; NEHEMIAH 13:25; EPHESIANS 4:26"> And I was full wroth, when I had heard the cry of them by these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20; LEVITICUS 25:36; EXODUS 22:25"> And mine heart thought within me, and I blamed the principal men, and magistrates; and I said to them, Ask ye not usuries, or increase, each man of your brethren. And I gathered together a great company against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:47–49; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:11; MATTHEW 25:29"> and I said to them, As ye know, we have by our power again-bought our brethren Jews, that were sold to heathen men; and ye now sell your brethren, and shall we again-buy them of you? And they held silence, and found not what they should answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:36; 2ND SAMUEL 12:14; ROMANS 2:24"> And I said to them, It is not good thing, that ye do; why go ye not in the dread of our God, and reproof be not said to us of heathen men, our enemies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:1; EXODUS 22:25–27; EZEKIEL 18:13"> Both I, and my brethren, and my servants, have lent to full many men money and wheat; in common, ask we not for this again; but forgive we alien money, or usury, which is due to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:6; LEVITICUS 6:4–5; LUKE 3:8"> And again yield ye to them today their fields, and their vineries &#91;or vines&#93;, their olive places, and their houses; and rather, give ye for them the hundred part of money, and of wheat, of wine, and of oil, which ye were wont to take of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZRA 10:5; JEREMIAH 34:8–10; MATTHEW 19:21–22"> And they said, We shall yield their goods to them, and we shall ask nothing of them; and we shall do so as thou speakest. And I called the priests, and I made them to swear, that they should do after this, that I had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:6; 1ST CHRONICLES 16:36; MATTHEW 10:14"> And furthermore I shook my bosom, and I said, So God shake away each man from his house, and from his travails, that fulfilleth not this word; and be he shaken away, and be he made void. And all the multitude said, Amen; and they praised God. And the people did, as it was said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:6; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:8–9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:4–15"> Forsooth from that day in which the king had commanded to me, that I should be duke in the land of Judah, from the twentieth year unto the two and thirtieth year of Artaxerxes king, that is, by twelve years, I and my brethren ate not the sustenances which were due to dukes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 5:9; ISAIAH 50:10; PROVERBS 16:6"> But the first dukes, that were before me, grieved the people, and took of them in bread, and wine, and money, each day forty shekels; and also their ministers oppressed the people. But I did not so, for the dread of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:9; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:5–6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:58"> but rather I subjected myself, and I builded in the work of the wall, and I bought no field, and all my servants were gathered together with me to the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:19; 1ST PETER 4:9–10; 2ND SAMUEL 9:7"> Also &#91;the&#93; Jews and the magistrates of them, an hundred and fifty men, and they that came to me from heathen men, that be in our compass, were eating in my table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST KINGS 4:22–23; PSALMS 37:21; PSALMS 37:26"> Forsooth by each day one ox was made ready to me, six chosen wethers, besides volatiles, and within ten days diverse wines; and I gave many other things; furthermore and I asked not for the sustenances of my duchy; for the people was made full poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:31; PSALMS 106:4; NEHEMIAH 13:14"> My God, have thou mind on me into good, by all things which I have done to this people.</span>
