<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 11:1; DANIEL 1:21; DANIEL 5:31–1"> In the first year of Darius, the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of Medes, that was emperor on the realm of Chaldees,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:10; JEREMIAH 25:11–12; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:21"> in the first year of his realm, I, Daniel, understood in books the number of years, of which number the word of the Lord was made to Jeremy, the prophet, that seventy years of desolation of Jerusalem should be &#91;ful&#93; filled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:3; JEREMIAH 29:10–13; JAMES 5:16–18"> And I setted my face to my Lord God, to pray and beseech in fastings, in sackcloth, and ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 8:28; EXODUS 20:6; DEUTERONOMY 5:10"> And I prayed my Lord God, and I acknowledged, and said, I beseech, thou Lord God, great and fearedful, keeping covenant and mercy to them that love thee, and keep thy command-ments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MALACHI 3:7; DANIEL 9:11; DANIEL 9:15"> We have sinned, we have done wickedness, we did unfaithfully, and went away, and bowed away from thy commandments and dooms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 9:10; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:15–16; LUKE 20:10–12"> We obeyed not to thy servants, prophets, that spake in thy name to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, and to all the people of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 44:15; JEREMIAH 3:25; JEREMIAH 2:26–27"> Lord, rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; is to thee, forsooth shame of face is to us, as is today to a man of Judah, and to the dwellers of Jerusalem, and to all Israel, to these men that be nigh, and to these men that be afar in all lands, to which thou castedest them out for the wickednesses of them, in which, Lord, they sinned against thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 9:6–7; LAMENTATIONS 1:7–8; LAMENTATIONS 1:18"> Shame of face is to us, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, that sinned;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:6–8; DANIEL 9:7; DANIEL 9:5"> but mercy and benignity is to thee, our Lord God. For we went away from thee,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 9:6; 2ND KI 18:12; NEHEMIAH 9:13–17"> and heard not the voice of our Lord God, that we should go in the law of him, which he setted to us by his servants, prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:5–10; ISAIAH 1:4–6; DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68"> And all Israel brake thy law, and bowed away, that they heard not thy voice; and cursing and loathing, which is written in the book of Moses, the servant of God, dropped on us, for we sinned to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:9; LAMENTATIONS 1:12; JOB 12:17"> And he ordained his words, which he spake on us, and on our princes, that deemed us, that they should bring in on us great evil, what manner evil was never under all heaven, by that that is done in Jerusalem,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68; ISAIAH 9:13; DANIEL 9:11"> as it is written in the law of Moses. All this evil came &#91;up&#93; on us, and, our Lord God, we prayed not thy face, that we should turn again from our wickednesses, and should think thy truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 9:7; NEHEMIAH 9:33; PSALMS 51:14"> And the Lord waked on &#91;his&#93; malice, and brought it &#91;up&#93; on us; our Lord God is just in all his works which he made, for we heard not his voice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:10; EXODUS 32:11; NEHEMIAH 1:10"> And now, our Lord God, that leddest thy people out of the land of Egypt in strong hand, and madest to thee a name by this day, we have sinned, we have done wickedness,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:3; JOEL 3:17; PSALMS 87:1–3"> Lord, against all thy rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;. I beseech, thy wrath and thy strong vengeance be turned away from thy city Jerusalem, and from thy holy hill; for why for our sins, and for the wickednesses of our fathers, Jerusalem and thy people be in shame, to all men by our compass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 80:19; LAMENTATIONS 5:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:20"> But now, our God, hear thou the prayer of thy servant, and the beseechings of him, and show thy face on thy saintuary, which is forsaken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:17; JEREMIAH 36:7; 2ND KI 19:16"> My God, for thyself bow down thine ear, and hear; open thine eyes, and see our desolation, and the city, on which thy name is called to help. For not in our justifyings we set forth meekly prayers before thy face, but in thy many merciful doings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 9:18; EPHESIANS 1:12; AMOS 7:2"> Lord, hear thou; Lord, be thou pleased, perceive thou, and do; my Lord God, tarry thou not, for thyself, for thy name is called to help on the city, and on thy people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 145:18; DANIEL 9:3–4; ROMANS 3:23"> And when I spake yet, and prayed, and acknowledged my sins, and the sins of my people Israel, that I should set forth meekly my prayers in the sight of my God, for the holy hill of my God,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 1:19; DANIEL 8:16; 1ST KINGS 18:36"> the while I spake yet in my prayer, lo! the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in vision at the beginning, flew soon, and touched me in the time of eventide sacrifice;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 9:24–27; DANIEL 10:21; DANIEL 8:16"> and he taught me, and he spake to me, and said, Daniel, now I went out, that I should teach thee, and thou shouldest understand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 10:19; MATTHEW 24:15; DANIEL 10:11–12"> From the beginning of thy prayers a word went out. Forsooth I came to show to thee, for thou art a man of desires; therefore perceive thou the word, and understand thou the vision.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:11–14; ROMANS 5:10; JEREMIAH 23:5–6"> Seventy weeks of years be abridged on thy people, and on thine holy city, that trespassing be ended, and sin take an end, and that wickedness be done away, and everlasting right-fulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; be brought, and that the vision and prophecy be &#91;ful&#93; filled, and the Holy Place of saints be anointed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZRA 4:24; ISAIAH 9:6; ISAIAH 55:4"> Therefore know thou, and perceive; from the going out of the word that Jerusalem be builded again, till to Christ, the duke, shall be seven weeks of years and two and sixty weeks of years; and again the street shall be builded, and walls, in the anguish of times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:8; NAHUM 1:8; MARK 13:2"> And after two and sixty weeks of years Christ shall be slain. And it shall not be his people, that shall deny him. And the people with the duke to coming shall destroy the city, and the saintuary; and the end thereof shall be destroying, and after the end of battle shall be ordained desolation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:15; LUKE 21:20; DANIEL 12:11"> Forsooth one week of years shall confirm the covenant to many men, and the offering and sacrifice shall fail in the midst of the week of years; and abomination of desolation shall be in the temple, and the desolation shall continue till to the performing and end.</span>
</div>
