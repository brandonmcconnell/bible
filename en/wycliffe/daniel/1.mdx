<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:5–7; 2ND KI 24:1–2; 2ND KI 24:13"> In the third year of the realm of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, Nebuchad-nezzar, and the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem, and besieged it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:7; ZECHARIAH 5:11; JEREMIAH 27:19–20"> And the Lord betook in his hand Jehoiakim, the king of Judah, and he took a part of the vessels of the house of God; and he bare out those into the land of Shinar, into the house of his god, and he took the vessels into the house of treasure of his god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 39:7; 2ND KI 20:17–18; 2ND KI 24:15"> And the king said to Ashpenaz, sovereign of his honest servants and chaste &#91;or provost of geldings&#93;, that he should bring in of the sons of Israel, and of the king’s seed, and the children of tyrants, &#91;or strong men&#93;,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 5:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:22; PROVERBS 22:29"> in which were no wem, fair in shape, and learned in all wisdom, wary in knowing, and taught in chastising, either learning, and that might stand in the palace of the king, that he should teach them the letters and language of Chaldees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 1:19; 1ST KINGS 10:8; DANIEL 1:8"> And the king ordained to them lifelode by each day of his meats, and of the wine whereof he drank; that they nourished by three years, should stand afterward before the sight of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:14; MATTHEW 24:15; DANIEL 2:17"> Therefore Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, of the sons of Judah, were among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 5:12; DANIEL 4:8; DANIEL 2:49"> And the sovereign of the honest servants and chaste putted to them names; to Daniel he putted Belte-shazzar; to Hananiah, Shadrach; to Mishael, Meshach, and to Azariah, Abednego.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:4; EZEKIEL 4:13–14; LEVITICUS 11:45–47"> Forsooth Daniel purposed in his heart, that he should not be defouled of the board of the king, neither of the wine of his drink; and he prayed the sovereign of the honest servants and chaste, that he should not be defouled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 39:21; PROVERBS 16:7; PSALMS 106:46"> Forsooth God gave grace and mercy to Daniel, in the sight of the prince of honest servants and chaste.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:16–18; JOHN 12:42–43; PROVERBS 29:25"> And the prince of honest servants and chaste said to Daniel, I dread my lord the king, that ordained to you meat and drink; and if he see your faces leaner than other young waxing men, your even-elders, ye shall condemn mine head to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Daniel said to Melzar, whom the prince of honest servants and chaste had ordained on Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 14:2; DANIEL 1:16; GENESIS 1:29–30"> I beseech, assay thou us thy servants by ten days, and pottages be given to us to eat, and water to drink;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and behold thou our cheers, and the cheers of children that eat the king’s meat; and as thou seest, so do thou with thy servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when he heard such a word, he assayed them ten days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 23:25; PROVERBS 10:22; 2ND KI 4:42–44"> Forsooth after ten days the cheers of them appeared better and fatter, than all the children that ate the king’s meat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 1:12"> Certainly Melzar took &#91;away&#93; the meats, and the wine of the drink of them, and gave them pottages.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:12; JAMES 1:5; DANIEL 2:21"> Forsooth to these children God gave knowing and learning in each book, and in all wisdom; but to Daniel God gave understanding of all visions and dreams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 1:5"> Therefore when the days were &#91;ful&#93; filled, after which the king said, that they should be brought in, the sovereign of honest servants and chaste brought in them, in the sight of Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:29; GENESIS 41:46; DANIEL 1:5"> And when the king had spoken to them, such were not found of all, as Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; and they stood in the sight of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 8:19; ISAIAH 47:12–14; 1ST KINGS 4:29–34"> And each word of wisdom and of understanding, which the king asked of them, he found in them tenfold, over all false diviners and astronomers or astrologers that were in all his realm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 6:28; DANIEL 10:1"> Forsooth Daniel was there till to the first year of King Cyrus.</span>
</div>
</div>
