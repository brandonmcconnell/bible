<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth in the tother day Holofernes commanded his hosts to go up against Bethulia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And there were with them sixscore thousand of footmen fighters, and twelve thousand knights &#91;or horsemen&#93;, without &#91;or besides&#93; those men, that were there ready, which the captivity had occupied, and were brought from provinces and cities, of all youth, or of able fighters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And all there together made them ready to battle against the sons of Israel; and they came by the side of an hill unto the cop or the height thereof, that beholdeth Dothan, from the place which is said Balbaim unto Cyamon, which is against Esdraelon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the sons of Israel, as soon as they saw the multitude of them, they bowed down themselves upon the earth, and threw &#91;or putting&#93; ashes upon their heads, and they prayed with one will, that God of Israel should show his mercy upon his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they took their armours &#91;or arms&#93; of battle, and they sat by the places by which the path of the straightway betwixt the hilly places is dressed, and they kept those places all the day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Certainly Holofernes, the while he went about by compass, found that the well, that flowed into the water conduit of them, was dressed at the south part without the city, and he commanded their water conduit to be cut asunder &#91;or to be hewn down&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Nevertheless wells were not far from the walls of the city, of which wells men of Israel were seen to draw water by stealth, or privily, rather to refresh them than to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But the sons of Ammon and of Moab nighed to Holofernes, and they said to him, The sons of Israel trust not in spear and arrow, but hills &#91;or the mountains&#93; defend them, and little hills set in the rock of stone make them strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore that thou mayest over-come them without assailing of battle, set thou &#91;or put&#93; keepers of their wells, that they draw not water of those &#91;or them&#93;; and thou shalt slay them without sword, either certainly when they be made faint &#91;or wearied&#93;for default of water, they shall betake to thee their city, the which city, for it is set in the hills, they guess it may not be overcome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And these words pleased before Holofernes, and before all his knights; and he ordained by compass by each well an hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And when by twenty days the keeping of the wells was fulfilled, the cisterns and the gatherings of waters failed to all the men of Israel dwelling in Bethulia &#91;or to all the dwellers in Bethulia&#93;, so that there was not of water within the city, whereof they should be fulfilled, namely one day, for the water was given by measure to the people each day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then all men and women, young men and eld, and little children, were gathered together to Uzziah, the chief priest, and all they said together with one voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The Lord deem betwixt us and thee, for thou, not willing to speak peaceably with the men of Assyria, hast done evil things against us, and for this thing God hath sold us into the hands of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And therefore there is none that helpeth us, when we be cast down in thirst, and in great loss before their eyes. &#91;And therefore there is not that helpeth, when we be thrown down before the eyes of them in thirst, and in great perdition.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And now therefore gather ye together all men, that be in this city, that all we peoples betake us-selves by free will to Holofernes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It is better that we be made prisoners &#91;or captive&#93;, and bless God and live, then that we die thus, and we be shame &#91;or reproof&#93; to each man, and since we see our wives and our young children die for default, before our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> We call into witnessing &#91;or to witness&#93; today heaven and earth, and the God of our fathers, that punisheth us after our sins, that now ye betake this city into the hands of the chivalry of Holofernes, and that our end be made short in the sharpness of sword, which end is now made longer in the dryness &#91;or the drought&#93; of thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when they had said these things, great weeping and yelling was made of all men in the great church, and by many hours they cried with one voice to the Lord, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> We and our fathers have sinned, we have done unjustly &#91;or unright-wisely&#93;, and we have done wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Thou, Lord, for thou art merciful, have mercy on us, and avenge our wickednesses with thy scourge; and, Lord, do not thou betake men acknowledging thee to a people that knoweth not thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> that they say not among heathen men &#91;or Gentiles&#93;, Where is the God of them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when they were made faint with these cries, and were made weary with these weepings, and were still, Uzziah rose up, all beshed with tears, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Brethren, be ye patient, and by these five days abide we the mercy of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> for in hap he shall cut &#91;or put&#93; away his indignation, and he shall give glory to his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Soothly if when these five days be passed, help cometh not, we shall do these words which ye have spoken.</span>
