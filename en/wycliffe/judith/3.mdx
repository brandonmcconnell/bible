<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the kings and princes of all cities and provinces, that is, of Syria, of Mesopotamia, and Coelesyria, and of Lebanon, and of Cilicia, sent their messengers &#91;or their legates&#93;. And when they came to Holofernes, they said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Thine indignation cease about us &#91;or Cease thine indignation about us&#93;; for it is better, that we live and serve Nebuchadnezzar, the great king, and to be subject to thee, than that we die, and suffer with our perishing the harms of our servage, or our thrall-dom, that is, suffer more harms then is servage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Each city of ours &#91;or All our cities&#93;, and all our possessions, all our mountains, and little hills, and our fields, and droves of oxen, and flocks of sheep, and of goats, and of horses, and of camels, and all our riches and our meines be in thy sight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> all things be under thy law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Also we and our children be thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Come thou and be thou a peaceable lord to us, and use thou our service, as it pleaseth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Holofernes came down from the hills, with knights &#91;or horse-men&#93; in great strength, and he got each city, and each man inhabiting the land or each man that dwelled in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And of all the cities he took to him helpers, strong men and chosen to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And so great dread of Holofernes lay &#91;or fell&#93; upon all provinces, that the dwellers of all cities, and the princes and men of honour, went together out with their peoples to meet him coming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and received him with crowns and lamps, and they led dances before him with pipes and tympans &#91;or trumps and timbrels&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Nevertheless they doing these things might not assuage the fierce-ness of his heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> for why both he destroyed their cities, and hewed down their &#91;maumet&#93; woods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For king Nebuchadnezzar had commanded to him, that he should destroy all the gods of earth &#91;or of the land&#93;, that is, that he alone should be said god of all these nations, that might be made subject/s by the power of Holofernes &#91;or might be under-yoked by the might of Holofernes&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Holofernes passed over all Coelesyria, and all the country of Appanie, and all Mesopotamia, and he came to Idumeans in the land of Geba;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and he took the cities of them, and dwelled there by thirty days, in which days he commanded all the host of his power to be gathered together.</span>
