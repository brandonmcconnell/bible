</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ESTHER 5:8; PROVERBS 29:11; ESTHER 3:15"> And she answered, If it pleaseth the king, I beseech, that thou come to me today, and Haman with thee, to the feast, that I have made ready.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ESTHER 6:14"> And anon the king said, Call ye anon Haman, that he obey to the will of Esther. And then the king and Haman came to the feast, which the queen had made ready to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ESTHER 7:2; ESTHER 5:3; ESTHER 9:12"> And the king said to Esther, after that he had drunk wine plenteously, What thing askest thou of me, that it be given to thee, and for what thing askest thou? Yea, though thou ask the half part of my realm, thou shalt get it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> To whom Esther answered, My asking and &#91;my&#93; prayers be these.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ESTHER 7:3; PROVERBS 16:9; ESTHER 8:5"> If I have found grace in the sight of the king, and if it pleaseth the king, that he give to me that thing, that I ask, and that he fulfill mine asking, the king and Haman come they tomorrow to the feast, that I have made ready to them; and tomorrow, I shall open my will to the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ESTHER 3:5; MATTHEW 2:16; PSALMS 27:3"> Therefore Haman went out glad and swift in that day. And when he had seen Mordecai sitting before the gates of the palace, and not only to have not risen up to him, but soothly neither moved from the place of his sitting, he was full wroth;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ESTHER 6:13; ECCLESIASTES 7:9; 2ND SAMUEL 13:22–23"> and Haman feigned him as not wroth therefore, and turned again in to his house, and he called together his friends, and Zeresh, his wife;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ESTHER 9:7–10; ESTHER 3:1; LUKE 12:19–20"> and he told to them the greatness of his riches, and the company of his children, and with how great glory the king had enhanced him above all his princes and servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:1; PSALMS 37:35–36; JOB 20:5–8"> And he said after these things, Also the queen Esther called none other man with the king to the feast, except me, with whom I shall eat also tomorrow with the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 15:20; JOB 18:4; PHILIPPIANS 4:11–12"> And though I have all these things, I guess that I have nothing, as long as I see Mordecai, the Jew, sitting before the king’s gates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

And the counsel of them pleased him, and he commanded an high cross, that is a gibbet, to be made ready.</span>
