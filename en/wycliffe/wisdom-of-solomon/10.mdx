<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> This wisdom of God kept him, that was formed first of God, the father of the world, when he alone was made of nought. And this wisdom led him out of his trespass, and led &#91;or brought&#93; him out of the slime of the earth,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and gave to him virtue to hold together all things, that is, gave to him the lordship over all lower things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> As the unjust man in his ire went away from this wisdom, brotherhood perished by the ire of man-quelling. &#91;From this as the unrightwise went away in his wrath, by the wrath of manslaughter perished fraternity.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For which thing when the water did away the earth, wisdom healed again; governing a just &#91;or the rightwise&#93; man by a despisable tree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> This wisdom also in the consent of pride, when nations had raised themselves, knew a just man &#91;or knew the rightwise&#93;, and kept without complaint &#91;or blame&#93; to God; and this wisdom kept strong mercy in sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And it delivered a just man &#91;or the rightwise&#93; fleeing from wicked &#91;or unpious&#93; men perishing, when fire came down into the place of five cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For which wicked men the land smoking is made desert, into witnessing of waywardness &#91;or shrewdness&#93;, and trees having fruits in uncertain time; and the mind of an unbelieveful soul standing an image of salt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For why men passing wisdom, not only fell in this, that they knew not goods &#91;or good things&#93;, but also they left to men the mind of their unwisdom, that in these sins, which they did, they might not be hid.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Forsooth wisdom delivered them from sorrows, that keep it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Soothly this wisdom led forth a just man &#91;or rightwise&#93; by rightful &#91;or right&#93; ways, that fled from the ire &#91;or the wrath&#93; of his brother; and it showed him the realm &#91;or the kingdom&#93; of God, and gave to him the knowing of saints; it made him honest in travails, and fulfilled his travails.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> It helped him in the fraud &#91;or guile&#93; of deceivers, and made him honest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> It kept him from enemies, and defended him from deceivers; and it gave to him a strong battle, that he should overcome, and know, that wisdom is the mightiest of all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> This wisdom forsook not the just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man sold, that is Joseph, when his brothers sold him to men of Ishmael, but delivered him from sinners; and it went down with him into a ditch, that is, to the prison of the king of Egypt;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and it forsook not him in bonds, till it brought to him the sceptre of the realm, and power against them that oppressed him; and it showed them liars, that defouled him, and it gave to him everlasting clearness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> This wisdom delivered a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; people, and wholly without complaint &#91;or blame&#93;, from nations that oppressed it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> It entered into the soul of God’s servant, and he stood against hideous kings, in great wonders and miracles &#91;or signs&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it yielded to just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men the meed of their travails, and led them forth in a wonderful &#91;or a marvellous&#93; way; and it was to them in covering of the day, and in the light of stars by night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And it translated, either led over, them through the Red or the Reed Sea; and bare them over through full much water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But it drowned down the enemies of them into the sea; and led them out from the depth of hells &#91;or the deepness of hell&#93;, that is, from the bottom of the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore just men take away the spoils of wicked men &#91;or rightwise men took away the spoils of unpious men&#93;; and, Lord, they magnified in song thine holy name, and praised together thine hand overcomer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For why wisdom opened the mouth of dumb men, and made the tongues of young children not speaking to be wise &#91;or fair speaking&#93;.</span>
</div>
</div>
