<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth ire &#91;or wrath&#93; without mercy came on wicked men &#91;or the unpious&#93; till into the last, that is, till to the drowning of them; for why God before-knew also the things to coming &#91;or to come&#93; of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For when they were turned, and had suffered, that they should lead out them, and had before-sent them with great busyness, the deeds of repenting pursued them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For they having yet mourning betwixt the hands, and they be-weeping at the sepulchres of dead men &#91;or weeping at the monuments of the dead&#93;, took to them another thought of unknowing; and they pursued those Hebrews, as fleers away, which they praying had sent forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For why worthy need &#91;or necessity&#93; led them to this end, and they lost remembering of these things, that had befallen, that punishing should &#91;ful&#93; fill those things, that failed &#91;or lacked&#93; of torments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and that soothly thy people should pass wonderfully; forsooth that they should find a new death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For why each creature serving to thine behests, was reformed to his kind at &#91;or from&#93; the beginning, that thy children should be kept unhurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For why a cloud beshadowed the castles &#91;or the tents&#93; of them, and dry earth appeared in water that was before, and a way without hindering appeared in the Reed Sea, and a field burgeoning from full great depth; &#91;For a cloud shadowed the tents of them, and in the water that was before, the earth appeared dry; and in the Red Sea way without hindrance, and a burgeoning field of full great depth;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> by which field all the nation passed, that was covered with thine hand; forsooth they saw thy marvels and wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For they as horses devoured &#91;or gnawed&#93; meat, that is, took the armours of Egyptians, which they saw dead on the brink of the sea, and as lambs they made full out joy, magnifying thee, Lord, that &#91;or for thou&#93; deliveredest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For they were mindful yet of those things, that were done in the dwelling of them among Egyptians; how the land brought forth flies, for the nation of beasts, and the flood brought forth multitude of paddocks &#91;or frogs&#93; for fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Forsooth at the last they saw a new creature of birds, when they were led by covetousness &#91;or lust&#93;, and asked meats of feast &#91;or of delicious eating&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For in the speaking to, that is, at the asking of them, of their desire, a curlew ascended &#91;or went up&#93; to them from the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and dis-eases &#91;or travails&#93; came on sinners, and not without provings &#91;or evidences&#93; of those things, that were done before by the fierceness &#91;or the force&#93; of floods. For they suffered justly, by &#91;or after&#93; their wickednesses; for they ordained more abominable unhospitality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Soothly some received not unknown comelings; soothly others took good men harboured into thralldom &#91;or good guests into servage&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And not only they did these things, but soothly also another beholding &#91;or respect&#93; of them was, that they against their will received strangers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Forsooth they that used the same ordinances, tormented with cruelest sorrows them, that received with gladness. &#91;Who forsooth with gladness received them, that had used the same informings, with most cruel sorrows they tormented.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Forsooth they were smitten with blindness, as they in the gates of the just man &#91;or the rightwise&#93;, when they were covered with sudden darknesses; each man sought the passing of his door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Forsooth while elements be turned into themselves, as the sound of manner &#91;or of quality&#93; is changed in organ, and all things keep their sound; wherefore it may be guessed of that certain sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Beasts of the field were turned into beasts of water; whatever were swimming things, went in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Fire in water had power above &#91;or strength over&#93; his virtue; and water forgot the kind quenching &#91;or his quenching kind&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Again-ward flames of corruptible beasts dis-eased not the fleshes of Hebrews going together; neither departed that good meat, that was departed lightly as ice. &#91;Flames again-ward travailed not the flesh of the corruptible beasts going together; nor dissolved it, that lightly was dissolved as ice, good meat.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Forsooth, Lord, thou magnifiedest thy people in all things, and honouredest; and despisedest not, and helpedest them in each time and in each place &#91;or in all place standing nigh to them&#93;.</span>
