<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> How fair is a chaste generation with clearness &#91;or clarity&#93;; for the mind thereof is undeadly, for it is known, both with God, and with men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> When it is present, they pursue &#91;or follow&#93; it; and they desire it, when it hath led out itself, and it over-coming getteth by victory the meed of battles undefouled, and is crowned without end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But the manyfold engendered multitude of wicked &#91;or unpious&#93; men shall not be profitable; and plantings of adultery they shall not give deep roots, neither shall set stable steadfastness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Though they burgeon in boughs &#91;or branches&#93; for a time, they set unsteadfastly shall be moved of the wind, and they shall be drawn out by the root of the greatness of winds &#91;or and of the muchliness of winds pulled up by the roots&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For why boughs &#91;or branches&#93; unperfect shall be broken altogether; and the fruits of them be unprofitable, and sour to eat, and covenable to nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For why all sons, that be born of wicked men, be witnesses of wicked-ness against fathers and mothers, in their asking.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But a just man &#91;or the rightwise&#93;, though he be before-occupied by death, shall be in refreshing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For why worshipful eld &#91;age&#93; is not of long time &#91;or long enduring&#93;, neither it is reckoned by the number of years;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> the wits of a man be hoary, and the age of eld is life without wem &#91;or undefouled&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He pleased God, and was made dearworthy &#91;or loved&#93;, and he living among sinners was translated, or borne over;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> he was ravished, lest malice should change his understanding, either lest feigning should deceive &#91;or beguile&#93; his soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For why deceiving of trifling maketh dark good things, and the unstableness of covetousness turneth over &#91;or over-turneth&#93; the wit without malice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He was ended in short time, and &#91;ful&#93; filled many times;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> for why his soul was pleasant to &#91;or pleased&#93; God; for this thing God hasted to lead him out from the midst of wickednesses;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> but peoples saw and understood not, neither setted &#91;or putted&#93; such things in their hearts or entrails. For the grace and mercy of God is on his saints, and beholding &#91;or respect&#93; of God is on his chosen men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Forsooth a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man dead condemneth quick &#91;or alive&#93; wicked men; and youth ended swiftlier condemneth the long life of an unjust man &#91;or the unrightwise&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For they shall see the end of a wise man, and they shall not understand, what thing God thought of him, and why the Lord made him less &#91;or diminished him&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For they shall see, and shall despise him; but the Lord shall scorn them. And after these things they shall be falling down without honour, and in despising among dead men without end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For he shall all-break them swollen without voice, and he shall move them from the foundaments; and they shall be desolate till to the last thing. And they shall be wailing, and the mind of them shall perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They shall come fearedful in the thought of their sins; and their wickednesses on the contrary side shall lead them over.</span>
</div>
</div>
