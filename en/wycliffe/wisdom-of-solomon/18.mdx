<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But full great light there was to thine holy servants&#91;or thy saints&#93;, and soothly enemies heard the voice of them, but they saw not the figure, either shape; and for also they suffered not by the same things, they magnified thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And for they were hurt before, they did thankings to thee, for they were not hurt; and that difference should be betwixt them and Egyptians, they asked thee, God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For which thing they had a burning pillar of fire, the leader of unknown way; and thou gavest the sun, without hurting of good harbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Forsooth they were worthy to want &#91;or to lack&#93; light, and to suffer the prison of darknesses, which held &#91;or kept&#93; thy sons enclosed; by which sons&#91;or by whom&#93; the uncorrupt light of law began to be given to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When they thought to slay the young children of just men &#91;or the infants of rightwise men&#93;; and when one son was put forth, and delivered, thou tookest away the multitude of sons, that is, of the first engendered of Egypt, for the leading-over of them, and thou lostest &#91;or spoiledest&#93; them altogether in strong water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Forsooth that night was known before of our fathers, that they witting verily to which oaths they believed, should be more patient &#91;or steadfast&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Forsooth health of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men was received verily of thy people, and also destroying of unjust &#91;or unrightwise&#93; men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For as thou hurtedest our adversaries, so thou excitedest also us, and magnifiedest us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For why just &#91;or the rightwise&#93; children of good men made sacrifice privily, and disposed the law of rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; into according; they disposed or assigned just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men to receive goods and evils in like manner, and sung &#91;or singing&#93; praisings to the Father of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But &#91;the&#93; unseemly voice of enemies sounded, and weepful wailing of beweepers of young children was heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Forsooth the servant was tormented by like pain with the lord; and a man of the people suffered things like the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore in like manner all men by &#91;or with&#93; one name of death had dead men unnumberable, for neither quick men sufficed to bury; for why the nation of them, that was clearer than others, was destroyed in one moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Forsooth of all Egyptians men not believing for benefices &#91;or benefits&#93;, promised them then to be God’s people, when the destroying of the first engendered &#91;or begotten&#93; things was first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsooth when all things held restful silence, and the night had the middle &#91;or the mean&#93; way in his course,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Lord, thy word almighty &#91;or thine almighty word&#93; coming &#91;or going out&#93; swiftly from heaven, came from the king’s seats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> a sharp sword bearing thy commandment not feigned, came forth, overcomer into the middle of the land of destroying &#91;or of death&#93;; and it stood, and filled all things with death, and it stood in earth, and stretched forth till to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then anon the sights of evil dreams disturbed them, and dreads not hoped came above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And another man cast forth half quick &#91;or half alive&#93; into another place, showed for what cause of death he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For why sights that disturbed them, before-warned these things, that they should perish not unwittingly &#91;or unknowingly&#93;, why they suffered evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Forsooth &#91;the&#93; temptation of death, that is, punishment by death, touched then also just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men, and moving altogether &#91;or a stirring&#93; of &#91;the&#93; multitude was made in desert &#91;or in wilderness&#93;; but thine ire &#91;or wrath&#93; dwelled not long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For a man without complaint hasted to beseech &#91;or to pray&#93; for peoples, and he brought forth prayer the shield of his service, and he alleged prayer by incense, and against-stood ire &#91;or withstood to the wrath&#93;; and he setted an end to the need, and showed that he was thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Forsooth he overcame companies, not by virtue of body, neither by armour of power; but he remembered the oaths, and the testaments of fathers, and by word he made him subject, that travailed himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For when dead men fell down by heaps, each on other, he stood betwixt &#91;or between&#93;dead men and living, or the dead and the quick, and cut away the fierceness of burning, and parted that way, that led to quick men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For why all the world, by signifying either figure, was in the cloth &#91;or the priest’s cape&#93; lasting to the heels, which he had; and the great things of fathers were engraved in four orders of stones; and, Lord, thy magnificence &#91;or great doing&#93; was written in the diadem of his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Forsooth he that destroyed, gave stead to these things, and dreaded these things, for why the temptation alone was sufficient to ire. &#91;To these forsooth he gave stead, that destroyed, and these things he full out dreaded; forsooth there was alone sufficient tempting of wrath.&#93;</span>
