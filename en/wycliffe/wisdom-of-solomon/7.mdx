</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and in the womb of my mother I was formed flesh. In the time of ten months I was curded together in blood, of the seed of man, and by according delight &#91;or covenable delighting&#93; of sleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I was born, and took common air, and in like manner I fell down into the earth made &#91;or fell down into the made earth&#93;; and I weeping sent out the first voice, like all men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I was nursed in wrappings &#91;or swaddling clothes&#93;, and in great busy-nesses;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> for why no man of kings had other beginning of birth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Therefore one entering to life is to all men, and like going out. &#91;One entry is to all to life, and like issue.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Wherefore I desired, and wit was given to me; and I inwardly called, and the spirit of wisdom came into me &#91;or there came to me the spirit of wisdom&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And I setted wisdom before realms, and seats; and I said, that riches be nought &#91;or nothing&#93; in comparison thereof,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and I comparisoned not a precious stone to it; for why all gold in comparison thereof is a little gravel, and silver shall be areckoned &#91;or esteemed&#93; as clay in the sight thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> I loved wisdom more than health and fairness; and I purposed to have it for light, for the light thereof may not be quenched &#91;or is unquenchable&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Forsooth all goods came together to me with it; and unnumberable honesty is by the works thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And I was glad in all things; for this wisdom went before me, and I knew not, for it is the mother of all goods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Which wisdom I learned without feigning, and I commune without envy; and I hide not the honesty thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For it is treasure without number to men, and they, that used that treasure, were made partners of God’s friendship, and were praised for the gifts of knowing &#91;or commended for the gifts of discipline&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Forsooth God gave to me to say of sentence, and to before take &#91;or to take before&#93; worthy things of these things that be given to me; for he is the leader of wisdom, and amender of wise men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For why both we, and our words, and all wisdom, and learning of knowing of works be in his hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Forsooth he gave to me the very knowing of these things that be, that I know the disposition of the world, and the virtues of elements;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the beginning, and the ending, and the middle of times; the changings of whiles, and the endings of times; the changings of manners, and partings &#91;or the divisions&#93; of times;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> the courses of the year, and the dispositions of stars;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> the kinds of beasts, and the wraths of wild beasts; the strength of winds, and the thoughts of men; the differences of trees, and the virtues of roots.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I learned whatever things be hid and unpurveyed;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> for why wisdom, the craftsmaker &#91;or craftsman&#93; of all things, taught me. For in that wisdom unmade is the spirit of understanding, holy, manyfold, one alone &#91;or only&#93;, subtle, temperate &#91;or mannerly&#93;, wise, moveable, undefouled, certain, sweet, loving a good deed, which spirit forbiddeth nothing to do well &#91;or that nothing prevented or hindered to do well&#93;;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> courteous, benign, stable, secure, having all virtue, beholding all things, and which taketh all spirits able to understand, he is clean, and subtle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For why wisdom is more moveable than all moveable things; forsooth it stretcheth forth everywhere &#91;or it attaineth over all&#93;, for his cleanness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For it is a breathing of God’s virtue, and it is some clean coming forth of the clearness of Almighty God; and therefore no defouled thing runneth into it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> For it is brightness of everlasting light, and it is a mirror without wem of God’s majesty, &#91;or a mirror without wem of the majesty of God&#93;, and it is an image of his goodness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And when it is one &#91;or And since it is one&#93;, it may all things; and it dwelleth in itself, and reneweth all things, and by nations it beareth over itself into holy souls; it maketh the friends of God, and prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For God loveth no man, but him that dwelleth with wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For why this wisdom is fairer than the sun, and is above all the disposition of stars; wisdom comparisoned to light, &#91;it&#93; is found the former.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For why night cometh after the light; but wisdom overcometh malice.</span>
</div>
