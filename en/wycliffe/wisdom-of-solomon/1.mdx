<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Ye that deem the earth, love rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;; feel ye of the Lord in goodness, and seek ye him in the simpleness of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For he is found of them, that tempt not him &#91;or that tempt him not&#93;; forsooth he appeareth to them, that have faith into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For why wayward thoughts part &#91;or sever&#93; from God; but proved virtue reproveth unwise men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For why wisdom shall not enter into an evil-willed soul; neither it shall dwell in a body subject to sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Forsooth the Holy Ghost of wisdom shall fly or flee away from a feigned man &#91;or a feigner&#93;, and he shall take away himself from thoughts, that be without understanding; and the unwise man shall be punished of wickedness coming above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the spirit of wisdom is benign, and he shall not deliver a cursed man from his lips; for why God is witness of his reins, and the searcher of his heart is true, and the hearer of his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For why the Spirit of the Lord hath filled the world; and this thing, that containeth all things, hath the knowing of voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore this he that speaketh wicked thing &#91;or For that he that speaketh wicked things&#93;, may not be hid; and doom and punishing shall not pass him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For why asking shall be in the thoughts of a wicked man &#91;or of the unpious&#93;. Forsooth the hearing of his words shall come to God, and to the punishing &#91;or the correction&#93; of his wickednesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> for the ear of fervent love &#91;or of the jealous&#93; heareth all things, and the noise of grutchings shall not be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore keep ye you from grutching, that profiteth nothing, and from backbiting spare ye the tongue; for a dark word shall not go into vain; forsooth the mouth that lieth, slayeth the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Do not ye covet death, in the error of your life, neither get ye perdition in the works of your hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> for God made not death, neither he is glad in the perdition of living men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For why God made of nought all things, that those should be; and he made the nations of the world able to be healed. For why medicine of destroying is not in those men, neither the realm of hells &#91;or of hell&#93; is in earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For rightfulness is everlasting, and undeadly; but unrightfulness is getting of death. &#91;Rightwiseness forsooth is perpetual, and undeadly; unrightwise-ness forsooth purchasing of death.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Forsooth wicked &#91;or unpious&#93; men called that unrightfulness by hands and words, and they guessed &#91;or esteemed&#93; it a friend, and floated away, and they putted promises to it; for they be worthy the death, that be of the part thereof.</span>
