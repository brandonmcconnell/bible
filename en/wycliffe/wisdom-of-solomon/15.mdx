<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For if we sin, we be thine, and know thy greatness; and if we sin not, we know, that we be acounted at thee &#91;or for with thee we be counted&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For why to know thee, is perfect rightfulness &#91;or full ended rightwise-ness&#93;; and to know thy rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and virtue, is the root of undeadliness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Forsooth the thinking out of evil craft of men brought not us into error, neither the shadow of painture &#91;or of painting&#93; travail without fruit, an image graven by diverse colours;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> whose beholding &#91;or sight&#93; giveth covetousness &#91;or coveting&#93; to an unwise man, and he loveth the like-ness of a dead image without soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lovers of evils be worthy the death, that have hope in such &#91;things&#93;; and they that make those, and they that love, and they that worship be worthy the death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But also a potter, thrusting &#91;the&#93; nesh earth, by great travail maketh each vessel to our uses; and of the same clay he maketh vessels that be clean to use, and in like manner those that be contrary to these; forsooth what use is of these vessels, the potter is judge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And he that was made of earth a little before, maketh a god of the same clay with vain travail; and the potter, asked to yield the debt of the soul which he had, leadeth himself after a little time to the earth, from whence he was taken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But he hath care, not for he shall travail, neither for his life is short, but he striveth with goldsmiths and silversmiths; but also he pursueth &#91;or followeth&#93; workers of brass, and setteth before glory; for he maketh superfluous &#91;or over-void&#93; things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the heart of him is ashes, and superfluous earth is his hope, and his life is viler &#91;or fouler&#93; than clay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For he knew not God, that made him, and that inspired a soul to him &#91;or that inspired to him a soul&#93;; and he loveth those things which he hath wrought; and he knew not God, that blowed &#91;or blew&#93; in him a spirit of life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But they guessed fleshly delighting &#91;or a playing place&#93; to be our life, and the conversation of life to be made to winning, either covetousness, and that it behooveth to get on each side, yea, of evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Forsooth this man that maketh frail vessels, and graven images of the matter of earth, knoweth that he trespasseth above all men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Forsooth, Lord, all unwise men and cursed be proud over the measure of their soul, and be enemies of thy people, and upbraid it &#91;or mis-saying to it&#93;;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> for they guessed all the idols of nations to be gods, that have neither sight of eyes to see, neither nostrils to perceive a spirit, either wind, neither ears to hear, neither fingers of hands to touch, but also their feet be slow to go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For why a man made those &#91;or made them&#93;, and he that borrowed a spirit, made those; for why no man may make a god like himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> For since he is deadly, by &#91;or with&#93; wicked hands he maketh a dead idol; for he is better than these gods, which he worshippeth; for soothly he lived, when he was deadly, but they lived never.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But also most wretched men worship beasts; for why unreasonable beasts, comparisoned to these men, be worse than they.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But neither by sight any man may of these beasts behold goods &#91;or good things&#93;; forsooth they have driven away &#91;or fled&#93; the praising of God, and his blessing.</span>
</div>
