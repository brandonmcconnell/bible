<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Forsooth the souls of just men be in the hand of God; and the torment of death shall not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> They seemed to the eyes of unwise men to die; and torment was deemed the out-going of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And from just way they went into destroying, and that that is of us the way of destroying &#91;or destruction&#93;; but they be in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Though they suffered torments before men, the hope of them is full of undeadliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> They were travailed in a few things, and they shall be disposed well in many things; for why God assayed them, and found them worthy to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He proved them as gold in a furnace, and he took them as the offering of burnt sacrifice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and the beholding of them shall be in time of yielding. Just men shall shine, and they shall run about as sparkles in a place of reeds. &#91;and in time shall be the beholding of them. They shall shine rightwise, and as sparkles in reedy places they shall run hither and thither.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They shall deem nations, and shall be lords of peoples; and the Lord of them shall reign without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They that trust on him, shall understand truth; and faithful men in love shall assent to him; for why gift and peace is to his chosen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But wicked men, by those things that they thought, shall have punishing; which despised just thing, and went away from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For he that casteth away wisdom and lore, is cursed &#91;or is unhappy&#93;; and the hope of wicked men is void, and their travails be without fruit, and their works be unhabitable, and unprofitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The women of them be unwitting &#91;or unwise&#93;, and the sons of them be full wayward &#91;or most wicked&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The creature of them is cursed; for why the woman barren and undefouled is blessed &#91;or happy is the barren, and the undefouled&#93;, that hath not known the bed in trespass; she shall have fruit in the beholding of holy souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And a man unmighty to engender or beget, &#91;or a gelding&#93;, is blessed, that hath not wrought wickedness by his hands, neither thought most wayward &#91;or wicked&#93; things against the Lord; for why a chosen &#91;free&#93; gift of faith shall be given to him, and a most acceptable &#91;or most allowed&#93; heritage in the temple of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For why the fruits of good travails is glorious, and the root of wisdom that falleth not down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But the sons of adulterers shall be in destroying, and the seed of a wicked bed shall be destroyed &#91;or outlawed&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And soothly though they shall be of long life, they shall be areckoned into nought; and the last eld age of them shall be without honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And if they be dead swiftlier &#91;or if swiftlier they shall be dead&#93;, they shall not have hope, neither allowing in the day of knowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Forsooth wicked nations be of hard ending.</span>
