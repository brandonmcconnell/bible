<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Therefore wisdom stretcheth forth from the end till to the end strongly, and disposeth all things sweetly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I loved this wisdom made&#91;or This I loved&#93;, and I sought it out from my youth; and I sought to take it a spousess to me, and I am made a lover of the fairness thereof &#91;or of the form of it&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He that hath the fellowship of God, glorifieth the gentleness thereof &#91;or It having the company of God, glorifieth the gentleness of it&#93;; but also the Lord of all things loved it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For it is the teacheress of the learning &#91;or of the discipline&#93; of God, and chooseress of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And if riches be coveted &#91;or be desired&#93; in life, what is richer than wisdom, that worketh all things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Soothly if wit worketh, who is a craftsmaker &#91;or craftsman&#93; more than wisdom, of these things that be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And if a man loveth rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, the travails of this wisdom have great virtues; for it teacheth soberness, and prudence, and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, and virtue; and nothing is profitabler &#91;or more profitable&#93; than these in life to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And if a man desireth multitude of knowing, wisdom knoweth things passed, and guesseth of things to coming &#91;or to come&#93;; it knoweth the fellnesses or falsenesses of words, and assoilings or absolvings of arguments; it knoweth signs and showings of things to coming, before that they be made &#91;or the tokens and wonders it knoweth, ere they be done&#93;; and the befallings &#91;or chances&#93; of times and of worlds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore I purposed to bring to me this wisdom&#91;or Then I purposed this to bring to me&#93;, to live together; witting that it shall commune with me of goods, and speaking together of my thoughts, and of mine annoyances, shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For this wisdom I shall have clearness at companies, and honour at elder men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I shall be found young and sharp in doom, and in the sight of mighty men I shall be wonderful, and the faces of princes shall worship me &#91;or shall marvel me&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They shall abide me, being still, and they shall behold me, speaking; and the while I speak many things, they shall set hands on their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Furthermore by this wisdom I shall have undeadliness; and I shall leave everlasting mind to them, that shall come after me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I shall dispose peoples; and nations shall be subject to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Hideous kings hearing me shall dread; and in multitude I shall be seen good, and strong in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I shall enter into mine house, and I shall rest with wisdom; for the conversation thereof hath no bitterness, and the dwelling together thereof hath none annoyance, but gladness and joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> I thought these things at me, and I remembered in mine heart; for why wisdom is undeadly in thought,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and good delighting is in the friendship thereof; and honesty with-out default is in the works of hands thereof; and wisdom is in the strife of speech thereof; and great clearness is in the communing of words thereof; I went about, seeking to take wisdom to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Forsooth I was a witting child, and I got a good soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And when I was more good, I came to a body undefouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And as I knew, that else I may not be chaste &#91;or continent&#93;, no but God give it, and this same thing was wisdom, to know whose this gift was; I went to the Lord, and I besought him, and I said, of all mine entrails &#91;or of all the entrails of mine heart&#93;.</span>
