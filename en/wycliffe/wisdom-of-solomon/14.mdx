<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Again another man thinking to sail in a ship &#91;or another thinking to sail&#93;, and beginning to make journey through fierce waves, inwardly calleth a tree more frail than the tree that beareth him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For why covetousness to get money &#91;or winning&#93; found that idol; and a craftsman made it by his wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But thou, Father, governest by purveyance, for thou gavest way in the sea &#91;or thou hast given in the sea way&#93;, and a most steadfast path among waves;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> showing that thou art mighty to make whole of &#91;or to heal&#93; all things, yea, if a man goeth to the sea without ship;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> but that the works of thy wisdom should not be void, for this thing men betake their lives, yea, to a little tree, and they pass the sea, and be delivered by a ship.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But at the beginning, when proud giants perished, the hope of the world fled to a ship, and sent again seed of birth to the world, which was governed by thine hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For why blessed is the tree, by which rightfulness was made &#91;or by the which is done rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But the idol which is made by hand is cursed, both it, and he that made it, for soothly he wrought great trespass &#91;or unbelievefulness&#93;; soothly that idol, when it was frail, was named God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Forsooth in like manner the wicked man and his wickedness, &#91;or the unpious and the unpiousness of him&#93;, be hateful to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For why that that is made shall suffer torments, with him that made it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For this thing and to the idols of nations shall not be beholding &#91;or reward&#93;; for the creatures of God be made into hatred, and into temptation to the soul of men, and into a trap &#91;or a mouse-catch&#93;, to the feet of unwise men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For the beginning of fornication, that is, idolatry, which is ghostly fornication, is the seeking out of idols, and the finding of those idols is the corruption of life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Forsooth those were not at the beginning, neither those shall be without end. &#91;Forsooth they were not from the beginning, nor shall be into without end.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For why the voidness of men found these idols into the world; and therefore the end of those is found shortly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For why the father making sorrow with bitter mourning, made soon to him an image of the son that was ravished; and began to worship him now as a god, that was dead then as a man; and he ordained holy things &#91;or temples&#93; and sacrifices among his servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Afterward in time coming betwixt, when the wicked custom was strong, this error was kept as a law, and images were worshipped by lordship of tyrants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The figure of them was brought from afar, which the men might not honour &#91;or worship&#93; in open, for they were &#91;a&#93; far; and they made an open image of the king, whom they would honour; that by their busyness they should worship him as present, that was absent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Forsooth the noble &#91;or the great&#93; diligence of a craftsman brought in also them, that knew not, to the worshipping of these things or of these kings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For he willing more to please the king, that took him, that is, chose him to make an image to the king, travailed perfectly by his craft, to make a likeness into better, that is, in making the king fairer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Soothly the multitude of men, deceived by the fairness of work, guessed &#91;or esteemed&#93; him now a god, that was honoured as a man before that time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And this was the deceit &#91;or the deceiving&#93; of man’s life; for why men serving greatly, either to affection, either to kings, putted to stones and trees the name that may not be communed &#91;or the uncommunicable name&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And it sufficed not, that they erred about the knowing of God; but also they living in great battle of unknowing, call so many and so great evils peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For either they slaying their sons in sacrifice, either making dark sacrifices, either having wakings &#91;or having watches&#93; full of madness,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> keep now neither clean life, neither clean weddings &#91;or spousals&#93;; but also one man slayeth another man by envy, either doing adultery maketh sorry his neighbor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And all things be meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; together, blood, manslaying &#91;or manslaughter&#93;, theft, and feigning, corruption, unfaithfulness, disturbing, and forswearing,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> noise, forgetting of goods of the Lord, defouling of souls, changing of birth, unsteadfastness of weddings, unordaining of lechery and of unchastity. &#91;strife, the unminding of the goods of the Lord, the defouling of souls, the mis-changing of birth, the unstableness of bridals, the unordaining of lechery and of uncleanness.&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For why the worshipping of cursed idols is the cause, and the beginning, and the end, of all evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For why either they wax mad, while they be glad; either certainly they prophesy false things, either they live unjustly, either they forswear soon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For the while they trust in idols, that be without soul, they swear evil, and hope not, that they shall be annoyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Therefore ever either shall come to them worthily; for they deemed evil of God, and gave attention to idols &#91;or taking heed to maumets&#93;, and they swore unjustly in an idol, and they despised rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For why an oath is not virtue, but the pain of sinners goeth forth ever-&#91;more&#93;, into the breaking of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; things.</span>
</div>
