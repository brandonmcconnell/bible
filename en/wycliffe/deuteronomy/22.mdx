<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 23:4–5; EZEKIEL 34:16; ISAIAH 58:7"> Thou shalt not see thy brother’s ox, either sheep, erring or wandering, and shalt pass thereby, but thou shalt bring it again to thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:12; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:6"> And if thy brother is not nigh, neither thou knowest him, thou shalt lead those beasts into thine house, and those &#91;or they&#93; shall be with thee, as long as thy brother seeketh them, and till he receive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In like manner thou shalt do of thy brother’s ass, and of his cloth, and of each thing of thy brother, that was lost; if thou findest it, be thou not negligent, as of an alien thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:1–2; HEBREWS 12:12–13; MATTHEW 5:44"> If thou seest that the ass, either the ox, of thy brother hath fallen in the way, thou shalt not despise, but thou shalt raise with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 11:4–15; DEUTERONOMY 18:12"> A woman shall not be clothed in a man’s clothes, neither a man shall use a woman’s clothes; for he that doeth these things is abominable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:28; PROVERBS 12:10; GENESIS 8:17"> If thou goest in the way, and findest a bird’s nest in a tree, either in the earth, and findest the mother sitting on the birds, either &#91;the&#93; eggs, thou shalt not hold the mother with the children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; PROVERBS 22:4"> but thou shalt suffer the mother&#91;to&#93; go, and shalt hold the sons taken, that it be well to thee, and that thou live in long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 11:2; ISAIAH 22:1; EXODUS 21:28–36"> When thou buildest a new house, thou shalt make a wall of the roof by compass, lest blood be shed out in thine house, and thou be guilty, if another man slideth, and falleth into a ditch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:3; MATTHEW 9:16"> Thou shalt not sow thy vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93; with another seed, lest both the seed which thou hast sown, and those things that come forth of the vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;, that is, the fruit of the vinery, be defouled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew word here signifieth both ‘defouling’ and ‘hallowing’.">a</a></sup>together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14–16"> Thou shalt not ear with an ox and an ass together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:19"> Thou shalt not be clothed in a cloth, which is woven together of wool and of flax.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:5; NUMBERS 15:37–41"> Thou shalt make little cords by four corners in the hems of thy mantle, with which thou art covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 29:21; GENESIS 29:31; EPHESIANS 5:28–29"> If a man weddeth a wife, and afterward hateth her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:19; PROVERBS 18:21; PROVERBS 18:8"> and seeketh occasions by which he may leave her, and he putteth against her the worst name, and saith, I have taken this wife, and I have entered to her, and I found not her a virgin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the father and mother of her shall take her, and they shall bear with them the tokens of her virginity to the elder men of the city, that be in the gate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and her father shall say, I gave my daughter wife to this man, and for he hateth her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> he putteth to her the worst name, that he saith, I found not thy daughter a virgin; and lo! these be the tokens of the virginity of my daughter; they shall spread forth a cloth before the elder men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21"> And the elder men of that city shall take the man, and shall beat him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:29; MATTHEW 19:8–9; DEUTERONOMY 24:1–4"> and furthermore they shall con-demn him in an hundred shekels of silver, which he shall give to the father of the damsel, for he defamed her by the worst name that may be upon a virgin of Israel; and he shall have her to wife, and he shall not be able to forsake her, in all his lifetime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> That if it is found sooth, that he putteth against her, and virginity is not found in the damsel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:5; GENESIS 34:7; JUDGES 20:6"> they shall cast her out of her father’s gates; and men of that city shall oppress her with stones, and she shall die, for she did &#91;an&#93; unleaveful thing in Israel, that she did lechery in her father’s house; and so thou shalt do away evil from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:10; EZEKIEL 23:45–47; JOHN 8:4–5"> If a man sleepeth with the wife of another man, ever either shall die, that is, the adulterer, and the adulteress; and thus thou shalt do away evil from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:18–19; DEUTERONOMY 20:7"> If a man espouseth a damsel virgin &#91;or a woman maiden&#93;, and another man findeth her in the city, and doeth lechery with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:21–22; DEUTERONOMY 21:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:13"> thou shalt lead ever either to the gate of that city, and they shall be killed with stones; the damsel shall be stoned, for she cried not, when she was in the city; the man shall be stoned, for he defouled his neighbour’s wife; and thus thou shalt do away evil from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 13:14"> But if a man findeth in the field a damsel, which is espoused, and he taketh her, and doeth lechery with her, he alone shall die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:22"> the damsel shall suffer nothing of evil, neither is guilty of death; for as a thief riseth against his brother, and slayeth him, so and the damsel suffered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 13:7"> she was alone in the field, she cried, and none was present, that should deliver her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 22:16–17"> If a man findeth a damsel virgin &#91;or a woman maiden&#93;, that hath no spouse, and taketh, and doeth lechery with her, and the thing cometh to the doom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:19; DEUTERONOMY 22:24; DEUTERONOMY 21:14"> he that slept with her shall give to the father of the damsel fifty shekels of silver, and he shall have her to wife, for he made her low; he shall not be able to forsake her, in all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:1; LEVITICUS 18:8"> A man shall not take his father’s wife, neither he shall show her privates.</span>
