</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:31; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; JOB 31:26–27"> that they go and serve alien gods, and worship them, the sun, and the moon, and all the knighthood of heaven, which things I commanded not;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 7:51; DEUTERONOMY 13:12–14; DEUTERONOMY 19:18"> and this is told to thee, and thou hearest it, and inquirest diligently, and thou findest that it is sooth, and that abomination is done in Israel;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:14; JOSHUA 7:25; LEVITICUS 24:16"> thou shalt lead out the man and the woman, that did that most cursed thing, to the gates of thy city, and they shall be oppressed with stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:30; HEBREWS 10:28; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:19"> He that shall be slain, shall perish in the mouth of twain &#91;or two&#93;, either of three witnesses; no man be slain, for one man saith witnessing against him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:13; DEUTERONOMY 17:12"> The hand of the witnesses shall first slay him, and at the last the hand of the other people shall be put to, for to throw him down with stones, that thou do away evil from the midst of thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:5; HAGGAI 2:11; DEUTERONOMY 1:17"> If thou perceivest, that hard and doubtful doom is with thee, betwixt blood and blood, cause and cause, leprosy and not leprosy, and thou seest that the words of &#91;the&#93; judges within thy gates be diverse in their deeming; rise thou, and go up to the place that thy Lord God hath chosen;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:24; HAGGAI 2:11; MALACHI 2:7"> and thou shalt come to the priests of the kin of Levi, and to the judge that is in that time, and thou shalt ask of them, which shall show to thee the truth of &#91;the&#93; doom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:2–3"> And thou shalt do, whatever thing they say, that be sovereigns in the place which the Lord choose, and teach thee by the law of the Lord;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:7; DEUTERONOMY 5:32; ROMANS 13:1–6"> thou shalt pursue &#91;or follow&#93; the sentence of them; thou shalt not bow therefrom to the right side, either to the left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:30; DEUTERONOMY 13:5; DEUTERONOMY 18:5"> For that man shall die, that is proud, and will not obey to the behest of the priest, that ministereth in that time to thy Lord God, and to the sentence of the judge, and thou shalt do away evil from the midst of Israel;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:11; DEUTERONOMY 19:20; NUMBERS 15:30–31"> and all the people shall hear, and dread, that no man from thenceforth swell with pride.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 8:19–20; 1ST SAMUEL 12:19; JOSHUA 1:13"> When thou hast entered into the land, which thy Lord God shall give to thee, and wieldest it, and dwellest therein, and sayest, I shall ordain a king on me, as all nations by compass have;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:21; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 12:23"> thou shalt ordain him, whom thy Lord God chooseth, of the number of thy brethren. Thou shalt not be able to make king a man of another folk, which man is not thy brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 31:1–3; EZEKIEL 17:15; DEUTERONOMY 28:68"> And when the king is ordained, he shall not multiply horses to him, neither he shall lead again the people into Egypt, neither he shall be raised into pride, or tyranny, by the number of knights, mostly since the Lord commanded to you, that ye turn no more again by the same way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 13:26; MATTHEW 19:5; PROVERBS 30:8–9"> The king shall not have many wives, that draw his mind to lusts, neither he shall have great weights of silver and of gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:9; 2ND KI 22:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:15"> Forsooth after that he hath set in the throne of his realm, he shall write to himself, that is, shall make to be written, the deuteronomy, that is, declaration, of this law in a book, and he shall take exemplar of priests of the kin of Levi;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:8; PSALMS 119:97–100; PSALMS 1:2"> and he shall have it with him, and he shall read it in all the days of his life, that he learn to dread his Lord God, and to keep his words and his ceremonies, that be commanded in the law;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:32; 1ST KINGS 15:5; PSALMS 131:1–2"> neither his heart be raised into pride on his brethren, neither bow he into the right side, either left side, that he reign long time, he and his sons on Israel.</span>
</div>
