<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:8–9; DEUTERONOMY 10:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:13–14"> Priests and deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, and all men that be of the same lineage, shall have no part and heritage with the tother people of Israel, for they shall eat the sacrifices of the Lord, and the offerings of him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:5–6; PSALMS 84:11; 1ST PETER 2:5"> and they shall not take any other thing of the possession of their brethren; for the Lord himself is their heritage, as he spake to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:30–34; DEUTERONOMY 12:27"> This shall be the doom of priests, that is, the thing justly given, either granted, of the people, and of them that offer sacrifices; whether they offer an ox, either a sheep, they shall give to the priest the shoulder, and the paunch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:44–47; NUMBERS 18:12–24; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:4–10"> the first fruits of wheat, and of wine, and of oil, and a part of wools of the shearing of sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:8; NUMBERS 3:10; EXODUS 28:1–14"> For thy Lord God hath chosen him of all thy lineages, that he stand and minister to the name of the Lord, he and his sons, without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:2–3; DEUTERONOMY 12:5; DEUTERONOMY 16:2"> If a deacon &#91;or Levite&#93; goeth out of one of thy cities of all Israel, in which he dwelleth, and will come and desireth the place which the Lord choose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 31:2–4"> he shall minister in the name of his Lord God, as all his brethren deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, that shall stand in that time before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:44; NEHEMIAH 12:47; 2ND CHRONICLES 31:4"> He shall take the same part of meats, that also other deacons shall take; besides that that is due to him in his city, by succession, either heritage, of father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:29–31; LEVITICUS 18:26–27; LEVITICUS 18:30"> When thou hast entered into the land which thy Lord God shall give to thee, be thou ware lest thou wilt pursue &#91;or follow&#93; the abominations of those folks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; EXODUS 22:18; LEVITICUS 19:26"> none be found in thee that cleanseth his son, either his daughter, and leadeth by the fire, either that asketh questions of diviners that divine about the altars, and that taketh heed to dreams, and chittering of birds; neither any witch be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; 1ST SAMUEL 28:11–14"> neither any enchanter or tregetour, that is, he who deceiveth men’s eyes so that a thing seem that which it is not; neither a man take counsel of them that have a fiend speaking within them, neither of false diviners, neither seek of dead men the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:24; DEUTERONOMY 9:4; LEVITICUS 18:27"> For the Lord hath abomination of all these things, and for such wicked-nesses he shall do away them in thine entering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:48; GENESIS 17:1; GENESIS 6:9"> Thou shalt be perfect, and without filth, with thy Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:16; DEUTERONOMY 18:10; GENESIS 20:6"> These heathen men, whose land thou shalt wield, hear them that work by chittering of birds, and false diviners; but thou art taught in other manner of thy Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:22–23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:37; JOHN 1:45"> Thy Lord God shall raise a prophet of thy folk, and of thy brethren, as me, to thee, thou shalt hear him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 20:19; DEUTERONOMY 5:23–28; DEUTERONOMY 9:10"> as thou askedest of thy Lord God in Horeb, when the company was gathered together, and thou saidest, I shall no more hear the voice of my Lord God, and I shall no more see this greatest fire, lest I die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:28"> And the Lord said to me, They have spoken well all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:15; JOHN 8:28; JOHN 12:49–50"> I shall raise to them a prophet, like thee, of the midst of their brethren, and I shall put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all things, which I shall command to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:22–23; HEBREWS 2:3; HEBREWS 10:26"> And I shall be avenger of him, that will not hear his words, which he shall speak in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:1–5; JEREMIAH 27:15; JEREMIAH 23:13–15"> Soothly a prophet shall be slain, which is depraved with pride, and will speak in my name those things, which I commanded not to him, that he should say, either by the name of alien gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:1–3; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:24; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:2"> That if thou answerest by privy thought, How may I understand the word, which the Lord spake not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:20; 2ND KI 20:1; JEREMIAH 28:1–17"> thou shalt have this sign, or token, if that that that prophet saith before in the name of the Lord, cometh not, the Lord spake it not, but he feigned it through pride of his soul, and there-fore thou shalt not dread him.</span>
