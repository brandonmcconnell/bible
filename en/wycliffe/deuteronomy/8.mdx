<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:1; DEUTERONOMY 5:32–3; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:1–2"> Be thou ware diligently, that thou do each commandment which I command to thee today, that ye may live, and be multiplied, and that ye enter, and wield the land, for which the Lord swore to your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JAMES 1:3; 1ST PETER 1:7; DEUTERONOMY 8:16"> And thou shalt have mind of all the way, by which thy Lord God led thee by forty years, in desert, that he should torment thee, and should assay thee; and that those things that were treated in thy soul should be known, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments, either nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:4; LUKE 4:4; HEBREWS 13:5–6"> And he tormented thee with neediness, and he gave to thee meat, manna to eat, which thou knewest not, and thy fathers knew not, that he should show to thee, that a man liveth not in bread alone, but in each word that cometh out of the Lord’s mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:21; DEUTERONOMY 29:5; MATTHEW 26:25–30"> Thy clothes, with which thou were covered, failed not for eldness, and thy foot was not bruised under-neath, lo! the fortieth year is;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; PROVERBS 3:12; 2ND SAMUEL 7:14"> that thou think in thine heart, for as a man teacheth his son, so thy Lord God hath taught thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 6:31; DEUTERONOMY 5:33; 1ST SAMUEL 12:24"> that thou keep the commandments of thy Lord God, and go in his ways, and dread him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:9–12; EXODUS 3:8; EZEKIEL 20:6"> For thy Lord God shall lead thee into a good land, into the land of rivers, and of standing waters or ponds, and of wells, in whose fields and mountains the depths of floods break out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 6:13; PSALMS 147:14; DEUTERONOMY 32:14"> into the land of wheat, of barley, and of vines, in which land fig trees, and pomegranates, and olives come forth; into the land of oil, and honey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 22:14; DEUTERONOMY 33:25; JOSHUA 22:8"> where thou shalt eat thy bread without neediness, and thou shalt use the plenty of all things; of which land the stones be iron, and metals of tin be digged of the hills thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 103:2; ROMANS 14:6; PROVERBS 3:9"> that when thou hast eaten, and art full-filled, thou bless thy Lord God for the best land which he hath given to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 106:21; HOSEA 2:8–9; PROVERBS 1:32"> Therefore keep thou, and beware, lest any time thou forget thy Lord God, and despise his commandments, and dooms, and ceremonies, which I command to thee today;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:9; DEUTERONOMY 28:47; DEUTERONOMY 32:15"> lest after that thou hast eaten, and art full-filled, hast builded fair houses, and hast dwelled in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 1:3; GENESIS 13:1–5; LUKE 12:13–21"> and hast droves of oxen, and flocks of sheep, and plenty of silver, and of gold, and of all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 106:21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 8:11"> thine heart be then raised, and thou think not upon thy Lord God, that led thee out of the land of Egypt, and from the house of servage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:6; NUMBERS 20:11; JEREMIAH 2:6"> and he was thy leader in the great wilderness and fearful, in which wilder-ness was a serpent burning with blast, and a scorpion, and dipsas, that is, an adder, that maketh them whom he stingeth to die for thirst, and utterly no waters were in the desert, the which Lord brought out streams of the hardest stone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:3; JAMES 1:12; EXODUS 16:15"> and he fed thee with manna in the wilderness, which manna thy fathers knew not. And after that the Lord had tormented thee, and proved thee, at the last he had mercy on thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:4; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:7; DANIEL 4:30"> lest thou wouldest say in thine heart, My strength, and the might of mine hand, hath given all these things to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:22; HOSEA 2:8; DEUTERONOMY 7:8"> But think thou upon thy Lord God, that he hath given strengths to thee, that he should fulfill his covenant, of which he swore to thy fathers, as this present day showeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:26; 1ST SAMUEL 12:25; ZEPHANIAH 1:18"> Forsooth if thou forgettest thy Lord God, and pursuest &#91;or followest&#93; alien gods, and worshippest them in heart, and honourest &#91;them&#93;withoutforth, lo! now I before-say to thee, that thou shalt perish utterly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 9:11–12; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:16–17"> as &#91;the&#93; heathen men perished, which the Lord did away in thine entering, so also ye shall perish, if ye shall be unobedient to the voice of your Lord God.</span>
