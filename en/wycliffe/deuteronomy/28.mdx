<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:19; EXODUS 23:22; LEVITICUS 26:3–13"> Forsooth if thou hearest the voice of thy Lord God, that thou do and keep all his commandments, which I command to thee today, thy Lord God shall make thee higher than all folks that live in earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:15; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:8"> And all these blessings shall come &#91;up&#93; on thee, and shall take thee; if nevertheless thou hearest his behests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 144:12–15; GENESIS 39:5; HAGGAI 2:19"> Thou shalt be blessed in &#91;the&#93; city, and blessed in the field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:22; GENESIS 49:25; DEUTERONOMY 7:13"> blessed shall be the fruit of thy womb, and the fruit of thy land, and the fruit of thy beasts, the flocks of thy great beasts, and the folds of thy sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> blessed shall be thy barns, and blessed shall be thy remnants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 121:8; NUMBERS 27:17; 2ND CHRONICLES 1:10"> thou shalt be blessed entering in, and going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:38–41; LEVITICUS 26:7–8; PSALMS 89:23"> The Lord shall give thine enemies to fall in thy sight, that shall rise against thee; by one way they shall come against thee, and by seven ways they shall flee from thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:10; LEVITICUS 26:10; PROVERBS 3:9–10"> The Lord shall send out blessings upon thy cellars, and upon all the works of thine hands; and he shall bless thee in the land which thou hast taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5–6; DEUTERONOMY 7:6; DEUTERONOMY 26:18–19"> The Lord shall raise thee to him-self into an holy people, as he swore to thee, if thou keepest the behests of thy Lord God, and goest in his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 7:14; DEUTERONOMY 11:25; JEREMIAH 33:9"> And all the peoples of other lands shall see, that the name of the Lord is inwardly called upon thee, and they shall dread thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:9; PROVERBS 10:22; LEVITICUS 26:9"> The Lord shall make thee to be plenteous in all goods, in the fruit of thy womb, and in the fruit of thy beasts, in the fruit of thy land, which the Lord swore to thy fathers, that he would give to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:6; JOEL 2:23–24; DEUTERONOMY 15:10"> The Lord shall open his best treasure, heaven, that he give rain to thy land in his time; and he shall bless all the works of thine hands; and thou shalt lend to many folks, and of no man thou shalt take borrowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:1; DEUTERONOMY 4:6–9; PHILIPPIANS 1:27"> The Lord God shall set thee into the head, and not into the tail, and ever&#91;more&#93; thou shalt be above, and not beneath; if nevertheless thou hearest the commandments of thy Lord God, which I command to thee today, and keepest, and doest them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:21; DEUTERONOMY 5:32; DEUTERONOMY 11:16"> and bowest not away from them, neither to the right side, neither to the left side, neither pursuest &#91;or followed&#93; alien gods, nor worshippest them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MALACHI 2:2; DEUTERONOMY 28:2; DEUTERONOMY 29:20"> That if thou wilt not hear the voice of thy Lord God, that thou keep and do all his behests, and ceremonies, which I command to thee today, all these cursings shall come upon thee, and shall take thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MALACHI 3:9–12; PROVERBS 3:33; JOEL 1:4"> Thou shalt be cursed in city, and cursed in field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HAGGAI 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:5; PSALMS 69:22"> Cursed shall be thy barn, and cursed shall be thy remnants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:26; LEVITICUS 26:19–20; JOB 18:16–19"> Cursed shall be the fruit of thy womb, and the fruit of thy land, the droves of thine oxen, and the flocks of thy sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:6; JUDGES 5:6–7; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:5"> Thou shalt be cursed going in, and cursed going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:15; MALACHI 2:2; DEUTERONOMY 4:26"> The Lord shall send upon thee hunger, and thirst, and blaming, into all the works which thou shalt do, till he all-break thee, and lose thee swiftly, for thy full wicked findings, in which thou hast forsaken me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:25; JEREMIAH 24:10; JEREMIAH 16:4"> The Lord shall join pestilence to thee, till he waste thee from the land, to which thou shalt enter to wield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="AMOS 4:9; LEVITICUS 26:16; HAGGAI 2:17"> The Lord shall smite thee with neediness, with fever, and cold, with burning, and heat, with corrupt air, and mildew, either rust; and he shall pursue thee till thou perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:19; JEREMIAH 14:1–6; 1ST KINGS 18:2"> Heaven, that is above thee, be it brazen; and the earth, that thou tread-est on, be it iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:12; ISAIAH 5:24; AMOS 4:11"> The Lord give dust for rain to thy land, and ash come down from heaven upon thee, till thou be all-broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 30:17; EZEKIEL 23:46; JEREMIAH 24:9"> The Lord give thee falling before thine enemies; by one way go thou &#91;out&#93; against them, and by seven ways flee thou from them, and be thou scattered by all the realms of &#91;the&#93; earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:20; JEREMIAH 16:4; JEREMIAH 7:33"> and thy dead body be it into meat to all birds of heaven, and to beasts of the earth, and none be that may drive them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 5:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:35; LEVITICUS 21:20"> The Lord smite thee with the botch of Egypt, and the Lord smite the part of body whereby ordures, or turds, be voided out; also the Lord smite thee with scab, and itching, so that thou mayest not be cured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–11; ISAIAH 19:11–17; ISAIAH 6:9–10"> The Lord smite thee with madness, and blindness, and wildness of thought;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:10; JOB 5:14; JUDGES 6:1–6"> and grope thou in midday, as a blind man is wont to grope in dark-nesses; and dress he not thy ways; in all time suffer thou false challenge, and be thou oppressed by violence, neither have thou any that shall deliver thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="AMOS 5:11; JEREMIAH 8:10; JOB 31:10"> Take thou a wife, and another man sleep with her; build thou an house, and dwell thou not therein; plant thou a vinery &#91;or vine&#93;, and gather thou not grapes thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOB 1:14–15; JUDGES 6:1"> Thine ox be offered before thee, and eat thou not thereof; thine ass be ravished in thy sight, and be it not yielded again to thee; thy sheep be given to thine enemies, and none be that help thee to recover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOEL 3:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:41; JOB 17:5"> Thy sons and thy daughters be given to another people, while thine eyes see, and fail at the sight of them all day; and no strength be in thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:17; DEUTERONOMY 28:29–30; ISAIAH 1:7"> A people whom thou knowest not, eat the fruits of thy land, and all thy travails; and ever&#91;more&#93; be thou suffering false challenges, and be thou oppressed in all days, or all time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 28:68; ISAIAH 33:14"> and be thou wondering at the fearfulness of those things which thine eyes shall see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:27; ISAIAH 3:24; ISAIAH 3:17"> The Lord smite thee with the worst botch in the knees, and in the hinder parts of the leg; and thou may not be healed from the sole of thy foot till to thy top or unto the noll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:13; DEUTERONOMY 4:28; 2ND KI 25:11"> And the Lord shall lead thee, and thy king, whom thou shalt ordain on thee, into a folk which thou knowest not, thou, and thy fathers; and thou shalt serve there to alien gods, to tree, and to stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:9; 1ST KINGS 9:7–8; JEREMIAH 24:9"> And thou shalt be lost, or forgotten, into a proverb, and into a fable, to all peoples, to whom the Lord shall bring thee in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="HAGGAI 1:6; MICAH 6:15; JOEL 1:4"> Thou shalt cast much seed into the earth, and thou shalt gather again little; for locusts shall devour all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ISAIAH 17:10–11; JOEL 2:2–4; JONAH 4:7"> Thou shalt plant, and dig a vinery &#91;or vine&#93;, and thou shalt not drink the wine of it, neither thou shalt gather thereof anything; for it shall be wasted with worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MICAH 6:15; PSALMS 23:5; PSALMS 104:15"> Thou shalt have olive trees in all thy coasts, and thou shalt not be anointed with oil of them; for they shall fall down, and perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:32; LAMENTATIONS 1:5; 2ND KI 24:14"> Thou shalt beget sons and daughters, and thou shalt not use them; for they shall be led into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="AMOS 7:1–2; DEUTERONOMY 28:38–39"> Rust, or mildew, shall waste all thy trees and the fruits of thy land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:13; 1ST SAMUEL 13:19–23; 2ND KI 17:20"> A comeling, that dwelleth with thee in the land, shall go up upon thee, and he shall be the higher; forsooth thou shalt go down, and shalt be the lower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:12–13; LAMENTATIONS 1:5"> He shall lend to thee, and thou shalt not lend to him; he shall be into the head, and thou shalt be into the tail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:15; ISAIAH 1:20; JEREMIAH 24:9–10"> And all these cursings shall come upon thee, and shall pursue thee, and take thee, till thou perish; for thou heardest not the voice of thy Lord God, neither keptest his commandments and ceremonies, which he commanded to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:18; DEUTERONOMY 28:37; EZEKIEL 14:8"> And signs, and great wonders shall be in thee, and in thy seed, till into without end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:11; DEUTERONOMY 12:7–12; NEHEMIAH 9:35–37"> for thou servedest not thy Lord God in joy and gladness of heart, for the abundance of all things that God sent thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JEREMIAH 28:13–14; LAMENTATIONS 5:2–6; EZEKIEL 4:16–17"> Thou shalt serve thine enemy, whom God shall send to thee, in hunger, and thirst, and in nakedness, and in poverty of all things; and he shall put an iron yoke on thy noll, till he all-break thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:19; JEREMIAH 49:22; JEREMIAH 48:40"> The Lord shall bring on thee a folk from far place, and from the last ends of &#91;the&#93; earth, into the likeness of an eagle flying with rush, of which folk thou mayest not understand their language;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="ISAIAH 47:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:17; LUKE 21:23–24"> a folk most greedy asker this shall be, that shall not give reverence to an eld &#91;or old&#93; man, neither have mercy upon a little child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:33; ISAIAH 62:8; LEVITICUS 26:26"> And it shall devour the fruit of thy beasts, and the fruits of thy land, till thou perishest, and this folk shall not leave to thee wheat, wine, and oil, nor droves of oxen, and flocks of sheep, till he lose thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:4–7; EZEKIEL 4:1–8; MATTHEW 24:15–16"> and all-break &#91;thee&#93; in all thy cities, and till thy firm and high walls be destroyed, in which thou haddest trust in all thy land. Thou shalt be besieged within thy gates in all thy land, which thy Lord God shall give to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:10; JEREMIAH 19:9; LEVITICUS 26:29"> And thou shalt eat the fruit of thy womb, and the flesh of thy sons, and of thy daughters, which thy Lord God shall give to thee, in the anguish, and in the destroying, by which thine enemies shall oppress thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:9; MATTHEW 20:15; DEUTERONOMY 13:6"> A man delicate of life, and full lecherous, shall have envy greatly to his brother, and to his wife that lieth in his bosom, and also toward the remnant of his children, that he hath left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:2; JEREMIAH 52:6; JEREMIAH 5:10"> lest he give to them of the flesh of his sons which he shall eat; for he hath none other thing in &#91;the&#93; besieging, and poverty, by which thine enemies shall waste thee within all thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:54; ISAIAH 3:16; LAMENTATIONS 4:3–6"> A tender woman and delicate, that might not go upon the earth, neither set a step of &#91;the&#93; foot, for her most softness and tenderness, shall have envy to her husband that lieth in her bosom, on the flesh of her son, and daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:15; GENESIS 49:10"> and on the filth of &#91;the&#93; skins, wherein the child is wrapped in the mother’s womb, that go out of the midst of her hip bones, or loins, and on &#91;the&#93; free children that be born in the same hour. They shall eat those children privily, for the scarcity of all things in besieging and destroying, by which thine enemy shall oppress thee within thy gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:8; JEREMIAH 7:9–10; MALACHI 1:14"> No but thou shalt keep and do all the words of this law, that be written in this volume, either book, and shalt dread his glorious name and fearful, that is, thy Lord God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="MARK 13:19; DEUTERONOMY 28:46; DANIEL 9:12"> the Lord shall increase thy wounds, or torments, and the wounds of thy seed; great wounds and continual, sicknesses worst and everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:15; EXODUS 15:26; DEUTERONOMY 28:27"> And he shall turn into thee all the torments of Egypt, which thou dread-edest, and those &#91;or they&#93; shall cleave to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:25–26"> Furthermore the Lord shall bring upon thee also all the sorrows and wounds, that be not written in the book of this law, till he all-break thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:22; NEHEMIAH 9:23; DEUTERONOMY 4:27"> And ye shall dwell few in number, that were before as the stars of heaven for multitude, for thou heardest not the voice of thy Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:26; JEREMIAH 32:41; DEUTERONOMY 30:9"> And as the Lord was glad before upon you, and did well to you, and multiplied you; so he shall be glad to lose &#91;or scatter&#93; you, and to destroy you, that ye be taken away from the land, to which thou shalt enter to wield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:27–28; LEVITICUS 26:33; JEREMIAH 16:13"> The Lord shall scatter thee into all peoples, from &#91;the&#93; highness of the earth unto the coasts thereof; and thou shalt serve there to alien gods, which thou knowest not, and thy fathers knew not, to trees and stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:36; ROMANS 11:10; AMOS 9:4"> Also thou shalt not rest in those folks, neither rest shall be to the step of thy foot. For the Lord shall give to thee there a fearful heart, and eyes failing, and a soul wasted with privy sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:13; HEBREWS 10:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:15–17"> And thy life shall be as hanging before thee; thou shalt dread night and day, and thou shalt not trust to thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:34; JOB 7:3–4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:6"> In the morrowtide thou shalt say, Who shall give the eventide to me? and in the eventide thou shalt say, Who shall give the morrowtide to me? for the dread of thine heart, by which thou shalt be made afeared, and for those things which thou shalt see with thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:12; JEREMIAH 43:7; HOSEA 8:13"> The Lord shall lead thee again by ships into Egypt, by the way of which he said to thee, Thou shouldest no more see it. There thou shalt be sold to thine enemies, into menservants and womenservants; and none shall be that shall deliver thee.</span>
