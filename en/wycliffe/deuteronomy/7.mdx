<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:19; DEUTERONOMY 4:38"> When thy Lord God hath led thee into the land, into which thou shalt enter to wield, and hath done away many folks before thee, Hittites, and Girgashites, and Amorites, Canaanites, and Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites; seven folks, of much greater number than thou art, and stronger than thou;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 2:2; EXODUS 23:32–33; DEUTERONOMY 20:16–17"> and when thy Lord God hath betaken them to thee, thou shalt smite them unto death, thou shalt not make with them a bond of peace, neither thou shalt have mercy upon them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:2; JOSHUA 23:12–13; EXODUS 34:15–16"> neither thou shalt fellowship marriages with them; thou shalt not give thy daughter to his son, neither thou shalt take his daughter to thy son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:15; JUDGES 2:11; DEUTERONOMY 4:26"> for she shall deceive thy son, that he pursue &#91;or follow&#93; not me, that he serve more alien gods; and then the fierce vengeance of the Lord shall be wroth, and shall do away thee soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 23:24; DEUTERONOMY 12:2–3; EXODUS 34:13"> But rather thou shalt do these things to them; destroy ye their altars, and break ye their molten images of metal, and cut ye down their woods, and burn ye their graven images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:2; EXODUS 19:5–6; 1ST PETER 2:9"> For thou art an holy people to thy Lord God; thy Lord God chose thee, that thou be a special people to him, of all peoples that be on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:22; ROMANS 9:11–15; ROMANS 11:6"> Not for ye overcame in number all folks, the Lord is joined to you, and chose you, since ye be fewer than all peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 20:2; EXODUS 32:13; EPHESIANS 2:4–5"> but for the Lord loved you, and kept the oath which he swore to your fathers; and he led you out in &#91;a&#93; strong hand, and again-bought you from the house of servage, from the house of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:23; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:13; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:3"> And thou shalt know, that thy Lord God himself is a strong God, and faithful, and keepeth covenant and mercy to them that love him, and to them that keep his commandments, into a thousand generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:18; NAHUM 1:2; ROMANS 12:19"> and he yieldeth anon to them that hate him, so that he destroy them, and defer, or tarry, no longer; restoring, or yielding, anon to them that that they deserve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 14:15; DEUTERONOMY 4:1; DEUTERONOMY 5:32"> Therefore keep thou the command-ments, and ceremonies, and dooms, which I command to thee today, that thou do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:1–14; DEUTERONOMY 7:9; LEVITICUS 26:3–13"> If after that thou hearest these dooms, thou keepest, and doest them, thy Lord God shall keep to thee covenant, and mercy, which he swore to thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 14:21; JOHN 15:10; DEUTERONOMY 28:3–5"> And he shall love thee, and multiply thee, and he shall bless the fruit of thy womb, and the fruit of thy land, thy wheat, and thy vintage, thine oil, and thy droves of beasts, and the flocks of thy sheep, on the land for which he swore to thy fathers, that he should give it to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 23:26–33; DEUTERONOMY 28:4; PSALMS 127:3"> Thou shalt be blessed among all peoples; none barren of ever either kind shall be with thee, as well in men, as in thy flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 15:26; PSALMS 105:36–37; LEVITICUS 26:3–4"> The Lord shall do away from thee all ache; and he shall not bring to thee the full evil sicknesses of Egypt, that thou hast known, but to all thine enemies these sicknesses shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 23:33; JUDGES 8:27; DEUTERONOMY 13:8"> And thou shalt devour, that is, destroy, all &#91;the&#93; peoples, which thy Lord God shall give to thee; thine eye shall not spare them, neither thou shalt serve their gods, lest they be into the falling of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:32; NUMBERS 33:53; DEUTERONOMY 18:21"> If thou sayest in thine heart, These folks be more than I, how may I do away them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 105:5; DEUTERONOMY 31:6; DEUTERONOMY 1:29"> do not thou dread, but have thou mind, what things thy Lord God did to Pharaoh, and all the Egyptians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:34; NEHEMIAH 9:10–11; JEREMIAH 32:20–21"> he did to them the greatest vengeances, which thine eyes saw, and miracles, and great wonders, and the strong hand, and an arm stretched out, that thy Lord God should lead thee out thence; so he shall do to all peoples which thou dreadest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:12; EXODUS 23:28–30"> Furthermore and thy Lord God shall send venomous flies into them, till he do away, and destroy all men, that fled thee, and they shall not be able to be hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 1:5; DEUTERONOMY 10:17; NEHEMIAH 9:32"> Thou shalt not dread them, for thy Lord God is in the midst of thee, a great God, and fearful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 23:29–30; JOSHUA 15:63"> He himself shall waste these nations in thy sight, little and little, and by parts; thou shalt not be able to do away them altogether, lest per-adventure &#91;the&#93; beasts of the earth be multiplied against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:20; DEUTERONOMY 2:15; JEREMIAH 17:18"> and thy Lord God shall give them to thee in thy sight, and he shall slay them, till they be done away utterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:8; JOSHUA 23:9; JOSHUA 1:5"> And he shall betake their kings into thine hands, and thou shalt destroy their names under heaven; none shall be able to against-stand thee, till thou all-break them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 32:20; 1ST CHRONICLES 14:12; DEUTERONOMY 17:1"> Thou shalt burn in fire their graven images; thou shalt not covet the silver and gold, of which those images be made, neither thou shalt take of those &#91;or them&#93; anything to thee, lest thou offend therefore, for it is the abom-ination of thy Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:17; HOSEA 14:8; EZEKIEL 11:18"> Neither thou shalt bring anything of the idol into thine house, lest thou be made cursed, as also that idol is; thou shalt loathe it as filth, and thou shalt have it as defouling, and as filths of abomination, for it is cursed.</span>
