<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:22; GENESIS 14:6; DEUTERONOMY 2:32–11"> Soothly Horims dwelled before in Seir, and when they were put out, and were done away, the sons of Esau dwelled there, as Israel did in the land of his possession, which the Lord gave to him.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 106:26; ISAIAH 66:14; PSALMS 32:4"> whose hand was against them, that they should perish from the midst of their tents.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:9; JUDGES 11:13–27; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:10"> and thou shalt nigh into the coasts of the sons of Ammon; be thou ware that thou fight not against them, nor be moved to battle; for I shall not give to thee of the land of the sons of Ammon, for I have given it to the sons of Lot into possession.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 1:28; DEUTERONOMY 2:22"> a much people, and great, and of noble length, as Anakim, which the Lord did away from the face of them, and made them to dwell for those giants,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 36:8; GENESIS 36:20–30; DEUTERONOMY 2:12"> as he did to the sons of Esau, that dwelled in Seir, and did away Horims, and gave to them the land of Horims, which the sons of Esau wield till into present time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 11:19; DEUTERONOMY 2:6; NUMBERS 21:21–23"> We shall pass through thy land, we shall go in the common way; we shall not bow neither to the right side, nor to the left side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:19"> Sell thou us meats for price, that we eat; give thou us water for money, and so we shall drink. Only it is that we ask of thee that thou grant passage to us,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:18; JUDGES 11:17–18; DEUTERONOMY 23:3–4"> as the sons of Esau did, that dwell in Seir, and the Moabites, that dwell in Ar, till we come to Jordan, and pass over to the land which our Lord God shall give to us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:23; EXODUS 4:21; JOSHUA 11:19–20"> And Sihon, king of Heshbon, would not give passage to us; for thy Lord God made hard his spirit, and made firm in evil the heart of him, that he should be betaken into thine hands, as thou seest now.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:8; DEUTERONOMY 2:24"> And the Lord said to me, Lo, I have begun to betake to thee Sihon, and his land; begin thou to wield it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:23–30; JUDGES 11:20–23; PSALMS 136:19"> And Sihon went out against us with all his people, to battle in Jahaz.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:2; JOSHUA 21:44; DEUTERONOMY 3:2–3"> And our Lord God betook him to us, and we have smitten him, with his sons, and all his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:2; 1ST SAMUEL 15:3; DEUTERONOMY 3:6"> And we took in that time all the cities, when the dwellers of those cities, men, and women, and children, were slain; we left not in them any-thing,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:27; DEUTERONOMY 20:14; NUMBERS 31:9–11"> except beasts that fell into the part of men taking prey, and except spoils of the cities which we took.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:9; DEUTERONOMY 4:48; DEUTERONOMY 3:12"> From Aroer, which is on the brink of the strand &#91;or the stream&#93; of Arnon, from the town which is set in the valley, unto Gilead, no town was, nor city, that escaped our hands. Our Lord God betook all to us;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:24; DEUTERONOMY 3:16; GENESIS 32:22"> except the land of the sons of Ammon, to which land we nighed not, and all things that lie to the strand &#91;or the stream&#93; of Jabbok, and except the cities of the mountains, and all the places from which our Lord God forbade us.</span>
