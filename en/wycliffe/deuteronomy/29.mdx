<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:2–3; HEBREWS 8:9; EXODUS 24:2–8"> These be the words of the bond of peace, which the Lord commanded to Moses, that he should make with the sons of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the bond of peace, which he covenanted with them in Horeb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 19:4; EXODUS 8:12; JOSHUA 24:5–6"> And Moses called all Israel, and said to them, Ye have seen all things which the Lord did before you in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to all his land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:32–35; NEHEMIAH 9:9–11; DEUTERONOMY 7:18–19"> the great temptations which thine eyes have seen, those signs, and the great wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:18; ISAIAH 6:9–10; PROVERBS 20:12"> And the Lord gave not to you an heart understanding, and eyes seeing, and ears that may hear, till into this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:4; NEHEMIAH 9:21; DEUTERONOMY 8:2"> He led you by forty years through desert; your clothes were not broken, neither the shoes of your feet were wasted by eldness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:3; EPHESIANS 5:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:25"> ye ate not bread, neither ye drank wine and cider, that ye should know that he is your Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:24–17; NUMBERS 32:33–42; PSALMS 135:10–12"> And ye came to this place; and Sihon, the king of Heshbon went out, and Og, the king of Bashan, and they came to us to battle. And we smote them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:12–13; NUMBERS 32:33"> and we took away their land, and we gave it to possession, to Reuben, and to Gad, and to the half lineage of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:3; DEUTERONOMY 4:6; LUKE 11:28"> Therefore keep ye the words of this covenant, and fulfill ye them, that ye understand all things which ye shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOEL 2:16–17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:12; DEUTERONOMY 31:12–13"> All ye stand today before your Lord God, your princes, and lineages, and the greater men in birth, and your teachers or doctors, all the people of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 12:38; JOSHUA 9:21–27; EXODUS 12:48–49"> your free children, and your wives, and the comelings that dwell with thee in the tents, besides the hewers of wood, and besides they &#91;or them&#93; that bear water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:12–15; 2ND KI 11:17; DEUTERONOMY 5:2–3"> that thou go into the bond of peace of thy Lord God, and in the oath which thy Lord God smiteth with thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 6:7; GENESIS 17:7; DEUTERONOMY 28:9"> that he raise thee up into a people to himself, and that he be thy Lord God, as he spake to thee, and as he swore to thy fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:31–34; HEBREWS 8:7–12"> And not to you alone I make this bond of peace, and confirm these oaths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:39; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:14; JEREMIAH 50:5"> but to all men, present and absent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:19; DEUTERONOMY 2:9; DEUTERONOMY 2:4"> For ye know how we dwelled in the land of Egypt, and how we passed by the midst of nations; which ye passed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:36"> and saw abominations and filths, that is, their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which they worshipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:15; DEUTERONOMY 11:16–17; DEUTERONOMY 13:1–15"> Lest peradventure among you be man either woman, meine either lineage, whose heart is turned away today from your Lord God, that he go, and serve the gods of those folks; and a root burgeoning gall and bitter-ness be among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:24; NUMBERS 15:30; NUMBERS 15:39"> and when he hath heard the words of this oath, he bless himself in his heart, and say, Peace shall be to me, and I shall go in the shrewdness of mine heart; and lest the drunken take the thirsty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 74:1; PSALMS 79:5; DEUTERONOMY 9:14"> and the Lord forgive not to him, but then full greatly his strong vengeance be fierce, and his fervour burst out against that man, and all the curses that be written in this book rest upon him; and the Lord do away his name from under heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:41; JOSHUA 7:1–26; EZEKIEL 13:9"> and waste him into perdition from all the lineages of Israel, by the curses that be contained in the book of this law, and of the bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:8"> And the generation pursuing &#91;or following&#93; shall see, and the sons that shall be born afterward, and pilgrims that shall come from &#91;a&#93; far, seeing the vengeances of that land, and the sick-nesses by which the Lord tormented that land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 2:9; JEREMIAH 17:6; ISAIAH 34:9"> burning that land with brimstone, and heat of the sun, so that it be no more sown, neither bring forth any green thing, into ensample of &#91;the&#93; destroying of Sodom, and Gomorrah, of Admah, and of Zeboiim, which the Lord destroyed in his wrath, and strong vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; 1ST KINGS 9:8–9; ROMANS 2:5"> And all folks shall say, Why did the Lord so to this land? What is the great wrath of his strong vengeance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:9; ISAIAH 47:6; JEREMIAH 50:7"> and they shall answer, For they forsook the covenant of the Lord, which he covenanted with their fathers, when he led them out of the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:64; JUDGES 2:12–13; JUDGES 5:8"> and they served alien gods, and worshipped them, which they knew not, and to which they were not given;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 9:11–14; DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68; DEUTERONOMY 27:15–26"> therefore the strong vengeance of the Lord was wroth against this land, that he brought in upon it all the curses that be written in this book;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 7:20; 1ST KINGS 14:15; PSALMS 52:5"> and he casted them out of their land, in wrath and strong vengeance, and in full great indignation; and he casted forth them into an alien land, as it is proved today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 2:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:7; ROMANS 11:33–34"> Diverse things be hid, or privy, of our Lord God, that is, in his before-knowing, which things be showed to us, and to our sons without end, that we do all the words of this law.</span>
