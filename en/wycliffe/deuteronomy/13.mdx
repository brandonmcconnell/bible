<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:25–28; 1ST JOHN 4:1; JEREMIAH 29:8"> If a prophet riseth in the midst of thee, either he that saith himself to have seen a dream, and he before-saith a sign, and a wonder to come after,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:6; MATTHEW 7:22–23; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:13–14"> and this thing that he said befalleth, and he saith to thee, Go we, and pursue &#91;or follow&#93; we alien gods, which thou knowest not, and serve we them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:19; PSALMS 66:10"> thou shalt not hear the words of that prophet, either of that dreamer; for your Lord God assayeth you, that he know openly whether ye love him, either nay, in all your heart, and in all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:10; DEUTERONOMY 10:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:17"> Pursue &#91;or follow&#93; ye your Lord God, and dread ye him; keep ye his commandments, and hear ye his voice; ye shall serve him, and ye shall cleave to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 5:13; DEUTERONOMY 18:20; JEREMIAH 14:15"> And that prophet, either feigner of dreams, shall be slain; for he spake that he should turn you away from your Lord God, that led you out of the land of Egypt, and again-bought you from the house of servage, that he make thee to err from the way that thy Lord God commanded to thee; and in killing of him thou shalt do away evil from the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:2–7; 2ND PETER 2:1; EPHESIANS 4:14"> If thy brother, the son of thy mother, either thy son, either thy daughter, either the wife which is in thy bosom, either thy friend, whom thou lovest as thy soul, will counsel thee, and saith privily, Go we and serve alien gods, which thou knowest not, and thy fathers know not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> of all the folks about, that be nigh either far, from the beginning unto the end of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:13; PROVERBS 1:10; DEUTERONOMY 7:16"> assent thou not to him, neither hear thou him, neither thine eye spare him, that thou have mercy of him, and hide him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:58; LUKE 14:26; MATTHEW 10:37"> but anon thou shalt slay him. Thine hand be first upon him, and after thee, all the people put to hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:25; LEVITICUS 24:14–16; EXODUS 20:2"> He shall be oppressed with stones, and shall be slain; for he would draw thee away from thy Lord God, that led thee out of the land of Egypt, from the house of servage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:20; DEUTERONOMY 17:13; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:20"> that all Israel hear this and dread, and do no more anything like this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1–17; JOSHUA 22:11–34"> If thou hearest any men saying in one of thy cities, which thy Lord God shall give thee to dwell in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:19; DEUTERONOMY 13:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:15"> The sons of Belial went out from the midst of thee, and turned away the dwellers of the city, and said, Go we, and serve alien gods, which ye knew not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:4; ISAIAH 11:3–4; JOHN 7:24"> inquire thou busily, and when the truth of the thing is beholden diligently, if thou findest that this thing is certain, that is said, and that this abomination is done indeed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 22:20; LEVITICUS 27:28; DEUTERONOMY 7:2"> anon thou shalt smite the dwellers of that city with the sharpness of sword, and thou shalt destroy that city, and all things that be therein, unto the beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:2; JOSHUA 8:28; ISAIAH 25:2"> Also whatever thing of appurtenance of household is found there, thou shalt gather it together in &#91;the&#93; midst of the streets thereof, and thou shalt burn it with that city, so that thou waste all things before thy Lord God, and it be a burial everlasting; it shall no more be builded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 26:4; DEUTERONOMY 7:26; JOSHUA 7:26"> And nothing of that cursing shall dwell in thine hand, that the Lord be turned away from the wrath of his strong vengeance, and have mercy upon thee, and multiply thee, as he swore to thy fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:28; MATTHEW 7:21; DEUTERONOMY 12:25"> When thou hast heard the voice of thy Lord God, thou shalt keep all his behests which I command to thee today, that thou do that thing that is pleasing in the sight of thy Lord God.</span>
