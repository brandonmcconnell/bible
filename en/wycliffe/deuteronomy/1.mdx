<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:1; JOSHUA 22:7; NUMBERS 33:17–18"> These be the words which Moses spake to all Israel over Jordan, in the wilderness of the field, against the Red Sea, betwixt Paran, and Tophel, and Laban, and Hazeroth, where is full much gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:8; NUMBERS 13:26; DEUTERONOMY 9:23"> by eleven days journey from Horeb by the way of the hill of Seir, till to Kadeshbarnea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:38; DEUTERONOMY 4:1–2; NUMBERS 20:1"> In the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, in the first day of the month, Moses spake to the sons of Israel all things which the Lord commanded to him that he should say to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:21–35; JOSHUA 12:2–6; JOSHUA 13:10–12"> after that he had smitten, or killed, Sihon, the king of Amorites, that dwelled in Heshbon, and Og, the king of Bashan, that dwelled in Ashtaroth, and in Edrei,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:8; DEUTERONOMY 32:46; DEUTERONOMY 31:9"> over Jordan, in the land of Moab. And Moses began to declare the law, and to say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 3:1; NUMBERS 10:11–13; EXODUS 19:1–2"> Our Lord God spake to us in Horeb, and said, It sufficeth to you that ye have dwelled in this hill;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:24; JOSHUA 10:40; AMOS 2:9"> turn ye again, and come ye to the hill of Amorites, and to other places that be next to it; and to the places of fields, and of hills, and to &#91;the&#93; lower places against the south, and beside the brink of the sea, to the land of Canaanites, and of Lebanon, till to the great flood Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 15:18; GENESIS 12:7; GENESIS 26:3–4"> Lo, he saith, I have given to you that land; enter ye, and have it in possession, on which the Lord swore to your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, that he should give that land to them, and to their seed after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 18:18; NUMBERS 11:17; NUMBERS 11:11–14"> And I said to you in that time, I may not alone sustain you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:22; EXODUS 32:13; GENESIS 22:17"> for your Lord God hath multiplied you, and ye be full many today, as the stars of heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 115:14; GENESIS 22:17; NUMBERS 6:27"> the Lord God of your fathers add to this number many thousands, and bless you, as he spake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:5; 1ST KINGS 3:7–9; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:16"> I may not alone sustain, or bear, your causes, and your burdens, and strives;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:21–23; NUMBERS 11:16–17"> give ye of you men wise, and witting, whose conversation is proved in your lineages, that I set &#91;or put&#93; them princes to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then ye answered to me, The thing is good which thou wilt do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:18; EXODUS 18:25–26; EPHESIANS 4:11"> And I took of your lineages men wise, and noble; and I ordained them to be your princes, your tribunes, and centurions, and quinquagenaries, and deans, which shall teach you all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 7:24; EXODUS 22:21; 2ND CHRONICLES 19:6–10"> And I commanded to them, and said, Hear ye the people, and deem ye that that is just, whether he be a citizen, or a pilgrim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:19; EXODUS 18:26; JAMES 2:9"> No difference shall be in doom of persons; ye shall hear so a little man, that is, poor, as a great man, neither ye shall take heed to the person of any man, for it is the doom of God. That if anything seemeth hard to you, tell ye that to me, and I shall hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:32; DEUTERONOMY 4:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:20"> And I commanded all things which ye ought to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:2; DEUTERONOMY 8:15; JEREMIAH 2:6"> Forsooth we went forth from Horeb, and passed by a fearedful desert, and greatest wilderness, which ye saw, by the way of the hill of Amorites, as our Lord God commanded to us. And when we had come into Kadeshbarnea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:7–8"> I said to you, Ye be come to the hill of Amorites, which your Lord God shall give to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; PSALMS 46:1; NUMBERS 14:8–9"> see thou, Israel, the land that the Lord God shall give to thee; go thou up, and wield it, as our Lord God spake to thy fathers; dread thou not, neither in thine heart be thou anything aghast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:1–20"> And all ye nighed to me, and ye said, Send we men, that shall behold the land, and shall tell us by what way we owe to go up thither, and to which cities we owe to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:3–33"> And when the word pleased to me, I sent of you twelve men, of each lineage one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:21–27; JOSHUA 2:1–2"> And when they had gone forth, and had gone up into the hilly places, they came unto the valley of Cluster; and when they had beheld the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> they took of the fruits thereof, to show the plenty of it, and they brought those fruits to us, and said, The land is good that our Lord God shall give to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:1–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; PSALMS 106:24–25"> And ye would not go up thither, but ye were unbelieveful to the word of our Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:28; PSALMS 106:25; NUMBERS 14:3"> And ye grutched in your taber-nacles, and ye said, The Lord hateth us, and therefore he led us out of the land of Egypt, that he should betake us in the hand of Amorites, and do away us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:28–33; DEUTERONOMY 9:1–2; JOSHUA 15:14"> Whither shall we ascend &#91;or go up&#93;? the messengers made afeared our heart, and said, A greatest multitude is, and larger in stature than we; the cities be great, and walled till to heaven; we saw there the sons of Anakim, that is, giants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And I said to you, Have ye no dread, nor be ye aghast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 14:14; NEHEMIAH 4:20; ROMANS 8:31"> the Lord God himself, which is your leader, shall fight for you, as he did in Egypt, while all men saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:3–4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:18; HOSEA 11:3–4"> And ye saw in the wilderness, thy Lord God bare thee, as a man is wont to bear his little son, in all the way by which ye went, till ye came to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 106:24; JUDE 1:5; HEBREWS 3:18–19"> And soothly neither so ye believed to your Lord God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:33; PSALMS 78:14; NEHEMIAH 9:12"> that went before you in the way, and measured the place in which ye ought to set your tents, and he showed in the night the way to you by fire, and in the day by a pillar of cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:22–30; PSALMS 95:11; EZEKIEL 20:15"> And when the Lord had heard the voice of your words, he was wroth, and swore, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 95:11"> None of the men of this worst generation shall see the good land, which I promised under an oath to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:24; JOSHUA 14:6–14; NUMBERS 32:12"> except Caleb, the son of Jephun-neh; forsooth he shall see it, and I shall give to him the land upon which he hath trodden, and to his sons, for he pursued &#91;or followed&#93; the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:12; DEUTERONOMY 4:21; DEUTERONOMY 34:4"> Neither the Lord’s indignation against the people is to be marvelled, since the Lord was wroth also to me for you, and said, Neither thou shalt enter thither,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:28; NUMBERS 14:30; DEUTERONOMY 31:7–8"> but Joshua, the son of Nun, thy servant, he shall enter into that land for thee; excite, and strengthen thou him, and he shall part the land by lot to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:15–16; NUMBERS 14:3; NUMBERS 14:31"> Your little children, of which ye said, that they should be led prisoners, and the sons that know not today the diversity of good and of evil, they shall enter thither; and I shall give to them the land, and they shall wield it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:25"> Soothly turn ye again, and go ye into the wilderness, by the way of the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:39–45; PROVERBS 19:3; NUMBERS 22:34"> And ye answered to me, We have sinned to the Lord; we shall go up, and we shall fight, as our Lord God commanded. And when ye were arrayed with arms, and went into the hill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:41–43; JOSHUA 7:8–13; ISAIAH 59:1–2"> the Lord said to me, Say thou to them, Do not ye go up, neither fight ye, for I am not with you, lest ye fall before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:44; ISAIAH 63:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51"> I spake this to you, and ye heard me not; but ye were adversaries to the commandment of the Lord, and swelling with pride, went up into the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 118:12; NUMBERS 14:45; NUMBERS 21:3"> Therefore Amorites went out, that dwelled in the hills, and he came against you, and pursued you, as bees be wont to pursue, and he killed you down from Seir unto Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:17; PSALMS 78:34"> And when ye turned again, and wept before the Lord, he heard not you, neither would assent to your voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:1; NUMBERS 20:22; JUDGES 11:16–17"> therefore ye sat in Kadesh by much time.</span>
</div>
