<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 11:1–3; 2ND SAMUEL 19:13; GENESIS 29:14"> And all the lineages of Israel came to David, in Hebron, and said, Lo! we be thy bone and thy flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:6; MICAH 5:4; EZEKIEL 34:23"> But also yesterday and the third day ago, when Saul was king upon us, thou leddest out, and leddest again Israel; forsooth the Lord said to thee, Thou shalt feed my people Israel, and thou shalt be duke upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 2:4; 1ST SAMUEL 16:13; 1ST SAMUEL 23:18"> Also and the elder men of Israel came to the king, into Hebron; and king David smote with them bond of peace in Hebron, before the Lord; and they anointed David into king upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:27; LUKE 3:23; 1ST KINGS 2:11"> David was a son of thirty years, when he began to reign, and he reigned forty years</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 2:11; 1ST CHRONICLES 3:4; 1ST KINGS 2:11"> in Hebron; he reigned upon Judah seven years and six months; and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty and three years upon all Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 1:8; JEREMIAH 37:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 11:4–9"> And the king went, and all &#91;the&#93; men that were with him, into Jerusalem, to Jebusites, the dwellers of the land. And it was said of them to David, Thou shalt not enter hither, no but thou do away blind men and lame, saying, David shall not enter hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:10; 2ND SAMUEL 6:16; 2ND SAMUEL 6:12"> Forsooth David took the tower of Zion; this is the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:16–17; 1ST CHRONICLES 11:6–9; 1ST SAMUEL 17:25"> For David had purposed in that day to have given meed to him, that had smitten Jebusites, and that had touched the gutters of the house roofs, and that had taken away lame men and blind, hating the life of David. Therefore it is said in common speech, A blind man and a lame shall not enter into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:15; 1ST KINGS 9:24; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:5"> And David dwelled in the tower, and called it the city of David; and he builded by compass from Millo, and within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:1; ROMANS 8:31; PSALMS 46:11"> And he entered profiting, and increasing; and the Lord God of hosts was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 14:1; 1ST KINGS 5:1–2; 1ST KINGS 5:18"> Also Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David, and cedar trees, and craftsmen of wood, and craftsmen of stones to make walls; and they builded the house of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:16; 1ST KINGS 10:9; 1ST CHRONICLES 14:2"> And David knew, that the Lord had confirmed him king upon Israel, and that he had enhanced his realm upon his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 3:9; DEUTERONOMY 17:17; 2ND CHRONICLES 13:21"> Therefore David took yet more concubines, and wives of Jerusalem, after that he came from Hebron; and also other sons and daughters were born to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 3:5–9; LUKE 2:31; 1ST CHRONICLES 14:4"> And these be the names of them that were born to him in Jerusalem; Shammuah, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 3:6; 1ST CHRONICLES 14:5"> and Ibhar, and Elishua, and Nepheg, and Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 3:8; 1ST CHRONICLES 14:7"> and Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphalet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 23:14; 1ST CHRONICLES 14:8–9; 1ST CHRONICLES 11:16"> Then the Philistines heard, that they had anointed David king upon Israel, and all the Philistines went up to seek David. And when David had heard this, he went down into a stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:8; 2ND SAMUEL 23:13; GENESIS 14:5"> And the Philistines came, and they were spread abroad in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 2:1; 1ST SAMUEL 23:2; 2ND SAMUEL 5:23"> And David counselled with the Lord, and said, Whether I shall go up to Philistines, and whether thou shalt give them into mine hand? And the Lord said to David, Go thou up, for I shall betake the Philistines, and I shall give them into thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:21"> Therefore David came into Baal-perazim, and smote them there, and said, The Lord hath parted mine enemies before me, as waters be parted. Therefore the name of that place was called Baalperazim, that is, The Field, either Plain, of Parting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 14:11–12; DEUTERONOMY 7:5; 1ST SAMUEL 5:2–6"> And they left there their sculptures, or their images, which David burnt, and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 14:13; 2ND SAMUEL 5:18; 1ST KINGS 20:22"> And &#91;the&#93; Philistines added yet, that they should ascend &#91;or go up&#93;, and they were spread abroad in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 14:14; MATTHEW 9:29–30; MATTHEW 8:23–25"> And David counselled with the Lord, and said, Whether I shall go up against the Philistines, and whether thou shalt betake them into mine hands? And the Lord answered, Thou shalt not go up against them, but compass thou them behind their back, and thou shalt come to them on the contrary side of the pear trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND KI 7:6; JUDGES 4:14; JUDGES 7:15"> And when thou shalt hear the sound of cry going in the tops of pear trees, then thou shalt begin battle; for then the Lord shall go out before thy face, that he smite the tents of Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 14:16; JOSHUA 16:10; ISAIAH 28:21"> Therefore David did as the Lord commanded to him; and he smote the Philistines from Geba till they came to Gazer.</span>
