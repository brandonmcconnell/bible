<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:3; NUMBERS 12:6; AMOS 3:7"> And it was done in that night, and lo! the word of the Lord, was made to Nathan, &#91;saying&#93;,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 23:3–32; 1ST KINGS 5:3–4; 1ST KINGS 8:16–19"> Go thou, and speak to my servant David, The Lord saith these things, Whether thou shalt build to me an house to dwell in?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:16; JOSHUA 18:1; EXODUS 40:18–19"> Soothly I have not dwelled in an house from the day in which I led the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt till into this day; but I have gone in a tabernacle and in a tent,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:6; 2ND SAMUEL 5:2; LEVITICUS 26:11–12"> by all places, to which I passed with all the sons of Israel? Whether I speaking spake to anyone of the lineages of Israel, to whom I commanded, that he should feed my people Israel, and said, Why builded-est thou not an house of cedar to me?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 78:70–71; 2ND SAMUEL 6:21; 2ND SAMUEL 12:7"> And now thou shalt say these things to my servant David, The Lord of hosts saith these things, I took thee from &#91;the&#93; pastures following flocks, that thou shouldest be duke on my people Israel,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 5:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:8; 1ST SAMUEL 18:14"> and I was with thee in all things, wherever thou hast gone, and I have killed all thine enemies from thy face, and I have made to thee a great name, by the name of great men that be in earth;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 9:15; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:9; JEREMIAH 24:6"> and I shall set a place to my people Israel, and I shall plant him, and I shall dwell with him, and he shall no more be troubled, and the sons of wickedness shall not add to, that they torment him as before,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:27; 2ND SAMUEL 7:1; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:10"> like they did from the day in which I ordained judges upon my people Israel; and I shall give rest to thee from all thine enemies. And the Lord before-saith to thee, that he shall make an house to thee;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:36"> and when thy days be fulfilled, and thou hast slept with thy fathers, that is, when thou hast died, I shall raise up thy seed after thee, which shall go out of thy womb, and I shall make steadfast his realm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 1:31–33; 1ST KINGS 8:19; 2ND SAMUEL 7:16"> He shall build an house to my name, and I shall make stable the throne of his realm till into without end;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:13"> I shall be to him into a father, and he shall be to me into a son; and if he shall do anything wickedly, I shall chastise him in the rod of men, and in the wounds of the sons of men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:23; 1ST SAMUEL 15:28; PSALMS 89:28"> Forsooth I shall not do away my mercy from him, as I did it away from Saul, whom I removed from my face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 1:32–33; PSALMS 89:36–37; 2ND SAMUEL 7:13"> And thine house shall be faithful, and thy realm shall be till into without end before my face, and thy throne shall be steadfast continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:27; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:3"> By all these words, and by all this revelation, so Nathan spake to David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 8:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:16; 1ST SAMUEL 18:18"> Forsooth David the king entered into the tabernacle, and he sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, Lord God, and what is mine house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:8–9; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:17; PSALMS 36:7"> But also this is seen little in thy sight, my Lord God; no but thou shouldest speak also of the house of thy servant into long time. For this is the law of Adam, Lord God;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 21:17; 1ST SAMUEL 16:7; PSALMS 139:1"> what therefore may David add yet, that he speak to thee? For thou, Lord God, knowest thy servant;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 115:1; PSALMS 138:2; EPHESIANS 1:9"> thou hast done all these great things, for thy word, and by thine heart, so that thou madest those known to thy servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 86:10; EXODUS 15:11; PSALMS 86:8"> Therefore, Lord God, thou art made great, for none is like thee, nor there is no God except thee, in all things which we have heard with our ears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:26; DEUTERONOMY 10:21; DEUTERONOMY 15:15"> Soothly what folk in earth is as the people of Israel, for which the Lord God went, that he should again-buy it to him into a people, and should set to himself a name, and should do to it great things, and horrible, on &#91;the&#93; earth, in casting out thereof the folks, and gods thereof, from the face of thy people, which thou again-boughtest to thee from Egypt?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:18; 1ST PETER 2:10; GENESIS 17:7"> And thou hast confirmed to thee thy people Israel into a people ever-lasting, and thou, Lord, art made into God to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 119:49; EZEKIEL 36:37; GENESIS 32:12"> Now therefore, Lord God, raise up without end the word that thou hast spoken upon thy servant, and upon his house, and do as thou hast spoken;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:23–24; PSALMS 72:18–19; MATTHEW 6:9"> and thy name be magnified or be made great till into without end, and be it said, The Lord of hosts is God upon Israel; and the house of thy servant David shall be stablished before the Lord;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:25–26; PSALMS 10:17; RUTH 4:4"> for thou, Lord of hosts, God of Israel, hast made revelation to the ear of thy servant, and saidest, I shall build an house to thee; therefore thy servant hath found by his heart, that he should pray thee by this prayer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 17:17; EXODUS 34:6; NUMBERS 23:19"> Now therefore, Lord God, thou art very God, and thy words shall be true; for thou hast spoken these good things to thy servant;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:24–26; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:27; PSALMS 115:12–15"> therefore begin thou, and bless the house of thy servant, that it be into without end before thee; for thou, Lord God, hast spoken these things, and through thy blessing the house of thy servant shall be blessed without end.</span>
