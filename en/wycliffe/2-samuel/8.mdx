<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:17; 1ST SAMUEL 10:27; 2ND SAMUEL 8:6"> And David smote Moab, and meted them with a cord, and he made them even to the earth; forsooth he meted them by two cords, one to slay, and one to quicken. And Moab served David under tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 14:47; 2ND SAMUEL 10:16; 2ND SAMUEL 10:19"> And David smote Hadadezer, the son of Rehob, king of Zobah, when he went forth to be lord over the flood Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:6; JOSHUA 11:9; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:4"> And when a thousand and seven hundred horsemen of his part were taken, and twenty thousand of foot-men, David hocked all &#91;the&#93; drawing beasts in chariots; but David left of those &#91;or them&#93; an hundred chariots, that is, the horses of an hundred chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:23–25; ISAIAH 7:8; JOB 9:13"> Also Syrians of Damascus came, that it should bear help to Hadadezer, king of Zobah; and David smote of Syrians two and twenty thousand of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:14; 2ND SAMUEL 7:9; PSALMS 18:34–46"> And David setted &#91;or put&#93; a strong-hold in Syria of Damascus, and Syria was made serving David under tribute. And the Lord kept David in all things, to whatever things he went forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:15–16; 1ST KINGS 14:26–27; 1ST KINGS 10:16–17"> And David took golden armours and bies, which the servants of Hadadezer had, and he brought those &#91;or them&#93; into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 18:8; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:16; EZEKIEL 47:16"> And of Betah, and of Berothai, the cities of Hadadezer, David &#91;the&#93; king took full much brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:65; 2ND CHRONICLES 8:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:9"> Forsooth Toi, king of Hamath, heard that David had smitten all the host of Hadadezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 13:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:10; PSALMS 129:8"> And Toi sent Joram, his son, to king David, that he should greet him, and thank, and do thankings, for he had overcome Hadadezer, and had smitten him; for Toi was enemy of Hadadezer; and vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and vessels of brass were in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:51; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:14–16"> And the same vessels king David hallowed to the Lord, with the silver and gold, which he had hallowed of all heathen men, which he had made subject</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 10:14; 2ND SAMUEL 8:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:11"> of Syria, and of Moab, and of the sons of Ammon, and of Philistines, and of Amalek, and of the spoils of Hadadezer, the son of Rehob, king of Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND KI 14:7; 2ND SAMUEL 7:9; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:12"> Also David made to him a name, when he turned again when Syria was taken, for eighteen thousand men were slain in the valley, where salt was made, and in Helam, to three and twenty thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 2nd Samuel 10:16-19.">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 27:29; GENESIS 25:23; 2ND SAMUEL 8:6"> And he setted keepers in Idumea, and ordained &#91;a&#93; stronghold, and all Idumea was made serving to David; and the Lord kept David in all things, to whatever things he went forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="AMOS 5:15; PSALMS 89:14; PSALMS 72:2"> And David reigned upon all Israel, and David did doom, and rightwise-ness to all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 4:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 11:6; 2ND SAMUEL 19:13"> And Joab, the son of Zeruiah, was over the host of David; and Jehosha-phat, the son of Ahilud, was recorder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 6:8; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:16; 1ST CHRONICLES 24:3–4"> and Zadok, the son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech, the son of Abiathar, were priests; and Seraiah was a scribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 30:14; 2ND SAMUEL 20:7; 2ND SAMUEL 20:23"> But Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, was over Cherethites and Pelethites, that is, over archers and arrowblasters; and the sons of David were priests.</span>
