<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 13:39; 2ND SAMUEL 2:18; 2ND SAMUEL 18:33"> Forsooth Joab, the son of Zeruiah, understood, that the heart of the king was turned to Absalom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 11:6; AMOS 1:1; RUTH 3:3"> and he sent to Tekoah, and took from thence a wise woman, and he said to her, Feign thee to mourn, and be thou clothed with a cloth of dole, and be thou not anointed with oil, that thou be as a woman by mourning now in full much time a dead man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:19; EXODUS 4:15; ISAIAH 51:16"> And thou shalt enter to the king, and thou shalt speak to him such manner words. And Joab put the words in her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 1:2; 2ND KI 6:26–28; 1ST SAMUEL 25:23"> Therefore when the woman of Tekoah had entered to the king, she felled before him on the earth, and worshipped, and said, O! king, keep thou me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 9:8–15; 2ND SAMUEL 12:1–3"> And the king said to her, What hast thou of cause? And she answered, Alas! I am a woman widow, for mine husband is dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 4:8; DEUTERONOMY 22:26–27; EXODUS 2:13"> and twain &#91;or two&#93; sons were of thine handmaid, which debated against themselves in the field, and none was that might forbid them, and the one smote the tother, and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:19; MATTHEW 21:38; 2ND SAMUEL 12:17"> And lo! all the kindred riseth against thine handmaid, and saith, Give thou him to us that killed his brother, that we slay him, for the life of his brother whom he killed, and that we do away the heir; and they seek to quench my spark that is left, that the name dwell not to mine husband, and that remnants be not to him on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:13; ISAIAH 11:3–4; JOB 29:16"> And the king said to the woman, Go into thine house, and I shall command for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:24; 1ST KINGS 2:33; MATTHEW 27:25"> And the woman of Tekoah said to the king, My lord the king, this wicked-ness be on me, and on the house of my father; forsooth the king and his throne be innocent or guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the king said, Bring thou him to me, that against-saith thee, and he shall no more add to, that he touch thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 14:45; MATTHEW 10:30; NUMBERS 35:19"> And she said, The king have mind on his Lord God, and the next kins-men of blood to take vengeance be not multiplied, and they shall not slay my son. And the king said, The Lord liveth, for none of the hairs of thy son shall fall upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 18:27; 1ST SAMUEL 25:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:1"> Therefore the woman said, Thine handmaid speak a word to my lord the king. And the king said, Speak thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 13:37–38; 2ND SAMUEL 12:7; JUDGES 20:2"> And the woman said, Why hast thou thought such a thing against the people of God? and the king spake this word, that he do sin, and bring not again his son who is cast out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOB 30:23; NUMBERS 35:28; HEBREWS 9:27"> All we die, and as waters that shall not turn again, we slide into the earth; and God will not that a soul perish, but he withdraweth, and thinketh, lest he perish utterly, which is cast away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Now therefore come thou, that I speak to my lord the king this word, while the people is present; and thine handmaid said, I shall speak to the king, if in any manner the king do the word of his handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 26:19"> And the king heard the words, that he should deliver his handmaid from the hands of all men, that would do away me, and my son together, from the heritage of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 29:9; 2ND SAMUEL 19:27; 2ND SAMUEL 14:20"> Therefore thine handmaid say, that the word of my lord the king be made as sacrifice, that is, that the sentence given of him be pleasant to God, as sacrifice pleaseth God; for as an angel of the Lord, so is my lord the king, that he be not moved by blessing neither by cursing. Wherefore and thy Lord God is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:25; 1ST SAMUEL 3:17–18; JEREMIAH 38:14"> And the king answered, and said to the woman, Hide thou not from me the word which I ask thee. And the woman said to him, Speak thou, my lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:3; 1ST SAMUEL 1:26; 1ST SAMUEL 20:3"> And the king said, Whether the hand of Joab is with thee in all these things? The woman answered, and said, By the health of thy soul, my lord the king, neither to the left side neither to the right side is anything of all these things, which my lord the king hath spoken. For thy servant Joab himself commanded to me, and he putted &#91;or put&#93; all these words into the mouth of thine handmaid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:17; 2ND SAMUEL 19:27; JOB 32:21–22"> that I should turn the figure of this word; for thy servant Joab commanded this thing. Forsooth thou, my lord the king, art wise, as an angel of God that hath wisdom, that thou understand all things on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:11; 1ST SAMUEL 14:39; MARK 6:26"> And the king said to Joab, Lo! I am pleased, and I have done thy word; therefore go thou, and again-call thou the child Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="RUTH 2:2; JOB 31:20; JOB 29:11"> And Joab felled upon his face to the earth, and he worshipped, and blessed the king; and Joab said, Thy servant hath understood today, that I have found grace in thine eyes, my lord king, for thou hast done the word of thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:3; 2ND SAMUEL 13:37–38"> Therefore Joab rose up, and went into Geshur, and brought Absalom into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:4; GENESIS 43:3; 2ND SAMUEL 3:13"> And the king said, Turn he again into his house, and see not he my face. Therefore Absalom turned again into his house, and saw not the face of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:35; JOB 2:7"> Soothly no man in all Israel was so fair as Absalom, and full comely; from the step of the foot unto the top, there was no wem in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 11:14; 2ND SAMUEL 18:9"> and inasmuch as he clipped more his hairs, by so much the more they waxed; but he was clipped once in the year, for his hair grieved him. And when he clipped the hairs, he weighed the hairs of his head by two hundred shekels by common weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:18; 2ND SAMUEL 13:1; ISAIAH 14:22"> And three sons, and a daughter, Tamar by name, of seemly shape, or excellent form, were born to Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Absalom dwelled in Jerusalem two years, and he saw not the face of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:30–31; ESTHER 1:12; MATTHEW 22:3"> Therefore he sent to Joab, that he should send him to the king; and Joab would not come to him. And when he had sent the second time, and Joab would not come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JUDGES 15:4–5; 2ND KI 10:6–7; 2ND KI 9:33"> Absalom said to his servants, Ye know the field of Joab beside my field having ripe barley; therefore go ye, and burn ye it &#91;up&#93; with fire. Therefore the servants of Absalom burnt the corn with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Joab rose up, and came to Absalom into his house, and said, Why have thy servants burnt &#91;up&#93; my corn with fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:8; EXODUS 14:12; PROVERBS 28:13"> And Absalom answered to Joab, I sent to thee, and besought that thou shouldest come to me, and that I should send thee to the king, that thou shouldest say to him, Why came I from Geshur? It was better to me to have been there; therefore I beseech, that I see the face of the king, that if he is mindful of my wickedness, slay he me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 33:4; LUKE 15:20; GENESIS 27:26"> Joab entered to the king, and told to him. And Absalom was called, and he entered to the king, and he worshipped on the face of &#91;the&#93; earth before him, and the king kissed Absalom.</span>
