<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 59:7–8; PSALMS 109:2–4; PSALMS 3:3–5"> Then Ahithophel said to Absalom, I shall choose to me twelve thousand of men, and I shall rise up, and pursue David in this night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:14; 1ST KINGS 22:31; ZECHARIAH 13:7"> And I shall fall on him, for he is weary, and with unbound hands I shall smite him. And when all the people fleeth that is with him, I shall smite the king left alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:3; ISAIAH 48:22; ISAIAH 57:21"> And I shall lead again all the people, as one man is wont to be turned again; for thou seekest one man, and all the people shall be in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 18:20–21; ROMANS 1:32; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:4"> And the words of Ahithophel pleased Absalom, and all the greater men in birth of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:32–37; 2ND SAMUEL 16:16–19"> And Absalom said, Call ye also Hushai of Archi, and hear we what also he saith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And when Hushai had come to Absalom, Absalom said to him, Ahith-ophel hath spoken such a word; owe we to do thereafter, either nay? what counsel givest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:8"> And Hushai said to Absalom, This is not good counsel, that Ahith-ophel hath given in this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HOSEA 13:8; 1ST SAMUEL 16:18; HEBREWS 11:32–34"> And again Hushai said, Thou knowest, that thy father, and the men that be with him, be most strong, and in bitter soul, as if a she bear is fierce in the forest, when her whelps be ravished from her; but also thy father is a man warrior, and he shall not dwell with the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 24:3; 1ST SAMUEL 14:14–15; JUDGES 20:32–33"> In hap now he is hid, either lurketh, in ditches, either in one place, in which he will hide him; and when any man falleth in the beginning, whoever shall hear it, he shall hear, and shall say, Vengeance is done in the people that pursued Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:9–11; EXODUS 15:15; GENESIS 49:9"> And each full strong man, whose heart is as the heart of a lion, shall be discomforted for dread; for all the people of Israel knoweth, that thy father is strong, and that all the men be strong, that be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; JUDGES 20:1; PSALMS 9:16"> But this seemeth to me to be rightful &#91;or right&#93; counsel; all Israel be gathered to thee, from Dan till to Beersheba, unnumberable as the sand of the sea; and thou shalt be in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 23:23; OBADIAH 1:3; 1ST KINGS 20:10"> And we shall fall upon him, in whatever place he is found, and we shall cover him, as dew is wont to fall on the earth; and we shall not leave of the men that be with him, soothly not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:2; MICAH 1:6"> That if he entereth into any city, all Israel shall compass that city with ropes, and we shall draw it into the strand &#91;or stream&#93;, yea that nothing be found, soothly not a little stone thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:34; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:19–20; PROVERBS 19:21"> And Absalom said, and all the men of Israel, The counsel of Hushai of Archi is better than the counsel of Ahithophel; and the profitable counsel of Ahithophel was destroyed by God’s will, that the Lord should bring in evil on Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:35–36"> And Hushai said to Zadok and to Abiathar, the priests, Ahithophel gave counsel to Absalom, and to the elder men of Israel in this and this manner, and I gave such and such counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:54; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:4"> Now therefore send ye soon, and tell ye to David, and say ye, Dwell thou not this night in &#91;the&#93; field places of the desert, but pass thou &#91;over&#93; without delay; lest peradventure the king be swallowed up, and all the people that is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:36; JOSHUA 18:16; 2ND SAMUEL 15:27"> And Jonathan and Ahimaaz stood beside the well of Rogel; and an handmaid went, and told to them, and they went forth to tell the message to king David; for they might not be seen, neither enter into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:16; 2ND SAMUEL 16:5; 2ND SAMUEL 19:16"> And a child saw them, and he showed it to Absalom; and they entered with swift going into the house of a man in Bahurim, that had a pit or well in his place, and they went down into that pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 1:19; JOSHUA 2:4–24"> And a woman took, and spread abroad a covering over the mouth of the pit, as if drying barley with the peel taken away, and so the thing was hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 1:19; JOSHUA 2:22–23; 1ST SAMUEL 19:12–17"> And when the servants of Absalom had come into the house, they said to the woman, Where is Ahimaaz and Jonathan? And the woman answered to them, They passed the river of waters, that is, Jordan. And when they that sought them had not found them, they turned again into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 17:15–16"> And when they had gone forth, they went up from the pit; and they went, and told to king David, and said, Rise ye up, and passeth soon &#91;over&#93; the flood, for Ahithophel hath given such counsel against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:16; JOHN 18:9; PROVERBS 27:12"> Therefore David rose up, and all the people that was with him, and they passed over Jordan, till it was clear day, before that the word was published; and soothly not one was left, that passed not over the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:5; 2ND KI 20:1; 2ND SAMUEL 15:12"> And Ahithophel saw, that his counsel was not done, and he saddled his ass, and rose up, and went into his house, and into his city; and when his house was disposed, he perished by hanging himself, and he was buried in the sepulchre of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 32:2; 2ND SAMUEL 2:8; JOSHUA 13:26"> And David came into the castles, and Absalom passed &#91;over&#93; Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 2:13; 2ND SAMUEL 19:13; 2ND SAMUEL 20:9–12"> And Absalom ordained Amasa for Joab upon his host; and Amasa was the son of a man that was called Ithra of Jezreel, the which entered to Abigail, the daughter of Nahash, and the sister of Zeruiah, that was the mother of Joab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:1; DEUTERONOMY 3:15; NUMBERS 32:1–42"> And Israel setted tents with Absalom in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:7; 2ND SAMUEL 10:1–2; 2ND SAMUEL 9:4"> And when David had come into the castles or Mahanaim, Shobi, the son of Nahash of Rabbah, of the sons of Ammon, and Machir, the son of Ammiel, of Lodebar, and Barzillai, of Gilead, of Rogelim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:18; OBADIAH 1:3; 2ND SAMUEL 16:1–2"> brought to him beddings, and tapets, and earthen vessels, and wheat, and barley, and meal, and flour, and beans, and lentils or vetches, and fried chickpeas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:2; ISAIAH 21:14; 2ND SAMUEL 16:14"> and honey, and butter, and sheep, and fat calves. And they gave those to David, and to the people that were with him, to eat; for they supposed the people to be made faint for hunger and thirst in desert.</span>
