</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:19; JAMES 1:14–15; PSALMS 51:1–19"> Then by messengers sent, David took her; and when she entered to him, he slept with her, and anon she was hallowed from her uncleanness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, from flux of unclean blood that should come till to the child bearing, for she conceived in that lying-by.">a</a></sup>. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:22; PROVERBS 6:34"> with a child conceived; and she sent, and told to David, and said, I have conceived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; GENESIS 4:7; 1ST SAMUEL 15:30"> And David sent to Joab, and said, Send thou Uriah the Hittite to me; and Joab sent Uriah to David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 37:14; 1ST SAMUEL 17:22; GENESIS 29:6"> And Uriah came to David; and David asked, how rightfully Joab did and the people, and how the battle was administered, or served.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 18:4; GENESIS 43:34; ISAIAH 29:15"> And David said to Uriah, Go into thine house, and wash thy feet. &#91;And&#93; Uriah went out from the house of the king, and the king’s meat pursued &#91;or followed&#93; him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30; JOB 5:12–14"> Soothly Uriah slept before the gate of the king’s house with other servants of his lord, and went not down to his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And it was told to David of men, saying, Uriah went not to his house. And David said to Uriah, Whether thou camest not from the way? why wentest thou not down into thine house?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:2; 2ND SAMUEL 20:6; 2ND SAMUEL 7:6"> And Uriah said to David, The ark of God, &#91;and&#93; Israel, and Judah dwell in tents, and my lord Joab, and the servants of my lord dwell upon the face of the earth, and shall I go into mine house, to eat and drink, and sleep with my wife? By thine health, and by the health of thy soul, I shall not do this thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:37; JEREMIAH 2:22–23"> Therefore David said to Uriah, Dwell thou here also today, and tomorrow I shall deliver thee. Uriah dwelled in Jerusalem in that day, and the tother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:15; GENESIS 19:32–35; PROVERBS 20:1"> And David called him, that he should eat and drink before him, and David made drunken Uriah; and he went out in the eventide, and slept in his bed with the servants of his lord; and went not down into his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:9; 1ST KINGS 21:8–10; PSALMS 19:13"> Therefore when the morrowtide was made, David wrote &#91;an&#93; epistle to Joab, and sent by the hand of Uriah,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:9; PSALMS 51:4; JEREMIAH 20:13"> and wrote in the epistle, Put ye Uriah even against the battle, where the battle is strongest, that is, where the adversaries be strongest, and forsake ye him, that he be smitten and perish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:12; 1ST SAMUEL 22:17–19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:29"> Therefore when Joab besieged the city, he setted &#91;or put&#93; Uriah in the place where he knew that strongest men were.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:9; PSALMS 51:14"> And &#91;the&#93; men went out of the city, and fought against Joab, and they killed of the people of the servants of David, and also Uriah the Hittite was dead there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Therefore Joab sent, and told all the words of the battle;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and he commanded to the mes-senger, and said, When thou hast fulfilled all the words of the battle to the king,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> if thou seest, that he is wroth, and saith, Why nighed ye to the wall to fight? whether ye knew not, that many darts, or arrows, be sent out from the wall above?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 7:1; JUDGES 9:50–54; 2ND SAMUEL 3:27"> who smote Abimelech, the son of Jerubbesheth? whether not a woman sent on him a gobbet of a millstone from the wall, and killed him in Thebez? why nighed ye beside the wall? thou shalt say, Also thy servant, Uriah the Hittite, died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Therefore the messenger went, &#91;and came&#93;, and told to David all things which Joab had commanded to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the messenger said to David, &#91;The&#93; Men had the mastery against us, and they went out to us into the field; and with great fierceness we pursued them unto the gate of the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And &#91;the&#93; archers sent darts to thy servants from the wall above, and some of the king’s servants be dead; and also thy servant, Uriah the Hittite, is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:26; JOSHUA 7:8–9; ECCLESIASTES 9:1–3"> And David said to the messenger, Thou shalt say these things to Joab, This thing break not thee; for the hap of battle is diverse, and sword wasteth now this man, &#91;and&#93; now that man; comfort thy fighters against the city, that thou destroy it, and stir or excite thou them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:2; GENESIS 27:41; 2ND SAMUEL 3:31"> And the wife of Uriah heard, that Uriah her husband was dead, and she bewailed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4–5; 2ND SAMUEL 12:9; DEUTERONOMY 22:29"> And when the mourning was passed, David sent, and brought her into his house; and she was made wife to him, and she childed a son to him.
