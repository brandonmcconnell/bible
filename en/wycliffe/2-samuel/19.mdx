<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:5; 2ND SAMUEL 18:14; PROVERBS 17:25"> Forsooth it was told to Joab, that the king wept, and bewailed his son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:12; PROVERBS 16:15"> and the victory in that day was turned into mourning to all the people; for the people heard, that it was said in that day, The king maketh sorrow on his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 31:27; 2ND SAMUEL 19:32; 2ND SAMUEL 17:24"> And the people eschewed to enter into the city in that day, as the people turned and fleeing from &#91;the&#93; battle is wont to bow away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:33; 2ND SAMUEL 15:30"> And the king covered his head, and cried with great voice, My son, Absalom! Absalom, my son!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 18:47–48; PSALMS 3:8; NEHEMIAH 9:27"> Therefore Joab entered to the king into the house, and said, Thou hast shamed today the cheers of all thy servants, that have made safe thy life, and the life of thy sons and of thy daughters, and the life of thy wives, and the life of thy secondary wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:5; 2ND SAMUEL 3:24–25; PROVERBS 19:9–10"> Thou lovest them that hate thee, and thou hatest them that love thee; and thou hast showed today that thou reckest not of thy dukes and of thy servants; and verily I have known now, that if Absalom lived, and all we had been dead, then it should please thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:28; GENESIS 34:3; PROVERBS 19:15"> Now therefore rise up, and go thou forth, and speak thou, and make satisfaction to thy servants; for I swear to thee by the Lord, that if thou shalt not go forth, soothly not one man shall dwell with thee in this night; and this shall be worse to thee, than all the evils that came &#91;up&#93; on thee from thy young waxing age till into this present time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:24; 2ND SAMUEL 18:4; 2ND SAMUEL 19:3"> Therefore the king rose up, and sat in the gate; and it was told to all the people, that the king sat in the gate, and all the multitude came before the king. Forsooth Israel fled into their tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:14; GENESIS 3:12–13; 2ND SAMUEL 8:1–14"> And all the people strived in all the lineages of Israel, and said, The king delivered us from the hand of all our enemies, and he saved us from the hand of Philistines; and now he hath fled from the land for Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:14; JUDGES 18:9; HOSEA 8:4"> Certainly Absalom, whom we anointed upon us, is dead in battle; how long be ye still, and bring not again the king? And the counsel of all Israel came to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:29; 1ST KINGS 2:35; 2ND SAMUEL 15:35–36"> And king David sent to Zadok and to Abiathar, the priests, and said, Speak ye to the greater men in birth of Judah, and say ye, Why came ye the last to bring again the king into his house? Soothly the word of all Israel came to the king, that they would bring him again into his house. For the king said, Ye shall say these things to the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 5:1; GENESIS 2:23; EPHESIANS 5:30"> Ye be my brethren, ye be my bone and my flesh; why the last bring ye again the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 17:25; 1ST KINGS 19:2; RUTH 1:17"> And say ye to Amasa, Whether thou art not my bone and my flesh? God do these things to me, and add these things too, if thou shalt not be master of chivalry, before me in all time after Joab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:32; PSALMS 110:2–3"> And David bowed to him the heart of all &#91;the&#93; men of Judah as of one man; and they sent to the king, and said, Turn thou again, and all thy servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:9; 1ST SAMUEL 11:14–15"> And the king turned again, and came till to Jordan; and all Judah came till into Gilgal to meet the king, and to lead him over the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:8; 2ND SAMUEL 16:5–13; 1ST KINGS 2:36–46"> But Shimei, the son of Gera, the son of Benjamin, of Bahurim, hasted, and came down with the men of Judah into the meeting of king David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:1–4; 2ND SAMUEL 9:2; 2ND SAMUEL 9:10"> with a thousand men of Benjamin; and Ziba, the child of the house of Saul, and fifteen sons of him, and twenty servants were with him; and they brake into the Jordan, before the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 81:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:9; PSALMS 66:3"> and they passed over the fords, that they should lead over the house of the king, and do by the behest of the king. Soothly Shimei, the son of Gera, kneeled before the king, when he had passed now Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 22:15; 2ND SAMUEL 13:20; 2ND SAMUEL 13:33"> and said to the king, My lord the king, areckon thou not wickedness to me, neither have thou mind of the wrongs of thy servant in the day, in which thou, my lord the king, wentest out of Jerusalem, neither set thou, king, those wrongs in thine heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:25; PSALMS 78:34–37; GENESIS 48:14"> for I thy servant acknowledge my sin; and therefore today I came the first of all the house of Joseph, and I came down into the meeting of my lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 22:28; 1ST SAMUEL 26:9; 1ST KINGS 21:10–11"> And Abishai, the son of Zeruiah, answered and said, Whether Shimei, that cursed the christ of the Lord, shall not be slain for these words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 11:13; 2ND SAMUEL 16:10; 2ND SAMUEL 3:39"> And David said, What is it to me and to you, ye sons of Zeruiah? Why be ye made to me today into Satan, that is, adversary? Therefore whether a man shall be slain today in Israel? Whether I know not me made king today on Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:46; 1ST KINGS 2:8–9; 1ST KINGS 2:37"> And the king said to Shimei, Thou shalt not die; and the king swore to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 41:5; MATTHEW 6:16; 2ND SAMUEL 15:30"> Also Mephibosheth, the son of Saul, came down with unwashed feet, and with his beard unclipped, into the coming of the king. And Mephibosheth had not washed his clothes, from the day in which the king went out of Jerusalem till to the day of his coming again in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:17"> And when at Jerusalem he had come to the king, the king said to him, Mephibosheth, why camest thou not with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:2–3; 2ND SAMUEL 4:4; 2ND SAMUEL 9:3"> And he answered and said, My lord the king, my servant despised me; and I thy servant said to him, that he should saddle the ass to me, and I should ascend, and I should go with the king; for I thy servant am crooked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:17; 2ND SAMUEL 14:20; 1ST SAMUEL 29:9"> Moreover and he accused me, thy servant, to thee, my lord the king; but thou, my lord the king, art as the angel of God; do thou that, that is pleasant &#91;or pleasing&#93; to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 9:13; 2ND SAMUEL 9:10; 2ND SAMUEL 21:6–9"> For the house of my father was no but guilty to &#91;the&#93; death to my lord the king; soothly thou hast set &#91;or puttest&#93; me thy servant among the guests of thy board; what therefore have I of just complaint, either what may I more cry to the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:15; PSALMS 101:5; PSALMS 82:2"> And the king said to him, What speakest thou more? that that I have spoken is steadfast; thou and Ziba part the possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:24; 2ND SAMUEL 1:26"> And Mephibosheth answered to the king, Yea, take he all things, after that my lord the king turned again peaceably into his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:7; NEHEMIAH 7:63; EZRA 2:61"> Also Barzillai of Gilead, a full eld &#91;or old&#93; man, came down from Rogelim, and led the king over Jordan, ready also to pursue &#91;or follow&#93; him over the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:2; JOB 1:3; PROVERBS 16:31"> And Barzillai of Gilead was full eld &#91;or old&#93;, that is, of fourscore years; and he gave meats to the king, when the king dwelled in castles; for Barzillai was a full rich man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:34–40; LUKE 22:28–30; 2ND SAMUEL 9:11"> And so the king said to Barzillai, Come thou with me, that thou rest securely with me in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JAMES 4:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:29; GENESIS 47:8–9"> And Barzillai said to the king, How many be the days of &#91;the&#93; years of my life, that I go up with the king into Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:33; EZRA 2:65; PSALMS 90:10"> I am of fourscore years today; whether my wits be quick to deem sweet thing either bitter, either meat and drink may delight thy servant, either may I hear more the voice of singers either of singsters? Why is thy servant to be a charge to my lord the king?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LUKE 6:38"> I thy servant shall go forth a little from the Jordan with thee, I have no need to this yielding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:7; JEREMIAH 41:17; 2ND SAMUEL 19:40"> but I beseech thee, that I thy servant turn again, and die in my city, and be buried beside the sepulchre of my father and of my mother; forsooth my son Chimham is thy servant, my lord the king, go he with thee, and do thou to him that that seemeth good to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Therefore the king said to Bar-zillai, Chimham go forth with me; and I shall do to him whatever thing pleaseth thee, and thou shalt get all thing, that thou askest of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 31:55; RUTH 1:14; GENESIS 47:7"> And when all the people and the king had passed &#91;over&#93; Jordan, the king abode; and the king kissed Barzillai, and blessed him; and he turned again into his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:9; GENESIS 49:10; 2ND SAMUEL 19:11–15"> Then the king passed forth into Gilgal, and Chimham with him. And all the people of Judah had led the king over, and the half part only of the people of Israel was present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JUDGES 8:1; JUDGES 12:1; JOHN 7:5–6"> Therefore all the men of Israel came together to the king, and said to him, Why have our brethren, the men of Judah, stolen thee, and have led the king and his house over Jordan, and all the men of David with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 19:12; 2ND SAMUEL 5:1; 1ST CHRONICLES 2:3–17"> And each man of Judah answered to the men of Israel, For the king is near of kin to me; why art thou wroth upon this thing? Whether we have eaten anything of the king’s, either gifts be given to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:20; 2ND SAMUEL 5:1; 1ST KINGS 11:30–31"> And a man of Israel answered to the men of Judah, and said, I am greater by ten parts to the king, and David pertaineth more to me than to thee; why hast thou done wrong to me, and it was not told to me the former, that I should bring again my king? Forsooth the men of Judah answered harder to the men of Israel.</span>
