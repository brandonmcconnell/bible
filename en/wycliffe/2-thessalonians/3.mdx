<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:25; COLOSSIANS 4:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:9"> Brethren, from henceforward pray ye for us, that the word of God run, and be clarified, as it is with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:3–4; ROMANS 15:31; DEUTERONOMY 32:20"> and that we be delivered from noxious and evil men; for faith is not of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:18; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:24; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:13"> But the Lord is true, that shall confirm you, and shall keep &#91;us&#93; from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:6; PHILEMON 1:21; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:10"> And, brethren, we trust of you in the Lord, for whatever things we command to you, both ye do and shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 119:36; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:18; DEUTERONOMY 30:6"> And the Lord address your hearts, in the charity of God, and in the patience of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:10–11; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:7; ROMANS 16:17"> But, brethren, we announce to you in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye withdraw you from each brother that wandereth out of order, &#91;or against good order&#93;, and not after the teaching, that they received of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:6; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:16"> For ye yourselves know, how it behooveth to pursue us. For we were not unpeaceable &#91;or unquiet&#93; among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:9; EPHESIANS 4:28"> neither without our own travail, we ate bread of any man, but in travail and weariness wrought night and day, that we grieved none of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:7; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:4–14"> Not as we had not power, but that we should give us-selves ensample to you to pursue us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 3:19; PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 20:4"> For also when we were among you, we announced this thing to you, that if any man will not work, neither eat he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:13; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:6; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:11"> For we have heard that some among you go unrestfully, &#91;or unquietly, or unpeaceably&#93;, and nothing work, but do curiously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:28; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:11; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:8"> But we announce to them that be such men, and beseech in the Lord Jesus Christ, that they work with silence, and eat their own bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:9–10; HEBREWS 12:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:1"> But do not ye, brethren, fail well-doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TITUS 3:10; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:11"> That if any man obey not to our word &#91;sent&#93; by epistle, mark ye him, and commune ye not with him, that he be ashamed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TITUS 3:10; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:5"> and do not ye guess him as an enemy, but reprove ye him as a brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 29:11; JOHN 16:33; MATTHEW 1:23"> And God himself of peace give to you everlasting peace in all place. The Lord be with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 16:21; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:5; COLOSSIANS 4:18"> My salutation by the hand of Paul; which sign in each epistle I write thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 16:20; ROMANS 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.</span>
