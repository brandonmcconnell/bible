<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 1:6; 2ND PETER 1:12–15; 1ST PETER 1:22"> Lo! ye most dearworthy brethren, I write to you this second epistle, in which I stir your clear soul by admonishing together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDE 1:17; 2ND PETER 2:21; LUKE 1:70"> that ye be mindful of those words, that I before-said of the holy prophets, and of the commandments of the holy apostles of the Lord and Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDE 1:18; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:1–2; 2ND PETER 2:10"> First know ye this thing, that in the last days deceivers &#91;or scorners&#93; shall come in deceit, going after their own covetings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:15; MALACHI 2:17; JEREMIAH 5:12–13"> saying, Where is the promise, or the coming of him? for since the fathers died &#91;or slept&#93;, all things last from the beginning of creature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:3; GENESIS 1:9; PSALMS 24:2"> But it is hid from them willing this thing, that heavens were before &#91;or first&#93;, and the earth of water was standing by water, by God’s word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:38–39; 2ND PETER 2:5; LUKE 17:27"> by which that same world cleansed, then by water perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:10; ISAIAH 51:6; 2ND PETER 3:12"> But the heavens that now be, and the earth, be kept by the same word, and be reserved to fire into the day of doom, and perdition of wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 90:4; ROMANS 11:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:1"> But, ye most dear, this one thing be not hid to you &#91;or be not unknown&#93;, that one day with God is as a thousand years, and a thousand years be as one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:4; ROMANS 2:4; HABAKKUK 2:3"> The Lord tarrieth not his promise, as some guess, but he doeth patiently for you, and will not that any man perish &#91;or not willing any to perish&#93;, but that all turn again to penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:1; MATTHEW 24:35; 2ND PETER 3:7"> For the day of the Lord shall come as a thief, in which heavens with great rush, &#91;or fierceness&#93;, shall pass, and elements shall be dissolved by heat, and the earth, and all the works that be in it, shall be burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:15; PHILIPPIANS 1:27; 1ST PETER 2:12"> Therefore when all these things shall be dissolved, what manner men behooveth it you to be in holy livings and piety,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:10; ISAIAH 34:4; MICAH 1:4"> abiding and hieing into the coming of the day of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom heavens burning shall be dissolved, and elements shall fail by burning &#91;or heat&#93; of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:1; ISAIAH 66:22"> Also we abide by his promises new heavens and new earth, in which rightwiseness dwelleth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:23; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:13; PHILIPPIANS 1:10"> For which thing, ye most dear, abiding these things, be ye busy to be found to him in peace, unspotted and undefouled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 2:4; 2ND PETER 3:9; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:16"> And deem ye long abiding of our Lord Jesus Christ your health, as also our most dear brother Paul wrote to you, by wisdom given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:6; HEBREWS 5:11; JAMES 1:8"> As and in all his epistles he speaketh in them of these things; in which be some hard things to under-stand, which unwise &#91;or untaught&#93; and unstable men deprave, as also they do other scriptures, to their own perdition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:14; COLOSSIANS 2:8; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:13–15"> Therefore ye, brethren, before-witting keep yourselves, lest ye be deceived &#91;or over-led&#93; by error of unwise men, and fall away from your own firmness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:10; EPHESIANS 4:15; 1ST PETER 2:2"> But wax ye in the grace and the knowing of our Lord Jesus Christ and our Saviour; to him be glory now and into the day of everlastingness. Amen.</span>
