<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:15; JUDE 1:4; DEUTERONOMY 13:1–3"> But also false prophets were in the people, as in you shall be masters liars, that shall bring in sects of perdition; and they deny that Lord that bought them, and bring on them-selves hasty perdition &#91;or damnation&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 2:24; JUDE 1:10; 1ST PETER 2:12"> And many shall pursue their lecheries, by whom the way of truth shall be blasphemed;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 2:17; DEUTERONOMY 32:35; ROMANS 16:18"> and they shall make merchandise of you in covetousness by feigned words. To whom doom now a while ago ceaseth not, and the perdition of them nappeth not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDE 1:6; MATTHEW 25:41; ROMANS 8:32"> For if God spared not angels sinning, but betook them to be tormented, and to be drawn down with bonds of hell into hell, to be kept into doom;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:7; 2ND PETER 3:6; JUDE 1:14–15"> and spared not the first world, but kept Noah, the eighth man, the before-goer of rightwiseness, and brought in the great flood &#91;or the deluge&#93; to the world of unfaithful men;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDE 1:7; LUKE 17:28–30; DEUTERONOMY 29:23"> and he drove into powder the cities of men of Sodom and of men of Gomorrah, and condemned by turn-ing upside-down, and put them the ensample of them that were to doing evil &#91;or were doing unpiously&#93;;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 19:16; GENESIS 19:29; GENESIS 19:7–8"> and delivered the just Lot, oppressed of the wrong, and of the lecherous conversation of cursed men;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 119:158; EZEKIEL 9:4; PSALMS 119:136"> for in sight and hearing he was just, and dwelled amongst them that from day into day tormented with wicked works a just soul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 34:15–19; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:10"> For the Lord knoweth how to deliver pious men from temptation, and keep wicked men into the day of doom, to be tormented;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDE 1:16; JUDE 1:10; JUDE 1:18"> but more them that walk after the flesh, in coveting of uncleanness, and despise lordshipping, and be bold, pleasing themselves, and dread not to bring in sects, blaspheming;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDE 1:9; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:7; PSALMS 104:4"> where angels, when they be more in strength and virtue, bear not the execrable doom &#91;or the cursed judgement&#93; against them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDE 1:10; JEREMIAH 12:3; GALATIANS 6:8"> But these be as unreasonable beasts, kindly, into taking, and into death, blaspheming in these things that they know not, and shall perish in their corruption,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 13:13; ROMANS 2:8–9; HEBREWS 2:2–3"> and receive the hire of unright-wiseness. And they guess delights of defouling and of wem, to be likings of the day, flowing in their feasts with delights, doing lechery with you,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:3; 2ND PETER 2:18; EPHESIANS 2:3"> and have eyes full of adultery, and unceasing trespass, deceiving unsteadfast souls, and have the heart exercised to covetousness; the sons of cursing,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:14; JUDE 1:11; DEUTERONOMY 23:4–5"> that forsake the right way, and erred, pursuing the way of Balaam of Bosor, which loved the hire of wickedness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:21–33; ECCLESIASTES 7:25; LUKE 16:11"> But he had reproving &#91;or correction&#93; of his madness; a dumb beast under yoke, that spake with voice of man, that forbade the unwisdom of the prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDE 1:12–13; EPHESIANS 4:14; JEREMIAH 14:3"> These be wells without water, and mists driven with whirlwinds &#91;or clouds driven with whirling winds&#93;, to whom the thick mist of darknesses is reserved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:20; JUDE 1:15–16; ROMANS 13:13"> And they speak in pride of vanity, and deceive in desires of flesh of lechery them, that escape a little. Which live in error,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 8:34; ROMANS 6:16–22; GALATIANS 5:13"> and promise freedom &#91;or liberty&#93; to them, when they be servants of corruption. For of whom any man is overcome, of him also he is servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:26–27; HEBREWS 6:4–8; PHILIPPIANS 3:19"> For if men forsake the unclean-nesses of the world, by the knowing of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, and again be enwrapped in these, and be overcome, the latter things be made to them worse than the former.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:24; HEBREWS 6:4–6; JAMES 4:17"> For it was better to them to not know the way of rightwiseness, than to turn again after the knowing, from that holy commandment that was betaken to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:11"> For that very proverb befelled to them, The hound turned again to his vomit, or casting, and a sow is washed in wallowing in fen &#91;or in clay&#93;.</span>
</div>
</div>
