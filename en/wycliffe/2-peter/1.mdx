<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TITUS 2:13; ROMANS 3:21–26; TITUS 1:4"> Simon Peter, servant and apostle of Jesus Christ, to them that have taken with us the even-faith, in the rightwiseness of our God and Saviour Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:18; JOHN 17:3; PHILIPPIANS 3:8"> grace and peace be &#91;full&#93;-filled to you, by the knowing of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:10; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:9; ROMANS 8:28–30"> How all things of his god-like virtue, that be to life and piety, be given to us, by the knowing of him, that called us for his own glory and virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:23–24; EZEKIEL 36:25–27; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:18"> By whom he gave to us most precious promises; that by these things ye shall be made fellows of God’s kind, and flee the corruption of that covetousness, that is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:8; PROVERBS 4:23; 2ND PETER 3:18"> And bring ye in all busyness, and minister in your faith virtue, and in virtue knowing &#91;or science&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:36; ROMANS 5:3–4; LUKE 21:19"> in knowing &#91;or in science&#93;, abstinence, in abstinence patience, in patience piety;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:1; ROMANS 12:10; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:12"> in piety, love of brotherhood, and in love of brotherhood charity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="TITUS 3:14; JOHN 15:2; HEBREWS 6:12"> For if these be with you, and overcome, &#91;or be plenteous&#93;, they shall not make you void, neither without fruit, in the knowing of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:18–20; EPHESIANS 5:26; TITUS 2:14"> But to whom these be not ready, he is blind, and gropeth with his hand, and forgetteth the purging of his old trespasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:17; LUKE 6:47–49; HEBREWS 6:11"> Wherefore, brethren, be ye more busy, that by good works ye make your calling and choosing certain; for ye doing these things, shall not do sin any time &#91;or shall not sin any time&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:10; MATTHEW 25:34; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:21"> For thus the entering into &#91;the&#93; everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, shall be ministered to you plenteously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:21; ROMANS 15:14–15; PHILIPPIANS 3:1"> For which thing I shall begin to admonish you evermore of these things; and I will, that ye be know ing, and confirmed in this present truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:1; 2ND PETER 1:14; HEBREWS 13:3"> Forsooth I deem justly &#91;or I deem just&#93;, as long as I am in this taber-nacle, to raise you in admonishing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:6; JOHN 21:18–19; DEUTERONOMY 31:14"> and I am certain, that the putting away &#91;or the putting off&#93; of my tabernacle is swift, by this that our Lord Jesus Christ hath showed to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:19–29; PSALMS 71:18; JOSHUA 24:24–29"> But I shall give busyness, that oft after my death ye have mind of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:14; JOHN 1:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:4"> For we not pursuing unwise tales, have made known to you the virtue and the before-knowing &#91;or the prescience&#93; of our Lord Jesus Christ; but we were made beholders of his greatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 9:7; MATTHEW 17:5; MATTHEW 3:17"> For he took of God the Father honour and glory, by such manner voice slid down to him from the great glory, This is my loved Son, in whom I have pleased to me; hear ye him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:6; JOSHUA 5:15; EXODUS 3:5"> And we heard this voice brought from heaven, when we were with him in the holy hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:16; ISAIAH 9:2; PSALMS 119:105"> And we have a firmer word of prophecy, to which ye giving attention do well, as to a lantern that giveth light in a dark place, till the day begin to give light, and the day star spring in your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:3; ROMANS 12:6; ROMANS 6:6"> And first understand ye this thing, that each prophecy of scripture is not made by proper, &#91;or own&#93;, interpretation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:15–17; 1ST PETER 1:11; 2ND SAMUEL 23:2"> for prophecy was not brought any time by man’s will, but the holy men of God inspired with the Holy Ghost spake.</span>
