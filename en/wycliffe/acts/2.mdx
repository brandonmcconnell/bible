<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:11; JOHN 1:32–33; JEREMIAH 23:29"> And diverse tongues &#91;or tongues diversely parted&#93; as fire appeared to them, and it sat on each of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARK 16:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:52; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:31"> And all were filled with the Holy Ghost, and they began to speak in diverse languages &#91;or with diverse tongues&#93;, as the Holy Ghost gave to them to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 2:25; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:2; COLOSSIANS 1:23"> And there were in Jerusalem dwelling Jews, religious men, of each nation that is under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:3; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:2"> And when this voice was made, the multitude came together, and were astonied, &#91;or was confounded&#93;, in thought, for each man heard them speaking in his own language &#91;+or in his own tongue&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:11; JOHN 7:52"> And all were astonied, and wondered, and said together, Whether not all these that speak be men of Galilee &#91;or be Galileans&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and how heard we &#91;or how have we heard&#93; each man our language in which we be born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:4"> Parthians, and Medes, and Elamites, and they that dwell at &#91;or in&#93; Mesopotamia, Judea, and Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:6; ZECHARIAH 8:23; ZECHARIAH 8:20"> Phrygia, and Pamphylia, Egypt, and the parts of Libya, that is about Cyrene, and comelings, Romans, and Jews, and proselytes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 12:10; EXODUS 15:11; PSALMS 78:4"> men of Crete, and of Arabia, we have heard them speaking in our languages &#91;or our tongues&#93; the great things of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 15:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:20; LUKE 18:36"> And all were astonied, and wondered, saying together, What will this thing be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 14:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:15; 1ST SAMUEL 1:14"> And others scorned, and said, For these men be full of must.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:7; ISAIAH 51:1; DEUTERONOMY 27:9"> But Peter stood with the eleven, and raised up his voice, and spake to them, Ye Jews, and all that dwell at Jerusalem, be this known to you, and with ears perceive ye my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:5–8; 1ST SAMUEL 1:15; MATTHEW 20:3"> For not as ye ween &#91;or as ye guess&#93;, these be drunken, when it is the third hour of the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOEL 2:28–32"> but this it is, that was said by the prophet Joel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOEL 2:28–32; ISAIAH 44:3; TITUS 3:4–6"> And it shall be in the last days, the Lord saith, I shall pour out my Spirit on each flesh &#91;or on all flesh&#93;; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your young men shall see visions, and your elders shall dream swevens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:28; COLOSSIANS 3:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 7:21–22"> And on my servants and on mine handmaidens in those days I shall pour out of my Spirit, and they shall prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOEL 2:30–31; ZEPHANIAH 1:14–18; MALACHI 4:1–6"> And I shall give great wonders in heaven above, and signs in earth beneath, blood, and fire, and heat &#91;or vapour&#93; of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:10; AMOS 8:9; MATTHEW 24:29"> The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before that the great and the open day of the Lord &#91;shall&#93; come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 10:12–13; JOEL 2:32; PSALMS 86:5"> And it shall be, each man which-ever shall call to help &#91;or in-call&#93; the name of the Lord, shall be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 3:2; JOHN 14:10–11; MATTHEW 12:28"> Ye men of Israel, hear ye these words. Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God before you by virtues, and wonders, and tokens, which God did by him in the middle of you, as ye know,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 22:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:18"> ye tormented, and killed him by the hands of wicked men, by counsel determined and betaken by the fore-knowing &#91;or the prescience&#93; of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:14; 1ST PETER 1:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:32"> Whom God raised, when &#91;the&#93; sorrows of hell were unbound, by that that it was impossible that he were holden of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 16:8–11; ISAIAH 41:13; PSALMS 62:6"> For David saith of him, I saw afar the Lord before me evermore, for he is on my right half, that I be not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 16:9; PSALMS 63:5; PSALMS 71:23"> For this thing mine heart joyed, and my tongue made full out joy &#91;or gladded&#93;, and moreover my flesh shall rest in hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 16:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:31; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:18"> For thou shalt not leave my soul in hell, neither thou shalt give thine Holy to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 16:11; PSALMS 25:4; PSALMS 21:6"> Thou hast made known to me the ways of life, thou shalt &#91;full&#93;-fill me in mirth with thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:36; 1ST KINGS 2:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:8–9"> Brethren, be it leaveful boldly to say to you of the patriarch David, for he is dead and buried, and his sepulchre is among us into this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11–18; PSALMS 89:3–4; JEREMIAH 33:14–15"> Therefore when he was a prophet, and knew, that with a great oath God had sworn to him, that of the fruit of his loins should one sit on his seat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:27; PSALMS 16:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:35"> he seeing afar spake of the resurrection of Christ, for neither he was left in hell, neither his flesh saw corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:22"> God raised this Jesus, to whom we all be witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:31; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:17"> Therefore he was enhanced by the right hand of God, and through the promise of the Holy Ghost that he took of the Father, he shedded out this Spirit, that ye see and hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; MATTHEW 22:42–45; HEBREWS 1:13"> For David ascended not into heaven; but he saith, The Lord said to my Lord, Sit thou on my right half,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:19–3; ROMANS 16:20; ISAIAH 60:14"> till I put thine enemies the stool of thy feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:30–31; ROMANS 14:8–12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:10"> Therefore most certainly know all the house of Israel, that God made him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus, whom ye crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 3:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:10; HEBREWS 4:12–13"> When they heard these things, they were compunct in heart; and they said to Peter and &#91;to&#93; other apostles, Brethren, what shall we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:16; MARK 16:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:12"> And Peter said to them, Do ye penance, and each of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ, into remission of your sins; and ye shall take the gift of the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:3; GENESIS 17:7–8; ROMANS 8:30"> For the promise &#91;or repromission&#93; is to you, and to your sons, and to all that be far, whichever &#91;or whomever&#93; our Lord God hath called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:15; DEUTERONOMY 32:5; MATTHEW 17:17"> Also with other words full many he witnessed to them, and admonished them, and said, Be ye saved from this shrewd &#91;or this wicked&#93; generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:47; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:31–34"> Then they that received his word were baptized, and in that day souls were increased, about three thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:25; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:14; 1ST JOHN 1:3"> and &#91;they&#93; were lasting stably in the teaching of the apostles, and in communing of the breaking of bread, and in prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 7:16; JOHN 14:12; LUKE 8:37"> And dread was made to each man. And many wonders and signs were done by the apostles in Jerusalem, and great dread was in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:32; 1ST JOHN 3:16–18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:14–15"> And all that believed were together, and had all things common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:17; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:18–19; JAMES 2:14–16"> They sold possessions and chattel, &#91;or substances, or goods&#93;, and parted those things to all men, as it was need to each.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:42; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:42"> And each day they dwelled stably with one will in the temple, and brake bread about houses, and took meat with full out joy &#91;or with gladness&#93; and simpleness of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:13–14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:41"> and praised together God, and had grace to all the folk &#91;or all the people&#93;. And the Lord increased them that were made safe, each day into the same thing.</span>
