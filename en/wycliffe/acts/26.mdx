<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:15; PROVERBS 1:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:2"> And Agrippa said to Paul, It is suffered to thee, to speak for thyself. Then Paul held forth the hand, and began to yield reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Of all things, in which I am accused of the Jews, thou king Agrippa, I guess me blessed at thee, when I shall defend me this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:7"> mostly for thou knowest all things that be among Jews, customs and questions. For which thing, I beseech, hear me patiently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:13; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:10; PHILIPPIANS 3:5"> For all Jews that before knew me from the beginning, know my life from youth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:5"> that from the beginning was in my folk in Jerusalem, if they will bear witnessing &#91;or bear witness&#93;, that by the most certain sect of our religion, I lived a Pharisee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TITUS 2:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:20"> And now for the hope of repromission, that is made to our fathers of God, I stand subject in doom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JAMES 1:1; PHILIPPIANS 3:11; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:5"> in which hope our twelve lineages, serving night and day hope to come; of which hope, sir king, I am accused of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:12–20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31–32"> What unbelieveful thing is deemed at you, if God raiseth dead men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:8; PHILIPPIANS 3:6"> And soothly I guessed, that I ought to do many contrary things against the name of Jesus of Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:13–14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:21"> Which thing also I did in Jeru-salem, and I enclosed many of the saints in prison, when I had taken power of the princes of priests. And when they were slain, I brought &#91;or I gave&#93; the sentence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:19; MATTHEW 10:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:45"> And by all synagogues oft I punished them, and constrained to blaspheme; and more I waxed mad against them, and pursued &#91;till&#93; into alien cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:5–11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:1–8"> In which, &#91;the&#93; while I went to Damascus, with power and suffering of the princes of priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:23; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:16"> at midday, in the way I saw, sir king, that from heaven a light shined about me, passing the shining of the sun, and about them that were together with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:40; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:7"> And when we all had fallen down into the earth, I heard a voice saying to me in Hebrew tongue, Saul, Saul, what persecutest &#91;or pursuest&#93; thou me? it is hard to &#91;or for&#93; thee, to kick against the prick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 15:20–21; MATTHEW 25:45; EXODUS 16:8"> And I said, Who art thou, Lord? And the Lord said, I am Jesus, whom thou persecutest &#91;or pursuest&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:14–15; EZEKIEL 2:1; DANIEL 10:11"> But rise up, and stand on thy feet. For why to this thing I appeared to thee, that I ordain thee minister and witness of those things that thou hast seen, and of those things in which I shall show &#91;or shall appear&#93; to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:15; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:7; JEREMIAH 1:19"> And I shall deliver thee from peoples and folks, to which now I send thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:9; EPHESIANS 1:18; EPHESIANS 5:8"> to open the eyes of them, that they be converted &#91;or turned&#93; from darkness to light, and from power of Satan to God, that they take remission of sins, and part among saints, by faith that is in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 50:5; GALATIANS 1:16; EZEKIEL 2:7–8"> Wherefore, sir king Agrippa, I was not unbelieveful to the heavenly vision;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38"> but I told &#91;or I showed&#93; to them, that be at Damascus first, and at Jerusalem, and by all the country of Judea, and to heathen men, that they should do penance, and be converted to God, and do worthy works of penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:30–31; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:3"> For this cause, Jews took me, when I was in the temple, to slay me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:14; JOHN 5:46; ROMANS 3:21"> But I was helped by the help of God &#91;till&#93; into this day, and stand, witnessing to less and to more. And I say nothing else than which things the prophets and Moses spake that shall come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:1–12; LUKE 24:26; COLOSSIANS 1:18"> if Christ is to suffer, if he is the first of the again-rising of dead men, that shall show light to the people and to heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:13–14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:10"> When he spake these things, and yielded reason, Festus said with great voice, Paul, thou maddest &#91;or thou waxest mad&#93;; many letters turn thee to madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:26; TITUS 1:9; TITUS 2:7–8"> And Paul said, I mad not, thou best Festus, but I speak out the words of truth and of soberness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:2–3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:1–12; ISAIAH 30:20"> For also the king, to whom I speak steadfastly, knoweth of these things; for I deem, that nothing of these is hid from him; for neither in a corner was aught of these things done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:22–23"> Believest thou, king Agrippa, to prophets? I know that thou believest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:26; MATTHEW 10:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:29"> And Agrippa said to Paul, In little thing thou counselest me &#91;for&#93; to be made a christian man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:20; COLOSSIANS 1:28; ROMANS 10:1"> And Paul said, I desire with God, both in little and in great, not only thee, but all these that hear today, to be made such as I am, except these bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:23"> And the king rose up, and the president, and Bernice, and they that sat nigh to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:18"> And when they went away, they spake together, and said, That this man hath not done anything worthy death, neither &#91;or&#93; bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:11–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:25"> And Agrippa said to Festus, This man might be delivered &#91;or dismissed&#93;, if he had not appealed to the emperor &#91;or to Caesar&#93;.</span>
