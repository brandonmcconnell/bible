<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:23–28; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:12"> And it befell, when Apollos was at Corinth, that Paul when he had gone the higher coasts, he came to Ephesus, and found some of the disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 7:39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:15–17; GALATIANS 3:5"> And he said to them, Whether ye that believe have received the Holy Ghost? And they said to him, But neither have we heard &#91;or neither we have heard&#93;, if the Holy Ghost is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:25; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:16"> And he said, Therefore in what thing be ye baptized? And they said, In the baptism of John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 1:29–34; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:5"> And Paul said, John baptized the people in the baptism of penance, and taught, that they should believe in him that was to coming &#91;or to come&#93; after him, that is, in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:16; ROMANS 6:3–4"> When they heard these things, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:4; MARK 16:17"> And when Paul had laid on them his hands, the Holy Ghost came into them &#91;or on them&#93;, and they spake with languages, and prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And all were almost twelve men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:20–22"> And he went into the synagogue, and spake with trust three months, disputing and treating &#91;or softly moving&#93; of the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:6–8"> But when some were harded &#91;or were made hard&#93;, and believed not, and cursed the way of the Lord before the multitude, he went away from them, and separated the disciples, and disputed in the school of a mighty man each day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:31; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:6"> This was done by two years, so that all that dwelled in Asia heard the word of the Lord, Jews and heathen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARK 16:17–20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:12; ROMANS 15:18–19"> And God did virtues not small &#91;or little&#93; by the hand of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:15; 2ND KI 13:20–21; MARK 16:17"> so that on sick men the sudaria, &#91;or sweating clothes&#93;, were borne from his body, and sicknesses departed from them, and wicked spirits went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:27; MARK 9:38; LUKE 11:19"> But also some of the Jewish exorcists went about, and assayed &#91;or attempted&#93; to &#91;in&#93;-call the name of the Lord Jesus Christ on them that had evil spirits, and said, I conjure you by Jesus, whom Paul preacheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And there were seven sons of a Jew, Sceva, a prince of priests, that did this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:17–18; LUKE 4:33–35; MARK 1:34"> But the evil spirit answered, and said to them, I know Jesus, and I know Paul; but who be ye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 8:29; MARK 5:3–4; MARK 5:15"> And the man in which was the worst devil, leaped on them, and had victory &#91;or lordship&#93; on both, and was strong against them, that they naked and wounded fled away from that house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 1:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:5; PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11"> And this thing was made known to all the Jews and heathen men, that dwelled at Ephesus; and dread fell down on them all, and they magnified the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 10:10; PSALMS 32:5; MATTHEW 3:6"> And many men believed, and came, acknowledging and telling their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:8; EXODUS 32:20; GENESIS 35:4"> And many of them that pursued curious things, brought together books, and burned them before all men; and when the prices of those were acounted, they found money of fifty thousand pence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:7; ISAIAH 55:11"> so strongly the word of God waxed, and was confirmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:11"> And when these things were &#91;ful&#93; filled, Paul purposed in spirit, after that Macedonia was passed and Achaia, to go to Jerusalem, and said, For after that I shall be there, it behooveth me also to see Rome &#91;or to see also Rome&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 16:23; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:3"> And he sent into Macedonia two men, that ministered to him, Timothy, and Erastus, and he dwelled for a time in Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> And a great troubling was made in that day, of &#91;or in&#93; the way of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:9–10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:19"> For a man, Demetrius by name, a worker in silver, made silver houses to Diana, &#91;that is, a false goddess&#93;, and gave to craftsmen much winning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:19; HOSEA 12:7–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:11–19"> which he called together them that were such manner workmen, and said, Men, ye know that of this craft winning is to us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:29; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:4; ISAIAH 44:10–20"> and ye see and hear, that this Paul counseleth and turneth away much people, not only of Ephesus, but almost of all Asia, and said, that they be not gods, that be made with hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 2:11; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:5; MATTHEW 23:13"> And not only this part shall be in peril to us, to come into reproof, but also the temple of the great Diana shall be acounted into nought; yea, and the majesty of her shall begin to be destroyed &#91;or but and the majesty of her shall be destroyed&#93;, whom all Asia and the world worshippeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:19–24"> When these things were heard, they were &#91;full&#93;-filled with ire, and cried, and said, Great is the Diana of the Ephesians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:4; COLOSSIANS 4:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:2"> And the city was filled with confusion, and they made an assault with one will into the theatre, and took Gaius and Aristarchus, men of Macedonia, fellows of Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:39; 2ND SAMUEL 18:2–3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:22–31"> And when Paul would have entered into the people, the disciples suffered not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:6"> And also some of the princes of Asia, that were his friends, sent to him, and prayed, that he should not give himself into the theatre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:34; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:40; MATTHEW 11:7–9"> And other men cried other thing; for the church was confused, and many knew not for what cause they were come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:17; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:20"> But of the people they drew away one Alexander, while Jews putted him forth. And Alexander asked with his hand silence, and would yield reason to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:28; MATTHEW 6:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:4"> And as they knew that he was a Jew, one voice of all men was made, crying as by twain hours, Great is Diana of Ephesians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:10–11; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:2; EPHESIANS 2:12"> And when the scribe, that is, a town clerk, had ceased the people, he said, Men of Ephesus, what man is he, that knoweth not, that the city of Ephesians is the worshipper of the great Diana, and of the child of Jupiter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:35–39; PROVERBS 25:8"> Therefore when it may not be gainsaid to these things, it behooveth you to be ceased &#91;or to be assuaged&#93;, and to do nothing follily;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:3; ROMANS 2:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:8"> for ye have brought these men, neither sacrilegers, neither blaspheming your goddess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:7; 1ST CORINTHIANS 6:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:14"> That if Demetrius, and the work-men that be with him, have cause against any man, there be courts, and dooms, and judges; accuse they each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> If ye seek aught of any other thing, it may be absolved in the lawful church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:5–8; MATTHEW 26:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:1"> For why we be in peril to be reproved of this day’s dissension &#91;or sedition&#93;, since no man is guilty, of whom we may yield reason of this running together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:17; PSALMS 65:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> And when he had said this thing, he let the people go.</span>
