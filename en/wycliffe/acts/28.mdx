<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:44; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:39"> And when we had escaped, then we knew that the isle was called Melita.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 1:14; HEBREWS 13:2; COLOSSIANS 3:11"> And the heathen men did to us not little courtesy &#91;or Soothly barbar-ians gave to us not little humanity&#93;. And when a fire was kindled, they refreshed us all, for the rain that came, and the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:33; MATTHEW 12:34; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:4"> But when Paul had gathered a quantity of cuttings of vines, and laid on the fire, an adder came forth from the heat, and took him by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 13:2; LUKE 13:4; JOHN 7:24"> And when the heathen men of the isle saw the beast hanging in his hand, they said together, For this man is a manslayer; and when he escaped from the sea, God’s vengeance suffer-eth him not to live in earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 10:19; MARK 16:18; PSALMS 91:13"> But he shook away the beast into the fire, and had none harm &#91;or suffered nothing of evil&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:11–13; MATTHEW 21:9"> And they guessed that he should be turned into swelling, and fall down suddenly, and die. But when they abided long, and saw that nothing of evil was done in him, they turned them together, and said, that he was God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:40–41; LUKE 19:6–9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:2"> And in those places were manors &#91;or fields&#93; of the prince of the isle, Publius by name, which received us by three days benignly &#91;or with good will&#93;, and ‘found’ us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:40; MARK 6:5; JAMES 5:14–16"> And it befell, that the father of Publius lay travailed with fevers and bloody flux. To whom Paul entered, and when he had prayed, and laid his hands on him, he healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:12; MARK 6:54–56; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:15"> And when this thing was done, all that in the isle had sicknesses came, and were healed &#91;or cured&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:6; PHILIPPIANS 4:11–12; MATTHEW 6:31–34"> Which also honoured us with many worships, and putted what things were necessary to us, when we shipped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 8:4; ISAIAH 45:20"> And after three months we shipped in a ship of Alexandria, that had wintered in the isle, to which was an excellent sign of Castor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And when we came to Syracuse, we dwelled there three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:13"> From thence we sailed about, and came to Rhegium; and after one day, while the south blew, in the second day we came to Puteoli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:1"> Where when we found brethren, we were prayed to dwell there with them seven days. And so we came to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:3; ROMANS 15:24; EXODUS 4:14"> And from thence, when brethren had heard, they came to us to the chapping of Appii, and to the Three Taverns. And when Paul had seen them, he did thankings to God, and took trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:30–31"> And when we came to Rome, it was suffered to Paul to dwell by himself, with a knight keeping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:10"> And after the third day, he called together the worthiest of the Jews. And when they came, he said to them, Brethren, I did nothing against the people either custom of fathers, and I was bound at Jerusalem, and was betaken into the hands of Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:31–32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:24–25"> And when they had asked of me, would have delivered me, for that no cause of death was in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:10–12"> But for the Jews gainsaid, I was constrained to appeal to the emperor &#91;or to Caesar&#93;; not as having anything to accuse my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:20; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:6–7"> Therefore for this cause I prayed to see you, and speak to you; for for the hope of Israel I am gird about with this chain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:17; ISAIAH 41:11; ISAIAH 50:8"> And they said to him, Neither we have received letters of thee from Judea, neither any of brethren coming showed, either spake, any evil thing of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:14; 1ST PETER 3:16"> But we pray to hear of thee, what things thou feelest; for of this sect it is known to us, that everywhere men gainsaith it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:2–3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:28"> And when they had ordained a day to him, many men came to him into the inn. To which he expounded, witnessing the kingdom of God, and counselled them of Jesus, of the law of Moses, and &#91;of&#93; prophets, from the morrow till to &#91;the&#93; eventide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:4–5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:8–9"> And some believed to these things that were said of Paul, some believed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:7; 2ND PETER 1:21; MARK 7:6"> And when they were not consent-ing together, they departed. And Paul said one word, For the Holy Ghost spake well by Isaiah, the prophet, to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:9–10; EZEKIEL 12:2; ISAIAH 42:19–20"> and said, Go thou to this people, and say to them, With ear ye shall hear, and ye shall not understand; and ye seeing shall see, and ye shall not behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:10"> For the heart of this people is greatly fatted, and with ears they heard heavily, and they closed together their eyes, lest peradventure they see with eyes, and with ears hear, and by heart understand, and be converted, and I heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 2:30–32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:17–18; ROMANS 3:29–30"> Therefore be it known to you, that this health of God, is sent to heathen men, and they shall hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And when he had said these things, Jews went out from him, and had much question, or musing, &#91;or seeking&#93;, among themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And he dwelled full two years in his hired place; and he received all that entered to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:29; EPHESIANS 6:19–20; MATTHEW 4:23"> and preached the kingdom of God, and taught those things that be of the Lord Jesus Christ, with all trust, without forbidding. Amen.</span>
