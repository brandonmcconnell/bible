<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:20; DANIEL 3:16–18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:40"> But Saul was consenting to his death. And great persecution was made that day in the church, that was in Jerusalem. And all men were scattered by the countries of Judea and Samaria, except the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 50:10–11; GENESIS 23:2; JEREMIAH 22:18"> But good &#91;or dread-full&#93; men buried Stephen, and made great mourning on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 1:13; GALATIANS 1:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:9"> But Saul greatly destroyed the church, and entered by houses, and drew out men and women, and betook them into prison &#91;or keeping&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:1"> And they that were scattered, passed forth, preaching &#91;or evangel-izing&#93; the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:35–36"> And Philip came down into a city of Samaria, and preached to them Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 4:41–42; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:12; MATTHEW 20:15–16"> And the people gave attention to these things that were said of Philip, with one will hearing and seeing the signs that he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:8–10; MARK 16:17–18"> For many of them that had unclean spirits, cried with great voice, and went out. And many sick in the palsy, and crooked, were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:52; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:48; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:39"> Therefore great joy was made in that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:36; JOHN 7:18"> But there was a man in that city, whose name was Simon, a witch, that had deceived the folk of Samaria, saying, that himself was some great man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:34; 2ND PETER 2:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:3"> &#91;To&#93; Whom all harkened, from the least to the most, and said, This is the virtue of God, which is called great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:9; GALATIANS 3:1; ISAIAH 44:25"> And they believed him, for long time he had madded them with his witchcrafts, &#91;or for this thing, that much time he had made them mad with his witchings&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:3; MATTHEW 28:19"> But when they had believed to Philip, preaching &#91;or evangelizing&#93; of the kingdom of God, men and women were baptized in the name of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:6–7; JOHN 5:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:11"> And then also Simon himself believed; and when he was baptized, he drew to Philip; and he saw also that signs and great virtues were done, he was astonied, and wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:13; 1ST THESSALONIANS 3:2; GALATIANS 2:9"> But when the apostles that were at Jerusalem, had heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent to them Peter and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; JOHN 16:23–24; JOHN 14:13–14"> And when they came, they prayed for them, that they should receive the Holy Ghost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:2; MATTHEW 28:19"> for he came not yet into any of them, but they were baptized only in the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:6; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:6"> Then they laid hands on them, and they received the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 6:5; MATTHEW 10:8; 2ND KI 8:9"> And when Simon had seen, that the Holy Ghost was given by laying on of &#91;the&#93; hands of the apostles, and he proffered &#91;or offered&#93; to them money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 14:7–11; 3RD JOHN 1:9; JOHN 5:44"> and said, Give ye also to me this power, that whomever I shall lay on mine hands &#91;or that on whomever I shall put on hands&#93;, that he receive the Holy Ghost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 5:17; MATTHEW 10:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38"> But Peter said to him, Thy money be with thee into perdition, for thou guessedest that the gift of God should be had for money &#91;or for thou guessedest the gift of God to be had, or wielded, by money&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:13; PSALMS 36:1; MATTHEW 6:22–24"> There is no part, nor lot to thee, in this word, for thine heart is not rightful before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:25–26; DANIEL 4:27; HEBREWS 4:12"> Therefore do thou penance for this wickedness of thee, and pray God, if peradventure this thought of thine heart be forgiven to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:6; PROVERBS 5:22; JOHN 8:34"> For I see that thou art in the gall of bitterness and in the bond of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 8:8; NUMBERS 21:7; JOB 42:8"> And Simon answered, and said, Pray ye for me to the Lord, that nothing of these things that ye have said, come on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:22–23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8"> And they witnessed, and spake the word of the Lord, and went again to Jerusalem, and preached &#91;or evangelized&#93; to many countries of Samaritans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:19; HEBREWS 1:14; 2ND KI 1:3"> And an angel of the Lord spake to Philip, and said, Rise thou, and go to meet the south, to the way that goeth down from Jerusalem into Gaza; this is desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:10; PSALMS 68:31; ISAIAH 56:3–8"> And he rose, and went forth. And lo! a man of Ethiopia, a mighty man-servant, a gelding of Candace, the queen of Ethiopians, which was on all her riches, came to worship in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:16; ISAIAH 1:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:25"> And he turned again, sitting on his chariot, and reading Isaiah, the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:22–23"> And the Spirit said to Philip, Nigh thou, and join thee to this chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 24:44–45; JOHN 5:39; EPHESIANS 5:17"> And Philip ran to, and heard him reading Isaiah, the prophet. And he said, Guessest thou, whether thou un-derstandest, what things thou readest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ROMANS 10:14; PSALMS 73:16–17; ISAIAH 29:18–19"> And he said, How may I, if no man show to me? And he prayed Philip, that he should come up, and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:7–8; 1ST PETER 2:21–24; JEREMIAH 51:40"> And the place of the scripture that he read, was this, As a sheep he was led to slaying, and as a lamb before a man that sheareth him is dumb without voice, so he opened not his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:12; ISAIAH 53:8; ZECHARIAH 13:7"> In meekness his doom was taken up; who shall tell out the generation of him? For his life shall be taken away from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:15; MATTHEW 13:36; MATTHEW 2:2–4"> And the gelding answered to Philip, and said, I beseech thee, of what prophet saith he this thing? of himself, or of any other?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKE 24:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:23"> And Philip opened his mouth, and began at this scripture, and preached &#91;or evangelized&#93; to him Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:47; JOHN 3:5; TITUS 3:5–6"> And while they went by the way, they came to a water &#91;or to some water&#93;. And the gelding said, Lo! water; who forbiddeth me to be baptized?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Philip said, If thou believest of all thine heart, it is leaveful. And he answered, and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 4:1; JOHN 3:22–23"> And he commanded the chariot to stand still. And they went down both into the water, Philip and the gelding, and Philip baptized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:12; 2ND KI 2:16; EZEKIEL 8:3"> And when they were come up of the water, the Spirit of the Lord ravished Philip, and the gelding saw him no more; and he went in his way joying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:33"> And Philip was found in Azotus; and he passed forth, and preached &#91;or evangelized&#93; to all cities, till he came to Caesarea.</span>
