<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:34; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:40"> Therefore when Festus came into the province, after the third day he went up to Jerusalem from Caesarea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:15; PROVERBS 4:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:1"> And the princes of priests, and the worthiest &#91;or the first&#93; of the Jews went to him against Paul, and prayed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:12–15; 1ST SAMUEL 23:19–21"> and asked grace against him, that he should command him to be led to Jerusalem; and they set ambush to slay him in the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:23"> But Festus answered, that Paul should be kept in Caesarea; soothly that he himself should proceed more advisedly &#91;or hastily&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:25; JOHN 18:29–30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:18–19"> Therefore he said, They that in you be mighty, come down together; and if any crime is in the man, accuse they him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:10; MATTHEW 27:19"> And he dwelled among them no more than eight either ten days, and came down to Caesarea; and the tother day he sat for doomsman, and commanded Paul to be brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:13; LUKE 23:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:24"> And when he was brought forth, Jews stood about him, which came down from Jerusalem, putting against him many and grievous causes, which they might not prove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:17; 2ND CORINTHIANS 1:12"> For Paul yielded reason in all things, That neither against the law of Jews, neither against the temple, neither against the emperor &#91;or Caesar&#93;, I sinned anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:20"> But Festus would do grace &#91;or willing to give grace&#93; to the Jews, and answered to Paul, and said, Wilt thou go up to Jerusalem, and there be deemed of these things before me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:18; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:18"> And Paul said, At the doom place of the emperor &#91;or of Caesar&#93; I stand, where it behooveth me to be deemed. I have not annoyed the Jews, as thou knowest well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:25"> For if I have annoyed, either done anything worthy death, I forsake not to die; but if nothing of those is, that they accuse me, no man may give me to them. I appeal to the emperor &#91;or to Caesar&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:21; PHILIPPIANS 1:12–14; PHILIPPIANS 1:20"> Then Festus spake with the coun-cil, and answered, To the emperor &#91;or To Caesar&#93; thou hast appealed, to the emperor &#91;or to Caesar&#93; thou shalt go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:40; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:27–28"> And when some days were passed, Agrippa king, and Bernice came down to Caesarea, to welcome &#91;or to greet&#93; Festus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:27"> And when they dwelled there many days, Festus showed to the king of Paul, and said, A man is left bound of Felix,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:1–3; ESTHER 3:9; LUKE 18:3–5"> of which, when I was at Jeru-salem, princes of priests, and the elder men of the Jews came to me, and asked damnation against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 7:51; DEUTERONOMY 19:17–18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:30"> To whom I answered, That it is not custom to Romans, to damn any man, before that he that is accused have his accusers present, and take place of defending, to put away the crimes, that be put against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:10"> Therefore when they came together hither, without any delay, in the day pursuing I sat for doomsman, and commanded the man to be brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when his accusers stood &#91;nigh&#93;, they said no cause of which things I had suspicion of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:3–4"> But they had against him some questions of their vain worshipping, &#91;or religion&#93;, and of one Jesus dead, whom Paul affirmed to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:9"> And I doubted of such manner questions, and said, Whether he would go to Jerusalem, and there be deemed of these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 4:16; LUKE 2:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:10–11"> But for Paul appealed, that he should be kept to the knowing of the emperor &#91;or of Caesar&#93;, I commanded him to be kept, till I send him to the emperor &#91;or to Caesar&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:15; MATTHEW 10:18; ISAIAH 52:15"> And Agrippa said to Festus, I myself would hear the man. And he said, Tomorrow thou shalt hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:13; 1ST JOHN 2:16"> And on the tother day, when Agrippa and Bernice came with great desire, &#91;or pride of state&#93;, and entered into the auditorium, with tribunes and the principal men of the city, when Festus bade, Paul was brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:2–3"> And Festus said, King Agrippa, and all men that be with us, ye see this man, of which all the multitude of Jews prayed me at Jerusalem, and asked, and cried, that he should live no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:11–12; LUKE 23:4"> But I found, that he had done nothing worthy of death; and I deemed to send him to the emperor &#91;or to Caesar&#93;, for he appealed this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:2–3"> Of which man I have not certain, what thing I shall write to the lord. For which thing I brought him to you, and mostly to thee, thou king Agrippa &#91;or O king Agrippa&#93;, that when asking is made, I have what I shall write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 7:51; PROVERBS 18:13"> For it is seen to me without reason, to send a bound man, and not to signify the cause of him.</span>
</div>
