<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JONAH 1:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:3"> And when we found a ship passing over to Phoenicia, we went up into it, and sailed forth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:22–23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:10–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:6–7"> And when we found disciples, we dwelled there seven days; which said by Spirit to Paul, that he should not go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:36; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:40; PSALMS 95:6"> And when the days were &#91;ful&#93; filled, we went forth, and all men with wives and children led forth us with-out the city; and we kneeled in the sea brink, and we prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 19:27; JOHN 1:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 2:13"> And when we had made farewell together, we went up into the ship; and they turned again into their own places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:24; 1ST SAMUEL 10:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:12"> And when the ship sailing was filled from Tyre, we came down to Ptolemais, and when we had greeted well the brethren, we dwelled one day at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:26–40; EPHESIANS 4:11"> And another day we went forth, and came to Caesarea. And we entered into the house of Philip evangelist, that was one of the seven, and dwelled at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:20; JOEL 2:28"> And to him were four daughters, virgins, that prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:16"> And when we dwelled there some days, a prophet, Agabus by name, came over from Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:33; JEREMIAH 13:1–11; 1ST SAMUEL 15:27–28"> This when he came to us, took the girdle of Paul, and bound together his feet and hands, and said, The Holy Ghost saith these things, Thus &#91;the&#93; Jews shall bind in Jerusalem the man, whose is this girdle; and they shall betake him into heathen men’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21–23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:4"> Which thing when we heard, we prayed, and they that were of that place, that he should not go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:24; PHILIPPIANS 2:17; PHILIPPIANS 1:20–21"> Then Paul answered, and said, What do ye, weeping and tormenting mine heart? For I am ready, not only to be bound, but also to die in Jerusalem for the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 22:42; MATTHEW 26:42; MATTHEW 26:39"> And when we might not counsel him, we were still, and said, The will of the Lord be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 25:9"> And after these days we were made ready, and went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:31; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:8; PSALMS 92:14"> And some of the disciples came with us from Caesarea, and led with them a man, Jason of Cyprus, an old disciple, at whom we should be harboured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:4; ROMANS 15:7; HEBREWS 13:1–2"> And when we came to Jerusalem, brethren received us willfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:19; GALATIANS 2:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:13"> And in the day pursuing, Paul entered with us to James, and all the elder men were gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:17; ROMANS 15:18–19"> Which when he had greeted, he told by all things, what &#91;things&#93; God had done in heathen men, by the ministry of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 10:2; GALATIANS 1:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:3"> And when they heard, they mag-nified God, and said to him, Brother, thou seest how many thousands be in Jews, that have believed to God, and all be lovers &#91;or pursuers&#93; of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:12–15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:19–21; GALATIANS 5:1–6"> And they heard of thee, that thou teachest departing from Moses of those Jews that be by heathen men, that say, that they owe not to circumcise their sons, neither owe to enter by &#91;or after&#93; custom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:32"> Therefore what is &#91;this&#93;? It behooveth that the multitude come together; for they shall hear, that thou art come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:18; NUMBERS 6:2–7"> Therefore do thou this thing, that we say to thee. There be to us four men, that have a vow on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:18"> Take thou these men, and hallow thee with them; hang on them, that they shave their heads; and that all men know, that those things that they heard of thee be false, but that thou walkest, and thyself keepest the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:19–20"> But of these that believed of heathen men, we have written, deeming that they abstain them from thing offered to idols, and from blood, and also from strangled thing, and from fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:24; NUMBERS 6:13–20"> Then Paul took the men, and in the day pursuing, he was purified with them, and entered into the temple, and showed the &#91;ful&#93; filling of days of purifying, till the offering was offered for each of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:50"> And when seven days were ended, the Jews that were of Asia, when they saw him in the temple, stirred all the people, and laid hands on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:5–6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:20–21"> and cried, Men of Israel, help ye us. This is the man, that against the people and the law and this place teacheth everywhere all men, more-over and hath led heathen men into the temple, and hath defouled this holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:4; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:19"> For they saw Trophimus of Ephesus in the city with him, whom they guessed that Paul had brought into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:21; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:19–22"> And all the city was moved, and a running together of the people was made. And they took Paul, and drew him out of the temple; and anon the gates were closed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 11:23–33; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:9–10"> And when they sought to slay him, it was told to the tribune of the company of knights, that all Jerusalem is confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:27; ISAIAH 3:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:40"> Which anon took knights, and centurions, and ran to them. And when they had seen the tribune, and the knights, they ceased to smite Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:23"> Then the tribune came, and caught him, and commanded, that he were bound with two chains; and asked, who he was, and what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:24"> But others cried other thing among the people. And when he might know no certain thing for the noise, he commanded him to be led into the castles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:2–3; PSALMS 55:9; PSALMS 58:2"> And when Paul came to the grees, it befell that he was borne of knights, for strength of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 19:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:22; LUKE 23:18"> For the multitude of the people pursued him, and cried, Take him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:34; LUKE 21:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:30"> And when Paul began to be led into the castles, he said to the tribune, Whether it is leaveful to me, to speak anything to thee? And he said, Knowest thou Greek?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:36–37; MATTHEW 5:11"> Whether thou art not the Egyptian, which before these days movedest a noise, and leddest out into desert four thousand of men, menslayers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:3; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:27"> And Paul said to him, For I am a Jew, of Tarsus of Cilicia, a citizen, which city is not unknown. And I pray thee, suffer me to speak to the people.</span>
