<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; NUMBERS 33:40; JOSHUA 12:14"> And when &#91;the&#93; Canaanite, the king of Arad, that dwelled at the south, had heard this, that is, that Israel came by the way of &#91;the&#93; spyers, he fought against them; and Canaanite was the overcomer, and he led away prey the men of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 11:30; GENESIS 28:20; 1ST SAMUEL 1:11"> And Israel bound himself by a vow to the Lord, and said, If thou shalt betake this people in mine hand, I shall do away their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:45; DEUTERONOMY 1:44; PSALMS 10:17"> And the Lord heard the prayers of Israel, and betook to them the Canaanites; and Israel killed him, and destroyed his cities; and Israel called the name of that place Hormah, that is, cursing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 6:9; JUDGES 11:18; NUMBERS 32:7"> Soothly Israel went forth from the hill of Hor, by the way that leadeth to the Red Sea, that they would compass the land of Edom; and it began to annoy the people, of the way and &#91;of the&#93; travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 78:19; NUMBERS 16:13–14; EXODUS 17:2–3"> And the people spake against the Lord, and Moses, and said, Why leddest thou us out of Egypt, that we should die in wilderness? bread faileth, waters be not; our soul loatheth now on this meat most light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:9; JEREMIAH 8:17; DEUTERONOMY 8:15"> Wherefore the Lord sent fired serpents or fiery adders into the people; at the wounds of which serpents, and the deaths of full many men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 8:24; NUMBERS 11:2; PSALMS 78:34"> they came to Moses, and said, We have sinned, for we spake against the Lord, and thee; pray thou, that he take away from us the serpents. And Moses prayed for the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 3:14; PSALMS 106:43–45; PSALMS 145:8"> and the Lord said to him, Make thou a &#91;brazen&#93; serpent, and set thou it in a perch; he that is smitten and beholdeth it, shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 3:14–15; HEBREWS 12:2; JOHN 6:40"> Therefore Moses made a serpent of brass, and setted in a perch; and men hurt, and beholding it, were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:43–45"> And the sons of Israel went forth, and setted tents in Oboth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> from whence they went forth, and setted tents in Iyeabarim, in the wilderness that beholdeth Moab, against the east coast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:13–14"> And they moved from thence, and came to the strand of Zared;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 11:18; NUMBERS 22:36; ISAIAH 16:2"> which they left, and setted tents against Arnon, which is in the desert, and it appeareth in the coasts of Amorites. Forsooth Arnon is the term of Moab, and parteth Moabites and Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:13; 2ND SAMUEL 1:18"> Wherefore it is said in the book of battles of the Lord, As he did in the Red Sea, so he shall do in the strands of Arnon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:28; DEUTERONOMY 2:18; DEUTERONOMY 2:9"> the hard rocks of the strands were bowed down, that they shall rest in Ar, or abide there, and should lie in the coasts of Moabites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:8; ISAIAH 12:3; ISAIAH 41:17–18"> From that place the pit appeared, of which the Lord spake to Moses, Gather thou together the people, and I shall give water to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JAMES 5:13; EXODUS 15:1–2; PSALMS 106:12"> Then Israel sang this song, The water of the pit, ascend or go up; they sang together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 33:22; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:17–18; JAMES 4:12"> The pit which the princes digged, and the dukes of the multitude made ready, in the giver of the law, and in their staves. And they went forth from the wilderness to Mattanah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> from Mattanah to Nahaliel, from Nahaliel into Bamoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:28; NUMBERS 23:14; DEUTERONOMY 1:5"> Bamoth is a valley in the country of Moab, in the top of Pisgah, that beholdeth against the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:26–28; NUMBERS 20:14–19; JUDGES 11:19–21"> Soothly Israel sent messengers to Sihon, king of Amorites, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:17"> I beseech thee, that it be leaveful to me to pass through thy land; we shall not bow into thy fields, and vineries &#91;or vines&#93;; we shall not drink waters of thy pits; we shall go in the king’s way, till we pass by thy terms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JUDGES 11:20; DEUTERONOMY 29:7–8; ISAIAH 15:4"> Which would not grant that Israel should pass through his coasts, but rather, when his host was gathered, he went out against Israel, into desert. And he came into Jahaz, and fought against Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="AMOS 2:9; JOSHUA 24:8; GENESIS 32:22"> of whom he was smitten in the sharpness of sword, and his land was wielded of Israel from Arnon unto Jabbok, and Ammon’s sons, or his host; for the coasts of Ammonites were holden with strong help, or power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:33–42; ISAIAH 16:8–9; JEREMIAH 48:2"> Therefore Israel took all his cities, and dwelled in the cities of Amorites, that is, in Heshbon, and in his towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The city of Heshbon was Sihon’s, king of Amorites, which Sihon fought against the king of Moab, and took all the land that was of his lordship, till to Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 14:4; NUMBERS 21:14; HABAKKUK 2:6"> Therefore it is said in proverb, Come ye into Heshbon, be it builded, and made, the city of Sihon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:15; JEREMIAH 48:45–46; ISAIAH 15:1–2"> fire went out of Heshbon, flame went out of the city of Sihon, and devoured Ar of Moabites, and the dwellers of the high places of Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JUDGES 11:24; 2ND KI 23:13; JEREMIAH 48:46"> Moab, woe to thee! thou, people of Chemosh, hast perished; it gave the sons thereof into flight, and the daughters into captivity to Sihon, king of Amorites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:18; ISAIAH 15:2; JEREMIAH 48:22"> the yoke, or lordship, of them perished, from Heshbon unto Dibon; the weary men came into Nophah, and unto Medeba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:8–12; NUMBERS 32:33–42; DEUTERONOMY 3:16–17"> And so Israel dwelled in the land of Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:32; NUMBERS 32:1; NUMBERS 32:35"> And Moses sent men that should espy Jaazer, whose towns they took, and wielded the dwellers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:12; DEUTERONOMY 1:4; DEUTERONOMY 29:7"> And they turned themselves, and went up by the way of Bashan. And Og, the king of Bashan, with all his people, came against them, to fight in Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 135:10–11; JOSHUA 8:7; DEUTERONOMY 3:2–3"> And the Lord said to Moses, Dread thou not him, for I have betaken him, and all his land, and all his people, in thine hand; and thou shalt do to him as thou didest to Sihon, king of Amorites, the dweller of Heshbon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:7–8; JOSHUA 13:12; DEUTERONOMY 3:3–17"> Therefore they smote Og with his sons, and all his people, unto &#91;the&#93; death; and they wielded his land.</span>
