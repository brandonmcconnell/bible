<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:10; NUMBERS 15:18; LEVITICUS 25:2"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, When ye have entered into the land of your habitation, which I shall give to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 29:18; GENESIS 8:21; PHILIPPIANS 4:18"> and ye shall make an offering to the Lord into burnt sacrifice, either a peaceable sacrifice, and ye pay avows, either offer gifts by free will, either in your solemnities ye burn odour of sweetness to the Lord, of oxen, either of sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 29:40; LEVITICUS 23:13; LEVITICUS 6:14"> whoever offereth the slain sacrifice, shall offer a sacrifice of flour, the tenth part of ephah, sprinkled altogether with oil, which oil shall have a measure the fourth part of hin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:7; NUMBERS 28:14; JUDGES 9:13"> and he shall give wine to &#91;the&#93; flowing sacrifices to be poured, of the same measure, into burnt sacrifice, and slain sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:12–14; NUMBERS 15:4"> By each lamb and ram shall be the sacrifice of &#91;tried&#93; flour, of two tenth parts, which shall be sprinkled alto-gether with oil, of the third part of hin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and he shall offer wine to the flowing sacrifice, of the third part of the same measure, into odour of sweet-ness to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:1; LEVITICUS 7:11–18; LEVITICUS 1:3"> Forsooth when thou makest a burnt sacrifice, either an offering, of oxen, that thou &#91;ful&#93; fill a vow, either peaceable sacrifice&#91;s&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:14; NUMBERS 28:12; LEVITICUS 6:14"> thou shalt give, by each ox, three tenth parts of tried flour, sprinkled altogether with oil, which shall have the half measure of hin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:5; NUMBERS 6:15"> and thou shalt give wine to &#91;the&#93; flowing sacrifice to be poured, of the same measure, into offering of the sweetest odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So ye shall do by each ox, and ram, and lamb, and kid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> (See verse 11 above.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> as well men born in the land, as pilgrims, shall offer sacrifices by the same custom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> (See verse 13 above.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:14; NUMBERS 15:29; EXODUS 12:49"> (See verse 16 below.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> one commandment and doom shall be, as well to you as to &#91;the&#93; comelings of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:2; DEUTERONOMY 26:1–15"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, When ye come into the land which I shall give to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:11–12"> and ye eat of the loaves of that country, ye shall separate a little cake of your pastes to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:14; EXODUS 34:26; DEUTERONOMY 26:2–10"> as ye shall separate the first fruits of your cornfloors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 29:28; NUMBERS 18:26"> so ye shall give the first fruits also of your sowls or pottage to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:2; LEVITICUS 4:27; LEVITICUS 5:15–17"> That if by ignorance ye pass by any of those things which the Lord spake to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and &#91;hath&#93; commanded by him to you, from the day in which he began to command, and over,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:23; LEVITICUS 4:13–21; LEVITICUS 5:15"> and the multitude hath forgotten to do this, it shall offer a calf of the drove, burnt sacrifice into sweetest odour to the Lord, and the sacrifices thereof, and flowing offerings, as the ceremonies thereof ask; and it shall offer a buck of goats for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:20; ROMANS 3:25; HEBREWS 2:17"> And the priest shall pray for all the multitude of the sons of Israel, and it shall be forgiven to them, for they sinned not willfully. And nevertheless they shall offer incense to the Lord for themselves, and for their sin, and their error;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:24"> and it shall be forgiven to all the people of the sons of Israel, and to the comelings that be pilgrims among them, for it is the sin of all the multitude by ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:27–28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:30; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:17"> That if a soul sinneth unwittingly, it shall offer a &#91;she&#93;-goat of one year for his sin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:35"> and the priest shall pray for that soul, for it sinned unwittingly before the Lord; and the priest shall get forgiveness to it, and the sin shall be forgiven to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:15; LEVITICUS 16:29; LEVITICUS 17:15"> As well to men born in the land, as to comelings, one law shall be of all that sin unwittingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:26; PSALMS 19:13; DEUTERONOMY 1:43"> Forsooth a man that doeth any sin by pride, shall perish from the people, whether he be a citizen, either a pilgrim, for he was rebel against the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:9; PROVERBS 13:13; EZEKIEL 18:20"> for he despised the word of the Lord, and made void his command-ment; therefore he shall be done away, and shall bear his own wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 35:2–3; EXODUS 31:14–15; EXODUS 20:8–10"> Soothly it was done, when the sons of Israel were in wilderness, and they had found a man gathering wood in the sabbath day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 8:3–20"> they brought him to Moses, and to Aaron, and to all the multitude;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:12; NUMBERS 9:8"> the which enclosed, or put, him into prison, and they knew not what they should do to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:58; EXODUS 31:14–15; 1ST KINGS 21:13"> And the Lord said to Moses, This man die by death; all the company oppress him with stones without or away from the tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:25"> And when they had led him withoutforth, they killed him with stones, and he was dead, as the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Also the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:5; DEUTERONOMY 22:12; MATTHEW 9:20"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, that they make to them hems by four corners of their mantles, and fasten they in them laces of jacinth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 106:39; PROVERBS 28:26; PSALMS 73:27"> and when they see those, have they mind of all the commandments of the Lord, lest they pursue &#91;or follow&#93; their &#91;own&#93; thoughts and their eyes, doing fornication by diverse things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="ROMANS 12:1; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:7; LEVITICUS 11:44–45"> but more be they mindful of the behests of the Lord, and do they those &#91;or them&#93;, and be they holy to their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:9–10; EZEKIEL 36:25–27; HEBREWS 11:16"> I am your Lord God, which led you out of the land of Egypt, that I should be your God.</span>
