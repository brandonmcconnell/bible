<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Also the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:11; GENESIS 8:21; NUMBERS 9:2–3"> Command thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, Offer ye by their times mine offering, and loaves, and incense of sweetest odour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:9; DANIEL 11:31; JOHN 1:29"> These be the sacrifices which ye owe to offer; two lambs of one year, without wem, each day, into ever-lasting burnt sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 9:21; 1ST KINGS 18:36; EXODUS 12:6"> Ye shall offer one lamb early, and the tother at eventide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:1; EXODUS 16:36; EXODUS 29:38–42"> The tenth part of ephah of tried flour, that shall be sprinkled with purest oil, and have it the fourth part of hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="AMOS 5:25; LEVITICUS 6:9; PSALMS 50:8"> It is continual burnt sacrifice, which ye offered in the hill &#91;or mount&#93; of Sinai, into odour of sweetest incense to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 29:42; NUMBERS 28:14; ISAIAH 57:6"> And ye shall offer the fourth part of hin of wine, by each lamb, in the saintuary of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And ye shall offer in like manner the tother lamb at eventide, by all the custom of the morrow sacrifice, and of the moist &#91;or liquor&#93; sacrifices thereof, an offering of sweetest odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:13; PSALMS 92:1–4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:10"> Soothly in the sabbath day ye shall offer two lambs of one year, without wem, and two tenth parts of tried flour sprinkled altogether with oil, in sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:4–5; NUMBERS 29:22; NUMBERS 29:31"> and the moist &#91;or liquor&#93; sacrifices that be poured by custom, by all sabbaths, into everlasting burnt sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:10; EZEKIEL 46:6; EZRA 3:5"> Soothly in the calends, that is, in the beginnings of months, ye shall offer burnt sacrifice to the Lord, twain &#91;or two&#93; calves of the drove, one ram, seven lambs of one year, without wem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:4–12; EZEKIEL 46:5–7; NUMBERS 29:10"> and three tenth parts of &#91;tried&#93; flour sprinkled altogether with oil, in the sacrifice, by each calf, and two tenth parts of &#91;tried&#93; flour sprinkled altogether with oil, by each ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the tenth part of the tenth of &#91;tried&#93; flour sprinkled altogether with oil, in the sacrifice, by each lamb; it is burnt sacrifice of sweetest odour, and of incense to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Soothly the moist &#91;or liquor&#93; sacrifices of wine, that shall be poured by all the slain sacrifices, shall be these; the half part of a hin by each calf, the third part by a ram, the fourth part by a lamb; this shall be the burnt sacrifices by each month, that come one after another while the year turneth about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:3; NUMBERS 28:22; NUMBERS 15:24"> Also a buck of goats shall be offered to the Lord for sins, into everlasting burnt sacrifice, with his moist &#91;or liquor&#93; offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:1–8; EXODUS 12:18; LEVITICUS 23:5–8"> Soothly in the first month, in the fourteenth day of the month, shall be pask, either passing over, of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:6; EXODUS 23:15; EXODUS 13:6"> and in the fifteenth day shall be the solemnity of therf loaves. By seven days ye shall eat therf loaves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 12:16; LEVITICUS 23:7–8"> of which the first day shall be worshipful and holy; ye shall not do any servile work therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:21; EZEKIEL 45:21–25; NUMBERS 29:8"> And ye shall offer burnt sacrifice to the Lord, two calves, one ram, seven lambs of one year, without wem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and the sacrifices of each by itself of &#91;tried&#93; flour, which be sprinkled altogether with oil, three tenth parts by each calf, and two tenth parts by a ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and the tenth part of the tenth by each lamb, that is, by seven lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 8:3"> And ye shall offer one buck of goats for sin, that cleansing be made for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:3; NUMBERS 28:10"> besides the burnt sacrifice of the morrowtide, which ye shall offer ever-&#91;more&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So ye shall do by each day of &#91;the&#93; seven days, into the nourishing of fire, and into the sweetest odour to the Lord, that shall rise of the burnt sacrifice, and of the moist &#91;or liquor&#93; sacrifices of each offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:8; NUMBERS 28:18; EXODUS 13:6"> Also the seventh day shall be most solemn and holy to you; ye shall not do any servile work therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 23:16; EXODUS 34:22; LEVITICUS 23:10"> Also the day of the first fruits, when ye shall offer new fruits to the Lord, when the weeks shall be fulfilled, shall be worshipful and holy; ye shall not do any servile work therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:18–19; NUMBERS 28:11; NUMBERS 28:19"> And ye shall offer burnt sacrifice to the Lord, into sweetest odour; two calves of the drove, one ram, and seven lambs of one year, without wem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and in the sacrifices of those &#91;or them&#93;ye shall offer three tenth parts of tried flour sprinkled altogether with oil, by each calf, two tenth parts by the rams,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> the tenth part of the tenth by the lambs, which be altogether seven lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:15; NUMBERS 28:22; 1ST PETER 2:24"> And ye shall offer a buck of goats, which is offered for cleansing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:19; NUMBERS 28:3; MALACHI 1:13–14"> besides &#91;the&#93; burnt sacrifice ever-lasting, and the moist &#91;or liquor&#93; sacrifices thereof; ye shall offer all things without wem, with their moist &#91;or liquor&#93; sacrifices.</span>
