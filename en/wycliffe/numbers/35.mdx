<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:54; NUMBERS 33:54; JOSHUA 21:1–42"> And those cities that shall be given of the possession of the sons of Israel, more cities shall be taken away from them that have more, and fewer from them that have less; all the sons of Israel by themselves shall give by the measure of their heritage, cities to the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;.</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:9; DEUTERONOMY 19:1–2; LEVITICUS 14:34"> Speak thou to the sons of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, When ye have passed &#91;over&#93; Jordan, in&#91;to&#93; the land of Canaan,</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:3–6; DEUTERONOMY 19:6; JOSHUA 20:9"> In which cities when the fleer hath fled, the kinsman of him that is slain, shall not be able to slay him, till he stand in the sight of the multitude, and the cause of him be deemed.</span>
</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:8–10; DEUTERONOMY 4:41–43; JOSHUA 20:7–9"> three shall be beyond Jordan, and three in the land of Canaan;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:16; GALATIANS 3:28; ROMANS 3:29"> as well to the sons of Israel as to comelings, and pilgrims; that he flee to those cities, that shedded blood not willfully.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:17; GENESIS 9:5–6; DEUTERONOMY 19:11–13"> If any man smiteth a man with iron, and he that is smitten is dead, the smiter shall be guilty of man-slaying, and he shall die.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:3; DEUTERONOMY 19:6; DEUTERONOMY 19:12"> The nigh kinsman of him that is slain shall slay the manslayer; anon as he taketh the manslayer, he shall slay him.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 29:7; LEVITICUS 4:3; ROMANS 3:24–26"> the innocent shall be delivered from the hand of the avenger, and by sentence of judges he shall be led again into the city, to which he fled, and he shall dwell there, till the great priest die, which is anointed with &#91;holy&#93; oil.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 22:2; DEUTERONOMY 19:10; DEUTERONOMY 19:6"> and he is slain of him that is avenger &#91;of the blood&#93;, he that slayeth him shall be without guilt;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:14; HEBREWS 10:26–30; HEBREWS 6:4–8"> for the exiled man ought to sit or abide in the city till to the death of the bishop; forsooth after that that bishop is dead, the manslayer shall turn again into his land.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:11; NUMBERS 27:1"> These shall be everlasting and lawful things in all your dwellings.</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:11–13; GENESIS 9:5–6; PSALMS 51:14"> Ye shall not take price of him which is guilty of blood, or death, anon and he shall die.</span>

<div className="bp">
