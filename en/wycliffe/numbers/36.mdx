<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:3–6; NUMBERS 27:1–7; NUMBERS 33:54"> and said, The Lord commanded to thee our lord, that thou shouldest part the land by lot to the sons of Israel, and that thou shouldest give to the daughters of Zelophehad, our brother, the possession due to their father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And if men of another lineage shall take to wives these daughters, their possession shall pursue &#91;or follow&#93; them, and it shall be translated or brought over to another lineage, and so it shall be decreased from our heritage;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:10–18; ISAIAH 61:2; LEVITICUS 25:23"> and so it shall be done, that when the jubilee, that is, the fiftieth year of remission, cometh, the parting of lots be confounded, or fail, and that the possession of other men pass to other men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:7; DEUTERONOMY 5:28"> Moses answered to the sons of Israel, and said, for the Lord com-manded, The lineage of the sons of Joseph hath spoken rightfully,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 36:12; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14; GENESIS 24:3"> and this law is announced of the Lord on the daughters of Zelophehad; be they wedded to which men they will, only to the men of their lineage;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST KINGS 21:3; NUMBERS 36:9"> lest the possession of the sons of Joseph be meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; from lineage into lineage. For all men shall wed wives of their lineage and kindred;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 23:22"> and each daughter, that shall have the heritage, shall be wife to one man of the kindred of her father,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> and &#91;the&#93; lineages be not meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; to themselves, but dwell so, as those be parted of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 30:12; MATTHEW 28:20; EXODUS 39:42–43"> And the daughters of Zelophehad did, as it was commanded to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:1; NUMBERS 26:33"> And Mahlah, and Tirzah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Noah, were wedded to the sons of their father’s brothers,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> of the meine of Manasseh, that was the son of Joseph; and the possession that was given to them, dwelled in the lineage, and in the meine, of their father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:34; LEVITICUS 26:46; NUMBERS 22:1"> These be the commandments and dooms, which the Lord commanded, by the hand of Moses, to the sons of Israel, in the field places of Moab, above Jordan, against Jericho.</span>
