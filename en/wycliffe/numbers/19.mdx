<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses and to Aaron, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:20–25; DEUTERONOMY 21:3; 1ST SAMUEL 6:7"> This is the religion of sacrifice, which the Lord ordained. Command thou to the sons of Israel, that they bring to thee a red cow of whole colour, in which is no wem, neither she hath borne yoke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:12; LEVITICUS 4:21; LEVITICUS 16:27"> And ye shall betake her to Eleazar, the priest, that shall offer her, led out of the tents, in the sight of all men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:17; LEVITICUS 4:6; LEVITICUS 16:14"> And he shall dip his finger in the blood thereof, and shall sprinkle seven times against the gates of the tabernacle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 29:14; LEVITICUS 4:11–12; PSALMS 22:14"> And he shall burn that cow, while all men see; and he shall give as well the skin, and the flesh thereof, as the blood, and the dung, to &#91;the&#93; burning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:4; LEVITICUS 14:49; PSALMS 51:7"> Also the priest shall put cedar wood, and hyssop, and red thread dyed twice, into the fire, that burneth the cow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; LEVITICUS 11:40; NUMBERS 19:8"> And then at the last, when the priest’s clothes and his body be washed, he shall enter into the tents, and he shall be defouled, or unclean, till to eventide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But also he that burnt the cow, shall wash his clothes, and his body, and he shall be unclean till to eventide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:13; NUMBERS 19:13; NUMBERS 19:20–21"> Soothly a clean man shall gather the ashes of the cow, and he shall pour them out without the tents, in a place most clean, that those ashes be to the multitude of the sons of Israel into keeping, and into water of sprinkling; for that cow is burnt for sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 12:49; NUMBERS 19:7–8; COLOSSIANS 3:11"> And when he that bare out the ashes of the cow, hath washed his clothes, he shall be unclean till to eventide. And the sons of Israel, and the comelings that dwell among them, shall have, or hold, this holy by ever-lasting law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:2; NUMBERS 31:19; LEVITICUS 21:1"> He that toucheth a dead body of a man, and is unclean for this by seven days,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:14; LEVITICUS 7:17"> shall be sprinkled of this water in the third &#91;day&#93;, and in the seventh day; and so he shall be cleansed. If he is not sprinkled in the third day, he shall not be able to be cleansed in the seventh day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:31; LEVITICUS 22:3; LEVITICUS 7:20"> Each that toucheth the dead body by itself of man’s soul, and is not sprinkled with this meddling &#91;or mingling&#93;, defouleth the tabernacle of the Lord, and he shall perish from Israel; for he is not sprinkled with the water of cleansing, he shall be unclean, and his filth shall dwell upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> This is the law of a man that dieth in the tabernacle; all that enter into his tent, and all the vessels that be there, shall be defouled by seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:36; LEVITICUS 11:32; NUMBERS 31:20"> A vessel that hath not a covering, neither a binding above, shall be unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:19; MATTHEW 23:27; LUKE 11:44"> If any man toucheth the dead body of a man slain in the field, either dead by himself, either a bone of him, either his sepulchre, he shall be unclean by seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 7:38; GENESIS 26:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:17"> And they shall take of the ashes of the burning, and of the sin, that is, of the cow offered for sin, and they shall put quick waters into a vessel, upon those ashes;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:25–27; HEBREWS 9:14; NUMBERS 19:9"> in which when a clean man hath dipped hyssop, he shall sprinkle there-with the tent, and all the appurtenance of the household, and the men also defouled with such defouling.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:7; HEBREWS 10:22; EZEKIEL 36:25"> And in this manner a clean man shall cleanse an unclean thing, in the third and in the seventh day; and he shall be cleansed in the seventh day. And he shall wash himself, and his clothes, and he shall be unclean till to eventide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 16:16; GENESIS 17:14; NUMBERS 19:13"> If any man is not cleansed by this custom, the soul of him shall perish from the midst of the church; for he defouleth the saintuary of the Lord, and he is not sprinkled with the water of cleansing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:13–14; HEBREWS 10:4; HEBREWS 9:10"> This behest shall be a lawful thing everlasting. Also he that shall sprinkle the waters shall wash his clothes; each man that toucheth the waters of cleansing, shall be unclean till to eventide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HAGGAI 2:13; LEVITICUS 7:19; MATTHEW 15:19–20"> Whatever thing an unclean man toucheth, he shall make unclean; and a soul that toucheth any of these things defouled so, shall be unclean till to eventide.</span>
</div>
</div>
