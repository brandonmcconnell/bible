<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:12; NUMBERS 22:19–20; JEREMIAH 12:2"> he answered, Dwell ye here tonight, and I shall answer to Balak whatever thing the Lord shall say to me. And the princes of Moab dwelled at Balaam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:5; EPHESIANS 1:3; GENESIS 12:2"> And God said to Balaam, Do not thou go with them, neither curse thou the people, for it is blessed.</span>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:13; 1ST KINGS 22:14; 2ND CHRONICLES 18:13"> Balaam answered, Though Balak shall give to me his house full of silver and of gold, I shall not be able to change the word of my God, that I speak either more or less.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

And when Balaam beat her, and would lead her again to the path,</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSEA 2:6; ISAIAH 26:11"> And nevertheless the angel went to a strait place, where was no going out of the way, neither to the right side, nor to the left, and stood against Balaam</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:16; LUKE 1:37; EXODUS 4:11"> And the Lord opened the mouth of the she-ass, and she spake, What have I done to thee? why smitest thou me, lo! now the third time?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:10; MATTHEW 15:19; PROVERBS 12:16"> Balaam answered, For thou hast dis-served, and hast scorned me; I would that I had a sword to slay thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:27–28"> And the she-ass said, Whether I am not thy beast upon which thou were wont to ride ever&#91;more&#93; till into this present day? say thou, what like thing to this I did ever to thee? </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 21:19; LUKE 24:31; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18"> Anon the Lord opened the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the angel standing in the way, holding a drawn sword in his hand; and Balaam worshipped him lowly into the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:10; NUMBERS 22:22"> To whom the angel said, Why hast thou thrice beaten thine she-ass? I came to be adversary to thee, for thy way is wayward, and contrary to me;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:33–35; NUMBERS 14:37; 1ST KINGS 13:24–28"> and if the she-ass had not bowed away from the way, and given place to the against-stander, I had slain thee, and the ass should have lived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOB 34:31–32; 2ND SAMUEL 12:13; 1ST SAMUEL 15:24"> Balaam said, I have sinned, not witting that thou stoodest against me; and now, if it displeaseth thee that I go, I shall turn again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–12; ISAIAH 37:26–29; NUMBERS 22:20–21"> The angel said, Go thou with these men, but beware, that thou speak not &#91;any&#93; other thing than I shall command to thee. Therefore Balaam went with the princes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ISAIAH 16:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:15; JEREMIAH 48:20"> And when Balak heard that Balaam was nigh, he went out into the coming of him, in Ar, the city of Moabites, which is set in the last coast of Arnon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:11; JOHN 5:44; MATTHEW 4:8–9"> And Balak said to Balaam, I sent messengers to call thee; why camest thou not anon to me? whether for I may not yield meed to thy coming?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:18; PROVERBS 19:21; NUMBERS 23:16"> To whom Balaam answered, Lo! I am present, whether I shall be able to speak other thing than that, that God shall put in my mouth?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Therefore they went forth together, and they came into a city, which was in the last coast of his realm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:14; PROVERBS 1:16; NUMBERS 23:2"> And when Balak had slain sheep, and oxen, he sent gifts to Balaam and the princes that were with him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:13; NUMBERS 25:2–3; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:15"> Forsooth when the morrowtide was made, Balak led Balaam to the high places of Baal, and he beheld the last part of the people, that is, all the host till to the last part.</span>
