<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 2:21; GENESIS 26:34–35; GENESIS 24:37"> And Marie spake and Aaron against Moses, for his wife a woman of Ethiopia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 35:12–13; NUMBERS 16:3; MICAH 6:4"> and they said, Whether God spake his will only by Moses? whether he spake not also to us in like manner? And when the Lord had heard this, he was wroth greatly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:29; JAMES 3:13; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:5"> for Moses was the mildest man, over all men that dwelled in earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 76:9; NUMBERS 16:16–21"> And suddenly the Lord spake to Moses and to Aaron and to Marie, Go out ye three alone to the tabernacle of the bond of peace. And when they were gone in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:25; EXODUS 34:5; PSALMS 99:7"> the Lord came down in a pillar of cloud, and he stood in the entering of the tabernacle, and called Aaron and Marie. And when they had gone forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:5; GENESIS 31:10–11; GENESIS 46:2"> he said to them, Hear ye my words; if any among you is a prophet of the Lord, I shall appear to him in revelation, either I shall speak to him by a dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:2–6; JOSHUA 1:1; PSALMS 105:26"> And he said, And my servant Moses is not such, the which is most faithful in all mine house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:12; EXODUS 33:11"> for I speak to him mouth to mouth, and he seeth God openly, and not by dark speeches, either dark likenesses, and figures. Why therefore dreaded ye not to backbite my servant Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 17:22; HOSEA 5:15; NUMBERS 11:1"> And the Lord was wroth against them, and he went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:9; 2ND KI 5:27; EXODUS 4:6"> And the cloud went away, that was on the tabernacle, and lo! Marie appeared shining with leprosy, white as snow. And when Aaron beheld her, and saw her besprinkled with leprosy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 19:19; 2ND SAMUEL 24:10; PROVERBS 30:32"> he said to Moses, My lord, I beseech thee, put thou not this sin upon us, which we did follily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 88:4–5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:8; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:6"> that this woman be not made as dead, and as a dead-born thing that is cast out of the mother’s womb; lo! now the half of her flesh is devoured, or over-covered, with leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 12:21; LUKE 6:28; JAMES 5:15"> And Moses cried to the Lord, and said, Lord, I beseech thee, heal thou her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:9; JOB 30:10; ISAIAH 50:6"> To whom the Lord answered, If her father had spit into her face, whether she ought not to be full-filled with shame, namely seven days? Therefore be she separated out of the tents by seven days, and afterward she shall be called again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:8–9; MICAH 7:8–9; MICAH 6:4"> And so Marie was excluded or put out of the tents by seven days; and the people was not moved from that place, till Marie was called again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:35; NUMBERS 33:18; NUMBERS 10:12"> And the people went forth from Hazeroth, when the tents were set in the desert of Paran.</span>
