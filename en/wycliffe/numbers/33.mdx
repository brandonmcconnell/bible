<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 77:20; MICAH 6:4; EXODUS 12:51"> These be the dwellings of the sons of Israel, that went out of the land of Egypt, by their companies, in the hand of Moses and Aaron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:17–23; DEUTERONOMY 10:11; NUMBERS 10:6"> which dwellings Moses described by the places of tents, that were changed by commandment of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 14:8; EXODUS 12:37; EXODUS 13:4"> Therefore the sons of Israel went forth in an high hand from Rameses, in the first month, in the fifteenth day of the first month, in the tother day of pask, that is, in the morrow of the offering of the lamb of pask, while all &#91;the&#93; Egyptians saw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 12:12; ISAIAH 19:1; EXODUS 18:11"> and the Egyptians buried their first begotten children, which the Lord had slain; for the Lord took vengeance also upon their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 12:37"> The sons of Israel setted tents in Succoth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 13:20"> and from Succoth they came into Etham, which is in the last coasts of the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 14:2; EXODUS 14:9; NUMBERS 33:8"> from thence they went forth, and came against Pihahiroth, which beholdeth Baalzephon, and setted tents before Migdol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 15:22–26; EXODUS 14:21–31"> And they went forth from Piha-hiroth, and they passed through the midst of the sea into wilderness, and they went three days by the desert of Etham, and setted tents in Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 15:27"> And they went forth from Marah, and came into Elim, where were twelve wells of water, and seventy palm trees; and there they setted tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 16:1; EXODUS 17:1"> But also they went forth from thence, and setted tents on the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 16:1"> And they went forth from the Red Sea, and setted tents in the desert of Sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> from whence they went forth, and came into Dophkah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they went forth from Dophkah, and setted tents in Alush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 17:1–8; EXODUS 19:2"> And they went forth from Alush, and setted tents in Rephidim, where water failed to the people to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 19:1–2; EXODUS 16:1"> And they went forth from Rephidim, and setted tents in the desert of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:34; NUMBERS 10:11–13; NUMBERS 10:33"> But also they went out of the wilderness of Sinai, and came to the Sepulchres of Covetousness, or Lust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:35"> And they went forth from the Sepulchres of Covetousness, or Lust, and setted tents in Hazeroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:16"> And from Hazeroth they came into Rithmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they went forth from Rithmah, and setted tents in Rimmonparez;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> from whence they went forth, and came into Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:1"> And from Libnah they setted tents in Rissah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they went forth from Rissah, and came into Kehelathah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> from whence they went forth, and setted tents in the hill of Shapher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And they went forth from the hill of Shapher, and they came into Haradah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> from thence they went forth, and setted tents in Makheloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they went forth from Makheloth, and came into Tahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> From Tahath they setted tents in Tarah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> from whence they went forth, and setted tents in Mithcah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And from Mithcah they setted tents in Hashmonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:5–6"> And they went forth from Hash-monah, and came into Moseroth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:6; GENESIS 36:27; 1ST CHRONICLES 1:43"> and from Moseroth they setted tents in Benejaakan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:7"> And they went forth from Bene-jaakan, and came into the hill of Gidgad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:7"> from whence they went forth, and setted tents in Jotbathah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And from Jotbathah they came into Ebronah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:8; 1ST KINGS 9:26; 1ST KINGS 22:48"> And they went forth from Ebronah, and setted tents in Eziongaber;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:1; NUMBERS 27:14; DEUTERONOMY 32:51"> from thence they went forth, and came into the desert of Zin; this is Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:4; NUMBERS 20:22–23; NUMBERS 20:16"> And they went forth from Kadesh, and they setted tents in the hill of Hor, in the last coasts of the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:6; DEUTERONOMY 32:50; NUMBERS 20:24–28"> And Aaron, the priest, ascended &#91;or went up&#93; into the hill of Hor, for the Lord commanded, and there he was dead, in the fortieth year of the going out of the sons of Israel from Egypt, in the fifth month, in the first day of the month;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> when he was of an hundred and three and twenty years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:1–9"> And the Canaanite, the king of Arad, that dwelled at the south, in the land of Canaan, heard that the sons of Israel came thither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:4"> And they went forth from the hill of Hor, and setted tents in Zalmonah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> from thence they went forth, and came into Punon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:10"> And they went forth from Punon, and setted tents in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:11"> And from Oboth they came into Iyeabarim, that is, into the wilderness of Abarim, which is in the ends of Moabites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they went forth from Iyea-barim, and they setted tents in Dibon of Gad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:18; EZEKIEL 6:14; ISAIAH 15:2"> from whence they went forth, and setted tents in Almon of Diblathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:49; DEUTERONOMY 34:1; NUMBERS 27:12"> And they went forth from Almon of Diblathaim, and they came to the hills of Abarim, against Nebo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:1"> And they went forth from the hills of Abarim, and passed to the field places of Moab, over Jordan, against Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:1; JOSHUA 13:20; NUMBERS 25:1–9"> And there they setted tents, from Bethjesimoth till to Abelshittim, in the plainer places of Moabites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:48–49"> where the Lord spake to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:17; DEUTERONOMY 9:1; DEUTERONOMY 7:1"> Command thou to the sons of Israel, and say thou to them, When ye have passed over Jordan, and have entered into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="EXODUS 23:24; LEVITICUS 26:1; DEUTERONOMY 20:16–18"> destroy ye all the dwellers of that country; break ye the titles, that is, altars, and drive ye to powder the images, and destroy ye all high things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:31; PSALMS 115:16; DEUTERONOMY 32:8"> and cleanse ye the land, and all the men dwelling therein. For I have given to you that land into possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:53–56; JOSHUA 18:11–48; JOSHUA 15:1–12"> which ye shall part to you by lot; to more men in number ye shall give larger land, and to fewer men in number straiter, or less, land, as the lot falleth to all men, so &#91;the&#93; heritage shall be given; the possession shall be parted to lineages and meines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JUDGES 2:3; PSALMS 106:34–36; DEUTERONOMY 7:16"> But if ye will not slay the dwellers of the land, they, that abide, shall be to you as nails in the eyes, and as spears in the sides; and they shall be adversaries to you in the land of your habitation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="LUKE 21:23–24; DEUTERONOMY 28:63; LEVITICUS 20:23"> and whatever thing I thought to do to them, I shall do to you.</span>
