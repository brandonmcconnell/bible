<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 28:38; HEBREWS 13:17; 1ST PETER 2:24"> And the Lord said to Aaron, Thou, and thy sons, and the house of thy father with thee, shall bear the wickedness of the saintuary; and thou and thy sons together shall suffer the sins of your priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 29:34; NUMBERS 18:4; EZEKIEL 44:15"> But also take thou with thee thy brethren of the lineage of Levi, and the sceptre, or power, of thy father, and be they ready, that they minister to thee. Forsooth thou and thy sons shall minister in the tabernacle of witnessing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:15; NUMBERS 16:40; NUMBERS 4:19–20"> and the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93; shall wake at thy commandments, and at all the works of the tabernacle; so only that they nigh not to the vessels of the saintuary, and to the altar, lest both they die, and ye, and perish together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 6:19; NUMBERS 1:51; NUMBERS 3:10"> Soothly be they with thee, and wake they in the keepings of the tabernacle, and in all the ceremonies thereof. An alien shall not be meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 27:21; LEVITICUS 24:3; NUMBERS 16:46"> Wake ye or Watch in the keeping of the saintuary, and in the service of the altar, lest indignation rise upon the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:9; NUMBERS 3:12; NUMBERS 3:45"> Lo! I have given to you your brethren, the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, from the midst of the sons of Israel, and I have given you them a free gift of the Lord, that they serve in the services of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 15:15–16; JOHN 3:27; NUMBERS 3:10"> Soothly thou and thy sons, keep your priesthood; and all things that pertain to the adorning of the altar, and be within the veil, shall be ministered by &#91;the&#93; priests; if any stranger nigheth thereto, he shall be slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:16; LEVITICUS 7:6; EXODUS 29:29"> The Lord spake to Aaron, Lo! I have given to thee the keeping of my first fruits; I have given to thee, and to thy sons, all things that be hallowed of the sons of Israel, for &#91;the&#93; priest’s office everlasting lawful things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:7; LEVITICUS 14:13; LEVITICUS 6:25–26"> Therefore thou shalt take these things of those things that be hallowed, and be offered to the Lord; each offering, and sacrifice, and whatever thing is yielded to me for sin and for trespass, and cometh into holy of holy things, shall be thine and thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:6; LEVITICUS 6:29; LEVITICUS 6:16"> Thou shalt eat it in the saintuary; males only shall eat thereof, for it is hallowed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:3; EXODUS 29:27–28; LEVITICUS 10:14"> Soothly I have given to thee, and to thy sons and thy daughters, by everlasting right, the first fruits which the sons of Israel avow and offer; he that is clean in thine house, shall eat those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:4; EXODUS 23:19; EXODUS 34:26"> I have given to thee all the mar-row, or the best, of oil, and of wine, and of wheat, whatever thing of the first fruits they shall offer to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 22:29; EXODUS 23:19; MICAH 7:1"> All the beginnings of fruits which the earth bringeth forth, and be brought to the Lord, shall fall into thine uses; he that is clean in thine house, shall eat of those &#91;or them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:28; EZEKIEL 44:29"> All things that the sons of Israel yield by a vow, shall be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 34:20; EXODUS 13:2; EXODUS 13:12–13"> Whatever thing cometh first forth of the womb of all flesh, which they offer to the Lord, whether it is of men, either of beasts, it shall be of thy right; so only that thou take price for the first begotten child of man, and that thou make each beast that is unclean to be bought again;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; NUMBERS 3:47; LEVITICUS 27:2–7"> whose again-buying shall be after one month, for five shekels of silver, by the weight of &#91;the&#93; saintuary; a shekel hath twenty halfpence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 29:16; DEUTERONOMY 15:19–22; LEVITICUS 3:2–5"> Forsooth thou shalt not make the first engendered of an ox, and of sheep, and of goat, to be again-bought, for those &#91;or they&#93; be hallowed to the Lord; only thou shalt pour the blood of those &#91;or them&#93; upon the altar, and thou shalt burn the inner fatness into sweetest odour to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:31–34; EXODUS 29:26–28"> Soothly the flesh shall fall into thine use, as the breast hallowed and the right shoulder, shall be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 13:5; LEVITICUS 2:13; NUMBERS 18:11"> I have given to thee, and to thy sons and thy daughters, by everlasting right, all the first fruits of the saintuary, which the sons of Israel offer to the Lord; it is everlasting covenant of salt before the Lord, to thee, and to thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:28; DEUTERONOMY 10:9; JOSHUA 13:33"> And the Lord said to Aaron, Ye shall not wield anything of heritage in the land of Israel, neither ye shall have part among them; I am thy part and thine heritage, in the midst of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 12:44; DEUTERONOMY 14:22–29; NEHEMIAH 10:37"> Soothly, I gave to the sons of Levi all the tithes of Israel into possession, for the service by which they serve me in the tabernacle of &#91;the&#93; bond of peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:9; NUMBERS 1:51–52; NUMBERS 3:38"> that the sons of Israel nigh no more to the tabernacle of &#91;the&#93; bond of peace, neither do deadly sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:7; NUMBERS 18:20; NUMBERS 18:1"> To the sons alone of Levi, serving me in the tabernacle, and bearing the people’s sins, it shall be a lawful thing everlasting in your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:26; MALACHI 3:8–10"> They shall wield none other thing, and they shall be satisfied with the offering of tithes, which I separated into &#91;the&#93; uses and necessaries of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And the Lord spake to Moses and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:38; NUMBERS 18:21"> Command thou, and announce to the deacons &#91;or Levites&#93;, When ye have taken tithes of the sons of Israel, which I gave to you, offer ye the first fruits of those &#91;or them&#93; to the Lord, that is, the tenth part of the tenth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:30; DEUTERONOMY 15:14; 2ND KI 6:27"> that it be areckoned to you into &#91;the&#93; offering of the first fruits, as well of the cornfloors, as of the presses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:20–10; GENESIS 14:18"> and of all things of which ye take tithes, offer ye the first fruits to the Lord, and give ye those to Aaron, the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> All things which ye shall offer of tithes, and shall separate into the gifts of the Lord, shall be the best, and all the choice things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:9–10; GENESIS 43:11; MATTHEW 6:33"> And thou shalt say to them, If ye offer to the Lord all the &#91;more&#93; clean and better things of tithes, it shall be areckoned to you, as if ye gave the first fruits of the cornfloor, and of the press.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 9:10–14; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:17–18; LUKE 10:7"> And ye shall eat those tithes in all your places, as well ye as your meines, for it is the price for the service, for which ye serve in the tabernacle of witnessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:8; LEVITICUS 22:2; MALACHI 1:7"> And ye shall not do sin on this thing, and reserve &#91;the&#93; noble things and &#91;the&#93; fat to you, lest ye defoul the offerings of the sons of Israel, and ye die.</span>
