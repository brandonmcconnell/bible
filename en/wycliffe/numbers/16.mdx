<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDE 1:11; DEUTERONOMY 11:6; EXODUS 6:21"> Forsooth Korah, the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and On, the son of Peleth, of the sons of Reuben,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:9; EZEKIEL 23:10; GENESIS 6:4"> rose against Moses, and others of the sons of Israel, two hundred men and fifty, princes of the synagogue, and which were called by their names in the time of counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 106:16; NUMBERS 14:14; EXODUS 19:6"> And when these stood against Moses and Aaron, they said, Suffice it to you, for all the multitude is of holy men, and the Lord is in them; why be ye raised up presumptuously on the people of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:5; NUMBERS 20:6; JOSHUA 7:6"> And when Moses had heard this, he fell down low upon his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 65:4; NUMBERS 17:5; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:19"> And he spake to Korah, and to all the multitude; he said, Early the Lord shall make known which men pertain to him, and he shall apply, or draw, to him holy men; and they which he hath chosen, shall nigh to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1; NUMBERS 16:46–48; 1ST KINGS 18:21–23"> Therefore do ye this thing; each man take his censer, thou Korah, and all thy counsel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:3; EPHESIANS 1:4; 1ST KINGS 18:17–18"> and tomorrow when fire is taken up, put ye incense above before the Lord, and whomever the Lord chooseth, he shall be holy. Ye sons of Levi be much raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And again Moses said to Korah, Ye sons of Levi, hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:13; DEUTERONOMY 10:8; EZEKIEL 44:10–11"> Whether it is little to you, that God of Israel &#91;hath&#93; separated you from all the people, and hath joined you to himself, that ye should serve him in the service of the tabernacle, and that ye should stand before the multitude of the people, and serve him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:10; 3RD JOHN 1:9; NUMBERS 18:7"> Made he therefore thee, and all thy brethren the sons of Levi, to nigh to himself, that ye challenge to you also priesthood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 16:7–8; EXODUS 17:2; NUMBERS 16:3"> and all thy gathering together stand against the Lord? For why, what is Aaron, that ye grutch against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:13–14; JUDE 1:8; PROVERBS 29:9"> Therefore Moses sent to call Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab; which answered, We come not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:35; EXODUS 2:14; NUMBERS 11:5"> Whether is it little to thee, that thou leddest us out of the land that flowed with milk and honey, to slay us in the desert, no but also thou be lord of us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:24; NUMBERS 36:8–10; NUMBERS 20:5"> Verily<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="They said this in scorn, to signify that Moses deceived the people by false promises.">a</a></sup>thou hast brought us into the land that floweth with streams of milk and honey, and&#91;thou&#93; hast given to us possession of fields, and of vineyards; whether also thou wilt put out our eyes? We come not to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 4:4–5; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:2; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:10"> And Moses was wroth greatly, and said to the Lord, Behold thou not the sacrifices of them; thou knowest that I took never of them a little ass, neither I tormented any of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 12:3; 1ST SAMUEL 12:7; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:14"> And Moses said to Korah, Thou and all thy congregation stand asides half before the Lord, and Aaron tomorrow by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 12:7"> Take ye all by yourselves your censers, and put ye incense in those, and offer ye to the Lord, twain &#91;or two&#93; hundred and fifty censers; and Aaron hold he his censer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when they had done this, while Moses and Aaron stood there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:10; NUMBERS 16:42; EXODUS 16:7"> and they had gathered all the multitude to the door of the tabernacle against them, the glory of the Lord appeared to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the Lord spake to Moses and to Aaron, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:45; EXODUS 32:10; PSALMS 73:19"> Be ye separated from the midst of this congregation, that I lose them suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:16; ZECHARIAH 12:1; JOB 12:10"> The which fell down low upon their faces, and said, Most strong God of the spirits of all flesh, whether thy wrath shall be fierce against all men, for one man sinneth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:21"> Command thou to all the people, that it be separated from the tabernacles of Korah, and of Dathan, and of Abiram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:16–17; NUMBERS 11:25; NUMBERS 11:30"> And Moses rose, and went to Dathan and Abiram; and while the elder men of Israel followed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:11; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:4"> he said to the company, Go ye away from the tabernacles of the wicked men, and do not ye touch those things that pertain to them, lest ye be wrapped in the sins of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOB 9:4; ISAIAH 28:14; JOB 40:10–11"> And when they had gone away from their tents by compass, Dathan and Abiram went out, and stood in the entry of their tents, with their wives, and their free children, and with all the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 6:38; EXODUS 3:12; JOHN 14:11"> And Moses said, In this ye shall know that the Lord sent me, that I should do all things which ye see, and that I brought them not forth of mine own heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:22; 1ST KINGS 22:28; EXODUS 32:34"> If they perish by customable death of men, and wound, either pestilence, visit them, by which also other men be wont to be visited, the Lord sent not me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 55:15; NUMBERS 16:33; JOB 31:3"> but if the Lord doeth a new thing, that the earth open his mouth, and swallow them, and all things that pertain to them, and they go down quick into hell, ye shall know that they blasphemed the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 106:17–18; NUMBERS 27:3; DEUTERONOMY 11:6"> Therefore anon as he ceased to speak, the earth was broken under their feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 106:17; NUMBERS 26:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:16"> and the earth opened his mouth, and devoured them, with their tabernacles, and all their chattel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:18; ISAIAH 14:9; PSALMS 9:15"> and they went down quick, into hell, and were covered with earth, and they perished from the midst of the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 14:5; ISAIAH 33:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:15–17"> And soothly all Israel that stood about, fled from the cry of men perishing, and said, Lest peradventure the earth swallow also us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:10; LEVITICUS 10:2; PSALMS 106:18"> But also fire went out from the Lord, and killed twain &#91;or two&#93; hundred and fifty men that offered incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:28; NUMBERS 16:18; NUMBERS 16:7"> Command thou to Eleazar, the son of Aaron, &#91;the&#93; priest, that he take, or gather up, the censers that lie in the burning, and that he scatter the fire hither and thither; for those censers be hallowed</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:10; EZEKIEL 14:8; 2ND PETER 2:6"> in the deaths of sinners; and that he bring forth those censers into plates, and nail them to the altar, for incense is offered in those &#91;or them&#93; to the Lord, and those be hallowed, that the sons of Israel see them for a sign and a memorial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Therefore Eleazar, the priest, took the brazen censers, in which they, which the burning had devoured had offered, and he beat out those censers into plates, and nailed them to the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:10; NUMBERS 18:4–7; 2ND CHRONICLES 26:16–21"> that the sons of Israel should have those censers with them afterward, by which they should remember this great vengeance of God, lest any alien, and which is not of the seed of Aaron, nigh to offer incense to the Lord; lest he suffer, as Korah suffered, and all his multitude, while the Lord spake to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:1–7; PSALMS 106:23; NUMBERS 14:2"> Forsooth all the multitude of the sons of Israel grutched in the day pursuing &#91;or following&#93; against Moses and Aaron, and said, Ye have slain the people of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:19; LEVITICUS 9:23; NUMBERS 20:6"> And when dissension arose, and the noise increased, Moses and Aaron fled to the tabernacle of the bond of peace; and after that they entered into it, a cloud covered the tabernacle, and the glory of the Lord appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> (See verse 42 above.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:21–22; NUMBERS 16:24; NUMBERS 20:6"> Go ye away from the midst of this multitude, also now I shall do away them. And when they lay in the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="NUMBERS 8:19; 1ST CHRONICLES 27:24; LEVITICUS 10:6"> Moses said to Aaron, Take thy censer, and when the fire is taken up from the altar, cast thou incense above, and go thou forth soon to the people, that thou pray for them; for now wrath is gone out from the Lord, and the vengeance is fierce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:44; DEUTERONOMY 33:10–11; PSALMS 106:29"> And when Aaron had done this, and had run to the midst of the multitude, which the burning wasted then, he offered incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 24:25; HEBREWS 7:24–25; NUMBERS 16:18"> and he stood betwixt the dead men and the living, and he prayed for the people, and the vengeance ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:9; NUMBERS 16:32–35; HEBREWS 2:1–3"> Soothly they that were slain were fourteen thousand of men and seven hundred, without them that perished in the dissension of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 21:26–30; NUMBERS 16:43"> And Aaron turned again to Moses, to the door of the tabernacle of the bond of peace, after that the perishing of men ceased.</span>
