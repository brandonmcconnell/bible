<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:7; PSALMS 53:5; ISAIAH 26:3–4"> A wicked man fleeth, when no man pursueth; but a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man as a lion trusting shall be without fearedfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST KINGS 15:28; 1ST KINGS 16:8–29; 2ND KI 15:8–31"> For the sins of the land there be many princes of it; and for the wisdom of a man, and for the knowing of these things that be said, the life of the duke shall be the longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:28–30"> A poor man falsely challenging poor men, is like a great rain, wherein hunger is made ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 1:32; EPHESIANS 5:11; MATTHEW 14:4"> They that forsake the law, praise the wicked man; they that keep the law, be kindled, or stirred&#91;up&#93;, against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 92:6; JAMES 1:5; 1ST JOHN 2:27"> Wicked men think not on doom; but they that seek the Lord, perceive all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:1; PROVERBS 28:18; LUKE 16:19–23"> Better is a poor man going in his simpleness, than a rich man in &#91;his&#93; shrewd ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:3–4; PROVERBS 29:3; PROVERBS 3:1–35"> He that keepeth the law, is a wise son; but he that feedeth gluttons, shameth his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 27:16–17; PROVERBS 13:22; ECCLESIASTES 2:26"> He that gathereth together riches by usuries, and free increases, gathereth those &#91;or them&#93; together against poor men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 66:18; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:3–4; ZECHARIAH 7:11–13"> His prayer shall be made cursed, that boweth away his ear, that he hear not the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:27; PSALMS 37:25–26; GALATIANS 2:4"> He that deceiveth just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men in an evil way, shall fall in his perishing; and just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men shall wield his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:17; ECCLESIASTES 9:15–17; PROVERBS 26:16"> A rich man seemeth wise to himself; but a poor man prudent shall search him out or know his counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:2; PROVERBS 11:10; PROVERBS 28:28"> In &#91;the&#93; enhancing of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men is much glory; when wicked men reign, fallings of men be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST JOHN 1:8–10; PSALMS 32:3–5; JOB 31:33"> He that hideth his great trespasses, shall not be made rightful; but he that acknowledgeth and forsaketh them, shall get mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:17; PSALMS 2:11; ISAIAH 66:2"> Blessed is the man, which is ever dreadful; but he that is of hard heart, shall fall into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8; MATTHEW 2:16; PROVERBS 19:12"> A roaring lion, and an hungry bear, is a wicked prince on a poor people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21; JEREMIAH 22:15–17; ECCLESIASTES 4:1"> A duke needy of prudence shall oppress many men by false challenge; but the days of him that hateth avarice shall be made long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 9:6; 1ST KINGS 21:19; EXODUS 21:14"> No man sustain a man that falsely challengeth the blood of a man, if he fleeth till to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:9; PROVERBS 28:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:3"> He that goeth simply shall be safe; he that goeth by wayward ways, shall fall down &#91;at&#93; once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:11; JUDGES 9:4; PROVERBS 27:23–27"> He that worketh his land, shall be filled with loaves; he that pursueth &#91;or followeth&#93; idleness, shall be filled with neediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 16:10–12; PROVERBS 13:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:10"> A faithful man shall be praised much; but he that hasteth to be made rich, shall not be innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:23; PROVERBS 18:5; EZEKIEL 13:19"> He that knoweth a face in doom, doeth not well; this man forsaketh truth, yea, for a morsel of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:6; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:9; PROVERBS 28:20"> A man that hasteth to be made rich, and hath envy to other men, knoweth not that neediness shall come &#91;up&#93; on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:5–6; MATTHEW 18:15; PSALMS 141:5"> He that reproveth a man, shall find grace afterward with him; more than he that deceiveth by flatterings of tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:4–6; PROVERBS 18:9; PROVERBS 19:26"> He that withdraweth anything from his father and from his mother, and saith that this is no sin, is partner of a man-queller.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:25; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:6; PROVERBS 13:10"> He that avaunteth himself, and alargeth, raiseth up strives; but he that hopeth in the Lord, shall be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5; JEREMIAH 17:9; JOB 28:28"> He that trusteth in his heart, is a fool; but he that goeth wisely, shall be praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; DEUTERONOMY 15:7; PSALMS 41:1–3"> He that giveth to a poor man, shall not be needy; he that despiseth a poor man beseeching, shall suffer neediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:2; JOB 24:4; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:23–24"> When unpious men rise, men shall be hid; and when they have perished, just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men shall be multiplied.</span>
