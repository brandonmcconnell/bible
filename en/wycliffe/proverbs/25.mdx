<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:1; 1ST KINGS 4:32; MICAH 1:1"> Also these be the Parables of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah, king of Judah, translated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:29; ROMANS 11:33–34; JOB 11:7–8"> The glory of God is to cover a word; and the glory of kings is to seek out a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 55:9; PSALMS 103:11; 1ST KINGS 4:29"> Heaven above, and the earth beneath, and the heart of kings is unsearchable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:20–21; EZEKIEL 22:18; PROVERBS 17:3"> Do thou away rust from silver, and a full clean vessel shall go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:12; PROVERBS 20:8; PSALMS 101:7–8"> Do thou away unpiety from the cheer of the king, and his throne shall be made steadfast by rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:2; PROVERBS 25:27; PROVERBS 16:19"> Appear thou not glorious before the king, and stand thou not in the place of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 14:7–11; LUKE 18:14; 1ST PETER 5:5"> For it is better, that it be said to thee, Ascend thou &#91;or Go up&#93; hither, than that thou be made low before the prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:25; PROVERBS 17:14; PROVERBS 18:6"> Bring thou not forth soon those things in strife, which thine eyes saw; lest afterward thou mayest not amend, when thou hast made thy friend unhonest or he hath shamed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:5–17; PROVERBS 11:13; PROVERBS 20:19"> Treat thy cause with thy friend, and show thou not private matter to a strange man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 119:39"> lest peradventure he have joy of thy fall, when he hath heard, and cease not to do shame to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:23; ISAIAH 50:4; ECCLESIASTES 12:10"> A golden pomme or pommel in beds of silver is he, that speaketh a word in his time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 141:5; PROVERBS 15:31–32; PROVERBS 15:5"> A golden earring, and a shining pearl is he, that reproveth a wise man, and an ear obeying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:17; PROVERBS 25:25; PHILIPPIANS 2:25–30"> As the cold of snow in the day of harvest, so a faithful messenger to him that sent him, maketh his soul to have rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:6; LUKE 14:11; 2ND PETER 2:15–19"> A cloud and wind, and rain not pursuing &#91;or following&#93;, is a glorious man, and not &#91;ful&#93; filling promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:1; ECCLESIASTES 10:4; PROVERBS 16:14"> A prince shall be made soft by patience; and a soft tongue shall break hardness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:27; EPHESIANS 5:18; PROVERBS 24:13–14"> Thou hast found honey, eat thou that that sufficeth to thee; lest per-adventure thou be &#91;full&#93;-filled, and spew it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 19:2–3; JUDGES 19:18–21; ROMANS 15:24"> Withdraw thy foot from the house of thy neighbour; lest sometime he be filled, that is, annoyed, and hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; PROVERBS 12:18; JAMES 3:6"> A dart, and a sword, and a sharp arrow, is a man that speaketh false witnessing against his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 36:6; EZEKIEL 29:6–7; JOB 6:14–20"> A rotten tooth, and a faint foot is he, that hopeth on an unfaithful man in the day of anguish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; ECCLESIASTES 3:4; ISAIAH 58:7"> and loseth his mantle in the day of cold. Vinegar in a vessel of salt is he, that singeth songs to the worst heart. As a moth annoyeth or harmeth a cloth, and a worm a tree, so the sorrow of a man annoyeth the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 12:20–21; MATTHEW 5:44; EXODUS 23:4–5"> If thine enemy hungereth, feed thou him; if he thirsteth, give thou him water to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:12; MATTHEW 10:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:18"> for thou shalt gather together coals on his head; and the Lord shall yield to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 37:22; PSALMS 15:3; ROMANS 1:30"> The north wind scattereth abroad rains; and a sorrowful face destroyeth a tongue backbiting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:19; PROVERBS 21:9; PROVERBS 27:15–16"> It is better to sit in the corner of an house without &#91;a&#93; roof, than with a woman full of chiding, and in a common house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:30; ROMANS 10:15; PSALMS 63:1"> Cold water to a thirsty man; and a good messenger from a far land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:18–19; 1ST SAMUEL 22:14–18; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:15"> A well disturbed with foot, and a vein broken, is a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man falling before a wicked man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 5:44; PROVERBS 27:2; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> As it is not good to him that eateth much honey; so he that is a searcher of majesty, shall be put down from glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:32; PROVERBS 22:24; 2ND CHRONICLES 32:5"> As a city open, and without compass of walls; so is a man that may not refrain his spirit in speaking.</span>
