<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:1; PSALMS 34:11; PROVERBS 1:8"> Sons, hear ye the teaching of your father; and perceive ye, that ye know prudence.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 22:5; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:1; ROMANS 12:16"> For why and I was the son of my father, a tender son, and one begotten, before my mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:1; EPHESIANS 6:4; JOHN 12:50"> And my father taught me, and said, Thine heart receive my words; keep thou my behests, and thou shalt live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; PROVERBS 3:13–18; PROVERBS 1:22–23"> Wield thou wisdom, wield thou prudence; forget thou not, neither bow thou away from the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:10; PROVERBS 4:21–22; EPHESIANS 3:17"> Forsake thou not it, and it shall keep thee; love thou it, and it shall keep thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:12; ECCLESIASTES 9:16–18; PROVERBS 23:23"> The beginning of wisdom, wield thou wisdom; and in all thy possession, get thou prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 12:3; 1ST SAMUEL 2:30; PROVERBS 3:35"> Take thou it, and it shall enhance thee; thou shalt be glorified of it, when thou hast embraced it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:9; 1ST PETER 5:4; HEBREWS 2:7–9"> It shall give increasings of graces to thine head; and a noble crown shall defend thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:13; PROVERBS 3:2; JEREMIAH 9:20"> My son, hear thou, and take my words; that the years of life be multiplied to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 25:4–5; ECCLESIASTES 12:9; PROVERBS 8:9"> I shall show to thee the way of wisdom; and I shall lead thee by the paths of equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:23; PSALMS 18:36; 1ST PETER 2:8"> Into which when thou hast entered, thy goings shall not be made strait; and thou shalt run, and shalt not have hurting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:1; PROVERBS 3:18; PROVERBS 23:23"> Hold thou teaching, and forsake &#91;thou&#93; it not; keep thou it, for it is thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33; PROVERBS 1:10"> Delight thou not in the paths of wicked men; and the way of evil men please not thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:11; JOB 22:23; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:22"> Flee thou from it, and pass thou not thereby; bow thou away, and forsake it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 36:4; MICAH 2:1; ISAIAH 57:20"> For they sleep not, no but they have done evil; and sleep is ravished from them, no but they have deceived simple men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JAMES 5:4–5; PSALMS 14:4; MICAH 6:12"> They eat the bread of unpiety, and drink the wine of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; JOB 11:17; DANIEL 12:3"> But the path of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men goeth forth as light shining, and increaseth till to &#91;a&#93; perfect day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 12:35; ISAIAH 59:9–10; JOB 18:5–6"> The way of wicked men is dark; they know not where they shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:1; ISAIAH 55:3; PROVERBS 7:1"> My son, hearken thou to my words; and bow down thine ears to my speeches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:21; PROVERBS 3:3; PROVERBS 7:1–2"> Go not they away from thine eyes; keep thou them in the middle of thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:10; PROVERBS 12:18; JEREMIAH 33:6"> For those be life to men finding those, and health of all flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 6:45; MATTHEW 12:34–35; MARK 7:21–23"> With all keeping keep thine heart, for life cometh forth of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JAMES 1:26; PROVERBS 8:8; EPHESIANS 4:25–31"> Remove thou a shrewd mouth from thee; and backbiting lips be far from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 119:37; MATTHEW 6:22; JOB 31:1"> Thine eyes see rightful &#91;or right&#93; things; and thine eyelids go before thy steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:13; PSALMS 37:23; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:3"> Address thou &#91;the&#93; paths to thy feet, and all thy ways shall be stablished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:32; JOSHUA 1:7; DEUTERONOMY 28:14"> Bow thou not to the right side, neither to the left side; turn away thy foot from evil.</span>
