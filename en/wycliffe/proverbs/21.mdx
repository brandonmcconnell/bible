<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:9; PROVERBS 20:24; EZRA 6:22"> As partings of waters, so the heart of the king is in the power of the Lord; whither ever he will, he shall bow it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:2; PROVERBS 16:25; LUKE 16:15"> Each way of a man seemeth rightful &#91;or right&#93; to himself; but the Lord weigheth the hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:22; HOSEA 6:6; MICAH 6:6–8"> To do mercy and doom, pleaseth more the Lord, than sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:17; 1ST PETER 5:5; ISAIAH 2:17"> Enhancing of eyes is &#91;the&#93; alarging of the heart; the lantern of wicked men is sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:4; PROVERBS 13:4; EPHESIANS 4:28"> The thoughts of a strong man be ever in abundance; but each slow man is ever in neediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:11; PROVERBS 10:2; PROVERBS 20:21"> He that gathereth treasures by the tongue of a lie maker, is vain, and without heart; and he shall be hurled to the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:21; EPHESIANS 5:6; PROVERBS 1:18–19"> The ravens of unpious men shall draw them down; for they would not do doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:15; 1ST JOHN 2:29; PSALMS 14:2–3"> The wayward way of a man is alien from God; but the work of him that is clean of sin, is rightful &#91;or right&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:13; PROVERBS 21:19; PROVERBS 27:15–16"> It is better to sit in the corner of an house without roof, than with a woman full of chiding, and in a common house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICAH 3:2–3; PROVERBS 3:29; PSALMS 36:4"> The soul of an unpious man desireth evil; he shall not have mercy on his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:25; PROVERBS 18:15; PROVERBS 9:9"> When a man full of pestilence is punished, a little man of wit shall be wiser; and if he pursueth &#91;or follow&#93; a wise man, he shall take knowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:11; PSALMS 37:35–36; 2ND PETER 3:6–7"> A just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man of the house of a wicked man thinketh, to withdraw wicked men from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:27; MATTHEW 6:14; PROVERBS 1:28"> He that stoppeth his ear at the cry of a poor man, shall cry also, and he shall not be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:16; PROVERBS 19:6; PROVERBS 17:8"> A gift hid quencheth chidings; and a gift in &#91;the&#93; bosom quencheth most indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:29; MATTHEW 7:23; MATTHEW 13:41–42"> It is joy to a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man to make doom; and it is dread to them that work wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:38; PSALMS 125:5; PSALMS 49:14"> A man that erreth from the way of doctrine, shall dwell in the company of giants, that is, of men evil ruled, either of fiends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:21; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:6; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:4"> He that loveth meats, shall be in neediness; he that loveth wine and fat things, shall not be made rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:8; ISAIAH 43:3–4; ISAIAH 53:4–5"> An unpious man shall be given for a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man; and a wicked man shall be given for a rightful &#91;or the right&#93; man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:9; PSALMS 120:5–6; PSALMS 55:6–7"> It is better to dwell in a desert land, than with a woman full of chiding, and wrathful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:3–4; PSALMS 112:3; ECCLESIASTES 7:11"> Desirable treasure and oil is in the dwelling place of a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man; and an unprudent man shall destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:58; PROVERBS 22:4"> He that pursueth rightfulness &#91;or followeth rightwiseness&#93; and mercy, shall find life &#91;and rightwiseness&#93; and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:19; PROVERBS 24:5; ECCLESIASTES 9:13–18"> A wise man ascended into the city of strong men, and destroyed the strength of &#91;the&#93; trust thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:3; PROVERBS 18:21; JAMES 1:26"> He that keepeth his mouth and his tongue, keepeth his soul from anguishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 1:1; PROVERBS 18:12; PROVERBS 1:22"> A proud man and boaster is called a fool, that worketh pride in ire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 12:24; MATTHEW 25:26"> Desires slay a slow man; for his hands would not work anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 37:26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:33–35; EPHESIANS 4:28"> All day he coveteth and desireth; but he that is a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man, shall give, and shall not cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 66:3; PROVERBS 15:8; JEREMIAH 6:20"> The offerings of wicked men, that be offered of great trespass, be abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:13; PROVERBS 19:5; PROVERBS 19:9"> A false witness shall perish; a man obedient shall speak victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 3:11; PROVERBS 11:5; HAGGAI 2:18–19"> A wicked man maketh firm his cheer unshamefastly; but he that is rightful &#91;or right&#93;, amendeth his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:39; JEREMIAH 9:23"> No wisdom there is, no prudence there is, no counsel there is, against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 20:7; ISAIAH 31:1; PSALMS 33:17–18"> An horse is made ready to the day of battle; but the Lord shall give health.</span>
