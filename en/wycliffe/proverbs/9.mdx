<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:14; PROVERBS 8:1–3; MATTHEW 22:3–4"> He sent his handmaids, that they should call to the tower; and to the walls of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:32; PROVERBS 9:16; PROVERBS 8:5"> If any man is little in wit, come he to me. And wisdom spake to unwise men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 6:27; SONG OF SONGS 5:1; PROVERBS 9:17"> Come ye, eat ye my bread; and drink ye the wine, which I have meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; PROVERBS 4:11; LUKE 13:24"> Forsake ye young childhood, and live ye; and go ye by the way of prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:12; PROVERBS 23:9; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:16"> He that teacheth a scorner, doeth wrong to himself; and he that reproveth a wicked man, engendereth a wem to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:5; PROVERBS 13:18; PROVERBS 23:9"> Do not thou reprove a scorner; lest he hate thee. Reprove thou a wise man; and he shall love thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:5; MATTHEW 13:11–12; 1ST JOHN 5:13"> Give thou occasion to a wise man; and wisdom shall be increased to him. Teach thou a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man; and he shall hasten to take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 111:10; JOB 28:28; PROVERBS 1:7"> The beginning of wisdom is the dread of the Lord; and prudence is the knowing of saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:27; PROVERBS 3:16; PROVERBS 3:2"> For thy days shall be multiplied by me; and &#91;the&#93; years of life shall be increased to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 22:2–3; 2ND PETER 3:3–4; EZEKIEL 18:20"> If thou art wise, thou shalt be wise to thyself, and to thy neighbours. Forsooth if thou art a scorner, thou alone shalt bear evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:11; PROVERBS 5:6; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:4"> A fond &#91;or foolish&#93; woman, and full of cry, and full of unleaveful lusts, and that knoweth nothing utterly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:3; PROVERBS 7:10–12"> sitteth in the doors of her house, on a seat, in an high place of the city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:25–27; PROVERBS 23:27–28; PROVERBS 7:13–15"> to call men passing by the way, and men going in their journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:4"> Who is a little man in wit; bow he to me. And she spake to a coward &#91;or to the sorry-hearted&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:17; PROVERBS 23:31–32; JAMES 1:14–15"> Waters of theft be sweeter, and bread hid is sweeter. &#91;Stolen waters be sweeter, and hid bread more sweet.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:27; PROVERBS 2:18–19; PROVERBS 5:5"> And &#91;he&#93; knew not that giants be there; and the guests of her be in the depths of hell. Soothly he that shall be applied, either fastened, to her, shall go down to hells &#91;or shall fall down into hell&#93;. For why he that goeth away from her shall be saved.</span>
