<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:1; LUKE 10:20; 1ST KINGS 1:47"> Better is a good name, than many riches; for good grace is above silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 31:15; PROVERBS 14:31; 1ST SAMUEL 2:7"> A rich man and a poor man met themselves; the Lord is &#91;the&#93; worker of ever either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:12; PROVERBS 14:16; PROVERBS 29:1"> A fell man seeth evil, and hideth himself; and an innocent man passed, and he was tormented by harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:33; JAMES 4:10; PSALMS 34:9–10"> The end of temperance is the dread of the Lord; riches, and glory, and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:19; 1ST JOHN 5:18; PROVERBS 16:17"> Armours &#91;or Arms&#93; and swords be in the way of a wayward man; but the keeper of his soul goeth away far from those &#91;or from them&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:4; DEUTERONOMY 6:7; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:15"> It is a proverb, A young waxing man after his way, and when he hath waxed eld &#91;or eldeth&#93;, he shall not go away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 4:1; PROVERBS 22:16; MATTHEW 18:25"> A rich man commandeth to poor men; and he that taketh borrowing, is servant of the lender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:7–8; JOB 4:8; ISAIAH 30:31"> He that soweth wickedness shall reap evils; and the rod of his ire &#91;or his wrath&#93; shall be ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; HEBREWS 6:10; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:18–19"> He that is ready to give mercy shall be blessed; for of his loaves he gave some to a poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:20–21; GENESIS 21:9–10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:5–6"> Cast thou out a scorner, and strife shall go out with him; and causes and despisings shall cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:13; MATTHEW 5:8; PSALMS 101:6"> He that loveth the cleanness of heart, shall have the king for a friend, for the grace of his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 16:9; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:8–9; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:8"> The eyes of the Lord keep knowing; and the words of a wicked man be deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:19; PROVERBS 26:13–16; NUMBERS 13:32–33"> A slow man shall say, A lion is withoutforth; I shall be slain in the midst of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:26; PROVERBS 23:27; PROVERBS 6:24–29"> The mouth of an alien woman is a deep ditch; he to whom the Lord is wroth shall fall into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:24; PROVERBS 29:15; PROVERBS 19:18"> Folly is bound together in the heart of a child; and a rod of chastising &#91;or discipline&#93; shall drive it away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:22–23; PSALMS 12:5; JAMES 2:13"> He that falsely challengeth a poor man, to increase his own riches, shall give to a richer man, and he shall be needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:12; PSALMS 90:12; PROVERBS 3:1"> My son, bow down thine ear, and hear thou the words of wise men; but set thou the heart to my teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:16; PROVERBS 2:10; PSALMS 119:103"> That shall be fair to thee, when thou hast kept it in thine heart, and it shall flow again in thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; JEREMIAH 17:7; 1ST PETER 1:21"> That thy trust be in the Lord; wherefore and I have showed it to thee today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:6; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:15–17; 2ND PETER 1:19–21"> Lo! I have described it in three manners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, in three parts of the Old Testament, that be, the law, holy stories, and prophets. (From a “Later Version” gloss.)">a</a></sup>, in thoughts and knowing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 1:3–4; 1ST PETER 3:15; JOHN 20:31"> that I should show to thee the firmness and speeches of truth; to answer of these things to them that sent thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 7:10; MALACHI 3:5; EXODUS 23:6"> Do thou not violence to a poor man, for he is poor; neither defoul thou a needy man in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 140:12; PROVERBS 23:11"> For the Lord shall deem his cause, and he shall torment them that tormented his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:14–17; PROVERBS 21:24"> Do not thou be friend to a wrathful man, neither go thou with a wrathful man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33; PROVERBS 13:20; PSALMS 106:35"> lest peradventure thou learn his ways, and take cause of stumbling to thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:15; PROVERBS 17:18; PROVERBS 6:1–5"> Do not thou be with them that fasten, or bind, their hands, and that proffer themselves to be borrows for debts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:16; 2ND KI 4:1; EXODUS 22:26–27"> for if he hath not whereof he shall restore, what of cause is, that thou take away covering from thy bed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:17; DEUTERONOMY 19:14; PROVERBS 23:10"> Go thou not over the eld marks &#91;or the old terms&#93;, or the boundary stones, which thy fathers have set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:28; MATTHEW 25:21; PROVERBS 12:24"> Thou hast seen a man smart in his work; he shall stand before kings, and he shall not be before unnoble men.</span>
