<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; PROVERBS 23:17; PROVERBS 3:31"> Pursue &#91;or follow&#93; thou not evil men, desire thou not to be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 7:14; PSALMS 28:3"> For the soul of them bethinketh on ravens, and their lips speak frauds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:1; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:9; PROVERBS 9:1"> An house shall be builded by wisdom, and it shall be made strong by prudence, or understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:6; PROVERBS 21:20; PROVERBS 20:15"> &#91;The&#93; Cellars shall be filled in teaching, with all riches precious and full fair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:19; COLOSSIANS 1:11; PROVERBS 21:22"> A wise man is strong, and a learned man is stalworth&#91;y&#93; and mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:14; PROVERBS 20:18; PROVERBS 15:22"> For why battle is begun with ordinance; and health shall be, where many counsels be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:6; PSALMS 10:5; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14"> Wisdom is high to a fool; in the gate he shall not open his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 21:11; EZEKIEL 38:10–11; PROVERBS 14:22"> He that thinketh to do evils, shall be called a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 10:5; ISAIAH 55:7"> The thought of a fool is sin; and a backbiter is abomination of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:28–31; EPHESIANS 3:13; JEREMIAH 51:46"> If thou hast slid, despairest in the day of anguish, thy strength shall be made less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 82:4; ISAIAH 58:6–7; 1ST JOHN 3:16–17"> Deliver thou them, that be led to death; and cease thou not to deliver them, that be drawn to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 34:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:23; JEREMIAH 17:10"> If thou sayest, Strengths or Strong-holds suffice not; he that is &#91;the&#93; beholder of the heart, understandeth, and nothing deceiveth the keeper of thy soul, and he shall yield to a man after his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 7:15; SONG OF SONGS 5:1; PROVERBS 25:16"> My son, eat thou honey, for it is good; and the honeycomb for it is full sweet to thy throat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JAMES 1:25; JEREMIAH 15:16; PROVERBS 2:10"> So and the teaching of wisdom is good to thy soul; and when thou hast found it, thou shalt have hope in the last things, and thine hope shall not perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:24; ISAIAH 32:18; 1ST SAMUEL 23:20–23"> Ambush thou not, and seek not wickedness in the house of a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man, neither waste thou, his resting place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 34:19; PSALMS 37:24; MICAH 7:8–10"> For a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man shall fall seven times in the day, and shall rise again; but wicked men shall fall into evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:12; JOB 31:29; PROVERBS 17:5"> When thine enemy falleth, have thou not joy; and thine heart have not full out joying in his falling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:15–16; LAMENTATIONS 4:21–22"> lest peradventure the Lord see, and it displease him, and he take away his ire &#91;or his wrath&#93; from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 119:115; PSALMS 37:1; PROVERBS 23:17"> Strive thou not with the worst men, neither pursue &#91;or follow&#93; thou wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:9; JOB 18:5–6; PSALMS 11:6"> For why evil men have not hope of things to come, and the lantern of wicked men shall be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 13:1–7; 1ST PETER 2:13–17; TITUS 3:1"> My son, dread thou God, and the king; and be thou not meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; with backbiters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 90:11; HOSEA 5:11; NUMBERS 16:31–35"> For their perdition shall rise together suddenly, and who knoweth the fall of ever either?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:21; PROVERBS 18:5; JOHN 7:24"> Also these things that pursue be to wise men. It is not good to know a person in doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:15; ISAIAH 5:23; PROVERBS 11:26"> Peoples shall curse them that say to a wicked man, Thou art just &#91;or rightwise&#93;; and lineages shall hold them abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 5:20; 1ST SAMUEL 3:13; PROVERBS 28:23"> They that reprove justly sinners, shall be praised; and blessing shall come &#91;up&#93; on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:23; GENESIS 41:38–57; PROVERBS 25:11–12"> He that answereth with rightful &#91;or right&#93; words, shall kiss lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 14:28–30; 1ST KINGS 5:17–18; 1ST KINGS 6:7"> Make ready thy work withoutforth, and work thy field diligently, that thou build thine house afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:25; MATTHEW 26:59–60; PROVERBS 25:18"> Be thou not a witness without reasonable cause against thy neighbour; neither flatter thou any man with thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:22; ROMANS 12:17–21; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15"> Say thou not, As he did to me, so I shall do to him, and I shall yield to each man after his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:11; PROVERBS 6:6–19; JOB 4:8"> I passed by the field of a slow man, and by the vinery &#91;or the vineyard&#93; of a fond &#91;or foolish&#93; man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:18; ISAIAH 5:5; JEREMIAH 4:3"> and lo! nettles had filled all, thorns had covered the higher part thereof, and the wall of stones without mortar was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:11; DEUTERONOMY 13:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:6"> And when I had seen this thing, I setted &#91;or put&#93; in mine heart, and by ensample, I learned teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:6–8; PROVERBS 6:4–11; ROMANS 13:11"> How long sleepest thou, slow man? when shalt thou rise from sleep? Soothly thou shalt sleep a little, thou shalt nap a little, thou shalt join together the hands a little, to take rest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:4; PROVERBS 13:4"> and then thy neediness as a courier shall come to thee, and thy beggary as an armed man.</span>
