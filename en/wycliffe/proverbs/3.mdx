<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:5; JOHN 14:21–24; DEUTERONOMY 6:6–9"> My son, forget thou not my law; and thine heart keep my command-ments.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:3; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:3; HEBREWS 10:16"> Mercy and truth forsake thee not; bind thou those to thy throat, and write them in the tables of thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 2:52; 1ST SAMUEL 2:26; ROMANS 14:18"> And thou shalt find grace, and good teaching before God and men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:5; JEREMIAH 17:7–8; PROVERBS 28:26"> Have thou trust in the Lord, of all thine heart; and lean thou not to thy prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:3; PSALMS 32:8; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> In all thy ways think on him, and he shall dress thy goings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 28:28; ROMANS 12:16; PROVERBS 26:12"> Be thou not wise with thyself; dread thou God, and go away from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:22; PSALMS 147:3; PROVERBS 16:24"> For why health shall be in thy navel, and moisting of thy bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:2–15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:2; EXODUS 23:19"> Honour thou the Lord of thy chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, and of the best of all thy fruits give thou to poor men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; DEUTERONOMY 28:8; PROVERBS 22:9"> and thy barns shall be filled with abundance, and thy pressers &#91;or presses&#93; shall flow with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 5:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; HEBREWS 12:5–12"> My son, cast thou not away the teaching of the Lord; and fail thou not, when thou art chastised of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; PROVERBS 29:17"> For the Lord chastiseth him, whom he loveth; and as a father in the son he pleaseth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:32–35; PROVERBS 4:5–9; ECCLESIASTES 9:15–18"> Blessed is the man that findeth wisdom, and that floweth with prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:16; PROVERBS 8:19; JOB 28:13–19"> The getting thereof is better than the merchandise &#91;or chaffering&#93; of gold and of silver; the fruits thereof be the first and the cleanest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:11; JOB 28:18; PSALMS 73:25–26"> It is more precious than all riches; and all things that be desired, may not be comparisoned to this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:10; PROVERBS 22:4"> Length of days is in the right half thereof, and riches and glory be in the left half thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 1:79; ISAIAH 26:3; PHILIPPIANS 4:8–9"> The ways thereof be fair ways, and all the paths thereof be peaceable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:30; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:7; PROVERBS 15:4"> It is a tree of life to them that take it; and he that holdeth it, is blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 104:24; JEREMIAH 10:12; JOHN 1:3"> The Lord founded the earth by wisdom; he stablished heavens by prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 7:11; PSALMS 65:9–12; JOB 36:27–28"> The depths of waters brake out by his wisdom; and clouds waxed alto-gether with dew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:8; DEUTERONOMY 6:6–9; DEUTERONOMY 32:46–47"> My son, these things float not away &#91;or not flow away&#93; from thine eyes; keep thou my law, and my counsel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:22; PROVERBS 1:9; ISAIAH 38:16"> and life shall be to thy soul, and grace to thy cheeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:12; PROVERBS 10:9; PSALMS 121:3"> Then thou shalt go trustily in thy way; and thy foot shall not stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 3:5; PSALMS 4:8; PROVERBS 6:22"> If thou shalt sleep, thou shalt not dread; thou shalt rest, and thy sleep shall be soft &#91;or and sweet shall be thy sleep&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 41:10–14; PSALMS 112:7; 1ST PETER 3:14"> Dread thou not of sudden fear, and the powers of wicked men falling in on thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 91:3; 1ST SAMUEL 2:9; PROVERBS 14:26"> For the Lord shall be at thy side; and he shall keep thy foot, that thou be not taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:10; JAMES 2:15–16; ROMANS 13:7"> Do not thou forbid to do well him that may; if thou mayest, also do thou well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:13; DEUTERONOMY 24:12–15; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:11"> Say thou not to thy friend, Go, and turn again, and tomorrow I shall give to thee; when thou mayest give anon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:14; PROVERBS 16:29–30; PSALMS 55:20"> Imagine thou not evil to thy friend, when he hath trust in thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:24; PROVERBS 29:22; ROMANS 12:18–21"> Strive thou not against a man without cause, when he doeth none evil to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:1; PSALMS 37:1; GALATIANS 5:21"> Pursue thou not an unjust &#91;or unrightwise&#93; man, pursue &#91;or follow&#93; thou not his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 25:14; JOHN 15:15; PROVERBS 11:20"> For each deceiver is abomination to the Lord; and his speaking is with simple men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 37:22; LEVITICUS 26:14–46; JOB 8:6–7"> Neediness is sent of the Lord in the house of a wicked man; but the dwelling places of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JAMES 4:6; 1ST PETER 5:5; PSALMS 138:6"> He shall scorn &#91;the&#93; scorners; and he shall give grace to mild men.</span>
