<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:5; PROVERBS 9:7–8; PROVERBS 15:20"> A wise son is the teaching of the father; but he that is a scorner, heareth not, when he is reproved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:14; PROVERBS 18:20; PROVERBS 10:11"> A man shall be filled with goods of the fruit of his mouth; but the soul of unpious men is wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:23; PROVERBS 18:21; PROVERBS 18:7"> He that keepeth his mouth, keepeth his soul; but he that is unwary to speak, shall feel evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:24; PROVERBS 12:11; 2ND PETER 1:5–11"> A slow man will, and will not; but the soul of them that work shall be made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:9; EPHESIANS 4:25; PSALMS 119:163"> A just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man shall loathe a false word; but a wicked man shameth, and shall be shamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:3; PROVERBS 11:5–6; PSALMS 140:11"> Rightfulness &#91;or Rightwiseness&#93; keepeth the way of an innocent man; but wickedness deceiveth a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 18:11–14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17; PROVERBS 11:24"> A man is as rich, when he hath nothing; and a man is as poor, when he is in many riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; 1ST PETER 1:18–19; PSALMS 49:6–10"> &#91;The&#93; Redemption of the soul of &#91;a&#93; man is his riches; but he that is poor, suffereth not blaming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:18; PROVERBS 24:20; PROVERBS 20:20"> The light of just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men maketh glad; but the lantern of wicked men shall be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:20; PROVERBS 12:15–16; PROVERBS 17:14"> Strives be ever among proud men; but they that do all things with counsel, be governed by wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:21; PROVERBS 10:2; JAMES 5:1–5"> Hasted chattel &#91;or substance&#93;, that is, gotten hastily, shall be made less; but that that is gathered little and little with hand, shall be multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 16:22; PROVERBS 13:19; PSALMS 119:81–83"> Hope which is delayed, tormenteth the soul; a tree of life is desire coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:16; 2ND JOHN 1:8"> He that backbiteth anything, bindeth himself into &#91;the&#93; time to coming &#91;or to come&#93;; but he that dreadeth the commandment, shall live in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 18:5; PROVERBS 10:11; PROVERBS 14:27"> The law of a wise man is a well of life; that he bow away from the falling of death, that is, of sin, and of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:19; PROVERBS 4:19; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10"> Good teaching shall give grace; a swallow is in the way of despisers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:2; PROVERBS 12:22–23; EPHESIANS 5:17"> A fell &#91;or witting&#93; man doeth all things with counsel; but he that is a fool, shall open folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:13; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:2; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:20"> The messenger of a wicked man shall fall into evil; a faithful messenger is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:5; PROVERBS 12:1; PROVERBS 15:31–32"> Neediness and shame is to him that forsaketh teaching; but he that assenteth to a blamer, shall be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:14–17; PROVERBS 13:12; PROVERBS 29:27"> Desire, if it is &#91;ful&#93; filled, delighteth the soul; fools loathe them that flee evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:33–34; PROVERBS 15:31; PROVERBS 9:6"> He that goeth with wise men, shall be wise; the friend of fools shall be made like them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 32:10; ISAIAH 3:10–11; ROMANS 2:7–10"> Evil pursueth sinners; and goods shall be given to just &#91;or rightwise&#93; men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:26; JOB 27:16–17; PROVERBS 28:8"> A good man shall leave after him heirs, sons, and the sons of sons; and the chattel &#91;or substance&#93; of a sinner is kept to a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:11; PROVERBS 28:19; ECCLESIASTES 5:9"> Many meats be in the newly-tilled fields of fathers; and be gathered to other men without doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:17; PROVERBS 23:13–14; PROVERBS 29:15"> He that spareth the rod, hateth his son; but he that loveth him, teacheth busily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 34:10; PROVERBS 10:3; HEBREWS 13:5"> A just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man eateth, and &#91;ful&#93; filleth his soul; but the womb of wicked men is unable to be filled.</span>
