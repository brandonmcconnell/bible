<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:20–21; MATTHEW 3:3; JOHN 7:37"> Whether wisdom crieth not oft; and prudence giveth her voice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:14; PROVERBS 9:3"> In most sovereign and high tops, above the way, in the midst of paths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 18:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:20; JOB 29:7"> and it standeth beside the gates of the city, in those enclosings, and speaketh, and saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:15; JOHN 3:16; PSALMS 50:1"> A! ye men, I cry oft to you; and my voice is to the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:17–18"> Little children, that is, little of wit, understand ye wisdom; and ye unwise men, perceiveth in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:26; MATTHEW 7:28–29; PROVERBS 23:16"> Hear ye, for I shall speak of great things; and my lips shall be opened, to preach rightful &#91;or even&#93; things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:30; JOHN 8:14; JOHN 17:17"> My throat shall bethink truth; and my lips shall curse a wicked man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 7:46; PSALMS 12:6; PROVERBS 8:13"> My words be just; no shrewd thing, neither wayward is in those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:6; JAMES 1:5; JOHN 7:17"> They be rightful &#91;or right&#93; to them that understand; and they be even to them that find knowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:4–5; PSALMS 119:127; PSALMS 119:72"> Take ye my chastising &#91;or my discipline&#93;, and not money; choose ye teaching, more than treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:14–15; PROVERBS 4:5–7; PROVERBS 16:16"> For wisdom is better than all riches most precious; and all desirable thing may not be comparisoned thereto.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:26; EXODUS 35:30–4; ROMANS 11:33"> I, wisdom, dwell in counsel; and I am among learned thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 97:10; PROVERBS 16:6; 1ST SAMUEL 2:3"> The dread of the Lord hateth evil; I curse boast, and pride, and a shrewd way, and a double-tongued mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:19; ISAIAH 9:6; ISAIAH 40:14"> Counsel is mine, and equity; prudence is mine, and strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 13:1; DANIEL 2:21; MATTHEW 28:18"> Kings reign by me; and makers of laws deem just things by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Princes command by me; and mighty men deem rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 14:23; JOHN 14:21; PSALMS 91:14"> I love them that love me; and they that wake early to me, shall find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:16; MATTHEW 6:33; LUKE 16:11–12"> With me be riches, and glory; sovereign riches, and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:14; PROVERBS 10:20; ECCLESIASTES 7:12"> My fruit is better than gold, and precious stone; and my seeds &#91;or my burgeonings&#93;be better than chosen silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:32; JOHN 10:27–28; PSALMS 32:8"> I go in the ways of rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;, in the midst of the paths of doom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:4; PROVERBS 24:4; MATTHEW 25:46"> that I make rich them that love me, and that I fill their treasures or their treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:17; JOHN 1:1–2; PROVERBS 3:19"> The Lord wielded me in the beginning of his ways; before that he made anything, at the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 17:24; PSALMS 2:6; GENESIS 1:26"> From without beginning I was ordained; and from eld &#91;or old&#93; times, before that the earth was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:20; GENESIS 1:2; 1ST JOHN 4:9"> &#91;The&#93; Depths of waters were not yet; and I was conceived then. The wells of waters had not broken out yet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 90:2; PSALMS 102:25–28; JOB 38:4–11"> and hills stood not altogether yet by firm heaviness; before little hills I was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 1:1–31"> Yet he had not made &#91;the&#93; earth; and floods, and the earths of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:22; JOB 26:10; PROVERBS 3:19"> When he made ready heavens, I was present; when he compassed &#91;or enclosed&#93; the depths of waters by certain law and compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When he made steadfast the air above; and weighed the wells of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 104:9; JEREMIAH 5:22; GENESIS 1:9–10"> When he compassed to the sea his mark; and setted &#91;or put&#93; law to waters, that they should not pass their coasts. When he weighed the foundaments of &#91;the&#93; earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 1:1–3; JOHN 1:18; ISAIAH 42:1"> I was making all things with him. And I delighted by all days, and played before him in all time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 16:3; JOHN 13:1; PSALMS 40:6–8"> and I played in the world; and my delights be to be with the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 11:28; PSALMS 119:1–2; PSALMS 1:1–4"> Now, therefore, sons, hear ye me; blessed be they that keep my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:1; PSALMS 81:11–12; PROVERBS 5:1"> Hear ye teaching, and be ye wise men; and do not ye cast it away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 11:28; MATTHEW 7:24; PROVERBS 3:13"> Blessed is the man that heareth me, and that watcheth at my gates all day; and keepeth &#91;or waiteth&#93; at the posts of my door&#91;s&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 3:36; 1ST JOHN 5:11–12; PROVERBS 12:2"> He that findeth me, shall find life; and he shall draw health of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:32; PROVERBS 1:31; 1ST CORINTHIANS 16:22"> But he that sinneth against me shall hurt his soul; all that hate me, love death.</span>
