<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:15–17; PROVERBS 2:1–6; PROVERBS 20:3"> He that will go away from a friend, seeketh occasions, that is, feigneth causes; in all time he shall be despisable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:7; PROVERBS 12:23; PSALMS 1:1–2"> A fool receiveth not the words of prudence; no but thou say those things, that be turned in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:10; 1ST PETER 4:14; PROVERBS 11:2"> A wicked man, when he cometh into depth of sins, despiseth whole-some lore, and commandment; but scandal and shame pursueth &#91;or followeth&#93; him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:5; PROVERBS 10:11; COLOSSIANS 4:6"> Deep water is the words of the mouth of a man; and a strand floating over is the well of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:15; DEUTERONOMY 16:19; PROVERBS 28:21"> It is not good to take the person of a wicked man in doom, that thou bow away from the truth of doom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:3; PROVERBS 16:27–28; PROVERBS 22:24–25"> The lips of a fool meddle &#91;or mingle&#93; themselves with chidings; and his mouth stirreth strives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:3; PROVERBS 12:13; PROVERBS 10:14"> The mouth of a fool is&#91;the&#93; defouling of him; and his lips be the falling of his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:18; LEVITICUS 19:16; PROVERBS 16:28"> The words of a double-tongued man be as simple; and they come unto the inner things of the womb. Dread casteth down a slow man; forsooth the souls of men turned into women’s condition shall have hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:4; HEBREWS 6:12; PROVERBS 24:30–34"> He that is nesh, and dissolute, either unsteadfast, in his work, is the brother of a man destroying his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 22:3; PSALMS 91:2; ISAIAH 26:4"> A full strong tower is the name of the Lord; a just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man runneth to him, and &#91;he&#93; shall be enhanced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:15; PSALMS 52:5–7; ECCLESIASTES 7:12"> The chattel &#91;or substance&#93; of a rich man is the city of his strength; and as a strong wall compassing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:33; 1ST PETER 5:5; EZEKIEL 28:2"> The heart of man is enhanced, before that it be broken; and it is made meek, before that it be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 7:51; DEUTERONOMY 13:14; PROVERBS 20:25"> He that answereth before that he heareth, showeth himself to be a fool; and worthy of shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:13; PROVERBS 17:22; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> The spirit of a man sustaineth his feebleness; but who may sustain a spirit light to be wroth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; PROVERBS 15:14; PROVERBS 1:5"> The heart of a prudent man shall hold steadfastly knowing; and the ear of wise men seeketh teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:6; GENESIS 43:11; PROVERBS 17:8"> The gift of a man alargeth his way; and maketh space to him before princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 24:12–13; 2ND SAMUEL 19:24–27"> A just &#91;or rightwise&#93; man is the first accuser of himself; his friend cometh, and shall search him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:33; NEHEMIAH 11:1; 1ST CHRONICLES 24:31"> Lot ceaseth against-sayings; and deemeth also among mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 15:39; PROVERBS 6:19"> A brother that is helped of a brother, is as a steadfast city; and &#91;the&#93; dooms be as the bars of cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:13–14; PROVERBS 25:11–12; PROVERBS 13:2"> A man’s womb shall be &#91;ful&#93; filled of the fruit of his mouth; and the seeds of his lips shall fill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:29; MATTHEW 12:35–37; COLOSSIANS 4:6"> Death and life be in the works of &#91;the&#93; tongue; they that love it, shall eat the fruits thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:14; PROVERBS 31:10–31; PROVERBS 12:4"> He that findeth a good woman, findeth a good thing; and of the Lord he shall draw up mirth. He that putteth away a good woman, putteth away a good thing; but he that holdeth an adulteress, is a fool and unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JAMES 2:3; JAMES 1:9–11; RUTH 2:7"> A poor man shall speak with beseechings; and a rich man shall speak sternly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:17; JOHN 15:13–15; PROVERBS 27:9"> A man friendly to fellowship shall more be a friend, than a brother.</span>
