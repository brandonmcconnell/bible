<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:3; LUKE 11:28"> My son, keep thou my words; and keep my behests to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 17:8; PROVERBS 4:4; LEVITICUS 18:5"> Keep thou my behests, and thou shalt live; and my law, as the apple of thine eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:18–20; PROVERBS 3:3; DEUTERONOMY 6:8–9"> Bind thou it in thy fingers; write thou it in the tables of thine heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:6–8; PROVERBS 2:2–4; SONG OF SONGS 8:1"> Say thou to wisdom, Thou art my sister; and call thou prudence thy love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:16; PROVERBS 6:24; PROVERBS 5:3"> That it keep thee from a strange woman; and from an alien woman, that maketh her words sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 26:8; JUDGES 5:28; 2ND SAMUEL 6:16"> For she saith, For why from the window of mine house, by the lattice, I beheld;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:32; PROVERBS 1:22; PROVERBS 1:32"> and I see little children, that is, fools that have little wit. I behold a young man coward &#91;or the sorry-hearted young man&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:8; PROVERBS 7:12; JUDGES 16:1"> that passeth by &#91;or through&#93; the streets, beside the corner; and he goeth nigh the way of her house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 24:13–15; ROMANS 13:12–14; EPHESIANS 5:11"> in dark time, when the day draweth to night, in the darkness and mist in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:9; ISAIAH 3:16–24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:2–3"> And lo! a woman, made ready with ornament of an whore to deceive souls, meeteth him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:13; TITUS 2:5; PROVERBS 31:10–31"> and she is a jangler, and going about, and unpatient of rest, and may not stand in the house with her feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:28; PROVERBS 9:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:3"> and now withoutforth, now in &#91;the&#93; streets, now beside &#91;the&#93; corners, she ambusheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 39:12; NUMBERS 25:6–8; GENESIS 39:7"> And she taketh, and kisseth the young man; and flattereth with wooing cheer, that is, unrestfully, and without shame, and saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:27; LEVITICUS 7:11; PROVERBS 17:1"> I owed sacrifices for health; today I have yielded my vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore I went out into thy meeting, and I desired to see thee; and I have found thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:9; EZEKIEL 27:7; 1ST KINGS 10:28"> I have made my bed with cords, I have arrayed it with tapets painted &#91;or with painted tapets&#93; of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; ISAIAH 57:7–9; SONG OF SONGS 4:13–14"> I have besprinkled my bed with myrrh, and aloes, and canel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Come thou, be we filled with touching of teats, and use we embracings that be coveted; till the day begin to be clear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:11; LUKE 12:45–46; LUKE 12:39"> For mine husband is not in his house; he is gone a full long way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 2:4"> He took with him a bag of money; he shall turn again into his house in the day of &#91;the&#93; full moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:3; PROVERBS 7:5; 2ND KI 4:8"> She bound him with many words; and she drew forth him with flatterings of lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 13:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:13"> Anon he as an ox led to slain sacrifice pursueth &#91;or followeth&#93; her, and as a jolly lamb and unknowing; and the fool knoweth not, that he is drawn to bonds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:12; PROVERBS 9:18; PROVERBS 1:17"> till an arrow pierce his maw. As if a bird hasteth to the snare; and knoweth not, that it is done of the peril of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:7; PROVERBS 4:1; 1ST JOHN 2:1"> Now therefore, my son, hear thou me; and perceive the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:8; ISAIAH 53:6; PROVERBS 6:25"> Lest thy soul be drawn away in the ways of her; neither be thou deceived in the paths of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:11; NEHEMIAH 13:26; 1ST KINGS 11:1–2"> For she hath cast down many wounded men; and all &#91;the&#93; strongest men were slain of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:5; PROVERBS 9:18; PROVERBS 2:18–19"> The ways of hell is her house; and pierce into the inner things of death.</span>
