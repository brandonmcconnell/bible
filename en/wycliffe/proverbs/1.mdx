<div className="bp">

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:25–26; LUKE 21:34–35; PSALMS 58:9"> When sudden wretchedness falleth in, and perishing befalleth as tempest; when tribulation and anguish cometh on you.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MICAH 3:4; JAMES 4:3; JEREMIAH 11:11"> Then they shall call me, and I shall not hear; they shall rise early, and they shall not find me.</span>
