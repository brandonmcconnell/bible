<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 1:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:6–1"> And I saw seven angels standing in the sight of God, and seven trumps were given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:4; PSALMS 141:2"> And another angel came, and stood before the altar, and had a golden censer; and many incenses were given to him, that he should give of the prayers of all saints on the golden altar, that is before the throne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 141:2; LUKE 1:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:3"> And the smoke of the incenses of the prayers of the holy men ascended or went up from the angel’s hand before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:19; EZEKIEL 10:2–7"> And the angel took the censer, and filled it of the fire of the altar, and cast &#91;it&#93; into the earth. And thunders, and voices, and lightnings were made, and a great earth-moving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:2"> And the seven angels, that had the seven trumps, made them ready, that they should trump.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:18"> And the first angel trumped; and hail was made, and fire mingled together in blood; and it was sent into the earth. And the third part of the earth was burnt, and the third part of trees was burnt, and all the green grass was burnt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:25; EXODUS 7:17–21; MARK 11:23"> And the second angel trumped; and as a great hill burning with fire was cast into the sea; and the third part of the sea was made blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:12"> and the third part of creature was dead, that had lives in the sea, and the third part of &#91;the&#93; ships perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:1; ISAIAH 14:12"> And the third angel trumped; and a great star burning as a little brand, fell from heaven; and it fell into the third part of floods, and into the wells of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:15; JEREMIAH 9:15; DEUTERONOMY 29:18"> And the name of the star is said Wormwood. And the third part of waters was made into wormwood; and many men were dead of the waters, for those were made bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 13:10; JOEL 2:31; JOEL 2:10"> And the fourth angel trumped; and the third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of &#91;the&#93; stars, so that the third part of them was darked, and the third part of the day shined not, and also of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:6"> And I saw, and heard the voice of an eagle flying by the middle of heaven, and saying with a great voice, Woe! woe! woe! to men that dwell in earth, of the other voices of the three angels, that shall trump after.</span>
