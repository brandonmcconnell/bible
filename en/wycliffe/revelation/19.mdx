<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:43; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:20"> for true and just be the dooms of him, which deemed the great whore, that defouled &#91;or corrupted&#93; the earth in her lechery, and avenged the blood of his servants, of the hands of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 34:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:18; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:11"> And again they said, Alleluia. And the smoke of it goeth up, into the worlds of worlds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:14; NEHEMIAH 8:6; PSALMS 106:48"> And the four and twenty elder men and the four beasts felled down, and worshipped God sitting on the throne, and said, Amen, Alleluia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 134:1; PSALMS 148:11–13; PSALMS 150:6"> And a voice went out of the throne, and said, All the servants of our God, say ye praisings to our God, and ye that dread God, small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 97:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:2; PSALMS 93:1"> And I heard a voice &#91;as&#93; of a great trump, as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of great thunders, saying, Alleluia; for our Lord God almighty hath reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:9"> Joy we, and make we mirth, &#91;or Joy we withinforth, and glad we withoutforth&#93;, and give glory to him; for the weddings of the lamb came, and the wife of him made ready herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 61:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:13–14; PSALMS 132:9"> And it was given to her, that she cover her&#91;self&#93; with white bissyn shining; for why bissyn is the justifyings of saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:6; MATTHEW 22:2–4; LUKE 14:15–16"> And he said to me, Write thou, Blessed be they that be called to the supper of the weddings of the lamb. And he said to me, These words of God be true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:8–9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:25–26; JOHN 5:39"> And I felled down before his feet, to worship him. And he said to me, See thou, that thou do not; I am a servant with thee, and of thy brethren, having the witnessing of Jesus; worship thou God. For the witnessing of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 96:13; ISAIAH 11:3–5; PSALMS 98:9"> And I saw heaven opened, and lo! a white horse, and he that sat on him was called Faithful and soothfast; and with rightwiseness he deemeth, and fighteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:16; LUKE 10:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:17–18"> And his eyes were as flame of fire, and in his head many diadems; and he had a name written, which no man knew, but he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 1:1; ISAIAH 63:1–6; JOHN 1:14"> And he was clothed in a cloth sprinkled with blood; and the name of him was called The Son of God &#91;or The Word of God&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDE 1:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:8"> And the hosts that be in heaven, pursued him on white horses, clothed with bissyn, white and clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 63:2–6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:27"> And a sword sharp on either side &#91;or on each side&#93; came forth of his mouth, that with it he smite folks; and he shall rule them with an iron rod. And he treadeth the presser of wine of strong vengeance of the wrath of almighty God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:15; DANIEL 2:47"> And he hath written in his cloth, and in the hip &#91;or in his hem&#93;, King of kings and Lord of lords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:21; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:13; EZEKIEL 39:17–20"> And I saw an angel, standing in the sun; and he cried with a great voice, and said to all birds that flew by the middle of heaven, Come ye, and be ye gathered &#91;together&#93; to the great supper of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:18–20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:15; 1ST SAMUEL 17:44"> that ye eat the flesh of kings, and &#91;the&#93; flesh of tribunes, and &#91;the&#93; flesh of strong men, and flesh of horses, and of those that sit on them, and the flesh of all free men and of bondmen, and of small and of great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:12–14"> And I saw the beast, and the kings of the earth, and the hosts of them gathered, to make battle with him, that sat on the horse, and with his host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 2:8–11"> And the beast was caught, and with her the false prophet, that made signs before her; in which he deceived them that took the character &#91;or the mark&#93; of the beast, and that worshipped the image of it. These two were sent quick into the pool of fire, burning with brimstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:11–15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:17–19"> And the others were slain with the sword of him that sat on the horse, that cometh forth of the mouth of him; and all birds were &#91;full&#93;-filled with the flesh of them.</span>
