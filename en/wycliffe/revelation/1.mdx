<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:6; GALATIANS 1:12"> Apocalypse of Jesus Christ &#91;or The revelation of Jesus Christ&#93;, which God gave to him to make open to his servants, which things it behooveth to be made soon. And he signified, sending by his angel to his servant John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:6; JOHN 19:35; 1ST JOHN 1:1"> which bare witnessing to the word of God, and witnessing of Jesus Christ, in these things, whatever things he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 11:28; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:10; ROMANS 13:11"> Blessed is he that readeth, and he that heareth the words of this prophecy, and keepeth those things that be written in it; for the time is nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 11:2; JOHN 1:1; HEBREWS 13:8"> John to the seven churches, that be in Asia, grace and peace to you, of him that is, and that was, and that is to coming &#91;or is to come&#93;; and of the seven spirits, that be in the sight of his throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 89:27; HEBREWS 9:14; 1ST JOHN 1:7"> and of Jesus Christ, that is a faithful witness, the first begotten of dead men, and prince of kings of the earth; which loved us, and washed us from our sins in his blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:6; EXODUS 19:6"> and made us a kingdom, and priests to God and to his Father; to him be glory and empire into worlds of worlds. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:30; ZECHARIAH 12:10; DANIEL 7:13"> Lo! he cometh with clouds, and each eye shall see him, and they that pricked him; and all the kindreds of the earth shall bewail themselves on him. Yea, Amen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:13; ISAIAH 43:10"> I am alpha and omega, the beginning and the end, saith the Lord God, that is, and that was, and that is to coming &#91;or is to come&#93;, almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:10; JOHN 16:33"> I, John, your brother, and partner in tribulation, and kingdom, and patience in Christ Jesus, was in an isle, that is called Patmos, for the word of God, and for the witnessing of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 12:2–4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:1–2"> I was in Spirit in the Lord’s day &#91;or in the Sunday&#93;, and I heard behind me a great voice, as of a trump,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:1; JEREMIAH 30:2; HABAKKUK 2:2"> saying to me, Write thou in a book that thing that thou seest, and send to the seven churches that be in Asia; to Ephesus, to Smyrna, and to Pergamum, and to Thyatira, and to Sardis, and to Philadelphia, and to Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:2; EXODUS 25:37; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:20–1"> And I turned, that I should see the voice that spake with me; and I turned, and I saw seven candlesticks of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13; DANIEL 10:5–6; DANIEL 10:16"> and in the middle of the seven golden candlesticks one like to the Son of man, clothed with a long garment, and girded at the teats with a golden girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 10:6; DANIEL 7:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:12"> And the head of him and his hairs were white, as white wool, and as snow; and the eyes of him as flame of fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:2; DANIEL 10:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:2"> and his feet like to latten &#91;or like the dross of gold&#93;, as in a burning chimney; and the voice of him as the voice of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:12"> And he had in his right hand seven stars, and a sword sharp on ever either side &#91;or on both sides&#93; went out of his mouth; and his face as the sun shineth in his virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:13; DANIEL 10:8–10"> And when I had seen him, I felled down at his feet, as dead. And he putted his right hand on me, and said, Do not thou dread; I am the first and the last;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 6:9; HEBREWS 7:25; ROMANS 14:8–9"> and I am alive, and I was dead; and lo! I am living into worlds of worlds, and I have the keys of death and of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:1–11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:11–3"> Therefore write thou which things thou hast seen, and which be, and which it behooveth to be done after these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:16"> The sacrament &#91;or The mystery&#93; of the seven stars, which thou sawest in my right hand, and the seven golden candlesticks; the seven stars be &#91;the&#93; angels of the seven churches, and the seven candlesticks be &#91;the&#93; seven churches.</span>
