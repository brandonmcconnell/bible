<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:27; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:16; JOHN 14:23"> And I heard a great voice from the throne, saying, Lo! the tabernacle of God is with men, and he shall dwell with them; and they shall be his people, and he God with them shall be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:26"> And God shall wipe away each tear from the eyes of them; and death shall no more be, neither mourning, neither crying, neither sorrow, shall be over; which first things went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 5:17; ISAIAH 42:9; ISAIAH 43:19"> And he said, that sat in the throne, Lo! I make all things new. And he said to me, Write thou, for these words be most faithful and true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 7:37–38; JOHN 4:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:17"> And he said to me, It is done; I am alpha and omega, the beginning and the end. I shall give freely of the well of quick water to him that thirsteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:18; HEBREWS 8:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:11"> He that shall overcome, shall wield these things; and I shall be God to him, and he shall be son to me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:1–17"> And one came of the seven angels, having vials full of the seven last vengeances &#91;or the seven last plagues&#93;. And he spake with me, and said, Come thou, and I shall show to thee the spousess, the wife of the lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:2; EZEKIEL 11:24; EZEKIEL 40:1–49"> And he took me up in Spirit into a great hill and high; and he showed to me the holy city of Jerusalem, coming down from heaven of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:1"> having the clarity of God; and the light of it like &#91;to&#93; a precious stone, as the stone jasper, as crystal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 48:31–34; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:4–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:17–21"> And it had a wall great and high, having twelve gates, and in the gates of it twelve angels, and names written in, that be the names of &#91;the&#93; twelve lineages of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 48:31–34"> from the east three gates, and from the north three gates, and from the south three gates, and from the west three gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:10; EPHESIANS 2:20; ISAIAH 54:11"> And the wall of the city had twelve foundaments, and in them, the twelve names of the twelve apostles, and of the lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:1–2; EZEKIEL 41:1–5"> And he that spake with me, had a golden measure of a reed, that he should mete the city, and the gates of it, and the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 48:35; EZEKIEL 48:8–20"> And the city was set in square; and the length of it is so much, as much as is the breadth. And he meted the city with the reed, by furlongs twelve thousands. And the height, and the length and the breadth of it, be even.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:4; DEUTERONOMY 3:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:18"> And he meted the walls of it, of an hundred and forty and four cubits, by measure of man, that is, of the angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:21; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:19"> And the building of the wall thereof was of the stone jasper. And the city itself was clean gold, like &#91;to&#93; clean glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:11–12; EXODUS 28:17–21; JOB 28:16–19"> And the foundaments of the wall of the city were adorned with all precious stone. The first foundament, jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:3"> the fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolyte; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprase; the eleventh, jacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:18; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 17:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:2"> And the twelve gates be twelve margarites, by each; and each gate was of each margarite. And the streets of the city were clean gold, as of glass full shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 4:23; 1ST KINGS 8:27; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:6"> And I saw no temple in it, for the Lord God almighty and the lamb, is the temple of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:19–20; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:11"> And the city hath no need of the sun, neither moon, that they shine in it; for the clarity of God shall lighten &#91;or shall light&#93; it; and the lamb is the lantern of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:26; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:2; ZECHARIAH 8:22–23"> And folks shall walk in the light of it; and the kings of the earth shall bring their glory and honour into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 60:11; ZECHARIAH 14:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:5"> And the gates of it shall not be closed by day; and night shall not be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:24"> And they shall bring the glory and honour of folks into it.</span>
