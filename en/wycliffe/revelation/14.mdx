<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:8; EZEKIEL 43:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:15"> And I heard a voice from heaven, as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of a great thunder; and the voice which I heard, was as of many harpers harping in their harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 5:9; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:3"> And they sung as a new song before the seat of God, and before the four beasts, and the elder men. And no man might say the song, but those hundred thousand and four and forty thousand, that be bought from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:4; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:2; JAMES 1:18"> These it be, that be not defouled with women; for they be virgins. These pursue the lamb, whither ever he shall go; these be bought of all men, the first fruits to God, and to the lamb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:27; PSALMS 32:2; ZEPHANIAH 3:13"> and in the mouth of them leasing is not found; for they be without wem before the throne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 16:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 8:13; EPHESIANS 3:9–11"> And I saw another angel, flying by the middle of heaven, having an everlasting gospel &#91;or the everlasting gospel&#93;, that he should preach &#91;or evangelize&#93; to men sitting on the earth, and on each folk, and lineage, and language, and people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 20:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:4; NEHEMIAH 9:6"> and said with a great voice, Dread ye the Lord, and give ye to him honour, for the hour of his doom cometh; and worship ye him, that made heaven and earth, the sea, and all things that be in them, and the wells of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 21:9; JEREMIAH 51:7–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:2–3"> And another angel pursued, saying, That great &#91;city&#93; Babylon fell down, fell down, which gave drink to all folks of the wine of &#91;the&#93; wrath of her fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:6–8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:3–6"> And the third angel pursued them, and said with a great voice, If any man worship the beast, and the image of it, and taketh the character in his forehead, either in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 51:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:19; JEREMIAH 25:15–17"> this shall drink of the wine of God’s wrath, that is mingled with clear wine in the cup of his wrath, and &#91;he&#93; shall be tormented with fire and brimstone, in the sight of &#91;the&#93; holy angels, and before the sight of the lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:3; ISAIAH 34:10; MATTHEW 25:41"> And the smoke of their torments shall ascend or go up into the worlds of worlds; neither they have rest day and night, which worship the beast and his image, and if any take the character of his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 13:10; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:17; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:10"> Here is the patience of saints, which keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:6; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:7–8; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:10"> And I heard a voice from heaven, saying to me, Write thou, Blessed be dead men, that die in the Lord; from henceforth now the Spirit saith, that they rest of their travails; for the works of them pursue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 7:13; ISAIAH 19:1; MATTHEW 17:5"> And I saw, and lo! a white cloud, and above the cloud a sitter &#91;or one sitting&#93;, like the son of man, having in his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOEL 3:13; MATTHEW 13:39; MARK 4:29"> And another angel went out of the temple, and cried with great voice to him that sat on the cloud, Send thy sickle, and reap, for the hour cometh, that it be reaped; for the corn of the earth is ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:19; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:14"> And he that sat on the cloud, sent his sickle into the earth, and reaped the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:18; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:14–15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:5–6"> And another angel went out of the temple, that is in heaven, and he also had a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOEL 3:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:15–16"> And another angel went out from the altar, that had power on fire; and he cried with a great voice to him that had the sharp sickle, and said, Send thy sharp sickle, and cut away &#91;or cut off&#93; the clusters of the vineyard of the earth, for the grapes of it be ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:32–33; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:15–21"> And the angel sent his sickle into the earth, and gathered grapes of the vineyard of the earth, and sent into the great pit of God’s wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:14–21; ISAIAH 63:1–3"> And the pit was trodden without the city, and the blood went out of the pit, till to the horses’ bridles, by furlongs a thousand and six hundred.</span>
