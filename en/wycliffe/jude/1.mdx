</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:17; 1ST PETER 2:17; 2ND PETER 2:10–12"> In like manner also these that defoul the flesh, and despise lordship, and blaspheme majesty.</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND PETER 2:18; 2ND PETER 2:10; PHILIPPIANS 2:14"> These be grutchers full of com-plaints, wandering after their desires;<br />and the mouth of them speaketh pride, worshipping persons, because of winning.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:3; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:3; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:1–5"> which said to you, that in the last times there shall come beguilers &#91;or scorners&#93;, wandering after their own desires, not in piety.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:14; PROVERBS 18:1; ROMANS 8:9"> These be, which separate them-selves, beastly men, not having Spirit.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:16; LAMENTATIONS 3:25–26; HEBREWS 9:28"> and keep yourselves in the love of God, and abide ye the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ into life ever-lasting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JAMES 5:19–20; GALATIANS 6:1; 1ST JOHN 5:16–18"> And reprove ye these men that be deemed,</span>

</div>
