</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 21:16–17; 2ND KI 18:15–16; 2ND KI 12:18"> Ye took away my silver and gold, and ye brought my desirable things and fairest things into your temples of idols.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:5–6; JEREMIAH 23:8; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:6"> Lo! I shall raise them from the place in which ye sold them; and I shall turn your yielding into your head.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:3–4; ISAIAH 8:9–10; EZEKIEL 38:7"> Cry ye this thing among heathen men, hallow ye battle, raise ye up strong men; all men warriors, nigheth, and ascend or goeth up.</span>
</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:1–12; ZECHARIAH 14:8; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:1–2"> And it shall be, in that day, mountains shall drop with sweetness, and little hills shall flow with milk, and waters shall go by all the rivers of Judah; and a well shall go out of the house of the Lord, and shall moist the strand of thorns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:1–15; JEREMIAH 51:35; ZECHARIAH 14:18–19"> Egypt shall be into desolation, and Idumea into desert of perdition; for that that they did wickedly against the sons of Judah, and shedded out innocent blood in their land.</span>
</div>
