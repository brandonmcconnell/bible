<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:9–10; NUMBERS 25:1; JUDGES 5:11"> My people, bethink, I pray, what Balak, king of Moab, thought, and what Balaam, son of Beor, of Shittim, answered to him till to Gilgal, that thou shouldest know the rightwiseness of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 51:16–17; ROMANS 10:2–3; PSALMS 40:6–8"> What worthy thing shall I offer to the Lord? shall I bow the knee to the high God? Whether shall I offer to him brunt sacrifices, and calves of one year?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND KI 16:3; 1ST SAMUEL 15:22; PSALMS 51:16"> Whether God may be satisfied in thousands of wethers, either in many thousands of fat goat bucks? <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:12–13; PROVERBS 21:3; COLOSSIANS 3:12"> I shall show to thee, thou man, what is good, and what the Lord asketh of thee; forsooth for to do doom, and for to love mercy, and be busy for to walk with thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; HOSEA 14:9; ISAIAH 30:27"> The voice of the Lord crieth to the city, and health shall be to all men dreading thy name. Ye lineages, hear; and who shall approve it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 3:10; AMOS 8:5–6; JEREMIAH 5:26–27"> Yet fire is in the house of unpious men, the treasures of wickedness, and a less measure, which make me full of wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 12:7; LEVITICUS 19:36; PROVERBS 16:11"> Whether I shall justify the wicked balance, and the guileful weights of a little sack,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:23; JEREMIAH 9:8; HOSEA 7:13"> in which rich men thereof be filled with wickedness? And men dwelling therein spake leasing, and the tongue of them was guileful in the mouth of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:23; DEUTERONOMY 28:21–22; HOSEA 13:16"> And I therefore began for to smite thee, into perdition on thy sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HOSEA 4:10; LEVITICUS 26:26; ISAIAH 65:13"> Thou shalt eat, and shalt not be &#91;ful&#93; filled, and thy meeking is in the middle of thee; and thou shalt take, and shalt not save; and which thou shalt save, I shall give into sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:13; AMOS 5:11; JEREMIAH 12:13"> Thou shalt sow, and shalt not reap; thou shalt tread the olive, and shalt not be anointed with oil; and make must, and shalt not drink wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:51; 1ST KINGS 16:25–33; JEREMIAH 7:24"> And thou keptest the behests of Omri, and all the work of the house of Ahab, and hast walked in the lusts of them, that I should give thee into perdition, and men dwelling in it into hissing, either scorning, and ye shall bear the shame of my people.</span>
