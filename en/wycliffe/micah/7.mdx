<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ISAIAH 28:4; ISAIAH 24:13; HOSEA 9:10"> Woe to me, for I am made as he that gathereth in harvest raisins of grapes; there is no cluster for to eat; my soul desired figs ripe before others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 12:1; ISAIAH 57:1; ISAIAH 59:7"> The holy perished from &#91;the&#93; earth, and rightful is not in men; all ambush, either set treason, in blood; a man hunteth his brother to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; PROVERBS 4:16–17; AMOS 5:12"> The evil of their hands they say good; the prince asketh, and the doomsman is in yielding; and a great man spake the desire of his soul, and they troubled altogether it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ISAIAH 22:5; EZEKIEL 2:6; ISAIAH 10:3"> He that is the best in them, is as a paliurus, that is, a teasel, either a sharp bush; and he that is rightful is as a thorn of hedge. The day of thy beholding, thy visiting cometh, now shall be destroying of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:4; PSALMS 118:8–9; MATTHEW 10:16"> Do not ye believe to a friend, and do not ye trust in a duke; from her that sleepeth in thy bosom, keep thou the closings of thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:35–36; LUKE 12:53; MATTHEW 10:21"> For the son doeth wrong to the father, and the daughter shall rise against her mother, and the wife of the son against the mother of her husband; the enemies of a man be the ones at home, either the house-hold meine, of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:25–26; PSALMS 37:7; 1ST JOHN 5:14–15"> Forsooth I shall behold to the Lord, I shall abide God my saviour; the Lord my God shall hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; ISAIAH 9:2; PSALMS 27:1"> Thou, mine enemy, be not glad on me, for I fell down, I shall rise up again; when I sit in darknesses, the Lord is my light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 37:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:5; 1ST SAMUEL 24:15"> I shall bear wrath of the Lord, for I have sinned to him, till he deem my cause, and make my doom; he shall lead out me into light, I shall see &#91;the&#93; rightwiseness of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:5; JEREMIAH 50:33–34; PSALMS 42:10"> And mine enemy shall behold me, and she shall be covered with confusion, which saith to me, Where is thy Lord God? Mine eyes shall see her, now she shall be into defouling, as clay, either fen, of streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="AMOS 9:11–15; NEHEMIAH 2:8; NEHEMIAH 2:17"> Day shall come, that thy walls be builded; in that day law shall be made afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ISAIAH 19:23–25; ISAIAH 11:16; HOSEA 11:11"> In that day, and Assur shall come till to thee, and till to strong cities, and from strong cities till to &#91;the&#93; flood; and to sea from sea, and to hill from hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 3:10–11; JEREMIAH 25:11; MICAH 6:13"> And &#91;the&#93; earth shall be into desolation for her dwellers, and for fruit of the thoughts of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 23:1–4; MICAH 5:4; PSALMS 100:3"> Feed thou thy people in thy rod, the flock of thine heritage, that dwell alone in &#91;the&#93; wild wood; in the middle of Carmel they shall be fed of Bashan and of Gilead, as by eld &#91;or old&#93; days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 78:12–72; EXODUS 3:20; PSALMS 68:22"> by days of thy going out of the land of Egypt. I shall show to him wonderful things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ISAIAH 26:11; ISAIAH 52:15; JOB 29:9–10"> heathen men shall see, and they shall be confounded on all their strength; they shall put hands on their mouth, the ears of them shall be deaf;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:23; PSALMS 72:9; PSALMS 18:45"> they shall lick dust as a serpent; as creeping things of &#91;the&#93; earth they shall be disturbed, or troubled, out of their houses; they shall not desire our Lord God, and they shall dread thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:25; ISAIAH 44:22; PSALMS 103:8–9"> God, who is like thee, that doest away wickedness, and bearest over the sin of the remnants of thine heritage? He shall no more send in his strong vengeance, for he is willing or desiring mercy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 38:17; ROMANS 6:14; ISAIAH 43:25"> he shall turn again, and have mercy on us. He shall put down our wickednesses, and shall cast far into deepness of the sea all our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 28:13–14; LUKE 1:72–74; DEUTERONOMY 7:8"> Thou shalt give truth to Jacob, mercy to Abraham, which thou sworest to our fathers from eld &#91;or old&#93; days.</span>
