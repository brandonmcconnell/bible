<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 1:12–13; JOHN 8:42; 1ST JOHN 2:29"> Each man that believeth that Jesus is Christ, is born of God; and each man that loveth him that engendered &#91;or that engendereth&#93;, loveth him that is born of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 13:34–35; 1ST JOHN 2:5; 1ST JOHN 3:22–24"> In this thing we know, that we love the children of God, when we love God, and do his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 14:15; JOHN 15:10; JOHN 14:21–24"> For this is the charity of God, that we keep his commandments; and his commandments be not heavy &#91;or grievous&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 15:57; 1ST JOHN 4:4; JOHN 16:33"> For all thing that is born of God, overcometh the world; and this is the victory that overcometh the world, our faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:15; 1ST JOHN 5:1"> And who is he that overcometh the world, but he that believeth that Jesus is the Son of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 15:26; HEBREWS 9:14; JOHN 16:13"> This is Jesus Christ, that came by water and blood; not in water only, but in water and blood. And the Spirit is he that witnesseth, that Christ is &#91;the&#93; truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:19; 2ND CORINTHIANS 13:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:32"> For three be, that give witnessing in heaven, the Father, the Son &#91;or the Word&#93;, and the Holy Ghost; and these three be one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST JOHN 5:6–7; JOHN 15:26; 1ST PETER 3:21"> And three be, that give witnessing in earth, the Spirit, water, and blood; and these three be one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:16–17; MATTHEW 17:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 17:31"> If we receive the witnessing of men, the witnessing of God is more; for this is the witnessing of God, that is more, for he witnessed of his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 8:16; GALATIANS 4:6; JOHN 3:33"> He that believeth in the Son of God, hath the witnessing of God in him. He that believeth not to the Son, maketh him a liar; for he believeth not in the witnessing that God witnessed of his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 6:23; 1ST JOHN 2:25; JOHN 3:36"> And this is the witnessing, for God gave to you &#91;or to us&#93; everlasting life, and this life is in his Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 3:36; JOHN 5:24; JOHN 1:12"> He that hath the Son of God, hath also life; he that hath not the Son of God, hath not life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 20:31; JOHN 1:12; 2ND PETER 1:10–11"> I write to you these things, that ye know, that ye have everlasting life, which believe in the name of God’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 14:13; JEREMIAH 33:3; JEREMIAH 29:12–13"> And this is the trust which we have to God &#91;or that we have in God&#93;, that whatever thing we ask after his will, he shall hear us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 11:9–10; MARK 11:24; PROVERBS 15:29"> And we know, that he heareth us, whatever thing we &#91;shall&#93; ask; we know, that we have the askings, that we ask of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:4–6; 1ST SAMUEL 2:25; JEREMIAH 11:14"> He that knoweth that his brother sinneth a sin not to death, ask he, and life shall be given to him that sinneth not to death. There is a sin to death; not for it I say, that any man pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:4; 1ST JOHN 5:16; JAMES 4:7–10"> Each wickedness is sin, and there is &#91;a&#93; sin to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST JOHN 3:9; 1ST JOHN 5:4; 1ST JOHN 5:1"> We know, that each man that is born of God, sinneth not; but the generation of God keepeth him, and the wicked toucheth him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4; 1ST JOHN 4:4–6; EPHESIANS 2:2"> We know, that we be of God, and all the world is set in evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 17:3; LUKE 24:45; JOHN 14:9"> And we know, that the Son of God came in flesh, and gave to us wit, that we know him very God, and be in the very Son &#91;Jesus&#93; of him. This is very God, and everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:14; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:7; EXODUS 20:3–4"> My little sons, keep ye you from maumets &#91;or from simulacra&#93;. My little sons, keep away from idols. &#91;Amen.&#93;</span>
