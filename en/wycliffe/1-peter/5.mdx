<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:3–4; LUKE 24:48; ROMANS 8:17–18"> Therefore I, an even-elder man, and a witness of Christ’s passions, which also am a communer of that glory, that shall be showed in time to come; beseech the elder men, that be among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TITUS 1:7; 1ST TIMOTHY 3:8; TITUS 3:1"> feed ye the flock of God, that is among you, and purvey ye, not as constrained, but willfully, by God; not for love of foul winning, but willfully,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:17; EZEKIEL 34:4"> neither as having lordship in the clergy, but that ye be made ensample to the flock, of will &#91;or by intent&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:25; 1ST JOHN 3:2"> And when the prince of shepherds shall appear, ye shall receive the crown of glory, that may never fade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 4:6; PROVERBS 3:34; EPHESIANS 5:21"> Also, ye young men, be ye subject to elder men, and all show ye together meekness; for the Lord withstandeth proud men, but he giveth grace to meek men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JAMES 4:10; LUKE 14:11; PROVERBS 29:23"> Therefore be ye meeked under the mighty hand of God, that he raise you in the time &#91;or in the day&#93; of visitation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 55:22; PHILIPPIANS 4:6; PSALMS 37:5"> and cast ye all your busyness into him, for to him is care of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:11; JAMES 4:7; EPHESIANS 4:27"> Be ye sober, and wake ye, for your adversary, the devil, as a roaring lion goeth about, seeking whom he shall devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 4:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 14:22; EPHESIANS 6:11–13"> Whom against-stand ye, strong in the faith, witting that the same passion is made to that brotherhood of you, that is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:17; 2ND THESSALONIANS 3:3; 1ST PETER 1:6–7"> And God of all grace, that called you into his everlasting glory, you suffering a little &#91;in Christ Jesus&#93;, he shall perform, and shall confirm, and shall make firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:11; ROMANS 11:36; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:6"> To him be glory and lordship, into worlds of worlds. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:1"> By Silvanus, &#91;a&#93; faithful brother to you as I deem, I wrote shortly; beseeching, and witnessing that this is the very grace of God, in which ye stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:25; PSALMS 87:4"> The church that is gathered in Babylon, and Marcus, my son, greeteth you well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; EPHESIANS 6:23; ROMANS 8:1"> Greet ye well together in holy kiss. Grace be to you all that be in Christ. Amen.</span>
