<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:24; 1ST PETER 3:18; COLOSSIANS 3:3–5"> Therefore for Christ suffered in flesh, be ye also armed by the same thinking; for he that suffered in flesh ceased from sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:22–24; 1ST PETER 1:14; TITUS 3:3–8"> that that is left now of time in flesh live not now to the desires of men, but to the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 6:11; EPHESIANS 5:18; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:5"> For the time that is passed is enough to the will of heathen men to be ended, which walked in lecheries, and lusts, in much drinking of wine, in unmeasurable &#91;or oft&#93; eatings, and drinkings, and unleaveful worshipping of maumets &#91;or of idols&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:16; 1ST PETER 2:12; JUDE 1:10"> In which now they be astonied, in which thing they wonder, for ye run not together into the same confusion of lechery, and blaspheme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 5:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:42; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:1"> And they shall give reason to him, that is ready to deem the quick and the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ROMANS 8:2; 1ST PETER 3:19; ROMANS 8:9–11"> For why for this thing it is preached &#91;or it is evangelized&#93; also to dead men, that they be deemed by men in flesh, and that they live by God in Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JAMES 5:8–9; 1ST PETER 1:13; 1ST PETER 5:8"> For the end of all things shall nigh. Therefore be ye prudent, and wake ye in prayers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:12; COLOSSIANS 3:14; PROVERBS 17:9"> before all things have ye charity each to other in yourselves always lasting; for charity covereth the multitude of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:2; ROMANS 12:13; PHILIPPIANS 2:14"> Hold ye hospitality together with-out grutching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 12:6–8; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:4–11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:1–2"> each man as he hath received grace, ministering it into each other &#91;or ministering each to other&#93;, as good dispensers of the manyfold grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:31; EPHESIANS 6:10; 1ST PETER 4:10"> If any man speaketh, speak he as the words of God; if any man ministereth, as of the virtue which God ministereth; that God be honoured in all things by Jesus Christ our Lord, to whom is glory and lordship into worlds of worlds. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 3:12; 1ST PETER 1:6–7; 1ST PETER 5:9"> Most dear brethren, do not ye go in pilgrimage in fervour, that is made to you to temptation, as if any new thing befall to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 8:17; JAMES 1:2–3; ROMANS 5:3"> but commune ye with the passions of Christ, and have ye joy, that also ye be glad, and have joy in the revelation of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:14; LUKE 6:22; MATTHEW 5:11"> If ye be despised for the name of Christ, ye shall be blessed; for that that is of the honour, and of the glory, and of the virtue of God, and the Spirit that is his, shall rest on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 3:11; 1ST THESSALONIANS 4:11; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:13"> But no man of you suffer as a manslayer, either a thief, either curser, either a desirer of other men’s goods &#91;or things&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:17–18; PHILIPPIANS 1:29; 1ST PETER 4:19"> but if he suffer as a christian man, shame he not, but glorify he God in this name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:29; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:8; ROMANS 2:9"> For time is, that doom begin at God’s house; and if it begin first at us, what end shall be of them, that believe not to the gospel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:31; EZEKIEL 18:24; LUKE 23:31"> And if a just man scarcely shall be saved, where shall the unfaithful man and the sinner appear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 37:5; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:12; 1ST PETER 3:17"> Therefore and they that suffer by the will of God, betake their souls in good deeds to the faithful Maker of nought.</span>
