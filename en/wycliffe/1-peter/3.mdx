<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:18; EPHESIANS 5:33; EPHESIANS 5:22–24"> Also women be they subject to their husbands; that if any man, that is, husbands, believe not to the word, by the conversation of women they be won without word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:12; 1ST PETER 1:15; PHILIPPIANS 1:27"> And behold ye in dread your holy conversation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST TIMOTHY 2:9–10; ROMANS 12:2; ISAIAH 3:18–24"> Of whom be there not without-forth curious adorning of hair, either doing about of gold, either adorning of clothing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:12; 1ST SAMUEL 16:7; ROMANS 2:29"> but that that is the hid man of heart, in uncorruption, and of mild spirit, &#91;or in uncorruptibility of quiet and mild spirit&#93;, which is rich in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TITUS 2:3–4; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:5; LUKE 2:37"> For so sometime holy women hoping in God adorned themselves, and were subject to their own husbands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 18:12; ROMANS 9:7–9; ISAIAH 57:11"> As Sarah obeyed to Abraham, and called him lord; of whom ye be daughters well-doing, and not dreading any perturbation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:19; GENESIS 2:23–24; MATTHEW 19:3–9"> Also men dwell together, and by knowing &#91;or science&#93;, give ye honour to the woman’s frailty, &#91;or to her vessel&#93;, as to the more feeble, and as to even-heirs of grace and of life, that your prayers be not hindered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 12:10; EPHESIANS 4:31–2; 1ST PETER 1:22"> And in faith all of one will, in prayer be ye each suffering with other, lovers of brotherhood, merciful, mild, meek; &#91;Forsooth in faith all of one understanding, or will, in prayer be ye compassionate, or each suffering with other, lovers of fraternity, merciful, mild, meek;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:22; ROMANS 12:17; ROMANS 12:14"> not yielding evil for evil, neither cursing for cursing, but on the contrary blessing; for in this thing ye be called, that ye wield blessing by heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 34:12–16; JAMES 1:26; 1ST PETER 2:1"> For he that will love life, and see good days, constrain his tongue from evil, and his lips, that they speak not guile &#91;or that they speak no guile&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:9; PSALMS 34:14; HEBREWS 12:14"> And bow he from evil, and do good; seek he peace, and perfectly follow it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 34:15–16; JOHN 9:31; PROVERBS 15:29"> For the eyes of the Lord be on just men, and his ears on the prayers of them; but the cheer &#91;or the face&#93; of the Lord is on men that do evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:7; ROMANS 8:28; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15"> And who is it that shall annoy you, if ye be pursuers and lovers of goodness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:28; 1ST PETER 2:19–20; MATTHEW 5:10–12"> But also if ye suffer anything for rightwiseness, ye be blessed; but dread ye not the dread of them, that ye be not disturbed &#91;or distroubled&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:6; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:25–26; LUKE 21:14–15"> But hallow ye the Lord Christ in your hearts, and evermore be ye ready to &#91;do&#93; satisfaction to each man asking you reason of that faith and hope that is in you, but with mildness, and dread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST PETER 2:12; HEBREWS 13:18; 1ST PETER 2:15"> having good conscience; that in that thing that they backbite of you, they be confounded, which challenge falsely your good conversation in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:14; 1ST PETER 4:19; MATTHEW 26:39"> For it is better that ye do well, and suffer, if the will of God will, than doing evil. &#91;Soothly it is better, if the will of God will, ye well-doing, to suffer, than evil-doing.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:28; HEBREWS 9:26; 2ND CORINTHIANS 5:21"> For also Christ once died for our sins, he just for unjust, that he should offer to God us &#91;or should offer us to God&#93;, made dead in flesh, but made quick in Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:6; ISAIAH 61:1; ISAIAH 42:7"> For which thing he came in Spirit, and also to them that were enclosed together in prison;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:7; 2ND PETER 2:5; GENESIS 6:3"> which were sometime unbelieve-ful, when they abided the patience of God in the days of Noah, when the ship &#91;or the ark&#93; was made, in which a few, that is to say, eight souls were made safe by water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARK 16:16; EZEKIEL 36:25–26; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38"> And so baptism of like form maketh us safe; not the putting away of the filths of flesh, but the asking of a good conscience in God, by the again-rising of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:24; HEBREWS 12:2; HEBREWS 10:12"> that is in the right half of God, and swalloweth death, that we should be made heirs of everlasting life. He went into heaven, and angels, and powers, and virtues be made subject to him.</span>
