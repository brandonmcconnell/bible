<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:17; PROVERBS 6:24; NEHEMIAH 13:23–27"> Forsooth king Solomon loved burningly many alien women, and the daughter of Pharaoh, and women of Moab, and Ammonites, and Idu-means, and Sidonians, and Hittites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 34:16; DEUTERONOMY 7:3–4; JOSHUA 23:12–13"> of the folks of which the Lord said to the sons of Israel, Ye shall not enter to those folks, neither any of them shall enter to you; for most certainly they shall turn away your hearts, that ye follow the gods of them. And so king Solomon was coupled to these women, by most burning love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:28; 2ND SAMUEL 5:13–16; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:21"> And wives as queens were seven hundred to him, and three hundred secondary wives; and the women turned away his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:4; 1ST KINGS 8:61; 1ST KINGS 11:6"> And when he was then eld &#91;or old&#93;, his heart was beshrewd by women, that he pursued &#91;or followed&#93; alien gods; and his heart was not perfect with his Lord God, as the heart of David, his father, was perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 2:13; 1ST KINGS 11:7; 2ND KI 23:13"> But Solomon worshipped Astarte, the goddess of Sidonians, and Chemosh, the god of Moabites, and Moloch, the idol of Ammonites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:24; JOSHUA 14:8; JOSHUA 14:14"> and Solomon did that, that pleased not before the Lord, and he full-filled not that he pursued &#91;or followed&#93; the Lord, as David, his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:29; JUDGES 11:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:43"> Then Solomon builded a temple to Chemosh, the idol of Moab, in the hill which is against Jerusalem, and to Moloch, the idol of the sons of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:11–12; 1ST KINGS 11:1; EZEKIEL 16:22–29"> And by this manner he did to all his alien wives, the which burnt incenses, and offered to their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:2; 1ST KINGS 3:5; DEUTERONOMY 7:4"> Therefore the Lord was wroth to Solomon, for his soul was turned away from the Lord God of Israel; that appeared to him the second time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST KINGS 6:12–13; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:17–22; 1ST KINGS 9:4–7"> and &#91;had&#93; commanded of this word, that he should not pursue &#91;or follow&#93; alien gods; and he kept not those things, which the Lord commanded to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:15–16; 1ST KINGS 11:31; 2ND KI 17:21"> Therefore the Lord said to Solomon, For thou haddest this thing with thee, and keptest not my covenant, and my behests, which I commanded to thee, I shall break, and I shall part thy realm, and I shall give it to thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND KI 20:19; 1ST SAMUEL 9:4–5; 2ND KI 22:19–20"> Nevertheless I shall not do it in thy days, for David, thy father; I shall cut it from the hand of thy son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:20; DEUTERONOMY 12:11; 1ST KINGS 11:32"> neither I shall do away all the realm, but I shall give one lineage to thy son, for David, my servant, and for Jerusalem, which I chose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 5:26; PSALMS 89:30–34; ISAIAH 13:17"> Forsooth the Lord raised to Solomon an adversary, Hadad the Idumean, of the king’s seed, that was in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:14; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:12–13; DEUTERONOMY 20:13"> For when David was in Idumea, and Joab, the prince of his chivalry, had gone up to bury them that were slain, and he had slain each male kind in Idumea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> for Joab, and all Israel, dwelled there by six months, till they had killed each male kind in Idumea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:13–14; EXODUS 2:1–10; 2ND KI 11:2"> Hadad himself fled, and some men of Idumea, of the servants of his father, with him, that he should enter into Egypt; soothly Hadad was a little child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:12; DEUTERONOMY 33:2; DEUTERONOMY 1:1"> And when they had risen from Midian, they came into Paran; and they took with them men of Paran, and entered into Egypt, to Pharaoh, king of Egypt; and Pharaoh gave an house to him, and ordained to him meats, and assigned to him land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 39:4; JEREMIAH 43:7–9; GENESIS 39:21"> And Hadad found grace before Pharaoh greatly, in so much that Pharaoh gave to him a wife, the sister of his wife, that is, the sister of the queen, the sister of Tahpenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 21:7; 1ST SAMUEL 1:24"> And the sister of Tahpenes engen-dered to him a son, Genubath; and Tahpenes nursed him in the house of Pharaoh; and Genubath dwelled before Pharaoh, with the sons of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:10; EXODUS 4:19; MATTHEW 2:20"> And when Hadad had heard in Egypt, that David slept with his fathers, and that Joab, the prince of chivalry, was dead, he said to Pharaoh, Suffer thou me, that I go into my land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 18:22–23; LUKE 22:35; MARK 14:31"> And Pharaoh said to him, And of what thing hast thou need with me, that thou seekest to go to thy land? And he answered, Of nothing; but I beseech thee, that thou deliver me or let me go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:14; 2ND SAMUEL 8:3; 2ND SAMUEL 10:15–18"> And God raised up another adversary to Solomon, Rezon, the son of Eliadah, that fled Hadadezer, king of Zobah, his lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 10:8; 2ND SAMUEL 10:18; 1ST KINGS 20:34"> and &#91;he&#93; gathered men against him, and was made the prince of thieves, when David killed them of Zobah; and they went to Damascus, and dwelled there; and they made him king in Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 15:2; PSALMS 106:40; 1ST KINGS 5:4"> And he was &#91;an&#93; adversary of Israel in all the days of Solomon; and this is besides the evil of Hadad, and his hatred against Israel; and he reigned in Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 13:6; 1ST KINGS 12:2; 1ST KINGS 11:28"> Also Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, of Ephraim of Zereda, the servant of Solomon, of which Jeroboam, a woman widow, Zeruah by name, was his mother, he also raised his hand against the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:24; 1ST KINGS 9:15; PROVERBS 30:32"> And this was &#91;the&#93; cause of his rebelty against the king; for Solomon builded the Millo, and made even the swallow of the city of David, his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:29; 2ND SAMUEL 19:20; JUDGES 1:22–23"> Forsooth Jeroboam was a mighty man and strong; and Solomon saw the young waxing man to be of good kindred, and witting in things to be done, and Solomon made him prefect, either sovereign, upon the tributes of all the house of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:15; 1ST KINGS 14:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:29"> Therefore it was done in that time, that Jeroboam went out of Jerusalem; and Ahijah of Shiloh, a prophet, covered with a new mantle, found him in the way; and they twain &#91;or two&#93; were alone in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 15:27–28; 1ST SAMUEL 24:4–5"> And Ahijah took his new mantle, with which he was covered, and he cut it into twelve parts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:11–12"> and said to Jeroboam, Take to thee ten cuttings of the mantle; for the Lord God of Israel saith these things, Lo! I shall cut the realm from the hand of Solomon, and I shall give to thee ten lineages;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:13; 1ST KINGS 12:20–21; 1ST KINGS 14:21"> but one lineage shall dwell to him, for David, my servant, and for Jerusalem, the city which I chose of all the lineages of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:9; 1ST KINGS 11:5–7; 1ST KINGS 9:5–7"> this cutting of the realm shall be; for Solomon forsook me, and worship-ped Astarte, the goddess of Sidonians, and Chemosh, the god of Moab, and Moloch, the god of the sons of Ammon; and &#91;he&#93; went not in my ways, that he did rightwiseness before me, and my behests, and my dooms, as David, his father, did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:12–13; 1ST KINGS 11:31; JOB 11:6"> And I shall not take away all the realm from his hand, but I shall put him duke in all the days of his life, for David, my servant, whom I chose, which kept my behests, and my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:20; 1ST KINGS 11:12; EXODUS 20:5–6"> Soothly I shall take away the realm from the hand of his son, and I shall give &#91;the&#93; ten lineages to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2ND KI 8:19; 1ST KINGS 11:13; 1ST KINGS 15:4"> forsooth I shall give one lineage to his son, that a lantern dwell to David, my servant, in all days before me in Jerusalem, the city which I chose, that my name should be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:26; 1ST KINGS 11:26; 2ND SAMUEL 3:21"> Forsooth I shall take thee, and thou shalt reign on all things which thy soul desireth, and thou shalt be king upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:11; JOSHUA 1:5; DEUTERONOMY 31:8"> Therefore if thou shalt hear all things which I shall command to thee, and if thou shalt go in my ways, and if thou shalt do that, that is rightful &#91;or right&#93; before me, and if thou shalt keep my commandments, and my behests, as David, my servant, did, I shall be with thee, and I shall build a faithful house to thee, as I builded an house to David, and I shall give Israel to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 1:32–33; LUKE 2:11; ISAIAH 11:1–10"> and I shall torment the seed of David on this thing, nevertheless not in all days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 12:2–9; ISAIAH 14:24–27; LAMENTATIONS 3:37"> Therefore Solomon would slay Jeroboam, which rose, and fled into Egypt, to Shishak, king of Egypt; and he was in Egypt unto the death of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:29–31"> Forsooth the residue of the words of Solomon, and all things which he did, and his wisdom, lo! all those things be written in the book of &#91;the&#93; words of &#91;the&#93; days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:11"> And the days in which Solomon reigned in Jerusalem upon all Israel, be forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2ND KI 21:18; 1ST KINGS 2:10; MATTHEW 1:7"> And Solomon slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David, his father; and Rehoboam, his son, reigned for him.</span>
