<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 9:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:1–19; JUDGES 9:1"> Forsooth Rehoboam came into Shechem; for all Israel was gathered thither to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:40; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:2–3; 1ST KINGS 11:26–31"> And soothly Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, when he was yet in Egypt, and fled from the face of king Solomon, turned again from Egypt, for the death of Solomon was heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and they sent, and called him. Therefore Jeroboam came, and all the multitude of Israel, and they spake to Rehoboam, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 8:11–18; 1ST KINGS 4:7; MATTHEW 11:29–30"> Thy father putted the most hard yoke upon us, therefore abate thou a little now of the hardest commandment of thy father, and of the full grievous yoke that he hath put upon us, and we shall serve to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:12"> And Rehoboam said to them, Go ye till to the third day, and turn ye again to me. And when the people had gone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 12:12; JOB 32:7; 2ND SAMUEL 16:20"> king Rehoboam took counsel with the elder men, that stood before Solomon, his father, while he lived yet; and Rehoboam said, What counsel give ye to me, that I answer to the people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:6–7; 1ST KINGS 12:13"> Which said to him, If thou obeyest today to this people, and servest this people, and givest stead to their asking, and speakest to them light, or easy, words, they shall be servants to thee in all days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:20; ECCLESIASTES 10:2–3; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:8"> And Rehoboam forsook the counsel of &#91;the&#93; eld &#91;or old&#93; men, which they gave to him, and took young men, that were nourished with him, and stood nigh &#91;to&#93; him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:6–8; 2ND SAMUEL 17:5–6; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:9"> and he said to them, What counsel give ye to me, that I answer to this people, that said to me, Make thou easier the yoke that thy father hath put upon us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 10:10–11; PROVERBS 10:14; ISAIAH 47:6"> And the young men, that were nourished with him, said to him, Thus speak thou to this people, that spake to thee, and said, Thy father made grievous our yoke, relieve thou us; thus thou shalt speak to them, My least finger is greater than the back of my father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:11; EZEKIEL 2:6; JEREMIAH 28:13–14"> and now though my father putted &#91;or put&#93; on you a grievous yoke, forsooth I shall add on your yoke; my father beat you with scourges, but I shall beat you with scorpions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, a kind of hardest scourge, that hath knots of lead, either of iron, in the ends of cords.">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:12–14"> Therefore Jeroboam, and all the people, came to Rehoboam, in the third day, as the king spake, saying, Turn ye again to me in the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 3:17; PROVERBS 13:20; ECCLESIASTES 10:12"> And the king answered hard things to the people, while the counsel of &#91;the&#93; elder men was forsaken, that they had given to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 5:16–18; 1ST KINGS 12:10–11; EXODUS 5:5–9"> and he spake to them by the counsel of &#91;the&#93; young men, and said, My father made grievous your yoke, forsooth I shall add to your yoke; my father beat you with scourges, but I shall beat you with scorpions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:24; DEUTERONOMY 2:30; JUDGES 14:4"> And the king assented not to the people, for the Lord had turned him away, that the Lord should raise up his word, that he had spoken in the hand of the prophet Ahijah of Shiloh to Jeroboam, the son of Nebat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 20:1; JEREMIAH 23:5–6; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:16"> Then the people saw, that the king would not hear them, and the people answered to the king, and said, What part is to us in David, either what heritage in the son of Jesse? Israel, turn thou again into thy tabernacles; now, David, see thou to thine house. And Israel went into his tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:36; 1ST KINGS 11:13; 2ND CHRONICLES 11:13–17"> Forsooth Rehoboam reigned on the sons of Israel, which dwelled in the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST KINGS 4:6; 2ND SAMUEL 20:24; 1ST KINGS 5:14"> Therefore king Rehoboam sent Adoram, that was on the tributes; and all the people of Israel stoned him, and he was dead. Forsooth king Rehoboam went up hastily upon his chariot, and fled into Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND KI 17:21; 2ND CHRONICLES 10:19; JOSHUA 4:9"> and Israel departed from the house of David, till into this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST KINGS 11:13; 1ST KINGS 11:32; HOSEA 8:4"> Forsooth it was done, when all Israel had heard that Jeroboam &#91;was&#93; turned again, they sent, and called him, when the company was gathered together, and they made him king upon all Israel; and no man followed the house of David, except the lineage alone of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 11:1–3; 1ST CHRONICLES 21:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 14:8"> And Rehoboam came to Jerusalem, and gathered together all the house of Judah, and the lineage of Benjamin, an hundred and fourscore thousand of chosen men and warriors, that they should fight against the house of Israel, and should bring again the realm to Rehoboam, the son of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 11:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 12:5–7; 1ST KINGS 13:1"> Forsooth the word of God was made to Shemaiah, the man of God, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:17"> Speak thou to Rehoboam, the son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all the house of Judah and of Benjamin, and to the residue of the people, and say thou,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:15; 2ND CHRONICLES 25:7–8; HOSEA 8:4"> The Lord saith these things, Ye shall not go up, neither ye shall fight against your brethren, the sons of Israel; turn each man again into his house, for this word is done of me. They heard the word of the Lord, and they turned again from the journey, as the Lord commanded to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JUDGES 8:17; JUDGES 8:8; JUDGES 9:45–49"> And Jeroboam builded Shechem, in the hill of Ephraim, and dwelled there; and he went out from thence, and builded Penuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:18–21; ISAIAH 7:9; MARK 2:6–8"> And Jeroboam said in his heart, Now the realm shall turn again to the house of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:5–7; PROVERBS 29:25; DEUTERONOMY 12:14"> if this people ascendeth &#91;or go up&#93; to Jerusalem, that it make sacrifice in the house of the Lord in Jerusalem; and then the heart of this people shall turn again to their lord, Rehoboam, king of Judah; and they shall slay me, and shall turn again to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2ND KI 10:29; EXODUS 32:8; 2ND KI 17:16"> And by counsel thought out, Jeroboam made twain &#91;or two&#93; golden calves, and he said to the people, Do not ye ascend &#91;or go up&#93; more into Jerusalem; Israel, lo! thy gods, that led thee out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 28:19; JUDGES 18:27–31; HOSEA 4:15"> And he set one calf in Bethel, and the tother in Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2ND KI 17:21; 1ST KINGS 13:34; 2ND KI 10:31"> And this word was made to Israel into sin; for the people went into Dan, to worship the calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 13:9; 2ND KI 17:32; 1ST KINGS 13:32–33"> And Jeroboam made temples in high places, and he made priests of the last men of the people, the which were not of the sons of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:5; 1ST KINGS 8:2; AMOS 7:10–13"> And the king ordained a solemn day in the eighth month, in the fifteen day of the month, by &#91;the&#93; likeness of the solemnity that was hallowed in Judah. And the king went up, and made in like manner an altar in Bethel, that he should offer to the calves, which he had made; and he ordained in Bethel priests of the high places, which he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST KINGS 13:1; NUMBERS 15:39; MATTHEW 15:6"> And he went up upon the altar, which he had builded in Bethel, in the fifteenth day of the eighth month, which he had feigned of his heart; and he made a solemnity to the sons of Israel, and he went upon the altar, that he should burn incense.</span>
