<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 47:29; DEUTERONOMY 31:14; 1ST TIMOTHY 1:18"> Forsooth the days of David nighed, that he should die; and he commanded to Solomon, his son, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:6–7; JOSHUA 23:14; EPHESIANS 6:10"> Lo! I enter into the way of all earth; be thou comforted, and be thou a strong man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:9; DEUTERONOMY 17:18–20; PROVERBS 3:1–4"> And keep thou the keepings and the behests of thy Lord God, that thou go in his ways, and keep his ceremonies, and his behests, and his dooms, and witnessings, as it is written in the law of Moses; that thou under-stand all things which thou doest, and whither ever thou shalt turn thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2ND KI 20:3; 2ND SAMUEL 7:25; 1ST KINGS 8:25"> That the Lord confirm his words, which the Lord spake of me, and said, If thy sons keep my ways, and go before me in truth, in all their heart, and in all their soul, a man shall not be taken away of thee from the throne of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:27; 2ND SAMUEL 20:10; 2ND SAMUEL 18:5"> Also thou knowest what things Joab, the son of Zeruiah, did to me; what things he did to &#91;the&#93; two princes of the host of Israel, to Abner, the son of Ner, and to Amasa, the son of Jether, which he killed, and shedded the blood of battle in peace; and putted &#91;or put&#93; the blood of battle in his girdle, that was about his loins, and in his shoes, that was in his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:9; ISAIAH 57:21; PSALMS 37:37"> Therefore thou shalt do by thy wisdom, and thou shalt not lead forth his hoariness peaceably to hells, either sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 17:27–29; 2ND SAMUEL 9:7; 2ND SAMUEL 19:31–40"> But also thou shalt yield grace to the sons of Barzillai of Gilead, and they shall be eating in thy board; for they met me, when I fled from the face of Absalom, thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:5–8; 1ST KINGS 2:36–46; 2ND SAMUEL 19:16–23"> Also thou hast with thee Shimei, the son of Gera, the son of Benjamin, of Bahurim, the which Shimei cursed me by the worst cursing, when I went to the defensible places; but for-thy he came down to me into my meeting, when I passed over the Jordan, and I swore to him by the Lord, and said, I shall not slay thee with sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:6; GENESIS 42:38; GENESIS 44:31"> do not thou suffer him to be unpunished; forsooth thou art a wise man, and thou shalt know what thou shalt do to him, and thou shalt lead forth his hoar hairs with blood to hells &#91;or hell&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:36; 1ST KINGS 1:21"> And David slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 5:4–5; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:26–27"> And the days, in which David reigned upon Israel, be forty years; in Hebron he reigned seven years, and in Jerusalem three and thirty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 1:1; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:23–25; PSALMS 132:12"> Forsooth Solomon sat upon the throne of David, his father, and his realm was made steadfast greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 16:4–5; 1ST KINGS 1:5–10; LUKE 10:5–6"> And Adonijah, the son of Haggith, entered to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon; and she said to him, Whether thine entering is peaceable? And he answered, It is peaceable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 14:12; LUKE 7:40"> And he added, A word of me is to thee. And she said, Speak thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 22:9–10; 1ST CHRONICLES 28:5–7; 1ST KINGS 1:25"> And he said, Thou knowest that the realm was mine, and all Israel purposed to make me into king to them; but the realm is translated or transferred, and is made my brother’s; for of the Lord it is ordained to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:7; PSALMS 132:10"> Now therefore I pray of thee one asking; shame thou not my face. And she said to him, Speak thou.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:8; 1ST KINGS 1:2–4; 2ND SAMUEL 3:7"> And he said, I pray, that thou say to Solomon the king; for he may not deny anything to thee; that he give me Abishag of Shunem to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:15"> And Bathsheba said, Well, I shall speak for thee to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 45:9; 1ST KINGS 15:13; LEVITICUS 19:32"> Therefore Bathsheba came to king Solomon, to speak to him for Adonijah; and the king rose against the coming of her, and worshipped her, and sat on his throne; and a throne was set to the mother of the king, and she sat at his right side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:7–11; MATTHEW 11:24; JOHN 2:3–4"> And she said to him, I pray of thee one little asking; shame thou not my face. And the king said to her, My mother, ask thou; for it is not leaveful that I turn away thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:3; 2ND SAMUEL 16:21–22"> And she said, Abishag of Shunem be given wife to Adonijah, thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 3:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 3:5; MATTHEW 20:22"> And king Solomon answered, and said to his mother, Why askest thou Abishag of Shunem to Adonijah? Ask thou to him also the realm; certainly he is mine elder brother, and he hath Abiathar, priest, and Joab, the son of Zeruiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="RUTH 1:17; PSALMS 140:9; PSALMS 64:8"> Therefore king Solomon swore by the Lord, and said, God do to me these things, and add these things too, for Adonijah hath spoken this word against his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 22:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:17"> And now the Lord liveth, that hath confirmed me, and hath set me on the throne of &#91;David&#93;, my father, and that hath made to me an house, as he spake, for Adonijah shall be slain today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:18; 1ST KINGS 2:46; JUDGES 8:20–21"> And king Solomon sent by the hand of Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada; and Benaiah slew Adonijah, and he was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:18; 1ST SAMUEL 22:20–23; JEREMIAH 1:1"> Also the king said to Abiathar, the priest, Go thou into Anathoth, to thy field; and soothly thou art a man of death, that is, worthy of death, for conspiring against me, and David, my father; but today I shall not slay thee, for thou barest the ark of the Lord God before David, my father, and thou sufferedest travail in all things, in which my father travailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 2:27–36; 1ST SAMUEL 3:12–14; JEREMIAH 7:12–14"> Therefore Solomon putted out Abiathar, that he should not be priest of the Lord, that the word of the Lord were &#91;ful&#93; filled, which he spake on the house of Eli in Shiloh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:50; 1ST KINGS 1:7; 2ND SAMUEL 18:2"> And a messenger came to Solo-mon, and said that Joab had bowed after Adonijah, and that he had not bowed after Solomon. Therefore Joab fled into the tabernacle of the Lord, and took the horn of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:25; EXODUS 21:14; 1ST KINGS 2:46"> And it was told to king Solomon, that Joab had fled into the tabernacle of the Lord, and was beside the altar; and Solomon sent Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, and said, Go thou, and slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Benaiah came to the taber-nacle of the Lord, and said to Joab, The king saith these things, Go thou out. And he said, I shall not go out, but I shall die here. Benaiah told the word to the king, and said, Joab spake these things, and answered these things to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:8–9; NUMBERS 35:33; EXODUS 21:14"> And the king said to Benaiah, Do thou as he hath spoken, and slay thou him, and bury him; and thou shalt remove the innocent blood, that was shed out of Joab, from me, and from the house of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JUDGES 9:24; JUDGES 9:57; PSALMS 7:16"> And the Lord yield on his head his blood, for he killed two just men, and better than himself, and he killed them by sword, while David, my father, knew not, Abner the son of Ner, the prince of the chivalry of Israel, and Amasa, the son of Jether, the prince of the host of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:25; 1ST KINGS 2:32; LUKE 2:14"> And the blood of them shall turn again into the head of Joab, and into the head of his seed without end; but peace be of the Lord till into without end to David, and to his seed, and to the house, and &#91;the&#93; throne of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:1; JOSHUA 15:61; 1ST KINGS 2:25"> Therefore Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, went up, and assailed Joab, and killed him; and Joab was buried in his house in desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:27; 1ST CHRONICLES 24:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:22"> And the king ordained Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, upon the host for Joab; and the king put Zadok the priest for Abiathar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:5–9; 1ST KINGS 2:8–9; PROVERBS 20:26"> Also the king sent, and called Shimei, and said to him, Build to thee an house in Jerusalem, and dwell thou there, and thou shalt not go out from thence hither and thither;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 1:16; 2ND SAMUEL 15:23; JOSHUA 2:19"> for in whatever day thou goest out, and passest &#91;over&#93; the strand &#91;or stream&#93; of Kidron, know thou thee worthy to be slain; thy blood shall be on thine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1ST KINGS 20:4; 2ND KI 20:19"> And Shimei said to the king, The word of the king is good; as my lord the king spake, so thy servant shall do. And so Shimei dwelled in Jerusalem many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 21:10; 1ST SAMUEL 27:2–3"> But it was done after three years, that the servants of Shimei fled to Achish, the son of Maachah, king of Gath; and it was told to Shimei, that his servants had gone into Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 12:15; PROVERBS 15:27; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:10"> And Shimei rose up, and saddled his ass, and went to Achish, into Gath, to seek his servants; and he brought them again from Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And it was told to king Solomon, that Shimei had gone to Gath from Jerusalem, and had come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 15:4; 1ST KINGS 2:36–38; LUKE 15:22"> And Solomon sent, and called him, and said to him, Whether I witnessed not to thee by the Lord, and before-said to thee, In whatever day thou shalt go out hither and thither, know thou that thou shalt die; and thou answeredest to me, The word is good, which I heard?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="ROMANS 13:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:12; ECCLESIASTES 8:2"> Why therefore keptest thou not the oath of the Lord, and the command-ment which I commanded to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 16:5–13; PSALMS 7:16; EZEKIEL 17:19"> And the king said to Shimei, Thou knowest all the evil, of which thine heart is guilty to thee, which evil thou didest to &#91;David&#93; my father; the Lord hath yielded thy malice into thine head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:5; 1ST KINGS 2:24; ISAIAH 9:6–7"> And king Solomon shall be blessed; and the throne of David shall be stable before the Lord till into without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 1:1; 1ST KINGS 2:12; 1ST KINGS 2:45"> Therefore the king commanded to Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada; and he assailed Shimei, and smote him, and he was dead. Therefore the realm was confirmed into the hands of Solomon;</span>
