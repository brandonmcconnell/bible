<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 14:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:3; 1ST KINGS 5:10"> Also Hiram, king of Tyre, sent his servants to Solomon; for he heard that they had anointed him king for his father; for Hiram was friend of David in all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 2:3"> And also Solomon sent to Hiram, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:8; PSALMS 110:1"> Thou knowest the will of David, my father, and for he might not build an house to the name of his God, for &#91;the&#93; battles nighing by compass, till the Lord gave them under the step of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 22:9; 1ST KINGS 4:24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:31"> But now my Lord God hath given rest to me by compass, and none adversary is, neither evil assailing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:12; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:10; 2ND SAMUEL 7:12–13"> wherefore I think to build a temple to the name of my Lord God, as God spake to David, my father, and said, Thy son, whom I shall give to thee for thee upon thy throne, he shall build an house to my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 2:8; 1ST KINGS 6:9–10; ROMANS 12:17"> Therefore command thou, that thy servants hew down to me cedars of the Lebanon; and my servants be with thy servants; and I shall give to thee the meed of thy servants, whatever thou shalt ask; for thou knowest, that in my people is not a man that can hew trees, as Sidonians or the men of Sidon can.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:24; ISAIAH 9:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:11–12"> Therefore when Hiram had heard the words of Solomon, he was full glad, and said, Blessed be the Lord God today, that hath given to David the son most wise upon this people full much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST KINGS 6:34; 2ND CHRONICLES 3:5; 2ND SAMUEL 6:5"> And Hiram sent to Solomon, and said, I have heard whatever things thou sentest to me; I shall do all thy will, in trees of cedars, and in trees of box.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZRA 3:7; EZEKIEL 27:17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:20"> My servants shall put down those trees from the Lebanon to the sea, and I shall array those trees in ships in the sea, unto the place that thou shalt signify to me; and I shall dress those &#91;or them&#93; there, that thou take those &#91;or them&#93;; and thou shalt give necessaries to me, that meat be given to mine house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And so Hiram gave to Solomon cedar trees, and box trees, by all his will;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 2:10; 1ST KINGS 4:22"> and Solomon gave to Hiram twenty thousand cors of wheat, into meat to his house, and twenty cors of purest oil; Solomon gave these things to Hiram by all years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:12; AMOS 1:9; JAMES 1:5"> Also the Lord gave wisdom to Solomon, as he spake to him; and peace was betwixt Hiram and Solomon, and both they smote together bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:15; 1ST KINGS 4:6"> And king Solomon chose work-men out of all Israel; and the sum was thirty thousand of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST KINGS 4:6–19; 1ST CHRONICLES 27:1–15"> And Solomon sent them into the Lebanon, ten thousand by each month by whiles, so that in two months by whiles they were in their houses; and Adoniram was on such a sum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:20–22; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:17–18; 2ND CHRONICLES 8:7–9"> And so seventy thousand of them, that bare burdens, were to Solomon, and fourscore thousand of masons in the hills,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:23; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:2"> without the sovereigns, that were masters of all the works, by the number of three thousand and three hundred, commanding to the people, and to them that made work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST KINGS 6:7; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:2; ISAIAH 28:16"> And the king commanded, that they should take great stones, and precious or heavy stones, into the foundament of the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:5; EZEKIEL 27:9; PSALMS 83:7"> and that they should make those square; which stones the masons of Solomon and the masons of Hiram hewed. And &#91;the&#93; men of Byblos made ready &#91;the&#93; trees and stones, to the house to be builded.</span>
