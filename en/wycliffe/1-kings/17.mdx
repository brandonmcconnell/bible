<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 4:25–26; JAMES 5:17; LUKE 1:17"> And Elijah the Tishbite, of the dwellers of Gilead, said to Ahab, The Lord God of Israel liveth, in whose sight I stand, dew and rain shall not be in these years, no but by the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:1; JEREMIAH 7:1; HOSEA 1:1–2"> And the word of the Lord was made to him, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:6; JEREMIAH 36:26; PSALMS 83:3"> Go thou away from hence, and go against the east, and be thou hid in the strand &#91;or stream&#93; of Cherith, that is against Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 17:9; PSALMS 147:9; JOB 38:41"> and there thou shalt drink of the strand; and I have commanded to &#91;the&#93; crows, that they feed thee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST KINGS 19:9; MATTHEW 16:24; JOHN 15:14"> Therefore he went, and did by the word of the Lord; and when he had gone, he sat in the strand &#91;or stream&#93; of Cherith, that is against Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 78:23–24; MATTHEW 6:31–33; JEREMIAH 37:21"> And &#91;the&#93; crows bare to him bread and flesh early; and in like manner in the eventide; and he drank of the strand &#91;or stream&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 54:10; ISAIAH 40:30–31"> And after some days the strand was dried; for it had not rained on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 22:14; 1ST KINGS 17:2; HEBREWS 13:6"> Therefore the word of the Lord was made to him, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 4:26; OBADIAH 1:20; 2ND CORINTHIANS 4:7"> Rise thou, and go into Zarephath of Sidonians, and thou shalt dwell there; for I have commanded to a woman widow there, that she feed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 4:7; GENESIS 24:17; GENESIS 21:15"> He rose, and went into Zarephath of Sidonians; and when he had come to the gate of the city, a woman widow gathering sticks appeared to him; and he called her, and said to her, Give thou to me a little of water in a vessel, that I drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:35–40; 1ST KINGS 18:4; HEBREWS 13:2"> And when she went to bring it, he cried behind her back, and said, I beseech, bring thou to me also a morsel of bread in thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:33–34; 1ST KINGS 17:1; GENESIS 21:16"> And she answered, Thy Lord God liveth, for I have no bread, no but as much of meal in a pot, as a fist&#91;ful&#93; may take, and a little of oil in a vessel; lo! I gather two sticks, that I enter, and make it to me, and to my son, that we eat, and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:33; MALACHI 3:10; GENESIS 22:1–2"> And Elijah said to her, Do not thou dread, but go, and make as thou saidest; nevertheless make thou first to me of that little meal a little loaf, baked under ashes, and bring thou it to me; soothly thou shalt make after-ward to thee and to thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:36–38; MATTHEW 14:17–20; 2ND KI 4:42–44"> Forsooth the Lord God of Israel saith these things, The pot of meal shall not fail, and the vessel of oil shall not be abated, till to the day in which the Lord shall give rain on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:17; MARK 12:43; MATTHEW 15:28"> And she went, and did by the word of Elijah; and he ate, and she, and her house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:28–30; JOHN 4:50–51; LUKE 1:37"> And from that day the pot of meal failed not, and the vessel of oil was not abated, by the word of the Lord, which he had spoken in the hand of Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:7; DANIEL 5:23; JOHN 11:14"> Forsooth it was done after these words, the son of a woman house-wife, was sick, and the sickness was full strong, so that breath dwelled not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 5:8; JOHN 2:4; 2ND KI 3:13"> Therefore she said to Elijah, What to me and to thee, thou man of God? Enteredest thou to me, that my wicked-ness should be remembered, and that thou shouldest slay my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND KI 4:32; 2ND KI 4:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:37"> And Elijah said to her, Give thy son to me. And he took that son from her bosom, and bare into the solar, where he dwelled; and he put him on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 5:15–18; MATTHEW 21:22; JOSHUA 7:8–9"> And he cried to the Lord, and said, My Lord God, whether thou hast tormented also the widow, with whom I am sustained in all manner, that thou killedest her son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:40; 2ND KI 4:33–35"> He spread abroad himself, and was meted &#91;or measured&#93; upon the child by three times; and he cried to the Lord, and said, My Lord God, I beseech, the soul of this child turn again into the entrails of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:39; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:11; HEBREWS 11:35"> The Lord heard the voice of Elijah, and the soul of the child turned again within him, and he lived again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2ND KI 4:36–37; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:41; LUKE 7:15"> And Elijah took the child, and put him down of the solar into the lower house, and betook him to his mother; and he said to her, Lo! thy son liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 16:30; JOHN 2:11; JOHN 3:2"> And the woman said to Elijah, Now in this I have known, that thou art a man of God, and the word of the Lord is sooth in thy mouth.</span>
