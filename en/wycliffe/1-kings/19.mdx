</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="RUTH 1:17; 2ND KI 6:31; 1ST KINGS 20:10"> And Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, and said, Gods do these things to me, and add these things too, no but tomorrow in this hour I shall put thy life as the life of one of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 21:31; MATTHEW 26:70–74; MATTHEW 26:56"> Therefore Elijah dreaded, and rose, and went whither ever his will bare him; and he came into Beersheba of Judah, and he left there his child;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JONAH 4:3; NUMBERS 11:15; JONAH 4:8"> and went into desert, the way of one day. And when he came, and sat under one juniper tree, he asked to his soul, that he should die; and he said, Lord, it sufficeth to me, take my soul; for I am not better than my fathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 34:7; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 12:7; HEBREWS 13:5"> And he casted forth himself, and slept in the shadow of the juniper tree. And lo! the angel of the Lord touched him, and said to him, Rise thou, and eat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 17:6; MATTHEW 4:11; MATTHEW 6:32"> He beheld, and, lo! at his head was a loaf baked under ashes, and a vessel of water. Therefore he ate, and drank, and slept again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 103:13–14; DEUTERONOMY 33:25"> And the angel of the Lord turned again the second time, and touched him; and he said to him, Rise thou, and eat; for a great way is to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 34:28; EXODUS 24:18; EXODUS 3:1"> And when he had risen, he ate, and drank; and he went in the strength of that meat forty days and forty nights, unto Horeb, the hill of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1ST KINGS 19:13; EXODUS 33:21–22; JEREMIAH 2:18"> And when he had come thither, he dwelled in a den; and lo! the word of the Lord was made to him, and said to him, Elijah, what doest thou here?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST KINGS 18:4; 1ST KINGS 18:22; EXODUS 34:14"> And he answered, By fervent love, that is, of all the heart, I have loved fervently, for the Lord God of hosts; for the sons of Israel have forsaken the covenant of the Lord; they have destroyed thine altars, and killed with sword thy prophets; and I am left alone, and they seek my life, that they do it away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:4; PSALMS 50:3; JOB 38:1"> And he said to Elijah, Go thou out, and stand in the hill, before the Lord. And lo! the Lord passeth by, and a great wind, and strong, turning upside-down hills, and all-breaking stones before the Lord; not in the wind is the Lord. And after the wind is a stirring; not in the stirring is the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 4:16; ZECHARIAH 4:6; DEUTERONOMY 4:33"> And after the stirring is a fire; not in the fire is the Lord. And after the fire is an hissing of thin wind, or breathing softly; there is the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 19:9; ISAIAH 6:2; GENESIS 16:8"> And when Elijah had heard this, he covered his face with a mantle, and he went out, and stood in the door of the den. And a voice spake to him, and said, Elijah, what doest thou here?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST KINGS 19:9–10; JEREMIAH 22:9; ISAIAH 62:1"> And he answered, With fervent love I have loved fervently, for the Lord God of hosts; for the sons of Israel have forsaken thy covenant; they have destroyed thine altars, and they have killed with sword thy prophets; and I am left alone, and they seek my life, that they do it away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ISAIAH 45:1; JEREMIAH 1:10; 2ND KI 8:7–15"> And the Lord said to him, Go, and turn again into thy way, by the desert, into Damascus; and when thou shalt come thither, thou shalt anoint Hazael king upon Syria;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND KI 9:1–14; 1ST KINGS 19:19–21; 2ND KI 2:9"> and thou shalt anoint king upon Israel, Jehu, the son of Nimshi; and thou shalt anoint a prophet for thee, Elisha, the son of Shaphat, that is of Abelmeholah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 13:22; 2ND KI 8:12; 2ND KI 13:3"> And it shall be, whoever shall flee the sword of Hazael, Jehu shall slay him; and whoever shall flee the sword of Jehu, Elisha shall slay him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 11:4–5; ISAIAH 1:9; ISAIAH 10:20–22"> And I shall leave to me in Israel seven thousand of men, of which the knees be not bowed before Baal, and each mouth that worshipped not him, and kissed not his hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2ND KI 2:8; 2ND KI 2:13–14; 1ST SAMUEL 28:14"> Therefore Elijah went forth from thence, and found Elisha, the son of Shaphat, earing in twelve yokes of oxen; and he was one in the twelve yokes of oxen, earing. And when Elijah had come to him, Elijah &#91;or he&#93; casted his mantle upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:21–22; LUKE 9:61–62; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:37"> And he ran anon after Elijah, when the oxen were left, and said, I pray thee, kiss I my father and my mother, and so I shall pursue &#91;or follow&#93; thee. And Elijah said to him, Go thou, and turn again, for I have done to thee that that was mine to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 24:22; 2ND TIMOTHY 4:11; PHILEMON 1:13"> Soothly he turned again from Elijah, and took twain &#91;or a pair of&#93; oxen, and killed them; and with the plow of the oxen he seethed the flesh, and gave to the people, and they ate; and he rose, and went, and pursued &#91;or followed&#93; Elijah, and ministered to him.</span>
</div>
