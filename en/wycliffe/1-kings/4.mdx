<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:12; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:30; 1ST KINGS 12:19–20"> Forsooth king Solomon was reign-ing on all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:28; 2ND SAMUEL 8:15–18"> And these were the princes which he had; Azariah, the son of Zadok, the priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:16; ISAIAH 62:6; 1ST CHRONICLES 18:15"> Elihoreph, and Ahiah, sons of Shisha, were scribes; Jehoshaphat, the son of Ahilud, was chancellor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:35; 1ST KINGS 2:26–27"> Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, was prince upon the host; and Zadok and Abiathar were priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 27:33; 2ND SAMUEL 15:37; 2ND SAMUEL 16:16"> Azariah, the son of Nathan, was upon them that stood nigh &#91;to&#93; the king; Zabud, the son of Nathan, was&#91;a&#93; priest, a friend of the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:15; 2ND SAMUEL 20:24; 1ST KINGS 12:18"> and Ahishar was steward of the house; and Adoniram, the son of Abda, was upon the tributes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 27:1–15"> Forsooth Solomon had twelve prefects, either chief ministers, on all Israel, that gave lifelode to the king, and to his house; soothly by each month by itself in the year, each prefect by himself ministered necessaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:33; JUDGES 19:1; JUDGES 17:1"> And these be the names of them; Ben-hur, in the hill of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:16; 1ST SAMUEL 6:20; 1ST SAMUEL 6:12"> Ben-dekar, in Makaz, and in Shaal-bim, and in Bethshemesh, and in Elon, and in Bethhanan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:35; JOSHUA 12:17; JOSHUA 17:2"> Ben-hesed, in Aruboth; and Soch-oh, and all the land of Hepher, was his;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:2; JOSHUA 12:23; JOSHUA 17:11"> Ben-abinadab, whose was all Naph-ath, had Dor Taphath, the daughter of Solomon, to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:11; JUDGES 5:19; JOSHUA 3:16"> Baana, the son of Ahilud, governed Taanach, and Megiddo, and all Bethshean, which is beside Zartanah, under Jezreel, from Bethshean unto Abelmeholah, even against Jokneam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:4; NUMBERS 32:41; 1ST KINGS 22:3"> Ben-geber, in Ramoth of Gilead, had Havoth-jair, of the son of Man-asseh, in Gilead; he was sovereign in all the country of Argob, which is in Bashan, to sixty great cities and walled, that had brazen locks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:26; 2ND SAMUEL 17:24; 2ND SAMUEL 2:8"> Ahinadab, the son of Iddo, was sovereign in Mahanaim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:32–39; 2ND SAMUEL 15:27; 1ST SAMUEL 18:18"> Ahimaaz was in Naphtali, but also he had Basmath, the daughter of Solomon, in wedlock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:24–31; 2ND SAMUEL 15:32"> Baanah, the son of Hushai, was in Asher, and in Aloth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:17–23"> Jehoshaphat, the son of Paruah, was in Issachar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:8; JOSHUA 18:20–28; ZECHARIAH 12:13"> Shimei, the son of Elah, was in Benjamin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:26–17; JOSHUA 13:9–12; NUMBERS 21:21–35"> Geber, the son of Uri, was in the land of Gilead, and in the land of Sihon, king of Amorites, and in the land of Og, king of Bashan, and upon all things that were in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; 1ST KINGS 3:8; GENESIS 32:12"> The people of Judah and Israel were unnumberable, as the sand of the sea in multitude, eating, and drinking, and being glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 15:18; JOSHUA 1:4; PSALMS 68:29"> Forsooth Solomon was in his lord-ship, and had all the realms, as from the flood of the land of Philistines, unto the last part of Egypt, of men offering gifts, that is, tributes, to him, and serving to him, in all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Forsooth the meat of Solomon was by each day, thirty cors of clean flour of wheat, and sixty cors of meal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 5:17–18"> ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen of the pasture, and an hundred wethers, besides hunting of harts, of goats, and of bugles, and of birds made fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 22:9; PSALMS 72:11; PSALMS 72:3"> For he held all the country that was beyond the flood, as from Tiphsah unto Azzah, and all the kings of those countries; and he had peace by each part in compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MICAH 4:4; ZECHARIAH 3:10; JUDGES 20:1"> And Judah and Israel dwelled without any dread, each man under his vine, and under his fig tree, from Dan unto Beersheba, in all the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 1:14; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:25; 2ND SAMUEL 8:4"> And Solomon had forty thousand cratches of horses for chariots, and twelve thousand of road horses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and the foresaid prefects or chief masters of the king nourished those horses. But also with great busyness they gave &#91;the&#93; necessaries to the board of king Solomon, in their time;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ESTHER 8:10; ESTHER 8:14; MICAH 1:13"> also they brought barley, and forage of horses and of work beasts, into the place where the king was, after it was ordained to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:12; JAMES 3:17; 1ST KINGS 4:20"> Also God gave to Solomon wisdom, and prudence full much, and largeness of heart, as the sand that is in the brink of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 5:11–12; GENESIS 25:6; DANIEL 1:20"> And the wisdom of Solomon passed the wisdom of all &#91;the&#93; east men, and Egyptians;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 2:6; 1ST CHRONICLES 15:19; 1ST KINGS 3:12"> and he was wiser than all men; he was wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and than Heman, and than Chalcol, and than Darda, the sons of Mahol; and he was named among all folks by compass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 1:1–17; MATTHEW 13:35; PROVERBS 1:1–31"> And Solomon spake three thousand parables, and his songs were a thousand and five;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND KI 19:23; PSALMS 51:7; PSALMS 92:12"> and he disputed of trees, from a cedar which is in Lebanon, till to the hyssop that goeth out of the wall; he disputed of work beasts, and birds, and of creeping beasts, and of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:23; 1ST KINGS 10:1; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:1"> And they came from all peoples to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of &#91;the&#93; earth, that heard his wisdom.</span>
