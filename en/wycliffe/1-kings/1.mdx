<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:1–2; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:27–28; 1ST CHRONICLES 23:1"> And king David waxed eld, and had full many days of age; and when he was covered with clothes, he was not made hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:11; GENESIS 16:5; MICAH 7:5"> Therefore his servants said to him, Seek we to our lord the king a young waxing virgin; and stand she before the king, and nurse she him, and sleep in his bosom, and make hot our lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:18; ESTHER 2:4; ESTHER 2:2"> Therefore they sought a young waxing virgin, fair in all the coasts of Israel; and they found Abishag of Shunem, and they brought her to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:25"> And the damsel was full fair, and she slept with the king, and ministered to him; forsooth the king knew not her fleshly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:1; 2ND SAMUEL 3:4; DEUTERONOMY 17:15–16"> And Adonijah, the son of Haggith, was raised up, and said, I shall reign. And he made to him a chariot, and knights, and fifty men, that ran before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:3–4; 1ST SAMUEL 3:13; HEBREWS 12:5–6"> Neither David, his father, reproved him any time, nor said, Why hast thou done this? But also he was full fair, the second child after Absalom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 20:25; 1ST KINGS 2:22; 1ST SAMUEL 22:20–23"> and his word was with Joab, the son of Zeruiah, and with Abiathar, priest, that helped the parts of Adonijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST KINGS 4:18; 2ND SAMUEL 23:8–39; 2ND SAMUEL 20:25"> But Zadok, the priest, and Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, and Nathan, the prophet, and Shimei, and Cherethites and Pelethites, and all the strength of the host of David, were not with Adonijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 17:17; 2ND SAMUEL 13:23–27; 2ND SAMUEL 15:11–12"> Therefore when rams were offered, and calves, and all fat things, beside the stone &#91;of&#93; Zoheleth, that was nigh the well of Rogel, Adonijah called all his brethren, the sons of the king, and all the men of Judah, servants of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 12:24; 1ST KINGS 1:19; 2ND SAMUEL 12:1–15"> Soothly he called not Nathan, the prophet, and Benaiah, and all the strong men, and Solomon, his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 3:4; 2ND SAMUEL 7:12–17; 2ND SAMUEL 12:24–25"> And so Nathan said to Bathsheba, the mother of Solomon, Whether thou hast heard, that Adonijah, the son of Haggith, hath reigned, and our lord David knoweth not this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 27:31; GENESIS 19:17"> Now therefore come thou, take thou counsel of me, and save thy life, and of Solomon, thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:30; 1ST KINGS 1:17; PSALMS 132:11–12"> Go thou, and enter to king David, and say thou to him, Whether not thou, my lord the king, hast sworn to me, thine handmaid, and saidest, that Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit in my throne? Why therefore reigneth Adonijah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 13:1; 1ST KINGS 1:17–27"> And yet while thou shalt speak there with the king, I shall come after thee, and fulfill thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:1"> Therefore Bathsheba entered to the king in the closet or bed-place; and the king was full eld, and Abishag of Shunem ministered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 25:23; 1ST SAMUEL 24:8; 1ST SAMUEL 20:41"> And Bathsheba bowed herself, and worshipped or honoured the king; to whom the king said, What wilt thou to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:13; 1ST KINGS 1:30; 1ST PETER 3:6"> And she answered, and said, My lord the king, thou hast sworn to thine handmaid by thy Lord God, Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit in my throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:24; 1ST KINGS 1:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:17"> and lo! Adonijah hath reigned now, while thou, my lord the king, knowest not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:25; 1ST KINGS 1:7–10"> he hath slain oxen, and all fat things, and full many rams; and he hath called all the sons of the king, also Abiathar &#91;the&#93; priest, and Joab, the prince of the chivalry; but he called not Solomon, thy servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:1; PSALMS 123:2; 1ST CHRONICLES 22:8–10"> Nevertheless, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel behold into thee, that thou show to them, who oweth to sit in thy throne, my lord the king, after thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:10; DEUTERONOMY 31:16; GENESIS 15:15"> and it shall be, when my lord the king hath slept with his fathers, I and my son Solomon shall be reckoned as sinners, that is, Adonijah shall put on us crimes, to deprive us from life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 1:16–18; GENESIS 24:15; DANIEL 9:20"> While she spake yet with the king, Nathan, the prophet, came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:16; 1ST PETER 2:17; ROMANS 13:7"> And they told to the king, and said, Nathan, the prophet, is present. And when he had entered in the sight of the king, and had worshipped him lowly to the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:17–18; 1ST KINGS 1:5; 1ST KINGS 1:13–14"> Nathan said, My lord the king, saidest thou, Adonijah reign after me, and sit he on my throne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 10:24; 1ST KINGS 1:19; 1ST KINGS 1:9"> For he came down today, and offered oxen, and fat things, and full many wethers; and he called all the sons of the king, &#91;and the princes of the host&#93;, and also Abiathar, &#91;the&#93; priest; and when they ate, and drank before him, and said, King Adonijah live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:8; 1ST KINGS 1:10; 2ND SAMUEL 7:2"> he called not me, thy servant, and Zadok, the priest, and Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, and Solomon, thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 15:15; 1ST KINGS 1:24; 2ND KI 4:27"> Whether this word went out from my lord the king, and thou showedest not to me, thy servant, who should sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And king David answered, and said, Call ye Bathsheba to me. And when she had entered before the king, and had stood before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 4:9; PSALMS 136:24; 1ST SAMUEL 14:45"> the king swore, and said, The Lord liveth, that hath delivered my life from all anguish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:13; 1ST KINGS 1:17"> for as I swore to thee by the Lord God of Israel, and said, Solomon, thy son, shall reign after me, and he shall sit on my throne for me, so I shall do today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DANIEL 2:4; DANIEL 5:10; DANIEL 3:9"> And Bathsheba, with her cheer bowed down into the earth, worship-ped the king, and said, My lord king David live without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:8; 1ST KINGS 1:38; 1ST KINGS 1:26"> And king David said, Call ye Zadok, the priest, to me, and Nathan, the prophet, and Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada. And when they had entered before the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 32:30; 1ST KINGS 1:38; 2ND CHRONICLES 33:14"> the king said to them, Take with you the servants of your lord, and put ye my son Solomon upon my mule, and lead ye him into Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:25; 2ND SAMUEL 15:10; 1ST SAMUEL 10:1"> And &#91;there&#93; Zadok, the priest, and Nathan, the prophet, anoint him into king upon Israel and Judah; and ye shall sing with a trump, and ye shall say, Live king Solomon!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:17; PSALMS 72:1–2; 1ST KINGS 1:13"> Ye shall go up after him, and ye shall come to Jerusalem; and he shall sit upon my throne, and he shall reign for me; and I shall command to him, that he be duke on Israel and on Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 28:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:16; PSALMS 72:19"> And Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, answered to the king, and said, Amen; so speak the Lord God of my lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 20:13; 1ST KINGS 1:47; JOSHUA 1:17"> As the Lord was with my lord the king, so be he with Solomon, and make he the throne of Solomon higher than the throne of my lord king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 8:18; 1ST KINGS 1:8; 1ST KINGS 1:33"> Then Zadok, the priest, went down, and Nathan, the prophet, and Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, and Cherethites, and Pelethites; and they putted &#91;or put&#93; Solomon upon the mule of David, the king, and they brought him into Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 89:20; 1ST KINGS 1:34; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:22"> And Zadok, the priest, took an horn of oil out of the tabernacle, and anointed Solomon; and they sang with a clarion; and all the people said, Live king Solomon!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2ND KI 11:14; ZECHARIAH 9:9; 2ND KI 11:20"> And all the multitude went up after him, and the people of men singing with pipes, and being glad with great joy; and the earth sounded of the cry of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOB 15:21–22; LUKE 17:26–29; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 21:31"> And Adonijah heard, and all that were called of him to the feast; and then the feast was ended. But also Joab said, when the voice of the trump was heard, What will or mean it to itself the cry of the city making noise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 15:36; 2ND SAMUEL 17:17; 2ND SAMUEL 18:27"> Yet while he spake, Jonathan, the son of Abiathar, the priest, came; to whom Adonijah said, Enter thou, for thou art a strong man, and telling good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Jonathan answered to Adon-ijah, Nay; for our lord king David hath ordained Solomon king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and David hath sent with Solomon Zadok, the priest, and Nathan, the prophet, and Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, and Cherethites, and Peleth-ites; and they have put Solomon upon the mule of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:40; DANIEL 5:26–28; 1ST SAMUEL 4:5"> And Zadok, the priest, and Nathan, the prophet, have anointed him king in Gihon; and they came down from thence being glad, and the city resound-ed; this is the voice that ye heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:23; PSALMS 132:11; 1ST KINGS 1:13"> But also Solomon sitteth on the throne of realm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="GENESIS 47:31; 1ST KINGS 1:37; EZRA 6:10"> and the servants of the king have entered, and have blessed our lord king David, and said, God make large the name of Solomon above thy name, and make great his throne above thy throne. And king David worshipped in his bed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:6; PSALMS 132:11–12; PSALMS 145:2"> and furthermore he spake these things, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, that hath given today a sitter in my throne, while mine eyes see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="ISAIAH 21:4–5; PROVERBS 28:1; DANIEL 5:4–6"> Therefore all that were called of Adonijah to the feast, were afeared, and rose up, and each man went into his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1ST KINGS 2:28; EXODUS 21:14; EXODUS 27:2"> And Adonijah dreaded Solomon, and rose up, and went into the tabernacle of the Lord, and he held the horn, or corner, of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And they told to Solomon, and said, Lo! Adonijah dreadeth the king Solomon, and he holdeth the horn, or corner, of the altar, and said, King Solomon swear to me today, that he shall not slay his servant with sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 14:45; 2ND SAMUEL 14:11; PROVERBS 13:6"> And Solomon said, If he is a good man, soothly not an hair of him shall fall into the earth; but if evil be found in him, he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1ST KINGS 1:16; 2ND SAMUEL 14:24; 2ND SAMUEL 1:2"> Therefore king Solomon sent, and led out Adonijah from the altar; and he entered, and worshipped king Solomon; and Solomon said to him, Go into thine house.</span>
