</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:5–6; MATTHEW 13:11; 1ST KINGS 10:1"> And Solomon taught her all &#91;the&#93; words which she had put forth; no word was, that might be hid from the king, and which he answered not to her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:42; 1ST KINGS 3:28; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:3–4"> And the queen of Sheba saw all the wisdom of Solomon, and the house that he had builded,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 26:16; 1ST KINGS 4:22–23; 2ND KI 16:18"> and the meats of his table, and the dwelling places of his servants, and the orders of the men serving him, and the clothes of them, and the butlers, and the burnt sacrifices which he offered in the house of the Lord; and she had no more spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:5–6"> And she said to the king, The word is true, that I heard in my land, of thy words, and of thy wisdom;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 2:9; JOHN 20:25–29; 1ST JOHN 3:2"> and I believed not to men telling to me, till I myself came, and saw with mine eyes, and proved that the half part was not told to me; thy wisdom is more and thy works, than the fame that I heard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:34; PROVERBS 10:21; LUKE 11:31"> Thy men be blessed, and thy servants be blessed, these that stand before thee ever&#91;more&#93;, and hear thy wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 72:2; 1ST KINGS 5:7; 2ND SAMUEL 8:15"> Blessed be thy Lord God, whom thou pleasedest, and hath set thee on the throne of Israel; for the Lord loved Israel without end, and hath ordained thee king, that thou shouldest do doom and rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST KINGS 10:2; MATTHEW 2:11; 1ST KINGS 9:14"> Therefore she gave to the king sixscore talents of gold, and full many sweet smelling things, and precious stones; so many sweet smelling things were no more brought, as those which the queen of Sheba gave to king Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1ST KINGS 9:27–28; 2ND CHRONICLES 8:18; PSALMS 45:9"> But also the ships of Hiram, that brought gold from Ophir, brought from Ophir full many trees of thyine, and precious stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 25:1–31; PSALMS 150:3–5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 14:2–3"> And king Solomon made of the trees of thyine undersettings of the house of the Lord, and of the king’s house, and harps, and citoles to singers; such trees of thyine were not brought there, neither seen again, till into this present day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; MATTHEW 15:28; PSALMS 20:4"> Soothly king Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all things which she would have, and asked of him, besides these things which he had given to her by the king’s gift will-fully; and she turned again, and went into her land with her servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:13–28; 1ST KINGS 9:28"> Forsooth the weight of gold, that was offered to Solomon by each year, was of six hundred and six and sixty talents of gold,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 72:10; 1ST CHRONICLES 9:24; ISAIAH 21:13"> besides that which the men that were on the tollages, that is, rents of things borne about in the land, and that merchants, and all men selling shields, and that all the kings of Arabia, and the dukes of the land, gave.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1ST KINGS 14:26–28; 2ND CHRONICLES 12:9–10; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:15–16"> And king Solomon made two hundred shields of purest gold; he gave six hundred shekels of gold into the plates of one shield;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:2; 1ST KINGS 14:26"> and he made three hundred buck-lers of proved gold; three hundred talents of gold covered one buckler. And the king put those bucklers in the house of the forest of Lebanon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; 1ST KINGS 10:22; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:11"> Also king Solomon made a great throne of ivory, and covered it with full fine gold;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the throne had six degrees; and the highness of the throne was round in the hinder part; and twain &#91;or two&#93; hands were on this side and on that side, holding the seat, and two lions stood beside each hand;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:9; GENESIS 49:9; NUMBERS 23:24"> and twelve little lions standing on &#91;the&#93; six degrees, on this side and on that side; such a work was not made in all realms.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST KINGS 10:17; 1ST KINGS 7:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:20–22"> But also all the vessels, of which king Solomon drank, were of gold, and all the appurtenance of the house of the forest of Lebanon was of purest gold; silver was not used at all, neither it was areckoned of any price in the days of Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:48; GENESIS 10:4; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:36–37"> For the ships of the king went once by three years with the ships of Hiram into Tharshish, and brought from thence gold, and silver, and teeth of elephants, and apes, and peacocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:12–13; COLOSSIANS 2:2–3; 1ST KINGS 4:29–34"> Therefore king Solomon was magnified above all &#91;the&#93; kings of &#91;the&#93; earth in riches and wisdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1ST KINGS 3:12; JAMES 1:5; 1ST KINGS 3:28"> And all earth desired to see the cheer of Solomon, to hear the wisdom of him, which wisdom God had given in his heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOB 42:11; PSALMS 72:10; PSALMS 72:15"> And all men brought gifts to him, vessels of gold, and of silver, clothes, and armours &#91;or arms&#93; of battle, and sweet smelling things, and horses, and mules, by each year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:25; 1ST KINGS 4:26; DEUTERONOMY 17:16"> And Solomon gathered together chariots, and horsemen; and a thou-sand and four hundred chariots were made to him, and twelve thousand horsemen; and he disposed them by &#91;the&#93; strengthened cities, and with the king in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 1:15–17; DEUTERONOMY 17:17; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:27"> And he made, that so great abundance of silver was in Jerusalem, how great was also of stones; and he gave the multitude of cedars as syca-mores, that grow in field places.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:16; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:28; ISAIAH 31:1–3"> And the horses of Solomon were led out of Egypt, and of Coa; for merchants of the king bought them of Coa, and brought them to him, for &#91;the&#93; price ordained.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:4; 2ND KI 7:6–7; MALACHI 1:1"> For a chariot went out of Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and an horse for an hundred and fifty shekels; and by this manner all the kings of Hittites, and of Syria, sold horses.</span>
</div>
