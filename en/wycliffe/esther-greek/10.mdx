<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Mordecai said, These things be done of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> I have mind on a dream, which I saw, signifying these same things, and nothing of those was void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A little well, that waxed &#91;or grew&#93; into a flood, and was turned into light, and sun, and turned again into full many waters, this well is Esther, whom the king took into wife, and would that she were his queen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the two dragons, I am, and Haman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and folks that came together, be these, that enforced or endeavoured to do away the name of Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But my folk Israel it is, that cried to the Lord; and the Lord made safe his people, and he hath delivered us from all evils, and he hath done great signs, or tokens, and wonders among heathen men &#91;or the Gentiles;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> and he hath commanded two lots to be, one of God’s people, and the other of all heathen men &#91;or all Gentiles&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And then ever either lot came into a day ordained, or determined, from that time before God and all folks. &#91;And either lot came into the set day before God now from that time to all Gentiles.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the Lord had mind on his people, and had mercy on his heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And these days shall be kept in the month Adar, or March, in the fourteenth and the fifteenth day of the same month, with all busyness and joy of the people gathered into one company, into all the generations of the people of Israel afterward.</span>
