<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:22; MICAH 6:2; ISAIAH 3:13–14"> Sons of Israel, hear ye the word of the Lord, for why doom is to the Lord with the dwellers of earth; for why truth is not, and mercy is not, and knowing of the Lord is not in earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HOSEA 6:9; JEREMIAH 6:7; HOSEA 7:1"> Curse, and leasing, and man-quelling, and theft, and adultery flowed, and blood touched blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:3; ISAIAH 24:4–12; NAHUM 1:4"> For this thing the earth shall mourn, and each that dwelleth in that land shall be sick, in the beast of the field, and in the bird of the air; but also the fishes of the sea shall be gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:12; HOSEA 4:17; AMOS 5:13"> Nevertheless each man deem not, and a man be not reproved; for thy people is as they that against-say the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:8; HOSEA 2:2; GALATIANS 4:26"> And thou shalt fall today, and the prophet also shall fall with thee; in the night I made thy mother to be still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ISAIAH 5:13; PROVERBS 19:2; HOSEA 6:6"> My people was still, for it had not knowing; for thou hast put away knowing, I shall put thee away, that thou use not priesthood to me; and for thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, also I shall forget thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSEA 13:6; PHILIPPIANS 3:19; 1ST SAMUEL 2:30"> By the multitude of them, so they sinned against me. I shall change the glory of them into shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; ISAIAH 56:11; TITUS 1:11"> They shall eat the sins of my people, and they shall raise the souls of them to the wickedness of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ISAIAH 24:2; JEREMIAH 5:31; MATTHEW 15:14"> And it shall be, as the people, so the priest; and I shall visit on him the ways of him, and I shall yield to him the thoughts of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICAH 6:14; LEVITICUS 26:26; HAGGAI 1:6"> And they shall eat, and they shall not be &#91;ful&#93; filled; they did fornication, and ceased not, for they forsook the Lord in not keeping his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:1; LUKE 21:34; ISAIAH 28:7"> Fornication, and wine, and drunkenness do away the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:27; HOSEA 5:4; HABAKKUK 2:19"> My people asked in his tree, and the staff thereof told to it; for the spirit of fornication deceived them, and they did fornication, going away from their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:29; JEREMIAH 3:6; EZEKIEL 6:13"> On the heads, either tops, of mountains they made sacrifice, and on the little hills they burnt incense under an oak, and a poplar, and a terebinth, for the shadow thereof was good. Therefore your daughters shall do fornication, and your wives shall be adulteresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HOSEA 4:1; JOHN 8:43; EPHESIANS 4:18"> I shall not visit on your daughters, when they do fornication, and on your wives, when they do adultery; for they lived with whores, and made sacrifice with men turned into women’s conditions. And the people that understandeth not, shall be beaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HOSEA 12:11; HOSEA 9:15; AMOS 4:4"> If thou, Israel, doest fornication, namely Judah, trespass not; and do not ye enter into Gilgal, and go ye not into Bethaven, neither swear ye, The Lord liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:24; JEREMIAH 8:5; JEREMIAH 3:11"> For as a wild cow Israel bowed away; now the Lord shall feed them as a lamb in broadness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 81:12; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:11; HOSEA 13:2"> Ephraim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Throughout Hosea (and also Isaiah), Ephraim is another name for the Northern Kingdom of Israel, consisting of the Ten Tribes.">a</a></sup>is the partner of idols, leave thou him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; AMOS 5:12; 2ND KI 17:7–17"> the feast of them is parted. By fornication they did fornication, the defenders thereof loved to bring shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 1:29; JEREMIAH 4:11–12; HOSEA 13:15"> The spirit bound him in his wings, and they shall be ashamed of their sacrifices.</span>
