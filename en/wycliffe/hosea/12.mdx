<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MICAH 6:2; ISAIAH 10:6; HOSEA 4:1"> Therefore the doom of the Lord is with Judah, and visiting is on Jacob; by the ways of him, and by the findings of him, he shall yield to him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 3:15; ISAIAH 42:8; PSALMS 135:13"> And the Lord God of hosts, the Lord, is the memorial of him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:40–43; NEHEMIAH 8:15–17; HOSEA 13:4"> And I am thy Lord God from the land of Egypt; yet I shall make thee to sit in tabernacles, as in the days of feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND KI 17:13; EZEKIEL 20:49; JEREMIAH 19:1"> And I spake by prophets, and I multiplied vision, either prophesy, and I was likened in the hand of prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 4:15; HOSEA 6:8; HOSEA 8:11"> If Gilead worshippeth an idol, therefore they err in vain offering to oxes in Gilgal; for why and the altars of them shall be as heaps on the furrows of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 31:41; GENESIS 27:43; GENESIS 28:1–22"> Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and served, either kept sheep for a wife.</span>
