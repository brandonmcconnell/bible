<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MICAH 5:13; 1ST KINGS 18:21; ZEPHANIAH 1:5"> In simulacra the heart of them is parted &#91;or The heart of them is parted&#93;, now they shall perish. He shall break the simulacra of them, he shall rob &#91;or spoil&#93; the altars of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 10:15; HOSEA 10:7; HOSEA 13:11"> For then they shall say, A king is not to us, for we dread&#91;ed&#93; not the Lord. And what shall a king do to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="AMOS 5:7; AMOS 6:12; HOSEA 4:2"> Speak ye words of unprofitable vision, and ye shall smite bond of peace with leasing; and doom as bitterness shall burgeon on the furrows of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HOSEA 9:11; HOSEA 5:8; 2ND KI 23:5"> The dwellers of Samaria worship-ped the kine of Bethaven. For the people thereof mourned on that calf, and the keepers of the house thereof; they made full out joy on it in the glory thereof, for it passed from that people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 5:13; ISAIAH 30:3; DANIEL 11:8"> For also it was borne to Assur, a gift to the king avenger. Confusion shall take Ephraim, and Israel shall be shamed in his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSEA 10:3; JUDE 1:13; 2ND KI 1:3"> Samaria made his king to pass as froth on the face of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 23:30; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:16; ISAIAH 2:19"> And the high things of idol, the sin of Israel, shall be lost. A clote and a briar shall ascend &#91;or go up&#93; on the altars of them. And they shall say to mountains, Cover ye us, and to little hills, Fall ye down on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSEA 9:9; JUDGES 20:5; JUDGES 19:22–30"> From the days of Gibeah Israel sinned; there they stood. Battle shall not overtake them in Gibeah, on the sons of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:13; HOSEA 4:9; JEREMIAH 16:16"> By my desire I shall chastise them; peoples shall be gathered together on them, when they shall be chastised for their two wickednesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 4:16; JEREMIAH 50:11; DEUTERONOMY 25:4"> Ephraim is a cow calf, taught for to love threshing; and I went on the fairness of the neck thereof. I shall go upon Ephraim. Judah shall ear, and Jacob shall break furrows to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:3–4; JAMES 3:18; ISAIAH 45:8"> Sow ye to you rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93; in truth, and reap ye in the mouth of mercy, and make ye new to you a field newly brought to tilth. Forsooth time is to seek the Lord, when he cometh, that shall teach you rightfulness &#91;or rightwiseness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 4:8; GALATIANS 6:7–8; PROVERBS 1:31"> Ye have eared unfaithfulness, ye have reaped wickedness, ye have eaten the corn of leasing. For thou trustedest in thy ways, and in the multitude of thy strong men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HOSEA 13:16; 2ND KI 18:9–10; AMOS 3:8"> Noise shall rise in thy people, and all thy strongholds shall be destroyed; as Shalman was destroyed of the house of him, that took vengeance on Betharbel; in the day of battle, when the mother was hurtled down on the sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HOSEA 10:5; HOSEA 10:7; ROMANS 7:13"> So Bethel did to you, for the face of malice of your wickednesses. As the morrowtide passeth, the king of Israel shall pass forth.</span>
