<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 32:19; EXODUS 31:18; PSALMS 119:89"> And afterward God said, Hew to thee two tables of stone at the like-ness of the former, and I shall write on those tables those words, which the tables, that thou brakest, had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 19:20; DEUTERONOMY 9:25; EXODUS 19:11"> Be thou ready in the morrowtide, that thou go up anon into the hill of Sinai; and thou shalt stand with me on the top of the hill;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 19:12–13; EXODUS 19:21; 1ST TIMOTHY 2:5"> no man go up with thee, neither any man be seen by all the hill; and oxen and sheep be not fed against the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Therefore Moses hewed two tables of stone, which manner the tables were before, and he rose by night, and went up into the hill of Sinai, as the Lord commanded to him; and he bare with him the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 33:19; NUMBERS 11:25; LUKE 9:34–35"> And when the Lord had come down by a cloud, Moses stood with him, and called inwardly the name of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 86:15; JOEL 2:13; PSALMS 103:8–13"> and when the Lord passed before him, he said, Lordshipper, Lord God, merciful, and pious, patient, and of much mercy doing, and soothfast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5–6; PSALMS 103:3; DEUTERONOMY 5:9–10"> which keepest covenant and mercy into thousands, which doest away wickedness, and trespasses, and sins, and no man by himself is innocent with thee, which yieldest the wicked-ness of fathers to their sons, and to the sons of their sons, into the third and the fourth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 4:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 20:18; GENESIS 17:3"> And hastily Moses was bowed low to &#91;the&#93; earth, and worshipped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 33:12; DEUTERONOMY 32:9; PSALMS 94:14"> and said, Lord, if I have found grace in thy sight, I beseech thee, that thou go with us, for the people is of hard noll; and that thou do away our wickednesses and sins, and wield us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:21; PSALMS 77:14; ISAIAH 64:3"> The Lord answered, I shall make covenant, and in sight of all men I shall make signs, that were never seen on &#91;the&#93; earth, neither in any folks, that this people, in whose midst thou art, see the fearedful work of the Lord, that I shall make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 33:2; EXODUS 3:17; EXODUS 3:8"> Keep thou all things, which I command to thee today; I myself shall cast out before thy face Amorites, and Canaanites, and Hittites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 23:32–33; JOSHUA 23:12–13; DEUTERONOMY 7:2"> Beware, lest any time thou join friendships with the dwellers of that land, which friendships be into falling to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2ND KI 18:4; EXODUS 23:24; DEUTERONOMY 7:25–26"> But also destroy thou their altars, break the images, and cut thou down their &#91;maumet&#93; woods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:24; DEUTERONOMY 32:21; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:22"> do not thou worship an alien God; a jealous lover is the Lord’s name, God is a fervent lover;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 2:17; PSALMS 106:28; NUMBERS 25:2"> make thou not covenant with the men of those countries, lest when they have done fornication, that is, idolatry, with their gods, and have worshipped the simulacra of them, any man call thee, that thou eat of the things offered to an idol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:3–4; 1ST KINGS 11:2–4; EZRA 9:2"> Neither thou shalt take a wife of their daughters to thy sons, lest after those daughters have done fornication, that is, idolatry, they make also thy sons to do fornication into their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:4; EXODUS 32:8; ISAIAH 46:6–7"> Thou shalt not make to thee molten gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 13:4; LEVITICUS 23:6; EXODUS 13:6–7"> Thou shalt keep the solemnity of therf loaves; seven days thou shalt eat therf loaves, as I commanded to thee, in the time of the month of new fruits; for in the month of ver or spring time thou wentest out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 13:2; EXODUS 22:29; EXODUS 13:12"> All thing of male kind that open-eth the womb shall be mine, of all living beasts, as well of oxen, as of sheep, it shall be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 13:13; EXODUS 23:15; DEUTERONOMY 16:16"> Thou shalt again-buy with a sheep the first engendered of an ass, else if thou givest not &#91;the&#93; price therefore, it shall be slain. Thou shalt again-buy the first begotten of thy sons; neither thou shalt appear void in my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 23:12; DEUTERONOMY 21:4; LUKE 13:14"> Six days thou shalt work, in the seventh day thou shalt cease to ear and to reap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 23:16; DEUTERONOMY 16:10–15; JOHN 7:2"> Thou shalt make to thee the solemnity of weeks, in the first things of fruits of thy ripe corn of wheat, and the solemnity when all things be gathered into barns, when the time of the year cometh again<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Exodus 23:16, and footnote #7.">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 23:14; DEUTERONOMY 16:16; EXODUS 23:17"> Each male kind of thee shall appear in three times of the year in the sight of the Lord Almighty, thy God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 78:55; EXODUS 33:2; PROVERBS 16:7"> For when I shall take away folks from thy face, and I shall alarge thy terms, none shall set treasons to thy land, while thou goest up, and appearest in the sight of thy Lord God, thrice in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 23:18; EXODUS 12:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:7–8"> Thou shalt not offer on sour-dough the blood of my sacrifice, neither anything of the slain sacrifice of the solemnity of pask shall abide unto the morrowtide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 23:19; DEUTERONOMY 14:21; DEUTERONOMY 26:2"> Thou shalt offer in the house of thy Lord God the first of the fruits of thy land. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in the milk of his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 24:4; DEUTERONOMY 31:9; DEUTERONOMY 4:13"> And the Lord said to Moses, Write thou these words, by which I smote a bond of peace, both with thee and with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 34:1; EXODUS 24:18; DEUTERONOMY 9:18"> Therefore Moses was there with the Lord by forty days and forty nights, and he ate not bread, and drank not water; and he wrote in &#91;the&#93; tables the ten words of the bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:2; LUKE 9:29; EXODUS 32:15"> And when Moses came down from the hill of Sinai, he held in his hands two tables of witnessing, and he wist not that his face was horned with wonderful shining beams, of the fellowship of God’s word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MARK 9:3; LUKE 5:8; NUMBERS 12:8"> Forsooth Aaron and the sons of Israel saw Moses’ face horned, and they dreaded to nigh &#91;or come&#93; nigh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 3:15; EXODUS 24:1–3; GENESIS 45:3"> and they were called of him, and they turned again, as well Aaron as the princes of the synagogue; and after that Moses spake,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1ST KINGS 22:14; EXODUS 24:3; MATTHEW 28:20"> they came to him, yea all the sons of Israel; to which he commanded all things, which he had heard of the Lord in the hill of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 4:4–6; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:13–18; ROMANS 10:4"> And when the words were fulfilled, he put a veil on his face;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 3:16; HEBREWS 10:19–22; HEBREWS 4:16"> and he entered to the Lord, and spake with him, and he did away that veil, till he went out; and then he spake to the sons of Israel all things that were commanded to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="EXODUS 34:29–30; JOHN 5:35; DANIEL 12:3"> which saw that the face of Moses going out was horned, but again he covered his face, if any time he spake to them.</span>
