</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:10–12"> And the Lord did by the word of Moses; and the paddocks &#91;or frogs&#93; were dead from &#91;the&#93; houses, and from &#91;the&#93; towns, and from &#91;the&#93; fields;</span>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 8:8; EXODUS 9:27; EXODUS 10:16"> And Pharaoh called Moses and Aaron, and said to them, Go ye, make ye sacrifice to the Lord your God, in this land.</span>
