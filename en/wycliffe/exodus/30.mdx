</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:50; 1ST CHRONICLES 27:24; EXODUS 38:25–26"> When thou shalt take the sum of the sons of Israel, all by themselves shall give by the number price for their souls to the Lord, and vengeance shall not be in them, when they be numbered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:25; NUMBERS 3:47; EZEKIEL 45:12"> Soothly each that passeth to the named, that is, of twenty years, shall give this price, half a shekel by the measure of the temple; a shekel hath twenty half-pence; the half part of a shekel shall be offered to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:20; NUMBERS 1:18; NUMBERS 1:3"> He that is had in the number, from twenty years and above, shall give this price;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:50; LEVITICUS 17:11; EXODUS 30:12"> a rich man shall not add to the half of a shekel, and a poor man shall nothing abate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 10:32–33; NUMBERS 16:40; EXODUS 38:25–31"> And thou shalt betake into the uses of the tabernacle of witnessing the money taken, which is gathered of the sons of Israel, that it be a mind of them before the Lord, and he shall be merciful to their souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 40:7; EXODUS 38:8; 1ST KINGS 7:23"> Also thou shalt make a great vessel of brass with his foundament to wash in, and thou shalt set it betwixt the tabernacle of witnessing and the altar; and when water is put therein,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 52:11; EXODUS 40:31–32; PSALMS 26:6"> Aaron and his sons shall wash therein their hands and their feet,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1–3; HEBREWS 12:28–29; 1ST CHRONICLES 13:10"> when they shall enter into the tabernacle of witnessing, and when they shall nigh to the altar, that they offer therein incense to the Lord, lest peradventure they die;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 28:43; EXODUS 27:21"> it shall be a lawful thing everlasting to him and to his seed by successions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:19; SONG OF SONGS 4:14; EZEKIEL 27:22"> Take to thee sweet smelling spiceries, of the first and chosen myrrh, five hundred shekels; and of canel the half, that is, two hundred and fifty shekels; &#91;and&#93; in like manner of calamus, that is, a sweet smelling tree, small and full of knots, two hundred and fifty shekels;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; EXODUS 29:40; NUMBERS 3:47"> also of cassia five hundred shekels, in the weight of &#91;the&#93; saintuary; the oil of olives trees, the measure of hin;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; 1ST CHRONICLES 9:30; EXODUS 40:9"> and thou shalt make the holy oil of anointing, an ointment made by the craft of an ointment maker.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:1; 1ST JOHN 2:20; ISAIAH 61:1"> And thou shalt anoint thereof the tabernacle of witnessing, and the ark of the testament,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and the board with his vessels, &#91;and&#93; the candlestick, and the appur-tenances thereof, the altars of incense,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and of burnt sacrifice, and all the appurtenance, that pertaineth to the adorning of those things.</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:12; EXODUS 28:3; LEVITICUS 8:30"> Thou shalt anoint Aaron, and his sons, and thou shalt hallow them, that they be set in priesthood to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; LEVITICUS 21:10; PSALMS 89:20"> And thou shalt say to the sons of Israel, This oil of anointing shall be holy to me into your generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 30:25; EXODUS 30:37–38; LEVITICUS 21:10"> The flesh of a man shall not be anointed thereof, and by the making thereof ye shall not make another such oil, for it is hallowed, and it shall be holy to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 30:38; GENESIS 17:14; EXODUS 12:15"> Whatever man maketh such oil, and giveth thereof to an alien, he shall be put out of his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; EXODUS 25:6; EXODUS 30:23"> Forsooth the Lord said to Moses, Take to thee sweet smelling spiceries, stacte, and onycha, galbanum of good odour, and most pure frankincense, all these shall be of even weight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:13; EXODUS 30:25; JOHN 12:3"> And thou shalt make incense, made by the craft of an ointment maker, meddled &#91;or mingled&#93; together diligently, and pure, and most worthy of hallowing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 25:22; EXODUS 30:6; EXODUS 16:34"> And when thou hast pounded all these things into smallest powder, thou shalt put thereof before the tabernacle of witnessing, in which place I shall appear to thee; the made incense shall be to you the holy of holy things.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; EXODUS 29:37; EXODUS 30:32–33"> Ye shall not make such a making into your &#91;own&#93; uses, for it is holy to the Lord.</span>
