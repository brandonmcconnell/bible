<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 34:32; EXODUS 31:1–11; ROMANS 2:13"> Therefore when all the company of the sons of Israel was gathered, Moses said to them, These things it be, which the Lord commanded to be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:3; LUKE 13:14–15; EXODUS 20:9–10"> Six days ye shall do work, the seventh day shall be holy to you, the sabbath and the rest of the Lord; he that doeth work in the sabbath shall be slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 16:23; NUMBERS 15:32–36; EXODUS 12:16"> Ye shall not kindle fire in all your dwelling places by the sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 25:1–2"> And Moses said to all the company of the sons of Israel, This is the word which the Lord commanded, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:7; PSALMS 110:3; EXODUS 25:2–7"> Separate ye at you the first fruits to the Lord; each willful man and of ready will offer them to the Lord, gold, and silver, and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 26:7–14; EXODUS 26:31; EXODUS 28:15"> and jacinth, and purple, and red silk twice-dyed, and bis, and hairs of goats,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and skins of rams made red, and skins of jacinth, and &#91;the&#93; wood of shittim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 27:20; EXODUS 25:1–40; EXODUS 30:23"> and oil to the lights to be ordained, and spices so that the ointment be made, and the incense most sweet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 39:6–14; EXODUS 28:9; EXODUS 25:5"> stones of onyx, and gems, to the adorning of the cloth on the shoulders &#91;or cape&#93;, and of the rational.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 31:1–6; EXODUS 36:1–4"> Whoever of you is wise, come he, and make that, that the Lord commanded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 26:1–37; EXODUS 36:8–34; EXODUS 31:7–9"> that is, the tabernacle, and the roof thereof, and the covering; rings, and the buildings of boards, with &#91;the&#93; bars, stakes, and foundaments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 25:10–22; EXODUS 26:31–33; EXODUS 37:1–9"> the ark, and bars; the propitiatory, and the veil, which is hanged before it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 25:23–30; LEVITICUS 24:5–6; EXODUS 37:10–16"> the board, with bars, and vessels, and with &#91;the&#93; loaves of setting forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 25:31–39; MATTHEW 5:14–15; EXODUS 37:17–24"> the candlestick to sustain &#91;the&#93; lights, the vessels, and lanterns thereof, and oil to the nourishing of fires;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 30:1–10; EXODUS 30:22–38; EXODUS 37:25–28"> the altar of incense, and the bars; the oil of anointing, and &#91;the&#93; incense of sweet smelling spiceries &#91;or spices&#93;; the curtain at the door of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 27:1–8; EXODUS 30:18–21; EXODUS 38:1–8"> the altar of burnt sacrifice, and his brazen griddle, with his bars, and vessels; the great washing vessel, and his foundament;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:2; EXODUS 38:9–20; EXODUS 27:9–19"> the curtains of the large entry, with the pillars, and their bases; the curtain in the doors of the porch;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 27:19"> the stakes of the tabernacle, and of the large entry, with their cords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 31:10; NUMBERS 4:5–15; EXODUS 28:1–43"> the clothes, whose use is in the service of the saintuary; the clothes of Aaron the bishop, and of his sons, that they be set in priesthood to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the multitude of the sons of Israel went out of the sight of Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 25:2; EXODUS 35:26; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:14"> and offered with most ready soul and devout the first things to the Lord, to make the work of the tabernacle of witnessing, whatever was needful to the adorning, and to the holy clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 7:70–72; MATTHEW 2:11; EZRA 2:68–69"> Men and women gave bies of the arms, and earrings, and other rings, and ornaments of their arms nigh the hand; each golden vessel was separated into the gifts of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 29:8; EXODUS 25:2–7; EXODUS 35:6–10"> If any man had jacinth, and purple, and red silk twice-dyed, bis, and the hairs of goats, skins of rams made red, and skins of jacinth, either blue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12"> metals of silver, and of brass, they offered to the Lord, and &#91;the&#93; wood of shittim into diverse uses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 16:12; LUKE 8:2–3; PROVERBS 14:1"> But also women taught gave those things, which they had spun, jacinth, and purple, and vermilion, and bis,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 35:21; EXODUS 36:8; EXODUS 35:29"> and the hairs of goats; and they gave all things by their own free will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZRA 2:68; 1ST CHRONICLES 29:6; EXODUS 35:9"> Forsooth princes offered stones of onyx, and gems, to the cloth on the shoulders &#91;or cape&#93;, and to the rational,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 30:23–38; EXODUS 35:8"> and sweet smelling spiceries &#91;or spices&#93;, and oil to the lights to be ordained, and to make ready the ointment, and to make the incense of the sweetest odour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:7; EXODUS 35:21–22; EXODUS 35:4"> All men and women offered gifts with devout souls, that the works should be made, which the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses; all the sons of Israel hallowed will-fully things to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 31:1–6; JAMES 1:17; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:11"> And Moses said to the sons of Israel, Lo! the Lord hath called Bezaleel by name, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the lineage of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JAMES 1:17; ISAIAH 11:2–5; ISAIAH 61:1–3"> and the Lord hath filled him with the spirit of God, of wisdom, and of understanding, and of knowing, and with all doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> to find out and to make work in gold, and silver, and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> and in stones to be engraved, and in work of carpentry; whatever thing may be found craftily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 31:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 2:14; EZRA 7:27"> the Lord hath given in his heart; and the Lord hath called Aholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the lineage of Dan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="EXODUS 35:31; 1ST KINGS 7:14; ISAIAH 28:26"> the Lord &#91;hath&#93; taught both with wisdom, that they make the works of a carpenter, of a stainer, and of an embroiderer, of jacinth, and of purple, and of red silk twice-dyed, and of bis, and that they make all things, and find all new things.</span>
