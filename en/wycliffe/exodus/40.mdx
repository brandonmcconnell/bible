<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spake to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 26:30; EXODUS 13:4; NUMBERS 7:1"> In the first month, in the first day of the month, thou shalt raise the tabernacle of witnessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 35:12; NUMBERS 4:5; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:19"> And thou shalt set the ark therein, and thou shalt leave a veil before it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:8; EXODUS 40:22–25; EXODUS 37:10–24"> And when the board is borne in, thou shalt set thereon those things that be commanded justly, either by the law. The candlestick shall stand with his lanterns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:19–22; HEBREWS 9:24; 1ST JOHN 2:1–2"> and the golden altar, wherein the incense is burnt before the ark of witnessing. Thou shalt set a curtain in the entering of the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 40:29; 1ST JOHN 4:9–10; HEBREWS 13:10"> and before it thou shalt set the altar of burnt sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:1; 1ST JOHN 1:7; PSALMS 26:6"> thou shalt set the washing vessel betwixt the altar and the tabernacle, which washing vessel thou shalt fill with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 40:33; EPHESIANS 4:11–12; MATTHEW 16:18"> And thou shalt encompass the great porch, and the entering thereof, with curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:10; NUMBERS 7:1; MATTHEW 3:16"> And when thou hast taken the oil of anointing, thou shalt anoint the tabernacle, with his vessels, that those &#91;or they&#93; be hallowed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 29:36–37; LUKE 1:35; ISAIAH 61:1"> the altar of burnt sacrifice, and all the vessels thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> the washing vessel, with his foundament or base. Thou shalt anoint all things with the oil of anointing, that they be holy of holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:1–13; LEVITICUS 9:1–24; EXODUS 29:1–35"> And thou shalt present Aaron and his sons to the door of the tabernacle of witnessing; and, when they be washed with water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 28:41; 1ST JOHN 2:20; 1ST JOHN 2:27"> thou shalt clothe them in holy clothes, that they minister to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ISAIAH 61:10; ROMANS 8:30; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30"> (See verse 13 above.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:13; HEBREWS 5:1–14; HEBREWS 8:1–10"> and that the anointing of them profit into everlasting priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:1; EXODUS 40:17–32; DEUTERONOMY 12:32"> And Moses did all things that the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:1; EXODUS 40:1–2; NUMBERS 9:1"> Therefore in the first month of the second year, in the first day of the month, the tabernacle was set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:27–28; 1ST PETER 1:5; LEVITICUS 26:11"> And Moses areared it, and he set the boards, and bases, and bars, and he ordained &#91;the&#93; pillars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 26:1–14; EXODUS 36:8–19"> and he stretched out the roof upon the tabernacle, and he put a covering above, as the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 16:34; DEUTERONOMY 10:5; HEBREWS 10:19–21"> He put also the witnessing, that is, the tables of the law, in the ark, and he set the bars within, and God’s answering place above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 26:33; EXODUS 40:3; EXODUS 35:12"> And when he had brought the ark into the tabernacle, he hanged a veil before it, that he should fulfill the commandment of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 26:35; JOHN 6:53–57; EPHESIANS 3:8"> He setted &#91;or put&#93; also the board in the tabernacle of witnessing, at the north coast, without the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 25:30; EXODUS 40:4; HEBREWS 9:2"> and he ordained the loaves of setting forth before, as the Lord commanded to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 26:35; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:5; JOHN 1:1"> He set &#91;or put&#93; also the candlestick in the tabernacle of witnessing, even against the board, in the south side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 40:4; EXODUS 25:37; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 4:5"> and he set the lanterns by order, by the commandment of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 40:5; HEBREWS 7:25; JOHN 11:42"> He put also the golden altar under the roof of witnessing, that is, of the tabernacle, against the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 30:7"> and he burnt thereon incense of sweet smelling spiceries &#91;or spices&#93;, as the Lord commanded to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 40:5; HEBREWS 10:19–20; EPHESIANS 2:18"> He set &#91;or put&#93; also a curtain in the entering of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 40:6; HEBREWS 13:5–6; HEBREWS 13:10"> and the altar of burnt sacrifice in the porch of the witnessing, and he offered there burnt sacrifice, and sacrifices, as the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 40:7; EXODUS 38:8; EZEKIEL 36:25"> Also he ordained the washing vessel, betwixt the tabernacle of witnessing and the altar, and filled it with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 30:19–20; PSALMS 26:6; JOHN 13:10"> And Moses, and Aaron, and his sons, washed their hands and their feet therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 73:19; EXODUS 30:19–20; EXODUS 40:19"> when they entered into the tabernacle of the bond of peace, and nighed to the altar, as the Lord commanded to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 40:8; EXODUS 27:9–16; JOHN 17:4"> He areared also the great porch, by compass of the tabernacle and of the altar, and setted a curtain in the entering thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 15:8; NUMBERS 9:15–23; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:2"> After that all things were perfectly made, a cloud covered the tabernacle of witnessing, and the glory of the Lord filled it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:11; LEVITICUS 16:2; 2ND CHRONICLES 7:2"> neither Moses might enter into the tabernacle of the bond of peace, while the cloud covered all things, and the majesty of the Lord shined, for the cloud covered all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:19; NUMBERS 9:17; EXODUS 13:21–22"> If any time the cloud left the tabernacle, the sons of Israel went forth by their companies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:19–22; PSALMS 31:15"> if the cloud hanged there above, they dwelled in the same place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="PSALMS 78:14; NUMBERS 9:15; EXODUS 13:21"> for the cloud of the Lord rested on the tabernacle by day, and fire in the night, in the sight of the people of Israel, by all their dwellings.</span>
