<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 14:2; DEUTERONOMY 32:10; NUMBERS 33:6–8"> but God led the people about by the way of desert, which way is beside the Red Sea. And the sons of Israel were armed, and went up from the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 50:24–25; JOSHUA 24:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:16"> And Moses took the bones of Joseph with him, for he had charged the sons of Israel, and had said, God shall visit you, and bear ye out from hence my bones with you.</span>
