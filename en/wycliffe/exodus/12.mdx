<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Also the Lord said to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 13:4; DEUTERONOMY 16:1; EXODUS 34:18"> This month, the beginning of months to you, shall be the first in the months of the year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:7; JOHN 1:29"> Speak ye to all the company of the sons of Israel, and say ye to them, In the tenth day of this month, each man take a lamb by his meines and houses;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> but if the number is less, that it may not suffice to eat the lamb, he shall take it with his neighbour, which is joined to his house, by the number of souls, that may suffice to the eating of the lamb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST PETER 1:18–19; HEBREWS 9:13–14; MALACHI 1:14"> Forsooth the lamb shall be a male of one year, without wem; by which custom ye shall take also a kid, if a lamb may not be had in good manner;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:5; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:15–18; NUMBERS 28:16"> and ye shall keep him till to the fourteenth day of this month; and all the multitude of the sons of Israel shall offer him at eventide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:28; EXODUS 12:22–23; HEBREWS 9:22"> And they shall take of his blood, and they shall put it on ever either doorpost, and in the lintels, or higher thresholds, of the houses, in which they shall eat him;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 34:25; DEUTERONOMY 16:7; EXODUS 13:3"> and in that night they shall eat flesh, roasted with fire, and therf loaves, with the herb lettuce or with bitternesses of the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 12:8; DEUTERONOMY 16:7; LAMENTATIONS 1:13"> Ye shall not eat thereof any raw thing, neither sodden in water, but roasted only by fire; ye shall devour the head with the feet, and with the entrails thereof;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 23:18; EXODUS 34:25; EXODUS 29:34"> neither anything thereof shall abide till to the morrowtide; if anything is left, ye shall burn it in the fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 12:27; EPHESIANS 6:15; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:7"> Forsooth thus ye shall eat him; ye shall gird your reins, and ye shall have shoes in your feet, and ye shall hold staves in your hands, and ye shall eat it hastily; for it is pask, that is, the passing &#91;forth&#93; of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:4; EXODUS 12:23; PSALMS 82:6"> And I shall pass through the land of Egypt in that night, and I shall smite all the first engendered or the first begotten things in the land of Egypt, from man till to beast; and I the Lord shall make dooms in all the gods of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:28; EXODUS 12:23; 1ST JOHN 1:7"> Forsooth &#91;the&#93; blood shall be to you into sign, in the houses in which ye shall be; and I shall see the blood, and I shall pass over you; neither a wound destroying shall be in you, when I shall smite the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND KI 23:21; EXODUS 12:24; EXODUS 13:9–10"> Forsooth ye shall have this day into mind, and ye shall make it solemn to the Lord in your generations, by everlasting worshipping.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 17:14; DEUTERONOMY 16:3; EXODUS 23:15"> Seven days ye shall eat therf bread; in the first day nothing dighted with sourdough shall be in your houses; whoever shall eat anything dighted with sourdough, from the first day till to the seventh day, that soul shall perish from Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:7–8; NUMBERS 28:18; EXODUS 16:23"> The first day shall be holy and solemn, and the seventh day shall be worshipful by the same hallowing; ye shall not do any work in those days, except these things that pertain to meat;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 7:5; EXODUS 13:3; EXODUS 12:41"> and ye shall keep therf bread. For in that same day I shall lead out of the land of Egypt your host; and ye shall keep this day in your generations by everlasting custom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:5–8; EXODUS 12:15; EXODUS 12:1–2"> In the first month, in the fourteenth day of the month, at eventide, ye shall eat therf bread, till to the one and twentieth day of the same month at eventide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 12:15; EXODUS 23:15; DEUTERONOMY 16:3"> In seven days nothing dighted with sourdough shall be found in your houses; if any eateth anything dighted with sourdough, his soul shall perish from the company of Israel, as well of comelings, that be heathen men converted to the faith of Jews, as of them that be born in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Ye shall not eat anything made with sourdough, and ye shall eat therf bread in all your dwelling places.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 12:3; LUKE 22:7–13; 2ND CHRONICLES 35:5–6"> Forsooth Moses called all the elder men of the sons of Israel, and said to them, Go ye, and take ye a beast by your meines, and offer ye pask;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:28; NUMBERS 19:18; PSALMS 51:7"> and dip ye a bundle of hyssop, in the blood which is in the threshold, either in a vessel beside the threshold, and sprinkle ye thereof on the lintel, and ever either doorpost; none of you shall go out at the door of his house till the morrowtide.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 7:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:4; HEBREWS 11:28"> For the Lord shall pass &#91;forth&#93; smiting the Egyptians; and when he shall see the blood in the lintel, and in ever either doorpost, he shall pass over the door of the house; and he shall not suffer the smiter to enter into your houses, and to hurt you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 12:14; GENESIS 17:8–10"> Keep thou this word; it shall be a lawful thing to thee and to thy sons till into without end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 3:17; EXODUS 3:8; DEUTERONOMY 12:8–9"> And when ye shall enter into the land which the Lord shall give to you, as he promised, ye shall keep these ceremonies;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 13:14–15; DEUTERONOMY 32:7; PSALMS 145:4"> and when your sons shall say to you, What is this religion?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 4:31; EXODUS 12:11; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:7"> ye shall say to them, It is the sacrifice of the passing of the Lord, when he passed over the houses of the sons of Israel in Egypt, and smote the Egyptians, and delivered our houses. And the people was bowed, and worshipped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:28"> And the sons of Israel went out, and did as the Lord commanded to Moses and Aaron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 78:51; EXODUS 4:23; PSALMS 105:36"> Forsooth it was done in the midst of the night, the Lord smote all the first begotten things in the land of Egypt, from the first begotten of Pharaoh, that sat in the throne of him, till to the first begotten of a captive woman, that was in prison, and all the first engendered of beasts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 11:6; AMOS 5:17; PROVERBS 21:13"> And Pharaoh rose in the night, and all his servants, and all Egypt; and a great cry was made in Egypt; for none house was, in which a dead man lay not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 6:1; EXODUS 10:9; EXODUS 3:19–20"> And when Moses and Aaron were called in the night, Pharaoh said, Rise ye, and go ye out from my people, both ye and the sons of Israel; go ye, offer ye to the Lord, as ye say;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 10:26; GENESIS 27:34; GENESIS 27:38"> take ye your sheep and &#91;your&#93; great beasts, as ye asked; and go ye, and bless ye me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 105:38; EXODUS 11:1; GENESIS 20:3"> And the Egyptians constrained the people to go out of the land swiftly, and said, All we shall die!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 8:3"> Therefore the people took meal sprinkled together, before that it was dighted with sourdough; and they bound it in mantles, and put it on their shoulders.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="EXODUS 3:21–22; EXODUS 11:2–3; GENESIS 15:14"> And the sons of Israel did as the Lord commanded to Moses; and they asked of the Egyptians silver vessels and golden, and full much clothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GENESIS 15:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:10; EXODUS 3:21–22"> Forsooth the Lord gave grace to the people before the Egyptians, that the Egyptians lent to them; and they made bare the Egyptians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:46; EXODUS 38:26; NUMBERS 33:3"> And the children of Israel went forth from Rameses into Succoth, almost six hundred thousand of footmen, without little children and women;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:4; ZECHARIAH 8:23"> but also the common people of males and of females unnumberable went up with them; sheep, and oxen, and full many beasts of diverse kind also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 11:1; EXODUS 6:1; EXODUS 12:31–33"> And they baked meal, which sprinkled altogether a while ago they took from Egypt, and made therf loaves baken under ashes; for the loaves might not be dighted with sourdough, for &#91;the&#93; Egyptians compelled them to go out, and suffered not them to make any tarrying, neither it was leisure to make any stew.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GENESIS 15:13; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:17"> Forsooth the dwelling of the sons of Israel, by which they dwelled in Egypt, was of four hundred and thirty years;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:3; JOHN 7:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:7"> and when those years were fulfilled, all the host of the Lord went out of the land of Egypt in the same day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:1–6; EXODUS 12:14; EXODUS 13:10"> This night is worthy to be kept in the worshipping of the Lord, when he led them out of the land of Egypt; all the sons of Israel ought to keep this night in their generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="EXODUS 12:48; NUMBERS 9:14; EXODUS 12:11"> Also the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, This is the religion of pask; each alien shall not eat thereof;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GENESIS 17:12–13; GENESIS 17:23"> soothly each servant bought shall be circumcised, and so he shall eat;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:10; EPHESIANS 2:12"> a comeling and a hired man shall not eat thereof;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:12; JOHN 19:36; JOHN 19:33"> it shall be eaten in one house; neither ye shall bear out the flesh thereof; neither ye shall break a bone thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="EXODUS 12:6; NUMBERS 9:13; EXODUS 12:3"> Each company of the sons of Israel shall make that pask;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:14; COLOSSIANS 3:11; GALATIANS 3:28"> that if any pilgrim will pass into your faith and worshipping, and make &#91;the&#93; pask of the Lord, each male kind of him shall be circumcised before the solemnity, and then he shall make it lawfully, and he shall be together with them as a man born of the land; forsooth if any man is not circum-cised, he shall not eat thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:15–16; NUMBERS 15:29; LEVITICUS 24:22"> The same law shall be to a man born of the land, and to a comeling, that taketh your faith, the which is a pilgrim with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:24–25; EXODUS 6:26; EXODUS 7:4"> And all the sons of Israel did as the Lord commanded to Moses and Aaron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="EXODUS 12:41; EXODUS 6:26"> And in the same day the Lord led out of the land of Egypt the sons of Israel, by their companies.</span>
</div>
