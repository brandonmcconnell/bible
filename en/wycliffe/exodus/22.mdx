<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 19:8; 2ND SAMUEL 12:6; PROVERBS 6:31"> If any man stealeth a sheep, or ox, and slayeth, or selleth, he shall restore five oxen for one ox, and four sheep for one sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:43; MATTHEW 6:19–20; NUMBERS 35:27"> And if a night thief breaking into an house, either undermining, is found, and he taken is dead by a wound, or hurt, the smiter shall not be guilty of his blood, or death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 21:2; ISAIAH 50:1; MATTHEW 18:25"> that if he did this when the sun was risen, he did manslaying, and he shall die. If a thief have not that, that he shall yield for &#91;the&#93; theft, he shall be sold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 22:7; EXODUS 22:1; EXODUS 22:9"> if that thing that he stole, is found quick at him, either ox, either ass, either sheep, he shall restore the double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 22:3; EXODUS 22:12; JOB 20:18"> If a man harmeth a field, or a vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;, and suffereth his beast, that it waste other men’s things, he shall restore for the value of &#91;the&#93; harm, the best things what-ever he hath in his field, either in his vinery &#91;or vineyard&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 22:9; EXODUS 21:33–34; 2ND SAMUEL 14:30–31"> If fire goeth out, and findeth, or burneth, ears of corn, and catcheth heaps of corn, or corns standing in fields, he that kindled the fire shall yield the harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 22:4; PROVERBS 6:30–31; JEREMIAH 2:26"> If a man betaketh into keeping money to a friend, or a vessel, and it is taken away by theft from him that received it, if the thief is found, he shall restore the double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 22:28; DEUTERONOMY 16:18; EXODUS 21:6"> If the thief is hid, or unknown, the lord of the house that received that good shall be brought to the gods, that is, to judges, and he shall swear, that he held not forth his hand into his neighbour’s thing, to defraud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:1; EXODUS 22:4; NUMBERS 5:6–7"> as well in ox, as in ass, and in sheep, and in clothes; and in what-ever thing may bring in harm, the cause of ever either shall come to the judges, and if they deem him guilty, he shall restore the double to his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 12:48; 2ND TIMOTHY 1:12; LUKE 16:11"> If any man betaketh to his neighbour ox, ass, sheep, and all work beast to keeping, and it is dead, or is made feeble, or is taken of enemies, and no man seeth this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:16; LEVITICUS 5:1; 1ST KINGS 2:42–43"> an oath shall be in the midst, that he held not forth his hand to the impairing of his neighbour’s thing; and the lord that owned that good shall receive his oath, and he to whom it was taken shall not be compelled to yield, or restore it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 31:39; EXODUS 22:7"> That if it is taken away by theft, he shall restore the harm to the lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NAHUM 2:12; MICAH 5:8; EZEKIEL 4:14"> if it is eaten of a beast, he shall bring to the lord that that is slain, and he shall not restore it otherwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20; EXODUS 21:34; DEUTERONOMY 15:2"> He that asketh of his neighbour anything of these foresaid things by borrowing, and it is enfeebled, either dead, while the lord thereof is not present, he shall be constrained to yield it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:10"> that if the lord is in presence, he shall not restore it, mostly if it came hired, that is, if to hire he took it, for meed of his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:28–29; GENESIS 34:2–4"> If a man deceiveth a virgin not yet wedded, and sleepeth with her, he shall give dower to her, and shall have her to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 34:12; 1ST SAMUEL 18:25; DEUTERONOMY 22:29"> If the father of the virgin will not give her to him, he shall give money, by the manner of dower, which virgins were wont to take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:27; DEUTERONOMY 18:10–11; 1ST SAMUEL 28:3"> Thou shalt not suffer witches to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:23; DEUTERONOMY 27:21; LEVITICUS 20:15–16"> He that doeth lechery with a beast, die he by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:2–5; NUMBERS 25:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 18:20"> He that offereth to gods, except to the Lord alone, be he slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 23:9; DEUTERONOMY 10:19; LEVITICUS 19:33"> Thou shalt not make sorrowful a comeling, neither thou shalt torment him; for also ye were comelings in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:17; ISAIAH 1:17; ISAIAH 1:23"> Ye shall not harm a widow, and a fatherless or a motherless child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 18:6; PSALMS 10:17–18; LUKE 18:7"> If ye hurt them, they shall cry to me, and I shall hear the cry of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 109:9; JEREMIAH 15:8; PSALMS 76:7"> and my great vengeance shall have indignation on you, and I shall smite you with sword, and your wives shall be widows, and your sons shall be fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 15:5; LEVITICUS 25:35–37; DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20"> If thou givest money to loan to my poor people, that dwelleth with thee, thou shalt not constrain him as an extortioner doeth, neither thou shalt oppress him by usuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:16; AMOS 2:8; DEUTERONOMY 24:6"> If thou takest of thy neighbour a cloth to wed or a cloak for a pledge, thou shalt yield it to him before the going down of the sun;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 34:6; 2ND CHRONICLES 30:9; PSALMS 136:10–11"> for that alone is the clothing of his flesh, with which he is covered, neither he hath another, in which he shall sleep; if he crieth to me, I shall hear him; for I am merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:5; ECCLESIASTES 10:20; EXODUS 22:8–9"> Thou shalt not backbite &#91;the&#93; gods, that is, priests, or judges, and thou shalt not curse the prince of thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 23:16; EXODUS 13:2; JAMES 1:18"> Thou shalt not tarry to offer to the Lord thy tithes, and thy first fruits. Thou shalt give to me the first begotten of thy sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:27; DEUTERONOMY 15:19; EXODUS 13:12"> also of &#91;thine&#93; oxen, and of sheep, thou shalt do in like manner; seven days be he with his mother, in the eighth day thou shalt yield him to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:14; LEVITICUS 19:2; DEUTERONOMY 14:21"> Ye shall be holy men to me; ye shall not eat the flesh that is before-tasted of beasts, but ye shall cast it forth to hounds.</span>
