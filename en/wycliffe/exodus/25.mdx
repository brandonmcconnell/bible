<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:8; GENESIS 41:42; EZEKIEL 16:10"> and jacinth, and purple, and red silk twice-dyed, and bis, that is, white silk, &#91;and&#93; hairs of goats,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 26:37–1; EXODUS 26:14–15; EXODUS 36:20"> and skins of wethers made red, and skins of jacinth, and wood of shittim,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 27:20; EXODUS 25:37; EXODUS 40:24–25"> and oil to lights to be ordained, sweet smelling spiceries into ointment, and incense of good odour,</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 28:4; EXODUS 28:6–30"> onyx stones, and gems to adorn ephod, that is, a chasuble, and the rational, that is, an ouch hanging on the priest’s breast, in which was written doom and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 6:16; 1ST KINGS 6:13; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:3"> And they shall make a saintuary to me, and I shall dwell in the midst of them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 25:40; HEBREWS 8:5; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:44"> by all the likeness of the taber-nacle that I shall show to thee, and of all the vessels of &#91;the&#93; adorning thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:4; EXODUS 37:1–3; DEUTERONOMY 10:1–3"> And thus ye shall make it; join together an ark, or a coffer, of the wood of shittim, whose length shall have two cubits and an half, the breadth shall have one cubit and an half, the height in like manner one cubit and an half.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 25:24; EXODUS 30:3; 2ND CHRONICLES 3:4"> And thou shalt overgild it with cleanest gold within and without; and thou shalt make a golden crown above by compass,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 27:7; EXODUS 25:26; EXODUS 25:15"> and four golden rings, which thou shalt set by &#91;the&#93; four corners of the ark; two rings be in &#91;the&#93; one side, and two rings in the other side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 15:15; NUMBERS 4:11; EXODUS 27:6"> Also thou shalt make bars of the wood of shittim, and thou shalt cover them with gold,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and thou shalt bring in by the rings that be in the sides of the ark, that it be borne in them,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1ST KINGS 8:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 5:9"> the which bars shall ever&#91;more&#93; be in the rings, neither they shall any time be drawn out of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:26; 1ST KINGS 8:9; HEBREWS 9:4"> And thou shalt put into the ark the witnessing, that is, law, which I shall give to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:5; EXODUS 37:6; HEBREWS 4:16"> And thou shalt make a propiti-atory<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Also known as ‘God’s answering place’ and ‘the mercy seat’.">a</a></sup>of cleanest gold; that is, a table covering the ark, &#91;or a place of purchasing mercy&#93;; the length thereof shall hold two cubits and an half, &#91;and&#93; the breadth shall hold one cubit and an half.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:20; HEBREWS 9:5; 1ST KINGS 8:6–7"> Also thou shalt make on ever either side of God’s answering place, two cherubims &#91;or cherubim&#93; of gold, and beaten out with an hammer;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> one cherub be on one side of God’s answering place, and the tother in the tother side; cover they ever either side of the propitiatory,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:18; 1ST KINGS 8:7; HEBREWS 9:5"> and hold they forth their wings, and cover they God’s answering place; and behold they themselves together, while their faces be turned in to the propitiatory,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 26:34; ROMANS 10:4; EXODUS 25:16–17"> with which the ark of the Lord shall be covered, in which ark thou shalt put the witnessing &#91;or the testimony&#93;, that is, the tables of the law, that I shall give to thee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 29:42–43; NUMBERS 7:89; ISAIAH 37:16"> From thence I shall command, and I shall speak to thee above the propitiatory, that is, from the midst of &#91;the&#93; two cherubims &#91;or cherubim&#93;, that shall be on the ark of witnessing, all things which I shall command by thee to the sons of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:2; EXODUS 37:10–16; 1ST KINGS 7:48"> Also thou shalt make a board of the wood of shittim, having two cubits of length, and one cubit of broadness, and one cubit and an half in height.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 25:11; 1ST KINGS 6:20–22"> And thou shalt overgild the board with most pure gold, and thou shalt make to it a golden brink about;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 37:2; EXODUS 30:3"> and thou shalt make to that brink a crown raised betwixt four fingers high, and thou shalt make on that another little golden crown.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou shalt make ready four golden rings, and thou shalt put them in &#91;the&#93; four corners of the same board, by all the feet thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 25:14; EXODUS 25:28"> Under the crown shall be golden rings, that the bars be put through them, and so the table may be borne.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 25:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:15; NUMBERS 10:17"> Thou shalt make the bars of the wood of shittim, and thou shalt com-pass them with gold to bear the board.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:7; EXODUS 37:16; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:20"> And thou shalt make ready vessels of vinegar, and vials, and censers, and cups of purest gold, in which flowing sacrifices shall be offered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:5–9; MATTHEW 12:4; EXODUS 39:36"> And thou shalt set on the board loaves of proposition, or of setting forth, in my sight ever&#91;more&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1ST KINGS 7:49; ZECHARIAH 4:2; EXODUS 37:17–24"> And thou shalt make a candle-stick beaten out with an hammer, of cleanest gold, &#91;and&#93; thou shalt make the shaft thereof, and &#91;the&#93; rods, and cups, and little roundels &#91;or balls&#93;, and lilies coming forth thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Six rods shall go out of the sides of it, three of the one side, and three of the other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 17:4–8; JEREMIAH 1:11–12; ZECHARIAH 4:3"> Three cups as in the manner of a nut by each rod, and &#91;the&#93; little roundels &#91;or balls&#93; together, and a lily, and in like manner three cups at the likeness of a nut in the tother rod, and little roundels together, and a lily; this shall be the work of six rods, that shall be brought forth &#91;out&#93; of the candlestick shaft.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Forsooth in that candlestick shall be four cups in the manner of a nut, and little roundels &#91;or balls&#93; and lilies by each cup;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and the little roundels &#91;or balls&#93; shall be under two rods by three places, the which rods altogether be made six, coming forth of one shaft;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 25:18; 1ST KINGS 10:16–17; 2ND CHRONICLES 9:15"> and therefore the little roundels and the rods thereof shall be all beaten out with an hammer, of cleanest gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 8:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:20–1; LEVITICUS 24:2–4"> And thou shalt make seven lanterns, and thou shalt set them on the candlestick, that they shine even against each other.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2ND KI 12:13; JEREMIAH 52:18; 2ND KI 25:14"> Also tongs to snuff the candles, and the vessels where those snuffs, that be snuffed out, be quenched, be made of cleanest gold.</span>
</div>
