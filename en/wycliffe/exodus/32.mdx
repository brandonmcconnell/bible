<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:40; DEUTERONOMY 9:9; EXODUS 24:18"> Forsooth the people saw, that Moses made tarrying to come down from the hill, and it was gathered together against Aaron, and said, Rise thou, and make gods to us, that shall go before us, for we wot not what is befallen to this man Moses, that led us out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 8:24–27; EXODUS 12:35–36; EZEKIEL 16:17"> And Aaron said to them, Take ye the golden earrings from the ears of your wives, of your sons, and of your daughters, and bring ye them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ISAIAH 46:6; ISAIAH 40:19–20; JEREMIAH 10:9"> The people did those things, that he commanded, and brought the earrings to Aaron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:18; DEUTERONOMY 9:16; 1ST KINGS 12:28"> and when he had taken those, he formed them by work of melting, and he made of them a molten calf. And they said, Israel, these be thy gods, that led thee out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND KI 10:20; LEVITICUS 23:2; LEVITICUS 23:37"> And when Aaron had seen this thing, he builded an altar before the calf, and he cried by the voice of a crier, and said, Tomorrow is the solemnity of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 10:7; NUMBERS 25:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:41–42"> And they rose early, and offered burnt sacrifices, and peaceable sacri-fices; and the people sat to eat and to drink, and they rose up to play, or to scorn, for idolatry is scorning of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 32:11; HOSEA 9:9; JUDGES 2:19"> And the Lord spake to Moses, and said, Go thou, go down, thy people hath sinned, whom thou leddest out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST KINGS 12:28; EXODUS 20:23; EXODUS 20:3–4"> They have gone away soon from the way that thou showedest them, and they have made to them a molten calf, and have worshipped it, and they have offered sacrifices to it, and said, Israel, these be thy gods, that led thee out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; DEUTERONOMY 9:13; ISAIAH 48:4"> And again the Lord said to Moses, I see &#91;well&#93;, that this people is of hard noll;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:14; NUMBERS 14:12; DEUTERONOMY 9:19"> suffer thou me, that my strong vengeance be wroth against them, and that I do away them; and I shall make thee into a great folk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:18–20; DEUTERONOMY 9:26–29; PSALMS 106:23"> Forsooth Moses prayed the Lord his God, and said, Lord, why is thy vengeance wroth against thy people, whom thou hast led out of the land of Egypt in great strength, and in a strong hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:13–16; DEUTERONOMY 9:28; JOSHUA 7:9"> I beseech, that &#91;the&#93; Egyptians say not, He led them out fellily or slyly, to slay in the hills, and to do them away from &#91;the&#93; earth; thine ire cease, and be thou quemeful on the wicked-ness of thy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:13; GENESIS 12:7; GENESIS 22:16"> Have thou mind of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Israel, thy servants, to which thou hast sworn by thyself, and saidest, I shall multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and I shall give to your seed all the land of which I spake, and ye shall wield it ever&#91;more&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JONAH 3:10; PSALMS 106:45; JEREMIAH 26:13"> And the Lord was pleased with Moses’ words, that he did not the evil which he spake against his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:15; EXODUS 40:20; DEUTERONOMY 5:22"> And Moses turned again from the hill, and bare in his hands two tables of witnessing, written in either side,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 31:18; HEBREWS 8:10; DEUTERONOMY 10:1"> and made by the work of God; and the writing of God was engraved in the tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 24:13; EXODUS 17:9; JOSHUA 6:20"> Forsooth Joshua heard the noise of the people crying &#91;out&#93;, and he said to Moses, Yelling of fighting is heard in the tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 15:1–18; DANIEL 5:4; DANIEL 5:23"> To whom Moses answered, It is not a cry of men exciting to battle, neither the cry of men compelled to fleeing, but I hear the voices of singers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:16–17; JEREMIAH 31:32; LAMENTATIONS 5:15"> And when Moses had nighed to the tents, he saw the calf, and dances; and he was wroth greatly, and he threw out of his hands the tables, and he brake them at the roots of the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:21; 2ND KI 23:6; 2ND KI 23:15"> And he took the calf, which they had made, and he burnt it, and brake it till to powder, which he sprinkled into the water, and gave thereof drink to the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 20:9; JOSHUA 7:19–26; 1ST KINGS 21:22"> And Moses said to Aaron, What did this people to thee, that thou hast brought in on them the greatest sin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:24; EXODUS 15:24; EXODUS 16:20"> To whom he answered, My lord, be not thou wroth, for thou knowest this people, that it is inclined, either ready, to evil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 32:1–4; EXODUS 32:8"> they said to me, Make thou gods to us, that shall go before us, for we wot not, what hath befallen to this Moses, that led us out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 32:4; LUKE 10:29; ROMANS 3:10"> To whom I said, Who of you hath gold? They took, and gave to me, and I casted it forth into the fire, and this calf went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 47:3; MICAH 1:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:15"> Therefore Moses saw the people, that it was made bare; for Aaron had spoiled it for the shame of the filthhood of making of the idol, and he had made the people naked among enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2ND KI 9:32; MATTHEW 12:30; 2ND SAMUEL 20:11"> And Moses stood in the gate of the tents, and said, If any man is of the Lord, be he joined to me; and all the sons of Levi were gathered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:5; LUKE 14:26; DEUTERONOMY 33:8–9"> To which he said, The Lord God of Israel saith these things, A man put his sword upon his hip, go ye, and turn ye again from gate unto gate by the middle of the tents, and each man slay his brother, his friend, and &#91;his&#93; neighbour, which consented to this idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:9; MALACHI 2:4–6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 10:8"> And the sons of Levi did by the word of Moses, and as three thousand of men felled down in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:11–13; MATTHEW 10:37; ZECHARIAH 13:3"> And Moses said, Ye have hallowed your hands today to the Lord, each man in his son, and &#91;his&#93; brother, that blessing be given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 12:20; NUMBERS 25:13; 2ND KI 17:21"> Soothly when the tother day was made, Moses spake to the people, Ye have sinned the most sin; I shall go up to the Lord, if in any manner I shall be able to beseech him for your felony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 20:23; DANIEL 9:11; DANIEL 9:8"> And he turned again to the Lord, and said, Lord, I beseech thee, this people hath sinned a great sin, and they have made golden gods to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 69:28; ROMANS 9:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:27"> either forgive thou this guilt to them, either if thou doest not, do away me from thy book, which thou hast written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:20; EZEKIEL 18:4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:12"> To whom the Lord answered, I shall do away from my book him that sinneth against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 23:20; DEUTERONOMY 32:35; MATTHEW 23:35"> forsooth go thou, and lead this people, whither I spake to thee; mine angel shall go before thee; forsooth in the day of vengeance I shall visit also this sin of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:41; EXODUS 32:25; 2ND SAMUEL 12:9–10"> Therefore the Lord smote the people, for the guilt of the calf, which Aaron made.</span>
