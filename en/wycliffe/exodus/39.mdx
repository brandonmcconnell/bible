<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 28:18; EZEKIEL 28:13"> in the second order was car-buncle, sapphire, jasper;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> in the third order was ligure, agate, amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> in the fourth order was crystallite, onyx, and beryl; compassed and enclosed with gold, by their orders.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 28:14; SONG OF SONGS 1:10"> on which rings &#91;the&#93; two golden chains hanged, which they setted in the hooks, that stood forth in the corners of the cloth on the shoulders &#91;or cape&#93;.</span>
