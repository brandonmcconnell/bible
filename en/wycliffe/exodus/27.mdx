<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 25:28; EXODUS 30:4; NUMBERS 4:13–14"> and thou shalt lead in the bars by the rings, and they shall be on ever either side of the altar, to bear.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 25:40; EXODUS 25:9; HEBREWS 8:5"> Thou shalt make that altar not massive or solid, but void, and hollow within, as it was showed to thee in the hill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 38:9–20; EZEKIEL 40:32; EZEKIEL 42:3"> Also thou shalt make a large porch of the tabernacle, in the manner of a churchyard, in whose midday coast against the south shall be curtains of bis folded again; one side shall hold an hundred cubits in length,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 26:19–21; JEREMIAH 52:21; EXODUS 36:38"> and twenty pillars, with so many brazen bases<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In the original text, throughout the rest of Exodus, ‘foundament/s’ and ‘base/s’ are used interchangeably in reference to the pillars. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In like manner in the north side, by the length, shall be curtains of an hundred cubits, twenty pillars, and brazen bases of the same number; and the hooks of the pillars, and the holdings of those, shall be of silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Forsooth in the breadth of the large porch, that beholdeth to the west, shall be curtains by fifty cubits, and ten pillars shall be, and as many bases.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In that breadth of the large porch, that beholdeth to the east, shall be fifty cubits,</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and in the other side shall be curtains holding fifteen cubits, and three pillars, and so many bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 26:36; JUDGES 5:30; EXODUS 28:39"> Forsooth in the large entry of the porch shall be made a curtain of twenty cubits, of jacinth, and purple, and of red silk twice-dyed, and of bis folded again, by embroidery work; it shall have four pillars, with so many bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars of the great porch by compass shall be clothed with plates of silver, with hooks of silver, and with bases of brass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The great porch shall occupy an hundred cubits in length, fifty in breadth; the height of the curtains shall be of five cubits; and it shall be made of bis folded again; and it shall have brazen bases.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 35:18; ISAIAH 22:23–25; NUMBERS 4:32"> Thou shalt make of brass all the vessels of the tabernacle, into all uses and ceremonies, as well the stakes thereof, as of the great entry.</span>
</div>

</div>
