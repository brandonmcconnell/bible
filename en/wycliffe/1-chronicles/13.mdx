<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 12:20; 2ND CHRONICLES 34:29–30; 2ND CHRONICLES 29:20"> Forsooth David took counsel with &#91;the&#93; tribunes, and centurions, and all &#91;the&#93; princes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:4–20; 1ST SAMUEL 31:1; 1ST CHRONICLES 10:7"> and he said to all the company of the sons of Israel, If it pleaseth you, and if the word that I speak goeth out from the Lord our God, send we to the remnant of our brethren to all the countries of Israel, and to &#91;the&#93; priests and deacons &#91;or Levites&#93; that dwell in the suburbs of cities, that they be gathered to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 7:1–2; 1ST SAMUEL 23:2; 1ST SAMUEL 14:18"> and that we bring again to us the ark of our God; for we sought not at it in the days of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ESTHER 8:5; 2ND SAMUEL 3:36; 1ST SAMUEL 18:20"> And all the multitude answered, that it should be done so; for the word pleased all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 6:21–1; 1ST KINGS 8:65; 2ND SAMUEL 6:1"> Therefore David gathered together all Israel, from Shihor of Egypt till that thou enter into Hamath, that he should bring the ark of God from Kiriathjearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 25:22; JOSHUA 15:9; 2ND KI 19:15"> And David went up, and all the men of Israel, to the hill of Kiriathjearim, which is in Judah, that he should bring from thence the ark of the Lord God sitting on cherubim, where his name was inwardly called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:15; 1ST SAMUEL 6:7; 1ST CHRONICLES 15:2"> And they putted &#91;or put&#93; the ark of the Lord God on a new wain from the house of Abinadab; and Uzza and his brethren drove the wain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 150:3–5; PSALMS 68:25–27; AMOS 6:5"> And David and all Israel played before the Lord, with all might, in songs, and in harps, and psalteries, and in tympans, and in cymbals, and trumps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 6:6"> And when they had come to the cornfloor of Chidon, Uzza stretched forth his hand to sustain, or stable, the ark; for the oxes waxing wild had bowed it &#91;down&#93; a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 15:15; 1ST CHRONICLES 15:13; NUMBERS 4:15"> Therefore the Lord was wroth against Uzza, and smote him, for he had touched the ark; and he was dead there before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 6:7; 2ND SAMUEL 6:9; GENESIS 32:32"> And David was sorry, for the Lord had parted, or slain, Uzza; and he called that place The Parting of Uzza, as it is still called unto this present day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 119:120; 1ST KINGS 8:27; JOB 25:5–6"> And David dreaded the Lord in that time, and said, How may I bring into me the ark of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 26:4; 1ST CHRONICLES 15:18; 2ND SAMUEL 4:3"> And for this cause he brought not it to him, that is, into the city of David, but he turned it into the house of Obededom of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 26:4–5; PROVERBS 10:22; PROVERBS 3:9–10"> Therefore the ark of God dwelled in the house of Obededom of Gath three months; and the Lord blessed his house, and all things that he had.</span>
