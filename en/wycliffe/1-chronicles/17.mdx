<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 6:7–9; 1ST CHRONICLES 15:1; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:5"> Forsooth when David dwelled in his house, he said to Nathan, the prophet, Lo! I dwell in an house of cedars; and the ark of &#91;the&#93; bond of peace of the Lord is under skins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 13:9; ZECHARIAH 8:23"> And Nathan said to David, Do thou all things that be in thine heart, for God is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; NUMBERS 12:6; ISAIAH 30:21"> Therefore in that night, the word of the Lord was made to Nathan, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 28:2–3; ISAIAH 55:8–9; 2ND CHRONICLES 6:8–9"> Go thou, and speak to David, my servant, The Lord saith these things, Thou shalt not build to me an house to dwell in;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:6; EXODUS 40:2–3; 1ST KINGS 8:16"> certainly I have not dwelled in an house, from that time in which I led Israel out of the land of Egypt till to this day, but ever&#91;more&#93; I have changed places of the tabernacle, and have dwelled in a tent with all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:7; NUMBERS 10:33–36; 2ND CORINTHIANS 6:16"> Whether I have spoken namely to one of the judges of Israel, to which I commanded that they should feed my people, and said, Why hast thou not builded to me an house of cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 6:21; EXODUS 3:1–10; 1ST SAMUEL 17:15"> Now therefore thou shalt speak thus to my servant David, The Lord of hosts saith these things, I took thee, when thou pursuedest &#91;or followedest&#93; the flock in the pastures, that thou shouldest be duke upon my people Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 18:14; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:2; PSALMS 113:7–8"> and I was with thee whither ever thou wentest, and I killed all thine enemies before thee, and I made to thee a name, as of one of the great men that be made worshipful, either famous, in &#91;the&#93; earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 2:23; EZEKIEL 28:4; EPHESIANS 5:6"> And I gave a place to my people Israel; it shall be planted, and shall dwell therein, and it shall no more be moved, and the sons of wickedness shall not defoul them, as from the beginning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:11; JUDGES 2:14–18; EXODUS 1:21"> from the days in which I gave judges to my people Israel; and I made low all thine enemies. There-fore I tell to thee, that the Lord shall build an house to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11; 1ST KINGS 1:21; 1ST CHRONICLES 28:5"> And when thou hast fulfilled thy days, that thou go to thy fathers, I shall raise up thy seed after thee, that shall be of thy sons, and I shall stablish his realm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST KINGS 5:5; DANIEL 2:44; ISAIAH 9:7"> he shall build to me an house, and I shall make steadfast his seat into without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:5; ISAIAH 55:3; 1ST CHRONICLES 17:12"> I shall be to him into a father, and he shall be to me into a son; and I shall not do away my mercy from him, as I took it away from him that was before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 89:36; LUKE 1:32–33; PSALMS 72:17"> and I shall ordain him in mine house and in my realm into without end; and his throne shall be most steadfast without end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:27; 2ND SAMUEL 7:17"> By all these words, and by all this revelation, so Nathan spake to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2ND SAMUEL 7:18; GENESIS 32:10; EPHESIANS 3:8"> And when king David had come, and had set before the Lord, he said, Lord God, who am I, and what is mine house, that thou shouldest give such things to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST CHRONICLES 17:11–15; PHILIPPIANS 2:8–11; ISAIAH 49:6"> But also this is seen little in thy sight, and therefore thou hast spoken of the house of thy servant, yea, into time to coming &#91;or to come&#93;; and thou hast made me worthy to be beholden over all men. My Lord God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 21:17; 1ST SAMUEL 2:30; 2ND SAMUEL 7:20–24"> what may David add more, since thou hast so glorified thy servant, and hast known him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ISAIAH 37:35; 2ND SAMUEL 7:21; DANIEL 9:17"> Lord, for thy servant, thou hast done by thine heart all this great doing, and thou wouldest that all great things be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ISAIAH 44:6; JEREMIAH 10:6–7; EPHESIANS 3:20"> Lord, none is like thee, and none other God is without thee, of all which we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:9–10; ISAIAH 63:9; ISAIAH 48:9"> For who is another as thy people Israel, one folk in &#91;the&#93; earth, to whom God went, to deliver and make a people to himself, and to cast out by his greatness and dreads nations from the face thereof, the which people he delivered from Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5–6; 1ST PETER 2:9; ROMANS 11:1–12"> And thou hast set thy people Israel into a people to thee into without end, and thou, Lord, art made the God thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:49; JEREMIAH 11:5; LUKE 1:38"> Now therefore, Lord, the word which thou hast spoken to thy servant, and on his house, be it confirmed with-out end, and do, as thou hast spoken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:13; JEREMIAH 31:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:3"> and thy name dwell, and be magnified without end; and be it said, The Lord of hosts is God of Israel, and the house of David, his servant, dwelling before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 9:15; EZEKIEL 36:37; PSALMS 10:17"> For thou, my Lord God, hast made revelation in the ear of thy servant, that thou wouldest build to him an house; and therefore thy servant hath found trust, that he pray before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 34:6–7; HEBREWS 6:18; TITUS 1:2"> Now therefore, Lord, thou art God, and hast spoken to thy servant so great benificences &#91;or benefits&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:3; ROMANS 11:29; PSALMS 72:17"> and thou hast begun to bless the house of thy servant, that it be ever-&#91;more&#93; before thee; for, Lord, for thou blessest, it shall be blessed without end.</span>
