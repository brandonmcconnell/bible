<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 12:41–44; JOHN 8:20; MATTHEW 27:6"> And he beheld, and saw the rich men, that cast their gifts into the treasury;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARK 12:42"> but he saw also a little poor widow casting two farthings &#91;or two little moneys&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 8:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:34; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:2–3"> And he said, Truly I say to you, that this poor widow cast more than all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:44–45; LUKE 15:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:34"> For why all these of &#91;the&#93; thing that was plenteous &#91;or abundant&#93; to them cast in to the gifts of God; but this widow of that thing that failed to her, cast all her livelode &#91;or all her lifelode&#93;, that she had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 2:20; MATTHEW 24:1–51; MARK 13:1–37"> And when some men said of the temple, that it was appareled &#91;or adorned&#93; with good stones and gifts, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:2; MARK 13:2; LAMENTATIONS 5:18"> These things that ye see, days shall come, in which a stone shall not be left on a stone, which shall not be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:6–7; MARK 13:14; MARK 13:3–4"> And they asked him, and said, Commander, when shall these things be? and what token shall be, when they shall begin to be done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1ST JOHN 4:1; MARK 13:5–6; EPHESIANS 5:6"> And he said, See ye, that ye be not deceived; for many shall come in my name, saying, For I am, and the time shall nigh; therefore do not ye go after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 21:28; MATTHEW 24:6–8; MARK 13:7–8"> And when ye shall hear battles and strives &#91;or dissensions&#93; within, do not ye be afeared; it behooveth first these things to be done, but not yet anon is an end &#91;or the end&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:19–20; MARK 13:8; 2ND CHRONICLES 15:5–6"> Then he said to them, Folk shall rise against folk, and realm against realm;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:29–30; LUKE 21:25–27"> and great movings of the earth shall be by places, and pestilences, and hungers, and dreads from heaven, and great tokens shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:12–14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:40; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:30"> But before all these things they shall set their hands on you, and shall pursue you, betaking into synagogues and keepings, &#91;either prisons&#93;, and drawing you to kings and to justices, for my name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:12; PHILIPPIANS 1:28; 2ND THESSALONIANS 1:5"> but it shall fall to you into witnessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 12:11–12; MARK 13:11; MATTHEW 10:19–20"> Therefore put ye in your hearts, not to think before, how ye shall answer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:9; EPHESIANS 6:19; LUKE 12:12"> for I shall give to you mouth and wisdom, to which all your adversaries shall not be able to against-stand, and gainsay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 12:53; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:9; MICAH 7:5–6"> And ye shall be taken &#91;or betrayed&#93; of father, and mother, and brethren, and cousins, and friends, and by death they shall torment &#91;some&#93; of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 15:19; LUKE 6:22; JOHN 15:21"> and ye shall be in hate to &#91;or be hated of&#93; all men for my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:30; 1ST SAMUEL 14:45; LUKE 12:7"> And an hair of your head shall not perish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:36; MATTHEW 10:22; JAMES 1:3"> in your patience ye shall wield your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 19:43; DANIEL 9:27; MARK 13:14"> But when ye shall see Jerusalem be environed with an host &#91;of battle&#93;, then know ye, that the desolation of it shall nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:1; GENESIS 19:17; LUKE 17:31–33"> Then they that be in Judea, flee to the mountains; and they that be in the middle of it, go away; and they that be in the countries, enter not into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSEA 9:7; ISAIAH 65:12–16; ISAIAH 63:4"> For these be the days of vengeance, that all things that be written, be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARK 13:17; MATTHEW 24:19; LUKE 23:29"> And woe to them, that be with child, and nourishing &#91;or nursing&#93; in those days; for a great dis-ease &#91;or pressure&#93; shall be on the earth, and wrath to this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 11:25; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 11:2; ISAIAH 63:18"> And they shall fall by the sharpness of sword, and they shall be led prisoners &#91;or led captive&#93; into all folks; and Jerusalem shall be defouled of heathen men, till the times of nations be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 13:10; JOEL 2:30–31; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:12–14"> And tokens shall be in the sun, and the moon, and in the stars; and in the earth overlaying of folks, for confusion of the sound of the sea and of floods &#91;or of waves&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:29; DEUTERONOMY 28:65–67; 2ND PETER 3:10–12"> for men shall wax dry for dread and abiding, that shall come into &#91;or on&#93; all the world; for virtues of heaven shall be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:30; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:7; MARK 13:26"> And then they shall see man’s Son coming in a cloud, with great power and majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ROMANS 8:23; ISAIAH 60:1–2; EPHESIANS 4:30"> And when these things begin to be made &#91;or beginning to be done&#93;, behold ye, and raise ye your heads, for your redemption nigheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:32–35; MARK 13:28–30"> And he said to them a likeness, See ye the fig tree, and all trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 12:57"> when they bring forth now of themselves fruit, ye know that summer is nigh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JAMES 5:9; HEBREWS 10:37; 1ST PETER 4:7"> so &#91;also&#93; ye, when ye see these things to be done, know ye, that the kingdom of God is nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 13:30; MATTHEW 24:34; MATTHEW 23:36"> Truly I say to you, that this generation shall not pass, till all things be done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ISAIAH 40:8; MARK 13:31; MATTHEW 24:35"> Heaven and earth shall pass, but my words shall not pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 4:19; LUKE 12:45–46; LUKE 12:40"> But take ye heed to yourselves, lest peradventure your hearts be grieved with gluttony, and drunken-ness, and busynesses of this life, and that day come suddenly on you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 16:15; PSALMS 11:6; JEREMIAH 48:43–44"> for as a snare it shall come on all men, that sit on the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:28; MATTHEW 25:13; 1ST PETER 5:8"> Therefore wake ye, praying in each time, that ye be had worthy to flee all these things that be to come &#91;or that shall come&#93;, and to stand before man’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 22:39; MARK 11:19; MATTHEW 21:17"> And in days he was teaching in the temple, but in nights he went out, and dwelled in the mount, that is called of Olivet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 8:1–2"> And all the people rose &#91;or came&#93; early, to come to him in the temple, for to hear him.</span>
