<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:23–27; LUKE 19:47–48; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 6:12"> And it was done in one of the days, when he taught the people in the temple, and preached the gospel &#91;or evangelizing&#93;, the princes of priests, and &#91;the&#93; scribes came together with the elder men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 2:14; MARK 11:28–33; JOHN 2:18"> and they said to him, Say to us, in what power thou doest these things, or who is he that gave to thee this power?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:2–3; LUKE 22:68; COLOSSIANS 4:6"> And Jesus answered, and said to them, And I shall ask you one word; answer ye to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 15:18; MATTHEW 11:7–19; MATTHEW 17:11–12"> Was the baptism of John of heaven, or of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 1:34; JOHN 1:15–18; JOHN 1:30"> And they thought within them-selves, saying, For if we say, Of heaven, he shall say, Why then believe ye not to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:26; MATTHEW 11:9; JOHN 10:41"> and if we say, Of men, all the people shall stone us; for they be certain, that John is a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ISAIAH 29:14; 2ND PETER 3:3; ISAIAH 41:28"> And they answered, that they knew not, of whence it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:4–5; MATTHEW 21:27; MARK 11:33"> And Jesus said to them, Neither I say to you, in what power I do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:33–46; MARK 12:1–12; ISAIAH 5:1–7"> And he began to say to the people this parable. A man planted a vineyard, and hired it to tillers; and he was gone in pilgrimage long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 36:15–16; NEHEMIAH 9:26; MATTHEW 21:34–36"> And in the time of gathering of grapes, he sent a servant to the tillers, that they should give to him of the fruit of the vineyard; which beat him, and let him go void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 10:1; HEBREWS 11:36–37; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:2"> And he thought yet to send another servant; and &#91;also&#93; they beat this, and tormented him sore, and let him go void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he thought yet to send the third, and him also they wounded, and casted him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:17; JOHN 3:16–17; JEREMIAH 36:7"> And the lord of the vineyard said, What shall I do? I shall send my dearworthy son; peradventure, when they see him, they shall dread &#91;or they shall be ashamed&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 8:17; HEBREWS 1:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:23"> And when the tillers saw him, they thought within themselves, and said, This is the heir, slay we him, that the heritage be ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:12; MARK 12:6–9; MATTHEW 21:37–40"> And they casted him out of the vineyard, and killed him. What shall then the lord of the vineyard do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 19:27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:46; MATTHEW 21:41"> He shall come, and destroy these tillers, and give the vineyard to others. And when this thing was heard, they said to him, God forbid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22; ISAIAH 28:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:11"> But he beheld them, and said, What then is this that is written, The stone which men building reproved, this is made into the head of the corner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ISAIAH 8:14–15; ZECHARIAH 12:3; DANIEL 2:44–45"> Each that shall fall on that stone, shall be bruised &#91;or be broken&#93;, but on whom it shall fall, it shall all-break him &#91;or it shall break him into small parts&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 19:47–48; LUKE 20:14; MATTHEW 21:45–46"> And the princes of priests, and &#91;the&#93; scribes, sought to lay on him hands &#91;or to lay hands on him&#93; in that hour, and they dreaded the people; for they knew that to them he said this likeness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 11:54; MATTHEW 22:15–22; MATTHEW 27:2"> And they espied, and sent spies, that feigned them&#91;selves&#93; just, that they should take him in word, and betake him to the power of the prince, and to the power of the justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:4–5; MATTHEW 26:49–50; MATTHEW 22:16"> And they asked him, and said, Master, we know, that rightly thou sayest and teachest &#91;or that thou sayest and teachest rightly&#93;; and thou takest not the person of man, but thou teachest in truth the way of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 9:37; MARK 12:14–17; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:37"> Is it leaveful to us to give tribute to the emperor &#91;or to Caesar&#93;, or nay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:18; 1ST CORINTHIANS 3:19; LUKE 5:22"> And he beheld the deceit of them, and said to them, What tempt ye me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 11:28; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:32"> Show ye to me a penny; whose image and superscription hath it? They answered, and said to him, The emperor’s &#91;or Caesar’s&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 13:6–7; MARK 12:17; 1ST PETER 2:13–17"> And he said to them, Yield ye therefore to the emperor those things that be the emperor’s &#91;or to Caesar those things that be Caesar’s&#93;, and those things that be of God, to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 20:20; PROVERBS 26:4–5; TITUS 1:10"> And they might not reprove his word before the people; and they wondered in his answer, and held peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:1–2; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:12; MARK 12:18–27"> Some of the Sadducees, that denied the again-rising from death to life &#91;or the resurrection&#93;, came, and asked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5–10; GENESIS 38:8; GENESIS 38:11"> and said, Master, Moses wrote to us, if the brother of any man have a wife, and he be dead &#91;or died&#93;, and he was without heirs &#91;or free children&#93;, that his brother take his wife, and raise seed to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:20; JEREMIAH 22:30"> And so there were seven brethren. The first took a wife, and is dead without heirs, &#91;or without sons, or free children&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the brother pursuing took her &#91;or the second took her&#93;, and he is dead without son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and the third took her; also and all seven, and they left not seed &#91;or no seed&#93;, but be dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:4; ECCLESIASTES 9:5; HEBREWS 9:27"> and the last of all the woman is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:24–28; MARK 12:19–23"> Therefore in the rising again, whose wife of them shall she be? for seven had her to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 16:8; LUKE 17:27; HEBREWS 13:4"> And Jesus said to them, Sons of this world wed, and be given to weddings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 1:5; JOHN 5:29; DANIEL 12:2–3"> but they that shall be had worthy of that world, and of the rising again from death, neither be wedded, nor wed wives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:4; PHILIPPIANS 3:21; MATTHEW 22:30"> neither they shall be able to die more; for they be even with angels, and be the sons of God, since they be the sons of the rising again from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EXODUS 3:2–6; MARK 12:26–27; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:30–32"> And that dead men rise again, also Moses showed beside the bush, as he saith, The Lord God of Abraham, and God of Isaac, and God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 11:25–26; COLOSSIANS 3:3–4; ROMANS 6:10–11"> And God is not of dead men, but of living men; for all men live to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:9; MARK 12:28–34; MATTHEW 22:34–40"> And some of the scribes answering, said, Master, thou hast well said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:46; MARK 12:34; PROVERBS 26:5"> And they durst no more ask him anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARK 12:35–37; MATTHEW 1:1; MATTHEW 22:41–45"> But he said to them, How say men, Christ to be the son of David &#91;or that Christ is the son of David&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:34–35; HEBREWS 1:13"> and David himself saith in the book of Psalms, The Lord said to my Lord, Sit thou on my right half,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 19:27; PSALMS 110:1; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 19:14–21"> till I put thine enemies a stool of thy feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 22:16; LUKE 1:31–35; MATTHEW 1:23"> Therefore David calleth him Lord, and how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:10; MARK 12:38–39; MARK 8:34"> And in hearing of all the people, he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 11:43; MARK 12:38–39; LUKE 14:7"> Be ye ware of the scribes, that will wander in stoles, and love salutations in the chapping, and the first chairs in the synagogues, and the first sitting places in &#91;the&#93; feasts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARK 12:40; JAMES 3:1; TITUS 1:16"> that devour the houses of widows, and feign long praying; these shall take the more damnation.</span>
