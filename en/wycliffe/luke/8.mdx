<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:23; ROMANS 10:15; MATTHEW 9:35"> And it was done afterward, and Jesus made journey by cities and castles, preaching and evangelizing the realm of God, and twelve with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARK 16:9; MATTHEW 27:55–56; MARK 15:40–41"> and some women that were healed of wicked spirits and sicknesses, Mary, that is called Magdalene, of whom seven devils went out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 4:46–53; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:36–39"> and Joanna, the wife of Chuza, the procurator of Herod, and Susanna, and many others, that ministered to him of their riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARK 4:1–9; MATTHEW 13:2–9"> And when much people was come together, and men hied &#91;or hasted&#93; to him, from the cities, he said by a similitude, &#91;or a likeness, or example&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:1; JAMES 1:23–24; MARK 4:15"> He that soweth, went out to sow his seed. And while he soweth, some fell beside the way, and was defouled, and birds of the air ate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:7–8; LUKE 8:13; MARK 4:5–6"> And other fell on a stone, and it sprang up, and dried, for it had not moisture &#91;or it had no moisture&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:3; LUKE 8:14; LUKE 21:34"> And other fell among thorns, and the thorns sprang up together, and strangled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:11; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 2:7"> And other fell into good earth, and it sprang up, and made an hundredfold fruit. He said these things, and cried, He that hath ears of hearing, hear he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 4:10; MATTHEW 13:10; MATTHEW 13:18"> But his disciples asked him, what this parable was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ISAIAH 6:9; MARK 4:11; MATTHEW 11:25"> And he said to them, To you it is granted to know the private &#91;or the mystery&#93; of the kingdom of God; but to other men in parables, that they seeing see not, and they hearing understand not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:19; MARK 4:14–20; JAMES 1:21"> And this is the parable. The seed is God’s word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–14; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:9; JAMES 1:23–24"> and they that be beside the way, be these that hear; and afterward the fiend cometh, and taketh away the word from their heart, lest they believing be made safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 6:4; JOHN 5:35; PROVERBS 12:12"> But they that fell on a stone, be these that when they have heard, receive the word with joy. And these have no roots; for at a time they believe, and in time of temptation they go away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1ST JOHN 2:15–17; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:9–10; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:17"> But that that fell among thorns, be these that heard, and of busy-nesses, and riches, and lusts of life they go forth, and be strangled, and bring forth no fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:36; GALATIANS 5:22–26; COLOSSIANS 1:10"> But that that fell into good earth, be these that, in a good heart, and best, hear the word, and hold, and bring forth fruit in patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 11:33; MATTHEW 5:15–16; MARK 4:21–22"> No man lighteth a lantern, and covereth it with a vessel, or putteth it under a bed, but on a candlestick, that men that enter see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 12:2–3; MATTHEW 10:26; MARK 4:22"> For there is no privy thing, which shall not be opened; neither hid thing, which shall not be known, and come into open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:12; LUKE 19:26; MARK 4:23–25"> Therefore see ye, how ye hear; for it shall be given to him that hath, and whoever hath not, also that that he weeneth &#91;or he guesseth&#93; that he have, shall be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:46–50; MARK 3:31–35; MARK 3:21"> And his mother and brethren came to him; and they might not come to him for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:14; MARK 6:3; MATTHEW 13:55–56"> And it was told to him, Thy mother and thy brethren stand withoutforth, willing to see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 11:27–28; JAMES 1:22; JOHN 13:17"> And he answered, and said to them, My mother and my brethren be these, that hear the word of God, and do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:23–27; MARK 4:35–41; MARK 6:45"> And it was done in one of the days, he went up into a boat, and his disciples. And he said to them, Pass we over the sea. And they went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 93:3–4; PSALMS 124:2–4; PSALMS 148:8"> And while they rowed, he slept. And a tempest of wind came down into the water, and they were driven hither and thither with waves, and were in peril.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 107:25–29; LUKE 4:39; PSALMS 104:6–9"> And they came nigh, and raised him, and said, Commander, we perish. And he rose up, and blamed the wind, and the tempest of the water; and it ceased, and peaceability was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:26; PROVERBS 30:4; MARK 4:40–41"> And he said to them, Where is your faith? Which dreading wondered, and said together, Who, guessest thou, is this? for he commandeth to the winds and to the sea, and they obey to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARK 5:1–20; MATTHEW 8:28–34"> And they rowed to the country of Gadarenes, that is against Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARK 5:2–5; 1ST SAMUEL 19:24; NUMBERS 19:16"> And when he went out to the land, a man ran to him, that had a devil &#91;now&#93; long time, and he was not clothed with cloth, neither dwelled in house, but in sepulchres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:29; 2ND PETER 2:4; MARK 5:6–8"> This, when he saw Jesus, fell down before him, and he crying with a great voice said, What to me and to thee, Jesus, the Son of the Highest God? I beseech thee, that thou torment me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARK 5:3–5; LUKE 9:39; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:25–26"> For he commanded the unclean spirit, that he should go out from the man. For he took him oft times, and he was bound with chains, and kept in stocks, and when the bonds were broken, he was led of the devil into desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:53; LUKE 8:2; MATTHEW 8:29"> And Jesus asked him, and said, What name is to thee? And he said, A legion; for many devils were &#91;or had&#93; entered into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:41; PHILIPPIANS 2:10–11; JOB 2:5"> And they prayed him, that he should not command them, that they should go into hell &#91;or into the deep-ness&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:4; ISAIAH 66:3; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 20:7"> And there was a flock of many swine &#91;or many hogs&#93; pasturing in an hill, and they prayed him, that he should suffer them to enter into them. And he suffered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:8; JOHN 8:44; LUKE 8:22–23"> And so the devils went out from the man, and entered into the swine &#91;or the hogs&#93;; and with a rush the flock went headlong into the pool &#91;or into the lake of water&#93;, and was drenched or drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 5:14; MATTHEW 8:33; MATTHEW 28:11"> And when the herders saw this thing done, they fled, and told into the city, and into the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKE 10:39; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:3; 1ST JOHN 3:8"> And they went out to see that thing that was done. And they came to Jesus, and they found the man sitting clothed, from whom the devils went out, and in whole mind at his feet; and they dreaded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:24"> And they that saw told to them, how he was made whole of the legion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:39; LUKE 8:28; LUKE 10:10–11"> And all the multitude of the country of Gadarenes prayed him, that he should go from them, for they were held with great dread. And he went up into a boat, and turned again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:13–16; MARK 5:18–20; PSALMS 145:3–12"> And the man of whom the devils were gone out &#91;or of whom the fiends went out&#93;, prayed him, that he should be with him. &#91;Soothly&#93; Jesus let him go, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 17:15–18; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:8; DEUTERONOMY 10:21"> Go again into thine house, and tell how great things God hath done to thee. And he went through all the city, and preached, how great things Jesus had done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:1; MARK 5:21; MARK 6:20"> And it was done, when Jesus was gone again &#91;or had gone again&#93;, the people received him; for all were abiding him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARK 5:22–43; LUKE 13:14; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:8"> And lo! a man, to whom the name was Jairus, and he was &#91;a&#93; prince of a synagogue; and he fell down at the feet of Jesus, and prayed him, that he should enter into his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 7:12; LUKE 8:45; JOB 1:18–19"> for he had but one daughter &#91;or an only daughter&#93;, almost of twelve years old, and she was dead. And it befell, the while he went, he was thronged of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MARK 5:25–26; MATTHEW 9:20–22; LEVITICUS 15:25–33"> And a woman that had a flux of blood twelve years, and had spended all her chattel &#91;or all her substance&#93; in leeches, and might not be cured of any,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MARK 5:27–28; DEUTERONOMY 22:12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:12"> and she came nigh behind, and touched the hem of his cloth, and anon the flux of her blood ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MARK 5:30–32; LUKE 9:13; LUKE 5:5"> And Jesus said, Who is it that touched me? And when all men denied, Peter said, and they that were with him, Commander, the people thrust &#91;or throng&#93; and dis-ease thee, and thou sayest, Who touched me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 6:19; LUKE 5:17; 1ST PETER 2:9"> And Jesus said, Some man hath touched me, for &#91;I have known&#93; that virtue went out of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARK 5:33; LUKE 17:15–16; 1ST CORINTHIANS 2:3"> And the woman seeing, that it was not hid from him, came trembling, and fell down at his feet, and for what cause she had touched him she showed before all the people, and how anon she was healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKE 7:50; MATTHEW 9:22; HEBREWS 4:2"> And he said to her, Daughter, thy faith hath made thee safe; go thou in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARK 5:35–43; ISAIAH 7:12; LUKE 7:6"> And yet while he spake, a man came from &#91;or to&#93; the prince of the synagogue, and said to him, Thy daughter is dead, do not thou travail the Master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JOHN 11:40; JOHN 11:25; MARK 5:36"> And when this word was heard, Jesus answered to the father of the damsel, Do not thou dread, but believe thou only &#91;or but only believe thou&#93;, and she shall be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MARK 14:33; MATTHEW 6:5–6; 1ST KINGS 17:19–23"> And when he came to the house, he suffered no man to enter with him, but Peter and John and James, and the father and the mother of the damsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="LUKE 23:27; JOHN 11:4; ZECHARIAH 12:10"> And all wept, and bewailed her. And he said, Do not ye weep, for the damsel is not dead, but sleepeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="LUKE 16:14; ISAIAH 53:3; JOHN 11:39"> And they scorned him, and knew that she was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:32; JOHN 11:43; MARK 1:31"> But he held her hand, and cried, and said, Damsel, rise up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JOHN 11:44; 1ST KINGS 17:21–23; MARK 5:43"> And her spirit turned again, and she rose anon. And he commanded to give to her to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:4; MATTHEW 9:30; LUKE 5:14"> And her father and mother wondered greatly; and he commanded them, that they should not say to any &#91;man&#93; that thing that was done.</span>
