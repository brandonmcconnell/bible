<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:7–8; LUKE 10:9; MATTHEW 3:2"> And he sent them to preach the kingdom of God, and to heal sick men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 22:35; PSALMS 37:3; LUKE 10:4–12"> And he said to them, Nothing take ye in the way, neither a staff &#91;or a rod&#93;, nor scrip, neither bread, nor money, and neither have ye two coats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:11; MARK 6:10; LUKE 10:5–8"> And into what&#91;ever&#93; house ye enter, dwell ye there, and go ye not out from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:51; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 18:6; MARK 6:11"> And whoever receive not you &#91;or whoever shall not receive you&#93;, go ye out of that city, and shake ye off the powder of your feet into witnessing on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 4:30; MARK 16:20; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:15"> And they went forth, and went about by castles, preaching &#91;or evangelizing&#93; and healing everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 6:14–28; MATTHEW 14:1–12; LUKE 3:1"> And Herod &#91;the&#93; tetrarch &#91;or prince of the fourth part&#93;, heard all &#91;the&#93; things that were done of him, and he doubted, for that it was said of some men, that John was risen from death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 1:21; LUKE 9:19; MARK 6:15"> and of some men, that Elijah had appeared; but of others, that one of the old prophets was risen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 23:8; LUKE 13:31–32"> And Herod said, I have beheaded John; and who is this, of whom I hear such things? And he sought to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:21; HEBREWS 13:17; MATTHEW 14:13–21"> And the apostles turned again, and told to him all things that they had done. And he took them, and went beside into a desert place, that is &#91;called&#93; Bethsaida.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 9:2; LUKE 5:31; HEBREWS 4:16"> And when the people knew this, they followed him. And he received them, and spake to them of the kingdom of God; and he healed them that had need of cure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 6:1; JOHN 6:5–15; HOSEA 13:5"> And the day began to bow down, and the twelve came, and said to him, Leave the people, that they go, and turn into the castles and towns, that be about, that they find meat, for we be here in a desert place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:24–25; JOHN 6:5–9; 2ND KI 4:42–43"> And he said to them, Give ye to them to eat. And they said, There be not to us more than five loaves and two fishes, but peradventure that we go, and buy meats to all this people &#91;or for all the company&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 8:6; 1ST CORINTHIANS 14:40; MARK 6:39–40"> And the men were almost five thousand. And he said to his disciples, Make ye them to sit to meat by companies, a fifty together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And they did so, and they made all men sit to &#91;or at the&#93; meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:19; 1ST TIMOTHY 4:4–5; MATTHEW 15:36"> And when he had taken the five loaves and two fishes, he beheld into heaven, and blessed them, and brake, and dealed to his disciples, that they should set &#91;or put&#93; forth before the companies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2ND KI 4:44; MATTHEW 15:37–38; JOHN 6:11–13"> And all men ate, and were full-filled &#91;or were filled&#93;; and that that left to them of broken meats was taken up, twelve coffins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:13–16; LUKE 11:1; MATTHEW 26:36"> And it was done, when he was alone praying, his disciples were with him, and he asked them, and said, Whom say the people that I am?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; LUKE 9:7–8; MATTHEW 14:2"> And they answered, and said, John Baptist, others say Elijah, and &#91;or but&#93; others say, one prophet of the former is risen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:15–17; JOHN 4:29; JOHN 7:41"> And he said to them, But whom say ye that I am? Simon Peter answered, and said, The Christ of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:20; MATTHEW 17:9; MARK 8:30–31"> And he blaming them, commanded &#91;them&#93; that they should say to no man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:1–12; LUKE 24:7; LUKE 18:31–34"> and said these things, For it behooveth man’s Son to suffer many things, and to be reproved of the elder men, and of the princes of priests, and of the scribes, and to be slain, and the third day to rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:38–39; LUKE 14:26–27; ROMANS 8:13"> And he said to all &#91;men&#93;, If any &#91;man&#93; will come after me, deny he himself, and take he his cross every day, and pursue he me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 17:33; JOHN 12:25; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 20:23–24"> For he that will make his life safe shall lose it; and he that loseth his life for me, shall make it safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MARK 8:36; MATTHEW 16:26; PSALMS 49:6–8"> And what profiteth &#91;it to&#93; a man, if he win all the world, and lose himself, and do impairing of himself &#91;or to himself&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:32–33; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:12; LUKE 12:8–9"> For whoso shameth me and my words, man’s Son shall shame him, when he cometh in his majesty, and of the Father’s, and of the holy angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:28; MARK 9:1; JOHN 21:22–23"> And I say to you, verily there be some standing here, which shall not taste death, till they see the realm of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:1–13; MATTHEW 26:37–39; MARK 14:33–36"> And it was done after these words almost eight days, and he took Peter and James and John, and he ascended into an hill, to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:2; MARK 9:2–3; EXODUS 34:29–35"> And while he prayed, the like-ness of his face was changed, and his clothing was white shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:3–6; 2ND CORINTHIANS 3:7–11; LUKE 1:17"> And lo! two men spake with him, and Moses and Elijah</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:21; 1ST PETER 1:11–12; 1ST PETER 5:10"> were seen in majesty; and they said his going out, which he should fulfill in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DANIEL 8:18; JOHN 1:14; DANIEL 10:9"> And Peter, and they that were with him, were heavy of sleep, &#91;or were grieved, or heavied, with sleep&#93;, and they waking saw his majesty, and the two men that stood with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARK 9:5–6; MARK 10:38; PSALMS 4:6–7"> And it was done, when they departed from him, Peter said to Jesus, Commander, it is good that we be here, and make we here three tabernacles, one to thee, and one to Moses, and one to Elijah. And he knew not what he should say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 40:34–38; MATTHEW 17:5–7; JUDGES 13:22"> But while he spake these things, a cloud was made, and overshadowed them; and they dreaded, when they entered into the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKE 3:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:22–23; MATTHEW 3:17"> And a voice was made out of the cloud, and said, This is my dear-worthy Son, hear ye him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:9; MARK 9:9–10; MARK 9:6"> And while the voice was made, Jesus was found alone. And they were still, and to no man said in those days any of those things, that they had seen, &#91;or they held peace, and said to no man in those days aught of those things that they had seen&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:14–20; MARK 9:14–29"> But it was done in the day pursuing, when they came down of the hill, much people met them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 12:10; JOHN 4:47; LUKE 7:12"> And lo! a man of the company cried, and said, Master, I beseech thee, behold my son, for I have no more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MARK 9:20; LUKE 8:29; LUKE 4:35"> and lo! a spirit taketh him, and suddenly he crieth, and hurtleth &#91;him&#93; down, and draweth him with foam &#91;or with froth&#93;, and scarcely he goeth away drawing him all to pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 9:1; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 19:13–16; LUKE 10:17–19"> And I prayed thy disciples, that they should cast him out, and they might not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:5; ROMANS 2:4; JOHN 20:27"> And Jesus answered and said to them, A! unfaithful generation and wayward, how long shall I be with you, and suffer you? bring hither thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MARK 9:26–27; LUKE 7:15; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 12:12"> And when he came nigh, the devil hurtled him down, and wrenched him. And Jesus blamed the unclean spirit, and healed the child, and yielded him to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 9:43–45; LUKE 5:26; MATTHEW 17:22"> And all men wondered greatly in the greatness of God. And when all men wondered in all things that he did, he said to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LUKE 9:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:23; LUKE 2:19"> Put ye these words in your hearts, for it is to come, that man’s Son be betrayed into the hands of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LUKE 18:34; LUKE 2:50; JOHN 12:16"> And they knew not this word, and it was hid before them, that they feeled it not; and they dreaded to ask him of this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:1–5; MARK 9:33–37; MATTHEW 20:20–22"> But a thought entered into them, who of them should be greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 139:23; JOHN 16:30; LUKE 5:22"> And Jesus, seeing the thoughts of the heart of them, took a child, and setted him beside him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1ST PETER 5:6; LUKE 14:11; MARK 9:37"> and said to them, Whoever receiveth this child in my name, receiveth me; and whoever receiveth me, receiveth him that sent me; for he that is least among you all, is the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARK 9:38–40; NUMBERS 11:27–29; MARK 10:13–14"> And John answered and said, Commander, we saw a man casting out fiends in thy name, and we have forbidden him, for he pursueth not thee with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:30; LUKE 11:23; 1ST CORINTHIANS 12:3"> And Jesus said to him, Do not ye forbid, for he that is not against us, is for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="ISAIAH 50:5–9; MARK 16:19; LUKE 13:22"> And it was done, when the days of his taking up were fulfilled, he set fast his face, to go to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:5; LUKE 17:16; LUKE 10:1"> and sent messengers before his sight. And they went, and entered into a city of Samaritans, to make ready to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="JOHN 4:9; JOHN 4:40–42; LUKE 9:48"> And they received not him, for the face of him was going into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2ND KI 1:10–14; JAMES 3:14–18; JAMES 1:19–20"> And when James and John, his disciples, saw, they said, Lord, wilt thou that we say, that fire come down from heaven, and waste them, &#91;as Elijah did&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:19; 1ST PETER 3:9; JEREMIAH 17:9"> And he turned, and blamed them, and said, Ye know not, whose spirits ye be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:28; ROMANS 12:21; JOHN 3:17"> for man’s Son came not to lose men’s souls, but to save &#91;them&#93;. And they went into another castle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:19–22; JOHN 13:37; LUKE 9:57–60"> And it was done, when they walked in the way, a man said to him, I shall pursue thee, whither ever thou &#91;shalt&#93; go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:20; LUKE 14:26–33; JAMES 2:5"> And Jesus said to him, Foxes have dens &#91;or ditches&#93;, and birds of the air have nests, but man’s Son hath not where he &#91;shall&#93; rest his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:24; MATTHEW 6:33; 1ST KINGS 19:20"> And he said to another, Pursue thou me. And he said, Lord, suffer me first to go, and bury my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="2ND TIMOTHY 2:3–4; 1ST TIMOTHY 5:6; JOHN 21:15–17"> And Jesus said to him, Suffer that dead men bury their dead men; but go thou, and tell the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="LUKE 14:26; LUKE 14:18–20; 1ST KINGS 19:20"> And another said, Lord, I shall pursue thee, but first suffer me to leave all things &#91;or to tell to them&#93; that be at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:13; HEBREWS 10:38; 2ND PETER 2:20–22"> And Jesus said to him, No man that putteth his hand to the plough, and beholding backward, is able to the kingdom of God.</span>
