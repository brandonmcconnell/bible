<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:2; JOHN 5:16; JOHN 5:9–11"> And some of the Pharisees said to them, What do ye that, that is not leaveful in the sabbaths?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1ST SAMUEL 21:3–6; MARK 2:25–26; MATTHEW 12:3–5"> And Jesus answered, and said to them, Have ye not read, what David did, when he hungered, and they that were with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:5–9"> how he entered into the house of God, and took loaves of proposition, and ate, and gave to them that were with him; which loaves it was not leaveful to eat, but only to priests &#91;or no but to priests alone&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARK 2:27; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 1:10; MARK 9:7"> And he said to them, For man’s Son is Lord, yea, of the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 3:1–6; MATTHEW 12:9–14; LUKE 4:31"> And it was done in another sabbath, that he entered into a synagogue, and taught. And a man was there, and his right hand was dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 20:20; PSALMS 38:12; LUKE 13:14"> And the scribes and the Pharisees espied him, if he would heal him in the sabbath, that they should find cause, whereof they should accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ISAIAH 42:4; MATTHEW 9:4; LUKE 5:22"> And he knew the thoughts of them, and he said to the man that had a dry hand, Rise up, and stand in the middle. And he rose, and stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 3:4; MATTHEW 12:12–13; LUKE 14:3"> And Jesus said to them, I ask you, if it is leaveful to do well in the sabbath &#91;day&#93;, or evil? to make a soul safe, or to lose?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 4:6–7; MARK 3:5; JOHN 5:8"> And when he had beheld all men about, he said to the man, Hold forth thine hand. And he held forth, and his hand was restored to health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 2:1–2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:33; ECCLESIASTES 9:3"> And they were full-filled &#91;or filled&#93; with unwisdom, and spake together, what they should do of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:7; MARK 6:46; MARK 1:35"> And it was done in those days, he went out into an hill to pray; and he was all night dwelling in the prayer of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARK 3:13–19; MATTHEW 9:36–4; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:14"> And when the day was come, he called his disciples, and chose twelve of them, which he called &#91;or he named&#93; also apostles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:13; MATTHEW 4:21; MATTHEW 4:18"> Simon, whom he called Peter, and Andrew, his brother, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:9; MARK 3:18; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:13"> Matthew and Thomas, James Alphaeus, and Simon, that is called Zelotes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 14:22; JOHN 6:70–71; MARK 3:18"> Judas of James, and Judas Iscariot, that was traitor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:21; MATTHEW 4:23–25; PSALMS 107:17–20"> And Jesus came down from the hill with them, and stood in a field place; and the company of his disciples, and a great multitude of people, of all Judea, and Jerusalem, and of the sea coasts, and of Tyre and Sidon, that came to hear him, and to be healed of their sicknesses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:15; MATTHEW 15:22; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:16"> and they that were travailed of &#91;or with&#93; unclean spirits, were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:36; MARK 3:10; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:15–16"> And all the people sought to touch him, for virtue went out of him, and healed all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 2:5; LUKE 6:20–24; 2ND CORINTHIANS 8:9"> And when his eyes were cast up, into his disciples, he said, Blessed be ye, poor men &#91;or poor&#93;, for the kingdom of God is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 6:35; MATTHEW 5:6; JAMES 1:12"> Blessed be ye, that now hunger, for ye shall be full-filled. Blessed be ye, that now weep, for ye shall laugh. &#91;Blessed be ye, that hunger now, for ye shall be filled. Blessed be ye that weep now, for ye shall laugh.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:10–12; MATTHEW 10:22; 1ST PETER 4:12–16"> Ye shall be blessed, when men shall hate you, and separate you away, and put reproof to you&#91;or shall put shame on you&#93;, and cast out your name as evil, for man’s Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:41; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 21:7; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 3:12"> Joy ye in that day, and be ye glad; for lo! your meed is much in heaven; for after these things the fathers of them did to prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:2; JAMES 5:1–6; PSALMS 49:6–7"> Nevertheless woe to you, rich men, that have your comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ISAIAH 65:13; JAMES 4:9; PROVERBS 14:13"> Woe to you that be full-filled, for ye shall hunger. Woe to you that now laugh, for ye shall mourn, and weep. &#91;Woe to you that be filled, for ye shall hunger. Woe to you that laugh now, for ye shall mourn, and weep.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 15:19; JAMES 4:4; MATTHEW 7:15"> Woe to you, when all men shall bless you; after these things the fathers of them did to &#91;false&#93; prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 5:15; LUKE 6:35; ROMANS 12:17–21"> But I say to you that hear, love ye your enemies, do ye well to them that hated you &#91;or that hate you&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:9; 1ST CORINTHIANS 4:12; ROMANS 12:14"> bless ye men that curse you, pray ye for men that defame you &#91;or that falsely challenge you&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:34; MATTHEW 5:39–42; 2ND CORINTHIANS 11:20"> And to him that smiteth thee on the one cheek, show also the other &#91;or give also the tother&#93;; and from him that taketh away from thee a cloth, do not thou forbid the coat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:7–10; 2ND CORINTHIANS 9:6–14; MATTHEW 6:12"> And give to each that asketh thee, and if a man taketh away those things that be thine, ask thou not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:12; GALATIANS 5:14; MATTHEW 22:39"> And as ye will that men do to you, do ye also to them in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:46–47; 1ST PETER 2:19–20"> And if ye love them that love you, what thank &#91;or what grace&#93; is to you? for sinful men love men that love them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And if ye do well to them that do well to you, what grace &#91;or what thank&#93; is to you? for sinful men do this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:42; LUKE 14:12–14; LUKE 6:35"> And if ye lend to them of which ye hope to take again, what thank &#91;or what grace&#93; is to you? for sinful men lend to sinful men, to take again as much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:44–45; JOHN 13:35; PROVERBS 19:17"> Nevertheless love ye your enemies, and do ye well, and lend ye, hoping nothing thereof, and your meed shall be much, and ye shall be the sons of the Highest, for he is benign, &#91;or of good will&#93;, on unkind men and evil men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:1–2; MATTHEW 5:48; 1ST PETER 1:15–16"> Therefore be ye merciful, as your Father is merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:32; JAMES 5:9; MATTHEW 7:1–5"> Do not ye deem, and ye shall not be deemed. Do not ye condemn, and ye shall not be condemned; forgive ye, and it shall be forgiven to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2ND CORINTHIANS 9:6–8; DEUTERONOMY 15:10; MARK 4:24"> Give ye, and it shall be given to you. They shall give into your bosom a good measure, and well-filled, and shaken together, and overflowing; for by the same measure, by which ye mete, it shall be meted again to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:14; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:13; 1ST TIMOTHY 6:3–5"> And he said to them a likeness, Whether the blind may lead the blind? nor fall they not both into the ditch? &#91;or whether they fall not both into the ditch?&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 13:16; MATTHEW 10:24–25; JOHN 15:20"> A disciple is not above his master; but each shall be perfect, if he be as his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 8:7; JAMES 1:24; ROMANS 2:1"> And what seest thou in thy brother’s eye a mote, but thou beholdest not a beam, that is in thine own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="ROMANS 2:21–29; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:10–12; PSALMS 51:9–13"> Or how mayest thou say to thy brother, Brother, suffer, I shall cast out the mote of thine eye, and thou beholdest not a beam that is in thine own eye? Hypocrite, first take out &#91;or cast out&#93; the beam of thine eye, and then thou shalt see to take out the mote of thy brother’s eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:21; MATTHEW 12:33; MATTHEW 7:16–20"> It is not a good tree, that maketh evil fruits, neither an evil tree, that maketh good fruits;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:33; JAMES 3:12; GALATIANS 5:19–23"> for every tree is known of his fruit. And men gather not figs of thorns, neither men gather a grape of a bush of briars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:23; MATTHEW 12:34–37; EPHESIANS 4:29"> A good man of the good treasure of his heart bringeth forth good things, and an evil man of the evil treasure bringeth forth evil things; for of the plenty of the heart the mouth speaketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:7; MALACHI 1:6; MATTHEW 7:21–23"> And what call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not those things that I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JAMES 1:22–25; MATTHEW 7:24–27; LUKE 11:28"> Each that cometh to me, and heareth my words, and doeth them, I shall show to you, to whom he is like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 3:10–15; PSALMS 62:2; MATTHEW 7:25–26"> He is like to a man that buildeth an house, that digged deep, and set &#91;or put&#93; the foundament on a stone. And when a great flood was made, the flood was hurled to that house, and it might not move it, for it was founded on a firm stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:31; JAMES 1:22–26; MATTHEW 23:3"> But he that heareth, and doeth not, is like to a man building his house on &#91;the&#93; earth, without foundament; into which the flood was hurled, and anon it fell down; and the falling down of that house was made great.</span>
