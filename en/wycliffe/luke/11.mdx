<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 19:14; LUKE 22:39–45; ROMANS 8:26–27"> And it was done, when he was praying in a place, as he ceased, one of his disciples said to him, Lord, teach us to pray, as John taught his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:6–15; ECCLESIASTES 5:2; PSALMS 72:18–19"> And he said to them, When ye pray, say ye, Father &#91;or Father ours&#93;, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come to; thy will be done on earth, as it is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:11; MATTHEW 6:34; PROVERBS 30:8"> Give to us today our each day’s bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:12–15; MATTHEW 26:41; ISAIAH 43:25–26"> And forgive to us our sins, as we forgive to each man that oweth to us. And lead us not into temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 18:1–8"> And he said to them, Who of you shall have a friend, and shall go to him at midnight, and shall say to him, Friend, lend to me three loaves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> for my friend cometh to me from the way, and I have not what I shall set &#91;or shall put&#93; before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 13:25; GALATIANS 6:17; LUKE 7:6"> And he withinforth answer and say, Do not thou be heavy to me; the door is now shut, and my children be with me in bed; I may not rise, and give to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:26; COLOSSIANS 4:12; ROMANS 15:30"> And if he shall dwell still knocking &#91;or if he shall continue knocking&#93;, I say to you, though he shall not rise, and give to him, for that that he is his friend, nevertheless for his continual asking, he shall rise, and give to him, as many as he hath need to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 11:24; JOHN 16:23–24; MATTHEW 21:22"> And I say to you, ask ye, and it shall be given to you; seek ye, and ye shall find; knock ye, and it shall be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 4:3; LAMENTATIONS 3:54–58; PSALMS 31:22"> For each that asketh, taketh, and he that seeketh, findeth; and to a man that knocketh, it shall be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:9; ISAIAH 49:15"> Therefore who of you asketh his father bread, whether he shall give &#91;to&#93; him a stone? or if he asketh fish, whether he shall give &#91;to&#93; him a serpent for the fish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="APOCALYPSE of JOHN 9:10; LUKE 10:19; EZEKIEL 2:6"> or if he asketh an egg, whether he shall areach &#91;to&#93; him a scorpion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:11; ISAIAH 44:3–4; ROMANS 8:32"> Therefore if ye, when ye be evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more your Father of heaven shall give a good Spirit to men that ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:22–24; MATTHEW 9:32–34; MARK 7:32–37"> And Jesus was casting out a fiend, and it was dumb. And when he had cast out the fiend, the dumb man spake; and the people wondered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:34; MATTHEW 12:24–30; JOHN 8:48"> And some of them said, In Beelzebub, prince of devils, he casteth out devils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 6:30; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:22; MARK 8:11–12"> And others tempting asked of him a token from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 3:23–27; MATTHEW 12:25–29; ISAIAH 19:2–3"> And as he saw the thoughts of them, he said to them, Every realm parted against itself shall be desolate, and an house shall fall on an house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:26; MATTHEW 12:31–34; LUKE 11:15"> And if Satan be parted against himself, how shall his realm stand? For ye say, that I cast out fiends in Beelzebub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 9:49; JOB 15:6; ROMANS 3:19"> And if I in Beelzebub cast out fiends, in whom cast out your sons? &#91;or in whom your sons cast out?&#93; Therefore they shall be your doomsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 8:19; MATTHEW 12:28; LUKE 10:9"> But if I cast out fiends in the finger of God, then the realm of God is come among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARK 3:27; MATTHEW 12:29"> When a strong, armed man keepeth his house, all things that he wieldeth be in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ISAIAH 49:24–25; ISAIAH 53:12; 1ST JOHN 3:8"> But if a stronger than he come upon him, and overcome him, he shall take away all his arms in which he trusted, and shall deal abroad his robberies &#91;or his spoils&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:30; LUKE 9:50; MARK 9:40"> He that is not with me, is against me; and he that gathereth not together with me, scattereth abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:43–45; 1ST PETER 5:8; JOB 2:2"> When an unclean spirit goeth out of a man, he wandereth by dry places, and seeketh rest; and he finding none, saith, I shall turn again into mine house, from whence I came out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2ND THESSALONIANS 2:9–12; 2ND PETER 2:10–19; JUDE 1:8–13"> And when he cometh, he findeth it cleansed with besoms, and fair arrayed &#91;or adorned&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 5:14; MATTHEW 12:45; 2ND PETER 2:20–22"> Then he goeth, and taketh with him, seven other spirits worse than himself, and they enter, and dwell there. And the last things of that man be made worse than the former.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 1:42; LUKE 1:48; LUKE 23:29"> And it was done, when he had said these things, a woman of the company raised &#91;up&#93; her voice, and said to him, Blessed be the womb that bare thee, and blessed be the teats that thou hast sucked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 6:47–48; LUKE 8:21; PSALMS 112:1"> And he said, But yea &#91;or Rather&#93;, blessed be they, that hear the word of God, and keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 11:16; MARK 8:11–12; 1ST CORINTHIANS 1:22"> And when the people ran together, he began to say, This generation is a wayward generation; it seeketh a token, and a token shall not be given to it, but the token of Jonah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:40–42; JONAH 1:17; JONAH 3:2–10"> For as Jonah was a token to men of Nineveh, so man’s Son shall be to this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 9:1; 1ST KINGS 10:1–13; ROMANS 2:27"> The queen of the south shall rise in doom with men of this generation, and shall condemn them; for she came from the ends of the earth, to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and lo! here is a greater than Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:26; LUKE 11:31; JONAH 3:5–4"> Men of Nineveh shall rise in &#91;the&#93; doom with this generation, and shall condemn it; for they did penance in &#91;or at&#93; the preaching of Jonah, and lo! here is a greater than Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 8:16–17; MARK 4:21–22; MATTHEW 5:15–16"> No man tendeth &#91;or lighteneth&#93; a lantern, and putteth it in huddles, neither under a bushel, but on a candlestick, that they that go in, see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:22–23; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 26:18; JEREMIAH 5:21"> The lantern of thy body is thine eye; if thine eye be simple, all thy body shall be light-&#91;full&#93;; but if it be wayward, all thy body shall be dark-full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:25; 2ND PETER 1:9; JAMES 3:13–17"> Therefore see thou, lest the light that is in thee, be darknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:27; PROVERBS 6:23; PROVERBS 4:18–19"> Therefore if all thy body be bright &#91;or light-full&#93;, and have no part of darknesses, it shall be all bright &#91;or light-full&#93;, and as a lantern of bright-ness, &#91;or shining&#93;, it shall give light to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 7:36; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:19–23; LUKE 14:1"> And when he spake, a Pharisee prayed him, that he should eat with him. And he entered, and sat to &#91;or at the&#93; meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:2–3; MARK 7:2–5; JOHN 3:25"> And the Pharisee began to say, guessing within himself, why he was not washed before &#91;the&#93; meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="TITUS 1:15; PROVERBS 30:12; GENESIS 6:5"> And the Lord said to him, Now ye Pharisees cleanse that that is with-outforth of the cup and the platter; but that thing that is within of you &#91;or that thing of you that is within&#93;, is full of raven and wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 12:20; 1ST CORINTHIANS 15:36; MATTHEW 23:26"> Fools, whether he that made that that is withoutforth, made not also that that is within?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 12:33; JAMES 1:27; TITUS 1:15"> Nevertheless that that is over-plus, &#91;or superfluous&#93;, give ye alms, and lo! all things be clean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:23; LUKE 18:12; PROVERBS 21:3"> But woe to you, Pharisees, that tithe mint, and rue, and each herb, and leave doom and the charity of God. For it behooved to do these things, and not to leave those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 20:46; MARK 12:38–39; LUKE 14:7–11"> Woe to you, Pharisees, that love the first chairs in synagogues, and salutations in chapping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:27–28; PSALMS 5:9; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 23:3"> Woe to you, that be as sepulchres, that be not seen &#91;or which appear not&#93;, and men walking above know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="JOHN 9:40; MATTHEW 22:35; JEREMIAH 6:10"> But one of the wise men of the law answered, and said to him, Master, thou saying these things, also to us doest despite &#91;or doest despite also to us&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 11:52; ISAIAH 10:1; MATTHEW 23:2–4"> And he said, Also woe to you, wise men of &#91;the&#93; law, for ye charge men with burdens which they may not bear, and ye yourselves with your one finger touch not the heavinesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1ST THESSALONIANS 2:15; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:51; MATTHEW 23:29–33"> Woe to you, that build tombs &#91;or the burials&#93; of prophets; and your fathers slew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JAMES 5:10; MATTHEW 23:31; 2ND CHRONICLES 36:16"> Truly ye witness, that ye consent to the works of your fathers; for they slew them, but ye build their sepulchres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 1:30; COLOSSIANS 2:3; EPHESIANS 4:11"> Therefore the wisdom of God said, I shall send to them prophets and apostles, and of them they shall slay and persecute &#91;or pursue&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="GENESIS 9:5–6; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 18:20–24; 2ND KI 24:4"> that the blood of all &#91;the&#93; prophets, that was shed from the making &#91;or the beginning&#93; of the world, be sought of this generation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 24:20–22; MATTHEW 23:35; HEBREWS 11:4"> from the blood of just Abel to the blood of Zechariah, that was slain betwixt &#91;or which perished between&#93; the altar and the House. So I say to you, it shall be sought of this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:13; MALACHI 2:7; LUKE 11:45–46"> Woe to you, wise men of the law, for ye have taken away the key of knowing; and ye yourselves entered not &#91;in&#93;, and ye have forbidden them that entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1ST CORINTHIANS 13:5; JEREMIAH 20:10; LUKE 20:20"> And when he said these things to them, the Pharisees and the wise men of the law began grievously to against-stand &#91;him&#93;, and stop his mouth of many things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MARK 12:13; MATTHEW 22:15; MATTHEW 22:35"> ambushing him, and seeking to take something of his mouth, to accuse him.</span>
