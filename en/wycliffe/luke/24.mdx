<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And when he had said this thing, he showed hands and feet to them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they proffered &#91;or offered&#93; to him a part of a fish roasted, and a honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 10:41"> And when he had eaten before them, he took that that left &#91;or the remnants&#93;, and gave to them;</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 16:14; 1ST JOHN 5:20; PSALMS 119:18"> Then he opened to them wit, that they should understand &#91;the&#93; scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ISAIAH 53:2–12; ISAIAH 50:6; LUKE 24:44"> And he said to them, For thus it is written, and thus it behooved Christ to suffer, and rise again from death the third day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 5:31; ISAIAH 49:6; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38"> and penance, and remission of sins to be preached in his name to all folks, beginning at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:32; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:31"> And ye be witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:8; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:4; JOEL 2:28–32"> And I shall send the promise of my Father into you; but sit ye in the city, till ye be clothed with virtue from on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 1:12; MATTHEW 21:17; MARK 11:1"> And he led them forth into Bethany, and when his hands were lifted up, he blessed them.</span>
