<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1ST PETER 4:17–18; LAMENTATIONS 2:20; EZEKIEL 9:5–7"> And some men were present in that time, that told to him of the Galileans, whose blood Pilate mingled with the sacrifices of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 9:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 28:4; JOB 22:5–16"> And he answered, and said to them, Ween ye that these men of Galilee were sinners more than all Galileans, for they suffered such things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 3:19; MATTHEW 3:2; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 2:38–40"> I say to you, nay; all ye shall perish in like manner, but ye have penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 3:15; JOB 1:19; JOHN 9:11"> And as those eighteen, on which the tower in Siloam fell down, and slew them, guess ye, that they were debtors more than all men that dwell in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:30; LUKE 13:3; ISAIAH 28:10–13"> I say to you, nay; but also all ye shall perish, if ye do not penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 15:16; ISAIAH 5:1–4; GALATIANS 5:22"> And he said this likeness, A man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard, and he came seeking fruit in it, and found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:19; LUKE 3:9; JOHN 15:6"> And he said to the tiller of the vineyard, Lo! three years be, since I come seeking fruit in this fig tree, and I find none; therefore cut it down, whereto occupieth it the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2ND PETER 3:9; JOEL 2:17; ROMANS 10:1"> And he answering said to him, Lord, suffer it also this year, the while I delve about it, and I shall dung it &#91;or and dung it&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 15:2; 1ST THESSALONIANS 2:15; HEBREWS 6:8"> if it shall make fruit, &#91;else&#93; if not, in time coming thou shalt cut it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:23; LUKE 4:44; LUKE 4:15–16"> And he was teaching in their synagogue in the sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 8:2; LUKE 8:27; LUKE 13:16"> And lo! a woman, that had a spirit of sickness eighteen years, and was crooked &#91;or bowed down&#93;, and neither in any manner might look upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; MATTHEW 8:16; LUKE 6:8–10"> Whom when Jesus had seen, he called &#91;her&#93; to him, and said to her, Woman, thou art delivered of thy sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20–22; MARK 16:18; MARK 5:23"> And he set &#91;or put&#93; on her his hands, and anon she stood upright, and glorified God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 20:9; MATTHEW 12:2; JOHN 9:14–16"> And the prince of the synagogue answered, having disdain for Jesus had healed in the sabbath; and he said to the people, There be six days, in which it behooveth to work; therefore come ye in these, and be ye healed, and not in the day of sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 14:5; JOHN 7:21–24; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:9–10"> But the Lord answered to him, and said, Hypocrite, whether each of you untieth not in the sabbath his ox, or ass, from the cratch, &#91;or the stall&#93;, and leadeth to water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARK 2:27; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:26; LUKE 19:9"> Behooved it not this daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo! eighteen years, to be unbound of this bond in the day of sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1ST PETER 3:16; PSALMS 132:18; ISAIAH 45:24"> And when he said these things, all his adversaries were ashamed, and all the people joyed in all things, that were gloriously done of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARK 4:30–34; LUKE 17:21; MATTHEW 13:31–32"> Therefore he said, To what thing is the kingdom of God like? and to what thing shall I guess it to be like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 4:12; DANIEL 4:21; DANIEL 2:44–45"> It is like to a corn of sinapi, which a man took, and cast into his garden &#91;or into his yard&#93;; and it waxed, and was made into a great tree, and fowls of the air rested in the branches thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And again he said, To what thing shall I guess the kingdom of God like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:33; 1ST CORINTHIANS 5:6; 1ST THESSALONIANS 5:23–24"> It is like to sourdough that a woman took, and hid it in three measures of meal, till all were soured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 9:51; MATTHEW 9:35; MARK 10:32–34"> And he went by cities and castles, teaching and making journey into Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:14; MATTHEW 22:14; JOHN 21:21–22"> And a man said to him, Lord, if there be few, that be saved? And he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:13–14; 2ND PETER 1:10; 1ST CORINTHIANS 9:24–27"> Strive ye to enter by the strait gate; for I say to you, many seek to enter &#91;in&#93;, and they shall not be able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:10–12; LUKE 13:27; MATTHEW 7:21–23"> For when the husbandman is entered, and the door is closed, ye shall begin to stand withoutforth, and knock at the door, and say, Lord, open to us. And he shall answer, and say to you, I know you not, of whence ye be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ISAIAH 58:2; TITUS 1:16; 2ND TIMOTHY 3:5"> Then ye shall begin to say, We have eaten before thee and drunk, and in our streets thou hast taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 6:8; MATTHEW 25:41; MATTHEW 7:22–23"> And he shall say to you, I know you not, of whence ye be; go away from me, all ye workers of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:11–12; MATTHEW 22:13; MATTHEW 25:30"> There shall be weeping and grinding of teeth, when ye shall see Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, and all the prophets in the kingdom of God; and you to be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ISAIAH 43:6; ISAIAH 66:18–20; ISAIAH 49:6"> And they shall come from the east and the west, and from the north and the south, and shall sit at the meat in the realm of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:30; MARK 10:31; MATTHEW 20:16"> And lo! they that were the first, be the last; and they that were the last, be the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NEHEMIAH 6:9–11; PSALMS 11:1–2; MATTHEW 14:1"> In that day some of the Pharisees came nigh, and said to him, Go out, and go from hence, for Herod will slay thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:10; HEBREWS 5:9; JOHN 17:4–5"> And he said to them, Go ye, and say to that fox, Lo! I cast out fiends, and I make perfectly healings, today and tomorrow, and the third day I am ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 9:53; MATTHEW 21:11; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 13:27"> Nevertheless it behooveth me today, and tomorrow, and the day that pursueth, to walk; for it falleth not &#91;for&#93; a prophet to perish out of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:37–39; DEUTERONOMY 32:11–12; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 7:52"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that slayest prophets, and stonest them that be sent to thee, how oft would I gather together thy sons, as a bird gathereth his nest under feathers &#91;or under wings&#93;, and thou wouldest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 118:26; MARK 11:9–10; MATTHEW 21:9"> Lo! your house shall be left to you desert. And I say to you, that ye shall not see me, till it come, when ye shall say, Blessed is he, that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span>
