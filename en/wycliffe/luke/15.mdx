<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And he spake to them this parable, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:16; EZEKIEL 34:11–12; MATTHEW 18:12–14"> What man of you that hath an hundred sheep, and if he hath lost one of them, whether he leaveth not ninety and nine in desert, and goeth to it that perished, till he find it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:2; LUKE 19:9; ISAIAH 46:3–4"> And when he hath found it, he joyeth, and layeth &#91;or putteth&#93; it on his shoulders;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 15:10; LUKE 15:24; 1ST PETER 2:25"> and he cometh home, and calleth together his friends and neighbours, and saith to them, Be ye glad with me, for I have found my sheep, that had perished &#91;or I had lost&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 5:32; LUKE 15:10; LUKE 15:32"> And I say to you, so joy shall be in heaven on one sinful man doing penance, more than on ninety and nine just, that have no need to &#91;or of&#93; penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; EZEKIEL 34:12; JOHN 10:16"> Or what woman having ten bezants &#91;or ten drachmas&#93;, and if she hath lost one bezant &#91;or one drachma&#93;, whether she tendeth not &#91;or lighteth not&#93; a lantern, and turneth upside-down the house, and seeketh diligently, till she find it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 15:6–7"> And when she hath found, she calleth together friends and neighbours, and saith, Be ye glad with me, for I have found the bezant &#91;or the drachma&#93; that I had lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 15:7; 2ND CORINTHIANS 7:10; EZEKIEL 33:11"> So I say to you, joy shall be before the angels of God on one sinful man doing penance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:23–31"> And he said, A man had two sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:16–17; MARK 12:44; LUKE 15:30"> and the younger of them said to the father &#91;or to his father&#93;, Father, give me the portion of chattel &#91;or substance&#93; that falleth to me. And he parted to them the chattel &#91;or the substance&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:3; JEREMIAH 2:5; PROVERBS 21:17"> And not after many days, when all things were gathered together, the younger son went forth in pilgrimage into a far country; and there he wasted his goods &#91;or his substance&#93; in living lecherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2ND CHRONICLES 33:11; AMOS 8:9–12; HOSEA 2:9–14"> And after that he had ended all things, a strong hunger was made in that country, and he began to have need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TITUS 3:3; 2ND TIMOTHY 2:25–26; ISAIAH 1:5"> And he went, and drew him to one of the citizens of that country. And he sent him into his town, to feed swine &#91;or hogs&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 6:19–21; LAMENTATIONS 4:5; HOSEA 12:1"> And he coveted to fill his womb of the pods that the hogs ate, and no man gave to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:28; EPHESIANS 2:4–5; LUKE 15:18–19"> And he turned again to himself, and said, How many hired men in my father’s house have plenty of loaves; and I perish here through hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:40; 1ST JOHN 1:8–10; LUKE 15:21"> I shall rise up, and go to my father, and I shall say to him, Father, I have sinned into &#91;or against&#93; heaven, and before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 42:6; LUKE 5:8; PSALMS 84:10"> and now I am not worthy to be called thy son, make me as one of thine hired men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 103:10–13; PSALMS 86:5; PSALMS 86:15"> And he rose up, and came to his father. And when he was yet afar, his father saw him, and was stirred by mercy. And he ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; LUKE 15:18–19; JEREMIAH 3:13"> And the son said to him, Father, I have sinned into &#91;or against&#93; heaven, and before thee; and now I am not worthy to be called thy son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 41:42; ESTHER 8:2; APOCALYPSE of JOHN 6:11"> And the father said to his servants, Swiftly bring ye forth the first stole, and clothe ye him, and give ye a ring in his hand, and shoes on his feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ISAIAH 25:6; MATTHEW 22:2–14; PSALMS 63:5"> and bring ye a fat calf &#91;or a calf made fat&#93;, and slay ye, and eat we, and make we feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:5; EZEKIEL 34:16; LUKE 19:10"> For this my son was dead, and hath lived again; he perished, and is found. And all men began to eat &#91;gladly&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 150:4; JEREMIAH 31:4; LUKE 15:11–12"> But his elder son was in the field; and when he came, and nighed to the house, he heard a symphony and a crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he called one of the servants, and asked, what these things were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEEDS OF APOSTLES 9:17; PHILEMON 1:16; DEEDS OF APOSTLES 22:13"> And he said to him, Thy brother is come, and thy father slew a fat &#91;or a fatted&#93; calf, for he received him safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JONAH 4:9; 1ST SAMUEL 18:8; JONAH 4:1–4"> And he was wroth, and would not come in. Therefore his father went out, and began to pray him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 18:11–12; ZECHARIAH 7:3; ISAIAH 65:5"> And he answered to his father, and said, Lo! so many years I serve thee, and I never brake thy commandment; and thou never gave to me a kid, that I with my friends should have eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:3; LUKE 15:12–13; LUKE 15:32"> But after that this thy son, that hath devoured his substance with whores, came, thou hast slain to him a fat &#91;or a fatted&#93; calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARK 7:27–28; ROMANS 9:4; ROMANS 11:1"> And he said to him, Son, thou art evermore with me, and all my things be thine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:1–10; LUKE 15:24; ISAIAH 35:10"> But it behooved to make feast, and to have joy; for this thy brother was dead, and lived again &#91;or liveth again&#93;; he perished, and is found.</span>
