<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 14:23; Esias 13:1; Jeremias 51:42"> THE VISION OF THE DESERT. As though a whirlwind should pass through the desert, coming from a desert, even from such a land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 33:1; Jeremias 49:34; Psalms 60:3"> so a fearful and a grievous vision was declared to me: he that is treacherous deals treacherously, the transgressor transgresses. The Elamites are upon me, and the ambassadors of the Persians come against me: now will I groan and comfort myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 13:8; Psalms 48:6; Esias 26:17"> Therefore are my loins filled with feebleness, and pangs have seized me as a travailing woman: I dealt wrongfully that I might not hear; I hasted that I might not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:34–36; Kings I 25:36–38; Daniel 5:1"> My heart wanders, and transgression<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. baptises">a</a></sup>overwhelms me; my soul is occupied with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 51:11; Jeremias 51:57; Jeremias 51:39"> Prepare the table, eat, drink: arise, you⌃ princes, and prepare your shields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:2–7; Jezekiel 3:17; Ambacum 2:1–2"> For thus said the Lord to me, Go and station a watchman for yourself, and declare whatever you shall see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 21:9; Esias 37:24; Hebrews 2:1"> And I saw two mounted horsemen, and a rider on an ass, and a rider on a camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ambacum 2:1–2; Esias 56:10; 1 Peter 5:8"> Listen with great attention, and call you Urias to the watch-tower: the Lord has spoken. I stood continually during the day, and I stood in the camp all night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 14:8; Jeremias 51:8"> and, behold, he comes riding in a chariot and pair: and he answered and said, Babylon is fallen, is fallen; and all her images and her idols have been crushed to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 51:33; Michaeas 4:13; Kings IV 13:7"> Hear, you⌃ that are left, and you⌃ that are in pain, hear what things I have heard of the Lord of hosts which the God of Israel has declared to us. THE VISION OF IDUMEA.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:14; Obdias 1:1–16; Amos 1:6"> Call to me out of Seir; guard you⌃ the bulwarks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:30–32; Acts 2:37–38; Jezekiel 18:30–32"> I watch in the morning and the night: if you would enquire, enquire, and dwell by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 25:23–24; Chronicles I 1:32; Jezekiel 27:15"> You may lodge in the forest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. of Arabia">b</a></sup>in the evening, or in the way of Daedan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Job 6:19; Romans 12:20"> You⌃ that dwell in the country of Thaeman, bring water to meet him that is thirsty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:19–20; Esias 13:14"> meet the fugitives with bread, because of the multitude of the slain, and because of the multitude of them that lose their way, and because of the multitude of swords, and because of the multitude of bent bows, and because of the multitude of them that have fallen in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; Esias 60:7; Esias 16:14"> For thus said the Lord to me, Yet a year, as the year of an hireling, and the glory of the sons of Kedar shall fail:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zacharias 1:6; Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35"> and the remnant of the strong bows of the sons of Kedar shall be small: for the Lord God of Israel has spoken it.</span>
