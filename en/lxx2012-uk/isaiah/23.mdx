<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:4–8; Amos 1:9–10; Genesis 10:4"> THE WORD CONCERNING TYRE. Howl, you⌃ ships of Carthage; for she has perished, and men no longer arrive from the land of the Citians: she is led captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 47:5; Esias 41:1; Jezekiel 28:2"> To whom are the dwellers in the island become like, the merchants of Phoenice, passing over the sea</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 2:18; Chronicles I 13:5; Jezekiel 27:33"> in great waters, a generation of merchants? as when the harvest is gathered in, so are these traders with the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Genesis 10:15; Jeremias 47:3–4"> Be ashamed, O Sidon: the sea has said, yes, the strength of the sea has said, I have not travailed, nor brought forth, nor have I brought up young men, nor reared virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Exodus 15:14–16; Revelation 18:17–19"> Moreover when it shall be heard in Egypt, sorrow shall seize them for Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 23:12; Esias 21:15; Esias 23:10"> Depart you⌃ to Carthage; howl, you⌃ that dwell in this island.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 22:2; Esias 32:13; Joshua 19:29"> Was not this your pride from the beginning, before she was given up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 10:8; Jeremias 50:44–45; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> Who has devised this counsel against Tyre? Is she inferior? or has she no strength? her merchants were the glorious princes of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 4:6; Esias 13:11; Job 40:11–12"> The Lord of hosts has purposed to bring down all the pride of the glorious ones, and to disgrace every glorious thing on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 12:21; Esias 23:14; Psalms 18:32"> Till your land; for ships no more come out of Carthage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zacharias 9:3–4; Exodus 14:21; Jeremias 47:7"> And your hand prevails no more by sea, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See chap 5. 25; 14:16.">a</a></sup>troubled kings: the Lord of hosts has given a command concerning Chanaan, to destroy the strength thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 18:22; Jezekiel 26:13–14; Esias 23:1–2"> And men shall say, You⌃ shall no longer at all continue to insult and injure the daughter of Sidon: and if you depart to the Citians, neither there shall you have rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ambacum 1:6; Esias 10:5; Kings IV 17:24"> And if you depart to the land of the Chaldeans, this also is laid waste by the Assyrians, for her wall is fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 23:1; Esias 23:6; Jezekiel 27:25–30"> Howl, you⌃ ships of Carthage: for your strong hold is destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 25:22; Jeremias 25:9–11; Daniel 7:14"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that Tyre shall be left seventy years, as the time of a king, as the time of a man: and it shall come to pass after seventy years, that Tyre shall be as the song of a harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 30:14; Proverbs 7:10–12"> Take a harp, go about, O city, you harlot that have been forgotten; play well on the harp, sing many songs, that you may be remembered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Naum 3:4; Jezekiel 27:6–36; Revelation 19:2"> And it shall come to pass after the seventy years, that God will visit Tyre, and she shall be again restored to her primitive state, and she shall be a mart for all the kingdoms of the world on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; Deuteronomy 26:12–14; Philippians 4:17–18"> And her trade and her gain shall be holiness to the Lord: it shall not be gathered for them, but for those that dwell before the Lord, even all her trade, to eat and drink and be filled, and for a covenant and a memorial before the Lord.</span>
