<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 18:1–21; Chronicles II 26:1–33; Esias 6:1"> The vision which Esaias the son of Amos saw, which he saw against Juda, and against Jerusalem, in the reign of Ozias, and Joatham, and Achaz, and Ezekias, who reigned over Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 30:19; Michaeas 1:2"> Hear, O heaven, and listen, O earth: for the Lord has spoken, saying, I have begotten and reared up children, but they have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, 'set me at nothing.'">a</a></sup>rebelled against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 8:7; Jeremias 4:22; Esias 44:18"> The ox knows his owner, and the ass his master's crib: but Israel does not know me, and the people has not regarded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 5:24; Jeremias 2:17; Jeremias 2:13"> Ah sinful nation, a people full of sins, an evil seed, lawless children: you⌃ have forsaken the Lord, and provoked the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:8–11; Jezekiel 24:13; Jeremias 5:3"> Why should you⌃ be struck any more, transgressing more and more? the whole head is pained, and the whole heart sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 6:14; Jeremias 33:6; Matthew 9:12"> From the feet to the head, there is no soundness in them; neither wound, nor bruise, nor festering ulcer are healed: it is not possible to apply a plaister, nor oil, nor bandages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 5:2; Deuteronomy 28:43; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Your land is desolate, your cities burnt with fire: your land, strangers devour it in your presence, and it is made desolate, overthrown by strange nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zacharias 9:9; John 12:15; Zacharias 2:10"> The daughter of Sion shall be deserted as a tent in a vineyard, and as a storehouse of fruits in a garden of cucumbers, as a besieged city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 9:29; Esias 37:31–32; Genesis 19:24"> And if the Lord of Sabaoth had not left us a seed, we should have been as Sodom, and we should have been made like Gomorrha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:8; Jezekiel 16:49; Amos 9:7"> Hear the word of the Lord, you⌃ rulers of Sodoma; attend to the law of God, you people of Gomorrha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 15:22; Amos 5:21; Jeremias 6:20"> Of what value to me is the abundance of your sacrifices? says the Lord: I am full of whole burnt offerings of rams; and I delight not in the fat of lambs, and the blood of bulls and goats:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:17; Michaeas 6:8"> neither shall you⌃ come with these to appear before me; for who has required these things at your hands? You⌃ shall no more tread my court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 66:3; Malachias 1:10; Matthew 15:9"> Though you⌃ bring fine flour, it is vain; incense is an abomination to me; I can’t bear your new moons, and your sabbaths, and the great day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:21; Esias 43:24; Esias 7:13"> your fasting, and rest from work, your new moons also, and your feasts my soul hates: you⌃ have become loathsome to me; I will no more pardon your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Michaeas 3:4; Esias 59:2–3; Proverbs 1:28"> When you⌃ stretch forth your hands, I will turn away my eyes from you: and though you⌃ make many supplications, I will not listen to you; for your hands are full of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Esias 55:6–7; 1 Peter 2:1"> Wash you, be clean; remove your iniquities from your souls before my eyes; cease from your iniquities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Michaeas 6:8; Jeremias 22:3; Psalms 82:3–4"> learn to do well; diligently seek judgement, deliver him that is suffering wrong, plead for the orphan, and obtain justice for the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 44:22; Revelation 7:14; Psalms 51:7"> And come, let us reason together, says the Lord: and though your sins be as purple, I will make them white as snow; and though they be as scarlet, I will make them white as wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15–16; Esias 3:10; Joel 2:26"> And if you⌃ be willing, and listen to me, you⌃ shall eat the good of the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 65:12; Esias 58:14; Numbers 23:19"> but if you⌃ be not willing, nor listen to me, a sword shall devour you: for the mouth of the Lord has spoken this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:1–23; Zacharias 8:3; Sophonias 3:1–3"> How has the faithful city Sion, once full of judgement, become a harlot! wherein righteousness lodged, but now murderers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 6:28–30; Lamentations 4:1–2; Osee 4:18"> Your silver is worthless, your wine merchants mix the wine with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Michaeas 7:3; Exodus 23:8; Zacharias 7:10"> Your princes are rebellious, companions of thieves, loving bribes, seeking after rewards; not pleading for orphans, and not heeding the cause of widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 60:16; Esias 35:4; Deuteronomy 28:63"> Therefore thus says the Lord, the Lord of hosts, Woe to the mighty men of Israel; for my wrath shall not cease against my adversaries, and I will execute judgement on my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Malachias 3:3; Jeremias 9:7; Esias 4:4"> And I will bring my hand upon you, and purge you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. to pureness.">b</a></sup>completely, and I will destroy the rebellious, and will take away from you all transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 33:7; Zacharias 8:3; Esias 62:1–2"> And I will establish your judges as before, and your counsellors as at the beginning: and afterward you shall be called the city of righteousness, the faithful mother-city of Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esias 62:12; Esias 45:21–25; Esias 5:16"> For her captives shall be saved with judgement, and with mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8–9; Psalms 1:6; Esias 30:13"> And the transgressors and the sinners shall be crushed together, and they that forsake the Lord shall be utterly consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esias 65:3; Esias 57:5; Esias 66:17"> For they shall be ashamed of their idols, which they delighted in, and they are made ashamed of the gardens which they coveted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 17:5–6; Jezekiel 17:9–10; Matthew 21:19"> For they shall be as a turpentine tree that has cast its leaves, and as a garden that has no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esias 66:24; Jezekiel 32:21; Malachias 4:1"> And their strength shall be as a thread of tow, and their works as sparks, and the transgressors and the sinners shall be burnt up together, and there shall be none to quench them.</span>
