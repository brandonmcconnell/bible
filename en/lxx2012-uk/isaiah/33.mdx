<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Esias 24:16; Esias 10:12"> Woe to them that afflict you; but no one makes you miserable: and he that deals perfidiously with you does not deal perfidiously: they that deal perfidiously shall be taken and given up, and as a moth on a garment, so shall they be spoiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; Esias 59:16; Esias 30:18–19"> Lord, have mercy upon us; for we have trusted in you: the seed of the rebellious is gone to destruction, but our deliverance was in a time of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 10:13–14; Esias 10:32–34; Esias 37:11–18"> By reason of the terrible sound the nations were dismayed for fear of you, and the heathen were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 33:23; Joel 2:25; Chronicles II 20:25"> And now shall the spoils of your small and great be gathered: as if one should gather locusts, so shall they mock you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 2:17; Ephesians 1:20–21; Esias 32:15–18"> The God who dwells on high is holy: Sion is filled with judgement and righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Proverbs 19:23; 1 Timothy 4:8"> They shall be delivered up to the law: our salvation is our treasure: there are wisdom and knowledge and piety towards the Lord; these are the treasures of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 18:18; Kings IV 18:37–3; Esias 36:22"> Behold now, these shall be terrified with fear of you: those whom you⌃ feared shall cry out because of you: messengers shall be sent, bitterly weeping, entreating for peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Esias 35:8; Lamentations 1:4"> For the ways of these shall be made desolate: the terror of the nations has been made to cease, and the covenant with these is taken away, and you⌃ shall by no means deem them men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 35:2; Naum 1:4; Esias 65:10"> The land mourns; Libanus is ashamed: Saron is become marshes; Galilee shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. manifest">a</a></sup>laid bare, and Chermel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 102:13–18; Amos 6:1"> Now will I arise, says the Lord, now will I be glorified; now will I be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Esias 59:4; James 1:15"> Now shall you⌃ see, now shall you⌃ perceive; the strength of your breath, shall be vain; fire shall devour you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 27:4; Kings II 23:6–7; Esias 10:17"> And the nations shall be burnt up; as a thorn in the field cast out and burnt up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 48:10; Ephesians 2:11–18; Daniel 3:27–3"> They that are afar off shall hear what I have done; they that draw near shall know my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Matthew 25:46; Esias 66:24"> The sinners in Sion have departed; trembling shall seize the ungodly. Who will tell you that a fire is kindled? Who will tell you of the eternal place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; 1 John 3:7; Ephesians 5:11–13"> He that walks in righteousness, speaking rightly, hating transgression and iniquity, and shaking his hands from gifts, stopping his ears that he should not hear the judgement of blood, shutting his eyes that he should not see injustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:29–31; Esias 32:18; Proverbs 1:33"> he shall dwell in a high cave of a strong rock: bread shall be given him, and his water shall be sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zacharias 9:17; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> You⌃ shall see a king with glory: your eyes shall behold a land from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 31:7–8; Esias 38:9–22"> Your soul shall meditate terror. Where are the scribes? where are the counsellors, where is he that numbers them that are growing up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 28:11; Deuteronomy 28:49–50; Jeremias 5:15"> even the small and great people? with whom he took not counsel, neither did he understand a people of deep speech, so that a despised people should not hear, and there is no understanding to him that hears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:5; Esias 32:18; Esias 54:2"> Behold the city of Sion,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, salvation">b</a></sup>our refuge: your eyes shall behold Jerusalem, a rich city, tabernacles which shall not be shaken, neither shall the pins of her tabernacle be moved for ever, neither shall her cords be at all broken:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 46:4–5; Esias 48:18; Esias 66:12"> for the name of the Lord is great to you: you⌃ shall have a place, even rivers and wide and spacious channels: you shall not go this way, neither a vessel with oars go thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 5:31; James 4:12; Sophonias 3:15–17"> For my God is great: the Lord our judge shall not pass me by: the Lord is our prince, the Lord is our king; the Lord, he shall save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 7:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Corinthians 1:27"> Your cords are broken, for they had no strength: your meat has given way, it shall not spread the sails, it shall not bear a signal, until it be given up for plunder; therefore shall many lame men take spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 50:20; 1 John 1:7–9; Revelation 21:4"> And the people dwelling amongst them shall by no means say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, I am sick">c</a></sup>I am in pain: for their sin shall be forgiven them.</span>
