<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 1:5; Galatians 1:15; Esias 44:2"> Listen to me, you⌃ islands; and attend, you⌃ Gentiles; after a long time it shall come to pass, says the Lord: from my mother's womb he has called my name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 1:16; Esias 50:4"> and he has made my mouth as a sharp sword, and he has hid me under the shadow of his hand; he has made me as a choice shaft, and he has hid me in his quiver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 42:1; 1 Peter 2:9; Esias 44:23"> and said to me, You are my servant, O Israel, and in you I will be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 35:4; Galatians 4:11; Esias 53:10–12"> Then I said, I have laboured in vain, I have given my strength for vanity and for nothing: therefore is my judgement with the Lord, and my labour before my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Matthew 17:5; John 3:35"> And now, thus says the Lord that formed me from the womb to be his own servant, to gather Jacob to him and Israel. I shall be gathered and glorified before the Lord, and my God shall be my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Esias 42:6; Luke 2:32"> And he said to me, It is a great thing for you to be called my servant, to establish the tribes of Jacob, and to recover the dispersion of Israel: behold, I have given you for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. a perpetual covenant.">a</a></sup>covenant of a race, for a light of the Gentiles, that you should be for salvation to the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; 1 Peter 2:4; Esias 53:3"> Thus says the Lord that delivered you, the God of Israel, Sanctify him that despises his life, him that is abhorred by the nations that are the servants of princes: kings shall behold him, and princes shall arise, and shall worship him, for the Lord's sake: for the Holy One of Israel is faithful, and I have chosen you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Esias 42:6; Psalms 69:13"> Thus says the Lord, In an acceptable time have I heard you, and in a day of salvation have I succored you: and I have formed you, and given you for a covenant of the nations, to establish the earth, and to cause to inherit the desert heritages:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 42:7; Esias 42:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> saying to them that are in bonds, Go forth; and bidding them that are in darkness show themselves. They shall be fed in all the ways, and in all the paths shall be their pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Matthew 5:6; Jezekiel 34:23"> They shall not hunger, neither shall they thirst; neither shall the heat nor the sun strike them; but he that has mercy on them shall comfort them, and by fountains of waters shall he lead them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 11:16; Esias 62:10; Esias 35:8–10"> And I will make every mountain a way, and every path a pasture to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 43:5–6; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 11:15"> Behold, these shall come from far: and these from the north and the west, and others from the land of the Persians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 44:23; Esias 51:3; Psalms 96:11–13"> Rejoice, you⌃ heavens; and let the earth be glad: let the mountains break forth with joy; for the Lard has had mercy on his people, and has comforted the lowly ones of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 40:27; Romans 11:1–5; Psalms 22:1"> But Sion said, The Lord has forsaken me, and, The Lord has forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 44:21; Psalms 103:13; Matthew 7:11"> Will a woman forget her child, so as not to have compassion upon the offspring of her womb? but if a woman should even forget these, yet I will not forget you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Aggaeus 2:23; Esias 62:6; Esias 60:18"> Behold, I have painted your walls on my hands, and you are continually before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 51:13; Esias 62:5; Jezekiel 28:24"> And you shall soon be built by those by whom you were destroyed, and they that made you desolate shall go forth of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 54:1–3; Esias 66:12–13; Esias 61:10"> Lift up your eyes round about, and look on them all; behold, they are gathered together, and are come to you. As I live, says the Lord, you shall clothe yourself with them all as with an ornament, and put them on as a bride her attire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zacharias 10:10; Esias 54:1–2; Jeremias 30:18–19"> For your desert and marred and ruined places shall now be too narrow by reason of the inhabitants, and they that devoured you shall be removed far from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Osee 1:10; Joshua 17:14–16; Galatians 4:26–28"> For your sons whom you have lost shall say in your ears, The place is too narrow for me: make room for me that I may dwell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 5:13; Esias 3:26; Lamentations 1:1–3"> And you shall say in your heart, Who has begotten me these? whereas I was childless, and a widow; but who has brought up these for me? and I was left alone; but whence came these to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Luke 13:29; Esias 14:2"> Thus says the Lord, even the Lord, Behold, I lift up my hand to the nations, and I will lift up my signal to the islands: and they shall bring your sons in their bosom, and shall bear your daughters on their shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 60:14; Esias 60:16; Esias 60:3"> And kings shall be your nursing fathers, and their princesses your nurses, they shall bow down to you on the face of the earth, and shall lick the dust of your feet; and you shall know that I am the Lord, and they that wait on me shall not be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:21–22; Psalms 124:6–7; Matthew 12:29"> Will any one take spoils from a giant? and if one should take a man captive unjustly, shall he be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 54:15–17; Esias 35:4; Esias 41:11–12"> For thus says the Lord, If one should take a giant captive, he shall take spoils, and he who takes them from a mighty man shall be delivered: for I will plead your cause, and I will deliver your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Esias 9:20; Esias 41:14–20; Revelation 16:6"> And they that afflicted you shall eat their own flesh; and they shall drink their own blood as new wine, and shall be drunken: and all flesh shall perceive that I am the Lord that delivers you, and that upholds the strength of Jacob.</span>
