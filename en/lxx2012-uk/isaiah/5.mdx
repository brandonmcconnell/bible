<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:1; Luke 20:9; John 15:1"> Now I will sing to my beloved a song of my beloved concerning my vineyard. My beloved had a vineyard on a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. horn, so Heb.">a</a></sup>high hill in a fertile place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 2:21; Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Mark 11:13"> And I made a hedge round it, and dug a trench, and planted a choice vine, and built a tower in the midst of it, and dug a place for the wine-vat in it: and I waited for it to bring forth grapes, and it brought forth thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Psalms 51:4; Michaeas 6:2–3"> And now, you⌃ dwellers in Jerusalem, and every man of Juda, judge between me and my vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 23:37; Michaeas 6:3–4; Jeremias 2:5"> What shall I do any more to my vineyard, that I have not done to it? Whereas I expected it to bring forth grapes, but it has brought forth thorns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Luke 21:24; Esias 28:18"> And now I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard: I will take away its hedge, and it shall be for a spoil; and I will pull down its walls, and it shall be left to be trodden down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 7:23–25; Jeremias 25:11; Esias 24:1–3"> And I will forsake my vineyard; and it shall not be pruned, nor dug, and thorns shall come up upon it as on barren land; and I will command the clouds to rain no rain upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 80:8–11; Jeremias 12:10; James 5:4"> For the vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. a man.">b</a></sup>the men of Juda his beloved plant: I expected it to bring forth judgement, and it brought forth iniquity; and not righteousness, but a cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Michaeas 2:2; Ambacum 2:9–12; Jeremias 22:13–17"> Woe to them that join house to house, and add field to field, that they may take away something of their neighbour's: will you⌃ dwell alone upon the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 23:38; Esias 22:14; Amos 5:11"> For these things have reached the ears of the Lord of hosts: for though many houses should be built, many and fair houses shall be desolate, and there shall be no inhabitants in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Aggaeus 2:16; Aggaeus 1:6"> For where ten yoke of oxen plough the land shall yield one , and he that sows six homers shall produce three measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–30; Romans 13:13; Proverbs 20:1"> Woe to them that rise up in the morning, and follow strong drink; who wait at it till evening: for the wine shall inflame them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:11–14; Psalms 28:5; Amos 6:4–6"> For they drink wine with harp, and lute, and drums, and pipes: but they regard not the works of the Lord, and consider not the works of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 4:6; Esias 1:3; Esias 27:11"> Therefore my people have been taken captive, because they know not the Lord: and there has been a multitude of dead bodies, because of hunger and of thirst for water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 30:16; Luke 21:34; Ambacum 2:5"> Therefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. Hades.">c</a></sup>hell has enlarged its desire and opened its mouth without ceasing: and her glorious and great, and her rich and her pestilent men shall go down into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 2:11; Esias 2:9; Jeremias 5:4–5"> And the mean man shall be brought low, and the great man shall be disgraced, and the lofty eyes shall be brought low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; Esias 12:4; Chronicles I 29:11"> But the Lord of hosts shall be exalted in judgement, and the holy God shall be glorified in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Sophonias 2:6; Sophonias 2:14; Esias 7:25"> And they that were spoiled shall be fed as bulls, and lambs shall feed on the waste places of them that are taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 23:14; Jezekiel 13:22; Jeremias 5:31"> Woe to them that draw sins to them as with a long rope, and iniquities as with a thong of the heifer's yoke:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:22; Jeremias 17:15; Jeremias 23:36"> who say, Let him speedily hasten what he will do, that we may see it: and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel come, that we may know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; 2 Timothy 3:1–5; Luke 16:15"> Woe to them that call evil good, and good evil; who make darkness light, and light darkness; who make bitter sweet, and sweet bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:18–20; Proverbs 3:7; Romans 12:16"> Woe to them that are wise in their own conceit, and knowing in their own sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esias 5:11; Ambacum 2:15; Esias 28:7"> Woe to the strong ones of you that drink wine, and the mighty ones that mingle strong drink:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 10:2; James 5:6; Psalms 94:21"> who justify the ungodly for rewards, and take away the righteousness of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 30:12; Job 18:16; Joel 2:5"> Therefore as stubble shall be burnt by a coal of fire, and shall be consumed by a violent flame, their root shall be as chaff, and their flower shall go up as dust: for they rejected the law of the Lord of hosts, and insulted the word of the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 10:4; Esias 9:21; Esias 9:17"> Therefore the Lord of hosts was greatly angered against his people, and he reached forth his hand upon them, and struck them: and the mountains were troubled, and their carcasses were as dung in the midst of the way: yet for all this his anger has not been turned away, but his hand is yet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. High.">d</a></sup>raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Zacharias 10:8; Deuteronomy 28:49; Esias 7:18"> Therefore shall he lift up a signal to the nations that are afar, and shall hiss for them from the end of the earth; and, behold, they are coming very quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joel 2:7–8; Job 12:18; Job 12:21"> They shall not hunger nor be weary, neither shall they slumber nor sleep; neither shall they loose their girdles from their loins, neither shall their shoe-latchets be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 45:5; Naum 2:3–4; Jezekiel 21:9–11"> Whose arrows are sharp, and their bows bent; their horses' hoofs are counted as solid rock: their chariot-wheels are as a storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esias 42:22; Zacharias 11:3; Michaeas 5:8"> They rage as lions, and draw near as a lion's whelps: and he shall seize, and roar as a wild beast, and he shall cast them forth, and there shall be none to deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Esias 8:22; Joel 2:10; Jeremias 4:23–28"> And he shall roar on account of them in that day, as the sound of the swelling sea; and they shall look to the land, and, behold, there shall be thick darkness in their perplexity.</span>
