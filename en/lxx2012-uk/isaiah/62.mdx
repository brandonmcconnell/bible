<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 62:6–7; Proverbs 4:18; Esias 32:15–17"> For Sion's sake I will not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem's sake I will not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. relax.">a</a></sup>rest, until her righteousness go forth as light, and my salvation burn as a torch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 62:12; Genesis 17:5; Esias 66:12"> And the Gentiles shall see your righteousness, and kings your glory: and one shall call you by a new name, which the Lord shall name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 28:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:19; Zacharias 9:16"> And you shall be a crown of beauty in the hand of the Lord, and a royal diadem in the hand of your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Sophonias 3:17; Osee 2:19–20; Esias 62:12"> And you shall no more be called Forsaken; and your land shall no more be called Desert: for you shall be called My Pleasure, and your land Inhabited: for the Lord has taken pleasure in you, and your land shall be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 62:4; Esias 65:19; Hebrews 12:2"> And as a young man lives with a virgin, so shall your sons dwell in you: and it shall come to pass that as a bridegroom will rejoice over a bride, so will the Lord rejoice over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 52:8; Jezekiel 3:17–21; Psalms 134:1–2"> And on your walls, O Jerusalem, have I set watchmen all day and all night, who shall never cease making mention of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 33:9; Sophonias 3:19–20; Esias 60:18"> For there is none like you, when he shall have established, and made Jerusalem a praise on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 65:21–23; Judges 6:3–6; Deuteronomy 28:33"> For the Lord has sworn by his glory, and by the might of his arm, I will no more give your corn and your provisions to your enemies; nor shall strangers any more drink your wine, for which you have laboured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7"> But they that have gathered them shall eat them, and they shall praise the Lord; and they that have gathered the grapes shall drink thereof in my holy courts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 57:14; Esias 40:3; Hebrews 12:13"> Go through my gates, and make a way for my people; and cast the stones out of the way; lift up a standard for the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zacharias 9:9; Matthew 21:5; John 12:15"> For behold, the Lord has proclaimed to the end of the earth, say you⌃ to the daughter of Sion, Behold, your Saviour has come to you, having his reward and his work before his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 26:19; Esias 62:4"> And one shall call them the holy people, the redeemed of the Lord: and you shall be called a city sought out, and not forsaken.</span>
