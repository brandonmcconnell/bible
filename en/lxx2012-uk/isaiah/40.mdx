<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Esias 51:12; Esias 51:3"> Comfort you⌃, comfort you⌃ my people, says God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zacharias 9:12; Esias 61:7; Osee 2:14"> Speak, you⌃ priests, to the heart of Jerusalem; comfort her, for her humiliation is accomplished, her sin is put away: for she has received of the Lord's hand double the amount of her sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachias 3:1; Mark 1:2–5; John 1:23"> The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare you⌃ the way of the Lord, make straight the paths of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 3:5; Jezekiel 21:26; Luke 18:14"> Every valley shall be filled, and every mountain and hill shall be brought low:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Luke 3. 5, with which Alex. agrees">a</a></sup>and all the crooked ways shall become straight, and the rough places plains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ambacum 2:14; Joel 2:28; Esias 52:10"> And the glory of the Lord shall appear, and all flesh shall see the salvation of God: for the Lord has spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:15–16; 1 Peter 1:24–25; James 1:10–11"> The voice of one saying, Cry; and I said, What shall I cry? All flesh is grass, and all the glory of man as the flower of grass:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 13:31; 1 Peter 1:25; Matthew 24:35"> The grass withers, and the flower fades: but the word of our God abides for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 52:7–8; Esias 12:2; Esias 25:9"> O you that bring glad tidings to Zion, go up on the high mountain; lift up your voice with strength, you that bring glad tidings to Jerusalem; lift it up, fear not; say to the cities of Juda, Behold your God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:12; Esias 62:11; Revelation 17:14"> Behold the Lord! The Lord is coming with strength, and his arm is with power: behold, his reward is with him, and his work before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; Michaeas 5:4; Psalms 23:1–6"> He shall tend his flock as a shepherd, and he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and shall soothe them that are with young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25–26"> Who has measured the water in his hand, and the heaven with a span, and all the earth in a handful? Who has weighed the mountains in scales, and the forests in a balance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Romans 11:34; Job 21:22"> Who has known the mind of the Lord? and who has been his counsellor, to instruct him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 21:22; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 12:4–6"> Or with whom has he taken counsel, and he has instructed him? or who has taught him judgement, or who has taught him the way of understanding;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. + Or who has first given to him, and it shall be recompensed to him again? Ro 11. 35; Heb. omits.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 40:22; Job 34:14–15; Jeremias 10:10"> since all the nations are counted as a drop from a bucket, and as the turning of a balance, and shall be counted as spittle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Michaeas 6:6–7; Psalms 40:6; Psalms 50:9–12"> And Libanus is not enough to burn, nor all beasts enough for a whole burnt offering:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Daniel 4:34–35; Esias 29:7"> and all the nations are as nothing, and counted as nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 46:5; Kings I 2:2; Esias 40:25"> To whom have you⌃ compared the Lord? and with what likeness have you⌃ compared him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 10:9; Ambacum 2:18–19; Esias 46:6–7"> Has not the artificer made an image, or the goldsmith having melted gold, gilt it over, and made it a similitude?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 10:3–4; Esias 41:7; Esias 46:7"> For the artificer chooses out a wood that will not rot, and will wisely enquire how he shall set up his image, and that so that it should not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 48:13; Psalms 50:6; Acts 14:17"> Will you⌃ not know? will you⌃ not hear? has it not been told you of old? Have you⌃ not known the foundations of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:27; Psalms 104:2; Esias 42:5"> It is he that comprehends the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants in it are as grasshoppers; he that set up the heaven as a chamber, and stretched it out as a tent to dwell in:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:21; Psalms 107:40; Esias 34:12"> he that appoints princes to rule as nothing, and has made the earth as nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 41:16; Esias 17:13; Aggaeus 1:9"> For they shall not plant, neither shall they sow, neither shall their root be fixed in the ground: he has blown upon them, and they are withered, and a storm shall carry them away like sticks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 40:18; Deuteronomy 4:33; Deuteronomy 4:15–18"> Now then to whom have you⌃ compared me, that I may be exalted? says the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; Jeremias 32:17–19; Esias 48:13"> Lift up your eyes on high, and see, who has displayed all these things? even he that brings forth his host by number: he shall call them all by name by means of his great glory, and by the power of his might: nothing has escaped you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 18:7–8; Esias 49:4; Esias 49:14"> For say not you, O Jacob, and why have you spoken, Israel, saying, My way is hid from God, and my God has taken away my judgement, and has departed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34; Psalms 147:5; Esias 55:8–9"> And now, have you not known? have you not heard? the eternal God, the God that formed the ends of the earth, shall not hunger, nor be weary, and there is no searching of his understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esias 41:10; Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> He gives strength to the hungry, and sorrow to them that are not suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 2:14; Psalms 34:10; Psalms 33:16"> For the young men shall hunger, and the youths shall be weary, and the choice men shall be powerless:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Psalms 27:13–14"> but they that wait on God shall renew their strength; they shall put forth new feathers like eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; they shall walk, and not hunger.</span>
