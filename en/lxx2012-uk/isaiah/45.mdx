<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 51:20–24; Esias 44:28; Esias 42:6"> Thus says the Lord God to my anointed Cyrus, whose right hand I have held, that nations might be obedient before him; and I will break through the strength of kings; I will open doors before him, and cities shall not be closed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 40:4; Luke 3:5; Esias 42:16"> I will go before you, and will level mountains: I will break to pieces brazen doors, and burst iron bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 48:15; Esias 43:1; Exodus 33:17"> And I will give you the treasures of darkness, I will open to you hidden, unseen treasures, that you may know that I, the Lord your God, that call you by name, am the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 41:8–9; Acts 17:23; Esias 44:1"> For the sake of my servant Jacob, and Israel my elect, I will call you by your name, and accept you: but you have not known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 44:8; Esias 44:6; Esias 46:9"> For I am the Lord God, and there is no other God beside me; I strengthened you, and you have not known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachias 1:11; Esias 45:5; Psalms 46:10"> That they that come from the east and they that come from the west may know that there is no God but me. I am the Lord God, and there is none beside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:13–14; Amos 3:6; Psalms 75:7"> I am he that prepared light, and formed darkness; who make peace, and create evil; I am the Lord God, that does all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Osee 10:12; Esias 61:11; Psalms 72:6"> Let the heaven rejoice from above, and let the clouds rain righteousness: let the earth bring forth, and blossom with mercy, and bring forth righteousness likewise: I am the Lord that created you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Esias 10:15"> What excellent thing have I prepared as clay of the potter? Will the ploughman plough the earth all day? shall the clay say to the potter, What are you doing that you do not work, nor have hands? shall the thing formed answer him that formed it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Hebrews 12:9; Malachias 1:6"> As though one should say to his father, What will you beget me? and to his mother, What are you bringing forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 33:3; Esias 48:17; Mark 11:24"> For thus says the Lord God, the Holy One of Israel, who has formed the things that are to come, Enquire of me concerning my sons, and concerning the works of my hands command me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 42:5; Esias 44:24; Jeremias 27:5"> I have made the earth, and man upon it: I with my hand have established the heaven; I have given commandment to all the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 41:2; Esias 13:17; Esias 41:25"> I have raised him up to be a king with righteousness, and all his ways are right: he shall build my city, and shall turn the captivity of my people, not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. with.">a</a></sup>for ransoms, nor for rewards, says the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 49:23; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Jeremias 16:19"> Thus says the Lord of hosts, Egypt has laboured for you; and the merchandise of the Ethiopians, and the Sabeans, men of stature, shall pass over to you, and shall be your servants; and they shall follow after you bound in fetters, and shall pass over to you, and shall do obeisance to you, and make supplication to you: because God is in you; and there is no God beside you, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 8:17; Psalms 44:24; Esias 57:17"> For you are God, yet we knew it not, the God of Israel, the Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 44:9; Esias 44:11; Esias 42:17"> All that are opposed to him shall be ashamed and confounded, and shall walk in shame: you⌃ isles,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See chap 41:1.">b</a></sup>keep a feast to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:3; Esias 54:4"> Israel is saved by the Lord with an everlasting salvation: they shall not be ashamed nor confounded for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 115:16; Jeremias 10:12; Esias 45:12"> Thus says the Lord that made the heaven, this God that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. showed.">c</a></sup>created the earth, and made it; he marked it out, he made it not in vain, but formed it to be inhabited: I am the Lord, and there is none beside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 48:16; Amos 5:4; Numbers 23:19–20"> I have not spoken in secret, nor in a dark place of the earth: I said not to the seed of Jacob, Seek vanity:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. I am, I am.">d</a></sup>I, even I, am the Lord, speaking righteousness, and proclaiming truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 10:5; Esias 44:17–20; Esias 43:9"> Assemble yourselves and come; take counsel together, you⌃ that escape of the nations: they that set up wood, even their graven image, have no knowledge, nor they who pray to gods that do not save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 45:5; Esias 43:11; Esias 44:7–8"> If they will declare, let them draw near, that they may know together, who has caused these things to be heard from the beginning: then was it told you. I am God, and there is not another beside me; a just God and a Saviour; there is none but me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:40; Michaeas 7:7; Hebrews 12:2"> Turn you⌃ to me, and you⌃ shall be saved, you⌃ that come from the end of the earth: I am God, and there is none other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Romans 14:10–12; Esias 55:11"> By myself I swear, righteousness shall surely proceed out of my mouth; my words shall not be frustrated; that to me every knee shall bend, and every tongue shall swear by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 41:11; Esias 54:17; Ephesians 6:10"> saying, Righteousness and glory shall come to him: and all that remove them from their borders shall be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 41:16; Galatians 3:27–29; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> By the Lord shall they be justified, and in God shall all the seed of the children of Israel be glorified.</span>
