<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; Psalms 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> O Lord God, I will glorify you, I will sing to your name; for you have done wonderful things, even an ancient and faithful counsel. So be it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 17:1; Esias 13:22; Esias 25:12"> For you have made cities a heap, even cities made strong that their foundations should not fall: the city of ungodly men shall not be built for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zacharias 14:9; Esias 49:23–26; Psalms 46:10–11"> Therefore shall the poor people bless you, and cities of injured men shall bless you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Sophonias 3:12; Esias 32:2; James 2:5"> For you have been a helper to every lowly city, and a shelter to them that were disheartened by reason of poverty: you shall deliver them from wicked men: you have been a shelter of them that thirst, and a refreshing air to injured men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Esias 49:10; Psalms 105:39"> We were as faint-hearted men thirsting in Sion, by reason of ungodly men to whom you did deliver us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; Psalms 63:5; Zacharias 9:16–17"> And the Lord of hosts shall make a feast for all the nations: on this mount they shall drink gladness, they shall drink wine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Esias 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> they shall anoint themselves with ointment in this mountain. Impart you all these things to the nations; for this is God's counsel upon all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> Death has prevailed and swallowed men up; but again the Lord God has taken away every tear from every face. He has taken away the reproach of his people from all the earth: for the mouth of the Lord has spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Michaeas 7:7"> And in that day they shall say, behold our God in whom we have trusted, and he shall save us: this is the Lord; we have waited for him, and we have exulted, and will rejoice in our salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 25:6; Esias 18:4; Sophonias 2:9"> God will give rest on this mountain, and the country of Moab shall be trodden down, as they tread the floor with wagons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 16:14; Esias 2:11; Esias 16:6"> And he shall spread forth his hands, even as he also brings down man to destroy him: and he shall bring low his pride in regard to the thing on which he has laid his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Esias 14:23"> And he shall bring down the height of the refuge of the wall, and it shall come down even to the ground.</span>
