<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 38:1–8; Chronicles II 32:24; Kings IV 20:1–11"> And it came to pass at that time, that Ezekias was sick even to death. And Esaias the prophet the son of Amos came to him, and said to him, Thus says the Lord, Give orders concerning your house: for you shall die, and not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Psalms 91:15; Kings III 8:30"> And Ezekias turned his face to the wall, and prayed to the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 18:20–27; Hebrews 6:10"> Remember, O Lord, how I have walked before you in truth, with a true heart, and have done that which was pleasing in your sight. And Ezekias wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the word of the Lord came to Esaias, saying, Go, and say to Ezekias,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 34:5–6; 1 John 5:14–15; Revelation 7:17"> Thus says the Lord, the God of David your father, I have heard your prayer, and seen your tears: behold, I will add to your time fifteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 37:35; Esias 31:4–5; Esias 12:6"> And I will deliver you and this city out of the hand of the king of the Assyrians: and I will defend this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:37–39; Esias 37:30; Esias 7:11–14"> And this shall be a sign to you from the Lord, that God will do this thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 20:9–11; Joshua 10:12–14; Matthew 16:1"> behold, I will turn back the shadow of the degrees of the dial by which ten degrees on the house of your father the sun has gone down—I will turn back the sun the ten degrees; so the sun went back the ten degrees by which the shadow had gone down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:1; Exodus 15:1–21; Job 5:18"> THE PRAYER OF EZEKIAS KING OF JUDEA, WHEN HE<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. was sick">a</a></sup>HAD BEEN SICK, AND WAS RECOVERED FROM HIS SICKNESS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:24; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 107:18"> I said in the end of my days, I shall go to the gates of the grave: I shall part with the remainder of my years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 27:13; Psalms 116:8–9; Psalms 31:22"> I said, I shall no more at all see the salvation of God in the land of the living: I shall no more at all see the salvation of Israel on the earth: I shall no more at all see man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 73:14; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 1:12"> My life has failed from amongst my kindred: I have parted with the remainder of my life: it has gone forth and departed from me, as one that having pitched a tent takes it down again: my breath was with me as a weaver's web, when she that weaves draws near to cut off the thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 51:8; Daniel 6:24; Psalms 50:22"> In that day I was given up as to a lion until the morning: so has he broken all my bones: for I was so given up from day even to night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 59:11; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 123:1–4"> As a swallow, so will I cry, and as a dove, so do I mourn: for my eyes have failed with looking to the height of heaven to the Lord, who has delivered me, and removed the sorrow of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; 1 Corinthians 11:32; Psalms 119:25"> Yes, O Lord, for it was told you concerning this; and you have revived my breath; and I am comforted, and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 43:25; Michaeas 7:18–19; Jonas 2:6"> For you have chosen my soul, that it should not perish: and you have cast all my sins behind me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 30:9; Psalms 6:5"> For they that are in the grave shall not praise you, neither shall the dead bless you, neither shall they that are in Hades hope for your mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Psalms 119:175; Psalms 78:3–6"> The living shall bless you, as I also do: for from this day shall I beget children, who shall declare your righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 150:4; Psalms 145:2"> O God of my salvation; and I will not cease blessing you with the lute all the days of my life before the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 20:7–8; Mark 7:33; John 9:6"> Now Esaias had said to Ezekias; Take a cake of figs, and mash them, and apply them as a plaster, and you shall be well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 118:18–19; Kings IV 20:8; John 5:14"> And Ezekias said, This is a sign to Ezekias, that I shall go up to the house of God.</span>
