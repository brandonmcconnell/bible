<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Esias 34:5–6; Psalms 137:7"> Who is this that is come from Edom, with red garments from Bosor? thus fair in his apparel, with mighty strength? I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. discourse, reason about.">a</a></sup>speak of righteousness and saving judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Revelation 19:15"> Therefore are your garments red, and your raiment as if fresh from a trodden winepress?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachias 4:3"> I am full of trodden grape, and of the nations there is not a man with me; and I trampled them in my fury, and dashed them to pieces as earth, and brought down their blood to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 34:8; Jeremias 51:6; Esias 35:4"> For the day of recompence has come upon them, and the year of redemption is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Esias 40:10; Psalms 98:1"> And I looked, and there was no helper; and I observed, and none upheld: therefore my arm delivered them, and my anger drew near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Esias 49:26"> And I trampled them in my anger, and brought down their blood to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> I remembered the mercy of the Lord, the praises of the Lord in all things wherein he recompenses us. The Lord is a good judge to the house of Israel; he deals with us according to his mercy, and according to the abundance of his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Esias 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> And he said, Is it not my people? the children surely will not be rebellious: and he became to them deliverance</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachias 3:1"> out of all their affliction: not an ambassador, nor a messenger, but himself saved them, because he loved them and spared them: he himself redeemed them, and took them up, and lifted them up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. all the days of the age.">b</a></sup>all the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 78:56"> But they disobeyed, and provoked his Holy Spirit: so he turned to be an enemy, he himself contended against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:20"> Then he remembered the ancient days, saying, Where is he that brought up from the sea the shepherd of the sheep? where is he that put his Holy Spirit in them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> who led Moses with his right hand, the arm of his glory? he forced the water to separate from before him, to make himself an everlasting name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 31:9; Psalms 106:9; Ambacum 3:15"> He led them through the deep, as a horse through the wilderness, and they fainted not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> and as cattle through a plain: the Spirit came down from the Lord, and guided them: thus you led your people, to make yourself a glorious name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 80:14"> Turn from heaven, and look from your holy habitation and from your glory: where is your zeal and your strength? where is the abundance of your mercy and of your compassions, that you have withholden yourself from us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 64:8; Malachias 2:10; Jeremias 3:19"> For you are our Father; for though Abraham knew us not, and Israel did not acknowledge us, yet do you, O Lord, our Father, deliver us: your name has been upon us from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Jezekiel 14:7–9"> Why have you caused us to err, O Lord, from your way? and has hardened our hearts, that we should not fear you? Return for your servants' sake, for the sake of the tribes of your inheritance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> that we may inherit a small part of your holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. + 'our adversaries have trodden down the sanctuary.'">c</a></sup>We are become as at the beginning, when you did not rule over us, and your name was not called upon us.</span>
