<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 3:4; Esias 10:32; Kings IV 14:7"> I will send as it were reptiles on the land: is not the mount of the daughter of Sion a desolate rock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 27:8; Judges 11:18; Jeremias 48:20"> For you shall be as a young bird taken away from a bird that has flown: even you shall be so, daughter of Moab: and then do you, O Arnon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 32:2; Kings III 18:4; Esias 25:4"> take farther counsel, and continually make you a shelter from grief: they flee in darkness at mid-day; they are amazed; be not you led captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 14:4; Esias 51:13; Esias 9:4"> The fugitives of Moab shall sojourn with you; they shall be to you a shelter from the face of the pursuer: for your alliance has been taken away, and the oppressing ruler has perished from off the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Michaeas 4:7; Esias 9:6–7"> And a throne shall be established with mercy; and one shall sit upon it with truth in the tabernacle of David, judging, and earnestly seeking judgements, and hasting righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:1; Obdias 1:3–4; Jeremias 48:29–30"> We have heard of the pride of Moab; he is very proud. I have cut off his pride: your prophecy shall not be thus, no not thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 3:25; Chronicles I 16:3; Jeremias 48:20"> Moab shall howl; for all shall howl in the land of Moab: but you shall care for them that dwell in Seth, and you shall not be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 15:4; Numbers 32:38; Jeremias 48:32"> The plains of Esebon shall mourn, the vine of Sebama: swallowing up the nations, trample you⌃ her vines, even to Jazer: you⌃ shall not come together; wander you⌃ in the desert: they that were sent are deserted, for they have gone over to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 40:12; Esias 15:4–5; Jeremias 40:10"> Therefore will I weep as with the weeping of Jazer for the vine of Sebama; Esebon and Eleale have cast down your trees; for I will trample on your harvest and on your vintages, and all your plants shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 48:33; Judges 9:27; Amos 5:17"> And gladness and rejoicing shall be taken away from the vineyards; and they shall not at all tread wine into the vats; for the vintage has ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 15:5; Jeremias 48:36; Osee 11:8"> Therefore my belly shall sound as a harp for Moab, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Compare the Hebrew">a</a></sup>you have repaired my inward parts as a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 15:2; Kings III 18:29; Jeremias 48:35"> And it shall be to your shame, (for Moab is wearied at the altars,) that he shall go in to the idols thereof to pray, but they shall not be at all able to deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 44:8"> This is the word which the Lord spoke against Moab, when he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 21:16; Jeremias 48:46–47; Esias 23:9"> And now I say, in three years, of the years of an hireling, the glory of Moab shall be dishonoured with all his great wealth; and he shall be left few in number, and not honoured.</span>
