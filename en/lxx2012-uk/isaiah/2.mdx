<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 1:1; Esias 13:1; Ambacum 1:1"> The word which came to Esaias the son of Amos concerning Judea, and concerning Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Michaeas 4:1–3; Esias 56:7; Zacharias 8:3"> For in the last days the mountain of the Lord shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, conspicuous.">a</a></sup>glorious, and the house of God shall be on the top of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills; and all nations shall come to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 50:4–5; Luke 24:47; Zacharias 8:20–23"> And many nations shall go and say, Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, and to the house of the God of Jacob; and he will tell us his way, and we will walk in it: for out of Sion shall go forth the law, and the word of the Lord out of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Osee 2:18; Michaeas 4:3; Esias 11:6–9"> And he shall judge amongst the nations, and shall rebuke many people: and they shall beat their swords into ploughshares, and their spears into sickles: and nation shall not take up sword against nation, neither shall they learn to war any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Luke 1:79; Esias 60:19–20"> And now, O house of Jacob, come, and let us walk in the light of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:20; Kings IV 1:2; Psalms 106:35"> For he has forsaken his people the house of Israel, because their land is filled as at the beginning with divinations, as the land of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, aliens, or Philistines. The LXX. generally render by Φιλιστεὶμ or Φυλιστιὶμ till about the middle of Judges, after which the word almost always used is ἀλλόφυλοι. In this there was probably some accommodation of sound to tense.">b</a></sup>Philistines, and many strange children were born to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Esias 30:16; Esias 31:1"> For their land is filled with silver and gold, and there was no number of their treasures; their land also is filled with horses, and there was no number of chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 115:4–8; Revelation 9:20; Jeremias 2:28"> And the land is filled with abominations, even the works of their hands; and they have worshipped the works which their fingers made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 5:15; Joshua 24:19; Romans 3:23"> And the mean man bowed down, and the great man was humbled: and I will not pardon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 6:15–16; Esias 2:19–21; Luke 23:30"> Now therefore enter you⌃ into the rocks, and hide yourselves in the earth, for fear of the Lord, and by reason of the glory of his might, when he shall arise to strike terribly the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 5:15–16; Psalms 18:27; Esias 2:17"> For the eyes of the Lord are high, but man is low; and the haughtiness of men shall be brought low, and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachias 4:1; Esias 23:9; Proverbs 16:5"> For the day of the Lord of hosts shall be upon every one that is proud and haughty, and upon every one that is high and towering, and they shall be brought down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 14:8; Zacharias 11:1–2; Jezekiel 31:3–12"> and upon every cedar of Libanus, of them that are high and towering, and upon every oak of Basan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 30:25; Esias 40:4; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> and upon every high mountain, and upon every high hill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 25:12"> and upon every high tower, and upon every high wall,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 10:22; Esias 23:1; Numbers 33:52"> and upon every ship of the sea, and upon every display of fine ships.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 48:29–30; Esias 2:11; Esias 13:11"> And every man shall be brought low, and the pride of men shall fall: and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 27:9; Jezekiel 36:25; Esias 21:9"> And they shall hide all idols made with hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 2:10; Luke 23:30; Osee 10:8"> having carried them into the caves, and into the clefts of the rocks, and into the caverns of the earth, for fear of the Lord, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See ver 10.">c</a></sup>by reason of the glory of his might, when he shall arise to strike terribly the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 30:22; Esias 31:7; Esias 46:6"> For in that day a man shall cast forth his silver and gold abominations, which they made in order to worship vanities and bats;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 2:19; Song of Songs 2:14; Esias 2:10"> to enter into the caverns of the solid rock, and into the clefts of the rocks, for fear of the Lord, and by reason of the glory of his might, when he shall arise to strike terribly the earth.</span>
