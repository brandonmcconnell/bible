<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:2; Kings I 10:19; Genesis 33:18–19"> And Joshua gathered all the tribe of Israel to Selo, and convoked their elders, and their officers, and their judges, and set them before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:15; Jezekiel 16:3; Genesis 31:30"> And Joshua said to all the people, Thus says the Lord God of Israel, Your fathers at first sojourned beyond the river, even Thara, the father of Abraam and the father of Nachor; and they served other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Genesis 21:2–3; Acts 7:2–3"> And I took your father Abraam from the other side of the river, and I guided him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. in.">a</a></sup>through all the land, and I multiplied his seed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Deuteronomy 2:5; Genesis 25:24–26"> and I gave to him Isaac, and to Isaac Jacob and Esau: and I gave to Esau mount Seir for him to inherit: and Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt, and became there a great and populous and mighty nation: and the Egyptians afflicted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 7:1–12; Psalms 105:26–36"> And I struck Egypt with the wonders that I wrought amongst them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Hebrews 11:29; Esias 63:12–13"> And afterwards God brought out our fathers from Egypt, and you⌃ entered into the Red Sea; and the Egyptians pursued after our fathers with chariots and horses into the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Exodus 14:10; Exodus 14:20"> And we cried aloud to the Lord; and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. gave.">b</a></sup>put a cloud and darkness between us and the Egyptians, and he brought the sea upon them, and covered them; and your eyes have seen all that the Lord did in the land of Egypt; and you⌃ were in the wilderness many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Psalms 135:10–11; Nehemiah 9:22"> And he brought us into the land of the Amorites that lived beyond Jordan, and the Lord delivered them into our hands; and you⌃ inherited their land, and utterly destroyed them from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Michaeas 6:5; Numbers 22:2"> And Balac, king of Moab, son of Sepphor, rose up, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. set himself in array.">c</a></sup>made war against Israel, and sent and called Balaam to curse us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 24:5–10; Esias 54:17"> But the Lord your God would not destroy you; and he greatly blessed us, and rescued us out of their hands, and delivered them to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 4:10–12; Joshua 3:14–17; Psalms 114:5"> And you⌃ crossed over Jordan, and came to Jericho; and the inhabitants of Jericho fought against us, the Amorite, and the Chananite, and the Pherezite, and the Evite, and the Jebusite, and the Chettite, and the Gergesite, and the Lord delivered them into our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Exodus 23:28; Psalms 44:3–6"> And he sent forth the hornet before you; and he drove them out from before you, even twelve kings of the Amorites, not with your sword, nor with your bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Deuteronomy 8:7; Joshua 21:45"> And he gave you a land on which you⌃ did not labour, and cities which you⌃ did not build, and you⌃ were settled in them; and you⌃<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, shall eat.">d</a></sup>eat of vineyards and olive yards which you⌃ did not plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 12:24; Deuteronomy 10:12; Genesis 35:2"> And now fear the Lord, and serve him in righteousness and justice; and remove the strange gods, which our fathers served beyond the river, and in Egypt; and serve the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 18:21; John 6:67–68; Ruth 1:15–16"> But if it seem not good to you to serve the Lord, choose to yourselves this day whom you⌃ will serve, whether the gods of your fathers that were on the other side of the river, or the gods of the Amorites, amongst whom you⌃ dwell upon their land: but I and my house will serve the Lord, for he is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38–39; Kings I 12:23; Romans 6:2"> And the people answered and said, Far be it from us to forsake the Lord, so as to serve other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Esias 63:7–14; Esias 46:4"> The Lord our God, he is God; he brought up us and our fathers from Egypt, and kept us in all the way wherein we walked, and amongst all the nations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. whom we passed through them. Hebraism See Mark 1:7; Luke 3:16; 1 Pet 2:24.">e</a></sup>through whom we passed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Michaeas 4:2; Exodus 10:2; Psalms 116:16"> And the Lord cast out the Amorite, and all the nations that inhabited the land from before us: yes, we will serve the Lord, for he is our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 5:16; Exodus 23:21; Psalms 99:9"> And Joshua said to the people, Indeed you⌃ will not be able to serve the Lord, for God is holy; and he being jealous will not forgive your sins and your transgressions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Jezekiel 18:24; Chronicles I 28:9"> Whenever you⌃ shall forsake the Lord and serve other gods, then he shall come upon you and afflict you, and consume you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, whereas on the contrary.">f</a></sup>because he has done you good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Esias 44:5; Deuteronomy 5:27–28"> And the people said to Joshua, Nay, but we will serve the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:173; Psalms 119:11; Job 15:6"> And Joshua said to the people, You⌃ are witnesses against yourselves, that you⌃ have chosen the Lord to serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16–18; Joshua 24:14; Hebrews 12:28–29"> And now take away the strange gods that are amongst you, and set your heart right towards the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Deuteronomy 5:27–29"> And the people said to Joshua, We will serve the Lord, and we will listen to his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:38; Chronicles II 23:16; Exodus 15:25"> So Joshua made a covenant with the people on that day, and gave them a law and an ordinance in Selo before the tabernacle of the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Genesis 35:4; Joshua 4:20–24"> And he wrote these words in the book of the laws of God: and Joshua took a great stone, and set it up under the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. Properly a pine or turpentine tree. So chap. 17:9.">g</a></sup>oak before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 22:34; Joshua 22:27–28"> And Joshua said to the people, Behold, this stone shall be amongst you for a witness, for it has heard all the words that have been spoken to it by the Lord; for he has spoken to you this day; and this stone shall be amongst you for a witness in the last days, whenever you⌃ shall deal falsely with the Lord my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 2:6"> And Joshua dismissed the people, and they went every man to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:8; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 34:5"> And it came to pass after these things that Joshua the son of Naue the servant of the Lord died, at the age of one hundred and ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Judges 2:9; Kings II 23:30"> And they buried him by the borders of his inheritance in Thamnasarach in the mount of Ephraim, northward of the mount of Galaad: there they put with him into the tomb in which they buried him, the knives of stone with which he circumcised the children of Israel in Galgala, when he brought them out of Egypt, as the Lord appointed them; and there they are to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 2:7; Deuteronomy 31:13; Deuteronomy 11:2"> And Israel served the Lord all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. drew out the time with Joshua. Hebraism.">h</a></sup>lived as long as Joshua, and all that knew all the works of the Lord which he wrought for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 50:25; Genesis 33:19; Exodus 13:19"> And the children of Israel brought up the bones of Joseph out of Egypt, and buried them in Sicima, in the portion of the land which Jacob bought of the Amorites who lived in Sicima for one hundred ewe-lambs; and he gave it to Joseph for a portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 22:13; Exodus 6:25; Esias 57:1–2"> And it came to pass afterwards that Eleazar the high-priest the son of Aaron died, and was buried in Gabaar of Phinees his son, which he gave him in mount Ephraim. In that day the children of Israel took the ark of God, and carried it about amongst them; and Phinees exercised the priest's office in the room of Eleazar his father till he died, and he was buried in his own place Gabaar: but the children of Israel departed every one to their place, and to their own city: and the children of Israel worshipped Astarte, and Astaroth, and the gods of the nations round about them; and the Lord delivered them into the hands of Eglom king of Moab and he ruled over them eighteen years.</span>
