<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:18–33; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> Then Joshua called together the sons of Ruben, and the sons of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:12–18; Deuteronomy 3:16–20; Numbers 32:20–29"> and said to them, You⌃ have heard all that Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you, and you⌃ have listened to my voice in all that he commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:23–27"> You⌃ have not deserted your brethren these many days: until this day you⌃ have kept the commandment of the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Numbers 32:18; Deuteronomy 3:20"> And now the Lord our God has given our brethren rest, as he told them: now then return and depart to your homes, and to the land of your possession, which Moses gave you on the other side Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; Exodus 20:6; Romans 8:28"> But take great heed to do the commands and the law, which Moses the servant of the Lord commanded you to do; to love the Lord our God, to walk in all his ways, to keep his commands, and to cleave to him, and serve him with all your mind, and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:13; Luke 24:50; Genesis 47:7"> And Joshua blessed them, and dismissed them; and they went to their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–12; Numbers 32:33; Joshua 13:29–31"> And to one half the tribe of Manasse Moses gave a portion in the land of Basan, and to the other half Joshua gave a portion with his brethren on the other side of Jordan westward: and when Joshua sent them away to their homes, then he blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 31:27; Deuteronomy 8:9–14; Proverbs 3:16"> And they departed with much wealth to their houses, and they divided the spoil of their enemies with their brethren; very much cattle, and silver, and gold, and iron, and much raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Numbers 32:26; Numbers 32:1"> So the sons of Ruben, and the sons of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse, departed from the children of Israel in Selo in the land of Chanaan, to go away into Galaad, into the land of their possession, which they inherited by the command of the Lord, by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:26–27; Genesis 31:46–52; Joshua 4:5–9"> And they came to Galaad of Jordan, which is in the land of Chanaan: and the children of Ruben, and the children of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse built there an altar by Jordan, a great altar to look at.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Leviticus 17:8–9; Joshua 2:7"> And the children of Israel heard say, Behold, the sons of Ruben, and the sons of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse have built an altar at the borders of the land of Chanaan at Galaad of Jordan, on the opposite side to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–11; Deuteronomy 13:15; Galatians 4:17–18"> And all the children of Israel gathered together to Selo, so as to go up and fight against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 25:7; Psalms 106:30–31; Exodus 6:25"> And the children of Israel sent to the sons of Ruben, and the sons of Gad, and to the sons of the half tribe of Manasse into the land of Galaad, both Phinees the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:4; Exodus 18:25"> and ten of the chiefs with him; there was one chief of every household out of all the tribes of Israel; (the heads of families are the captains of thousands in Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:3; Kings I 15:23; Deuteronomy 30:17"> And they came to the sons of Ruben, and to the sons of Gad, and to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. halves, adj. q.d. dimidios viros">a</a></sup>half tribe of Manasse into the land of Galaad; and they spoke to them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:13–14"> Thus says the whole congregation of the Lord, What is this transgression that you⌃ have transgressed before the God of Israel, to turn away today from the Lord, in that you⌃ have built for yourselves an altar, so that you⌃ should be apostates from the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–18; Psalms 106:28–29; Deuteronomy 4:3–4"> Is the sin of Phogor too little for you, whereas we have not been cleansed from it until this day, though there was a plague amongst the congregation of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Joshua 22:16; Joshua 7:11"> And you⌃ have this day revolted from the Lord; and it shall come to pass if you⌃ revolt this day from the Lord, that to-morrow there shall be wrath upon all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Chronicles II 11:16–17; Acts 10:14–15"> And now if the land of your possession be too little, cross over to the land of the possession of the Lord, where the tabernacle of the Lord dwells, and receive you⌃ an inheritance amongst us; and do not become apostates from God, neither do you⌃ apostatize from the Lord, because of your having built an altar apart from the altar of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:1; Joshua 7:5; Joshua 7:18"> Behold! did not Achar the son of Zara commit a trespass taking of the accursed thing, and there was wrath on the whole congregation of Israel? and he himself died alone in his own sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 16:1; 1 Peter 3:15"> And the sons of Ruben, and the sons of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse answered, and spoke to the captains of the thousands of Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 8:39; Psalms 44:21; Deuteronomy 10:17"> God even God is the Lord, and God even God himself knows, and Israel he shall know; if we have transgressed before the Lord by apostasy, let him not deliver us this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 20:16; Deuteronomy 18:19; Jezekiel 3:18"> And if we have built to ourselves an altar, so as to apostatize from the Lord our God, so as to offer upon it a sacrifice of whole burnt offerings, so as to offer upon it a sacrifice of peace-offering, —the Lord shall require it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Deuteronomy 6:20; Genesis 30:33"> But we have done this for the sake of precaution concerning this thing, saying, Lest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. to-morrow.">b</a></sup>hereafter your sons should say to our sons, What have you⌃ to do with the Lord God of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 12:16; Ezra 4:2–3; Kings I 26:19"> Whereas the Lord has set boundaries between us and you, even Jordan, and you⌃ have no portion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. of.">c</a></sup>in the Lord: so your sons shall alienate our sons, that they should not worship the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. spoke.">d</a></sup>gave orders to do thus, to build this altar, not for burnt offerings, nor for meat-offerings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Genesis 31:48; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> but that this may be a witness between you and us, and between our posterity after us, that we may do service to the Lord before him, with our burnt offerings and our meat-offerings and our peace-offerings: so your sons shall not say to our sons, hereafter, You⌃ have no portion in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Jezekiel 43:10–11; Hebrews 8:5"> And we said, If ever it should come to pass that they should speak so to us, or to our posterity hereafter; then shall they say, Behold the likeness of the altar of the Lord, which our fathers made, not for the sake of burnt offerings, nor for the sake of meat-offerings, but it is a witness between you and us, and between our sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 24:16; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Joshua 22:23"> Far be it from us therefore that we should turn away from the Lord this day so as to apostatize from the Lord, so as that we should build an altar for burnt offerings, and for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A double translation in Greek.">e</a></sup>peace-offerings, besides the altar of the Lord which is before his tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:33; Esther 1:21; Proverbs 15:1"> And Phinees the priest and all the chiefs of the congregation of Israel who were with him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. having heard.">f</a></sup>heard the words which the children of Ruben, and the children of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse spoke; and it pleased them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:2; Leviticus 26:11–12; Esias 12:6"> And Phinees the priest said to the sons of Ruben, and to the sons of Gad, and to the half of the tribe of Manasse, To-day we know that the Lord is with us, because you⌃ have not trespassed grievously against the Lord, and because you⌃ have delivered the children of Israel out of the hand of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 22:12–14; Proverbs 25:13"> So Phinees the priest and the princes departed from the children of Ruben, and from the children of Gad, and from the half tribe of Manasse out of Galaad into the land of Chanaan to the children of Israel; and reported the words to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:20; Luke 2:28; Daniel 2:19"> And it pleased the children of Israel; and they spoke to the children of Israel, and blessed the God of the children of Israel, and told them to go up no more to war against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. them.">g</a></sup>the others to destroy the land of the children of Ruben, and the children of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse: so they lived upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 22:27; Matthew 4:10; Esias 43:10"> And Joshua gave a name to the altar of the children of Ruben, and the children of Gad, and the half tribe of Manasse; and said, It is a testimony in the midst of them, that the Lord is their God.</span>
