<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> And the Lord said to Joshua, Fear not, nor be timorous: take with you all the men of war, and arise, go up to Gai; behold, I have given into your hands the king of Gai, and his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; Chronicles II 20:22"> And you shall do to Gai, as you did to Jericho and its king; and you shall take to yourself the spoil of its cattle; set now for yourself an ambush for the city behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> And Joshua and all the men of war rose to go up to Gai; and Joshua chose out thirty thousand mighty men, and he sent them away by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> And he charged them, saying, Do you⌃ lie in ambush behind the city: do not go far from the city, and you⌃ shall all be ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> And I and all with me will draw near to the city: and it shall come to pass when the inhabitants of Gai shall come forth to meet us, as before, that we will flee from before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> And when they shall come out after us, we will draw them away from the city; and they will say, These men flee from before us, as also before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; Kings IV 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> And you⌃ shall rise up out of the ambuscade, and go into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> You⌃ shall do according to this word, behold! I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> And Joshua sent them, and they went to lie in ambush; and they lay between Baethel and Gai, westward of Gai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> And Joshua rose up early in the morning, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. reviewed.">a</a></sup>numbered the people; and he went up, he and the elders before the people to Gai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the men of war went up with him, and they went forward and came over against the city eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> And the ambuscade was on the west side of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And it came to pass when the king of Gai saw it, he hasted and went out to meet them direct to the battle, he and all the people that were with him: and he knew not that there was an ambuscade formed against him behind the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> And Joshua and Israel saw, and retreated from before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> And they pursued after the children of Israel, and they themselves went to a distance from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Esias 19:11–13"> There was no one left in Gai who did not pursue after Israel; and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; Kings I 17:41"> And the Lord said to Joshua, Stretch forth your hand with the spear that is in your hand towards the city, for I have delivered it into your hands; and the liers in wait shall rise up quickly out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Joshua stretched out his hand and his spear towards the city, and the ambuscade rose up quickly out of their place; and they came forth when he stretched out his hand; and they entered into the city, and took it; and they hasted and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, set it on fire.">b</a></sup>burnt the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> And when the inhabitants of Gai looked round behind them, then they saw the smoke going up out of the city to heaven, and they were no longer able to flee this way or that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambuscade<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. took.">c</a></sup>had taken the city, and that the smoke of the city went up to heaven; and they turned and struck the men of Gai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> And these came forth out of the city to meet them; and they were in the midst of the army, some being on this side, and some on that; and they struck them until there was not left of them one who survived and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> And they took the king of Gai alive, and brought him to Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> And when the children of Israel had ceased slaying all that were in Gai, and in the fields, and in the mountain on the descent, from whence they pursued them even to the end, then Joshua returned to Gai, and struck it with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> And they that fell in that day, men and women, were twelve thousand: they killed all the inhabitants of Gai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; Psalms 50:10"> Beside the spoils that were in the city, all things which the children of Israel took as spoil for themselves according to the command of the Lord, as the Lord commanded Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremias 49:2; Kings IV 19:25"> And Joshua burnt the city with fire: he made it an uninhabited heap for ever, even to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; Kings II 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> And he hanged the king of Gai on a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. double tree.">d</a></sup>gallows; and he remained on the tree till evening: and when the sun went down, Joshua gave charge, and they took down his body from the tree, and cast it into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. the pit or trench. For vv. 30-35, see chap. 8:2.">e</a></sup>a pit, and they set over him a heap of stones until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4–6; Exodus 20:24; Genesis 12:7–8"> Then Joshua built an altar to the Lord God of Israel in mount Gaebal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–7; Exodus 20:24–25; Joshua 8:34–35"> as Moses the servant of the Lord commanded the children of Israel, as it is written in the law of Moses, an altar of unhewn stones, on which iron<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. was not lifted up.">f</a></sup>had not been lifted up; and he offered there whole burnt offerings to the Lord, and a peace-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2–3; Deuteronomy 27:8"> And Joshua wrote upon the stones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, a Deuteronomy.">g</a></sup>a copy of the law, even the law of Moses, before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; Deuteronomy 31:12; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And all Israel, and their elders, and their judges, and their scribes, passed on one side and on the other before the ark; and the priests and the Levites took up the ark of the covenant of the Lord; and the stranger and the native were there, who were half of them near mount Garizin, and half near mount Gaebal, as Moses the servant of the Lord commanded at first, to bless the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; Deuteronomy 31:10–12"> And afterwards Joshua read accordingly all the words of this law, the blessings and the curses, according to all things written in the law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; Jeremias 26:2"> There was not a word of all that Moses charged Joshua, which Joshua read not in the ears of all the assembly of the children of Israel, the men, and the women, and the children, and the strangers that joined themselves to Israel.</span>
