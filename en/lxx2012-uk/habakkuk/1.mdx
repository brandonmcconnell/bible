<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Naum 1:1; Esias 22:1"> The burden which the prophet Ambacum saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 22:1–2; Jeremias 14:9; Psalms 13:1–2"> How long, O Lord, shall I cry out, and you will not listen? how long shall I cry out to you being injured, and you will not save?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremias 9:2–6; Jeremias 20:8"> Therefore have you shown me troubles and griefs to look upon, misery and ungodliness? judgement is before me, and the judge receives a reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Esias 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> Therefore the law is frustrated, and judgement proceeds not effectually, for the ungodly man prevails over the just; therefore perverse judgement will proceed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Esias 29:14"> Behold, you⌃ despisers, and look, and wonder marvellously, and vanish: for I work a work in your days, which you⌃ will in no wise believe, though a man declare it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 24:2; Jeremias 5:15; Esias 23:13"> Therefore, behold, I stir up the Chaldeans, the bitter and hasty nation, that walks upon the breadth of the earth, to inherit tabernacles not his own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 39:5–9; Jeremias 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> He is terrible and famous; his judgement shall proceed of himself, and his dignity shall come out of himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 4:13; Sophonias 3:3; Osee 8:1"> And his horses shall bound more swiftly than leopards, and they are fiercer than the wolves of Arabia: and his horsemen shall ride forth, and shall rush from far; and they shall fly as an eagle hasting to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 25:9; Osee 13:15; Osee 1:10"> Destruction shall come upon ungodly men, resisting with their adverse front, and he shall gather the captivity as the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:6; Jeremias 32:24; Esias 14:16"> And he shall be at his ease with kings, and princes are his toys, and he shall mock at every strong-hold, and shall cast a mound, and take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremias 4:11–12"> Then shall he change his spirit, and he shall pass through, and make an atonement, saying, This strength belongs to my god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremias 30:11; Jeremias 46:28"> Are not you from the beginning, O Lord God, my Holy One? and surely we shall not die. O Lord, you have established it for judgement, and he has formed me to chasten with his correction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremias 12:1–2"> His eye is too pure to behold evil doings, and to look upon grievous afflictions: therefore do you look upon despisers? will you be silent when the ungodly swallows up the just?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> And will you make men as the fishes of the sea, and as the reptiles which have no guide?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremias 16:16; Psalms 10:9"> He has brought up destruction with a hook, and drawn one with a casting net, and caught another in his drags: therefore shall his heart rejoice and be glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ambacum 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore will he sacrifice to his drag, and burn incense to his casting-net, because by them he has made his portion fat, and his meats choice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ambacum 2:5–8; Jezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremias 25:9–26"> Therefore will he cast his net, and will not spare to kill the nations continually.</span>
