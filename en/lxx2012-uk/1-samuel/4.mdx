<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 29:1; Kings I 7:12; Kings I 5:1"> And it came to pass in those days that the Philistines<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. gather.">a</a></sup>gathered themselves together against Israel to war; and Israel went out to meet them and encamped at Abenezer, and the Philistines encamped in Aphec.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; Kings I 17:8; Joshua 7:5–8"> And the Philistines prepare to fight with Israel, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. the war.">b</a></sup>the battle was turned against them; and the men of Israel fell before the Philistines, and there were struck in the battle in the field four thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Numbers 10:35; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5"> And the people came to the camp, and the elders of Israel said, Why has the Lord caused us to fall this day before the Philistines? let us take the ark of our God out of Selom, and let it proceed from the midst of us, and it shall save us from the hand of our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 6:2; Numbers 7:89; Psalms 80:1"> And the people sent to Selom, and they take thence the ark of the Lord who dwells between the cherubs: and both the sons of Heli, Ophni and Phinees, were with the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:5; Job 20:5; Amos 6:3"> And it came to pass when the ark of the Lord entered into the camp, that all Israel cried out with a loud voice, and the earth resounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 32:17–18"> And the Philistines heard the cry, and the Philistines said, What is this great cry in the camp of the Hebrews: and they understood that the ark of the Lord was come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Exodus 14:25; Exodus 15:14–16"> And the Philistines feared, and said, These are the Gods that are come to them into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:14; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 78:43–51"> Woe to us, O Lord, deliver us today for such a thing has not happened aforetime: woe to us, who shall deliver us out of the hand of these mighty Gods? these are the Gods that struck Egypt with every plague, and in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Judges 13:1; Kings II 10:12"> Strengthen yourselves and behave yourselves like men, O you⌃ Philistines, that you⌃ may not serve the Hebrews as they have served us, but be you⌃ men and fight with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; Kings IV 14:12; Deuteronomy 28:25"> And they fought with them; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. the man.">c</a></sup>the men of Israel fall, and they fled every man to his tent; and there was a very great slaughter; and there fell of Israel thirty thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. ranks.">d</a></sup>fighting men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 2:34; Psalms 78:64; Kings I 2:32"> And the ark of God was taken, and both the sons of Heli, Ophni, and Phinees, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Nehemiah 9:1; Kings II 15:32"> And there ran a man of Benjamin out of the battle, and he came to Selom on that day: and his clothes were torn, and earth was upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 1:9; Kings I 4:18; Joshua 7:9"> And he came, an behold, Heli was upon the seat by the gate looking along the way, for his heart was greatly alarmed for the ark of God: and the man entered into the city to bring tidings; and the city cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Heli heard the sound of the cry, and said, What is the voice of this cry? and the men hasted and went in, and reported to Heli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 3:2; Psalms 90:10; Kings III 14:4"> Now Heli was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. a son of ninety years.">e</a></sup>ninety years old, and his eyes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. rose up. Heb. קמה rose or stood.">f</a></sup>were fixed, and he saw not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 1:4; Kings I 3:6; Joshua 7:19"> And Heli said to them that stood round about him, What is the voice of this sound? And the man hasted and advanced to Heli, and said to him, I am he that is come out of the camp, and I have fled from the battle today: and Heli said, What is the even, my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 3:11; Kings I 4:10–11"> And they young man answered and said, The men of Israel fled from the face of the Philistines, and there was a great slaughter amongst the people, and both your sons are dead, and the ark of God is taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 4:21–22; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Psalms 69:9"> And it came to pass, when he mentioned the ark of God, that he fell from the seat backward near the gate, and his back was broken, and he died, for he was an old man and heavy: and he judged Israel twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And his daughter-in-law the wife of Phinees was with child, about to bring forth; and she heard the tidings, that the ark of God was taken, and that her father-in-law and her husband were dead; and she wept and was delivered, for her pains came upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:17–18; Psalms 77:2; John 16:21"> And in her time she was at the point of death; and the women that stood by her, said to her, Fear not, for you have born a son: but she answered not, and her heart did not regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Jeremias 2:11; Psalms 78:61"> And she called the child Uaebarchaboth, because of the ark of God, and because of her father-in-law, and because of her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:5–6; John 2:17"> And they said, The glory of Israel is departed, forasmuch as the ark of the Lord is taken.</span>
