<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Israel and the Philistines formed their lines one opposite the other.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:26; Kings I 11:2; Kings I 14:6"> and David spoke to the men who stood with him saying, Shall it indeed be done thus to the man who shall strike that Philistine, and take away reproach from Israel? for who is this uncircumcised Philistine that he has defied the army of the living God?</span>

<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And the king said, Do you ask whose son this youth is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Kings I 17:54"> And as David returned from the slaughter of the Philistine, Abener took him and brought him before Saul, and the head of the Philistine was in his hand.</span>
