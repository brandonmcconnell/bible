<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 9:1; Kings I 15:23; Psalms 78:68–71"> And the Lord said to Samuel, How long do you mourn for Saul, whereas I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? Fill your horn with oil, and come, I will send you to Jessae, to Bethleem; for I have seen amongst his sons a king for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 9:12; Kings I 20:29; Matthew 10:16"> And Samuel said, How can I go? whereas Saul will hear of it, and kill me: and the Lord said, Take a heifer in your hand and you shall say, I am come to sacrifice to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 9:16; Kings I 16:12–13; Kings II 15:11"> And you shall call Jessae to the sacrifice, and I will make known to you what you shall do; and you shall anoint him whom I shall mention to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 2:13; Chronicles I 12:17–18; Kings I 21:1"> And Samuel did all that the Lord told him; and he came to Bethleem: and the elders of the city were amazed at meeting him, and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. is 'your coming in peace.'">a</a></sup>Do you come peaceably, you Seer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Leviticus 20:7–8; Exodus 19:10"> And he said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. peace.">b</a></sup>Peaceably: I am come to sacrifice to the Lord. Sanctify yourselves, and rejoice with me this day: and he sanctified Jessae and his sons, and he called them to the sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 17:13; Chronicles I 2:13; Kings I 17:22"> And it came to pass when they came in, that he saw Eliab, and said, Surely the Lord's anointed is before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:24; Jeremias 17:10; Luke 16:15"> But the Lord said to Samuel, Look not on his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, face.">c</a></sup>appearance, nor on his stature, for I have rejected him; for God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. will not see.">d</a></sup>sees not as man looks; for man looks at the outward appearance, but God looks at the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 17:13; Chronicles I 2:13"> And Jessae called Aminadab, and he passed before Samuel: and he said, Neither has God chosen this one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 17:13; Chronicles I 2:13; Kings II 13:3"> And Jessae caused Sama to pass by: and he said, Neither has God chosen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. in this one.">e</a></sup>this one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles I 2:13–15"> And Jessae caused his seven sons to pass before Samuel: and Samuel said, the Lord has not chosen these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; Kings II 7:8; Kings II 13:3"> And Samuel said to Jessae,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. Are your sons come to an end?">f</a></sup>Have you no more sons? And Jessae said, There is yet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, the youngest.">g</a></sup>a little one; behold, he tends the flock. And Samuel said to Jessae, Send and fetch him for we may not sit down till he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:7; Psalms 89:19–20; Song of Songs 5:10"> And he sent and fetched him: and he was ruddy, with beauty of eyes, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. goodly in the sight of the Lord.">h</a></sup>very goodly to behold. And the Lord said to Samuel, Arise, and anoint David, for he is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 10:6; Hebrews 1:9; John 3:34"> And Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. leapt upon.">i</a></sup>came upon David from that day forward: and Samuel arose, and departed to Armathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:11; Kings I 18:12; Judges 9:23"> And the Spirit of the Lord departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. choked him.">j</a></sup>tormented him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And Saul's servants said to him, Behold now, an evil spirit from the Lord torments you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 3:15; Kings III 10:8; Kings I 19:9"> Let now your servants speak before you, and let them seek for our lord a man skilled to play on the harp; and it shall come to pass when an evil spirit comes upon you and he shall play on his harp, that you shall be well, and he shall refresh you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Saul said to his servants, Look now out for me a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. man playing skilfully.">k</a></sup>skilful player, and bring him to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 3:19; Genesis 39:23; Genesis 39:2"> And one of his servants answered and said, Behold, I have seen a son of Jessae the Bethleemite, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. him understanding.">l</a></sup>he understands playing on the harp, and the man is prudent, and a warrior, and wise in speech, and the man is handsome, and the Lord is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 16:11; Kings I 17:15; Psalms 78:70–72"> And Saul sent messengers to Jessae, saying, Send to me your son David who is with your flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 10:27; Proverbs 18:16; Kings I 17:18"> And Jessae took a homer of bread, and a bottle of wine, and one kid of the goats, and sent them by the hand of his son David to Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:46; Proverbs 22:29; Psalms 146:3"> And David went in to Saul, and stood before him; and he loved him greatly; and he became his armour-bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Saul sent to Jessae, saying, Let David, I pray you, stand before me, for he has found grace in my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings I 16:14–16; Kings I 18:10–11; Luke 11:24–26"> And it came to pass when the evil spirit was upon Saul, that David took his harp, and played with his hand: and Saul was refreshed, and it was well with him, and the evil spirit departed from him.</span>
