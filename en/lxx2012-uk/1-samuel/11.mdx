<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 12:12; Kings III 20:34; Jezekiel 17:13"> And it came to pass about a month after this, that Naas the Ammanite went up, and encamped against Jabis Galaad: and all the men of Jabis said to Naas the Ammanite, Make a covenant with us, and we will serve you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 17:26; Genesis 34:14; Numbers 16:14"> Naas the Ammanite said to them, On these terms will I make a covenant with you, that I should<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. dig out.">a</a></sup>put out all your right eyes, and I will lay a reproach upon Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the men of Jabis say to him, Allow us seven days, and we will send messengers into all the coasts of Israel: if there should be no one to deliver us, we will come out to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 2:4; Kings I 10:26; Kings I 15:34"> And the messengers came to Gabaa to Saul, and they speak the words into the ears of the people; and all the people lifted up their voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> And, behold, Saul came after the early morning out of the field: and Saul said, Why does the people week? and they tell him the words of the men of Jabis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 10:10; Kings I 16:13; Judges 6:34"> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon Saul when he heard these words, and his anger was greatly kindled against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:5–11"> And he took two cows, and cut them in pieces, and sent them into all the coasts of Israel by the hand of messengers, saying, Whoso comes not forth after Saul and after Samuel, so shall they do to his oxen: and a transport from the Lord came upon the people of Israel, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. cried out.">b</a></sup>came out to battle as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; Kings II 24:9; Chronicles II 17:12–19"> And he reviews them at Bezec in Bama, every man of Israel six hundred thousand, and the men of Juda seventy thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:17"> And he said to the messengers that came, Thus shall you⌃ say to the men of Jabis, To-morrow you⌃ shall have deliverance when the sun is hot; and the messengers came to the city, and told the men of Jabis, and they rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings I 11:2–3"> And the men of Jabis said to Naas the Ammanite, To-morrow we will come forth to you, and you⌃ shall do to us what seems good in your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> And it came to pass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. after the morrow.">c</a></sup>on the morrow, that Saul<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. put.">d</a></sup>divided the people into three companies, and they go into the midst of the camp in the morning watch, and they struck the children of Ammon until the day was hot; at it came to pass that those who were left were scattered, and there were not left amongst them two together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 10:27; Luke 19:27; Psalms 21:8"> And the people said to Samuel, Who has said that Saul shall not reign over us? Give up the men, and we will put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 19:22; Kings I 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> And Saul said, No man shall die this day, for today the Lord has wrought deliverance in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 10:8; Kings I 7:16; Chronicles I 12:38–39"> And Samuel spoke to the people, saying, Let us go to Galgala, and there renew the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 10:8; Kings I 10:17; Chronicles I 29:21–24"> And all the people went to Galgala, and Samuel anointed Saul there to be king before the Lord in Galgala, and there he offered meat-offerings and peace-offerings before the Lord: and Samuel and all Israel rejoiced exceedingly.</span>
