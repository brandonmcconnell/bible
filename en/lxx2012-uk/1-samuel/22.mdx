<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:1–11; Psalms 142:1–7; Chronicles I 11:15"> And David departed thence, and escaped; and he comes to the cave of Odollam, and his brethren hear, and the house of his father, and they go down to him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; Kings I 30:22–24; Psalms 72:12–14"> And there gathered to him every one that was in distress, and every one that was in debt, and every one that was troubled in mind; and he was a leader over them, and there were with him about four hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 3:18; Kings II 15:25–26; Judges 11:29"> And David departed thence to Massephath of Moab, and said to the king of Moab, Let, I pray you, my father and my mother be with you, until I know what God will do to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 11:1; Chronicles I 12:16; Chronicles I 29:29"> And he persuaded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. the face of the king.">a</a></sup>the King of Moab, and they dwell with him continually, while David was in the hold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:25; Chronicles I 21:9; Chronicles I 29:29"> And Gad the prophet said to David, Dwell not in the hold: go, and you shall enter the land of Juda. So David went, and came and lived in the city of Saric.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 19:9; Kings I 18:10; Kings I 20:33"> And Saul heard that David was discovered, and his men with him: now Saul lived in the hill below the field that is in Rama, and his spear was in his hand, and all his servants stood near him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 12:16; Kings I 8:14–15; Kings I 22:13"> And Saul said to his servants that stood by him, Hear now, you⌃ sons of Benjamin, will the son of Jessae indeed give all of you fields and vineyards, and will he make you all captains of hundreds and captains of thousands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 18:3; Kings I 23:21; Kings I 20:13–17"> That you⌃ are conspiring against me, and there is no one that informs me, whereas my son has made a covenant with the son of Jessae, and there is no one of you that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. labours.">b</a></sup>is sorry for me, or informs me, that my son has stirred up my servant against me for an enemy, as it is this day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–9; Kings I 14:3; Jezekiel 22:9"> And Doec the Syrian who was over the mules of Saul<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. answers.">c</a></sup>answered and said, I saw the son of Jessae as he came to Nomba to Abimelech son of Achitob the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; Kings I 30:8; Kings I 23:4"> And the priest enquired of God for him, and gave him provision, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:15"> And the king sent to call Abimelech son of Achitob and all his father's sons, the priests that were in Nomba; and they all came to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 9:6; Kings I 22:7; Esias 65:1"> And Saul said, Hear now, you son of Achitob. And he said, Behold! I am here, speak, my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 22:8; Luke 23:2–5; Amos 7:10"> And Saul said to him, Why have you and the son of Jessae conspired against me, that you should give him bread and a sword, and should enquire of God for him, to raise him up against me as an enemy, as he is this day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 20:32; Kings I 19:4–5; Kings I 22:13"> And he answered the king, and said, And who is there amongst all your servants faithful as David, and he is a son-in-law of the king, and he is executor of all your commands, and is honourable in your house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 25:36; 1 Peter 3:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Have I begun today to enquire of God for him? By no means: let not the king bring a charge against his servant, and against the whole of my father's house; for your servant knew not in all these matters anything great or small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 28:15; Daniel 3:19–20; Kings III 19:2"> And king Saul said, You shall surely die, Abimelech, you, and all your father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:17; Kings I 14:45; Kings IV 1:13–14"> And the king said to the footmen that attended on him, Draw near and kill the priests of the Lord, because their hand is with David, and because they knew that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. flees.">d</a></sup>fled, and they did not inform me. But the servants of the king would not lift their hands to fall upon the priest of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:10–11; Osee 7:3; Kings I 2:18"> And the king said to Doec, Turn you, and fall upon the priests: and Doec the Syrian turned, and killed the priests of the Lord in that day, three hundred and five men, all wearing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex. a linen ephod.">e</a></sup>an ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 15:3; Osee 10:14; Kings I 21:1"> And he struck Nomba the city of the priest with the edge of the sword, both man, and woman, infant and suckling, and calf, and ox, and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 23:6; Kings I 30:7; Kings III 2:26–27"> And one son of Abimelech son of Achitob escapes, and his name was Abiathar, and he fled after David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Abiathar told David that Saul had slain all the priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 21:1–9; Psalms 44:22"> And David said to Abiathar, I knew it in that day, that Doec the Syrian would surely tell Saul: I am guilty of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. souls or lives.">f</a></sup>death of the house of your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 2:26; Hebrews 12:1–3; John 10:28–30"> Dwell with me; fear not, for wherever I shall seek a place of safety for my life, I will also seek a place for your life, for you are safely guarded while with me.</span>
