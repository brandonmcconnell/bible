<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:34; Chronicles I 6:25–27; Kings I 1:19"> There was a man of Armathaim Sipha, of mount Ephraim, and his name was Helkana, a son of Jeremeel the son of Elias the son of Thoke, in Nasib Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:1–2; Judges 13:2; Genesis 29:23–29"> And he had two wives; the name of the one was Anna, and the name of the second Phennana. And Phennana had children, but Anna had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:41; Joshua 18:1; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> And the man went up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. from days to days.">a</a></sup>from year to year from his city, from Armathaim, to worship and sacrifice to the Lord God of Sabaoth at Selom: and there were Heli and his two sons Ophni and Phinees, the priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17–18; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> And the day came, and Helkana sacrificed, and gave portions to his wife Phennana and her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:2; Genesis 29:30–31; Genesis 45:22"> And to Anna he gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. one portion.">b</a></sup>a prime portion, because she had no child, only Helkana loved Anna more than the other; but the Lord had closed her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:14; Job 24:21; Leviticus 18:18"> For the Lord gave her no child in her affliction, and according to the despondency of her affliction; and she was dispirited on this account, that the Lord shut up her womb so as not to give her a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 2:19"> So she did year by year, in going up to the house of the Lord; and she was dispirited, and wept, and did not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:15; Job 6:14; Esias 54:6"> And Helkana her husband said to her, Anna: and she said to him, Here am I, my lord: and he said to her, What ails you that you weep? and why do you not eat? and why does your heart strike you? am I not better to you than ten children?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 3:3; Psalms 27:4; Psalms 5:7"> And Anna rose up after they had eaten in Selom, and stood before the Lord: and Heli the priest was on a seat by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. lintels.">c</a></sup>threshold of the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 7:11; Luke 22:44; Job 10:1"> And she was very much grieved in spirit, and prayed to the Lord, and wept abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Numbers 6:5; Genesis 29:32"> And she vowed a vow to the Lord, saying, O Lord God of Sabaoth, if you welt indeed look upon the humiliation of your handmaid, and remember me, and give to your handmaid a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. seed of men.">d</a></sup>boy, then will I indeed dedicate him to you till the day of his death; and he shall drink no wine nor strong drink, and no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. iron.">e</a></sup>razor shall come upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16; Luke 18:1"> And it came to pass, while she was long praying before the Lord, that Heli the priest marked her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:26; Acts 2:13; Genesis 24:42–45"> And she was speaking in her heart, and her lips moved, but her voice was not heard: and Heli accounted her a drunken woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Job 8:2; Proverbs 6:9"> And the servant of Heli said to her, How long will you be drunken? take away your wine from you, and go out from the presence of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Lamentations 2:19; Psalms 42:4"> And Anna answered and said, Nay, my lord, I live<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Comp. ἐν σκληρᾳ ἡμέρᾳ.">f</a></sup>in a hard day, and I have not drunk wine or strong drink, and I pour out my soul before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 2:12; Job 6:2–3; Matthew 12:34–35"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. give not.">g</a></sup>Count not your handmaid for a pestilent woman, for by reason of the abundance of my importunity I have continued my prayer until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Judges 18:6; Psalms 20:3–5"> And Heli answered and said to her, Go in peace: the God of Israel give you all your petition, which you have asked of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Romans 15:13; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And she said, Your handmaid has found favour in your eyes: and the woman went her way, and entered into her lodging, and ate and drank with her husband, and her countenance was no more sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:1; Genesis 30:22; Kings I 1:11"> And they rise early in the morning, and worship the Lord, and they go their way: and Helkana went into his house at Armathaim, and knew his wife Anna; and the Lord remembered her, and she conceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:51–52; Matthew 1:21; Exodus 2:10"> And it came to pass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. in the season of days.">h</a></sup>when the time was come, that she brought forth a son, and called his name Samuel, and said, Because I asked him of the Lord God of Sabaoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 1:3; Deuteronomy 12:11; Joshua 24:15"> And the man Helkana and all his house went up to offer in Selom the yearly sacrifice, and his vows, and all the tithes of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 1:28; Kings I 1:11; Luke 2:22"> But Anna did not go up with him, for she said to her husband, I will not go up until the child goes up, when I have weaned him, and he shall be presented before the Lord, and he shall abide there continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 7:25; Luke 11:27; Genesis 21:7–8"> And Helkana her husband said to her, Do that which is good in your eyes, abide still until you shall have weaned him; but may the Lord establish that which comes out of your mouth: and the woman tarried, and suckled her son until she had weaned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 15:9–10; Joshua 18:1; Kings I 4:3–4"> And she went up with him to Selom with a calf<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or, in its third year.">i</a></sup>of three years old, and loaves, and an ephah of fine flour, and a bottle of wine: and she entered into the house of the Lord in Selom, and the child with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 18:15–16; Luke 2:22"> And they brought him before the Lord; and his father killed his offering which he offered from year to year to the Lord; and he brought near the child, and killed the calf; and Anna the mother of the child brought him to Heli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 4:30; Kings IV 2:4; Kings IV 2:6"> And she said, I pray you, my lord, as your soul lives, I am the woman that stood in your presence with you while praying to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 5:15; Psalms 118:5; Matthew 7:7"> For this child I prayed; and the Lord has given me my request that I asked of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:52; 2 Timothy 3:15"> And I lend him to the Lord all his days that he lives, a loan to the Lord: and she said,</span>
