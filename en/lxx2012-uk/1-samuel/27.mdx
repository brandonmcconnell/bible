<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 40:27–31; Kings I 21:10–15; Esias 51:12"> And David said in his heart, Now shall I be one day delivered for death into the hands of Saul; and there is no good thing for me unless I should escape into the land of the Philistines, and Saul should cease from seeking me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. into.">a</a></sup>through every coast of Israel: so I shall escape out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 21:10; Kings I 25:13; Kings III 2:39–40"> So David arose, and the six hundred men that were with him, and he went to Anchus, son Ammach, king of Geth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 25:42–43; Kings I 30:5; Kings I 25:18–35"> And David lived with Anchus, he and his men, each with his family; and David and both his wives, Achinaam, the Jezraelitess, and Abigaia the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 26:21"> And it was told Saul that David had fled to Geth; and he no longer sought after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And David said to Anchus, If now your servant has found grace in your eyes, let them give me, I pray you, a place in one of the cities in the country, and I will dwell there: for why does your servant dwell with you in a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. city reigned over.">b</a></sup>royal city?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:28; Joshua 15:31; Chronicles I 12:20"> And he gave him Sekelac in that day: therefore Sekelac came into possession of the king of Judea to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 29:3"> And the number of the days that David lived in the country of the Philistines was four months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 15:7–8; Joshua 13:2; Joshua 13:13"> And David and his men went up, and made an attack on all the Gesirites and on the Amalekites: and behold, the land was inhabited, (even the land<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Th LXX have rendered מעולס ... שוה of old to Shur, by ἀπὸ Γελαμψοὺρ.">c</a></sup>from Gelampsur) by those who come from the fortified cities even to the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 15:3; Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 25:17–19"> And he struck the land, and saved neither man nor woman alive; and they took flocks, and herds, and asses, and camels, and raiment; and they returned and came to Anchus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Chronicles I 2:9; Chronicles I 2:25"> And Anchus said to David, On whom have you⌃ made an attack today? And David said to Anchus, On the south of Judea, and on the south of Jesmega, and on the south of the Kenezite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Proverbs 12:19; Kings I 22:22"> And I have not saved man or woman alive to bring them to Geth, saying, Lest they carry a report to Geth against us, saying, These things David does. And this was his manner all the days that David lived in the country of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Kings I 13:4"> So David had the full confidence of Anchus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. saying.">d</a></sup>who said, He is thoroughly disgraced amongst his people in Israel and he shall be my servant for ever.</span>
