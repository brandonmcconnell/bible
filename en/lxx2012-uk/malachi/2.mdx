<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 5:1; Malachias 1:6; Jeremias 13:13"> And now, O priests, this commandment is to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:18; Revelation 14:7; Psalms 69:22"> If you⌃ will not listen, and if you⌃ will not lay it to heart, to give glory to my name, says the Lord Almighty, then I will send forth the curse upon you, and I will bring a curse upon your blessing: yes, I will curse it, and I will scatter your blessing, and it shall not exist amongst you, because you⌃ lay not this to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Naum 3:6; Malachias 2:9; Job 20:7"> Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. separate the shoulder from you.">a</a></sup>I turn my back upon you, and I will scatter dung upon your faces, the dung of your feasts, and I will carry you away at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Jezekiel 38:23; Matthew 3:12"> And you⌃ shall know that I have sent this commandment to you, that my covenant might be with the sons of Levi, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 25:12–13; Jezekiel 34:25; Jezekiel 37:26"> My covenant of life and peace was with him, and I gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, power, or, charge to fear me, etc.">b</a></sup>it him that he might reverently fear me, and that he might<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See 2 Cor 8:20.">c</a></sup>be awe-struck at my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 2 Timothy 2:15–16; Revelation 14:5"> The law of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found in his lips: he walked before me directing his way in peace, and he turned many from unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Leviticus 10:11; Deuteronomy 21:5"> For the priest's lips<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. shall.">d</a></sup>should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; Jeremias 18:15; Esias 30:11"> But you⌃ have turned aside from the way, and caused many to fail in following the law: you⌃ have corrupted the covenant of Levi, says the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 2:30; Deuteronomy 1:17; Matthew 19:17–18"> And I have made you despised and cast out amongst all the people, because you⌃ have not kept my ways, but have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. accepted persons.">e</a></sup>been partial in the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Malachias 2:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> Have you⌃ not all one father? Did not one God create you? why have you⌃ forsaken every man his brother, to profane the covenant of your fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> Juda has been forsaken, and an abomination has been committed in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Juda has profaned the holy things of the Lord, which he delighted in, and has gone after other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:29; Leviticus 20:3; Jezekiel 24:21"> The Lord will utterly destroy the man that does these things, until he be even cast down from out of the tabernacles of Jacob, and from amongst them that offer sacrifice to the Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 1:11–15; Jeremias 14:12; Psalms 78:34–37"> And these things which I hated, you⌃ did: you⌃ covered with tears the altar of the Lord, and with weeping and groaning because of troubles: is it meet for me to have respect to your sacrifice, or to receive anything from your hands as welcome?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachias 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Esias 54:6"> Yet you⌃ said, Therefore? Because the Lord has borne witness between you and the wife of your youth, whom you have forsaken, and yet she was your partner, and the wife of your covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachias 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> And did he not do well? and there was the residue of his spirit. But you⌃ said, What does God seek but a seed? But take you⌃ heed to your spirit, and forsake not the wife of your youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Matthew 5:31–32; Mark 10:2–12"> But if you should hate your wife and put her away, says the Lord God of Israel, then ungodliness shall cover your thoughts, says the Lord Almighty: therefore take you⌃ heed to your spirit, and forsake them not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 43:24; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> you⌃ that have provoked God with your words. But you⌃ said, Wherein have we provoked him? In that you⌃ say, Every one that does evil is a pleasing object in the sight of the Lord, and he takes pleasure in such;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, and, Where, etc.">f</a></sup>and where is the God of justice?</span>
