<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:30; Jezekiel 33:17; Jezekiel 3:11"> Son of man, speak to the children of your people, and you shall say to them, On whatever land I shall bring a sword, and the people of the land take one man of them, and set him for their watchman:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 8:1; Nehemiah 4:20; Nehemiah 4:18"> and he shall see the sword coming upon the land, and blow the trumpet, and sound an alarm to the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:13; Acts 18:6; Jeremias 6:17"> and he that hears the sound of the trumpet shall hear indeed, and yet not take heed, and the sword shall come upon him, and overtake him, his blood shall be upon his own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 9:19–21; Hebrews 11:7; John 8:39"> Because he heard the sound of the trumpet, and took no heed, his blood shall be upon him: but the other, because he took heed, has delivered his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:18–20; Jezekiel 33:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> But if the watchman see the sword coming, and do not sound the trumpet, and the people do not watch; and the sword come, and take a soul from amongst them, that soul is taken because of its iniquity; but the blood thereof will I require at the watchman's hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:17–21; Jeremias 26:2; Jezekiel 2:7–8"> And you, son of man, I have set you as a watchman to the house of Israel, and you shall hear a word from my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 20:26–27; Jezekiel 33:6; Jezekiel 33:14"> When I say to the sinner, You shall surely die; if you speak not to warn the wicked from his way, the wicked himself shall die in his iniquity; but his blood will I require at your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:19; Acts 20:26; Proverbs 15:10"> But if you forewarn the wicked of his way to turn from it, and he turn not from his way, he shall die in his ungodliness; but you have delivered your own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 49:14; Jezekiel 37:11; Jezekiel 24:23"> And you, son of man, say to the house of Israel; Thus have you⌃ spoken, saying, Our errors, and our iniquities weigh upon us, and we pine away in them, and how then shall we live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Jezekiel 18:23; 2 Peter 3:9"> Say to them, Thus says the Lord; As I live, I desire not the death of the ungodly, as that the ungodly should turn from his way and live: turn you⌃ heartily from your way; for why will you⌃ die, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:14; Jezekiel 33:18–19; Romans 3:25"> Say to the children of your people, The righteousness of the righteous shall not deliver him, in the day wherein he errs: and the iniquity of the ungodly shall not harm him, in the day wherein he turns from his iniquity, but the righteous erring shall not be able to deliver himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 10:38; Jezekiel 18:4"> When I say to the righteous, You shall live; and he trusts in his righteousness, and shall commit iniquity, none of his righteousnesses shall be remembered; in his unrighteousness which he has wrought, in it shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:27; Jeremias 18:7–8; Osee 14:1"> And when I say to the ungodly, You shall surely die; and he shall turn from his sin, and do judgement and justice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:11; Luke 19:8; Exodus 22:1–4"> and return the pledge, and repay that which he has robbed, and walk in the ordinances of life, so as to do no wrong; he shall surely live, and shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 43:25; Jezekiel 18:22; Michaeas 7:18–19"> None of his sins which he has committed shall be remembered: because he has wrought judgement and righteousness; by them shall he live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–26; Jezekiel 18:25; Jezekiel 18:29"> Yet the children of your people will say, The way of the Lord is not straight: whereas this their way is not straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:12–13; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:38"> When the righteous turns away from his righteousness, and shall commit iniquities, then shall he die in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:27–28; Jezekiel 33:14"> And when the sinner turns from his iniquity, and shall do judgement and righteousness, he shall live by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Jezekiel 18:25; Jezekiel 33:17"> And this is that which you⌃ said, The way of the Lord is not straight. I will judge you, O house of Israel, every one for his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:2; Jeremias 39:1–8; Kings IV 25:4"> And it came to pass in the tenth year of our captivity, in the twelfth month, on the fifth day of the month, that one that had escaped from Jerusalem came to me, saying, The city is taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:3; Jezekiel 3:26–27; Jezekiel 24:26–27"> Now the hand of the Lord had come upon me in the evening, before he came; and he opened my mouth, when he came to me in the morning: and my mouth was open, it was no longer kept closed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:27; Esias 51:2; Acts 7:5"> Son of man, they that inhabit the desolate places on the land of Israel say, Abram was one, and he possessed the land: and we are more numerous; to us the land is given for a possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 13:6; Jeremias 42:22; Jezekiel 39:4"> Therefore say to them, Thus says the Lord God, As I live, surely they that are in the desolate places shall fall by swords and they that are in the open plain shall be given for food to the wild beasts of the field, and them that are in the fortified cities and them that are in the caves I will kill with pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:24; Jeremias 44:2; Jeremias 44:6"> And I will make the land desert, and the pride of her strength shall perish; and the mountains of Israel shall be made desolate by reason of no man passing through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:7; Jezekiel 36:17–18; Jezekiel 22:2–15"> And they shall know that I am the Lord; and I will make their land desert, and it shall be made desolate because of all their abominations which they have wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 22:16–17; Jeremias 23:35; Esias 29:13"> And as for you, son of man, the children of your people are they that speak concerning you by the walls, and in the porches of the houses, and they talk one to another, saying, Let us come together, and let us hear the words that proceed from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 13:22; Psalms 78:36–37; James 1:22–24"> They approach you as a people comes together, and sit before you, and hear your words, but they will not do them: for there is falsehood in their mouth, and their heart goes after their pollutions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Mark 4:16–17; John 5:35"> And you are to them as a sound of a sweet, well-tuned lute, and they will hear your words, but they will not do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremias 28:9; Kings I 3:19–20; Jezekiel 2:5"> But whenever it shall come to pass, they will say, Behold, it is come: and they shall know that there was a prophet in the midst of them.</span>
