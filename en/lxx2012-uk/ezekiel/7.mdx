<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the word of the Lord came to me, saying, Also, you, son of man, say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 8:2; 1 Peter 4:7; Jezekiel 7:3"> Thus says the Lord; An end is come to the land of Israel, the end is come on the four corners of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:30; Jezekiel 5:13; Jezekiel 33:20"> The end is come on you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:7; Jezekiel 11:21; Jezekiel 5:11"> the inhabitant of the land: the time is come, the day has drawn near, not with tumult, nor with pangs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Naum 1:9; Kings IV 21:12–13; Jezekiel 5:9"> Now I will pour out my anger upon you near at hand, and I will accomplish my wrath on you: and I will judge you for your ways, and recompense upon you all your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zacharias 13:7; Jezekiel 7:3; 2 Peter 2:5"> My eye shall not spare, nor will I have any mercy: for I will recompense your ways upon you, and your abominations shall be in the midst of you; and you shall know that I am the Lord that strike you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:28; Jezekiel 7:12; Esias 22:5"> Now the end is come to you, and I will send judgement upon you: and I will take vengeance on your ways, and will recompense all your abominations upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 9:8; Jezekiel 14:19; Jezekiel 20:8"> My eye shall not spare, nor will I have any mercy: for I will recompense your way upon you, and your abominations shall be in the midst of you; and you shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Michaeas 6:9; Esias 9:13"> For thus says the Lord; Behold, the end is come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Behold, the day of the Lord! although the rod has blossomed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Sophonias 1:18; Jezekiel 7:23; Esias 59:6–8"> pride has sprung up, and will break the staff of the wicked one, and that not with tumult, nor with haste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Jezekiel 7:5–7"> The time is come, behold the day: let not the buyer rejoice, and let not the seller mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> For the buyer shall never again return to the seller, neither shall a man cleave with the eye of hope to his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 4:5; Jeremias 7:20; Jeremias 51:27"> Sound you⌃ the trumpet, and pass sentence on all together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 14:18; Jezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> There shall be war with the sword without, and famine and pestilence within: he that is in the field shall die by the sword; and famine and pestilence shall destroy them that are in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 59:11; Esias 38:14; Ezra 9:15"> But they that escape of them shall be delivered, and shall be upon the mountains: and I will kill all the rest, every one for his iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremias 6:24"> All hands shall be completely weakened, and all thighs shall be defiled with moisture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Esias 15:2–3; Esias 3:24"> And they shall gird themselves with sackcloth, and amazement shall cover them; and shame shall be upon them, even upon every face, and baldness upon every head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Sophonias 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> Their silver shall be cast forth in the streets, and their gold shall be despised: their souls shall not be satisfied, and their bellies shall not be filled: for it was the punishment of their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:21; Jeremias 7:30; Jezekiel 8:15–16"> As for their choice ornaments, they employed them for pride, and they made of them images of their abominations: therefore have I made them uncleanness to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; Chronicles II 36:18–19"> And I will deliver them into the hands of strangers to make them a prey, and to the pests of the earth for a spoil; and they shall profane them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> And I will turn away my face from them, and they shall defile my charge, and shall go in to them unguardedly, and profane them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 9:9; Jeremias 27:2; Jezekiel 11:6"> And they shall work uncleanness: because the land is full of strange nations, and the city is full of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Jezekiel 21:31"> And I will turn back the boasting of their strength; and their holy things shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 8:15–16; Esias 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> And though propitiation shall come, and one shall seek peace, yet there shall be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 4:20; Jezekiel 14:1; Michaeas 3:6"> There shall be woe upon woe, and there shall be message upon message; and a vision shall be sought from a prophet; but the law shall perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:30; Jezekiel 7:4–8; Jezekiel 26:16"> The prince shall clothe himself with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land shall be made feeble: I will do to them according to their ways, and according to their judgements will I punish them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
