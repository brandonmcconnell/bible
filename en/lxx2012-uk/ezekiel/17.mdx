<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Son of man, relate a tale, and speak a parable to the house of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 8:1; Jeremias 48:40; Jezekiel 17:7"> and you shall say, Thus says the Lord; A great eagle with large wings, spreading them out very far, with many claws, which has the design of entering into Libanus—and he took the choice branches of the cedar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 43:14; Esias 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> he cropped off the ends of the tender twigs, and brought them into the land of Chanaan; he laid them up in a walled city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremias 37:1"> And he took of the seed of the land, and sowed it in a field planted by much water; he set it in a conspicuous place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> And it sprang up, and became a weak and little vine, so that the branches thereof appeared upon it, and its roots were under it: and it became a vine, and put forth shoots, and sent forth its tendrils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:4; Jezekiel 17:15; Kings IV 24:20"> And there was another great eagle, with great wings and many claws: and, behold, this vine bent itself round towards him, and her roots were turned towards him, and she sent forth her branches towards him, that he might water her together with the growth of her plantation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:5–6"> She thrives in a fair field by much water, to produce shoots and bear fruit, that she might become a great vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:10; Jeremias 32:5; Esias 30:1–7"> Therefore say, Thus says the Lord; Shall it prosper? shall not the roots of her tender stem and her fruit be blighted? yes, all her early shoots shall be dried up, and that not by a mighty arm, nor by many people, to tear her up from her roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> And, behold, it thrives: shall it prosper? shall it not wither as soon as the east wind touches it? it shall be withered together with the growth of its shoots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Moreover the word of the Lord came to me saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:19; Jezekiel 17:3; Jezekiel 1:2"> Son of man, say now to the provoking house, Know you⌃ not what these things were? say to them, Whenever the king of Babylon shall come against Jerusalem, then he shall take her king and her princes, and shall take them home to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:13; Kings IV 24:15–17; Jezekiel 17:5"> And he shall take of the seed royal, and shall make a covenant with him, and shall bind him with an oath: and he shall take the princes of the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; Kings I 2:30"> that it may become a weak kingdom, so as never to lift itself up, that he may keep his covenant, and establish it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 52:3; Jeremias 38:18; Jezekiel 17:7"> And if he shall revolt from him, to send his messengers into Egypt, that they may give him horses and much people; shall he prosper? shall he that acts as an adversary be preserved? and shall he that transgresses the covenant be preserved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:13; Jeremias 52:11; Jezekiel 17:18–19"> As I live, says the Lord, verily in the place where the king is that made him king, who dishonoured my oath, and who broke my covenant, shall he die with him in the midst of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 37:7; Jezekiel 4:2; Jezekiel 29:6–7"> And Pharaoh shall make war upon him not with a large force or great multitude, in throwing up a mound, and in building of forts, to cut off souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:24; Chronicles II 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> Whereas he has profaned the oath so as to break the covenant, when, behold, I engage his hand, and he has done all these things to him, he shall not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremias 5:9; Jeremias 5:2"> Therefore say, Thus says the Lord; As I live, surely my oath which he has profaned, and my covenant which he has transgressed, I will even recompense it upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:13; Jezekiel 32:3; Jeremias 2:35"> And I will spread a net upon him, and he shall be caught in its snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 25:5; Kings IV 25:11; Jezekiel 12:14"> In every battle of his they shall fall by the sword, and I will scatter his remnant to every wind: and you⌃ shall know that I the Lord have spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esias 53:2; Zacharias 6:12–13; Esias 11:1–5"> For thus says the Lord; I will even take of the choice branches of the cedar from the top thereof, I will crop off their hearts, and I will plant it on a high mountain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Jezekiel 31:6; Esias 2:2"> and I will hang it on a lofty mountain of Israel: yes, I will plant it, and it shall put forth shoots, and shall bear fruit, and it shall be a great cedar: and every bird shall rest beneath it, even every fowl shall rest under its shadow: its branches shall be restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Jezekiel 24:14"> And all the trees of the field shall know that I am the Lord that bring low the high tree, and exalt the low tree, and wither the green tree, and cause the dry tree to flourish: I the Lord have spoken, and will do it.</span>
