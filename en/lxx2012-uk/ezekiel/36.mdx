<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:4; Jezekiel 34:14; Jezekiel 37:22"> And you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel, and say to the mountains of Israel, Hear you⌃ the word of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Jezekiel 25:3; Jezekiel 35:10"> Thus says the Lord God; Because the enemy has said against you, Aha, the old waste places are become a possession for us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:13–14; Lamentations 2:15–16; Jeremias 18:16"> therefore prophesy, and say, Thus says the Lord God; Because you⌃ have been dishonoured, and hated by those round about you, that you⌃ might be a possession to the remainder of the nations, and you⌃ became a byword, and a reproach to the nations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 11:11; Jezekiel 36:6"> therefore, you⌃ mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord; Thus says the Lord to the mountains, and to the hills, and to the streams, and to the valleys, and to the places that have been made desolate and destroyed, and to the cities that have been deserted, and have become a spoil and a trampling to the nations that were left round about;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Michaeas 7:8; Jezekiel 36:3; Psalms 137:7"> therefore, thus says the Lord; Verily in the fire of my wrath have I spoken against the rest of the nations, and against all Idumea, because they have appropriated my land to themselves for a possession with joy, disregarding the lives of the inhabitants, to destroy it by plunder:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 123:3–4; Jezekiel 34:29; Psalms 74:23"> therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and say to the mountains, and to the hills, and to the valleys, and to the forests, Thus says the Lord; Behold, I have spoken in my jealousy and in my wrath, because you⌃ have borne the reproaches of the heathen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:5; Amos 1:1–15; Jezekiel 20:15"> therefore I will lift up my hand against the nations that are round about you; they shall bear their reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 27:6; Hebrews 10:37; Jezekiel 34:26–29"> But your grapes and your fruits, O mountains of Israel, shall my people eat; for they are hoping to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:11; Osee 2:21–23; Jezekiel 36:34"> For, behold, I am towards you, and I will have respect to you, and you⌃ shall be tilled and sown:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:33; Jeremias 33:12; Jezekiel 36:37"> and I will multiply men upon you, even all the house of Israel to the end: and the cities shall be inhabited, and the desolate land shall be built upon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 33:12; Jeremias 23:5–8; Jeremias 31:27"> And I will multiply men and cattle upon you; and I will cause you to dwell as at the beginning, and will treat you well, as in your former times: and you⌃ shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 15:7; Jezekiel 36:13; Jeremias 32:44"> And I will increase men upon you, even my people Israel; and they shall inherit you, and you⌃ shall be to them for a possession; and you⌃ shall no more be bereaved of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 13:32"> Thus says the Lord God: Because they said to you, You land devour men, and have been bereaved of your nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:25–28; Amos 9:15; Esias 60:21"> therefore you shall no more devour men, and you shall no more bereave your nation, says the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:29; Psalms 89:50; Jezekiel 36:6"> And there shall no more be heard against you the reproach of the nations, and you⌃ shall no more bear the revilings of the peoples, says the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 2:7; Numbers 35:33–34; Michaeas 2:10"> Son of man, the house of Israel lived upon their land, and defiled it by their way, and with their idols, and with their uncleannesses; and their way was before me like the uncleanness of a removed woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:21; Jezekiel 7:8; Lamentations 2:4"> So I poured out my wrath upon them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 39:24; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jezekiel 5:12"> and I dispersed them amongst the nations, and utterly scattered them through the countries: I judged them according to their way and according to their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Esias 52:5; Jeremias 33:24"> And they went in amongst the nations, amongst which they went, and they profaned my holy name, while it was said of them, These are the people of the Lord, and they came forth out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 48:9; Psalms 74:18; Jezekiel 20:9"> But I spared them for the sake of my holy name, which the house of Israel profaned amongst the nations, amongst whom they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Jezekiel 36:32; Psalms 115:1–2"> Therefore say to the house of Israel, Thus says the Lord; I do not this, O house of Israel, for your sakes, but because of my holy name, which you⌃ have profaned amongst the nations, amongst whom you⌃ went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:41; Esias 5:16; Jezekiel 39:7"> And I will sanctify my great name, which was profaned amongst the nations, which you⌃ profaned in the midst of them; and the nations shall know that I am the Lord, when I am sanctified amongst you before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:21; Jezekiel 34:13; Amos 9:14–15"> And I will take you out from the nations, and will gather you out of all the lands, and will bring you into your own land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 3:5; Jeremias 33:8"> and I will sprinkle clean water upon you, and you⌃ shall be purged from all your uncleannesses, and from all your idols, and I will cleanse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 11:19–20; Psalms 51:10; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> And I will give you a new heart, and will put a new spirit in you: and I will take away the heart of stone out of your flesh, and will give you a heart of flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Titus 3:3–6; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Corinthians 3:16"> And I will put my Spirit in you, and will cause you to walk in my ordinances, and to keep my judgements, and do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremias 31:33; Jeremias 32:38; Jezekiel 37:27"> And you⌃ shall dwell upon the land which I gave to your fathers; and you⌃ shall be to me a people, and I will be to you a God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 1:7–9; Matthew 1:21; Osee 2:21–23"> And I will save you from all your uncleannesses: and I will call for the corn, and multiply it, and will not bring famine upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:27; Joel 2:26; Deuteronomy 29:23–28"> And I will multiply the fruit of the trees, and the produce of the field, that you⌃ may not bear the reproach of famine amongst the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:9; Jezekiel 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> And you⌃ shall remember your evil ways and your practices that were not good, and you⌃ shall be hateful in your own sight for your transgressions and for your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Jezekiel 36:22; 2 Timothy 1:9"> Not for your sakes do I this, says the Lord God, as it is known to you: be you⌃ ashamed and confounded for your ways, O house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Esias 58:12; Jezekiel 36:10; Jeremias 33:10"> Thus says the Lord God; In the day wherein I shall cleanse you from all your iniquities I will also cause the cities to be inhabited, and the waste places shall be built upon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:14; Jeremias 25:9–11; Chronicles II 36:21"> and the desolate land shall be cultivated, whereas it was desolate in the eyes of every one that passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Esias 51:3; Joel 2:3; Jeremias 33:9"> And they shall say, That desolate land is become like a garden of delight; and the waste and desolate and ruined cities are inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Numbers 23:19; Jezekiel 17:24"> And the nations, as many as shall have been left round about you, shall know that I the Lord have built the ruined cities and planted the waste lands: I the Lord have spoken, and will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 5:14; Hebrews 10:21–22; Jezekiel 14:3"> Thus says the Lord God; Yet for this will I be sought by the house of Israel, to establish them; I will multiply them even men as sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings III 8:63; Acts 2:5–11; John 10:16"> as holy sheep, as the sheep of Jerusalem in her feasts; thus shall the desert cities be full of flocks of men: and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
