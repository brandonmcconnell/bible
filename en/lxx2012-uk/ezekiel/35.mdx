<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:1; Jezekiel 21:1; Jezekiel 34:1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Jezekiel 25:8"> Son of man, set your face against mount Seir, and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 6:12; Jezekiel 35:7; Jezekiel 6:14"> and say to it, Thus says the Lord God; Behold, I am against you, O mount Seir, and I will stretch out my hand against you, and will make you a waste, and you shall be made desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 35:9; Malachias 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> And I will cause desolation in your cities, and you shall be desolate, and you shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Obdias 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> Because you have been a perpetual enemy, and have laid wait craftily for the house of Israel, with the hand of enemies with a sword, in the time of injustice, at the last:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obdias 1:15"> Therefore, as I live, says the Lord God, verily you have sinned even to blood, therefore blood shall pursue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:11; Jezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> And I will make mount Seir a waste, and desolate, and I will destroy from off it men and cattle:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:12; Jezekiel 32:4–5; Jezekiel 39:4–5"> and I will fill your hills and your valleys with slain men, and in all your plains there shall fall in you men slain with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:13; Malachias 1:3–4; Jezekiel 6:7"> I will make you a perpetual desolation, and your cities shall not be inhabited any more: and you shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:5; Psalms 83:4–12; Jezekiel 48:35"> Because you said, The two nations and the two countries shall be mine, and I shall inherit them; whereas the Lord is there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalms 73:17–18"> therefore, as I live, says the Lord, I will even deal with you according to your enmity, and I will be made known to you when I shall judge you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:2; Psalms 83:12; Jezekiel 6:7"> and you shall know that I am the Lord. I have heard the voice of your blasphemies, whereas you have said, The desert mountains of Israel are given to us for food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Esias 36:20; Jeremias 29:23"> and you have spoken swelling words against me with your mouth: I have heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 51:48; Esias 14:7–8; Esias 65:13–15"> Thus says the Lord; When all the earth is rejoicing, I will make you desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Esias 34:5–6"> You shall be desert, O mount Seir, and all Idumea; and it shall be utterly consumed: and you shall know that I am the Lord their God.</span>
