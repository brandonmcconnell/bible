<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 7:20; Jezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 21:5"> And you, son of man, take you a sword sharper than a barber's razor; you shall procure it for yourself, and shall bring it upon your head, and upon your beard: and you shall take a pair of scales, and shall separate the hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:12; Leviticus 26:33; Jezekiel 4:1–8"> A fourth part you shall burn in the fire in the midst of the city, at the fulfilment of the days of the siege: and you shall take a fourth part, and burn it up in the midst of it: and a fourth part you shall cut with a sword round about it: and a fourth part you shall scatter to the wind; and I will draw out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 52:16; Jeremias 40:6; Jeremias 39:10"> And you shall take thence a few in number, and shall wrap them in the fold of your garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 4:4; Jeremias 41:1–18; Kings IV 25:25"> And you shall take of these again, and cast them into the midst of the fire, and burn them up with fire: from thence shall come forth fire; and you shall say to the whole house of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:1; Deuteronomy 4:6; Jeremias 6:6"> Thus says the Lord; This is Jerusalem: I have set her and the countries round about her in the midst of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 11:10; Jezekiel 16:47–48; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> And you shall declare my ordinances to the lawless one from out of the nations; and my statutes to the sinful one of the countries round about her: because they have rejected my ordinances, and have not walked in my statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 2:10–11; Chronicles II 33:9; Kings IV 21:9–11"> Therefore thus says the Lord, Because your occasion for sin has been taken from the nations round about you, and you⌃ have not walked in my statutes, nor kept my ordinances, nay, you⌃ have not even done according to the ordinances of the nations round about you; therefore thus says the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 15:7; Jeremias 24:9; Jezekiel 21:3"> Behold, I am against you, and I will execute judgement in the midst of you in the sight of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:21; Daniel 9:12; Amos 3:2"> And I will do in you things which I have not done, and the like of which I will not do again, for all your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 2:6; Deuteronomy 28:64; Jezekiel 12:14"> Therefore the fathers shall eat their children in the midst of you, and children shall eat their fathers; and I will execute judgements in you, and I will scatter all that are left of you to every wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:20; Jezekiel 7:9; Jezekiel 11:18"> Therefore, as I live, says the Lord; surely, because you have defiled my holy things with all your abominations, I also will reject you; my eye shall not spare, and I will have no mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:2; Jeremias 15:2; Jeremias 43:10–11"> A fourth part of you shall be cut off by pestilence, and a fourth part of you shall be consumed in the midst of you with famine: and as for another fourth part of you, I will scatter them to every wind; and a fourth part of you shall fall by sword round about you, and I will draw out a sword after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 1:24; Jezekiel 36:5–6; Jezekiel 21:17"> And my wrath and my anger shall be accomplished upon them: and you shall know that I the Lord have spoken in my jealousy, when I have accomplished my anger upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:4; Nehemiah 2:17; Psalms 79:1–4"> And I will make you desolate, and your daughters round about you, in the sight of every one that passes through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:17; Jeremias 22:8–9; Kings III 9:7"> And you shall be mourned over and miserable amongst the nations round about you, when I have executed judgements in you in the vengeance of my wrath. I the Lord have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:16; Deuteronomy 32:23–24; Jezekiel 14:13"> And when I have sent against them shafts of famine, then they shall be consumed, and I will break the strength of your bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:22; Jezekiel 14:21; Leviticus 26:22"> So I will send forth against you famine and evil beasts, and I will take vengeance upon you; and pestilence and blood shall pass through upon you; and I will bring a sword upon you round about. I the Lord have spoken.</span>
