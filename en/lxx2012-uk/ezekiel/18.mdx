<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 31:29–30; Lamentations 5:7; Jezekiel 37:11"> Son of man, what mean you⌃ by this parable amongst the children of Israel, saying, The fathers have eaten unripe grapes, and the children's teeth have been set on edge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:19–20; Jezekiel 33:11–20; Jezekiel 18:30"> As I live, says the Lord, surely this parable shall no more be spoken in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:20; Romans 6:23; Numbers 16:22"> For all souls are mine; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son, they are mine: the soul that sins, it shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:14; 1 John 3:7; Proverbs 21:3"> But the man who shall be just, who executes judgement and righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:15; Deuteronomy 4:19; Jezekiel 6:13"> who shall not eat upon the mountains, and shall not at all lift up his eyes to the devices of the house of Israel, and shall not defile his neighbour's wife, and shall not draw near to her that is removed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:16; Exodus 22:26; Jezekiel 18:12"> and shall not oppress any man, but shall return the pledge of the debtor, and shall be guilty of no plunder, shall give his bread to the hungry, and clothe the naked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Zacharias 8:16; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> and shall not lend his money upon usury, and shall not receive usurious increase, and shall turn back his hand from injustice, shall execute righteous judgement between a man and his neighbour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:11; Ambacum 2:4; Amos 5:4"> and has walked in my commandments and kept my ordinances, to do them; he is righteous, he shall surely live, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 John 3:12; John 18:40"> And if he beget a mischievous son, shedding blood and committing sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:14; Matthew 7:21–27; Kings III 13:8"> who has not walked in the way of his righteous father, but has even eaten upon the mountains, and has defiled his neighbour's wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:17; Jezekiel 8:6; Kings IV 21:11"> and has oppressed the poor and needy, and has committed robbery, and not restored a pledge, and has set his eyes upon idols, has wrought iniquities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 20:9; Jezekiel 3:18"> has lent upon usury, and taken usurious increase; he shall by no means live: he has wrought all these iniquities; he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:21; Proverbs 23:24; Jeremias 8:6"> And if he beget a son, and the son see all his father's sins which he has wrought, and fear, and not do according to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:6–7; Jezekiel 18:11–13"> and if he has not eaten on the mountains, and has not set his eyes on the devices of the house of Israel, and has not defiled his neighbour's wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:7; Esias 58:7–10; Job 22:7"> and has not oppressed a man, and has not retained the pledge, nor committed robbery, has given his bread to the hungry, and has clothed the naked,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:8–9; Matthew 18:27–35; Jezekiel 18:13"> and has turned back his hand from unrighteousness, has not received interest or usurious increase, has wrought righteousness, and walked in my ordinances; he shall not die for the iniquities of his father, he shall surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:18; Jezekiel 18:26; Jezekiel 18:24"> But if his father grievously afflict, or plunder, he has wrought enmity in the midst of my people, and shall die in his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Zacharias 1:3–6; Deuteronomy 5:9"> But you⌃ will say, Why has not the son borne the iniquity of the father? Because the son has wrought judgement and mercy, has kept all my statues, and done them, he shall surely live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Kings IV 14:6; Jezekiel 18:4"> But the soul that sins shall die: and the son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, nor shall the father bear the iniquity of the son: the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the iniquity of the transgressor shall be upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:27–28; Titus 2:11–14; Jezekiel 33:19"> And if the transgressor turn away from all his iniquities which he has committed, and keep all my commandments, and do justice and mercy, he shall surely live, and shall by no means die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Michaeas 7:19; Jezekiel 33:16; Esias 43:25"> None of his trespasses which he has committed shall be remembers: in his righteousness which he has done he shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:11; 1 Timothy 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9"> Shall I at all desire death of the sinner, says the Lord, as I desire that he should turn from his evil way, and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:18; Proverbs 21:16; 2 John 1:8"> But when the righteous man turns away from his righteousness, and commits iniquity, according to all the transgressions which the transgressor has wrought, none of his righteousness which he has wrought shall be at all remembered: in his trespass wherein he has trespassed, and in his sins wherein he has sinned, in them shall he die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:17; Malachias 3:13–15; Malachias 2:17"> Yet you⌃ have said, The way of the Lord is not straight. Hear now, all the house of Israel; will not my way be straight? Is your way straight?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When the righteous turns away from his righteousness and commits a trespass, and dies in the trespass he has committed, he shall even die in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:21; Esias 1:18; Esias 55:7"> And when the wicked man turns away from his wickedness that he has committed, and shall do judgement and justice, he has kept his soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Luke 15:17–18; James 2:10–12"> and has turned away from all his ungodliness which he has committed: he shall surely live, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:25; Proverbs 19:3; Jezekiel 18:2"> Yet the house of Israel say, The way of the Lord is not right. Is not my way right, O house of Israel? is not your way wrong?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:21; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 3:2"> I will judge you, O house of Israel, says the Lord, each one according to his way: be converted, and turn from all your ungodliness, and it shall not become to you the punishment of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Jezekiel 36:26; Jezekiel 11:19"> Cast away from yourselves all your ungodliness wherein you⌃ have sinned against me; and make to yourselves a new heart and a new spirit: for why should you⌃ die, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Jezekiel 18:23; Jezekiel 33:11"> For I desire not the death of him that dies, says the Lord.</span>
