<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 13:6; Jezekiel 21:12; Joel 1:11"> Son of man, prophesy, and say, Thus says the Lord; Woe, woe worth the day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obdias 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 1:15"> For the day of the Lord is near, a day of cloud; it shall be the end of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:19; Jezekiel 29:8; Esias 16:7"> And the sword shall come upon the Egyptians, and there shall be tumult in Ethiopia, and in Egypt men shall fall down slain together, and her foundations shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 25:20; Jezekiel 27:10; Jeremias 25:24"> Persians, and Cretans, and Lydians, and Libyans, and all the mixed multitude, and they of the children of my covenant, shall fall by the sword therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:10; Esias 20:3–6; Esias 31:3"> And the supports of Egypt shall fall; and the pride of her strength shall come down from Magdol to Syene: they shall fall by the sword in it, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:12; Jeremias 25:18–26; Jezekiel 32:18–32"> And it shall be made desolate in the midst of desolate countries, and their cities shall be desolate in the midst of desolate cities:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:6; Jezekiel 29:16; Amos 1:7"> and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I shall send fire upon Egypt, and when all that help her shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 18:1–2; Esias 23:5; Jezekiel 32:9–10"> In that day shall messengers go forth hasting to destroy Ethiopia utterly, and there shall be tumult amongst them in the day of Egypt: for, behold it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:19; Jezekiel 32:11–16; Jezekiel 29:4–5"> Thus says the Lord God; I will also destroy the multitude of the Egyptians by the hand of Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:7; Jezekiel 32:12; Jezekiel 39:11–20"> his hand and his people's; they are plagues sent forth from the nations to destroy the land: and they all shall unsheath their swords against Egypt, and the land shall be filled with slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:3; Naum 1:4; Revelation 16:12"> And I will make their rivers desolate, and will destroy the land and the fulness of it by the hands of strangers: I the Lord have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zacharias 10:11; Jeremias 46:14; Jeremias 2:16"> For thus says the Lord God; I will also destroy the nobles from Memphis, and the princes of Memphis out of the land of Egypt; and they shall be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:14; Jeremias 46:25; Psalms 78:43"> And I will destroy the land of Phathore, and will send fire upon Tanis, and will execute vengeance on Diospolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 16:1; Naum 1:6; Psalms 11:6"> And I will pour out my wrath upon Sais the strength of Egypt, and will destroy the multitude of Memphis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:8–9; Jezekiel 28:18"> And I will send fire upon Egypt; and Syene shall be sorely troubled; and there shall be a breaking in Diospolis, and waters shall be poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 41:45"> The youths of Heliopolis and Bubastum shall fall by the sword, and the women shall go into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:3; Jezekiel 29:15; Esias 10:27"> And the day shall be darkened in Taphnae, when I have broken there the sceptres of Egypt: and the pride of her strength shall perish there: and a cloud shall cover her, and her daughters shall be taken prisoners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:14; Revelation 17:1; Jezekiel 5:8"> And I will execute judgement on Egypt; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:17; Jezekiel 26:1; Jezekiel 29:1"> And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 30:13; Jeremias 46:11; Psalms 37:17"> Son of man, I have broken the arms of Pharao, king of Egypt; and, behold, it has not been bound up to be healed, to have a plaster put upon it, or to be strengthened to lay hold of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 37:17; Jeremias 37:7; Kings IV 24:7"> Therefore thus says the Lord God; Behold, I am against Pharao king of Egypt, and I will break his strong and outstretched arms, and will strike down his sword out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:26; Jezekiel 30:17–18; Jezekiel 29:12–13"> And I will disperse the Egyptians amongst the nations, and will utterly scatter them amongst the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:25; Sophonias 2:12; Esias 45:5"> And I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and put my sword into his hand: and he shall bring it upon Egypt, and shall take her plunder and seize her spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:21; Jezekiel 32:15; Jezekiel 29:16"> Yes, I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and the arms of Pharao shall fail: and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I have put my sword into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he shall stretch it out over the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:42; Jezekiel 29:12; Jezekiel 30:23"> And I will disperse the Egyptians amongst the nations, and utterly scatter them amongst the countries; and they all shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
