<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 1:2–9; Amos 3:2; Jezekiel 12:3–16"> And you, son of man, take you a brick, and you shall set it before your face, and shall portray on it the city, even Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:22; Jeremias 6:6; Luke 19:42–44"> And you shall besiege it, and build works against it, and throw up a mound round about it, and pitch camps against it, and set up engines round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:6; Esias 8:18; Esias 20:3"> And take you to yourself an iron pan, and you shall set it for an iron wall between you and the city: and you shall set your face against it, and it shall be in a siege, and you shall besiege it. This is a sign to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 16:22; Leviticus 10:17; Numbers 18:1"> And you shall lie upon your left side, and lay the iniquities of the house of Israel upon it, according to the number of the hundred and fifty days during which you shall lie upon it: and you shall bear their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Esias 53:6"> For I have appointed you their iniquities for a number of days, for one hundred and ninety days: so you shall bear the iniquities of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> And you shall accomplish this, and then shall lie on your right side, and shall bear the iniquities of the house of Juda forty days: I have appointed you a day for a year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 52:10; Jezekiel 21:2; Jezekiel 6:2"> So you shall set your face to the siege of Jerusalem, and shall strengthen your arm, and shall prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 3:25"> And, behold, I have prepared bonds for you, land you may not turn from your one side to the other, until the days of your siege shall be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:13; Esias 28:25; Jezekiel 4:16"> Take you also to you wheat, and barley, and beans, and lentils, and millet, and bread-corn; and you shall cast them into one earthen vessel, and shall make them into loaves for yourself; and you shall eat them one hundred and ninety days, according to the number of the days during which you sleep on your side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Jezekiel 45:12; Deuteronomy 28:51–68"> And you shall eat your food by weight, twenty shekels a day: from time to time shall you eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 5:13; John 3:34; Jezekiel 4:16"> And you shall drink water by measure, even from time to time you shall drink the sixth part of a hin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> And you shall eat them as a barley cake: you shall bake them before their eyes in man's dung.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Osee 9:3–4"> And you shall say, Thus says the Lord God of Israel; Thus shall the children of Israel eat unclean things amongst the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 10:14; Jezekiel 20:49; Jezekiel 9:8"> Then I said, Not so, Lord God of Israel: surely my soul has not been defiled with uncleanness; nor have I eaten, that which died of itself or was torn of beasts from my birth until now; neither has any corrupt flesh entered into my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said to me, Behold, I have given you dung of oxen instead of man's dung, and you shall prepare your loaves upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:16; Esias 3:1; Leviticus 26:26"> And he said to me, Son of man, behold, I break the support of bread in Jerusalem: and they shall eat bread by weight and in lack; and shall drink water by measure, and in a state of ruin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jezekiel 33:10"> that they may lack bread and water; and a man and his brother shall be brought to ruin, and they shall pine away in their iniquities.</span>
