<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Esias 14:13–14"> And you, son of man, say to the prince of Tyrus, Thus says the Lord; Because your heart has been exalted, and you have said, I am God, I have inhabited the dwelling of God in the heart of the sea; yet you are man and not God, though you have set your heart as the heart of God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:27–28"> are you wiser than Daniel? or have not the wise instructed you with their knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Proverbs 23:4–5; Proverbs 18:11"> Have you gained power for yourself by your own knowledge or your own prudence, and gotten gold and silver in your treasures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Psalms 52:7; Jezekiel 28:2"> By your abundant knowledge and your traffic you have multiplied your power; your heart has been lifted up by your power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:2; Exodus 9:17; James 1:11"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Since you have set your heart as the hart of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:12; Jezekiel 32:12; Jezekiel 30:11"> because of this, behold, I will bring on you strange plagues from the nations; and they shall draw their swords against you, and against the beauty of your knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:26–27; Jezekiel 27:34; Proverbs 1:12"> and they shall bring down your beauty to destruction. And they shall bring you down; and you shall die the death of the slain in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:2; Esias 31:3; Psalms 82:7"> Will you indeed say, I am God, before them that kill you? whereas you are man, and not God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 31:18; Jezekiel 32:19; Philippians 3:3"> You shall perish by the hands of strangers amongst the multitude of the uncircumcised: for I have spoken it, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 19:1; Jezekiel 26:17; 2 Corinthians 1:22"> Son of man, take up a lamentation for the prince of Tyre, and say to him, Thus says the Lord God; You are a seal of resemblance, and crown of beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:15; Genesis 2:8; Jezekiel 27:16"> You were in the delight of the paradise of God; you have bound upon you every precious stone, the sardius, and topaz, and emerald, and carbuncle, and sapphire, and jasper, and silver, and gold, and ligure, and agate, and amethyst, and chrysolite, and beryl, and onyx: and you have filled your treasures and your stores in you with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 14:12–15; Exodus 30:26; Jezekiel 28:13"> From the day that you were created you was with the cherub: I set you on the holy mount of God; you were in the midst of the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Jezekiel 28:17–18; Romans 7:9"> You were faultless in your days, from the day that you were created, until iniquity was found in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Ambacum 2:8; Ambacum 2:17"> Of the abundance of your merchandise you have filled your storehouses with iniquity, and have sinned: therefore you have been cast down wounded from the mount of God, and the cherub has brought you out of the midst of the stones of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:5; Jezekiel 31:10; James 4:6"> Your heart has been lifted up because of your beauty; your knowledge has been corrupted with your beauty: because of the multitude of your sins I have cast you to the ground, I have caused you to be put to open shame before kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachias 4:3; Revelation 18:8; Amos 1:9–10"> Because of the multitude of your sins and the iniquities of your merchandise, I have profaned your sacred things; and I will bring fire out of the midst of you, this shall devour you; and I will make you to be ashes upon your land before all that see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:21; Revelation 18:21; Revelation 18:15–19"> And all that know you amongst the nations shall groan over you: you are gone to destruction, and you shall not exist any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremias 25:22"> Son of man, set your face against Sidon, and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Jezekiel 20:41"> and say, Thus says the Lord; Behold, I am against you, O Sidon; and I will be glorified in you; and you shall know that I am the Lord, when I have wrought judgements in you, and I will be sanctified in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:22; Jezekiel 26:6; Jezekiel 25:11"> Blood and death shall be in your streets; and men wounded with swords shall fall in you and on every side of you; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> And there shall no more be in the house of Israel a thorn of bitterness and a pricking briar proceeding from them that are round about them, who dishonoured them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 32:37; Jezekiel 20:41; Jezekiel 36:28"> Thus says the Lord God; I will also gather Israel from the nations, amongst whom they have been scattered, and I will be sanctified amongst them, and before the peoples and nations: and they shall dwell upon their land, which I gave to my servant Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:24; Jezekiel 28:22; Jeremias 32:15"> Yes, they shall dwell upon it safely, and they shall build houses, and plant vineyards, and dwell securely, when I shall execute judgement on all that have dishonoured them, even on those that are round about them; and they shall know that I am the Lord their God, and the God of their fathers.</span>
