<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The hopes of a man void of understanding are vain and false: and dreams lift up fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Whoso regards dreams is like him that catches at a shadow, and follows after the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another, even as the likeness of a face to a face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Of an unclean thing what can be cleansed? and from that thing which is false what truth can come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Divinations, and soothsayings, and dreams, are vain: and the heart fancies, as a woman's heart in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> If they be not sent from the most High in your visitation, set not your heart upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For dreams have deceived many, and they have failed that put their trust in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The law shall be found perfect without lies: and wisdom is perfection to a faithful mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> A man that has travelled knows many things; and he that has much experience will declare wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He that has no experience knows little: but he that has travelled is full of prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> When I travelled, I saw many things; and I understand more than I can express.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> I was ofttimes in danger of death: yet I was delivered because of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The spirit of those that fear the Lord shall live; for their hope is in him that saves them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Whoso fears the Lord shall not fear nor be afraid; for he is his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Blessed is the soul of him that fears the Lord: to whom does he look? and who is his strength?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him, he is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat, and a cover from the sun at noon, a preservation from stumbling, and an help from falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He raises up the soul, and lightens the eyes: he gives health, life, and blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He that sacrifices of a thing wrongfully gotten, his offering is ridiculous; and the gifts of unjust men are not accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The most High is not pleased with the offerings of the wicked; neither is he pacified for sin by the multitude of sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Whoso brings an offering of the goods of the poor does as one that kills the son before his father's eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The bread of the needy is their life: he that defrauds him thereof is a man of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He that takes away his neighbour's living slays him; and he that defrauds the labourer of his hire is a bloodshedder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When one builds, and another pulls down, what profit have they then but labour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> When one prays, and another curses, whose voice will the Lord hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He that washes himself after the touching of a dead body, if he touch it again, what avails his washing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So is it with a man that fasts for his sins, and goes again, and does the same: who will hear his prayer? or what does his humbling profit him?</span>
