<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Set your heart upon your goods; and say not, I have enough for my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Follow not your own mind and your strength, to walk in the ways of your heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And say not, Who shall control me for my works? for the Lord will surely revenge your pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Say not, I have sinned, and what harm has happened to me? for the Lord is longsuffering, he will in no wise let you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Concerning propitiation, be not without fear to add sin to sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And say not His mercy is great; he will be pacified for the multitude of my sins: for mercy and wrath come from him, and his indignation rests upon sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Make no tarrying to turn to the Lord, and put not off from day to day: for suddenly shall the wrath of the Lord come forth, and in your security you shall be destroyed, and perish in the day of vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Set not your heart upon goods unjustly gotten, for they shall not profit you in the day of calamity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Winnow not with every wind, and go not into every way: for so does the sinner that has a double tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Be stedfast in your understanding; and let your word be the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Be swift to hear; and let your life be sincere; and with patience give answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If you have understanding, answer your neighbour; if not, lay your hand upon your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Honour and shame is in talk: and the tongue of man is his fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Be not called a whisperer, and lie not in wait with your tongue: for a foul shame is upon the thief, and an evil condemnation upon the double tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Be not ignorant of any thing in a great matter or a small.</span>
