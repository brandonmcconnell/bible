<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Hear me your father, O children, and do thereafter, that you⌃ may be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For the Lord has given the father honour over the children, and has confirmed the authority of the mother over the sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Whoso honours his father makes an atonement for his sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he that honours his mother is as one that lays up treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Whoso honours his father shall have joy of his own children; and when he makes his prayer, he shall be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that honours his father shall have a long life; and he that is obedient to the Lord shall be a comfort to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He that fears the Lord will honour his father, and will do service to his parents, as to his masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Honour your father and mother both in word and deed, that a blessing may come upon you from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For the blessing of the father establishes the houses of children; but the curse of the mother roots out foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Glory not in the dishonour of your father; for your father's dishonour is no glory to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father; and a mother in dishonour is a reproach to the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> My son, help your father in his age, and grieve him not as long as he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if his understanding fail, have patience with him; and despise him not when you are in your full strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For the relieving of your father shall not be forgotten: and instead of sins it shall be added to build you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> In the day of your affliction it shall be remembered; your sins also shall melt away, as the ice in the fair warm weather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He that forsakes his father is as a blasphemer; and he that angers his mother is cursed: of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> My son, go on with your business in meekness; so shall you be beloved of him that is approved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The greater you are, the more humble yourself, and you shall find favour before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Many are in high place, and of renown: but mysteries are revealed to the meek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the power of the Lord is great, and he is honoured of the lowly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Seek not out things that are too hard for you, neither search the things that are above your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But what is commanded you, think thereupon with reverence, for it is not needful for you to see with your eyes the things that are in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Be not curious in unnecessary matters: for more things are showed to you than men understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For many are deceived by their own vain opinion; and an evil suspicion has overthrown their judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Without eyes you shall lack light: profess not the knowledge therefore that you have not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A stubborn heart shall fare evil at the last; and he that loves danger shall perish therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> An obstinate heart shall be laden with sorrows; and the wicked man shall heap sin upon sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the punishment of the proud there is no remedy; for the plant of wickedness has taken root in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The heart of the prudent will understand a parable; and an attentive ear is the desire of a wise man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Water will quench a flaming fire; and alms makes an atonement for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And he that requites good turns is mindful of that which may come hereafter; and when he falls, he shall find a stay.</span>
