<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> When you will do good know to whom you do it; so shall you be thanked for your benefits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Do good to the godly man, and you shall find a recompence; and if not from him, yet from the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> There can no good come to him that is always occupied in evil, nor to him that gives no alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Give to the godly man, and help not a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Do well to him that is lowly, but give not to the ungodly: hold back your bread, and give it not to him, lest he overmaster you thereby: for else you shall receive twice as much evil for all the good you shall have done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For the most High hates sinners, and will repay vengeance to the ungodly, and keeps them against the mighty day of their punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Give to the good, and help not the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> A friend can’t be known in prosperity: and an enemy can’t be hidden in adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In the prosperity of a man enemies will be grieved: but in his adversity even a friend will depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Never trust your enemy: for like as iron rusts, so is his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Though he humble himself, and go crouching, yet take good heed and beware of him, and you shall be to him as if you had wiped a lookingglass, and you shall know that his rust has not been altogether wiped away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Set him not by you, lest, when he has overthrown you, he stand up in your place; neither let him sit at your right hand, lest he seek to take your seat, and you at the last remember my words, and be pricked therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Who will pity a charmer that is bitten with a serpent, or any such as come near wild beasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So one that goes to a sinner, and is defiled with him in his sins, who will pity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For a while he will abide with you, but if you begin to fall, he will not wait.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> An enemy speaks sweetly with his lips, but in his heart he imagines how to throw you into a pit: he will weep with his eyes, but if he find opportunity, he will not be satisfied with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If adversity come upon you, you shall find him there first; and though he pretend to help you, yet shall he undermine you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He will shake his head, and clap his hands, and whisper much, and change his countenance.</span>
