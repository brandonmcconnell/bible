<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Many have sinned for a small matter; and he that seeks for abundance will turn his eyes away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> As a nail sticks fast between the joinings of the stones; so does sin stick close between buying and selling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Unless a man hold himself diligently in the fear of the Lord, his house shall soon be overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> As when one sifts with a sieve, the refuse remains; so the filth of man in his talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The furnace proves the potter's vessels; so the trial of man is in his reasoning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The fruit declares if the tree have been dressed; so is the utterance of a conceit in the heart of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Praise no man before you hear him speak; for this is the trial of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> If you follow righteousness, you shall obtain her, and put her on, as a glorious long robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The birds will resort to their like; so will truth return to them that practise in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As the lion lies in wait for the prey; so sin for them that work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The discourse of a godly man is always with wisdom; but a fool changes as the moon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If you be amongst the indiscreet, observe the time; but be continually amongst men of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The discourse of fools is irksome, and their sport is the wantonness of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The talk of him that swears much makes the hair stand upright; and their brawls make one stop his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The strife of the proud is blood shedding, and their revilings are grievous to the ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Whoso discovers secrets loses his credit; and shall never find friend to his mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Love your friend, and be faithful to him: but if you betray his secrets, follow no more after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For as a man has destroyed his enemy; so have you lost the love of your neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> As one that lets a bird go out of his hand, so have you let your neighbour go, and shall not get him again</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Follow after him no more, for he is too far off; he is as a roe escaped out of the snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> As for a wound, it may be bound up; and after reviling there may be reconcilement: but he that betrays secrets is without hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He that winks with the eyes works evil: and he that knows him will depart from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When you are present, he will speak sweetly, and will admire your words: but at the last he will writhe his mouth, and slander your sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> I have hated many things, but nothing like him; for the Lord will hate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Whoso casts a stone on high casts it on his own head; and a deceitful stroke shall make wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Whoso digs a pit shall fall therein: and he that sets a trap shall be taken therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He that works mischief, it shall fall upon him, and he shall not know whence it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Mockery and reproach are from the proud; but vengeance, as a lion, shall lie in wait for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They that rejoice at the fall of the righteous shall be taken in the snare; and anguish shall consume them before they die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Malice and wrath, even these are abominations; and the sinful man shall have them both.</span>
