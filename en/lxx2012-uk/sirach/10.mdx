<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A wise judge will instruct his people; and the government of a prudent man is well ordered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> As the judge of the people is himself, so are his officers; and what manner of man the ruler of the city is, such are all they that dwell therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> An unwise king destroys his people; but through the prudence of them which are in authority the city shall be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The power of the earth is in the hand of the Lord, and in due time he will set over it one that is profitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> In the hand of God is the prosperity of man: and upon the person of the scribe shall he lay his honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Bear not hatred to your neighbour for every wrong; and do nothing at all by injurious practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Pride is hateful before God and man: and by both does one commit iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Because of unrighteous dealings, injuries, and riches got by deceit, the kingdom is translated from one people to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Why is earth and ashes proud? There is not a more wicked thing than a covetous man: for such an one sets his own soul to sale; because while he lives he casts away his bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The physician cuts off a long disease; and he that is to day a king to morrow shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For when a man is dead, he shall inherit creeping things, beasts, and worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The beginning of pride is when one departs from God, and his heart is turned away from his Maker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For pride is the beginning of sin, and he that has it shall pour out abomination: and therefore the Lord brought upon them strange calamities, and overthrew them utterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The Lord has cast down the thrones of proud princes, and set up the meek in their stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The Lord has plucked up the roots of the proud nations, and planted the lowly in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The Lord overthrew countries of the heathen, and destroyed them to the foundations of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He took some of them away, and destroyed them, and has made their memorial to cease from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Pride was not made for men, nor furious anger for them that are born of a woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They that fear the Lord are a sure seed, and they that love him an honourable plant: they that regard not the law are a dishonourable seed; they that transgress the commandments are a deceivable seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Amongst brethren he that is chief is honourably; so are they that fear the Lord in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The fear of the Lord goes before the obtaining of authority: but roughness and pride is the losing thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Whether he be rich, noble, or poor, their glory is the fear of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> It is not meet to despise the poor man that has understanding; neither is it convenient to magnify a sinful man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Great men, and judges, and potentates, shall be honoured; yet is there none of them greater than he that fears the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> To the servant that is wise shall they that are free do service: and he that has knowledge will not grudge when he is reformed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Be not overwise in doing your business; and boast not yourself in the time of your distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Better is he that labores, and abounds in all things, than he that boasts himself, and lacks bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> My son, glorify your soul in meekness, and give it honour according to the dignity thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Who will justify him that sins against his own soul? and who will honour him that dishonours his own life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The poor man is honoured for his skill, and the rich man is honoured for his riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He that is honoured in poverty, how much more in riches? and he that is dishonourable in riches, how much more in poverty?</span>
