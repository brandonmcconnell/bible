<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that revenges shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins in remembrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Forgive your neighbour the hurt that he has done to you, so shall your sins also be forgiven when you pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> One man bears hatred against another, and does he seek pardon from the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He shows no mercy to a man, which is like himself: and does he ask forgiveness of his own sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> If he that is but flesh nourish hatred, who will entreat for pardon of his sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Remember your end, and let enmity cease; remember corruption and death, and abide in the commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Remember the commandments, and bear no malice to your neighbour: remember the covenant of the Highest, and wink at ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Abstain from strife, and you shall diminish your sins: for a furious man will kindle strife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> A sinful man disquiets friends, and makes debate amongst them that be at peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As the matter of the fire is, so it burns: and as a man's strength is, so is his wrath; and according to his riches his anger rises; and the stronger they are which contend, the more they will be inflamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> An hasty contention kindles a fire: and an hasty fighting sheds blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If you blow the spark, it shall burn: if you spit upon it, it shall be quenched: and both these come out of your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Curse the whisperer and double-tongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> A backbiting tongue has disquieted many, and driven them from nation to nation: strong cities has it pulled down, and overthrown the houses of great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> A backbiting tongue has cast out virtuous women, and deprived them of their labours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Whoso hearkens to it shall never find rest, and never dwell quietly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The stroke of the whip makes marks in the flesh: but the stroke of the tongue breaks the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Many have fallen by the edge of the sword: but not so many as have fallen by the tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Well is he that is defended through the venom thereof; who has not drawn the yoke thereof, nor has been bound in her bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the yoke thereof is a yoke of iron, and the bands thereof are bands of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The death thereof is an evil death, the grave were better than it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> It shall not have rule over them that fear God, neither shall they be burnt with the flame thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Such as forsake the Lord shall fall into it; and it shall burn in them, and not be quenched; it shall be sent upon them as a lion, and devour them as a leopard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Look that you hedge your possession about with thorns, and bind up your silver and gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And weigh your words in a balance, and make a door and bar for your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Beware you slide not by it, lest you fall before him that lies in wait.</span>
