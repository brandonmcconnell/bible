<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Watching for riches consumes the flesh, and the care thereof drives away sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Watching care will not let a man slumber, as a sore disease breaks sleep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The rich has great labour in gathering riches together; and when he rests, he is filled with his delicates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The poor labores in his poor estate; and when he leaves off, he is still needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that loves gold shall not be justified, and he that follows corruption shall have enough thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Gold has been the ruin of many, and their destruction was present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> It is a stumbling block to them that sacrifice to it, and every fool shall be taken therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Blessed is the rich that is found without blemish, and has not gone after gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Who is he? and we will call him blessed: for wonderful things has he done amongst his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Who has been tried thereby, and found perfect? then let him glory. Who might offend, and has not offended? or done evil, and has not done it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> His goods shall be established, and the congregation shall declare his alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If you sit at a bountiful table, be not greedy upon it, and say not, There is much meat on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Remember that a wicked eye is an evil thing: and what is created more wicked than an eye? therefore it weeps upon every occasion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Stretch not your hand wherever it looks, and thrust it not with him into the dish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Judge not your neighbour by yourself: and be discreet in every point.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Eat as it becomes a man, those things which are set before you; and devour note, lest you be hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Leave off first for manners' sake; and be not unsatiable, lest you offend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When you sit amongst many, reach not your hand out first of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> A very little is sufficient for a man well nurtured, and he fetches not his wind short upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Sound sleep comes of moderate eating: he rises early, and his wits are with him: but the pain of watching, and choler, and pangs of the belly, are with an unsatiable man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And if you have been forced to eat, arise, go forth, vomit, and you shall have rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> My son, hear me, and despise me not, and at the last you shall find as I told you: in all your works be quick, so shall there no sickness come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Whoso is liberal of his meat, men shall speak well of him; and the report of his good housekeeping will be believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But against him that is a niggard of his meat the whole city shall murmur; and the testimonies of his niggardness shall not be doubted of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Show not your valiantness in wine; for wine has destroyed many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The furnace proves the edge by dipping: so does wine the hearts of the proud by drunkenness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Wine is as good as life to a man, if it be drunk moderately: what life is then to a man that is without wine? for it was made to make men glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Wine measurably drunk and in season brings gladness of the heart, and cheerfulness of the mind:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But wine drunken with excess makes bitterness of the mind, with brawling and quarrelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Drunkenness increases the rage of a fool till he offend: it diminishes strength, and makes wounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Rebuke not your neighbour at the wine, and despise him not in his mirth: give him no despiteful words, and press not upon him with urging him to drink.</span>
