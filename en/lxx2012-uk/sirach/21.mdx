<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> My son, have you sinned? do so no more, but ask pardon for your former sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Flee from sin as from the face of a serpent: for if you come too near it, it will bite you: the teeth thereof are as the teeth of a lion, slaying the souls of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> All iniquity is as a two edged sword, the wounds whereof can’t be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> To terrify and do wrong will waste riches: thus the house of proud men shall be made desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> A prayer out of a poor man's mouth reaches to the ears of God, and his judgement comes speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that hates to be reproved is in the way of sinners: but he that fears the Lord will repent from his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> An eloquent man is known far and near; but a man of understanding knows when he slips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that builds his house with other men's money is like one that gathers himself stones for the tomb of his burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The congregation of the wicked is like tow wrapped together: and the end of them is a flame of fire to destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The way of sinners is made plain with stones, but at the end thereof is the pit of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He that keeps the law of the Lord gets the understanding thereof: and the perfection of the fear of the Lord is wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He that is not wise will not be taught: but there is a wisdom which multiplies bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood: and his counsel is like a pure fountain of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The inner parts of a fool are like a broken vessel, and he will hold no knowledge as long as he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> If a skilful man hear a wise word, he will commend it, and add to it: but as soon as one of no understanding hears it, it displeases him, and he casts it behind his back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The talking of a fool is like a burden in the way: but grace shall be found in the lips of the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They enquire at the mouth of the wise man in the congregation, and they shall ponder his words in their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> As is a house that is destroyed, so is wisdom to a fool: and the knowledge of the unwise is as talk without sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Doctrine to fools is as fetters on the feet, and like manacles on the right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A fool lifts up his voice with laughter; but a wise man does scarce smile a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Learning is to a wise man as an ornament of gold, and like a bracelet upon his right arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A foolish man's foot is soon in his neighbour's house: but a man of experience is ashamed of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A fool will peep in at the door into the house: but he that is well nurtured will stand without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> It is the rudeness of a man to listen at the door: but a wise man will be grieved with the disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The lips of talkers will be telling such things as pertain not to them: but the words of such as have understanding are weighed in the balance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The heart of fools is in their mouth: but the mouth of the wise is in their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> When the ungodly curses Satan, he curses his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> A whisperer defiles his own soul, and is hated wherever he dwells.</span>
