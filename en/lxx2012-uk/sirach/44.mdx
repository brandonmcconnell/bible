<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Let us now praise famous men, and our fathers that became the father of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The Lord has wrought great glory by them through his great power from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Such as did bear rule in their kingdoms, men renowned for their power, giving counsel by their understanding, and declaring prophecies:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Leaders of the people by their counsels, and by their knowledge of learning meet for the people, wise and eloquent are their instructions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Such as found out musical tunes, and recited verses in writing:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Rich men furnished with ability, living peaceably in their habitations:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> All these were honoured in their generations, and were the glory of their times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> There be of them, that have left a name behind them, that their praises might be reported.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And some there be, which have no memorial; who are perished, as though they had never been; and are become as though they had never been born; and their children after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But these were merciful men, whose righteousness has not been forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> With their seed shall continually remain a good inheritance, and their children are within the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Their seed stands fast, and their children for their sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Their seed shall remain for ever, and their glory shall not be blotted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their bodies are buried in peace; but their name lives for evermore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The people will tell of their wisdom, and the congregation will show forth their praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Enoch pleased the Lord, and was translated, being an example of repentance to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Noah was found perfect and righteous; in the time of wrath he was taken in exchange for the world; therefore was he left as a remnant to the earth, when the flood came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> An everlasting covenant was made with him, that all flesh should perish no more by the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Abraham was a great father of many people: in glory was there none like to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Who kept the law of the most High, and was in covenant with him: he established the covenant in his flesh; and when he was proved, he was found faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Therefore he assured him by an oath, that he would bless the nations in his seed, and that he would multiply him as the dust of the earth, and exalt his seed as the stars, and cause them to inherit from sea to sea, and from the river to the utmost part of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> With Isaac did he establish likewise for Abraham his father's sake the blessing of all men, and the covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And made it rest upon the head of Jacob. He acknowledged him in his blessing, and gave him an heritage, and divided his portions; amongst the twelve tribes did he part them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And he brought out of him a merciful man, which found favour in the sight of all flesh, even Moses, beloved of God and men, whose memorial is blessed.</span>
