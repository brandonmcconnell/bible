<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In three things I was beautified, and stood up beautiful both before God and men: the unity of brethren, the love of neighbours, a man and a wife that agree together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Three sorts of men my soul hates, and I am greatly offended at their life: a poor man that is proud, a rich man that is a liar, and an old adulterer that doats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> If you have gathered nothing in your youth, how can you find any thing in your age?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O how comely a thing is judgement for grey hairs, and for ancient men to know counsel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> O how comely is the wisdom of old men, and understanding and counsel to men of honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Much experience is the crown of old men, and the fear of God is their glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> There be nine things which I have judged in my heart to be happy, and the tenth I will utter with my tongue: A man that has joy of his children; and he that lives to see the fall of his enemy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Well is him that dwells with a wife of understanding, and that has not slipped with his tongue, and that has not served a man more unworthy than himself:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Well is him that has found prudence, and he that speaks in the ears of them that will hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> O how great is he that finds wisdom! yet is there none above him that fears the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But the love of the Lord passes all things for illumination: he that holds it, whereto shall he be likened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of his love: and faith is the beginning of cleaving to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Give me any plague, but the plague of the heart: and any wickedness, but the wickedness of a woman:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And any affliction, but the affliction from them that hate me: and any revenge, but the revenge of enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> There is no head above the head of a serpent; and there is no wrath above the wrath of an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I had rather dwell with a lion and a dragon, than to keep house with a wicked woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The wickedness of a woman changes her face, and darkens her countenance like sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Her husband shall sit amongst his neighbours; and when he hears it shall sigh bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> All wickedness is but little to the wickedness of a woman: let the portion of a sinner fall upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> As the climbing up a sandy way is to the feet of the aged, so is a wife full of words to a quiet man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Stumble not at the beauty of a woman, and desire her not for pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A woman, if she maintain her husband, is full of anger, impudence, and much reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A wicked woman abates the courage, makes an heavy countenance and a wounded heart: a woman that will not comfort her husband in distress makes weak hands and feeble knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of the woman came the beginning of sin, and through her we all die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Give the water no passage; neither a wicked woman liberty to gad abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If she go not as you would have her, cut her off from your flesh, and give her a bill of divorce, and let her go.</span>
