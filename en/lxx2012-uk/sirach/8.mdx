<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Strive not with a mighty man' lest you fall into his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Be not at variance with a rich man, lest he overweigh you: for gold has destroyed many, and perverted the hearts of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Strive not with a man that is full of tongue, and heap not wood upon his fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Jest not with a rude man, lest your ancestors be disgraced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Reproach not a man that turns from sin, but remember that we are all worthy of punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Dishonour not a man in his old age: for even some of us wax old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Rejoice not over your greatest enemy being dead, but remember that we die all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Despise not the discourse of the wise, but acquaint yourself with their proverbs: for of them you shall learn instruction, and how to serve great men with ease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Miss not the discourse of the elders: for they also learnt of their fathers, and of them you shall learn understanding, and to give answer as need requires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Kindle not the coals of a sinner, lest you be burnt with the flame of his fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Rise not up in anger at the presence of an injurious person, lest he lie in wait to entrap you in your words</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Lend not to him that is mightier than yourself; for if you lend him, count it but lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Be not surety above your power: for if you be surety, take care to pay it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Go not to law with a judge; for they will judge for him according to his honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Travel not by the way with a bold fellow, lest he become grievous to you: for he will do according to his own will, and you shall perish with him through his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Strive not with an angry man, and go not with him into a solitary place: for blood is as nothing in his sight, and where there is no help, he will overthrow you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Consult not with a fool; for he can’t keep counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Do no secret thing before a stranger; for you know not what he will bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Open not your heart to every man, lest he requite you with a shrewd turn.</span>
