<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that lives for ever Hath created all things in general.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The Lord only is righteous, and there is none other but he,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Who governs the world with the palm of his hand, and all things obey his will: for he is the King of all, by his power dividing holy things amongst them from profane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> To whom has he given power to declare his works? and who shall find out his noble acts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Who shall number the strength of his majesty? and who shall also tell out his mercies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As for the wondrous works of the Lord, there may nothing be taken from them, neither may any thing be put to them, neither can the ground of them be found out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> When a man has done, then he begins; and when he leaves off, then he shall be doubtful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> What is man, and whereto serves he? what is his good, and what is his evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The number of a man's days at the most are one hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As a drop of water to the sea, and a gravelstone in comparison of the sand; so are a thousand years to the days of eternity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore is God patient with them, and pours forth his mercy upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He saw and perceived their end to be evil; therefore he multiplied his compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The mercy of man is towards his neighbour; but the mercy of the Lord is upon all flesh: he reproves, and nurtures, and teaches and brings again, as a shepherd his flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He has mercy on them that receive discipline, and that diligently seek after his judgements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> My son, blemish not your good deeds, neither use uncomfortable words when you give any thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Shall not the dew asswage the heat? so is a word better than a gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Behold, is not a word better than a gift? but both are with a gracious man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A fool will upbraid churlishly, and a gift of the envious consumes the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Learn before you speak, and use physick or ever you be sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Before judgement examine yourself, and in the day of visitation you shall find mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Humble yourself before you be sick, and in the time of sins show repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let nothing hinder you to pay your vow in due time, and defer not until death to be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Before you pray, prepare yourself; and be not as one that tempts the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Think upon the wrath that shall be at the end, and the time of vengeance, when he shall turn away his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> When you have enough, remember the time of hunger: and when you are rich, think upon poverty and need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> From the morning until the evening the time is changed, and all things are soon done before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the day of sinning he will beware of offence: but a fool will not observe time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Every man of understanding knows wisdom, and will give praise to him that found her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They that were of understanding in sayings became also wise themselves, and poured forth exquisite parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Go not after your lusts, but refrain yourself from your appetites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If you give your soul the desires that please her, she will make you a laughing stock to your enemies that malign you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Take not pleasure in much good cheer, neither be tied to the expense thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Be not made a beggar by banqueting upon borrowing, when you have nothing in your purse: for you shall lie in wait for your own life, and be talked on.</span>
