<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> My son, if you come to serve the Lord, prepare your soul for temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Set your heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Cleave to him, and depart not away, that you may be increased at your last end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Whatsoever is brought upon you take cheerfully, and be patient when you are changed to a low estate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Believe in him, and he will help you; order your way aright, and trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> You⌃ that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; and go not aside, lest you⌃ fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> You⌃ that fear the Lord, believe him; and your reward shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> You⌃ that fear the Lord, hope for good, and for everlasting joy and mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded? or did any abide in his fear, and was forsaken? or whom did he ever despise, that called upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the Lord is full of compassion and mercy, longsuffering, and very pitiful, and forgives sins, and saves in time of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Woe be to fearful hearts, and faint hands, and the sinner that goes two ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Woe to him that is faint-hearted! for he believes not; therefore shall he not be defended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Woe to you that have lost patience! and what will you⌃ do when the Lord shall visit you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> They that fear the Lord will not disobey his Word; and they that love him will keep his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They that fear the Lord will seek that which is well, pleasing to him; and they that love him shall be filled with the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They that fear the Lord will prepare their hearts, and humble their souls in his sight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Saying, We will fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men: for as his majesty is, so is his mercy.</span>
