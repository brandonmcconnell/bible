<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> All wisdom comes from the Lord, and is with him for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Who can number the sand of the sea, and the drops of rain, and the days of eternity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Who can find out the height of heaven, and the breadth of the earth, and the deep, and wisdom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Wisdom has been created before all things, and the understanding of prudence from everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The word of God most high is the fountain of wisdom; and her ways are everlasting commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> To whom has the root of wisdom been revealed? or who has known her wise counsels?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> To whom has the knowledge of wisdom been made manifest? and who has understood her great experience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> There is one wise and greatly to be feared, the Lord sitting upon his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He created her, and saw her, and numbered her, and poured her out upon all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> She is with all flesh according to his gift, and he has given her to them that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The fear of the Lord is honour, and glory, and gladness, and a crown of rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The fear of the Lord makes a merry heart, and gives joy, and gladness, and a long life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Whoso fears the Lord, it shall go well with him at the last, and he shall find favour in the day of his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> To fear the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: and it was created with the faithful in the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> She has built an everlasting foundation with men, and she shall continue with their seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> To fear the Lord is fulness of wisdom, and fills men with her fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> She fills all their house with things desirable, and the garners with her increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The fear of the Lord is a crown of wisdom, making peace and perfect health to flourish; both which are the gifts of God: and it enlarges their rejoicing that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Wisdom rains down skill and knowledge of understanding standing, and exalts them to honour that hold her fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The root of wisdom is to fear the Lord, and the branches thereof are long life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The fear of the Lord drives away sins: and where it is present, it turns away wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A furious man can’t be justified; for the sway of his fury shall be his destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> A patient man will tear for a time, and afterward joy shall spring up to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He will hide his words for a time, and the lips of many shall declare his wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The parables of knowledge are in the treasures of wisdom: but godliness is an abomination to a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If you desire wisdom, keep the commandments, and the Lord shall give her to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For the fear of the Lord is wisdom and instruction: and faith and meekness are his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Distrust not the fear of the Lord when you are poor: and come not to him with a double heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Be not an hypocrite in the sight of men, and take good heed what you speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Exalt not yourself, lest you fall, and bring dishonour upon your soul, and so God discover your secrets, and cast you down in the midst of the congregation, because you came not in truth to the fear of the Lord, but your heart is full of deceit.</span>
