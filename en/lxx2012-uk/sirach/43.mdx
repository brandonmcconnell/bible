<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The pride of the height, the clear firmament, the beauty of heaven, with his glorious show;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The sun when it appears, declaring at his rising a marvellous instrument, the work of the most High:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> At noon it parches the country, and who can abide the burning heat thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A man blowing a furnace is in works of heat, but the sun burns the mountains three times more; breathing out fiery vapours, and sending forth bright beams, it dims the eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Great is the Lord that made it; and at his commandment runs hastily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He made the moon also to serve in her season for a declaration of times, and a sign of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> From the moon is the sign of feasts, a light that decreases in her perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her changing, being an instrument of the armies above, shining in the firmament of heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The beauty of heaven, the glory of the stars, an ornament giving light in the highest places of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> At the commandment of the Holy One they will stand in their order, and never faint in their watches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Look upon the rainbow, and praise him that made it; very beautiful it is in the brightness thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> It encircles the heaven about with a glorious circle, and the hands of the most High have bended it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> By his commandment he makes the snow to fall apace, and sends swiftly the lightnings of his judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Through this the treasures are opened: and clouds fly forth as fowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> By his great power he makes the clouds firm, and the hailstones are broken small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> At his sight the mountains are shaken, and at his will the south wind blows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The noise of the thunder makes the earth to tremble: so does the northern storm and the whirlwind: as birds flying he scatters the snow, and the falling down thereof is as the lighting of grasshoppers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The eye marvels at the beauty of the whiteness thereof, and the heart is astonished at the raining of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The hoarfrost also as salt he pours on the earth, and being congealed, it lies on the top of sharp stakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> When the cold north wind blows, and the water is congealed into ice, it remains upon every gathering together of water, and clothes the water as with a breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> It devours the mountains, and burns the wilderness, and consumes the grass as fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> A present remedy of all is a mist coming speedily, a dew coming after heat refreshes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> By his counsel he appeases the deep, and plants islands therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They that sail on the sea tell of the danger thereof; and when we hear it with our ears, we marvel thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For therein be strange and wondrous works, variety of all kinds of beasts and whales created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> By him the end of them has prosperous success, and by his word all things consist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> We may speak much, and yet come short: therefore in sum, he is all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> How shall we be able to magnify him? for he is great above all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The Lord is terrible and very great, and marvellous is his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> When you⌃ glorify the Lord, exalt him as much as you⌃ can; for even yet will he far exceed: and when you⌃ exalt him, put forth all your strength, and be not weary; for you⌃ can never go far enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Who has seen him, that he might tell us? and who can magnify him as he is?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> There are yet hid greater things than these be, for we have seen but a few of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> For the Lord has made all things; and to the godly has he given wisdom.</span>
