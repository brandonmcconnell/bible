<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 20:35–41; Psalms 51:1–19; Kings II 11:10–17"> And the Lord sent Nathan the prophet to David; and he went in to him, and said to him, There were two men in one city, one rich and the other poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings II 15:16; Job 1:3; Kings II 5:13–16"> And the rich man had very many flocks and herds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Michaeas 7:5; Kings II 11:3; Proverbs 5:18–19"> But the poor man had only one little ewe lamb, which he had purchased, and preserved, and reared; and it grew up with himself and his children in common; it ate of his bread and drank of his cup, and slept in his bosom, and was to him as a daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 1:14; Kings II 11:3–4; Genesis 18:2–7"> And a traveller came to the rich man, and he spared to take of his flocks and of his herds, to dress for the traveller that came to him; and he took the poor man's lamb, and dressed it for the man that came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Romans 2:1; Kings I 26:16"> And David was greatly moved with anger against the man; and David said to Nathan, As the Lord lives, the man that did this thing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. is the son of death.">a</a></sup>shall surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:1; Luke 19:8; Proverbs 6:31"> And he shall restore the lamb sevenfold, because he has not spared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:14; Kings I 16:13; Kings II 22:1"> And Nathan said to David, You are the man that has done this. Thus says the Lord God of Israel, I anointed you to be king over Israel, and I rescued you out the hand of Saul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Romans 8:32; Kings II 9:7"> and I gave you the house of your lord, and the wives of your lord into your bosom, and I gave to you the house of Israel and Juda; and if that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. is little, I will give, etc.">b</a></sup>had been little, I would have given you yet more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 15:19; Psalms 139:1–2; Kings I 15:23"> Why have you set at nothing the word of the Lord, to do that which is evil in his eyes? you have slain Urias the Chettite with the sword, and you have taken his wife to be your wife, and you have slain him with the sword of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 18:14–15; Matthew 26:52; Romans 2:4"> Now therefore the sword shall not depart from your house for ever, because you have set me at nothing, and you have taken the wife of Urias the Chettite, to be your wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 16:21–22; Deuteronomy 28:30; Kings II 15:10"> Thus says the Lord, Behold, I will raise up against you evil out of your house, and I will take your wives before your eyes, and will give them to your neighbour, and he shall lie with your wives in the sight of this sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 16:22; Kings II 11:4–15; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For you did it secretly, but I will do this thing in the sight of all Israel, and before the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Kings II 24:10; Proverbs 28:13"> And David said to Nathan, I have sinned against the Lord. And Nathan said to David, And the Lord has put away your sin; you shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Esias 52:5; Psalms 89:31–33"> Only because you have given great occasion of provocation to the enemies of the Lord by this thing, your son also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. born.">c</a></sup>that is born to you shall surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 25:38; Psalms 104:29; Chronicles II 13:20"> And Nathan departed to his house. And the Lord struck the child, which the wife of Urias the Chettite bore to David, and it was ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 13:31; Acts 9:9; Psalms 69:10"> And David enquired of God concerning the child, and David fasted, and went in and lay all night upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 3:35; Kings I 28:23"> And the elders of his house arose and went to him to raise him up from the ground, but he would not rise, nor did he eat bread with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 20:15"> And it came to pass on the seventh day that the child died: and the servants of David were afraid to tell him that the child was dead; for they said, Behold, while the child was yet alive we spoke to him, and he listened not to our voice; and you should we tell him that the child is dead?—so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. will.">d</a></sup>would he do himself harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And David understood that his servants were whispering, and David perceived that the child was dead: and David said to his servants, Is the child dead? and they said, He is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:20; Ruth 3:3; Lamentations 3:39–41"> Then David rose up from the earth, and washed, and anointed himself, and changed his raiment, and went into the house of God, and worshipped him; and went into his own house, and called for bread to eat, and they set bread before him and he ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:15"> And his servants said to him, What is this thing that you have done concerning the child? while it was yet living you did fast, and weep, and watch: and when the child was dead you did rise up, and did eat bread, and drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esias 38:1–3; Esias 38:5; Jonas 3:9–10"> And David said, While the child yet lived, I fasted and wept; for I said, Who knows if the Lord will pity me, and the child live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:35; Job 30:23; Luke 23:43"> But now it is dead, why should I fast thus? shall I be able to bring him back again? I shall go to him, but he shall not return to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:6; Chronicles I 28:5–6; Chronicles I 22:9–10"> And David comforted Bersabee his wife, and he went in to her, and lay with her; and she conceived and bore a son, and he called his named Solomon, and the Lord loved him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 7:4; Nehemiah 13:26; Kings III 1:11"> And he sent by the hand of Nathan the prophet, and called his name Jeddedi, for the Lord's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles I 20:1–3; Deuteronomy 3:11; Kings II 11:25"> And Joab fought against Rabbath of the children of Ammon, and took the royal city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:20; Kings II 11:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And Joab sent messengers to David, and said, I have fought against Rabbath, and taken the city of waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 7:18"> And now gather the rest of the people, and encamp against the city, and take it beforehand; lest I take the city first, and my name be called upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And David gathered all the people, and went to Rabbath, and fought against it, and took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Chronicles I 20:2"> And he took the crown of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex. rightly omits Molchom. 'Their king' in Heb.">e</a></sup>Molchom their king from off his head, and the weight of it was a talent of gold, with precious stones, and it was upon the head of David; and he carried forth very much spoil of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles I 20:3; Psalms 21:8–9; Amos 1:3"> And he brought forth the people that were in it, and put them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. in.">f</a></sup>under the saw, and under iron harrows, and axes of iron, and made them pass through the brick-kiln: and thus he did to all the cities of the children of Ammon. And David and all the people returned to Jerusalem.</span>
