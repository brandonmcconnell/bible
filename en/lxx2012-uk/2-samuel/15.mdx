<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 1:5; Kings II 12:11; Kings I 8:11"> And it came to pass after this that Abessalom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. made.">a</a></sup>prepared for himself chariots and horses, and fifty men to run before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:14; Ruth 4:1; Job 24:14"> And Abessalom rose early, and stood by the side of the way of the gate: and it came to pass that every man who had a cause, came to the king for judgement, and Abessalom cried to him, and said to him, Of what city are you? And he said, Your servant is of one of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 8:15; Jezekiel 22:7; 1 Peter 2:17"> And Abessalom said to him, See, your affairs are right and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. easy to be understood.">b</a></sup>clear, yet you have no one appointed of the king to hear you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:29; Judges 9:1–5; Proverbs 27:2"> And Abessalom said, O that one would make me a judge in the land; then every man who had a dispute or a cause would come to me, and I would judge him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 14:33; Psalms 55:21; Proverbs 26:25"> And it came to pass when a man came near to do him obeisance, that he stretched out his hand, and took hold of him, and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Peter 2:3; Proverbs 11:9"> And Abessalom did after this manner to all Israel that came to the king for judgement; and Abessalom gained the hearts of the men of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 58:4; Kings I 16:13; Kings I 16:1–2"> And it came to pass<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. from the end of, etc.">c</a></sup>after forty years, that Abessalom said to his father, I will go now, and pay my vows, which I vowed to the Lord in Chebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:20–21; Kings II 13:37–38; Kings I 1:11"> For your servant vowed a vow when I lived at Gedsur in Syria, saying, If the Lord should indeed restore me to Jerusalem, then will I serve the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And the king said to him, Go in peace. And he arose and went to Chebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:38; Kings II 5:5; Kings II 3:2–3"> And Abessalom sent spies throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, When you⌃ hear the sound of the trumpet, then shall you⌃ say, Abessalom is become king in Chebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 9:13; Kings I 22:15; Romans 16:18–19"> And there went with Abessalom two hundred chosen men from Jerusalem; and they went in their simplicity, and knew not anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Kings II 15:31; Joshua 15:51"> And Abessalom sent to Achitophel the Theconite, the counsellor of David, from his city, from Gola, where he was sacrificing: and there was a strong<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="So the Alex. which reads σὺστρεμμα. See Acts 23:12.">d</a></sup>conspiracy; and the people with Abessalom were increasingly numerous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 9:3; Matthew 27:22; Kings II 15:6"> And there came a messenger to David, saying, the heart of the men of Israel is gone after Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 19:9; Jezekiel 46:18; Psalms 55:3–11"> And David said to all his servants who were with him in Jerusalem, Rise, and let us flee, for we have no refuge from Abessalom: make haste and go, lest he overtake us speedily, and bring evil upon us, and strike the city with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:66–69; Proverbs 18:24; Luke 22:28–29"> And the king's servants said to the king, In all things which our lord the king chooses, behold we are your servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 16:21–22; Kings II 20:3; Psalms 3:1"> And the king and all his house went out on foot: and the king left ten women of his concubines to keep the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:1–2; Ecclesiastes 10:7; Psalms 66:12"> And the king and all his servants went out on foot; and abode in a distant house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings II 8:18; Kings I 30:14; Kings II 20:7"> And all his servants passed on by his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. hand.">e</a></sup>side, and every Chelethite, and every Phelethite, and they stood by the olive tree in the wilderness: and all the people marched near him, and all his court, and all the men of might, and all the men of war, six hundred: and they were present at his side: and every Chelethite, and every Phelethite, and all the six hundred Gittites that came on foot out of Geth, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, they that.">f</a></sup>they went on before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings II 18:2; Ruth 1:11–13"> And the king said to Ethi, the Gittite, Why do you also go with us? return, and dwell with the king, for you are a stranger, and you have come forth as a sojourner out of your place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 23:13; Kings II 2:6; John 1:17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. it.">g</a></sup>Whereas you came yesterday, shall I today cause you to travel with us, and shall you thus change your place? you did come forth yesterday, and today shall I set you in motion to go along with us? I indeed will go wherever I may go: return then, and cause your brethren to return with you, and may the Lord deal mercifully and truly with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–17; Proverbs 17:17; Kings IV 4:30"> And Ethi answered the king and said, As the Lord lives and as my lord the king lives, in the place wherever my lord shall be, whether it be for death or life, there shall your servant be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the king said to Ethi, Come and pass over with me. So Ethi the Gittite and the king passed over, and all his servants, and all the multitude with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 16:2; Kings III 2:37; John 18:1"> And all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. land.">h</a></sup>country wept with a loud voice. And all the people passed by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. in.">i</a></sup>over the brook of Kedron; and the king crossed the brook Kedron: and all the people and the king passed on towards the way of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Kings II 8:17; Kings II 20:25"> And behold also Sadoc, and all the Levites were with him, bearing the ark of the covenant of the Lord from Baethar: and they set down the ark of God; and Abiathar went up, until all the people had passed out of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 25:30; Psalms 26:8; Psalms 43:3–4"> And the king said to Sadoc, Carry back the ark of God into the city: if I should find favour in the eyes of the Lord, then will he bring me back, and he will show me it and its beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings I 3:18; Kings II 22:20; Kings III 10:9"> But if he should say thus, I have no pleasure in you; behold, here I am, let him do to me according to that which is good in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 9:9; Kings II 17:17; Kings II 24:11"> And the king said to Sadoc the priest, Behold, you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. do.">j</a></sup>shall return to the city in peace, and Achimaas your son, and Jonathan the son of Abiathar, your two sons with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings II 17:16; Kings II 15:23; Kings II 17:1"> Behold, I continue in arms in Araboth of the desert, until there come tidings from you to report to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So Sadoc and Abiathar brought back the ark of the Lord to Jerusalem, and it continued there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Esther 6:12; Kings II 19:4; Luke 19:41"> And David went up by the ascent of the mount of Olives, ascending and weeping, and had his head covered, and went barefooted: and all the people that were with him covered every man his head; and they went up, ascending and weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings II 17:14; Kings II 17:23; James 3:15"> And it was reported to David, saying, Achitophel also is amongst the conspirators with Abessalom. And David said, O Lord my God, disconcert, I pray you, the counsel of Achitophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 16:2; Kings II 1:2; Psalms 3:3–5"> And David came as far as Ros, where he worshipped God: and behold, Chusi the chief friend of David came out to meet him, having torn his garment, and earth was upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings II 19:35"> And David said to him, If you should go over with me, then will you be a burden to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Kings II 16:16–19; Matthew 10:16"> but if you shall return to the city, and shall say to Abessalom, Your brethren are passed over, and the king your father is passed over after me: and now I am your servant, O king, suffer me to live: at one time even of late I was the servant of your father, and now I am your humble servant—so shall you disconcert for me, the counsel of Achitophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings II 17:15–16"> And, behold, there are there with you Sadoc and Abiathar the priests; and it shall be that every word that you shall hear of the house of the king, you shall report it to Sadoc and Abiathar the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings II 17:17; Kings II 15:27; Kings II 18:19–33"> Behold, there are there with them their two sons, Achimaas the son of Sadoc, and Jonathan the son of Abiathar; and by them you⌃ shall report to me every word which you⌃ shall hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings II 16:15–16; Chronicles I 27:33"> So Chusi the friend of David went into the city, and Abessalom was lately gone into Jerusalem.</span>
