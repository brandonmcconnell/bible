<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:1; Chronicles I 27:23–24; James 1:13–14"> And the Lord caused his anger to burn forth again in Israel, and Satan stirred up David against them, saying, Go, number Israel and Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings II 3:10; Judges 20:1; Kings II 20:23"> And the king said to Joab commander of the host, who was with him, Go now through all the tribes of Israel and Juda, from Dan even to Bersabee, and number the people, and I will know the number of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Psalms 115:14; Proverbs 14:28"> And Joab said to the king, Now may the Lord add to the people one hundred-fold as many as they are, and may the eyes of my lord the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. seeing.">a</a></sup>see it: but why does my lord the king desire this thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Ecclesiastes 8:4; Chronicles I 21:4"> Nevertheless the word of the king prevailed against Joab and the captains of the host: And Joab and the captains of the host went out before the king to number the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:16"> And they went over Jordan, and encamped in Aroer, on the right of the city which is in the midst of the valley of Gad and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 19:28; Genesis 31:47–48; Judges 18:28–29"> And they came to Galaad, and into the land of Thabason, which is Adasai, and they came to Danidan and Udan, and compassed Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:29; Joshua 11:3; Genesis 21:22–33"> And they came to Mapsar of Tyre, and to all the cities of the Evite and the Chananite: and they came by the South of Juda to Bersabee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And they compassed the whole land; and they arrived at Jerusalem at the end of nine months and twenty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:23–24; Chronicles I 21:5–6; Numbers 1:44–46"> And Joab gave in the number of the census of the people to the king: and Israel consisted of eight hundred thousand men of might that drew sword; and the men of Juda, five hundred thousand fighting men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 12:13; Kings I 13:13; Proverbs 28:13"> And the heart of David struck him after he had numbered the people; and David said to the Lord, I have sinned grievously, O Lord, in what I have now done: remove, I pray you, the iniquity of your servant, for I have been exceedingly foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 22:5; Kings I 9:9; Chronicles I 29:29"> And David rose early in the morning, and the word of the Lord came to the prophet Gad, the seer, saying, Go, and speak to David, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6–10; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12"> Thus says the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word εἰμὶ is simply redundant.">b</a></sup>I bring one of three things upon you: now choose you one of them, and I will do it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:12; Leviticus 26:25; Kings II 21:1"> And Gad went in to David, and told him, and said to him, Choose one of these things to befall you, whether there shall come upon you for three years famine in your land; or that you should flee three months before your enemies, and they should pursue you; or that there should be for three days mortality in your land. Now then decide, and see what answer I shall return to him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:1; Psalms 119:156; Chronicles I 21:13"> And David said to Gad, On every side<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. things are very narrow to me.">c</a></sup>I am much straitened: let me fall now into the hands of the Lord, for his compassions are very many; and let me not fall into the hands of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:14; Chronicles I 27:24; Numbers 25:9"> So David chose for himself the mortality: and they were the days of wheat harvest; and the Lord sent a pestilence upon Israel from morning till<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lit. dinner time.">d</a></sup>noon, and the plague began amongst the people; and there died of the people from Dan even to Bersabee seventy thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:13–14; Genesis 6:6; Exodus 12:23"> And the angel of the Lord stretched out his hand against Jerusalem to destroy it, and the Lord repented of the evil, and said to the angel that destroyed the people, It is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. much.">e</a></sup>enough now, withhold your hand. And the angel of the Lord was by the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Chronicles I 21:16–17; Kings II 24:10"> And David spoke to the Lord when he saw the angel striking the people, and he said, Behold, it is I that have done wrong,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Alex. adds, 'and I the shepherd have done wickedly.'">f</a></sup>but these sheep what have they done? Let your hand, I pray you, be upon me, and upon my father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:18–30; Kings II 24:11"> And Gad came to David in that day, and said to him, Go up, and set up to the Lord an altar in the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:26; Chronicles II 36:16"> And David went up according to the word of Gad, as the Lord commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Ruth 2:10; Chronicles I 21:20–21"> And Orna<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. stooped. See 1 Pet. 1:12; also John 20:5.">g</a></sup>looked out, and saw the king and his servants coming on before him: and Orna went forth, and did obeisance to the king with his face to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:22; Numbers 25:8; Kings II 21:3–14"> And Orna said, Why has my lord the king come to his servant? and David said, To buy of you the threshing floor, in order to build an altar to the Lord that the plague may be restrained from off the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 19:21; Kings I 6:14; Chronicles I 21:22"> And Orna said to David, Let my lord the king take and offer to the Lord that which is good in his eyes: behold, here are oxen for a whole burnt offering, and the wheels and furniture of the oxen for wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:40–41; Esias 32:8; Psalms 20:3–4"> Orna gave all to the king: and Orna said to the king, The Lord your God bless you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:24–25; Malachias 1:12–14; Genesis 23:13"> And the king said to Orna, Nay, but I will surely buy it of you at a fair price, and I will not offer to the Lord my God a whole burnt offering for nothing. So David purchased the threshing floor and the oxen for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. silver of fifty shekels.">h</a></sup>fifty shekels of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 21:14; Lamentations 3:32–33; Genesis 8:20"> And David built there an altar to the Lord, and offered up whole burnt offerings and peace-offerings: and Solomon made an addition to the altar afterwards, for it was little at first. And the Lord listened to the land, and the plague was stayed from Israel.</span>
