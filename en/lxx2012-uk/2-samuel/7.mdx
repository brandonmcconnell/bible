<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; Kings III 5:4"> And it came to pass when the king sat in his house, and the Lord had given him an inheritance on every side free from all his enemies round about him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:1; Kings II 5:11; Aggaeus 1:4"> that the king said to Nathan the prophet, Behold now, I live in a house of cedar, and the ark of the Lord dwells in the midst of a tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:7; Kings III 8:17–18; Kings IV 4:27"> And Nathan said to the king, Go and do all that is in your heart, for the Lord is with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> And it came to pass in that night, that the word of the Lord came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:3–32; Kings III 5:3–4; Kings III 8:16–19"> Go, and say to my servant David, Thus says the Lord, You shall not build me a house for me to dwell in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> For I have not lived in a house from the day that I brought up the children of Israel out of Egypt to this day, but I have been walking in a lodge and in a tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:6; Kings II 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> wherever I went with all Israel. Have I ever spoken to any of the tribes of Israel, which I commanded to tend my people Israel, saying, Why have you⌃ not built me a house of Cedar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; Kings II 6:21; Kings II 12:7"> And now thus shall you say to my servant David, Thus says the Lord Almighty, I took you from the sheep-cote, that you should be a prince over my people, over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 5:10; Chronicles I 17:8; Kings I 18:14"> And I was with you wherever you went, and I destroyed all your enemies before you, and I made you renowned according to the renown of the great ones on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; Chronicles I 17:9; Jeremias 24:6"> And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. it.">a</a></sup>them, and they shall dwell by themselves, and shall be no more distressed; and the son of iniquity shall no more afflict them, as he has done from the beginning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 7:27; Kings II 7:1; Chronicles I 17:10"> from the days when I appointed judges over my people Israel: and I will give you rest from all your enemies, and the Lord will tell you that you shall build a house to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> And it shall come to pass when your days shall have been fulfilled, and you shall sleep with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, even your own issue, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; Kings III 8:19; Kings II 7:16"> He shall build for me a house to my name, and I will set up his throne even for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; Chronicles I 17:13"> I will be to him a father, and he shall be to me a son. And when he happens to transgress, then will I chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 15:23; Kings I 15:28; Psalms 89:28"> But my mercy I will not take from him, as I took it from those whom I removed from my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 89:36–37; Kings II 7:13"> And his house shall be made sure, and his kingdom for ever before me, and his throne shall be set up for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Chronicles I 17:16; Kings I 18:18"> And king David came in, and sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord, my Lord, and what is my house, that you have loved me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, so much as this.">b</a></sup>hitherto?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 55:8–9; Chronicles I 17:17; Psalms 36:7"> Whereas I was very little before you, O Lord, my Lord, yet you spoke concerning the house of your servant for a long time to to come. And is this the law of man, O Lord, my Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; Kings I 16:7; Psalms 139:1"> And what shall David yet say to you? and now you know your servant, O Lord, my Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Psalms 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> And you have wrought for your servant's sake, and according to your heart you have wrought all this greatness, to make it known to your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 86:8"> that he may magnify you, O my Lord; for there is no one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. as you.">c</a></sup>like you, and there is no God, but you amongst all of whom we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> And what other nation in the earth is as your people Israel? whereas God was his guide, to redeem for himself a people to make you a name, to do mightily and nobly, so that you should cast out nations and their tabernacles from the presence of your people, whom you did redeem for yourself out of Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> And you have prepared for yourself your people Israel to be a people for ever, and you, Lord, are become their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:49; Jezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> And now, O my Lord, the Almighty Lord God of Israel, confirm the word for ever which you have spoken concerning your servant and his house: and now as you have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:23–24; Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> Let your name be magnified for ever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. add, 'and let the house of your servant David be established before you.'">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:25–26; Psalms 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> Almighty Lord God of Israel, you have uncovered the ear of your servant, saying, I will build you a house: therefore your servant has found in his heart to pray this prayer to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> And now, O Lord my Lord, you are God; and your words will be true, and you have spoken these good things concerning your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; Chronicles I 17:27; Psalms 115:12–15"> And now begin and bless the house of your servant, that it may continue for ever before you; for you, O Lord, my Lord, have spoken, and the house of your servant shall be blessed with your blessing so as to continue for ever.</span>
