<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 12:16; Chronicles II 10:16; Deuteronomy 13:13"> And there was a transgressor so called there, and his name was Sabee, a Benjamite, the son of Bochori: and he blew the trumpet, and said, We have no portion in David, neither have we any inheritance in the son of Jessae: to your tents, O Israel, every one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:66–68; Acts 11:23; Kings II 19:15"> And all the men of Israel went up from following David after Sabee the son of Bochori: but the men of Juda adhered to their king, from Jordan even to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 16:21–22; Kings II 15:16; Genesis 40:3"> And David went into his house at Jerusalem: and the king took the ten women his concubines, whom he had left to keep the house, and he put them in a place of custody, and maintained them, and went not in to them; and they were kept living as widows, till the day of their death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 19:13; Kings II 17:25; Chronicles I 2:17"> And the king said to Amessai, Call to me the men of Juda for three days, and do you be present here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 19:13; Kings I 13:8"> And Amessai went to call Juda, and delayed beyond the time which David appointed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 21:17; Kings II 11:11; Kings III 1:33"> And David said to Amessai, Now shall Sabee the son of Bochori do us more harm than Abessalom: now then take you with you the servants of your lord, and follow after him, lest he find for himself strong cities, so will he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e. escape us.">a</a></sup>blind our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 8:18; Kings III 1:38; Kings II 20:23"> And there went out after him Amessai and the men of Joab, and the Cherethites, and the Phelethites, and all the mighty men: and they went out from Jerusalem to pursue after Sabee the son of Bochori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 3:30; Kings II 2:13; Kings II 20:4–5"> And they were by the great stone that is in Gabaon: and Amessai went in before them: and Joab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. was girded about with.">b</a></sup>had upon him a military cloak over his apparel, and over it he was girded with a dagger fastened upon his loins in its scabbard: and the dagger came out, it even came out and fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Matthew 26:48–49; Michaeas 7:2"> And Joab said to Amessai, Are you in health, my brother? and the right hand of Joab took hold of the beard of Amessai to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 2:23; Kings II 3:27; Judges 3:21"> And Amessai observed not the dagger that was in the hand of Joab: and Joab struck him with it on the loins, and his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. belly.">c</a></sup>bowels were shed out upon the ground, and he did not repeat the blow, and he died: and Joab and Abessai his brother pursued after Sabee the son of Bochori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 20:13; Kings II 20:21; Kings IV 9:32"> And there stood over him one of the servants of Joab, and said, Who is he that is for Joab, and who is on the side of David following Joab?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21–22; Psalms 9:16; Kings II 17:25"> And Amessai was weltering in blood in the midst of the way. And a man saw that all the people stood still; and he removed Amessai out of the path into a field, and he cast a garment upon him, because he saw every one that came to him standing still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 6:12; Esias 7:3; Kings IV 18:17"> And when he was quickly removed from the road, every man of Israel passed after Joab to pursue after Sabee the son of Bochori.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 15:29; Kings III 15:20; Chronicles II 16:4"> And he went through all the tribes of Israel to Abel, and to Bethmacha; and all in Charri too were assembled, and followed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 19:32; Jeremias 32:24; Kings III 15:20"> And they came and besieged him in Abel and Phermacha: and they raised a mound against the city and it stood close to the wall; and all the people with Joab proposed to throw down the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 14:2; Ecclesiastes 9:14–18; Kings I 25:32–33"> And a wise woman cried from the wall, and said, Hear, hear; say, I pray you⌃, to Joab, Draw near hither, and I will speak to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 14:12; Kings I 25:24"> And he drew near to her, and the woman said to him, Are you Joab? and he said, I am. And she said to him, Hear the words of your handmaid; and Joab said, I do hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–11"> And she spoke, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. they spoke a word amongst the first, saying.">d</a></sup>Of old time they said thus, Surely one was asked in Abel, and Dan, whether the faithful in Israel failed in what they purposed; they will surely ask in Abel, even in like manner, whether they have failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 26:19; Kings II 21:3; Kings II 17:16"> I am a peaceful one of the strong ones in Israel; but you seek to destroy a city and a mother city in Israel: why do you seek to ruin the inheritance of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:29; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremias 17:9"> And Joab answered and said, Far be it, far be it from me, that I should ruin or destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 26:9; Judges 2:9; Kings II 17:2–3"> Is not the case thus, that a man of mount Ephraim, Sabee, son of Bochori by name, has even lifted up his hand against king David? Give him only to me, and I will depart from the city. And the woman said to Joab, Behold, his head shall be thrown to you over the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 20:1; Ecclesiastes 7:19; Kings II 11:6–21"> And the woman went in to all the people, and she spoke to all the city in her wisdom; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. it or she. i.e. ἡ πόλις.">e</a></sup>they took off the head of Sabee the son of Bochori; and took it away and threw it to Joab: and he blew the trumpet, and the people separated from the city away from him, every man to his tent: and Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 8:16–18; Chronicles I 18:15–17; Kings III 4:3–6"> And Joab was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. to.">f</a></sup>over all the forces of Israel: and Banaias the son of Jodae was over the Cherethites and over the Phelethites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 4:3; Kings III 12:18; Kings III 4:6"> And Adoniram was over the tribute: and Josaphath the son of Achiluth was recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 4:4; Kings II 8:17; Chronicles I 18:16"> And Susa was scribe: and Sadoc and Abiathar were priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings II 23:38; Exodus 2:14; Genesis 41:45"> Moreover Iras the son of Iarin was priest to David.</span>
