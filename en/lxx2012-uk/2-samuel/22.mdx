<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Judges 5:1; Esias 12:1–6"> And David spoke to the Lord the words of this song, in the day in which the Lord rescued him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 18:2–50; Psalms 31:3; Psalms 71:3"> And the song was thus: O Lord, my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Esias 12:2; Proverbs 18:10"> my God; he shall be to me my guard, I will trust in him: he is my protector, and the horn of my salvation, my helper, and my sure refuge; you shall save me from the unjust man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:3; Psalms 48:1; Psalms 96:4"> I will call upon the Lord who is worthy to be praised, and I shall be saved from my enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonas 2:3; Psalms 69:14–15; Revelation 17:15"> For the troubles of death compassed me, the floods of iniquity amazed me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:3; Proverbs 13:14; Psalms 140:5"> the pangs of death surrounded me, the agonies of death prevented me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Psalms 116:4; Exodus 3:7"> When I am afflicted I will call upon the Lord, and will cry to my God, and he shall hear my voice out of his temple, and my cry shall come into his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 26:11; Psalms 97:4; Judges 5:4"> And the earth was troubled and quaked, and the foundations of heaven were confounded and torn asunder, because the Lord was angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Psalms 18:15; Psalms 18:8"> There went up a smoke in his wrath, and fire out of his mouth devours: coals were kindled at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 8:12; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 144:5"> And he bowed the heavens, and came down, and there was darkness under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Kings I 4:4"> And he rode upon the cherubs and did fly, and was seen upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Kings II 22:10; Job 36:29"> And he made darkness his hiding-place; his tabernacle round about him was the darkness of waters, he condensed it with the clouds of the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 22:9"> At the brightness before him coals of fire were kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Kings I 2:10; Job 37:2–5"> The Lord thundered out of heaven, and the Most High uttered his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Joshua 10:10; Ambacum 3:11"> And he sent forth arrows, and scattered them, and he flashed lightning, and dismayed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Naum 1:4; Ambacum 3:8–10; Psalms 114:3–7"> And the channels of the sea were seen, and the foundations of the world were revealed, at the rebuke of the Lord, at the blast of the breath of his anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 43:2; Psalms 144:7; Psalms 18:16"> He sent from above and took me; he drew me out of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; 2 Timothy 4:17; Psalms 56:9"> He delivered me from my strong enemies, from them that hated me, for they were stronger than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 71:20–21; Kings I 23:26–27; Psalms 118:10–13"> The days of my affliction prevented me; but the Lord was my stay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 31:8; Psalms 118:5; Kings II 15:26"> And he brought me into a wide place, and rescued me, because he delighted in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Kings I 26:23; Psalms 7:8"> And the Lord recompensed me according to my righteousness; even according to the purity of my hands did he recompense me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32; Genesis 18:19; Psalms 128:1"> Because, I kept the ways of the Lord, and did not wickedly depart from my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:30; Psalms 119:102; John 15:14"> For all his judgements and his ordinances were before me: I departed not from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 6:9; Job 1:1; Genesis 17:1"> And I shall be blameless<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. to him.">a</a></sup>before him, and will keep myself from my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 22:21; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:7–8"> And the Lord will recompense me according to my righteousness, and according to the purity of my hands in his eye-sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; James 2:13"> With the holy you will be holy, and with the perfect man you will be perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 18:26; Leviticus 26:23–28"> and with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, upon the haughty.">b</a></sup>excellent you will be excellent, and with the froward you will be froward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–13; Esias 5:15; James 4:6–7"> And you will save the poor people, and will bring down the eyes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, upon the haughty.">c</a></sup>of the haughty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; John 8:12; Job 29:3"> For you, Lord, are my lamp, and the Lord shall shine forth to me in my darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:29; Philippians 4:13; Psalms 118:10–12"> For by you shall I run<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="i.e. a strong man or warrior.">d</a></sup>as a girded man, and by my God shall I leap over a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 18:30"> As for the Mighty One, his way is blameless: the word of the Lord is strong and tried in the fire: he is a protector to all that put their trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings I 2:2; Deuteronomy 32:31; Jeremias 10:6–7"> Who is strong, but the Lord? and who will be a Creator except our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Esias 41:10; Philippians 4:13"> It is the Mighty One who strengthens me with might, and has prepared my way without fault.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Esias 58:14; Ambacum 3:19"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. making.">e</a></sup>He makes my feet like hart's feet, and sets me upon the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 144:1; Psalms 18:33–34; Jezekiel 39:9–10"> He teaches my hands to war, and has broken a brazen bow by my arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 6:16; Psalms 115:14; Psalms 84:11"> And you have given me the shield of my salvation, and your propitious dealing has increased me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 17:5; Kings I 2:9"> so as to make room under me for my going, and my legs did not totter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 21:8–9; Kings II 5:18–25; Kings II 10:14"> I will pursue my enemies, and will utterly destroy them; and I will not turn again till I have consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Malachias 4:3; Malachias 4:1; Psalms 110:1"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Alex. and Heb. consume.">f</a></sup>I will crush them, and they shall not rise; and they shall fall under my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 44:5; Esias 60:14; Colossians 1:11"> And you shall strengthen me with power for the war; you shall cause them that rise up against me to bow down under me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Joshua 10:24; 2 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> And you have caused<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. as for my enemies, you have given me the back.">g</a></sup>my enemies to flee before me, even them that hated me, and you have slain them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Esias 1:15; Kings I 28:6; Michaeas 3:4"> They shall cry, and there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. is.">h</a></sup>shall be no helper; to the Lord, but he hearkens not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Michaeas 7:10; Esias 10:6; Kings IV 13:7"> And I ground them as the dust of the earth, I beat them small as the mire of the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Kings II 8:1–14; Esias 55:5; Kings II 3:1"> And you shall deliver me from the striving of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A.V. 'my people.'">i</a></sup>peoples, you shall keep me to be the head of the Gentiles: a people which I knew not served me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 81:15; Psalms 66:3; Esias 56:3"> The strange children feigned obedience to me; they listened to me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. at the hearing of the ear.">j</a></sup>as soon as they heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Michaeas 7:17; James 1:11; Esias 64:6"> The strange children shall be cast away, and shall be overthrown out of their hiding places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 89:26; Kings II 22:3; Job 19:25"> The Lord lives, and blessed be my guardian, and my God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. the keeper of my salvation.">k</a></sup>my strong keeper, shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 94:1; Psalms 144:2; Kings II 18:31"> The Lord who avenges me is strong, chastening the nations under me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 140:1; Kings II 7:8–9; Numbers 24:7"> and bringing me out from my enemies: and you shall set me on high from amongst those that rise up against me: you shall deliver me from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. a man of wrongs.">l</a></sup>the violent man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Romans 15:9; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 145:1–2"> Therefore will I confess to you, O Lord, amongst the Gentiles, and sing to your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 89:29; Psalms 18:50; Psalms 89:20"> He magnifies the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. salvations.">m</a></sup>salvation of his king, and works mercy for his anointed, even for David and for his seed for ever.</span>
