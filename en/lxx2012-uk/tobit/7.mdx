<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And when they were come to Ecbatane, they came to the house of Raguel, and Sara met them: and after they had saluted one another, she brought them into the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then said Raguel to Edna his wife, How like is this young man to Tobit my cousin!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Raguel asked them, From whence are you⌃, brethren? To whom they said, We are of the sons of Nephthalim, which are captives in Nineve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then he said to them, Do you⌃ know Tobit our kinsman? And they said, We know him. Then said he, Is he in good health?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And they said, He is both alive, and in good health: and Tobias said, He is my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Raguel leapt up, and kissed him, and wept,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And blessed him, and said to him, You are the son of an honest and good man. But when he had heard that Tobit was blind, he was sorrowful, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And likewise Edna his wife and Sara his daughter wept. Moreover they entertained them cheerfully; and after that they had killed a ram of the flock, they set store of meat on the table. Then said Tobias to Raphael, Brother Azarias, speak of those things of which you did talk in the way, and let this business be dispatched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So he communicated the matter with Raguel: and Raguel said to Tobias, Eat and drink, and make merry:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For it is meet that you should marry my daughter: nevertheless I will declare to you the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> I have given my daughter in marriage to seven men, who died that night they came in to her: nevertheless for the present be merry. But Tobias said, I will eat nothing here, till we agree and swear one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Raguel said, Then take her from henceforth according to the manner, for you are her cousin, and she is your, and the merciful God give you good success in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then he called his daughter Sara, and she came to her father, and he took her by the hand, and gave her to be wife to Tobias, saying, Behold, take her after the law of Moses, and lead her away to your father. And he blessed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And called Edna his wife, and took paper, and did write an instrument of covenants, and sealed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then they began to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> After Raguel called his wife Edna, and said to her, Sister, prepare another chamber, and bring her in there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Which when she had done as he had bidden her, she brought her there: and she wept, and she received the tears of her daughter, and said to her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Be of good comfort, my daughter; the Lord of heaven and earth give you joy for this your sorrow: be of good comfort, my daughter.</span>
