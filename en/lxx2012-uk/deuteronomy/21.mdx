<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 26:21; Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 28:17"> And if one be found slain with the sword in the land, which the Lord your God gives you to inherit, having fallen in the field, and they do not know who has struck him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; Deuteronomy 16:18–19"> your elders and your judges shall come forth, and shall measure the distances of the cities round about the slain man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:2; Matthew 11:28–30; Jeremias 31:18"> and it shall be that the city which is nearest to the slain man the elders of that city shall take a heifer of the herd, which has not laboured, and which has not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. drawn.">a</a></sup>borne a yoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21–24; 1 Peter 3:18"> And the elders of that city shall bring down the heifer into a rough valley, which has not been tilled and is not sown, and they shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. cut the sinews. i.e. of the neck.">b</a></sup>kill the heifer in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 23:13; Malachias 2:7; Deuteronomy 10:8"> And the priests the Levites shall come, because the Lord God has chosen them to stand by him, and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. his name. Hebraism.">c</a></sup>bless in his name, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. at their mouth.">d</a></sup>by their word shall every controversy and every stroke be decided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:13; Psalms 26:6; Psalms 51:7"> And all the elders of that city who draw near to the slain man shall wash their hands over the head of the heifer which was slain in the valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 21:21–23; Job 21:31–34; Kings II 16:8"> and they shall answer and say, Our hands have not shed this blood, and our eyes have not seen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonas 1:14; Kings IV 24:4; Jezekiel 23:24–25"> Be merciful to your people Israel, whom you have redeemed, O Lord, that innocent blood<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. may not be in your people.">e</a></sup>may not be charged on your people Israel: and the blood shall be atoned for to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:12–13; Kings IV 10:30–31; Deuteronomy 13:18"> And you shall take away innocent blood from amongst you, if you should do that which is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–16; Joshua 21:44"> And if when you go out to war against your enemies, the Lord your God should deliver them into your hands, and you should take their spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 12:14–15"> and should see amongst the spoil a woman beautiful in countenance, and should<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. think about her.">f</a></sup>desire her, and take her to yourself for a wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 14:9; Numbers 6:9; Ephesians 4:22"> and should bring her within your house: then shall you shave her head, and pare her nails;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 45:10–11; Luke 14:26–27"> and shall take away her garments of captivity from off her, and she shall abide in your house, and shall bewail her father and mother the days of a month; and afterwards you shall go in to her and dwell with her, and she shall be your wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Genesis 34:2; Deuteronomy 22:19"> And it shall be if you do not delight in her, you shall send her out free; and she shall not by any means be sold for money, you shall not treat her contemptuously, because you have humbled her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:33; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 29:18"> And if a man have two wives, the one loved and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. one of them.">g</a></sup>the other hated, and both the loved and the hated should have born him children, and the son of the hated should be firstborn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:10; Chronicles I 5:2; Chronicles II 21:3"> then it shall be that whenever he shall divide by inheritance his goods to his sons, he shall not be able to give the right of the firstborn to the son of the loved one, having overlooked the son of the hated, which is the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Genesis 25:31–34; Psalms 105:36"> But he shall acknowledge the firstborn of the hated one to give to him double of all things which shall be found by him, because he is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. the beginning or chief.">h</a></sup>first of his children, and to him belongs the birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 20:12; Amos 4:11–12"> And if any man has a disobedient and contentious son, who hearkens not to the voice of his father and the voice of his mother, and they should correct him, and he should not listen to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zacharias 13:3; Deuteronomy 21:2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> then shall his father and his mother take hold of him, and bring him forth to the elders of his city, and to the gate of the place:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:19–21"> and they shall say to the men of their city, This our son is disobedient and contentious, he hearkens not to our voice, he is a reveler and a drunkard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:19–20; Deuteronomy 13:10–11; Leviticus 24:16"> And the men of his city shall stone him with stones, and he shall die; and you shall remove the evil one from yourselves, and the rest shall hear and fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:31–38; Acts 26:31; Luke 23:33"> And if there be sin in any one, and the judgement of death be upon him, and he be put to death, and you⌃ hang him on a tree:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; John 19:31; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> his body shall not remain all night upon the tree, but you⌃ shall by all means bury it in that day; for every one that is hanged on a tree is cursed of God; and you⌃ shall by no means defile the land which the Lord your God gives you for an inheritance.</span>
