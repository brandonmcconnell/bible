<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 1:19; Exodus 23:22; Leviticus 26:3–13"> And it shall come to pass, if you will indeed hear the voice of the Lord your God, to observe and do all these commands, which I charge you this day, that the Lord your God shall set you on high above all the nations of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zacharias 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:15; 1 Timothy 4:8"> and all these blessings shall come upon you, and shall find you. If you will indeed hear the voice of the Lord your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:12–15; Genesis 39:5; Aggaeus 2:19"> blessed shall you be in the city, and blessed shall you be in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 49:25; Deuteronomy 7:13"> Blessed shall be the offspring of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. belly.">a</a></sup>body, and the fruits of your land, and the herds of your oxen, and the flocks of your sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Blessed shall be your barns, and your stores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 121:8; Numbers 27:17; Chronicles II 1:10"> Blessed shall you be in your coming in, and blessed shall you be in your going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 22:38–41; Leviticus 26:7–8; Psalms 89:23"> The Lord deliver your enemies that withstand you utterly broken before your face: they shall come out against you one way, and they shall flee seven ways from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Leviticus 26:10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The Lord send upon you his blessing in your barns, and on all on which you shall put your hand, in the land which the Lord your God gives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 26:18–19"> The Lord raise you up for himself a holy people, as he swore to your fathers; if you will hear the voice of the Lord your God, and walk in all his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:14; Deuteronomy 11:25; Jeremias 33:9"> And all the nations of the earth shall see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, see that the name, etc.">b</a></sup>you, that the name of the Lord is called upon you, and they shall stand in awe of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:9; Proverbs 10:22; Leviticus 26:9"> And the Lord your God shall multiply you for good in the offspring of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. belly.">c</a></sup>body, and in the offspring of your cattle, and in the fruits of your land, on your land which the Lord swore to your fathers to give to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:6; Joel 2:23–24; Deuteronomy 15:10"> May the Lord open to you his good treasure, the heaven, to give rain to your land in season: may he bless all the works of your hands: so shall you lend to many nations, but you shall not borrow; and you shall rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1; Deuteronomy 4:6–9; Philippians 1:27"> The Lord your God make you the head, and not the tail; and you shall then be above and you shall not be below, if you will listen to the voice of the Lord your God, in all things that I charge you this day to observe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 30:21; Deuteronomy 5:32; Deuteronomy 11:16"> You shall not turn aside from any of the commandments, which I charge you this day, to the right hand or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachias 2:2; Deuteronomy 28:2; Deuteronomy 29:20"> But it shall come to pass, if you will not listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to observe all his commandments, as many as I charge you this day, then all these curses shall come on you, and overtake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Malachias 3:9–12; Proverbs 3:33; Joel 1:4"> Cursed shall you be in the city, and cursed shall you be in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Aggaeus 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:5; Psalms 69:22"> Cursed shall be your barns and your stores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Leviticus 26:19–20; Job 18:16–19"> Cursed shall be the offspring of your body, and the fruits of your land, the herds of your oxen, and the flocks of your sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Judges 5:6–7; Chronicles II 15:5"> Cursed shall you be in your coming in, and cursed shall you be in your going out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 66:15; Malachias 2:2; Deuteronomy 4:26"> The Lord send upon you lack, and famine, and consumption of all things on which you shall put your hand, until he shall have utterly destroyed you, and until he shall have consumed you quickly because of your evil devices, because you have forsaken me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Jeremias 24:10; Jeremias 16:4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. may the Lord.">d</a></sup>The Lord cause the pestilence to cleave to you, until he shall have consumed you off the land into which you go to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Amos 4:9; Leviticus 26:16; Aggaeus 2:17"> The Lord strike you with distress, and fever, and cold, and inflammation, and blighting, and paleness, and they shall pursue you until they have destroyed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremias 14:1–6; Kings III 18:2"> And you shall have over your head a sky of brass, and the earth under you shall be iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Esias 5:24; Amos 4:11"> The Lord your God make the rain of your land dust; and dust shall come down from heaven, until it shall have destroyed you, and until it shall have quickly consumed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 30:17; Jezekiel 23:46; Jeremias 24:9"> The Lord give you up for slaughter before your enemies: you shall go out against them one way, and flee from their face seven ways; and you shall be a dispersion in all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 34:20; Jeremias 16:4; Jeremias 7:33"> And your dead men shall be food to the birds of the sky, and to the beasts of the earth; and there shall be none to scare them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 5:6; Deuteronomy 28:35; Leviticus 21:20"> The Lord strike you with the botch of Egypt in the seat, and with a malignant scab, and itch, so that you can not be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Esias 19:11–17; Esias 6:9–10"> The Lord strike you with insanity, and blindness, and astonishment of mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Esias 59:10; Job 5:14; Judges 6:1–6"> And you shall grope at mid-day, as a blind man would grope in the darkness, and you shall not prosper in your ways; and then you shall be unjustly treated, and plundered continually, and there shall be no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Jeremias 8:10; Job 31:10"> you shall take a wife, and another man shall have her; you shall build a house, and you shall not dwell in it; you shall plant a vineyard, and shall not gather the grapes of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 1:14–15; Judges 6:1"> Your calf shall be slain before you, and you shall not eat of it; your ass shall be violently taken away from you, and shall not be restored to you: your sheep shall be given to your enemies, and you shall have no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joel 3:6; Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 17:5"> Your sons and your daughters shall be given to another nation, and your eyes wasting away shall look for them: your hand shall have no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremias 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:29–30; Esias 1:7"> A nation which you know not shall eat the produce of your land, and all your labours; and you shall be injured and crushed always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 16:10–11; Deuteronomy 28:68; Esias 33:14"> And you shall be distracted, because of the sights of your eyes which you shall see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27; Esias 3:24; Esias 3:17"> The Lord strike you with an evil sore, on the knees and the legs, so that you shall not be able to be healed from the sole of your foot to the crown of your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 16:13; Deuteronomy 4:28; Kings IV 25:11"> The Lord carry away you and your princes, whom you shall set over you, to a nation which neither you nor your fathers know; and you shall there serve other gods, wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremias 25:9; Kings III 9:7–8; Jeremias 24:9"> And you shall be there for a wonder, and a parable, and a tale, amongst all the nations, to which the Lord your God shall carry you away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Aggaeus 1:6; Michaeas 6:15; Joel 1:4"> You shall carry forth much seed into the field, and you shall bring in little, because the locust shall devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Esias 17:10–11; Joel 2:2–4; Jonas 4:7"> You shall plant a vineyard, and dress it, and shall not drink the wine, neither shall you delight yourself with it, because the worm shall devour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. them. i.e. the fruits of it.">e</a></sup>it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Michaeas 6:15; Psalms 23:5; Psalms 104:15"> You shall have olive trees in all your borders, and you shall not anoint you with oil, because your olive shall utterly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. flow, or fall down.">f</a></sup>cast its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:32; Lamentations 1:5; Kings IV 24:14"> You shall beget sons and daughters, and they shall not be yours, for they shall depart into captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 7:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:38–39"> All your trees and the fruits of your land shall the blight consume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:13; Kings I 13:19–23; Kings IV 17:20"> The stranger that is within you shall get up very high, and you shall come down very low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Lamentations 1:5"> He shall lend to you, and you shall not lend to him: he shall be the head, and you shall be the tail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15; Esias 1:20; Jeremias 24:9–10"> And all these curses shall come upon you, and shall pursue you, and shall overtake you, until he shall have consumed you, and until he shall have destroyed you; because you did not listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep his commands, and his ordinances which he has commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Esias 8:18; Deuteronomy 28:37; Jezekiel 14:8"> And these things shall be signs in you, and wonders amongst your seed for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7–12; Nehemiah 9:35–37"> because you did not serve the Lord your God with gladness and a good heart, because of the abundance of all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremias 28:13–14; Lamentations 5:2–6; Jezekiel 4:16–17"> And you shall serve your enemies, which the Lord will send forth against you, in hunger, and in thirst, and in nakedness, and in the lack of all things; and you shall wear upon your neck a yoke of iron until he shall have destroyed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Lamentations 4:19; Jeremias 49:22; Jeremias 48:40"> The Lord shall bring upon you a nation from the extremity of the earth, like the swift flying of an eagle, a nation whose voice you shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. bear.">g</a></sup>understand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Esias 47:6; Chronicles II 36:17; Luke 21:23–24"> a nation bold in countenance, which shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. wonder at.">h</a></sup>respect the person of the aged and shall not pity the young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Esias 62:8; Leviticus 26:26"> And it shall eat up the young of your cattle, and the fruits of your land, so as not to leave to you corn, wine, oil, the herds of your oxen, and the flocks of your sheep, until it shall have destroyed you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Jeremias 52:4–7; Jezekiel 4:1–8; Matthew 24:15–16"> and have utterly crushed you in your cities, until the high and strong walls be destroyed, in which you trust, in all your land; and it shall afflict you in your cities, which he has given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Jeremias 19:9; Leviticus 26:29"> And you shall eat the fruit of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. belly.">i</a></sup>body, the flesh of your sons and of your daughters, all that he has given you, in your straitness and your affliction, with which your enemy shall afflict you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:9; Matthew 20:15; Deuteronomy 13:6"> He that is tender and very delicate within you shall look with an evil eye upon his brother, and the wife in his bosom, and the children that are left, which may have been left to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jeremias 34:2; Jeremias 52:6; Jeremias 5:10"> so as not to give to one of them of the flesh of his children, whom he shall eat, because of his having nothing left him in your straitness, and in your affliction, with which your enemies shall afflict you in all your cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:54; Esias 3:16; Lamentations 4:3–6"> And she that is tender and delicate amongst you, whose foot has not assayed to go upon the earth for delicacy and tenderness, shall look with an evil eye on her husband in her bosom, and her son and her daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Esias 49:15; Genesis 49:10"> and her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lit. afterbirth.">j</a></sup>offspring that comes out between her feet, and the child which she shall bear; for she shall eat them because of the lack of all things, secretly in your straitness, and in your affliction, with which your enemy shall afflict you in your cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Esias 42:8; Jeremias 7:9–10; Malachias 1:14"> If you will not listen to do all the words of this law, which have been written in this book, to fear this glorious and wonderful name, the Lord your God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Mark 13:19; Deuteronomy 28:46; Daniel 9:12"> then the Lord shall magnify your plagues, and the plagues of your seed, great and wonderful plagues, and evil and dwelling diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 28:27"> And he shall bring upon you all the evil pain of Egypt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. which you feardst before their face. Hebraism.">k</a></sup>of which you were afraid, and they shall cleave to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:25–26"> And the Lord shall bring upon you every sickness, and every plague that is not written, and every one that is written in the book of this law, until he shall have destroyed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Nehemiah 9:23; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And you⌃ shall be left few in number, whereas you⌃ were as the stars of the sky in multitude; because you did not listen to the voice of the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Proverbs 1:26; Jeremias 32:41; Deuteronomy 30:9"> And it shall come to pass that as the Lord rejoiced over you to do you good, and to multiply you, so the Lord will rejoice over you to destroy you; and you⌃ shall be quickly removed from the land, into which you⌃ go to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27–28; Leviticus 26:33; Jeremias 16:13"> And the Lord your God shall scatter you amongst all nations, from one end of the earth to the other; and you shall there serve other gods, wood and stone, which you have not known, nor your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Romans 11:10; Amos 9:4"> Moreover amongst those nations he will not give you quiet, neither by any means shall the sole of your foot have rest; and the Lord shall give you there another and a misgiving heart, and failing eyes, and a wasting soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Hebrews 10:27; Revelation 6:15–17"> And your life shall be in suspense before your eyes; and you shall be afraid by day and by night, and you shall have no assurance of your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:34; Job 7:3–4; Revelation 9:6"> In the morning you shall say, Would it were evening! and in the evening you shall say, Would it were morning! for the fear of your heart with which you shall fear, and for the sights of your eyes which you shall see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Jeremias 44:12; Jeremias 43:7; Osee 8:13"> And the Lord shall bring you back to Egypt in ships, by the way of which I said, You shall not see it again; and you⌃ shall be sold there to your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and none shall buy you.</span>
