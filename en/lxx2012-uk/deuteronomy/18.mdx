<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–9; Deuteronomy 10:9; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> The priests, the Levites, even the whole tribe of Levi, shall have no part nor inheritance with Israel; the burnt offerings of the Lord are their inheritance, they shall eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 1:5–6; Psalms 84:11; 1 Peter 2:5"> And they shall have no inheritance amongst their brethren; the Lord himself is his portion, as he said to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Deuteronomy 12:27"> And this is the due of the priests in the things coming from the people from those who offer sacrifices, whether it be a calf or a sheep; and you shall give the shoulder to the priest, and the cheeks, and the great intestine:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; Numbers 18:12–24; Chronicles II 31:4–10"> and the first fruits of your corn, and of your wine, and of your oil; and you shall give to him the first fruits of the fleeces of your sheep:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 3:10; Exodus 28:1–14"> because the Lord has chosen him out of all your tribes, to stand before the Lord your God, to minister and bless in his name, himself and his sons amongst the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:2–3; Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 16:2"> And if a Levite come from one of the cities of all the children of Israel, where he himself dwells, accordingly as his mind desires, to the place which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e. God.">a</a></sup>he shall have chosen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 31:2–4"> he shall minister to the name of the Lord his God, as all his brethren the Levites, who stand there present before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; Chronicles II 31:4"> He shall eat an allotted portion, besides the sale of his hereditary property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> And when you shall have entered into the land which the Lord your God gives you, you shall not learn to do according to the abominations of those nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> There shall not be found in you one who purges his son or his daughter with fire, one who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. divines.">b</a></sup>uses divination, who deals with omens, and augury,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Kings I 28:11–14"> a sorcerer employing incantation, one who has in him a divining spirit, an observer of signs, questioning the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> For every one that does these things is an abomination to the Lord your God; for because of these abominations the Lord will destroy them from before your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> You shall be perfect before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> For all these nations whose land you shall inherit, they will listen to omens and divinations; but the Lord your God has not permitted you so to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> The Lord your God shall raise up to you a prophet of your brethren, like me; him shall you⌃ hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> according to all things which you did desire of the Lord your God in Choreb in the day of the assembly, saying, We will not again hear the voice of the Lord your God, and we will not any more see this great fire, and so we shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> And the Lord said to me, They have spoken rightly all that they have said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> I will raise up to them a prophet of their brethren, like you; and I will put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them as I shall command him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> And whatever man shall not listen to whatever words that prophet shall speak in my name, I will take vengeance on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremias 27:15; Jeremias 23:13–15"> But the prophet whoever shall impiously speak in my name a word which I have not commanded him to speak, and whoever shall speak in the name of other gods, that prophet shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> But if you shall say in your heart, How shall we know the word which the Lord has not spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Kings IV 20:1; Jeremias 28:1–17"> Whatsoever words that prophet shall speak in the name of the Lord, and they shall not come true, and not come to pass, this is the thing which the Lord has not spoken; that prophet has spoken wickedly: you⌃ shall not spare him.</span>
