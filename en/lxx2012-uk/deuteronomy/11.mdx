<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5; Deuteronomy 10:12; Luke 1:74–75"> Therefore you shall love the Lord your God, and shall observe his appointments, and his ordinances, and his commandments, and his judgements, always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2–5; Deuteronomy 29:10; Acts 26:22"> And you⌃ shall know this day; for I speak not to your children, who know not and have not seen the discipline of the Lord your God, and his wonderful works, and his strong hand, and his high arm,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:19; Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 105:27–45"> and his miracles, and his wonders, which he wrought in the midst of Egypt on Pharao king of Egypt, and all his land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 106:11; Exodus 14:23–31; Exodus 15:19"> and what he did to the host of the Egyptians, and to their chariots, and their cavalry, and their host; how he made the water of the Red Sea to overwhelm the face of them as they pursued after you, and the Lord destroyed them until this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; Psalms 106:12–48; Psalms 78:14–72"> and all the things which he did to you in the wilderness until you⌃ came into this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:9–10; Psalms 106:17; Numbers 16:1–35"> and all the things that he did to Dathan and Abiron the sons of Eliab the son of Ruben, whom the earth opening her mouth swallowed up, and their houses, and their tents, and all their substance that was with them, in the midst of all Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–6; Psalms 145:12; Psalms 150:2"> for your eyes have seen all the mighty works of the Lord, which he wrought amongst you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:6–7; Ephesians 3:16"> And you⌃ shall keep all his commandments, as many as I command you today, that you⌃ may live, and be multiplied, and that you⌃ may go in and inherit the land, into which you⌃ go across Jordan to inherit it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 6:2"> that you⌃ may live long upon the land, which the Lord swore to your fathers to give to them, and to their seed after them, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 14:18"> For the land into which you go to inherit it, is not as the land of Egypt, whence you⌃ came out, whenever they sow the seed, and water it with their feet, as a garden of herbs:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Psalms 65:12–13"> but the land into which you go to inherit it, is a land of mountains and plains; it shall drink water of the rain of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 24:6; Psalms 33:18; Kings III 9:3"> A land which the Lord your God surveys continually, the eyes of the Lord your God are upon it from the beginning of the year to the end of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Deuteronomy 4:29; Deuteronomy 6:17"> Now if you⌃ will indeed listen to all the commands which I charge you this day, to love the Lord your God, and to serve him with all your heart, and with all your soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 5:7; Leviticus 26:4; Jezekiel 34:26"> then he shall give to your land the early and latter rain in its season, and you shall bring in your corn, and your wine, and your oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Deuteronomy 6:11; Joel 2:19"> And he shall give food in your fields to your cattle; and when you have eaten and are full,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:27; Hebrews 3:12; Hebrews 2:1"> take heed to yourself that your heart be not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr.  made broad.">a</a></sup>puffed up, and you⌃ transgress, and serve other gods, and worship them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 8:35; Deuteronomy 6:15; Chronicles II 6:26"> and the Lord be angry with you, and restrain the heaven; and there shall not be rain, and the earth shall not yield its fruit, and you⌃ shall perish quickly from off the good land, which the Lord has given you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Proverbs 3:1"> And you⌃ shall store these words in your heart and in your soul, and you⌃ shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and it shall be fixed before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Esias 38:19; Deuteronomy 4:9–10"> And you⌃ shall teach them to your children, so as to speak about them when you sit in the house, and when you walk by the way, and when you sleep, and when you rise up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:9"> And you⌃ shall write them on the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, thresholds.">b</a></sup>lintels of your houses, and on your gates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 3:2; Proverbs 9:11"> that your days may be long, and the days of your children, upon the land which the Lord swore to your fathers to give to them, as the days of heaven upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:17; Deuteronomy 11:13; Deuteronomy 10:20"> And it shall come to pass that if you⌃ will indeed listen to all these commands, which I charge you to observe this day, to love the Lord our God, and to walk in all his ways, and to cleave close to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> then the Lord shall cast out all these nations before you, and you⌃ shall inherit great nations and stronger than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 14:9; Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21"> Every place whereon the sole of your foot shall tread shall be yours; from the wilderness and Antilibanus, and from the great river, the river Euphrates, even as far as the west sea shall be your coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Exodus 23:27; Joshua 1:5"> No one shall stand before you; and the Lord your God will put the fear of you and the dread of you on the face of all the land, on which you⌃ shall tread, as he told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:1; Deuteronomy 30:15–20; Galatians 3:10"> Behold, I set before you this day the blessing and the curse;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esias 1:19; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Esias 3:10"> the blessing, if you⌃ listen to the commands of the Lord your God, all that I command you this day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 3:10"> and the curse, if you⌃ do not listen to the commands of the Lord our God, as many as I command you this day, and you⌃ wander from the way which I have commanded you, having gone to serve other gods, which you⌃ know not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:12–26; Joshua 8:30–35"> And it shall come to pass when the Lord your God shall have brought you into the land into which you go over to inherit it, then you shall put blessing on mount Garizin, and the curse upon mount Gaebal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Judges 7:1; Joshua 4:19"> Behold! are not these beyond Jordan, behind, westward in the land of Chanaan, which lies westward near Golgol, by the high oak?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 1:11; Deuteronomy 9:1; Joshua 3:13–17"> For you⌃ are passing over Jordan, to go in and inherit the land, which the Lord our God gives you to inherit always, and you⌃ shall dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Deuteronomy 12:32; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> And you⌃ shall take heed to do all his ordinances, and these judgements, as many as I set before you today.</span>
