<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Deuteronomy 1:16–17; Deuteronomy 17:8–9"> And if there should be a dispute between men, and they should come forward to judgement, and the judges judge, and justify the righteous, and condemn the wicked:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:40"> then it shall come to pass, if the unrighteous should be worthy of stripes, you shall lay him down before the judges, and they shall scourge him before them according to his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 18:3; James 2:2–3; Luke 15:30"> And they shall scourge him with forty stripes in number, they shall not inflict more; for if you should scourge him with more stripes beyond these stripes, your brother will be disgraced before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Proverbs 12:10"> You shall not muzzle the ox that treads out the corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:24; Luke 20:28; Mark 12:19"> And if brethren should live together, and one of them should die, and should not have seed, the wife of the deceased shall not marry out of the family to a man not related: her husband's brother shall go in to her, and shall take her to himself for a wife, and shall dwell with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> And it shall come to pass that the child which she shall bear, shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. constituted.">a</a></sup>named by the name of the deceased, and his name shall not be blotted out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> And if the man should not be willing to take his brother's wife, then shall the woman go up to the gate to the elders, and she shall say, My husband's brother will not raise up the name of his brother in Israel, my husband's brother<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. has not been willing.">b</a></sup>has refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> And the elders of his city shall call him, and speak to him; and if he stand and say, I will not take her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Esias 50:6"> then his brother's wife shall come forward before the elders, and shall loose one shoe from off his foot, and shall spit in his face, and shall answer and say, Thus shall they do to the man who will not build his brother's house in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And his name shall be called in Israel, The house of him that has had his shoe loosed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> And if men should strive together, a man with his brother, and the wife of one of them should advance to rescue her husband out of the hand of him that smites him, and she should stretch forth her hand, and take hold of his private parts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> you shall cut off her hand; your eye shall not spare her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> You shall not have in your bag various weights, a great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. or.">c</a></sup>and a small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You shall not have in your house various measures, a great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. or.">d</a></sup>and a small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> You shall have a true and just weight, and a true and just measure, that you may live long upon the land which the Lord your God gives you for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For every one that does this is an abomination to the Lord your God, even every one that does injustice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what things Amalec did to you by the way, when you went forth out of the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 16:6"> how he withstood you in the way, and harassed your rear, even those that were weary behind you, and you did hunger and was weary; and he did not fear God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; Kings I 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> And it shall come to pass whenever the Lord your God shall have given you rest from all your enemies round about you, in the land which the Lord your God gives you to inherit, you shall blot out the name of Amalec from under heaven, and shall not forget to do it.</span>
