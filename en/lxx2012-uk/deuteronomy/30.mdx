<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; Kings III 8:47–48; Deuteronomy 30:19"> And it shall come to pass when all these things shall have come upon you, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before your face, and you shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. receive them into your heart.">a</a></sup>call them to mind amongst all the nations, wherein the Lord shall have scattered you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Nehemiah 1:9; Deuteronomy 4:28–31"> and shall return to the Lord your God, and shall listen to his voice, according to all things which I charge you this day, with all your heart, and with all your soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 29:14; Jezekiel 36:24; Psalms 147:2"> then the Lord shall heal your iniquities, and shall pity you, and shall again gather you out from all the nations, amongst which the Lord has scattered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:9; Jezekiel 39:25–29; Deuteronomy 28:64"> If your dispersion be from one end of heaven to the other, thence will the Lord your God gather you, and thence will the Lord your God take you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 29:14"> And the Lord your God shall bring you in from thence into the land which your fathers have inherited, and you shall inherit it; and he will do you good, and multiply you above your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Jezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremias 32:39"> And the Lord shall purge your heart, and the heart of your seed, to love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, that you may live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:15; Lamentations 4:21–22; Jeremias 51:24–26"> And the Lord your God will put these curses upon your enemies, and upon those that hate you, who have persecuted you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:16; Jezekiel 36:27; Jezekiel 11:19–20"> And you shall return and listen to the voice of the Lord your God, and shall keep his commands, all that I charge you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:11–14; Jeremias 32:41; Jeremias 33:9"> And the Lord your God shall bless you in every work of your hands, in the offspring of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. belly.">b</a></sup>body, and in the offspring of your cattle, and in the fruits of your land, because the Lord your God will again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Deuteronomy 4:29; Nehemiah 1:9"> if you will listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep his commandments, and his ordinances, and his judgements written in the book of this law, if you turn to the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 45:19; Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 16:25–26"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Rom 6, 7, 8.">c</a></sup>For this command which I give you this day is not grievous, neither is it far from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 10:6–8; Proverbs 30:4; John 3:13"> It is not in heaven above, as if there were one saying, Who shall go up for us into heaven, and shall take it for us, and we will hear and do it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:33; Matthew 12:42; Proverbs 2:1–5"> Neither is it beyond the sea, saying, Who will go over for us to the other side of the sea, and take it for us, and make it audible to us, and we will do it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 10:8–10; Jeremias 12:2; Matthew 7:21"> The word is very near you, in your mouth, and in your heart, and in your hands to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:26; Deuteronomy 30:19; 1 John 5:11–12"> Behold, I have set before you this day life and death, good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Matthew 22:37–38; 1 John 5:2–3"> If you will listen to the commands of the Lord your God, which I command you this day, to love the Lord your God, to walk in all his ways, and to keep his ordinances, and his judgements; then you⌃ shall live, and shall be many in number, and the Lord your God shall bless you in all the land into which you go to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Hebrews 3:12; Proverbs 1:32"> But if your heart change, and you will not listen, and you shall go astray and worship other gods, and serve them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19–20; Esias 63:17–18; Deuteronomy 4:26"> I declare to you this day, that you⌃ shall utterly perish, and you⌃ shall by no means live long upon the land, into which you⌃ go over Jordan to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15; Deuteronomy 11:26; Psalms 119:30"> I call both heaven and earth to witness this day against you, I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the curse: choose you life, that you and your seed may live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 17:3"> to love the Lord your God, to listen to his voice, and cleave to him; for this is your life, and the length of your days, that you should dwell upon the land, which the Lord swore to your fathers, Abraam, and Isaac, and Jacob, to give to them.</span>
