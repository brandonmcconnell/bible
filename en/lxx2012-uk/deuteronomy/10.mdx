<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 34:1–2; Exodus 25:10–15; Deuteronomy 10:3–4"> At that time the Lord said to me, Hew for yourself two stone tables as the first, and come up to me into the mountain, and you shall make for yourself an ark of wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 8:9; Deuteronomy 10:5; Deuteronomy 4:13"> And you shall write upon the tables the words which were on the first tables which you did break, and you shall put them into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:4; Exodus 37:1–9; Exodus 25:10"> So I made an ark of boards of incorruptible wood, and I hewed tables of stone like the first, and I went up to the mountain, and the two tables were in my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:10; Exodus 34:28; Hebrews 12:18–19"> And he wrote upon the tables according to the first writing the ten commandments, which the Lord spoke to you in the mountain out of the midst of the fire, and the Lord gave them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:29; Exodus 40:20; Deuteronomy 10:2"> And I turned and came down from the mountain, and I put the tables into the ark which I had made; and there they were, as the Lord commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:23–28; Numbers 33:30–33"> And the children of Israel departed from Beeroth of the sons of Jakim to Misadai: there Aaron died, and there he was buried, and Eleazar his son was priest in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:32–34"> Thence they departed to Gadgad; and from Gadgad to Etebatha, a land wherein are torrents of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Deuteronomy 18:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> At that time the Lord separated the tribe of Levi, to bear the ark of the covenant of the Lord, to stand near before the Lord, to minister and bless in his name to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:28; Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Therefore the Levites have no part nor inheritance amongst their brethren; the Lord himself is their inheritance, as he said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 34:28"> And I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. stood.">a</a></sup>remained in the mount forty days and forty nights: and the Lord heard me at that time also, and the Lord would not destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:1; Exodus 32:34"> And the Lord said to me, Go, set out before this people, and let them go in and inherit the land, which I swore to their fathers to give to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Michaeas 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:5; Luke 10:27"> And now, Israel, what does the Lord your God require of you, but to fear the Lord your God, and to walk in all his ways, and to love him, and to serve the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 32:39; Deuteronomy 6:24; James 1:25"> to keep the commandments of the Lord your God, and his ordinances, all that I charge you today, that it may be well with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Psalms 24:1; Psalms 115:16"> Behold, the heaven and the heaven of heavens belong to the Lord your God, the earth and all things that are in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:37; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 9:13–23"> Only the Lord chose your fathers to love them, and he chose out their seed after them, even you, beyond all nations, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 4:4; Deuteronomy 30:6; Leviticus 26:41"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. and.">b</a></sup>Therefore you⌃ shall circumcise the hardness of your heart, and you⌃ shall not harden your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; Daniel 2:47"> For the Lord your God, he is God of gods, and the Lord of lords, the great, and strong, and terrible God, who does not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. wonder at or admire a face.">c</a></sup>accept persons, nor will he by any means accept a bribe:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5; Jeremias 49:11"> executing judgement for the stranger and orphan and widow, and he loves the stranger to give him food and raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Leviticus 19:33–34; Luke 17:18"> And you⌃ shall love the stranger; for you⌃ were strangers in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 13:4; Luke 4:8"> You shall fear the Lord your God, and serve him, and shall cleave to him, and shall swear by his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 17:14; Psalms 106:21–22; Exodus 15:2"> He is your boast, and he is your God, who has wrought in the midst of you these great and glorious things, which your eyes have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5; Genesis 15:5"> With seventy souls your fathers went down into Egypt; but the Lord your God has made you as the stars of heaven in multitude.</span>
