<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 4:45"> And these are the commands, and the ordinances, and the judgements, as many as the Lord our God gave commandment to teach you to do so in the land on which you⌃ enter to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 13:4; Exodus 20:20"> That you⌃ may fear the Lord your God, keep you⌃ all his ordinances, and his commandments, which I command you today, you, and your sons, and your sons' sons, all the days of your life, that you⌃ may live many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Exodus 3:8; Genesis 26:4"> Hear, therefore, O Israel, and observe to do them, that it may be well with you, and that you⌃ may be greatly multiplied, as the Lord God of your fathers said that he would give you a land flowing with milk and honey: and these are the ordinances, and the judgements, which the Lord commanded the children of Israel in the wilderness, when they had gone forth from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Esias 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Hear, O Israel, The Lord our God is one Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> And you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and all your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremias 31:33"> And these words, all that I command you this day, shall be in your heart and in your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 78:4–6"> And you shall teach them to your children, and you shall speak of them sitting in the house, and walking by the way, and lying down, and rising up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> And you shall fasten them for a sign upon your hand, and it shall be immoveable before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Esias 30:8; Esias 57:8"> And you⌃ shall write them on the lintels of your houses and of your gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalms 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> And it shall come to pass when the Lord your God shall have brought you into the land which he swore to your fathers, to Abraam, and to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give you great and beautiful cities which you did not build,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Jezekiel 16:10–20"> houses full of all good things which you did not fill,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, pits or pools.">a</a></sup>wells dug in the rock which you did not dig, vineyards and olive yards which you did not plant, then having eaten and been filled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> beware lest you forget the Lord your God that brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> You shall fear the Lord your God, and him only shall you serve; and you shall cleave to him, and by his name you shall swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> Go you⌃ not after other gods of the gods of the nations round about you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> for the Lord your God in the midst of you is a jealous God, lest the Lord your God be very angry with you, and destroy you from off the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> You shall not tempt the Lord your God, as you⌃ tempted him in the temptation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalms 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You shall by all means keep the commands of the Lord your God, the testimonies, and the ordinances, which he commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> And you shall do that which is pleasing and good before the Lord your God, that it may be well with you, and that you may go in and inherit the good land, which the Lord swore to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> to chase all your enemies from before your face, as the Lord said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> And it shall come to pass when your son shall ask you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. to-morrow.">b</a></sup>at a future time, saying, What are the testimonies, and the ordinances, and the judgements, which the Lord our God has commanded us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> Then shall you say to your son, We were slaves to Pharao in the land of Egypt, and the Lord brought us forth thence with a mighty hand, and with a high arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> And the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. gave.">c</a></sup>wrought signs and great and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. evil.">d</a></sup>grievous wonders in Egypt, on Pharao and on his house before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> And he brought us out thence to give us this land, which he swore to give to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 32:39; Psalms 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> And the Lord charged us to observe all these ordinances; to fear the Lord our God, that it may be well with us for ever, that we may live, as even today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> And there shall be mercy to us, if we take heed to keep all these commands before the Lord our God, as he has commanded us.</span>
