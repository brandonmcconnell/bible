<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 8:40; Job 7:1; Deuteronomy 4:19"> And these are the ordinances and the judgements, which you⌃ shall observe to do in the land, which the Lord God of your fathers gives you for an inheritance, all the days which you⌃ live upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Kings IV 16:4; Numbers 33:51–52"> You⌃ shall utterly destroy all the places in which they served their gods, whose land you⌃ inherit, on the high mountains and on the hills, and under the thick tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 2:2; Zacharias 13:2; Numbers 33:52"> And you⌃ shall destroy their altars, and break in pieces their pillars, and you⌃ shall cut down their groves, and you⌃ shall burn with fire the graven images of their gods, and you⌃ shall abolish their name out of that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 20:18; Deuteronomy 16:21–22"> You⌃ shall not do so to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:11; Deuteronomy 26:2; Chronicles II 7:12"> But in the place which the Lord your God shall choose in one of your cities to name his name there, and to be called upon, you⌃ shall even seek him out and go there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:19–20; Malachias 3:10; Leviticus 27:32–33"> And you⌃ shall carry there your whole burnt offerings, and your sacrifices, and your first fruits, and your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. vows.">a</a></sup>vowed-offerings, and your free will offerings, and your offerings of thanksgiving, the firstborn of your herds, and of your flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:18; Leviticus 23:40; Deuteronomy 14:26"> And you⌃ shall eat there before the Lord your God, and you⌃ shall rejoice in all the things on which you⌃ shall lay your hand, you⌃ and your houses, as the Lord your God has blessed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 21:25; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 21:2"> You⌃ shall not do altogether as we do here today, every man that which is pleasing in his own sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 8:56; Psalms 95:11; Deuteronomy 25:19"> For hitherto you⌃ have not arrived at the rest and the inheritance, which the Lord our God gives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 33:11; Jezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 3:27"> And you⌃ shall go over Jordan, and shall dwell in the land, which the Lord our God takes as an inheritance for you; and he shall give you rest from all your enemies round about, and you⌃ shall dwell safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Deuteronomy 15:20; Deuteronomy 17:8"> And there shall be a place which the Lord your God shall choose for his name to be called there, there shall you⌃ bring all things that I order you today; your whole burnt offerings, and your sacrifices, and your tithes, and the first fruits of your hands, and every choice gift of yours, whatever you⌃ shall vow to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 10:9; Deuteronomy 26:12"> And you⌃ shall rejoice before the Lord your God, you⌃ and your sons, and your daughters, and your menservants and your maidservants, and the Levite that is at your gates; because he has no portion or inheritance with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 12:28–32; Kings III 15:34; Chronicles II 15:17"> Take heed to yourself that you offer not your whole burnt offerings in any place which you shall see;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:11; 2 Corinthians 5:19; Deuteronomy 12:5"> save in the place which the Lord your God shall choose, in one of your tribes, there shall you⌃ offer your whole burnt offerings, and there shall you do all things whatever I charge you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:5; Deuteronomy 15:22–23; Deuteronomy 14:26"> But you shall kill according to all your desire, and shall eat flesh according to the blessing of the Lord your God, which he has given you in every city; the unclean that is within you and the clean shall eat it on equal terms, as the doe or the stag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Deuteronomy 15:23; Deuteronomy 12:23–24"> Only you⌃ shall not eat the blood; you⌃ shall pour it out on the ground as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:14; Deuteronomy 12:6; Deuteronomy 14:22–29"> You shall not be able to eat in your cities the tithe of your corn, and of your wine, and of your oil, the firstborn of your herd and of your flock, and all your vows as many as you⌃ shall have vowed, and your thank-offerings, and the first fruits of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:7; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5"> But before the Lord your God you shall eat it, in the place which the Lord your God shall choose for himself, you, and your son, and your daughter, your man-servant, and your maidservant, and the stranger that is within your gates; and you shall rejoice before the Lord your God, on whatever you shall lay your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles II 31:4–21; Nehemiah 10:34–39; Deuteronomy 14:27–29"> Take heed to yourself that you do not desert the Levite all the time that you live upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8; Deuteronomy 11:24; Exodus 34:24"> And if the Lord your God shall enlarge your borders, as he said to you, and you shall say, I will eat flesh; if your soul should desire to eat flesh, you shall eat flesh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. in all.">b</a></sup>according to all the desire of your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Kings III 14:21; Deuteronomy 16:11"> And if the place be far from you, which the Lord your God shall choose for himself, that his name be called upon it, then you shall kill of your herd and of your flock which God shall have given you, even as I commanded you, and you shall eat in your cities according to the desire of your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> As the doe and the stag are eaten, so shall you eat it; the unclean in you and the clean shall eat it in like manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 17:13–14"> Take diligent heed that you eat no blood, for blood is the life of it; the life shall not be eaten with the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Deuteronomy 15:23"> You⌃ shall not eat it; you⌃ shall pour it out on the ground as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 11:38; Deuteronomy 4:40; Esias 3:10"> You shall not eat it, that it may be well with you and with your sons after you, if you shall do that which is good and pleasing before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Numbers 5:9–10; Deuteronomy 12:11"> But you shall take your holy things, if you have any, and your vowed-offerings, and come to the place which the Lord your God shall choose to have his name named upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 1:9"> And you shall sacrifice your whole burnt offerings, you shall offer the flesh upon the altar of the Lord your God; but the blood of your sacrifices you shall pour out at the foot of the altar of the Lord your God, but the flesh you shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; John 15:14; John 15:10"> Beware and listen, and you shall do all the commands which I charge you, that it may be well with you and with your sons for ever, if you shall do that which is pleasing and good before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 23:4; Deuteronomy 19:1; Deuteronomy 9:3"> And if the Lord your God shall utterly destroy the nations, to whom you go in there to inherit their land, from before you, and you shall inherit it, and dwell in their land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 10:2; Jezekiel 20:32; Deuteronomy 7:16"> take heed to yourself that you seek not to follow them after they are destroyed before you, saying, How do these nations act towards their gods? I will do likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremias 32:35; Deuteronomy 18:9–10; Kings IV 17:15–17"> You shall not do so to your God; for they have sacrificed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. in, or, amongst.">c</a></sup>to their gods the abominations of the Lord which he hates, for they burn their sons and their daughters in fire to their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Revelation 22:18–19; Joshua 1:7"> Every word that I command you this day, it shall you observe to do: you shall not add to it, nor diminish from it.</span>
