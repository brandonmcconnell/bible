<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:10; Exodus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:2–4"> Every seven years you shall make a release.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 7:42; James 2:13; Luke 6:34–38"> And this is the ordinance of the release: you shall remit every private debt which your neighbour owes you, and you shall not ask payment of it from your brother; for it has been called a release to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:20; Matthew 17:25–26; 1 Corinthians 6:6–7"> Of a stranger you shall ask again whatever he has of yours, but to your brother you shall remit his debt to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 58:10–11; Proverbs 28:27; Proverbs 11:24–25"> For thus there shall not be a poor person in the midst of you, for the Lord your God will surely bless you in the land which the Lord your God gives you by inheritance, that you should inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–15; Esias 1:19–20; Joshua 1:7"> And if you⌃ shall indeed listen to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep and do all these commandments, as many as I charge you this day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12–13; Luke 6:35; Nehemiah 9:27"> (for the Lord your God has blessed you in the way of which he spoke to you,) then you shall lend to many nations, but you shall not borrow; and you shall rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:16–17; Deuteronomy 15:9; Proverbs 21:13"> And if there shall be in the midst of you a poor man of your brethren in one of your cities in the land, which the Lord your God gives you, you shall not harden your heart, neither shall you by any means close up your hand from your brother who is in lack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:34; Matthew 5:42"> You shall surely open your hands to him, and shall lend to him as much as he wants according to his need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; Matthew 20:15; Jeremias 17:10"> Take heed to yourself that there be not a secret thing in your heart, an iniquity, saying, The seventh year, the year of release, draws near; and your eye shall be evil to your brother that is in lack, and you shall not give to him, and he shall cry against you to the Lord, and there shall be great sin in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:9; Hebrews 13:16; Esias 58:10"> You shall surely give to him, and you shall lend him as much as he wants, according as he is in need; and you shall not grudge in your heart as you give to him, because on this account the Lord your God will bless you in all your works, and in all things on which you shall lay your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11; John 12:8"> For the poor shall not fail off your land, therefore I charge you to do this thing, saying, You shall surely open your hands to your poor brother, and to him that is distressed upon your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 34:14; Leviticus 25:39–41; Exodus 21:2–6"> And if your brother or sister, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, be sold to you, he shall serve you six years, and in the seventh year you shall send him out free from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachias 3:5; Jeremias 22:13; Proverbs 3:27–28"> And when you shall send him out free from you, you shall not send him out empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Proverbs 10:22; Psalms 68:10"> You shall give him provision for the way from your flock, and from your corn, and from your wine; as the Lord your God has blessed you, you shall give to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:12; Matthew 18:32–33; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> And you shall remember that you were a servant in the land of Egypt, and the Lord your God redeemed you from thence; therefore I charge you to do this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:5–6; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 40:6"> And if he should say to you, I will not go out from you, because he continues to love you and your house, because he is well with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 1:22; Leviticus 25:39–42"> then you shall take an awl, and bore his ear through to the door, and he shall be your servant for ever; and in like manner shall you do to your maidservant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 17:7–8; Esias 16:14; Esias 21:16"> It shall not seem hard to you when they are sent out free from you, because your servant has served you six years according to the annual hire of a hireling; so the Lord your God shall bless you in all things whatever you may do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12; Leviticus 27:26"> Every firstborn that shall be born amongst your kine and your sheep, you shall sanctify the males to the Lord your God; you shall not work with your firstborn calf, and you shall not shear the firstborn of your sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17; Deuteronomy 14:23; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> You shall eat it before the Lord year by year in the place which the Lord your God shall choose, you and your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachias 1:7–8; Leviticus 22:19–25"> And if there be in it a blemish, if it be lame or blind, an evil blemish, you shall not sacrifice it to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:21–22; Deuteronomy 12:15–16"> You shall eat it in your cities; the unclean in you and the clean shall eat it in like manner, as the doe or the stag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Leviticus 7:26; Deuteronomy 12:23"> Only you⌃ shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it out on the earth as water.</span>
