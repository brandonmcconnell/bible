<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Kings I 26:1; Psalms 20:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen, O God, to my prayer; and disregard not my supplication.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 130:2; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 13:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Attend to me, and listen to me: I was grieved in my meditation, and troubled;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:14; Psalms 36:1; Psalms 53:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">because of the voice of the enemy, and because of the oppression of the sinner: for they brought iniquity against me, and were wrathfully angry with me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Psalms 118:6–7; Esias 41:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart was troubled within me; and the fear of death fell upon me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 143:12; Psalms 89:49; Psalms 31:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fear and trembling came upon me, and darkness covered me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:9; Psalms 147:1; Psalms 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I said, O that I had wings as those of a dove! then would I flee away, and be at rest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 59:10; Psalms 92:11; Psalms 112:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold! I have fled afar off, and lodged in the wilderness. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I waited for him that should deliver me from distress of spirit and tempest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Destroy, O Lord, and divide their tongues: for I have seen iniquity and gain saying in the city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Day and night</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, it, sc. iniquity.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he shall go round about it upon its walls: iniquity and sorrow and unrighteousness are in the midst of it;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">and usury and craft have not failed from its streets.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For if an enemy had reproached me, I would have endured it; and if one who hated me had spoken vauntingly against me, I would have hid myself from him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, O man like minded, my guide, and my acquaintance,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">who in companionship with me sweetened our food: we walked in the house of God in concord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let death come upon them, and let them go down alive into Hades, for iniquity is in their dwellings, in the midst of them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cried to God, and the Lord listened to me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Evening, and morning, and at noon I will declare and make known my wants: and he shall hear my voice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">He shall deliver my soul in peace from them that draw near to me: for they were with me in many cases.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">God shall hear, and bring them low, even he that has existed from eternity. Pause.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, they have nothing to give in exchange, q. d. to redeem their souls. Compare Ps. 48. 7,8. Mark 8:37.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they suffer no reverse, and therefore they have not feared God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has reached forth his hand for retribution; they have profaned his covenant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They were scattered at the anger of his countenance, and his heart drew near</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Compare Heb. and A. V.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">them. His words were smoother than oil, yet are they darts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cast your care upon the Lord, and he shall</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, nourish.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">sustain you; he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, O God, shall bring them down to the pit of destruction; bloody and crafty men shall not live out half their days; but I will hope in you, O Lord.</span></span>
