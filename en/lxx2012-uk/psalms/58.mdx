<div className="hs">(59) For the end. Destroy not: by David for a memorial, when Saul sent, and watched his house to kill him.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:1; Deuteronomy 16:18–19; Psalms 72:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from my enemies, O God; and ransom me from those that rise up against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:20; Esias 10:1; Jezekiel 22:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 51:5; Esias 48:8; Proverbs 22:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For, behold, they have hunted after my soul; violent men have set upon me: neither is it my iniquity, nor my sin, O Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Without iniquity I ran and directed my course aright: awake to help me, and behold.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 8:17; Esias 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And you, Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, draw near to visit all the heathen; pity not any that work iniquity. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Job 4:10–11; Osee 5:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall return at evening, and hunger like a dog, and go round about the city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalms 64:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, they shall utter a voice with their mouth, and a sword is in their lips; for who, say they, has heard?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalms 37:35–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, Lord, will laugh them to scorn; you will utterly set at nothing all the heathen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">will keep my strength, looking to you; for you, O God, are my helper.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 68:23; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 64:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for my God, his mercy shall go before me: my God will show me vengeance on my enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 67:4; Esias 3:10; Psalms 64:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Slay them not, lest they forget your</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="some read λαοῦ 'people'.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">law; scatter them by your power; and bring them down, O Lord, my defender.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the sin of their mouth, and the word of their lips, let them be even taken in their pride.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And for their cursing and falsehood shall utter destruction be denounced: they shall fall by the wrath of utter destruction, and shall not be; so shall they know that the God of Jacob is Lord of the ends of the earth. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall return at evening, and be hungry as a dog, and go round about the city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall be scattered hither and there for meat; and if they be not satisfied, they shall even murmur.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will sing to your strength, and in the morning will I exult in your mercy; for you have been my supporter, and my refuge in the day of my affliction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are my helper; to you, my God, will I sing; you are my supporter, O my God, and my mercy.</span></span>
