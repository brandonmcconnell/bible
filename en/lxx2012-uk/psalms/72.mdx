<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 52:7; Psalms 96:11–13; Psalms 72:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I was jealous of the transgressors, beholding the tranquillity of sinners.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 11:4; Jezekiel 34:15–16; Psalms 109:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">For there is no sign of reluctance in their death: and they have firmness under their affliction.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Zacharias 9:10; Psalms 80:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have taken counsel and spoken in wickedness: they have uttered unrighteousness loftily.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 49:23; Michaeas 7:17; Luke 19:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have set their mouth against heaven, and their tongue has gone through upon the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 60:6; Psalms 68:29; Esias 49:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore shall my people return hither: and full days shall be found</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. in.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with them.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:16; Psalms 109:31; Matthew 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I said, Verily in vain have I justified my heart, and washed my hands in innocency.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 116:15; Revelation 19:2; Psalms 69:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I was plagued all the day, and my reproof was every morning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 10:14; Revelation 5:8–14; Jude 1:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I said, I will speak thus; behold, I should have broken covenant with the generation of your children.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Psalms 92:12–14; Esias 30:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I undertook to understand this, but it is too hard for me,</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 106:48; Chronicles I 29:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely you have appointed judgements to them because of their crafty dealings: you have cast them down when they were lifted up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:21; Matthew 6:10; Psalms 41:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">How have they become desolate! suddenly they have failed: they have perished because of their iniquity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings II 23:1; Job 31:40; Jeremias 51:64"> <span className="pl pl-1">As the dream of one awakening, O Lord, in your city you will despise their image.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my heart has rejoiced, and my reins have been</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. changed.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">gladdened.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I was vile and knew not: I became brutish before you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet I am continually with you: you have holden my right hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have guided me by your counsel, and you have taken me to yourself with glory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">For what have I in heaven but you? and what have I desired upon the earth beside you?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart and my flesh have failed: but God is the strength of my heart, and God is my portion for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For, behold, they that remove themselves far from you shall perish: you have destroyed every one that goes a whoring from you.</span><br /></span>
