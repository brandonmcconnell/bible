<div className="hs">(68) For the end, a Psalm of a Song by David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 4:6; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Psalms 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let God arise, and let his enemies be scattered; and let them that hate him flee from before him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 2:11; Psalms 98:2–3; Acts 18:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">As smoke vanishes, let them vanish: as wax melts before the fire, so let the sinners perish from before God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 67:5; Psalms 74:21; Psalms 45:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But let the righteous rejoice; let them exult before God: let them be delighted with joy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 98:9; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 9:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to God, sing praises to his name: make a way for him that rides upon the west (the Lord is his name) and exult before him. They shall be troubled before the face of him,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 67:3; Matthew 6:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">who is the father of the orphans, and judge of the widows: such is God in his holy place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 26:4; Exodus 3:15; Osee 2:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">God settles the solitary in a house; leading forth prisoners mightily, also them that act provokingly, even them that dwell in tombs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Acts 13:26; Esias 45:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, when you went forth before your people, when you went through the wilderness; Pause:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">the earth quaked, yes, the heavens dropped water at the presence of the God of Sina, at the presence of the God of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, you will grant to your inheritance a gracious rain; for it was weary, but you did refresh it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See the Hebrew.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">creatures dwell in it: you have in your goodness prepared for the poor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord God will give a word to them that preach it in a great company.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The king of the forces of the beloved, of the beloved, will even grant them for the beauty of the house to divide the spoils.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if you⌃ should lie amongst the lots, you⌃ shall have the wings of a dove covered with silver, and her breast with</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. greenness of gold.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">yellow gold.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the heavenly One scatters kings upon it, they shall be made snow-white in Selmon.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountain of God is a rich mountain; a</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. curdled like cheese.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">swelling mountain, a rich mountain.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore do you⌃ conceive evil, you⌃ swelling mountains? this is the mountain which God has delighted to dwell in; yes, the Lord will dwell in it for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The chariots of God are ten thousand fold, thousands of rejoicing ones: the Lord is amongst them, in Sina, in the holy place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. having gone up.">d</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">You are gone up on high, you have led captivity captive, you have received gifts for man, yes, for they were rebellious, that you might dwell amongst them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be the Lord God, blessed be the Lord daily; and the God of our salvation shall prosper us. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our God is the God of salvation; and to the Lord belong the issues from death.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">But God shall crust the heads of his enemies; the hairy crown of them that go on in their trespasses.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord said, I will bring again from Basan, I will bring my people again through the depths of the sea.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">That your foot may be dipped in blood, and the tongue of your dogs be stained with that of your enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your goings, O God, have been seen; the goings of my God, the king, in the sanctuary.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The princes went first, next before the players on instruments, in the midst of damsels playing on timbrels.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise God in the congregations, the Lord from the fountains of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is Benjamin the younger one in ecstasy, the princes of Juda their rulers, the princes of Zabulon, the princes of Nephthali.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, command you your strength: strengthen, O God, this which you have wrought in us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of your temple at Jerusalem shall kings bring presents to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rebuke the wild beasts of the reed: let the crowd of bulls with the heifers of the nations be rebuked, so that they who have been proved with silver may not be shut out: scatter you the nations that wish for wars.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ambassadors shall arrive out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall hasten to stretch out her hand readily to God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to God, you⌃ kingdoms of the earth; sing psalms to the Lord. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing to God that</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. mounts, or, has mounted.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rides on the heaven of heaven, eastward: behold, he will utter a mighty sound with his voice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give you⌃ glory to God: his excellency is over Israel, and his power is in the clouds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is wonderful</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, amongst his holy ones.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in his holy places, the God of Israel: he will give power and strength to his people: blessed be God.</span></span>
