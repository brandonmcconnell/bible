<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Psalms 27:4–6; Psalms 84:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart said to you, I have diligently sought your face: your face, O Lord, I will seek.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Psalms 139:19; Matthew 25:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">Turn not your face away from me, turn not you away from your servant in anger: be you my helper, forsake me not; and, O God my Saviour, overlook me not.</span><br /></span>
