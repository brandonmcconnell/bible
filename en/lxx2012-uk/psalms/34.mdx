<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:30; Luke 1:46; Psalms 148:1–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bring forth a sword, and stop the way against them that persecute me: say to my soul, I am your salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; Matthew 7:7; Psalms 116:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them that seek my soul be ashamed and confounded: let them that devise evils against me be turned back and put to shame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Psalms 123:1–2; Psalms 18:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be as dust before the wind, and an angel of the Lord afflicting them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 34:17–19; Psalms 3:4; Psalms 10:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their way be dark and slippery, and an angel of the Lord persecuting them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Daniel 6:22; Hebrews 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For without cause they have hid for me their destructive snare: without a cause they have reproached my soul.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 8:32; Philippians 4:19; Psalms 23:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my soul shall exult in the Lord: it shall delight in his salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Matthew 6:32; Psalms 104:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">All my bones shall say, O Lord, who is like to you? delivering the poor out of the hand of them that are stronger than he, yes, the poor and needy one from them that spoil him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 2:1–9; Psalms 111:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Unjust witnesses arose, and asked me of things I new not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:10–12; Deuteronomy 30:20; Deuteronomy 6:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They rewarded me evil for good, and bereavement to my soul.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 3:5–10; Psalms 141:3; Proverbs 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I, when they troubled me, put on sackcloth, and humbled my soul with fasting: and my prayer shall return to my own bosom.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 37:27; 1 Peter 3:11; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I behaved agreeably towards them as if it had been our neighbour or brother: I humbled myself as one mourning and sad of countenance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 34:17; Psalms 33:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet they rejoiced against me, and plagues were</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Gr. is repeated, by a Hebraism.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">plentifully brought against me, and I knew it not: they were scattered, but</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Acts 2:37.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">repented not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Jeremias 17:13; Jeremias 44:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">They tempted me, they sneered at me most contemptuously, they gnashed their teeth upon me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:18–20; Esias 65:24; Psalms 34:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, when will you look upon me? Deliver my soul from their mischief, my only-begotten one from the lions.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 147:3; Psalms 145:18; Esias 57:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give thanks to you even in a great congregation: in an abundant people I will praise you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:17; 1 Peter 4:12–13; Psalms 34:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let not them that are my enemies without a cause rejoice against me; who hate me for nothing, and wink with their eyes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:36; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 91:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For to me they spoke peaceably, but imagined deceits in their anger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 94:23; Proverbs 24:16; Esias 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they opened wide their mouth upon me; they said Aha, aha, our eyes have seen it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 103:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 31:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have seen it, O Lord: keep not silence: O Lord, withdraw not yourself from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Awake, O Lord, and attend to my judgement, even to my cause, my God and my Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Judge me, O Lord, according to your righteousness, O Lord my God; and let them not rejoice against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them not say in their hearts, Aha, aha, it is pleasing to our soul: neither let them say, We have devoured him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be confounded and ashamed together that rejoice at my afflictions: let them be clothed with shame and confusion that speak great swelling words against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them that rejoice in my righteousness exult and be glad: and let them say continually, The Lord be magnified, who desire the peace of his servant.</span><br /></span>
