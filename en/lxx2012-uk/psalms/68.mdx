<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles I 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their habitation be made desolate; and let there be no inhabitant in their tents:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Esias 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because they persecuted him whom you have struck; and they have added to the grief of my wounds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Add iniquity to their iniquity; and let them not come into your righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and let them not be written with the righteous.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Esias 60:6–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am poor and sorrowful; but the salvation of your countenance has helped me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremias 50:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will praise the name of my God with a song, I will magnify him with praise;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esias 45:14; Sophonias 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">and this shall please God more than a young calf having horns and hoofs.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the poor see and rejoice; seek the Lord diligently, and you⌃ shall live.</span><br /></span>
