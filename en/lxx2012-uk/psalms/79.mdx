<div className="hs">(80) For the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Ps 44, and 68, titles.">a</a></sup>end, for alternate strains, a testimony for Asaph, a Psalm concerning the Assyrian.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Michaeas 3:12; Chronicles II 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Attend, O Shepherd of Israel, who guide Joseph like a flock; you who sit upon the cherubs, manifest yourself;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 7:33; Jeremias 34:20; Jeremias 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasse, stir up your power, and come to deliver us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 16:4; Jeremias 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Turn us, O God, and cause your face to shine; and we shall be delivered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord God of hosts, how long are you angry with the prayer of your servant?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will feed us with bread of tears; and will cause us to drink tears by measure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made us a strife to our neighbours; and our enemies have mocked at us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 51:34–35; Zacharias 1:15; Esias 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and cause your face to shine; and we shall be saved. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have transplanted a vine out of Egypt: you have cast out the heathen, and planted it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 14:7; Psalms 25:11; Chronicles II 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made a way before it, and did cause its roots to strike, and the land was filled with it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Its shadow covered the mountains, and its shoots equalled the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. Cedars of God, see Gen. 30:8; Jonah 3:3; Acts 7:20.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">goodly cedars.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Esias 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">It sent forth its branches to the sea, and its shoots to the river.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore have you broken down its hedge, while all that pass by the way pluck it?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Esias 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The boar out of the wood has laid it waste, and the wild beast has devoured it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God of hosts, turn, we pray you: look on us from heaven, and behold and visit this vine;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">and restore that which your right hand has planted: and look on the son of man whom you did strengthen for yourself.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is burnt with fire and dug up: they shall perish at the rebuke of your</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. See Ps. 20:9.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">presence.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let your hand be upon the man of your right hand, and upon the son of man whom you did strengthen for yourself.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">So will we not depart from you: you shall quicken us, and we will call upon your name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and make your face to shine; and we shall be saved.</span></span>
