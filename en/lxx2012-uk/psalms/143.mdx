<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, what is man, that you are made known to him? or the son of man, that you take account of him?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 142:3; Psalms 77:3; Psalms 102:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Man is like to vanity: his days pass as a shadow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Michaeas 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, bow your heavens, and come down: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Send lightning, and you shall scatter them: send forth your arrows, and you shall discomfit them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 69:17; Psalms 28:1; Psalms 27:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Send forth your hand from on high; rescue me, and deliver me out of great</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. many.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">waters, out of the hand of strange children;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Esias 48:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, I will sing a new song to you: I will play to you on a lute of ten strings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even to him who gives</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, victory.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">salvation to kings: who redeems his servant David from the hurtful sword.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me, and rescue me from the hand of strange children, whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> <span className="pl pl-1">whose children are as plants, strengthened in their youth: their daughters are beautiful, sumptuously adorned after the similitude of a temple.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their garners are full, and bursting with one kind of store after another; their sheep are prolific, multiplying in their streets.</span><br /></span>
