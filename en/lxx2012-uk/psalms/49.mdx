<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The God of gods, the Lord, has spoken, and called the earth from the rising of the sun to the going down thereof.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremias 5:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Out of Sion comes the excellence of his beauty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, our God, shall come manifestly, and shall not keep silence: a fire shall be kindled before him, and round about him there shall be a very great tempest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He shall summon the heaven above, and the earth, that he may judge his people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Assemble you⌃ his saints to him, those that have engaged in a covenant with him upon sacrifices.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremias 9:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, my people, and I will speak to you, O Israel: and I will testify to you: I am God, your God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will not reprove you on account of your sacrifices; for your whole burnt offerings are before me continually.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will take no bullocks out of your house, nor he-goats out of your flocks.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For all the wild beasts of the thicket are mine, the cattle on the mountains, and oxen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know all the birds of the sky; and the beauty of the field is mine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I should be hungry, I will not tell you: for the world is mine, and the fullness of it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachias 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Offer to God the sacrifice of praise; and pay your vows to the Most High.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And call upon me in the day of affliction; and I will deliver you, and you shall glorify me. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But to the sinner God has said, Why do you declare my ordinances, and take up my covenant in your mouth?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whereas you have hated instruction, and have cast my words behind you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you saw a thief, you</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 Pe 4:4.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">ran along with him, and have cast in your lot with adulterers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your mouth has multiplied wickedness, and your tongue has framed deceit.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="From these words in Alex. to Ps. 79. 11, thirty psalms are lacking.">c</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You did sit and speak against your brother, and did scandalize your mother's son.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">These things you did, and I kept silence: you thought wickedly that I should be like you, but I will reprove you, and set your offences before you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now consider these things, you⌃ that forget God, lest he rend you, and there is no deliverer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sacrifice of praise will glorify me: and</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. there.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that is the way wherein I will show to him the salvation of God.</span></span>
