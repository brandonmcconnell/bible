<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have shed their blood as water, round about Jerusalem; and there was none to bury them.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; Kings I 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, O God our Saviour; for the glory of your name, O Lord, deliver us; and be merciful to our sins, for your name's sake.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lest haply they should say amongst the heathen, Where is their God? and let the avenging of your servant's blood that has been shed be known amongst the heathen before our eyes.</span><br /></span>
