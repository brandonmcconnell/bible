<div className="hs">(104) A Psalm of David.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 57:7–11; John 4:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, you are very great; you have clothed yourself with praise and honour:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 63:7; Psalms 116:12; Psalms 105:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">who do robe yourself with light as with a garment; spreading out the heaven as a curtain.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Esias 53:5; Jeremias 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who covers his chambers with waters; who makes the clouds his chariot; who walks on the wings of the wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Psalms 71:23; Psalms 103:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who makes his angels spirits, and his ministers a flaming fire.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 40:31; Psalms 107:9; 1 Timothy 6:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who establishes the earth on her sure foundation: it shall not be moved for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 12:5; Psalms 72:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The deep, as it were a garment, is his covering: the waters shall stand</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, above.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the hills.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:13; Psalms 147:19; Psalms 78:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">At your rebuke they shall flee; at the voice of your thunder they shall be alarmed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Joel 2:13; Exodus 34:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">They go up to the mountains, and down to the plains, to the place which you have founded for them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 57:16; Psalms 30:5; Michaeas 7:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have set a bound which they shall not pass, neither shall they turn again to cover the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Nehemiah 9:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">He sends forth his fountains amongst the valleys: the waters shall run between the mountains.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 57:10; Luke 1:50; Psalms 36:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall give drink to all the wild beasts of the field: the wild asses shall take of them to quench their thirst.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 43:25; 1 John 1:7; Michaeas 7:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">By them shall the birds of the sky lodge: they shall utter a voice out of the midst of the rocks.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:11–12; Hebrews 12:5–11; Luke 15:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">He waters the mountains from his chambers: the earth shall be satisfied with the fruit of your works.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 10:9; Psalms 78:38–39"> <span className="pl pl-1">He makes grass to grow for the cattle, and green herb for the service of men, to bring bread out of the earth;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; James 1:10–11; Esias 51:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">and wine makes glad the heart of man, to make his face cheerful with oil: and bread strengthens man's heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 40:7; Job 20:9; Job 14:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The trees of the plain shall be full of sap; even the cedars of Libanus which he has planted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Exodus 20:6; Ephesians 2:4–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">There the sparrows will build their nests; and the house of the heron takes the lead amongst them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 132:12; Luke 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The high mountains are a refuge for the stags, and the rock for the rabbits.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Philippians 2:9–10; Psalms 9:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knows his going down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 148:2; Luke 1:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You did make darkness, and it was night; in it all the wild beasts of the forest will be abroad:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 22:19; Nehemiah 9:6; Luke 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">even young lions roaring for prey, and to seek meat for themselves from God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esias 49:13; Psalms 145:10; Esias 42:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun arises, and they shall be gathered together, and shall lie down in their dens.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Man shall go forth to his work, and to his labour till evening.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">How great are your works, O Lord! in wisdom have you wrought them all: the earth is filled with your creation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">So is this great and wide sea: there are things creeping innumerable, small animals and great.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">There go the ships; and this dragon whom you have made to play in it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">All wait upon you, to give them their food in due season.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you have given it them, they will gather it; and when you have opened your hand, they shall all be filled with good.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="pl pl-1">But when you have turned away your face, they shall be troubled: you will take away their breath, and they shall fail, and return to their dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> <span className="pl pl-1">You shall send forth your Spirit, and they shall be created; and you shall renew the face of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the glory of the Lord be for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> <span className="pl pl-1">who looks upon the earth, and makes it tremble; who touches the mountains, and they smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will sing to the Lord while I live; I will sing praise to my God while I exist.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let my meditation be sweet to him: and I will rejoice in the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the sinners fail from off the earth, and transgressors, so that they shall be no more. Bless the Lord, O my soul.</span></span>
</div>
