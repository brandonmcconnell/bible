<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You did establish the sea, in your might, you did break to pieces the heads of the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, serpents.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">dragons in the water.</span><br /></span>
