<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are more beautiful than the sons of men: grace has been shed forth on your lips: therefore God has blessed you for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Gird your sword upon your thigh, O Mighty One, in your comeliness, and in your beauty;</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:13; Psalms 60:12; Psalms 118:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your weapons are sharpened, Mighty One, (the nations shall fall under you) they are in the heart of the king's enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Osee 1:7; Kings I 17:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your throne, O God, is for ever and ever: the sceptre of your kingdom is a sceptre of righteousness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 136:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have loved righteousness, and hated iniquity: therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness beyond your fellows.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremias 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Myrrh, and stacte, and cassia are exhaled from your garments, and out of the ivory palaces,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 60:10; Psalms 60:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">with which kings' daughters have gladdened you for your honour: the queen stood by on your right hand, clothed in vesture wrought with gold, and arrayed in various colours.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, O daughter, and see, and incline your ear; forget also your people, and your father's house.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because the king has desired your beauty; for he is your Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Esias 52:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the daughter of Tyre shall adore him with gifts; the rich of the people of the land shall supplicate your</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. face.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">favour.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Psalms 80:6; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">All her glory is that of the daughter of the king</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. ἔσωθεν, within, so Heb.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of Esebon, robed as she is in golden fringed garments,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 24:9; Kings IV 19:21; Job 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">in embroidered clothing: virgins shall be brought to the king after her: her fellows shall be brought to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 69:7; Jeremias 3:25; Psalms 89:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall be brought with gladness and exultation: they shall be led into the king's temple.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Psalms 74:10; Psalms 74:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead of your fathers children are born to you: you shall make them princes over all the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall make mention of your name from generation to generation: therefore shall the nations give thanks to you</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. to the age of the age.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for ever, even for ever and ever.</span></span>
