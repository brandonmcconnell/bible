<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <span className="pl pl-1">My hands formed a musical instrument, and my fingers tuned a lute.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And who shall tell my Lord? the Lord himself, he himself hears.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He sent forth his angel, and took me from my father's sheep, and he anointed me with the oil of his anointing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">My brothers were handsome and tall; but the Lord did not take pleasure in them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I went forth to meet the Philistine; and he cursed me by his idols.</span><br /></span>
