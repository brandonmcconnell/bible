<div className="hs">(44) For the end, a Psalm for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. understanding.">a</a></sup>instruction, for the sons of Core.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 35:1; Psalms 26:1; Psalms 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, we have heard with our ears, our fathers have told us, the work which you wrought in their days, in the days of old.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 28:7; Ephesians 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your hand utterly destroyed the heathen, and you did plant them: you did afflict the nations, and cast them out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 36:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; John 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">For they inherited not the land by their own sword, and their own arm did not deliver them; but your right hand, and your arm, and the light of your countenance, because you were well pleased in them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 61:10; Ambacum 3:17–18; Psalms 66:13–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are indeed my King and my God, who command deliverances for Jacob.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 42:5; Psalms 42:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">In you will we push down our enemies, and in your name will we bring to nothing them that rise up against us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will not trust in my bow, and my sword shall not save me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you have saved us from them that afflicted us, and have put to shame them that hated us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">In God</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. will be praised etc. See Ps. 106.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will we make our boast all the day, and to your name will we give thanks for ever. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now you have cast off, and put us to shame; and you will not go forth with our hosts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have turned us back before our enemies; and they that hated us spoiled for themselves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made us as sheep for meat; and you scattered us amongst the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have sold your people without price, and there was no profit by their exchange.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made us a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and derision them that are round about us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made us a proverb amongst the Gentiles, a shaking of the head amongst the nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the day my shame is before me, and the confusion of my face has covered me,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">because of the voice of the slanderer and reviler; because of the enemy and avenger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">All these things are come upon us: but we have not forgotten you, neither have we dealt unrighteously in your covenant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">And our heart has not gone back; but you have turned aside our paths from your way.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you have laid us low in a place of affliction, and the shadow of death has covered us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have spread out our hands to a strange god; shall not God search these things out?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">for he knows the secrets of the heart.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">For, for your sake we are killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for slaughter.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Awake, therefore sleep you, O Lord? arise, and do not cast us off for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore turn you your face away, and forget our poverty and our affliction?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">For our soul has been brought down to the dust; our belly has cleaved to the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for your name's sake.</span></span>
