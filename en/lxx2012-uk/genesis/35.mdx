<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:14; Exodus 19:10; Jezekiel 18:31"> And Jacob said to his house, and to all that were with him, Remove the strange gods that are with you from the midst of you, and purify yourselves, and change your clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 43:2; Genesis 32:7; Genesis 31:3"> And let us rise and go up to Baethel, and let us there make an altar to God who listened to me in the day of calamity, who was with me, and preserved me throughout in the journey, by which I went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Deuteronomy 7:25; Exodus 32:20"> And they gave to Jacob the strange gods, which were in their hands, and the ear-rings which were in their ears, and Jacob hid them under the turpentine tree which is in Secima, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, lost.">a</a></sup>destroyed them to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Chronicles II 17:10; Joshua 5:1"> So Israel departed from Secima, and the fear of God was upon the cities round about them, and they did not pursue after the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 28:22; Genesis 12:8"> And Jacob came to Luza, which is in the land of Chanaan, which is Baethel, he and all the people that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:13"> And he built there an altar, and called the name of the place Baethel; for there God appeared to him, when he fled from the face of his brother Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:59; Kings I 31:13; Judges 2:5"> And Deborrha, Rebecca's nurse, died, and was buried below Baethel under the oak; and Jacob called its name, The Oak of Mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 35:1; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 26:2"> And God appeared to Jacob once more in Luza, when he came out of Mesopotamia of Syria, and God blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:5; Genesis 32:27–28; Genesis 17:15"> And God said to him, Your name shall not be called Jacob, but Israel shall be your name; and he called his name Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 22:17"> And God said to him, I am your God; increase and multiply; for nations and gatherings of nations shall be of you, and kings shall come out of your loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:3–4"> And the land which I gave to Abraam and Isaac, I have given it to you; and it shall come to pass that I will give this land also to your seed after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Genesis 18:33; Judges 6:21"> And God went up from him from the place where he spoke with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–19; Kings I 7:12; Exodus 17:15"> And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where God spoke with him, even a pillar of stone; and offered a libation upon it, and poured oil upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 28:19"> And Jacob called the name of the place in which God spoke with him, Baethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Michaeas 5:2; Genesis 48:7; Kings IV 5:19"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Note. — The words between brackets form the 21st verse of this chapter in the Hebrew.">b</a></sup>&#91;And Jacob removed from Baethel, and pitched his tent beyond the tower of Gader,&#93; and it came to pass when he drew near to Chabratha, to enter into Ephratha, Rachel travailed; and in her travail she was in hard labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:24; Kings I 4:19–21"> And it came to pass in her hard labour, that the midwife said to her, Be of good courage, for you shall also have this son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Acts 7:59; Luke 23:46"> And it came to pass in her giving up the ghost (for she was dying), that she called his name, The son of my pain; but his father called his name Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Michaeas 5:2; Genesis 48:7; Ruth 4:11"> So Rachel died, and was buried in the way of the course of Ephratha, this is Bethleem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 10:2; Genesis 35:14; Kings II 18:17–18"> And Jacob set up a pillar on her tomb; this is the pillar on the tomb of Rachel, until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Michaeas 4:8; Luke 2:8"> And it came to pass when Israel lived in that land, that Ruben went and lay with Balla, the concubine of his father Jacob; and Israel heard, and the thing appeared grievous before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:1; Leviticus 18:8; Kings II 20:3"> And the sons of Jacob were twelve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 30:18–20; Genesis 46:8–15; Genesis 29:32–35"> The sons of Lea, the firstborn of Jacob; Ruben, Symeon, Levi, Judas, Issachar, Zabulon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 35:16–18"> And the sons of Rachel; Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 46:23–25; Genesis 37:2; Genesis 30:4–8"> And the sons of Balla, the handmaid of Rachel; Dan and Nephthalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 30:9–13; Genesis 46:16–18; Genesis 25:20"> And the sons of Zelpha, the handmaid of Lea; Gad and Aser. These are the sons of Jacob, which were born to him in Mesopotamia of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 18:1"> And Jacob came to Isaac his father to Mambre, to a city of the plain; this is Chebron in the land of Chanaan, where Abraam and Isaac sojourned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 25:7; Genesis 50:26; Genesis 47:28"> And the days of Isaac which he lived were one hundred and eighty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 49:33; Genesis 49:31"> And Isaac gave up the ghost and died, and was laid to his family, old and full of days; and Esau and Jacob his sons buried him.</span>
