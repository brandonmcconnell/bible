<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; James 2:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And it came to pass after these things that God tempted Abraam, and said to him, Abraam, Abraam; and he said, Behold! I am here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:32; Hebrews 11:17"> And he said, Take your son, the beloved one, whom you have loved—Isaac, and go into the high land, and offer him there for a whole burnt offering on one of the mountains which I will tell you of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26"> And Abraam rose up in the morning and saddled his ass, and he took with him two servants, and Isaac his son, and having split wood for a whole burnt offering, he arose and departed, and came to the place of which God spoke to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:32; Matthew 17:23; Osee 6:2"> on the third day; and Abraam having<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. look up with.">a</a></sup>lifted up his eyes, saw the place afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:19; Hebrews 12:1"> And Abraam said to his servants, Sit you⌃ here with the ass, and I and the lad will proceed thus far, and having worshipped we will return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:17; 1 Peter 2:24; Esias 53:6"> And Abraam took the wood of the whole burnt offering, and laid it on Isaac his son, and he took into his hands both the fire and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="μάχαιραν, a short dagger used both for defence and sacrifice, etc.">b</a></sup>knife, and the two went together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; Matthew 26:39; Genesis 8:20"> And Isaac said to Abraam his father, Father. And he said, What is it, son? And he said, Behold the fire and the wood, where is the sheep for a whole burnt offering?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:36; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> And Abraam said, God will provide himself a sheep for a whole burnt offering, my son. And both having gone together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 2:21; Hebrews 11:17–19; Philippians 2:7–8"> came to the place which God spoke of to him; and there Abraam built the altar, and laid the wood on it, and having bound the feet of Isaac his son together, he laid him on the altar upon the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:21–23; Hebrews 11:17–19; Esias 53:6–12"> And Abraam stretched forth his hand to take the knife to kill his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 21:17; Exodus 3:4; Genesis 16:9–10"> And an angel of the Lord called him out of heaven, and said, Abraam, Abraam. And he said, Behold, I am here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:18; Kings I 15:22"> And he said, Lay not your hand upon the child, neither do anything to him, for now I know that you fear God, and for my sake you have not spared your beloved son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Psalms 40:6–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And Abraam lifted up his eyes and saw, and behold! a ram caught by his horns in a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Heb. in a thicket.">c</a></sup>plant of Sabec; and Abraam went and took the ram, and offered him up for a whole burnt offering in the place of Isaac his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:13; Exodus 17:15; Genesis 22:8"> And Abraam called the name of that place, The Lord has seen; that they might say today, In the mount the Lord was seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And an angel of the Lord called Abraam the second time out of heaven, saying, I have sworn by myself, says the Lord, because you have done this thing, and on my account have not spared your beloved son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–14; Luke 1:73; Psalms 105:9"> surely blessing I will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and as the sand which is by the shore of the sea, and your seed shall inherit the cities of their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 24:60; Jeremias 33:22"> And in your seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed, because you have listened to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16"> And Abraam returned to his servants, and they arose and went together to the well of the oath; and Abraam lived at the well of the oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Judges 20:1; Joshua 15:28"> And it came to pass after these things, that it was reported to Abraam,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Men, understood.">d</a></sup>saying, Behold, Melcha herself too has born sons to Nachor your brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:24; Proverbs 25:25"> Uz the firstborn, and Baux his brother, and Camuel the father of the Syrians, and Chazad, and</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Job 32:2; Psalms 60:1–12"> Azav and Phaldes, and Jeldaph, and Bathuel, and Bathuel begot Rebecca;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> these are eight sons, which Melcha bore to Nachor the brother of Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 28:5; Genesis 24:51"> And his concubine whose name was Rheuma, she also bore Tabec, and Taam, and Tochos, and Mocha.</span>
