<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Genesis 3:15; Genesis 4:25"> And Adam knew Eve his wife, and she conceived and brought forth Cain and said, I have gained a man through God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 3:15; 1 John 3:12; Luke 11:51"> And she again bore his brother Abel. And Abel was a keeper of sheep, but Cain was a tiller of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 18:12; Leviticus 2:1–11; Kings III 17:7"> And it was so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. after days.">a</a></sup>after some time that Cain brought of the fruits of the earth a sacrifice to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Proverbs 3:9; Exodus 13:12"> And Abel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. he also.">b</a></sup>also brought of the first born of his sheep and of his fatlings, and God looked upon Abel and his gifts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Job 5:2; Luke 15:28–30"> but Cain and his sacrifices he regarded not, and Cain was exceedingly sorrowful and his countenance fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 1:18; Job 5:2; Luke 15:31–32"> And the Lord God said to Cain, Why are you become very sorrowful and why is your countenance fallen?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:15; Romans 2:6–10; Esias 3:10–11"> Have you not sinned if you have brought it rightly, but not rightly divided it? be still, to you shall be his submission, and you shall rule over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 23:35; 1 John 3:12–15; Jude 1:11"> And Cain said to Abel his brother, Let us go out into the plain; and it came to pass that when they were in the plain Cain rose up against Abel his brother, and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; John 8:44; Psalms 9:12"> And the Lord God said to Cain, Where is Abel your brother? and he said, I know not, am I my brother's keeper?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Psalms 72:14; Hebrews 11:4"> And the Lord said, What have you done? the voice of your brother's blood cries to me out of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Job 16:18; Genesis 3:14"> And now you are cursed from the earth which has opened her mouth to receive your brother's blood from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Osee 9:17; Leviticus 26:36; Leviticus 26:20"> When you till the earth, then it shall not continue to give its strength to you: you shall be groaning and trembling on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:22; Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:11"> And Cain said to the Lord God, My crime is too great for me to be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Psalms 143:7; Job 15:20–24"> If you cast me out this day from the face of the earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, then shall I be.">c</a></sup>and I shall be hidden from your presence, and I shall be groaning and trembling upon the earth, then it will be that any one that finds me shall kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 9:4; Psalms 79:12; Genesis 4:24"> And the Lord God said to him, Not so, any one that slays Cain shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. pay seven penalties.">d</a></sup>suffer sevenfold vengeance; and the Lord God set a mark upon Cain that no one that found him might kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 23:39; Job 2:7; Genesis 4:14"> So Cain went forth from the presence of God and lived in the land of Nod over against Edem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; Ecclesiastes 2:4–11; Daniel 4:30"> And Cain knew his wife, and having conceived she bore Enoch; and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, was building.">e</a></sup>built a city; and he named the city after the name of his son, Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> And to Enoch was born Gaidad; and Gaidad begot Maleleel; and Maleleel begot Mathusala; and Mathusala begot Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:8; Genesis 2:18"> And Lamech took to himself two wives; the name of the one was Ada, and the name of the second Sella.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Jeremias 35:9–10; John 8:44"> And Ada bore Jobel; he was the father of those that dwell in tents, feeding cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 21:12; Esias 5:12; Genesis 31:27"> And the name of his brother was Jubal; he it was who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. made known.">f</a></sup>invented the lute and harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 8:9; Chronicles II 2:7"> And Sella<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. she also.">g</a></sup>also bore Thobel; he was a smith, a manufacturer both of brass and iron; and the sister of Thobel was Noema.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 23:18; Exodus 20:13; Leviticus 19:18"> And Lamech said to his wives, Ada and Sella, Hear my voice, you⌃ wives of Lamech, consider my words, because I have slain a man to my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. wound.">h</a></sup>sorrow and a youth to my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. hurt.">i</a></sup>grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:22; Genesis 4:15"> Because vengeance has been exacted seven times on Cain's behalf, on Lamech's it shall be seventy times seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Genesis 4:10–11; Chronicles I 1:1"> And Adam knew Eve his wife, and she conceived and bore a son, and called his name Seth, saying, For God has raised up to me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; Kings III 18:24; Joel 2:32"> And Seth had a son, and he called his name Enos: he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, trusted, q.d. had faith to call &amp;c.">j</a></sup>hoped to call on the name of the Lord God.</span>
