<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Colossians 3:10; Genesis 1:26–27"> This is the genealogy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. book of generation.">a</a></sup>of men in the day in which God made Adam; in the image of God he made him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Mark 10:6; Genesis 2:23"> male and female he made them, and blessed them; and he called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. their.">b</a></sup>his name Adam, in the day in which he made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalms 51:5"> And Adam lived two hundred and thirty years, and begot a son after his own form, and after his own image, and he called his name Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> And the days of Adam, which he lived after his begetting Seth, were seven hundred years; and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:8; Genesis 5:11; Genesis 3:19"> And all the days of Adam which he lived were nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> Now Seth lived two hundred and five years, and begot Enos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Seth lived after his begetting Enos, seven hundred and seven years, and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; Chronicles I 1:2"> And Enos lived one hundred and ninety years, and begot Cainan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Enos lived after his begetting Cainan, seven hundred and fifteen years, and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Cainan lived one hundred and seventy years, and he begot Maleleel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Cainan lived after his begetting Maleleel, seven hundred and forty years, and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 1:2"> And Maleleel lived one hundred and sixty and five years, and he begot Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Maleleel lived after his begetting Jared, seven hundred and thirty years, and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And all the days of Maleleel were eight hundred and ninety and five years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Chronicles I 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> And Jared lived one hundred and sixty and two years, and begot Enoch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and Jared lived after his begetting Enoch, eight hundred years, and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty and two years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> And Enoch lived one hundred and sixty and five years, and became the father of Mathusala.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> And Enoch was well-pleasing to God after his begetting Mathusala, two hundred years, and he begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty and five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 11:5–6; 1 John 1:7; Genesis 5:22"> And Enoch was well-pleasing to God, and was not found, because God translated him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> And Mathusala lived<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Alex 187 years.">c</a></sup>one hundred and sixty and seven years, and begot Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Mathusala lived after his begetting Lamech<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex 782.">d</a></sup>eight hundred and two years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And all the days of Mathusala which he lived, were nine hundred and sixty and nine years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Lamech lived one hundred and eighty and eight years, and begot a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> And he called his name Noe, saying, This one will cause us to cease from our works, and from the toils of our hands, and from the earth, which the Lord God has cursed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Lamech lived after his begetting Noe, five hundred and sixty and five years, and begot sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And all the days of Lamech were seven hundred and fifty-three years, and he died.</span>
