<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> And there was a famine in the land, besides the former famine, which was in the time of Abraam; and Isaac went to Abimelech the king of the Phylistines to Gerara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> And the Lord appeared to him and said, Go not down to Egypt, but dwell in the land, which I shall tell you of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> And sojourn in this land; and I will be with you, and bless you, for I will give to you and to your seed all this land; and I will establish my oath which I swore to your father Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> And I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven; and I will give to your seed all this land, and all the nations of the earth shall be blest in your seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> Because Abraam your father listened to my voice, and kept my injunctions, and my commandments, and my ordinances, and my statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> And Isaac lived in Gerara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> And the men of the place questioned him concerning Rebecca his wife, and he said, She is my sister, for he feared to say, She is my wife, lest at any time the men of the place should kill him because of Rebecca, because she was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. fair of countenance.">a</a></sup>fair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> And he remained there a long time, and Abimelech the king of Gerara leaned to look through the window, and saw Isaac sporting with Rebecca his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Abimelech called Isaac, and said to him, Is she then your wife? why have you said, She is my sister? And Isaac said to him, I did so, for I said, Lest at any time I die on her account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> And Abimelech said to him, Why have you done this to us? one of my kindred<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="q.d. had almost.">b</a></sup>within a little had lain with your wife, and you would have brought a sin of ignorance upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> And Abimelech charged all his people, saying Every man that touches this man and his wife shall be liable to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> And Isaac sowed in that land, and he found in that year barley one hundred-fold, and the Lord blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> And the man was exalted, and advancing he increased, till he became very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> And he had cattle of sheep, and cattle of oxen, and many tilled lands, and the Phylistines envied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> And all the wells which the servants of his father had dug in the time of his father, the Phylistines stopped them, and filled them with earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> And Abimelech said to Isaac, Depart from us, for you are become much mightier than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Isaac departed thence, and rested in the valley of Gerara, and lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zacharias 13:2; Osee 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> And Isaac dug again the wells of water, which the servants of his father Abraam had dug, and the Phylistines had stopped them, after the death of his father Abraam; and he gave them names, according to the names by which his father named them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 4:10–11; John 7:38; Song of Songs 4:15"> And the servants of Isaac dug in the valley of Gerara, and they found there a well of living water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> And the shepherds of Gerara strove with the shepherds of Isaac, saying that the water was theirs; and they called the name of the well, Injury, for they injured him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> And having departed thence he dug another well, and they strove also for that; and he named the name of it, Enmity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> And he departed thence and dug another well; and they did not strive about that; and he named the name of it, Room, saying, Because now the Lord has made room for us, and has increased us upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> And he went up thence to the well of the oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Esias 43:1–2"> And the Lord appeared to him in that night, and said, I am the God of Abraam your father; fear not, for I am with you, and I will bless you, and multiply your seed for the sake of Abraam your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> And he built there an altar, and called on the name of the Lord, and there he pitched his tent, and there the servants of Isaac dug a well in the valley of Gerara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> And Abimelech came to him from Gerara, and so did Ochozath his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="q.d. he that gives away in marriage.">c</a></sup>friend, and Phichol the commander-in-chief of his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> And Isaac said to them, Therefore have you⌃ come to me? whereas you⌃ hated me, and sent me away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Esias 45:14; Esias 61:9"> And they said, We have surely seen that the Lord was with you, and we said, Let there be an oath between us and you, and we will make a covenant with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> that you shall do no wrong by us, as we have not abhorred you, and according as we have treated you well, and have sent you forth peaceably; and now you are blessed of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> And he made a feast for them, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings I 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> And they arose in the morning, and swore each to his neighbour; and Isaac sent them forth, and they departed from him in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> And it came to pass in that day, that the servants of Isaac came and told him of the well which they had dug; and they said, We have not found water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> And he called it, Oath: therefore he called the name of that city, the Well of Oath, until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> And Esau was forty years old; and he took as wife Judith the daughter of Beoch the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. Hivite.">d</a></sup>Chettite, and Basemath, daughter of Helon the Chettite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> And they were provoking to Isaac and Rebecca.</span>
