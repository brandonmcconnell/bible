<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Exodus 31:17; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And God finished on the sixth day his works which he made, and he ceased on the seventh day from all his works which he made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:12; Mark 2:27; Leviticus 23:3"> And God blessed the seventh day and sanctified it, because in it he ceased from all his works which God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, made in the beginning. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> This is the book of the generation of heaven and earth, when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, it took place.">c</a></sup>they were made, in the day in which the Lord God made the heaven and the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> and every herb of the field before it was on the earth, and all the grass of the field before it sprang up, for God had not rained on the earth, and there was not a man to cultivate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But there rose a fountain out of the earth, and watered the whole face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> And God formed the man of dust of the earth, and breathed upon his face the breath of life, and the man became a living soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> And God planted a garden eastward in Edem, and placed there the man whom he had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> And God made to spring up also out of the earth every tree beautiful<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. for sight.">d</a></sup>to the eye and good for food, and the tree of life in the midst of the garden, and the tree of learning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, that which is to be known. Comp. Rom 1:19.">e</a></sup>the knowledge of good and evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalms 46:4"> And a river proceeds out of Edem to water the garden, thence it divides itself into four heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; Kings I 15:7"> The name of the one, Phisom, this it is which encircles the whole land of Evilat, where there is gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Jezekiel 28:13"> And the gold of that land is good, there also is carbuncle and emerald.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Esias 11:11"> And the name of the second river is Geon, this it is which encircles the whole land of Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> And the third river is Tigris, this is that which flows forth over against the Assyrians. And the fourth river is Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> And the Lord God took the man whom he had formed, and placed him in the garden of Delight, to cultivate and keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And the Lord God gave a charge to Adam, saying, Of every tree which is in the garden you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, eat for food.">f</a></sup>may freely eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> but of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil—of it you⌃ shall not eat, but in whatever day you⌃ eat of it, you⌃ shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, die by death.">g</a></sup>surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone, let us make for him a help<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. according to him.">h</a></sup>suitable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> And God formed yet farther out of the earth all the wild beasts of the field, and all the birds of the sky, and he brought them to Adam, to see what he would call them, and whatever Adam called any living creature,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. soul.">i</a></sup>that was the name of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> And Adam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. called.">j</a></sup>gave names to all the cattle and to all the birds of the sky, and to all the wild beasts of the field, but for Adam there was not found a help like to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; Kings I 26:12"> And God brought a trance upon Adam, and he slept, and he took one of his ribs, and filled up the flesh instead thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> And God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. built.">k</a></sup>formed the rib which he took from Adam into a woman, and brought her to Adam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Kings II 5:1"> And Adam said, This now is bone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, out of. See the force of ἐκ in Eph 5:30.">l</a></sup>of my bones, and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or, wife.">m</a></sup>woman, because she was taken out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="In the Heb. the reason of the name appears. She shall be called Issha because she was taken out of Ish.">n</a></sup>of her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother and shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. be cemented. Mat 19:5.">o</a></sup>cleave to his wife, and they two shall be one flesh.</span>
