<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Rebecca heard Isaac speaking to Esau his son; and Esau went to the plain to procure venison for his father.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:15; Kings I 16:20"> And go to the cattle and take for me thence two kids, tender and good, and I will make them meats for your father, as he likes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And you shall bring them in to your father, and he shall eat, that your father may bless you before he dies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Genesis 25:27; Malachias 1:14"> Peradventure my father may feel me, and I shall be before him as one ill-intentioned, and I shall bring upon me a curse, and not a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Kings I 25:24; Kings II 14:9"> And his mother said to him, On me be your curse, son; only listen to my voice, and go and bring them me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:9; Proverbs 23:2–3; Genesis 25:28"> So he went and took and brought them to his mother; and his mother made meats, as his father liked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 27:27; Luke 20:46; Luke 15:22"> And Rebecca having taken the fine raiment of her elder son Esau which was with her in the house, put it on Jacob her younger son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she put on his arms the skins of the kids, and on the bare parts of his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she gave the meats, and the loaves which she had prepared, into the hands of Jacob her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he brought them to his father, and said, Father; and he said, Behold I am here; who are you, son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zacharias 13:3–4"> And Jacob said to his father, I, Esau your firstborn, have done as you told me; rise, sit, and eat of my venison, that your soul may bless me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> And Isaac said to his son, What is this which you have quickly found? And he said, That which the Lord your God presented before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:8; Esias 57:19; Genesis 27:12"> And Isaac said to Jacob, Draw near to me, and I will feel you, son, if you are my son Esau or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Jacob drew near to his father Isaac, and he felt him, and said, The voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are the hands of Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> And he knew him not, for his hands were as the hands of his brother Esau, hairy; and he blessed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22"> and he said, Are you my son Esau? and he said, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> And he said, Bring hither, and I will eat of your venison, son, that my soul may bless you; and he brought it near to him, and he ate, and he brought him wine, and he drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Isaac his father said to him, Draw near to me, and kiss me, son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 65:10; Hebrews 11:20"> And he drew near and kissed him, and smelled the smell of his garments, and blessed him, and said, Behold, the smell of my son is as the smell of an abundant field, which the Lord has blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 33:13; Psalms 133:3"> And may God give you of the dew of heaven, and of the fatness of the earth, and abundance of corn and wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Esias 49:23"> And let nations serve you, and princes bow down to you, and be you lord of your brother, and the sons of your father shall do you reverence; accursed is he that curses you, and blessed is he that blesses you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass after Isaac had ceased blessing his son Jacob, it even came to pass, just when Jacob had gone out from the presence of Isaac his father, that Esau his brother came in from his hunting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> And he also had made meats and brought them to his father; and he said to his father, Let my father arise and eat of his son's venison, that your soul may bless me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> And Isaac his father said to him, Who are you? And he said, I am your firstborn son Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Romans 11:29; Ephesians 1:3"> And Isaac was amazed with very great amazement, and said, Who then is it that has procured venison for me and brought it to me? and I have eaten of all before you came, and I have blessed him, and he shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Proverbs 1:31; Kings I 30:4"> And it came to pass when Esau heard the words of his father Isaac, he cried out with a great and very bitter cry, and said, Bless, I pray you, me also, father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Job 13:7; Malachias 2:10"> And he said to him, Your brother has come with subtlety, and taken your blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; John 1:47; Genesis 32:28"> And he said, Rightly was his name called Jacob, for behold! this second time has he supplanted me; he has both taken my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing; and Esau said to his father, Have you not left a blessing for me, father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings II 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> And Isaac answered and said to Esau, If I have made him your lord, and have made all his brethren his servants, and have strengthened him with corn and wine, what then shall I do for you, son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Esias 65:14"> And Esau said to his father, Have you only one blessing, father? Bless, I pray you, me also, father. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. — Isaac being troubled.">a</a></sup>Isaac being troubled, Esau cried aloud and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Hebrews 11:20; Joshua 24:4"> And Isaac his father answered and said to him, Behold, your dwelling shall be of the fatness of the earth, and of the dew of heaven from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Kings IV 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; Chronicles II 25:11–12"> And you shall live by your sword, and shall serve your brother; and there shall be a time when you shall break and loosen his yoke from off your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 John 3:12–15; Genesis 37:4; Genesis 50:3–4"> And Esau was angry with Jacob because of the blessing, with which his father blessed him; and Esau said in his mind, Let the days of my father's mourning draw near, that I may kill my brother Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–20; Kings I 30:5; Genesis 42:21–22"> And the words of Esau her elder son were reported to Rebecca, and she sent and called Jacob her younger son, and said to him, Behold, Esau your brother threatens you to kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> Now then, my son, hear my voice, and rise and depart quickly into Mesopotamia to Laban my brother into Charran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:38; Genesis 31:41"> And dwell with him certain days, until your brother's anger</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Genesis 4:8–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> and rage depart from you, and he forget what you have done to him; and I will send and fetch you thence, lest at any time I should be bereaved of you both in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> And Rebecca said to Isaac, I am weary of my life, because of the daughters of the sons of Chet; if Jacob shall take a wife of the daughters of this land, therefore should I live?</span>
