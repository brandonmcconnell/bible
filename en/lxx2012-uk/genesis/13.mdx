<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:3; Esias 51:3; Genesis 14:8"> And Lot having lifted up his eyes, observed all the country round about Jordan, that it was all watered, before God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrha, as the garden of the Lord, and as the land of Egypt, until you come to Zogora.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> And Lot chose for himself all the country round Jordan, and Lot went from the east, and they were separated each from his brother. And Abram lived in the land of Chanaan.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 49:18; Deuteronomy 3:27; Genesis 28:14"> And God said to Abram after Lot was separated from him, Look up with your eyes, and behold from the place where you now are northward and southward, and eastward and seaward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> for all the land which you see, I will give it to you and to your seed for ever.</span>
