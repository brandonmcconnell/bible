<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Esias 40:14; Job 33:13"> Nay, gird up now your loins like a man; and I will ask you, and do you answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Do not set aside my judgement: and do you think that I have dealt with you in any other way, than that you might appear to be righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> Have you an arm like the Lord's? or do you thunder with a voice like his?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> Assume now a lofty bearing and power; and clothe yourself with glory and honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; Psalms 50:3–4"> And send forth messengers with wrath; and lay low every haughty one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Bring down also the proud man; and consume at once the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Esias 28:18; Job 32:2"> And hide them together in the earth; and fill their faces with shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Esias 45:9"> Then will I confess that your right hand can save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Psalms 149:4; Esias 59:17"> But now look at the wild beasts with you; they eat grass like oxen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Esias 2:11–12; Esias 2:17"> Behold now, his strength is in his loins, and his force is in the navel of his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 63:3; Malachias 4:3; Psalms 60:12"> He sets up his tail like a cypress; and his nerves are wrapped together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> His sides are sides of brass; and his backbone is as cast iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> This is the chief of the creation of the Lord; made to be played with by his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> And when he has gone up to a steep mountain, he causes joy to the quadrupeds in the deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He lies under trees of every kind, by the papyrus, and reed, and bulrush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> And the great trees make a shadow over him with their branches, and so do the bushes of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 48:4; Job 7:12"> If there should be a flood, he will not perceive it; he trust that Jordan will rush up into his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Esias 27:1"> Yet one shall take him in his sight; one shall catch him with a cord, and pierce his nose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:26; Psalms 104:14; Job 40:15"> But will you catch the serpent with a hook, and put a halter about his nose?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 35:7; Esias 19:6–7"> Or will you fasten a ring in his nostril, and bore his lip with a clasp?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Esias 15:7"> Will he address you with a petition? softly, with the voice of a suppliant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Esias 37:25; Esias 28:16"> And will he make a covenant with you? and will you take him for a perpetual servant?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 41:1–2"> And will you play with him as with a bird? or bind him as a sparrow for a child?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And do the nations feed upon him, and the nations of the Phoenicians share him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And all the ships come together would not be able to bear the mere skin of his tail; neither shall they carry his head in fishing vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But you shall lay your hand upon him once, remembering the war that is waged by his mouth; and let it not be done any more.</span>
