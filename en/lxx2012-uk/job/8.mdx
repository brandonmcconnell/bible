<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> Though then your beginning should be small, yet your end should be unspeakably great.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> Thus then shall be the end of all that forget the Lord: for the hope of the ungodly shall perish.</span>
