<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:11; Jezekiel 14:14; Jezekiel 14:20"> There was a certain man in the land of Ausis, whose name was Job; and that man was true, blameless, righteous, and godly, abstaining from everything evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Job 42:13; Psalms 128:3"> And he had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 29:25; Job 42:12; Judges 6:3"> And his cattle consisted of seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen, five hundred she-asses in the pastures, and a very great household, and he had a great husbandry on the earth; and that man was most noble of the men of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Psalms 133:1"> And his sons visiting one another prepared a banquet every day, taking with them also their three sisters to eat and drink with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 42:8; Genesis 8:20; Kings I 16:5"> And when the days of the banquet were completed, Job sent and purified them, having risen up in the morning, and offered sacrifices for them, according to their number, and one calf for a sin-offering for their souls: for Job said, Lest perhaps my sons have thought evil in their minds against God. Thus, then Job did continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 38:7; Zacharias 3:1"> And it came to pass on a day, that behold, the angels of God came to stand before the Lord, and the devil came with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:12–17; Matthew 12:43"> And the Lord said to the devil, Whence are you come? And the devil answered the Lord, and said, I am come from compassing the earth, and walking up and down in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:3; Job 1:1; Numbers 12:7–8"> And the Lord said to him, Have you diligently considered my servant Job, that there is none like him on the earth, a man blameless, true, godly, abstaining from everything evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 4:8; Job 2:10"> Then the devil answered, and said before the Lord, Does Job worship the Lord for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Zacharias 2:5; 1 Peter 1:5"> Have you not made a hedge about him, and about his household, and all his possessions round about? and have you not blessed the works of his hands, and multiplied his cattle upon the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 19:21; Revelation 16:11"> But put forth your hand, and touch all that he has: verily he will bless you to your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:31–32; John 3:35–36; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> Then the Lord said to the devil, Behold, I give into your hand all that he has, but touch not himself. So the devil went out from the presence of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Proverbs 27:1; Job 1:4"> And it came to pass on a certain day, that Job's sons and his daughters were drinking wine in the house of their elder brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 15:13; Jeremias 51:31; Kings I 4:17"> And, behold, there came a messenger to Job, and said to him, The yokes of oxen were ploughing, and the she-asses were feeding near them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Job 6:19; Esias 45:14"> and the spoilers came and took them for a prey, and killed the servants with the sword; and I having escaped alone am come to tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 1:12; Genesis 19:24; Kings III 18:38"> While he was yet speaking, there came another messenger, and said to Job, Fire has fallen from heaven, and burnt up the sheep, and devoured the shepherds like wise; and I having escaped alone am come to tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 11:28; Ambacum 1:6; Genesis 11:31"> While he was yet speaking, there came another messenger, and said to Job, The horsemen formed three companies against us, and surrounded the camels, and took them for a prey, and killed the servants with the sword; and I only escaped, and am come to tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 1:4; Job 1:13; Job 27:14"> While he is yet speaking, another messenger comes, saying to Job, While your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking with their elder brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:36; Luke 13:1–5; Judges 16:30"> suddenly a great wind came on from the desert, and caught the four corners of the house, and the house fell upon your children, and they are dead; and I have escaped alone, and am come to tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; Kings II 12:16–20; Genesis 37:34"> So Job arose, and tore his garments, and shaved the hair of his head, and fell on the earth, and worshipped,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:15; 1 Timothy 6:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> and said, I myself came forth naked from my mother's womb, and naked shall I return there; the Lord gave, the Lord has taken away: as it seemed good to the Lord, so has it come to pass; blessed be the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 2:10; 1 Peter 1:7"> In all these events that befell him Job sinned not at all before the Lord, and did not impute folly to God.</span>
