<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Ambacum 2:13; Matthew 6:33"> He has set a bound to darkness, and he searches out every limit: a stone is darkness, and the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> There is a cutting off the torrent by reason of dust: so they that forget the right way are weakened; they are removed from amongst men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 28:25–29; Psalms 104:14–15; Genesis 1:11–12"> As for the earth, out of it shall come bread: under it has been turned up as it were fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:16; Exodus 24:10; Esias 54:11"> Her stones are the place of the sapphire: and her dust supplies man with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:6; Job 28:21–23; Job 38:24"> There is a path, the fowl has not known it, neither has the eye of the vulture seen it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> neither have the sons of the proud trodden it, a lion has not passed upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Naum 1:4–6"> He has stretched forth his hand on the sharp rock, and turned up mountains by the roots:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ambacum 3:9; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 24:4"> and he has interrupted the whirlpools of rivers, and my eye has seen every precious thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Esias 45:2–3; Esias 44:27"> And he has laid bare the depths of rivers, and has brought his power to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–6; Job 28:20; James 1:5"> But whence has wisdom been discovered? and what is the place of knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 8:18–19; Proverbs 3:14–15"> A mortal has not known its way, neither indeed has it been revealed amongst men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 11:33–34"> The depth said, It is not in me: and the sea said, It is not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:10–11; Proverbs 8:19"> One shall not give fine gold instead of it, neither shall silver be weighed in exchange for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 13:12; Jezekiel 28:13; Psalms 45:9"> Neither shall it be compared with gold of Sophir, with the precious onyx and sapphire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 21:11"> Gold and crystal shall not be equalled to it, neither shall vessels of gold be its exchange.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:15; Proverbs 31:10; Lamentations 4:7"> Coral and fine pearl shall not be mentioned: but do you esteem wisdom above the most precious things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 8:19; Exodus 39:10; Jezekiel 28:13"> The topaz of Ethiopia shall not be equalled to it; it shall not be compared with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 28:12; James 1:5; James 1:17"> Whence then is wisdom found? and of what kind is the place of understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 49:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:7–10; Matthew 11:25"> It has escaped the notice of every man, and has been hidden from the birds of the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 83:10–12; Job 26:6; Job 28:14"> Destruction and Death said, We have heard the report of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 8:22–31; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Jude 1:25"> God has well ordered the way of it, and he knows the place of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Zacharias 4:10; Chronicles II 16:9"> For he surveys the whole earth under heaven, knowing the things in the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Esias 40:12; Job 12:15"> all that he has made; the weight of the winds, the measures of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 38:25; Job 37:3; Zacharias 10:1"> When he made them, thus he saw and numbered them, and made a way for the pealing of the thunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Proverbs 8:22–29"> Then he saw it, and declared it: he prepared it and traced it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 9:10"> And he said to man, Behold, godliness is wisdom: and to abstain from evil is understanding.</span>
