<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 29:1; Numbers 24:15; Numbers 23:7"> And Job further continued and said in his parable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 34:5; Numbers 14:21; Kings IV 4:27"> As God lives, who has thus judge me; and the Almighty, who has embittered my soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Job 33:4; Job 32:8"> verily, while my breath is yet in me, and the breath of God which remains to me is in my nostrils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:7; 2 Corinthians 11:10; John 8:55"> my lips shall not speak evil words, neither shall my soul meditate unrighteous thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:9; Job 13:15; Job 42:7"> Far be it from me that I should justify you till I die; for I will not let go my innocence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:3; 1 John 3:20–21; Acts 24:16"> but keeping fast to my righteousness I will by no means let it go: for I am not conscious to myself of having done any thing amiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 4:19; Kings I 25:26; Kings II 18:32"> Nay rather, but let my enemies be as the overthrow of the ungodly, and they that rise up against me, as the destruction of transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 8:13; Job 11:20"> For what is the hope of the ungodly, that he holds to it? will he indeed trust in the Lord and be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 1:15; Proverbs 1:28; Jeremias 14:12"> Will God hear his prayer? or, when distress has come upon him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 22:26–27; Psalms 37:4; Ambacum 3:18"> has he any confidence before him? or will God hear him as he calls upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:17; Esias 8:11; Job 4:3–4"> Yet now I will tell you what is in the hand of the Lord: I will not lie concerning the things which are with the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Behold, you⌃ all know that you⌃ are adding vanity to vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:19–29; Job 15:20–35; Esias 3:11"> This is the portion of an ungodly man from the Lord, and the possession of oppressors shall come upon them from the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 20:10; Luke 23:29"> And if their children be many, they shall be for slaughter: and if they grow up, they shall beg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremias 22:18; Kings III 14:10–11"> And they that survive of him shall utterly perish, and no one shall pity their widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zacharias 9:3; Matthew 6:19; Ambacum 2:6"> Even if he should gather silver as earth, and prepare gold as clay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26; Proverbs 28:8"> All these things shall the righteous gain, and the true-hearted shall possess his wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Esias 51:8"> And his house is gone like moths, and like a spider's web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 58:9; Psalms 73:19–20"> The rich man shall lie down, and shall not continue: he has opened his eyes, and he is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:21; Job 18:11; Job 21:18"> Pains have come upon him as water, and darkness has carried him away by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 83:15; Psalms 58:9"> And a burning wind shall catch him, and he shall depart, and it shall utterly drive him out of his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–5; Jezekiel 24:14; Job 11:20"> And God shall cast trouble upon him, and not spare: he would fain flee out of his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Lamentations 2:15; Kings III 9:8; Sophonias 2:15"> He shall cause men to clap their hands against them, and shall hiss him out of his place.</span>
