<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> I know of a truth that it is so: for how shall a mortal man be just before the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Esias 57:15–16"> For if he would enter into judgement with him, God would not listen to him, so that he should answer to one of his charges of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> For he is wise in mind, and mighty, and great: who has hardened himself against him and endured?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Ambacum 3:10"> Who wears out the mountains, and men know it not: who overturns them in anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Aggaeus 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> Who shakes the earth under heaven from its foundations, and its pillars totter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> Who commands the sun, and it rises not; and he seals up the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 44:24; Esias 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> Who alone has stretched out the heavens, and walks on the sea as on firm ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> Who makes Pleias, and Hesperus, and Arcturus, and the chambers of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> Who does great and unsearchable things; glorious also and excellent things, innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> If ever he should go beyond me, I shall not see him: if he should pass by me, neither thus have I known it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> If he would take away, who shall turn him back? or who shall say to him, What have you done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Esias 30:7"> For if he has turned away his anger, the whales under heaven have stooped under him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> Oh then that he would listen to me, or judge my cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> For though I be righteous, he will not listen to me: I will entreat his judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> And if I should call and he should not listen, I can’t believe that he has listened to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> Let him not crush me with a dark storm: but he has made by bruises many without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> For he suffers me not to take breath, but he has filled me with bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> For indeed he is strong in power: who then shall resist his judgement?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> For though I should seem righteous, my mouth will be profane: and though I should seem blameless, I shall be proved perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> For even if I have sinned, I know it not in my soul: but my life is taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> Therefore I said, Wrath slays the great and mighty man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> For the worthless die, but the righteous are laughed to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremias 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> For they are delivered into the hands of the unrighteous man: he covers the faces of the judges of the earth: but if it be not he, who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> But my life is swifter than a post: my days have fled away, and they knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ambacum 1:8; Esias 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> Or again, is there a trace of their path left by ships? or is there one of the flying eagle as it seeks its prey?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremias 8:18; Job 7:11"> And if I should say, I will forget to speak, I will bow down my face and groan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> I quake in all my limbs, for I know that you will not leave me alone as innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> But since I am ungodly, why have I not died?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> For if I should wash myself with snow, and purge myself with pure hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esias 64:6; Esias 59:6; Job 15:6"> you had thoroughly plunged me in filth, and my garment had abhorred me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For you are not man like me, with whom I could contend, that we might come together to judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings I 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> Would that he our mediator were present, and a reprover, and one who should hear the cause between both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let him remove his rod from me, and let not his fear terrify me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> so shall I not be afraid, but I will speak: for I am not thus conscious of guilt.</span>
