<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> What is this that you think to be according to right? who are you that you have said, I am righteous before the Lord?</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> Look up to the sky and see; and consider the clouds, how high they are above you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Esias 55:9; Naum 1:3"> If you have sinned, what will you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> and if too you have transgressed much, what can you perform?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> And suppose you are righteous, what will you give him? or what shall he receive of your hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Your ungodliness may affect a man who is like to you; or your righteousness a son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> They that are oppressed of a multitude will be ready to cry out; they will call for help because of the arm of many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> But none said, Where is God that made me, who appoints the night-watches;</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There they shall cry, and none shall listen, even because of the insolence of wicked men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Esias 1:15; James 4:3"> For the Lord desires not to look on error, for he is the Almighty One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Michaeas 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> He beholds them that perform lawless deeds, and he will save me: and do you plead before him, if you can praise him, as it is possible even now.</span>
