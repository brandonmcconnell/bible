<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elius continued, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Hear me, you⌃ wise men; listen, you⌃ that have knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 12:11; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Job 33:2"> For the ear tries words, and the mouth tastes meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Judges 19:30; Romans 12:2"> Let us choose judgement to ourselves: let us know amount ourselves what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 33:9; Job 16:17"> For Job has said, I am righteous: the Lord has removed my judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:4; Job 27:4–6; Job 16:13"> And he has erred in my judgement: my wound is severe without unrighteousness of mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 15:16; Deuteronomy 29:19; Proverbs 1:22"> What man is as Job, drinking scorning like water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20"> saying, I have not sinned, nor committed ungodliness, nor had fellowship with workers of iniquity, to go with the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:14–16; Job 35:3; Malachias 3:14"> For you should not say, There shall be no visitation of a man, whereas there is a visitation on him from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:14; Deuteronomy 32:4; Job 8:3"> Therefore hear me, you⌃ that are wise in heart: far be it from me to sin before the Lord, and to pervert righteousness before the almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:6; Revelation 22:12"> Yes, he renders to a man accordingly as each of them does, and in a man's path he will find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:3; Psalms 11:7; Psalms 145:17"> And think you that the Lord will do wrong, or will the Almighty who made the earth wrest judgement?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:11; Esias 40:13–14; Romans 11:34–36"> And who is he that made the whole world under heaven, and all things therein?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Job 7:17; Job 9:4"> For if he would confine, and restrain his spirit with himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Esias 57:16"> all flesh would die together, and every mortal would return to the earth, whence also he was formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:2–6; Job 12:3"> Take heed lest he rebuke you: hear this, listen to the voice of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 40:8; Genesis 18:25; Kings II 23:3"> Behold then the one that hates iniquities, and that destroys the wicked, who is for ever just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; Proverbs 17:26; Romans 13:7"> He is ungodly that says to a king, You are a transgressor, that says to princes, O most ungodly one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; James 2:5"> Such a one as would not reverence the face of an honourable man, neither knows how to give honour to the great, so as that their persons should be respected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; Kings I 25:37–39; Kings I 26:10"> But it shall turn out vanity to them, to cry and beseech a man; for they dealt unlawfully, the poor being turned aside from their right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Job 31:4; Jeremias 16:17"> For he surveys the works of men, and nothing of what they do has escaped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Amos 9:2–3; Proverbs 10:29"> Neither shall there be a place for the workers of iniquity to hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Psalms 119:137; Romans 9:20"> For he will not lay upon a man more than right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Psalms 94:5; Daniel 2:21"> For the Lord looks down upon all men, who comprehends unsearchable things, glorious also and excellent things without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Osee 7:2; Job 34:20; Amos 8:7"> Who discovers their works, and will bring night about upon them, and they shall be brought low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 21:21; Esias 66:24"> And he quite destroys the ungodly, for they are seen before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 28:5; Kings I 15:11; Esias 5:12"> Because they turned aside from the law of God, and did not regard his ordinances,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 5:4; Exodus 3:7; Job 35:9"> so as to bring before him the cry of the needy; for he will hear the cry of the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 23:13; John 14:27; Psalms 27:9"> And he will give quiet, and who will condemn? and he will hide his face, and who shall see him? whether it be done against a nation, or against a man also:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2–12; Job 34:21; Kings IV 21:9"> causing a hypocrite to be king, because of the waywardness of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Michaeas 7:9; Job 33:27; Job 42:6"> For there is one that says to the Mighty One, I have received blessings; I will not take a pledge:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:4–5; Psalms 139:23–24"> I will see apart from myself: do you show me if I have done unrighteousness; I will not do so any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 33:32; Esias 45:9; Psalms 135:6"> Will he take vengeance for it on you, whereas you will put it far from you? for you shall choose, and not I; and what you know, speak you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:15; Job 34:4; Job 34:16"> Because the wise in heart shall say this, and a wise man listens to my word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 35:16; Job 38:2; Job 15:2"> But Job has not spoken with understanding, his words are not uttered with knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Job 21:7; Job 23:16; Job 12:6"> Howbeit do you learn, Job: no longer make answer as the foolish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 27:23; Esias 1:19–20; Kings I 15:23"> that we add not to our sins: for iniquity will be reckoned against us, if we speak many words before the Lord.</span>
