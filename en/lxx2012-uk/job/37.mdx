<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:2–4; Jeremias 5:22; Exodus 19:16"> At this also my heart is troubled, and moved out of its place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 37:5; Job 36:33; Psalms 104:7"> Hear you a report by the anger of the Lord's wrath, and a discourse shall come out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 38:13; Matthew 24:27; Esias 11:12"> His dominion is under the whole heaven, and his light is at the extremities of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 29:3–9; Psalms 68:33; Exodus 15:7–8"> After him shall be a cry with a loud voice; he shall thunder with the voice of his excellency, yet he shall not cause men to pass away, for one shall hear his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 5:9; Romans 11:33; Esias 40:28"> The Mighty One shall thunder wonderfully with his voice: for he has done great things which we knew not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:22; Job 36:27; Jezekiel 13:13"> commanding the snow, Be you upon the earth, and the stormy rain, and the storm of the showers of his might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 109:27; Esias 26:11"> He seals up the hand of every man, that every man may know his own weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:22; Job 38:40"> And the wild beasts come in under the covert, and rest in their lair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:9; Psalms 104:3; Esias 21:1"> Troubles come on out of the secret chambers, and cold from the mountain-tops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 38:29–30; Psalms 147:16–18; Psalms 78:47"> And from the breath of the Mighty One he will send frost; and he guides the water in whatever way he pleases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 36:27–30; Matthew 17:5; Esias 18:4"> And if a cloud obscures what is precious to him, his light will disperse the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 148:8; Joel 2:23; James 5:17–18"> And he will carry round the encircling clouds by his governance, to perform their works: whatever he shall command them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 18:45; Job 38:26–27; Kings I 12:18–19"> this has been appointed by him on the earth, whether for correction, or for his land, or if he shall find him an object for mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Psalms 145:10–12; Exodus 14:13"> Listen to this, O Job: stand still, and be admonished of the power of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:30–32; Esias 40:26; Job 37:11"> We know that god has disposed his works, having made light out of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:4; Proverbs 3:19–20; Esias 40:22"> And he knows the divisions of the clouds, and the signal overthrows of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 147:18; Job 6:17; Job 38:31"> But your robe is warm, and there is quiet upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 44:24; Psalms 104:2; Esias 40:22"> Will you establish with him foundations for the ancient heavens? they are strong as a molten mirror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 38:2; Psalms 73:22; Job 42:3"> Therefore teach me, what shall we say to him? and let us cease from saying much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 11:7–8; Psalms 139:4; Job 6:3"> Have I a book or a scribe my me, that I may stand and put man to silence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:32; Job 26:9; Job 38:25"> But the light is not visible to all: it shines afar off in the heavens, as that which is from him in the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 104:1; Jude 1:25; Hebrews 12:29"> From the north come the clouds shining like gold: in these great are the glory and honour of the Almighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:16; Romans 11:33; Psalms 99:4"> and we do not find another his equal in strength: as for him that judges justly, do you not think that he listens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Corinthians 1:26; Psalms 130:4"> Therefore men shall fear him; and the wise also in heart shall fear him.</span>
