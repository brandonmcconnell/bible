<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 15:15; Job 15:8–10; Job 4:18"> But call, if any one will listen to you, or if you shall see any of the holy angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Osee 7:11; Genesis 30:1"> For wrath destroys the foolish one, and envy slays him that has gone astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:18–20; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 92:7"> And I have seen foolish ones taking root: but suddenly their habitation was devoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:5; Job 18:16–19; Psalms 119:155"> Let their children be far from safety, and let them be crushed at the doors of vile men, and let there be no deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Osee 8:7; Jeremias 51:44"> For what they have collected, the just shall eat; but they shall not be delivered out of calamities: let their strength be utterly exhausted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; Kings I 6:9"> For labour can’t by any means come out of the earth, nor shall trouble spring out of the mountains:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> yet man is born to labour, and even so the vulture's young seek the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Job 22:21; Psalms 37:5"> Nevertheless I will beseech the Lord, and will call upon the Lord, the sovereign of all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Job 9:10; Psalms 86:10"> who does great things and untraceable, glorious things also, and marvellous, of which there is no number:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 5:24; Acts 14:17; Psalms 147:8"> who gives rain upon the earth, sending water on the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 113:7; Kings I 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> who exalts the lowly, and raises up them that are lost:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:10–11; Psalms 21:11; Esias 8:10"> frustrating the counsels of the crafty, and their hands shall not perform the truth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; Psalms 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> who takes the wise in their wisdom, and subverts the counsel of the crafty</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Esias 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> In the day darkness shall come upon them, and let them grope in the noon-day even as in the night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 140:12; Psalms 109:31"> and let them perish in war, and let the weak escape from the hand of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 107:42; Kings I 2:8–9; Psalms 63:11"> And let the weak have hope, but the mouth of the unjust be stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But blessed is the man whom the Lord has reproved; and reject not you the chastening of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Osee 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Esias 30:26"> for he causes a man to be in pain, and restores him again: he smites, and his hands heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> Six time he shall deliver you out of distresses: and in the seventh harm shall not touch you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Osee 13:14; Psalms 37:19"> In famine he shall deliver you from death: and in war he shall free you from the power of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:20; Psalms 91:5–7; Jeremias 18:18"> He shall hide you from the scourge of the tongue: and you shall not be afraid of coming evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:25; Esias 35:9; Psalms 91:13"> You shall laugh at the unrighteous and the lawless: and you shall not be afraid of wild beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Osee 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> For the wild beasts of the field shall be at peace with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 121:7–8; Psalms 91:10; Esias 4:5–6"> Then shall you know that your house shall be at peace, and the provision for your tabernacle shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> And you shall know that your seed shall be abundant; and your children shall be like the herbage of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; Psalms 91:16"> And you shall come to the grave like ripe corn reaped in its season, or as a heap of the corn-flour collected in proper time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> Behold, we have thus sought out these matters; these are what we have heard: but do you reflect with yourself, if you have done anything wrong.</span>
