<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Elius further continued, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Wait for me yet a little while, that I may teach you: for there is yet speech in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Having fetched my knowledge from afar, and according to my works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> I will speak just things truly, and you shall not unjustly receive unjust words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremias 10:12"> But know that the Lord will not cast off an innocent man: being mighty in strength of wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> he will not by any means save alive the ungodly: and he will grant the judgement of the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; Kings I 2:8"> He will not turn away his eyes from the righteous, but they shall be with kings on the throne: and he will establish them in triumph, and they shall be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> But they that are bound in fetters shall be holden in cords of poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> And he shall recount to them their works, and their transgressions, for such will act with violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Esias 55:6–7"> But he will listen to the righteous: and he has said that they shall turn from unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 7:23; Esias 1:19; Jeremias 26:13"> If they should hear and serve him, they shall spend their days in prosperity, and their years in honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Esias 3:11"> But he preserves not the ungodly; because they are not willing to know the Lord, and because when reproved they were disobedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> And the hypocrites in heart will array wrath against themselves; they will not cry, because he has bound them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> Therefore let their soul die in youth, and their life be wounded by messengers of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; Chronicles II 12:8"> Because they afflicted the weak and helpless: and he will vindicate the judgement of the meek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> And he has also enticed you out of the mouth of the enemy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> there is a deep gulf and a rushing stream beneath it, and your table came down full of fatness. Judgement shall not fail from the righteous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> but there shall be wrath upon the ungodly, by reason of the ungodliness of the bribes which they received for iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Let not your mind willingly turn you aside from the petition of the feeble that are in distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> And draw not forth all the mighty men by night, so that the people should go up instead of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> But take heed lest you do that which is wrong: for of this you have made choice because of poverty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Behold, the Mighty One shall prevail by his strength: for who is powerful as he is?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Esias 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> And who is he that examines his works? or who can say, he has wrought injustice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Remember that his works are great beyond those which men have attempted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Every man has seen in himself, how many mortals are wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Behold, the Mighty One is great, and we shall not know him: the number of his years is even infinite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> And the drops of rain are numbered by him, and shall be poured out in rain to form a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> The ancient heavens shall flow, and the clouds overshadow innumerable mortals: he has fixed a time to cattle, and they know the order of rest. Yet by all these things your understanding is not astonished, neither is your mind disturbed in your body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> And though one should understand the outspreadings of the clouds, or the measure of his tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> behold he will stretch his bow against him, and he covers the bottom of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> For by them he will judge the nations: he will give food to him that has strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> He has hidden the light in his hands, and given charge concerning it to the interposing cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; Kings II 22:14"> The Lord will declare concerning this to his friend: but there is a portion also for unrighteousness.</span>
