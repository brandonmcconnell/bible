<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> Then Eliphaz the Thaemanite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Osee 12:1"> Will a wise man give for answer a mere breath of wisdom? and does he fill up the pain of his belly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> reasoning with improper sayings, and with words wherein is no profit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Have not you moreover cast off fear, and accomplished such words before the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> You are guilty by the words of your mouth, neither have you discerned the words of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Let your own mouth, and not me, reprove you: and your lips shall testify against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> What! are you the first man that was born? or were you established before the hills?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremias 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Or have you heard the ordinance of the Lord? or has God used you as his counsellor? and has wisdom come only to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> For what know you, that, we know not? or what understand you, which we do not also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> Truly amongst us are both the old and very aged man, more advanced in days than your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; Kings III 22:24"> You have been scourged for but few of your sins: you have spoken haughtily and extravagantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> What has your heart dared? or what have your eyes aimed at,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> that you have vented your rage before the Lord, and delivered such words from your mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> For who, being a mortal, is such that he shall be blameless? or, who that is born of a woman, that he should be just?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Forasmuch as he trusts not his saints; and the heaven is not pure before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> Alas then, abominable and unclean is man, drinking unrighteousness as a draught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> But I will tell you, listen to me; I will tell you now what I have seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Esias 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> things wise men say, and their fathers have not hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> To them alone the earth was given, and no stranger came upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> All the life of the ungodly is spent in care, and the years granted to the oppressor are numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> And his terror is in his ears: just when he seems to be at peace, his overthrow will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> Let him not trust that he shall return from darkness, for he has been already made over to the power of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> And he has been appointed to be food for vultures; and he knows within himself that he is doomed to be a carcass: and a dark day shall carry him away as with a whirlwind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Distress also and anguish shall come upon him: he shall fall as a captain in the first rank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> For he has lifted his hands against the Lord, and he has hardened his neck against the Almighty Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; Chronicles II 32:13–17"> And he has run against him with insolence, on the thickness of the back of his shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremias 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> For he has covered his face with his fat, and made layers of fat upon his thighs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremias 51:37; Michaeas 7:18"> And let him lodge in desolate cities, and enter into houses without inhabitant: and what they have prepared, others shall carry away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> Neither shall he at all grow rich, nor shall his substance remain: he shall not cast a shadow upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> Neither shall he in any wise escape the darkness: let the wind blast his blossom, and let his flower fall off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esias 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> Let him not think that he shall endure; for his end shall be vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Osee 9:16"> His harvest shall perish before the time, and his branch shall not flourish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Esias 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> And let him be gathered as the unripe grape before the time, and let him fall as the blossom of the olive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> For death is the witness of an ungodly man, and fire shall burn the houses of them that receive gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Osee 10:13; Esias 59:4–5"> And he shall conceive sorrows, and his end shall be vanity, and his belly shall bear deceit.</span>
