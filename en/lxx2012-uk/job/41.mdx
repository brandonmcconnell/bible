<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 74:14; Psalms 104:26; Job 3:8"> Have you not seen him? and have you not wondered at the things said of him? Do you not fear because preparation has been made by me? for who is there that resists me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 37:29; Jezekiel 29:4–5; Kings IV 19:28"> Or who will resist me, and abide, since the whole world under heaven is mine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; Psalms 55:21"> I will not be silent because of him: though because of his power one shall pity his antagonist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; Psalms 8:5–6"> Who will open the face of his garment? and who can enter within the fold of his breastplate?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> Who will open the doors of his face? terror is round about his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> His inwards are as brazen plates, and the texture of his skin as a smyrite stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> One part cleaves fast to another, and the air can’t come between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; Kings IV 10:4"> They will remain united each to the other: they are closely joined, and can’t be separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 28:19; Kings I 3:11; Luke 21:11"> At his sneezing a light shines, and his eyes are as the appearance of the morning star.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> Out of his mouth proceed as it were burning lamps, and as it were hearths of fire are cast abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> Out of his nostrils proceeds smoke of a furnace burning with fire of coals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> His breath is as live coals, and a flame goes out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> And power is lodged in his neck, before him destruction runs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> The flesh also of his body is joined together: if one pours violence upon him, he shall not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremias 9:23"> His heart is firm as a stone, and it stands like an unyielding anvil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when he turns, he is a terror to the four-footed wild beasts which leap upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If spears should come against him, men will effect nothing, either with the spear or the breast-plate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> For he considers iron as chaff, and brass as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> The bow of brass shall not wound him, he deems a slinger as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 1:13–14"> Mauls are counted as stubble; and he laughs to scorn the waving of the firebrand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Esias 30:33; Ambacum 3:5"> His lair is formed of sharp points; and all the gold of the sea under him is an immense quantity of clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Osee 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> He makes the deep boil like a brazen cauldron; and he regards the sea as a pot of ointment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and the lowest part of the deep as a captive: he reckons the deep as his range.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 5:3; Esias 48:4; Zacharias 7:12"> There is nothing upon the earth like to him, formed to be sported with by my angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonas 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> He beholds every high thing: and he is king of all that are in the waters.</span>
