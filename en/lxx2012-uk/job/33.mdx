<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 13:6; Mark 4:9; Psalms 49:1–3"> Howbeit hear, Job, my words, and listen to my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:1; Matthew 5:2; Psalms 78:2"> For behold, I have opened my mouth, and my tongue has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 27:4; Proverbs 20:15; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–4"> My heart shall be found pure by my words; and the understanding of my lips shall meditate purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:8; Genesis 2:7; Job 27:3"> The Divine Spirit is that which formed me, and the breath of the Almighty that which teaches me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:32–33; Job 32:1; Psalms 50:21"> If you can, give me an answer: wait therefore; stand against me, and I will stand against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 4:19; Job 10:9; Job 13:12"> You are formed out of the clay as also I: we have been formed out of the same substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:34; Psalms 32:4"> My fear shall not terrify you, neither shall my hand be heavy upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Jeremias 29:23"> But you have said in my ears, (I have heard the voice of your words;) because you say, I am pure, not having sinned;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 16:17; Job 9:21"> I am blameless, for I have not transgressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:11; Job 13:24–25; Job 9:30–31"> Yet he has revealed a charge against me, and he has reckoned me as an adversary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 31:4; Daniel 4:35"> And he has put my foot in the stocks, and has watched all my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:25; Job 36:5; Job 1:22"> For how say you, I am righteous, yet he has not listened to me? for he that is above mortals is eternal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 40:2; Esias 45:9; Romans 11:34"> But you say, Why has he not heard every word of my cause?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24; Chronicles II 33:10; Job 33:29"> For when the Lord speaks once, or a second time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Job 4:13; Genesis 20:3"> sending a dream, or in the meditation of the night; (as when a dreadful alarm happens to fall upon men, in slumberings on the bed:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:10; Job 36:15; Psalms 40:6"> then opens he the understanding of men: he scares them with such fearful visions:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 2:11; James 4:10; Genesis 20:6"> to turn a man from unrighteousness, and he delivers his body from a fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:30; Job 33:28; 2 Peter 3:9"> He spares also his soul from death, and suffers him not to fall in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 30:17; Job 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> And again, he chastens him with sickness on his bed, and the multitude of his bones is benumbed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 107:17–18; Job 3:24; Genesis 3:6"> And he shall not be able to take any food, though his soul shall desire meat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 19:20; Job 13:28; Proverbs 5:11"> until his flesh shall be consumed, and he shall show his bones bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:28; Kings II 24:16; Job 17:13–16"> His soul also draws near to death, and his life is in Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Malachias 3:1; Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> Though there should be a thousand messengers of death, not one of them shall wound him: if he should purpose in his heart to turn to the Lord, and declare to man his fault, and show his folly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> he will support him, that he should not perish, and will restore his body as fresh plaster upon a wall; and he will fill his bones with morrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 103:5; Kings IV 5:14; Joshua 14:10–11"> And he will make his flesh tender as that of a babe, and he will restore him amongst men in his full strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 30:5; Acts 9:11"> And he shall pray to the Lord, and his prayer shall be accepted of him; he shall enter with a cheerful countenance, with a full expression of praise: for he will render to men their due.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 6:21; Kings II 12:13; Psalms 14:2"> Even then a man shall blame himself, saying, What kind of things have I done? and he has not punished me according to the full amount of my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:24; Job 3:16; Job 33:22"> Deliver my soul, that it may not go to destruction, and my life shall see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:11; 1 Corinthians 12:6; Philippians 2:13"> Behold, all these things, the Mighty One works in a threefold manner with a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Esias 38:17; Job 33:28"> And he has delivered my soul from death, that my life may praise him in the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 18:2; Job 13:6; Job 21:2"> Listen, Job, and hear me: be silent, and I will speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:27; Job 15:4–5; Job 22:5–9"> If you have words, answer me: speak, for I desire you to be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 5:1–2; Psalms 49:3"> If not, do you hear me: be silent, and I will teach you.</span>
