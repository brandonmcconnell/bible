<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zacharias 14:5; Chronicles II 26:1–23; Kings III 19:19"> The words of Amos which came to him in Accarim out of Thecue, which he saw concerning Jerusalem, in the days of Ozias king of Juda, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joas king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 3:16; Jeremias 25:30; Amos 9:3"> And he said, The Lord has spoken out of Sion, and has uttered his voice out of Jerusalem; and the pastures of the shepherds have mourned, and the top of Carmel is dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 8:12; Amos 1:9; Esias 8:4"> And the Lord said, For three sins of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn away from it; because they sawed with iron saws the women with child of the Galaadites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 49:27; Kings IV 6:24; Kings IV 13:25"> And I will send a fire on the house of Azael, and it shall devour the foundations of the son of Ader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 16:9; Amos 9:7; Jeremias 51:30"> And I will break to pieces the bars of Damascus, and will destroy the inhabitants out of the plain of On, and will cut in pieces a tribe out of the men of Charrhan: and the famous people of Syria shall be led captive, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Chronicles II 28:18; Obdias 1:11"> Thus says the Lord; For three sins of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn away from them; because they took prisoners the captivity of Solomon, to shut them up into Idumea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 18:8; Jeremias 47:1; Sophonias 2:4"> And I will send forth a fire on the walls of Gaza, and it shall devour its foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:16; Esias 14:29–31; Jeremias 47:4–5"> And I will destroy the inhabitants out of Azotus, and a tribe shall be cut off from Ascalon, and I will stretch out my hand upon Accaron: and the remnant of the Philistines shall perish, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 9:11–14; Esias 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8"> Thus says the Lord; For three transgressions of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn away from it; because they shut up the prisoners of Solomon into Idumea, and remembered not the covenant of brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 9:4; Amos 1:7; Amos 1:4"> And I will send forth a fire on the walls of Tyre, and it shall devour the foundations of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:12–14; Chronicles II 28:17; Malachias 1:4"> Thus says the Lord; For three sins of Idumea, and for four, I will not turn away from them; because they pursued their brother with the sword, and destroyed the mother upon the earth, and summoned up his anger for a testimony, and kept up his fury to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 49:7; Obdias 1:9–10; Jeremias 49:20"> And I will send forth a fire upon Thaman, and it shall devour the foundations of her walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 13:16; Jezekiel 25:2–7; Jeremias 49:1–6"> Thus says the Lord; For three sins of the children of Ammon, and for four, I will not turn away from him; because they ripped up the women with child of the Galaadites, that they might widen their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 49:2; Amos 2:2; Deuteronomy 3:11"> And I will kindle a fire on the walls of Rabbath, and it shall devour her foundations with shouting in the day of war, and she shall be shaken in the days of her destruction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 49:3"> and her kings shall go into captivity, their priests and their rulers together, says the Lord.</span>
