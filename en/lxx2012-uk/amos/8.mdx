<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Amos 7:7; Amos 7:4"> And behold a fowler's basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Lamentations 4:18; Jeremias 24:1–3"> And he said, What see you, Amos? And I said, A fowler's basket. And the Lord said to me, The end is come upon my people Israel; I will not pass by them any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:23; Amos 6:9–10; Jeremias 9:21–22"> And the ceilings of the temple shall howl in that day, says the Lord God: there shall be many a fallen one in every place; I will bring silence upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 14:4; Amos 5:11"> Hear now this, you⌃ that oppress the poor in the morning, and drive the needy ones by tyranny from the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Michaeas 6:10–11; Osee 12:7"> saying, When will the month pass away, and we shall sell, and the sabbath, and we shall open the treasure, to make the measure small, and to enlarge the weight, and make the balance unfair?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:6; Leviticus 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> That we may buy the poor for silver, and the needy for shoes; and we will trade in every kind of fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Osee 9:9; Osee 8:13"> The Lord swears against the pride of Jacob, None of your works shall ever be forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:5; Osee 4:3; Jeremias 46:8"> And shall not the land be troubled for these things, and shall not every one who dwells in it mourn? whereas destruction shall come up as a river, and shall descend as the river of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45; Jeremias 15:9"> And it shall come to pass in that day, says the Lord God, that the sun shall go down at noon, and the light shall be darkened on the earth by day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 6:26; Zacharias 12:10; Job 20:23"> and I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation; and I will bring up sackcloth on all loins, and baldness on every head; and I will make them as the mourning of a beloved friend, and those with them as a day of grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 74:9; Kings I 3:1; Chronicles II 15:3"> Behold, the days come, says the Lord, that I will send forth a famine on the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but a famine of hearing the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:7–10; Matthew 24:23–26; Daniel 12:4"> And the waters shall be troubled from sea to sea, and from the north to the east shall men run hither and there, seeking the word of the Lord, and they shall not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 2:3; Lamentations 1:18; Lamentations 2:21"> In that day shall the fair virgins and the young men faint for thirst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:5; Kings III 12:28–29; Deuteronomy 9:21"> they who swear by the propitiation of Samaria, and who say, Your god, O Dan, lives; and, Your god, O Bersabee, lives; and they shall fall, and shall no more rise again.</span>
