<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 83:5–8; Esias 16:6; Chronicles II 32:1"> And after this came the children of Moab, and the children of Ammon, and with them some of the Minaeans, against Josaphat to battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:7; Kings I 23:29; Joshua 15:62"> And they came and told Josaphat, saying, There is come against you a great multitude from Syria, from beyond the sea; and, behold, they are in Asasan Thamar, this is Engadi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Chronicles II 19:3; Kings I 7:6"> And Josaphat was alarmed, and set his face to seek the Lord earnestly, and he proclaimed a fast in all Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 60:10–12; Psalms 69:35"> And Juda gathered themselves together to seek after the Lord: even from all the cities of Juda they came to seek the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 19:15–19; Chronicles II 6:12–13; Chronicles II 34:31"> And Josaphat stood up in the assembly of Juda in Jerusalem, in the house of the Lord, in front of the new court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Chronicles I 29:11–12; Daniel 4:17"> And he said, O Lord God of my fathers, are not you God in heaven above, and are not you Lord of all the kingdoms of the nations? and is there not in your hand the might of dominion, and there is no one who can resist you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 41:8; James 2:23; Psalms 44:2"> Are not you the Lord that did destroy the inhabitants of this land before the face of your people Israel, and did give it to your beloved seed of Abraham for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:10; Chronicles II 2:4"> And they lived in it, and built in it a sanctuary to your name, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:28–30; Chronicles II 6:20; Kings III 8:33"> If there should come upon us evils, sword, judgement, pestilence, famine, we will stand before this house, and before you, (for your name is upon this house,) and we will cry to you because of the affliction, and you shall hear, and deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 2:9"> And now, behold, the children of Ammon, and Moab, and mount Seir, with regard to whom you did not permit Israel to pass through their border, when they had come out of the land of Egypt, (for they turned away from them, and did not destroy them;)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–12; Judges 11:23–24; Jeremias 18:20"> yet now, behold, they make attempts against us, to come forth to cast us out from our inheritance which you gave us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 25:15; Psalms 141:8"> O Lord our God, will you not judge them? for we have no strength to resist this great multitude that is come against us; and we know not what we shall do to them: but our eyes are towards you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:1; Acts 21:5; Deuteronomy 29:10"> And all Juda was standing before the Lord, and their children, and their wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:1; Chronicles II 24:20; Acts 10:31"> And Oziel the son of Zacharias, of the children of Banaias, of the sons of Eleiel, the sons of Matthanias the Levite, of the sons of Asaph, —upon him came the Spirit of the Lord in the assembly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 14:13–14"> and he said, Hear you⌃, all Juda, and the dwellers in Jerusalem, and king Josaphat: Thus says the Lord to you, even you, Fear not, neither be alarmed, before all this great multitude; for the battle is not years, but God's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> To-morrow go you⌃ down against them: behold, they come up by the ascent of Assis, and you⌃ shall find them at the extremity of the river of the wilderness of Jeriel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–14; Chronicles II 15:2; Esias 41:10"> It is not for you to fight: understand these things, and see the deliverance of the Lord with you, Juda and Jerusalem: fear not, neither be afraid to go forth to-morrow to meet them; and the Lord shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Chronicles II 7:3; Genesis 24:26"> And Josaphat bowed with his face to the ground with all Juda and the dwellers in Jerusalem, and they fell before the Lord to worship the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles I 15:16–22; Chronicles I 16:42; Chronicles I 16:5"> And the Levites of the children of Caath, and they of the sons of Core, rose up to praise the Lord God of Israel with a loud voice on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:31; John 14:1; John 11:40"> And they rose early in the morning and went out to the wilderness of Thecoe: and as they went out, Josaphat stood and cried, and said, Hear me, Juda, and the dwellers in Jerusalem; put your trust in the Lord God, and your trust shall be honoured; trust in his prophet, and you⌃ shall prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:1; Psalms 106:1; Chronicles II 5:13"> And he took counsel with the people, and set appointed men to sing psalms and praises, to give thanks, and sing the holy songs of praise in going forth before the host: and they said, Give thanks to the Lord, for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 14:20; Esias 19:2; Jezekiel 38:21"> And when they began the praise and thanksgiving, the Lord caused the children of Ammon to fight against Moab, and the inhabitants of mount Seir that came out against Juda; and they were routed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:22; Kings I 14:20; Jezekiel 35:2–3"> Then the children of Ammon and Moab rose up against the dwellers in mount Seir, to destroy and consume them; and when they had made an end of destroying the inhabitants of Seir, they rose up against one another so that they were utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 37:36; Psalms 110:6; Chronicles I 5:22"> And Juda came to the watch-tower of the wilderness, and looked, and saw the multitude, and, behold, they were all fallen dead upon the earth, not one escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings I 30:19–20; Kings IV 7:9–16; Numbers 31:15"> And Josaphat and his people went out to spoil them, and they found much cattle, and furniture, and spoils, and precious things: and they spoiled them, and they were three days gathering the spoil, for it was abundant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:15; Genesis 32:20; Chronicles II 5:9"> And it came to pass on the fourth day they were gathered to the Valley of Blessing; for there they blessed the Lord: therefore they called the name of the place the Valley of Blessing, until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:43; Hebrews 6:20; Psalms 30:1"> And all the men of Juda returned to Jerusalem, and Josaphat led them with great joy; for the Lord gave them joy over their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 14:2–3; Psalms 57:8; Psalms 92:3"> And they entered into Jerusalem with lutes and harps and trumpets, going into the house of the lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:10; Kings IV 7:6; Exodus 23:27"> And there was a terror of the Lord upon all the kingdoms of the land, when they heard that the Lord fought against the enemies of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Chronicles II 15:15; Chronicles II 14:6–7; Kings II 7:1"> And the kingdom of Josaphat was at peace; and his God gave him rest round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings III 22:41–44"> And Josaphat reigned over Juda, being thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Azuba, daughter of Sali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Chronicles II 14:2–5; Kings III 15:11; Chronicles II 14:11–13"> And he walked in the ways of his father Asa, and turned not aside from doing that which was right in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:6; Chronicles II 19:3; Chronicles II 12:14"> nevertheless the high places yet remained; and as yet the people did not direct their heart to the Lord God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 16:1; Chronicles II 19:2; Kings III 16:7"> And the rest of the acts of Josaphat, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the history of Jeu the son of Anani, who wrote the book of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 22:48–49; Kings IV 1:2–16"> And afterwards Josaphat king of Juda entered into an alliance with Ochozias king of Israel, (now this was an unrighteous man,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 10:22; Chronicles II 9:21; Kings III 9:26"> by acting with and going to him, to build ships to go to Tharsis: and he built ships in Gasion Gaber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Chronicles II 19:2; Chronicles II 9:21"> And Eliezer you son of Dodia of Marisa prophesied against Josaphat, saying, Forasmuch as you have allied yourself with Ochozias, the Lord has broken your work, and your vessels have been wrecked. And they could not go to Tharsis.</span>
