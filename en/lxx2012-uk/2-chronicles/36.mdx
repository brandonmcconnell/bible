<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 22:11; Chronicles II 33:25; Chronicles II 26:1"> And the people of the land took Joachaz the son of Josias, and anointed him, and made him king over Jerusalem in the room of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Joachaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Amital, daughter of Jeremias of Lobna. And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers had done. And Pharao Nechao bound him in Deblatha in the land of Aemath, that he might not reign in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 23:33"> And the king brought him over to Egypt; and imposed a tribute on the land, one hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 22:10–12; Kings IV 23:34–35; Jezekiel 19:3–4"> And Pharao Nechao made Eliakim the son of Josias king over Juda in the room of his father Josias, and changed his name to Joakim. And Pharao Nechao took his brother Joachaz and brought him into Egypt, and he died there: but he had given the silver and gold to Pharao. At that time the land began to be taxed to give the money at the command of Pharao; and every one as he could borrowed the silver and the gold of the people of the land, to give to Pharao Nechao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 23:36–37; Jeremias 22:13–19; Jeremias 26:1"> Joachim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Zechora, daughter of Nerias of Rama. And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers did. In his days came Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon into the land, and he served him three years, and then revolted from him. And the Lord sent against them the Chaldeans, and plundering parties of Syrians, and plundering parties of the Moabites, and of the children of Ammon, and of Samaria; but after this they departed, according to the word of the Lord by the hand of his servants the prophets. Nevertheless the wrath of the Lord was upon Juda, so that they should be removed from his presence, because of the sins of Manasses in all that he did, and for the innocent blood which Joakim shed, for he had filled Jerusalem with innocent blood; yet the Lord would not utterly destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:11; Kings IV 24:5–6; Jezekiel 19:5–9"> And Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon came up against him, and bound him with brazen fetters, and carried him away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 24:13; Daniel 5:2–4; Jeremias 27:16–18"> And he carried away a part of the vessels of the house of the Lord to Babylon, and put them in his temple in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings IV 24:5–6; Matthew 1:11–12; Chronicles I 3:16–17"> And the rest of the acts of Joakim, and all that he did, behold, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda? And Joakim slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in Ganozae: and Jechonias his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 24:8–17"> Jechonias was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem, and did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 37:1; Chronicles II 36:7; Kings IV 24:10–17"> And at the turn of the year, king Nabuchodonosor sent, and brought him to Babylon, with the precious vessels of the house of the Lord, and made Sedekias his father's brother king over Juda and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 24:18–20; Jeremias 52:1–3; Jeremias 27:1"> Sedekias was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and be reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:23; Jeremias 37:2–21; Jeremias 38:14–28"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord his God: he was not ashamed before the prophet Jeremias, nor because of the word of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 52:2–3; Chronicles II 30:8; Jezekiel 17:11–20"> in that he rebelled against king Nabuchodonosor, which he adjured him by God not to do: but he stiffened his neck, and hardened his heart, so as not to return to the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:8; Jezekiel 22:6; Michaeas 3:9–11"> And all the great men of Juda, and the priests, and the people of the land transgressed abundantly in the abominations of the heathen, and polluted the house of the Lord which was in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 35:15; Jeremias 7:13; Jeremias 25:3–4"> And the Lord God of their fathers sent by the hand of his prophets; rising early and sending his messengers, for he spared his people, and his sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 5:12–13; Chronicles II 30:10; Luke 22:63–64"> Nevertheless they sneered at his messengers, and set at nothing his words, and mocked his prophets, until the wrath of the Lord rose up against his people, till there was no remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:20; Ezra 9:7; Jezekiel 9:5–7"> And he brought against them the king of the Chaldeans, and killed their young men with the sword in the house of his sanctuary, and did not spare Sedekias, and had no mercy upon their virgins, and they led away their old men: he delivered all things into their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:7; Chronicles II 36:10; Esias 39:6"> And all the vessels of the house of God, the great and the small, and the treasures of the house of the Lord, and all the treasures of the king and the great men; he brought all to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; Kings III 9:8; Jeremias 52:13–15"> And he burnt the house of the Lord, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, and burnt its palaces with fire, and utterly destroyed every beautiful vessel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 27:7; Ezra 1:1–11; Chronicles II 36:22"> And he carried away the remnant to Babylon; and they were servants to him and to his sons until the establishment of the kingdom of the Medes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 29:10; Leviticus 26:34–35; Zacharias 1:12"> That the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremias might be fulfilled, until the land should enjoy its sabbaths in resting and sabbath keeping all the days of its desolation, till the accomplishment of seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 25:12; Ezra 1:1–3; Jeremias 29:10"> In the first year of Cyrus king of the Persians, after the fulfilment of the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremias, the Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of the Persians, and told him to make proclamation in writing throughout all his kingdom, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 44:26–28; Ezra 7:13; Romans 8:31"> Thus says Cyrus king of the Persians to all the kingdoms of the earth, The Lord God of heaven has given me power, and he has commanded me to build a house to him in Jerusalem, in Judea. Who is there of you of all his people? His God shall be with him, and let him go up.</span>
