<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Joshua 20:7; Chronicles I 12:38"> And Roboam came to Sychem: for all Israel came to Sychem to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 11:40; Kings III 12:2; Kings III 11:28"> And it came to pass when Jeroboam the son of Nabat heard it, (now he was in Egypt, forasmuch as he had fled there from the face of king Solomon, and Jeroboam lived in Egypt,) that Jeroboam returned out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 12:3"> And they sent and called him: and Jeroboam and all the congregation came to Roboam, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 12:4; Exodus 2:23; Matthew 11:29–30"> Your father made our yoke grievous: now then abate somewhat of your father's grievous rule, and of his heavy yoke which he put upon us, and we will serve you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 12:5; Proverbs 3:28"> And he said to them, Go away for three days, and then come to me. So the people departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 32:7; Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 27:10"> And king Roboam assembled the elders that stood before his father Solomon in his life-time, saying, How do you⌃ counsel me to return an answer to this people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Genesis 49:21; Kings III 12:7"> And they spoke to him, saying, If you would this day befriend this people, and be kind to them, and speak to them good words, then will they be your servants for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 17:14; Proverbs 1:25; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> But he forsook the advice of the old men, who took counsel with him, and he took counsel with the young men who had been brought up with him, who stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 10:6; Kings II 17:5–6; Kings III 22:6–8"> And he said to them, What do you⌃ advise that I should answer this people, who spoke to me, saying, Ease somewhat of the yoke which your father laid upon us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 13:16; Kings II 17:7–13"> And the young men that had been brought up with him spoke to him, saying, Thus shall you speak to the people that spoke to you, saying, Your father made our yoke heavy, and do you lighten somewhat of it from us; thus shall you say, My little finger shall be thicker than my father's loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; Esias 47:6; Revelation 9:10"> And whereas my father chastised you with a heavy yoke, I will also add to your yoke: my father chastised you with whips, and I will chastise you with scorpions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 12:12–15; Chronicles II 10:5"> And Jeroboam and all the people came to Roboam on the third day, as the king had spoken, saying, Return to me on the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 10:8; Proverbs 19:27; Kings I 25:10–11"> And the king answered harshly; and king Roboam forsook the counsel of the old men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 12:5; Ecclesiastes 10:16"> and spoke to them according to the counsel of the young men, saying, My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add to it: my father chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 11:29–39; Kings I 2:25; Chronicles II 25:16–20"> And the king listened not to the people, for there was a change of their minds from God, saying, The Lord has confirmed his word, which he spoke by the hand of Achia the Selonite concerning Jeroboam the son of Nabat, and concerning all Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 20:1; Chronicles II 10:19; Psalms 76:10"> for the king did not listen to them. And the people answered the king, saying, What portion have we in David, or inheritance in the son of Jessae? to your tents, O Israel: now see to your own house, David. So all Israel went to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:1; Kings III 12:17; Kings III 11:36"> But the men of Israel, even those who lived in the cities of Juda, remained and made Roboam king over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 5:14; Kings III 4:6; Acts 7:57–58"> And king Roboam sent to them Adoniram that was over the tribute; and the children of Israel stoned him with stones, and he died. And king Roboam hasted to mount his chariot, to flee to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 12:19–20; Ezra 9:7; Chronicles II 5:9"> So Israel rebelled against the house of David until this day.</span>
