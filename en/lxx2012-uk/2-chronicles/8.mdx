<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 9:10–28"> And it came to pass after twenty years, in which Solomon built the house of the Lord, and his own house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 9:11–18"> that Solomon rebuilt the cities which Chiram had given to Solomon, and caused the children of Israel to dwell in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Chronicles I 18:3; Kings II 8:3"> And Solomon came to Baesoba, and fortified it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 9:17–19"> And he built Thoedmor in the wilderness, and all the strong cities which he built in Emath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 7:24; Joshua 16:5; Chronicles II 14:7"> And he built Baethoron the upper, and Baethoron the lower, strong cities, —they had walls, gates, and bars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 7:2; Chronicles II 8:4; Chronicles II 1:14"> and Balaath, and all the strong cities which Solomon had, and all his chariot cities, and cities of horsemen, and all things that Solomon desired according to his desire of building, in Jerusalem, and in Libanus, and in all his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 7:1; Kings III 9:20–22"> As for all the people that was left of the Chettites, and the Amorites, and the Pherezites, and the Evites, and the Jebusites, who are not of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Kings III 9:21; Kings III 4:6"> but were of the children of them whom the children Israel destroyed not, that were left after them in the land, even them did Solomon make tributaries to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 8:11–12; Exodus 19:5–6; Galatians 4:31"> But Solomon did not make any of the children of Israel servants in his kingdom; for, behold, they were warriors and rulers, and mighty men, and captains of chariots and horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 9:23; Kings III 5:16; Chronicles II 2:18"> And these are the chiefs of the officers of king Solomon, two hundred and fifty overseeing the work amongst the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 3:1; Kings III 7:8; Kings III 9:24"> And Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharao from the city of David to the house which he had built for her: for he said, My wife shall not dwell in the city of David, the king of Israel, for the place is holy into which the ark of the Lord has entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:1; Chronicles II 15:8; John 10:23"> Then Solomon offered up to the Lord whole burnt offerings on the altar which he had built to the Lord before the temple,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 29:38–42; Jezekiel 46:3–15"> according to the daily rate, to offer up sacrifices according to the commandments of Moses, on the sabbaths, and at the new moons, and at the feasts, three times in the year, at the feast of unleavened bread, and at the feast of weeks, and at the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles I 9:17; Ezra 6:18; Nehemiah 12:36"> And he established, according to the order of his father David, the courses of the priests, and that according to their public ministrations: and the Levites were appointed over their charges, to praise and minister before the priests according to the daily order: and the porters were appointed according to their courses to the different gates: for thus were the commandments of David the man of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles II 30:12; Chronicles I 9:29; Kings III 7:51"> They transgressed not the commandments of the king concerning the priests and the Levites with regard to everything else, and with regard to the treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 5:18; Kings III 6:7"> Now all the work had been prepared from the day when the foundation was laid, until Solomon finished the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 14:22; Deuteronomy 2:8; Numbers 33:35"> Then Solomon went to Gasion Gaber, and to Aelath near the sea in the land of Idumea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:10; Chronicles II 9:13; Ecclesiastes 2:8"> And Chiram sent by the hand of his servants ships, and servants skilled in naval affairs; and they went with the servants of Solomon to Sophira, and brought thence four hundred and fifty talents of gold, and they came to king Solomon.</span>
