<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; Kings III 10:1–13"> And the queen of Saba heard of the name of Solomon, and she came to Jerusalem with a very large force, to prove Solomon with hard questions, and she had camels bearing spices in abundance, and gold, and precious stones: and she came to Solomon, and told him all that was in her mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:15; Kings III 3:12; Mark 4:34"> And Solomon told her all her words; and there passed not a word from Solomon which he told her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:1–4; Kings III 10:3; Kings III 6:1–7"> And the queen of Saba saw the wisdom of Solomon, and the house which he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Kings III 4:22–23; Psalms 143:7"> and the meat of the tables, and the sitting of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and their raiment; and his cupbearers, and their apparel; and the whole burnt offerings which he offered up in the house of the Lord; then she was in ecstasy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 10:6"> And she said to the king, It was a true report which I heard in my land concerning your words, and concerning your wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 10:7; Chronicles II 9:5; Kings III 4:34"> Yet I believed not the reports until I came, and my eyes saw: and, behold, the half of the abundance of your wisdom was not told me: you have exceeded the report which I heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:21"> Blessed are your men, blessed are these your servants, who stand before you continually, and hear your wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:11; Chronicles I 29:23; Esias 32:1–2"> Blessed be the Lord your God, who took pleasure in you, to set you upon his throne for a king, to the Lord your God: forasmuch as the Lord your God loved Israel to establish them for ever, therefore he has set you over them for a king to execute judgement and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:1; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> And she gave the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and spices in very great abundance, and precious stones: and there were not any where else such spices as those which the queen of Saba gave king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:18; Kings III 10:11; Kings III 10:22"> And the servants of Solomon and the servants of Chiram brought gold to Solomon out of Suphir, and pine timber, and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 10:12; Chronicles I 23:5; Psalms 92:1–3"> And the king made of the pine timber steps to the house of the Lord, and to the king's house, and harps and lutes for the singers: and such were not seen before in the land of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 10:13; Ephesians 3:20; Psalms 20:4"> And king Solomon gave to the queen of Saba all that she requested, besides all that she brought to king Solomon: and she returned to her own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 72:15; Psalms 68:29; Kings III 10:14–28"> And the weight of the gold that was brought to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:29; Psalms 72:10"> besides what the men who were regularly appointed and the merchants brought, and all the kings of Arabia and princes of the land: all brought gold and silver to king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 10:16–17; Chronicles II 12:9–10"> And king Solomon made two hundred shields of beaten gold: there were six hundred shekels of pure gold to one shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 7:2"> And three hundred buckles of beaten gold: the weight of three hundred gold shekels went to one buckler: and the king placed them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 10:18–20; Psalms 45:8; Revelation 20:11"> And the king made a great throne of ivory, and he gilded it with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Revelation 5:5; Numbers 23:24"> And there were six steps to the throne, riveted with gold, and elbows on either side of the seat of the throne, and two lions standing by the elbows:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 21:12"> and twelve lions standing there on the six steps on each side. There was not the like in any other kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 10:21; Esther 1:7; Chronicles II 9:27"> And all king Solomon's vessels were of gold, and all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were covered with gold: silver was not thought anything of in the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 22:48; Job 39:13; Kings III 10:22"> For a ship went for the king to Tharsis with the servants of Chiram: once every three years came vessels from Tharsis to the king, laden with gold, and silver, and ivory, and apes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:12; Psalms 89:27; Kings III 4:30–31"> And Solomon exceeded all other kings both in riches and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:5; James 3:17; Proverbs 2:6"> And all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom, which God had put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 10:25; Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10"> And they brought every one his gifts, silver vessels and golden vessels, and raiment, myrrh and spices, horses and mules, a rate every year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 4:26; Kings III 10:26; Chronicles II 1:14"> And Solomon had four thousand mares for chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen; and he put them in the chariot cities, and with the king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 4:21; Genesis 15:18; Kings III 4:24"> And he rules over all the kings from the river even to the land of the Philistines, and to the borders of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:15–17; Esias 9:10; Kings III 10:27–29"> And the king made gold and silver in Jerusalem as stones, and cedars as the sycamore trees in the plain for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:16; Kings III 10:28; Esias 31:1"> And Solomon imported horses from Egypt, and from every other country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:29; Kings III 11:41–43; Kings III 11:29"> And the rest of the acts of Solomon, the first and the last, behold, these are written in the words of Nathan the prophet, and in the words of Achia the Selonite, and in the visions of Joel the seer concerning Jeroboam the son of Nabat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings III 11:42–43"> And Solomon reigned over all Israel forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings III 2:10; Kings II 7:12; Kings III 1:21"> And Solomon fell asleep, and they buried him in the city of David his father: and Roboam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
