<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:5; Chronicles II 21:6; Chronicles II 19:2"> And Josaphat had yet great wealth and glory, and he connected himself by marriage with the house of Achaab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 9:1; Chronicles II 19:2; Kings III 1:9"> And he went down after a term of years to Achaab to Samaria: and Achaab killed for him sheep and calves, in abundance, and for the people with him, and he much desired him to go up with him to Ramoth of the country of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 22:4; Kings IV 3:7; 2 John 1:10–11"> And Achaab king of Israel said to Josaphat king of Juda, Will you go with me to Ramoth of the country of Galaad? And he said to him, As I am, so also are you, as your people, so also is my people with you for the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 21:2; Kings II 5:19; Kings II 2:1"> And Josaphat said to the king of Israel, Seek, I pray you, the Lord today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; Michaeas 3:11; Jeremias 28:1–17"> And the king of Israel gathered the prophets, four hundred men, and said to them, Shall I go to Ramoth Galaad to battle, or shall I forbear? And they said, Go up, and God shall deliver it into the hands of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 22:7–9; Kings IV 3:11–13"> And Josaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of the Lord besides, that we may enquire of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Michaeas 2:7; Kings III 21:20"> And the king of Israel said to Josaphat, There is yet one man by whom to enquire of the Lord; but I hate him, for he does not prophesy concerning me for good, for all his days are for evil: this is Michaias the son of Jembla. And Josaphat said, Let not the king say so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 22:9; Esias 39:7; Chronicles II 18:25–26"> And the king called an eunuch, and said, Fetch quickly Michaias the son of Jembla.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 14:9; Daniel 7:9; Matthew 19:28"> And the king of Israel and Josaphat king of Juda were sitting each on his throne, and clothed in their robes, sitting in the open space at the entrance of the gate of Samaria: and all the prophets were prophesying before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 23:17; Zacharias 1:18–21; Jeremias 28:2–3"> And Sedekias son of Chanaan made for himself iron horns, and said, Thus says the Lord, With these you shall thrust Syria until it be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 24:24–25; Chronicles II 18:33–34; Jude 1:16"> And all the prophets prophesied so, saying, Go up to Ramoth Galaad, and you shall prosper; and the Lord shall deliver it into the hands of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Michaeas 2:6; Psalms 10:11; Michaeas 2:11"> And the messenger that went to call Michaias spoke to him, saying, Behold, the prophets have spoken favourably concerning the king with one mouth; let now, I pray you, your words be as the words of one of them, and do you speak good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 22:35; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18–20"> And Michaias said, As the Lord lives, whatever God shall say to me, that will I speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 22:15; Lamentations 4:21; Amos 4:4–5"> And he came to the king, and the king said to him, Michaias, shall I go up to Ramoth Galaad to battle, or shall I forbear? And he said, Go up, and you shall prosper, and they shall be given into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; Kings I 14:24; Kings III 22:16"> And the king said to him, How often shall I solemnly charge you that you speak to me nothing but truth in the name of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Jezekiel 34:5–6; Numbers 27:17"> And he said, I saw Israel scattered on the mountains, as sheep without a shepherd: and the Lord said, These have no commander; let each return to his home in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:7; Kings III 22:18; Proverbs 29:1"> And the king of Israel said to Josaphat, Said I not to you, that he would not prophesy concerning me good, but evil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Esias 6:1–5; Kings III 22:19–23"> But he said, Not so. Hear you⌃ the word of the Lord: I saw the Lord sitting on his throne, and all the host of heaven stood by on his right hand and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 14:9; Chronicles II 25:19; Proverbs 11:5"> And the Lord said, Who will deceive Achaab king of Israel, that he may go up, and fall in Ramoth Galaad? And one spoke this way, and another spoke that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:6; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And there came forth a spirit, and stood before the Lord, and said, I will deceive him. And the Lord said, Whereby?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:44; Kings III 22:21–22; 1 John 4:6"> And he said, I will go forth, and will be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets. And the Lord said, You shall deceive him, and shall prevail: go forth, and do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esias 19:14; Jezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16"> And now, behold, the Lord has put a false spirit in the mouth of these your prophets, and the Lord has spoken evil against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Jeremias 20:2; Chronicles II 18:10"> Then Sedekias the son of Chanaan drew near, and struck Michaias on the cheek, and said to him, By what way passed the Spirit of the Lord from me to speak to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 29:21–22; Esias 26:20; Jeremias 28:16–17"> And Michaias said, Behold, you shall see in that day, when you shall go from chamber to chamber to hide yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:8; Chronicles II 34:8; Jeremias 38:6–7"> And the king of Israel said, Take Michaias, and carry him back to Emer the governor of the city, and to Joas the captain, the king's son;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:10; Psalms 80:5; Luke 23:2"> and you⌃ shall say, Thus said the king, Put this fellow into the prison house, and let him eat the bread of affliction, and drink the water of affliction, until I return in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:45–46; Amos 9:10; Michaeas 1:2"> And Michaias said, If you do at all return in peace, the Lord has not spoken by me. And he said, Hear, all you⌃ people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 22:29–33"> So the king of Israel, and Josaphat king of Juda, went up to Ramoth Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings I 28:8; Jeremias 23:24; Proverbs 26:25"> And the king of Israel said to Josaphat, Disguise me, and I will enter into the battle: and do you put on my raiment. so the king of Israel disguised himself, and entered into the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:11; Deuteronomy 1:17; Kings III 20:42"> Now the king of Syria had commanded the captains of the chariots that were with him, saying, Fight neither against small nor great, but only against the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:14; Psalms 118:13; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw Josaphat, that they said, It is the king of Israel: and they compassed him about to fight against him: and Josaphat cried out, and the Lord delivered him; and God turned them away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings III 22:33"> And it came to pass, when the captains of the chariots saw that it was not the king of Israel, that they turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 35:23; Kings II 15:11; Kings III 22:34–35"> And a man drew a bow with a good aim, and struck the king of Israel between the lungs and the breast-plate: and he said to the charioteer, Turn your hand, drive me out of the battle, for I am wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 13:21; Chronicles II 18:27; Proverbs 28:17"> And the battle turned in that day; and the king of Israel remained on the chariot against Syria until evening, and died at sunset.</span>
