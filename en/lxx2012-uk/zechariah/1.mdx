<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:4; Zacharias 7:1; Zacharias 1:7"> In the eighth month, in the second year of the reign of Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zacharias, the son of Barachias, the son of Addo, the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 44:6; Lamentations 5:7; Jezekiel 22:31"> The Lord has been very angry with your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachias 3:7; Jeremias 3:22; Jeremias 4:1"> And you shall say to them, Thus says the Lord Almighty: Turn to me, says the Lord of hosts, and I will turn to you, says the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:8; Psalms 106:6–7; Jeremias 35:15"> And be you⌃ not as your fathers, whom the prophets before charged, saying, Thus says the Lord Almighty: Turn you⌃ from your evil ways, and from your evil practices: but they listened not, and attended not to listen to me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Job 14:10–12; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Where are your fathers, and the prophets? Will they live for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:15; Jezekiel 12:25–28"> But do you⌃ receive my words and my ordinances, all that I command by my Spirit to my servants the prophets, who lived in the days of your fathers; and they answered and said, As the Lord Almighty determined to do to us, according to our ways, and according to our practices, so has he done to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zacharias 1:1"> On the twenty-fourth day in the eleventh month, this is the month Sabat, in the second year of the reign of Darius, the word of the Lord came to Zacharias, the son of Barachias, the son of Addo, the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 6:4; Esias 55:13; Zacharias 13:7"> I saw by night, and behold a man mounted on a red horse, and he stood between the shady mountains; and behind him were red horses, and grey, and piebald, and white.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zacharias 4:4–5; Zacharias 5:5; Zacharias 2:3"> And I said, What are these, my lord? And the angel spoke with me said to me, I will show you what these things are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 1:11; Zacharias 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> And the man that stood between the mountains answered, and said to me, These are they whom the Lord has sent forth to go round the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zacharias 1:10; Zacharias 1:15; Psalms 103:20–21"> And they answered the angel of the Lord that stood between the mountains, and said, We have gone round all the earth, and, behold, all the earth is inhabited, and is at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zacharias 7:5; Jeremias 29:10; Psalms 102:13"> Then the angel of the Lord answered and said, O Lord Almighty, how long will you have no mercy on Jerusalem, and the cities of Juda, which you have disregarded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. this seventies year.">a</a></sup>these seventy years?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 29:10; Esias 40:1–2; Jeremias 30:10–22"> And the Lord Almighty answered the angel that spoke with me good words and consolatory sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Esias 40:1; Zacharias 1:17"> And the angel that spoke with me said to me, Cry out and say, Thus says the Lord Almighty; I have been jealous for Jerusalem and Sion with great jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremias 51:24; Esias 54:8"> And I am very angry with the heathen that combine to attack her: forasmuch as I indeed was a little angry, but they combined to attack her for evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zacharias 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; Zacharias 2:10–11"> Therefore thus says the Lord: I will return to Jerusalem with compassion; and my house shall be rebuilt in her, says the Lord Almighty, and a measuring line shall yet be stretched out over Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 51:3; Zacharias 2:12; Sophonias 3:15–17"> And the angel that spoke with me said to me, Cry yet, and say, Thus says the Lord Almighty; Yet shall cities be spread abroad through prosperity; and the Lord shall yet have mercy upon Sion, and shall choose Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 5:13; Kings IV 24:1–20; Zacharias 5:9"> And I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold four horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zacharias 1:21; Zacharias 8:14; Zacharias 2:2"> And I said to the angel that spoke with me, What are these things, my lord? And he said to me, These are the horns that have scattered Juda, and Israel, and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zacharias 10:3–5; Zacharias 12:2–6; Zacharias 9:12–16"> And the Lord showed me four artificers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Zacharias 1:19; Psalms 75:4–5"> And I said, What are these coming to do? And he said, These are the horns that scattered Juda, and they broke Israel in pieces, and none of them lifted up his head: and these are come forth to sharpen them for their hands, even the four horns, the nations that lifted up the horn against the land of the Lord to scatter it.</span>
