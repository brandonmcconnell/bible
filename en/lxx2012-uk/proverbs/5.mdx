<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:17; Proverbs 4:20; Proverbs 2:1"> My son, attend to my wisdom, and apply your ear to my words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23; Proverbs 15:7; Malachias 2:6–7"> that you may keep good understanding, and the discretion of my lips gives you a charge. Give no heed to a worthless woman;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 7:5"> for honey drops from the lips of a harlot, who for a season pleases your palate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 55:21"> but afterwards you will find her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. a more bitter thing.">a</a></sup>more bitter than gall, and sharper than a two-edged sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19"> For the feet of folly lead those who deal with her down to the grave with death; and her steps are not established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26; Proverbs 5:21; Psalms 16:11"> For she goes not upon the paths of life; but her ways are slippery, and not easily known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Proverbs 7:24; Proverbs 22:17–21"> Now then, my son, hear me, and make not my words of none effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 6:13"> Remove your way far from her; draw not near to the doors of her house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Osee 4:13–14; Genesis 38:23–26"> lest you give away your life to others, and your substance to the merciless:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 7:9; Proverbs 6:35; Proverbs 31:3"> lest strangers be filled with your strength, and your labours come into the houses of strangers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:21; Revelation 22:15; Hebrews 13:4"> And you repent at last, when the flesh of your body is consumed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 15:5"> and you shall say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart avoided reproofs!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:8; Luke 15:18; Hebrews 13:7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. omits 'not.'">b</a></sup>I heard not the voice of him that instructed me, and taught me, neither did I apply my ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Peter 2:10–18; 1 Corinthians 10:6–8"> I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2–5; Hebrews 13:4; Proverbs 5:18–19"> Drink waters out of your own vessels, and out of your own springing wells.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3; Esias 48:21"> Let not waters out of your fountain be spilled by you, but let your waters go into your streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Let them be only your own, and let no stranger partake with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Malachias 2:14–15; Song of Songs 4:15"> Let your fountain of water be truly your own; and rejoice with the wife of your youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Songs 7:3; Song of Songs 8:14; Song of Songs 4:5"> Let your loving hart and your graceful colt company with you, and let her be considered your own, and be with you at all times; for ravished with her love you shall be greatly increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 7:5; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 23:33"> Be not intimate with a strange woman, neither fold yourself in the arms of a woman not your own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Proverbs 15:3; Job 34:21"> For the ways of a man are before the eyes of God, and he looks on all his paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:5; Psalms 9:15; Proverbs 1:18"> Iniquities ensnare a man, and every one is bound in the chains of his own sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 36:12; Job 4:21; Proverbs 10:21"> Such a man dies with the uninstructed; and he is cast forth from the abundance of his own substance, and has perished through folly.</span>
