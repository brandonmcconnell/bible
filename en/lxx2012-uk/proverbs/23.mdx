<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> If you sit to sup at the table of a prince, consider attentively the things set before you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> and apply your hand, knowing that it behoves you to prepare such meats: but if you are very insatiable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> desire not his provisions; for these belong to a false life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> If you are poor, measure not yourself with a rich man; but refrain yourself in your wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> If you should fix your eye upon him, he will disappear; for wings like an eagle's are prepared for him, and he returns to the house of his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Sup not with an envious man, neither desire you his meats:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> so he eats and drinks as if any one should swallow a hair, and do not bring him in to yourself, nor eat your morsel with him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> for he will vomit it up, and spoil your fair words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Say nothing in the ears of a fool, lest at any time he sneer at your wise words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zacharias 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Remove not the ancient landmarks; and enter not upon the possession of the fatherless:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremias 51:36"> for the Lord is their redeemer; he is mighty, and will plead their cause with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Apply your heart to instruction, and prepare your ears for words of discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Refrain not from chastening a child; for if you beat him with the rod, he shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> For you shall beat him with the rod, and shall deliver his soul from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> Son, if your heart be wise, you shall also gladden my heart;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> and your lips shall converse with my lips, if they be right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Let not your heart envy sinners: but be you in the fear of the Lord all the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremias 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> For if you should keep these things, you shall have posterity; and your hope shall not be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Hear, my son, and be wise, and rightly direct the thoughts of your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Esias 5:22"> Be not a wine-bibber, neither continue long at feasts, and purchases of flesh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> for every drunkard and whoremonger shall be poor; and every sluggard shall clothe himself with tatters and ragged garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Listen, my son, to your father which begot you, and despise not your mother because she is grown old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A righteous father brings up his children well; and his soul rejoices over a wise son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Let your father and your mother rejoice over you, and let her that bore you be glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes observe my ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a strange house is a vessel full of holes; and a strange well is narrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> For such a one shall perish suddenly; and every transgressor shall be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Esias 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who has woe? who trouble? who has quarrels? and who vexations and disputes? who has bruises without a cause? whose eyes are livid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Esias 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> Are not those of them that stay long at wine? are not those of them that haunt the places where banquets are? Be not drunk with wine; but converse with just men, and converse with them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. in public walks.">a</a></sup>openly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Kings II 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> For if you should set your eyes on bowls and cups, you shall afterwards go more naked than a pestle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Esias 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> But at last such a one stretches himself out as one struck by a serpent, and venom is diffused through him as by a horned serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Whenever your eyes shall behold a strange woman, then your mouth shall speak perverse things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; Kings III 20:16–22; Kings I 30:16–17"> And you shall lie as in the midst of the sea, and as a pilot in a great storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Esias 56:12; Jeremias 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> And you shall say, They struck me, and I was not pained; and they mocked me, and I knew it not: when will it be morning, that I may go and seek those with whom I may go in company?</span>
