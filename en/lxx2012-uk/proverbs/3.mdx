<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 4:5; John 14:21–24; Deuteronomy 6:6–9"> My son, forget not my laws; but let your heart keep my words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; Proverbs 4:10; Psalms 91:16"> for length of existence, and years of life, and peace, shall they add to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 7:3; 2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16"> Let not mercy and truth forsake you; but bind them about your neck:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Kings I 2:26; Romans 14:18"> so shall you find favour:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. + 'and write them on the table of your heart.'">a</a></sup>and do you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Rom 12:17.">b</a></sup>provide things honest in the sight of the Lord, and of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:5; Jeremias 17:7–8; Proverbs 28:26"> Trust in God with all your heart; and be not exalted in your own wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; Psalms 32:8; Philippians 4:6"> In all your ways acquaint yourself with her, that she may rightly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. divide. See 2 Tim 2:13.">c</a></sup>direct your paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Romans 12:16; Proverbs 26:12"> Be not wise in your own conceit; but fear God, and depart from all evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Psalms 147:3; Proverbs 16:24"> Then shall there be health to your body, and good keeping to your bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2–15; 1 Corinthians 16:2; Exodus 23:19"> Honour the Lord with your just labours, and give him the first of your fruits of righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 28:8; Proverbs 22:9"> that your storehouses may be completely filled with corn, and that your presses may burst forth with wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:17; Revelation 3:19; Hebrews 12:5–12"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb 12. 5, 6.">d</a></sup>My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; nor faint when you are rebuked of him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:5; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 29:17"> for whom the Lord loves, he rebukes, and scourges every son whom he receives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–35; Proverbs 4:5–9; Ecclesiastes 9:15–18"> Blessed is the man who has found wisdom, and the mortal who knows prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:16; Proverbs 8:19; Job 28:13–19"> For it is better to traffic for her, than for treasures of gold and silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Job 28:18; Psalms 73:25–26"> And she is more valuable than precious stones: no evil thing shall resist her: she is well known to all that approach her, and no precious thing is equal to her in value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:2; 2 Corinthians 6:10; Proverbs 22:4"> For length of existence and years of life are in her right hand; and in her left hand are wealth and glory: out of her mouth proceeds righteousness, and she carries law and mercy upon her tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:79; Esias 26:3; Philippians 4:8–9"> Her ways are good ways, and all her paths are peaceful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:30; Revelation 2:7; Proverbs 15:4"> She is a tree of life to all that lay hold upon her; and she is a secure help to all that stay themselves on her, as on the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:24; Jeremias 10:12; John 1:3"> God by wisdom founded the earth, and by prudence he prepared the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 65:9–12; Job 36:27–28"> By understanding were the depths broken up, and the clouds dropped water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Deuteronomy 32:46–47"> My son, let them not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Heb 2:1.">e</a></sup>pass from you, but keep my counsel and understanding:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 4:22; Proverbs 1:9; Esias 38:16"> that your soul may live, and that there may be grace round your neck; and it shall be health to your flesh, and safety to your bones:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Proverbs 10:9; Psalms 121:3"> that you may go confidently in peace in all your ways, and that your foot may not stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 4:8; Proverbs 6:22"> For if you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. sit down.">f</a></sup>rest, you shall be undismayed; and if you sleep, you shall slumber sweetly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 41:10–14; Psalms 112:7; 1 Peter 3:14"> And you shall not be afraid of alarm coming upon you, neither of approaching attacks of ungodly men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 91:3; Kings I 2:9; Proverbs 14:26"> For the Lord shall be over all your ways, and shall establish your foot that you be not moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; James 2:15–16; Romans 13:7"> Forbear not to do good to the poor, whenever your hand may have power to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:12–15; 2 Corinthians 8:11"> Say not, Come back another time, to-morrow I will give; while you are able to do him good: for you know not what the next day will bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 6:14; Proverbs 16:29–30; Psalms 55:20"> Devise not evil against your friend, living near you and trusting in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24; Proverbs 29:22; Romans 12:18–21"> Be not ready to quarrel with a man without a cause, lest he do you some harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 24:1; Psalms 37:1; Galatians 5:21"> Procure not the reproaches of bad men, neither do you covet their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; John 15:15; Proverbs 11:20"> For every transgressor is unclean before the Lord; neither does he sit amongst the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Leviticus 26:14–46; Job 8:6–7"> The curse of God is in the houses of the ungodly; but the habitations of the just are blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 4:6; 1 Peter 5:5; Psalms 138:6"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See 1 Pet 5:5.">g</a></sup>The Lord resists the proud; but he gives grace to the humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Esias 65:13–15; Psalms 73:24"> The wise shall inherit glory; but the ungodly have exalted their own dishonour.</span>
