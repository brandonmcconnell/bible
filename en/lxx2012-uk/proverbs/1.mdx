<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Chronicles I 22:9; Chronicles I 28:5"> The Proverbs of Solomon son of David, who reigned in Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> to know wisdom and instruction, and to perceive words of understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> to receive also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. turnings, q.d. knotty words. See Heb.">a</a></sup>hard saying, and to understand true justice, and how to direct judgement;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> that he might give subtlety to the simple, and to the young man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, discretion.">b</a></sup>discernment and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> For by the hearing of these a wise man will be wiser, and man of understanding will gain direction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> and will understand a parable, and a dark speech; the saying of the wise also, and riddles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The fear of the Lord is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, sum, or, top.">c</a></sup>beginning of wisdom; and there is good understanding to all that practise it: and piety towards God is the beginning of discernment; but the ungodly will set at nothing wisdom and instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> Hear, my son, the instruction of your father, and reject not the rules of your mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For you shall receive for your head a crown of graces, and a chain of gold round your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, let not ungodly men lead you astray, neither consent you to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they should exhort you, saying, Come with us, partake in blood, and let us unjustly hide the just man in the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> and let us swallow him alive, as Hades would, and remove the memorial of him from the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> let us seize on his valuable property, and let us fill our houses with spoils:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> but do you cast in your lot with us, and let us all provide a common purse, and let us have one pouch:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> go not in the way with them, but turn aside your foot from their paths:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="this verse is not in the text. The Alexandrine text is shown here">d</a></sup>For their feet run to do evil, and are swift to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremias 8:7"> for nets are not without cause spread for birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> For they that are concerned in murder store up evils for themselves; and the overthrow of transgressors is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> These are the ways of all that perform lawless deeds; for by ungodliness they destroy their own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom sings aloud in passages, and in the broad places speaks boldly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> And she makes proclamation on the top of the walls, and sits by the gates of princes; and at the gates of the city boldly says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> So long as the simple cleave to justice, they shall not be ashamed: but the foolish being lovers of haughtiness, having become ungodly have hated knowledge, and are become subject to reproofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Jezekiel 33:11; Jezekiel 18:27–30"> Behold, I will bring forth to you the utterance of my breath, and I will instruct you in my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremias 7:13"> Since I called, and you⌃ did not listen; and I spoke at length, and you⌃ gave no heed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> but you⌃ set at nothing my counsels, and disregarded my reproofs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> therefore I also will laugh at your destruction; and I will rejoice against you when ruin comes upon you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> yes when dismay suddenly comes upon you, and your overthrow shall arrive like a tempest; and when tribulation and distress shall come upon you, or when ruin shall come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Michaeas 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremias 11:11"> For it shall be that when you⌃ call upon me, I will not listen to you: wicked men shall seek me, but shall not find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> For they hated wisdom, and did not choose the word of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> neither would they attend to my counsels, but derided my reproofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremias 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> Therefore shall they eat the fruits of their own way, and shall be filled with their own ungodliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremias 2:19; John 3:36"> For because they wronged the simple, they shall be slain; and an inquisition shall ruin the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Esias 26:3"> But he that hearkens to me shall dwell in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. hope. See Ps 16.">e</a></sup>confidence, and shall rest securely from all evil.</span>
</div>
