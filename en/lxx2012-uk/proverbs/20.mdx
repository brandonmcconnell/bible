<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 19:11"> It is a glory to a man to turn aside from railing; but every fool is entangled with such matters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 13:4"> A sluggard when reproached is not ashamed: so also he who borrows corn in harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:4; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Psalms 64:6"> Counsel in a man's heart is deep water; but a prudent man will draw it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 12:1; Matthew 6:2; Luke 18:8"> A man is valuable, and a merciful man precious: but it is hard to find a faithful man.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Kings III 8:46"> Who will boast that he has a pure heart? or who will boldly say that he is pure from sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 20:23; Leviticus 19:35"> A large and small weight, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. double.">a</a></sup>various measures, are even both of them unclean before the Lord; and so is he that makes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Luke 6:43–44; Proverbs 21:8"> A youth when in company with a godly man, will be restrained in his devices, and then his way will be straight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 13:13–16; Psalms 94:9; Psalms 119:18"> The ear hears, and the eye sees: even both of them are the Lord's work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:11; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 10:4"> Love not to speak ill, lest you be cut off: open your eyes, and be filled with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Proverbs 30:11; Matthew 15:4"> The lamp of him that reviles father or mother shall be put out, and his eyeballs shall see darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 28:20"> A portion hastily gotten at first shall not be blessed in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 12:17–19; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Proverbs 24:29"> Say not, I will avenge myself on my enemy; but wait on the Lord, that he may help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:10; Proverbs 11:1; Jezekiel 45:10"> A double weight is an abomination to the Lord; and a deceitful balance is not good in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Jeremias 10:23; Psalms 25:12"> A man's goings are directed of the Lord: how then can a mortal understand his ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–6; Matthew 5:33; Numbers 30:2–16"> It is a snare to a man hastily to consecrate some of his own property: for in that case repentance comes after vowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; Kings II 12:31; Psalms 101:5–8"> A wise king utterly crushes the ungodly, and will bring a wheel upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:11; Hebrews 4:12–13; Job 32:8"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Comp. chap 11:13.">b</a></sup>spirit of man is a light of the Lord, who searches the inmost parts of the belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:14; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 101:1"> Mercy and truth are a guard to a king, and will surround his throne with righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Proverbs 16:31; Leviticus 19:32"> Wisdom is an ornament to young men; and grey hairs are the glory of old men.</span>
