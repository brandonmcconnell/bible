<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> My son, envy not bad men, nor desire to be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For their heart meditates falsehoods, and their lips speak mischiefs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> A house is built by wisdom, and is set up by understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> By discretion the chambers are filled with all precious and excellent wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> A wise man is better than a strong man; and a man who has prudence than a large estate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> War is carried on with generalship, and aid is supplied to the heart of a counsellor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom and good understanding are in the gates of the wise: the wise turn not aside from the mouth of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Jezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> but deliberate in council. Death befalls uninstructed men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Esias 55:7"> The fools also dies in sins; and uncleanness attaches to a pestilent man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremias 51:46"> He shall be defiled in the evil day, and in the day of affliction, until he be utterly consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Esias 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> Deliver them that are led away to death, and redeem them that are appointed to be slain; spare not your help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremias 17:10"> But if you should say, I know not this man; know that the Lord knows the hearts of all; and he that formed breath for all, he knows all things, who renders to every man according to his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 7:15; Song of Songs 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> My son, eat honey, for the honeycomb is good, that your throat may be sweetened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremias 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> Thus shall you perceive wisdom in your soul: for if you find it, your end shall be good, and hope shall not fail you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Esias 32:18; Kings I 23:20–23"> Bring not an ungodly man into the dwelling of the righteous: neither be deceived by the feeding of the belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Michaeas 7:8–10"> For a righteous man will fall seven times, and rise again: but the ungodly shall be without strength in troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obdias 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> If your enemy should fall, rejoice not over him, neither be elated at his overthrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zacharias 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> For the Lord will see it, and it will not please him, and he will turn away his wrath from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Rejoice not in evil-doers, neither be envious of sinners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For the evil man shall have no posterity: and the light of the wicked shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My son, fear God and the king; and do not disobey either of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Osee 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For they will suddenly punish the ungodly, and who can know the vengeance inflicted by both?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. omits to the end.">a</a></sup>&#91;A son that keeps the commandment shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. be outside of.">b</a></sup>escape destruction; for such an one has fully received it. Let no falsehood be spoken by the king from the tongue; yes, let no falsehood proceed from his tongue. The king's tongue is a sword, and not one of flesh; and whoever shall be given up to it shall be destroyed: for if his wrath should be provoked, he destroys men with cords, and devours men's bones, and burns them up as a flame, so that they are not even fit to be eaten by the young eagles. My son, reverence my words, and receive them, and repent.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> And this thing I say to you that are wise for you to learn: It is not good to have respect of persons in judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Esias 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> He that says of the ungodly, He is righteous, shall be cursed by peoples, and hateful amongst the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; Kings I 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> But they that reprove him shall appear more excellent, and blessing shall come upon them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> and men will kiss lips that answer well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; Kings III 5:17–18; Kings III 6:7"> Prepare your works for your going forth, and prepare yourself for the field; and come after me, and you shall rebuild your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Be not a false witness against your fellow citizen, neither exaggerate with your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, As he has treated me, so will I treat him, and I will avenge myself on him for that wherein he has injured me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> A foolish man is like a farm, and a senseless man is like a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Esias 5:5; Jeremias 4:3"> If you let him alone, he will altogether remain barren and covered with weeds;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. grass.">c</a></sup>and he becomes destitute, and his stone walls are broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Afterwards I reflected, I looked that I might receive instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> The sluggard says, I slumber a little, and I sleep a little, and for a little while I fold my arms across my breast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> But if you do this, your poverty will come speedily; and your lack like a swift courier.</span>
