<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:1; Luke 10:20; Kings III 1:47"> A fair name is better than much wealth, and good favour is above silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:15; Proverbs 14:31; Kings I 2:7"> The rich and the poor meet together; but the Lord made them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:12; Proverbs 14:16; Proverbs 29:1"> An intelligent man seeing a bad man severely punished is himself instructed, but fools pass by and are punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; James 4:10; Psalms 34:9–10"> The fear of the Lord is the offspring of wisdom, and wealth, and glory, and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; 1 John 5:18; Proverbs 16:17"> Thistles and snares are in perverse ways; but he that keeps his soul will refrain from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 4:1; Proverbs 22:16; Matthew 18:25"> The rich will rule over the poor, and servants will lend to their own masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Job 4:8; Esias 30:31"> He that sows wickedness shall reap troubles; and shall fully receive the punishment of his deeds.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See 2 Cor 9:7. Compare Heb.">a</a></sup>God loves a cheerful and liberal man; but a man shall fully prove the folly of his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> He that has pity on the poor shall himself be maintained; for he has given of his own bread to the poor. He that gives liberally secures victory an honour; but he takes away the life of them that posses them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> Cast out a pestilent person from the council, and strife shall go out with him; for when he sits in the council he dishonours all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> The Lord loves holy hearts, and all blameless persons are acceptable with him: a king rules with his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> But the eyes of the Lord preserve discretion; but the transgressor despises wise words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> The sluggard makes excuses, and says, There is a lion in the ways, and murderers in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The mouth of a transgressor is a deep pit; and he that is hated of the Lord shall fall into it. Evil ways are before a man, and he does not like to turn away from them; but it is needful to turn aside from a perverse and bad way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Folly is attached to the heart of a child, but the rod and instruction are then far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> He that oppresses the poor, increases his own substance, yet gives to the rich so as to make it less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Incline your ear to the words of wise men: hear also my word, and apply your heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> that you may know that they are good: and if you lay them to heart, they shall also gladden you on your lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremias 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> That your hope may be in the Lord, and he may make your way known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> And do you too repeatedly record them for yourself on the table of your heart, for counsel and knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> I therefore teach you truth, and knowledge good to hear; that you may answer words of truth to them that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 Peter 3:15.">b</a></sup>question you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zacharias 7:10; Malachias 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Do no violence to the poor, for he is needy: neither dishonour the helpless man in the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> For the Lord will plead his cause, and you shall deliver your soul in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Be not companion to a furious man; neither lodge with a passionate man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; Psalms 106:35"> lest you learn of his ways, and get snares to your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Become not surety from respect of a man's person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Kings IV 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> For if those have not whence to give compensation, they will take the bed that is under you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10"> Remove not the old<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. eternal.">c</a></sup>landmarks, which your fathers placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 11:28; Matthew 25:21; Proverbs 12:24"> It is fit that an observant man and one diligent in his business should attend on kings, and not attend on slothful men.</span>
