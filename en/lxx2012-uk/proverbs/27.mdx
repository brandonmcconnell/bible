<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 4:13–16; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> Boast not of to-morrow; for you know not what the next day shall bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Corinthians 10:12"> Let your neighbour, and not your own mouth, praise you; a stranger, and not your own lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; 1 John 3:12; Genesis 49:7"> A stone is heavy, and sand cumbersome; but a fool's wrath is heavier than both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Proverbs 14:30; James 3:14–16"> Wrath is merciless, and anger sharp: but envy can bear nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:23; 1 Timothy 5:20; Galatians 2:14"> Open reproofs are better than secret love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19; Job 5:17–18"> The wounds of a friend are more to be trusted than the spontaneous kisses of an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:16–17; Numbers 11:18–20; Numbers 21:5"> A full soul scorns honeycombs; but to a hungry soul even bitter things appear sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:16; Esias 16:2; Jonas 1:10–17"> As when a bird flies down from its own nest, so a man is brought into bondage whenever he estranges himself from his own place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23–24; Proverbs 16:21; Psalms 23:5"> The heart delights in ointments and wines and perfumes: but the soul is broken by calamities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Proverbs 17:17; Kings III 12:6–8"> Your own friend, and your father's friend, forsake not; and when you are in distress go not into your brother's house: better is a friend that is near than a brother living far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Psalms 119:42; Proverbs 23:15–16"> Son, be wise, that your heart may rejoice; and remove you from yourself reproachful words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Proverbs 18:10; Hebrews 11:7"> A wise man, when evils are approaching, hides himself; but fools pass on, and will be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Proverbs 6:1–4; Proverbs 22:26–27"> Take away the man's garment, (for a scorner has passed by) whoever lays waste another's goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 16:16–19; Acts 12:22–23; Jeremias 28:2–4"> Whosoever shall bless a friend in the morning with a loud voice, shall seem to differ nothing from one who curses him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 21:19"> On a stormy day drops of rain drive a man out of his house; so also does a railing woman drive a man out of his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 12:3"> The north wind is sharp, but it is called by name propitious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:24; Proverbs 27:9; Job 4:3–4"> Iron sharpens iron; and a man sharpens his friend's countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:7; 2 Timothy 2:6; Song of Songs 8:12"> He that plants a fig tree shall eat the fruits of it: so he that waits on his own master shall be honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:22–25; Psalms 33:15; Mark 7:21"> As faces are not like other faces, so neither are the thoughts of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:8; 1 John 2:16; Proverbs 30:15–16"> Hell and destruction are not filled; so also are the eyes of men insatiable.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. omits to ver 21.">a</a></sup>He that fixes his eye is an abomination to the Lord; and the uninstructed do not restrain their tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 17:3; Zacharias 13:9; Psalms 66:10"> Fire is the trial for silver and gold; and a man is tried by the mouth of them that praise him. The heart of the transgressor seeks after mischiefs; but an upright heart seeks knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 5:3; Proverbs 23:35; Esias 1:5"> Though you scourge a fool, disgracing him in the midst of the council, you will still in no wise remove his folly from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2; John 21:15–17; Chronicles II 26:10"> Do you thoroughly know the number of your flock, and pay attention to your herds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 23:5; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; Sophonias 1:18"> For a man has not strength and power for ever; neither does he transmit it from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Psalms 104:14"> Take care of the herbage in the field, and you shall cut grass, and gather the mountain hay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 31:20"> that you may have wool of sheep for clothing: pay attention to the land, that you may have lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 30:8–9"> My son, you have from me words very useful for your life, and for the life of your servants.</span>
