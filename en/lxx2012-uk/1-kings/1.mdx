<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:1–2; Chronicles I 29:27–28; Chronicles I 23:1"> And king David was old and advanced in days, and they covered him with clothes, and he was not warmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:11; Genesis 16:5; Michaeas 7:5"> And his servants said, Let them seek for the king a young virgin, and she shall wait on the king, and cherish him, and lie with him, and my lord the king shall be warmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Esther 2:4; Esther 2:2"> So they sought for a fair damsel out of all the coasts of Israel; and they found Abisag the Somanite, and they brought her to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:25"> And the damsel was extremely beautiful, and she cherished the king, and ministered to him, but the king knew her not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 15:1; Kings II 3:4; Deuteronomy 17:15–16"> And Adonias the son of Aggith exalted himself, saying, I will be king; and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. made.">a</a></sup>prepared for himself chariots and horses, and fifty men to run before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 3:3–4; Kings I 3:13; Hebrews 12:5–6"> And his father never at any time checked him, saying, Why have you done thus? and he was also very handsome in appearance, and his mother bore him after Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 20:25; Kings III 2:22; Kings I 22:20–23"> And he conferred with Joab the son of Saruia, and with Abiathar the priest, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. came to his assistance.">b</a></sup>followed after Adonias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 4:18; Kings II 23:8–39; Kings II 20:25"> But Sadoc the priest, and Banaeas the son of Jodae, and Nathan the prophet, and Semei, and Resi, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lit. mighty sons. Hebraism.">c</a></sup>mighty men of David, did not follow Adonias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 17:17; Kings II 13:23–27; Kings II 15:11–12"> And Adonias sacrificed sheep and calves and lambs by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="So the Alex. The Vat. renders אבן as if it were אחי or אחה.">d</a></sup>stone of Zoelethi, which was near<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. the fountain of Rogel.">e</a></sup>Rogel: and he called all his brethren, and all the adult men of Juda, servants of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 12:24; Kings III 1:19; Kings II 12:1–15"> But Nathan the prophet, and Banaeas, and the mighty men, and Solomon his brother, he did not call.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 3:4; Kings II 7:12–17; Kings II 12:24–25"> And Nathan spoke to Bersabee the mother of Solomon, saying, Have you not heard that Adonias the son of Aggith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">f</a></sup>reigns, and our lord David<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. knew.">g</a></sup>knows it not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Acts 27:31; Genesis 19:17"> And now come, let me, I pray, give you counsel, and you shall rescue your life, and the life of your son Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 1:30; Kings III 1:17; Psalms 132:11–12"> Haste, and go in to king David, and you shall speak to him, saying, Have not you, my lord, O king, sworn to your handmaid, saying, Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne? why then does Adonias reign?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1; Kings III 1:17–27"> And behold, while you are still speaking there with the king, I also will come in after you, and will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. fulfil.">h</a></sup>confirm your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 1:1"> So Bersabee went in to the king into the chamber: and the king was very old, and Abisag the Somanite was ministering to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 25:23; Kings I 24:8; Kings I 20:41"> And Bersabee bowed, and did obeisance to the king; and the king said, What is your request?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 1:13; Kings III 1:30; 1 Peter 3:6"> And she said, My lord, you did swear by the Lord your God to your handmaid, saying, Your son Solomon shall reign after me, and shall sit upon my throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 1:24; Kings III 1:11; Acts 3:17"> And now, behold, Adonias reigns, and you, my lord, O king, know it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 1:25; Kings III 1:7–10"> And he has sacrificed calves and lambs and sheep in abundance, and has called all the king's sons, and Abiathar the priest and Joab the commander-in-chief of the host; but Solomon your servant he has not called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:1; Psalms 123:2; Chronicles I 22:8–10"> And you, my lord, O king, —the eyes of all Israel are upon you, to tell them who shall sit upon the throne of my lord the king after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 2:10; Deuteronomy 31:16; Genesis 15:15"> And it shall come to pass, when my lord the king shall sleep with his fathers, that I and Solomon my son shall be offenders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 1:16–18; Genesis 24:15; Daniel 9:20"> And behold, while she was yet talking with the king, Nathan the prophet came. And it was reported to the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 1:16; 1 Peter 2:17; Romans 13:7"> Behold, Nathan the prophet is here: and he came in to the king's presence, and did obeisance to the king with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 1:17–18; Kings III 1:5; Kings III 1:13–14"> And Nathan said, My lord, O king, did you say, Adonias shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings I 10:24; Kings III 1:19; Kings III 1:9"> For he has gone down today, and has sacrificed calves and lambs and sheep in abundance, and has called all the king's sons, and the chiefs of the army, and Abiathar the priest; and, behold, they are eating and drinking before him, and they said, Long live king Adonias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings III 1:8; Kings III 1:10; Kings II 7:2"> But he has not invited me your servant, and Sadoc the priest, and Banaeas the son of Jodae, and Solomon your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:15; Kings III 1:24; Kings IV 4:27"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. if.">i</a></sup>Has this matter happened by the authority of my lord the king, and have you not made known to your servant who shall sit upon the throne of my lord the king after him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And king David answered and said, Call me Bersabee: and she came in before the king, and stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 4:9; Psalms 136:24; Kings I 14:45"> And the king swore, and said, As the Lord lives who redeemed my soul out of all affliction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings III 1:13; Kings III 1:17"> as I swore to you by the Lord God of Israel, saying, Solomon your son shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne in my stead, so will I do this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 5:10; Daniel 3:9"> And Bersabee bowed with her face to the ground, and did obeisance to the king, and said, Let my lord king David live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings III 1:8; Kings III 1:38; Kings III 1:26"> And king David said, Call me Sadoc the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Banaeas the son of Jodae: and they came in before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:30; Kings III 1:38; Chronicles II 33:14"> And the king said to them, Take the servants of your lord with you, and mount my son Solomon upon my own mule, and bring him down to Gion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 1:25; Kings II 15:10; Kings I 10:1"> And there let Sadoc the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him to be king over Israel, and do you⌃ sound the trumpet, and you⌃ shall say, Let king Solomon live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 1:17; Psalms 72:1–2; Kings III 1:13"> And he shall sit upon my throne, and reign in my stead: and I have given charge that he should be for a prince over Israel and Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 28:6; 1 Corinthians 14:16; Psalms 72:19"> And Banaeas the son of Jodae answered the king and said, So let it be: may the Lord God of my lord the king confirm it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings I 20:13; Kings III 1:47; Joshua 1:17"> As the Lord was with my lord the king, so let him be with Solomon, and let him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. magnify.">j</a></sup>exalt his throne beyond the throne of my lord king David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings II 8:18; Kings III 1:8; Kings III 1:33"> And Sadoc the priest went down, and Nathan the prophet, and Banaeas son of Jodae, and the Cherethite, and the Phelethite, and they mounted Solomon upon the mule of king David, and led him away to Gion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 89:20; Kings III 1:34; Chronicles I 29:22"> And Sadoc the priest took the horn of oil out of the tabernacle, and anointed Solomon, and blew the trumpet; and all the people said, Let king Solomon live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Kings IV 11:14; Zacharias 9:9; Kings IV 11:20"> And all the people went up after him, and they danced in choirs, and rejoiced with great joy, and the earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. burst.">k</a></sup>quaked with their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Job 15:21–22; Luke 17:26–29; Acts 21:31"> And Adonias and all his guests heard, and they had just left off eating: and Joab heard the sound of the trumpet, and said, What means the voice of the city in tumult?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings II 15:36; Kings II 17:17; Kings II 18:27"> While he was yet speaking, behold, Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came in: and Adonias said, Come in, for you are a mighty man, and you come to bring glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Jonathan answered and said, Verily our lord king David has made Solomon king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and the king has sent with him Sadoc the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Banaeas the son of Jodae, and the Cherethite, and the Phelethite, and they have mounted him on the king's mule;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Kings III 1:40; Daniel 5:26–28; Kings I 4:5"> and Sadoc the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him in Gion, and have gone up thence rejoicing, and the city resounded: this is the sound which you⌃ have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Chronicles I 29:23; Psalms 132:11; Kings III 1:13"> And Solomon is seated upon the throne of the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 47:31; Kings III 1:37; Ezra 6:10"> And the servants of the king have gone in to bless our lord king David, saying, God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. do good or make good.">l</a></sup>make the name of Solomon better than your name, and make his throne greater than your throne; and the king worshipped upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Kings III 3:6; Psalms 132:11–12; Psalms 145:2"> Moreover thus said the king, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who has this day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. given.">m</a></sup>appointed one of my seed sitting on my throne, and my eyes see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Esias 21:4–5; Proverbs 28:1; Daniel 5:4–6"> And all the guests of Adonias were dismayed, and every man went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Kings III 2:28; Exodus 21:14; Exodus 27:2"> And Adonias feared because of Solomon, and arose, and departed, and laid hold on the horns of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And it was reported to Solomon, saying, Behold, Adonias fears king Solomon, and holds the horns of the altar, saying, Let Solomon swear to me this day, that he will not kill his servant with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Kings I 14:45; Kings II 14:11; Proverbs 13:6"> And Solomon said, If he should be a valiant man, there shall not a hair of his fall to the ground; but if evil be found in him, he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Kings III 1:16; Kings II 14:24; Kings II 1:2"> And king Solomon sent, and they brought him away from the altar; and he went in and did obeisance to king Solomon: and Solomon said to him, Go to your house.</span>
