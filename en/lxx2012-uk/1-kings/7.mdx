<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:11; Chronicles II 2:13–14; Kings III 7:40"> And king Solomon sent, and took Chiram out of Tyre,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:14; Chronicles II 4:16; Exodus 31:2–6"> the son of a widow woman; and he was of the tribe of Nephthalim, and his father was a Tyrian; a worker in brass, and accomplished in are and skill and knowledge to work every work in brass: and he was brought in to king Solomon, and he wrought all the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 52:21; Chronicles II 3:15–17; Kings III 7:21"> And he cast the two pillars for the porch of the house: eighteen cubits was the height of each pillar, and a circumference of fourteen cubits encompassed it, even the thickness of the pillar: the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, embossed or hollow work.">b</a></sup>flutings were four fingers wide, and thus was the other pillar formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 38:19; Chronicles II 4:12–13; Exodus 38:17"> And he made two molten capitals to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. give.">c</a></sup>put on the heads of the pillars: five cubits was the height of one chapiter, and five cubits was the height of the other chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 28:24–25; Exodus 28:22"> And he made two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. nets.">d</a></sup>ornaments of network to cover the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. chapiter.">e</a></sup>capitals of the pillars; even a net for one chapiter, and a net for the other chapiter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 28:14; Exodus 39:15–18; Exodus 28:22"> And hanging work, two rows of brazen pomegranates,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="q.d. netted.">f</a></sup>formed with network, hanging work, row upon row: and thus he framed the ornaments for the second chapiter. 21 And he set up the pillars of the porch of the temple: and he set up the one pillar, and called its name Jachum: and he set up the second pillar, and called its name Boloz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 7:22; Kings III 6:32–35; Kings III 6:18"> And on the heads of the pillars he made lily-work against the porch, of four cubits,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:16; Chronicles II 4:13; Jeremias 52:22–23"> and a chamber over both the pillars, and above the sides an addition equal to the chamber in width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:2; Kings IV 25:13; Jeremias 52:17"> And he made the sea, ten cubits from one rim to the other, the same was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. round in a circle.">g</a></sup>completely circular round about: its height was five cubits, and its circumference thirty-three cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 6:18; Chronicles II 4:3; Exodus 37:17–22"> And stays underneath its rim round about compassed it ten cubits round;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 52:20; Chronicles II 4:4–5; Matthew 28:19"> And there were twelve oxen under the sea: three looking to the north, and three looking to the west, and three looking to the south, and three looking to the east: and all their hinder parts were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Lit. to or into the house.">h</a></sup>inward, and the sea was above upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:5; Kings III 7:38; Kings III 6:18"> and its rim was as the work of the rim of a cup, a lily-flower, and the thickness of it was a span.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:14; Kings IV 25:13; Jeremias 52:17"> And he made ten brazen bases: five cubits was the length of one base, and four cubits the breadth of it, and its height was six cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And this work of the bases was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. shut up.">i</a></sup>formed with a border the them, and there was a border between the ledges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Osee 5:14; Jezekiel 10:14; Exodus 37:7"> And upon their borders between the projection were lions, and oxen, and cherubs: and on the projections, even so above, and also below were the places of lions and oxen, hanging work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 10:10–13; Jezekiel 3:13; Jezekiel 1:15–21"> And there were four brazen wheels to one base; and there were brazen bases, and their four sides answering to them, side<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, bearings, or shoulders; A.V. undersetters.">j</a></sup>pieces under the bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And there were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. hands.">k</a></sup>axles in the wheels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. in.">l</a></sup>under the base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the height of one wheel was a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jezekiel 1:18; Jezekiel 1:16"> And the work of the wheels was as the work of chariot wheels: their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. their hands and their backs.">m</a></sup>axles, and their felloes, and the rest of their work, were all molten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The four side pieces were at the four corners of each base; its shoulders were formed of the base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And on the top of the base half a cubit was the size of it, there was a circle on the top of the base, and there was the top of its spaces and its borders: and it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. was opened.">n</a></sup>was open at the top of its spaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 7:29; Kings III 6:29; Jezekiel 41:18–20"> And its borders were cherubs, and lions, and palm-trees, upright, each was joined in front and within and round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> According to the same form he made all the ten bases, even one order and one measure to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 40:11–12; Zacharias 13:1; 1 John 1:7"> And he made ten brazen lavers, each laver containing forty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. gallons. The Gr. seems to have this meaning, but the Heb. word means more.">o</a></sup>baths, and measuring four cubits, each laver placed on a several base throughout the ten bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:10; Chronicles II 4:6"> And he put five bases on the right side of the house, and five on the left side of the house: and the sea was placed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. on the shoulder-piece of the house on the right.">p</a></sup>on the right side of the house eastward in the direction of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:11–16; Exodus 24:6; Exodus 39:32–43"> And Chiram made the cauldrons, and the pans, and the bowls; and Chiram finished making all the works that he wrought for king Solomon in the house of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Kings III 7:15–22; Chronicles II 4:12"> two pillars and the wreathen works of the pillars on the heads of the two pillars; and the two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. nets.">q</a></sup>networks to cover both the wreathen works of the flutings that were upon the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings III 7:20"> The four hundred pomegranates for both the networks, two rows of pomegranates for one network, to cover both the wreathen works of the bases belonging to both pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And the ten bases, and the ten lavers upon the bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Kings III 7:23; Kings III 7:25"> And one sea, and the twelve oxen under the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Exodus 38:3; Chronicles II 4:16"> And the cauldrons, and pans, and bowls, and all the furniture, which Chiram made for king Solomon for the house of the Lord: and there were eight and forty pillars of the house of the king and of the house of the Lord: all the works of the king which Chiram made were entirely of brass.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. Brenton places verse 46 after verse 47 - here shown as 46).">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:14; Chronicles I 22:3; Chronicles II 4:18"> There was no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gr. weight.">s</a></sup>reckoning of the brass of which he made all these works, from the very great abundance, there was no end of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Gr. weights.">t</a></sup>weight of the brass. 46 In the country round about Jordan did he cast them, in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="thick part  of the land.">u</a></sup>clay land between Socchoth and Sira.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–16; Chronicles II 4:8; Exodus 39:36"> And king Solomon took the furniture which Chiram made for the house of the Lord, the golden altar, and the golden table of show bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Chronicles II 4:7; Chronicles II 4:20; Zacharias 4:11–14"> And he put the five candlesticks on the left, and five on the right in front of the oracle, being of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Lit. golden, shut up.">v</a></sup>pure gold, and the lamp-stands, and the lamps, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Perhaps small vessels with lips for pouring oil.">w</a></sup>snuffers of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; Chronicles II 4:21–22; Exodus 27:3"> And there were made the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or, rather work about the door.">x</a></sup>porches, and the nails, and the bowls, and the spoons, and the golden censers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Gr. shut up.">y</a></sup>of pure gold: and the panels of the doors of the innermost part of the house, even the holy of holies, and the golden doors of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:1; Kings II 8:7–11; Chronicles I 28:11–18"> So the work of the house of the Lord which Solomon wrought was finished; and Solomon brought in the holy things of David his father, and all the holy things of Solomon; he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Gr. gave.">z</a></sup>put the silver, and the gold, and the furniture, into the treasures of the house of the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Gr. See note at verse 1 for the following verses shown as 1) to 12).">aa</a></sup>1) And Solomon built a house for himself in thirteen years. 2) And he built the house with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Lit. thicket.">ab</a></sup>wood of Libanus; its length was one hundred cubits, and its breadth was fifty cubits, and its height was of thirty cubits, and it was made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="2) Gr. of.">ac</a></sup>with three rows of cedar pillars, and the pillars had<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="2) Or, shoulders, or shoulder pieces.">ad</a></sup>side-pieces of cedar. 3) And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="3) Gr. ceiled or wainscoted the house.">ae</a></sup>formed the house with chambers above on the sides of the pillars, and the number of the pillars was each row forty and five, 4) and there were three chambers, and space against space in three rows. 5) And all the doors and spaces formed like chambers were square, and from door to door was a correspondence in three rows. 6) And he made the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="6) Or, portico.">af</a></sup>porch of the pillars, they were fifty cubits long and fifty broad, the porch joining them in front; and the other pillars and the thick beam were in front of the house by the porches. 7) And there was the Porch of seats where he would judge, the porch of judgement. 8) And their house where he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="8) Gr. will.">ag</a></sup>would dwell, had one court communicating with these according to this work; and he built the house for the daughter of Pharao whom Solomon had taken, according to this porch. 9) All these were of costly stones, sculptured at intervals within even from the foundation even to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="9) Gr. capitals, mouldings, etc.">ah</a></sup>top, and outward to the great court, 10) founded with large costly stones, stones of ten cubits and eight cubits long. 11) And above with costly stones, according to the measure of hewn stones, and with cedars. 12) There were three rows of hewn stones round about the great hall, and a row of sculptured cedar: and Solomon finished all his house.</span>
