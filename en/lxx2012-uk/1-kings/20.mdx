<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 15:18; Kings III 15:20; Kings III 22:31"> And Nabuthai the Jezraelite had a vineyard, near the threshing floor of Achaab king of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 36:2–22; Kings IV 19:9; Esias 37:9–10"> And Achaab spoke to Nabuthai, saying, Give me your vineyard, and I will have it for a garden of herbs, for it is near my house: and I will give you another vineyard better than it; or if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. it is pleasing before you.">a</a></sup>it please you, I will give you money, the price of this your vineyard, and I will have it for a garden of herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Esias 10:13–14"> And Nabuthai said to Achaab, My God forbid me that I should give you the inheritance of my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 15:11–13; Deuteronomy 28:48; Kings I 13:6–7"> And the spirit of Achaab was troubled, and he lay down upon his bed, and covered his face, and ate no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jezabel his wife went in to him, and spoke to him, saying, Why is your spirit troubled, and why do you eat no bread?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 25:34; Joel 3:5; Kings IV 18:31–32"> And he said to her, Because I spoke to Nabuthai the Jezraelite, saying, Give me your vineyard for money; or if you will, I will give you another vineyard for it: and he said, I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 5:7; Proverbs 6:14; Kings III 8:1"> And Jezabel his wife said to him, Do you now thus act the king over Israel? arise, and eat bread, and be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, like yourself.">b</a></sup>your own master, and I will give you the vineyard of Nabuthai the Jezraelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And she wrote a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. book.">c</a></sup>letter in the name of Achaab, and sealed it with his seal, and sent the letter to the elders, and to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Possibly nobles is here meant.">d</a></sup>freemen who lived with Nabuthai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. had been written.">e</a></sup>it was written in the letters, saying, Keep a fast, and set Naboth in a chief place amongst the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 19:2; Exodus 11:8; Kings IV 19:23–24"> And set two men, sons of transgressors, before him, and let them testify against him, saying, He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Prof. Lee on the word ברך in Job 2:9 Heb. Gram. p.92.">f</a></sup>blessed God and the king: and let them lead him forth, and stone him, and let them die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Esias 10:15–16; Kings I 17:44–47"> And the men of his city, the elders, and the nobles who lived in his city, did as Jezabel sent to them, and as it had been written in the letters which she sent to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 16:9; Kings III 20:16; Proverbs 31:4–5"> And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. called.">g</a></sup>proclaimed a fast, and set Nebuthai in a chief place amongst the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 20:28; Jezekiel 20:22; Esias 7:1–9"> And two men, sons of transgressors, came in, and sat opposite him, and bore witness against him, saying, You have blessed God and the king. And they led him forth out of the city, and stoned him with stones, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings I 17:50; Genesis 14:14–16; Kings III 18:44"> And they sent to Jezabel, saying, Nabuthai is stoned, and is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 7:16; Kings I 14:2; Judges 7:7"> And it came to pass, when Jezabel heard it, that she said to Achaab, Arise,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. inherit.">h</a></sup>take possession of the vineyard of Nabuthai the Jezraelite, who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. sold it not.">i</a></sup>would not sell it to you: for Nebuthai is not alive, for he is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16–17; Osee 4:11; Kings III 16:9"> And it came to pass, when Achaab heard that Nabuthai the Jezraelite was dead, that he tore his garments, and put on sackcloth. And it came to pass afterward, that Achaab arose and went down to the vineyard of Nabuthai the Jezraelite,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. to inherit it.">j</a></sup>to take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 20:14–15; Kings III 20:19"> And the Lord spoke to Eliu the Thesbite, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 14:8–12; Kings I 14:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> Arise, and go down to meet Achaab king of Israel, who is in Samaria, for he is in the vineyard of Nabuthai, for he has gone down there to take possession of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And you shall speak to him, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. these things.">k</a></sup>Thus says the Lord, Forasmuch as you have slain and taken possession, therefore thus says the Lord, In every place where the swine and the dogs have licked the blood of Nabuthai, there shall the dogs lick your blood; and the harlots shall wash themselves in your blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 30:16–17; Kings IV 19:36; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> And Achaab said to Eliu, Have you found me, my enemy? and he said, I have found you: because you have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. foolishly, or in vain.">l</a></sup>wickedly sold yourself to work evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 3:18; Judges 3:28; Kings IV 3:24"> behold, I bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. evils.">m</a></sup>evil upon you: and I will kindle a fire after you, and I will utterly destroy every male of Achaab, and him that is shut up and him that is left in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 11:1; Kings III 20:26; Kings III 20:13"> And I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. give.">n</a></sup>make your house as the house of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, and as the house of Baasa son of Achia, because of the provocations wherewith you have provoked me, and caused Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 14:23; Kings I 4:8; Psalms 50:21–22"> And the Lord spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or, to Jezabel.">o</a></sup>of Jezabel, saying, The dogs shall devour her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or, by.">p</a></sup>within the fortification of Jezrael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 22:31; Proverbs 21:30; Kings III 20:16"> Him that is dead of Achaab in the city shall the dogs eat, and him that is dead of him in the field shall the birds of the sky eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 10:3"> But Achaab did wickedly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. who sold, etc.">q</a></sup>in that he sold himself to do that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as his wife Jezabel led him astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 13:17; Kings III 20:22; Kings I 4:1"> And he did very abominably in following after the abominations, according to all that the Amorite did, whom the Lord utterly destroyed from before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:5; Kings I 13:5–8; Kings I 14:2"> And because of the word, Achaab was pierced with sorrow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. the face, as it were, of the Lord.">r</a></sup>before the Lord, and he both went weeping, and tore his garment, and girded sackcloth upon his body, and fasted; he put on sackcloth also in the day that he struck Nabuthai the Jezraelite, and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 20:13; Kings III 17:18; Jezekiel 12:16"> And the word of the Lord came by the hand of his servant Eliu concerning Achaab, and the Lord said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:17; Chronicles II 28:6; Psalms 10:16"> Have you seen how Achaab has been pricked to the heart before me? I will not bring on the evil in his days, but in his son's days will I bring on the evil.</span>
