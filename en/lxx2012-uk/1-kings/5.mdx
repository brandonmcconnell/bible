<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 14:1; Chronicles II 2:3; Kings III 5:10"> And Chiram king of Tyre sent his servants to anoint Solomon in the room of David his father, because Chiram always loved David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:3"> And Solomon sent to Chiram, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:3; Chronicles I 22:8; Psalms 110:1"> You knew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, that my father David, etc.">a</a></sup>my father David, that he could not build a house to the name of the Lord my God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. from the face of. Hebraism.">b</a></sup>because of the wars that compassed him about, until the Lord put them under the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, steps.">c</a></sup>soles of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:9; Kings III 4:24; Acts 9:31"> And now the Lord my God has given me rest round about; there is no one plotting against me, and there is no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. ἀπαντήμα. See συναντήματα, Ex. 9:14.">d</a></sup>evil trespass against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:12; Chronicles I 22:10; Kings II 7:12–13"> And, behold, I intend to build a house to the name of the Lord my God, as the Lord God spoke to my father David, saying, Your son whom I will set on your throne in your place, he shall build a house to my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:8; Kings III 6:9–10; Romans 12:17"> And now command, and let men cut wood for me out of Libanus: and, behold, my servants shall be with your servants, and I will give you the wages of your service, according to all that you shall say, because you know that we have no one skilled in cutting timber like the Sidonians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24; Esias 9:6; Chronicles II 2:11–12"> And it came to pass, as soon as Chiram heard the words of Solomon, that he rejoiced greatly, and said, Blessed be God today, who has given to David a wise son over this numerous people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 6:34; Chronicles II 3:5; Kings II 6:5"> And he sent to Solomon, saying, I have listened concerning all that you have sent to me for: I will do all your will: as for timber of cedar and fir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Jezekiel 27:17; Acts 12:20"> my servants shall bring them down from Libanus to the sea: I will form them into rafts, and bring them to the place which you shall send to me about; and I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Lit. shake them off.">e</a></sup>land them there, and you shall take them up: and you shall do my will, in giving bread to my household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So Chiram gave to Solomon cedars, and fir trees, and all his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:10; Kings III 4:22"> And Solomon gave to Chiram twenty thousand measures of wheat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some read μαχὰλ, or μαχὰθ, as if from מכרח cibus.">f</a></sup>as food for his house, and twenty thousand baths of beaten oil thus Solomon gave to Chiram yearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings III 3:12; Amos 1:9; James 1:5"> And the Lord gave wisdom to Solomon as he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. spoke to him.">g</a></sup>promised him; and there was peace between Chiram and Solomon, and they made a covenant between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 9:15; Kings III 4:6"> And the king raised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Lit. tribute, etc.">h</a></sup>a levy out of all Israel, and the levy was thirty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 4:6–19; Chronicles I 27:1–15"> And he sent them to Libanus, ten thousand taking turn every month: they were a month in Libanus and two months at home: and Adoniram was over the levy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 9:20–22; Chronicles II 2:17–18; Chronicles II 8:7–9"> And Solomon had seventy thousand bearers of burdens, and eighty thousand hewers of stone in the mountain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 9:23; Chronicles II 2:2"> besides the rulers that were appointed over the works of Solomon, there were three thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Heb. 300. Alex. 500.">i</a></sup>six hundred<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. overseers of the people.">j</a></sup>masters who wrought in the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 6:7; Chronicles I 22:2; Esias 28:16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Verse 17 is not in Vat. The following is from Alex.">k</a></sup>And the king commanded and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. bring.">l</a></sup>brought great stones, precious stones for the foundation of the house, and unhewn stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Jezekiel 27:9; Psalms 83:7"> And they prepared the stones and the timber during three years.</span>
