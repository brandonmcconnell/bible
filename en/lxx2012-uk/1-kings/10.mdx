<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; Chronicles II 9:1–12"> And the queen of Saba heard of the name of Solomon, and the name of the Lord, and she came to try him with riddles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 25:23; Psalms 4:4; Job 4:2"> And she came to Jerusalem with a very great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. force.">a</a></sup>train; and there came camels bearing spices, and very much gold, and precious stones: and she came in to Solomon, and told him all that was in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5–6; Matthew 13:11; Kings III 10:1"> And Solomon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. related to her all her words.">b</a></sup>answered all her questions: and there was not a question overlooked by the king which he did not answer her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Kings III 3:28; Chronicles II 9:3–4"> And the queen of Saba saw all the wisdom of Solomon, and the house which he built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:16; Kings III 4:22–23; Kings IV 16:18"> and the provision of Solomon and the sitting of his attendants, and the standing of his servants, and his raiment, and his cupbearers, and his whole burnt offering which he offered in the house of the Lord, and she was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, in ecstasy.">c</a></sup>utterly amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:5–6"> And she said to king Solomon, It was a true report which I heard in my land of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. word.">d</a></sup>words and your wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; John 20:25–29; 1 John 3:2"> But I believed not them that told me, until I came and my eyes saw: and, behold, the words as they reported to me are not the half: you have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. added good things to them.">e</a></sup>exceeded in goodness all the report which I heard in my land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Proverbs 10:21; Luke 11:31"> Blessed are your wives, blessed are these your servants who stand before you continually, who hear all your wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 72:2; Kings III 5:7; Kings II 8:15"> Blessed be the Lord your God, who has taken pleasure in you, to set you upon the throne of Israel, because the Lord loved Israel to establish him for ever; and he has made you king over them, to execute judgement with justice, and in their causes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 10:2; Matthew 2:11; Kings III 9:14"> And she gave to Solomon one hundred and twenty talents of gold, and very many spices, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. precious stone.">f</a></sup>precious stones: there had not come any other spices so abundant as those which the queen of Saba gave to king Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 9:27–28; Chronicles II 8:18; Psalms 45:9"> And the ship of Chiram which brought the gold from Suphir, brought very much hewn timber and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles I 25:1–31; Psalms 150:3–5; Revelation 14:2–3"> And the king made the hewn timber into buttresses of the house of the Lord and the king's house, and lyres and harps for singers: such hewn timber had not come upon the earth, nor have been seen anywhere until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Matthew 15:28; Psalms 20:4"> And king Solomon gave to the queen of Saba all that she desired, whatever she asked, besides all that he had given her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="of his royal bounty, A.V.">g</a></sup>by the hand of king Solomon: and she returned, and came into her own land, she and her servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:13–28; Kings III 9:28"> And the weight of gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:10; Chronicles I 9:24; Esias 21:13"> Besides the tributes of them that were subjects, both merchants and all the kings of the country beyond the river, and of the princes of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 14:26–28; Chronicles II 12:9–10; Chronicles II 9:15–16"> And Solomon made three hundred spears of beaten gold: three hundred shekels of gold were upon one spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 7:2; Kings III 14:26"> And three hundred<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. arms.">h</a></sup>shields of beaten gold: and three pounds of gold were in one shield: and the king put them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Kings III 10:22; Revelation 20:11"> And the king made a great ivory throne, and gilded it with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The throne had six steps, and calves in bold relief to the throne behind it, and side-pieces on either hand of the place of the seat, and two lions standing by the side-pieces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 23:24"> and twelve lions standing there on the six steps on either side: it was not so done in any other kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 10:17; Kings III 7:2; Chronicles II 9:20–22"> And all the vessels made by Solomon were of gold, and the lavers were golden, and all the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon were of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Lit. 'shut up with gold,' a frequent phrase in Heb. and Gr. for 'pure gold.' See chap. 6:20.">i</a></sup>pure gold; there was no silver, for it was not accounted of in the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 22:48; Genesis 10:4; Chronicles II 20:36–37"> For Solomon had a ship of Tharsis in the sea with the ships of Chiram: one ship came to the king every three years out of Tharsis, laden with gold and silver, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. turned.">j</a></sup>wrought stones, and hewn stones.<br />This was the arrangement of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This word more commonly means 'spoil' in the O.T.">k</a></sup>provision which king Solomon fetched to build the house of the Lord, and the house of the king, and the wall of Jerusalem, and the citadel; to fortify the city of David, and Assur, and Magdal, and Gazer, and Baethoron the upper, and Jethermath, and all the cities of the chariots, and all the cities of the horsemen, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. work.">l</a></sup>fortification of Solomon which he purposed to build in Jerusalem and in all the land, so that none of the people should rule over him that was left of the Chettite and the Amorite, and the Pherezite, and the Chananite, and the Evite, and the Jebusite, and the Gergesite, who were not of the children of Israel, their descendants who had been left with him in the land, whom the children of Israel could not utterly destroy; and Solomon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. reduced them to tribute.">m</a></sup>made them tributaries until this day. But of the children of Israel Solomon made nothing; for they were the warriors, and his servants and rulers, and captains of the third order, and the captains of his chariots, and his horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 3:12–13; Colossians 2:2–3; Kings III 4:29–34"> And Solomon increased beyond all the kings of the earth in wealth and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 3:12; James 1:5; Kings III 3:28"> And all the kings of the earth sought the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. face.">n</a></sup>presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom which the Lord had put into his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 42:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> And they brought every one their gifts, vessels of gold, and raiment, and stacte, and spices, and horses, and mules, a rate year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:25; Kings III 4:26; Deuteronomy 17:16"> And Solomon had four thousand mares for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen: and he put them in the cities of his chariots, and with the king in Jerusalem: and he ruled over all the kings from the river to the land of the Philistines, and to the borders of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:15–17; Deuteronomy 17:17; Chronicles II 9:27"> And the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. gave.">o</a></sup>made gold and silver in Jerusalem as stones, and he made cedars as the sycamores in the plain for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Chronicles II 9:28; Esias 31:1–3"> And the goings forth of Solomon's horsemen was also out of Egypt, and the king's merchants were of Thecue; and they received them out of Thecue at a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Kings IV 7:6–7; Malachias 1:1"> And that which proceeded out of Egypt went up thus, even a chariot for one hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for fifty shekels of silver: and thus for all the kings of the Chettians, and the kings of Syria, they came out by sea.</span>
