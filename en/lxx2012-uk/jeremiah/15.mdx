<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 14:14; Jezekiel 14:20–21; Jeremias 14:11"> And the Lord said to me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before my face, my soul could not be towards them: dismiss this people, and let them go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 43:11; Zacharias 11:9; Revelation 13:10"> And it shall be, if they say to you, Whither shall we go forth? then you shall say to them, Thus says the Lord; As many as are for death, to death; and as many as are for famine, to famine; and as many as are for the sword, to the sword; and as many as are for captivity, to captivity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:26; Leviticus 26:22; Leviticus 26:16"> And I will punish them with four kinds of death, says the Lord, the sword to kill, and the dogs to tear, and the wild beasts of the earth, and the birds of the sky to devour and destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 24:9; Kings IV 23:26–27; Kings IV 24:3–4"> And I will deliver them up for distress to all the kingdoms of the earth, because of Manasses son of Ezekias king of Juda, for all that he did in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 51:19; Naum 3:7; Jeremias 21:7"> Who will spare you, O Jerusalem? and who will fear for you? or who will turn back to ask for your welfare?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 7:24; Sophonias 1:4; Esias 1:4"> You have turned away from me, says the Lord, you will go back: therefore I will stretch out my hand, and will destroy you, and will no more spare them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; Jeremias 51:2; Esias 41:16"> And I will completely scatter them; in the gates of my people they are bereaved of children: they have destroyed my people because of their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 3:25–1; Jeremias 6:4–5; Luke 21:35"> Their widows have been multiplied more than the sand of the sea: I have brought young men against the mother, even distress at noon-day: I have suddenly cast upon her trembling and anxiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 2:5; Esias 47:9; Lamentations 1:1"> She that bore seven is spent; her soul has fainted under trouble; her sun is gone down while it is yet noon; she is ashamed and disgraced: I will give the remnant of them to the sword before their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 6:22; Exodus 22:25; Proverbs 26:2"> Woe is me, my mother! you have born me as some man of strife, and at variance with the whole earth; I have not helped others, nor has any one helped me; my strength has failed amongst them that curse me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Jeremias 29:11–14; Psalms 106:46"> Be it so, Lord, in their prosperity; surely I stood before you in the time of their calamities, and in the time of their affliction, for their good against the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 40:9; Jeremias 21:4–5; Esias 45:9"> Will iron be known? whereas your strength is a brazen covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 17:3; Psalms 44:12; Esias 52:3"> Yes, I will give your treasures for a spoil as a recompence, because of all your sins and that in all your borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 16:13; Deuteronomy 32:22; Jeremias 17:4"> And I will enslave you to your enemies round about, in a land which you have not known; for a fire has been kindled out of my wrath; it shall burn upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 12:3; Psalms 69:7–9; Luke 21:17"> O Lord, remember me, and visit me, and vindicate me before them that persecute me; do not bear long with them; know how I have met with reproach for your sake, from those who set at nothing your words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 10:9–10; Jezekiel 3:1–3; Psalms 119:101–103"> consume them; and your word shall be to me for the joy and gladness of my heart: for your name has been called upon me, O Lord Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Lamentations 3:28; Psalms 26:4–5"> I have not sat in the assembly of them as they mocked, but I feared because of your power: I sat alone, for I was filled with bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 30:15; Job 34:6; Michaeas 1:9"> Why do they that grieve me prevail against me? my wound is severe; whence shall I be healed? it is indeed become to me as deceitful water, that has no faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zacharias 3:7; Luke 21:15; Leviticus 10:10"> Therefore thus says the Lord, If you will return, then will I restore you, and you shall stand before my face: and if you will bring forth the precious from the worthless, you shall be as my mouth: and they shall return to you; but you shall not return to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 1:18–19; Acts 18:9–10; Jezekiel 3:9"> And I will make you to this people as a strong brazen wall; and they shall fight against you, but they shall by no means prevail against you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Esias 49:24–25; Jeremias 50:34"> for I am with you to save you, and to deliver you out of the hand of wicked men; and I will ransom you out of the hand of pestilent men.</span>
