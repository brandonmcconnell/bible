<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 41:11; Jeremias 40:13; Jeremias 6:13"> THE WORD THAT CAME TO JEREMIAS from the Lord in the days of Joakim, king of Juda, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 36:7; Leviticus 26:22; Acts 8:24"> Go to the house of the Archabin, and you shall bring them to the house of the Lord, into one of the courts, and give them wine to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Proverbs 3:6; Psalms 25:4–5"> So I brought forth Jechonias the son of Jeremin the son of Chabasin, and his brethren, and his sons, and all the family of the Archabin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 12:23; Kings I 3:17–18; Exodus 8:29"> and I brought them into the house of the Lord, into the chamber of the sons of Joanan, the son of Ananias, the son of Godolias, a man of God, who dwells near the house of the princes that are over the house of Maasaeas the son of Selom, who kept the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:50; Judges 11:10; Malachias 3:5"> And I set before them a jar of wine, and cups, and I said, Drink you⌃ wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 7:23; Deuteronomy 5:29; Romans 8:7"> But they said, We will on no account drink wine, for our father Jonadab the son of Rechab commanded us, saying, You⌃ shall on no account drink wine, neither you⌃, nor your sons for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Esias 28:16; Ambacum 2:3"> nor shall you⌃ at all build houses, nor sow any seed, nor shall you⌃ have a vineyard: for you⌃ shall dwell in tents all your days; that you⌃ may live many days upon the land, in which you⌃ sojourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 42:1; Jeremias 41:11–16; Jeremias 43:2–5"> And we listened to the voice of Jonadab our father, so as to drink no wine all our days, we, and our wives, and our sons, and our daughters;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 19:4; Jeremias 42:2; Kings IV 19:6"> and so as to build no houses to dwell in: and we have had no vineyard, nor field, nor seed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 31:28; Jezekiel 36:36; Jeremias 24:6"> but we have lived in tents, and have listened, and done according to all that Jonadab our father commanded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Jeremias 1:19; Jeremias 1:8"> And it came to pass, when Nabuchodonosor came up against the land, that we said we would come in; and we entered into Jerusalem, for fear of the host of the Chaldeans, and for fear of the host of the Assyrians: and we lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Psalms 106:45–46; Proverbs 16:7"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 44:16; Exodus 5:2; Jeremias 42:10"> Thus says the Lord, Go, and say to the men of Juda, and to them that dwell in Jerusalem, Will you⌃ not receive correction to listen to my words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 41:17; Jeremias 4:19; Esias 31:1"> The sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have kept the word which he commanded his children, that they should drink no wine; and they have not drunk it: but I spoke to you early, and you⌃ listened not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Jeremias 44:12–14; Jeremias 42:17"> And I sent to you my servants the prophets, saying, Turn you⌃ every one from his evil way, and amend your practices, and go not after other gods to serve them, and you⌃ shall dwell upon the land which I gave to you and to your fathers: but you⌃ inclined not your ears, and listened not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 44:27; Jezekiel 11:8; Amos 9:1–4"> But the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have kept the command of their father; but this people has not listened to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 42:22; Jeremias 44:28; Jeremias 44:13–14"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Behold, I will bring upon Juda and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the evils which I pronounced against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 7:20; Jeremias 18:16; Jeremias 29:18"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Since the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have listened to the command of their father, to do as their father commanded them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; Esias 30:1–7; Nehemiah 9:26"> there shall never be lacking a man of the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab to stand before my face while the earth remains.</span>
