<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 30:22; Jeremias 30:24; Zacharias 13:9"> Thus has the Lord said concerning MOAB, Woe to Nabau! for it has perished: Cariathaim is taken: Amath and Agath are put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28"> There is no longer any healing for Moab, nor glorying in Esebon: he has devised evils against her: we have cut her off from being a nation, and she shall be completely still: after you shall go a sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Esias 54:8–9; Osee 11:4"> for there is a voice of men crying out of Oronaim, destruction and great ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 33:7; Jeremias 31:13; Amos 5:2"> Moab is ruined, proclaim it to Zogora:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:30"> for Aloth is filled with weeping: one shall go up weeping by the way of Oronaim; you⌃ have heard a cry of destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 50:4–5; Michaeas 4:1–3; Jeremias 50:19"> Flee you⌃, and save your lives, and you⌃ shall be as a wild ass in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Psalms 28:9; Esias 37:31"> Since you have trusted in your strong-hold, therefore you shall be taken: and Chamos shall go forth into captivity, and his priests, and his princes together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:16; Esias 42:16; Jeremias 23:8"> And destruction shall come upon every city, it shall by no means escape; the valley also shall perish, and the plain country shall be completely destroyed, as the Lord has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Jeremias 3:4; Exodus 4:22"> Set marks upon Moab, for she shall be touched with a plague-spot, and all her cities shall become desolate; whence shall there be an inhabitant for her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 40:11; Jezekiel 34:12; Deuteronomy 30:4"> Cursed is the man that does the works of the Lord carelessly, keeping back his sword from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 48:20; Titus 2:14; Esias 44:23"> Moab has been at ease from a child, and trusted in his glory; he has not poured out his liquor from vessel to vessel, and has not gone into banishment, therefore his taste remained in him, and his smell departed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 35:10; Esias 58:11; Joel 3:18"> Therefore, behold, his days come, says the Lord, when I shall send upon him bad leaders, and they shall lead him astray, and they shall utterly break in pieces his possessions, and shall cut his horns asunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Esias 61:3; Esias 51:11"> And Moab shall be ashamed of Chamos, as the house of Israel was ashamed of Baethel their hope, having trusted in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 31:25; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremias 33:9"> How will you⌃ say, We are strong, and men strong for war?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 2:16–18; Jeremias 40:1; Psalms 77:2"> Moab is ruined, even his city, and his choice young men have gone down to slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 2:12; Jeremias 30:3; Hebrews 6:10"> The day of Moab is near at hand, and his iniquity moves swiftly to vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:21; Jeremias 29:11–16; Lamentations 3:18"> Shake the head at him, all you⌃ that are round about him; all of you utter his name; say you⌃, How is the glorious staff broken to pieces, the rod of magnificence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> Come down from your glory, and sit down in a damp place: Daebon shall be broken, because Moab is destroyed: there has gone up against you one to ravage your strong-hold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:31; Jeremias 3:25; Jezekiel 21:12"> Stand by the way, and look, you that dwell in Arer; and ask him that is fleeing, and him that escapes, and say, What has happened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 55:7; Michaeas 7:18–19; Esias 63:15"> Moab is put to shame, because he is broken: howl and cry; proclaim in Arnon, that Moab has perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 50:5; Esias 62:10; Psalms 84:5"> And judgement is coming against the land of Misor, upon Chelon, and Rephas, and Mophas,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 49:4; Jeremias 7:24; Matthew 1:21"> and upon Daebon, and upon Nabau, and upon the house of Daethlathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zacharias 8:3; Esias 1:26; Jeremias 50:7"> and upon Cariathaim, and upon the house of Gaemol, and upon the house of Maon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zacharias 8:4–8; Jezekiel 36:10; Zacharias 2:4"> and upon Carioth, and upon Bosor, and upon all the cities of Moab, far and near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Matthew 11:28; John 4:14"> The horn of Moab is broken, and his arm is crushed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zacharias 4:1–2"> Make you⌃ him drunk; for he has magnified himself against the Lord: and Moab shall clap with his hand, and shall be also himself a laughing stock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Osee 2:23; Zacharias 10:9; Jezekiel 36:9"> For surely Israel was to you a laughing stock, and was found amongst your thefts, because you did fight against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremias 24:6; Jeremias 1:10; Jeremias 18:7–9"> The inhabitants of Moab have left the cities, and lived in rocks; they have become as doves nestling in rocks, at the mouth of a cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Jezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremias 31:30"> And I have heard of the pride of Moab, he has greatly heightened his pride and his haughtiness, and his heart has been lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:20; Esias 3:11; Deuteronomy 24:16"> But I know his works: is it not enough for him? has he not done thus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Jezekiel 37:26; Jeremias 31:31–34"> Therefore howl you⌃ for Moab on all sides; cry out against the shorn men in a gloomy place. I will weep for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 24:6–8; Deuteronomy 5:3; Exodus 19:5"> O vine of Aserema, as with the weeping of Jazer: your branches are gone over the sea, they reached the cities of Jazer: destruction has come upon your fruits, and upon your grape gatherers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> Joy and gladness have been utterly swept off the land of Moab: and though there was wine in your presses, in the morning they trod it not, neither in the evening did they raise the cry of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esias 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> From the cry of Esebon even to Aetam their cities uttered their voice, from Zogor to Oronaim, and their tidings as a heifer of three years old, for the water also of Nebrin shall be dried up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremias 10:16; Esias 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> And I will destroy Moab, says the Lord, as he comes up to the altar, and burns incense to his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Esias 54:9–10; Jeremias 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> Therefore the heart of Moab shall sound as pipes, my heart shall sound as a pipe for the shorn men; forasmuch as what every man has gained has perished from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremias 33:24–26; Esias 40:12; Jeremias 33:22"> They shall all have their heads shaved in every place, and every beard shall be shaved; and all hands shall beat the breasts, and on all loins shall be sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zacharias 14:10; Kings IV 14:13; Jeremias 30:18"> And on all the housetops of Moab, and in his streets shall be mourning: for I have broken him, says the Lord, as a vessel, which is useless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zacharias 2:1–2; Jezekiel 40:8"> How has he changed! how has Moab turned his back! Moab is put to shame, and become a laughing stock, and an object of anger to all that are round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Kings II 15:23; Joel 3:17; Kings IV 11:16"> For thus said the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Carioth is taken, and the strongholds have been taken together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Moab shall perish from being a multitude, because he has magnified himself against the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> A snare, and fear, and the pit, are upon you, O inhabitant of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> He that flees from the terror shall fall into the pit, and he that comes up out of the pit shall even be taken in the snare: for I will bring these things upon Moab in the year of their visitation.</span>
