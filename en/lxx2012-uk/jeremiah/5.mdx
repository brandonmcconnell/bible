<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–32; Chronicles II 16:9; Jezekiel 22:30"> Run you⌃ about in the streets of Jerusalem, and see, and know, and seek in her broad places, if you⌃ can find one, if there is any one that does judgement, and seeks faithfulness; and I will pardon them, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremias 4:2; Osee 4:1–2"> The Lord lives, they say; do they not therefore swear falsely?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 2:30; Jeremias 7:28; Esias 9:13"> O Lord, your eyes are upon faithfulness: you have scourged them, but they have not grieved; you have consumed them; but they would not receive correction: they have made their faces harder than a rock; and they would not return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 8:7; Osee 4:6; Esias 28:9–13"> Then I said, It may be they are poor; for they are weak, for they know not the way of the Lord, or the judgement of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 2:20; James 2:5–7; Matthew 19:23–26"> I will go to the rich men, and will speak to them; for they have known the way of the Lord, and the judgement of God: but, behold, with one consent they have broken the yoke, they have burst the bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 4:7; Ambacum 1:8; Sophonias 3:3"> Therefore has a lion out of the forest struck them, and a wolf has destroyed them even to their houses, and a leopard has watched against their cities: all that go forth from them shall be hunted: for they have multiplied their ungodliness, they have strengthened themselves in their revoltings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Joshua 23:7; Jeremias 2:11"> In what way shall I forgive you for these things? Your sons have forsaken me, and sworn by them that are no gods: and I fed them to the full, and they committed adultery, and lodged in harlots' houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 13:27; Deuteronomy 5:18; Kings II 11:2–4"> They became as wanton horses: they neighed each one after his neighbour's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 9:9; Jeremias 5:29; Esias 1:24"> Shall I not visit for these things? says the Lord: and shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 4:27; Matthew 22:7; Amos 9:8"> Go up upon her battlements, and break them down; but make not a full end: leave her buttresses: for they are the Lord's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 3:20; Esias 48:8; Osee 6:7"> For the house of Israel have indeed dealt treacherously against me, says the Lord: the house of Juda also</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:16; Jezekiel 12:22–28; Jeremias 23:14–17"> have lied to their Lord, and they have said, These things are not so; no evils shall come upon us; and we shall not see sword or famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 14:15; Job 8:2; Jeremias 14:13"> Our prophets became wind, and the word of the Lord was not in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 23:29; Jeremias 1:9; Osee 6:5"> Therefore thus says the Lord Almighty, Because you⌃ have spoken this word, behold, I have made my words in your mouth fire, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Jeremias 4:16; Esias 33:19"> Behold, I will bring upon you a nation from far, O house of Israel, says the Lord; a nation the sound of whose language one shall not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 5:28; Psalms 5:9; Romans 3:13"> They are all mighty men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:33; Osee 8:14"> and they shall devour your harvest, and your bread; and shall devour your sons, and your daughters; and they shall devour your sheep, and your calves, and devour your vineyards, and your fig-plantations, and your olive yards: and they shall utterly destroy your strong cities, wherein you⌃ trusted, with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 5:10; Romans 11:1–5; Jeremias 4:27"> And it shall come to pass in those days, says the Lord your God, that I will not utterly destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Jeremias 22:8–9"> And it shall come to pass, when you⌃ shall say, Therefore has the Lord our God done all these things to us? that you shall say to them, Because you⌃ served strange gods in your land, so shall you⌃ serve strangers in a land that is not yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Proclaim these things to the house of Jacob, and let them be heard in the house of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 28:26; Jezekiel 12:2; Esias 6:9–10"> Hear you⌃ now these things, O foolish and senseless people; who have eyes, and see not; and have ears, and hear not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:9; Job 26:10"> will you⌃ not be afraid of me? says the Lord; and will you⌃ not fear before me, who have set the sand for a bound to the sea, as a perpetual ordinance, and it shall not pass it: yes, it shall rage, but not prevail; and its waves shall roar, but not pass over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 6:28; Deuteronomy 21:18; Psalms 95:10"> But this people has a disobedient and rebellious heart; and they have turned aside and gone back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Joel 2:23; Genesis 8:22"> and they have not said in their heart, Let us fear now the Lord our God, who gives us the early and latter rain, according to the season of the fulfilment of the ordinance of harvest, and has preserved it for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 59:2; Psalms 107:17; Psalms 107:34"> Your transgressions have turned away these things, and your sins have removed good things from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 1:11; Psalms 64:5; Jezekiel 22:2–12"> For amongst my people were found ungodly men; and they have set snares to destroy men, and have caught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Michaeas 1:12; Jeremias 9:6; Osee 12:7–8"> As a snare which has been set is full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit: therefore have they grown great, and become rich:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esias 1:23; Deuteronomy 32:15; Jeremias 7:6"> and they have transgressed the rule of judgement; they have not judged the cause of the orphan, nor have they judged the cause of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremias 5:9; Malachias 3:5; Jeremias 9:9"> Shall I not visit for these things? says the Lord: and shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Osee 6:10; Jeremias 23:14; Esias 1:2"> Shocking and horrible deeds have been done on the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jezekiel 13:6; Jeremias 14:14; Michaeas 2:11"> the prophets utter unrighteous prophecies, and the priests have clapped their hands: and my people has loved to have it thus: and what will you⌃ do for the future.</span>
