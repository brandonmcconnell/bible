<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 27:1; Joshua 9:3; Esias 9:15"> Thus says the Lord; Behold, I stir up against Babylon, and against the Chaldeans dwelling therein, a deadly burning wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Michaeas 3:11; Jezekiel 13:5–16; Jeremias 27:2–12"> And I will send forth against Babylon spoilers, and they shall spoil her, and shall ravage her land. Woe to Babylon round about her in the day of her affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 24:13; Chronicles II 36:10; Daniel 1:2"> Let the archer bend his bow, and him that has armour put it on: and spare you⌃ not her young men, but destroy you⌃ all her host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 22:24; Kings IV 25:27–30; Jeremias 28:2"> And slain men shall fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and men pierced through shall fall without it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 28:1; Jeremias 26:2; Jeremias 19:14"> For Israel and Juda have not been forsaken of their God, of the Lord Almighty; whereas their land was filled with iniquity against the holy things of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Kings III 1:36; Jeremias 11:5"> Flee you⌃ out of the midst of Babylon, and deliver every one his soul: and be not overthrown in her iniquity; for it is the time of her retribution from the Lord; he is rendering to her a recompence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 22:28"> Babylon has been a golden cup in the Lord's hand, causing all the earth to be drunken: the nations have drunk of her wine; therefore they were shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:26–27; Kings III 17:1; Kings III 22:8"> And Babylon is fallen suddenly, and is broken to pieces: lament for her; take balm for her deadly wound, if by any means she may be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:22; Jeremias 6:14; Jeremias 4:10"> We tried to heal Babylon, but she was not healed: let us forsake her, and depart every one to his own country: for her judgement has reached to the heaven, it has mounted up to the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 27:2; Jeremias 28:4; Malachias 3:13"> The Lord has brought forth his judgement: come, and let us declare in Sion the works of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:7; Chronicles II 18:22–23; Jeremias 27:2–12"> Prepare the arrows; fill the quivers: the Lord has stirred up the spirit of the king of the Medes: for his wrath is against Babylon, to destroy it utterly; for it is the Lord's vengeance, it is the vengeance of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 1:2; Daniel 9:2; Kings IV 20:4"> Lift up a standard on the walls of Babylon, prepare the quivers, rouse the guards, prepare the weapons: for the Lord has taken the work in hand, and will execute what he has spoken against the inhabitants of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 149:8; Jeremias 27:15; Lamentations 2:14"> dwelling on many waters, and amidst the abundance of her treasures; your end is come verily into your bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Jeremias 27:6–7; Daniel 2:38"> For the Lord has sworn by his arm, saying, I will fill you with men as with locusts; and they that come down shall cry against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 14:14–15; Jeremias 27:15; Jeremias 23:21"> The Lord made the earth by his power, preparing the world by his wisdom, by his understanding he stretched out the heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 29:32; Genesis 7:4; Exodus 32:12"> At his voice he makes a sound of water in the heaven, and brings up clouds from the extremity of the earth; he makes lightnings for rain, and brings light out of his treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zacharias 1:6; Esias 44:25–26"> Every man has completely lost understanding; every goldsmith is confounded because of his graven images: for they have cast false gods, there is no breath in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They are vain works, objects of scorn; in the time of their visitation they shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Not such is Jacob's portion; for he that formed all things, he is his inheritance; the Lord is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> You scatter for me the weapons of war: and I will scatter nations by you, and will destroy kings by means of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And by you I will scatter the horse and his rider; and by you I will scatter chariots and them that ride in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And by you I will scatter youth and maid; and by you I will scatter man and woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And by you I will scatter the shepherd and his flock; and by you I will scatter the husbandman and his husbandry; and by you I will scatter leaders and the captains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And I will recompense to Babylon and to all the Chaldeans that dwell there all their mischiefs that they have done to Sion before your eyes, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Behold, I am against you, the ruined mountain, that destroys the whole earth; and I will stretch out my hand upon you, and will roll you down upon the rocks, and will make you as a burnt mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they shall not take from you a stone for a corner, nor a stone for a foundation: for you shall be a desolation for ever, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Lift up a standard in the land, sound the trumpet amongst the nations, consecrate the nations against her, raise up kings against her by me, and that for the people of Achanaz; set against her engines of war; bring up against her horses as a multitude of locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Bring up nations against her, even the king of the Medes and of the whole earth, his rulers, and all his captains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The earth has quaked and been troubled, because the purpose of the Lord has risen up against Babylon, to make the land of Babylon a desolation, and uninhabitable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The warrior of Babylon has failed to fight; they shall sit there in the siege; their power is broken; they are become like women; her tabernacles have been set on fire; her bars are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> One shall rush, running to meet another runner, and one shall go with tidings to meet another with tidings, to bring tidings to the king of Babylon, that his city is taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> At the end of his passages they were taken, and his cisterns they have burnt with fire, and his warriors are going forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> For thus says the Lord, The houses of the king of Babylon shall be threshed as a floor in the season; yet a little while, and her harvest shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> He has devoured me, he has torn me asunder, airy darkness has come upon me; Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon has swallowed me up, as a dragon has he filled his belly with my delicacies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> My troubles and my distresses have driven me out into Babylon, shall she that dwells in Sion say; and my blood shall be upon the Chaldeans dwelling there, shall Jerusalem say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Therefore thus says the Lord, Behold, I will judge your adversary, and I will execute vengeance for you; and I will waste her sea, and dry up her fountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And Babylon shall be a desolation, and shall not be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> For they rose up together as lions, and as lions' whelps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> In their heat I will give them a draught, and make them drunk, that they may be stupified, and sleep an everlasting sleep, and not awake, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And bring you them down as lambs to the slaughter, and rams with kids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> How has the boast of all the earth been taken and caught in a snare! how has Babylon become a desolation amongst the nations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The sea has come up upon Babylon with the sound of its waves, and she is covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Her cities are become like a dry and trackless land; not so much as one man shall dwell in it, neither shall a son of man lodge in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And I will take vengeance on Babylon, and bring forth out of her mouth what she has swallowed down, and the nations shall no more be gathered to her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> and in Babylon the slain men of all the earth shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Go forth of the land, you⌃ that escape, and stay not; you⌃ that are afar off, remember the Lord, and let Jerusalem come into your mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> We are ashamed, because we have heard our reproach; disgrace has covered our face; aliens are come into our sanctuary, even into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Therefore, behold, the days come, says the Lord, when I will take vengeance upon her graven images: and slain men shall fall in all her land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> For though Babylon should go up as the heaven, and though she should strengthen her walls with her power, from me shall come they that shall destroy her, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> A sound of a cry in Babylon, and great destruction in the land of the Chaldeans:</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> for the Lord has utterly destroyed Babylon, and cut off from her the great voice sounding as many waters: he has consigned her voice to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> For distress has come upon Babylon, her warriors are taken, their bows are useless: for God recompenses them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> The Lord recompenses, and will make her leaders and her wise men and her captains completely drunk, says the King, the Lord Almighty is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Thus says the Lord, The wall of Babylon was made broad, but it shall be completely broken down, and her high gates shall be burnt with fire; and the peoples shall not labour in vain, nor the nations fail in their rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> THE WORD WHICH THE LORD COMMANDED THE PROPHET JEREMIAS to say to Saraeas son of Nerias, son of Maasaeas, when he went from Sedekias king of Juda to Babylon, in the fourth year of his reign. And Saraeas was over the bounties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And Jeremias wrote in a book all the evils which should come upon Babylon, even all these words that are written against Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And Jeremias said to Saraeas, When you are come to Babylon, and shall see and read all these words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> then you shall say, O Lord God, you have spoken against this place, to destroy it, and that there should be none to dwell in it, neither man nor beast; for it shall be a desolation for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> And it shall come to pass, when you shall cease from reading this book, that you shall bind a stone upon it, and cast it into the midst of Euphrates;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> and shall say, Thus shall Babylon sink, and not rise, because of the evils which I bring upon it.</span>
</div>
