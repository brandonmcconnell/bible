<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word that came from the Lord to</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1; Acts 9:6; Jeremias 19:1–2"> Jeremias, saying, Arise, and go down to the potter's house, and there you shall hear my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 15:14; Jonas 1:3; Acts 26:19"> So I went down to the potter's house, and behold, he was making a vessel on the stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:20–23; Jeremias 18:6; Esias 45:9"> And the vessel which he was making with his hands fell: so he made it again another vessel, as it seemed good to him to make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Jeremias 18:4"> Shall I not be able, O house of Israel, to do to you as this potter? behold, as the clay of the potter are you⌃ in my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 1:10; Jeremias 12:14–17; Jeremias 45:4"> If I shall pronounce a decree upon a nation, or upon a kingdom, to cut them off, and to destroy them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:21; Jeremias 26:13; Jonas 3:9–10"> and that nation turn from all their sins, then will I repent of the evils which I purposed to do to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 31:28; Amos 9:11–15; Jeremias 1:10"> And if I shall pronounce a decree upon a nation and kingdom, to rebuild and to plant it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:18; Kings I 2:30; Jezekiel 18:24"> and they do evil before me, so as not to listen to my voice, then will I repent of the good which I spoke of, to do it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 17:13; Esias 55:6–7; Jeremias 35:15"> And now say to the men of Juda, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, Behold, I prepare evils against you, and devise a device against you: let every one turn now from his evil way, and amend your practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 2:25; Jeremias 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> And they said, We will quit ourselves like men, for we will pursue our perverse ways, and we will perform each the lusts of his evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 5:30; Osee 6:10; Jeremias 23:14"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Enquire now amongst the nations, who has heard such very horrible things as the virgin of Israel has done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 6:68"> Will fertilising streams fail to flow from a rock, or snow fail from Libanus? will water violently impelled by the wind turn aside?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 6:16; Jeremias 2:32; Osee 11:2"> For my people have forgotten me, they have offered incense in vain, and they fail in their ways, leaving the ancient tracks, to enter upon impassable paths;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:7; Jeremias 25:9; Jeremias 19:8"> to make their land a desolation, and a perpetual hissing; all that go through it shall be amazed, and shall shake their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 13:24; Jeremias 2:27; Job 27:21"> I will scatter them before their enemies like an east wind; I will show them the day of their destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremias 11:19; Malachias 2:7"> Then they said, Come, and let us devise a device against Jeremias; for the law shall not perish from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, and let us strike him with the tongue, and we will hear all his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremias 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> Hear me, O Lord, and hear the voice of my pleading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> Forasmuch as evil is rewarded for good; for they have spoken words against my soul, and they have hidden the punishment they meant for me; remember that I stood before your face, to speak good for them, to turn away your wrath from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> Therefore do you deliver their sons to famine, and gather them to the power of the sword: let their women be childless and widows; and let their men be cut off by death, and their young men fall by the sword in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Sophonias 1:10–11"> Let there be a cry in their houses: you shall bring upon them robbers suddenly: for they have formed a plan to take me, and have hidden snares for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> And you, Lord, know all their deadly counsel against me: account not their iniquities guiltless, and blot not out their sins from before you: let their weakness come before you; deal with them in the time of your wrath.</span>
