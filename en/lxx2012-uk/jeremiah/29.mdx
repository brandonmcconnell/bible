<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 9:20; Acts 15:23; Hebrews 13:22"> THUS SAITH THE LORD AGAINST THE PHILISTINES;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 22:24–28; Jeremias 27:20; Chronicles II 36:9–10"> Behold, waters come up from the north, and shall become a sweeping torrent, and it shall sweep away the land, and its fulness; the city, and them that dwell in it: and men shall cry and all that dwell in the land shall howl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:11; Chronicles II 34:20; Jeremias 39:14"> at the sound of his rushing, at the sound of his hoofs, and at the rattling of his chariots, at the noise of his wheels: the fathers turned not to their children because of the weakness of their hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 24:5; Esias 10:5–6; Esias 45:7"> in the day that is coming to destroy all the Philistines: and I will utterly destroy Tyre and Sidon and all the rest of their allies: for the Lord will destroy the remaining inhabitants of the islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 29:28; Jeremias 29:10; Jezekiel 28:26"> Baldness is come upon Gaza; Ascalon is cast away, and the remnant of the Enakim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 24:3–4; Genesis 24:51; Genesis 28:1–4"> How long will you strike, O sword of the Lord? how long will it be ere you are quiet? return into your sheath, rest, and be removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:1–2; Ezra 6:10; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> How shall it be quiet, whereas the Lord has given it a commission against Ascalon, and against the regions on the sea-coast, to awake against the remaining countries! CONCERNING IDUMEA, thus says the Lord; There is no longer wisdom in Thaeman, counsel has perished from the wise ones, their wisdom is gone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; Jeremias 5:31; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> their place has been deceived. Dig deep for a dwelling, you⌃ that inhabit Daedam, for he has wrought grievously: I brought trouble upon him in the time at which I visited him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 27:15; Jeremias 29:31; Jeremias 29:23"> For grape gatherers are come, who shall not leave you a remnant; as thieves by night, they shall lay their hand upon your possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zacharias 7:5; Jeremias 24:6–7"> For I have stripped Esau, I have uncovered their secret places; they shall have no power to hide themselves, they have perished each by the hand of his brother, my neighbour, and it is impossible</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 55:8–12; Psalms 33:11; Michaeas 4:12"> for your fatherless one to be left to live, but I shall live, and the widows trust in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Jeremias 33:3; Psalms 50:15"> For thus says the Lord; They who were not appointed to drink the cup have drunk it; and you shall by no means be cleared:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:29–31; Psalms 119:2; Matthew 7:7"> for by myself I have sworn, says the Lord, that you shall be in the midst of her an impassable land, and a reproach, and a curse; and all her cities shall be desert for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:1–38; Jeremias 30:3; Chronicles I 28:9"> I have heard a report from the Lord, and he has sent messengers to the nations, saying, Assemble yourselves, and come against her; rise you⌃ up to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 29:8–9; Jeremias 28:1–17; Jezekiel 1:3"> I have made you small amongst the nations, utterly contemptible amongst men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 38:2–3; Jeremias 38:17–23; Jezekiel 17:12–21"> Your insolence has risen up against you, the fierceness of your heart has burst the holes of the rocks, it has seized upon the strength of a lofty hill; for as an eagle he set his nest on high: thence will I bring you down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 24:8–10; Jeremias 29:18; Jeremias 24:1–3"> And Idumea shall be a desert: every one that passes by shall hiss at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:8; Jeremias 15:4; Jeremias 42:18"> As Sodom was overthrown and Gomorrha and they that sojourned in her, says the Lord Almighty, no man shall dwell there, nor shall any son of man inhabit there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 6:19; Jeremias 26:5; Jeremias 7:24–26"> Behold, he shall come up as a lion out of the midst of Jordan to the place of Aetham: for I will speedily drive them from it, and do you⌃ set the young men against her: for who is like me? and who will withstand me? and who is this shepherd, who shall confront me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 24:5; Michaeas 4:10; Jezekiel 3:15"> Therefore hear you⌃ the counsel of the Lord, which he has framed against Idumea; and his device, which he has devised against the inhabitants of Thaeman: surely the least of the sheep shall be swept off; surely their dwelling shall be made desolate for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 14:14–15; Lamentations 2:14; Jeremias 29:8–9"> For at the sound of their fall the earth was scared, and the cry of the sea was not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esias 65:15; Daniel 3:6; Daniel 3:21"> Behold, he shall look upon her as an eagle, and spread forth his wings over her strongholds; and the heart of the mighty men of Idumea shall be in that day as the heart of a woman in her pangs.</span>
