<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 10:21; Zacharias 11:15–17; Jezekiel 34:2–10"> Woe to the shepherds that destroy and scatter the sheep of their pasture!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 32:34; Jeremias 21:12; Michaeas 7:4"> Therefore thus says the Lord against them that tend my people; You⌃ have scattered my sheep, and driven them out, and you⌃ have not visited them: behold, I will take vengeance upon you according to your evil practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:14–15; Jeremias 32:37; Esias 43:5–6"> And I will gather in the remnant of my people in every land, whither I have driven them out, and will set them in their pasture; and they shall increase and be multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 3:14–15; 1 Peter 1:5; Jezekiel 34:23–31"> And I will raise up shepherds to them, who shall feed them: and they shall fear no more, nor be alarmed, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Esias 4:2; Esias 9:7"> Behold, the days come, says the Lord, when I will raise up to David a righteous branch, and a king shall reign and understand, and shall execute judgement and righteousness on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Jeremias 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> In his days both Juda shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell securely: and this is his name, which the Lord shall call him, Josedec amongst the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 16:14–15; Jeremias 23:3; Esias 43:18–19"> Therefore, behold, the days come, says the Lord, when they shall no more say, The Lord lives, who brought up the house of Israel out of the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 43:5–6; Amos 9:14–15; Jezekiel 34:13"> but The Lord lives, who has gathered the whole seed of Israel from the north land, and from all the countries whither he had driven them out, and has restored them into their own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ambacum 3:16; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 60:3"> My heart is broken within me; all my bones are shaken: I am become as a broken-down man, and as a man overcome with wine, because of the Lord, and because of the excellence of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 4:2–3; Psalms 107:34; Jeremias 9:2"> For because of these things the land mourns; the pastures of the wilderness are dried up; and their course is become evil, and so also their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Sophonias 3:4; Jeremias 6:13; Matthew 21:12–13"> For priest and prophet are defiled; and I have seen their iniquities in my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 13:16; Jeremias 11:23; Psalms 35:6"> Therefore let their way be to them slippery and dark: and they shall be tripped up and fall in it: for I will bring evils upon them, in the year of their visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 2:8; Esias 9:16; Kings III 18:18–21"> And in the prophets of Samaria I have seen lawless deeds; they prophesied by Baal, and led my people Israel astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 1:9–10; Jeremias 29:23; Jezekiel 22:25"> Also in the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen horrible things: as they committed adultery, and walked in lies, and strengthened the hands of many, that they should not return each from his evil way: they are all become to me as Sodom, and the inhabitants thereof as Gomorrha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 8:14; Jeremias 9:15; Lamentations 3:5"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Behold, I will feed them with pain, and give them bitter water to drink: for from the prophets of Jerusalem has defilement gone forth into all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 14:14; Jezekiel 13:6; Galatians 1:8–9"> Thus says the Lord Almighty, Listen not to the words of the prophets: for they frame a vain vision for themselves; they speak from their own heart, and not from the mouth of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Michaeas 3:11; Jeremias 13:10; Amos 9:10"> They say to them that reject the word of the Lord, There shall be peace to you; and to all that walk after their own lusts, and to everyone that walks in the error of his heart, they have said, No evil shall come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 23:22; Esias 40:13–14; Amos 3:7"> For who has stood in the counsel of the Lord, and seen his word? who has listened, and heard?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 30:23; Jeremias 25:32; Amos 1:14"> Behold, there is an earthquake from the Lord, and anger proceeds to a convulsion, it shall come violently upon the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 30:24; Esias 55:11; Zacharias 1:6"> And the Lord's wrath shall return no more, until he have accomplished it, and until he have established it, according to the purpose of his heart: at the end of the days they shall understand it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 14:14; Jeremias 27:15; Jeremias 23:32"> I sent not the prophets, yet they ran: neither spoke I to them, yet they prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 23:18; Zacharias 1:4; Jeremias 25:5"> But if they had stood in my counsel, and if they had listened to my words, then would they have turned my people from their evil practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–10; Kings III 20:23; Kings III 20:28"> I am a God near at hand, says the Lord, and not a God afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 139:7; Psalms 90:8"> Shall any one hide himself in secret places, and I not see him? Do I not fill heaven and earth? says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 23:32; Jeremias 23:28; Jeremias 29:8"> I have heard what the prophets say, what they prophesy in my name, saying falsely, I have seen a night vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1–2; Jeremias 14:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> How long shall these things be in the heart of the prophets that prophesy lies, when they prophesy the purposes of their own heart?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 8:33–34; Judges 3:7; Acts 13:8"> who devise that men may forget my law by their dreams, which they have told every one to his neighbour, as their fathers forgot my name in the worship of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Timothy 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13"> The prophet who has a dream, let him tell his dream; and he in whom is my word spoken to him, let him tell my word truly: what is the chaff to the corn? so are my words, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Jeremias 5:14; Luke 24:32"> Behold, are not my words as fire? says the Lord; and as an axe cutting the rock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Jezekiel 13:8; Jeremias 14:14–15"> Behold, I am therefore against the prophets, says the Lord God, that steal my words every one from his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremias 23:17; Chronicles II 18:10–12; Chronicles II 18:5"> Behold, I am against the prophets that put forth prophecies of mere words, and slumber their sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 13:7–18; Sophonias 3:4; Lamentations 2:14"> Therefore, behold, I am against the prophets that prophesy false dreams, and have not told them truly, and have caused my people to err by their lies, and by their errors; yet I sent them not, and commanded them not; therefore, they shall not profit this people at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Malachias 1:1; Jeremias 12:7; Ambacum 1:1"> And if this people, or the priest, or the prophet, should ask, What is the burden of the Lord? then you shall say to them, You⌃ are the burden, and I will dash you down, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zacharias 13:3; Lamentations 2:14"> As for the prophet, and the priests, and the people, who shall say, The burden of the Lord, I will even take vengeance on that man, and on his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremias 31:34; Jeremias 42:4; Jeremias 33:3"> Thus shall you⌃ say every one to his neighbour, and every one to his brother, What has the Lord answered? and, what has the Lord said?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 10:10; 2 Peter 3:16; Esias 3:8"> And do you⌃ name no more the burden of the Lord; for his own word shall be a man's burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But therefore, say you⌃, has the Lord our God spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:13–14"> Therefore thus says the Lord our God; Because you⌃ have spoken this word, The burden of the Lord, and I sent to you, saying, you⌃ shall not say, The burden of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremias 23:33; Jezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 13:13"> therefore, behold, I will seize, and dash down you and the city which I gave to you and your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremias 20:11; Jezekiel 5:14–15; Jeremias 42:18"> And I will bring upon you an everlasting reproach, and everlasting disgrace, which shall not be forgotten.</span>
