<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:6; John 15:7; 2 Peter 1:5–8"> Your teeth are as flocks of shorn sheep, that have gone up from the washing; all of them bearing twins, and there is not a barren one amongst them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:7; Song of Songs 5:16; Joshua 2:18"> Your lips are as a thread of scarlet, and your speech is comely: like the rind of a pomegranate is your cheek without your veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Songs 7:4; Ephesians 4:15–16; Nehemiah 3:19"> Your neck is as the tower of David, that was built for an armoury: a thousand shields hang upon it, and all darts of mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:16; Song of Songs 6:3; Song of Songs 7:3"> Your two breasts are as two twin fawns, that feed amongst the lilies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:17; Song of Songs 4:14; Luke 1:78"> Until the day dawn, and the shadows depart, I will betake me to the mountain of myrrh, and to the hill of frankincense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:15; Song of Songs 4:1; Jude 1:24"> You are all fair, my companion, and there is no spot in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Song of Songs 2:13; Psalms 45:10"> Come from Libanus, my bride, come from Libanus: you shall come and pass from the top of Faith, from the top of Sanir and Hermon, from the lions' dens, from the mountains of the leopards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:1–2; Song of Songs 4:12; Proverbs 5:19–20"> My sister, my spouse, you have ravished my heart; you have ravished my heart with one of your eyes, with one chain of your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:2–4; Song of Songs 7:6; Galatians 5:22"> How beautiful are your breasts, my sister, my spouse! how much more beautiful are your breasts than wine, and the smell of your garments than all spices!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:1; Song of Songs 7:9; Genesis 27:27"> Your lips drop honeycomb, my spouse: honey and milk are under your tongue; and the smell of your garments is as the smell of Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:15–18; Revelation 21:27; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> My sister, my spouse is a garden enclosed; a garden enclosed, a fountain sealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:14; Song of Songs 6:11; Ecclesiastes 2:5"> Your shoots are a garden of pomegranates, with the fruit of choice berries; camphor, with spikenard:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 30:23; Song of Songs 4:6; Song of Songs 6:2"> spikenard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon; with all woods of Libanus, myrrh, aloes, with all chief spices:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:10; John 7:38; John 4:14"> a fountain of a garden, and a well of water springing and gurgling from Libanus.</span>
