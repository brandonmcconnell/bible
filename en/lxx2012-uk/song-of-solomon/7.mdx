<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:4; Song of Songs 4:8–9; Psalms 144:12"> Your neck is as an ivory tower; your eyes are as pools in Esebon, by the gates of the daughter of many: your nose is as the tower of Libanus, looking towards Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 35:2; Song of Songs 4:1; Michaeas 7:14"> Your head upon you is as Carmel, and the curls of your hair like scarlet; the king is bound in the galleries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:15–16; Psalms 45:11; Song of Songs 4:10"> How beautiful are you, and how sweet are you, my love!</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; John 14:21–23; Song of Songs 2:5"> I said, I will go up to the palm tree, I will take hold of its high boughs: and now shall your breasts be as clusters of the vine, and the smell of your nose of apples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:16; Esias 62:8–9; Acts 4:31–32"> and your throat as good wine, going well with my kinsman, suiting my lips and teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:11; Song of Songs 2:16; Song of Songs 6:3"> I am my kinsman's, and his desire is towards me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:8; Song of Songs 2:10–13; Song of Songs 1:4"> Come, my kinsman, let us go forth into the field; let us lodge in the villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:11; Ephesians 6:24; Song of Songs 7:6"> Let us go early into the vineyards; let us see if the vine has flowered, if the blossoms have appeared, if the pomegranates have blossomed; there will I give you my breasts.</span>
