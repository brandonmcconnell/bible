<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 36:4; Esias 32:7; Psalms 7:14–16"> They meditated troubles, and wrought wickedness on their beds, and they put it in execution with the daylight; for they have not lifted up their hands to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 5:8; Jeremias 22:17; Amos 8:4"> And they desired fields, and plundered orphans, and oppressed families, and spoiled a man and his house, even a man and his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 8:3; Amos 5:13; Esias 2:11–12"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Behold, I devise evils against this family, out of which you⌃ shall not lift up your necks, neither shall you⌃ walk upright speedily: for the time is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ambacum 2:6; Jeremias 9:10; Jeremias 9:17–21"> In that day shall a parable be taken up against you, and a plaintive lamentation shall be uttered, saying, We are thoroughly miserable: the portion of my people has been measured out with a line, and there was none to hinder him so as to turn him back; your fields have been divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Joshua 18:4; Joshua 18:10"> Therefore you shall have no one to cast a line for the lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 2:12; Esias 30:10; Acts 5:40"> Weep not with tears in the assembly of the Lord, neither let any weep for these things; for he shall not remove the reproaches,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 15:2; Jeremias 15:16"> who says, The house of Jacob has provoked the Spirit of the Lord; are not these his practices? Are not the Lord's words right with him? and have they not proceeded correctly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 120:6–7; Kings II 20:19; Esias 9:21"> Even beforetime my people withstood him as an enemy against his peace; they have stripped off his skin to remove hope in the conflict of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ambacum 2:14; Jezekiel 39:21; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> The leaders of my people shall be cast forth from their luxurious houses; they are rejected because of their evil practices; draw you⌃ near to the everlasting mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Psalms 106:38; Kings IV 17:6"> Arise you, and depart; for this is not your rest because of uncleanness: you⌃ have been utterly destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 5:31; Esias 30:10–11; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> you⌃ have fled, no one pursuing you: your spirit has framed falsehood, it has dropped on you for wine and strong drink. But it shall come to pass, that out of the dropping of this people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Michaeas 4:6–7; Michaeas 7:18; Osee 1:11"> Jacob shall be completely gathered with all his people: I will surely receive the remnant of Israel; I will cause them to return together, as sheep in trouble, as a flock in the midst of their fold: they shall rush forth from amongst men through the breach made before them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 52:12; Zacharias 9:14–15; Esias 45:1–2"> they have broken through, and passed the gate, and gone out by it: and their king has gone out before them, and the Lord shall lead them.</span>
