<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 1:10; Jeremias 5:4–5; Psalms 82:1–5"> And he shall say, Hear now these words, you⌃ heads of the house of Jacob, and you⌃ remnant of the house of Israel; is it not for you to know judgement?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:27; Proverbs 28:4; Psalms 53:4"> who hate good, and seek evil; who tear their skins off them, and their flesh off their bones:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 14:4; Jezekiel 11:6–7; Jezekiel 11:3"> even as they devoured the flesh of my people, and stripped their skins off them, and broke their bones, and divided them as flesh for the cauldron, and as meat for the pot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; Esias 1:15; Jezekiel 8:18"> thus they shall cry to the Lord, but he shall not listen to them; and he shall turn away his face from them at that time, because they have done wickedly in their practices against themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 6:14; Jeremias 14:14–15; Jezekiel 22:25–29"> Thus says the Lord concerning the prophets that lead my people astray, that bit with their teeth, and proclaim peace to them; and when nothing was put into their mouth, they raised up war against them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 8:20–22; Amos 8:9–10; Esias 29:10"> therefore there shall be night to you instead of a vision, and there shall be to you darkness instead of prophecy; and the sun shall go down upon the prophets, and the day shall be dark upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zacharias 13:4; Esias 44:25; Kings I 28:6"> And the seers of night-visions shall be ashamed, and the prophets shall be laughed to scorn: and all the people shall speak against them, because there shall be none to listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 58:1; 1 Corinthians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> Surely I will strengthen myself with the Spirit of the Lord, and of judgement, and of power, to declare to Jacob his transgressions, and to Israel his sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 1:23; Psalms 58:1–2; Proverbs 17:15"> Hear now these words, you⌃ chiefs of the house of Jacob, and the remnant of the house of Israel, who hate judgement, and pervert all righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ambacum 2:9–12; Jeremias 22:13–17; Matthew 27:25"> who build up Sion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 6:13; Esias 1:23; Michaeas 3:5"> The heads thereof have judged for gifts, and the priests thereof have answered for hire, and her prophets have divined for silver: and yet they have rested on the Lord, saying, Is not the Lord amongst us? no evil shall come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 26:18; Psalms 79:1; Michaeas 4:1–2"> Therefore on your account Sion shall be ploughed as a field, and Jerusalem shall be as a storehouse of fruits, and the mountain of the house as a grove of the forest.</span>
