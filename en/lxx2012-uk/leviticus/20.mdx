<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:27"> You shall also say to the children of Israel, If there shall be any of the children of Israel, or of those who have become proselytes in Israel, who shall give of his seed to Moloch, let him be surely put to death; the nation upon the land shall stone him with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Jezekiel 5:11; Jezekiel 23:38–39"> And I will set my face against that man, and will cut him off from his people, because he has given of his seed to Moloch, to defile my sanctuary, and profane the name of them that are consecrated to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Acts 17:30; Kings III 20:42"> And if the natives of the land should in anyway overlook that man in giving of his seed to Moloch, so as not to put him to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Osee 2:13; Osee 2:5"> then will I set my face against that man and his family, and I will destroy him, and all who have been of one mind with him, so that he should go a whoring to the princes, from their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Esias 8:19; Leviticus 20:27"> And the soul that shall follow those who have in them divining spirits, or enchanters, so as to go a whoring after them; I will set my face against that soul, and will destroy it from amongst its people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> And you⌃ shall be holy, for I the Lord your God am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; James 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> And you⌃ shall observe my ordinances, and do them: I am the Lord that sanctifies you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Mark 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:16"> Every man who shall speak evil of his father or of his mother, let him die the death; has he spoken evil of his father or his mother? he shall be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:4–5; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Deuteronomy 5:18"> Whatever man shall commit adultery with the wife of a man, or whoever shall commit adultery with the wife of his neighbour, let them die the death, the adulterer and the adulteress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; Deuteronomy 27:23; Amos 2:7"> And if any one should lie with his father's wife, he has uncovered his father's nakedness: let them both die the death, they are guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; Leviticus 18:23; Genesis 38:18"> And if any one should lie with his daughter-in-law, let them both be put to death; for they have wrought impiety, they are guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9; Leviticus 18:22; Jude 1:7"> And whoever shall lie with a male as with a woman, they have both wrought abomination; let them die the death, they are guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 18:17; Deuteronomy 27:23; Joshua 7:15"> Whosoever shall take a woman and her mother, it is iniquity: they shall burn him and them with fire; so there shall not be iniquity amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Exodus 22:19"> And whoever shall lie with a beast, let him die the death; and you⌃ shall kill the beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Exodus 19:13; Hebrews 12:20"> And whatever woman shall approach any beast, so as to have connexion with it, you⌃ shall kill the woman and the beast: let them die the death, they are guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:9; Deuteronomy 27:22; Jezekiel 22:11"> Whosoever shall take his sister by his father or by his mother, and shall see her nakedness, and she see his nakedness, it is a reproach: they shall be destroyed before the children of their family; he has uncovered his sister's nakedness, they shall bear their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:24; Leviticus 18:19; Jezekiel 18:6"> And whatever man shall lie with a woman that is set apart for a flux, and shall uncover her nakedness, he has uncovered her fountain, and she has uncovered the flux of her blood: they shall both be destroyed from amongst their generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6; Leviticus 18:12–30; Exodus 6:20"> And you shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister, or of the sister of your mother; for that man has uncovered the nakedness of one near akin: they shall bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 18:14; Job 18:19; Luke 23:29"> Whosoever shall lie with his near kinswoman, has uncovered the nakedness of one near akin to him: they shall die childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:16; Matthew 14:3–4"> Whoever shall take his brother's wife, it is uncleanness; he has uncovered his brother's nakedness; they shall die childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 18:25–28; Jezekiel 36:27; Leviticus 18:4–5"> And keep you⌃ all my ordinances, and my judgements; and you⌃ shall do them, and the land shall not be aggrieved with you, into which I bring you to dwell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Leviticus 18:24; Leviticus 18:30"> And walk you⌃ not in the customs of the nations which I drive out from before you; for they have done all these things, and I have abhorred them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 6:8; Deuteronomy 7:6"> and I said to you, You⌃ shall inherit their land, and I will give it to you for a possession, even a land flowing with milk and honey: I am the Lord your God, who have separated you from all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:3–21; Leviticus 11:1–47; Acts 10:11–15"> And you⌃ shall make a distinction between the clean and the unclean cattle, and between clean and unclean birds; and you⌃ shall not defile your souls with cattle, or with birds, or with any creeping things of the earth, which I have separated for you by reason of uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 20:7; Leviticus 20:24"> And you⌃ shall be holy to me; because I the Lord your God am holy, who separated you from all nations, to be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 20:6; Kings I 28:7–9"> And as for a man or woman whoever of them shall have in them a divining spirit, or be an enchanter, let them both die the death: you⌃ shall stone them with stones, they are guilty.</span>
