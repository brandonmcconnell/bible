<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then he commanded to bring her in where his plate was set; and bade that they should prepare for her of his own meats, and that she should drink of his own wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Judith said, I will not eat thereof, lest there be an offence: but provision shall be made for me of the things that I have brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then Holofernes said to her, If your provision should fail, how should we give you the like? for there be none with us of your nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then said Judith to him As your soul lives, my lord, your handmaid shall not spend those things that I have, before the Lord work by my hand the things that he has determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the servants of Holofernes brought her into the tent, and she slept till midnight, and she arose when it was towards the morning watch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And sent to Holofernes, saving, Let my lord now command that your handmaid may go forth to prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Holofernes commanded his guard that they should not stay her: thus she abode in the camp three days, and went out in the night into the valley of Bethulia, and washed herself in a fountain of water by the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when she came out, she implored the Lord God of Israel to direct her way to the raising up of the children of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So she came in clean, and remained in the tent, until she did eat her meat at evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And in the fourth day Holofernes made a feast to his own servants only, and called none of the officers to the banquet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then said he to Bagoas the eunuch, who had charge over all that he had, Go now, and persuade this Hebrew woman which is with you, that she come to us, and eat and drink with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For, behold, it will be a shame for our person, if we shall let such a woman go, not having had her company; for if we draw her not to us, she will laugh us to scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then went Bagoas from the presence of Holofernes, and came to her, and he said, Let not this fair damsel fear to come to my lord, and to be honoured in his presence, and drink wine, and be merry with us and be made this day as one of the daughters of the Assyrians, which serve in the house of Nabuchodonosor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then said Judith to him, Who am I now, that I should gainsay my lord? surely whatever pleases him I will do speedily, and it shall be my joy to the day of my death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So she arose, and decked herself with her apparel and all her woman's attire, and her maid went and laid soft skins on the ground for her over against Holofernes, which she had received of Bagoas for her daily use, that she might sit and eat upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now when Judith came in and sat down, Holofernes his heart was ravished with her, and his mind was moved, and he desired greatly her company; for he waited a time to deceive her, from the day that he had seen her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then said Holofernes to her, Drink now, and be merry with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So Judith said, I will drink now, my lord, because my life is magnified in me this day more than all the days since I was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then she took and ate and drank before him what her maid had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Holofernes took great delight in her, and drank more wine than he had drunk at any time in one day since he was born.</span>
