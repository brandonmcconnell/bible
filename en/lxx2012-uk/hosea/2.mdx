<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Osee 2:23; Zacharias 13:9"> Say to your brother, My people, and to your sister, Pitied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 50:1; Jezekiel 16:25; Jezekiel 23:45"> Plead with your mother, plead: for she is not my wife, and I am not her husband: and I will remove her fornication out of my presence, and her adultery from between her breasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:22; Esias 32:13–14; Jezekiel 19:13"> that I may strip her naked, and make her again as she was at the day of her birth: and I will make her desolate, and make her as a dry land, and will kill her with thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 13:14; Osee 1:6; Jezekiel 8:18"> And I will not have mercy upon her children; for they are children of fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 1:21; Osee 3:1; Jeremias 44:17–18"> And their mother went a-whoring: she that bore them disgraced them: for she said, I will go after my lovers, that give me my bread and my water, and my garments, and my linen clothes, my oil and my necessaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 19:8; Job 3:23; Lamentations 3:7–9"> Therefore, behold, I hedge up her way with thorns, and I will stop the ways, and she shall not find her path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 2:2; Osee 5:13; Jezekiel 23:4"> And she shall follow after her lovers, and shall not overtake them; and she shall seek them, but shall not find them: and she shall say, I will go, and return to my former husband; for it was better with me than now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 1:3; Osee 4:11; Osee 13:2"> And she knew not that I gave her her corn, and wine, and oil, and multiplied silver to her: but she made silver and gold images for Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Osee 2:3; Aggaeus 1:6–11; Malachias 1:4"> Therefore I will return, and take away my corn in its season, and my wine in its time; and I will take away my raiment and my linen clothes, so that she shall not cover her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:29; Jeremias 13:26; Osee 13:7–8"> And now I will expose her uncleanness before her lovers, and no one shall by any means deliver her out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 1:13–14; Amos 5:21; Jeremias 7:34"> And I will take away all her gladness, her feasts, and her festivals at the new moon, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 7:23; Esias 5:5; Osee 2:5"> And I will utterly destroy her vines, and her fig-trees, all things of which she said, These are my hire which my lovers have given me: and I will make them a testimony, and the wild beasts of the field, and the birds of the sky, and the reptiles of the earth shall devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 13:6; Judges 3:7; Osee 13:1"> And I will recompense on her the days of Baalim, wherein she sacrificed to them, and put on her ear-rings, and her necklaces, and went after her lovers, and forgot me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 40:1–2; Revelation 12:6; Jezekiel 20:35–36"> Therefore, behold, I will cause her to err, and will make her as desolate, and will speak comfortably to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 2:2; Jezekiel 28:26; Osee 11:1"> And I will giver her possessions from thence, and the valley of Achor to open her understanding: and she shall be afflicted there according to the days of her infancy, and according to the days of her coming up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 54:5; Osee 2:7; Jeremias 3:14"> And it shall come to pass in that day, says the Lord, that she shall call me, My husband, and shall no longer call me Baalim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 16:4"> And I will take away the names of Baalim out of her mouth, and their names shall be remembered no more at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:25; Jezekiel 39:9–10; Psalms 46:9"> And I will make for them in that day a covenant with the wild beasts of the field, and with the birds of the sky, and with the reptiles of the earth: and I will break the bow and the sword and the battle from off the earth, and will cause you to dwell safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 21:2; Esias 54:8–10"> And I will betroth you to myself for ever; yes, I will betroth you to myself in righteousness, and in judgement, and in mercy, and in tender compassions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 5:20; John 17:3; Jeremias 24:7"> and I will betroth you to myself in faithfulness: and you shall know the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zacharias 8:12; Esias 65:24; Romans 8:32"> And it shall come to pass in that day, says the Lord, I will listen to the heaven, and it shall listen to the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joel 2:19; Osee 1:4; Osee 1:11"> and the earth shall listen to the corn, and the wine, and the oil; and they shall listen to Jezrael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Zacharias 13:9"> And I will sow her to me on the earth; and will love her that was not loved, and will say to that which was not my people, You are my people; and they shall say, You are the Lord my God.</span>
