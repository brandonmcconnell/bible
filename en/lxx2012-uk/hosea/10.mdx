<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 8:11; Leviticus 26:1; Philippians 2:21"> Israel is a vine with goodly branches, her fruit is abundant: according to the multitude of her fruits she has multiplied her altars; according to the wealth of his land, he has set up pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Michaeas 5:13; Kings III 18:21; Sophonias 1:5"> They have divided their hearts; now shall they be utterly destroyed: he shall dig down their altars, their pillars shall mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 10:15; Osee 10:7; Osee 13:11"> Because now they shall say, We have no king, because we feared not the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Amos 6:12; Osee 4:2"> and what should a king do for us, speaking false professions as his words? he will make a covenant: judgement shall spring up as a weed on the soil of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Osee 9:11; Osee 5:8; Kings IV 23:5"> The inhabitants of Samaria shall dwell near the calf of the house of On; for the people of it mourned for it: and as they provoked him, they shall rejoice at his glory, because he has departed from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Osee 5:13; Esias 30:3; Daniel 11:8"> And having bound it for the Assyrians, they carried it away as presents to king Jarim: Ephraim shall receive a gift, and Israel shall be ashamed of his counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Osee 10:3; Jude 1:13; Kings IV 1:3"> Samaria has cast off her king as a twig on the surface of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Revelation 6:16; Esias 2:19"> And the altars of On, the sins of Israel, shall be taken away: thorns and thistles shall come up on their altars; and they shall say to the mountains, Cover us; and to the hills, Fall on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Osee 9:9; Judges 20:5; Judges 19:22–30"> From the time the hills existed Israel has sinned: there they stood: war waged against the children of iniquity</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:13; Osee 4:9; Jeremias 16:16"> to chastise them shall not overtake them on the hill, the nations shall be gathered against them, when they are chastened for their two sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Osee 4:16; Jeremias 50:11; Deuteronomy 25:4"> Ephraim is a heifer taught to love victory, but I will come upon the fair part of her neck: I will mount Ephraim; I will pass over Juda in silence; Jacob shall prevail against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 4:3–4; James 3:18; Esias 45:8"> Sow to yourselves for righteousness, gather in for the fruit of life: light you⌃ for yourselves the light of knowledge; seek the Lord till the fruits of righteousness come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 1:31"> Therefore have you⌃ passed over ungodliness in silence, and reaped the sins of it? you⌃ have eaten false fruit; for you have trusted in your sins, in the abundance of your power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Osee 13:16; Kings IV 18:9–10; Amos 3:8"> Therefore shall destruction rise up amongst your people, and all your strong places shall be ruined: as a prince Solomon departed out of the house of Jeroboam, in the days of battle they dashed the mother to the ground upon the children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Osee 10:5; Osee 10:7; Romans 7:13"> thus will I do to you, O house of Israel, because of the unrighteousness of your sins.</span>
