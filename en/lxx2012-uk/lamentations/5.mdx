<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 44:13–16; Psalms 89:50–51; Lamentations 3:61"> Remember, O Lord, what has happened to us: behold, and look on our reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 1:7; Sophonias 1:13; Psalms 79:1–2"> Our inheritance has been turned away to aliens, our houses to strangers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 18:21; Exodus 22:24; Jeremias 15:8"> we are become orphans, we have no father, our mothers are as widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 3:1; Jezekiel 4:9–17; Deuteronomy 28:48"> We have drunk our water for money; our wood is sold to us for a burden on our neck:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:36–37; Lamentations 1:14; Jeremias 28:14"> we have been persecuted, we have laboured, we have had no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Osee 12:1; Osee 9:3; Osee 7:11"> Egypt gave the hand to us, Assur to their own satisfaction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 31:29; Jeremias 16:12; Jezekiel 18:2"> Our fathers sinned, and are not: we have borne their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:15; Zacharias 11:6; Proverbs 30:22"> Servants have ruled over us: there is none to ransom us out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:16–17; Jeremias 40:9–12; Jezekiel 12:18–19"> We shall bring in our bread with danger of our lives, because of the sword of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Job 30:30; Psalms 119:83"> Our skin is blackened like an oven; they are convulsed, because of the storms of famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 13:16; Zacharias 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:30"> They humbled the women in Sion, the virgins in the cities of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Esias 47:6; Lamentations 2:20"> Princes were hanged up by their hands: the elders were not honoured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 16:21; Exodus 11:5; Matthew 23:4"> The chosen men lifted up the voice in weeping, and the youths fainted under the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 7:34; Esias 24:7–11; Jezekiel 26:13"> And the elders ceased from the gate, the chosen men ceased from their music.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Jeremias 25:10; James 4:9–10"> The joy of our heart has ceased; our dance is turned into mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 89:39; Jeremias 13:18; Esias 3:9–11"> The crown has fallen from our head: yes, woe to us! for we have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 1:5; Job 17:7; Lamentations 2:11"> For this has grief come; our heart is sorrowful: for this our eyes are darkened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Michaeas 3:12; Jeremias 9:11; Psalms 74:2–3"> Over the mountain of Sion, because it is made desolate, foxes have walked therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 102:12; Hebrews 1:8–12; Psalms 45:6"> But you, O Lord, shall dwell for ever; your throne shall endure to generation and generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 44:24; Psalms 13:1; Jeremias 14:19–21"> Therefore will you utterly forget us, and abandon us a long time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 80:3; Psalms 80:7; Psalms 85:4"> Turn us, O Lord, to you, and we shall be turned; and renew our days as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 60:1–2; Jezekiel 37:11; Psalms 44:9"> For you have indeed rejected us; you have been very angry against us.</span>
