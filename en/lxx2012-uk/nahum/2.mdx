<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:9–11; Chronicles II 25:8; Jeremias 51:20–23"> It is all over with him, he has been removed, one who has been delivered from affliction has come up panting into your presence, watch the way, strengthen your loins, be very valiant in your strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 80:12–13; Jezekiel 37:23; Sophonias 3:11"> For the Lord has turned aside the pride of Jacob, as the pride of Israel: for they have utterly rejected them, and have destroyed their branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:14–15; Esias 63:1–3; Zacharias 6:2"> They have destroyed the arms of their power from amongst men, their mighty men sporting with fire: the reins of their chariots shall be destroyed in the day of his preparation, and the horsemen shall be thrown into confusion</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 4:13; Jezekiel 26:10; Naum 3:2–3"> in the ways, and the chariots shall clash together, and shall be entangled in each other in the broad ways: their appearance is as lamps of fire, and as gleaming lightnings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 46:12; Jeremias 51:27–28; Jeremias 50:29"> And their mighty men shall bethink themselves and flee by day; and they shall be weak as they go; and they shall hasten to her walls, and shall prepare their defences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 45:1–2; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> The gates of the cities have been opened, and the palaces have fallen into ruin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 38:14; Esias 59:11; Luke 23:48"> and the foundation has been exposed; and she has gone up, and her maidservants were led away as doves moaning in their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 51:13; Revelation 17:1; Jeremias 46:5"> And as for Nineve, her waters shall be as a pool of water: and they fled, and staid not, and there was none to look back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:12; Daniel 11:8; Naum 2:12–13"> They plundered the silver, they plundered the gold, and there was no end of their adorning; they were loaded with it upon all their pleasant vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:6; Psalms 22:14; Daniel 5:6"> There is thrusting forth, and shaking, and tumult, and heart-breaking, and loosing of knees, and pangs on all loins; and the faces of all are as the blackening of a pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 5:29; Jeremias 2:15; Esias 31:4"> Where is the dwelling-place of the lions, and the pasture that belonged to the whelps? where did the lion go, that the lion's whelp should enter in there, and there was none to scare him away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 51:34; Psalms 17:12; Esias 10:6–14"> The lion seized enough prey for his whelps, and strangled for his young lions, and filled his lair with prey, and his dwelling-place with spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 21:13; Psalms 46:9; Naum 3:5"> Behold, I am against you, says the Lord Almighty, and I will burn up your multitude in the smoke, and the sword shall devour your lions; and I will utterly destroy your prey from off the land, and your deeds shall no more at all be heard of.</span>
