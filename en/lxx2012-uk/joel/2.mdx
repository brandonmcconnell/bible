<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:15; Joel 1:15; 1 Peter 4:7"> Sound the trumpet in Sion, make a proclamation in my holy mountain, and let all the inhabitants of the land be confounded: for the day of the Lord is near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 1:6; Sophonias 1:14–15; Joel 2:10–11"> for a day of darkness and gloominess is near, a day of cloud and mist: a numerous and strong people shall be spread upon the mountains as the morning; there has not been from the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. age.">a</a></sup>beginning one like it, and after it there shall not be again even to the years of many generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 51:3; Joel 1:19–20; Genesis 2:8"> Before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. it, sc. the people.">b</a></sup>them is a consuming fire, and behind them is a flame kindled: the land before them is as a paradise of delight, and behind them a desolate plain: and there shall none<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lit. to him, sc. the people.">c</a></sup>of them escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:7"> Their appearance is as the appearance of horses; and as horsemen, so shall they pursue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:9; Esias 5:24; Esias 30:30"> As the sound of chariots on the tops of mountains shall they leap, and as the sound of a flame of fire devouring stubble, and as a numerous and strong people setting themselves in array for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Naum 2:10; Esias 13:8; Jeremias 30:6"> Before them shall the people be crushed: every face shall be as the blackness of a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, pot.">d</a></sup>cauldron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 30:27; Kings II 1:23; Kings II 2:18–19"> As warriors shall they run, and as men of war shall they mount on the walls; and each shall move in his right path, and they shall not turn aside from their tracks:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 23:10; Job 33:18; Chronicles II 32:5"> and not one shall stand aloof from his brother: they shall go on weighed down with their arms, and they fall upon their weapons, yet shall they in no wise be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 9:21; John 10:1; Exodus 10:6"> They shall seize upon the city, and run upon the walls, and go up upon the houses, and enter in through the windows as thieves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Esias 13:10; Joel 3:15–16"> Before them the earth shall be confounded, and the sky shall be shaken: the sun and the moon shall be darkened, and the stars shall withdraw their light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Malachias 3:2; Naum 1:6; Jeremias 30:7"> And the Lord shall utter his voice before his host: for his camp is very great: for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. works.">e</a></sup>execution of his words is mighty: for the day of the Lord is great, very glorious, and who shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. sufficient for it?">f</a></sup>able to resist it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:8–9; Esias 55:6–7; Chronicles II 7:13–14"> Now therefore, says the Lord your God, turn to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with lamentation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Psalms 34:18; Exodus 34:6–7"> and rend your hearts, and not your garments, and turn to the Lord your God: for he is merciful and compassionate, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy, and repents of evils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jonas 3:9; Aggaeus 2:19; Kings IV 19:4"> Who knows if he will return, and repent, and leave a blessing behind him, even a meat-offering and a drink-offering to the Lord your God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:14; Joel 2:1; Kings III 21:12"> Sound the trumpet in Sion, sanctify a fast, proclaim a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See ch 1:14.">g</a></sup>solemn service:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 29:5; Chronicles II 20:13; 1 Corinthians 7:5"> gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the elders, gather the infants at the breast: let the bridegroom go forth of his chamber, and the bride out of her closet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 115:2; Jezekiel 8:16; Psalms 79:10"> Between the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. base.">h</a></sup>porch and the altar let the priests that minister to the Lord weep, and say, Spare your people, O Lord, and give not your heritage to reproach, that the heathen should rule over them, lest they should say amongst the heathen, Where is their God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zacharias 8:2; Psalms 103:13; Zacharias 1:14"> But the Lord was jealous of his land, and spared his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:29; Jezekiel 36:15; Esias 62:8–9"> And the Lord answered and said to his people, Behold, I will send you corn, and wine, and oil, and you⌃ shall be satisfied with them: and I will no longer make you a reproach amongst the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zacharias 14:8; Amos 4:10; Esias 34:3"> And I will chase away from you the northern adversary, and will drive him away into a dry land, and I will sink<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. cause to disappear.">i</a></sup>his face in the former sea, and his back parts in the latter sea, and his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. corruption.">j</a></sup>ill savour shall come up, and his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See Job 6,7.">k</a></sup>stink come up, because he has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. magnified his works.">l</a></sup>wrought great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Sophonias 3:16–17; Esias 54:4; Esias 41:10"> Be of good courage, O land; rejoice and be glad: for the Lord has done great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zacharias 8:12; Psalms 65:12; Psalms 67:6"> Be of good courage, you⌃ beasts of the plain, for the plains of the wilderness have budded, for the trees have borne their fruit, the fig tree and the vine have yielded their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 5:7–8; Philippians 4:4; Leviticus 26:4"> Rejoice then and be glad, you⌃ children of Sion, in the Lord your God: for he has given you food<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. to exactness.">m</a></sup>fully, and he will rain on you the early and the latter rain, as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachias 3:10; Amos 9:13; Joel 3:18"> And the floors shall be filled with corn, and the presses shall overflow with wine and oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zacharias 10:6; Joel 1:4–7; Joel 2:2–11"> And I will recompense you for the years which the locust, and the caterpillar, and the palmerworm, and the cankerworm have eaten, even my great army, which I sent against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 22:26; Romans 5:5"> And you⌃ shall eat abundantly, and be satisfied, and shall praise the name of the Lord your God for the things which he has wrought wonderfully with you: and my people shall not be ashamed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:26–28; Leviticus 26:11–12; Sophonias 3:17"> And you⌃ shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and that there is none else beside me; and my people shall no more be ashamed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:16–21; Jezekiel 39:29; Esias 32:15"> And it shall come to pass afterward, that I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your old men shall dream dreams, and your young men shall see visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11"> And on my servants and on my handmaids in those days will I pour out of my Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Luke 21:11; Matthew 24:29"> And I will show wonders in heaven, and upon the earth, blood, and fire, and vapour of smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 6:12–13; Matthew 24:29; Malachias 4:1"> The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and glorious day of the Lord come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 10:11–14; Acts 2:20–21; Esias 46:13"> And it shall come to pass that whoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved: for in mount Sion and in Jerusalem shall the saved one be as the Lord has said, and they that have glad tidings preached to them, whom the Lord has called.</span>
