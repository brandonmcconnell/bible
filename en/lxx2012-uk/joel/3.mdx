<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Sophonias 3:19–20; Jeremias 30:3; Jezekiel 39:25"> For, behold, in those days and at that time, when I shall have turned the captivity of Juda and Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zacharias 14:2–4; Esias 66:16; Sophonias 3:8"> I will also gather all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, nations. See Mat 25,31.">a</a></sup>Gentiles, and bring them down to the valley of Josaphat, and will plead with them there for my people and my heritage Israel, who have been dispersed amongst the Gentiles; and these Gentiles have divided my land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Naum 3:10; Obdias 1:11; Amos 2:6"> and cast lots over my people, and have given their boys to harlots, and sold their girls for wine, and have drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 1:6–10; Jeremias 47:4; Esias 59:18"> And what have you⌃ to do with me, O Tyre, and Sidon, and all Galilee of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="ἀλλοφυλων, usually Philistines.">b</a></sup>Gentiles? do you⌃ render me a recompense? or do you⌃ bear malice against me? quickly and speedily will I return your recompense on your own heads:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:16–17; Kings IV 18:15–16; Kings IV 12:18"> because you⌃ have taken my silver and my gold, and you⌃ have brought my choice ornaments into your temples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:13; Joel 3:3; Deuteronomy 28:68"> and you⌃ have sold the children of Juda and the children of Jerusalem to the children of the Greeks, that you⌃ might expel them from their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 43:5–6; Jeremias 23:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:6"> Therefore, behold, I will raise them up out of the place whither you⌃ have sold them, and I will return your recompense on your own heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 60:14; Job 1:15; Esias 14:1–2"> And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hands of the children of Juda, and they shall sell them into captivity to a far distant nation: for the Lord has spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 46:3–4; Esias 8:9–10; Jezekiel 38:7"> Proclaim these things amongst the Gentiles;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. sanctify.">c</a></sup>declare war, arouse the warriors, draw near and go up, all you⌃ men of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 12:8; Michaeas 4:3; Chronicles II 25:8"> Beat your ploughshares into swords, and your sickles into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, daggers.">d</a></sup>spears: let the weak say, I am strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 13:3; Sophonias 3:8; Jezekiel 38:9–18"> Gather yourselves together, and go in, all you⌃ nations round about, and gather yourselves there; let the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. meek.">e</a></sup>timid become a warrior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Esias 2:4; Psalms 96:13"> Let them be aroused, let all the nations go up to the valley of Josaphat: for there will I sit to judge all the Gentiles round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:15–20; Mark 4:29; Osee 6:11"> Bring forth the sickles, for the vintage is come: go in, tread the grapes, for the press is full: cause the vats to overflow; for their wickedness is multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Philippians 3:2; Esias 34:2–8"> Noises have resounded in the valley of judgement: for the day of the Lord is near in the valley of judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31"> The sun and the moon shall be darkened, and the stars shall withdraw their light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 2:10–11; Aggaeus 2:6"> And the Lord shall cry out of Sion, and shall utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heaven and the earth shall be shaken, but the Lord shall spare his people, and shall strengthen the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Naum 1:15; Esias 52:1"> And you⌃ shall know that I am the Lord your God, who dwell in Sion my holy mountain: and Jerusalem shall be holy, and strangers shall not pass through her anymore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 47:1–12; Zacharias 14:8; Revelation 22:1–2"> And it shall come to pass in that day that the mountains shall drop sweet wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See chap 1:20.">f</a></sup>fountains of Juda shall flow with water, and a fountain shall go forth of the house of the Lord, and water the valley of flags.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 19:1–15; Jeremias 51:35; Zacharias 14:18–19"> Egypt shall be a desolation, and Idumea shall be a desolate plain, because of the wrongs of the children of Juda, because they have shed righteous blood in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:25; Amos 9:15; Esias 33:20"> But Judea shall be inhabited for ever, and Jerusalem to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 4:4; Revelation 21:3; Jezekiel 36:25"> And I will make inquisition for their blood, and will by no means leave it unavenged: and the Lord shall dwell in Sion.</span>
