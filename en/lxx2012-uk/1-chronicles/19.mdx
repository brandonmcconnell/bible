<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings I 11:1–2; Kings II 10:1–19; Kings I 12:12"> And it came to pass after this, that Naas the king of the children of Ammon died, and Anan his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3–6; Kings I 30:26; Genesis 19:37–38"> And David said, I will act kindly towards Anan the son of Naas, as his father acted kindly towards me. And David sent messengers to condole with him on the death of his father. So the servants of David came into the land of the children of Ammon to Anan, to comfort him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:23–24; 1 Corinthians 13:5–7; Kings III 12:8–11"> And the chiefs of the children of Ammon said to Anan, Is it to honour your father before you, that David has sent comforters to you? Have not his servants come to you that they might search the city, and to spy out the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 10:4–5; Esias 15:2; Mark 12:4"> And Anan took the servants of David, and shaved them, and cut off the half of their garments as far as their tunic, and sent them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:24–26; Matthew 18:31; Kings III 16:34"> And there came men to report to David concerning the men: and he sent to meet them, for they were greatly disgraced: and the king said, Dwell in Jericho until your beards have grown, and return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 18:5; Genesis 34:30; Chronicles I 18:9"> And the children of Ammon saw that the people of David were ashamed, and Anan and the children of Ammon sent a thousand talents of silver to hire for themselves chariots and horsemen out of Syria of Mesopotamia, and out of Syria Maacha, and from Sobal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Joshua 13:9; Joshua 13:16"> And they hired for themselves two and thirty thousand chariots, and the king of Maacha and his people; and they came and encamped before Medaba: and the children of Ammon assembled out of their cities, and came to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:6; Chronicles I 11:10–47; Kings II 23:8–39"> And David heard, and sent Joab and all the host of mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 20:1; Kings III 20:24; Esias 28:6"> And the children of Ammon came forth, and set themselves in array for battle by the gate of the city: and the kings that were come forth encamped by themselves in the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 10:9–14; Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:22"> And Joab saw that they were fronting him to fight against him before and behind, and he chose some out of all the young men of Israel, and they set themselves in array against the Syrian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles I 18:12; Chronicles I 11:20; Chronicles I 19:9"> And the rest of the people he gave into the hand of his brother Abesai, and they set themselves in array against the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:27–28; Nehemiah 4:20; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> And he said, If the Syrian should prevail against me, then shall you deliver me: and if the children of Ammon should prevail against you, then will I deliver you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:14; Psalms 27:14; Kings II 10:12"> Be of good courage, and let us be strong, for our people, and for the cities of our God: and the Lord shall do what is good in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 20:19–21; Chronicles II 13:5–16; Jeremias 46:15–16"> So Joab and the people that were with him set themselves in battle array against the Syrians, and they fled from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Leviticus 26:7"> And the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians fled, and they also fled from before Abesai, and from before Joab his brother, and they came to the city: and Joab came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 10:16; Zacharias 14:1–3; Michaeas 4:11–12"> And the Syrian saw that Israel had defeated him, and he sent messengers, and they brought out the Syrians from beyond the river; and Sophath the commander-in-chief of the forces of Adraazar was before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 22:6–7; Chronicles I 19:9"> And it was told David; and he gathered all Israel, and crossed over Jordan, and came upon them, and set the battle in array against them. So David set his army in array to fight against the Syrians, and they fought against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings II 10:18; Psalms 33:16; Psalms 18:32"> And the Syrians fled from before Israel; and David killed of the Syrians seven thousand riders in chariots, and forty thousand infantry, and he killed Sophath the commander-in-chief of the forces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings II 10:19; Joshua 9:9–11; Kings III 20:1"> And the servants of Adraazar saw that they were defeated before Israel, and they made peace with David and served him: and the Syrians would not any more help the children of Ammon.</span>
