<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 7:6; Nehemiah 3:26; Chronicles II 34:15"> And the seventh month arrived, and the children of Israel were settled in their cities; and all the people were gathered as one man to the broad place before the water-gate, and they told Esdras the scribe to bring the book of the law of Moses, which the Lord commanded Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Malachias 2:7; Numbers 29:1"> So Esdras the priest brought the law before the congregation both of men and women, and every one who had understanding was present to listen, on the first day of the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:22; Revelation 2:29; Hebrews 2:1–3"> And he read in it from the time of sunrise to the middle of the day, before the men and the women; and they understood it, and the ears of all the people were attentive to the book of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 10:29; Nehemiah 12:13; Nehemiah 10:20"> And Esdras the scribe stood on a wooden stage, and there stood next to him Mattathias, and Samaeas, and Ananias, and Urias, and Chelcia, and Massia, on his right hand; and on his left Phadaeas, and Misael, and Melchias, and Asom, and Asabadma, and Zacharias, and Mesollam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:20; Kings III 8:14; Luke 4:16–17"> And Esdras opened the book before all the people, for he was above the people; and it came to pass when he had opened it, that all the people stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Lamentations 3:41; Exodus 4:31"> And Esdras blessed the Lord, the great God: and all the people answered, and said, Amen, lifting up their hands: and they bowed down and worshipped the Lord with their face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:4; Leviticus 10:11; Nehemiah 3:23"> And Jesus and Banaias and Sarabias instructed the people in the law, and the people stood in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Acts 28:23; Luke 24:32"> And they read in the book of the law of God, and Esdras taught, and instructed them distinctly in the knowledge of the Lord, and the people understood the law in the reading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Nehemiah 7:70; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And Neemias, and Esdras the priest and scribe, and the Levites, and they that instructed the people, spoke and said to all the people, It is a holy day to the Lord our God; do not mourn, nor weep. For all the people wept when they heard the words of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 28:7–8; Proverbs 17:22; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> And the governor said to them, Go, eat the fat, and drink the sweet, and send portions to them that have nothing; for the day is holy to our Lord: and faint not, for the Lord is our strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:30"> And the Levites caused all the people to be silent, saying, Be silent, for it is a holy day, and despond not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:111; Psalms 119:130"> So all the people departed to eat, and to drink, and to send portions, and to make great mirth, for they understood the words which he made known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–6; Proverbs 8:33–34; Proverbs 12:1"> And on the second day the heads of families assembled with all the people, also the priests and Levites, to Esdras the scribe, to attend to all the words of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34; Deuteronomy 16:13–15; Leviticus 23:40–43"> And they found written in the law which the Lord commanded Moses, that the children of Israel should dwell in booths, in the feast in the seventh month:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40; Deuteronomy 16:16; Leviticus 23:4"> and that they should sound with trumpets in all their cities, and in Jerusalem. And Esdras said, Go forth to the mountain, and bring branches of olive, and branches of cypress trees, and branches of myrtle, and branches of palm trees, and branches of every thick tree, to make booths, according to that which was written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Kings IV 14:13; Nehemiah 12:37"> And the people went forth, and brought them, and made booths for themselves, each one upon his roof, and in their courts, and in the courts of the house of God, and in the streets of the city, and as far as the gate of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 8:13; Ezra 3:4; Chronicles II 7:8–10"> And all the congregation who had returned from the captivity, made booths, and lived in booths: for the children of Israel had not done so from the days of Jesus the son of Naue until that day: and there was great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 29:35; Leviticus 23:36; Deuteronomy 31:10–13"> And Esdras read in the book of the law of God daily, from the first day even to the last day: and they kept the feast seven days; and on the eighth day a solemn assembly, according to the ordinance.</span>
