<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Sisinnes the governor of Celosyria and Phenice, and Sathrabuzanes, with their companions following the commandments of king Darius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Did very carefully oversee the holy works, assisting the ancients of the Jews and governors of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And so the holy works prospered, when Aggeus and Zacharias the prophets prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And they finished these things by the commandment of the Lord God of Israel, and with the consent of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes, kings of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And thus was the holy house finished in the three and twentieth day of the month Adar, in the sixth year of Darius king of the Persians</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the children of Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and others that were of the captivity, that were added to them, did according to the things written in the book of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And to the dedication of the temple of the Lord they offered one hundred bullocks two hundred rams, four hundred lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And twelve goats for the sin of all Israel, according to the number of the chief of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The priests also and the Levites stood arrayed in their vestments, according to their kindred, in the service of the Lord God of Israel, according to the book of Moses: and the porters at every gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the children of Israel that were of the captivity held the passover the fourteenth day of the first month, after that the priests and the Levites were sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They that were of the captivity were not all sanctified together: but the Levites were all sanctified together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And so they offered the passover for all them of the captivity, and for their brethren the priests, and for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the children of Israel that came out of the captivity did eat, even all they that had separated themselves from the abominations of the people of the land, and sought the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they kept the feast of unleavened bread seven days, making merry before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For that he had turned the counsel of the king of Assyria towards them, to strengthen their hands in the works of the Lord God of Israel.</span>
