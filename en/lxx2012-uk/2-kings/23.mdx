<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:29–33; Chronicles II 30:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> So they reported the word to the king: and the king sent and gathered all the elders of Juda and Jerusalem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, to his house.">a</a></sup>to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 22:8; Deuteronomy 31:10–13; Revelation 20:12"> And the king went up to the house of the Lord, and every man of Juda and all who lived in Jerusalem with him, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the people small and great; and he read in their ears all the words of the book of the covenant that was found in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings IV 11:14; Kings IV 11:17; Deuteronomy 13:4"> And the king stood by a pillar, and made a covenant before the Lord, to walk after the Lord, to keep his commandments and his testimonies and his ordinances with all the heart and with all the soul, to confirm the words of this covenant; even the things written<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. on.">b</a></sup>in this book. And all the people stood<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. in.">c</a></sup>to the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 21:3; Kings IV 21:7; Chronicles II 33:3"> And the king commanded Chelcias the high priest, and the priests of the second order, and them that kept the door, to bring out of the temple of the Lord all the vessels that were made for Baal, and for the grove, and all the host of heaven, and he burnt them without Jerusalem in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Gr. is from the Heb. word.">d</a></sup>fields of Kedron, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. cast.">e</a></sup>took the ashes of them to Baethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 44:17–19; Kings IV 21:3–4; Osee 10:5"> And he burnt the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Heb. הבמויס.">f</a></sup>idolatrous priests, whom the kings of Juda had<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. given.">g</a></sup>appointed, (and they burnt incense in the high places and in the cities of Juda, and the places around about Jerusalem); and them that burnt incense to Baal, and to the sun, and to the moon, and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or. the twelve signs. Heb. ולמולות compare Job 38:32.">h</a></sup>Mazuroth, and to all the host of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:4; Kings IV 23:15; Kings III 14:23"> And he carried out the grove from the house of the Lord to the brook Kedron, and burnt it at the brook Kedron, and reduced it to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or, ashes.">i</a></sup>powder, and cast its powder on the sepulchres of the sons of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 15:12; Jezekiel 16:16; Kings III 14:24"> And he pulled down the house of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The Gr. is from the Heb. word.">j</a></sup>sodomites that were by the house of the Lord, where the women wove tents for the grove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 15:22; Joshua 21:17; Kings III 19:3"> And he brought up all the priest from the cities of Juda, and defiled the high places where the priests burnt incense, from Gaebal even to Bersabee; and he pulled down the house of the gates that was by the door of the gate of Joshua the ruler of the city, on a man's left hand at the gate of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:10–14; Kings I 2:36; Malachias 2:8–9"> Only the priests of the high places went not up to the altar of the Lord in Jerusalem, for they only ate leavened bread in the midst of their brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 30:33; Jeremias 19:6; Leviticus 18:21"> And he defiled Taphes which is in the valley of the son of Ennom, constructed for a man to cause his son or his daughter to pass through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. in fire.">k</a></sup>fire to Moloch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Chronicles II 14:5"> And he burnt the horses which the king of Juda had given to the sun in the entrance of the house of the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. to.">l</a></sup>by the treasury of Nathan the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Heb. Nathan-melech.">m</a></sup>king's eunuch, in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. again from the Heb.">n</a></sup>suburbs; and he burnt the chariot of the sun with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 21:5; Sophonias 1:5; Jeremias 19:13"> And the altars that were on the roof of the upper chamber of Achaz, which the kings of Juda had made, and the altars which Manasses had made in the two courts of the house of the Lord, did the king pull down and forcibly remove from thence, and cast their dust into the brook of Kedron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 11:7; Kings III 11:5; Numbers 21:29"> And the king defiled the house that was before Jerusalem, on the right hand of the mount of Mosthath, which Solomon king of Israel built to Astarte the abomination of the Sidonians, and to Chamos the abomination of Moab, and to Moloch the abomination of the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 23:24; Kings IV 23:16"> And he broke in pieces the pillars, and utterly destroyed the groves, and filled their places with the bones of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 23:6; Kings III 14:16; Kings III 21:22"> Also the high altar in Baethel, which Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who made Israel to sin, had made, even that high altar he tore down, and broke in pieces the stones of it, and reduced it to powder, and burnt the grove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 13:1–2; Kings III 13:32; John 10:35"> And Josias turned aside, and saw the tombs that were there in the city, and sent, and took the bones out of the tombs, and burnt them on the altar, and defiled it, according to the word of the Lord which the man of God spoke, when Jeroboam stood by the altar at the feast: and he turned and raised his eyes to the tomb of the man of God that spoke these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 13:1; Kings III 13:30–31"> And he said, What is that mound which I see? And the men of the city said to him, It is the grave of the man of God that came out of Juda, and uttered these imprecations which he imprecated upon the altar of Baethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 13:31; Kings III 13:1–22"> And he said, Let him alone; let no one disturb his bones. So his bones were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. delivered.">o</a></sup>spared, together with the bones of the prophet that came out of Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Chronicles II 34:6–7; Kings IV 17:9; Jezekiel 8:17–18"> Moreover Josias removed all the houses of the high places that were in the cities of Samaria, which the kings of Israel made to provoke the Lord, and did to them all that he did in Baethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 11:18; Chronicles II 34:5; Exodus 22:20"> And he sacrificed all the priests of the high places that were there on the altars, and burnt the bones of men upon them, and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Numbers 9:2–5; Chronicles II 35:1–19"> And the king commanded all the people, saying, Keep the passover to the Lord your God, as it is written in the book of this covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles II 30:1–3; Chronicles II 30:13–20; Chronicles II 35:3–19"> For a passover such as this had not been kept from the days of the judges who judged Israel, even all the days of the kings of Israel, and of the kings of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But in the eighteenth year of king Josias, was the passover kept to the Lord in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Kings IV 21:6; Genesis 31:19"> Moreover Josias removed the sorcerers, and the wizards, and the theraphin, and the idols, and all the abominations that had been set up in the land of Juda and in Jerusalem, that he might<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Gr. establish or confirm.">p</a></sup>keep the words of the law that were written in the book, which Chelcias the priest found in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 18:5; Jeremias 29:13; Deuteronomy 6:5"> There was no king like him before him, who turned to the Lord with all his heart, and with all his soul, and with all his strength, according to all the law of Moses; and after him there rose not one like him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 21:11–13; Kings IV 24:4; Jeremias 3:7–10"> Nevertheless the Lord turned not from the fierceness of his great anger, wherewith he was angry in his anger against Juda,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. upon or against.">q</a></sup>because of the provocations, wherewith Manasses provoked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings IV 18:11; Kings IV 21:13; Kings IV 21:4"> And the Lord said, I will also remove Juda from my presence, as I removed Israel, and will reject this city which I have chosen even Jerusalem, and the house of which I said, My name shall be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings IV 20:20"> And the rest of the acts of Josias, and all that he did, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremias 46:2; Chronicles II 35:20–24; Judges 5:19"> And in his days went up Pharao Nechao king of Egypt against the king of the Assyrians to the river Euphrates: and Josias went out to meet him: and Nechao killed him in Mageddo when he saw him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings IV 9:28; Chronicles II 36:1–4; Kings IV 21:24"> And his servants carried him dead from Mageddo, and brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his sepulchre: and the people of the land took Joachaz the son of Josias, and anointed him, and made him king in the room of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:15; Kings IV 24:18; Jeremias 22:11"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. a son of 23 years was Joachaz in his reigning.">r</a></sup>Twenty and three years old was Joachaz when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Amital, daughter of Jeremias of Lobna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings IV 21:2–7; Kings IV 21:21–22"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his fathers did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings III 8:65; Kings IV 25:6; Jeremias 39:5–6"> And Pharao Nechao removed him to Rablaam in the land of Emath, so that he should not reign in Jerusalem; and imposed a tribute on the land, one hundred talents of silver, and one hundred talents of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jezekiel 19:3–4; Kings IV 24:17; Chronicles I 3:15"> And Pharao Nechao made Eliakim son of Josias king of Juda king over them in the place of his father Josias, and he changed his name to Joakim, and he took Joachaz and brought him to Egypt, and he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings IV 23:33; Kings IV 15:19–20"> And Joakim gave the silver and the gold to Pharao; but he assessed the land to give the money at the command of Pharao: they gave the silver and the gold each man according to his assessment together with the people of the land to give to Pharao Nechao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:5; Jeremias 26:1; Chronicles I 3:15"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gr. a son of 23 years was Joakim in his reigning.">s</a></sup>Twenty-five years old was Joakim when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Jeldaph, daughter of Phadail of Ruma.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:22–23; Jezekiel 19:5–9; Jeremias 36:31"> And he did that which was evil in the eyes of the lord, according to all that his fathers had done.</span>
