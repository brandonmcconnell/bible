<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:27; Romans 15:18; Proverbs 21:31"> Now Naiman, the captain of the host of Syria, was a great man before his master, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. wondered at in countenance.">a</a></sup>highly respected, because by him the Lord had given deliverance to Syria, and the man was mighty in strength, but a leper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 6:23; Kings IV 13:20; Kings I 13:17–18"> And the Syrians went forth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. light armed, etc.">b</a></sup>in small bands, and took captive out of the land of Israel a little maid: and she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. was before.">c</a></sup>waited on Naiman's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:29; Luke 17:12–14; Numbers 11:29"> And she said to her mistress, O that my lord were before the prophet of God in Samaria; then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. will detach him.">d</a></sup>would recover him from his leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26–27; John 4:28–29; John 1:42–46"> And she went in and told her lord, and said, Thus and thus spoke the maid from the land of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 14:12; Kings IV 8:8–9; Numbers 22:17–18"> And the king of Syria said to Naiman, Go to, go, and I will send a letter to the king of Israel. And he went, and took in his hand ten talents of silver, and six thousand pieces of gold, and ten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. changing robes.">e</a></sup>changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he brought the letter to the king of Israel, saying, Now then, as soon as this letter shall reach you, behold, I have sent to you my servant Naiman, and you shall recover him from his leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 2:6; Kings III 20:7; Genesis 30:2"> And it came to pass, when the king of Israel read the letter, that he tore his garments, and said, Am I God, to kill and to make alive, that this man sends to me to recover a man of his leprosy? consider, however, I pray you, and see that this man seeks an occasion against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 18:36–37; Osee 12:13; Jezekiel 2:5"> And it came to pass, when Elisaie heard that the king of Israel had torn his garments, that he sent to the king of Israel, saying, Therefore have you torn your garments? Let Naiman, I pray you, come to me, and let him know that there is a prophet in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 16:37–39; Kings IV 6:32; Esias 60:14"> So Naiman came with horse and chariot, and stood at the door of the house of Elisaie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:7; 1 Corinthians 6:11; Leviticus 14:7"> And Elisaie sent a messenger to him, saying, Go and wash seven times in Jordan, and your flesh shall return to you, and you shall be cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Luke 14:11; Matthew 8:8"> And Naiman was angry, and departed, and said, Behold, I said, He will by all means come out to me, and stand, and call on the name of his God, and lay his hand upon the place, and recover the leper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:9; Kings IV 5:17; Kings IV 2:14"> Are not the Abana and Pharphar, rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. shall I not, etc.">f</a></sup>may I not go and wash in them, and be cleansed? and he turned and went away in a rage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 6:21; Kings IV 13:14; Kings IV 2:12"> And his servants came near and said to him, Suppose the prophet had spoken a great thing to you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. will you.">g</a></sup>would you not perform it? yet he has but said to you, Wash, and be cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 33:25; Luke 4:27; Luke 5:13"> So Naiman went down, and dipped himself seven times in Jordan, according to the word of Elisaie: and his flesh returned to him as the flesh of a little child, and he was cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Daniel 6:26–27; Joshua 2:9–11"> And he and all his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. army or camp.">h</a></sup>company returned to Elisaie, and he came and stood before him, and said, Behold, I know that there is no God in all the earth, save only in Israel: and now receive a blessing of your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 5:26; Kings IV 5:20; Kings IV 3:14"> And Elisaie said, As the Lord lives, before whom I stand, I will not take one. And he pressed him to take one: but he would not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 26:18; Kings IV 5:12; Romans 14:1"> And Naiman said, Well then, if not, let there be given to your servant, I pray you, the load of a yoke of mules; and you shall give me of the red earth: for henceforth your servant will not offer whole burnt offering or sacrifice to other gods, but only to the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Probably this last clause belongs to ver. 18.">i</a></sup>by reason of this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 7:2; Kings IV 7:17; Kings IV 17:35"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, The Lord shall be, etc.">j</a></sup>let the Lord be propitious to your servant when my master goes into the house of Remman to worship there, and he shall lean on my hand, and I shall bow down in the house of Remman when he bows down in the house of Remman; even let the Lord, I pray, be merciful to your servant in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 1:17; Mark 5:34; Exodus 4:18"> And Elisaie said to Naiman, Go in peace. And he departed from him a little way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 4:12; Kings IV 4:31; Kings IV 6:31"> And Giezi the servant of Elisaie said, Behold, my Lord has spared this Syrian Naiman, so as not to take of his hand what he has brought: as the Lord lives, I will surely run after him, and take somewhat of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 7:6–7; Kings IV 9:17–22; Kings IV 4:26"> So Giezi followed after Naiman: and Naiman saw him running after him, and turned back from his chariot to meet him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Alex.+ 'Is all well?'">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 5:5; Esias 59:3; Acts 5:3–4"> And Giezi said, All is well: my master has sent me, saying, Behold, now are there come to me two young men of the sons of the prophets from mount Ephraim; give them, I pray you, a talent of silver, and two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. changing robes.">l</a></sup>changes of raiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 12:10; Kings III 20:7; Kings IV 2:17"> And Naiman said, Take two talents of silver. And he took two talents of silver in two bags, and two changes of raiment, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. gave.">m</a></sup>put them upon two of his servants, and they bore them before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 21:16; Joshua 7:11–12; Joshua 7:1"> And he came to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. a dark place, or the dark.">n</a></sup>a secret place, and took them from their hands, and laid them up in the house, and dismissed the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 5:22; Genesis 16:8; Proverbs 30:20"> And he went in himself and stood before his master; and Elisaie said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Corinthians 5:3; Kings IV 6:12"> Whence come you, Giezi? and Giezi said, Your servant has not been hither or there. And Elisaie said to him, Went not my heart with you, when the man returned from his chariot to meet you? and now you have received silver, and now you have received raiment, and olive yards, and vineyards, and sheep, and oxen, and menservants, and maidservants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:6; Numbers 12:10; Kings IV 15:5"> The leprosy also of Naiman shall cleave to you, and to your seed for ever. And he went out from his presence leprous, like snow.</span>
