<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:25; Kings IV 2:3; Nehemiah 5:2–5"> And one of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried to Elisaie, saying, Your servant my husband is dead; and you know that your servant feared the Lord: and the creditor is come to take my two sons to be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings III 17:12; James 2:5; Kings IV 6:26–27"> And Elisaie said, What shall I do for you? tell me what you have in the house. And she said, Your servant has nothing in the house, except oil wherewith I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. shall anoint.">a</a></sup>anoint myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 2:7; Psalms 81:10; John 16:24"> And he said to her, Go, borrow for yourself vessels without of all your neighbours, even empty vessels; borrow not a few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 8:5–9; Matthew 6:6; Ephesians 3:20"> And you shall go in and shut the door upon you and upon your sons, and you shall pour forth into these vessels, and remove that which is filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 5:11; Hebrews 11:7–8; Kings III 17:15–16"> And she departed from him, and shut the door upon herself and upon her sons: they brought the vessels near to her, and she poured in until the vessels were filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Kings IV 13:19; Luke 6:19"> And she said to her sons, Bring me yet a vessel. And they said to her, There is not a vessel more. And the oil stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–12; Kings III 12:22; Romans 12:17"> And she came and told the man of God: and Elisaie said, Go, and sell the oil, and you shall pay your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. interest, pl.">b</a></sup>debts, and you and your sons shall live of the remaining oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Genesis 19:3; Kings IV 4:11–12"> And a day came, when Elisaie passed over to Soman, and there was a great lady there, and she constrained him to eat bread: and it came to pass as often as he went into the city, that he turned aside to eat there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 17:24; Proverbs 31:10–11; Kings IV 4:7"> And the woman said to her husband, See now, I know that this is a holy man of God who comes over continually to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:13; Matthew 10:41–42; Matthew 25:40"> Let us now make for him an upper chamber, a small place; and let us put there for him a bed, and a table, and a stool, and a candlestick: and it shall come to pass that when he comes in to us, he shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, turn aside.">c</a></sup>turn in there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And a day came, and he went in there, and turned aside into the upper chamber, and lay there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 8:4–5; Kings IV 3:11; Kings IV 4:29–31"> And he said to Giezi his servant, Call me this Somanite. and he called her, and she stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:16–18; Philippians 4:18–19; Hebrews 6:10"> And he said to him, Say now to her, Behold, you have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. been astonished with all this astonishment.">d</a></sup>taken all this trouble for us; what should I do for you? Have you any<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. word or business.">e</a></sup>request to make to the king, or to the captain of the host? And she said, I dwell in the midst of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:7; Genesis 15:2–3; Kings I 1:8"> And he said to Giezi, What must we do for her? and Giezi his servant said, Indeed she has no son, and her husband is old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he called her, and she stood by the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Luke 1:13; Kings IV 4:28"> And Elisaie said to her, At this time next year, as the season is, you shall be alive, and embrace a son. And she said, Nay, my lord, do not lie to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Luke 1:36; Genesis 21:1"> And the woman conceived, and bore a son at the very time, as the season was, being alive, as Elisaie said to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:4"> And the child grew: and it came to pass when he went out to his father to the reapers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 14:1–2; Jeremias 4:19"> that he said to his father, My head, my head. and his father said to a servant, carry him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:12; John 11:3; John 11:14"> And he carried him to his mother, and he lay upon her knees till noon, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 4:32; Kings IV 4:10; Kings IV 4:7"> And she carried him up and laid him on the bed of the man of god; and she shut the door upon him, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:38; Kings IV 4:26; John 11:3"> And she called her husband, and said, Send now for me one of the young men, and one of the asses, and I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. run.">f</a></sup>ride quickly to the man of God, and return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Numbers 28:11; Chronicles I 23:31"> And he said, Why are you going to him today? It is neither new moon, nor the Sabbath. And she said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. peace.">g</a></sup>It is well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 4:20; Kings III 13:13; Kings I 25:19–20"> And she saddled the ass, and said to her servant, Be quick, proceed: spare not on my account to ride, unless I shall tell you. Go, and you shall proceed, and come to the man of God to mount Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 2:25; Esias 35:2; Kings III 18:19"> And she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. went.">h</a></sup>rode and came to the man of God to the mountain: and it came to pass when Elisaie saw her coming, that he said to Giezi his servant, See now, that Somanite comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:36; Kings IV 4:23; Kings I 17:18"> Now run to meet her, and you shall say, Is it well with you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. peace.">i</a></sup>is it well with your husband? is it well with the child? and she said, It is well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings II 7:3; Genesis 18:17; Amos 3:7"> And she came to Elisaie to the mountain, and laid hold of his feet; and Giezi drew near to thrust her away. And Elisaie said, Let her alone, for her soul is much grieved in her, and the Lord has hidden it from me, and has not told it me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 30:1; Kings IV 4:16"> And she said, Did I ask a son of my lord? For did I not say,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. You shall not.">j</a></sup>Do not deal deceitfully with me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings IV 2:14; Kings III 18:46; Exodus 14:16"> And Elisaie said to Giezi, Gird up your loins, and take my staff in your hand, and go: if you meet any man, you shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. bless.">k</a></sup>salute him, and if a man salute you you shall not answer him: and you shall lay my staff on the child's face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–18; Kings IV 2:2; Exodus 33:12–16"> And the mother of the child said, As the Lord lives and as your soul lives, I will not leave you. And Elisaie arose, and went after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:11; Mark 5:39; Mark 9:19–29"> And Giezi went on before her, and laid his staff on the child's face: but there was neither voice nor any hearing. So he returned to meet him, and told him, saying, The child is not awaked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 8:52–53; John 11:17; Kings III 17:17"> And Elisaie went into the house, and, behold, the dead child was laid upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Kings IV 4:4; Acts 9:40"> And Elisaie went into the house, and shut the door upon themselves, the two, and prayed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 17:21; Acts 20:10"> And he went up, and lay upon the child, and put his mouth upon his mouth, and his eyes upon his eyes, and his hands upon his hands; and bowed himself upon him, and the flesh of the child grew warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings IV 8:5; Kings IV 13:21; Kings III 17:22"> And he returned, and walked up and down in the house: and he went up, and bowed himself on the child seven times; and the child opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; Kings III 17:23; Luke 7:15"> And Elisaie cried out to Giezi, and said, Call this Somanite. So he called her, and she came in to him: and Elisaie said, Take your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; Kings IV 2:15; Kings III 17:24"> And the woman went in, and fell at his feet, and did obeisance bowing to the ground; and she took her son, and went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings IV 8:1; Kings IV 2:3; Kings IV 2:1"> And Elisaie returned to Galgala: and a famine was in the land; and the sons of the prophets sat before him: and Elisaie said to his servant, Set on the great pot, and boil pottage for the sons of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremias 2:21; Esias 5:4; Matthew 15:13"> And he went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a vine in the field, and gathered of it wild<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. sing. colosynth.">l</a></sup>gourds, his garment full; and he cast it into the cauldron of pottage, for they knew them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 16:18; Exodus 15:23; Exodus 10:17"> And he poured it out for the men to eat: and it came to pass, when they were eating of the pottage, that behold! they cried out, and said, There is death in the pot, O man of God. And they could not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Kings IV 2:21; Exodus 15:25; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> And he said, Take meal, and cast it into the pot. And Elisaie said to his servant Giezi, Pour out for the people, and let them eat. And there was no longer there any hurtful thing in the pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings I 9:7; John 6:9; Matthew 14:16–21"> And there came a man over from Baetharisa, and brought to the man of God twenty barley loaves and cakes of figs, of the first fruits. And he said, Give to the people, and let them eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:13; Matthew 14:20; John 6:9"> And his servant said, Why should I set this before one hundred men? and he said, Give to the people, and let them eat; for thus says the Lord, They shall eat and leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they ate and left, according to the word of the Lord.</span>
