<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord: he departed not from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who led Israel to sin.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings IV 15:9; Kings IV 15:18"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord: he departed not from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who made Israel to sin.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the rest of the acts of Phakesias, and all that he did, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And the rest of the acts of Phakee, and all that he did, behold, these are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 27:1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. a son of 25 years in his reigning.">n</a></sup>Twenty and five years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Jerusa daughter of Sadoc.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles II 26:4–5; Kings IV 15:3–4; Chronicles II 27:2"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all things that his father Azarias did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings IV 12:3; Chronicles II 23:20; Chronicles II 27:3–9"> Nevertheless he took not away the high places: as yet the people sacrificed and burnt incense on the high places. He built the upper gate of the Lord's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Chronicles II 27:4–9; Kings IV 15:6–7"> And the rest of the acts of Joatham, and all that he did, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
