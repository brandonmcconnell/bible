<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:5–7; Kings IV 24:1–2; Kings IV 24:13"> In the third year of the reign of Joakim king of Juda, came Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon to Jerusalem, and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:7; Zacharias 5:11; Jeremias 27:19–20"> And the Lord gave into his hand Joakim king of Juda, and part of the vessels of the house of God: and he brought them into the land of Sennaar to the house of his god; and he brought the vessels into the treasure-house of his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 39:7; Kings IV 20:17–18; Kings IV 24:15"> And the king told Asphanez his chief eunuch, to bring in some of the captive children of Israel, and of the seed of the kingdom, and of the princes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 5:11; Acts 7:22; Proverbs 22:29"> young men in whom was no blemish, and beautiful in appearance, and skilled in all wisdom, and possessing knowledge, and acquainted with prudence, and who had ability to stand in the house before the king, and the king gave commandment to teach them the learning and language of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; Kings III 10:8; Daniel 1:8"> And the king appointed them a daily portion from the king's table, and from the wine which he drank; and gave orders to nourish them three years, and that afterwards they should stand before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 14:14; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 2:17"> Now these were amongst them of the children of Juda, Daniel, and Ananias, and Azarias, and Misael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:12; Daniel 4:8; Daniel 2:49"> And the chief of the eunuchs gave them names: to Daniel, Baltasar; and to Ananias, Sedrach; and to Misael, Misach; and to Azarias, Abdenago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Jezekiel 4:13–14; Leviticus 11:45–47"> And Daniel purposed in his heart, that he would not defile himself with the king's table, nor with the wine of his drink: and he entreated the chief of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:21; Proverbs 16:7; Psalms 106:46"> Now God had brought Daniel into favour and compassion with the chief of the eunuchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 6:16–18; John 12:42–43; Proverbs 29:25"> And the chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, I fear my lord the king, who has appointed your meat and your drink, lest he see your countenances gloomy in comparison of the young men your equals; also shall you⌃ endanger my head to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Daniel said to Amelsad, whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Ananias, Misael, and Azarias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:2; Daniel 1:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> Prove now your servants ten days; and let them give us pulse, and let us eat, and let us drink water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And let our countenances be seen by you, and the countenances of the children that eat at the king's table; and deal with your servants according as you shall see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he listened to them, and proved them ten days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:25; Proverbs 10:22; Kings IV 4:42–44"> And at the end of the ten days their countenances appeared fairer and stouter in flesh, than the children that fed at the king's table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:12"> So Amelsad took away their supper and the wine of their drink, and gave them pulse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 3:12; James 1:5; Daniel 2:21"> And as for these four children, God gave them understanding and prudence in all learning and wisdom: and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 1:5"> And at the end of the days, after which the king had given orders to bring them in, then the chief of the eunuchs brought them in before Nabuchodonosor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:46; Daniel 1:5"> And the king spoke with them; and there were not found out of them all any like Daniel, and Ananias and Misael, and Azarias: and they stood before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Esias 47:12–14; Kings III 4:29–34"> And in every matter of wisdom and knowledge wherein the king questioned them, he found them ten times wiser than all the enchanters and sorcerers that were in all his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 10:1"> And Daniel continued till the first year of king Cyrus.</span>
