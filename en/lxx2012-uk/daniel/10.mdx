<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 8:26"> In the third year of Cyrus king of the Persians a thing was revealed to Daniel, whose name was called Baltasar; and the thing was true, and great power and understanding in the vision was given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:4; Daniel 9:24–27; Ezra 9:4–5"> In those days I Daniel was mourning three full weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:17; Daniel 11:8; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> I ate no pleasant bread, and no flesh or wine entered into my mouth, neither did I anoint myself with oil, until three whole weeks were accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:14; Daniel 8:2; Jezekiel 1:3"> On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was near the great river, which is Tigris Eddekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13–15; Daniel 12:6–7; Jezekiel 9:2"> And I lifted up my eyes, and looked, and behold a man clothed in linen, and his loins were girded with gold of Ophaz:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 19:12; Revelation 1:13–17; Matthew 17:2"> and his body was as Tharsis, and his face was a the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as lamps of fire, and his arms and his legs as the appearance of shining brass, and the voice of his words as the voice of a multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Acts 22:9; Genesis 3:10"> And I Daniel only saw the vision: and the men that were with me saw not the vision; but a great amazement fell upon them, and they fled in fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Ambacum 3:16; Daniel 8:27"> So I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there was no strength left in me, and my glory was turned into corruption, and I retained no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; Job 4:13; Genesis 15:12"> Yet I heard the voice of his words: and when I heard him I was pricked in the heart, and I fell with my face to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Jeremias 1:9; Daniel 8:18"> And, behold, a hand touched me, and it raised me on my knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 26:16; Jezekiel 2:1; Daniel 8:16–17"> And he said to me, O Daniel, man greatly beloved, understand the words which I speak to you, and stand upright: for I am now sent to you. And when he had spoken to me this word, I stood trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:30–31; Daniel 9:20–23; Daniel 10:19"> And he said to me, Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that you did set your heart to understand, and to afflict yourself before the Lord your God, they words were heard, and I am come because of your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Daniel 10:20–21"> But the prince of the kingdom of the Persians withstood me twenty-one days: and behold, Michael, one of the princes, came to help me; and I left him there with the chief of the kingdom of the Persians:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Daniel 2:28; Daniel 12:4"> and I have come to inform you of all that shall befall your people in the last days: for the vision is yet for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Jezekiel 24:27; Daniel 10:9"> And when he had spoken with me according to these words, I turned my face to the ground, and was pricked in the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 6:7; Daniel 8:15; Jeremias 1:9"> And, behold, as it were the likeness of a son of man touched my lips; and I opened my mouth, and spoke, and said to him that stood before me, O my lord, at the sight of you my bowels were turned within me, and I had no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 6:1–5; Exodus 24:10–11; Mark 12:36"> And how shall your servant be able, O my lord, to speak with this my lord? and as for me, from henceforth strength will not remain in me, and there is no breath left in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Luke 22:43; Daniel 8:18"> And there touched me again as it were the appearance of a man, and he strengthened me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 35:4; Joshua 1:9; Judges 6:23"> and said to me, Fear not, man greatly beloved: peace be to you, quit yourself like a man, and be strong. And when he had spoken with me, I received strength, and said, Let my lord speak; for you have strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:2–4; Daniel 8:5–8"> And he said, Know you, therefore I am come to you? and now I will return to fight with the prince of the Persians: and I was going in, and the prince of the Greeks came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:13; Daniel 12:1; Revelation 12:7"> But I will tell you that which is ordained in the scripture of truth: and there is no one that holds with me in these matters but Michael your prince.</span>
