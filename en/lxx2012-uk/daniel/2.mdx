<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 4:5; Esther 6:1; Job 33:15–17"> In the second year of his reign Nabuchodonosor dreamt a dream, and his spirit was amazed, and his sleep departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 1:20; Daniel 4:6; Genesis 41:8"> And the king gave orders to call the enchanters, and the magicians, and the sorcerers, and the Chaldeans, to declare to the king his dreams. And they came and stood before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 40:8; Genesis 41:15; Daniel 4:5"> And the king said to them, I have dreamt, and my spirit was troubled to know the dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 3:9; Daniel 5:10; Ezra 4:7"> And the Chaldeans spoke to the king in the Syrian language, saying, O king, live for ever: do you tell the dream to your servants, and we will declare the interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 3:29; Ezra 6:11; Kings IV 10:27"> The king answered the Chaldeans, The thing has departed from me: if you⌃ do not make known to me the dream and the interpretation, you⌃ shall be destroyed, and your houses shall be spoiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 5:7; Daniel 2:48; Daniel 5:29"> But if you⌃ make known to me the dream, and the interpretation thereof, you⌃ shall receive of me gifts and presents and much honour: only tell me the dream, and the interpretation thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 2:4; Daniel 2:9; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> They answered the second time, and said, Let the king tell the dream to his servants, and we will declare the interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Ephesians 5:16"> And the king answered and said, I verily know that you⌃ are trying to gain time, because you⌃ see that the thing has gone from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 41:23; Daniel 7:25; Esther 4:11"> If then you⌃ do not tell me the dream, I know that you⌃ have concerted to utter before me a false and corrupt tale, until the time shall have past: tell me my dream, and I shall know that you⌃ will also declare to me the interpretation thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 2:27"> The Chaldeans answered before the king, and said, There is no man upon the earth, who shall be able to make known the king's matter: forasmuch as no great king or ruler asks such a question of an enchanter, magician, or Chaldean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:11; Esias 57:15; Genesis 41:39"> For the question which the king asks is difficult, and there is no one else who shall answer it before the king, but the gods, whose dwelling is not with any flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:13; Matthew 2:16; Daniel 3:19"> Then the king in rage and anger commanded to destroy all the wise men of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:19–20; Esther 3:12–15; Esias 10:1"> So the decree went forth, and they began to kill the wise men; and they sought Daniel and his fellows to kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:24; Jeremias 52:12; Kings II 20:16–22"> Then Daniel answered with counsel and prudence to Arioch the captain of the royal guard, who was gone forth to kill the wise men of Babylon; saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Chief magistrate of the king, therefore has the preemptory command proceeded from the king? So Arioch made known the matter to Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:18–19; Daniel 2:9–11"> And Daniel entreated the king to give him time, and that he might thus declare to the king the interpretation of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 1:6–7; Daniel 3:12; Daniel 1:11"> So Daniel went into his house, and made known the matter to Ananias, and Misael, and Azarias, his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 33:3; Matthew 18:19; Matthew 18:12"> And they sought mercies from the God of heaven concerning this mystery; that Daniel and his friends might not perish with the rest of the wise men of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7; Daniel 7:7"> Then the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a vision of the night; and Daniel blessed the God of heaven, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 113:2; Psalms 145:1–2; Revelation 5:12"> May the name of God be blessed from everlasting and to everlasting: for wisdom and understanding are his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 75:5–7; Psalms 31:14–15; Psalms 113:7–8"> And he changes times and seasons: he appoints kings, and removes them, giving wisdom to the wise, and prudence to them that have understanding:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 12:22; 1 John 1:5; Jeremias 23:24"> he reveals deep and secret matters; knowing what is in darkness, and the light is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 2:18; Exodus 3:15; Daniel 2:29–30"> I give thanks to you, and praise you, O God of my fathers, for you have given me wisdom and power, and has made known to me the things which we asked of you; and you have made known to me the king's vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Daniel 2:12–14"> And Daniel came to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy the wise men of Babylon, and said to him; Destroy not the wise men of Babylon, but bring me in before the king, and I will declare the interpretation to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Daniel 6:13; Daniel 5:13"> Then Arioch in haste brought in Daniel before the king, and said to him, I have found a man of the children of the captivity of Judea, who will declare the interpretation to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:12"> And the king answered and said to Daniel, whose name was Baltasar, Canst you declare to me the dream which I saw, and the interpretation thereof?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esias 47:12–14; Daniel 2:2; Esias 44:25"> And Daniel answered before the king, and said, The mystery which the king asks the explanation of is not in the power of the wise men, magicians, enchanters, or soothsayers to declare to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 40:8; Amos 4:13; Daniel 10:14"> But there is a God in heaven revealing mysteries, and he has made known to king Nabuchodonosor what things must come to pass in the last days. Your dream, and the visions of your head upon your bed, are as follows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:28; Amos 4:13; Daniel 2:47"> O king: your thoughts upon your bed arose as to what must come to pass hereafter: and he that reveals mysteries has made known to you what must come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 41:16; Acts 3:12; Daniel 2:17–18"> Moreover, this mystery has not been revealed to me by reason of wisdom which is in me beyond all others living, but for the sake of making known the interpretation to the king, that you might know the thoughts of your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ambacum 1:7; Jezekiel 28:7; Daniel 7:3–17"> You, O king, saw, and behold an image: that image was great, and the appearance of it excellent, standing before your face; and the form of it was terrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremias 51:7; Daniel 7:4–6; Revelation 17:4"> It was an image, the head of which was of fine gold, its hands and breast and arms of silver, its belly and thighs of brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 7:19–26; Daniel 7:7–8; Daniel 2:40–43"> its legs of iron, its feet, part of iron and part of earthenware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Daniel 2:44–45; Daniel 8:25; Zacharias 12:3"> You saw until a stone was cut out of a mountain without hands, and it struck the image upon its feet of iron and earthenware, and utterly reduced them to powder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Esias 2:2–3; Psalms 37:10; Revelation 20:11"> Then once for all the earthenware, the iron, the brass, the silver, the gold, were ground to powder, and became as chaff from the summer threshing floor; and the violence of the wind carried them away, and no place was found for them: and the stone which had struck the image became a great mountain, and filled all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Daniel 2:23–24"> This is the dream; and we will tell the interpretation thereof before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:7; Jeremias 27:6–7; Ezra 7:12"> You, O king, are a king of kings, to whom the God of heaven has given a powerful and strong and honourable kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Daniel 4:21–22; Psalms 50:10–11; Jeremias 27:5–7"> in every place where the children of men dwell: and he has given into your hand the wild beasts of the field, and the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea, and he has made you lord of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Esias 44:28–5; Daniel 2:32"> You are the head of gold. And after you shall arise another kingdom inferior to you, an a third kingdom which is the brass, which shall have dominion over all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 7:19–26; Daniel 11:36–45"> and a fourth kingdom, which shall be strong as iron: as iron beats to powder and subdues all things, so shall it beat to powder and subdue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Revelation 17:12; Daniel 2:33–35; Revelation 12:3"> And whereas you saw the feet and the toes, part of earthenware and part of iron, the kingdom shall be divided; yet there shall be in it of the strength of iron, as you saw the iron mixed with earthenware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 7:24; Revelation 13:1"> And whereas the toes of the feet were part of iron and part of earthenware, part of the kingdom shall be strong, and part of it shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Whereas you saw the iron mixed with earthenware, they shall be mingled with the seed of men: but they shall not cleave together, as the iron does not mix itself with earthenware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Luke 1:32–33; Daniel 6:26"> And in the days of those kings the God of heaven shall set up a kingdom which shall never be destroyed: and his kingdom shall not be left to another people, but it shall beat to pieces and grind to powder all other kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Esias 28:16; Genesis 41:32; Deuteronomy 10:17"> Whereas you saw that a stone was cut out of a mountain without hands, and it beat to pieces the earthenware, the iron, the brass, the silver, the gold; the great God has made known to the king what must happen hereafter: and the dream is true, and the interpretation thereof sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 10:25; Acts 14:13; Ezra 6:10"> Then king Nabuchodonosor fell upon his face, and worshipped Daniel, and gave orders to offer to him gifts and incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Daniel 2:28; Amos 3:7"> And the king answered and said to Daniel, Of a truth your God is a God of gods, and Lord of kings, who reveals mysteries; for you have been able to reveal this mystery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Daniel 2:6; Daniel 4:9; Daniel 5:11"> And the king promoted Daniel, and gave him great and abundant gifts, and set him over the whole province of Babylon, and made him chief satrap over all the wise men of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Esther 2:19; Daniel 3:12–30"> And Daniel asked of the king, and he appointed Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, over the affairs of the province of Babylon: but Daniel was in the king's palace.</span>
