<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:18; Daniel 2:1; Esther 2:23"> But the Lord removed sleep from the king that night: and he told his servant to bring in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. letters.">a</a></sup>books, the registers of daily events, to read to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 2:21–22"> And he found the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. letters.">b</a></sup>records written concerning Mardochaeus, how he had told the king concerning the two chamberlains of the king, when they were keeping guard, and sought to lay hands on Artaxerxes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:15; Genesis 40:23; Acts 28:8–10"> And the king said, What honour or favour have we done to Mardochaeus? And the king's servants said, You have not done anything to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:14; Esther 4:11; Esther 5:1"> And while the king was enquiring about the kindness of Mardochaeus, behold, Aman was in the court. And the king said, Who is in the court? Now Aman was come in to speak to the king, that he should hang Mardochaeus on the gallows, which he had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the king's servants said, Behold, Aman stands in the court. And the king said, Call him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Obdias 1:3; Esias 62:4–5"> And the king said to Aman, What shall I do to the man whom I wish to honour? And Aman said within himself, Whom would the king honour but myself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 6:11; Esther 6:9"> and he said to the king, As for the man whom the king wishes to honour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 1:33; Luke 15:22; Esther 1:11"> let the king's servants bring the robe of fine linen which the king puts on, and the horse on which the king rides,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 41:43; Kings III 1:33–34; Zacharias 9:9"> and let him give it to one of the king's noble friends, and let him array the man whom the king loves; and let him mount him on the horse, and proclaim through the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, wide space.">c</a></sup>street of the city, saying, Thus shall it be done to every man whom the king honours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; Daniel 4:37; Kings IV 10:10"> Then the king said to Aman, You have well said: so do to Mardochaeus the Jew, who waits in the palace, and let not a word of what you have spoken be neglected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 1:52; Esther 9:3; Revelation 3:9"> So Aman took the robe and the horse, and arrayed Mardochaeus, and mounted him on the horse, and went through the street of the city, and proclaimed, saying, Thus shall it be to every man whom the king wishes to honour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 15:30; Jeremias 14:3–4; Job 20:5"> And Mardochaeus returned to the palace: but Aman went home mourning, and having his head covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Osee 14:9; Genesis 40:19"> And Aman related the events that had befallen him to Zosara his wife, and to his friends: and his friends and his wife said to him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, if it be M. etc. before whom.">d</a></sup>If Mardochaeus be of the race of the Jews, and you have begun to be humbled before him, you will assuredly fall, and you will not be able to withstand him, for the living God is with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:35–36"> While they were yet speaking, the chamberlains arrived, to hasten Aman to the banquet which Esther had prepared.</span>
