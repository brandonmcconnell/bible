<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Genesis 12:6"> And Jerobaal rose early, the same is Gedeon, and all the people with him, and encamped at the fountain of Arad; and the camp of Madiam was to the north of him, reaching from Gabaathamorai, in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> And the Lord said to Gedeon, The people with you are many, so that I may not deliver Madiam into their hand, lest at any time Israel boast against me, saying, My hand has saved me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Revelation 17:14; Revelation 21:8"> And now speak in the ears of the people, saying, Who is afraid and fearful? let him turn and depart from mount Galaad: and there returned of the people twenty-two thousand, and ten thousand were left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 33:16; Kings I 16:7; Kings I 14:6"> And the Lord said to Gedeon, The people is yet numerous; bring them down to the water, and I will purge them there for you: and it shall come to pass that of whoever I shall say to you, This one shall go with you, even he shall go with you; and of whoever I shall say to you, This one shall not go with you, even he shall not go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he brought the people down to the water; and the Lord said to Gedeon, Whosoever shall lap of the water with his tongue as if a dog should lap, you shall set him apart, and also whoever shall bow down upon his knees to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And the number of those that lapped with their hand to their mouth was three hundred men; and all the rest of the people bowed upon their knees to drink water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 14:6; Esias 41:14–16; Judges 7:18–22"> And the Lord said to Gedeon, I will save you by the three hundred men that lapped, and I will give Madiam into your hand; and all the rest of the people shall go every one to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:20; Numbers 10:9; Joshua 6:4"> And they took the provision of the people in their hand, and their horns; and he sent away every man of Israel each to his tent, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, encouraged.">a</a></sup>strengthened the three hundred; and the army of Madiam were beneath him in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 46:2–3; Judges 3:28; Acts 18:9–10"> And it came to pass in that night that the Lord said to him, Arise, go down into the camp, for I have delivered it into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Judges 4:8–9"> And if you are afraid to go down, go down you and your servant Phara into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Judges 7:13–15; Esias 35:3–4"> And you shall hear what they shall say, and afterwards your hands shall be strong, and you shall go down into the camp: and he went down and Phara his servant to the extremity of the companies of fifty, which were in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:10; Judges 6:5; Joshua 11:4"> And Madiam and Amalec and all the children of the east were scattered in the valley, as the locust for multitude; and there was no number to their camels, but they were as the sand on the seashore for multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; Esias 41:14–15; Judges 4:9"> And Gedeon came, and behold a man was relating to his neighbour a dream, and he said, Behold, I have dreamt a dream, and behold, a cake of barley bread rolling into the camp of Madiam, and it came as far as a tent, and struck it, and it fell, and it turned it up, and the tent fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 7:6–7; Joshua 2:9; Joshua 2:24"> And his neighbour answered and said, This is none other than the sword of Gedeon, son of Joas, a man of Israel: God has delivered Madiam and all the host into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–6; Genesis 40:8; Chronicles II 20:18–19"> And it came to pass when Gedeon heard the account of the dream and the interpretation of it, that he worshipped the Lord, and returned to the camp of Israel, and said, Rise, for the Lord has delivered the camp of Madiam into our hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7"> And he divided the three hundred men into three companies, and put horns in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. hand.">b</a></sup>hands of all, and empty pitchers, and torches in the pitchers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; Matthew 16:24; Hebrews 13:7"> and he said to them, You⌃ shall look<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. from. q.d. at the actions proceeding from me.">c</a></sup>at me, and so shall you⌃ do; and behold, I will go into the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, corner. See Acts 10:11.">d</a></sup>beginning of the host, and it shall come to pass that as I do, so shall you⌃ do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:15–17; Kings I 17:47"> And I will sound with the horn, and all you⌃ with me shall sound with the horn round about the whole camp, and you⌃ shall say, For the Lord and Gedeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Exodus 14:24; Matthew 25:6"> And Gedeon and the hundred men that were with him came to the extremity of the army in the beginning of the middle watch; and they completely roused the guards, and sounded with the horns, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. shook off.">e</a></sup>broke the pitchers that were in their hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 27:13; 2 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> and the three companies sounded with the horns, and broke the pitchers, and held the torches in their left hands, and in their right hands their horns to sound with; and they cried out, A sword for the Lord and for Gedeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:17; Proverbs 28:1; Kings IV 7:6–7"> And every man stood in his place round about the host; and all the host ran, and sounded an alarm, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings III 4:12; Joshua 6:20; Joshua 6:4"> And they sounded with the three hundred horns; and the Lord set every man's sword in all the host against his neighbour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 6:35; Kings I 14:21–22"> And the host fled as far as Bethseed Tagaragatha Abel-meula to Tabath; and the men of Israel from Nephthali, and from Aser, and from all Manasse, came to help, and followed after Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 3:27–28; Philippians 1:27; Romans 15:30"> And Gedeon sent messengers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. in.">f</a></sup>into all mount Ephraim, saying, Come down to meet Madiam, and take to yourselves the water as far as Baethera and Jordan: and every man of Ephraim cried out, and they took the water before hand to Baethera and Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 10:26; Judges 8:3–4; Psalms 83:11–12"> And they took the princes of Madiam, even Oreb and Zeb; and they killed Oreb in Sur Oreb, and they killed Zeb in Jakephzeph; and they pursued Madiam, and brought the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. head.">g</a></sup>heads of Oreb and Zeb to Gedeon from beyond Jordan.</span>
