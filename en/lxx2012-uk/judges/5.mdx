<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 68:7–8; Psalms 77:17"> O Lord, in your going forth on Seir, when you went forth out of the land of Edom, the earth quaked and the heaven dropped dews, and the clouds dropped water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Psalms 97:5; Esias 64:1–3"> The mountains were shaken before the face of the Lord Eloi, this Sina before the face of the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 33:8; Judges 3:31; Lamentations 1:4"> In the days of Samegar son of Anath, in the days of Jael, they deserted the ways, and went in by-ways; they went in crooked paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 49:23; Kings II 20:19; Esther 9:19"> The mighty men in Israel failed, they failed until Debbora arose, until she arose a mother in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–17; Judges 2:12; Judges 2:17"> They chose new gods; then the cities of rulers fought;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. if there should be seen.">a</a></sup>there was not a shield or spear seen amongst forty thousand in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 5:2; 2 Corinthians 8:3–4; 2 Corinthians 9:5"> My heart inclines to the orders given in Israel; you⌃ that are willing amongst the people, bless the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 12:14; Judges 10:4; Psalms 105:2"> You⌃ that mount a she-ass at noon-day, you⌃ that sit on the judgement-seat, and walk by the roads of them that sit in judgement by the way; declare</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Michaeas 6:5; Judges 5:8; Kings I 12:7"> you⌃ that are delivered from the noise of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. noisy ones.">b</a></sup>disturbers amongst the drawers of water; there shall they relate righteous acts: O Lord, increase righteous acts in Israel: then the people of the Lord went down to the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8; Psalms 68:18; Psalms 57:8"> Awake, awake, Debbora; awake, awake, utter a song: arise, Barac, and lead your captivity captive, son of Abineem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 2:26–27; Romans 8:37; Psalms 75:7"> Then went down the remnant to the strong, the people of the Lord went down for him amongst the mighty ones from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 12:15; Numbers 32:39–40; Judges 3:27"> Ephraim rooted them out in Amalec, behind you was Benjamin amongst your people: the inhabitants of Machir came down with me searching out the enemy, and from Zabulon came they that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. Rather, draw or handle the scribe's pen, etc.">c</a></sup>draw with the scribe's pen of record.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 4:14; Proverbs 22:13; Acts 20:13"> And princes in Issachar were with Debbora and Barac, thus she sent Barac on his feet in the valleys into the portions of Ruben; great pangs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. reaching.">d</a></sup>reached to the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 32:24; Numbers 32:1–5; Judges 5:15"> Why did they sit between the sheepfolds to hear the bleating of flocks for the divisions of Ruben? there were great searchings of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 13:25; Joshua 19:24–31; Joshua 13:31"> Galaad is on the other side of Jordan where he pitched his tents; and why does Dan remain in ships? Aser sat down on the sea-coasts, and he will tabernacle at his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, places of egress, etc.">e</a></sup>ports.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:10; Judges 4:6; Esther 4:16"> The people Zabulon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. reproached.">f</a></sup>exposed their soul to death, and Nephthali came to the high places of their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Judges 1:27; Kings III 4:12"> Kings set themselves in array, then the kings of Chanaan fought in Thanaach at the water of Mageddo; they took no gift of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 10:11; Kings I 7:10; Psalms 77:17–18"> The stars from heaven set themselves in array, they set themselves to fight with Sisara out of their paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 4:7; Michaeas 7:10; Psalms 44:5"> The brook of Kison swept them away, the ancient brook, the brook Kison: my mighty soul will trample him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10–11; Jeremias 47:4"> When the hoofs of the horse were entangled, his mighty ones earnestly hasted</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 48:10; Judges 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> to curse Meroz: Curse you⌃ it, said the angel of the Lord; cursed is every one that dwells in it, because they came not to the help of the Lord, to his help amongst the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:28; Judges 4:17"> Blessed amongst women be Jael wife of Chaber the Kenite; let her be blessed above women in tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 4:19–21"> He asked for water, she gave him milk in a dish; she brought butter of princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings I 17:49–51; Judges 4:21; Kings II 20:22"> She stretched forth her left hand to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, pin of the tent.">g</a></sup>the nail, and her right to the hand workman's hammer, and she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Lit. hammered.">h</a></sup>struck Sisara with it, she nailed through his head and struck him; she nailed through his temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; James 2:13; Matthew 7:2"> He rolled down between her feet; he fell and lay between her feet; he bowed and fell: where he bowed, there he fell<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="q.d. having departed this life.">i</a></sup>dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 4:15; James 5:7; Song of Songs 8:14"> The mother of Sisara looked down through the window out of the loophole, saying, Why was his chariot ashamed? why did the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, feet of his horses. lit. feet of his chariots.">j</a></sup>wheels of his chariots wait?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Her wise ladies answered her, and she returned answers to herself, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Kings II 13:18; Genesis 37:3"> Will they not find him dividing the spoil? he will surely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="i.e. be gracious and kind in allowing the claim of each to a share.">k</a></sup>be gracious to every man: there are spoils of dyed garments for Sisara, spoils of various dyed garments, dyed embroidered garments, they are the spoils for his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; Kings II 23:4; Psalms 19:4–5"> Thus let all your enemies perish, O Lord: and they that love him shall be as the going forth of the sun in his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the land had rest forty years.</span>
