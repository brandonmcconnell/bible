<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:14; Judges 8:30; Hebrews 2:14"> Speak, I pray you, in the ears of all the men of Sychem, saying, Which is better for you, that seventy men, even all the sons of Jerobaal, should reign over you, or that one man should reign over you? and remember that I am your bone and your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Psalms 10:3; Proverbs 1:11–14"> And his mother's brethren spoke concerning him in the ears of all the men of Sychem all these words; and their heart turned after Abimelech, for they said, He is our brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Acts 17:5; Chronicles II 13:7"> And they gave him seventy pieces of silver out of the house of Baalberith; and Abimelech hired for himself vain and cowardly men, and they went after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 11:1–2; Chronicles II 21:4; Judges 6:24"> And he went to the house of his father to Ephratha, and killed his brethren the sons of Jerobaal, seventy men upon one stone; but Joatham the youngest son of Jerobaal was left, for he hid himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 5:9; Kings III 12:20; Kings IV 12:20"> And all the men of Sicima, and all the house of Bethmaalo, were gathered together, and they went and made Abimelech king by the oak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. מעכ. this is an instance of double translation, στάσεως being given for מעכ, and εὐρετῆ having reference to the word מעא to find; but Alex. rightly omits εὐρετῆ.">a</a></sup>of Sedition, which was at Sicima.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; John 4:20; Deuteronomy 27:12"> And it was reported to Joatham, and he went and stood on the top of mount Garizin, and lifted up his voice, and wept, and said to them, Hear me, you⌃ men of Sicima, and God shall hear you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 8:22–23; Daniel 4:10–18; Kings IV 14:9"> The trees<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. went going.">b</a></sup>went forth on a time to anoint a king over them; and they said to the olive, Reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:7; Acts 4:27"> But the olives said to them, Shall I leave my fatness, with which men shall glorify God, and go to be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. moved.">c</a></sup>promoted over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the trees said to the fig tree, Come, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 13:6–7"> But the fig tree said to them, Shall I leave my sweetness and my good fruits, and go to be promoted over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the trees said to the vine, Come, reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Numbers 15:5"> And the vine said to them, Shall I leave my wine that cheers God and men, and go to be promoted over the trees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 14:9"> Then all the trees said to the bramble, Come you and reign over us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 30:2; Judges 9:20; Osee 14:7"> And the bramble said to the trees, If you⌃ in truth anoint me to reign over you, come, stand under my shadow; and if not, let fire come out from me and devour the cedars of Libanus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:35"> And now, if you⌃ have done it in truth and integrity, and have made Abimelech king, and if you⌃ have wrought well with Jerobaal, and with his house, and if you⌃ have done to him according to the reward of his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:4; Romans 5:8; Revelation 12:11"> as my father fought for you, and put his life in jeopardy, and delivered you out of the hand of Madiam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:5–6; Judges 8:30–31; Judges 9:14"> and you⌃ are risen up this day against the house of my father, and have slain his sons, being seventy men, upon one stone, and have made Abimelech the son of his bondwoman king over the men of Sicima, because he is your brother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 8:6; James 4:16; Philippians 3:3"> if then you⌃ have done truly and faithfully with Jerobaal, and with his house this day, rejoice you⌃ in Abimelech, and let him also rejoice over you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:15; Judges 9:56–57; Chronicles II 20:22–23"> but if not, let fire come out from Abimelech, and devour the men of Sicima, and the house of Bethmaalo; and let fire come out from the men of Sicima and from the house of Bethmaalo, and devour Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 21:16; Kings II 20:14; Joshua 19:8"> And Joatham fled, and ran away, and went as far as Baeer, and lived there out of the way of his brother Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And Abimelech reigned over Israel three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 33:1; Esias 19:14; Esias 19:2"> And God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the men of Sicima; and the men of Sicima<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, despised.">d</a></sup>dealt treacherously<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, in the house. Hebraism.">e</a></sup>with the house of Abimelech:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings III 2:32; Psalms 7:16; Esther 9:25"> to bring the injury done to the seventy sons of Jerobaal, and to lay their blood upon their brother Abimelech, who killed them, and upon the men of Sicima, because they strengthened his hands to kill his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:12–13; Proverbs 1:11–12; Joshua 8:4"> And the men of Sicima set liers in wait against him on the top of the mountains, and robbed every one who passed by them on the way; and it was reported to the king Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 19:7"> And Gaal son of Jobel came, and his brethren, and passed by Sicima, and the men of Sicima trusted in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 9:4; Judges 9:46; Psalms 109:17"> And they went out into the field, and gathered their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. vines.">f</a></sup>grapes, and trod them, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. made ellulim, a Hebrew word. Alex. χορὺς">g</a></sup>made merry; and they brought the grapes into the house of their god, and ate and drank, and cursed Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 34:2; Genesis 34:6; Kings I 25:10"> And Gaal the son of Jobel said, Who is Abimelech, and who is the son of Sychem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerobaal, and is not Zebul his steward, his servant with the son of Emmor the father of Sychem? and why should we serve him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 15:4; Kings II 2:14–17; Esias 36:8–9"> And would that this people were under my hand!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. and I will.">h</a></sup>then would I remove Abimelech, and I would say to him, Multiply your host, and come out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Zebul the ruler of the city heard the words of Gaal the son of Jobel, and he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And he sent messengers to Abimelech secretly, saying, Behold, Gaal the son of Jobel and his brethren are come to Sychem; and behold, they have besieged the city against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Psalms 36:4; Proverbs 1:11–16"> And now rise up by night, you and the people with you, and lay wait in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings I 10:7; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Leviticus 25:26"> And it shall come to pass in the morning at sunrising, you shall rise up early and draw towards the city; and behold, he and the people with him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. are coming, etc.">i</a></sup>will come forth against you, and you shall do to him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. whatever they hand shall find.">j</a></sup>according to your power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And Abimelech and all the people with him rose up by night, and formed an ambuscade against Sychem in four companies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Gaal the son of Jobel went forth, and stood by the door of the gate of the city: and Abimelech and the people with him rose up from the ambuscade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 8:24; Jezekiel 7:7"> And Gaal the son of Jobel saw the people, and said to Zebul, Behold, a people comes down from the top of the mountains: and Zebul said to him, You see the shadow of the mountains as men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:14"> And Gaal continued to speak and said, Behold, a people comes down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, by the sea. A double rendering, perhaps to מעס, the first κατὰ θάλασσαν - the second, ὐπὸ τοῦ ἐχόμενα.">k</a></sup>westward from the part bordering on the middle of the land, and another company comes by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Alex. translates the words “the way of the oak of the seers.”">l</a></sup>the way of Helon Maonenim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 9:28–29; Kings IV 14:8–14; Kings II 2:26–27"> And Zebul said to him, And where is your mouth as you spoke, Who is Abimelech that we should serve him? Is not this the people whom you despised? go forth now, and set the battle in array against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Gaal went forth before the men of Sychem, and set the battle in array against Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Kings III 20:18–21; Kings III 20:30"> And Abimelech pursued him, and he fled from before him; and many fell down slain as far as the door of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Judges 9:30"> And Abimelech entered into Arema, and Zebul cast out Gaal and his brethren, so that they should not dwell in Sychem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And it came to pass on the second day that the people went out into the field, and one brought word to Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And he took the people, and divided them into three companies, and formed an ambush in the field; and he looked, and, behold, the people went forth out of the city, and he rose up against them, and struck them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Judges 9:15; Judges 9:20"> And Abimelech and the chiefs of companies that were with him rushed forward, and stood by the door of the gate of the city; and the two other companies rushed forward upon all that were in the field, and struck them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Kings IV 3:25; Deuteronomy 29:23; James 2:13"> And Abimelech fought against the city all that day, and took the city, and killed the people that were in it, and destroyed the city, and sowed it with salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 9:4; Kings III 8:26"> And all the men of the tower of Sychem heard, and came to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Some read ὀχύρωμα.">m</a></sup>gathering of Baethel-berith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And it was reported to Abimelech, that all the men of the tower of Sychem were gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 68:14; Proverbs 1:11–12; Judges 7:17–18"> And Abimelech went up to the mount of Selmon, and all the people that were with him; and Abimelech took<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. axes.">n</a></sup>an axe in his hand, and cut down a branch of a tree, and took it, and laid it on his shoulders; and said to the people that were with him, What you⌃ see me doing, do quickly as I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; James 3:16; Judges 9:20"> And they cut down likewise even every man a branch, and went after Abimelech, and laid them against the place of gathering, and burnt the place of gathering over them with fire; and they died, even all the men of the tower of Sicima, about a thousand men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And Abimelech went out of Baethel-berith, and encamped against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. in.">o</a></sup>Thebes, and took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And there was a strong tower in the midst of the city; and there all the men and the women of the city fled, and shut the door without them, and went up on the roof of the tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Judges 9:48–49; Kings IV 14:10; Kings IV 15:16"> And Abimelech drew near to the tower, and they besieged it; and Abimelech drew near to the door of the tower to burn it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Kings II 11:21; Job 31:3; Jeremias 49:20"> And a woman cast a piece of a millstone upon the head of Abimelech, and broke his skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Kings I 31:4–5"> And he cried out quickly to the young man his armour-bearer, and said to him, Draw your sword, and kill me, lest at any time they should say, A woman killed him: and his young man thrust him through and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Kings II 18:16; Kings II 20:21–22; Kings III 22:35–36"> And the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead; and they went each to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Psalms 94:23; Judges 9:24; Proverbs 5:22"> So God requited the wickedness of Abimelech, which he wrought against his father, in slaying his seventy brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Judges 9:20; Joshua 6:26; Kings III 16:34"> And all the wickedness of the men of Sychem God requited upon their head; and the curse of Joatham the son of Jerobaal came upon them.</span>
