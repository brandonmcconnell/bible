<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 26:30; Exodus 13:4; Numbers 7:1"> On the first day of the first month, at the new moon, you shall set up the tabernacle of witness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:12; Numbers 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> and you shall place in it the ark of the testimony, and shall cover the ark with the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 24:8; Exodus 40:22–25; Exodus 37:10–24"> and you shall bring in the table and shall set forth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Setting forth of it.">a</a></sup>that which is to be set forth on it; and you shall bring in the candlestick and place its lamps on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Hebrews 9:24; 1 John 2:1–2"> And you shall place the golden altar, to burn incense before the ark; and you shall put a covering of a veil on the door of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 40:29; 1 John 4:9–10; Hebrews 13:10"> And you shall put the altar of burnt offerings by the doors of the tabernacle of witness, and you shall set up the tabernacle round about, and you shall hallow all that belongs to it round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:10; Numbers 7:1; Matthew 3:16"> And you shall take the anointing oil, and shall anoint the tabernacle, and all things in it; and shall sanctify it, and all its furniture, and it shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 29:36–37; Luke 1:35; Esias 61:1"> And you shall anoint the altar of burnt offerings, and all its furniture; and you shall hallow the altar, and the altar shall be most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 8:1–13; Leviticus 9:1–24; Exodus 29:1–35"> And you shall bring Aaron and his sons to the doors of the tabernacle of witness, and you shall wash them with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; 1 John 2:20; 1 John 2:27"> And you shall put on Aaron the holy garments, and you shall anoint him, and you shall sanctify him, and he shall minister to me as priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 61:10; Romans 8:30; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> And you shall bring up his sons, and shall put garments on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 25:13; Hebrews 5:1–14; Hebrews 8:1–10"> And you shall anoint them as you did anoint their father, and they shall minister to me as priests; and it shall be that they shall have an<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 John 2. 27; The anointing remains etc.">b</a></sup>everlasting anointing of priesthood, throughout their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Exodus 40:17–32; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And Moses did all things whatever the Lord commanded him, so did he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:1; Exodus 40:1–2; Numbers 9:1"> And it came to pass in the first month, in the second year after their going forth out of Egypt, at the new moon, that the tabernacle was set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 37:27–28; 1 Peter 1:5; Leviticus 26:11"> And Moses set up the tabernacle, and put on the capitals, and put the bars into their places, and set up the posts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 26:1–14; Exodus 36:8–19"> And he stretched out the curtains over the tabernacle, and put the veil of the tabernacle on it above as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:34; Deuteronomy 10:5; Hebrews 10:19–21"> And he took the testimonies, and put them into the ark; and he put the staves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. under.">c</a></sup>by the sides of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; Exodus 40:3; Exodus 35:12"> And he brought the ark into the tabernacle, and put on it the covering of the veil, and covered the ark of the testimony, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; John 6:53–57; Ephesians 3:8"> And he put the table in the tabernacle of witness, on the north side without the veil of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Exodus 40:4; Hebrews 9:2"> And he put on it the show bread before the Lord, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 26:35; Revelation 2:5; John 1:1"> And he put the candlestick into the tabernacle of witness, on the side of the tabernacle towards the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 4:5"> And he put on it its lamps before the Lord, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Hebrews 7:25; John 11:42"> And he put the golden altar in the tabernacle of witness before the veil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 30:7"> and he burnt on it incense of composition, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 40:6; Hebrews 13:5–6; Hebrews 13:10"> And he put the altar of the burnt offerings by the doors of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 30:19–20; Psalms 26:6; John 13:10"> And he set up the court round about the tabernacle and the altar; and Moses accomplished all the works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Numbers 9:15–23; Chronicles II 7:2"> And the cloud covered the tabernacle of witness, and the tabernacle was filled with the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 8:11; Leviticus 16:2; Chronicles II 7:2"> And Moses was not able to enter into the tabernacle of testimony, because the cloud overshadowed it, and the tabernacle was filled with the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Numbers 9:17; Exodus 13:21–22"> And when the cloud went up from the tabernacle, the children of Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. harnessed again.">d</a></sup>prepared to depart with their baggage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 9:19–22; Psalms 31:15"> And if the cloud went not up, they did not prepare to depart, till the day when the cloud went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 78:14; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 13:21"> For a cloud was on the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night before all Israel, in all their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, preparations etc.">e</a></sup>journeyings.</span>
