<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Now when the nations round about heard that the altar was built and the sanctuary renewed as before, it displeased them very much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Therefore they thought to destroy the generation of Jacob that was amongst them, and thereupon they began to kill and destroy the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then Judas fought against the children of Esau in Idumea at Arabattine, because they besieged Gael: and he gave them a great overthrow, and abated their courage, and took their spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Also he remembered the injury of the children of Bean, who had been a snare and an offence to the people, in that they lay in wait for them in the ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He shut them up therefore in the towers, and encamped against them, and destroyed them utterly, and burnt the towers of that place with fire, and all that were therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Afterward he passed over to the children of Ammon, where he found a mighty power, and much people, with Timotheus their captain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So he fought many battles with them, till at length they were discomfited before him; and he struck them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And when he had taken Jazar, with the towns belonging thereto, he returned into Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then the heathen that were at Galaad assembled themselves together against the Israelites that were in their quarters, to destroy them; but they fled to the fortress of Dathema.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And sent letters to Judas and his brethren, The heathen that are round about us are assembled together against us to destroy us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they are preparing to come and take the fortress whereunto we are fled, Timotheus being captain of their host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Come now therefore, and deliver us from their hands, for many of us are slain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Yes, all our brethren that were in the places of Tobie are put to death: their wives and their children also they have carried away captives, and borne away their stuff; and they have destroyed there about a thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> While these letters were yet reading, behold, there came other messengers from Galilee with their clothes torn, who reported on this wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And said, They of Ptolemais, and of Tyrus, and Sidon, and all Galilee of the Gentiles, are assembled together against us to consume us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now when Judas and the people heard these words, there assembled a great congregation together, to consult what they should do for their brethren, that were in trouble, and assaulted of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then said Judas to Simon his brother, Choose you out men, and go and deliver your brethren that are in Galilee, for I and Jonathan my brother will go into the country of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So he left Joseph the son of Zacharias, and Azarias, captains of the people, with the remnant of the host in Judea to keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> To whom he gave commandment, saying, Take you⌃ the charge of this people, and see that you⌃ make not war against the heathen until the time that we come again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Now to Simon were given three thousand men to go into Galilee, and to Judas eight thousand men for the country of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then went Simon into Galilee, where he fought many battles with the heathen, so that the heathen were discomfited by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And he pursued them to the gate of Ptolemais; and there were slain of the heathen about three thousand men, whose spoils he took.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And those that were in Galilee, and in Arbattis, with their wives and their children, and all that they had, took he away with him, and brought them into Judea with great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Judas Maccabeus also and his brother Jonathan went over Jordan, and travelled three days' journey in the wilderness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Where they met with the Nabathites, who came to them in a peaceful manner, and told them every thing that had happened to their brethren in the land of Galaad:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And how that many of them were shut up in Bosora, and Bosor, and Alema, Casphor, Maked, and Carnaim; all these cities are strong and great:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And that they were shut up in the rest of the cities of the country of Galaad, and that against to morrow they had appointed to bring their host against the forts, and to take them, and to destroy them all in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Hereupon Judas and his host turned suddenly by the way of the wilderness to Bosora; and when he had won the city, he killed all the males with the edge of the sword, and took all their spoils, and burnt the city with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> From whence he removed by night, and went till he came to the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And betimes in the morning they looked up, and, behold, there was an innumerable people bearing ladders and other engines of war, to take the fortress: for they assaulted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> When Judas therefore saw that the battle was begun, and that the cry of the city went up to heaven, with trumpets, and a great sound,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> He said to his host, Fight this day for your brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So he went forth behind them in three companies, who sounded their trumpets, and cried with prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Then the host of Timotheus, knowing that it was Maccabeus, fled from him: therefore he struck them with a great slaughter; so that there were killed of them that day about eight thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> This done, Judas turned aside to Maspha; and after he had assaulted it he took and killed all the males therein, and received the spoils thereof and burnt it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> From thence went he, and took Casphon, Maged, Bosor, and the other cities of the country of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> After these things gathered Timotheus another host and encamped against Raphon beyond the brook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> So Judas sent men to espy the host, who brought him word, saying, All the heathen that be round about us are assembled to them, even a very great host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> He has also hired the Arabians to help them and they have pitched their tents beyond the brook, ready to come and fight against you. Upon this Judas went to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Then Timotheus said to the captains of his host, When Judas and his host come near the brook, if he pass over first to us, we shall not be able to withstand him; for he will mightily prevail against us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> But if he be afraid, and camp beyond the river, we shall go over to him, and prevail against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Now when Judas came near the brook, he caused the scribes of the people to remain by the brook: to whom he gave commandment, saying, Suffer no man to remain in the camp, but let all come to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> So he went first over to them, and all the people after him: then all the heathen, being discomfited before him, cast away their weapons, and fled to the temple that was at Carnaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> But they took the city, and burnt the temple with all that were therein. Thus was Carnaim subdued, neither could they stand any longer before Judas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Then Judas gathered together all the Israelites that were in the country of Galaad, from the least to the greatest, even their wives, and their children, and their stuff, a very great host, to the end they might come into the land of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Now when they came to Ephron, (this was a great city in the way as they should go, very well fortified) they could not turn from it, either on the right hand or the left, but must needs pass through the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Then they of the city shut them out, and stopped up the gates with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Whereupon Judas sent to them in peaceful manner, saying, Let us pass through your land to go into our own country, and none shall do you any hurt; we will only pass through on foot: howbeit they would not open to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Therefore Judas commanded a proclamation to be made throughout the host, that every man should pitch his tent in the place where he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> So the soldiers pitched, and assaulted the city all that day and all that night, till at the length the city was delivered into his hands:</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Who then killed all the males with the edge of the sword, and rased the city, and took the spoils thereof, and passed through the city over them that were slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> After this they went over Jordan into the great plain before Bethsan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And Judas gathered together those that came behind, and exhorted the people all the way through, till they came into the land of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> So they went up to mount Sion with joy and gladness, where they offered burnt offerings, because not one of them were slain until they had returned in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> Now what time as Judas and Jonathan were in the land of Galaad, and Simon his brother in Galilee before Ptolemais,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Joseph the son of Zacharias, and Azarias, captains of the garrisons, heard of the valiant acts and warlike deeds which they had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Therefore they said, Let us also get us a name, and go fight against the heathen that are round about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> So when they had given charge to the garrison that was with them, they went towards Jamnia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Then came Gorgias and his men out of the city to fight against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And so it was, that Joseph and Azarias were put to flight, and pursued to the borders of Judea: and there were slain that day of the people of Israel about two thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> Thus was there a great overthrow amongst the children of Israel, because they were not obedient to Judas and his brethren, but thought to do some valiant act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> Moreover these men came not of the seed of those, by whose hand deliverance was given to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> Howbeit the man Judas and his brethren were greatly renowned in the sight of all Israel, and of all the heathen, wherever their name was heard of;</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> Insomuch as the people assembled to them with joyful acclamations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> Afterward went Judas forth with his brethren, and fought against the children of Esau in the land towards the south, where he struck Hebron, and the towns thereof, and pulled down the fortress of it, and burnt the towers thereof round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> From thence he removed to go into the land of the Philistines, and passed through Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> At that time certain priests, desirous to show their valour, were slain in battle, for that they went out to fight unadvisedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> So Judas turned to Azotus in the land of the Philistines, and when he had pulled down their altars, and burnt their carved images with fire, and spoiled their cities, he returned into the land of Judea.</span>
