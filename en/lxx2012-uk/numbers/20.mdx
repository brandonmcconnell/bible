<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> And the children of Israel, even the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Sin, in the first month, and the people abode in Cades; and Mariam died there, and was buried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> And there was no water for the congregation: and they gathered themselves together against Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> And the people reviled Moses, saying, Would we had died in the destruction of our brethren before the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalms 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> And therefore have you⌃ brought up the congregation of the Lord into this wilderness, to kill us and our cattle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremias 2:2; Jeremias 2:6"> And therefore is this? You⌃ have brought us up out of Egypt, that we should come into this evil place; a place where there is no sowing, neither figs, nor vines, nor pomegranates, neither is there water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> And Moses and Aaron went from before the assembly to the door of the tabernacle of witness, and they fell upon their faces; and the glory of the Lord appeared to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> Take your rod, and call the assembly, you and Aaron your brother, and speak you⌃ to the rock before them, and it shall give forth its waters; and you⌃ shall bring forth for them water out of the rock, and give drink to the congregation and their cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> And Moses took his rod which was before the Lord, as the Lord commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> And Moses and Aaron assembled the congregation before the rock, and said to them, Hear me, you⌃ disobedient ones; must we bring you water out of this rock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And Moses lifted up his hand and struck the rock with his rod twice; and much water came forth, and the congregation drank, and their cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Jezekiel 36:23"> And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, Because you⌃ have not believed me to sanctify me before the children of Israel, therefore you⌃ shall not bring this congregation into the land which I have given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> This is the water of Strife, because the children of Israel spoke insolently before the Lord, and he was sanctified in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obdias 1:10–12"> And Moses sent messengers from Cades to the king of Edom, saying, Thus says your brother Israel; You know all the distress that has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. found us">a</a></sup>come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> And how our fathers went down into Egypt, and we sojourned in Egypt many days, and the Egyptians afflicted us and our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> And we cried to the Lord, and the Lord heard our voice, and sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt; and now we are in the city of Cades, at the extremity of your coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> We will pass through your land: we will not go through the fields, nor through the vineyards, nor will we drink water out of your cistern: we will go by the king's highway; we will not turn aside to the right hand or to the left, until we have passed your borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Edom said to him, You shall not pass through me, and if otherwise, I will go forth to meet you in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> And the children of Israel say to him, We will pass by the mountain; and if I and my cattle drink of your water, I will pay you: but it is no matter of importance, we will go by the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> And he said, You shall not pass through me; and Edom went forth to meet him with a great host, and a mighty hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> So Edom refused to allow Israel to pass through his borders, and Israel turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> And they departed from Cades; and the children of Israel, even the whole congregation, came to Mount Or.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron in mount Or, on the borders of the land of Edom, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> Let Aaron be added to his people; for you⌃ shall certainly not go into the land which I have given the children of Israel, because you⌃ provoked me at the water of strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> Take Aaron, and Eleazar his son, and bring them up to the mount Or before all the congregation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Esias 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> and take Aaron's apparel from off him, and put it on Eleazar his son: and let Aaron die there and be added to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Moses did as the Lord commanded him, and took him up to mount Or, before all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> And he took Aaron's garments off him, and put them on Eleazar his son, and Aaron died on the top of the mountain; and Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; Chronicles II 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> And all the congregation saw that Aaron was dead: and they wept for Aaron thirty days, even all the house of Israel.</span>
