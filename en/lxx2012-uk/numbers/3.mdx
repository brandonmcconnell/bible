<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Genesis 10:1; Exodus 6:16"> And these are the generations of Aaron and Moses, in the day in which the Lord spoke to Moses in mount Sina.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60; Exodus 28:1"> And these are the names of the sons of Aaron; Nadab the firstborn; and Abiud, Eleazar and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests whom they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. accomplished their hands to minister: according to the Hebrew idiom, filled their hands.">a</a></sup>consecrated to the priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> And Nadab and Abiud died before the Lord, when they offered strange fire before the Lord, in the wilderness of Sina; and they had no children; and Eleazar and Ithamar ministered in the priests' office with Aaron their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> Take the tribe of Levi, and you shall set them before Aaron the priest, and they shall minister to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> and shall keep his charges, and the charges of the children of Israel, before the tabernacle of witness, to do the works of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Esias 52:11"> And they shall keep all the furniture of the tabernacle of witness, and the charges of the children of Israel as to all the works of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> And you shall give the Levites to Aaron, and to his sons the priests; they are given for a gift to me of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> And you shall appoint Aaron and his sons over the tabernacle of witness; and they shall keep their charge of priesthood, and all things belonging to the altar, and within the veil; and the stranger that touches them shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> Behold, I have taken the Levites from the midst of the children of Israel, instead of every male that opens the womb from amongst the children of Israel: they shall be their ransom, and the Levites shall be mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> For every firstborn is mine; in the day in which I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I sanctified to myself every firstborn in Israel: both of man and beast, they shall be mine: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sina, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Take the number of the sons of Levi, according to the houses of their families, according to their divisions; number you⌃ them every male from a month old and upwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> And Moses and Aaron numbered them by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. voice.">b</a></sup>word of the Lord, as the Lord commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> And these were the sons of Levi by their names; Gedson, Caath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles I 6:17; Chronicles I 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> And these are the names of the sons of Gedson according to their families; Lobeni and Semei:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Chronicles I 6:18; Chronicles I 15:17–21"> and the sons of Caath according to their families; Amram and Issaar, Chebron and Oziel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; Chronicles I 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> and the sons of Merari according to their families, Mooli and Musi; these are the families of the Levites according to the houses of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> To Gedson belongs the family of Lobeni, and the family of Semei: these are the families of Gedson.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> The numbering of them according to the number of every male from a month old and upwards, their numbering was seven thousand and five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> And the sons of Gedson shall encamp westward behind the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the ruler of the household of the family of Gedson was Elisaph the son of Dael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; Chronicles I 23:32; Chronicles I 9:14–33"> And the charge of the sons of Gedson in the tabernacle of witness was the tent and the veil, and the covering of the door of the tabernacle of witness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> and the curtains of the court, and the veil of the door of the court, which is by the tabernacle, and the remainder of all its works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Chronicles I 26:23; Numbers 3:19; Chronicles I 23:12"> To Caath belonged one division, that of Amram, and another division, that of Issaar, and another division, that of Chebron, and another division, that of Oziel: these are the divisions of Caath, according to number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> Every male from a month old and upward, eight thousand and six hundred, keeping the charges of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The families of the sons of Caath, shall encamp beside the tabernacle towards the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the chief of the house of the families of the divisions of Caath, was Elisaphan the son of Oziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> And their charge was the ark, and the table, and the candlestick, and the altars, and all the vessels of the sanctuary wherewith they do holy service, and the veil, and all their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> And the chief over the chief of the Levites, was Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, appointed to keep the charges of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; Chronicles I 6:19; Chronicles I 23:21"> To Merari belonged the family of Mooli, and the family of Musi: these are the families of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> The mustering of them according to number, every male from a month old and upwards, was six thousand and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> And the head of the house of the families of the division of Merari, was Suriel the son of Abichail: they shall encamp by the side of the tabernacle northwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> The oversight of the charge of the sons of Merari included the capitals of the tabernacle, and its bars, and its pillars, and its sockets, and all their furniture, and their works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and the pillars of the court round about, and their bases, and their pins, and their cords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> They that encamp before the tabernacle of witness on the east shall be Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charges of the sanctuary according to the charges of the children of Israel; and the stranger that touches them, shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> All the numbering of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the word of the Lord, according to their families, every male from a month old and upwards, were two and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalms 87:6"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, Count every firstborn male of the children of Israel from a month old and upwards, and take the number by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> And you shall take the Levites for me—I am the Lord—instead of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites instead of all the firstborn amongst the cattle of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And Moses counted, as the Lord commanded him, every firstborn amongst the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> And all the male firstborn in number by name, from a month old and upwards, were according to their numbering twenty-two thousand and two hundred and seventy-three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn of the sons of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites instead of their cattle, and the Levites shall be mine; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> And for the ransoms of the two hundred and seventy-three which exceed the Levites in number of the firstborn of the sons of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> you shall even take five shekels a head; you shall take them according to the holy didrachm, twenty oboli to the shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And you shall give the money to Aaron and to his sons, the ransom of those who exceed in number amongst them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And Moses took the silver, the ransom of those that exceeded in number<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e. the number redeemed by the Levites.">c</a></sup>the redemption of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> He took the silver from the firstborn of the sons of Israel, a thousand and three hundred and sixty-five shekels, according to the holy shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; Kings I 12:3–4"> And Moses gave the ransom of them that were over to Aaron and his sons, by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. voice.">d</a></sup>word of the Lord, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
