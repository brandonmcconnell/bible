<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they departed from Remmon Phares, and encamped in Lebona.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they departed from Ressan, and encamped in Makellath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And they departed from Makellath, and encamped in Saphar.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And they departed from Makeloth, and encamped in Kataath.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:7"> And they departed from the mountain Gadgad, and encamped in Etebatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they departed from Etebatha, and encamped in Ebrona.</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Numbers 20:22–23; Numbers 20:16"> And they departed from Cades, and encamped in mount Or near the land of Edom.</span>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Aaron was one hundred and twenty-three years old, when he died in mount Or.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 21:1–9"> And Arad the Chananitish king (he too lived in the land of Chanaan) having heard when the children of Israel were entering the land—</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 21:4"> then they departed from mount Or, and encamped in Selmona.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they departed from Selmona, and encamped in Phino.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 21:10"> And they departed from Phino, and encamped in Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 21:11"> And they departed from Oboth, and encamped in Gai, on the other side Jordan on the borders of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they departed from Gai, and encamped in Daebon Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jeremias 48:18; Jezekiel 6:14; Esias 15:2"> And they departed from Daebon Gad, and encamped in Gelmon Deblathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:49; Deuteronomy 34:1; Numbers 27:12"> And they departed from Gelmon Deblathaim, and encamped on the mountains of Abarim, over against Nabau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> And they departed from the mountains of Abarim, and encamped on the west of Moab, at Jordan by Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Joshua 2:1; Joshua 13:20; Numbers 25:1–9"> And they encamped by Jordan between Aesimoth, as far as Belsa to the west of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 33:48–49"> And the Lord spoke to Moses at the west of Moab by Jordan at Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1"> Speak to the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, You⌃ are to pass over Jordan into the land of Chanaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> And you⌃ shall destroy all that dwell in the land before your face, and you⌃ shall abolish their high places, and all their molten images you⌃ shall destroy, and you⌃ shall demolish all their pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:31; Psalms 115:16; Deuteronomy 32:8"> And you⌃ shall destroy all the inhabitants of the land, and you⌃ shall dwell in it, for I have given their land to you for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Joshua 18:11–48; Joshua 15:1–12"> And you⌃ shall inherit their land according to your tribes; to the greater number you⌃ shall give the larger possession, and to the smaller you⌃ shall give the less possession; to whatever part<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. his">b</a></sup>a man's name shall go forth by lot, there shall be his property: you⌃ shall inherit according to the tribes of your families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Judges 2:3; Psalms 106:34–36; Deuteronomy 7:16"> But if you⌃ will not destroy the dwellers in the land from before you, then it shall come to pass that whoever of them you⌃ shall leave shall be thorns in your eyes, and darts in your sides, and they shall be enemies to you on the land on which you⌃ shall dwell;</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 21:23–24; Deuteronomy 28:63; Leviticus 20:23"> and it shall come to pass that as I had determined to do to them, so I will do to you.</span>
