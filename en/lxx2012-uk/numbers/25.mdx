<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Numbers 33:49; Joshua 2:1"> And Israel sojourned in Sattin, and the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. חלל to begin and to profane etc.">a</a></sup>profaned itself by going a-whoring after the daughters of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:15–16"> And they called them to the sacrifices of their idols; and the people ate of their sacrifices, and worshipped their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; Psalms 106:28–29; Osee 9:10"> And Israel consecrated themselves to Beel-phegor; and the Lord was very angry with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> And the Lord said to Moses, Take all the princes of the people, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, put them to shame. See Heb. 6.6">b</a></sup>make them examples of judgement for the Lord in the face of the sun, and the anger of the Lord shall be turned away from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Kings III 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> And Moses said to the tribes of Israel, Slay you⌃ every one his friend that is consecrated to Beel-phegor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremias 36:23"> And, behold, a man of the children of Israel came and brought his brother to a Madianitish woman before Moses, and before all the congregation of the children of Israel; and they were weeping at the door of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalms 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> And Phinees the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, and rose out of the midst of the congregation, and took a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. dagger.">c</a></sup>javelin in his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalms 106:29–31; Chronicles I 21:22"> and went in after the Israelitish man into the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. furnace, κάμινον. Trom renders lupanar. Heb. הקכה.">d</a></sup>chamber, and pierced them both through, both the Israelitish man, and the woman through her womb; and the plague was stayed from the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> And those that died in the plague were four and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalms 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> Phinees the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest has caused my wrath to cease from the children of Israel, when I was exceedingly jealous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, with Or, against them. Hebraism">e</a></sup>amongst them, and I did not consume the children of Israel in my jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachias 2:4–5; Malachias 3:1; Esias 54:10"> Thus do you say to him, Behold, I give him a covenant of peace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremias 33:22"> and he and his seed after him shall have a perpetual covenant of priesthood, because he was zealous for his God, and made atonement for the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; Chronicles II 19:7"> Now the name of the struck Israelitish man, who was struck with the Madianitish woman, was Zambri son of Salmon, prince of a house of the tribe of Symeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> And the name of the Madianitish woman who was struck, was Chasbi, daughter of Sur, a prince of the nation of Ommoth: it is a chief house amongst the people of Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, Speak to the children of Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> Plague the Madianites as enemies, and strike them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> for they are enemies to you by the treachery wherein they ensnare you through Phogor, and through Chasbi their sister, daughter of a prince of Madiam, who was struck in the day of the plague because of Phogor.</span>
