<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2–3"> Behold, I will send my messenger, And he shall prepare the way before me; And the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple; And the messenger of the covenant, whom ye desire, Behold, he shall come, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Isaiah 4:4; 1 Corinthians 3:13–15"> But who shall abide the day of his coming? And who shall stand when he appeareth? For he shall be like the fire of the refiner, And like the soap of the fuller.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Proverbs 17:3; Zechariah 13:9"> And he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver, And he shall purify the sons of Levi, And shall refine them as gold and silver, That, being holy to Jehovah, they may bring an offering in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> Then shall the offering of Judah and Jerusalem be pleasing to Jehovah, As in the days of old, And as in former years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:11"> And I will come near to you to judgment; And I will be a swift witness Against sorcerers, and against adulterers, and against false swearers, And against those who defraud the hireling of his hire, And oppress the widow and the fatherless, And turn aside the stranger from his right, And fear not me, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17"> For I am Jehovah; I change not; Therefore, ye sons of Jacob, are ye not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:3; Romans 10:21; Romans 10:3"> From the days of your fathers have ye turned aside from my statutes, And have not kept them; Return to me, and I will return to you, Saith Jehovah of hosts. But ye say, “Wherein shall we return?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Matthew 22:21; Mark 12:17"> Shall a man rob God, That ye have robbed me? But ye say, “Wherein have we robbed thee?” In tithes and offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–19; Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:14–17"> Ye are cursed with a curse, For ye have robbed me, even this whole nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, That there be food in my house; And try me now in this, Saith Jehovah of hosts, Whether I will not open to you the windows of heaven, And pour out upon you a blessing, until there is none left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Joel 2:22; Zechariah 8:12"> And I will rebuke for you the devourer, And he shall not destroy the fruits of your ground, Nor shall your vine be barren in the field, Saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Isaiah 62:4; Zechariah 8:23"> And all nations shall call you blessed; For ye shall be a delightful land, Saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 2:17; Isaiah 37:23; Romans 9:20"> Your words have been bold against me, saith Jehovah; Yet ye say, “What have we spoken against thee?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 73:8–13"> Ye have said, “It is a vain thing to serve God”; And, “What profit is it that we have kept his ordinances, And that we have walked mournfully before Jehovah of hosts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Malachi 2:17"> Therefore we call the proud happy; Yea, they that do wickedness are built up; Yea, they tempt God, and are delivered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; Psalms 56:8; Malachi 4:2"> Then they that feared Jehovah spake to one another, And Jehovah gave ear and heard; And a book of remembrance was written before him, For them that feared Jehovah, And that thought upon his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 7:6; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> And they shall be to me, saith Jehovah of hosts, In the day which I appoint, as my own possession; And I will spare them, As a father spareth his own son that serveth him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 25:46; Psalms 58:10–11; Genesis 18:25"> Then shall ye return and see What is the difference between the righteous and the wicked, Between him who serveth God And him who serveth him not.</span>
