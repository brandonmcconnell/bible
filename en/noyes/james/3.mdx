<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> My brethren, be not many teachers, knowing that we shall receive the greater condemnation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For in many things we all offend. If any one offend not in speech, the same is a perfect man, able to bridle the whole body also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 32:9; Psalms 39:1; James 1:26"> For when we put the bits into the horses' mouths, that they may obey us, we turn about also their whole body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Behold also the ships, which, though they are so great, and driven by fierce winds, are yet turned about with a very small rudder, whithersoever the steersman chooseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> So also the tongue is a little member, and boasteth great things. Behold, how great a forest a little fire kindleth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire, a world of iniquity! The tongue among our members is that which defileth the whole body, and setteth on fire the wheel of life, and is itself set on fire by hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every kind of beasts and of birds, of creeping things and things in the sea, is tamed and hath been tamed by mankind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 140:3; Psalms 55:21"> but the tongue can no man tame; it is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 62:4"> Therewith bless we the Lord and Father, and therewith curse we men, who have been made after the likeness of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing. My brethren, these things ought not so to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Doth a fountain from the same opening send forth sweet water and bitter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> Can a fig-tree, my brethren, bear olives, or a vine, figs? Neither can salt water yield fresh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who is wise and endued with knowledge among you P let him show out of a good course of conduct his works in meekness of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if ye have bitter rivalry and strife in your hearts, do not glory and lie against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This wisdom is not that which descendeth from above, but earthly, sensual, devilish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where there is rivalry and strife, there is confusion and every evil work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, easy to be entreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, without hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by those who make peace.</span>
