<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; James 2:9; Leviticus 19:15"> My brethren, hold not the faith of our Lord of glory, Jesus Christ, with respect of persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Esther 3:10; Esther 8:2"> For if there come into your assembly a man with a gold ring, in splendid apparel, and there come in also a poor man in vile raiment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:6; Luke 7:44–46"> and ye have respect to him that weareth the splendid apparel, and say, Sit thou here in a good place, and say to the poor man, Stand thou there, or, Sit under my footstool,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:24; Job 34:19; Matthew 7:1–5"> have ye not been partial among yourselves, and become judges with evil thoughts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20; Revelation 2:9"> Hearken, my beloved brethren. Did not God choose the poor as to the world to be rich in faith, and heirs of the kingdom which he promised to them that love him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 17:5; 1 Corinthians 11:22; Proverbs 14:31"> but ye have despised the poor man. Do not the rich oppress you, and do not they drag you before the judgment-seats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Revelation 13:5–6; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Do not they blaspheme the worthy name by which ye are called?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> If indeed ye fulfill the royal law, according to the scripture, “Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself,” ye do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; James 2:1–4; 1 John 3:4"> But if ye have respect to persons, ye commit sin, and are convicted by the law as transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Matthew 5:18–19; Deuteronomy 27:26"> For whoever hath kept the whole law, and yet hath offended in one point, hath become guilty of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:13–14; Matthew 5:21–28; Deuteronomy 5:17–18"> For he who said, “Do not commit adultery,” said also, “Do not kill.” Now if thou commit no adultery, yet if thou kill, thou hast become a transgressor of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:25; James 2:8; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> So speak, and so do, as they that shall be judged by the law of liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 6:15; Proverbs 21:13"> For the judgment shall be without mercy to him that showed no mercy. Mercy glorieth against judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Matthew 7:21–23; James 1:22–25"> What doth it profit, my brethren, if any one say that he hath faith, and have not works? Can his faith save him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 3:11; Matthew 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> If a brother or sister be naked, and destitute of daily food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; 1 John 3:16–18; Matthew 25:42–45"> and one of you say to them, Depart in peace, be warmed and be filled, notwithstanding ye give them not the things needful for the body, what doth it profit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 2:26; 2 Peter 1:5–9; James 2:19–20"> So also faith, if it hath not works, is dead in itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:13; James 2:14; Galatians 5:6"> But some one will say, Thou hast faith, and I have works; show me thy faith without works, and I will show thee my faith by my works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 4:34; Mark 1:24; Matthew 8:29"> Thou believest that God is one; thou doest well; the demons also believe, and tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:17; James 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is idle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 22:9–12; John 8:39"> Was not Abraham our father accepted as righteous through works, when he offered Isaac his son upon the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:3; Hebrews 11:17–19; Galatians 5:6"> Thou seest that faith wrought with his works, and by works was faith made perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Isaiah 41:8; 2 Chronicles 20:7"> And the scripture was fulfilled, which saith, “Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness;” and he was called the friend of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:15–18; Psalms 60:12"> Ye see that by works a man is accounted as righteous, and not by faith only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:22–25; Joshua 2:19–21"> And in like manner was not also Rahab the harlot accounted as righteous through works, when she received the messengers, and sent them out another way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:20; James 2:17; James 2:14"> For as the body without the spirit is dead, so also faith without works is dead.</span>
