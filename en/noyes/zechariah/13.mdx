<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; John 1:29; Psalms 51:2"> In that day there shall be a fountain opened To the house of David, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, For sin and for uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; Jeremiah 23:14–15; Hosea 2:17"> And it shall come to pass in that day, Saith Jehovah of hosts, That I will cut off the names of the idols out of the land, And they shall no more be remembered; And the prophets also, and the impure spirit, Will I cause to pass out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6–11; Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 14:9"> And it shall come to pass, when any one shall yet prophesy, That his father and mother shall say to him, Even they that begat him, “Thou shalt not live; For thou hast spoken falsehood in the name of Jehovah.” And his father and mother that begat him shall pierce him through when he prophesieth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:6–7; Matthew 3:4; Revelation 11:3"> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the prophet shall be ashamed Every one of his vision when he prophesieth; Neither shall they wear a garment of hair, to deceive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 7:14; Acts 19:17–20"> But each shall say, “I am no prophet; I am a man that tilleth the ground; For a man hath purchased me from my youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:35; Psalms 22:16; Proverbs 27:5–6"> And when one shall say to him, “What are these wounds in thy hands?” He shall answer, “Those with which I was wounded in the house of my friends.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:27; Matthew 26:31; John 16:32"> Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, Even against the man who is my fellow, saith Jehovah of hosts! Smite the shepherd, and let the sheep be scattered! I will also turn my hand against the lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Revelation 16:19; Ezekiel 5:2–4"> And it shall come to pass in all the land, saith Jehovah, That two parts therein shall be cut off and die; But the third part shall be left therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Jeremiah 29:11–12; Acts 2:21"> And I will bring the third part through the fire, And will refine them, as silver is refined, And will try them, as gold is tried; They shall call upon me, and I will hear them; I will say, “It is my people.” And they shall say, “Jehovah is our God.”</span>
