<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 13:9; Isaiah 2:12; Isaiah 13:6"> Behold, the day of Jehovah cometh, When thy spoil shall be divided in the midst of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 13:16; Romans 9:27–29"> For I will gather all nations against Jerusalem to battle; And the city shall be taken, And the houses shall be rifled, And the women shall be ravished; And half of the city shall go forth into captivity, And the residue of the people shall not be cut off from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 12:9; Zephaniah 3:19; Zechariah 2:8–9"> Then shall Jehovah go forth, And fight against those nations, As he once fought in the day of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Ezekiel 11:23; Acts 1:11–12"> And his feet shall stand in that day upon the Mount of Olives, Which is before Jerusalem on the east; And the Mount of Olives shall be cloven in the middle of it, eastward and westward, Into a very great valley; And half of the mountain shall remove toward the north, And half of it toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jude 1:14–15; Amos 1:1; Matthew 25:31"> And ye shall flee through the valley of my mountains, For the valley of the mountains shall reach to Azal; Yea, ye shall flee, as ye fled before the earthquake In the days of Uzziah, king of Judah. And Jehovah, my God, will come, And all his holy ones with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:10–11; Psalms 112:4; John 12:46"> And it shall come to pass in that day That there shall he no light, but cold and ice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:2; Daniel 12:4"> And there shall be one day, (Known to Jehovah is it,) It shall be neither day nor night; But at the time of evening there shall be light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–2; John 7:38; John 4:14"> And it shall come to pass in that day That living waters shall go out from Jerusalem, Half of them toward the eastern sea, And half of them toward the western sea; In summer and in winter shall they be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5–6; Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27"> And Jehovah shall be king over all the earth; In that day shall Jehovah be one, and his name one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Joshua 15:32; 1 Kings 15:22"> The whole land shall be turned into a plain, From Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem; And the city shall be exalted and inhabited in her place, From the gate of Benjamin to the place of the former gate, And to the corner gate, And from the tower of Hananeel to the king's wine-presses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:3; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Amos 9:15"> Men shall dwell therein, And there shall be no more destruction; But Jerusalem shall dwell securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Isaiah 66:15–16; Micah 4:11–13"> And this shall be the plague With which Jehovah will smite all the nations Which warred against Jerusalem: Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, And their eyes shall consume away in their sockets, And their tongue shall consume away in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 12:4; Revelation 17:12–17; Judges 7:22"> And it shall come to pass in that day, That there shall be a great tumult among them from Jehovah; And they shall lay hold every one on the hand of his neighbor, And his hand shall rise up against the hand of his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Zechariah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 20:25–27"> And Judah also shall fight against Jerusalem; But the wealth of all the nations round about shall be gathered together, Gold and silver and apparel in great abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12"> And so shall be the plague of the horse, Of the mule, of the camel, and of the ass, And of every beast which shall be in those camps, Even as this plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 14:17–19; Isaiah 66:23; Isaiah 66:18–21"> And it shall be, that every one that is left Of all the nations which come against Jerusalem, Even they shall go up, from year to year, To worship the King, Jehovah of hosts, And to keep the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 4:7–8; Isaiah 60:12; Jeremiah 14:4"> And it shall be that whoso will not go up, Of the families of the earth, to Jerusalem, To worship the King, Jehovah of hosts, Upon them shall be no rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 14:15; Deuteronomy 11:10–11; Zechariah 14:12"> And if the family of Egypt go not up, and come not, Then not for them shall there be water. The plague shall come upon them With which Jehovah shall smite the nations Which go not up to keep the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 3:19"> This shall be the punishment of Egypt, And the punishment of all the nations That come not up to keep the feast of tabernacles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Exodus 39:30; Leviticus 8:9"> In that day, upon the bells of the horses Shall be written, “Holy to Jehovah.” And the pots in the house of Jehovah Shall be as the bowls before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:9; Romans 14:6–7; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> And every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah Shall be holy to Jehovah of hosts; And all that sacrifice shall come, And shall take of them, and seethe therein. And there shall be no more a trader In the house of Jehovah of hosts, in that day.</span>
