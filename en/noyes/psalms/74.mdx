<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 79:13; Psalms 100:3"> “A psalm of Asaph.” O God! why hast thou cast us off for ever? Why doth thine anger smoke against the flock of thy pasture?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Exodus 15:16; Exodus 15:13"> Remember the people which thou didst purchase of old; Thine own inheritance, which thou didst redeem; That Mount Zion, where thou once didst dwell!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 79:1; Daniel 9:17"> Hasten thy steps to those utter desolations! Every thing in the sanctuary hath the enemy abused!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 2:7; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 6:27"> Thine enemies roar in the place of thine assemblies; Their own symbols have they set up for signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 5:6; 2 Chronicles 2:14; Jeremiah 46:22–23"> They appear like those who raise the axe against a thicket;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29; 1 Kings 6:35"> They have broken down the carved work of thy temple with axes and hammers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 89:39; Ezekiel 24:21"> They have cast fire into thy sanctuary; They have profaned, and cast to the ground, the dwelling-place of thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 83:4; 2 Kings 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> They said in their hearts, “Let us destroy them all together!” They have burned all God's places of assembly in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 8:11; Ezekiel 7:26; 1 Samuel 3:1"> We no longer see our signs; There is no prophet among us, Nor any one that knoweth how long this desolation shall endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:50–51; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 89:46"> How long, O God! shall the adversary revile? Shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:3; Psalms 78:65–66; Psalms 44:23"> Why withdrawest thou thy hand, even thy right hand? Take it from thy bosom, and destroy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 44:4; Exodus 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> Yet God was our king of old, Working salvation in the midst of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Psalms 78:13; Ezekiel 29:3"> Thou didst divide the sea by thy power; Thou didst crush the heads of the sea-monsters in the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 104:25–26"> Thou didst break in pieces the head of the crocodile; Thou gavest him for food to the inhabitants of the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; Psalms 105:41"> Thou didst cleave forth the fountain and the stream; Thou didst dry up perennial rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Matthew 5:45; Psalms 104:19"> Thine is the day, and thine the night; Thou didst prepare the light and the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:8; Acts 17:26; Genesis 8:22"> Thou didst establish all the boundaries of the earth; Thou didst make summer and winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:8; Revelation 16:19; Deuteronomy 32:6"> O remember that the enemy hath reviled Jehovah; That an impious people hath blasphemed thy name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:14; Matthew 10:16; Psalms 9:18"> Give not up the life of thy turtle-dove to the wild beast; Forget not for ever thine afflicted people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Genesis 17:7–8; Hebrews 8:10"> Have regard to thy covenant! For all the dark places of the land are full of the abodes of cruelty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 35:10; Psalms 9:18"> O let not the afflicted go away ashamed! Let the poor and needy praise thy name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 74:18; Psalms 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> Arise, O God! maintain thy cause! Remember how the impious revileth thee daily!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Psalms 65:7; Jonah 1:2"> Forget not the clamor of thine adversaries,—The noise of thine enemies, which continually increaseth!</span>
