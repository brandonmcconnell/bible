<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 142:6; Psalms 61:1; 1 John 3:21"> “A psalm of David.” Hear the righteous cause, O LORD! Attend to my cry; Give ear to my prayer from lips without deceit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:6; Ezekiel 18:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9"> May my sentence come forth from thy presence; May thine eyes behold uprightness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Job 23:10; Psalms 26:2"> Provest thou my heart, visitest thou me in the night, Triest thou me like gold, thou shalt find nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 119:9–11; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> My thoughts do not vary from my lips. As to the deeds of men, Through the word of thy lips I have kept me from the paths of the destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; Psalms 119:133; 1 Samuel 2:9"> Support my steps in thy paths, That my feet may not slip!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 116:2; Psalms 86:7; Psalms 88:2"> I call upon thee, O God! for thou wilt hear me; Incline thine ear to me, and listen to my prayer!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 60:5; Psalms 5:11–12; Psalms 31:21"> Show forth thy loving-kindness, O thou that savest by thy right hand Them that seek refuge in thee from their adversaries!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Psalms 91:4; Psalms 91:1"> Guard me as the apple of the eye; Hide me under the shadow of thy wings</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 7:5"> From the wicked who assault me, From my deadly enemies who compass me about!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:3; Psalms 123:4; Psalms 31:18"> They shut up their hard heart; With their mouth they speak haughtily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 37:14; Proverbs 6:13–14; Psalms 10:8–10"> They encompass us in all our steps; They fix their eyes upon us, that they may cast us on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; 1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 10:9"> They are like a lion, eager for his prey; Like a young lion, lurking in secret places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 44:26; Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6"> Arise, O LORD! disappoint them, cast them down! Deliver me from the wicked by thy sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:18; John 17:14; Luke 16:25"> From men, by thy hand, O LORD! from men of the world, Whose portion is in life; whom thou loadest with thy treasure; Whose children have enough, and leave their superfluity to their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; Matthew 5:6; Psalms 11:7"> But I through righteousness shall see thy face; I shall be satisfied with the revival of thy countenance.</span>
