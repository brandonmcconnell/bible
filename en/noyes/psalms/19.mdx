<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:19–20; Psalms 8:3; Psalms 50:6"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” The heavens declare the glory of God; The firmament showeth forth the work of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Psalms 134:1–3; Genesis 8:22"> Day uttereth instruction unto day, And night showeth knowledge unto night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19"> They have no speech nor language, And their voice is not heard;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 10:18; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 98:3"> Yet their sound goeth forth to all the earth, And their words to the ends of the world. In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Philippians 3:13–14; 1 Corinthians 9:24–26"> Which cometh forth like a bridegroom from his chamber, And rejoiceth, like a strong man, to run his course.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:5; Job 22:14; Colossians 1:23"> He goeth forth from the extremity of heaven, And maketh his circuit to the end of it; And nothing is hid from his heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 119:130; Psalms 111:7; Colossians 3:16"> The law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul; The precepts of the LORD are sure, making wise the simple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:105; Psalms 12:6; Jeremiah 15:16"> The statutes of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart; The commandments of the LORD are pure, enlightening the eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Psalms 119:142; Psalms 119:137–138"> The fear of the LORD is clean, enduring for ever; The judgments of the LORD are true and righteous altogether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:103; Proverbs 3:13–15; Psalms 119:72"> More precious are they than gold; yea, than much fine gold; Sweeter than honey and the honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 2 John 1:8; Hebrews 11:6–7"> By them also is thy servant warned, And in keeping of them there is great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 90:8; Job 6:24; Psalms 139:23–24"> Who knoweth his own offences? Oh, cleanse thou me from secret faults!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:133; Romans 6:12–14; 1 Samuel 25:39"> Keep back also thy servant from presumptuous sins; Let them not have dominion over me! Then shall I be upright; I shall not be polluted with gross transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:34; Psalms 51:15; Psalms 119:108"> May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart Be acceptable in thy sight, O LORD, my strength and my redeemer!</span>
