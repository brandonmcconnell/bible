<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 119:27; Psalms 72:18"> I will speak of the glorious honor of thy majesty, And of thy wonderful works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Daniel 3:28–29; Psalms 107:31–32"> Men shall speak of the might of thy terrible deeds, And I will declare thy greatness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 63:7; Psalms 36:10; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> They shall pour forth the praise of thy great good And sing of thy righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:18; Psalms 103:8; Psalms 86:5"> The LORD is gracious, and full of compassion, Slow to anger, and rich in mercy.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 19:1; Psalms 103:22; Revelation 7:9–12"> All thy works praise thee, O LORD! And thy holy ones bless thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Psalms 93:1–2"> They speak of the glory of thy kingdom, And talk of thy power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:10; Psalms 105:1; Matthew 28:18"> To make known to the sons of men his mighty deeds, And the glorious majesty of his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Daniel 7:14; Psalms 10:16"> Thy kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, And thy dominion endureth throughout all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; Psalms 37:24; Psalms 94:18"> The LORD upholdeth all that fall, And raiseth up all that are bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 104:27; Acts 17:25"> The eyes of all wait upon thee, And thou givest them their food in due season;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 104:28; Psalms 107:9; Psalms 132:15"> Thou openest thine hand, And satisfiest the desire of every living thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 45:21"> The LORD is righteous in all his ways, And merciful in all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 34:18; Deuteronomy 4:7"> The LORD is nigh to all that call upon him, To all that call upon him in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Ephesians 3:16–20; John 15:7"> He fulfilleth the desire of them that fear him; He heareth their cry, and saveth them.</span>
