A psalm of Heman, the Ezrahite.” O LORD, God of my salvation! To thee do I cry by day, And by night is my prayer before thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> Let my supplication come before thee; Incline thine ear to my cry!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> For my soul is full of misery, And my life draweth near to the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> I am counted with those who are going down to the pit; I am like one who hath no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Isaiah 14:9–12"> I am left to myself among the dead, Like the slain who lie in the grave, Whom thou no more rememberest, And who are cut off from thy &#91;&#91;protecting&#93;&#93; hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> Thou hast placed me in a deep pit, In a dark and deep abyss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> Thy wrath presseth hard upon me, And thou afflictest me with all thy waves! &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Isaiah 49:7; Jeremiah 32:2"> Thou hast put mine acquaintances far from me, Yea, thou hast made me their abhorrence: I am shut up, and cannot go forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> Mine eyes languish by reason of my affliction. I call upon thee daily, O LORD! To thee do I stretch out my hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> Canst thou show wonders to the dead? Shall the dead arise, and praise thee? &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> Shall thy goodness be declared in the grave, Or thy faithfulness in the place of corruption?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Isaiah 8:22"> Shall thy wonders be known in the dark, And thy justice in the land of forgetfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> To thee do I cry, O LORD! In the morning doth my cry come before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> Why, O LORD! dost thou cast me off? Why hidest thou thy face from me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> I have been afflicted and languishing from my youth; I suffer thy terrors, and am distracted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Isaiah 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> Thy fierce wrath overwhelmeth me; Thy terrors utterly destroy me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> They surround me daily like water; They compass me about together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> Lover and friend hast thou put far from me; My acquaintances are withdrawn from my sight.</span>
