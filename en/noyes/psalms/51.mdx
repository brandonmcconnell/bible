<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came unto him, after his intercourse with Bathsheba.” Be gracious unto me, O God! according to thy loving kindness; According to the greatness of thy mercy, blot out my transgressions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 1:7–9; Psalms 51:7; Hebrews 10:21–22"> Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, And cleanse me from my sin!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 59:12; Psalms 38:18"> For I acknowledge my transgressions, And my sin is ever before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13–14; Luke 15:21; Genesis 20:6"> Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, And in thy sight have I done evil; So that thou art just in thy sentence, And righteous in thy judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Job 14:4; Psalms 58:3"> Behold! I was born in iniquity, And in sin did my mother conceive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Peter 3:4"> Behold! thou desirest truth in the heart; So teach me wisdom in my inmost soul!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Isaiah 1:18; Hebrews 9:13–14"> Purge me with hyssop, until I be clean; Wash me, until I be whiter than snow!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 5:17–18; Hosea 6:1–2; Matthew 5:4"> Make me to hear joy and gladness, So that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:1"> Hide thy face from my sins, And blot out all my iniquities!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 4:22–24"> Create within me a clean heart, O God! Renew within me a steadfast spirit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Kings 13:23; Isaiah 63:10–11"> Cast me not away from thy presence, And take not thy holy spirit from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 13:5; Isaiah 61:10"> Restore to me the joy of thy protection, And strengthen me with a willing spirit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 22:32; Acts 26:18–20; Acts 3:19"> Then will I teach thy ways to transgressors, And sinners shall be converted to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; 2 Samuel 12:9; Psalms 26:9"> Deliver me from the guilt of blood, O God, the God of my salvation! That my tongue may sing aloud of thy goodness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 63:3–5; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 9:14"> O Lord! open thou my lips, That my mouth may show forth thy praise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 40:6; Hosea 6:6; Hebrews 10:5–6"> For thou desirest not sacrifice, else would I give it; Thou delightest not in burnt-offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:2; Psalms 147:3"> The sacrifice which God loveth is a broken spirit; A broken and contrite heart, O God! thou wilt not despise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 102:16; Psalms 122:6–9; Luke 12:32"> Do good to Zion according to thy mercy; Build up the walls of Jerusalem!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 4:5; Ephesians 5:2; Malachi 3:3"> Then shalt thou be pleased with sacrifices of righteousness, With burnt-offerings and complete offerings; Then shall bullocks be offered upon thine altar.</span>
