<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 69:14–15; Isaiah 43:2; Lamentations 3:54"> “For the leader of the music. Upon the Shoshannim. A psalm of David.” Save me, O God! For the waters press in to my very life!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 40:2; Jeremiah 38:6; Matthew 7:25"> I sink in deep mire, where is no standing; I have come into deep waters, and the waves flow over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 119:82; Isaiah 38:14"> I am weary with crying; my throat is parched; Mine eyes are wasted, while I wait for my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:25; Psalms 35:19; Psalms 109:3–5"> More numerous than the hairs of my head are they who hate me without reason; Mighty are they who seek to destroy me, being my enemies without cause: I must restore what I took not away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 38:9; Psalms 17:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> O God! thou knowest my offences, And my sins are not hidden from thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 25:3; Psalms 35:26; Luke 24:19–21"> Let not them that trust in thee through me be put to shame, O Lord Jehovah, God of hosts! Let not them that seek thee through me be confounded, O God of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:22; Jeremiah 15:15; Isaiah 50:6"> For on account of thee do I suffer reproach, And shame covereth my face!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; John 1:11; Matthew 26:70–74"> I am become a stranger to my brothers; Yea, an alien to my mother's sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 15:3; John 2:14–17; Psalms 119:139"> For zeal for thy house consumeth me, And the reproaches of them that reproach thee fall upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:13; Psalms 102:8–9; Luke 7:33–34"> When I weep and fast, That is made my reproach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Job 17:6"> When I clothe myself in sackcloth, Then I become their by-word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:15–16; Mark 15:17–19; Daniel 5:23"> They who sit in the gate speak against me, And I am become the song of drunkards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:2; Isaiah 49:8; Psalms 55:16–17"> Yet will I address my prayer to thee, O LORD! May it be in an acceptable time according to thy great goodness! Hear, O God! and afford me thy sure help!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 144:7; Psalms 109:21; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> Save me from the mire, and let me not sink; May I be delivered from my enemies,—from the deep waters!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 12:15–16; Acts 2:24; Jonah 2:2–7"> Let not the water-flood overflow me; Let not the deep swallow me up, And let not the pit close her mouth upon me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 109:21; Psalms 25:16; Psalms 63:3"> Hear me, O LORD! since great is thy loving-kindness; According to the abundance of thy tender mercies look upon me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 27:9; Psalms 102:2; Psalms 70:1"> Hide not thy face from thy servant; I am greatly distressed, O make haste to mine aid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:1; Psalms 111:9"> Draw near to me, and redeem my life; Deliver me because of my enemies!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 53:3; 1 Peter 2:23"> Thou knowest my reproach, and dishonor, and shame; All my adversaries are in thy view!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 63:5; Psalms 142:4; Mark 14:50"> Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am full of heaviness; I look for pity, but there is none; For comforters, but find none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:23"> For my food they give me gall, And in my thirst they give me vinegar to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Romans 11:8–10; 1 Peter 2:8"> May their table be to them a snare; May it be a trap to them, while they are at ease!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:6; Romans 11:10; Isaiah 6:9–10"> May their eyes be darkened, that they may not see; And cause their loins continually to shake!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 79:6; Hosea 5:10; Exodus 15:15"> Pour out upon them thine indignation, And may the heat of thine anger overtake them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 1:20; Matthew 23:38; Jeremiah 7:12–14"> Let their habitation be desolate, And let none dwell in their tents!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; Zechariah 1:15; 2 Chronicles 28:9"> For they persecute those whom thou hast smitten, And talk of the pain of those whom thou hast wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Nehemiah 4:5; 2 Timothy 4:14"> Add iniquity to their iniquity, And let them never come into thy favor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Philippians 4:3; Exodus 32:32–33; Luke 10:20"> Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; Let not their names be written with the righteous!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 91:14–16; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 20:1"> But I am poor, and sorrowful: May thine aid, O God! set me on high!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 28:7; Psalms 34:3; Psalms 118:28–29"> Then I will praise the name of God in a song; I will give glory to him with thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 50:13–14; Psalms 50:23"> More pleasing shall this be to the LORD Than a full-horned and full-hoofed bullock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 22:26; Psalms 34:2; John 16:22"> The afflicted shall see, and rejoice; The hearts of them that fear God shall be revived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 68:6; Acts 5:18–19; Zechariah 9:11–12"> For the LORD heareth the poor, And despiseth not his people in their bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 96:11; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 150:6"> Let the heaven and the earth praise him; The sea, and all that move therein!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 44:26; Psalms 51:18; Isaiah 46:13"> For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah, That they may dwell therein, and possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 102:28; Psalms 37:29; Acts 2:39"> Yea, the posterity of his servants shall possess it, And they that love him shall dwell therein.</span>
