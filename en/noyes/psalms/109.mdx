<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 83:1"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” O God of my praise! be not silent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 52:4; Proverbs 6:17; Acts 6:13"> For the mouths of the wicked and the deceitful are opened against me; They speak against me with a lying tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:7; Psalms 35:20"> They assault me on every side with words of hatred; They fight against me without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:20; Psalms 69:12–13; Psalms 35:7"> For my love they are my adversaries: But I give myself unto prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Genesis 44:4; Psalms 38:20"> They repay me evil for good, And hatred for love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Matthew 5:25; Matthew 27:4"> Set thou a wicked man over him, And let an adversary stand at his right hand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> When he is judged, may he be condemned, And may his prayer be a crime!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; Acts 1:16–26; Matthew 27:5"> May his days be few, And another take his office!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 18:21; Lamentations 5:3"> May his children be fatherless, And his wife a widow!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; Genesis 4:12–14; Isaiah 16:2"> May his children be vagabonds and beggars, And from their ruined dwellings seek their bread!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 5:5; Job 20:18; Job 18:9–19"> May a creditor seize on all that he hath, And a stranger plunder his substance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Psalms 137:8–9; James 2:13"> May there be none to show him compassion, And none to pity his fatherless children!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 37:28; Job 18:19"> May his posterity be cut off; In the next generation may his name be blotted out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Nehemiah 4:5; Jeremiah 18:23"> May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered by the LORD, And may the sin of his mother never be blotted out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Psalms 90:8; Job 18:17"> May they be before the LORD continually; And may he cut off their memory from the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; James 2:13; Psalms 37:14"> Because he remembered not to show pity, But persecuted the afflicted and the poor man, And sought the death of the broken-hearted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Proverbs 14:14; Ezekiel 35:6"> As he loved cursing, let it come upon him; As he delighted not in blessing, let it be far from him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:6; Numbers 5:22; Matthew 27:3–5"> May he be clothed with cursing as with a garment; May it enter like water into his bowels, And like oil into his bones!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:18"> May it be to him like the robe that covereth him, Like the girdle with which he is constantly girded!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 94:23"> May this be the wages of mine adversaries from the LORD, And of them that speak evil against me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:3; Psalms 69:16; Psalms 79:9–10"> But do thou, O LORD, my God! take part with me, For thine own name's sake! Because great is thy mercy, O deliver me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 86:1; Psalms 40:17; Psalms 102:4"> For I am afflicted and needy, And my heart is wounded within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 10:19; 1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 102:10–11"> I am going like a shadow; I am driven away as the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:3–4; Hebrews 12:12; Matthew 4:2"> My knees totter from fasting, And my flesh faileth of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 22:6–7; Psalms 69:19–20; Romans 15:3"> I am a reproach to my enemies; They gaze at me; they shake their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:86; Psalms 57:1; Psalms 69:13"> Help me, O LORD, my God! O save me, according to thy mercy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 37:7; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Exodus 8:19"> That they may know that this is thy hand; That thou, O Lord! hast done it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:20; John 16:22; Psalms 109:17"> Let them curse, but do thou bless! When they arise, let them be put to shame; But let thy servant rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22"> May my enemies be clothed with ignominy; May they be covered with their shame, as with a mantle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 22:22–25"> I will earnestly praise the LORD with my lips; In the midst of the multitude I will praise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 121:5; Psalms 16:8; Psalms 140:12"> For he standeth at the right hand of the poor, To save him from those who would condemn him.</span>
