<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 31:1–3; Psalms 25:2–3; 1 Peter 2:6"> In thee, O LORD! do I put my trust! Let me never be put to shame!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 116:1–2"> In thy goodness deliver and rescue me; Incline thine ear to me, and save me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 91:9; Proverbs 18:10"> Be thou the rock of my abode, where I may continually resort! Thou hast granted me deliverance; For thou art my rock and my fortress!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:1–4; Psalms 59:1–2; 2 Samuel 17:1–2"> Save me, O my God! from the hand of the wicked,-From the hand of the unjust and cruel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7; Psalms 39:7; Psalms 119:81"> For thou art my hope, O Lord Jehovah! Thou hast been my trust from my youth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 22:9–10; Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 139:15–16"> Upon thee have I leaned from my birth; From my earliest breath thou hast been my support; My song hath been continually of thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; 1 Corinthians 4:9; Acts 4:13"> I am a wonder to many, But thou art my strong refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 35:28; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 104:1"> Let my mouth be filled with thy praise; Yea, all the day long, with thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalms 71:18"> Cast me not off in mine old age; Forsake me not, When my strength faileth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 83:3; Psalms 56:6; Jeremiah 20:10"> For my enemies speak against me, And they who lay wait for my life consult together:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Psalms 3:2; Matthew 27:46"> “God,” say they, “hath forsaken him; Pursue and seize him; for he hath none to deliver him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:22; Psalms 38:21–22; Psalms 70:5"> O God! be not far from me! Come speedily to mine aid, O my God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Psalms 71:24; Psalms 35:26"> Let them perish with shame who are my enemies; Let them be covered with contempt and dishonor who seek my hurt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; 1 John 3:3; Psalms 71:6"> But I will hope continually; I shall yet praise thee more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Psalms 35:28; Psalms 71:8"> My mouth shall speak of thy goodness,—Of thy sure protection all the day long; For thy mercies are more than I can number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Psalms 51:14; Philippians 3:9"> I will celebrate thy mighty deeds, O Lord Jehovah! I will make mention of thy goodness, of thine only!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 71:5; Psalms 119:9; Psalms 119:102"> O God! thou hast taught me from my youth, And thus far have I declared thy wondrous deeds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Psalms 71:9; Psalms 78:4"> And now, when I am old and gray-headed, O God! forsake me not, Until I make known thine arm to the next generation,-Thy mighty power to all that are to come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 36:5–6; Psalms 57:10"> For thy goodness, O God! reacheth to the heavens; Wonderful things doest thou! O God! who is like unto thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 138:7; Psalms 60:3; Psalms 86:13"> Thou hast suffered us to see great and grievous troubles; Thou wilt again give us life, And wilt bring us back from the depths of the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 12:1; 2 Samuel 3:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Thou wilt increase my greatness; Thou wilt again comfort me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:1–3; 2 Kings 19:22; Psalms 33:2"> Then will I praise thee with the psaltery; Even thy faithfulness, O my God! To thee will I sing with the harp, O Holy One of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 103:4; Luke 1:46–47"> My lips shall rejoice, when I sing to thee; And my soul, which thou hast redeemed from death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 71:13; Psalms 18:37–43; 1 Corinthians 15:25"> My tongue also shall continually speak of thy righteousness: For all who seek my hurt are brought to shame and confounded.</span>
