<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:23–24; Psalms 100:1; Psalms 98:4"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm.” Shout joyfully unto God. all ye lands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 105:2–3; Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 49:13"> Sing ye the honor of his name; Make his praise glorious!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:44; Psalms 65:5; Psalms 81:15"> Say unto God, How terrible are thy doings! Through the greatness of thy power thine enemies are suppliants to thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 117:1; Psalms 96:1–2"> Let all the earth worship thee; Let it sing praise to thee, let it sing praise to thy name! &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 126:1–3; Numbers 23:23; Psalms 66:16"> Come, behold the works of God! How terrible his doings among the sons of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Exodus 15:1–21; Joshua 3:14"> He turned the sea into dry land; They went through the deep on foot; Then we rejoiced in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 11:4; Matthew 28:18; Psalms 140:8"> By his power he ruleth for ever; His eyes are fixed upon the nations; Let not the rebellious exalt themselves! &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 98:4; Revelation 19:1; Jeremiah 33:11"> O bless our God, ye nations. And make the voice of his praise to be heard!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 112:6; Psalms 94:18"> It is he who preserveth our lives, And suffereth not our feet to stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Psalms 17:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> Thou hast, indeed, proved us, O God! Thou hast tried us as silver is tried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 1:13; Ezekiel 12:13; Lamentations 3:2–66"> Thou broughtest us into a snare, And didst lay a heavy burden upon our backs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 51:23; Psalms 40:2–3"> Thou didst cause men to ride upon our heads, And we have gone through fire and water: But thou hast brought us to a place of abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 100:4; Psalms 56:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4"> I will go into thy house with burnt-offerings! I will pay thee my vows,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 11:35–36; Psalms 18:6"> The vows which my lips uttered. Which my mouth promised in my trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 51:19; 2 Samuel 6:17–19; 1 Chronicles 16:1–3"> Burnt sacrifices of fatlings will I offer to thee with the fat of rams; Bullocks, with he-goats, will I sacrifice to thee. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 71:15; Psalms 34:11; Psalms 71:18"> Come and hear, all ye who fear God, And I will relate what he hath done for me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:6; Psalms 145:1; Psalms 116:12"> I called upon him with my mouth, And praise is now upon my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 9:31; Proverbs 28:9; Proverbs 15:29"> If I had meditated wickedness in my heart, The Lord would not have heard me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 116:1–2; Hebrews 5:7; Lamentations 3:55–56"> But surely God hath heard me; He hath had regard to the voice of my supplication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 86:12–13; Psalms 68:35; Psalms 51:11"> Blessed be God, who did not reject my prayer, Nor withhold his mercy from me!</span>
