<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 10:35; Isaiah 51:9–10; Isaiah 42:13–14"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” Let God arise, and his enemies are scattered, And they who hate him flee before him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:4; Psalms 37:20; Isaiah 9:18"> As smoke is dispersed, so thou dispersest them; As wax melteth before the fire, So perish the wicked before the face of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Psalms 100:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 5:16"> But the righteous are glad and rejoice in his presence, Yea, they exult exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:26; Isaiah 12:4–6; Psalms 66:4"> Sing unto God; sing praises to his name! Prepare a way for him who rideth through the desert! Jehovah is his name; be joyful in his presence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Jeremiah 49:11; Psalms 82:3–4"> The father of the fatherless, and the protector of the widow, Is God in his holy habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:33; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 107:10"> God causeth the forsaken to dwell in houses; He leadeth forth to prosperity them that are bound; But the rebellious shall dwell in a barren land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Judges 5:4; Judges 4:14"> O God! when thou didst go before thy people, When thou didst march through the wilderness. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 5:23–25"> The earth quaked, and the heavens dropped at the presence of God; This Sinai trembled at the presence of God, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Psalms 65:9–13; Psalms 78:24–27"> Thou, O God! didst send a plentiful rain; Thou didst strengthen thy wearied inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:9–10; Psalms 74:19; Matthew 11:5"> Thy people established themselves in the land; Thou, O God! in thy goodness, didst prepare it for the needy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Psalms 40:3; Exodus 17:9–16"> The Lord gave the song of victory Of the maidens publishing glad tidings to the mighty host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 10:16; Judges 5:19; 1 Samuel 30:24"> “The kings with their armies have fled, —have fled! And the matron at home divideth the spoil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 105:37; Genesis 49:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> Truly ye may repose yourselves in the stalls, Like the wings of a dove covered with silver, And her feathers with shining gold.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 9:48; Psalms 51:7; Isaiah 1:18"> When the Most High destroyed the kings in the land, It was white &#91;&#91;with their bones&#93;&#93; like Salmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 87:1–2; Psalms 78:68–69; Deuteronomy 3:10"> Ye lofty hills, ye hills of Bashan, Ye many-topped hills, ye hills of Bashan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5; Psalms 132:13–14; Revelation 21:2–3"> Why frown ye, ye many-topped hills, At the hill in which God is pleased to dwell, In which Jehovah will dwell for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 7:10; Revelation 5:11"> The chariots of God are myriads, yea, thousands of thousands; The Lord is in the midst of them, as upon Sinai, in the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:8–10; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Revelation 21:3"> Thou hast ascended on high, Thou hast led captive the vanquished, Thou hast received gifts among men, Even the rebellious, that here thou mightst dwell, O LORD God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 46:4; Lamentations 3:23; Psalms 55:22"> Praised be the Lord daily! When we are heavy-laden, the Mighty One is our help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 1:18; 1 Samuel 2:6"> Our God is a God of salvation; From the Lord Jehovah cometh deliverance from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:13; Psalms 7:12; Psalms 110:6"> But God smiteth the head of his enemies, Even the hairy crowns of those who go on in their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:33; Ezekiel 36:24; Amos 9:2–3"> “I will bring them back,” saith the Lord, “from Bashan; I will bring them back from the deep sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Psalms 58:10; 1 Kings 22:38"> That thy foot may be dipped in their blood, That thy dogs may drink the blood of thine enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 63:2; 2 Samuel 6:12–17; 1 Chronicles 15:16–24"> We have seen thy procession, O God! The procession of my God, my king, to his sanctuary!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20"> The singers go before, the minstrels follow, Amidst damsels playing on timbrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1; Deuteronomy 33:28; Psalms 26:12"> Praise ye God in your assemblies; Praise the Lord, all ye from the fountain of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Judges 20:35; Judges 21:6–25"> Here is Benjamin, the youngest, and his leaders; The chiefs of Judah, and their band; The chiefs of Zebulon, and the chiefs of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 138:8; Psalms 71:3; Psalms 42:8"> Thy God has ordained thy strength, &#91;&#91;O Israel!&#93;&#93; Show forth thy might, O God! thou who hast wrought for us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 10:10; Psalms 76:11; Isaiah 60:6–11"> Because of thy temple in Jerusalem Shall kings bring presents to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Psalms 22:12–13; Jeremiah 50:11"> Rebuke the wild beast of the reeds, The multitude of bulls with the calves of the nations, So that they shall cast themselves down with masses of silver; Scatter thou the nations that delight in war!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 44:20"> Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall haste with outstretched hands to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 67:2–5; Romans 15:10–11; Psalms 117:1–2"> Ye kingdoms of the earth, sing unto God; Sing praises to Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 104:3; Psalms 18:10; Psalms 68:4"> To him who rideth upon the ancient heaven of heavens! Behold, he uttereth his voice, his mighty voice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 19:6; 2 Peter 1:17; 1 Chronicles 16:28–29"> Give glory to God, Whose majesty is in Israel, and whose might is in the clouds!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13"> Terrible art thou, O God! from thy sanctuary! The God of Israel giveth strength and power to his people. Praised be God!</span>
