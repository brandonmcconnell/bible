<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:1; Deuteronomy 16:18–19; Psalms 72:1–4"> “For the leader of the music. To the tune of “Do not destroy.” A psalm of David.” Do ye, indeed, administer justice faithfully, ye mighty ones? Do ye judge with uprightness, ye sons of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:20; Isaiah 10:1; Ezekiel 22:27"> Nay, in your hearts ye contrive iniquity; Your hands weigh out violence in the land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 51:5; Isaiah 48:8; Proverbs 22:15"> The wicked are estranged, from their very birth; The liars go astray as soon as they are born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 140:3; James 3:8; Ecclesiastes 10:11"> They have poison, like the poison of a serpent; Like the deaf adder's, which stoppeth her ear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; Deuteronomy 18:11"> Which listeneth not to the voice of the charmer, And of the sorcerer, skilful in incantations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Job 4:10–11; Hosea 5:14"> Break their teeth, O God! in their mouths! Break out the great teeth of the lions, O LORD!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:10; Joshua 7:5; Psalms 64:3"> May they melt like waters, which flow away; May their arrows, when they aim them, be as if cut in pieces!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:16; Ecclesiastes 6:3; Psalms 37:35–36"> May they be like the snail, which melteth away as it goeth; Like the abortion of a woman, that seeth not the sun!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Proverbs 10:25; Ecclesiastes 7:6"> Before your pots feel the heat of the thorns, Whether fresh, or burning, may they be blown away!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 68:23; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 64:10"> The righteous shall rejoice, when he seeth such vengeance; He shall bathe his feet in the blood of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; Psalms 64:9"> Then shall men say, “Truly there is a reward for the righteous! Truly there is a God who is judge upon the earth!”</span>
