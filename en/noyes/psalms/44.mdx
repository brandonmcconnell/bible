<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:3–6; Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 6:20"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of the sons of Korah.” O God! we have heard with our ears, Our fathers have told us, What deeds thou didst in their days, In the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 78:55; Exodus 15:17; Joshua 11:23"> With thine own hand didst thou drive out the nations, And plant our fathers; Thou didst destroy the nations, And cause our fathers to flourish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Joshua 24:12"> For not by their own swords did they gain possession of the land, Nor did their own arms give them victory; But thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance; For thou didst favor them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 74:12; Psalms 42:8; Psalms 149:2"> Thou art my king, O God! O send deliverance to Jacob!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:13; Psalms 60:12; Psalms 118:10–13"> Through thee we may cast down our enemies; Through thy name we may trample upon our adversaries!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Hosea 1:7; 1 Samuel 17:47"> I trust not in my bow, Nor can my sword save me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Psalms 132:18; Psalms 136:24"> But it is thou only who savest us from our enemies, And puttest to shame those who hate us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 34:2; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31; Jeremiah 9:24"> In God will we glory continually; Yea, we will praise thy name for ever! &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 60:10; Psalms 60:1"> Yet now thou hast cast us off, and put us to shame; Thou goest not forth with our armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:8; Leviticus 26:17; Joshua 7:12"> Thou makest us turn back from the enemy, And they who hate us make our goods their prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:27; Deuteronomy 28:64; Romans 8:36"> Thou makest us like sheep destined for food, And scatterest us among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Deuteronomy 32:30; Isaiah 52:3–4"> Thou sellest thy people for nought, And increasest not thy wealth by their price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Psalms 80:6; Psalms 89:41"> Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbors, A scorn and a derision to those who are around us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 19:21; Job 16:4"> Thou makest us a byword among the nations, And causest the people to shake their heads at us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 69:7; Jeremiah 3:25; Psalms 89:45"> My ignominy is continually before me, And shame covereth my face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 8:2; Psalms 74:10; Psalms 74:18"> On account of the voice of the scoffer and the reviler, And on account of the enemy and the avenger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 9:13; Deuteronomy 8:14; Deuteronomy 6:12"> All this hath come upon us; Yet have we not forgotten thee, Nor have we been false to thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:51; Psalms 119:157; Psalms 78:57"> Our hearts, have not wandered from thee, Nor have our feet gone out of thy path;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 3:5; Psalms 23:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> Though thou hast crushed us in a land of jackals, And covered us with thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 81:9; Job 11:13; Deuteronomy 6:14"> If we had forgotten the name of our God, Or stretched forth our hands to a strange God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 23:24; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Surely God would search it out; For he knoweth the secrets of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:36; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 44:11"> But for thy sake we are killed all the day; We are counted as sheep for the slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:65; Psalms 7:6; Psalms 35:23"> Awake! why sleepest thou, O Lord? Arise! cast us not off for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:24; Deuteronomy 32:20; Psalms 42:9"> Wherefore dost thou hide thy face, And forget our affliction and oppression?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:25; Lamentations 4:5; Isaiah 51:23"> Our soul is bowed down to the dust; Our body cleaveth to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 130:7–8; Psalms 25:22; Psalms 26:11"> Arise, O thou, our strength! And deliver us, for thy mercy's sake!</span>
