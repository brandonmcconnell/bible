<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 62:1; Psalms 76:11; Psalms 116:17–18"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” To thee belongeth trust, to thee praise, O God in Zion! And to thee shall the vow be performed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; Jeremiah 29:12–13; Psalms 86:9"> O Thou that hearest prayer! To thee shall all flesh come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 79:9; Hebrews 9:14"> My iniquities are heavy upon me; But thou wilt forgive our transgressions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Psalms 84:4; Psalms 23:6"> Happy is he whom thou choosest, And bringest near thee to dwell in thy courts! May we be satisfied with the blessings of thy house, Thy holy temple!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Psalms 66:3; Revelation 15:3–4"> By wonderful deeds dost thou answer us in thy goodness, O God, our salvation! Who art the confidence of all the ends of the earth, And of the most distant seas!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Micah 6:2; Habakkuk 3:6"> Thou makest fast the mountains by thy power, Being girded with strength!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 89:9; Isaiah 17:12–13; Psalms 93:3–4"> Thou stillest the roar of the sea, The roar of its waves, And the tumult of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 148:3; Psalms 136:8; Psalms 104:20–23"> They who dwell in the ends of the earth are awed by thy signs; Thou makest the outgoings of the morning and of the evening to rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:4; Psalms 104:13–15; Psalms 68:9–10"> Thou visitest the earth and waterest it; Thou enrichest it exceedingly; The river of God is full of water. Thou suppliest the earth with corn, When thou hast thus prepared it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; 1 Corinthians 3:6–7; Deuteronomy 32:2"> Thou waterest its furrows, And breakest down its ridges; Thou makest it soft with showers, And blessest its increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joel 2:21–26; Psalms 103:4; Psalms 5:12"> Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; Thy footsteps drop fruitfulness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 38:26–27; Psalms 104:10–13; Joel 2:22"> They drop it upon the pastures of the wilderness, And the hills are girded with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Acts 14:17; Isaiah 35:1–2"> The pastures are clothed with flocks, And the valleys are covered with corn; They shout, yea, they sing for joy.</span>
