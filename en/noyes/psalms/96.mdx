<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Isaiah 49:13; Psalms 98:7–9"> Let the heavens be glad, and the earth rejoice; Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12–13; Isaiah 35:1; Isaiah 42:10–11"> Let the fields be joyful, with all that is therein; Let all the trees of the forest rejoice</span>
