<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 24:19"> “A psalm of David.” Be not thou angry on account of the wicked, Nor be envious of those who do iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 92:7; Psalms 90:5–6; James 1:10–11"> For soon shall they be cut down like grass, And wither like the green herb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Matthew 6:31–33; Psalms 62:8"> Trust in the LORD, and do good; Abide in the land, and delight in faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 145:19; John 15:16"> Place thy delight in the LORD. And he will give thee thy heart's desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Peter 5:7; Philippians 4:6–7"> Commit thy way to the LORD; Trust in him, and he will give thee success!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Job 11:17; Isaiah 58:8"> He will cause thy justice to shine forth like the light, And thy righteousness like the noonday's brightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 5:7–11; Psalms 27:14; Psalms 62:5"> Hope thou patiently on the LORD, And in him place thy trust! Be not angry on account of the prosperous,—On account of him that deviseth deceit!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Proverbs 16:32; James 1:19–20"> Cease from anger; give not way to wrath; Be not provoked, so as to do evil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:21; Psalms 25:13; Psalms 37:29"> For evil-doers shall be rooted out; But they who trust in the LORD, they shall inherit the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 103:16; Psalms 37:35–36"> Yet a little while, and the wicked shall be no more; Thou mayst look for his place, and he will not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:5; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 26:3"> But the meek shall inherit the land, And delight themselves in the fulness of prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Psalms 35:16; Psalms 31:13"> The wicked man plotteth against the just, And gnasheth at him with his teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 1:26; 1 Samuel 26:10"> The Lord laugheth at him; For he seeth that his day is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Psalms 11:2; 1 John 3:12"> The wicked draw the sword, And bend their bow, To cast down the afflicted and the needy, And to slay the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14–15; Psalms 35:8; Jeremiah 51:56"> Their swords shall enter their own hearts, And their bows shall be broken in pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; 1 Timothy 6:6"> Better is the little of the righteous man Than the great abundance of the wicked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 63:8; Psalms 145:14"> For the arms of the wicked shall be broken, But the LORD will uphold the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:6; Romans 6:23; Psalms 103:17"> The LORD careth for the life of the upright, And their inheritance shall endure for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:20–22"> They shall not be ashamed in the evil time, And in the days of famine they shall have enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 68:2; Psalms 102:3; Luke 13:3"> But the wicked shall perish; Yea, the enemies of the LORD shall be consumed, like the glory of the fields; They shall be consumed into smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Psalms 112:5; Proverbs 22:7"> The wicked borroweth, and repayeth not; But the righteous is merciful and bountiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; 1 Corinthians 16:22; Galatians 3:13"> For they who are blessed by God shall inherit the land, And they who are cursed by him shall be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Psalms 119:133; Proverbs 4:26"> The steps of the good man are directed by the LORD; He delighteth himself in his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalms 145:14; Psalms 94:18"> Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down, For the LORD holdeth him by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 37:28; Psalms 112:2"> I have been young, and now am old; Yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken, Nor his offspring begging bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 112:5; Matthew 5:7; Psalms 37:21"> He is ever merciful and lendeth, And his offspring shall be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; Proverbs 16:6; 1 John 2:16–17"> Depart from evil, and do good; So thou shalt dwell in the land for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Proverbs 2:22; Psalms 21:10"> For the LORD loveth righteousness, And forsaketh not his servants; They are preserved for ever; But the posterity of the wicked shall be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:21; Psalms 37:9; 2 Peter 3:13"> The righteous shall inherit the land, And shall dwell therein for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Proverbs 25:11–13; Ephesians 4:29"> The mouth of the righteous uttereth wisdom, And his tongue speaketh what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 40:8; Deuteronomy 6:6; Isaiah 51:7"> The law of his God is in his heart; His footsteps shall not slip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Psalms 10:8–10; Psalms 37:12"> The wicked watcheth the righteous, And seeketh to slay him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 109:31; 2 Peter 2:9; Romans 8:1"> The Lord will not leave him in his hand, Nor suffer him to be condemned, when he is judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Psalms 37:9; Proverbs 20:22"> Trust in the LORD, and keep his way, And he will exalt thee to the possession of the land, Whilst thou shalt see the destruction of the wicked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 5:3; Ezekiel 31:18; Isaiah 14:14–19"> I have seen a wicked man in great power, And spreading himself like a green cedar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Acts 12:22–23; Isaiah 10:16–19"> But he passed away, and, lo! he was no more; Yea, I sought him, but he was not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 57:1–2; 2 Timothy 4:6–8"> Mark the righteous man, and behold the upright, That posterity is to the man of peace!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Proverbs 14:32; Psalms 52:5; Psalms 1:4–6"> But transgressors will all be destroyed; The posterity of the wicked shall be rooted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 91:15; Isaiah 12:2"> The salvation of the just is from the LORD. He is their strength in the time of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 31:5; 1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 6:23"> The LORD will help and deliver them; He will deliver them from their enemies, and save them, Because they trust in him.</span>
