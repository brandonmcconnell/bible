<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 32:11; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 147:1"> Rejoice, O ye righteous, in the LORD! For praise becometh the upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 150:3–6; Psalms 98:4–5; Psalms 144:9"> Praise the LORD with the harp; Sing to him with the ten-stringed psaltery!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Isaiah 42:10; 1 Chronicles 25:7"> Sing to him a new song; Play skilfully amid the sound of trumpets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 12:6; Titus 1:2; Deuteronomy 32:4"> For the word of the LORD is right, And all his acts are faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 11:7; Psalms 119:64; Psalms 104:24"> He loveth justice and equity; The earth is full of the goodness of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; 2 Peter 3:5; Job 33:4"> By the word of the LORD were the heavens made, And all the hosts of them by the breath of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:8; Joshua 3:16; Joshua 3:13"> He gathereth the waters of the sea, as a heap; He layeth up the deep in storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 14:6–7; Psalms 96:9–10; Psalms 22:27"> Let all the earth fear the LORD; Let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 1:3; Psalms 33:6; Psalms 148:5–6"> For he spake, and it was done; He commanded, and it stood fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Job 5:12–13; Isaiah 44:25"> The LORD bringeth the devices of the nations to nothing; He frustrateth the designs of kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10; Job 23:13"> The purposes of the LORD stand for ever; The designs of his heart, to all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 144:15; 1 Peter 2:9; John 15:16"> Happy the nation whose God is Jehovah; The people whom he hath chosen for his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 28:24; Psalms 11:4; Psalms 53:2"> The LORD looketh down from heaven; He beholdeth all the children of men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; 1 Kings 8:27; Isaiah 57:15"> From his dwelling-place he beholdeth all the inhabitants of the earth,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Psalms 44:21; Hosea 7:2"> He that formed the hearts of all, And observeth all their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Jeremiah 9:23; Psalms 44:6–7"> A king is not saved by the number of his forces, Nor a hero by the greatness of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; Psalms 20:7; Psalms 147:10"> The horse is a vain thing for safety, Nor can he deliver his master by his great strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalms 147:11; Job 36:7"> Behold, the eye of the LORD is upon them that fear him,—Upon them that trust in his goodness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 37:19; Proverbs 10:3; Job 5:19–22"> To save them from the power of death, And keep them alive in famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 27:14; Psalms 130:5–6"> The hope of our souls is in the LORD; He is our help and our shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Zechariah 10:7; Psalms 30:10–12"> Yea, in him doth our heart rejoice; In his holy name we have confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 13:5; Psalms 119:76; Matthew 9:29"> May thy goodness be upon us, O LORD! According as we trust in thee!</span>
