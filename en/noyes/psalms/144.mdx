<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 8:4; Job 7:17; Hebrews 2:6"> LORD, what is man, that thou art mindful of him, Or the son of man, that thou makest account of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:11; Psalms 39:5–6; Psalms 109:23"> Man is like a vapor; His day is like a shadow that passeth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:32; Psalms 18:9; Exodus 19:18"> Bow thy heavens, O LORD! and come down; Touch the mountains, so that they shall smoke!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:23; Psalms 18:13–14; Deuteronomy 32:42"> Cast forth lightnings, and scatter them; Shoot forth thine arrows, and destroy them!</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 41:6; Psalms 62:4"> Whose mouth uttereth deceit, And whose right hand is a right hand of falsehood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 33:2–3; Psalms 40:3; Psalms 149:1"> I will sing to thee a new song, O God! Upon a ten-stringed psaltery will I sing praise to thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:50; Psalms 140:7; Psalms 33:16–18"> To thee, who givest salvation to kings, Who deliverest David, thy servant, from the destructive sword!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:7–8; Isaiah 44:20; Psalms 12:2"> Rescue and deliver me from the hands of aliens, Whose mouth uttereth deceit, And whose right hand is a right hand of falsehood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; 1 Peter 3:3–6; Isaiah 44:3–5"> That our sons may be as plants, Grown up in their youth; Our daughters as corner-pillars, Hewn like those of a palace!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:13–14; Leviticus 26:10; Malachi 3:10"> That our garners may be full, Affording all kinds of store; That our sheep may bring forth thousands And ten thousands in our streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3–5; Deuteronomy 28:7; Isaiah 24:11"> That our cattle may be fruitful; That there be no breaking in, or going out; And no outcry in our streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:12; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 146:5"> Happy the people that is in such a state! Yea, happy the people whose God is Jehovah!</span>
