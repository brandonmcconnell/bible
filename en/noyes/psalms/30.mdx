<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> “A psalm of David. To the air of songs for the dedication of a house.” I will extol thee, O LORD! for thou hast lifted me up, And hast not suffered my enemies to rejoice over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; 2 Kings 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> O Jehovah, my God! I called upon thee, and thou hast healed me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonah 2:4–6"> O LORD! thou hast raised me up from the underworld; Thou hast kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; 1 Chronicles 16:4"> Sing unto the LORD, O ye his servants! And praise his holy name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> For his anger endureth but a moment, But his favor through life; In the evening sorrow may be a guest, But joy cometh in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> I said in my prosperity, “I shall never be moved!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> Thou, O LORD! by thy favor, hast made my mountain to stand strong; Thou didst hide thy face, and I was troubled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> I cried unto thee, O LORD! To the LORD I made supplication:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> “What will my blood profit thee, that I should go down to the pit? Can dust praise thee? Can it declare thy faithfulness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> Hear, O LORD! and have pity upon me! Be thou, LORD! my helper!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Jeremiah 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> Thou didst turn my mourning into dancing; Thou didst loose my sackcloth, and gird me with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> Therefore I will sing praise to thee, and not be silent. O Jehovah, my God! I will give thanks to thee for ever!</span>
