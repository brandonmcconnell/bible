<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:6; Psalms 7:1–2; Psalms 7:5"> For the enemy pursueth my life; He hath smitten me to the ground; He hath made me dwell in darkness, As those that have been dead of old.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:5–6; Psalms 77:10–12; Micah 6:5"> I remember the days of old; I meditate on all thy works; I muse on the deeds of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 63:1; Job 11:13; Psalms 84:2"> I stretch forth my hands unto thee; My soul thirsteth for thee, like a parched land.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 48:17"> Cause me so see thy loving-kindness speedily; For in thee do I trust! Make known to me the way which I should take; For to thee do I lift up my soul!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 18:10; Psalms 61:3–4; Psalms 59:1"> Deliver me, O LORD! from mine enemies; For in thee do I seek refuge!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; John 14:26; Hebrews 13:21"> Teach me to do thy will; For thou art my God! Let thy good spirit lead me in a plain path!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:1; Ephesians 2:4–5; Psalms 71:2"> Revive me, O LORD! for thy name's sake! In thy righteousness, bring me out of my distress!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 54:5; Psalms 116:16; Psalms 119:94"> And, in thy compassion, cut off mine enemies, And destroy all that distress me! For I am thy servant.</span>
