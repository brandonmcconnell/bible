<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 89:17; Psalms 45:7"> But my horn thou exaltest like the buffalo's; I am anointed with fresh oil.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 1:3; Psalms 52:8"> The righteous shall flourish like the palm-tree; They shall grow up like the cedars of Lebanon;</span>
