<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 78:1; Psalms 33:8; Matthew 11:15"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of the sons of Korah.” Hear this, all ye nations; Give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 62:9; Proverbs 22:2; Jeremiah 5:4–5"> Both high and low, rich and poor alike!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Proverbs 22:17; Matthew 12:35"> My mouth shall speak wisdom, And the meditation of my mind shall be understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Matthew 13:35; Numbers 12:8"> I will incline mine ear to a poem; I will utter my song upon the harp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 27:24; Romans 8:33–34; Amos 5:13"> Why should I fear in the days of adversity, When the iniquity of my adversaries encompasseth me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Psalms 52:7; Jeremiah 9:23"> They who trust in their riches, And glory in the greatness of their wealth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 2:6; 1 Peter 1:18"> No one can redeem his brother from death, Nor give a ransom for him to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 36:18–19"> That he should live to eternity, And not see the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:48; Psalms 16:10; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Too costly is the redemption of his life, And he giveth it up for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 39:6; Hebrews 9:27"> For he seeth that wise men die, As well as the foolish and the ignorant; They perish alike, And leave their wealth to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; Psalms 5:9; Psalms 64:6"> They imagine that their houses will endure for ever, And their dwelling-places from generation to generation: Men celebrate their names on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18–21; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 82:7"> Yet man, who is in honor, abideth not; He is like the beasts that perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 16:27–28"> Such is the way which is their confidence! And they who come after them approve their maxims. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2; Daniel 7:22"> Like sheep they are cast into the underworld; Death shall feed upon them; And the upright shall soon trample upon them. Their form shall be consumed in the underworld, And they shall no more have a dwelling-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Psalms 56:13; Psalms 86:13"> But God will redeem my life from the underworld; Yea, he will take me under his care. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 37:1; Revelation 21:26"> Be not thou afraid, when one becometh rich; When the glory of his house is increased!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:7; Job 27:19; Luke 16:24"> For, when he dieth, he will carry nothing away; His glory will not descend after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:19; Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3"> Though in his life he thought himself happy,—Though men praised thee, while thou wast in prosperity,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 33:30; Genesis 15:15; Psalms 56:13"> Yet shalt thou go to the dwelling-place of thy fathers, Who never more shall see the light!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Ecclesiastes 3:18–19; Job 4:21"> The man who is in honor, but without understanding, Is like the beasts that perish.</span>
