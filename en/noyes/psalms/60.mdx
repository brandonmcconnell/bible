O revive us again!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Hosea 6:1; Isaiah 30:26"> Thou hast made the land tremble; thou hast rent it; O heal its breaches, for it tottereth!</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 108:6–13; Psalms 17:7; Isaiah 41:10"> That thy beloved may be delivered, Save with thy right hand, and answer me!</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Deuteronomy 33:17; Joshua 13:31"> Gilead shall be mine, and mine Manasseh; Ephraim shall be my helmet, And Judah my sceptre;</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:26–31; 2 Samuel 11:1; Judges 1:12"> Who will bring me to the strong city? Who will lead me into Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 108:11; Joshua 7:12; Psalms 60:1"> Wilt not thou, O God! who didst forsake us, And didst not go forth with our armies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 146:3; Psalms 108:12; Psalms 130:8"> Give us thine aid in our distress, For vain is the help of man!</span>
