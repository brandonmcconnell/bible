<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> “For the leader of the music of the Jeduthunites. A psalm of Asaph.” I call upon God; I cry aloud for help; I call upon God, that he would hear me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> In the day of my trouble I seek the Lord; In the night is my hand stretched forth continually; My soul refuseth to be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> I remember God, and am disquieted; I think of him, and my spirit is overwhelmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> Thou keepest mine eyelids from closing; I am distressed, so that I cannot speak!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> I think of the days of old,—The years of ancient times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> I call to remembrance my songs in the night; I meditate in my heart, And my spirit inquireth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> Will the Lord be angry for ever? Will he be favorable no more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> Is his mercy utterly withdrawn for ever? Doth his promise fail from generation to generation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> Hath God forgotten to be gracious? Hath he in anger shut up his compassion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> Then I say, “This is mine affliction, A change in the right hand of the Most High.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> I remember the deeds of Jehovah; I think of thy wonders of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> I meditate on all thy works, And talk of thy doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> Thy ways, O God! are holy! Who so great a god as our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> Thou art a God who doest wonders; Thou hast manifested thy power among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> With thy strong arm thou didst redeem thy people,—The sons of Jacob and Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Habakkuk 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> The waters saw thee, O God! The waters saw thee, and feared, And the deep trembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; 2 Samuel 22:15; Habakkuk 3:11"> The clouds poured out water, The skies sent forth thunder, And thine arrows flew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; 2 Samuel 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> Thy thunder roared in the whirlwind; Thy lightning illumined the world; The earth trembled and shook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Nahum 1:3–4"> Thy way was through the sea, And thy path through great waters; And thy footsteps could not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> Thou didst lead thy people like a flock, By the hands of Moses and Aaron.</span>
