<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 12:4; Psalms 145:11–12; Romans 10:13"> O give thanks unto the LORD; Call upon his name; Make known his deeds among the people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:27; Psalms 98:5; Isaiah 12:5–6"> Sing unto him; sing psalms unto him; Tell ye of all his wondrous works!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:29"> Glory ye in his holy name; Let the hearts of them that seek the LORD rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 27:8; Zephaniah 2:2–3; 2 Chronicles 6:41"> Seek the LORD, and his majesty; Seek his face continually!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:11; Psalms 119:13; Deuteronomy 32:7"> Remember the wonders he hath wrought, His miracles and the judgments of his mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 44:1–2; John 15:16; 1 Peter 2:9"> Ye offspring of Abraham his servant, Ye children of Jacob his chosen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:9; Deuteronomy 26:17–18; Exodus 20:2"> Jehovah, he is our God, His judgments are over all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Psalms 106:45; Psalms 111:5"> He remembereth his covenant for ever, And the promise to a thousand generations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:2; Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 26:3"> The covenant which he made with Abraham, And the oath which he gave to Isaac;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; 2 Samuel 23:5; Genesis 28:13–15"> Which he confirmed to Jacob for a decree, And to Israel for an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Genesis 15:18; Psalms 78:55"> “To thee,” said he, “will I give the land of Canaan For the lot of your inheritance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Deuteronomy 7:7; Hebrews 11:9"> When they were yet few in number, Very few, and strangers in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When they went from nation to nation, From one kingdom to another people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 35:5; Exodus 7:16–17; Genesis 26:14–33"> He suffered no man to oppress them; Yea, he rebuked kings for their sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 26:11; Genesis 20:7"> “Touch not,” said he, “mine anointed, And do my prophets no harm!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Genesis 41:54; Isaiah 3:1"> Again, when he commanded a famine in the land, And broke the whole staff of bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 7:9; Genesis 37:36; Genesis 45:4–5"> He sent a man before them; Joseph was sold as a slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 16:24; Genesis 39:20; Genesis 40:15"> His feet they hurt with fetters; He was bound in chains of iron;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:10; Proverbs 21:1; Genesis 40:20–21"> Until his prediction came to pass, And the word of the LORD proved him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:14"> Then the king sent, and loosed him; The ruler of nations, and set him free;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:40–44; Genesis 45:8; Genesis 41:55"> He made him governor of his house, And lord of all his possessions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Genesis 41:33; Genesis 41:38"> To bind his princes at his pleasure, And teach his counsellors wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 7:11–15; Acts 13:17; Psalms 78:51"> Israel also came into Egypt, And Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:3; Exodus 1:7–9; Acts 7:17"> Where God increased his people greatly, And made them stronger than their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:19; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 9:16"> He turned their hearts to hate his people, And form devices against his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 29:5–46; Exodus 28:12"> Then sent he Moses his servant, And Aaron, whom he had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:43–51; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Psalms 105:23"> They showed his signs among them, And his wonders in the land of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 99:7; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:2"> He sent darkness upon them, and made it dark; And they did not disobey his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 7:20–21; Psalms 78:44; Isaiah 50:2"> He turned their waters into blood, And caused their fish to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:13–14; Exodus 8:3–14; Psalms 78:45"> Their land brought forth frogs in abundance, Even in the chambers of their kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 8:16–18; Exodus 8:21–24; Isaiah 7:18"> He spake, and there came flies, And lice in all their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 78:47–48; Exodus 9:18–28; Revelation 8:7"> Instead of rain he gave them hail, And flaming fire in their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 78:47; Revelation 9:4"> He smote also their vines and fig-trees, And broke the trees of their coasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 78:46; Joel 1:4–7"> He spake, and the locusts came. Destructive locusts without number,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Which ate up all the herbage in their land, And devoured the fruits of their fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Exodus 12:29–30; Psalms 78:51"> Then he smote all the first-born in their land, The first-fruits of all their strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Exodus 3:22; Acts 13:17"> He led forth his people with silver and gold; Nor was there one feeble person in all their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 15:16; Genesis 35:5"> Egypt was glad when they departed, For their terror had fallen upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 78:14; Isaiah 4:5"> He spread out a cloud for a covering, And fire to give light by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 78:18; Numbers 11:31–33; Joshua 5:12"> They asked, and he brought quails, And satisfied them with the bread of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; 1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6"> He opened the rock, and the waters gushed forth, And ran in the dry places like a river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Luke 1:72–73; Genesis 12:7"> For he remembered his holy promise, Which he had made to Abraham his servant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Acts 7:36; Jeremiah 31:11–12"> And he led forth his people with joy, And his chosen with gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–11; Joshua 24:13; Psalms 78:55"> He gave to them the lands of the nations, And they inherited the labor of the peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Titus 2:14; Ephesians 2:8–10"> That they might observe his statutes, And obey his laws. Praise ye the LORD!</span>
