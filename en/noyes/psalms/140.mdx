<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:2; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 119:57"> I say to Jehovah, Thou art my God; Hear, O Jehovah! the voice of my supplication!</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 21:9; Revelation 21:8"> Let burning coals fall upon them; May they be cast into the fire, And into deep waters from which they shall not arise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:21; Proverbs 18:21; Isaiah 3:11"> The slanderer shall not be established upon the earth; Evil shall pursue the violent man to destruction.</span>
