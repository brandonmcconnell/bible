<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Psalms 56:1; Psalms 31:23"> “A psalm off David.” Preserve me, O God! for to thee do I look for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; Psalms 31:14; Psalms 89:26"> I have said to Jehovah, Thou art my Lord; I have no happiness beyond thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 12:26; Hebrews 6:10; Proverbs 13:20"> The holy that are in the land, and the excellent,—in them is all my delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Joshua 23:7; Psalms 32:10"> They who hasten after other gods shall have multiplied sorrows; Their drink-offerings of blood I will not offer, Nor will I take their names upon my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Psalms 142:5; Lamentations 3:24"> Jehovah is my portion and my cup; Thou wilt maintain my lot!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:19; Ephesians 1:18; Psalms 21:1–3"> My portion hath fallen to me in pleasant places; Yea, I have a goodly inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:24; Isaiah 50:4; Psalms 77:6"> I will bless the LORD, who careth for me; Yea, in the night my heart admonisheth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 62:6; Acts 2:25–28; Psalms 121:5"> I set the LORD before me at all times; Since he is at my right hand, I shall not fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:26; Psalms 30:12; Psalms 57:8"> Therefore my heart is glad, and my spirit rejoiceth; Yea, my flesh dwelleth in security.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:27–31; Acts 13:35–38; Psalms 49:15"> For thou wilt not give me up to the underworld; Nor wilt thou suffer thy holy one to see the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Jude 1:24; Psalms 36:7–8"> Thou wilt show me the path of life; In thy presence is fulness of joy; At thy right hand are pleasures for evermore.</span>
