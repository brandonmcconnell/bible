<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 57:7–11; Psalms 145:21–2; Psalms 71:15"> “A psalm of David.” O God! my heart is strengthened! I will sing and give thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 69:30; Psalms 81:2; Psalms 33:2"> Awake, my soul! awake, my psaltery and harp! I will wake with the early dawn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Psalms 22:22; Psalms 117:1"> I will praise thee, O LORD! among the nations; I will sing to thee among the peoples!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Ephesians 2:4–7; Psalms 103:11"> For thy mercy reacheth to the heavens, And thy truth above the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 57:5; Matthew 6:9–10; Psalms 8:1"> Exalt thyself, O God! above the heavens, And thy glory above all the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 60:5–12; 2 Chronicles 32:20–22; Colossians 3:12"> That thy beloved ones may be delivered, Save with thy right hand, and answer me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Joshua 20:7; 2 Peter 1:3–4"> God promiseth in his holiness; I will rejoice; I shall yet divide Shechem, And measure out the valley of Succoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 49:10; Psalms 122:5; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Gilead shall be mine, and mine Manasseh; Ephraim shall be my helmet, And Judah my sceptre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29–32; Ruth 4:7–8; 2 Samuel 21:15–22"> Moab shall be my washbowl; Upon Edom shall I cast my shoe; I shall triumph over Philistia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Obadiah 1:3–4; Isaiah 63:1–6; Psalms 20:6–8"> Who will bring me to the strong city? Who will lead me into Edom?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:9; 2 Chronicles 13:12; 1 Samuel 17:36"> Wilt not thou, O God! who didst forsake us, Who didst not go forth with our armies?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–8; Psalms 146:3–5; Isaiah 31:3"> Give us thine aid in our distress, For vain is the help of man!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:6–13; Psalms 60:12; Isaiah 63:3"> Through God we shall do valiantly; For he will tread down our enemies.</span>
