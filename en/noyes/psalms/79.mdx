<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 3:12; 2 Chronicles 36:19; Psalms 74:1–4"> “A psalm of Asaph.” O God! the nations have come into thine inheritance; They have polluted thy holy temple; They have made Jerusalem a heap of ruins!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> They have given the dead bodies of thy servants to be food for the birds of heaven, The flesh of thy holy ones to the wild beasts of the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> Their blood have they shed like water around Jerusalem, And there was none to bury them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> We have become the reproach of our neighbors,—The scorn and derision of those around us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> How long, O LORD! wilt thou be angry for ever? How long shall thy jealousy burn like fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> Pour out thy wrath on the nations which acknowledge thee not. And on the kingdoms which call not upon thy name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> For they have devoured Jacob, And laid waste his dwelling-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> O remember not against us former iniquities; Let thy tender mercy speedily succor us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> For we are brought very low! Help us, O God of our salvation! for the honor of thy name; F or thy name's sake save us, and forgive our iniquities!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?” May the revenging of the blood of thy servants, which hath been shed, Be manifested among the nations before our eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> Let the cry of the prisoner come before thee! According to the greatness of thy power preserve those that are appointed to die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> And return sevenfold into our neighbors' bosoms The reproach with which they have reproached thee, O Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> So shall we, thy people, and the flock of thy pasture, Give thanks to thee for ever. And show forth thy praise to all generations.</span>
