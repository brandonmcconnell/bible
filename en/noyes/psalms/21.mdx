<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Psalms 62:7; Psalms 28:7"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” The king rejoiceth in thy strength, O LORD! Yea, he doth greatly exult in thy protection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Psalms 20:4–5; Psalms 2:8–9"> Thou hast given him his heart's desire, And hast not denied him the request of his lips. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> Yea, thou hast met him with rich blessings, Thou hast placed a crown of pure gold upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; Revelation 1:18; Psalms 119:77"> He asked life of thee; thou gavest it him; Even long life, enduring for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; John 17:22; Revelation 5:8–13"> Great is his glory through thine aid; Honor and majesty hast thou laid upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 2:28; Psalms 45:7; Psalms 16:11"> Thou hast made him blessed for evermore; Thou hast made him glad with the joy of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 91:2; Psalms 20:7–8"> For the king trusteth in the LORD; And through the goodness of the Most High he shall never fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10; Psalms 72:9; Psalms 110:1–2"> Thy hand shall overtake all thine enemies; Thy right hand shall overtake them that hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 18:8"> Thou wilt make them like a burning oven in the time of thine anger; Jehovah shall swallow them up in his wrath, And the fire shall devour them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; 1 Kings 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> Their offspring shalt thou destroy from the earth, And their posterity from the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:2; Psalms 2:1; Psalms 31:13"> For they spread a net of mischief against thee; They devised plots against thee, but they did not prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> Therefore thou wilt cause them to turn their backs; Thou wilt make ready thine arrows upon the strings against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 19:1–6; Revelation 15:3–4"> Exalt thyself, O LORD! by thy strength! So will we sing, and praise thy mighty deeds.</span>
