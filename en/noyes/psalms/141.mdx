<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:5; Revelation 3:19; Galatians 6:1"> Let the righteous smite me,—it shall be a kindness; Let him reprove me, and it shall be oil for my head; Let him do it again, and my head shall not refuse it; But now I pray against their wickedness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:12; 1 Chronicles 12:38; 1 Chronicles 11:1–3"> When their judges are hurled over the side of the rock, They shall hear how pleasant are my words.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 119:110; Luke 20:20"> Preserve me from the snares which they have laid for me, And from the nets of evil-doers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:8; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 64:7–8"> Let the wicked fall together into their own traps, Whilst I make my escape!</span>
