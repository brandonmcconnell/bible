<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 12:3; Jeremiah 17:9–10"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” O LORD! thou hast searched me and known me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:4; 2 Kings 19:27; Psalms 94:11"> Thou knowest my sitting-down and my rising-up; Thou understandest my thoughts from afar!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> Thou seest my path and my lying-down, And art acquainted with all my ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalms 50:19–21"> For before the word is upon my tongue, Behold, O LORD! thou knowest it altogether!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> Thou besettest me behind and before, And layest thine hand upon me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; It is high, I cannot attain to it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Jonah 1:3; Jonah 1:10"> Whither shall I go from thy spirit, And whither shall I flee from thy presence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonah 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> If I ascend into heaven, thou art there! If I make my bed in the underworld, behold, thou art there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 19:6; Psalms 74:16–17; Malachi 4:2"> If I take the wings of the morning, And dwell in the remotest parts of the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 73:23"> Even there shall thy hand lead me, And thy right hand shall hold me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremiah 23:24; Psalms 94:7"> If I say, “Surely the darkness shall cover me;” Even the night shall be light about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee, But the night shineth as the day; The darkness and the light are both alike to thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 44:2"> For thou didst form my reins; Thou didst weave me in my mother's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 1:26–27; Psalms 111:2; Psalms 104:24"> I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made; Marvellous are thy works, And this my soul knoweth full well!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 139:13; Job 10:8–11"> My frame was not hidden from thee, When I was made in secret. When I was curiously wrought in the lower parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 56:8; Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12"> Thine eyes did see my substance, while yet unformed, And in thy book was every thing written; My days were appointed before one of them existed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Isaiah 55:8–9"> How precious to me are thy thoughts, O God! How great is the sum of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 3:5; Psalms 139:3; Psalms 40:5"> If I should count them, they would outnumber the sand: When I awake, I am still with thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 5:6; Psalms 119:115; Isaiah 11:4"> O that thou wouldst slay the wicked, O God! Ye men of blood, depart from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jude 1:15; Exodus 20:7; Psalms 74:18"> For they speak against thee wickedly; Thine enemies utter thy name for falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; Psalms 31:6; Psalms 26:5"> Do I not hate them that hate thee, O LORD? Do I not abhor them that rise up against thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 101:3–8; Luke 14:26"> Yea, I hate them with perfect hatred; I count them mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 26:2; Job 31:6; 1 Peter 1:7"> Search me, O God! and know my heart; Try me, and know my thoughts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Jeremiah 17:9–10; Psalms 25:8–9"> And see if the way of trouble be within me, And lead me in the way everlasting!</span>
