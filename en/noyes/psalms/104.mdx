<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 103:22; Psalms 145:3; Psalms 96:6"> Bless the LORD, O my soul! O LORD, my God! thou art very great! Thou art clothed with glory and majesty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 45:12"> He covereth himself with light as with a garment; He spreadeth out the heavens like a curtain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 9:6; 2 Samuel 22:11; Isaiah 19:1"> He layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters; He maketh the clouds his chariot; He rideth upon the wings of the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; 2 Kings 2:11; 2 Kings 6:17"> He maketh the winds his messengers, The flaming lightnings his ministers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 26:7; Psalms 24:2; Psalms 33:9"> He established the earth on its foundations; It shall not be removed for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:19; 2 Peter 3:5; Genesis 1:2–10"> Thou didst cover it with the deep as with a garment; The waters stood above the mountains!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Genesis 8:1; Mark 4:39"> At thy rebuke they fled; At the voice of thy thunder they hasted away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 8:5; Psalms 33:7"> The mountains rose, the valleys sank, In the place which thou didst appoint for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Genesis 9:11–15; Jeremiah 5:22"> Thou hast established a bound which the waters may not pass, That they may not return, and cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:18; Psalms 107:35; Isaiah 35:7"> He sendeth forth the springs in brooks; They run among the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 145:16; Job 39:5–8; Psalms 104:13"> They give drink to all the beasts of the forest; In them the wild asses quench their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:11; Psalms 148:10; Matthew 8:20"> About them the birds of heaven have their habitation; They sing among the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Jeremiah 10:13; Jeremiah 14:22"> He watereth the hills from his chambers; The earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Job 28:5; Psalms 147:8–9"> He causeth grass to spring up for cattle, And herbage for the service of man, To bring forth food out of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 10:19; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And wine that gladdeneth the heart of man, Making his face to shine more than oil, And bread that strengtheneth man's heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 24:6; Psalms 92:2; Psalms 29:5"> The trees of the LORD are full of sap, The cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 104:12; Leviticus 11:19; Daniel 4:21"> There the birds build their nests; In the cypresses the stork hath her abode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 30:26; Job 39:1; Leviticus 11:5"> The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats, And the rocks for the conies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 136:7–9"> He appointed the moon to mark seasons; The sun knoweth when to go down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Psalms 74:16; Amos 1:13"> Thou makest darkness, and it is night, When all the beasts of the forest go forth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 38:39; Joel 1:20; Psalms 34:10"> The young lions roar for prey, And seek their food from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 37:8; Nahum 3:17; John 3:20"> When the sun ariseth, they withdraw themselves, And lie down in their dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 5:12; Ephesians 4:28"> Man goeth forth to his work, And to his labor, until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:12; Psalms 40:5; Psalms 50:10–12"> O LORD! how manifold are thy works! In wisdom hast thou made them all! The earth is full of thy riches!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 69:34; Psalms 95:4–5; Genesis 1:28"> Lo! this great and wide sea! In it are moving creatures without number, Animals small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 107:23; Isaiah 27:1; Psalms 74:14"> There go the ships; There is the leviathan, which thou hast made to play therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> All these wait on thee To give them their food in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 145:16"> Thou givest it to them, they gather it; Thou openest thine hand, they are satisfied with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 34:14–15; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> Thou hidest thy face, they are confounded; Thou takest away their breath, they die, And return to the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Revelation 21:5"> Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created, And thou renewest the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; 2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:21"> The glory of the LORD shall endure for ever; The LORD shall rejoice in his works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 144:5; Psalms 97:4–5; Exodus 19:18"> He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth; He toucheth the hills, and they smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Psalms 146:2; Psalms 145:1–2"> I will sing to the LORD as long as I live, I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:17–18; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 1:2"> May my meditation be acceptable to him! I will rejoice in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 103:22–1; Psalms 37:38; Psalms 106:48"> May sinners perish from the earth, And the wicked be no more! Bless the LORD, O my soul! Praise ye the LORD!</span>
