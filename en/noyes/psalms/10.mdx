<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Psalms 27:9; Psalms 13:1–3"> Why standest thou afar off, O LORD? Why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 59:12; Psalms 36:11; Psalms 7:16"> Through the haughtiness of the wicked the poor are in distress; They are caught in the wiles which are contrived for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 94:4; Luke 12:19; Zechariah 11:5–8"> The wicked boasteth of his heart's desire; The rapacious renounceth and contemneth Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 14:1–2; Acts 8:22; Romans 1:28"> The wicked &#91;&#91;saith&#93;&#93; in his haughtiness, “He careth not!” All his thoughts are, “There is no God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:5–6; Isaiah 26:11; Psalms 12:5"> His course is always prosperous; Far in the heights are thy judgments from him; As for all his enemies, he puffeth at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:11; Matthew 24:48; Isaiah 56:12"> He saith in his heart, “I shall never fall; I shall never be in adversity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 140:3"> His mouth is full of perjury, deceit, and oppression; Mischief and injustice are upon his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:14; 1 Samuel 22:18; Jeremiah 22:17"> He sitteth in the lurking-places of the villages; In secret places doth he murder the innocent; His eyes are secretly fixed upon the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 17:12; Psalms 59:3; Micah 7:2"> He secretly lieth in wait, like a lion in a thicket; He lieth in wait to seize upon the helpless; He catcheth the poor, drawing him into his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21–22; 1 Samuel 18:21–26; 2 Samuel 15:5"> He croucheth, and lowereth himself, And the wretched fall into his paws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 94:7; Psalms 73:11"> He saith in his heart, “God doth forget; He hideth his face; he doth never see it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 5:9; Psalms 9:12; Psalms 94:2"> Arise, O LORD! O God, lift up thine hand: Forget not the distressed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9–10; Psalms 74:10; Genesis 9:5"> Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God, And say in his heart, “He careth not for it”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 146:9; Psalms 55:22"> Thou dost see it; yea, thou beholdest malice and oppression, And markest it upon thy hand! The poor committeth himself to thee; Thou art the helper of the fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:12; Psalms 37:17; Job 38:15"> Break thou the arm of the unjust and wicked man; Seek out his wickedness, till thou canst find none!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:10; Jeremiah 10:10; 1 Timothy 1:17"> Jehovah is king for ever and ever; The gentiles shall perish out of his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; 2 Chronicles 34:27; Psalms 34:15"> Thou, O LORD! wilt hear the desires of the distressed; Thou wilt strengthen their hearts; Thou wilt lend a listening ear!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:9; Psalms 82:3; Isaiah 11:4"> Thou wilt maintain the cause of the fatherless and the oppressed, That henceforth none may be driven from the land.</span>
