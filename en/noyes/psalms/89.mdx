<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 101:1; Psalms 89:8; Psalms 119:90"> “A psalm of Ethan, the Ezrahite.” I will sing of the mercies of the LORD for ever; With my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 36:5; Nehemiah 9:31; Psalms 89:5"> For I know that thy mercy endureth for ever; Thou hast established thy truth like the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; 2 Samuel 7:10–16; Psalms 89:34–35"> “I have made a covenant with my chosen; I have sworn to David, my servant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–16; Luke 1:32–33; Psalms 132:12"> Thy family I will establish for ever, And build up thy throne to all generations.” &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 19:1; Isaiah 44:23"> The heavens shall praise thy wonders, O LORD! And the assembly of the holy ones thy truth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 86:8; Psalms 71:19; Psalms 113:5"> Who in the heavens can be compared to Jehovah? Who is like Jehovah among the sons of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 47:2; Psalms 76:7–11; Hebrews 12:28–29"> A God greatly to be feared in the assembly of the holy ones, And to be had in reverence above all who are around him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 71:19; 1 Samuel 2:2; Jeremiah 32:17"> O Jehovah, God of hosts! Who is mighty like thee, O Jehovah? And thy faithfulness is round about thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:25–29; Mark 4:41"> Thou rulest the raging of the sea; When the waves thereof rise, thou stillest them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 87:4; Psalms 68:30; Psalms 144:6"> Thou didst break Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; Thou didst scatter thine enemies with thy mighty arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:11; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 115:16"> The heavens are thine; thine also is the earth; The world and all that is therein, thou didst found them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:1; Job 26:7; Joshua 19:22"> The North and the South were created by thee; Tabor and Hermon rejoice in thy name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:34–35; Psalms 89:10; Psalms 62:11"> Thine is a mighty arm; Strong is thy hand, and high thy right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Proverbs 16:12; Psalms 45:6–7"> Justice and equity are the foundation of thy throne; Mercy and truth go before thy face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Job 29:3; Psalms 4:6"> Happy the people that know the trumpet's sound! They walk, O LORD! in the light of thy countenance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:9; Luke 1:47"> In thy name they daily rejoice, And in thy righteousness they glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Psalms 92:10; Psalms 148:14"> For thou art the glory of their strength; Yea, through thy favor our horn exalteth itself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 47:9; Psalms 71:22; Isaiah 33:22"> For from Jehovah is our shield, And from the Holy One of Israel is our king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; 1 Kings 11:34; Psalms 89:3"> Once thou spakest in a vision to thy holy one, And saidst,—”I have laid help on one that is mighty; I have exalted one chosen from the people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; Isaiah 61:1–3; 1 Samuel 16:1"> I have found David, my servant; With my holy oil have I anointed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 18:32–39; Psalms 89:13"> With him shall my hand be established, And my arm shall strengthen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:9; 2 Samuel 7:10; Matthew 4:1–10"> The enemy shall not have power over him, Nor shall the unrighteous man oppress him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 3:1; Psalms 18:40"> For I will beat down his foes before him, And overthrow them that hate him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:20; Psalms 91:14"> My faithfulness and mercy shall be with him, And through my name shall his horn be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Psalms 2:8; Psalms 72:8–11"> I will extend his hand to the sea, And his right hand to the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 2 Samuel 22:47; Psalms 95:1"> He shall say to me, 'Thou art my father, My God, and the rock of my salvation!'</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; Colossians 1:18; Psalms 2:7"> I will also make him my first-born, Highest of the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 33:20–21; Acts 13:32–34"> My mercy I will continue to him for ever; My covenant with him shall be steadfast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 89:4; Deuteronomy 11:21; Psalms 89:36"> I will make his family to endure for ever; And his throne shall be as lasting as the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 119:53"> Should his children forsake my law, And walk not in my statutes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 55:20"> Should they break my commandments, And observe not my precepts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:14; Hebrews 12:6–11; Proverbs 3:11–12"> I will punish their transgressions with a rod, And their iniquity with stripes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; 2 Samuel 7:15; Lamentations 3:31–32"> But my kindness will I not withdraw from him, Nor suffer my faithfulness to fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35; James 1:17"> I will not break my covenant, Nor alter what hath gone from my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 132:11; Hebrews 6:13; Titus 1:2"> Once have I sworn in my holiness, That I will not be false unto David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 72:5; Psalms 89:29; Isaiah 59:21"> His family shall endure for ever, And his throne as the sun before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35–36; Genesis 1:14–18; Psalms 104:19"> It shall be established for ever like the moon; Like the faithful witness in the sky.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Deuteronomy 32:19; Psalms 106:40"> But now thou forsakest and abhorrest, And art angry with, thine anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Lamentations 5:16; Psalms 74:7; Psalms 89:34–36"> Thou hast made void the covenant with thy servant; Thou hast cast his crown to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Psalms 80:12; Lamentations 2:2; Lamentations 2:5"> Thou hast broken down all his hedges; Thou hast brought his strongholds to ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Lamentations 5:1; Ezekiel 5:14–15"> All who pass by plunder him; He is a reproach to his neighbors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Revelation 11:10; Deuteronomy 28:43"> Thou hast lifted up the right hand of his enemies; Thou hast made all his adversaries to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:8–12; Numbers 14:42; Psalms 44:10"> Yea, thou hast turned the edge of his sword, And made him unable to stand in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 4:1–2; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–10; Psalms 89:39"> Thou hast brought his glory to an end, And hast cast down his throne to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 109:29; Psalms 44:15; Micah 7:10"> Thou hast shortened the days of his youth; Thou hast covered him with shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 79:5; Psalms 78:63; Psalms 88:14"> How long, O LORD! wilt thou hide thyself for ever? How long shall thine anger burn like fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Job 14:1; Job 10:9; Job 7:7"> Remember how short is my life, To what frailty thou hast created all men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Hebrews 11:5; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> What man liveth, and seeth not death? Who can deliver himself from the underworld?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:15; Hebrews 7:21; Isaiah 55:3"> Where, Lord, is thy former loving-kindness Which thou didst swear to David in thy truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 74:22; Psalms 74:18; Psalms 69:9"> Remember, O Lord! the reproach of thy servants, How I bear in my bosom the taunts of all the many nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14–16; Psalms 74:10; 2 Samuel 16:7–8"> With which thine enemies have reproached me, O LORD! With which they have reproached the footsteps of thine anointed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Psalms 41:13; 1 Timothy 1:17"> &#91;Praised be Jehovah for ever! Amen, yea, amen!&#93;</span>
