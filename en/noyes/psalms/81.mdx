<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:7; Psalms 66:1; Psalms 100:1–2"> “For the leader of the music. On the Gittith. A psalm of Asaph.” Sing joyfully to God, our strength! Shout with gladness to the God of Jacob!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 149:1–3; Psalms 92:3; Colossians 3:16"> Raise a song, and strike the timbrel, The sweet-sounding harp, and the psaltery!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 10:1–10; Numbers 15:3; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> Blow the trumpet at the new moon; At the full moon, also, on our festal day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For this is a statute for Israel, A law of the God of Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 114:1; Deuteronomy 28:49; 1 Corinthians 14:21–22"> He appointed it as a memorial in Joseph, When he went out of the land of Egypt, Where he heard a language which he knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Isaiah 10:27; Exodus 6:6"> “I relieved &#91;&#91;said he&#93;&#93; thy shoulders from their burden; Thy hands were removed from the hod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 19:19; Numbers 20:13; Exodus 2:23"> Thou didst call in trouble, and I delivered thee; In the secret place of thunder I answered thee; I proved thee at the waters of Meribah. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:7; John 3:11; Isaiah 1:19"> Hear, O my people! and I will admonish thee! O Israel! that thou wouldst hearken to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:12; Deuteronomy 32:12; Deuteronomy 6:14"> Let there be no strange god within thee, Nor worship thou any foreign god!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:7; Psalms 107:9; Exodus 20:2"> I Jehovah am thy God, Who brought thee out of the land of Egypt: Open wide thy mouth, and I will fill it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 7:23–24; Jeremiah 2:11–13"> But my people would not listen to my voice, And Israel would not hearken to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:24; Jeremiah 7:24; Isaiah 30:1"> So I gave them up to the obstinacy of their hearts, And they walked according to their own devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 5:29; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> “O that my people had hearkened to me! That Israel had walked in my ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 2:20–23; Numbers 14:9"> Soon would I have brought low their enemies, And turned my hand against their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 1:30; Exodus 20:5"> The haters of Jehovah should have become suppliants to them, And their prosperity should have endured for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 147:14; 1 Samuel 14:25–26"> With the finest of the wheat I would have fed them, And with honey out of the rock would I have satisfied them.”</span>
