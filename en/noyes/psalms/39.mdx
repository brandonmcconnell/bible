<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Psalms 141:3; James 1:26"> “A psalm of David. For the leader of the music of the Jeduthunites.” I said, I will take heed to my ways, That I may not sin with my tongue; I will keep my mouth with a bridle, While the wicked is before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:20; Psalms 38:13–14; Job 32:19–20"> I was dumb with silence; I spake not even what was good; But my pain was increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:32; Jeremiah 20:9; Ezekiel 3:14"> My heart was hot within me; In my anguish the fire burst forth, And I spake with my tongue:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Psalms 103:14; Job 14:13"> LORD, make me to know mine end, And the number of my days, That I may know how frail I am!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 90:9–10; Psalms 144:4; Psalms 89:47"> Behold, thou hast made my days as a hand-breadth, And my life is as nothing before thee; Yea, every man in his firmest state is altogether vanity. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:20–21; James 4:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> Surely every man walketh in a vain show; Surely he disquieteth himself in vain; He heapeth up riches, and knoweth not who shall gather them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Psalms 38:15; Psalms 119:81"> What, then, O Lord! is my hope? My hope is in thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 44:13; Psalms 65:3; Joel 2:17"> Deliver me from all my transgressions; Let me not be the reproach of scoffers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:10; 2 Samuel 16:10; Leviticus 10:3"> Yet I am dumb; I open not my mouth; For thou hast done it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:34; Job 13:21; Psalms 25:16–17"> But remove from me thine infliction; For I am perishing by the blow of thine hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:28; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32; Isaiah 50:9"> When thou with rebukes dost chasten man for iniquity, Thou causest his glory to waste away like a moth! Surely every man is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13; 2 Kings 20:5"> Hear my prayer, O LORD! Give ear to my cry; Be not silent at my tears! For I am but a stranger with thee, A sojourner, as all my fathers were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 10:20–21; Job 14:10–12; Job 14:5–6"> O spare me, that I may recover strength, Before I go away, and be no more!</span>
