<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 34:4; Psalms 143:1–3; Lamentations 3:55–56"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” Hear my voice, O God! when I pray! Preserve my life from the terrors of the enemy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 143:9; Psalms 59:2; Psalms 109:2–3"> Hide me from the assembly of the wicked, From the brawling crowd of evil-doers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:7; James 3:6–8"> For like a sword they sharpen their tongues, Like arrows they aim their poisoned words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:19; Psalms 11:2; 1 Peter 2:22–23"> To shoot in secret at the upright; Suddenly do they shoot at him without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 140:5; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> They prepare themselves for an evil deed; They commune of laying secret snares: “Who,” say they, “will see them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9–10; Matthew 26:59; Psalms 5:9"> They meditate crimes: “We have finished,” say they, “our plans!” The heart and bosom of every one of them are deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Proverbs 6:15"> But God will shoot an arrow at them; Suddenly shall they be wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 12:13; Psalms 140:9"> Thus their own tongues shall bring them down; All who see them will flee away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:10; Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 40:3"> Then will all men stand in awe, And declare what God hath done, And attentively consider his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 32:11–1; Psalms 40:3; 1 Corinthians 1:30–31"> The righteous shall rejoice, and trust in the LORD; All the upright in heart shall glory.</span>
