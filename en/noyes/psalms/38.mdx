<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; Hebrews 12:5–11"> “A psalm of David. To bring to remembrance.” O LORD! rebuke me not in thy wrath, Nor chasten me in thy hot displeasure!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:4; Psalms 32:4; Psalms 64:7"> For thine arrows have deeply pierced me, And thy hand hath been heavy upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> There is no soundness in my flesh, because of thine anger; Nor rest in my bones, because of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> For my iniquities have gone over my head; Like a heavy burden, they are more than I can bear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:5; Jeremiah 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> My wounds putrefy and are loathsome on account of my folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 30:28; Psalms 35:14; Psalms 42:9"> I am bent; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> For my loins are full of burning heat, And there is no soundness in my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> I am weakened and bruised exceedingly; I roar by reason of the disquietude of my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> O Lord! thou knowest all my desire, And my groaning is not hidden from thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> My heart panteth; my strength faileth me; The very light of my eyes is gone from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Job 19:13–17; Luke 23:49"> My friends and acquaintance keep aloof from my woe, And my kinsmen stand afar off:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:20; Psalms 140:5; 2 Samuel 17:1–3"> While they who seek my life lay snares for me; They who seek my hurt threaten destruction, And meditate deceit all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; Psalms 39:9"> But I, like a deaf man, hear not; And, like a dumb man, open not my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:13; John 8:6; Mark 15:3–5"> I am like one who heareth nothing, And in whose mouth is no reply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 39:7; Psalms 17:6; Psalms 138:3"> For in thee, O LORD! do I put my trust; Thou wilt hear, O Lord, my God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; Deuteronomy 32:35; Psalms 35:24–26"> For I have prayed, “Let them not rejoice over me; Let them not exult at the slipping of my feet!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 38:6; Psalms 6:6; Psalms 35:15"> For I am ready to fall, And my pain doth never leave me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Job 33:27"> For I confess my iniquity, And am troubled on account of my sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; John 15:18–25; Psalms 69:4"> But my enemies flourish and are strong; They who hate me without cause are multiplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:12; 1 John 3:12; Jeremiah 18:20"> They who repay good with evil are my enemies, Because I follow that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 22:19; Psalms 22:24; Psalms 22:11"> Forsake me not, O LORD! O my God! be not far from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 27:1; Psalms 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> Make hast to mine aid, O Lord, my salvation!</span>
