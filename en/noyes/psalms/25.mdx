<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:8; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 24:4"> “A psalm of David.” To thee, O LORD! do I lift up my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 41:11; Psalms 71:1"> O my God! I trust in thee; let me not be put to shame! Let not my enemies triumph over me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Isaiah 40:31; Isaiah 49:23"> Yea, none that hope in thee shall be put to shame: They shall be put to shame who wickedly forsake thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 86:11; Psalms 27:11; Psalms 143:8"> Cause me to know thy ways, O LORD! Teach me thy paths!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Isaiah 30:18; Psalms 25:10"> Lead me in thy truth, and teach me! For thou art the God from whom cometh my help; In thee do I trust at all times!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Colossians 3:12; Luke 1:50"> Remember thy loving-kindness, O LORD! and thy tender mercy, Which thou hast exercised of old!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 38:17; Job 13:26"> Remember not the faults and transgressions of my youth! According to thy mercy remember thou me, For thy goodness' sake, O LORD!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:5; Psalms 32:8; Ephesians 1:17–18"> Good and righteous is the LORD; Therefore showeth he to sinners the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 23:3; Matthew 5:5"> The humble he guideth in his statutes, And the humble he teacheth his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 5:11; Romans 8:28; Hebrews 8:8–12"> All the doings of the LORD are mercy and truth To those who keep his covenant and his precepts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; 1 John 2:12; Isaiah 43:25"> For thy name's sake, O LORD, Pardon my iniquity; for it is great!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 32:8; John 7:17"> Who is the man that feareth the LORD? Him doth he show the way which he should choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; Proverbs 1:33; Proverbs 19:23"> He shall himself dwell in prosperity, And his offspring shall inherit the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:15; John 14:21–23; John 7:17"> The friendship of the LORD is with them that fear him, And he will teach them his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 141:8; Psalms 124:7–8; Psalms 31:4"> Mine eyes are ever directed to the LORD, For he will pluck my feet from the net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 86:16; Daniel 9:17; Micah 7:19"> Look upon me, and pity me; For I am desolate and afflicted!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:6; Psalms 34:19; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> Lighten the sorrows of my heart, And deliver me from my troubles!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Psalms 119:132; Psalms 51:8–9"> Look upon my affliction and distress, And forgive all my sins!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 56:2; Psalms 52:2; Psalms 38:19"> Consider how many are my enemies, And with what violence they hate me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 71:1–2; Psalms 121:7; Joel 2:26–27"> Guard thou my life, and deliver me! Let me not be put to shame, for I have trusted in thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Psalms 41:12; Proverbs 20:7"> Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, For on thee do I rest my hope!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 130:8; Psalms 51:18–19; Psalms 14:7"> Redeem Israel, O God! from all his troubles!</span>
