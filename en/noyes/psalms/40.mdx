<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 27:13–14; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David.” I trusted steadfastly in the LORD, And he listened, and heard my cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:23; Psalms 119:133; Psalms 27:5"> He drew me out of a horrible pit, Out of the miry clay; He set my feet upon a rock, And made my steps firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 103:1–5; Psalms 34:1–6"> He hath put into my mouth a new song, A song of praise to our God. Many shall see, and fear, And put their trust in the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12; Psalms 34:8"> Happy the man who maketh the LORD his trust, And resorteth not to men of pride and falsehood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 139:17–18; Exodus 15:11"> Many, O LORD, my God! are the wonderful works which thou hast done; Many have been thy gracious purposes towards us; None can be compared to thee! Would I declare and rehearse them, they are more than can be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5–12; Isaiah 1:11; 1 Samuel 15:22"> In sacrifice and oblation thou hast no pleasure; Mine ears thou hast opened; Burnt-offering and sin-offering thou requirest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:7–9; Luke 24:44; John 5:39"> Therefore I said, “Lo, I come; In the scroll of the book it is prescribed to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:22; John 4:34; Jeremiah 15:16"> O my God! to do thy will is my delight, And thy law dwelleth in my heart!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 22:25; Mark 16:15–16; Psalms 22:22"> I have proclaimed thy righteousness in the great assembly; Lo, I have not restrained my lips, O LORD! thou knowest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:16–17; Acts 20:20–21; Romans 10:9–10"> I hide not thy justice in my heart; I declare thy faithfulness and thy salvation; I conceal not thy mercy and truth from the great assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 43:3; Psalms 57:3"> Withdraw not from me thy tender mercies, O LORD! May thy loving-kindness and thy truth continually preserve me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 73:26; Psalms 69:4"> For evils without number have encompassed me; My iniquities have overtaken me; I cannot see the end of them; They are more than the hairs of my head, And my heart dieth within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 25:17–18; Psalms 70:1–5; Psalms 38:22"> May it please thee, O LORD! to deliver me! O LORD! make haste to mine aid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 35:4; Psalms 71:13"> May they all be confounded and covered with shame Who seek to take away my life! Let them be driven back with disgrace Who desire to do me injury!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:21; Psalms 69:24–25; Psalms 35:25"> Let them be overwhelmed with confusion Who cry out to me, Aha! aha!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 35:27; Psalms 68:3; Acts 19:17"> But let all who seek thee Be glad and rejoice in thee! Let those who love thy protection Ever say, “Great is Jehovah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 70:5; Hebrews 13:6; Isaiah 41:17"> I am poor and afflicted, yet the Lord thinketh upon me; Thou art my help and my deliverer; My God! make no delay!</span>
