<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 4:6–8; Isaiah 44:22; Psalms 85:2"> “A psalm of David.” Happy is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is pardoned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:47; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21; Revelation 14:5"> Happy the man to whom the LORD imputeth not iniquity, And in whose spirit there is no guile!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Psalms 38:8; Psalms 38:3"> While I kept silence, my bones were wasted, By reason of my groaning all the day long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Psalms 38:2–8; Psalms 39:10–11"> For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me; My moisture dried up, as in summer's drought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 38:18"> At length I acknowledged to thee my sin, And did not hide my iniquity. I said, “I will confess my transgression to the LORD;” And thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin! &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Isaiah 43:2; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> Therefore shall every pious man pray to thee, while thou mayst be found; Surely the floods of great waters shall not come near him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 119:114; Psalms 9:9"> Thou art my hiding-place; thou preservest me from trouble; Thou compassest me about with songs of deliverance. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Psalms 33:18; Psalms 25:8–10"> I will instruct thee, and show thee the way thou shouldst go; I will give thee counsel, and keep mine eye upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:3"> Be ye not like the horse and the mule, which have no understanding, Whose mouths must be pressed with the bridle and curb, Because they will not come near thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 12:2–3; Proverbs 16:20; Proverbs 13:21"> The wicked hath many sorrows; But he that trusteth in the LORD is encompassed with mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 64:10; Philippians 4:4; Psalms 97:12"> Rejoice in the LORD, and be glad, ye righteous; Shout for joy, all ye that are upright in heart!</span>
