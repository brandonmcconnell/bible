<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 85:5; Isaiah 58:2–3; Psalms 59:5"> O LORD, God of hosts! How long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Isaiah 30:20; Psalms 102:9"> For thou causest them to eat the bread of tears, And givest them tears to drink, in full measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 79:4; Isaiah 36:8; Isaiah 37:23"> Thou hast made us the object of strife to our neighbors, And our enemies hold us in derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:10; Isaiah 64:5; Jeremiah 4:14"> Bring us back, O God of hosts! And cause thy face to shine that we may be saved!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Psalms 44:2; Ezekiel 17:6"> Thou didst bring a vine out of Egypt; Thou didst expel the nations, and plant it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Isaiah 27:6; 1 Kings 4:20"> Thou didst prepare a place for it; It spread its roots, and filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 104:16"> The mountains were covered with its shade, And its branches were like the cedars of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 72:8; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 1 Kings 4:24"> It sent out its boughs to the sea, And its branches to the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:5; Psalms 89:40–41; Isaiah 18:5–6"> Why hast thou now broken down its hedges, So that all who pass by do pluck from it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 39:1–3"> The boar from the wood doth waste it, And the wild beast of the forest doth devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:15; Psalms 90:13; Daniel 9:16–19"> O God of hosts! return, we beseech thee, Look down from heaven, and behold, And have regard to this vine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:1; Psalms 80:8; Jeremiah 2:21"> Protect what thy right hand planted; The branch which thou madest strong for thyself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; John 15:6; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> It is burnt with fire; it is cut down; Under thy rebuke they perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 89:21; Psalms 110:1"> May thy hand be over the man of thy right hand, The man whom thou madest strong for thyself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:107; Psalms 119:25; Psalms 119:40"> So will we no more turn back from thee: Revive us, and upon thy name alone will we call!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:22–23; Psalms 80:3; Psalms 27:4"> Bring us back, O LORD, God of hosts! And cause thy face to shine, that we may he saved!</span>
