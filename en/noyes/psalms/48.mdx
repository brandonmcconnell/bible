<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 4:1; Zechariah 8:3; Psalms 96:4"> “A psalm of the sons of Korah.” Great is Jehovah, and greatly to be praised In the city of our God, upon his holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:2; Lamentations 2:15; Matthew 5:35"> Beautiful in its elevation is Mount Zion, The joy of the whole earth; The joy of the farthest North is the city of the great king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 2:4–5; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 46:7"> In her palaces God is known as a refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6–19; Revelation 17:12–14; Revelation 20:8–9"> For, lo! kings were assembled against it; They passed away together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 14:25; 2 Kings 7:6–7; 2 Kings 19:35–37"> As soon as they saw, they were astonished; They were confounded, and hasted away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:13; Isaiah 13:6–8; Isaiah 21:3"> There terror seized upon them,—Pain, as of a woman in travail;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Ezekiel 27:25–26; 1 Kings 22:48"> As when the east wind breaketh in pieces The ships of Tarshish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 87:5; Psalms 78:3–6; Psalms 46:5"> As we have heard, so have we seen In the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God; God will establish it for ever. &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 26:3; Psalms 40:10; Psalms 77:10–14"> We think of thy loving-kindness, O God! In the midst of thy temple!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Deuteronomy 28:58; Psalms 113:3"> As thy name, O God! so thy praise, extendeth to the ends of the earth; Thy right hand is full of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 97:8; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> Mount Zion rejoiceth, The daughters of Judah exult, On account of thy righteous judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 33:18–20; Nehemiah 12:31–40; Matthew 24:1–2"> Go round about Zion; number her towers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 71:18; Psalms 122:7; Deuteronomy 11:19"> Mark well her bulwarks; count her palaces; That ye may tell it to the next generation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Psalms 23:3–4; Psalms 73:24"> For this God is our God for ever and ever; He will be our guide unto death.</span>
