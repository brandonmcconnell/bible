<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:22; Isaiah 38:13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> Lest mine enemy tear me like a lion; Lest he rend me in pieces, while there is none to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; 1 Samuel 24:11; 1 Samuel 26:18–19"> O Jehovah, my God! if I have done this,—If there be iniquity upon my hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 55:20; 1 Samuel 22:14; 1 Samuel 24:17–19"> If I have rendered evil to my friend, Or have despoiled him that without cause is mine enemy,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Zechariah 10:5; Jeremiah 17:13"> Let my adversary pursue and take me; Let him trample me to the ground, And lay me prostrate in the dust! &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 35:23; Psalms 3:7"> Arise, O LORD! in thine anger; Lift thyself up against the rage of mine enemies; Awake for me, ordain judgment!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 57:15; Revelation 16:5–7; Revelation 18:20"> Let the assembly of the nations compass thee about, And on their account return to the height!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 43:1"> The LORD judgeth the nations; Judge me, O LORD! according to my righteousness, And requite me according to my integrity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:20; Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 20:12"> Oh, let the wickedness of the wicked be at an end; But establish the righteous! For the righteous God trieth the heart and the reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 125:4; Psalms 84:11; Proverbs 28:18"> My shield is with God, Who saveth the upright in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nahum 1:2; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 94:15"> God is a righteous judge, And a God who is angry every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Matthew 3:10; Deuteronomy 32:41"> If he do not desist, He sharpeneth his sword; He bendeth his bow, and maketh it ready;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 45:5; Psalms 64:7"> He prepareth for him the instruments of death; He shooteth his burning arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Isaiah 33:11"> Behold, he travailed with iniquity, And conceived mischief, But hath brought forth disappointment!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 4:8; Psalms 141:10; Proverbs 26:27"> He made a pit and digged it. And is fallen into the ditch which he made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esther 9:25; Psalms 37:12–13; 1 Kings 2:32"> His mischief returneth upon his own head, And his violence cometh down upon his own skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:2; Psalms 98:2; Psalms 71:15–16"> I will praise the LORD according to his righteousness; I will sing praise to the name of the LORD most high.</span>
