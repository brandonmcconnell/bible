<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalms 105:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34"> Praise ye the LORD! O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; For his mercy endureth for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ephesians 1:19; Psalms 145:3–12"> Who can utter the mighty deeds of the LORD? Who can show forth all his praise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 15:2; James 1:25; John 15:14"> Happy are they who have regard to justice, Who practise righteousness at all times!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:132; Luke 23:42; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O LORD! with the favor promised to thy people; O visit me with thy salvation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:16; Psalms 105:43; Psalms 1:3"> That I may see the prosperity of thy chosen, That I may rejoice in the joy of thy people, That I may glory with thine inheritance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Psalms 78:8; 1 Kings 8:47"> We have sinned with our fathers; We have committed iniquity; we have done wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Psalms 78:42; Psalms 78:11"> Our fathers in Egypt did not regard thy wonders; They remembered not the multitude of thy mercies; But rebelled at the sea, the Red sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 20:14"> Yet he saved them for his own name's sake. That he might make his mighty power to be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 18:15; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 63:11–14"> He rebuked the Red sea, and it was dried up, And he led them through the deep as through a desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; Psalms 107:2; Micah 6:4"> He saved them from the hand of him that hated them, And redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 15:5; Psalms 78:53; Exodus 14:13"> The waters covered their enemies; There was not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 14:31–21; Luke 8:13; John 8:30–31"> Then believed they his words, And sang his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:11; Exodus 15:24; Isaiah 48:17–18"> But they soon forgot his deeds, And waited not for his counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> They gave way to appetite in the wilderness, And tempted God in the desert;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 11:31–34; Isaiah 10:16; Psalms 78:29–31"> And he gave them their request, But sent upon them leanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–50; Leviticus 21:10–12; Leviticus 21:6–8"> They also envied Moses in the camp, And Aaron, the holy one of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:6; Numbers 16:29–33; Numbers 26:10"> Then the earth opened, and swallowed up Dathan, And covered the company of Abiram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Numbers 16:35–40; Numbers 16:46"> And a fire was kindled in their company; The flames burned up the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:4–8; Exodus 32:35; Nehemiah 9:18"> They made a calf in Horeb, And worshipped a molten image;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 20:4–5; Romans 1:22–23"> They changed their God of glory Into the image of a grass-eating ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 78:42–51; Psalms 106:13"> They forgot God, their saviour, Who had done such great things in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 78:51; Exodus 14:25–28"> Such wonders in the land of Ham. Such terrible things at the Red sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:13–14; Deuteronomy 9:25; Ezekiel 22:30"> Then he said that he would destroy them; Had not Moses, his chosen, stood before him in the breach, To turn away his wrath, that he might not destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:32; Ezekiel 20:6; Hebrews 3:18–19"> They also despised the pleasant land, And believed not his word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:22; Deuteronomy 1:26–27; Hebrews 3:15"> But murmured in their tents, And would not hearken to the voice of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Numbers 14:28–35; Hebrews 3:11"> Then he lifted up his hand against them, And swore that he would make them fall in the wilderness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 44:11; Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> That he would overthrow their descendants among the nations, And scatter them in the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hosea 9:10; Numbers 25:1–3; Numbers 25:5"> They also gave themselves to the worship of Baal-peor, And ate sacrifices offered to lifeless idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16–21; 1 Corinthians 10:8; Romans 1:21–24"> Thus they provoked his anger by their practices, And a plague broke in upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 25:6–8; Joshua 7:12; Numbers 25:14–15"> Then stood up Phinehas, and executed judgment, And the plague was stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 25:11–13; Mark 14:3–9; Deuteronomy 24:13"> And this was counted to him for righteousness, To all generations for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 20:2–13; Numbers 20:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:21"> They provoked him also at the waters of Meribah &#91;&#91;strife&#93;&#93;, And evil befell Moses on their account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 20:10–11; Psalms 107:11; James 3:2"> For they provoked his spirit, So that he spake inconsiderately with his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 1:21"> They did not destroy the nations, As Jehovah had commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 3:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> They mingled themselves with the peoples, And learned their practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Judges 2:3; Exodus 23:33"> They even worshipped their idols, Which became to them a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 32:17"> Their sons and their daughters they sacrificed to demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Deuteronomy 21:9; Isaiah 26:21"> And shed innocent blood, The blood of their own sons and daughters, Whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan; And the land was polluted with blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:18; Numbers 15:39; Leviticus 17:7"> Thus they defiled themselves with their works, And played the harlot with their practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 9:29; Judges 2:20"> Then burned the anger of the LORD against his people, So that he abhorred his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Nehemiah 9:27–38; Judges 3:8"> And he gave them into the hand of the nations, And they who hated them ruled over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 4:3; Judges 10:12"> Their enemies oppressed them, And they were bowed down under their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Judges 2:16–18; Psalms 1:1"> Many times did he deliver them; But they provoked him by their devices, And they were brought low for their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Judges 4:3; 1 Samuel 7:8–12"> Yet, when he heard their cries, He had regard to their affliction;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; Lamentations 3:32; Judges 2:18"> He remembered his covenant with them, And repented according to the greatness of his mercy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Jeremiah 42:12; Jeremiah 15:11"> And caused them to find pity Among all that carried them captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:35–36; Revelation 7:10–12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Save us, O Jehovah, our God! and gather us from among the nations, That we may give thanks to thy holy name, And glory in thy praise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; Psalms 89:52; Psalms 105:45–1"> &#91;Blessed be Jehovah, the God of Israel, From everlasting to everlasting! And let all the people say, Amen! Praise ye Jehovah!&#93;</span>
