<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Nahum 1:2; Romans 12:19"> O LORD! thou God of vengeance! O thou God of vengeance! shine forth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 7:6; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Psalms 31:23"> Rouse thyself, thou judge of the earth! Render a recompense to the proud!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 20:5; Revelation 6:10; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> How long, O LORD! shall the wicked, How long shall the wicked triumph?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:18; Psalms 52:1; Revelation 13:5–6"> How long shall their lips pour forth insolence? How long shall all the evil-doers boast?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 3:15; Psalms 79:2–3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> O LORD! they trample upon thy people, And oppress thine inheritance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 10:2; Malachi 3:5; Jeremiah 7:6"> They slay the widow, and the stranger, And murder the fatherless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 10:11–13; Luke 18:3–4; Zephaniah 1:12"> And they say, “The LORD doth not see, The God of Jacob doth not regard!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; Titus 3:3; Psalms 49:10"> Be instructed, ye most stupid of mankind! O when, ye fools, will ye be wise?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalms 11:4"> He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? He that formed the eye, shall he not see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 35:11; Isaiah 28:26; Psalms 44:2"> He that chastiseth nations, shall not he punish? He that teacheth man knowledge, shall not he know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:20; 1 Corinthians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 1:21"> The LORD knoweth the thoughts of men, That they are vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17; Hebrews 12:5–11"> Happy the man, O LORD! whom thou correctest, Whom by thy teaching thou makest wise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 55:23; 2 Peter 2:9; Psalms 9:15"> To give him peace in the days of adversity, Until a pit be digged for the wicked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:22; Psalms 37:28; Romans 11:1–2"> For the LORD will not forsake his people, Nor abandon his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Psalms 97:2; Psalms 37:34"> For judgment shall return to justice, And all the upright in heart shall follow it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Numbers 25:6–13; John 7:50–51"> Who will rise up for me against the wicked? Who will stand up for me against the evil-doers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 124:1–2; Psalms 31:17; Psalms 118:13"> If the LORD had not been my help, I had well nigh dwelt in the land of silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 119:116–117"> When I think that my foot is slipping, Thy goodness, O LORD! holdeth me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; 2 Corinthians 1:4–5; Psalms 77:2–10"> In the multitude of anxieties within me, Thy consolations revive my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 58:2; Isaiah 10:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> Shall with thee be allied the throne of iniquity, Which deviseth mischief against law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 59:3; Matthew 27:1; Proverbs 17:15"> They band together against the life of the righteous, And condemn innocent blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Psalms 27:1–3; Psalms 59:16–17"> But the LORD is my fortress, And my God the rock of my refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Proverbs 14:32; Proverbs 2:22"> He will bring upon them their own iniquity; Yea, through their own wickedness he will cut them off; Yea, the LORD, our God, will cut them off.</span>
