<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:4; Isaiah 55:3; Psalms 74:1"> “A psalm of Asaph.” Give ear, O my people, to my instruction! Incline your ears to the words of my mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 4:34; Matthew 13:11–13"> I will open my mouth in a psalm; I will utter sayings of ancient times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:1; Exodus 13:14–15; Exodus 12:26–27"> What we have heard and learned, And our fathers have told us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 145:4–6; Deuteronomy 4:9"> We will not hide from their children; Showing to the generation to come the praises of Jehovah, His might, and the wonders he hath wrought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 4:45"> For he appointed statutes in Jacob, And established a law in Israel, Which he commanded our fathers To make known to their children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 102:18; Joel 1:3; Esther 9:28"> So that the generation to come might know them; The children, which should be born, and rise up, Who should declare them to their children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Revelation 14:12; 1 John 5:3"> That they might put their trust in God, And not forget his deeds, But keep his commandments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 78:37; Exodus 32:9; 2 Kings 17:14"> And might not be, like their forefathers, A stubborn and rebellious generation,—A generation whose heart was not fixed upon God, And whose spirit was not steadfast toward the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:41–44; 1 Samuel 31:1; Psalms 78:57"> The children of Ephraim were like armed bowmen, Who turn their backs in the day of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:20; Deuteronomy 31:16; Nehemiah 9:26–29"> They kept not the covenant of God, And refused to walk in his law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:13; Deuteronomy 32:18; Psalms 106:21–22"> And forgot his mighty deeds, And the wonders he had shown them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 19:11"> Marvellous things did he in the sight of their fathers, In the land of Egypt, in the field of Zoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:8; Psalms 66:6"> He divided the sea, and caused them to pass through; Yea, he made the waters to stand as a heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:39; Exodus 14:24; Exodus 13:21–22"> By day he led them by a cloud, And all the night by a light of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 105:41; Numbers 20:11; Exodus 17:6"> He clave the rocks in the wilderness, And gave them drink, as from the great deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Numbers 20:10–11; Deuteronomy 8:15"> From the rock he brought flowing streams, And made water to run down like rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; Hebrews 3:16–19; Deuteronomy 9:12–22"> Yet still they sinned against him, And provoked the Most High in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; Numbers 11:4; 1 Corinthians 10:9"> They tempted God in their hearts, By asking food for their delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 11:4; Exodus 16:3"> Yea, they spake against God, and said, “Can God spread a table in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 20:11; Numbers 11:21–23; Psalms 78:41"> Behold! he smote the rock, and the water flowed, And streams gushed forth: Is he also able to give bread? Can he provide flesh for his people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Numbers 11:1–3"> When, therefore, the LORD heard this, he was wroth: So a fire was kindled against Jacob, And anger arose against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:18–19; Hebrews 3:12; Jude 1:5"> Because they believed not in God, And trusted not in his aid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10; Psalms 33:9"> Yet he had commanded the clouds above, And had opened the doors of heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; Psalms 105:40; John 6:31–71"> And had rained down upon them manna for food, And had given them the corn of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; Matthew 15:37; Psalms 103:20"> Every one ate the food of princes; He sent them bread to the full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 11:31; Psalms 135:7"> Then he caused a strong wind to blow in the heavens, And by his power he brought a south wind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 11:18–19; Exodus 16:12–13; Numbers 11:32"> He rained down flesh upon them as dust, And feathered fowls as the sand of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He caused them to fall in the midst of their camp, Round about their habitations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 106:15; Numbers 11:19–20"> So they did eat, and were filled; For he gave them their own desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 11:33–34; Luke 16:19–23; Numbers 22:20–22"> Their desire was not yet satisfied, And their meat was yet in their mouths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> When the wrath of God came upon them, And slew their strong men, And smote down the chosen men of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 78:22; Psalms 78:11"> For all this they sinned still, And put no trust in his wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 14:35; Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65"> Therefore he consumed their days in vanity, And their years in sudden destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hosea 5:15; Numbers 21:7; Judges 3:12–15"> When he slew them, they sought him; They returned, and sought earnestly for God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 41:14; Exodus 15:13"> And remembered that God was their rock, And the Most High their redeemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; Isaiah 29:13; Hosea 11:12"> But they only flattered him with their mouths, And spake falsely to him with their tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 8:21; Psalms 78:8; Psalms 51:10"> For their hearts were not true to him, Nor were they steadfast in his covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Numbers 14:18–20; Ezekiel 20:17"> Yet, being full of compassion, he forgave their iniquity, And would not utterly destroy them; Often he restrained his indignation, And stirred not up all his anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 6:3; James 4:14; Job 7:7"> He remembered that they were but flesh,—A breath, that passeth and cometh not back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 95:8–10; Hebrews 3:15–17"> How often did they provoke him in the wilderness! How often did they anger him in the desert!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Hebrews 3:8–11; Numbers 14:22; Acts 7:39"> Again and again they tempted God, And offended the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Judges 8:34; Psalms 78:11; Jeremiah 32:21"> They remembered not his hand, Nor the day when he delivered them from the enemy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:22; Exodus 4:21; Exodus 3:19–20"> What signs he had wrought in Egypt, And what wonders in the fields of Zoan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 105:29; Revelation 16:3–6; Exodus 7:17–21"> He turned their rivers into blood, So that they could not drink of their streams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Exodus 8:21–24; Psalms 105:30–31; Exodus 8:2–15"> He sent amongst them flies, which devoured them, And frogs, which destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 10:12–15; Psalms 105:34–35; Amos 7:1–2"> He gave also their fruits to the caterpillar, And their labor to the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 105:32–33; Exodus 9:18–34"> He destroyed their vines with hail, And their sycamore-trees with frost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 9:19; Exodus 9:28"> He also gave up their cattle to hail, And their flocks to hot thunderbolts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Exodus 12:13; 1 Kings 22:21–22; Lamentations 4:11"> He sent against them the fierceness of his anger, Wrath, indignation, and woe,—A host of angels of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; 2 Peter 2:4–5; Romans 8:32"> He made a way for his anger, He spared them not from death, But gave up their lives to the pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 105:23; Psalms 135:8; Psalms 106:22"> He smote all the firstborn in Egypt; The first-fruits of their strength in the tents of Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Psalms 77:20; John 10:11–42"> But he led forth his own people like sheep, And guided them like a flock in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Exodus 14:19–20; Exodus 15:10; Exodus 14:27–28"> He led them on safely, so that they feared not, While the sea overwhelmed their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Exodus 15:17; Isaiah 11:9"> He brought them to his own sacred border, Even to this mountain which his right hand had gained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Joshua 13:7; Psalms 44:2; Nehemiah 9:22–25"> He cast out the nations before them, And divided their land by a measuring-line, as an inheritance, And caused the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Judges 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 32:15–21; Psalms 78:40–41"> Yet they tempted and provoked God, the Most High, And kept not his statutes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Ezekiel 20:27–28; Psalms 78:8"> Like their fathers they were faithless, and turned back; They turned aside, like a deceitful bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Leviticus 26:30; 1 Kings 12:31"> They provoked his anger by their high places, And stirred up his jealousy by their graven images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Psalms 106:40; Lamentations 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:19"> God saw this, and was wroth, And greatly abhorred Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:4–11; Jeremiah 26:6–9"> So that he forsook the habitation at Shiloh, The tabernacle where he dwelt among men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Samuel 4:21–2; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And delivered his strength into captivity, And his glory into the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Psalms 89:38; Isaiah 64:9; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> His own people he gave up to the sword, And was wroth with his own inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Jeremiah 16:9; Deuteronomy 32:22"> Fire consumed their young men, And their maidens did not bewail them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:17; Job 27:15; Ezekiel 24:23"> Their priests fell by the sword, And their widows made no lamentation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Psalms 44:23; Psalms 7:6; Isaiah 51:9"> But at length the Lord awaked as from sleep, As a hero who had been overpowered by wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 23:40; Job 40:12"> He smote his enemies, and drove them back, And covered them with everlasting disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:21–1; 2 Samuel 6:2; 2 Samuel 6:17"> Yet he rejected the tents of Joseph, And chose not the tribe of Ephraim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 87:2; Psalms 132:12–14; Ruth 4:17–22"> But chose the tribe of Judah, The Mount Zion which he loved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Job 26:7; 1 Chronicles 29:19; 1 Samuel 2:8"> Where he built, like the heavens, his sanctuary; Like the earth, which he hath established for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11–12; Acts 13:22; Exodus 3:1"> And he chose David, his servant, And took him from the sheepfolds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 11:2; Ezekiel 34:23–24"> From tending the suckling ewes he brought him To feed Jacob his people, And Israel his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 2 Timothy 2:15; Isaiah 11:2–4"> He fed them with an upright heart, And guided them with skilful hands.</span>
