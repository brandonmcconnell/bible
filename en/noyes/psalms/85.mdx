<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Psalms 32:1; Colossians 2:13"> Thou didst forgive the iniquity of thy people, And cover all their sins! &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 80:18; Isaiah 57:15"> Wilt thou not revive us again, That thy people may rejoice in thee?</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:13; Zechariah 2:5; John 7:17"> Yea, his salvation is near to those who fear him, That glory may dwell in our land.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 84:11; Psalms 67:6"> Yea, Jehovah will give prosperity, And our land shall yield her increase.</span>
