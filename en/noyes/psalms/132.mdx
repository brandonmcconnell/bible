<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 120:1; Psalms 129:1; Lamentations 5:1"> “A psalm of the steps, or the goings up.” O LORD! remember David, And all his affliction!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Psalms 65:1; Psalms 119:106"> How he sware to Jehovah, And vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Haggai 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Matthew 6:33"> “I will not go into my house, Nor lie down on my bed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:4; Ruth 3:18; Genesis 24:33"> I will not give sleep to my eyes, Nor slumber to my eyelids,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Acts 7:46–49; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> Until I find a place for Jehovah, A habitation for the Mighty One of Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:12; 1 Samuel 7:1; Micah 5:2"> Behold, we heard of it at Ephratah; We found it in the fields of the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 99:5; Psalms 5:7; Lamentations 2:1"> Let us go into his habitation; Let us worship at his footstool!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:41–42; Numbers 10:35–36; Psalms 68:1"> Arise, O LORD! into thy rest, Thou, and the ark of thy strength!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 29:14; Revelation 19:8; Psalms 104:1"> Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness, And thy holy ones shout for joy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 84:9; 2 Chronicles 6:42; 1 Kings 11:34"> For the sake of thy servant David, Reject not the prayer of thine anointed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12; Acts 2:30; Psalms 89:3–4"> Jehovah hath sworn in truth unto David, And he will not depart from it: “Of the fruit of thy body will I place upon the throne for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Acts 2:30; Isaiah 59:21"> If thy children keep my covenant, And my statutes, which I teach them, Their children also throughout all ages Shall sit upon thy throne.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 78:68–69; Hebrews 12:22; Psalms 87:2"> For Jehovah hath chosen Zion; He hath desired it as his dwelling-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 132:8; 1 Kings 8:13; Psalms 76:2"> “This is my resting-place for ever; Here will I dwell, for I have chosen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 147:14; Leviticus 26:4–5; Proverbs 3:9–10"> I will abundantly bless her provision; I will satisfy her poor with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:41; Psalms 132:9; John 16:24"> I will also clothe her priests with salvation, And her holy ones shall shout aloud for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:69; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Chronicles 21:7"> There will I cause to spring forth a horn for David; I have prepared a light for mine anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Job 8:22; Psalms 21:8–9"> His enemies will I clothe with shame, And the crown shall glitter upon his head.”</span>
