<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 84:2; Psalms 42:1–2"> “A psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah.” O God! thou art my God! earnestly do I seek thee! My soul thirsteth, my flesh longeth for thee, In a dry, thirsty land. where is no water!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 27:4; Psalms 105:4; Psalms 84:2–11"> Thus I look toward thee in thy sanctuary, To behold thy power and thy glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 69:16; Psalms 51:15; Philippians 1:23"> For thy loving-kindness is better than life; Therefore my lips shall praise thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:33; Psalms 134:2; Psalms 28:2"> Thus will I bless thee, while I live; In thy name will I lift up my hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 36:7–9; Isaiah 25:6; Ezra 3:11–13"> My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness, And with joyful lips my mouth shall praise thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 119:147–148; Psalms 149:5"> When I think of thee upon my bed, And meditate on thee in the night-watches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Psalms 61:4; Psalms 27:9"> For thou art my help, And in the shadow of thy wings I rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Psalms 18:35; Psalms 73:23"> My soul cleaveth to thee; Thy right hand holdeth me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Psalms 9:17; Psalms 40:14"> While they who seek to destroy my life Shall themselves go down into the depths of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 39:4"> They shall be given up to the sword, And be a portion for jackals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Psalms 21:1; Isaiah 65:16"> But the king shall rejoice in God; All that swear by him shall be honored; But the mouth of liars shall be stopped.</span>
