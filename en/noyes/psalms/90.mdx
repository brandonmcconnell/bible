<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 71:3; 1 John 4:16"> “BOOK IV. A prayer of Moses, the man of God.” LORD! thou hast been our dwelling-place In all generations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Genesis 1:1; Psalms 102:24–27"> Before the mountains were brought forth, Or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, Even from everlasting to everlasting thou art God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Psalms 146:4"> But man thou turnest again to dust, And sayst, “Return, ye children of men!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 39:5; Matthew 24:43"> For a thousand years are, in thy sight, As yesterday when it is past, And as a watch in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6; Psalms 73:20; Job 22:16"> Thou carriest him away as with a flood; He is a dream; In the morning he springeth up like grass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:2; Psalms 92:7; James 1:11"> Which flourisheth and shooteth up in the morning, And in the evening is cut down, and withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 39:11; Psalms 59:13; Exodus 14:24"> For we are consumed by thine anger, And by thy wrath are we destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Thou settest our iniquities before thee, Our secret sins in the light of thy countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 78:33; Psalms 39:5; Psalms 90:4"> By reason of thine anger all our days vanish away; We spend our years like a thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; 2 Samuel 19:35"> The days of our life are threescore years and ten, And, by reason of strength, may be fourscore years: Yet is the pride of them weariness and sorrow; For it vanisheth swiftly, and we fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Isaiah 33:14; Nahum 1:6"> Yet who attendeth to the power of thine anger? Who with due reverence regardeth thine indignation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:16–17; Psalms 39:4; John 9:4"> Teach us so to number our days, That we may apply our hearts to wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Psalms 135:14; Deuteronomy 32:36"> Desist, O LORD! How long—? Have compassion upon thy servants!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 23:6; Psalms 86:4; Psalms 31:7"> Satisfy us speedily with thy mercy, That we may rejoice and be glad all our days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 30:5; John 16:20"> Make us glad according to the time in which thou hast afflicted us; According to the years in which we have seen adversity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:2; Psalms 44:1; Deuteronomy 32:4"> Let thy deeds be known to thy servants, And thy glory to their children!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 16:3; 1 Corinthians 3:7; Job 22:28"> Let the favor of the LORD our God be upon us, And establish for us the work of our hands; Yea, the work of our hands, establish thou it!</span>
