<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Psalms 51:10; Luke 12:32"> “BOOK III. A psalm of Asaph.” Truly God is good to Israel,—To those who are pure in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:18; 1 Samuel 2:9; Psalms 116:8"> Yet my feet almost gave way; My steps had well nigh slipped:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:7; Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17"> For I was envious of the proud, When I saw the prosperity of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 21:23–24; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Luke 16:22"> For they have no pains even to their death; Their bodies are in full health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 21:9; Psalms 73:12; Revelation 3:19"> They have not the woes of other men, Neither are they smitten like other men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 109:18; 1 Peter 5:5; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore pride encircleth their neck as a collar; Violence covereth them as a garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> From their bosom issueth their iniquity; The designs of their hearts burst forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Psalms 17:10; Psalms 10:2"> They mock, and speak of malicious oppression; Their words are haughty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:6; James 3:6; Exodus 5:2"> They stretch forth their mouth to the heavens, And their tongue goeth through the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 75:8"> Therefore his people walk in their ways, And there drink from full fountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:2; Zephaniah 1:12; Job 22:13–14"> And they say, “How doth God know? How can there be knowledge with the Most High?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 52:7; Psalms 49:6; Hosea 12:7–8"> Behold these are the ungodly! Yet they are ever prosperous; they heap up riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 21:15; Psalms 26:6; Job 34:9"> Verily I have cleansed my heart in vain; In vain have I washed my hands in innocence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; Psalms 94:12; Job 7:3–4"> For every day have I been smitten; Every morn have I been chastened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:6–7; Malachi 2:8; 1 Peter 2:9"> If I should resolve to speak like them, Surely I should be treacherous to the family of thy children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:17; John 16:18–19; Psalms 36:6"> So, when I studied to know this, It was painful to my eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Psalms 77:13; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13"> Until I went into the sanctuaries of God, And considered what was their end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:6; Jeremiah 23:12; Deuteronomy 32:35"> Behold! thou hast set them on slippery places; Thou castest them down into unseen pits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; Daniel 5:6"> How are they brought to desolation in a moment, And utterly consumed with sudden destruction!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:8; Psalms 78:65; Psalms 90:5"> As a dream when one awaketh, Thou, O Lord! when thou awakest, wilt make their vain show a derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Psalms 73:3; Job 16:13"> When my heart was vexed And I was pierced in my reins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:18; Psalms 92:6; Psalms 49:10"> Then was I stupid and without understanding; I was like one of the brutes before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 63:8; Matthew 28:20"> Yet am I ever under thy care; By my right hand thou dost hold me up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; Proverbs 3:5–6"> Thou wilt guide me with thy counsel, And at last receive me in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Psalms 16:2; Psalms 63:3"> Whom have I in heaven but thee, And whom on earth do I love in comparison with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; Psalms 138:3"> Though my flesh and my heart fail, God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:4; Psalms 119:155; Exodus 34:15"> For, lo! they who are far from thee perish; Thou destroyest all who estrange themselves from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalms 40:5; Hebrews 10:19–22"> But it is good for me to draw near to God; I put my trust in the Lord Jehovah, That I may declare all thy works.</span>
