<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 15:34; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> “For the leader of the music. To the tune of “The hind of the morning.” A psalm of David.” My God, my God! why hast thou forsaken me? Why so far from mine aid, and from the words of my cry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 42:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 22:41–46"> O my God! I cry during the day, but thou hearest not; In the night also, but I have no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 50:23; Deuteronomy 10:21; Isaiah 6:3"> And yet thou art holy, Dwelling amid the praises of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Exodus 14:13–14; Hebrews 11:8–32"> Our fathers trusted in thee; They trusted, and thou didst save them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Isaiah 49:23; Romans 9:33"> They called upon thee, and were delivered; They trusted in thee, and were not put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3; Job 25:6; Isaiah 49:7"> But I am a worm, and not a man; The reproach of men, and the scorn of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 15:29; Psalms 109:25; Matthew 27:39–40"> All who see me scoff at me; They open wide the lips; they shake the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:30–32; Psalms 91:14"> “He trusted in the LORD, let him help him; Let him deliver him, since he delighted in him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:6; Isaiah 49:1–2; Psalms 71:17"> Surely thou art he that didst bring me into the world; Thou didst make me lie secure upon my mother's breast!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:1"> Upon thee have I cast myself from my birth; Thou hast been my God from my earliest breath!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:12; Psalms 72:12; Psalms 10:1"> Oh, be not far from me, for trouble is near; For there is none to help!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 4:1–3; Psalms 68:30; Acts 4:27"> Many bulls surround me; Strong bulls of Bashan close me in on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Psalms 35:21; Lamentations 3:46"> They open their mouths wide against me, Like a ravening and roaring lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 31:10; Daniel 5:6; Job 30:16"> I am poured out like water, And all my bones are out of joint; My heart is become like wax; It melteth in my bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:28; Psalms 69:3; Psalms 69:21"> My strength is dried up like an earthen vessel, And my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; Thou hast brought me to the dust of death!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:37; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 27:35"> For dogs have surrounded me; Bands of evil-doers have encompassed me,—Like lions my hands and my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Job 33:21; Matthew 27:39–41"> I can count all my bones; They gaze, and feast their eyes upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:35; Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24"> They divide my garments among them, And for my vesture they cast lots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 22:11; Psalms 40:13; Psalms 40:17"> But be not thou far from me, O LORD! O my strength! make haste to mine aid!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:17; Zechariah 13:7; Psalms 17:13"> Deliver my life from the sword; My blood from the power of the dog;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17; Numbers 23:22; 1 Peter 5:8"> Save me from the lion's mouth; Shield me from the horns of the buffaloes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 2:11–12; Romans 8:29; Psalms 40:9–10"> I will proclaim thy name to my brethren; In the midst of the congregation will I praise thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 135:19–20; Revelation 15:4; Psalms 107:1–2"> Praise him, ye worshippers of Jehovah! Extol him, all ye race of Jacob. And fear him, all ye race of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Isaiah 50:6–9; Psalms 116:3–6"> For he hath not despised nor abhorred the misery of the afflicted. Nor hath he hid his face from him; But when he cried unto him, he heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:9–10; Psalms 35:18; Psalms 111:1"> My praise shall be of thee in the great congregation; I will pay my vows before them that fear him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 69:32; Psalms 40:16; Isaiah 65:13"> The afflicted shall eat, and be satisfied; They that seek the LORD shall praise him; Your hearts shall be glad for ever and ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Isaiah 45:22; Psalms 2:8"> All the ends of the earth shall remember, and turn to Jehovah; All the families of the nations shall worship before thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Psalms 47:7–8; Revelation 11:15"> For the kingdom is Jehovah's; He is the governor of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Isaiah 26:19; Psalms 45:12"> All the rich of the earth shall eat and worship; Before him shall they also bow, who are going down to the dust, Who cannot keep themselves alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 2:13; Psalms 14:5; 1 Peter 2:9"> The future generation shall serve him; The race which is to come shall hear of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 78:6; Psalms 86:9; Romans 1:17"> They shall come, and declare his righteousness; His mighty deeds to the people that shall be born.</span>
