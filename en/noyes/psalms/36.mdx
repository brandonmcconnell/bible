<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Proverbs 16:6; Psalms 143:12"> “For the leader of the music. A psalm of David, the servant of the Lord.” To speak of the ungodliness of the wicked is in my heart. He hath no fear of God before his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:19; Psalms 10:3; Psalms 49:18"> For he flattereth himself in his own eyes, Till his iniquity is found out and hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 10:7; Jeremiah 4:22"> The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit; He neglecteth to be wise and to do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Proverbs 4:16; Romans 12:9"> He deviseth mischief upon his bed; He persevereth in an evil way; He abhorreth not sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 100:5; Psalms 108:4; Psalms 103:11"> Thy goodness, O LORD! reacheth to the heavens, And thy faithfulness to the clouds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Romans 11:33; Psalms 71:19"> Thy righteousness is like the high mountains; Thy judgments are a great deep; Thou, O LORD! preservest man and beast!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Psalms 91:4; Ruth 2:12"> How precious is thy loving-kindness, O God! Yea, the sons of men seek refuge under the shadow of thy wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 65:4; Isaiah 58:11"> They are satisfied with the abundance of thy house, And thou causest them to drink of the full stream of thy pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 8:12; John 4:14; 1 John 1:7"> For with thee is the fountain of life; Through thy light we see light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:7; Hebrews 8:11; Psalms 97:10–11"> O continue thy loving-kindness to them that know thee, And thy favor to the upright in heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 16:8; Psalms 17:8–14; Psalms 119:51"> Let not the foot of the proud come upon me, Nor the hand of the wicked remove me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 1:5; Psalms 58:10–11; Psalms 55:23"> Lo! already are the workers of iniquity fallen; They are cast down; they are unable to rise!</span>
