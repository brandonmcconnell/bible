<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:21; Psalms 12:2; Jeremiah 9:8–9"> Draw me not away with the impious, and with evil-doers, Who speak peace to their neighbors, while mischief is in their hearts!</span>
