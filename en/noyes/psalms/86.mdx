<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5; Psalms 146:1–2; Romans 15:6"> I will praise thee, O Lord, my God! with my whole heart; I will give glory to thy name for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:8–12; Luke 1:58; Psalms 56:13"> For thy kindness to me hath been great; Thou hast delivered me from the depths of the underworld!</span>
