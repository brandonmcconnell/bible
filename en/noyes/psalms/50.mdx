<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 113:3; Joshua 22:22; Matthew 25:32"> “A psalm of Asaph.” The mighty God, Jehovah, speaketh, and calleth the earth, From the rising of the sun to its going down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Psalms 80:1; Revelation 21:23"> Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shineth forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 10:2"> Our God cometh, and will not be silent; Before him is a devouring fire, And around him a raging tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> He calleth to the heavens on high, And to the earth, while he judgeth his people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 26:28; Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 13:20"> “Gather together before me my godly ones, Who have made a covenant with me by sacrifice!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:6; Psalms 75:7; Psalms 89:5"> (And the heavens shall declare his righteousness, For it is God himself that is judge.) &#91;&#91;Pause.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Psalms 81:8; Jeremiah 2:9"> “Hear, O my people, and I will speak! O Israel, and I will testify against thee! For I am God, thine own God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; Hebrews 10:4–10; Hosea 6:6"> I reprove thee not on account of thy sacrifices; For thy burnt-offerings are ever before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 6:6–8; Hebrews 10:4–6; Psalms 69:31"> I will take no bullock from thy house, Nor he-goat from thy folds;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5–6; Psalms 8:6–8; Psalms 104:14"> For all the beasts of the forest are mine, And the cattle on a thousand hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–22; Matthew 6:26; Luke 12:24"> I know all the birds of the mountains, And the wild beasts of the field are before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 10:14; Psalms 24:1–2"> If I were hungry, I would not tell thee; For the world is mine, and all that is therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do I eat the flesh of bulls, Or drink the blood of goats?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 56:12; Deuteronomy 23:21"> Offer to God thanksgiving, And pay thy vows to the Most High!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 107:28; Psalms 91:15; Psalms 34:3–4"> Then call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Psalms 78:36–38; Romans 2:17–24"> And to the wicked God saith, “To what purpose dost thou talk of my statutes? And why hast thou my laws upon thy lips?—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Thou, who hatest instruction And castest my words behind thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 1:32; 1 Timothy 5:22; Hebrews 13:4"> When thou seest a thief, thou art in friendship with him, And hast fellowship with adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 52:2–4; Psalms 55:21"> Thou lettest loose thy mouth to evil, And thy tongue frameth deceit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Proverbs 10:18; Leviticus 19:16"> Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; Thou slanderest thine own mother's son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:4–5; Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; Hence thou thoughtest that I was altogether like thyself: But I will reprove thee, and set it in order before thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 8:13; Psalms 9:17; Hosea 4:6"> Mark this, ye that forget God, Lest I tear you in pieces, and none deliver you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 91:16; James 3:13; Psalms 24:4–5"> Whoso offereth praise honoreth me; And to him who hath regard to his ways Will I show salvation from God.”</span>
