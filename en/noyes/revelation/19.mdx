<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:10–12; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Revelation 11:15"> After these things I heard as it were a loud voice of a great multitude in heaven, saying, Hallelujah I The salvation, and the glory, and the power, belong to our God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:43; Revelation 6:10; Revelation 18:20"> for true and righteous are his judgments; for he hath judged the great harlot, who corrupted the earth with her fornication, and hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 34:10; Revelation 18:18; Revelation 14:11"> And a second time they said, Hallelujah! and her smoke goeth up for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 5:14; Nehemiah 8:6; Psalms 106:48"> And the twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell down and worshipped God that sat on the throne, saying, Amen, hallelujah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 134:1; Psalms 148:11–13; Psalms 150:6"> And a voice came forth from the throne, saying, Praise our God, all ye his servants, and ye that fear him, the small and the great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:1; Revelation 14:2; Psalms 93:1"> And I heard as it were the voice of a great multitude, and as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of mighty thunders, saying, Hallelujah! for the Lord our God the Almighty reigneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 21:9; Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:9"> Let us rejoice and exult, and we will give to him the glory; for the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Revelation 7:13–14; Psalms 132:9"> and it was given to her that she should be clothed in fine linen, shining and pure. For the fine linen is the righteousness of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 22:6; Matthew 22:2–4; Luke 14:15–16"> And he saith to me, Write, Blessed are they who are called to the marriage-supper of the Lamb. And he saith to me, These are the true words of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 10:25–26; John 5:39"> And I fell at his feet to worship him; and he saith to me, See thou do it not; I am a fellow-servant of thee, and of thy brethren who maintain the testimony to Jesus; worship God. For the testimony to Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:13; Isaiah 11:3–5; Psalms 98:9"> And I saw heaven opened, and behold, a white horse, and he who sat upon him was called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he doth judge and make war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Luke 10:22; Revelation 2:17–18"> His eyes were a flame of fire, and on his head were many diadems; and he had names written, and a name written which no one knoweth but he himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 63:1–6; John 1:14"> and he was clothed with a garment dipped in blood; and his name is called, The Word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 19:8"> And the armies which are in heaven followed him upon white horses, clothed in fine linen, white and pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:27"> And out of his mouth goeth a sharp sword, that with it he may smite the nations; and he will rule them with a rod of iron; and he treadeth the wine-press of the fierceness of the wrath of God Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 6:15; Daniel 2:47"> And he hath on his garment and on his thigh a name written: KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:21; Revelation 8:13; Ezekiel 39:17–20"> And I saw an angel standing in the sun; and he cried with a loud voice, saying to all the birds that fly in mid-heaven, Come, gather yourselves together to the great supper of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:18–20; Revelation 6:15; 1 Samuel 17:44"> that ye may eat the flesh of kings, and the flesh of captains, and the flesh of mighty men, and the flesh of horses, and of those who sit on them, and the flesh of all men, both free and bond, lieth small and great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:16; Revelation 16:14; Revelation 17:12–14"> And I saw the beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, gathered together to make war against him who sat on the horse, and against his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:10; Revelation 21:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–11"> And the beast was seized and he who was with him, the false prophet who wrought the signs in his presence, with which he deceived those who received the mark of the beast, and who worshipped his image; the two were cast alive into the lake of fire burning with brimstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 19:11–15; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 19:17–19"> And the rest were slain with the sword of him who sat upon the horse, the sword which proceeded out of his mouth; and all the birds were glutted with their flesh.</span>
