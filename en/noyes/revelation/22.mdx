<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; Psalms 36:8–9"> And he showed me a river of water of life, clear as crystal, coming out of the throne of God and of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; 1 Peter 2:24; Revelation 2:7"> Between the street of the city and the river, on one side and on the other, is the tree of life, hearing twelve kinds of fruit, and yielding its fruit every month; and the leaves of the tree are for the healing of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Revelation 21:3–4; John 12:26"> And there shall be no more curse; and the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will serve him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 3:12"> and they will see his face, and his name will be upon their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4"> And there will be no night, and no need of lamp or light, for the Lord God will shine upon them; and they will reign for ever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:1; Revelation 21:5; Revelation 19:9"> And he said unto me, These words are faithful and true; and the Lord God of the spirits of the prophets sent his angel to show to his servants what must shortly come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:3; Revelation 3:11; Revelation 22:20"> And behold, I come quickly. Blessed is he that keepeth the words of the prophecy of this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 1:1; Revelation 19:19"> And I John am he who heard and saw these things; and when I had heard and when I had seen, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who showed me these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> And he saith to me, See thou do it not; I am a fellow-servant of thee, and of thy brethren the prophets, and of those who keep the words of this book; worship God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> And he saith to me, Seal not the words of the prophecy of this book; the time is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> He that is unjust, let him be unjust still, and he that is filthy, let him be filthy still; and he that is righteous, let him do righteousness still, and he that is holy, let him be holy still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> Behold, I come quickly, and my reward is with me, to give to every one according as his work is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed are they that wash their robes, that they may have a right to the tree of life, and may enter by the gates into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> Without are dogs, and sorcerers, and fornicators, and murderers, and idolaters, and whoever loveth and practiseth falsehood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> I Jesus sent my angel to testify these things to you for the churches. I am the shoot and the offspring of David, the bright morning-star.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 55:1–3"> And the Spirit and the bride say, Come! And let him that heareth say, Come! And let him that thirsteth come! Whoever will, let him take the water of life freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:6"> I testify to every one that heareth the words of the prophecy of this book, If any one shall add to them, God will add to him the plagues that are written in this book;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> and if any one shall take away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God will take away his part from the tree of life, and out of the holy city, which are written of in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 22:7; Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 22:12"> He who testifieth these things saith, Yea, I come quickly. Amen; come, Lord Jesus!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all.</span>
