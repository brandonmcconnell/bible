<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 38:1; Job 40:6"> Moreover Jehovah spake to Job, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:14; Job 33:13"> Will the censurer of the Almighty contend with him? Will the reprover of God answer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then Job answered Jehovah, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:32; Job 29:9; Job 21:5"> Behold, I am vile! what can I answer thee? I will lay my hand upon my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:14; Job 9:3; Job 9:15"> Once have I spoken, but I will not speak again; Yea, twice, but I will say no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 38:1; 2 Peter 3:10–12; Psalms 50:3–4"> Then spake Jehovah to Job out of the whirlwind, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:3; Job 42:4; Job 23:3–4"> Gird up now thy loins like a man! I will ask thee, and do thou instruct me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Isaiah 28:18; Job 32:2"> Wilt thou even disannul my right? Wilt thou condemn me, that thou mayst be righteous?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:13; Job 37:4–5; Isaiah 45:9"> Hast thou an arm like God's? Or canst thou thunder with thy voice like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 93:1; Psalms 149:4; Isaiah 59:17"> Deck thyself with grandeur and majesty, And array thyself in splendor and glory!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 2:11–12; Isaiah 2:17"> Send forth the fury of thy wrath! Look upon every proud one, and abase him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Malachi 4:3; Psalms 60:12"> Look upon every proud one, and bring him low; Yea, tread down the wicked in their place!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 2:10; Esther 7:8; Job 14:13"> Hide them in the dust together; Shut up their faces in darkness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–9; Romans 5:6; Psalms 44:6"> Then, indeed, will I give thee the praise, That thine own right hand can save thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 40:20; Psalms 104:14; Genesis 1:24–26"> Behold the river-horse, which I have made as well as thyself; He feedeth on grass like the ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Behold, what strength is in his loins! And what force in the muscles of his belly!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 41:23"> He bendeth his tail, like the cedar, And the sinews of his thighs are twisted together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 48:4; Job 7:12"> His bones are pipes of brass, And his limbs are bars of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 26:13; Isaiah 27:1"> He is chief among the works of God; He that made him gave him his sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 104:26; Psalms 104:14; Job 40:15"> For the mountains supply him with food, Where all the beasts of the field play.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7; Isaiah 19:6–7"> He lieth down under the lote-plants, In the covert of reeds, and in the fens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5; Leviticus 23:40; Isaiah 15:7"> The lote-plants cover him with their shadow, And the willows of the brook compass him about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 37:25; Isaiah 28:16"> Lo! the stream overfloweth, but he starteth not; He is unmoved though Jordan rush forth even to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 41:1–2"> Can one take him before his eyes, Or pierce his nose with hooks?</span>
