<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11"> Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 15:2; 1 Kings 19:11"> How long wilt thou speak such things? How long shall the words of thy mouth be like a strong wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Genesis 18:25"> Will God pervert judgment? Or will the Almighty pervert justice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 1:18–19; Job 5:4"> As thy children sinned against him, He hath given them up to their transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 4:7–10; Hebrews 3:7–8; Job 11:13"> But if thou wilt seek early to God, And make thy supplication to the Almighty,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; Proverbs 15:8"> If thou wilt be pure and upright, Surely he will yet arise for thee, And prosper thy righteous habitation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:12–13; Matthew 13:31–32; Proverbs 19:20"> So that thy beginning shall be small, And thy latter end very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Deuteronomy 4:32; Job 15:18"> For inquire, I pray thee, of the former age, And mark what hath been searched out by their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; Psalms 144:4; Job 14:2"> (For we are of yesterday and know nothing, Since our days upon the earth are but a shadow;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 12:35; Job 12:7–8"> Will not they instruct thee, and tell thee, And utter words from their understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> “Can the paper-reed grow up without mire? Can the bulrush grow without water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:6; Psalms 129:6–7; Matthew 13:20"> While it is yet in its greenness, and is not cut down, It withereth before any other herb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Job 11:20; Job 13:16"> Such is the fate of all who forget God; So perisheth the hope of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> His confidence shall come to nought, And his trust shall prove a spider's web.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 27:18; Luke 6:47–49; Matthew 7:24–27"> He shall lean upon his house, and it shall not stand; He shall lay fast hold on it, but it shall not endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 80:11; Job 21:7–15; Job 5:3"> He is in full green before the sun, And his branches shoot forth over his garden;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Mark 11:20; Jude 1:12"> His roots are entwined about the heap, And he seeth the place of stones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:10; Psalms 37:36; Job 7:8"> When he shall be destroyed from his place, It shall deny him, saying, 'I never saw thee.'</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 20:5; Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:8"> Lo! such is the joy of his course! And others shall spring up from his place”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 4:7; Psalms 37:37; Psalms 37:24"> Behold, God will not cast away an upright man; Nor will he help the evil-doers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 126:2; Genesis 21:6; Psalms 126:6"> While he filleth thy mouth with laughter, And thy lips with gladness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 132:18; Psalms 109:29; Psalms 35:26"> They that hate thee shall be clothed with shame, And the dwelling-place of the wicked shall come to nought.</span>
