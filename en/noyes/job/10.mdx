<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 7:11; 1 Kings 19:4; Numbers 11:15"> I am weary of my life; I will let loose within me my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:23–24; Job 9:29; Psalms 143:2"> I will say unto God, Do not condemn me! Show me wherefore thou contendest with me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Psalms 138:8; Job 14:15"> Is it a pleasure to thee to oppress, And to despise the work of thy hands, And to shine upon the plans of the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Luke 16:15; Job 9:32"> Hast thou eyes of flesh, Or seest thou as man seeth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 3:8; Psalms 90:2–4; Psalms 102:24–27"> Are thy days as the days of a man, Are thy years as the days of a mortal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 14:16; Psalms 10:15; Zephaniah 1:12"> That thou seekest after my iniquity, And searchest after my sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Daniel 3:15; Psalms 139:1–2"> Though thou knowest that I am not guilty, And that none can deliver from thy hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:73; Isaiah 43:7; Job 10:3"> Have thy hands completely fashioned and made me In every part, that thou mightst destroy me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 64:8; Genesis 3:19"> O remember that thou hast moulded me as clay! And wilt thou bring me again to dust?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 139:14–16"> Thou didst pour me out as milk, And curdle me as cheese;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4–8; Ephesians 4:16; Job 40:17–18"> With skin and flesh didst thou clothe me, And strengthen me with bones and sinews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> Thou didst grant me life and favor, And thy protection preserved my breath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 23:13; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 45:15"> Yet these things thou didst lay up in thy heart! I know that this was in thy mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 7:21; Psalms 130:3; Exodus 34:7"> If I sin, then thou markest me, And wilt not acquit me of mine iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Job 9:15; Job 9:20–21"> If I am wicked,—then woe unto me! Yet if righteous, I dare not lift up my head; I am full of confusion, beholding my affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Lamentations 3:10; Job 5:9"> If I lift it up, like a lion thou huntest me, And again showest thyself terrible unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 1:21; Job 16:8; Job 16:11–16"> Thou renewest thy witnesses against me, And increasest thine anger toward me; New hosts continually rise up against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 11:20"> Why then didst thou bring me forth from the womb? I should have perished, and no eye had seen me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:8"> I should be as though I had not been; I should have been borne from the womb to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:1; Psalms 39:13; Psalms 103:15–16"> Are not my days few? O spare then, And let me alone, that I may be at ease a little while,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 3:5; 2 Samuel 12:23; Job 16:22"> Before I go— whence I shall not return—To the land of darkness and death-shade,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 88:12; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 13:16"> The land of darkness like the blackness of death-shade, Where is no order, and where the light is as darkness.</span>
