<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 4:17; Romans 3:20; Job 25:4"> Of a truth, I know that it is so: For how can man be just before God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 10:2; Job 40:2; Isaiah 57:15–16"> If he choose to contend with him, He cannot answer him to one charge of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 36:5; Romans 11:33; Ephesians 3:20"> He is excellent in wisdom, mighty in strength: Who hath hardened himself against him, and prospered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Luke 21:11; Habakkuk 3:10"> He removeth the mountains, and they know it not; He overturneth them in his anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Haggai 2:6; Hebrews 12:26; Psalms 75:3"> He shaketh the earth out of her place, And the pillars thereof tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Amos 8:9; Luke 21:25–26"> He commandeth the sun, and it riseth not, And he sealeth up the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:24; Isaiah 40:22; Psalms 104:2–3"> He alone spreadeth out the heavens, And walketh upon the high waves of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Genesis 1:16; Job 38:31–41"> He made the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, And the secret chambers of the South.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 5:9; Psalms 72:18; Ephesians 3:20"> He doeth great things past finding out, Yea, wonderful things without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 23:8–9; Job 35:14; Psalms 77:19"> Lo! he goeth by me, but I see him not; He passeth along, but I do not perceive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 45:9; Job 11:10; Job 23:13"> Lo! he seizeth, and who can hinder him? Who will say to him, What doest thou?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 89:10; Job 26:12; Isaiah 30:7"> God will not turn away his anger; The proud helpers are brought low before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Job 9:3; Job 4:19"> How much less shall I answer him, And choose out words to contend with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 10:15; Job 8:5; Job 10:2"> Though I were innocent, I would not answer him; I would cast myself on the mercy of my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:24; Exodus 6:9; Psalms 66:18–20"> Should I call, and he make answer to me, I could not believe that he listened to my voice,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 16:14; Job 2:3; Job 34:6"> He who falleth upon me with a tempest, And multiplieth my wounds without cause!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:19; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 88:15–18"> Who will not suffer me to take my breath, But filleth me with bitterness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:32–33; Job 9:4; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> If I look to strength, “Lo! here am I!” &#91;&#91;saith he,&#93;&#93; If to justice, “Who shall summon me to trial?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:5–6; Philippians 3:12–15; Psalms 143:2"> Though I were upright, yet must my own mouth condemn me; Though I were innocent, He would prove me perverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Psalms 139:23–24; Proverbs 28:26"> Though I were innocent, I would not care for myself; I would despise my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:1–3; Luke 13:2–4; Job 10:8"> It is all one; therefore I will affirm, He destroyeth the righteous and the wicked alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 24:12; Job 1:13–19; Job 4:7"> When the scourge bringeth sudden destruction, He laugheth at the sufferings of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 10:3; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Psalms 73:3–7"> The earth is given into the hands of the wicked; He covereth the face of the judges thereof; If it be not He, who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 7:6–7; James 4:14; Psalms 90:9–10"> My days have been swifter than a courier; They have fled away; they have seen no good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Isaiah 18:2; Job 39:27–30"> They have gone by like the reed-skiffs; Like the eagle, darting upon his prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 7:13; Jeremiah 8:18; Job 7:11"> If I say, I will forget my lamentation, I will change my countenance, and take courage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15–16; Job 21:6"> Still am I in dread of the multitude of my sorrows; For I know that thou wilt not hold me innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 21:27; Job 10:14–17"> I shall be found guilty; Why then should I labor in vain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Psalms 26:6; 1 John 1:8"> If I wash myself in snow, And cleanse my hands with lye,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; Isaiah 59:6; Job 15:6"> Still wilt thou plunge me into the pit, So that my own clothes will abhor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 9:20; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Psalms 143:2"> For He is not a man, as I am, that I may contend with him, And that we may go together into judgment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:25; 1 John 2:1–2; Job 9:19"> There is no umpire between us, Who may lay his hand upon us both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 39:10; Job 13:11; Job 33:7"> Let him take from me his rod, And not dismay me with his terrors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Then I will speak, and not be afraid of him: For I am not so at heart.</span>
