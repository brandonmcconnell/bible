<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 33:9; Job 10:2"> So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> Then was kindled the wrath of Elihu, the son of Barachel, the Buzite, of the family of Ram; against Job was his wrath kindled, because he accounted himself righteous rather than God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> Against his three friends also was his wrath kindled, because they had not found an answer, and yet had condemned Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> Now Elihu had delayed to reply to Job, because they were older than himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> But when Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, his wrath was kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 15:10; 1 Timothy 5:1; Titus 2:6"> Then spake Elihu, the son of Barachel, the Buzite, and said: I am young, and ye are very old; Therefore I was afraid, And durst not make known to you my opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> I said, “Days should speak, And the multitude of years should teach wisdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But it is the spirit in man, Even the inspiration of the Almighty, that giveth him understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> Great men are not always wise, Nor do the aged always understand what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Therefore, I pray, listen to me: I also will declare my opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> Behold, I have waited for your words, I have listened to your arguments, Whilst ye searched out what to say;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> Yea, I have attended to you; And behold, none of you hath refuted Job, Nor answered his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> Say not, then, “We have found out wisdom; God must conquer him, not man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He hath not directed his discourse against me, And with speeches like yours will I not answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> They were confounded! they answered no more! They could say nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> I waited, but they spake not; They stood still; they answered no more!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> Therefore will I answer, on my part; I also will show my opinion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> For I am full of matter; The spirit within me constraineth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> Behold, my bosom is as wine that hath no vent; Like bottles of new wine, which are bursting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> I will speak, that I may be relieved; I will open my lips and answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> I will not be partial to any man's person, Nor will I flatter any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> For I know not how to flatter; Soon would my Maker take me away.</span>
