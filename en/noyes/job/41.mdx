<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 25:15; Psalms 55:21"> Will he make many entreaties to thee? Will he speak soft words to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 21:6; Deuteronomy 15:17; Psalms 8:5–6"> Will he make a covenant with thee? Canst thou take him for a servant for ever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 16:25–30; Job 28:11"> Canst thou play with him, as with a bird? Or canst thou bind him for thy maidens?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:11"> Do men in company lay snares for him? Do they divide him among the merchants?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Canst thou fill his skirt with barbed irons, Or his head with fish-spears?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Luke 14:31–32; 2 Kings 10:4"> Do but lay thy hand upon him,—Thou wilt no more think of battle!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:19; 1 Samuel 3:11; Luke 21:11"> Behold, his hope is vain! Is he not cast down at the very sight of him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:4; Psalms 2:11–12; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> None is so fierce that he dare stir him up; Who then is he that can stand before me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:35; Psalms 24:1; Job 35:7"> Who hath done me a favor, that I must repay him? Whatever is under the whole heaven is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:25"> I will not be silent concerning his limbs, And his strength, and the beauty of his armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Psalms 32:9; James 3:3"> Who can uncover the surface of his garment? Who will approach his jaws?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:4; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 58:6"> Who will open the doors of his face? The rows of his teeth are terrible!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Revelation 5:2–3; Jeremiah 9:23"> His glory is his strong shields, United with each other, as with a close seal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They are joined one to another, So that no air can come between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They cleave fast to each other, They hold together, and cannot be separated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 3:9; Revelation 1:14"> His sneezing sendeth forth light, And his eyes are like the eyelashes of the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:8"> Out of his mouth go flames, And sparks of fire leap forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:13–14"> From his nostrils issueth smoke, as from a heated pot, or caldron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; Habakkuk 3:5"> His breath kindleth coals, And flames issue from his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Job 40:16; 1 Corinthians 15:55–57"> In his neck dwelleth strength, And terror danceth before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The flakes of his flesh cleave fast together; They are firm upon him, and cannot be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Isaiah 48:4; Zechariah 7:12"> His heart is solid like a stone; Yea, solid like the nether millstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:4–6; Psalms 107:28"> When he riseth up, the mighty are afraid; Yea, they lose themselves for terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 39:21–24"> The sword of him that assaileth him doth not stand, The spear, the dart, nor the habergeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He regardeth iron as straw, And brass as rotten wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:10; Job 39:7"> The arrow cannot make him flee; Sling-stones to him become stubble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:14"> Clubs are accounted by him as straw; He laugheth at the shaking of the spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15"> Under him are sharp potsherds; He spreadeth out a thrashing-sledge upon the mire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He maketh the deep to boil like a caldron; He maketh the sea like a pot of ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 38:30; Genesis 1:2; Genesis 15:15"> Behind him he leaveth a shining path; One would think the deep to be hoary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 40:19"> Upon the earth there is not his master; He is made without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Psalms 73:6; Job 28:8"> He looketh down upon all that is high; He is king over all the sons of pride.</span>
