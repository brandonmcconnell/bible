<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 42:7; Job 2:11; Job 22:1"> Then answered Eliphaz the Temanite, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 6:26; Job 8:2; Hosea 12:1"> Should a wise man answer with arguments of wind, Or fill his bosom with the east wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Matthew 12:36–37; Job 13:4–5"> Should he argue with speech that helpeth him not, And with words which do not profit him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 3:31; Luke 18:1; Amos 6:10"> Behold, thou makest the fear of God a vain thing, And discouragest prayer before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–5; James 3:5–8; Luke 6:45"> Yea, thy own mouth proclaimeth thy iniquity, Though thou choosest the tongue of the crafty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 12:37; Job 9:20"> Thy own mouth condemneth thee, and not I; Thy own lips testify against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Job 12:12; Job 38:4–41"> Art thou the first man that was born? Wast thou formed before the hills?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 11:34; Jeremiah 23:18; Job 13:5–6"> Hast thou listened in the council of God, And drawn all wisdom to thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 11:5; Job 12:3"> What dost thou know, that we know not also? What dost thou understand, that is a secret to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 32:6–7; Job 8:8–10; Job 12:12"> With us are the aged and hoary-headed; Much older than thy father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:2; 2 Corinthians 1:3–5; 1 Kings 22:24"> Dost thou despise the consolations of God, And words so full of kindness to thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Mark 7:21–22"> Why hath thy passion taken possession of thee? And why this winking of thine eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:25–27; James 3:2–6; Job 9:4"> For against God hast thou turned thy spirit, And uttered such words from thy mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Job 14:4; Proverbs 20:9"> What is man, that he should be pure, And he that is born of woman, that he should be innocent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 25:5; Job 4:18; Job 5:1"> Behold, He putteth no trust in his ministering spirits, And the heavens are not pure in his sight;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 34:7; Proverbs 19:28; Romans 3:9–19"> Much less, abominable and polluted man, Who drinketh iniquity as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 36:2; Job 34:2; Job 13:5–6"> Hear me, and I will show thee, And that which I have seen will I declare;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 8:8; Isaiah 38:19; Psalms 71:18"> Which the wise men have told, And not kept concealed, as received from their fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Genesis 10:32; Genesis 10:25"> To whom alone the land was given, And among whom not a stranger wandered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:13; James 5:1–6; Luke 12:19–21"> “All his days the wicked man is in pain; Yea, all the years, that are laid up for the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Leviticus 26:36"> A fearful sound is in his ears; In peace the destroyer cometh upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 20:24–25; Matthew 27:5; Job 9:16"> He hath no hope that he shall escape from darkness; He is set apart for the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 109:10; Psalms 59:15; Job 18:12"> He wandereth about, seeking bread; He knoweth that a day of darkness is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 24:34; Proverbs 6:11; Job 6:2–4"> Distress and anguish fill him with dread; They prevail against him like a king ready for the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Psalms 52:7; Psalms 73:9"> Because he stretched forth his hand against God, And bade defiance to the Almighty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 18:40; Job 16:12; 2 Chronicles 32:13–17"> And ran against him with outstretched neck, With the thick bosses of his bucklers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 17:10; Jeremiah 5:28; Psalms 73:7"> Because he covered his face with fatness, And gathered fat upon his loins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 3:14; Jeremiah 51:37; Micah 7:18"> And dwelt in desolated cities, In houses which no man inhabiteth, That are ready to become heaps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Psalms 49:16–17; James 1:11"> He shall not be rich; his substance shall not endure, And his possessions shall not be extended upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 4:9; Job 15:22; Job 18:5–6"> He shall not escape from darkness, And the flame shall dry up his branches; Yea, by the breath of His mouth shall he be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 59:4; Galatians 6:3; Psalms 62:10"> “Let not man trust in vanity! he will be deceived; For vanity shall be his recompense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:17; Psalms 55:23; Hosea 9:16"> He shall come to his end before his time, And his branch shall not be green.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:39–40; Isaiah 33:9; Revelation 6:13"> He shall shake off his unripe fruit like the vine, And shed his blossoms like the olive-tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 8:13; Job 27:8; Job 8:22"> The house of the unrighteous shall be famished, And fire shall consume the tents of bribery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Hosea 10:13; Isaiah 59:4–5"> They conceive mischief, and bring forth misery, And their breast deviseth deceit.”</span>
