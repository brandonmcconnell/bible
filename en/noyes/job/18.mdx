<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 8:1; Job 25:1"> Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> How long ere ye make an end of words? Understand, and then we will speak!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> Why are we accounted as brutes, And reputed vile in your sight?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> Thou that tearest thyself in thine anger! Must the earth be deserted for thee, And the rock removed from its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> Behold, the light of the wicked shall be put out, And the flame of his fire shall not shine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> Light shall become darkness in his tent, And his lamp over him shall go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> His strong steps shall be straitened, And his own plans shall cast him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> He is brought into the net by his own feet, And he walketh upon snares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> The trap layeth hold of him by the heel, And the snare holdeth him fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> A net is secretly laid for him on the ground, And a trap for him in the pathway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremiah 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Terrors affright him on every side, And harass him at his heels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> His strength is wasted by hunger, And destruction is ready at his side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 2:6; Zechariah 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> His limbs are consumed, Yea, his limbs are devoured by the first-born of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> He is torn from his tent, which was his confidence, And is borne away to the king of terrors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 34:9–10"> They who are none of his shall dwell in his tent; Brimstone shall be scattered upon his habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 9:16; Isaiah 5:24; Job 15:30"> His roots below shall be dried up, And his branches above shall be withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> His memory perisheth from the earth, And no name hath he in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> He shall be thrust from light into darkness, And driven out of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:30; Isaiah 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He hath no son, nor kinsman among his people, Nor any survivor in his dwelling-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Jeremiah 50:27; Ezekiel 21:25"> They that come after him shall be amazed at his fate, As they that were before them were struck with horror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremiah 9:3; Jeremiah 10:25"> Yea, such is the dwelling of the unrighteous man; Such is the place of him who knoweth not God!</span>
