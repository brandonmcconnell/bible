<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 20:1"> Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; James 1:19; Job 18:2"> Shall not the multitude of words receive an answer? Shall the man of words be justified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 24:25; Titus 2:8"> Shall thy boastings make men hold their peace? Shalt thou mock, and none put thee to shame?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 6:10; Job 35:2"> Thou sayest, My speech is pure; I am clean in thine eyes, &#91;&#91;O God!&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 38:1–2; Job 31:35; Job 33:6–18"> But O that God would speak, And open his lips against thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:13; Lamentations 3:22; Daniel 2:28"> That he would show thee the secrets of his wisdom,—His wisdom, which is unsearchable! Then shouldst thou know that God forgiveth thee many of thine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalms 145:3"> Canst thou search out the deep things of God? Canst thou find out the Almighty to perfection?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 22:12; Psalms 139:6–8; Isaiah 55:9"> 'Tis high as heaven, what canst thou do? Deeper than hell, what canst thou know?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 139:9–10; Job 28:24–25; Psalms 65:5–8"> The measure thereof is longer than the earth, And broader than the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:12–13; Job 12:14; Revelation 3:7"> If he apprehend, and bind, and bring to trial, Who shall oppose him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 10:14; Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23"> For he knoweth the unrighteous; He seeth iniquity, when they do not observe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:20; Ecclesiastes 3:18; James 3:13–17"> But vain man is without understanding; Yea, man is born a wild ass's colt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 143:6; Psalms 88:9; 1 Samuel 7:3"> If thou direct thy heart, And stretch out thy hands, toward him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:23; James 4:8; Job 4:7"> If thou put away iniquity from thy hand, And let not wickedness dwell in thy habitation,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 1 John 3:19–22"> Then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot; Yea, thou shalt be steadfast, and have no fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 65:16; Ecclesiastes 5:20; John 16:21"> For thou shalt forget thy misery, Or remember it as waters that have passed away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8–10; Psalms 37:6; Proverbs 4:18"> Thy life shall be brighter than the noon-day; Now thou art in darkness, thou shalt then be as morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24–26; Psalms 43:5; Psalms 3:5"> Thou shalt be secure, because there is hope; Now thou art disappointed, thou shalt then rest in safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:12; Isaiah 60:14; Job 42:8–9"> Thou shalt lie down, and none shall make thee afraid; And many shall make suit unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:65; Job 31:16; Job 17:5"> But the eyes of the wicked shall be wearied out; They shall find no refuge; Their hope is—the breathing forth of life.</span>
