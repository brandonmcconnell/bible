<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> O that my grief were weighed thoroughly! That my calamities were put together in the balance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> Surely they would be heavier than the sand of the sea; On this account were my words rash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Almighty have pierced me; Their poison drinketh up my spirit; The terrors of God set themselves in array against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremiah 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> Doth the wild ass bray in the midst of grass? Or loweth the ox over his fodder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Can that which is unsavory be eaten without salt? Is there any taste in the white of an egg?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Ezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> That which my soul abhorreth to touch Hath become my loathsome food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> O that I might have my request, And that God would grant me that which I long for!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3; Jonah 4:8"> That it would please God to destroy me; That he would let loose his hand, and make an end of me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Isaiah 57:15"> Yet it should still be my consolation, Yea, in unsparing anguish I would exult, That I have not denied the commands of the Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> What is my strength, that I should hope? And what mine end, that I should be patient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones? Or is my flesh brass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Alas, there is no help within me! Deliverance is driven from me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> To the afflicted, kindness should be shown by a friend; Else he casteth off the fear of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> But my brethren are faithless like a brook; Like streams of the valley that pass away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Which are turbid by reason of the ice, And the snow, which hideth itself in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Job 24:19"> As soon as they flow forth, they vanish; When the heat cometh, they are dried up from their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The caravans turn aside to them on their way; They go up into the desert, and perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremiah 25:23"> The caravans of Tema look for them; The companies of Sheba expect to see them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They are ashamed that they have relied on them; They come to their place, and are confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> So ye also are nothing; Ye see a terror, and shrink back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; 1 Samuel 12:3"> Have I said, Bring me gifts? Or, Give a present for me out of your substance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> Or, Deliver me from the enemy's hand? Or, Rescue me from the hand of the violent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Convince me, and I will hold my peace; Cause me to understand wherein I have erred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> How powerful are the words of truth! But what do your reproaches prove?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Do ye mean to censure words? The words of a man in despair are but wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Truly ye spread a net for the fatherless; Ye dig a pit for your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> Look now upon me, I pray you; For to your very face can I speak falsehood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Return, I pray, and let there be no unfairness; Yea, return;—still is my cause righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> Is there iniquity on my tongue? Cannot my taste discern what is sinful?</span>
