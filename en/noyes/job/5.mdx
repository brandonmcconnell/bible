<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 127:5; Job 18:16–19; Psalms 119:155"> His children are far from safety; They are oppressed at the gate, and there is none to deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Hosea 8:7; Jeremiah 51:44"> His harvest the hungry devour, Carrying it even through the thorns; And a snare gapeth after his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Lamentations 3:38; 1 Samuel 6:9"> For affliction cometh not from the dust, Nor doth trouble spring up from the ground;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 14:1; Genesis 3:17–19; Ecclesiastes 5:15–17"> Behold, man is born to trouble, As the sparks fly upward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Job 22:21; Psalms 37:5"> I would look to God, And to God would I commit my cause,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; Job 9:10; Psalms 86:10"> Who doeth great things and unsearchable; Yea, marvellous things without number;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:24; Acts 14:17; Psalms 147:8"> Who giveth rain upon the earth, And sendeth water upon the fields;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 113:7; 1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 6:21"> Who placeth the lowly in high places, And restoreth the afflicted to prosperity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 33:10–11; Psalms 21:11; Isaiah 8:10"> Who disappointeth the devices of the crafty, So that their hands cannot perform their enterprises;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19; Psalms 9:15–16; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Who taketh the wise in their own craftiness, And bringeth to nought the counsel of the artful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 12:25; Isaiah 59:10; Deuteronomy 28:29"> They meet with darkness in the daytime; They grope at noon as if it were night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 35:10; Psalms 140:12; Psalms 109:31"> So he saveth the persecuted from their mouth; The oppressed from the hand of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 107:42; 1 Samuel 2:8–9; Psalms 63:11"> So the poor hath hope, And iniquity stoppeth her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 94:12; Proverbs 3:11–12"> Behold, happy is the man whom God correcteth; Therefore despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 6:1; Deuteronomy 32:39; Isaiah 30:26"> For he bruiseth, and bindeth up; He woundeth, and his hands make whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Proverbs 24:16"> In six troubles will he deliver thee; Yea, in seven shall no evil touch thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 33:19; Hosea 13:14; Psalms 37:19"> In famine he will redeem thee from death, And in war from the power of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 31:20; Psalms 91:5–7; Jeremiah 18:18"> Thou shalt be safe from the scourge of the tongue, And shalt not be afraid of destruction, when it cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:25; Isaiah 35:9; Psalms 91:13"> At destruction and famine thou shalt laugh, And of the wild beasts of the land shalt thou not be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:6; Hosea 2:18; Daniel 6:22"> For thou shalt be in league with the stones of the field; Yea, the beasts of the forest shall be at peace with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 121:7–8; Psalms 91:10; Isaiah 4:5–6"> Thou shalt find that thy tent is in peace; Thou shalt visit thy dwelling, and not be disappointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 112:2; Psalms 72:16; Deuteronomy 28:4"> Thou shalt see thy descendants numerous, And thine offspring as the grass of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 42:16–17; Proverbs 9:11; Psalms 91:16"> Thou shalt come to thy grave in full age, As a shock of corn gathered in its season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 111:2; Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 9:12"> Lo! this we have searched out; so it is: Hear it, and lay it up in thy mind!</span>
