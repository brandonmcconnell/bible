<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> Moreover Job continued his discourse, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremiah 31:28"> O that I were as in months past, In the days when God was my guardian;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> When his lamp shined over my head, And when by his light I walked through darkness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As I was in the autumn of my days, When the friendship of God was over my tent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When the Almighty was yet with me, And my children were around me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> When I bathed my steps in milk, And the rock poured me out rivers of oil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I went forth to the gate by the city, And took my seat in the market-place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The young men saw me and hid themselves, And the aged arose and stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> The princes refrained from speaking, And laid their hand upon their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Ezekiel 3:26"> The nobles held their peace, And their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> When the ear heard me, then it blessed me; And when the eye saw me, it gave witness to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> For I delivered the poor, when they cried; And the fatherless, who had none to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me, And I caused the heart of the widow to sing for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; Isaiah 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> I clothed myself with righteousness, and it clothed itself with me; And justice was my robe and diadem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was eyes to the blind, And feet was I to the lame;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was a father to the poor, And the cause of him I knew not I searched out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; 1 Samuel 17:35"> And I broke the teeth of the wicked, And plucked the spoil from his jaws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> Then said I, “I shall die in my nest; I shall multiply my days as the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8; Hosea 14:5–7"> My root is spread abroad to the waters, And the dew abideth on my branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory is fresh with me, And my bow gathereth strength in my hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> To me men gave ear, and waited, And kept silence for my counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Solomon 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> To my words they made no reply, When my speech dropped down upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hosea 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zechariah 10:1"> Yea, they waited for me as for the rain; They opened their mouths wide as for the latter rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> If I smiled upon them, they believed it not; Nor did they cause the light of my countenance to fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> When I came among them, I sat as chief; I dwelt as a king in the midst of an army, As a comforter among mourners.</span>
