<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> At length Job opened his mouth, and cursed the day of his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> And Job spake and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:14–18; Jeremiah 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Perish the day in which I was born, and the night which said, “A man-child is conceived!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> Let that day be darkness; Let not God seek it from above; Yea, let not the light shine upon it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> Let darkness and the shadow of death redeem it; Let a cloud dwell upon it; Let whatever darkeneth the day terrify it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As for that night, let darkness seize upon it; Let it not rejoice among the days of the year; Let it not come into the number of the months!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 24:8; Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> O let that night be unfruitful! Let there be in it no voice of joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> Let them that curse the day curse it, Who are skilful to stir up the leviathan!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremiah 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let the stars of its twilight be darkened; Let it long for light, and have none; Neither let it see the eyelashes of the morning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> Because it shut not up the doors of my mother's womb, And hid not trouble from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Isaiah 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> Why died I not at my birth? Why did I not expire when I came forth from the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> Why did the knees receive me, And why the breasts, that I might suck?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> For now should I lie down and be quiet; I should sleep; then should I be at rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> With kings and counsellors of the earth, Who built up for themselves—ruins!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; Job 22:25; Zephaniah 1:18"> Or with princes that had gold, And filled their houses with silver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> Or, as a hidden untimely birth, I had perished; As infants which never saw the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> There the wicked cease from troubling; There the weary are at rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Isaiah 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> There the prisoners rest together; They hear not the voice of the oppressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> The small and the great are there, And the servant is free from his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:18; Job 7:15–16; 1 Samuel 1:10"> Why giveth He light to him that is in misery, And life to the bitter in soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; 1 Kings 19:4"> Who long for death, and it cometh not, And dig for it more than for hid treasures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Who rejoice exceedingly, Yea, exult, when they can find a grave?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Isaiah 40:27"> Why is light given to a man from whom the way is hid, And whom God hath hedged in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> For my sighing cometh before I eat, And my groans are poured out like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> For that which I dread overtaketh me; That at which I shudder cometh upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I have no peace, nor quiet, nor respite: Misery cometh upon me continually.</span>
