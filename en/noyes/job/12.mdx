<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; Job 8:8–10; 1 Corinthians 6:5"> No doubt ye are the whole people! And wisdom will die with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you: Yea, who knoweth not such things as these?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> I am become a laughing-stock to my friend,—I who call upon God, that he would answer me! The innocent and upright man is held in derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> To calamity belongeth contempt in the mind of one at ease; It is ready for them that slip with the feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> The tents of robbers are in prosperity, And they who provoke God are secure, Who carry their God in their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremiah 8:7"> For ask now the beasts, and they will teach thee; Or the fowls of the air, and they will tell thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Or speak to the earth, and it will instruct thee; And the fishes of the sea will declare unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:20; Jeremiah 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who among all these doth not know That the hand of Jehovah doeth these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, And the breath of all mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> Doth not the ear prove words, As the mouth tasteth meat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> With the aged is wisdom, And with length of days is understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> With Him are wisdom and strength; With Him counsel and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Isaiah 22:22; Malachi 1:4"> Lo! he pulleth down, and it shall not be rebuilt; He bindeth a man, and he shall not be set loose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> Lo! he withholdeth the waters, and they are dried up; He sendeth them forth, and they lay waste the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> With him are strength and wisdom; The deceived and the deceiver are his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; 2 Samuel 17:23"> He leadeth counsellors away captive, And judges he maketh fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> He looseth the authority of kings, And bindeth their loins with a cord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He leadeth priests away captive, And overthroweth the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He removeth speech from the trusty, And taketh away judgment from the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> He poureth contempt upon princes, And looseth the girdle of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> He revealeth deep things out of darkness, And bringeth the shadow of death to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> He exalteth nations, and destroyeth them; He enlargeth nations, and leadeth them captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> He taketh away the understanding of the great men of the land, And causeth them to wander in a wilderness, where is no path;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> They grope in the dark without light; He maketh them stagger like a drunken man.</span>
