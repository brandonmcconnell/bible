<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover Elihu proceeded, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:36–37; Job 19:6–7; Job 16:17"> Dost thou then think this to be right? Thou hast said, “I am more righteous than God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 34:9; Malachi 3:14; Job 9:30–31"> For thou askest, “What advantage have I? What have I gained, more than if I had sinned?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Job 34:8"> I will answer thee, And thy companions with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 22:12; Isaiah 55:9; Nahum 1:3"> Look up to the heavens, and see! And behold the clouds, which are high above thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:19; Proverbs 8:36; Proverbs 9:12"> If thou sinnest, what doest thou against Him? If thy transgressions be multiplied, what doest thou to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; Psalms 16:2; Luke 17:10"> If thou art righteous, what dost thou give him? Or what receiveth he at thy hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Job 42:8; Acts 27:24"> Thy wickedness injureth only a man like thyself, And thy righteousness profiteth only a son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Job 34:28; Exodus 3:7"> The oppressed cry out on account of the multitude of wrongs; They cry aloud on account of the arm of the mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Acts 16:25; Psalms 149:5"> But none saith, “Where is God, my Maker, Who giveth songs in the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 94:12; Job 36:22; Job 32:8"> Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, And maketh us wiser than the birds of heaven?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 1:28; John 9:31; Psalms 123:3–4"> There they cry aloud on account of the pride of the wicked; But he giveth no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; James 4:3"> For God will not hear the vain supplication, Nor will the Almighty regard it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 9:11; Micah 7:7–9; Psalms 37:5–6"> Much less when thou sayest thou canst not see him: Justice is with him,—only wait thou for him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 89:32; Job 9:14; Job 13:15"> But now, because he hath not visited in his anger, Nor taken strict note of transgression,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 38:2; Job 33:8–12; Job 34:35–37"> Therefore hath Job opened his mouth rashly, And multiplied words without knowledge.</span>
