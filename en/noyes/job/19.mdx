<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 64:3; Job 18:2; Job 27:2"> How long will ye vex my soul, And break me in pieces with words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; Nehemiah 4:12; Job 18:4–21"> These ten times have ye reviled me; Without shame do ye stun me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Proverbs 9:12; Ezekiel 18:4"> And be it, indeed, that I have erred, My error abideth with myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 35:26; Psalms 38:16; Psalms 55:12"> Since, indeed, ye magnify yourselves against me, And plead against me my reproach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 27:2; Job 16:11–14"> Know then that it is God who hath brought me low; He hath encompassed me with his net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Lamentations 3:8; Job 31:35–36"> Behold, I complain of wrong, but receive no answer; I cry aloud, but obtain no justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 3:7; Job 3:23; Proverbs 4:19"> He hath fenced up my way, so that I cannot pass, And hath set darkness in my paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:44; Job 29:7–14; Psalms 89:39"> He hath stripped me of my glory, And taken the crown from my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 24:20; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9"> He hath destroyed me on every side, and I am gone! He hath torn up my hope like a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:24; Job 16:9; Job 33:10"> He kindleth his anger against me, And counteth me as his enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 30:12; Job 16:13; Isaiah 51:23"> His troops advance together against me; They throw up for themselves a way to me, And encamp around my dwelling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 69:8"> My brethren he hath put far from me, And my acquaintance are wholly estranged from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremiah 20:10; 2 Samuel 16:23"> My kinsfolk have forsaken me, And my bosom friends have forgotten me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 31:31–32; Psalms 123:3"> The foreigners of my house, yea, my own maid-servants, regard me as a stranger; I am an alien in their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:15–17; Job 1:19"> I call my servant, and he maketh no answer; With my own mouth do I entreat him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 2:9–10; Job 17:1"> My breath is become strange to my wife, And my prayers also to my own mother's sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 2:23; Job 30:12; Job 30:1"> Even young children despise me; When I rise up, they speak against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Psalms 109:4–5; Psalms 55:12–14"> All my bosom friends abhor me, And they whom I loved are turned against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 102:5; Lamentations 4:8; Job 33:19–22"> My bones cleave to my flesh and my skin, And I have scarcely escaped with the skin of my teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:11; Psalms 38:2; Job 2:5"> Have pity upon me, O ye my friends! have pity upon me; For the hand of God hath smitten me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 69:26; Job 31:31; Job 16:13–14"> Why do ye persecute me like God, And are not satisfied with my flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Job 31:35; Isaiah 8:1"> O that my words were now written! O that they were marked down in a scroll!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Exodus 28:21; Exodus 32:16"> That with an iron pen, and with lead, They were engraven upon the rock for ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Isaiah 54:5; Psalms 78:35"> Yet I know that my Vindicator liveth, And will hereafter stand up on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; 1 John 3:2; Psalms 17:15"> And though with my skin this body be wasted away, Yet without my flesh shall I see God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Isaiah 26:19; Numbers 24:17"> Yea, I shall see him my friend; My eyes shall behold him, and not another: For this, my soul panteth within me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 19:22; Psalms 69:26; 1 Kings 14:13"> Since ye say, “How may we persecute him, And find grounds of accusation against him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Psalms 58:10–11"> Be ye afraid of the sword! For malice is a crime for the sword; That ye may know that judgment cometh.</span>
