<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:14–15; 1 John 2:16; Matthew 5:28–29"> I made a covenant with mine eyes; How then could I gaze upon a maid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 20:29; Job 27:13; Hebrews 13:4"> For what is the portion appointed by God from above, And the inheritance allotted by the Almighty from on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 21:30; Job 34:22; Matthew 7:13"> Is not destruction for the wicked, And ruin for the workers of iniquity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 34:21; Proverbs 5:21; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> Doth He not see my ways, And number all my steps?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Psalms 44:20–21; Psalms 12:2"> If I have walked with falsehood, And if my foot hath hasted to deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 7:8–9; Daniel 5:27; Isaiah 26:7"> Let him weigh me in an even balance; Yea, let God know my integrity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Matthew 5:29"> If my steps have turned aside from the way, And my heart gone after mine eyes, Or if any stain hath cleaved to my hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:38"> Then I may sow, and another eat; And what I plant, may it be rooted up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Ecclesiastes 7:26; Judges 16:5"> If my heart hath been enticed by a woman, Or if I have watched at my neighbor's door,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:10; 2 Samuel 12:11; Isaiah 47:2"> Then let my wife grind for another, And let other men lie with her!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Job 31:28"> For this were a heinous crime, Even a transgression to be punished by the judges;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 6:27; Jeremiah 5:7–9; Job 15:30"> Yea, it were a fire that would consume to destruction, And root out all my increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:14–15; Exodus 21:26–27; Leviticus 25:46"> If I have refused justice to my man-servant or maid-servant, When they had a controversy with me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 2:13; Psalms 9:12; Zechariah 2:13"> Then what shall I do when God riseth up? And when he visiteth, what shall I answer him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Malachi 2:10; Job 34:19"> Did not He that made me in the womb make him? Did not one fashion us in the womb?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Deuteronomy 15:7–10; Deuteronomy 28:32"> If I have refused the poor their desire, And caused the eyes of the widow to fail;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 29:12–16; John 13:29; Job 22:7"> If I have eaten my morsel alone, And the fatherless hath not partaken of it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> (Nay, from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, And I have helped the widow from my mother's womb;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 22:6; Luke 3:11; Job 24:4"> If I have seen any one perishing for want of clothing, Or any poor man without covering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Job 29:11"> If his loins have not blessed me, And he hath not been warmed with the fleece of my sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 22:9; Job 29:12; Micah 7:3"> If I have shaken my hand against the fatherless, Because I saw my help in the gate,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Job 38:15; Job 31:40"> Then may my shoulder fill from its blade, And my fore-arm be broken from its bone!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 13:11; Psalms 119:120; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> For destruction from God was a terror to me, And before his majesty I could do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–7; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Proverbs 11:28"> If I have made gold my trust, Or said to the fine gold, Thou art my confidence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 62:10; Habakkuk 1:16; Isaiah 10:13–14"> If I have rejoiced, because my wealth was great, And my hand had found abundance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3"> If I have beheld the sun in his splendor, Or the moon advancing in brightness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Deuteronomy 11:16; Psalms 2:12"> And my heart hath been secretly enticed, And my mouth hath kissed my hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; Job 31:11; Psalms 50:6"> This also were a crime to be punished by the judge; For I should have denied the God who is above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17–18; Proverbs 17:5; Psalms 35:13–14"> If I have rejoiced at the destruction of him that hated me, And exulted when evil came upon him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:14; Exodus 23:4–5"> (Nay, I have not suffered my mouth to sin, By asking with curses his life;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 1:11–12; Micah 3:2–3"> If the men of my tent have not exclaimed, “Who is there that hath not been satisfied with his meat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:20–21"> The stranger did not lodge in the street; I opened my doors to the traveller.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Hosea 6:7; Acts 5:8"> Have I, after the manner of men, hidden my transgression, Concealing my iniquity in my bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 29:25; Esther 4:11"> Then let me be confounded before the great multitude! Let the contempt of families cover me with shame! Yea, let me keep silence! let me never appear abroad!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 13:21–22; Job 40:4–5; Job 17:3"> O that there were one who would hear me! Behold my signature! let the Almighty answer me. And let mine adversary write down his charge!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 22:22; Job 29:14; Isaiah 62:3"> Truly I would wear it upon my shoulder; I would bind it upon me as a crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Job 13:15; Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:15–16"> I would disclose to him all my steps; I would approach him like a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; James 5:4; Psalms 65:13"> If my land cry out against me, And its furrows bewail together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; James 5:4; Isaiah 26:21"> If I have eaten of its fruits without payment, And wrung out the life of its owners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; Malachi 1:3; Psalms 72:20"> Let thorns grow up instead of wheat, And noxious weeds instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.</span>
