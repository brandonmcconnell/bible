<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> Can a man, then, profit God? Behold, the wise man profiteth himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> Is it a pleasure to the Almighty, that thou art righteous; Or a gain to him, that thou walkest uprightly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Will he contend with thee because he feareth thee? Will he enter with thee into judgment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Hath not thy wickedness been great? Have not thine iniquities been numberless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Ezekiel 18:12; Ezekiel 18:16"> For thou hast taken a pledge from thy brother unjustly, And stripped the poor of their clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Isaiah 58:7"> Thou hast given the weary no water to drink, And withholden bread from the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> But the man of power, his was the land, And the honorable man dwelt in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Ezekiel 22:7"> Thou hast sent widows away empty, And broken the arms of the fatherless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore snares are round about thee, And sudden fear confoundeth thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonah 2:3"> Or darkness, through which thou canst not see, And floods of water cover thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Is not God in the height of heaven? And behold the stars, how high they are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Ezekiel 9:9"> Hence thou sayest, “What doth God know? Can he govern behind the thick darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> Dark clouds are a veil to him, and he cannot see; And he walketh upon the arch of heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Wilt thou take the old way Which wicked men have trodden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> Who were cut down before their time, And whose foundations were swept away by a flood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Who said unto God, “Depart from us!” And, “What can the Almighty do to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> And yet he filled their houses with good things!—Far from me be the counsel of the wicked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The righteous see their fate, and rejoice; And the innocent hold them in derision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> “Truly our adversary is destroyed, And fire hath consumed his abundance!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Isaiah 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Acquaint now thyself with him, and be at peace: Thus shall prosperity return to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> Receive, I pray thee, instruction from his mouth, And lay up his words in thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zechariah 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up; If thou put away iniquity from thy tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:12; 1 Kings 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> Cast to the dust thy gold, And the gold of Ophir to the stones of the brook:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> Then shall the Almighty be thy gold, Yea, treasures of silver unto thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Isaiah 58:14; Job 27:10"> For then shalt thou have delight in the Almighty, And shalt lift up thy face unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> Thou shalt pray to him, and he shall hear thee, And thou shalt perform thy vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> The purpose which thou formest shall prosper with thee, And light shall shine upon thy ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> When men are cast down, thou shalt say, “There lifting up!” And the humble person he will save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He will deliver even him that is not innocent. The purity of thy hands shall save him.</span>
