<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 29:9; 1 Samuel 24:2; Psalms 104:18"> Knowest thou the time when the wild goats of the rock bring forth? Or canst thou observe when the hinds are in labor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24"> Canst thou number the months they fulfill, And know the season when they bring forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> They bow themselves; they bring forth their young; They cast forth their pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Their young ones are strong; they grow up in the fields; They go away, and return not to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 11:12; Job 24:5; Job 6:5"> Who hath sent forth the wild ass free? Who hath loosed the bands of the wild ass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Job 24:5; Deuteronomy 29:23"> To whom I have given the wilderness for his house, And the barren land for his dwelling-place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 5:18; Job 3:18; Exodus 5:13–16"> He scorneth the tumult of the city, And heedeth not the shouting of the driver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 104:27–28; Psalms 145:15–16; Job 40:15"> The range of the mountains is his pasture; He seeketh after every green thing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:22; Deuteronomy 33:17; Psalms 92:10"> Will the wild-ox consent to serve thee? Will he pass the night at thy crib?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 10:10–11; Psalms 129:3; Job 39:7"> Canst thou bind the wild-ox with the harness to the furrow? Or will he harrow the valleys after thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 1:26; Psalms 144:14; Genesis 42:26"> Wilt thou rely upon him because his strength is great, And commit to him thy labor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15; Proverbs 3:16; Amos 2:13"> Wilt thou trust him to bring home thy grain, And gather in thy harvest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Zechariah 5:9; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> The wing of the ostrich moveth joyfully; But is it with loving pinion and feathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Nay, she layeth her eggs on the ground; She warmeth them in the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And forgetteth that the foot may crush them, And that the wild beast may break them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 4:3; Deuteronomy 28:56–57; 1 Kings 3:26–27"> She is cruel to her young, as if they were not hers; Her labor is in vain, yet she feareth not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Job 17:4; Isaiah 57:17"> Because God hath denied her wisdom, And hath not given her understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 41:29; Job 39:22; Job 5:22"> Yet when she lasheth herself up on high, She laugheth at the horse and his rider.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 147:10; Psalms 93:1; Psalms 104:1"> Hast thou given the horse strength? Hast thou clothed his neck with his trembling mane?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Job 41:20–21; Joel 2:5"> Hast thou taught him to bound like the locust? How majestic his snorting! how terrible!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:6; Psalms 19:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–10"> He paweth in the valley; he exulteth in his strength, And rusheth into the midst of arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 41:33; Job 39:16; Job 39:18"> He laugheth at fear; he trembleth not, And turneth not back from the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 41:26–29"> Against him rattle the quiver, The flaming spear, and the lance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Luke 24:41; Job 37:20"> With rage and fury he devoureth the ground; He will not believe that the trumpet soundeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 70:3; Ezekiel 26:2; Ezekiel 36:2"> At every blast of the trumpet, he saith, Aha! And snuffeth the battle afar off,—The thunder of the captains, and the war-shout.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:15; Song of Solomon 2:12; Leviticus 16:11"> Is it by thy wisdom that the hawk flieth, And spreadeth his wings toward the south?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Obadiah 1:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Exodus 19:4"> Doth the eagle soar at thy command, And build his nest on high?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:4"> He dwelleth and lodgeth upon the rock, Upon the peak of the rock, and the stronghold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 9:26"> From thence he spieth out prey; His eyes discern it from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Luke 17:37; Ezekiel 39:17–19"> His young ones suck up blood; And where the slain are, there is he.</span>
