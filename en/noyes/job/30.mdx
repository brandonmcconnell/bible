<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 12:4; Psalms 35:15–16; Psalms 69:12"> But now they that are younger than I hold me in derision, Whose fathers I would have disdained to set with the dogs of my flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Of what use to me would be even the strength of their hands, To whom old age is lost?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 24:5; Hebrews 11:38; Job 24:13–16"> By want and hunger they are famished; They gnaw the dry desert, The darkness of desolate wastes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:16; 2 Kings 4:38–39; Amos 7:14"> They gather purslain among the bushes, And the root of the broom is their bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:32–33; Psalms 109:10"> They are driven from the society of men; There is a cry after them as after a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1–2; Judges 6:2; Isaiah 2:19"> They dwell in gloomy valleys, In caves of the earth and in rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 6:5; Job 11:12; Genesis 16:12"> They bray among the bushes; Under the brambles are they stretched out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:22; Job 40:4; Isaiah 32:6"> An impious and low-born race, They are beaten out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Lamentations 3:63; Job 17:6; Lamentations 3:14"> And now I am become their song; Yea, I am their by-word!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Isaiah 50:6; Numbers 12:14"> They abhor me, they stand aloof from me; They forbear not to spit before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:21; Ruth 1:21; Job 12:18"> Yea, they let loose the reins, and humble me; They cast off the bridle before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 19:12; Isaiah 3:5; Job 19:18"> On my right hand riseth up the brood; They thrust away my feet; They cast up against me their destructive ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 69:26"> They break up my path; They hasten my fall,—They who have no helper!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Psalms 18:4; Psalms 69:14–15"> They come upon me as through a wide breach; Through the ruins they rush in upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 13:3; Job 6:4; Isaiah 44:22"> Terrors are turned against me; They pursue my prosperity like the wind, And my welfare passeth away like a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 22:14; Isaiah 53:12"> And now my soul poureth itself out upon me; Days of affliction have taken hold of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 7:4; Job 33:19–21; Isaiah 38:13"> By night my bones are pierced; they are torn from me, And my gnawers take no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 7:5; Job 19:20; Job 2:7"> Through the violence of my disease is my garment changed; It bindeth me about like the collar of my tunic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:31; Job 42:6; Genesis 18:27"> He hath cast me into the mire, And I am become like dust and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 19:7; Psalms 80:4–5; Psalms 22:2"> I call upon Thee, but thou dost not hear me; I stand up before thee, but thou regardest me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 10:14–17; Job 16:9–14; Job 19:6–9"> Thou art become cruel to me; With thy strong hand dost thou lie in wait for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 27:21; Psalms 1:4; Hosea 4:19"> Thou liftest me up, and causest me to ride upon the wind; Thou meltest me away in the storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5–7; Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> I know that thou wilt bring me to death, To the place of assembly for all the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Matthew 27:39–44; Job 19:7"> When He stretcheth out his hand, prayer availeth nothing; When He bringeth destruction, vain is the cry for help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 35:13–14; Jeremiah 13:17"> Did not I weep for him that was in trouble? Was not my soul grieved for the poor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15; Job 3:25–26; Jeremiah 14:19"> But when I looked for good, then evil came; When I looked for light, then came darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11; Jeremiah 31:20; Jeremiah 4:19"> My bowels boil, and have no rest; Days of anguish have come upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 42:9; Psalms 38:6; Psalms 43:2"> I am black, but not by the sun; I stand up, and utter my cries in the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Psalms 102:6; Psalms 44:19"> I am become a brother to jackals, And a companion to ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Psalms 102:3; Psalms 119:83"> My skin is black, and falleth from me, And my bones burn with heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 5:15; Isaiah 21:4; Isaiah 22:12"> My harp also is turned to mourning, And my pipe to notes of grief.</span>
