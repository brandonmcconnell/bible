<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Elihu also proceeded, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 21:3; 2 Corinthians 5:20; Job 13:7–8"> Bear with me a little while, that I may show thee! For I have yet words in behalf of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> I will bring my knowledge from afar, And assert the justice of my Maker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:7–8; Job 37:16; Psalms 49:3"> Truly my words shall not be false: A man of sound knowledge is before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 22:24; Psalms 138:6; Jeremiah 10:12"> Behold, God is great, but despiseth not any; Great is he in strength of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 72:12–14; Psalms 140:12; 2 Peter 2:9"> He suffereth not the wicked to prosper, But rendereth justice to the oppressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Psalms 33:18; 1 Samuel 2:8"> He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous; But establisheth them for ever with kings on the throne, That they may be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 107:10; Lamentations 3:9"> And if they be bound in fetters, And holden in the cords of affliction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:71; Job 15:25; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Then showeth he them their deeds, And how they have set him at defiance by their transgressions;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Job 36:15; Isaiah 55:6–7"> He also openeth their ears to admonition, And commandeth them to return from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Isaiah 1:19; Jeremiah 26:13"> If they obey and serve him, They spend their days in prosperity, And their years in pleasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:21; Romans 2:8–9; Isaiah 3:11"> But if they obey not, they perish by the sword; They die in their own folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Job 36:8; Job 27:8–10"> The corrupt in heart treasure up wrath; They cry not to God, when he bindeth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:16; Job 15:32; Deuteronomy 23:17"> They die in their youth; They close their lives with the unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 36:10; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> But he delivereth the poor in their distress; He openeth their ears in affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Psalms 118:5; Psalms 18:19"> He will bring thee also from the jaws of distress To a broad place, where is no straitness; And the provision of thy table shall be full of fatness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Romans 1:32; Job 34:36"> But if thou art full of the judgment of the wicked, Judgment and justice shall take hold of thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:24; Ephesians 5:6; Romans 1:18"> For if wrath be with him, beware lest he take thee away by his stroke, So that a great ransom shall not save thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 11:21; James 5:3"> Will he esteem thy riches? No! neither thy gold, nor all the abundance of thy wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Job 34:20; Exodus 12:29"> Long not thou for that night To which nations are taken away from their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:25; Psalms 66:18; 1 Peter 3:17"> Take heed, turn not thine eyes to iniquity! For this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Luke 1:52; Romans 13:1"> Behold, God is exalted in his power: Who is a teacher like him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14; Job 8:3"> Who hath prescribed to him his way? Or who can say to him, “Thou hast done wrong”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 15:3; Psalms 111:2–4; Psalms 72:18"> Forget not to magnify his work, Which men celebrate with songs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> All mankind gaze upon it; Mortals behold it from afar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 145:3; Job 11:7–9"> Behold, God is great; we cannot know him, Nor search out the number of his years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 147:8; Psalms 148:8; Psalms 65:9–13"> Lo! he draweth up the drops of water, Which distil rain from his vapor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 7:11–12; Job 37:11–13"> The clouds pour it down, And drop it upon man in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:16; Job 37:2–5; Psalms 18:13"> Who can understand the spreading of his clouds, And the rattling of his pavilion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 38:8–11; Job 38:34–35; Genesis 1:9"> Behold, he spreadeth around himself his light, And he clotheth himself with the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 136:25; Acts 14:17; Job 37:13"> By these he punisheth nations, And by these he giveth food in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 147:8–9; Psalms 148:8"> His hands he covereth with lightning; He giveth it commandment against an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 37:2; Job 36:29; 2 Samuel 22:14"> His thunder maketh him known; Yea, to the herds, as he ascendeth on high.</span>
