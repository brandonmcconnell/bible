<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> But Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Job 34:2; Job 15:11"> Hear attentively my words, And let this be your consolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 17:2; Job 16:10; Job 16:20"> Bear with me, that I may speak; And after I have spoken, mock on!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 102:1; Psalms 42:11; Psalms 142:2–3"> Is my complaint concerning man? Why then should I not be angry?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 40:4; Judges 18:19"> Look upon me, and be astonished, And lay your hand upon your mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Psalms 119:120; Psalms 88:15"> When I think of it, I am confounded; Trembling taketh hold of my flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 73:3–12; Job 12:6; Jeremiah 12:1–3"> Why is it that the wicked live, Grow old, yea, become mighty in substance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Job 5:3–4; Job 20:10"> Their children are established in their sight with them, And their offspring before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 9:34; Psalms 73:5; Psalms 73:19"> Their houses are in peace, without fear, And the rod of God cometh not upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Luke 16:19; Deuteronomy 7:13–14"> Their bull gendereth, and faileth not; Their cow calveth, and casteth not her calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 107:41; Psalms 127:3–5"> They send forth their little ones like a flock, And their children dance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:4–6; Isaiah 5:12; Genesis 4:21"> They sing to the timbrel and harp, And rejoice at the sound of the pipe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 36:11; Luke 12:19–20; Psalms 73:4"> They spend their days in prosperity, And in a moment go down to the under-world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:17; Romans 8:7; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> And yet they say unto God, “Depart from us! We desire not the knowledge of thy ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Job 34:9; Malachi 1:13–14"> Who is the Almighty, that we should serve him? And what will it profit us, if we pray to him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Job 22:18; Genesis 49:6"> &#91;&#91;Ye say,&#93;&#93; “Lo! their prosperity is not secure in their hands! Far from me be the conduct of the wicked!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 18:5–6; Proverbs 20:20; Proverbs 13:9"> How often is it, that the lamp of the wicked is put out? And that destruction cometh upon them, And that He dispenseth to them tribulations in his anger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Psalms 83:13; Job 13:25"> How often are they as stubble before the wind, Or as chaff, which the whirlwind carrieth away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Ezekiel 18:19–20; Ezekiel 18:2"> “But” &#91;&#91;say ye&#93;&#93; “God layeth up his iniquity for his children.” Let him requite the offender, and let him feel it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Isaiah 51:17; Psalms 75:8"> Let his own eyes see his destruction, And let him drink of the wrath of the Almighty!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 14:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Psalms 55:23"> For what concern hath he for his household after him, When the number of his own months is completed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:34; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Isaiah 40:13–14"> Who then shall impart knowledge to God,—To him that judgeth the highest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 20:22–23; Luke 12:19–21; Psalms 73:4–5"> One dieth in the fulness of his prosperity, Being wholly at ease and quiet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:8; Psalms 17:10; Job 15:27"> His sides are full of fat, And his bones moist with marrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Job 7:11; Proverbs 14:10"> Another dieth in bitterness of soul, And hath not tasted pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2; Isaiah 14:11; Job 20:11"> Alike they lie down in the dust, And the worms cover them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 5:22; Psalms 119:86; Psalms 59:4"> Behold, I know your thoughts, And the devices by which ye wrong me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:22; Job 20:7; Habakkuk 2:9–11"> For ye say, “Where is the house of the oppressor, And where the dwelling-places of the wicked?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 129:8"> Have ye never inquired of travellers, And do ye not know their tokens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; 2 Peter 2:9–17; 2 Peter 3:7"> That the wicked is spared in the day of destruction, And that he is borne to his grave in the day of wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:11; Deuteronomy 7:10; Romans 12:19"> Who will charge him with his conduct to his face, And who will requite him for the evil he hath done?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 16:22; Psalms 49:14; Ezekiel 32:21–32"> Even this man is borne with honor to the grave; Yea, he watcheth over his tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 24:24; Job 30:23"> Sweet to him are the sods of the valley: And all men move after him, As multitudes without number before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 16:2; Job 32:3; Job 13:4"> Why then do ye offer your vain consolations? Your answers continue false.</span>
