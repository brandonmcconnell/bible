<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 29:1; Numbers 24:15; Numbers 23:7"> Moreover Job continued his discourse, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 34:5; Numbers 14:21; 2 Kings 4:27"> As God liveth, who hath rejected my cause, And the Almighty, who hath afflicted my soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Job 33:4; Job 32:8"> As long as my breath is in me, And the spirit of God is in my nostrils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:7; 2 Corinthians 11:10; John 8:55"> Never shall my lips speak falsehood, Nor my tongue utter deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:9; Job 13:15; Job 42:7"> God forbid that I should acknowledge you to be just: To my last breath will I assert my integrity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:3; 1 John 3:20–21; Acts 24:16"> I will hold fast my innocence, and not let it go; My heart reproacheth me for no part of my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 4:19; 1 Samuel 25:26; 2 Samuel 18:32"> May mine enemy be as the wicked, And he that riseth up against me as the unrighteous!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; Job 8:13; Job 11:20"> For what is the hope of the wicked, when God cutteth off his web, And taketh away his life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:28; Jeremiah 14:12"> Will he listen to his cry, When trouble cometh upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 22:26–27; Psalms 37:4; Habakkuk 3:18"> Can he delight himself in the Almighty, And call at all times upon God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 71:17; Isaiah 8:11; Job 4:3–4"> I will teach you concerning the hand of God; That which is with the Almighty I will not conceal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 21:3; Job 16:3; Job 19:2–3"> Behold, ye yourselves have all seen it; Why then do ye cherish such vain thoughts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:19–29; Job 15:20–35; Isaiah 3:11"> This is the portion of the wicked man from God,—The inheritance which oppressors receive from the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:41; Job 20:10; Luke 23:29"> If his children be multiplied, it is for the sword; And his offspring shall not be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremiah 22:18; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> Those of them that escape shall be buried by Death, And their widows shall not bewail them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 9:3; Matthew 6:19; Habakkuk 2:6"> Though he heap up silver as dust, And procure raiment as clay,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26; Proverbs 28:8"> He may procure, but the righteous shall wear it, And the innocent shall share the silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 1:8; Job 8:14–15; Isaiah 51:8"> He buildeth his house like the moth, Or like the shed which the watchman maketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 24:24; Psalms 58:9; Psalms 73:19–20"> The rich man lieth down, and is not buried; In the twinkling of an eye he is no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 15:21; Job 18:11; Job 21:18"> Terrors pursue him like a flood; A tempest stealeth him away in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Psalms 83:15; Psalms 58:9"> The east wind carrieth him away, and he perisheth; Yea, it sweepeth him away from his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–5; Ezekiel 24:14; Job 11:20"> God sendeth his arrows at him, and doth not spare; He would fain escape from His hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Lamentations 2:15; 1 Kings 9:8; Zephaniah 2:15"> Men clap their hands at him, And hiss him away from his place.</span>
