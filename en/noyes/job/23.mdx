<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Job answered and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 10:1; Psalms 32:4; Job 7:11"> Still is my complaint bitter; But my wound is deeper than my groaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 16:21; Jeremiah 14:7; Job 13:3"> O that I knew where I might find him! That I might go before his throne!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 13:18; Daniel 9:18–19; Exodus 32:12–13"> I would order my cause before him, And fill my mouth with arguments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 13:22–23; Job 10:2; 1 Corinthians 4:3–4"> I should know what he would answer me, And understand what he would say to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:33–34; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Would he contend with me with his mighty power? No! he would have regard to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:3; Romans 8:1; Isaiah 1:18"> Then would an upright man contend with him, And I should be fully acquitted by my judge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 10:1; Job 9:11; Job 35:14"> But, behold, I go eastward, and he is not there; And westward, but I cannot perceive him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Isaiah 8:17"> To the north, where he worketh, but I cannot behold him; He hideth himself on the south, and I cannot see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; James 1:2–4; 1 Peter 1:7"> But he knoweth the way which is in my heart; When he trieth me, I shall come forth as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:18; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Job 17:9"> My feet have trodden in his steps; His way I have kept, and have not turned aside from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Psalms 119:103; Psalms 119:11"> I have not neglected the precepts of his lips; Above my own law have I esteemed the words of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 46:10"> But he is of one mind, and who can turn him? And what he desireth, that he doeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> He performeth that which is appointed for me; And many such things are in his mind!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; Psalms 77:3; Job 31:23"> Therefore I am in terror on account of him; When I consider, I am afraid of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 22:14; Job 27:2; Psalms 88:16"> For God maketh my heart faint; Yea, the Almighty terrifieth me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 19:8; Isaiah 57:1; 2 Kings 22:20"> Because I was not taken away before darkness came, And he hath not hidden darkness from mine eyes.</span>
