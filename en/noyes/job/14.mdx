<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:23; Job 25:4"> Man, that is born of woman, Is of few days, and full of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; 1 Peter 1:24; Job 8:9"> He cometh forth as a flower, and is cut down; He fleeth also as a shadow, and continueth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 144:3; Psalms 143:2; Psalms 8:4"> And dost thou fix thine eyes upon such a one? And dost thou bring me into judgment with thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 3:6; Job 15:14; Ephesians 2:3"> Who can produce a clean thing from an unclean? Not one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Acts 17:26; Hebrews 9:27"> Seeing that his days are determined, And the number of his months, with thee, And that thou hast appointed him bounds which he cannot pass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 7:19; Psalms 39:13; Job 7:1–2"> O turn thine eyes from him, and let him rest, That he may enjoy, as a hireling, his day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Isaiah 11:1; Job 14:14"> For there is hope for a tree, If it be cut down, that it will sprout again, And that its tender branches will not fail;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36; Isaiah 26:19; John 12:24"> Though its root may have grown old in the earth, And though its trunk be dead upon the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Ezekiel 17:3–10; Ezekiel 17:22–24"> Through the scent of water it will bud, And put forth boughs, like a young plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 7:7–10; Job 14:12"> But man dieth, and he is gone! Man expireth, and where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 19:5; Jeremiah 15:18; Job 6:15–18"> The waters fail from the lake; And the stream wasteth and drieth up;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Job 3:13; Ecclesiastes 3:19–21"> So man lieth down, and riseth not; Till the heavens be no more, he shall not awake, Nor be roused from his sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Isaiah 57:1–2; Isaiah 26:20–21"> O that thou wouldst hide me in the under-world! That thou wouldst conceal me till thy wrath be past! That thou wouldst appoint me a time, and then remember me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Job 7:1; Acts 26:8"> If a man die, can he live again? All the days of my war-service would I wait, Till my change should come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 13:22; Psalms 138:8; Job 10:8"> Thou wilt call, and I will answer thee; Thou wilt have compassion upon the work of thy hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 10:6; Job 31:4; Psalms 139:1–4"> But now thou numberest my steps; Thou watchest over my sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34; Hosea 13:12; Job 21:19"> My transgression is sealed up in a bag; Yea, thou addest unto my iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Job 18:4; Jeremiah 4:24"> As the mountain falling cometh to nought, And the rock is removed from its place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 7:6; Job 27:8; Job 19:10"> As the waters wear away the stones, And the floods wash away the dust of the earth, So thou destroyest the hope of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:14; Job 2:12; Ecclesiastes 8:8"> Thou prevailest against him continually, and he perisheth; Thou changest his countenance, and sendest him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:18–19; Isaiah 63:16"> His sons come to honor, but he knoweth it not; Or they are brought low, but he perceiveth it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 19:26; Job 19:20; Luke 16:23–24"> But his flesh shall have pain for itself alone; For itself alone shall his soul mourn.</span>
