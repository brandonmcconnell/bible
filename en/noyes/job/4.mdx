<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 8:1; Job 22:1; Job 15:1"> Then spake Eliphaz the Temanite, and said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 32:18–20; Acts 4:20; Jeremiah 6:11"> If one attempt a word with thee, wilt thou be offended? But who can refrain from speaking?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:12; Proverbs 10:21"> Behold, thou hast admonished many; Thou hast strengthened feeble hands;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Psalms 145:14; Hebrews 12:12"> Thy words have upheld him that was falling, And thou hast given strength to feeble knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 19:21; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Job 2:5"> But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; It toucheth thee, and thou art confounded!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:26; Proverbs 14:26; Job 1:1"> Is not thy fear of God thy hope, And the uprightness of thy ways thy confidence?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:25; 2 Peter 2:9; Job 36:7"> Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished being innocent? Or where have the righteous been cut off?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 22:8; Hosea 8:7"> According to what I have seen, they who plough iniquity, And sow mischief, reap the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> By the blast of God they perish, And by the breath of his nostrils they are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 58:6; Proverbs 30:14; Psalms 3:7"> The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, And the teeth of the young lions are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Job 8:3–4; Job 1:19"> The fierce lion perisheth for lack of prey, And the whelps of the lioness are scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 26:14; Psalms 62:11; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> A word was once secretly brought to me, And mine ear caught a whisper thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 33:14–16; Genesis 2:21; Genesis 31:24"> Amid thoughts from visions of the night, When deep sleep falleth upon men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Job 33:19; Luke 1:12"> A fear and a horror came upon me, Which made all my bones to shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Daniel 5:6; Psalms 104:4"> Then a spirit passed before my face, The hair of my flesh rose on end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 19:12"> It stood still, but its form I could not discern; An image was before mine eyes; There was silence, and I heard a voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 9:2; Job 25:4; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> “Shall mortal man be more just than God? Shall man be more pure than his Maker?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 25:5–6; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> Behold, he putteth no trust in his ministering spirits, And his angels he chargeth with frailty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 10:9; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> What then are they who dwell in houses of clay, Whose foundation is in the dust, Who crumble to pieces, as if moth-eaten!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 20:7; Job 14:14; Job 16:22"> Between morning and evening are they destroyed; They perish for ever, and none regardeth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 36:12; Psalms 39:11; Job 8:22"> The excellency that is in them is torn away; They die before they have become wise.”</span>
