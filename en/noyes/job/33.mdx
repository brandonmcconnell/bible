<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 13:6; Mark 4:9; Psalms 49:1–3"> Hear, therefore, my discourse, I pray thee, O Job! And attend unto all my words!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:1; Matthew 5:2; Psalms 78:2"> Behold, I am opening my mouth; My tongue is now speaking in my palate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 27:4; Proverbs 20:15; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–4"> My words shall be in the uprightness of my heart; My lips shall utter knowledge purely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:8; Genesis 2:7; Job 27:3"> The spirit of God made me, And the breath of the Almighty gave me life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 33:32–33; Job 32:1; Psalms 50:21"> If thou art able, answer me; Set thyself in array against me; stand up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 4:19; Job 10:9; Job 13:12"> Behold, I, like thee, am a creature of God; I also was formed of clay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 13:21; Job 9:34; Psalms 32:4"> Behold, my terror cannot dismay thee, Nor can my greatness be heavy upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Jeremiah 29:23"> Surely thou hast said in my hearing, I have heard the sound of thy words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 10:7; Job 16:17; Job 9:21"> “I am pure, and without transgression; I am clean, and there is no iniquity in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 19:11; Job 13:24–25; Job 9:30–31"> Behold, He seeketh causes of hostility against me; He regardeth me as his enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 31:4; Daniel 4:35"> He putteth my feet in the stocks; He watcheth all my paths.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:25; Job 36:5; Job 1:22"> Behold, in this thou art not right; I will answer thee; For God is greater than man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 40:2; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 11:34"> Why dost thou contend with Him? For he giveth no account of any of his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24; 2 Chronicles 33:10; Job 33:29"> For God speaketh once, Yea, twice, when man regardeth it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; Job 4:13; Genesis 20:3"> In a dream, in a vision of the night, When deep sleep falleth upon men, In slumber upon the bed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 36:10; Job 36:15; Psalms 40:6"> Then openeth he the ears of men, And sealeth up for them admonition;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; James 4:10; Genesis 20:6"> That he may turn man from his purpose, And hide pride from man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 33:30; Job 33:28; 2 Peter 3:9"> Thus he saveth him from the pit, Yea, his life from perishing by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 30:17; Job 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, And with a continual agitation of his bones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 107:17–18; Job 3:24; Genesis 3:6"> So that his mouth abhorreth bread, And his taste the choicest food;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 19:20; Job 13:28; Proverbs 5:11"> His flesh is consumed, that it cannot be seen, And his bones, that were invisible, are naked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:28; 2 Samuel 24:16; Job 17:13–16"> Yea, his soul draweth near to the pit, And his life to the destroyers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> But if there be with him a messenger, An interpreter, one of a thousand, Who may show unto man his duty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 49:7–8; Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> Then will God be gracious to him, and say, “Save him from going down to the pit: I have found a ransom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 103:5; 2 Kings 5:14; Joshua 14:10–11"> His flesh shall became fresher than a child's; He shall return to the days of his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 30:5; Acts 9:11"> He shall pray to God, and he will be favorable to him, And permit him to see his face with joy, And restore unto man his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:13; Psalms 14:2"> He shall sing among men, and say, “I sinned; I acted perversely; Yet hath he not requited me for it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:24; Job 3:16; Job 33:22"> He hath delivered me from going down to the pit, And my life beholdeth the light.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 1:11; 1 Corinthians 12:6; Philippians 2:13"> Lo! all these things doeth God Time after time with man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 56:13; Isaiah 38:17; Job 33:28"> That he may bring him back from the pit, That he may enjoy the light of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 18:2; Job 13:6; Job 21:2"> Mark well, O Job! hearken to me! Keep silence, and I will speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 21:27; Job 15:4–5; Job 22:5–9"> Yet if thou hast any thing to say, answer me! Speak! for I desire to pronounce thee innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 5:1–2; Psalms 49:3"> But if not, do thou listen to me! Keep silence, and I will teach thee wisdom!</span>
