<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Romans 15:13; John 16:33"> Therefore being accepted as righteous through faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 12:12"> through whom also we have had admission into this grace in which we stand, and rejoice in the hope of the glory which God will confer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:2–3; James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> And not only so, but we rejoice in afflictions also, knowing that affliction produceth endurance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> and endurance proof &#91;&#91;of faith&#93;&#93;, and proof &#91;&#91;of faith giveth&#93;&#93; hope;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; Ephesians 3:16–19; Philippians 1:20"> and hope will not disappoint us; because the love of God hath been shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Spirit which hath been given to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–5; Romans 5:8; Colossians 2:13"> For while we were yet without strength, in due season Christ died for the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 15:13; 1 John 3:16; Romans 16:4"> Now hardly for a righteous man will one die; perhaps, however, for a benefactor one might even dare to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:13; John 3:16; Romans 5:6"> But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 8:1; Ephesians 2:13"> Much more then, being now accepted as righteous through his blood, we shall be saved through him from the &#91;&#91;coming&#93;&#93; wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:32"> For if while enemies we were reconciled to God through the death of his Son, much more having been reconciled shall we be saved by his life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–19; Romans 5:10; Isaiah 61:10"> and not this only, but also having joy in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received the reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; Romans 5:19; Romans 6:23"> So then as through one man sin entered into the world, and through sin death, and thus &#91;&#91;death&#93;&#93; came through unto all men, because all sinned—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56; 1 John 3:4"> (for all the time before the Law sin was in the world; but sin is not set to one's account when there is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:45; Romans 5:17; Hebrews 9:27"> Yet death reigned from Adam to Moses, even over those who had not sinned in the manner in which Adam transgressed; who is a type of him who was to come.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:12"> But the free gift was not as the transgression. For if through the offence of the one the many died, much more hath the grace of God, and the gift which is by the grace of the one man Jesus Christ, abounded to the many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:38–39; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 44:22"> And the free gift is not like what happened through one man who sinned. For sentence of condemnation followed one offence; but the free gift is a justification after many offences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:21–22; 1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 5:12"> For if by one trespass death reigned through the one man, much more will they who receive the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man Jesus Christ.)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12; Romans 5:15"> As then through one trespass all men have come under condemnation, so through one act of righteousness all obtain the gift of righteousness unto life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 5:18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Philippians 2:8"> For as through the disobedience of the one man the many were made sinners, so through the obedience of the one man will the many be made righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Titus 3:3–7; Romans 6:14"> Moreover the law came in in addition, that the trespass might abound; but where sin abounded, grace abounded much more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 2:11; Romans 5:14; Romans 8:10"> that as sin reigned in death, so grace might reign through righteousness to everlasting life, through Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
