<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> I say then, Hath God cast off his people? Far be it I For I myself am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God hath not cast off his people, which he foreknew. Do ye not know what the Scripture saith in the passage concerning Elijah? how he pleadeth to God against Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed thy prophets, they have dug down thine altars; and I am left alone, and they are seeking my life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> But what saith the answer of God to him? “I have reserved to myself seven thousand men, who have not bowed the knee to Baal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> In the same way then at this present time also there is a remnant, according to the election of grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> And if it is by grace, it is no longer on account of works; otherwise grace ceaseth to be grace; but if it is of works, there is then no grace; otherwise work ceaseth to be work. How is it then?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What Israel seeketh after, that Israel did not obtain; but the elect obtained it, and the rest were hardened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> as it is written: “God gave them a spirit of slumber, eyes that were not to see, and ears that were not to hear, unto this day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> And David saith, “Let their table become a snare, and a trap, and a stumbling-block, and a recompense to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> let their eyes be darkened, that they may not see; and bow down their back alway.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I say then, Did they stumble in order to fall? God forbid! But by their offence salvation is come to the gentiles to excite them to emulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> But if their offence is the riches of the world, and their loss the riches of the gentiles, how much more will their fullness be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> For I am speaking to you gentiles; inasmuch as I am the apostle of the gentiles, I magnify my office,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> that I may, if possible, excite to emulation those who are my flesh, and may save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> For if the rejection of them is the reconciliation of the world, what will the reception of them be, but life from the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> And if the first portion of the dough is holy, so also will be the lump; and if the root is holy, so will be the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> And if some of the branches have been broken off, and thou, a wild olive, hast been grafted in among them, and become a partaker with them of the root and fatness of the olive-tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> boast not over the branches; for if thou boast, thou dost not bear the root, but the root thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> Thou wilt say then, The branches were broken off, that I might be grafted in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> Be it so. It was for their unbelief that they were broken off, and thou standest through thy faith; be not high-minded, but fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> For if God spared the natural branches, take care lest he spare not thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> Behold then the goodness and the severity of God; toward those who fell, severity; but toward thee God's goodness, if thou continue in his goodness; otherwise thou also wilt be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And they also, if they do not continue in their unbelief, will be grafted in; for God is able to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> For if thou hast been cut off from an olive-tree wild by nature, and hast against thy nature been ingrafted into a good olive-tree, how much more shall these, the natural branches, be ingrafted into their own olive-stock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> For I would not have you ignorant, brethren, of this mystery, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits, that blindness hath to some extent come upon Israel, until the fullness of the gentiles shall have come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> And thus will all Israel be saved; as it is written, “There shall come out of Zion the Deliverer; he shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> And this will be my covenant with them, when I shall have taken away their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> In regard to the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes; but in regard to God's choice, they are beloved for the fathers' sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> For in respect to his gifts and his calling, there is no change of purpose with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> For as ye in times past were disobedient to God, but have now obtained mercy through their disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so they too have now become disobedient, that they also may obtain mercy through the mercy shown to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> For God delivered up all to disobedience, that he might have mercy upon all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalms 92:5"> O the depth of the riches, and of the wisdom, and of the knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his purposes, and his ways past finding out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> For “who hath known the mind of the Lord? or who hath been made his counselor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> or “who first gave to him, and shall receive a return?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> For from him, and through him, and to him are all things. To him be the glory for ever! Amen.</span>
