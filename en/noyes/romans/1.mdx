<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 15:16; 1 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, a servant of Christ Jesus, a called apostle, set apart to preach the gospel of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:2; Luke 1:70; Romans 3:21"> which he had promised before by his prophets in the Holy Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Galatians 4:4; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> the gospel concerning his Son, who was born of the seed of David as to the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 5:5–6; Ephesians 1:19–23"> and shown with power to be the Son of God as to his spirit of holiness, by the resurrection of the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 16:26; Acts 6:7"> through whom we received grace and the office of an apostle in behalf of his name, in order to produce obedience to the faith among all nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 17:14; 2 Peter 1:10"> among whom are ye also, the called of Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Jude 1:1–2; Ephesians 1:2"> to all the beloved of God at Rome, called, holy: Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 16:19; 1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> In the first place, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, that your faith is spoken of throughout the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 2 Timothy 1:3; Ephesians 1:16–19"> For God is my witness, whom I serve with my spirit in the gospel of his Son, how constantly I make mention of you, always in my prayers</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:30–32; Acts 18:21; Philemon 1:22"> supplicating that, if it be possible, I may at last through the will of God be favored with an opportunity of coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Romans 16:25; Romans 15:29"> For I long to see you, that I may impart to you some spiritual gift, which may be for your confirmation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:7–10; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> that is, that I may be edified among you, and you also, through each other's faith, both yours and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 19:21; Philippians 4:17; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that I often purposed to come to you, though I have been hindered hitherto, that I might have some fruit of my labors among you also, as among the other gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–23; Acts 9:15; Colossians 3:11"> I am debtor both to Greeks and barbarians, both to the wise and the unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> So, according to my ability, I am ready to preach the gospel to you also in Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 8:38; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 4:16"> For I am not ashamed of the gospel; for to every believer, to the Jew first and also to the Greek, it is the power of God unto salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Galatians 3:11; Hebrews 10:38"> For therein is revealed the righteousness which is of God from faith to faith; as it is written, “But the righteous shall live by faith.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; Romans 1:32"> For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all impiety and unrighteousness of men, who keep down the truth in unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:16–17; Psalms 19:1–6; Romans 1:20"> Because that which may be known of God is manifest within them; for God made it manifest to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:19; Isaiah 40:26; Psalms 19:1–6"> For, ever since the creation of the world, his invisible attributes, even his eternal power and divinity, being perceived from his works, are clearly seen, so that they might be without excuse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17–18; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:5"> Because though they knew God, they did not glorify him as God, nor were they thankful to him; but became perverse in their reasonings, and their senseless minds were darkened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:19–21; Romans 11:25; Proverbs 26:12"> professing to be wise, they became fools,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11; Acts 17:29"> and for the glory of the incorruptible God they substituted images of corruptible man, and of birds and four-footed beasts and creeping things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Acts 7:42; Acts 14:16"> Wherefore God also gave them over in the lusts of their hearts to impurity, to debase their bodies with one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 13:25; 1 John 2:15–16"> because they changed the true God for false gods, and adored and worshipped created things rather than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Romans 1:24"> For this cause God gave them up to vile passions. For even their women indulged in unnatural lust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Leviticus 18:22; Romans 1:23–24"> and in like manner the men also, neglecting the natural use of the female, burned with lust for one another, men with men practising that which is shameful, and receiving in themselves the due recompense of their error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Romans 1:21; Titus 1:16"> And as they did not choose to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them up to a reprobate mind, to do things which are shameful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 41:7"> being filled with all unrighteousness, malice, covetousness, wickedness; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, malignity; backbiters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2; Jude 1:16; 2 Peter 2:18"> slanderers, hated of God, insolent, proud, boasters, inventors of mischief, disobedient to parents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:3; Proverbs 18:2; Romans 3:11"> senseless, faithless, without natural affection, without pity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 2:1–5; Romans 1:21; Romans 6:21"> who, although knowing the ordinance of God, that they who practise such things deserve death, not only do them themselves, but approve of those who do them.</span>
