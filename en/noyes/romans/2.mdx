<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:3; John 8:7–9"> Wherefore thou art without excuse, O man that judgest, whoever thou art. For wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest doest the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 9:7–8; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 145:17"> But we know that the judgment of God is according to truth against those who practise such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:5; Luke 12:14; Romans 1:32–1"> And dost thou suppose, O man, who art judging those who do such things, and art thyself doing the same, that thou wilt escape the judgment of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Isaiah 30:18; Exodus 34:6"> Or dost thou despise the riches of his goodness and forbearance and longsuffering, not knowing that the goodness of God is leading thee to repentance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 1:18; Romans 2:2–3"> But according to thy hardness and impenitent heart, thou art treasuring up for thyself wrath against the day of wrath and of the manifestation of the righteous judgment of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; Jeremiah 32:19; Matthew 16:27"> who will render to every one according to his works;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7–8; Luke 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:10"> everlasting life to those who by patient continuance in well-doing seek for glory, and honor, and incorruption;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:18–21; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; 1 Timothy 6:3–4"> but to those who are contentious, and disobedient to the truth; but obey unrighteousness, there will be wrath and indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; Ezekiel 18:4; Romans 1:16"> Tribulation and distress will be upon every soul of man whose works are evil, of the Jew first, and also of the Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 26:12; Numbers 6:26"> but glory, honor, and peace, to every one whoso works are good, to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> For there is no respect of persons with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:21; Galatians 3:10; Galatians 2:16–19"> For as many as have sinned without a law, will also perish without a law; and as many as have sinned under a law, will be judged by a law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:22–25; 1 John 3:7; James 2:21–25"> for it is not the hearers of a law who are righteous before God, but the doers of a law will be accounted righteous;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 1:19–20; Romans 2:27"> for when the gentiles, who have no law, do by nature what is required by the Law, these, having no law, are a law to themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Romans 1:18–19"> since they show that what the Law requireth is written in their hearts, their conscience bearing witness, and their thoughts in turn accusing or defending them;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Acts 17:31"> in the day when God shall judge the secrets of men by Jesus Christ, according to the gospel which I have preached.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 2:23; Micah 3:11; Isaiah 48:1–2"> But if thou art called a Jew, and restest on the Law, and makest thy boast of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Deuteronomy 4:8; James 4:17"> and knowest his will, and approvest the things that are more excellent, being instructed out of the Law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 3:17–18; Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 49:6"> and art confident that thou thyself art a guide of the blind, a light to those who are in darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 1:13"> an instructor of those who lack wisdom, a teacher of babes, having the form of knowledge and of the truth in the Law,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:27; Matthew 23:3–28; Luke 12:47"> dost thou then who teachest another, not teach thyself? Thou who proclaimest that others should not steal, dost thou steal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 3:8; Matthew 12:39; Mark 11:17"> Dost thou who forbiddest to commit adultery, thyself commit adultery? Thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou rob temples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 4:16–17; Romans 2:17; Romans 3:2"> Dost thou who boastest of the Law, dishonor God by breaking the Law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:20–23; Isaiah 52:5; 2 Peter 2:2"> For, as it is written, “the name of God is on your account blasphemed among the gentiles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Galatians 5:3–6; Acts 7:51"> For circumcision is indeed a benefit to thee, if thou keep the Law; but if thou art a breaker of the Law, thy circumcision hath become uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 7:18–19; Romans 8:4"> If then he who is uncircumcised keep the precepts of the Law, shall not he though uncircumcised be regarded as circumcised?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; Romans 2:29; 2 Corinthians 3:6"> Yea, those who are by nature uncircumcised, if they perform the law, will judge thee, who having a written Law and circumcision, art a breaker of the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Matthew 3:9; Romans 9:6–8"> For he is not a Jew, who is one outwardly, nor is that circumcision, which is outward, in the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Philippians 3:3; Deuteronomy 10:16"> but he is a Jew who is one inwardly; and circumcision is of the heart, spiritual, not literal, whose praise is not of men, but of God.</span>
