<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:1–51; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 10:19"> And as he went out of the temple, one of his disciples saith to him, Teacher, see! what stones, and what buildings!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Matthew 24:2; 1 Kings 9:7–8"> And Jesus said to him, Seest thou these great buildings? There will not be left one stone upon another, that will not be thrown down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> And as he was sitting on the Mount of Olives, over against the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> Tell us, when will these things be? and what will be the sign when all these things are about to be accomplished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> And Jesus began to say to them, See that no one deceive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> Many will come in my name, saying, I am He; and will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> And when ye hear of wars, and rumors of wars, be not troubled; they must come to pass; but not yet is the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; there will be earthquakes in divers places; there will be famines. These things are the beginning of travail-pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> But do ye take heed to yourselves; they will deliver you up to councils, and ye will be taken into synagogues and beaten; and ye will be brought before governors and kings for my sake, for a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> And the glad tidings must first be published to all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> And when they lead you away to deliver you up, be not anxious beforehand as to what ye shall speak; but whatever shall be given you in that hour, that speak; for it is not ye that speak, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> And brother will deliver up brother to death, and the father his child; and children will rise up against parents, and put them to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> And ye will be hated by all on account of my name. But he that endureth to the end will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> But when ye see the abomination of desolation standing where it ought not,—let him that readeth understand,—then let those who are in Judaea flee to the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> let not him that is on the house-top go down, nor enter, to take anything out of his house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and let not him that is in the field turn back to take his garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> And woe to the women with child, and to those with children at the breast, in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And pray that it may not be in winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> For those days will be a time of distress, such as hath not been from the beginning of the creation which God created until now, nor shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> And if the Lord had not shortened the days, no flesh would have been saved; but for the sake of the chosen whom he chose, he shortened the days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> And then if any one shall say to you, See! here is the Christ; see! there; believe him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> For false Christs and false prophets will rise up, and will do signs and wonders, to lead astray, if possible, the chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> But do ye take heed! I have told you all beforehand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> But in those days, after that distress, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give her light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> and the stars will be falling from heaven, and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> And then will they see the Son of man coming in the clouds with great power and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> And then will he send forth the angels, and will gather the chosen ones from the four winds, from the uttermost part of the earth to the uttermost part of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> And from the fig-tree learn its parable. When its branch hath now become tender, and putteth forth its leaves, ye know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> So also, when ye see these things coming to pass, know ye that he is near, at the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> Truly do I say to you, that this generation will not pass away, till all these things take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> But that day or hour knoweth no one, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Take heed, watch; for ye know not when the time is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> As a man going abroad, having left his house and given authority to his servants, to each one his work, also commanded the porter to watch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> so also watch ye; for ye know not when the master of the house is coming, whether at evening, or at midnight, or at the cock-crowing, or in the morning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> lest coming suddenly, he find you sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> And what I say to you, I say to all: Watch.</span>
