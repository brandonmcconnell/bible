<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> And as soon as it was morning, the chief priests, having held a consultation with the elders and the scribes, and the whole council, bound Jesus, and carried him away, and delivered him up to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> And Pilate asked him, Art thou the king of the Jews? And he answering saith to him, I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> And the chief priests brought many charges against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> And Pilate asked him again, Dost thou make no answer? See what things they are testifying against thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalms 71:7"> But Jesus made no further answer; so that Pilate marveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> Now at the feast he was wont to release to them one prisoner, whom they might ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> And there was one named Barabbas, who lay bound with insurgents who had committed murder in the insurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the multitude, coming up, began to ask him to do as he had been wont to do for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> And Pilate answered them, saying, Will ye that I release to you the king of the Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> For he knew that for envy the chief priests had delivered him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests stirred up the multitude, that he should rather release to them Barabbas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> And Pilate answering again said to them, What then would ye have me do with him whom ye call the king of the Jews?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they cried out again, Crucify him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> Then Pilate said to them, Why, what evil hath he done? And they cried out the more, Crucify him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> And Pilate, wishing to satisfy the multitude, released to them Barabbas; and, when he had scourged Jesus, delivered him up to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> And the soldiers led him away into the court, which is the Praetorium; and they call together the whole band.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> And they clothe him with purple, and having platted a crown of thorns, they put it on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> And they began to salute him: Hail, king of the Jews!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> And they struck him on the head with a reed, and spit upon him, and kneeling down, did him homage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> And after they had made sport of him, they stripped him of the purple robe, and put his own garments on him. And they lead him out to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> And they compel one Simon a Cyrenaean, who was passing by, coming from the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to carry his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> And they bring him to the place Golgotha; which is, when interpreted, Place of a Skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> And they gave him wine mingled with myrrh; but he did not take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> And they crucify him, and divide his garments, casting lots for them, what each should take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> And it was the third hour when they crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> And on the cross the inscription of the charge against him was written: “The king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> And with him they crucify two robbers; one on his right hand, and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> </span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> And they that passed by reviled him, wagging their heads, and saying, Ha! thou that destroyest the temple, and buildest it up in three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save thyself, and come down from the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> In like manner also the chief priests, with the scribes, making sport among themselves, said, He saved others, cannot he save himself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> Let the Christ, the king of Israel, come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe. And they that were crucified with him were reviling him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> And when the sixth hour had come, there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> And at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani? which is, when interpreted, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> And some of those who stood by, when they heard it, said, See! he is calling for Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> And one ran and filled a sponge with vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave it to him to drink, saying, Hold! Let us see whether Elijah will come to take him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> And Jesus, having uttered a loud cry, expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> And the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top to the bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> And the centurion who stood over against him, seeing that he so expired, said, Truly this man was the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> There were also women looking on from a distance; among whom was Mary the Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joses, and Salome;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> who, when he was in Galilee, followed him, and rendered him their services; and many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> And evening having now come, because it was the preparation, that is, the day before the sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathaea, an honorable councilor, who was himself waiting for the kingdom of God, came, and boldly went in to Pilate, and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> And Pilate wondered that he was already dead; and calling to him the centurion, he asked him whether he had been long dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> And having been informed by the centurion, he gave the body to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> And having bought fine linen, he took him down, and wrapped him in the linen, and laid him in a tomb which was hewn out of a rock, and rolled a stone to the door of the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> And Mary the Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where he was laid.</span>
