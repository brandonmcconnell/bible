<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And he entered again into a synagogue; and there was a man there having a withered hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> and they watched him, whether he would heal him on the sabbath, that they might accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And he saith to the man having the withered hand, Stand up in the midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he saith to them, Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath, or to do evil? to save life, or to kill? But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And looking round on them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their hearts, he saith to the man, Stretch forth thy hand. And he stretched it forth; and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> And the Pharisees went forth, and immediately had a consultation with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> And Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the lake; and a great multitude from Galilee, and from Judaea followed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem, and from Idumaea, and from beyond the Jordan, and the people about Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, when they heard what great things he was doing, came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And he gave direction to his disciples, that a boat should be in readiness for him because of the multitude, that they might not throng him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For he had healed many, so that as many as had plagues pressed upon him to touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And the unclean spirits, when they saw him, fell down before him, and cried out, saying, Thou art the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And he strictly charged them that they should not make him known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And he goeth up into the mountain, and calleth to him whom he would; and they came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And he appointed twelve to be with him, and whom he might send forth to preach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have authority to cast out demons. And he appointed the twelve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> and Simon he surnamed Peter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and he surnamed them Boanerges, that is, Sons of thunder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon of Cana,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed him. And he cometh into the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And again a multitude cometh together, so that they could not so much as eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> And his relations hearing of it went out to lay hold of him; for they said, He is beside himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribes who came down from Jerusalem said, He hath Beelzebul; and, He casteth out the demons through the prince of the demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And calling them to him, he said to them in parables: How can Satan cast out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And if a kingdom be divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> and if a house be divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and if Satan rise up against himself, he is divided, and cannot stand, but hath an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> Moreover, no one can enter into a strong man's house, and plunder his goods, unless he first bind the strong man; and then he will plunder his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> Truly do I say to you, All sins will be forgiven the sons of men, and the blasphemies wherewith they shall blaspheme;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Spirit hath no forgiveness forever, but is exposed to everlasting sin.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> Because they said, He hath an unclean spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> And his mother and his brothers came; and, standing without, sent to him, to call him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And a multitude was sitting about him; and they say to him, Lo! thy mother and thy brothers and thy sisters are without, seeking for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And he answering saith to them, Who is my mother, and my brothers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And looking round on those who sat about him, he saith, Behold my mother and my brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> Whoever shall do the will of God, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.</span>
