He that fleeth of them shall not flee from danger, And he that escapeth of them shall not escape into safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:53; Psalms 139:7–10; Obadiah 1:4"> Though they dig down to the under-world, Thence shall my hand take them; Though they climb up to heaven, Thence will I bring them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:16; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Isaiah 27:1"> Though they hide themselves on the top of Carmel, There will I search for them, and take them away; Though they hide themselves from mine eyes in the bottom of the sea, There will I command the serpent, and he shall bite them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 44:11"> Though they go into captivity before their enemies, There will I command the sword, and it shall slay them; I will set mine eyes upon them for evil, and not for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:8; Psalms 46:6; Isaiah 64:1"> For the Lord Jehovah of hosts Is he, that toucheth the earth, and it shall melt, And all that dwell therein shall mourn; All of it shall rise in waves like a river, And shall be overflowed, as by the river of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Amos 4:13; Psalms 104:3"> He that buildeth his upper rooms in the heavens, And foundeth his arch upon the earth,—That calleth the waters of the sea, And poureth them out upon the face of the earth,—Jehovah is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Deuteronomy 2:23; 2 Kings 16:9"> Are ye not as the Ethiopians to me, O children of Israel? saith Jehovah. Did I not bring Israel from the land of Egypt, And the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Syrians from Kir?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremiah 44:27"> Behold, the eyes of the Lord Jehovah are upon the sinful kingdom, And I will destroy it from the face of the earth; Yet will I not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Deuteronomy 28:64; Isaiah 30:28"> For, behold, I will command, And I will sift the house of Israel among all the nations, As one sifteth corn with a sieve, And not a grain shall fall upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:3; Isaiah 33:14; Amos 6:1"> But all the sinners of my people shall die by the sword, Who say, Evil shall not approach, nor fall upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 15:15–17; Jeremiah 33:14–16; Isaiah 16:5"> In that day I will raise up the fallen tabernacle of David, And I will close up the breaches thereof, And raise up its ruins, And I will build it, as in the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:7; Isaiah 65:1; Acts 15:17"> That they may possess the remnant of Edom, And all the nations, which shall be called by my name. Thus saith Jehovah, who doeth this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 3:18; John 4:35; Leviticus 26:5"> Behold the days come, saith Jehovah, That the plougher shall draw near to the reaper, And the treader of grapes to the sower of the seed; And the mountains shall drop new wine, And all the hills shall melt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:4; Jeremiah 30:3; Jeremiah 30:18"> I will bring back the captives of my people Israel, And they shall build the desolate cities, and shall inhabit them; And they shall plant vineyards, and drink their wine; They shall also make gardens, and eat their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Ezekiel 37:25; Isaiah 60:21"> I will plant them in their land, And they shall no more be rooted up from the land which I have given them, Saith Jehovah, thy God.</span>
