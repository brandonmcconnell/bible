<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Jeremiah 7:29"> Hear these words, which I utter concerning you; This lamentation, O house of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:17; Lamentations 2:13; Jeremiah 2:27"> She is fallen, she shall rise no more, The virgin of Israel! She is prostrate on her own ground, there is none to raise her up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Ezekiel 12:16; Isaiah 6:13"> For thus saith the Lord Jehovah; The city which sent out a thousand shall have a hundred left, And that which sent out a hundred shall have ten left, To the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Zephaniah 2:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> For thus saith Jehovah to the house of Israel: Seek ye me, and ye shall live!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Amos 4:4; Amos 8:14"> Seek not Bethel, And go not to Gilgal, And pass not over to Beersheba! For Gilgal shall surely go into captivity! And Bethel shall come to naught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:11; Isaiah 55:6; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> Seek Jehovah, and ye shall live! Lest he rush like a fire on the house of Joseph, And it devour, and there be none to quench it in the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 6:12; Amos 5:11–12; Zephaniah 1:6"> Ye who turn justice into wormwood, And cast righteousness down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Job 9:9; Amos 9:6"> Seek him, that made the Pleiades and Orion; That changeth death-like darkness into morning, And darkeneth day into night; That calleth up the waters of the sea, And poureth them out upon the face of the earth; Jehovah is his name!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Jeremiah 37:10; Micah 5:11"> Who sendeth sudden destruction upon the strong, And bringeth desolation upon the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:21; 1 Kings 22:8; John 8:45–47"> They hate him that pleadeth in the gate, And abhor him that speaketh uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:30; Micah 6:15; Zephaniah 1:13"> Since, then, ye trample upon the poor, And take from him presents of wheat, Though ye build houses of hewn stone, ye shall not dwell in them; Though ye plant pleasant vineyards, ye shall not drink their wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 2:6–7; Amos 5:10; Isaiah 1:23"> For I know that your sins are many, And your transgressions manifold, Ye who afflict the righteous, and take a bribe, And oppress the poor in the gate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:1; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> Therefore the wise man shall be silent at that time, For it shall be an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Matthew 6:33; Micah 6:8"> Seek ye good, and not evil, that ye may live; Then shall Jehovah, the God of hosts, be with you, as ye boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 97:10"> Hate ye evil, and love good, And establish justice in the gate; It may be that Jehovah, the God of hosts, will have pity upon the remnant of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 1:11; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Jeremiah 9:10"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of hosts, the Lord: In all the streets shall there be wailing, And in all the highways shall they cry, Alas! alas! They shall call the husbandmen to mourning, And those who are skilful in lamentation to wailing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Jeremiah 48:33; Isaiah 16:10"> And in all vineyards shall be sounds of woe, For I will pass through the midst of thee, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Joel 2:1–2; Joel 2:31"> Woe unto them that ask for the day of Jehovah! What is the day of Jehovah to you? It shall be darkness, and not light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 24:17–18; Jeremiah 15:2–3; Acts 28:4"> As if a man fled from a lion, And a bear met him; Or went into a house and leaned his hand on a wall, And a serpent bit him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Ezekiel 34:12; Zephaniah 1:15"> So shall the day of Jehovah be darkness, and not light, Even thick darkness, and no brightness in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–16; Proverbs 28:9; Leviticus 26:31"> I hate, I despise your feasts; I have no delight in your solemn assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; Amos 4:4–5; Isaiah 66:3"> When ye offer me burnt-offerings and flour-offerings, I will not accept them; And upon the thank-offerings of your fatlings I will not look.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 6:5; Amos 8:10; Amos 8:3"> Take ye away from me the noise of your songs, And the music of your harps let me not hear!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Hosea 6:6"> Let justice flow forth as waters, And righteousness as a mighty stream!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 7:42–43; Ezekiel 20:8; Nehemiah 9:18"> Did ye offer me sacrifices and offerings In the wilderness, for forty years, O house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 23:12–13; 1 Kings 11:33; Leviticus 20:2–5"> But ye bore the tabernacle of your king, And the shrine of your images, The star of your god, which ye made for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:43; 2 Kings 17:6; Amos 4:13"> Therefore will I cause you to go into captivity beyond Damascus, Saith Jehovah, whose name is the God of hosts!</span>
