<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Amos 8:1; Joel 2:25"> The Lord Jehovah showed me this vision: Behold, he formed locusts In the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; Behold, it was the latter growth after the king's mowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; Jeremiah 42:2"> And when they had devoured the grass of the land, Then said I, O Lord Jehovah, forgive, I beseech thee! How shall Jacob stand? For he is small!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Jonah 3:10; Hosea 11:8"> Jehovah repented of this; It shall not be, said Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; Hebrews 1:7"> The Lord Jehovah also showed me this vision: Behold, the Lord Jehovah commanded the fire to execute judgment; And it devoured the great deep, And it devoured the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; Jeremiah 30:19"> Then said I, O Lord Jehovah, desist, I beseech thee! How shall Jacob stand? For he is small!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 7:3; Judges 2:18; Psalms 90:13"> Jehovah repented of this; This also shall not be, said the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Revelation 21:15; Isaiah 28:17"> He also showed me this vision: Behold, the Lord stood upon a wall, built with a plumb-line, And in his hand was a plumb-line;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Lamentations 2:8; Ezekiel 7:2–9"> And Jehovah said to me, What seest thou, Amos? And I said, A plumb-line. And the Lord said, Behold I will set a plumb-line in the midst of my people Israel; I will not spare them any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:8–10; Genesis 46:1; Amos 8:14"> The high places of Isaac shall be desolate, And the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste, And I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; 1 Kings 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> Then sent Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, To Jeroboam, the king of Israel, and said: Amos conspireth against thee in the midst of the house of Israel; The land is not able to bear all his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Psalms 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> For thus hath Amos said: Jeroboam shall die by the sword, And Israel shall surely be led captive from their own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; 1 Samuel 9:9"> And Amaziah said to Amos, Go, thou seer! flee into the land of Judah! There eat thy bread, and there prophesy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32; Amos 2:12; 1 Kings 12:29"> But prophesy no more at Bethel, For it is the king's sanctuary, And it is the king's abode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 2:5"> Then answered Amos, and said to Amaziah: I was no prophet, nor the son of a prophet; I was a shepherd, and a gatherer of sycamore fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> And Jehovah took me from the flock; And Jehovah said to me, Go, prophesy to my people Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> Now, therefore, hear the word of Jehovah: Thou sayest, Prophesy not against Israel, And speak no word against the house of Isaac!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; 2 Kings 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah: “Thy wife shall be put to shame in the city, And thy sons and daughters shall fall by the sword; Thy land shall be divided by the line, And thou shalt die in a polluted land, And Israel shall surely be led captive from his own land.”</span>
