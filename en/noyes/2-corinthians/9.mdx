<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 8:4–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–10"> For concerning the ministering to the saints, it is superfluous for me to write to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:10; 2 Corinthians 8:8; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> For I know your readiness of mind, of which I boast in behalf of you to the Macedonians, that Achaia was prepared a year ago; and your zeal stirred up the greater part of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 7:14; Titus 3:1"> But I sent the brethren, that our boasting of you should not prove unfounded in this respect; that, as I said, ye may be prepared;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> lest, should the Macedonians come with me, and find you unprepared, we (not to say ye) should be put to shame in respect to this confidence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:17"> I thought it necessary therefore to exhort the brethren to go before to you, and make up beforehand your bounty, which was already announced, that the same might be ready, as a matter of bounty, and not of covetousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Luke 6:38; Proverbs 22:9"> But this there is to say: He that soweth sparingly, shall reap also sparingly; and he that soweth bountifully, shall reap also bountifully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 20:35; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> Each one, as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity; for God loveth a cheerful giver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Philippians 4:18–19; Proverbs 10:22"> And God is able to make every blessing abound toward you, that ye, always having all sufficiency in everything, may abound to every good work;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 112:9; Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> as it is written: “He dispersed abroad, he gave to the poor; his righteousness remaineth for ever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12"> Now, he that ministereth seed to the sower, and bread for food, will supply and multiply your seed sown, and increase the fruits of your righteousness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Chronicles 29:12–14; 1 Timothy 6:17–18"> while ye are enriched in everything to all liberality, which worketh out through us thanksgiving to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15; Philemon 1:4–7; Philippians 2:25"> for the ministration of this service not only supplieth the wants of the saints, but also overfloweth through many thanksgivings to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Matthew 5:16; 2 Corinthians 2:12"> while by the proof afforded by this ministration they glorify God for your obedience to your profession in regard to the gospel of Christ, and for the liberality of your contribution in regard to them and in regard to all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:14; Philippians 4:1; Philippians 4:18–19"> while with supplication for you they long after you on account of the exceeding grace of God to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Ephesians 5:20; John 3:16"> Thanks be to God for his unspeakable gift.</span>
