<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 3:18; John 8:32"> The elder to the elect Cyria, and to her children, whom I love in truth, and not I only, but also all that know the truth,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:14; 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:17"> for the sake of the truth which abideth in us, and will be with us for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:10; Romans 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:14"> Grace, mercy, peace shall be with you from God the Father, and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="3 John 1:3–4; Ephesians 5:8; Ephesians 5:2"> I rejoiced greatly, that I found some of thy children walking in truth, as we received commandment from the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 3:11; John 13:34–35; John 15:12"> And now I beseech thee, Cyria, not as writing to thee a new commandment, but that which we have had from the beginning, that we love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:24; John 15:10"> And this is love, that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, as ye have heard from the beginning, that ye should walk in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:26; 1 John 4:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22"> For many deceivers went out into the world, who do not acknowledge Jesus Christ coming in the flesh; this is the deceiver and the antichrist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Revelation 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:14"> Look to yourselves, that ye lose not the things which ye wrought, but receive a full reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:16–17; Colossians 3:16; 1 John 2:22–24"> Whoever goeth beyond, and abideth not in the teaching of Christ, hath not God. He that abideth in the teaching, he hath both the Son and the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:10; Romans 16:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> If any one cometh to you, and bringeth not this teaching, receive him not into your house, and do not bid him good speed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; Ephesians 5:11; Revelation 18:4"> For he that biddeth him good speed taketh part in his evil deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="3 John 1:13–14; John 16:12; 1 Corinthians 16:5–7"> Having many things to write to you, I would not write with paper and ink; but I hope to come to you, and to speak face to face, that our joy may be full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:13; 2 John 1:1–13"> The children of thy elect sister salute thee.</span>
