<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:1; Luke 10:20; 1 Kings 1:47"> A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches; And better is good-will than silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:15; Proverbs 14:31; 1 Samuel 2:7"> The rich and the poor meet together; The LORD is the Maker of them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:12; Proverbs 14:16; Proverbs 29:1"> The prudent man seeth the evil, and hideth himself; But the simple rush on, and are punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; James 4:10; Psalms 34:9–10"> By humility and the fear of the LORD Are riches and honor and life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; 1 John 5:18; Proverbs 16:17"> Thorns and snares are in the way of the deceitful; He that will preserve his life will be far from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:7; 2 Timothy 3:15"> Train up a child in accordance with his way, And when he is old he will not depart from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Proverbs 22:16; Matthew 18:25"> The rich ruleth over the poor, And the borrower is servant to the lender.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:7–8; Job 4:8; Isaiah 30:31"> He who soweth iniquity shall reap calamity, And the rod of his punishment is prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 6:10; 1 Timothy 6:18–19"> He who hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed, Because he giveth of his bread to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:20–21; Genesis 21:9–10; 1 Corinthians 5:5–6"> Cast out the scoffer, and contention will go out; Yea, strife and reproach will cease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:13; Matthew 5:8; Psalms 101:6"> He who loveth purity of heart, Grace is upon his lips, and the king will be his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:9; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> The eyes of the LORD watch over knowledge; But he overthroweth the words of the treacherous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 26:13–16; Numbers 13:32–33"> The slothful man saith, “There is a lion without; I shall be slain in the streets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 23:27; Proverbs 6:24–29"> The mouth of strange women is a deep pit; He with whom the LORD is angry shall fall therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 19:18"> Folly is bound to the heart of a child; But the rod of correction will drive it far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 22:22–23; Psalms 12:5; James 2:13"> He that oppresseth the poor to increase his wealth, And he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Psalms 90:12; Proverbs 3:1"> Incline thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, And give heed to my instruction!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10; Psalms 119:103"> For it will be a pleasant thing, if thou keep them in thy bosom, When they are altogether established upon thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 62:8; Jeremiah 17:7; 1 Peter 1:21"> That thy trust may be in the LORD, I have this day given to thee instruction, yea, to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> Behold, I have written to thee excellent things Concerning counsel and knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:3–4; 1 Peter 3:15; John 20:31"> That I may make thee know rectitude, and words of truth; That thou mayst bring back words of truth to them that send thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 7:10; Malachi 3:5; Exodus 23:6"> Rob not the poor man, because he is poor, Nor crush thou the destitute at the gate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Psalms 140:12; Proverbs 23:11"> For the LORD will maintain their cause, And despoil their spoilers of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:22; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Proverbs 21:24"> Make no friendship with a passionate man, Nor be the companion of a man prone to wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 13:20; Psalms 106:35"> Lest thou learn his ways, And take to thyself a snare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5"> Be not thou one of those who strike hands, Of those who are sureties for debts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; 2 Kings 4:1; Exodus 22:26–27"> When thou hast nothing to pay, Why should thy bed be taken from under thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 23:10"> Remove not the ancient landmark, Which thy fathers have made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 11:28; Matthew 25:21; Proverbs 12:24"> Seest thou a man diligent in his business? He shall be the minister of kings; He shall not serve obscure men.</span>
