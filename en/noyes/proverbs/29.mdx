<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Proverbs 1:24–31"> He who, being often reproved, hardeneth his neck, Shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the righteous are powerful, the people rejoice; But when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:11; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20"> He who loveth wisdom rejoiceth his father; But he who is a companion of harlots destroyeth his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 29:14; Proverbs 20:8; Daniel 11:20"> A king by equity establisheth a land; But he who receiveth gifts overthroweth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Job 17:5; Psalms 5:9"> A man who flattereth his neighbor Spreadeth a net for his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Exodus 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:26"> In the transgression of a wicked man there is a snare; But the righteous shall sing and rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 29:16; Psalms 41:1; Proverbs 21:13"> A righteous man careth for the cause of the poor; A wicked man discerneth not knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 11:11; Jeremiah 15:1; James 5:15–18"> Scoffers kindle a city into a flame; But wise men pacify wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4; Ecclesiastes 10:13; Matthew 7:6"> If a wise man contend in a cause with a fool, Whether he rage or laugh, there will be no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:18–19; 1 John 3:12–13; Genesis 4:5–8"> The bloodthirsty man hateth the upright; But the righteous seek to preserve his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:11; Proverbs 12:16; Micah 7:5"> A fool letteth all his anger come out; But a wise man keepeth it back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; 2 Kings 10:6–7; Psalms 52:2–4"> If a ruler listen to words of falsehood, All his servants become wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:45; Proverbs 22:2; Psalms 13:3"> The poor man and the oppressor meet together: The LORD giveth light to the eyes of them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Isaiah 11:4; Psalms 72:2–4"> The king that judgeth the poor with uprightness, His throne shall be established for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 17:25; Proverbs 29:17"> The rod and reproof give wisdom; But a child left to himself bringeth shame to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:36; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 58:10"> When the wicked are powerful, transgression increaseth; But the righteous shall see their fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 23:13–14"> Chastise thy son, and he will give thee rest; Yea, he will give delight to thy soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:25; Luke 11:28; John 13:17"> Where there is no vision, the people become unruly; But he that keepeth the law, happy is he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Job 19:16; Proverbs 30:22"> A servant will not be corrected by words; For, though he understand, he will not obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:29"> Seest thou a man hasty in his words? There is more hope of a fool than of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He that bringeth up his servant delicately from childhood Shall have him become a son at the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 17:19; Proverbs 26:21"> An angry man stirreth up strife, And a passionate man aboundeth in transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 11:2"> A man's pride will bring him low; But he that is of a humble spirit shall obtain honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Proverbs 1:11–19; Proverbs 8:36"> He who shareth with a thief hateth himself: He heareth the curse, but maketh no discovery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 118:8; Luke 12:4; Proverbs 18:10"> The fear of man bringeth a snare; But whoso putteth his trust in the LORD shall be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 20:9"> Many are they who seek the ruler's favor; But every man's judgment cometh from the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 15:17–19; Psalms 139:21"> As the unjust man is an abomination to the righteous, So the upright in his way is an abomination to the wicked.</span>
