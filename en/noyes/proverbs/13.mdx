<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 15:20"> A wise son listeneth to the instruction of his father; But a scoffer listeneth not to rebuke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:14; Proverbs 18:20; Proverbs 10:11"> By the fruit of a man's mouth he shall eat good; But the appetite of transgressors shall be sated with violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:7"> He who keepeth his mouth keepeth his life; But destruction shall be to him who openeth wide his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:24; Proverbs 12:11; 2 Peter 1:5–11"> The appetite of the sluggard longeth, and hath nothing; But the appetite of the diligent is fully satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Psalms 119:163"> A righteous man hateth words of falsehood; But a wicked man causeth disgrace and shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:3; Proverbs 11:5–6; Psalms 140:11"> Righteousness preserveth him who is upright in his way; But wickedness overthroweth the sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 18:11–14; Revelation 3:17; Proverbs 11:24"> There is who maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing, Who maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Psalms 49:6–10"> A man's wealth is the ransom of his life; But the poor man heareth no threatenings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 4:18; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> The light of the righteous shall rejoice; But the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Proverbs 12:15–16; Proverbs 17:14"> By pride cometh only contention; But with the well-advised is wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:21; Proverbs 10:2; James 5:1–5"> Wealth gotten by vanity will become small; But he who gathereth it into the hand increaseth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 16:22; Proverbs 13:19; Psalms 119:81–83"> Hope deferred maketh the heart sick; But the desire accomplished is a tree of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 15:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 John 1:8"> He that despiseth the word shall be destroyed; But he who revereth the commandment shall be rewarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:5; Proverbs 10:11; Proverbs 14:27"> The instruction of the wise is a fountain of life; By it men escape from the snares of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:19; Proverbs 4:19; Acts 7:10"> A good understanding winneth favor; But the way of transgressors is hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 12:22–23; Ephesians 5:17"> Every prudent man acteth with knowledge; But a fool spreadeth abroad his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> A wicked messenger falleth into trouble; But a faithful ambassador is health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 15:31–32"> Poverty and shame are for him who rejecteth instruction; But he that regardeth reproof shall come to honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 7:14–17; Proverbs 13:12; Proverbs 29:27"> The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul; But it is an abomination to fools to depart from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33–34; Proverbs 15:31; Proverbs 9:6"> He who walketh with wise men shall be wise; But the companion of fools shall be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:10; Isaiah 3:10–11; Romans 2:7–10"> Calamity pursueth the wicked; But the righteous is rewarded with good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:26; Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 28:8"> The good man leaveth his substance to his children's children; But the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 28:19; Ecclesiastes 5:9"> There is much food from the fallow-ground of the poor; But there is who is brought low for want of uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 29:17; Proverbs 23:13–14; Proverbs 29:15"> He that spareth the rod hateth his son; But he who loveth him chasteneth him early.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 34:10; Proverbs 10:3; Hebrews 13:5"> The righteous man eateth to the satisfying of his desire; But the stomach of the wicked suffereth want.</span>
