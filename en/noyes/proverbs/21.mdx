<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Proverbs 20:24; Ezra 6:22"> As streams of water, So is the heart of the king in the hand of the LORD; He turneth it whithersoever he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:2; Proverbs 16:25; Luke 16:15"> All the ways of a man are right in his own eyes; But the LORD weigheth the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8"> To do justice and equity Is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:17; 1 Peter 5:5; Isaiah 2:17"> The lofty look, the proud heart, The lamp of the wicked, is ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Ephesians 4:28"> The plans of the diligent tend only to plenty; But the hasty hasteneth only to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:11; Proverbs 10:2; Proverbs 20:21"> The getting of treasures by a false tongue Is the fleeting breath of them that seek death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:21; Ephesians 5:6; Proverbs 1:18–19"> The rapine of the wicked shall snatch them away, Because they refuse to do justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:15; 1 John 2:29; Psalms 14:2–3"> The way of the guilty man is crooked; But he that is pure, his doings are right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 27:15–16"> Better is it to dwell in a corner of the housetop Than with a brawling woman in a large house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Proverbs 3:29; Psalms 36:4"> The soul of the wicked longeth to do evil; His neighbor findeth no compassion in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:25; Proverbs 18:15; Proverbs 9:9"> When the scoffer is punished, the simple is made wise; When the wise man is taught, he receiveth knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 14:11; Psalms 37:35–36; 2 Peter 3:6–7"> The righteous man hath regard to the house of the wicked; He casteth the wicked headlong into ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Matthew 6:14; Proverbs 1:28"> Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, He also shall cry aloud, but shall not be heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 17:8"> A gift in secret pacifieth anger; And a present in the bosom, strong wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Matthew 7:23; Matthew 13:41–42"> To do justice shall be joy to the righteous; But destruction is for them that do iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38; Psalms 125:5; Psalms 49:14"> A man who wandereth from the way of discretion Shall rest in the assembly of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; 1 Timothy 5:6; 2 Timothy 3:4"> He that loveth pleasure will be a poor man; He that loveth wine and oil will not be rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:8; Isaiah 43:3–4; Isaiah 53:4–5"> The wicked shall be a ransom for the righteous! And in the room of the upright shall be the transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:9; Psalms 120:5–6; Psalms 55:6–7"> It is better to dwell in a desert land Than with a contentious and fretful woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:3–4; Psalms 112:3; Ecclesiastes 7:11"> Precious treasure and oil are in the dwelling of the wise; But the foolish man swalloweth them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; 1 Corinthians 15:58; Proverbs 22:4"> He who followeth after righteousness and mercy Shall find life, prosperity, and honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Ecclesiastes 9:13–18"> A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, And bringeth down the strength in which it trusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; Proverbs 18:21; James 1:26"> Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue Keepeth his soul from trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:22"> The proud and haughty,—scoffer is his name; He acteth with haughty arrogance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 12:24; Matthew 25:26"> The desire of the sluggard will destroy him; For his hands refuse to labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:26; Acts 20:33–35; Ephesians 4:28"> The covetous man coveteth all the day long; But the righteous man giveth, and doth not withhold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Proverbs 15:8; Jeremiah 6:20"> The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination; How much more when he bringeth it with an evil design!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:13; Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9"> A false witness shall perish; But a man that hearkeneth shall speak forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:11; Proverbs 11:5; Haggai 2:18–19"> The wicked man hardeneth his face; But the upright directeth his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Acts 5:39; Jeremiah 9:23"> Wisdom is nothing, and understanding is nothing, And devices are nothing, against the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 20:7; Isaiah 31:1; Psalms 33:17–18"> The horse is prepared for the day of battle; But victory is from the LORD.</span>
