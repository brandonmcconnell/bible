<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Proverbs 28:16; Esther 4:6"> As snow in summer, and as rain in harvest, So honor is not becoming to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:8; Nehemiah 13:2; Psalms 109:28"> As the sparrow wandereth, and the swallow flieth away, So the curse without cause shall not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> A whip for the horse, a bridle for the ass, And a rod for the back of the fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Answer not a fool according to his folly, Lest thou also become like to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Answer a fool according to his folly, Lest he be wise in his own conceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He cutteth off the feet, and drinketh damage, Who sendeth a message by the hand of a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> The legs of a lame man hang loose; So is it with a proverb in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> As he who bindeth a stone in a sling, So is he that giveth honor to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> As a thorn lifted up by the hand of a drunkard, So is a proverb in the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> As an archer who woundeth every one, So is he who hireth fools and hireth wayfarers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As a dog returneth to that which he hath vomited, So a fool repeateth his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> Seest thou a man wise in his own conceit? There is more hope of a fool than of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> The slothful man saith, “There is a lion in the way; There is a lion in the streets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> As a door turneth upon its hinges, So doth the sluggard upon his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> The sluggard dippeth his hand into the dish; It grieveth him to bring it again to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> The sluggard is wiser in his own conceit Than seven men who can render a reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> As one that taketh a dog by the ears, So is he who, passing by, is enraged on account of the quarrel of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As a madman That casteth about darts, arrows, and death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> So is the man who deceiveth his neighbor, And saith, “Was I not in sport?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> Where there is no wood, the fire goeth out; So, where there is no talebearer, contention ceaseth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; 2 Samuel 20:1"> As coal is for heat, and as wood for fire, So is a contentious man for kindling strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Ezekiel 22:9"> The words of a talebearer are like dainties; For they go down to the innermost parts of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> As drossy silver spread over an earthen vessel, So are warm lips and an evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> The hater dissembleth with his lips, And layeth up deceit within him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalms 28:3"> When he speaketh fair, believe him not! For there are seven abominations in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 55:21–23; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:21"> His hatred is covered by deceit; His wickedness shall be revealed in the great assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> He that diggeth a pit shall fall therein; And he that rolleth a stone, it shall return upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:44–49; Luke 20:20–21; John 10:32–33"> A lying tongue hateth those whom it woundeth, And a flattering mouth worketh ruin.</span>
