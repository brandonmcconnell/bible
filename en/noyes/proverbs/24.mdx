<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 37:7; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 3:31"> Be not thou envious of wicked men, And desire not to be with them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 7:14; Psalms 28:3"> For their heart studieth destruction, And their lips speak mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; 1 Corinthians 3:9; Proverbs 9:1"> Through wisdom is a house builded, And by understanding is it established;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 15:6; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 20:15"> Yea, by knowledge are the chambers filled With all precious and goodly substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Colossians 1:11; Proverbs 21:22"> The wise man is strong; Yea, the man of understanding establisheth his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> For by wise counsel shalt thou make war, And by the multitude of counsellors cometh success.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; Psalms 10:5; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> Wisdom is too high for the fool; He openeth not his mouth at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 21:11; Ezekiel 38:10–11; Proverbs 14:22"> He that deviseth to do evil Shall be called mischief-master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Isaiah 55:7"> The purpose of folly is sin; And a scoffer is an abomination to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:28–31; Ephesians 3:13; Jeremiah 51:46"> If thy spirit faint in the day of adversity, Faint will be thy strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 82:4; Isaiah 58:6–7; 1 John 3:16–17"> Deliver thou those who are dragged to death, And those who totter to the slaughter,—O keep them back!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 34:11; Revelation 2:23; Jeremiah 17:10"> If thou sayst, “Behold, we knew it not!” Doth not he that weigheth the heart observe it? Yea, he that keepeth thy soul knoweth it. And he will render to every man according to his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 25:16"> Eat honey, my son, for it is good, And the honeycomb, which is sweet to thy taste;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:25; Jeremiah 15:16; Proverbs 2:10"> So learn thou wisdom for thy soul! When thou hast found it, there shall be a reward, And thy expectation shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Isaiah 32:18; 1 Samuel 23:20–23"> Plot not, O wicked man! against the habitation of the righteous; Spoil not his resting-place!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Psalms 37:24; Micah 7:8–10"> For though the righteous fall seven times, yet shall he rise up again; But the wicked shall fall into mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Obadiah 1:12; Job 31:29; Proverbs 17:5"> Rejoice not when thine enemy falleth, And let not thy heart be glad when he stumbleth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15–16; Lamentations 4:21–22"> Lest the LORD see, and it displease him, And he turn away his anger from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 119:115; Psalms 37:1; Proverbs 23:17"> Fret not thyself on account of evil men, Neither be thou envious of the wicked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Job 18:5–6; Psalms 11:6"> For there shall be no posterity to the evil man; The lamp of the wicked shall be put out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Titus 3:1"> My son, fear thou the LORD and the king; And mingle not with them that are given to change!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Hosea 5:11; Numbers 16:31–35"> For their calamity shall rise up suddenly, And their ruin, coming from them both, in a moment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 28:21; Proverbs 18:5; John 7:24"> These also are words of the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Isaiah 5:23; Proverbs 11:26"> He that saith to the wicked, “Thou art righteous,” Him shall the people curse; Nations shall abhor him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 1 Samuel 3:13; Proverbs 28:23"> But it shall be well with them that punish him, And the blessing of prosperity shall come upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Genesis 41:38–57; Proverbs 25:11–12"> He that giveth a right answer Kisseth the lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 14:28–30; 1 Kings 5:17–18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Arrange thy work without, And prepare it in thy field: Afterwards thou mayst build thy house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Matthew 26:59–60; Proverbs 25:18"> Be not a witness without cause against thy neighbor, And deceive not with thy lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; Romans 12:17–21; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Say not, “As he hath done to me, So will I do to him; I will render to the man according to his doings.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Proverbs 6:6–19; Job 4:8"> I passed by the field of the slothful, And by the vineyard of the man void of understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:18; Isaiah 5:5; Jeremiah 4:3"> And, lo! it was all overgrown with thorns, And the face thereof was covered with nettles, And the stone wall thereof was broken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; Deuteronomy 13:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Then I saw, and considered it well; I looked upon it. and received instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–8; Proverbs 6:4–11; Romans 13:11"> “A little sleep, a little slumber! A little folding of the hands to rest!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4"> So shall poverty come upon thee like a highwayman; Yea, want like an armed man.</span>
