<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:20–21; Matthew 3:3; John 7:37"> Doth not wisdom cry aloud, And understanding put forth her voice?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 9:3"> Upon the top of the high places, By the wayside, In the cross-ways, She taketh her station.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:20; Acts 5:20; Job 29:7"> By the side of the gates, In the entrance of the city, In the approaches to the doors, she crieth aloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; John 3:16; Psalms 50:1"> “To you, O men! do I call, And my voice is to the sons of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Acts 26:18; Revelation 3:17–18"> O ye simple ones! learn wisdom, And ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 7:28–29; Proverbs 23:16"> Hear, for I speak excellent things, And my lips utter that which is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; John 8:14; John 17:17"> For my mouth speaketh truth, And wickedness is an abomination to my lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 7:46; Psalms 12:6; Proverbs 8:13"> All the words of my mouth are in uprightness; There is nothing crooked or deceitful in them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 14:6; James 1:5; John 7:17"> They are all plain to the man of understanding, And right to those who find knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 2:4–5; Psalms 119:127; Psalms 119:72"> Receive my instruction, and not silver. And knowledge rather than choice gold!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14–15; Proverbs 4:5–7; Proverbs 16:16"> For wisdom is better than pearls, And no precious things are to be compared with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 28:26; Exodus 35:30–4; Romans 11:33"> “I, wisdom, dwell with prudence, And find out the knowledge of sagacious counsels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 97:10; Proverbs 16:6; 1 Samuel 2:3"> The fear of the LORD is to hate evil; Pride, and arrogance, and the evil way, And the deceitful mouth, do I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:14"> Counsel is mine, and sound reason; I am understanding; I have strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 13:1; Daniel 2:21; Matthew 28:18"> By me kings reign, And princes decree justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> By me princes rule, And nobles, even all the judges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; Psalms 91:14"> I love them that love me, And they who seek me early shall find me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:16; Matthew 6:33; Luke 16:11–12"> Riches and honor are with me; Yea, durable riches and prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:20; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> My fruit is better than gold, yea, than fine gold. And my revenue than choice silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; John 10:27–28; Psalms 32:8"> I walk in the way of righteousness, In the midst of the paths of equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; Proverbs 24:4; Matthew 25:46"> I cause those who love me to possess substance; Yea, I fill their treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:17; John 1:1–2; Proverbs 3:19"> “The LORD created me, the firstling of his course, Before his works, of old;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:24; Psalms 2:6; Genesis 1:26"> I was anointed from everlasting, From the beginning, even before the earth was made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:20; Genesis 1:2; 1 John 4:9"> When as yet there were no deeps, I was brought forth. When there were no springs, abounding with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 90:2; Psalms 102:25–28; Job 38:4–11"> Before the mountains were settled, Yea, before the hills, I was brought forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 1:1–31"> Ere yet he had made the land and the wastes, And the first of the clods of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Job 26:10; Proverbs 3:19"> When he framed the heavens, I was there; When he drew a circle upon the face of the deep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When he made firm the sky above, And the fountains of the deep rushed forth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 104:9; Jeremiah 5:22; Genesis 1:9–10"> When he gave to the sea its bounds, that the waters should not pass their border; When he marked out the foundations of the earth,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–3; John 1:18; Isaiah 42:1"> Then was I by him as a master-builder; I was his delight day by day, Exulting before him at all times;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 16:3; John 13:1; Psalms 40:6–8"> Exulting in the habitable part of his earth, And my delight was with the sons of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Psalms 119:1–2; Psalms 1:1–4"> “Now, therefore, ye children, hearken to me! For happy are they who keep my ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Psalms 81:11–12; Proverbs 5:1"> Hear instruction, and be wise! Yea, reject it not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Matthew 7:24; Proverbs 3:13"> Happy the man who hearkeneth to me, Who watcheth day by day at my gates, Who waiteth at the posts of my doors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12; Proverbs 12:2"> For he that findeth me findeth life, And obtaineth favor from the LORD;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 15:32; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Corinthians 16:22"> But he who misseth me doeth violence to himself; All they who hate me love death.”</span>
