<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 15:17; Psalms 37:16; Proverbs 21:19"> Better is a dry morsel, and quietness therewith, Than a house full of flesh-banquets with strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Ecclesiastes 4:13; Proverbs 14:35"> A prudent servant shall rule over a son who causeth shame; Yea, with brothers he shall share the inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:10; Jeremiah 17:10; Psalms 26:2"> The refining-pot is for silver, and the furnace for gold; But the LORD trieth hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; 1 John 4:5; Proverbs 28:4"> An evil-doer listeneth to mischievous lips; And a liar giveth ear to a destructive tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 14:31; Job 31:29; 1 John 3:17"> Whoso mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker; He that is glad at calamities shall not go unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Proverbs 13:22; Proverbs 16:31"> Children's children are the crown of the aged, And their fathers the glory of sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 12:19; Psalms 50:16–17"> Excellent speech becometh not the base; How much less lying lips the noble!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Micah 7:3; 1 Samuel 25:35"> A gift is a precious stone in the eyes of him who taketh it; Whithersoever it turneth it hath success.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 16:28"> He who covereth an offence seeketh love; But he who recurreth to a matter removeth a friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 9:8–9; Proverbs 15:5"> A reproof will penetrate deeper into a wise man Than a hundred stripes into a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:46; 2 Samuel 16:5–9; 2 Samuel 18:15"> An evil man seeketh only rebellion; Therefore shall a cruel messenger be sent against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:8; 2 Samuel 17:8; Proverbs 28:15"> Let a man meet a bear robbed of her whelps, Rather than a fool in his folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 109:4–13; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17"> Whoso returneth evil for good, Evil shall not depart from his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Timothy 2:23–24"> The beginning of strife is as when one letteth out water; Therefore leave off contention before it rolleth onward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 18:5; Isaiah 5:23"> He that justifieth the wicked, And he that condemneth the just, Both alike are an abomination to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 21:25–26; Proverbs 23:23; Proverbs 18:15"> Why should a price be in the hand of a fool To get wisdom, seeing he hath no sense?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; John 15:13–14; 1 Samuel 20:17"> A friend loveth at all times; But in adversity he is born a brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:15; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> A man who lacketh understanding striketh hands, And becometh surety in the presence of his friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Samuel 25:36–38"> He who loveth strife loveth transgression; He who raiseth high his gate seeketh ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6–8; Proverbs 10:31; Proverbs 6:12–15"> He that is of a deceitful heart shall find no good; And he that turneth about with his tongue shall fall into mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 17:25"> Whoso begetteth a fool doeth it to his sorrow; Yea, the father of the fool hath no joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 12:25; Proverbs 18:14"> A merry heart doeth good to the body; But a broken spirit drieth up the bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 17:8; Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8"> The wicked man taketh a gift out of the bosom, To pervert the ways of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:14; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Proverbs 14:6"> Wisdom is before the face of him that hath understanding; But the eyes of a fool are in the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 2 Samuel 13:1–22; Proverbs 19:13"> A foolish son is a grief to his father, And bitterness to her that bore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 17:15; Proverbs 18:5; John 18:22"> Moreover, to punish the righteous is not good, Nor to smite the noble for their equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:18; James 1:19; Proverbs 10:19"> He that spareth his words is imbued with knowledge; And he that is of a cool spirit is a man of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:5; Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> Even a fool, when he is silent, is accounted wise; He that shutteth his lips is a man of understanding.</span>
