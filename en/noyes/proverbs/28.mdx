<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Psalms 53:5; Isaiah 26:3–4"> The wicked flee when no one pursueth; But the righteous is as bold as a lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 16:8–29; 2 Kings 15:8–31"> Through the transgression of a land many are its rulers; But through men of prudence and understanding the prince shall live long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 18:28–30"> A poor man who oppresseth the needy Is a sweeping rain which leaveth no food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 14:4"> They who forsake the law praise the wicked; But they who keep the law contend with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 92:6; James 1:5; 1 John 2:27"> Wicked men understand not equity; But they who seek the LORD understand all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Proverbs 28:18; Luke 16:19–23"> Better is a poor man who walketh in uprightness, Than he who is perverse in his ways, though he be rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3–4; Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 3:1–35"> He that keepeth the law is a wise son; But he that is the companion of prodigals bringeth shame on his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 27:16–17; Proverbs 13:22; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> He that increaseth his substance by usurious gain Gathereth it for him who will pity the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 66:18; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Zechariah 7:11–13"> He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, Even his prayer shall be an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 37:25–26; Galatians 2:4"> He that causeth the righteous to go astray in an evil way Shall himself fall into his own pit; But the upright shall have good things in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:17; Ecclesiastes 9:15–17; Proverbs 26:16"> The rich man is wise in his own conceit; But the poor man, who hath understanding, will search him through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the righteous rejoice, there is great glorying; But, when the wicked are exalted, men hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 1:8–10; Psalms 32:3–5; Job 31:33"> He that covereth his sins shall not prosper; But he that confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 23:17; Psalms 2:11; Isaiah 66:2"> Happy the man who feareth always! But he who hardeneth his heart shall fall into mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 2:16; Proverbs 19:12"> As a roaring lion and a hungry bear, So is a wicked ruler over a needy people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Jeremiah 22:15–17; Ecclesiastes 4:1"> The prince who is weak in understanding is great in oppression; But he who hateth unjust gain shall prolong his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 1 Kings 21:19; Exodus 21:14"> A man who is burdened with life-blood—Let him flee to the pit! let no man stay him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Proverbs 28:6; Revelation 3:3"> He who walketh uprightly shall be safe; But he who is perverse in his ways shall fall at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 12:11; Judges 9:4; Proverbs 27:23–27"> He who tilleth his land shall have bread enough; And he that followeth after worthless persons shall have poverty enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 16:10–12; Proverbs 13:11; Revelation 2:10"> A faithful man shall abound with blessings; But he that maketh haste to be rich shall not go unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; Proverbs 18:5; Ezekiel 13:19"> To have respect to persons is not good; Since for a piece of bread that man will transgress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; 1 Timothy 6:9; Proverbs 28:20"> He who hath an evil eye hasteth after wealth, And considereth not that poverty will come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalms 141:5"> He who rebuketh a man shall afterwards find favor More than he who flattereth with his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:4–6; Proverbs 18:9; Proverbs 19:26"> Whoso stealeth from his father or his mother, And saith, “It is no transgression,” The same is the companion of a robber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 11:25; 1 Timothy 6:6; Proverbs 13:10"> He who is of a proud heart stirreth up strife; But he that trusteth in the LORD shall be rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> He who trusteth in his own understanding is a fool; But he who walketh wisely shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Deuteronomy 15:7; Psalms 41:1–3"> He who giveth to the poor shall not want; But he that hideth his eyes shall have many a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 29:2; Job 24:4; Acts 12:23–24"> When the wicked are exalted, men hide themselves; But, when they perish, the righteous increase.</span>
