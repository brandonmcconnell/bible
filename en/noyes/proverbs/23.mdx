<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:12; Genesis 43:32–34"> When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, Consider well what is before thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Matthew 18:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> For thou wilt put a knife to thy throat, If thou art a man given to appetite!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 23:6; Psalms 141:4; Ephesians 4:22"> Long not for his dainties. For they are deceitful meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; Proverbs 28:20; Matthew 6:19"> Toil not to become rich; Cease from this, thy wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 27:24; 1 John 2:16; James 5:1–2"> Wilt thou let thine eyes fly toward them? They are gone! For riches truly make to themselves wings; They fly away like the eagle toward heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Proverbs 23:3; Daniel 1:8–10"> Eat not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, And long not for his dainties;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Psalms 55:21; Judges 16:15"> For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he. “Eat and drink!” saith he to thee; But his heart is not with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The morsel, which thou hast eaten, thou shalt vomit up; And thou wilt have thrown away thy sweet words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Matthew 7:6; Proverbs 26:4–5"> Speak not in the ears of a fool; For he will despise the wisdom of thy words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 22:28; Zechariah 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:17"> Remove not the ancient landmark, And enter not into the fields of the fatherless!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Job 19:25; Jeremiah 51:36"> For their avenger is mighty; He will maintain their cause against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1–2; Proverbs 2:2–6; Proverbs 23:19"> Apply thy heart to instruction, And thine ears to the words of knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 19:18; Proverbs 29:15"> Withhold not correction from a child; If thou beat him with the rod, he will not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:5; Proverbs 22:15; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> Beat him thyself with the rod, And thou shalt rescue him from the underworld.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24–25; 2 John 1:4; Proverbs 29:3"> My son, if thy heart be wise, My heart shall rejoice, even mine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6; Ephesians 5:4; James 3:2"> Yea, my reins shall exult, When thy lips speak right things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:17"> Let not thy heart envy sinners, But continue thou in the fear of the LORD all the day long;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Jeremiah 29:11; Psalms 9:18"> For surely there shall be a reward, And thine expectation shall not be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26; Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 4:10–23"> Hear thou, my son, and be wise; And let thy heart go forward in the way!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:22"> Be not thou among winebibbers, And riotous eaters of flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 21:17; Proverbs 19:15; Deuteronomy 21:20"> For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty, And drowsiness will clothe a man with rags.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 1:8; John 19:26–27"> Hearken to thy father, who begat thee, And despise not thy mother when she is old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 18:15; Matthew 13:44; Psalms 119:72"> Buy the truth, and sell it not; Buy wisdom and instruction and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20; Proverbs 23:15–16"> The father of a righteous man shall greatly rejoice; Yea, he who begetteth a wise child shall have joy in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 17:25; Luke 11:27–28; Luke 1:58"> Let thy father and thy mother have joy; Yea, let her that bore thee rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Psalms 119:9–11; Deuteronomy 6:5"> My son, give me thy heart, And let thine eyes observe my ways!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14"> For a harlot is a deep ditch; Yea, a strange woman is a narrow pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 7:12; Proverbs 22:14"> Like a robber she lieth in wait, And increaseth the treacherous among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 23:21"> Who hath woe? Who hath sorrow? Who contentions? Who anxiety? Who wounds without cause? Who dimness of eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 5:18; Isaiah 5:11; Proverbs 20:1"> They that tarry long at the wine; They that go in to seek mixed wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:16; 2 Samuel 11:2; Psalms 119:37"> Look not thou upon the wine when it is red, When it sparkleth in the cup, When it goeth down smoothly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; Isaiah 28:7–8; Amos 9:3"> At the last it biteth like a serpent, And stingeth like an adder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Psalms 69:12; Daniel 5:4"> Thine eyes will look upon strange women, And thy heart will utter perverse things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–7; 1 Kings 20:16–22; 1 Samuel 30:16–17"> Yea, thou shalt be as one that lieth down in the midst of the sea, And as one that lieth down upon the top of a mast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; Jeremiah 5:3; 2 Peter 2:22"> They have stricken me &#91;&#91;shalt thou say&#93;&#93;,—I suffered no pain! They have beaten me,—I felt it not! When shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.</span>
