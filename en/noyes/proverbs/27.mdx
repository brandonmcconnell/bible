<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 4:13–16; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> Boast not thyself of to-morrow; For thou knowest not what a day may bring forth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:18; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Corinthians 10:12"> Let another man praise thee, and not thine own mouth; A stranger, and not thine own lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; 1 John 3:12; Genesis 49:7"> A stone is heavy and sand is weighty; But a fool's wrath is heavier than both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Proverbs 14:30; James 3:14–16"> Wrath is cruel, and anger overwhelming; But who is able to stand before jealousy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:23; 1 Timothy 5:20; Galatians 2:14"> Better is open rebuke Than love kept concealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19; Job 5:17–18"> Faithful are the wounds of a friend; But the kisses of an enemy are deceitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:16–17; Numbers 11:18–20; Numbers 21:5"> He who is fed to the full loatheth the honeycomb; But to the hungry any bitter thing is sweet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:16; Isaiah 16:2; Jonah 1:10–17"> As a bird that wandereth from its nest, So is a man who wandereth from his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23–24; Proverbs 16:21; Psalms 23:5"> Oil and perfume gladden the heart; Sweet also is one's friend by hearty counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Proverbs 17:17; 1 Kings 12:6–8"> Thine own friend and thy father's friend forsake not; And go not into thy brother's house in the day of thy calamity. Better is a neighbor that is near, than a brother far off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Psalms 119:42; Proverbs 23:15–16"> Be wise, my son, and make my heart glad, That I may give an answer to him that reproacheth me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 22:3; Proverbs 18:10; Hebrews 11:7"> A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself; The simple pass on, and are punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:16; Proverbs 6:1–4; Proverbs 22:26–27"> Take his garment who is surety for another; Yea, take a pledge of him who is bound for a stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:16–19; Acts 12:22–23; Jeremiah 28:2–4"> He who blesseth his neighbor with a loud voice, rising early for it, It shall be accounted to him as a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 19:13; Proverbs 21:19"> A continual dropping in a very rainy day And a quarrelsome wife are alike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 12:3"> He who restraineth her restraineth the wind; And his right hand layeth hold of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:24; Proverbs 27:9; Job 4:3–4"> Iron sharpeneth iron; So one man sharpeneth the face of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:7; 2 Timothy 2:6; Song of Solomon 8:12"> He that watcheth the fig-tree shall eat its fruit; So he that is careful for his master shall come to honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:22–25; Psalms 33:15; Mark 7:21"> As in water face answereth to face, So doth the heart of man to man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:8; 1 John 2:16; Proverbs 30:15–16"> The realms of the dead are never full; So the eyes of man are never satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 17:3; Zechariah 13:9; Psalms 66:10"> The refining-pot is for silver, and the furnace for gold; So let a man be to the mouth that giveth him praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Proverbs 23:35; Isaiah 1:5"> Though thou shouldst beat a fool in a mortar, Among bruised wheat, with a pestle, Yet will not his folly depart from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2; John 21:15–17; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, And look well to thy herds!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 23:5; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; Zephaniah 1:18"> For riches last not for ever; Not even a crown endureth from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Psalms 104:14"> The hay disappeareth, and the tender grass showeth itself, And the herbage of the mountains is gathered in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 31:20"> The lambs are thy clothing, And the goats the price of thy field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 30:8–9"> There is goat's milk enough for thy food, For the food of thy household, And for the sustenance of thy maidens.</span>
