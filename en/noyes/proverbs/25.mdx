<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 4:32; Micah 1:1"> These also are proverbs of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah, king of Judah, collected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Romans 11:33–34; Job 11:7–8"> It is the glory of God to conceal a thing; But it is the glory of kings to search out a matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; Psalms 103:11; 1 Kings 4:29"> As the heavens for their height, And as the earth for its depth, So is the heart of kings unsearchable!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:20–21; Ezekiel 22:18; Proverbs 17:3"> Take away the dross from the silver, And there will come forth a vessel for the founder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Proverbs 20:8; Psalms 101:7–8"> Take away the wicked man from the presence of the king, And his throne will be established by righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 25:27; Proverbs 16:19"> Put not thyself forth in the presence of the king, Nor set thyself in the place of the great;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; Luke 18:14; 1 Peter 5:5"> For better is it that one should say to thee, “Come up hither!” Than that he should put thee in a lower place, In the presence of the prince whom thine eyes behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 17:14; Proverbs 18:6"> Go not forth hastily to engage in a suit, Lest thou know not what to do in the end of it, When thine adversary hath put thee to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 18:5–17; Proverbs 11:13; Proverbs 20:19"> Maintain thy cause with thine adversary, But reveal not another's secret;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 119:39"> Lest he that heareth it put thee to shame, And thy infamy depart not from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; Ecclesiastes 12:10"> A word spoken in season Is like apples of gold in figured-work of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 15:5"> As a ring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold, So is a wise reprover to an attentive ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 13:17; Proverbs 25:25; Philippians 2:25–30"> As the cold of snow in the time of harvest, So is a faithful messenger to them that send him; For he refresheth the spirit of his masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:6; Luke 14:11; 2 Peter 2:15–19"> As clouds and wind without rain, So is the man that boasteth falsely of giving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Proverbs 16:14"> By long forbearing is a prince appeased; And a soft tongue breaketh bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 25:27; Ephesians 5:18; Proverbs 24:13–14"> Hast thou found honey? eat what is sufficient for thee, Lest thou be surfeited with it, and vomit it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 19:2–3; Judges 19:18–21; Romans 15:24"> Let thy foot be seldom in the house of thy friend, Lest he be surfeited with thee and hate thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 57:4; Proverbs 12:18; James 3:6"> A battle-hammer, and a sword, and a sharp arrow, Is the man who beareth false witness against his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Job 6:14–20"> As a broken tooth, and a wavering foot, So is trust in an unfaithful man in time of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Ecclesiastes 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> As he that taketh off a garment on a cold day, As vinegar upon nitre, So is he that singeth songs to a heavy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Matthew 5:44; Exodus 23:4–5"> If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat; And if he be thirsty, give him water to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Matthew 10:13; 1 Corinthians 15:18"> For thou wilt heap coals of fire upon his head, And the LORD will reward thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 37:22; Psalms 15:3; Romans 1:30"> As the north wind bringeth forth rain, So a backbiting tongue maketh an angry countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 21:19; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 27:15–16"> Better is it to dwell in a corner of the housetop, Than with a quarrelsome woman in a large house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:30; Romans 10:15; Psalms 63:1"> As cold water to the thirsty, So is good news from a far country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:18–19; 1 Samuel 22:14–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15"> As a troubled fountain, and as a corrupted spring, So is a righteous man falling before the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 5:44; Proverbs 27:2; Philippians 2:3"> To eat much honey is not good; So the search of high things is weariness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 22:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> As a city broken through and without a wall, So is he that hath no rule over his spirit.</span>
