<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Hosea 12:7–8; Ecclesiastes 1:10"> “Bad! bad!” saith the buyer; But when he hath gone his way, then he boasteth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 8:11; Proverbs 25:12; Proverbs 16:24"> There is gold and abundance of pearls; But the lips of knowledge are a precious vase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:13; Exodus 22:26–27; Proverbs 22:26–27"> Take his garment who is surety for another; Yea, take a pledge of him who is bound for a stranger.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:13; Romans 16:18; Proverbs 13:3"> He who goeth about as a tale-bearer revealeth secrets; Therefore associate not with him who keepeth open his lips.</span>
