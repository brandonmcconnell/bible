<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 18:16; Proverbs 17:8"> Many are they who caress the noble, And every one is the friend of him who giveth gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:4; 1 John 3:17–18; James 2:15–16"> All the brethren of the poor man hate him; How much more do his friends go far from him! He runneth after their words,—they are gone!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 16:20; Proverbs 8:35–36; Proverbs 3:18"> He that getteth wisdom loveth himself; He that keepeth understanding shall find good.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 30:21–22; Proverbs 26:1; Luke 16:19"> Luxury is not seemly for a fool; Much less should a servant have rule over princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Proverbs 14:29; James 1:19"> A man of understanding is slow to anger; Yea, it is his glory to pass over an offence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 14:5; Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 16:14–15"> The wrath of a king is like the roaring of a lion; But his favor, like dew upon the grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:15; Proverbs 21:9; Proverbs 10:1"> A foolish son is a calamity to his father, And the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:22; Proverbs 31:10–31; Proverbs 13:22"> Houses and riches are an inheritance from fathers; But a prudent wife is from the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:21; Proverbs 20:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep, And the idle person shall suffer hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:28; Proverbs 13:13; Luke 11:28"> He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his life; But he that neglecteth his ways shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 28:27; Hebrews 6:10; Luke 6:38"> He who hath pity on the poor lendeth to the LORD, And that which he giveth will he repay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 13:24; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 22:15"> Chasten thy son because there is hope, But let not thy soul desire to slay him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 25:28; 2 Samuel 16:5–6; 1 Samuel 24:17–22"> A man of great wrath will suffer punishment; For if thou deliver him, yet must thou do it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 12:15; Proverbs 8:34–35; Proverbs 2:1–9"> Listen to counsel and receive instruction, That thou mayst be wise in thy latter years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; Isaiah 14:24; Proverbs 16:1"> Many are the devices in the heart of a man; But the purpose of the LORD, that shall stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 14:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Chronicles 6:8"> The charm of a man is his kindness; And better is a poor man than a liar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Proverbs 14:26–27; Proverbs 12:21"> The fear of the LORD tendeth to life, And he that hath it shall abide satisfied; He shall not be visited with evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:19; Proverbs 6:9–10; Proverbs 24:30–34"> The slothful man dippeth his hand into the dish: He doth not bring it back even to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 21:11; Proverbs 17:10; Proverbs 15:5"> Strike the scoffer, and the simple will become prudent; Reprove a man of understanding, and he will discern knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Proverbs 17:2; Proverbs 28:24"> The son that causeth shame and disgrace doeth violence to his father, And chaseth away his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 4:24; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Matthew 7:15"> Cease, my son, to listen to the instruction That causeth thee to wander from the words of knowledge!</span>
