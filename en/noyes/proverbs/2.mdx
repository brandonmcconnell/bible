<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 3:1; Proverbs 1:3; Proverbs 4:1"> Oh, my son, that thou wouldst receive my words, And treasure up my precepts within thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 18:1; Psalms 119:111–112"> That thou wouldst apply thine ear to wisdom, And incline thy heart to understanding!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:5; Ephesians 1:17–18; Proverbs 3:6"> For if thou wilt call aloud to knowledge, And lift up thy voice to understanding,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Proverbs 16:16; Matthew 19:29"> If thou wilt seek her as silver, And search for her as for hidden treasures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Jeremiah 24:7; Jeremiah 32:40–41"> Then shalt thou understand the fear of the LORD, And find the knowledge of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; James 1:5; Job 32:8"> For the LORD giveth wisdom; From his mouth proceed knowledge and understanding:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 3:15–17; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 84:11"> He layeth up safety for the righteous; He is a shield to them that walk uprightly:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; John 10:28–29"> He guardeth the paths of equity, And defendeth the way of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Psalms 143:8–10; Matthew 7:13–14"> Then shalt thou understand righteousness and equity And uprightness, yea, every good path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Colossians 3:16; Job 23:12"> When wisdom entereth into thy heart, And knowledge is pleasant to thy soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6; Ephesians 5:15; Psalms 25:21"> Discretion will guard thee, Understanding will preserve thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 13:20"> It will deliver thee from the way of the wicked, From the men who speak perverse things;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Proverbs 21:16"> Who forsake the paths of uprightness, To walk in the ways of darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Proverbs 10:23; Jeremiah 11:15"> Who rejoice in doing evil, And delight in the perverseness of the wicked;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 125:5; Proverbs 21:8; Philippians 2:15"> Whose paths are crooked, And who are froward in their ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 23:27"> It will deliver thee from the wife of another, From the stranger, who useth smooth words;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:59–60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 3:4"> Who forsaketh the friend of her youth, And forgetteth the covenant of her God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 9:18; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Proverbs 5:4–14"> For her house sinketh down to Death, And her paths to the shades of the dead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 81:12; Hosea 4:14"> None that go to her return again; They will not attain the paths of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 119:63"> Therefore walk thou in the way of good men, And keep the paths of the righteous:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 37:29; Psalms 37:9"> For the upright shall dwell in the land, And the righteous shall remain in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 52:5; Deuteronomy 7:22; Isaiah 3:10–11"> But the wicked shall be cut off from the land, And transgressors shall be rooted out of it.</span>
