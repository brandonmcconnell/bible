<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 16:18; Revelation 3:12; 1 Kings 7:2–3"> Wisdom hath builded her house; She hath hewn out her seven pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 22:3–14; Isaiah 25:6; Luke 14:16–17"> She hath killed her fatlings; She hath mingled her wine; Yea, she hath furnished her table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 9:14; Proverbs 8:1–3; Matthew 22:3–4"> She hath sent forth her maidens; She crieth aloud upon the highest places of the city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 9:16; Proverbs 8:5"> “Whoever is simple, let him turn in hither!” To him that is void of understanding she saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 6:27; Song of Solomon 5:1; Proverbs 9:17"> “Come, eat of my bread, And drink of the wine which I have mingled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Proverbs 4:11; Luke 13:24"> Forsake folly, and live! And go forward in the way of understanding!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:12; Proverbs 23:9; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> “He who correcteth a scoffer Bringeth shame upon himself; And he who rebuketh the wicked Bringeth upon himself a stain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:5; Proverbs 13:18; Proverbs 23:9"> Rebuke not a scoffer, lest he hate thee; Rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5; Matthew 13:11–12; 1 John 5:13"> Give instruction to a wise man, and he will be yet wiser; Teach a righteous man, and he will increase his learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 111:10; Job 28:28; Proverbs 1:7"> The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, And the knowledge of the Most Holy is understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Proverbs 3:16; Proverbs 3:2"> Yea, through me thy days shall be multiplied, And the years of thy life shall be increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 22:2–3; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ezekiel 18:20"> If thou art wise, thou art wise for thyself; And if thou art a scoffer, thou alone must bear it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 7:11; Proverbs 5:6; 1 Timothy 6:4"> The foolish woman is clamorous; She is very simple, and careth for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Proverbs 7:10–12"> She sitteth at the door of her house, Upon a seat in the high places of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25–27; Proverbs 23:27–28; Proverbs 7:13–15"> To call aloud to those that pass by, Who go straight forward in their ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 9:4"> “Whoever is simple, let him turn in hither!” And to him that is void of understanding she saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:17; Proverbs 23:31–32; James 1:14–15"> “Stolen water is sweet, And bread eaten in secret is pleasant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19; Proverbs 5:5"> But he considereth not that the dead are there, That in the vales of the under-world are her guests.</span>
