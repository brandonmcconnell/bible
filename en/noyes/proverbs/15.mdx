<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:12; Proverbs 25:15; Proverbs 15:18"> A soft answer turneth away wrath; But harsh words stir up anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 12:23; Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 13:16"> The tongue of the wise maketh knowledge pleasing; But the mouth of fools poureth forth folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Jeremiah 16:17; Jeremiah 23:24"> The eyes of the LORD are in every place; They behold the evil and the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 16:24; Proverbs 18:8"> A mild tongue is a tree of life; But perverseness therein is a wound in the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Proverbs 13:1; Proverbs 13:18"> The fool despiseth the correction of his father; But he that regardeth reproof is prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:21; Proverbs 21:20; Proverbs 10:22"> In the house of the righteous is much wealth; But in the revenues of the wicked there is trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; James 3:6; Ephesians 4:29"> The lips of the wise spread abroad knowledge; But the heart of the foolish is not sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Proverbs 21:27; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD; But the prayer of the righteous is his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Proverbs 21:21; Matthew 7:13"> The way of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD; But him who followeth after righteousness he loveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; John 3:20; Proverbs 13:1"> Sore chastisement shall be to him that forsaketh the way; He that hateth reproof shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 44:21; Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 139:8"> The underworld, yea, the region of death, is before the LORD; How much more the hearts of the sons of men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Job 21:14; Proverbs 9:7–8"> The scoffer loveth not his reprover; He will not resort to the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 17:22; John 14:1; Proverbs 18:14"> A joyous heart maketh a bright countenance; But by sorrow of the heart the spirit is broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 9:9; Isaiah 30:10; Proverbs 18:15"> The heart of the man of understanding seeketh knowledge; But the mouth of fools feedeth on folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:10; Romans 12:12; Proverbs 15:13"> The days of the afflicted are all evil; But he that hath a cheerful heart hath a continual feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Psalms 37:16; Ecclesiastes 5:10–12"> Better is a little, with the fear of the LORD, Than much treasure, and trouble therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 17:1; Proverbs 21:19; 1 John 4:16"> Better is a dinner of herbs, where there is love, Than a fatted ox, and hatred therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 26:21; Proverbs 29:22"> The passionate man stirreth up strife; But he who is slow to anger appeaseth strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:8; Proverbs 22:5; Isaiah 30:21"> The way of the slothful is as a hedge of thorns; But the way of the righteous is a highway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 23:15–16"> A wise son maketh a glad father; But a foolish man despiseth his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 14:16"> Folly is joy to him who lacketh wisdom; But the man of understanding walketh uprightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:14; Proverbs 20:18; Ecclesiastes 8:6"> Without counsel, plans come to nought; But with a multitude of counsellors they are established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Proverbs 25:11–12; Ephesians 4:29"> A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth; And a word in due season, how good it is!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–2; Psalms 16:11; Matthew 7:14"> The path of life is upward for the wise, So that he turneth away from the underworld beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5–6; Proverbs 14:11"> The LORD destroyeth the house of the proud; But he will establish the border of the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremiah 4:14; Proverbs 15:23"> Evil devices are an abomination to the LORD; But pleasant words are pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 1:19; Jeremiah 17:11"> He who is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; But he who hateth bribes shall live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 29:11; Ecclesiastes 5:2"> The heart of the righteous meditateth on his answer; But the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 9:31; Psalms 145:18–19; Proverbs 15:8"> The LORD is far from the wicked: But he heareth the prayer of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:23"> The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart, And good tidings make the bones fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:5; Proverbs 25:12; John 15:3–4"> The ear that hearkeneth to the reproof of life Shall dwell among the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–33; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:7"> He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own life; But he that hearkeneth to rebuke getteth understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Proverbs 18:12; Proverbs 1:7"> The fear of the LORD guideth to wisdom, And before honor is humility.</span>
