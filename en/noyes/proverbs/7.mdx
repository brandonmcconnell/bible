<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:8; Revelation 1:3; Luke 11:28"> My son, keep my words, And treasure up my commandments with thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Proverbs 4:4; Leviticus 18:5"> Keep my commandments and live! Yea, my teaching, as the apple of thine eye!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:18–20; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 6:8–9"> Bind them upon thy fingers, Write them upon the tablet of thy heart!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6–8; Proverbs 2:2–4; Song of Solomon 8:1"> Say unto wisdom, “Thou art my sister!” And call understanding thy near acquaintance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 5:3"> That they may keep thee from the wife of another, From the stranger, that useth smooth words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 26:8; Judges 5:28; 2 Samuel 6:16"> For through the window of my house, Through the lattice I was looking forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 1:32"> And I saw among the simple ones, I discerned among the youths, A young man void of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:8; Proverbs 7:12; Judges 16:1"> He was passing through the street near her corner, And was going the way to her house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:13–15; Romans 13:12–14; Ephesians 5:11"> At twilight, in the evening, At midnight, yea, in the thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Isaiah 3:16–24; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> And, behold, a woman met him, In the attire of a harlot, and subtle of heart,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 9:13; Titus 2:5; Proverbs 31:10–31"> One noisy and unruly, Whose feet abide not in her house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:28; Proverbs 9:14; Revelation 18:3"> Who is now in the streets, now in the broad places, And lurketh near every corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 39:12; Numbers 25:6–8; Genesis 39:7"> She caught hold of him and kissed him, And with a shameless face said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 21:27; Leviticus 7:11; Proverbs 17:1"> “Thank-offerings have been upon me, And this day have I performed my vows;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore came I forth to meet thee,—Diligently to seek thy face, and I have found thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:9; Ezekiel 27:7; 1 Kings 10:28"> I have spread my bed with coverlets, With tapestry of the thread of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Isaiah 57:7–9; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> I have sprinkled my bed With myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning; Let us solace ourselves with caresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:11; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:39"> For the good-man is not at home; He is gone a long journey;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4"> He hath taken a purse of money with him; At the day of the full moon he will return.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 5:3; Proverbs 7:5; 2 Kings 4:8"> By her much fair speech she seduced him; By the smoothness of her lips she drew him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 13:27; Acts 16:24; Acts 14:13"> He goeth after her straightway, As an ox goeth to the slaughter, Or as one in fetters to the chastisement of the fool,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Proverbs 9:18; Proverbs 1:17"> Till an arrow strike through his liver;—As a bird hasteneth into the snare, And knoweth not that it is laid for its life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 5:7; Proverbs 4:1; 1 John 2:1"> Now, therefore, ye children, hearken to me, And attend to the words of my mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 5:8; Isaiah 53:6; Proverbs 6:25"> Let not thy heart turn aside to her ways; Go not astray in her paths!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Nehemiah 13:26; 1 Kings 11:1–2"> For many are the wounded which she hath cast down; Yea, countless is the number of those slain by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 5:5; Proverbs 9:18; Proverbs 2:18–19"> Her house is the way to the under-world, Leading down to the chambers of death.</span>
