<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 22:17; Proverbs 4:20; Proverbs 2:1"> My son, attend to my wisdom. And bow thine ear to my understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23; Proverbs 15:7; Malachi 2:6–7"> That thou mayest keep discretion, And that thy lips may preserve knowledge!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 7:5"> Truly, the lips of a strange woman drop honey, And her mouth is smoother than oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 55:21"> But her end is bitter as wormwood, Sharp as a two-edged sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 7:27; Proverbs 2:18–19"> Her feet go down to death; Her steps lay hold of the under-world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26; Proverbs 5:21; Psalms 16:11"> That she may not ponder the way of life, Her paths waver when she heedeth it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1; Proverbs 7:24; Proverbs 22:17–21"> Hear me now, therefore, O children! And turn not away from the words of my mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 7:25; Ephesians 5:11; Matthew 6:13"> Remove thy way far from her. And come not nigh the door of her house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:26; Hosea 4:13–14; Genesis 38:23–26"> Lest thou give thy bloom to others, And thy years to a cruel one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Proverbs 6:35; Proverbs 31:3"> Lest strangers be filled with thy wealth, And thine earnings be in the house of an alien;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:21; Revelation 22:15; Hebrews 13:4"> And lest thou mourn in thy latter end. When thy flesh and thy body are consumed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 15:5"> And say, “How have I hated instruction! And how hath my heart despised reproof!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:8; Luke 15:18; Hebrews 13:7"> I have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, Nor inclined mine ear to my instructors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Peter 2:10–18; 1 Corinthians 10:6–8"> I have well-nigh fallen into utter misery; In the midst of the congregation and the assembly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2–5; Hebrews 13:4; Proverbs 5:18–19"> Drink water out of thine own cistern. And running water out of thine own well:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 48:21"> So shall thy fountains overflow in the streets, In the wide streets, as streams of water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They shall belong to thee alone, And not to strangers with thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:9; Malachi 2:14–15; Song of Solomon 4:15"> And thy fountain shall be blessed, Yea, thou shalt have joy in the wife of thy youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:3; Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 4:5"> A lovely hind, a graceful doe, Her breasts shall satisfy thee at all times, And thou shalt be always ravished with her love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 7:5; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 23:33"> Why, then, my son, wilt thou be ravished with a wanton, And embrace the bosom of a stranger?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Proverbs 15:3; Job 34:21"> For the ways of man are before the eyes of the LORD, And he weigheth well all his paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 11:5; Psalms 9:15; Proverbs 1:18"> His own iniquities shall ensnare the wicked; Yea, he shall be held fast by the cords of his own sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 36:12; Job 4:21; Proverbs 10:21"> He shall die for want of instruction; Yea, through the greatness of his folly he shall stagger.</span>
