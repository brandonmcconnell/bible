<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 7:4; Deuteronomy 4:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:15–17"> That one may learn wisdom and instruction, And receive words of understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–9; Job 22:22; Proverbs 8:10–11"> That one may gain the instruction of prudence, Justice, equity, and uprightness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 8:5; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 119:9"> Which will give caution to the simple, To the young man wisdom and discretion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 12:1; Proverbs 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> Let the wise man hear, and he will increase his knowledge, And the man of understanding will gain wise counsels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:2; Mark 4:11; Psalms 49:4"> So as to understand a proverb and a deep maxim, The words of the wise and their dark sayings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10–1; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge; Fools despise wisdom and instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:20; Proverbs 30:17; Proverbs 5:1–2"> Hear, O my son! the instruction of thy father, And neglect not the teaching of thy mother!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> For they shall be a graceful wreath for thy head, And a chain around thy neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Proverbs 16:29; Psalms 1:1"> My son, if sinners entice thee, Consent thou not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26; Proverbs 1:18; Proverbs 12:6"> If they say, “Come with us, Let us lie in wait for blood, Let us lurk secretly for him who is innocent in vain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 124:3; Psalms 28:1; Romans 3:13"> Let us swallow them up alive, like the underworld, Yea, in full health, as those that go down into the pit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15; Job 24:2–3"> We shall find all kinds of precious substance, We shall fill our houses with spoil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Thou shalt cast thy lot among us; We will all have one purse;”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> My son, walk thou not in their way, Refrain thy foot from their path!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:18; Proverbs 4:16"> For their feet run to evil, And make haste to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:11; Proverbs 7:23; Jeremiah 8:7"> For as the net is spread in vain Before the eyes of any bird,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Esther 7:10; Proverbs 5:22–23"> So they lie in wait for their own blood; They lurk secretly for their own lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 2 Peter 2:3; James 5:1–4"> Such are the ways of every one greedy of unjust gain; It taketh away the life of the possessor thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 9:3; Colossians 2:3; Luke 11:49"> Wisdom crieth out in the highway; In the market-place she uttereth her voice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:20; Proverbs 9:3; Matthew 13:2"> At the head of the noisy streets she crieth aloud; At the entrances of the gates, throughout the city, she proclaimeth her words &#91;&#91;saying&#93;&#93;:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 1:29; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 5:12"> How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity? How long will scoffers delight themselves in scoffing, And fools hate knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Ezekiel 33:11; Ezekiel 18:27–30"> Turn ye at my reproof! Behold, I will pour out my spirit to you; I will make known my words to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Romans 10:21; Jeremiah 7:13"> “Because I have called, and ye have refused,—Because I have stretched out my hand, and no one hath regarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> Because ye have rejected all my counsel, And have slighted my rebuke,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> I also will laugh at your calamity, I will mock when your fear cometh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> When your fear cometh upon you like a storm. And destruction overtaketh you like a whirlwind, When distress and anguish come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; James 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> Then will they call upon me, but I will not answer! They will seek me early, But they shall not find me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> Because they have hated knowledge, And have not chosen the fear of the LORD,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Proverbs 1:25; Luke 14:18–20"> Because they would not attend to my counsel, And have despised all my reproof,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 14:14; Jeremiah 6:19; Proverbs 22:8"> Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way, And be filled to the full with their own devices;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 8:36; Jeremiah 2:19; John 3:36"> Yea, the turning away of the simple shall slay them, And the carelessness of fools shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 25:12–13; Psalms 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> But whoso hearkeneth to me shall dwell securely, And shall not be disquieted with the fear of evil.”</span>
