<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:13–16; Proverbs 16:11; Proverbs 20:23"> False scales are an abomination to the LORD; But a perfect weight is his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:23; Proverbs 15:33; Proverbs 16:18–19"> When pride cometh, then cometh disgrace; But with the humble is wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:6; Proverbs 28:18; Psalms 26:1"> The integrity of the upright shall guide them; But the perverseness of transgressors shall destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Proverbs 10:2; Genesis 7:1"> Riches do not profit in the day of wrath; But righteousness delivereth from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:22; Psalms 9:15–16; Proverbs 1:31–32"> The righteousness of the good man maketh his way plain; But the wicked falleth through his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:8; 1 Kings 2:44; Psalms 7:15–16"> The righteousness of the upright delivereth them; But transgressors are ensnared in their own mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Job 8:13–14; Job 11:20"> When the wicked man dieth, his hope cometh to an end; Yea, the expectation of the unjust cometh to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 6:23–24; Proverbs 21:18; Esther 7:9–10"> The righteous man is delivered from trouble, And the wicked cometh into it in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10–16; Proverbs 4:5–6; 1 John 2:27"> By his mouth the vile man destroyeth his neighbor; But by the knowledge of the righteous are men delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Esther 8:15–16; Psalms 58:10–11"> When it goeth well with the righteous, the city rejoiceth; And when the wicked perish, there is shouting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:34; Proverbs 29:8; James 3:6"> By the blessing of the upright the city is exalted; But it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 10:19; 1 Peter 2:23; Luke 18:9"> He who despiseth his neighbor is void of understanding; A man of discernment holdeth his peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; Proverbs 25:9; Leviticus 19:16"> He who goeth about as a tale-bearer revealeth secrets; But he who is of a faithful spirit concealeth a matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 15:22; Proverbs 20:18"> Where there is no counsel, the people fall; But in a multitude of counsellors there is safety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 17:18; Proverbs 6:1–5; Proverbs 22:26–27"> He that is surety for another shall smart for it; But he that hateth suretyship is sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 31:30–31; Luke 10:42; Acts 16:14–15"> A graceful woman obtaineth honor, Even as strong men obtain riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 25:34–40; James 2:13"> He that doeth good to himself is a man of kindness; But he that tormenteth his own flesh is cruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 6:8–9; Ecclesiastes 10:8; Hosea 10:12–13"> The wicked toileth for deceitful wages; But he who soweth righteousness shall have a sure reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 10:16; Romans 2:8–9; Proverbs 12:28"> As righteousness tendeth to life, So he who pursueth evil pursueth it to his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:1; Proverbs 21:29; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> The perverse in heart are the abomination of the LORD; But the upright in their way are his delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–2; Acts 2:39; Jeremiah 32:39"> From generation to generation the wicked shall not go unpunished; But the posterity of the righteous shall be delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–4; Proverbs 31:30; Nahum 3:4–6"> As a jewel of gold in a swine's snout, So is a beautiful woman who is without discretion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Romans 2:8–9; Matthew 5:6"> The desire of the righteous is only good; But the expectation of the wicked is wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 6:38; Deuteronomy 15:10"> There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; And there is that withholdeth more than is right, yet he cometh to want.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 9:6–9; Isaiah 58:7–11"> The bountiful man shall be enriched, And he that watereth shall himself be watered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 29:13; Genesis 42:6; Amos 8:4–6"> Him that keepeth back corn the people curse; But blessing shall be upon the head of him that selleth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 17:11; Psalms 9:15–16"> He, who earnestly seeketh good, seeketh favor; But he that seeketh mischief, it shall come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Psalms 52:7–8"> He who trusteth in his riches shall fall; But the righteous shall flourish as a leaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:19; Proverbs 15:27"> He that harasseth his household shall inherit wind; And the fool shall be the servant of the wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; James 5:20; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> The fruit of a righteous man is a tree of life; And the wise man winneth souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Jeremiah 25:29; 1 Corinthians 11:30–32"> Behold, the righteous man is requited on the earth; Much more the wicked man and the sinner!</span>
