<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Song of Solomon 6:2; Song of Solomon 4:16; Revelation 22:17"> &#91;&#91;Lov.&#93;&#93; I am come to my garden, my sister, my spouse! I gather my myrrh with my balsam, I eat my honeycomb with my honey, I drink my wine with my milk. Eat, O friends! Drink, yea, drink abundantly, my loved companions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Song of Solomon 2:14; Song of Solomon 6:9"> &#91;&#91;M.&#93;&#93; I slept, but my heart was awake; It was the voice of my beloved, who was knocking: “Open to me, my sister, my love, My dove, my perfect one! For my head is filled with dew, And my locks with the drops of the night.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Romans 7:22–23; Proverbs 13:4"> “I have taken off my vest &#91;&#91;said I&#93;&#93;; How shall I put it on? I have washed my feet; How shall I soil them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:4; Genesis 43:30; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> My beloved put in his hand by the hole of the door, And my heart was moved for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:13; Ephesians 3:17; Song of Solomon 5:2"> I rose up to open to my beloved, And my hands dropped with myrrh, And my fingers with self-flowing myrrh, upon the handles of the bolt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Solomon 5:2; Revelation 3:19; Hosea 5:15"> I opened to my beloved; But my beloved had withdrawn himself, and was gone. I was not in my senses while he spake with me! I sought him, but could not find him; I called him, but he gave me no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:3; Matthew 21:33–41; Acts 5:40–41"> The watchmen that go about the city found me; They smote me, they wounded me; The keepers of the walls took away from me my veil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:7; Song of Solomon 2:5; Song of Solomon 3:5"> I charge you, O ye daughters of Jerusalem! If ye should find my beloved,—What will ye tell him? That I am sick with love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; Song of Solomon 6:1; Song of Solomon 6:9–10"> &#91;&#91;Lad.&#93;&#93; What is thy beloved more than another beloved, O thou fairest among women! What is thy beloved more than another beloved, That thus thou dost charge us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:2; Song of Solomon 2:1; Philippians 2:9–11"> &#91;&#91;M.&#93;&#93; My beloved is white and ruddy, The chief among ten thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:5; Song of Solomon 5:2; Daniel 7:9"> His head is as the most fine gold; His locks waving palm-branches, Black as a raven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 4:1; Song of Solomon 1:15; Hebrews 4:13"> His eyes are doves by streams of water, Washed with milk, dwelling in fulness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:10; Song of Solomon 2:1; Song of Solomon 6:2"> His cheeks are like a bed of balsam, Like beds of spices; His lips are lilies Dropping self-flowing myrrh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 54:11; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> His hands are gold rings set with chrysolite; His body is wrought-work of ivory, overlaid with sapphires;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 1:15–16; 1 Kings 4:33; Matthew 17:2"> His legs are marble pillars, resting on pedestals of fine gold; His aspect is like Lebanon, Majestic like the cedars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 45:2; Song of Solomon 7:9; Isaiah 9:6–7"> His mouth is sweetness; His whole being, loveliness. This is my beloved, This my friend, O ye daughters of Jerusalem!</span>
