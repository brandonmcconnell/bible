<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; John 15:9–15; Romans 5:8–10"> He hath brought me to his banqueting-house, And his banner over me is love. Strengthen me with raisins,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Isaiah 54:5–10; Jeremiah 32:41"> His left hand is under my head, And his right hand embraceth me!</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:27; Revelation 3:20; John 3:29"> &#91;&#91;M.&#93;&#93; The voice of my beloved! Behold, he cometh, Leaping upon the mountains, Bounding upon the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:14; Song of Solomon 2:17; John 5:39"> Like a gazelle is my beloved, Or a young hind. Behold, he standeth behind our wall; He is looking through the windows; He glanceth through the lattice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:13; Song of Solomon 4:7–8; Revelation 22:17"> My beloved speaketh, and saith to me, “Rise up, my love, my fair one, and come away!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–2; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Isaiah 54:6–8"> For, lo, the winter is past, The rain is over and gone;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 55:12; Colossians 3:16; Psalms 89:15"> The flowers appear on the earth; The time of the singing of birds is come, And the voice of the turtle is heard in our land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:10; Song of Solomon 7:11–13; Isaiah 55:10–11"> The fig-tree is spicing its green fruit; The vines in blossom give forth fragrance. Arise, my love, my fair one, and come away!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezra 9:5–6; Song of Solomon 5:2; Song of Solomon 1:5"> O my dove, that art in the recesses of the rock, In the hiding-places of the steep craggy mountain, Let me see thy face, Let me hear thy voice! For sweet is thy voice, And thy face lovely.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 13:32; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Song of Solomon 7:12"> Take ye for us the foxes, The little foxes that spoil the vines; For our vines are now in blossom.</span>
