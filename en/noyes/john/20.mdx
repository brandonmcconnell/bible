<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:9; Matthew 27:60; John 19:25"> But on the first day of the week Mary the Magdalene cometh early, while it was yet dark, to the tomb, and seeth that the stone had been taken away from the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:13; John 21:24"> She runneth therefore, and cometh to Simon Peter, and to the other disciple, whom Jesus loved, and saith to them, They have taken away the Lord out of the tomb, and we know not where they have laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:12"> Peter therefore went forth, and the other disciple, and they set out for the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> And they ran both together; and the other disciple outran Peter, and came first to the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:40; John 20:11; John 11:44"> And stooping down, he seeth the linen cloths lying; but he did not go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:17; John 21:7; Matthew 16:15–16"> Then cometh also Simon Peter following him, and he went into the tomb; and he beholdeth the linen cloths lying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 11:44"> and the napkin that was about his head, not lying with the linen cloths, but folded up in a place by itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:25; John 20:4; John 1:50"> Then therefore went in also the other disciple, who came first to the tomb; and he saw, and believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 25:8; Isaiah 53:10–12"> For not even yet did they know the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> So the disciples went away again to their home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:5"> But Mary was standing without by the tomb weeping. And as she wept, she stooped down into the tomb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> and beholdeth two angels in white sitting, one at the head, and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> And they say to her, Woman, why dost thou weep? She saith to them, Because they have taken away my Lord, and I know not where they have laid him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> Having said this, she turned back, and beheld Jesus standing; and knew not that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus saith to her, Woman, why dost thou weep? whom dost thou seek? She, supposing that it was the gardener, saith to him, Sir, if thou hast borne him hence, tell me where thou hast laid him, and I will take him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus saith to her, Mary! She turned and saith to him in Hebrew, Rabboni! Which signifieth, Teacher!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:28; Ephesians 4:8–10; Galatians 4:6–7"> Jesus saith to her, Touch me not; for I have not yet ascended to the Father; but go to my brethren, and say to them, I ascend to my Father and your Father, and my God and your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:10; Matthew 28:10; John 20:1"> Mary the Magdalene cometh, bringing word to the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that he had said these things to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> When therefore it was evening on that day, which was the first day of the week, and the doors where the disciples were had been shut for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the midst, and said to them, Peace be to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> And having said this, he showed them his hands and his side. The disciples therefore were glad, when they saw the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> Then he said to them again, Peace be to you! As the Father hath sent me, I also send you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 33:4; Genesis 2:7; Acts 8:15"> And having said this, he breathed on them, and saith to them, Receive the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Matthew 16:19; Mark 2:5–10"> Whosever sins ye remit, they are remitted to them; whosever ye retain, they are retained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> But Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> The other disciples therefore said to him, We have seen the Lord. But he said to them, Unless I see in his hands the print of the nails, and put my finger into the place of the nails, and put my hand into his side, I shall not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> And after eight days the disciples were again within, and Thomas with them. Jesus cometh, the doors being shut, and stood in the midst, and said, Peace be to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then he saith to Thomas, Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands, and reach forth thy hand, and put it into my side; and be not faithless, but believing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> Thomas answered and said to him, My Lord and my God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus saith to him, Because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed; blessed are they who have not seen, and yet have believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Many other signs did Jesus in the presence of his disciples, which are not written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> But these have been written, that ye may believe that Jesus is the Christ the Son of God, and that believing ye may have life in his name.</span>
