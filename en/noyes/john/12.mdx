<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Jesus, therefore, six days before the passover, came to Bethany, where Lazarus was, whom Jesus raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> They made therefore a supper for him there, and Martha served; but Lazarus was one of those who reclined at the table with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Then Mary, taking a pound of ointment of pure spikenard, very costly, anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair; and the house was filled with the odor of the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> But Judas Iscariot, one of his disciples, he who was about to betray him, saith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> Why was not this ointment sold for three hundred denaries, and given to the poor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> And this he said, not because he cared for the poor, but because he was a thief, and kept the purse, and bore what was put therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> Then said Jesus, Let her alone, that she may keep it until the day of my burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> For the poor ye have always with you, but me ye have not always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> The great multitude of the Jews therefore knew that he was there; and they came, not only on account of Jesus, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> But the priests consulted together that they might put Lazarus also to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> because on his account many of the Jews were going away, and believing in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the next day, a great multitude that had come to the feast, hearing that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> took branches of palm-trees, and went forth to meet him, crying aloud, Hosanna! blessed is he who cometh in the name of the Lord, even the king of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> And Jesus, having found a young ass, sat thereon, as it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> “Fear not, daughter of Zion! lo! thy king cometh, sitting on an ass's colt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> These things his disciples did not understand at the first; but when Jesus had been glorified, then they remembered that these things were written of him, and that they had done these things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> The multitude therefore that was with him when he called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from the dead bore witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> For this cause also the multitude met him, because they had heard that he had wrought, this sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, Ye see that ye effect nothing; o! the world hath gone after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Now there were certain Greeks among those who came up to worship at the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> These came therefore to Philip, who was of Bethsaida of Galilee, and asked him, saying, Sir, we wish to see Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip goeth and telleth Andrew; Andrew and Philip go and tell Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> But Jesus answereth them, saying, The hour hath come that the Son of man should be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> Truly, truly do I say to you, Unless a grain of wheat fall into the ground and die, itself abideth alone; but if it die, it beareth much fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> He that loveth his life loseth it; and he that hateth his life in this world will keep it unto everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> If any one be a servant to me, let him follow me; and where I am, there also will my servant be; if any one serve me, the Father will honor him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> Now is my soul troubled; and what shall I say? Father, save me from this hour! But for this cause I came to this hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, glorify thy name! Then came there a voice from heaven: Yea, I have glorified it, and will glorify it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> The multitude therefore that stood by, hearing this, said that it thundered. Others said, An angel hath spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered and said, This voice hath come not for my sake, but for yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> Now is the judgment of this world; now will the prince of this world be cast out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> and I, if I be lifted up from the earth, shall draw all men to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> This he said, signifying what kind of death he was to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The multitude therefore answered him, We have heard out of the Law that the Christ abideth for ever; how then dost thou say that the Son of man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Jesus therefore said to them, Yet a little while is the light among you. Walk while ye have the light, that darkness may not overtake you; and he that walketh in the darkness knoweth not whither he goeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> While ye have the light, believe in the light, that ye may become sons of light. These things spoke Jesus, and went away, and hid himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> But though he had wrought so many signs before them, they did not believe in him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> that what was spoken by Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, “Lord, who hath believed our report? and to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> For this cause they could not believe, because Isaiah said again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> “He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart; lest they should see with their eyes, and understand with their heart, and turn from their ways, and I should heal them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> These things said Isaiah, because he saw his glory, and spoke of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Yet even of the rulers many believed in him; but on account of the Pharisees they did not acknowledge him, lest they should be put of the synagogue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> for they loved the glory that is of men more than the glory that is of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> But Jesus cried aloud, and said, He that believeth in me, believeth not in me, but in him that sent me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> and he that beholdeth me, beholdeth him that sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> I have come a light into the world, that whoever believeth in me may not remain in the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> And if any one hear my words, and keep them not, I do not judge him; for I came not to judge the world, but to save the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> He that rejecteth me, and receiveth not my words, hath one that judgeth him; the word that I have spoken, that will judge him in the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> Because I have not spoken from myself; but the Father who sent me hath himself committed to me what I should say, and what I should speak;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> and I know that what he hath committed to me is everlasting life. What I speak therefore, I speak as the Father hath directed me.</span>
