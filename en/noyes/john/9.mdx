<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And as he was passing along, he saw a man who had been blind from his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> And his disciples asked him, saying, Rabbi, who sinned, this man, or his parents, that he was born blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus answered, Neither did this man sin, nor his parents; but that the works of God might be made manifest in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> We must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day; the night is coming, when no one can work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> Having thus spoken, he spat on the ground, and made clay with the spittle, and anointed his eyes with the clay,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> and said to him, Go, wash in the pool of Siloam; (which is, when translated, Sent.) He went away therefore, and washed and came seeing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> The neighbors therefore, and they who before had seen him as a beggar, said, Is not this he that sits and begs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some said, This is he. Others said, No, but he is like him. He said, I am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> They said therefore to him How then were thine eyes opened?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He answered, The man who is called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes, and said to me, Go to Siloam, and wash. I went therefore and washed, and received sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> And they said to him, Where is that man? He saith, I do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They bring to the Pharisees him who had once been blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> And the day on which Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes was the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> Again therefore the Pharisees also asked him how he received his sight. He said to them, He put clay on my eyes, and I washed them, and I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> Therefore some of the Pharisees said, This man is not from God, because he keepeth not the sabbath. Others said, How can a man that is a sinner do such signs. And there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> They say therefore to the blind man again, What dost thou say of him for having opened thine eyes? And he said, He is a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jews therefore did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind, and received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> And they asked them, saying, Is this your son, who ye say was born blind? How then doth he now see?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents, answered and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> but by what means he now seeth, we know not; or who opened his eyes, we know not; ask him, he is of age; he will speak for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> This his parents said, because they feared the Jews. For the Jews had already agreed, that if any one should acknowledge him as the Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> Therefore his parents said, He is of age; ask him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> They therefore called a second time the man that had been blind, and said to him, Give glory to God; we know that this man is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> He therefore answered, Whether he is a sinner, I know not; one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They said to him, What did he do to thee? How did he open thine eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He answered them, I have already told you, and ye did not hear; why would ye hear it again? Are ye also inclined to become his disciples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They reviled him, and said, Thou art his disciple; but we are disciples of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God hath spoken to Moses; but as for this man, we know not whence he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalms 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said to them, Why, herein is a marvelous thing, that ye know not whence he is, and yet he opened my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God heareth not sinners; but if any one is a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him he heareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> Since the world began, it was never heard that any one opened the eyes of a man born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said to him, Thou wast wholly born in sins, and dost thou teach us? And they cast him out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had cast him out; and he found him, and said, Dost thou believe in the Son of man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and said, And who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalms 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> Jesus said to him, Thou hast both seen him, and it is he that talketh with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> And he said, I believe, Lord; and he prostrated himself before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said, For judgment came I into this world; that they who see not might see, and that they who see might become blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Some of the Pharisees who were with him heard this, and said to him, Are we also blind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, If ye were blind, ye would not have sin; but now ye say, We see. Your sin remaineth.</span>
