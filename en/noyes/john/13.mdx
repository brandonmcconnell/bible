<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Now before the feast of the passover, Jesus, knowing that his hour had come that he should depart out of this world to the Father, having loved his own who were in the world, loved them unto the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> And supper being served,—the Devil having already put it into the heart of Judas Iscariot, the son of Simon, to betray him,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> he, knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands, and that he came forth from God, and was going to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> riseth from the supper, and layeth aside his garments, and took a towel, and girded himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalms 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> Then he poureth water into the basin, and began to wash the disciples' feet, and to wipe them with the towel wherewith he was girded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> So he cometh to Simon Peter; who saith to him, Lord, dost thou wash my feet?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus answered and said to him, What I do thou knowest not now, but thou wilt know hereafter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter saith to him, Thou shalt never wash my feet. Jesus answered him, If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Psalms 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter saith to him, Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus saith to him, He that hath bathed needeth not to wash himself, but is wholly clean; and ye are clean, but not all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> For he knew who was about to betray him; for this reason he said, Ye are not all clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> So after he had washed their feet, and had taken his garments, and placed himself again at the table, he said to them, Know ye what I have done to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> Ye call me the Teacher, and the Lord; and ye say well; for so I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, have washed your feet, ye also ought to wash one another's feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> For I have given you an example, that ye also should do as I have done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> Truly, truly do I say to you, A servant is not greater than his lord, nor one who is sent greater than he that sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> If ye know these things, happy are ye if ye do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> I speak not of you all; I know whom I chose; but that the Scripture may be fulfilled, “He that eateth bread with me, hath lifted up his heel against me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> I tell you now before it hath come to pass, that when it hath come to pass ye may believe that I am He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> Truly, truly do I say to you, He that receiveth whomever I send, receiveth me; and he that receiveth me, receiveth him who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> Having said this, Jesus was troubled in spirit, and testified, and said, Truly, truly do I say to you, that one of you will betray me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> The disciples therefore looked at one another, doubting of whom he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> There was reclining on Jesus' bosom one of his disciples, whom Jesus loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> To him therefore Simon Peter maketh a sign, and saith to him, Tell who it is of whom he is speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> He, therefore, leaning back on the breast of Jesus, saith to him, Lord, who is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Jesus answereth, It is he for whom I shall dip the morsel and give it to him. Having therefore dipped the morsel, he taketh and giveth it to Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And after the morsel, Satan entered into him. Jesus therefore saith to him, What thou doest, do quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Now no one at the table knew for what intent he said this to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> For some thought, because Judas kept the purse, that Jesus said to him, Buy what we need for the feast; or that he should give something to the poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> He then, having received the morsel, went out immediately; and it was night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> When therefore he had gone out, Jesus saith, Now is the Son of man glorified, and God is glorified in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> If God is glorified in him, God will also glorify him in himself, and will immediately glorify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> My children, yet a little while I am with you. Ye will seek me; and, as I said to the Jews, Whither I go, ye cannot come, so now I say to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> A new commandment I give you, that ye love one another; as I have loved you, that ye also love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> By this will all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one for another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter saith to him, Lord, whither dost thou go? Jesus answered, Whither I go, thou canst not follow me now; but thou wilt follow me afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter saith to him, Lord, why cannot I follow thee now? I will lay down my life for thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus answereth, Wilt thou lay down thy life for me? Truly, truly do I say to thee, A cock will not crow, till thou hast thrice denied me.</span>
