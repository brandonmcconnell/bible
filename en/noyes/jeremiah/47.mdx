<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:20; Genesis 10:19; 1 Kings 4:24"> The word of Jehovah which came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh had smitten Gaza.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:20; Jeremiah 8:16; Isaiah 8:7–8"> Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, waters rise up out of the North, And they shall become an overflowing torrent, And shall overflow the land, and all that is therein,—The city, and them that dwell therein; And the men shall cry aloud, And all the inhabitants of the land shall wail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:16; Judges 5:22; Nahum 3:2–3"> At the noise of the stamping of the horses, At the rattling of his chariots, And at the rumbling of his wheels, Fathers look not back for their children, For feebleness of hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:7; Deuteronomy 2:23; Isaiah 20:6"> Because of the day which cometh, To lay waste all the Philistines, To cut off from Tyre and from Sidon Every helper that remaineth. For Jehovah will lay waste the Philistines, The remnant of the country of Caphtor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:37; Jeremiah 25:20; Jeremiah 41:5"> Baldness is come upon Gaza, Askelon is in ruins, And the remnant of their valley! How long wilt thou cut thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:3–5; Jeremiah 12:12; John 18:11"> Ah! sword of Jehovah, How long ere thou wilt be quiet? Retire into thy scabbard, Rest, and be still!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 10:6"> But how canst thou be at rest, Since Jehovah hath given thee a charge against Askelon, And against the coast of the sea? There hath he appointed it.</span>
