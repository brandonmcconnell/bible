<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Daniel 2:37–38; 2 Kings 25:1–9"> The word which came to Jeremiah from Jehovah, when Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth that were under his dominion, and all the nations, made war against Jerusalem, and against all the cities thereof, saying:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 34:22; Jeremiah 37:8–10"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel: Go and speak to Zedekiah, the king of Judah, and say to him, Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall burn it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:4; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 25:4–7"> And thou shalt not escape out of his hand, but shalt surely be taken and delivered into his hand; and thine eyes shall behold the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he shall speak with thee mouth to mouth, and thou shalt go to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Yet hear the word of Jehovah, O Zedekiah, king of Judah! Thus saith Jehovah concerning thee: Thou shalt not die by the sword;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 16:14; 2 Kings 22:20"> in peace shalt thou die; and according to the burnings of thy fathers, the former kings, who were before thee, so shall they burn for thee; and they shall lament for thee, saying, “Ah, lord!” for I have spoken the word, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:16–24; 1 Samuel 3:18; 1 Kings 21:19"> And Jeremiah the prophet spake all these words to Zedekiah, king of Judah, at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Jeremiah 4:5; 2 Kings 18:13–14"> And the army of the king of Babylon fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish, and against Azekah; for these fortified cities remained of the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; 2 Kings 23:2–3; 2 Kings 11:17"> The word which came to Jeremiah from Jehovah, after King Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people that were at Jerusalem, to proclaim liberty to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; Leviticus 25:39–46; Exodus 3:18"> that every one should let his man-servant or his maid-servant, being a Hebrew or Hebrewess, go free, that no one should retain his brother, a Jew, in servitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:16; Jeremiah 26:10; Jeremiah 38:4"> And all the princes, and all the people, who had entered into covenant to let every one his man-servant and every one his maid-servant go free, and retain them in servitude no longer, obeyed; they obeyed, and let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Proverbs 26:11; Matthew 12:43–45"> But they returned afterwards, and took back the men-servants and the maid-servants whom they had let go free, and brought them into subjection as men-servants and as maid-servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then came the word of Jehovah to Jeremiah from Jehovah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 6:8; Joshua 24:17; Deuteronomy 5:27"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel: I made a covenant with your fathers in the day when I brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondmen, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:12; 1 Kings 9:22; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> “At the end of seven years ye shall let go every man his brother, a Hebrew, who shall have been sold to thee; when he shall have served thee six years, thou shalt let him go free from thee.” But your fathers hearkened not to me, nor inclined their ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:8; 2 Kings 23:3; Nehemiah 10:29"> And when ye had turned at this time, and had done what was right in mine eyes, in proclaiming liberty every one to his neighbor, and had entered into a covenant before me in the house which is called by my name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:12; Exodus 20:7; Jeremiah 34:11"> then ye returned and profaned my name, and took back every one his man-servant, and every one his maid-servant, whom he had set at liberty at their pleasure, and brought them into subjection to be men-servants and maid-servants to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 28:25; Galatians 6:7"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah: Ye have not hearkened to me in proclaiming liberty every one to his brother, and every one to his neighbor. Behold, I proclaim a liberty for you, saith Jehovah, to the sword, to pestilence, and to famine, and I will give you up to oppression in all the kingdoms of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; Genesis 15:17–18; Hosea 6:7"> and I will make the men who have transgressed my covenant, who have not performed the words of the covenant which they made before me, like the calf which they cut in twain, and passed between the parts thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:10; Zephaniah 3:3–4; Daniel 9:6"> the princes of Judah, and the princes of Jerusalem, the eunuchs, and the priests, and all the people of the land, that passed between the parts of the calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 19:7"> I will give them into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life; and their dead bodies shall be food for the birds of heaven, and for the beasts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 39:6; Jeremiah 52:10"> And Zedekiah, king of Judah, and his princes, will I give into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life, even into the hand of the king of Babylon's army, which are gone up from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 39:8; Jeremiah 39:1–2"> Behold, I will command, saith Jehovah, and cause them to return to this city, and they shall fight against it, and take it, and burn it with fire; and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation without an inhabitant.</span>
