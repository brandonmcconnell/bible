<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 7:1"> The word of Jehovah came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 11:6"> Go and proclaim in the ears of Jerusalem, Thus saith Jehovah: I remember the kindness shown thee in thy youth, The love with which I espoused thee, When thou wast led by me through the wilderness, Through a land that was not sown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 14:4; James 1:18; Isaiah 41:11"> Israel was a sacred thing to Jehovah, His first ripe fruit. All that devoured him were held guilty; Evil came upon them, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Micah 6:1; Isaiah 51:1–4"> Hear ye the word of Jehovah, O house of Jacob, And all the families of the house of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Romans 1:21; Jeremiah 10:14–15"> Thus saith Jehovah: What fault did your fathers find in me, That they went far from me, And walked after vanity, and practised folly?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Deuteronomy 8:14–16; Hosea 13:4"> They said not, Where is Jehovah, Who brought us up from the land of Egypt, Who led us through the wilderness, Through a land of deserts and pits, Through a land of drought and deathlike gloom, Through a land which no man passed through, And where no man dwelt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 16:18; Numbers 13:27"> I brought you to a land of fruitful fields, To eat the fruit thereof, and the good thereof; But when ye had come in, ye defiled my land, And made my inheritance an abomination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 10:21"> The priests said not, Where is Jehovah? And the teachers of the law knew me not; The rulers also rebelled against me; The prophets prophesied in the name of Baal, And walked after things that could not profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:35–36; Jeremiah 2:35; Exodus 20:5"> Therefore I will yet contend with you, saith Jehovah; Yea, with your children's children will I contend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; 1 Chronicles 23:1; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For pass over to the islands of the Chittaeans, and see; Send ye to Kedar, and inform yourselves well; And see if such a thing hath been done!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Isaiah 37:19; Jeremiah 16:20"> Hath any nation changed their gods, even those that are no gods? But my people have changed their glory for that which cannot profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Jeremiah 6:19"> Be astonished, O ye heavens, at this! Shudder, and be amazed! saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:14; John 7:37; Jeremiah 17:13"> For my people have committed two evils; Me have they forsaken, the fountain of living water, And they have hewn out for themselves cisterns, Broken cisterns, that can hold no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:22; Isaiah 50:1; Genesis 15:3"> Is Israel a slave? Is he a home-born servant? Why then hath he become a spoil?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 9:11"> The young lions roar over him; They lift up their voice; They have made his land a desolation; His cities are burned so as to be without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Jeremiah 44:1"> Even the sons of Noph and Tahpanhes have consumed the crown of thy head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 2:13"> Hath not this come upon thee, Because thou didst forsake Jehovah thy God, When he would have led thee in the way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Isaiah 31:1; Jeremiah 2:36"> And now what hast thou to do with the way of Egypt, To drink the waters of the Nile? Or what hast thou to do with the way of Assyria, To drink the waters of the Euphrates?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 3:9; Jeremiah 4:18; Jeremiah 2:17"> Thy wickedness shall chasten thee, And thy transgressions reprove thee; And thou shalt know and see that it is an evil and bitter thing That thou hast forsaken Jehovah thy God, And that the fear of me is not with thee, Saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Leviticus 26:13; Jeremiah 17:2"> Of old hast thou broken thy yoke, And burst thy bands, And said, “I will not be in subjection!” For upon every high hill, And under every green tree, Hast thou reclined, playing the harlot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:4; Exodus 15:17; Psalms 44:2"> Yet I had planted thee a noble vine, Wholly a genuine seed; How then art thou changed to the degenerate shoot of a strange vine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:1; Job 14:17; Hosea 13:12"> For though thou wash thee with nitre, And take thee much soap, Yet is thine iniquity black before me, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:31; Proverbs 30:12; Proverbs 30:20"> How canst thou say, “I am not polluted,” “I have not gone after Baals”? Behold thy way in the valley! Know what thou hast done, A swift young camel, traversing her ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:6; Jeremiah 2:27; Hosea 5:15"> A wild ass, used to the wilderness, That in her desire snuffeth up the wind, In her occasion, who can turn her aside? All that seek her do not weary themselves; In her month they will find her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 3:13"> Withhold thy feet from being unshod, And thy throat from thirst! But thou sayest, There is no remedy! No! For I love strangers, And after them I will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; Nehemiah 9:32–34; Jeremiah 32:32"> As a thief is ashamed when he is taken, So is the house of Israel ashamed, They, their kings, and their princes, And their priests, and their prophets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Hosea 7:14; Psalms 78:34–37"> That say to a stock, Thou art my father, And to a stone, Thou hast brought me forth! For they turn the back to me, and not the face. Yet in the time of their trouble they say, Arise and save us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:13; Deuteronomy 32:37; Judges 10:14"> But where are thy gods, which thou hast made thee? Let them arise, if they can save thee in the time of thy trouble! For according to the number of thy cities are thy gods, O Judah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:13; Daniel 9:11; Jeremiah 5:1"> Why do ye enter into controversy with me? Ye have all rebelled against me, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:52"> In vain have I smitten your children; They would receive no correction; Your own sword hath devoured your prophets, Like a destroying lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:7–9; Deuteronomy 32:15; Micah 6:9"> O generation! Behold ye the word of Jehovah! Have I been a wilderness to Israel? Or a land of darkness? Why then say my people, “We will rove at pleasure, We will come no more to thee”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Jeremiah 13:25; Jeremiah 3:21"> Can a virgin forget her ornaments, Or a bride her belt? Yet my people have forgotten me, days without number!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 57:7–10; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezekiel 16:27"> Why trimmest thou thy way to seek love? On this account hast thou accustomed thy ways to wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Jeremiah 19:4; 2 Kings 24:4"> Upon thy skirts is found the blood of the innocent poor, Whom thou didst not find committing violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; 1 John 1:8–10; Job 33:9"> But notwithstanding all this, thou sayest, I am innocent; Surely his anger is turned away from me. Behold, I will enter into judgment with thee, Because thou sayest, “I have not sinned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:22; Hosea 5:13; 2 Chronicles 28:16"> Why dost thou run so eagerly, changing thy way? By Egypt also shalt thou be put to shame, Even as thou hast been put to shame by Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:19; Jeremiah 37:7–10; Jeremiah 17:5"> From Egypt also shalt thou go forth With thy hands upon thy head. For Jehovah hath rejected the objects of thy confidence, Therefore shalt thou not prosper in them.</span>
