<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:15; Jeremiah 1:1–2"> The word which came to Jeremiah from Jehovah, saying:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8; Habakkuk 2:2–3"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel: Write thee all the words which I have spoken to thee in a book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 16:15; Jeremiah 30:18"> For behold the days come, saith Jehovah, when I will bring back the captives of my people Israel and Judah, saith Jehovah, and cause them to return to the land which I gave to their fathers, and they shall possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And these are the words which Jehovah spake concerning Israel and concerning Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> Behold, thus saith Jehovah: The voice of trembling do we hear; There is alarm, and no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Joel 2:6; Jeremiah 4:31"> Ask ye now and see, Whether a male doth bring forth? Why then do I see every man's hands upon his loins, like a woman in travail? And why are all faces turned into paleness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> Alas! that day is great, So that there is none like it; It is a time of trouble for Jacob, Yet shall he be saved from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nahum 1:13; Ezekiel 34:27; Isaiah 9:4"> For in that day, saith Jehovah of hosts, I will break his yoke from his neck, And his bands will I burst asunder, And he shall be subject to strangers no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 55:3–5; Luke 1:69"> But they shall serve Jehovah, their God, And David, their king, whom I will raise up for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27–28; Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 29:14"> Therefore fear thou not, O my servant Jacob, saith Jehovah, And be not thou dismayed, O Israel! For, behold, I will bring thee safe from afar, And thy posterity from the land of their captivity; And Jacob shall return, and be at rest; Yea, he shall be quiet, and none shall make him afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:24; Jeremiah 1:19; Isaiah 8:10"> For I will be with thee, saith Jehovah, to save thee; When I shall make a full end of all the nations Whither I have dispersed thee, Yet will I not make a full end of thee; I will correct thee in measure, Yet must I not leave thee wholly unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> For thus saith Jehovah: Thy bruise is incurable; Thy wound is mortal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Jeremiah 8:22; Jeremiah 30:17"> No one offereth his help for thy cure; No healing medicines are applied to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:6; Job 30:21; Lamentations 1:2"> All thy lovers have forgotten thee; They inquire not after thee. For with the wound of an enemy have I smitten thee, With the chastisement of a cruel one, For the multitude of thine iniquities, Because thy sins were increased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:14; Jeremiah 30:12; Jeremiah 6:6–7"> Why criest thou because of thy bruise? Thy pain is without remedy. For the multitude of thine iniquities, Because thy sins were increased, Have I done these things to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; Isaiah 33:1; Exodus 23:22"> Yet all they that devour thee shall be devoured, And all thine enemies, yea, all of them, shall go into captivity, And they that spoil thee shall become a spoil, And all that plunder thee will I give up to plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Jeremiah 33:6; 1 Peter 2:24"> For I will restore soundness to thee, And I will heal thee of thy wounds, saith Jehovah; For they have called thee the outcast, Zion, whom no man careth for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Haggai 2:7–9; Psalms 102:13; Jeremiah 30:3"> Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will bring back the captives of the tents of Jacob, And I will have pity upon his dwelling-places, And the city shall be builded upon her heap, And the palace shall be inhabited as of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> And out of them shall go forth thanksgiving, And the voice of them that make merry. And I will multiply them, and they shall not be few, And I will exalt them, and they shall not be low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> And their children shall be as aforetime, And their congregation shall be established before me, And I will punish all that oppress them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Acts 5:31"> And their princes shall be of themselves, And their governor shall proceed from the midst of them. And I will cause them to approach, and they shall come near to me; For who is he that would dare to come near to me? saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 31:33"> And ye shall be my people, And I will be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19–20; Zechariah 9:14; Jeremiah 25:32"> Behold, a whirlwind from Jehovah, Furious doth it go forth, Even a sweeping whirlwind; Upon the head of the wicked shall it rush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:20; Hosea 3:5; Micah 4:1"> The fierce anger of Jehovah shall not turn back Until he shall have executed, until he shall have accomplished, the purpose of his heart; In a future day ye shall understand it fully.</span>
