<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Jeremiah 42:15; Amos 7:16"> Hear ye the word which Jehovah speaketh to you, O house of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Leviticus 18:3; Luke 21:25–28"> Thus saith Jehovah: Conform ye not to the way of the heathen, And be not dismayed at the signs of the heavens, Because the heathen are dismayed at them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–20; Leviticus 18:30; 1 Peter 1:18"> The customs of the nations are vanity. For a tree of the wood is cut down, It is wrought by the hands of the artificer with the axe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; Isaiah 40:19–20; Psalms 115:4"> It is decked with silver and gold, And with nails and with hammers is it fastened, That it may not totter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 46:7; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 41:23–24"> They are like a turned palm-tree pillar, and cannot speak; They must be borne by men, for they cannot walk. Be not afraid of them, for they cannot hurt, Nor is it in their power to do good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 96:4; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 48:1"> There is none like thee, O Jehovah! Thou art great, And great is thy name by mighty deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 15:4; Psalms 22:28; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20"> Who shall not fear thee, O king of nations, For to thee doth it belong! For among all the wise men of the nations, And in all their kingdoms, there is none like thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 10:2; Jeremiah 10:14; Psalms 115:8"> They are all brutish and without understanding; A doctrine of vanities is the stock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 115:4; Daniel 10:5; 1 Kings 10:22"> Beaten silver is brought from Tarshish, And gold from Uphaz, The work of the artificer and the founder; Blue and purple is their clothing; The work of the skilful is it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Isaiah 57:15; Nahum 1:6"> But Jehovah is the true God, He is the living God, and an everlasting king; At his wrath the earth trembleth, And the nations are not able to abide his indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 96:5; Isaiah 2:18; Lamentations 3:66"> &#91;&#91;Thus shall ye say to them: The gods, that have not made the heavens and the earth, Shall perish from the earth and from under these heavens.&#93;&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 1:1; Job 9:8; Proverbs 3:19"> He made the earth by his power; He established the world by his wisdom, And by his understanding he spread out the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Job 38:22; Job 38:34–35"> When he uttereth his voice there is an abundance of water in the heavens; He causeth clouds to ascend from the ends of the earth; He maketh lightnings with rain; He bringeth the wind from his storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 135:16–18; Psalms 115:4–8; Isaiah 46:7–8"> Brutish is every one who hath not this knowledge; By his image is every founder put to shame, For his molten-work is deceit; There is no breath in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:22; Isaiah 41:24; Jeremiah 51:18"> They are vanity, deceptive work; In the time of their punishment shall they perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 32:18; Jeremiah 31:35"> Not like them is He who is the portion of Jacob; He is the former of all things, And Israel is his allotted inheritance; Jehovah of hosts is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3–12; Jeremiah 6:1; Matthew 24:15"> Gather up thy goods out of the land, O inhabitant of the fortress!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; Zechariah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:63–64"> For thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will sling forth the inhabitants of the land at this time; I will distress them, so that they shall be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 7:9; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 14:17"> “Ah me! my wound!” &#91;&#91;saith the daughter of Zion;&#93;&#93; “My bruise is deadly; Yet I say, This is my affliction, and I must bear it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Jeremiah 31:15; Isaiah 54:2"> My tent is torn down, And all my cords broken. My children are gone forth from me, and are no more; There is none to stretch forth my tent any more, Or to set up my curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–10; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> For the shepherds have become brutish; They have not sought Jehovah; Therefore have they not prospered, And all their flock is dispersed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Habakkuk 1:6–9; Jeremiah 6:22"> &#91;&#91;Hark!&#93;&#93; the sound of tiding! Behold it cometh, And a great tumult from the land of the North, To make the cities of Judah desolate, A dwelling-place for jackals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:24; Proverbs 16:1; Psalms 37:23"> “I know, O Jehovah, that the way of a man is not within his power, That it is not within the power of a man that walketh to establish his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:1; Psalms 6:1; Jeremiah 30:11"> Chasten me, O Jehovah, but in measure; Not in thine anger, lest thou bring me to nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 79:6–7; Jeremiah 8:16; Job 18:21"> Pour out thy wrath on the nations which acknowledge thee not, And on the kingdoms which call not on thy name! For they have devoured Jacob; They have devoured and consumed him, And laid waste his dwelling-place.”</span>
