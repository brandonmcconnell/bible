<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 3:3; 2 Corinthians 3:3; Proverbs 7:3"> The sin of Judah is written with a pen of iron, With the point of a diamond is it engraved Upon the tablet of their hearts, Upon the horns of their altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:18; Jeremiah 2:20; Judges 3:7"> While their children remember the altars, and the images of Astarte, Near the green trees, And upon the high hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:13; Jeremiah 26:18; 2 Kings 24:13"> My mountain in the field! thy substance and all thy treasures will I give up for spoil; Thy high places for sin in all thy borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:14; Lamentations 5:2; Isaiah 5:25"> Thou shalt of thyself cease to possess the inheritance which I gave thee, And I will cause thee to serve thy enemies in a land which thou knowest not; For ye have kindled a fire in my anger, Which shall burn forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 118:8–9; Psalms 146:3–4; Isaiah 2:22"> Thus saith Jehovah: Cursed be the man who trusteth in man, And maketh flesh his arm, And whose heart departeth from Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 1:4"> He shall be like a poor wanderer in the desert, Who seeth not when good cometh, But dwelleth in the parched places of the desert, In a salt land, and uninhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Psalms 34:8; Psalms 40:4"> Blessed is the man who trusteth in Jehovah; Who in Jehovah placeth his hope!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Isaiah 58:11; Ezekiel 47:12"> He shall be like a tree planted by the water-side, That spreadeth out her roots by the stream, That feeleth not when the heat cometh, But whose leaf is green; That careth not in the year of drought, Nor ceaseth from yielding fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Matthew 15:19; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> The heart is deceitful above all things; Yea, it is corrupt; who can know it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Romans 8:27; Jeremiah 11:20"> I, Jehovah, search the heart, And try the reins, To give to every man according to his ways, And according to the fruit of his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 28:20; Proverbs 21:6; Jeremiah 22:17"> As the partridge sitteth on eggs which she hath not laid, So is he that getteth riches, and not by right; In the midst of his days shall he leave them, And in his latter end find himself a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 96:6; Jeremiah 3:17; Ezekiel 43:7"> A glorious, lofty throne from the beginning Hath been the place of our sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; John 7:37–38"> O thou hope of Israel, Jehovah! All that forsake thee shall be put to shame; Yea, all that depart from me shall be written in dust, Because they have forsaken the fountain of living water, they have forsaken Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Deuteronomy 32:39; Luke 4:18"> Heal me, O Jehovah, and I shall be healed; Save me, and I shall be saved; For thou art my praise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 5:19; Jeremiah 20:7–8; Ezekiel 12:22"> Behold, they say to me, “Where is the word of Jehovah? Let it come to pass!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:20; Jeremiah 4:19–20; Acts 20:27"> I have not refused to follow thee, as thy shepherd, Neither have I desired the day of woe, as thou knowest! That which came from my lips hath been before thine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nahum 1:7; Psalms 59:16; Jeremiah 16:19"> Be not thou a terror to me, Thou, my refuge in the day of distress! Let my persecutors be confounded, but let not me be confounded!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 40:14; Psalms 83:17–18"> Let them be dismayed, but let not me be dismayed! Bring upon them the day of calamity, And destroy them with double destruction!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:2; Jeremiah 26:2; Acts 5:20"> Thus said Jehovah to me: Go and stand in the people's gate, Through which the kings of Judah come in and go out, And in all the gates of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:3; Jeremiah 22:2; Psalms 49:1–2"> And say to them, Hear the word of Jehovah, ye kings of Judah, All Judah, and all ye who dwell at Jerusalem, Who enter in through these gates!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; Numbers 15:32–36; John 5:9–12"> Thus saith Jehovah: Take heed to yourselves, And carry no burden on the sabbath-day, And bring none through the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Exodus 20:8–10; Isaiah 58:13"> Bear no burden from your houses on the sabbath-day, And do no kind of work; But keep ye holy the sabbath-day, As I commanded your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Jeremiah 11:10; Acts 7:51"> But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear, But made their necks stiff, and refused to hear, And refused to receive instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–22; Deuteronomy 11:13; 2 Peter 1:5–10"> If ye will now diligently hearken to me, saith Jehovah, And bring no burden through the gates of this city on the sabbath-day, But keep holy the sabbath-day, Doing no work therein,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:4; Isaiah 9:7; Hebrews 12:22"> Then shall enter the gates of this city kings and princes, Who sit upon the throne of David, Riding in chariots and on horses, They and their chieftains, The men of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem; And this city shall be inhabited forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Jeremiah 32:44; Zechariah 7:7"> Then from the cities of Judah, and the places around Jerusalem, From the land of Benjamin, and from the plain, From the mountains, and from the South, Shall they come bringing burnt-offerings, and sacrifices, And flour-offerings, and incense, And bringing thank-offerings to the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 21:14"> But if ye will not hearken to me, To keep holy the sabbath-day, And to carry no burden through the gates of Jerusalem on the sabbath-day, Then will I kindle in her gates an unquenchable fire, Which shall devour the palaces of Jerusalem.</span>
