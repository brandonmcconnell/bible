<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Hosea 13:15; Amos 3:6"> Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will raise up against Babylon, And against them that dwell in the midst of mine adversaries, A destroying wind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Matthew 3:12; Isaiah 41:16"> And I will send against Babylon winnowers, And they shall winnow her, and empty her land; For they shall come against her on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:14; Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:21"> Against him that bendeth, let the archer bend his bow, And against him that lifteth himself up in his brigandine! And spare ye not her young men; Destroy ye utterly her whole host!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:26; Jeremiah 50:30; Isaiah 13:15"> Thus shall they fall down slain in the land of the Chaldaeans, And thrust through in her streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:1–2; Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 12:8"> For Israel is not forsaken, nor Judah abandoned of his God, of Jehovah of hosts; For their land is full of guilt against the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:8; Jeremiah 25:14; Jeremiah 50:28"> Flee ye out of Babylon, And save ye every man his life, That ye be not cut off in her punishment! For this is the time of Jehovah's vengeance; He will render to her a recompense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 17:2"> Babylon has been a golden cup in the hand of Jehovah, That made all the earth drunken; The nations have drunken of her wine, Therefore the nations are mad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:11; Isaiah 21:9; Revelation 14:8"> Babylon is suddenly fallen, and broken; Howl ye over her; Bring ye balm for her wounds, If so be she may be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:16; Revelation 18:5; Isaiah 13:14"> “We would have healed Babylon, But she cannot be healed. Forsake her, and let us go every one to his own country! For her punishment reacheth to the heavens, And ascendeth even to the skies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:28; Psalms 37:6; Isaiah 51:11"> Jehovah hath brought forth our deliverance; Come ye, and let us declare in Zion The work of Jehovah our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:4; Jeremiah 50:45; Jeremiah 46:9"> Make sharp the arrows; put on the shields! Jehovah hath roused up the spirit of the kings of the Medes; For his purpose is against Babylon, to destroy it; For this is the vengeance of Jehovah, The vengeance for his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:2; Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:29"> Against the walls of Babylon set up a standard, Make strong the guard, appoint the watchmen, prepare the ambush! For Jehovah deviseth and doeth that which he spake Against the inhabitants of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:1; Isaiah 45:3; Revelation 17:15"> O thou that dwellest by great waters, That aboundest in treasures, Thine end is come; The measure of thy rapaciousness is full!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:25; Jeremiah 50:15; Jeremiah 49:13"> Jehovah of hosts hath sworn by himself, &#91;&#91;saying,&#93;&#93; Surely I will fill thee with men, as with caterpillars, Who shall raise the war-shout against thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Jeremiah 10:12–16; Jeremiah 32:17"> He made the earth by his power, He established the world by his wisdom, And by his understanding he spread out the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 135:7; Psalms 18:13; Jonah 1:4"> When he uttereth his voice there is an abundance of water in the heavens; He causeth clouds to ascend from the ends of the earth; He bringeth the wind from his storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:14; Isaiah 44:18–20"> Brutish is every man who hath not this knowledge; By his images is every founder put to shame; For his molten work is deceit; There is no breath in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; Isaiah 19:1; Zephaniah 2:11"> They are vanity, deceptive work; In the time of their punishment shall they perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 73:26; Jeremiah 10:16; Psalms 33:12"> Not like them is he who is the portion of Jacob; He is the former of all things, And Israel is his allotted inheritance. Jehovah of hosts is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15–16; Micah 4:13; Isaiah 37:26"> Thou hast been to me my battle-hammer, My weapon of war. And with thee I broke in pieces the nations, And with thee I destroyed the kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:1; Zechariah 10:5; Haggai 2:22"> And with thee I broke in pieces the horse and his rider, And with thee I broke in pieces the chariot and its driver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:18; 1 Samuel 15:3"> With thee I broke in pieces man and woman, And with thee I broke in pieces the old and the young, And with thee I broke in pieces the young man and the maiden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57"> With thee I broke in pieces the shepherd and his flock, And with thee I broke in pieces the husbandman and his team; And with thee I broke in pieces prefects and governors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:15; Revelation 18:24; Isaiah 63:1–4"> But now before your eyes will I repay to Babylon, And to all the inhabitants of Chaldaea, All the evil which they have done in Zion, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 8:8; Zechariah 4:7; Jeremiah 50:31"> O thou destroying mountain, that destroyest the whole earth, Behold, I am against thee, saith Jehovah! And I will stretch out my hand against thee, And roll thee down from the rocks, And make thee a burnt mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Jeremiah 51:43; Revelation 18:20–24"> So that none shall take from thee a corner-stone, or a foundation-stone; But thou shalt be a perpetual desolation, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Genesis 8:4; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> Set ye up a standard in the land, Sound a trumpet among the nations; Enlist the nations against Babylon, Call together against her the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashchenaz; Appoint captains against her, Cause the horses to come up like the bristled locusts!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 25:25; Esther 1:3"> Enlist ye against her the nations, The kings of the Medes, and their prefects, And all their governors, And all the lands under their dominion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11–12; Jeremiah 51:43; Jeremiah 8:16"> So that the earth shall tremble and quake! For the purpose of Jehovah against Babylon is to be fulfilled, To make the land of Babylon a desolation, without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nahum 3:13; Jeremiah 50:36–37; Lamentations 2:9"> The mighty men of Babylon refuse to fight; They remain in their stronghold; Their strength hath failed; They have become women; Her habitations are burned; Her bars are broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6; 2 Samuel 18:19–31; Esther 8:14"> Courier runs to meet courier, And messenger to meet messenger, To make known to the king of Babylon, That his city is taken from end to end;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:37–38; Isaiah 44:27; Jeremiah 51:30"> That the passages are taken, That the reeds are burned with fire, And that the men of war are struck with terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 21:10; Hosea 6:11; Joel 3:13"> For thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel: The daughter of Babylon shall be like a thrashing-floor when it is thrashed; Yet a little while, and the time of harvest for her shall come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:17; Jeremiah 51:44; Matthew 23:13"> Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, hath devoured us; He hath made an end of us; He hath made us empty vessels; He hath swallowed us up like a dragon; He hath filled his maw with our delicacies; He hath cast us out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Jeremiah 50:29; Isaiah 26:20–21"> “The violence done to me and to my flesh be upon Babylon!” Shall the inhabitant of Zion say; And, “My blood be upon the inhabitants of Chaldaeans!” shall Jerusalem say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:38; Romans 12:19; Psalms 140:12"> Therefore, thus saith Jehovah: Behold, I will maintain thy cause, And take vengeance for thee; And I will dry up her sea, And make her springs dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 18:21–23; Jeremiah 18:16"> And Babylon shall become heaps, The dwelling-place of jackals, An astonishment and a hissing, Without an inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:15; Psalms 58:6; Judges 16:20"> Together shall they roar like lions; They shall yell like lions' whelps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 25:27; Nahum 3:11"> In their heat I will prepare them a drink, And will make them drunk, so that they shall rejoice, And then sleep an everlasting sleep, And awake no more, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 34:6; Psalms 44:22"> I will bring them down like lambs to the slaughter, Like rams with he-goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:26; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 49:25"> How is Sheshach taken! How is the praise of the whole earth fallen! How is Babylon become an astonishment among the nations!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 9:26; Psalms 18:4"> The sea is come up over Babylon; With the roaring of its waves is she covered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Isaiah 13:20; Jeremiah 2:6; Jeremiah 51:29"> Her cities have become a desolation, A dry land and a desert, A land in which no man dwelleth, And which no son of man passeth through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 2:2"> I will punish Bel at Babylon, And bring forth from his mouth that which he hath swallowed, And the nations shall flow to him no more; Even the wall of Babylon shall fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Revelation 18:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Go ye out of her, my people, And save ye every man his life From the fierce anger of Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3–5; Isaiah 19:2; Isaiah 21:2–3"> And let not your heart faint, And fear ye not on account of the rumors which are heard in the land, When in one year a rumor cometh, And after it in another year another rumor, And violence is in the land, Ruler against ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 51:52"> Therefore, behold the days come When I will punish the graven images of Babylon, And her whole land shall be confounded; And all her slain shall fall in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Revelation 18:20; Isaiah 44:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> Then the heavens shall shout over Babylon, And the earth and all that is therein; For out of the North come spoilers against her, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:29; Jeremiah 51:24; Psalms 137:8–9"> As Babylon caused the slain of Israel to fall, So at Babylon shall fall the slain of all the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:28; Psalms 122:6; Revelation 18:4"> Ye that have escaped her sword, go! Tarry not! Remember Jehovah afar off, And let Jerusalem come into your mind!</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Lamentations 1:10; Psalms 44:13–16; Psalms 74:3–7"> We have been confounded, because we have heard reproach; Shame hath covered our faces, For strangers have come into the sanctuaries of the house of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:47; Jeremiah 50:38; Daniel 5:30–31"> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith Jehovah, When I will punish her graven images, And through all her land shall the wounded groan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 11:4; Jeremiah 49:16; Psalms 139:8–10"> Though Babylon should mount up to the heavens, And make inaccessible the height of her strength, Yet from me shall spoilers come upon her, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:22; Jeremiah 48:3–5; Jeremiah 50:46"> Hark! a cry from Babylon, Of great destruction from the land of the Chaldaeans!</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 15:1"> For Jehovah destroyeth Babylon, And bringeth to silence her loud tumult; Their waves roar like great waters; Their tumultuous noise resounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Psalms 46:9; Jeremiah 51:48; Psalms 94:1–2"> For the spoiler is come upon her, upon Babylon; And their mighty men are taken, And all their bows are broken; For Jehovah is a God of retribution; He will surely requite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:18; Jeremiah 25:27; Jeremiah 48:15"> And I will make drunk her princes, and her wise men, Her prefects, and her governors, and her mighty men; And they shall sleep an everlasting sleep, And shall no more awake, saith the King, Whose name is Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:13; Jeremiah 51:64; Jeremiah 51:44"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts: The broad walls of Babylon shall be razed to the foundations, And her high gates shall be burned with fire; So that nations shall have labored for naught, And kingdoms have wearied themselves for fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:12; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 45:1"> The word which Jeremiah the prophet commanded Seraiah, the son of Neriah, the son of Maaseiah, when he went with Zedekiah, the king of Judah, into Babylon, in the fourth year of his reign. This Seraiah was chief chamberlain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:2–3; Jeremiah 36:32; Isaiah 30:8"> And Jeremiah wrote all the evil that was to come upon Babylon in one book, all these words that are written concerning Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Mark 13:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:27; 1 Thessalonians 4:18"> And Jeremiah said to Seraiah, When thou comest to Babylon, see that thou read all these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:13; Jeremiah 50:3; Ezekiel 35:9"> and say, O Jehovah, thou hast spoken against this place to destroy it, so that none shall remain in it, neither man nor beast, but that it shall remain desolate forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Revelation 18:21; Jeremiah 19:10–11"> And when thou hast made an end of reading this book, bind a stone upon it, and cast it into the midst of the Euphrates,</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Job 31:40; Nahum 1:8–9"> and say: So shall Babylon sink, and shall not rise again from the evil which I bring upon her. And they shall utterly fail. Thus far the words of Jeremiah.</span>
