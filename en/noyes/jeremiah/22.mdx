<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:2; Luke 3:19–20; 2 Samuel 24:11–12"> Thus said Jehovah: Go down to the house of the king of Judah, and there speak these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:32; Isaiah 9:7; Jeremiah 22:4"> Hear the word of Jehovah, thou king of Judah, That sittest upon the throne of David, Thou, and thy servants, and thy people, Who go in and out through these gates!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:12; Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:17"> Thus saith Jehovah: Do justice and righteousness; Deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor; To the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow Do no wrong, do no violence, And shed no innocent blood in this place!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25"> For if ye shall do this, Then shall there enter in through the gates of this house Kings, who shall sit upon the throne of David, Riding in chariots and upon horses, Each one with his servants and his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 22:16"> But if ye will not hearken to these words, By myself do I swear, saith Jehovah, That this house shall become a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 27:10"> For thus saith Jehovah to the house of the king of Judah: Gilead art thou to me, yea, the summit of Lebanon; Yet will I make thee a desert, As cities not inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Isaiah 10:33–34; Jeremiah 4:6–7"> I will appoint against thee destroyers, Every one with his weapons; And they shall cut down thy choice cedars, And cast them into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 9:8–9; 2 Chronicles 7:20–22; Deuteronomy 29:23–26"> And many nations shall pass by this city, And they shall say one to another, “Wherefore hath Jehovah done thus to this great city?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 2 Chronicles 34:25; Jeremiah 50:7"> Then shall they answer: “Because they forsook the covenant of Jehovah their God, And worshipped strange gods, And served them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:2; Jeremiah 22:11; Isaiah 57:1"> Weep not for him that is dead, Make no lamentation for him! Weep, weep ye for him who is gone away; For he shall return no more, Nor see his native land!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:15; 2 Chronicles 36:1–4; 2 Kings 23:34"> For thus saith Jehovah Concerning Shallum, the son of Josiah, king of Judah, Who reigned instead of Josiah his father, Who went forth from this place: He shall return to it no more;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 23:34; Jeremiah 22:18"> But in the place whither they have led him captive shall he die, And shall see this land no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Micah 3:10; Leviticus 19:13"> Woe to him that buildeth his house with injustice, And his upper apartments with wrong; That exacteth a man's service without wages, And giveth him no recompense!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> That saith, “I will build me a large house, And spacious apartments,” And that cutteth out windows, And ceileth it with cedar, And painteth it with vermilion!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> Shalt thou reign because thou rivallest others in cedar? Did not thy father eat and drink? Yet he had regard to justice and equity; Therefore it was well, with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Isaiah 1:17; 1 John 2:3–4"> He maintained the cause of the poor and needy; Then was it well with him; Was not this to know me, saith Jehovah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Jeremiah 22:3"> But thine eyes and thy heart are only upon thine own gain, And the shedding of innocent blood, And deeds of violence and oppression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:30; Jeremiah 16:6; Jeremiah 16:4"> Therefore thus saith Jehovah, Concerning Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, king of Judah: They shall not lament for him, saying, “Alas, my brother!” or, “Alas, sister!” They shall not lament for him, saying, “Alas, lord!” or, “Alas, his glory!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; 1 Kings 21:23–24; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> With the burial of an ass shall he be buried, Dragged along and cast forth beyond the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Ezekiel 23:22; Lamentations 1:2"> Go thou up to Lebanon and cry, &#91;&#91;O Jerusalem!&#93;&#93; And upon Bashan lift up thy voice; Cry aloud from Abarim! For all thy lovers are fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:30; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 7:22–28"> I spake to thee in thy prosperity, But thou saidst, “I will not hear.” This hath been thy manner from thy youth; Thou hast not obeyed my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:30–31; Jeremiah 12:10; Jeremiah 23:1–2"> The wind shall consume all thy shepherds, And thy lovers shall go into captivity; Then shalt thou be ashamed And confounded for all thy wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:24; Jeremiah 4:30–31; Jeremiah 22:6"> O thou that dwellest in Lebanon, That makest thy nest in cedars, How wretched shalt thou be, when anguish cometh upon thee, Pain, as of a woman in travail!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Song of Solomon 8:6; 1 Chronicles 3:16"> As I live, saith Jehovah, Thou Coniah, son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, Though thou wert the signet upon my right hand, Even thence would I pluck thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 24:15–16"> And I will give thee into the hand of them that seek thy life, And into the hand of them that thou fearest, Even into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, And into the hand of the Chaldaeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 24:15; Jeremiah 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> And I will cast thee forth, And thy mother that bore thee, Into a foreign land, Where ye were not born; And there shall ye die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; 2 Kings 25:27–30; Psalms 86:4"> To the land to which they desire to return, Thither shall they not return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Hosea 8:8; Jeremiah 48:38"> “Is then this man Coniah a contemptible broken vessel? Is he a vessel which no man careth for? Wherefore are he and his offspring cast forth, And thrown into a land which they know not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2; Jeremiah 6:19"> O land, land, land, hear the word of Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; Psalms 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> Thus saith Jehovah: Write ye this man childless, A man that shall not be prosperous through his life; For none of his offspring shall prosper, So as to sit on the throne of David, And reign hereafter in Judah.</span>
