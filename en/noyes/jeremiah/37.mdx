<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24; 2 Kings 24:17; 2 Kings 24:12"> Now King Zedekiah, the son of Josiah, reigned instead of Coniah, the son of Jehoiakim, whom Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, made king in the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:19–20; Proverbs 29:12; 2 Chronicles 36:12–16"> But neither he nor his servants nor the people of the land regarded the words of Jehovah, which he spake by the prophet Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:1–2; Jeremiah 29:25; Jeremiah 42:20"> And Zedekiah the king sent Jehucal, the son of Shelemiah, and Zephaniah, the son of Maaseiah the priest, to Jeremiah the prophet, saying, “Pray now to Jehovah our God for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:15; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> Now Jeremiah came in and went out among the people, for they had not cast him into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:15; Jeremiah 37:11; Jeremiah 34:21"> And Pharaoh's army had come forth out of Egypt; and the Chaldaeans, who were besieging Jerusalem, having heard tidings of them, had departed from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then came the word of Jehovah to Jeremiah the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 4:17; 2 Kings 22:18; Ezekiel 17:17"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel: Thus shall ye say to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of me: Behold, the army of Pharaoh, which is come forth to help you, shall return to Egypt, into their own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2–8; Jeremiah 38:23; Jeremiah 34:21–22"> And the Chaldaeans shall come again, and fight against this city, and take it, and burn it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Obadiah 1:3; Jeremiah 29:8; Galatians 6:3"> Thus saith Jehovah: Deceive not yourselves, saying, “The Chaldaeans shall surely depart from us”; for they shall not depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:45; Isaiah 30:17; Joel 2:11"> For though ye had smitten the whole army of the Chaldaeans that fight against you, and there remained only wounded men among them, each one in his tent, yet should they rise up and burn this city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5"> And it came to pass, when the army of the Chaldaeans had marched away from Jerusalem because of the army of Pharaoh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:1; Nehemiah 6:11; 1 Kings 19:3"> that Jeremiah was going forth from Jerusalem, to go into the land of Benjamin, to receive thence his inheritance among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; Jeremiah 38:7; Luke 23:2"> And when he was in the gate of Benjamin, a captain of the ward was there, whose name was Irijah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Hananiah; and he seized Jeremiah the prophet, saying, “Thou art going over to the Chaldaeans.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–2; Jeremiah 40:4–6; Psalms 27:12"> And Jeremiah said, “It is false; I am not going over to the Chaldaeans.” But he hearkened not to him; so Irijah took Jeremiah and brought him to the princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:26; Jeremiah 20:1–3; Jeremiah 26:16"> And the princes were enraged against Jeremiah, and smote him, and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the scribe; for they had made that a prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:6; Jeremiah 38:10–13; Lamentations 3:53"> And when Jeremiah had gone into the dungeon, and into the cells, and had remained there a long time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:7; Jeremiah 24:8; 1 Kings 14:1–4"> then Zedekiah the king sent and took him out; and the king asked him in his house privately, and said, “Hast thou any word from Jehovah?” And Jeremiah said, “I have.” And he said, “Into the hand of the king of Babylon shalt thou be delivered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 25:8; John 10:32; Daniel 6:22"> And Jeremiah said to King Zedekiah, What offence have I committed against thee, or against thy servants, or against this people, that ye have put me in prison?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Jeremiah 28:10–17; Jeremiah 6:14"> And where are your prophets, who prophesied to you, saying, “The king of Babylon shall not come against you, nor against this land?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:7; Jeremiah 38:6–9; Acts 28:18–19"> But now hear, I pray thee, my lord the king! let my supplication, I pray thee, be accepted before thee, and cause me not to return to the house of Jonathan the scribe, lest I die there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:13; Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 38:9"> Then Zedekiah the king commanded that they should commit Jeremiah into the court of the prison, and that they should give him daily a piece of bread out of the bakers' street, until all the bread in the city should be consumed. And Jeremiah remained in the court of the prison.</span>
