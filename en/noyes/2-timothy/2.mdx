<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> Thou therefore, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> and the things which thou didst hear from me before many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, such as will be able to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Endure hardship with me as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No one serving as a soldier entangleth himself with the affairs of life, that he may please him who chose him to be a soldier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And if a man contendeth in the games, he is not crowned, unless he contendeth lawfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The husbandman that laboreth must be the first partaker of the fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Understand what I say; for the Lord will give thee apprehension in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Bear in mind Jesus Christ of the seed of David, as raised from the dead, according to my gospel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> in which I suffer hardship even unto bonds as an evil-doer; but the word of God is not bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> For this cause I endure all things for the sake of the elect, that they may also obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus, with everlasting glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> True is the saying: for if we died with him, we shall also live with him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> if we endure, we shall also reign with him; if we deny him, he also will deny us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> if we are faithless, he remaineth faithful; for he cannot deny himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Of these things put them in remembrance, charging them before the Lord not to carry on a strife of words, to no useful purpose, but rather to the subverting of the hearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Study to present thyself approved unto God, a workman not ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But shun the profane babblings; for they will go on to a higher degree of ungodliness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and their word will eat as doth a canker; of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> who have erred concerning the truth, saying that the resurrection hath already taken place, and overthrow the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> Nevertheless God's firm foundation standeth, having this seal, “The Lord knoweth them that are his;” and, “Let every one that nameth the name of the Lord depart from iniquity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also wooden and earthen ones; and some for honor, and some for dishonor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> If then any one shall purge himself from these, he will be a vessel for honor, hallowed, useful for the householder, prepared for every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> But flee youthful lusts, and follow righteousness, faith, love, peace, with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But the foolish and ignorant questionings avoid, knowing that they gender quarrels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> and a servant of the Lord must not quarrel, but be gentle to all, apt in teaching, patient of wrong,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> in meekness admonishing those that oppose themselves; if haply God may give them repentance to attain the full knowledge of the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> and they may awake to their senses out of the snare of the Devil, by whom they have been taken captive to do his will.</span>
