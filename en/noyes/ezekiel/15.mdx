<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Psalms 80:8–16; Isaiah 5:1–7"> Son of man, what is the wood of the vine more than any wood,—a branch, that is among the trees of the forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 14:34–35; Jeremiah 24:8; Mark 9:50"> Shall wood be taken from it to be wrought into any work? Or will men take a pin of it to hang a vessel upon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:6; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 80:16"> Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel; the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the middle of it is burned. Is it then fit for any work?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:16"> Behold, when it was whole, nothing could be made of it; how much less can anything be made of it when the fire hath devoured it and it is burned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: As the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so will I give to it the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:8; Psalms 34:16; Isaiah 24:18"> And I will set my face against them; they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them; and ye shall know that I am Jehovah, when I set my face against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Zephaniah 1:18; Isaiah 6:11"> And I will make the land desolate, because they have grievously trespassed, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
