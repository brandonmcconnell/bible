<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 3:16; Ezekiel 11:24; Acts 7:56"> Now it came to pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, in the fifth day of the month, as I was among the captives by the river Chebar, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–15; Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> On the fifth day of the month, in the fifth year of the captivity of King Jehoiachin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 3:22; 2 Kings 3:15"> the word of Jehovah came to Ezekiel, the son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldaeans, by the river Chebar; and the hand of Jehovah was there upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:27; Ezekiel 8:2; Jeremiah 23:19"> And I looked, and behold, a stormy wind came from the north, a great cloud, and a mass of fire; and a brightness was round about it, and in the midst of it the appearance of bright brass, in the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:6–8; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 6:6"> And in the midst of it were the forms of four living creatures. And this was their appearance. They had the form of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7–8; Ezekiel 1:15"> And every one had four faces, and every one had four wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:15; Daniel 10:6; Psalms 104:4"> And their feet were upright; and the sole of their feet was as the sole of a calf's foot; and they sparkled like polished brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:21; Ezekiel 10:11; Isaiah 6:6"> And they had the hands of a man under their wings on their four sides, and all four had faces and wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:11–12"> Their wings were joined one to another; they turned not about when they went; they went every one straight forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> As for the form of their faces, all four had the face of a man, and the face of a lion on the right side, and all four had the face of an ox on the left side, and all four had also the face of an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2; Ezekiel 1:23; Ezekiel 10:19"> Thus were their faces. And their wings were expanded upward; two wings of every one were joined, and two covered their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 10:22; Hebrews 1:14"> And they went every one straight forward; whither the spirit was to go, they went, and they turned not about when they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Psalms 104:4; Matthew 28:3"> And the appearance of the living creatures was as coals of fire, burning like torches; and the fire moved about among the living creatures, and shone forth brightly; and out of the fire came forth lightning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Zechariah 4:10; Mark 13:27"> And the living creatures ran backward and forward like a flash of lightning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Ezekiel 10:9; Ezekiel 1:19–21"> Now as I beheld the living creatures, behold, there stood a wheel upon the earth by each of the living creatures, with its four sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Ezekiel 10:9–11; Exodus 39:13"> The appearance of the wheels, and their work, was like that of a chrysolite, and all four had one form, and their appearance and their work was as if a wheel had been within a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 1:12; Isaiah 55:11"> When they went, they went upon their four sides, and they turned not about when they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:12; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 4:8"> As for their felloes, they were high and terrible; and their felloes were full of eyes round about upon all four of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 10:19; Psalms 103:20"> And when the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:12; Ezekiel 10:17; 1 Corinthians 14:32"> Whithersoever the spirit was to go, they went, whithersoever the spirit was to go; and the wheels were lifted up beside them; for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:17; Ezekiel 1:19–20; Romans 8:2"> When those went, these went; and when those stood, these stood; and when those were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up beside them; for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 4:6"> And over the heads of the living creatures was the appearance of a firmament, like crystal, terrible, spread out over their heads above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 4:18; Ezekiel 1:11–12; Psalms 89:7"> And under the firmament were their wings upright, the one toward the other; and every one had two, which covered his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 1:15"> And I heard the sound of their wings, as the sound of great waters, as the voice of the Almighty, when they went,—a sound like the noise of a host. And when they stood still, they let down their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:22"> And there was a voice from the firmament, that was over their heads; and they stood still, and let down their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Exodus 24:10; Revelation 1:13"> And above the firmament over their heads was an appearance like a sapphire-stone, the form of a throne; and upon the form of the throne there was a form like that of a man above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 8:2; Hebrews 12:29"> And I saw what had the appearance of bright brass, what had the appearance of fire round about within it, from the appearance of his loins and upward. And from the appearance of his loins downward I saw what had the appearance of fire, and its brightness was round about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 10:1; Revelation 4:3; Genesis 17:3"> As the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud in the day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness round about. This was the appearance of the glory of Jehovah. And when I saw it I fell upon my face, and I heard the voice of one who spoke.</span>
