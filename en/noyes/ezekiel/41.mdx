<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:2–3; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 41:23"> Afterwards he brought me to the temple; and he measured the projecting wall-pillars, six cubits broad on this side, and six cubits broad on that side, according to the breadth of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:17; 2 Chronicles 3:3; 1 Kings 6:2"> And the breadth of the door was ten cubits; and the sides of the door were five cubits on this side; and five cubits on that side. Then he measured the length, forty cubits, and the breadth, twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16"> And he went inward, and measured the border of the door, two cubits; and the door, six cubits; and the breadth of the door, seven cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20; Hebrews 9:3–8; Exodus 26:33–34"> Then he measured twenty cubits in length, and twenty cubits in breadth in the temple, and said to me, This is the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 42:3–14; Ezekiel 41:6–9"> And he measured the wall of the house, six cubits; and the breadth of every side-chamber, four cubits round about, round about the house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 6:10; 1 Kings 6:5–6; Ezekiel 40:17"> And the side-chambers were chamber to chamber, three and thirty times; and they entered into the wall of the house of the side-chambers round about, and were connected with it; but they were not connected with the wall of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:8; Hebrews 6:1; Matthew 13:32"> And, the side-chambers became wider upward, having a winding ascent. For the circuit of the house went still upward, round about the house; therefore the greater breadth of the house was upward; and so they ascended from the lowest story to the highest through the middle story.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:5; Revelation 21:16"> I saw also the height of the house round about; the foundations of the side-chambers were a full reed, six cubits to the knuckles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:11; Ezekiel 42:4; Ezekiel 42:1"> The thickness of the wall of the side-chambers outward was five cubits, and the space which was left was the place of the side-chambers of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:17"> And between the rooms was the breadth of twenty cubits round about the house on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:9; Ezekiel 42:4"> And the doors of the side-chambers were toward the space that was left, one door toward the north, and another door toward the south; and the breadth of the space that was left was five cubits round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:13–15"> And the building which was before the separate place, at the side toward the west, was seventy cubits broad; and the wall of the building was five cubits thick round about, and the length thereof was ninety cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:47"> Then he measured the length of the house, a hundred cubits; and the length of the separate place and the building and the walls thereof, a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Also the breadth towards the front of the house and of the separate place toward the east was a hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:3; Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 41:12"> And he measured the length of the building before the separate place, and that which was behind it, and the galleries thereof on this side and on that side, a hundred cubits; and the inner temple, and the porches of the court,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 6:4; Ezekiel 40:25; Ezekiel 40:16"> the thresholds, and the windows that were closed, and the galleries round about the three sides. Over against the threshold was a table of wood round about, from the ground to the windows, and the windows were covered with it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:15"> upon the space above every door, and upon the inner house, and without, and upon all the wall round about within and without, according to measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:36; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 40:22"> And cherubs and palm-trees were made, one palm-tree between two cherubs, and every cherub had two faces;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Ezekiel 1:10"> and the face of a man was toward a palm-tree on one side, and the face of a lion toward a palm-tree on the other side. So was it made upon all the house round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> From the ground to above the door were cherubs and palm-trees made, and on the wall of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:1; 1 Kings 6:33; Ezekiel 40:14"> The posts of the temple were four-square; and the front of the sanctuary had the same appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; Ezekiel 23:41; Malachi 1:7"> The altar was of wood, three cubits high, and two cubits long; and the corners thereof and the base thereof and the walls thereof were of wood. And he said to me, This is the table that standeth before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 6:31–35; 2 Chronicles 4:22; Ezekiel 41:1"> And the temple and the sanctuary had two doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; Ezekiel 40:48"> And the two doors had two leaves apiece, two turning leaves; two leaves for one door, and two leaves for the other door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:18"> And there were made on them, even on the doors of the temple, cherubs and palm-trees, such as were made upon the walls, and a wooden threshold was before the porch without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:16; Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:5"> And at the closed windows were palm-trees on this side, and on that side, on the sides of the porch, and on the side-chambers of the house, and on the thresholds.</span>
