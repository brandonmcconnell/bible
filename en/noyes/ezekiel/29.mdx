<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, in the tenth month, in the twelfth day of the month, the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> Son of man, set thy face against Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and prophesy against him, and against all Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Speak, and say, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I am against thee, Pharaoh, king of Egypt, thou great dragon, that liest in the midst of thy rivers, and sayest, “My river is my own, and I have made it for myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> And I will put hooks in thy jaws, and I will cause the fish of thy rivers to cleave to thy scales, and I will draw thee forth from the midst of thy rivers, and all the fish of thy rivers which cleave to thy scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> And I will cast thee into the desert, thee and all the fish of thy rivers; thou shalt fall upon the open fields; thou shalt not be brought together, nor gathered; to the beasts of the field and the birds of heaven I have given thee for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> And all the inhabitants of Egypt shall know that I am Jehovah; because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> When they took hold of thee by thy handle, thou didst break and pierce their whole shoulder; and when they leaned upon thee, thou didst go to pieces, and madest all their loins to shake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I will bring a sword upon thee, and cut off man and beast out of thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> and the land of Egypt shall be desolate and waste; and they shall know that I am Jehovah, because he hath said, “The river is mine, and I have made it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> Therefore, behold, I am against thee, and against thy rivers; and I will make the land of Egypt utterly waste and desolate, from Migdol even to Syene, and to the borders of Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> There shall not pass through it the foot of man, and the foot of beast shall not pass through it, neither shall it be inhabited forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> I will make the land of Egypt desolate amidst the lands that are desolate, and her cities, among the cities that are laid waste, shall be desolate forty years; and I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> Yet thus saith the Lord Jehovah: At the end of forty years will I gather the Egyptians from the nations whither they are scattered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> and I will bring back the captives of Egypt, and will cause them to return into the land of Pathros, into the land of their origin; and they shall be there a mean kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> it shall be the meanest of all kingdoms; it shall no more exalt itself above the nations; for I will diminish them, that they shall no more rule over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And it shall be no more the confidence of the house of Israel, bringing to my remembrance their iniquity in looking to them for help; and they shall know that I am the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> And it came to pass in the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, that the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, caused his army to serve a great service against Tyre; every head was made bald, and every shoulder was peeled; yet neither he nor his army had wages from Tyre for the service which he served against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I will give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, and he shall take her multitude, and take her spoil, and take her plunder; and it shall be wages for his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> For his wages for his service I will give him the land of Egypt; for they wrought for me, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalms 132:17"> In that day will I cause a horn to grow forth to the house of Israel, and I will give thee to open the mouth in the midst of them; and they shall know that I am Jehovah.</span>
