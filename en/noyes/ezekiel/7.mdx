<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover, the word of Jehovah came to me, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 8:2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:3"> Thou son of man, thus saith the Lord Jehovah concerning the land of Israel:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 33:20"> The end cometh, the end cometh, Upon the four corners of the land! Now cometh the end upon thee! For I will send my anger upon thee, And will judge thee according to thy ways, And will recompense upon thee all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 11:21; Ezekiel 5:11"> And mine eye shall not spare thee, neither will I have pity; But I will recompense thy ways upon thee, And thine abominations shall be in the midst of thee; And ye shall know that I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Kings 21:12–13; Ezekiel 5:9"> Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: An evil, an unheard-of evil, behold, it cometh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Ezekiel 7:3; 2 Peter 2:5"> The end cometh, the end cometh! It awaketh against thee, behold, it cometh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> Thy fate cometh upon thee, thou that dwellest in the land! The time is come, The day of tumult is near, When no sound of joy shall be upon the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:8; Ezekiel 14:19; Ezekiel 20:8"> Now will I shortly pour out my fury upon thee, And accomplish mine anger against thee; And I will judge thee according to thy ways, And recompense upon thee all thine abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> And mine eye shall not spare, neither will I have pity; I will recompense thee according to thy ways, And thine abominations shall come upon thee; And ye shall know that I, Jehovah, smite you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Behold, the day, behold, it cometh! The destiny draweth near; The rod hath blossomed, pride hath flourished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> Violence is risen up into a rod of wickedness; None of them shall remain, none of their multitude, none of their crowd; Nor shall there be wailing for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Ezekiel 7:5–7"> The time cometh, the day draweth near! Let not the buyer rejoice, Nor the seller mourn; For wrath is against their whole multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> For the seller shall not return to that which is sold, Though he be yet alive; For the vision against their whole multitude shall not return void, And none that liveth in his iniquity shall strengthen himself</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 51:27"> “Blow ye the trumpet, and let all be ready!” Yet none goeth to the battle; For mine anger is against their whole multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Ezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> The sword is without, and pestilence and famine within; He that is in the field shall die by the sword; And he that is in the city, famine and pestilence shall devour him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Isaiah 38:14; Ezra 9:15"> And those of them that escape shall be upon the mountains like doves of the valleys, All of them mourning, every one for his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremiah 6:24"> All hands shall be feeble, And all knees shall flow with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Isaiah 15:2–3; Isaiah 3:24"> They shall also gird themselves with sackcloth, And horror shall cover them. And upon all their faces shall be shame, And upon all their heads baldness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Zephaniah 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> Their silver shall they cast into the streets, And their gold shall be as an unclean thing. Their silver and their gold shall not be able to deliver them In the day of the wrath of Jehovah; Their hunger shall not be satisfied, Nor their body filled with it; For it was the stumbling-block of their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:15–16"> For the beauty of their ornaments they turned into pride, And the images of their abominations and of their detestable things they made with it; Therefore will I make it to them as an unclean thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; 2 Chronicles 36:18–19"> And I will give it into the hands of strangers for a prey, And to the wicked of the earth for a spoil, And they shall pollute it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> And I will turn my face from them, So that my secret place shall be polluted; Robbers shall enter into it, and pollute it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Jeremiah 27:2; Ezekiel 11:6"> Make a chain! For the land is full of blood-guiltiness, And the city is full of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> Therefore will I bring the most cruel among the nations, And they shall possess your houses; I will also make the pride of the strong to cease, And their holy places shall be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> Destruction cometh; And they shall seek peace, and not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Ezekiel 14:1; Micah 3:6"> Calamity shall come upon calamity, And rumor shall be upon rumor; And they shall seek a vision from the prophet in vain; Instruction shall perish from the priests, And counsel from the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> The king shall mourn, And the prince shall be clothed with amazement; And the hands of the people of the land shall be troubled. I will do to them according to their way, and according to their deserts will I judge them, And they shall know that I am Jehovah.</span>
