<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:12; Galatians 3:26; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> Behold what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called children of God l For this cause the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Beloved, now are we children of God, and it hath not yet been manifested what we shall be. We know that, when it shall be manifested, we shall be like him; because we shall see him as he is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 John 2:6; 2 Peter 3:14"> And every one that hath this hope in Him purifieth himself, even as He is pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 5:17; Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15"> Whoever committeth sin transgresseth thereby the law; for sin is a transgression of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> And ye know that he was manifested to take away sins; and in him is no sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> Whoever abideth in him sinneth not; whoever sinneth hath not seen him, nor known him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> My children, let no one deceive you; he that doeth righteousness is righteous, even as he is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> He that committeth sin is of the Devil; for the Devil sinneth from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the Devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Whoever hath been born of God doth not commit sin, because his seed abideth in him; and he cannot sin, because he hath been born of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> In this are manifest the children of God and the children of the Devil. Whoever doeth not righteousness is not of God, and he that loveth not his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> For this is the message that ye have heard from the beginning, that we should love one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> not as Cain was of the Evil One, and slew his brother. And wherefore did he slay him? Because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> Wonder not, brethren, if the world hateth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have passed out of death into life, because we love the brethren; he that loveth not abideth in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Whoever hateth his brother is a murderer; and ye know that no murderer hath everlasting life abiding in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> Herein we know love, in that he laid down his life for us; and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> But whoever hath this world's goods, and seeth his brother having need, and shutteth up his compassion from him, how dwelleth the love of God in him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> My children, let us not love in word, nor in tongue, but in deed and in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> And hereby we know that we are of the truth, and shall assure our hearts before him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> because if our heart condemn us, God is greater than our heart, and knoweth all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Beloved, if our heart condemn us not, we have confidence toward God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> and whatever we ask, we receive of him, because we keep his commandments, and do the things that are pleasing in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> And this is his commandment, that we should believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and should love one another, as he gave commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> And he that keepeth his commandments abideth in him, and he in him; and hereby we know that he abideth in us, by the Spirit, which he gave us.</span>
