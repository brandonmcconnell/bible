<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> And it came to pass in those days that there went out a decree from Caesar Augustus, that all the world should be registered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> (This registering was the first made while Quirinius was governor of Syria.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And all went to be registered, each to his own city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Joseph also went up from Galilee out of the city of Nazareth into Judaea, to the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and lineage of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> to be registered with Mary who had been betrothed to him, who was with child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> And it came to pass, while they were there, that the days for her delivery were completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swathing-clothes, and laid him in a manger, because there was no room for them in the inn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> And there were in the same country shepherds abiding in the field, and keeping watch over their flock by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And an angel of the Lord came to them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them; and they were in great fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> And the angel said to them, Fear not; for lo! I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to the whole people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> for to you hath been born this day, in the city of David, a Saviour, who is the Christ, the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> And this shall be the sign to you: Ye shall find a babe wrapped in swathing-clothes, &#91;&#91;lying&#93;&#93; in a manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host praising God, and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace among men of good will!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> And it came to pass, when the angels had gone away from them into heaven, that the shepherds said one to another, Let us go at once to Bethlehem, and see that which hath come to pass, which the Lord hath made known to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> And they came with haste, and found Mary and Joseph, and the babe lying in the manger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> And when they had seen it, they made known abroad what had been told them concerning this child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> And all who heard wondered at the things which were told them by the shepherds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary kept all these things, and pondered them in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all which they had heard and seen, as had been told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> And when eight days were completed for him to be circumcised, his name was called Jesus, the name given by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> And when the days of their purification according to the law of Moses were completed, they brought him to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> as it is written in the law of the Lord: “Every first-born male shall be called holy to the Lord,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> and to offer a sacrifice, according to what is directed in the law of the Lord: “A pair of turtle doves, or two young pigeons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> And lo! there was a man in Jerusalem, whose name was Simeon; and he was a righteous and devout man, waiting for the consolation of Israel. And the Holy Spirit was upon him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> and it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit, that he should not see death before he had seen the Christ of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> And he came in the Spirit into the temple; and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him according to the custom of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> he took him in his arms, and blessed God, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> Lord! now lettest thou thy servant depart in peace, according to thy word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> for mine eyes have seen thy salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> which thou hast prepared before the face of all the peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> a light to enlighten the gentiles, and to be the glory of thy people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> And his father and his mother marveled at what was spoken concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> And Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary his mother, Behold, this child is appointed for the fall and rising of many in Israel, and for a sign that will be spoken against;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> yea, a sword will pierce through thine own soul,—that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> And there was Anna, a prophetess, daughter of Phanuel: of the tribe of Asher; she was of great age, and had lived with a husband seven years from her virginity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalms 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and she was a widow eighty-four years old, who never left the temple, worshipping with fastings and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> And she came up at this very time, and gave thanks to God, and spoke of him to all that were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And when they had performed all things according to the law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee, to their own city Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> And the child grew, and waxed strong, being filled with wisdom; and the grace of God was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> Now his parents used to go yearly to Jerusalem at the feast of the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when he was twelve years old, and they went up, after the custom of the feast,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> and had completed the days, on their returning, the child Jesus tarried behind in Jerusalem; and his parents did not know it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> but, supposing him to be in the company, went a day's journey; and they sought him among their kinsfolk and acquaintance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and not finding him, they returned to Jerusalem in search of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> And it came to pass, that after three days they found him in the temple sitting in the midst of the teachers, both listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> And all who heard him were astonished at his understanding and his answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> And when they saw him, they were amazed; and his mother said to him, Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I have been seeking thee in much distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> And he said to them, Why is it that ye have been seeking me? Did ye not know that I must be about my Father's business?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> And they understood not what he spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> And he went down with them, and came to Nazareth, and was subject to them. And his mother kept all these things in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.</span>
