<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> And he spoke a parable to them to show that they ought to pray always, and not be faint-hearted:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> saying, There was in a certain city a judge, who feared not God, nor regarded man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> And there was a widow in that city; and she kept coming to him, saying, Avenge me of my adversary!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> And he would not for some time. But afterward he said within himself, Though I neither fear God, nor regard man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> yet, because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her; lest by coming for ever she weary me out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> And will not God avenge his chosen, who cry to him day and night, though he be slow to punish in their behalf?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I tell you that he will avenge them speedily. But yet, when the Son of man cometh, will he find faith on the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> And to some who trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and despised all others, he spoke this parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> Two men went up into the temple to pray; the one a Pharisee, and the other a publican.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee stood and prayed by himself thus: O God, I thank thee that I am not as the rest of men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even as this publican.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice in the week; I give tithes of all that I gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> But the publican, standing afar off, would not even lift up his eyes to heaven; but smote his breast, saying, O God, be merciful to me a sinner!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other; for every one that exalteth himself will be humbled; but he that humbleth himself will be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Matthew 19:13–15; 1 Samuel 1:24"> And they brought to him infants also, that he might touch them; and the disciples, on seeing it, rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But Jesus called them to him, saying, Suffer the little children to come to me, and forbid them not; for to such belongeth the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Mark 10:15; Psalms 131:1–2"> Truly do I say to you, Whoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, will not enter therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> And a certain ruler asked him, saying, Good teacher, what shall I do to inherit everlasting life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> And Jesus said to him, Why dost thou call me good? None is good but one, that is, God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> Thou knowest the commandments: “Do not commit adultery; Do not kill; Do not steal; Do not bear false witness; Honor thy father and thy mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> And he said, All these have I kept from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> And Jesus hearing this said to him, One thing thou still lackest: sell all that thou hast, and distribute to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven; and come, follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when he heard this, he became very sorrowful; for he was very rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> And Jesus seeing him said, How hardly do they that have riches enter the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> And those who heard this said, Who then can be saved?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> But he said, What is impossible with men is possible with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> Then Peter said, Lo! we left what we had, and followed thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> And he said to them, Truly do I say to you, There is no one that hath left house, or wife, or brothers, or parents, or children, for the sake of the kingdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who will not receive many fold more in the present time, and in the world to come everlasting life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> And taking the twelve aside, he said to them, Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and all that hath been written by the prophets concerning the Son of man will be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> For he will be delivered up to the gentiles, and will be mocked, and insulted, and spit upon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> and they will scourge him, and put him to death; and the third day he will rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> And they understood none of these things; and the meaning of these words was hidden from them, and they did not comprehend what was said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> And it came to pass, as he drew near to Jericho, that a certain blind man was sitting by the wayside begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> And hearing a multitude passing by, he asked what this meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> And they told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> And he cried out, saying, Jesus, Son of David, have pity on me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> And they who went before sharply bade him hold his peace. But he cried out the more, Son of David, have pity on me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> And Jesus stopped, and ordered him to be brought to him. And when he had come near, he asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> What dost thou wish me to do for thee? And he said, Lord, to restore my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> And Jesus said to him, Receive thy sight; thy faith hath saved thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> And immediately he received his sight, and followed him, giving glory to God; and all the people on seeing it gave praise to God.</span>
