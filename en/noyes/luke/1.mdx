<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:1–3; 2 Peter 1:16–19; John 20:31"> Inasmuch as many have undertaken to arrange a narrative of those things which are fully believed among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:27; Acts 26:16; Hebrews 2:3"> even as they were delivered to us by those who were eye-witnesses from the beginning and became ministers of the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 1:1; Acts 26:25; Acts 11:4"> it seemed good to me also, having accurately traced up all things from the first, to write to thee a connected account, most excellent Theophilus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> that thou mightst know the exact truth with regard to those things in which thou wast instructed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> There was in the days of Herod, king of Judaea, a certain priest named Zachariah, of the course of Abijah; and he had a wife of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> And they were both righteous in the sight of God, walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> And they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they were both far advanced in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> And it came to pass, that, while he was serving as priest before God in the order of his course,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> according to the custom of the priesthood the lot fell to him to go into the temple of the Lord and burn the incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> And the whole multitude of the people was praying without at the time of the incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> And there appeared to him an angel of the Lord, standing on the right of the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> And Zachariah was troubled at the sight, and fear fell upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said to him, Fear not, Zachariah; for thy prayer was heard, and thy wife Elizabeth shall bear thee a son; and thou shalt call his name John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> And thou wilt have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice for his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> For he will be great in the sight of the Lord; and will drink neither wine nor strong drink; and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit from his very birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> And many of the sons of Israel will he turn to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> And he will go before his face in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers to children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a prepared people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> And Zachariah said to the angel, Whereby shall I know this? for I am an old man, and my wife is far advanced in years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> And the angel answering said to him, I am Gabriel, who stand in the presence of God; and I was sent to speak to thee, and to tell thee these glad tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> And lo! thou shalt be silent, and not able to speak, until the day when these things shall come to pass, because thou didst not believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> And the people were looking for Zachariah, and wondering that he remained so long in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; John 13:24; Acts 21:40"> And on coming out, he was not able to speak to them; and they perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple; and he was beckoning to them, and remained speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> And it came to pass, when the days of his ministration were completed, that he returned to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And after those days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and hid herself five months, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> Thus hath the Lord dealt with me in the days wherein he looked on me to take away my reproach among men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; John 7:41"> And in her sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee, called Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name was Mary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> And the angel came in to her and said, Hail, thou that art highly favored! the Lord is with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> And she was troubled at the words, and was considering what this salutation could mean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> And the angel said to her, Fear not, Mary; for thou hast found favor with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:21; Matthew 1:25"> And lo! thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and hear a son, and shalt call his name Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Isaiah 16:5; 2 Samuel 7:11–13"> He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Most High; and the Lord God will give to him the throne of David his father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> and he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever; and of his kingdom there shall be no end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> Then said Mary to the angel, How shall this be, since I know not a man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; John 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> And the angel answering said to her, The Holy Spirit will come upon thee, and the power of the Most High will overshadow thee; wherefore the holy one that is to be born will be called the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And lo! Elizabeth, thy kinswoman, she also hath conceived a son in her old age; and this is the sixth month with her who is called barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Mark 10:27"> For nothing will be impossible with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Psalms 119:38; Psalms 116:16"> And Mary said, Lo, the handmaid of the Lord! May it be to me according to thy word. And the angel departed from her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:65; Joshua 15:48–59; Joshua 10:40"> And Mary arose and went in those days into the hill-country with haste, into a city of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And she entered the house of Zachariah, and saluted Elizabeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 1:67; Acts 4:8; Luke 1:44"> And it came to pass, when Elizabeth heard the salutation of Mary, that the babe leaped in her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 1:28; Judges 5:24; Genesis 22:18"> and cried out with a loud voice, and said, Blessed art thou among women; and blessed is the fruit of thy womb!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 20:28; John 13:13; Luke 2:11"> And whence is this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For lo! as soon as the voice of thy salutation came to my ears, the babe leaped in my womb in exultation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 20:29; John 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> And blessed is she who hath believed that there will be an accomplishment of those things which were told her from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:1–10; Psalms 34:2–3; Psalms 35:9"> And Mary said, My soul magnifieth the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 35:9; 1 Timothy 2:3; Habakkuk 3:18"> and my spirit hath exulted in God my Saviour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Malachi 3:12; 1 Corinthians 1:26–28"> for he hath looked upon the low estate of his handmaiden. For lo! from this time all generations will call me blessed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Psalms 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> for the Mighty One hath done great things for me; and holy is his name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> and his mercy is from generation to generation on them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> He hath wrought mightily with his arm; he hath scattered the proud in the device of their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; James 4:10; Psalms 107:40–41"> He hath cast down princes from thrones, and exalted men of low degree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; Psalms 34:10"> The hungry he hath filled with good things, and the rich he hath sent away empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> He hath given help to Israel his servant, in remembrance of his mercy</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; Psalms 132:11–17"> toward Abraham and his offspring for ever, as he spoke to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And Mary abode with her about three months, and returned to her own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> Now Elizabeth's full time came that she should be delivered; and she brought forth a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Luke 1:14; Romans 12:15"> And her neighbors and her kinsfolk heard that the Lord had showed great mercy toward her; and they rejoiced with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 17:12; Leviticus 12:3; Philippians 3:5"> And it came to pass that on the eighth day they came to circumcise the child; and they were about to call him Zachariah, after the name of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> And his mother answering said, Not so; but he shall be called John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they said to her, There is no one of thy kindred that is called by this name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> And they made signs to his father, how he would have him named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> And he asked for a writing-tablet, and wrote, saying, His name is John. And they all marveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> And his mouth was opened immediately, and his tongue was loosed; and he spoke, blessing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> And fear came on all that dwelt around them; and all these things were talked of throughout the whole of the hill-country of Judaea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> And all that heard them laid them up in their hearts, saying, What then will this child be? For the hand of the Lord was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Luke 1:41; Luke 1:15"> And Zachariah his father was filled with the Holy Spirit, and prophesied, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Luke 7:16; Psalms 41:13"> Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel! for he hath visited and redeemed his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Psalms 18:2; Ezekiel 29:21; 2 Samuel 22:3"> and hath raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of David his servant,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> as he spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets of old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Psalms 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> salvation from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–18; Micah 7:20; Psalms 106:45"> to accomplish his mercy toward our fathers, and to remember his holy covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Psalms 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> the oath which he swore to Abraham our father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> to grant to us, that being delivered from the hand of our enemies, we might worship him without fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> in holiness and righteousness before him, all our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> And thou, child, shalt be called a prophet of the Most High; for thou shalt go in advance before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Acts 2:38; Acts 10:43"> to give knowledge of salvation to his people in the remission of their sins</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="Malachi 4:2; 2 Peter 1:19; Revelation 22:16"> through the tender mercy of our God, whereby the day-spring from on high hath visited us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v79" data-v="79" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Matthew 4:16; Acts 26:18"> to give light to those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Mark 1:3–4; Luke 2:52"> And the child grew, and waxed strong in spirit; and was in the deserts till the day of his manifestation to Israel.</span>
