<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> And he looked up and saw the rich men casting their gifts into the treasury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> And he saw also a certain poor widow casting in thither two mites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> And he said, In truth I say to you, that this poor widow hath cast in more than they all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> For all these out of their abundance cast in to the offerings; but she out of her penury cast in all the living that she had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> And as some were saying of the temple, that it was adorned with goodly stones, and sacred gifts, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> As for these things which ye behold, the days will come, in which there will not be left here one stone upon another, that will not be throw down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> And they asked him, saying, Teacher, when then will these things be? and what will be the sign when these things are about to come to pass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> And he said, Take heed that ye be not deceived. For many will come in my name, saying, I am He, and the time is at hand. Go not after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> And when ye hear of wars and tumults, be not terrified; for these things must first come to pass; but not immediately is the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then he said to them, Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> and there will be great earthquakes, and in divers places famines and pestilences; and there will be fearful sights and great signs from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But before all these things they will lay their hands on you and persecute you, delivering you up to synagogues, and into prisons, and bringing you before kings and governors on account of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> It will turn out to you an opportunity for bearing testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> Settle it therefore in your hearts not to meditate l beforehand what ye shall answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> For I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries will not be able to withstand or gainsay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> And ye will be delivered up both by parents and brothers and kinsmen and friends; and some of you will they put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> And ye will be hated by all on account of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> But not a hair of your head will be lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 10:22; James 1:3"> By your constancy secure your lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Daniel 9:27; Mark 13:14"> But when ye see Jerusalem encompassed by armies, then know that her desolation is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> Then let those who are in Judaea flee to the mountains; and let those who are within the city go out of it, and let not those in the open country enter it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> For these are days of vengeance, that all things which are written may be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> But woe to the women with child, and to those having children at the breast, in those days! For there will be great distress in the land, and wrath upon this people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Revelation 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> and they will fall by the edge of the sword, and will be led away captive into all the nations; and Jerusalem will be trodden down by the gentiles, until the times of the gentiles are fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:30–31; Revelation 6:12–14"> And there will be signs in the sun and moon and stars; and on the earth distress of nations, in perplexity at the roaring of the sea and waves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> men's hearts failing them from fear, and from looking for those things which are coming on the world; for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:7; Mark 13:26"> And then will they see the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 8:23; Isaiah 60:1–2; Ephesians 4:30"> And when these things begin to come to pass, look up, and lift up your heads; for your redemption draweth nigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:32–35; Mark 13:28–30"> And he spoke to them a parable: Behold the fig-tree and all the trees;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:57"> when they have already shot forth, ye see it, and know of yourselves that summer is now nigh at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> So also, when ye see these things coming to pass, know ye that the kingdom of God is nigh at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> Truly do I say to you, that this generation will not pass away, till all shall have taken place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Mark 13:31; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 4:19; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:40"> But take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be weighed down with surfeiting and drunkenness and worldly cares, and that day come upon you unawares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Psalms 11:6; Jeremiah 48:43–44"> For as a snare will it come on all that dwell on the face of the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Matthew 25:13; 1 Peter 5:8"> But watch at all times, and pray that ye may be able to escape all the things that are about to come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 22:39; Mark 11:19; Matthew 21:17"> And during the daytime he was teaching in the temple; but at night he went out, and lodged in the mount called the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 8:1–2"> And all the people came early in the morning to him in the temple, to hear him.</span>
