<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> When he had ended all his discourse in the hearing of the people, he entered Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> And a certain centurion's servant, who was dear to him, was sick, and about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> And having heard about Jesus, he sent to him elders of the Jews, and besought him to come and save his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> And they came to Jesus, and besought him earnestly, saying, He is worthy that thou shouldst do this for him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> for he loveth our nation, and himself built the synagogue for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> And Jesus went with them. And when he was now not far from the house, the centurion sent friends, saying, Lord, trouble not thyself; for I am not worthy that thou shouldst come under my roof;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Psalms 33:9; Luke 4:36"> on which account I did not think myself worthy to come to thee; but command with a word, and let my servant be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> For even I am a man set under authority, having soldiers under me; and I say to this one, Go, and he goeth; and to another, Come, and he cometh; and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> And Jesus hearing this, wondered at him; and turning round said to the multitude that followed him, I say to you, Not even in Israel have I found such faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> And they who were sent, returning to the house, found the servant well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> And it came to pass the day after, that he was going to a city called Nain; and many of his disciples were with him, and a great multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> And as he came near the gate of the city, lo! there was carried out dead an only son of his mother, and she was a widow; and a great multitude from the city was with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 86:15"> And when the Lord saw her, he was moved with compassion for her, and said to her, Weep not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> And he came up, and touched the bier; and they who bore it stood still; and he said, Young man, I say to thee, Rise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> And he that was dead sat up and began to speak; and he gave him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> And fear seized on all, and they gave glory to God, saying, A great prophet hath risen up among us; and, God hath visited his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> And this report about him went forth in all Judaea, and in all the neighboring country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> And the disciples of John told him all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> And calling to him two of his disciples, John sent them to the Lord, saying, Art thou he that is to come, or are we to look for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> And the men came to him and said, John the Baptist hath sent us to thee, saying, Art thou he that is to come, or are we to look for another?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; Psalms 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> In that hour he cured many of diseases, and plagues, and evil spirits, and to many who were blind he gave sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> And he answered and said to them, Go and tell John what ye have seen and heard; that the blind receive sight, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, to the poor good tidings are brought;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> and blessed is he, whoever shall find no occasion of stumbling in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> And when the messengers of John had departed, he began to say to the multitudes concerning John, What have ye gone out into the wilderness to see? the reeds shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> But what have ye gone out to see? a man clothed in soft raiment? Lo! they who wear gorgeous apparel, and live luxuriously, are in kings' palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> But what have ye gone out to see? A prophet? Yea, I say to you, and more than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> This is he of whom it is written, “Lo! I send my messenger before thy face, who shall prepare thy way before thee.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> I say to you, Among those born of women there is no greater prophet than John; but he that is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> And all the people when they heard him, and the publicans, acknowledged God as righteous by being baptized with the baptism of John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> But the Pharisees and the lawyers rejected the purpose of God toward themselves, not being baptized by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> To what then shall I compare the men of this generation, and what are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They are like children sitting in the market-place, and calling one to another, saying, We piped to you, and ye did not dance; we sung a dirge, and ye did not weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> For John the Baptist hath come not eating bread, nor drinking wine; and ye say, He hath a demon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> The Son of man hath come eating and drinking; and ye say, Behold, a glutton and a wine-bibber, a friend of publicans and sinners!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> But wisdom is acknowledged by all her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> And one of the Pharisees asked him to eat with him; and he went into the Pharisee's house, and reclined at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> And lo! a woman who was in the city, a sinner, learning that he was at table in the Pharisee's house, brought an alabaster-bottle of ointment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> and standing behind at his feet, weeping, began to wet his feet with tears, and wiped them with the hair of her head, and kissed his feet, and anointed them with the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> And the Pharisee who had invited him, when he saw this, said within himself, This man, if he were a prophet, would know who, and what sort of woman, this is that toucheth him; for she is a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> And Jesus answering said to him, Simon, I have somewhat to say to thee. And he saith, Teacher, say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> A certain money-lender had two debtors; one owed five hundred denaries, and the other fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> When they had nothing to pay, he freely remitted the debt of both. Which of them, now, will love him the most?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> Simon answering said, He, I suppose, to whom he remitted the most. And he said to him, Thou hast judged rightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> And turning to the woman, he said to Simon, Seest thou this woman? I entered thy house, no water didst thou give me for my feet; but she wet my feet with tears, and wiped them with her hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> No kiss didst thou give me; but she, from the time I came in, did not cease to kiss my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> My head with oil thou didst not anoint; but she anointed my feet with costly ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> Wherefore, I say to thee, her many sins have been forgiven; for she loved much; but he to whom little is forgiven loveth little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> And he said to her, Thy sins have been forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> And those who were at table with him began to say within themselves, Who is this that even forgiveth sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> But he said to the woman, Thy faith hath saved thee; go in peace.</span>
