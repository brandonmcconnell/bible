<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:19; Acts 16:1; 1 Thessalonians 1:1–10"> Paul, and Silvanus, and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 1:8"> Grace be to you, and peace, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; 2 Peter 1:5–10; Proverbs 4:18"> We ought to thank God always for you, brethren, as is fit, because your faith increaseth exceedingly, and the love of every one of you all toward each ether aboundeth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; James 5:7–8; Hebrews 12:1–3"> so that we ourselves glory in you among the churches of God, for your constancy and faith in all your persecutions and the afflictions which ye endure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:28; Acts 14:22; Luke 20:35"> an indication of the righteous judgment of God, by which ye will be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which ye are also suffering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Colossians 3:25; Psalms 94:20–23"> since it will be just with God to repay distress to them that distress you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Matthew 16:27; Jude 1:14–15"> and to you the distressed rest with us, when the Lord Jesus shall be manifested from heaven, with the angels of his might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 2 Peter 3:7; Hebrews 10:27"> in flaming fire, taking vengeance on them that know not God, and that obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 14:10–11; Matthew 25:46"> who will be punished with everlasting destruction proceeding from the presence of the Lord and from the glory of his power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:18; 1 Corinthians 3:13; John 17:10"> when he shall come in that day to be glorified in his saints, and to be admired in all who believed; for our testimony to you was believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:9–13; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> For which end we also pray always for you, that our God may count you worthy of your calling, and perfect &#91;&#91;in you&#93;&#93; all pleasure in goodness, and the work of faith, with power;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 4:14; John 17:10"> that the name of our Lord Jesus Christ may be glorified in you, and ye in him, according to the grace of our God and of the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
