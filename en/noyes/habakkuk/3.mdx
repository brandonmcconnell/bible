<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 7:1–17; Psalms 86:1–17; Psalms 90:1–17"> The prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, in the form of an ode.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 85:6; Psalms 90:13–17; Hosea 6:2–3"> O Jehovah, I have heard thy words, and tremble. O Jehovah, revive thy work in the midst of the years, In the midst of the years make it known, In wrath remember mercy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 4:4; Obadiah 1:9"> God cometh from Teman, And the Holy One from mount Paran; His glory covereth the heavens, And the earth is full of his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Matthew 17:2; Job 26:14"> His brightness is as the light; Rays stream forth from his hand, And there is the hiding-place of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:29–30; Numbers 16:46–49; Psalms 78:50–51"> Before him goeth the pestilence, And the plague followeth his steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 49:26; Deuteronomy 33:15; Nahum 1:5"> He standeth, and measureth the earth; He beholdeth, and maketh the nations tremble; The everlasting mountains are broken asunder; The eternal hills sink down; The eternal paths are trodden by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 15:14–16; Genesis 10:6–7; Genesis 25:1–4"> I see the tents of Cushan in affliction, And the canopies of the land of Midian tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:17; Deuteronomy 33:26–27; Exodus 14:21–22"> Is the anger of Jehovah kindled against the rivers, Is thy wrath against the rivers, Is thy indignation against the floods, That thou ridest on with thy horses, Upon thy chariots of victory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 105:41; Psalms 78:15–16"> Thy bow is made bare; Curses are the arrows of thy word; Thou causest rivers to break forth from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:24; Joshua 4:23–24; Psalms 93:3"> The mountains see thee and tremble; The flood of waters overflows; The deep uttereth his voice, And lifteth up his hands on high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 144:5–6; Psalms 18:12–14; Joshua 10:11–13"> The sun and the moon remain in their habitation, At the light of thine arrows which fly, At the brightness of the lightning of thy spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:12–13; Numbers 21:23–35"> Thou marchest through the land in indignation; Thou thrashest the nations in anger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:6; Psalms 105:15; Psalms 68:19–23"> Thou goest forth for the deliverance of thy people, For the deliverance of thine anointed. Thou smitest the head of the house of the wicked; Thou destroyest the foundation even to the neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:14; Daniel 11:40; Psalms 118:10–12"> Thou piercest with his own spears the chief of his captains, Who rushed like a whirlwind to scatter us; Who exulted, as if they should devour the distressed in a hiding-place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 77:19; Habakkuk 3:8; Exodus 15:8"> Thou ridest through the sea with thy horses, Through the raging of mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:9; Habakkuk 1:5–11; Habakkuk 3:2"> I have heard, and my heart trembleth; My lips quiver at the voice; Rottenness entereth into my bones, and my knees tremble, That I must wait in silence for the day of trouble, When the invader shall come up against my people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:17; Joel 1:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:15–18"> For the fig-tree shall not blossom, And there shall be no fruit upon the vine; The produce of the olive shall fail, And the fields shall yield no food. The flocks shall he cut off from the folds, And there shall be no herd in the stalls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 13:15; Philippians 4:4; Micah 7:7"> Yet will I rejoice in Jehovah, I will exult in God, my helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:34; Psalms 18:33; Philippians 4:13"> The Lord Jehovah is my strength; He will make my feet like the hind's, And cause me to walk upon my high places. “To the leader of the music on my stringed instruments.”</span>
