<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> The prophecy which was revealed to the prophet Habakkuk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; Psalms 13:1–2"> How long, O Jehovah, do I cry, and thou dost not hear! How long do I complain to thee of violence, and thou dost not save!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> Why dost thou suffer me to see iniquity, And why dost thou look upon wickedness? For spoiling and violence are before me; There is contention, and strife exalteth itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> Therefore the law faileth, And judgment is not pronounced according to truth; For the wicked encompasseth the righteous, Therefore wrong judgment is pronounced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> Behold ye among the nations, and look! Yea, wonder, and be astonished! For I do a work in your days Which ye will not believe though it be told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> For behold, I raise up the Chaldeans, A fierce and swift people, Which go over the breadth of the earth, To take possession of dwelling-places that are not their own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> They are terrible and dreadful; From themselves go forth their law and their dignity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> Their horses are swifter than leopards, And fiercer than evening wolves. Their horsemen leap proudly; Their horsemen come from far; They fly like an eagle, hastening to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> All of them come for violence; The multitude of their faces is directed forwards, And they gather captives as sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> They also scoff at kings, And princes are to them a laughing-stock; They deride every stronghold, For they heap up earth and take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> Then their spirit is uplifted, and they transgress, and become guilty; This their strength is made their god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> Art thou not from everlasting, O Jehovah, my God, my Holy One? We shall not die! Thou, O Jehovah, hast appointed them for judgment; Thou, O Rock, hast ordained them for chastisement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil, And canst not look on wickedness; Why then dost thou look on transgressors, And art silent, when the wicked swalloweth up the man that is more righteous than he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> And why makest thou men as the fishes of the sea, As the reptiles that have no ruler?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 10:9"> They take up all of them with the hook, They catch them in their net, And gather them in their drag; Therefore they rejoice and exult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore they sacrifice to their net, And burn incense to their drag; Because by them their portion is fat, And their food plenteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> Shall they therefore empty the net, And slay the nations continually without mercy?</span>
