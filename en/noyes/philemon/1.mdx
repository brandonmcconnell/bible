<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy the brother, to Philemon our beloved friend and fellow-laborer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> and to the beloved Apphia, and to Archippus our fellow-soldier, and to the church in thy house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I thank my God at all times, making mention of thee in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> hearing of thy love and faith, which thou hast toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> that the fellowship of thy faith may become effectual in the knowledge of every good thing which is in us, for Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> For we have great thankfulness and comfort on account of thy love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed by thee, brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Wherefore, though I have much boldness in Christ to enjoin upon thee that which is befitting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> yet for love's sake I beseech thee rather; being such a one as Paul an old man, and now also a prisoner of Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I beseech thee for my child, whom I begot in my bonds, Onesimus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> who in time past was unprofitable to thee, but is now profitable to thee and to me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> whom I have sent back; and do thou &#91;&#91;receive&#93;&#93; him, that is, my own flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> Whom I would have retained with me, that in thy stead he might minister to me in the bonds of the gospel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> but I chose to do nothing without thy consent, that thy benefit may be not as from necessity, but willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> For perhaps he was separated from thee for a season to this end, that thou shouldst receive him back as thine for ever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> no longer as a bond-servant, but above a bond-servant, a brother beloved, especially to me, but how much more to thee, both in the flesh, and in the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If thou then regardest me as a partner, receive him as myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> And if he wronged thee in anything, or oweth thee, put that to my account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I Paul have written it with my own hand, I will repay it; not to say to thee, that to me thou owest even thy own self besides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yea, brother, let me have joy of thee in the Lord; refresh my heart in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Having confidence in thy obedience I have written to thee, knowing that thou wilt do even more than I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> And at the same time make ready a lodging for me; for I hope that through your prayers I shall be granted to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras, my fellow-prisoner in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow-laborers, salute thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.</span>
