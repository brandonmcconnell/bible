<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> Jesus answered and spoke to them again in parables, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> The kingdom of heaven is like a certain king, who made a marriage-feast for his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> and sent forth his servants to call to the feast those who had been invited; and they would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again, he sent other servants, saying, Tell those who have been invited, Behold, I have prepared my dinner; my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready; come to the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they made light of it, and went away; one to his farm, another to his merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> And the rest seized his servants, and ill-treated and slew them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> And the king was enraged; and sent forth his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then saith he to his servants, The feast is ready, but those who were invited were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Go therefore into the thoroughfares, and as many as ye find, invite to the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> So those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together all, as many as they found, both bad and good; and the bride-chamber was fully furnished with guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And the king, coming in to view the guests, saw there a man who had not on a wedding garment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> and he saith to him, Friend, how camest thou in here, not having a wedding garment? And he was struck dumb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king said to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and cast him out into the outer darkness; there will be wailing and gnashing of teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are called, but few are chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalms 2:2"> Then the Pharisees went and consulted together how l they might ensnare him in speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they send out to him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, Teacher, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of God in truth, and thou carest for no one; for thou regardest not the person of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Tell us, therefore, What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But Jesus, perceiving their wickedness, said, Why are ye trying me, hypocrites?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Show me the tribute money. And they brought to him a denary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And Jesus saith to them, Whose is this image and inscription?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They say, Caesar's. Then saith he to them, Render then to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> And hearing this, they wondered, and left him, and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> The same day there came to him Sadducees, saying that there is no resurrection; and they asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, Teacher, Moses said, “If a man die having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed to his brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were with us seven brothers; and the first married and died; and, having no offspring, left his wife to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And so also the second, and the third, even to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And last of all the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the resurrection, then, of which of the seven will she be the wife? For they all had her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> Jesus answered and said to them, Ye err, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read that which was spoken to you by God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> “I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob”? He is not a God of the dead, but of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And when the multitudes heard this, they were astonished at his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> And the Pharisees, hearing that he had put the Sadducees to silence, assembled together;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> and one of them, a lawyer, asked, trying him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> Teacher, which commandment is great in the Law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he said to him, “Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the great and first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> There is a second like it: “Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 7:12; 1 John 4:19–21"> On these two commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 22:34"> And while the Pharisees were assembled, Jesus asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; John 6:68–69; Matthew 16:13–17"> saying, What think ye concerning the Christ? whose son is he? They say to him, David's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:2; Revelation 4:2; Mark 12:36"> He saith to them, How then doth David in the Spirit call him lord? saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Acts 2:34–35"> “The Lord said to my lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I put thine enemies under thy feet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Revelation 22:16; John 8:58"> If David then calleth him lord, how is he his son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 12:34; Luke 20:40; Luke 14:6"> And no one was able to answer him a word; nor durst any one from that day question him any more.</span>
