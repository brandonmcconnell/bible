<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Luke 4:1–13; Mark 1:12–15"> Then was Jesus led up by the Spirit into the wilderness, to be tempted by the Devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Exodus 34:28; 1 Kings 19:8"> And when he had tasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> And the tempter came and said to him, If thou art the Son of God, command that these stones become loaves of bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> But he answered and said, It is written, “Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that proceedeth from the mouth of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 4:9; Matthew 27:53; Revelation 11:2"> Then the Devil taketh him into the holy city, and setteth him on the pinnacle of the temple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalms 34:7"> and saith to him, If thou art the Son of God, cast thyself down; for it is written, “He will give his angels charge concerning thee; and in their hands they will bear thee up, that thou mayst not dash thy foot against a stone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalms 78:18"> Jesus said to him, Again it is written, “Thou shalt not make trial of the Lord thy God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Revelation 11:15; Psalms 49:16–17"> Again, the Devil taketh him m an exceeding high mountain, and showeth him all the kingdoms of the world, and their glory;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Daniel 5:18–19; John 16:11"> and said to him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13–14; Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 24:14"> Then saith Jesus to him, Be gone, Satan! for it is written, “Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Hebrews 1:14; Luke 22:43"> Then the Devil leaveth him; and lo! angels came and ministered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> Now when Jesus heard that John had been cast into prison, he withdrew into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> And leaving Nazara, he came and dwelt in Capernaum, on the shore of the lake, in the borders of Zebulun and Naphtali;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> “The land of Zebulun, and the land of Naphtali, by the way of the lake beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> the people that sat in darkness, saw a great light; and upon those who sat in the region and shadow of death, light arose.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From that time Jesus began to preach, and to say, Repent; for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> And while walking by the lake of Galilee, he saw two brothers, Simon called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the lake; for they were fishers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> And he saith to them, Come after me, and I will make you fishers of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> And they immediately left their nets, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> And going on from thence, he saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets; and he called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> And they immediately left the boat and their father, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> And he went round the whole of Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the glad tidings of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> And his fame went forth into all Syria; and they brought to him all that were sick, afflicted with various diseases and torments, and those who were possessed by demons, and lunatics, and those who were struck with palsy; and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> And great multitudes followed him from Galilee and Decapolis and Jerusalem and Judaea, and from beyond the Jordan.</span>
