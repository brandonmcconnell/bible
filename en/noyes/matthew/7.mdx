<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> Judge not, that ye be not judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> For with what judgment ye judge, ye will be judged; and with what measure ye mete, it will be measured to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> And why dost thou look at the mote that is in thy brother's eye, and not perceive the beam in thine own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me take the mote out of thine eye; when, lo! the beam is in thine own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalms 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> Hypocrite! first cast the beam out of thine own eye; and then wilt thou see clearly to take the mote out of thy brother's eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> Give not that which is holy to dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine; lest they trample them under their feet, and turn upon you and rend you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> Ask, and it will be given you; seek, and ye will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 81:10; Psalms 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> For every one that asketh, receiveth; and he that seeketh, findeth; and to him that knocketh, the door will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Or what man is there of you, who, if his son ask for bread, will give him a stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> or, if he ask for a fish, will give him a serpent?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> If ye then, though evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who is in heaven give good things to them that ask him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> All things, then, whatever ye would that men should do to you, do ye also so to them; for this is the Law and the Prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> Enter in at the strait gate; for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction; and many are they who go in thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> For strait is the gate, and narrow the way, that leadeth to life; and few are they who find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> Beware of the false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> Ye may know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> So every good tree beareth good fruit; but a bad tree beareth bad fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, nor call a bad tree bear good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that beareth not good fruit is cut down, and cast into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> So then ye may know them by their fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> Not every one that saith to me, Lord, Lord, will enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in thy name, and in thy name cast out demons, and in thy name work many miracles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalms 6:8; Luke 13:25"> And then will I declare to them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> Every one then that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, shall be likened to a wise man, who built his house upon a rock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalms 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> and the rain descended, and the streams came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not; for it was founded upon a rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened to a foolish man, who built his house upon the sand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> and the rain descended, and the streams came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell; and great was its fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Mark 1:22; John 7:46"> And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended these sayings, that the multitudes were astonished at his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 20:8; Deuteronomy 18:18–19"> For he taught them as having authority, and not as their scribes.</span>
