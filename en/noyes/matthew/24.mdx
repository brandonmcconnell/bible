<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 13:1–37; Luke 21:5–36; Ezekiel 8:6"> And Jesus went out, and was going from the temple; and his disciples came to him, to show him the buildings of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Jeremiah 26:18; Micah 3:12"> And he answering said to them, See ye not all these? Truly do I say to you, There will not be left here one stone upon another, that will not be thrown down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 13:3–4; Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11"> And as he was sitting upon the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to him privately, saying, Tell us, when will these things be? and what will be the signs of thy coming, and of the end of the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:3"> And Jesus answering said to them, See that no one deceive you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 24:11; Matthew 24:24; Jeremiah 14:14"> For many will come in my name, saying, I am the Christ, and will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:7–8; Luke 21:9; 1 Peter 3:14–15"> And ye are to hear of wars, and rumors of wars; see that ye be not troubled; for these things must come to pass; but not yet is the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:11; Revelation 6:8; Isaiah 19:2"> For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there will be famines and earthquakes in divers places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:59"> But all these things are the beginning of travail-pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 16:2; John 15:19–20; Luke 21:16–17"> Then will they deliver you up to distress, and will kill you; and ye will be hated by all nations on account of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Luke 21:16; Matthew 11:6"> And then will many fall away; and will deliver up one another, and will hate one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Matthew 7:15; Matthew 24:5"> And many false prophets will arise, and will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 2:4–5; James 4:1–4; Revelation 3:15"> And because iniquity shall abound, the love of the many will wax cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; Mark 13:13; Revelation 2:10"> But he that endureth to the end will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 14:6; Romans 10:18; Romans 16:25–26"> And these glad tidings of the kingdom will be published throughout the whole world for a testimony to all the nations; and then will come the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Mark 13:14"> When, therefore, ye see the abomination of desolation, spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place, (let him that readeth understand,)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:15–17; Luke 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:7"> then let those who are in Judaea flee to the mountains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> let not him that is on the house-top go down to take away the things belonging to his house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and let not him that is in the field turn back to take his garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 23:29–30; Deuteronomy 28:53–56; Luke 21:23"> And woe to the women with child, and to those with children at the breast, in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:29; Acts 1:12"> And pray that your flight be not in winter, nor on a sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Malachi 4:1"> For there will then be great distress, such as hath not been from the beginning of the world to this time; no, nor shall ever be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 13:20; 2 Timothy 2:10; Matthew 24:31"> And were not those days to shortened, no flesh would be saved; but, for the sake of the chosen, these days will be shortened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Luke 17:23–24; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> Then if any one say to you, Lo! here is the Christ; or there, believe him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Mark 13:22; Revelation 13:13–14"> For there will rise up false Christs, and false prophets, and will show great signs and wonders, so that, if possible, even the chosen will be deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:1; Luke 21:13; Isaiah 48:5–6"> Lo! I have told you beforehand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 3:2–3; Isaiah 40:3; Matthew 3:1"> If therefore they say to you, Lo! he is in the wilderness; go not forth; lo! he is in the private chambers; believe them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 17:24–37; Zechariah 9:14; Job 37:3"> For as the lightning cometh from the east, and shineth even to the west, so will be the coming of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 17:37; Job 39:27–30; Deuteronomy 28:49"> Wherever the carcass is, there will the eagles be gathered together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Amos 8:9; Joel 3:15; Isaiah 13:10"> And immediately after the distress of those days will the sun be darkened, and the moon will not give her light, and the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Daniel 7:13; Luke 21:27"> And then will appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven; and all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and will see the Son of man coming upon the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:27; Isaiah 27:13"> And he will send forth his angels with a great trumpet, and they will gather his chosen from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:28–29; Luke 21:29–30"> And from the fig-tree learn its parable. When its branch hath now become tender, and putteth forth its leaves, ye know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 7:2–14"> So also, when ye see all these things, know ye that he is near, at the doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 13:30–31; Luke 21:32–33; Matthew 16:28"> Truly do I say to you, This generation will not pass away, till all these things take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25; Numbers 23:19"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> But that day and hour knoweth no one, not even the angels of heaven, nor the Son, but the Father only.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> But as were the days of Noah, so will be the coming of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> For as in the days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day when Noah entered the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> and knew not, until the flood came, and took them all away, so also will be the coming of the Son of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> Then will two men be in the field; one will be taken, and one left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> Two women will be grinding at the mill; one will be taken, and one left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> Watch, therefore, for ye know not on what day your Lord is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> But be sure of this, that if the master of the house had known in what watch the thief was coming, he would have watched, and would not have suffered his house to be broken through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> Therefore be ye also ready; for at an hour when ye think not, the Son of man cometh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Matthew 25:23; 1 Corinthians 4:1–2"> Who then is the faithful and wise servant, whom his lord placed over his household, to give them their food in due season?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> Happy is that servant whom his lord, when he cometh, shall find so doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> Truly do I say to you, that he will place him in charge of all his substance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> But if the bad servant say in his heart, My lord is tarrying long;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Revelation 17:6; Revelation 16:6; Micah 3:5"> and begin to beat his fellow-servants, and eat and drink with the drunken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Revelation 3:3; Matthew 24:42–44"> the lord of that servant will come in a day when he looketh not for it, and at an hour when he is not aware;</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 22:13; Luke 12:46"> and will cut him asunder, and appoint him his portion with the hypocrites; there will be wailing and gnashing of teeth.</span>
