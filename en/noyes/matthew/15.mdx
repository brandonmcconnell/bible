<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then come to Jesus Pharisees and scribes from Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> But he answered and said to them, Why do ye too transgress the commandment of God for the sake of your tradition?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> For God commanded, saying, “Honor thy father and thy mother;” and, “He that curseth father or mother, let him surely die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> But ye say, Whoever shall say to his father or his mother, Whatever thou mightst receive in aid from me is a gift &#91;&#91;to God&#93;&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> he shall not honor his father or his mother. Thus have ye made void the law of God for the sake of your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> Hypocrites! well did Isaiah prophesy concerning you, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> “This people honoreth me with their lips, but their heart is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But in vain do they worship me, teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> And calling the multitude, he said to them, Hear, and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man; but that which cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then come the disciples, and say to him, Dost thou know that the Pharisees were offended, when they heard that saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> But he answered and said, Every plant which my heavenly Father did not plant will be rooted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> Let them alone; they are blind leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both will fall into a ditch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> And Peter answering said to him, Explain to us that dark saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> And he said, Are ye too still without discernment?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> Do ye not understand, that whatever entereth the mouth goeth into the stomach, and is cast out into the drain?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But the things which proceed from the mouth come out of the heart; and these defile a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> For out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimony, blasphemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> These are the things which defile a man; but to eat with unwashed hands defileth not a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> And Jesus, going from thence, withdrew to the parts of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> And, lo! a woman of Canaan came out from those borders, and cried out, saying, Have compassion on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is grievously afflicted with a demon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But he did not answer her a word. And his disciples came and besought him, saying Send her away, for she is crying out after us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> But he answered and said, I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> Then she came and fell down before him, saying, Lord, help me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> But he answering said, It is not allowable to take the children's bread, and throw it to the little dogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> And she said, Yea, Lord; for the little dogs eat of the crumbs which fall from the table of their masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> Then Jesus answering said to her, O woman, great is thy faith; be it done to thee even as thou wilt. And her daughter was cured from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> And departing thence, Jesus came near the lake of Galilee; and going up the mountain, he sat down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> And great multitudes came to him, having with them those who were lame, blind, dumb, maimed, and many others; and they laid them down at his feet, and he healed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> so that the multitude wondered, when they saw the dumb speaking, the maimed sound, and the lame walking, and the blind seeing; and they gave glory to the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> And Jesus having called his disciples to him, said, I have compassion on the multitude, because they have remained with me now three days, and have nothing to eat; and I am not willing to send them away fasting, lest they faint on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> And the disciples say to him, Whence should we have so many loaves in a wilderness, as to fill so great a multitude?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> And Jesus saith to them, How many loaves have ye? And they said, Seven, and a few small fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> And bidding the multitude to lie down on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> he took the seven loaves and the fishes, and gave thanks, and broke, and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples to the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> And they all ate, and were filled; and they took up of the fragments that remained, seven baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And they who ate were four thousand men, besides children and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> Then he sent away the multitudes, and went on board the boat, and came into the borders of Magadan.</span>
