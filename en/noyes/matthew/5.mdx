<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:13; Matthew 15:29; Mark 4:1"> And seeing the multitudes, he went up into the mountain; and when he had sat down, his disciples came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 8:35; Luke 6:20–26; Acts 10:34"> And he opened his mouth, and taught them, saying:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Proverbs 29:23; Psalms 34:18"> Blessed are the poor in spirit; for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; 2 Corinthians 7:9–10; John 16:20–22"> (5:5) Blessed are they who mourn; for they will be comforted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:11; Isaiah 29:19; James 3:13"> (5:4) Blessed are the meek; for they will inherit the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 107:9; John 6:27"> Blessed are they who hunger and thirst after righteousness; for they will be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32–1; Matthew 6:14–15; 2 Samuel 22:26"> Blessed are the merciful; for they will obtain mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 24:4; Psalms 51:10; Hebrews 10:22"> Blessed are the pure in heart; for they will see God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; James 3:16–18; Romans 12:18"> Blessed are the peace-makers; for they will be called sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Peter 3:13–14; 2 Corinthians 4:17"> Blessed are they who are persecuted for righteousness' sake; for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Luke 21:17; Mark 13:13"> Blessed are ye, when men revile you, and persecute you, and say every thing that is bad against you, falsely, for my sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:23; James 1:2; Colossians 3:24"> Rejoice, and be exceeding glad; for great is your reward in heaven; for so did they persecute the prophets that were before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 14:34–35; Mark 9:49–50"> Ye are the salt of the earth. But if the salt have lost its savor, wherewith shall itself be salted? It is thenceforth good for nothing, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot by men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; John 8:12; Ephesians 5:8–14"> Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Mark 4:21; Luke 8:16"> nor do men light a lamp, and put it under the bushel, but on the lamp-stand; and it giveth light to all that are in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Titus 2:7; John 15:8"> In like manner let your light shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Luke 16:17; Galatians 4:4–5"> Think not that I came to destroy the Law or the Prophets: I came not to destroy, but to fulfill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:17; Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25"> For truly do I say to you, Not till heaven and earth pass away, shall one jot or one tittle pass from the Law, till all be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; James 2:10–11; Matthew 28:20"> Whoever therefore shall break one of these least commandments, and shall teach men so, will be called the least in the kingdom of heaven; but whoever shall do and teach them, he will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Philippians 3:9; Romans 9:30–32"> For I say to you, Unless your righteousness shall exceed that of the scribes and Pharisees, ye will not enter the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:17; Exodus 20:13; Numbers 35:30–34"> Ye have heard that it was said to them of old time, “Thou shalt not kill; and whoever shall kill, shall be in danger of the Judges.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:14–15; 1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 2:9"> But I say to you, that whoever is angry with his brother shall be in danger of the Judges; and whoever shall say to his brother, Simpleton! shall be in danger of the Council; and whoever shall say, Fool! shall be in danger of hell-fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 11:25; Leviticus 6:2–6; Matthew 5:24"> Therefore, if thou bring thy gift to the altar, and there remember that thy brother hath aught against thee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:16; Romans 12:17–18; Matthew 18:15–17"> leave there thy gift before the altar, and go away; first be reconciled to thy brother, and then come and offer thy gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 12:58–59; Proverbs 25:8; Hebrews 3:13"> Agree with thine adversary at law quickly, while thou art on the road with him; lest the adversary deliver thee to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and thou be cast into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:34; Luke 12:59; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Truly do I say to thee, Thou wilt not come out thence, till thou hast paid the last farthing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:18; Proverbs 6:32; Exodus 20:14"> Ye have heard that it was said, “Thou shalt not commit adultery.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; Proverbs 6:25"> But I say to you, that whoever looketh on a woman to lust after her, hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Mark 9:43–48; Colossians 3:5"> And if thy right eye cause thee to offend, pluck it out, and cast it from thee; for it is better for thee that one of thy members should perish, than that thy whole body should be cast into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:29; 1 Peter 2:8"> And if thy right hand cause thee to offend, cut it off, and cast it from thee; for it is better for thee that one of thy members should perish, than that thy whole body should go away into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 19:7"> And it was said, “Whoever shall put away his wife, let him give her a writing of divorcement.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 7:3; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Luke 16:18"> But I say to you, that whoever putteth away his wife, unless it be on account of fornication, causeth her to commit adultery; and whoever shall marry her when put away, committeth adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:21; Leviticus 19:12; Ecclesiastes 5:4–6"> Again ye have heard that it was said to them of old time, “Thou shalt not swear falsely, but shalt perform to the Lord thine oaths.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="James 5:12; Isaiah 66:1; Deuteronomy 23:21–23"> But I say to you, Swear not at all; neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 48:2; Malachi 1:14; 2 Chronicles 6:6"> nor by the earth, for it is his footstool; nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the Great King;</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 12:25; Matthew 6:27; Matthew 23:16–21"> nor shalt thou swear by thy head, for thou canst not make one hair white or black.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:12; Colossians 4:6; Colossians 3:9"> But let your language be, Yea, yea; Nay; nay; for whatever is more than these cometh of evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 24:19–20; Deuteronomy 19:21; Exodus 21:22–27"> Ye have heard that it was said, “An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:15; 1 Peter 3:9; Romans 12:17–19"> But I say to you, that ye resist not the evil-doer; but whoever smiteth thee on the right cheek, turn to him the other also;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 6:29; 1 Corinthians 6:7"> and if any one intends to sue thee at the law, and take thy coat, give up to him thy cloak also;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:26; Matthew 27:32; Mark 15:21"> and whoever shall compel thee to go one mile, go with him two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Hebrews 13:16; Luke 6:30–36"> Give to him that asketh of thee; and from him that would borrow of thee, turn not away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Psalms 139:21–22; Galatians 5:13–14"> Ye have heard that it was said, “Thou shalt love thy neighbor, and hate thine enemy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–28"> But I say to you, Love your enemies, and pray for those who persecute you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 145:9; Luke 6:35; Acts 14:17"> that ye may become sons of your Father who is in heaven; for he maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:32–35; 1 Peter 2:20–23; Matthew 9:10–11"> For if ye love those who love you, what reward have ye? Do not even the publicans the same?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Luke 6:32; 1 Peter 2:20"> And if ye salute your brethren only, what do ye that excelleth? Do not even the heathen the same?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; James 1:4; Leviticus 19:2"> Be ye therefore perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect.</span>
