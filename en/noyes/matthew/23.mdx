<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spoke to the multitudes, and to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit in the seat of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> All, therefore, whatever they bid you, do and observe; but do not according to their works; for they say, and do not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> They bind heavy burdens, and lay them on men's shoulders, and will not themselves move them with a finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> And all their works they do to be observed by men. They make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge their fringes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> and love the first place at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> and salutations in the markets, and to be called by men, Rabbi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> But be not ye called Rabbi; for one is your teacher; and ye are all brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> And call no one your father on the earth; for one is your father, he who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Nor be ye called leaders; for one is your leader, the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> But the greatest among you will be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> And whoever shall exalt himself will be humbled; and whoever shall humble himself will be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites I because ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men; for ye go not in yourselves, nor suffer those who are entering to go in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> </span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte; and when he is made, ye make him two-fold mole a son of hell than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> Woe to you, blind guides, that say, Whoever sweareth by the temple, it is nothing; but whoever sweareth by the gold of the temple, is bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalms 94:8"> Fools and blind I for which is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifieth the gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And, Whoever sweareth by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever sweareth by the gift that is upon it, is bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> Blind men! For which is greater, the gift, or the altar flint sanctifieth the gift?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He then who sweareth by the altar, sweareth by it, and by all that is upon it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> and he that sweareth by the temple, sweareth by it, and by him who dwelleth in it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalms 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> and he who sweareth by heaven, sweareth by the throne of God, and by him who sitteth thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye pay tithes of the mint, and the dill, and the cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the Law, justice, and mercy, and faith; these ought ye to have done, and not to leave those undone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> Blind guides! who strain out a gnat, and swallow a camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye make clean the outside of the cup and the platter, but within they are full of robbery and licentiousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> Blind Pharisee! cleanse first the inside of the cup, that its outside side also may become clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like whited sepulchres, which outwardly indeed appear beautiful, but within are full of dead men's bones, and of all uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalms 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous to men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! because ye build the sepulchres of the prophets, and adorn the tombs of the righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> and say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> So that ye bear witness against yourselves, that ye are the sons of those who killed the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> Serpents, brood of vipers! how can ye escape the punishment of hell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> Therefore, behold, I send to you prophets, and wise men, and scribes; some of them ye will kill and crucify, and some of them ye will scourge in your synagogues, and persecute from city to city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> that on you may come all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zechariah, son of Barachiah, whom ye slew, between the temple and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> Truly do I say to you, All these things will come upon this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalms 91:4"> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that killeth the prophets, and stoneth those who are sent to her! How often would I have gathered thy children together, as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Lo! your house is left to you desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> For I say to you, Ye will not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.</span>
