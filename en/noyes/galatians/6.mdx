<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; John 13:34"> Bear ye one another's burdens, and thus fulfill the law of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Corinthians 8:2; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> For if a man thinketh himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalms 26:2; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> But let each one prove his own work, and then will he have his ground for boasting in himself alone, and not in comparison with another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Romans 14:10–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10–11"> for every one must bear his own load.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Romans 15:27; Deuteronomy 12:19"> Let him that is taught in the word share with the teacher in all good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12; Job 4:8"> Be not deceived; God is not mocked; for whatever a man soweth, that shall he also reap;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:13; James 3:18; 2 Peter 2:19"> for he that soweth to his flesh, shall of the flesh reap corruption; but he that soweth to the Spirit, shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> And let us not be faint-hearted in well-doing; for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to all, especially to those who are of the household of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> See in what large letters I have written to you with my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> As many as desire to make a fair show in the flesh, these are constraining you to be circumcised, only that they may not suffer persecution for the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> For not even do they who become circumcised themselves keep the Law; but they desire to have you circumcised, that they may glory in your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> But God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ; through whom the world is crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> For neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> And as many as walk by this rule, peace be upon them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:5; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> Henceforth let no one trouble me; for I bear the marks of Jesus on my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Timothy 4:22; Philemon 1:25"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brethren. Amen.</span>
