<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:7–8; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Galatians 1:6"> O foolish Galatians, who bewitched you? before whose eyes Jesus Christ was plainly set forth among you crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> This only I desire to learn from you: Was it from the works of the Law that ye received the Spirit, or by the preaching of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> Are ye so foolish? Having begun with the Spirit, do ye now end with the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Have ye suffered so much in vain? if indeed it be really in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> Doth he then who is supplying to you the Spirit, and working miracles among you, do it by the works of the Law, or by the preaching of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> Even as Abraham “believed God, and it was accounted to him as righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> Know then that they who have faith, these are the sons of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> Moreover the Scripture, foreseeing that God was to accept the gentiles as righteous by faith, proclaimed beforehand the glad tidings to Abraham, saying, “In thee shall all nations be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 4:16; Galatians 3:29; Romans 4:24"> So then they who have faith are blessed with believing Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> For as many as rely on the works of the Law are under a curse; for it is written, “Cursed is every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the Law, to do them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> But further, that through the observance of the Law no one is accepted as righteous with God is evident; for “the righteous shall live by faith.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> And the Law hath nothing to do with faith; but &#91;&#91;its language is&#93;&#93;, “He that hath done them shall live in them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ redeemed us from the curse of the Law, having become a curse for us; for it is written, “Cursed is every one that is hanged on a beam of wood,”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> to the end that in Christ Jesus the blessing promised to Abraham might come to the gentiles, that we through faith might receive the Spirit which was promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brethren, I speak according to what is practised among men; no one sets aside even a human covenant, or makes additions to it, after it has been ratified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> But the promises were made to Abraham and “to his offspring.” He doth not say, “and to offsprings,” as speaking of many, but, as speaking of one, “and to thy offspring,” which is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> And what I mean is this; that a covenant that was before ratified by God, the Law, which came four hundred and thirty years after, cannot annul, so as to make void the promise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> for if the inheritance cometh from the Law, it ceaseth to be the consequence of the promise; but to Abraham God gave it by promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> To what end then was the Law? It was added because of transgressions, till the offspring should come to whom the promise belongeth, having been ordained through angels by the hand of a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> Now no mediator is a mediator of one; but God is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> Is then the Law against the promises of God? Far be it! For if a law had been given which was able to give life, righteousness would indeed have been by the Law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> but the Scripture shut up all under sin, that the blessing promised through faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> But before faith came, we were kept in ward under the Law, shut up unto the faith which was to be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> So then the Law hath been our schoolmaster, to lead us to Christ, that we might be accepted as righteous through faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> but faith having come, we are no longer under a schoolmaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> For ye are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> for as many of you as were baptized into Christ, did put on Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is no male and female; for ye are all one in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> and if ye belong to Christ, then are ye Abraham's offspring, heirs according to the promise.</span>
