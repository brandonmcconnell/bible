<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:19–22; Lamentations 2:19; Isaiah 1:21"> How is the gold become dim! how is the most fine gold changed! The hallowed stones are cast forth at the head of every street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:14; Jeremiah 19:11; Romans 9:21–23"> The noble sons of Zion, comparable to fine gold, How are they esteemed as earthen pitchers, the work of the hands of the potter!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Luke 23:28–29; Lamentations 2:20"> The very jackals reach forth the breast; they suckle their young; But the daughter of my people is become cruel, like the ostriches of the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 22:15; Lamentations 2:11–12; Matthew 7:9–11"> The tongue of the sucking child cleaveth to the roof of his mouth for thirst; Young children ask for bread, and no man breaketh it for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:3–7; Luke 16:19; Luke 15:16"> Those that fed on dainties are desolate in the streets; Those that have been brought up in scarlet embrace the dunghill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 19:25; Luke 12:47; Luke 10:12"> The punishment of the daughter of my people is greater than the punishment of Sodom, Which was overthrown in a moment, though no hands came against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 51:7; Song of Solomon 5:10; Psalms 144:12"> Her princes were purer than snow, whiter than milk; More ruddy than coral was their body; Their visage was of sapphire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 5:10; Psalms 102:3–5; Job 30:30"> Now darker than a coal is their countenance; they are not known in the streets. Their skin cleaveth to their bones; it is become dry, like wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Jeremiah 16:4"> More fortunate are the slain by the sword than the slain by famine; For these pine away, stricken through for want of the fruits of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:20; Isaiah 49:15; 2 Kings 6:26–29"> The hands of tender-hearted women cooked their own children; They were their food, in the destruction of the daughter of my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:20; Ezekiel 22:31; Jeremiah 17:27"> Jehovah hath spent upon them his fury; he hath poured out his fierce anger; He hath kindled a fire in Zion, which hath devoured its foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Psalms 48:4–6"> The kings of the earth believed not, nor all the inhabitants of the world, That the adversary would enter, and the enemy, within the gates of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Jeremiah 6:13; Jeremiah 26:8–9"> It was on account of the sins of her prophets, and the iniquities of her priests, Who shed in the midst of her the blood of the righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:34; Isaiah 56:10; Isaiah 1:15"> They stumbled like blind men through the streets, polluted with blood, So that men could not touch their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46"> “Depart! unclean!” men cried to them. “Depart, depart, touch not!” As they fled, they stumbled; men said among the nations, “They shall dwell there no more.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Lamentations 5:12; Isaiah 9:14–16; Deuteronomy 28:64–65"> The anger of Jehovah hath scattered them; he will no more care for them; They paid no regard to the priests, they had no compassion for the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; Isaiah 20:5; 2 Kings 24:7"> Still did our eyes fail, looking for help in vain; On our watch-tower did we watch for a nation that could not save us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Ezekiel 7:2–12; Lamentations 3:52"> They laid snares for our steps, so that we could not go in our streets; Our end is near; our days are accomplished, yea, our end is come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Jeremiah 4:13; Isaiah 5:26–28"> Swifter were our pursuers than the eagles of heaven; They chased us upon the mountains; they laid wait for us in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:21; Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 39:5"> The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of Jehovah, was taken in their pits, Under whose shadow we said that we should live among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Revelation 16:15; Job 1:1"> Rejoice and be glad, O daughter of Edom, that dwellest in the land of Uz! Yet to thee also shall the cup come! thou shalt be drunken, and shalt expose thy nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 50:20"> Thy punishment is at an end, O daughter of Zion! no more will he carry thee into captivity; But thine iniquity will he punish, O daughter of Edom! he will uncover thy sins.</span>
