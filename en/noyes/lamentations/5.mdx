<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; Exodus 22:24; Jeremiah 15:8"> We are orphans; we are without a father; Our mothers are as widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 3:1; Ezekiel 4:9–17; Deuteronomy 28:48"> Our water we drink for money; Our wood is sold to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:36–37; Lamentations 1:14; Jeremiah 28:14"> With the yoke upon our necks, we are driven; We are wearied, and have no rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 12:1; Hosea 9:3; Hosea 7:11"> We have given the hand to the Egyptians, And to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:29; Jeremiah 16:12; Ezekiel 18:2"> Our fathers sinned; they are no more, And we bear their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:15; Zechariah 11:6; Proverbs 30:22"> Servants rule over us; There is none that delivereth out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16–17; Jeremiah 40:9–12; Ezekiel 12:18–19"> With the peril of our lives we get our bread, Because of the sword of the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 4:8; Job 30:30; Psalms 119:83"> Our skin is parched like an oven Because of the burnings of hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:16; Zechariah 14:2; Deuteronomy 28:30"> Matrons in Zion have they ravished, And maidens in the cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Isaiah 47:6; Lamentations 2:20"> Princes were hanged up by their hand; The faces of the elders were not honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 16:21; Exodus 11:5; Matthew 23:4"> Young men carried millstones, And boys fell under burdens of wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 24:7–11; Ezekiel 26:13"> The elders sit no more at the gate; The young men have ceased from their music.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Jeremiah 25:10; James 4:9–10"> The joy of our heart is at an end; Our dancing is turned into mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 89:39; Jeremiah 13:18; Isaiah 3:9–11"> The crown is fallen from our head; Woe unto us, that we have sinned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; Job 17:7; Lamentations 2:11"> For this is our heart faint, For these things our eyes are dim;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:12; Jeremiah 9:11; Psalms 74:2–3"> On account of mount Zion, which is desolate; Foxes roam over it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 102:12; Hebrews 1:8–12; Psalms 45:6"> But thou, O Jehovah, sittest as king forever; Thy throne endureth from generation to generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 44:24; Psalms 13:1; Jeremiah 14:19–21"> Wherefore dost thou wholly forget us, And abandon us, for so long a time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 80:3; Psalms 80:7; Psalms 85:4"> Turn us again to thee, O Jehovah, that we may be restored! Renew our days as of old!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 60:1–2; Ezekiel 37:11; Psalms 44:9"> For shouldst thou utterly reject us? Shouldst thou be so exceedingly wroth against us?</span>
