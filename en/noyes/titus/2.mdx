<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3; Titus 2:11–14"> But do thou speak the things which become sound teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> that aged men be sober, grave, discreet, sound in faith, in love, in patience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> that aged women likewise be in behavior as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> that they may teach the young women to love their husbands, to love their children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> to be discreet, chaste, workers at home, good, in subjection to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; 1 Peter 5:5; Joel 2:28"> The younger men likewise exhort to be sober-minded;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> in all things showing thyself a pattern of good works, in teaching showing uncorruptness, gravity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> sound speech that cannot be condemned; that he that is opposed to us may be put to shame, having no evil thing to say of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–25; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Colossians 3:22–25"> Exhort bond-servants to be in subjection to their own masters, in all things to be well-pleasing to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 John 1:9"> not contradicting, not purloining, but showing all good faith; that they may adorn the teaching of God our Saviour in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the grace of God, that bringeth salvation to all men, was manifested,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in the present world;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> looking for the blessed hope, and appearing of the glory of the great God and of our Saviour Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify to himself a people to be his own, zealous in good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; 1 Timothy 4:12–13; Titus 1:13"> These things speak and exhort, and rebuke with all authority. Let no one despise thee.</span>
