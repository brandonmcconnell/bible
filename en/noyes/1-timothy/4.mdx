<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:1–9; 2 Peter 2:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Now the Spirit saith expressly, that in the latter times some will depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits and teachings of demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> through the hypocrisy of speakers of lies, who bear a brand on their own conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> forbidding to marry, and commanding to abstain from food which God created to be received with thanksgiving, for those who believe and know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be received with thanksgiving;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Luke 4:4"> for it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> If thou lay these things before the brethren, thou wilt be a good minister of Christ Jesus, nourished in the words of the faith, and of the good teaching, with which thou art well acquainted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But avoid the profane and old wives' fables; and exercise thyself unto godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; Psalms 37:3–4"> For bodily exercise is profitable for little; but godliness is profitable for all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> True is the saying, and worthy of all acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> For to this end we both labor and suffer reproach, because we have placed our hope in the living God, who is the Saviour of all men, especially of believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> These things command and teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Let no one despise thy youth, but become an example to the believers, in word, in behavior, in love, in faith, in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Till I come, give attention to reading, to exhortation, to teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Neglect not the gift that is in thee, which was given thee by prophecy, with the laying on of the hands of the presbytery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; Psalms 143:5"> Meditate on these things, give thyself wholly to them; that thy progress may be manifest to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 John 1:8–9"> Give heed to thyself, and to thy teaching; continue in them; for in doing this thou wilt save both thyself and them that hear thee.</span>
