<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> Do not sharply rebuke an aged man, but exhort him as a father; the younger men, as brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Matthew 12:50; Philippians 4:8"> the elder women, as mothers; the younger, as sisters, with all purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honor as widows those that are widows indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> But if any widow have children or grandchildren, let them learn first to show piety to their own family, and to requite their parents; for this is acceptable before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> Now she that is a widow indeed, and left alone, hath set her hope on God, and continueth in supplications and prayers night and day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> but she that giveth herself up to pleasure is dead while she liveth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> These things also enjoin, that they may be blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> But if any one provideth not for his own, and especially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an unbeliever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> Let a widow be put on the list when not less than sixty years old, having been the wife of one husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> well reported of for good works; if she have brought up children, if she have lodged strangers, if she have washed the saints' feet, if she have relieved the afflicted, if she have diligently followed every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> But younger widows refuse; for when they become wanton against Christ, they desire to marry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> falling into condemnation, because they have cast off their first faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> and withal they learn to be idle, going about from house to house; and not only idle, but tattlers also, and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> I desire therefore that the younger widows marry, bear children, guide the house, give no occasion to the adversary to speak reproachfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> For some have already turned aside after Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> If any man or woman that is a believer have widows, let them relieve them, and let not the church be burdened, that it may relieve those that are widows indeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honor; especially they who labor in the word and in teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> For the Scripture saith, “Thou shalt not muzzle the ox while he is treading out the grain”; and, “The laborer is worthy of his wages.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Against an elder receive not an accusation without two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Those that sin rebuke before all, that the rest also may fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> I charge thee before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels, that thou observe these things without prejudging, doing nothing with partiality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Lay hands hastily on no one, neither share in other men's sins. Keep thyself pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> No longer drink water only, but use a little wine for thy stomach's sake, and thy frequent infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> Some men's sins are openly manifest, going before them to judgment; and some men they follow after.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; Psalms 37:5–6"> In like manner also the good works of some are openly manifest; and those that are otherwise cannot be hid.</span>
