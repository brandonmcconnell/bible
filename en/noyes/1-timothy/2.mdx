<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:3; Matthew 6:9–10; James 5:16"> I exhort then, first of all, that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks be made for all men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> for kings, and all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and tranquil life in all godliness and propriety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> whose will is that all men should be saved, and come to the knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> who gave himself a ransom for all; to which the testimony was to be borne in its own due times,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> whereunto I was appointed a herald and an apostle, (I speak the truth, I lie not,) a teacher of the gentiles in faith and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 134:2; Psalms 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> I desire, then, that the men pray in every place, lifting up holy hands, without wrath and doubting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalms 149:4"> In like manner also, that women, in seemly attire, adorn themselves with modesty and sobriety, not with braided hair, and gold, or pearls, or costly apparel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but, as becometh women professing godliness, with good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> Let the woman learn in silence with all subjection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> But I suffer not the woman to teach, nor to have authority over the man, but to be in silence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was first formed, then Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> And Adam was not deceived; but the woman being deceived fell into transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> But she will be saved through child-bearing, if they continue in faith, and love, and holiness, with sobriety.</span>
