<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Peter 2:17–20; Titus 2:8–10"> Let all who are under the yoke as bond-servants count their own masters worthy of all honor, that the name of God and his doctrine be not blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; 1 Timothy 4:11; Genesis 16:4–5"> And they that have believing masters, let them not despise them because they are brethren; but rather do them service, because they who receive the benefit are faithful and beloved. These things teach and exhort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:3; Romans 16:17; 1 Timothy 1:10"> If any one teacheth other doctrine, and assenteth not to sound words, the words of our Lord Jesus Christ, and to the doctrine which is according to godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 3:4; 1 Timothy 3:6"> he is puffed up with pride, knowing nothing, but doting about questions and strifes of words, from which cometh envy, strife, railings, evil surmisings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3"> incessant disputings of men corrupted in their minds, and destitute of the truth, supposing that godliness is gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:8; Proverbs 16:8"> But godliness with contentment is great gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 1:21; Psalms 49:17; Luke 12:20–21"> For we brought nothing into the world; and it is certain we can carry nothing out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:25–33"> If we have, then, food and raiment, we will be therewith content.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 3:7"> But they who desire to be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 1:19; 1 Timothy 6:9; Psalms 32:10"> For the love of money is a root of all evils; which some coveting have strayed away from the faith, and have pierced themselves through with many pangs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 3:17; 1 Timothy 4:12"> But do thou, O man of God, flee these things; and follow after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25–26"> Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on everlasting life, to which thou wast called, and didst profess the good profession before many witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:36–37; Matthew 27:11; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I charge thee before God, who giveth life to all things, and before Christ Jesus, who under Pontius Pilate testified the good profession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> that thou keep the commandment without spot, without reproach, until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 1:17"> which in his own times he shall show, who is the blessed and only Potentate, the King of kings, and Lord of lords,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; John 1:18; 1 John 1:5"> who only hath immortality, dwelling in light unapproachable, whom no man hath seen, or can see; to whom be honor and power everlasting. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; Acts 14:17; Proverbs 27:24"> Charge those who are rich in this world that they be not high-minded, nor place their hope in uncertain riches, but in God, who giveth us richly all things to enjoy; that they do good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 John 3:17; Psalms 37:3"> that they be rich in good works, liberal in imparting, willing to communicate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; 1 Timothy 6:12; 2 Timothy 2:19"> laying up in store for themselves a good foundation against the time to come, that they may lay hold on the true life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 2 Timothy 2:16; 2 Timothy 1:12–14"> O Timothy, keep that which is committed to thy trust, avoiding the profane babblings, and oppositions of the falsely-called knowledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:18; 1 Timothy 1:6; 2 Timothy 4:22"> which some professing, have erred concerning the faith. Grace be with thee.</span>
