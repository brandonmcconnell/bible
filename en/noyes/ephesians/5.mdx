<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> Be therefore imitators of God, as beloved children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> and walk in love, as Christ also loved you, and gave himself for you an offering and a sacrifice to God, of a sweet odor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> But fornication, and all uncleanness, or covetousness, let it not even be named among you, as becometh saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> neither obscenity, nor foolish talking, nor indecent jesting, which are not becoming; but rather giving of thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> For of this ye are sure, since ye know that no whore-monger, nor unclean person, nor covetous man, who is an idolater, hath an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Let no one deceive you with vain words; for because of these things the wrath of God cometh upon the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Be not therefore partakers with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> For ye were once darkness, but now ye are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> —for the fruit of the light is in all goodness, and righteousness, and truth,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 19:14"> proving what is acceptable to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> and have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather even reprove them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For the things done in secret by them it is a shame even to speak of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> But all things, when reproved, are made manifest by the light; for whatever maketh manifest is light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> Wherefore he saith, “Awake, thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead, and Christ will give thee light.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> See then that ye walk circumspectly; not as unwise men, but as wise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> buying up for yourselves opportunities, because the days are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> Wherefore be not unwise, but understanding what the will of the Lord is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> And be not drunk with wine, in which is dissoluteness, but be filled with the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalms 95:2"> speaking to one another in psalms, and hymns, and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> giving thanks always for all things to God, the Father, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> submitting yourselves one to another in the fear of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands, as to the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> for the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church,—he, who is the Saviour of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> But as the church is subject to Christ, so let wives be to their husbands in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, love your wives, as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself up for it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> that he might sanctify it, having cleansed it by the bath of the water in the word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> that he himself might present to himself the church, glorious, having no spot or wrinkle, or any such thing, but that it should be holy and without blemish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> In like manner husbands ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his own wife loveth himself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> for no one ever yet hated his own flesh, but nourisheth and cherisheth it, even as Christ doth the church;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> for we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> “For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife, and they two shall be one flesh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This mystery is a great one; but I am speaking of Christ and of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> However, do ye also severally love each one his own wife as himself; and let the wife see that she reverence her husband.</span>
