<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> I exhort you, therefore, I the prisoner in the Lord, to walk worthily of the calling with which ye were called,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> with all humility and meekness, with long-suffering; bearing with one another in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> endeavoring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye were called in one hope of your calling;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one God and Father of all, who is over all, and through all, and in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> But to each one of us was given the grace &#91;&#91;which he hath&#93;&#93; according to the measure of the gift of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> Wherefore he saith: “When he ascended on high, he led captive a train of captives, and gave gifts to men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> Now what is implied in his ascending, but that he also descended into the lower parts of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> He who descended is the same as he who ascended far above all the heavens, that he might fill all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> And he gave some to be apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> for the perfecting of the saints for the work of ministration, for the building up of the body of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> till we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to a full-grown man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> that we may no longer be children, tossed to and fro and borne about by every wind of teaching, through the dishonest tricks of men, and their cunning in the wily arts of error;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> but cleaving to truth in love, may grow up in all things unto him who is the head, even Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> from whom the whole body, well put together and compacted by means of every supplying joint, is, according to the working of each part in its proportion, building itself up in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> This therefore I say, and charge you in the Lord, that ye no longer walk as the rest of the gentiles walk in the vanity of their mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> having their understanding darkened, being alienated from the life of God on account of the ignorance that is in them, on account of the hardness of their hearts;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> who, being past feeling, have given themselves up to wantonness, to work all uncleanness in greediness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But not so did ye learn Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> if indeed ye heard him, and were taught in him, as the truth is in Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> that as to your former way of life ye should put off the old man, who perisheth according to the lusts of deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> and be renewed in the spirit of your mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and that ye put on the new man, who was created according to God in righteousness and holiness of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Wherefore having put away falsehood, speak truth every one with his neighbor; for we are members one of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> “Be angry, and sin not;” let not the sun go down upon your wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> and do not give place to the Devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> Let him that stealeth steal no more, but rather let him labor, working with his hands at that which is good, that he may have to give to him that is in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no foul language proceed out of your mouth, but whatever is good for edification, as the need may be, that it may benefit the hearers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> and grieve not the Holy Spirit of God, whereby ye were sealed unto the day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamor, and evil-speaking, be put away from you, with all malice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> and be kind to one another, tender-hearted, forgiving one another, even as God in Christ forgave you.</span>
