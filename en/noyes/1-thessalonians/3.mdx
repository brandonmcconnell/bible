<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Romans 5:3; Acts 9:16"> so that no one should be shaken by these afflictions; for yourselves know that to this we are appointed;</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:13–14; Matthew 4:3; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> For this cause, when I too could no longer forbear, I sent in order to know your faith, lest by some means the tempter might have tempted you, and our labor should prove in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> But when Timothy just now came to us from you, and brought us good tidings of your faith and love, and that ye have good remembrance of us always, desiring greatly to see us, as we also to see you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> for this cause, brethren, we were comforted over you in all our distress and affliction by your faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Revelation 3:11"> for now we live, if ye stand fast in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:14–15; Psalms 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> For what thanks can we render to God for you, for all the joy wherewith we rejoice for your sakes before our God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> night and day praying exceedingly that we may see your face, and may supply that which is lacking in your faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now God himself, our Father, and our Lord Jesus direct our way to you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> and the Lord make you to increase and abound in love toward one another and toward all, even as we do in love toward you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; Ephesians 5:27"> in order that he may establish your hearts unblamable in holiness before God, our Father, at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his holy ones.</span>
