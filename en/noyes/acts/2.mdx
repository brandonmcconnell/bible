<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> And on the day of Pentecost they were all together in one place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalms 18:10"> And suddenly there came out of heaven a sound, as of a rushing mighty wind; and it filled the whole house where they were sitting;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and there appeared to them tongues as of fire, distributing themselves; and one sat upon each of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and began to speak with other tongues, even as the Spirit gave them utterance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> Now there were dwelling at Jerusalem Jews, devout men, from every nation under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> And when this sound took place, the multitude came together, and were confounded, because every one heard them speaking in his own language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> And they were amazed, and marveled, saying, Behold, are not all these who speak Galilaeans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and how is it that we every one hear them in our own language, wherein we were born?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and those who inhabit Mesopotamia, Judaea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya about Cyrene, and Romans who sojourn here, both Jews and Proselytes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 78:4"> Cretans and Arabians—how is it that we hear them speaking in our tongues the wonderful works of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> And they were all amazed and were in doubt, saying one to another, What can this mean?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> Others making sport of it, said, They are full of new wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> But Peter, standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice, and said to them, Men of Judea, and all that dwell at Jerusalem, be this known to you, and hearken to my words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> For these are not drunken, as ye suppose; for it is the third hour of the day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> but this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> “It shall be in the last days, saith God, that I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters will prophesy, and your young men will see visions, and your old men will dream dreams;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> and even on my servants, and on my handmaids, I will pour out of my Spirit in those days, and they will prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> And I will show wonders in heaven above, and signs on the earth beneath, blood, and fire, and vapor of smoke;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> the sun will be turned into darkness and the moon into blood, before the day of the Lord cometh, the great and notable day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> And it shall be that every one that calleth on the name of the Lord shall be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> Men of Israel, hear these words! Jesus the Nazarene, a man approved of God to you by miracles, and wonders, and signs, which God wrought by him in the midst of you, as ye yourselves know,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> this man, being delivered up by the settled purpose and foreknowledge of God, ye, by the hand of godless men, crucified and slew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> But God raised him up, having loosed the pains of death, because it was not possible that he should be held by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> For David saith concerning him, “I saw the Lord always before me; because he is on my right hand, that I should not be moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> Therefore my heart rejoiced, and my tongue exulted; moreover also, my flesh shall dwell in hope;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> because thou wilt not abandon my soul to the underworld, nor wilt thou suffer thy holy one to see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> Thou didst make known to me the ways of life; thou wilt make me full of joy with thy countenance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> Brethren, I may speak to you with freedom of the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is among us to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> Being then a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn to him with an oath that he would set one sprung from his loins upon his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> he foresaw and spoke of the resurrection of Christ, that neither was he abandoned to the underworld, nor did his flesh see corruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus God raised up, whereof we all are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Being therefore exalted by the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promised Holy Spirit, he hath poured forth this, which ye both see and hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David did not ascend into the heavens; but he himself saith, The Lord said to my lord, “Sit thou on my right hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> until I make thine enemies thy footstool.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly that God hath made him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus whom ye crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> And when they heard this, they were pierced to the heart, and said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, Brethren, what must we do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> But Peter said to them, Repent, and let every one of you be baptized to the name of Jesus Christ for forgiveness of sins, and ye will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For the promise is to you and to your children, and to all that are afar off, as many as the Lord our God shall call.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> And with many other words did he testify and exhort, saying, Save yourselves from this perverse generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> They therefore received his word, and were baptized; and there were added on that day about three thousand souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> And they were constantly attending on the teaching of the apostles, and the imparting &#91;&#91;of their substance&#93;&#93;, the breaking of bread, and the prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> And fear came upon every soul; and many wonders and signs were wrought through the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And all that believed were together, and had all things common;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> and they sold their possessions and goods, and divided them among all, as any one had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> And attending daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread in a private house, they partook of their food with gladness and singleness of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God, and having favor with all the people. And the Lord added to the church daily those who were in the way of salvation.</span>
