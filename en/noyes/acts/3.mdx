<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 55:17; Acts 10:3; Acts 4:13"> And Peter and John were going up together to the temple, at the hour of prayer, which is the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:8; John 9:8; Luke 16:20"> And a certain man lame from his birth was carried along, whom they laid daily at the gate of the temple which is called Beautiful, to ask alms of those who entered the temple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> who, seeing Peter and John about to go into the temple, asked alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:9–10; John 11:40; Luke 4:20"> And Peter fixing his eyes upon him, with John, said, Look upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he gave heed to them, expecting to receive something from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 3:16; Acts 10:38; Acts 4:10"> Then Peter said, Silver and gold have I none; but what I have I give thee. In the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, rise up and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Mark 9:27; Acts 9:41"> And grasping him by the right hand, he raised him up; and immediately his feet and ankles received strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 14:10; Isaiah 35:6; Psalms 107:20–22"> And leaping forth he stood, and walked, and entered with them into the temple, walking and leaping, praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:21; Acts 4:16; Luke 13:17"> And all the people saw him walking, and praising God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:2; John 5:20; John 9:3"> and they recognized him as the man that sat for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple; and they were filled with wonder and amazement at that which had happened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:12; John 10:23; Luke 8:38"> And as he held fast to Peter and John, all the people ran together to them in the porch that is called Solomon's greatly wondering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; John 3:27–28; Daniel 2:28–30"> But Peter seeing it, answered the people: Men of Israel, why wonder ye at this? Or why look ye earnestly on us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made this man to walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 22:32; Acts 7:32; Matthew 27:17–25"> The God of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, the God of our fathers, hath glorified his servant Jesus, whom ye indeed delivered up, and denied him in the presence of Pilate, when he had decided to release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Acts 7:52; 1 Peter 3:18"> But ye denied the holy and righteous one, and asked that a murderer should be granted to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Revelation 21:6; Acts 2:32"> But the author of life ye killed; whom God raised from the dead, whereof we are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Acts 4:10; Acts 14:9"> And his name, through faith in his name, made this man strong, whom ye see and know; yea, the faith which is through him gave him this perfect soundness in the presence of you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Acts 13:27; John 16:3"> And now, brethren, I know that ye acted in ignorance, as did also your rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 2:23"> But God thus fulfilled what he had before announced by the mouth of all the prophets, that his Christ should suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> Repent therefore and turn from your ways, that your sins may be blotted out, in order that the times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:30–36; Revelation 19:11–16; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> and that he may send forth Christ Jesus, who was before appointed for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:21; Isaiah 1:26; Acts 1:11"> whom heaven indeed must receive until the times of a restoration of all things; of which times God spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets from the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 7:37; Galatians 4:4"> Moses indeed said, “A prophet will the Lord your God raise up to you from among your brethren, as be raised up me; him shall ye hear in all things, whatever he shall speak to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:19; John 12:48; Hebrews 10:28–30"> And it shall come to pass, that every soul which doth not hear that prophet shall be utterly destroyed from among the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 24:27; 1 Samuel 3:20"> And all the prophets also, Samuel and those who followed, as many as spoke, announced these days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:8; Acts 2:39"> Ye are the sons of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying to Abraham, “And in thy posterity shall all the families of the earth be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 1:21; Acts 3:22; Jude 1:24"> To you first, God, having raised up his servant, sent him to bless you in turning away every one of you from your iniquities.</span>
