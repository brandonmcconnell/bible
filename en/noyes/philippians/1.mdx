<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:1; Acts 16:12–15; Romans 1:1"> Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus, to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are at Philippi, together with the bishops and deacons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:2–3; Philemon 1:4–5"> I thank my God in all my remembrance of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Romans 1:9; 2 John 1:4"> always in every prayer of mine for you all making my prayer with joy</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:14–15; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 3:14"> on account of your fellowship in the cause of the gospel from the first day until now;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 4:12"> being confident of this very thing, that he who began in you a good work, will perfect it until the day of Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3; Philippians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> even as it is right for me to think this of you all, because I have you in my heart both in my bonds, and in the defence and confirmation of the gospel, all of you being sharers of the grace bestowed on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:4"> For God is my witness how much I long for you all in the tender affection of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:12; Colossians 1:9; 2 Peter 1:5–6"> And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and in all discernment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> so that ye may approve the things that are most excellent, in order that ye may be pure and without offence against the day of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; John 15:8; John 15:4–5"> being filled with the fruit of righteousness which is through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Romans 8:28; Acts 11:19–21"> But I wish you to know, brethren, that things with me have resulted in the furtherance of the gospel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Ephesians 3:1; Philippians 1:7"> so that my bonds have become known in connection with Christ in the whole camp of the imperial guards, and to all the rest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Luke 1:74"> and that the great part of the brethren, made confident in the Lord by my bonds, are much more bold to speak the word without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:17–18; Philippians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> Some indeed preach Christ even from envy and strife, and some also from good will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:1–2; Psalms 69:26"> (1:17) but they who are of a factious spirit preach Christ with no pure intent, thinking to stir up affliction to my bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:11–12; Philippians 2:3; Philippians 1:7"> (1:16) They who are of love, knowing that I am set for the defence of the gospel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 9:50; Mark 9:38–40; 1 Corinthians 15:11"> What then? Notwithstanding, in every way, whether in pretence or in truth, Christ is preached; and therein do I rejoice, yea, and shall rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Acts 16:7; 2 Corinthians 1:11"> For I know that this will turn out to my salvation through your prayer, and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Isaiah 54:4"> according to my earnest expectation and hope, that in nothing I shall be put to shame, but that with all boldness, as always, so also now, Christ will be magnified in my body, whether by life, or by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:8; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Galatians 2:20; Philippians 1:24"> But if to live in the flesh, if this is to me the fruit of my labor, then what I should choose, I cannot say;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalms 17:15; Revelation 14:13"> but I am held in a strait by the two, having a desire to depart, and to be with Christ; for it is far better;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:7; Philippians 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> but to abide in the flesh is more needful for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 2:24; 2 Corinthians 1:24; Ephesians 4:11–13"> And being persuaded of this, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all for your advancement and joy in your faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 2 Corinthians 5:12; Philippians 3:3"> that your glorying in me, in the cause of Christ Jesus, may be more abundant by my coming to you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 Corinthians 16:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Only conduct yourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ, that whether I come and see you, or remain absent, I may hear of your affairs, that ye stand fast in one spirit, with one soul striving together for the faith of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Matthew 10:28"> and in nothing terrified by your adversaries; which is to them an evident token of perdition, but to you of salvation; and that from God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13; Acts 5:41"> for to you it was given in behalf of Christ, not only to believe in him, but in his behalf to suffer also;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 16:19–40; 2 Timothy 4:7"> as ye have the same conflict which ye saw in me, and now hear of in me.</span>
</div>
