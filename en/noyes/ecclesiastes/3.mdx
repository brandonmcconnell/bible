<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> For every thing there is a fixed period, and an appointed time to every thing under heaven:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> A time to be born, and a time to die. A time to plant, and a time to pluck up what is planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> A time to kill, and a time to heal. A time to breaking down, and a time to build up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalms 30:5; James 4:9"> A time to weep, and a time to laugh. A time to mourn, and a time to dance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> A time to cast stones asunder, and a time to gather stones together. A time to embrace, and a time to refrain from embracing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> A time to seek, and a time to lose. A time to keep, and a time to cast away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> A time to rend, and a time to sew. A time to keep silence, and a time to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> A time to love, and a time to hate. A time of war, and a time of peace.—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What profit hath he who laboreth from that with which he wearieth himself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen the business which God hath given to the sons of men to exercise themselves therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> God maketh every thing good in its time; but he hath put the world into the heart of man, so that he understandeth not the work which God doeth, from the beginning to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> I know that there is nothing better for a man than that he should rejoice and enjoy good his life long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> But when a man eateth and drinketh, and enjoyeth good through all his labor, this is the gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalms 33:11; Romans 11:36"> I know that whatever God doeth, that shall be for ever. Nothing can be added to it, and nothing taken from it; and God doeth it that men may fear before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> That which is, was long ago; and that which is to be, hath already been; and God recalleth that which is past.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalms 94:21–22"> Moreover, I saw under the sun that in the place of justice there was iniquity; and in the place of righteousness, iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> Then said I in my heart, “God will judge the righteous and the wicked.” For there shall be a time for every matter and for every work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Psalms 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I said in my heart concerning the sons of men, that God will prove them, in order that they may see that they are like the beasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 49:12; Psalms 49:20; Psalms 89:47–48"> For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts: one lot befalleth both. As the one dieth, so dieth the other. Yea, there is one spirit in them, and a man hath no pre-eminence above a beast; for all is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> All go to one place; all are from the dust, and all turn to dust again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> Who knoweth the spirit of man, whether it goeth upward, and the spirit of a beast, whether it goeth downward to the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> And so I saw that there is nothing better than that a man should rejoice in his labors; for that is his portion. For who shall bring him to see what shall be after him?</span>
