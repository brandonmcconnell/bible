<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:16; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Isaiah 5:7"> Then I turned and saw all the oppressions which take place under the sun; and, behold, there were the tears of the oppressed, and they had no comforter; and from the hand of their oppressors there was violence, and they had no comforter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:11–26; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 9:4–6"> Therefore I praised the dead, who have been long ago dead, more than the living, who are yet alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:29; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Matthew 24:19"> Yea, better than both of them is he who hath not yet been, who hath not seen the evil work which is done under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> And I saw all labor, and all success in work, that for this a man is envied by his neighbor. This also is vanity, and striving after wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> The fool foldeth his hands together and eateth his own flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; Psalms 37:16"> Better is a hand full of quietness, than both hands full of weariness and striving after wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Psalms 78:33; Zechariah 1:6"> Then I turned and saw other vanity under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> There is one who is alone, and no one with him; yea, he hath neither son nor brother; yet is there no end to all his labor, and his eye is not satisfied with riches. “For whom, then &#91;&#91;saith he&#93;&#93;, do I labor and deprive myself of good?” This also is vanity; yea, it is an evil thing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> Two are better than one, because they have a good reward for their labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Isaiah 35:3–4"> For if they fall, the one will lift his fellow up; but woe to him who is alone when he falleth, and hath not another to help him up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1–4"> Again, if two lie together, then they have heat; but how can one be warm alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; 2 Samuel 23:9"> And if an enemy prevail against one, two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord is not quickly broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> Better is a child poor but wise, than a king old and foolish, who will no more be admonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> For out of prison cometh forth such a one to reign; for in his own kingdom he was born a poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:6"> I saw that all the living, who walk under the sun, were with the child who stood up in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 Kings 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> There was no end to all the people before whom he went forth; yet they that come afterwards shall not rejoice in him. This also is vanity, and striving after wind.</span>
