<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13"> There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it lieth heavy upon men;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:19; Psalms 17:14; Psalms 73:7"> a man to whom God hath given riches, wealth, and honor, and nothing is wanting to him of all which he desireth, yet God giveth him not to taste thereof; but a stranger enjoyeth it. This is vanity, yea, a grievous evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 3:16; Jeremiah 22:19; Ecclesiastes 4:3"> Though a man have a hundred children, and live many years, and though the days of his years be many, if his soul be not satisfied with good, and he have no burial, I say that an untimely birth is better than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 109:13"> This, indeed, cometh in nothingness, and goeth down into darkness, and its name is covered with darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Job 14:1; Job 3:10–13"> it hath not seen the sun, nor known it; yet hath it rest rather than the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 3:20; Job 30:23"> Yea, though he live a thousand years twice told, and see no good, — do not all go to one place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 6:27; Proverbs 16:26; 1 Timothy 6:6–8"> All the labor of man is for his mouth, and yet his desires are not satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Psalms 101:2"> For what advantage hath the wise man over the fool? What advantage hath the poor, who knoweth how to walk before the living?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Job 31:7; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> Better is the sight of the eyes than the wandering of the desire. This also is vanity, and striving after wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:32; Isaiah 45:9–10; Job 40:2"> That which is was long ago called by name; and it was known that he is a man, and that he cannot contend with Him who is mightier than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:16; Ecclesiastes 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:3–11"> Seeing there are many things which increase vanity, what advantage hath man &#91;&#91;from them&#93;&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:14; Job 14:2; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> For who knoweth what is good for man in life, in all the days of his vain life, which he spendeth as a shadow? For who can tell a man what shall be after him under the sun?</span>
