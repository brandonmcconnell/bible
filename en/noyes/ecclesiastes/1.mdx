<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 144:4; Romans 8:20; Ecclesiastes 5:10"> Vanity of vanities, saith the Preacher, vanity of vanities, all is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:9; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Ecclesiastes 2:22"> What profit hath a man by all his labor with which he wearieth himself under the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 104:5; Zechariah 1:5; Ecclesiastes 6:12"> One generation passeth away, and another generation cometh; while the earth abideth for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 19:4–6; Habakkuk 3:11; Psalms 42:1"> The sun riseth, and the sun goeth down, and hasteneth to the place whence it arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:8; Ecclesiastes 11:5; Psalms 107:25"> The wind goeth toward the south, and turneth about to the north; round and round goeth the wind, and returneth to its circuits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 38:10–11; Psalms 104:6–9"> All the rivers run into the sea, yet the sea is not full; to the place whence the rivers come, thither they return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 27:20; Matthew 11:28; Ecclesiastes 4:8"> All words become weary; man cannot express it; the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:15; Ecclesiastes 6:10; Ecclesiastes 2:12"> The thing that hath been is that which shall be, and that which hath been done is that which shall be done; and there is no new thing under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Luke 17:26–30; Matthew 23:30–32"> Is there any thing of which one may say, “Behold, this is new”? It was long ago, in the times which were before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:16; Psalms 9:6; Ecclesiastes 9:5"> There is no remembrance of former things, and of things that are to come there shall be no remembrance to those who live afterwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:1; 1 Kings 4:1–19"> I, the Preacher, was king over Israel at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:10; Genesis 3:19; 1 Timothy 4:15"> And I gave my mind to seek and to search out with wisdom concerning all things which are done under heaven; an evil business, which God hath given to the sons of men, in which to employ themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I saw all the things which are done under the sun; and, behold, it was all vanity, and striving after wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:12–13; Isaiah 40:4; Ecclesiastes 3:14"> That which is crooked cannot be made straight, and that which is wanting cannot be numbered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:9; 1 Kings 4:30; 1 Kings 10:7"> I communed with my heart, saying, “Behold, I have gained more and greater wisdom than all who have been before me at Jerusalem; yea, my mind hath seen much wisdom and knowledge.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ecclesiastes 1:13–14"> And I gave my mind to know wisdom, and to know senselessness and folly; I perceived that this also is striving after wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 28:28; Ecclesiastes 12:12–13; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20"> For in much wisdom is much vexation, and he that increaseth knowledge increaseth sorrow.</span>
