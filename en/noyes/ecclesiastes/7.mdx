<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Proverbs 22:1; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> A good name is better than precious perfume, and the day of one's death than the day of his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 90:12; Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 9:27"> It is better to go to the house of mourning than to go to the house of feasting; for that is the end of all men; and the living will lay it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:9–11; Romans 5:3–4; James 4:8–10"> Sorrow is better than laughter; for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–16; 1 Samuel 25:36; Luke 7:12–13"> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning; but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 15:31–32; Psalms 141:5; Revelation 3:19"> It is better for a man to hear the rebuke of the wise than to hear the song of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 58:9; Psalms 118:12; Ecclesiastes 2:2"> For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of a fool. This also is vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 23:8; Proverbs 17:23"> Surely the gain of oppression maketh a wise man foolish, and a gift corrupteth the understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 126:5–6"> Better is the end of a thing than its beginning. Better is the patient in spirit than the proud in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 14:17; Ephesians 4:26–27"> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry; for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 6:13; Psalms 14:2–3; Genesis 6:11–12"> Say not, “What is the cause that the former days were better than these?” For thou dost not inquire wisely concerning this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:7; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Ecclesiastes 2:13"> Wisdom is as good as an estate? yea, it hath an advantage over it for them that see the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 8:35; Proverbs 2:7; Proverbs 3:18"> For wisdom is a defence, and money is a defence. But knowledge hath the advantage. For wisdom giveth life to them that have it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:15; Job 12:14; Psalms 107:43"> Consider the work of God! Who can make straight that which he hath made crooked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:4; Ecclesiastes 3:22; Ecclesiastes 12:13"> In the day of prosperity be joyful; but look for a day of adversity! for this also, as well as the other, hath God appointed, to the end that a man should not find out any thing which shall be after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:12–14; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Psalms 73:3–13"> All this have I seen in my days of vanity. There are righteous men who perish in their righteousness, and there are wicked men who live long in their wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Proverbs 25:16; Colossians 2:18"> Be not righteous overmuch; neither make thyself over-wise! Why shouldest thou destroy thyself?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:27; Psalms 55:23; Job 22:16"> Be not overmuch wicked; neither be thou a fool! Why shouldst thou die before thy time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Malachi 4:2; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> It is good that thou shouldst take hold of this; yea, also, from that withdraw not thy hand. For he that feareth God shall escape all those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 21:22; Colossians 1:9–11; Proverbs 24:5"> Wisdom strengtheneth the wise more than ten mighty men who are in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Isaiah 53:6; 1 John 1:8–10"> Truly there is not a righteous man upon the earth who doeth good and sinneth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 30:10; Isaiah 29:21; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Give no heed to all the words which are spoken, lest thou hear thy servant curse thee!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 3:9; Matthew 18:32–35; John 8:7–9"> For many times thine own heart knoweth also that even thou thyself hast cursed others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Genesis 3:5; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> All this have I tried by wisdom. I said, “I will be wise;” but it was far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:33; 1 Timothy 6:16; Isaiah 55:8–9"> That which is far off and exceeding deep, who can find it out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:13; Joshua 7:13; Ecclesiastes 2:20"> I applied my mind earnestly to know, and to search, and to seek out wisdom and intelligence, and to know wickedness and folly, yea, foolishness and madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 5:3–5; Proverbs 2:18–19"> And I found more bitter than death the woman whose heart is snares and nets, and her hands bands. He that pleaseth God shall escape from her; but the sinner shall be caught by her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:8–10; Ecclesiastes 1:1–2"> Behold, this have I found, saith the Preacher, putting one thing to another to find knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 33:23; 1 Kings 11:1–3; Psalms 12:1"> That which my soul hath hitherto sought, and I have not found, is this: a man among a thousand I have found, but a woman among a thousand have I not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:22; Mark 7:8–9; Titus 3:3"> Lo, this only have I found, that God made man upright, but they have sought out many devices.</span>
