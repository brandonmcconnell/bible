<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 69:1–2"> Thou didst cast me into the deep, into the heart of the sea, And the flood compassed me about; All thy billows and thy waves passed over me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 31:22; Psalms 5:7; 2 Chronicles 6:38"> And I said, “I am cast out from before thine eyes; Yet I will look again to thy holy temple!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Psalms 40:2"> The waters compassed me about, even to the life, The deep enclosed me round about; Sea-weeds were bound around my head;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 30:3; Psalms 16:10; Isaiah 38:17"> I sank down to the foundations of the mountains; The bars of the earth were about me forever; Yet hast thou brought up my life from the pit, O Jehovah, my God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 18:6; 2 Chronicles 30:27; Psalms 11:4"> When my soul fainted within me, I remembered Jehovah, And my prayer came to thee, To thy holy temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:6; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:13"> They that honor lying vanities forsake their mercy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 3:8; Psalms 50:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> But I will sacrifice to thee with the voice of thanksgiving; I will pay that which I have vowed; Salvation is from Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 8:26–27; Psalms 33:9; Genesis 1:3"> And Jehovah commanded the fish, and it cast out Jonah upon the dry land.</span>
