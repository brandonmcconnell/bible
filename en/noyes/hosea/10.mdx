<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 8:11; Leviticus 26:1; Philippians 2:21"> Israel is a luxuriant vine, That bringeth forth fruit; But according to the abundance of his fruit hath he abounded in altars; According to the goodness of his land hath he made goodly images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 5:13; 1 Kings 18:21; Zephaniah 1:5"> Their heart is divided; now shall they suffer for it; He will break down their altars, And destroy their images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:15; Hosea 10:7; Hosea 13:11"> For soon shall they say, We have no king, Because we fear not Jehovah; What can a king do for us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 5:7; Amos 6:12; Hosea 4:2"> They utter empty words, Swearing falsely, making covenants, And now judgment springeth up, as hemlock in the furrows of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Hosea 5:8; 2 Kings 23:5"> For the calf of Bethaven shall the inhabitants of Samaria be in fear; Yea, its people shall grieve for it, And its priests shall tremble for it, Because its glory has departed from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Isaiah 30:3; Daniel 11:8"> It shall be carried to Assyria, As a present to the hostile king. Ephraim shall be covered with confusion, And Israel shall be ashamed of his doings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:3; Jude 1:13; 2 Kings 1:3"> Samaria shall be brought to destruction; Her king shall be as a twig upon the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Revelation 6:16; Isaiah 2:19"> The high places of Aven, the sin of Israel, shall be destroyed; The thorn and the thistle shall come up on their altars. And they shall say to the mountains, Cover us! And to the hills, Fall on us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Judges 20:5; Judges 19:22–30"> More than in the days of Gibeah hast thou sinned, O Israel! There they stood; The battle in Gibeah against the sons of iniquity did not overtake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Hosea 4:9; Jeremiah 16:16"> Now will I chastise them according to my pleasure, And the nations shall be gathered together against them, When I shall bind them for their two iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 4:16; Jeremiah 50:11; Deuteronomy 25:4"> Ephraim is a trained heifer, that loveth to tread out the corn; But I will lay the yoke upon her fair neck; I will cause Ephraim to draw, Judah shall plough, Jacob shall harrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3–4; James 3:18; Isaiah 45:8"> Sow for yourselves to righteousness, and ye shall reap according to your piety; Break up your fallow ground; For it is a time to seek Jehovah, Till he come and rain righteousness upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7–8; Proverbs 1:31"> Ye plough wickedness, ye shall reap injustice; Ye shall eat the fruit of falsehood. Because thou trustest in thy way, in the multitude of thy mighty men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 18:9–10; Amos 3:8"> There shall arise a tumult among thy people, And all thy fortresses shall be destroyed, As Shalman destroyed Betharbel in the day of battle, When the mother was dashed in pieces with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Hosea 10:7; Romans 7:13"> Such things shall Bethel bring upon you Because of your great wickedness. In the morning shall the king of Israel be destroyed.</span>
