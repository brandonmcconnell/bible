<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 11:2; Joshua 3:7; Numbers 13:16"> Once when Ephraim spake, there was trembling; he was exalted in Israel; But he offended through Baal, and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 44:17–20; 1 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 46:6"> And now they sin more and more, And have made to themselves molten images; Of their silver by their skill have they made idols; All of it is the work of artificers. They say concerning them, Whoever will sacrifice, let him kiss the calves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; Psalms 1:4; Psalms 68:2"> Therefore shall they be as the morning cloud, And as the early dew, which passeth away; As chaff driven with a whirlwind from the thrashing-floor, And as smoke from the chimney.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 45:21–22; Hosea 12:9; Isaiah 43:10–13"> Yet I, Jehovah, have been thy God from the land of Egypt, And thou hast known no God but me; Yea, there was no saviour besides me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 32:10"> I cared for thee in the desert, In the land of great drought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–15; Deuteronomy 8:12–14; Nehemiah 9:35"> As they were fed, so they were filled; They were filled, and their heart was lifted up; Therefore they forgot me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:14; Jeremiah 5:6; Lamentations 3:10"> Therefore have I become to them as a lion; As a leopard I watch for them in the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:8; Isaiah 5:29; Psalms 50:22"> I will meet them as a bear bereaved of her whelps, And I will rend the caul of their heart, And there will I devour them as a lioness; The wild beast shall tear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:19; Deuteronomy 33:26"> It hath been thy destruction, O Israel, That against me, against thy help, thou hast rebelled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 8:4; 1 Samuel 8:5–6; Hosea 10:3"> Where is now thy king? Let him save thee in all thy cities! And where thy judges, In regard to whom thou saidst, Give me a king and princes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:19; 1 Samuel 15:22–23; 1 Kings 14:7–16"> I gave thee a king in mine anger, And I have taken him away in my wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:34–35; Job 14:17; Romans 2:5"> the iniquity of Ephraim is treasured up; His guilt is laid up in store.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 37:3; Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6"> The pangs of a travailing woman shall come upon him; He is an unwise son, For else would he not tarry long in the place of the breaking forth of children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Isaiah 26:19"> I will ransom them from the power of the grave; I will redeem them from death; O death, where is thy plague? O grave, where is thy destruction? Repentance is hidden from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:12; Ezekiel 17:10; Genesis 41:52"> Though he be fruitful among his brethren, An east wind shall come, A wind of Jehovah shall come up from the desert, And his spring shall become dry, And his fountain shall be dried up, And the treasure of all his pleasant vessels shall be spoiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 15:16; Isaiah 13:16"> Samaria shall suffer for her guilt, For she hath rebelled against her God. They shall fall by the sword; Their infants shall be dashed in pieces, And their women with child shall be ripped up.</span>
