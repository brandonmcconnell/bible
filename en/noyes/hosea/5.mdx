<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 9:8; Micah 7:2; Micah 3:9"> Hear ye this, O ye priests, And hearken, O house of Israel, And give ear, O house of the king! For judgment is coming upon you, Because ye have been a snare at Mizpah, And an outspread net upon Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 9:15; Isaiah 29:15; Hosea 6:9"> By their sacrifices they commit deep transgression, And I will bring chastisement upon them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Hosea 13:1"> I know Ephraim, And Israel is not hidden from me; For thou committest fornication, O Ephraim, And Israel is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Hosea 4:1"> They will not frame their doings To return to their God; For a spirit of fornication is within them, And they have no regard to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:10; Amos 5:2; Ezekiel 23:31–35"> The pride of Israel testifieth to his face; Therefore shall Israel and Ephraim fall in their iniquity; Judah also shall fall with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> With their flocks and with their herds shall they go to seek Jehovah, And shall not find him; He hath withdrawn himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 3:20"> They have been false to Jehovah, For they have begotten strange children; Now shall the new moon consume them with their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 4:15; Hosea 9:9; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Blow ye the trumpet in Gibeah, And the cornet in Ramah; Cry aloud at Bethaven! Look behind thee, O Benjamin!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 46:10; Hosea 9:11–17"> Ephraim shall be desolate in the day of rebuke; Among the tribes of Israel do I make known what is sure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> The princes of Judah are like them that remove the land mark; I will pour out my wrath upon them like water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> Ephraim is oppressed; he is crushed with punishment, Because he willingly walked after the decree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 51:8; Job 13:28; Isaiah 50:9"> I am as a moth to Ephraim, And as rottenness to the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 7:11; Hosea 12:1; Hosea 10:6"> When Ephraim saw his sickness, And Judah his wound, Then went Ephraim to the Assyrian, And Judah sent to the hostile king; But he will not be able to heal you, Nor will he cure you of your wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Micah 5:8; Hosea 13:7–8"> For I will be as a lion to Ephraim, And as a young lion to the house of Judah. I, even I, will tear, and will depart; I will take away, and none shall rescue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:16; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I will go back to my place, Till they have suffered for their sin, and seek my face! In their affliction they will seek me early.</span>
