<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 2:15; Exodus 4:22; Hosea 13:4"> When Israel was a child, I loved him, And called my son out of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:15; Hosea 2:13; Isaiah 65:7"> But they turned away from those that called them. They sacrificed to images of Baal; They burned incense to idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:17; Deuteronomy 1:31; Exodus 15:26"> I helped Ephraim to go, Yea, I took them up in my arms; Yet they marked not that I healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:32; Leviticus 26:13; 2 Corinthians 5:14"> I drew them with human cords, with bands of love; I was to them as those who lift up the yoke from their jaws; I dealt gently with them, and gave them food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 7:16; Hosea 8:13; 2 Kings 17:13–14"> They shall no more go down to Egypt; For the Assyrian shall be their king, Because they refuse to return to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Hosea 10:6; Hosea 10:14"> The sword shall fall upon their cities, It shall consume their bars, and devour Because of their devices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5; Amos 5:14–15; Hosea 7:16"> For my people persevere in turning away from me; Though they are called to the Most High, None will exalt him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Matthew 23:37; Hosea 6:4"> How shall I give thee up, Ephraim? How shall I abandon thee, Israel? How shall I make thee as Admah? How shall I set thee as Zeboim? My heart is changed within me; Yea, my compassion is kindled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Isaiah 55:8–9; Micah 7:18–20"> I will not execute the fierceness of mine anger; I will not again destroy Ephraim; For I am God, and not man, The Holy One in the midst of thee, And I will not come in anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Joel 3:16; Amos 1:2"> They shall walk after Jehovah, when he shall roar like a lion, When he shall roar, then shall their sons hasten from the west;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Isaiah 60:8; Zechariah 10:10"> They shall hasten as a bird from Egypt, And as a dove from the land of Assyria, And I will place them in their houses, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 12:7; Revelation 3:21; 2 Chronicles 29:1–32"> Ephraim compasseth me about with falsehood, And the house of Israel with deceit; Judah also is inconstant toward God, Toward the holy and faithful one.</span>
