<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 68:5; Psalms 10:14; Isaiah 31:1"> Assyria shall not help us; We will not ride on horses; And no more will we say to the work of our hands, Ye are our Gods! For from thee the fatherless obtaineth mercy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 57:18; Ephesians 2:4–9; Jeremiah 3:22"> “I will heal their rebellion; I will love them freely; For my anger is turned away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:2; Isaiah 27:6; Ephesians 3:17"> I will be as the dew to Israel; He shall blossom as the lily, And strike his roots like Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 52:8; John 15:1; 2 Corinthians 2:14–15"> His sprouts shall spread forth, And his beauty shall be as the olive-tree, And his fragrance as Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 91:1; Psalms 85:6; 1 Corinthians 15:36–38"> They that dwell under his shadow Shall revive as the corn; They shall shoot forth as the vine; Their name shall be like the wine of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 34:32; Isaiah 55:13; James 1:17"> Ephraim shall say, What have I more to do with idols? I will hear him; I will care for him; I will be like a green cypress-tree; From me shall thy fruit be found.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Psalms 107:43; John 8:47"> Who is wise, that he may understand these things, Prudent, that he may know them? For the ways of Jehovah are right, And the righteous walk in them; But in them transgressors stumble.</span>
