<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 6:7; Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 5:8"> Put the trumpet to thy mouth! Like an eagle cometh an enemy against the house of Jehovah, Because they have transgressed my covenant, And have rebelled against my law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Matthew 7:21; Luke 13:25"> They shall say to me, My God, we know thee, we, thine Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 3:66; Deuteronomy 28:25; 1 Timothy 5:12"> Israel hath cast away what is good; The enemy shall pursue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 15:10–30; Hosea 13:9–10; Hosea 2:8"> They have set up kings, but not by me; They have made princes, and I knew it not. Of their silver and their gold have they made themselves idols, That they may be brought to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Jeremiah 13:27; Proverbs 1:22"> An abomination is thy calf, O Samaria! Mine anger is kindled against them; How long will it be ere they can attain to purity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 10:2; Isaiah 44:9–20; Psalms 135:15–18"> For from Israel it came; The workman made it, and it is no God; Yea, into fragments shall the calf of Samaria be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 22:8; Job 4:8; Galatians 6:7"> They have sown the wind, And they shall reap the whirlwind. They shall have no standing harvest; The ear shall yield no meal; If perchance it yield, strangers shall devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:28; Jeremiah 51:34; Romans 9:22"> Israel is swallowed up; Soon shall they become among the nations As a vessel which no one desireth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:24; Hosea 7:11; Ezekiel 16:33–34"> For they have gone up to Assyria, Like a solitary wild-ass; Ephraim hireth lovers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:37; Ezekiel 23:22–26; Isaiah 10:8"> But though they hire among the nations, Soon will I gather the nations against them; Then shall they rest a little while from the burden of their king, and their princes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 12:11; Hosea 10:1–2; Hosea 10:8"> Ephraim hath built many altars for sin, Therefore shall he have altars for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:6–8; Nehemiah 9:26; Proverbs 22:20"> Though I write for him many laws, They are accounted as a strange thing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Hosea 9:6; Amos 8:7"> As to the sacrifices which they should offer me, they slay flesh and eat it; Jehovah hath no pleasure in them. Now will he remember their iniquity, And punish their sins; To Egypt shall they return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:18; Jeremiah 17:27; Amos 2:5"> For Israel hath forgotten his Maker, and builded palaces, And Judah hath multiplied fenced cities; But I will send a fire upon his cities, And it shall devour his palaces.</span>
