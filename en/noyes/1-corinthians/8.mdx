<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; Romans 14:3"> Now concerning the things offered in sacrifice to idols, we know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge puffeth up, but love edifieth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Proverbs 26:12"> if any one think that he knoweth anything, he knoweth nothing yet, as he ought to know;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> but if any one love God, the same is known by Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Isaiah 41:24; 1 Corinthians 10:19–20"> Concerning the eating of the things offered in sacrifice to idols, then, we know that an idol is nothing in the world, and that there is no other God but one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; Galatians 4:8"> For though there are those that are called gods, whether in heaven or on earth; as there are gods many, and lords many;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> yet to us there is but one God, the Father, from whom are all things, and we to him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, through whom are all things, and we through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 14:14; 1 Corinthians 10:28–29; Romans 14:22–23"> But there is not in all men this knowledge; for some, with a conscience directed toward the idol even now, eat of it as a thing offered in sacrifice to an idol, and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:17; Hebrews 13:9; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> But food will not recommend us to God; if we do not eat, we are not the worse; nor if we do eat, are we the better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 14:20–21; 1 Peter 2:16"> But take heed, lest this liberty of yours become a stumbling-block to the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:28–29; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; Romans 14:23"> For if any one see thee, who hast knowledge, at table in an idol's temple, will not the conscience of him that is weak be emboldened to eat the things offered to idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:15; Romans 15:1–3; 1 Corinthians 8:13"> For through thy knowledge he that is weak perisheth,—the brother for whom Christ died!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Matthew 25:45; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> But when ye so sin against the brethren, and wound their weak conscience, ye sin against Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 14:21; 2 Corinthians 6:3; 2 Corinthians 11:29"> Wherefore, if food cause my brother to fall, I will eat no flesh for ever, lest I cause my brother to fall. Am I not free?</span>
