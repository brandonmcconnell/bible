<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 14:29; Nehemiah 9:11–12; Psalms 78:13–14"> For I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–3; 1 Corinthians 1:13–16; Exodus 14:31"> and were all baptized to Moses in the cloud and in the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Nehemiah 9:15; Exodus 16:35"> and all ate the same spiritual food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; John 7:37; Isaiah 48:21"> and all drank the same spiritual drink; for they drank of the spiritual rock that accompanied them; and the rock was Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 3:17; Jude 1:5; Numbers 14:37"> but with most of them God was not well pleased; for they were laid low in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> Now these things were warnings for us, in order that we should not lust after evil things, as they lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalms 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> And do not ye become idolaters, as some of them did; as it is written: “The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to sport.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalms 106:29"> Nor let us commit fornication, as some of them did, and fell in one day three and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; Psalms 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> Nor let us tempt Christ, as some of them tempted, and were destroyed by the serpents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> And do not ye murmur, as some of them murmured, and perished by the Destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> Now these things happened to them as warnings, and were recorded for our admonition, to whom the ends of the ages have come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth, take heed lest he fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> No temptation hath come upon you, but such as is common to man; but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted beyond what ye are able to endure, but will with the temptation furnish also the way to escape, that ye may be able to endure it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> Wherefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I speak as to wise men, judge ye what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not a partaking of the blood of Christ? the loaf which we break, is it not a partaking of the body of Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For we, the many, are one loaf, one body; for we all share in that one loaf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> Look at Israel by natural descent. Have not those who eat of the sacrifices, communion with the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> What do I say then? That what is offered in sacrifice to idols is anything? Or that an idol is anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalms 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> Nay, but that what they sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons, and not to God; and I would not that ye should have communion with demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> Ye cannot drink the cup of the Lord, and the cup of demons; ye cannot be partakers of the Lord's table, and the table of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Shall we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> All things are lawful, but all things are not profitable; all things are lawful, but all things are not edifying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let no one seek his own pleasure, but the good of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Whatever is sold in the market that eat, without asking questions for the sake of conscience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalms 50:12"> for “the earth is the Lord's, and the fullness thereof.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> And if one who is an unbeliever inviteth you to a feast, and ye choose to go, eat whatever is set before you, without asking any questions for the sake of conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if any one say to you, This hath been offered in sacrifice to an idol, do not eat of it, on account of him that showed you this, and from a regard to conscience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> conscience I mean, not thine own, but that of the other. For why is my liberty to be judged by another conscience &#91;&#91;than my own&#93;&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> If I partake with thankfulness, why am I to be evil spoken of in a matter for which I give thanks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> Whether therefore ye eat or drink, or whatever ye do, do all to the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> Give no occasion of stumbling either to Jews or Greeks, or to the church of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> as I also strive to please all in all things, not seeking my own advantage, but that of the many; that they may be saved.</span>
