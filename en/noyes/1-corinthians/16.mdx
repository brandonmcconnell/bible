<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 2 Corinthians 8:1–9; 2 Corinthians 9:12–15"> Now concerning the collection for the saints, according to the directions which I gave to the churches of Galatia, so also do ye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:9–10; Deuteronomy 15:11–14; Deuteronomy 8:18"> Every first day of the week let each of you lay by him something in store, according as he hath prospered; that the collections may not have to be made when I come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> And when I am with you, I will send with letters whomever you may approve to carry your bounty to Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> and if it be worth while for me to go also, they shall go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> Now I will come to you, when I have passed through Macedonia; for I am about to pass through Macedonia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> and perhaps I may remain, or even spend the winter with you, that ye may set me forward on my journey whithersoever I may be going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> For I am unwilling to see you now in passing; for I hope to stay some time with you if the Lord permit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> But I shall remain at Ephesus until the Pentecost;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> for a door hath been opened to me great and effective, and there are many adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> Now if Timothy come, see that he be with you without fear; for he is laboring in the work of the Lord, as I am;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> let no one therefore despise him. But conduct him on in peace, that he may come to me; for I am waiting for him with the brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> As regards Apollos, the brother, I urged him much to come to you with the brethren; and it was not at all his will to come at this time; but he will come when he hath a convenient opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Watch, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> let all your doings be in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> And I exhort you, brethren,—ye know the family of Stephanas, that they are the first-fruits of Achaia, and that they have devoted themselves to the service of the holy,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> that ye submit yourselves to such as they are, and to every one that worketh with us, and laboreth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> I am glad of the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus; for what was lacking on your part, they supplied;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> for they refreshed my spirit and yours. Pay regard then to those that are such.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; Philemon 1:2; Romans 16:3–5"> The churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Prisca, with the church that is in their house, send you many salutations in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> All the brethren salute you. Salute one another with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> The salutation of me, Paul, with my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:24; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> If any one loveth not the Lord, let him be accursed! The Lord is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> My love is with you all in Christ Jesus.</span>
