<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; 1 Peter 4:8; Galatians 5:22"> Though I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, and have not love, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:3; 1 John 4:20–21"> And though I have the gift of prophecy, and understand all mysteries and all knowledge, and though I have all faith, so as to remove mountains, and have not love, I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:14–17; John 15:13; Matthew 23:5"> And though I bestow all my goods to feed the poor, and though I give up my body that I may be burned, and have not love, it profiteth me nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 5:21–22"> Love suffereth long, is kind; love envieth not; love vaunteth not herself, is not puffed up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:3–5; 1 John 3:16–17"> doth not behave herself unseemly, seeketh not her own, is not easily provoked, maketh no account of an injury,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 12:9; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 John 1:4"> rejoiceth not at iniquity, but rejoiceth in the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4; 2 Timothy 2:24"> beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Galatians 5:6"> Love never faileth; but whether there are prophesyings, they will come to an end; whether tongues, they will cease; whether knowledge, it will be done away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 2:2–3; Ephesians 3:18–19; 1 Corinthians 2:9"> For we know in part, and we prophesy in part;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; Isaiah 60:19–20; 2 Corinthians 5:7–8"> but when that which is perfect is come, that which is in part will be done away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2; Galatians 4:1"> When I was a child, I spoke as a child, I had the feelings of a child, I thought as a child; since I have become a man, I have put away the things of the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 3:2; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 13:9–10"> For now we see in a mirror, obscurely; but then face to face; now I know in part, but then I shall fully know even as I also am fully known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:14; Colossians 3:14; 1 John 4:7–18"> And now there abide faith, hope, love, these three; but the greatest of these is love.</span>
