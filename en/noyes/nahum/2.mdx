<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 3:9–11; 2 Chronicles 25:8; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> The ravager cometh up against thee, &#91;&#91;O Nineveh!&#93;&#93; Guard the fortress; watch the way; Gird up the loins; confirm the strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 80:12–13; Ezekiel 37:23; Zephaniah 3:11"> For Jehovah restoreth the glory of Jacob, As the glory of Israel; For the wasters have wasted them, And destroyed their branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:14–15; Isaiah 63:1–3; Zechariah 6:2"> The shields of his mighty men are red; His warriors are clothed in crimson; His chariots glitter with the fire of steel in the day of his preparation, And the spears are brandished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 26:10; Nahum 3:2–3"> The chariots rave in the streets; They run to and fro in the broad ways; Their appearance is like torches; They run like lightnings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:12; Jeremiah 51:27–28; Jeremiah 50:29"> He calleth for his mighty men; They stumble on their way; They hasten to the wall; But the mantelet is prepared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> The gates of rivers are opened, And the palace melteth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 38:14; Isaiah 59:11; Luke 23:48"> Huzzab is uncovered; she is carried away captive, Her maidservants sigh with the voice of doves, And smite their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:13; Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 46:5"> Nineveh was like a pool full of water of old; Yet shall they flee away; Stand! stand! shall they cry; But none shall look back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:12; Daniel 11:8; Nahum 2:12–13"> Seize the silver; seize the gold; There is no end to the treasures; There is abundance of all precious furniture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:6; Psalms 22:14; Daniel 5:6"> She hath become void, and empty, and desolate; The heart melteth, and the knees smite together; Pangs are in all their loins, And the faces of all gather blackness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:29; Jeremiah 2:15; Isaiah 31:4"> Where now is the dwelling of the lions, And the feeding-place of the young lions, Where the lion and the lioness walked, And the lion's whelp, and none made them afraid?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34; Psalms 17:12; Isaiah 10:6–14"> The lion tore in pieces for his whelps, And strangled for his lionesses, And filled his dens with prey, And his lairs with ravin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:13; Psalms 46:9; Nahum 3:5"> Behold! I am against thee, saith Jehovah of hosts, And I will burn thy chariots into smoke, And the sword shall devour thy young lions. And I will cut off thy prey from the earth, And the voice of thy messengers shall no more be heard.</span>
