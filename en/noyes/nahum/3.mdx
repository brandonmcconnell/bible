<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:1–3; Habakkuk 2:12; Hosea 4:2"> Woe to the city of blood! She is all full of deceit and robbery; She ceaseth not from plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:3; Nahum 2:3–4; Job 39:22–25"> &#91;&#91;Hark!&#93;&#93; The noise of the whip! The noise of the rattling of the wheels, And of the prancing horses, And of the bounding chariots!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Ezekiel 39:4; Isaiah 34:3"> The horseman lifteth up the flame of the sword, And the lightning of the spear; There is a multitude of the slain; heaps of dead bodies; There is no end to the carcasses; they stumble over the carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 47:9; Isaiah 47:12–13; Revelation 17:1–5"> It is because of the many whoredoms of the harlot, The graceful beauty, the mistress of enchantments, That sold nations by her whoredoms, And kingdoms by her enchantments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:26; Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:22"> Behold, I am against thee, saith Jehovah of host, And I will lift up thy trail over thy face, And I will show the nations thy nakedness, And the kingdoms thy shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 2:9; Jeremiah 51:37; Job 9:31"> And I will cast abominable filth upon thee, And I will dishonor thee, and make thee a gazing-stock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:5; Isaiah 51:19; Revelation 18:10"> And all that see thee shall flee from thee, And shall say, “Nineveh is laid waste; Who will bemoan her? Whence shall I seek comforters for thee?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:2; Ezekiel 30:14–16; Jeremiah 46:25–26"> Art thou better than No-Ammon, That dwelt by the rivers, That had the waters round about her, Whose fortress was the sea, And whose wall was from the waters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:5; Ezekiel 27:10; Isaiah 20:5"> Ethiopia and Egypt were her strength, a countless multitude; Phut and Lybia were thy helpers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Joel 3:3; Hosea 13:16"> Yet was she carried away; she went into captivity; Her children were dashed in pieces at the head of all the streets; For her honorable men they cast lots, And all her great men were bound in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Isaiah 2:10; Psalms 75:8"> Thou also shalt drink to the full; Thou, too, shalt be hidden; Thou shalt seek a refuge from the enemy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 6:13; Isaiah 28:4; Habakkuk 1:10"> All thy strong-holds shall be like fig-trees with the first ripe figs; If they be shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Isaiah 19:16; Jeremiah 50:37"> Behold, thy people shall be women in the midst of thee; The gates of thy land shall be set wide open to thine enemies; The fire shall devour thy bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:3–4; Nahum 2:1; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> Draw thee water for the siege, Fortify thy strongholds. Go into the clay, and tread the mortar; Repair the brick-kiln!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Nahum 2:13; Nahum 3:13"> Then shall the fire devour thee; The sword shall cut thee off, It shall devour thee like the locust; Though thou art increased like the locusts, Though thou art increased like the thick locusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Nehemiah 9:23; Jeremiah 33:22"> Thy merchants have been more numerous than the stars of heaven; The locusts spread themselves and fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27; Revelation 9:7"> Thy princes are like locusts, And thy captains like swarms of locusts, Which encamp in the hedges in the time of cold; But when the sun ariseth, they flee away, And the place is not known where they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Psalms 76:5–6; Jeremiah 50:18"> Thy shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria! Thy nobles take their rest, Thy people are scattered on the mountains, and none gathereth them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 1:9; Lamentations 2:15; Job 27:23"> Thy bruise is incurable; Thy wound is mortal. All that hear of thee shall clap their hands over thee; For upon whom hath not thy wickedness passed continually?</span>
