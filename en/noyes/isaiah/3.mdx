<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 14:13; Isaiah 1:24"> For behold, the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, Taketh away from Jerusalem and from Judah every stay and support; The whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 2 Kings 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> The mighty man, and the warrior, The judge, the prophet, the diviner, and the sage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> The captain of fifty, and the honorable man, The counsellor, the expert in arts, and the skilful in charms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I will make boys their princes, And children shall rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–8; Malachi 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> And the people shall oppress one another, Man striving against man, and neighbor against neighbor; The boy shall behave himself insolently toward the aged, And the base toward the honorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> Then shall a man take hold of his brother in his father's house, &#91;&#91;and say,&#93;&#93; Thou hast yet clothing, Be thou our ruler, And take this ruin into thy hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Ezekiel 34:4"> But in that day shall he lift up his hand, and say: I am no healer; In my house is neither bread nor raiment; Make not me ruler of the people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Ezekiel 9:9; Malachi 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem tottereth, and Judah falleth, Because their tongues and their deeds are against Jehovah, To provoke his holy eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; 2 Kings 9:30"> Their very countenance witnesseth against them; They publish their sin like Sodom; they hide it not; Woe to them, for they bring evil upon themselves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Say ye of the righteous that it shall be well with him, For he shall eat the fruit of his doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; Psalms 62:12"> Woe to the wicked, it shall be ill with him, For the work of his hands shall be repaid him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4; Nahum 3:13; 2 Kings 11:1"> As for my people, children are their oppressors, And women rule over them. O my people, thy leaders cause thee to err, And destroy the way in which thou walkest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Hosea 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> Jehovah standeth up to maintain his cause; He standeth up to judge his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; Psalms 143:2; Isaiah 3:2–3"> Jehovah entereth into judgment with the elders of his people, and their princes: “So then ye have consumed the vineyard; The plunder of the poor is in your houses!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Psalms 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> What mean ye, that ye crush my people, And grind the faces of the poor?” Saith Jehovah, the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 4:4"> Thus, also, saith Jehovah: Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, And walk with outstretched necks, And glance their eyes wantonly, Mincing their steps as they go, And tinkling with their foot-clasps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:22; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> Therefore will the Lord make their heads bald, And Jehovah will expose their nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> In that day shall the Lord take from them The ornaments of the foot-clasps, and the net-works, and the crescents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> The ear-rings, and the bracelets, and the veils;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Ezekiel 16:12"> The turbans, and the ankle-chains, and the belts; The perfume-boxes, and the amulets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> The finger-rings, and the nose-jewels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The embroidered robes, and the tunics, and the cloaks, and the purses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> The mirrors, and the linen shifts, and the head-bands, and the large veils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:31; Isaiah 22:12"> And instead of perfume there shall be corruption; Instead of a belt, a rope; Instead of curled locks, baldness; Instead of a wide mantle, a covering of sackcloth; Fire-scars instead of beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:9; Isaiah 1:20; Amos 9:10"> Thy men shall fall by the sword, Yea, thy mighty men in battle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jeremiah 14:2; Lamentations 1:4"> Her gates shall lament and mourn, And she, being desolate, shall sit upon the ground.</span>
