<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Isaiah 34:5–6; Psalms 137:7"> Who is this that cometh from Edom? In scarlet garments from Bozrah? This that is glorious in his apparel, Proud in the greatness of his strength? I that proclaim deliverance, And am mighty to save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:13; Revelation 19:15"> Wherefore is thine apparel red, And thy garments like those of one that treadeth the wine-vat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 19:13–15; Revelation 14:19–20; Malachi 4:3"> I have trodden the wine-vat alone, And of the nations there was none with me. And I trod them in mine anger, And I trampled them in my fury, So that their life-blood was sprinkled upon my garments. And I have stained all my apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 34:8; Jeremiah 51:6; Isaiah 35:4"> For the day of vengeance was in my heart, And the year of my deliverance was come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 44:3; Isaiah 40:10; Psalms 98:1"> And I looked, and there was none to help, And I wondered, that there was none to uphold; Therefore my own arm wrought salvation for me, And my fury, it sustained me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 14:10; Revelation 16:19; Isaiah 49:26"> I trod down the nations in my anger; I crushed them in my fury, And spilled their blood upon the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> I will celebrate the mercy of Jehovah, the glory of Jehovah, According to all that he hath done for us; His great goodness to the house of Israel, Which he hath bestowed on it in his tender mercy, and his great kindness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 17:7; Isaiah 12:2; Exodus 6:7"> He said, Truly they are my people; Children that will not be false; So he was their deliverer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Exodus 23:20–21; Malachi 3:1"> In all their straits they had no distress; An angel of his presence saved them; In his love and compassion he redeemed them, He took them up and bore them all the days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Ephesians 4:30; Psalms 78:56"> But they rebelled, and grieved his holy spirit; Then did he change himself into their enemy; He himself fought against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Exodus 14:22; Psalms 77:20"> Then remembered his people the ancient days, the days of Moses, &#91;&#91;saying,&#93;&#93; Where is he that brought them up from the sea with the shepherd of his flock? Where is he that put his holy spirit within him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 15:16; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:16"> That caused his glorious arm to accompany the right hand of Moses, That divided the water before them, To make to himself an everlasting name?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 106:9; Habakkuk 3:15"> That led them through the deep, As a horse through a desert, without stumbling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 63:12; Hebrews 4:8–11; Joshua 23:1"> As the herd descendeth into the valley, The spirit of Jehovah led them to rest. So didst thou lead thy people, To make for thyself a glorious name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:20; Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalms 80:14"> Look down from heaven, and behold from thy holy and glorious habitation! Where is thy zeal and thy might? Thy pity and compassion for me, are they restrained?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Malachi 2:10; Jeremiah 3:19"> Thou, surely, art our father; Abraham is ignorant of us, And Israel knoweth us not. Thou, O Jehovah, art our father; Our deliverer wast thou of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Joshua 11:20; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Why, O Jehovah, dost thou suffer us to wander from thy ways, And harden our hearts against thy fear? Return, for thy servants' sake, The tribes of thine inheritance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 74:3–7; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 7:6"> But a little while did thy holy people possess the land; Then our enemies trampled upon thy sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:4; Acts 14:16; Romans 9:4"> It has been with us as if thou hadst never ruled over us, As if we had not been called by thy name.</span>
