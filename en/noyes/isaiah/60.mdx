<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 5:8; John 8:12"> Arise, shine! for thy light is come, And the glory of Jehovah is risen upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:9; Acts 17:30–31"> For, behold, darkness shall cover the earth, And gross darkness the nations; But upon thee shall Jehovah arise, And his glory shall be seen upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:24"> Nations shall come to thy light, And kings to the brightness that riseth upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 42:6; Isaiah 49:20–22; Isaiah 49:18"> Lift up thine eyes round about and see! They all gather themselves together, and come to thee; Thy sons come from afar, And thy daughters are carried at thy side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 61:6; Isaiah 23:18; Isaiah 60:11"> Then shalt thou see, and be bright with joy; Thy heart shall throb, and swell with delight, When the riches of the sea shall be turned toward thee, And the wealth of the nations shall come to thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:11; Psalms 72:10; Psalms 72:15"> A multitude of camels shall cover thee, Dromedaries from Midian and Ephah; From Sheba shall they all come. Gold and frankincense shall they bring, And proclaim the praises of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Genesis 25:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> All the flocks of Kedar shall be gathered to thee, And the rams of Nebaioth shall minister to thee; They shall ascend mine altar, an acceptable offering, And my glorious house I will adorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Luke 13:29; Isaiah 60:4"> Who are these that fly like clouds, And like doves to their habitations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:5; Isaiah 2:16; Isaiah 51:5"> Behold, the distant coasts shall wait for me, And the ships of Tarshish among the first, To bring thy sons from afar, And their silver and their gold with them, Because of the name of Jehovah thy God, Of the Holy One of Israel, for he glorifieth thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 61:5; Isaiah 49:23; Revelation 21:26"> The sons of the stranger shall build up thy walls, And their kings shall minister to thee; For in my wrath I smote thee, But in my mercy will I have pity upon thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:25–26; Isaiah 60:5; Isaiah 60:18"> Thy gates shall be open continually; They shall not be shut by day or by night, That the treasures of the nations may be brought to thee, And that their kings may come with their retinues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12–19; Isaiah 54:15; Psalms 2:12"> For that nation and that kingdom Which will not serve thee shall perish; Yea, those nations shall be utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; Isaiah 35:2; Psalms 132:7"> The glory of Lebanon shall come to thee, The cypress, the plane-tree, and the larch together, To adorn the place of my sanctuary, That I may make the place where my feet rest glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Isaiah 49:23; Hebrews 12:22"> The sons of thine oppressors shall come bending before thee; They that despised thee shall fall down at thy feet; And they shall call thee the city of Jehovah, The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 61:7; Jeremiah 30:17; Isaiah 49:14–23"> Instead of being forsaken and hated, So that no one passed through thee, I will make thee an everlasting glory; The joy of many generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 66:11–12; Isaiah 66:14"> Thou shalt also suck the milk of the nations, And be nursed from the breast of kings; And thou shalt know that I, Jehovah, am thy saviour, That thy redeemer is the Mighty One of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:40; Isaiah 30:26; Isaiah 1:26"> Instead of brass will I bring gold; And instead of iron will I bring silver, And instead of wood, brass, And instead of stones, iron; I will make thine officers peace, And thy magistrates righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:1; Isaiah 11:9; Zechariah 9:8"> Violence shall no more be heard in thy land, Wasting or destruction within thy borders; Thou shalt call thy walls Salvation, And thy gates Praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:23; Revelation 22:5; Zechariah 2:5"> No more shall the sun be thy light by day, Nor with her brightness shall the moon enlighten thee: But Jehovah shall be to thee an everlasting light, And thy God thy glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Psalms 84:11; Isaiah 35:10"> Thy sun shall no more go down, Neither shall thy moon be hid; For Jehovah shall be thine everlasting light, And the days of thy mourning shall be ended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Psalms 37:22; Psalms 37:11"> Thy people shall be all righteous; Forever shall they possess the land, The scion of my planting, The work of my hands, that I may be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Hebrews 10:36; Luke 18:7"> The little one shall become a thousand, And the small one a strong nation; I, Jehovah, will hasten it in its time.</span>
