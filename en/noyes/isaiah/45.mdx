<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 44:28; Isaiah 42:6"> Thus saith Jehovah to his anointed, To Cyrus, whom I hold by his right hand, To subdue nations before him, And ungird the loins of kings; To open before him the two-leaved gates, And the doors shall not be shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 40:4; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 42:16"> I will go before thee, And make the high places plain; I will break in pieces the gates of brass, And cut in sunder the bars of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 48:15; Isaiah 43:1; Exodus 33:17"> I will give thee the treasures of darkness, And hidden riches of secret places, That thou mayst know that I am Jehovah Who calleth thee by name, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Acts 17:23; Isaiah 44:1"> For the sake of Jacob, my servant, And Israel, my chosen, I have called thee by thy name; I have spoken to thee as a friend, though thou hast not known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:8; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 46:9"> I am Jehovah, and none else; There is no God besides me; I have girded thee, though thou hast not known me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 45:5; Psalms 46:10"> That men may know from the rising of the sun, And from the West, that there is none besides me; I am Jehovah, and none else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:13–14; Amos 3:6; Psalms 75:7"> I form the light, and create darkness; I make peace, and create evil; I, Jehovah, do all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 61:11; Psalms 72:6"> Pour forth, ye heavens, from above; Ye clouds, shower down prosperity! Let the earth open, and bring forth salvation; Yea, let righteousness spring up together! I, Jehovah, create it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 10:15"> Woe to him that contendeth with his Maker! A potsherd of the potsherds of the earth! Shall the clay say to him that fashioneth it, What makest thou? Or thy work say of thee, He hath no hands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> Woe to him that saith to his father, Why dost thou beget? Or to his mother, Why dost thou bring forth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Isaiah 48:17; Mark 11:24"> Thus saith Jehovah, the Holy One of Israel, and his Maker, Ask of me concerning things to come; My children, the work of my hands, leave them to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; Isaiah 44:24; Jeremiah 27:5"> I made the earth, And created man upon it; My hands spread out the heavens, And all their host did I arrange.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:2; Isaiah 13:17; Isaiah 41:25"> I have raised him up for salvation, And I will make all his ways plain; He shall build my city, and release my captives, Not for price, and not for ransom, Saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; 1 Corinthians 14:25; Jeremiah 16:19"> Thus saith Jehovah: The wealth of Egypt and the merchandise of the Ethiopians and Sabeans, men of stature, Shall come over to thee, and be thine; They shall follow thee; in chains shall they pass along; They shall fall down to thee, and make supplication to thee: “In thee alone is God, and there is none else; There is no other God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Psalms 44:24; Isaiah 57:17"> Truly thou art a God that hidest thyself, O God of Israel, the saviour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9; Isaiah 44:11; Isaiah 42:17"> They shall all be ashamed and confounded, They shall go to confusion together, That are makers of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:11; Psalms 25:3; Isaiah 54:4"> But Israel shall be saved by Jehovah with an everlasting salvation; Ye shall never be ashamed, nor confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 115:16; Jeremiah 10:12; Isaiah 45:12"> For thus saith Jehovah, who created the heavens; The God that formed the earth and made it; he that made it firm; He created it not in vain; he formed it to be inhabited: I am Jehovah, and none else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:16; Amos 5:4; Numbers 23:19–20"> I have not spoken in secret, in a dark place of the earth; I have not said to the race of Jacob, Seek ye me in vain! I, Jehovah, speak truth; I declare that which is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; Isaiah 44:17–20; Isaiah 43:9"> Assemble yourselves and come; Gather yourselves together, ye that are escaped of the nations! They are without understanding, who carry about with them the wood of their graven image, And pray to a god that cannot save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 45:5; Isaiah 43:11; Isaiah 44:7–8"> Proclaim ye, and bring them near, And let them take counsel together: Who hath made this known from ancient time? Who hath declared it of old? Is it not I, Jehovah, besides whom there is no God? A God that uttereth truth, and giveth salvation; there is none besides me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:40; Micah 7:7; Hebrews 12:2"> Look to me, and be saved, all ye ends of the earth! For I am God, and there is none else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; Romans 14:10–12; Isaiah 55:11"> By myself have I sworn, The truth hath gone from my mouth, The word, that shall not return, That to me every knee shall bow, That to me every tongue shall swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 54:17; Ephesians 6:10"> “Only in Jehovah,” shall men say, “Is salvation and strength; To him shall come and be put to shame, All that are incensed against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 41:16; Galatians 3:27–29; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> Through Jehovah shall all the race of Israel be delivered, And in him shall they glory.</span>
