<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:18–21; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 61:1"> Behold my servant, whom I uphold, My chosen, in whom my soul delighteth, I have put my spirit upon him; He shall cause law to go forth to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:16–20; 2 Timothy 2:24; Matthew 11:29"> He shall not cry aloud, nor lift up his voice, Nor cause it to be heard in the street.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalms 147:3"> The bruised reed shall he not break, And the glimmering flax shall he not quench; He shall send forth law according to truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 52:13–15"> He shall not fail, nor become weary, Until he shall have established justice in the earth. And distant nations shall wait for his law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 40:22; Isaiah 45:12; Jeremiah 10:12"> Thus saith God Jehovah, Who created the heavens and stretched them out, Who spread forth the earth, and that which springeth forth from it, Who gave breath to the people upon it, And spirit to them that walk thereon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:32; John 8:12; Acts 13:47"> I, Jehovah, have called thee for salvation; I will hold thee by the hand; I will defend thee, and make thee a covenant to the people, A light to the nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Ephesians 1:17–18; Isaiah 35:5"> To open the blind eyes, To bring out the prisoners from the prison, And them that sit in darkness out of the prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 48:11; Isaiah 43:11; Exodus 34:14"> I am Jehovah, that is my name; And my glory will I not give to another, Nor my praise to graven images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19; Isaiah 46:9–10; John 13:19"> The former things, behold! they are come to pass, And new things do I now declare; Before they spring forth, I make them known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 40:3; Romans 15:9–11"> Sing to Jehovah a new song; His praise to the ends of the earth; Ye that go down upon the sea, and all that fill it; Ye distant coasts, and ye that dwell therein!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 32:16; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 41:18–19"> Let the desert cry aloud, and the cities thereof; The villages, that Kedar inhabiteth; Let the inhabitants of the rock sing; Let them shout from the top of the mountains!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:9–11; Psalms 117:1–2; Revelation 7:9–12"> Let them give glory to Jehovah, And proclaim his praise in distant lands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–3; Psalms 78:65; Isaiah 59:16–19"> Jehovah shall march forth like a hero; Like a mighty warrior shall he rouse his indignation, He shall cry aloud; he shall shout the war-cry, And show himself mighty against his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 83:1–2; Ecclesiastes 8:11–12; 2 Peter 3:15"> “I have long held my peace; I have been still and refrained myself; But now will I cry like a woman in travail; I will destroy and swallow up at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 50:2; Nahum 1:4–6; Isaiah 44:27"> I will lay waste mountains and hills, And dry up all their herbs. I will make the river solid land, And dry up the pools of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; Luke 3:5; Isaiah 60:19–20"> Then will I lead the blind in a way which they know not, And in unknown paths will I guide them; I will make darkness light before them, And crooked paths straight; These things will I do for them, and not forsake them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Isaiah 1:29; Isaiah 44:11"> Then shall they be turned back, and be put to shame, Who trust in graven images; Who say to molten images, “Ye are our gods!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 43:8; Proverbs 20:12; Mark 7:34–37"> Hear, O ye deaf! And look, ye blind, and see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21"> Who is blind, if not my servant? And who so deaf as my messenger, whom I send? Who so blind as the friend of God, So blind as the servant of Jehovah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:2–4; Romans 2:21; Acts 28:22–27"> Thou seest many things, but regardest them not; Thou hast thine ears open, but hearest not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Romans 3:31; Isaiah 46:12–13"> It pleased Jehovah for his goodness' sake To give him a law, great and glorious;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Jeremiah 50:17; Isaiah 51:23"> And yet it is a robbed and plundered people; They are all of them bound in prisons, And hid in dungeons; They have become a spoil, and none delivereth; A prey, and none saith, “Restore!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Isaiah 48:18; Deuteronomy 32:29"> Who is there among you that will give ear to this, That will listen and attend for the time to come?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; Isaiah 10:5–6; Matthew 22:7"> Who gave Jacob to be a spoil, And Israel to plunderers? Was it not Jehovah, against whom we sinned, In whose ways we would not walk, And whose laws we would not obey?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 7:9; Isaiah 57:11; Malachi 2:2"> Therefore hath he poured out upon Israel the fury of his wrath, and the violence of war; It kindled a flame around about him, yet he did not regard it; It set him on fire, yet he laid it not to heart.</span>
