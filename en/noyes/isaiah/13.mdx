<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 50:1–64; Isaiah 1:1"> A prophecy concerning Babylon, which Isaiah, the son of Amoz, saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 5:26; Jeremiah 50:2"> Upon the bare mountain lift up a banner; Cry aloud to them, wave the hand, That they may enter the gates of the tyrants!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 3:11; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Revelation 17:12–18"> I have given orders to my consecrated ones, Yea, I have called upon my mighty ones to execute mine anger, My proud exulters!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 3:14; Revelation 19:11–21; Joel 2:25"> The noise of a multitude upon the mountains, like that of a great people! The tumultuous noise of kingdoms, of nations gathered together! Jehovah of hosts mustereth his army for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–46; Matthew 24:31; Jeremiah 50:9"> They come from a distant country, From the end of Heaven, Jehovah and the instruments of his indignation. To lay waste the whole land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Isaiah 13:9; Amos 5:18"> Howl ye, for the day of Jehovah is at hand! Like a destruction from the Almighty, it cometh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Nahum 2:10; Nahum 1:6"> Therefore shall all hands hang down, And every heart of man shall melt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Nahum 2:10"> They shall be in consternation; Distress and anguish shall lay hold of them; As a woman in travail shall they writhe; They shall look upon one another with amazement: Their faces shall glow like flames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> Behold! the day of Jehovah cometh, Terrible, full of wrath and burning indignation, To make the land a waste, And to destroy the sinners out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> For the stars of heaven, and the constellations thereof, Shall not give their light; The sun shall be darkened at his going forth, And the moon shall withhold her light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> For I will punish the world for its guilt, And the wicked for their iniquity. I will put an end to the arrogance of the proud, And I will bring down the haughtiness of the tyrants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; Isaiah 24:6; Job 28:16"> I will make men scarcer than gold; Yea, men than the gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–27; Joel 3:16; Matthew 24:29"> Therefore I will make the heavens tremble, And the earth shall be shaken out of her place, In the anger of Jehovah of hosts, In the day of his burning indignation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Jeremiah 50:16; 1 Kings 22:36"> Then shall they be like a chased doe; Like a flock, which no one gathereth together; Every one shall turn to his own people, And every one flee to his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:19–22; Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 47:9–14"> Every one that is overtaken shall be thrust through, And every one that is caught shall fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Hosea 10:14; Psalms 137:8–9"> Their children shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes; Their houses shall be plundered, and their wives ravished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremiah 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> Behold, I stir up against them the Medes, Who make no account of silver, And as to gold, they do not regard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:16"> Their bows shall strike down the young men, And on the fruit of the womb they shall have no compassion; Their eye shall not pity the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:30; Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 51:41"> So shall Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, The proud ornament of the Chaldeans, Be like Sodom and Gomorrah, which God overthrew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> It shall never more be inhabited; Nor shall it be dwelt in through all generations. Nor shall the Arabian pitch his tent there, Nor shall shepherds make their folds there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Isaiah 34:11–15"> But there shall the wild beasts of the desert lodge, And owls shall fill their houses; And ostriches shall dwell there, And satyrs shall dance there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 35:7"> Wolves shall howl in their palaces, And jackals in their pleasant edifices. Her time is near, And her days shall not be prolonged.</span>
