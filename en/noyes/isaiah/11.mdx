<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16; Zechariah 6:12"> Then shall spring forth a shoot from the stem of Jesse, And a sprout grow up from his roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; John 16:13; Ephesians 1:17–18"> The spirit of Jehovah shall rest upon him, The spirit of wisdom and understanding, The spirit of counsel and might, The spirit of the knowledge and of the fear of Jehovah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; John 8:15–16; Luke 2:52"> He shall take delight in the fear of Jehovah; He shall not judge by the sight of his eyes, Nor decide by the hearing of his ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 19:15; Malachi 4:6"> But with righteousness shall he judge the poor, And decide with equity for the afflicted of the land; He shall smite the earth with the rod of his mouth; With the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; 1 John 1:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> Righteousness shall be the girdle of his loins, And faithfulness the girdle of his reins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 65:25; Hosea 2:18; Ezekiel 34:25"> Then shall the wolf dwell with the lamb, And the leopard shall lie down with the kid; The calf, and the young lion, and the fatling shall be together, And a little child shall lead them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The cow and the bear shall feed together, Together shall their young lie down, And the lion shall eat straw like the ox.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5; Psalms 140:3"> The suckling shall play upon the hole of the asp, And the near-weaned child lay his hand on the hiding-place of the basilisk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:14; Isaiah 52:10; Psalms 98:2–3"> They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain; For the land shall be full of the knowledge of Jehovah, As the waters cover the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:9–12; Luke 2:32; Isaiah 11:1"> In that day shall the shoot of Jesse stand as a banner to the nations, And to him shall the gentiles repair, And his dwelling-place shall be glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 7:12; Isaiah 10:20; Zechariah 10:8–12"> In that day shall Jehovah the second time stretch forth his hand To recover the remnant of his people, That remaineth, from Assyria, and from Egypt, And from Pathros, and from Ethiopia, and from Elam, And from Shinar, and from Hamath, And from the islands of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; Isaiah 43:6; Zephaniah 3:10"> He shall set up a banner to the nations, And gather the outcasts of Israel, And bring together the dispersed of Judah, From the four extremities of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:18; Hosea 1:11; Ezekiel 37:16–24"> Then shall the jealousy of Ephraim depart, And the enmity in Judah be at an end; Ephraim shall not be jealous of Judah, And Judah shall not contend with Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 3:19; Isaiah 25:10; Jeremiah 49:28"> But they shall fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines at the sea; Together shall they plunder the children of the East; On Edom and Moab shall they lay their hand, And the sons of Ammon shall be subject to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Revelation 16:12; Isaiah 50:2"> Then will Jehovah utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea, And shake his hand over the river with a mighty wind, And smite it into seven streams, So that men may go over it dry-shod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 19:23; Isaiah 35:8–10; Isaiah 11:11"> And it shall be a highway for the remnant of the people, Which shall remain, from Assyria, As there was to Israel, When he came up from the land of Egypt.</span>
