<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Psalms 68:26; Isaiah 65:16"> Hear this, O house of Jacob! Ye that are called by the name of Israel; Ye that have come forth from the fountain of Judah; Ye that swear by the name of Jehovah, And praise the God of Israel, But not in truth and sincerity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Romans 2:17; Isaiah 52:1"> For they call themselves of the holy city, And stay themselves on the God of Israel, Whose name is Jehovah of hosts:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 42:9; Joshua 21:45; Isaiah 41:22"> What hath happened I declared to you long ago; From my mouth it proceeded, and I made it known; On a sudden I effected it, and it came to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Exodus 32:9; Zechariah 7:11–12"> Because I knew that thou art obstinate, And that thy neck is a bar of iron, And that thy brow is brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 48:3; Luke 1:70; Acts 15:18"> I declared it to thee long ago; Before it came to pass, I made it known to thee; Lest thou shouldst say, My idol effected it, And my graven image, and my molten image ordained it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; Isaiah 42:9; Romans 16:25–26"> Thou hast heard it; now see it all! And will ye not confess it? From this time I make you hear a new thing, Even a hidden thing, which thou hast not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> It is produced now, and not long ago; Before this day thou hast not heard of it, Lest thou shouldst say, Behold, I knew it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 58:3; Hosea 5:7; Jeremiah 6:10"> Yea, thou heardest it not; yea, thou knewest it not; Yea, it was not disclosed to thee long ago; For I knew that thou wast wholly faithless, And wast called rebellious from thy birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:9; Psalms 106:8; Psalms 143:11"> For the sake of my name I will defer my anger, And for the sake of my praise I will restrain it from thee, That I may not utterly cut thee off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12; Proverbs 17:3; Job 23:10"> Behold, I have melted thee, and found no silver; I have tried thee in the furnace of affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; Ezekiel 20:9; Isaiah 37:35"> For mine own sake will I do it; For how would my name be blasphemed? And my glory will I not give to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Revelation 22:13; Revelation 1:8"> Hearken to me, O Jacob, And Israel, whom I have called! I am He, I am the first, and I the last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:10–12; Psalms 102:25; Isaiah 40:26"> Yea, my hand hath founded the earth, And my right hand hath spread out the heavens; I called them; they stood forth together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 43:9; Jeremiah 50:21–29; Isaiah 13:17–18"> Assemble yourselves, all of you, and hear! Who among you hath declared these things? He whom Jehovah loveth will execute his pleasure upon Babylon, And his power upon the Chaldaeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 45:1–2; Psalms 45:4"> I, even I, have spoken; yea, I have called him; I have brought him, and his way shall be prosperous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 18:20; John 20:21–22; John 3:34"> Draw near to me, and hear ye this! I spake not in secret from the beginning; And since it began to be, I have been there; And now hath the Lord Jehovah sent me with his spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Psalms 25:12; Job 22:21–22"> Thus saith Jehovah, thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel; I am Jehovah, thy God, who teacheth thee what will profit thee; Who leadeth thee in the way thou shouldst go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:29; Isaiah 66:12; Psalms 119:165"> O that thou wouldst hearken to my commandments! Then shall thy peace be as a river, And thy prosperity as the waves of the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22; Genesis 22:17; Romans 9:27"> Then shall thy posterity be as the sand, And the fruit of thy body as the offspring of the sea; Thy name shall not be cut off, nor destroyed before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:45; Jeremiah 50:8; Isaiah 52:9"> Come ye forth from Babylon, flee ye from the land of the Chaldaeans with the voice of joy! Publish ye this, and make it known; Let it resound to the ends of the earth! Say: “Jehovah hath redeemed his servant Jacob;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; Isaiah 43:19–20; Psalms 105:41"> They thirst not in the deserts through which he leadeth them; Waters from the rock he causeth to flow for them; He cleaveth the rock, and the waters gush forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 57:21; Job 15:20–24; Luke 19:42"> There is no peace, saith Jehovah, for the wicked.”</span>
