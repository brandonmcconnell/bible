<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:1–21; 2 Chronicles 26:1–33; Isaiah 6:1"> The visions of Isaiah, the son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 30:19; Micah 1:2"> Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth! For Jehovah speaketh: “I have nourished and brought up children, And they have rebelled against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:7; Jeremiah 4:22; Isaiah 44:18"> The ox knoweth his owner, And the ass his master's crib; But Israel doth not know; My people do not consider.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Jeremiah 2:17; Jeremiah 2:13"> Ah, sinful nation! a people laden with iniquity! A race of evil-doers! degenerate children! They have forsaken Jehovah; they have despised the Holy One of Israel; They have gone backward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:8–11; Ezekiel 24:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> Where can ye be smitten again, Since ye renew your rebellion? The whole head is sick, and the whole heart faint;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 33:6; Matthew 9:12"> From the sole of the foot even to the head, there is no soundness in it; It is all bruises, and stripes, and flesh wounds, Neither pressed, nor bound up, nor softened with ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Lamentations 5:2; Deuteronomy 28:43; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Your country is desolate; Your cities are burnt with fire; Your ground, strangers devour it before your eyes; It is become desolate, destroyed by an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 12:15; Zechariah 2:10"> And the daughter of Zion is left as a shed in a vineyard, As a hut in a garden of cucumbers, As a besieged city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 9:29; Isaiah 37:31–32; Genesis 19:24"> Had not Jehovah of hosts left us a small remnant, We had soon become as Sodom; We had been like to Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:8; Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 9:7"> Hear ye the word of Jehovah, ye princes of Sodom! Give ear to the instruction of our God, ye people of Gomorrah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Amos 5:21; Jeremiah 6:20"> What to me is the multitude of your sacrifices? saith Jehovah; I am satiated with burnt-offerings of rams, and the fat of fed beasts; In the blood of bullocks and of lambs and of goats I have no delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 34:23; Exodus 23:17; Micah 6:8"> When ye come to appear before me, Who hath required this of you, to tread my courts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Malachi 1:10; Matthew 15:9"> Bring no more false oblations! Incense is an abomination to me, The new moon also, and the sabbath, and the calling of the assembly; Iniquity and festivals I cannot endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 5:21; Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 7:13"> Your new moons and your feasts my soul hateth; They are a burden to me; I am weary of bearing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 59:2–3; Proverbs 1:28"> When ye spread forth your hands, I will hide mine eyes from you; Yea, when ye multiply prayers, I will not hear: Your hands are full of blood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Isaiah 55:6–7; 1 Peter 2:1"> Wash you; make you clean; Put away your evil doings from before mine eyes;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Jeremiah 22:3; Psalms 82:3–4"> Cease to do evil; Learn to do well; Seek justice; relieve the oppressed; Defend the fatherless; plead for the widow!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Revelation 7:14; Psalms 51:7"> Come, now, and let us argue together, saith Jehovah. Though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow; Though they be red as crimson, they shall be like wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:15–16; Isaiah 3:10; Joel 2:26"> If ye be willing and obedient, Ye shall consume the good of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; Isaiah 58:14; Numbers 23:19"> But if ye refuse, and be rebellious, The sword shall consume you; For the mouth of Jehovah hath said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1–23; Zechariah 8:3; Zephaniah 3:1–3"> How is the faithful city become a harlot, She that was full of equity! Once justice dwelt in her, but now murderers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:28–30; Lamentations 4:1–2; Hosea 4:18"> Thy silver is become dross; Thy wine is adulterated with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Micah 7:3; Exodus 23:8; Zechariah 7:10"> Thy princes are faithless, companions of thieves; Every one of them loveth gifts, and seeketh rewards; They render not justice to the fatherless, And the cause of the widow cometh not before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 35:4; Deuteronomy 28:63"> Wherefore, thus saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, the Mighty One of Israel: Ha! I will ease me of mine adversaries, And avenge me of mine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Malachi 3:3; Jeremiah 9:7; Isaiah 4:4"> And I will again turn my hand toward thee, And wholly purge away thy dross, And take away all thy alloy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 62:1–2"> And I will restore thee judges, as at the first, And counsellors, as at the beginning. Then shalt thou be called the city of righteousness, the faithful city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Isaiah 45:21–25; Isaiah 5:16"> Through justice shall Zion be delivered, And her reformed sons through righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:8–9; Psalms 1:6; Isaiah 30:13"> But destruction shall fall at once on the rebels and sinners; Yea, they that forsake Jehovah shall be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 65:3; Isaiah 57:5; Isaiah 66:17"> For ye shall be ashamed of the terebinths in which ye delighted; Ye shall blush for the gardens which ye loved;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; Ezekiel 17:9–10; Matthew 21:19"> And ye shall be as a terebinth-tree whose leaves are withered, And as a garden in which is no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; Ezekiel 32:21; Malachi 4:1"> The strong shall become tow, And his work a spark of fire; Both shall burn together, And none shall quench them.</span>
