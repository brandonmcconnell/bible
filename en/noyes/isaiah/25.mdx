<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 15:2; Psalms 118:28; Numbers 23:19"> O Jehovah, thou art my God; I will exalt thee; I will praise thy name, For thou hast done wonderful things; Thine ancient purposes are faithfulness and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1; Isaiah 13:22; Isaiah 25:12"> Thou hast made the city a heap; The fortified city a ruin. The palace of the barbarians is to be no more a city! It shall never be built again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; Isaiah 49:23–26; Psalms 46:10–11"> Therefore shall mighty kingdoms praise thee; The cities of the terrible nations shall honor thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:12; Isaiah 32:2; James 2:5"> For thou hast been a defence to the poor; A defence to the needy in his distress; A refuge from the storm, a shadow from the heat, When the rage of tyrants was like a storm against a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 20:8–9; Isaiah 49:10; Psalms 105:39"> As heat in a dry land is made to vanish, So thou puttest down the tumult of the barbarians; As heat is allayed by a thick cloud, So the triumph of the tyrants is brought low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 8:11; Psalms 63:5; Zechariah 9:16–17"> Then in this mountain shall Jehovah of hosts prepare for all nations A feast of fat things, and wines kept on the lees; Of fat things full of marrow, of wines kept on the lees well refined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 60:1–3; Matthew 27:51"> He will destroy in this mountain the covering that was cast over all people, And the veil that was spread over all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:54"> He will destroy death forever; The Lord Jehovah will wipe away the tears from all faces, And the reproach of his people will he take away from the whole earth; For Jehovah hath said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Genesis 49:18; Micah 7:7"> In that day shall men say, “Behold, this is our God; We waited for him, and he hath saved us; This is Jehovah, for whom we waited; Let us rejoice and exult in his salvation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Isaiah 18:4; Zephaniah 2:9"> For the hand of Jehovah shall rest upon this mountain, And Moab shall be trodden down in his place, As straw is trodden down in a dung-pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:14; Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 16:6"> And he shall stretch out his hands in the midst of it, As the swimmer stretcheth out his hands to swim, But God shall put down his pride, Together with the devices of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:5; Revelation 18:21; Isaiah 14:23"> And the high bulwarks of thy walls will he lay low; He will bring them down to the ground; he will lay them in the dust.</span>
