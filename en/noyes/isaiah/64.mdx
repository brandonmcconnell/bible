<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Mark 1:10; Revelation 20:11"> O that thou wouldst rend the heavens, and come down; That the mountains might tremble at thy presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 99:1; Jeremiah 33:9; Ezekiel 38:22–23"> As fire kindleth the dry stubble, As fire causeth water to boil, To make known thy name to thine adversaries, That the nations might tremble at thy presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Psalms 65:5–6; Psalms 66:3"> As thou once didst wonderful things, which we looked not for, And camest down, so that the mountains trembled at thy presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; Psalms 31:19; James 5:7"> For never have men heard, nor perceived by the ear, Nor hath eye seen, a God beside thee. Who doeth such things for those that trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:13–15; Hebrews 4:16; Acts 10:35"> Thou art the friend of those who joyfully do righteousness, Those who remember thee in thy ways. Behold, thou art angry, and we are punished; Long doth the punishment endure, before we are delivered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 7:18; Romans 7:24; Ephesians 2:1–2"> We are all of us an unclean thing; Like a filthy garment is all our righteousness; We are all withered like a leaf, And our sins, like a storm, have blown us away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 59:16"> There is none that calleth upon thy name, That stirreth himself up to cleave to thee; For thou hidest thy face from us, And lettest us perish by our iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Psalms 119:73; Jeremiah 18:2–6"> But now, O Jehovah, thou art our father; We are the clay, and thou hast formed us; We are all of us the work of thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; Psalms 74:1–2; Psalms 79:13"> Be not wroth, O Jehovah, to the uttermost, Nor remember our iniquity forever! Behold, look upon us, we beseech thee, we are all thy people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; Luke 21:24; 2 Chronicles 36:19–21"> Thy holy cities have become a wilderness; Zion is become a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 25:9; Psalms 74:5–7; Lamentations 1:7"> Our holy and glorious house, Where our fathers praised thee, Is burned with fire, And all our precious things are laid waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 42:14; Psalms 74:10–11; Psalms 83:1"> Wilt thou contain thyself at these things, O Jehovah, Wilt thou keep silence, and still grievously afflict us?</span>
