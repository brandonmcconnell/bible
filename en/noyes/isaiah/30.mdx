<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2; Isaiah 1:2; Hosea 7:13"> Woe to the rebellious children, saith Jehovah, Who form plans, and not from me, And make covenants without my spirit, That they may add sin to sin!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Numbers 27:21; Deuteronomy 28:68"> Who go down into Egypt, Without inquiring at my mouth, To seek refuge in Pharaoh's protection, And to trust in the shadow of Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 20:5; Romans 10:11; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> The protection of Pharaoh shall be your shame; Your trust in the shadow of Egypt your confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Isaiah 57:9; Jeremiah 43:7"> For their princes are at Zoan, Their ambassadors have arrived at Hanes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:36; Isaiah 30:7; Isaiah 30:16"> But they are all ashamed of a people that do not profit them; That are no help and no profit, But only a shame and a reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:15; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 11:4"> The loaded beasts go southward. Through a land of anguish and distress, Whence come forth the lioness, and the fierce lion, The viper, and the flying fiery serpent; On the shoulders of young asses they carry their wealth, And on the bunches of camels their treasures, To a people that will not profit them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 2:22; Isaiah 7:4; Psalms 118:8–9"> Vain and empty is the help of Egypt; Wherefore I call her, The Blusterer that sitteth still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:22; Habakkuk 2:2; Isaiah 8:1"> Go now, write this on a tablet before them; Note it down upon a book, That it may remain for future times, A testimony forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Acts 7:51; Zechariah 1:4–6"> For this is a rebellious people, false children; Children who will not hear the law of Jehovah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–10; Romans 16:18; Amos 2:12"> Who say to the seers, “See not!” And to the prophets, “Prophesy not right things; Speak to us smooth things, Prophesy falsehood!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Romans 1:30; John 15:23–24"> Turn aside from the way, Depart from the path, Remove from our sight the Holy One of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1–3; Isaiah 5:24; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> Wherefore thus saith the Holy One of Israel: Since ye despise this word, And trust in oppression and perverseness, And lean thereon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 29:5; Ezekiel 13:10–15; 1 Kings 20:30"> Therefore shall this iniquity be to you Like a breach ready to give way, That swelleth out in a high wall, Whose fall cometh suddenly, in an instant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:10–11; 2 Peter 2:4–5"> It is broken like a potter's vessel, Which is dashed in pieces and not spared, So that among its fragments not a sherd is found to take up fire from the hearth, Or to dip water from the cistern.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3–4; Isaiah 32:17; Jeremiah 3:22–23"> For thus said the Lord Jehovah, the Holy One of Israel: By a return and by rest shall ye be saved; In quietness and confidence is your strength;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 31:1; Isaiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 28:25"> But ye would not. Ye said, “No! we will bound along upon horses”; Truly ye shall bound along in flight. “We will ride upon swift coursers”; But they shall be swift that pursue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 32:30"> A thousand shall flee at the rebuke of one, And ten thousand at the rebuke of five, Till what remains of you shall be as a beacon on the top of a mountain, As a banner upon a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25–26; 2 Peter 3:9; Proverbs 16:20"> And yet will Jehovah wait to be gracious to you, And yet will he arise to have mercy upon you; For Jehovah is a righteous God; Happy are all they who wait for him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 60:20; Psalms 50:15; Isaiah 65:24"> For, O people of Zion, that dwellest in Jerusalem, Thou shalt not always weep! He will be very gracious to thee at the voice of thy cry; No sooner shall he hear it, than he will answer thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; Psalms 80:5; Amos 8:11–12"> Though Jehovah hath given thee the bread of distress, and the water of affliction, Yet shall thy teachers be hidden from thee no more; But thine eyes shall see thy teachers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 32:8; Proverbs 3:5–6; Isaiah 58:11"> And thine ears shall hear a voice behind thee, Saying, “This is the way, walk ye in it!” When ye turn aside to the right hand, or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 17:3–4; Isaiah 31:7; Zechariah 13:2"> Ye shall treat as defiled the silver coverings of your graven images, And the golden clothing of your molten images, Ye shall cast them away as an unclean thing; Away! shall ye say to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:11–12; Isaiah 32:20; Psalms 65:9–13"> Then will he give rain for thy seed, With which thou shalt sow the ground, And the bread-corn, the produce of the land, shall be rich and nourishing; Then shall thy cattle feed in large pastures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 45:6; Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Samuel 8:12"> The oxen also, and the young asses, that till the ground, Shall eat well-seasoned provender, Which hath been winnowed with the shovel and the fan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:19–20; Isaiah 35:6–7; Isaiah 2:14–15"> And on every lofty mountain, And on every high hill, Shall be brooks and streams of water, In the day of the great slaughter, When the towers fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 22:5"> Then shall the light of the moon be as the light of the sun, And the light of the sun shall be sevenfold, &#91;&#91;As the light of seven days,&#93;&#93; When Jehovah bindeth up the bruises of his people, And healeth the wound which they have received.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:8; Nahum 1:5–6; Psalms 18:7–9"> Behold, the name of Jehovah cometh from afar, His anger burneth, and violent is the flame, His lips are full of indignation, And his tongue like a devouring fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Isaiah 11:4; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> His breath is like an overflowing torrent, That reacheth even to the neck; He will toss the nation with the winnowing-fan of destruction; He will put a bridle upon the jaws of the people, that shall lead them astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; Psalms 95:1–2; Exodus 15:1–21"> But ye shall then sing as in the night of a solemn festival; Your heart shall be glad, like his who marcheth with the sound of the pipe To the mountain of Jehovah, to the rock of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 18:13–14; Joshua 10:11; Isaiah 29:6"> Jehovah will cause his glorious voice to be heard, And the blow of his arm to be seen, With furious anger, and flames of devouring fire; With flood, and storm, and hailstones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 9:4; Micah 5:5–6"> For by the voice of Jehovah shall the Assyrian be beaten down; He will smite him with the rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 11:15; Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 2:19"> And as often as the appointed rod shall strike, Which Jehovah shall lay heavily upon him, It shall be accompanied with tabrets and harps; And with fierce battles will he fight against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Genesis 19:24; Jeremiah 19:6"> For long hath the burning place been prepared; Yea, for the king hath it been made ready; The pile is made deep and broad; There is fire and wood in abundance; The breath of Jehovah, like a stream of brimstone, kindle it.</span>
