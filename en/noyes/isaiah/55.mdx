<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 7:37–38; Revelation 21:6; Revelation 22:17"> Ho, every one that thirsteth, come ye to the waters! Even ye that have no money, come ye, buy and eat! Yea, come, buy wine and milk, Without money and without price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Jeremiah 2:13; Psalms 22:26"> Wherefore do ye spend your money for that which is not bread, And your substance for that which doth not satisfy? Listen attentively to me; so shall ye eat that which is good, And your soul shall delight itself with delicacies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:40; Proverbs 4:20; Matthew 11:28"> Incline your ear, and come to me; Hear, and your soul shall live! And I will make with you an everlasting covenant; I will give you the sure mercies of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–24; Isaiah 9:6–7; Daniel 9:25"> Behold, I gave him for a commander to the nations; For a prince, and a lawgiver to the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:9; Isaiah 60:5; Zechariah 2:11"> Behold, the nation which thou knowest not thou shalt call; And the nation which knew not thee shall run to thee, For the sake of Jehovah, thy God, And of the Holy One of Israel, for he hath glorified thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–14; Psalms 145:18; Isaiah 45:19"> Seek ye Jehovah, while he may be found; Call upon him while he is near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 4:8–10; Ezekiel 18:21–23"> Let the wicked forsake his way, And the unrighteous man his thoughts; Let him return to Jehovah, and he will have mercy upon him, And to our God, for he will abundantly pardon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:5; Psalms 40:5; Hosea 14:9"> For my thoughts are not your thoughts, Neither are your ways my ways, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:31–36; Psalms 103:11; Matthew 11:25"> For as the heavens are higher than the earth, So are my ways higher than your ways, And my thoughts than your thoughts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 32:2; Isaiah 30:23"> For as the rain and the snow descend from heaven, And return not thither, But water the earth, and make it bear and put forth its increase, That it may give seed to the sower, and bread to the eater;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Isaiah 46:10; Romans 10:17"> So shall my word be, that goeth forth from my mouth; It shall not return to me void; But it shall bring to pass that which is my pleasure, And it shall accomplish that for which I send it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:22; Isaiah 44:23; Psalms 105:43"> For ye shall go out with joy And be led forth with peace; The mountains and the hills shall break forth before you into singing, And all the trees of the field shall clap their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Isaiah 41:19; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> Instead of the thorn shall grow up the cypress-tree, And instead of the bramble shall grow up the myrtle-tree, And it shall be to Jehovah for a name; For an everlasting memorial, that shall not pass away.</span>
