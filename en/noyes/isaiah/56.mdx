<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:11–14; Psalms 85:9; Matthew 3:2"> Thus saith Jehovah: Keep ye justice, and practise righteousness; For the coming of my salvation is near, And my deliverance is soon to be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 31:13–16; Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 112:1"> Happy the man that doeth this, And the son of man that holdeth it fast; That keepeth the sabbath, and profaneth it not, And restraineth his hand from doing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:17; Acts 10:34; Matthew 19:12–30"> And let not the stranger that joineth himself to Jehovah say, Jehovah hath wholly separated me from his people. And let not the eunuch say, Behold, I am a dry tree!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:111; Luke 10:42; Isaiah 27:5"> For thus saith Jehovah concerning the eunuchs: They that keep my sabbaths, And take pleasure in doing my will, And hold fast my covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:12; 1 John 3:1; Revelation 3:5"> To them will I give in my house, and within my walls, a portion and a name, Better than of sons and daughters; An everlasting name will I give them, That shall never fade away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 56:2–4; Isaiah 58:13"> The strangers, also, that join themselves to Jehovah, to serve him, To love the name of Jehovah, and to be his servants. Every one that keepeth the sabbath, and profaneth it not, And holdeth fast my covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 11:17; Isaiah 2:2–3; Malachi 1:11"> Them will I bring to my holy mountain, And I will make them rejoice in my house of prayer; Their burnt-offerings and sacrifices shall be accepted on mine altar; For my house shall be called a house of prayer for all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 54:7; Isaiah 60:3–11"> Thus saith the Lord Jehovah, That gathereth the outcasts of Israel; Yet will I gather others to him, Besides those that are already gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:9; Revelation 19:17–18; Isaiah 18:6"> Come, all ye beasts of the field, Yea, all ye beasts of the forest, to devour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 29:10; Philippians 3:2; Isaiah 58:1"> His watchmen are all blind; they know nothing; They are all dumb dogs, that cannot bark, Dreaming, lying down, loving to slumber;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2–3; Ezekiel 13:19; Micah 3:11"> Yet are they greedy dogs that cannot be satisfied; The shepherds themselves will not attend; They all turn aside to their own way, Every one of them to their own gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 10:6; Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:19–20"> “Come on, let me bring wine, And let us fill ourselves with strong drink, And to-morrow shall be as to-day, And even much more abundant.”</span>
