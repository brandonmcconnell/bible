<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 14:23; Isaiah 13:1; Jeremiah 51:42"> The prophecy concerning the desert of the sea. As storms which rush along through the south, So it cometh from the desert, From the terrible land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Jeremiah 49:34; Psalms 60:3"> A grievous vision was revealed to me; The plunderer plundereth, and the destroyer destroyeth. “Go up, O Elam! Besiege, O Media! All sighing do I make to cease.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Psalms 48:6; Isaiah 26:17"> Therefore are my loins full of pain; Pangs have seized me, as the pangs of a woman in travail; For convulsions I cannot hear; For anguish I cannot see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:34–36; 1 Samuel 25:36–38; Daniel 5:1"> My heart panteth, Terror hath seized upon me; The evening of my desire is changed into horror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:11; Jeremiah 51:57; Jeremiah 51:39"> The table is prepared; the watch is set; They eat; they drink; “Arise, ye princes! Anoint the shield!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:2–7; Ezekiel 3:17; Habakkuk 2:1–2"> For thus said the Lord unto me: “Go, set a watchman, Who shall declare what he seeth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Isaiah 37:24; Hebrews 2:1"> And he saw a troop, horsemen in pairs, Riders on asses, and riders on camels, And he watched with the utmost heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1–2; Isaiah 56:10; 1 Peter 5:8"> Then he cried like a lion: “My Lord, I stand continually upon the watch-tower in the daytime, And keep my post all the night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:8"> And behold, there cometh a troop, Horsemen in pairs.” Again also he lifted up his voice, and said: “Fallen, fallen is Babylon, And all the graven images of her gods are cast broken to the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Micah 4:13; 2 Kings 13:7"> O my threshing, and the corn of my floor! What I have heard from Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel, That have I declared to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:14; Obadiah 1:1–16; Amos 1:6"> The prophecy concerning Dumah. A voice came to me from Seir: “Watchman, what of the night? Watchman, what of the night?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:30–32; Acts 2:37–38; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> The watchman saith: “Morning cometh, and also night. If ye will inquire, inquire! Return, come!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:23–24; 1 Chronicles 1:32; Ezekiel 27:15"> The prophecy against the Arabians. In the thickets of Arabia shall ye lodge, O ye caravans of Dedan!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; Job 6:19; Romans 12:20"> The inhabitants of the land of Tema Bring water to the thirsty; They come to meet the fugitive with bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:19–20; Isaiah 13:14"> For they flee from swords, From the drawn sword, And from the bent bow, And from the fury of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 120:5; Isaiah 60:7; Isaiah 16:14"> For thus saith the Lord to me: Within one year, according to the years of a hireling, Shall all the glory of Kedar be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Numbers 23:19; Matthew 24:35"> The remainder of the mighty bowmen of the sons of Kedar shall be diminished; For Jehovah, the God of Israel, hath said it.</span>
