<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Zechariah 9:1; Amos 1:3–5"> The prophecy concerning Damascus. Behold, Damascus shall be no more a city; It shall become a heap of ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:5; Jeremiah 7:33; Numbers 32:34"> The cities of Aroer shall be forsaken; They shall be pastures for flocks, Which shall lie down, and none shall make them afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 9:11; Isaiah 8:4; Isaiah 7:16"> The fortress shall cease from Ephraim, And the kingdom from Damascus, and the rest of Syria; It shall be with them as with the glory of the children of Israel, Saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> In that day shall the glory of Jacob sink away, And the fatness of his flesh become leanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremiah 51:33"> And it shall be as when the harvest-man gathereth the corn, And reapeth the ears with his arm; Yea, as when one gleaneth ears in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> There shall be left in it only a gleaning, as in the olive-harvest, Two or three berries on the top of the highest bough, Four or five on the fruitful branches, Saith Jehovah, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 7:7; Hosea 6:1; Hosea 3:5"> In that day shall a man have regard to his Maker, And his eyes shall look to the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> He shall not look to the altars, the work of his hands, Nor have respect to that which his fingers have made, The images of Astarte and the Sun-pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> In that day shall his fortified cities be like ruins in the forests, or on the mountain tops, Which the enemy left, in flight from the children of Israel; And the land shall be a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> For thou hast forgotten the God of thy salvation, And hast not been mindful of the rock of thy strength; Therefore though thou plant beautiful plants, And set shoots from a foreign soil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Hosea 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> When thou hast planted them, though thou hedge them in, And in the morning bring thy plants to the blossom, Yet shall the harvest flee away, In the day of pain and desperate sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:23; Luke 21:25; Psalms 18:4"> Alas! a tumult of many nations! They rage with the raging of the sea. Alas! a roaring of kingdoms! They roar with the roaring of mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:5; Psalms 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> Like the roaring of mighty waters do the nations roar; He rebuketh them, and they flee away, Driven like the chaff of the mountains before the wind, Like stubble before the whirlwind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:35; Proverbs 22:23; Jeremiah 13:25"> At the time of evening, behold, terror! Before morning, behold, they are no more! This is the portion of them that spoil us, And the lot of them that plunder us.</span>
