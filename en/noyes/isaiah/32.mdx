<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 33:15; Hebrews 1:8–9"> Behold! a king shall reign in righteousness, And princes shall rule with equity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; Isaiah 44:3; Isaiah 9:6"> Every one of them shall be a hiding-place from the wind, And a shelter from the tempest; As streams of water in a dry place, As the shadow of a great rock in a weary land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 29:18; Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> The eyes of them that see shall no more be blind, And the ears of them that hear shall hearken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 29:24; Luke 21:14–15; Matthew 16:17"> The heart of the rash shall gain wisdom, And the tongue of the stammerer learn to speak plainly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:20; Psalms 15:4; Malachi 3:18"> The vile shall no more be called liberal, Nor the niggard said to be bountiful;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 15:19; Matthew 23:13; Isaiah 3:15"> For the vile will still utter villany, And his heart will devise iniquity; He will practise deception, and speak impiety against God; He will take away the food of the hungry, And deprive the thirsty of drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:26–28; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 26:14–16"> The instruments also of the niggard are evil; He plotteth mischievous devices, To destroy the poor with lying words, Even when the cause of the needy is just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29–30; Luke 6:33–35"> But the liberal deviseth liberal things, And in liberal things will he persevere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:9; Isaiah 3:16; Isaiah 28:23"> Arise, hear my voice, ye women that are at ease! Give ear to my speech, ye careless daughters!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 7:23; Jeremiah 8:13; Zephaniah 1:13"> One year more, and ye shall tremble, ye careless women! For the vintage shall fail; the harvest shall not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 33:14; Isaiah 20:4"> Tremble, O ye that are at ease! Be in dismay, ye careless ones! Strip you, make you bare, gird ye sackcloth upon your loins!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:15; Ezekiel 20:6; Nahum 2:7"> They shall smite themselves on their breasts, On account of the pleasant fields, On account of the fruitful vine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Hosea 9:6; Isaiah 7:23"> Upon the land of my people shall come up thorns and briers; Yea, upon all the houses of joy in the joyous city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 24:12; Isaiah 24:10; Isaiah 25:2"> For the palace shall be forsaken; The tumult of the city shall be solitary; The fortified hill and the tower shall be dens forever; The joy of wild-asses, the pasture of flocks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 29:17; Ezekiel 39:29; Isaiah 44:3"> Until the spirit from on high be poured upon us, And the wilderness become a fruitful field, And the fruitful field be esteemed a forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 94:14–15; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Isaiah 60:21"> Then shall justice dwell in the wilderness, And righteousness in the fruitful field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 26:3; Psalms 119:165; Philippians 4:6–9"> And the effect of righteousness shall be peace, And the fruit of righteousness quiet and security forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 2:5; Hosea 2:18–23; Jeremiah 33:16"> Then shall my people dwell in peaceful habitations, In secure dwellings, in quiet resting-places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:17; Isaiah 30:30; Isaiah 28:2"> But the hail shall descend, and the forest shall fall; And the city shall be brought very low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:1; Isaiah 30:23–24; Isaiah 55:10–11"> Happy ye who sow beside all waters; Who send forth thither the feet of the ox and the ass!</span>
