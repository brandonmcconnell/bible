<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Isaiah 51:17; Zechariah 3:4"> Awake, awake, put on thy majesty, O Zion! Put on thy beautiful garments, O Jerusalem, the holy city! For no more shall come into thee the uncircumcised and the unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Jeremiah 51:50; Luke 4:18"> Shake thyself from the dust, Arise and sit erect, O Jerusalem! Loose thyself from the bands of thy neck, O captive daughter of Zion!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18; Isaiah 45:13; Psalms 44:12"> For thus saith Jehovah: For naught were ye sold, And without money shall ye be ransomed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 46:6; Psalms 69:4; Acts 7:14–15"> For thus saith the Lord Jehovah: My people went down to Egypt formerly to sojourn there, And the Assyrian oppressed them without cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Psalms 44:16; Psalms 74:10"> And now, what have I here to do, saith the Lord, When my people is taken away for naught, And their tyrants exult, saith Jehovah, And all the day long my name is blasphemed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10–11; Ezekiel 37:13–14; Ezekiel 20:44"> Therefore shall my people know my name; Therefore in that day shall they know That I am he that said, Behold, here am I!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:12–15; Nahum 1:15; Isaiah 61:1–3"> How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, of him that proclaimeth peace! That bringeth good tidings, that proclaimeth salvation! That saith to Zion, “Thy God is king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6; Ezekiel 33:7; Jeremiah 32:39"> Hark! the voice of thy watchmen! They lift up the voice together; yea, they shout! For eye to eye shall they behold, When Jehovah returneth to Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Psalms 98:4; Isaiah 48:20"> Break forth into joy; shout together, ye ruins of Jerusalem! For Jehovah hath pity on his people; he redeemeth Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 3:6; Isaiah 51:9; Psalms 98:1–3"> Jehovah maketh bare his holy arm In the sight of all the nations; All the ends of the earth Behold the salvation from our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17; Zechariah 2:6–7; Isaiah 48:20"> Depart, depart ye; go ye out from thence; Touch no unclean thing! Go ye out from the midst of her; Be ye clean, ye that bear the vessels of Jehovah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:8; Deuteronomy 20:4; Exodus 14:19–20"> For not in haste shall ye go forth, Nor in flight shall ye pass along; For Jehovah shall march in your front, And the God of Israel bring up your rear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–11; Matthew 28:18; John 3:31"> Behold, my servant shall prosper; He shall be lifted up, and set on high, and greatly exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:2–5; Matthew 26:67"> As many were amazed at the sight of him,—So disfigured and scarcely human was his visage, And so unlike that of a man was his form,—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20–21; Isaiah 49:7; Isaiah 55:5"> So shall he cause many nations to exult on account of him; Kings shall shut their mouths before him. For what had never been told them shall they see, And what they never heard shall they perceive.</span>
