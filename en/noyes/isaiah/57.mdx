<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 22:20; Psalms 12:1; 2 Chronicles 34:28"> The righteous man perisheth, and no one layeth it to heart; And pious men are taken away, and none considereth That because of the evil the righteous man is taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Luke 2:29"> He entereth into peace; He resteth in his bed, Every one that walketh in uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 3:10; Matthew 16:4; Hosea 1:2"> But draw near hither, ye sons of the sorceress, Ye brood of the adulterer and the harlot!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Psalms 35:21; Isaiah 1:4"> Of whom do ye make your sport, And at whom do ye make wide the mouth, And draw out the tongue? Are ye not rebellious children, a treacherous brood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; 2 Kings 16:3–4; Ezekiel 16:20"> Burning with lust for idols Under every green tree, Slaying children in the valleys, Under the clefts of the rocks?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:9; Jeremiah 7:18; Habakkuk 2:19"> With the smooth stones of the valley is thy portion; These, these are thy lot; Here thou pourest out thy drink-offering, And presentest thy meat-offering; Can I see such things, and be at rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:16; Ezekiel 23:41; Jeremiah 2:20"> Upon a high and lofty mountain settest thou thy bed; Thither dost thou go up to offer sacrifice;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:32; Ezekiel 23:2–20; Ezekiel 16:25–28"> Behind the doors and the posts dost thou place thy memorial; Thou departest from me, and uncoverest, and ascendest, and enlargest thy bed. Thou makest an agreement with them; Thou desirest their bed; Thou choosest a place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:16; Colossians 2:18; Ezekiel 23:40"> Thou goest to the king with oil, And takest much precious perfume; Thou sendest thine ambassadors afar, Yea, down to the under-world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 18:12; Jeremiah 5:3"> In the length of thy journeys thou hast wearied thyself, But thou sayest not, “I will desist”; Thou yet findest life in thy hand, Therefore thou art not discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:12–13; Proverbs 29:25; Psalms 50:21"> On account of whom art thou anxious, and of whom art thou afraid, that thou hast proved false, And hast not remembered me, nor laid it to heart? Behold, I have been silent a long time; Therefore thou fearest me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Micah 3:2–4; Isaiah 1:11–15"> But now I announce thy deliverance, And thy works do not profit thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 37:9; Psalms 37:3; Isaiah 56:7"> When thou criest, let thy host of idols deliver thee! But the wind shall bear them all away; A breath shall take them off; But he that putteth his trust in me Shall possess the land, And shall inherit my holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 62:10; Isaiah 35:8; Isaiah 40:3"> Men shall say, Cast up, cast up, prepare the road; Remove every obstruction from the way of my people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 34:18; Isaiah 66:1–2; Psalms 138:6"> For thus saith the high and lofty One That inhabiteth eternity, whose name is Holy: I dwell in the high and holy place; With him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit; To revive the spirit of the humble, And to revive the heart of the contrite ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Jeremiah 10:24; Psalms 103:9–16"> For I will not contend forever, Nor will I be always angry; For life would fail before me, And the souls which I created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Jeremiah 6:13; Colossians 3:5"> For the guilt of his covetousness I was angry; I smote him, I hid myself, and was angry; But yet he went on perversely in the way of his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6; Revelation 7:17; Jeremiah 3:22"> I have seen his ways, yet will I heal him; I will guide him, and I will restore comfort To him and to his mourners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:15; Acts 2:39; Luke 2:14"> I create the fruit of the lips. Peace, peace to him that is far off, and to him that is nigh, Saith Jehovah; I will heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 3:11; Proverbs 4:16–17; Job 18:5–14"> But the wicked is like the troubled sea, Which cannot rest, Whose waters cast up mire and dirt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:22; Isaiah 3:11; 2 Kings 9:22"> There is no peace, saith my God, for the wicked.</span>
