<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Isaiah 43:7; Zechariah 13:9"> But now thus saith Jehovah, that created thee, O Jacob, That formed thee, O Israel: Fear not, for I have redeemed thee; I have called thee by name; thou art mine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Joshua 1:9; Psalms 66:12"> When thou passest through waters, I will be with thee; And through rivers, they shall not overflow thee; When thou walkest through fire, thou shalt not be burned, And the flame shall not consume thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 60:16; Isaiah 45:15; 2 Chronicles 14:9–14"> For I am Jehovah, thy God, The Holy One of Israel, thy saviour. I will give Egypt for thy ransom, Ethiopia and Seba for thee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 31:3"> Because thou art precious in my sight, Because thou art honored, and I love thee, Therefore will I give men for thee, And nations for thy life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Isaiah 49:12; Luke 13:29"> Fear not, for I am with thee! I will bring thy children from the East, And gather thee from the West.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18; Jeremiah 3:14; Jeremiah 3:18–19"> I will say to the North, “Give them up!” And to the South, “Withhold them not! Bring my sons from afar, And my daughters from the ends of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 43:21; Ephesians 1:12"> Every one that is called by my name, That I have created for my glory, That I have formed and made!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:18–20"> Bring forth the blind people, having eyes, And the deaf, having ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 46:10; Isaiah 44:7–9; Isaiah 41:21–26"> Let all the nations be gathered together, And the kingdoms be assembled! Who among them hath declared this, And can show us former predictions? Let them produce their witnesses that they are right; That men may hear, and say, It is true!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:4; Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 46:8–9"> Ye are my witnesses, saith Jehovah, And my servant whom I have chosen, That ye may know and believe me, And understand that I am He. Before me was no god formed, And after me there shall be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 13:4; Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 45:21–22"> I, I am Jehovah, And besides me there is no saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:12; Psalms 81:9–10; Isaiah 43:10"> I have declared and have saved; I made it known, when there was no strange god among you; Ye are my witnesses, saith Jehovah, That I am God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 14:27; Isaiah 46:10; Psalms 90:2"> Even from the beginning of time I have been He, And none can rescue from my hand; I undertake, and who can hinder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 23:13; Jeremiah 51:24"> Thus saith Jehovah, Your redeemer, the Holy One of Israel; For your sakes have I sent to Babylon, And caused all her fugitives, And the Chaldeans, to descend to the ships of their delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 43:3; Matthew 25:34; Isaiah 48:17"> I, Jehovah, am your Holy One, The creator of Israel, your king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 43:2; Psalms 77:19"> Thus saith Jehovah,—He that made a way in the sea, And a path in the mighty waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:4–9; Isaiah 1:31; Ezekiel 38:8–18"> That caused the chariot and the horse, the army and the forces, to march forth; There they lay down together; they rose no more; They were extinguished; they were quenched like a torch;—</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; 2 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Chronicles 16:12"> Remember not the former things; The things of old regard no more!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 48:6; Isaiah 42:9; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Behold, I do a new thing; Now shall it spring forth; yea, ye shall see it. Behold, I make a way in the wilderness, And streams in the desert;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:17; Isaiah 48:21; Isaiah 49:10"> The beasts of the forest shall honor me, The jackals and the ostriches; For I make rivers in the wilderness And streams in the desert, To give drink to my people, my chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Hebrews 13:15; Psalms 102:18"> This people, which I have formed for myself Shall make known my praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Malachi 3:14; Malachi 1:13"> Yet thou hast not called upon me, O Jacob, So as to have wearied thyself for me, O Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Zechariah 7:5–6; Malachi 3:8"> Thou hast not brought me thy lambs for a burnt-offering, Nor honored me with thy sacrifices; I have not burdened thee with oblations, Nor wearied thee with incense;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Jeremiah 6:20; Isaiah 1:14"> Thou hast bought me no sweet-smelling reed with silver, Neither hast thou satisfied me with the fat of thy sacrifices; With thy sins hast thou burdened me, And wearied me with thine iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:12; Micah 7:18–19; Hebrews 10:17"> I, I myself, blot out thy transgressions for my own sake, And will not remember thy sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 43:9; Romans 8:33"> Put me in remembrance; let us plead together; Speak that thou mayst justify thyself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 28:7; Jeremiah 5:31; Ezekiel 22:25–28"> Thy forefathers sinned, And thy teachers were rebellious against me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Isaiah 65:15; Zechariah 8:13"> Therefore have I profaned the princes of the sanctuary, And given up Jacob to a curse, And Israel to reproach.</span>
