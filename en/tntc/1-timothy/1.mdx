## Paul refers to their last conversation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 John 1:9–10"> You recall that I urged you to remain in Ephesus, when I went into Macedonia, in order that you should command<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Evidently Paul deputized Timothy, giving him the authority to give commands, and the local elders would need to be informed about it.">d</a></sup>certain persons to stop teaching a different doctrine</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> and occupying themselves with myths and endless genealogies,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This sounds like Gnosticism.">e</a></sup>which cause disputes rather than the godly training which is by faith.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="‘Disputes’ are conducted on the basis of logic/reason, not faith, and God requires that we respond to Him in faith (see Hebrews 11:6).">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> Now the goal of that command is love, out of a pure heart and a good conscience and a sincere faith;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This is a beautiful picture, surely: a love that emanates from pure heart, good conscience and sincere faith will probably be quite similar to God's love.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> some having strayed from these have been turned aside<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The verb is in the passive voice; once they strayed they came under outside influence (see Ephesians 2:2).">h</a></sup>to empty talk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> desiring to be teachers of the law, not understanding either what they say or the things that they affirm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This is typical of people whose ambition exceeds their mental ability—they end up faking it.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; Psalms 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> Now we know that the law is good if one uses it correctly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> knowing that law is not made for a righteous person but for lawless and rebellious ones, for ungodly and sinners, for unholy and profane, for father-smiters and mother-smiters, for murderers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="I follow the best line of transmission, as well as a plurality of the Greek manuscripts, in rendering “father-smiter” rather than ‘father-killer’. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> for fornicators, for sodomites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The reference is specifically to those who play the role of the male in homosexual intercourse (anal sex), as distinct from catamites (1 Corinthians 6:9), who play the role of the female. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> as defined by the Gospel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Notice that law is supposed to be based on God's Word. On the contrary, it is protected and encouraged.">m</a></sup>of the glory<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The Gospel reflects God's glory, and may even be said to be about that glory, since it reflects His character.">n</a></sup>of the blessed God, with which I was entrusted.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="If you are entrusted with something you are supposed to take care of it and protect it. Paul demonstrates this attitude.">o</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The grace of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I thank Christ Jesus our Lord who enables me, because He considered me faithful, putting me into a ministry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> I, who was formerly a blasphemer and a persecutor and arrogant; but I was shown mercy, because being ignorant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Paul was highly instructed in the Jewish religion, but was ignorant where it really mattered.">p</a></sup>I did it in unbelief;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> the grace of our Lord overflowed, along with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="I take it that Paul is saying that he received an outpouring of faith and love, as well as grace. Well, to transform Saul of Tarsus into Paul the apostle to the nations no doubt required a little ‘extra’.">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; John 3:16–17"> Here is a trustworthy word, worthy of total acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am chief;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> precisely for this reason I was shown mercy, that in me, the ‘chief’, Jesus Christ might display all his forbearance, as an example for those who are going to rely on Him into eternal life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="If God could save Paul, He can save anybody—there is hope for any sinner; which calls for a doxology (next verse).">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Jude 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> Now to the King eternal, immortal, invisible, to God who alone is wise,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Most modern versions, following 2% of the Greek manuscripts (of objectively inferior quality), omit “wise”, leaving ‘the only God’. To be sure, the God of the Bible is the only true God, so the shortened form is theologically correct, but He is also the only One who is totally wise, and that information should not be omitted on such poor evidence.">s</a></sup>be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>

## Wage the good warfare

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> Son Timothy, I am giving you this charge in accordance with the prophecies about you that lead the way,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="“Lead the way” is in the present tense, and so does not refer to the past. The content of the “charge” is developed in chapter 2.">t</a></sup>that in them you may wage the good warfare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> holding on to faith and a good conscience<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="When a person starts faking it he rejects a good conscience.">u</a></sup>(which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="“Which” is singular and refers to the good conscience, presumably: if you are not honest with the Faith, you lose it.">v</a></sup>some having rejected have suffered shipwreck concerning the Faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander—whom I have handed over to Satan that they may be taught<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="The verb here is in the passive voice, which makes Satan the teacher!
