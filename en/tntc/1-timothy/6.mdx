## Slaves honor owners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Peter 2:17–20; Titus 2:8–10"> As many as are under the yoke of slavery should consider their own owners to be worthy of full respect, so that God's name and doctrine not be slandered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 4:1; 1 Timothy 4:11; Genesis 16:4–5"> Those who have owners who are believers must not disregard them because they are brothers; instead they must serve them even better, because those receiving the good service are believers and beloved.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Observe that there is no indication that a slave who converts to Christ should expect to be freed from that condition, even if the owner is a Christian.">a</a></sup>Conclusion to ‘Relationships’ Teach and exhort these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:3; Romans 16:17; 1 Timothy 1:10"> If anyone teaches differently and does not consent to sound words, the words of our Lord Jesus Christ, and to the doctrine that accords with godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 3:4; 1 Timothy 3:6"> he is conceited and understands nothing. Rather he is obsessed with controversies and hair-splitting word battles, from which come envy, strife, slanderings, evil suspicions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3"> constant wranglings of men with corrupted minds and destitute of the Truth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The clear implication is that these people have not been regenerated, and are being used by the enemy. There is no lack of such people in the churches.">b</a></sup>who suppose that godliness is a means to financial gain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The religious mercenaries are with us still.">c</a></sup>Keep away from such people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Less than 3% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit this last sentence (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Godliness with contentment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:8; Proverbs 16:8"> Now godliness with contentment is great gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 1:21; Psalms 49:17; Luke 12:20–21"> We brought nothing into the world and it is certain that neither can we take anything out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="But if you belong to Christ you can send it on ahead, by investing in God's Kingdom here.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Matthew 6:11; Matthew 6:25–33"> So having food and clothing we will be content with that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 3:7"> But those who want to get rich fall into temptation and a snare and into many foolish and harmful desires, that plunge people into ruin and loss;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 1:19; 1 Timothy 6:9; Psalms 32:10"> because the love of money is a root<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is right, a root, not ‘the’ root. Love of money is only one of the causes that lead men to do evil.">f</a></sup>of all kinds of evil; in their greediness some have been led away from the faith and have impaled themselves on many distresses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="As the Text says, “The way of the transgressor is hard” (Proverbs 13:15). Recall that the Sovereign said, “You cannot serve God and mammon” (Luke 16:13). Someone who switches from God to ‘mammon’ will probably never switch back.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Lay hold on eternal life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 3:17; 1 Timothy 4:12"> But you, O man of God, flee these things; rather pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, gentleness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25–26"> Fight the good fight of faith; take hold of the eternal life into which you were called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Flee”, “pursue”, “fight”, “take hold”—these are things that are up to us; Paul is emphasizing human responsibility here.">h</a></sup>—indeed, you confessed the good confession in the presence of many witnesses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Might this have been when he was baptized with water?">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 18:36–37; Matthew 27:11; 1 Timothy 5:21"> In the presence of the God who gives life to all things, and of Christ Jesus who testified the good confession before Pontius Pilate,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="So just what was that “good confession”? The Lord demonstrated absolutely no fear, and told Pilate plainly that he could only do what the Father allowed.">j</a></sup>I charge you</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> to keep this commandment without spot, blameless until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Evidently Paul considered that the “appearing” could happen during Timothy's lifetime.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 1:17"> which He will manifest at the proper time—He who is the blessed and only Sovereign, the King of kings and the Lord of lords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; John 1:18; 1 John 1:5"> who alone has immortality, residing in unapproachable light;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="But once we have been glorified I believe we will be able to approach and endure, even enjoy! Oh praise God!">l</a></sup>whom no man has seen or can see; to whom be honor and eternal power. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## To the rich

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; Acts 14:17; Proverbs 27:24"> Command<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Paul commands Timothy to issue a command to the rich (see James 5:1-3).">m</a></sup>those who are rich in this present world not to be haughty, nor to rely upon the uncertainty of riches but on the living<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Some 7% of the Greek manuscripts omit “the living” (as in NIV, NASB, TEV, etc.).">n</a></sup>God, who richly provides us with everything to enjoy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 John 3:17; Psalms 37:3"> they are to do good, to be rich in good works, to be generous sharers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Whenever God blesses us with material abundance, it is not for us to hoard or squander on ourselves—it is to be invested in the Kingdom (there are various ways of doing that).">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; 1 Timothy 6:12; 2 Timothy 2:19"> —storing up for themselves a good foundation against the time to come, so that they may lay hold on the eternal life.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A closing exhortation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 2 Timothy 2:16; 2 Timothy 1:12–14"> O Timothy, guard the deposit!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="What does Paul mean by “the deposit”? I suppose it would be the sum of revealed truth surrounding the person and work of Jesus Christ—a synonym of ‘the Faith’.">p</a></sup>Turn away from the godless, empty chatter and inconsistencies of what is falsely called ‘science’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:18; 1 Timothy 1:6; 2 Timothy 4:22"> —by professing it some have erred concerning the Faith.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="For many generations the theory of evolution has been foisted on the populace at large as ‘science’. It dominates the schools, the media, the marketplace, the government. The spiritual damage has been incalculable—multiplied millions will spend eternity in the Lake because of it.">q</a></sup>The Grace be with you. Amen.</span>
</div>
