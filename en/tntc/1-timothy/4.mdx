## Things taught by demons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:1–9; 2 Peter 2:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Now the Spirit says explicitly that in later times some will fall away from the faith, paying attention to deceiving spirits and to things taught by demons</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> —through hypocritical liars whose own consciences have been cauterized,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Be not deceived, the churches (with exceptions, of course) are filled with a variety of ‘doctrines’ of demonic origin. The enemy uses a certain type of person to ‘sell’ them.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> who forbid to marry and to eat foods that God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and really know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> For every creature of God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Luke 4:4"> because it is consecrated through the Word of God and prayer.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="I gain the impression that the dietary regulations in the Mosaic Law have been superseded. I may enjoy a pork chop if I first consecrate it. (The trouble is that many of us forget about the consecrating.)">b</a></sup></span>
</div>

## A good servant of Jesus Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> If you instruct the brothers in these things you will be a good servant of Jesus Christ, brought up in the words of the faith and of the good doctrine that you have faithfully followed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="To get to be a “good servant of Jesus Christ” is a process.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But reject godless myths and old-wives' tales; rather, exercise yourself toward godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; Psalms 37:3–4"> Now physical exercise has some value,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Since a sick body definitely slows us down, we should give some attention to keeping physically fit.">d</a></sup>but godliness has value in all things, holding promises<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="I follow 60% of the Greek manuscripts, including the best line of transmission, in reading the plural, “promises”—there are promises for down here and others for up there.">e</a></sup>for both this present life and the one to come.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A trustworthy word

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> Here is a trustworthy word, worthy of total acceptance</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> (which is also why we labor and suffer reproach):<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Instead of “suffer reproach”, perhaps 7% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘strive’ (as in NIV, NASB, TEV, etc.).">f</a></sup>We have set our hope on the living God, who is ‘Savior of all men’,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This involves an exclusivist claim, which continues to give rise to opposition. If our God is “Savior of all men” then there cannot be any other ‘saviors’, at least not legitimate ones. Jesus Himself said, “I am the way, the truth and the life; no one comes to the Father except through me” (John 14:6). For preaching that exclusivist claim Paul suffered reproach, and anyone who follows his example today will also suffer.">g</a></sup>especially of those who believe.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“Savior of all men” is a potentiality; for the salvation to become effective, one must believe.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Watch your life and doctrine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> Command and teach these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Let no one look down on your youthfulness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="As a professor of mine once said, “Your youth is not the problem; it's the immaturity that goes with it”.">i</a></sup>but be an example to the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some 9% of the Greek manuscripts omit “in spirit” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">j</a></sup>in faith, in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Until I come give attention to public reading &#91;of Scripture&#93;, to exhortation, to teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Do not neglect the gift that is in you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The obvious implication is that a gift can be neglected.">k</a></sup>which was ‘given’ to you through prophecy with the laying on of the hands of the body of elders.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="According to 1 Corinthians 12:11 the Holy Spirit distributes gifts as He chooses, but it is to ‘each one’. The impression I gain is that many (most?) Christians are not aware that they have a gift (or perhaps their church will not allow them to use it). But a prophetic declaration, backed by the authority of the body of elders, should remove all doubt. Note that “elders” is plural.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; Psalms 143:5"> Cultivate these things; give yourself wholly to them, so that your progress may be evident to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 John 1:8–9"> Take pains with yourself and the doctrine; persevere in those things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Paul refers back to the “these things” at the beginning of verse 15.">m</a></sup>because by doing so you will save both yourself and those who hear you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Paul here emphasizes human responsibility. If Timothy goes astray, those who trust him in spiritual matters will also go astray; if he remains firm those who follow him will too. This reminds me of James 3:1. “My brethren, let not many of you become teachers, knowing that we shall receive a stricter judgment.”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
