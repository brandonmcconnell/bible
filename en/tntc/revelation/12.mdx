## Satan excluded from heaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> War was declared in heaven; Michael and his angels were to wage war with the dragon; so the dragon and his angels made war,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The dragon knew that Michael had received the order, so he decided to get in the first blow.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> but he was not strong enough; neither was there any place found for him in heaven any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> So the great dragon was expelled, that ancient serpent, who is called Slanderer and Satan, who deceives the whole inhabited world; he was thrown into the earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Into the earth”, including its atmosphere. The idea seems to be that at this point Satan is confined to this planet. From the content of the following verses I take it that Satan's expulsion occurs in the middle of the seven-year period. So he is still in heaven accusing us (accusing us of what? There would be no point in bringing false accusations, so we must be providing Satan with ‘ammunition’—not a nice thought!).">g</a></sup>and his angels were expelled with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> And I heard a loud voice in the heaven saying: “Now the salvation and the power have come,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Wait a minute! <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> And they conquered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is what the Text says. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; Psalms 96:11–13"> Therefore rejoice, O heavens, yes, you who are dwelling in them!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="It is the inhabitants that do the rejoicing, not the place.">k</a></sup>Woe to the earth and the sea! ## Footnotes

<ol className="fnl">
<li id="fn-a"><a href="#fnref-a" className="fnb">a</a> <span className="fnr">12:1</span> Just as Michael is the boss-angel in charge of Israel, as a nation (Dan. 10:21, 12:1), presumably each tribe has its own boss-angel (lower in rank than Michael). Assuming that the woman represents Israel, I imagine that these ‘stars’ are the tribal angels (because of the number twelve).</li>
<li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">12:4</span> It is generally understood that this refers to those angels who joined Lucifer in his rebellion—note that verse 7 refers to the dragon and his angels. If the ⅔ are over 100 million (5:11) then this ⅓ must be over 50 million—more than enough trouble to go around.</li>
<li id="fn-c"><a href="#fnref-c" className="fnb">c</a> <span className="fnr">12:5</span> That is what the Text says. Since “Son” is obviously masculine, adding “a male” emphasizes the gender. What the first man lost the second Man (1 Corinthians 15:47) recovered; the first man allowed his wife to lead, the second Man will rule with a rod of iron.</li>
<li id="fn-d"><a href="#fnref-d" className="fnb">d</a> <span className="fnr">12:5</span> Here the Child is clearly identified as the Messiah—since He was born some 2,000 years ago we are obviously looking at an historical parenthesis. The rebellion of Lucifer and his angels goes back at least 6,000 years. We do not normally associate ‘shepherd’ with ‘a rod of iron’—the Messianic Millennial Kingdom will be a benevolent dictatorship.</li>
<li id="fn-e"><a href="#fnref-e" className="fnb">e</a> <span className="fnr">12:6</span> Verses 1-5 are historical, but verse 6 brings us back to the Great Tribulation. The narrative is interrupted to give us some background.</li>
<li id="fn-f"><a href="#fnref-f" className="fnb">f</a> <span className="fnr">12:7</span> The dragon knew that Michael had received the order, so he decided to get in the first blow.</li>
<li id="fn-g"><a href="#fnref-g" className="fnb">g</a> <span className="fnr">12:9</span> “Into the earth”, including its atmosphere. The idea seems to be that at this point Satan is confined to this planet. From the content of the following verses I take it that Satan's expulsion occurs in the middle of the seven-year period. So he is still in heaven accusing us (accusing us of what? There would be no point in bringing false accusations, so we must be providing Satan with ‘ammunition’—not a nice thought!).</li>
<li id="fn-h"><a href="#fnref-h" className="fnb">h</a> <span className="fnr">12:10</span> Wait a minute! If it is only “Now”, where have the Kingdom and Authority been in the meantime? As long as a government is being challenged, its rule is not complete or tranquil. I find it instructive that Satan still has sufficient power to wage war, in Heaven!</li>
<li id="fn-i"><a href="#fnref-i" className="fnb">i</a> <span className="fnr">12:10</span> “Our brothers”—I wonder who is speaking, since the ‘brothers’ are saved by the blood of the lamb.</li>
<li id="fn-j"><a href="#fnref-j" className="fnb">j</a> <span className="fnr">12:11</span> That is what the Text says. Comparing 6:11 and 7:13, perhaps it is necessary for a certain number of God's servants to be willing to die for the Cause to bring about the accuser's expulsion.</li>
<li id="fn-k"><a href="#fnref-k" className="fnb">k</a> <span className="fnr">12:12</span> It is the inhabitants that do the rejoicing, not the place.</li>
<li id="fn-l"><a href="#fnref-l" className="fnb">l</a> <span className="fnr">12:17</span> Only two very late Greek manuscripts add “Christ”, as in KJV and NKJV. Over 99% do not. The dragon will persecute Jews in general and Christians in particular.</li>
</ol>
