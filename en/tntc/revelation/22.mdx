<ol className="fnl">
<li id="fn-a"><a href="#fnref-a" className="fnb">a</a> <span className="fnr">22:1</span> This river should not be confused with the one in Ezekiel 47. <li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">22:6</span> Two of the three main independent lines of transmission (including the best one) have “of the spirits”. The familiar ‘the holy’ of the KJV is based on the third main line.</li>
<li id="fn-c"><a href="#fnref-c" className="fnb">c</a> <span className="fnr">22:6</span> This is a restatement of 1:1.</li>
<li id="fn-d"><a href="#fnref-d" className="fnb">d</a> <span className="fnr">22:7</span> Here Jesus Christ speaks (and so in verses 12, 16 and 18); see 1:3.</li>
<li id="fn-e"><a href="#fnref-e" className="fnb">e</a> <span className="fnr">22:9</span> I wonder if John was not always sure who was a real angel and who was the Son, appearing as an angel, and so just to make sure…</li>
<li id="fn-f"><a href="#fnref-f" className="fnb">f</a> <span className="fnr">22:15</span> So who are the ‘dogs’? In Deuteronomy 23:18 ‘dog’ apparently refers to a male prostitute, a catamite, and is declared to be an abomination. In OT times dogs were scavengers and therefore unclean, and were generally looked down upon. In Jesus' day Jews referred to Gentiles as ‘dogs’, but since many Gentiles are in the Church that is presumably not the intended meaning here. The ‘dogs’ in Matthew 7:6 react in an aggressively hostile manner against what is holy; I take it that they are people who are overtly serving evil (Philippians 3:2 may be referring to this sort of person). So who are the ‘dogs’ here? At the very least they are the unclean. Take it from there.</li>
<li id="fn-g"><a href="#fnref-g" className="fnb">g</a> <span className="fnr">22:15</span> Relativism is an idol; Humanism is an idol; Materialism is an idol. One wonders how many Christians today have a worldview that is really Biblical. The world's values are not compatible with Christ's values. Don't forget 1 John 2:15-16!</li>
<li id="fn-h"><a href="#fnref-h" className="fnb">h</a> <span className="fnr">22:18</span> Since God the Son is speaking, I imagine that what He wants will be done!</li>
<li id="fn-i"><a href="#fnref-i" className="fnb">i</a> <span className="fnr">22:19</span> “Words”, plural, includes the individual words that make up the whole. Those textual critics who have wantonly removed words from the Text, on the basis of satanically inspired presuppositions, are out. Those who interpret the Text in such a way as to avoid its plain meaning, likewise. Jehovah the Son affirms that the words are “true and faithful”, and He expects us to interpret them that way.</li>
<li id="fn-j"><a href="#fnref-j" className="fnb">j</a> <span className="fnr">22:21</span> “The Lord Jesus Christ” is now the full name or title of Jehovah the Son.</li>
