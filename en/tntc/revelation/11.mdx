## The two witnesses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:15; Ezekiel 42:15–20; Zechariah 2:1–2"> I was given a reed like a measuring rod. And the angel stood<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="I get the impression that the angel shifted position, probably with both feet on land—his legs had been spread or straddled, now he stands normally.">a</a></sup>saying: “Rise and measure the temple of God and the altar, and those who worship there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Daniel 7:25; Luke 21:24"> But leave out the outer court of the temple and do not measure it, because it has been given to the nations; and they will trample the holy city for forty-two months.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Comparing forty-two months with 1,260 days we get 30-day months. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> And I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Presumably the angel is still speaking—might He be God the Son? <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> These are the two olive trees, even the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Zechariah 4:3,14.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> And if anyone wants to harm them fire comes out of their mouths and consumes their enemies. So if anyone wants to harm them he must be killed in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Psalms 105:26–36"> They have authority to shut up the sky so that no rain falls during the days of their prophecy; and they have authority over the waters to turn them into blood, and to strike the earth with every plague, as often as they wish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="They have the authority to do these things, but it is discretionary; it is up to them to decide when, where, and how much.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> When they finish their witness, the Beast of prey that comes up out of the Abyss<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This would seem to confirm that the “locusts” and their king, in chapter nine, are on Satan's side. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> —and leave their corpses in the street of the great city! (which is called Sodom and Egypt, spiritually speaking), even where their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Over 99% of the Greek manuscripts have “their” Lord, not ‘our’ as in KJV and NKJV. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> And those from the peoples, tribes, languages and ethnic nations look at their corpses three and a half days, and will not allow their corpses to be buried.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="People in past generations had trouble imagining how this could happen, but with modern technology people around the world will watch those corpses.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> And those who dwell on the earth rejoice over them, and they will enjoy themselves and send gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented those who dwell on the earth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="They will celebrate, but not for long!">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> And after three and a half days a breath of life from God entered them and they stood on their feet, and a great fear fell on those who were watching them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> And I heard a loud voice from the heaven saying to them, “Come up here!” And they went up to heaven in a cloud, and their enemies watched them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> And in that day there was a severe earthquake and a tenth of the city fell, and seven thousand individuals were killed in the earthquake. And the rest became fearful and gave glory to the God of heaven.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="At least they are no longer pretending that God does not exist.">l</a></sup>)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second woe is past. Look out, here comes the third woe!</span>

## The seventh trumpet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Revelation 12:10; Daniel 7:27"> So the seventh angel trumpeted, and there were loud voices in heaven saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The kingdom of the world has become</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the kingdom of our Lord and of His Christ,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and He shall reign forever and ever!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> And the twenty-four elders, who sit on their thrones in God's presence, fell on their faces and worshiped God</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We thank You, O Lord God Almighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He who is and who was and who is coming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because You have taken up your great power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and begun to reign.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 19:5; Revelation 20:12; Revelation 6:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nations were angry and your wrath came,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even the time for the dead to be judged</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to give the reward to Your slaves the prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to the saints</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="“Slaves” and “saints”—again we seem to have two distinct categories.">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those who fear your name,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">small and great,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to destroy those who have corrupted the earth.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="One gets the impression that the elders had been waiting for this day. And there were lightnings, noises, thunderings and huge hail.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="If this were all there was to the seventh trumpet it would be anti climactic, ‘small potatoes’. ## Footnotes

<ol className="fnl">
<li id="fn-a"><a href="#fnref-a" className="fnb">a</a> <span className="fnr">11:1</span> I get the impression that the angel shifted position, probably with both feet on land—his legs had been spread or straddled, now he stands normally.</li>
<li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">11:2</span> Comparing forty-two months with 1,260 days we get 30-day months. A year computed on the basis of these ‘prophetic’ months, as they are sometimes called, is five days shorter than a calendar year. Forty-two calendar months would give 1,277/8 days.</li>
<li id="fn-c"><a href="#fnref-c" className="fnb">c</a> <span className="fnr">11:3</span> Presumably the angel is still speaking—might He be God the Son? Could a mere angel say “my two witnesses”?</li>
<li id="fn-d"><a href="#fnref-d" className="fnb">d</a> <span className="fnr">11:3</span> Note that their ‘ministry’ covers 3½ years; the Text does not actually say that it will be simultaneous with the trampling of the holy city, but I imagine that it will come close. When I see the perversity in the world I am sometimes tempted to wish I could do what they are going to do, until I get to the ‘sackcloth’—walking around in sackcloth for 3½ years does not sound like much fun.</li>
<li id="fn-e"><a href="#fnref-e" className="fnb">e</a> <span className="fnr">11:4</span> See Zechariah 4:3,14.</li>
<li id="fn-f"><a href="#fnref-f" className="fnb">f</a> <span className="fnr">11:6</span> They have the authority to do these things, but it is discretionary; it is up to them to decide when, where, and how much.</li>
<li id="fn-g"><a href="#fnref-g" className="fnb">g</a> <span className="fnr">11:7</span> This would seem to confirm that the “locusts” and their king, in chapter nine, are on Satan's side. That is also where Satan will be confined during the Millennium (20:3). If this beast is not Abbaddon he is presumably another high ranking demon, or so I thought until I got to 13:1 and 17:8, which see.</li>
<li id="fn-h"><a href="#fnref-h" className="fnb">h</a> <span className="fnr">11:8</span> Over 99% of the Greek manuscripts have “their” Lord, not ‘our’ as in KJV and NKJV. If these two “olive trees” are the ones in Zechariah 4:3 and 14, then the “Lord of the whole earth” there is Jehovah the Son.</li>
<li id="fn-i"><a href="#fnref-i" className="fnb">i</a> <span className="fnr">11:8</span> We might expect “Sodom and Egypt” to apply to Rome, rather than Jerusalem, but the Lord was crucified in Jerusalem.</li>
<li id="fn-j"><a href="#fnref-j" className="fnb">j</a> <span className="fnr">11:9</span> People in past generations had trouble imagining how this could happen, but with modern technology people around the world will watch those corpses.</li>
<li id="fn-k"><a href="#fnref-k" className="fnb">k</a> <span className="fnr">11:10</span> They will celebrate, but not for long!</li>
<li id="fn-l"><a href="#fnref-l" className="fnb">l</a> <span className="fnr">11:13</span> At least they are no longer pretending that God does not exist.</li>
<li id="fn-m"><a href="#fnref-m" className="fnb">m</a> <span className="fnr">11:18</span> “Slaves” and “saints”—again we seem to have two distinct categories.</li>
<li id="fn-n"><a href="#fnref-n" className="fnb">n</a> <span className="fnr">11:18</span> One gets the impression that the elders had been waiting for this day. I think I know how they feel—the destruction of “those who have corrupted the earth” cannot come too soon to suit me.</li>
<li id="fn-o"><a href="#fnref-o" className="fnb">o</a> <span className="fnr">11:19</span> Presumably this is the original ark of which the one made under Moses' supervision was a copy (Hebrews 8:5, Exodus 25:9, 40, Hebrews 9:23).</li>
<li id="fn-p"><a href="#fnref-p" className="fnb">p</a> <span className="fnr">11:19</span> If this were all there was to the seventh trumpet it would be anti climactic, ‘small potatoes’. I take it that the seven ‘bowls’ (see chapter 16) give the full content of the third woe.</li>
</ol>
