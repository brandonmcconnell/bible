## A request for prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly and be glorified, as also with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Romans 15:31; Deuteronomy 32:20"> and that we may be delivered from evil and malignant men; for not all have faith.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Since everyone believes something, has a worldview, the reference is presumably to faith in God.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 10:13"> But the Lord is faithful, who will strengthen you and guard you from the malignant one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="‘The’ malignant one is Satan.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Philemon 1:21; 1 Thessalonians 4:10"> We have confidence in the Lord concerning you, that you both do and will do the things we command.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Observe that Paul considers that he is in a position to command.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:36; 1 Chronicles 29:18; Deuteronomy 30:6"> Now may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God and into the perseverance of Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="As Paul makes clear in the opening paragraph, they are already manifesting love and perseverance, so I take the point of the prayer to be progress into God's kind of love and Christ's kind of perseverance. They have made a good start, they are on the right road; Paul asks God to help them on toward the goal.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Don't be irresponsible or lazy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 3:7; Romans 16:17"> Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you withdraw from every brother who behaves irresponsibly and not according to the tradition that they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="There were several of them.">e</a></sup>received from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we did not behave irresponsibly among you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; Ephesians 4:28"> neither<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The distinction implied here indicates that the irresponsible behavior covered a wider area than just idleness or freeloading.">f</a></sup>did we eat anyone's bread without paying; rather we worked night and day, with difficulty and hardship, so as not to be a burden to any of you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 2:6; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> not because we do not have the right, but in order to offer ourselves to you as a model for you to imitate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> Yes, because when we were with you we gave you this command: “If anyone does not want to work,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Does not want to work”—the reference is not to those who want to work but cannot find a job; it is to the lazy.">g</a></sup>neither let him eat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:11"> Yet we hear that some among you are behaving irresponsibly; not working, just meddling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:8"> Now to such individuals we command and exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ that they work in quietness and eat their own bread.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Isolate the disobedient

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9–10; Hebrews 12:3; 2 Corinthians 4:1"> But as for you, brothers, do not lose heart in well doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> If anyone does not obey our word in this letter, take note of him and do not associate with him, that he may be shamed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> yet do not regard him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The discipline is to be corrective, not punitive. Note that Paul clearly claims authority; he expects to be obeyed.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 29:11; John 16:33; Matthew 1:23"> Now may the Lord of peace Himself give you the peace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="“The peace”: there is no lack of counterfeit ‘peace’, but the reference here is presumably to ‘the peace of God, that surpasses all understanding’ (Philippians 4:7)—and only “the Lord of peace” can give it.">i</a></sup>during every situation, in every way.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Since they were being actively persecuted, this was a very appropriate blessing.">j</a></sup>The Lord be with you all.</span>
</div>
