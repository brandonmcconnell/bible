## Encouragement by example

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Keep thinking of Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, of David's seed; this is my gospel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This brief statement evokes much of the essence of the Gospel. “David's seed” recalls that the Creator really became human, with David's genes, and fulfills the prophecies about the Messiah. “Raised from the dead” recalls everything connected with His death and resurrection. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> for which I am suffering to the point of being in chains like a criminal, but the Word of God is not chained.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> This is a trustworthy word:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">If, indeed, we died together,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="We must really identify with Christ's death, which involves commitment and a change of life, a change of worldview.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will also live together;</span></span>

## Approved and disapproved

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Make every effort to render yourself approved by God, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, handling the Word of the Truth correctly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But shun godless, empty chatter, because those who do it will become increasingly godless,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Comparing this with what the Lord Jesus said in Matthew 12:34-37, those who speak godlessly do so because their heart is godless; and the more they do it the worse they get, because they continually reinforce their godlessness.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and their word will spread like gangrene—among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> who have wandered away from the Truth, saying that the resurrection has already occurred; and they overturn the faith of some.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Surely; if the resurrection has already happened and you are still here, what hope have you?">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> Nevertheless, the solid foundation of God stands firm, having this seal: “The Lord knows those who are His,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="See Numbers 16:5.">p</a></sup>and let everyone who claims the name of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Instead of “the Lord”, perhaps 10% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘Christ’ (as in AV and NKJV).">q</a></sup>keep away from unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> Now in a large house there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay; some, to be sure, are for honor, but some are for dishonor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> So if anyone cleanses himself from the latter, he will be a vessel for honor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The analogy here is not clear to me, but I take the point to be that the believer should avoid contamination from those who are dishonorable.">r</a></sup>sanctified and useful to the Master, prepared for every good work.</span>
</div>

## The true source of opposition

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> Flee the lusts of youth;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Do not play with it, to see if you are strong enough to resist—just run!">s</a></sup>but pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace, along with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="This requires fellowship with others of like mind.">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> Refuse foolish and ignorant arguments, knowing that they breed quarrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> Now a slave of the Lord should not quarrel but be gentle towards all, skillful at teaching, forbearing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="I take the basic meaning of this term to be to handle opposition with maturity.">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> correcting courteously those who oppose him, in hopes that God may grant them repentance leading to a real knowledge of Truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalms 124:7"> and that they may retrieve their senses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Without our help they almost certainly will not.">v</a></sup>out of the devil's snare (having been taken captive by him to do his will).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Verses 23-26 provide a perspective that is not generally understood. At least some (most?) [all?] of the time that people oppose Biblical values they do so because of satanic influence in their minds. Since Satan is the ‘father of lies’ (John 8:44), he is behind any and every lie. So whenever anyone embraces a lie (materialism, humanism, relativism, spiritism, animism, whatever) they invite Satan into their minds. Once there he goes about setting up strongholds. As Ephesians 2:2 says, he is actively at work in the ‘sons of the disobedience’—when you embrace a lie you reject the Truth; but then you are taken captive by the lie and wind up doing Satan's will. This process goes area by area, which explains why people can be good in one area and bad in another (the reference is not to physical skills). So when someone opposes God's truth and His values, we need to understand and remember how the person got that way; actually, they are being used. To lash out at them will not help them. A gentle, courteous probing of their presuppositions just might. More to the point, we have access to spiritual authority whereby we can break Satan's strongholds, but that gets us into the area of biblical spiritual warfare (extremely important, but too extensive to expound here). At one time I did a lot of study and work in this area and have some material that is available from my site: www.prunch.org.">w</a></sup></span>
</div>
