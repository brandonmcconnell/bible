## Proclaim the Word!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Timothy 5:21"> Now then, in the presence of God and the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Perhaps 5% of the Greek manuscripts omit “the Lord” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">a</a></sup>Jesus Christ, who is about to judge both living and dead, with a view to His appearing and His Kingdom, I give you this charge:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The charge that Paul gives Timothy is “with a view to His appearing and His Kingdom”.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Timothy 4:15–16; Titus 2:15"> Proclaim the Word; be ready in season and out of season; correct, rebuke and exhort, with lots of patience and teaching.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="If Timothy obeys this charge, he will at least slow down the process described in verses 3-4. And if we also obey this charge we should produce at least some improvement in our own context.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 3:1–6; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> Because the time will come when they will not tolerate the sound doctrine; rather, they will accumulate for themselves a great many teachers who will tickle their ears according to their lusts</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; 1 Timothy 1:4; Acts 7:57"> —yes, they will turn their ears away from the Truth and be turned aside to fables.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Notice the progression: first they choose to turn away from the Truth, but after that someone else takes over and leads them into ever greater stupidities.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 1:13; 2 Timothy 2:3"> But you, be sober in all things, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In view of his own impending death Paul urges Timothy to get, and stay, serious.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Timothy will no longer have Paul to help him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; Philippians 2:17; 2 Peter 1:14–15"> For I am already being poured out like a drink offering, and the time of my departure has come.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Evidently God told Paul he was about to die.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Hebrews 12:1–2"> I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4"> Now the crown of the righteousness is reserved for me, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that Day; and not only to me, but also to all those who have longed for His appearing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="If we are afraid of what might happen, we will not “long” for that “appearing”. The implication is that they also are fighting the good fight and keeping the faith.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## “Please come quickly”

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 2 Timothy 4:21"> Do your best to come to me quickly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 2 Timothy 1:15; Colossians 4:14–15"> for Demas, loving this present world, has deserted me and gone to Thessalonica—Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="I take it that what is said of Demas does not apply to Crescens or Titus, just that they traveled.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 12:25"> Only Luke is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for ministry.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Comparing this statement with Acts 15:37-39, it appears that Barnabas was right in investing in Mark, and Paul here recognizes his success. It is possible that Paul wanted Mark to wait on him personally.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12"> Tychicus I sent to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:11; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 16:11"> Bring the cloak that I left with Carpus at Troas, when you come—and the books, especially the parchments.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The parchments may have been a subset among the books, possibly being OT Scriptures; in that event the other books might have been NT Scriptures, but we can only speculate.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith did me a lot of harm; may the Lord repay him according to his deeds.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="He is not asking God to forgive the man.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Philippians 3:2; Matthew 10:16–17"> You also guard against him, because he strongly opposed our words.</span>
</div>
</div>

## People will disappoint you

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:60; John 16:32; Mark 14:50"> At my first defense no one came to help me, but all deserted me. May it not be held against them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Note the difference in attitude toward these people and toward the coppersmith in verse 14.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Philippians 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, so that through me the message might be fully proclaimed and all the nations might hear.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="I suppose that Paul wrote some of his letters after this first trial, and thus “the nations” are still ‘hearing’.">m</a></sup>Also, I was delivered out of a lion's mouth;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="I suppose this is literal; someone tried to have him condemned to face a lion in the arena, but the plan was foiled.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; Jude 1:24–25"> and the Lord will deliver me from every evil work and save me into His heavenly Kingdom;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="To get into the “heavenly Kingdom” is the goal.">o</a></sup>to whom be the glory for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Farewell

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–4"> Greet Prisca<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="It is generally considered that ‘Prisca’ is an alternate spelling, or shortened form, for ‘Priscilla’.">p</a></sup>and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 19:22; Philippians 2:26–27"> Erastus stayed in Corinth, while I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Evidently Paul was not able to heal him.">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 4:22; 3 John 1:14"> Do your best to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia and all the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philemon 1:25; Galatians 6:18; Colossians 4:18"> The Lord Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Perhaps 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit “Jesus Christ” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">r</a></sup>be with your spirit. The Grace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="‘Grace’ occurs with the definite article = ‘the grace’; so I capitalized it.">s</a></sup>be with you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="“Your spirit” is singular, referring to Timothy, but the “you” here is plural—I take it that Paul knew the letter would be read by others.">t</a></sup>Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
