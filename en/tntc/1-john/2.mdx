## Jesus Christ is our Advocate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Romans 8:34; John 14:6"> My dear children, I am writing these things to you so that you not sin; if someone does sin, we have an Advocate in the Father's presence, righteous Jesus Christ—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:14; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 4:10"> also, He Himself is the propitiation for our sins;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Now there is a good deal! Having Himself paid my debt, when the Son pleads my case before the Father, I will be cleared. Thank you, Lord! The term ‘propitiation’ is not so well known these days, but it has a specific theological content that needs to be preserved. It refers to an atoning sacrifice that is required to satisfy God's outraged character, outraged by our sin.">a</a></sup>not concerning ours only but also concerning those of the whole world.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“The whole world” cannot be limited to the elect, if language has verifiable meaning. Just because many do not avail themselves of the propitiation (most have never heard about it) does not mean it is not available.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>

## We must keep His commandments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; Luke 6:46"> Now by this we know that we have come to know Him, if we keep His commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Titus 1:16; James 2:14–16"> He who says, “I have come to know Him,” yet does not keep His commandments, is a liar and the Truth is not in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A statement both strong and uncomfortable, yet true. Deliberate sin indicates an inadequate knowledge of God.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; 1 John 4:12–13"> But whoever keeps His Word, God's love has truly been perfected in him: by this we know that we are in Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21; John 13:15; 1 Corinthians 11:1"> He who claims to be abiding in Him really needs to act just like He acted.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="I imagine John was remembering the upper room—John 14:12.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## An old/new commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Leviticus 19:18; Mark 12:29–34"> Brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some 15% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘beloved’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">e</a></sup>I am not writing a new commandment to you but an old one that you have had from the beginning; this old commandment is the word that you have heard from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; John 13:34"> Again, I am writing a new commandment to you, which thing is true in Him and in you, because the darkness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="I take “the darkness” to be a reference to Satan's kingdom, while “the true Light is already shining” would refer to the outworking of Christ's victory, which in a certain sense turned all old commandments into new ones—the indwelling Holy Spirit enables us to do what we could not, before. Darkness is merely the absence of light; to introduce light in any situation reduces the darkness, which is why the world hates us.">f</a></sup>is passing away and the true Light is already shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:11; 1 John 3:13–17"> He who claims to be in the Light while hating his brother is in the darkness until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 3:14; Romans 9:32–33; Romans 14:13"> He who keeps on loving his brother is staying in the Light, and there is no occasion for stumbling in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:35; Titus 3:3; 1 John 1:6"> But he who keeps on hating his brother is in the darkness and keeps walking in the darkness; he does not know where he is going because the darkness has blinded his eyes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A bit of poetry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Acts 13:38; Ephesians 1:7"> Dear children, I am writing to you because your sins have been forgiven for His name's sake<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Verse 12 is an introductory cover statement; in verse 13 John divides the cover group into three subgroups; in verse 14 he returns to the first two categories [I confess that I do not understand the purpose of verse 14, unless it be that John wanted to enlarge on what he says to the young men but did not want to pass over the fathers].">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:7; 1 John 2:14; Luke 10:22"> —fathers, I am writing to you because you have come to know Him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="An increasingly adequate knowledge of God has everything to do with spiritual maturity.">h</a></sup>who is from the beginning; young men, I am writing to you because you have overcome the evil one;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Spiritual warfare is not for ‘children’.">i</a></sup>children, I am writing to you because you have come to know the Father—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; 1 John 2:13; Colossians 3:16"> fathers, I have written to you because you have come to know Him who is from the beginning; young men, I have written to you because you are strong, and the Word of God remains in you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="For the Word to abide in you, you have to abide in it (John 8:31).">j</a></sup>and you have overcome the evil one.</span>
</div>

## We must not love the world

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 4:4; Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:24"> Do not love the world, neither the things in the world;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The crucial issue is the values that the surrounding culture seeks to impose on us—materialism, relativism, humanism; these values are directly opposed to the Father's values, and therefore to His love.">k</a></sup>if anyone loves the world, the Father's love is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 13:14; Galatians 5:17; Psalms 119:36–37"> Because everything in the world—the craving of the flesh and the craving of the eyes and the pretension of life—is not from the Father but is from the world.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The world system is controlled by Satan (5:19 below), so that system with its values is against the Creator. As the Sovereign said in Matthew 6:24, it is impossible to serve two masters—the more so if one is God and the other is Satan!">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 7:31"> The world, with its craving, is passing away, but he who does God's will remains forever.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Note that we have to do the will. “Whoever does the will of God, the same is my brother, my sister, my mother” (Mark 3:25). The Sovereign also said that it is a son who abides in the house forever (John 8:35).">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## A bunch of little antichrists

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7; Matthew 24:24"> Children, it is the last hour, and just as you have heard that the Antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have appeared, by which we know that it is the last hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="One gets the impression that the early Christians expected the Lord's return within their lifetime. The Creator is not bound by our notions of time.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 20:30; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Luke 8:13"> They went out from us but were not of us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Those who have left the Christian community are often nastier than those who have never been a part of it. Notice that John calls them “antichrists”! There have always been defectors from the Faith, and they have always been bad news.">o</a></sup>for if they had been of us, they would have continued with us—they left so that they might be exposed, that none of them were of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:27; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22"> You have an Anointing from the Holy One and understand all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Presumably the “Anointing” refers to the Holy Spirit; to receive understanding we need to listen to Him.">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Jude 1:5; Proverbs 1:5"> I have not written to you because you do not know the truth, but because you do know it, and because no lie is of the Truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The Spirit of the Truth cannot lie (John 14:17, 15:26, 16:13; Titus 1:2), just as Satan cannot tell the truth (John 8:44) [if he ever does it is distorted so as to deceive].">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## We must abide in the truth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 John 1:7; 1 John 4:3; 1 John 2:18"> Who is the liar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The Text has the definite article—behind every liar is the father of lies. Those who claim that Jesus was only a good man, a great teacher, are on Satan's side.">r</a></sup>but the one denying that Jesus is the Christ? This is the Antichrist, the one denying the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 4:15; John 8:19; John 5:23"> Whoever denies the Son does not have the Father either.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Some 20% of the Greek manuscripts add, “whoever confesses the Son has the Father also”, as in most versions. I take it that John is making the point that to deny either the Father or the Son is to deny them both—the addition is unnecessary. The 80%, including the best line of transmission, are correct. Note that John rejects Unitarianism (Sabellianism, modalism). Also, this verse is directly relevant to how one should present the Gospel to Muslims.">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 14:23; John 15:9–10; 1 John 4:13"> So you, let what you have heard from the beginning abide in you; if what you have heard from the beginning abides in you, you also will abide in the Father and in the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:28; Titus 3:7; Jude 1:21"> This is the promise that He Himself promised us—the eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 John 1:7; Acts 20:29–30"> I have written these things to you concerning those who are trying to deceive you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:20–21; 1 Corinthians 2:13"> but really, you do not need anyone to teach you, because the Anointing that you received from Him abides in you; further, as the same Anointing teaches you concerning all things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Compare John 16:13.">t</a></sup>and it is true and no lie—well just as He has taught you, you will abide in Him.</span>
</div>
</div>
