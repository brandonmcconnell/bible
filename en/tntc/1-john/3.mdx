<div className="bp">

## “Whoever abides in Him does not sin”

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 5:17; Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15"> Everyone who commits sin commits lawlessness as well; yes, sin is lawlessness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lawlessness is rebellion against a known standard. It follows that John is not referring to the ‘sins of ignorance’ in the OT">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> And you know that He was revealed in order to remove our sins—in Him there is no sin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="An important statement—that is why He can be our Savior.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> Whoever is abiding in Him does not sin;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In order to sin one must stop abiding.">h</a></sup>indeed, whoever is sinning has neither seen Him nor known Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="An uncomfortable statement; again, deliberate sin indicates an inadequate knowledge of God.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> Dear children, let no one mislead you; he who keeps doing righteousness is righteous, just as He is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> He who keeps doing sin is of the devil,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="God demands holiness. Belonging to God involves living a holy life (see verse 10 below).">j</a></sup>because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The Son of God was manifested for this purpose: to undo<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Many versions render ‘destroy’, but I prefer ‘undo’—to undo a work involves undoing its consequences as well. In John 20:21 Sovereign Jesus said, “As the Father has sent me, I also send you”. So we are here to undo Satan's works—there is certainly no lack of opportunity or challenge! However, Satan does not take kindly to any such attempt, so you need to know who you are in Christ, and be living in submission to the Holy Spirit.">k</a></sup>the devil's works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Whoever has been begotten by God does not commit sin, because His seed remains in him; actually, he is not able to sin, because he has been begotten by God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="This statement appears to be so contrary to our experience that it has given rise to all sorts of evasive action. When explaining this verse in a church I ask all who have been born of God to raise a hand (raising my own)—most do. I then ask, “When were you born of God, from your mother's womb?” “Oh no”, they will say, “it was when I was saved/born again.” I then ask for a volunteer who was saved as an adult—‘John Doe’ offers, saying he was saved when he was 30. So I say, “Tell me, John, for the first 29 years of your life you were John Doe, right? Your family, your friends, everybody knew you.” “That's right.” “So when you were saved at 30 did you stop being John Doe? Nobody knows you any more?” “Everybody still knows me.” “So for 29 years you were John Doe and you continue to be John Doe?” “That's right.” “Okay, so what do you have that was born of God? It can't be anything that was there during the first 29 years.” Well, the answers will vary according to the theological sophistication of the person, but the Bible speaks of a ‘new man’ or ‘new nature’ that the Holy Spirit produces in us. It is that new nature that is God's “seed” within us, and that seed cannot sin. But the old nature still exists, and it does indeed sin—recall the Apostle Paul's struggle (Romans 7:13-25).">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Children of God X children of the devil

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> In this the children of God and the children of the devil are distinguished: whoever is not doing righteousness is not of God, also whoever is not loving his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> Because this is the message that you heard from the beginning, that we should love one another</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> —not like Cain, who was of the evil one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Satan got Cain.">m</a></sup>and murdered his brother. And why did he murder him? Because his own works were evil, while those of his brother were righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> My brothers, do not be surprised if the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have transferred out of the death into the life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“The death” refers to Satan's kingdom; “the life” refers to Christ's Kingdom.">n</a></sup>because we love our brothers; he who does not love his brother<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Some 4% of the Greek manuscripts omit “his brother” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">o</a></sup>remains in the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Whoever is hating his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life abiding within himself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Note the “is hating”—someone who murdered in the past can repent and be saved.">p</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Love in deed and truth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> We know the Love by this: He laid down His life in our behalf; we also are obligated to lay down our lives in behalf of our brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> Now whoever has this world's goods and sees his brother in need, yet closes his heart toward him—how does the love of God abide in him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> My dear children, let us not love in word or with the tongue only, but in deed and truth</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> —yes, by this we know that we are of the Truth and may put our hearts at ease in His presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> and that, if our heart should condemn us, God is greater than our heart and knows all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Well, the heart is ‘deceitful above all things’ (Jeremiah 17:9), so presumably Satan can use it to accuse us falsely, but the Righteous Judge has all the facts. ## Confidence toward God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Dear ones, if our heart does not condemn us, we have confidence toward God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> and whatever we may ask we receive from Him, because we keep His commandments and do the things that are pleasing in His sight.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="I take the point to be this: if we are doing “the things that are pleasing in His sight”, then we will not ask for something that will displease Him.">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> Now this is His commandment, that we believe in the name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="With reference to salvation the Text always has ‘believe into Jesus/His name’—a change of position is involved, from being outside to being inside, which also involves commitment. <span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> He who keeps His commandments abides in Him, and He in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Note that the reciprocal abiding depends on our keeping His commandments.
