## Food for 15,000

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> Upon hearing it, Jesus departed from there by boat to a deserted place privately.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="From Mark we learn that Jesus and the disciples were under heavy pressure from the crowds—no time even to eat. So when He hears of John's death, and under such ignominious circumstances, Jesus wants to get away so He can grieve and assimilate the news.">d</a></sup>But when the crowds heard it, they followed Him on foot from the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> So when Jesus disembarked He saw a large crowd, and He had compassion on them and healed their sick.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In Mark it is even clearer that the people ran along the shore and got to the destination first, before the boat (please see the note at Mark 6:34). Naturally the faster runners were there first, but others kept arriving so that in the end there were thousands of people. In the town there was not space for everybody to get to Jesus, but out in the open—so when the news got around people came from all over.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> Now with evening coming on His disciples approached Him saying: “The place is uninhabited and the hour is already late. Dismiss the crowds, that they may go into the villages and buy themselves food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them: “They do not need to go away. You give them to eat.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Really now, with what? Although Jesus had to take the initiative, the disciples did participate in the miracle.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> But they say to Him, “We have nothing here except five loaves and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So He said, “Bring them here to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> Then He commanded the crowds to recline on the grass. And taking the five loaves and two fish and looking up to heaven, He blessed; and breaking the loaves He gave to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> So they all ate and were filled; and they took up the fragments that remained, twelve baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> Now those who ate were about five thousand men, besides women and children.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Since any large crowd is likely to have more women and children than men, there were probably at least 15,000 people there. “They were filled”—how much food does it take to ‘fill’ 15,000 hungry people? Notice the care not to waste anything—the fragments were collected.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Peter walks on water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> Immediately Jesus made His disciples get into the boat and go ahead of Him to the other side, while He dismissed the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> And having dismissed the crowds He went up on the mountain by Himself to pray. So when night came He was alone there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Finally, Jesus manages to be alone, to think and pray.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> But the boat was already in the middle of the sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Perhaps 2% of the Greek manuscripts, of inferior quality, read “many furlongs away from the land” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">i</a></sup>being harassed by the waves, for the wind was contrary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> So in the fourth watch of the night<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The fourth watch, using Roman time, was from 3 to 6 a.m.—the disciples had had a rough night!">j</a></sup>Jesus went to them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> And when the disciples saw Him walking on the sea, they became agitated saying, “It's a ghost!” And they cried out for fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But immediately Jesus spoke to them saying: “Courage! It is I; don't be afraid!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> So in answer Peter said, “Lord, since it's you, command me to come to you on the water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> So He said to him, “Come!” And stepping down from the boat Peter walked on the water to go to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But seeing the strong wind he became afraid, and beginning to sink<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Peter knew how to swim (John 21:7), but he was walking on top of the water (a totally new experience) and then he was wading; and the storm was scary.">k</a></sup>he cried out saying, “Lord, save me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalms 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> And immediately Jesus reached out His hand and caught him and says to him: “You little-faith! Why did you doubt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And when they got into the boat the wind ceased.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Why did the wind cease? Was it a supernatural wind? If so, who caused it?">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalms 2:7; Romans 1:4"> Then those who were in the boat came and worshiped Him saying, “Truly you are the Son of God!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="They took a lot of convincing, but are we not the same way?">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
