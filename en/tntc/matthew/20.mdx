## The desperate landowner

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> “For the kingdom of the heavens is like a certain landowner who went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> And agreeing with the workers for a denarius a day he sent them into his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> And going out about the third hour &#91;9 a.m.&#93; he saw others standing idle in the marketplace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> And he said to them, ‘You also go into the vineyard and I will give you whatever is right.’ So off they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> He went out again about the sixth hour, and the ninth, and did the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> Now about the eleventh hour he went out and found others standing idle, and he says to them, ‘Why have you stood here idle all day?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="How did he know they had been there all day? He had seen them, and had himself refused to hire them until the end. As his desperation level increased, he became less and less choosy about the quality of the workers [I take it that for some reason the grapes had to be collected that day; any that were left until later would be lost]. As the Church is in its eleventh hour, or later, it seems to me that we see God doing just like the landowner—He is using some rather ‘unusual’ workers.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> They say to him, ‘Because no one hired us.’ He says to them, ‘You also go into the vineyard, and you will receive whatever is right.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Perhaps 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit the last clause (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> So when evening had come the owner of the vineyard says to his foreman, ‘Call the workers and pay them their wage, beginning at the last to the first.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Presumably the usual procedure would be to pay the first ones first, so they could head for home; by inverting the order the owner provoked the confrontation. What about our own notions of ‘fairness’? People who trust in Christ at the end of a misspent life get to go to Heaven, just like those who have served God all their lives. Of course there is the matter of rewards, but they scarcely compare with the fact of spending eternity in Heaven, rather than Hell.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> The eleventh hour ones came and each received a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So when the first ones came they supposed that they would receive more; yet each of them also received a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> When they received it they began grumbling against the landowner</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> saying, ‘These last worked only one hour, and you made them equal to us who bore the burden and the heat of the day!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> But in answer he said to one of them: ‘Friend, I am not wronging you. Did you not agree with me for a denarius?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take what is yours and go. I wish to give to this last one just as to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> Or is it not permissible for me to do what I want with what is mine? Is your eye evil because I am good?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> Just so the last will be first, and the first last. For many are called, but few are chosen.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Just 1.5% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit the last sentence (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus alerts the disciples, again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> As Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem He took the twelve disciples aside on the road and said to them privately:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> “Listen! We are going up to Jerusalem and the Son of the Man will be betrayed to the chief priests and scribes; and they will condemn Him to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> and they will hand Him over to the Gentiles to mock, to scourge and to crucify. And on the third day He will rise!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Selfish ambition rebuked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then the mother of Zebedee's sons approached Him, with her sons, and kneeling down she asked something from Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> So He said to her, “What do you wish?” She says to Him, “State that these my two sons may sit one on Your right and one on Your left in Your kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> But in answer Jesus said: “You (pl.) do not know what you are asking.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jesus sees that the sons had put their mother up to it and answers them, not her.">e</a></sup>Are you able to drink the cup which I am about to drink, or to be baptized with the baptism that I am being baptized with?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Perhaps 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit “or be baptized with the baptism that I am being baptized with” and also the corresponding clause in verse 23 (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">f</a></sup>They say to Him, “We are able.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> And He says to them: “You will indeed drink my cup, and you will be baptized with the baptism that I am being baptized with, but to sit on my right and on my left is not mine to give; rather it is for those for whom it has been prepared by my Father.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="We may well be surprised at the Father's choices.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> Now when the ten heard it they were indignant at the two brothers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Why were they indignant, unless they had similar ideas?">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> But summoning them Jesus said: “You know that the rulers of the nations lord it over them, and the great exercise authority over them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> but it must not be so among you. Rather, whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> and whoever wants to be first among you must be your slave;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> just like the Son of the Man—He did not come to be served but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many.”</span>
</div>
