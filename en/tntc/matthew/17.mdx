## A tough kind of demon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> As they came to the crowd a man approached Him and kneeling to Him said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> “Lord, have mercy on my son, because he is moonstruck and suffers badly! For he often falls into the fire and often into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> So I brought him to your disciples, but they could not cure him.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> And Jesus rebuked the demon and it came out of him, and the boy was healed from that very hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 9:28; Mark 4:10"> Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and said, “Why were we not able to cast it out?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; Mark 8:31"> and they will kill Him, and on the third day He will be raised.” And they were seriously grieved.</span>

Dying to self is a prerequisite for moving mountains.</li>
