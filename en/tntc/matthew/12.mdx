## Jesus is Lord of the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Luke 6:1–5"> At that time Jesus went through the grain fields on the Sabbath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Text actually has ‘the Sabbaths’; the parallel passage in Luke 6:1 has ‘a second-first Sabbath’. <span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; John 7:21–24; Luke 14:3"> And upon seeing it the Pharisees<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Most probably the Pharisees had ‘observers’ tagging along everywhere Jesus went.">b</a></sup>said to Him, “Hey, your disciples are doing what is not lawful to do on a Sabbath!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 6:3; Mark 2:25–26"> But He said to them: “Have you not read what David did when he became hungry, he and those who were with him,</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:9–10; John 7:22–23; Nehemiah 13:17"> Or have you not read in the Law that on the Sabbaths the priests in the temple profane the Sabbath and are guiltless?</span>

<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 13:14; John 9:16; John 8:6"> And there was a man with a withered hand! And they asked Him saying, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”—so that they might accuse Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:4; Luke 14:5; Luke 13:15–17"> So He said to them: “What man will there be among you who has one sheep, and should this one fall into a ditch on the Sabbath, will he not lay hold of it and lift it out?</span>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 15:18; Ephesians 4:29"> Brood of vipers!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="At no time did the Lord make any effort to conciliate the Pharisees.
