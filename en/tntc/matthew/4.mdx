<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2; Isaiah 42:6–7; Isaiah 60:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people sitting in darkness saw a great light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and upon those sitting in the place and shadow of death</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">light has dawned.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="See Isaiah 9:1-2.">n</a></sup></span>

## Jesus calls four fishermen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:38; Mark 1:14–15"> From then on Jesus began to preach and to say, “Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has come near.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Right! <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> And walking by the Sea of Galilee He saw two brothers, Simon (the one called Peter) and Andrew his brother, casting a circular net into the sea (for they were fishermen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> And He says to them, “Follow me and I will make you fishers of men.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> So leaving the boat and their father immediately they followed Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="All four of these men had spent time with Jesus already, so this was not ‘out of the blue’.">q</a></sup></span>
