## That which defiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> Then summoning the crowd He said to them: “Hear and understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> Not what goes into the mouth defiles the man, but what comes out of the mouth—this defiles the man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then His disciples came and said to Him, “Do you know that upon hearing this saying the Pharisees were offended?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> But in answer He said: “Every plant that my heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> Leave them alone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jesus makes no effort to conciliate or to convert the Pharisees. For all that, some did convert.">e</a></sup>They are blind guides of the blind; and if the blind guides the blind, both will fall into a ditch.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> Then Peter answered and said to Him, “Explain this parable to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> So Jesus said: “Are you also still without understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> Do you not yet understand that whatever enters the mouth goes into the stomach and is expelled into the sewer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But the things coming out of the mouth actually come from the heart; those are the things that defile the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> Because out of the heart proceed malignant thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This is one of a number of places where ‘adultery’ and ‘fornication’ both occur in a list of sins—it is clear that they are distinct in meaning, and so ‘fornication’ does not include ‘adultery’; and so to render ‘fornication’ as ‘sexual sin/immorality’ is misleading.">f</a></sup>thefts, false witness, blasphemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> These are the things that defile the man, but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile the man.”</span>
</div>

## A crumb for a little dog

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> Going out from there Jesus withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> And then, a Canaanite woman coming from those parts cried out to Him saying: “Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is severely demonized.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But He answered her not a word.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Son of David”—as a Canaanite she appealed to the Jewish Messiah, upon whom she had no claim. But how did she know that? I suspect there is more to this story than meets the eye.">g</a></sup>So His disciples came and urged Him saying, “Send her away, because she is crying out after us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> But in answer He said, “I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Although His ultimate mission included the whole world (see the Great Commission in Matthew 28:19-20), His earthly ministry was directed to the “house of Israel”.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> So she came and worshiped Him saying, “Lord, help me!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Here she drops the appeal to the Messiah—evidently the Lord spoke loudly enough for her to hear.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> But in answer He said, “It is not good to take the children's bread and throw it to the little dogs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> So she said, “Yes, Lord, yet even the little dogs eat the crumbs that fall from their masters' table.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="We may feel that Jesus was harsh with her, but in this way He put into bold relief her faith and humility—and she got her crumb!">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalms 145:19"> Then Jesus answered and said to her: “O woman, great is your faith! Let it be to you as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that very hour.</span>
</div>

## Food for 4,000 men

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> Moving on from there, Jesus went alongside the Sea of Galilee, and going up on the mountain He sat down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> And large crowds approached Him, having with them lame, blind, mute, maimed and many others, and they placed them at Jesus' feet, and He healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> So the crowds marveled—seeing mutes speaking, maimed made whole, lame walking, and blind seeing—and they kept glorifying the God of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> Then Jesus summoned His disciples and said: “I have compassion on the crowd, because they have already remained with me three days, and have nothing to eat; and I do not want to send them away hungry, lest they faint on the way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> So His disciples say to Him, “Where could we get, in an uninhabited place, enough bread so as to satisfy such a great crowd?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> Jesus says to them, “How many loaves do you have?” And they said, “Seven, and a few small fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> So He commanded the crowds to recline on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> And taking the seven loaves and the fish, He gave thanks, broke and gave to His disciples, and the disciples to the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> So they all ate and were filled; and they took up seven hampers full of the fragments that remained.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Where did the “hampers” come from? Probably from fishing boats that stopped by.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Now those who ate were four thousand men, besides women and children.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Again, the crowd was much larger than just the 4,000 men; probably at least 10,000 people.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> And having dismissed the crowds He got into the boat and went to the region of Magdala.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Magdala was Mary Magdalene's hometown; perhaps this was when Jesus delivered her from the demons (Mark 16:9). The parallel passage in Mark 8:10 has ‘the region of Dalmanutha’—I assume that Magdala was in that region.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
