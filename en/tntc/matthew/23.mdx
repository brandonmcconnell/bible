## Jesus excoriates the scribes and Pharisees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to His disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> saying: “The scribes and the Pharisees are seated upon Moses' chair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> so whatever they may tell you to observe, you should observe and do; but do not do according to their deeds, for they say and do not do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> For they package loads that are heavy and hard to bear, and lay them on the shoulders of the people; but they themselves do not want to move them with one of their fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> “Rather, they do all their works in order to be seen by people. They make their phylacteries broad and the tassels on their garments large;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="They were big on ostentation.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> they love the place of honor at dinners and the first seats in the synagogues,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> and the greetings in the marketplaces, namely to be called ‘Rabbi, Rabbi’ by the people.</span>
</div>
</div>

## We have only one Teacher and one Father

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> “But you (pl.), do not be called ‘Rabbi’; because your Teacher is one, the Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts omit “the Christ” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">b</a></sup>and you are all brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> And do not call anyone on earth your ‘father’; because your Father is one, He who is in the heavens.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Since the second person here is plural, the Lord is evidently referring to calling someone your spiritual father; He is not saying not to acknowledge your physical father. “Your (pl.) Father is one”—obviously they did not all have the same physical father.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Neither be called leaders/guides; because your Leader is one, the Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This may be why we have no record in Scripture of a Christian calling someone his disciple; even in 1 Corinthians 3:4 Paul evidently avoids using the term. I take it that our Lord is forbidding any effort by one Christian to control the spiritual life of another. He had already told the Samaritan woman that the Father must be worshiped “in spirit and truth” (John 4:23-24). Therefore, that worship may not be forced or imposed, or controlled by someone else.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> On the contrary, the greatest among you must be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> And whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> “Woe to you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you devour the houses of the widows,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="How did that work? I suppose that if the widow was in debt, they would just foreclose and put her out on the street.">e</a></sup>while making long prayers as a pretense. Therefore you will receive a greater condemnation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="They are already condemned, but are making it worse. Perhaps 2% of the Greek manuscripts, of inferior quality, omit this verse (as in NIV, [NASB], LB, [TEV], etc.). A very small minority, perhaps another 1%, reverse the order of verses 13 and 14 (as in KJV and NKJV).">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Woe to you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites!! Because you bar the entrance to the kingdom of the heavens in the face of the people; for you neither go in yourselves nor do you allow those who are trying to enter to go in.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Those guys were really perverse! If you don't want to go in yourself, that's your problem; but to try to keep others from going in is satanic, really and truly satanic.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> Woe to you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites!!! Because you travel all over, land and sea, to make one convert, and when he joins up you make him twice as much a son of Hell<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Where do you suppose a ‘son of hell’ is going to spend eternity?">h</a></sup>as yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> “Woe to you blind guides, who say, ‘Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the temple is obligated.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalms 94:8"> Stupid and blind! For which is greater, the gold or the temple that sanctifies the gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And, ‘Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift that is on it is obligated.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> Stupid and blind!! For which is greater, the gift or the altar that sanctifies the gift?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Wow, they really were stupid and blind! which raises the obvious question: How did they get that way? According to Ephesians 2:2, ‘sons of the disobedience’ suffer direct satanic influence.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore he who swears by the altar swears by it and by all the things on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> And he who swears by the temple swears by it and by Him who used to dwell in it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some 10% of the Greek manuscripts have “dwell” in the present tense (as in most versions), rather than the past. I take it that Jesus was telling them that God had left their temple (see verse 38). Thus verse 22 is in contrast with verse 21, with reference to God's presence.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalms 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> And he who swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by Him who sits on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> “Woe to you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you pay a tithe of mint and dill and cummin, but have neglected the weightier items of the law: justice and mercy and faith. These it was obligatory to do, while not neglecting those.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> Blind guides, who strain out a gnat but gulp down a camel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> “Woe to you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites!! Because you cleanse the outside of the cup and the dish, but inside they are full of plunder and injustice.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Instead of “injustice”, perhaps 5% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘self-indulgence’ (as in most versions).">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> Blind Pharisee! First cleanse the inside of the cup and the dish, so that their outside may also be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> “Woe to you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites!!! Because you resemble whitewashed tombs, which indeed appear beautiful outwardly but inside are full of dead bones and of all uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalms 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> Just so, you also outwardly appear to be righteous to the people, but inside you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> “Woe to you scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites!!!! Because you build the tombs of the prophets and adorn the monuments of the righteous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> yet you say, ‘If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> Therefore you testify against yourselves that you are sons of those who murdered the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> So fill up the measure of your fathers' guilt!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Snakes!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> “Snakes! Brood of vipers! How can you escape from the condemnation of Hell?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Would you say that Jesus is trying real hard not to hurt their poor little feelings? So are we supposed to avoid hurting people's feelings? When and why?">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> So take note: I will send you prophets and wise men and Biblical scholars;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Jesus makes this statement knowing that He will die within a few days. Obviously He also knows that He will rise from the dead and return to the Father's right hand—it is from that position that He will do the sending.">m</a></sup>some of them you will kill, even crucify; some of them you will flog in your synagogues and persecute from city to city</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> —so that upon you may come all the righteous blood shed on the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel up to the blood of Zechariah son of Berechiah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The only ‘Zechariah son of Berechiah’ recorded in the OT was the author of the book of prophecy that bears his name. The temple was rebuilt during his time, so it would be possible for him to have been killed there, but his death is not recorded. The Lord may have known something we do not, or He may have been referring to someone else.">n</a></sup>whom you murdered between the temple and the altar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A terrible statement! But Jesus is addressing persons who will condemn the Messiah to death with total premeditated malice.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> Assuredly I say to you that all these things will come upon this generation.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus laments over Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalms 91:4"> “Jerusalem, Jerusalem, she who kills the prophets and stones those who are sent to her! How often I wanted to gather your children, just like a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, but you did not want to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Look, your house is left to you abandoned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> For I say to you, you will not see me again until you say, ‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord’!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="See Psalm 118:26. Apparently He did not return to the temple anymore; He had declared judgment upon it: “your house is left to you abandoned”.">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
