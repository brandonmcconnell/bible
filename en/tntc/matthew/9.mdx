## Jesus raises the dead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> While He was saying these things to them, there he was; a certain ruler came and worshiped Him, saying, “My daughter has just died, but come and lay your hand on her and she will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> So Jesus got up and followed him, with His disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> And then—a woman who had been hemorrhaging for twelve years came from behind and touched the hem of His garment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> For she kept saying to herself, “If only I may touch His garment I will be healed.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="It is clear from Mark 5:27-30 that the woman was healed before Jesus turned around. <span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> But Jesus, turning around and seeing her, said, “Take courage, daughter; your faith has made you well.” And the woman was healed from that very hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> When Jesus came into the ruler's house and saw the flutists and the crowd making a commotion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> He says to them, “Go away, for the girl is not dead, but sleeping.” So they began to ridicule Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="They knew that the girl was dead.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> But when the crowd had been put outside, He went in and grasped her hand, and the girl got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> And the report of this went out into all that land.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Mark 10:46–48"> As Jesus went on from there two blind men followed Him, crying out and saying, “Have mercy on us, Son of David!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 13:58; Matthew 8:2"> And arriving in the house, the blind men approached Him; so He says to them, “Do you believe that I am able to do this?” They say to Him, “Yes, Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then He touched their eyes saying, “According to your faith let it be to you.”</span>
