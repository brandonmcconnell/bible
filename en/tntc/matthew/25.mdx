## Parable of the talents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 13:34; Luke 19:12–27; 1 Peter 4:9–11"> “Because the kingdom is also like a man about to go on a long journey, who called his own servants and entrusted his possessions to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:24; Luke 19:13–14; Luke 12:48"> To one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one, to each according to his own ability; and immediately he went on the journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; 2 Timothy 2:6"> So the one who received the five talents went and traded with them, and made another five talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Ephesians 5:16; 1 Peter 4:10"> So also the one who received the two gained another two as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; Malachi 1:10"> But he who received the one went off and dug in the ground and hid his lord's silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Luke 19:16–17; Colossians 1:29"> “So the one who had received the five talents approached and brought five more talents saying, ‘Lord, you entrusted five talents to me; see, I gained five more talents besides them!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Luke 16:10; John 12:26"> His lord said to him: ‘Super, you good and faithful servant! You were faithful over a few things, I will put you in charge of many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; 2 Corinthians 8:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:1–3"> So the one who had received the two talents also approached and said, ‘Lord, you entrusted two talents to me; see, I gained two more talents besides them!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Mark 14:8–9; Mark 12:41–44"> His lord said to him: ‘Super, you good and faithful servant! Enter into the joy of your lord.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The two-talent man was just as faithful as the first one—he also doubled the principal—so he gets the same commendation. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 19:20–22; Luke 15:29; Malachi 3:14–15"> “Then he who had received the one talent also approached and said: ‘Lord, I knew that you are a hard man, reaping where you did not sow and gathering where you did not scatter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6–7; Romans 8:15; Revelation 21:8"> And being afraid I went off and hid your talent in the ground; here, take what is yours.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Evidently the third man did not identify with his lord's interests. ## Judgment in preparation for the Millennium

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7–8; Matthew 19:28"> “When the Son of the Man comes in His glory,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The Lord Jesus uses “the coming of the Son of the Man” to refer to both the Rapture and the Second Advent.">j</a></sup>and all the holy angels with Him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts omit “holy” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.). <span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Matthew 13:49; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> And all the nations will be gathered before Him, and He will separate them one from another,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="It is clear that the King is dealing with individuals here; nations are made up of people, and each person will be evaluated individually.">l</a></sup>just as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; John 10:26–28; John 21:15–17"> And the sheep He will place on His right, but the goats on His left.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 3:16–19; James 2:15–16; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> For I was hungry and you gave me food; I was thirsty and you gave me drink; I was a stranger and you took me in;</span>
