## Parable of the soils explained

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> “Therefore hear the parable of the sower:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> When anyone hears the word of the kingdom and does not understand, the malignant one comes and snatches away what was sown in his heart—this is the seed sown alongside the road.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="In sowing, the seed is scattered on top of the soil, not planted, and is therefore vulnerable—maybe that is why the great commission is to disciple. Observe that Jesus is teaching that the devil can invade a person's mind.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalms 106:12–13; John 5:35"> But the seed sown on stony places—this is he who hears the word and directly receives it with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> but he has no root in himself and is short-lived; for when trial or persecution comes because of the word, directly he is offended.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This is typical of the ‘prosperity gospel’.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> But the seed sown among the thorns—this is he who hears the word, but the care of this age and the deceitfulness of riches choke out the word, and it becomes fruitless.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Both the seed on stony places and among thorns sprouted—there was life.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> Now the seed sown on the good ground—this is he who hears the word and understands, who indeed bears fruit and produces: some a hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Not all saved persons serve the Lord at the same level of intensity; some are more committed, others less.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Mustard seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 13:18–19; Matthew 17:20; Mark 4:30–32"> He put another parable to them, saying: “The kingdom of the heavens is like a mustard seed which a man took and sowed in his field;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:6; Daniel 4:12; Ezekiel 17:23–24"> which indeed is smaller than all the seeds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, seeds normally planted in gardens.">k</a></sup>but when it is grown it is greater than all the vegetables and becomes a tree, so that the birds of the air come and rest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The verb I have rendered ‘to rest’ is a compound form.
