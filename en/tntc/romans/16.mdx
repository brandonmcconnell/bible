<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> I commend to you our sister Phoebe, who is a servant of the congregation in Cenchrea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> so that you may receive her in the Lord in a manner worthy of the saints, and assist her in whatever matter she may need from you; for indeed she herself has been a helper of many, including my own self.</span>

## Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> who risked their own necks for my life;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="So far as I know, we do not have a record of just how, when or where this happened.">a</a></sup>to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the congregations of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> And greet the congregation that is in their house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In the beginning, congregations met in people's homes. An underground Church will have little use for ‘buildings’.">b</a></sup>Greet my beloved Epaenetus, who is the first convert to Christ from Achaia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="4% of the Greek manuscripts read “Asia” instead of “Achaia” (these are very different places), to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Greet Mary, who worked very hard for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Although scarcely a fourth of the Greek manuscripts read “you” (instead of “us”), that fourth includes the best line of transmission.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Greet Andronicus and Junias, my countrymen and my fellow prisoners, who are outstanding among the apostles, and who were in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Greet Amplias, my beloved in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my beloved Stachys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Greet Apelles, approved in Christ. Greet those who are of the household of Aristobulus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Greet Herodian, my countryman. Greet those who are of the household of Narcissus, who are in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, who have worked hard in the Lord. Greet the beloved Persis, who has worked very hard in the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The three in this verse are women.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, also his mother, and mine.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="If Rufus were his physical brother, presumably Paul would have expressed himself differently, so the ‘mother’ here is not literally Paul's.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brothers who are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, also Olympus and all the saints who are with them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Since Paul had never been to Rome, so far, I wonder how he knew so many believers who were there (he knew them well enough to give details about them). <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Greet one another with a holy kiss. The congregations of Christ greet you.</span>

## Warning

<div className="bp">

## Footnotes

<ol className="fnl">
<li id="fn-a"><a href="#fnref-a" className="fnb">a</a> <span className="fnr">16:4</span> So far as I know, we do not have a record of just how, when or where this happened.</li>
<li id="fn-b"><a href="#fnref-b" className="fnb">b</a> <span className="fnr">16:5</span> In the beginning, congregations met in people's homes. This avoids the expense of erecting buildings. I believe the days are upon us when all serious followers of Jesus Christ will be increasingly persecuted, including in North America and Europe. An underground Church will have little use for ‘buildings’.</li>
<li id="fn-c"><a href="#fnref-c" className="fnb">c</a> <span className="fnr">16:5</span> 4% of the Greek manuscripts read “Asia” instead of “Achaia” (these are very different places), to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding.</li>
<li id="fn-d"><a href="#fnref-d" className="fnb">d</a> <span className="fnr">16:6</span> Although scarcely a fourth of the Greek manuscripts read “you” (instead of “us”), that fourth includes the best line of transmission.</li>
<li id="fn-e"><a href="#fnref-e" className="fnb">e</a> <span className="fnr">16:12</span> The three in this verse are women.</li>
<li id="fn-f"><a href="#fnref-f" className="fnb">f</a> <span className="fnr">16:13</span> If Rufus were his physical brother, presumably Paul would have expressed himself differently, so the ‘mother’ here is not literally Paul's.</li>
<li id="fn-g"><a href="#fnref-g" className="fnb">g</a> <span className="fnr">16:15</span> Since Paul had never been to Rome, so far, I wonder how he knew so many believers who were there (he knew them well enough to give details about them). I suppose most of them had migrated there from places Paul had been, and he would have to know about that migration.</li>
<li id="fn-h"><a href="#fnref-h" className="fnb">h</a> <span className="fnr">16:18</span> Some 13% of the Greek manuscripts omit “Jesus”, as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.</li>
<li id="fn-i"><a href="#fnref-i" className="fnb">i</a> <span className="fnr">16:18</span> Guileless people are often not prepared to recognize guile in others. So those who are responsible for the spiritual welfare of the flock need to warn the people about who should be avoided.</li>
<li id="fn-j"><a href="#fnref-j" className="fnb">j</a> <span className="fnr">16:19</span> When the first pair ate of the forbidden tree, this was not the result.</li>
<li id="fn-k"><a href="#fnref-k" className="fnb">k</a> <span className="fnr">16:20</span> The sooner, the better!</li>
<li id="fn-l"><a href="#fnref-l" className="fnb">l</a> <span className="fnr">16:20</span> 1% of the Greek manuscripts (of objectively inferior quality) omit “Christ”, to be followed by NIV and NASB—an inferior proceeding.</li>
<li id="fn-m"><a href="#fnref-m" className="fnb">m</a> <span className="fnr">16:23</span> One gains the clear impression that the people mentioned in verse 23 knew that Tertius was penning this letter. Not only that, they asked to be included! I wonder who Quartus may have been.</li>
<li id="fn-n"><a href="#fnref-n" className="fnb">n</a> <span className="fnr">16:24</span> I have followed perhaps 20% of the Greek manuscripts, including the best line of transmission, in putting ‘us’ rather than the familiar ‘you’. If verses 22-24 were not dictated by Paul, then the first person is especially appropriate, coming from Tertius.</li>
<li id="fn-o"><a href="#fnref-o" className="fnb">o</a> <span className="fnr">16:24</span> 3.2% of the Greek manuscripts omit verse 24, while 7.2% supply 14:24-26 at this point.</li>
