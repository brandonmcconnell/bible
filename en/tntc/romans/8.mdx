## Spirit X flesh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Galatians 5:19–25; John 3:6"> Those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the Spirit, the things of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:7; Romans 8:13"> Further, the mindset of the flesh yields death, but the mindset of the Spirit yields life and peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 John 2:15–16; James 4:4"> because the mindset of the flesh represents enmity against God, since it does not submit to God's law, nor indeed can it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:5–6; Hebrews 11:5–6; Romans 8:9"> So, those who are ‘in flesh’ cannot please God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This is a ‘genetic’ incapability, so we need a new birth.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:6; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Timothy 1:14"> You, however, are not ‘in flesh’ but ‘in Spirit’, if indeed God's Spirit dwells in you. Now if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he is not His.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> But if Christ is in you, the body is dead through sin, but the Spirit is life through righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> And if the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, then He who raised the Christ from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Where, down here? ## Redemption of the body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17–18; 1 Peter 4:13; 1 Peter 1:5–7"> I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory that is going to be revealed in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; Romans 8:23; 1 John 3:2"> You see, the creation waits with eager anticipation for the revelation of God's sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; Romans 8:22"> because without choice the creation was subjected to futility, due to the One who did the subjecting, based on the hope</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 22:3–5; Acts 3:21; 2 Peter 3:13"> that the creation itself will also be set free from the bondage to decay into the freedom of the glory of God's children.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="In the beginning, when the Creator turned the administration of this planet over to Adam, everything was ‘good’. But then came the Fall, and the administrator was no longer good. How could a fallen ruler administer a perfect creation? Since the creation was merely a setting for the man, the Creator reduced it to the same level, based on ‘the hope’ of a future restoration for both man and nature (see 1 Peter 1:19-20).">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; John 16:21"> Yes, we know that the whole creation has been groaning and in labor pains until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–5; Hebrews 9:28"> Not only that, we ourselves also who have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we groan within ourselves, eagerly awaiting adoption, the redemption of our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1; 2 Corinthians 4:18"> Yes, we were saved in the hope,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Since the restoration of the planet depends on the restoration of Adam's descendants, at least some of them, and since this is a time consuming process, we start out in ‘the hope’.">l</a></sup>but a hope that is seen is not hope—why would anyone hope for what he sees?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 27:14"> But if we hope for what we do not see, we eagerly await it with endurance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; Ephesians 6:18; Galatians 4:6"> Likewise also the Spirit comes to the aid of the hope in our weaknesses, since we do not know what we need to pray for. Indeed the Spirit Himself intercedes on our behalf with inexpressible groanings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; 1 John 5:14–15; Ephesians 2:18"> while He who searches the hearts knows what is on the Spirit's mind, since He prays for the saints in accordance with God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The intercession of the Spirit represents two members of the Trinity!
