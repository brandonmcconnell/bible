## The remnant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> So now I say, God did not reject His people, did He? Of course not! Why I myself am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, from the tribe of Benjamin!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Observe that Paul knew what tribe he belonged to; this at the beginning of the Christian era. If God would save Paul, then He is still saving Jews (and God knows what tribe they are from, even if they no longer do).">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God has not rejected His people whom He foreknew. Do you not know what the Scripture says about Elijah, how he appeals to God against Israel saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed your prophets and torn down your altars; I am the only one left, and they are trying to take my life!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 Kings 19:10,14.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> But what does the divine response say to him? “I have reserved for myself seven thousand men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The term here refers specifically to males.">c</a></sup>who have not bowed the knee to Baal.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See 1 Kings 19:18. Evidently Elijah knew nothing about them, and perhaps each of them also thought he was the only one. I see a practical application here: I must never assume that what God is doing to and through me is the whole show, or even a major part of it. Now, much more than in Elijah's day, God is doing all sorts of interesting things all over the world!">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> Even so then at this present time, there is a remnant according to an election by grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> Now if by grace, it is no longer from works; otherwise the grace is no longer grace. But if from works, it is no longer grace; otherwise the work is no longer work.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“But if from works, it is no longer grace; otherwise the work is no longer work” is omitted by 3.5% of the Greek manuscripts, to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What then? What Israel is seeking, this it did not obtain; but the election<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="‘Election’ acts as a mass noun, including all the elected individuals.">f</a></sup>did obtain it, while the rest were dulled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> Just as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes that were not to see and ears that were not to hear, to this very day.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 29:4 and Isaiah 29:10.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalms 28:4"> And David says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let their table be made into a snare and into a trap,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">into a stumbling block and into a retribution to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let their eyes be darkened so as not to see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their back always be bowed down.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See Psalm 69:22-23.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The restoration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> So now I say, surely they have not stumbled so as to fall &#91;and stay fallen&#93;? Of course not! Rather, through their transgression the salvation has come to the Gentiles, to provoke them to jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> Now if their transgression means riches for the world, and their loss riches for the nations, how much more their fulfillment!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> So I am speaking to you Gentiles (inasmuch as I am ‘apostle to the Gentiles’, I magnify my ministry),</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> if somehow I can provoke my own people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, ‘flesh’.">i</a></sup>to jealousy and save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> For if their being rejected means reconciliation for the world, what will their restoration be but life from the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> Now if the ‘firstfruit’ is holy, so is the whole batch; and if the root is holy, so are the branches.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Grafted olive branches

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> Now if some of the branches were broken off, while you, being a wild olive shoot, were grafted in among them, and with them have become a partaker of the root and the rich sap of the cultivated olive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> do not boast against those branches (since if you do boast—it is not you who support the root but the root you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> You will say then, “Branches were broken off so that I might be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> Granted. Because of unbelief they were broken off, but you stand by faith. Do not be arrogant, but fear!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> Because if God did not spare the natural branches, perhaps He will not spare you either!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> So consider the kindness and severity of God: upon those who fell, severity; but toward you, kindness, if you continue in that kindness; otherwise you too will be cut off.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Here is a plain statement. ‘Continuing’ wins, and it is something that is up to us.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And even they, if they do not persist in the unbelief, will be grafted back in, because God has the power to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> For if you were cut out of a wild by nature olive tree, and were grafted contrary to nature into a cultivated olive tree, how much more will these, the natural branches, be grafted into their own olive tree?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="One is reminded of Ezekiel 18, the whole chapter—if you have not read it for a while, I suggest you refresh your memory.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Hardening

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> Brothers, I do not want you to be ignorant of this ‘mystery’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="This term is used of information that God has not yet made generally available, not about something mysterious.">l</a></sup>(so that you not be conceited): hardening has come upon Israel in part until the full number of the Gentiles has come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> And so all Israel will be saved, just as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Deliverer will come out of Zion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and He will turn ungodliness away from Jacob;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="‘Jacob’ is Jacob, not the Church!">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">this is my covenant with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I take away their sins.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="See Isaiah 59:20-21 and 27:9. When will this happen? Presumably at the beginning of the millennial Messianic Kingdom. The conversion of the last Gentile will be followed by the Rapture. All the Israelites that enter the Kingdom will be saved (all rebels will have been executed).">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> On the one hand, regarding the Gospel, they are enemies because of you; on the other, regarding the election, they are beloved on account of the forefathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> Because God's gracious gifts and calling are irrevocable.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="That is, God does not take them back; what we do with them is another story.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> For just as you also were once disobedient to God, but now have been shown mercy through their disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so also these have now been disobedient that through the mercy shown to you they also may be shown mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> Yes, God has given everyone over to disobedience so that He might show mercy to all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="A bitter pill, but apart from God's mercy we do not obey Him.">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
