## Israel needs salvation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers, my heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel is for their salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I bear them witness that they have a zeal for God, but not according to real knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For they, failing to understand God's righteousness and seeking to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted to God's righteousness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## How to be saved

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> Now for everyone who believes into righteousness, Christ is the end of the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, as a means of salvation.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> —Moses writes about the righteousness that is from the law: “The person who does these things will live by them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Leviticus 18:5.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness that is from faith speaks like this: “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 30:12.">c</a></sup>(that is, to bring Christ down)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> “or, ‘Who will descend into the abyss?’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 30:13. The ‘abyss’ here is presumably not the eschatological one.">d</a></sup>(that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> So what does it say? “The message is near you, in your mouth and in your heart”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 30:14.">e</a></sup>(that is, the message of faith that we proclaim):</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> That if you confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> Because with the heart one believes into righteousness, and with the mouth one promises<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This refers to a solemn commitment made publicly—even a demon can mouth the phrase, ‘Jesus is Lord’, as can any person, except that the demon knows it is really true.">f</a></sup>into salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> Because the Scripture says: “Everyone who believes on Him will never be put to shame”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Isaiah 28:16.">g</a></sup>;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> because there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, since the same Lord of all is rich to all who call upon Him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> because: “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See Joel 2:32. This has always been true, although the level of opportunity differs from time to time and people to people.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The message is necessary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then will they call on Him in whom they have not believed? And how can they believe on Him of whom they have not heard? And how will they hear without someone proclaiming it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how will they proclaim unless they are sent? As it is written: “How timely is the arrival<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally, ‘beautiful the feet’.">i</a></sup>of those who bring good news of peace,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Of those who bring good news of peace” is omitted by 10.5% of the Greek manuscripts, to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding.">j</a></sup>of those who announce the good things.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="See Isaiah 52:7 and Nahum 1:15.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> (However, they did not all obey the gospel; because Isaiah says: “Lord, who has believed our message?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="See Isaiah 53:1.">l</a></sup>)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> So, the faith is from a message, and that message is by the Word of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Perhaps 2% of the Greek manuscripts read ‘Christ’ rather than “God”, to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding. It is the Word of God that gives authority to our message. Actually, ‘word’ here is a spoken word, precisely the messenger proclaiming his good news.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## A rebellious people

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I say, did they really not hear? Indeed yes:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their voice has gone out into all the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their spoken words</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the ends of the inhabited world.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="See Psalm 19:4.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> Again I say, did Israel really not know? First Moses says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will provoke you to jealousy by a non-nation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will move you to anger by a senseless nation.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 32:21.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> But Isaiah is so bold as to say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I was found by those who were not seeking me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I was revealed to those who were not asking for me.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="See Isaiah 65:1.">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But concerning Israel he says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“All day long I have held out my hands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to a disobedient and contrary people.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="See Isaiah 65:2. To be ‘disobedient’ and ‘contrary’, there must be a revealed will, one that they knew about.">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
