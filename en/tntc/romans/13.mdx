## Submit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Let every soul submit to the governing authorities, because there is no authority except under God, and the existing authorities have been placed by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> Therefore whoever resists the authority is opposed to God's ordinance, and those who oppose will bring judgment on themselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="God does not like anarchy.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> Now rulers are not a terror to good deeds but to bad ones. So if you wish not to fear the authority, do the good and you will have praise from the same;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> because it is God's minister to you with a view to the good. But if you do wrong, be afraid, because it does not bear the sword for nothing; because it is God's minister, an avenger with a view to wrath on the wrongdoer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> Therefore it is necessary to submit, not only because of the wrath, but also because of the conscience.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Presumably regimes that are overtly evil are not in view. Where a regime overtly combats the good, while promoting the evil, divine guidance is especially necessary for representatives of the good, because even an evil regime exists by God's permission. That said, however, in Luke 10:19 Sovereign Jesus gives us “the authority over all the power of the enemy”. It is therefore within the sphere of our competence to forbid any use of Satan's power in the administration of the country, in the surrounding culture, in the entertainment industry, and so on. The sky is the limit.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> This is precisely why you pay taxes, for they are God's servants, continually attending on this very thing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Have you ever seen a government that didn't want more money?">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> Render therefore to all their due: taxes to whom taxes, customs to whom customs, respect to whom respect, honor to whom honor.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Owe no one anything except to love one another, since he who loves the other has fulfilled the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Of course this is agape love.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> Because—“You shall not commit adultery,” “You shall not murder,” “You shall not steal,” “You shall not bear false witness,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="About a third of the Greek manuscripts omit “You shall not bear false witness”, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">e</a></sup>“You shall not covet,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Exodus 20:13-17 and Deuteronomy 5:17-21.">f</a></sup>and whatever further commandment—everything is summed up in this word, namely: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Leviticus 19:18.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love does no harm to a neighbor; therefore love is the fulfillment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The Greek word here could also be rendered ‘complement’.">h</a></sup>of the law.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Put on Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> Besides this, knowing the time, it is high time for us to be awakened from sleep, because our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night is nearly over, and the day is near;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="When a servant of Christ dies physically, he leaves the ‘night’ of this world behind and his ‘day’ of glory begins. Each day we live brings us closer to that moment, so we need to make the best possible use of the time we have left.">i</a></sup>so let us get rid of the works of the darkness, and let us put on the weapons of the light.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is right, ‘weapons’. Light repels darkness. To make a difference, weapons must be used.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> Let us walk properly, as in the day, not in carousing and drunkenness, not in sexual orgies and licentiousness, not in strife and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, with a view to lusts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Of course we have to eat and drink to maintain life and health; it is the abuse of these appetites that is in view; in fact, Paul's terminology indicates ‘heavy’ abuses.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
