<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> Now I say that Christ Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Jesus” is omitted by perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts, to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding.">d</a></sup>has become a minister to the circumcised on behalf of God's truth, in order to confirm the promises to the fathers;</span>

</div>

## Paul's mission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> Now I myself am really confident about you, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish others.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Instead of “others”, some 7% of the Greek manuscripts read ‘one another’, as in most versions.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> Nevertheless I have written to you quite boldly on some points, brothers, as a reminder to you, because of the grace given to me by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> that I should be a minister of Jesus Christ to the nations, serving the Gospel of God as a priest so that the Gentiles might become an acceptable offering,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The concept is interesting: Paul is a priest, and the nations are his offering. I habitually present my own work to God as an offering.">n</a></sup>sanctified by Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> So in Christ Jesus I do have a boast in things pertaining to God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> because I do not presume to speak of anything except those things that Christ has accomplished through me to make the Gentiles obedient—by word and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> by the power of signs and wonders, by the power of God's Spirit, so that I have fully proclaimed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Note the ‘word and deed’, the ‘signs and wonders’. Can someone who has only ‘word’ claim to have ‘fully proclaimed’ the Gospel?">o</a></sup>the Gospel of the Christ from Jerusalem all the way around to Illyricum.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Illyricum lies to the north of Macedonia, in what used to be Yugoslavia (a few decades ago).">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> It has always been my aim to evangelize where Christ has not been named, so that I would not be building on someone else's foundation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> rather, as it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Those to whom He was not announced will see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who have not heard will understand.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="See Isaiah 52:15.">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## A visit to Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> That is why I have been hindered these many times from coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> But now, no longer having a place in these parts, and having a longing these many years to come to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> whenever I travel to Spain I will come to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="“I will come to you” is omitted by 4.1% of the Greek manuscripts, to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding.">r</a></sup>Because I hope to see you while passing through, and to be sent on my way there by you, if I may first enjoy your company for a while.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> Now, however, I am traveling to Jerusalem, serving the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> Because Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to make a certain contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> Yes, they were pleased, and they are indebted to them. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they are also obligated to minister to them in physical things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> So when I have finished this and have sealed to them this ‘fruit’, I will go by way of you to Spain.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="I am not aware of any record that Paul ever got to Spain.">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalms 16:11"> And I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of the Gospel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="“Of the Gospel” is omitted by 3.7% of the Greek manuscripts, to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, LB, etc.—an inferior proceeding.">t</a></sup>of the Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Now I implore you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in prayers to God on my behalf,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="If Paul needed prayer, how about us?">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> that I may be delivered from those in Judea who do not believe, and that my service to Jerusalem may be well received by the saints;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> so that I may come to you with joy by the will of God and be refreshed together with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.</span>
</div>
