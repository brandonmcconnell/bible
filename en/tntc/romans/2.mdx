## Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:3; John 8:7–9"> Now then, you there, whoever you are who judges someone else for things you practice yourself—you condemn yourself and are inexcusable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 9:7–8; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 145:17"> Further, we know that God's judgment against those who practice such things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The ‘such things’ refers back to the content of 1:29-31.">a</a></sup>is according to truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:5; Luke 12:14; Romans 1:32–1"> So then, you there, you who judge those who practice such things while doing the same, do you really imagine that you will escape God's judgment?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="What sort of blindness could this be, for such a person to imagine that he will escape judgment?">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Isaiah 30:18; Exodus 34:6"> Or do you scorn the riches of His kindness, tolerance and longsuffering, not recognizing that the goodness of God is leading you toward repentance?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Just because God is good, kind, tolerant and longsuffering, people imagine that He is weak, or even nonexistent [!]—how wrong they are!">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; Romans 1:18; Romans 2:2–3"> Rather, due to your hardness and unrepentant heart, you are treasuring up wrath for yourself in the day of God's wrath and revelation and righteous judgment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The full revelation of God's wrath and righteous judgment is going to take a lot of people by surprise. To ‘treasure up’ such wrath is incredibly stupid, if not insane.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; Jeremiah 32:19; Matthew 16:27"> who will repay each one according to his works:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Psalm 62:12 and Proverbs 24:12. Each one of us must give an accounting, based on what we did (not on what we said we believed).">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7–8; Luke 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:10"> to those who seek for glory, honor and incorruption, by persevering in doing good—eternal life;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="One is reminded of Cornelius as a case in point (Acts 10).">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:18–21; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; 1 Timothy 6:3–4"> but to those who, due to self-seeking,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The essence of sin is self. It is our egocentricity that destroys us spiritually. All babies are self-centered, as they must be in order to survive physically in this world. But as they grow they need to be taught a better way.">g</a></sup>are actually disobeying the truth (while obeying the unrighteousness)—fury and wrath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; Ezekiel 18:4; Romans 1:16"> tribulation and anguish, upon every human soul who works at the evil,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="I suppose that God is wrathful because of the damage that such people inflict upon others, at least in part.">h</a></sup>whether Jew (first) or Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 26:12; Numbers 6:26"> but glory, honor and peace to everyone who works the good, whether Jew (first) or Greek.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Working good looks to be a better deal than working evil!">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Acts 10:34; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> Now there is no favoritism with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:21; Galatians 3:10; Galatians 2:16–19"> For as many as have sinned without law will also perish without law; while as many as have sinned with law will be judged by law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="God is just, and His judgment takes into account the amount of light that a person had, but all will be judged. Our Lord's words in Luke 12:47-48 spell this out.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:22–25; 1 John 3:7; James 2:21–25"> For it is not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the law will be justified<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Over and over the Text makes clear that we have to do.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 1:19–20; Romans 2:27"> (indeed, whenever the ethnic nations that do not have law do by nature the things of the law, these, although not having law, are a law to themselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Romans 1:18–19"> who show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience also bearing witness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Everyone is born with a conscience, and with intelligence to see that some things produce a good result while others produce a bad result.">l</a></sup>and their reasonings among themselves accusing or even excusing)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Acts 17:31"> in the day when God, according to my Gospel, will judge people's secrets by Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="In John 5:22 the Lord Jesus stated plainly that the Father has committed all judgment to the Son, developing the theme in verses 23-29; see also Acts 17:31 and Revelation 19:15. That there are no ‘secrets’ unknown to God is made clear in passages like Psalm 139:1-16 and Hebrews 4:12-13. That the Day of God involves judging is clear in the OT, but it is only from the NT that we learn of the role that Jesus will play in that judging.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 2:23; Micah 3:11; Isaiah 48:1–2"> Look, you declare yourself a Jew, and rest on the Law, and boast in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Deuteronomy 4:8; James 4:17"> and know the Will, and approve the superior things, being instructed out of the Law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 3:17–18; Matthew 15:14; Isaiah 49:6"> Further, you are confident that you yourself are a guide to the blind, a light to those in darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 1:13"> an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of the immature, having in the Law the embodiment of knowledge and truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“The embodiment of knowledge and truth” is an excellent description of the Scriptures.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:27; Matthew 23:3–28; Luke 12:47"> You then, who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who preach not to steal, do you steal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 3:8; Matthew 12:39; Mark 11:17"> You who say not to commit adultery, do you adulterate? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 4:16–17; Romans 2:17; Romans 3:2"> You who boast in the Law, do you dishonor God through the transgression of the Law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:20–23; Isaiah 52:5; 2 Peter 2:2"> For, just as it is written: “The name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="See Isaiah 52:5 and Ezekiel 36:22. The problem of not practicing what one preaches has been around for quite a while.">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Circumcision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Galatians 5:3–6; Acts 7:51"> Now circumcision does have value if you keep the Law, but if you are a transgressor of the Law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 7:18–19; Romans 8:4"> So if the uncircumcised keeps the righteous requirements of the Law, will not his uncircumcision be counted as circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:41–42; Romans 2:29; 2 Corinthians 3:6"> And will not the physically uncircumcised who fulfills the law judge you, complete with written code and circumcision, who are a transgressor of the Law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Matthew 3:9; Romans 9:6–8"> Because a person is not a &#91;true&#93; Jew who is only one outwardly, nor is &#91;true&#93; circumcision something outward in the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Philippians 3:3; Deuteronomy 10:16"> but he is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is of the heart—in spirit, not letter—whose praise is not from men but from God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="God looks on the heart. Those who worship Him must do so ‘in spirit and in truth’ (John 4:23-24). We do not choose who will procreate us, nor does a baby boy choose to be circumcised, so such factors cannot guarantee God's acceptance.">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
