## Peter's death is imminent

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:21; Romans 15:14–15; Philippians 3:1"> So then, I will not neglect to keep reminding you about these things, though you know and are established in the present truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 3:1; 2 Peter 1:14; Hebrews 13:3"> Yes, I think it right to stir you up by a reminder, as long as I am in this ‘tent’,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; John 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> knowing that the shedding of my ‘tent’ is imminent, just as our Lord Jesus Christ has made clear to me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="The Lord told Peter his time was up, and evidently did the same for Paul (2 Timothy 4:6). “All the days ordained for me were written in your book before one of them came to be” (Psalm 139:16). <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; Psalms 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> Moreover I will take pains to provide a reminder of these things for you to have,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Verses 12 &amp; 13 refer to repeated reminders while he is still in his ‘tent’, which would be his own ongoing activity; so why the ‘moreover’ in verse 15?
