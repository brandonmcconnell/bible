## About having the mind of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:12; 1 John 4:12; 1 John 4:7–8"> Therefore, if there is any encouragement in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship from the Spirit, if any tenderness and acts of compassion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 1 Peter 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> make my joy full by being like-minded, having the same love, being one in spirit and purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 12:10; Galatians 5:26; Ephesians 5:21"> Do nothing out of selfish ambition or conceit, but in humility regard one another as being better than yourselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Personally I have a hard time with this one. But it may have more to do with social level and prestige within the congregation than with personal qualities and abilities.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Romans 15:1; James 2:8"> Let each of you look out not only for his own concerns, but also for what concerns others.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Agape love is the opposite of selfishness.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 2:21"> In fact, let this mindset be in you that was also in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 5:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Hebrews 1:3"> who, existing in God's form, did not consider equality with God a thing to be grasped,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Since Jehovah the Son was (and is) equally God, the point appears to be that He did not insist on hanging on to His prerogatives.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; Matthew 20:28; John 1:14"> but divested Himself, taking a slave's form, coming to be in the likeness of men.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Note that to be a human being is to be a slave. We are born slaves, live slaves and die slaves. We are born slaves to self, sin and death (with Satan behind the scenes); Christ offers us the option of being slaves to God, good and life. You can't stop being a slave, but you can choose your owner.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:18; Hebrews 12:2; Romans 5:19"> And being found in appearance as a man, He humbled Himself, becoming obedient to the point of death<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrews 5:8 says that Jehovah the Son “learned obedience by the things that He suffered”. He had never experienced anything like that before. So what happens to us if we have the same mindset?">e</a></sup>—even death on a cross!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Matthew 28:18; 1 Peter 3:22"> Indeed, that is why God highly exalted Him and gave Him the name that is above every name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; Matthew 28:18; Revelation 5:13–14"> so that at the name of Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Note that it is at the name of Jesus, not Christ or even Jehovah, that every knee will bow. ‘Jesus’ is His human name—recall that in Revelation the slain Lamb (5:6,12; 7:14; 12:11) receives glory and praise along with the Father. You cannot kill God, so the Lamb is Jesus. Recall further that in John 5:22 Jesus affirmed that the Father had “committed all judgment” to Him, which means that it will be Jesus sitting on the “great white throne” (Revelation 20:11), not to mention a certain “winepress” (Revelation 19:15). Although at the moment I see no way of ‘closing the question’ (as we say here in Brazil), I verily suspect that the correct transliteration of ‘Jesus’ into Hebrew will prove to be יֵסוּס and not יֵשׁוּעַ.">f</a></sup>every knee will bow—of those in heaven,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This presumably includes the angels, whether or not they have literal ‘knees’.">g</a></sup>those on earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Those actually alive on the earth when this decree is imposed; I suppose it will be during the millennial Messianic Kingdom.">h</a></sup>and those under the earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Since Hades is inside the earth (Matthew 12:40, Ephesians 4:9, 1 Samuel 28:13), this will presumably include the wicked dead, as well as the demons in the Abyss (at least). Just suppose that God wanted to confine evil to this planet; the logical place for a ‘prison’ would be beneath its crust.">i</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 14:11; John 13:13"> and every tongue will acknowledge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The verbs ‘bow’ and ‘acknowledge’ are in the subjunctive mood in the Text, so many versions render ‘should bow’ rather than “will bow”. However, the mood of the verbs is governed by the conjunction ινα, that normally takes the subjunctive; in the context the author evidently does not intend to leave any doubt about the extent of the recognition.">j</a></sup>that Jesus Christ is Sovereign, to the glory of God the Father.</span>
</div>

## Act like children of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–10; Luke 13:23–24; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> So then, my dear ones, just as you have always obeyed (not only in my presence but now much more in my absence), keep working out your own salvation with fear and trembling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:21; 2 Corinthians 3:5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> because God Himself is the One at work in you, both to will and to produce,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Paul places our responsibility and God's sovereignty side by side; we must consciously cooperate with Him.">k</a></sup>for His good pleasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 4:9; 1 Corinthians 10:10; Philippians 2:3"> Do all things without grumbling or arguing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="When we grumble or argue we are rejecting the Holy Spirit's control, and the result is not purity.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:12; Matthew 5:45"> so that you may become blameless and pure, children of God without fault in the midst of a crooked and depraved generation,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Can there be any doubt that our generation is crooked and depraved? And it is not getting any better.">m</a></sup>among whom you shine as light bearers in the world,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="As we both live out and actively promote biblical values, the “Word of Life”, we will stand out like a bright torch at night. The darker it is, the farther any light can be seen.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26; Galatians 2:2; 1 John 1:1"> holding fast the Word of Life, so that I may rejoice in the day of Christ that I did not run in vain nor labor for nothing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="At the Accounting, one measure of our success will be the extent to which others bought into our commitment.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6; Romans 15:16; Hebrews 13:15–16"> Yes, even if I am a libation being poured on the sacrifice and service of your faith, I am glad and rejoice with all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Philippians 4:4; Philippians 3:1"> For the same reason you also be glad and rejoice with me.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; Ephesians 3:13"> since he was longing for you all, and was distressed because you had heard that he was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; Philippians 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> Well, he really was sick, nearly to death, but God had mercy on him, and not only on him but also on me, that I might not have sorrow upon sorrow.</span>
