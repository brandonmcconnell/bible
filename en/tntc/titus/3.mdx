## What a marvelous salvation!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–10; Titus 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:10"> But when the kindness of our Savior God and His love toward mankind manifested itself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Remember John 3:16.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> He saved us—not because of righteous deeds that we ourselves had done but according to His mercy, by means of the bath of regeneration and renewal by the Holy Spirit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The regenerating and renewing are performed by the Holy Spirit. In verse 6 the “He” who does the pouring is the Father, based on the saving work of the Son—here we have an overt reference to the Trinity.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Romans 5:5; Acts 10:45"> whom He poured out on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Titus 2:11; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> so that having been justified by His grace we might become heirs according to the hope of eternal life.</span>
</div>

## So let's do good

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Titus 1:9; Psalms 16:2–3"> The above is a trustworthy statement and I want you to insist on these things, so that those who have trusted in God may be concerned to take the lead in good works.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="I take that to be the normal meaning of the Text—Christians should take the lead in their communities with projects that are for the common good.">f</a></sup>These things are good and profitable for mankind.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="If everyone in the world lived according to Biblical principles it would be a much better place! There would be no need for jails, police departments, armies, rehabilitation centers, most hospitals, etc.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; 2 Timothy 2:23; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But avoid foolish arguments and genealogies and contentions and disputes about the law, because they are unprofitable and useless.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some people like to argue for the sake of arguing, but Sovereign Jesus said we will be judged for every idle word (Matthew 12:36).">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 John 1:10; Matthew 18:15–17"> Excommunicate a divisive man after a first and second admonition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26–28"> knowing that such a person has been perverted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The passive voice indicates that the person was perverted by an outside force, in this context presumably by Satan; but he went along with the process, allowed it to happen (or he would not be “self-condemned”). <li id="fn-h"><a href="#fnref-h" className="fnb">h</a> <span className="fnr">3:9</span> Some people like to argue for the sake of arguing, but Sovereign Jesus said we will be judged for every idle word (Matthew 12:36).</li>
