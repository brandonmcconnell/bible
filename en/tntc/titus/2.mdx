<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> similarly older women are to be reverent in behavior, not slanderers nor enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is good</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> —so that they may instruct the young women<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Note that the young women are to be instructed by older women, who can do a much better job on that particular front than can male pastors.">a</a></sup>to love their husbands, to love their children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> to be prudent, pure, good homemakers, subordinating themselves to their own husbands, so that the Word of God not be maligned.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> in all things presenting yourself as an example of good works:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Again, if we are going to represent God in an official capacity, we need to be clean.">c</a></sup>showing integrity, dignity and incorruptibility<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some 17% of the Greek manuscripts omit “incorruptibility” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">d</a></sup>in your teaching</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> —healthy speech, beyond reproach—so that the opponent may be put to shame, having nothing slighting to say about us.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A significant minority of the Greek manuscripts (20%) have ‘you’ (as in AV and NKJV), but the majority (including the best line of transmission) has “us”. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 John 1:9"> not pilfering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A slave could doubtless find opportunity to pilfer, and could convince himself that he was justified in so doing. ## Saving grace

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the saving grace of God has appeared for all men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live prudently, righteously and godly in this present age,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="God's saving grace has been made available for everyone, but most people have not heard of it. If they are to hear, we must live godly lives.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> looking for the blessed hope,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="If we are to live “looking”, then we regard it as something that could happen in our lifetime (otherwise there is no point in ‘looking’).">i</a></sup>even the glorious appearing of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> who gave Himself for us, so that He might redeem us from all lawlessness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Since any ‘law’ that God promulgates derives from His character, I take the point here to be that God wants to deliver us from anything and everything that is not in conformity to His character.">j</a></sup>and purify for Himself a special people, zealous for good works.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="There is repeated emphasis on doing what is good, not just professing correct doctrine.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>

<li id="fn-k"><a href="#fnref-k" className="fnb">k</a> <span className="fnr">2:14</span> There is repeated emphasis on doing what is good, not just professing correct doctrine.</li>
