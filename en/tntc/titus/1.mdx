## They must face opposition

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:6; 1 John 4:1; Galatians 2:4"> Because there really are lots of rebels, loudmouths and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision group,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:5; Romans 3:19"> who must be silenced—they are ruining whole households, teaching things that they ought not, and for dishonest gain!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Apparently those people were not acting out of strong personal conviction, but just for material gain. Pitiful. Unfortunately, such people are still very much with us.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 1 Timothy 4:2; Acts 17:28"> One of them, a prophet of their own, said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 2:2; 1 Timothy 5:20; Titus 2:15"> This testimony is true.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Nothing like telling it like it is! (Paul had evidently not heard about being ‘politically correct’.)">m</a></sup>Therefore correct them rigorously,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Ingrained habits and cultural characteristics cannot be changed with a single rebuke; Paul is talking about a sustained effort to bring about a change in their attitudes and cultural values.">n</a></sup>that they may be sound in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:4; Colossians 2:22; Galatians 4:9"> not paying attention to Jewish fables or to commands of men who are rejecting the truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Before listening to someone, you need to check out their presuppositions.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:23"> To the pure, all things are pure, but to those who are defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure; in fact, both their minds and their consciences are defiled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="I am reminded of Matthew 6:22-23, words of the Sovereign Creator while He walked this earth: “The lamp of the body is the eye. So if your eye is sound your whole body will be full of light. But if your eye is evil your whole body will be full of darkness. So if the light that is in you is darkness, how great is that darkness!” Of course we have two eyes, but the Text has “eye” in the singular. I take it that the reference is to the way we interpret what we see (which is our real ‘eye’)—two people, one pure and one vile, observing the same scene will give very different interpretations to it. ‘Evil’ here has the idea of malignant—aggressively evil. Someone with a malignant mind will give an evil interpretation to everything he sees, and in consequence his being will be filled with unrelenting darkness. That is what it says here in Titus 1:15; to someone who is defiled nothing is pure. With a defiled mind and conscience such a person is simply incapable of giving a decent interpretation to anything at all in this whole wide world. That is why Paul goes on to say in the next verse that such a person is disqualified for any good work. Surely, if you are full of evil, how can you do good?">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; Ephesians 5:6"> They profess to know God, but by their actions they deny Him, being detestable, disobedient and disqualified for any good work.</span>
</div>
