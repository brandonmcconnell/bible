## What the Enabler will do

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 13:36; John 7:33; John 16:28"> “Now then, I am going away to Him who sent me, yet none of you asks me, ‘Where are you going?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:1; John 16:20–22; Luke 24:17"> But because I have said these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 15:26"> Nevertheless I am telling you the truth: it is to your advantage that I go away, because if I do not go away the Enabler will not come to you, but if I do go I will send Him to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus could only be physically present in one place at a time. The Holy Spirit can be in all believers all around the world all the time—clearly to our advantage.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Jude 1:15; Zechariah 12:10"> “Now when He comes he will convict the world about sin and about righteousness and about judgment:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:22–25; John 8:23–24; John 12:47–48"> first about sin, because they do not believe into me;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Since to believe into Jesus is the only cure for sin, to reject Him is the ultimate sin—you lock yourself into the consequences = spiritual death.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 10:3–4"> then about righteousness, because I am going away to my Father and you will no longer see me;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The resurrection was the proof that the Father accepted the sacrifice of the Lamb (Romans 4:25, he “was raised because of our justification”)—having committed no sin of His own, Jesus could pay for ours. If He had committed sin on His own, He could not return to the Father.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:31; 1 John 3:8; Hebrews 2:14"> then about judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="If the big boss has been judged, then no one under him will escape.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:33; John 15:15; 1 Corinthians 3:1–2"> “I still have many things to say to you but you cannot bear it now.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="We have no record that He told them those things during the 40 days before His Ascension, so it may have been left for the Spirit to do (verse 13)—although probably a lot happened during those days that is not recorded.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10–13; John 14:17"> However, when He, the Spirit of the Truth, has come, He will guide you into all the truth; for He will not speak on His own, but He will speak whatever He hears.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Whenever the Son spoke, it was really the Father speaking (John 12:50); the same is true of the Holy Spirit. The Trinity works together.">i</a></sup>He will also communicate to you the things that are coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:13–14; 2 Corinthians 3:14–18; John 15:26"> He will glorify me because He will take of what is mine and communicate it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 17:10; Matthew 11:27; John 3:35"> All that the Father has is mine;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Wow!">j</a></sup>that is why I said that He takes from what is mine and will communicate it to you.</span>

## Sorrow to joy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:33; Acts 1:3; John 14:18–24"> “In just a little you will not see me, and in another little you will see me, because I am going to the Father.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Perhaps 0.5% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit “because I am going to the Father” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:16; Mark 9:32; John 16:5"> Then some of His disciples said among themselves: “What is this that He is telling us, ‘In just a little you will not see me, and in another little you will see me,’ and because ‘I am going to the Father’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 24:25; Matthew 16:9–11; Hebrews 5:12"> So they said: “What is this ‘little’ that He mentions? We do not know what He is talking about.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="With hindsight it appears that the first ‘little’ referred to the less than 24 hours before His burial, when He would be hidden from view. The second ‘little’ would refer to the forty days between resurrection and ascension, during which they did indeed see Him.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:30; John 16:16; John 14:19"> Well Jesus knew that they were wanting to question Him and said to them: “Are you inquiring among yourselves about what I said, ‘In just a little you will not see me, and in another little you will see me’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:2; Matthew 5:4; Psalms 126:5–6"> Most assuredly I say to you that you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice; you will become grieved,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="This refers to the first ‘little’—upon His death they will really be grieved.">m</a></sup>but your grief will be turned into joy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="This refers to the second ‘little’—upon His resurrection there will be joy.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> When a woman gives birth she has pain, because her time has come; but when the child is born she no longer remembers the anguish, for joy that a human being has been born into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; Acts 13:52; John 16:6"> So in your turn you are now experiencing grief, but I will see you again and your heart will rejoice, and no one will take your joy from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 15:7; John 14:13–14; Matthew 21:22"> In that day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Perhaps the ‘day’ here is different from the one in 14:20; and yet, even after seeing the risen Lord, they still got discouraged. By the sea shore Peter still asked Him a question, so maybe the ‘day’ is the same as in 14:20. It was the coming of the Holy Spirit that gave them joy that could not be stolen.">o</a></sup>you will not question me about anything. “Most assuredly I say to you that whatever you ask the Father in my name He will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; James 4:2–3; John 15:11"> Until now you have not asked anything in my name. Ask and you will receive, so that your joy may be made full.</span>
</div>

## Figures of speech

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 8:34; John 17:24; John 17:19"> In that day you will ask in my name; I do not say to you that I will request the Father on your behalf,</span>

## “Take heart!”

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 9:44–45; John 13:38"> Jesus answered them: “Do you believe now, really?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Matthew 26:31; John 8:29"> Listen, an hour is coming, actually it has already arrived, that you will be scattered, each to his own, and you will leave me alone.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="It happened that very night.">t</a></sup>Yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 14:27; 1 John 5:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> I have spoken these things to you so that in me you may have peace. In the world you have oppression;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Just so.
