## Jesus prays

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:4–5; Philippians 2:9–11"> Jesus spoke these things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="I take it that ‘these things’ is cataphoric, referring forward to the content of the prayer.">a</a></sup>raising His eyes to Heaven and saying: For Himself “Father, the hour has come! Glorify Your Son, so that Your Son also may glorify You,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:28–29; John 6:39; Daniel 7:14"> just as You gave Him authority over all humanity, so that He will give eternal life to all those whom You have given Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here is a clear statement of divine participation in our salvation. The whole process is under the authority of the Father.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:20; 1 John 5:11–12; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> Now eternal life is this: that they may know You,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="I take it that the reference here is to experiential knowledge. ‘Eternal life’ refers mainly to a quality of life (more than longevity), and that quality is defined by relationship with the Father. The person who conceives of God as a distant, detached authority figure doesn't really know Him.">c</a></sup>the only true God, and the One whom You sent—Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This is the first recorded time that the Lord used this title, ‘Jesus Christ’ (in Matthew 16:20 there is ‘Jesus, the Christ’—as in over 95% of the Greek manuscripts). He did so when He could also say, “I have finished the work that You have given me to do” (next verse) (He still had a few hours to go, but is declaring His victory). In Acts and the epistles the title is very frequent—it affirms that Jesus is the Messiah. Also, what happens to those who only ‘know’ the Father, but not the Son?">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:34; Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:7"> I have glorified You on the earth. I have finished the work that You have given me to do.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The older I get, finishing ‘the work You have given me to do’ looms larger and larger in my mind.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:15–17; John 10:30"> So now, Father, You glorify me by Your side with the glory that I had along with You before the world existed!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="As it says in 1 Peter 1:19-20, the Lamb, with His blood shed, was foreknown before the foundation of the world. Colossians 1:16, Hebrews 1:10 and John 1:3,10 make clear that the Son was the primary agent in the creation of this planet. So Jehovah the Son is both Creator and Lamb—before He created He knew that man would rebel and He Himself would have to pay the terrible ransom. From our Lord's statement here I get the impression that as soon as the Son started dealing with this planet He gave up some of His glory. So now He is asking to have it back. Ephesians 1:20-21 makes clear that the Father granted His request, the Son is back where He was before. And He took us with Him (Ephesians 2:6)! Thank You, Lord!! Also, our Lord's statement here goes against any ‘Gnostic’ type view that has Jesus beginning as an inferior being and working up through successive levels of perfection, or what have you.">f</a></sup></span>

## For the Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 17:26; John 14:21–24; 1 John 5:20"> “I have revealed Your name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Your name” = “You”; a person's name represents that person. This is very clear in John 3:18. Jesus revealed the Father to the world.">g</a></sup>to the men whom You have given me out of the world. They were Yours and You gave them to me, and they have kept Your word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> They now know that everything You have given to me is from You;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 17:14; John 15:15; John 16:30"> because I have given them the sayings which You have given to me; and they have received and known for sure that I came forth from You,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Over and over again the Lord returns to the theme of their knowing and believing where He came from, His divine origin. So this must be important! Someone who does not believe that Jesus is God cannot be a true Christian.">h</a></sup>and they have believed that You sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> I am praying for them; I am not praying for the world but for those whom You have given me, because they are yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> Actually, all my things are Yours and Yours are mine, and I have been glorified in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> Now I am no longer in the world,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="He would still be around for another forty days, or so, but the countdown has begun.">i</a></sup>yet these are in the world, and I am coming to You. “Holy Father, keep them in Your name which You have given to me, so that they may be one just as we are.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="I imagine that this will only be fully realized after we are glorified.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> While I was with them in the world I kept them in Your name. I protected those whom You gave me, and not one of them was lost except the son of perdition, so that the Scripture might be fulfilled.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This is perhaps the clearest of a very few passages in the Bible that might be said to indicate what has been called ‘double predestination’ or ‘preterition’, that is, that someone is predestined to be lost. (I have not been able to locate a clear text to that effect, but there are indirect ones.)">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> But now I am coming to You, and I speak these things in the world so that they may have my joy made full in them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Although He was addressing the Father, Jesus evidently did so out loud—precisely so that the disciples would hear, with a view to joy.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> “I have given them Your word;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Yes! Oh praise God! That Word delivers us from Satan's kingdom in every way, including worldview. So it is only natural that Satan's ‘world’ will hate us.">m</a></sup>and the world has hated them because they are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> I am not asking that You take them out of the world, but that You keep them from the malignant one.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“The malignant one” is Satan. Satan got one of the twelve, but must not get any more of them.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 17:14"> They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="He repeats what He just said in verse 14, so it must be important. Jesus was not of this world because He literally came from Heaven, which could not be said of those disciples. But in the context of verse 15 the reference is probably to the ‘world’ dominated by Satan, the system. Their allegiance is no longer to Satan's system.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalms 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> Sanctify them by Your Truth; Your Word is Truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="The Word is what the Holy Spirit uses to sanctify us; it is His ‘sword’ (Ephesians 6:17, Hebrews 4:12).">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> Just as You sent me into the world, I also have sent them into the world.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="See John 20:21.">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> And for their sakes I sanctify myself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="To ‘sanctify’ something to God means to separate/dedicate/consecrate it for His exclusive use. While on earth Jesus concentrated His attention and energies on a single goal, to execute the Plan, the Father's will. To the extent that we pay attention to the Truth, we will do the same.">r</a></sup>so that they also may be sanctified in truth.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> In fact, the glory that You gave to me I have given to them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="What ‘glory’ was that?
