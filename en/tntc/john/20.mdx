## Disciples commissioned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> Then at evening on that first day of the week, the doors being locked where the disciples were assembled, for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the middle, and He says to them, “Peace to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> And upon saying this He showed them His hands and His side. Well, the disciples were overjoyed when they saw the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 17:18–19; John 13:20; John 3:17"> So Jesus said to them again: “Peace to you!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This is what He said “again”, having already said it (verse 19).">k</a></sup>Just as the Father sent me, I also send you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="This is tremendous! So how did They do it? The Father determined and the Son obeyed: “Behold, I have come to do your will, O God” (Hebrews 10:7). And what was that will?
