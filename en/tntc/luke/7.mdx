## The faith of a centurion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> Now when He concluded all His sayings in the hearing of the people, He entered Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> Well a certain centurion's slave, who was valuable to him, was sick and about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> So when he heard about Jesus, he sent elders of the Jews to Him, asking Him to come and rescue his slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> And when they came to Jesus they started pleading earnestly with Him, saying that the one for whom He would do this was worthy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> “Because he loves our nation, and he himself built our synagogue.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> So Jesus went with them. But when He was no longer far from the house, the centurion sent friends to Him, saying to Him: “Lord, do not trouble yourself, for I am not worthy that you should come under my roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Psalms 33:9; Luke 4:36"> In fact I did not even consider myself worthy to come to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This event is not the same as that recorded in Matthew 8:5-13. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> because I also am a man placed under authority,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="He was part of a chain of command, with people both above and beneath him. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> Well upon hearing these things Jesus marveled at him, and turning to the crowd following Him, He said, “I say to you, not even in Israel have I found such great faith!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="If Jesus were here today, He could say the same thing about almost all churches.">c</a></sup></span>

## A widow's son raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> Now it happened on the next day that He went to a town called Nain, and many of His disciples went with Him, also a large crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> But as He approached the town gate, mercy, a dead man was being carried out, the only son of his mother, and she was a widow; a large crowd from the town was with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 86:15"> When the Lord saw her He had compassion on her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="She needed compassion; her situation was desperate, with no means of support.">d</a></sup>and said to her, “Don't cry!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> And advancing He took hold of the bier and the bearers stood still. Then He said, “Young man, to you I say, get up!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jesus was always specific when He brought someone back from the dead, so as not to empty the graveyard.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> So the dead man sat up and began to talk! Yes, He gave him back to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> Fear took hold of all, and they began glorifying God, saying, “A great prophet has arisen among us!” and, “God has visited His people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> And this report about Him went throughout Judea, as well as all the surrounding region.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Nain is in Galilee, but besides the surrounding region the report of this particular miracle made it to Judea as well.">f</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">

## Jesus praises John

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> Now when John's messengers had departed, He began to speak to the crowds about John: “What did you go out into the wilderness to observe, a reed being shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> But what did you go out to see, a man clothed in soft garments? Really, those with gorgeous apparel and living in luxury are in palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> But what did you go out to see, a prophet? Yes, I say to you, and much more than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> This is he about whom it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Take note, I am sending my messenger before your face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will prepare your way before you.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See Malachi 3:1.">j</a></sup></span>

## Inconsistency of the Pharisees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> “To what then shall I compare the men of this generation, and to what are they similar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They are like children sitting in the marketplace and calling to one another, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘We played the flute for you, and you did not dance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we mourned to you, and you did not cry.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> Because John the Baptizer came neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and you say, ‘He has a demon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> The Son of the Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, ‘Just look, a glutton and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> Still, by all her children wisdom is justified.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="In other words, the Pharisees were not children of ‘wisdom’.">n</a></sup></span>

## A lesson in forgiveness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> Then one of the Pharisees invited Him to eat with Him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="This was not a friendly invitation.">o</a></sup>so He entered the Pharisee's house and reclined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> But then, a woman in the town who was a sinner, when she found out that He was reclining in the Pharisee's house, she brought an alabaster flask of perfume,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> and as she stood behind Him at His feet weeping,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="He was reclining on a ‘couch’ with His head in toward the table, so His feet were ‘available’.">p</a></sup>she began to wet His feet with her tears and kept wiping them with the hair of her head; and she kept kissing His feet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="To do all of this she would have to be kneeling. She started by standing, and probably waited a minute to see if anyone would object.">q</a></sup>and anointing them with the perfume.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="This is a moving picture. She was ashamed and distressed, because of what she was, but she desperately wanted to change, and she saw in Jesus the way out.">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> Now as the Pharisee who had invited Him observed this, he was saying to himself, “If this man were a prophet, he would know who is touching him, including what sort of woman she is—because she is a sinner!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Evidently the woman was well known to the locals.">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> So Jesus reacted by saying to him, “Simon, I have something to say to you.” And he said, “Teacher, say on.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> “A certain creditor had two debtors. One owed five hundred denarii, and the other fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> And when they had no way to repay, he freely forgave them both. Now tell me, which of them will love him more?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> So Simon answered and said, “I suppose the one to whom he forgave more.” And He said to him, “You have judged correctly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> Then He turned toward the woman and said to Simon: “Do you see this woman? I entered your house; you gave me no water for my feet, but she has wet my feet with her tears and wiped them with the hair of her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> You gave me no kiss, but she has not stopped kissing my feet since the time I came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> You did not anoint my head with oil, but she has anointed my feet with perfume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> For this reason, I say to you, her many sins have been forgiven, because she loved much; but to whom little is forgiven, the same loves little.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> Then He said to her, “Your sins are forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> The other recliners began to say within themselves, “Who is this who even forgives sins?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> Then He said to the woman, “Your faith has saved you; go into peace.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="That is what the Text says, ‘into’ not ‘in’—from then on her life would be different, one characterized by peace. She got what she so desperately wanted.">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
