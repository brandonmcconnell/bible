## The purpose of parables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 4:10; Matthew 13:10; Matthew 13:18"> Then His disciples asked Him saying, “What does this parable mean?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; Mark 4:11; Matthew 11:25"> So He said: “To you it has been given to know the mysteries of the Kingdom of God, but to the rest only in parables, so that ‘seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Isaiah 6:9.
