## Parable of a lost sheep

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> Then all the tax collectors and ‘sinners’ were getting close to Him to hear Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> Well the Pharisees and the scribes started complaining, saying, “This man welcomes ‘sinners’ and eats with them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So He told them this parable, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> “What man among you, having a hundred sheep and losing one of them, does not abandon the ninety-nine in the wild<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Notice that the 99 are left in the wild, not a sheepfold. So what happens if a wolf comes upon the 99 while the shepherd is off looking for the stray? I confess that I never liked this parable, for that reason.">a</a></sup>and go after the lost one until he finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> And upon finding it he joyfully puts it on his shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> And when he comes home, he calls together his friends and neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep that was lost!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> I say to you that likewise there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous ones who have no need of repentance.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="If God prefers sinners, what is the point of being righteous? I suppose the point to be that no one is so righteous that they need no repentance. Since we are all sinners, we all need to repent—and when we do so, God is happy.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Parable of a lost coin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> “Or what woman having ten silver coins, if she loses one coin, does not light a lamp, sweep the house, and search carefully until she finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> And when she finds it, she calls together her friends and neighbors saying, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin that I lost!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> Just so, I say to you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner who repents.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="God rejoices in the presence of the angels.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Parable of a lost son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> Then He said: “A certain man had two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> And the younger one said to the father, ‘Father, give me the appropriate share of the estate.’ So he divided the property between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> Not many days later the younger son, having converted it all, journeyed to a distant country; and there he squandered his wealth living dissolutely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> But when he had spent it all, there was a severe famine throughout that country, and he began to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> Then he went and attached himself to a citizen of that country, who sent him into his fields to feed pigs.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="For a Jew, that would be about as low as one could get.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> He would gladly have filled his stomach with the carob pods<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="I suppose those pods were not edible for a human being, or he could doubtless have managed a few.">e</a></sup>that the pigs were eating, but no one gave him anything.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Besides the economic crisis, he was a foreigner, and a stupid one at that. Why throw good money after bad?">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> But when he came to himself he said: ‘How many of my father's hired servants have an abundance of bread, while I am dying of hunger!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> I will get up and go to my father and say to him: Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> and I am no longer worthy to be called your son; make me as one of your hired servants.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> “So he got up and went to his own father. But while he was still a good ways off, his father saw him and was moved with compassion—he ran and fell on his neck and kissed him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> Then the son said to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you, and I am no longer worthy to be called your son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> But the father said to his slaves: ‘Bring out the best robe and put it on him, and put a ring on his hand and sandals on his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> And bring in and kill the fatted calf, and let us eat and celebrate;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> because this son of mine was dead and came to life; he was lost and is found!’ So they began to celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> “Now his older son was in the field; and as he came approaching the house he heard music and dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So he called one of the servants and asked what these things meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> He said to him, ‘Your brother has come, and your father has killed the fatted calf, because he has received him back safe and sound.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> So he became angry and refused to go in. Then his father came out and began to plead with him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Doubtless the servant had informed the father.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> But in answer he said to the father: ‘Just look at how many years I have been serving you without ever transgressing your commandment; yet you never gave me even a young goat that I might celebrate with my friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> But when this son of yours came, who has devoured your livelihood with prostitutes, you killed the fatted calf for him!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The older brother's attitude really wasn't all that unreasonable. Why should the younger son be rewarded for his irresponsible conduct? The father was celebrating the recovery, not the irresponsibility. The fatted calf was for a special occasion, and the older son's faithful service was routine. However, he probably did get a young goat, after that.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> So he said to him: ‘Son, you are always with me, and all that I have is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> But it was right that we celebrate and be glad, because this brother of yours was dead and came to life; he was lost and is found.’ ”</span>
</div>
