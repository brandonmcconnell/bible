<li id="fn-y"><a href="#fnref-y" className="fnb">y</a> <span className="fnr">6:48</span> Perhaps 1.5% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, read “because it was built well”, instead of “because it was founded on the bedrock” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.). If the bedrock refers to the Lord Jesus and His teaching, then this alteration is bad.</li>
<li id="fn-z"><a href="#fnref-z" className="fnb">z</a> <span className="fnr">6:49</span> Notice the change in tense: ‘hears and does’ changes to ‘heard and did nothing’. The second person heard the Truth, but rejected it, and so is without excuse.</li>
</ol>
