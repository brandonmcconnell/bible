<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 10:28; Revelation 14:7"> Rather, I will advise you whom you should fear—fear Him who after He kills has authority to cast into Gehenna;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="‘Gehenna’ was a euphemism for Hell, or the Lake of Fire. ## Parable of a rich fool

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:12–14; Hosea 2:8; Genesis 41:47–49"> Then He told them a parable, saying: “The ground of a certain rich man produced well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Isaiah 58:7; Romans 12:13"> And he reasoned within himself saying, ‘What shall I do, because I have no place to store my crops?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:15; James 4:15; Luke 12:21"> Then he said: ‘This is what I will do. I will tear down my barns and build bigger ones, and there I will store all my produce and my goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; James 4:13–15; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> And I will say to my soul: Soul, you have many goods laid up for many years. Take life easy; eat, drink and enjoy yourself!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:8; Jeremiah 17:11; 1 Timothy 6:7"> But God said to him: ‘Stupid! This very night your life is being taken back from you;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="‘Taken back’—we have our life on loan, like a trust, and an accounting will be required.">i</a></sup>then who will get the things you have prepared?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Luke 12:33; James 2:5"> Just like that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Just like that” = “Stupid!” To be rich toward God is to invest in His Kingdom.">j</a></sup>is he who accumulates treasure for himself and is not rich toward God.”</span>
</div>

## The mentality of the Kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 13:5"> Then He said to His disciples: “Therefore I say to you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat; nor about the body, what you will wear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 2:6; Proverbs 13:8; Job 2:4"> Life is more than food, and the body more than clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:31; Psalms 147:9; Luke 12:7"> Consider the ravens: they neither sow nor reap, they have neither storeroom nor barn, and God feeds them. You are far more valuable than the birds!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:27; Matthew 5:36; Luke 19:3"> And who among you can add one cubit to his height by worrying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalms 39:6; Ecclesiastes 7:13"> So if you cannot do even such a little thing, why worry about the rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:28–30; Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> “Consider the lilies, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin; I say to you, not even Solomon in all his splendor was clothed like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Isaiah 40:6; Matthew 6:30"> So if God so clothes the grass that today is in the field and tomorrow is thrown into an oven, how much more you, O little-faiths!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 6:31; Luke 12:22; Luke 22:35"> Do not concentrate on what you may eat or what you may drink; do not be anxious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Matthew 6:8; Ephesians 4:17"> All these things really occupy the nations of the world, and your Father knows that you need them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 34:9"> Rather, concentrate on the Kingdom of God, and all these things will be provided for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Note that what is promised is just the basics—food, drink, covering. Everything we invest in the Kingdom will be on deposit in heaven.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:28; 1 Peter 1:3–5; Revelation 1:6"> “Do not fear, little flock, because it pleased the Father to give you the Kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; Acts 4:34–35; Acts 2:45"> Sell your possessions and give to charity. Make for yourselves ‘purses’ that will not wear out, an unfailing treasure in the heavens, where a thief cannot approach, nor a moth destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Matthew 6:21; Philippians 3:20"> Because where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="There is the crucial question: Where is your treasure?">l</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Watchfulness enjoined

<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jude 1:20–21; James 5:7–8; Revelation 3:20"> and you yourselves like men waiting for their master when he returns from the wedding celebration, so that when he comes and knocks they may open to him immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Peter 3:14; Matthew 24:42; John 13:4–5"> Blessed are those slaves whom the master will find watching when he comes. Assuredly I say to you that he will gird himself and have them recline, and will come and serve them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="He is doubtless in a very good mood, and for his servants to have stayed awake for hours when they wanted to be in bed, just so they could receive him when he came—he probably figures they need some refreshment.">m</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 16:15"> But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would have kept watch and not allowed his house to be broken into.</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Blessed is that slave whom his master will find so doing when he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 22:29–30; Matthew 24:47; Luke 19:17–19"> I tell you truly that he will put him in charge of all his possessions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="He was director of food services; if he proved faithful and competent there, he would be made general director.">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 24:48–50; Revelation 18:7–8; Philippians 3:18–19"> But if that servant should say in his heart, ‘My master won't come for a while,’ and should begin to hit the male and female servants, and to eat and drink and get drunk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 12:40; Matthew 24:51; Psalms 11:5"> the master of that slave will come on a day when he does not expect him, and at an hour that he does not know, and will cut him in two and appoint his portion with the unbelievers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="What is involved here is two deaths: physical (cut in two) and spiritual—the ‘portion’ of unbelievers is the Lake of Fire.">q</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 3:1; Leviticus 5:17; Luke 16:10–12"> But he who did not know, yet did things worthy of blows, will be beaten with few.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Note that even sins done in ignorance are punished. However, the more one knows, the more severe the punishment.">r</a></sup>Everyone to whom much has been given, from him much will be required; and to whom much was entrusted, of him much more will be asked.</span>

## Christ causes division

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Luke 12:51–52; Matthew 3:10–12"> “I came to bring fire to the earth, and how I wish it were already kindled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 12:27–28; John 19:30; John 7:6–8"> But I have a baptism to undergo, and how distressed I am until it is completed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:34–36; Luke 12:49; Matthew 24:7–10"> Do you imagine that I came to provide peace on the earth? Not at all, I tell you, but rather division.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="So why is He called the ‘Prince of Peace’? <span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Acts 14:1–4; John 15:18–21; Micah 7:5–6"> Because from now on there will be five in one house divided: three against two and two against three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Matthew 10:21–22; Matthew 24:10"> Father will be divided against son and son against father, mother against daughter and daughter against mother, mother-in-law against her daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="This is precisely true.">t</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 16:2–4; 1 Kings 18:44–45"> Then He also said to the crowds: “Whenever you see a cloud rising from the west, immediately you say, ‘A rainstorm is coming,’ and so it does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Job 37:17; Matthew 20:12"> And whenever a south wind blows, you say, ‘It will be hot,’ and it happens.</span>
