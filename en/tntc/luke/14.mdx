## Jesus heals a man with dropsy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalms 62:4"> Then it happened, when He entered the house of one of the rulers of the Pharisees to eat bread on the Sabbath, that they were watching Him closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And then, there in front of Him was a man who had dropsy!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It seems scarcely credible that such a person could have gotten in there by accident. The Pharisees were desperately looking for some way to accuse Him.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> And Jesus reacted by saying to the lawyers and Pharisees, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they kept silent. So He took hold of him, healed him, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> Then He addressed them saying, “Which of you, if a son<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Instead of ‘son’, some 26% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘donkey’ (as in TR, AV, NKJV). The 74% includes the best line of transmission, which I follow.">b</a></sup>or an ox falls into a pit, will not immediately pull him out on the Sabbath day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they could not answer Him regarding these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A lesson about humility

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> Then He told a parable to those who were invited, having observed how they kept trying for the best places, saying to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> “Whenever you are invited by someone to a wedding feast, do not recline in the place of honor, in case someone more honorable than you has been invited by him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> then he who invited you both will come and say, ‘You, give this man place!’ and then, with shame, you start to take the lowest place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Of course; everyone else has been trying for the best place he can get, so by that time the only one left is the lowest!">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> Rather, whenever you are invited, go and recline in the lowest place, so that when your host comes, he may say to you, ‘Friend, move up higher.’ Then you will have honor in the presence of your fellow guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> Because everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and he who humbles himself will be exalted.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This truth is repeated several times in Scripture.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> Then He said directly to His host: “Whenever you give a dinner or a supper, do not invite your friends, nor your brothers, nor your relatives, nor rich neighbors, lest they also invite you back, and you be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But whenever you make a banquet, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, the blind;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> and you will be blessed, because they cannot repay you—you will be repaid at the resurrection of the righteous.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="You cannot take it with you, but you can send it on ahead.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## An unusual banquet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> Well when one of the fellow-recliners heard these things, he said to Him, “Blessed is he who will eat dinner<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Instead of ‘dinner’, perhaps 20% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘bread’ (as in most versions).">f</a></sup>in the Kingdom of God!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Now there you have a nice, safe, pious-sounding statement! I suppose he was trying to change the subject.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> So He said to him: “A certain man prepared a great banquet and invited many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> And at meal time he sent his slave to say to those who were invited, ‘Come, because everything is now ready.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> But they all alike began to make excuses. The first said to him: ‘I bought a field, and I need to go and see it. I ask you to have me excused.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Another said: ‘I bought five yoke of oxen, and I am going to test them. I ask you to have me excused.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> Yet another said, ‘I have married a wife, and so I cannot come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> So that slave came and reported these things to his master. Then the owner of the house became angry and said to his slave, ‘Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in here the poor and crippled and blind and lame.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the slave said, ‘Master, what you ordered has been done, and there is still room.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> Then the master said to the slave: ‘Go out to the roads and hedges<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hedges were used to delimit fields, and there would often be a path along the outside of the hedge. First the slave was sent into the city, then out to the countryside.">h</a></sup>and make people come in, so that my house may be filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> For I tell you that none of those men who were invited will get a taste of my banquet!’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Perhaps 45% of the Greek manuscripts add, “for many are called, but few chosen”.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus defines discipleship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> Now large crowds were traveling with Him, and turning He said to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> “If anyone comes to me and does not hate his father and mother, wife and children, brothers and sisters, yes, and even his own life, he cannot be my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> And whoever does not carry his cross, and come after me, cannot be my disciple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> “Further, which of you, intending to build a tower, does not sit down first and calculate the cost, whether he has enough to complete it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> This so he does not lay a foundation without being able to finish, and all who see it begin to ridicule him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> saying, ‘This man began to build and was not able to finish!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> “Or what king, going to engage another king in battle, does not sit down first and consider whether he is able with ten thousand to meet the one coming against him with twenty thousand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> And if not, while the other is still far away he sends a delegation and asks for terms for peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> “So likewise, any of you who does not renounce all his own possessions cannot be my disciple.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Notice that three times Sovereign Jesus says, “cannot be my disciple”, and the ‘cannot’ is a strong negative. Jesus demands first place in our lives and hearts—above family, material things, our very selves.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> Salt is good; but should the salt become insipid, with what can it be seasoned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is fit for neither soil nor fertilizer; it is thrown out.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="We followers of Christ are supposed to be ‘salt’ and ‘light’ in this world. To abdicate these functions is to become useless, and to be thrown out. ‘Neutrality’ is not a valid option.">k</a></sup>He who has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span>
</div>
</div>
