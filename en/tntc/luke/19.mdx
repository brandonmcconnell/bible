## Zacchaeus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> He entered and started passing through Jericho.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This would be the new Jericho.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> A man named Zacchaeus was there—he was a chief tax collector, and he was wealthy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> He was trying to see who Jesus was, but could not because of the crowd, because he was short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> And running on ahead he climbed up into a sycamore tree, in order to see Him, since He was about to pass by there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> And when Jesus came to the place, He looked up and saw him, and said to him, “Zacchaeus, come down quickly, because today I must stay at your house.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jesus called him by name. How did He know that? Might that have contributed to the man's conversion?">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> So he came down quickly, and received Him joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> But when they saw it, they all started complaining, saying, “He has entered to lodge with a sinful man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> Then Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, “Indeed, Lord, I herewith give half of my goods to the poor, and if I have extorted anything from anyone, I restore fourfold.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The man knew his Bible; see Exodus 22:1.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> So Jesus said about him, “Today salvation has happened in this house,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Yes indeed, that was a complete change of direction!">d</a></sup>in that he also is a son of Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> because the Son of the Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost.”</span>

## Parable of a despised king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> Now as they heard these things, He continued with a parable, because He was near Jerusalem and they were supposing that the Kingdom of God was on the verge of being revealed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This seems to have been a fixation in their minds that kept them from understanding the Lord's repeated warnings that He was about to be killed.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> So He said: “A certain nobleman went to a distant country to receive for himself a kingdom and return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> He called ten of his slaves, gave them ten ‘minas’, and said to them, ‘Conduct business until I come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his citizens hated him and sent a delegation after him, saying, ‘We do not want this man to reign over us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> It happened that he did receive the kingdom, and upon returning he ordered that those slaves to whom he had given the money be called to him, to know who had gained what by trading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> So the first one came and said, ‘Master, your mina earned ten minas!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="How long would it take to multiply an investment ten times? Probably quite a while! That the man had to go to a distant country strikes me as unusual, and after he got there he may not have received the kingdom right away. The parable clearly relates to our Lord's absence and Return. We need to do the best we can with what He has given us.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> He said to him: ‘Super, you good slave! Because you were faithful in a very little, have authority over ten cities!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> And the second one came and said, ‘Master, your mina made five minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> Likewise he said to him, ‘You also be over five cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> Then a different one came, saying: ‘Master, here is your mina, that I kept laid away in a piece of cloth</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> —I feared you because you are a hard man, removing what you did not place, and reaping what you did not sow.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> So he said to him: ‘I will judge you out of your own mouth, you wicked slave! You knew that I am a hard man, removing what I did not place, and reaping what I did not sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> Why then didn't you put my money on deposit, and upon coming I would have collected it with interest?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> And to those standing by he said, ‘Take the mina from him and give it to him who has the ten minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> (They even said to him, ‘Master, he already has ten minas!’)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> ‘Indeed I tell you, to everyone who has, more will be given;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="There is a sense in which blessing is earned; the more you do, the more you get. In John 12:26 Sovereign Jesus said, “If anyone serves me, my Father will honor him.”">g</a></sup>but from him who does not have, even what he has will be taken from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> But as for those enemies of mine who did not want me to reign over them, bring them here and slaughter them in front of me!’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The man had accepted the description given by the third slave, and here he demonstrates that he really is a ‘hard’ man, but don't forget that he rewarded the faithful slaves. And don't forget Revelation 19:15!">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The ‘Triumphal Entry’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> Having said these things, He went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> When He drew near to Bethphage<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some 44% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘Bethphage’ (as in most versions), but the 56% includes the best line of transmission, which I follow. In any case, it is a mere matter of spelling.">i</a></sup>and Bethany, at the mountain called ‘of Olives’, it happened like this: He sent two of His disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> saying: “Go to the village opposite you, where as you enter you will find a colt tied, on which no man ever yet sat. Untie and bring it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> And if anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ just say this to him: ‘The Lord needs it.’ ”</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> saying: “If you, yes you, especially in this your day, had known the things pertaining to your peace! But now they are hidden from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> Yes, the days will come upon you when your enemies will build an embankment around you; indeed they will surround you and hem you in on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> And they will raze you, and your children within you, to the ground—they will not leave in you one stone upon another—all because you did not recognize the time of your visitation.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The implication would appear to be that if they had recognized it, their history would have been different.">l</a></sup></span>
