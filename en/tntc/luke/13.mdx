<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; Lamentations 2:20; Ezekiel 9:5–7"> Now at that time there were some present who told Him about the Galileans whose blood Herod had mixed with their sacrifices.</span>

## Parable of a barren fig tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Isaiah 5:1–4; Galatians 5:22"> Then He told this parable: “A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard, and he came looking for fruit on it and found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Luke 3:9; John 15:6"> So he said to the gardener: ‘Look, for three years I have come looking for fruit on this fig tree and have not found any. Cut it down; just why must it waste the soil?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 3:9; Joel 2:17; Romans 10:1"> But in answer he said to him, ‘Sir, let it be this year also, until I dig around it and apply fertilizer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Hebrews 6:8"> in case it produces fruit—but if not, then cut it down.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Lord's use of ‘three years’ is doubtless deliberate; He may well have been referring to His own ministry in Israel. In that event the owner would be the Father, and He Himself would be the gardener. The three years of public ministry He had already spent had not produced the desired fruit.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Jesus heals a woman bound by Satan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Luke 4:44; Luke 4:15–16"> Now as Jesus was teaching in one of the synagogues on the Sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Luke 8:27; Luke 13:16"> He saw a woman there who had been crippled by a spirit for eighteen years—she was bent over and could not straighten up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Matthew 8:16; Luke 6:8–10"> So He called her over<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In a synagogue the women were separated from the men, so He called her over to the men's side.">c</a></sup>and said to her, “Woman, you are loosed from your infirmity!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 107:20–22; Mark 16:18; Mark 5:23"> He placed His hands on her and immediately she was made straight and started glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:9; Matthew 12:2; John 9:14–16"> But the ruler of the synagogue reacted with indignation, because Jesus had healed on the Sabbath, and he said to the crowd, “There are six days in which one should work; therefore come and be healed on them, and not on the Sabbath.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Of course the woman had not come to be healed; she came to worship God. It was the Lord's initiative, so the ruler's complaint was really directed at Him.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 14:5; John 7:21–24; Acts 13:9–10"> So the Lord answered him and said: “Hypocrites! Does not each one of you on the Sabbath loose his ox or donkey from the stall and lead it away to water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 2:27; 2 Timothy 2:26; Luke 19:9"> So ought not this woman, being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="By using the enemy's proper name, Jesus is declaring that it was actually Satan who was behind the problem.">e</a></sup>kept bound during eighteen long years, be freed from this bond on the Sabbath day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Psalms 132:18; Isaiah 45:24"> When He said these things all His adversaries were put to shame, while all the crowd was rejoicing over all the glorious things that were being done by Him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The narrow gate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Matthew 9:35; Mark 10:32–34"> He was traveling through various towns and villages, teaching and journeying toward Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:14; Matthew 22:14; John 21:21–22"> Then someone said to Him, “Lord, are the ones being saved few?” So He said to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27"> “Exert yourselves to enter through the narrow gate, because many, I say to you, will try to enter and not manage it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 25:10–12; Luke 13:27; Matthew 7:21–23"> When once the master of the house gets up and shuts the door, and you begin to stand outside and knock on the door saying, ‘Lord, Lord, open to us!’ and in answer he will say to you, ‘I do not know you; where are you from?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 58:2; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> then you will begin by saying, ‘We ate and drank in your presence, and you taught in our streets.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 6:8; Matthew 25:41; Matthew 7:22–23"> But he will say: ‘I tell you, I do not know where you are from. Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Lord is stating plainly that there are many who think they are in the Kingdom, but actually are not.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:11–12; Matthew 22:13; Matthew 25:30"> “There will be weeping there, and gnashing of teeth, when you see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets in the Kingdom of God, but you yourselves being thrown out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 43:6; Isaiah 66:18–20; Isaiah 49:6"> They will even come from the east, the west, the north and the south, and recline in the Kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 20:16"> Yes indeed, there are last who will be first, and there are first who will be last.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A word for Herod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9–11; Psalms 11:1–2; Matthew 14:1"> On that same day some Pharisees approached saying to Him, “Get out of here and go away, because Herod wants to kill you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Those Pharisees may just have been trying to scare Jesus. The metaphor ‘fox’ was doubtless understood by His hearers, but we have since lost the relevant cultural information.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 5:9; John 17:4–5"> He said to them: “Go tell that ‘fox’, ‘Take note, I am casting out demons and performing cures today and tomorrow, and on the third I will be perfected.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Matthew 21:11; Acts 13:27"> Nevertheless I must keep going today and tomorrow and the day following, because it is not credible that a prophet should perish outside of Jerusalem!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus laments over Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 23:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:11–12; Acts 7:52"> “Jerusalem, Jerusalem, she who kills the prophets and stones those who are sent to her! How often I wanted to gather your children together, like a hen gathers her brood under her wings, but you were not willing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:26; Mark 11:9–10; Matthew 21:9"> So then, your house is left to you desolate. I tell you that you will definitely not see me until the time comes when you say, ‘Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="See Psalm 118:26. John 10:22-39 probably happened between verses 33 and 34 here. That ‘Feast of Dedication’ would have fallen on a Monday, December 17, 29. </div>
