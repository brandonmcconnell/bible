## A lesson in giving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 12:41–44; John 8:20; Matthew 27:6"> Looking up He saw the rich putting their gifts into the treasure chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 12:42"> But He also saw a certain poor widow putting in two mites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3"> So He said: “I tell you truly that this poor widow put in more than all of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:44–45; Luke 15:12; Acts 4:34"> because all these others put in gifts for God out of their abundance, but she, out of her destitution, put in all she had to live on.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It is a fact of life; people who are poor are often more generous, more willing to share, than those who are rich. ## Things will get bad, really bad

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then He said to them: “Nation will be raised up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> And there will be severe earthquakes in various places, and famines and pestilences; and there will be fearful events and great signs from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But before all these things, they will lay their hands on you and persecute you, delivering you up to synagogues and prisons, bringing you before kings and governors on account of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> But it will turn out for you as an occasion for testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> So settle it in your hearts not to practice giving a defense beforehand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> because I will give you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="To do this He has to be alive!">d</a></sup>a mouth and wisdom such that all your adversaries will not be able to contradict or withstand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This was true of Stephen; they couldn't answer him, so they killed him.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 12:53; Revelation 6:9; Micah 7:5–6"> You will be betrayed even by parents and relatives, friends and ‘brothers’; and they will put some of you to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:19; Luke 6:22; John 15:21"> And you will be hated by all because of my name.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This is already happening around the world. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 10:30; 1 Samuel 14:45; Luke 12:7"> (Not even one hair of your head will be wasted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="I have to take this by faith.
