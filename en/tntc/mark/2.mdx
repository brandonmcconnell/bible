<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:18–26; Matthew 9:1–8; Matthew 4:24"> Then four men came, carrying a paralytic to Him.</span>

## Jesus is Lord of the Sabbath2:23 Between verses 22 and 23 all of John chapter 5 takes place—that chapter revolves around the second Passover of His public ministry, in 28 ad A year and a half have passed since His baptism.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–8; Deuteronomy 23:24–25; Luke 6:1–5"> Now it happened, on a Sabbath, that He was passing through some grain fields, and His disciples began to make a path, picking the heads of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:10; Hebrews 12:3; Matthew 12:2"> So the Pharisees said to Him, “Just look, why are they doing on a Sabbath that which is not permitted?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 10:26; Matthew 22:31"> And He said to them: “Did you never read what David did when he was in need and hungry, he and those with him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; Leviticus 24:5–9; 1 Samuel 21:1"> How he entered the house of God (making Abiathar high priest)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="My rendering is rather different than the ‘in the days of Abiathar the high priest’ of the AV. For a complete discussion please see the Appendix: Abiathar is not Ahimelech.">l</a></sup>and ate the consecrated bread, which only priests are permitted to eat, and shared it with those who were with him?”</span>
