## The end of the age

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> Then as He was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="I take the point to be that He could see the temple from where He was sitting.">b</a></sup>Peter, James, John and Andrew asked Him privately:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> “Tell us, when will these things be? And what will be the sign whenever all these things are about to be fulfilled?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="There are two questions: “When these things?” and “What the sign?” The answer to the first is given in Luke 21:20-24. The answer to the second begins here in verse 5.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> So by way of an answer to them Jesus began by saying: “Take care that no one deceives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> Because many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he,’ and will deceive many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> But whenever you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not let it distress you; because happen they must, but the end is not yet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> Because nation will be raised against nation,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The verb is in the passive voice, so the necessary question is, who does the raising?">d</a></sup>and kingdom against kingdom; and there will be earthquakes in various places, and there will be famines and tumults.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="In our day we have seen violence and looting in the wake of earthquakes and food shortages. Perhaps 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit ‘and tumults’ (to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">e</a></sup>These things are the beginning of birth pains.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Text has ‘birth pains’, so what is the ‘baby’? I suppose the ‘baby’ is the Messianic Kingdom.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> “But watch out for yourselves, because they will deliver you up to councils and flog you in synagogues, and you will be brought before rulers and kings for my sake, as a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> Also, the Gospel must first be proclaimed to all the ethnic nations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See the footnote at Matthew 24:14.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> Now whenever they lead you away and deliver you up, do not worry beforehand nor premeditate what you will say;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="In Seminary I was taught that this instruction is for martyrs, not preachers! But churches today would be better off if their pastors got their messages from the Holy Spirit.">h</a></sup>rather, whatever is given to you in that hour, speak that, because it will not be you doing the speaking, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> “Further, brother will betray brother to death and a father his child, and children will rise up against parents and cause them to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> And you will be hated by all for my name's sake,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="It is already the case in many places that anyone who takes a public stand in defense of biblical values is vituperated by the media—some have gone to prison in what once were ‘Christian’ countries.">i</a></sup>yet he who endures to the end, he will be saved.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="We must endure persecution, without denying the Lord—if you deny Him, He will deny you.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The Great Tribulation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> “Now whenever you see the ‘abomination of desolation’, the one spoken of by Daniel the prophet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Perhaps 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit ‘the one spoken of by Daniel the prophet’, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">k</a></sup>standing where he should not”—let the reader understand—“then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> Let him who is on the housetop not go down into the house, nor enter to take anything out of his house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="I confess that I cannot imagine a situation where a few seconds would make any difference. Evidently the attack will be sudden.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Let him who is in the field not return to his things to get his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> But woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing babies in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And pray that your flight not take place in winter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="I find this instruction to be curious. Is the Lord really saying that by praying we can influence the time of year when the ‘abomination’ will take place? I wonder.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> because those will be days of affliction such as there has not been the like from the beginning of the creation that God created until now,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The Lord Jesus Christ affirms that God created this world; anyone who embraces the evolutionary hypothesis of origins is calling Him a liar.">n</a></sup>and never again shall be.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="There has always been affliction and distress in the world, but not like what is coming.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> If the Lord did not cut those days short, no one would be saved; but for the sake of the elect, whom He chose, He did shorten the days.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="The tense of the verb is definitely past. The Lord is evidently referring to something that has already been determined.">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> “Then if anyone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ ‘Look, there!’ do not believe it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> because false christs and false prophets will be raised up,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Again, the voice is passive; I suppose it is Satan that does the raising up.">q</a></sup>and they will show signs and wonders so as to even mislead the elect, if that were possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> So you watch out! See, I have told you everything in advance.</span>
</div>

## The return of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> “But in those days, after that tribulation, ‘the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> the stars of heaven will be falling, and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="See Isaiah 13:10, 34:4. The reference is presumably to angelic beings, not literal stars (just one would demolish the planet).">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> And then they will see the Son of the Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Note that this Return will be visible, in contrast to the Rapture (as I understand it).">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> And then He will send His angels and gather together His elect from the four winds, from earth's extreme point to heaven's extreme point.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="I have difficulty understanding this statement, if it is to be taken literally. That the elect will be collected from all over the world, no problem, but from all over heaven? However, since resurrected saints will participate in the administration of the Messianic Kingdom (1 Corinthians 6:2, 2 Timothy 2:12, Revelation 5:10, 20:6), perhaps they are included in this statement.">t</a></sup></span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> “Now learn this example from the fig tree: whenever its branch becomes tender and sprouts leaves, one knows that the summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> Even so you also, whenever you see these things happening, know that it is near—at the doors!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> Assuredly I tell you that this generation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="“This generation” could refer to the Jewish race (render ‘this race’), but I think it more probable that it refers to the generation that “sees all these things”. <span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> The heaven and the earth will pass away, but my words will certainly not pass away.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Jesus affirms that His words have eternal validity/authority.">v</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Watch! Keep alert! Pray!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Since the Return draws ever closer, of necessity, we, of all people, should be doing this. <span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> Therefore be watchful, because you do not know when the master of the house is coming—at evening or at midnight or at cockcrow or early morning—</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> lest coming suddenly he find you sleeping.</span>
