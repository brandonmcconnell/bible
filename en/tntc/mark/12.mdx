<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> At the proper time he sent a slave to the farmers, that he might receive from them some of the fruit of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> But they took him, beat him, and sent him away empty-handed.</span>

## Render to Caesar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> Then they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The reference is presumably to the leaders mentioned in 11:27. They were hoping to be able to denounce Jesus to the Governor, who was Caesar's representative.">d</a></sup>sent some of the Pharisees and of the Herodians to Him, in order to trap Him with a question.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> Upon arriving they said to Him: “Teacher, we know that you are honest—you do not ‘protect’ anyone, because you do not consider the position of people, but teach the way of God in truth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="They were really almost too obvious.">e</a></sup>—is it right to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> Should we pay, or should we not?” But knowing their hypocrisy, He said to them: “Why are you testing me? Bring me a denarius to look at.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> So they did, and He said to them, “Whose image and inscription is this?” They said to Him, “Caesar's.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> Then Jesus answered them by saying, “Render to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="We are to do both: tithe and pay taxes. ## A hypothetical situation12:18 If you ever have to deal with someone who wants to argue on the basis of a hypothetical situation—be careful! Like these Sadducees, they will have a hidden agenda.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Then some Sadducees came to Him—they say there is no resurrection—and asked Him, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man's brother dies and leaves behind a wife, but no children, then his brother should take that wife and raise up offspring for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> Well there were seven brothers. The first took a wife, and dying left no offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the second one took her, and died; and neither did he leave any offspring. The third likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In fact, all seven took her, and left no offspring. Last of all the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, whenever they may rise, whose wife will she be?—because all seven had her as wife.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Then in answer Jesus said to them: “You do not know the Scriptures nor the power of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="What a sad commentary! <span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> Whenever people rise from the dead, they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in the heavens.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jesus does not say that angels do not have gender. <span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of dead people, but the God of living ones. ## The greatest commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> Then one of the scribes came on the scene, heard them arguing, realized that He had answered them well, and asked Him, “Which is the first commandment of all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus answered him:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="I take it that Jesus answered without hesitation—this one was easy.">j</a></sup>“The first of all the commandments is: ‘Hear, O Israel, the Lord your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="I follow the best line of transmission in reading ‘your’, albeit some 75% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘our’, as in all versions.">k</a></sup>(pl) God, the Lord is one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> and you (sg) shall love the Lord your (sg) God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 6:4-5. The ‘love’ here cannot be limited to emotion, obviously. We are to love God with our whole being, all we have. But how does that work? Love for God is demonstrated by obedience, so all we do is to be oriented by God's will.">l</a></sup>This is the first commandment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Just over 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit ‘this is the first commandment’ (to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> And the second, like it, is this: ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="See Leviticus 19:18.">n</a></sup>There is no other commandment greater than these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> So the scribe said to Him: “Well said, teacher; you said truly that He is one, and there is no other except He.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And to love Him with all the heart and with all the understanding and with all the soul<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Perhaps 2% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit ‘and with all the soul’ (to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">o</a></sup>and with all the strength, and to love the neighbor as oneself, is more than all the whole burnt offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> Now when Jesus saw that he answered wisely, He said to him, “You are not far from the Kingdom of God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Quite right—all the man had to do was put it into practice.">p</a></sup>And after that no one dared ask Him any question.</span>

## Is the Christ David's son?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> Then, teaching in the temple, Jesus reacted by saying: “How is it that the scribes say that the Messiah is David's son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> Because David himself said by the Holy Spirit:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Sit at my right hand, until I place your enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as a stool for your feet.” ’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="See Psalm 110:1.
