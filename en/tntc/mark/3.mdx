<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> So they watched Him closely, whether He would heal him on the Sabbath, so that they might accuse Him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> Then He said to them: “Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill?” But they remained silent.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> from Jerusalem, from Idumea and beyond Jordan; even those around Tyre and Sidon. A huge crowd came to Him, having heard the sorts of things He kept doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> So He told His disciples that a small boat should be kept ready for Him because of the crowd, lest they should press in on Him.</span>

## The Twelve chosen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> He went up on the mountain and summoned those whom He wanted, and they came to Him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> He appointed twelve,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Less than 2% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, add ‘whom He also named apostles’, presumably imported from Luke 6:13, to be followed by NIV, LB, TEV, etc.">e</a></sup>that they might be with Him and that He might send them out to preach</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> —also to have authority to heal sicknesses and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Perhaps 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit ‘to heal diseases and’, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">f</a></sup>to cast out demons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> namely Peter (a name He gave to Simon);</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> James son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James (and a name He gave to them was Boanerges, that is, ‘Sons of thunder’);</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> Andrew, Phillip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Cananite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed Him.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> Then they went into a house;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This may well have been His own house in Capernaum. <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> Well upon hearing this His family came to apprehend Him, because they were saying, “He is out of his mind!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Go down to verse 31 for more about this.">h</a></sup></span>

## Scribes blaspheme the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> Then some scribes who had come down from Jerusalem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="They had come all the way to Galilee, just to combat Jesus.">i</a></sup>started saying, “He has Beelzebul,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="All Greek manuscripts have ‘Beelzebul’, rather than the familiar ‘Beelzebub’, that is a carryover from the Latin.">j</a></sup>and “It is by the ruler of the demons that he casts out demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> So summoning them He started saying to them in parables: “How can Satan cast out Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalms 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> And if a household is divided against itself, that household cannot stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Satan has risen up against himself and become divided, he cannot stand, but is finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> No one can plunder the strong man's goods,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Since the definite article occurs with ‘strong man’ the first time the phrase occurs, the entity has already been introduced, so the reference is to Satan. Here is a biblical basis for binding Satan, which is now possible because of Christ's victory. Hebrews 2:14 informs us that Jehovah the Son took on human form to destroy the devil, while 1 John 3:8 affirms that He was manifested to undo the works of the devil. But in John 20:21 the resurrected Jesus said, “As the Father has sent me, so send I you”, and not long after that He returned to the Father. He defeated Satan alright, but it is up to us to ‘undo the works’.">k</a></sup>invading his house, unless he first binds the strong man—then he may plunder the house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> “Assuredly I say to you: all the sins of the sons of men can be forgiven, including whatever blasphemies they may utter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit never has forgiveness, but is subject to eternal condemnation”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Perhaps 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, read ‘sin’ instead of ‘condemnation’, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> —because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Those scribes committed the unpardonable sin. They said that the Holy Spirit was Satan; that His work was Satan's. Are there not those in our day who have done the same thing?">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
