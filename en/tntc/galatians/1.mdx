<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brothers with me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="It would be interesting to know who those “brothers” were.">c</a></sup>to the congregations in Galatia:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“The congregations in Galatia”—Paul evidently intended that the letter be widely circulated. ## Paul is disappointed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I am sadly surprised that you are turning away so quickly from the one who called you by the grace of Christ, to a different gospel</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> —it is not a mere variation, but certain people are unsettling you and wanting to distort the Gospel of the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> Now even if we, or an angel out of heaven, should preach any other gospel to you than what we have preached to you, let him be accursed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As we have just said, I here emphatically repeat: If anyone preaches any other gospel to you than what you have received, let him be accursed!!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="‘Other gospels’ would seem to be in plentiful supply; those who promote them are under a curse. Note that Paul is claiming to be competent to define the only true Gospel of Christ, and he can only do so genuinely by divine inspiration.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> Am I just now appealing to men, or to God?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Since it is God who applies the curse, he is appealing to God to back him up. I myself do this habitually.">h</a></sup>Or am I trying to please men? You see, if I were still pleasing men I would not be a slave of Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Oops! Come on Paul, you cannot be serious. Do you really mean that pleasing men and being a slave of Christ are mutually exclusive? On the basis of my experience I would have to agree with Paul, with the understanding that fellow slaves are not included in the ‘pleasing men’. (When I am pleasing Christ His other slaves should be pleased as well.)">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> Now I want you to know, brothers, that the Gospel preached by me is not according to man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> because I did not receive it from any man, nor was I taught it; rather it came through a revelation from Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="I follow what I understand to be the best line of transmission (though in a minority here, 30%) that reads ‘Christ’ without ‘Jesus’. I take it that ‘Christ’ is in the ablative case: ‘from’ rather than ‘of’. Paul is claiming revelation—this is the plain meaning of verses 11-12. Saul of Tarsus got his training in Judaism at the feet of Gamaliel, but his understanding of Christianity came from no such human teacher.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## How he was before

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> You have heard of my former conduct while in Judaism, how I was rabid in my persecution of God's Church and tried to annihilate it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> indeed I was advancing in Judaism beyond many Jews of my own age, being far more zealous for the traditions of my forefathers.</span>

## How he was recycled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when God—who set me apart from my mother's womb and called me through His grace—resolved</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> to reveal His Son in me so that I might proclaim Him among the nations/Gentiles, I did not start by consulting with flesh and blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before I was; rather I went off into Arabia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Do you suppose he went to Mt. Horeb?">k</a></sup>and then returned to Damascus.</span>
</div>

## A new life and ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Subsequently, after three years,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The Text does not say that he spent three years in Arabia; part of the time he was in Damascus.">l</a></sup>I went up to Jerusalem to compare notes with Peter,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="98% of the Greek manuscripts have “Peter”, not ‘Cephas’. In 2:1 below it is 93%.">m</a></sup>and stayed with him fifteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> (I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Note that this James, though not one of the Twelve, is declared to be an apostle.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> Really, before God, I am not lying in what I write to you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> So I remained unknown by face to the congregations of Judea (those in Christ)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Every town with a synagogue would have a local congregation, but usually not “in Christ”.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> —they just kept hearing that “He who once persecuted us now proclaims the faith he formerly tried to destroy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> And they glorified God about me.</span>
