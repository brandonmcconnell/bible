## Boast in the cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> See with what large letters I have written to you with my own hand.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This verse, coupled with 4:15 above, gives credence to the idea that Paul's physical infirmity had to do with his eyes.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> Those who want to make a good showing in the flesh are pressuring you to be circumcised, but only so as not to be persecuted for the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> For not even those who are circumcised keep the law themselves, yet they want you to be circumcised that they may boast in your flesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Again Paul makes clear that he is referring to the religious exercise when practiced on adults, not the physical fact (that is properly practiced on eight-day-old babies).">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> But as for me, may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> Because in Christ Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit “because in Christ Jesus” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">i</a></sup>neither circumcision nor uncircumcision avails anything, but a new creation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="If you are not a new creation, you are not saved.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> Now as for those who conform to this rule, peace and mercy be upon them, and upon the Israel of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="According to Greek grammar, the repetition of the preposition ‘upon’ in two phrases joined by ‘and’ makes clear that the objects of the prepositions refer to distinct entities.
