Who has bewitched you that you not be persuaded by the Truth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Perhaps 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit “that you not be persuaded by the Truth” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.), which weakens the statement considerably.">a</a></sup>—Jesus Christ was clearly portrayed among you as having been crucified, before your very eyes!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="It almost sounds like there was a dramatic demonstration.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> Just tell me one thing: Did you receive the Spirit by works of law, or by hearing of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> Can you be so foolish? Having begun in spirit, do you finish in flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Have you suffered so much for nothing—if it really was for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> Furthermore, He who supplies the Spirit to you and works miracles among you, is He activated by works of law, or by hearing of faith?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Although I have capitalized the pronoun, there must have been a human being involved as well.">c</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> just as Abraham ‘believed God and it was credited to him as righteousness.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This is a quote from Genesis 15:6, but is not presented as such.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> Now you must know that only those who are of faith are ‘sons’ of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> Further, the Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the nations by faith,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The form of the verb is ambiguous as to mood; it could be either Indicative or Subjunctive. ## The curse of the law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> Now as many as are ‘by works of law’ are under a curse; because it stands written, “Cursed is everyone who does not continue in all the things written in the Book of the Law, to do them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This is a direct quote from Deuteronomy 27:26.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> Further, it is evident that no one is justified by law before God, because “the righteous one will live by faith.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This is a quote from Habakkuk 2:4.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> While the law is not of faith but, “the man who does them will live by them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This is a quote from Leviticus 18:5.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law, having become a curse in our place—because it stands written, “Cursed is everyone who is hung on a tree”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="This is a direct quote from Deuteronomy 21:23.">j</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> so that the blessing of Abraham might come to the nations/Gentiles through Christ Jesus; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through the faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The covenant with Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brothers: as a man I speak a covenant; though only of a man, once it has been ratified no one annuls or adds to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> But to Abraham were spoken the promises,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Paul draws a contrast between a hypothetical covenant he might speak as a man, and the promises actually spoken to Abraham and his Seed.">k</a></sup>and to his Seed. He does not say “and to seeds,” as about many, but “and to your seed,” as about one, who is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> Further, I say this: a covenant confirmed beforehand by God in Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Less than 3% of the Greek manuscripts, of inferior quality, omit “in Christ” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.). Without Christ, the Seed, there would have been no promise. The incarnation of Jehovah the Son was in the Plan before the creation of this world (1 Peter 1:19-20).">l</a></sup>cannot be annulled by the law that came four hundred and thirty years later,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Abraham left Haran in 1921 bc, when he was 75. The Exodus occurred in 1491 bc. 1921 – 1491 = 430. For an explanation of these dates see the excellent discussion by Floyd Nolen Jones in his Chronology of the Old Testament: A Return to the Basics, Kings Word Press, 1999 (14th edition).">m</a></sup>so as to make the promise of no effect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> For if the inheritance were of law, it would no longer be of promise, but God granted it to Abraham by promise.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The law was a guardian

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> Why then the law? It was added on account of the transgressions, until the Seed should come to whom the inheritance had been promised; and it was ordered through angels by the hand of a mediator.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“By the hand of a mediator” would presumably refer to Moses, since he passed the Law on to the people, but the reference to “angels” puzzles me. The OT account has God dealing directly with Moses, or so it seems to me. Perhaps God used angels to communicate some of the lesser details, or to refresh Moses' memory.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> (Now that mediator is not for just one, but God is one.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="I take this verse to be parenthetical because it does not seem to contribute to the argument. The word ‘mediator’ that ends verse 19 gives rise to the parenthetical comment, because Paul then used the definite article with the same word at the beginning of verse 20, which is why I render “that mediator”. Paul then states the obvious—for someone to be a mediator there must be at least two parties at odds—which he contrasts with something even more obvious—God is one. Perhaps the point is that which lies behind the question in verse 21: since God is the source of both Law and Promise, and He being one cannot be at odds with Himself, then Law and Promise cannot be at odds. Moses functioned as mediator between God and the people.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> Is the law then against the promises of God? Of course not! Rather, if a law had been given that was capable of imparting life, truly righteousness would have been by the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> But the Scripture imprisoned everything under sin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="“Everything”, not ‘everyone’—that is what the Text says. Romans 8:19-22 spells it out—the consequences of Adam's fall extended to and included the creation. How could a perfect world be administered by a fallen man?">p</a></sup>so that the promise by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> Now before this faith came, we were kept in custody under the law, in confinement until the coming faith was revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> So then, the law became our guardian until Christ, so that we might be justified by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> Now that the faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> So all of you are sons of God through the faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> As many of you as were baptized into Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="See 1 Corinthians 12:13.">q</a></sup>have clothed yourselves with Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> —there is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no ‘male and female’;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The Text does not have ‘neither male nor female’; the formula changes, as I have indicated. I suppose the reference is to Genesis 1:27, and the reason for the female in Genesis 2:18. All are saved on the same basis.">r</a></sup>all of you are one in Christ Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="The reference is to the spiritual realm, not the physical—a Jew who believes into Jesus does not stop being a physical Jew, a slave who believes into Jesus does not automatically change social status, a male who believes into Jesus does not stop being a physical male, etc. But if we are all “clothed with Christ”, verse 27, we will all look alike, at least from a distance. People looking at us should think they are looking at Him.">s</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> so if you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's seed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Abraham's seed, not Jacob's.">t</a></sup>and heirs according to the promise.</span>
</div>
