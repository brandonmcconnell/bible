<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Colossians 1:23; Acts 17:2"> On the Sabbath day we went outside the city by a river, where prayer was customarily made,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Instead of “where prayer was customarily made”, perhaps 2% if the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, have ‘where we supposed a place of prayer to be’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">i</a></sup>and sitting down we started speaking to the assembled women.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Evidently there was no synagogue in Philippi. ## Prison

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> Now it happened, as we were going to prayer, that a certain slave girl having a spirit of divination met us, who brought considerable profit to her owners by divination.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Peter 2:16; Mark 5:7"> Following Paul and Silas<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="I follow the best line of transmission in reading ‘Silas’; some 60% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘us’, as in most versions. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19; Acts 3:6"> She kept this up for many days!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The ‘hour of prayer’ was observed every day by God-fearing Jews. <span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Acts 21:30; Acts 8:3"> But when her owners saw that their hope of profit was gone, they seized Paul and Silas and dragged them into the market place to the rulers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="This was a put up job. <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 4:4; John 15:18–20; Acts 28:22"> and bringing them before the magistrates they said, “These men, being Jews, are agitating our city,</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:9; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> When they had laid many stripes on them, they threw them into prison, charging the jailer to keep them securely;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 33:11; Jeremiah 29:26"> who, having received such a charge, threw them into the inner cell and fastened their feet in the stocks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Their backs are bleeding from ‘many stripes’ and their feet are in stocks (you try to sit up so as not to lie on your wounded back; all of which gets pretty ‘old’ pretty fast)—a great time for a praise meeting!">p</a></sup></span>

## The jailer converts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:16–18; Psalms 119:62; James 1:2"> Well about midnight Paul and Silas were singing hymns to God in prayer; and the other prisoners were listening to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:31; Acts 5:19; Acts 12:10"> Suddenly there was a great earthquake, enough to shake the foundations of the prison; immediately all the doors were opened and everyone's chains were loosed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="A proper earthquake could shake the doors open, but not unlock chains (unless it was their anchors that came loose from the walls).">q</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:12; Acts 2:38–39; John 3:36"> So they said, “Believe upon the Lord Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Perhaps 3% of the Greek manuscripts, of inferior quality, omit ‘Christ’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">u</a></sup>and you will be saved, you and your household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Colossians 1:27–28; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> Then they spoke the Word of the Lord to him and to all who were in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Acts 16:15; Acts 16:25"> In that same hour of the night he took them aside and washed their wounds, and thereupon he and all his family were baptized.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Very appropriate—he washed their wounds and restored their dignity, so they could baptize him. <span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Acts 2:46; Romans 5:11"> Then he brought them into his house and set a meal before them, and he was really rejoicing, having believed in God with his whole family.</span>

## Magistrates rebuked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 76:10; Acts 5:40"> Now when it was day the magistrates sent the officers saying, “Let those men go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 15:33; John 14:27; 1 Samuel 1:17"> So the jailer reported these words to Paul, “The magistrates have sent to release you; so now you can leave and go in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 22:25–29; Psalms 58:1–2; Matthew 10:16"> But Paul said to them:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="The officers were still there. No way! Rather, let them come themselves and escort us out!”</span>
