## Back to Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:23–28; 1 Corinthians 16:12; 1 Corinthians 1:12"> Now it happened that while Apollos was at Corinth, Paul, having passed through the upper country, came to Ephesus. Disciples of John<br />And finding certain disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:39; Acts 11:15–17; Galatians 3:5"> he said to them, “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?” So they said to him, “But we have not so much as heard that there is a Holy Spirit!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 8:16"> So he said, “Into what then were you baptized?” So they said, “Into John's baptism.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Perhaps they were the result of some of Apollos' early ministry. Paul evidently sensed that there was something lacking in them.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:29–34; Acts 11:16; Acts 1:5"> So Paul said, “John indeed baptized with a baptism of repentance, saying to the people that they should believe into the One who would come after him, that is, into Jesus, the Christ.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts omit ‘the Christ’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> There were about twelve men in all.</span>

## The school of Tyrannus becomes the base

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 28:23; Acts 1:3; Acts 9:20–22"> During three months Paul kept going to the synagogue and speaking boldly, reasoning and persuading concerning the things of the Kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 19:23; Acts 9:2; Acts 18:6–8"> But when some became hardened and disobedient, maligning the Way before the crowd, he withdrew from them and separated the disciples, reasoning daily in the school of a certain Tyrannus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Paul had already done this sort of thing in Corinth; it was a good strategy.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 20:31; 2 Timothy 1:15; Acts 16:6"> Now this continued for two years, so that all who lived in Asia, both Jews and Greeks, heard the Word of the Lord Jesus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Perhaps 13% of the Greek manuscripts omit ‘Jesus’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 16:17–20; Acts 5:12; Romans 15:18–19"> Further, God kept working unusual miracles by the hands of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; Mark 16:17"> so that even handkerchiefs or aprons that he touched were applied to the sick, and the diseases left them and the wicked spirits went out from them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This sort of thing is definitely ‘news’, and it would spread far and wide.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Some fake exorcists

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 12:27; Mark 9:38; Luke 11:19"> Well some among the itinerant Jewish exorcists attempted to invoke the name of the Lord Jesus over those who had wicked spirits saying, “We adjure you by the Jesus whom Paul preaches.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> In fact there were seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, who were doing this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 16:17–18; Luke 4:33–35; Mark 1:34"> But a particular wicked spirit reacted and said, “Jesus I know, and I am acquainted with Paul, but who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 8:29; Mark 5:3–4; Mark 5:15"> And the man in whom the wicked spirit was jumped on them and overpowered them; he was so much stronger that they ran out of that house naked and wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:12; Acts 5:5; Philippians 2:9–11"> Now this became known to all, both Jews and Greeks, who lived in Ephesus; and fear fell on them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was being exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:10; Psalms 32:5; Matthew 3:6"> And many of those who had believed started coming, confessing and disclosing their practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> In fact, many of those who had practiced magic brought their books together and burned them up, in front of everybody<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="When people start doing this, you know their faith is real, and to do it in public would really have an impact on the populace (that was a lot of money!).">g</a></sup>(they had calculated their value and it totaled fifty thousand pieces of silver).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> With power like that it was that the word of the Lord kept growing and prevailing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:22; Acts 20:16; Acts 23:11"> Now after these things were accomplished, Paul resolved in his spirit to go to Jerusalem, passing through Macedonia and Achaia, saying, “After I have been there, I must also see Rome.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="His apostolic spirit wanted not only Rome, but Spain (Romans 15:28).">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 16:3"> So he sent two of those who ministered to him, Timothy and Erastus, on to Macedonia, while he himself stayed on in Asia for a time.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Demetrius

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 24:22; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> Now it was during that time that a serious disturbance concerning the Way occurred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Acts 16:16; Acts 16:19"> A certain man named Demetrius, a silversmith, who made silver shrines of Artemis, habitually brought in plenty of business for the artisans;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; Revelation 18:11–19"> he called them together, along with the workmen in related trades, and said: “Men, you know that our prosperity depends on this trade.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 17:29; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> And you observe and hear that not only in Ephesus but throughout almost all Asia this Paul has persuaded and turned away many people, saying that handmade things are not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; 1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 23:13"> So not only is this trade of ours in danger of falling into disrepute,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The crucial point was the threat to their finances, not that to Diana's reputation!">i</a></sup>but also the temple of the great goddess Artemis may be discredited, and even her own majesty be destroyed, whom all Asia and the world worship.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Revelation 17:13; Acts 16:19–24"> Now upon hearing this they were filled with anger and began shouting, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Colossians 4:10; Acts 27:2"> So the whole city was filled with confusion, and they rushed into the theater with one accord (they had seized Gaius and Aristarchus, Macedonians, Paul's traveling companions).</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; Acts 17:22–31"> When Paul wanted to go in to the people, the disciples would not let him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:10; Acts 21:12; Acts 16:6"> Even some of the officials of Asia, being his friends, sent word to him and urged him not to venture into the theater.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="After two years of fruitful ministry, some of the city officials would have converted, or at least appreciate the healing and deliverance he had brought.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Acts 19:40; Matthew 11:7–9"> The assembly was in confusion: some were shouting one thing and some another; most of them did not know why they had come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 12:17; 2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Then the Jews pushed Alexander forward,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="I wonder whether those Jews were friends or foes, and what they hoped to achieve.">k</a></sup>out from the crowd. So Alexander motioned with his hand and would have made a defense to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:28; Matthew 6:7; Revelation 13:4"> But when they realized he was a Jew, they all shouted in unison for about two hours,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="How could their voices last for two hours? And what animated them to keep on going for so long? I suspect there was a little demonic participation.">l</a></sup>“Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:2; Ephesians 2:12"> When the city clerk had quieted the crowd, he said: “Men of Ephesus, what man is there who does not know that the city of the Ephesians is temple guardian of the great goddess Artemis and of what fell from Zeus?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="They had an image that reputedly had fallen intact from the sky.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Acts 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> Therefore, since these things are undeniable, you ought to be quiet and not do anything rash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Romans 2:22; Acts 25:8"> You have arrested these men who are neither temple thieves nor blasphemers of your goddess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:7; 1 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 18:14"> Now if Demetrius and his fellow artisans have a complaint against anyone, courts are available, and there are proconsuls; let them bring charges against one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But if you want to debate any other matter, it will be settled in the legal assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 17:5–8; Matthew 26:5; Acts 20:1"> Indeed, we are in danger of being charged with a riot, because of today, there being no reason at all that we can give to account for this commotion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:17; Psalms 65:7; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> And having said these things he dismissed the assembly.</span>
</div>
</div>
