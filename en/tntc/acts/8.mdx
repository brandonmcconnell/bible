## Simon, the sorcerer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> Now a certain man named Simon was in the city first, practicing sorcery and astounding the people of Samaria, affirming himself to be someone great,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> to whom they used to pay attention, from the least to the greatest, saying, “This man is the great power of God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is what the Text says. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> They listened to him because he had amazed them with his sorceries for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But, when they believed Philip, as he proclaimed the good news concerning the Kingdom of God and the name, Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="He was proclaiming Jesus as the Messiah.">e</a></sup>they were baptized, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Even Simon himself believed, and having been baptized he stayed right with Philip; observing miracles and signs occurring, he was amazed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Why? </div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> Now when the Apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them,</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> because He had not yet fallen upon any of them; they had only been baptized into the name of Jesus, the Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some 30% of the Greek manuscripts read ‘Lord’ instead of ‘Christ’, as in most versions.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then they laid their hands upon them and they received Holy Spirit.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> Well, upon observing that the Holy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="‘Holy’ is omitted by two (2) manuscripts (against over 600), to be followed by NIV, NASB, TEV, etc. Those two are ancient, but of objectively inferior quality.">h</a></sup>Spirit was given through the laying on of the Apostles' hands, Simon offered them money</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> saying, “Give this power to me also, that anyone on whom I lay hands may receive Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter said to him: “May your silver go with you into perdition,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Peter is really upset and does not mince words; he evidently felt that the sin was so serious as to place Simon's eternal destiny in doubt (see verse 22, “in case”). <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> You have neither part nor portion in this matter, because your heart is not right before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Turn away therefore from this your wickedness and make petition to God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some 5% of the Greek manuscripts read ‘Lord’ instead of ‘God’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">j</a></sup>in case the intent of your heart may be forgiven you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> for I see you being into a gall of bitterness and a fetter of unrighteousness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The Text has ‘into’ not ‘in’, thereby emphasizing (I suppose) that this was a long-standing condition. <span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> So in answer Simon said, “You<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The Text is emphatic. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> So when they had both<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="This word goes with the verbs ‘testify’ and ‘speak’, not Peter and John. </div>

## The Ethiopian treasurer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Then an angel of the Lord spoke to Philip saying, “Get up and go south on the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="From Samaria to the Gaza road would be a walk of at least 60 miles, over accidented terrain. Notice that God removes Philip from a very successful ministry to reach one man (albeit a very strategic one).">n</a></sup>it is desolate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> So he got up and went; and there, a man, an Ethiopian, a eunuch, a court-official of Candace the queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasury, who had come worshiping to Jerusalem<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="So how did this man know about Jehovah, and why had he learned to read Hebrew (unless he was reading a Greek translation)? <span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> —he was returning, sitting in his chariot and reading aloud the prophet Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> And the Spirit said to Philip, “Go forward and join this chariot.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> Now as they were going down the road they came upon some water, and the eunuch said: “Look, water! What is keeping me from being baptized?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The AV and NKJV have verse 37: Then Philip said, “If you believe with all your heart, you may.” And he answered and said, “I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.” It is the sort of thing that makes us think, “If Philip did not say that, he should have”. <span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> Now when they came up out of the water, Spirit of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Again there is no definite article with ‘Spirit’, nor with ‘Lord’—I would like to translate ‘Spirit of Jehovah’, but God's personal name never occurs in the New Testament.">s</a></sup>snatched Philip away, and the eunuch did not see him any more, because he went on his way rejoicing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="He did not stop to look for Philip or attempt any further contact with him; Ethiopia was a long way away and he just kept on going.
