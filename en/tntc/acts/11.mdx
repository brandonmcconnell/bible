## Peter has to explain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 22:27; Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 32:15"> Now the Apostles and the brothers throughout Judea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Talk about news spreading like wildfire; well this news was really ‘hot’!">a</a></sup>heard that the Gentiles had also received the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 10:9; Acts 15:1"> When Peter went up to Jerusalem, those of the circumcision party started contending with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Galatians 2:12; Luke 15:2"> saying, “You went in to uncircumcised men and ate with them!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In Matthew 28 the resurrected Jesus had commanded that disciples be made among all ethnic nations—so how can you disciple someone without talking to him or associating with him? In fact, the ‘Great Commission’ imposed a significant shift in worldview upon Jesus' Jewish followers. The transition was not easy, and many never made it.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Acts 14:27; Joshua 22:21–31"> So Peter began by explaining it to them in order, saying:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 10:9–32; Acts 9:10; Acts 22:17"> “I was in the town of Joppa praying, and in a trance I saw a vision: a certain object like a great sheet coming down out of the sky, being lowered by its four corners; and it came to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 4:20; Acts 3:4"> Upon peering into it I observed and distinguished the quadrupeds of the earth—both the wild animals and the reptiles—and the birds of the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then I heard a voice saying to me, ‘Get up, Peter, slaughter and eat!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Hosea 9:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> So I said: ‘No way, Lord! Never has anything ‘common’ or unclean entered my mouth!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:9; Hebrews 9:13–14; Acts 10:34–35"> But the voice answered me, a second time, from heaven, ‘Things that God has made clean you must not call ‘common’.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 21:17; Numbers 24:10; 2 Corinthians 12:8"> Well this was done three times, and it was all pulled back up into the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 4:27; Acts 9:10–12; Exodus 4:14"> At that very moment three men stopped in front of the house where I was, having been sent from Caesarea to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Romans 3:22; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> The Spirit told me to go with them, doubting nothing. These six brothers also went with me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Peter presumably knew he would be called on the carpet, so he took his witnesses along.">c</a></sup>and we entered the man's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:30–32; Acts 10:3–6; Acts 9:43"> He then related to us how he had seen the angel standing in his house and saying to him, ‘Send to Joppa and summon Simon, who is called Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:15; Acts 10:22; Acts 16:31–34"> who will speak words to you by which you and all your household will be saved.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 10:34–46; Acts 2:2–12"> Well as I began to speak,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Peter was set to give a proper discourse, but the Spirit intervened.">d</a></sup>the Holy Spirit fell on them, just as on us at the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Mark 1:8; Proverbs 1:23"> I remembered the word of the Lord, how He said, ‘John indeed baptized with water, but you will be baptized with Holy Spirit.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Acts 1:5. This was spoken by the Lord Jesus just before His ascension.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:47; Romans 9:20–26; Daniel 4:35"> So if God gave them the same gift as He gave us when we believed upon the Lord Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Notice that there is no mention of keeping the Law, being circumcised, or following Jewish customs. Peter is saying that what God did indicates that the key to salvation is believing upon the Lord Jesus, whether Jew or Gentile. I follow the best line of transmission that reads ‘the Lord Jesus’; some 75% of the Greek manuscripts add ‘Christ’.">f</a></sup>who was I to be able to withstand God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 36:26"> Upon hearing these things they acquiesced and started glorifying God, saying, “So then, God has also granted to the Gentiles repentance into life!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Their religious culture had twisted the OT Scriptures so as to exclude Gentiles. So now they are surprised.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Enter Barnabas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 21:2; Acts 15:3"> Now those who had been scattered by the persecution that came about in connection with Stephen traveled as far as Phoenicia, Cyprus and Antioch, speaking the word to no one except Jews only.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This was before the Cornelius episode.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 1:23–24"> But there were some of them, men of Cyprus and Cyrene, who upon entering Antioch started speaking to the Greek-speakers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="In verse 19, those who spoke to Jews only, presumably used Hebrew, which would not be understood by outsiders. In verse 20, by preaching in Greek, the whole populace could understand, even if Greek-speaking Jews were the main target. Verse 21 implies that the number of believers exceeded the number of Jews, so Gentiles also were believing.">i</a></sup>preaching the good news of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:66; Acts 2:47; Acts 5:14"> And the hand of the Lord was with them, and a great number believed and turned to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:27; Acts 8:14; Acts 13:1–3"> Well the report concerning them reached the ears of the church in Jerusalem, and they sent out Barnabas to go as far as Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:43"> Upon arriving and seeing the grace of God, he was glad, and he kept encouraging them all to remain true to the Lord with purpose of heart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Barnabas gives us a good example to follow.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 5:14; Acts 9:31"> (for he was a good man, full of Holy Spirit and of faith). And a considerable crowd was added to the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 9:11; Acts 9:27; Acts 9:30"> Then Barnabas departed for Tarsus to look for Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; Acts 26:28; 1 Peter 4:14"> and upon finding him he brought him to Antioch. So it was that for a whole year they met with the congregation and taught a great many people. The disciples were first called Christians in Antioch.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Enter Agabus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Ephesians 4:11"> Now in these days, prophets came from Jerusalem to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 21:10; Acts 18:2; Luke 3:1"> One of them, named Agabus, stood up and indicated by the Spirit that a great famine was about to come upon the whole world (this indeed happened while Claudius was Caesar).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12–14; Galatians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–4"> Then the disciples, to the extent that any had plenty, each of them determined to send relief to the brothers who lived in Judea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:23; Acts 12:25; Acts 15:4"> which they also did, sending it to the elders by Barnabas and Saul.</span>
</div>
