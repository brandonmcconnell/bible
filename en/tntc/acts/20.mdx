<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1; Colossians 1:28"> When he had gone through those parts and encouraged them with many words, he came into Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:19; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 9:23–24"> When he had stayed three months, as he was about to set sail for Syria, the Jews made a plot against him, so he decided to return through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Ephesians 6:21; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Now Sopater of Berea, Aristarchus and Secundus of the Thessalonians, Gaius of Derbe, Timothy, and Tychicus and Trophimus of Asia were going to accompany him as far as Asia;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Just 1.1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit ‘as far as Asia’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">a</a></sup></span>

## Troas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 2:42; John 20:19"> Now on the first day of the week,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Note that the disciples are meeting on Sunday.">c</a></sup>the disciples being assembled to break bread, Paul started addressing them, and because he intended to leave the next day he continued his message until midnight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 22:12"> There were many lamps in the upper room where we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Instead of “we”, perhaps 15% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘they’ (as in AV and NKJV).">d</a></sup>were assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 13:36; Matthew 26:40–41; Jonah 1:5–6"> Well a certain young man named Eutychus sat in a window and was sinking into a deep sleep as Paul kept on talking; when he was overcome by the sleep he fell down from the third story and was picked up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 5:39; Matthew 9:23–24; 1 Kings 17:21–22"> So Paul went down, threw himself on him and embracing him said, “Do not be distressed, because his life is in him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 20:7; Acts 20:9"> Then he went back up and broke bread, ate, and kept on speaking until daybreak—that is how he left!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 2 Corinthians 1:4"> (On their part, they led the boy away alive, and were greatly comforted.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="One gets the impression that Paul simply imposed his will on the local congregation (he had already taken the bit in his teeth and was doing things his own way). <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35; Mark 6:31–33"> As for us, we went to the ship and set sail for Assos, intending to take Paul on board there—so it had been arranged, he himself intending to go on foot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="His baggage went on the boat, so he walked without a load—good exercise.">f</a></sup></span>

## Miletus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; 1 Timothy 5:17"> From Miletus he sent to Ephesus and summoned the elders of the congregation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The distance was some 30 miles, as the crow flies, and the elders had not received advance warning, so at least two days would have elapsed before they arrived (even if the messenger ran, the elders would not).">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Acts 18:19; Acts 19:1"> So when they had come to him he said to them: “You yourselves know, from the first day that I arrived in Asia, how I lived the whole time I was with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; 2 Corinthians 2:4; Romans 12:11"> serving the Lord with all humility, and with many tears and trials, the ones that happened to me by the plots of the Jews;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 5:42"> how I did not shrink from declaring to you anything that was profitable, teaching you publicly and from house to house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 11:18; 1 John 5:11–13"> solemnly proclaiming to both Jews and Greeks the repentance toward God and the faith into our Lord Jesus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some 25% of the Greek manuscripts add ‘Christ’, as in AV and NKJV.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; 2 Peter 1:14"> Now then, I am going to Jerusalem bound in my spirit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="This was his own spirit, not the Holy Spirit. Why did he do it?">j</a></sup>not knowing the things that will happen to me there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 9:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:4"> except that the Holy Spirit keeps warning me in every city, saying that fetters and afflictions are just waiting for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6–8; 2 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 12:10"> However none of this moves me, nor do I regard my life as valuable to myself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Paul's statement is a non sequitur. When the Holy Spirit gives repeated warnings, He is telling us to STOP!">k</a></sup>just so that I may complete my course with joy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="How can you complete your course with joy if you are disobedient? He is not thinking clearly. Some 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit ‘with joy’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">l</a></sup>even the ministry that I received from the Lord Jesus, to solemnly proclaim the Good News of the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Acts 20:38; Matthew 4:23"> “Furthermore, I know that none of you among whom I have gone about proclaiming the Kingdom of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Some 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit ‘of God’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, etc.).">m</a></sup>will ever see my face again.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="How did he know this?">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21; Ezekiel 33:2–9"> Therefore I testify to you this day that I am innocent of the blood of all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 20:20; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:7–10"> because I did not shrink from declaring to you the whole counsel of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="The congregation in Ephesus had been very well grounded indeed, but even so they lost their ‘lampstand’ (Revelation 2:5).">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> So take heed to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the Holy Spirit has placed you as overseers, to shepherd the congregation of the Lord and God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="The sheep belong to the Lord, not to the elders. Some 7% of the Greek manuscripts omit ‘the Lord and’, as in most versions. “The Lord and God” refers to Jesus.">p</a></sup>which He purchased with His own blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 2 Peter 2:1; John 10:12"> Because I know this, that after my departure savage wolves will come in among you, not sparing the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19–20; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Proverbs 23:33"> Yes, men will rise up from among you yourselves, speaking distorted things, to draw away the disciples after them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Whenever you see someone trying to create his own group of followers, beware!">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:8; Acts 20:19; Acts 19:10"> Therefore be alert, remembering that during three years, night and day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="I suppose Paul is saying he was always available.">r</a></sup>I never stopped admonishing each one, with tears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 3:24"> “And so now, brothers, I entrust you to God and to the Word of His grace, which is able to build you up and to give you an inheritance among all those who have been sanctified.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Yes. When we receive God's Word as having objective authority over us, and interpret it honestly under the Holy Spirit's direction (without preconceived doctrinal ‘packages’), we will indeed grow in understanding and sanctification.">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:18"> I have not coveted anyone's silver or gold or clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 18:3; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> You yourselves know that these hands have supplied my own needs, and of those who were with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="He did receive offerings from time to time, but material gain was never an objective.">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 19:17; 2 Corinthians 9:6–12"> In every way I showed you that working hard like this it is necessary to help the weak, and to remember the word of the Lord Jesus, that He Himself said, ‘It is more blessed to give than to receive.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="This precise statement is not recorded in the Gospels, but Paul got it from one of those who actually heard it. It is an important truth. (Compare Luke 14:12-14.)">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 21:5; Luke 22:41; Philippians 4:6"> When he had said these things, he knelt down and prayed with them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> Then they all wept freely, and falling on Paul's neck kept kissing him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 15:3; 1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 20:25"> sorrowing most of all because of the word that he had spoken, that they would see his face no more. And they accompanied him to the ship.</span>
</div>
