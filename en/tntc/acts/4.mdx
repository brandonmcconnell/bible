## Peter and John arrested

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> Now as they were speaking to the people, the priests, the captain of the temple and the Sadducees came at them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> being upset because they were teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The high priest and family were Sadducees; they did not believe in resurrection (for anybody). So they were doubly disturbed, because the Apostles were affirming that Jesus had already done it, and in consequence others would too.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> And they laid hands on them and put them in custody until the next day, because it was already evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> (However, many of those who heard the message believed; the number of the men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word used refers exclusively to males, so with women and children the total number of believers would have been several times larger. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> So the next day an assembly of their rulers, both elders and scribes, occurred in Jerusalem,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The impression one gets is that they were called in from surrounding areas.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> along with Annas the high priest,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Annas was the real high priest—once installed, the office was for life.">d</a></sup>and Caiaphas, John and Alexander, and as many as were of high-priestly descent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> And having placed them in the middle &#91;of the assembly&#93; they started questioning: “By what power, or by what name did you do this?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="“This” suggests that the healed man was present, as verse 10 confirms.">e</a></sup></span>

## Peter's defense

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Then Peter, full of Holy Spirit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Again, no definite article.">f</a></sup>said to them: “Rulers of the people and elders of Israel:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Perhaps 2% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit “of Israel” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> If we are being examined today about a good deed done to a helpless man, by what means he has been made well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> be it known to you all, and to all the people of Israel, that by the name of Jesus Christ the Natsorean, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Peter is being neither cautious nor conciliatory! In affirming the resurrection he goes right to the sore point.">h</a></sup>by Him this man stands here before you whole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> This is ‘the stone which was despised by you, the builders, which has become the chief cornerstone.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> Also, the salvation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The Text has the definite article; there may be many ‘salvations’ in life, but this is a very specific one. ## Rulers impressed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> Now upon observing the boldness of Peter and John and perceiving that they were uneducated and unskilled men,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The disciples had not received a theological education nor been trained in verbal skills (of course Peter was a skilled fisherman, but that is not the point here).">j</a></sup>they marveled; and they recognized that they had been with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> Further, seeing the man who had been healed standing with them, they could say nothing against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> So ordering them to go out from the council they began to confer among themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> saying: “What can we do to these men? Because, indeed, that a notable miracle has been done through them is evident to all who dwell in Jerusalem, and we cannot deny it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="They knew that Jesus was the Messiah but had repudiated Him anyway. Here again the facts are clear, but they are determined to maintain their repudiation of Jesus, and to impose their view on the people. Having committed the unpardonable sin, they were under satanic control.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> But so that it spreads no further among the people, let us severely threaten them to no longer speak to anyone in this name.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## They are forbidden to use the name ‘Jesus’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> So summoning them they commanded them absolutely not to speak or teach in the name of ‘Jesus’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But in answer Peter and John said to them: “Whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you rather than to God, judge for yourselves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> because we cannot help but speak the things we have seen and heard.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="There are times when we must not obey those in authority; mainly when they demand that we deny God's truth and His values.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> So threatening them some more they released them, not finding any way to punish them, because of the people, since they were all glorifying God over what had happened;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> because the man on whom this miracle of healing had been performed was over forty years old.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="He had been lame for over forty years.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The reaction of the believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; Psalms 119:63"> So upon being released they went to their own group and reported all that the chief priests and elders had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> Well upon hearing it, with one mind they called out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The Text actually says ‘they lifted voice to God’ (since God is not deaf, there is no need to shout).">n</a></sup>to God and said: “O Sovereign!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Our ‘despot’ is a transliteration of the Greek word here. <span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> You who said through the mouth of Your servant David:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Some 8% of the Greek manuscripts replace the opening clause with, “You spoke by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of your servant, our father David” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Why did nations snort</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="One can ‘snort’ in anger or in disdain, or perhaps both. <span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of the earth took a stand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the rulers were gathered together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against the Lord and against His Messiah.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="See Psalm 2:1-2.">s</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> Well, in fact, both Herod and Pontius Pilate, together with Gentiles and peoples of Israel, were gathered together<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Perhaps 10% of the Greek manuscripts add ‘in this city’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">t</a></sup>against Your holy Servant Jesus, whom You anointed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> to do whatever Your hand and Your counsel foreordained to happen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="They have a clear understanding that God's Plan prevailed, while not denying human responsibility.">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> As for the present, Lord, consider their threats, and grant to Your slaves to speak Your word with all boldness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> while You stretch out Your hand to heal and that signs and wonders may occur through the name of Your holy Servant Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> Well when they had prayed, the place where they were gathered was shaken, and they were all filled with Holy Spirit and spoke the Word of God with boldness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Their request was granted. <div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> Now the multitude of those who believed was of one heart and soul; indeed not one was saying that any of his belongings was his own, but they had all things in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> (Also the Apostles were giving witness to the resurrection of the Lord Jesus with great power.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="So how did that work? <span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> and placing them at the Apostles' feet, and they were distributed to each according as anyone had need.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> So Joses, who was named Barnabas by the Apostles (which is, being translated, ‘Son of encouragement’), a Levite of the country of Cyprus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> having a field, sold it, brought the money and placed it at the Apostles' feet.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Since many were doing it, one wonders why Luke singled out Barnabas; perhaps because he would be an important player later on.">x</a></sup></span>
