## The ‘rights’ of an apostle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am I not an apostle? Am I not free? Have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="One of the requirements for a replacement for Judas Iscariot was to have seen the risen Christ (Acts 1:22). Perhaps 5% of the Greek manuscripts omit “Christ”, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">a</a></sup>Are you not my work in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> If I am not an apostle to others, yet at least I am to you, because you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> My defense to those who are judging me is this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Do we have no right to eat and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> Do we have no right to take along a believing wife, just as the rest of the apostles and the Lord's brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This refers to the sons that Mary had with Joseph after Jesus was born. ‘Cephas’ was another name for Peter.">b</a></sup>and Cephas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Or is it only Barnabas and I who have no right to forego working?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Who ever serves as a soldier at his own expense? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat of its fruit? Or who tends a flock and does not drink<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Actually the Text says ‘eat’, but milk is a liquid; unless it be that Paul is thinking of cheese, that is derived from milk.">c</a></sup>of its milk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> I am not saying these things as a mere man, am I? Does not the Law also say the same?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; Psalms 104:27"> For it stands written in the Law of Moses: “You shall not muzzle an ox that is threshing.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 25:4.">d</a></sup>Is it really about the oxen that God is concerned,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Obviously human beings are more important to God than are the animals, but God is telling us to treat the animals with some consideration.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> or does He surely say it for our sakes? Yes, it was written for us, that he who plows should plow in hope, and he who threshes in hope should partake of his hope.</span>
</div>
</div>

## To live from the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> Since we planted spiritual things in you, is it a big deal if we reap material things from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others have a share in this right from you, do not we even more? Nevertheless we have not used this right, but we put up with everything so as not to cause any hindrance to the Gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Do you not know that those who minister the sacred things eat from the temple, and those who serve at the altar have a share in the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> So also the Lord has instructed those who proclaim the Gospel to live from the Gospel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="When was that? When Jesus sent out the twelve and later the seventy, He told them not to take money but to depend on the hospitality of those to whom they ministered. Later on, however, He reversed that instruction, but “to live” refers to basic needs, not luxury.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## To forego a right

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> Now I have not used any of these rights, nor have I written these things that it should be done so in my case; for it would be better for me to die than that anyone should make my boasting void</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> —I cannot boast because I preach the Gospel, because I am compelled to do so; indeed, woe is me if I do not preach it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> (If I do this of my own volition, I have a reward; but if otherwise, I have been entrusted with a commission.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> So what is my reward? That when I evangelize I may present the Gospel of Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts omit “of Christ”, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">g</a></sup>without charge, so as not to use my rights in the Gospel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="If you only do something because you are compelled, because you can't get out of it, you get no credit for it. Paul preaches the Gospel because he has to, he is compelled, so gets no credit. But Paul wants some credit, some reward! So how can he get it? Not by preaching, since that is obligatory—by preaching without charge! Paul has the right to charge but foregoes it so as to get some credit/reward. Evidently this was rather important to him (verse 15)—I confess that I don't understand why he said it would be better to die.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## All things to all men

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> Though being free from all men, I have made myself a slave to all, in order that I might win the more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> To the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might win Jews; to those under law as under law, that I might win those under law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> to those without law as without law (not being without law toward God but under law toward Christ), that I might win those without law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> to the weak I became as weak, that I might win the weak. I have become all things to all men, that I might by all means save some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> Now I do this for the sake of the Gospel, so as to become its partner.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="If you are a partner, you get a share of the profits; evidently Paul's share was being deposited in the Bank of Heaven.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Even an apostle can be rejected

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Do you not know that in a stadium race all the runners run, but one gets the prize? Run like that, so that you may win.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> All athletic competitors exercise general self-control—they do it in order to receive a perishable crown, but we an imperishable one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> So that is how I ‘run’, with a definite goal; that is how I fight, with well-aimed blows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> Yes, I discipline my body and bring it into subjection, lest, having preached to others, I myself should be rejected.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="This is a strong term. If Paul was concerned, we had better be too! Note that this has to do with disciplining the body.">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
