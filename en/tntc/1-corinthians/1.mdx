## A word of thanksgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; John 14:26; 1 Timothy 1:14"> Concerning you, I always thank my God because of the grace of God that was given you in Christ Jesus;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:11; 2 Corinthians 8:7; Colossians 2:3"> for in Him you were enriched in every way, in all speech and all knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Timothy 2:6"> even as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="As the Message was progressively confirmed in them, they were progressively enriched with the things mentioned.">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 2 Peter 3:12; Hebrews 9:28"> so that you lack no spiritual gift as you expectantly await the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="It appears that the early Christians expected the Lord to return at any time, which would tend to have a sobering effect upon one's style of living.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Colossians 1:22"> who will also confirm you until the end, blameless in the Day of our Lord, Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The “Day of our Lord Jesus Christ” includes the accounting for all those who are in Him. So the promise here is major, and verse 9 starts with ‘God is faithful’.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Hebrews 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> God is faithful, by whom you were called into fellowship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“Called into fellowship” sounds like an ongoing exchange.">k</a></sup>with His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="To the ‘Western’ mind it may seem like Paul was being a bit repetitious; but Paul was a Jew—he is making very sure that they know where he is coming from, and where he wants to take them.">l</a></sup></span>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:1–7; 1 Corinthians 12:25"> Now I appeal to you, brothers, by the name<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The name represents the person.">m</a></sup>of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all speak the same thing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="They are not being told to repeat things like parrots, but to be in essential agreement about important points.">n</a></sup>and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be restored<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="They have drifted.">o</a></sup>to the same mind and to the same purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Timothy 2:23–25"> You see, my brothers, it has been reported to me concerning you, by some from Chloe's household,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Paul cites his source.">p</a></sup>that there are contentions among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:21–23; John 1:42; Matthew 23:9–10"> What I mean is that you are individually saying: “I am of Paul,” or “I am of Apollos,” or “I am of Cephas,” or “I am of Christ!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; 2 Corinthians 11:4; Acts 2:38"> Has Christ been divided? It was not Paul who was crucified for you, was it? You were not baptized into the name of Paul, were you?</span>
</div>

## A disclaimer about baptism

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 18:8; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:4"> I thank God that I did not baptize any of you except Crispus and Gaius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; John 3:28–29; John 7:18"> lest anyone should say that I had baptized into my own name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 16:33"> O yes, I also baptized the household of Stephanus; besides them I do not remember if I baptized anyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:1; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:16"> Because Christ did not send me to baptize, but to preach the Gospel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="If water baptism were essential to salvation, I fail to see how Paul could have expressed himself in this way—he is clearly implying that water baptism is not an essential part of the Gospel. In the early Church people were baptized immediately, not after weeks of ‘preparation’, because the convert was publicly breaking with Satan and his kingdom and formally placing himself under Christ's protection. Such baptism is an important procedure, and its value should be explained to any new convert.">q</a></sup>—not with eloquent ‘wisdom’, lest the cross of Christ be emptied of power.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Dear me, is eloquence the enemy of power? Does not eloquence give glory to the speaker rather than to Christ? Perhaps not inescapably, but the tendency is definitely there.">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Foolishness to the lost

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:10"> The message of the cross, you see, is foolishness to those who are being wasted,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="We are accustomed to ‘perishing’. The verb here has a considerable semantic area and can be rendered—destroy, kill, deprive, void, lose, perish—depending on the context, but I believe the root idea is ‘waste’. The only way to fulfill the purpose for which you were created, to realize your potential, is to turn your life over to Jesus. The alternative is to waste your life, both now and forever. Of course the enemy works to make people think the opposite.">s</a></sup>but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> For it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will destroy the wisdom of the wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and confound the shrewdness of the intelligent.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="See Isaiah 29:14.">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Job 12:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Where is the wise one? Where is the scholar? Where is the debater of this age? Has not God made foolish the wisdom of this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 10:21; Romans 1:20–22; Matthew 11:25"> For since, in the wisdom of God, the world through its ‘wisdom’ did not get to know God, it pleased God to save the believing ones through the ‘foolishness’ of what was preached—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:1–4; Matthew 12:38–39; Mark 8:11"> since Jews request a sign while Greeks seek after wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Peter 2:8"> but we proclaim a crucified Christ: an offense to Jews, foolishness to Greeks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="To the Jews, their Messiah would be a conquering king, and since death on a cross was viewed as a curse (Deuteronomy 21:23, Galatians 3:13), a crucified Messiah was simply unthinkable. What did the Greeks want with someone else's Messiah, especially a dead one—sheer nonsense!">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> Now to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 11:33–36; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> because the ‘foolishness’ of God is wiser than men, and the ‘weakness’ of God is stronger than men.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="We like to forget this, but it is best not to do so. Arrogant servants of Satan often use stronger terms than ‘foolishness’—they will use ‘repugnant’, ‘ridiculous’, ‘intolerable’, etc.">v</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## God uses ‘nothings’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:5; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20; James 1:9–11"> Just look at your calling, brothers: not many are wise, not many are powerful, not many are of noble birth, by human standards;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 11:25; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> but God has chosen the foolish things of the world that He might put the wise to shame, and God has chosen the weak things of the world that He might put the strong things to shame;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; Romans 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> and God has chosen the lowly things of the world and the despised things—even the ‘nothings’—that He might nullify the ‘somethings’;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; Ephesians 2:9"> so that no flesh should boast in God's presence.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="This is the bottom line. Several times the Text declares that God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble. “Flesh” here refers to human beings.">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1"> It is from Him that you are in Christ Jesus, who was made to us wisdom from God—also righteousness and sanctification and redemption<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="We receive Christ's righteousness to get started; the sanctification is to keep us clean along the road; the redemption is the final victory. Now really, isn't that a wonderful salvation? Thank you, Jesus!">x</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 10:17; Philippians 3:3"> so that, as it is written: “He who boasts, let him boast in the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="See Jeremiah 9:24. Since God has given us all we have, to glory in self is wrong.">y</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
