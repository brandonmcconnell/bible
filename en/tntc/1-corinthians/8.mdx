## About things offered to idols

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; Romans 14:3"> Now concerning things offered to idols: We know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge puffs up, but love edifies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Proverbs 26:12"> And if anyone thinks that he knows anything, he has come to know nothing yet as he ought to know.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="It is probably true that none of us has complete knowledge on any subject. I follows that we should refrain from acting as if we did!">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> But if anyone loves God, this one is known by Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is one way to make sure God knows who you are!">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Idols are really nothing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Isaiah 41:24; 1 Corinthians 10:19–20"> Therefore, concerning the eating of things offered to idols, we know that an idol is nothing in the world, and no one else is God except the One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; Galatians 4:8"> For even if there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on the earth (since there are many ‘gods’ and many ‘lords’),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> yet for us there is one God, the Father, out of whom are all things and we into Him, and one Sovereign Jesus Christ, through whom are all things and we through Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Everything starts with the Father, including the possibility that we become His children. Colossians 1:16, Hebrews 1:10 and John 1:10 make clear that the Son was the principal agent in the Creation (although the Trinity was doubtless involved), which includes our own existence.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Respect the other's conscience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 14:14; 1 Corinthians 10:28–29; Romans 14:22–23"> However, this knowledge is not in everyone; but some, with consciousness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Instead of “with consciousness of”, less than 5% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘accustomed to’, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.—clearly an inferior proceeding.">d</a></sup>of the idol, still eat it as offered to an idol, and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:17; Hebrews 13:9; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> But food does not commend us to God; for neither if we eat are we better off, nor if we do not eat are we worse off.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Spiritually speaking.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 14:20–21; 1 Peter 2:16"> But take care lest somehow this ‘right’ of yours become a stumbling block to those who are weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:28–29; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; Romans 14:23"> For if someone who is weak sees you with your knowledge eating in an idol's temple, will not his conscience be emboldened to eat things offered to idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:15; Romans 15:1–3; 1 Corinthians 8:13"> And so the weak brother, for whom Christ died, will be wasted because of your knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Matthew 25:45; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> But when you thus sin against the brothers and wound their weak conscience, you sin against Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 14:21; 2 Corinthians 6:3; 2 Corinthians 11:29"> So then, if food causes my brother to fall, I will never eat meat again, lest I cause my brother to fall.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The ‘fall’ here is serious, including the possibility that he may not get up again. This is sometimes called the ‘law of love’, giving up a right for the sake of a brother.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
