## As stewards we must be faithful

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:9"> Let a man consider us like this: as Christ's subordinates and stewards of God's mysteries.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="What does a steward of God's mysteries do? He explains them to others. The function of a teacher is similar.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Moreover, what is required of stewards is that each be found faithful.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="By the owner; see the last clause of verse 4, below.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> So to me it is a very small thing that I should be judged by you or by a human court; in fact, I do not even judge myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:2; 1 John 3:20–21; Psalms 130:3"> For I am conscious of nothing against myself, although I am not justified by this; it is the Lord who judges me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:7; 2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> Therefore judge nothing before the time, until the Lord comes, who will both bring to light the hidden things of darkness and expose the motives of the hearts.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Part of judging correctly is knowing why the accused did it. Since ‘knowing the motives of the hearts’ is generally beyond us [even our own at times], we had better leave the judging up to the Judge.">c</a></sup>At that time the praise that comes to each will be from God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="I was brought up on the AV that says: “then shall every man have praise of God”, which led me to believe that everyone would get some praise. However, I believe the Text says something different, as indicated in my rendering. Paul is not affirming that all will get praise (of what use is ‘praise’ to someone in the Lake of fire?), but that the Judge will distribute whatever praise is merited.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## No room for boasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 12:19"> I have illustrated these things using myself and Apollos, brothers, for your sakes, so that you may learn from us not to think beyond what is written,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Written where? By whom? I suppose the reference is to principles in the Scriptures.">e</a></sup>that no one of you be puffed up in favor of the one against the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:27; 1 Peter 4:10; James 1:17"> Now who is distinguishing you? Or what do you have that you did not receive?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Here is the fundamental recipe against boasting. None of us chose who our parents would be, where we would be born, what language would be our native tongue. But these circumstances dictate the opportunities that a person will have, quite apart from talents and abilities. Things that were given to us do not form a proper basis for boasting.">f</a></sup>So if you did indeed receive it, why do you boast as though you did not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> You are already stuffed! You are already rich! You have become ‘kings’ without us! I could wish that you really did reign, so that we might be kings with you too!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Presumably a bit of sarcasm.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The cost of being an apostle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:33; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 4:8–12"> For I keep thinking that God has displayed us, the apostles, at the end of the line, like men sentenced to death;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The reference seems to be to a returning Roman commander's victory march, where some of the conquered enemy soldiers would be displayed at the end of the line, and be executed later.">h</a></sup>because we have been made a spectacle to the world, both to angels and to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> We are fools for Christ while you are wise! We are weak but you are strong! You are esteemed, we are despised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:35; 2 Corinthians 11:23–27; Philippians 4:12"> To this very hour we go hungry and thirsty; we are poorly dressed, brutally treated, and wander homeless;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:11; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> yes, we labor, working with our own hands. Upon being reviled, we bless; upon being persecuted, we endure it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; Acts 22:22"> upon being slandered, we exhort. We have been made as the refuse of the world, the off-scouring of whatever, to this moment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Wow, what a ‘job description’! Any applicants? How many of the plague of self-styled ‘apostles’ in our day meet these qualifications? We need to understand what Paul is saying here. To be looked down on and criticized by believers among whom one has labored is one thing. Local people with personal ambition know how to do that. For God to make us “as the refuse of the world” is something very different. How should we understand this? If we insist on proclaiming a ‘gospel’ that the world considers to be stupid, abject foolishness, we will certainly be ridiculed. But if we insist on biblical values that the world has declared to be ‘hate crimes’, we will certainly be hated and persecuted, treated as refuse. The choice of Hebrews 13:13 is upon us: “So then, let us go out to Him, outside the camp, bearing His disgrace.”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Children imitate fathers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 3 John 1:4"> I am not writing these things to shame you; I am admonishing you as my dear children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 1 Corinthians 3:10; 1 Corinthians 9:14"> Because even if you were to have thousands of tutors in Christ, you would not have many fathers, because I am the one who begot you in Christ Jesus, through the Gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17"> Therefore I am urging you, become my imitators.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Our spiritual children will naturally look to us for example. If people imitate us, how badly will they be damaged?">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:10; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> That is why I sent you Timothy, who is my beloved and faithful son in Sovereign, who will remind you of my ways in Christ, just as I teach everywhere in every congregation.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Not in word but in power

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:6–8; 1 Corinthians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> Now some have been puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:1–4; Acts 18:21; Acts 19:21"> But I will come to you shortly, if the Lord wills, and I will know, not the word of those who have been puffed up, but the power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> Because the Kingdom of God is not in word but in power.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Here is a plain statement. On this basis, how many churches and ministries that you know of are part of God's Kingdom? Notice that I did not capitalize ‘word’; the reference is presumably to human speech, not the Sword.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> What do you prefer? Shall I come to you with a rod, or in love and a gentle spirit?</span>
</div>
