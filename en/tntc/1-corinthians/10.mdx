## Examples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> Now these things became examples for us, to the intent that we should not lust after evil things as they also lusted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalms 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> And do not become idolaters, just like some of them; as it is written: “The people sat down to eat and drink, and got up to amuse themselves.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Exodus 32:6.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalms 106:29"> And let us not fornicate, just as some of them did—and in one day twenty-three thousand died!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Numbers 25:1-9. Another thousand died after the first day, giving a total of twenty-four thousand deaths.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; Psalms 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> Neither let us test the Christ, just as also some of them did—and were destroyed by the snakes!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Numbers 21:6-9.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> And do not grumble, just as also some of them did—and were executed by the destroyer!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="See Numbers 14:37 and 16:49. ## Eschew idolatry!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I speak as to wise men; judge for yourselves what I say.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This is cataphoric; they are to judge what follows.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> The cup of blessing that we bless,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Those officiating bless the ‘cup’, which then blesses those who partake. Yes, to partake worthily brings blessing.">l</a></sup>is it not a sharing of the blood of Christ?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Although one finds it difficult to agree with the Roman doctrine of transubstantiation, that the words of the one officiating transform the wine into literal blood, Paul evidently takes a very serious view of the ‘Lord's Table’. It is not a mere symbolism.">m</a></sup>The bread that we break, is it not a sharing of the body of Christ?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="“The body of Christ” here refers to His physical body on the cross, just as “the blood of Christ” refers to His blood shed on the cross.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Since the loaf is one, we, the many, are one body, because we all partake of that single loaf.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Evidently they did not cut/break the bread up into little pieces beforehand—there was a whole loaf on the table.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> Consider the physical Israel: are not those who eat the sacrifices partakers of the altar?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Idol = demon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> So what am I getting at? That an idol is anything, or what is offered to idols is anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalms 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> Rather, that the things pagans sacrifice they sacrifice to demons and not to God. I do not want you to become participants with the demons.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Obviously demons exist. The visible idols represent invisible demons. When you worship an idol you subordinate yourself to its demon. There are ‘idols’ that are not physical images.">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; you cannot partake of the Lord's table and of the table of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Are we going to provoke the Lord's jealousy? Are we stronger than He?</span>
</div>
</div>

## About conscience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> All things are ‘permissible’ for me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Perhaps 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit “for me” (both times in this verse), to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc. The difference in meaning is significant.">q</a></sup>but not all things are expedient. All things are ‘permissible’ for me, but not all things edify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let no one seek his own, but each one the other's well-being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Eat whatever is being sold in the meat market, asking no question for the sake of conscience;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalms 50:12"> since “the earth is the Lord's, and all its fullness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="See Psalm 24:1.">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> If some unbeliever invites you and you decide to go, eat whatever is set before you, asking no questions for the sake of conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if anyone says, “This was offered to idols,” do not eat it for the sake of the speaker and of the conscience; since “the earth is the Lord's, and all its fullness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="At first blush this repetition of the quote seems out of place. <span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> ‘Conscience’ I say, not your own but that of the other. Do all to the glory of God<br />Now just why should my freedom be judged by another's conscience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> If I partake with thanks, why be slandered over something I give thanks for?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Paul wants to avoid being slandered, even if it is for doing something good and legitimate.">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> Therefore, whether you eat or you drink, or whatever you do, do all to the glory of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Here is a basic principle that furnishes us with the key for deciding about doubtful things, and things produced by modern technology that are not mentioned in the Bible. If I want to go watch a movie, I should ask myself, “Why do I want to do this? Is it for the glory of God?” If I want to buy a new, super-duper cell phone, I should ask the same questions. If we really want to please God, we will give an honest answer and act accordingly.">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> Give no offense, either to Jews or to Greeks or to the Church of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="This text makes clear that during the Church Age there are three categories of people: Jews, non-Jews and the Church (made up of both Jews and non-Jews who are in Christ). Before Pentecost there were two categories: Jews and non-Jews. There are those who think that after Pentecost there were still just two: Church and non-Church, wherein the Church replaced Israel. But it is not so; Israel still exists as a separate entity in God's plan. Chapters nine, ten and eleven of Romans go into some detail on this point.">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> —even as I try to please everyone in every way, not seeking my own profit but that of the many, that they may be saved.</span>
