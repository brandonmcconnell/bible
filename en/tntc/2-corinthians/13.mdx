## I will not spare

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6"> This is the third time I am coming to you. “At the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Deuteronomy 19:15. It almost seems that Paul is comparing his visits to witnesses.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:23; 2 Corinthians 12:20–21; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> As I already said while with you the second time, I now repeat in writing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Perhaps 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit “in writing”, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">b</a></sup>while absent, to those who sinned before and to all the rest: If I come again I will not spare,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:20; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Luke 21:15"> since you want proof that Christ is speaking through me—He who is not weak toward you but powerful among you</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 3:18; Romans 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:3–4"> (although He was crucified in weakness, He lives by the power of God). Now we also are weak in Him, but we will live with Him by the power of God toward you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Being weak in God's presence is one thing; being weak in our work for Him is quite another. I take Paul to be saying that being weak in God's presence triggers the outflow of His power in our work.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Examine yourselves

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Corinthians 11:28; Psalms 139:23–24"> Examine yourselves as to whether you are in the faith; test yourselves. Do you yourselves really not know that Jesus Christ is in you?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Sin does have a way of undermining confidence. A Christian who keeps on sinning tends to become less and less sure of his salvation.">d</a></sup>—unless indeed you are disqualified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:3–4"> Well I trust that you will know that we are not disqualified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:12; 1 Timothy 2:2; 2 Corinthians 6:8–9"> Now I pray to God that you do no evil; not that we may appear approved, but that you may do what is right (even though we may appear to have failed).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="I don't understand this.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Mark 9:39; Acts 4:28–30"> For we cannot do anything against the Truth, but only for the Truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:10"> Now we are glad when we are weak and you are strong. And we also pray for this, your perfecting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Titus 1:13"> This is why I write these things while absent, so that when present I may not have to deal harshly, according to the authority that the Lord gave me, for building up and not for tearing down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Romans 12:16; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Finally, brothers, rejoice; restore each other; encourage one another; be of one mind; live in peace; and the God of the love and the peace will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Peter 5:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> Greet one another with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 16:16; Romans 16:21–23; Hebrews 13:24"> All the saints greet you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 2:22; Romans 5:5; Romans 16:20"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Here we have a clear reference to the Trinity.">f</a></sup>Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
