## A godly jealousy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; 1 Corinthians 1:21; 2 Corinthians 11:19"> I could wish that you would put up with a little of my foolishness, but indeed you already are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Ephesians 5:26–27; Isaiah 54:5"> For I am jealous over you with a godly jealousy, because I betrothed you to one man to present you to Christ as a pure virgin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Galatians 1:6; Ephesians 4:14"> But I am afraid that somehow, as the serpent deceived Eve with his cunning, so your minds may be corrupted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The implication is clear: Satan does the corrupting, directly or indirectly, and it is their thinking that he is corrupting (and that is serious—Proverbs 23:7).">a</a></sup>from the integrity that is in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–8; 1 Corinthians 3:11; 1 Timothy 2:5"> For if someone comes and preaches another ‘Jesus’ whom we did not preach, or you receive a different spirit from the one you received, or a different gospel from the one you accepted, you easily put up with it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The impression one gets is that the Corinthian believers were lacking in discernment, or commitment, or both.">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul ministered without charge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11–12; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Galatians 2:6–9"> Now I consider that I am not at all inferior to the very best apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> Though I may not be a trained speaker, I do have knowledge—but we have been fully manifested to you in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 10:1"> Or did I commit a sin in humbling self so that you might be exalted, in that I proclaimed God's Gospel to you free of charge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 4:14–16"> I ‘robbed’ other congregations, receiving support from them so as to serve you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> and when I was with you and in need, I did not burden anyone; because the brothers who came from Macedonia supplied my need. Yes, I kept myself from being a burden to you in anything, and will keep on.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exposing deceivers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:1; Romans 1:9; Acts 18:12"> The truth of Christ is in me: this boasting will not be silenced in me in the regions of Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:15; 2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 7:3"> Why? Because I do not love you? God knows!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> Further, I will keep on doing what I do in order to cut off the opportunity from those who desire an opportunity to be considered equal with us in the things of which they boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 1:7; Revelation 2:2; 1 John 4:1"> Such men are really false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into ‘apostles’ of Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="There have always been those who want to ‘get on the bandwagon’, to get a free ride; who traffic in spiritual things for personal, temporal advantage. Since such people only do damage, Paul's desire to expose them stems from his concern for the Corinthians' welfare.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:8; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And no wonder, because Satan himself masquerades as an angel of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Revelation 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> So it is no great thing if his servants also masquerade as ministers of righteousness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="It is well to remember that neither Satan nor his servants are in the habit of appearing with horns and tails. Just because someone ‘looks good’ doesn't mean that he is.">d</a></sup>whose end will be according to their works.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A little ‘necessary’ boasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> Again I say, let no one think me a fool. But should anyone do so, at least receive me as a fool, that I also may boast a little bit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:12; Philippians 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> What I am going to say I do not say according to the Lord, but as though foolishly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The implication would appear to be that whatever we do that is not ‘according to the Lord’ is foolish.">e</a></sup>in this confident boasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23; 2 Corinthians 10:12–18"> Since many are boasting according to the flesh, I will too</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Corinthians 10:15; 1 Corinthians 8:1"> (for you put up with fools gladly, being so wise yourselves!).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:4; Galatians 4:3; Galatians 5:1"> In fact, you even put up with someone who enslaves you, who ‘devours’ you, who takes advantage, who exalts himself, who beats on your face!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Paul is really worked up. Even allowing for his sarcasm, there were evidently negative things going on at Corinth that we do not know about.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 10:1–2"> Is it disrespectful to say that we were ‘weak’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="I found this sentence to be especially difficult to translate, and am not sure I got it right. Compared to the types described in verse 20, he might have come across as being ‘weak’.">g</a></sup><br />In whatever anyone is bold (I speak foolishly), I am bold also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 3:5; Romans 11:1; Romans 9:4"> Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they descendants of Abraham? So am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Are they ministers of Christ? (I'm being irrational) I am more: in labors more abundantly, in beatings beyond count, in prison more frequently, in ‘deaths’ often<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Verses 24 and 25 spell out the ‘deaths’; verse 26 resumes with the ‘in’s. We have no record of most of the experiences listed, an impressive list! Humanly speaking, he should have died several times.">h</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Deuteronomy 25:2–3; Matthew 10:17"> five times from the Jews I received the ‘forty lashes minus one’;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Deuteronomy 25:3 forbids exceeding forty blows, so the Jews always stopped one short, just in case they had miscounted.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 16:37; Hebrews 11:37"> three times I was beaten with rods; once I was stoned; three times I was shipwrecked (I spent twenty-four hours in the open sea)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:5; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:50"> in frequent journeys, in danger from rivers, in danger from robbers, in danger from my countrymen, in danger from Gentiles, in danger in cities, in danger in wildernesses, in danger in the sea, in danger among false brothers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> in toil and hardship, in many sleepless nights, in hunger and thirst, in frequent fastings, in cold and nakedness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="When the Lord told Ananias to go and restore Saul's sight, and he protested, the Lord continued, “For I will show him how many things he must suffer for my name's sake” (Acts 9:16). Paul paid for what he did against the early Church. By the way, we usually have to pay for our misdeeds down here (how can you pay in Heaven?).">j</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; Romans 15:16; Acts 20:18–35"> quite apart from the other things, my daily disturbances, my concern for all the congregations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Galatians 4:8–20"> Who is weak, and I do not feel it? Who is caused to stumble, and I do not burn with indignation?</span>
</div>
</div>

## When I am weak, then I am strong

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:5–11; Proverbs 25:27; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> Well, if I must boast, I will boast of the things that concern my weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 9:5; Ephesians 1:3; John 10:30"> The God and Father of the Lord Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts omit “Christ”, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">k</a></sup>who is blessed forever, knows that I am not lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:24–25; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> In Damascus the governor under King Aretas was guarding the city of the Damascenes, wanting to arrest me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 1 Samuel 19:12; Joshua 2:18"> but I was lowered in a basket through a window in the wall and escaped from his hands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Apparently Paul considered this to be a humiliating experience.">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
