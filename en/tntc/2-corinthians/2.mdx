<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> So, if anyone has caused grief, he has not so much grieved me as he has all of you, to some extent—not to be too ‘heavy’.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> so that now, on the other side, you should forgive and comfort him, so that he not be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Therefore I urge you to reaffirm your love to him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The purpose of the discipline was to restore, not destroy.">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> so that we not be exploited by Satan; for we are not ignorant of his intentions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Well, at least we shouldn't be, but how many Christians today really take the enemy seriously? ## A fragrance of life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> Now thanks be to the God who always leads us in triumph in the Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The title ‘Christ’ is usually accompanied by the definite article, ‘the Christ’—we are not to forget what the title really means.">f</a></sup>and through us spreads the fragrance of the knowledge of Him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The antecedent of the pronoun could be either the Father or the Son, but in this context most likely refers to the Son—getting to know Him is in view.">g</a></sup>in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> Because we are the aroma of Christ for God, among those who are being saved and among those who are being wasted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="We are accustomed to ‘perishing’. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> to these a smell of death into death, while to those a fragrance of life into life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The Gospel of Christ represents both life and death: if you receive it, you get life; if you reject it, you get death. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> However, we are not like the rest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The Greek manuscripts are about evenly divided between “the rest” and ‘the many’, but I follow the best line of transmission.">j</a></sup>who retail<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A retailer makes his living by buying goods wholesale and reselling them piecemeal, at a profit.
