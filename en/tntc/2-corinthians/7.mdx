<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> But the God who encourages the lowly encouraged us by the coming of Titus;</span>

## The effect of the first letter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 20:7–9; Matthew 26:21–22"> Even though that letter caused you sorrow, I do not regret it (though I almost did), because I perceive that the letter made you sorry, though only for a while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:7; Jeremiah 31:18–20; 2 Corinthians 7:10–11"> Now I rejoice, not that you were made sorry, but that your sorrow led to repentance. For you were made sorry as God intended, so as not to be harmed by us in any way.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The implication is that it is possible for those in spiritual authority to cause harm, through the misuse of that authority. I imagine that we have all seen this sort of thing happen in fact.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Acts 11:18; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Now godly sorrow produces repentance into salvation without regret, but the world's sorrow produces death.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Creator made us with emotions, which when properly used are beneficial, but that under the world's control are destructive.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Just consider your own being caused to sorrow in a godly manner, how much diligence it produced in you—what self-defense, what indignation, what alarm, what longing, what zeal, what punishment! At every point you have demonstrated yourselves to be clear in the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 Timothy 3:5; 2 Corinthians 2:17"> So even though I wrote to you, it was not for the sake of the wrongdoer, nor for that of the victim, but, before God, so that your real commitment to us might be made clear to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="God's agenda in dealing with us often includes several objectives.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> We have been encouraged by all this. Titus again<br />Because of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Instead of “your”, perhaps 3% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘our’, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">e</a></sup>encouragement, we rejoiced all the more over the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Thessalonians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 1:18–20"> Because wherein I boasted to him about you, I was not put to shame, but just as everything we spoke to you was true, so also our boasting to Titus proved to be true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 John 3:17"> Also, his affection for you is all the greater, as he remembers your collective obedience as you received him with fear and trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:3; Philemon 1:8; Philemon 1:21"> I am so glad that I have complete confidence in you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Paul knew how to ‘accentuate the positive’, as we used to say.">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
