## What it costs to be Christ's ambassadors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> (We give no occasion for offense in anything, that the ministry not be faulted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> rather we commend ourselves as God's servants in every way with great endurance—in afflictions, in hardships, in distress,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> in beatings, in imprisonments, in tumults, in hard work, in sleepless nights, in fastings;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> by purity, by knowledge, by patience, by kindness, by the Holy Spirit, by sincere love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> by truthful speech, by God's power, with the weapons of the righteousness in the right hand and in the left;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Why right and left? A right-handed soldier would have a shield in his left hand, for defense, with a sword or spear in his right, for offense—our weapons are for both offense and defense.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> through glory and dishonor, through defamation and good repute; as ‘deceivers’ and true,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> as unknown and well known, as dying and we live on, as chastened and not killed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> as sorrowful yet always rejoicing, as poor yet making many rich, as having nothing and yet possessing everything!)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Well, well, well, there really ought to be an easier way to make a living! Well, it depends on what kind of living quarters you want in Heaven. When I get there I hope to see many individuals who can say to me, “You made me rich; thank you!”.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Separation is a ‘must’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Do not enter a mismatch with unbelievers;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This does not refer only to marriage, as the explanation that follows makes clear.">e</a></sup>for what do righteousness and lawlessness have in common? And what fellowship does light have with darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> And what agreement is there between Christ and Belial? Or what portion can a believer share with an unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> Further, what agreement can a temple of God have with idols? Because you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Instead of “you”, perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘we’, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">f</a></sup>are a temple of the living God, just as God said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will dwell in them and walk among them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will be their God and they will by my people.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="See Leviticus 26:12 and Ezekiel 37:27.">g</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> And,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will be a Father to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will be sons and daughters to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the Lord Almighty.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="This appears to be an application based on several passages.">i</a></sup></span>
