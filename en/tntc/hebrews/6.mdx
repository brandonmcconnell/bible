## No one kills Jesus twice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> Because, it is impossible to bring back into repentance those who were once for all enlightened, who have partaken<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The verb here—whether ‘to taste’, ‘to partake’, ‘to obtain’ or ‘to come to know’—has to do with experience.">c</a></sup>of the heavenly gift and were made sharers in the Holy Spirit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="It seems clear enough that the reference is to people who have the Holy Spirit.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> who have experienced God's good event<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="I take it that “God's good event” refers to regeneration.">e</a></sup>and capabilities of the coming age,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Eternal life is a quality of life; it begins down here for those who have been regenerated.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and yet have fallen away—they would be crucifying the Son of God all over again, just for themselves, holding Him up to contempt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="No amount of theological shifting and squirming can nullify the plain meaning of this verse. The only way to crucify Jesus again is if you have already done so once before. No one is going to kill Jesus twice; if the first time was not good enough for you, you have had it! Anyone who holds Jesus “up to contempt” can expect wrath.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> Now the ground that drinks in the rain that frequently falls on it and produces a crop useful to those for whom it is really cultivated receives blessing from God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> but should it bear thorns and thistles, it is disqualified, almost a curse, whose destiny is a burning.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This ground was cultivated, not an unplowed weed patch, and planted with good seed; if it still produces thorns and thistles there is something wrong with it. Thistle seeds need to be burned, not plowed under.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## A word of encouragement

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> For all that, dear ones, we are confident of the better things concerning you—things that accompany salvation—even though we speak like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> Because God is not unjust so as to ignore your work, even the labor of love you have invested in His name, in that you have ministered to the saints and continue to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> You see, we really do want each of you to show this same diligence to the very end, so as to fulfill the hope;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> so that you not become lazy, but rather imitators of those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="We inherit the promises through faith and perseverance—the author keeps insisting: we have to persevere, hang on, stay with it, whatever, until the end of our earthly life.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Our secure anchor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> Now when God made a promise to Abraham, since He had no one greater to swear by, He swore by Himself</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, “Indeed, blessing I will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="I did not want to lose the Hebrew idiom; a freer rendering would be, ‘Indeed, I will bless you greatly, and I will multiply you greatly’—the “multiply” refers to descendants. See Genesis 22:17.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so, after he had been patient,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Well, his ‘patience’ was an up and down thing (Ishmael, and all that), but he never gave up altogether.">k</a></sup>he obtained the promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> Because since men swear by the greater, and for them the confirming oath puts an end to every dispute,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> similarly God, being determined to show even more clearly to the heirs of the promise the unchanging nature of His purpose, guaranteed it by an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> so that by two unalterable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we should have a strong encouragement, we who have taken refuge by grasping the hope set before us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> which hope we have as an anchor for the soul, both certain and secure,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Oh, praise God! With an “anchor” like that we have every reason to persevere.">l</a></sup>actually entering the inner sanctuary behind the curtain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> where Forerunner Jesus has entered on our behalf, having become High Priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.</span>
</div>
</div>
