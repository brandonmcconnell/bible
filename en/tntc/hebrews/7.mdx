## Melchizedek greater than Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; Acts 2:29; Acts 7:8–9"> Now consider how great this personage was, to whom even the patriarch Abraham gave a tenth of the spoils:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Why would Abraham give a tenth of the spoils to a stranger? Something about Melchizedek was very impressive. When Jehovah the Son appeared to Abraham to announce the destruction of Sodom, he knew who He was.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> You see, those of the sons of Levi who receive the priesthood have a command, according to the Law, to collect a tithe from the people—that is, from their brothers—even though they too came out of Abraham's body;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Hebrews 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> but he whose descent is not counted among them collected a tithe from Abraham and blessed him who had the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> Now without dispute the lesser is blessed by the greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20; Hebrews 5:6; Revelation 1:18"> Also, here men who die receive tithes, but there he of whom it is testified that he lives</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Genesis 14:20; Hebrews 7:4"> —in a word, even Levi, who receives tithes, paid the tithe through Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Hebrews 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> because he was still in the reproductive system of his forefather when Melchizedek met him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This is instructive. The inspired author affirms that Levi was in Abraham and also paid the tithe to Melchizedek, but when Abraham met Melchizedek not even Isaac had been born yet, much less Jacob and Levi. So the human spirit, the ‘image’ of the Creator, is transmitted by the male sperm. This agrees with Romans 5:12-21, where we are informed that Adam's sin contaminated all his descendants. When Eve sinned, she sinned alone. When Adam sinned, we did too.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Melchizedek greater than Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 8:7; Galatians 2:21; Galatians 4:9"> Now then, if there could be perfection through the Levitical priesthood (for on the basis of it the people had received the Law), what further need would there be for a different kind of priest to arise according to the order of Melchizedek, rather than being called according to the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> (Because when the priesthood is changed there is also a change of law, of necessity.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> For He of whom these things are said belongs to a different tribe, from which no one has officiated at the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; Luke 3:33"> For it is evident that our Lord arose out of Judah, of which tribe Moses spoke nothing concerning priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17–21; Hebrews 7:3"> And it all becomes clearer still if a different kind of priest, in the likeness of Melchizedek, appears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Colossians 2:20; Hebrews 7:28"> who has come, not on the basis of a law about a physical rule but on the basis of the power of an indestructible life.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“An indestructible life”—that is how come He can be “a priest forever”.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> Because He testifies:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">according to the order of Melchizedek.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:3; Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:9"> You see, there is an annulling of the former rule because of its weakness and uselessness</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 9:9"> (for the Law made nothing perfect), and a bringing in of a better hope, through which we draw near to God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Our eternal High Priest represents us eternally

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:6; Psalms 110:4"> Further, inasmuch as it was not without the taking of an oath</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 6:16–18"> —they indeed had become priests without an oath-taking, but He&#91;S&#93; with an oath by Him&#91;F&#93; who said to Him&#91;S&#93;:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Lord has sworn and will not change His&#91;F&#93; mind;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you are a priest forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">according to the order of Melchizedek”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some 2% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit “according to the order of Melchizedek” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:25; Genesis 44:32; Hebrews 8:6–12"> —by so much Jesus has become the guarantee of a better covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; Hebrews 7:8"> Also, there have been many priests because death prevented them from continuing in office;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 12:34; Hebrews 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> while He&#91;S&#93;, because He continues forever, has an unchangeable priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Therefore He is able to save absolutely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Wonderful! He is always ‘there’ for us.">f</a></sup>those who come to God through Him, since He always lives and can intercede on their behalf.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:14–15; 1 Peter 2:22"> Now such a High Priest was fitting for us—holy, innocent, undefiled, having been set apart from sinners, and having become higher than the heavens—</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:14"> who, unlike those high priests, does not need to offer up daily sacrifices, first for His own sins and then for the sins of the people; because He did this once for all when He offered up Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 7:21; Hebrews 1:2"> The Law appoints as high priest men who have weakness, but the word of the oath, that came after the Law, appointed Son, forever perfect.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Text has, literally, ‘having been perfected’ (or ‘completed’, or ‘consecrated’); the result of ‘having been perfected’ is to be perfect, and it is the result that is eternal, not the process.">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
