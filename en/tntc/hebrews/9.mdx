## The earthly sanctuary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Hebrews 9:10–11; Hebrews 8:2"> Now that first covenant did indeed have regulations for divine service and the earthly sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 26:35"> Yes, a tabernacle was set up: the first part, which is called the Holy Place, in which were the lamp stand, the table and the showbread;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 40:3; Matthew 27:51"> and behind the second curtain an area called the Holy of Holies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 17:10; 2 Chronicles 5:10; Exodus 40:3"> having a golden censer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The normal meaning of the Greek word here is precisely ‘censer’, but unfortunately modern versions like NIV, TEV, LB, NASB render ‘altar of incense’, thus setting up a contradiction with the Old Testament. According to Exodus 30:6 the altar of incense was placed in front of the curtain leading into the Holy of Holies, and so it was in the Holy Place, not the Holy of Holies. The only reference to this particular censer appears to be in Leviticus 16:12, where it was to be used behind the second curtain to hide the Ark with smoke. Since that censer would only be used once a year (on the day of atonement), it may well have been stored just behind a corner of the second curtain (where the high priest could retrieve it without looking in) and thus the author of Hebrews would be correct in saying that the censer was behind the second curtain, whereas the altar was in front of it. In any event, evidently that censer was only used within the Holy of Holies, and so it would be appropriate to say that the area ‘had’ a golden censer.">a</a></sup>and the ark of the covenant overlaid on all sides with gold—in it were a golden jar holding the manna, Aaron's rod that budded, and the stone tablets of the covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:2; Numbers 7:89; Exodus 25:17–22"> while above it were cherubim of glory overshadowing the mercy seat (this is not the place to go into detail about them).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Oh, but I wish he had!">b</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The service of the sanctuary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Exodus 27:21; Luke 1:8–11"> Now when these things had been thus prepared, the priests continually went into the first part, performing the divine services,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 16:34; Leviticus 5:18; Hebrews 10:19–20"> but into the second one only the high priest could go, once a year and only with blood, which he offered for himself and for the people's sins of ignorance;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="There was no sacrifice for deliberate sin.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 14:6; Hebrews 3:7; Hebrews 10:19–22"> the Holy Spirit was making clear that the way into the Most Holy Place was not yet open to the public while the first tabernacle was still in existence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:21; Hebrews 5:1; Romans 5:14"> It was a figure during the time then present, a time when both gifts and sacrifices were being offered that were incapable of clearing the conscience of the worshiper—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:15; Colossians 2:16; Leviticus 11:2–47"> they were only about foods and drinks and various ceremonial washings and physical regulations, things imposed until the time of restructuring.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, ‘the time of getting it right’.">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ serves in the heavenly sanctuary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 8:1–2"> But Christ entered once for all into the real Holy Places, having obtained eternal redemption—He had come as High Priest of the good things that are about to be, with the greater and more perfect tabernacle not made with hands, that is to say, not of this creation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:15"> and with His own blood, not that of goats and calves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 19:2–21; Leviticus 16:14–16; Acts 15:9"> For if the blood of bulls and goats, and the ashes of a heifer sprinkling those who had been ceremonially defiled, restored ceremonial purity to the body,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 Peter 1:19; 1 John 1:7"> how much more will the blood of Christ, who through the Holy Spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="We are accustomed to ‘the Eternal Spirit’, an attractive reading; I have followed the best line of transmission, albeit supported by only some 30% of the Greek manuscripts, plus the Latin tradition.">e</a></sup>offered Himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="If Christ “offered Himself”, He was both High Priest and Lamb. The Holy Spirit was with Him all the time. Verse 14 overtly mentions all three persons of the Trinity.">f</a></sup>unblemished to God, cleanse your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Romans 8:30; Romans 8:28"> For this reason He is mediator of a new covenant, so that those who have been called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Who have been called”—the author alludes to God's sovereignty.">g</a></sup>may receive the promised eternal inheritance—since a death has occurred for the redemption of the transgressions under the first covenant.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="All the animals sacrificed under the first covenant could not redeem; their effect was temporary. The salvation of all Old Testament saints depends upon the shed blood of God's Lamb.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The earthly sanctuary inaugurated with blood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12"> Now where there is a will, the death of the one who made it must be established;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:21; John 14:27; Galatians 3:15"> because a will is in force after men are dead, since it never takes effect while the one who made it lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 9:22"> Therefore, not even the first covenant was inaugurated without blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 24:5–11; Numbers 19:6; Hebrews 10:4"> because when every commandment of the Law had been proclaimed by Moses to all the people, he took the blood of calves and goats, with water and scarlet wool and hyssop, and sprinkled both the scroll itself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some of the Law had already been written down.">i</a></sup>and all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 24:8; Matthew 26:28; Hebrews 13:20"> saying, “This is the blood of the covenant which God has decreed to you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="See Exodus 24:8.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 8:19; Exodus 29:36"> Then he sprinkled both the tabernacle and all the vessels of the divine service with the blood in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 6:7; Leviticus 4:35"> In fact, according to the Law nearly everything is cleansed with blood, and without shedding of blood there is no remission.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="“Without shedding of blood there is no remission”—an important statement.">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The heavenly sanctuary inaugurated with Christ's blood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–21; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:14"> So then, it was necessary for the copies of the things in the heavens to be cleansed with such, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than those.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Dear me, how could anything in Heaven be in need of cleansing? Well, Satan (Lucifer) had been there, and probably did some contaminating before his open rebellion. In fact, he still appears before God's throne.">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:11–12; Hebrews 9:23"> For Christ did not enter into Holy Places made by hands, mere copies of the true ones,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The ‘real’ world is the spirit world, not the physical.">m</a></sup>but into Heaven itself, now to appear in God's presence on our behalf;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 9:14"> but not in order to offer Himself repeatedly, like the high priest who entered the Most Holy Place year by year with another's blood</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; 1 Peter 1:20; Hebrews 1:2"> —otherwise He would have had to suffer many times since the creation of the world; but now, once for all at the end of the ages, He has been revealed to nullify sin by the sacrifice of Himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> Also, inasmuch as men are destined to die but once, after which the judgment,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="This verse contradicts any notion of ‘reincarnation’. We only die once, physically.">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Titus 2:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Romans 6:10"> so the Christ also, having been offered once for all to bear the sins of many—He will appear a second time to those who are eagerly waiting for Him, apart from sin, into salvation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="I suppose this is a reference to the Rapture, but however we receive our glorified bodies, at that point we receive our definitive transferal “into salvation”.">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
