## “Let us run with endurance”

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Philippians 2:16"> So then, since we are surrounded by such a great cloud of witnesses, we too must run with endurance the race that is set before us, laying aside every impediment and the sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="I suppose that each of us has his own ‘besetting sin’—what gives me trouble will not necessarily be the same thing that gives you trouble.">a</a></sup>that so easily ensnares,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:23–24"> looking unto Jesus, the Founder and Perfecter of the Faith, who for the joy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="I imagine that that “joy” has something to do with His Bride.">b</a></sup>that was set before Him endured a cross, scorning its ignominy, and took His seat at the right hand of the throne of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## All legitimate children receive discipline

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> Do consider Him who endured such hostility from sinners against Himself, so as not to grow weary, losing courage in your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> In your struggle against sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="In the context this presumably refers to sin in the world, not within.">c</a></sup>you have not yet resisted to the point of bloodshed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Text does not say whose blood.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> And you have forgotten the exhortation that instructs you as sons:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My son, do not make light of the Lord's discipline,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor lose heart when you are reproved by Him;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; Psalms 119:75"> <span className="pl pl-1">because whom the Lord loves He chastens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yes scourges every son whom He accepts.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Proverbs 3:11-12. When I was a boy my father had a horse, a gentle creature, and a proper horsewhip to go with it. I found myself on the business end of that whip more than once (more often than did the horse), and I can assure the reader that it was not a pleasant experience. So God's scourgings tend not to be very pleasant; but if we end up partaking of His holiness (verse 10 below), it will be worth it.">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> If you are enduring discipline, God is dealing with you as with sons; for what son is there whom a father does not discipline?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; Psalms 73:1"> But if you are without discipline (something everyone undergoes),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="At that time there was probably no culture dominated by relativistic humanism.">f</a></sup>then you are illegitimate and not sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Furthermore, we have had our human fathers as correctors and respected them. Shall we not much more readily be in subjection to the Father of spirits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“The Father of spirits”—an interesting concept. All created spirits owe their existence to Him.">g</a></sup>and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> Now they indeed disciplined us during a short period as seemed best to them, but He for our profit, so that we may partake of His holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> Now no discipline seems to be pleasant at the time, but painful; yet afterward it yields the peaceable fruit of righteousness to those who have been exercised by it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is the secret—we must be “exercised” by it; that is, we must learn whatever lesson the Lord is trying to teach us.">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Watch your step!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore strengthen the listless hands and weakened knees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; Galatians 6:1"> and make straight paths for your feet, so that the lame not be turned aside but rather be healed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="I take the point to be that we should not turn our Christian walk into a bunch of complicated rules—keep it simple.">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> Pursue peace with all, and the holiness without which no one will see the Lord;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="If we need to “pursue” this holiness, then it is experiential, not positional. God rewards those who “earnestly seek Him” (11:6 above).">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> taking care that no one come short of the grace of God, that no root of bitterness springing up cause trouble and thereby many be defiled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> that no one be a fornicator, or worldly like Esau, who in exchange for one meal gave up his birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> Because you do indeed know that afterward, when he wanted to inherit the blessing he was rejected; he found no place for a change of mind,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Neither Isaac nor God changed his mind; Jacob was in and Esau was out.">k</a></sup>though he sought it diligently with tears.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Sinai X Zion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> Now you have not come to a touchable mountain burning with fire, to blackness and darkness, to tempest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> to a trumpet blast and spoken words such that those who heard begged that no further word be spoken to them</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> (because they could not bear what was commanded: “If even an animal touches the mountain it must be stoned!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts add ‘or shot with an arrow’ (as in AV and NKJV).">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> and the sight was so terrifying that Moses said, “I am terrified and trembling!”);</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> but you have come to Mount Zion, even to the City of the Living God, Heavenly Jerusalem; to myriads of angels in festal gathering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> to an assembly of firstborn ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="I wonder who these “firstborn ones” are.">m</a></sup>who have been enrolled in heaven; to God, Judge of all; to the spirits of the perfected righteous;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> to Jesus, Mediator of a new covenant, and to a blood of sprinkling that speaks better things than that of Abel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Let me try to list the ‘reception committee’: 1) the City; 2) angels; 3) firstborn ones (different from angels?); 4) God the Father; 5) the perfected righteous; 6) Jesus; 7) the Blood. An impressive list! What did Abel's blood say?—murder and punishment! What does Jesus' blood say?—redemption and forgiveness! Oh praise God!">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Warning: “Our God is a consuming fire”

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> See to it that you not refuse Him who speaks. Because if they did not escape who refused Him who gave divine warning on earth, how much less will we, if we turn away from Him who warns from Heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> Then His voice shook the earth, but now He has promised, saying, “Yet once more I shake not only the earth but also the heaven.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="See Haggai 2:6.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> Now the ‘yet once more’ clearly indicates the removal of the things being shaken (created things), so that the unshakables may remain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> Therefore, since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us hold on to the grace by which we serve God acceptably, with reverence and godly fear;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> because our God is indeed a consuming fire.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="One gets the impression that taking God, and our life in Christ, for granted is definitely not a good idea.">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
