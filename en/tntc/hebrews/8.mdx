## Priest and Mediator of a better covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> Now the main point of the things we are saying is this: We do have such a High Priest, who sat down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="He took His seat; it was His prerogative.">a</a></sup>at the right of the Majesty's throne in the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> Minister of the Holy Places, that is, of the true Tabernacle which the Lord set up, not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices (it follows that it is necessary for this One also to have something He might offer)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> —if He were on earth He would not be a priest, there already being priests who offer the gifts according to the Law;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This presumably indicates that this missive was written before the destruction of the temple in 70 AD, when the sacrifices ceased.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> who serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Tabernacle assembled by Moses was but a ‘shadow’ of the real thing; which probably means that the spiritual realm is more ‘real’ than the physical one—which is difficult for us to believe, so very few live on that basis.">c</a></sup>(that is why Moses was warned by God when he was ready to assemble the Tabernacle: “See to it,” He says, “that you make everything according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain”)—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> but now He has obtained a more excellent ministry, just as He is Mediator of a better covenant, which was enacted as law on the basis of better promises.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Wow! How is that for convoluted syntax—verses 3-6 form a single sentence. It almost feels ‘Pauline’!">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## A new covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> Now if there had been nothing wrong with that first covenant, no place would have been sought for a second one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> but finding fault with them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is right, the people, since they did not live up to the covenant. The following quote is from Jeremiah 31:31-34.">e</a></sup>He says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Listen! Days are coming,” says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“when I will establish a new covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The author equates the second covenant, inaugurated by Christ, with Jeremiah's new covenant, so the reference to ‘the house of Israel’ and ‘the house of Judah’ means that there are no ‘lost’ tribes—God knows who they are and where they are (see Revelation 7:4-8).">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">—not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day when I took them by the hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to lead them out of the land of Egypt,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“I took them by the hand”—recall that they were led by the column of cloud/fire from the very first.">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they did not abide by my covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and so I disregarded them,” says the Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Now this is the covenant that I will make</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the house of Israel after those days,” says the Lord,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="“The house of Israel” is not the Church! I would say that verses 10 and 11 here will be fulfilled during the Messianic Millennial Kingdom. Notice “they will be my people”.">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will put my laws into their mind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and write them on their hearts;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will be their God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be my people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">Further, not one of them will teach his neighbor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and not one his brother saying, ‘Get to know the Lord!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they all will know me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the least of them to the greatest of them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will be merciful to their wrongdoings;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not at all remember their sins and their lawless deeds.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some 3.5% of the Greek manuscripts omit “and their lawless deeds” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 7:11–12"> By saying ‘new’ He has made the first one ‘old’; and what is obsolete and aging is near the end.</span>
</div>
