## Redeem the time

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17–18; Philippians 4:6; Ephesians 6:18"> Continue earnestly in prayer, being vigilant in it with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; Acts 14:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:1–2"> at the same time praying for us, that God may open to us a door for the Word, to speak the mystery of Christ (for which I am also in chains),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:1–4; Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 4:6"> that I may make it manifest, as I ought to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15–17; 1 Thessalonians 4:12; James 3:13"> Behave wisely toward outsiders, making the most of the opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; 1 Peter 3:15; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let your word always be with grace, seasoned with salt,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="I wonder what “seasoned with salt” means. At least what we say should not be ‘insipid’, pointless.">b</a></sup>to know how you ought to answer each one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21–23; Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12"> Tychicus will tell you all the news about me; he is a dear brother, a faithful minister and fellow slave in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:22; Colossians 2:2; Philippians 2:28"> I am sending him to you for this very purpose, that he may know your circumstances and encourage your hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philemon 1:10–19; Colossians 4:7"> with Onesimus, a faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you; they will tell you everything that is happening here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 19:29"> Aristarchus my fellow prisoner greets you, as does Mark the cousin of Barnabas (about whom you received instructions; if he comes to you, welcome him),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Evidently Barnabas succeeded in ‘recovering’ Mark, which Paul here recognizes.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 11:2; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 10:45"> and Jesus who is called Justus. These are my only fellow workers for the Kingdom of God who are of the circumcision; they have been a comfort to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:30; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:7"> Epaphras, who is one of you, a slave of Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Instead of “Christ”, perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘Christ Jesus’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">d</a></sup>greets you; he is always wrestling in prayer for you, that you may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="We need more people like Epaphras, and that is a great way to pray.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 2:1; Romans 10:2; Revelation 1:11"> I bear him witness that he has a great zeal for you, and for those in Laodicea and those in Hierapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philemon 1:24; 2 Timothy 4:10–11"> Luke the beloved physician<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Presumably this is where we get that title for Luke.">f</a></sup>greets you, also Demas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:5; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Greet the brothers who are in Laodicea, and Nymphas and the congregation at his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Perhaps 1% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, have ‘her’ (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">g</a></sup>house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:27; 2 Thessalonians 3:14"> Now when this letter has been read among you, see that it is also read in the Laodicean congregation,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Paul expected his letters to have a wide circulation.">h</a></sup>and that you in turn read the letter from Laodicea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="We have no copy of this letter, so presumably it was not inspired, and therefore not preserved. It is possible, even probable, that Paul wrote other letters that were not preserved.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philemon 1:2; Acts 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> And say to Archippus, “See to it that you complete the work that you received in the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="This personal word is unusual in a letter to the church. Since Paul had never been to Colosse, he must have known Archippus from somewhere else, but happened to know that he was in Colosse at that time. However, that exhortation is good for all of us. The older I get, the larger that exhortation looms in my mind.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; 2 Timothy 4:22; 1 Timothy 6:21"> This greeting is in my own hand: Paul.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The letter was dictated to a scribe or secretary, but Paul wrote the last line himself; this was his signature, authenticating the letter.">k</a></sup>Remember my chains. The Grace be with you. Amen.</span>
</div>
