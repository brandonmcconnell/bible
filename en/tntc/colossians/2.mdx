## A wealth of confident understanding

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:29; Philippians 1:30; Revelation 3:14–22"> I want you to know how great is my concern for you and those in Laodicea, even all who have not met me personally,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Paul plainly states that he has yet to visit Colosse and Laodicea.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; Colossians 4:8; Ephesians 6:22"> that their hearts may be encouraged, being united in love and into a great wealth of confident understanding,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="To have a confident understanding of God's Truth is indeed a great treasure (but it is not for the lazy).">b</a></sup>into a real knowledge of the mystery of the God and Father and of the Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some 3% of the Greek manuscripts omit “and Father and of the” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Romans 11:33"> in whom all the treasures of the wisdom and the knowledge are hidden.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="As the primary agent in the creation of our world, the Son is the Source of all true wisdom and knowledge relative to life on this planet.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Ephesians 4:14; Romans 16:18–19"> Now I say this so that no one may deceive you with specious arguments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> For although in fact I am physically absent, yet my spirit is with you, rejoicing as I observe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The basic meaning of the verb rendered ‘observe’ is to observe with the physical eye, which implies being within range and with no obstacles. But Paul obviously was not there as he wrote, in fact had never been there. He affirms that it is his spirit that is doing the seeing and rejoicing. The Protestant Reformation was a result, in part, of the Renaissance, with its emphasis on reason, so Protestants in general have had trouble understanding the spirit world. So the tendency of commentators has been to ‘spiritualize’ Paul's statement, rather than take it literally. Of course they have done the same with 2 Kings 5:26 (where Elisha says his spirit went with Gehazi) and 6:12 (where Elisha listens in on the king of Syria), not to mention John 1:48 (where the Lord Himself saw Nathanael when not physically present). Here in Brazil we have many former Spiritists and Satanists who have been converted and they affirm that they used to project their spirits outside their bodies and go all over the place—several of my former students, now genuinely converted, have told me they used to do it—but they could do so only with the assistance of a fallen angel (demon). Satan's ‘thing’ is to be like the Most High, so he is always trying to imitate Him; perhaps God's servants used to know how to do this, only without such assistance [I have heard of a few in our day who still do].">e</a></sup>your orderliness and the firmness of your faith in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 1 John 2:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:1"> So then, keep on walking in the Christ, Jesus the Sovereign, just as you received Him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:17; Jeremiah 17:8; Ephesians 2:20–22"> having been rooted and being built up in Him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Having been rooted” refers to what has already happened; “being built up” refers to the ongoing process.">f</a></sup>and being established in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding in it with thanksgiving.</span>
</div>

## Christ VS ‘the world’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:20; Ephesians 5:6; Matthew 7:15"> Be careful that no one captures you through philosophy and empty deceit, according to human tradition, according to the basic principles of the world and not according to Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The “basic principles of the world”, including the academic community (and the media, and commerce, etc.), are controlled by Satan, and are contrary to the values of Christ's Kingdom. Unfortunately very few churches give adequate teaching on this subject.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:19; John 10:30; John 1:14"> Because all the Fullness of the Godhead dwells in Him in bodily form,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 3:19; Colossians 1:16–18; Ephesians 1:20–23"> and you are complete in Him who is the head over all rule and authority.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Wow! There is a human body (glorified) seated at the Father's right, and anyone who is in Christ is there too (Ephesians 2:6).">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 2:29; Philippians 3:3; Romans 6:6"> In Him you were also ‘circumcised’ by the putting off of the body of the sins of the flesh, not with a circumcision done by hands but with the circumcision of the Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 6:3–5; 1 Peter 3:21; Galatians 3:27"> having been buried with Him in the baptism.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="I find it curious that Paul, writing to non-Jews, uses a very Jewish figure, which the non-Jews probably would not understand without instruction on the subject (although there would be converted Jews in the congregation). It is the resurrection that guarantees our future.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5–6; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 2:1"> Yes you, though being dead in your trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh—He has made you alive together with Him; having forgiven us all our trespasses;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This paragraph is made up of a series of participial phrases wherein Paul switches from the 2nd person, in the independent clause, to the 1st person, making inclusive statements.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; Ephesians 2:14–16; Isaiah 43:25"> having wiped out the written record about us that was against us (based on the ordinances),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The “written record” has generally been understood to refer to the Law in some way, but I believe it makes better sense to take it as the record of what each of us has done (remember ‘the books’ in Revelation 20:12), which of course is negative. Having been forgiven, the record is nailed to the cross, and that is very good news.">l</a></sup>indeed He has taken it out of the way, having nailed it to the cross;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; Ephesians 4:8; Matthew 12:29"> having stripped the principalities and the authorities, He made a public spectacle of them, having triumphed over them by it &#91;the cross&#93;.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="This verse gives an important aspect of Christ's victory by the cross and resurrection. Jehovah the Son took on human form in order to destroy Satan (Hebrews 2:14), and He succeeded. A victorious Roman general would bring back a certain number of the conquered soldiers and parade them, naked and in chains, before the Roman populace—total humiliation. Colossians 2:15 says that Christ did something similar to Satan and the high ranking angels that fell with him.">m</a></sup></span>

## Don't be disqualified!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 14:5–6; Romans 14:10; Galatians 4:10"> So do not let anyone judge you about food or drink, or with respect to a feast day or a new moon or Sabbaths,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 8:5; John 1:17"> which things are but a shadow of those to come, while the body is Christ's.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; 1 John 4:1–2; Colossians 2:23"> Nor let anyone disqualify you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="If you release your hold on “the Head” to get involved in the sorts of things mentioned, you will be disqualified. An athlete who is disqualified does not win. Have you ever heard teaching on the subject of ‘taking possession’? Be careful!">n</a></sup>taking pleasure in a subservience and devotion to the angels, ‘taking possession’ of things that he has not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Some 1.5% of the Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, omit “not” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">o</a></sup>seen, being puffed up by his carnal mind to no useful purpose,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:15–16; John 15:4–6; 2 Peter 3:18"> and not holding fast to the Head, from whom all the body, supported and held together by its joints and ligaments, grows with the increase that is from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Colossians 2:16; Galatians 4:3"> Now then, since you died with Christ away from the basic principles of the world, why, as though still living in it, are you subjecting yourselves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="They are doing this voluntarily, but they shouldn't.">p</a></sup>to regulations</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; Genesis 3:3; Isaiah 52:11"> —Don't handle! Don't taste! Don't touch!—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:14; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> (all of which result in corruption through overuse)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Actually, there are things that it is better not to taste or handle, but the overuse of such restrictions will give negative results.">q</a></sup>according to the commands and teachings of men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:8; Colossians 2:18; Colossians 2:8"> Such things do indeed have an appearance of wisdom in self-made religion and subservience and asceticism, but are not of any value against the indulgence of the flesh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="“The flesh” is used to refer to our ‘old man’ or sin nature, not the meat on our bones.
