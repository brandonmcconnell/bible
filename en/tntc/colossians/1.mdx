## The supremacy of Christ described

<div className="bt">

<li id="fn-al"><a href="#fnref-al" className="fnb">al</a> <span className="fnr">1:26</span> I consider that ‘secret’ is a better rendering than ‘mystery’. The truth about the Church is not all that mysterious, it just had not been explained before.</li>
<li id="fn-am"><a href="#fnref-am" className="fnb">am</a> <span className="fnr">1:27</span> Although God's saving interest has always included the whole world (“all the families of the earth”—Genesis 12:3), throughout the OT God worked mainly through His specially chosen people, Israel. Now the Bride of Christ includes Jews and non-Jews on an equal footing.</li>
<li id="fn-an"><a href="#fnref-an" className="fnb">an</a> <span className="fnr">1:27</span> Christ is ‘the wealth of the glory’, but He must be in us for us to have any hope of sharing in that glory; and we must proclaim Him to the nations for them to have any hope.</li>
<li id="fn-ao"><a href="#fnref-ao" className="fnb">ao</a> <span className="fnr">1:28</span> In the Great Commission (Matthew 28:19) Christ commanded His followers to ‘make disciples’ (which involves a whole lot more than just ‘winning souls’), and Paul declares that to be his objective. Instead of “Christ”, perhaps 60% of the Greek manuscripts have ‘Christ Jesus’ (as in AV and NKJV). I follow the best line of transmission.</li>
<li id="fn-ap"><a href="#fnref-ap" className="fnb">ap</a> <span className="fnr">1:29</span> If we want to see God manifesting His power through us, we must subordinate ourselves to His working.</li>
</ol>
