## The Day of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:9; Matthew 24:36"> Now concerning the times and the seasons, brothers, you do not need to be written to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; 2 Peter 3:10; Matthew 24:42–44"> for you yourselves know very well that the Day of the Lord comes just like a thief in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 21:34–35; Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:26–30"> For whenever they say, “Peace and safety!” then sudden destruction is standing near them, like labor pains to a pregnant woman, and they will not escape.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The destruction will not necessarily strike when they speak. Just as a pregnant woman knows she will have labor pains (no Caesarean section)—the only uncertainty is when, not if—so the destruction will be inescapable.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Live as sons of light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:8; 2 Peter 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But you, brothers, are not in darkness so that this Day should come upon you like a thief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; John 12:36"> You are all sons of light and sons of day; we are not of night, nor of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 1:13; Matthew 24:42; Luke 22:46"> So then, let us not sleep,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here the reference is to physical sleep or mental lethargy, not death.">b</a></sup>like the rest, but let us stay alert and sober;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="If the Rapture is ‘partial’ (Matthew 25:1-13), only the ‘alert’ will escape.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 13:13; 1 Corinthians 15:34; 2 Peter 2:13"> because those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk, get drunk at night;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:12–13; Ephesians 6:11; 1 Peter 1:13"> but we being of day, let us be sober, putting on a breastplate of faith and love, and a helmet of hope of salvation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Faith and love for the heart, hope for the head.">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 2:10; 2 Timothy 2:10"> For God did not appoint us to undergo wrath but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="I suppose this refers to both manifestations of the Wrath: the Tribulation and the Lake.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:8–9; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 8:34"> who died for us so that whether awake or asleep we may live together with Him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Here is the bottom line: to be with the Creator throughout eternity.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 10:25; Romans 15:2"> Therefore encourage one another and build each other up, as in fact you do.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Esteem spiritual leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 5:17–18"> Now we urge you, brothers, to recognize those who labor among you and are over you in the Lord and admonish you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:50; Hebrews 12:14; Galatians 6:6"> and to esteem them very highly in love because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A variety of instructions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Romans 14:1; Acts 20:35"> Now we exhort you, brothers: admonish the disorderly, encourage the fainthearted, be supportive of the weak, be patient toward all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Notice that the response is appropriate to the occasion, or the need.">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Galatians 6:10; Leviticus 19:18"> See that no one pays back bad for bad to anyone,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The term ‘bad’ is less strong than the term for malignant evil, so the reference is not to malignant activity.">h</a></sup>but always pursue the good, both for one another and for all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The verb ‘pursue’ does not suggest a passive attitude; we should be actively promoting the good for the society at large wherever we live. The result will be a better place to live.">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Philippians 4:4; Matthew 5:12"> Rejoice always!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:12; Colossians 4:2; Ephesians 6:18"> Pray continually!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:20; Philippians 4:6; Colossians 3:17"> Give thanks in everything, for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="There are those who say we should give thanks for everything, as distinct from in everything. I doubt that our Lord in the garden of Gethsemane gave thanks for the suffering He was facing and already enduring. To give thanks in a distressful situation is a declaration of confidence in God and His disposition of our affairs.">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; Acts 7:51; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Do not quench the Spirit!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="When you quench a lighted candle, you put out its light. To quench the Spirit is presumably to ‘put out’ or refuse His light, to suppress or ignore His voice when He speaks to us—this would include any rejection of the revealed will of God.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:3–6; 1 Corinthians 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> Do not disdain prophesies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:10"> but test everything;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="All prophesy should be tested, but not disdained or rejected.">l</a></sup>hold on to the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Philippians 4:8; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33"> Keep away from every form of evil!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The evil here is aggressive, or malignant.">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Complete sanctification

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:24; Hebrews 4:12; 1 Corinthians 1:8–9"> Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole spirit and soul and body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="I take it that the grammatical structure of this phrase, “the spirit and the soul and the body” (in Greek), demands a tripartite/trichotomous view of the human being. I confess that I have trouble imagining complete sanctification for the body, in this life.">n</a></sup>be preserved blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Numbers 23:19; 1 Corinthians 1:9"> He who calls you is faithful, and He will do it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Farewell

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 13:18–19; Philippians 1:19; Philemon 1:22"> Brothers, pray for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Corinthians 16:20"> Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 4:16; Acts 19:13; 1 Timothy 1:3"> I adjure you by the Lord that this letter be read to all the holy brothers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="To adjure by the Lord that the letter be read is tantamount to claiming inspiration for it. By extension, does not “all the holy brothers” include us?">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.</span>
</div>
