## The Rapture4:13 This paragraph defines the Rapture: Jesus comes down into the atmosphere of this planet (‘clouds’) and collects all those who belong to Him; the bodies of those who have died physically will be resurrected and reunited with their spirits; those still living will not have to die (death is separation); all their bodies will be glorified; the Christ will have His Bride.

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> because the Lord Himself—with a commanding shout,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="One is reminded of Jesus' words in John 5:25-29.
