## Concern leads to action

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:15; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17"> So when we could not stand it any longer, we thought it good to be left in Athens alone</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:22–23; Ephesians 6:21–22; Philippians 2:19–25"> and sent Timothy, our brother and minister of God, also our fellow worker<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Instead of “minister of God, also our fellow worker”, just two Greek manuscripts, of objectively inferior quality, have ‘fellow worker of God’, to be followed by NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.">a</a></sup>in the Gospel of Christ, to confirm you and encourage you concerning your faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Romans 5:3; Acts 9:16"> that no one be unsettled by these afflictions; for you yourselves know that we are appointed to this.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="We are appointed to affliction in this life. How many sermons have you heard on this topic? If the Son learned obedience through suffering (Hebrews 5:8), how about us? “Whom the Lord loves He chastens, and scourges every son whom He receives” (Hebrews 12:6). Have you ever been scourged, literally? I have—I didn't enjoy it.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; 2 Corinthians 8:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> For indeed, when we were with you we kept telling you in advance that we were going to be afflicted;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="It was all a calculated risk. The messengers had their eyes open and wanted to make sure their hearers went into their new life with their eyes open—there were no misleading promises of peace and prosperity!">c</a></sup>just as it happened, in fact, as you know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:13–14; Matthew 4:3; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> Yes, that is why, no longer standing it, I sent to find out about your faith, for fear that somehow the tempter had successfully tempted you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Paul was well aware that Satan would not leave them alone.">d</a></sup>and our labor come to be for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> But now that Timothy has come to us from you and brought us glad news of your faith and love, and that you have good memories of us always, longing to see us, just as we also long to see you</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> —because of this, brothers, in all our affliction and distress we were encouraged about you by your faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Revelation 3:11"> for now we live, if you are standing firm in the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Again, I find the level of Paul's involvement with them to be surprising. (It would be physically and emotionally impossible to be this involved with every church he started.) But as a responsible father, he is properly concerned for their growth and well being (verse 10).">e</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Paul's desire to go to them

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 71:14–15; Psalms 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> With what thankfulness can we repay God for you, for all the joy with which we are rejoicing in the presence of our God because of you;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="To see our spiritual children growing in their walk with God is a source of considerable joy.">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> praying most earnestly night and day, that we may see your face and complete the things lacking in your faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now may our God and Father Himself and our Lord Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Perhaps 4% of the Greek manuscripts omit “Christ” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">g</a></sup>direct our way to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> And may the Lord cause you to increase and abound in love toward each other and toward all, just as we also do toward you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; Ephesians 5:27"> so as to establish your hearts blameless in holiness before our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Perhaps 20% of the Greek manuscripts omit “Christ” (as in NIV, NASB, LB, TEV, etc.).">h</a></sup>with all His holy ones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Another reference to the Accounting: a heart “blameless in holiness” is the ticket.
