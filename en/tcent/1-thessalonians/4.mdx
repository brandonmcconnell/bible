## Living to Please God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:18"> Finally, brothers, we ask and urge you in the Lord Jesus that, just as you learned from us how you ought to walk and to please<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="God, ¦ God, just as you are doing, that CT">a</a></sup>God, you do so more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:10"> For you know the commands we gave you by the authority of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:23"> It is God's will that you be sanctified, that you abstain from fornication,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:4"> and that each of you know how to control your own body in holiness and honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:8; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:30–31; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:17–19"> not in lustful passion like the Gentiles who do not know God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:4; Exodus 20:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:19"> In this matter no one should wrong or take advantage of his brother, because the Lord is an avenger in all these things, as we told you before and solemnly warned you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9–12"> For God did not call us to be impure, but to live in holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:48"> Therefore whoever rejects this command does not reject man but God, who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="has given ¦ gives NA SBL WH">b</a></sup>has given his Holy Spirit to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="you ¦ us TR">c</a></sup>you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:23"> Now concerning brotherly love, you have no need for anyone to write to you, for you yourselves have been taught by God to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3"> And indeed you do love all the brothers throughout Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers, to do so more and more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:4"> We urge you to aspire to lead a quiet life, to attend to your own affairs, and to work with your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="own ¦ — SBL TH WH">d</a></sup>own hands, just as we commanded you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:17"> so that you may walk properly toward outsiders and not be in need of anything.</span>

## The Coming of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:10"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="we ¦ I TR">e</a></sup>we do not want you to be uninformed, brothers, about those who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="have fallen ¦ are CT">f</a></sup>have fallen asleep, so that you may not be grieved like others who do not have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> For since we believe that Jesus died and rose again, we also believe that God will bring with Jesus those who have fallen asleep in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:51–53; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:19"> According to the word of the Lord, we declare to you that we who are alive, who are left behind until the coming of the Lord, will certainly not precede those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:51–52; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:30–31"> For the Lord himself will descend from heaven with a commanding shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet call of God, and the dead in Christ will rise first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:52; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 11:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:3"> Then we who are alive, who are left behind, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And so we will always be with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:14"> Therefore encourage one another with these words.</span>
</div>
