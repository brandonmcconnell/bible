## The Day of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 1:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:36"> Now concerning the times and seasons, brothers, you have no need for anything to be written to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 16:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:42–44"> For you yourselves know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:34–35; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:37–39; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 17:26–30"> When people are saying, “Peace and safety,” destruction will come upon them suddenly, like labor pains upon a woman with child, and they will certainly not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> But you, brothers, are not in darkness so that this day should catch you like<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="a thief ¦ thieves WH">a</a></sup>a thief.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:34; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:13"> For those who sleep, sleep at night; and those who get drunk, get drunk at night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:12–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:13"> But since<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="we ¦ you ANT">c</a></sup>we are of the day, let us be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, and the hope of salvation as a helmet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:10"> For God has not appointed us to suffer wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:8–9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:34"> who died for us so that whether we are awake or asleep we may live together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:29; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:2"> Therefore encourage one another and build each other up, even as you are doing.</span>

## Final Instructions and Benediction

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:17–18"> Now we ask you, brothers, to recognize those who labor among you, who lead you in the Lord and admonish you,</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:1; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:35"> And we urge you, brothers, to admonish those who are idle, encourage the fainthearted, support the weak, and be patient toward all.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:3–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:1; Numbers 11:25–29"> Do not despise<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="prophecies, but test ¦ prophecies. <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:24; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:8–9"> Now may the God of peace himself sanctify you completely, and may your entire spirit, soul, and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
