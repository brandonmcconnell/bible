## Paul's Ministry in Thessalonica

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:58"> For you yourselves know, brothers, that our visit to you was not in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:22–24; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 4:13; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:2–9"> On the contrary, even though we had previously suffered and been mistreated in Philippi, as you know, we had boldness in our God to declare to you the gospel of God in the face of strong opposition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:7"> For our exhortation does not come from error or impurity or with deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:7"> but just as we have been approved by God to be entrusted with the gospel, so we speak, not as pleasing men, but God, who examines our hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:17; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:33"> God is our witness that we never came with words of flattery or with a pretext for greed, as you well know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 5:41"> Nor did we seek glory from men (neither from you nor from others), though as Christ's apostles we could have asserted our authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:24–25"> Instead, we were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="gentle 85.9% ¦ infants NA WH 11.5%">a</a></sup>gentle among you, like a nursing mother caring for her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:17"> We had such strong affection for you that we were pleased to share with you not only the gospel of God but also our own selves, because you had become dear to us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:7–9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:9; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:3"> For you remember, brothers, our labor and hardship. Working night and day so as not to be a burden on any of you, we preached to you the gospel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:12"> You are witnesses, and so is God, of how devoutly, righteously, and blamelessly we conducted ourselves among you who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:14–15; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:21"> As you know, we dealt with each one of you like a father deals with his own children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 1:6–7"> exhorting you, encouraging you, and testifying that you should walk in a manner worthy of God, who calls you into his own kingdom and glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:23"> We also give thanks to God without ceasing because, when you received the word of God that you heard from us, you accepted it not as the word of men, but as it truly is, the word of God, which is at work in you who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:33–34; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:7–8"> For you, brothers, became imitators of the churches of God in Christ Jesus that are in Judea, because you suffered the same things from your own countrymen that they did from the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 11:48–53; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 7:52"> who killed the Lord Jesus and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="their own 94% ¦ the CT 5%">b</a></sup>their own prophets, and who also drove<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="us ¦ you ST">c</a></sup>us out. They displease God and are hostile to all men</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:13; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:16"> by hindering us from telling the Gentiles how they can be saved. In this way they are always filling up the measure of their sins, but wrath has come upon them at last.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Longing to See the Thessalonians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:6"> As for us, brothers, when we were bereaved of you for a brief hour (being physically absent, but with you in our hearts), we longed with great eagerness to see you face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:13; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Therefore ¦ For CT">d</a></sup>Therefore we wanted to come to you—certainly I, Paul, tried to do so again and again—but Satan hindered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:16"> For what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting in the presence of our Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ ANT TR">e</a></sup>Jesus at his coming? Is it not you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:7; Proverbs 17:6"> Yes, you are our glory and joy!</span>
</div>
