## The Thessalonians' Good Example

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8–9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:15–16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:4"> We always give thanks to God for you all, making mention of you in our prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:13; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:6"> remembering without ceasing, in the presence of our God and Father, your work of faith, your labor of love, and your endurance of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:4–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:10"> For we know, brothers beloved by God, that you have been chosen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:7–9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:2–5"> because our gospel did not come to you in word only, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and with much conviction. You know what sort of men we proved to be among you for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:16; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:52"> And you became imitators of us and of the Lord when you received the word in much affliction with the joy of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:12"> As a result you have become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="examples ¦ an example CT">b</a></sup>examples to all the believers in Macedonia and Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:14–18; Isaiah 52:7"> For not only has the word of the Lord sounded forth from you in Macedonia and Achaia, but reports of your faith in God have gone out everywhere, so that we have no need to say anything about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:8–9"> For the people themselves report what kind of reception we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="had ¦ have ST">c</a></sup>had among you, and how you turned away from idols to serve the living and true God,</span>
