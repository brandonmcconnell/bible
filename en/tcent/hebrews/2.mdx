## Warning Against Neglecting Salvation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 8:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> Therefore we must pay greater attention to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 7:53; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:19; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:28"> For if the message spoken through angels was valid and every transgression and disobedience received a just retribution,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:28–29; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:25; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:9"> how will we escape if we neglect so great a salvation? This salvation was first announced by the Lord, and it was confirmed to us by those who heard him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:20"> God also bore further witness with signs, wonders, various miracles, and gifts of the Holy Spirit distributed according to his will.</span>
</div>

## Jesus the Author of Salvation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 11:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:5"> For it was not to angels that God subjected the world to come, about which we are speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Psalms 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But in one place someone has testified:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is man that yoʋ are mindful of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or the son of man that yoʋ care for him?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 8:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yoʋ made him a little lower than the angels</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and crowned him with glory and</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="honor; yoʋ 73.6% ¦ honor; yoʋ set him over the works of yoʋr hands, and TR WH 26.1%">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">honor;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:27; Psalms 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">yoʋ put everything in subjection under his feet.”</span><br />In subjecting everything<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="to him ¦ — SBL">b</a></sup>to him, God left nothing that is not subjected to him. But now we do not yet see everything subjected to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:7–9; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 5:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:9–10"> But we do see Jesus, who for a little while was made lower than the angels, now crowned with glory and honor because he suffered death, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="by the grace of ¦ apart from SBL">c</a></sup>by the grace of God he might taste death for everyone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:36; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:31; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:46"> For in bringing many sons to glory, it was fitting that God, for whom and through whom all things exist, should make the author of their salvation perfect through suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:14"> For he who sanctifies and those who are being sanctified all have one Father. That is why Jesus is not ashamed to call them brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 22:22; Psalms 40:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 18:20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will declare yoʋr name to my brothers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of the congregation I will sing yoʋr praise.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalms 91:2"> And again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will put my trust in him.”</span><br />And again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Here I am, with the children God has given me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:15"> Therefore, since the children all have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="flesh and blood ¦ blood and flesh CT">d</a></sup>flesh and blood, Jesus himself took on flesh and blood as well, so that through death he might destroy the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:74–75"> and free those who all their lives were held in slavery by the fear of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:16–25"> For surely it is not angels that he helps, but the descendants of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:14–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:7–8; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:14"> Therefore he had to become like his brothers in every way, so that he could be a merciful and faithful high priest in service to God, in order to make atonement for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:15–16; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:24"> Because he himself suffered when he was tempted, he is able to help those who are being tempted.</span>
