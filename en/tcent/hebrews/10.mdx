## Christ's Sacrifice Once for All

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:5; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:17"> Since the law has only a shadow of the good things to come and not the true form of those things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the same sacrifices offered year after year can never ¦ it can never, by the same sacrifices offered year after year, ANT NA PCK SBL TR">a</a></sup>the same sacrifices offered year after year can never perfect those who draw near to worship.</span>

## A Call to Persevere in the Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:18; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:16"> Therefore, brothers, since we have confidence to enter the holy places by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 10:9; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:3"> by the new and living way he opened for us through the veil (that is, through his flesh),</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:14–16; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:1"> and since we have a great priest over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:26"> let us draw near with true hearts, in full assurance of faith, with our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:3"> Let us hold fast to the confession of our hope without wavering, for he who promised is faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:18; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:1"> And let us consider how to spur one another on to love and good works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:42; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:20"> not neglecting to meet together, as is the habit of some, but encouraging one another, and all the more as you see the Day drawing near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:20–22; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:4–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 9:41"> For if we continue sinning deliberately after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but only a fearful expectation of judgment, and a fury of fire that will consume God's adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> Anyone who has rejected the law of Moses dies without mercy on the testimony of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> How much worse punishment do you think will be deserved by someone who has trampled the Son of God underfoot, who has profaned the blood of the covenant by which he was sanctified, and who has insulted the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know him who said, “Vengeance is mine; I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="repay, says the Lord. 95.7% ¦ repay. <span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:3–13"> But remember the earlier days when, after you were enlightened, you endured a hard struggle in the face of suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:36"> At times you were publicly exposed to reproach and affliction, and at other times you were partners with those who were treated that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:11–12"> For you sympathized with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="me when I was 91% ¦ those CT 6.6%">e</a></sup>me when I was in prison, and you accepted the plundering of your possessions with joy, knowing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="that you have for yourselves ¦ in yourselves that you have ANT TR ¦ that you yourselves have CT">f</a></sup>that you have for yourselves a better and lasting possession<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="in heaven 93.6% ¦ — CT 1.7%">g</a></sup>in heaven.</span>
