## The Earthly Sanctuary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:10–11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:2"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="even ¦ — SBL">a</a></sup>even the first<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="covenant ¦ tabernacle ANT ST &#123;Note: The word covenant does not appear in the main Greek text, but is supplied in the English translation. However, the word tabernacle does appear in the Greek text of ANT and ST.&#125;">b</a></sup>covenant had regulations for worship and an earthly sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 26:35"> For a tabernacle was prepared. In the first room were the lampstand, the table, and the bread of the Presence; this is called the Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 40:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 27:51"> Behind the second veil was a room called the Holy of Holies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 17:10; 2 Chronicles 5:10; Exodus 40:3"> It had a golden censer and the ark of the covenant, which was overlaid on all sides with gold. In the ark were the golden jar containing the manna, the rod of Aaron that had budded, and the tablets of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:2; Numbers 7:89; Exodus 25:17–22"> Above the ark were the cherubim of glory, overshadowing the mercy seat. But we cannot discuss these things in detail now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Exodus 27:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:8–11"> When these things were all in place, the priests regularly entered the first room of the tabernacle, performing their sacred duties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 16:34; Leviticus 5:18; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:19–20"> But into the second room, the high priest alone entered only once a year, and never without blood, which he offered for himself and for the unintentional sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:6; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:19–22"> By this arrangement the Holy Spirit was showing that the way into the holy places had not yet been disclosed as long as the first tabernacle was still standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:14"> This is a symbol for the present time, during which gifts and sacrifices are offered that cannot perfect the conscience of the worshiper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:16; Leviticus 11:2–47"> but deal only with food and drink, and various<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="washings and regulations for the flesh, 93.2% ¦ washings, regulations for the flesh CT 4.2%">c</a></sup>washings and regulations for the flesh, imposed until the time of reformation.</span>
</div>

## Redemption Through the Blood of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:23–24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:1–2"> But when Christ came as high priest of the good things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="to 97% ¦ that have NA SBL WH 0.9%">d</a></sup>to come, through the greater and more perfect tabernacle, not made with hands (that is, not of this creation),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:18–19; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:15"> he entered once for all into the holy places, not by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood, thus obtaining eternal redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 19:2–21; Leviticus 16:14–16; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:9"> For if the blood of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="bulls and goats ¦ goats and bulls CT">e</a></sup>bulls and goats, and the ashes of a heifer sprinkled on those who are defiled, sanctify for the purification of the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 1:7"> how much more will the blood of Christ, who through the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="eternal ¦ Holy PCK">f</a></sup>eternal Spirit offered himself without blemish to God, purify<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="your ¦ our NA SBL WH">g</a></sup>your consciences from dead works to serve the living God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:24–26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28"> That is why he is the mediator of a new covenant, so that those who are called may receive the promised eternal inheritance, since a death has occurred that redeems them from the transgressions committed under the first covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12"> For in the case of a will, it is necessary to establish the death of the one who made it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:15"> because a will takes effect only after the person's death; it is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="never … alive. ¦ not … alive, is it? WH">h</a></sup>never in force while the person who made it is alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 24:3–8; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:22"> That is why even the first covenant was not inaugurated without blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 24:5–11; Numbers 19:6; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:4"> For when Moses had proclaimed every commandment of the law to all the people, he took the blood of calves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="and goats 77.5% • NA TH WH 5% ¦ — SBL 16.4%">i</a></sup>and goats, along with water, scarlet wool, and hyssop, and sprinkled the scroll itself and all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 24:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 26:28; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:20"> saying, “This is the blood of the covenant that God has ordained for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 8:19; Exodus 29:36"> In the same way, he sprinkled with blood the tabernacle and all the vessels used in worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 6:7; Leviticus 4:35"> Indeed, according to the law nearly everything is purified with blood, and without the shedding of blood there is no remission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:19–21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14"> So it was necessary for the copies of the things in heaven to be purified with these sacrifices, but for the heavenly things themselves to be purified with better sacrifices than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:11–12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:23"> For Christ did not enter holy places made with hands, which are copies of the true ones; he entered heaven itself, so that he might now appear before God on our behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14"> Nor did he enter heaven to offer himself many times, as the high priest enters the holy places year after year with blood that is not his own,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:20; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 1:2"> for then he would have had to suffer again and again since the foundation of the world. But as it is, he has appeared once for all at the end of the ages to remove sin by the sacrifice of himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> And just as it is appointed for men to die once and then face judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:10"> so also Christ, having been offered once to bear the sins of many, will appear a second time, not to bear sin, but to bring salvation to those who are eagerly waiting for him.</span>
