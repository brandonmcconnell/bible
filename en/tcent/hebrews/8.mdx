## The High Priest of the New Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 1:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:1; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:21"> Now the main point of what we are saying is this: We have such a high priest, who sat down at the right hand of the Majesty's throne in heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:23–24"> where he serves as a minister of the holy places and the true tabernacle, which was set up by the Lord, not by man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14"> For every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices; therefore, it was necessary for this priest also to have something to offer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:11–15; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="For ¦ Now CT">a</a></sup>For if he were on earth, he would not even be a priest, since there are already priests who offer gifts according to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:1"> They serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things. For when Moses was about to build the tabernacle, he was warned by God, “Be sure to make everything according to the pattern that was shown to yoʋ on the mountain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:16–21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:24"> But as it is, the ministry Jesus has received is as superior to the old priesthood as the covenant he mediates is superior to the old covenant, since it has been enacted on better promises.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:18"> For if that first covenant had been faultless, there would have been no need to look for a second one.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">not like the covenant I made with their fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they did not continue in my covenant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so I had no regard for them, says the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">after those days, says the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my laws into their minds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and write them on their hearts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will be my people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">No longer will a man teach his</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="fellow citizen ¦ neighbor PCK TR">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fellow citizen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or his brother, saying, ‘Know the Lord,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they will all know me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the least of them to the greatest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will be merciful toward their iniquities;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their sins</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="and their lawless deeds 90.6% ¦ — CT 3.5%">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their lawless deeds I will remember no more.”</span></span>
</div>
