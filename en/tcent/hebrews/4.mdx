## Jesus the Great High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:24; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:12"> Therefore, since we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast to our confession.</span>
