## The Discipline of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:24–27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:16"> Therefore, since we have such a great cloud of witnesses surrounding us, let us lay aside every weight and the sin that so easily entangles us, and let us run with endurance the race that lies before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:23–24"> fixing our eyes upon Jesus, the author and perfecter of our faith. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:16; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:3"> Consider him who endured such opposition from sinners against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="himself 90.7% • NA TH 2.8% ¦ themselves SBL WH 1.1%">a</a></sup>himself, so that you will not grow weary or lose heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:13; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:32–34"> In your struggle against sin, you have not yet resisted to the point of shedding your blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:19"> And you have completely forgotten the exhortation that addresses you as sons:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My son, do not think lightly of the discipline of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do not lose heart when he rebukes you.</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="For the sake of discipline you must endure. ¦ If you are enduring discipline, 𝔐pt ANT PCK TR">b</a></sup>For the sake of discipline you must endure. God is treating you as sons. For what son is not disciplined by his father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:9–10; Psalms 73:1"> If you do not experience discipline, which is something everyone receives, then you are illegitimate children and not sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Furthermore, we have all had earthly fathers who disciplined us, and we respected them. Should we not submit even more to the Father of spirits and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:14"> For our fathers disciplined us for a short time as seemed best to them, but God disciplines us for our good, so that we may share in his holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:3–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:5–6"> Now no discipline seems pleasant at the time, but painful. Later on, however, it produces the peaceful fruit of righteousness for those trained by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:14"> Therefore, lift up your drooping hands and strengthen your weak knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:1"> Make straight paths for your feet, so that what is lame may not be put out of joint but healed instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:15–16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:7"> Pursue peace with everyone, and holiness, for without it no one will see the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:4"> Make sure no one falls short of the grace of God and that no root of bitterness springs up and causes trouble, which would result in many becoming defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:15–20; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 22:15"> And make sure there is no fornicator or profane person like Esau, who sold his birthright in exchange for a single meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:11–12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:23"> You know that afterward, when he desired to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no opportunity for repentance, even though he sought the blessing with tears.</span>

## Mount Sinai and Mount Zion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> For you have not come to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="a mountain 93% ¦ something CT 2.1%">c</a></sup>a mountain that can be touched, to a blazing fire, darkness, gloom, and a whirlwind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> You have not come to a trumpet blast and a voice whose words made the hearers beg that no further word be spoken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10"> For they could not bear the order that was given: “If even a beast touches the mountain, it shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="stoned ¦ stoned or shot with an arrow TR">d</a></sup>stoned.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> Indeed, the sight was so terrifying that Moses said, “I am trembling with fear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:20; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 14:1"> But you have come to Mount Zion, to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem. You have come to myriads of angels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:18; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:40; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:20"> to the festive gathering and assembly of the firstborn, whose names are enrolled in heaven. You have come to God, the judge of all, and to the spirits of righteous people who have been made perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:15; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:28; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:4"> And you have come to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood that speaks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="a better word ¦ better things TR">e</a></sup>a better word than the blood of Abel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:1–3"> Be careful not to refuse him who is speaking. For if the people did not escape when they refused him who warned them on earth, how much less will we escape if we turn away from him who warns us from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> At that time his voice shook the earth, but now he has promised, “Yet once more I will shake not only the earth, but also the heavens.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:31; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:35; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:10–11"> Now the phrase, “Yet once more,” indicates the removal of what can be shaken—that is, created things—so that what cannot be shaken may remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:1–2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> Therefore, since we are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us be thankful.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="It is by our thankfulness that we ¦ And by our thankfulness let us ANT NA SBL TR WH">f</a></sup>It is by our thankfulness that we offer God acceptable worship, with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="reverence and godly fear 89.7% ¦ godly fear and awe CT 1.8%">g</a></sup>reverence and godly fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:27"> For our God is a consuming fire.</span>
