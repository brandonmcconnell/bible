## Warning Against Falling Away

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:12–14; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14"> Therefore, let us leave behind the elementary teaching about Christ and go on to maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:13–57; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:31–32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:3–5"> and of instruction about baptisms, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 18:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:19"> And this we will do, if God permits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:26–29; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:20–22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:13"> For it is impossible to renew to repentance those who have once been enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift, who have shared in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 8:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:20–21"> who have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the coming age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:29; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:4"> and yet have fallen away, since, to their own harm, they are crucifying the Son of God all over again and exposing him to public shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalms 65:9–13"> For land that has drunk the rain that often falls upon it, and produces a crop useful to those for whom it is cultivated, receives a blessing from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 13:7–9"> But land that bears thorns and thistles is worthless and on the verge of being cursed; its end is to be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:39; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:4–6"> Even though we speak in this way, beloved, we are confident of better things in your case, things that pertain to salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:42; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:10"> For God is not unjust; he will not forget your work and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="labor of 86.6% ¦ — CT 8.6%">a</a></sup>labor of love that you have shown toward his name by serving the saints, as you still do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–8; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:2–5"> We want each one of you to show the same earnestness to have the full assurance of hope to the very end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:36; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:25"> so that you will not be sluggish, but imitators of those who inherit the promises through faith and patience.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Certainty of God's Promise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> When God made his promise to Abraham, since he had no one greater to swear by, he swore by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying, “I will surely bless yoʋ and multiply yoʋ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so Abraham, having patiently endured, obtained the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> People swear by someone greater than themselves, and in all their disputes an oath is final for confirmation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> So when God wanted to show the unchangeable nature of his purpose even more clearly to the heirs of the promise, he guaranteed it with an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> so that by two unchangeable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled for refuge would have strong encouragement to take hold of the hope set before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 43:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:5–10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:7"> We have this hope as a sure and steadfast anchor for the soul. It enters into the inner sanctuary behind the veil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:6"> where Jesus has entered as a forerunner for us, because he has become a high priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.</span>
</div>
