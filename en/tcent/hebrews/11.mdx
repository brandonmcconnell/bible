## The Meaning of Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:24–25"> Now faith is the assurance of what we hope for and the conviction of what we do not see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:39; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:4"> Because of their faith, the people of old were commended.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:3"> By faith we understand that the universe was formed by the word of God. Consequently, we know that what can be seen did not come from anything visible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:24; Genesis 4:3–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:11–12"> By faith Abel offered to God a better sacrifice than Cain, through which he was commended as righteous when God gave approval to his gifts. And by faith he still speaks, even though he is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:21–24; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:6"> By faith Enoch was taken up so that he would not see death, and he was not to be found, because God had taken him. Now before he was taken, he was commended as one who pleased God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:17; Jeremiah 29:13–14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:33"> And without faith it is impossible to please God, for anyone who comes to him must believe that God exists and that he rewards those who earnestly seek him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 6:13–1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:20; Ezekiel 14:14"> By faith Noah, when he was warned about things not yet seen, was moved with reverent fear and built an ark to save his household. By faith he condemned the world and became an heir of the righteousness that comes by faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 7:2–4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:24–25"> By faith Abraham obeyed when he was called to go out to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the ¦ a CT">a</a></sup>the place that he was to receive as an inheritance. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:20; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:22"> For he was looking forward to the city that has foundations, whose designer and builder is God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 32:12; Genesis 15:5"> And so from one man, who was as good as dead, came descendants as numerous as the stars in the sky and as countless as the sand on the seashore.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 23:4; Psalms 39:12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:27"> All these people died in faith without receiving the things that were promised. But they saw them from a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="distance ¦ distance, were assured of them, TR">c</a></sup>distance and welcomed them. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:18–7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:16; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:14"> For people who say such things make it clear that they are seeking a homeland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 24:6–8; Genesis 12:10; Genesis 32:9–11"> If they had been thinking of the land they left behind, they would have had an opportunity to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:20"> Instead, they were longing for a better land, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore God is not ashamed to be called their God, for he has prepared a city for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Genesis 22:1–12; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:21–24"> By faith Abraham, when he was tested, offered up Isaac. He who had received the promises was ready to offer up his one and only son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:7; Genesis 17:19"> even though God had said to him, “Through Isaac yoʋr descendants will be counted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:17–21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 9:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:20"> He reasoned that God could even raise him from the dead. And figuratively speaking, he did receive Isaac back from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 27:27–40; Genesis 28:2–3"> By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau concerning things to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:31–1; Genesis 48:5–22"> By faith Jacob, when he was dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph, and worshiped as he leaned on the top of his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32"> By faith Joseph, when his end was near, mentioned the exodus of the sons of Israel and gave instructions concerning his bones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Exodus 1:16; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:6"> By faith Moses, when he was born, was hidden for three months by his parents, because they saw that he was a beautiful child, and they did not fear the decree of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 2:10–11; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 7:21–24"> By faith Moses, when he had grown up, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:3–10; Job 36:21"> choosing to be mistreated with the people of God rather than to enjoy the fleeting pleasure of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:35; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:14"> He considered the reproach of Christ to be greater wealth than the treasures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="of ¦ in TR">d</a></sup>of Egypt, for he was focused on the reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:18; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:1"> By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the wrath of the king, for he endured as though he could see him who is invisible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 12:21–30; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:19; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:24"> By faith he celebrated the Passover and the sprinkling of the blood, so that the destroyer of the firstborn would not touch the Israelites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–21; Psalms 106:9–11; Psalms 78:13"> By faith they passed through the Red Sea as on dry land, but when the Egyptians tried to do so, they were drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 6:3–20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:4–5"> By faith the walls of Jericho fell after they had been encircled for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:25; Joshua 6:22–25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:5"> By faith Rahab the prostitute did not perish with those who were disobedient, because she had welcomed the spies in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:20; 1 Samuel 16:13"> And what more shall I say? For time will fail me if I tell of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 144:1–2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:12–15; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:36"> who by faith conquered kingdoms, carried out justice, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16–18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; 1 Samuel 17:51–52"> quenched the power of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, were made strong from weakness, became mighty in battle, and routed the armies of foreigners.</span>

They went around in sheepskins and goatskins; they were destitute, afflicted, and mistreated.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:23–24; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:2"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="All these people were ¦ They were all SBL">f</a></sup>All these people were commended for their faith, but they did not receive what was promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 6:11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:6; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:11–14"> For God had planned something better for us, so that they would not be made perfect without us.</span>
