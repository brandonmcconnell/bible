## The Priestly Order of Melchizedek

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:18–20; Psalms 57:2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:20"> Now this Melchizedek was king of Salem and priest of God Most High. When Abraham was returning from the slaughter of the kings, Melchizedek met him and blessed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 18:21; Psalms 85:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:17"> and Abraham gave him a tenth of all the spoils. His name means “king of righteousness,” but he is also “king of Salem,” which means, “king of peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:23–28; Exodus 6:20–27"> He is without father, without mother, and without genealogy; there is no beginning to his days or end to his life. But resembling the Son of God, he remains a priest forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:29; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 7:8–9"> Consider how great this man must be, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="even ¦ — SBL WH">a</a></sup>even Abraham the patriarch gave him a tenth of the spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> Now the law commands the sons of Levi who receive the priestly office to collect tithes from the people, that is, their brothers, even though their brothers are also descended from Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> But Melchizedek, who was not among the descendants of Levi, received a tithe from Abraham. He also blessed Abraham, who had received the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:16"> It is beyond all dispute that the inferior is blessed by the superior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:20; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:6; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:18"> In the one case, tithes are received by mortal men, but in the other case, they are received by one who is attested to be living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12; Genesis 14:20; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:4"> One might even say that Levi himself, who receives tithes, paid tithes through Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> For Levi was still in the loins of his ancestor Abraham when Melchizedek met him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Compared to Melchizedek

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:9"> Now if perfection had been attainable through the Levitical priesthood (for under this priesthood the people received the law), what further need would there have been for another priest to arise according to the order of Melchizedek rather than the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> For when the priesthood is changed, the law must also be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> Now he of whom these things are spoken belongs to a different tribe, from which no one has ever served at the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:33"> For it is clear that our Lord arose from Judah, and in connection with that tribe Moses said nothing about<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="priesthood 63.4% • TR 20.2% ¦ priests CT 1.5%">b</a></sup>priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:17–21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:3"> Now this point becomes even more clear when there arises another priest like Melchizedek,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:20; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:28"> one who has become a priest, not through a legal requirement concerning physical descent, but through the power of an endless life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:6; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:21"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="God testifies ¦ it is attested of him CT">c</a></sup>God testifies,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yoʋ are a priest forever</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">according to the order of Melchizedek.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:3; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:39; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:9"> The former commandment is set aside because of its weakness and ineffectiveness</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:39; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:16; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:9"> (for the law made nothing perfect), and a better hope is introduced, by which we draw near to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:6; Psalms 110:4"> And none of this happened without an oath. Aaron's descendants become priests without an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:16–18"> but Jesus was made a priest with an oath by the one who said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Lord has sworn</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will not change his mind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Yoʋ are a priest forever</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="according to the order of Melchizedek 97.8% ¦ — CT 1.9%">d</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">according to the order of Melchizedek.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:25; Genesis 44:32; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:6–12"> Accordingly, Jesus has become the guarantor of a better covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:8"> Now the former priests were many in number, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:34; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> but because Jesus continues forever, he has a permanent priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:34; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:1–2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:5"> Therefore he is able to save to the uttermost those who come to God through him, because he always lives to intercede for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:14–15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:22"> It was fitting for us to have such a high priest, one who is holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and exalted above the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:28; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14"> He has no need, like the other high priests, to offer up daily sacrifices, first for his own sins, and then for the sins of the people. For he did this once for all when he offered up himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 1:2"> For the law appoints as high priests men who have weakness, but the word of the oath, which came after the law, appoints the Son, who has been made perfect forever.</span>
