<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:42; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:21"> I solemnly charge yoʋ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="therefore 83.5% ¦ — CT 9%">a</a></sup>therefore in the presence of God and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the Lord Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus CT">b</a></sup>the Lord Jesus Christ, who will judge the living and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="dead when he appears with ¦ dead, and by his appearing and CT">c</a></sup>dead when he appears with his kingdom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:15–16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:15"> Preach the word, be prepared whether the time is favorable or not, reprove, rebuke, and encourage, with complete patience and careful instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:1–3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:1–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:1–3"> For a time is coming when people will not tolerate sound doctrine, but having itching ears they will surround themselves with teachers to suit their own desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:4; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 7:57"> They will turn their ears away from the truth and be turned aside to myths.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:14–15"> For I am already being poured out like a drink offering, and the time of my departure is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:24–27; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:1–2"> I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.</span>

## Personal Instructions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:21"> Make every effort to come to me soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:15–16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:14–15"> For Demas, who is in love with this present world, has deserted me and gone to Thessalonica. Crescens has gone to Galatia, and Titus to Dalmatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 12:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:24; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 12:25"> Luke alone is with me. Get Mark and bring him with yoʋ, for he is useful to me for ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:21; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:12"> Tychicus I have sent to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:27; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:11"> When yoʋ come, bring the cloak that I left with Carpus in Troas, as well as my scrolls, especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:6; Psalms 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith did me great harm.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="May the Lord ¦ The Lord will CT">d</a></sup>May the Lord repay him according to his works.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 7:60; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:32; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 14:50"> At my first defense no one came to stand by me; instead, they all deserted me. May it not be counted against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might be fully made and all the Gentiles might hear. And I was rescued from the lion's mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:3; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:24–25"> And the Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and preserve me for his heavenly kingdom. To him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
