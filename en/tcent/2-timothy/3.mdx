## Hard Times in the Last Days

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:1–3"> But know this: Hard times will come in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:29–31; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:16"> For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemous, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:31; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:21"> without natural affection, irreconcilable, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, having no love for what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:6; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:4"> traitors, reckless, puffed up, and lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:27–28"> having a form of godliness but denying its power. Avoid such people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3"> Among them are those who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="worm their way ¦ press PCK">a</a></sup>worm their way into houses and captivate vulnerable women who are overwhelmed with sins and swayed by various evil desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:4"> Such women are always learning yet never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:8–11; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:5"> Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these false teachers also oppose the truth. They are corrupted in mind and have proven themselves to be unqualified with respect to the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:8; Exodus 7:12; Jeremiah 28:15–17"> But they will not make any further progress, for their folly will become obvious to everyone, just like the folly of Jannes and Jambres.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Continue in What You Have Learned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–7"> Yoʋ, however, have closely followed my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, patience, love, and endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:5–6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:23–28"> Yoʋ know how much persecution and suffering I endured in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. Yet the Lord rescued me from it all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:12–16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:20–21"> Indeed, everyone who wants to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3"> while evil people and imposters will become worse and worse, deceiving and being deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13"> But as for yoʋ, continue in what yoʋ have learned and firmly believed, knowing from whom yoʋ have learned it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:98–99; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:45; Proverbs 22:6"> and how from childhood yoʋ have known the sacred writings, which are able to give yoʋ wisdom for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:19–21"> All Scripture is inspired by God and is beneficial for teaching, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:8"> so that the man of God may be complete, fully equipped for every good work.</span>
</div>
</div>
