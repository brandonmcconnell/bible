## Paul's Charge to Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8–9; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 23:1"> I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience as my forefathers did, as night and day I constantly remember yoʋ in my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:17–1"> Recalling yoʋr tears, I long to see yoʋ so that I may be filled with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:5; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:15"> I am reminded of the sincere faith that is in yoʋ, which dwelt first in yoʋr grandmother Lois and in yoʋr mother Eunice, and now, I am sure, dwells in yoʋ also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10–11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:6–8"> Therefore I remind yoʋ to rekindle the gift of God that is in yoʋ through the laying on of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:22"> For God did not give us a spirit of timidity, but a spirit of power, love, and sound judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:38; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:13–15"> Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord or of me his prisoner. Rather, join with me in suffering for the gospel as you rely on the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8–9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:4–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:3–4"> He saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace. This grace was given to us in Christ Jesus before time began,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 1:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:26; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:18"> but has now been revealed through the appearing of our Savior<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus NA WH">b</a></sup>Jesus Christ, who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:7–8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:15"> For this gospel I was appointed to be a preacher, an apostle, and a teacher<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="of the Gentiles 98.1% ¦ — CT 0.9%">c</a></sup>of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:8; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:19"> That is why I am suffering these things. But I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am persuaded that he is able to guard what has been entrusted to me until that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:9"> Hold to the pattern of sound teaching that yoʋ have heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:20"> By the Holy Spirit who dwells within us, guard the good deposit that has been entrusted to yoʋ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:21; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 19:10"> Yoʋ know that everyone in Asia has turned away from me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:7"> May the Lord give mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 28:30–31"> On the contrary, when he arrived in Rome, he sought me out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="very 91.6% ¦ — CT 3.1%">d</a></sup>very diligently and found me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:3"> May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day! Yoʋ know very well all the ways he helped me in Ephesus.</span>
</div>
