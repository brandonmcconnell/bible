## Judgment on False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:27; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:20"> Beloved, although I was quite eager to write to you about our common salvation, I found it necessary to write to you and urge you to contend for the faith that was once for all delivered to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:1–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:10"> For certain men whose condemnation was written about long ago have crept in unnoticed. They are ungodly men who pervert the grace of our God, turning it into a license for sensuality. By doing so, they deny<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="God, who is our only Master, and also the Lord 78.7% ¦ our only Master and Lord, ANT CT 12.9%">b</a></sup>God, who is our only Master, and also the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:16–2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:1–12"> Now I want to remind you, although you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="already know this, that after the Lord 69.4% • MSS 6.7% • MSS 5.4% ¦ already know all things, that after Jesus ECM NA28 SBL 0.2% • TH 0.2% ¦ know all things, that after the Lord once for all NA27 0% ¦ already know all things, that after the Lord WH 0%">c</a></sup>already know this, that after the Lord saved a people out of the land of Egypt, he subsequently destroyed those who did not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:41; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:12"> And the angels who did not keep within their proper domain, but left their own dwelling, he has kept under darkness in everlasting chains for the judgment of the great day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:26–27"> Likewise, Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, who indulged in fornication and went after strange flesh in a manner similar to those angels, serve as an example by undergoing the punishment of eternal fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:10–12"> In the same way these ungodly men, relying on their dreams, defile the flesh, reject authority, and revile glorious beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 3:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:11; Deuteronomy 34:6"> But when Michael the archangel was contending with the devil and disputing about the body of Moses, he did not dare to bring a reviling judgment against him, but said, “May the Lord rebuke yoʋ!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:21–22"> Yet these men slander whatever they do not understand, and they are corrupted by what they, like irrational beasts, instinctively comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:12; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:14"> Woe to them! For they went in the way of Cain, they rushed for reward to the error of Balaam, and they perished in the rebellion of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:17–20; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:13; Ezekiel 34:8"> These men are hidden reefs at your love feasts, feeding themselves as they feast among you without fear. They are clouds without water, carried along by winds; fruitless trees in late autumn, twice dead, pulled up by the roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:19; Isaiah 57:20"> They are wild waves of the sea, foaming out their own shameful deeds; wayward stars, for whom the thick gloom of darkness has been reserved forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 50:3–5; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:5–6"> Enoch, in the seventh generation from Adam, prophesied about these men, saying, “Behold, the Lord is coming with thousands upon thousands of his holy ones</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:16; Ecclesiastes 11:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:5"> to execute judgment against all and to convict<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="all the ungodly among them 79.5% ¦ all the ungodly SBL TH WH 15.8% ¦ every person ECM NA 0.6%">d</a></sup>all the ungodly among them of all their ungodly deeds that they have done in an ungodly way, and to convict ungodly sinners of all the harsh words they have spoken against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:14"> These men are grumblers and complainers, walking according to their own lusts. They speak bombastic words with their mouths, admiring people for their own advantage.</span>
