## God Is Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:20–1; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:22"> Beloved, let us love one another, because love is from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:16; Psalms 86:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:11"> Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:32"> In this the love of God was revealed among us, that God sent his one and only Son into the world so that we might live through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:4–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:8–10; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:19"> In this is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son to be the atoning sacrifice for our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:12–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:34"> Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:17–18"> No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God abides in us, and his love is perfected in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20–22; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16–17"> We know that we abide in him and he in us because he has given us his Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:16–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 4:42"> And we have seen and we testify that the Father has sent the Son to be the Savior of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:32"> If anyone confesses that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ WH">d</a></sup>Jesus is the Son of God, God abides in him, and he in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:24; Psalms 36:7–9; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:1"> And we have come to know and to trust in the love that God has for us. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:12"> There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear, because fear has to do with punishment. Whoever fears has not been perfected in love.</span>
