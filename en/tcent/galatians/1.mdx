## No Other Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I am astonished that you are so quickly turning away from him who called you by the grace of Christ and turning to a different gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:10"> not that there is another gospel, but there are some who are troubling you and wish to pervert the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:13–14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:9; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 22:18–19"> But even if we or an angel from heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="should 𝔐176 48.5% • TH 1% • WH 0.8% ¦ — 𝔐160 HF 39.7%">b</a></sup>should preach to you a gospel contrary to the one we preached to you, let him be accursed!</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:4; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 5:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:6"> Am I now seeking the approval of men, or of God? Or am I trying to please men? If I were still trying to please men, I would not be a servant of Christ.</span>

## How Paul Became an Apostle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:9–10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:23"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="But ¦ For NA SBL WH">c</a></sup>But I make known to you, brothers, that the gospel I preached did not originate with man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:1"> For I did not receive it from man, nor was I taught it, but I received it by a revelation of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus ¦ — PCK">d</a></sup>Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 8:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:9; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 22:3–5"> For you have heard of my former way of life in Judaism, how I persecuted the church of God beyond measure and tried to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:4–6"> I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my own age among my people, being far more zealous for the traditions of my fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="God, who … grace, ¦ he who … grace SBL">e</a></sup>God, who set me apart before I was born and called me by his grace, was pleased</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:5–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:12"> to reveal his Son in me so that I could preach good news about him among the Gentiles, I did not immediately confer with flesh and blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:20–25; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:32–33; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:18"> nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away to Arabia and returned again to Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:22–23; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:26–29; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 22:17–18"> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to become acquainted with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Peter 97.7% ¦ Cephas CT 2.3%">f</a></sup>Peter, and I stayed with him for fifteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:55; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:46"> But I saw none of the other apostles except James, the brother of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:31; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:10–11"> (Now in what I am writing to you, I assure you before God that I am not lying.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:41; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:30; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 6:9"> Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:1"> But I was personally unknown to the churches of Judea that are in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:8–10; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13–16"> They were only hearing people say, “The man who once persecuted us is now preaching the good news of the faith he once tried to destroy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 9:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 11:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 15:10"> So they were glorifying God because of me.</span>
