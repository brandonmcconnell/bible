## Paul's Concern for the Galatians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:11–12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:8"> At that time, however, when you did not know God, you were enslaved to beings that by nature are not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:3"> But now that you have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, how is it that you are turning back again to the weak and worthless elements? Do you wish to be enslaved to them once more?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:16–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> You observe days, months, seasons, and years!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:2"> I am afraid for you, lest somehow I have labored for you in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> I plead with you, brothers: Become as I am, because I also have become as you are. You did me no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:7–10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:30"> But you know that it was because of a physical infirmity that I preached the gospel to you the first time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:40; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:2"> And you did not despise or reject me even though my physical condition was a trial<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="for me 83.7% ¦ for you CT 1.1% ¦ — MSS 11.4%">c</a></sup>for me. On the contrary, you received me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:16–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="What then has become of ¦ Where then is CT">d</a></sup>What then has become of that sense of blessing you had? For I testify to you that, if possible, you would have plucked out your own eyes and given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:45"> Have I now become your enemy by telling you the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:13–15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:3"> Those false teachers are not zealous for you in a good way, but rather they wish to shut you out so that you will be zealous for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:12; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:27"> Now it is good to be zealous for a good purpose, and to be so at all times and not just when I am with you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:17–18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:19–21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:9"> how I wish that I could be with you now and change my tone, for I am perplexed about you!</span>

## The Allegory of Hagar and Sarah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:5–6"> Tell me, you who wish to be under the law, do you not listen to the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the slave woman and one by the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:7–8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:28–29"> But the son of the slave woman was born according to the flesh, while the son of the free woman was born through the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> This can be explained allegorically, for these women are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="two ¦ the two TR">f</a></sup>two covenants. One is from Mount Sinai, bearing children for slavery; this is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 19:44; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="For 90% ¦ Now NA SBL WH 5.1%">g</a></sup>For Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to the present<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Jerusalem; ¦ Jerusalem, for CT">h</a></sup>Jerusalem; she is in slavery with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> But the Jerusalem that is above is the free woman; she is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="the mother of us all ¦ our mother CT">i</a></sup>the mother of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, O barren woman, who has never given birth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">break forth and shout, yoʋ who have never been in labor!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For many are the children of the desolate woman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">more than those of the woman who has a husband.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:8–9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:13–18"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="we ¦ you NA SBL TH">j</a></sup>we, brothers, like Isaac, are children of the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:11"> But just as at that time the child who was born according to the flesh persecuted the child who was born according to the Spirit, so it is now also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:35; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:15–17"> But what does the Scripture say? “Cast out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman shall not inherit with the son of the free woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:36; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:12–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:1"> So, brothers, we are not children of the slave woman, but of the free woman.</span>
