## Paul Accepted by the Other Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:3; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:23"> Then after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus along also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:5"> I went up in response to a revelation and presented to them the gospel that I preach among the Gentiles, but privately to those who were held in high esteem, to make sure that I was not running or had not run in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:2–6; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:20–21"> But not even Titus, who was with me, was compelled to be circumcised, even though he is a Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:3"> Now this matter arose because of the false brothers who were secretly brought in, who slipped in to spy on the freedom we have in Christ Jesus, so that they might enslave us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:5"> But we did not yield to them in submission for even an hour, so that the truth of the gospel might be preserved for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:34; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:11"> Now from those who were esteemed to be something (what sort of men they once were makes no difference to me; God does not show partiality)—those, I say, who were held in high esteem added nothing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:16"> On the contrary, they saw that I had been entrusted with the gospel to the uncircumcised, just as Peter had been entrusted with the gospel to the circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:15; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 22:21; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 2:14–41"> For he who worked through Peter in his apostleship to the circumcised also worked through me in my apostleship to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:3; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:2"> When James, Cephas, and John, who were esteemed as pillars of the church, recognized the grace given to me, they gave the right hand of fellowship to Barnabas and me, agreeing that we should go to the Gentiles and that they should go to the circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16"> They only asked us to remember the poor, the very thing I was eager to do.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Rebuke of Peter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:16; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 15:1"> But when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Peter 92.3% ¦ Cephas CT 6.3%">a</a></sup>Peter came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face, because he was blameworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:28; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 15:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 11:2–3"> For he regularly ate with the Gentiles until certain men came from James. But when they came, he drew back and separated himself because he was afraid of the circumcision faction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:33; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:9"> And the rest of the Jews acted hypocritically along with him, so that even Barnabas was led astray by their hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:5; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:28; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 15:10–11"> But when I saw that they were not walking uprightly in accordance with the truth of the gospel, I said to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Peter 91.7% ¦ Cephas CT 4.8%">b</a></sup>Peter in front of them all, “If yoʋ, though a Jew, live like a Gentile and not like a Jew,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="why do ¦ how is it that CT">c</a></sup>why do yoʋ compel the Gentiles to live like Jews?”</span>
</div>

## Justification by Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:11–12"> We<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="who are … sinners ¦ are … sinners, yet we ANT CT">d</a></sup>who are Jews by nature and not Gentile sinners</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:9; Psalms 143:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:30"> know that a person is not justified by works of the law but through faith in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus WH">e</a></sup>Jesus Christ. So we too have put our faith in Christ Jesus so that we may be justified by faith in Christ and not by works of the law, because no flesh will be justified by works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:1–2; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:8–10; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:5"> But if we ourselves have also been found to be sinners while seeking to be justified in Christ, is Christ then an agent of sin? Certainly not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:9–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:4–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:12–16"> For if I again build up the very things that I tore down, I show myself to be a transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:10"> For through the law I died to the law so that I might live for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:4–6"> I have been crucified with Christ, and it is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me. The life that I now live in the flesh, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself up for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:11"> I do not nullify the grace of God, for if righteousness comes through the law, then Christ died for nothing.</span>
</div>
