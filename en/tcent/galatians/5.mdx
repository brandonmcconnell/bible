## Do Not Submit Again to a Yoke of Slavery

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:16"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Stand fast therefore in the freedom for which Christ has set us free 65% ¦ For freedom Christ has set us free; stand fast therefore CT 1%">a</a></sup>Stand fast therefore in the freedom for which Christ has set us free, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:31–32"> Behold, I, Paul, say to you that if you let yourselves be circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:25; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:10–11"> I testify again to every man who lets himself be circumcised that he is obligated to keep the entire law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:31–32; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:20–22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:6"> You who are seeking to be justified by the law are alienated from Christ; you have fallen away from grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 130:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:10; Psalms 62:5"> For through the Spirit, by faith, we eagerly await the hope of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:19; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:14–26"> For in Christ Jesus neither does circumcision have any significance, nor uncircumcision, but what matters is faith working through love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:1"> You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28"> This persuasion does not come from him who calls you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:33; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:6–7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:15"> A little leaven leavens the whole lump.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:7"> I have confidence in you in the Lord that you will adopt no other mindset. But the one who is troubling you will bear his judgment, whoever he may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:29"> Now, brothers, if I am still preaching circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the offense of the cross has been done away with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:10"> Oh that those who are unsettling you would cut themselves off!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:9"> You were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but serve one another in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:2"> For the entire law is fulfilled in this one commandment: “Yoʋ shall love yoʋr neighbor as yoʋrself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:3; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:14–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:26"> But if you bite and devour one another, watch out, or you will be consumed by one another.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Fruit of the Spirit and the Works of the Flesh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:24–25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:12–14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:8"> I say then, walk by the Spirit and you will certainly not gratify the desires of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:5–8"> For the flesh desires what is contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit desires what is contrary to the flesh. These are opposed to each other, to keep you from doing the very things you want to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:14–15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:25"> But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:9–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:5–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:18–19"> Now the works of the flesh are obvious:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="adultery, ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>adultery, fornication, impurity, sensuality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:19; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 21:8"> idolatry, sorcery, hostilities,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="quarrels ¦ quarreling CT PCK">c</a></sup>quarrels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="jealousies ¦ jealousy CT">d</a></sup>jealousies, fits of rage, selfish ambitions, dissensions, factions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:9–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:13"> envy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="murder, ¦ — NA SBL WH">e</a></sup>murder, drunkenness, revelries, and the like. I warn you, just as I warned you before, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:12–17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:4–7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:5"> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 24:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:2"> gentleness, and self-control; against such things there is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13"> Now those who belong to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Christ ¦ Christ Jesus NA TH WH">f</a></sup>Christ have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:4–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:2"> Since we live by the Spirit, let us also keep in step with the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:14–16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:1–3; THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:16"> Let us not become conceited, provoking one another and envying one another.</span>
</div>
