## Bear One Another's Burdens

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:25; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:15"> Brothers, if anyone is caught in any transgression, you who are spiritual should restore him in a spirit of gentleness, paying attention to yoʋrself so that yoʋ too are not tempted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:34"> Bear one another's burdens, and in this way fulfill the law of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:18"> For if anyone thinks that he is something, when he is nothing, he deceives himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:5; Psalms 26:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:28"> But each person should test his own work, and then he will have reason for boasting in regard to himself alone, and not in regard to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:10–12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:10–11"> For each person will bear his own load.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:27; Deuteronomy 12:19"> Now the one who is instructed in the word must share all good things with the one who instructs him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:6; Hosea 10:12; Job 4:8"> Do not be misled: God is not mocked. A person reaps what he sows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:19"> For he who sows to his own flesh will reap corruption from the flesh, but he who sows to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:58; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> So let us not grow weary in doing good, for in due time we will reap, if we do not give up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:15"> Therefore, as we have opportunity, let us do good to all, and especially to those of the household of faith.</span>

## Final Warning and Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:21–23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:22"> See what large letters I am using as I write to you with my own hand!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:23; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:1"> It is those who want to make a good appearance in the flesh who compel you to be circumcised, but only so that they may not be persecuted for the cross of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Christ ¦ Christ Jesus WH">a</a></sup>Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:17–25"> For not even those who are circumcised keep the law, but they want you to be circumcised so that they can boast about your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20"> But as for me, may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:10–11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:10"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in Christ Jesus 95.5% ¦ — CT 2.7%">b</a></sup>in Christ Jesus neither<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="does circumcision have any significance ¦ is circumcision anything CT">c</a></sup>does circumcision have any significance, nor uncircumcision, but what matters is whether we become a new creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:6–8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> As for all who follow this standard, peace and mercy be upon them and upon the Israel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:23–25"> From now on let no one cause me trouble, for I bear the marks of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the Lord ¦ — CT">d</a></sup>the Lord Jesus on my body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:25"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers.<span className="desc">Amen.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The following scribal note is included in the colophons of many Greek manuscripts: Written to the Galatians from Rome.">e</a></sup></span>
