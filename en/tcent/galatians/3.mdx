## Law or Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:7–8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:6"> O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="to keep you from obeying the truth 92% ¦ — CT 3.1%">a</a></sup>to keep you from obeying the truth?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="In your presence, before 86.5% ¦ Before CT 9%">b</a></sup>In your presence, before your very eyes, Jesus Christ was publicly portrayed as crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:16–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:14; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 15:8"> Let me ask you this one question: Did you receive the Spirit by works of the law or by hearing with faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:4–8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:7–10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:16–19"> Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now trying to be perfected by the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:32–39"> Did you suffer so much in vain?—if indeed it was in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:9–10; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:11–12"> Does God supply you with the Spirit and work miracles among you because you perform the works of the law or because you hear with faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:21–22; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:23"> Remember, “Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:7–8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:26–29"> Therefore you must understand that those who have faith are the sons of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> Now the Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham, saying, “All the nations will be blessed in yoʋ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:24"> So then, those who have faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:19–20"> For all who rely on the works of the law are under a curse, because it is written, “Cursed is everyone who does not continue to do everything written in the Book of the Law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:38"> Now it is evident that no one is justified before God by the law, because “the righteous will live by faith.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> But the law is not based on faith; on the contrary, “<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The person 92.6% ¦ He CT 6.6%">c</a></sup>The person who does these things will live by them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:12"> Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us, for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:28–29; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> He redeemed us so that the blessing of Abraham would come to the Gentiles in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Christ Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ WH">d</a></sup>Christ Jesus, so that we could receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Law and the Promise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:5"> Brothers, let me give an example from everyday life: When a man-made covenant is ratified, no one annuls it or adds to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:55"> Now the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his descendant. Scripture does not say, “And to his descendants,” referring to many, but referring to one man it says, “And to yoʋr descendant,” who is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:13–14"> My point is this: The law, which came four hundred and thirty years later, cannot annul a covenant previously ratified by God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="to Christ ¦ — CT">e</a></sup>to Christ, so as to invalidate the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:12–15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:13–16"> For if the inheritance comes by the law, it no longer comes by the promise; but God granted it to Abraham by a promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 7:53; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:7–13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:2"> Why then was the law given? It was added because of transgressions, until the descendant should come to whom the promise had been made. It was ordained through angels by the hand of a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> Now a mediator does not represent just one party, but God is one.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Slaves and Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:31; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:20–22"> Is the law then opposed to the promises of God? Certainly not! For if a law had been given that was able to give life, truly righteousness would have come through the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:32; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:23"> But the Scripture has confined all under sin, so that the promise might be given on the basis of faith in Jesus Christ to those who believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:24–25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:19"> Now before faith came, we were kept in custody under the law, confined until the faith that was to come would be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:4; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:38–39; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:24–25"> The law, then, was our guardian until Christ came, so that we could be justified by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:15–18"> But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:12–13"> for you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:3–4"> For all of you who were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:29–30"> There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is neither male nor female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:7–8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:7"> And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's descendants and heirs according to the promise.</span>
</div>
