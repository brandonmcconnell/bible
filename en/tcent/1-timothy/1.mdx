## Warning Against False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:6–7; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:9–10"> Just as I urged yoʋ when I was going to Macedonia, remain in Ephesus so that yoʋ may charge certain people not to teach different doctrines</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:9"> or to occupy themselves with myths and endless genealogies, which promote disputes rather than the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="plan of God that operates by ¦ edification of God, which is in ELZ SCR">e</a></sup>plan of God that operates by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:22"> The goal of our instruction is love from a pure heart, from a good conscience, and from sincere faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:9"> Some have strayed from these and have turned aside to meaningless talk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:12"> desiring to be teachers of the law, although they do not understand what they are talking about or what they so confidently affirm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:12–13; Psalms 19:7–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:16"> Now we know that the law is good, if one uses it lawfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:20"> We know that the law is not laid down for the righteous, but for the lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for those who kill their father or mother, for murderers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3"> fornicators, homosexuals, slave traders, liars, perjurers, and whatever else is opposed to the sound teaching</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:15"> that conforms to the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, which has been entrusted to me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Testimony of God's Mercy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> I give thanks to Christ Jesus our Lord, who has given me strength, because he considered me to be faithful and appointed me to serve him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:4–8"> even though I was formerly a blasphemer, a persecutor, and an insolent man. But I received mercy because I had acted ignorantly in unbelief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:7–8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:10"> and the grace of our Lord abounded to me greatly with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 2:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 19:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:16–17"> This saying is trustworthy and deserving of full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 7:47"> But I received mercy for this reason, that in me, as the foremost sinner,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus NA SBL WH">f</a></sup>Jesus Christ might show complete patience, making me an example for those who would come to believe in him for eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:20–21; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:25; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:15–16"> Now to the King of the ages, immortal, invisible, the only<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="wise 96.6% ¦ — CT 1.8%">g</a></sup>wise God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:1–3"> This charge I entrust to yoʋ, Timothy, my child, according to the prophecies once made about yoʋ, so that by them yoʋ may fight the good fight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:15–16; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:10"> having faith and a good conscience. By rejecting this, some have shipwrecked their faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:4–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:32"> Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan so that they may learn not to blaspheme.</span>
</div>
</div>
