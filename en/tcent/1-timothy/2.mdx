## Instruction About Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:9–10; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:16"> I urge then, first of all, that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> for kings and all who are in authority, so that we may lead quiet and peaceful lives in all godliness and dignity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="For this ¦ This CT">a</a></sup>For this is good and acceptable before God our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> who wants all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:20; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:15"> For there is one God and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 20:28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:1–2"> who gave himself as a ransom for all, a testimony given at the proper time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:1"> For this testimony I was appointed to be a preacher, an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth. (I am speaking the truth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in Christ ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>in Christ; I am not lying.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 134:2; Psalms 63:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:22"> Therefore I want the men in every place to pray, lifting up holy hands without anger or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="argument ¦ arguments WH">c</a></sup>argument.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:3–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:3–5; Psalms 149:4"> Likewise, I want the women to adorn themselves in respectable apparel, with modesty and discretion, not with elaborately braided<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="hair, gold, pearls, ¦ hair and gold, or pearls CT">d</a></sup>hair, gold, pearls, or expensive clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:3–5; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:19"> but with good works, which is proper for women who profess godly reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:34–35; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:22–24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:3"> A woman should learn quietly with full submission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:34"> I do not permit a woman to teach or to have authority over a man; rather, she is to remain quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was formed first, then Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> And Adam was not the one who was deceived; it was the woman who was deceived and fell into transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:7"> Nevertheless, women will be saved through childbearing, if they continue in faith, love, and holiness, with self-control.</span>
</div>
