## Instructions for the Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:2"> Do not rebuke an older man, but appeal to him as you would to a father. Speak to younger men as brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:50; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:8"> older women as mothers, and younger women as sisters, in all purity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honor widows who are truly widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:1–3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 19:26–27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:4–6"> But if any widow has children or grandchildren, they should first learn to show godliness toward their own household and to make some repayment to their parents, for this is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="acceptable ¦ good and acceptable ANT TR">a</a></sup>acceptable in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 2:37; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:32"> Now a woman who is truly a widow and left all alone has put her hope in God and continues night and day in her supplications and prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:5; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 18:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 15:24"> But she who lives in self-indulgence is dead even while she lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:1"> Give these commands as well, so that no one will be open to blame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 11:11–13"> But if anyone does not provide for his own relatives, and especially for those of his own household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 2:36–37"> A widow may be put on the list to receive support if she is at least sixty years old, has been the wife of one husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:8; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:36"> and has a reputation for good works, that is, if she has brought up children, shown hospitality, washed the feet of the saints, helped the afflicted, and devoted herself to every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:14; Hosea 13:6; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:5"> But reject younger widows, for when their sensual desires draw them away from Christ, they wish to marry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:6; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:34"> thus bringing judgment upon themselves because they have broken their previous pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:6–11; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:30"> At the same time, they learn to be idle, wandering about from house to house. And they are not only idle, but also gossips and busybodies, saying things they should not say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:8"> So I advise the younger widows to marry, bear children, and manage their households, so as to give the adversary no opportunity to revile us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:2; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:4–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:20–22"> For some have already turned away to follow Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:3–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:8"> If any believing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="man or woman … they 95.1% ¦ woman … she CT 1.9%">b</a></sup>man or woman has relatives who are widows, they must help them; the church should not be burdened, so that it may help those who are truly widows.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:12–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:6"> Elders who lead well should be considered worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in preaching and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:7–10"> For the Scripture says, “Yoʋ shall not muzzle an ox while it is threshing,” and,<span className="woj">“The worker is worthy of his wages.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:1"> Do not accept an accusation against an elder unless it is confirmed by two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:20"> As for those who sin, reprove them in the presence of all, so that others will be afraid to follow their example.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:13"> I solemnly charge yoʋ before God and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the Lord Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus CT">c</a></sup>the Lord Jesus Christ and the chosen angels to observe these things without partiality, doing nothing out of favoritism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:6"> Do not lay hands on anyone hastily, and do not take part in the sins of others; keep yoʋrself pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:4"> (No longer drink only water, but use a little wine for the sake of yoʋr stomach and yoʋr frequent illnesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:10; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:19–21"> The sins of some are obvious, going before them to judgment, but the sins of others trail along behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 11:33; Psalms 37:5–6"> In the same way, good works are obvious, and even those that are not obvious cannot remain hidden.</span>
</div>
