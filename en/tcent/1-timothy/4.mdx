## Some Will Fall Away from the Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:1–9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:13–15"> Now the Spirit expressly says that in latter times some will fall away from the faith and occupy themselves with deceitful spirits and the teachings of demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:19; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:18"> being influenced by the hypocrisy of liars who are seared in their own consciences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:6"> They forbid marriage and demand abstinence from foods that God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:14"> For everything created by God is good, and nothing is to be rejected when it is received with thanksgiving,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 4:4"> since it is sanctified through the word of God and prayer.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Good Servant of Jesus Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:7–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:52"> If yoʋ point these things out to the brothers, yoʋ will be a good servant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus CT">a</a></sup>Jesus Christ, nourished by the words of the faith and the sound doctrine that yoʋ have closely followed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:4"> Have nothing to do with profane myths and old wives' tales. Rather train yoʋrself for godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:33; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:6; Psalms 37:3–4"> For bodily training is beneficial to a certain extent, but godliness is beneficial in every way, since it holds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="promise ¦ promises ANT PCK">b</a></sup>promise for both the present life and the life to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:15"> This saying is trustworthy and deserving of full acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:6"> For this is why we labor and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="suffer reproach ¦ strive NA TH WH">c</a></sup>suffer reproach, because we have put our hope in the living God, who is the Savior of all people, and especially of those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:15"> Command and teach these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:15"> Let no one despise yoʋ because yoʋ are young, but set an example for the believers in yoʋr speech, conduct, love,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="spirit, 90.4% ¦ — CT 9%">d</a></sup>spirit, faith, and purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 6:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2"> Until I come, give attention to the public reading of Scripture, to exhortation, and to teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:9–11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:18"> Do not neglect the gift that is in yoʋ, which was given to yoʋ through prophecy when the council of elders laid their hands on yoʋ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:15–16; Psalms 143:5"> Attend to these things and immerse yourself in them, so that yoʋr progress may be evident to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:8–9"> Watch yoʋr life and doctrine closely. Persevere in these things, for as yoʋ do this, yoʋ will save both yoʋrself and those who listen to yoʋ.</span>
</div>
