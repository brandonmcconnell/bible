## Qualifications for Overseers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:7; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:2"> This saying is trustworthy: If anyone aspires to the office of overseer, he desires a good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:6–9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:13"> Therefore the overseer must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, sober-minded, sensible, respectable, hospitable, able to teach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:2"> not given to wine, not violent,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="not greedy for sordid gain, 69.5% ¦ — CT 29.5%">a</a></sup>not greedy for sordid gain, but gentle, not contentious, and not a lover of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:6; Genesis 18:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:12"> He must lead his own household well, keeping his children in submission with all dignity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:24; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:28"> (For if a man does not know how to lead his own household, how will he take care of God's church?)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:12–13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:2"> He must not be a new convert, or he might become puffed up and fall into the condemnation of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:5"> He must also have a good reputation among outsiders, so that he does not fall into the reproach and snare of the devil.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Qualifications for Deacons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:23; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 6:3–6"> Deacons likewise must be dignified, not double-tongued, not given to much wine, and not greedy for sordid gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:19; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:9–10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:5"> They must hold the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:6–7"> They must first be tested; if they are above reproach, let them serve as deacons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:2"> Their wives likewise must be dignified, not slanderers, but sober-minded and faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:4–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:1"> A deacon must be the husband of one wife and must lead his children and his own household well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:10; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 21:35"> For those who have served well as deacons obtain a good standing for themselves along with great boldness in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Mystery of Godliness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:34; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:15–17"> I am writing these things to yoʋ, hoping to come to yoʋ soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:21–22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:9"> But if I delay, I am writing so that yoʋ may know how one ought to conduct himself in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, a pillar and foundation of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 1:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:19"> Beyond all question, great is the mystery of godliness:<br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="God ¦ He CT">b</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">God was revealed in the flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">vindicated by the Spirit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">seen by angels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">preached among nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">believed on in the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and taken up in glory.</span></span>
