## The Judgment at Christ's Coming

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–10; Proverbs 4:18"> We ought always to give thanks to God for you, brothers, as is only fitting, because your faith is greatly increasing and the love that each of you has for one another is abounding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:36; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:7–8; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:1–3"> As a result we ourselves boast about you in the churches of God for your perseverance and faithfulness in all the persecutions and tribulations that you are enduring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:28; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 20:35"> This is evidence of the righteous judgment of God, and it is happening so that you may be considered worthy of the kingdom of God, for which you are suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:25; Psalms 94:20–23"> It is indeed right for God to repay with affliction those who afflict you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 14:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:27; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:14–15"> and to give to you who are being afflicted relief along with us at the revelation of the Lord Jesus from heaven with his mighty angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:27"> With flaming fire he will inflict vengeance on those who do not know God and who do not obey the gospel of our Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ 𝔐pt ANT HF PCK TR">b</a></sup>Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:41; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 14:10–11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:46"> They will suffer the punishment of eternal destruction, separated from the presence of the Lord and from the glory of his might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 17:10"> on that day when he comes to be glorified by his saints and to be marveled at by all who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="have believed ¦ believe TR">c</a></sup>have believed, including you, because you have believed our testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:9–13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3"> With this in mind, we always pray for you that our God will make you worthy of his calling and by his power bring to fulfillment every good intention and work of faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 17:10"> so that the name of our Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ 𝔐pt ANT PCK TR">d</a></sup>Jesus will be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace of our God and Lord, Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
