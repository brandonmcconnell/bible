## Pray for Us

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:9"> Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly and be glorified, just as it was with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:3–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:31; Deuteronomy 32:20"> And pray that we may be rescued from deviant and evil men, for not all have faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:13"> But the Lord is faithful; he will establish you and guard you from the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:10"> We have confidence in the Lord about you, that you are doing and will continue to do what we command<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="you ¦ — CT">a</a></sup>you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 119:36; 1 Chronicles 29:18; Deuteronomy 30:6"> May the Lord direct your hearts to the love of God and the steadfastness of Christ.</span>

## Warning Against Idleness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:10–11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:17"> Now we command you, brothers, in the name of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="our ¦ the WH">b</a></sup>our Lord Jesus Christ, to keep away from every brother who walks in idleness and does not live according to the teaching we passed on to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="them ¦ him TR ¦ you WH">c</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:16"> For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we were not idle when we were with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:28"> nor did we eat bread from anyone free of charge. On the contrary, with labor and hardship we worked day and night so as not to be a burden to any of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:4–14"> We did this, not because we do not have the right to be supported, but to present ourselves as an example for you to imitate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> For even when we were with you, we gave you this command: “If anyone is not willing to work, neither should he eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:11"> For we hear that some among you are walking in idleness, not working at all, but being busybodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:8"> Now we command and exhort such people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="by our ¦ in the CT">d</a></sup>by our Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and eat their own bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:9–10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:1"> But as for you, brothers, do not grow weary in doing good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:11"> If anyone does not obey what we say in this letter, take note of him and do not associate with him, so that he will feel ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:5"> Yet do not regard him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.</span>
</div>
