## Teach Sound Doctrine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:11–14"> But as for yoʋ, teach what is consistent with sound doctrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:8"> Elderly men are to be sober-minded, dignified, sensible, and sound in faith, love, and patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:3–5"> Elderly women likewise are to be reverent in behavior, not slanderous or enslaved to much wine, but teaching what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:11"> so that they can admonish the young women to be loving to their husbands, loving to their children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:10"> sensible, pure,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="homemakers ¦ working at home CT">a</a></sup>homemakers, kind, and submissive to their own husbands, so that the word of God may not be discredited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:5; Joel 2:28"> In the same way, urge the younger men to be of sound mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:2"> Show yoʋrself to be a model of good works in every way, and in yoʋr teaching show integrity, dignity,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="incorruptibility, 74.2% ¦ — CT ELZ 12.7%">b</a></sup>incorruptibility,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:16"> and sound speech that is above reproach, so that any opponent may be put to shame because he has nothing bad to say about<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="us ¦ you TR">c</a></sup>us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:18–25; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:22–25"> Slaves are to be submissive to their own masters and well-pleasing in everything, not talking back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:9"> not pilfering, but showing all good faith, so that in every way they will make the teaching of God our Savior attractive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="saving grace of God has appeared ¦ grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation CT">d</a></sup>saving grace of God has appeared to all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:19"> instructing us to deny ungodliness and worldly lusts and to live in a sensible, righteous, and godly manner in the present age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:27"> as we wait for the blessed hope—the appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus WH">e</a></sup>Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9"> He gave himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and to purify for himself a people for his own possession, zealous for good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:12–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:13"> Declare these things; encourage and rebuke with all authority. Let no one despise yoʋ.</span>
</div>
