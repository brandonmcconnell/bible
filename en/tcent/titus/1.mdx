## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:25"> Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, appointed to further the faith of God's chosen ones and the knowledge of the truth that is in accordance with godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:25; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9"> in the hope of eternal life, which God, who does not lie, promised before time began;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:4–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:1"> in his own time he revealed his word through the preaching with which I was entrusted by the command of God our Savior;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:2; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:1"> to Titus, my true child in our common faith:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Grace, mercy, 91.3% ¦ Grace CT 4.2%">a</a></sup>Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the Lord Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus CT">b</a></sup>the Lord Jesus Christ our Savior.</span>

## Qualifications for Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:3; Ecclesiastes 12:9"> The reason I left yoʋ in Crete was for yoʋ to put in order what remained to be done and to appoint elders in every town, as I directed yoʋ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:2–7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:6–8"> An elder must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, with faithful children who are not rebellious and cannot be accused of debauchery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10"> For an overseer, as God's steward, must be above reproach, not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not given to wine, not violent, not greedy for sordid gain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:7; 1 Kings 5:7"> but hospitable, a lover of goodness, sensible, just, holy, and self-controlled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:3"> holding firmly to the trustworthy message as it has been taught, so that he can encourage others with sound doctrine and refute those who contradict it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:4"> For there are many rebellious people, full of meaningless talk and deception, especially those of the circumcision faction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:19"> They must be silenced, because they are ruining entire households by teaching what they ought not to teach, for the sake of sordid gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:28"> One of their very own prophets said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, and lazy gluttons.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:15"> This testimony is true. Therefore rebuke them sharply, so that they may become sound in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:9"> not paying any attention to Jewish myths or the commandments of men who turn away from the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:23"> To the pure, all things are pure; but to those who are defiled and do not believe, nothing is pure. In fact, both their minds and their consciences are defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:4; Isaiah 29:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:6"> They profess to know God, but by their works they deny him. They are detestable, disobedient, and have proven themselves to be unqualified for any good work.</span>
</div>
</div>
