## Be Ready for Every Good Work

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:1–7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:13–17; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> Remind the people to be subject to rulers and authorities, to be obedient, to be ready for every good work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:31; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:24–25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:2"> to slander no one, and to be peaceable and considerate, always showing gentleness toward everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:1–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:21"> For we ourselves were once foolish, disobedient, led astray, and enslaved to various lusts and pleasures. Living in wickedness and envy, we were loathsome and hated one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:4–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:10–11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:10"> But when the kindness of God our Savior and his love for mankind appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8–9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:11"> he saved us, not because of any righteous works we had done, but according to his mercy, through the washing of regeneration and the renewal of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:5; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:45"> He richly poured out his Spirit upon us through Jesus Christ our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:11"> so that, having been justified by his grace, we might become heirs according to the hope of eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:9; Psalms 16:2–3"> This saying is trustworthy. I want yoʋ to insist on these things, so that those who have put their trust in God may be careful to devote themselves to good works. These things are good and beneficial for people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:14; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:7"> But avoid foolish controversies, genealogies, quarrels, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="disputes ¦ any dispute PCK WH">a</a></sup>disputes about the law, for they are unprofitable and futile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:15–17"> Reject a divisive person after a first and second warning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 19:22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:26–28"> For yoʋ know that such a person is corrupted and sinful; he is self-condemned.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Instructions and Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:8–9"> When I send Artemas to yoʋ, or Tychicus, make every effort to come to me at Nicopolis, for I have decided to spend the winter there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 22:35; THE THIRD LETTER OF JOHN 1:6–8"> Do everything yoʋ can to help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their way, and make sure they lack nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:19"> Our people must learn to devote themselves to good works in order to meet pressing needs, so that they will not be unfruitful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:23–24; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:1–2"> All who are with me greet yoʋ. Greet those who love us in the faith.<br />Grace be with you all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Amen. ¦ — CT">b</a></sup><span className="desc">Amen.</span></span>
</div>
