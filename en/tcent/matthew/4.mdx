<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:40–42; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:14"> As Jesus was walking by the Sea of Galilee, he saw two brothers, Simon (who is called Peter) and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:17–18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:24"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.”</span></span>
