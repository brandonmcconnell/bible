## The Death of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 9:7–9"> At that time Herod the tetrarch heard the report about Jesus</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 10:41; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:28"> and said to his servants, “This man is John the Baptist; he has been raised from the dead, and that is why these mighty powers are at work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:19–20; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 4:12"> For Herod had arrested John, bound him, and put him in prison on account of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:18"> For John had been saying to him, “It is not lawful for yoʋ to have her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:19–20"> Although Herod wanted to kill John, he was afraid of the crowd, because they regarded John as a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herod's birthday<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="was celebrated ¦ came CT">a</a></sup>was celebrated, the daughter of Herodias danced before the guests and pleased Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> So he promised with an oath to give her whatever she might ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> Prompted by her mother, she said, “Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptist.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:26"> The king was grieved, but because of his oaths and his guests, he commanded it to be given</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 17:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:35–36; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 9:9"> and sent orders to have John beheaded in prison.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 8:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 27:58–61"> Later, John's disciples came and took his body and buried it. Then they went and brought word to Jesus.</span>

## The Feeding of the Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 9:10–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 14:1–2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:30–44"> When Jesus heard this, he withdrew from there in a boat to a desolate place by himself. But the crowds heard about it and followed him on foot from the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 9:36; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:17"> And when Jesus went ashore and saw a large crowd, he was moved with compassion for them and healed their sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:35–36"> As evening approached,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="his ¦ the CT">b</a></sup>his disciples came to him and said, “This place is desolate, and the hour is now late. Send the crowds away so that they can go into the villages and buy themselves some food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“They have no need to go away; you give them something to eat.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:33–34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:37–38; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 9:13"> They said to him, “We have here only five loaves and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He said,<span className="woj">“Bring them here to me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:6–7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> Then he commanded the crowds to sit down on the grass, and taking the five loaves and the two fish, he looked up to heaven and said the blessing. Then he broke the loaves and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:42–44; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:8–10"> They all ate and were filled, and the disciples picked up what was left over of the broken pieces, twelve baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:19; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:8–11; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:4"> Now those who had eaten were about five thousand men, besides women and children.</span>

## Jesus Walks on Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:45–51; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:15–21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:39"> Immediately Jesus compelled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the ¦ his ANT PCK TR">c</a></sup>the disciples to get into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the ¦ a SBL WH">d</a></sup>the boat and go ahead of him to the other side of the sea, while he sent the crowds away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:46; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 26:36"> After sending the crowds away, he went up on the mountain by himself to pray. When evening came, he was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:48; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 8:24"> Meanwhile, the boat was already<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="in the middle of the sea ¦ some distance from the land NA SBL WH">e</a></sup>in the middle of the sea, being tossed by the waves, for the wind was against them.</span>

## Jesus Heals the Sick in Gennesaret

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:53–56; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 5:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:24–25"> When they had crossed the sea, they came to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="the land of ¦ land at CT">k</a></sup>the land of Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 4:24–25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:55; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:28–34"> And when the men of that place recognized Jesus, they sent word into the entire surrounding region, and people brought to him all who were sick.</span>
