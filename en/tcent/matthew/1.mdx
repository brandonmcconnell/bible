## The Genealogy of Jesus Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:16"> The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 7:8; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> Abraham was the father of Isaac, Isaac was the father of Jacob, Jacob was the father of Judah and his brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> Judah was the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar, Perez was the father of Hezron, Hezron was the father of Ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> Ram was the father of Amminadab, Amminadab was the father of Nahshon, Nahshon was the father of Salmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:25"> Salmon was the father of Boaz by Rahab, Boaz was the father of Obed by Ruth, Obed was the father of Jesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> and Jesse was the father of David the king. David<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the king ¦ — CT">a</a></sup>the king was the father of Solomon by the wife of Uriah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> Solomon was the father of Rehoboam, Rehoboam was the father of Abijah, Abijah was the father of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Asa, Asa ¦ Asaph, Asaph CT">b</a></sup>Asa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> Asa was the father of Jehoshaphat, Jehoshaphat was the father of Joram, Joram was the father of Uzziah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> Uzziah was the father of Jotham, Jotham was the father of Ahaz, Ahaz was the father of Hezekiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> Hezekiah was the father of Manasseh, Manasseh was the father of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Amon, Amon ¦ Amos, Amos CT">c</a></sup>Amon, Amon was the father of Josiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> and Josiah was the father of Jechoniah and his brothers, at the time of the Babylonian resettlement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:27"> After the Babylonian resettlement, Jechoniah was the father of Shealtiel, Shealtiel was the father of Zerubbabel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Zerubbabel was the father of Abiud, Abiud was the father of Eliakim, Eliakim was the father of Azor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Azor was the father of Zadok, Zadok was the father of Achim, Achim was the father of Eliud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Eliud was the father of Eleazar, Eleazar was the father of Matthan, Matthan was the father of Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 27:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 4:25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:23"> and Jacob was the father of Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:2"> So in all there were fourteen generations from Abraham to David, fourteen generations from David to the Babylonian resettlement, and fourteen generations from the Babylonian resettlement to the Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Birth of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:4–5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> Now the birth of Jesus Christ took place in this way. When his mother Mary had been betrothed to Joseph, before they came together, she was found to be with child from the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:4–5"> Because her husband Joseph was a righteous man and did not want to shame her publicly, he intended to send her away privately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:18"> But as he thought about these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, “Joseph, Son of David, do not be afraid to receive Mary as yoʋr wife, for the child that is conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:23; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:29"> She will give birth to a son, and yoʋ shall name him Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:44; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> All this took place to fulfill what the Lord had spoken through the prophet:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalms 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the virgin will conceive and give birth to a son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will name him Immanuel”</span><br />(which means, “God with us”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:14; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:21–26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 2:5–8"> When Joseph woke from his sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord had commanded him. He took his wife</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 2:21; Exodus 13:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:21"> but had no marital relations with her until she had given birth to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="her firstborn 98.6% ¦ a CT 0.5%">d</a></sup>her firstborn son. And he named him Jesus.</span>
</div>
