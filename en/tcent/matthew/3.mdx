## The Preaching of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:6–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:76"> In those days John the Baptist came preaching in the wilderness of Judea, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 4:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:15; Daniel 2:44"> “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near.”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:6"> Now John wore a garment of camel's hair and a leather belt around his waist, and his food was locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:23"> Then Jerusalem was going out to him, and so were all Judea and all the region around the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 19:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 3:13–16; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:38–41"> They were being baptized by him in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jordan ¦ Jordan River CT">a</a></sup>Jordan, confessing their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:34; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:9"> But when John saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, “You brood of vipers, who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:4–8"> Produce<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="fruit ¦ fruits TR">b</a></sup>fruit consistent with repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:7–8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:27–29"> And do not think that you can say to yourselves, ‘We have Abraham as our father,’ for I tell you that God is able from these stones to raise up children for Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:19"> Even now the axe is laid to the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:16; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 1:5"> “I baptize you with water for repentance, but he who is coming after me is mightier than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you with the Holy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Spirit ¦ Spirit and fire ANT CT TR">c</a></sup>Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:30; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:17"> His winnowing fork is in his hand, and he will thoroughly clean out his threshing floor and gather his wheat into the barn, but the chaff he will burn up with unquenchable fire.”</span>

## The Baptism of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:32–34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 2:22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:9–11"> Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to be baptized by John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:3–7; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 1:5–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:6–8"> But John tried to prevent him, saying, “I need to be baptized by yoʋ, and yet yoʋ are coming to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 4:34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:10; Psalms 40:7–8"> But Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“Permit it for now, for thus it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness.”</span>Then John permitted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:21–22"> After being baptized, Jesus immediately went up from the water. And behold, the heavens were opened<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="to him 99.3% ¦ — SBL WH 0.3%">d</a></sup>to him, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and alighting on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalms 2:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 17:5"> And behold, a voice from heaven said, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”</span>
