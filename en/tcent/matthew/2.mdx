<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 23:5; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 22:16"> saying, “Where is the child who has been born king of the Jews? For we saw his star in the east and have come to worship him.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:29; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 6:12"> So he assembled all the chief priests and scribes of the people, and inquired of them where the Christ was to be born.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:19–20; Jeremiah 41:5–7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 20:20–21"> He sent them to Bethlehem and said, “Go search diligently for the child, and when you have found him, bring me word so that I too may go and worship him.”</span>

## The Escape to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:7; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 5:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:20"> After they were gone, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, saying, “Rise, take the child and his mother and flee to Egypt, and stay there until I tell yoʋ, for Herod is about to seek the child, to destroy him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 2:20–21; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:24"> So Joseph rose, took the child and his mother by night, and departed for Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Exodus 4:22; Numbers 24:8"> where he stayed until the death of Herod. This was to fulfill what the Lord had said through the prophet: “Out of Egypt I called my son.”</span>

## The Return to Nazareth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:20; Daniel 8:25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 2:12–13"> After Herod died, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:19; 1 Kings 12:1–3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 2:13"> saying, “Rise, take the child and his mother, and go to the land of Israel, for those who were seeking the child's life are dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:8; Genesis 6:22"> So Joseph rose, took the child and his mother, and went to the land of Israel.</span>

And being warned in a dream, he withdrew to the district of Galilee</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 13:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:45–46; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:24"> and came and dwelt in a town called Nazareth, so that what had been spoken by the prophets might be fulfilled: “He will be called a Nazarene.”</span>
