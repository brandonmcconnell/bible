## A Living Hope

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:13"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! In his great mercy he caused<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="us ¦ you ELZ">a</a></sup>us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 21:27; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:4"> to an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, reserved in heaven for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="you ¦ us ELZ ST">b</a></sup>you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:13–14"> who by the power of God are being guarded through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:12"> In this you rejoice, even if now for a little while you have had to suffer various trials</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:12"> so that the proven character of your faith—far more precious than gold that perishes even though it is tested by fire—may result in praise,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="honor, and glory ¦ glory, and honor CT PCK">c</a></sup>honor, and glory when Jesus Christ is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:29; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:13"> Although you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="once did not know ¦ have not seen CT SCR &#123;Note: The word once is not in the main Greek text but has been added for stylistic purposes.&#125;">d</a></sup>once did not know him, you love him; although you do not now see him, you believe in him and rejoice with an unspeakable and glorious joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:22; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:13"> because you are receiving the end result of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="your ¦ — WH">e</a></sup>your faith—the salvation of your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:25–27"> Concerning this salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace that would come to you searched and carefully investigated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:25–27"> inquiring about the time and circumstances that the Spirit of Christ within them was indicating when he testified in advance to the sufferings of Christ and the glories that would follow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:10"> It was revealed to them that they were not serving themselves, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="you ¦ us TR">f</a></sup>you, in regard to the things that have now been announced to you through those who preached the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven. Even angels long to catch a glimpse of these things.</span>

## Be Holy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:48; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:14"> But just as he who called you is holy, you also must be holy in all your conduct,</span>
