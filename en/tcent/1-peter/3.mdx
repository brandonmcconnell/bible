<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:25–26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:14–15"> but sanctify<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="the Lord God ¦ Christ the Lord CT">h</a></sup>the Lord God in your hearts. <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:5; Genesis 6:3"> who disobeyed long ago when God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="waited ¦ once waited TR">m</a></sup>waited patiently in the days of Noah, while the ark was being prepared, in which a few, that is, eight people, were saved through water.</span>
