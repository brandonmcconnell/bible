## Principles of Marriage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:26–27; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:37–38"> Now concerning the matters about which you wrote<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="to me ¦ — CT">a</a></sup>to me: “It is good for a man not to touch a woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:33; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:9"> But because of the cases of fornication, each man should have his own wife, and each woman should have her own husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:7; Exodus 21:10"> The husband should fulfill his marital duty to his wife, and likewise the wife to her husband.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Joel 2:16; Zechariah 12:12–14"> Do not deprive one another, except by mutual consent for a time, so that you may devote yourselves to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="fasting and 91.5% ¦ — CT 6.8%">b</a></sup>fasting and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="prayer and ¦ prayer, but PCK">c</a></sup>prayer and then come together again so that Satan may not tempt you because of your lack of self-control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:25; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:12"> Now I say this as a concession, not as a command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 19:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="For ¦ — CT">d</a></sup>For I wish that all people were as I am. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:32; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:34–35; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:26–27"> Now to the unmarried and the widows I say: It is good for them if they remain even as I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:11"> But if they cannot exercise self-control, they should marry. For it is better to marry than to burn with passion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:32; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 16:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 19:3–9"> Now to the married I give this command (not I, but the Lord): A wife must not separate from her husband</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:25"> Now to the rest I say this (I, not the Lord): If any brother has an unbelieving wife, and she consents to dwell with him, he must not divorce her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if a woman has an unbelieving husband and he consents to dwell with her, she must not divorce him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15–16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:15"> For the unbelieving husband is sanctified through his wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="her husband 94.7% ¦ the brother CT 3.5%">f</a></sup>her husband. <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:19; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:11; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:17–18"> But if the unbelieving spouse separates, let it be so. The brother or the sister is not enslaved in such cases. God has called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="us ¦ you NA WH">g</a></sup>us to live in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:1–2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:14"> For how do yoʋ know, O wife, whether yoʋ will save yoʋr husband? Or how do yoʋ know, O husband, whether yoʋ will save yoʋr wife?</span>

## Live the Life Assigned to You

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:3–8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:24"> Nevertheless, each person should live the life that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="God 86% ¦ the Lord CT 4.5%">h</a></sup>God has assigned to him and to which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="the Lord ¦ God CT">i</a></sup>the Lord has called him. This is the rule I lay down in all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:5; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:28; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:24"> Was any man already circumcised when he was called? He should not remove the marks of circumcision. Was any man uncircumcised when he was called? He should not become circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:3–4"> Circumcision is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing, but what matters is keeping the commandments of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:12"> Each person should remain in the calling in which he was called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:18–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:40–41"> Were yoʋ a slave when yoʋ were called? Do not be concerned about it, but if yoʋ are able to become free, make the most of the opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:22–24"> For he who was called in the Lord as a slave is the Lord's freedman. In the same way, he who was called as a free man is Christ's slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:18–19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:14"> You were bought with a price; do not become slaves of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:23–24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:17"> Brothers, each person should remain with God in the condition in which he was called.</span>

## The Unmarried and the Widowed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:40"> Now concerning virgins, I do not have a command from the Lord, but I give my judgment as one who has been shown mercy by the Lord to be trustworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:17"> I think it is good then, on account of the present distress, for a man to remain as he is.</span>
