## The Ministry of the Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:9"> People ought to regard us as servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 12:42; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 16:10–12"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Now ¦ In this regard CT">a</a></sup>Now it is required of stewards that they be found faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:15; 1 Samuel 16:7"> But for me it is a very small thing if I am judged by you, or by any human court. Indeed, I do not even judge myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 143:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:20–21; Psalms 130:3"> For I am conscious of nothing against myself, yet I am not thereby acquitted; but he who judges me is the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:13"> So then, do not judge anything before the appointed time. Wait until the Lord comes, who will bring to light what is hidden in darkness and reveal the intentions of the heart. At that time each person will receive his praise from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:19"> Now, brothers, I have applied these things to myself and Apollos for your sake, so that through us you may learn<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="not to think beyond what is written, ¦ the meaning of the saying, “Nothing beyond what is written,” CT">b</a></sup>not to think beyond what is written, so that you may not be puffed up, with each one of you supporting one man against another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:27; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:17"> For who regards yoʋ as superior? What do yoʋ have that yoʋ did not receive? And if yoʋ did receive it, why do yoʋ boast as though yoʋ did not receive it?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:25; Proverbs 13:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:6"> You are already full! You have already become rich! You have begun to reign without us! And how I wish that you really were reigning, so that we could reign with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:33; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:36; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:8–12"> For I think that God has put us apostles on display at the end of the procession, as sentenced to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, both to angels and to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:3; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:18"> We are fools for the sake of Christ, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You are esteemed, but we are dishonored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:35; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:23–27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:12"> To the present hour we hunger and thirst; we are poorly clothed and beaten down, with no home of our own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:12–14"> We labor, working with our own hands. When we are reviled, we bless; when we are persecuted, we endure;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:45; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 22:22"> when we are slandered, we encourage. We have become like the refuse of the world, the scum of all things, even to this present time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:15; THE THIRD LETTER OF JOHN 1:4"> I am not writing this to shame you, but rather<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="I am admonishing ¦ to admonish NA SBL WH">c</a></sup>I am admonishing you as my beloved children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:14"> For even if you had ten thousand guardians in Christ, you would not have many fathers. For in Christ Jesus I became a father to you through the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:17"> Therefore I urge you to be imitators of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:17"> That is why I have sent you Timothy, who is my beloved and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you of my ways in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Christ 87.1% ¦ Christ Jesus NA SBL WH 12%">d</a></sup>Christ, just as I teach them everywhere in every church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:6–8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:21"> But some of you have become puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:1–4; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:21; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:21"> But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out not the talk of those who have become puffed up, but their power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:4–5"> For the kingdom of God is demonstrated not by talk but by power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:7"> What do you prefer? Shall I come to you with a rod, or with love and a spirit of gentleness?</span>
</div>
