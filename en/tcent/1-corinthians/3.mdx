## Division Is of the Flesh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:14–15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:13–14"> And so, brothers, I could not address you as spiritual men, but as men of the flesh, as infants in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:11–14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:2"> I fed you milk, not solid food, for you were not yet able to receive it. Even now you are still not able,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3"> for you are still of the flesh. For where there is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="jealousy, strife, and division 91.2% ¦ jealousy and strife CT 4.8%">a</a></sup>jealousy, strife, and division among you, are you not of the flesh and behaving like mere men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:6"> For when one says, “I follow Paul,” and another says, “I follow Apollos,” are you not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of the flesh 94.9% ¦ mere men CT 2.7%">b</a></sup>of the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Who ¦ What CT">c</a></sup>Who then is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Paul, and who is Apollos 87.5% ¦ Apollos, and what is Paul CT 3%">d</a></sup>Paul, and who is Apollos,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="but servants … each? ¦ Servants … each. CT &#123;Note: The reading of CT would change the immediately preceding punctuation from a comma to a question mark.&#125;">e</a></sup>but servants through whom you believed, as the Lord has assigned to each?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:9–10; Proverbs 11:25; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:14–15"> I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:3; Psalms 115:1"> So then, neither he who plants is anything, nor he who waters, but only God who gives the growth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:27; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:8"> He who plants and he who waters are one, and each will receive his own reward according to his own labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20–22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:1"> For we are fellow workers of God; you are God's field, God's building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:20"> According to the grace of God given to me, like a wise master builder I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="have ¦ — CT">f</a></sup>have laid a foundation, and another builds upon it. But each person must be careful how he builds upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20; Isaiah 28:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:19"> For no one can lay a foundation other than the one that has been laid, which is Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:20"> If anyone builds upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="this ¦ the CT">g</a></sup>this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:14–15; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 20:12"> his work will become evident, for the Day will make it clear, because it is revealed by fire. And the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="fire ¦ fire itself CT">h</a></sup>fire will test each person's work and reveal what sort of work it is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:21–23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:5"> If the work that anyone has built remains, he will receive a reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:12–13; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:23"> If anyone's work is burned up, he will suffer loss; yet he himself will be saved, but only as through fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:19; Ezekiel 36:27; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:16"> Do you not know that you are the temple of God and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:18–20; Ezekiel 43:12; Numbers 19:20"> If anyone destroys the temple of God, God will destroy him. For the temple of God is holy, which is what you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Isaiah 5:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:3"> Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, he should become foolish so that he may become truly wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 5:13; Psalms 141:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:19–20"> For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. As it is written, “He catches the wise in their craftiness,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 94:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:21"> and again, “The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> So then, let no one boast in men. For everything belongs to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:19–22"> whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas, or the world, or life or death, or things present or things to come—everything belongs to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:29; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 17:9–10"> and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.</span>
