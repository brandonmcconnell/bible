## The Resurrection of the Dead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:32; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:18"> Now if it is preached that Christ has been raised from the dead, how can some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 11:25–26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:14"> If there is no resurrection of the dead, then not even Christ has been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:2"> And if Christ has not been raised, then our preaching is in vain, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="your ¦ our WH">a</a></sup>your faith is also in vain.</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if the dead are not raised, not even Christ has been raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:25; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:14"> And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is futile; you are still in your sins.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12–21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:45–49; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 5:21–29"> For just as all die in Adam, so all will be made alive in Christ.</span>
