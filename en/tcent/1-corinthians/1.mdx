## Thanksgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:14"> I always give thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="my ¦ — WH">c</a></sup>my God for you because of the grace of God that has been given to you in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:3"> For you have been enriched in him in every way, in all speech and all knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:6"> just as the testimony of Christ was confirmed among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:28"> so that you are not lacking in any gift as you eagerly await the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:23–24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:22"> He will strengthen you to the end, so that you will be blameless on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:3; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> God is faithful, by whom you were called into the fellowship of his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Divisions in the Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:1–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:1–7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:25"> Now I urge you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all agree in what you say and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be knit together in the same mind and in the same purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:23–25"> For it has been disclosed to me about you, my brothers, by those of Chloe's household, that there are quarrels among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:21–23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:42; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:9–10"> What I mean is this: One of you says, “I follow Paul,” while another says, “I follow Apollos,” or, “I follow Cephas,” or, “I follow Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:4; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 2:38"> Has Christ been divided? Was Paul crucified for you, or were you baptized into the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:4"> I give thanks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="to God ¦ — SBL TH WH">d</a></sup>to God that I baptized none of you, except Crispus and Gaius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:28–29; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:18"> so that no one can say that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="I baptized into my own ¦ you were baptized into my CT">e</a></sup>I baptized into my own name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:17; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:33"> (I also baptized the household of Stephanas. Beyond that, I do not recall if I baptized anyone else.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:16"> For Christ did not send me to baptize, but to preach the gospel, and not with cleverness of speech, so that the cross of Christ would not be deprived of its power.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ the Power and Wisdom of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:10"> For the message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:19"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will destroy the wisdom of the wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the intelligence of the intelligent I will nullify.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:22; Job 12:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:19"> Where is the wise man? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has not God made the wisdom of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="this ¦ the CT">f</a></sup>this world foolish?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:20–22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:25"> For since, in the wisdom of God, the world through its wisdom did not know God, it pleased God through the foolishness of our proclamation to save those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:1–4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:38–39; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:11"> For Jews ask for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="a sign ¦ signs CT">g</a></sup>a sign and Greeks seek wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:8"> but we preach Christ crucified, a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Greeks ¦ Gentiles CT">h</a></sup>Greeks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:18"> but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:33–36; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> For the foolishness of God is wiser than men, and the weakness of God is stronger than men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:18–20; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:9–11"> Consider your calling, brothers, that not many of you were wise according to the flesh, not many were powerful, not many were of noble birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:25; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:20"> But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise, and God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> God chose what is lowly and despised in the world, things that are regarded as nothing, to nullify the things that are regarded as something,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:9"> so that no flesh might boast before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:1"> It is because of him that you are in Christ Jesus, who has become for us wisdom from God, and in whom we have righteousness, sanctification, and redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> Therefore, as it is written, “Let him who boasts, boast in the Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>
