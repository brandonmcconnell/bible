## The Rights of an Apostle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:19"> Am I not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="an apostle? Am I not free 92% ¦ free? Am I not an apostle CT 5.8%">a</a></sup>an apostle? Am I not free? Have I not seen Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Christ ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>Christ our Lord? Are you not my work in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:27; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:1–3"> If to others I am not an apostle, at least I am to you, for you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:16–19; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:10"> This is my defense to those who examine me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:8–9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:7"> Do we not have a right to eat and to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:42; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 8:14"> Do we not have a right to take along a wife who is a sister in Christ, as do the other apostles, the brothers of the Lord, and Cephas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:7–9; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:1–2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 4:36"> Or is it only Barnabas and I who must work to support ourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:3–4"> Who at any time serves as a soldier while supplying his own provisions? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="of ¦ — CT">c</a></sup>of its fruit?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or who ¦ Who SBL">d</a></sup>Or who tends a flock and does not drink of its milk?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:31"> Do I say this on human authority? Does not the law also say the same thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:18; Psalms 104:27"> For in the law of Moses it is written, “Yoʋ shall not muzzle an ox while it is threshing.” Is it for oxen that God is concerned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:23–24"> or does he speak entirely for our sake? Yes, this was written for our sake, because he who plows ought to plow in hope, and he who threshes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="in hope ought to partake of his hope 90.1% ¦ ought to thresh in hope of partaking CT 3.5%">e</a></sup>in hope ought to partake of his hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:15"> If we have sown spiritual blessings among you, is it too much if we reap material benefits from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:12"> If others have a share in this right over you, do not we have it even more?<br />Nevertheless, we have not made use of this right, but we endure everything so that we may not give any hindrance to the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Do you not know that those who perform sacred services eat of the temple sacrifices, and those who attend to the altar have a share in the altar sacrifices?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:17–18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:4"> In the same way, the Lord has commanded that those who proclaim the gospel should earn their living by the gospel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:12"> But I have not used any of these rights, and I am not writing this so that such may be done in my case. For I would rather die<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="than have anyone ¦ than—no one will CT">f</a></sup>than have anyone deprive me of my reason for boasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:17"> If I preach the gospel, I have no reason to boast, because I am compelled to preach, and woe to me if I do not preach the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> If I do this voluntarily, I have a reward; but if I do it under compulsion, it is because I have been entrusted with a responsibility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:7–9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:6"> What then is my reward? That when I preach, I may present the gospel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="of Christ ¦ — CT">g</a></sup>of Christ free of charge, so as not to make full use of my right in the gospel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:15; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:19–20"> For though I am free from all, I have made myself a servant to all, in order to win as many as possible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:18; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:20–26"> To the Jews I became like a Jew, so that I might win the Jews. To those under the law I became like one under the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="law 92.1% ¦ law (though I myself am not under the law) CT 6.9%">h</a></sup>law, so that I might win those under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:2"> To those outside the law I became like one outside the law (though I am not outside the law of God but under the law of Christ), so that I might win those outside the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:33; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:19"> To the weak I became<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="like one who is ¦ — CT">i</a></sup>like one who is weak, so that I might win the weak. I have become all things to all people, so that by all means I might save some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:35; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:25–27"> I do<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="this 91.4% ¦ it all CT 7.4%">j</a></sup>this for the sake of the gospel, so that I may share in its blessings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:7–8"> Do you not know that those who run in a race all run, but only one receives the prize? Run in such a way that you may obtain it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:5; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:7–8"> Every athlete who competes exercises self-control in all things. They do so to receive a perishable wreath, but we do so to receive an imperishable crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:5"> Therefore I do not run aimlessly, nor do I box as though beating the air;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:5"> but I discipline my body and make it my slave, lest somehow after preaching to others I myself should be disqualified.</span>
</div>
