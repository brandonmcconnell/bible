## Prophecy and Tongues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:39; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:1"> Pursue love, and earnestly desire spiritual gifts, but even more that you may prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 19:6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:46"> For he who speaks in a tongue speaks not to men but to God; for no one understands him, but he speaks mysteries in the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:13; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:20"> But he who prophesies speaks edification, encouragement, and comfort to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17"> He who speaks in a tongue edifies himself, but he who prophesies edifies the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:28–30; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17"> Now I would like all of you to speak in tongues, but even more that you would prophesy.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="For greater ¦ Greater CT">a</a></sup>For greater is he who prophesies than he who speaks in tongues, unless someone interprets, so that the church may be edified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:16"> Now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, how will I benefit you unless I speak to you with a revelation or knowledge or prophecy or teaching?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 7:32; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:1"> It is the same way with lifeless instruments that produce sound, such as the flute or harp. If they do not produce distinct notes, how will anyone recognize what is being played?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> And if the trumpet does not produce a distinct sound, who will prepare himself for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:26"> It is the same with you. If you do not produce intelligible speech with your tongue, how will anyone know what you are saying? You will just be speaking into the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are undoubtedly many kinds of languages in the world, and none<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of them 89.7% ¦ — CT SCR 7%">b</a></sup>of them is without meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 28:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11"> If then I do not know the meaning of a particular language, I will be a foreigner to him who is speaking, and he who is speaking will be a foreigner to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:3–4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:1"> It is the same with you. Since you earnestly desire spiritual gifts, seek to abound in them for the edification of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:30; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:13–14"> Therefore anyone who speaks in a tongue should pray for the ability to interpret what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:15–16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:19"> For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 4:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:18"> What then should I do? I will pray with my spirit, but I will pray with my mind also. I will sing praise with my spirit, but I will sing praise with my mind also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalms 106:48; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 5:14"> Otherwise, if yoʋ bless with yoʋr spirit, how will anyone in the position of an outsider say “Amen” when yoʋ give thanks, since he does not know what yoʋ are saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:6"> For yoʋ may very well be giving thanks, but the other person is not edified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:4–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:7"> I give thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="my ¦ — CT">c</a></sup>my God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="speaking ¦ that I speak CT">d</a></sup>speaking in tongues more than you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:21–22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:4"> Nevertheless, in church I would rather speak five words with my mind to instruct others, than ten thousand words in a tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:19"> Brothers, do not be childish in your way of thinking. Rather, as to malice be infants, but in your way of thinking be mature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> In the law it is written, “With foreign tongues and with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="foreign lips ¦ the lips of foreigners CT">e</a></sup>foreign lips I will speak to this people, and even then they will not listen to me, says the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:6–12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:1"> So then, tongues are a sign not for believers but for unbelievers, while prophecy is a sign not for unbelievers but for believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:13; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:18"> So if the whole church comes together and everyone is speaking in tongues, and outsiders or unbelievers come in, will they not say that you are out of your minds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:37; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:15; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:12–13"> But if an unbeliever or outsider comes in while everyone is prophesying, he will be convicted by all and called to account by all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalms 72:11"> The secrets of his heart will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="thus 92.9% ¦ — CT 5.6%">f</a></sup>thus be revealed, and he will fall on his face and worship God, declaring that God is truly among you.</span>

## Orderly Worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:7–10"> What then should you do, brothers? When you come together, each one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="of you 95% ¦ — CT 2.7%">g</a></sup>of you has a psalm, a teaching, a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="tongue, a revelation ¦ revelation, a tongue CT">h</a></sup>tongue, a revelation, or an interpretation. Everything must be done for edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:26"> If any speak in a tongue, only two, or at the most three, should speak; they should each speak in turn, and someone must interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But if there is no interpreter, they should keep quiet in the church and speak in tongues privately to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:19–21; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:1–3"> Two or three prophets should speak, and the others should evaluate what they say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> But if something is revealed to another who is seated, the first speaker should be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:11"> For you can all prophesy one by one, so that all may learn and all may be encouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:19–20; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> Remember that the spirits of prophets are subject to the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:17–18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:40; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:16"> For God is not a God of disorder but of peace; this is true in all the churches of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Your women ¦ Women CT">i</a></sup>Your women should be silent in the churches, for they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="have not been ¦ are not CT">j</a></sup>have not been permitted to speak, but must be in submission, just as the law says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:25–27"> If they want to learn anything, they should ask their own husbands at home; for it is shameful for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="women ¦ a woman CT">k</a></sup>women to speak in church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:15"> Or was it from you that the word of God came? Or are you the only ones it has reached?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:2"> If anyone thinks that he is a prophet or spiritual person, he should acknowledge that the things I am writing to you are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="commandments ¦ a commandment NA TH WH ¦ — SBL">l</a></sup>commandments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 22:11–12"> But if anyone ignores this,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="let him be ignorant ¦ he will be ignored CT">m</a></sup>let him be ignorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:31; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:20"> So then,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="brothers ¦ my brothers CT">n</a></sup>brothers, earnestly desire to prophesy and do not forbid speaking in tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:26–33"> Let all things be done decently and in order.</span>
