## Lawsuits Among Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:15–17; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 19:38; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:33"> When any of you have a dispute against another, do yoʋ dare to go to court before the unrighteous and not before the saints?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 19:28; Daniel 7:22; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 20:4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Do ¦ Or do CT">a</a></sup>Do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you not competent to judge trivial cases?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:41; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:4"> Do you not know that we will judge angels, not to mention matters of this life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:12; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 6:2–4"> If then you have disputes about such matters, do you seat as judges those who have no standing in the church?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:34; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:14; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:13–18"> I say this to your shame. Is there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="not a wise man among you, not even one, ¦ no one wise among you CT">b</a></sup>not a wise man among you, not even one, who would be able to decide between a man and his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:14–15; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:9–11; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:11–15"> but one brother goes to court against another, and they do so before unbelievers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:17–19; Proverbs 20:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:19–23"> As it is, to have lawsuits with one another is already a defeat for you. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be defrauded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 10:19"> But you yourselves wrong, and you yourselves defraud, and you do<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="these things ¦ this CT">c</a></sup>these things to your own brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:19–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:4–5"> Do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived! Neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor homosexuals, nor sodomites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:21; Ezekiel 22:29"> nor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the greedy, nor thieves ¦ thieves, nor the greedy CT TR">d</a></sup>the greedy, nor thieves, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:5–7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:2"> And that is what some of you were. But you were washed, you were sanctified, you were justified in the name of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the Lord Jesus ¦ the Lord Jesus Christ NA ¦ our Lord Jesus Christ TH WH">e</a></sup>the Lord Jesus and by the Spirit of our God.</span>

## Flee Fornication

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:23–33; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:27; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:15–16"> “All things are lawful for me,” but not all things are beneficial. “All things are lawful for me,” but I will not be mastered by anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:12"> Food is for the stomach and the stomach for food, but God will do away with them both. The body is not meant for fornication but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:19–20"> Now God raised the Lord and will also raise us up by his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:15–16"> Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Shall I then take the members of Christ and make them members of a prostitute? Certainly not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:31; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 10:8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Do ¦ Or do 𝔐pt ANT CT PCK TR">f</a></sup>Do you not know that he who is joined to a prostitute is one with her in body? For it says, “The two will become one flesh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 17:21–23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:13"> But he who is joined to the Lord is one with him in spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:5"> Flee fornication. Every other sin that a person commits is outside the body, but he who commits fornication sins against his own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:5"> Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9"> for you were bought with a price. Therefore glorify God with your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="body and with your spirit, which belong to God. 93.4% ¦ body. CT 3.8%">g</a></sup>body and with your spirit, which belong to God.</span>
</div>
