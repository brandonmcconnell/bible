## Proclaiming Christ Crucified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:4"> When I came to you, brothers, I did not come proclaiming to you the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="testimony 93.1% ¦ mystery NA WH 4.4%">a</a></sup>testimony of God with lofty speech or wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 17:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:22–25"> For I resolved to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:10–13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:5–10"> I came to you in weakness and fear, and with much trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:17"> And my word and my preaching were not with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="persuasive words of human 66.9% ¦ persuasion by words of human MSS 11.1% ¦ persuasive human words of MSS 8.5% ¦ persuasive words of NA TH 3.9% • WH 0.6% ¦ the persuasion of SBL 0%">b</a></sup>persuasive words of human wisdom, but with a demonstration of the Spirit and of power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:7"> so that your faith would not be based on the wisdom of men, but on the power of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Spiritual Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:14; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:4"> Now we speak wisdom among the mature, but not a wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:26–27; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:25"> On the contrary, we speak the hidden wisdom of God in a mystery, which God foreordained for our glory before time began.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:27; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:1; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 7:2"> None of the rulers of this age understood this wisdom, for if they had understood it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalms 31:19; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:12"> But, as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“No eye has seen, no ear has heard,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and no heart has imagined</span><br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="what ¦ all that SBL WH">c</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">what God has prepared for those who love him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:21"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Now ¦ For SBL WH">d</a></sup>Now God has revealed these things to us through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="his ¦ the CT">e</a></sup>his Spirit. For the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:33–34"> For who among men knows the thoughts of a man except the man's spirit within him? In the same way, no one knows the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:5–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:20"> Now we did not receive the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, so that we might understand what God has freely given us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:17"> We speak about this, not with words taught by human wisdom but with words taught by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Holy ¦ — CT">f</a></sup>Holy Spirit, interpreting spiritual things to spiritual people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:5–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:11–17"> The natural man does not accept what comes from the Spirit of God, because it is foolishness to him; he is not able to understand it because it is spiritually discerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:14"> But he who is spiritual makes judgments about all things, and yet he himself is not subject to any man's judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:13–16"> “For who has known the mind of the Lord so as to advise him?” But we have the mind of Christ.</span>
