## One Body with Many Members

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:4–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:27"> For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members constitute one body even though they are many, so it is with Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:28"> For by one Spirit we were all baptized into one body—whether Jews or Greeks, slave or free—and we have all been given<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="to drink into one Spirit ¦ one Spirit to drink CT">e</a></sup>to drink into one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:27–28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:25"> Even so the body does not consist of one member, but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> If the foot should say, “Because I am not a hand, I am not a part of the body,” is it for that reason not a part of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> And if the ear should say, “Because I am not an eye, I am not a part of the body,” is it for that reason not a part of the body?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:21; Psalms 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body were an eye, where would the sense of hearing be? If the whole body were an ear, where would the sense of smell be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:11"> But as it is, God has placed each one of the members in the body just as he desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If they were all one member, where would the body be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12"> As it is, there are many members, yet one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> The eye cannot say to the hand, “I have no need of yoʋ,” nor in turn can the head say to the feet, “I have no need of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> On the contrary, the members of the body that seem to be weaker are essential,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> and the members of the body that we consider to be less honorable are clothed with more abundant honor. And our unpresentable parts are treated with greater modesty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> but our presentable parts have no need of it. Instead, God has put the body together, giving more abundant honor to the part that lacked it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:10–12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 17:21–26"> so that there would be no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="divisions ¦ division ANT CT PCK TR">f</a></sup>divisions in the body, but that the members would have the same concern for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:3"> If one member suffers, all the other members<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="suffer ¦ should suffer PCK">g</a></sup>suffer with it; if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="one ¦ a SBL TH WH">h</a></sup>one member is honored, all the other members<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="rejoice ¦ should rejoice PCK">i</a></sup>rejoice with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:30"> Now you are the body of Christ, and individually members of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:11–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:6–8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:18"> And those whom God has appointed in the church are as follows: first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, next miracles, then gifts of healing, helping, leading, and various kinds of tongues.</span>
