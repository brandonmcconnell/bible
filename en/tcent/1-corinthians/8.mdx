## Food Sacrificed to Idols

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:3"> Now concerning food sacrificed to idols, we know that “we all have knowledge.” Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:18; Proverbs 26:12"> If anyone thinks he knows something, he has not yet come to know<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="anything as he ought to know it ¦ as he ought to know CT">a</a></sup>anything as he ought to know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> But if anyone loves God, he is known by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Isaiah 41:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:19–20"> With regard then to eating food sacrificed to idols, we know that “an idol is nothing in the world,” and that “there is no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="other ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>other God but one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:8"> For even if there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on earth (as indeed there are many “gods” and many “lords”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:36; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> for us there is one God, the Father, from whom are all things, and we exist for him; and there is one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom are all things, and we exist through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:28–29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:22–23"> However, not everyone possesses this knowledge. But some<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="have idols in their conscience even now and 93.3% ¦ are accustomed to idols and even now CT 4.4%">c</a></sup>have idols in their conscience even now and eat such food as something sacrificed to idols, and since their conscience is weak, it is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:13"> But food<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="does ¦ will CT">d</a></sup>does not bring us close to God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="For we are no better off if we eat, and no worse off if we do not 80.4% ¦ We are no better off if we eat, and no worse off if we do not TH 1.4% ¦ We are no worse off if we do not eat, and no better off if we do NA WH 0.8%">e</a></sup>For we are no better off if we eat, and no worse off if we do not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:20–21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:16"> But be careful that this right of yours does not somehow become a stumbling block to the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:28–29; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:20–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:23"> For if someone sees yoʋ, who have knowledge, reclining for a meal in the temple of an idol, will not his weak conscience be emboldened to eat food sacrificed to idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1–3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Should 90.5% • MSS 5.2% ¦ For he CT 0.8%">f</a></sup>Should<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="the weak brother, for whose sake Christ died, perish because of yoʋr knowledge? 90% ¦ who is weak perishes by yoʋr knowledge, the brother for whose sake Christ died. CT 1.4%">g</a></sup>the weak brother, for whose sake Christ died, perish because of yoʋr knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:45; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12"> When you sin in this way against your brothers and wound their weak conscience, you sin against Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:29"> Therefore, if food causes my brother to stumble, I will certainly never eat meat, so that I will not cause my brother to stumble.</span>
