## The Way of Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:22"> If I speak in the tongues of men and of angels, but do not have love, I am a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:20–21"> If I have the gift of prophecy and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith, so as to move mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:14–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:5"> If I give away all my possessions to feed others, and if I deliver up my body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="to be burned 𝔐181 45.4% • 𝔐178 ANT PCK SBL TH 49.7% ¦ in order to boast NA WH 1.5%">a</a></sup>to be burned, but do not have love, I receive no benefit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:21–22"> Love is patient and kind. Love does not envy or boast. It is not puffed up</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:16–17"> or rude. It does not seek its own way, it is not irritable, and it keeps no record of wrongs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:9; THE THIRD LETTER OF JOHN 1:3–4; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:4"> It does not rejoice over unrighteousness, but rejoices with the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:24"> It bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, and endures all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:6"> Love never fails. But as for prophecies, they will come to an end; as for tongues, they will cease; as for knowledge, it will come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:2–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:18–19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Now ¦ For CT TR">b</a></sup>Now we know in part, and we prophesy in part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:12; Isaiah 60:19–20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:7–8"> Yet when the perfect comes, the partial will come to an end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:1"> When I was a child, I spoke like a child, I thought like a child, and I reasoned like a child. But when I became a man, I set aside childish ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:9–10"> For now we see in a mirror by means of an obscure image, but then we will see face to face. Now I know in part, but then I will know fully, just as I have been fully known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:7–18"> And now faith, hope, and love abide, these three; but the greatest of these is love.</span>
