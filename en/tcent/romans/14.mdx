## Do Not Judge Yoʋr Brother

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:7–13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:22"> Receive anyone who is weak in faith, but not for the purpose of disputing about opinions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:22–23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:14"> One person believes that he may eat anything, but he who is weak eats only vegetables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:16–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 18:9"> The one who eats must not despise the one who does not eat, and the one who does not eat must not judge the one who eats, for God has received him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:31–39"> Who are yoʋ to judge the servant of another? To his own master he stands or falls. And he will be made to stand, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="God ¦ the Lord CT">a</a></sup>God is able to make him stand.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:16–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:9–10"> One person considers one day to be more important than another, while another person considers every day to be alike. Each one should be fully convinced in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:30–31; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3–5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 14:19"> He who observes a particular day, observes it in honor of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lord, while he who abstains from observing a particular day, abstains in honor of the Lord. 92.8% ¦ Lord. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:19–20"> For none of us lives for himself, and none of us dies for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:23"> If we live, we live for the Lord; and if we die, we die for the Lord. Therefore, whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:26; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:2"> For this reason Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="died and then rose ¦ died CT">c</a></sup>died and then rose and lived again, so that he might be Lord over both the dead and the living.</span>
</div>

## Do Not Cause Yoʋr Brother to Stumble

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:3; THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:9–13"> Therefore let us no longer judge one another. Instead, resolve not to put any stumbling block or snare before your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:4"> I know and am convinced in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in and of itself, but it is unclean for anyone who considers it to be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:2–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:13"> If yoʋr brother is distressed by what yoʋ eat, yoʋ are no longer walking in love. Do not let what yoʋ eat destroy someone for whom Christ died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:29–30"> And do not let what you consider good to be spoken of as evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:33; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:20"> For the kingdom of God is not a matter of eating and drinking, but of righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:20"> Anyone who serves Christ in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="these things ¦ this way CT">f</a></sup>these things is acceptable to God and approved by men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> So then, let us pursue what brings about peace and mutual edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:14–15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:15"> Do not tear down the work of God for the sake of food. Everything is indeed clean, but it is wrong for a person to eat anything that causes another to stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1–2"> It is good not to eat meat or drink wine or do anything by which yoʋr brother<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="stumbles or is offended or made weak 86.5% ¦ is offended or stumbles or is made weak MSS 8.2% ¦ stumbles NA WH 2.2%">g</a></sup>stumbles or is offended or made weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:5; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Do yoʋ have a firm belief about these things? Have ¦ Any firm belief that yoʋ have about these things, have CT">h</a></sup>Do yoʋ have a firm belief about these things? Have it privately before God. Blessed is he who does not condemn himself by what he approves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:29–31"> But whoever doubts is condemned if he eats, because his action is not based on faith; and anything that is not based on faith is sin.</span>
