## Be Subject to the Governing Authorities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Every person must be subject to the governing authorities. For there is no authority except from God, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the authorities ¦ those CT">a</a></sup>the authorities that exist have been appointed by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:5"> So then, whoever resists authority opposes what God has instituted, and those who do so will bring judgment on themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For rulers are not a terror to good<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="works, but to evil works 94.3% ¦ conduct, but to evil conduct CT 3.7%">b</a></sup>works, but to evil works. Do yoʋ wish to have no fear of the one in authority? Do what is good, and yoʋ will have praise from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> For he is God's servant for your good. But if yoʋ do evil, be afraid, for he does not bear the sword in vain. As God's servant, he is an avenger who executes wrath on the one who does evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:19"> Therefore it is necessary to be subject to authority, not only because of wrath, but also because of conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 22:17–21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 12:14–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 17:24–27"> That is also why you pay taxes, for the authorities are servants of God, attending continually to this work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:17–18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 20:25"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Therefore render ¦ Render CT">c</a></sup>Therefore render to all their due: taxes to whom taxes are due, revenue to whom revenue is due, fear to whom fear is due, and honor to whom honor is due.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Love One Another

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 22:39–40; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:14"> Owe nothing to anyone, except to love one another, for he who loves others has fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 22:39; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:27"> For the commandments, “Yoʋ shall not commit adultery, yoʋ shall not murder, yoʋ shall not steal,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="yoʋ shall not give false testimony, ¦ — ANT CT HF RP">d</a></sup>yoʋ shall not give false testimony, yoʋ shall not covet,” and whatever other commandments there may be, are summed up in this one command: “Yoʋ shall love yoʋr neighbor as yoʋrself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 22:39–40; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:4–7"> Love does no harm to its neighbor; therefore love is the fulfillment of the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:5–8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:14"> And do this, knowing that the hour has now come for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="us ¦ you CT">e</a></sup>us to be awakened from sleep. For our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:7"> The night is nearly over, and the day is almost here. So let us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="lay 98.2% ¦ cast SBL 0.7%">f</a></sup>lay aside the works of darkness and put on the armor of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:3–5"> Let us walk properly, as in the day, not in revelries and drinking bouts, not in illicit affairs and sensual indulgences, not in strife and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:16–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:27"> But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, to gratify its desires.</span>
</div>
