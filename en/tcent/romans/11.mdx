## God Has Not Rejected Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:5"> I ask then, has God rejected his people? Certainly not! For I too am an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 94:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God has not rejected his people whom he foreknew. Do you not know what the Scripture says in the passage about Elijah, how he pleads with God against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Israel, saying, ¦ Israel: CT">a</a></sup>Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed yoʋr<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="prophets and ¦ prophets, they have CT">b</a></sup>prophets and demolished yoʋr altars, and I alone am left, and they are seeking my life”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> But what does the divine response say to him? “I have reserved for myself seven thousand men who have not bowed down to Baal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:11"> So then, in the present time also there is a remnant chosen by grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:4–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9"> Now if it is by grace, it is no longer by works, otherwise grace would no longer be grace.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="But if it is by works, it is no longer grace, otherwise work would no longer be work. 86.2% ¦ — CT 3.5%">c</a></sup>But if it is by works, it is no longer grace, otherwise work would no longer be work.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:4"> What then? Israel did not obtain what it was seeking. The chosen obtained it, but the rest were hardened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God gave them a spirit of stupor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">eyes not to see</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ears not to hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">down to this very day.”</span></span>

## The Salvation of the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:46–48; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:31; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:19"> I ask then, did the Israelites stumble so as to fall? Certainly not! Rather, by their trespass salvation has come to the Gentiles, to provoke Israel to jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:15"> Now if their trespass means riches for the world, and their loss means riches for the Gentiles, how much more will it mean when their full number is included?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:8"> Now I am speaking to you who are Gentiles. Inasmuch as I am an apostle to the Gentiles, I glorify my ministry</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:3"> if somehow I provoke my own people to jealousy and save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:10–11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:20–21"> For if their rejection means reconciliation for the world, what will their acceptance mean but life from the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> If the dough offered as firstfruits is holy, so is the whole lump; and if the root is holy, so are the branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:11–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:43"> Now if some of the branches have been broken off, and yoʋ, a wild olive branch, have been grafted in among them and have become a fellow partaker of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="root and richness ¦ rich root CT">d</a></sup>root and richness of the olive tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:19–20"> do not boast against the branches. But if yoʋ do boast against them, remember that yoʋ do not sustain the root, but the root sustains yoʋ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:23–24"> Yoʋ will say then, “Branches were broken off so that I could be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:12"> Right! They were broken off because of unbelief, but yoʋ stand because of faith. So do not be arrogant, but be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> For if God did not spare the natural branches,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="perhaps he will not spare yoʋ either ¦ neither will he spare yoʋ SBL TH WH">e</a></sup>perhaps he will not spare yoʋ either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:2"> Consider therefore the kindness and severity of God: to those who have fallen, severity; but to yoʋ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="kindness ¦ God's kindness CT">f</a></sup>kindness, if yoʋ continue in his kindness; otherwise yoʋ too will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:39"> And if they do not continue in unbelief, they will be grafted in; for God is able to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:30"> For if yoʋ were cut off from what is by nature a wild olive tree and were grafted contrary to nature into a cultivated olive tree, how much more will the natural branches be grafted back into their own olive tree?</span>
</div>
</div>

## God's Mercy for All

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:14–16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:25"> I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of this mystery, lest you be wise in your own estimation: A partial hardening has come upon Israel until the full number of Gentiles has come in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalms 14:7"> and in this way all Israel will be saved, as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Deliverer will come from Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will remove ungodliness from Jacob.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And this will be my covenant with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I take away their sins.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:43"> With respect to the gospel, the Israelites are enemies for your sake; but with respect to being chosen, they are beloved for the sake of the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> For the gifts and calling of God are irrevocable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:1–2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:25"> Just as you were once disobedient to God but have now received mercy because of their disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:25"> so they too have now become disobedient in order that, by the mercy shown to you, they also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="may ¦ may now NA SBL WH">g</a></sup>may receive mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:4–6"> For God has confined all in disobedience so that he might have mercy on all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalms 92:5"> Oh the depth of the riches, wisdom, and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his judgments and untraceable his ways!</span>
