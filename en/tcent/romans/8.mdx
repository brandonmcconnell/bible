<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:5–6; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:9"> That is why those who are in the flesh cannot please God.</span>

Father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:5"> The Spirit himself testifies with our spirit that we are children of God.</span>

## Future Glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:17–18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:5–7"> I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing to the glory that will be revealed to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:2"> The creation waits with eager longing for the sons of God to be revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–19; Genesis 5:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:22"> For the creation was subjected to futility, not willingly, but because of him who subjected it, in hope</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Jeremiah 12:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:21"> For we know that all creation has been groaning together with labor pains until now.</span>
