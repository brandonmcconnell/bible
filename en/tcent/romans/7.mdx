## Released from the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:3"> Do you not know, brothers (for I am speaking to those who know the law), that the law has dominion over a person only as long as he lives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 2:23–24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:39; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:4"> For a married woman is bound by the law to her husband as long as he is alive, but if her husband dies, she is released from the law concerning her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:32; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 10:6–12"> So then, she will be called an adulteress if she is joined to another man while her husband is alive. But if her husband dies, she is free from that law and is not committing adultery if she is joined to another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:22–23; Isaiah 54:5"> So then, my brothers, you also were put to death with respect to the law through the body of Christ, so that you might be joined to another, to him who was raised from the dead, so that we might bear fruit for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:16–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:24"> For when we were in the flesh, our sinful passions, which were aroused by the law, were at work in our members to bear fruit leading to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:23–25; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:4"> But now we have been released from the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="law, having died to that by which we were held ¦ law since that by which we were held has died SCR">a</a></sup>law, having died to that by which we were held, so that we may serve in the new way of the Spirit and not in the old way of the written code.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Law and Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:56"> What then shall we say? Is the law sin? Certainly not! Yet I would not have known sin except through the law. For I would not have known covetousness if the law had not said, “Yoʋ shall not covet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:56; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:15"> But sin, seizing an opportunity through the commandment, produced in me every kind of covetousness. For apart from the law sin is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:7; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:10–11"> Once I was alive apart from the law, but when the commandment came, sin came to life and I died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:5; Leviticus 18:5"> So I found that the very commandment that was intended to bring life actually brought death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 3:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:8; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:13"> For sin, seizing an opportunity through the commandment, deceived me and through it killed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:8; Psalms 119:137; Psalms 19:7–12"> So then, the law is holy, and the commandment is holy, righteous, and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:13–15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:3"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Has … brought ¦ Did … bring CT">b</a></sup>Has that then which is good brought death to me? Certainly not! But sin, so that it would be shown to be sin, produced death in me through that which is good, so that through the commandment sin might become utterly sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 17:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:18"> For we know that the law is spiritual, but I am of the flesh, sold as a slave to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:19–20; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> I do not understand what I do. Instead of doing what I want to do, I do the very thing I hate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:8"> Now if I do the very thing I do not want to do, I agree that the law is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:8–9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:18"> But now it is no longer I who do it, but sin that dwells in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:6; Psalms 51:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:17"> For I know that nothing good dwells in me, that is, in my flesh. I have the desire to do what is right, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="I find that the ability to carry it out is lacking 91.8% ¦ not the ability to carry it out CT 2.3%">c</a></sup>I find that the ability to carry it out is lacking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:15"> For I do not do the good that I want to do, but I do the very evil that I do not want to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:17"> Now if I do the very thing I do not want to do, it is no longer I who do it, but sin that dwells in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:23; Psalms 119:133"> I find then the law that when I want to do what is right, evil is present with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 1:2; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 119:35"> For I delight in the law of God in my inner being,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:17; THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:2"> but I see another law at work in my members, warring against the law of my mind and making me a prisoner to the law of sin that is in my members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:14"> Wretched man that I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:17–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="I thank God that ¦ Thanks be to God, who NA SBL WH">d</a></sup>I thank God that he will do so through Jesus Christ our Lord! So then, with my mind I serve the law of God, but with my flesh I serve the law of sin.</span>
</div>
