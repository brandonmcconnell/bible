</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness demonstrates the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is God, who inflicts wrath, unrighteous? (I speak in a human way.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:31; Job 8:3"> Certainly not! Otherwise, how could God judge the world?</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:20"> And why not say (just as some slanderously claim that we say), “Let us do evil that good may come”? Their condemnation is just.</span>

## No One is Righteous

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? Are we Jews any better off? Not at all! For we have previously charged that both Jews and Greeks are all under sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 53:1–3; Psalms 14:1–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:23"> just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There is none righteous, not even one;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 14:2–4; Psalms 53:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">there is none who understands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is none who seeks after God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All have turned aside; together they have become useless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is none who shows kindness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">not even one.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:9; Psalms 140:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their throat is an open grave;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with their tongues they deceive.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The venom of asps is under their lips.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 10:7; Psalms 109:17–18; Psalms 59:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Their feet are swift to shed blood.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ruin and misery are in their paths,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:79"> <span className="pl pl-1">and the way of peace they have not known.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“There is no fear of God before their eyes.”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:22–23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever the law says, it says to those who are under the law, so that every mouth may be stopped and all the world may be brought under the judgment of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:16; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:39; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:28"> For by the works of the law no flesh will be justified in his sight, for through the law comes knowledge of sin.</span>

## Justification by Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:9"> But now the righteousness of God has been revealed apart from the law, attested by the Law and the Prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:1"> that is, the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ for all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="and upon all 92.6% ¦ — CT 6.6%">b</a></sup>and upon all who believe; for there is no distinction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 1:8–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:9"> For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:18–19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:5–7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:14"> being freely justified by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:23–24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:18–20; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:10"> whom God put forward as a sacrifice of atonement, through faith, by his blood. He did this to demonstrate his righteousness, because in his divine forbearance he had passed over the sins that had previously been committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:30"> He did so to prove in the present time that he is righteous and that he justifies the one who has faith in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:29–31"> Where then is boasting? It is excluded. By what kind of law? By a law of works? No, but by the law of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="We conclude therefore ¦ For we conclude CT">c</a></sup>We conclude therefore that a person is justified by faith apart from the works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Or is God the God of the Jews only? Is he not the God of the Gentiles also? Yes, of the Gentiles also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> since God is one, who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:8–10"> Do we then nullify the law through faith? Certainly not! On the contrary, we uphold the law.</span>
</div>
