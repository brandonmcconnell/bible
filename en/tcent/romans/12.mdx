## Living Sacrifices to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:15–16"> I urge you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as living sacrifices, holy and acceptable to God, your reasonable religious service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22–24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:17"> Do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may determine what is the good, acceptable, and perfect will of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For by the grace given to me I tell everyone among you not to think more highly of himself than he ought to think, but to think sensibly, in accordance with the measure of faith that God has distributed to each one of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:27; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12–14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:15–16"> For just as in one body we have many members, and all the members do not have the same function,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:27–28"> so we, who are many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10–11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:28–31"> We have different gifts according to the grace given to us. If someone's gift is prophecy, he should prophesy in proportion to his faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:1"> if it is service, he should serve; if it is teaching, he should teach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:28"> if it is exhortation, he should exhort; if it is giving, he should do so generously; if it is leadership, he should do so diligently; if it is showing mercy, he should do so cheerfully.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:5"> Love must be without hypocrisy. Abhor what is evil; cling to what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:34–35; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:1"> Be devoted to one another in brotherly love. Take the lead in honoring one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:10–11; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:25; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:15–16"> Do not lack diligence in zeal, but be fervent in spirit, serving the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lord ¦ time ST">a</a></sup>Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:36; Psalms 62:8"> Rejoice in hope, persevere in tribulation, and persist in prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:17"> Contribute to the needs of the saints, and pursue hospitality.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:44; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:15"> Bless those who persecute<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="you ¦ — SBL WH">b</a></sup>you; bless, and do not curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:26; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> Rejoice with those who rejoice, and weep with those who weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:13–17"> Be of the same mind toward one another. Do not be haughty, but associate with the lowly. Do not be wise in your own estimation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:39"> Repay no one evil for evil, but have regard for what is right in the sight of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:19"> If possible, as far as it depends on you, be at peace with everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:30; Proverbs 24:29"> Do not avenge yourselves, beloved, but leave room for the wrath of God, for it is written, “Vengeance is mine; I will repay, says the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lord.” Therefore, “If 86.9% ¦ Lord. If MSS 6% ¦ Lord.” On the contrary, “If CT 6%">c</a></sup>Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:44; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:27"> Therefore, “If yoʋr enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink; for by doing so, yoʋ will heap coals of fire on his head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.</span>
</div>
</div>
