## Salvation for All Who Believe

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> Moses writes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="about the righteousness that is by the law: “The person who does these things will live by them.” ¦ that the person who practices the righteousness that is by the law will live by it. SBL WH ¦ about the righteousness that is by the law: “The person who does these things will live by it.” TH">c</a></sup>about the righteousness that is by the law: “The person who does these things will live by them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:8–10"> But the righteousness that is by faith speaks like this: “Do not say in yoʋr heart, ‘Who will go up to heaven?’ ” (that is, to bring Christ down)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:20; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:25"> “or, ‘Who will go down into the abyss?’ ” (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:16–17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:6"> But what does it say? “The word is near yoʋ, in yoʋr mouth and in yoʋr heart” (that is, the word of faith that we preach),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 12:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:31; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:11"> because if yoʋ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="confess ¦ confess the word WH">d</a></sup>confess with yoʋr mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe with yoʋr heart that God raised him from the dead, yoʋ will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:16"> For with the heart belief is exercised, resulting in righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made, resulting in salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:33; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:6"> As the Scripture says, “No one who believes in him will be put to shame.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:19; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 17:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:28"> For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek; the same Lord is Lord of all and richly blesses all who call upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For “everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:15–16; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:3"> How then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="will ¦ can CT">e</a></sup>will they call upon him in whom they have not believed? And how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="will ¦ can CT">f</a></sup>will they believe in him whom they have not heard? And how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="will ¦ can CT">g</a></sup>will they hear without someone preaching?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:17"> And how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="will ¦ can CT">h</a></sup>will they preach unless they are sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="who preach the gospel of peace, 89.3% ¦ — CT 10.4%">i</a></sup>who preach the gospel of peace, who preach good news of good things!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:38–40"> But not all the Israelites have obeyed the gospel. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our report?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:13"> So then, faith comes by hearing, and hearing comes through the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="God ¦ Christ CT">j</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:14"> But I ask, have they not heard? On the contrary,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The message has gone out into all the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the words to the ends of the world.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:14"> Again I ask, did Israel not understand? First Moses says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will provoke you to jealousy by that which is not a nation;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by a nation that has no understanding I will provoke you to anger.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> And Isaiah is even bold enough to say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I was found by those who were not seeking me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I became known to those who were not asking for me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But to Israel he says, “All day long I have held out my hands to a disobedient and contrary people.”</span>
