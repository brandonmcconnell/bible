## Paul's Ministry to the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:21"> I myself am convinced about you, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to admonish<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="others ¦ one another ANT CT TR">f</a></sup>others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:5"> But I have written to you rather boldly in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="part, brothers, 91.6% ¦ part CT 2.9%">g</a></sup>part, brothers, by way of reminder, because of the grace that was given to me by God</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:14–16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:11–21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:1"> Therefore I have a reason for boasting in Christ Jesus in my service to God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:13–16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:9–15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20–22"> And in this way I aspire to preach the gospel where the name of Christ has not been heard, so that I do not build upon another's foundation,</span>
