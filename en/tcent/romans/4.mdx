## The Example of Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:22"> What then shall we say<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="that Abraham our father has discovered according to the flesh ¦ that Abraham, our forefather according to the flesh, has discovered NA SBL TH ¦ about Abraham, our forefather according to the flesh WH">a</a></sup>that Abraham our father has discovered according to the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:31; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:9"> If Abraham was justified by works, he has something to boast about, but not before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:6–8"> For what does the Scripture say? “Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:35"> Now to the one who works, his wages are not counted as a gift, but as something owed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3–7"> However, to the one who does not work but believes in him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is counted as righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:30"> just as David also speaks of the blessedness of the one to whom God counts righteousness apart from works:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 32:1–2; Psalms 130:3–4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 7:47–50"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed are those whose lawless deeds are forgiven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and whose sins are covered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 32:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the man against whom the Lord will never count sin.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:26–28"> Is this blessedness then only for the circumcised, or is it also for the uncircumcised? For we say, “Faith was counted to Abraham as righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:6; Genesis 17:10"> How then was it counted? When he was circumcised, or uncircumcised? Not when he was circumcised, but when he was uncircumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 19:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:22"> He received the sign of circumcision as a seal of the righteousness that he had by faith while he was still uncircumcised, so that he might be the father of all who believe when they are uncircumcised, so that righteousness might be counted to them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="as well ¦ — SBL TH WH">b</a></sup>as well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:39–40; Proverbs 2:20"> and so that he might be the father of those who are not only circumcised in the flesh, but who also walk in the footsteps of the faith that our father Abraham had when he was uncircumcised.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Promise Granted Through Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:29; Genesis 22:17–18; Genesis 28:14"> For the promise to Abraham, or to his offspring, that he would be the heir of the world was not through the law, but through the righteousness of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:18–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:16"> For if those who are of the law are heirs, faith has been made void and the promise has been invalidated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:19–20"> because the law brings wrath;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="for ¦ but CT">c</a></sup>for where there is no law, there is no transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:24–26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:22"> For this reason the promise comes by faith, so that it may be according to grace and be guaranteed to all Abraham's offspring, not only to the one who is of the law, but also to the one who is of the faith of Abraham, who is the father of us all</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:28; Genesis 17:4–5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 5:21"> (just as it is written, “I have made yoʋ the father of many nations”). He is our father in the presence of the God in whom he believed, who gives life to the dead and calls into being what does not yet exist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:24; Genesis 15:5–6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 27:25"> Against all hope Abraham believed in hope that he would become the father of many nations according to what had been spoken: “So shall yoʋr offspring be.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:11–19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:20–21; Genesis 17:17"> Because he was not weak in faith, he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="did not consider … or ¦ considered … and CT">d</a></sup>did not consider his own body,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="now ¦ — SBL">e</a></sup>now as good as dead (since he was somewhere around a hundred years old), or the deadness of Sarah's womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:45; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:13; Daniel 10:19"> And he did not waver in unbelief at the promise of God but was strengthened in faith, giving glory to God</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:37; Jeremiah 32:27; Genesis 18:14"> and being fully assured that God was able to do what he had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:3"> Therefore, “it was counted to him as righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:6"> Now the statement “it was counted to him” was not written only for Abraham's sake,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:21; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:30"> but for ours also. It will be counted to us who believe in him who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:9–10; Isaiah 53:5–6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:21"> who was delivered up for our trespasses and raised for our justification.</span>
</div>
