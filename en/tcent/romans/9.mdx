## God's Choice of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:20"> I am speaking the truth in Christ. My conscience confirms through the Holy Spirit that I am not lying when I say</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:18; Psalms 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> that I have great sorrow and unceasing pain in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:14"> For I could wish that I myself were accursed, cut off from Christ for the sake of my brothers, my kinsmen according to the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:12; Psalms 147:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:6"> They are Israelites, and to them belong the adoption as sons, the glory, the covenants, the giving of the law, the temple service, and the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 10:30; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:1–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:16–19"> To them belong the patriarchs, and from them, according to the flesh, came the Christ, who is God over all, blessed forever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:28–29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:1–2"> It is not as though the word of God has failed. For not all Israelites truly belong to Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:37–39"> and not all of Abraham's children are his true descendants, but, “Through Isaac yoʋr descendants will be counted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:11–16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:26–29"> That is, it is not the children of the flesh who are the children of God; rather, the children of the promise are counted as descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For this is what God promised: “About this time next year I will come, and Sarah will have a son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 16:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:11"> Not only that, but when Rebecca had conceived by one man, our father Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:4–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:10"> though her sons were not yet born and had done nothing good or evil, in order for the purpose of God's choice to stand (not because of works but because of him who calls),</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> she was told, “The older will serve the younger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> As it is written, “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalms 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What then shall we say? Is there injustice with God? Certainly not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For he says to Moses, “I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:25–26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9–10"> So then, it does not depend on human will or effort, but on God's mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the Scripture says to Pharaoh, “I have raised yoʋ up for the very purpose of showing my power in yoʋ, so that my name might be declared in all the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:15–16"> So then, God has mercy on whom he wants to have mercy, and he hardens whom he wants to harden.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:21"> What if God, although willing to show his wrath and to make his power known, endured with much patience vessels of wrath fitted for destruction,</span>
