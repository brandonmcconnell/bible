## The Righteous Judgment of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:37; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:7–9"> Therefore yoʋ are without excuse, O man, every one of you who judges. For on whatever grounds yoʋ judge another, yoʋ condemn yoʋrself, for yoʋ who judge do the same things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 9:7–8; Zephaniah 3:5; Psalms 145:17"> Now we know that the judgment of God against those who do such things is in accordance with truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 16:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 12:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:32–1"> Do yoʋ suppose, O man—yoʋ who judge those who do such things and yet do them yoʋrself—that yoʋ will escape the judgment of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:9; Isaiah 30:18; Exodus 34:6"> Or do yoʋ despise the riches of his kindness, forbearance, and patience, not knowing that the kindness of God leads yoʋ to repentance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:2–3"> But according to yoʋr stubbornness and yoʋr unrepentant heart, yoʋ are storing up wrath for yoʋrself on the day of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="wrath, revelation, and ¦ wrath and the revelation of CT TR">a</a></sup>wrath, revelation, and the righteous judgment of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 62:12; Jeremiah 32:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:27"> who will repay each person according to his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:7–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 8:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:10"> To those who by patient endurance in good works seek glory, honor, and immortality, he will give eternal life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:18–21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:3–4"> but for those who are self-seeking and obey unrighteousness instead of the truth, there will be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="anger and wrath ¦ wrath and anger CT">b</a></sup>anger and wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:17; Ezekiel 18:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:16"> There will be tribulation and distress for everyone who does evil, the Jew first and also the Greek;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 32:17; Isaiah 26:12; Numbers 6:26"> but there will be glory, honor, and peace for everyone who does good, the Jew first and also the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:34; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> For there is no partiality with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:16–19"> For all who have sinned without the law will also perish without the law, and all who have sinned under the law will be judged by the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:22–25; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:7; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:21–25"> For it is not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the law will be justified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:19–20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:27"> For when Gentiles, who do not have the law, by nature do what is required by the law, they are a law to themselves, even though they do not have the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:18–19"> They show that what the law requires is written in their hearts, as their conscience bears witness with them and their conflicting thoughts accuse or even defend them</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:5; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:31"> on the day when, according to my gospel, God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="will judge ¦ judges NA SBL WH">c</a></sup>will judge the secret thoughts of men through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus NA SBL WH">d</a></sup>Jesus Christ.</span>

## The Jews and the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:23; Micah 3:11; Isaiah 48:1–2"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Behold, ¦ But if CT">e</a></sup>Behold, yoʋ call yoʋrself a Jew and rely on the law and boast in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="God. Yoʋ ¦ God; if yoʋ CT">f</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:21; Deuteronomy 4:8; THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:17"> Yoʋ know his will and approve what is excellent, because yoʋ are instructed from the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="law. And ¦ law; if CT">g</a></sup>law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:17–18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:14; Isaiah 49:6"> And yoʋ have confidence that yoʋ yoʋrself are a guide for the blind, a light for those who are in darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13"> an instructor of the foolish, and a teacher of children, because yoʋ have in the law the embodiment of knowledge and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="truth. Yoʋ ¦ truth—yoʋ CT">h</a></sup>truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:3–28; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 12:47"> Yoʋ then who teach others, do yoʋ not teach yoʋrself? Yoʋ who preach against stealing, do yoʋ steal?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Malachi 3:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:39; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:17"> Yoʋ who tell others not to commit adultery, do yoʋ commit adultery? Yoʋ who abhor idols, do yoʋ rob temples?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:16–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:2"> Yoʋ who boast in the law, do yoʋ dishonor God by transgressing the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:20–23; Isaiah 52:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:2"> As it is written, “The name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:3–6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 7:51"> Circumcision does indeed benefit yoʋ if yoʋ observe the law, but if yoʋ transgress the law, yoʋr circumcision has become uncircumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:18–19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:4"> If then an uncircumcised man keeps the ordinances of the law, will not his uncircumcision be regarded as circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:41–42; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:29; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:6"> Then he who is physically uncircumcised and carries out the law will judge yoʋ who, despite having the letter of the law and circumcision, transgress the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 3:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:6–8"> For he is not a Jew who is one outwardly, neither is circumcision something outward in the flesh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3; Deuteronomy 10:16"> but he is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the letter. Such a man's praise comes not from men but from God.</span>
