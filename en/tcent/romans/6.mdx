## Dead to Sin, Alive in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:15"> What then shall we say? Should we continue in sin so that grace may abound?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:4"> Certainly not! How can we who have died to sin still live in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:8"> Or do you not know that all of us who have been baptized into Christ Jesus have been baptized into his death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22–24"> Therefore we were buried with him through baptism into death, so that just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, we too might walk in newness of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:10–11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:12"> For if we have become united with him in the likeness of his death, we will certainly also be united with him in the likeness of his resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:24"> For we know that our old self has been crucified with him so that the body of sin might be done away with, and we might no longer be enslaved to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:1–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:4"> For he who has died has been set free from sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:3–4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:10–14"> Now if we have died with Christ, we believe that we will also live with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:14; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:18; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:24–28"> For we know that Christ, having been raised from the dead, dies no more; death no longer has dominion over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:18"> For the death that he died, he died to sin once for all; but the life that he lives, he lives to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:3–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:2"> So you also must consider yourselves to be dead to sin but alive to God in Christ Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="our Lord 94.5% ¦ — CT 3.5%">a</a></sup>our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13"> Therefore do not let sin reign in your mortal bodies so that you obey<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="it in the body's 81.1% ¦ the body's CT 7.5% ¦ it in its MSS 6.1%">b</a></sup>it in the body's sinful desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:5"> And do not present your members to sin as instruments of unrighteousness, but present yourselves to God as alive from the dead, and present your members to God as instruments of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:4–11"> For sin will have no dominion over you, for you are not under law but under grace.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Slaves to Righteousness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:11–14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:1–2"> What then? Should we sin because we are not under law but under grace? Certainly not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:24; Joshua 24:15"> Do you not know that when you present yourselves to someone as obedient slaves, you are slaves of the one whom you obey, either of sin leading to death, or of obedience leading to righteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8"> But thanks be to God that, though you were slaves of sin, you have become obedient from the heart to the pattern of teaching to which you were entrusted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:1"> and having been set free from sin, you have become slaves to righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:2–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:5–7"> (I am speaking in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh.) For just as you presented your members as slaves to impurity and to lawlessness leading to more lawlessness, so now present your members as slaves to righteousness leading to sanctification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:16–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:34"> For when you were slaves of sin, you were free with regard to righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:23"> So what fruit did you have at that time from the things of which you are now ashamed? The end result of those things is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:2"> But now that you have been set free from sin and become slaves of God, you have fruit that leads to sanctification, and the end result is eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:36; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:15"> For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
