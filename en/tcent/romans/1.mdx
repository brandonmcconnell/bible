## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:1"> Paul, a servant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus NA SBL">a</a></sup>Jesus Christ, called to be an apostle, set apart for the gospel of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:70; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:21"> which he promised beforehand through his prophets in the holy Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 1:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:4; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> concerning his Son, who was descended from David according to the flesh</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 5:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:19–23"> and who was declared to be the Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness by the resurrection from the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:26; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 6:7"> through whom we have received grace and apostleship to bring about the obedience of faith for the sake of his name among all the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 17:14; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:10"> including you who are also called to belong to Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:2; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:2"> to all who are in Rome, beloved by God and called to be saints: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Desire to Visit Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:8–9"> First, I give thanks to my God through Jesus Christ for you all, because your faith is proclaimed in all the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:16–19"> For God is my witness, whom I serve with my spirit in the gospel of his Son, that I continually remember you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:30–32; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 18:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:22"> always pleading in my prayers that somehow by God's will I may now at last succeed in coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:29"> For I long to see you so that I may impart to you some spiritual gift, that you may be established,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:7–10; THE THIRD LETTER OF JOHN 1:3–4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:13"> that is, that while I am among you we may be mutually encouraged by each other's faith, both yours and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 19:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:17"> I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that I have often intended to come to you (but have been hindered until the present time) so that I might have some fruit among you as well, just as I have had among the other Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:16–23; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 9:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11"> I am under obligation both to Greeks and to barbarians, both to the wise and to the foolish;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:20; Isaiah 6:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:17"> thus the eagerness on my part to preach the gospel to you also who are in Rome.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Power of the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:38; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:16"> For I am not ashamed of the gospel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of Christ ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>of Christ, because it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, both to the Jew first and also to the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:38"> For in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith, just as it is written, “The righteous will live by faith.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## God's Wrath Against Unrighteousness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:32"> For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who suppress the truth by their unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:16–17; Psalms 19:1–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:20"> Because what is known about God is evident among them, for God has made it evident to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:19; Isaiah 40:26; Psalms 19:1–6"> For his invisible qualities, both his everlasting power and his divinity, have been plainly seen from the creation of the world, being perceived through what has been made, so that they are without excuse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:17–18; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:5"> For although they knew God, they did not glorify him as God or give thanks, but they became futile in their thoughts, and their senseless hearts were darkened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:19–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:25; Proverbs 26:12"> Claiming to be wise, they became fools</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:29"> and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="exchanged ¦ exchanged for themselves PCK">c</a></sup>exchanged the glory of the immortal God for images resembling mortal man, birds, four-footed animals, and reptiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 7:42; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:16"> Therefore God gave them up in the lusts of their hearts to impurity, to dishonor their bodies among themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 13:25; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:15–16"> because they exchanged the truth of God for falsehood and worshiped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is blessed forever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:24"> For this reason God gave them up to dishonorable passions, for their women exchanged the natural function for that which is contrary to nature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Leviticus 18:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:23–24"> In the same way the men also, having abandoned the natural function of the female, were inflamed in their lust for one another, men committing shameful acts with men and receiving in themselves the due penalty for their error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:10–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:16"> And just as they considered it worthless to have God in their knowledge, God gave them up to a worthless mind, to do what is not proper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:20; Proverbs 16:28; Psalms 41:7"> They are filled with all unrighteousness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="fornication, ¦ — CT">d</a></sup>fornication, evil, covetousness, and malice. They are full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, and maliciousness. They are gossips,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:2; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:18"> slanderers, haters of God, insolent, proud, boasters, inventors of evil, disobedient to parents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:3; Proverbs 18:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:11"> senseless, untrustworthy, without natural affection,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="irreconcilable, ¦ — CT">e</a></sup>irreconcilable, and unmerciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:1–5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:21"> Though they know the ordinance of God, that those who practice such things deserve death, they not only do them but also approve of those who practice them.</span>
</div>
</div>
