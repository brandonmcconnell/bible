<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2–3; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:14"> Not only that, but we also boast in our tribulations, knowing that tribulation produces endurance,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:4"> Rarely will anyone die for a righteous person, though perhaps for a good person someone might even dare to die.</span>

## Death Through Adam, Life Through Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:21–22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:23"> Therefore, just as sin came into the world through one man, and death came through sin, in this way death has come to all men, because all have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:56; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:4"> For until the law sin was in the world, but sin is not charged to anyone's account when there is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:45; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:17; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:27"> Nevertheless, death reigned from Adam until Moses, even over those whose sins were not like the transgression of Adam, who was a type of the one to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12"> But the gift is not like the trespass. For if many died through the trespass of the one man, how much more have the grace of God and the gift by the grace of the one man Jesus Christ abounded to many!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:38–39; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 44:22"> And the gift cannot be compared to the result of the one man's sin; for the judgment that arose from one trespass brought condemnation, but the gift that arose from many trespasses brought justification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:21–22; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:49; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12"> For if, by the trespass of the one man, death reigned through the one man, how much more will those who receive the abundance of grace and the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man Jesus Christ!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:15"> So then, just as one trespass led to condemnation for all men, so one act of righteousness leads to justification and life for all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:8"> For just as many were made sinners through the disobedience of one man, so also many will be made righteous through the obedience of one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3–7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:14"> Now the law came in so that the trespass might abound. But where sin abounded, grace abounded all the more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:10"> so that just as sin reigned in death, so also grace might reign through righteousness leading to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
</div>
