## Fullness of Life in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:1"> Therefore, just as you received Christ Jesus the Lord, continue to walk in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:17; Jeremiah 17:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20–22"> being rooted and built up in him and established in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="in it ¦ — NA SBL TH">b</a></sup>in it with thanksgiving.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:15"> Be careful that no one takes you captive through philosophy and empty deceit, according to the tradition of men and the elements of the world, and not according to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 10:30; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:14"> For all the fullness of God's nature dwells bodily in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:16–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:20–23"> and you have been filled by him, who is the head over every ruler and authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:6"> In him you were also circumcised with a circumcision not done by hands, by putting off the body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="of the sins 92.3% ¦ — CT 6.9%">c</a></sup>of the sins of the flesh in the circumcision of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:3–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:27"> Having been buried with him in baptism, you have also been raised with him through faith in the powerful working of God, who raised him from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:1"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="although you … flesh, God made you ¦ you who … flesh God made TR">d</a></sup>although you were dead in your trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, God made you alive together with Christ. He forgave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="us … our ¦ you … your ELZ SCR">e</a></sup>us all our trespasses</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 44:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:14–16; Isaiah 43:25"> by blotting out the certificate of debt that stood against us with its legal demands; he took it away by nailing it to the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:29"> He stripped the rulers and authorities of their power and made a public display of them, leading a triumphal procession over them by the cross.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:10"> Therefore let no one judge you in regard to food<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="or ¦ and NA WH">f</a></sup>or drink, or in the matter of a feast, a New Moon celebration, or a Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:17"> These are a shadow of the things to come, but the substance is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:23"> Let no one who delights in false humility and angelic religion disqualify you. Such a person takes his stand on things he has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="not 92.6% ¦ — CT 1.8%">g</a></sup>not seen, being puffed up without reason by the mind of his flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:15–16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:4–6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:18"> while not holding fast to the head, from whom the entire body, being supported and knit together by its joints and tendons, grows with a growth that is from God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:3"> If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="then ¦ — CT HF RP">h</a></sup>then you have died with Christ to the elements of the world, why, as though you were living in the world, do you submit to regulations such as,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:3; Genesis 3:3; Isaiah 52:11"> “Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:13"> Such regulations are based on the commandments and doctrines of men, and apply to things that will all decay with use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:8"> Although these regulations have a semblance of wisdom in self-imposed piety, false humility, and severe treatment of the body, they are of no value in restraining fleshly indulgence.</span>
</div>
