## Put On the New Self

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:33; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:18"> If then you have been raised with Christ, seek the things above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:15–17; 1 Chronicles 22:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:1"> Set your minds on the things above, not the things on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:20"> For you have died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 11:25; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20"> When Christ, who is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="our ¦ your NA SBL TH">a</a></sup>our life, is revealed, then you also will be revealed with him in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:19–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:24"> Put to death therefore whatever belongs to your earthly nature: fornication, impurity, lust, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:14"> On account of these things the wrath of God is coming<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="upon the sons of disobedience ¦ — WH">b</a></sup>upon the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:3–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:19–20"> You too once walked in these ways when you lived among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:20–21"> But now you must put them all aside: wrath, anger, malice, slander, and filthy language from your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:25; Leviticus 19:11"> Do not lie to one another, since you have put off the old self with its practices</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:10"> and have put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of its Creator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:28–29; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:12"> Here there is no distinction between Greek and Jew, circumcised and uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave and free, but Christ is all and in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:19"> Therefore, as God's chosen people, holy and beloved, put on tenderhearted mercy, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:32; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:25; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:13"> bearing with one another and forgiving one other if any one of you has a complaint against another; just as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Christ ¦ the Lord CT">c</a></sup>Christ forgave you, so you also must forgive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:1–13"> Above all, put on love, which is the bond of perfection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:6–7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:27; Isaiah 26:3"> Let the peace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="God 95.2% ¦ Christ CT 4.8%">d</a></sup>God rule in your hearts, since as members of one body you were called to peace. And be thankful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:7"> Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly, teaching and admonishing one another with all wisdom as you sing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the Lord ¦ God CT">e</a></sup>the Lord with gratitude in your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:31; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:23; Proverbs 3:6"> And whatever you do, in word or in deed, do it all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="our God and ¦ God the CT">f</a></sup>our God and Father through him.</span>
</div>

## Rules for Christian Households

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:22–9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:4–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:3"> Wives, submit to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:7; Genesis 2:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:21"> Husbands, love your wives, and do not be embittered against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:1–3; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in everything, for this pleases the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:1–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:4; Psalms 103:13"> Fathers, do not provoke your children, lest they become discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:5–7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:10"> Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything. Do not try to please them only when they are watching you, but obey with sincerity of heart, fearing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="God ¦ the Lord CT">g</a></sup>God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:6–7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:8"> Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord and not for men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:10; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:32"> knowing that you will receive an inheritance from the Lord as your reward;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="for ¦ — CT">h</a></sup>for you serve the Lord Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:34; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="But ¦ For CT">i</a></sup>But he who does wrong will be paid back the wrong that he has done, and there is no partiality.</span>
</div>
