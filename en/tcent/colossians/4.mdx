<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:18"> Devote yourselves to prayer, being watchful in it with thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:19–20; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:27; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:1–2"> At the same time pray for us as well, that God may open a door to us for the word so that we may speak the mystery of Christ, for which I am in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:1–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:6"> Pray that I may make it known in the way I ought to speak.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:29; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:15; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let your speech always be with grace, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how to answer each person.</span>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:21–23; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:12"> Tychicus will tell you all the news about me. He is our beloved brother, a faithful minister and fellow servant in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:28"> I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="he may know how you are doing and 85% ¦ you may know how we are doing and that he may NA SBL WH 9%">a</a></sup>he may know how you are doing and encourage your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:10–19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:7"> With him I have sent Onesimus, our faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will tell you everything that is happening here.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:11; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:29"> Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you, and so does Mark the cousin of Barnabas (about whom you have received instructions: if he comes to you, welcome him).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 11:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:1; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:45"> Jesus who is called Justus also greets you. These men are my only fellow workers for the kingdom of God who are of the circumcision, and they have been a comfort to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:7"> Epaphras, who is one of you and a servant of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Christ ¦ Christ Jesus NA TH WH">b</a></sup>Christ, greets you, always striving for you in his prayers, so that you may stand mature and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="complete ¦ fully assured CT">c</a></sup>complete in all the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:2; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:11"> For I testify about him that he has much<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="zeal ¦ toil CT">d</a></sup>zeal for you and for those in Laodicea and Hierapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:10–11"> Luke the beloved physician greets you, and so does Demas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:9"> Give my greetings to the brothers in Laodicea, and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Nymphas … his ¦ Nympha … her NA SBL WH ¦ Nympha … their TH">e</a></sup>Nymphas and the church that meets in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:27; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:14"> After this letter has been read among you, have it read in the church of the Laodiceans as well, and make sure you also read the letter from Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 1:17; Ezekiel 44:23–24"> Tell Archippus, “Be sure to complete the ministry yoʋ have received in the Lord.”</span>
</div>
