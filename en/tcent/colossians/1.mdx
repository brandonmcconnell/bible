## The Preeminence of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:9–11; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:5"> For this reason, from the day we heard this, we have not ceased praying for you and asking that you may be filled with the knowledge of God's will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:2–3"> so that you may walk in a manner worthy of the Lord with every desire to please him: bearing fruit in every good work and increasing in the knowledge of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:2"> being strengthened with all power, according to his glorious might, so that you may have great endurance and patience with joy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:18"> giving thanks to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="the ¦ our God and ANT">g</a></sup>the Father, who has enabled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="us ¦ you CT">h</a></sup>us to share in the inheritance of the saints in light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9"> He has rescued us from the dominion of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of his beloved Son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:24–25; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:19–20"> in whom we have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="redemption ¦ redemption through his blood 𝔐pt PCK TR">i</a></sup>redemption, the remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 1:3; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:1"> He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:36; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6"> For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions, rulers or authorities—all things were created through him and for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 1:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:28"> He is before all things, and in him all things hold together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:22–23; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 1:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:15–16"> He is the head of the body, the church. He is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, so that in everything he may be preeminent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:23"> For in him all the fullness of God was pleased to dwell,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:18–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:9–10"> and through him to reconcile all things to himself, whether on earth or in heaven, making peace through the blood of his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3–7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:1–3"> At one time you were alienated from God and hostile in your minds because of your evil works. But now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="God has reconciled you ¦ you have been reconciled SBL">j</a></sup>God has reconciled you to himself</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:27; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:4"> through the death of Christ in his physical body,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="in order to ¦ so that he might SBL">k</a></sup>in order to bring you into his own presence as holy, unblemished, and above reproach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:13–14"> if indeed you continue in the faith, grounded and steadfast, without shifting away from the hope of the gospel that you heard. This gospel has been preached in all creation under heaven, and of this gospel I, Paul, have become a servant.</span>

## Paul's Labor for the Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:5–8"> I rejoice now in my sufferings for you, and in my flesh I am filling up what is lacking in Christ's afflictions for the sake of his body, that is, the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:7–8"> I became a servant of the church according to the stewardship from God that was given to me for you, to make the word of God fully known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 8:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:25–26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:3–10"> the mystery that has been hidden for ages and generations but has now been revealed to his saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:16–17"> To them God resolved to make known how great among the Gentiles are the glorious riches of this mystery, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:27; Jeremiah 3:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:11–13"> He is the one we proclaim, admonishing everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, so that we may present everyone mature in Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Jesus ¦ — CT PCK">l</a></sup>Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:25–27"> To this end I labor, striving according to his energy that is powerfully at work within me.</span>
