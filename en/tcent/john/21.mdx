## Jesus Appears to Seven Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 28:16"> After this Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="again ¦ — PCK">a</a></sup>again manifested himself to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="the ¦ his PCK">b</a></sup>the disciples, by the Sea of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Tiberias. ¦ Tiberias after he had been raised from the dead. PCK">c</a></sup>Tiberias. He manifested himself in this way:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 4:21–22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:45–1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 4:46"> Simon Peter, Thomas (called Didymus), Nathanael (from Cana of Galilee), the sons of Zebedee, and two of Jesus' other disciples were together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 5:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 4:18–20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:7–9"> Simon Peter said to them, “I am going fishing.” They said to him, “We will go with yoʋ.” So they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="immediately ¦ — CT">d</a></sup>immediately went out and got into the boat, but that night they caught nothing.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:15–16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:31"> When daybreak came, Jesus stood on the shore, but the disciples did not know it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:11–13; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:13"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Children, have you caught any fish to eat?”</span>They answered him, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 5:4–7; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 2:41; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 2:5"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.”</span>So they cast it and were unable to draw it in because of the large number of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 14:28–29"> Then the disciple whom Jesus loved said to Peter, “It is the Lord!” When Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he put on his outer garment (for he had taken it off) and plunged into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> But the other disciples came in the boat, dragging the net full of fish, for they were not far from shore, only about a hundred yards away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:10"> When they had gone ashore, they saw a charcoal fire there with a fish lying on it, and bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Bring some of the fish you have just caught.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 5:6–8; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 2:41"> So Simon Peter went aboard and drew the net ashore, full of large fish, one hundred fifty-three of them. Although there were so many, the net had not torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:41; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 9:45; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:19"> Then Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Come eat breakfast.”</span>Now none of the disciples dared to ask him, “Who are yoʋ?” because they knew it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:9; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:41; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:42–43"> Then Jesus came, took the bread, and gave it to them. He did likewise with the fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:19"> This was now the third time that Jesus had been revealed to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="his ¦ the CT">e</a></sup>his disciples after he had been raised from the dead.</span>
</div>

## Tend My Sheep

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1"> When they had eaten breakfast, Jesus said to Simon Peter,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Jonah ¦ John CT">f</a></sup><span className="woj">Jonah, do yoʋ love me more than these?”</span>Peter said to him, “Yes, Lord, yoʋ know that I care for yoʋ.” Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Feed my lambs.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:25"> Jesus said to him a second time,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Jonah ¦ John CT">g</a></sup><span className="woj">Jonah, do yoʋ love me?”</span>Peter said to him, “Yes, Lord, yoʋ know that I care for yoʋ.” Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Tend my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="sheep ¦ little sheep WH">h</a></sup><span className="woj">sheep.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:38; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:30; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 14:72"> Then Jesus said to him the third time,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Jonah ¦ John CT">i</a></sup><span className="woj">Jonah, do yoʋ care for me?”</span>Peter was grieved that Jesus said to him the third time,<span className="woj">“Do yoʋ care for me?”</span>And he said to him, “Lord, yoʋ know everything; yoʋ know that I care for yoʋ.” Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Feed my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="sheep ¦ little sheep WH">j</a></sup><span className="woj">sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:36; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 12:3–4; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:11"> <span className="woj">Truly, truly, I say to yoʋ, when yoʋ were younger, yoʋ would dress yoʋrself and walk wherever yoʋ wanted to go; but when yoʋ grow old, yoʋ will stretch out yoʋr hands, and another will dress yoʋ and bring yoʋ where yoʋ do not want to go.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:38"> (He said this to indicate the kind of death by which Peter would glorify God.) After saying this, he said to Peter,<span className="woj">“Follow me.”</span></span>

## The Beloved Disciple and His Book

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:24"> Then Peter turned around and saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following them (the one who had leaned back on Jesus' chest at the supper and said, “Lord, who is it that is going to betray yoʋ?”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 13:23–24; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 1:6–7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:3–4"> When Peter saw him, he said to Jesus, “Lord, what about him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:25; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 9:1"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“If I wish for him to remain until I come, what is that to yoʋ? Yoʋ follow me!”</span></span>

</div>
