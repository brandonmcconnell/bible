## The Miracle at Cana

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:2"> On the third day there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee, and Jesus' mother was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:40–49; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:40–42; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:39"> Jesus was also invited to the wedding, and so were his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> When the wine ran out, Jesus' mother said to him, “They have no wine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:30"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Woman, what concern is that to yoʋ or to me? My hour has not yet come.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:14; Genesis 6:22; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:8"> But his mother said to the servants, “Do whatever he tells you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:25; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 9:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:26"> Now six stone water jars had been set there for the Jewish rites of purification, each holding twenty to thirty gallons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 2:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 2:3"> Jesus said to the servants,<span className="woj">“Fill the jars with water.”</span>So they filled them up to the brim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:7"> Then he said to them,<span className="woj">“Now draw some out and take it to the master of the feast.”</span>So they took it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 4:46; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> and the master of the feast tasted the water, which had been turned into wine. He did not know where it had come from, but the servants who had drawn the water knew. Then the master of the feast called the bridegroom aside</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 16:25"> and said to him, “Everyone else serves the good wine first, and then the inferior wine after the guests have drunk freely; but yoʋ have kept the good wine until now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:30–31; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:2"> Jesus did this in Cana of Galilee as the first of his signs. He revealed his glory, and his disciples believed in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:46; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:55–56; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:3"> After this he went down to Capernaum with his mother, his brothers, and his disciples, and they stayed there for a few days.</span>
</div>

## Jesus Cleanses the Temple Courts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 2:41; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 11:55; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 2:23"> Now the Passover of the Jews was near, so Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 19:45–46; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:17"> In the temple courts he found those who were selling oxen, sheep, and doves, and he also found the money changers sitting at their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:4"> So he made a whip of cords and drove them all out of the temple courts, along with their sheep and oxen. He also poured out the coins of the money changers and overturned their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:17"> Then he said to those who were selling doves,<span className="woj">“Take these things away from here; stop making my Father's house a marketplace.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> And his disciples remembered that it is written, “Zeal for yoʋr house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="will consume ¦ has consumed TR">a</a></sup>will consume me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 8:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:27–28"> In response the Jews said to him, “What sign can yoʋ show us to prove yoʋr authority to do these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 14:58; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 15:29; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 27:40"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Jews said, “It has taken forty-six years to build this temple, and will yoʋ raise it up in three days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20–22"> But he was speaking about the temple of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:7–8"> So when he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="this ¦ this to them TR">b</a></sup>this, and they believed the Scripture and the word that Jesus had spoken.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Knows What Is in Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:31; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 8:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:14"> While Jesus was in Jerusalem during the feast of the Passover, many believed in his name because they saw the signs he was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:64; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:13; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 1:24"> But Jesus did not entrust himself to them, for he knew all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 9:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:64"> He did not need anyone to testify about man, for he himself knew what was in man.</span>
