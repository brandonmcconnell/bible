## Alive with Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:5–6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:1–4"> You were dead in the trespasses and sins</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:3"> in which you once walked when you followed the ways of this world and the ruler of the realm of the air, the spirit that is now at work in the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:16–24"> Among them we all once lived in the lusts of our flesh, carrying out the desires of our flesh and our impulses, and we were by nature children of wrath, like the rest of mankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:7; Psalms 86:15"> Yet God, who is rich in mercy, because of the great love with which he loved us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:10"> made us alive together with Christ even when we were dead in our trespasses—by grace you have been saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:1–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:4–5"> And God raised us up with Christ and seated us with him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:16–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:4"> so that in the ages to come he might show the surpassing riches of his grace through his kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:22–26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:16"> For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not of yourselves; it is the gift of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:3–5"> not by works, so that no one may boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:21"> For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand so that we would walk in them.</span>

## Unity in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11"> Therefore remember that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="you, once ¦ at one time you CT">a</a></sup>you, once Gentiles in the flesh, who are called “the uncircumcision” by those who call themselves “the circumcision,” which is made in the flesh by hands—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> remember that you were apart from Christ at that time, excluded from the citizenship of Israel and strangers to the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:39; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:20–22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:13–14"> But now in Christ Jesus you who were once far away have been brought near by the blood of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:15"> For he is our peace, who has united both the Jews and Gentiles into one people and has broken down the wall of hostility that divided us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:21–22"> He did this by abolishing the law with its commandments and ordinances in order to make peace between the two groups and create in himself one new people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:20–22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:18–21"> He reconciled both groups to God in one body through the cross, by which he put the hostility to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:36; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:1"> He came and preached the good news of peace to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="you ¦ us PCK">b</a></sup>you who were far away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="and 91.1% ¦ and peace CT 6.3%">c</a></sup>and to those who were near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:2"> For through him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:10"> So then you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:7–8"> built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus CT">d</a></sup>Jesus Christ himself being the chief cornerstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16–17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:16"> In him the whole building is joined together and rises to become a holy temple in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:4–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:16"> And in him you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God in the Spirit.</span>
