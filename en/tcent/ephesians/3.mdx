## Prayer for the Ephesians to Know the Love of Christ

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> I pray that, according to the riches of his glory, he would grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in your inner being,</span>
