## Children and Parents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:20–25; Proverbs 23:22; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 27:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:4–6"> “Honor yoʋr father and mother” (which is the first commandment with a promise),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:28"> “that it may be well with yoʋ and yoʋ may live long on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:21; Proverbs 22:6; Proverbs 29:15"> Fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the training and admonition of the Lord.</span>
</div>

## Slaves and Masters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:18–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:9–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:17–24"> Slaves, obey your earthly masters with fear and trembling, and with sincerity of heart, just as you would obey Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:22–23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:10"> Do not try to please them only when they are watching you, but act like slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:5–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:31"> Serve with a good attitude,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="as ¦ — ELZ PCK ST">a</a></sup>as rendering service to the Lord and not men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 14:14; Proverbs 11:18"> knowing that whatever good anyone does he will receive back from the Lord, whether he is a slave or free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:25–1; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:34; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:11"> Masters, do the same for your slaves and stop threatening them, because you know that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="your own Master ¦ he who is both their Master and yours CT">b</a></sup>your own Master is in heaven, and there is no partiality with him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Full Armor of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> Finally,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="my brothers, 88.3% ¦ brothers, MSS 7.4% ¦ — CT 3.4%">c</a></sup>my brothers, be strong in the Lord and in the power of his might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 13:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:13"> Put on the full armor of God so that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:2"> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the darkness of this age 96.9% ¦ this darkness CT 3%">d</a></sup>the darkness of this age, and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:36; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:11–17"> Therefore take up the full armor of God so that you may be able to resist the enemy on the day of evil, and after you have done everything, to stand firm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> Stand firm therefore by fastening the belt of truth around your waist, putting on the breastplate of righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:18–21"> and shodding your feet with the readiness of the gospel of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:8–9; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Above all ¦ In all circumstances CT">e</a></sup>Above all, take up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 19:15"> Take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> Pray in the Spirit at all times, with every prayer and supplication, and to this end, stay alert and be persistent in praying for all the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:3; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 4:29; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:1"> Pray also for me, so that when I open my mouth to speak, a message may be given to me to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:9"> for which I am an ambassador in chains. Pray that I may declare it boldly, as I should.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:12"> Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will give you a full report of how I am doing so that you may be informed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:7–8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:17"> I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know how we are doing and that he may encourage your hearts.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 22:37; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:13"> Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Amen. ¦ — CT SCR">f</a></sup><span className="desc">Amen.</span></span>
