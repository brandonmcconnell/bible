## Unity in the Body of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:27; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:12"> Therefore I, a prisoner for the Lord, urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling you have received,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:12–13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:4–5"> with all humility and gentleness, and with patience, bearing with one another in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:13–15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12–13"> and being diligent to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:4–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:4–13"> There is one body and one Spirit (just as you were called to one hope at your calling),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:36; Malachi 2:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6"> one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="us ¦ you TR ¦ — CT">a</a></sup>us all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:7–11"> Now each one of us was given grace according to the measure of Christ's gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 68:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> Therefore it says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When he ascended on high, he led captivity captive</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and gave gifts to men.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:28–29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:20"> It was he who appointed some to be apostles, some to be prophets, some to be evangelists, and some to be pastors and teachers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:11–14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:27"> to equip the saints for the work of ministry and to build up the body of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:28; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 17:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:5"> until we all reach unity in the faith and in the knowledge of the Son of God, growing into maturity with a stature measured by Christ's fullness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:17–18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:20"> So we must no longer be infants, tossed back and forth by waves and carried about by every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of men and their craftiness in deceitful scheming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> Rather, speaking the truth in love, we must grow up in every way into him who is the head, that is, Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:2"> From him the whole body, joined and held together by every supporting joint, grows and builds itself up in love as each individual part does its work.</span>
</div>

## Living the New Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:3–4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:1–2"> Therefore, I solemnly declare in the Lord that you should no longer walk as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the rest of 90.8% ¦ — CT 5.6%">c</a></sup>the rest of the Gentiles do, in the futility of their minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:21–23"> They are darkened in their understanding, being excluded from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them due to the hardness of their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:24–26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2"> They became callous and gave themselves up to lewd behavior, greedy to engage in every kind of impurity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:11–14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:45; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:29"> But you did not learn Christ in that way!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:7–8"> Surely you have heard about him and were taught in him, just as the truth is in Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:6; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:1; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:21"> to put off your old self, which belongs to your former way of life and is being corrupted by its deceitful desires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:6; Psalms 51:10"> to be renewed in the spirit of your minds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:10–14; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:10"> and to put on the new self, created according to the image of God in true righteousness and holiness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Therefore, having laid aside falsehood, each of you must speak the truth with his neighbor, because we are members of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:19; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 37:8"> Do not sin in your anger. Do not let the sun go down while you are still angry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:16"> and do not give any opportunity to the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:10"> He who steals must no longer steal; rather he must labor, doing honest work with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="his 71.7% ¦ his own NA SBL TH 23.3%">d</a></sup>his hands, so that he may have something to contribute to anyone in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Let no foul word come out of your mouth, but only what is good for edification, as fits the occasion, that it may give grace to those who hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:13–14"> And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:2–3"> Put away all bitterness, wrath, anger, outbursts, and slander, together with all malice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:12–13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:14–15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:25"> Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ has forgiven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="us ¦ you NA PCK SBL TR WH">e</a></sup>us.</span>
