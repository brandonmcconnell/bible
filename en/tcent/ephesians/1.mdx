## Spiritual Blessings in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:6"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28–30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:10"> just as he chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world to be holy and unblemished before him in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:29–30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:5–6"> He foreordained us for adoption as his sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of his will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:22–26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:13"> to the praise of the glory of his grace,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="by which he bestowed favor ¦ which he freely bestowed CT">b</a></sup>by which he bestowed favor upon us in the Beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 1:7–9"> In him we have redemption through his blood, the remission of our trespasses, according to the riches of God's grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 5:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:15"> which he lavished upon us, along with all wisdom and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:26–28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:25–27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:3–9"> He made known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure, which he purposed in Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:9–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:4"> as a plan for the fullness of times, to bring everything together in Christ, things in heaven and things on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> In Christ we have obtained an inheritance, having been foreordained according to the purpose of him who accomplishes all things according to the counsel of his own will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 1:13–14"> so that we who were the first to hope in Christ, might live for the praise of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:30; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:19"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="And when you heard the word of the truth, the gospel of your salvation, and believed in Christ, ¦ We also heard the word of the truth about Christ, the gospel of your salvation; by whom, when you believed, PCK">c</a></sup>And when you heard the word of the truth, the gospel of your salvation, and believed in Christ, you were also sealed with the promised Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:30"> who is the guarantee of our inheritance until the redemption of God's own possession, to the praise of his glory.</span>
</div>

## Paul's Prayer for the Ephesians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:3–4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3"> For this reason, ever since I heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="your love for 93.9% ¦ toward WH 1.3%">d</a></sup>your love for all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8–9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:3"> I have not ceased giving thanks for you as I remember you in my prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:9–10"> I pray that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation as you come to know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:45"> I pray that the eyes of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="heart ¦ understanding TR">e</a></sup>heart may be enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of his calling, what are the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:12"> and what is the surpassing greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his great power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:19; Psalms 110:1"> He exercised this power in Christ by raising him from the dead and seating him at his right hand in the heavenly places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:15–16"> far above every ruler and authority, power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the age to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:25–27"> God put all things in subjection under Christ's feet and appointed him as head over all things for the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:18–19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:9–10"> which is his body, the fullness of him who fills everything in every way.</span>
</div>
