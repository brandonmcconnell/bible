## The Preaching of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 18:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:80"> during the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="high priesthood of ¦ time of the high priests TR">a</a></sup>high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John the son of Zechariah in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:77; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 3:5–6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:24"> He went into all the region around the Jordan, preaching a baptism of repentance for the remission of sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:76–79"> as it is written in the book of the words of the prophet Isaiah,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The voice of one crying out in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Prepare the way for the Lord;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">make his paths straight.</span><br /></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:33; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:34; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:10"> John said to the crowds that were coming out to be baptized by him, “You brood of vipers, who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 13:9"> Even now the axe is laid to the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:37; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:14; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:30"> So the crowds asked him, “What then should we do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:7–11; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:18"> John answered them, “Whoever has two tunics must share with him who has none, and whoever has food must do likewise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 7:29; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 15:1–2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 18:13"> Some tax collectors also came to be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="baptized ¦ baptized by him PCK">b</a></sup>baptized, and they said to him, “Teacher, what should we do?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:5–6; Exodus 20:16; Exodus 23:1"> Then some soldiers asked him, “And what should we do?” He said to them, “Do not extort money from anyone by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="threats or false accusations ¦ false accusations or threats ANT">c</a></sup>threats or false accusations, and be content with your wages.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 10:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:28–29; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:19–28"> Now as the people were waiting in expectation and were all questioning in their hearts whether John might be the Christ,</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:30; Micah 4:12"> His winnowing fork is in his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="hand, and he will thoroughly clean out his threshing floor and ¦ hand to thoroughly clean out his threshing floor and to CT">d</a></sup>hand, and he will thoroughly clean out his threshing floor and gather the wheat into his barn, but the chaff he will burn up with unquenchable fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:29–36; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:29"> With these and many other exhortations John preached good news to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:1; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 6:17–18"> But when John rebuked Herod the tetrarch because of his marriage to Herodias, his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="brother's ¦ brother Philip's TR">e</a></sup>brother's wife, and because of all the evils he had done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:17–22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:15–16"> Herod added this to everything else: He locked John up in prison.</span>
