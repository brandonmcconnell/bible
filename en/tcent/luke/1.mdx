## Gabriel Announces John's Birth to Zechariah

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:15"> They were both righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and regulations of the Lord.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 8:3; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:14"> At the hour of incense a great crowd was assembled outside, praying.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said to him, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah, for yoʋr prayer has been heard. Yoʋr wife Elizabeth will bear yoʋ a son, and yoʋ shall name him John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> Yoʋ will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth,</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:7"> Zechariah said to the angel, “How can I know this for certain? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in her days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> The angel answered him, “I am Gabriel. I stand in the presence of God and was sent to speak to yoʋ and bring yoʋ this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:22"> But behold, yoʋ will be silent and unable to speak until the day these things take place, because yoʋ did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at their appointed time.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> Meanwhile, the people were waiting for Zechariah and wondering about his delay in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:62; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:24; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:40"> When he came out, he was unable to speak to them, and they perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple, because he kept making signs to them and remained unable to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> And when the days of his service had come to an end, he returned to his home.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> After those days his wife Elizabeth conceived and kept herself in seclusion for five months, saying,</span>
