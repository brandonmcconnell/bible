<div className="bp">

## Jesus Heals a Paralytic

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 8:46; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:5"> On one of those days, as he was teaching, some Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting there. <span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 2:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:17"> But when they could not find a way to bring him in because of the crowd, they went up on the housetop and let him down through the tiles, together with his mat, into the middle of the crowd in front of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 9:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 2:5"> When Jesus saw their faith, he said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="to the man ¦ — CT">g</a></sup>to the man,<span className="woj">“Man, yoʋr sins are forgiven yoʋ.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalms 139:2"> Aware of their thoughts, Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Why are you questioning in your hearts?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 9:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 7:16"> Amazement seized them all, and they too began glorifying God. Filled with awe, they said, “We have seen extraordinary things today.”</span>
