## Confirm Your Calling

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28–30"> His divine power has given us everything we need for life and godliness, through the knowledge of him who called us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="by ¦ by his own ECM NA SBL TH">c</a></sup>by glory and virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:23–24; Ezekiel 36:25–27; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:18"> Through these he has given us his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="precious and magnificent 75.8% • ECM† NA SBL WH 5.9% ¦ magnificent and precious TR 1% • MSS 6.8%">d</a></sup>precious and magnificent promises, so that through them you may become partakers of the divine nature, having escaped from the corruption that is in the world because of evil desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:8; Proverbs 4:23; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:18"> For this very reason, make every effort to supplement your faith with virtue, your virtue with knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:36; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:3–4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:19"> your knowledge with self-control, your self-control with endurance, your endurance with godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:12"> your godliness with brotherly affection, and your brotherly affection with love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:2; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:12"> For if you possess these qualities and continue to grow in them, they will keep you from being ineffective and unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:18–20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:14"> For whoever lacks these qualities is so nearsighted that he is blind, having forgotten the cleansing of his former sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:47–49; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:11"> Therefore, brothers, be all the more diligent to confirm that you are among those whom God has called and chosen, because if you do these things, you will never stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 5:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:34; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:21"> For in this way, entry into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly provided for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:14–15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:1"> Therefore, I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="will not neglect 95.2% ¦ intend CT 2.8%">e</a></sup>will not neglect to keep reminding you about these things, though you know them and are established in the truth you now have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:3"> I think it is right, as long as I am in this bodily tent, to stir you up by way of reminder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 21:18–19; Deuteronomy 31:14"> because I know that this tent will soon be laid aside, as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19–29; Psalms 71:18; Joshua 24:24–29"> And I will make every effort to ensure that after my departure you will be able to recall these things at any time.</span>
</div>

## Eyewitnesses of His Majesty

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:4"> For we did not follow cleverly devised myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we were eyewitnesses of his majesty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 9:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 17:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 3:17"> For he received honor and glory from God the Father when that voice came to him from the Majestic Glory: “This is my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="beloved Son, 97.6% ¦ Son, my beloved, ECM NA SBL WH 0.4%">f</a></sup>beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 17:6; Joshua 5:15; Exodus 3:5"> We ourselves heard this voice come from heaven when we were with him on the holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 22:16; Isaiah 9:2; Psalms 119:105"> So we have the prophetic word more fully confirmed. You will do well to pay attention to it, as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 3:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:6"> First of all, you must understand that no prophecy of Scripture is a matter of one's own interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:15–17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:11; 2 Samuel 23:2"> For no prophecy ever came by the will of man, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="holy men of God spoke 83.5% ¦ men spoke from God CT 5.6% ¦ the holy men of God spoke ST 4.4%">g</a></sup>holy men of God spoke as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.</span>
