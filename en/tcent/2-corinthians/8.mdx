## Paul's Appeal to Complete the Collection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:4"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="we ¦ I ANT">a</a></sup>we make known to you, brothers, the grace of God that has been given among the churches of Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:5; Proverbs 11:25; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:10"> During a severe trial of affliction, their abundant joy and their deep poverty abounded in a wealth of generosity on their part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:29; Exodus 35:5; Exodus 35:21–22"> For I testify that according to their ability, and even beyond their ability, they gave of their own accord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 24:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:1"> urgently begging us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="for the privilege of contributing to the gift for ¦ to receive their gift and contribution to support PCK TR">b</a></sup>for the privilege of contributing to the gift for the saints in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:7–9; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> And they did not do this in the way we expected, but they gave themselves first to the Lord and then to us by the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:19; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:4"> So we have urged Titus, who previously encouraged you to begin gathering money, to return and help you complete this gracious gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3"> But just as you abound in everything—in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in complete earnestness, and in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="your love for us ¦ our love for you NA SBL WH">c</a></sup>your love for us—make sure you abound in this gracious gift as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:17–19; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:14–16"> I am not saying this as a command, but I want to test the sincerity of your love by comparing it with the earnestness of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:6–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 20:28"> For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though he was rich, yet for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="your ¦ our PCK">d</a></sup>your sakes he became poor, so that by his poverty you could become rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:18–19; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:2"> So I am giving my advice about what is best for you in this matter. Last year you were not only the first who wanted to give, but you were also the first to begin doing so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:2"> Now finish doing it, so that your eager willingness to give may be matched by your completion of the gift, according to what you have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 12:42–44; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:1–4"> For if the eager willingness is there, it is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what one does not have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:34; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:26–27"> I do not mean there should be relief for others and hardship for you, but it is a matter of equality. At the present time your abundance should provide for their need,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:12; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:34"> so that in turn their abundance may provide for your need, and thus there may be equality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 22:35"> As it is written, “He who gathered much had nothing left over, and he who gathered little had no lack.”</span>

## Titus and His Companions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 17:17; Jeremiah 32:40; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:13–14"> But thanks be to God, who has put into the heart of Titus the same earnest care for you that I have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:10"> For Titus not only accepted our appeal, but with great enthusiasm he is coming to you of his own accord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:17"> With him we are sending the brother who is praised by all the churches for his preaching of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:3–4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:15; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:8"> And not only that, but he has also been appointed by the churches to travel with us as we administer this gracious gift for the glory of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="himself ¦ — TH WH">e</a></sup>himself and to show<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="our ¦ your TR">f</a></sup>our eager willingness to help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:15"> We are taking this precaution so that no one will discredit us with respect to this large sum that we are administering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:8"> For we are giving careful thought to do what is right, not only in the sight of the Lord but also in the sight of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:20–22"> We are also sending with them our brother whom we have often tested and found to be earnest in many matters, and now he is even more earnest because of his great confidence in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6"> If there is any question about Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker in your service. If there is any question about our brothers, they are delegates of the churches, the glory of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:14; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:2–4"> Therefore show them in front of all the churches the proof of your love and the reason for our boasting about you.</span>
