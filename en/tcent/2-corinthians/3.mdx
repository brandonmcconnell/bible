## Ministers of the New Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:12"> Are we beginning to commend ourselves again?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Do ¦ Or do CT SCR">a</a></sup>Do we need, as some do, letters of recommendation to you or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="letters of recommendation ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>letters of recommendation from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:1–2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:8"> You are our letter, written on our hearts, known and read by all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Jeremiah 31:33; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:10"> You show that you are a letter from Christ, delivered by us, written not with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God, not on stone tablets, but on tablets of human hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:14"> Such is the confidence that we have in God through Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:10"> Not that we are competent in and of ourselves to claim that anything comes from us, but our competence comes from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:63; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:6; Jeremiah 31:31"> He has made us competent to be ministers of a new covenant, not of the letter, but of the Spirit. For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:9–11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:4; Exodus 31:18"> Now if the ministry of death, which was engraved in letters on stone, came with glory, so that the sons of Israel could not gaze at the face of Moses because of its glory (a glory that was fading away),</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:14"> will not the ministry of the Spirit be even more glorious?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 3:21–22; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:18–21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:21"> For if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="the ministry of condemnation was glorious ¦ there was glory in the ministry of condemnation NA SBL TH">c</a></sup>the ministry of condemnation was glorious, the ministry of righteousness abounds in glory even more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:17; Haggai 2:7–9; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:13"> For what was once glorious now has no glory in comparison with the glory that surpasses it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:20–21; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:13; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 7:21–25"> And if what was fading away came with glory, what remains will be even more glorious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:4; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:20"> Therefore, since we have such a hope, we act with great boldness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:33–35; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:1–9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:14–15"> We are not like Moses, who would put a veil over his face so that the sons of Israel would not gaze at the end of what was fading away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:3–4; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:25"> But their minds were hardened, for to this day the same veil remains unlifted when the old covenant is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="read. Only in Christ is this veil ¦ read, because only in Christ is it ANT CT HF PCK">d</a></sup>read. Only in Christ is this veil taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:27–29"> But to this day,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="when ¦ whenever CT">e</a></sup>when Moses is read, a veil lies over their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:34; Isaiah 29:18; Isaiah 25:7"> But whenever someone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 8:32"> Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:2"> And we all, with unveiled faces reflecting the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another; this comes from the Lord, who is the Spirit.</span>
