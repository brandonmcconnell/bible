## Paul's Perseverance in Ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:13"> Therefore, since we have this ministry through God's mercy, we do not lose heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:3–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:11"> Rather, we have renounced shameful hidden deeds; we do not act deceitfully or distort the word of God, but by openly proclaiming the truth we commend ourselves to everyone's conscience in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:9–11"> But even if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled among those who are perishing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:40; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:14; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:18"> The god of this age has blinded the minds of unbelievers like them, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, might not dawn upon them ¦ they might not see the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God CT">a</a></sup>the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, might not dawn upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:23; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:5–6"> For what we preach is not ourselves, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Christ Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ NA">b</a></sup>Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your servants for Jesus' sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 60:2; Genesis 1:3; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:9"> For God, who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="commanded light to shine out of darkness, ¦ said, “Light shall shine out of darkness,” CT">c</a></sup>commanded light to shine out of darkness, has shone in our hearts to give us the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Jesus ¦ — SBL WH">d</a></sup>Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:5–6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:8–9"> But we have this treasure in jars of clay, to show that this extraordinary power is from God and not from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:35–37; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:12–14"> We are afflicted in every way, but not crushed; perplexed, but not in despair;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Micah 7:8; Psalms 37:24–25"> persecuted, but not forsaken; struck down, but not destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:13"> always carrying about in our bodies the death of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the Lord ¦ — CT">e</a></sup>the Lord Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:49; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:36; Psalms 44:22"> For we who live are always being delivered up to death for Jesus' sake, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our mortal flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:10; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:24"> So then, death is at work in us, but life is at work in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 116:10; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:1–40; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:12"> It is written, “I believed, therefore I spoke.” With that same spirit of faith we also believe, therefore we also speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:11; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:24"> For we know that he who raised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="the Lord ¦ — SBL">f</a></sup>the Lord Jesus will also raise us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="through ¦ with CT">g</a></sup>through Jesus and bring us with you into his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:20–21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:11–12"> All of this is for your benefit, so that grace, as it extends to more and more people, may cause thanksgiving to abound to the glory of God.</span>
</div>

## Living by Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:10; Isaiah 40:29"> Therefore we do not lose heart. Even though on the outside we are wasting away, on the inside we are being renewed day by day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:3–5"> For our light and momentary affliction is bringing about for us an eternal weight of glory far beyond all measure,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:24–25; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:1"> as we look not to what is seen, but to what is unseen. For what is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal.</span>
</div>
