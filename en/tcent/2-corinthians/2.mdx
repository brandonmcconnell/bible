## Forgive the Sinner

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:12–13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> Now if anyone has caused sorrow, he has caused it not to me, but in some measure (not to put it too severely) to all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:4–5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:11"> This punishment by the majority is enough for that person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:32; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:13"> So you should forgive and comfort him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="instead ¦ — WH">b</a></sup>instead, so that he will not be overwhelmed with excessive sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:22–23; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:10"> Therefore I urge you to reaffirm your love for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:22"> I wrote to you because I wanted to test your character and see if you would be obedient in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 20:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:20"> Now if you forgive anyone of anything, I also forgive him. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:11–12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:3"> so that we might not be exploited by Satan. For we are not ignorant of his schemes.</span>

## Triumph in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:27; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:3"> Now when I came to Troas to preach the gospel of Christ, a door was opened for me in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:5–6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:4"> But I had no rest in my spirit because I did not find my brother Titus. So I took leave of the people there and went on to Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:37; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:15–16"> But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumphal procession in Christ, and through us spreads the fragrance of the knowledge of Christ everywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are a sweet fragrance of Christ ascending to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:5–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 9:39; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:7–8"> to the latter we are a smell of death leading to death, but to the former we are a fragrance of life leading to life. And who is equal to such a task?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:1–3"> For we are not like<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="others 𝔐191 51.9% ¦ so many others 𝔐172 CT TR 46.1%">d</a></sup>others who peddle the word of God for profit. On the contrary, in Christ we speak with sincerity in the presence of God, as men sent from God.</span>
