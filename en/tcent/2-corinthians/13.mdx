## Final Warnings and Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6"> This is the third time I am coming to you. Every matter must be established by the mouth of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:23; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:20–21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:5"> I gave a warning when I was present the second time, and now I am giving this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="written ¦ — CT">a</a></sup>written warning while absent: If I come again I will not spare those who sinned previously or any of the others,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:15"> since you seek proof that Christ is speaking in me. He is not weak in dealing with you, but is powerful among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:3–4"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="though he was crucified in weakness, ¦ he was crucified in weakness, but CT">b</a></sup>though he was crucified in weakness, he lives by the power of God. Now we also are weak in him, but in dealing with you we will live with him by the power of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:28; Psalms 139:23–24"> Examine yourselves to see if you are in the faith; test yourselves. Or do you not realize that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus TH">c</a></sup>Jesus Christ is in you?—unless, of course, you fail to meet the test.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:3–4"> But I hope you will recognize that we have not failed the test.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:8–9"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="I ¦ We CT">d</a></sup>I pray to God that you will not do anything wrong, not so that we may appear to have met the test, but so that you may do what is right, even though we may appear to have failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 9:39; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 4:28–30"> For we cannot do anything against the truth, but only for the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:10"> We rejoice when we are weak and you are strong, and we pray for your restoration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:13"> The reason I am writing these things to you while I am absent is so that, when I am present, I will not need to be harsh in my use of authority, which the Lord has given me for building up and not for tearing down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:10"> Finally, brothers, rejoice. Be restored, be comforted, be of the same mind, and live in peace, and the God of love and peace will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:26"> Greet one another with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:21–23; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:24"> All the saints greet you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:20"> The grace of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="the ¦ our PCK">e</a></sup>the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Amen. ¦ — CT">f</a></sup><span className="desc">Amen.</span></span>
</div>
