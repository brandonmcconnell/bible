## Paul's Change of Plans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:13–18"> Now this is the reason for our confidence: Our conscience testifies that we have conducted ourselves in the world, and especially toward you, with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="integrity 94.3% ¦ holiness SBL TH WH 4.5%">e</a></sup>integrity and godly sincerity, not by worldly wisdom, but by the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:6"> For we are not writing anything to you other than what you can read and understand. And I hope that you will understand completely—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:19–20"> just as you have understood us in part—that on the day of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="the ¦ our NA SBL WH">f</a></sup>the Lord Jesus you will be proud of us just as we will be proud of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:29"> Because I was confident of this, I intended to come to you first, so that you would<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="be blessed by a second visit ¦ have a second experience of joy WH">g</a></sup>be blessed by a second visit.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:37; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:2–3"> Was I vacillating when I planned to do this? <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:24–25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:22; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 7:12"> For in him every one of God's promises is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="“Yes,” and in 90.3% ¦ “Yes.” And so through CT 6.8%">j</a></sup>“Yes,” and in him we say “Amen” to the glory of God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:9"> I call upon God as a witness against me, that the reason I did not return to Corinth was to spare you.</span>
