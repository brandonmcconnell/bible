## Paul's Joy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:33; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:12–13; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:9"> Make room for us in your hearts. We have wronged no one, we have corrupted no one, we have taken advantage of no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:11–12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:11"> I do not say this to condemn you, for I have said before that you are in our hearts so that we die together with you and live together with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:12; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2"> Great is my boldness toward you; great is my boasting on your behalf. I am filled with comfort; I am overflowing with joy in all our affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:1"> For even when we came to Macedonia, our bodies had no rest, but we were afflicted at every turn, with conflicts on the outside and fears within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:3–4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:16–17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:13"> But God, who comforts the downcast, comforted us by the arrival of Titus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:2; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:16"> and not only by his arrival, but also by the comfort you had given him. He told us about your longing, your contrition, and your deep concern for me, so that I rejoiced all the more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:19; Jeremiah 20:7–9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 26:21–22"> For even if I caused you sorrow by my letter, I do not regret it (though I did regret<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="it, for ¦ it; SBL WH">a</a></sup>it, for I see that my letter caused you sorrow, though only for an hour).</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 15:7; Jeremiah 31:18–20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:10–11"> But now I rejoice, not that you were made sorrowful, but that you were made sorrowful to the point of repentance. For you felt a godly sorrow, so that you suffered no loss through us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 3:19; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 11:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:25–26"> For godly sorrow produces a repentance that leads to salvation, leaving no regret, but worldly sorrow produces death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:21–22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:15"> Consider what earnestness this godly sorrow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="of yours ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>of yours has produced in you, what eagerness to clear yourselves, what indignation, what alarm, what longing, what zeal, what righting of wrong! In everything you have shown yourselves to be clear in the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:17"> So although I wrote to you, it was not on account of the one who did the wrong, nor on account of the one who was wronged, but so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="your earnestness for us ¦ our earnestness for you SCR">c</a></sup>your earnestness for us might be revealed to you in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:13"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In this we find comfort. And in addition to the comfort we received from you, ¦ Therefore we have been comforted in your comfort. And 𝔐pt PCK TR ¦ In this we find comfort. And in addition to our own comfort, CT &#123;Note: The reading of 𝔐pt, PCK, and TR would move the paragraph break to the beginning of verse 13.&#125;">d</a></sup>In this we find comfort. And in addition to the comfort we received from you, we rejoiced all the more at the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:18–20"> For if I made any boast to him about you, I was not put to shame. But just as everything we said to you was true, so our boasting to Titus has also proven to be true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:17"> And his affection for you is even greater when he remembers the obedience of you all, how you received him with fear and trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:21"> I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="rejoice, ¦ rejoice therefore, SCR">e</a></sup>rejoice, because I have complete confidence in you.</span>
