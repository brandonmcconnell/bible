## Paul Defends His Ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:29; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:2"> Now I, Paul, myself appeal to you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ—I who am humble when with you face to face, but bold toward you when absent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:16–25"> I ask that when I am present I need not show boldness by daring to oppose those who think that we walk according to the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:1–2"> For though we walk in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:13–18; Jeremiah 1:10"> For the weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh, but are powerful in God for tearing down strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 4:12; Isaiah 55:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:14–15"> We tear down arguments and every high thing that exalts itself against the knowledge of God, and we take every thought captive to the obedience of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:9; Numbers 16:26–30; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:15"> We are ready to punish any disobedience, once your obedience is complete.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 14:37"> Are you looking only at outward appearances? If anyone is confident that he belongs to Christ, he should remind himself that just as he belongs to Christ, so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="we also belong to Christ ¦ do we CT">a</a></sup>we also belong to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:1"> For even if I boast somewhat further about our authority, which the Lord has given<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="us ¦ — CT">b</a></sup>us for building you up and not for tearing you down, I will not be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:19–21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:5"> For I do not want to seem as though I am trying to frighten you by my letters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:17; Exodus 4:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:13–14"> For, “His letters,” they say, “are weighty and strong, but his bodily presence is weak, and his speech amounts to nothing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:2–3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:10"> The people who say such things should realize that what we say in our letters when we are absent, we will demonstrate by our actions when we are present.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 26:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 18:11"> For we do not dare to classify or compare ourselves with some who commend themselves. When they measure themselves with one another and compare themselves to one another, they do not show good sense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:10"> We, however, will not boast beyond certain limits, but will confine our boasting to the area of ministry that God has assigned to us, which reaches even as far as you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:16"> For we are not overextending ourselves, as though we did not reach you; we were the first to come all the way to you with the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:13"> We do not go beyond our limits by boasting in the labors of others. On the contrary, our hope is that, as your faith increases, our area of ministry among you will be greatly expanded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:24–28; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:21"> so that we can preach the gospel in the regions beyond you, without boasting about work already done in someone else's area of ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:31; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:9–10"> But, “Let him who boasts, boast in the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 16:15; Proverbs 21:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:5"> For it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.</span>
</div>
</div>
