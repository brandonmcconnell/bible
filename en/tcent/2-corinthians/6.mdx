## Do Not Be Unequally Yoked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:33; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:21; THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:4"> Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership is there between righteousness and lawlessness? What fellowship does light have with darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:20–21; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:11–13"> What harmony does Christ have with Belial? What portion does a believer have with an unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 21:3; Exodus 29:45; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 8:10"> And what agreement does the temple of God have with idols? For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="you ¦ we CT">a</a></sup>you are the temple of the living God, just as God said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will dwell in them and walk among them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will be their God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will be my people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 18:4; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, come out from among them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and be separate, says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Touch no unclean thing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will receive you.</span><br /></span>
