<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:24"> For I know how eager you are to help, and I keep boasting about you to the Macedonians, saying that Achaia has been prepared to give since last year; and your zeal has stirred up most of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:1"> But I have sent the brothers so that our boasting about you in this matter may not prove to be empty, but that you may be prepared, just as I said you would be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:26; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:24"> Otherwise, if any of the Macedonians were to come with me and find you unprepared, we (not to mention you) would be ashamed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="of this confident boasting 84.8% ¦ for being so confident CT 3.6%">a</a></sup>of this confident boasting.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 112:9; Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> As it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He has distributed freely, he has given to the poor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his righteousness endures forever.”</span></span>
