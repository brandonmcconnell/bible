<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:8; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:14"> But I am afraid that just as the serpent deceived Eve by his craftiness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="so 94.9% ¦ — CT 2%">b</a></sup>so your minds will be led astray from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="simple 94% ¦ simple and pure CT 4%">c</a></sup>simple devotion to Christ.</span>

## Paul's Sufferings as an Apostle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:1; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:11"> I repeat: Let no one consider me to be a fool. But if you do, receive me just as you would a fool, so that I too may boast a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3–6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:4"> What I say in this confidence of boasting, I am not saying in accordance with the Lord, but as a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:21–23; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:12–18"> Since many boast according to the flesh, I too will boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:1"> For you gladly bear with fools since you are so wise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:1"> You bear with it if anyone enslaves you, devours you, takes advantage of you, exalts himself, or hits you in the face.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:4–5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:9–10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:9"> Are they servants of Christ? (I am speaking like I am out of my mind!) I am even more so, with far more labors, with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="beatings beyond measure, with far more imprisonments 93.6% ¦ far more imprisonments, with beatings beyond measure CT 1.6%">f</a></sup>beatings beyond measure, with far more imprisonments, and often facing death.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:5; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:5; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:50"> I have often been on journeys, exposed to dangers from rivers, dangers from robbers, dangers from my own people, dangers from the Gentiles, dangers in the city, dangers in the wilderness, dangers at sea, and dangers from false brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:5; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:9"> I have endured labor and hardship, many sleepless nights, and hunger and thirst. I have often gone without food and have been cold due to a lack of sufficient clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:16; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:18–35"> Apart from these external things, there is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="my daily burden ¦ the daily burden on me CT">g</a></sup>my daily burden: my concern for all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 4:8–20"> Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is made to stumble, and I do not burn with indignation?</span>
</div>
