## The Testing of Your Faith Produces Endurance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:6–8; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> Count it all joy, my brothers, when you experience various trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:3–4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:36; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:11"> because you know that the testing of your faith produces endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:19; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:48"> And let endurance have its full effect, so that you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> If any of you lacks wisdom, he should ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach, and it will be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:21–22; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 11:22–24"> But he must ask in faith without doubting, for he who doubts is like a wave of the sea that is driven by the wind and tossed about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> That person should not expect to receive anything from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:24; THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:8"> A double-minded man is unstable in all his ways.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lowly Brother and the Rich Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> The lowly brother should rejoice in being exalted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:17; Job 14:2"> and the rich man should rejoice in being made low, because he will pass away like a flower of grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 49:6–14"> For the sun rises with its scorching heat and withers the grass, and its flower falls, and its beautiful appearance is lost. So also will the rich man fade away in his pursuits.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Enduring Temptation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:6–8; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:10"> Blessed is the man who endures temptation, for when he has stood the test, he will receive the crown of life, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="the Lord 89.1% ¦ he CT 2%">a</a></sup>the Lord has promised to those who love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 9:19–20"> No one should say when he is tempted, “I am being tempted by God,” for God is not tempted by evil, and he himself tempts no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 7:21–22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:22; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:18"> But each person is tempted when he is drawn away and enticed by his own evil desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Job 15:35; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:12–21"> And when desire has conceived, it gives birth to sin. And when sin is fully grown, it brings forth death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:7; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:12"> Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:11; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:8"> Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow of turning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:29–31; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 1:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:3–5"> Of his own will he gave us birth by the word of truth so that we would be a kind of firstfruits of his creatures.</span>
</div>
</div>
