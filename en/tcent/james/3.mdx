## The Wisdom from Above

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:23; THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:5"> Who is wise and understanding among you? By his good conduct he should show that his works are done with the gentleness that comes from wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 13:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast or deny the truth.</span>
