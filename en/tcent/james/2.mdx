## Faith and Works

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:26–27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 7:21–23; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:22–25"> What is the benefit, my brothers, if someone claims to have faith but does not have works? Can that faith save him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 3:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> If a brother or sister is poorly clothed and lacks daily food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:16–18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 25:42–45"> and one of you says to them, “Go in peace, be warmed and filled,” without giving them anything to address their physical needs, what is the benefit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:26; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–9; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:19–20"> In the same way faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:13; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:6"> But someone will say, “Yoʋ have faith, and I have works.” Show me yoʋr faith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="by 87.7% ¦ apart from CT SCR 11.1%">f</a></sup>by yoʋr works, and I, by my works, will show yoʋ my faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 4:34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 8:29"> Do yoʋ believe that God is one? Yoʋ do well; even the demons believe—and shudder!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:17; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> Do yoʋ want to be shown, O foolish man, that faith without works is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="dead 98.2% ¦ useless CT 1.6%">g</a></sup>dead?</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:3; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:17–19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:6"> Do yoʋ see how faith was active along with his works, and by works his faith was brought to completion?</span>
