<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 17:12; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 12:9; Daniel 7:24"> Then another sign appeared in heaven: behold, a great fiery red dragon with seven heads and ten horns, and seven crowns on his heads.</span>
