## James Killed and Peter Imprisoned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 24:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:20; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:17–18"> About that time King Herod arrested some who belonged to the church in order to harm them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:37; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 20:23; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 4:21–22"> He killed James the brother of John with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:43; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:27"> And when he saw that this pleased the Jews, he proceeded to arrest Peter also. (This took place during the Feast of Unleavened Bread.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 22:33; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 21:12"> After seizing him, he put him in prison, handing him over to be guarded by four squads of four soldiers each. Herod planned to bring him out to the people after the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:17; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:16"> So Peter was kept in prison, but the church was earnestly praying to God for him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Angel Frees Peter from Prison

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:33; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:6–7"> The very night before Herod was going to bring him out, Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains, while the guards in front of the door were keeping watch over the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:19; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:26; Psalms 146:7"> And behold, an angel of the Lord stood before Peter, and a light shone in the cell. The angel struck Peter on the side and woke him up, saying, “Get up quickly!” And the chains fell off Peter's hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the angel said to him, “Get dressed and put on yoʋr sandals.” And Peter did so. Then the angel said to him, “Put on yoʋr cloak and follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:1; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 11:8; Genesis 45:26"> So Peter went out and followed him, but he did not realize that what the angel was doing was really happening; he thought he was seeing a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:19; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:26; Isaiah 45:1–2"> After they passed the first and second guard, they came to the iron gate leading into the city. It opened for them on its own, and they went out. When they had gone the length of one street, the angel left him suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:28; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:9"> When Peter came to his senses, he said, “Now I truly know that the Lord has sent his angel and rescued me from the hand of Herod and from everything the Jewish people were expecting to happen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 5:14–15; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 12:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:19–20"> When Peter realized this, he went to the house of Mary the mother of John (who was also called Mark), where many had gathered together and were praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 12:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 13:25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 18:16–17"> Peter knocked at the door of the gate, and a servant girl named Rhoda came to answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:41; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 28:8"> When she recognized Peter's voice, she was so overjoyed that she did not open the gate but ran in and reported that Peter was standing in front of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 18:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 24:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:14"> They said to her, “Yoʋ are out of yoʋr mind.” But when she kept insisting that it was so, they said, “It is his angel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Meanwhile Peter continued knocking, and when they opened the gate, they saw him and were astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:40; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:16; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:33"> But motioning to them with his hand to be silent, he told them how the Lord had brought him out of prison. He also said, “Tell these things to James and to the brothers.” Then he left and went to another place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:22–25; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:27; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:23"> When daybreak came, there was a great commotion among the soldiers as to what had become of Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:27; Psalms 37:32–33; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 12:10–11"> When Herod searched for him but did not find him, he questioned the guards and ordered them to be led away and executed. Then he went down from Judea to Caesarea and spent time there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Herod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:21–22; Ezekiel 27:17"> Now Herod was having an angry dispute with the people of Tyre and Sidon. So they came to him with one accord, and having won over Blastus, the king's personal attendant, they asked for peace, because their country was supplied with food from the king's country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> On an appointed day Herod dressed himself in royal clothing, sat down on the platform, and made a speech to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 14:10–13; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 13:4; THE LETTER OF JUDE 1:16"> The people kept shouting, “The voice of a god and not of a man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:25–26; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:4"> Immediately an angel of the Lord struck Herod down because he did not give<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="glory ¦ the glory ANT CT TR">a</a></sup>glory to God, and he was eaten by worms and breathed his last breath.</span>
