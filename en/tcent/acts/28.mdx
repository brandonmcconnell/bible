<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:31–32; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:29; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 22:24–25"> When they examined me, they were willing to release me because there were no grounds for death in my case.</span>
