<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:20–21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 11:53"> Some of the Epicurean and Stoic philosophers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="also ¦ — ANT TR">d</a></sup>also conversed with him.
