<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:25; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:12; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 10:1"> When it was decided that we would sail to Italy, they transferred Paul and some other prisoners to a centurion named Julius, of the Augustan Cohort.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:17"> For this very night an angel of the God to whom I belong and whom I serve stood beside me</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:11; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:16–17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:28"> and said, ‘Do not be afraid, Paul; yoʋ must stand before Caesar. <span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 4:20–21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:45; Numbers 23:19"> Therefore be of good cheer, men, for I have faith in God that it will happen just as I have been told.</span>

<div className="bp">

## The Shipwreck

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 33:23; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 27:29–30"> So they cut away the anchors and left them in the sea, at the same time loosening the bands of the rudders. <span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:25–26; Ezekiel 27:26; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 27:26–29"> But they struck a sandbar and ran the ship aground. The bow stuck fast and remained immovable, and the stern was being broken apart by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="force of the waves 97.2% ¦ forceful impact SBL WH 1.2%">o</a></sup>force of the waves.</span>
