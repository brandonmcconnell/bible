## Paul and Silas Imprisoned in Philippi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> One day, as we were on our way to prayer, we were met by a slave girl who had a spirit of divination. She had brought her masters much profit by fortune-telling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 5:7"> She followed along behind Paul and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="us ¦ Silas 𝔐pt ANT PCK">h</a></sup>us and kept crying out, “These men are servants of the Most High God, who proclaim to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="us ¦ you CT">i</a></sup>us a way of salvation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:17–19; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 3:6"> She kept on doing this for many days, and Paul became so annoyed that he turned and said to the spirit, “I command yoʋ in the name of Jesus Christ to come out of her.” And it came out that very hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 21:30; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 8:3"> When her masters saw that their hope for profit was gone, they seized Paul and Silas, dragged them to the marketplace, and set them before the authorities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 4:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:18–20; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 28:22"> After bringing them to the magistrates, they said, “These men are seriously disturbing our city. They are Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 3:8; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 26:3; Jeremiah 10:3"> and they are proclaiming customs that are not lawful for us as Romans to accept or practice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:2; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:23–25"> The crowd rose up together against them, and the magistrates tore their garments off them and ordered them to be beaten with rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:9; 1 Samuel 23:22–23"> After inflicting many blows upon them, they threw them into prison, ordering the jailer to keep them under close guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 13:27; Job 33:11; Jeremiah 29:26"> When he received this order, he put them in the inner prison and fastened their feet in the stocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:16–18; Psalms 119:62; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2"> About midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God, and the prisoners were listening to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 4:31; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 5:19; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 12:10"> Suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken. Immediately all the doors opened, and all the chains came loose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 12:19; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 27:5; Judges 9:54"> Then the jailer was awakened, and when he saw that the doors of the prison were open, he drew his sword and was about to kill himself, assuming that the prisoners had escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 23:34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 6:27–28"> But Paul cried out with a loud voice, “Do yoʋrself no harm, for we are all here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:9; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 24:25; Isaiah 60:14"> Calling for lights, the jailer rushed in and fell down trembling before Paul and Silas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:37; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 6:27–29; Isaiah 58:9"> Then he brought them out and said, “Sirs, what must I do to be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:12; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:38–39; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 3:36"> They said, “Believe in the Lord Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Christ ¦ — CT">j</a></sup>Christ, and yoʋ will be saved, yoʋ and yoʋr household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:27–28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:13–16"> Then they spoke the word of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="the Lord ¦ God WH">k</a></sup>the Lord to him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="and to ¦ together with CT">l</a></sup>and to everyone in his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:16; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:15; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:25"> He then took them in that hour of the night and washed their wounds. Then he was baptized at once, along with his entire household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:13; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 2:46; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:11"> After bringing Paul and Silas into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="his ¦ the CT">m</a></sup>his house, he set a meal before them. And he rejoiced, along with his entire household, because he had come to believe in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:22; Psalms 76:10; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 5:40"> When daybreak came, the magistrates sent their officers, saying, “Release those men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:33; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:27; 1 Samuel 1:17"> So the jailer reported<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="these ¦ their WH">n</a></sup>these words to Paul, saying, “The magistrates have sent orders for you to be released. Therefore come out now and go in peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 22:25–29; Psalms 58:1–2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:16"> But Paul said to them, “They beat us in public without a trial, even though we are Roman citizens, and they threw us into prison. And now they are sending us away secretly? Certainly not! Rather, let them come and escort us out themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 22:29; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 21:46; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 14:5"> So the officers reported these words to the magistrates, and the magistrates were afraid when they heard that the men were Roman citizens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 8:34; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 5:17; Daniel 6:23"> So they came and spoke to them in a conciliatory manner. As they escorted them out, they begged them to leave the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:2–3; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:14; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:22"> So Paul and Silas came out of the prison and went to Lydia's house. And after seeing the brothers and encouraging them, they departed.</span>
