## Paul and Barnabas in Iconium

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 18:4; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:51; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:46"> At Iconium Paul and Barnabas went together into the synagogue of the Jews and spoke in such a way that a great multitude of both Jews and Greeks believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:50; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 21:27–30; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:19"> But the Jews who refused to believe stirred up and corrupted the minds of the Gentiles to be against the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 2:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:18–20; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 20:32"> So Paul and Barnabas spent a long time there, speaking boldly for the Lord, who was testifying to the message of his grace<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="by ¦ and TR">a</a></sup>by granting signs and wonders to be done by their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 28:24; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:14; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 11:21–23"> But the population of the city was divided; some were with the Jews, and some were with the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:19; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:11; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:5"> And when an attempt was made by both the Gentiles and the Jews, together with their rulers, to mistreat them and stone them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:20–21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:23; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:11"> Paul and Barnabas became aware of it and fled for refuge to the Lycaonian cities of Lystra and Derbe and to the surrounding region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:21; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:10; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:2"> There they continued to preach the gospel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Barnabas in Lystra

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 3:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 9:1–2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 5:3–5"> Now there was a man sitting in Lystra who could not use his feet. He had been lame from his mother's womb and had never walked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 3:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 15:28; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 9:28–29"> This man listened to Paul as he was speaking. Paul looked at him intently, and when he saw that he had faith to be healed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 5:8–9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:12"> he said with a loud voice, “Stand up on yoʋr feet!” So the man leaped up and began to walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 28:6; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 8:10; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 12:22"> When the crowds saw what Paul had done, they lifted up their voices, saying in Lycaonian, “The gods have come down to us in the likeness of men.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 19:35"> Barnabas they called Zeus, and Paul they called Hermes, since he was the one who took the lead in speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 2:46; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:25"> Then the priest of the shrine of Zeus that was located in front of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="their ¦ the CT">b</a></sup>their city brought bulls and garlands to the city gates, intending to offer sacrifices together with the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 26:65; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:5–6; 2 Kings 5:7"> But when the apostles Barnabas and Paul heard about it, they tore their clothes and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="rushed ¦ rushed out CT">c</a></sup>rushed into the crowd, crying out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 10:26; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:4; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:17"> “Men, why are you doing these things? We also are men with the same nature as you. We are bringing you good news, telling you to turn from these worthless things to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="the ¦ a CT">d</a></sup>the living God, who made the heavens, the earth, the sea, and all that is in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:30; Psalms 81:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:28"> In past generations he allowed all the nations to go their own way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:19–20; Joel 2:23; Job 5:10"> although he did not leave himself without a witness in that he did good by giving<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="you ¦ us TR">e</a></sup>you rain from heaven and fruitful seasons, filling<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="our ¦ your ANT CT">f</a></sup>our hearts with food and gladness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16–17; Genesis 19:9; Genesis 11:6"> Yet even by saying these things, they barely restrained the crowds from offering sacrifices to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:25; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:11; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:50–51"> Then some Jews came from Antioch and Iconium and persuaded the crowds. They stoned Paul and dragged him out of the city, supposing that he was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:9–10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:9; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:6"> But when the disciples gathered around him, he rose and entered the city. The next day he went on with Barnabas to Derbe.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Barnabas Return to Antioch in Syria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:19; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 14:1; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:51"> After preaching the gospel in that city and making many disciples, Paul and Barnabas returned to Lystra, Iconium, and Antioch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 3:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:33"> strengthening the souls of the disciples, exhorting them to continue in the faith, and telling them that through many tribulations we must enter the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:5; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 11:30; THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:14"> And when they had appointed elders for them in every church, with prayer and fasting they entrusted them to the Lord in whom they had come to believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 13:13–14; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:38"> After passing through Pisidia, they came to Pamphylia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and when they had spoken the word in Perga, they went down to Attalia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:40; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 11:19; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:12"> From there they sailed to Antioch, where they had been committed to the grace of God for the work they had now completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:9; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:3"> After they arrived and gathered the church together, they reported all that God had done through them, and how he had opened a door of faith to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 11:26; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 15:35"> And they stayed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="there ¦ — CT">g</a></sup>there with the disciples for some time.</span>
</div>
