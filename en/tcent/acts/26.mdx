## Paul Tells of His Preaching to Jews and Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 21:30–31; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 21:27; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 25:3"> That is why<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="the ¦ some CT">j</a></sup>the Jews seized me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="in ¦ while I was in ECM† NA">k</a></sup>in the temple courts and were trying to kill me.</span>
