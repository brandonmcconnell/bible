## Paul's Trial Before Festus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:34; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:5; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:40"> Three days after arriving in the province, Festus went up to Jerusalem from Caesarea,</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:23"> But Festus replied that Paul was being kept in custody in Caesarea, and that he himself was about to go there shortly.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:17; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 27:19"> After staying among them for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="more than ¦ not more than eight or CT">c</a></sup>more than ten days, Festus went down to Caesarea. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:13; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 23:10; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:24"> When Paul arrived, the Jews who had come down from Jerusalem stood around him, bringing many serious charges<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="against him ¦ — CT">d</a></sup>against him that they could not prove.</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:27; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 12:3; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:20"> But Festus, wishing to gain favor with the Jews, responded to Paul, “Are yoʋ willing to go up to Jerusalem and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="stand trial before ¦ be tried by PCK">e</a></sup>stand trial before me there on these charges?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 27:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 28:18"> Paul said, “I am standing before the judgment seat of Caesar, where I ought to stand trial. I have done the Jews no wrong, as yoʋ yoʋrself know very well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 28:19; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:32; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:25"> If I am in the wrong and have done something that deserves death, I do not seek to escape death. But if none of the things these men accuse me of is true, no one can hand me over to them. I appeal to Caesar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:21; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:12–14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:20"> After conferring with the council, Festus answered, “Yoʋ have appealed to Caesar; to Caesar yoʋ shall go.”</span>

## Festus Consults King Agrippa

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:40; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:1; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:27–28"> After several days had passed, King Agrippa and Bernice arrived at Caesarea and greeted Festus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:27"> Since<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="he was staying … to him ¦ they were staying … to the king ANT CT PCK TR">f</a></sup>he was staying there for several days, Festus presented Paul's case to him, saying, “There is a man who was left as a prisoner by Felix.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:1–3; Esther 3:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 18:3–5"> When I was in Jerusalem, the chief priests and the elders of the Jews presented their case and asked for a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="judgment ¦ sentence of condemnation CT">g</a></sup>judgment against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:51; Deuteronomy 19:17–18; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:30"> I answered them that it is not the custom of the Romans to hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="anyone over for destruction 89.9% ¦ over anyone CT 7.4%">h</a></sup>anyone over for destruction before the accused faces his accusers and has an opportunity to make his defense concerning the charges.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When his accusers stood up, they brought no charge against him of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="the ¦ any evils of the SBL WH ¦ any evil of the NA TH">i</a></sup>the sort I was expecting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:29; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:15; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:3–4"> Instead they had certain points of disagreement with him about their own religion and about a man named Jesus who was dead, but whom Paul claimed was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:9"> Since I was at a loss as to how to investigate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="this matter ¦ these matters CT PCK">j</a></sup>this matter, I asked if he might be willing to go to Jerusalem and stand trial there on these charges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 2:1; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:10–11"> But Paul appealed to be kept in custody for the decision of His Majesty the Emperor, so I ordered him to be held until I could send him to Caesar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:15; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:18; Isaiah 52:15"> Then Agrippa said to Festus, “I would like to hear the man myself.” Festus said, “Tomorrow yoʋ will hear him.”</span>

## Paul Brought Before King Agrippa

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:30; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:16"> So the next day Agrippa and Bernice came with great pomp and entered the auditorium along with the military commanders and the distinguished men of the city. Then Festus gave the command, and Paul was brought in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 22:22; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:7; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:2–3"> Festus said, “King Agrippa and all you men who are present with us, you see this man, concerning whom the entire Jewish community has appealed to me, both in Jerusalem and here in this place, crying out that he ought not to live any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:29; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:11–12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 23:4"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="when ¦ — CT">k</a></sup>when I found that he had done nothing deserving death, and since he himself appealed to His Majesty the Emperor, I decided to send him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:2–3"> Yet I have nothing definite to write to my lord about this man. Therefore I have brought him before you all, and especially before yoʋ, King Agrippa, so that after we have had this preliminary hearing, I may have something to write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:51; Proverbs 18:13"> For it seems unreasonable to me to send a prisoner without indicating the charges against him.”</span>
