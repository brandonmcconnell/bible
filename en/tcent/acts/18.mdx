## Apollos Speaks Boldly in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:5–6"> Meanwhile a Jew named Apollos, an Alexandrian by birth, arrived in Ephesus. He was an eloquent man, well-versed in the Scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 19:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:11; Hosea 14:9"> He had been instructed in the way of the Lord.
