## Paul in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:23–28; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:12"> While Apollos was in Corinth, Paul went through the interior regions and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="came ¦ came down NA">a</a></sup>came to Ephesus, where he came across some disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:39; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 11:15–17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 3:5"> He said to them, “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?” They said to him, “No, we have not even heard that there is a Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:25; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:13; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:16"> So Paul said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="to them 64.4% ¦ — CT PCK 18.3%">b</a></sup>to them, “Into what then were you baptized?” They said, “Into John's baptism.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:29–34; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 11:16; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 1:5"> Then Paul said, “John baptized with a baptism of repentance, telling the people to believe in the one who was to come after him, that is, in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Christ Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ ANT ¦ Jesus CT">c</a></sup>Christ Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:12; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 6:3–4"> On hearing this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 6:6; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 2:4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17"> And when Paul laid his hands on them, the Holy Spirit came upon them, and they began speaking in tongues and prophesying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> There were about twelve men in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 28:23; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 1:3; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:20–22"> Over a period of three months Paul would go into the synagogue and speak boldly, reasoning and persuading the people about the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:23; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 9:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:6–8"> But some became hardened and refused to believe, speaking evil of the Way in front of the congregation. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:31; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:15; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:6"> This continued for two years, so that all who dwelt in Asia, both Jews and Greeks, heard the word of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Jesus ¦ — CT">e</a></sup>Jesus.</span>

## The Sons of Sceva

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17–20; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:18–19"> Now God was performing extraordinary miracles by the hands of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 16:17"> so that even cloths or aprons that had touched his skin were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="placed upon ¦ carried off to CT">f</a></sup>placed upon the sick, and their diseases left them, and evil spirits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="came out of them ¦ went out CT">g</a></sup>came out of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 12:27; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 9:38; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 11:19"> Then some of the itinerant Jewish exorcists<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="attempted ¦ also attempted CT">h</a></sup>attempted to invoke the name of the Lord Jesus over those who had evil spirits. They would say, “<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="We ¦ I CT">i</a></sup>We adjure you by the Jesus whom Paul preaches.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish high priest, were doing this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:17–18; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 4:33–35; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 1:34"> But one day an evil spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="responded ¦ responded to them CT">j</a></sup>responded, “I know Jesus, and I am acquainted with Paul, but who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 8:29; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 5:3–4; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 5:15"> Then the man who had the evil spirit jumped on them, overpowered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="them ¦ them all CT &#123;Note: The reading of CT is literally them both. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:12; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11"> When this became known to all the Jews and Greeks who dwelt in Ephesus, fear fell upon them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was being magnified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 10:10; Psalms 32:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 3:6"> Many who had become believers came forward, confessing and disclosing their practices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> while many who practiced magical arts brought their books together and began burning them in front of everyone. <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 12:24; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> In this way the word of the Lord was increasing and prevailing mightily.</span>

## The Riot at Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:22; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:16; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:11"> After these things had taken place, Paul resolved in his spirit to pass through Macedonia and Achaia and go on to Jerusalem, saying, “After I have been there, I must see Rome also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:23; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:20; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:3"> So he sent two of his helpers, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia, while he himself stayed in Asia for a period of time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:9; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 24:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> About that time a great disturbance arose concerning the Way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:9–10; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:16; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:19"> For a man named Demetrius, a silversmith who made silver shrines of Artemis, brought in a great deal of business to the craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 18:11–19"> He gathered these men together, along with those who worked in similar trades, and said, “Men, you know that our wealth is derived from this business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:29; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> You also see and hear that, not only in Ephesus, but in nearly all of Asia, this man Paul has persuaded and drawn away a considerable crowd, saying that gods made by hands are not gods at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:5; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 23:13"> Not only is there the danger that this trade of ours might come into disrepute, but also that the temple of the great goddess Artemis might be regarded as nothing, and that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="her magnificence might be brought down ¦ she might be deposed of her magnificence CT">l</a></sup>her magnificence might be brought down, whom all Asia and the world worship.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 13:4; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 17:13; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:19–24"> When they heard this, they were filled with rage and began crying out, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:10; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 27:2"> So the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="whole ¦ — CT">m</a></sup>whole city was filled with confusion, and they rushed with one accord into the amphitheater, seizing Gaius and Aristarchus, Macedonians who were Paul's traveling companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:22–31"> Although Paul wanted to go into the public assembly, the disciples would not let him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:10; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:12; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:6"> Even some of the provincial officials of Asia, who were friends of Paul, sent word to him, begging him not to venture into the amphitheater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 21:34; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:40; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:7–9"> Now some were shouting one thing, and some were shouting another, for the assembly was confused, and the majority did not know for what reason they had come together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 12:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:20"> So the Jews put forward Alexander, who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="was then brought before ¦ then gave instructions to CT">n</a></sup>was then brought before the crowd. Alexander motioned with his hand, wishing to make a defense to the public assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 19:28; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:7; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 13:4"> But when they realized he was a Jew, a single outcry arose from them all as they shouted for about two hours, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:10–11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:12"> When the city clerk had quieted the crowd, he said, “Men of Ephesus, who is there that does not know that the city of the Ephesians is the temple keeper of the great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="goddess ¦ — CT">o</a></sup>goddess Artemis and of the image that fell down from Zeus?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> Therefore, since these facts are undeniable, you must keep calm and not do anything rash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 2:22; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 25:8"> For you have brought these men here who are neither temple robbers nor blasphemers of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="your ¦ our CT">p</a></sup>your goddess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 13:7; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:1; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 18:14"> So if Demetrius and his fellow craftsmen have a complaint against anyone, the courts are open, and there are proconsuls; let them bring charges against one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But if you seek anything<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="concerning other matters 91.7% ¦ further CT 4.7%">q</a></sup>concerning other matters, it must be settled in a lawful assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 17:5–8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 26:5; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:1"> For we are in danger of being charged with rioting in connection with today's events, since there is no reason we can give to account for this disorderly gathering.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:17; Psalms 65:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> And after saying this, he dismissed the assembly.</span>
</div>
