<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 9:50"> I urge Euodia and Syntyche to be of the same mind in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:5; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 13:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 10:20"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Yes, ¦ — TR">a</a></sup>Yes, I ask yoʋ also, my true companion, to assist these women, who have contended for the gospel at my side, along with Clement and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:16–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:12; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2–4"> Rejoice in the Lord always, and again I say, rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 5:8–9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:2"> Let your gentleness be known to everyone. The Lord is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:7; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:25–33"> Be anxious about nothing, but in everything, by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, make your requests known to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 14:27; Isaiah 26:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:16"> And the peace of God that surpasses all understanding will guard your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 3:17; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 2:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:9–21"> Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is right, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if anything is virtuous or praiseworthy, think about such things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 4:1–8"> Practice what you have learned and received from me, and what you have heard and seen in me. And the God of peace will be with you.</span>

## Acknowledgment of the Philippians' Gift

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:6; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:6–7"> I have great joy in the Lord that now at last you have revived your concern for me. Indeed, you were concerned but had no opportunity to show it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:6–9; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:5–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 6:31–34"> Not that I am speaking about being in need, for I have learned to be content in whatever circumstances I find myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:29; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:7–10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:27"> I know how to be brought low, and I know how to abound. I have learned the secret of being content in any and every situation, whether well fed or hungry, whether having plenty or being in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:11"> I can do all things through<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Christ ¦ him CT">b</a></sup>Christ who strengthens me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 10:34"> Nevertheless, you did well by sharing with me in my affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:8–12; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 16:40–5"> As you Philippians know, in the early days of the gospel, when I set out from Macedonia, no church partnered with me in the matter of giving and receiving except you alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:9; THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES 17:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:18"> Even when I was in Thessalonica, you sent me help for my needs more than once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 3:14; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 6:10; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:9–13"> Not that I seek the gift, but I seek the fruit that abounds to your account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:16; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:12"> I have received everything in full and have an abundance. I am amply supplied, now that I have received from Epaphroditus the gifts you sent. They are an aroma of a sweet fragrance, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:8–11; Psalms 84:11; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10"> And my God will fully supply your every need through Christ Jesus according to his riches in glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:36; Psalms 115:1; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 14:7"> Glory be to our God and Father forever and ever! Amen.</span>
</div>
