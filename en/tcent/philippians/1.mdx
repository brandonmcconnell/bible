## Paul's Prayer for the Philippians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:2–3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:4–5"> I give thanks to my God every time I remember you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF JOHN 1:4"> always praying with joy in every one of my prayers for you all</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:14–15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:7; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 3:14"> because of your partnership with me in the gospel from the first day until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:12"> I am confident that he who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus HF NA SBL TH RP">b</a></sup>Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 7:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:5; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 4:18"> It is right for me to think this way about you all, since I have you in my heart. For you all share with me in God's grace, both in my imprisonment and in my defense and confirmation of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 1:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:4"> For God is my witness that I long for you all with the affection of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Jesus Christ ¦ Christ Jesus CT">c</a></sup>Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 1:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–6"> And I pray that your love may abound more and more in knowledge and all discernment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:13; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:23"> so that you may approve what is excellent in order to be pure and blameless in the day of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:8; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 15:4–5"> filled with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="fruits … come 63.8% ¦ fruit … comes CT 26.5%">d</a></sup>fruits of righteousness that come through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul's Imprisonment Advances the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 11:19–21"> Now I want you to know, brothers, that what has happened to me has actually helped advance the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 2:9; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:1; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:7"> so that it has become known among the entire imperial guard and to everyone else that my imprisonment is for Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 6:19–20; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:2; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 1:74"> And the greater part of the brothers in the Lord have gained confidence through my imprisonment and are far more bold to speak the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="word 72.9% ¦ word of God WH 16.3%">e</a></sup>word without fear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:17–18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:13"> Some preach Christ out of envy and rivalry, but others do it out of good will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:1–2; Psalms 69:26"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="&#123;verse 16 before verse 17&#125; 85.1% ¦ &#123;verse 17 before verse 16&#125; NA SBL TH 9.4%">f</a></sup>The former preach Christ out of selfish ambition, not sincerely but intending to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="add affliction to 90.9% ¦ raise up affliction for CT 4.3% &#123;Note: In CT this variant is in verse 17.&#125;">g</a></sup>add affliction to me in my imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:11–12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:7"> The latter do it out of love, knowing that I am appointed for the defense of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 9:50; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MARK 9:38–40; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:11"> But what does it matter? Only that in every way, whether in pretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed, and in that I rejoice. Yes, and I will continue to rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:28; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:11"> For I know that this will result in my deliverance through your prayers and the provision of the Spirit of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 6:20; Isaiah 54:4"> My eager expectation and hope is that I will not be put to shame in anything, but will speak with complete boldness so that now, as always, Christ will be magnified in my body, whether I live or die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:1"> For to me, to live is Christ and to die is gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:24"> But if I am to live in the flesh, it will mean fruit from my labor. Yet I do not know which I prefer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:8; Psalms 17:15; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 14:13"> I am hard pressed between the two. I long to depart and be with Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="which ¦ for that ANT CT ELZ">h</a></sup>which is far better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:7; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:25–26"> But to remain in the flesh is more necessary for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:24; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:11–13"> Convinced of this, I know that I will remain and continue with you all for the sake of your progress and joy in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:14; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:12; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> so that your boasting may abound in Christ Jesus because of me when I come to you again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:1; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:13–14; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:10"> Only live as citizens in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or am absent, I may hear that you are standing firm in one spirit, striving together with one mind for the faith of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:7–8; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 1:5–6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 10:28"> and not being intimidated in any way by those who oppose you. For them this is an indication of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="destruction, but for you it is an indication of ¦ their destruction, but of your CT">i</a></sup>destruction, but for you it is an indication of salvation, and that from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:13; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 5:41"> For it has been granted to you on behalf of Christ not only to believe in him, but also to suffer for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 2:2; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 16:19–40; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:7"> since you are experiencing the same conflict that you saw I had and now hear that I still have.</span>
</div>
