## Righteousness Through Faith in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:4; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2"> Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord! To write this to you again is no trouble for me, and it is safe for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:13; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO TITUS 1:16; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 22:15"> Watch out for the dogs, watch out for the evil workers, watch out for those who mutilate the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:11; Deuteronomy 30:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 7:6"> For we are the circumcision, we who worship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="by the Spirit of God ¦ God by the Spirit TR">a</a></sup>by the Spirit of God, who boast in Christ Jesus, and who put no confidence in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:18–22"> even though I myself might put confidence in the flesh. If anyone else presumes to have confidence in the flesh, I have more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 11:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 11:1; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:6"> circumcised on the eighth day, of the people of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew of Hebrews; as to the law, a Pharisee;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 1:13–14; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 8:3; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 26:9–10"> as to zeal, a persecutor of the church; as to righteousness under the law, blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 14:33; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:26; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 13:44–46"> But whatever was gain to me I have counted as loss for the sake of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:7"> More than that, I count all things as loss because of the surpassing value of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For his sake I have suffered the loss of all things and count them as rubbish so that I may gain Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 5:21; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:30; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:16"> and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the law, but the righteousness that is through faith in Christ, which comes from God on the basis of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:20; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:1–2"> I want to know Christ and the power of his resurrection and the fellowship of his sufferings, becoming like him in his death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 20:35–36; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 20:5; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 23:6"> in the hope of attaining the resurrection from the dead.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Pressing On Toward the Goal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 6:11–12; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–8; Psalms 138:8"> Not that I have already obtained it or have already been made perfect, but I press on to take hold of that for which Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Jesus ¦ — SBL">b</a></sup>Jesus has taken hold of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 9:62; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:12"> Brothers, I do not consider myself to have taken hold of it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="yet ¦ — NA HF RP SBL TH TR">c</a></sup>yet. But one thing I do: Forgetting what is behind and reaching forward to what is ahead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:10; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:21"> I press on toward the goal to win the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 7:17; THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:4–5; Proverbs 2:3–6"> Therefore all of us who are mature should adopt this mindset, and if you adopt a different mindset about anything, God will reveal that to you as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 2:6; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 3:3; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 2:4–5"> Nevertheless, let us live up to the same standard that we have already<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="attained; let us adopt the same mindset. 81.2% ¦ attained. CT 1.6%">d</a></sup>attained; let us adopt the same mindset.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 5:3"> Join together in imitating me, brothers, and pay close attention to those who walk according to the example you have in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 4:17; Isaiah 8:11; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 6:12"> For I have often told you, and tell you now with tears, that many walk as enemies of the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 16:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 8:5–7; THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 2:3"> Their end is destruction, their god is their belly, and their glory is in their shame. Their minds are set on earthly things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 2:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:1–3; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:18–1"> But our citizenship is in heaven, and we eagerly await from there a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 3:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:4; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:42–56"> who will transform our lowly bodies to be like his glorious body, by the power that enables him to subject all things to himself.</span>
