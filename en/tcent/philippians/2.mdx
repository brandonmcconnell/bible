## Imitating Christ's Humility

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE COLOSSIANS 3:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:7–8"> So if there is any encouragement in Christ, if there is any comfort from love, if there is any fellowship in the Spirit, if there is any compassion or mercy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:8–9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 1:10"> make my joy complete by adopting the same mindset, having the same love, being united in spirit, and having one purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 5:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 5:21"> Do nothing from selfish ambition or vain conceit, but in humility regard others as more important than yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:1; THE LETTER OF JAMES 2:8"> None of you should look out for your own interests, but for the interests of others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 2:6; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 11:29; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:21"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Let the same mind be in you ¦ Have the same mind in yourselves CT">a</a></sup>Let the same mind be in you that was in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 5:18; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:4; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 1:3"> who, though he was in the form of God, did not consider equality with God as something to be exploited,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 8:9; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 20:28; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 1:14"> but emptied himself by taking the form of a servant and being born in the likeness of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 10:18; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 12:2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 5:19"> And being found in appearance as a man, he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="THE REVELATION TO JOHN 19:16; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 28:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 3:22"> Therefore God highly exalted him and gave him the name that is above every other name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 45:23–25; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 28:18; THE REVELATION TO JOHN 5:13–14"> so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 4:15; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 14:11; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 13:13"> and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Shining as Lights in the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PETER 1:5–10; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO LUKE 13:23–24; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 15:58"> Therefore, my beloved friends, just as you have always obeyed, not only in my presence, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:21; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 3:5; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:9"> for God is the one at work in you, granting you the will and power to do so, according to his good pleasure.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 4:9; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> Do everything without grumbling or arguing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:14–16; THE FIRST LETTER OF PETER 2:12; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 5:45"> so that you may be blameless and pure, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you shine as lights in the world</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 9:26; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE GALATIANS 2:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF JOHN 1:1"> as you hold firmly to the word of life. Then I will have a reason to boast in the day of Christ that I did not run or labor in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 4:6; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 15:16; THE LETTER TO THE HEBREWS 13:15–16"> But even if I am being poured out as a drink offering upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I am glad and rejoice with you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="THE LETTER OF JAMES 1:2–4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 3:1"> In the same way you also should be glad and rejoice with me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Timothy and Epaphroditus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:17; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:6–8; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 3:2"> I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon so that I too may be encouraged when I receive news about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:2; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:10; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:22"> I have no one else like-minded who will be genuinely concerned about your welfare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 10:24; THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO MATTHEW 16:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:4"> For they all seek their own interests, not those of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Christ Jesus ¦ Jesus Christ NA SBL TH">b</a></sup>Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 4:17; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 2:9; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:2"> But you know Timothy's proven character, how as a son with a father he has served with me in the work of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:3; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:19"> Therefore I hope to send him to you at once, as soon as I find out what is going to happen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="THE THIRD LETTER OF JOHN 1:14; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:22; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 1:25–26"> And I am confident in the Lord that I myself will also come soon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:18; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO PHILEMON 1:1–2; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:3"> But for now I thought it necessary to send back to you Epaphroditus—my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier, who is also your messenger and minister to my need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE ROMANS 12:15; Proverbs 12:25; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE EPHESIANS 3:13"> For he has been longing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="to see ¦ for ANT HF NA PCK RP SBL TH TR">c</a></sup>to see you all and was distressed because you heard that he was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:30; Psalms 103:3–4"> Indeed he was sick, almost to the point of death. But God had mercy on him, and not only on him but also on me, so that I would not have one sorrow after another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO JOHN 16:22; THE SECOND LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 1:4; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 2:26–27"> Therefore I am all the more eager to send him, so that when you see him again you can rejoice and I can be less anxious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE THESSALONIANS 5:12; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:18; THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO TIMOTHY 5:17"> So receive him in the Lord with all joy, and honor men like him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="THE FIRST LETTER OF PAUL TO THE CORINTHIANS 16:17; THE ACTS OF THE HOLY APOSTLES 20:24; THE LETTER OF PAUL TO THE PHILIPPIANS 4:10"> because he drew near to death for the work of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Christ ¦ the Lord WH">d</a></sup>Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="disregarding ¦ risking CT">e</a></sup>disregarding his own life to complete what was lacking in your service to me.</span>
