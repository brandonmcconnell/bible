## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The number and offices of the singers and players on musical instruments; and their division by lot into twenty-four courses, vv. 1-31.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:1](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v1)</div>
<p>David and the captains of the host - The chiefs of those who formed the several orders: not military captains.</p>
<p>Should prophesy - Should accompany their musical instruments with prayer and singing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:2](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v2)</div>
<p>Which prophesied - Sung hymns and prayed. But the Targum understands this of prophesying in the proper sense of the term; and therefore says, "Who prophesied by the Holy Spirit." Jarchi is of the same opinion and quotes the case of Elisha, Kg2 3:15; While the minstrel played, the hand of the Lord &#91;i.e., the spirit of prophecy&#93; was upon him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:3](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v3)</div>
<p>The sons of Jeduthun - six - That is, six with their father, otherwise, there are but five. Hence it is said, they were under the hands of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with a harp, etc.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:5](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v5)</div>
<p>To lift up the horn - "The horn of prophecy," says Jarchi; "to sound with the trumpet in the words of prophecy before the Lord." - T.</p>
<p>Three daughters - These also were employed among the singers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:7](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v7)</div>
<p>Two hundred fourscore and eight - That it twelve classes of twenty-four Levites each; for two hundred and eighty-eight divided by twelve quotes twenty-four.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:9](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v9)</div>
<p>For Asaph to Joseph - His first-born.</p>
<p>The second to Gedaliah - The first-born of Jeduthun.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:10](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v10)</div>
<p>The third to Zaccur - The first-born of Asaph.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:11](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v11)</div>
<p>The fourth to Izri - The second son of Jeduthun.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:12](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v12)</div>
<p>The fifth to Nethaniah - The third son of Asaph. Thus we find the lot did not run in any particular kind of order.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:14](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v14)</div>
<p>Jesharelah - Supposed to be the same with Uzziel, son of Heman.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 25:31](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/25#v31)</div>
<p>Romamti-ezer - Both these names belong to the same person. He is mentioned also Ch1 25:4.</p>
<p>With this immense parade of noise and show, (David's own invention), Christianity has nothing to do.</p>
</div>

</div>
