## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>David consults Nathan about building a temple for God, Ch1 17:1, Ch1 17:2. God sends him an answer by Nathan, informing him that Solomon shall build the house, Ch1 17:3-14. David receives the Divine purpose with humility and joy, and gives God praise, Ch1 17:15-27.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:1](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v1)</div>
<p>Now it came to pass - See every thing recorded in this chapter amply detailed in the notes on Sa2 7:1 (note), etc.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:5](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v5)</div>
<p>But have gone from tent to tent - "I have transferred my tabernacle from Gilgal to Nob, from Nob to Shiloh, and from Shiloh to Gibeon." - Targum and Jarchi.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:9](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v9)</div>
<p>Neither shall the children of wickedness - They shall no more be brought into servitude as they were in the time they sojourned in Egypt. This is what is here referred to.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:12](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v12)</div>
<p>I will establish his throne for ever - David was a type of Christ; and concerning him the prophecy is literally true. See Isa 9:7, where there is evidently the same reference.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:13](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v13)</div>
<p>I will not take my mercy away from him - I will not cut off his family from the throne, as I did that of his predecessor Saul.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:16](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v16)</div>
<p>And what is mine house, that thou hast brought me hitherto? - I am not of any regal family, and have no natural right to the throne.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:25](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v25)</div>
<p>Hath found in his heart to pray - The Targum expresses a full sense: "Therefore thy servant hath found an opening of mouth, that he might pray before Thee.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 17:27](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/17#v27)</div>
<p>For thou blessest, O Lord - "Thou beginnest to bless the house of thy servant, therefore it shall be blessed for ever." - T.</p>
<p>The reader is requested to refer to 2 Samuel 7 (note), and the notes there for many particulars that belong to the parallel places here, and which it should answer no good purpose to repeat in this place.</p>
</div>
