## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>David smites the Philistines, and takes Gath, Ch1 18:1. Reduces the Moabites, Ch1 18:2. Vanquishes Hadarezer, king of Zobah, Ch1 18:3, Ch1 18:4. Overcomes the Syrians of Damascus, and takes several of their cities, Ch1 18:5-8. Tou, king of Hamath, congratulates him on his victory, and sends him vessels of silver, gold, and brass, Ch1 18:9, Ch1 18:10. Those and the different spoils he had taken from the conquered nations, he dedicates to God, Ch1 18:11. Abishai defeats the Edomites, Ch1 18:12, Ch1 18:13. David reigns over all Israel, Ch1 18:14. His officers, Ch1 18:15-17.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 18:1](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/18#v1)</div>
<p>David - took Gath and her towns - See the comparison between this chapter and Sa2 8:1 (note), etc., in the notes on the latter.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 18:2](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/18#v2)</div>
<p>Brought gifts - Were laid under tribute.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 18:9](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/18#v9)</div>
<p>Tou king of Hamath - Called Toi in Sa2 8:9.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 18:12](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/18#v12)</div>
<p>Abishai - slew of the Edomites - This victory is attributed to David, Sa2 8:13. He sent Abishai against them, and he defeated them: this is with great propriety attributed to David as commander-in-chief; qui facit per alterum, facit per se.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 18:15](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/18#v15)</div>
<p>Joab - was over the host - General-in-chief.</p>
<p>Jehoshaphat - recorder - The king's remembrancer, or historiographer royal.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 18:16](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/18#v16)</div>
<p>Zadok - and Abimelech - priests - Both high priests; one at Gibeon, and the other at Jerusalem, as we have seen Ch1 16:39.</p>
<p>Shavsha was scribe - Called Seraiah, Sa2 8:17.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 18:17](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/18#v17)</div>
<p>Cherethites and the Pelethites - See the note on Sa2 8:18.</p>
<p>The Targum says, "Benaiah was over the great Sanhedrin and the small Sanhedrin, and consulted Urim and Thummim. And at his command the archers and slingers went to battle."</p>
<p>The sons of David - These were the highest in authority.</p>
</div>
