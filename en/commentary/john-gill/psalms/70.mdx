<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 70:1](/en/kjv/psalms/70#v1)</div>
<p>Make haste, O God, to deliver me,.... The phrase, "make haste", is supplied from the following clause in Psalm 40:13; it is, "be pleased, O Lord", or "Jehovah". The Targum renders it, "to deliver us"; very wrongly;</p>
<p>make haste to help me, O Lord; See Gill on Psalm 22:19.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 70:2](/en/kjv/psalms/70#v2)</div>
<p>Let them be ashamed and confounded,.... In Psalm 40:14 it is added, "together"; See Gill on Psalm 40:14;</p>
<p>that seek after my soul; or "life"; in Psalm 40:14 it is added, "to destroy it"; for that was the end of their seeking after it;</p>
<p>let them be turned backward, &amp;c. See Gill on Psalm 40:14.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 70:3](/en/kjv/psalms/70#v3)</div>
<p>Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame,.... In Psalm 40:15 it is, "let them be desolate"; which seems to respect their land and houses, here their persons; See Gill on Psalm 40:15;</p>
<p>that say; in Psalm 40:15 it is added, "to me"; not to his people, but himself,</p>
<p>aha, aha: rejoicing at his calamity and distress. The Targum is,</p>
<p>"we are glad, we are glad;''</p>
<p>See Gill on Psalm 40:15, and compare with this Ezekiel 25:3.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 70:4](/en/kjv/psalms/70#v4)</div>
<p>Let all those that seek thee,.... The Targum is,</p>
<p>"that seek doctrine (or instruction) from thee.''</p>
<p>rejoice and be glad in thee; the Targum paraphrases it,</p>
<p>"rejoice and be glad in thy word.''</p>
<p>and let such as love thy salvation say continually, let God be magnified; the Targum is,</p>
<p>"let the glory of the Lord be increased;''</p>
<p>and in Psalm 40:16, instead of "God", it is "the Lord", or "Jehovah": See Gill on Psalm 40:16.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 70:5](/en/kjv/psalms/70#v5)</div>
<p>But I am poor and needy,.... In Psalm 40:17 it follows, yet "the Lord thinketh on me"; instead of which it is here; see Gill on Psalm 40:17;</p>
<p>make haste unto me, O God; which repeats for sense the same petition as in Psalm 71:1;</p>
<p>thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying; in Psalm 40:17 it is, "O my God".</p>
</div>
