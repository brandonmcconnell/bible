## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 14:1-35)</p>
<p>Every wise, &amp;c.--literally, "The wisdoms" (compare Pro 9:1) "of women," plural, a distributive form of speech.</p>
<p>buildeth . . . house--increases wealth, which the foolish, by mismanagement, lessen.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v2)</div>
<p>uprightness--is the fruit of fearing God, as falsehood and ill-nature (Pro 2:15; Pro 3:32) of despising Him and His law.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v3)</div>
<p>rod of pride--that is, the punishment of pride, which they evince by their words. The words of the wise procure good to them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v4)</div>
<p>crib is clean--empty; so "cleanness of teeth" denotes want of food (compare Amo 4:6). Men get the proper fruit of their doings (Gal 6:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v5)</div>
<p>A faithful witness, &amp;c.--one tested to be such.</p>
<p>utter lies--or, "breathe out lies"--that is, habitually lies (Pro 6:19; compare Act 9:1). Or the sense is, that habitual truthfulness, or lying, will be evinced in witness-bearing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v6)</div>
<p>An humble, teachable spirit succeeds in seeking (Pro 8:9; Joh 7:17; Jam 1:5-6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v7)</div>
<p>Avoid the society of those who cannot teach you.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v8)</div>
<p>Appearances deceive the thoughtless, but the prudent discriminate.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v9)</div>
<p>Fools make a mock at sin--or, "Sin deludes fools."</p>
<p>righteous . . . favour--that is, of God, instead of the punishment of sin.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v10)</div>
<p>Each one best knows his own sorrows or joys.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v11)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 12:7). The contrast of the whole is enhanced by that of house and tabernacle, a permanent and a temporary dwelling.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v12)</div>
<p>end thereof--or, "reward," what results (compare Pro 5:4).</p>
<p>ways of death--leading to it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v13)</div>
<p>The preceding sentiment illustrated by the disappointments of a wicked or untimely joy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v14)</div>
<p>filled . . . ways--receive retribution (Pro 1:31).</p>
<p>a good man . . . himself--literally, "is away from such," will not associate with him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v15)</div>
<p>The simple . . . word--He is credulous, not from love, but heedlessness (Pro 13:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v16)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:7; Pro 28:14).</p>
<p>rageth--acts proudly and conceitedly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v17)</div>
<p>He . . . angry--literally, "short of anger" (compare Pro 14:29, opposite idea).</p>
<p>man . . . hated--that is, the deliberate evildoer is more hated than the rash.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v18)</div>
<p>inherit--as a portion (compare Pro 3:35).</p>
<p>are crowned--literally, "are surrounded with it," abound in it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v19)</div>
<p>Describes the humbling of the wicked by the punishment their sins incur.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v20)</div>
<p>This sad but true picture of human nature is not given approvingly, but only as a fact.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v21)</div>
<p>For such contempt of the poor is contrasted as sinful with the virtuous compassion of the good.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v22)</div>
<p>As usual, the interrogative negative strengthens the affirmative.</p>
<p>mercy and truth--that is, God's (Psa 57:3; Psa 61:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v23)</div>
<p>labour--painful diligence.</p>
<p>talk . . . penury--idle and vain promises and plans.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v24)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:16).</p>
<p>foolishness . . . folly--Folly remains, or produces folly; it has no benefit.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v25)</div>
<p>Life often depends on truth-telling.</p>
<p>a deceitful . . . lies--He that breathes out lies is deceit, not to be trusted (Pro 14:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v26)</div>
<p>The blessings of piety descend to children (Pro 13:22; Pro 20:7; Exo 20:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v27)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 13:14).</p>
<p>fear of the Lord--or, "law of the wise," is wisdom (Psa 111:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v28)</div>
<p>The teaching of a true political economy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v29)</div>
<p>slow . . . understanding--(Compare Pro 14:17).</p>
<p>hasty--(Compare Pro 14:17).</p>
<p>exalteth folly--makes it conspicuous, as if delighting to honor it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v30)</div>
<p>A sound heart--both literally and figuratively, a source of health; in the latter sense, opposed to the known effect of evil passions on health.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v31)</div>
<p>reproacheth his Maker--who is the God of such, as well as of the rich (Pro 22:2; Job 31:15; and specially Sa1 2:8; Psa 113:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v32)</div>
<p>driven--thrust out violently (compare Psa 35:5-6).</p>
<p>hath hope--trusteth (Pro 10:2; Pro 11:4; Psa 2:12), implying assurance of help.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:33](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v33)</div>
<p>resteth--preserved in quietness for use, while fools blazon their folly (Pro 12:23; Pro 13:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v34" data-v="34">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:34](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v34)</div>
<p>Righteousness--just principles and actions.</p>
<p>exalteth--raises to honor.</p>
<p>is a reproach--brings on them the ill-will of others (compare Pro 13:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v35" data-v="35">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 14:35](/en/kjv/proverbs/14#v35)</div>
<p>wise--discreet or prudent.</p>
<p>causeth shame-- (Pro 10:5; Pro 12:4) acts basely.</p>
