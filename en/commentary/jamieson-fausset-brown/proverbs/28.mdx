## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 28:1-28)</p>
<p>A bad conscience makes men timid; the righteous are alone truly bold (Pro 14:26; Psa 27:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v2)</div>
<p>Anarchy producing contending rulers shortens the reign of each.</p>
<p>but by a man . . . prolonged--or, "by a man of understanding--that is, a good ruler--he who knows or regards the right, that is, a good citizen, shall prolong (his days)." Good rulers are a blessing to the people. Bad government as a punishment for evil is contrasted with good as blessing to the good.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v3)</div>
<p>A poor man, &amp;c.--Such, in power, exact more severely, and so leave subjects bare.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v4)</div>
<p>They that forsake . . . wicked--Wrongdoers encourage one another.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Joh 7:17). Ignorance of moral truth is due to unwillingness to know it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v6)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:6). Riches cannot compensate for sin, nor the want of them affect integrity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v7)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 17:25).</p>
<p>riotous men--or, "gluttons" (Pro 23:20-21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v8)</div>
<p>usury . . . unjust gain--(Compare Margin). The two terms, meaning nearly the same, may denote excessive interest. God's providence directs the proper use of wealth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v9)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:8; Pro 21:27).</p>
<p>hearing--that is, obeying. God requires sincere worshippers (Psa 66:18; Joh 4:24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v10)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 26:27).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v11)</div>
<p>A poor but wise man can discover (and expose) the rich and self-conceited.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v12)</div>
<p>great glory--or, cause for it to a people, for the righteous rejoice in good, and righteousness exalts a nation (Pro 14:34).</p>
<p>a man . . . hidden--that is, the good retire, or all kinds try to escape a wicked rule.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v13)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 32:3-5). Concealment of sin delivers none from God's wrath, but He shows mercy to the humble penitent (Psa 51:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v14)</div>
<p>feareth--that is, God, and so repents.</p>
<p>hardeneth his heart--makes himself insensible to sin, and so will not repent (Pro 14:16; Pro 29:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v15)</div>
<p>The rapacity and cruelty of such beasts well represent some wicked men (compare Psa 7:2; Psa 17:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v16)</div>
<p>The prince . . . understanding--that is, He does not perceive that oppression jeopards his success. Covetousness often produces oppression, hence the contrast.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v17)</div>
<p>doeth violence . . . blood, &amp;c.--or, that is oppressed by the blood of life (Gen 9:6), which he has taken.</p>
<p>to the pit--the grave or destruction (Pro 1:12; Job 33:18-24; Psa 143:7).</p>
<p>stay him--sustain or deliver him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:9; Pro 17:20). Double dealing is eventually fatal.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v19)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:4; Pro 20:4).</p>
<p>vain persons--idle, useless drones, implying that they are also wicked (Pro 12:11; Psa 26:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v20)</div>
<p>maketh haste . . . rich--implying deceit or fraud (Pro 20:21), and so opposed to "faithful" or reliable.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v21)</div>
<p>respect of persons-- (Pro 24:23). Such are led to evil by the slightest motive.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v22)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 28:20).</p>
<p>evil eye--in the general sense of Pro 23:6, here more specific for covetousness (compare Pro 22:9; Mat 20:15).</p>
<p>poverty . . . him--by God's providence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v23)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 9:8-9; Pro 27:5). Those benefited by reproof will love their monitors.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v24)</div>
<p>(Compare Mat 15:4-6). Such, though heirs, are virtually thieves, to be ranked with highwaymen.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v25)</div>
<p>of a proud heart--literally, "puffed up of soul"--that is, self-confident, and hence overbearing and litigious.</p>
<p>made fat--or, "prosperous" (Pro 11:25; Pro 16:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v26)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:6-8).</p>
<p>walketh wisely--that is, trusting in God (Pro 22:17-19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v27)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 11:24-26).</p>
<p>hideth his eyes--as the face (Psa 27:9; Psa 69:17), denotes inattention.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v28)</div>
<p>The elevation of the wicked to power drives men to seek refuge from tyranny (compare Pro 28:12; Pro 11:10; Psa 12:8).</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 29</p>
</div>
